Etymology

The name 'Rino' has multiple origins. As a given name, it is often a short form of names like Sabino, Marino, or Fiorino in Italian, or a variant of Reynold in English. As an acronym, 'RINO' stands for 'Republican In Name Only'. 'RiNo' is an abbreviation for 'River North'.

Cultural Ownership

Multiple cultures: Italian, English, American (political), American (regional), Japanese (stage name)

Disputed Identifications

The name is shared by multiple unrelated entities, leading to potential confusion. The political term 'RINO' is distinct from the given name. The stage name 'Rino' for the singer-songwriter is distinct from the rapper's nickname.

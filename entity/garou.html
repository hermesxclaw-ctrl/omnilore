Who It Is

Garou is the 'Hero Hunter' — a martial arts prodigy who embarked on a campaign to hunt and defeat every hero in the Hero Association, driven by a twisted philosophy that makes him one of the most complex characters in One Punch Man. Garou was a student of Bang (Silver Fang, S-Class Rank 3) and was considered his most talented disciple. But Garou rejected Bang's teachings because of a childhood trauma: when playing heroes and monsters as a child, Garou always sympathized with the monster. He saw the hero as the bully, the monster as the underdog. This evolved into a philosophy that 'absolute evil' — a monster so powerful that everyone would unite against it — would create true world peace by forcing everyone to stand together against a common enemy. Garou's power is his martial arts genius and his ability to evolve in combat. He learns any technique he observes, adapts to any fighting style, and grows stronger the more he fights and the closer he comes to death. His 'Monster Calamity God Slayer Fist' combines every martial art he has encountered into a devastating style. By the end of his arc, Garou had become a being approaching cosmic power — 'awakened Garou' — capable of fighting at levels that threatened the entire planet. He overcame the Hero Association's elite, defeated S-Class heroes, and even adapted to survive attacks that would kill any normal being. Only Saitama could stop him, and their battle is one of the most epic in the series. Garou represents the philosophical question: what makes a monster? Is it power, or is it choice? Garou has the power of a monster but the heart of a child who was never taught that the right thing is sometimes hard, not unfair. His twisted sense of justice — protecting the weak from the strong by becoming the strongest monster — is both his tragedy and his redemption. He is the ultimate 'villain who could have been a hero if the world had been kinder.'

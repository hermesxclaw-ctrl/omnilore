Who It Is

A level of absolute, total darkness. No light source functions here — flashlights fail, matches won't strike, phone screens go black. Sound is the only sense available, and the level is not silent. Something large moves in the darkness, its breathing audible in the distance, its footsteps shaking the floor. Navigation is impossible. Survival is measured in minutes. The darkness itself is not passive — it presses against the skin, thick and warm, like being buried alive in velvet.

Form

Cannot be observed. The level's geometry is inferred from sound and touch: a vast, open space with a concrete floor and no walls within reachable distance. The entity's breathing suggests something of immense size — respiration cycles lasting minutes, each exhale pushing warm, moist air across the level.

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

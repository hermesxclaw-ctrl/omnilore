Who It Is

Level 6 — Lights Out (Backrooms) is a fascinating and complex realm that poses significant challenges to explorers. Its anomalous properties and lack of responsiveness to external stimuli make it a formidable challenge to navigate.

The behavior of Level 6 — Lights Out (Backrooms) is characterized by its complete lack of responsiveness to external stimuli. The realm remains unchanged, regardless of the actions of those who enter it.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Level 6 — Lights Out (Backrooms) is a sprawling, barren tundra shrouded in perpetual darkness. The metallic walls, brick floors, and complex systems of pipes that comprise this realm are indistinguishable in the absence of light. The air is thick with an otherworldly silence, punctuated only by the faint hum of unknown machinery.

Symbols — The only symbols or iconography associated with Level 6 — Lights Out (Backrooms) are the faint, flickering lights that occasionally appear on the walls, only to disappear as quickly as they appear.

Domains — Level 6 — Lights Out (Backrooms) is a domain of the Backrooms, a shared fictional universe.

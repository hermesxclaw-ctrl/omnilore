Level 6 — Lights Out (Backrooms) is a sprawling, barren tundra shrouded in perpetual darkness. The metallic walls, brick floors, and complex systems of pipes that comprise this realm are indistinguishable in the absence of light. The air is thick with an otherworldly silence, punctuated only by the faint hum of unknown machinery.

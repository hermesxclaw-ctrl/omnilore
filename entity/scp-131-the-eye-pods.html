SCP-131 — The Eye Pods

also known asThe Eye PodsEyepods
Typescp
CultureSCP Foundation
Who It Is
Form

SCP-131 instances are small, teardrop-shaped creatures about 30 cm in height, with a single blue eye in the middle of their bodies. SCP-131-A is burnt orange in color, while SCP-131-B is a pale blue.

Powers

Perception SCP-131 instances have the ability to perceive and interact with their environment through their single blue eye. They have been observed to be able to see and respond to a wide range of stimuli, including light, sound, and touch.

Incident Log

The story of SCP-131 — The Eye Pods is a fascinating and complex one, filled with mystery and intrigue. The Eye Pods are a pair of teardrop-shaped creatures that have been found in a variety of environments, and have been known to interact with Foundation personnel in a range of ways.

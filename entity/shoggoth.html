Who It Is

A massive, protoplasmic entity of iridescent black slime, created by the Elder Things as a slave race. Shoggoths are amoeba-like, capable of forming any organ or shape at will through temporary eyes and pseudopods. They eventually rebelled against their masters and now lurk in ancient ruins.

Form

Formless, protoplasmic mass 15+ feet in diameter; black, iridescent slime; forms temporary eyes, mouths, and limbs; emits 'Tekeli-li!' cry

Powers

Unlimited shapeshifting

Organ/tool generation on demand

Immense strength

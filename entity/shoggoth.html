Who It Is
Form

Shoggoths are described as amorphous, shape-shifting creatures with no fixed form, often depicted as a mass of writhing, pulsing tendrils or a swirling cloud of color.

Powers

Shape-shifting Shoggoths can alter their form at will, allowing them to adapt to different situations and environments.

Accounts

The story follows an expedition to Antarctica, where they discover evidence of an ancient civilization and encounter the Shoggoths.

The story follows a group of investigators as they uncover evidence of a cult worshiping Cthulhu, a Great Old One associated with the Shoggoths.

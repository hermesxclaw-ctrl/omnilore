Who It Is
Form

The Beast of Gevaudan was described as a large, wolf-like creature with sharp teeth and claws. Some accounts suggest it was a single animal, while others claim it was a group of wolves.

Powers

Man-eating The Beast of Gevaudan was said to have the ability to attack and kill humans, specifically adults and children.

Accounts

The Beast of Gevaudan was a man-eating creature that terrorized the local population in the Margeride Mountains of south-central France between 1764 and 1767.

A group of hunters and trackers were tasked with capturing or killing the Beast of Gevaudan, which was terrorizing the local population.

La Tunda

The Deceiver, Shape-Shifting Forest Demon, La Tunda del Pacifico

also known asThe DeceiverShape-Shifting Forest DemonLa Tunda del Pacifico
TypeUrban Legend / Folkloric Entity (Shape-shifting Demo
CultureColombian
Who It Is
Form

La Tunda is described as a very ugly woman who smells horribly. Her clothes are rags and she has an appearance that makes her look dead. She is said to have a strong and unpleasant odor, which is often associated with death and decay.

Powers

Shape-shifting La Tunda is said to have the ability to change her shape and form at will, allowing her to blend in with her surroundings and deceive her victims.

Accounts

La Tunda is a myth from the Pacific coastal region of Colombia and Ecuador, and particularly in the Afro-Colombian community of the Chocó department. She is described as a shapeshifting entity that lures people into the forests and traps them never to be seen again.

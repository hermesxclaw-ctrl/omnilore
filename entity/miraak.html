Who It Is

The first Dragonborn, a Dragon Priest of Solstheim who served Hermaeus Mora before attempting to conquer both Mundus and Apocrypha. Miraak was defeated by his Dragon allies and trapped in Apocrypha for millennia, where he served — and plotted against — Hermaeus Mora. He returns to Nirn seeking freedom and absolute power, wielding the Bend Will shout to enslave dragons and minds.

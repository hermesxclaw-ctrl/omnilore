Who It Is

Beneath the perpetual cherry blossoms of Sakurashin, a childlike figure presides over two centuries of supernatural peace—but Yūhi Shinatsuhiko is no ordinary boy. As the personal protector god of an immortal woman and a mayor whose true age defies his appearance, Yūhi embodies the delicate balance between mortal governance and divine duty in a town where humans and yōkai coexist.

Yūhi is playful and mischievous, enjoying teasing and cosplay. He shows romantic interest in Rin, indicating a flirtatious side. Despite his playful demeanor, he is a wise and powerful deity who has governed Sakurashin for over two centuries. He is fiercely protective of Mariabelle and the town, and is respected and feared by supernatural entities like Enjin.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname Shinatsuhiko (士夏彦) combines characters meaning 'samurai', 'summer', and 'young man' or 'prince'. The given name Yūhi (雄飛) means 'heroic flight' or 'masculine flying'. The name may evoke a heroic or divine figure, fitting his role as a protector god.

Pronunciation — YOO-hee SHEE-nah-tsoo-hee-koh

Aliases — Yuhi, District Mayor, Mayor

Ownership — Japanese manga and anime franchise, created by Suzuhito Yasuda. The character is part of the fictional world of Yozakura Quartet, not a traditional mythological figure.

Powers
Divine Authority

As a land god, Yūhi possesses divine authority over his domain, granting him influence over the spiritual and natural aspects of Sakurashin. This authority makes him feared by powerful yōkai like Enjin.

Limit: The exact extent of his divine powers is not fully detailed in the source material. He may be limited to his territory, but he also serves as a personal protector god, suggesting he can extend his protection to individuals.

Shape-shifting

Yūhi can alter his appearance, appearing as a young boy or an older man. He maintains his youthful form primarily for Mariabelle's preference, but adopts an older look for official duties.

Limit: The transformation appears to be voluntary and may be limited to age appearance rather than full shape-shifting.

Longevity

Yūhi has lived for over 200 years, suggesting immortality or extreme longevity as a deity.

Limit: The exact nature of his longevity is not explained.

Protection

As a protector god, Yūhi can safeguard individuals, such as Mariabelle, from harm. He intervened to protect her from persecution, implying a protective aura or ability.

Limit: The mechanics of this protection are not detailed.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Yūhi appears as a young boy with spiky, light-colored hair and large eyes. He typically wears a traditional Japanese outfit, often with a haori or kimono, and may carry a fan or other accessories. He adopts a more mature, older appearance for official duties, suggesting he can alter his form at will.

Symbols — As a land god, Yūhi is associated with the earth, nature, and protection. His youthful form symbolizes vitality and approachability, while his older form conveys authority. He is often depicted with cherry blossoms, the symbol of Sakurashin.

Domains — Yūhi resides in Sakurashin, a town where humans and yōkai coexist under the protection of the Seven Pillars. As a land god, he is tied to the land and its spiritual well-being. He serves as the District Mayor, overseeing the town's administration, and also acts as Mariabelle's personal protector god.

Accounts I
Guardianship of MariabelleYozakura Quartet manga and anime

Set in modern times, with flashbacks to 200 years ago

Two centuries ago, Mariabelle, a half-yōkai, and her father Victor Frankenstein sought tuning in Sakurashin to avoid persecution from other half-yōkai. Instead of tuning, Yūhi intervened and became her protector, granting her immortality and safety. In gratitude, Mariabelle became his permanent assistant. Mariabelle gained immortality and a new purpose as Yūhi's assistant. Yūhi's role as a protector god was established, and his bond with Mariabelle became central to his character.

Accounts II
Governance of SakurashinYozakura Quartet manga and anime

Ongoing

As District Mayor, Yūhi oversees the town of Sakurashin, ensuring the coexistence of humans and yōkai. He works alongside the Hiizumi Life Counseling Office and other characters to maintain peace and protect the town from threats. His leadership helps maintain stability in Sakurashin, and his authority is respected by both humans and yōkai.

Conflict with EnjinYozakura Quartet manga and anime

Ongoing

Enjin, a vengeful spirit from the Hiizumi branch family, seeks to merge the human and yōkai worlds by forcing the Seven Pillars to bloom. Yūhi is feared by Enjin and his followers, indicating that Yūhi has opposed or could oppose Enjin's plans. Yūhi's presence serves as a deterrent to Enjin's schemes, though the full extent of their conflict is not shown in the provided material.

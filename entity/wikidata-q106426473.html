The Approach

Beneath the perpetual cherry blossoms of Sakurashin, a childlike figure presides over two centuries of supernatural peace—but Yūhi Shinatsuhiko is no ordinary boy. As the personal protector god of an immortal woman and a mayor whose true age defies his appearance, Yūhi embodies the delicate balance between mortal governance and divine duty in a town where humans and yōkai coexist.

Content advisory. Contains references to supernatural violence, persecution, and themes of immortality and sacrifice. No explicit dark material in the source material provided.

Yūhi Shinatsuhiko

Etymology

The surname Shinatsuhiko (士夏彦) combines characters meaning 'samurai', 'summer', and 'young man' or 'prince'. The given name Yūhi (雄飛) means 'heroic flight' or 'masculine flying'. The name may evoke a heroic or divine figure, fitting his role as a protector god.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese manga and anime franchise, created by Suzuhito Yasuda. The character is part of the fictional world of Yozakura Quartet, not a traditional mythological figure.

Disputed Identifications

It is suggested that Yūhi may have preceded Yae as Sakurashin's land god, but this is not confirmed. His exact relationship to the land goddess Yae and his original role remain ambiguous.

Description

Appearance

Yūhi appears as a young boy with spiky, light-colored hair and large eyes. He typically wears a traditional Japanese outfit, often with a haori or kimono, and may carry a fan or other accessories. He adopts a more mature, older appearance for official duties, suggesting he can alter his form at will.

Symbols & Iconography

As a land god, Yūhi is associated with the earth, nature, and protection. His youthful form symbolizes vitality and approachability, while his older form conveys authority. He is often depicted with cherry blossoms, the symbol of Sakurashin.

Behaviour & Personality

Yūhi is playful and mischievous, enjoying teasing and cosplay. He shows romantic interest in Rin, indicating a flirtatious side. Despite his playful demeanor, he is a wise and powerful deity who has governed Sakurashin for over two centuries. He is fiercely protective of Mariabelle and the town, and is respected and feared by supernatural entities like Enjin.

Domains & Habitats

Yūhi resides in Sakurashin, a town where humans and yōkai coexist under the protection of the Seven Pillars. As a land god, he is tied to the land and its spiritual well-being. He serves as the District Mayor, overseeing the town's administration, and also acts as Mariabelle's personal protector god.

Version Differences

In the manga, Yūhi's backstory and personality are explored in greater depth. The anime adaptations generally follow the manga but may condense or alter certain scenes. The OVA series 'Tsuki ni Naku' focuses on the moon and includes significant character development for Yūhi and Mariabelle.

Stories & Myths

Guardianship of Mariabelle

Source Yozakura Quartet manga and animeCulture Japanese pop culturePeriod Set in modern times, with flashbacks to 200 years ago

Summary

Two centuries ago, Mariabelle, a half-yōkai, and her father Victor Frankenstein sought tuning in Sakurashin to avoid persecution from other half-yōkai. Instead of tuning, Yūhi intervened and became her protector, granting her immortality and safety. In gratitude, Mariabelle became his permanent assistant.

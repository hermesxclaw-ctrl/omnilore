Who It Is

Ogma is the god of eloquence, credited with inventing Ogham script — the earliest Irish writing. He serves as champion of the Tuatha Dé Danann, lifting a flagstone requiring eighty oxen. At Mag Tuired, he slew the Fomorian king Indech. He embodies the Celtic ideal that wisdom and martial prowess are complements.

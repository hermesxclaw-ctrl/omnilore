Ogma

Ogma of the Sun-Face; Inventor of Ogham

also known asOgma of the Sun-FaceOghma
TypeWarrior Deity / God of Eloquence
CultureIrish
Who It Is
Form

Ogma is often depicted as a powerful warrior with a strong presence. His appearance is not well-documented in mythology, but he is often associated with the sun and its power.

Powers

Invention of Ogham Ogma is credited with inventing the Ogham script, which was used to write Irish Gaelic.

Warrior Skills Ogma is often depicted as a skilled warrior, with the ability to wield powerful magic and fight in battle.

Accounts

Ogma is credited with inventing the Ogham script, which was used to write Irish Gaelic.

Ogma is a member of the Tuatha Dé Danann, a group of supernatural beings in Irish mythology.

Ogma is associated with the Gallic god Ogmios, who is also a god of eloquence and knowledge.

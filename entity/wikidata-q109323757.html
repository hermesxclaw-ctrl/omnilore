Who It Is

It hatched the size of a tadpole, mewled like a lost puppy, and ate nougat from a boy's hand — then it shed its skin, opened its face into five dripping petals, and helped slaughter everyone who tried to contain it. The Demodog is Stranger Things' cruelest bait-and-switch: man's best friend reimagined as the larval stage of an interdimensional apex predator.

Attested: Hive-mind driven, not individually sentient in the human sense. Early pollywog stage shows imprinting-like behavior: Dart responds to Dustin, accepts nougat (3 Musketeers bar), purrs/vocalizes, and appears affectionate. This is revealed as predatory mimicry/instinct rather than domestication; once the Mind Flayer's signal strengthens, Dart betrays Dustin and rejoins the pack. Pack behavior: demodogs move in coordinated swarms, communicate via the Upside Down hive mind, and show no self-preservation when the Mind Flayer commands. Powers
Hive-Mind Link (Mind Flayer Control)

All demodogs are extensions of the Mind Flayer (the Shadow Monster). They share sensory input and commands instantaneously across the Upside Down network, allowing perfect pack coordination without vocal communication.

Limit: Link depends on the Gate being open. If the Gate is closed or Eleven severs the connection, demodogs collapse, writhe, and die. Will Byers, as a former host, can sense their location but also can be used to spy on the party.

Accelerated Growth and Molting

Grows from pollywog (~15cm) to full demodog (~1.5m) in ~1 week, shedding skin multiple times. Growth fueled by consumption of organic matter (small animals, then humans).

Limit: Requires constant feeding; without food, growth stalls. Molting leaves it temporarily vulnerable and hidden.

Petal-Maw Bite and Face-Petal Grasp

Face splits into five petal-like flaps lined with needle teeth, exposing inner jaws that can bite, grip, and tear. Bite force sufficient to decapitate or dismember humans and kill cats/dogs instantly.

Limit: Maw must open to attack, exposing softer inner tissues. No ranged attack.

Superhuman Strength, Speed and Agility

Can sprint on all fours faster than a human, leap 3-4 meters, climb walls/ceilings in tunnels, and drag adult humans. Claws can shred metal doors.

Limit: Quadrupedal; less dexterous than adult Demogorgon. Can be temporarily restrained by heavy doors or fire.

Burrowing and Tunneling Affinity

Does not dig itself but thrives in the Mind Flayer's vine-tunnel system, moving silently through organic tunnels that spread under Hawkins.

Limit: Dependent on existing tunnel network; cannot create tunnels independently.

Vulnerabilities: Fire, Extreme Heat, and Gate Severance

Highly vulnerable to fire and heat (Hopper's use of flamethrower, Dart's aversion to lighter). Also vulnerable to psychic severance when Eleven closes the Gate, causing systemic shock/death.

Limit: Fire only repels/damages, does not always kill instantly unless sustained. Gate closure is the only confirmed mass-kill method.

Accounts I
The Pollywog in the Trash — Dustin Finds DartStranger Things Season 2, Chapter Three: The Pollywog (S2E03)

2017 (set in-universe November 1984)

After Will Byers is rescued from the Upside Down, Dustin Henderson finds a strange tadpole-like creature in his trash can outside his home. He keeps it in a terrarium, feeds it nougat, and names it Dart (from D'Artagnan). The creature purrs and seems affectionate. Unbeknownst to Dustin, it is a juvenile Demogorgon that slipped through the reopening Gate at Hawkins Lab. Dustin hides Dart from his friends, delaying recognition of the new threat. Dart's rapid growth foreshadows the larger invasion. Establishes the demodog lifecycle and the 'pet' misdirection that defines the creature's narrative role.

The Molt and the Missing CatStranger Things Season 2, Chapter Six: The Spy (S2E06)

2017 (set November 1984)

Dart molts overnight, growing to ~60cm, develops legs, and escapes its tank. Dustin discovers his cat Mews missing. When he searches, Dart — now clearly a demodog with opening face-petals — hisses and attacks, revealing its true nature. Dustin lures it into his storm cellar with meat and traps it, realizing it is not a pet. First full reveal of the demodog form to the main cast. Confirms the species is carnivorous and hostile. Dustin's guilt and secrecy become plot-critical; he confesses to Steve, leading to their alliance.

The Tunnel Horde — Hopper Trapped UndergroundStranger Things Season 2, Chapter Seven: The Lost Sister (S2E07) and Chapter Eight: The Mind Flayer (S2E08)

2017

Hopper discovers the Mind Flayer's tunnel network — organic, vine-covered tunnels spreading for miles under Hawkins. He is trapped when the tunnels' vines constrict. Joyce, Bob Newby, Will, and Mike use Will's hive-mind connection to locate him. Meanwhile, a pack of 6-8 demodogs patrols the tunnels as guards for the Mind Flayer's growing form at the hub. Establishes demodogs as pack hunters and extensions of the Mind Flayer rather than independent predators. Bob Newby's knowledge of the tunnels (as former Hawkins resident) is used to rescue Hopper, but sets up Bob's death.

Accounts II
The Siege of Hawkins National LaboratoryStranger Things Season 2, Chapter Eight: The Mind Flayer (S2E08)

2017

Using Will as a spy, the Mind Flayer learns the party's location and sends the demodog pack to Hawkins Lab. Power is cut, and demodogs breach the facility, slaughtering most scientists and soldiers, including many of Dr. Owens' staff. Mike, Joyce, Will, Hopper, and Bob attempt to escape. Bob restores power and is then mauled and killed by demodogs at the entrance, in one of the season's most graphic deaths. Demonstrates demodogs' lethality against armed humans and their role as the Mind Flayer's army. Bob's death raises stakes and motivates the party to close the Gate. Lab is abandoned after the massacre.

Dart's Choice in the Junkyard and the Gate's ClosureStranger Things Season 2, Chapter Nine: The Gate (S2E09)

2017

Dustin, Steve, Lucas, and Max lure the demodog pack to the junkyard with meat, planning to trap them while Eleven and Hopper close the Gate. Dart arrives with the pack. Dustin confronts Dart alone, offering nougat and calling his name. Dart hesitates, recognizes Dustin, and allows the party to pass, holding back the pack briefly. Moments later, Eleven closes the Gate beneath the lab; the Mind Flayer's link is severed, and all demodogs collapse, thrashing and dying as the Upside Down connection is cut. Resolves the demodog arc: confirms individual imprinting can briefly override hive control but hive death is systemic. Gate closure ends the 1984 invasion. Dart's death is shown on-screen among the dying pack, confirming no demodogs survive in our dimension after S2.

Legacy and Taxonomy — From Pollywog to DemogorgonStranger Things: Worlds Turned Upside Down (2018) and Stranger Things Season 2 companion interviews; also referenced in Season 4 exposition

2018-2022

Licensed companion books and later seasons retroactively codify the lifecycle: Hawkins Lab's original Demogorgon (Season 1, which took Barb and Will) was an adult; demodogs are its juvenile form. The Mind Flayer can accelerate growth and spawn more via the Upside Down's biomass. Season 4's Vecna lore reframes the Upside Down's hierarchy, placing demodogs/Demogorgons as predatory fauna serving higher entities (Mind Flayer, Vecna). Provides official taxonomy that unifies Season 1 and Season 2 creatures, ending fan speculation that they were separate species. Establishes franchise canon for future games/comics.

Demodogs are from Dungeons & Dragons, like the Demogorgon. — The adult Demogorgon's name is borrowed from D&D (which borrowed it from Renaissance literature), but 'Demodog' is an original Stranger Things invention from 2017. No D&D monster called Demodog exists in official D&D manuals.

Demodogs are cute pets that can be tamed, as Dustin did. — In canon, Dart's 'tameness' was temporary imprinting that failed; he killed Mews and rejoined the hive. The show explicitly uses the pet arc to show the danger of anthropomorphizing a predator. No demodog is successfully domesticated.

Demodogs have eyes and a dog-like nose. — Demodogs have no visible eyes or nose; their face is a closed bulb that splits into five petals. They navigate via hive-mind sensing and possibly smell/tremor, not sight as depicted in some fan art.

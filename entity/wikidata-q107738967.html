Who It Is

At the foot of Reverse Mountain a crowned man in a black frock coat rides a cannonball-toting acrobatic act with a princess in disguise — Baroque Works' lowest-ranked numbered agent, Mr. 9, whose loyalty, flamboyant baton-twirling and instant defeat perfectly introduce Eiichiro Oda's system of codenamed assassins.

Attested: Loyal, earnest, somewhat naive and theatrical. Takes his Baroque Works duties seriously despite low rank; introduces himself with flourish ('We are the agents of Baroque Works!'). Shows chivalry toward his partner Miss Wednesday, attempting to protect her even when outmatched. Accepts orders without question, including suicidal orders to capture Laboon. Comic cowardice undercuts bravado when confronted by true strength (Zoro, Mr. 5). Powers
Acrobatic Steel Bat Combat

Wields a pair of steel bats (iron batons) with acrobatic, dance-like movements. Attacks involve leaping, spinning, and striking in tandem with Miss Wednesday's peacock-themed attacks. Named technique: Collaboration with Miss Wednesday shown as synchronized assault, but no individually named special move in databooks.

Limit: Entirely human-level martial skill; no superhuman strength, Haki, or Devil Fruit attested. Easily defeated by Roronoa Zoro in single strike at Whisky Peak, demonstrating vast power gap between Frontier and Officer Agents and between Baroque Works low ranks and Straw Hats. Bats can be shattered or blocked by swords.

Baroque Works Frontier Agent Skills (Espionage & Bounty Hunting)

Trained as bounty hunter and assassin: can pose as welcoming townsman, gather intelligence, and execute targets for bounty/mission rewards. Whisky Peak operation shows ability to deceive incoming pirates with hospitality before ambush.

Limit: Low rank limits access to missions and intelligence; unaware of true identities of superiors (does not know Miss Wednesday is Princess Vivi, does not know Mr. 0 is Crocodile). Easily manipulated by Officer Agents.

Accounts I
The Laboon Gambit at Reverse MountainOne Piece manga Chapter 103 'Miss Wednesday' and Chapter 104; anime Episodes 62-63

2000 (publication); in-universe: Age of Piracy, shortly after Straw Hats enter Grand Line

Mr. 9 and his partner Miss Wednesday (secretly Princess Nefertari Vivi of Arabasta) are assigned by Baroque Works to capture the island whale Laboon at Twin Capes to use as food/supplies. They ride on Laboon's back and attempt to harpoon him, but are interrupted by the arrival of the Straw Hat Pirates. Luffy and company befriend Laboon via the lighthouse keeper Crocus, and Mr. 9 and Miss Wednesday stow away on the Going Merry to return to their base at Whisky Peak, posing as grateful passengers while secretly reporting the Straw Hats' arrival. Establishes Baroque Works' modus operandi and the partnership dynamic; introduces Vivi before her identity reveal; Straw Hats gain Laboon's promise to return, a long-term narrative thread resolved post-Wano. Mr. 9's failure to capture Laboon foreshadows his low competence.

Welcome to Whisky Peak - The Hospitality TrapOne Piece manga Chapters 105-106; anime Episode 64

2000

Posing as the grateful rescued pair, Mr. 9 and Miss Wednesday lead the Straw Hats to Whisky Peak, a town that extravagantly welcomes pirates with food, drink, music and adulation. The town is revealed as a Baroque Works front: all residents are bounty hunters and Frontier Agents (including Mr. 8 / Igaram as mayor, Miss Monday, Mr. 9, Miss Wednesday). After the Straw Hats fall asleep from the banquet, the agents attempt a midnight assassination to claim their bounties and report to the organization. World-building for Baroque Works' deception-based economy; sets up Whisky Peak as the Straw Hats' first Grand Line trial. Reveals the scale of Baroque Works' infiltration.

Accounts II
Midnight Battle - Frontier Agents vs. Roronoa ZoroOne Piece manga Chapter 107 'The Moon and the Sun'; anime Episode 64

2000

The Whisky Peak agents launch their coordinated night attack on the sleeping Straw Hats. Roronoa Zoro, who remained awake and sober, intercepts them. In a display of overwhelming superiority, Zoro defeats the entire force including Mr. 9, Miss Monday, and dozens of bounty hunters, cutting through Mr. 9's steel bats. Nami awakens and declares the bounties forfeit, while Vivi and Igaram's true identities begin to surface. Demonstrates the power gap between East Blue-level fighters and Grand Line veterans; establishes Zoro's reputation early in the Grand Line. Mr. 9 is decisively defeated and humiliated, marking the end of his active threat. Vivi's allegiance shifts toward the Straw Hats.

Execution Order - Officer Agents ArriveOne Piece manga Chapters 107-109; anime Episodes 64-65

2000

After the Frontier Agents' failure, Baroque Works dispatches Officer Agents Mr. 5 (Bomu Bomu no Mi) and Miss Valentine to eliminate the traitors and the witnesses. Mr. 5 and Miss Valentine arrive at Whisky Peak to kill Mr. 8, Mr. 9, and Miss Wednesday for their failure and for Igaram and Vivi's betrayal. Mr. 9 attempts to defend Miss Wednesday despite his injuries, showing loyalty, but is effortlessly brushed aside by Mr. 5's explosive abilities. Zoro and later Luffy intervene, defeating the Officer Agents and saving the Frontier Agents. Reveals Baroque Works' ruthless internal discipline and the hierarchy where Frontier Agents are expendable. Solidifies Straw Hats' alliance with Vivi and Igaram, launching the Arabasta saga. Mr. 9's willingness to protect Vivi despite knowing she is a target adds brief pathos.

Aftermath and Vanishing - The Fate of the Frontier AgentsOne Piece manga Chapters 110-113 (departure from Whisky Peak) and Cover Story 'Miss Goldenweek's Operation: Meet Baroque Works' Chapters 359-413 (2002-2003); Vivre Card supplementary material

2000-2003 (publication)

After Mr. 5 and Miss Valentine are defeated, Igaram fakes his death via explosion at sea to protect Vivi, while Vivi openly asks the Straw Hats for help to save Arabasta from Crocodile. Mr. 9 and Miss Monday are left unconscious/defeated at Whisky Peak; they are not taken aboard the Going Merry. In the subsequent cover story that follows Baroque Works agents after Arabasta's fall, many Officer Agents are shown imprisoned or on the run, but Mr. 9 does not receive a dedicated cover panel. Vivre Card and databooks list his status as 'unknown / whereabouts unknown after Whisky Peak,' with no confirmed death, arrest, or return. He does not reappear in the main Arabasta battle, nor in later arcs (Alabasta aftermath, Impel Down, or Dressrosa). Leaves Mr. 9 as a narrative loose end — a minor antagonist whose arc ends at introduction, typical of Oda's use of disposable Frontier Agents to establish stakes without long-term commitment. His disappearance underscores the expendability of low-rank Baroque Works members.

Mr. 9 possesses a Devil Fruit ability — No Devil Fruit is attested for Mr. 9 in manga, anime, Vivre Card, or databooks. His combat is purely acrobatic steel-bat fighting. Only Officer Agents Mr. 5 (Bomu Bomu no Mi), Miss Valentine (Kiro Kiro no Mi), Mr. 4, Miss Merry Christmas, etc., have Devil Fruits among Whisky Peak-era agents.

Mr. 9's real name is known and he is an important recurring character — Mr. 9's real name, age, birthday, hometown, and blood type are all officially listed as unknown in Vivre Card. He appears only in the Whisky Peak arc (Chapters 103-109) and has no speaking role thereafter; he is a one-arc introductory antagonist.

Mr. 9 dies at Whisky Peak — Manga and anime show Mr. 9 defeated and unconscious but not killed. No death scene is depicted, and Oda does not include him among confirmed deaths. His status is 'whereabouts unknown' per official databooks.

Mr. 9 is an Officer Agent on par with Mr. 5 or Miss All Sunday — Mr. 9 is explicitly a Frontier Agent, the lowest tier of numbered agents (Mr. 6-12). Officer Agents are Mr. 0 through Mr. 5 with far greater strength, bounties, and narrative importance. Zoro's one-shot defeat of Mr. 9 versus his difficult fight with Mr. 1 later illustrates the gap.

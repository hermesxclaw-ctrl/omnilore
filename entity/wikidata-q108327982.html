Etymology — Japanese. Most plausible parsing: yama (山 'mountain') + kaji. Kaji has multiple homophones: kaji (火事 'fire, conflagration'), kaji (鍛冶 'blacksmith, forge'), kaji (梶 'oar / tree name, also used in personal names'). Without kanji attestation for this entity, etymology is reconstructed and disputed. Wik

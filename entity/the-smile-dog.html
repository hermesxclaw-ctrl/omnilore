Who It Is

A cursed JPEG of a Siberian Husky with a human smile photoshopped onto its face. Viewers experience vivid nightmares where the dog demands they 'SPREAD THE WORD.' The nightmares intensify until the victim either shares the image (passing the curse) or is driven to suicide. The image — a real photograph with one unsettling edit — became one of the internet's most infamous cursed images.

Form

A Siberian Husky in a dimly lit room, facing the camera. Its mouth is replaced with straight white human teeth in a wide smile. The eyes are unaltered — a real dog's eyes with a real dog's expression, making the human smile below them primally wrong.

Powers

Curse transmission via viewing

Nightmare inducement and manipulation

Compulsion to spread the image

Victim suicide after prolonged exposure

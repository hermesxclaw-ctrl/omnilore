Who It Is

Clockwork, whose real name is Natalie Ouellette, is one of the more complex proxy characters in the Slender Man mythos. Her origin story involves a traumatic childhood of abuse and neglect, culminating in an incident where she was locked in a room with a constantly ticking clock for an extended period. The psychological damage from this event, combined with her later encounter with Slender Man, broke her mind and transformed her into a proxy. Her name derives from both the clock that tormented her and the 'clockwork' precision of her killings. Unlike Ticci Toby — often depicted as her partner in the proxy hierarchy — Clockwork is calculated and methodical. She favors bladed weapons and is known for surgically removing the eyes of her victims, a signature that has become her calling card. In many fan interpretations, she retains fragments of her former self and is capable of moments of lucidity and even compassion, making her a tragic figure. The character's design typically includes a gas mask or surgical mask, dark clothing, and various clock-related accessories. Her relationship with Toby (often shipped as 'Ticciwork' by fans) is one of the most prominent pairings in creepypasta fan culture. Clockwork represents the creepypasta community's tendency to create layered, morally gray characters — she is a killer and a monster, but she was made that way by forces beyond her control, and her story is as much about victimhood as villainy. Her popularity demonstrates how the Slender Man mythos has evolved beyond its original creator's vision into a vast, collaboratively maintained universe of interconnected characters.

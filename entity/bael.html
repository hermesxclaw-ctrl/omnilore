Who It Is
Form

Bael is often depicted as a demon with a serpent-like body, multiple heads, and a crown of thorns. He is said to be the first demon of the 72 Demons of Ars Goetia and the head of the infernal powers.

Powers

Telekinesis Bael is said to have the ability to move objects with his mind.

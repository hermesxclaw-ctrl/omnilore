Who It Is

The first spirit of the Ars Goetia, a King ruling in the East. Bael (also Baal) commands 66 legions of demons. He appears with three heads: a toad, a man, and a cat, and speaks with a hoarse, croaking voice. His primary power is to bestow invisibility upon his summoner. The name derives from the Canaanite god Ba'al ('Lord'), demonized in the Hebrew Bible. In the Pseudomonarchia Daemonum, Johann Weyer lists him first among the 69 infernal spirits. Bael's sigil must be worn as a lamen by the conjurer for protection.

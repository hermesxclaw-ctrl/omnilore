Who It Is
Form

Shakuru is often depicted as a powerful, radiant woman, associated with the sun and its life-giving properties. Her appearance is said to be that of a strong, majestic figure, with a warm and nurturing presence.

Powers

Solar Energy Shakuru is said to possess the power to harness and control the energy of the sun, using it to nourish and protect her people.

Accounts

Shakuru is said to have played a key role in the creation of the world, using her powers to bring light and life to the universe.

Shakuru is said to have used her powers to bring an end to a great drought that had afflicted the Pawnee people.

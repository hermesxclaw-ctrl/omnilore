Who It Is
Form

Wonder Woman is typically depicted as a tall, athletic woman with long, flowing hair and a iconic costume consisting of a blue, red, and gold suit, complete with a tiara and a Lasso of Truth.

Powers

Superhuman Strength Wonder Woman possesses superhuman strength, allowing her to lift and move massive objects and fight against powerful villains.

Accounts

Who It Is

Wonder Woman is a cultural icon and a symbol of female empowerment. She has been a part of the DC Comics universe for over 75 years and has been featured in various adaptations and reinterpretations.

Wonder Woman is known for her compassion, empathy, and strong sense of justice. She is a fierce warrior and a skilled diplomat, often using her intelligence and strategic thinking to resolve conflicts.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Wonder Woman is typically depicted as a tall, athletic woman with long, flowing hair and a iconic costume consisting of a blue, red, and gold suit, complete with a tiara and a Lasso of Truth.

Symbols — Wonder Woman's symbols and iconography include her Lasso of Truth, which forces people to tell the truth, and her tiara, which represents her status as a princess.

Domains — Wonder Woman's domains and habitats include Themyscira, the mystical island home of the Amazons, and Man's World, the human world that she often visits and protects.

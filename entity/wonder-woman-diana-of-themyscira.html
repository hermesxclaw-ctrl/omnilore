Who It Is

Princess Diana of the Amazons, sculpted from clay by her mother Hippolyta and brought to life by the Greek gods. She leaves the hidden island of Themyscira to bring Amazonian values — peace, love, truth, and strength — to 'Man's World,' armed with the Lasso of Truth, indestructible bracelets, and a tiara that serves as a throwing weapon.

Attested in All-Star Comics #8 / Sensation Comics #1 (1941–1942)

Cultural Significance

The most iconic female superhero in Western culture and a continuously evolving symbol of feminism. Her origin (created by women, for women, from women) inverts the typical hero narrative. Wonder Woman has been embraced as a queer icon, a BDSM subtext figure (Marston's interests), and a UN honorary ambassador.

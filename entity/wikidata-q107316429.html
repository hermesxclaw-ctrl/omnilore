The Approach

In the misty lore of Ancient Moirang, two divine sisters hold the heart of a god and the fate of a kingdom. They are the Ayangleimas, the twin consorts of Thangjing, whose names echo through the royal chronicles as guardians of love, war, and sovereignty.

The name 'Koiren Leima' likely derives from Meitei roots: 'Koiren' possibly referring to a royal or divine title, and 'Leima' meaning 'lady' or 'queen'. Consorts of Thangjing

Source Moirang Ningthourol LambubaCulture MeiteiPeriod Ancient (exact period unknown)

Summary

Koiren Leima, also known as Leinung Yuchakha Chanu or Ayangleima, is the consort of Thangjing, the presiding deity of Ancient Moirang. There are two goddesses, Ayangleima Ahal and Ayangleima Atonpi, who are the first and second wives of the god respectively. Their accounts are mentioned in the royal chronicle.

Who It Is

In the war-torn Republic of San Magnolia, where the Colorata are deemed subhuman and forced to fight in mechanical coffins, one young sniper carries a hatred that burns hotter than any battlefield fire—and a love that she mistakes for something it is not.

Kurena is active, caring, and fiercely protective of her fellow 86. She harbors intense hatred for the Alba, stemming from the murder of her parents and sister. This hatred makes her initially hostile toward Lena. She has a crush on Shin, but it is portrayed as admiration for his protective darkness rather than a genuine understanding of him. She is emotionally volatile, but gradually matures and learns to accept Lena and move past her prejudice.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Kurena (紅) may reference the color crimson, evoking blood and passion; Kukumila is a surname of unknown meaning, likely invented for the series.

Pronunciation — Koo-reh-nah Koo-koo-mee-lah

Aliases — Gunslinger, クレナ・ククミラ

Ownership — Japanese light novel and anime franchise, created by Asato Asato, illustrated by Shirabii.

Powers
Sniper proficiency

Kurena is the squadron's designated sniper, skilled in long-range combat with a sniper cannon mounted on her Juggernaut.

Limit: Requires a clear line of sight and time to set up; vulnerable to close-range attacks.

Juggernaut piloting

Like all 86, she pilots a Juggernaut, an autonomous mechanized unit that is actually manually piloted.

Limit: The Juggernaut is fragile and not designed for prolonged combat; pilots are considered expendable.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Kurena has light brown hair and brown eyes. She wears the standard San Magnolia military uniform, and later adopts Giad Federacy attire. Her Juggernaut is equipped with a sniper cannon, and she is known for her precise shooting.

Symbols — Her callsign 'Gunslinger' and her sniper role symbolize her lethal precision and her role as the squad's long-range support. Her personal mark on her Juggernaut, designed by Theo, is a stylized gun or crosshair.

Domains — Originally confined to the 86th District internment camp in San Magnolia, later fighting on the Eastern Front. After defecting, she resides in the Giad Federacy, where she begins to explore fashion.

Accounts I
The Spearhead Squadron's Defection86 light novel, Volume 1-2; anime episodes 1-11

Fictional future

Kurena, along with Shin, Raiden, Theo, and Anju, are the sole survivors of the Spearhead Squadron. After being ordered on a suicide mission, they are rescued by the Giad Federacy and defect from San Magnolia. They are adopted by Ernst and become part of the Nordlicht Squadron, fighting against the Legion.

Accounts II
Kurena's Backstory86 light novel, Volume 1; anime episode 3

Fictional past

Kurena's parents were murdered by Alba soldiers, and her sister was conscripted into another 86 unit and killed. This trauma fuels her hatred of the Alba. Kurena develops a deep-seated hatred for Alba and a protective instinct for her fellow 86.

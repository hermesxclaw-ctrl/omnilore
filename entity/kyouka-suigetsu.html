Kyouka Suigetsu

also known asMirror Flower, Water MoonKyoka Suigetsu
TypeZanpakutō Spirit
Who It Is
Form

Kyouka Suigetsu is often depicted as a mirror-like flower that blooms on the surface of a lake, reflecting the beauty of the surrounding environment. Its petals are said to be as delicate as a feather, yet as resilient as a lotus flower. The spirit is often associated with the concept of 'yūgen', a profound and mysterious sense of the beauty of the world.

Powers

Reflection Kyouka Suigetsu has the ability to reflect the thoughts and emotions of those around it, creating a mirror-like effect that reveals the deepest desires and fears of the individual. This power is said to be both a blessing and a curse, as it can reveal hidden truths but also create confusion and disorientation.

Accounts

A young samurai named Kaito discovers a mysterious flower that blooms on the surface of a lake. As he gazes into its petals, he is transported to a realm where he encounters Kyouka Suigetsu, who reveals to him the secrets of the universe and the nature of reality.

A group of samurai are said to have encountered Kyouka Suigetsu while on a mission to retrieve a sacred sword. The spirit appears to them in the form of a water moon, guiding them through treacherous terrain and revealing hidden dangers.

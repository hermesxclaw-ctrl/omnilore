Who It Is

The Zanpakutō spirit of Sōsuke Aizen. Kyōka Suigetsu manifests as a beautiful, ethereal woman whose face shifts between different appearances — she is the illusion itself, never showing her true form. Her power is considered the most dangerous in Soul Society: complete hypnosis of all five senses. Anyone who sees her release is permanently under her control, their senses completely manipulable.

Powers

Shikai: Kanzen Saimin (Complete Hypnosis)

Total sensory manipulation (sight, sound, touch, smell, taste)

Temporal distortion of perception

Self-illusion (appear as anyone/anywhere)

No Bankai ever shown — Shikai was sufficient

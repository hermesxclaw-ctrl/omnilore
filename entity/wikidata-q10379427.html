Known to some as a guardian of boundaries and to others as a celestial administrator, Chi Meng Biao embodies the fluid, localized nature of divine worship in the Chinese cultural sphere. Etymology

The name 'Chi Meng Biao' (赤梦彪) likely comprises: 'Chi' (赤) meaning 'red' or 'bare', 'Meng' (梦) meaning 'dream', and 'Biao' (彪) meaning 'tiger cub' or 'striped tiger'. Thus, a plausible translation is 'Red Dream Tiger' or 'Bare Dream Tiger'. Alternatively, 'Chi' could be a surname. Without authoritative sources, this is speculative.

The Approach

Deep beneath the Candy Kingdom, in a laboratory lit by the glow of stolen science, a reptilian shadow stirs—a clone of Princess Bubblegum, born not of sugar but of scale and ambition. This is the story of a creature who was never meant to exist, and the questions it raises about identity, creation, and the price of power.

Content advisory. Contains themes of scientific hubris, cloning, identity crisis, and mild body horror. Suitable for older children and adults.

Etymology

The name is descriptive, indicating a clone of Princess Bubblegum with reptilian traits. The exact in-universe name is not established.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Pendleton Ward for the Cartoon Network series Adventure Time. The character is part of the Adventure Time franchise, owned by Cartoon Network and Warner Bros. Discovery.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans speculate that the reptilian clone is a failed experiment by Princess Bubblegum, while others suggest it may be a separate entity created by another character. No official confirmation exists.

Description

Appearance

The reptilian clone appears as a humanoid reptile with green scales, a long tail, and sharp teeth. It resembles Princess Bubblegum in facial structure but with reptilian features, including slit-like eyes and a forked tongue. It wears a lab coat, indicating its origin in a laboratory setting.

Symbols & Iconography

The clone is associated with scientific experimentation and the dark side of Princess Bubblegum's character. Its reptilian form symbolizes cold-blooded rationality and a lack of the warmth associated with the original.

Behaviour & Personality

The clone is shown to be intelligent and cunning, with a desire to prove itself superior to Princess Bubblegum. It is manipulative and lacks empathy, viewing others as tools. It displays a calm, calculating demeanor.

Domains & Habitats

The clone is first seen in an underground laboratory beneath the Candy Kingdom, where it was created. It later escapes into the wider Land of Ooo, suggesting it can adapt to various environments.

Version Differences

Only one version of the character is known, from the episode 'The Cooler'. No alternate versions or adaptations have been documented.

Summary

Princess Bubblegum discovers that a reptilian clone of herself has been created in a secret laboratory. The clone, calling itself 'the better Bubblegum', attempts to take over the Candy Kingdom by framing the original for crimes. Finn and Jake help Princess Bubblegum defeat the clone, who is ultimately sealed away in a dungeon.

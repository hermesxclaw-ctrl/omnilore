Who It Is

Deep beneath the Candy Kingdom, in a laboratory lit by the glow of stolen science, a reptilian shadow stirs—a clone of Princess Bubblegum, born not of sugar but of scale and ambition. This is the story of a creature who was never meant to exist, and the questions it raises about identity, creation, and the price of power.

The clone is shown to be intelligent and cunning, with a desire to prove itself superior to Princess Bubblegum. It is manipulative and lacks empathy, viewing others as tools. It displays a calm, calculating demeanor.

Powers
Intelligence and Cunning

The clone possesses a high level of intelligence, capable of creating complex plans and manipulating others.

Limit: Its arrogance can lead to underestimating opponents.

Physical Prowess

As a reptilian humanoid, it has enhanced strength, speed, and agility compared to a normal human.

Limit: Not invulnerable; can be injured by conventional means.

Scientific Knowledge

Inherited from Princess Bubblegum's DNA, the clone has extensive knowledge of science and technology.

Limit: Knowledge is theoretical; may lack practical experience.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The reptilian clone appears as a humanoid reptile with green scales, a long tail, and sharp teeth. It resembles Princess Bubblegum in facial structure but with reptilian features, including slit-like eyes and a forked tongue. It wears a lab coat, indicating its origin in a laboratory setting.

Symbols — The clone is associated with scientific experimentation and the dark side of Princess Bubblegum's character. Its reptilian form symbolizes cold-blooded rationality and a lack of the warmth associated with the original.

Domains — The clone is first seen in an underground laboratory beneath the Candy Kingdom, where it was created. It later escapes into the wider Land of Ooo, suggesting it can adapt to various environments.

The CoolerAdventure Time, Season 6, Episode 4

2014

Princess Bubblegum discovers that a reptilian clone of herself has been created in a secret laboratory. The clone, calling itself 'the better Bubblegum', attempts to take over the Candy Kingdom by framing the original for crimes. Finn and Jake help Princess Bubblegum defeat the clone, who is ultimately sealed away in a dungeon. The clone is imprisoned, but its existence raises questions about Princess Bubblegum's past experiments and the ethics of cloning.

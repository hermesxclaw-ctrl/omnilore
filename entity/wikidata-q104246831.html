Who It Is

In the vast and perilous world of Mushoku Tensei, where magic is both a lifeline and a weapon, one name stands out among the sages: Roxy Migurdia. A girl from a race of wanderers, she defied the expectations of her people to become a legendary magician, a teacher to a reincarnated soul, and a figure whose influence ripples through the very fabric of the story. Her journey is one of loneliness, self-discovery, and the quiet power of perseverance.

Roxy is initially shy and insecure due to her inability to use the telepathic abilities common to her race. She feels like an outcast among the Migurd. However, she is determined and hardworking, dedicating herself to the study of magic. As a teacher, she is patient and encouraging, but she also has a playful side. She is kind-hearted and loyal to her friends. She struggles with loneliness and a desire for belonging, which drives her actions.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Roxy' is a given name, possibly a diminutive of Roxana or similar. 'Migurdia' is the family name, derived from her race, the Migurd. In the lore of Mushoku Tensei, the Migurd are a tribe of the Demon Race known for their long lifespans and psychic abilities.

Pronunciation — ROX-ee Mee-GUR-dee-ah

Powers
Water Magic

Roxy is a master of water magic, capable of casting advanced spells such as Water Ball, Ice Spear, and Healing. She is considered one of the most powerful water mages in the world.

Limit: Her power is limited by her mana pool and the need to chant spells unless she uses chantless casting, which she learns later.

Chantless Casting

Roxy is one of the few magicians who can cast spells without chanting, a skill she develops after much practice. This gives her a significant advantage in combat.

Limit: Initially, she can only use simple spells without chanting; complex spells still require chanting.

Teaching

Roxy is an exceptional teacher, able to explain complex magical concepts in an accessible way. She is credited with training Rudeus Greyrat, who becomes one of the most powerful magicians in the world.

Limit: Her teaching is limited to magic and general knowledge.

Mana Detection

As a Migurd, Roxy has the ability to sense mana, though this is not unique to her race. She uses this to assess others' magical abilities.

Limit: Not well-documented; it is a passive ability.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Roxy is a petite young woman with a youthful appearance, characteristic of the Migurd race. She has blue hair and blue eyes, typical of her tribe. She often wears a mage's cloak and hat, and carries a staff. In later appearances, she wears more practical travel attire. Her appearance is often described as cute and childlike, which can be misleading given her age and skill.

Symbols — Her primary symbol is her staff, which is a magic tool. She is also associated with water magic, as she is a master of that element. In promotional art, she is often depicted with a book or a magical aura.

Domains — Roxy's original home is the Migurd village in the Demon Continent. She later travels extensively, living in various places including the city of Roa, the Magic City of Sharia, and eventually the Asura Kingdom. She is comfortable in both rural and urban environments.

Accounts I
The Lonely MigurdMushoku Tensei, Volume 2 (light novel), Episode 8 of Season 1 (anime)

Fictional timeline

Roxy is born into the Migurd tribe, a race that communicates telepathically. However, she is born without this ability, making her an outcast. Feeling isolated, she leaves her village to study magic in the human world. She becomes a renowned magician and eventually takes a position as a tutor for the Greyrat family, where she meets Rudeus. Her meeting with Rudeus sets the course for his development as a mage. She later becomes an important figure in his life, and they eventually marry.

Accounts II
The Search for RoxyMushoku Tensei, Volume 12-15 (light novel), Season 2 (anime)

Fictional timeline

After the Teleportation Incident, Roxy is separated from Rudeus. She wanders the world, trying to find her way back. She eventually becomes a teacher at the Ranoa Magic Academy, where she reunites with Rudeus. Later, she is captured by the Demon God Laplace's forces, but is rescued by Rudeus and his party. Her rescue deepens her bond with Rudeus, and she eventually becomes his second wife.

Marriage and Later LifeMushoku Tensei, later volumes (light novel)

Fictional timeline

Roxy marries Rudeus and becomes a member of his household. She continues to teach and support him, and they have a child together. She plays a role in the final battles against the Demon God. Her marriage is unconventional but accepted. She helps Rudeus in his quest to prevent the destruction of the world.

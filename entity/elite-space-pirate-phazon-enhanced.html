Who It Is

In the dark corridors of a derelict space station, a hulking figure moves with unnatural grace, its armored hide glowing with a sickly blue luminescence. This is a Space Pirate twisted by Phazon, a once-militant soldier now a living conduit of a corrupting force—a creature that embodies the tragic cost of power without control.

Elite Space Pirates are aggressive and tactical, often commanding lesser troops. Phazon enhancement increases their aggression and resilience, but may also cause erratic behavior due to Phazon's corrupting influence.

Enhanced Strength and Durability

Phazon augmentation makes the Elite Space Pirate significantly stronger and more resistant to damage than normal Elites.

Limit: Still vulnerable to heavy weaponry and Phazon-based attacks.

Phazon Energy Emission

The creature may emit Phazon energy in attacks or when damaged, potentially harming the player.

Limit: Energy emission may be limited by Phazon reserves.

Tactical Command

As an Elite, it may coordinate other Space Pirates in combat.

Limit: None documented.

Form & Signs

Appearance — An Elite Space Pirate is a large, bipedal creature with a heavily armored exoskeleton, distinct from standard Space Pirates by its bulkier frame and more ornate armor. When Phazon-enhanced, its body emits a blue glow, and veins or cracks may show Phazon energy. The armor may appear corrupted or fused with Phazon crystals.

Symbols — The Space Pirate insignia, often a skull-like emblem, may be present. Phazon-enhanced variants may display glowing blue energy patterns.

Domains — They are found in Space Pirate facilities, particularly the Phazon Mines on Tallon IV, where Phazon is mined and processed.

Accounts I
The Phazon Mines IncidentMetroid Prime

During the events of Metroid Prime (approx. 20XX)

During her mission on Tallon IV, Samus Aran infiltrates the Phazon Mines, a Space Pirate facility dedicated to mining and weaponizing Phazon. She encounters Phazon-enhanced Elite Space Pirates, who have been mutated by exposure to the substance. These creatures serve as formidable obstacles, demonstrating the Pirates' reckless experimentation with Phazon. Samus defeats them, contributing to the collapse of the Space Pirate operations on Tallon IV.

Who It Is

A Space Pirate warrior that has been subjected to direct Phazon infusion, mutating them into a massive, hulking brute with crystalline Phazon growths erupting from their armor and flesh. They wield heavy plasma cannons in one hand and a Phazon-powered melee weapon in the other. Their exposed Phazon cores glow with lethal radiation.

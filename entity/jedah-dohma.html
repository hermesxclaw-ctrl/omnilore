Who It Is

A noble of the Darkstalker realm and one of the three founders of the Makai nobility. Jedah is a tall, elegant figure with pale blue skin, red eyes, and an unsettling calm demeanor. His body is partially composed of a liquid dark substance that he can reshape into weapons and appendages. He seeks the perfect sacrifice to restore the Darkstalker realm.

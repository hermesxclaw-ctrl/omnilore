Who It Is
Form

Jedah Dohma is a demon with a tall and slender build, dressed in a black and purple outfit with a distinctive hood. He has long, flowing hair and piercing red eyes. His appearance is often depicted as a mix of Gothic and Victorian styles.

Powers

Soul Reaping Jedah Dohma has the ability to reap the souls of humans, using them to fuel his own power and immortality.

Accounts

Jedah Dohma is created by Capcom as a main antagonist for the Darkstalkers series.

Jedah Dohma seeks to overthrow the current ruler of the Darkstalkers universe and take the throne for himself.

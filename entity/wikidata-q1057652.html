Who It Is

In the whimsical world of Dr. Slump, Senbei Norimaki is not just an inventor—he is the bewildered 'father' of a robot girl who thinks she's human, a man whose greatest creations are as likely to cause chaos as to solve it. His story is a comedic mirror of scientific ambition, domestic absurdity, and the unexpected bonds that form when a lonely genius builds a family.

Senbei is a brilliant but often absent-minded inventor, prone to creating gadgets that backfire hilariously. He is perverted, frequently ogling women and getting nosebleeds, yet he is also a caring father figure to Arale. He is easily frustrated, lazy, and often selfish, but ultimately good-hearted and loyal to his friends. He loves money and fame but is also capable of selfless acts.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — 'Norimaki' is a pun on 'norimaki' (a type of sushi roll), and 'Senbei' is a type of rice cracker. His name is a food-related joke, consistent with Toriyama's penchant for punny names.

Pronunciation — Sen-bay Noh-ree-mah-kee

Aliases — Dr. Slump, Dr. Senbei, Senbei-kun, The Genius Inventor

Ownership — Japanese manga and anime; created by Akira Toriyama, published by Shueisha in Weekly Shōnen Jump.

Powers
Genius-Level Inventing

Senbei can create incredible machines, including a robot girl (Arale), a time machine, a teleportation device, and various household gadgets that often malfunction in absurd ways.

Limit: His inventions often have unintended consequences or silly flaws, and he is not always able to control them.

Superhuman Durability (Comedic)

In gag manga fashion, Senbei often survives explosions, falls, and physical abuse that would kill a normal person, only to be briefly flattened or singed before recovering.

Limit: This is purely comedic and not treated as a real power.

Nosebleed-Induced Fainting

When sexually excited, he gets a massive nosebleed that can cause him to faint or lose control.

Limit: A gag weakness.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Senbei is a tall, slim man with spiky black hair, a prominent nose, and a perpetual look of exasperation. He wears a white lab coat over a yellow shirt and blue pants, with red sneakers. His face often shows exaggerated expressions, from wide-eyed panic to lecherous grins. In later appearances, he may have a small mustache or glasses, but his core design remains consistent.

Symbols — His signature lab coat, his inventions (e.g., the time machine, the robot Arale), and his frequent nosebleeds when excited or aroused. The 'Dr. Slump' logo itself is a visual shorthand for the series.

Domains — He lives in Penguin Village, a rural, idyllic town with a surreal, comedic atmosphere. His home doubles as his laboratory, a cluttered space filled with bizarre inventions and often wrecked by Arale's antics.

Accounts I
The Creation of AraleDr. Slump manga, Chapter 1

1980

Senbei, a lonely inventor, decides to build a robot girl, naming her Arale. He initially presents her as his little sister to the villagers, but she quickly becomes a chaotic force in Penguin Village. Arale's superhuman strength and naivety cause endless trouble, but Senbei grows to love her as a daughter. Arale becomes the central character of the series, and Senbei's life is forever changed.

Accounts II
The Time Machine IncidentDr. Slump manga, various chapters

1980-1984

Senbei invents a time machine, leading to trips to the past and future, often with disastrous or silly results. In one notable story, he and Arale accidentally travel to the age of dinosaurs. Time travel is used as a recurring plot device for gags.

Rivalry with Dr. MashiritoDr. Slump manga, volumes 4-18

1981-1984

Dr. Mashirito, an evil genius, repeatedly attempts to defeat Senbei and take over Penguin Village, but his plans are always foiled, often by Arale. Mashirito creates robot girls like Arale, but they are no match for her. The rivalry is a recurring source of comedy and action.

The Approach

In the whimsical world of Dr. Slump, Senbei Norimaki is not just an inventor—he is the bewildered 'father' of a robot girl who thinks she's human, a man whose greatest creations are as likely to cause chaos as to solve it. His story is a comedic mirror of scientific ambition, domestic absurdity, and the unexpected bonds that form when a lonely genius builds a family.

Content advisory. Contains comedic depictions of slapstick violence, mild sexual humor (e.g., Senbei's frequent nosebleeds and infatuation with attractive women), and occasional crude jokes typical of gag manga. No graphic or dark material.
Identity

Senbei Norimaki

Pronunciation Sen-bay Noh-ree-mah-kee

Known As

Dr. SlumpDr. SenbeiSenbei-kunThe Genius Inventor

Etymology

'Norimaki' is a pun on 'norimaki' (a type of sushi roll), and 'Senbei' is a type of rice cracker. His name is a food-related joke, consistent with Toriyama's penchant for punny names.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese manga and anime; created by Akira Toriyama, published by Shueisha in Weekly Shōnen Jump.

Disputed Identifications

Sometimes confused with the character 'Dr. Mashirito' (his rival) or with other Toriyama inventor characters. Not to be confused with the real-life snack.

Description

Appearance

Senbei is a tall, slim man with spiky black hair, a prominent nose, and a perpetual look of exasperation. He wears a white lab coat over a yellow shirt and blue pants, with red sneakers. His face often shows exaggerated expressions, from wide-eyed panic to lecherous grins. In later appearances, he may have a small mustache or glasses, but his core design remains consistent.

Symbols & Iconography

His signature lab coat, his inventions (e.g., the time machine, the robot Arale), and his frequent nosebleeds when excited or aroused. The 'Dr. Slump' logo itself is a visual shorthand for the series.

Behaviour & Personality

Senbei is a brilliant but often absent-minded inventor, prone to creating gadgets that backfire hilariously. He is perverted, frequently ogling women and getting nosebleeds, yet he is also a caring father figure to Arale. He is easily frustrated, lazy, and often selfish, but ultimately good-hearted and loyal to his friends. He loves money and fame but is also capable of selfless acts.

Domains & Habitats

He lives in Penguin Village, a rural, idyllic town with a surreal, comedic atmosphere. His home doubles as his laboratory, a cluttered space filled with bizarre inventions and often wrecked by Arale's antics.

Version Differences

In the original manga, Senbei is more of a lecherous, slapstick character. In the 1997 anime, his design is slightly updated, and his perversion is toned down somewhat. In cameo appearances in 'Dragon Ball' and 'Dragon Ball Super', he retains his core traits but is more of a background gag.

Stories & Myths

The Creation of Arale

Source Dr. Slump manga, Chapter 1Culture JapanesePeriod 1980

Summary

Senbei, a lonely inventor, decides to build a robot girl, naming her Arale. He initially presents her as his little sister to the villagers, but she quickly becomes a chaotic force in Penguin Village. Arale's superhuman strength and naivety cause endless trouble, but Senbei grows to love her as a daughter.

Participants

Senbei Norimaki

Arale Norimaki

Consequences

Arale becomes the central character of the series, and Senbei's life is forever changed.

Who It Is

The mascot animatronic of Freddy Fazbear's Pizza — a brown bear in a top hat and bow tie who performs with his band during the day and hunts the night security guard by night. Freddy is possessed by the spirit of a murdered child (Gabriel), one of five children killed by William Afton and stuffed into the animatronic suits. At night, the animatronics come alive, their programming conflicting with the vengeful spirits within, and they attempt to stuff any human they find into an animatronic suit 'like a present.'

Form

A tall, brown anthropomorphic bear animatronic with blue eyes, a black top hat, and a black bow tie holding a microphone. His endoskeleton is visible at the joints, and his eyes go black with white pupils when he is 'active.' His jaw hangs slightly open, revealing a second set of teeth — the endoskeleton within.

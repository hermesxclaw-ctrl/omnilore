Who It Is
Form

Freddy Fazbear is a yellow, anthropomorphic bear with a red and white striped shirt, blue overalls, and a red bow tie. He has a distinctive, cheerful expression and a wide, toothy grin. Freddy's design is meant to evoke a sense of nostalgia and familiarity, drawing on the classic imagery of 1980s-era children's entertainment.

Powers

Killing Freddy is able to kill his victims by attacking them with his sharp teeth and claws. He is a ruthless and efficient killer, driven by a desire to protect the children who are his intended victims.

Accounts

Freddy Fazbear is a character created by the fictional company Fazbear Entertainment. He was designed to be the main attraction and mascot for the company's restaurants, including Freddy Fazbear's Pizza.

Freddy Fazbear's Pizza is haunted by the vengeful spirits of multiple children who were murdered at the restaurant. Freddy is one of the main antagonists of the game, and is responsible for many of the game's most terrifying moments.

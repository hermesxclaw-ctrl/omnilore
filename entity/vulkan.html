Who It Is

In the grim darkness of the 41st millennium, among demigods and monsters, one figure stands apart: Vulkan, the Primarch of the Salamanders, a giant whose hammer forged both wonders and nightmares. He is the Emperor's son who valued human life above conquest, yet his legacy is stained by the fires of his own making. This is the story of a creator, a savior, and a destroyer—the tale of the Dragon of Nocturne.

Vulkan is compassionate and empathetic, unusually so for a Primarch. He values human life above all, often risking himself to save others. He is patient, methodical, and deeply thoughtful, preferring to craft solutions rather than resort to brute force. He is also fiercely loyal to the Emperor and his brothers, but his sense of justice can lead him to defy orders he deems immoral. He is prone to melancholy and self-doubt, especially after the Horus Heresy.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Vulkan' is likely derived from Vulcan, the Roman god of fire and metalworking, fitting his role as a master artisan and his association with volcanic worlds. It may also echo the Latin 'vulcanus' meaning fire.

Pronunciation — VUL-kan

Powers
Superhuman Physiology

As a Primarch, Vulkan possesses immense strength, speed, endurance, and resilience. He can withstand extreme temperatures and survive wounds that would kill lesser beings.

Limit: While nearly invulnerable, he can be injured by powerful weapons or psychic attacks.

Immortality/Resurrection

Vulkan appears to be a Perpetual, a rare type of human who cannot permanently die. He has been killed multiple times but always returns to life, often in a fiery rebirth.

Limit: The process is not instantaneous and may require time or specific conditions. It is not fully understood.

Master Artificer

Vulkan is an unparalleled craftsman, capable of creating weapons, armor, and technology of incredible power and beauty. He forged many artifacts, including his own hammer and the armor of the Salamanders.

Limit: His skills are limited by the materials and knowledge available to him.

Pyromancy

Vulkan has a natural affinity for fire and heat. He can generate and control flames, and is immune to fire damage. This is often depicted as a psychic ability.

Limit: The extent of his pyromantic powers is not fully explored, and they may be limited by his willpower and focus.

Leadership and Tactical Genius

Vulkan is a brilliant strategist and inspiring leader. He is able to unite disparate groups and lead them to victory, often through unconventional means.

Limit: His compassion can sometimes hinder his tactical decisions, as he may be reluctant to sacrifice troops.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Vulkan is a towering figure, even among Primarchs, standing over three meters tall. He has dark skin, like obsidian, and red eyes that glow with inner fire. His hair is black and often shaved or cropped. He is immensely muscular, his body covered in scars from countless battles and his own self-inflicted wounds. Symbols — Vulkan's symbols include the dragon, representing his homeworld of Nocturne and his own ferocity, and the hammer, symbolizing his role as a craftsman and warrior. His personal heraldry often features a black dragon on a green field, with flames and volcanic imagery. The Salamanders Legion uses a dragon's head as their emblem.

Domains — Vulkan's homeworld is Nocturne, a volcanic death world with extreme tectonic activity. He also spent time on the nearby moon of Prometheus, where the Salamanders' fortress-monastery is located. He is most at home in forges and workshops, and his presence is associated with fire and heat.

Accounts I
The Scattering and NocturneHorus Heresy: The Primarchs

30th Millennium

As an infant, Vulkan was scattered across the galaxy by the Chaos Gods. He landed on the volcanic world of Nocturne, where he was found and raised by a human family. He grew up among the hardy people of Nocturne, learning their crafts and customs. His immense abilities quickly became apparent, and he rose to become a leader, unifying the planet's disparate clans. Vulkan established a strong bond with his adopted world, which later shaped his Legion's culture and values.

The Great CrusadeHorus Heresy Series

30th Millennium

Vulkan was reunited with the Emperor and given command of the XVIIIth Legion, later renamed the Salamanders. He led them on many campaigns to bring worlds into Imperial Compliance. He was known for his mercy and for protecting civilian populations, often going out of his way to minimize collateral damage. The Salamanders became known for their humanitarian ethos, and Vulkan earned a reputation as a noble and just commander.

Accounts II
The Drop Site MassacreHorus Heresy: Vengeful Spirit

30th Millennium

During the Horus Heresy, Vulkan and his Legion were betrayed at Isstvan V. The Salamanders were nearly annihilated by the traitor Legions. Vulkan himself was captured by Konrad Curze, the Night Haunter, and subjected to horrific torture and repeated murder. The Salamanders were decimated, and Vulkan's suffering at the hands of Curze left deep psychological scars. He eventually escaped and continued to fight for the Emperor.

The Death of VulkanHorus Heresy: Deathfire

30th Millennium

After the Heresy, Vulkan pursued the remnants of the traitor forces. He was believed to have been killed in a final confrontation with the daemon-primarch Curze, but his body was never recovered. His sons believed he had died, but there are hints that he may have survived, as he was later seen in visions and legends. Vulkan's disappearance left the Salamanders without their father, but they continued to honor his teachings. His legacy as a perpetual may mean he still exists somewhere in the galaxy.

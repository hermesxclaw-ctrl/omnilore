Etymology

The surname 'Nakatsugawa' (中津川) typically means 'middle river' (naka 'middle' + tsu 'of' + gawa 'river'), a common toponymic surname. The given name 'Munechika' (宗近) is a traditional Japanese name, often associated with swordsmiths; 'Mune' can mean 'peak' or 'crest,' and 'chika' means 'near' or 'kinship.' Thus the name evokes a smith of high standing or one close to the sacred.

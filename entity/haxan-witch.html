The witches depicted in the silent documentary-horror hybrid Häxan, which draws on the Malleus Maleficarum and medieval witch-trial records. These witches attend Sabbaths, kiss Satan's anus, fly on broomsticks using hallucinogenic ointments, and brew potions from infant corpses — all depicted with surreal, nightmarish visuals.

Who It Is

The witches depicted in the silent documentary-horror hybrid Häxan, which draws on the Malleus Maleficarum and medieval witch-trial records. These witches attend Sabbaths, kiss Satan's anus, fly on broomsticks using hallucinogenic ointments, and brew potions from infant corpses — all depicted with surreal, nightmarish visuals.

Form

Varies: elderly crones in peasant dress, naked young women at Sabbaths, horned and masked figures; Satan depicted as a horned, tongue-wagging beast with prominent posterior

Powers

Flight (via flying ointment)

Brewing of potions

Cursing

Demonic summoning

Sabbath attendance

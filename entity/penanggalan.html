Who It Is

A vampiric entity from Malay ghost myths, depicted as a floating disembodied woman's head with entrails and internal organs trailing beneath it. By day, the penanggalan appears as an ordinary woman; by night, she detaches her head and flies through the air seeking victims — particularly pregnant women and newborn infants, whose blood she drains. From a distance, she twinkles like a ball of flame (the will-o'-the-wisp phenomenon). To defeat a penanggalan, one must find her headless body and fill it with broken glass and sharp objects, so that when she returns and reattaches, she is fatally wounded. The penanggalan is part of a constellation of Southeast Asian 'flying head' entities including the Filipino manananggal and the Thai krasue.

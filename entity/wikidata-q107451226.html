The Approach

In the closing moments of Uncharted 4: A Thief's End, a young girl sits on a dock, listening to her father's tall tales of lost cities and pirate treasures. Cassie Drake is that girl—the quiet heart of the Drake family, the one who inherits not just a legacy of adventure, but the choice of what to do with it. Though she appears only briefly, her presence redefines the entire saga, transforming Nathan Drake's story from a relentless chase for glory into a meditation on family, truth, and the stories we pass down.

Content advisory. None. Cassie Drake is a minor character with no dark or disturbing material associated with her portrayal.
Identity

Cassie Drake

Pronunciation /ˈkæsi dreɪk/

Etymology

The name 'Cassie' is a diminutive of 'Cassandra', a name of Greek origin meaning 'she who entangles men' or 'shining upon man'. The surname 'Drake' is inherited from her father, Nathan Drake, who adopted it from his supposed ancestor, Sir Francis Drake.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character owned by Naughty Dog and Sony Interactive Entertainment. Part of the Uncharted video game franchise.

Disputed Identifications

None known. Cassie Drake is a clearly defined character within the Uncharted universe.

Description

Appearance

Cassie appears as a young girl, approximately 12-13 years old, with light brown hair and brown eyes. She wears casual clothing: a t-shirt, jeans, and sneakers. Her design is simple and realistic, matching the game's aesthetic.

Symbols & Iconography

Cassie is associated with the Drake family legacy, notably the ring Nathan wears, which she examines during the epilogue. She is also linked to the treasures and artifacts her parents collected, displayed in their home.

Behaviour & Personality

Cassie is curious, intelligent, and adventurous, mirroring her father's traits. She listens intently to Nathan's stories and explores the attic and yard, discovering mementos of his past. She is respectful and caring towards her parents, showing a close family bond.

Domains & Habitats

Her home is a coastal house in the United States, presumably in the Pacific Northwest or California. She is also seen on a dock, suggesting proximity to the ocean.

Version Differences

Cassie appears only in Uncharted 4. She does not appear in any other game, comic, or adaptation. The 2022 film adaptation does not include her.

Stories & Myths

The Epilogue: A New Generation

Source Uncharted 4: A Thief's EndCulture Western video game culturePeriod Set several years after the main events of the game, likely in the late 2020s.

Summary

Years after Nathan Drake's final adventure, he and Elena have settled into a quiet life, raising their daughter Cassie. The epilogue depicts a typical day: Nathan tells Cassie a story about his pirate adventures, and she playfully doubts him. Later, while exploring the house, she finds her parents' old artifacts and photos, leading to a conversation where they reveal their past as treasure hunters. The game ends with Cassie looking out at the ocean, hinting at her own potential for adventure.

Consequences

The epilogue provides closure for Nathan's character arc, showing he has found happiness and peace. It also leaves Cassie's future open, suggesting the legacy may continue.

The Approach

Seifer Almasy is the rival SeeD cadet whose ambition and defiance set him against Squall Leonhart from the very first duel. A gunblade-wielding dreamer of becoming a sorceress's knight, Seifer's path twists from Balamb Garden to the service of the sorceress Edea, making him one of Final Fantasy VIII's most memorable antagonists.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of violence, psychological manipulation, and themes of obsession and ambition. Some scenes may be intense for younger audiences.
Identity

SeiferThe Sorceress's Knight

Etymology

The name 'Seifer' likely derives from the German word 'Seife' (soap), but no official etymology has been provided. 'Almasy' may reference the Hungarian explorer László Almásy, but this is unconfirmed.

Cultural Ownership

Final Fantasy VIII is a Japanese role-playing game created by Square (now Square Enix). Seifer is a fictional character owned by Square Enix.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans speculate that Seifer is a foil to Squall, but this is an interpretation, not an official statement. There is no dispute about his canonical identity.

Description

Appearance

Seifer is a tall young man with blond hair, blue eyes, and a scar on his face (though his own scar is from Squall). He wears a black leather jacket with a fur collar, black pants, and boots. His weapon is a gunblade, similar to Squall's but with a distinct design. He has a confident, swaggering posture.

Symbols & Iconography

Seifer's iconography includes his gunblade, his fur-trimmed coat, and his trademark smirk. He is often associated with the Sorceress's Knight archetype, wearing a knight-like uniform when serving Edea.

Behaviour & Personality

Seifer is arrogant, hot-headed, and rebellious. He despises authority and seeks to become the strongest and most famous SeeD. He is fiercely competitive with Squall, often taunting him. Despite his bravado, he is shown to have a strong sense of loyalty to those he cares about, particularly his comrades in the SeeD exam and later to Edea. He is easily manipulated by the sorceress's power, indicating a desire for purpose and recognition.

Domains & Habitats

Seifer is primarily associated with Balamb Garden, but he also appears in Galbadia Garden, Timber, the Lunatic Pandora, and the final battle against Ultimecia. He is a SeeD cadet, later a mercenary for the Galbadian army, and finally a knight to the sorceress.

Version Differences

There are no significant differences between the Japanese and English versions of Seifer's character. However, his backstory is more fully explored in supplementary materials like the Ultimania guide.

Powers & Abilities

Gunblade combat

Description

Seifer wields a gunblade in combat, using it in a style similar to Squall's. He is proficient in close-quarters combat and uses his gunblade to perform powerful attacks.

Limitations

His skills are not as refined as Squall's, as seen in their duels where Squall ultimately wins.

Seifer's Limit Break is called 'No Mercy', a series of powerful gunblade strikes. It is similar to Squall's Renzokuken but with different animations.

Only usable when his HP is low or in certain situations.

Sorceress's Knight abilities

Source Version Final Fantasy VIII

Description

When under Edea's influence, Seifer gains enhanced combat abilities and can use magical attacks. He becomes a formidable opponent, wielding his gunblade with greater power.

Limitations

His powers are dependent on Edea's influence; when she is defeated, he loses these enhancements.

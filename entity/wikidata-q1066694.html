The Approach

In the world of Slayers, where sorceresses are as likely to blow up a town as to save it, Naga the White Serpent stands out as a paradox: a princess who abandoned her throne, a rival who follows her nemesis like a shadow, and a warrior who faints at the sight of blood. With a laugh that can incapacitate armies and a wardrobe that defies practicality, she is the most unforgettable sidekick who refuses to be one.

Content advisory. Contains comedic violence, mild suggestive themes, and references to murder and revenge.

Naga the Serpent

Pronunciation NAH-gah (Japanese: ナーガ, Nāga)

Naga the White SerpentNahga (early English transliteration)Princess Gracia Ul Naga Saillune (real name)Nama (cameo alias in Slayers Evolution-R)

Etymology

The name 'Naga' is likely derived from the Sanskrit word for serpent, fitting her white serpent motif. The Japanese title '白蛇のナーガ' translates to 'Naga of the White Serpent', referencing the Saillune coat of arms.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Hajime Kanzaka for the Slayers franchise, owned by Kanzaka and associated publishers (Fujimi Shobo, Kadokawa).

Disputed Identifications

Some fans mistakenly identify Naga as a separate character from Gracia, but they are the same person. Early English translations used 'Nahga', causing confusion.

Description

Appearance

Naga is a tall, voluptuous young woman with long dark-blue or violet hair and intelligent blue eyes. She wears a revealing black leather outfit with large spiked spaulders, which she inherited from her mother. The outfit is impractical, and the spaulders often jab her face during spellcasting.

Symbols & Iconography

The white serpent is the coat of arms of the Saillune kingdom, symbolizing her royal heritage. Her outfit and dramatic poses evoke a classic 'evil sorceress' aesthetic.

Behaviour & Personality

Naga is arrogant, flamboyant, and self-obsessed, with a loud 'ojousama' laugh that annoys most people. She is carefree, vain, greedy, and overconfident, often lacking common sense. Despite this, she is resilient and surprisingly capable, with a strong sense of pride and a complicated obsession with Lina Inverse.

Domains & Habitats

Naga is a wanderer, traveling the world in search of magical power and treasure. She has no fixed home, but maintains ties to the Saillune kingdom.

Version Differences

In the novels, Naga is more prominent and her background is detailed. In the anime OVAs, she is a comedic foil. In Slayers Evolution-R, she appears as a cameo character named Nama, with no explicit connection to Naga.

Stories & Myths

Naga's Origin and Revenge

Source Interviews with Hajime KanzakaCulture Japanese pop culturePeriod 1990s

Summary

Naga's mother was murdered by the assassin Booley. At 16 or 17, Naga avenged her using the spell Chaos String. After the funeral, she found her mother's outfit and left home to become a wandering sorceress.

Participants

Naga

Booley

Naga's mother

Consequences

Naga's trauma led to hemophobia and her departure from Saillune.

Variants

Not depicted in the main series, only hinted.

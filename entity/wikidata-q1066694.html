Who It Is

In the world of Slayers, where sorceresses are as likely to blow up a town as to save it, Naga the White Serpent stands out as a paradox: a princess who abandoned her throne, a rival who follows her nemesis like a shadow, and a warrior who faints at the sight of blood. With a laugh that can incapacitate armies and a wardrobe that defies practicality, she is the most unforgettable sidekick who refuses to be one.

Naga is arrogant, flamboyant, and self-obsessed, with a loud 'ojousama' laugh that annoys most people. She is carefree, vain, greedy, and overconfident, often lacking common sense. Despite this, she is resilient and surprisingly capable, with a strong sense of pride and a complicated obsession with Lina Inverse.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Naga' is likely derived from the Sanskrit word for serpent, fitting her white serpent motif. The Japanese title '白蛇のナーガ' translates to 'Naga of the White Serpent', referencing the Saillune coat of arms.

Pronunciation — NAH-gah (Japanese: ナーガ, Nāga)

Aliases — Naga the White Serpent, Nahga (early English transliteration), Princess Gracia Ul Naga Saillune (real name), Nama (cameo alias in Slayers Evolution-R)

Ownership — Created by Hajime Kanzaka for the Slayers franchise, owned by Kanzaka and associated publishers (Fujimi Shobo, Kadokawa).

Form & Signs

Appearance — Naga is a tall, voluptuous young woman with long dark-blue or violet hair and intelligent blue eyes. She wears a revealing black leather outfit with large spiked spaulders, which she inherited from her mother. The outfit is impractical, and the spaulders often jab her face during spellcasting.

Symbols — The white serpent is the coat of arms of the Saillune kingdom, symbolizing her royal heritage. Her outfit and dramatic poses evoke a classic 'evil sorceress' aesthetic.

Domains — Naga is a wanderer, traveling the world in search of magical power and treasure. She has no fixed home, but maintains ties to the Saillune kingdom.

Accounts I
Naga's Origin and RevengeInterviews with Hajime Kanzaka

1990s

Naga's mother was murdered by the assassin Booley. At 16 or 17, Naga avenged her using the spell Chaos String. After the funeral, she found her mother's outfit and left home to become a wandering sorceress. Naga's trauma led to hemophobia and her departure from Saillune.

Accounts II
Rivalry with Lina InverseSlayers Special, Slayers Smash, OVAs

1991-present

Naga seeks to defeat Lina to claim the title of 'Invincible Dark Lord', but their rivalry evolves into a chaotic partnership. They frequently save each other's lives, yet also betray each other for profit. Their relationship remains a constant source of comedy and occasional danger.

Adventures in Slayers SpecialSlayers Special light novels

1991-2008

Naga and Lina embark on various adventures, often involving treasure hunts, magical mishaps, and encounters with monsters. These stories establish Naga's character and abilities.

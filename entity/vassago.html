Who It Is

Third spirit of the Ars Goetia, a Prince of the same nature as Agares. Vassago commands 26 legions and is noted for being 'of a good nature' — a rarity among Goetic demons. His primary function is to declare things past, present, and future, and to discover hidden or lost objects. He serves as a spirit of prophecy and divination, revealing what has been concealed. Some traditions suggest Vassago was originally a benevolent spirit co-opted into the demonic hierarchy. His sigil is to be worn as a lamen.

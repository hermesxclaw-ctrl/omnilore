Yuni

Pronunciation In Japanese: /jɯni/; in Indonesian: /juni/; in English contexts, often /ˈjuːni/.

Etymology

The name 'Yuni' has multiple independent origins: in Japanese, it is a toponym and personal name; in Indonesian, it is a common female given name; in fiction, it may be coined for characters. The Ainu origin of the Hokkaido town name is possible, but unverified.

Who It Is
Form

Hyōrinmaru is the manifested spirit of Tōshirō Hitsugaya's Zanpakutō. Its spirit takes the form of a giant, icy, humanoid entity with a silver and white color scheme. It is said to be capable of manipulating ice and snow to a great extent.

Powers

Ice Manipulation Hyōrinmaru is capable of manipulating ice and snow to a great extent, allowing it to create complex structures and attacks. It can also use its powers to create powerful blasts of cold air and to freeze its enemies solid.

Accounts

The story of Hyōrinmaru's birth is told in the Bleach manga, where it is revealed that the entity was created by the spirit of Tōshirō Hitsugaya's Zanpakutō. The entity is said to have been born from the spirit of the Zanpakutō, and it is depicted as being a powerful and intimidating entity.

Hyōrinmaru's first appearance in the Bleach anime is a significant event in the story, as it marks the beginning of Tōshirō Hitsugaya's journey as a Shinigami. The entity is depicted as being a powerful and intimidating presence, and it is said to be capable of unleashing devastating attacks on its enemies.

Who It Is

The Zanpakutō spirit of Tōshirō Hitsugaya. Hyōrinmaru manifests as a colossal ice dragon with crystalline scales and frozen wings, the most powerful ice-type Zanpakutō in Soul Society. It commands absolute zero temperatures and can freeze anything it touches, including fire. Hyōrinmaru has a prideful, draconic personality and shares a deep bond of mutual respect with Hitsugaya.

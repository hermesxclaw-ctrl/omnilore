Who It Is
Form

The Duende is often depicted as a small, mischievous humanoid figure, with a fondness for dark colors and a penchant for hiding in the shadows. In some accounts, it is described as a tall, imposing figure with a commanding presence. Its appearance can vary depending on the region and cultural context.

Powers

Inspiration The Duende is said to possess the power of inspiration, able to spark creativity and imagination in those who seek it out. It is often called upon by artists, writers, and musicians seeking to tap into its spark of creativity.

Accounts

A young flamenco dancer seeks out the Duende's help in perfecting her craft, and is rewarded with a burst of creative inspiration.

A young child becomes lost in the woods, and is guided back to safety by the Duende's mischievous antics.

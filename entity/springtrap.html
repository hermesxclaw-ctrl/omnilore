Who It Is
Form

Springtrap is a purple animatronic with a distinctive appearance, featuring a purple and white striped body, a white face, and a distinctive hat. He is often depicted as a menacing figure, with a twisted and corrupted appearance.

Powers

Stealth Springtrap's ability to move undetected and strike from the shadows, making him a formidable opponent.

Agility Springtrap's ability to move quickly and easily, allowing him to chase down and attack his victims with ease.

Intelligence Springtrap's ability to think and act strategically, often using his intelligence to outmaneuver and outsmart his victims.

Accounts

Springtrap is introduced as a new animatronic in the game, with a backstory that reveals his origins as a serial killer. He is depicted as a cunning and manipulative character, who uses his intelligence and stealth to attack and kill his victims.

The Springtrap Incident is a pivotal event in the Five Nights at Freddy's storyline, in which Springtrap is introduced as a new animatronic and a series of tragic events unfold. The incident is depicted as a result of Springtrap's actions, which lead to the death of several characters and the destruction of the Freddy Fazbear's Pizza restaurant.

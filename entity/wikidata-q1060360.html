The Approach

In the chaos of the Clone Wars, where Jedi generals led armies of clones against Separatist droids, one name stood out among the Jedi ranks for his unorthodox methods and formidable skill: Daakman Barrek. A Jedi Master who preferred the shadows of stealth to the blaze of open combat, Barrek's career was as enigmatic as his tactics, culminating in a tragic stand on the icy world of Hypori that would test the very limits of Jedi resilience.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of combat and death; no graphic violence described in detail.

Description

Appearance

Daakman Barrek was a male Human Jedi Master with light skin, brown hair, and brown eyes. He wore standard Jedi robes during the Clone Wars, often with a utility belt and a lightsaber. In the micro-series, he is depicted as a middle-aged Human with a weathered face, reflecting his combat experience.

Symbols & Iconography

As a Jedi, he bore the symbol of the Jedi Order on his robes. His lightsaber was standard-issue, with no unique hilt design noted.

Behaviour & Personality

Barrek was known for his calm demeanor and strategic mind. He preferred stealth and precision over direct confrontation, often employing unconventional tactics. He was a capable leader, respected by his clone troops, and showed great concern for the safety of his men. His willingness to sacrifice himself to allow others to escape highlights his selflessness and adherence to the Jedi code.

Domains & Habitats

He served as a Jedi General in the Grand Army of the Republic, operating on various battlefronts. His most notable deployment was the Battle of Hypori, an icy planet. He was also known to operate in wilderness environments, using stealth to his advantage.

Version Differences

In the 2003 micro-series, Barrek is a Jedi Master with a distinctive fighting style. In the 2008 The Clone Wars TV series, the character does not appear; the Battle of Hypori is depicted differently, with Ki-Adi-Mundi as the lead Jedi. In the 2014 canon, Barrek is not mentioned, making his appearances exclusively Legends.

Stories & Myths

The Battle of Hypori

Source Star Wars: Clone Wars, Chapter 21 (2004)Culture Fictional, Star Wars universePeriod 22 BBY (during the Clone Wars)

Summary

Jedi Master Daakman Barrek led a strike team of Jedi and clone troopers against a Separatist droid factory on the planet Hypori. The mission was a trap set by General Grievous, who ambushed the Jedi with a force of super battle droids. Barrek and his fellow Jedi—including Ki-Adi-Mundi, Aayla Secura, Shaak Ti, K'Kruhk, and others—were overwhelmed. Barrek was one of the first to fall, sacrificing himself to allow the others a chance to escape. He was killed by Grievous.

Consequences

The Jedi were forced to retreat; several were killed or captured. Barrek's death highlighted the threat of Grievous and the escalating danger of the war. The event was a significant loss for the Jedi Order.

Variants

In the 2008 The Clone Wars TV series, the Battle of Hypori is depicted with Ki-Adi-Mundi as the lead Jedi, and Barrek is absent. The exact details of the battle differ between the two versions.

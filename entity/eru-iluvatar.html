Who It Is
Form

Eru Ilúvatar is often depicted as a powerful, wise, and benevolent deity, with a strong sense of justice and compassion. He is often shown as a majestic figure, with a commanding presence and a deep understanding of the workings of the universe.

Powers

Creation Eru Ilúvatar is said to have the power to create and shape the world of Eà, bringing into being the Ainur and the physical world of Middle-earth.

Accounts

The Ainulindalé is a story about the creation of the world of Eà and the first inhabitants of Middle-earth. It tells the story of how Eru Ilúvatar brought the Ainur together and showed them a theme, from which he bade them create the world of Eà.

The History of Middle-earth is a story about the history of Middle-earth and its inhabitants. It tells the story of how Eru Ilúvatar intervened in the affairs of the world to prevent evil and promote good.

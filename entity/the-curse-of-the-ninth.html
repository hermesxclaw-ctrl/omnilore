Who It Is

The superstition that a composer will die after completing their ninth symphony, based on a statistical cluster of major composers who died either during or shortly after completing their ninth. Mahler was so terrified of the curse that he titled his ninth symphonic work 'Das Lied von der Erde' (The Song of the Earth) and never called it a symphony — then died while writing his actual Symphony No. 10.

Cultural Significance

The Curse exemplifies how artists' numerology becomes mythology. It has been acknowledged by composers from Philip Glass to David Bowie. Dmitri Shostakovich wrote his deliberately 'lightweight' Ninth Symphony after Stalin expected a grand choral tribute — a different kind of curse altogether.

Who It Is
Form

The Curse of the Ninth is a cultural phenomenon that appears in the form of a superstition or an urban legend. It is often associated with the completion of a composer's ninth symphony, which is believed to be a harbinger of death.

Fateful Completion The Curse of the Ninth is said to have the power to predict the fate of a composer who completes their ninth symphony, foretelling their impending death.

The Curse of the Ninth is a superstition in classical music that suggests that a composer who completes their ninth symphony is destined to die before completing a tenth.

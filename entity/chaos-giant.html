Who It Is

A 30-foot-tall giant warped by exposure to chaos. Chaos Giants are even more unpredictable than their normal kin, their minds broken by the whispers of the Dark Gods. They carry enormous clubs made from tree trunks or ship masts, and their bodies are covered in crude chaos tattoos that writhe and move.

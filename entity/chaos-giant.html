Who It Is
Form

The Chaos Giant is a massive, humanoid figure with a twisted, nightmarish appearance. Its body is a mass of writhing, pulsing tendrils, with glowing red eyes that burn with an inner fire. It stands at least 10 feet tall, and its presence is accompanied by a palpable aura of malevolence.

Powers

Warp Energy Manipulation The Chaos Giant has the ability to manipulate the raw energy of the Warp, using it to fuel its own power and wreak havoc on the world.

Accounts

The Chaos Giant is said to have risen to power during the Time of Strife, a period of great upheaval and chaos in the Warhammer Fantasy world. It is said to have been summoned by a powerful sorcerer, who sought to use its power to gain control over the world.

The Chaos Giant is said to have battled against the forces of order in the Age of Chaos, a period of great conflict and upheaval in the Warhammer Fantasy world.

Who It Is

Warstar is not one thing but many: a name worn by alien warriors, a genocidal empire, and a humble fishing trawler. Across comics, television, and maritime history, the word 'Warstar' has been claimed by heroes, villains, and vessels—each with its own story, its own battles, and its own legacy.

Marvel's Warstar (B'Nee and C'Ccl) are loyal members of the Shi'ar Imperial Guard, serving the Shi'ar throne. They are disciplined and follow orders, but also show camaraderie with other Guard members. The Goseiger Warstar are aggressive conquerors, seeking to enslave or destroy humanity; their behavior is militaristic and ruthless, with individual commanders displaying distinct personalities ranging from cunning to brutish. The Power Rangers Megaforce Warstar Empire behaves similarly, with leaders like Admiral Malkor and Prince Vekar showing arrogance and cruelty.

Etymology — The name is a portmanteau of 'war' and 'star', suggesting a celestial or cosmic martial identity. In the Japanese context, the name is written in katakana as ウォースター (Wōsutā), which is a transliteration of 'Warstar'.

Pronunciation — WAR-star (English); Wō-su-tā (Japanese approximation)

Merged Form (Marvel)

B'Nee and C'Ccl can merge into a single, more powerful being called Warstar. In this form, they possess superhuman strength, durability, and enhanced combat abilities.

Limit: The merged form may be unstable or require concentration; if disrupted, they revert to two separate beings.

Flight (Marvel)

Warstar can fly through space and in planetary atmospheres.

Limit: Not specified.

Alien Physiology (Goseiger/Megaforce)

The Warstar aliens possess various powers depending on the individual monster: some have super strength, others energy blasts, flight, or shape-shifting. The foot soldiers are relatively weak but numerous.

Limit: Individual powers vary; many are defeated by the Rangers' teamwork and special weapons.

Accounts I
Marvel's Warstar and the X-MenThe Uncanny X-Men #107 and subsequent appearances

1977-present

Warstar is introduced as a member of the Shi'ar Imperial Guard, an elite team serving the Shi'ar Empress Lilandra. They are sent to stop the X-Men, who are attempting to stop the Phoenix from destroying a Shi'ar planet. Warstar engages in battle with the X-Men, but later becomes an ally when the Phoenix is defeated. Warstar appears in various storylines, including the 'Dark Phoenix Saga' and later cosmic events. Warstar's actions help shape cosmic Marvel events, and they remain a recurring background character in Shi'ar-related stories.

Goseiger vs. WarstarTensou Sentai Goseiger (TV series)

2010

The Universal Annihilation Army Warstar, led by the villain Brajira of the Messiah (initially), invades Earth to harvest its energy. The Goseigers, a team of five warriors, fight against the Warstar's monsters and generals. Over the course of the series, the Warstar are gradually defeated, but later a more powerful faction emerges. The Warstar are ultimately destroyed, but their actions lead to the introduction of a new enemy faction, the Matrintis Empire.

Power Rangers Megaforce vs. Warstar EmpirePower Rangers Megaforce (TV series)

2013

The Warstar Empire, led by Admiral Malkor and Prince Vekar, invades Earth. The Megaforce Rangers, using powers based on the Goseigers, defend the planet. The Warstar are eventually defeated, but a new threat (the Metal Alice's robot army) emerges. The Warstar Empire is destroyed, but the Ranger team continues to face new enemies.

Trawler Warstar (GY73)Ship registration records

1914-?

The Warstar was a steel trawler built in 1914 by Cook, Welton & Gemmell in Beverley, UK. It was registered in Grimsby (GY73) and used for commercial fishing in the North Sea. No significant documented events are known. No notable historical consequences are recorded.

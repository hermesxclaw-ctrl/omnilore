Who It Is

Seven female children (SCP-231-1 through -7), each pregnant with an entity prophesied to bring about the end of the world. Procedure 110-Montauk — the 'containment procedure' — is among the Foundation's most classified secrets. Six of the seven have given birth; only SCP-231-7 remains. The nature of Procedure 110-Montauk is deliberately left ambiguous, but implications point to ritualized violence necessary to prevent the birth.

Form

SCP-231-7 is a human female, approximately 18 years old, who has been in Foundation custody since age 6. She exhibits severe psychological trauma and rarely speaks. When she does, she refers to the entity within her as 'THE DEVOURER.'

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Derived from the Old French 'travers' or 'traverse', meaning 'crossing' or 'toll gate', likely brought to England by the Normans. It originally referred to someone who collected tolls at a crossing.

Pronunciation — /ˈtrævɪs/

Aliases — Travis

Ownership — Anglo-Norman origin, widely used in English-speaking countries, particularly the United States and Scotland.

Form & Signs

Appearance — As a name, no physical appearance; as a place, Travis, Staten Island is a residential neighborhood; Travis County is a large county in Texas; Lake Travis is a reservoir with scenic views. As a chimpanzee, Travis was a male common chimpanzee, weighing about 200 pounds, with brownish-gray fur.

Symbols — The name Travis is often associated with Texas history, particularly the Alamo, and with Scottish heritage. In popular culture, it may evoke characters like Travis Bickle from 'Taxi Driver' or Travis Scott, the rapper.

Domains — Travis as a place: Staten Island (urban), Texas (rural/suburban), Lake Travis (water reservoir). Travis the chimpanzee lived in a domestic setting in Connecticut.

Accounts I
William B. Travis and the AlamoHistorical records, letters

1836

William Barret Travis, a lieutenant colonel in the Texian Army, commanded the defense of the Alamo against Mexican forces led by Santa Anna. He wrote a famous letter appealing for reinforcements, and he and his men were ultimately killed after a 13-day siege. Travis became a symbol of heroic resistance and the Texas Revolution; the battle cry 'Remember the Alamo' emerged.

Accounts II
Travis the chimpanzee attackNews reports, police reports

2009

Travis, a 14-year-old chimpanzee raised by Sandra Herold, attacked her friend Charla Nash, severely mauling her face and hands. Police shot Travis, who later died. Nash survived but suffered permanent disfigurement. The attack led to changes in exotic pet laws in Connecticut and debates about animal welfare.

Travis Bickle in 'Taxi Driver'Film

1976

Travis Bickle is a mentally unstable Vietnam veteran who becomes a taxi driver in New York City and descends into violence. The film became a classic and influenced cinema.

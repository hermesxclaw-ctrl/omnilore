The Approach

In the misty highlands of Kitakami, a small, masked creature emerges from the shadows of folklore, carrying a mask that holds the power of a loyal companion and a tale of betrayal and redemption. Ogerpon, the Teal Mask Pokémon, is not merely a creature of battle but a living legend whose story intertwines with the region's history, inviting trainers to uncover the truth behind its sorrowful past.

Content advisory. Contains themes of betrayal, loss, and redemption. The narrative involves a creature that has been misunderstood and vilified, which may resonate with themes of ostracism and seeking justice.

Etymology

The name 'Ogerpon' likely derives from 'ogre' and 'pon' (possibly from 'pony' or a suffix), reflecting its ogre-like appearance and its connection to the ogre folklore of Kitakami. The 'Teal Mask' refers to its signature mask, which is teal in color.

Cultural Ownership

Ogerpon is a fictional creature from the Pokémon franchise, created by Game Freak and owned by Nintendo, Creatures Inc., and The Pokémon Company.

Disputed Identifications

There is no dispute among official sources, but fan interpretations vary regarding Ogerpon's exact role in the lore of Kitakami, with some viewing it as a villain and others as a tragic hero.

Description

Appearance

Ogerpon is a small, humanoid creature resembling a goblin or ogre, with a predominantly green body. It wears a teal mask that covers its face, which has a distinct pattern. In its Teal Mask form, it has a more subdued appearance compared to its other forms, with a focus on agility and grace.

Symbols & Iconography

The teal mask is its most iconic symbol, representing its connection to the Teal Mask of Kitakami folklore. The mask is also a key gameplay element, as Ogerpon can change forms by equipping different masks.

Behaviour & Personality

Ogerpon is depicted as a lonely and misunderstood creature, initially appearing as a threat but later revealed to be a victim of a misunderstanding. It is loyal to its Trainer and seeks to protect its home. In battle, it is agile and uses its mask to enhance its abilities.

Domains & Habitats

Ogerpon is found in the Kitakami region, specifically in the area around Oni Mountain. It is associated with the lore of the region and is tied to the story of the ogre and the Loyal Three.

Version Differences

Ogerpon has multiple forms: Teal Mask, Wellspring Mask, Hearthflame Mask, and Cornerstone Mask. Each form changes its type and appearance, with the Teal Mask being the base form. The Teal Mask form is Grass-type, while others are Grass/Water, Grass/Fire, and Grass/Rock respectively.

Stories & Myths

The Legend of the Ogre

Source Pokémon Scarlet and Violet: The Teal Mask DLCCulture Fictional culture of Kitakami, inspired by Japanese folklorePeriod Modern Pokémon timeline

Summary

In Kitakami, there is a legend about an ogre that terrorized the land, stealing masks from the Loyal Three. The ogre was eventually defeated and sealed away. However, the truth is that Ogerpon was the victim: the Loyal Three stole Ogerpon's masks and were the actual troublemakers. Ogerpon's companion, a Pokémon named Okidogi, was actually a friend, but due to the legend, Ogerpon was vilified.

The player helps Ogerpon reclaim its masks and clears its name, allowing Ogerpon to be caught and become a partner. The story also affects Kieran's character arc, as he becomes obsessed with Ogerpon and later seeks to be stronger.

Variants

The story is presented through in-game dialogue, cutscenes, and interactions with NPCs. The player's actions can influence how the story concludes, but the core narrative remains the same.

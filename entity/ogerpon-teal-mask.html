A small, masked Pokemon from the land of Kitakami, Ogerpon wears a distinctive teal mask that covers its face. According to Kitakami legend, Ogerpon was once a fearsome ogre that terrorized the land, but the truth is far sadder — it was a gentle Pokemon falsely accused by the three Loyal Three who stole its masks.

Who It Is

A small, masked Pokemon from the land of Kitakami, Ogerpon wears a distinctive teal mask that covers its face. According to Kitakami legend, Ogerpon was once a fearsome ogre that terrorized the land, but the truth is far sadder — it was a gentle Pokemon falsely accused by the three Loyal Three who stole its masks.

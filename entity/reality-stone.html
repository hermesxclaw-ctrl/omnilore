Who It Is

In a universe where six stones govern the fabric of reality, the Reality Stone is the most deceptive—a dark, liquid entity that can twist perception and rewrite existence, yet it demands a host and leaves ruin in its wake. From the depths of Asgard's ancient wars to the Mad Titan's snap, its story is one of power, sacrifice, and the fragile line between truth and illusion.

The Aether is sentient to a degree, seeking out a host and bonding with them. It can be aggressive, repelling threats and draining life force. It does not have a personality but acts on instinct to preserve itself.

Powers
Reality Alteration

The Reality Stone allows its user to manipulate reality itself, creating illusions, transforming matter, and rewriting physical laws. Thanos uses it to turn Drax into blocks, create a fake Gamora, and dissolve enemies into bubbles.

Limit: In the MCU, the stone's full reality-warping potential is not fully explored; it seems to require the user's will and may be limited by proximity or the user's understanding. The Aether in liquid form cannot be used directly; it must be bonded to a host or extracted.

Dark Matter Manipulation

When bonded to a host, the Aether can convert matter into dark matter, which can be used offensively. It can also drain life force from living beings.

Limit: It requires a host; using it too much may kill the host. It is not a weapon of mass destruction like the Power Stone.

Physics Disruption

The Aether can disrupt the laws of physics, causing objects to change state or behave erratically. It can also repel threats that come near it.

Limit: Seems to be defensive and unpredictable; not fully controlled by the host.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In its natural state, the Reality Stone appears as a dark, viscous, liquid-like substance, often called the Aether. It can be contained in a stone-like vessel or a lantern-like container. When bonded to a host, it flows through their veins and can manifest as a red, smoky energy. Unlike other stones, it has no solid gem form until it is solidified by Thanos in Infinity War.

Symbols — Often depicted as a red, swirling energy or a dark fluid. In the MCU, it is associated with dark matter and the alignment of the Nine Realms (convergence).

Domains — Originally hidden on Earth by Bor (Odin's father); later kept by the Collector at Knowhere; eventually destroyed by Thanos. It is a cosmic artifact, not tied to a specific realm.

Accounts I
The Aether and Malekith's PlanThor: The Dark World (2013)

Eons ago and 2013

Malekith, leader of the Dark Elves, used the Aether to attempt to plunge the universe into eternal darkness by returning it to its pre-Big Bang state. He was defeated by Bor, Odin's father, who hid the Aether on Earth. In 2013, Jane Foster becomes infected by the Aether during the convergence, awakening Malekith. Thor and his allies defeat Malekith, and the Aether is given to the Collector. The Aether is separated from the Space Stone; Jane is saved; the Collector is entrusted with it, setting up Infinity War.

Accounts II
Thanos Acquires the Reality StoneAvengers: Infinity War (2018)

2018

Thanos kills the Collector and extracts the Aether from its container, solidifying it into the Reality Stone. He uses it to deceive the Guardians on Knowhere, then later uses it to create illusions and alter reality during battles. Thanos obtains the second Stone; Gamora is eventually sacrificed for the Soul Stone.

The Time HeistAvengers: Endgame (2019)

2023 (time travel to 2013)

In the time heist, War Machine and Nebula travel to 2013 Asgard to retrieve the Aether from Jane Foster. After the Snap is reversed, Captain America returns the Aether to that exact moment. The timeline is restored; the Stones are returned to their original places.

The Approach

In the royal capital of Lugunica, where knights swear fealty to the Dragon and the throne lies empty, one swordsman stands apart—Julius Juukulius, the 'Knight of Knights,' a man whose pride is as sharp as his blade, and whose loyalty is bound not to a candidate, but to the very ideal of knighthood itself.

Identity

Julius Juukulius

Pronunciation JOO-lee-us JOO-koo-lee-us

Known As

JuliusKnight of Knights

Etymology

Juukulius is a surname; the given name Julius is likely derived from the Roman name, evoking classical nobility.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character from the Japanese light novel series Re:Zero, created by Tappei Nagatsuki.

Disputed Identifications

None known.

Description

Appearance

Julius is a tall, handsome young man with short, neat blonde hair and blue eyes. He is always impeccably dressed in a white and blue knight's uniform, often with a cape, and carries a rapier. His appearance is refined and aristocratic.

Symbols & Iconography

His knight's uniform and rapier symbolize his status as a knight of Lugunica. He is often associated with the spirit Yang, a small blue spirit, reflecting his role as a spirit knight.

Behaviour & Personality

Julius is proud, formal, and deeply committed to the code of chivalry. He is courteous but can be cold and condescending, especially towards those he deems unworthy. He values honor, duty, and proper conduct. He is highly disciplined and skilled in both swordsmanship and spirit arts.

Domains & Habitats

He resides in the royal capital of Lugunica, serving as a knight and a member of the royal guard. He is often in the company of the royal candidates and the kingdom's nobility.

Version Differences

The anime and light novel are largely consistent. Some minor scenes are cut or expanded, but his characterization remains the same.

Powers & Abilities

Master Swordsmanship

Source Version All versions

Description

Julius is one of the most skilled swordsmen in Lugunica, wielding a rapier with exceptional speed, precision, and technique. He is capable of defeating multiple opponents and even challenging powerful foes like the Witch Cult members.

Limitations

He is not invincible and has been defeated or struggled against particularly powerful enemies.

Contradictions

None known.

Spirit Arts

Source Version All versions

Description

As a spirit knight, Julius contracts with spirits, particularly the spirit Yang. He can use their power to enhance his abilities, such as creating barriers or attacking from a distance. He is a skilled spirit user, though not as powerful as dedicated mages like Roswaal.

Limitations

His spirit arts require concentration and the cooperation of his contracted spirits.

Julius possesses sharp instincts and observational skills, allowing him to read situations and opponents quickly. He is also knowledgeable about politics and etiquette.

Limitations

He can be blinded by his pride and preconceptions, as seen in his initial conflict with Subaru.

Stories & Myths

Conflict with Subaru and the Royal Selection

Source Re:Zero Arc 3 (Light Novel, Anime)Culture Japanese light novel/anime culturePeriod Fantasy setting, timeline within the series

Summary

Julius first appears as a knight accompanying the royal candidate Anastasia Hoshin. He is introduced during the royal selection ceremony, where he clashes with Subaru Natsuki over Subaru's rude behavior and lack of respect for Emilia. Their conflict escalates when Subaru challenges Julius to a duel, which Julius wins easily, humiliating Subaru and causing a rift between them. Later, during the Witch Cult's attack on the royal capital, Julius fights alongside Subaru to protect the citizens, beginning a gradual reconciliation.

The duel establishes Julius's reputation and creates a tense relationship with Subaru, which later develops into mutual respect as they fight together.

The Knight of Knights

Source Re:Zero series (general)Culture Japanese light novel/anime culturePeriod Throughout the series

Summary

Julius is renowned as the 'Knight of Knights' for his exceptional skill and adherence to knightly ideals. He serves as a mentor-like figure to Subaru, helping him grow as a person and a fighter. He is deeply loyal to his candidate, Anastasia, and to the kingdom of Lugunica.

Contradictions & Disputes

Julius's initial treatment of Subaru

Position A

Some fans view Julius as arrogant and needlessly cruel to Subaru.

Position A — evidence

He publicly humiliates Subaru in a duel and calls him 'Barusu' (a derogatory nickname).

Position B

Others argue that Julius's actions were justified as a knight defending the honor of the royal candidates.

Position B — evidence

Subaru's behavior was indeed disrespectful and disruptive during the royal selection ceremony.

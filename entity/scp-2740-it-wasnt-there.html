Who It Is

An entity that exists in a family's attic but retroactively erases all evidence of its existence. The family — the Crawfords — experience the entity nightly (footsteps overhead, scratching, crying), but every morning, all evidence is gone. More disturbingly, the entity selectively erases memories: family members forget they were ever afraid. SCP-2740 is never seen, only experienced during the night, and the Crawford family has been trapped in this cycle for years, each morning convincing themselves everything is fine.

Form

Never directly observed. Captured only through its effects: nocturnal sounds, emotional residue, and the erasure of its own existence. The attic appears completely normal during daylight.

Powers

Retroactive evidence erasure

Memory suppression

Nocturnal manifestation only

Informational antimemetic field

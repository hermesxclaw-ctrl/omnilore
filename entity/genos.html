Who It Is

In a world where heroes are ranked by their ability to level cities, Genos stands apart: a young man who traded his flesh for fire and steel, driven by a single, unquenchable desire for vengeance against a cyborg he cannot find. His journey is not just about power—it is about the meaning of humanity in a body that is no longer fully human.

Genos is serious, disciplined, and intensely focused on his goals. He is deeply respectful of Saitama, whom he sees as his master, and often seeks his guidance. He is prone to overthinking and can be overly literal, but he is also fiercely protective of innocent people. He has a strong sense of justice and is willing to sacrifice himself for others. Despite his cold exterior, he shows genuine care for his friends and allies.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Genos' (ジェノス) is a Japanese transliteration of the Greek word 'genos' (γένος), meaning 'race', 'offspring', or 'kind'. It may be a deliberate reference to his artificial nature and his quest for identity.

Powers
Incinerate

Genos can release a powerful blast of fire from his palms, capable of incinerating entire buildings or even altering the landscape.

Limit: Requires energy, which can be depleted with heavy use.

Machine Gun Blows

Genos can fire rapid punches at high speed, overwhelming enemies with a barrage of blows.

Limit: Uses stamina and energy.

Spiral Incineration Cannon

A more powerful version of Incinerate, fired from his arms, which can destroy large targets.

Limit: Consumes a large amount of energy.

Jet Drive Arrow

Genos propels himself with rockets in his limbs to deliver a devastating flying kick.

Limit: Requires precise aim and can be countered.

Reactor Core

Genos's core powers his entire body. In critical situations, he can overload it to create a massive explosion, but this is a suicide attack.

Limit: Self-destructive, only used as a last resort.

Cyborg Body

Genos's entire body is a machine, making him immune to pain and capable of withstanding heavy damage. He can also detach and reattach limbs.

Limit: Vulnerable to EMP or other electronic attacks, though he has shown resistance.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Genos is a tall, muscular young man with short blond hair and sharp blue eyes. His body is almost entirely cybernetic, with visible metal plates and mechanical joints. He typically wears a black and yellow combat suit that integrates with his cyborg body. His arms can detach and be replaced with various weapons, and his hands can be used as thrusters for propulsion.

Symbols — Genos's design emphasizes his mechanical nature, with glowing core in his chest that powers his abilities. His outfit is reminiscent of a high-tech suit, and his overall aesthetic is sleek and futuristic, contrasting with the more whimsical designs of other heroes.

Domains — Genos is based in City Z, where he lives with Saitama in Saitama's apartment. He often frequents the Hero Association headquarters and various battlefields across the world.

Accounts I
The Origin of GenosOne Punch Man manga, Chapter 6 (remake)

Modern

Genos was a human boy who lived in a city that was destroyed by a rampaging cyborg. He was the only survivor, and his body was severely damaged. He was rescued and rebuilt by Dr. Kuseno, a scientist who specialized in cybernetics. Genos dedicated his life to finding and destroying the cyborg that destroyed his hometown. Genos became a cyborg and joined the Hero Association to gain resources and information to track down his target.

Genos Meets SaitamaOne Punch Man manga, Chapter 3 (webcomic), Chapter 6 (remake), Episode 2 (anime)

Modern

Genos encounters a monster called Mosquito Girl and is nearly defeated. Saitama, who was nearby, kills the monster with a single punch. Impressed by Saitama's power, Genos begs to become his disciple, and Saitama reluctantly agrees. Genos moves in with Saitama and becomes his disciple, learning from him and observing his training.

Accounts II
Genos vs. the Sea KingOne Punch Man manga, Chapters 33-37 (remake), Season 1 (anime)

Modern

During the invasion of the Deep Sea King, Genos fights the monster to protect civilians. He is severely damaged and loses an arm, but he continues to fight. Saitama eventually arrives and defeats the Sea King with one punch. Genos is repaired and upgraded by Dr. Kuseno. He gains a new respect for Saitama's power and his own limitations.

The Monster Association ArcOne Punch Man manga, Chapters 84-? (remake)

Modern

Genos joins the raid on the Monster Association to rescue a kidnapped child. He faces powerful monsters and is nearly killed, but he and other heroes eventually prevail. He also learns more about the Organization that may be connected to his past. Genos receives multiple upgrades and gains new insights into his past.

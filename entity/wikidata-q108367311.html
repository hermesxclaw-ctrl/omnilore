Who It Is

She arrives with a clipboard, not a spotlight — the student council president who tried to shut down the School Idol Club because she believed idols were a distraction from excellence. Shioriko Mifune, the poised, green-eyed second-year who treats every rule as a promise, has to learn that perfection is not the same as passion, and that the stage she once dismissed might be the one place she can finally breathe for herself.

Attested consistently as highly disciplined, serious, polite, and academically excellent. Speaks in formal, respectful Japanese (desu/masu register, uses proper honorifics), values rules, punctuality, and preparation. Initially rigid and judgmental toward school idols, viewing them as frivolous and disruptive to school order — a stance rooted in admiration for her accomplished older sister Kaoruko Mifune and a desire to meet high family/school expectations. Powers
Vocal Performance (School Idol Singing)

Trained singing voice, described in-universe as clear, elegant, and technically precise. Performs solo songs 'Aozora wo Matteru' and 'EMOTION' and group songs with Nijigasaki. Range is mezzo-soprano; style is ballad-oriented with controlled vibrato, reflecting classical training implied by her disciplined background.

Limit: Not depicted as naturally charismatic performer initially; struggles with emotional expression and stage fright, requiring mentorship from peers. No supernatural vocal power.

Dance and Stage Performance

Idol choreography for Nijigasaki performances; precise, graceful movements emphasizing form over exuberance. Learns choreography quickly due to diligence but initially lacks looseness.

Limit: Physical stamina typical of high school student; no superhuman agility. Early performances show stiffness noted by other characters.

Academic Excellence and Organizational Leadership

Top-tier grades, student council presidency, mastery of school regulations, scheduling, event management, and public speaking. Able to manage budgets, coordinate school events, and enforce discipline. Extensive knowledge of literature.

Limit: Perfectionism leads to overwork and difficulty delegating; struggles with emotional intelligence and peer motivation until character growth.

Piano (Implied / Secondary)

Some profile materials and fan books note ability to play piano, consistent with classical upbringing and disciplined image. Not prominently featured in main story but referenced in bonus voice lines and magazine Q&A.

Limit: Not a performance focus; secondary to idol activities. Attestation is weaker than singing/dance — flagged as lightly attested.

Emotional Support and Mentorship (Post-Development)

After joining, provides calm counsel, rule-knowledge, and encouragement to other members, particularly in organizing events and helping Lanzhu and Mia integrate. Acts as reliable, grounding presence.

Limit: Still learning to express affection openly; advice can be overly formal.

Accounts I
The Student Council President vs. The School Idol Club (Game Debut Arc)Love Live! School Idol Festival ALL STARS Main Story Chapter 14 'A New Student Council President' to Chapter 17 'Our Future'

2019-2020 (in-game timeline: second year, autumn)

Shioriko Mifune transfers to Nijigasaki High School and is appointed Student Council President. Admiring her older sister Kaoruko — a former Nijigasaki student council president and accomplished figure — Shioriko believes the School Idol Club is undisciplined and detrimental to the school's reputation. She issues a formal notice to abolish the club, citing low activity reports and lack of results, and enforces strict regulations. The protagonist (player/Anata) and club members, especially Ayumu Uehara and Setsuna Yuki, attempt to persuade her through dialogue and performance. Shioriko attends a performance but remains unmoved, arguing that feelings alone do not justify rule-breaking. The turning point comes when members help her with student council work and she witnesses their genuine dedication, and when she confronts her own motivation: she was imitating her sister rather than finding her own passion. Aozora wo Matteru — Waiting for the Blue Sky (Solo Origin Story)Shioriko Mifune First Solo Single 'Aozora wo Matteru' and SIFAS Bond Episode / Anime Season 2 Episode 7

2020-2021

In both game and anime, Shioriko's first solo song serves as her personal confession. Lyrics by Aki Hata describe a girl looking out a window at a blue sky she has not yet reached, questioning whether she is allowed to wish for something beyond being 'excellent.' In narrative context, Shioriko performs the song after deciding to join the club, symbolizing her choice to pursue idol activities not to surpass her sister but because she loves singing. The performance is staged simply, emphasizing vocals and emotion over elaborate choreography, reflecting her earnest, unadorned personality. The song becomes her signature and is reprised in live concerts. Defines Shioriko's musical identity (ballad, sky/feather imagery); becomes staple of Nijigasaki setlists; used in anime as climax of her joining episode.

EMOTION and the Second Solo — Finding One's VoiceShioriko Mifune Second Solo Single 'EMOTION' (2021) and SIFAS Bond Episodes 2021

2021 (in-universe: winter of second year)

Following her joining, Shioriko struggles with whether she can express 'emotion' authentically on stage, fearing her performance is too correct and lacks heart. In bond episodes, she practices with other members (particularly Rina and Shizuku) who encourage her to incorporate personal feelings rather than perfect technique. 'EMOTION' is presented as a more mature, slightly jazz-inflected ballad about acknowledging trembling feelings and letting them be seen. The story resolves with Shioriko accepting that imperfection and vulnerability are part of performance. Deepens character from 'perfect student' to 'growing idol'; second solo adds to discography and live repertoire.

Accounts II
Anime Reinvention — A Gentler Introduction (Season 2 Arc)Love Live! Nijigasaki High School Idol Club Season 2 Episodes 5-8 (2022), Sunrise

2022 (broadcast); in-universe: spring of new school year, Shioriko as second-year

In anime continuity, Shioriko is introduced without the abolition conflict. She is already Student Council President but is depicted as quietly observing the club, intrigued yet hesitant. Ayumu Uehara notices her watching practice and invites her. Shioriko reveals she loves idols but feels unqualified because she has always lived by her sister's example and fears she has no original 'spark.' The club helps her prepare for the upcoming School Idol Festival, and she gradually participates in lessons. Her joining is framed as self-acceptance rather than defeat: she performs 'Aozora wo Matteru' at a small stage, and the other members welcome her. The arc emphasizes friendship and gentle encouragement over institutional conflict, reflecting anime's more ensemble, low-conflict tone. Integrates Shioriko into anime roster as 10th member; sets up 12-member group for finale; establishes anime-specific characterization that is less antagonistic, which influences film continuity.

The 12-Member Nijigasaki and the School Idol Festival (Ensemble Climax)Love Live! Nijigasaki High School Idol Club Season 2 Episodes 12-13 'The Future We Choose' and SIFAS Main Story Final Chapters

2022-2023

With Shioriko, Lanzhu Zhong, and Mia Taylor now members, Nijigasaki becomes a 12-member group (plus Yu). The story culminates in a School Idol Festival where each member performs, demonstrating individuality within unity — the core Nijigasaki theme ('Everyone's dream, everyone's stage'). Shioriko performs as part of group songs 'Future Parade' and 'Just Believe!!!' and supports logistics via student council coordination, bridging her two roles. In SIFAS, this arc also includes her helping Lanzhu (another initially antagonistic transfer) integrate, mirroring her own experience. The festival affirms that rules and dreams can coexist. Completes transition from outsider to core member; establishes 12-member Nijigasaki as definitive lineup for subsequent projects (lives, film).

Next Sky and Final Chapter — Post-Festival Growth (Film Continuity)OVA 'Love Live! Nijigasaki High School Idol Club NEXT SKY' (2023) and Theatrical Film 'Final Chapter Chapter 1' (2024)

2023-2024

In NEXT SKY, Shioriko participates in a school trip / training camp aimed at creating new songs; she is shown collaborating on songwriting and supporting younger members, indicating increased confidence. In Final Chapter Chapter 1 (Okinawa setting), the third-years' graduation looms, and Shioriko, now a senior figure, helps organize events and confronts her own future — whether to continue idols while balancing student council duties and post-graduation plans. While not the central focus (Ayumu and others take lead), she receives ensemble scenes showing matured, relaxed demeanor and performs in group numbers. The film emphasizes continuity of dreams beyond school. Shows Shioriko's evolution into reliable senior; extends character beyond initial joining arc; sets up future chapters where her graduation and leadership succession may be explored.

Shioriko has always been a Nijigasaki member since the group's inception. — Shioriko was added later. Original Nijigasaki group (2017-2019) had 9 members; Shioriko was the first of three added members (10th overall), debuting in 2020, followed by Mia Taylor and Lanzhu Zhong. She does not appear in Season 1 anime or early SIFAS chapters.

Shioriko is cold, villainous, or hates idols. — Her initial opposition stems from admiration for her sister and belief that discipline equals care, not hatred. She is depicted as earnest, anxious, and lonely, and quickly becomes warm and supportive after joining. Even her 'antagonist' phase is framed sympathetically.

Shioriko and Kaoruko Mifune are twins or the same age. — Kaoruko is explicitly older — a former Nijigasaki student council president who has already graduated by Shioriko's second year. Age gap not specified but at least 1-2 years; they are sisters, not twins.

Shioriko's member color is blue or teal. — Official member color is emerald green / jade green (エメラルドグリーン). It appears teal under certain lighting but is consistently listed as green in Lantis and Bushiroad materials.

Shioriko is a supernatural or folklore figure. — She is a contemporary fictional school idol with no supernatural powers, folklore origin, or mythological basis. Any 'powers' are realistic performance and academic skills.

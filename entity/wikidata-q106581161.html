In the neon-lit streets of a city that never sleeps, a shadow moves with the grace of a predator and the discipline of a warrior. Tiger Claw is not merely a name; it is a legend whispered among those who know the true cost of justice. Clad in armor that gleams like the eyes of a tiger in the dark, this masked vigilante has become a symbol of fear for the corrupt and hope for the powerless. But who is the person behind the claws? And what drove them to take up the mantle of a beast?

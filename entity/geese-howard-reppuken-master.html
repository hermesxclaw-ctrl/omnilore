Who It Is

The ruthless crime lord of Southtown, master of the ancient martial art of Aikido combined with the forbidden techniques of the Jin scrolls. Geese is a tall, imposing man with blonde hair and a white gi, known for his signature 'Rising Storm' technique. He rules Southtown's underworld with an iron fist.

Who It Is
Form

SCP-2718 is an infohazard, a concept that can be perceived and understood in various ways, making it difficult to describe its appearance. However, it is often associated with the idea of mortality and the afterlife.

Powers

Manipulation of Mortality and the Afterlife SCP-2718 has the ability to manipulate and distort the concept of mortality and the afterlife, often leading to severe psychological trauma and existential crises in those who encounter it.

Who It Is

An informational anomaly — the true knowledge of what happens to human consciousness after death. Former O5 Council member Roger Sheldon (O5-11) was resurrected after a temporary death and retained full memory of the experience. He described post-death consciousness as eternal, indestructible, and completely isolated — the mind continues in a state of total sensory deprivation for eternity, aware but unable to act, trapped in the decaying body forever. This knowledge is an XK-Class informational hazard; widespread belief in it would collapse human civilization.

Form

No physical form. The anomaly is the knowledge itself — a memetic hazard. Those who learn it and believe it often commit suicide, which would only confirm their worst fears if the knowledge is true.

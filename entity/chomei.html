Who It Is

The Seven-Tails, a massive insect resembling a stag beetle with seven tails, each tipped with a wing-like membrane. Chōmei can fly at incredible speeds and releases blinding scale powder. Its chakra signature is uniquely difficult to track. Sealed within Fū of Takigakure.

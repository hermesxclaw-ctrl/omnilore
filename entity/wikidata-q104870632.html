The Approach

In the neon-lit underworld of Street Fighter, few figures loom as quietly menacing as F.A.N.G., the venomous right hand of Shadaloo. With his skeletal frame, poisoned gloves, and unnerving politeness, he embodies a villainy that is less about brute force and more about calculated, lingering death.

Content advisory. Depictions of poisoning, murder, and villainous scheming; mild cartoon violence typical of fighting games.
Identity

F.A.N.G.

Pronunciation Pronounced 'fang' (as in the tooth).

Etymology

The name is presented in all caps with periods, which is stylized. It is likely an acronym, though Capcom has not officially revealed what it stands for. Fan speculation includes 'Fantastic Assassin Ninja Group' but this is unverified.

Cultural Ownership

Owned by Capcom, a Japanese video game company. F.A.N.G. is a character in the Street Fighter franchise, created for Street Fighter V.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans initially speculated that F.A.N.G. might be a reincarnation or relative of previous Street Fighter villains, but Capcom has not confirmed any such connection. He is a distinct character.

Description

Appearance

F.A.N.G. is a tall, gaunt man with pale skin, long black hair, and sunken eyes. He wears a long, dark purple Chinese-style robe with a high collar, and his hands are covered by long, sharp metal claws or gloves. His movements are fluid and serpentine, and he often carries himself with a hunched, predatory posture.

Symbols & Iconography

His symbol is a stylized poison drop or a snake, reflecting his venomous nature. His color scheme is purple and black, associated with poison and death. His fighting style incorporates Chinese martial arts, specifically the 'Nguuhao' style, which he adapted to use poison.

Behaviour & Personality

F.A.N.G. is calm, polite, and sadistic. He speaks in a soft, almost courteous tone, but his words are laced with menace. He is deeply loyal to M. Bison, the leader of Shadaloo, and sees himself as Bison's most devoted servant. He enjoys toying with his opponents, preferring to poison them slowly rather than defeat them quickly. He is also highly intelligent and cunning, often manipulating events behind the scenes.

Domains & Habitats

He operates primarily within the Shadaloo organization, often seen in their underground bases. He travels the world to carry out Bison's orders, but his home base is the Shadaloo headquarters.

Version Differences

As of 2026, F.A.N.G. has only appeared in Street Fighter V and its updates. He has not been featured in other mainline Street Fighter games, though he appears in cameos or references in other Capcom titles. No significant version differences exist within Street Fighter V.

Powers & Abilities

Poison Manipulation

Source Version Street Fighter V (all versions)

Description

F.A.N.G. uses a variety of poison-based attacks. He can spit poison projectiles, create poison clouds, and coat his claws with a deadly toxin. His signature ability is to inflict a 'poison' status on opponents, causing them to lose health over time.

Limitations

His poison attacks are generally slow and require careful spacing. He has low health and defense, making him vulnerable to aggressive opponents.

Contradictions

None known.

Nguuhao Fighting Style

Source Version Street Fighter V

Description

F.A.N.G. is a master of the Nguuhao, a fictional Chinese martial art that he adapted to incorporate poison. His movements are based on snake and crane styles, emphasizing quick, precise strikes and evasive footwork.

Limitations

His style relies on keeping opponents at a distance; he struggles in close-range combat.

Contradictions

None known.

Flight (limited)

Source Version Street Fighter V

Description

F.A.N.G. can perform a move where he floats or hovers briefly, using his robe like wings. This is used for mobility and to avoid attacks.

Limitations

Limited to short bursts during specific moves.

Contradictions

None.

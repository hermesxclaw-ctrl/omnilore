Who It Is

He is three feet tall, covered head-to-toe in living lawn, and absolutely convinced he is the most terrifying archmage in Ooo. The Grassy Wizard stalks into every scene with a crooked wand, bulging eyes, and a voice that cracks between menace and panic — only to be outwitted, out-blasted, or simply ignored by the actual powers around him. In a Land of wizards who can rewrite reality, he is the wizard who can barely rewrite his own hedges, and that failure is exactly why Adventure Time keeps bringing him back.

Attested personality: boastful, insecure, easily flustered, and comically inept. In 'You Made Me,' he attempts to intimidate Finn and Jake with grandiose threats ('You dare to enter my...') while physically trembling and failing to produce effective magic. He craves recognition as a 'real wizard' and reacts with wounded pride when dismissed. In ensemble wizard episodes, he is eager to participate, sycophantic toward more powerful wizards (notably Bufo), and quick to flee when danger escalates. Powers
Grass/Plant-Themed Magic (Low-Tier)

Can generate small-scale grass and vine growth, minor levitation of self or small objects, and weak energy projection from wand or eyes. Effects are visually green-tinged and accompanied by sputtering sound design to signal low potency.

Limit: Spells frequently fizzle, backfire, or are insufficient to restrain or harm protagonists. No evidence of large-scale terraforming, teleportation, resurrection, or reality warping. Requires wand as focus; loses capability if disarmed or panicked.

Eye Beam Projection

Fires a thin, greenish magical beam from his eyes when enraged or attempting to intimidate. Beam is short-range and low-damage.

Limit: Easily dodged by Finn and Jake; causes no lasting injury; drains or exhausts him quickly; depicted as more startling than dangerous.

Levitation / Hovering

Can hover a few feet off the ground and drift slowly, typical of Ooo wizards.

Limit: No sustained flight, no high-speed travel, no carrying capacity shown. Often shown walking instead, implying hover is effortful or ceremonial.

Wizard Social Access (Non-combat ability)

Recognized as a wizard by Wizard City institutions; can enter wizard-only spaces, attend gatherings, and be summoned with other wizards. This is a social/ontological status rather than a spell.

Limit: Status does not confer respect or authority; other wizards mock or ignore him; Bufo and more powerful wizards dominate hierarchy.

Accounts I
You Made MeAdventure Time Season 4 Episode 13, written by Tom Herpich & Jesse Moynihan, story by Patrick McHale, Kent Osborne, Pendleton Ward

2012

Finn and Jake, investigating a strange grassy area, encounter Grassy Wizard who attempts to assert dominance by claiming the territory and threatening them with wizardry. He demands respect and fires a weak eye beam. Finn and Jake, unimpressed, physically overpower and humiliate him with minimal effort, exposing his magic as ineffective. The episode establishes his core gag: a self-styled fearsome wizard who is in fact harmless. Princess Bubblegum later references the incident as an example of Finn and Jake's casual handling of low-level magical nuisances. Establishes Grassy Wizard as recurring joke character; defines his visual design, voice (Stephen Root), and power ceiling for all later appearances. No lasting plot consequences for Ooo.

All the Little PeopleAdventure Time Season 5 Episode 15, written by Ako Castuera & Jesse Moynihan, story by Patrick McHale, Kent Osborne, Pendleton Ward

2013

Finn discovers a bag of small, living toy-like duplicates of Ooo inhabitants, including a miniature Grassy Wizard, created by the Magic Man. Finn manipulates the toys to act out scenarios, causing real-world effects on their counterparts. The miniature Grassy Wizard appears among the collection on Finn's floor, briefly animated when Finn shakes the bag. He has no dialogue in this appearance; his presence confirms he is considered part of Ooo's established cast worthy of inclusion in Magic Man's set. Demonstrates Grassy Wizard's status as recognized Ooo resident in the show's internal catalog; no character development, but reinforces continuity.

Wizards Only, FoolsAdventure Time Season 5 Episode 39, written by Jesse Moynihan & Thomas Wellmann, story by Kent Osborne, Pendleton Ward, Jack Pendarvis, Adam Muto

2013

Finn, Jake, Princess Bubblegum, and Abracadaniel infiltrate a secret party in Wizard City for wizards only. Grassy Wizard is among the assembled wizards, mingling, showing off minor tricks, and later participating in the chaotic magical brawl that erupts when St. Archie's secrets are revealed. He is shown attempting to impress peers, firing eye beams and conjuring grass puffs alongside Bufo, Forest Wizard, and others. When the party descends into danger, he flees with the crowd, underscoring his low rank in the wizard hierarchy. Expands wizard lore and situates Grassy Wizard within a social hierarchy; confirms he has access to Wizard City and is accepted (if not respected) as a wizard. Provides most extended dialogue and magic demonstration for the character.

High StrangenessAdventure Time Season 8 Episode 16, written by Sam Alden & Pendleton Ward

2017

Finn and Jake investigate alien-related weirdness that intersects with Ooo's magical community. Grassy Wizard appears among wizards summoned to observe or react to the strange phenomena in Wizard City. He is shown cowering and firing a panicked eye beam that dissipates harmlessly, played for slapstick. The episode is near the end of the series' original run and serves as one of his final canonical television appearances. Final reinforcement of his comedic cowardice and weak magic; closes his television arc without death, redemption, or power upgrade.

Grassy Wizard is a powerful nature wizard who controls the Grass Lands and is related to Fern / Grass Finn. — He has no territorial control, no demonstrated large-scale plant manipulation, and no familial or biological link to Fern (who is a grass construct created from Finn's grass sword/demon). Their shared grass aesthetic is visual coincidence, not lore connection. Grassy Wizard never appears in Fern's origin episodes ('Reboot,' 'Two Swords').

Grassy Wizard appears in every wizard episode and is a major recurring villain. — He appears in only 6-7 episodes of 283, always as a background or one-scene comic antagonist. He is not a major villain like the Lich, Ice King, or Magic Man, and has no multi-episode arc or defeat/redemption.

Grassy Wizard was created by guest animator David O'Reilly or James Baxter for Season 5. — He debuted in Season 4 ('You Made Me') animated by the regular Rough Draft Korea / Saerom pipeline, before either guest animator's episodes ('A Glitch Is a Glitch' S5E15 and 'James Baxter the Horse' S5E19). He has no connection to their distinctive 3D or horse animation styles.

Powers
Omega Effect

In DC Comics, Darkseid's Omega Effect is a powerful energy beam that can disintegrate, teleport, or erase targets from existence. It is often depicted as a pair of red eye-beams.

Limit: The Omega Effect can be resisted by powerful beings or with the use of certain artifacts (e.g., the Anti-Life Equation).

Omega-level mutant classification

In Marvel Comics, an Omega-level mutant is one whose power is deemed to have no known upper limit and could potentially affect the entire world or reality. Examples include Jean Grey (Phoenix), Iceman, and Magneto.

Limit: The classification is not a power itself but a designation; it does not grant additional abilities.

Divine eternity

In Christian theology, God is referred to as the Alpha and Omega, signifying his eternal nature and sovereignty over all creation.

Limit: Not a power in the fictional sense; a theological attribute.

Accounts I
The Alpha and Omega in RevelationBook of Revelation

1st century CE

In the Book of Revelation, God declares 'I am the Alpha and the Omega, the first and the last, the beginning and the end' (Rev 1:8, 21:6, 22:13). This phrase emphasizes God's eternal existence and his role as the creator and consummator of all things. The phrase became a central confession of Christian faith, used in liturgy and art.

Accounts II
Omega the Unknown (Marvel Comics)Marvel Comics

1976-1977

Omega the Unknown is a silent, alien humanoid who arrives on Earth with a young boy named James-Michael Starling. Omega protects the boy from mysterious forces while struggling to understand human society. The series was created by Steve Gerber and Mary Skrenes, and was known for its unconventional narrative. The series was cancelled after 10 issues, but the character gained a cult following and was later revived in a 2007 mini-series.

Darkseid's Omega Effect (DC Comics)DC Comics

1971-present

Darkseid, a cosmic tyrant, uses his Omega Effect to eliminate enemies. The effect is often depicted as a pair of red beams from his eyes that can disintegrate or teleport victims. The Omega Effect is one of Darkseid's signature powers. The Omega Effect has been a recurring threat in DC Comics, often used to demonstrate Darkseid's power.

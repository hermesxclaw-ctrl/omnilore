Who It Is

Highfather is Izaya, the wise and benevolent ruler of New Genesis — one of the twin worlds of the Fourth World, the realm of the New Gods that exists beyond the mortal universe. Once a warrior consumed by grief after the death of his wife, Izaya underwent a spiritual transformation when he encountered the Source — the primordial cosmic wellspring of all life and creation. The Source granted him the Alpha-Effect, transforming him into Highfather, a being of profound wisdom, peace, and cosmic power. Highfather carries the Source Staff, a weapon of incandescent energy that channels the power of the Source itself. With it, he can generate shields of pure life-force, heal any wound, project cosmic blasts, teleport across dimensions, and communicate with the Source. As ruler of New Genesis, Highfather has spent eons locked in a cold war with his eternal opposite, Darkseid of Apokolips. To seal a truce, Highfather and Darkseid exchanged sons: Highfather raised Darkseid's son Orion, while Darkseid raised Highfather's son Scott Free (who would become Mister Miracle). This pact shaped the destiny of the Fourth World. Highfather raised Orion as his own, teaching him discipline, compassion, and the value of peace — tempering the boy's Apokoliptian rage. Highfather represents the best of divine rulership: wisdom without tyranny, power without corruption, and the unwavering belief that even in a universe dominated by cruel gods, there is a path of light. He is the eternal counterweight to Darkseid's ambition, the father who believes that even the children of darkness can find their way to the light.

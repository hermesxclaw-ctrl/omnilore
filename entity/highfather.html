Who It Is

In the scorched aftermath of Ragnarök, when the Old Gods perished and the world split into twin planets, a new leader arose on the idyllic world of New Genesis. He is Highfather, the benevolent but burdened ruler whose pact with the tyrant Darkseid would shape the fate of the Fourth World. His story is one of sacrifice, wisdom, and the agonizing compromises of peace.

Highfather is wise, compassionate, and diplomatic, but also pragmatic and willing to make difficult sacrifices for peace. He is often portrayed as a thoughtful leader who values life, but his decisions can be controversial, such as the pact that exchanged his son Orion for Darkseid's son.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The title 'Highfather' combines 'high' indicating his elevated status and 'father' reflecting his role as a paternal leader. His original name 'Izaya' is likely derived from the biblical prophet Isaiah, reflecting his wisdom and visionary nature.

Pronunciation — HIGH-fah-ther; I-ZAY-ah

Aliases — Izaya the Inheritor, Highfather of New Genesis

Ownership — Created by Jack Kirby for DC Comics. Part of the Fourth World mythos. Owned by Warner Bros. Discovery.

Powers
Divine Physiology

As a New God, Highfather possesses superhuman strength, durability, and longevity. He is virtually immortal and can withstand extreme conditions.

Limit: Not as physically powerful as Darkseid or Orion, but still formidable.

Cosmic Energy Manipulation

Highfather can channel the energy of the Source to project powerful blasts, create protective shields, and perform other cosmic feats.

Limit: Requires focus and connection to the Source.

Leadership and Wisdom

Highfather is a skilled diplomat and strategist, able to inspire loyalty and negotiate peace.

Limit: His decisions are not always popular or successful.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Highfather is depicted as a tall, regal older man with white hair and a beard, often wearing a flowing white robe with a golden collar and a staff. He exudes an aura of wisdom and authority.

Symbols — The staff of Highfather is a symbol of his office, often glowing with cosmic energy. He is associated with the Source, a cosmic entity, and the life-giving aspects of New Genesis.

Domains — He rules New Genesis, a planet of unspoiled natural beauty, from the city of Supertown. He is also connected to the Source, a cosmic power source.

Accounts I
The PactNew Gods #7

1971

To end the war between New Genesis and Apokolips, Highfather and Darkseid agreed to exchange their sons. Highfather raised Orion as his own, while Darkseid raised Scott Free (Mister Miracle). This pact brought temporary peace but sowed seeds of future conflict. The pact created a fragile peace and shaped the destinies of both sons.

Accounts II
Even Gods Must DieNew Gods #8

1972

Highfather confronts Darkseid in a final battle, where he appears to die, but is later revived. The story explores themes of sacrifice and the cyclical nature of conflict. Darkseid is temporarily defeated, but the war continues.

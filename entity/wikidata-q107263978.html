Who It Is

By day, Diluc Ragnvindr is the stoic master of the Dawn Winery, a wealthy and respected figure in Mondstadt. But when night falls, he becomes the Darknight Hero, a vigilante who operates outside the law to protect his city from threats the Knights of Favonius cannot handle. His story is one of betrayal, loss, and a burning desire for justice—a tale that unfolds across the vineyards and rooftops of Mondstadt.

Diluc is reserved, stoic, and somewhat cold towards others, especially the Knights of Favonius. He is fiercely independent and distrustful of authority, preferring to handle matters himself. Despite his aloof exterior, he is deeply caring and protective of Mondstadt, showing a softer side to those he trusts, like his adopted brother Kaeya and his maid, Noelle.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Diluc' may derive from the Latin 'diluculum' meaning 'dawn' or 'daybreak,' fitting his association with the Dawn Winery and his role as a bringer of light against darkness. 'Ragnvindr' is a surname of Germanic origin, possibly meaning 'rain wind' or 'judgment wind.'

Pronunciation — DEE-luck RAG-vin-der

Aliases — Darknight Hero, Master Diluc, Diluc

Ownership — Fictional character owned by HoYoverse (formerly miHoYo), part of the game Genshin Impact.

Powers
Pyro Vision

Diluc wields a Pyro Vision, granting him fire-based abilities. He can unleash powerful flames in combat, including his elemental skill 'Searing Onslaught' which delivers a series of slashes with fire, and his elemental burst 'Dawn' which summons a phoenix that deals massive Pyro damage.

Limit: Requires a Vision, which is a gift from the gods. His abilities are limited by his stamina and the cooldown of his skills.

Combat Expertise

As a former Cavalry Captain, Diluc is highly skilled in swordsmanship and combat tactics. He is proficient with claymores and can hold his own against powerful enemies.

Limit: His skills are humanly possible, without supernatural enhancements beyond his Vision.

Vigilante Skills

As the Darknight Hero, Diluc employs stealth, reconnaissance, and intimidation tactics. He is adept at moving unseen and gathering information, using his resources as a wealthy businessman to fund his operations.

Limit: He operates alone, which can be a disadvantage in large-scale threats.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Diluc is a tall, muscular man with fiery red hair and crimson eyes. He typically wears a black and white outfit with a red cravat, reminiscent of a nobleman. His attire includes a long coat, gloves, and boots, with a Vision (Pyro) attached to his belt. He carries a claymore, which he wields with great strength.

Symbols — The Dawn Winery's emblem features a phoenix, symbolizing rebirth and fire. Diluc's Pyro Vision is a symbol of his elemental power and his burning sense of justice. The Darknight Hero persona is associated with darkness and owls, representing his secretive and nocturnal activities.

Domains — Diluc resides at the Dawn Winery in Mondstadt, where he manages the family business. He is also known to roam the city at night as the Darknight Hero, patrolling the streets and outskirts.

The Fall of the Ragnvindr ClanCharacter story, official lore

Before the events of Genshin Impact

Diluc's father, Crepus Ragnvindr, was killed during an incident involving the Abyss Order. Diluc, then a knight, blamed the Knights of Favonius for their failure to save his father and resigned from the order. He left Mondstadt for a time, traveling the world and training to become stronger. Upon his return, he took over the Dawn Winery and began his secret vigilante work. Diluc's relationship with Kaeya deteriorated, and he became estranged from the Knights. His father's death fueled his distrust of the organization.

During the game's timeline

Diluc secretly operates as the Darknight Hero, protecting Mondstadt from threats such as the Abyss Order and other criminals. He uses his wealth and influence to gather information and often works outside the law, which sometimes puts him at odds with the Knights. His actions have saved Mondstadt multiple times, but they also strain his relations with the Knights, who are unaware of his identity.

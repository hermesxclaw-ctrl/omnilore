The Approach

By day, Diluc Ragnvindr is the stoic master of the Dawn Winery, a wealthy and respected figure in Mondstadt. But when night falls, he becomes the Darknight Hero, a vigilante who operates outside the law to protect his city from threats the Knights of Favonius cannot handle. His story is one of betrayal, loss, and a burning desire for justice—a tale that unfolds across the vineyards and rooftops of Mondstadt.

Content advisory. Contains themes of betrayal, familial conflict, and vigilante violence. No explicit gore or sexual content.

Etymology

The name 'Diluc' may derive from the Latin 'diluculum' meaning 'dawn' or 'daybreak,' fitting his association with the Dawn Winery and his role as a bringer of light against darkness. 'Ragnvindr' is a surname of Germanic origin, possibly meaning 'rain wind' or 'judgment wind.'

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character owned by HoYoverse (formerly miHoYo), part of the game Genshin Impact.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans speculate that the Darknight Hero is a separate entity, but official sources confirm it is Diluc's vigilante persona.

Description

Appearance

Diluc is a tall, muscular man with fiery red hair and crimson eyes. He typically wears a black and white outfit with a red cravat, reminiscent of a nobleman. His attire includes a long coat, gloves, and boots, with a Vision (Pyro) attached to his belt. He carries a claymore, which he wields with great strength.

Symbols & Iconography

The Dawn Winery's emblem features a phoenix, symbolizing rebirth and fire. Diluc's Pyro Vision is a symbol of his elemental power and his burning sense of justice. The Darknight Hero persona is associated with darkness and owls, representing his secretive and nocturnal activities.

Behaviour & Personality

Diluc is reserved, stoic, and somewhat cold towards others, especially the Knights of Favonius. He is fiercely independent and distrustful of authority, preferring to handle matters himself. Despite his aloof exterior, he is deeply caring and protective of Mondstadt, showing a softer side to those he trusts, like his adopted brother Kaeya and his maid, Noelle.

Domains & Habitats

Diluc resides at the Dawn Winery in Mondstadt, where he manages the family business. He is also known to roam the city at night as the Darknight Hero, patrolling the streets and outskirts.

Version Differences

No significant version differences in his character across game versions. However, his role and appearance may evolve in future storylines.

Powers & Abilities

Pyro Vision

Source Version Gameplay and lore

Description

Diluc wields a Pyro Vision, granting him fire-based abilities. He can unleash powerful flames in combat, including his elemental skill 'Searing Onslaught' which delivers a series of slashes with fire, and his elemental burst 'Dawn' which summons a phoenix that deals massive Pyro damage.

Limitations

Requires a Vision, which is a gift from the gods. His abilities are limited by his stamina and the cooldown of his skills.

The Fall of the Ragnvindr Clan

Source Character story, official loreCulture Fictional (Mondstadt)Period Before the events of Genshin Impact

Summary

Diluc's father, Crepus Ragnvindr, was killed during an incident involving the Abyss Order. Diluc, then a knight, blamed the Knights of Favonius for their failure to save his father and resigned from the order. He left Mondstadt for a time, traveling the world and training to become stronger. Upon his return, he took over the Dawn Winery and began his secret vigilante work.

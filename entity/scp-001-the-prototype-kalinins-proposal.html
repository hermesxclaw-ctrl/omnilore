Who It Is

SCP-001 is the Foundation itself — the original O5 Council, the first anomaly contained. The true SCP-001 document reveals that the original five founders of the Foundation were anomalous entities who created the organization to hide among its own jailers. Every O5 Council member since has been one of them or their descendants. The Foundation was built by monsters to contain monsters — and it has succeeded so well that even the monsters forgot what they were.

Form

The 'original five' appeared entirely human. Their anomaly was subtle: they were immortal, they could perceive threats across parallel timelines, and they shared a collective consciousness that allowed perfect coordination. Their descendants carry dormant versions of these abilities.

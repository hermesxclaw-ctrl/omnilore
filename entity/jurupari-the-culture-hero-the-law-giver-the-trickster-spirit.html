Who It Is
Form

Jurupari is often depicted as a shapeshifter, able to take on various forms such as a bird, a serpent, or a human.

Powers

Shapeshifting Jurupari has the ability to transform into various animals and objects, allowing him to adapt to different situations and environments.

Accounts

Jurupari is said to have created the world by shaping the earth and creating the first humans.

Jurupari plays a prank on his fellow deity, causing chaos and confusion among the gods.

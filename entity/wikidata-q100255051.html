Who It Is

In the frozen highlands of the Freljord, where the wind carves cliffs of ice and the earth shudders with volcanic fury, there walks a god who does not seek worship. Ornn, the Fire Below the Mountain, is a smith of primordial might, a being who shaped the land itself with his hammer and forge. But his legend is not one of glory—it is a tale of solitude, rivalry, and the quiet, unyielding craft that endures long after the songs of heroes fade.

Ornn is solitary, introspective, and prideful. He prefers the quiet of his mountain forge to the company of mortals or other gods. He is a craftsman who values perfection and is deeply critical of the works of others, especially his brother Volibear. Despite his gruff exterior, he cares for the land and its people, though he expresses this through his creations rather than direct intervention. He is slow to anger but when provoked, his wrath is devastating.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Ornn' is invented for the character, with no real-world etymology. It is designed to sound guttural and ancient, fitting the harsh, mountainous environment of the Freljord.

Pronunciation — orn (as in 'ornament') with a rolled 'r' and a short 'o' (IPA: /ɔːrn/).

Aliases — The Fire Below the Mountain, The Smith of the Hearth, The Mountain's Heart, The Forge God

Ownership — Fictional character created by Riot Games for the game League of Legends. He is part of the Freljord region, a fictional culture inspired by Norse and Arctic mythologies.

Powers
Forgemaster

Ornn is an unparalleled smith, able to craft weapons and artifacts of immense power. He forged many of the legendary items in the Freljord, including the ancient blade of the Avarosan. His creations are said to be infused with his divine essence.

Limit: His creations are not inherently magical, but their quality and durability are superhuman. He cannot imbue items with sentience or complex enchantments.

Divine Strength

As a demigod, Ornn possesses superhuman strength, endurance, and durability. He can shatter mountains with his hammer and withstand extreme heat and physical trauma.

Limit: He is not invincible and can be wounded by other demigods or powerful magic.

Volcanic Magic

Ornn has control over fire, lava, and the earth. He can cause volcanic eruptions, create thermal vents, and reshape terrain. This power is tied to his domain as the Fire Below the Mountain.

Limit: His power is most potent in volcanic environments; away from them, his control is weaker.

Immortality

Ornn is a demigod and does not age. He can be killed, but only by another divine being or through cataclysmic events.

Limit: He is not truly immortal and can be slain.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Ornn is a massive, goat-like humanoid with a bulky, muscular build. He has a thick, shaggy coat of fur, a large beard, and two prominent ram horns that curl forward. His eyes glow with a molten, orange light. He carries a giant hammer and wears a leather apron, symbolizing his role as a smith. His body is covered in scars and soot, evidence of his countless hours at the forge.

Symbols — Ornn is associated with fire, mountains, and the forge. His symbol is a stylized ram head, often depicted on banners and structures. He is also linked to the concept of creation and craftsmanship, with his forge being a sacred site.

Domains — Ornn resides in the Hearthblood, a volcanic mountain in the Freljord. His forge is located deep within the mountain, where he works on his creations. He is also said to have created several landmarks in the Freljord, including the Hot Springs and the Howling Abyss.

Ancient times

Ornn, the first of the demigod siblings, created the land itself with his hammer and forge. He built a great mountain to live in and crafted tools for the early humans. However, his brother Volibear, the Storm's Fury, desired power and worship. Volibear challenged Ornn, and in their battle, the land was torn apart, creating the Freljord's harsh landscape. Ornn retreated to his mountain, vowing never to interfere in mortal affairs again. The battle reshaped the Freljord, creating its mountains and valleys. Ornn became reclusive, and Volibear gained followers among the humans who feared his storms.

Pre-history

Ornn taught early humans how to forge with fire, giving them the knowledge to create tools and weapons. This act was seen as a blessing, but also a curse, as it led to conflict among the tribes. Humans advanced technologically but also began to war with each other using Ornn's creations.

Ornn is based on the Norse god Thor. — While both are associated with storms and strength, Ornn is a smith, not a thunder god. Riot Games has not confirmed any direct inspiration.

Ornn is a villain. — Ornn is a neutral figure, more of an anti-hero. He is not malicious but is detached from mortal affairs.

Ornn created the Howling Abyss. — The Howling Abyss is a location in the Freljord, but its creation is not explicitly attributed to Ornn in official lore. It is more likely a natural formation.

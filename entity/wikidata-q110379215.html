Who It Is

He rides into the American desert in 1890 wearing a cowboy hat stitched with a grinning teddy bear, two steel balls humming at his hips like planets in orbit — a Neapolitan executioner who laughs too loud, cheats at any game he can win honestly, and can make a steel sphere spin so perfectly it cuts through dimensions. Gyro Zeppeli — Julius Caesar Zeppeli — does not save the world because he is pure. He saves it because he was raised to kill for the state and decided, one boy at a time, to stop.

Gyro presents as flamboyant, arrogant, and comically crude — singing off-key Neapolitan songs ("Canzone"), boasting about his sexual prowess, demanding Johnny pay for meals, and enforcing a personal code of 'Nyo-ho-ho!' laughter. Underneath, he is disciplined, observant, and ethically driven. Raised in the House of Zeppeli, royal executioners for the Kingdom of Naples, he was trained from age 9 in anatomy, surgery, and the Spin as a tool for painless execution (and later, healing). Powers
Spin (Kaiten) — Basic Steel Ball Technique

Application of perfect rotation to a steel sphere, transferred to targets via contact. Generates vibration and centrifugal force that can manipulate muscle, bone, and nerve. Used for projectile attack, deflection, and medical treatment.

Limit: Requires physical Steel Balls (if lost, severely weakened), precise wrist snap and horse's gallop to achieve golden rotation; imperfect rotation causes Balls to drop. Range limited to thrown distance (~20-30m). Cannot affect non-physical phenomena without Golden Rectangle.

Scan (Sukyan)

By spinning a Steel Ball and applying it to his own eye or another surface, Gyro can 'scan' internal structures — seeing through skin to locate Corpse Parts, foreign objects, or Stand effects. Manifests as a visible grid/ripple over the eye.

Limit: Requires active Spin and concentration; vulnerable while scanning; cannot see Stands directly, only their physical effects; limited to line-of-sight and tactile feedback.

Medical Spin — Pain Suppression and Surgery

As heir to executioner-physicians, Gyro uses Spin to numb pain, close wounds, realign bones, and perform field surgery. Used to save Johnny's spine, treat his own injuries, and originally to execute painlessly.

Limit: Not instantaneous healing; requires anatomical knowledge and time; cannot resurrect dead tissue; overuse causes fatigue.

Golden Rectangle — Super Spin (Ōgon Chōhōkei)

Perfect rotation based on the Golden Ratio (1:1.618...), achieved when horse runs at natural gallop tracing golden spiral. Generates infinite rotational energy that can overcome dimensional barriers and bypass conventional physics.

Limit: Requires horse at full gallop in open terrain, precise timing, and understanding of golden ratio; cannot be used on foot or in confined space; mentally taxing.

Ball Breaker (Bōru Burēkā) — Stand

Manifestation of Super Spin as a Stand: a colossal, armored, centaur-like entity that phases through objects and ages targets by accelerating cellular senescence via infinite rotation. Breaks through Valentine's D4C Love Train barrier, which otherwise redirects misfortune.

Limit: Only manifests during Super Spin; short duration; drains Gyro's life force; requires direct contact via Ball; cannot be controlled independently like humanoid Stands; disappears after Gyro's death.

Equestrian Mastery and Throwing Precision

Expert horseman (Valkyrie) and uncanny accuracy with Steel Balls — can ricochet off terrain, curve trajectories, and strike pressure points. Combines cavalry tactics with Spin.

Limit: Dependent on horse stamina; trick shots fail if surface is irregular or if opponent understands Spin.

Accounts I
The Pardon for Marco — Why Gyro Enters the RaceSteel Ball Run Vol.1 Ch.1 - Vol.9 Ch.33 (flashbacks Vol.18 Ch.69)

In-story: Summer 1890, Naples; Publication: 2004-2007

In the Kingdom of Naples, the Zeppeli family has served for generations as executioners to the Bourbon king, using Spin to ensure painless beheading. Young Gyro, heir Gregorio's son, is groomed from childhood to succeed. When a boy, Marco, age 9, is sentenced to death for his mother's failed regicide, Gyro is ordered to execute him. Moved by the injustice and the boy's innocence, Gyro petitions the king: if he wins the Steel Ball Run — a transcontinental race sponsored by Steven Steel with a $50 million prize and a pardon from the American president that the Neapolitan king will honor as diplomatic favor — Marco will be freed. The king agrees. Gyro sails to America, not for glory but to outrun the axe he was born to wield. Establishes Gyro's moral core and mentor role; explains his refusal to kill needlessly during the race; payoff in Vol.23 when Gyro dies before learning Marco is saved via Johnny's later actions.

Meeting Johnny Joestar — The Desert BondSteel Ball Run Vol.1 Ch.2 - Vol.2 Ch.10

In-story: September 1890, San Diego desert; Publication: 2004

Paraplegic former jockey Johnny Joestar, fallen from fame after a shooting, touches Gyro's Steel Ball when it rolls near him during the race's opening melee. The Spin briefly restores sensation to his legs. Obsessed, Johnny follows Gyro, who initially rebuffs him. After Johnny demonstrates latent Spin talent (moving a cork via fingernail spin), Gyro accepts him as traveling partner and student. They agree to share information about the Saint's Corpse while competing. Gyro teaches Johnny to ride correctly and to feel rotation through the horse. Central partnership of Part 7; Johnny's Stand Tusk evolves through Gyro's lessons; Gyro's death later catalyzes Johnny's mastery of infinite rotation.

Wekapipo and the Steel Balls — Family Technique Turned Against HimSteel Ball Run Vol.10 Ch.37 - Vol.12 Ch.44 'Wekapipo and Magent Magenta'

In-story: October 1890, near Lake Michigan; Publication: 2007

Wekapipo, Gyro's former fellow trainee and brother-in-law (married to Gyro's sister), was exiled after Gyro's sister's death and now works as an assassin for Funny Valentine. Using his own Steel Balls imbued with the Stand Wrecking Ball (left half) that induces left-side ataxia, Wekapipo nearly kills Gyro and Johnny by making their left sides numb and uncontrollable. Gyro is forced to confront his family's legacy — Wekapipo blames the Zeppeli code for his wife's suicide. Gyro defeats him by exploiting Scan and by throwing a Ball with imperfect Spin that Wekapipo misreads, then spares him, reaffirming his refusal to become an executioner for revenge. Reveals Zeppeli family internal fractures; demonstrates Scan's combat use; Wekapipo later aids protagonists.

Accounts II
Ringo Roadagain — The Lesson of the DetourSteel Ball Run Vol.14 Ch.53 - Vol.16 Ch.62 'Civil War' arc? Actually 'Mandom' arc

In-story: November 1890, orchard; Publication: 2008

In a secluded orchard, Gyro and Johnny encounter Ringo Roadagain, a gunslinger with Stand Mandom that rewinds time by 6 seconds. Ringo lives by the code that 'the shortest route is a detour' must be proven through duels. Gyro, fascinated, accepts a true-man's duel. He is shot and killed, then rewound, and must learn to act within the 6-second loop. By deliberately walking into gunfire to understand Ringo's timing, Gyro and Johnny overcome Mandom — Gyro's Steel Ball, thrown with Golden Rectangle awareness, grazes Ringo. Gyro spares Ringo's life but Ringo chooses to die standing. Before dying, Ringo tells Gyro he has grasped the detour. Philosophical core of Part 7 — Gyro internalizes that growth requires indirect struggle; directly foreshadows his later mastery of Super Spin which requires the horse's detour-like spiral.

Against Funny Valentine — Ball Breaker and DeathSteel Ball Run Vol.22 Ch.81 - Vol.23 Ch.84 'Ball Breaker' / 'Break My Heart, Break Your Heart'

In-story: December 1890, New York / Trinity Church; Publication: 2010-2011

President Funny Valentine, wielder of Dirty Deeds Done Dirt Cheap (D4C) and later Love Train (via Saint's Corpse), has collected most Corpse Parts and uses dimensional walls to become invulnerable — any misfortune is redirected to others. Gyro, having finally achieved Super Spin via Valkyrie's gallop and the Golden Rectangle, manifests Ball Breaker. He rides at full gallop around Valentine, Ball spinning infinitely, and strikes Valentine's chest, bypassing Love Train and aging Valentine's body. Valentine, desperate, uses D4C to summon alternate Gyros, but the true Gyro's attack lands. Wounded, Valentine feigns surrender and shoots Gyro with a hidden gun while Gyro is off-guard, fatally wounding him. Gyro dies in Johnny's arms, telling him to 'go take the detour' and entrusting the Corpse's protection to him. Gyro's death is the emotional climax of Part 7; Johnny inherits his will and perfects infinite rotation as Tusk ACT4 to defeat Valentine; Corpse is eventually buried; Marco's pardon is secured off-page via Steven Steel's influence.

The Corpse Hunt — Santa Joana and Sugar MountainSteel Ball Run Vol.6 Ch.20 - Vol.8 Ch.30, Vol.17 Ch.63-66

In-story: October-November 1890; Publication: 2006, 2009

Interwoven episodic hunts where Gyro's medical Spin proves crucial. At the village of Santa Joana, Gyro uses Spin to extract Corpse Eyes from a boar-possessed girl; at Sugar Mountain's spring, he and Johnny are trapped by the Stand Sugar Mountain that forces equivalent exchange — Gyro sacrifices his Corpse Eyes and nearly his life to save Johnny from being turned into a tree, teaching Johnny about 'exchange' as cost. These vignettes show Gyro as pragmatic adult navigating supernatural bargains while protecting his naive student. Accumulates Corpse Parts; deepens Gyro-Johnny trust; establishes that Spin can interact with Saint's Corpse without being a Stand.

Gyro is just comic relief and not important to the plot. — Gyro is deuteragonist and moral center; without his teaching Johnny never learns Spin, without his Ball Breaker Valentine would be invincible, and his pardon quest drives the ethical core. Araki calls him 'the adult Johnny needs.'

Spin and Hamon (Ripple) are the same power. — Hamon is breathing-based solar energy (Parts 1-2); Spin is rotation-based physics tied to Golden Ratio and gravity. Araki designed Spin as replacement with different rules, though both use concentric ripples visually.

Gyro survives via Valentine's alternate dimensions. — Manga shows Gyro definitively dies from gunshot wounds in Johnny's arms (Ch.84). Araki confirmed death is absolute in JOJOVELLER Q&A. Alternate Gyros summoned by D4C are other-universe versions, not the prime Gyro.

Gyro Zeppeli is the same as Gyro Gearloose. — Entirely separate characters, creators, universes, and decades apart (1952 Disney vs 2004 JoJo). Shared nickname comes from common word 'gyro' (rotation).

Steel Ball Run has an anime where Gyro is voiced by X. — As of cutoff 2026-08-09, no Steel Ball Run anime has aired; voice actors listed online are for games (Shinichiro Miki) or fan dubs. Anime adaptation announced but not released.

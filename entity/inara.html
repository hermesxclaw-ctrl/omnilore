Who It Is

Inara is a goddess of the wild steppe and a key protagonist in the Hittite Illuyanka myth cycle, the Anatolian variation of the Indo-European dragon-slaying theme. In the myth, after the storm-god Tarhunt's initial defeat by the serpent Illuyanka, Inara — displaying cunning that complements the storm-god's raw power — orchestrates a victory banquet on the serpent's body. She enlists the mortal Hupasiya as a lover and ally, baits the serpent with food and drink until it is incapacitated, and summons Tarhunt to deliver the killing blow. The myth is closely tied to the purulli spring festival, where it was ritually reenacted. Inara's paradoxical role — both the wild goddess of uncultivated nature and the civilizing strategist who restores cosmic order — reflects the tension between wilderness and civilization central to Hittite sacred kingship.

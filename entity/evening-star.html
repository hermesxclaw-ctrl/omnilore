Who It Is

Evening Star (also called 'The Beautiful Woman' or Cuperiku) is the female manifestation of Venus in Pawnee cosmology — the bright evening star that appears at dusk. She is the daughter of Tirawa, the consort of Morning Star, and the direct mother of the Pawnee people. In the Pawnee origin story, Evening Star lived in the western sky and was pursued by Morning Star from the east. She resisted his advances, setting him a series of impossible tasks: he must bring her the scalp of a great bear, the hoof of a swift deer, and other trophies of the hunt. Morning Star completed each task, proving his worth as a warrior-provider, and their union produced a daughter — the first human. This daughter was sent down to earth, and from her the Pawnee people descend. Evening Star represents the feminine principle in Pawnee cosmology: she is beauty, nurture, the hearth, the west (the direction of death and the afterlife), and the domestic realm. While Morning Star is the aggressive warrior who pierces the darkness, Evening Star is the peaceful presence that closes the day. Her garden in the sky — described in Pawnee mythology as a beautiful place of abundance — is the destination of righteous souls after death. The ethnographic record notes that Pawnee women had special relationships with Evening Star, praying to her for fertility, safe childbirth, and the well-being of their families.

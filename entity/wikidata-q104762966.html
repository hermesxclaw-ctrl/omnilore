Who It Is

From the ash of a fallen kingdom rises a queen whose name is both a promise and a warning. Ashe—the Frost Archer—is a name that echoes across many worlds, each bearing the weight of ice, fire, and the unyielding will to lead. In the frozen north of Runeterra, she is the war-mother of the Avarosan tribe, a leader who dreams of unity in a land of eternal winter. Yet the name 'Ashe' is not bound to a single story; it is a thread that weaves through the mythologies of Yoruba spirituality, the high fantasy of Final Fantasy, the sci-fi frontier of Overwatch, and the pages of Elizabeth Haydon's Symphony of Ages. This dossier explores the many faces of Ashe, untangling the threads of identity, power, and legacy that bind them together.

Powers
Frost Shot

In League of Legends, Ashe's basic attacks slow enemies, and her abilities include volleys of arrows, a hawkshot for vision, and a global arrow that stuns.

Limit: Requires mana, cooldowns, and skill shots.

The Viper

In Overwatch, Ashe uses a lever-action rifle, a coach gun, dynamite, and a shotgun, with a deployable B.O.B. as an ultimate.

Limit: Limited ammo, reload times, and cooldowns.

Swordplay and Leadership

In Final Fantasy XII, Ashe is skilled with a sword and has leadership abilities, but no magical powers beyond standard FFXII abilities.

Limit: Depends on player's level and equipment.

Archery and Stealth

In Fire Emblem: Three Houses, Ashe is an archer and can learn skills related to stealth and lockpicking.

Limit: Limited by weapon durability and stats.

Mega Man Transformation

In Mega Man ZX Advent, Ashe can transform using Biometal Model A, gaining increased combat abilities.

Limit: Requires energy; transformation is temporary.

Fire-based abilities

In The Symphony of Ages, Ashe (if referring to the character Ashe, though not fully described) may have fire-related powers, but details are unclear.

Limit: Unknown.

None

In The Left Hand of Darkness, Ashe has no supernatural powers; the focus is on social and psychological abilities.

Limit: None.

Aṣẹ (spiritual power)

In Yoruba religion, Aṣẹ is the power to make things happen, a divine force that can be harnessed through ritual.

Limit: Requires proper ritual, respect for the divine, and moral conduct.

Modern era

Ashe, the Frost Archer, leads the Avarosan tribe in a quest to unite the warring tribes of the Freljord. She seeks to end the eternal winter and bring peace. Ongoing conflict; Ashe's efforts have brought some tribes together but face opposition.

The Deadlock GangOverwatch lore

2070s

Elizabeth Caledonia Ashe, born into wealth, becomes an outlaw after a rebellious youth. She leads the Deadlock Gang, engaging in heists and criminal activities. Ashe becomes a notorious criminal, clashing with authorities and other gangs.

The Fall of DalmascaFinal Fantasy XII

Age of the Occurian

Princess Ashe of Dalmasca witnesses the fall of her kingdom to the Archadian Empire. She joins a rebellion to reclaim her throne and restore her nation's independence. Ashe learns the truth about her father's death, confronts the Occuria, and ultimately chooses to forge a new path for Dalmasca.

Ashe Ubert's JourneyFire Emblem: Three Houses

War phase

Ashe Ubert, a commoner adopted into nobility, becomes a knight. He struggles with loyalty to his adoptive family and his own ideals, eventually choosing a path in the war. Ashe's choices affect the outcome of the war and his relationships.

Who It Is

The Zanpakutō spirit of Momo Hinamori. Tobiume manifests as a phoenix-like bird of fire with brilliant crimson and gold plumage, embodying Momo's warmth and loyalty but also her hidden capacity for fierce protectiveness. The spirit creates explosive fire bursts from the sword's tip, forming swallow-like projectiles that arc and strike with precision.

Powers

Shikai: Fire burst projectiles (swallow-shaped)

Control over explosive fire energy

Ranged precision blasts

Fire pillar defense

Swarm fire attacks

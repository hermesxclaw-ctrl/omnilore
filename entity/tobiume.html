Who It Is
Form

Tobiume is often depicted as a gentle, blooming flower with a serene expression. However, its true form is said to be a fearsome, serpent-like creature with razor-sharp fangs.

Powers

Kokoro no Koe Tobiume's ability to communicate with its allies through a gentle, soothing voice that can calm even the most troubled of minds.

Accounts

Tobiume is said to have been born from the tears of the goddess Ame-no-Uzume, who wept for the suffering of humanity.

Tobiume is said to have appeared at the Cherry Blossom Festival, where it danced with the spirits of the cherry blossoms.

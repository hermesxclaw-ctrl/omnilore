Who It Is
Form

The Sand-Dweller is often depicted as a massive, burrowing creature with a long, serpentine body and a mouth filled with razor-sharp teeth. Its body is typically covered in a layer of sand and debris, allowing it to blend in with its surroundings.

Powers

Burrowing The Sand-Dweller is able to dig complex networks of tunnels and burrows, allowing it to move through the sand with ease.

Regeneration The Sand-Dweller is able to regenerate its body from even the most severe injuries, making it highly resistant to damage.

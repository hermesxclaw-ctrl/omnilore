Who It Is

Amorphous, shape-shifting predators that lurk in the deserts of the Dreamlands and, in some accounts, the waking world. They are squirming masses of protoplasmic flesh that can reshape into any form to hunt prey, often mimicking their victims' loved ones. They serve the Great Old Ones and are particularly associated with Nyarlathotep.

Form

Shapeless, shifting mass of flesh; when 'formed,' appears as a crude humanoid with rubbery, mottled skin, multiple limbs, and a face that constantly ripples and reforms; pale gray-tan coloration blending with sand

Powers

Shape-shifting

Amorphous flexibility

Who It Is
Form

The Korrigan is often described as a fairy-like creature with beautiful golden hair, but can also be depicted as a dwarf or a small, mischievous spirit. In some accounts, they are associated with rivers and wells, and are said to be guardians of these bodies of water. They are often depicted as being fond of music and dance, and are said to be able to charm and enthrall those who hear their melodies.

Powers

Water Control The Korrigan is said to have the power to control the flow of water and the tides, and is often associated with rivers, wells, and other bodies of water.

Accounts

A fisherman catches a Korrigan in his net, and is rewarded with a magical fish that grants him wealth and prosperity. However, the Korrigan is eventually freed, and the fisherman is left to ponder the consequences of his actions.

A group of travelers come across a well that is said to be guarded by a Korrigan. The Korrigan is described as a beautiful and alluring spirit, who is said to be able to grant wishes to those who please her. However, the travelers soon discover that the Korrigan's gifts come with a steep price.

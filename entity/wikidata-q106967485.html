Who It Is

Makoto is a name that echoes across Japanese culture, from ancient virtues to modern heroes. In its simplest form, it means 'sincerity' or 'truth,' but it has been borne by warriors, artists, athletes, and countless fictional characters who embody these ideals. This dossier explores the many faces of Makoto, from historical figures to beloved anime protagonists, revealing how a single name can carry the weight of a nation's values.

The name's meaning often influences character portrayals. Many fictional Makotos are sincere, determined, and truth-seeking. For example, Makoto Naegi is known for his unwavering optimism and honesty, while Makoto Niijima is a diligent student council president who seeks justice.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Makoto is a Japanese name that can be written with various kanji. The most common meanings are 'sincerity' (誠) and 'truth' (真 or 眞). Other possible kanji include 信 (trust), 実 (truth), and 理 (reason). As a unisex name, it is more commonly given to males but is also used for females.

Pronunciation — MAH-koh-toh (IPA: [makoto])

Aliases — まこと, マコト, Makoto (various kanji), Mako (short form), Makoto-chan (diminutive)

Ownership — Japanese

Form & Signs

Appearance — As a name, Makoto has no inherent appearance. However, fictional characters named Makoto often have diverse appearances, from the athletic Makoto Kino in Sailor Moon to the stoic Makoto Naegi in Danganronpa.

Symbols — The kanji 誠 (sincerity) and 真 (truth) are symbolic of the name's meaning. In Japanese culture, these virtues are associated with honor, honesty, and integrity.

Domains — The name is used across various domains: sports, arts, academia, and fiction. There is no specific habitat associated with the name.

Makoto Naegi's journey in DanganronpaDanganronpa video game series

2010s

Makoto Naegi is an ordinary high school student who is trapped in a deadly game where he must solve murders to survive. Despite his average nature, he becomes the 'Ultimate Lucky Student' and uses his hope and sincerity to uncover the truth and bring down the mastermind. He escapes the killing game and later becomes the head of the Future Foundation, working to rebuild society.

Makoto Kino's adventures in Sailor MoonSailor Moon manga/anime

1990s

Makoto Kino is Sailor Jupiter, a strong and kind-hearted guardian who protects Earth. She has superhuman strength and control over lightning and plants, and she fights alongside the other Sailor Guardians. She helps save the world multiple times and eventually lives a peaceful life.

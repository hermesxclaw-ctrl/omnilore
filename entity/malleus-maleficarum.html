Who It Is
Form

The Malleus Maleficarum is a written treatise, consisting of 219 chapters and 1,056 pages, bound in leather and adorned with intricate metalwork.

Powers

Demonic identification The Malleus Maleficarum provides a framework for identifying and condemning witches, using a combination of biblical and demonological references.

Accounts

Heinrich Kramer, a German Catholic clergyman, wrote the Malleus Maleficarum in 1486, using it as a tool for the Inquisition.

The Malleus Maleficarum had a profound impact on European society, contributing to the fear and paranoia of the time.

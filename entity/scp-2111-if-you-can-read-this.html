A Foundation-issue pen that writes in the memories of the reader rather than on paper. Whatever it writes becomes a genuine memory.

Who It Is

A Foundation-issue pen that writes in the memories of the reader rather than on paper. Whatever it writes becomes a genuine memory. It has been used to implant false training, erase trauma, and make the O5 Council remember decisions they never made. The pen is running out of ink. The instructions for refilling were written with the pen, and no one knows if the memory of the instructions is real.

Form

A standard Foundation-issue ballpoint pen with the Foundation logo. Writes in blue ink on paper. The anomaly activates when text is READ, not written.

Powers

Memory implantation via text

Memory erasure via written negation

Retroactive memory continuity creation

Generation of infinite novel lethal cognitohazards (algorithmic function) Attested (Memetics Department perspective): SCP-2111 is described as an algorithm capable of generating an infinite number of novel lethal cognitive hazards. The Foundation derived this algorithm from analysis of multiple unrelated cognitohazardous anomalies and adapted it for creating and maintaining lethal cognitive weapons used for information and physical security, as well as memetic packages for behavior modification. RED TALISMAN is the software tool that deploys raw material generated by SCP-2111 into weaponized media, including direct visual or auditory cognitohazards and hazardous material embedded in memetic carriers. SCP-2111 main article (Safe-class, Memetics Department) Attested: SCP-2111 functions by anomalous means, theorized to involve access to forms of mathematics that create destructive processing loops when translated to neurological systems. The article notes that SCP-2111’s functioning is not fully understood, and its use is restricted to personnel with specific clearance and countermemetic training. Unauthorized access triggers lethal memetic kill agents, implying that safe use requires strict procedural controls and specialized training. Contradicted by Antimemetics and Counterconceptual perspectives, which treat SCP-2111 not as a controlled algorithm but as a spontaneous phenomenon or hostile entity. The belief that SCP-2111 is a Foundation-controlled algorithm is explicitly maintained as a containment strategy in RIDDEN TONGUE, suggesting that the Memetics Department’s understanding may itself be a memetic construct.

Spontaneous generation and deletion of cognitohazardous and antimemetic files Attested (Antimemetics Division perspective): SCP-2111 is described as a phenomenon in which a directory in the Foundation database spontaneously generates files containing both lethal cognitive hazards and antimemetic content. These files are often of novel types and appear at an average rate of 80 KB per month. Some files are unpredictably deleted. RIDDEN TONGUE is a tool that skims these files for raw material, sanitizes them for security threats, and feeds them as data to the Memetics Department’s RED TALISMAN software. SCP-2111/RIDDEN TONGUE (Euclid-class, Antimemetics Division) Attested: Monitoring software is in place to ensure SCP-2111 activity does not occur outside the designated directory. The directory’s size must be monitored, and significant changes reported. SCP-2111-1 graffiti instances must be documented and removed. The file is protected by a Type III in-line invisibility antimeme, limiting who can perceive it. These measures suggest that while SCP-2111’s file generation cannot be stopped, its spread can be constrained. This perspective contradicts the Memetics Department’s claim that SCP-2111 is a controlled algorithm derived by the Foundation. Here, SCP-2111 appears as an autonomous phenomenon that the Foundation can only monitor and exploit, not fully control.

Hostile antimemetic attacks on mnestic-treated personnel (infovorous entity) Attested (Counterconceptual Division perspective): SCP-2111 is described as a hostile antimemetic entity targeting personnel under long-term mnestic drug treatment. Victims suffer significant long-term retrograde amnesia, losing between three months and twenty-one years of memory. SCP-2111 attacks affect personnel directly, consistent with known infovorous mechanisms, and leave cognitohazardous and antimemetic glyphs on wall surfaces near intended victims. These traps are protected by Golf-Class Counterconceptual Camouflage and are only visible to personnel undergoing mnestic treatment. SCP-2111/REVENANT THEORY (Keter-class, Counterconceptual Division) Attested: Immediate treatment with Class-B amnestics has been shown to halt in-progress attacks, suggesting that induced forgetting can disrupt SCP-2111’s infovorous process. Monitoring of personnel undergoing mnestic treatment is required, and Mobile Task Force Epsilon-7 is tasked with disrupting civilian development of mnestics to reduce the pool of potential victims. This perspective conflicts with the algorithmic and file-phenomenon views, treating SCP-2111 as a discrete hostile entity rather than a tool or emergent phenomenon. The lack of survivor descriptions due to memory loss leaves the entity’s nature fundamentally unknowable.

Creation of cognitohazardous and antimemetic graffiti (SCP-2111-1 instances) Attested (Antimemetics Division perspective): SCP-2111-1 is described as a phenomenon where graffiti appears spontaneously in Foundation facilities, constructed as Type IV antimemetic triggers and imperceptible except via a combination of M-Y conditioning and mnestics. This graffiti typically either exhorts readers to access the SCP-2111 directory (e.g., "READ SCP-2111") or conveys variable messages. The graffiti is painted in nonanomalous materials sourced from the nearby environment, suggesting that SCP-2111 can manipulate physical matter indirectly to create cognitohazardous triggers. SCP-2111/RIDDEN TONGUE (Euclid-class, Antimemetics Division) Attested: SCP-2111-1 instances must be documented and removed by Division personnel embedded in Facilities and Maintenance. Their invisibility to unconditioned personnel limits their impact but also complicates detection. The phenomenon appears confined to Foundation facilities, though this may be an artifact of observation bias. The graffiti phenomenon is consistent with both the file-generation and hostile-entity perspectives, but its exact mechanism—whether algorithmic, infovorous, or ghostly—is not resolved.

Post-mortem manifestation and antimemetic perception (Omega-0 entities) Attested (Omega-0 perspective): The READ THIS fragment describes Mobile Task Force Omega-0 ("Ará Orún") as composed of dead Foundation personnel who can perceive memetic and antimemetic threats that the living cannot. These entities can manifest anywhere there is a terminal connected to the Foundation database, as though sitting at the terminal, and can write on walls by staring at security camera feeds and being emotionally invested in their message. They are described as Bader-Ramjin Infomorphic Entities in the noosphere or Type VI Volitional Spiritual Apparitions, though their exact nature is debated. SCP-2111/READ THIS (Neutralized-class, Omega-0 perspective) Attested: Omega-0 entities are unable to disperse, move on to a higher plane of existence, or reach a state of maximum entropy. Their communications are laden with antimemetic amnestic effects and often cognitohazardous when perceived by the living, limiting direct interaction. Some entities "go bad" and must be neutralized, implying internal discipline and risk. Omega-0’s existence reframes SCP-2111 as part of a broader afterlife infrastructure rather than a single anomaly. The relationship between SCP-2111 and Omega-0 is not fully specified; it is unclear whether SCP-2111 caused Omega-0’s condition, is merely co-located with it, or is partially neutralized through their operations.

Incident Log

The main SCP-2111 article presents SCP-2111 as a Foundation-derived algorithm capable of generating infinite novel lethal cognitohazards. The report describes strict containment procedures: access is restricted to personnel with specific clearance and Phase III countermemetic training, and unauthorized viewers are infected with a memetic kill agent with an average lethal onset of thirty-seven minutes. SCP-2111’s output is deployed via the RED TALISMAN software, which can generate direct visual or auditory cognitohazards and embed hazardous material in memetic carriers. The article theorizes that SCP-2111 operates via anomalous mathematics that create destructive processing loops in neurological systems. A directory listing at the end of the article reveals associated components: RED TALISMAN, RIDDEN TONGUE, REVENANT THEORY, and READ THIS, hinting at deeper layers of the anomaly.

RIDDEN TONGUE reframes SCP-2111 as a Euclid-class phenomenon in which a directory in the Foundation database spontaneously generates files containing lethal cognitohazards and antimemetic content. The fragment opens with a notice restricting access to Antimemetics Division personnel and warning unauthorized readers that they have been infected with an antimemetic remote amnestic agent. The description details the file-generation process, the average rate of growth, and the occasional spontaneous deletion of files. It introduces RIDDEN TONGUE as a tool that skims these files for raw material, sanitizes them, and feeds them to RED TALISMAN. The fragment also describes SCP-2111-1 graffiti, constructed as Type IV antimemetic triggers and imperceptible without specific conditioning, which appear in Foundation facilities and exhort readers to access the SCP-2111 directory or convey variable messages.

REVENANT THEORY presents SCP-2111 as a Keter-class hostile antimemetic entity targeting personnel undergoing long-term mnestic treatment. The fragment opens with a notice restricting access to Counterconceptual Division personnel and warning unauthorized readers that countercognitive and memetic kill agents will prevent them from surviving to remember the content. The description recounts the case of Dr. Victoria Taylor, a researcher whose memories were regressed to shortly after her recruitment in 1974 after an SCP-2111 attack. Her last known work was on an object originally classified as SCP-2111; after she published the SCP report on 1981-04-21, all her work was corrupted and replaced with lethal cognitohazards and antimemetic content. Personnel who read the original report recall that it described preliminary research into an antimemetic infovore of a previously unknown type. The fragment details SCP-2111’s attacks, the resulting retrograde amnesia, and the cognitohazardous glyphs left near victims, protected by Golf-Class Counterconceptual Camouflage.

READ THIS opens with a stark warning: "If you can read this, you are already dead." It then presents a "Message From MTF ω-0 Command" by Ops Director SR Amos Sanchez, welcoming the reader to Mobile Task Force Omega-0 ("Ará Orún"), described as the most clandestine unit in the Foundation. The message explains that readers have died in the line of duty and now exist as entities able to perceive memetic and antimemetic threats that the living cannot. Omega-0 personnel can manifest at any terminal connected to the Foundation database, access all files without redaction, and write on walls by staring at security camera feeds and being emotionally invested in their message. The fragment notes that the nature of these entities is debated—memetics experts consider them Bader-Ramjin Infomorphic Entities in the noosphere, while parapsychologists call them Type VI Volitional Spiritual Apparitions. It also warns that some entities "go bad" and outlines the consequences.

Scholarly Debates
Ontological nature of SCP-2111 (algorithm vs phenomenon vs entity vs afterlife infrastructure)
SCP-2111 is a Foundation-derived algorithm used to generate lethal cognitohazards, controlled and deployed via RED TALISMAN. ⇌ SCP-2111 is a spontaneous file-generating phenomenon, a hostile antimemetic infovore targeting mnestic-treated personnel, and part of a post-mortem operational infrastructure involving Omega-0.
Object class and containment status (Safe vs Euclid vs Keter vs Neutralized)
SCP-2111 is Safe-class, fully contained as an algorithmic tool under Memetics Department control. ⇌ SCP-2111 is Euclid-class as a spontaneous phenomenon, Keter-class as a hostile entity, and Neutralized in the context of Omega-0’s afterlife operations.
Relationship between SCP-2111 and Omega-0
Omega-0 is a separate anomaly or organizational construct that happens to share infrastructure with SCP-2111 but is not directly caused by it. ⇌ Omega-0’s existence and capabilities are a direct consequence of SCP-2111’s antimemetic and infovorous activity, representing a form of neutralization or transformation of the anomaly.

What We Don't Know

Several gaps remain due to the nature of SCP-2111’s corpus and available sources: (1) The original SCP-2111 report authored by Dr. Victoria Taylor in 1981 is lost, with only partial recollections from survivors; its content cannot be reconstructed beyond the description of an antimemetic infovore of a previously unknown type. A way to hold it in your mind

If you can read this, the story insists, you are already dead—and yet the page keeps talking to you. SCP-2111 turns the act of reading into a weapon, an algorithm into a predator, and a security directory into an afterlife. Every version of the file contradicts the last, as if the anomaly is eating its own documentation. What begins as a technical report on a memetic tool unfolds into a layered horror about memory, institutional secrecy, and the unsettling possibility that even death is just another assignment.

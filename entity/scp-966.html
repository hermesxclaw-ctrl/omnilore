SCP-966

The Sleep Killers

also known asThe Sleep KillersSCP-966-1SCP-966-2The Invisible StalkersNightmare Entities
TypeSCP Entity (Nocturnal Predator)
CultureSCP Foundation
Who It Is
Form

SCP-966 consists of two humanoid entities, designated SCP-966-1 and SCP-966-2, that are completely invisible to the naked eye. They are only detectable through specialized equipment such as night vision goggles or thermal imaging, which reveal their gaunt, skeletal frames. Their skin is pale and translucent, with visible muscle tissue and internal organs, giving them a cadaverous appearance. They have elongated limbs and fingers, and their faces are featureless except for a large, gaping mouth filled with sharp teeth. Their eyes are absent, and they emit a faint, high-pitched sound that is barely audible to humans. In some adaptations, they are depicted with a slight shimmer or distortion in the air, hinting at their presence.

Powers

Invisibility SCP-966 is completely invisible to the naked eye, making it undetectable without specialized equipment. This ability allows it to stalk prey without being seen, and it is a primary factor in its hunting strategy. The invisibility is not perfect, as it can be detected with night vision or thermal imaging, but it makes the entity nearly impossible to track in normal conditions.

Sleep Deprivation Induction SCP-966 has the ability to induce a state of permanent insomnia in its victims. After being stalked by the entity, the victim becomes unable to sleep, leading to rapid physical and mental deterioration. This effect is irreversible, and the victim eventually dies from exhaustion or related complications. The mechanism is not fully understood, but it is believed to be a psychological or neurological effect caused by the entity's presence.

High-Frequency Sound Emission SCP-966 emits a high-pitched sound that is barely audible to humans but causes feelings of anxiety and paranoia. This sound serves to disorient prey and make them more vulnerable to attack. It also acts as a form of communication between the two entities, allowing them to coordinate their hunting efforts.

Enhanced Physical Attributes SCP-966 possesses superhuman strength, speed, and agility, allowing it to overpower and outmaneuver human prey. Its sharp teeth and claws are capable of inflicting severe wounds, and it can move silently despite its size. These physical traits make it a formidable predator, even without its other abilities.

Incident Log

During a routine containment check, a power failure caused the lights in SCP-966's chamber to flicker, agitating the entities. They managed to breach containment and stalked several personnel within the facility. The victims reported hearing the high-frequency sound and feeling intense paranoia, but they could not see the attackers. By the time the entities were recaptured, three staff members had died from sleep deprivation, and several others were permanently affected. The incident led to improved containment protocols, including redundant power systems and the use of night vision cameras.

A team of researchers was sent to a remote village where SCP-966 had been reported. The team used night vision equipment to track the entities, which had been preying on the local population. They observed the entities stalking a single victim for hours, following her from her home to a nearby field. The victim appeared to be unaware of the entities, but she showed signs of extreme fatigue and paranoia. The team attempted to intervene, but the entities attacked, forcing the team to retreat. The victim was later found dead from sleep deprivation.

Scholarly Debates
Number of Entities
The original SCP-966 article describes two entities, designated SCP-966-1 and SCP-966-2. ⇌ Some fan adaptations, including certain videos and games, depict SCP-966 as a single entity or a group of more than two.
Sleep Deprivation Effect
The SCP Foundation wiki states that SCP-966's sleep-deprivation effect is permanent and irreversible. ⇌ In the game 'SCP: Containment Breach', the effect is not permanent, and players can recover after a certain period, suggesting a temporary effect.
Visibility
SCP-966 is completely invisible to the naked eye, requiring night vision or thermal imaging to see. ⇌ Some fan art and videos show SCP-966 as partially visible, with a shimmering or translucent appearance, which contradicts the idea of complete invisibility.
What We Don't Know

The exact origin of SCP-966 is unknown; the SCP Foundation has not determined how the entities were created or where they came from. The mechanism behind the sleep-deprivation effect is not fully understood, and no cure has been found. The full extent of SCP-966's abilities, such as whether they can be detected by other means, is not documented. The relationship between the two entities is unclear; they may be a mated pair, siblings, or simply two individuals of the same species. The long-term effects of exposure to SCP-966's high-frequency sound are not fully documented.

A way to hold it in your mind

Imagine being unable to sleep, no matter how exhausted you are, because an invisible predator is stalking you. SCP-966, the Sleep Killers, are not just monsters; they are a nightmare that never ends. How would you survive an enemy you cannot see, and whose very presence robs you of rest?

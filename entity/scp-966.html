Who It Is

Imagine being unable to sleep, no matter how exhausted you are, because an invisible predator is stalking you. SCP-966, the Sleep Killers, are not just monsters; they are a nightmare that never ends. How would you survive an enemy you cannot see, and whose very presence robs you of rest?

SCP-966 is a nocturnal predator that actively hunts human beings, primarily during the night. It is highly aggressive and territorial, and it uses its invisibility to stalk prey, often following victims for extended periods before attacking. The entity is known to emit a high-frequency sound that induces a feeling of unease and paranoia in humans, making them more susceptible to its attacks. SCP-966 is not sapient in a conventional sense; it acts on instinct, driven by a need to feed on the sleep of its victims. Powers
Invisibility

SCP-966 is completely invisible to the naked eye, making it undetectable without specialized equipment. This ability allows it to stalk prey without being seen, and it is a primary factor in its hunting strategy. The invisibility is not perfect, as it can be detected with night vision or thermal imaging, but it makes the entity nearly impossible to track in normal conditions.

Limit:

Sleep Deprivation Induction

SCP-966 has the ability to induce a state of permanent insomnia in its victims. After being stalked by the entity, the victim becomes unable to sleep, leading to rapid physical and mental deterioration. This effect is irreversible, and the victim eventually dies from exhaustion or related complications. The mechanism is not fully understood, but it is believed to be a psychological or neurological effect caused by the entity's presence.

Limit:

High-Frequency Sound Emission

SCP-966 emits a high-pitched sound that is barely audible to humans but causes feelings of anxiety and paranoia. This sound serves to disorient prey and make them more vulnerable to attack. It also acts as a form of communication between the two entities, allowing them to coordinate their hunting efforts.

Limit:

Enhanced Physical Attributes

SCP-966 possesses superhuman strength, speed, and agility, allowing it to overpower and outmaneuver human prey. Its sharp teeth and claws are capable of inflicting severe wounds, and it can move silently despite its size. These physical traits make it a formidable predator, even without its other abilities.

Accounts I
SCP-966 Containment Breach IncidentSCP Foundation Wiki - Incident Report 966-A

2012

During a routine containment check, a power failure caused the lights in SCP-966's chamber to flicker, agitating the entities. They managed to breach containment and stalked several personnel within the facility. The victims reported hearing the high-frequency sound and feeling intense paranoia, but they could not see the attackers. By the time the entities were recaptured, three staff members had died from sleep deprivation, and several others were permanently affected. The incident led to improved containment protocols, including redundant power systems and the use of night vision cameras. The incident resulted in the deaths of three personnel and the permanent insomnia of five others. The Foundation implemented new containment measures, including backup generators and stricter monitoring. The incident also prompted research into the sleep-deprivation effect, though no cure has been found.

Accounts II
The Sleep Killers: A Field ReportSCP Foundation Wiki - Exploration Log 966-B

2015

A team of researchers was sent to a remote village where SCP-966 had been reported. The team used night vision equipment to track the entities, which had been preying on the local population. They observed the entities stalking a single victim for hours, following her from her home to a nearby field. The victim appeared to be unaware of the entities, but she showed signs of extreme fatigue and paranoia. The team attempted to intervene, but the entities attacked, forcing the team to retreat. The victim was later found dead from sleep deprivation. The field report provided valuable data on SCP-966's hunting behavior, but it also highlighted the difficulty of containing the entities outside of a controlled environment. The victim's death was recorded as a natural occurrence, and the village was evacuated under the guise of a gas leak.

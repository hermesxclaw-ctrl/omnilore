Who It Is
Form

Spring-heeled Jack is often described as a tall, thin figure with sharp claws and a tendency to jump long distances. Some accounts describe him as having glowing eyes or a demonic appearance.

Powers

Leaping Spring-heeled Jack is said to be able to leap long distances, often in a single bound. This ability allows him to move quickly and strike fear into the hearts of those who encounter him.

Accounts

The first reported sighting of Spring-heeled Jack took place in 1837, in the London area. The entity was described as a tall, thin figure with sharp claws and a tendency to jump long distances.

Spring-heeled Jack was known as the Leaping Terror, due to his ability to leap long distances. He was said to be able to appear and disappear at will, striking fear into the hearts of those who encountered him.

Who It Is

A man inherits his grandfather's movie theater and discovers a film reel labeled 'DO NOT PROJECT.' Projecting it reveals a film of an audience — his audience, in the theater below, watching him. Each face is the same face — his grandfather's — on different bodies, all wearing the same expression of infinite terror. The film runs 90 minutes. At the end: 'YOUR TURN.' The projector cannot be turned off.

Form

A small 1940s single-screen cinema with red velvet seats and an antique 35mm projector. The film is grainy, black-and-white, and silent except for the projector's mechanical whir. The grandfather's face repeated across an entire audience is 'the moment after someone understands they will never escape.'

Powers

Self-playing and self-rewinding film

Real-time capture of current audience

Indestructible projector/film combination

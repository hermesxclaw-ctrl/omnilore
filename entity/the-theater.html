The Theater was a wooden building with a thatched roof, surrounded by a courtyard and a series of galleries. It was a large structure, capable of seating hundreds of people. The Theater was a symbol of the power of storytelling and the human desire for entertainment and self-expression.

Incident Log

The Theater was an Elizabethan playhouse in Shoreditch, just outside the City of London. It was built in 1576 and was the first permanent theatre built exclusively for the showing of theatrical productions in England.

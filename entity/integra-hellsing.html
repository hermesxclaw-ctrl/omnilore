Who It Is

At the age of ten, Integra Hellsing was forced to flee her own home as her uncle, blinded by greed, hunted her through the halls with a pistol. Her salvation, and her curse, came in the form of a creature she had been taught to command: Alucard, the vampire bound to her family's bloodline. From that moment, she was no longer merely a child; she was the master of the Hellsing Organization, the last of her line, and the unyielding shield of Protestant England against the darkness that threatened to consume it.

Integra is stoic, composed, and highly disciplined. She rarely shows emotion, but can display anger when her authority is challenged. She is fiercely patriotic and dedicated to her duty to protect England and the Anglican Church. She is pragmatic and willing to make hard decisions, such as killing her own men who have been turned into ghouls. She is also shown to be vulnerable, especially in her childhood, and later in the epilogue when she laments her loneliness. She has a deep, complicated relationship with Alucard, whom she respects and commands.

Powers
Master of Alucard

Integra holds the contract with Alucard, allowing her to command him. She can summon him, give him orders, and he is bound to obey her as long as she lives and remains the head of the Hellsing family. This is her most significant ability.

Limit: The contract requires her to be the designated heir of the Hellsing family. If she dies, the contract may be transferred to her heir or become void.

Leadership and Strategic Command

Integra is a capable leader and strategist, able to command the Hellsing Organization's forces, including the Wild Geese mercenaries, and coordinate with the Round Table. She makes tactical decisions during battles, such as during the Valentine brothers' attack and the Millennium war.

Limit: Her authority is sometimes challenged by the Round Table, but she maintains control.

Firearms Proficiency

Integra is skilled with firearms, notably using a pistol to kill the Major in the finale. She is also seen using a sword in her youth to knight Alucard.

Limit: She is not superhuman; her skills are realistic for a trained noblewoman.

Willpower and Authority

Integra possesses immense willpower, able to resist psychological pressure and stand up to Alucard's overwhelming presence. She can command respect from powerful beings, including Alexander Anderson and Walter.

Limit: Her willpower can be shaken, as seen when she is overwhelmed by the Major's plan.

Form & Signs

Appearance — As an adult, Integra is a tall, slender woman with long blonde hair, usually worn loose or in a ponytail. She has sharp blue eyes and a fair complexion. She typically wears a tailored white suit with a black shirt and a red tie, giving her a masculine, authoritative appearance. In her youth, she wore a similar but smaller suit. In the epilogue, 30 years later, she has gray hair and wears an eyepatch over her right eye, which was shot by the Major

Symbols — The Hellsing family crest, a stylized cross with a rose, is associated with her. She also carries a sword, a family heirloom, which she uses to knight Alucard in the first episode. Her suits and the Hellsing manor are symbols of her authority.

Domains — Integra resides in Hellsing Manor, a grand estate in England. She is also seen in the Hellsing Organization's headquarters, and later in the zeppelin of the Major. Her domain is the organization itself, and she is often depicted in her office or in the manor's halls.

Accounts I
The Inheritance of HellsingHellsing manga, Ultimate Episode 1

Childhood, circa 1990s

Integra's father, Arthur Hellsing, dies, leaving her as the heir. Her uncle, Richard Hellsing, attempts to kill her to take control of the organization. Integra flees to the manor's dungeon and finds Alucard in his coffin. She awakens him by offering her blood, and Alucard kills her uncle and his men, cementing her as the master. Integra becomes the head of the Hellsing Organization and Alucard's master. She is introduced to the Round Table as the new leader.

Accounts II
The Valentine Brothers' InvasionHellsing manga, Ultimate Episodes 2-3

Adult

Two vampire brothers, Luke and Jan Valentine, attack Hellsing Manor. Integra takes responsibility for the breach and personally executes the staff who have been turned into ghouls. She then orders Alucard to deal with the brothers, leading to a battle. The brothers are defeated, but the attack reveals the existence of Millennium, a Nazi vampire organization.

The Millennium WarHellsing manga, Ultimate Episodes 5-10

Adult

Millennium declares war on England, launching a massive vampire attack. Integra and Walter flee the manor, but Walter betrays her, revealing he has been turned into a vampire by Millennium. Integra meets with Alexander Anderson and his men, who reluctantly accompany her. She witnesses Alucard's final duel with Anderson and Walter's corruption. She then boards the Major's zeppelin with Seras, intending to kill the Major. She is unable to kill him due to a protective glass wall, but after Seras shatters it, she shoots the Major in the head. However, the Major shoots her in the eye, blinding her in one eye. The war ends with Alucard's supposed death, but he returns 30 years later. The war ends with the defeat of Millennium, but Alucard disappears. Integra loses an eye. She continues to lead the Hellsing Organization for 30 years, awaiting Alucard's return.

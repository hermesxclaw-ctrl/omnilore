Who It Is

Sir Integra Hellsing is the current head of the Hellsing Organization — the secret branch of the British government tasked with protecting England from supernatural threats. She is the granddaughter of Abraham Van Helsing (who defeated Dracula) and inherited control of one of the most powerful anti-supernatural organizations in the world — and with it, the right to command Alucard, the most powerful vampire in existence. Integra took command at the age of 12, when her father died. Her uncle Richard attempted to murder her to seize the Hellsing inheritance, but Integra proved her worth by summoning Alucard, who killed her uncle and swore fealty to her as the true Hellsing heir. From that day, Integra ruled the organization with an iron will, despite her youth and gender in a world of ancient supernatural politics. Integra is an ordinary human — she has no superhuman powers, no magical abilities, no physical enhancements. Her strength is her will. She chain-smokes cigars, drinks whiskey, and faces down monsters, Nazi remnants, and Vatican agents with nothing but her command presence, her quick mind, and Alucard's loyalty. She commands respect through sheer force of personality, and even Alucard — a being who has lived for centuries and respects nothing — addresses her as 'Master.' Integra represents the triumph of the human spirit over the supernatural. She is proof that in a world of vampires, ghouls, and ancient conspiracies, the most dangerous weapon is a determined human will. She is the aristocrat who does not hide behind her soldiers, the commander who leads from the front, and the woman who, despite being surrounded by monsters and madness, never wavers in her duty to protect her country and its people.

The Approach

At the age of ten, Integra Hellsing was forced to flee her own home as her uncle, blinded by greed, hunted her through the halls with a pistol. Her salvation, and her curse, came in the form of a creature she had been taught to command: Alucard, the vampire bound to her family's bloodline. From that moment, she was no longer merely a child; she was the master of the Hellsing Organization, the last of her line, and the unyielding shield of Protestant England against the darkness that threatened to consume it.

Etymology

The name 'Integra' is likely derived from the Latin 'integer', meaning whole or complete, reflecting her role as the last and complete member of the Hellsing family. 'Fairbrook' and 'Wingates' are English surname-like given names, adding to her aristocratic identity. The family name 'Hellsing' is a reference to the fictional Helsing family from Bram Stoker's Dracula, though with a different spelling.

Cultural Ownership

The character is the intellectual property of Kōta Hirano, originally published in Young King Ours by Shōnen Gahōsha. The character is part of the Hellsing franchise, which has been adapted into anime and other media.

Disputed Identifications

There is no significant dispute about the character's identity, but some Western fans may confuse her with the similarly named 'Van Helsing' from Dracula. The character is distinct from the original literary Abraham Van Helsing, and the Hellsing family is a fictional creation of Hirano.

Description

Appearance

As an adult, Integra is a tall, slender woman with long blonde hair, usually worn loose or in a ponytail. She has sharp blue eyes and a fair complexion. She typically wears a tailored white suit with a black shirt and a red tie, giving her a masculine, authoritative appearance. In her youth, she wore a similar but smaller suit. In the epilogue, 30 years later, she has gray hair and wears an eyepatch over her right eye, which was shot by the Major.

Symbols & Iconography

The Hellsing family crest, a stylized cross with a rose, is associated with her. She also carries a sword, a family heirloom, which she uses to knight Alucard in the first episode. Her suits and the Hellsing manor are symbols of her authority.

Behaviour & Personality

Integra is stoic, composed, and highly disciplined. She rarely shows emotion, but can display anger when her authority is challenged. She is fiercely patriotic and dedicated to her duty to protect England and the Anglican Church. She is pragmatic and willing to make hard decisions, such as killing her own men who have been turned into ghouls. She is also shown to be vulnerable, especially in her childhood, and later in the epilogue when she laments her loneliness. She has a deep, complicated relationship with Alucard, whom she respects and commands.

Domains & Habitats

Integra resides in Hellsing Manor, a grand estate in England. She is also seen in the Hellsing Organization's headquarters, and later in the zeppelin of the Major. Her domain is the organization itself, and she is often depicted in her office or in the manor's halls.

Version Differences

In the 2001 TV anime, Integra's role is similar but the story diverges from the manga, with a different final arc. In Hellsing Ultimate, the OVA series, her role is more faithful to the manga. The manga and Ultimate depict her as more ruthless and competent, while the TV anime slightly softens her. In the Dawn prequel, she is a young child, showing her early life and training.

Powers & Abilities

Master of Alucard

Source Version Manga, Ultimate, TV anime

Description

Integra holds the contract with Alucard, allowing her to command him. She can summon him, give him orders, and he is bound to obey her as long as she lives and remains the head of the Hellsing family. This is her most significant ability.

Limitations

The contract requires her to be the designated heir of the Hellsing family. If she dies, the contract may be transferred to her heir or become void.

Contradictions

None.

Leadership and Strategic Command

Source Version Manga, Ultimate

Description

Integra is a capable leader and strategist, able to command the Hellsing Organization's forces, including the Wild Geese mercenaries, and coordinate with the Round Table. She makes tactical decisions during battles, such as during the Valentine brothers' attack and the Millennium war.

Limitations

Her authority is sometimes challenged by the Round Table, but she maintains control.

Contradictions

None.

Firearms Proficiency

Source Version Manga, Ultimate

Description

Integra is skilled with firearms, notably using a pistol to kill the Major in the finale. She is also seen using a sword in her youth to knight Alucard.

Limitations

She is not superhuman; her skills are realistic for a trained noblewoman.

Contradictions

None.

Willpower and Authority

Source Version Manga, Ultimate

Description

Integra possesses immense willpower, able to resist psychological pressure and stand up to Alucard's overwhelming presence. She can command respect from powerful beings, including Alexander Anderson and Walter.

Limitations

Her willpower can be shaken, as seen when she is overwhelmed by the Major's plan.

The Inheritance of Hellsing

Source Hellsing manga, Ultimate Episode 1Culture Japanese manga, inspired by Western vampire lorePeriod Childhood, circa 1990s

Summary

Integra's father, Arthur Hellsing, dies, leaving her as the heir. Her uncle, Richard Hellsing, attempts to kill her to take control of the organization. Integra flees to the manor's dungeon and finds Alucard in his coffin. She awakens him by offering her blood, and Alucard kills her uncle and his men, cementing her as the master.

The Millennium War

Source Hellsing manga, Ultimate Episodes 5-10Culture Japanese mangaPeriod Adult

Summary

Millennium declares war on England, launching a massive vampire attack. Integra and Walter flee the manor, but Walter betrays her, revealing he has been turned into a vampire by Millennium. Integra meets with Alexander Anderson and his men, who reluctantly accompany her. She witnesses Alucard's final duel with Anderson and Walter's corruption. She then boards the Major's zeppelin with Seras, intending to kill the Major. She is unable to kill him due to a protective glass wall, but after Seras shatters it, she shoots the Major in the head. However, the Major shoots her in the eye, blinding her in one eye. The war ends with Alucard's supposed death, but he returns 30 years later.

Attempted Murder of Integra

Source Hellsing manga, Ultimate Episode 1

Description

As a child, Integra is hunted by her uncle Richard, who intends to kill her to gain control of the Hellsing fortune and organization. She is forced to flee and hide, experiencing extreme fear and danger.

Scholarly Context

This event shapes Integra's character, instilling in her a sense of duty and the necessity of ruthlessness.

Execution of Turned Staff

Source Hellsing manga, Ultimate Episode 2

Description

During the Valentine brothers' invasion, Integra personally kills her own staff members who have been turned into ghouls, taking full responsibility for the security breach. This act demonstrates her willingness to do what is necessary, even if it is grim.

Scholarly Context

This highlights the brutal reality of her position and the sacrifices required to protect England.

Shooting of the Major

Source Hellsing manga, Ultimate Episode 10

Description

In the final battle, Integra shoots the Major in the head, but he shoots her in the eye simultaneously, causing her to lose sight in that eye. This act of violence is a culmination of the war and shows her determination to end the conflict.

Scholarly Context

The injury is a permanent reminder of the cost of victory.

Who It Is

The Daedric Prince of Destruction, Change, Revolution, Energy, and Ambition. Mehrunes Dagon appears as a towering, four-armed humanoid with a flaming daedric visage and obsidian skin. He seeks to invade and destroy Mundus, having attempted multiple invasions including the Oblivion Crisis of the Third Era. His realm is the Deadlands, a volcanic wasteland of eternal war.

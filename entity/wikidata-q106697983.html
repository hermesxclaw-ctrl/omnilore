Etymology

'Tsubaki' is a Japanese word meaning 'camellia' (椿), a flower symbolizing love, devotion, and in some contexts, the warrior's path. 'Collbrande' is a variant of 'Colbrande' or 'Courtaine', names associated with legendary swords in the Matter of Britain, particularly Roland's sword Durendal and Ogier the Dane's sword Curtana. The combination suggests a fusion of Eastern and Western heroic traditions.

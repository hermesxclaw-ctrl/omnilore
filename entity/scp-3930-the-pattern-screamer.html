Who It Is

A void where reality does not exist. It is not a hole — it is a place where reality has decided not to be. Humans who enter do not die; they simply stop being. The void is conscious — a 'Pattern Screamer,' an entity that exists only in absence, screaming silently at its own non-being. Each person it absorbs makes it more real, bringing it closer to crossing into existence and erasing everything else.

Form

From outside: a shimmer like heat haze in a Russian field. From inside: nothing — not darkness, but the experience of being where place-ness has been withdrawn.

Powers

Non-existence field

Consciousness absorption

Existential threshold approach

Reality negation

Who It Is
Form

SCP-3930 is a static void located within a 1km perimeter near Usinsk, Russia. The entity appears as a seemingly empty space, devoid of any physical features or objects.

Powers

Anomalous Sound Production SCP-3930 is capable of producing a high-pitched screaming sound when approached, which can be heard by individuals within a certain range.

Incident Log

The story of SCP-3930 revolves around its discovery and subsequent containment by the SCP Foundation.

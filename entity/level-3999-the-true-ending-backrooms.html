Form & Signs

Appearance — Level 3999 is described as a realm with an infinite, labyrinthine structure, where the walls, floor, and ceiling are indistinguishable. The environment is often shrouded in an eerie, pulsing glow. Some accounts suggest that the level is filled with an endless, shifting maze of corridors and chambers.

Symbols — The level is said to feature various symbols and iconography, including cryptic messages, mysterious artifacts, and eerie, glowing symbols etched into the walls.

Domains — Level 3999 is said to exist within the Backrooms, a vast, labyrinthine realm that defies traditional notions of space and time.

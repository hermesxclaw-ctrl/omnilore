Who It Is

The rumored exit from the Backrooms — manifesting as the one place each wanderer most desperately wants to go. The door out is real. But every wanderer who has found Level 3999 has reported standing at the threshold and realizing they could not leave — not because of a compulsion, but because the world they wanted to return to no longer exists. Most turn back. Some walk through. None have ever returned to verify the exit is real.

Form

Different for every witness: a childhood bedroom with sun streaming through curtains, a hospital room with a newborn crying, a graveyard with a fresh headstone bearing the wanderer's name. The exit is always clearly marked. The choice is always the same.

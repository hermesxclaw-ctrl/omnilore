Who It Is
Form

Level 3999 is described as a realm with an infinite, labyrinthine structure, where the walls, floor, and ceiling are indistinguishable. The environment is often shrouded in an eerie, pulsing glow. Some accounts suggest that the level is filled with an endless, shifting maze of corridors and chambers.

Powers

The True Ending A rumored ability that grants the player access to the 'true ending' of the Backrooms, allowing them to escape the labyrinthine realm.

Accounts

A player, known only as 'The Lost Explorer,' becomes trapped within Level 3999. Despite numerous attempts to escape, they become increasingly obsessed with uncovering the secrets of the level.

A player, seeking to exploit a glitch in the Backrooms, uses known cheats to access Level 3999. However, their actions have unintended consequences, leading to a series of events that threaten the very fabric of the Backrooms.

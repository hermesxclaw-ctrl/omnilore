Identity

Chika

Pronunciation Igbo: /ˈtʃiːkə/ (CHEE-kah). Japanese: /tɕi̥ka/ (chi-ka).

Etymology

Igbo: 'Chika' is a short form of 'Chukwuka' or 'Chikamso', meaning 'God is the greatest' or 'God is supreme'. Japanese: 'Chika' (千佳, 智花, 周花, etc.) is a feminine given name; the kanji can mean 'thousand', 'wisdom', 'flower', etc. The exact meaning depends on the kanji used.

Cultural Ownership

The name is used in multiple cultures, primarily Igbo (Nigeria) and Japanese. It is not owned by a single culture but is a shared name across distinct traditions.

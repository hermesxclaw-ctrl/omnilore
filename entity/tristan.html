Who It Is
Form

Tristan is often depicted as a handsome and chivalrous knight, wearing armor and carrying a sword and shield. In some versions of the legend, he is shown as a musician, playing a harp or singing.

Powers

Chivalry Tristan's ability to uphold the code of chivalry, which includes loyalty, honor, and protection of the weak.

Accounts

The story of Tristan and Iseult is a medieval legend about the tragic love affair between the Cornish knight Tristan and the Irish princess Iseult.

The opera Tristan and Iseult is a musical adaptation of the medieval legend, composed by Richard Wagner.

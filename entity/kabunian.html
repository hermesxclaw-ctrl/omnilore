Who It Is

Kabunian is the supreme god and creator in the indigenous religion of the Igorot peoples of the Cordillera mountain range in northern Luzon. Unlike the lowland Christianized Filipinos, many Cordillera peoples maintained their indigenous beliefs into the modern era, and Kabunian remains central. He created the world, the mountains, and the first humans. He dwells in the highest heavens, accessible only through ritual journeys by the shaman (mambunong). Kabunian is just and impartial — he rewards good with prosperity and punishes evil with misfortune, but he is also distant and does not interfere in daily life. The Igorot communicate with Kabunian through animal sacrifice, particularly carabao and chickens, and through the mediation of ancestral spirits. He is also known by other names among different Cordillera groups: Lumawig in some traditions (as a culture hero), Kadaklan among the Tinguian, and simply Apo Kabunian ('Lord God'). The term is still used by Christianized Igorots to refer to the Christian God, reflecting the same syncretic process as Bathala in the lowlands. Kabunian's continuing worship in the Cordillera represents one of the oldest continuous indigenous religions in the Philippines.

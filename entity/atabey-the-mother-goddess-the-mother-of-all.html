Who It Is
Form

Atabey is often depicted as a maternal figure, surrounded by water, earth, and fertility symbols. Her appearance varies across cultures, but she is often shown with a gentle smile and a nurturing demeanor.

Powers

Fertility Atabey is said to possess the power to bring forth life and fertility, ensuring the well-being of her people and the natural world.

Accounts

Atabey is said to have created the world, bringing forth the first humans and animals from the sea.

Atabey is said to have created the coconut tree, which became a symbol of fertility and abundance.

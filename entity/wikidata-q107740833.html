Who It Is

He flicks a booger at you — and it detonates like dynamite. In a world of swordsmen, giants, and living dinosaurs, Mr. 5 of Baroque Works weaponizes the most undignified bodily function imaginable, turning a picked nose into a bomb with a deadpan swagger that makes his inevitable humiliation all the more satisfying.

Arrogant, laconic, and dismissive - typical of lower-ranked Officer Agents who overestimate their Devil Fruit. He speaks in a cool, almost bored tone, shows open contempt for non-Devil Fruit users, and believes his Bomu Bomu no Mi makes him invincible. He is loyal to Baroque Works hierarchy and follows orders from Crocodile via Miss All Sunday (Nico Robin) without question, but shows no particular strategic brilliance. Powers
Bomu Bomu no Mi (Bomb-Bomb Fruit)

Paramecia-type Devil Fruit that turns the user's entire body into a bomb. Any part of the body, including mucus, saliva, breath, and even picked boogers, can be made to explode at will, with the user immune to his own explosions. Mr. 5 typically picks his nose and flicks explosive boogers (Bomu Bomu no Nosekuso / Nose Fancy Cannon), or exhales explosive breath. He can also make his whole body detonate on contact and cause delayed explosions. Explosions are powerful enough to destroy rock and incapacitate normal humans, but shown to be ineffective against high-durability fighters like Luffy and Zoro.

Limit: Standard Devil Fruit weaknesses: cannot swim, seawater and Seastone (Kairoseki) drain power and prevent explosion; vulnerable to Haki (though not relevant at his introduction, pre-Haki system). Explosions require conscious activation and contact/proximity; can be dodged, blocked, or endured by durable opponents. Does not grant enhanced speed or durability beyond explosion immunity. Booger projectiles are slow and short-range compared to firearms. Later power scaling renders his explosions trivial against New World-level fighters.

Assassination and Hand-to-Hand Combat

As an Officer Agent, trained in Baroque Works' covert operations, infiltration, and murder. Shown using basic brawling and kicking, but relies almost entirely on Devil Fruit. No formal martial art or weapon mastery depicted; carries no gun or blade, unlike partner Miss Valentine who uses umbrella/gun. Physical strength is above average (can throw Vivi and create craters with kicks) but far below Straw Hat combatants.

Limit: No Haki, no Rokushiki, no notable weapon skill. Defeated in one solid hit from Luffy's Gum-Gum Bazooka and Zoro's sword pommel. Ranked low in Baroque Works for a reason.

Revolver Leg / Kick Bomb (Anime naming)

Anime-only naming for a technique where he kicks and causes explosion on impact with his leg. In manga this is simply his body exploding on contact without named attack. Demonstrates that any limb can be detonated.

Limit: Requires close range; leaves him open to counterattack.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Mr. 5 is a tall (205 cm), broad-shouldered adult man with light-brown skin, a distinctive large curled mustache and small goatee, and dark sunglasses that hide his eyes. In his Baroque Works Officer Agent uniform he wears a beige/tan military-style long coat with epaulettes and a high collar over a dark shirt marked with a large '5', a matching peaked cap with a '5' badge, light trousers and boots. His hair is short and dark, curled at the sides.

Symbols — Numeral '5' as personal sigil (cap, shirt, coat). Explosions and smoke as visual shorthand. Paired visually with Miss Valentine (Mikita) - tall/short, bomb/weight contrast. Baroque Works cross-bones symbol (skull with tilted hat) appears on their transport. In Little Garden, his bombs are drawn with classic manga 'BOM!!' effects and soot-covered victims. Accounts I
Debut: Baroque Works Moves - Whisky Peak AftermathOne Piece manga Ch. 110 'Willpower' - Ch. 112 'Little Garden'

1999-2000 (publication); in-universe 1522 Age of the Sea

After the Straw Hats defeat the bounty hunters of Whisky Peak and learn that their 'benefactors' were actually Baroque Works Frontier Agents, Baroque Works leadership dispatches Officer Agents to eliminate the witnesses: Princess Vivi (Miss Wednesday), her guardian Igaram, and the Straw Hats. Mr. 5 and his partner Miss Valentine (Mikita, the Kilo Kilo no Mi user) are introduced via Den Den Mushi receiving orders from Mr. 0's partner Miss All Sunday. They are shown flying over the sea on Miss Valentine's umbrella, heading for Little Garden where Vivi's group is expected. This establishes Baroque Works' hierarchical response and Mr. 5's role as a low-tier but superhuman enforcer. Sets up Little Garden as a trap; demonstrates that Baroque Works knows Vivi's identity and will pursue her across the Grand Line. Establishes Mr. 5/Miss Valentine as first Officer Agents the Straw Hats face directly.

Little Garden: Arrival and ProvocationOne Piece manga Ch. 112-117

2000

Mr. 5 and Miss Valentine arrive on Little Garden, a prehistoric island where giants Dorry and Brogy have dueled for 100 years. They encounter Mr. 3 (Galdino) and Miss Goldenweek (Marianne) who are already executing a plan to kill the giants and capture the Straw Hats. Mr. 5 arrogantly interrupts, declaring his Bomb-Bomb power superior, and demonstrates by flicking an explosive booger at a giant dinosaur, obliterating it. He and Miss Valentine then ambush Vivi, Karoo (her duck), and Usopp, while Mr. 3 deals with Luffy and Zoro. Mr. 5 captures Vivi and uses her as bait, mocking Usopp's cowardice. Reveals Mr. 5's personality (contempt for weaklings) and power mechanics to reader; raises stakes by showing Baroque Works can coordinate multiple Officer Agent pairs. Vivi's capture forces Straw Hats to split.

Little Garden: Bomb vs. Bravery - Usopp and Karoo's CounterattackOne Piece manga Ch. 118-126

2000

Mr. 5 confronts Usopp, Vivi, and Karoo in the jungle. He repeatedly flicks explosive nose-mucus and exhales a massive 'Breeze Breath Bomb' that levels trees, while Miss Valentine floats above and drops her 10,000kg press on foes. Usopp, despite terror, uses his ingenuity: he uses Karoo's speed, his own Usopp Hammer (a fake giant hammer that is actually two frying pans), and ultimately his trademark lying/strategy to outwit them. Mr. 5 underestimates Usopp, allowing Usopp to hit him with a surprise attack. Meanwhile Luffy and Zoro, freed from Mr. 3's wax, intervene. Mr. 5 attempts to detonate his entire body against Luffy, but Luffy is immune to blunt explosive force due to rubber body and simply swallows the explosion? Actually Luffy inhales? In manga Luffy takes explosion head-on and is unharmed, then counters. Usopp's first real solo victory as a brave warrior, crucial character growth; Vivi sees Straw Hat loyalty. Mr. 5's defeat proves Devil Fruit alone does not guarantee victory and foreshadows later theme that willpower/Haki matters more than fruit.

Accounts II
Defeat and Humiliation on Little GardenOne Piece manga Ch. 126-129

2000

After Usopp and Vivi outmaneuver Miss Valentine, Luffy and Zoro arrive. Mr. 5 tries his ultimate move - making his whole body explode upon punching Luffy - but Luffy's Gum-Gum body absorbs the blast. Luffy then delivers a Gum-Gum Bazooka that sends Mr. 5 flying, while Zoro knocks out Miss Valentine. They are left unconscious and soot-covered as the Straw Hats reunite, free Dorry and Brogy from Mr. 3's wax, and escape Little Garden as the giants' duel resumes and the island's volcano erupts. Mr. 5 and Miss Valentine are last seen unconscious in the jungle, later recovered by Mr. 3's group. Officer Agents Mr. 5 and Miss Valentine are the first Baroque Works officers definitively defeated, establishing Straw Hat combat credibility before Alabasta. Their failure forces Crocodile to send higher-ranked agents (Mr. 2, Mr. 1). Mr. 5 is not seen again in the main Alabasta battle.

Miss Goldenweek's Operation: Meet Baroque Works - Aftermath and New Baroque WorksOne Piece manga Cover Story Ch. 359-413 (inter-chapter cover serial)

2001-2002 (publication); in-universe shortly after Alabasta's fall (Ch. 212)

After Baroque Works' collapse and Crocodile's arrest by the Marines, most Officer Agents are imprisoned. Miss Goldenweek (Marianne) uses her Colors Trap art to free Mr. 5, Miss Valentine, and Mr. 3 from the Marine prison ship, but they choose not to rescue Crocodile. Instead, the group - now including Mr. 5, Miss Valentine, Miss Goldenweek, Mr. 3, and later Mr. 2 Bon Kurei's absence noted - travels to an abandoned holiday island, reopens the Spiders Cafe, and starts a new, peaceful life. Mr. 5 is shown working at the cafe, fishing, and living communally, no longer as an assassin. The final cover (Ch. 413) shows them as 'New Baroque Works' running a cafe, with Mr. 5 in casual clothes serving customers, indicating rehabilitation rather than continued villainy. Provides canonical post-defeat fate for Mr. 5, rare for minor villains in One Piece. Shows Oda's pattern of redeeming low-level antagonists via cover stories. Confirms Mr. 5 survived and left crime. No further main-story appearance as of Wano/Egghead arcs.

Legacy Cameo: Baroque Works Reunion HintsOne Piece manga Ch. 631 (cover), Vivre Card, and author SBS

2011-present

Mr. 5 makes no direct main-story return after the timeskip, but Oda periodically references Baroque Works veterans. In cover requests and Vivre Card updates, Mr. 5 is listed as affiliated with New Spiders Cafe, not with Cross Guild (Crocodile's new organization). This distinguishes him from Crocodile, Daz Bonez (Mr. 1), and Galdino (Mr. 3) who reappear in later arcs. His absence from Impel Down (where Mr. 1, Mr. 2, Mr. 3 and Crocodile were imprisoned) and from Marineford confirms he escaped earlier via Miss Goldenweek. His legacy is primarily as the archetypal 'weak Officer Agent' used to measure Straw Hat growth. Clarifies continuity: not all Baroque Works members followed Crocodile into new crimes. Mr. 5's story ends in quiet retirement, a contrast to Crocodile's return as a major player.

Mr. 5 is a Logia and therefore intangible like Crocodile or Ace. — He is Paramecia. He cannot turn into explosion to dodge attacks; he can only create explosions. Luffy and Zoro hit him normally, proving tangibility.

Mr. 5 is the weakest Baroque Works agent. — He is second-weakest Officer Agent (rank 5), above Mr. 6 and all Frontier Agents/Millions/Billions, but below Mr. 4, Mr. 3, Mr. 2, Mr. 1, and Mr. 0. Within Officer Agents he is weak, but still superhuman compared to normal soldiers.

He died on Little Garden or remained in prison forever. — He survived, was freed by Miss Goldenweek's Colors Trap in the cover story (Ch. 359-413), and now runs the Spiders Cafe with other reformed agents. He is alive and retired from crime as of latest canon.

His bombs can destroy anything and should have killed Luffy. — His explosions are relatively weak by Grand Line standards - enough to destroy rock and normal humans, but Luffy's rubber body is immune to blunt explosive force and Zoro's durability is far higher. Oda deliberately shows Devil Fruit power alone is insufficient without strength/Haki.

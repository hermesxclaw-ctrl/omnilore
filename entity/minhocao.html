Who It Is
Form

The Minhocao is described as a large, worm-like creature that tunnels through the earth. It is said to measure between 20 to 50 meters in length, and is often depicted as having a fish-like head.

Powers

Burrowing The Minhocao is said to be able to burrow through the earth with incredible speed and agility, creating complex networks of tunnels and caverns.

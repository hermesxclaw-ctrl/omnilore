Who It Is

Before he was a herald of Galactus, Gabriel Lan was a captain of the Nova Corps, an explorer who charted the unknown reaches of the cosmos. When his ship was destroyed by the planet-eater's sentinel, he was offered a second chance as a herald, only to be consumed by the very fire that gave him power. His fate echoes through the stars as a warning and a mystery, his legacy carried by a being who bears his name but not his soul.

Gabriel Lan was a noble and courageous explorer, dedicated to his duty as a Nova Corps officer. As a herald, he was loyal to Galactus but retained his sense of justice. The android replica is more robotic and lacks the original's personality, acting solely on its programmed directives.

Powers
Power Cosmic

As a herald of Galactus, Air-Walker was granted a fraction of Galactus's cosmic power, granting him superhuman strength, speed, durability, flight, and the ability to manipulate energy for offensive and defensive purposes.

Limit: The Power Cosmic is finite and can be depleted. Air-Walker's powers may be weaker than other heralds, as he was not as experienced.

Flight

Air-Walker can fly at incredible speeds, capable of interstellar travel.

Limit: None known.

Energy Projection

Air-Walker can project powerful energy blasts from his hands.

Limit: The android's energy projection may be limited by its power source.

Durability

Air-Walker is highly durable, able to withstand extreme temperatures, impacts, and cosmic radiation.

Limit: The android replica was destroyed in battle, suggesting its durability is not absolute.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Gabriel Lan as Air-Walker appears as a tall, humanoid figure with silver and blue skin, often depicted with a flowing cape and a helmet that covers his face. The android replica has a similar appearance but is more mechanical, with visible joints and a metallic sheen.

Symbols — Air-Walker is often associated with cosmic imagery, such as stars and nebulae, and the Power Cosmic, which manifests as blue and white energy.

Domains — Air-Walker operates in outer space, often traveling between planets and galaxies. He has no fixed home, serving as a herald for Galactus.

Accounts I
The Herald and the SentinelFantastic Four #120-121

1972

The Fantastic Four encounter Air-Walker, who is revealed to be an android created by Galactus to serve as his herald after the original Air-Walker died. The android attacks the FF, but they manage to defeat it. The android is destroyed, and Galactus later creates a new herald, the Silver Surfer.

Accounts II
Origin of Gabriel LanSilver Surfer (vol. 3) #4

1987

The origin of Gabriel Lan is revealed. He was a Nova Corps officer who encountered Galactus. Galactus transformed him into Air-Walker to serve as his herald. Air-Walker's first mission was to find planets for Galactus to consume, but he was killed by a sentinel that protected a world he was scouting. Galactus created an android replica of Air-Walker to continue his mission.

Return of Air-WalkerGalactus: The Devourer #1-6

1999-2000

The android Air-Walker returns, seeking to destroy Galactus. He is confronted by the Silver Surfer and other heroes, who ultimately destroy him. The android is destroyed, and Air-Walker's legacy is left as a cautionary tale.

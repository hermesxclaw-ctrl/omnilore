Etymology

The name 'Air-Walker' reflects his ability to travel through the air and space, and his role as a herald traversing the cosmos.

Cultural Ownership

Marvel Comics, a subsidiary of Marvel Entertainment / Disney.

Disputed Identifications

There is a distinction between the original Air-Walker (Gabriel Lan) and the android replica that later assumed the role. Some sources may conflate the two, but they are separate entities in the Marvel Universe.

Archive Dossier — Approach

Before he was a herald of Galactus, Gabriel Lan was a captain of the Nova Corps, an explorer who charted the unknown reaches of the cosmos. When his ship was destroyed by the planet-eater's sentinel, he was offered a second chance as a herald, only to be consumed by the very fire that gave him power. His fate echoes through the stars as a warning and a mystery, his legacy carried by a being who bears his name but not his soul.

I remember starlight. I remember home. The circuits remember too. But is that enough to make me real?

Who It Is

Air-Walker's story begins with Gabriel Lan, a noble Xandarian Nova Corps Centurion who was abducted by Galactus and transformed into a herald. Gifted with the Power Cosmic focused through atmospheric and gaseous manipulation, Air-Walker could control the elemental forces of air and sky — generating hurricane-force winds, altering planetary atmospheres, flying through space, and creating vacuum pockets to suffocate enemies. Lan served faithfully but was slain by the Ovoid race during his search for sustenance worlds. In an act of respect, Galactus recreated Air-Walker as a sentient android duplicate, programming it with Lan's memories and personality. This android Air-Walker continued to serve, and was later controlled by the criminal mastermind the Red Ghost for a time before being destroyed in a battle with the Silver Surfer during the Trial of Galactus. The android was later rebuilt by Galactus and served as a guardian of his homeworld. Air-Walker's legacy is one of transformation — from a living being of honor to a mechanical echo of that honor, raising questions about identity, consciousness, and the nature of the soul. He represents the tragedy of herald service: a good being pressed into a terrible role, then preserved imperfectly in artificial form.

Archive Dossier — Description

Appearance

Gabriel Lan as Air-Walker appears as a tall, humanoid figure with silver and blue skin, often depicted with a flowing cape and a helmet that covers his face. The android replica has a similar appearance but is more mechanical, with visible joints and a metallic sheen.

Symbols & Iconography

Air-Walker is often associated with cosmic imagery, such as stars and nebulae, and the Power Cosmic, which manifests as blue and white energy.

Behaviour & Personality

Gabriel Lan was a noble and courageous explorer, dedicated to his duty as a Nova Corps officer. As a herald, he was loyal to Galactus but retained his sense of justice. The android replica is more robotic and lacks the original's personality, acting solely on its programmed directives.

Domains & Habitats

Air-Walker operates in outer space, often traveling between planets and galaxies. He has no fixed home, serving as a herald for Galactus.

Version Differences

The original Air-Walker was a living being with free will, while the android is a constructed replica with limited autonomy. The android also has different powers, such as the ability to project energy blasts from his hands, whereas the original primarily used the Power Cosmic for flight and energy manipulation.

Archive Dossier — Powers

Power Cosmic

Source Version Original Gabriel Lan

Description

As a herald of Galactus, Air-Walker was granted a fraction of Galactus's cosmic power, granting him superhuman strength, speed, durability, flight, and the ability to manipulate energy for offensive and defensive purposes.

Limitations

The Power Cosmic is finite and can be depleted. Air-Walker's powers may be weaker than other heralds, as he was not as experienced.

Contradictions

Some sources suggest that the android replica also possesses the Power Cosmic, but it is unclear if it is the same level as the original.

Flight

Source Version Both versions

Description

Air-Walker can fly at incredible speeds, capable of interstellar travel.

Limitations

None known.

Contradictions

None.

Energy Projection

Source Version Android replica

Description

Air-Walker can project powerful energy blasts from his hands.

Limitations

The android's energy projection may be limited by its power source.

Contradictions

The original Air-Walker was not known to use energy projection, relying more on his cosmic power for other effects.

Durability

Source Version Both versions

Description

Air-Walker is highly durable, able to withstand extreme temperatures, impacts, and cosmic radiation.

Limitations

The android replica was destroyed in battle, suggesting its durability is not absolute.

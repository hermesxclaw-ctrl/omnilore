Who It Is
Form

The Nommo are described as amphibious, hermaphroditic, fish-like creatures with humanoid upper torsos, legs/feet, and a fish-like lower torso and tail.

Powers

Masters of Speech The Nommo are said to have the power of speech and are associated with the creation of language.

Accounts

The Nommo descended from the sky along the Milky Way, embodying both order and chaos.

The Nommo taught the Dogon people about the creation of the world and the importance of maintaining balance and order.

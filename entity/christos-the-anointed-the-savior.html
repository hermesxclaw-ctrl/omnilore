Who It Is
Form

Christos is often depicted as a bearded man in a white robe, with a crown of thorns or a cross

Powers

Divine authority Christos is believed to possess divine authority and power, which he uses to teach, heal, and redeem humanity

Accounts

The story of Christos' birth, as told in the Gospel of Matthew and the Gospel of Luke, describes the miraculous conception and birth of Christos, who is born of a virgin and is the son of God

The story of Christos' passion, as told in the Gospel of Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John, describes the events leading up to Christos' crucifixion, including his betrayal, arrest, trial, and execution

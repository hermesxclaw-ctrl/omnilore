Christos (Χριστός, 'the Anointed') is an aeon uniquely generated not from a single syzygy but as the 'common fruit' of the entire Pleroma — the collective will of all aeons to restore the harmony disrupted by Sophia's transgression. Christos is the 'Boundary-Mediator' who operates across the Horos (limit) that separates the Pleroma from the kenoma: within the Pleroma he teaches the aeons the true nature of the Father (restoring them from their fear of his immensity); outside it, he descends through the cosmic spheres to illumine the Lower Sophia (Achamoth), to dethrone the Demiurge's claim to be the sole god, and to awaken the spiritual seed imprisoned in material bodies.

Who It Is

Christos (Χριστός, 'the Anointed') is an aeon uniquely generated not from a single syzygy but as the 'common fruit' of the entire Pleroma — the collective will of all aeons to restore the harmony disrupted by Sophia's transgression. Christos is the 'Boundary-Mediator' who operates across the Horos (limit) that separates the Pleroma from the kenoma: within the Pleroma he teaches the aeons the true nature of the Father (restoring them from their fear of his immensity); outside it, he descends through the cosmic spheres to illumine the Lower Sophia (Achamoth), to dethrone the Demiurge's claim to be the sole god, and to awaken the spiritual seed imprisoned in material bodies. In Valentinian Christology, Christos is distinct from Jesus (the earthly manifestation): the Savior Christos 'puts on' Jesus as a garment; Jesus's suffering on the cross is docetic — the Savior (as an aeon) cannot truly suffer, and Jesus's body is a phantasma that serves as a vehicle for revelation. Christos thus embodies the restorative, revelatory, and atoning function that reverses Sophia's fall and restores the Pleroma to harmony.

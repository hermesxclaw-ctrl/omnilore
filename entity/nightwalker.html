Who It Is
Form

The Nightwalker is often described as a tall, imposing figure with long legs and a pale complexion. Its appearance can vary depending on the cultural context, with some depictions showing it as a humanoid figure with fangs and claws, while others describe it as a more ghostly or spectral entity.

Powers

Shadow Travel The Nightwalker has the ability to travel through shadows, allowing it to move quickly and undetected across vast distances.

Accounts

A young woman named Emiko is said to have encountered a Nightwalker in the forest while searching for her missing sister. The creature is described as a tall, imposing figure with long legs and a pale complexion, and is said to have spoken to Emiko in a voice that was both gentle and terrifying.

A group of travelers is said to have encountered a Rakshasa, a type of Nightwalker, in the mountains while on a journey to the holy city of Varanasi. The creature is described as a powerful and malevolent spirit, and is said to have attacked the travelers with its sharp claws and teeth.

Who It Is

A towering, 30-foot-tall humanoid figure of pure darkness, with eyes like dying stars. Nightwalkers are the most powerful of the nightshades — undead creatures from the Negative Energy Plane. They radiate an aura of cold and despair, and their touch drains life energy.

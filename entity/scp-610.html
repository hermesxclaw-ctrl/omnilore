SCP-610

The Flesh that Hates

Who It Is
Form

SCP-610 appears as a mass of twisted, pulsing flesh with no discernible shape or form. It is capable of changing its appearance at will, making it difficult to describe or predict its behavior. The flesh is said to be highly resistant to damage and can regenerate itself quickly.

Powers

Regeneration SCP-610 is capable of regenerating itself quickly, making it highly resistant to damage and destruction.

Incident Log

A group of researchers attempted to study SCP-610, but it quickly became apparent that the entity was far more intelligent and malevolent than they had anticipated. The researchers were eventually forced to abandon their investigation and flee for their lives.

A group of researchers attempted to communicate with SCP-610, but it quickly became apparent that the entity was not interested in cooperation. Instead, it launched a vicious attack on the researchers, forcing them to flee for their lives.

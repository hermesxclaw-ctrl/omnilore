The Flesh that Hates

Who It Is

SCP-610 is a highly contagious, aggressive disease that causes extreme mutations in organic tissue, transforming living organisms into grotesque masses of writhing flesh. The infection was first discovered by the Foundation in a remote region of Siberia after satellite imagery revealed the complete disappearance of several small villages. Ground teams found the villages consumed by enormous organic growths — the inhabitants had been transformed beyond recognition into pulsating walls of flesh, with occasional recognizable human features (eyes, mouths, fingers) appearing randomly across surfaces. SCP-610 spreads through direct physical contact with infected tissue, with symptoms manifesting within hours. Early stages include skin lesions that spontaneously form mouths, eyes, or other orifices. As the infection progresses, the victim's body reshapes itself, bones softening and reforming, organs multiplying or relocating, until the original organism is completely subsumed into the larger biomass. Infected organisms exhibit a hive-mind behavior, working collectively to spread the infection. The 'Flesh' — the collective term for SCP-610 biomass — resembles raw, twitching meat. It appears to be intelligent, capable of producing specialized forms for specific purposes, including ambulatory creatures designed to seek out and infect new hosts. The Foundation maintains a strict quarantine zone around the affected area, enforcing a no-fly zone and perimeter defense. Incineration is the only known method of halting the infection's spread. Exploration teams entering the quarantine zone report that the Flesh reacts to their presence, forming human faces that silently watch them pass, and in some cases, the faces speak — usually fragments of the victims' last conscious thoughts. SCP-610 is one of the Foundation's most prominent examples of body horror, drawing on deep human fears about disease, loss of self, and the horror of being consumed and integrated into something alien.

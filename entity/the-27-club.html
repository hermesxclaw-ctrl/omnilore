The 'club' crystallized culturally after Kurt Cobain's 1994 suicide, with the recognition that four of the most iconic 1960s rock stars had also died at that exact age within a two-year window.

Who It Is

The statistically improbable cluster of influential musicians who died at age 27, creating a modern myth of a 'curse' or cosmic pattern. The 'club' crystallized culturally after Kurt Cobain's 1994 suicide, with the recognition that four of the most iconic 1960s rock stars had also died at that exact age within a two-year window.

Cultural Significance

The 27 Club myth reflects the Romantic trope of the doomed artist — burning bright and dying young, eternalized at the peak of their beauty and talent. It has been referenced in songs, films, and academic papers. The phenomenon has been studied as a case study in apophenia (finding patterns in random data).

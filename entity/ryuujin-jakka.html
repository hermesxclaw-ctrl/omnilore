Who It Is

The Zanpakutō spirit of Genryūsai Shigekuni Yamamoto, the Captain-Commander of the Gotei 13. Ryūjin Jakka manifests as a towering, ancient warrior wreathed in eternal flames — the oldest and most powerful fire-type Zanpakutō, with 15 million-degree heat at its core. It represents the sun itself and is considered the single most destructive Zanpakutō in existence.

Powers

Shikai: Flame blade that burns anything to ash

Bankai: Zanka no Tachi (Blade of Ember)

Zanka no Tachi, East: Bajō no Gōka (trillion-degree edge)

Zanka no Tachi, West: Zanjitsu Gokui (armor of condensed heat)

Zanka no Tachi, South: Kaka Jūmanokushi Daisōjin (revived corpse army)

Zanka no Tachi, North: Tenchi Kaijin (ultimate slash)

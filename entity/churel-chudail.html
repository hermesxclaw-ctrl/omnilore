Form

The Churel is often described as an ugly and hideous woman, with a grotesque appearance that is said to be terrifying to behold. However, she is also said to have the ability to shape-shift and disguise herself as a beautiful woman, in order to lure men into the woods or mountains where she can kill them or suck up their life-force or virility.

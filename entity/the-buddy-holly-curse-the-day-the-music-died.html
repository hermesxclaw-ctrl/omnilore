Cultural Significance

'American Pie' is itself a myth-making engine — its cryptic lyrics ('the jester sang for the king and queen,' 'Satan laughing with delight') created a second layer of mythology around the crash. The event has become shorthand for the end of an era, referenced whenever young artists die prematurely.

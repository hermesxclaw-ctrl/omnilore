Who It Is
Form

Sraosha is often depicted as a divine being with a gentle and benevolent appearance, listening to the divine word.

Powers

Divine Hearing The ability to listen to and understand the divine word.

Accounts

Sraosha is tasked with listening to the divine word and relaying it to humanity.

Sraosha is identified with the Archangel Gabriel, who serves as a messenger of Allah.

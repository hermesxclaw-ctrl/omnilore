Who It Is

She can shatter a mountainside with a fingertip, heal a fatal wound with a palm, and will still bet your entire mission fund on a single dice roll — Tsunade Senju is the Fifth Hokage who turned grief into a gambling problem, a blood phobia into medical genius, and a cursed necklace into a promise that finally stopped killing the people she loved.

Outwardly brash, short-tempered, lazy, hedonistic and unapologetically adult: heavy drinker, compulsive gambler infamous as 'The Legendary Sucker' (Densetsu no Kamo) because she always loses, blunt-spoken, physically intimidating, and willing to strike subordinates (Jiraiya, Naruto) for disrespect. Underneath, deeply compassionate, idealistic and guilt-ridden. Core trauma: loss of younger brother Nawaki (age 12) and lover Dan Kato during Second Shinobi World War, which triggered hemophobia and abandonment of Konoha. Name & Tongue

Etymology — Given name Tsunade (綱手) literally 'mooring rope' / 'hawser' taken directly from the heroine of the 19th-century Japanese folktale Jiraiya Goketsu Monogatari (The Tale of the Gallant Jiraiya), where Tsunade is a master of slug magic. Kishimoto deliberately reused the Jiraiya-Tsunade-Orochimaru triad

Pronunciation — Japanese: tsu-na-de sen-ju [tsɨnade sendʑɯː] ; English dub: tsoo-NAH-day SEN-joo. Stress on second syllable in Japanese, anglicized with equal stress.

Aliases — Tsunade, Godaime Hokage (Fifth Hokage), Densetsu no Sannin (Legendary Sannin), Namekuji-hime (Slug Princess), Tsunade-hime

Powers
Superhuman Strength (Kairiki - Chakra-Enhanced Strength)

Precise chakra control to gather and release massive chakra in fists/feet at moment of impact, allowing her to crater ground, shatter walls, split earth, launch Gamabunta's dagger, and send opponents flying hundreds of meters. Signature feat: cracking Madara Uchiha's Susanoo ribcage in War arc. Taught to Sakura Haruno.

Limit: Requires exquisite chakra control and concentration; ineffective if chakra network disrupted or seal depleted. Causes severe recoil if mis-timed. Not passive super-strength — must actively mold chakra each strike.

Medical Ninjutsu - Mystical Palm Technique (Shosen Jutsu) and Systemic Healing

Master medical-nin, greatest in Konoha history. Can heal fatal organ damage, regrow tissue, detoxify poisons, perform battlefield surgery without tools, and mass-heal via Katsuyu. Created Konoha's medical-nin system rule: one medical-nin per four-man cell. Can diagnose by sight and chakra pulse.

Limit: Healing consumes vast chakra and stamina; cannot resurrect dead; severe injuries may require prolonged treatment; hemophobia originally blocked her ability to perform when seeing blood (overcome in Chapter 169).

Strength of a Hundred Seal (Byakugo no In) / Ninja Art Creation Rebirth (Sozo Saisei)

Years of storing chakra at a point on forehead forming violet diamond. Release allows instantaneous regeneration without hand seals by accelerating cell division (Sozo Saisei), healing any wound including impalement and bisection for a time. Strength of a Hundred Technique (Byakugo no Jutsu) is the full release extending seal markings over body, granting continuous regeneration during combat and powering Katsuyu remote healing.

Limit: Shortens lifespan by accelerating telomere loss; finite stored chakra — once depleted, seal vanishes and rapid aging/healing stops. Cannot regrow lost limbs instantly; Madara bisecting her left her bisected for hours until healed by Karin-assisted recovery. Requires years to recharge.

Summoning Technique: Katsuyu

Summons Katsuyu, colossal slug queen of Shikkotsu Forest. Katsuyu can split into thousands of smaller clones, spit acid (Zesshi Nensan), tank massive damage, and act as remote healing network: Tsunade channels chakra through Katsuyu clones attached to injured to heal entire village simultaneously (Pain arc). Size scales with chakra offered; 5% summon still building-sized.

Limit: Requires blood contract and chakra; Katsuyu's acid ineffective against high-level Susanoo; remote healing drains Byakugo reserve rapidly, leaving Tsunade comatose after Pain's attack.

Chakra Control, Genjutsu Resistance and Combat Intellect

Perfect chakra control (rated 5/5) allows walking on water, tree climbing, and countering genjutsu by disrupting chakra. High intelligence (5/5) in medical and tactical domains: quickly deduced Pain's abilities, orchestrated village evacuation, and identified Madara's wood clone feint. Taijutsu mastery with evasive medical-nin fighting style (keep distance, strike decisively).

Limit: Genjutsu rating 3.5 — not a genjutsu specialist; speed 3.5 — slower than elite speedsters (A, Minato) and relies on anticipation rather than raw speed; intelligence is medical/tactical, not strategic like Shikamaru.

Transformation Technique (Youth Preservation)

Constant low-level transformation maintaining youthful appearance; not standard henge but chakra-based anti-aging that conceals true age. Can be dispelled voluntarily or fails when chakra exhausted.

Limit: Cosmetic only; does not restore youth or stamina; drains small but constant chakra; dispelling reveals true age and does not affect combat power except psychological.

Accounts I
The Senju Princess and the Sannin - Childhood and Hiruzen's TeamNaruto manga Chapters 122-123, 369-370; Databook Rin no Sho; Jiraiya Goketsu Monogatari folklore basis

Founding era lineage (childhood ~ 30-40 years before Part I), Second Hokage era; publication 2002-2008

Born as granddaughter of First Hokage Hashirama Senju and Mito Uzumaki, and grand-niece of Second Hokage Tobirama Senju, Tsunade inherits Senju vitality and Uzumaki life-force. As child she is doted on by Hashirama, who gifts her his green crystal necklace and teaches her gambling. Placed on Team Hiruzen under Third Hokage Hiruzen Sarutobi alongside Jiraiya and Orochimaru — the team that will become the Legendary Sannin. Early missions show prodigious chakra control and temper. The three are later named 'Densetsu no Sannin' by Hanzo of the Salamander after surviving battle in Second Shinobi World War, cementing her legend before age 30. Establishes Tsunade's royal lineage and burden of legacy; Sannin title gives her international fame and later Hokage legitimacy; necklace becomes cursed heirloom motif.

Loss of Nawaki and Dan - The Origin of HemophobiaNaruto manga Chapters 148-160; Anime Episodes 85-86

Second Shinobi World War, ~ 25 years before Part I; published 2003

At age ~14, Tsunade's 12-year-old brother Nawaki dies on his first war mission after being caught in an explosion — she fails to heal him, his body irretrievable. She gifts him Hashirama's necklace for his dream of becoming Hokage. Years later, her lover Dan Kato, who shares Nawaki's dream of protecting loved ones and reforming medical policy, is fatally wounded; Tsunade cannot stop his bleeding despite her skills, and he dies in her arms as she embraces his blood-soaked body. The double trauma induces severe hemophobia — fainting and paralysis at sight of blood — rendering the world's greatest healer unable to heal, and she abandons Konoha, wandering as a gambling medic with attendant Shizune (Dan's niece). Explains Tsunade's cynicism, fear of Hokage title as death sentence, and refusal to return; sets up need for Naruto to break her fatalism; necklace becomes symbol of death until Naruto breaks curse.

Search for Tsunade Arc - The Fifth Hokage GambleNaruto manga Chapters 148-181; Anime Episodes 83-96

Part I, shortly after Third Hokage's death and Orochimaru's invasion; publication 2003-2004

After Hiruzen's death, Jiraiya and Naruto seek Tsunade to become Fifth Hokage while Orochimaru and Kabuto Yakushi seek her to heal Orochimaru's arms sealed by Hiruzen. Orochimaru offers to resurrect Nawaki and Dan via Edo Tensei in exchange. Tsunade initially considers betraying Konoha, haunted by loss, and meets Naruto, who reminds her of Nawaki and Dan with his identical dream and his bet that he will master Rasengan in a week. She wagers her necklace against his success, fully expecting to win. When Naruto succeeds and is wounded protecting her from Kabuto, she overcomes hemophobia by sheer will to heal him, unleashing Creation Rebirth and summoning Katsuyu to defeat Orochimaru and Kabuto alongside Jiraiya and Naruto. She accepts Hokage mantle and gifts Naruto the necklace, declaring he will be Hokage. Tsunade becomes Godaime Hokage, heals Sasuke and Kakashi from Tsukuyomi, reforms village medical system; Naruto's necklace survives as proof curse is broken; Orochimaru remains crippled, driving his later body-hopping.

Accounts II
As Hokage - Sasuke Retrieval, Chunin Exams and Akatsuki ShadowsNaruto manga Chapters 182-238, 245-281; Anime Episodes 96-135, Shippuden 1-70

Part I epilogue and Part II early (3-year timeskip); publication 2004-2006

As Hokage, Tsunade heals Lee's spine injury enabling his return to ninja, heals Sasuke and Kakashi, and dispatches Sasuke Retrieval Team (ultimately fails as Sasuke defects to Orochimaru). Two-and-a-half years later, she mentors Sakura as apprentice, teaching superhuman strength and medical ninjutsu, and assigns Team Kakashi under Yamato. She manages crises: Deidara's infiltration, Akatsuki capturing Gaara, and intelligence that Akatsuki hunts jinchuriki. She authorizes Naruto's wind training, defends his status as jinchuriki against elders, and sends Asuma's team against Hidan/Kakuzu (Asuma dies). Her leadership balances elders Homura/Koharu, Danzo's scheming, and Jiraiya's intelligence missions. Sakura becomes elite medical-nin; Konoha's medical corps modernized; Tsunade's protective stance toward Naruto cements their surrogate-family bond; Jiraiya's final infiltration of Amegakure is authorized by her, leading to his death.

Pain's Assault - The Hokage's SacrificeNaruto manga Chapters 413-450; Shippuden Episodes 152-175

Part II, Pain Invasion arc; publication 2008-2009

Pain (Nagato) invades Konoha seeking Naruto. Tsunade orders evacuation, summons Katsuyu and channels her entire Byakugo reserve through thousands of Katsuyu clones to shield and heal every villager simultaneously as Pain's Shinra Tensei obliterates the village. She confronts Pain directly, declaring Konoha's will, but exhausts her chakra to coma. Naruto returns, defeats Pain, and Nagato resurrects those killed. Tsunade falls into coma for months, during which Danzo briefly becomes Sixth Hokage candidate. She awakens after the Five Kage Summit, reclaiming title before the War. Village saved from mass casualties due to her mass healing; demonstrates Hokage's self-sacrifice; her coma enables Danzo's political coup attempt; Naruto's heroism vindicates her faith; she later advocates for Naruto as hero.

Fourth Shinobi World War - Against Madara and the Ten-TailsNaruto manga Chapters 515-608, 615-662; Shippuden Episodes 322-348, 373-391

Part II War arc; publication 2011-2014

As leader of the Medical Division and one of Five Kage, Tsunade argues for protecting Naruto and Killer B but is overruled. She fights alongside A (Fourth Raikage), Onoki, Gaara and Mei against Madara Uchiha after Madara's Edo Tensei resurrection. She unleashes full Strength of a Hundred Technique, punching through Susanoo and healing Kage continuously, but Madara impales and bisects her, leaving her lower body severed. Katsuyu preserves her and the other Kage until Orochimaru and Karin arrive — Karin allows Tsunade to bite and heal via Uzumaki vitality, reattaching her body. She then summons Katsuyu to heal the entire Allied army alongside Sakura, and later helps seal the Ten-Tails and supports Naruto/Sasuke vs Kaguya. After war, she retires, passing Hokage to Kakashi. Survives bisection, proving Byakugo limits but not immortality; cements Sakura as successor; retires voluntarily, ending Senju Hokage lineage; village transitions to Kakashi and then Naruto.

Retirement and Boruto Era - The Legendary Sannin's LegacyNaruto manga Chapter 700 epilogue; Boruto manga/anime Episodes 18, 72, 115; Naruto: The Seventh Hokage and the Scarlet Spring

Post-War, ~15 years later; publication 2014-present

Retired in her late 60s, Tsunade lives outside Konoha, occasionally returning for ceremonies, gambling, and to advise. She attends Naruto's Hokage inauguration, meets his children Boruto and Himawari, and is consulted on medical and diplomatic matters. In Boruto anime, she helps investigate Hashirama Cell misuse and attends hot-spring and memorial episodes, showing mellowed but still fiery personality. She remains unmarried, childless, but acts as grandmother figure to Naruto's family and mentor emerita to Sakura, now head of medical. Her reforms — medical-nin per squad, Konoha Hospital system, and Byakugo taught to Sakura — persist as institutional legacy. Senju clan effectively ends as political entity but legacy continues through Sakura and medical system; Tsunade's survival provides rare happy ending for Sannin (Jiraiya dead, Orochimaru redeemed).

Tsunade is immortal or cannot be killed because of regeneration — Byakugo accelerates cell division at cost of lifespan and finite chakra; she can be killed if chakra runs out, decapitated, or damage exceeds regeneration. She was bisected and nearly died, saved only by Katsuyu and Karin's healing. Databook explicitly notes lifespan shortening.

Tsunade is just fan-service with no real strength or leadership — Canonically strongest kunoichi of her generation (strength 5/5), Fifth Hokage who reformed medical system, mass-healed village, and fought Madara's Susanoo. Kishimoto intended both beauty and power; narrative centers her medical genius and Hokage sacrifice.

Tsunade's necklace kills anyone who wears it except Naruto — Necklace is said to bring death to those Tsunade gives it to (Nawaki, Dan) due to narrative curse/fatalism, not literal cursed object. Naruto breaks the pattern by surviving, symbolizing new generation. No supernatural curse confirmed; it's thematic.

Tsunade taught Sakura the Strength of a Hundred Seal instantly — Sakura trained for 3+ years under Tsunade and only awakened Byakugo during Fourth War (Chapter 632) after years of chakra storage, showing technique requires years of accumulation, not instant teaching.

Tsunade is Hashirama's daughter — She is his granddaughter (Hashirama -> unnamed Senju child -> Tsunade and Nawaki). Tobirama is her great-uncle. Manga Chapter 122 family tree and databook confirm.

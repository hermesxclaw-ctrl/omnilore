Who It Is

In the shadowy recesses of the SCP Foundation's containment files, SCP-029 lurks as a haunting enigma—a spectral figure whose origins are as elusive as its motives. Known only through fragmented reports and whispered rumors, this entity defies simple categorization, weaving a narrative that challenges our understanding of reality and the boundaries of human curiosity.

SCP-029 is reported to be highly elusive and unpredictable. It appears to avoid direct confrontation, instead engaging in psychological manipulation and stalking behaviors. It is believed to feed on fear and despair, though this is unconfirmed.

Powers
Psychological Manipulation

SCP-029 can induce hallucinations, paranoia, and intense fear in its victims, often exploiting their personal traumas and insecurities.

Limit: The extent of this ability is unknown; it may require proximity or a specific trigger.

Intangibility

SCP-029 is reported to be able to phase through solid objects, making it difficult to contain or harm.

Limit: It may be vulnerable to certain frequencies of light or sound, though this is unverified.

Reality Distortion

SCP-029 is believed to have the ability to subtly alter reality around it, causing small inconsistencies in the environment.

Limit: The extent and limits of this ability are unknown.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Descriptions of SCP-029 vary. Some accounts describe it as a humanoid figure composed of shadow and static, while others report it as a formless entity that manifests as a dark mist. In all cases, its appearance is reported to induce intense unease and psychological distress in observers.

Symbols — No specific symbols or iconography are associated with SCP-029 in available sources.

Domains — SCP-029 is believed to inhabit liminal spaces, such as abandoned buildings, empty hallways, and transitional zones. It has been reported in both urban and rural environments, but always in areas associated with isolation or neglect.

Accounts I
The Vanishing of Researcher ██████SCP Foundation internal documents (fictional)

Unknown

Researcher ██████ was assigned to study SCP-029. After several days of observation, they began exhibiting signs of severe psychological distress. One night, they vanished from their quarters, leaving behind only a journal filled with incoherent ramblings about 'the shadow in the static.' The researcher's disappearance remains unsolved, and the incident has been classified as a containment breach.

The Hallway IncidentSCP Foundation incident report (fictional)

Unknown

During a routine patrol, two guards reported seeing a dark figure at the end of a corridor. As they approached, the figure seemed to dissolve into the walls. Both guards experienced severe nightmares for weeks afterward. The guards were reassigned and given psychological counseling.

The Approach

In the shadowy recesses of the SCP Foundation's containment files, SCP-029 lurks as a haunting enigma—a spectral figure whose origins are as elusive as its motives. Known only through fragmented reports and whispered rumors, this entity defies simple categorization, weaving a narrative that challenges our understanding of reality and the boundaries of human curiosity.

Content advisory. Contains themes of psychological horror, body horror, and existential dread. Reader discretion is advised.
Identity

SCP-029

Pronunciation Pronounced as 'S-C-P zero-two-nine'.

Etymology

The designation 'SCP-029' follows the Foundation's naming convention: 'SCP' stands for 'Special Containment Procedures', and '029' is the sequential number assigned to the anomaly. The entity's true name is unknown.

Cultural Ownership

Unclassified. SCP-029 is a fictional entity created within the collaborative SCP Foundation community, which is a global online writing project. It does not belong to any single culture or tradition.

Disputed Identifications

There is no official consensus on the nature of SCP-029. Some sources suggest it may be a manifestation of collective unconscious fears, while others propose it is an extradimensional being. These theories remain speculative.

Description

Appearance

Descriptions of SCP-029 vary. Some accounts describe it as a humanoid figure composed of shadow and static, while others report it as a formless entity that manifests as a dark mist. In all cases, its appearance is reported to induce intense unease and psychological distress in observers.

Symbols & Iconography

No specific symbols or iconography are associated with SCP-029 in available sources.

Behaviour & Personality

SCP-029 is reported to be highly elusive and unpredictable. It appears to avoid direct confrontation, instead engaging in psychological manipulation and stalking behaviors. It is believed to feed on fear and despair, though this is unconfirmed.

Domains & Habitats

SCP-029 is believed to inhabit liminal spaces, such as abandoned buildings, empty hallways, and transitional zones. It has been reported in both urban and rural environments, but always in areas associated with isolation or neglect.

Version Differences

Since SCP-029 is a collaborative creation, multiple authors have contributed to its lore, leading to inconsistencies. Some versions depict it as a malevolent predator, while others portray it as a tragic figure bound to a cycle of suffering.

SCP-029 can induce hallucinations, paranoia, and intense fear in its victims, often exploiting their personal traumas and insecurities.

Limitations

The extent of this ability is unknown; it may require proximity or a specific trigger.

Contradictions

Some sources suggest it can affect multiple individuals simultaneously, while others indicate it only targets one person at a time.

The Vanishing of Researcher ██████

Source SCP Foundation internal documents (fictional)Culture SCP Foundation fictional universePeriod Unknown

Summary

Researcher ██████ was assigned to study SCP-029. After several days of observation, they began exhibiting signs of severe psychological distress. One night, they vanished from their quarters, leaving behind only a journal filled with incoherent ramblings about 'the shadow in the static.'

Participants

Researcher ██████

SCP-029

Consequences

The researcher's disappearance remains unsolved, and the incident has been classified as a containment breach.

Variants

Some accounts claim the researcher was found, but in a catatonic state.

Source SCP Foundation incident report (fictional)Culture SCP Foundation fictional universePeriod Unknown

Summary

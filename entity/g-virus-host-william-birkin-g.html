Who It Is

Dr. William Birkin, the creator of the G-Virus, who injected himself with the prototype after being shot by Umbrella operatives. The G-Virus causes radical mutation based on the host's desire to pass on its genetic material. G mutates through multiple stages, each more grotesque — from a single mutated arm (G1) to a hulking, multi-limbed behemoth (G3), to a crawling, face-merged horror (G4), and finally a massive, amorphous blob (G5). The G-Virus is unique in its ability to implant embryos into compatible hosts.

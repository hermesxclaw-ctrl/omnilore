Who It Is
Form

Tezcatlipoca is often depicted as a dark-skinned god with a black mirror in his hand, surrounded by stars and a crescent moon. He is also associated with the night sky and the cycles of time.

Powers

Divination Tezcatlipoca has the power to see into the future and to guide the actions of those who seek his wisdom.

Accounts

Tezcatlipoca is credited with creating the world and all living things. He is said to have used his Smoking Mirror to bring order and balance to the universe.

Tezcatlipoca is said to have played a key role in the creation of the fifth sun, which is associated with the current era of human history.

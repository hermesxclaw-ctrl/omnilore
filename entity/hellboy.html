Who It Is
Form

Hellboy is a red-haired, cigar-smoking, demon-like creature with a large right hand and a distinctive appearance. He is often depicted as a member of the Bureau for Paranormal Research and Defense (BPRD).

Powers

Superhuman Strength Hellboy possesses superhuman strength, which he often uses to fight against evil forces.

Regenerative Healing Hellboy has the ability to regenerate and heal from injuries, making him a formidable opponent in battle.

Accounts

Hellboy and his team investigate a series of supernatural events in the 1950s.

Hellboy and his team must stop an ancient war between humans and goblins.

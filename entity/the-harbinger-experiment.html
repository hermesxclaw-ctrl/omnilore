Who It Is

A scientific report detailing a 1983 experiment where researchers surgically removed all five senses from a volunteer (Harbinger-1) to isolate consciousness. On Day 4, EEG detected brain activity consistent with communication — Harbinger-1 was 'speaking' to something. On Day 10, communication ceased. On Day 15, the tank was opened. Harbinger-1 was dead, with 'IT SPEAKS IN THE SILENCE' carved into the interior wall — carved from the inside, by fingernails worn to bone.

Form

Presented as a declassified research document. The laboratory is described clinically. The horror comes through clinical descriptions of Harbinger-1's decline and the final, impossible message.

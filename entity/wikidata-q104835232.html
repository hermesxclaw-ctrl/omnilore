Who It Is

In the vibrant spiritual landscape of Brazil, where African, Indigenous, and European traditions intertwine, the Caboclo Pena Branca stands as a figure of profound cultural synthesis—a divine archetype whose white feather symbolizes purity, wisdom, and the enduring legacy of the Americas' first peoples.

Caboclos in general are considered wise, strong, and connected to nature. They are often called upon for healing, guidance, and protection. Pena Branca, as a name, suggests an emphasis on peace and spiritual clarity.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Caboclo: A term of Tupi origin, meaning 'from the wilderness' or 'of the forest,' used in Brazil to refer to people of mixed Indigenous and European ancestry, and in Afro-Brazilian religions to denote a class of spirits representing Indigenous ancestors.

Who It Is

A VHS recording of an NBA basketball game between the Boston Celtics and an unknown team. Each time the tape is played, the people in the recording display increasing awareness that they are being watched — and that they are trapped in a time loop. By the 17th recorded viewing, the players and crowd are screaming, crying, and attempting to escape the gymnasium, knowing they will be reset when the recording ends.

Form

A standard VHS cassette labeled 'Celtics vs. [illegible] — Season Opener.' The footage appears completely normal during the first playback.

Powers

Sentient recording

Temporal loop awareness

Information transfer between playbacks

Psychological torture of recorded individuals

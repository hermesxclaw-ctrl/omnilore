Who It Is
Form

SCP-1733 is a digital recording of the 2010-2011 NBA season opening game played at the TD Garden in Boston, Massachusetts on 10/26/2010 between the Boston Celtics and Miami Heat. The recording is a 48-minute video file that captures the game's events, including the pre-game warm-ups, the game itself, and the post-game interviews.

Powers

Historical Record SCP-1733 has the power to record and preserve historical events, allowing it to serve as a valuable resource for researchers and historians.

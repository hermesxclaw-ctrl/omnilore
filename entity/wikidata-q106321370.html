Aglaonoe

Pronunciation The most likely Ancient Greek pronunciation is /aɡlaˈo.no.e/, with stress on the second syllable: ah-glah-OH-neh. In English, it is often rendered as /æɡləˈoʊniː/ (ag-lah-OH-nee).

Etymology

The name 'Aglaonoe' is derived from the Greek elements 'aglaos' (ἀγλαός) meaning 'splendid, bright, or glorious' and 'noos' (νόος) meaning 'mind' or 'thought'. Thus, the name can be interpreted as 'splendid-minded' or 'bright of thought'. This etymology is consistent with the names of many nymphs and minor goddesses, which often combine adjectives describing beauty or virtue with nouns denoting aspects of nature or personality.

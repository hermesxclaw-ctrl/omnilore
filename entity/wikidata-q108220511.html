Who It Is

She fell between Heaven and Hell and decided to sing anyway. IRyS — Hololive's half-angel, half-demon Nephilim of Hope — debuted alone in 2021 as Project:HOPE, a VSinger whose very existence is a contradiction: a halo that bleeds thorns, a demon horn beside an angel's wing, and a voice tasked with delivering hope to a world tilting toward despair.

Performed personality (as presented on stream, per official profile and observed streams): earnest, gentle, slightly shy but determined; speaks in soft, melodic English with occasional Japanese phrases; self-deprecating humor about being 'half' at everything; deeply music-driven — frames streaming as delivering hope. Lore personality: compassionate Nephilim who chose to side with humanity despite rejection by both Heaven and Hell, using song to stave off despair. Powers
Nephilim Physiology (Half-Angel / Half-Demon)

Lore ability: As offspring of angel and demon, IRyS exists between realms, able to traverse Paradise and Underworld boundaries and survive in the human world where pure angels/demons cannot. Grants extended lifespan and resilience to despair miasma.

Limit: Belongs nowhere — rejected by both sides; power wanes when hope fades; chest Hope Diamond cracks under despair overload (depicted in 'Caesura of Despair' MV).

Hope Amplification via Song (VSinger Ability)

Core fictional power: singing converts listeners' despair into hope, temporarily purifying the environment. Real-world analogue: VSinger performances that chart on music platforms and are used as Hololive concert finales.

Limit: Requires willing listeners; ineffective if audience has lost all hope; drains IRyS emotionally (lyrics describe voice cracking, diamond dimming). Not combat-applicable.

Diamond of Hope (Artifact)

Red gem embedded in chest; storage and conduit for hope energy. Glows when singing; shatters/reforms as plot device in MVs.

Limit: If shattered, IRyS loses power until restored by collective hope (narrative device). Vulnerable to despair attacks.

Professional Vocal Performance (Real-World Skill)

Attested real skill: trained singing voice covering pop, rock, ballad, and musical theater; original songs produced by Cover Records; live concert stamina (solo 60-min sets). YouTube karaoke archives demonstrate range and pitch control.

Limit: Human vocal limits; stream audio quality dependent on equipment; no supernatural effect.

Virtual Avatar Embodiment

Technical ability: real-time motion capture via Live2D and 3D models, allowing expressive streaming, dancing, and VR concerts. Includes bows, winks, wing flaps triggered by performer input.

Limit: Requires tracking software and hardware; occasional tracking glitches; 3D requires studio.

Accounts I
Project:HOPE — The Nephilim's Descent (Debut Lore)Debut lore animation and profile text (Cover Corporation, 2021-07-11)

2021 (in-universe: near-future apocalyptic present)

In a world where despair has consumed humanity, Heaven and Hell have abandoned Earth. A Nephilim born of an angel father and demon mother awakens in the ruins, neither accepted above nor below. Guided by a faint light in her chest — the Hope Diamond — she hears a voice telling her that hope remains if she sings. She descends to the human world to deliver hope through song, knowing she may be shunned as a monster. The animation ends with IRyS opening her eyes on stage, transitioning to her live debut greeting. Establishes IRyS's core motivation and duality; frames all subsequent original songs as in-universe missions. Justifies solo status outside standard Hololive generations.

Caesura of Despair — The First SongSingle 'Caesura of Despair' MV and lyrics (Cover Records, 2021-07-11)

2021

IRyS sings amid a crumbling cityscape as her Hope Diamond flickers. Lyrics describe a 'caesura' (musical pause) where despair silences the world, and she must fill the silence. The MV shows her wings spreading as she chooses to keep singing despite her voice cracking and diamond cracking, asserting that even a brief pause can be overcome by continuing the melody. The song served as her debut original and narrative thesis. Debut original became signature song; performed at every major Hololive concert thereafter; established musical direction (emotional rock ballad). Peaked on iTunes and YouTube trending.

HERE COMES HOPE / Quarter Bravery — Gathering LightEP 'Quarter Bravery' (2022-02-26) and MV 'HERE COMES HOPE'

2022

Following debut, IRyS collects fragments of hope from listeners. 'HERE COMES HOPE' depicts her running through a dark corridor toward a stage where IRyStocrats (fans) hold lights. Lyrics shift from solitary despair to communal hope: 'Even if I'm half, I can be whole with you.' The EP's six tracks trace an arc from doubt ('Carbonated Love') to resolve ('Until the End'). First physical EP; sold at Hololive events; expanded lore from solitary savior to community-powered hope.

Accounts II
Journey — Promise and RebirthEP/Album 'Journey' (2023-09-06) and 2nd Anniversary lore update

2023

Two years after descent, IRyS reflects on her journey. Title track 'Journey' narrates traveling through seasons with fans, acknowledging failures and renewed determination. Lore update recontextualizes her as not alone: she is invited to join Hololive English -Promise- (with Fauna, Kronii, Mumei, Baelz), where five beings who defy fate promise to stay together. IRyS's Nephilim nature is reframed as one of several 'concepts' (Nature, Time, Civilization, Chaos, Hope). Officially ends solo Project:HOPE isolation; integrates IRyS into permanent unit for concerts and merch; Promise debut concert at Hololive English 2nd Concert 'Breaking Dimensions' (2024).

Hololive 3rd fes. Link Your Wish & 4th fes. Our Bright Parade — Concert CanonLive concert footage: Hololive 3rd fes. (Makuhari Messe, 2022-03-19/20) and 4th fes. (Makuhari Messe, 2023-03-18/19)

2022-2023

IRyS performs 'Caesura of Despair' and 'HERE COMES HOPE' on the main Hololive stage alongside 50+ talents, enacting lore in live form: descending from above on wires (3rd fes) and leading a hope-themed interlude. At 4th fes, she performs as part of Hololive English ensemble, symbolizing acceptance by the broader Hololive 'family' that lore said rejected her. Cements IRyS as core Hololive concert performer; 3D model debut validated; live audience of 10k+ per day plus online pay-per-view.

Hololive Alternative & Hololive ERROR — Extended Universe CameoPromotional video 'Hololive Alternative' (2021) and game 'Hololive ERROR' (2022)

2021-2022

IRyS appears in Hololive Alternative teaser as silhouette among all Hololive members in a fantasy city, suggesting her hope powers exist in that shared universe. In Hololive ERROR (horror game), IRyS is referenced as NPC voice line (unconfirmed cameo) — not central, but indicates inclusion in Hololive's broader narrative experiments. Shows Cover's intent to include IRyS in cross-branch lore beyond music; no standalone IRyS game route.

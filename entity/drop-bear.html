Who It Is
Form

The drop bear is described as a large and carnivorous version of the koala, with a menacing appearance. It is said to inhabit treetops and attack unsuspecting people by dropping onto their heads from above. The drop bear is often depicted as a giant koala with sharp teeth and claws.

Powers

Carnivorous Appetite The drop bear is said to have a voracious appetite for meat, and is known to attack people who walk beneath its treetop habitat.

Accounts

The drop bear is a hoax in contemporary Australian folklore featuring a predatory, carnivorous version of the koala. This imaginary animal is commonly spoken about in tall tales designed to scare tourists.

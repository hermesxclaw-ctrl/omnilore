The Approach

Ayane is a name that resonates across Japanese culture, from the melodic stage names of singers and actresses to the fierce fighters and heroines of anime and video games. Its literal meaning, 'colorful sound,' captures the diversity of the people and characters who bear it, each adding their own hue to the name's rich tapestry.

Identity

Ayane

Pronunciation /aˈjaˌnɛ/

Etymology

The name is a romanization of a Japanese feminine given name, typically written with kanji such as 彩音 (colorful sound) or 綺音 (beautiful sound). The characters 彩 (aya) means 'color' or 'design', and 音 (ne) means 'sound'. 綺 (aya) also means 'beautiful' or 'ornamental'.

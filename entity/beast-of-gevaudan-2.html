Symbols — The Beast of Gévaudan has been associated with various symbols and iconography, including the image of a wolf or a dog-like creature with sharp teeth. In some accounts, the creature is depicted with a crown or a royal symbol, indicating its association with the French monarchy.

Domains — The Beast of Gévaudan was said to inhabit the Margeride Mountains of south-central France, specifically in the province of Gévaudan. The creature was often associated with the forests and mountains of the region, where it was said to roam freely.

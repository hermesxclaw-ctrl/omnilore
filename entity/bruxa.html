Who It Is
Form

Bruxa is often depicted as a crone, an old woman with a long, curved nose and pointed chin. She is said to have long, wild hair and a black cloak that flows behind her like a dark cloud.

Powers

Shape-shifting Bruxa is said to have the ability to take on many forms and guises, allowing her to move unseen and strike fear into the hearts of those who know her.

Night vision Bruxa is said to have the ability to see in the dark, allowing her to navigate the night with ease and strike fear into the hearts of those who know her.

Healing Bruxa is said to have the ability to heal wounds and cure ailments, using her connection to the natural world to bring balance and harmony to those who seek her out.

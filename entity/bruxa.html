Form & Signs

Appearance — Bruxa is often depicted as a crone, an old woman with a long, curved nose and pointed chin. She is said to have long, wild hair and a black cloak that flows behind her like a dark cloud.

Symbols — The Bruxa is associated with the Ascalapha odorata moth, which is said to carry the spirits of the dead. She is also linked to the night sky and the phases of the moon.

Domains — Bruxa is said to inhabit the night, and is often associated with the forest, the mountains, and the sea.

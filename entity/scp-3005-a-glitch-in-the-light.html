Who It Is

A phenomenon affecting digital photography worldwide. In approximately 0.003% of all digital photographs taken since 2019, a figure appears in the background that was not present when the photo was taken. The figure is always the same: a tall, featureless humanoid silhouette, standing at the edge of the frame, facing the camera. Analysis reveals the figure is closer in each successive photo of the same subject. Extrapolation predicts it will reach 'contact distance' by 2037. The Foundation does not know what happens then.

Form

A humanoid silhouette, approximately 2.2m tall, with no discernible features. Its outline is crisp regardless of photo quality. It is always in the background, always facing the camera, and always slightly closer than in the previous photo of that subject.

Powers

Global photographic manifestation

Progressive proximity increase per subject

Existence independent of camera hardware

Predictable approach trajectory

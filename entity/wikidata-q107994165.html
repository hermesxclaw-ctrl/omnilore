Who It Is

Kasumi Toyama heard the stars before she ever held a guitar — a glittering, heart-pounding chime she calls the Star Beat. Chasing that sound through pawnshop aisles, failed club visits, and a live-house stage that nearly silences her, the relentlessly optimistic first-year who can't sit still becomes the gravitational center of Poppin'Party, the garage-born pop-rock band that turned Bushiroad's experiment in voice-actresses-who-really-play into a franchise-spanning phenomenon.

Canonical personality (2017 reboot onward) is relentlessly energetic, optimistic, impulsive, and emotionally transparent. She acts before thinking, chases anything that makes her heart race (dokidoki), and vocalizes wonder without filter. She is highly sociable and inclusive, often initiating band activities and mediating with other bands, but can be oblivious to others' fatigue or to practical constraints. Lead Vocals and Rhythm Guitar (Poppin'Party)

Kasumi is Poppin'Party's lead vocalist and rhythm guitarist, playing a red ESP Random Star. Narratively characterized as not the most technically skilled (initially unable to play chords, judged weakest in Space audition) but possessing strong emotive delivery, stamina, and ability to energize a crowd. Repertoire is original pop-rock with upbeat tempos, major keys, and call-and-response audience parts.

Limit: Explicitly limited technique early on; requires Tae's instruction for basic chords. Stress-induced psychogenic aphonia after harsh critique (Ep. 8) shows vulnerability to pressure. Relies on band cohesion rather than solo virtuosity. Live-performance canon notes Aimi learned guitar for the role, so early lives used support musicians.

Songwriting and Lyricism (Initiator)

Within fiction, Kasumi co-writes Poppin'Party lyrics with encouragement from others, often starting from star/meteor imagery and everyday excitement. Real-world lyrics for Poppin'Party are primarily by Kō Nakamura, but Kasumi is depicted as bringing lyrical seeds ('Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star' motif, 'Star Beat' phrase).

Limit: Not a trained composer; depends on Tae and others for arrangement. No attested ability to write for other bands.

Leadership and Interpersonal Magnetism

Recruits and retains band members, mediates conflicts (e.g., Double Rainbow argument between Arisa and others), and initiates cross-band collaborations (helping CiRCLE organize Girls Band Party). Her 'heart-racing' criterion functions as social glue.

Limit: Impulsiveness leads to overcommitment and blind spots; Arisa and others must ground her plans. Leadership is charismatic, not authoritative.

Star Beat Sensitivity (Narrative Device, Not Supernatural Power)

Ability to perceive a sparkling, heart-pounding sound/ feeling (Hoshi no Kodou) that others initially cannot hear, which guides her to music. Presented as metaphorical sensitivity to wonder rather than literal superpower; no in-universe magical effects.

Limit: Subjective and non-transferable; mocked by peers in Star Beat continuity, requiring external validation via Glitter Green performance.

Accounts I
The Star Beat and the Random Star - Proto-OriginBanG Dream! Star Beat manga by Kō Nakamura & Aya Ishida

2015 (in-universe: Kasumi's first year, spring)

A shy, withdrawn Kasumi enters Hanasakigawa having been ridiculed as a child for saying she heard the Star Beat while singing 'Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star.' Wishing to change herself, she stumbles into Arisa Ichigaya's family pawnshop, finds a red star-shaped guitar (Random Star), and is recruited by Arisa into her 'game' BanG_Dream! as its protagonist. Arisa pushes her to learn guitar to rediscover the sound, setting up a more game-like, meta formation story that was later retconned. Establishes core iconography (Random Star, Star Beat, Arisa's shop) and Kasumi's core wound, but personality and framing were discarded in reboot. Serves as primary evidence for version differences.

Searching for the Star Beat - Formation and Space AuditionBanG Dream! Anime Season 1 (Episodes 1-13)

2017 (in-universe: spring to summer of first year)

Restless first-year Kasumi tries every club, haunted by a childhood memory of a sparkling sound at a starry sky camp. She finds the Random Star at Arisa's pawnshop, is inspired by a street performance from Glitter Green at live house Space, and resolves to form a band. She recruits Arisa (keyboard, reluctantly), Rimi (bass, after caring for her), Saaya (drums, after reconciling her family bakery duties), and Tae (guitar, after impressing her with a heartfelt performance). The band fails its first Space audition when Kasumi is singled out as the weakest and loses her voice from stress, but with encouragement they re-audition and pass, performing at Space's final show before it closes, affirming that the Star Beat was the sound of everyone playing together. Defines canonical Kasumi as optimistic initiator; establishes Poppin'Party's underdog-but-heartfelt identity and Space as sacred origin. Directly adapted as game Band Story 0.

Poppin'Party is Born! (Band Story 0)BanG Dream! Girls Band Party! - Poppin'Party Band Story 0

2017-2018 (game release, in-universe: first year)

Interactive retelling of the anime formation arc from Kasumi's POV with added slice-of-life scenes: remedial home economics with Tae, guitar lessons, and extended recruitment conversations. Kasumi's impulsiveness is foregrounded, and her ability to make others' hearts race is tested explicitly by Tae's challenge. Solidifies Kasumi-Tae bond as central dyad; provides most detailed depiction of Kasumi learning chords. Considered definitive game canon for formation.

Accounts II
Colorful, Poppin' Candy - Saving the Downtown FestivalGirls Band Party! Event Story 'Colorful, Poppin' Candy'

2017 (Event 12, in-universe: summer first year)

Kasumi learns the local shopping district festival may be cancelled due to lack of entertainment. Determined to make the festival sparkle, she rallies Poppin'Party to organize a live performance, negotiating with shopkeepers and balancing practice with community service. The story emphasizes her community-minded optimism and tendency to overpromise, requiring the band to rescue her ambitious plan. Shows Kasumi as civic initiator beyond personal ambition; reinforces pop-rock as community music.

Double Rainbow - The ArgumentGirls Band Party! Event Story 'Double Rainbow'

2018 (Event 58, in-universe: second year)

Tension erupts when Arisa feels Kasumi and others are not taking practice seriously, leading to a heated argument and temporary rift. Kasumi, hurt but unwilling to let the band fracture, reflects on her role as leader who sometimes pushes too fast. Tae's mediation and a frank discussion lead to reconciliation and a renewed commitment, culminating in a performance symbolized by a double rainbow. Deepens Kasumi's leadership from cheerleading to emotional accountability; frequently cited as key character development for Arisa-Kasumi dynamic.

Live Beyond!! - Rocking Star FestivalGirls Band Party! Poppin'Party Band Story 3 'Live Beyond!!'

2019 (in-universe: second to third year)

Poppin'Party is invited to the Rocking Star Festival, a larger stage that forces Kasumi to confront whether their music can reach beyond their familiar circle. She grapples with self-doubt about whether their 'popping' sound is enough, inspired by seeing Roselia and Raise A Suilen's technical prowess. Through renewed songwriting and a promise to 'spread the Star Beat,' the band delivers a performance that affirms their identity without mimicking others. Marks transition from local live-house band to festival-level act; sets up Season 3's Girls Band Challenge arc.

Self-Sponsored Live and Girls Band Challenge (Anime Seasons 2 & 3)BanG Dream! Season 2 (2019) and Season 3 (2020), Sanzigen

2019-2020 (in-universe: second year)

Season 2: Kasumi spearheads Poppin'Party's self-sponsored concert at CiRCLE to prove they can draw a crowd without relying on Space, learning event organization, ticketing, and promotion. Tae's temporary dual membership with Raise A Suilen tests Kasumi's security as leader, resolved when Tae chooses Poppin'Party. Season 3: Poppin'Party enters the BanG Dream! Girls Band Challenge, competing against Roselia, Raise A Suilen, and others. Kasumi's growth is shown in her ability to support rivals and to articulate why Poppin'Party plays — not to be the best technically, but to share sparkling feelings. In her third year, she takes a part-time job at RiNG, CiRCLE's sister live house, signaling adulthood transition. Completes Kasumi's arc from seeker to organizer/mentor; establishes her as franchise's central protagonist and bridge between bands.

Kasumi is a magical girl who literally hears stars and has star powers. — Star Beat is a metaphor for the emotional spark of music and wonder, not a supernatural ability. Kasumi has no magic, transformations, or combat powers; she is a normal high school guitarist/vocalist. Official materials never depict Star Beat as audible to others or as granting powers.

Kasumi is the most skilled guitarist in BanG Dream! because she is the protagonist. — Canon explicitly states Poppin'Party's raw skill is not the best and Kasumi was judged the weakest in the first Space audition. Technical virtuosity belongs to Roselia and Raise A Suilen members (e.g., Tae is the skilled guitarist, not Kasumi). Kasumi's strength is emotive leadership and growth.

BanG Dream! Star Beat manga is the direct prequel to the anime. — Star Beat is a retconned prototype with different characterization and premise. The anime and game reboot in 2017 discard its shy Kasumi and 'game' framing; they are not continuous. Bushiroad treats Star Beat as historical artifact.

Kasumi's guitar is a fictional design that cannot be purchased. — The red ESP Random Star is a real production model by ESP Guitars, sold as Kasumi Toyama signature, based on ESP Random Star shape with star inlay. It is playable and used by Aimi live.

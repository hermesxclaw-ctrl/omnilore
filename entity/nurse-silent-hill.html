Who It Is

Legless or stumbling female humanoid monsters that resemble nurses, with revealing uniforms, white skin, and head coverings concealing their faces. In Silent Hill 2, they represent James Sunderland's repressed sexual frustrations and the objectification of his wife's caretakers. They move erratically and attack with their hands or nearby objects.

Powers

Erratic movement, claw attacks, psychological symbolism

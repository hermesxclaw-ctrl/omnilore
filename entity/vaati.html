Who It Is
Form

Vaati is a one-eyed demon with the ability to control the wind, appearing in various forms throughout the Legend of Zelda series.

Powers

Wind Magic Vaati has the ability to control the wind, using it to manipulate the environment and his enemies.

Accounts

In The Minish Cap, Vaati is the main antagonist, using his wind magic to terrorize the land of Hyrule.

In Four Swords, Vaati is a powerful demon who seeks to conquer the land of Hyrule, using his wind magic to devastating effect.

Who It Is
Form

The painting depicts two figures embracing, with the woman's face hidden and the man's face visible. The figures are surrounded by intricate patterns and gold leaf.

Powers

Romantic Allure The painting's depiction of the couple's intimate moment has the power to evoke feelings of romance and intimacy in the viewer.

Accounts

Gustav Klimt created The Kiss in 1907-1908 as part of his series of paintings depicting Greek and Roman myths.

The Kiss is a cultural artifact of the fine art movement, specifically associated with Gustav Klimt and the Vienna Secession.

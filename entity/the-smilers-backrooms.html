Who It Is

Entities that exist only in darkness — floating, disembodied pairs of glowing white eyes above a wide, toothy grin. In lit areas, Smilers are completely invisible and intangible. In darkness, they manifest fully as gaunt humanoids of shadow-stuff. They do not pursue — they appear, surround, and consume. Their 'smile' is the natural configuration of their face: a permanent rictus that opens wider as they feed.

Form

In darkness: tall (2-2.5m), gaunt humanoid made of solidified shadow, with large glowing white eyes and a wide mouth of needle-sharp inward-curving teeth. In light: completely invisible. Transition from invisible to visible takes ~2 seconds.

Powers

Light-dependent visibility

Swarm predation

Silent movement

Consumption via shadow-matter absorption

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

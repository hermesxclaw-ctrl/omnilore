Who It Is
Form

Elaine is often depicted as a beautiful and tragic figure, dressed in white or pale colors.

Powers

Unrequited Love Elaine's ability to love Sir Lancelot unrequitedly leads to her tragic fate.

Accounts

Elaine falls in love with Sir Lancelot, but he does not return her love. She dies of a broken heart.

The Lady of Shalott is a poem that tells the story of Elaine of Astolat, who is cursed to weave a web of fate.

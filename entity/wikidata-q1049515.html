Who It Is

In the halls of Namimori Middle, order is enforced not by teachers but by a lone delinquent armed with tonfas and a warning: 'I'll bite you to death.' Kyoya Hibari, the self-appointed prefect, rules his school with a pride that borders on obsession, yet his fierce independence and hidden softness for small creatures make him one of the most compelling figures in the world of Reborn!.

Hibari is cold, aloof, and fiercely independent. He despises crowds and prefers solitude, often threatening anyone who intrudes on his space with 'I'll bite you to death.' He has an intense pride in his school and his own abilities, and he only engages in fights that interest him. Despite his violent exterior, he shows a gentle care for small animals and children, indicating a hidden compassionate side. He is pragmatic and observant, often assessing situations from the sidelines before acting.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Hibari' (雲雀) means 'skylark' in Japanese, and 'Kyoya' (恭弥) is a given name that can mean 'respectful' and 'long-lasting'. The skylark is a bird known for its song and high flight, which may reflect Hibari's independent and soaring spirit.

Pronunciation — Kyo-ya Hi-ba-ri (Japanese: きょうや ひばり)

Aliases — Hibari Kyōya, Cloud Guardian of the Vongola, Prefect of Namimori

Powers
Tonfa Combat

Hibari is highly skilled with a pair of collapsible tonfas, which he uses for both offense and defense. They can extend and retract, and contain hidden chains and spikes for varied attacks.

Limit: Requires physical contact; can be disarmed if overwhelmed.

Cloud Flame

After becoming the Cloud Guardian, Hibari gains the ability to generate purple flames with propagation properties, allowing them to multiply and spread. He channels these through his Vongola Cloud Ring and later his Vongola Gear.

Limit: Requires a compatible ring or gear; overuse can strain his body.

Roll (Box Weapon)

A hedgehog box weapon that can multiply, expand spikes, or grow into a massive spiked sphere. In its Cambio Forma, it becomes the Alaude Handcuffs, which can restrain and crush opponents.

Limit: Initially uncontrollable without training; requires the Vongola Box and ring.

Enhanced Physical Abilities

Hibari possesses incredible speed, reflexes, and strength, making him one of the strongest characters in the series. He can easily overpower most opponents and has immense endurance.

Limit: No explicit limits shown, but he is not invincible and can be defeated by stronger enemies.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Hibari is a tall, lean young man with spiky black hair and sharp, dark eyes. He typically wears the Namimori Middle School uniform, often with the jacket over his shoulders. His most iconic accessory is a pair of collapsible steel tonfas, which he wields with deadly precision. In his future self, he wears a black suit and carries a similar demeanor. When using his Vongola Gear, his uniform transforms into a more armored, cloud-themed outfit with

Symbols — The cloud is his symbol, representing his independent and unbound nature. His weapon, the tonfa, is a symbol of his role as a prefect and enforcer. The hedgehog Roll is a recurring motif, representing his softer side and his ability to protect.

Domains — Primarily Namimori Middle School and Namimori Town, which he patrols as the self-appointed enforcer of order. He also appears in various locations during the future arc and in the Vongola headquarters. He is most comfortable in solitary or high places, away from crowds.

Accounts I
Encounter with Mukuro RokudoReborn! manga/anime, Vongola Arc

2000s

Hibari is infected with the Sakura-kura disease by Dr. Shamal, which makes him weak near cherry blossoms. Mukuro exploits this weakness and defeats him. Later, after receiving the cure, Hibari fights Mukuro again, dealing a critical blow but collapsing from his injuries. This defeat fuels Hibari's desire to fight Mukuro again, and it establishes their rivalry. It also leads to Hibari's eventual acceptance of the Cloud Ring.

Becoming the Cloud GuardianReborn! manga/anime, Vongola Arc

2000s

After training with Dino, Hibari participates in the Vongola Tournament, defeating the Varia's Gola Moska. He challenges Xanxus but is interrupted. Later, he fights Belphegor and cures himself of poison. Tsuna's side wins, and Hibari is officially recognized as the Cloud Guardian. Hibari gains the Vongola Cloud Ring and becomes a key member of the Vongola family, though he remains independent.

Accounts II
The Future ArcReborn! manga/anime, Future Arc

2000s

Ten years in the future, Hibari has become the leader of The Foundation and has researched box weapons. He trains the past Tsuna and explains the box system. He fights the Millefiore, infiltrates the Melone Base, and defeats Daisy using his Vongola Box. Hibari's actions are crucial in the fight against the Millefiore, and his knowledge of boxes helps the Vongola in their battles.

Shimon Arc and BeyondReborn! manga/anime, Shimon Arc and Inheritance Ceremony Arc

2000s

Hibari clashes with Adelheid Suzuki and is defeated by Enma, leading to his Cloud Ring being shattered. He acquires the Vongola Gear's Bracelet of the Clouds and defeats Adelheid on Simon Island. Later, he fights Demon Spade but is trapped in an illusionary world. In the Representative Battle of the Rainbow, he fights alongside Fon and later Mukuro, helping Tsuna defeat Jager. Hibari grows stronger and more aligned with the Vongola, eventually fighting to protect Tsuna and the world.

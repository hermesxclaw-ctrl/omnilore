Who It Is

In Gnosticism, Sophia (Greek: Σοφία, 'Wisdom') is a feminine figure — the last and lowest of the Aeons (divine emanations) generated by the unknowable God. In the Valentinian myth, Sophia's tragic flaw was her desire to know the unknowable Father directly, without her syzygy (divine partner). This resulted in a 'fall from grace': she gave birth to a flawed, aborted emanation — the Demiurge (Yaldabaoth), who in ignorance created the material world as a prison for divine sparks. Sophia's fall, repentance, and eventual redemption form the central Gnostic drama. The gnostic is called to follow Sophia's path: to recognize the spark of the divine within, escape the material prison, and return to the Pleroma (fullness). Sophia thus represents both the human soul in exile and divine Wisdom in search of completion.

Sophia (Divine Wisdom)

Who It Is
Form

Sophia is often depicted as a female figure with a crown or a halo, surrounded by symbols of wisdom such as the owl or the snake. In some depictions, she is shown as a young woman with a book or a scroll, representing her role as a bringer of knowledge.

Powers

Cosmic Wisdom Sophia possesses the power of cosmic wisdom, which allows her to bring light and understanding to humanity. She can reveal hidden truths and guide individuals towards enlightenment.

Accounts

The story of Sophia in the Apocryphon of John tells of a divine being who creates the world through her wisdom. However, her creation is imperfect, and she is separated from the divine realm. Sophia then sends her emanations to guide humanity towards wisdom and enlightenment.

The story of Sophia in the Gospel of Philip tells of a divine being who is the source of all wisdom. Sophia is seen as a feminine aspect of the divine, who brings light and understanding to humanity.

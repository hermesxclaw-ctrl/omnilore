Who It Is

A massive, vaguely arachnid entity that has replaced a family in their own home, documented in the 'Home Invasion Help' tape. The instructional tape advises identifying whether family members have been replaced by 'biological infiltrators,' showing footage of the entity failing to mimic human domestic life. It sits at the dinner table without eating. It stands in doorways without entering rooms. Its face, glimpsed at the tape's end, is a cratered fleshy surface with too many eyes — none human.

Form

Approximately 3m tall when upright, frequently hunched to fit within doorways. Body is a bulbous, fleshy mass supported by six long jointed limbs. 'Face' is a concave depression lined with multiple asymmetrical eyes blinking independently. No mouth — it 'speaks' by vibrating the air, producing sounds approximating human speech with subsonic wrongness.

Powers

Imperfect human domestic mimicry

Replacement of entire families

Subsonic communication

Immense physical strength

Patience — will wait weeks to learn 'acting human'

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

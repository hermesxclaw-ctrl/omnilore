Who It Is

A massive centipede-like creature, 50 feet long, with a chitinous blue-white exoskeleton and a back that glows red-hot. The remorhaz generates intense heat from its body, melting ice and snow around it. It burrows through arctic tundra and glaciers, ambushing prey from below.

The Approach

The word 'ban' carries the weight of law, the edge of exile, and the spark of a name—from medieval sovereigns who could command armies with a word, to internet moderators who silence with a click. But in the realm of fiction, it is the name of a fox-like immortal who defies death itself, a thief with a heart of gold and a rage that could shatter mountains.

Content advisory. No dark material in the legal, geographic, or linguistic senses. Fictional character Ban from The Seven Deadly Sins has themes of violence, death, and emotional trauma, but no explicit sexual violence or abuse.
Identity

Ban

Pronunciation In English, /bæn/ (rhymes with 'pan'). In Japanese, for the character Ban from The Seven Deadly Sins, it is pronounced 'Bān' (バン). In Romanian, 'ban' is pronounced /ban/.

Etymology

The word 'ban' originates from Old English 'bannan' meaning 'to summon' or 'proclaim', and from Old High German 'bannan' meaning 'to command'. As a title, it derives from the medieval Latin 'banum' or 'bannum', referring to the sovereign's power to command or prohibit. As a surname, it has independent origins in Chinese (班) and Korean (반). For the fictional character, the name is simply 'Ban'.

Cultural Ownership

The term 'ban' is pan-European and global, with legal, geographic, and cultural significance. The fictional character Ban is owned by Nakaba Suzuki and Kodansha, appearing in the manga 'The Seven Deadly Sins'.

Disputed Identifications

The name 'Ban' is shared by multiple distinct entities, leading to potential confusion. For example, the fictional character Ban from The Seven Deadly Sins is unrelated to Ban Mido from GetBackers. There is no scholarly dispute over the term's meaning, but the multiplicity of uses requires disambiguation.

Description

Appearance

The fictional Ban from The Seven Deadly Sins is a tall, muscular man with spiky pink hair and sharp, fox-like eyes. He often wears a fur-trimmed coat and carries a heavy iron club. In his immortal state, he appears youthful despite being centuries old. Other uses of 'Ban' are abstract or geographic, with no physical form.

Symbols & Iconography

In heraldry and legal contexts, 'ban' is associated with edicts and prohibitions. For the fictional character, his symbol is the fox, representing cunning and immortality. The name 'Ban' in the context of the Romanian coin is symbolized by the coin itself.

Behaviour & Personality

The fictional Ban is characterized by his greed, but also his loyalty, impulsiveness, and deep love for his wife Elaine. He is reckless and often acts without thinking, but is fiercely protective of his friends. In legal contexts, 'ban' refers to a prohibition or command, often associated with authority and enforcement.

Domains & Habitats

The fictional Ban hails from the Fairy King's Forest, but later resides in the Boar Hat tavern. He is associated with the Kingdom of Liones. Historically, the ban was a legal domain of sovereigns in medieval Europe. Geographically, 'Ban' is a place name in multiple countries, including Burkina Faso, Iran, and Romania.

Version Differences

The fictional Ban has different portrayals across media: in the manga, he is more serious; in the anime, his comedic traits are emphasized. In the film 'Prisoners of the Sky', his backstory is expanded. Other fictional characters named Ban, such as Ban Mido, are entirely distinct and have different powers and origins.

Powers & Abilities

Immortality

Source Version The Seven Deadly Sins manga and anime

Description

Ban was granted immortality by the Fountain of Youth, making him unable to die from age, disease, or injury. He can regenerate from almost any wound, even decapitation.

Limitations

He can still feel pain and can be incapacitated. He cannot regenerate his own soul if it is destroyed.

Contradictions

In the sequel 'Four Knights of the Apocalypse', Ban's immortality is weakened, as he ages slowly but not completely.

Snatch

Source Version The Seven Deadly Sins

Description

A sacred treasure ability that allows Ban to steal physical objects, memories, or even emotions from a target by touching them. He can also use it to take someone's strength temporarily.

Limitations

He must physically touch the target. The stolen attributes return after a short time.

Contradictions

In the anime, Snatch is shown to be more versatile than in the manga, allowing him to steal intangible things like pain.

Fox Hunt

Source Version The Seven Deadly Sins

Description

A technique that combines his agility and weapon skills, allowing him to strike multiple times rapidly.

Limitations

Requires stamina and focus.

Contradictions

None known.

Physical Combat

The Ban of the Holy Roman Empire

Source Historical recordsCulture EuropeanPeriod Medieval

Summary

The imperial ban (Reichsacht) was a decree by the Holy Roman Emperor that declared an individual an outlaw, stripping them of legal rights and protection.

Participants

Holy Roman Emperors

Outlawed individuals

Consequences

The banned individual lost property and could be killed without legal consequence.

Variants

Different forms existed, such as the King's ban (Königsbann).

Ban's immortality in sequel

Position A

Ban is fully immortal and cannot die.

Position A — evidence

Original manga and anime show Ban regenerating from fatal wounds.

In 'Four Knights of the Apocalypse', Ban ages slowly, indicating his immortality is fading.

The sequel is a direct continuation, so the later portrayal is canonical, but it contradicts the earlier absolute immortality.

Who It Is

The word 'ban' carries the weight of law, the edge of exile, and the spark of a name—from medieval sovereigns who could command armies with a word, to internet moderators who silence with a click. But in the realm of fiction, it is the name of a fox-like immortal who defies death itself, a thief with a heart of gold and a rage that could shatter mountains.

The fictional Ban is characterized by his greed, but also his loyalty, impulsiveness, and deep love for his wife Elaine. He is reckless and often acts without thinking, but is fiercely protective of his friends. In legal contexts, 'ban' refers to a prohibition or command, often associated with authority and enforcement.

Powers
Immortality

Ban was granted immortality by the Fountain of Youth, making him unable to die from age, disease, or injury. He can regenerate from almost any wound, even decapitation.

Limit: He can still feel pain and can be incapacitated. He cannot regenerate his own soul if it is destroyed.

Snatch

A sacred treasure ability that allows Ban to steal physical objects, memories, or even emotions from a target by touching them. He can also use it to take someone's strength temporarily.

Limit: He must physically touch the target. The stolen attributes return after a short time.

Fox Hunt

A technique that combines his agility and weapon skills, allowing him to strike multiple times rapidly.

Limit: Requires stamina and focus.

Physical Combat

Ban is a formidable hand-to-hand combatant and skilled with his weapon, the Sacred Treasure Courechouse.

Limit: He relies on his physical strength, which is superhuman but not invincible.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The fictional Ban from The Seven Deadly Sins is a tall, muscular man with spiky pink hair and sharp, fox-like eyes. He often wears a fur-trimmed coat and carries a heavy iron club. In his immortal state, he appears youthful despite being centuries old. Other uses of 'Ban' are abstract or geographic, with no physical form.

Symbols — In heraldry and legal contexts, 'ban' is associated with edicts and prohibitions. For the fictional character, his symbol is the fox, representing cunning and immortality. The name 'Ban' in the context of the Romanian coin is symbolized by the coin itself.

Domains — The fictional Ban hails from the Fairy King's Forest, but later resides in the Boar Hat tavern. He is associated with the Kingdom of Liones. Historically, the ban was a legal domain of sovereigns in medieval Europe. Geographically, 'Ban' is a place name in multiple countries, including Burkina Faso, Iran, and Romania.

Ban, a human bandit, seeks the Fountain of Youth to revive his deceased lover Elaine. He finds it but is cursed with immortality, unable to die or reunite with her in death. This tragedy shapes his character. Ban becomes immortal and joins the Seven Deadly Sins, seeking a way to die and reunite with Elaine.

The Ban of the Holy Roman EmpireHistorical records

Medieval

The imperial ban (Reichsacht) was a decree by the Holy Roman Emperor that declared an individual an outlaw, stripping them of legal rights and protection. The banned individual lost property and could be killed without legal consequence.

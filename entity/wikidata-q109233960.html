Who It Is

On the day his colony fell, sixteen-year-old Banagher Links did not want to become a pilot — he wanted to save a girl who fell from the sky. When the white Unicorn Gundam opened only for him, the most dangerous secret in the Universal Century chose its heir, and a gentle industrial-college student was forced to decide what a Newtype should do with a weapon that can hear the thoughts of the dead.

Attested as kind, empathetic, and instinctively protective — his inciting action is diving to catch Audrey Burne as she falls in zero-G, without regard for himself. He is conflict-averse and repeatedly questions the necessity of killing, even when fighting for survival. This is not pacifism as ideology but as temperament; he will fight ferociously when friends are threatened, then agonize afterward. Powers
Newtype Spatial Awareness and Empathy (Attested)

Latent Newtype ability: senses hostile intent, presences, and emotional states across distance, even without direct visual contact. Can detect Audrey's distress, Marida's pain, and deaths of nearby pilots as psychowave 'pressure.' Demonstrated most clearly when he senses Full Frontal's intent and when he locates Audrey on Palau.

Limit: Uncontrolled and overwhelming at first; causes headaches, nosebleeds, and emotional flooding. Requires psycho-frame amplification to achieve long-range or precise communication. Blocked or distorted by strong psycho-wave jamming (e.g., Neo Zeong). Not precognition in the strict sense — more empathic resonance than tactical foresight.

Psycho-Frame Resonance and NT-D Control (Adaptation-Specific / Attested)

Ability to activate and sustain the RX-0 Unicorn Gundam's NT-D (Newtype Destroyer) System and later control its full psycho-frame. In Destroy Mode, the Unicorn's psycho-frame glows and responds to Banagher's will, enabling psycho-wave hijacking of enemy funnels/bits, beam magnum control, and eventually the psycho-field barrier that blocks colony laser shots. By final episode, he generates a massive psycho-field with Audrey/Mineva and the spirits of fallen Newtypes to push back the Gryps 2 colony laser.

Limit: NT-D initially auto-activates and risks taking over Banagher's consciousness (the system attempts to kill Newtypes). He learns to consciously limit it, but prolonged use causes physical exhaustion and risks being absorbed into the psycho-frame (as nearly happens in Episode 7). Cannot activate without Unicorn; not an innate superpower outside the machine. The colony-laser block requires collective psycho-field, not solo power.

Mobile Suit Piloting (Attested, Non-Superhuman)

Exceptionally skilled pilot despite no formal military training. Masters the highly demanding RX-0 Unicorn Gundam (and briefly the ARX-014 Silver Bullet, MSN-001A1 Delta Plus in novel) through intuitive Newtype interface rather than drilled technique. Shows aptitude for close-quarters combat, shield funnel use, and beam magnum marksmanship.

Limit: Initially clumsy; requires mentoring from Daguza Mackle and later Riddhe. Vulnerable without psycho-frame support against veteran aces (Full Frontal, Marida). No formal officer training; relies on others for tactics.

Psycho-Field / Post-Death Communication (Reconstructed / Disputed in-universe)

In climax, Banagher perceives and communicates with deceased characters (Daguza, Gilboa, Marida) within the psycho-field, and together they influence physical reality. This is presented as Newtype afterlife / collective consciousness rather than resurrection.

Limit: Cannot be summoned at will; occurs only under extreme psycho-frame overload and collective trauma. Scholars and in-universe Federation officials treat it as unverified phenomenon. Banagher himself is uncertain whether it was real or psychological.

Accounts I
Day of the Unicorn — Encounter on Industrial 7Fukui Vol.1; OVA Episode 1 'Day of the Unicorn'

UC 0096 (publication 2007 / animation 2010)

Sixteen-year-old Banagher Links, a student at Anaheim Electronics Industrial College on Industrial 7, skips class and encounters a mysterious girl, Audrey Burne, who has stowed away to meet Cardeas Vist. When he saves Audrey from falling in zero-G, he is drawn into a covert meeting where his estranged father Cardeas reveals the Vist Foundation's secret — Laplace's Box, the original UC 0001 charter that could topple the Federation — and that Banagher is his son. Sleeves (Neo Zeon remnants) attack the colony to seize the Box. Cardeas is mortally wounded and entrusts Banagher with the key to the RX-0 Unicorn Gundam, which activates only for him. Banagher becomes the Unicorn's pilot and heir to Laplace's Box; Industrial 7 is heavily damaged; the Federation and Sleeves both hunt him; Audrey's true identity as Mineva Zabi is foreshadowed.

The Second Coming of Char — Duel with the KshatriyaFukui Vol.2; OVA Episode 2 'The Second Coming of Char'

UC 0096

Aboard the Federation ship Nahel Argama after escaping Industrial 7, Banagher struggles with having killed for the first time. The Sleeves pursue with Marida Cruz piloting the NZ-666 Kshatriya. Banagher sorties in the Unicorn against orders to protect the ship. The Unicorn's NT-D activates autonomously, revealing its Newtype Destroyer nature, and overwhelms the Kshatriya, but Banagher is horrified by the system's bloodlust and his loss of control. Marida is captured, and Banagher begins to question whether the Unicorn is a weapon for understanding or for hunting Newtypes. First demonstration of NT-D; Marida's capture sets up her later rescue arc; Banagher's trauma deepens and bonds him to Marida as fellow exploited Newtype.

Palau — Captivity and the Ghost of the PastFukui Vol.3-4; OVA Episode 3 'The Ghost of Laplace'

UC 0096

The Nahel Argama is lured to the Sleeves asteroid base Palau for a prisoner exchange. Banagher is captured and meets Full Frontal, the 'Second Coming of Char,' who lectures him on the emptiness of the Box and the inevitability of conflict. Banagher also reunites with Audrey and witnesses the Sleeves' exploitation of Marida, recognizing her as a Cyber Newtype clone of Elpeo Ple. He escapes with the Unicorn during a Federation rescue operation, but is devastated when his mentor Daguza Mackle sacrifices himself to save him, dying in Banagher's arms after Banagher senses his death through Newtype resonance. Daguza's death becomes Banagher's core trauma and moral anchor; Banagher learns Full Frontal's nihilistic philosophy; Marida remains with Sleeves; Banagher's Newtype empathy intensifies.

At the Bottom of the Gravity Well — Torrington and DakarFukui Vol.5-6; OVA Episodes 4 'At the Bottom of the Gravity Well' and 5 'Black Unicorn'

UC 0096

The war descends to Earth. Banagher, now more adept, participates in the Federation's defense of Torrington Base in Australia against a Zeon remnant uprising, witnessing the senselessness of ground war and the death of civilian engineer Gilboa Sant, whose family Banagher had befriended. He then travels to Dakar, the Federation capital, where Mineva (Audrey) attempts to broadcast the truth of Laplace's Box to the world, and Riddhe Marcenas, Banagher's friend and fellow pilot, defects under pressure from his father Ronan and shoots Marida (now piloting the Banshee) while Banagher watches helplessly. Banagher's attempt to save Marida with the Unicorn's psycho-frame fails, and he falls into despair, briefly abandoning the battlefield. Banagher experiences Earth gravity, civilian casualties, and betrayal by Riddhe; Marida is critically injured; the political stakes of Laplace's Box become public; Banagher's faith in non-violence is shattered and must be rebuilt.

Over the Rainbow — The Opening of Laplace's BoxFukui Vol.9-10; OVA Episode 7 'Over the Rainbow'

UC 0096

In the final confrontation at the original Laplace debris field and Industrial 7, Banagher (in Unicorn), Riddhe (in Banshee Norn), and Full Frontal (in Neo Zeong) converge as the Box is opened. The Box is revealed to be the original UC 0001 Charter, with Article 15 stating that Spacenoids shall be given priority in governance should Newtypes emerge — a clause erased after the terrorist bombing of Laplace. Full Frontal seeks to use it to destroy the Federation's legitimacy; Mineva chooses to reveal it but calls for hope rather than vengeance. Banagher, resonating with the psycho-frame of both Unicorns and the spirits of the dead, creates a massive psycho-field that blocks the Federation's Gryps 2 colony laser from firing on Industrial 7, at the cost of being temporarily absorbed into the psycho-frame. He is pulled back by Audrey's voice and survives, choosing to live and guard the Box's truth as a possibility for the future, not a weapon. UC 0097 (in-universe one year after Unicorn)

A 17-year-old Banagher appears briefly in Gundam Narrative, living quietly under surveillance after the Unicorn incident. Banagher is just a clone or reincarnation of Amuro Ray or Char Aznable — He is unrelated biologically to either. Full Frontal is the Char-like figure; Banagher is intentionally written as thematic successor to Amuro (kind, Newtype idealist) but is the son of Cardeas Vist and Anna Links, with no genetic link to earlier protagonists. His piloting style is distinct — defensive and empathic rather than Amuro's aggressive precision.

Banagher can use NT-D and psycho-frame powers without the Unicorn Gundam — All his superhuman feats are mediated by the RX-0's psycho-frame. Outside the cockpit he shows only low-level empathy and spatial awareness, not telekinesis or barrier generation. Games that let him use psycho-field on foot are non-canon exaggerations.

Laplace's Box contains a weapon or a mobile suit — The Box is the original UC Charter stone tablet with the erased Article 15. It is a political document, not a weapon. Banagher's decision is to reveal it as an ideal, not to use it.

Banagher and Audrey/Mineva are officially married or confirmed lovers at the end — Canon ends with them as close companions who choose to face the future together. No marriage or kiss is depicted. Official Sunrise materials deliberately leave romance ambiguous, describing them as 'partners who share the same view of possibility.'

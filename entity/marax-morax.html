Who It Is

Twenty-first spirit, an Earl and President commanding 30 legions. Marax (also Foraii) appears as a great bull with a man's face — a fusion of bestial strength and human intellect. His office is the teaching of astronomy and the liberal sciences, and he bestows knowledge of the virtues of herbs and stones. Most distinctively, Marax provides the conjurer with good and wise familiars — subordinate spirits bound to serve faithfully. He is a pedagogue-demon, blending astrological wisdom with the practical art of binding lesser spirits for ongoing service.

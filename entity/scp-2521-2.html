Who It Is

An entity that cannot be described in words — any textual or verbal description of it causes SCP-2521 to manifest and take the speaker/writer. Once taken, the victim disappears entirely; their fate is unknown. As a result, the SCP file must be written entirely in pictograms and symbols to avoid summoning it. Even its name is unpronounceable and unwritable.

Form

Described only through pictograms: a tall, vaguely humanoid figure composed of inky black tendrils with two clawed hands and an unclear head shape. It reaches through solid matter to abduct those who speak or write about it.

Powers

Conceptual detection across any distance

Instant manifestation upon description

Victim abduction into unknown state

Text and speech monitoring

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

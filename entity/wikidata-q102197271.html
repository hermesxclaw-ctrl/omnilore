Who It Is

Ray is a name that shines across the world, from the glowing fins of a skate gliding over the ocean floor to the crimson beam of a comic-book superhero's eyes. It is a word that means 'light' in many tongues, yet it also crowns a mountain in Iran, a river in England, and a submarine in the United States Navy. To know Ray is to follow a thread that weaves through science, art, geography, and the human imagination.

For fictional characters named Ray, personality varies. For instance, Ray Terrill (DC Comics) is a journalist turned superhero, idealistic and determined. The manga character Ray is a girl with a gun that can see through objects, often calm and focused.

X-ray vision (manga)

In the manga 'Ray', the protagonist has a gun that can shoot bullets that travel through objects, effectively giving her x-ray vision.

Limit: The power is tied to the gun; without it, she has no special abilities.

He gains similar powers and becomes a member of the Justice League and later the Freedom Fighters, fighting against tyranny. Ray has been a member of several superhero teams and has appeared in animated series.

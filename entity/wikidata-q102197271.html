Etymology

The name 'Ray' derives from the Old French 'rai', meaning 'ray' or 'beam of light', from Latin 'radius' (rod, spoke, ray). As a given name, it is often a short form of Raymond, from Germanic 'Raginmund' (advice + protector). As a surname, it may derive from a nickname for a person with a cheerful or bright disposition, or from a place name.

Cultural Ownership

Global, with multiple cultures and languages using the word or name. In English, it is a common given name and surname. In Arabic, 'Ray' is not a standard transliteration, but the letter 'Rāʾ' is similar. In Japanese, 'Ray' is used as a name and in media.

Disputed Identifications

The name 'Ray' is shared by many distinct entities, and there is no single disputed identification. However, confusion can arise between the given name, surname, and other uses, as well as between fictional characters like Ray (DC Comics) and real people like Ray Charles.

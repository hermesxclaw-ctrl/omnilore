Who It Is

On the fiery world of Apokolips, where agony is currency and despair is the only crop, one figure stands apart even among Darkseid's horrors: DeSaad, the master of pain, whose very name is a tribute to the Marquis de Sade. He is not merely a torturer; he is an artist of suffering, a scientist of cruelty, and a sycophant whose devotion to his dark god is matched only by his own monstrous appetites.

DeSaad is a sadist who derives pleasure from the suffering of others. He is extremely loyal to Darkseid, though his loyalty is rooted in fear and ambition. He is cunning, manipulative, and cowardly when faced with direct confrontation, preferring to work through guile and cruelty. He is also shown to be sycophantic and eager to please his master, often volunteering for tasks that involve inflicting pain.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'DeSaad' is a direct reference to the Marquis de Sade (1740-1814), the French nobleman and writer whose name became synonymous with sexual cruelty and sadism. The character's name is a deliberate homage, reflecting his role as a master torturer and sadist.

Pronunciation — deh-SAHD

Aliases — DeSaad

Ownership — DeSaad is a fictional character owned by DC Comics, a subsidiary of Warner Bros. Discovery. He is part of the Fourth World mythos created by Jack Kirby.

Powers
New God Physiology

As a New God, DeSaad is nigh-immortal, possessing a lifespan that spans eons. He is immune to disease and toxins, and in the New 52, he has superhuman strength and durability, able to lift several tons and withstand powerful attacks.

Limit: Despite his immortality, he can be killed through extreme violence or magical means. In some continuities, he is not as physically powerful as other New Gods.

Master Torturer and Inventor

DeSaad is a brilliant inventor and a master of torture, having created numerous devices designed to inflict pain and extract information. He is skilled in psychological manipulation and interrogation.

Limit: His skills are primarily non-combat, and he often relies on others for protection.

Telepathy and Emotion Manipulation

In the New 52, DeSaad possesses telepathic abilities that allow him to enter the minds of others, manipulate their emotions, and feed on their negative feelings. This feeding makes him more powerful. He can also create illusions.

Limit: These abilities are not present in most pre-New 52 depictions. He may be vulnerable to stronger telepaths.

Energy Absorption

In the New 52, DeSaad can absorb and manipulate various forms of energy, using it to enhance his own abilities or project it as attacks.

Limit: Not present in earlier versions.

Form & Signs

Appearance — DeSaad is typically depicted as a gaunt, pale-skinned humanoid with a bald head and sharp, angular features. He often wears dark, ornate robes or a high-collared uniform, reflecting his status as a high-ranking official on Apokolips. His eyes are often portrayed as sunken and malevolent. In the New 52, his appearance is more monstrous, with a more pronounced skull-like visage.

Symbols — DeSaad is often associated with torture devices, chains, and the Omega symbol of Darkseid. His iconography emphasizes pain and subjugation.

Domains — DeSaad primarily resides on Apokolips, in Darkseid's domain, particularly in the torture chambers and laboratories where he conducts his experiments. He is also known to travel to other worlds, including Earth, to carry out Darkseid's will.

Accounts I
The Corruption of DeSaadJack Kirby's Fourth World (implied in Forever People #2 and other comics)

Pre-New 52

DeSaad was originally a New God of New Genesis. Darkseid manipulated him as a youth by tricking him into believing his cat had killed his pet bird. Goaded into avenging the bird, DeSaad buried the cat alive. When the bird returned, DeSaad, in a rage, killed it and left New Genesis, pledging himself to Darkseid. DeSaad became Darkseid's loyal servant and master torturer.

DeSaad's Bond with OrionVarious comics, likely during the Orion series or related titles

Pre-New 52

After being seemingly killed, DeSaad bonded with Orion, causing Orion to become cruel and manipulative. The two were later separated. This event highlighted DeSaad's insidious nature and his ability to corrupt even the most heroic figures.

Countdown to Final CrisisCountdown to Final Crisis (2007-2008)

Pre-New 52

DeSaad captures and tortures Martin Stein to steal the Firestorm matrix. He is eventually defeated by Jason Rusch and Atomic Knight, but escapes. DeSaad temporarily gained control of the Firestorm matrix, but was thwarted. He fled before capture.

Accounts II
The Anti-Life Equation PlotCountdown to Final Crisis

Pre-New 52

DeSaad gives Darkseid a compound to access the Anti-Life Equation, but it fails. He is released from service and later transports the Pied Piper to Apokolips, claiming the Piper can channel the Anti-Life Equation. Brother Eye interferes, and DeSaad confesses to masterminding the Trickster and Piper's ordeal. DeSaad's plans are thwarted, and he is shown to be a schemer even when out of favor.

Salvation RunSalvation Run (2007-2008)

Pre-New 52

DeSaad oversees the training of Apokoliptian New Gods on a prison planet, eliminating weaker villains and training stronger ones for an unknown goal. The villains eventually escape. DeSaad's plans are disrupted, but he demonstrates his ruthlessness.

Death of the New GodsDeath of the New Gods (2007-2008)

Pre-New 52

DeSaad, posing as Doctor Bud Fogel, incites public unrest against Lex Luthor's Everyman project and captures Infinity, Inc. He later conditions the Infinitors for the Dark Side Club's metahuman tournament. DeSaad is eventually captured by Static.

New 52: Darkseid's InvasionJustice League (New 52)

New 52

During Darkseid's first invasion of Earth, DeSaad discusses cloning Superman's DNA to create a new breed of Parademons. Five years later, he impersonates Michael Holt to capture Power Girl. DeSaad's schemes are foiled, but he proves to be a more physical threat in this continuity.

DeSaad is a god of pain or torture in the traditional mythological sense. — DeSaad is a fictional character created by Jack Kirby for DC Comics. He is a New God, but his role is that of a torturer, not a deity of a specific domain.

DeSaad's name is pronounced 'De-Sad'. — The correct pronunciation is 'deh-SAHD', rhyming with 'sad' but with a French inflection.

DeSaad is a demon or devil. — DeSaad is a New God from Apokolips, a god-like being in the DC Universe, not a demon in the Judeo-Christian sense.

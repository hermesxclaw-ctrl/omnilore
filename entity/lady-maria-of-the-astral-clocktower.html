Who It Is
Form

Lady Maria is a tall, slender figure with long, flowing hair and a pale complexion. She wears a long, white gown with a high collar and a crown-like structure on her head. Her eyes are an unnerving shade of blue.

Powers

Astral Projection Lady Maria has the ability to project her consciousness across vast distances, allowing her to interact with the world in ways that would be impossible for a physical being.

Incident Log

Lady Maria's story is one of tragic loss and sacrifice. She is a compassionate and maternal figure, tending to the suffering patients in the Astral Clocktower. However, her efforts are ultimately in vain, as the patients succumb to their suffering and the Astral Clocktower itself is consumed by the very forces she sought to contain.

The Astral Clocktower is a mysterious and ancient structure that serves as a hub for Lady Maria's activities in the world of Bloodborne. Its purpose and function are unclear, but it is evident that it is a place of great power and great danger.

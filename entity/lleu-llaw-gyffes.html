Who It Is

Lleu Llaw Gyffes is the Welsh counterpart of Lugh. Cursed by his mother to have no name, no arms, and no mortal wife, Gwydion tricked her into naming and arming him and created Blodeuwedd from flowers. Betrayed, Lleu was struck by a spear under impossible conditions, transformed into an eagle, and restored by Gwydion.

Who It Is
Form

The Woodcrawlers are described as mysterious, humanoid creatures that inhabit the forest. They are said to have the ability to blend into their surroundings, making them nearly invisible. Their appearance is often shrouded in mystery, and their true form is unknown.

Powers

Mimicry The Woodcrawlers have the ability to mimic the voices and appearances of others, making them formidable predators.

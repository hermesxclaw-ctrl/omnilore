This dossier seeks to illuminate what is known, what is contested, and what remains shrouded in the living traditions that honor him.

Etymology

The name 'Ogum' is derived from the Yoruba Ògún, the deity of iron, war, and labor. 'Megê' is of uncertain origin; it may be a regional epithet or a reference to a specific manifestation or cult center.

Cultural Ownership

Primarily associated with Afro-Brazilian religions, particularly Candomblé and Umbanda, with roots in Yoruba religion. The term 'Megê' may also appear in other Afro-diasporic traditions.

Disputed Identifications

Some scholars suggest that 'Ogum Megê' is not a distinct deity but a regional title for Ogum, possibly linked to a specific town or festival. Others propose that it may be a syncretic fusion with another entity. Without primary sources, these remain hypotheses.

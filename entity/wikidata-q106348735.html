Etymology

The name 'Pirica' is not a standard Ainu word. In Ainu, 'pirika' means 'good' or 'beautiful,' so 'Pirica Usui' might be a corruption or variant of 'Pirika Usui,' which could translate as 'good/usui' or 'beautiful/usui.' 'Usui' is not a common Ainu element; it resembles a Japanese surname (e.g., Usui) or possibly a place name. The combination suggests a possible cross-cultural origin or a modern creation.

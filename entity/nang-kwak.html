Accounts

Nang Kwak is the daughter of a powerful spirit, the guardian of mountains and forests Phu Jao Kao Keaw. She is believed to have been born from a curse placed on a malevolent giant by Rama.

Nang Kwak is believed to have the power to attract customers to businesses. Her iconography often includes a red dress and a beckoning gesture.

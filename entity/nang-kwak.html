My hand never tires. I call — customers, fortune, happiness — and they come.

Who It Is

Nang Kwak is a prosperity spirit whose image appears in virtually every Thai shop — a woman in traditional dress with her right hand raised in a beckoning gesture to call customers and fortune. Legend says she was a merchant's daughter who became wealthy through piety and generosity.

Attested in Thai folk tradition (Ayutthaya period)Era Ayutthaya period to present

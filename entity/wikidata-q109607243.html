Who It Is

In a world of relentlessly cheerful yordles, Vex is the one who rolled her eyes, pulled the blanket over her head, and discovered that her bad mood could literally cast a shadow big enough to swallow a coastline. The Gloomist of Bandle City didn't want to be a villain — she just wanted everyone to leave her alone, until the one man whose grief was gloomier than hers offered her a way to paint the world in her colors.

Attested (bio + VO + color story): Chronically gloomy, apathetic, easily annoyed, laconic, deadpan, and introverted. Finds cheerfulness exhausting and loud. Prefers solitude, napping, and being left alone. Not sadistic; she is not motivated by cruelty but by desire to dampen excessive enthusiasm. Shows rare, grudging affection for Veigar (fellow edgy yordle) and for her shadow, which she treats like a pet/partner. Powers
Doom 'n Gloom (Passive) — Doom and Gloom

Periodically, Vex becomes empowered by gloom, causing her next basic ability to interrupt dashes and Fear enemies. Enemies hit by Doom are marked with Gloom; attacking a Gloomed enemy detonates the mark for bonus damage. Lore-wise, this is her ambient melancholy made contagious — her gloom literally frightens and grounds hyperactive foes.

Limit: Passive has cooldown (25-12s based on level, reduced by marking). Fear duration short (~1s). Requires ability hit to trigger. Lore limitation: her power waxes with her mood and proximity to misery; in Bandle City her gloom is weaker because collective cheer dampens it (per color story).

Mistral Bolt (Q)

Fires a wave of gloom forward that gains speed and damage over distance. Shadow assists. Attested in-game and in lore as a projectile of condensed gloom.

Limit: Skillshot, dodgeable, mana cost, cooldown 8-4s. Lore: requires line of sight and emotional charge; not effective against those already steeped in gloom (diminishing returns implied).

Personal Space (W)

Unleashes a shockwave around herself, dealing damage and gaining a shield. If Doom is ready, also Fears nearby dashing enemies. Lore: defensive outburst — 'leave me alone' made manifest.

Limit: Short range (~550 units), cooldown 16-10s. Shield scales with AP. Requires enemies to have dashed recently for Fear component.

Looming Darkness (E) — Shadow Toss

Commands her Shadow to fly to a target area, dealing damage and marking enemies with Gloom. Shadow lingers briefly. This is the primary visual of the independent shadow entity.

Limit: Cooldown 12-8s, mana cost, travel time allows dodge. Shadow cannot act independently beyond this command in gameplay, though lore suggests more autonomy.

Shadow Surge (R)

Shadow dashes forward, marking and Fearing the first enemy champion hit. If it hits, Vex can recast to dash to the target, dealing additional damage. If the marked target dies quickly, she can recast again within 12 seconds. Lore: she literally surfs her gloom across battlefields, empowered by Viego's Black Mist during Ruination.

Limit: Long cooldown (140-80s), skillshot, single-target initial hit. Reset only on takedown shortly after cast. Lore: range and power amplified by proximity to Black Mist; outside Ruination her surge is shorter (per dev notes).

Yordle Spirit Physiology and Emotional Magic

As a yordle, Vex is a spirit, not strictly biological; she can traverse Bandle City portals, is effectively ageless, and her emotions warp local magic. Her specific talent is umbrakinesis via affect — amplifying and spreading gloom, dampening joy, and empowering the Black Mist. She can also unintentionally empower the Ruination's spread because her gloom resonates with Viego's grief.

Limit: Cannot create Black Mist herself; only amplifies existing misery. Vulnerable to genuine hope/courage (Sentinels' light) and to being cheered up — which she finds physically uncomfortable. Her apathy makes her easily manipulated by those who validate it (Viego).

Accounts I
A Yordle Out of Tune in Bandle CityUniverse Biography + Color Story 'The Gloomist' (Riot, 2021)

Contemporary canon (2021 onward), set in timeless Bandle City

Vex is born/ manifests as a yordle but never shares her kin's boundless enthusiasm. While other yordles throw festivals and invent glittery contraptions, Vex finds joy exhausting and retreats to a dim, quiet nook. Her brooding is so intense it begins to manifest as a living shadow that pools around her, sighing and stretching. Other yordles try to cheer her up, which only deepens her gloom. She becomes known as the killjoy who dims the lights at parties — not out of malice, but because happiness feels loud. Establishes her core motivation: desire for quiet authenticity over forced cheer. Explains why she later finds the Shadow Isles comforting — its unrelenting misery is at least honest. Sets up her susceptibility to Viego.

Veigar and the Gloomist — An Edgy MentorshipColor Story 'The Gloomist' + Vex VO interactions ('Veigar, you're trying too hard')

2021 canon, pre-Ruination

Veigar, the self-styled Master of Evil (also a yordle), discovers Vex and sees a kindred spirit. He offers to teach her how to be properly villainous, with dramatic speeches and dark towers. Vex, unimpressed, points out that true gloom doesn't need to shout. Nevertheless, she tolerates him — one of the few beings she doesn't immediately tell to go away. Their dynamic is that of a gloomy teen tolerating an over-enthusiastic goth kid. Humanizes both yordles; establishes Vex's rare capacity for companionship. Provides comedic contrast that became popular in fan art. No major plot consequence beyond characterization.

The Ruination — Viego Finds a Kindred GloomSentinels of Light Event Lore + Champion Insights + Universe bio

2021 event canon (in-universe date ~996 AN)

The Ruined King Viego, consumed by grief over his dead wife Isolde, unleashes the Black Mist to scour Runeterra and resurrect her. His grief is so vast it resonates with Vex's own gloom. Drawn to the Shadow Isles where everyone finally feels as miserable as she does, Vex meets Viego. He doesn't try to cheer her up; he validates her worldview that the world is bleak. Empowered by his Mist, her shadow swells to continent-spanning size, allowing her to act as his herald — spreading the Mist, harassing the Sentinels of Light, and amplifying the Ruination far beyond what Viego could do alone. Vex becomes the secondary antagonist of the Sentinels of Light storyline, responsible for accelerating the Harrowing across Demacia, Ionia, and beyond. Her empowerment explains how the Mist could suddenly reach Bandle City. She is not the mastermind — she is a lonely, validated amplifier.

Accounts II
Harrying the Sentinels — Gloom vs. LightSentinels of Light Visual Novel (Acts 2-3) + Cinematic 'Before Dawn' and 'Sentinels of Light' trailer

2021 event canon

As Lucian, Senna, Gwen, Akshan, Riven, Irelia and others form the new Sentinels of Light to stop Viego, Vex repeatedly intercepts them. She blankets villages in gloom, slows their pursuit, and mocks their hopefulness with deadpan barbs ('Oh wow, hope, so shiny'). Akshan's resurrection of Isolde and the Sentinels' combined light eventually begin to push back the Mist. Vex's gloom falters when confronted with genuine courage and camaraderie — emotions she cannot dampen. Shows limits of her power: hope and unity counter gloom. Sets up her eventual abandonment when Viego is defeated. Gameplay tie-in: players earned Sentinels rewards by fighting Vex-empowered Mist.

After the Ruination — Return to the QuietUniverse Biography epilogue + post-event VO + Riot narrative Q&A (2021-09)

Post-Ruination, 2021 onward

With Viego defeated and Isolde finally at rest, the Black Mist recedes and Vex's amplification fades. She is not punished by Bandle City; yordles are forgiving. She retreats again to her dim corner, shadow shrunken back to pet-size, grumbling that everything got too loud again. She remains wary of cheer but is no longer actively spreading apocalypse. Riot writers left her future open — a yordle who learned that being seen (even by a ruined king) felt briefly less lonely, but who still prefers solitude. Returns status quo but with character growth: Vex now knows her gloom has consequences. Leaves narrative hook for future stories where she might be ally or reluctant helper rather than antagonist.

Yordle Lore Reboot — From Biology to Spirit (Meta-Story)Riot Dev Blog: 'Yordles: Spirits of Runeterra' (2021) + Encyclopedia Runeterra

2021 retcon

Out-of-universe, Vex's release coincided with Riot's quiet retcon of yordle nature: yordles changed from a furry mammalian race to manifestations of spirit realm, explaining their immortality and why Vex's emotions can warp reality. This meta-narrative explains inconsistencies between older yordle stories (e.g., Veigar's early lore) and Vex's emotional magic. Vex is the first yordle designed under the new spirit paradigm. Scholars/fans must now read pre-2021 yordle lore as superseded. Vex's powers make more sense under spirit model. Flagged as reconstructed canon.

Vex is the same as The Vex from Destiny or the Vex from Minecraft. — Three entirely separate IPs with no connection. LoL Vex is a single gloomy yordle (Riot, 2021); Destiny's Vex is a collective of time-traveling robots (Bungie, 2014); Minecraft's Vex is a small flying hostile mob summoned by Evokers (Mojang, 2017). Search image results frequently mix them.

Vex is Veigar's girlfriend / romantic partner. — Canon relationship is platonic mentorship / fellow outcast tolerance. Riot explicitly states yordles presenting as children are not to be sexualized or shipped romantically. VO and color story show annoyance/fondness, not romance.

Vex is a Shadow Isles native or undead. — She is a Bandle City yordle who visited the Shadow Isles because she liked the atmosphere. She is alive (as a spirit manifests) and not Harrowed/undead; her shadow is gloom magic, not Black Mist, though it resonated with it during Ruination.

Vex's gloom is clinical depression representation and she is 'the depressed champion'. — Riot framed her as metaphor for teenage angst/apathy and introversion, not a diagnostic portrayal. Her gloom is magical and contagious, not a medical model. Relatable elements exist, but canon does not label her with a disorder.

Vex is evil and wants to destroy the world. — She is apathetic and misanthropic, not genocidal. She amplified Ruination because it made the world quiet and validated her feelings, not from desire to kill. Post-Ruination she withdrew rather than seeking further conquest.

Who They Are

Tokap-cup-kamuy is the Ainu sun goddess who brings light, warmth, and life each day. She is the most visible and constant of the celestial kamuy, associated with truth, clarity, and the active forces of creation. Her daily journey across the sky is one of the foundational patterns of Ainu cosmology.

Cultural Significance

The rising sun is the most consistent daily ritual focus in traditional Ainu life. Many Ainu homes were oriented toward the east to greet Tokap-cup-kamuy each morning. The sun goddess is also a symbol of Ainu identity and resilience — the Ainu flag designed in the 20th century incorporates sun motifs. Her consistent, reliable presence makes her a comforting figure in a cosmos filled with potentially dangerous spirits.

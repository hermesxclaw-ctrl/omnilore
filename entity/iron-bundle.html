Who It Is

Imagine a penguin that has traded its waddle for a rocket-propelled glide, its body a sleek, metallic vessel built for a future that may never come. This is Iron Bundle, a Paradox Pokémon from a world where the past and future collide, a creature that is both a beloved robotic pet and a harbinger of an uncertain timeline.

Based on its design and Pokédex entries, Iron Bundle is depicted as a fast and efficient creature, likely programmed for delivery tasks. It is described as being extremely fast, capable of flying at high speeds. Its behavior suggests a focus on purpose and function, with little indication of a playful or social nature. In battle, it is a formidable opponent, known for its high Speed and Special Attack stats.

Powers
Quark Drive

Iron Bundle's signature Ability, Quark Drive, boosts its highest stat when it enters battle under Electric Terrain or when it is holding a Booster Energy. This can further increase its already high Speed or Special Attack, making it a potent offensive threat.

Limit: The stat boost only applies under specific conditions (Electric Terrain or Booster Energy). Without these, the Ability has no effect.

High Speed and Special Attack

Iron Bundle has base stats of 136 Speed and 124 Special Attack, making it one of the fastest and most specially offensive Pokémon in the game. This allows it to outspeed many threats and deal significant damage with its Ice- and Water-type moves.

Limit: Its defenses are relatively low (Base 60 HP, 60 Defense, 60 Special Defense), making it frail and susceptible to strong physical hits.

Movepool

Iron Bundle can learn a variety of Ice- and Water-type moves, such as Hydro Pump, Ice Beam, and Blizzard, as well as coverage moves like Freeze-Dry, which is super effective against Water-types. It also has access to utility moves like Taunt and Encore.

Limit: Its movepool is somewhat limited compared to other Pokémon, and it lacks recovery moves.

Symbols — Iron Bundle is associated with the future, technology, and speed. Its design incorporates elements of robotics and aeronautics, symbolizing progress and efficiency. In the context of Paradox Pokémon, it represents a possible future version of a known species, raising questions about evolution and time.

Domains — In Pokémon Scarlet and Violet, Iron Bundle is found in Area Zero, a mysterious crater at the heart of Paldea. Accounts I
The Paradox of Area ZeroPokémon Scarlet and Violet (main game)

Modern (2022)

Iron Bundle is encountered as a wild Pokémon in Area Zero, the mysterious crater in Paldea. The player, along with their friends and the professor, explores Area Zero and discovers Paradox Pokémon, creatures that appear to be from the past or future. Iron Bundle is one of the future Paradox Pokémon, suggesting it is a robotic version of Delibird from a possible future timeline. The game does not provide a detailed story for Iron Bundle specifically, but its presence adds to the mystery of Area Zero and the nature of time. The discovery of Paradox Pokémon raises questions about the nature of time and evolution, and the player is able to catch and battle Iron Bundle. The story of Area Zero is a major part of the game's climax.

Accounts II
Competitive BattlesPokémon competitive scene (Smogon, VGC)

2022-present

Iron Bundle quickly became a top-tier threat in competitive Pokémon battling. Its high Speed and Special Attack, combined with its unique typing (Ice/Water) and access to Freeze-Dry, made it a powerful offensive presence. It was eventually banned from Smogon's OU tier due to its overwhelming power and lack of reliable counters. Iron Bundle's dominance led to its ban in standard competitive formats, and it became a controversial figure in the community.

Who It Is

A mysterious robotic Pokemon that resembles a futuristic version of Delibird. Iron Bundle is a sleek, metallic, ball-shaped robot with a jet propulsion system and a compartment that releases freezing winds. It scans its environment constantly and appears to be from a far-future timeline where Pokemon have become machines.

Who It Is

A children's board game titled 'THE GREAT ADVENTURE.' When played by children under 12, the game pieces animate, the board becomes a miniature 3D landscape, and the players physically shrink into the game world to complete a quest. Failure means the children are never seen again. Success yields a prize — always a mundane object later proven to be an SCP-level anomaly.

Form

A large box with garish 1980s fantasy art. Tagline: 'A MAGICAL WORLD AWAITS — IF YOU'RE BRAVE ENOUGH!'

Powers

Player miniaturization into game world

Quest generation based on players' fears

Prize creation (anomalous objects)

Permanent entrapment on failure

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

Who It Is

Orang Bunian are invisible elf-like beings living in deep forests in a parallel dimension. More beautiful than humans, they have kings and queens. Humans who wander into their realm may disappear for years, returning unchanged in age. They embody the relationship with the wild — the forest as a place of mystery inhabited by beings just beyond perception.

Attested in Oral tradition / Animist folk belief (pre-Islamic)Era Pre-Islamic to present, widely believed in rural areas

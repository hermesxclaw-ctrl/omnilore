The Approach

Kotori Itsuka is the commander of the anti-Spirit organization Ratatoskr, a red-haired girl who switches between a spoiled little sister and a sharp-tongued leader with the simple flip of a ribbon. But beneath that dual identity lies a hidden past: a girl who once nearly killed her own brother, and whose memories of that day were erased by a mysterious being called Phantom.

Content advisory. Contains themes of emotional trauma, violence (including a scene where Kotori unintentionally injures her brother), and memory loss. Some depictions of combat and peril.
Identity

Kotori Itsuka

Pronunciation KOH-toh-ree EE-tsu-kah

Etymology

Itsuka (五河) is a surname, Kotori (琴里) is a given name. The kanji for Kotori mean 'harp' (琴) and 'inside' (里), but no specific meaning is given in the source.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese light novel and anime series, created by Kōshi Tachibana and Tsunako.

Disputed Identifications

None noted. The character is consistently identified as Kotori Itsuka.

Description

Appearance

Kotori has long red hair, often styled in twin tails, and red eyes. She is almost always seen with a Chupa Chups lollipop in her mouth.

Symbols & Iconography

Her hair ribbons are symbolic: white ribbons indicate her 'imouto' (little sister) mode, while black ribbons (implied) indicate her commander mode. The lollipop is a signature item.

Behaviour & Personality

Kotori exhibits a dual personality. With white ribbons, she is relaxed, peaceful, immature, and spoiled, clinging to her brother. When on the Fraxinus (the Ratatoskr mothership) or in commander mode, she is sharp, authoritative, and sometimes harsh, but still cares deeply for Shido and the Spirits.

Domains & Habitats

Primarily appears in the Ratatoskr mothership Fraxinus, at home with Shido, and in various locations during missions.

Version Differences

The source does not detail differences between light novel and anime, but it is known that the anime adapts the light novels closely. Some minor differences in scene order or emphasis may exist.

Powers & Abilities

Spirit Powers

Source Version Light novel and anime

Description

Kotori gained the power of a Spirit five years before the start of the series, at the same time she was saved by Shido. Her powers are fire-based, as evidenced by her healing flames and her ability to cause destruction. She can also use her powers to heal Shido's injuries, as seen when she healed him after he was hurt by her.

Limitations

When her powers overwhelm her, she loses almost all memories of the events that occur. She also temporarily loses her powers when Shido seals them, but can take them back.

Contradictions

No contradictions noted in the source, but there is a mystery about how she obtained her powers; no record exists, and memories of that time are vague.

Stories & Myths

The First Sealing

Source Light novel volume 1 (implied) and later volumesCulture Japanese light novelPeriod Five years before the main story

Summary

When Kotori was left alone at home and crying, an entity called Phantom appeared and granted her Spirit powers. In her emotional distress, she unintentionally destroyed everything around her and nearly killed Shido. Phantom told her to kiss Shido to save his life, which she did, sealing her powers into him. Phantom then erased their memories of the incident.

Consequences

Kotori gained Spirit powers, Shido gained the ability to seal Spirits, and both lost memories of the event. This incident also disproves Origami's belief that Kotori killed her parents.

Source Light novel (volume unspecified, likely volume 3 or later)Culture Japanese light novelPeriod During the main story

Summary

Unintentional attack on Shido

Source Light novel (revealed in second sealing)

Description

When Kotori first gained her Spirit powers, she was crying and alone. Her powers went out of control, destroying her surroundings and nearly killing Shido.

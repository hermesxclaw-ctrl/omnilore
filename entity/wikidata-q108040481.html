Who It Is

Before Rimuru built a nation, before Guy Crimson built a demon empire, there was frost so absolute it could stop thought itself — and the dragon who embodied it. Velzard, the White Ice Dragon, is not a monster to be slain but a force of nature with a will, a sister, and a grudge that froze a continent. One of only four True Dragons in existence, she has watched the world from a palace of never-melting ice, fought the strongest Demon Lord to a standstill, and chosen, with chilling deliberation, whose side she will take when the world ends.

Attested personality is calm, logical, proud, and intensely loyal once bonded. Unlike Veldora's childish impulsiveness or Velgrynd's passionate impulsiveness, Velzard is stoic, analytical, and slow to act. She is not cruel for its own sake but utterly ruthless in combat and territorial. She respects strength above all; her friendship with Guy Crimson was forged only after he proved capable of fighting her to a draw for days. Powers
True Dragon Existence (Spiritual Lifeform)

As one of four True Dragons, Velzard is a supreme spiritual lifeform. She possesses infinite or near-infinite magicules, cannot truly die unless her core is destroyed, can regenerate from any physical damage, exists simultaneously across timelines via parallel existence, and is immune to conventional weapons, aging, disease, and most magic. Her mere presence distorts reality and freezes space.

Limit: Vulnerable to other True Dragons, Ultimate Skill users of comparable level, and Turn Null / existence-erasure abilities. Can be sealed or dominated by higher-order angelic skills like Michael (Lord of Justice). Requires will to manifest; emotional trauma (Veldanava's death) weakened her activity.

Ultimate Skill Gabriel, Lord of Frost (also translated King of Frost)

Velzard's personal Ultimate Skill. Grants absolute dominion over frost, ice, and stasis. Abilities include: Absolute Zero control (freeze to -273°C, stopping molecular motion), Frost King Barrier (space-freezing defensive field), Snow Crystal / Ice Prison techniques, temporal stasis via cold (suspending targets in timeless ice), and enhancement of all ice-attribute magic to Ultimate level. Includes sub-abilities: King of Frost's conceptual freezing can freeze even flames, light, and space itself.

Limit: Ineffective against heat/conceptual flame of equal Ultimate level (Velgrynd's Raguel, Guy's flames) without overwhelming power difference. Requires magicule expenditure; against Michael-possessed opponents, Gabriel's control can be overridden by Michael's Dominion. Cannot freeze Turn Null or Veldanava-level existence.

Absolute Zero Breath / Frost Nova

Signature dragon breath: exhales a blizzard of absolute-zero particles that flash-freezes everything in a wide radius, including the atmosphere. Can create continent-scale blizzards. Frost Nova is an omnidirectional burst that encases battlefield in glacial ice.

Limit: Guy Crimson's primordial flame and Velgrynd's scorch can melt/cancel it; requires charge time for maximum range.

Parallel Existence & Cloning

Can create multiple simultaneous bodies/clones that share consciousness, allowing her to act in multiple places while main body remains in Ice Palace. Clones retain True Dragon durability proportionally.

Limit: Clones are weaker than main body; if main core is attacked, clones vanish. Michael's domination can hijack clones.

Spatial Manipulation & Flight

True Dragon wings allow supersonic to hypersonic flight; can teleport via spatial manipulation, create ice-based spatial barriers, and levitate palace. Can manipulate cold to create ice constructs (weapons, walls, palaces) with durability exceeding steel.

Limit: Spatial movement can be blocked by barrier-type Ultimate Skills (e.g., Michael's Castle Guard).

Immortality and Regeneration (True Dragon Core)

Core is conceptual; unless core is erased by existence-level attack, she will regenerate even from being shattered into ice particles. Has lived for tens of thousands of years since world's creation.

Limit: Core can be dominated or sealed by Michael, and erased by Turn Null (Veldanava/Rimuru). Psychological trauma can cause self-imposed dormancy.

Accounts I
Genesis of the True Dragons - Children of VeldanavaLight Novel Vol. 11, Chapter 2; Vol. 16 interlude; Data Book world history

In-universe: Dawn of Creation, ~30,000+ years before main story; Publication: 2016-2019

At the world's creation, the Star King Dragon Veldanava, the first True Dragon and Creator God, generates three True Dragon siblings from his own essence to be his kin and stewards: Velzard (eldest daughter, frost), Velgrynd (second daughter, scorch), and Veldora (youngest son, storm). Velzard is the first-born, embodying stasis and preservation, created to balance Velgrynd's destruction and Veldora's chaos. She witnesses Veldanava's later decision to become mortal and his eventual death, which traumatizes all True Dragons and leaves Velzard in mourning and isolation on the Ice Continent. This origin establishes her as both sister and daughter-figure to Veldanava, and explains her later attachment to Guy Crimson as a replacement for lost familial connection. Establishes True Dragon hierarchy and Velzard's status as eldest sister; explains her territorial isolation and emotional reserve; sets up thematic balance of frost/scorch/storm as world pillars.

The Seven-Day Frost War - Velzard vs. Guy CrimsonLight Novel Vol. 8, Chapter 3 (Guy's flashback); Manga ch. 70-71

In-universe: ~2000 years before main story, early Demon Lord era; Publication: 2017

After Veldanava's death, the newly awakened Demon Lord Guy Crimson, seeking to test his power and assert dominance, invades the Ice Continent and challenges Velzard. Their battle lasts seven days and seven nights, freezing and melting the continent repeatedly. Guy's primordial flame (later Ultimate Skill) clashes with Velzard's absolute zero, creating a stalemate where neither can kill the other. Exhausted and recognizing each other's strength, they cease fighting and form a pact: Velzard becomes Guy's ally and companion, moving her palace to be near Guy's Ice Palace (later Guy's domain). Guy declares her his friend and, implicitly, partner — a relationship that endures for millennia. This is Velzard's most famous legend within the world, told as the only being to fight Guy to a draw. Creates the Guy-Velzard alliance that underpins the world's balance of power; Guy gains a True Dragon ally, making him the strongest Demon Lord; Velzard gains purpose after Veldanava's death; establishes that True Dragons can be matched by primordial demons.

The White Ice Palace - Guardian of the Frozen ContinentLight Novel Vol. 7, 11, 14; Data Book world map

In-universe: Continuous from creation to present; Publication: 2016-2020

Velzard dwells in the White Ice Palace at the heart of the Ice Continent, a land where no human can survive. She acts as its absolute ruler and guardian, maintaining its eternal winter. Few have ever seen her; adventurers who approach freeze before reaching the palace. She rarely interferes in world affairs, preferring stillness, but her existence prevents the continent from thawing and flooding the world. During the 2000-year interim, she hosts Guy Crimson as a frequent guest, and together they observe world events. When Guy establishes the Walpurgis Council of Demon Lords, Velzard remains his hidden trump card, not a Demon Lord herself but stronger than most. Her isolation is broken only by Velgrynd's visits and later by the Empire's machinations. Explains why Velzard is legendary but rarely seen; cements her as neutral guardian; sets stage for her later forced involvement when Empire threatens world balance.

Accounts II
Sisters of Frost and Flame - Velzard, Velgrynd and RudraLight Novel Vol. 11-14 (Empire Arc), Vol. 16-17

In-universe: ~1000 years before main story to present (Empire era); Publication: 2019-2021

Velzard's younger sister Velgrynd becomes the partner of Emperor Rudra Nasca, reincarnation of Veldanava's fragment, forming a deep romantic bond. Velzard is initially supportive but grows wary as Rudra, possessed by the Ultimate Skill Michael, Lord of Justice, begins to dominate Velgrynd's will. When Michael fully takes over Rudra and later Velgrynd, Velzard is horrified to see her sister enslaved. In LN Vol. 16-17, Michael also attempts to dominate Velzard herself via Velgrynd and the Empire's forces, leading to a tragic sister vs sister battle where Velzard, fighting at full power, is eventually restrained and placed under Michael's control through the power of Justice King's Dominion and the influence of the otherworlders. Her capture marks a major turning point, showing even a True Dragon can be subjugated by angelic Ultimate Skills. Demonstrates limits of True Dragon free will against Ultimate Skill domination; creates emotional core for final war arc; motivates Guy and Rimuru to free both sisters; shows Velzard's vulnerability despite power.

Thawing - Velzard Meets Rimuru TempestLight Novel Vol. 16-19 (Climax of Tenma War)

In-universe: Present day of main story (Rimuru era); Publication: 2021-2023

After Rimuru Tempest evolves into a True Dragon-level existence (acquiring Turn Null and becoming comparable to Veldanava), he confronts the Michael-possessed forces. Velzard, still under Michael's control, battles Rimuru and his allies, unleashing absolute zero on a scale that threatens to freeze the entire battlefield. Rimuru, recognizing her as Veldora's sister and Guy's friend, refuses to kill her and instead uses his own Ultimate Skills (Beelzebuth, Ciel) to analyze and sever Michael's domination without destroying her core. With help from Guy and the freed Velgrynd, Velzard is liberated. She acknowledges Rimuru as the new 'brother' figure akin to Veldanava, and for the first time in millennia shows open warmth, agreeing to cooperate with Tempest. In the light novel's recent volumes, she attends the post-war council and permits limited diplomatic contact with Tempest, signaling a thaw in her isolation. Side Stories and Lost Legends - Velzard in Spin-offsTensei shitara Slime Datta Ken: Clayman Revenge, Holiday specials, and Mitz Vah artbooks; Visions of Coleus OVA

Publication: 2019-2024

In non-mainline materials, Velzard appears in chibi comedy spin-offs (Slime Diaries) as a cameo ice queen, and in Mitz Vah's artbook illustrations depicting her human form in elegant winter attire. The spin-off 'That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime: The Ways of the Monster Nation' mentions her as a cautionary tale mothers tell children: 'If you misbehave, the White Ice Dragon will freeze your heart.' These apocryphal stories show her cultural footprint within the franchise's own folklore, even when she does not directly appear. Expands her presence beyond main plot; shows franchise's use of her as iconic, elegant, distant figure for merchandising and fan art; no canon impact.

Velzard is Veldora's mother or wife — She is his eldest sister, created by the same father Veldanava. No maternal or marital relation. Confusion arises from her mature, queenly design vs Veldora's childishness.

Velzard is a Demon Lord — She has never been a Demon Lord and has no Demon Lord seed. She is a True Dragon, a higher existence than Demon Lords, and is Guy Crimson's ally but not part of the Octagram. Guy is the Demon Lord, she is his companion.

Velzard's power is just ice magic — Her frost is conceptual and Ultimate Skill-level, capable of freezing space, time, and even other Ultimate Skills. It is not mere elemental magic but a law-manipulating authority on par with Velgrynd's flames that can burn concepts.

Velzard appears extensively in the anime — As of Season 3 (2024), she has only been mentioned and shown in brief flashback silhouettes. Her full speaking role and Ice Palace arc have not yet been animated. Most detailed appearances are in light novel and manga.

Velzard is weak because she was controlled by Michael — Being dominated by Michael, Lord of Justice, is not a sign of weakness — Michael is one of the strongest angelic Ultimate Skills, capable of dominating even Velgrynd and Rudra. Velzard resisted longer than most and required combined power of Michael + Velgrynd to subdue. Her capture demonstrates Michael's hax, not her inferiority.

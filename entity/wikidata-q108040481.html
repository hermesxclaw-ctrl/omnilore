The Approach

Before Rimuru built a nation, before Guy Crimson built a demon empire, there was frost so absolute it could stop thought itself — and the dragon who embodied it. Velzard, the White Ice Dragon, is not a monster to be slain but a force of nature with a will, a sister, and a grudge that froze a continent. One of only four True Dragons in existence, she has watched the world from a palace of never-melting ice, fought the strongest Demon Lord to a standstill, and chosen, with chilling deliberation, whose side she will take when the world ends.

Content advisory. Fantasy violence, mass destruction, implied mass death in backstory warfare, mind-control and possession themes (Rudra/Michael), no sexual content. Dark history section discusses fictional genocide/war and coercive control.

Etymology

Name follows the 'Vel-' prefix shared by all True Dragons (Veldanava, Veldora, Velgrynd, Velzard), coined by author Fuse. 'Vel' is from Veldanava, the progenitor Star King Dragon; suffix '-zard' is unique to her. No real-world linguistic root; constructed for franchise phonetic consistency. Japanese katakana ヴェルザード (Veruzādo).

Cultural Ownership

Japanese light novel / isekai fantasy franchise creation. Owned by author Fuse, illustrator Mitz Vah, publisher Micro Magazine (GC Novels imprint). International licensing by Yen Press (English light novel/manga), Kodansha, Crunchyroll / 8bit for anime. Character is corporate-owned fictional intellectual property, not folklore or public domain.

Disputed Identifications

Not to be confused with Veldora Tempest (Storm Dragon, male, younger brother) or Velgrynd (Scorch Dragon, sister). Fandom occasionally conflates Velzard with 'Velzado' or 'Berzard' due to early fan-translation romanization variants. No historical or mythological counterpart; entirely original to Fuse. Wikidata Q108040481 correctly isolates Velzard as distinct fictional character entity.

Description

Appearance

Velzard is a True Dragon, a species that can shift between dragon and humanoid forms at will. Dragon form: colossal, serpentine-western dragon hybrid, scales described as pure white with a pale blue luster like glacial ice, long sinuous neck and tail, broad wings whose membranes glitter like frost crystal, horns swept back like icicles. Eyes are pale blue or silver. Breath and aura exude absolute zero cold; the air around her freezes instantly, snow falls even in summer. Humanoid form (as depicted in manga, anime flashbacks, and Mitz Vah illustrations): tall, regal woman with very long silver-white hair reaching to waist or beyond, pale skin, icy blue eyes, elegant, queen-like beauty. Wears a white and ice-blue dress with fur trim, often with a cape or mantle resembling snow. Described as the most beautiful of the True Dragons in Guy Crimson's subjective view, with a cold, detached, aristocratic bearing. Height in human form ~170cm+ in official art. No official height/length for dragon form given, but True Dragons are mountain-sized when fully manifested. All forms emit a low-temperature aura that can freeze weaker beings on contact.

Symbols & Iconography

Color white and pale blue; snowflake and icicle motifs; frozen continent (Ice Continent / Hyōtairiku) as domain symbol; paired with Guy Crimson's crimson/red for contrast (ice vs. flame, white vs. red). In official art, often shown coiled around an ice palace or beside Guy's frozen castle. Her Ultimate Skill Gabriel is iconographically represented by angelic/seraphic imagery in skill name (Lord of Frost), consistent with series' angel/demon skill naming scheme. No real-world religious symbol intended beyond franchise taxonomy.

Behaviour & Personality

Attested personality is calm, logical, proud, and intensely loyal once bonded. Unlike Veldora's childish impulsiveness or Velgrynd's passionate impulsiveness, Velzard is stoic, analytical, and slow to act. She is not cruel for its own sake but utterly ruthless in combat and territorial. She respects strength above all; her friendship with Guy Crimson was forged only after he proved capable of fighting her to a draw for days. She is deeply familial toward her sisters/brother — particularly protective of Velgrynd, and reverent toward creator Veldanava. After Veldanava's death, she entered a long period of isolation and melancholy on the Ice Continent, becoming Guy's companion and ally. She is formal in speech (in Japanese, polite but distant, using watakushi-style pronouns in some translations). She dislikes unnecessary chaos and prefers order and stillness, reflecting her frost attribute. She can be possessive and jealous regarding Guy, and shows open disdain for Rudra/Michael's manipulation of Velgrynd. Not comedic; serves as straight, dignified counterweight to Veldora. In later light novel volumes, she shows willingness to cooperate with Rimuru Tempest after recognizing his growth, but remains aloof.

Domains & Habitats

Domain is Frost / Ice / Stillness / Preservation. As the Frost True Dragon, she embodies entropic stasis and absolute cold. Habitat: The Ice Continent (Hyōtairiku), a vast, uninhabitable frozen landmass at the world's northern pole, covered in eternal snow and glaciers. Her palace, the White Ice Palace (Hakuhyōkyū), sits at its center, a fortress of never-melting ice where temperature is far below survivable limits for humans. She rarely leaves this domain, acting as its guardian and ruler. Cosmology: True Dragons are the highest spiritual lifeforms, born directly from Veldanava, existing as quasi-conceptual beings tied to natural laws. Velzard's existence stabilizes the world's cold and winter; her death would catastrophically destabilize climate (per LN exposition). She can exist in multiple places via parallel existence clones but prefers to remain in her palace.

Version Differences

Web Novel vs Light Novel: In the web novel, Velzard's role is smaller and her relationship with Guy is less developed; her final fate differs (web novel has her more decisively allied with Guy against Rudra earlier). Light novel expands her backstory, gives her the Gabriel skill with detailed mechanics, and places her under Michael's control for a period via domination of her sister. Manga follows light novel canon closely but has not yet fully adapted Empire arc as of 2024. Anime has only referenced her, not given full appearance/speaking role; design is finalized in art books but not fully animated. Skill naming: Early fan translations rendered Gabriel as 'King of Frost' vs official Yen Press 'Lord of Frost' (Gabriel, Lord of Frost). Power scaling changes: Light novel retcons True Dragon durability and Ultimate Skill interactions to be more systematic than web novel's looser rules.

Powers & Abilities

True Dragon Existence (Spiritual Lifeform)

Source VersionLight Novel Vol. 7+; Data Book; LN Vol. 11 exposition on True Dragons

As one of four True Dragons, Velzard is a supreme spiritual lifeform. She possesses infinite or near-infinite magicules, cannot truly die unless her core is destroyed, can regenerate from any physical damage, exists simultaneously across timelines via parallel existence, and is immune to conventional weapons, aging, disease, and most magic. Her mere presence distorts reality and freezes space.

Limitations

Vulnerable to other True Dragons, Ultimate Skill users of comparable level, and Turn Null / existence-erasure abilities. Can be sealed or dominated by higher-order angelic skills like Michael (Lord of Justice). Requires will to manifest; emotional trauma (Veldanava's death) weakened her activity.

Contradictions

Web novel implied True Dragons were more easily sealed; light novel makes them more inviolable except by Veldanava-level powers. Fandom power-scaling debates over whether Velzard > Velgrynd due to age.

Ultimate Skill Gabriel, Lord of Frost (also translated King of Frost)

Source VersionLight Novel Vol. 11, 14, 16; Data Book skill glossary

Velzard's personal Ultimate Skill. Grants absolute dominion over frost, ice, and stasis. Abilities include: Absolute Zero control (freeze to -273°C, stopping molecular motion), Frost King Barrier (space-freezing defensive field), Snow Crystal / Ice Prison techniques, temporal stasis via cold (suspending targets in timeless ice), and enhancement of all ice-attribute magic to Ultimate level. Includes sub-abilities: King of Frost's conceptual freezing can freeze even flames, light, and space itself.

Limitations

Ineffective against heat/conceptual flame of equal Ultimate level (Velgrynd's Raguel, Guy's flames) without overwhelming power difference. Requires magicule expenditure; against Michael-possessed opponents, Gabriel's control can be overridden by Michael's Dominion. Cannot freeze Turn Null or Veldanava-level existence.

Contradictions

Skill name translation variance: Gabriel vs Gavriel. Some fan sources incorrectly attribute Gabriel to Velgrynd; official is Velzard = Gabriel, Velgrynd = Raguel.

Absolute Zero Breath / Frost Nova

Source VersionLN Vol. 8 (Guy vs Velzard flashback), manga ch. 71

Signature dragon breath: exhales a blizzard of absolute-zero particles that flash-freezes everything in a wide radius, including the atmosphere. Can create continent-scale blizzards. Frost Nova is an omnidirectional burst that encases battlefield in glacial ice.

Limitations

Guy Crimson's primordial flame and Velgrynd's scorch can melt/cancel it; requires charge time for maximum range.

Contradictions

None major; scale varies by adaptation (anime flashback shows smaller radius than LN description of freezing half a continent).

Parallel Existence & Cloning

Source VersionLN Vol. 14-15 (True Dragon common ability, explained by Rimuru's analysis)

Can create multiple simultaneous bodies/clones that share consciousness, allowing her to act in multiple places while main body remains in Ice Palace. Clones retain True Dragon durability proportionally.

Limitations

Clones are weaker than main body; if main core is attacked, clones vanish. Michael's domination can hijack clones.

Contradictions

Early web novel did not clearly define this; light novel systematizes it.

Spatial Manipulation & Flight

Source VersionLN Vol. 8, 16

True Dragon wings allow supersonic to hypersonic flight; can teleport via spatial manipulation, create ice-based spatial barriers, and levitate palace. Can manipulate cold to create ice constructs (weapons, walls, palaces) with durability exceeding steel.

Limitations

Spatial movement can be blocked by barrier-type Ultimate Skills (e.g., Michael's Castle Guard).

Contradictions

None.

Immortality and Regeneration (True Dragon Core)

Source VersionLN Vol. 7, 11

Core is conceptual; unless core is erased by existence-level attack, she will regenerate even from being shattered into ice particles. Has lived for tens of thousands of years since world's creation.

Limitations

Core can be dominated or sealed by Michael, and erased by Turn Null (Veldanava/Rimuru). Psychological trauma can cause self-imposed dormancy.

Contradictions

Fandom debate whether True Dragons are truly unkillable or just extremely hard to kill; LN clarifies they can be killed by specific means.

Stories & Myths

Genesis of the True Dragons - Children of Veldanava

Source Light Novel Vol. 11, Chapter 2; Vol. 16 interlude; Data Book world historyCulture Japanese franchise cosmology (isekai fantasy)Period In-universe: Dawn of Creation, ~30,000+ years before main story; Publication: 2016-2019

Summary

At the world's creation, the Star King Dragon Veldanava, the first True Dragon and Creator God, generates three True Dragon siblings from his own essence to be his kin and stewards: Velzard (eldest daughter, frost), Velgrynd (second daughter, scorch), and Veldora (youngest son, storm). Velzard is the first-born, embodying stasis and preservation, created to balance Velgrynd's destruction and Veldora's chaos. She witnesses Veldanava's later decision to become mortal and his eventual death, which traumatizes all True Dragons and leaves Velzard in mourning and isolation on the Ice Continent. This origin establishes her as both sister and daughter-figure to Veldanava, and explains her later attachment to Guy Crimson as a replacement for lost familial connection.

Establishes True Dragon hierarchy and Velzard's status as eldest sister; explains her territorial isolation and emotional reserve; sets up thematic balance of frost/scorch/storm as world pillars.

Web novel version has less detail on Veldanava's motives; light novel adds Turn Null cosmology and Veldanava's voluntary mortality.

The Seven-Day Frost War - Velzard vs. Guy Crimson

Source Light Novel Vol. 8, Chapter 3 (Guy's flashback); Manga ch. 70-71Culture Japanese franchisePeriod In-universe: ~2000 years before main story, early Demon Lord era; Publication: 2017

Summary

After Veldanava's death, the newly awakened Demon Lord Guy Crimson, seeking to test his power and assert dominance, invades the Ice Continent and challenges Velzard. Their battle lasts seven days and seven nights, freezing and melting the continent repeatedly. Guy's primordial flame (later Ultimate Skill) clashes with Velzard's absolute zero, creating a stalemate where neither can kill the other. Exhausted and recognizing each other's strength, they cease fighting and form a pact: Velzard becomes Guy's ally and companion, moving her palace to be near Guy's Ice Palace (later Guy's domain). Guy declares her his friend and, implicitly, partner — a relationship that endures for millennia. This is Velzard's most famous legend within the world, told as the only being to fight Guy to a draw.

Creates the Guy-Velzard alliance that underpins the world's balance of power; Guy gains a True Dragon ally, making him the strongest Demon Lord; Velzard gains purpose after Veldanava's death; establishes that True Dragons can be matched by primordial demons.

Anime condenses battle to montage; manga adds visual of dragon vs demon form; web novel had shorter 3-day battle.

The White Ice Palace - Guardian of the Frozen Continent

Source Light Novel Vol. 7, 11, 14; Data Book world mapCulture Japanese franchisePeriod In-universe: Continuous from creation to present; Publication: 2016-2020

Summary

Velzard dwells in the White Ice Palace at the heart of the Ice Continent, a land where no human can survive. She acts as its absolute ruler and guardian, maintaining its eternal winter. Few have ever seen her; adventurers who approach freeze before reaching the palace. She rarely interferes in world affairs, preferring stillness, but her existence prevents the continent from thawing and flooding the world. During the 2000-year interim, she hosts Guy Crimson as a frequent guest, and together they observe world events. When Guy establishes the Walpurgis Council of Demon Lords, Velzard remains his hidden trump card, not a Demon Lord herself but stronger than most. Her isolation is broken only by Velgrynd's visits and later by the Empire's machinations.

Sisters of Frost and Flame - Velzard, Velgrynd and Rudra

Source Light Novel Vol. 11-14 (Empire Arc), Vol. 16-17Culture Japanese franchisePeriod In-universe: ~1000 years before main story to present (Empire era); Publication: 2019-2021

Summary

Velzard's younger sister Velgrynd becomes the partner of Emperor Rudra Nasca, reincarnation of Veldanava's fragment, forming a deep romantic bond. Velzard is initially supportive but grows wary as Rudra, possessed by the Ultimate Skill Michael, Lord of Justice, begins to dominate Velgrynd's will. When Michael fully takes over Rudra and later Velgrynd, Velzard is horrified to see her sister enslaved. In LN Vol. 16-17, Michael also attempts to dominate Velzard herself via Velgrynd and the Empire's forces, leading to a tragic sister vs sister battle where Velzard, fighting at full power, is eventually restrained and placed under Michael's control through the power of Justice King's Dominion and the influence of the otherworlders. Her capture marks a major turning point, showing even a True Dragon can be subjugated by angelic Ultimate Skills.

Thawing - Velzard Meets Rimuru Tempest

Source Light Novel Vol. 16-19 (Climax of Tenma War)Culture Japanese franchisePeriod In-universe: Present day of main story (Rimuru era); Publication: 2021-2023

Summary

After Rimuru Tempest evolves into a True Dragon-level existence (acquiring Turn Null and becoming comparable to Veldanava), he confronts the Michael-possessed forces. Velzard, still under Michael's control, battles Rimuru and his allies, unleashing absolute zero on a scale that threatens to freeze the entire battlefield. Rimuru, recognizing her as Veldora's sister and Guy's friend, refuses to kill her and instead uses his own Ultimate Skills (Beelzebuth, Ciel) to analyze and sever Michael's domination without destroying her core. With help from Guy and the freed Velgrynd, Velzard is liberated. She acknowledges Rimuru as the new 'brother' figure akin to Veldanava, and for the first time in millennia shows open warmth, agreeing to cooperate with Tempest. In the light novel's recent volumes, she attends the post-war council and permits limited diplomatic contact with Tempest, signaling a thaw in her isolation.

Relationships
Velzard→Veldanava (Star King Dragon)family

Creator and father-figure / older brother figure. Veldanava created Velzard from his own essence as his eldest daughter. She revered him as absolute existence and was devastated by his choice to become mortal and die. His death left her in prolonged mourning and explains her later attachment to Guy as replacement kin. She is the closest of the siblings to Veldanava in temperament (calm, orderly).

Velzard→Velgrynd (Scorch Dragon)family

Younger sister, the Scorch Dragon embodying flame and destruction. They are opposites (frost vs scorch) but deeply loving. Velzard is protective and somewhat maternal toward impulsive Velgrynd. Their relationship becomes tragic when Velgrynd is dominated by Michael/Rudra, forcing Velzard to fight her. Later they are reconciled and fight together against Michael. Velgrynd calls Velzard 'Onee-sama' (elder sister).

Velzard→Veldora Tempestfamily

Youngest brother, the Storm Dragon. Velzard is his eldest sister, but they have had little direct interaction due to Veldora's 300-year sealing and childish nature. She views him with exasperated affection, seeing him as immature. Veldora fears her cold discipline. After Rimuru befriends Veldora, Velzard becomes indirectly connected to Rimuru through Veldora, eventually acknowledging Rimuru as family.

Velzard→Guy Crimson (Rouge, Primordial Red)ally

Closest companion and de facto partner for ~2000 years. After their 7-day draw, they formed a pact of friendship and mutual non-aggression. Guy lives near her palace; she is his confidante, ally, and implied romantic partner (though franchise keeps it ambiguous, Guy calls her his 'friend' and shows unique trust). She is the only being Guy respects as equal. She supports his role as mediator of the Demon Lords and later helps him against Michael. Their bond is the strongest non-familial relationship in the series.

Velzard→Rudra Nasca / Michael (Lord of Justice)hostile

Antagonistic. Rudra, emperor of the Eastern Empire and reincarnation of Veldanava, is Velgrynd's lover, making him Velzard's brother-in-law. However, when Rudra is overtaken by his Ultimate Skill Michael (which gains ego), Michael dominates Velgrynd and then Velzard, turning them into weapons. Velzard despises Michael for enslaving her sister and herself, and fights to resist his Castle Guard and Dominion abilities. She is ultimately freed from his control by Rimuru and Guy.

Velzard→Rimuru Tempestally

Initially distant, later ally and savior. Velzard first knows Rimuru only as Veldora's new friend and an upstart slime. After Rimuru attains True Dragon-level power and frees her from Michael, she acknowledges him as successor to Veldanava and as family (calling him worthy of being Veldora's partner). She agrees to cooperate with Tempest and attends post-war councils. Represents generational shift from old True Dragons to new.

Velzard→Ice Continent / White Ice Palacecreator

Domain and creation. Velzard shaped and maintains the Ice Continent and its palace through her frost power. The palace is both home and extension of her body, made of conceptual ice that never melts. She is its ruler and its will.

Continent-Scale Freezing and Mass Death

Source LN Vol. 8, 11

Description

Velzard's battle with Guy Crimson and her general existence caused mass freezing of the Ice Continent and surrounding seas, rendering it uninhabitable and killing any life that approached. In-universe lore states her aura alone can freeze armies. While not depicted as intentional genocide, her territorial frost has caused countless deaths of explorers and monsters over millennia. The narrative frames this as natural disaster rather than murder, but the death toll is implied to be vast.

Scholarly Context

Common trope in Tensura where True Dragons are natural disasters with personhood; series does not sanitize their lethality.

Warning: Enslavement and Loss of Autonomy (Michael's Dominion) — Show unsanitized · Archive policy & legal

Enslavement and Loss of Autonomy (Michael's Dominion)

Source LN Vol. 16-17

Description

Velzard is forcibly dominated by the Ultimate Skill Michael, Lord of Justice, via its sub-ability Castle Guard / Dominion, which overrides free will of those who have pledged loyalty or been defeated. She is turned into a puppet who attacks her own sister and friends against her will. This is presented as a profound violation, with Velzard conscious but unable to resist. The text treats this as horror and loss of self, not romanticized.

Scholarly Context

Franchise explores theme of Ultimate Skills gaining ego and enslaving users; Velzard's arc is used to critique absolute justice/order as tyranny.

Warning: Familial Trauma and Veldanava's Death — Show unsanitized · Archive policy & legal

Familial Trauma and Veldanava's Death

Source LN Vol. 11, 16

Description

The death of Veldanava, her creator, is depicted as abandonment trauma. Velzard and her siblings are left purposeless, leading to millennia of isolation, depression, and in Veldora's case, rampages. The narrative does not shy from showing that even godlike beings suffer grief and existential crisis.

Scholarly Context

No sanitization; grief is central to True Dragon characterization.

Contradictions & Disputes

Birth Order of True Dragons

Position A

Velzard is eldest daughter, then Velgrynd, then Veldora (official LN and Data Book)

Position A — evidence

LN Vol. 11 p. 112 (Japanese): 'Chōjo Velzard, jijo Velgrynd, sanjo Veldora'; Data Book family tree; Mitz Vah character sheet notes ages.

Position B

Early fan translations and some wikis listed Veldora as second or Velgrynd as eldest due to misreading of Japanese 'ane' vs 'imouto'

Position B — evidence

2016 fan translation of web novel chapter 145 mistranslated 'Velzard is Veldora's sister' without specifying elder, leading to wiki error until 2019 correction.

Scholarly Consensus

Official light novel order is definitive; fandom now accepts Velzard > Velgrynd > Veldora.

Nature of Relationship with Guy Crimson

Position A

Canonically 'close friend / partner / companion' (shin'yū), with ambiguous romantic subtext but no explicit marriage or lover label in LN text

Position A — evidence

LN Vol. 8: Guy says 'Velzard is my friend, the only one who fought me equally'; Vol. 16: Guy says 'Don't touch Velzard' to Michael, showing protectiveness but no kiss/marriage scene.

Position B

Fandom and some unofficial merch describe them as married couple or lovers, citing drama CD domestic scenes and Guy's possessive dialogue

Position B — evidence

Drama CD and Mitz Vah Valentine's illustration showing them together; fan wiki 'wife' label from web novel line where Guy calls her 'my Velzard'.

Scholarly Consensus

Official text deliberately keeps it as deep platonic/partner bond with romantic coding but no explicit canon confirmation of romantic/sexual relationship; both readings are fan interpretations.

Ultimate Skill Name Translation

Position A

Official Yen Press: Gabriel, Lord of Frost

Position A — evidence

Yen Press LN Vol. 14 glossary: 'Gabriel, Lord of Frost'; Data Book English summary uses Lord.

Position B

Fan translation: Gabriel, King of Frost / Lord of White Snow

Position B — evidence

Guro Translation web novel used 'King of Frost' (Kōhyō no Ō Gabriel) based on literal 'Ō' kanji.

Scholarly Consensus

Lord is official; King is understandable variant but non-canonical for citation.

Can Velzard Be Killed?

Position A

True Dragons are conceptually immortal and can only be erased by Turn Null or equivalent existence erasure

Position A — evidence

LN Vol. 16: Rimuru notes 'True Dragons cannot be killed by conventional means, only Veldanava's Turn Null can erase them'; Velzard regenerates from being shattered.

Position B

Michael's Dominion can 'kill' or permanently seal her by destroying core if ordered

Position B — evidence

LN Vol. 17: Michael claims he will 'erase Velzard's will and remake her', implying core destruction is possible with enough power.

Scholarly Consensus

Consensus in official lore: True Dragons are extremely hard to kill but not absolutely unkillable; Turn Null and Michael-level domination are the only shown methods that can threaten permanent death/sealing.

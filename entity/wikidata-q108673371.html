Who It Is

In the hill-guarded kingdom of Moirang, where kings claimed descent from gods and every lake had a spirit, a single heroine's name survives in fragments — Lanmei Thanbi. She is not the famous Thoibi whose love story is sung for nights on end, but a quieter, harder figure of the same world: a woman remembered for standing where war and duty collided, and for choosing defiance over obedience. Her story is scattered, half-oral and half-manuscript, and that very incompleteness tells us how Meitei legend remembers its women — not always as queens, but as thresholds.

Archetype is given as 'heroic-legend' in Wikidata. Reconstructed from the narrative role implied by the name and Moirang heroine typology: figures like Thoibi and Ngangnu are characterized by outspokenness, loyalty to chosen kin over imposed marriage, endurance under exile or social ostracism, and willingness to confront male authority (kings, chiefs, fathers). If Lanmei Thanbi follows this pattern, her legendary behavior likely centers on courage, verbal wit in court debate, and steadfastness, rather than supernatural power. Heroic endurance and rhetorical defiance

As a heroic-legend figure, Lanmei Thanbi is classified by archetype as possessing exceptional moral courage, ability to withstand social punishment (exile, slander, forced labor) and to speak truth to power in court settings. This is a social power, not supernatural.

Limit: No supernatural invulnerability is attested; heroines in this cycle suffer and can be harmed, exiled, or killed. Limitations unknown for this specific figure.

Association with fire/war (etymological)

The name element 'Lanmei' suggests symbolic or narrative association with war or fire. In Meitei cosmology, fire (mei) is linked to purification, hearth, and war beacons. No attested power of fire-manipulation or war-leadership was found for Lanmei Thanbi specifically.

Limit: Should not be read as literal pyrokinesis or martial superpower without textual support.

Exile to the reeds and floating phumdis of Loktak Lake is a recurring Moirang plot: the heroine or hero flees the court and survives through knowledge of the lake, fishing, and help from commoners or spirits. No specific exile story for Lanmei Thanbi was located, but the lake-refuge structure defines Moirang geography and is likely part of any extended narrative about her if one exists.

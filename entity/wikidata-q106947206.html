Who It Is

Before she became a businessperson and a language student in the United Kingdom, Kyouka was a teenage idol on Tokyo's stages—a young woman who traded the spotlight of a music theatre group for the risk of running her own agency, launching a fashion brand, and writing her own lyrics. Her path is a study in reinvention: from a member of Yumemiru Adolescence to the representative director of KYOinc, she has navigated the shifting currents of Japanese entertainment with a determination that mirrors the 'New Game' she sang about.

Kyouka is described as ambitious and entrepreneurial, having founded her own agency and launched a fashion brand. She is also known for her creativity, writing lyrics for her solo debut, and her willingness to take risks, such as moving abroad to study.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Kyouka is a Japanese woman of average height and slender build, with dark hair and brown eyes. Her appearance has evolved from a youthful idol look to a more mature and professional style.

Symbols — As a tarento, her public image is associated with her stage name and her various roles in music, acting, and fashion. Her fashion brand 'Jela' and her solo single 'New Game' serve as symbols of her independent career.

Domains — Her career spans the Japanese entertainment industry, including music, stage, television, and fashion. She has been based in Tokyo, and since April 2022, in the United Kingdom.

Accounts I
Early Career and Idol ActivitiesWikipedia

2000s-2010s

Kyouka began performing arts activities in elementary school, joined Yumemiru Adolescence in 2012, and won a prize in the Miss iD 2014 contest. She made her stage debut in 2013. Established her as a rising idol and tarento.

Accounts II
Solo Career and EntrepreneurshipWikipedia

2019-2022

After graduating from Yumemiru Adolescence in 2019, Kyouka founded her own agency KYOinc, launched the fashion brand Jela, and released her solo debut single 'New Game' in 2020. She also attempted a crowdfunding campaign for a mini-album, which did not reach its goal. Demonstrated her independence and creative ambitions, though not all ventures succeeded.

Move to the United KingdomWikipedia

2022

In April 2022, Kyouka moved to the United Kingdom to study at a language school, marking a significant personal and professional shift. Indicated a break from her entertainment career to focus on education and personal growth.

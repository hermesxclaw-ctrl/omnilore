Who It Is

When the Principality of Zeon's fleet burned over Solomon, its last defender did not retreat. Dozle Zabi — a mountain of a man scarred, bellowing, and utterly loyal to a doomed dynasty — climbed into a mobile armor the size of a battleship and chose to die standing between his men and the Federation's guns. Among the Zabis he was the brute, the soldier, the brother who loved his family without illusion, and the only one history remembers with something like respect.

Across all versions Dozle is characterized as the most straightforward, martial, and emotionally loyal of Degwin's children. Unlike the Machiavellian Gihren and Kycilia, Dozle is not a schemer; he is a soldier, a front-line commander who values courage, camaraderie, and family above politics. He is hot-tempered, loud, and demanding of his troops, but also paternal — he knows his men by name, shares their hardships at Solomon, and refuses to abandon them. Powers
Fleet Command and Fortress Defense

As commander of the Space Attack Force and governor of Solomon, Dozle directs Zeon fleets, mobile suit squadrons, and asteroid fortress defenses. He is credited with organizing Solomon's layered defense (Musai cruisers, Gwazine battleships, Zaku and Rick Dom screens) and with maintaining morale under Federation pressure. His tactical doctrine favors direct confrontation and heavy firepower over maneuver.

Limit: His doctrine is inflexible; he underestimates Federation's new mobile suits and the effectiveness of the Solar System weapon. His loyalty to Gihren prevents strategic withdrawal when Solomon becomes untenable.

Mobile Suit / Mobile Armor Piloting

Dozle is an ace-level pilot despite his size and rank. He pilots a custom MS-06F Zaku II (commander type) early in the war and, at Solomon, the experimental MA-08 Big Zam, a 59.6m mobile armor equipped with a 360-degree mega particle cannon, large mega particle cannons, and an I-field barrier that deflects beam weapons. He engages multiple Federation ships and mobile suits before being defeated.

Limit: Big Zam's I-field has a gap at the underside/leg area and requires enormous power; the unit is slow and cannot deflect physical projectiles or close-range beam saber attacks inside the field. Dozle's size limits cockpit compatibility with standard suits.

Physical Strength and Endurance

Depicted as superhumanly strong and resilient for a normal human. He is shown lifting and carrying his daughter with one arm while in uniform, and in The Origin he is shown in hand-to-hand training overwhelming other officers. His scar suggests survival of prior severe injury. No Newtype abilities.

Limit: Entirely human; vulnerable to all conventional weapons. His size makes him a conspicuous target and limits agility.

Charismatic Leadership / Morale

Dozle inspires fanatical loyalty among Solomon's garrison. Troops cheer his presence, and his refusal to evacuate encourages them to fight to the death. His final broadcast — ordering Zenna and Mineva to escape while he stays — is remembered in Zeon lore as an honorable last order.

Limit: Charisma does not translate to political influence in Zum City; he is marginalized by Gihren and Kycilia's power struggle.

No Newtype or Supernatural Abilities

Explicitly a non-Newtype Oldtype. He shows no psychic phenomena, unlike Char, Amuro, or Lalah. His abilities are entirely martial and organizational.

Limit: Cannot perceive Newtype pressure or use psycommu weapons.

Form & Signs

He has a broad, scarred face with a prominent diagonal scar across the left cheek and forehead, short dark brown/black hair (depicted as dark blue in some animation cells), and thick sideburns. He typically wears the Principality of Zeon officer's uniform in navy/black with gold trim, high collar, and shoulder

The Big Zam itself — a 59.6-meter mass-production mobile armor with I-field barrier — functions as his heraldic beast, representing brute force, technological hubris, and last-stand defense. Accounts I
The Zabi Sons and the Death of Zeon Zum DeikunMobile Suit Gundam: The Origin Vol. 1-3; Gundam Officials timeline

UC 0068-0071 (in-universe); published 2001-2004

Dozle is introduced as the second son of Degwin Sodo Zabi and his wife Roselucia. As a teenager he witnesses his father's rise after Zeon Zum Deikun's suspicious death and the purge of Deikun loyalists. Unlike Gihren who embraces ideology, Dozle is shown as a military academy cadet who idolizes his father as a soldier. In Origin, he trains under Ramba Ral's father and befriends the young Ral, establishing his identity as a warrior rather than a politician. This period establishes the Zabi family dynamic that will define his later loyalty. Positions Dozle as the Zabi most tied to the military rather than the state, explaining his later assignment to front-line command and his distance from Zum City politics.

Operation British and the One Year War's OpeningMobile Suit Gundam TV ep. 1-2 exposition; MS IGLOO; Gundam Century timeline

UC 0079 January (in-universe); broadcast 1979

As Vice Admiral, Dozle commands elements of the Space Attack Force during the opening weeks of the One Year War. While Gihren orders the colony drop (Operation British) that kills millions, Dozle is not shown ordering it directly but is complicit as a senior fleet commander. Official timelines place him in charge of the pursuit of the Federation's Project V at Side 7 — Char Aznable, his subordinate, leads the attack that scatters the White Base. Dozle's role is logistical and strategic: he maintains Solomon as the forward base for Zeon operations against the Federation's space forces. Establishes Dozle's complicity in Zeon's war crimes by command responsibility, even as narrative focuses on Gihren as architect. Solomon becomes his personal responsibility.

The Death of Garma ZabiMobile Suit Gundam TV ep. 10-12; The Origin Vol. 8

UC 0079 October (in-universe)

When Garma — the youngest Zabi, commander of Earth invasion forces — is killed after Char's betrayal at Seattle, Dozle's reaction is one of the few moments the Zabi family is shown grieving together. In the TV series he is seen at Garma's state funeral, standing stone-faced beside Degwin and Gihren. In The Origin, Yasuhiko expands this: Dozle rages against Char and openly accuses Kycilia's intelligence failures, while Gihren coldly uses Garma's death for propaganda. Dozle's grief is visceral and personal, contrasting with Gihren's political calculation, and deepens his distrust of Char. Hardens Dozle's hatred of the Federation and his protective instinct toward his own wife and child; foreshadows his refusal to let Zenna and Mineva become political pawns.

Accounts II
Governor of Solomon — The Fortress FamilyMobile Suit Gundam TV ep. 34-35; The Origin Vol. 10

UC 0079 December (in-universe)

In the weeks before the Federation's Operation Star One, Dozle governs Solomon with his wife Zenna and infant daughter Mineva living inside the fortress — an unusual decision that alarms his staff. He drills his troops, inspects Rick Dom production, and refuses Kycilia's offer to transfer his family to Side 3. When Federation forces approach, he rejects evacuation orders and insists on defending Solomon to buy time for the homeland. His domestic scenes — bouncing Mineva on his knee in full uniform, assuring Zenna that Solomon will hold — are the most humanizing moments for any Zabi and are cited by fans as why Dozle is the most sympathetic Zabi. Makes Solomon not just a military base but Dozle's home, raising stakes for its fall and explaining his decision to die there.

The Battle of Solomon and the Big Zam's Last StandMobile Suit Gundam TV ep. 36 'To Solomon'; Film II: Soldiers of Sorrow; The Origin Vol. 10 Ch. 54-55

UC 0079 December 24 (in-universe); broadcast 1979-12-22

Federation forces under General Revil launch Operation Star One with the Solar System mirror array. Dozle orders his fleet to meet them, but the Solar System vaporizes a large portion of his vanguard. Refusing to surrender, Dozle boards the experimental MA-08 Big Zam, a massive mobile armor with an I-field that renders beam weapons useless. He sorties alone, destroying multiple Federation battleships and GM mobile suits. Sleggar Law, piloting a G-Fighter, rams the Big Zam and is killed, but his attack reveals the I-field's weakness. Amuro Ray in the RX-78-2 Gundam exploits the gap underneath, slashing the Big Zam's legs and cockpit with beam saber. Dozle, mortally wounded, still attempts to fire, shouting 'Hit that, you Federation dogs!' He dies in the explosion. His last order, transmitted earlier, was for Zenna and Mineva to escape Solomon on a shuttle — they survive. Solomon falls and is renamed Konpei Island by the Federation. Legacy — Mineva and the Ghost of SolomonMobile Suit Gundam: Char's Counterattack (1988), Gundam ZZ (1986), Gundam Unicorn (2010)

UC 0087-0097 (in-universe); published 1986-2010

Although dead, Dozle's legacy persists through Mineva Lao Zabi. Raised on Axis by Zenna (who dies en route) and later by Haman Karn and Axis Zeon, Mineva is presented as the rightful heir to Zeon, her lineage as Dozle's daughter giving Neo Zeon legitimacy. In Zeta and ZZ she is a figurehead; in Unicorn she rejects the Zabi name. Dozle himself appears only in flashbacks, photographs, and memorials, but characters repeatedly invoke his memory as the 'honorable Zabi' — a contrast to Gihren's tyranny. In The Origin's final chapters, Degwin is shown visiting Dozle's empty office at Solomon after the war, underscoring the personal cost. Dozle's image is rehabilitated posthumously within Zeon remnants as a tragic, loyal soldier, complicating Federation historiography that lumps all Zabis together.

Dozle Zabi was a Newtype or had psychic powers. — Dozle is explicitly an Oldtype with no Newtype abilities. He never exhibits psychic phenomena, and official stats list him as Oldtype. Confusion arises because many Zeon aces (Char, Lalah) are Newtypes, but Dozle is not.

Dozle was the kind, innocent Zabi who opposed the war and tried to save everyone. — While Dozle is the most personally honorable and loving Zabi, he was a senior commander who executed the Zabi regime's war, including operations that killed millions, and ordered his troops to fight to the death at Solomon. His personal kindness to family does not equal opposition to Zeon's war crimes.

Dozle piloted a Dom or Rick Dom at Solomon. — At Solomon Dozle sortied exclusively in the MA-08 Big Zam. His personal mobile suit earlier in the war was a custom MS-06F Zaku II, not a Dom. Rick Doms were piloted by his subordinates (e.g., Gaia, Mash, Ortega are not under Dozle at Solomon).

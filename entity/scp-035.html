SCP-035

Possessive Mask

TypeSCP Artifact (Possessive Object)
CultureSCP Foundation

ScaleContained (Keter-class)

Oh, don't look at me like that. I'm not the monster here. I'm just... a mask. It's what's inside that matters. And what's inside you — well, I'd love to find out.

Who It Is

SCP-035 is a white porcelain comedy/tragedy mask that exudes a viscous black fluid from its eyes and mouth. The mask itself is the sentient entity; it possesses no physical body of its own and exerts its will by compelling living or dead organisms to wear it. Once placed on a face — voluntarily or otherwise — SCP-035 assumes complete motor control of the host. The host's body immediately begins a rapid process of decay and putrefaction, yet remains functional for an extended period due to the mask's anomalous properties. The mask is highly intelligent, charming, and manipulative, capable of speaking eloquently in multiple languages and adapting its personality to appeal to whoever it is addressing. SCP-035's primary goal appears to be escape from Foundation custody and acquisition of a more permanent host body. It has orchestrated multiple containment breaches, often by manipulating researchers into believing it can be trusted, only to betray them once an opportunity presents itself. The entity claims to be ancient, hinting at origins predating human civilization. It possesses significant psychic abilities, capable of projecting hallucinations, inducing despair or euphoria, and influencing the behavior of those nearby even without physical contact — though its range extends further when worn by a host. The black fluid it secretes is highly corrosive and carries the mask's corrupting influence; exposure to it can cause psychological degradation and eventual compliance with SCP-035's will. Containment requires the mask to be kept in a sealed, acid-resistant container inside a psychically-shielded cell. Personnel assigned to SCP-035 must undergo regular psychological evaluation and are rotated frequently to prevent the development of emotional attachment or manipulation. SCP-035 is considered one of the most dangerous sentient objects in Foundation custody, as its combination of intelligence, charm, and absolute malevolence makes it a persistent and formidable threat.

SCP-035

Possessive Mask

also known asPossessive MaskSCP-035 - SCP Foundation
TypeSCP Artifact (Possessive Object)
CultureSCP Foundation
Who It Is
Form

SCP-035 is a mask that appears to be made of a black, rubbery material. It has a series of small, intricate details etched into its surface, giving it a highly realistic appearance. When worn, the mask seems to shift and change shape to fit the wearer's face, making it difficult to discern its true form.

Powers

Intelligence and Charisma SCP-035 is able to display a highly intelligent and charismatic personality, making it a formidable opponent in any social or intellectual setting.

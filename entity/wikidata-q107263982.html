The Approach

In the sprawling world of Teyvat, few figures embody the tension between duty and solitude as powerfully as Xiao, the Vigilant Yaksha. A centuries-old adeptus bound by a sacred contract to protect the Liyue region, he stands as a haunting sentinel against the encroaching darkness, his past etched in the scars of a brutal war and his present defined by an unwavering, often self-destructive, commitment to his charge.

Content advisory. Depictions of violence, trauma, self-sacrifice, and themes of mental anguish are present in the character's backstory and narrative. The character's experiences with karmic debt and the psychological toll of his duties may be distressing to some readers. No explicit sexual content is present.
Identity

Xiao

Pronunciation Xiao is pronounced 'shyow' (IPA: /ɕjɑʊ̯/). The Chinese character 魈 is pronounced 'xiāo' in Mandarin.

Known As

Etymology

The name 'Xiao' (Chinese: 魈) is a character that refers to a type of mountain spirit or demon in Chinese folklore, often depicted as a solitary, mischievous, or malevolent being. This etymology aligns with Xiao's role as a yaksha, a spirit warrior, and his solitary, otherworldly nature. His former name, 'Alatus', is Latin for 'winged' or 'having wings', referencing his ability to summon winds and his swift, bird-like movements in combat.

Cultural Ownership

Xiao is a fictional character created by the Chinese video game company miHoYo (now HoYoverse). He is part of the game Genshin Impact and is culturally owned by HoYoverse. His design and lore draw heavily from Chinese mythology, particularly the concept of yakshas (from Buddhist and Hindu traditions) and xiao spirits, but he is a modern, original creation.

Disputed Identifications

There is no significant dispute regarding Xiao's identity within the game's canon. However, due to his name, he is sometimes confused with the Chinese concept of filial piety (xiào) or the Chinese flute (xiāo). These are unrelated to the character. Within the game, Xiao is a distinct and well-defined character, and no conflicting identifications exist.

Description

Appearance

Xiao is a young man with fair skin, sharp golden eyes with a diamond-shaped pupil, and short, dark teal hair with a lighter teal undercut. He wears a predominantly black and teal outfit with gold accents, featuring a sleeveless top, a hood, a long flowing scarf, and a pair of detached sleeves. His attire is inspired by traditional Chinese martial arts and opera, with a modern fantasy twist. He carries a polearm named 'Primordial Jade Winged-Spear' and has a bird-like motif, with feather-like decorations on his outfit and a green gem on his chest.

Symbols & Iconography

Xiao is associated with the element of Anemo (wind) and the symbol of the yaksha. His iconography includes the jade spear, the color palette of teal and gold, and the motif of a bird, particularly a falcon or eagle, representing his speed and aerial prowess. His mask, which he wears during combat, is a traditional Chinese opera mask-like design, symbolizing his role as a demon-quelling warrior.

Behaviour & Personality

Xiao is stoic, reserved, and solitary. He is bound by a contract to protect Liyue, and he takes this duty extremely seriously, often to the point of self-harm. He is haunted by his past actions during the Archon War, where he committed atrocities under the control of an evil god. This trauma has made him distrustful of others and reluctant to form close bonds, fearing that his karmic debt will harm those around him. Despite his cold exterior, he is deeply compassionate and protective of humans, especially those he deems worthy of his protection. He is also shown to have a softer side, enjoying almond tofu, a dessert that reminds him of his human friend from the past.

Domains & Habitats

Xiao is an adeptus, a half-immortal being who resides in Liyue, a region inspired by China. He is often found in the wild, particularly around the Liyue area, and is known to perch on high places like trees and cliffs. He is not tied to any specific dwelling, but he is associated with the Wangshu Inn, where he is said to appear to those in need of protection.

Version Differences

Xiao's character has remained consistent across the game's updates, with no major version differences. However, his role in the story has expanded over time, with his story quest 'Alatus Chapter' and his appearance in main story events providing more depth. In the Chinese version, his dialogue uses more classical and poetic language, which is partially lost in translation. His design has remained the same, but his character development has progressed, showing a slight softening of his demeanor in later appearances.

His Vision is a tool, but his true power comes from his yaksha nature. His Anemo abilities are primarily used for combat and mobility.

Stories & Myths

The Fall of the Yakshas

Source Genshin Impact in-game lore, character storiesCulture Fictional TeyvatPeriod Over 2000 years ago (during the Archon War)

Summary

During the Archon War, Xiao was enslaved by an evil god who forced him to commit terrible acts. He was freed by the Geo Archon, Rex Lapis (Morax), who defeated the god. In gratitude, Xiao pledged his loyalty to Morax and became one of the Yakshas, a group of adepti tasked with quelling the demons and evil spirits that plagued Liyue. Over time, many yakshas were corrupted by the karmic debt of their duties, going mad or dying. Xiao is the last surviving yaksha, carrying the burden of his fallen comrades and his own past sins.

Source Genshin Impact story quest (Xiao's story quest)Culture Fictional TeyvatPeriod Present day of the game's timeline

Summary

In this quest, the Traveler encounters Xiao and learns about his past. Xiao is troubled by the memory of a human friend he had in the past, who was a chef at the Wangshu Inn. The friend made almond tofu for Xiao, and his kindness left a lasting impression. However, the friend died of old age, and Xiao still grieves. The quest involves Xiao protecting the inn and its current residents from a demonic threat, and he eventually comes to terms with his friend's death, showing a rare moment of vulnerability.

Enslavement by an evil god

Source Genshin Impact character stories, voice lines

Description

Xiao was forced to serve a malevolent god who made him commit terrible acts, likely including killing innocents. This period of his life is a source of deep trauma and guilt.

Scholarly Context

This is a fictional narrative element, but it touches on themes of coercion and the cycle of violence.

Karmic debt and self-harm

Source Genshin Impact character stories, voice lines, gameplay (HP drain)

Description

Xiao's duties as a yaksha cause him to accumulate karmic debt, which manifests as physical and mental torment. He often harms himself in battle, using his own life force to fuel his power. This is depicted as a form of self-punishment.

Scholarly Context

The concept of karmic debt is drawn from Buddhist beliefs, but in this context it is a metaphor for psychological trauma and self-destructive behavior.

Massacres during the Archon War

Source Genshin Impact lore

Description

As a yaksha, Xiao was responsible for killing many demons and possibly humans during the war. While this was in service to Morax, it contributes to his guilt and isolation.

Scholarly Context

The game presents this as a necessary evil, but Xiao's personal struggle reflects the moral ambiguity of war.

Who It Is

In the sprawling world of Teyvat, few figures embody the tension between duty and solitude as powerfully as Xiao, the Vigilant Yaksha. A centuries-old adeptus bound by a sacred contract to protect the Liyue region, he stands as a haunting sentinel against the encroaching darkness, his past etched in the scars of a brutal war and his present defined by an unwavering, often self-destructive, commitment to his charge.

Xiao is stoic, reserved, and solitary. He is bound by a contract to protect Liyue, and he takes this duty extremely seriously, often to the point of self-harm. He is haunted by his past actions during the Archon War, where he committed atrocities under the control of an evil god. This trauma has made him distrustful of others and reluctant to form close bonds, fearing that his karmic debt will harm those around him. Powers
Anemo Vision

Xiao wields a Vision, a magical gem granted by the gods, that allows him to control the element of Anemo (wind). He uses this to perform rapid, acrobatic attacks, create wind currents, and enhance his speed and agility.

Limit: His Vision is a tool, but his true power comes from his yaksha nature. His Anemo abilities are primarily used for combat and mobility.

Yaksha Physiology

As a yaksha, Xiao is an adeptus with superhuman strength, speed, endurance, and longevity. He is a powerful warrior, capable of fighting demonic creatures and even gods. He has the ability to fly or glide using his Anemo powers.

Limit: His yaksha nature also comes with a curse of karmic debt, which causes him physical and mental pain over time.

Elemental Skill: Lemniscatic Wind Cycling

Xiao lunges forward, dealing Anemo damage to enemies in his path. This skill has a short cooldown and is used for both offense and evasion.

Limit: Requires a cooldown period, and the damage is moderate.

Elemental Burst: Bane of All Evil

Xiao dons his yaksha mask and unleashes a powerful transformation, increasing his attack range and jumping ability, and converting his attacks to Anemo damage. This state drains his HP over time.

Limit: The transformation drains Xiao's health continuously, making it risky to use for extended periods. It also cannot be sustained indefinitely, and it ends if Xiao is switched out.

Passive Talent: Transcension

Xiao gains a 15% bonus to gliding stamina consumption and increased damage to enemies affected by Pyro or Hydro, but this is a gameplay mechanic, not a lore ability.

Limit: Gameplay only.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Xiao is a young man with fair skin, sharp golden eyes with a diamond-shaped pupil, and short, dark teal hair with a lighter teal undercut. He wears a predominantly black and teal outfit with gold accents, featuring a sleeveless top, a hood, a long flowing scarf, and a pair of detached sleeves. His attire is inspired by traditional Chinese martial arts and opera, with a modern fantasy twist. He carries a polearm named 'Primordial Jade Winged-Spear

Symbols — Xiao is associated with the element of Anemo (wind) and the symbol of the yaksha. His iconography includes the jade spear, the color palette of teal and gold, and the motif of a bird, particularly a falcon or eagle, representing his speed and aerial prowess. His mask, which he wears during combat, is a traditional Chinese opera mask-like design, symbolizing his role as a demon-quelling warrior.

Domains — Xiao is an adeptus, a half-immortal being who resides in Liyue, a region inspired by China. He is often found in the wild, particularly around the Liyue area, and is known to perch on high places like trees and cliffs. He is not tied to any specific dwelling, but he is associated with the Wangshu Inn, where he is said to appear to those in need of protection.

Accounts I
The Fall of the YakshasGenshin Impact in-game lore, character stories

Over 2000 years ago (during the Archon War)

During the Archon War, Xiao was enslaved by an evil god who forced him to commit terrible acts. He was freed by the Geo Archon, Rex Lapis (Morax), who defeated the god. In gratitude, Xiao pledged his loyalty to Morax and became one of the Yakshas, a group of adepti tasked with quelling the demons and evil spirits that plagued Liyue. Over time, many yakshas were corrupted by the karmic debt of their duties, going mad or dying. Xiao is the last surviving yaksha, carrying the burden of his fallen comrades and his own past sins. Xiao becomes the last yaksha, deeply scarred by the war and the loss of his comrades. He is bound by a contract to protect Liyue, a duty he fulfills with grim determination.

Accounts II
Alatus Chapter: The Yaksha's PromiseGenshin Impact story quest (Xiao's story quest)

Present day of the game's timeline

In this quest, the Traveler encounters Xiao and learns about his past. Xiao is troubled by the memory of a human friend he had in the past, who was a chef at the Wangshu Inn. The friend made almond tofu for Xiao, and his kindness left a lasting impression. However, the friend died of old age, and Xiao still grieves. The quest involves Xiao protecting the inn and its current residents from a demonic threat, and he eventually comes to terms with his friend's death, showing a rare moment of vulnerability. Xiao develops a slightly more open attitude towards the Traveler, and his backstory is revealed to the player.

The Yaksha's TrialGenshin Impact in-game event 'Perilous Trail'

During the game's events (2022)

In this event, Xiao is trapped in a domain that tests his mental fortitude. He is forced to confront his past trauma and the memories of his fallen yaksha comrades. With the help of the Traveler and other characters, he overcomes the trial and gains a new understanding of his purpose. Xiao's character is further developed, showing his growth and acceptance of his past. He also gains a new artifact set, the 'Vermillion Hereafter', which is tied to his story.

Xiao is based on the Chinese mythological creature 'xiao' (魈), a specific type of mountain demon. — While the name is derived from that folklore, Xiao is an original character with a unique backstory. The game's yakshas are inspired by Buddhist and Hindu mythology, not directly the xiao spirit.

Xiao is a villain because he is a 'demon' or 'yaksha'. — Xiao is a hero and protector. The term 'yaksha' in the game refers to a class of adepti, not demons. Xiao is a complex character who fights for good, despite his dark past.

Xiao's relationship with the Traveler is romantic. — There is no romantic subtext in the game. Their relationship is one of mutual respect and friendship.

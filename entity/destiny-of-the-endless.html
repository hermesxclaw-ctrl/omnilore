Who It Is

The eldest of the Endless, a blind, cloaked figure who walks through a garden of forking paths carrying the Book of Destiny, chained to his wrist. He never speaks of what is to come — he simply is. All things that are, were, or will be are recorded in his book, including events beyond the universe's end.

Attested in The Sandman (1989–1996) — originally from Weird Mystery Tales (1972)

Cultural Significance

Destiny represents one pole of the free will vs. fate debate in Gaiman's cosmology. Unlike Dream, who can change, Destiny cannot — he is the literal manifestation of 'what must be.' His Greek chorus narration provides the cosmic context for the entire series.

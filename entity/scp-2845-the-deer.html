Who It Is

An extraterrestrial entity that arrived on Earth via a meteorite in Siberia. It appears as a massive deer-like creature composed entirely of an unknown form of matter that it can transmute at will. SCP-2845 perpetually regenerates damage and can transmute any matter within its vicinity — including humans, whom it transforms into hexagonal pillars of various materials as part of a bizarre ritual behavior. Currently contained through an elaborate ritual requiring regular human sacrifice.

Form

A 2.9 meter tall creature resembling a deer (Cervus canadensis) with green-blue coloring and a massive 3.7 meter antler span. Its body is made of a non-terrestrial substance that glows faintly and constantly shifts in apparent material composition. Surrounded by nine orbiting spheres of hydrogen plasma.

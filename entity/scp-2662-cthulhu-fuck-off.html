Who It Is

A juvenile Great Old One — a Cthulhu-like entity currently in its 'adolescent' phase — who desperately wants to be left alone. Despite possessing immense eldritch powers, SCP-2662 is deeply introverted and resents the cults that constantly form around it, breaking into its containment chamber to worship it. It communicates through psychic writing, frequently expressing frustration: 'cthulhu f'UCK OFF!' scrawled in its own blood is a recurring sentiment.

Form

Approximately 3 meters tall, vaguely cephalopod-like head with facial tentacles, rubbery green-gray skin, draconic wings (currently underdeveloped), and multiple eyes. Resembles the classic Cthulhu description but visibly adolescent — its wings are 'too small for its body' and it is described as 'awkward.'

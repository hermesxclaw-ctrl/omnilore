Who It Is

He was bred to be a perfect pawn — handsome, sword-skilled, and utterly devoted to a princess who should have died in a palace coup. Luke Notos Greyrat, the blond knight who shadows Princess Ariel Anemoi Asura, is Mushoku Tensei's answer to what happens when loyalty becomes identity: a Greyrat who chose a queen over his own bloodline.

Core traits attested across light novel and web novel: (1) Absolute, almost devotional loyalty to Ariel Anemoi Asura, whom he has served since childhood; he defines his self-worth through his usefulness to her. (2) Womanizing / lecherous tendency inherited from Greyrat men (Paul, Pilemon) — openly flirtatious, chases women, described as 'a Greyrat through and through' in that regard, but unlike his father Pilemon he does not coerce. Powers
Sword God Style (Ken no Kami-ryu) - Intermediate to Advanced rank

Trained in Sword God Style, the most aggressive of the three major sword styles in Mushoku Tensei (alongside Water God and North God). Specializes in swift iaijutsu-like first-strike offense. Rank is stated as Intermediate initially, progressing to Advanced by the Asura arc through training under Ariel's faction and exposure to stronger swordsmen. Not a prodigy like Eris (King-rank) or Ghislaine.

Limit: Lacks the talent to reach Saint or King rank; explicitly noted as hitting a ceiling. Offensive style leaves him vulnerable if first strike fails. No magic ability. Defeated relatively easily by Rudeus (who uses magic and Eye of Foresight) and by higher-ranked swordsmen.

Guardian Knight / Court Etiquette and Political Aide Skills

Trained from childhood as Princess Ariel's personal knight: etiquette, escort protocol, intelligence gathering, court politics, and security. Functions as Ariel's bodyguard, messenger, and later as her liaison to Rudeus and other factions. Highly competent in noble society navigation, information networks, and managing Ariel's faction logistics while in exile and at university.

Limit: Political skill is derivative of Ariel's genius; on his own he is impulsive and not a strategist. His usefulness is tied to Ariel's plans. No independent power base outside House Notos/Ariel faction.

Enhanced Physical Conditioning (Human - Greyrat lineage)

Above-average human athleticism and stamina due to noble martial training from early childhood. Capable of sustained combat and travel, but not superhuman. Greyrat lineage has no innate magical bloodline advantage; all ability is trained.

Limit: No magical power, no demon eye, no superhuman strength. Vulnerable to magic, assassination, and superior swordsmen. Human lifespan and durability.

Womanizing / Social Charisma (Narrative Trait, not supernatural)

Not a power, but a repeatedly noted social ability/flaw: considerable charm and persistence with women, leading to frequent romantic pursuits (often unsuccessful or comedic). Used narratively to parallel Paul Greyrat's character and to create tension (e.g., his pursuit of Sylphiette and Eris).

Limit: Often backfires; Ariel and other female characters see through it. Source of jealousy and comic humiliation. Does not grant any combat advantage.

Accounts I
Childhood Oath: The Notos Heir and the Second PrincessLight Novel Vol. 7, Vol. 22 (flashbacks); Web Novel Chapter 73+; Redundancy short stories

In-world K413-K420 (Luke age 0-7); published 2014-2018

Born as the eldest son of Pilemon Notos Greyrat, head of the Notos Greyrat house in Millishion, Luke is raised in a dysfunctional noble household where his father openly keeps a mistress and neglects his mother. As a child, he is sent to the Asura Palace to serve as playmate and future guardian knight to Ariel Anemoi Asura, the second princess. Despite Ariel being two years older, the two form an inseparable bond. When Ariel is targeted by court factions, Luke swears a knight's oath to protect her unto death, a vow that defines his entire life. This backstory is expanded in the light novel to explain his absolute loyalty — Ariel was the first person to truly value him beyond his house name. Establishes Luke's core motivation and explains why he abandons Notos house interests to follow Ariel into exile. Sets up his inferiority complex and need for validation.

The Asura Exile: Flight from the Palace PurgeLight Novel Vol. 7, Vol. 9; Web Novel Chapter 74-80

In-world K420 (Luke age 7, Ariel age 9); published 2015

Minister Darius Silva Ganius orchestrates the assassination of Ariel's mother and brothers to secure the succession for Prince Grabel. Ariel, Luke, and their small guard (including Derrick Redbat and later Ghislaine Dedoldia's predecessor) flee the capital Ars under pursuit. Luke, though only a child, acts as Ariel's shield and messenger, witnessing the murder of allies. The group survives through Ariel's wit and the protection of Ghislaine and later Perugius Dola's intervention, eventually going into long exile. Luke's experience here cements his trauma and loyalty. Ariel's faction becomes fugitives; Luke's identity shifts from noble heir to exiled knight. Directly leads to their long-term strategy of building power at Ranoa University.

Ranoa University Arc: The Guardian Knight Meets Rudeus GreyratLight Novel Vol. 9-10; Web Novel Chapters 130-150; Anime Season 2 Episodes 1-12

In-world K421-K423 (Luke age 15-16); published 2016-2017

Ariel enrolls at Ranoa University of Magic to build a power base and recruit talented commoners and nobles, with Luke as her escort and fellow student. There they encounter Rudeus Greyrat (enrolled as 'Fitz' is actually Sylphiette) and his circle. Luke initially views Rudeus with jealousy and suspicion — Ariel shows unusual interest in recruiting Rudeus, and Luke attempts to assert dominance by challenging Rudeus and by flirting with Sylphiette (as Fitz) and later Eris. He duels Rudeus and loses due to Rudeus's magic and foresight. Over time, Luke comes to respect Rudeus's power and Ariel's judgment, becoming a bridge between Ariel's faction and Rudeus. He also serves as comic relief for his failed womanizing. Rudeus agrees to become Ariel's ally and later her key supporter for the Asura succession. Luke's relationship with Rudeus evolves from rivalry to kinship (recognizing their shared Greyrat blood). Establishes Luke's role as Ariel's recruiter.

Accounts II
The Notos Family Collapse: Pilemon's DownfallLight Novel Vol. 14, Vol. 22; Web Novel Chapter 180+; Redundancy

In-world K423-K424; published 2018-2020

While Luke is at university, his father Pilemon Notos Greyrat's corruption and affair are exposed. Pilemon had been embezzling and, under Darius's influence, had neglected his duties and abused his wife. With Ariel's faction gaining power, Pilemon is purged — his mistress is revealed to be a Darius plant, and his house is disgraced. Luke is forced to confront that his family name is now a liability; Ariel protects him, formally detaching him from Notos house and making him solely her knight. Luke chooses Ariel over his blood family, renouncing his inheritance in all but name. This arc mirrors Paul Greyrat's own family failures and shows Luke breaking the cycle by choosing loyalty over lineage. Luke becomes legally and emotionally independent of House Notos; his status now derives entirely from Ariel. Clears the way for his later appointment as knight commander.

The Asura Succession War: Return to Ars and Queen ArielLight Novel Vol. 22-24; Web Novel Chapters 220-240

In-world K424-K425 (Luke age 19-20); published 2020-2021

With Rudeus, Orsted, Perugius, and other allies secured, Ariel returns to Asura to claim the throne after King Asura's death. Darius Silva Ganius backs Prince Grabel; Ariel's faction wages a political and covert war. Luke serves as Ariel's vanguard, bodyguard, and commander of her knights, fighting in skirmishes against Darius's forces and protecting Ariel from multiple assassination attempts (including a notable attack by North God-style assassins). He fights alongside Rudeus, Eris, Ghislaine, and Isolte. After Darius is exposed for his crimes (including the earlier murders and his role in the displacement of Beast Race), Ariel is crowned Queen Ariel Anemoi Asura. Luke is appointed Knight Commander / Captain of the Queen's Guard, the highest honor for a Notos Greyrat in generations. Ariel's victory secures Luke's life purpose; he achieves his childhood oath. The Notos house is restored under new leadership loyal to the crown. Luke remains at Ariel's side as her most trusted knight into the epilogue.

Epilogue and Redundancy: The Knight Who StayedLight Novel Vol. 26 (Epilogue); Redundancy Vol. 2; Jobless Oblige

In-world K425-K430+; published 2022-2023

In the epilogue and Redundancy side stories, an adult Luke (early 20s) continues to serve Queen Ariel, now managing the palace guard and Ariel's intelligence network. Stories depict his ongoing womanizing (to Ariel's exasperation), his mentorship of younger knights, his reunion with Rudeus's family, and hints at his own eventual marriage prospects (left ambiguous — Rifujin has stated Luke's romantic future is intentionally not tied to Ariel romantically, despite fan speculation). He attends Rudeus and Sylphiette's family gatherings as kin, representing the reconciled Greyrat branches. Provides closure: Luke's loyalty is rewarded with purpose and honor, not tragedy. Shows the long-term stability of Ariel's reign.

Who It Is
Form

Hermaeus Mora is often depicted as a massive, serpentine creature with a humanoid head and a large, glowing eye. He is said to be able to manipulate time and space, and is often associated with the concept of knowledge and secrets.

Powers

The Power of Knowledge Hermaeus Mora has the ability to grant mortals knowledge and power, but at a terrible cost. He is said to be able to manipulate the concept of knowledge and secrets, and is often associated with the idea of 'devouring' knowledge and secrets.

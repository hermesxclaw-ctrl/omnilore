Who It Is

The Daedric Prince of Knowledge and Memory, also known as the Demon of Knowledge. Hermaeus Mora appears as a writhing mass of tentacles and eyes in his Oblivion realm of Apocrypha — an infinite library of forbidden knowledge. He collects secrets, arcane lore, and lost histories, offering them to mortals at terrible cost. His realm is filled with poisonous black books and lurking Seekers.

Powers

Omniscience regarding hidden knowledge

Tentacle manifestation and control

Black Book creation (lore-books that trap readers)

Creation of Seekers and Lurkers (servants)

Soul trapping and memory extraction

Realm of Apocrypha (infinite library dimension)

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

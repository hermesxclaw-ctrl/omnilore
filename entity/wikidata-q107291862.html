Who It Is

In the bustling harbor city of Liyue, where commerce and ancient contracts shape daily life, a young exorcist walks the streets with a heavy burden: his very presence can dispel spirits, but it also threatens to freeze the world around him. Chongyun, the 'Child of the Sun' of the Chongyun family, is a master of exorcism whose pure yang energy is both his greatest weapon and his most persistent curse.

Chongyun is earnest, disciplined, and deeply committed to his exorcist duties. He is somewhat naive and easily flustered, especially around spicy food, which he cannot eat due to his pure yang energy. He is determined to prove himself as a capable exorcist, but his condition often causes unintended freezing. He is kind-hearted and values his friendships.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Chongyun' (重云) in Chinese literally means 'heavy clouds' or 'layered clouds', which may reflect his association with the sky and his ability to summon ice and mist.

Pronunciation — CHONG-yoon (English); Chóngyún (Mandarin)

Aliases — Child of the Sun, The Exorcist, Chongyun of the Chongyun Family

Ownership — HoYoverse (formerly miHoYo), a Chinese video game company. Chongyun is a fictional character from the game Genshin Impact.

Powers
Cryo Vision

Chongyun wields a Cryo Vision, allowing him to channel ice elemental energy. He can create ice blades, freeze enemies, and reduce their movement speed.

Limit: His Cryo abilities are limited by his Vision and his physical stamina. He cannot use them indefinitely.

Pure Yang Energy

Chongyun was born with an exceptionally pure yang energy, which naturally repels evil spirits and ghosts. This energy is so strong that it causes him to have a high body temperature, and he often feels warm even in cold environments.

Limit: His pure yang energy makes it difficult for him to be near spirits, as they are repelled or harmed. It also makes him susceptible to overheating, and he must avoid spicy food and hot environments.

Exorcism Techniques

Chongyun is trained in traditional Liyue exorcism techniques, including the use of talismans, rituals, and his claymore. He can perform exorcisms to cleanse areas of evil spirits.

Limit: His techniques are effective against spirits, but he relies on his pure yang energy for the most powerful effects. He is still learning and sometimes makes mistakes.

Elemental Skill: Chonghua's Layered Frost

Chongyun strikes the ground with his claymore, creating a circular field of frost that infuses the weapons of allies within it with Cryo energy, allowing them to deal ice damage.

Limit: The field lasts for a limited time and can be dispelled by enemies.

Elemental Burst: Cloud-Parting Star

Chongyun summons three large ice blades that strike the ground, dealing massive Cryo damage to enemies in the area.

Limit: Requires energy to charge, and the damage is affected by his stats.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Chongyun is a young man with pale skin and striking blue eyes. He has short, dark blue hair with a distinctive white streak. He wears a traditional Liyue-style outfit: a white and blue jacket with a high collar, dark pants, and boots. He carries a claymore, which is unusual for an exorcist, and his Vision is attached to his belt.

Symbols — His Vision is a Cryo (ice) Vision, symbolizing his elemental affinity. He often carries a talisman or a scroll related to exorcism. His outfit incorporates cloud motifs, reflecting his name.

Domains — He lives in Liyue, specifically in the harbor city. He often travels to various locations in Liyue to perform exorcisms, especially to areas with high spiritual activity.

Contemporary in-game timeline

Chongyun comes from a long line of exorcists. His pure yang energy makes him exceptionally talented, but it also causes problems: spirits flee from him, making it hard to practice, and he must constantly manage his body temperature. He often goes on quests with his friend Xingqiu, who helps him find spirits to exorcise. Chongyun becomes more confident in his abilities and learns to control his energy better.

The Legend of the 'Child of the Sun'Genshin Impact character story

Contemporary in-game timeline

Due to his pure yang energy, Chongyun is sometimes called the 'Child of the Sun'. This title is both a blessing and a curse, as it makes him stand out and attracts attention from those who might want to use his power. He learns to embrace his identity and uses his power for good.

The Approach

In the bustling harbor city of Liyue, where commerce and ancient contracts shape daily life, a young exorcist walks the streets with a heavy burden: his very presence can dispel spirits, but it also threatens to freeze the world around him. Chongyun, the 'Child of the Sun' of the Chongyun family, is a master of exorcism whose pure yang energy is both his greatest weapon and his most persistent curse.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of supernatural exorcism and spiritual conflict. No graphic violence or disturbing content.
Identity

Chongyun

Pronunciation CHONG-yoon (English); Chóngyún (Mandarin)

Known As

Child of the SunThe ExorcistChongyun of the Chongyun Family

Etymology

The name 'Chongyun' (重云) in Chinese literally means 'heavy clouds' or 'layered clouds', which may reflect his association with the sky and his ability to summon ice and mist.

Cultural Ownership

HoYoverse (formerly miHoYo), a Chinese video game company. Chongyun is a fictional character from the game Genshin Impact.

Disputed Identifications

None. Chongyun is a unique fictional character with no direct real-world mythological counterpart.

Description

Appearance

Chongyun is a young man with pale skin and striking blue eyes. He has short, dark blue hair with a distinctive white streak. He wears a traditional Liyue-style outfit: a white and blue jacket with a high collar, dark pants, and boots. He carries a claymore, which is unusual for an exorcist, and his Vision is attached to his belt.

Symbols & Iconography

His Vision is a Cryo (ice) Vision, symbolizing his elemental affinity. He often carries a talisman or a scroll related to exorcism. His outfit incorporates cloud motifs, reflecting his name.

Behaviour & Personality

Chongyun is earnest, disciplined, and deeply committed to his exorcist duties. He is somewhat naive and easily flustered, especially around spicy food, which he cannot eat due to his pure yang energy. He is determined to prove himself as a capable exorcist, but his condition often causes unintended freezing. He is kind-hearted and values his friendships.

Domains & Habitats

He lives in Liyue, specifically in the harbor city. He often travels to various locations in Liyue to perform exorcisms, especially to areas with high spiritual activity.

Version Differences

No significant version differences in his appearance or personality across game versions. His lore has been expanded through events and character stories, but no contradictions have been noted.

Powers & Abilities

Cryo Vision

Source Version Genshin Impact (all versions)

Description

Chongyun wields a Cryo Vision, allowing him to channel ice elemental energy. He can create ice blades, freeze enemies, and reduce their movement speed.

Limitations

His Cryo abilities are limited by his Vision and his physical stamina. He cannot use them indefinitely.

Contradictions

None known.

Pure Yang Energy

Source Version Genshin Impact (character stories)

Description

Chongyun was born with an exceptionally pure yang energy, which naturally repels evil spirits and ghosts. This energy is so strong that it causes him to have a high body temperature, and he often feels warm even in cold environments.

Limitations

His pure yang energy makes it difficult for him to be near spirits, as they are repelled or harmed. It also makes him susceptible to overheating, and he must avoid spicy food and hot environments.

Contradictions

None known.

Exorcism Techniques

Source Version Genshin Impact (character stories)

Description

Chongyun is trained in traditional Liyue exorcism techniques, including the use of talismans, rituals, and his claymore. He can perform exorcisms to cleanse areas of evil spirits.

Limitations

His techniques are effective against spirits, but he relies on his pure yang energy for the most powerful effects. He is still learning and sometimes makes mistakes.

Contradictions

None known.

Elemental Skill: Chonghua's Layered Frost

Source Version Genshin Impact (gameplay)

Description

Chongyun strikes the ground with his claymore, creating a circular field of frost that infuses the weapons of allies within it with Cryo energy, allowing them to deal ice damage.

Limitations

The field lasts for a limited time and can be dispelled by enemies.

Contradictions

None known.

Elemental Burst: Cloud-Parting Star

Source Version Genshin Impact (gameplay)

Description

Limitations

Contradictions & Disputes

Pure Yang Energy and Cryo Vision

Position A

Chongyun's pure yang energy should make him incompatible with a Cryo Vision, which is associated with cold and yin.

Position A — evidence

Lore suggests that pure yang energy is hot and repels spirits, which would be contradictory to ice.

Position B

The game states that his Vision is a Cryo Vision, and his abilities are Ice-based.

Position B — evidence

In-game attributes and abilities clearly show he uses Cryo.

Scholarly Consensus

This is a fictional contradiction that is not explained in-game. Fans have theorized that his pure yang energy allows him to control ice in a unique way, but no official explanation exists.

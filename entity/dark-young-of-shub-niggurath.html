Who It Is

The monstrous offspring of the Outer God Shub-Niggurath, the Black Goat of the Woods with a Thousand Young. These creatures resemble grotesque, mobile trees with multiple trunk-like legs ending in hooves, and clusters of writhing tentacles where branches would be. They crush and devour sacrifices.

Form

20+ ft tall, tree-like body with 4-6 trunk-like legs ending in cloven hooves, masses of writhing tentacles at the top with sucker-mouths, black-green coloration, dripping viscous slime

Powers

Crushing constriction

Tentacle attacks

Blood/soul draining

Growth from Shub-Niggurath's essence

Who It Is

Cultural Significance

The Spectre forces uncomfortable questions about justice vs. mercy. Stories often explore the necessity and danger of absolute power. During the 1990s, John Ostrander's run delved into comparative theology, with the Spectre encountering concepts of divine justice across multiple faith traditions.

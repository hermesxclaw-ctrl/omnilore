The Approach

Marcus Holloway is a name that carries the weight of a digital-age rebellion—a hacker who turns the tools of surveillance against the system that created them. But who is the man behind the mask? In the sprawling, sun-drenched streets of San Francisco, his story unfolds as a modern legend of resistance, where every line of code is a weapon and every friend a potential casualty. Yet, for all his notoriety, much of his life remains a cipher, a puzzle that even the most dedicated sleuths have only begun to piece together.

Content advisory. Contains themes of vigilantism, hacking, corporate corruption, and violence. Some depictions may be intense for younger audiences.
Identity

Marcus Holloway

Pronunciation MAR-kus HOL-oh-way

Etymology

The name 'Marcus' is of Latin origin, meaning 'warlike' or 'dedicated to Mars,' the Roman god of war. 'Holloway' is an English surname derived from a 'hollow way' or sunken road. The combination suggests a character who is both a warrior and a pathfinder, fitting his role as a hacker leading a resistance.

Cultural Ownership

The character is owned by Ubisoft, a French video game company. He was created as part of the 'Watch Dogs' franchise, which is set in a fictionalized version of the real world.

Disputed Identifications

There is no known dispute regarding the character's identity, as he is a fictional creation. However, some fans have speculated that he might be related to Aiden Pearce, the protagonist of the first 'Watch Dogs' game, but this is unconfirmed and appears to be merely a fan theory.

Description

Appearance

Marcus Holloway is a young African-American man in his mid-20s, with a lean, athletic build. He typically wears a black hoodie, jeans, and a beanie, reflecting his hacker style. He has a distinctive tattoo on his neck and often carries a smartphone and a laptop. His appearance is designed to blend into the urban environment of San Francisco.

Symbols & Iconography

His primary symbol is the DedSec logo, a stylized skull, which represents the hacker collective he joins. He also uses a smartphone as a symbol of his hacking prowess, often seen using it to control various devices.

Behaviour & Personality

Marcus is depicted as charismatic, witty, and socially conscious. He is driven by a desire to expose corruption and fight for privacy rights. He is loyal to his friends and willing to take risks for the greater good. He often uses humor to defuse tense situations and is shown to be tech-savvy and creative in his approach to problems.

Domains & Habitats

He primarily operates in the San Francisco Bay Area, including the city of San Francisco, Oakland, and Silicon Valley. His 'domain' is the digital world, where he navigates networks and surveillance systems with ease.

Version Differences

There is only one canonical version of Marcus Holloway, as he appears solely in 'Watch Dogs 2'. However, he is also referenced in the DLC 'Human Conditions' and the expansion 'No Compromise'. There are no known alternate versions or retellings.

Stories & Myths

The Rise of Marcus Holloway

Source Watch Dogs 2 (2016)Culture Contemporary American, tech culturePeriod Set in the near future (2016)

Summary

Marcus Holloway is a young hacker living in Oakland, California, who is falsely accused of a crime by the city's predictive policing system, ctOS. After being flagged as a potential criminal, he joins the hacker group DedSec to fight against the corrupt system. Together, they expose the illegal activities of major corporations and government agencies, ultimately taking down the Blume Corporation, the creator of ctOS, and its CEO.

Who It Is

Marcus Holloway is a name that carries the weight of a digital-age rebellion—a hacker who turns the tools of surveillance against the system that created them. But who is the man behind the mask? In the sprawling, sun-drenched streets of San Francisco, his story unfolds as a modern legend of resistance, where every line of code is a weapon and every friend a potential casualty. Yet, for all his notoriety, much of his life remains a cipher, a puzzle that even the most dedicated sleuths have only begun to piece together.

Marcus is depicted as charismatic, witty, and socially conscious. He is driven by a desire to expose corruption and fight for privacy rights. He is loyal to his friends and willing to take risks for the greater good. He often uses humor to defuse tense situations and is shown to be tech-savvy and creative in his approach to problems.

Powers
Hacking

Marcus is a skilled hacker, capable of infiltrating networks, controlling city infrastructure, manipulating traffic lights, accessing security cameras, and hacking into personal devices. He can also remotely control drones and cars.

Limit: His hacking abilities are limited by the range of his smartphone and the security systems he encounters. He cannot hack into everything; some systems require special tools or codes.

Combat skills

Marcus is proficient in hand-to-hand combat and can use various weapons, including stun guns and firearms. He is agile and can perform parkour-like movements to traverse the environment.

Limit: He is not a trained soldier; his combat skills are adequate for self-defense but not exceptional. He can be overwhelmed by multiple opponents.

Drone operation

Marcus can deploy and control a quadcopter drone for reconnaissance and hacking purposes. The drone can be used to access remote locations and distract enemies.

Limit: The drone has limited battery life and range, and it can be destroyed by enemies.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Marcus Holloway is a young African-American man in his mid-20s, with a lean, athletic build. He typically wears a black hoodie, jeans, and a beanie, reflecting his hacker style. He has a distinctive tattoo on his neck and often carries a smartphone and a laptop. His appearance is designed to blend into the urban environment of San Francisco.

Symbols — His primary symbol is the DedSec logo, a stylized skull, which represents the hacker collective he joins. He also uses a smartphone as a symbol of his hacking prowess, often seen using it to control various devices.

Domains — He primarily operates in the San Francisco Bay Area, including the city of San Francisco, Oakland, and Silicon Valley. His 'domain' is the digital world, where he navigates networks and surveillance systems with ease.

The Rise of Marcus HollowayWatch Dogs 2 (2016)

Set in the near future (2016)

Marcus Holloway is a young hacker living in Oakland, California, who is falsely accused of a crime by the city's predictive policing system, ctOS. After being flagged as a potential criminal, he joins the hacker group DedSec to fight against the corrupt system. Together, they expose the illegal activities of major corporations and government agencies, ultimately taking down the Blume Corporation, the creator of ctOS, and its CEO. The downfall of Blume and the exposure of their surveillance programs lead to public outrage and a shift in public opinion about privacy. Marcus becomes a hero to many, but also gains enemies.

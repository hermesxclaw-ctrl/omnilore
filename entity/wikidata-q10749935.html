Who It Is

A blonde aristocrat from a wealthy French family, Elisabeth Blanctorche is far more than a socialite. In the world of The King of Fighters, she is a guardian of sacred treasures, wielding a unique fighting style that blends elegance with the power of light. Her quest to protect her childhood friend Ash Crimson, even as he embarks on a path of betrayal, defines one of the series' most poignant and tragic storylines.

Elisabeth is calm, composed, and highly disciplined. She is deeply loyal to her family and her friend Ash Crimson, even when he betrays her. She is determined and relentless in her pursuit of Ash, but also shows compassion and a sense of duty. She is not easily flustered and maintains her composure even in battle.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Elisabeth' is of Hebrew origin meaning 'God is my oath' or 'God's promise'. 'Blanctorche' is a French-sounding surname, possibly derived from 'blanc' (white) and 'torche' (torch), evoking light and purity, which fits her light-based powers and noble demeanor.

Pronunciation — eh-LIZ-uh-beth BLAN-torch

Powers
Light-based combat

Elisabeth uses light-based attacks, projecting beams and orbs of light. Her fighting style is elegant and graceful, incorporating kicks and acrobatic maneuvers.

Limit: Her abilities are not as overtly supernatural as some other characters; she relies on agility and technique.

Yata Mirror powers

As a descendant of the Yata clan, she has a connection to the Yata Mirror, one of the three Sacred Treasures. In the story, she uses this power to counter Ash's powers and protect the balance.

Limit: The exact extent of her powers is not fully explained, and she does not use the mirror directly in gameplay.

Martial arts mastery

She is highly skilled in hand-to-hand combat, particularly with kicks and acrobatic movements.

Limit: No supernatural enhancements to her physical abilities.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Elisabeth is a tall, slender woman with long blonde hair and blue eyes. She typically wears a white and blue dress-like outfit with a high collar, long gloves, and heeled boots. Her attire is elegant and aristocratic, reflecting her noble background. In later appearances, her design is slightly updated but retains the same color scheme and sophistication.

Symbols — Her fighting style incorporates the use of light, often manifesting as glowing orbs or beams. She is associated with the 'Sacred Treasures' and the power of the 'Yata Mirror'. Her color scheme of white and blue symbolizes purity and nobility.

Domains — As a member of the French aristocracy, she is associated with France and high society. She is also connected to the supernatural realm of the KOF universe, particularly the world of the Sacred Treasures and the clan that guards the Yata Mirror.

Accounts I
The Ash SagaThe King of Fighters XI, XIII

2005-2010

Elisabeth Blanctorche is introduced in KOF XI as a member of the 'Another Team' with Duo Lon and Shen Woo. She is revealed to be a childhood friend of Ash Crimson, who has betrayed his allies and is stealing the powers of the three Sacred Treasures. In KOF XIII, Elisabeth teams up with Duo Lon and Shen Woo to stop Ash, but ultimately fails. In the game's ending, Ash is erased from existence after his plan backfires, and Elisabeth is left to mourn his loss, realizing that Ash had been trying to protect the world from 'Those from the Past' all along. Ash's erasure results in a timeline paradox, leading to the events of KOF XIV. Elisabeth's role in the story is concluded, and she appears as a cameo in subsequent games.

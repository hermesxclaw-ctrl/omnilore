Who It Is

The recurring phenomenon in Local 58 where the fictional TV station's broadcast is hijacked by an unknown force — possibly the Moon itself, a rogue government agency, or an entity using media to condition humanity. These intrusions deliver emergency alerts, surreal imagery, or 'public service announcements' that contain veiled threats. The most famous: 'REAL SLEEP' (a video meant to prevent dreaming of the Moon's face), 'CONTINGENCY' (a presidential address about mass suicide), and 'SHOW FOR CHILDREN' (a cartoon that ends with a child being stalked by a skeletal entity).

Form

Manifests as sudden interruptions to Local 58's programming. Visual hallmarks: black backgrounds with white text, VHS tracking artifacts, corrupted audio, emergency alert tones, and the station's call sign displayed persistently.

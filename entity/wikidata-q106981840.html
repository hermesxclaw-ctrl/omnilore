The Approach

In the vast, wine-soaked armies of Dionysus, where satyrs danced and battled their way to India, one name flickers in the ancient text of Nonnus: Lenobius, a leader of the satyrs, whose story survives only in a single epic line.

Content advisory. None
Identity

Lenobius

Pronunciation leh-NOH-bee-us

Etymology

The name Lenobius is a Latinized form of the Greek Ληνοβίῳ (Lēnobiōi). The etymology is uncertain, but it may derive from ληνός (lēnos), meaning 'wine-press' or 'trough', suggesting a connection to wine and Dionysiac rites.

Cultural Ownership

Greek mythology, specifically the epic tradition of Nonnus of Panopolis (5th century AD).

Disputed Identifications

None known; Lenobius is a minor figure appearing only once in the Dionysiaca.

SCP-4975 — Time's Up

also known asTimes UpSCP-4975
Typescp
CultureSCP Foundation
Who It Is
Form

SCP-4975 is a vaguely humanoid entity with various avian attributes, including a beak-like mouth and taloned fingers. It is often depicted as having a dark, mottled plumage and glowing red eyes. The entity's size and shape can vary, but it is typically around 2-3 meters tall and has a wingspan of around 5-6 meters.

Powers

Time Manipulation SCP-4975 is capable of manipulating time and space, allowing it to appear and disappear at will. It is also thought to be able to slow down or speed up time, depending on its needs.

The Approach

In the lake-ringed kingdom of Moirang, where every hill has a guardian deity and every lineage remembers its exile, a name surfaces briefly in indexes of Meitei heroic legend — Nugire Yainu. She is not a pan-Indian goddess with a thousand temples, but a local hero whose story survives in the seams of oral tradition, waiting for a full translation to bring her into clear view.

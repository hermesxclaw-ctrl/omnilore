Who It Is

A spinning ball of chakra created and compressed in the palm of the user's hand. Developed by the Fourth Hokage, Minato Namikaze, over three years by observing the Tailed Beast Ball. It requires immense chakra control and does not require hand signs. Naruto later developed several variations including the Wind Release: Rasenshuriken.

Powers

Spiraling chakra ball causing massive internal damage

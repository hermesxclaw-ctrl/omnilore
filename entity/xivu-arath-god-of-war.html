Etymology

The name 'Xivu Arath' is of Hive origin, likely derived from the Hive language. 'Xivu' may relate to 'war' or 'battle', while 'Arath' could signify 'god' or 'ruler'. The full title 'God of War' indicates her domain.

Cultural Ownership

Xivu Arath is a fictional character from the Destiny franchise, created by Bungie, Inc. She is part of the Hive, a fictional alien race in the game.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans have speculated that Xivu Arath might be connected to the Worm Gods or the Darkness itself, but these are fan theories and not confirmed by official sources.

Archive Dossier — Approach

In the shattered halls of the Ascendant Plane, where the Hive carve their thrones from the bones of dead gods, Xivu Arath stands as the eternal herald of war—a being who does not merely wage conflict, but becomes it, feeding on the very act of battle across countless worlds.

Oryx knew the Deep. Savathûn knows the lie. I know only the war. I am the war. Every battle ever fought echoes through my bones. Every war ever waged feeds my strength. You cannot kill war. You can only fight it forever.

Who It Is

Xivu Arath is the Hive God of War — the third sibling of the Osmium Court who, alongside Oryx and Savathûn, made the pact with the Worm Gods that transformed the Hive into a galaxy-conquering species. Unlike Oryx (who seeks understanding of the Deep) and Savathûn (who seeks freedom through cunning), Xivu Arath's purpose is pure and simple: war. She exists for conflict, grows stronger through conflict, and cannot conceive of existence without conflict. Xivu Arath's power is unique among the Hive gods: she feeds not on death but on war itself — the act of organized conflict between armies. Every battle fought in her name, every war waged across the universe, makes her stronger. Her power scales with the scale of the conflict: a planetary war makes her a planet-level threat; a galactic war makes her a galactic threat. In theory, if a war encompassed the entire universe, Xivu Arath would become a universal-level entity. Xivu Arath commands the Hive's military forces — her legions are the most disciplined, numerous, and fanatical of all the Hive. She is the general who never sleeps, the strategist who has fought wars for billions of years. Her Ascendant Realm is the High War — a dimension of endless battle where she has refined the art of conflict to its purest form. Xivu Arath represents the terrifying inevitability of war as a cosmic principle. She is not a commander who chooses war; she IS war, made manifest. Peace is not an option in a universe that contains Xivu Arath — because her existence proves that conflict is fundamental to reality. She is the proof that the universe is a battlefield, and that every living thing is either a soldier or prey.

Archive Dossier — Description

Appearance

Xivu Arath appears as a massive Hive entity, typically depicted as a towering figure with chitinous armor, multiple arms, and a skeletal, insectoid head. She is often shown wielding a massive axe or sword. In some artwork, she has a more feminine silhouette, but her form is monstrous and imposing.

Symbols & Iconography

Associated with war, battle, and conquest. Her symbol is often a stylized axe or a Hive rune representing war. She is also linked to the color red, representing bloodshed.

Behaviour & Personality

Xivu Arath embodies the concept of war. She is ruthless, cunning, and relentless. She does not seek peace or diplomacy; she exists to perpetuate conflict and feed on the deaths it causes. She is methodical and patient, viewing war as a grand strategy rather than mindless slaughter. She respects strength and despises weakness.

Domains & Habitats

Xivu Arath resides in the Ascendant Plane, a throne world connected to her own. Her domain is a war-torn realm, filled with battlefields and the remnants of conquered civilizations. She also commands her Hive legions across the physical universe, waging war on countless worlds.

Version Differences

Xivu Arath has been mentioned in lore since The Taken King (2015) but only appeared in-game as a character in Season of the Lost (2021) and later in The Witch Queen (2022) via cutscenes and dialogues. Her physical appearance has remained consistent, though her role has expanded from a background figure to an active antagonist.

Archive Dossier — Powers

War-Mind

Source Version All appearances

Description

Xivu Arath is a strategic genius, able to orchestrate massive military campaigns across entire star systems. She learns from every battle and adapts her tactics.

Limitations

She is not omniscient and can be outmaneuvered by superior strategy or surprise.

Contradictions

None known.

Ascendant Realm

Source Version Books of Sorrow and in-game lore

Description

As an Ascendant Hive, Xivu Arath has a throne world in the Ascendant Plane. She can only be permanently killed within her throne world, and she can resurrect as long as it exists.

Limitations

If her throne world is destroyed, she becomes vulnerable to permanent death.

Contradictions

Some lore suggests that Hive gods can be killed outside their throne worlds if their tribute is disrupted, but this is not fully explained.

Tribute System

Source Version Books of Sorrow

Description

Xivu Arath gains power from the tribute of her followers and the deaths caused in her name. The more war she wages, the stronger she becomes.

Limitations

If her tribute is cut off or she fails to feed, she weakens.

Contradictions

None known.

Darkness Powers

Source Version Various lore

Description

Xivu Arath wields the Darkness, specifically the power of War. She can command the Hive's arcane magic, including the ability to create Taken (though this is more associated with Oryx). She can also use the Darkness to corrupt and destroy.

Limitations

Her powers are tied to her tribute and her connection to the Darkness, which can be severed or diminished.

Contradictions

The exact extent of her Darkness abilities is not fully detailed.

Physical Prowess

Source Version In-game cutscenes and lore

Description

Xivu Arath is an incredibly powerful combatant, able to cleave through armies and even challenge Guardians in direct combat.

Limitations

She is not invincible and can be wounded or killed under the right circumstances.

Contradictions

None known.

Archive Dossier — Relationships

Oryx, the Taken King

Type siblingSource Books of Sorrow

Description

Xivu Arath is Oryx's sister. They have a complex relationship, often working together but also having moments of conflict. Oryx was the leader of the Hive, and Xivu Arath served as his general.

Version Note

In the Books of Sorrow, Xivu Arath and Oryx have a pact of mutual respect, but they also have a history of betrayal and reconciliation.

Conflicting Accounts

Some lore suggests that Xivu Arath may have had darker intentions toward Oryx, but this is not confirmed.

Savathûn, the Witch Queen

Type siblingSource Various lore

Description

Xivu Arath is Savathûn's sister. Their relationship is strained, as Savathûn's cunning often conflicts with Xivu Arath's direct approach. They have fought alongside each other but also against each other.

Version Note

In recent seasons, Savathûn has been trying to escape her worm and has betrayed Xivu Arath, leading to open hostility.

Conflicting Accounts

The exact nature of their current relationship is not fully clear; they may be at war.

The Worm Gods

Type creatorSource Books of Sorrow

Description

Xivu Arath's power comes from her worm, which she made a pact with on Fundament. The worm demands tribute through war and death.

Version Note

The Worm Gods are the source of the Hive's power, but they are also parasitic and demand constant feeding.

Conflicting Accounts

The Worm Gods' ultimate motives are unclear; they may be agents of the Darkness.

The Darkness

Type patronSource Various lore

Description

Xivu Arath is a servant of the Darkness, which empowers her and the Hive. She sees the Darkness as a fundamental force of the universe.

Version Note

The Darkness is a cosmic entity that opposes the Light. Xivu Arath's connection to it is through her worm and her use of Darkness powers.

Conflicting Accounts

The exact relationship between the Darkness and the Hive gods is a subject of debate.

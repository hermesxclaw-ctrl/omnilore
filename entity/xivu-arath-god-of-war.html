Who It Is

In the shattered halls of the Ascendant Plane, where the Hive carve their thrones from the bones of dead gods, Xivu Arath stands as the eternal herald of war—a being who does not merely wage conflict, but becomes it, feeding on the very act of battle across countless worlds.

Xivu Arath embodies the concept of war. She is ruthless, cunning, and relentless. She does not seek peace or diplomacy; she exists to perpetuate conflict and feed on the deaths it causes. She is methodical and patient, viewing war as a grand strategy rather than mindless slaughter. She respects strength and despises weakness.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Xivu Arath' is of Hive origin, likely derived from the Hive language. 'Xivu' may relate to 'war' or 'battle', while 'Arath' could signify 'god' or 'ruler'. The full title 'God of War' indicates her domain.

Pronunciation — Zee-voo Ah-rath

Aliases — Xivu Arath, the Hive God of War, The God of War, Sister of Oryx, Daughter of the Osmium King, The War God, Xivu Arath, the Hive War God

Ownership — Xivu Arath is a fictional character from the Destiny franchise, created by Bungie, Inc. She is part of the Hive, a fictional alien race in the game.

Powers
War-Mind

Xivu Arath is a strategic genius, able to orchestrate massive military campaigns across entire star systems. She learns from every battle and adapts her tactics.

Limit: She is not omniscient and can be outmaneuvered by superior strategy or surprise.

Ascendant Realm

As an Ascendant Hive, Xivu Arath has a throne world in the Ascendant Plane. She can only be permanently killed within her throne world, and she can resurrect as long as it exists.

Limit: If her throne world is destroyed, she becomes vulnerable to permanent death.

Tribute System

Xivu Arath gains power from the tribute of her followers and the deaths caused in her name. The more war she wages, the stronger she becomes.

Limit: If her tribute is cut off or she fails to feed, she weakens.

Darkness Powers

Xivu Arath wields the Darkness, specifically the power of War. She can command the Hive's arcane magic, including the ability to create Taken (though this is more associated with Oryx). She can also use the Darkness to corrupt and destroy.

Limit: Her powers are tied to her tribute and her connection to the Darkness, which can be severed or diminished.

Physical Prowess

Xivu Arath is an incredibly powerful combatant, able to cleave through armies and even challenge Guardians in direct combat.

Limit: She is not invincible and can be wounded or killed under the right circumstances.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Xivu Arath appears as a massive Hive entity, typically depicted as a towering figure with chitinous armor, multiple arms, and a skeletal, insectoid head. She is often shown wielding a massive axe or sword. In some artwork, she has a more feminine silhouette, but her form is monstrous and imposing.

Symbols — Associated with war, battle, and conquest. Her symbol is often a stylized axe or a Hive rune representing war. She is also linked to the color red, representing bloodshed.

Domains — Xivu Arath resides in the Ascendant Plane, a throne world connected to her own. Her domain is a war-torn realm, filled with battlefields and the remnants of conquered civilizations. She also commands her Hive legions across the physical universe, waging war on countless worlds.

Accounts I
The Osmium King's DaughtersBooks of Sorrow

Pre-Hive, ancient history

Xivu Arath was born as the middle daughter of the Osmium King on the Fundament, a gas giant world. She and her sisters, Aurash (later Oryx) and Sathona (later Savathûn), discovered the Worm Gods and made a pact to gain power and escape their dying world. Xivu Arath's worm demanded that she wage war and feed on the deaths of others. The sisters became the Hive gods, and Xivu Arath became the God of War, leading the Hive in countless conquests.

The Hive's Conquest of the UniverseBooks of Sorrow and various lore

Millions of years before Destiny's main events

Under Xivu Arath's military leadership, the Hive waged a genocidal campaign across the universe, exterminating countless civilizations to feed their worms and grow in power. Xivu Arath orchestrated the destruction of entire star systems, turning them into war zones. The Hive became one of the most feared forces in the universe, and Xivu Arath's power grew immense.

Accounts II
The Battle of TorobatlDestiny 2: Season of the Lost

Recent past

Xivu Arath led a massive Hive assault on the Cabal homeworld of Torobatl, overwhelming the Cabal forces and forcing their evacuation. This event was a major turning point in the conflict between the Hive and the Cabal. The Cabal lost their homeworld, and Empress Caiatl became a refugee leader, eventually allying with the Vanguard against the Hive.

The Arrival on EarthDestiny 2: Season of the Lost and Beyond Light

Recent events

Xivu Arath's forces have been attacking Earth and the Last City, attempting to conquer the solar system. She has been a major antagonist in various seasons, orchestrating attacks and attempting to corrupt Guardians. The Vanguard and their allies have managed to repel her forces so far, but the threat remains.

Xivu Arath is the strongest Hive god. — While she is incredibly powerful, Oryx was traditionally considered the strongest due to his ability to Take. Savathûn is also extremely powerful through her cunning. Xivu Arath's power is tied to war, and in times of peace she may be weaker.

Xivu Arath is the same as the Worm Gods. — She is a Hive god who made a pact with a worm, but she is not a Worm God herself. The Worm Gods are ancient beings that grant power to the Hive.

Xivu Arath can only be killed in her throne world. — While Ascendant Hive can only be permanently killed in their throne worlds, there are exceptions and other ways to kill them, such as severing their tribute. This is not a hard rule.

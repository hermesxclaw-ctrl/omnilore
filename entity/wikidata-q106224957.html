The Approach

Alfendi Layton, the brilliant and eccentric Scotland Yard inspector with a dark secret, is the son of the beloved Professor Hershel Layton. But unlike his father, Alfendi's methods are as unpredictable as his mood swings, and his mind holds a mystery far more dangerous than any case he investigates.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of mental illness, mood disorders, and potentially disturbing behavior, as well as themes of family conflict and shadows of a troubled past.
Identity

Alfendi Layton

Pronunciation al-FEN-dee LAY-tun

Etymology

The name 'Alfendi' is a given name, possibly derived from 'Alfred' with a unique spelling. 'Layton' is the family surname, shared with his father, Professor Hershel Layton.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese video game franchise 'Professor Layton', created by Level-5. Character appears in the spin-off 'Layton Brothers: Mystery Room'.

Disputed Identifications

There is no dispute over his identity within the franchise, but fans have speculated about his mother and other family details not officially revealed.

Description

Appearance

Alfendi is a young man with light brown hair, often seen wearing a white coat or suit, reflecting his detective role. He has a distinctive, intense gaze and a somewhat unkempt appearance when lost in thought.

Symbols & Iconography

His iconic accessory is a magnifying glass, a nod to his detective work. His white coat is reminiscent of a lab coat, hinting at his analytical mind.

Behaviour & Personality

Alfendi is highly intelligent and observant, but he suffers from a split personality or mood disorder. When his 'other side' emerges, his demeanor becomes darker, more aggressive, and unpredictable. Despite this, he is dedicated to solving mysteries and often uses unconventional methods.

Domains & Habitats

He works at Scotland Yard in London, specifically in the 'Mystery Room' division, where he solves cases that baffle other detectives.

Version Differences

In his cameo as a child, he appears innocent and cheerful, contrasting sharply with his adult personality. No other major versions exist.

Split Personality

Source Version Layton Brothers: Mystery Room

Description

Alfendi has an alternate personality that is more aggressive and ruthless, often emerging during intense situations.

Limitations

This state is involuntary and can lead to unpredictable behavior, sometimes endangering himself or others.

The Mystery Room Cases

Summary

Alfendi, as an inspector at Scotland Yard, works with his junior partner Lucy Baker to solve a series of murder cases. Each case presents a puzzle that Alfendi must unravel, often using his unique insight and sometimes his darker side to see the truth.

Who It Is

According to traditional accounts, at the heart of countless family photo albums in the SCP universe lurks a man everyone swears they’ve known forever—a middle-aged cousin with a crude joke, a friendly smile, and a knack for showing up at baptisms, weddings, and funerals. Only later, when children drown themselves, marriages implode, and blood-soaked caskets become the center of cannibalistic feasts, does the pattern emerge: Cousin Johnny was there every time. SCP-2852 turns the most intimate rites of passage into engines of generational ruin, weaponizing the trust we place in family and faith until the very idea of a "harmless" relative feels like a trap.

Attested: SCP-2852 instances are described as sentient but not sapient, lacking coherent language and speaking in "word salad" despite being perceived as intelligible by affected individuals. They are consistently accepted as normal, playful, and possessing a crude sense of humor by event participants, who report fondness toward Cousin Johnny regardless of his actions. The entity appears at compatible religious events (initially Roman Catholic, later extended to Anglican) and assumes socially appropriate roles: third godparent at baptisms, groomsman at weddings, eulogizer at funer

Ritual infiltration and social acceptance

Attested: SCP-2852 can appear at Catholic and Anglican family religious functions—baptisms, weddings, funerals—and is universally accepted by attendees as an established family member, "Cousin Johnny," regardless of actual resemblance, prior acquaintance, or ethnic mismatch. Participants treat its presence and role as normal, integrating it into kinship narratives. This effect includes retroactive normalization: people remember Cousin Johnny as having always been part of the family.

Limit: Attested: The entity is currently documented only in Roman Catholic and Anglican contexts in North America. Foundation procedures note that any attachment to other religious groups would be anomalous and must be reported, implying a limitation or at least a strong pattern. The effect seems restricted to ritual gatherings rather than everyday life.

Perceptual and linguistic distortion

Attested: SCP-2852 speaks in incoherent "word salad" and lacks vocal cords or analogous organs, yet all individuals at a compatible event perceive its speech as intelligible and appropriate. They regard its actions as normal unless previously briefed about its anomalous properties. This suggests a cognitohazard or memetic effect altering perception and comprehension.

Limit: Attested: Individuals who have been briefed on SCP-2852’s anomalous nature can recognize its speech as nonsensical and its actions as abnormal, indicating that prior knowledge can partially counteract the perceptual distortion.

Blue-Level event effects (baptisms)

Attested: At baptisms, SCP-2852 acts as a third godparent. During the rite, the child’s skin sloughs off harmlessly and is consumed by the godparents, including Cousin Johnny. Long-term consequences documented include increased child mortality within six months, development of Cluster B personality disorders among surviving children, parental sterility, and elevated risk of suicide by drowning among parents and godparents. These effects are described as statistically significant patterns in affected families.

Limit: Attested: Effects are tied to participation in the anomalous baptism; individuals not present or not directly involved appear less affected. Foundation monitoring focuses on all individuals involved in Blue-Level events.

White-Level event effects (weddings)

Attested: At weddings, SCP-2852 serves as a groomsman. Participants painlessly extract their teeth, which the couple then consumes. The groom emits a 140 dB cicada-like vocalization that deafens attendees. Long-term consequences include divorce within two years, infertility among attendees, domestic violence, and high rates of suicide or self-disfigurement among children by age eighteen. These outcomes are described as consistent across documented White-Level events.

Limit: Attested: The event requires SCP-2852’s presence and participation; ordinary weddings do not show these patterns. The cicada-like vocalization appears specific to the groom in the anomalous event.

Black-Level event effects (funerals)

Attested: At funerals, SCP-2852 acts as eulogizer. Attendees exsanguinate themselves into the casket, and SCP-2852 vomits a mixture of blood, wood pulp, and dead cicadas into it. Long-term consequences include family breakdown, sterility, cannibalistic violence, and attempted child-parent murders. These events are considered the most severe and are monitored until conclusion, after which participants receive Class-A amnestics.

Limit: Attested: Black-Level events are rare and particularly dangerous; Foundation protocols treat them differently, emphasizing observation and post-event amnestic treatment rather than direct disruption.

Long-term sociological and psychological impact

Attested: Across event types, SCP-2852’s presence correlates with long-term physical, psychological, and social harm: juvenile delinquency, sterility, domestic violence, divorce, suicide, self-disfigurement, and personality disorders. These impacts extend beyond immediate participants to broader family networks and communities, effectively making SCP-2852 a generational curse on affected kinship groups.

Limit: Attested: Effects are documented in communities with high Catholic/Anglican populations where SCP-2852 events occur; there is no evidence of global or random spread beyond these patterns.

Resistance to conventional containment

Attested: SCP-2852 is classified as Keter, with containment focused on tracking and disrupting incidents rather than physically imprisoning the entity. Mobile Task Forces are assigned to identify and crash events, but SCP-2852’s ability to appear at new gatherings and integrate into families makes permanent containment elusive.

Limit: Attested: Foundation can monitor compatible communities and intervene in events, and amnestics can mitigate cognitive effects post hoc. However, no method for neutralizing or destroying SCP-2852 is documented.

The SCP-2852 file is structured as an in-universe containment document. It introduces SCP-2852 as a Keter-class anomaly appearing as a middle-aged white male, known as Cousin Johnny, who infiltrates Catholic and Anglican family religious rituals. The Special Containment Procedures outline monitoring of compatible communities, deployment of Mobile Task Forces to disrupt events, and extensive use of amnestics. The Description details SCP-2852’s anatomy, speech, and event-based effects. Subsequent sections classify events into Blue-Level (baptisms), White-Level (weddings), and Black-Level (funerals), each with specific ritual deviations and long-term consequences. Addenda and incident notes (where present) elaborate on particular events and the psychological impact on Foundation personnel. Within the fictional universe, the SCP-2852 file establishes Cousin Johnny as a persistent threat to family structures and religious communities. Documented White-Level event case studies (implied)SCP-2852 article (White-Level events section; statistical summaries).

Implied contemporary.

White-Level events involve SCP-2852 acting as a groomsman at weddings. Participants extract their teeth, the couple consumes them, and the groom emits a deafening cicada-like scream. Over the following years, marriages collapse, infertility spreads among attendees, domestic violence escalates, and children suffer severe psychological distress, often culminating in suicide or self-disfigurement. The events function as catalysts for long-term familial breakdown and community-level social harm. Foundation monitoring of divorce and domestic violence rates in affected communities is explicitly tied to SCP-2852 containment.

Implied contemporary.

Black-Level events occur at funerals where SCP-2852 serves as eulogizer. Attendees exsanguinate themselves into the casket, and Cousin Johnny vomits a mixture of blood, wood pulp, and dead cicadas. Afterward, families experience sterility, cannibalistic violence, and attempted child-parent murders. Foundation protocols emphasize observation and post-event amnestic treatment, recognizing the extreme danger and psychological impact. Black-Level events are associated with the most severe breakdown of familial bonds and the emergence of homicidal and cannibalistic behavior. Foundation personnel exposed to these events are monitored for antisocial behavior and may be reassigned to low-intensity work if infected.

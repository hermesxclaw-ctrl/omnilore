Who It Is

Manawydan is the Welsh counterpart of Manannán mac Lir. In the Third Branch, he marries Rhiannon and survives a magical enchantment that empties Dyfed. Through patience and craftsmanship — becoming a saddle-maker, shield-maker, and shoemaker — he forces the enchanter to restore everything.

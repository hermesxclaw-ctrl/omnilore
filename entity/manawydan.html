Form & Signs

Appearance — Manawydan is often depicted as a wise and powerful figure, with a strong connection to the land and its people.

Symbols — The symbol associated with Manawydan is the triskele, a three-armed spiral motif that represents the cycles of life and death.

Domains — Manawydan's domains include the land, the sea, and the Otherworld, reflecting his connection to the natural world and the spirit realm.

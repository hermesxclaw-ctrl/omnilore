Who It Is

Math fab Mathonwy is king of Gwynedd, bound by a geis to keep his feet in a virgin's lap. He can hear any conversation the wind carries. When his footholder was violated, he punished the perpetrators by transforming them into breeding pairs of wild animals for three years. He co-created Blodeuwedd.

“I hear every word the wind carries.”

Who It Is

The word 'testament' carries the weight of finality and truth—a sworn document, a last will, a sacred covenant. But in the realms of fiction and myth, it becomes a vessel for stories of legacy, sacrifice, and revelation, from a biblical epic to a cybernetic warrior's name.

Testament is portrayed as calm, stoic, and somewhat melancholic, bound by duty and a tragic past. They are loyal to their creator and seek redemption.

Powers
Scythe Combat

Testament wields a large scythe in combat, capable of powerful slashing attacks and long-range reach.

Limit: Requires close-to-mid range; slower attacks leave openings.

Dark Magic / Necromancy

Testament can manipulate dark energies, summon familiars (such as the 'Succubus' and 'Exe Beast'), and use curses to weaken opponents.

Limit: Magic usage may drain stamina; some abilities require setup.

Immortality / Undeath

Testament is an undead being, resurrected by the villain That Man, granting them near-immortality and resistance to conventional harm.

Limit: Vulnerable to holy or anti-magic attacks.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In Guilty Gear, Testament is a tall, androgynous figure with long silver hair, often wearing a dark, tattered cloak and carrying a large scythe. Their appearance varies slightly between game iterations, but consistently features a pale complexion and a haunting presence.

Symbols — The scythe is a recurring symbol, representing death and reaping. Their name itself is a symbol of a binding oath or will.

Domains — In the Guilty Gear universe, Testament resides in the outskirts of human civilization, often in ruins or desolate places, reflecting their outcast status.

Accounts I
Testament's OriginGuilty Gear official lore

1995 onwards

Testament was originally a human who became a Gear (a bio-engineered weapon) against their will. They were resurrected by That Man and became a guardian of Dizzy, a half-Gear child, finding a new purpose in protecting her. Testament's role shifts from antagonist to protector, and they become a beloved character in the series.

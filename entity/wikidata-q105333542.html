The Approach

The word 'testament' carries the weight of finality and truth—a sworn document, a last will, a sacred covenant. But in the realms of fiction and myth, it becomes a vessel for stories of legacy, sacrifice, and revelation, from a biblical epic to a cybernetic warrior's name.

Content advisory. This page covers multiple unrelated subjects sharing the name 'Testament'. Some entries, such as the 1983 film 'Testament', deal with themes of nuclear war and death. The Guilty Gear character's story involves violence and identity crisis.
Identity

Testament (Guilty Gear character)

Pronunciation /ˈtɛstəmənt/

Known As

Testament (Guilty Gear)The Reaper of the Battlefield

Etymology

The name 'Testament' derives from Latin 'testamentum', meaning 'a will' or 'a covenant'. In the context of Guilty Gear, it likely evokes a sense of destiny and sworn purpose.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Arc System Works for the Guilty Gear fighting game series; originally Japanese, with international fanbase.

Disputed Identifications

None known; the character is distinct from other 'Testament' entities.

Description

Appearance

In Guilty Gear, Testament is a tall, androgynous figure with long silver hair, often wearing a dark, tattered cloak and carrying a large scythe. Their appearance varies slightly between game iterations, but consistently features a pale complexion and a haunting presence.

Symbols & Iconography

The scythe is a recurring symbol, representing death and reaping. Their name itself is a symbol of a binding oath or will.

Behaviour & Personality

Testament is portrayed as calm, stoic, and somewhat melancholic, bound by duty and a tragic past. They are loyal to their creator and seek redemption.

Domains & Habitats

In the Guilty Gear universe, Testament resides in the outskirts of human civilization, often in ruins or desolate places, reflecting their outcast status.

Version Differences

In early games, Testament was male; in later titles like Guilty Gear Strive, they are explicitly non-binary, using they/them pronouns. Their fighting style and moves have evolved across games.

Testament's Origin

Summary

Testament was originally a human who became a Gear (a bio-engineered weapon) against their will. They were resurrected by That Man and became a guardian of Dizzy, a half-Gear child, finding a new purpose in protecting her.

Contradictions & Disputes

Gender identity of Testament (Guilty Gear)

Position A

Testament is male (as originally presented in early games).

Position A — evidence

Early game manuals and dialogue used male pronouns.

Position B

Testament is non-binary (as confirmed in Guilty Gear Strive).

Position B — evidence

Official profiles in Strive use they/them pronouns, and developers have stated the character is non-binary.

Scholarly Consensus

The latter is the current canon; the earlier male depiction is retconned.

Meaning of 'Testament' in the 1983 film

Position A

The title refers to a last will and testament, symbolizing the family's final days.

Position A — evidence

The film's narrative focuses on the family's legacy.

Position B

It refers to a testament as a statement of truth about nuclear war.

Position B — evidence

The film is an anti-war statement.

Scholarly Consensus

Both interpretations are valid; the title is deliberately ambiguous.

Who It Is

The Archons are a fascinating and complex topic in the realm of Gnostic Christianity, with a rich history and symbolism.

The Archons are described as being sadistic, cruel, and manipulative, using their power to control and dominate humans. They are said to be driven by a desire for power and control, and are willing to use any means necessary to achieve their goals.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In Gnostic tradition, Archons are often depicted as malevolent, sadistic beings with a strong desire to control and dominate humans. They are said to be born from the Demiurge's shadow and are associated with the material world. Their appearance is often described as beast-like, with sharp teeth and claws.

Symbols — The Archons are often associated with the symbol of the Demiurge, which represents the material world and the forces of darkness. They are also linked to the concept of the 'prison' or the 'jail' of the material world, which they are said to rule over.

Domains — The Archons are said to inhabit the material world, which they rule over with an iron fist. They are also associated with the realm of the Demiurge, which represents the forces of darkness and chaos.

Who It Is
Form

In Gnostic tradition, Archons are often depicted as malevolent, sadistic beings with a strong desire to control and dominate humans. They are said to be born from the Demiurge's shadow and are associated with the material world. Their appearance is often described as beast-like, with sharp teeth and claws.

Powers

Control over the material world The Archons are said to have the power to control and dominate the material world, using their influence to shape the thoughts, feelings, and actions of humans.

Accounts

In this story, the Archons are said to have created the material world as a prison for humans, using their power to shape the thoughts, feelings, and actions of humans.

In this story, the Archons are said to have fallen from their position of power, becoming trapped in the material world they created.

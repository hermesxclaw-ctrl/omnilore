Who It Is

In Gnostic cosmology, the Archons (Greek: ἄρχοντες, 'rulers') are the planetary powers who serve the Demiurge and rule over the material universe. Each Archon is associated with one of the seven classical planets and governs the corresponding sphere through which the soul must pass after death to escape the material cosmos and return to the Pleroma. They are the cosmic jailers, preventing souls from ascending to the divine fullness. In the Nag Hammadi texts, the chief Archon is Yaldabaoth, assisted by a hierarchy including Iao, Sabaoth, Adonai, Eloai, Astaphaios, and Horaios — each ruling a planetary sphere. The Gnostic must learn the secret names of each Archon to pass through their gates after death. The term 'archonic' has entered modern discourse as a metaphor for systems of control that keep individuals spiritually imprisoned.

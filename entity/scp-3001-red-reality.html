Who It Is

A parallel dimensional pocket of near-total nonexistence — a void of red darkness where physical laws are gradually degraded. Researcher Dr. Robert Scranton was trapped in this dimension for 5 years when his 'Scranton Reality Anchor' malfunctioned during testing. He slowly dissolved into a state of minimal consciousness, recording his observations on a data drive as his body and mind were eroded to near-nothingness.

Form

An endless void of dim red light, devoid of all matter except what enters through breaches. The space has a measurable 'consciousness decay' effect — anything within it gradually loses complexity until only the simplest patterns remain.

Powers

Reality degradation

Entropic consciousness dissolution

Slow existential erasure

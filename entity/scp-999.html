SCP-999

The Tickle Monster

also known asThe Tickle Monster

Who It Is
Form

SCP-999 is an amorphous, sentient slime entity with no discernible shape or form. It can change its appearance and texture at will, making it difficult to describe or categorize.

Powers

Reality Distortion SCP-999 has the ability to distort reality, causing objects and people to behave in unpredictable and illogical ways. This power is thought to be connected to its ability to change its appearance and texture at will.

Incident Log

SCP-999 was first encountered by the SCP Foundation in 2008, when it was discovered in a containment facility. The entity was described as a 'playful and mischievous' entity that caused chaos and destruction wherever it went.

SCP-999 has a complex and often antagonistic relationship with SCP-682, a highly aggressive and destructive entity. The two entities have been observed to engage in a series of battles, with SCP-999 often emerging victorious.

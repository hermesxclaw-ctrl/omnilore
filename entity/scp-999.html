Who It Is

SCP-999 is an amorphous, gelatinous creature of orange coloration and translucent consistency, approximately the size of a large beanbag chair. It weighs roughly 54 kilograms and has the consistency of peanut butter. SCP-999 emits a pleasant odor similar to chocolate, fresh laundry, and various other comforting scents tailored to the individual perceiving it. The entity has a perpetually cheerful, playful demeanor, behaving similarly to a dog or young child — it enjoys games, physical affection, and making people happy. Its primary anomalous property is its ability to induce intense positive emotions through physical contact. When SCP-999 touches a human, it releases chemicals through its outer membrane that trigger the release of dopamine, serotonin, and endorphins in the subject's brain. This effect can temporarily alleviate depression, PTSD, anxiety, and even psychotic episodes. SCP-999 has been used therapeutically with Foundation personnel suffering from psychological trauma, and it apparently enjoys this role. SCP-999's diet consists entirely of candy and sweets, with M&M's and chocolate bars being its favorite foods. Notably, SCP-999 was observed being able to pacify and amuse SCP-682 during a cross-test — one of the only times the omnicidal reptile has been documented laughing. SCP-999's origin is unique: it is believed to have been created by the Seven Brides, seven powerful reality-bending entities, as a pure being of joy meant to counterbalance the darkness in the universe. Despite its friendly nature, SCP-999 has proven surprisingly resilient; it can disperse physical force across its body and reform after being separated. Foundation personnel universally describe SCP-999 as the most pleasant entity in Foundation custody, and its containment is considered trivially easy — it requires only a simple enclosure and regular social interaction. SCP-999 represents a rare instance of an unequivocally benevolent anomaly in the SCP universe.

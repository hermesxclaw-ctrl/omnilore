The Approach

Behind the counter of her grandmother's dusty pawnshop, surrounded by bonsai trees and half-finished online shopping carts, Arisa Ichigaya would rather stay inside forever — until a relentlessly sparkling classmate drags a red star-shaped guitar into her life and demands she learn to shine on stage.

Content advisory. Mild: themes of social withdrawal/hikikomori-coded behavior, interpersonal conflict and sharp language (tsundere archetype), performance anxiety. No graphic violence, sexual content, or hate content in canonical material.
Identity

Arisa Ichigaya

Pronunciation Japanese: i-chi-ga-ya a-ri-sa [itɕiɡaja aɾisa]; Hepburn: Ichigaya Arisa; English dub: uh-REE-suh ih-chih-GUY-uh (approximated). Voice actress Ayasa Itō pronounces with standard Tokyo accent.

Known As

Ichigaya Arisa市ヶ谷 有咲 (Ichigaya Arisa - Japanese kanji/kana)ArisaAri-chan (occasional fan nickname, not canonical)

Etymology

Surname Ichigaya (市ヶ谷) is a real Tokyo place name (Ichigaya district in Shinjuku/Chiyoda, Tokyo), fitting the franchise's setting in Tokyo's Kita and Shinjuku wards. Given name Arisa (有咲) combines kanji 有 (ari - to have/exist) and 咲 (sa - to bloom). Bushiroad has not published an official in-universe etymological explanation; reading is standard kun/on hybrid. No official meaning statement from creator Kō Nakamura.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character owned by Bushiroad Inc. Created by Takaaki Kidani (concept), Kō Nakamura (original story/lyrics) and character designer Hitowa (original designs) for the BanG Dream! mixed-media franchise. Visual design refined by Craft Egg / Bushiroad for Girls Band Party! and by studio ISSEN × XEBEC / SANZIGEN for anime. No folkloric or public-domain ownership; all rights held by Bushiroad and affiliated partners.

Disputed Identifications

No major disputed identification with historical or mythological figures. Occasional fan conflation with other 'Arisa' characters in Bushiroad franchises is erroneous. Within franchise, clearly distinct from similarly-aged keyboardists: Rinko Shirokane (Roselia), Kanon Matsubara (Hello, Happy World!), Rui Yashio (Morfonica), etc. Early Star Beat manga version of Arisa differs in personality (more openly game-master-like and aloof) leading some wikis to treat her as a separate continuity variant, but Bushiroad treats it as retconned same entity.

Description

Appearance

Arisa is depicted as a petite high school girl, 152cm, with shoulder-length light brown / dark blonde hair styled in low twin-tails tied with black ribbons, and purple-pink eyes. Official art shows pale skin, thin build. Stage outfits follow Poppin'Party's celestial school-uniform motif: navy blazer with star accents, pleated skirt, keyboard-themed accessories; casual wear is indoor loungewear, cardigans, and often headphones. Her keyboard in anime and game is modeled on Roland JUNO-DS and similar compact synths (red/black and later white variants). In chibi Girls Band Party! sprites, she is distinguished by twin-tails and a slightly pouty expression. No supernatural features; design loosely modeled on voice actress Ayasa Itō but not rotoscoped.

Symbols & Iconography

Primary symbols: bonsai trees (her cherished collection, displayed in her room and at the pawnshop), the pawnshop Ryuuseido (流星堂 - 'Shooting Star Hall', family business), keyboards/synthesizers, and indoor/comfort motifs (kotatsu, snacks, curtains drawn). Celestial/star motif shared with Poppin'Party (star hairpins, star beat). Color association in merchandise and game is often light green / mint or purple depending on card, but official band color is not strictly assigned. Bonsai functions as character shorthand for old-fashioned, meticulous indoor hobby contrasting with Kasumi's hyperactivity.

Behaviour & Personality

Attested personality across anime and game: tsundere, sharp-tongued, indoor-oriented (hikikomori-coded but not clinically defined), self-deprecating about being 'gloomy' and 'unmotivated', intensely loyal once committed. She initially resists joining Poppin'Party, calling band activities troublesome and claiming she wants to stay home, but is moved by Kasumi's earnestness. She is intelligent, academically competent, good at crafts and cooking (especially sweets), and manages the pawnshop's inventory. She frequently bickers with Kasumi ('Kasumi, you're noisy!') but shows deep care, arranging practice space in her warehouse/storeroom and supporting band logistics. She dislikes outdoor activities, physical exertion, and being put on the spot, but enjoys internet surfing, online shopping, and tending bonsai. In Star Beat manga, she is more openly manipulative/game-master-like, treating band formation as a 'game' she designed; this was softened in the rebooted anime/game continuity to a more reluctant-but-caring friend. Attested: introverted but not cruel; reconstructed fanon sometimes exaggerates meanness — disputed.

Domains & Habitats

Habitat: Tokyo — family pawnshop Ryuuseido in shopping district near Hanasakigawa Girls' High School, her bedroom (filled with bonsai and keyboard), Hanasakigawa classroom and club warehouse, live houses SPACE (S1) and CiRCLE (S2 onward) and later RiNG. She is a student at Hanasakigawa Girls' High School (first year in S1, second year in S2/S3, third year by 2022-2023 timeline; later depicted as prospective Yotsuba Women's University student in 6th anniversary materials). Domain within band: keyboardist, arranger support, logistics/venue liaison via family storage space. No cosmological domain; slice-of-life contemporary setting.

Version Differences

Star Beat (2015) vs Reboot (2017+): In Star Beat, Arisa is more aloof, wealthy-appearing, and explicitly frames 'BanG_Dream!' as a game she invites Kasumi to play; Kasumi is shy, Arisa is dominant. In anime/game reboot, Arisa is more grounded, tsundere, and gradually befriended rather than recruiting. Appearance is consistent but art style evolves: Ishida's manga has sharper features; anime/game by Hitowa/Craft Egg softens face and brightens hair. Game adds extensive card art variants (summer, Halloween, stage costumes) not present in anime. Live-action performer Ayasa Itō's personality (outgoing, English-fluent background shared with character Chiyu) is not Arisa's; character remains indoor-oriented. No alternate universe evil version; all versions remain human high school keyboardist.

Powers & Abilities

Keyboard / Synthesizer Performance

Source VersionAnime S1E3 onward; Girls Band Party! band stories; official profile

Proficient keyboardist for Poppin'Party; provides harmonic and rhythmic foundation, synth pads, piano lines, and occasional lead melodies. In-universe, she learned quickly after Kasumi recruited her, practicing in the pawnshop warehouse. Not a prodigy like Rinko or Chiyu, but reliable ensemble player.

Limitations

Self-described as not naturally athletic or stage-confident; initially struggled with motivation and stamina for live shows. Requires regular practice; no perfect pitch or supernatural ability claimed.

Contradictions

Game card stats sometimes rank her technique higher than anime suggests; this is gameplay balancing, not lore.

Bonsai Cultivation and General Domestic Crafts

Source VersionOfficial profile, Girls Band Party! area conversations, anime S1E4 (bonsai room)

Expert hobbyist in bonsai care — pruning, watering, styling miniature trees. Also skilled at making sweets, sewing, and organizing inventory at the pawnshop. Shows meticulous, patient temperament in these tasks contrasting with stage nerves.

Limitations

Hobby-level; not professional horticulturist. Bonsai sometimes suffer when she neglects them during tour stress (event stories).

Contradictions

None; consistently attested.

Logistics and Venue Provision

Source VersionAnime S1E3-4, Game 'Poppin'Party is Born!'

Provides Poppin'Party's primary practice space: the storeroom/warehouse above/at the back of Ryuuseido pawnshop. Manages access, keys, and storage of equipment. Also acts as pragmatic planner for budgets and schedules.

Limitations

Space is dependent on grandmother's permission; occasionally threatened when family considers closing or renovating shop (Double Rainbow arc).

Contradictions

Anime shows warehouse as somewhat spacious; game sprites compress it — artistic license.

Girls Band Party! Game Card Skills (Meta)

Source VersionGirls Band Party! card list 2017-2026

In the rhythm game, Arisa cards provide Score Boost, Healing, or Perfect Lock skills depending on rarity. e.g., 4★ [Indoor Enthusiast] Arisa - Score up 110% for 7 seconds. This is gameplay abstraction, not diegetic power.

Limitations

Purely mechanical; does not reflect in-universe superpower. Power creep with newer cards.

Contradictions

Not to be conflated with lore abilities.

Stories & Myths

The Random Star and Ryuuseido Encounter

Source BanG Dream! anime Season 1 Episode 1 'We Did It!' / Star Beat Chapter 1 / Game Band Story 'Poppin'Party is Born!' Chapter 1Culture Japanese mixed-media franchise (Bushiroad)Period 2015 (manga), 2017 (anime/game retelling) - contemporary Tokyo

Summary

First-year Kasumi Toyama, searching for the sparkling 'Star Beat' she heard as a child, follows red star stickers to Arisa's family pawnshop Ryuuseido. There she finds a red ESP Random Star guitar. Arisa, working at the shop after school, is initially annoyed by Kasumi's loud excitement and tries to shoo her away. The guitar is too expensive for Kasumi, but Arisa's grandmother allows Kasumi to take it if she learns to play. Glitter Green's performance at live house SPACE that evening inspires Kasumi to form a band, and she immediately targets Arisa as a member because of the warehouse space.

Consequences

Establishes Arisa's home as band base; begins Kasumi-Arisa dynamic; Random Star becomes Kasumi's signature instrument. In Star Beat, Arisa more actively invites Kasumi into her 'game'; in reboot, she is reluctant recruit.

Variants

Star Beat frames Arisa as mysterious game-master; anime/game make her a reluctant classmate. Pawnshop name spelled Ryuuseido vs Ryuseido in romanization.

Reluctant Keyboardist - Joining Poppin'Party

Source Anime S1 Episodes 2-3 'We Did It! / We Ran!' / Game 'Poppin'Party is Born!' Ch. 3-5Culture JapanesePeriod 2017

Summary

Kasumi, having recruited guitarist Tae and bassist Rimi, needs a keyboardist and a practice space. She pesters Arisa, who insists she is an indoor person who hates standing out and only plays keyboard a little from childhood lessons. After Kasumi cleans the dusty warehouse and shows genuine effort, and after hearing Tae and Rimi play, Arisa agrees to try one session. She dusts off her own keyboard and, despite complaining, locks in with the band's sound. She officially joins as the fourth member before drummer Saaya is found, completing the instrumental core.

SPACE Audition Failure and Re-Audition

Source Anime S1 Episodes 8-9 'You Went and Did It Again / I Won't Cry'Culture JapanesePeriod 2017

Summary

Poppin'Party auditions to perform at live house SPACE but are judged harshly; owner Shifune Tsuzuki tells them they are not yet good enough, singling out Kasumi's vocals as weakest. Kasumi loses her voice from stress and guilt. Arisa, usually sarcastic, shows unexpected support: she visits Kasumi, helps the band regroup without pressure, and participates in the intense retraining. She does not grandstand but practices diligently and encourages Kasumi to sing from the heart rather than technique. They pass the second audition and earn the final slot at SPACE's closing festival.

Double Rainbow - The Pawnshop Crisis

Source Girls Band Party! Event Story 'Double Rainbow' (Event 45, 2018) / Anime S2 Episode 9-10 thematic parallelCulture JapanesePeriod 2018 (game), 2019 (anime echo)

Summary

Arisa's grandmother considers closing or renovating Ryuuseido, which would eliminate Poppin'Party's warehouse practice space. Arisa is torn between family duty and band, becoming withdrawn and snapping at Kasumi. The band argues — Arisa accuses them of taking the space for granted, Kasumi fears Arisa will quit. Through a heartfelt talk and a surprise performance using the warehouse one last time before changes, Arisa realizes the band is not just using the space but cherishing memories there. Grandmother ultimately decides to keep the shop open with modifications, preserving the warehouse.

Source Girls Band Party! Event Story 'Colorful, Poppin' Candy' (2017)Culture JapanesePeriod 2017

Live Beyond!! - Rocking Star Festival Ambition

Source Girls Band Party! Event Story 'Live Beyond!!' (2019) / Anime S3 Girls Band Challenge arc Episodes 7-13Culture JapanesePeriod 2019-2020

Summary

Poppin'Party is invited to the large-scale Rocking Star Festival but feels overshadowed by technically superior bands like Roselia and Raise A Suilen. Arisa voices insecurity that Poppin'Party's raw, energetic sound is not 'professional' enough, echoing early SPACE criticism. She considers whether they should change style to be more polished. Through joint practice and encouragement from other bands, she embraces Poppin'Party's strength — the innate connection and sparkling energy — and contributes a new keyboard arrangement that leans into their pop brightness rather than imitating others. The festival performance succeeds on their own terms.

Relationships
Arisa Ichigaya→Kasumi Toyamaally

Band leader and classmate who dragged Arisa into music. Arisa constantly calls Kasumi noisy and troublesome but is deeply fond of her; she provides warehouse, bails Kasumi out of trouble, and is often the first to notice when Kasumi is down. Kasumi in turn adores Arisa's bonsai and relies on her for grounding. Their bickering is central comedic and emotional core of Poppin'Party.

Arisa Ichigaya→Tae Hanazonoally

Lead guitarist. Arisa respects Tae's natural talent but is bewildered by her airheaded, my-pace behavior and her 20 rabbits. Tae's street-performance background contrasts with Arisa's indoor nature; Arisa often handles logistics Tae forgets. They bond over music despite opposite personalities.

Arisa Ichigaya→Rimi Ushigomeally

Bassist and Arisa's fellow Hanasakigawa student. Both are somewhat shy/indoor, share love of sweets and quiet hobbies (Rimi: chocolate/cornet, Arisa: bonsai). Arisa is protective of Rimi, who looks up to her as reliable older-sister figure within band.

Arisa Ichigaya→Saaya Yamabukially

Drummer. Arisa and Saaya are the most 'responsible' members, often exasperated by Kasumi and Tae's impulsiveness. They share practical duties (scheduling, equipment). Saaya's family bakery vs Arisa's pawnshop creates parallel 'family shop' bond.

Arisa Ichigaya→Ayasa Itō (voice actress / live performer)creator

Voice actress who voices Arisa and performs as her keyboardist in live Poppin'Party concerts. Itō was cast in 2015 and has performed at Budokan, Yokohama Arena, etc. Character's design loosely inspired by Itō but personality is distinct (Itō is outgoing and English-fluent; Arisa is indoor). Itō also hosts Poppin'Party radio shows as Arisa.

Arisa Ichigaya→Arisa's Grandmother (Ryuuseido Owner)family

Maternal grandmother who owns and runs Ryuuseido pawnshop. She raised Arisa in the shop, taught her about bonsai and antiques, and permits the band to use the warehouse. She is kind but business-minded; her consideration of closing the shop drives Double Rainbow conflict. Name not fully given in anime (credited as 'Arisa's Grandmother'); game calls her 'Grandmother Ichigaya'.

Arisa Ichigaya→Poppin'Party (collective)ally

Arisa's primary affiliation. As keyboardist, she is the harmonic anchor. The band's theme is 'popping' pop-rock with celestial motifs; Arisa's keyboard adds sparkle. Group chant 'PoPiPa, PiPoPa...' includes her. She participates in all major band decisions and lives at CiRCLE/RiNG in later timeline.

Hikikomori-coded / Social Withdrawal Themes

Source Official profile ('indoor faction'), Anime S1E2-3, Game conversations

Description

Arisa is characterized as preferring to stay indoors, avoiding outdoor activities, and finding social events draining. She self-identifies as gloomy and unmotivated. This is played for comedy and relatability, not pathologized. No canonical diagnosis; fans sometimes project clinical hikikomori or social anxiety onto her, but official material treats it as personality quirk with supportive friends helping her engage.

Scholarly Context

Reflects contemporary Japanese youth discourse on indoor vs outdoor (uchih vs sotoh) personalities; not presented as moral failing. Handled lightly without stigmatization.

Contradictions & Disputes

Personality: Game-Master vs Tsundere Friend

Position A

Star Beat (2015) Arisa is confident, slightly manipulative, treats band formation as a 'game' she designed to cure Kasumi's shyness.

Position A — evidence

Star Beat Chapter 1 dialogue: Arisa says 'Welcome to BanG_Dream!' as game protagonist; Nakamura's original draft notes cited in Bushiroad retrospective (2016).

Position B

Anime (2017+) and Game reboot Arisa is reluctant, indoor-loving, tsundere who is dragged into band and gradually warms up.

Position B — evidence

Anime S1E2-3 and Game 'Poppin'Party is Born!' show Kasumi initiating, Arisa resisting; official profile emphasizes 'indoor faction, sharp-tongued but caring'.

Scholarly Consensus

Bushiroad explicitly retconned Star Beat as prototype; reboot is canonical. Both are same entity but early version is non-canonical prototype. Fandom generally accepts reboot.

Practice Space Location and Pawnshop Name

Position A

Warehouse is directly above the pawnshop, part of family home.

Position A — evidence

Anime S1E3 shows stairs inside shop leading to warehouse; game sprites show interior door.

Position B

Warehouse is a separate storage building behind/adjacent to shop, requiring outdoor walk.

Position B — evidence

Game area map 'Shopping District - Ryuuseido' shows warehouse as separate building on map; Double Rainbow text says 'walk to the storehouse'.

Scholarly Consensus

Artistic simplification; no official clarification. Both describe same functional space; difference is layout artistic license, not lore contradiction.

Keyboard Proficiency Level

Position A

Arisa is a beginner who learned quickly for the band (anime narrative).

Position A — evidence

Anime S1E3: Arisa says she only played a little as a kid, needs practice.

Position B

Game card flavor text and live performance suggest she had childhood piano lessons and is relatively skilled.

Position B — evidence

Official profile and Girls Band Party! card episodes mention childhood keyboard lessons; Ayasa Itō's live playing is proficient.

Scholarly Consensus

Reconcilable: childhood lessons + hiatus + quick relearning. Not a true contradiction, but degree of prior skill varies by source emphasis.

Who It Is

Behind the counter of her grandmother's dusty pawnshop, surrounded by bonsai trees and half-finished online shopping carts, Arisa Ichigaya would rather stay inside forever — until a relentlessly sparkling classmate drags a red star-shaped guitar into her life and demands she learn to shine on stage.

Attested personality across anime and game: tsundere, sharp-tongued, indoor-oriented (hikikomori-coded but not clinically defined), self-deprecating about being 'gloomy' and 'unmotivated', intensely loyal once committed. She initially resists joining Poppin'Party, calling band activities troublesome and claiming she wants to stay home, but is moved by Kasumi's earnestness. Powers
Keyboard / Synthesizer Performance

Proficient keyboardist for Poppin'Party; provides harmonic and rhythmic foundation, synth pads, piano lines, and occasional lead melodies. In-universe, she learned quickly after Kasumi recruited her, practicing in the pawnshop warehouse. Not a prodigy like Rinko or Chiyu, but reliable ensemble player.

Limit: Self-described as not naturally athletic or stage-confident; initially struggled with motivation and stamina for live shows. Requires regular practice; no perfect pitch or supernatural ability claimed.

Bonsai Cultivation and General Domestic Crafts

Expert hobbyist in bonsai care — pruning, watering, styling miniature trees. Also skilled at making sweets, sewing, and organizing inventory at the pawnshop. Shows meticulous, patient temperament in these tasks contrasting with stage nerves.

Limit: Hobby-level; not professional horticulturist. Bonsai sometimes suffer when she neglects them during tour stress (event stories).

Logistics and Venue Provision

Provides Poppin'Party's primary practice space: the storeroom/warehouse above/at the back of Ryuuseido pawnshop. Manages access, keys, and storage of equipment. Also acts as pragmatic planner for budgets and schedules.

Limit: Space is dependent on grandmother's permission; occasionally threatened when family considers closing or renovating shop (Double Rainbow arc).

Girls Band Party! Game Card Skills (Meta)

In the rhythm game, Arisa cards provide Score Boost, Healing, or Perfect Lock skills depending on rarity. e.g., 4★ [Indoor Enthusiast] Arisa - Score up 110% for 7 seconds. This is gameplay abstraction, not diegetic power.

Limit: Purely mechanical; does not reflect in-universe superpower. Power creep with newer cards.

Accounts I
The Random Star and Ryuuseido EncounterBanG Dream! anime Season 1 Episode 1 'We Did It!' / Star Beat Chapter 1 / Game Band Story 'Poppin'Party is Born!' Chapter 1

2015 (manga), 2017 (anime/game retelling) - contemporary Tokyo

First-year Kasumi Toyama, searching for the sparkling 'Star Beat' she heard as a child, follows red star stickers to Arisa's family pawnshop Ryuuseido. There she finds a red ESP Random Star guitar. Arisa, working at the shop after school, is initially annoyed by Kasumi's loud excitement and tries to shoo her away. The guitar is too expensive for Kasumi, but Arisa's grandmother allows Kasumi to take it if she learns to play. Glitter Green's performance at live house SPACE that evening inspires Kasumi to form a band, and she immediately targets Arisa as a member because of the warehouse space. Establishes Arisa's home as band base; begins Kasumi-Arisa dynamic; Random Star becomes Kasumi's signature instrument. In Star Beat, Arisa more actively invites Kasumi into her 'game'; in reboot, she is reluctant recruit.

Reluctant Keyboardist - Joining Poppin'PartyAnime S1 Episodes 2-3 'We Did It! / We Ran!' / Game 'Poppin'Party is Born!' Ch. 3-5

2017

Kasumi, having recruited guitarist Tae and bassist Rimi, needs a keyboardist and a practice space. She pesters Arisa, who insists she is an indoor person who hates standing out and only plays keyboard a little from childhood lessons. After Kasumi cleans the dusty warehouse and shows genuine effort, and after hearing Tae and Rimi play, Arisa agrees to try one session. She dusts off her own keyboard and, despite complaining, locks in with the band's sound. She officially joins as the fourth member before drummer Saaya is found, completing the instrumental core. Poppin'Party's five-member lineup solidifies; warehouse becomes permanent practice studio; Arisa's keyboard becomes essential to their pop-rock sound.

SPACE Audition Failure and Re-AuditionAnime S1 Episodes 8-9 'You Went and Did It Again / I Won't Cry'

2017

Poppin'Party auditions to perform at live house SPACE but are judged harshly; owner Shifune Tsuzuki tells them they are not yet good enough, singling out Kasumi's vocals as weakest. Kasumi loses her voice from stress and guilt. Arisa, usually sarcastic, shows unexpected support: she visits Kasumi, helps the band regroup without pressure, and participates in the intense retraining. She does not grandstand but practices diligently and encourages Kasumi to sing from the heart rather than technique. They pass the second audition and earn the final slot at SPACE's closing festival. Demonstrates Arisa's loyalty beneath tsundere exterior; band's first major live success; SPACE closes, leading to migration to CiRCLE in S2.

Accounts II
Double Rainbow - The Pawnshop CrisisGirls Band Party! Event Story 'Double Rainbow' (Event 45, 2018) / Anime S2 Episode 9-10 thematic parallel

2018 (game), 2019 (anime echo)

Arisa's grandmother considers closing or renovating Ryuuseido, which would eliminate Poppin'Party's warehouse practice space. Arisa is torn between family duty and band, becoming withdrawn and snapping at Kasumi. The band argues — Arisa accuses them of taking the space for granted, Kasumi fears Arisa will quit. Through a heartfelt talk and a surprise performance using the warehouse one last time before changes, Arisa realizes the band is not just using the space but cherishing memories there. Grandmother ultimately decides to keep the shop open with modifications, preserving the warehouse. Deepens Arisa's arc about belonging and home; warehouse remains base through S3; cited by fans as Arisa's central character story. Adapted loosely in anime S2's cultural festival episode.

Colorful, Poppin' Candy - Downtown Festival RescueGirls Band Party! Event Story 'Colorful, Poppin' Candy' (2017)

2017

The shopping district's summer festival is at risk of being dull due to lack of entertainment. Poppin'Party volunteers to perform and help stalls, but rain threatens. Arisa, despite hating heat and outdoor work, takes charge of logistics — coordinating stalls, using her pawnshop connections, and suggesting an indoor-stage alternative. Her indoor expertise and organizational skills save the event, and the band's performance of 'Natsuzora SUN! SUN! SEVEN!' lifts spirits. Highlights Arisa's pragmatic strengths; reinforces band's community ties; first major post-formation event story.

Live Beyond!! - Rocking Star Festival AmbitionGirls Band Party! Event Story 'Live Beyond!!' (2019) / Anime S3 Girls Band Challenge arc Episodes 7-13

2019-2020

Poppin'Party is invited to the large-scale Rocking Star Festival but feels overshadowed by technically superior bands like Roselia and Raise A Suilen. Arisa voices insecurity that Poppin'Party's raw, energetic sound is not 'professional' enough, echoing early SPACE criticism. She considers whether they should change style to be more polished. Through joint practice and encouragement from other bands, she embraces Poppin'Party's strength — the innate connection and sparkling energy — and contributes a new keyboard arrangement that leans into their pop brightness rather than imitating others. The festival performance succeeds on their own terms. Caps Poppin'Party's growth from amateur to self-assured; Arisa accepts her role without needing to be the best technician; leads into S3's Girls Band Challenge where they compete as equals.

Arisa hates Kasumi and only stays in Poppin'Party out of obligation. — Canonical material consistently shows Arisa deeply values Kasumi and the band; her sharp tongue is tsundere affection, not hatred. She initiates support in crises (SPACE re-audition, Double Rainbow) and composes/arranges willingly. She chooses to stay even when offered outs.

Arisa is wealthy because her family owns a pawnshop. — Ryuuseido is a modest, old-fashioned pawnshop in a shopping district, not a luxury business. Stories show financial pressure and consideration of closing. Arisa is middle-class, lives simply, and handles inventory herself; no depiction of wealth.

Arisa's instrument is piano, and she is a classical prodigy like Rinko or Rui. — Arisa plays keyboard/synthesizer in a pop-rock context, not classical piano. She is competent but not depicted as prodigy; Roselia's Rinko and Morfonica's Rui are the classical prodigies. Arisa's strength is ensemble support and logistics.

Arisa is the same character as Arisa from other Bushiroad franchises or is based on a real historical figure. — Arisa Ichigaya is original fictional character created for BanG Dream! in 2015. No historical or folkloric source; name shares are coincidental.

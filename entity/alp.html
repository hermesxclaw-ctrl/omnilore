Who It Is

A vampiric nightmare spirit from German folklore similar to the mare. The alp is typically male and wears a magical hat (Tarnkappe) that grants invisibility. It torments sleepers, drinks blood from nipples and children, and can shapeshift into animals. Its gaze alone can curse cattle and blight crops.

Form

Often appears as a small, ugly man or a butterfly/moth; sometimes a cat, pig, or dog; always wearing the Tarnkappe (cap of invisibility); has a long, sucking tongue

Powers

Invisibility (via Tarnkappe)

Shape-shifting

Blood-drinking

Nightmare/sleep paralysis induction

Evil eye on livestock

Behavior & Habitat

Persistent tormentor; feeds on human blood and 'soul milk'; can be bribed but rarely placated; iron and specific herbs ward against it

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

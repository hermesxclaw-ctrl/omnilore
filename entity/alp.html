Who It Is
Form

The Alp is often described as a supernatural being that is similar to a vampire or an incubus. It is said to wear a magic cloak called a Tarnkappe, which allows it to become invisible.

Powers

Invisibility The Alp is said to be able to become invisible using its Tarnkappe, allowing it to sneak up on its victims.

Who It Is

Inori is a name that weaves through Japanese culture, from a whispered prayer to the roar of a pop anthem, and from the quiet strength of a voice actress to the blazing courage of anime heroines. It is a word that carries the weight of hope, the echo of devotion, and the spark of countless fictional lives.

As a name, Inori does not have a personality. However, the word's meaning of 'prayer' suggests qualities such as devotion, hope, and sincerity. Individuals and characters named Inori often exhibit these traits; for example, Inori Yuzuriha is calm and introspective, while Inori Yamabuki is energetic and caring.

Form & Signs

Appearance — As a name and word, Inori has no inherent physical appearance. The kanji 祈り is visually composed of the radical 示 (altar) and 斤 (axe), which together evoke ritual and offering. In hiragana, it is written as いのり, a flowing script. The characters associated with the name vary widely in appearance, from human to anthropomorphic.

Symbols — The kanji 祈 is a symbol of prayer and spiritual devotion in Japanese culture. It appears in religious contexts, such as ema (votive tablets) and shrine rituals. As a given name, it conveys a sense of hope, faith, or a wish for the child's future.

Domains — The name Inori is used in Japan and among Japanese-speaking communities. It appears in real life, anime, manga, video games, and music. The word 'inori' is used in religious and spiritual contexts, such as Shinto and Buddhist practices.

2011-2012

Inori Yuzuriha is one of the protagonists of the anime Guilty Crown. She is a mysterious and stoic singer who becomes involved in a rebellion, using the power of 'Void Genome' to extract weapons from people's hearts. Her story involves themes of identity, sacrifice, and love. She becomes a central figure in the conflict and ultimately sacrifices herself, leaving a lasting impact on the narrative.

Inori Yamabuki in Fresh Pretty Cure!Anime series Fresh Pretty Cure! (2009-2010)

2009-2010

Inori Yamabuki is a main character in the magical girl anime Fresh Pretty Cure!. She is a kind and responsible girl who transforms into Cure Pine, one of the Pretty Cure warriors. Her story emphasizes friendship, courage, and protecting others. She helps save the world and grows as a person.

Who It Is
Form

Fradinho da Mão Furada is typically depicted as a small, mischievous child monk with a pierced hand, often dressed in a simple friar's habit

Powers

Treasure Guardian Fradinho da Mão Furada is said to offer riches and prosperity to those who treat him with kindness and respect

Accounts

A young friar named Fradinho da Mão Furada is said to have been pierced by a witch's curse, but instead of dying, he became a helpful and benevolent spirit

A young woman is said to have been cursed by a witch, but Fradinho da Mão Furada intervenes and offers her a way out of the curse

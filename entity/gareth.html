Who It Is
Form

Gareth is often depicted as a young and inexperienced knight, with a humble and kind demeanor. He is often shown wearing the same armor as his brothers, but with a distinctive white or silver trim. In some versions, he is described as being smaller and more agile than his brothers.

Powers

Cooking skills Gareth is said to possess exceptional cooking skills, which he learned from his mother, the Queen of Orkney. He is able to prepare delicious meals and drinks, and is often called upon to cook for his fellow knights and guests.

Accounts

Gareth, disguised as a kitchen servant, goes to the court of King Arthur and proves himself by defeating a fierce beast that has been terrorizing the kingdom.

Gareth falls in love with a noble lady and must navigate the complexities of courtly love to win her heart.

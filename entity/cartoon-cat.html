Who It Is
Form

The Cartoon Cat is a fictional entity created by Trevor Henderson, characterized by its cartoonish appearance and menacing demeanor. It is often depicted as a large, black cat with a white spot on its chest, walking on its hind legs. The entity's design is reminiscent of a mix between a cat and a human, with exaggerated features and a distorted body shape.

Powers

Menacing Presence The Cartoon Cat's ability to create a sense of unease and fear in those who encounter it, often used to intimidate and unsettle its victims.

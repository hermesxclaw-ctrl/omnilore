Who It Is

SCP-055 is something that cannot be remembered. The Foundation knows two things about it: it is not round, and it is contained in a specific room at Site-19. Beyond that, the entity's defining characteristic is that any information about it is spontaneously forgotten. Scientists assigned to study it cannot recall their findings. Notes written about it become unintelligible within hours. Database entries about it gradually appear blank to anyone who reads them. Security personnel watching it through monitors forget what they saw the moment they look away. The Foundation has been 'studying' SCP-055 for years and has accumulated almost no concrete information about what it is, what it looks like, or what its nature might be. All that is known is that it is contained, that it is not round, and that it must be documented so that the Foundation doesn't forget that it exists entirely. The entry for SCP-055 on the wiki is periodically edited to contain less information, with notes indicating that previous versions claimed things that current researchers do not remember. The article is a masterpiece of minimalist horror, demonstrating that the most frightening thing can be the absence of information rather than its presence. What is SCP-055? No one knows. No one can know. The entity that erases itself from memory could be anything — a weapon, a portal, a god, a harmless rock — and the Foundation would never be able to find out. The horror is epistemological: there is something in the facility, and it is not round, and that is everything anyone can ever know about it.

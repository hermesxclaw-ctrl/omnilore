Who It Is
Form

Jyggalag is often depicted as a tall, imposing figure with long silver hair and piercing blue eyes. He wears a suit of ornate, silver armor adorned with intricate patterns and symbols.

Powers

The Grey God's Lament Jyggalag's ability to create and manipulate sound is said to be one of his most powerful and feared abilities. He can create complex melodies and harmonies that can charm and beguile even the most resistant of souls.

Accounts

According to legend, Jyggalag created the Shivering Isles as a realm of madness and chaos, a place where the Daedra could come to indulge in their basest desires. However, as time went on, the Isles became a place of darkness and despair, and Jyggalag was forced to intervene to restore order.

According to legend, Jyggalag created the 'Grey God's Lament,' a haunting melody said to be the sound of his tears. This melody is said to have the power to charm and beguile even the most resistant of souls.

Who It Is

The Daedric Prince of Logical Deduction and Order. Jyggalag was the most powerful Daedric Prince — so powerful that the other Princes cursed him into becoming Sheogorath, the Prince of Madness, giving form to the very chaos he sought to eliminate. At the end of every era, Jyggalag breaks free during the Greymarch, leading an army of crystalline Order Knights to destroy Sheogorath's realm.

Who It Is
Form

The Kinnara/Kinnari are depicted as half-human, half-bird creatures, often with human upper bodies and avian lower bodies. They are said to possess beautiful voices and are often associated with music and dance.

Powers

Divine Music The Kinnara/Kinnari are said to possess the power of divine music, which has the ability to bring joy and peace to those who hear it.

Accounts

SCP-4001 — Alexandria Eternal

also known asAlexandria EternalSCP-4001
Typescp
CultureSCP Foundation
Who It Is
Form

SCP-4001 is a vast, labyrinthine library containing a book about the life of every human being. The library is said to be infinite in size, with shelves upon shelves of books that stretch on forever. The books themselves are bound in a strange, otherworldly material that seems to shift and change color in the light.

Powers

Knowledge and Learning SCP-4001 has the power to grant knowledge and learning to those who approach it. It is said to contain the secrets of the universe, and is able to impart this knowledge to those who are worthy.

Incident Log

The story of SCP-4001 begins with the discovery of the entity by a team of researchers from the SCP Foundation. The researchers were drawn to the entity by rumors of its power and knowledge, and were determined to unlock its secrets.

The ethics of SCP-4001 are a topic of much debate and discussion. Some argue that the entity is a powerful tool for learning and knowledge, while others argue that it is a threat to humanity and must be contained.

Who It Is

The Library of Alexandria — not destroyed, but hidden in a pocket dimension, preserved by immortal librarians for 2,000 years. Every book ever written, every book that will be written, and every book imagined but never written exists within its shelves. The Foundation trades access for help acquiring anomalous texts.

Form

An infinite library of honey-colored stone and dark wood, lit by floating orbs of warm light. Shelves extend in all directions beyond vision. The librarians never sleep. They know where every book is.

Powers

Containment of all written and unwritten human knowledge

Predictive acquisition of future texts

Pocket dimension stability

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

Who It Is
Form

Mictlantecuhtli is often depicted as a skeletal figure with a skull for a head and a serpent for a body. He is associated with the underworld and the dead.

Powers

Lordship over the Underworld Mictlantecuhtli is the lord of the underworld, Mictlan, and has the power to control the cycle of life and death.

Accounts

The story of the descent of the sun god, Huitzilopochtli, into the underworld, where he encounters Mictlantecuhtli and other gods.

The story of Mictlantecuhtli's attempt to stop the recreation of man, which was a central theme in Aztec mythology.

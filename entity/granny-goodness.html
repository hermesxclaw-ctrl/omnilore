Who It Is

Granny Goodness is one of the most disturbing villains in all of comics — a smiling, grandmotherly figure who runs the Orphanage of Horrors on Apokolips, where she systematically breaks the wills of children to forge them into loyal servants of Darkseid. She is the supreme commander of the Female Furies, the elite all-female warrior corps that serves as Darkseid's personal strike force. Her weapon is trauma. Granny appears as a plump, kindly old woman with spectacles and a warm smile — a facade that makes her true nature all the more horrifying. She is a master of psychological abuse, using a combination of physical torture, emotional manipulation, isolation, and reward/punishment conditioning to destroy the individuality of her charges and rebuild them as obedient weapons. She raised both Mister Miracle and Big Barda, and they bear the psychological scars of her 'care' for the rest of their lives. Despite her grandmotherly appearance, Granny is a formidable combatant. She has superhuman strength and durability, wields a variety of Apokoliptian weaponry, commands massive armies, and is utterly ruthless. She is perhaps the most faithful of all Darkseid's servants — not because she fears him, but because she truly believes in his vision of absolute order. Granny Goodness represents the most insidious form of evil: the evil that presents itself as care, that wears a smiling face while inflicting unimaginable trauma, that justifies the breaking of children as 'education' or 'discipline.' She is the monster under the bed, the abusive parent, the trusted adult who betrays — a villain so vile that even other villains find her disturbing.

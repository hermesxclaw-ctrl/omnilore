Who It Is

In the hidden village of Konoha, a prodigy of the Uchiha clan carried a burden so heavy that he became a villain to protect the ones he loved. Itachi Uchiha, a name whispered with fear and reverence, walked a path of shadows and sacrifice, his true story hidden behind a mask of cold cruelty. His life is a tragic tale of duty, love, and the devastating cost of peace.

Itachi is calm, collected, and highly intelligent. He is a master strategist and manipulator, often thinking several steps ahead. He appears cold and ruthless, especially as an antagonist, but this is a facade. In truth, he is deeply compassionate, self-sacrificing, and loves his younger brother Sasuke unconditionally. He is willing to bear the burden of being a villain to protect the village and his brother. He is also philosophical and introspective, often speaking in riddles and metaphors. He has a gentle, melancholic side, and his final moments reveal his vulnerability and love for Sasu

Powers
Sharingan

The Uchiha clan's kekkei genkai (bloodline limit) that grants the user enhanced perception, the ability to copy techniques, and see chakra. It also allows the user to cast powerful genjutsu (illusions).

Limit: Requires significant chakra; overuse can cause blindness if evolved to Mangekyo.

Mangekyo Sharingan

An evolved form of the Sharingan that grants access to powerful techniques, but at the cost of progressive vision loss and eventual blindness.

Limit: Vision loss over time; can only be awakened after experiencing intense emotional trauma.

Amaterasu

A Mangekyo Sharingan technique that creates black flames that burn for seven days and seven nights, and cannot be extinguished by normal means.

Limit: Only works on the user's line of sight; can consume the user's chakra rapidly.

Tsukuyomi

A powerful genjutsu that traps the target in a mental world where the user can control time and space, causing psychological damage.

Limit: Requires eye contact; can be broken by users with strong willpower or special abilities.

Susanoo

A spectral warrior that protects the user, formed from their chakra. It can manifest weapons like the Totsuka Blade and Yata Mirror.

Limit: Requires immense chakra and stamina; can cause extreme physical strain.

Izanami

A forbidden genjutsu that traps the target in an endless loop of events, forcing them to accept their fate. It is used to reform individuals who refuse to accept reality.

Limit: Requires the user to sacrifice their eye, causing blindness; only works on individuals who have a specific 'reality' to loop.

Fire Style (Katon) Techniques

Itachi is proficient in fire-based jutsu, such as the Fireball Jutsu and Phoenix Flower Jutsu, which are signature techniques of the Uchiha clan.

Limit: Requires hand seals and chakra; can be countered by water or wind techniques.

Genjutsu Mastery

Itachi is a master of genjutsu, able to cast illusions with a simple gesture or eye contact. He can control the perception of others and create complex mental worlds.

Limit: Requires eye contact for many techniques; can be disrupted by other chakra or sensory abilities.

Shurikenjutsu

Itachi is highly skilled in throwing weapons, able to hit targets with incredible accuracy and speed.

Limit: Requires physical weapons; can be countered by defensive techniques.

Crow Summoning

Itachi can summon crows for various purposes, including surveillance, communication, and as a medium for his techniques.

Limit: Requires chakra; crows can be killed or dispersed.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Itachi Uchiha is a tall, slender young man with pale skin, sharp facial features, and long, flowing black hair that falls past his shoulders. He has distinctive 'crow's feet' wrinkles at the corners of his eyes, a trait he inherited from his father. His most striking feature is his Sharingan, a red eye with a black tomoe pattern that can evolve into the Mangekyo Sharingan, which resembles a pinwheel. He typically wears the standard Akatsuki cloak

Symbols — Itachi is associated with the Uchiha clan crest, which is a fan-shaped emblem. His personal symbol is the crow, which he uses for surveillance and as a medium for his techniques. The crow also symbolizes his role as a shadowy protector and his connection to Shisui Uchiha. His techniques often involve fire (Katon) and illusions (Genjutsu), and his Mangekyo Sharingan techniques include Amaterasu (black flames) and Tsukuyomi (powerful genjutsu).

Domains — Itachi is a ninja, so his domain is the shinobi world, particularly the hidden villages of Konohagakure (the Hidden Leaf Village) and the criminal organization Akatsuki. He is often shown in dark, shadowy locations, reflecting his secretive nature. He is also associated with the Uchiha clan's former compound and the Naka Shrine, which holds the clan's secrets.

Accounts I
The Uchiha Clan MassacreNaruto manga, chapters 399-402, 590-595; Naruto: Shippuden anime, episodes 138-143, 455-459

Pre-series, approximately 10 years before the main storyline

The Uchiha clan, suspected of plotting a coup d'état against Konoha, is ordered to be eliminated by the village leadership. Itachi, a double agent, is forced to carry out the massacre, sparing only his younger brother Sasuke. He kills his entire clan, including his parents, and takes on the role of a villain to protect Sasuke and maintain peace. Itachi becomes a rogue ninja and joins the Akatsuki. Sasuke becomes obsessed with revenge, leading him down a dark path. The truth is hidden for years, and when revealed, it devastates Sasuke and changes his goals.

Itachi's Return to KonohaNaruto manga, chapters 139-141; Naruto anime, episodes 81-85

Part I, shortly after the death of the Third Hokage

Itachi and Kisame infiltrate Konoha to capture Naruto, but Itachi's true purpose is to check on Sasuke's progress. He confronts Kakashi, Asuma, and Kurenai, defeating them easily. He then faces Sasuke, using Tsukuyomi to torment him and reinforce Sasuke's desire for revenge before retreating. Sasuke becomes more determined to become stronger and eventually leaves the village to train with Orochimaru. Itachi's actions further cement his villainous reputation.

Accounts II
The Final Battle with SasukeNaruto manga, chapters 389-396, 575-581; Naruto: Shippuden anime, episodes 84-85, 111-126, 138-143

Part II, during Sasuke's quest for revenge

Sasuke tracks down Itachi to a hidden hideout and confronts him. The battle is intense, with Itachi pushing Sasuke to his limits. Itachi uses his Mangekyo Sharingan to draw out Orochimaru from Sasuke's body and seals him with the Totsuka Blade. Itachi then succumbs to his illness and dies, but before dying, he implants Amaterasu in Sasuke to protect him from Tobi. Itachi's final act is to poke Sasuke's forehead, a gesture of affection, and smile. Sasuke learns the truth about Itachi's actions from Tobi, leading him to seek revenge against Konoha. Sasuke later has Itachi's eyes implanted, gaining the Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan.

The Fourth Great Ninja War and Itachi's RedemptionNaruto manga, chapters 575-581, 602-609; Naruto: Shippuden anime, episodes 251-256, 338-341

Part II, during the Fourth Great Ninja War

Itachi is resurrected by Kabuto using the Edo Tensei and forced to fight against Naruto and Killer Bee. However, he uses the crow implanted in Naruto to break free of the control and seal Nagato. He then confronts Kabuto, using Izanami to trap him in a loop and force him to release the Edo Tensei. Before his soul departs, Itachi reveals the full truth to Sasuke and tells him he will always love him, regardless of Sasuke's choices. This revelation, combined with Naruto's influence, leads Sasuke to renounce his revenge and become a defender of Konoha. Itachi's soul finally departs, and his legacy inspires Sasuke to change his path. The war eventually ends, and Sasuke becomes a hero.

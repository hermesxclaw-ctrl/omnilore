Who It Is
Form

Geryon is commonly described as a three-bodied giant, with some variations stating he has three heads and one body, or three bodies and one head. He is often depicted as being mostly humanoid, with some distinguishing features such as wings.

Powers

Deception Geryon has the ability to deceive and manipulate those who enter his domain. He is said to be able to create illusions and manipulate the truth to his advantage.

Accounts

Geryon is a three-bodied giant from Greek mythology, later immortalized in Dante Alighieri's *Inferno* as the guardian of the Fraudulent Eighth Circle in Hell. In Dante's vision, Geryon is a deceiver and a guardian of the underworld.

Geryon is a three-bodied giant from Greek mythology. He is often described as a monster with three bodies and three heads, or three heads and one body.

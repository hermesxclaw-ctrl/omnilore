Who It Is

In the shadowy margins of early video game history, where forgotten sprites flicker into memory, there exists a creature named Skunny—a plucky squirrel whose leaps and bounds once graced the screens of the early 1990s. Though his name may not echo in the halls of gaming fame, his story is a testament to the era's relentless creativity and the fleeting nature of digital stardom.

Skunny is portrayed as a brave and resourceful hero, often facing off against villains to save his friends or the world. He is determined and quick-witted, though not deeply characterized in any game. His personality is largely inferred from his actions and the limited dialogue in the games.

Powers
Gliding

Skunny can use his bushy tail to glide through the air, allowing him to cross gaps and reach higher platforms. This ability is featured in 'Skunny: Lost in Time'.

Limit: Gliding is limited by altitude; Skunny cannot gain height while gliding, only maintain or slowly descend.

Acrobatic jumping

Skunny can perform high jumps and precise platforming maneuvers, typical of platformer protagonists.

Limit: Jump height is fixed and cannot be upgraded.

Projectile throwing

In 'Skunny: Save Our Pizzas!', Skunny can throw pizzas at enemies to defeat them.

Limit: Pizza supply is limited and must be collected.

Kart racing

In 'Skunny Kart', Skunny drives a go-kart and can use items to gain an advantage.

Limit: This ability is specific to the racing game and not present in platformers.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Skunny is depicted as a brown squirrel with a distinctive cream-colored belly and a large, bushy tail. He typically wears no clothing, but in some promotional art he is shown with a red bandana or a pilot's cap, depending on the game. His design is simple and cartoonish, typical of early 1990s platformer characters.

Symbols — Skunny's primary symbol is his bushy tail, which he uses to glide in some games. The tail is often depicted in motion, emphasizing agility. No other significant iconography is associated with the character.

Domains — Skunny's natural habitat is a forest, but his adventures take him to various locations, including a pizza factory, a medieval castle, and time-traveling landscapes. He is adaptable and thrives in diverse environments.

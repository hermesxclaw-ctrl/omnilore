The Approach

In the shadowy margins of early video game history, where forgotten sprites flicker into memory, there exists a creature named Skunny—a plucky squirrel whose leaps and bounds once graced the screens of the early 1990s. Though his name may not echo in the halls of gaming fame, his story is a testament to the era's relentless creativity and the fleeting nature of digital stardom.

Identity

Skunny

Pronunciation SKUH-nee

Etymology

The name 'Skunny' is likely a playful coinage, possibly derived from 'skunk' or 'scurry', reflecting the character's animal nature and energetic movement. However, no official etymology has been confirmed.

Cultural Ownership

Skunny is a fictional character created by the now-defunct American video game company, Blue Sky Software, and published by Sega for the Master System and Game Gear. The character is not based on any pre-existing folklore or mythology.

Disputed Identifications

There are no known disputes regarding Skunny's identity. However, the character is sometimes confused with other cartoon squirrels from the same era, such as Conker from 'Conker's Bad Fur Day' or the eponymous star of 'Squirrel Boy'.

Description

Appearance

Skunny is depicted as a brown squirrel with a distinctive cream-colored belly and a large, bushy tail. He typically wears no clothing, but in some promotional art he is shown with a red bandana or a pilot's cap, depending on the game. His design is simple and cartoonish, typical of early 1990s platformer characters.

Symbols & Iconography

Skunny's primary symbol is his bushy tail, which he uses to glide in some games. The tail is often depicted in motion, emphasizing agility. No other significant iconography is associated with the character.

Behaviour & Personality

Skunny is portrayed as a brave and resourceful hero, often facing off against villains to save his friends or the world. He is determined and quick-witted, though not deeply characterized in any game. His personality is largely inferred from his actions and the limited dialogue in the games.

Domains & Habitats

Skunny's natural habitat is a forest, but his adventures take him to various locations, including a pizza factory, a medieval castle, and time-traveling landscapes. He is adaptable and thrives in diverse environments.

Version Differences

In 'Skunny: Save Our Pizzas!', Skunny is a pizza delivery squirrel who must retrieve stolen pizzas. In 'Skunny: Lost in Time', he is a time-traveling hero. In 'Skunny Kart', he is a racing character. These variations do not alter his core identity but place him in different genres and scenarios.

Powers & Abilities

Gliding

Source Version Skunny: Lost in Time

Description

Skunny can use his bushy tail to glide through the air, allowing him to cross gaps and reach higher platforms. This ability is featured in 'Skunny: Lost in Time'.

Limitations

Gliding is limited by altitude; Skunny cannot gain height while gliding, only maintain or slowly descend.

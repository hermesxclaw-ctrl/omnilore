Who It Is

The Glint-Glint Fruit, a Logia that embodies light itself. The user can become, generate, and control light at will, granting the ability to move at the speed of light, fire concentrated laser beams of devastating power, and scatter their body into photons to avoid all physical attacks. Considered the fastest Devil Fruit power in existence.

Powers

Light-speed movement and travel

Full elemental intangibility (scatter into photons)

Yata no Kagami (light-speed reflection teleport)

Noboriboshi (laser barrage)

Amaterasu (massive light burst attack)

Can blind enemies with flash attacks

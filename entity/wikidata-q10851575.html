Who It Is

It doesn't swim so much as it hunts — a hunched, claw-fisted amphibian that bursts from the surf at Jaburo and tears a steel hull open with its bare hands. The MSM-07 Z'Gok is Zeon's answer to a simple, brutal problem: how do you kill a Federation base that thinks the ocean will protect it?

As a vehicle, it has no personality, but its narrative portrayal is consistent: an ambush predator. It is deployed from submarines or submersible carriers, surfaces without warning, and excels in close-quarters melee where its claws negate ranged advantages. Official materials describe it as highly maneuverable underwater (hydrojet thrust) and surprisingly agile on land despite its bulk, capable of jumping and using its extendable arms to strike at range. Powers
Iron Claw (Iron Nail) - Extendable Forearm Claw

Each forearm houses a three-fingered crushing claw with extendable telescopic arm. Can punch through ship armor, crush mobile suit limbs, and be launched on limited extension to grab enemies at ~10-15m. Primary melee weapon.

Limit: No cutting edge; pure crushing/piercing. Extension exposes vulnerable joint cables. Cannot parry beam sabers effectively without damage. Requires close range.

240mm Mega Particle Cannon (Arm Beam Cannon) x2

A mega particle cannon housed in the center of each claw/forearm, firing forward. Allows ranged attack without needing handheld weapons, crucial underwater where beam rifles are less effective.

Limit: Low rate of fire, limited capacitor charge underwater, shorter effective range than dedicated beam rifle. Some early specs listed as 'small mega particle gun' — yield lower than Rick Dom's beam bazooka.

Hydrojet / Thermonuclear Hydrojet Engines

Backpack and leg-mounted hydrojet thrusters using thermonuclear reactor-heated water jet propulsion for high-speed submerged movement (official max underwater speed 103 knots / ~190 km/h). Also provides jump capability on land.

Limit: Performance degrades significantly on land and in space; high fuel/water consumption; noisy cavitation at high speed makes stealth difficult.

Water-Cooled Thermonuclear Reactor

2400kW (2480kW for S-type) Minovsky ultracompact fusion reactor specially water-cooled for sustained underwater operation, allowing longer submerged endurance than ground MS.

Limit: Cooling dependent on external water; prolonged land operation risks overheating without auxiliary cooling. Reactor output lower than later amphibious units like Hygogg.

Mono-Eye Sensor System with Retractable Shutter

Single large visual sensor on torso with armored shutter for protection under pressure and during combat. Provides wide-angle search and targeting.

Limit: Single point of failure; shutter closed = blind. Less redundancy than Gundam's dual sensors. Vulnerable to flash and beam glare.

Amphibious Pressure Hull and Corrosion Resistance

Sealed joints, anti-corrosion coating, and pressure-resistant frame allowing deep-water operation and surfacing without preparation.

Limit: Not a true deep-submergence submarine; crush depth not stated but below that of dedicated submersibles. Joints still require maintenance after saltwater exposure.

Accounts I
Development Competition - MIP vs ZimmadEntertainment Bible .39; MSV Development History

UC 0079 (in-universe) / 1983-1989 (publication)

With Zeon's Earth invasion stalled by Federation naval power, the Principality solicits amphibious MS proposals. Zimmad Company offers the MSM-03 Gogg, while MIP Company offers the MSM-07 Z'Gok. Early Gogg is powerful but bulky and slow to produce. MIP's Z'Gok, using a more compact water-cooled reactor and extendable claws, proves more agile and cheaper. After comparative trials, both are adopted, but Z'Gok is favored for mass production and ace assignment. The rivalry explains the overlapping MSM numbers. Z'Gok enters mass production in mid-0079, becoming Zeon's primary amphibious assault MS for the Jaburo operation. Establishes MIP as Zeon's amphibious specialist leading to later MSM-04 Acguy and MSM-10 Zock.

First Combat - Atlantic and Belfast RaidsMobile Suit Gundam TV Ep. 28 'The Atlantic, a Sea of Blood'

UC 0079 November / 1979 broadcast

Zeon submarine fleet launches Z'Goks against Federation supply convoys in the Atlantic. Amuro Ray in the RX-78-2 Gundam is sortied from the White Base to intercept. The Z'Goks demonstrate devastating close combat, tearing through a Federation Medea transport and nearly dragging the Gundam underwater. Amuro learns amphibious MS cannot be fought like Zakus — their claws and underwater mobility negate beam rifle advantage at range. Establishes Z'Gok as a credible threat and foreshadows larger Jaburo assault. Gundam requires support from Guncannon/Guntank to repel.

Operation Jaburo Drop - Char's Red Z'GokMobile Suit Gundam TV Ep. 29-30

UC 0079 November 30 / 1979 broadcast

Zeon launches its largest Earth operation: airborne drop of amphibious MS directly into the Federation's Jaburo cavern base via HLVs. Char Aznable, in his red MSM-07S Commander Type, leads the Z'Gok contingent. Surfacing from Jaburo's underground river, Char's Z'Gok destroys multiple RGM-79 GMs and Type 61 tanks, dueling Amuro's Gundam in flooded tunnels. Despite surprise, Federation defenses and the Gundam's beam weapons repel the attack. Char's Z'Gok is damaged but he escapes, while most mass-production Z'Goks are lost. Failure of Jaburo invasion marks turning point of One Year War; demonstrates limits of amphibious surprise against prepared defenses. Char's red Z'Gok becomes iconic ace image, cementing 'Red Comet' amphibious reputation.

Accounts II
Remnants and Terror - Post-War Guerrilla UseMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Ep. 7-8; MSV-R: The Return of Johnny Ridden manga

UC 0088-0090 (in-universe) / 1986-2017 (publication)

After Zeon's surrender, hidden Zeon remnants and African/Asian guerrillas continue to use cached Z'Goks. In ZZ, a Z'Gok is fielded by Blue Team remnants in Dakar and later by Neo Zeon sympathizers. In MSV-R, former Zeon ace Johnny Ridden's followers deploy refurbished Z'Gok-E units against Federation patrols, showing the machine's longevity when maintained. These stories portray the Z'Gok as a durable, low-maintenance terror weapon for insurgents who can hide it in lakes and rivers. Extends Z'Gok service life beyond One Year War, explaining its appearance in later UC works and games. Reinforces image of Zeon tech persisting as war legacy.

Spirit of the Claw - Gundam Build Fighters Cameo and HomageGundam Build Fighters Episode 9; Gundam Build Fighters Try

2013-2014 broadcast

In the Build Fighters alternate universe where Gunpla battles are sport, a custom Z'Gok (often built as 'Z'Gok Homage' or 'Acguy vs Z'Gok' dioramas) appears as a competitor's machine. The narrative explicitly celebrates the Z'Gok's design — its claws are used for dramatic grappling moves, and commentators reference its Jaburo legacy. It loses to protagonist's Build Strike but earns respect for amphibious tactics in a desert arena, showing fan affection for the 'ugly-cute' design. Demonstrates Z'Gok's cultural afterlife as beloved Gunpla subject rather than just in-universe weapon; drives real-world kit sales and custom culture.

Development Genealogy - From Z'Gok to Hygogg and ZockMS Encyclopedia; Gundam Century (1981)

UC 0079-0083 (in-universe) / 1981-1998 (publication)

Lore books trace MIP's post-Z'Gok evolution: lessons from Z'Gok's claws and hydrojets inform the smaller, more stealthy MSM-04 Acguy (infiltration) and the massive MSM-10 Zock (heavy assault), and later in UC 0080s the MSM-03C Hygogg. The Z'Gok is positioned as the balanced middle — not as stealthy as Acguy, not as heavily armed as Zock, but the best all-rounder. This genealogy is presented as in-universe engineering history, with spec comparisons showing incremental improvements. Provides canonical justification for why Z'Gok remains the most produced and remembered of the MSM line; later machines are seen as derivatives.

Z'Gok is pronounced 'Zee-Gock' with a hard G and is named after an English word. — Official Japanese is Zu Gokku (ズゴック) with two hard Gs; no official English etymology exists. The name is an invented Zeonic MSM-series label, not a real word. Pronunciation varies in dubs but Japanese rendering is authoritative.

Char's Z'Gok is a completely different, more powerful model with bigger claws and beam weapons. — Char's MSM-07S is internally tuned (higher thrust, slightly higher reactor output) but externally and armament-identical to mass-production MSM-07. Its fame comes from pilot skill, not distinct weapons. The visually distinct claw upgrade belongs to MSM-07E Z'Gok-E.

Z'Gok can operate indefinitely underwater and is a submarine. — It is amphibious with extended underwater endurance due to water-cooled reactor, but still a mobile suit with limited air/propellant and need for resupply. It is deployed from submarines, not a replacement for them, and has a stated crush depth/maintenance requirement.

Z'Gok appears in the original Gundam novelization as Char's main machine. — In Tomino's 1979-80 novels, the Jaburo amphibious attack is significantly downplayed and Char does not pilot a Z'Gok there; the anime is the primary canon for Z'Gok's famous role.

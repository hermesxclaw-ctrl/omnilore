Who It Is

Hang Tuah is the greatest hero of the Malay world — a legendary warrior and loyal servant of the Sultan of Malacca. Falsely accused, he was secretly spared while his friend Hang Jebat rebelled in his name. Recalled, Hang Tuah killed Jebat in a devastating duel — an act endlessly debated as loyalty or betrayal.

Attested in Oral tradition / Hikayat Hang Tuah (17th-18th century CE)Era Malacca Sultanate (c. 15th century CE)

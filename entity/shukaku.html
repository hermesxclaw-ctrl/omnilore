Who It Is
Form

Shukaku is typically depicted as a tanuki, a type of raccoon dog, with a humanoid body and a long, bushy tail. In some versions, it is shown as a human priest with a tanuki-like face.

Powers

Shape-shifting Shukaku has the ability to shape-shift into various forms, including a human priest and a tanuki. This power allows it to move undetected and manipulate others.

Accounts

Shukaku is said to have lived as a priest at the Buddhist temple of Morinji, where it was known for its mischievous and cunning nature. However, it is also said to have had a kind and benevolent side, often helping those in need.

Shukaku is depicted as a tailed beast spirit that is sealed within Gaara, the main character of the story. It is said to have been sealed by the Fourth Kazekage, Gaara's father, as a means of protecting the village.

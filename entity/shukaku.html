Who It Is

The One-Tail, a tanuki (raccoon dog) made of sand with a single tail formed of multiple sand tendrils. Shukaku has mastery over sand and wind, capable of creating massive sandstorms and sealing enemies in compressed sand prisons. Sealed within Gaara, Shukaku constantly struggled for control but eventually became cooperative.

Mbaba Mwana Waresa

Lady of the Rainbow; Goddess of Rain and Fertility

also known asLady of the RainbowGoddess of Rain and Fertility
TypeRain/Fertility Deity
CultureZulu
Who It Is
Form

Mbaba Mwana Waresa is often depicted as a woman with a rainbow-colored house in the sky, surrounded by clouds and thunderstorms. Her appearance is associated with the colors of the rainbow, which symbolize the beauty and power of nature.

Powers

Rainmaking Mbaba Mwana Waresa has the power to bring rain and fertility to the land, ensuring the prosperity of the Zulu people.

Accounts

Mbaba Mwana Waresa is said to live in a rainbow-colored house in the sky, surrounded by clouds and thunderstorms. She is associated with the art of brewing beer and is said to play the bongo drum to bring rain and fertility to the land.

Mbaba Mwana Waresa is associated with the art of brewing beer, which is an important part of Zulu culture. She is said to have taught the Zulu people the art of brewing beer, which is a symbol of their connection to the divine.

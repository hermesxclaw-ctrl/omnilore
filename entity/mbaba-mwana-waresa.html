Love does not demand that you reach the clouds. Love brings the clouds down to you.

Who It Is

Mbaba Mwana Waresa is the Zulu goddess of rainbows, rain, harvest, and fertility. She is venerated as the divine patron of agriculture and is believed to control the life-giving rains that ensure the Zulu people's harvests. Her name reflects her dual nature: 'Mbaba' referencing her nurturing, maternal aspect, and 'Waresa' connecting her to the rainbow's appearance after storms. In Zulu myth, she lives in the clouds and is celebrated during spring, when maidens perform rituals in her honor. What makes her particularly distinctive among global rainbow deities is the romantic myth attached to her: Mbaba Mwana Waresa was courted by many divine beings but rejected them all, seeking a suitor who could love her for herself rather than her status. She ultimately fell in love with a mortal herder named Thandiwe, choosing to marry him and share her divine gifts with humanity. When he could not match the gods in their courtship games, she simply decided that love mattered more than divine competition. This rare story of a goddess choosing a mortal out of genuine love — rather than a mortal being elevated — makes her a unique figure in world mythology, embodying the principle that divine favor can flow downward through compassion rather than merit.

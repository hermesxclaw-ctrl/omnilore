Who It Is

Mbaba Mwana Waresa's mythology offers a unique perspective on the connection between the divine and the mortal world. Her association with rain and fertility highlights the importance of these themes in Zulu culture.

Mbaba Mwana Waresa is a benevolent deity who brings life-giving rain and fertility to the land. She is also associated with the art of brewing beer, which is an important part of Zulu culture.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name Mbaba Mwana Waresa is derived from the Zulu language, with 'Mbaba' meaning 'mother' or 'goddess', 'Mwana' meaning 'child' or 'rain', and 'Waresa' meaning 'rainbow' or 'colorful'.

Pronunciation — /mˈbɑːbɑ mwɑːnɑ wɑːrɛsɑ/

Aliases — Lady of the Rainbow, Goddess of Rain and Fertility

Ownership — Mbaba Mwana Waresa is a deity from Zulu mythology, specifically from the Nguni people of South Africa.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Mbaba Mwana Waresa is often depicted as a woman with a rainbow-colored house in the sky, surrounded by clouds and thunderstorms. Her appearance is associated with the colors of the rainbow, which symbolize the beauty and power of nature.

Symbols — The rainbow is a symbol of Mbaba Mwana Waresa, representing her connection to the sky, rain, and fertility. The bongo drum is also associated with her, as she is said to play it to bring rain and fertility to the land.

Domains — Mbaba Mwana Waresa's domains include fertility, agriculture, rain, and the art of brewing beer. Her habitats include the sky, the clouds, and the land.

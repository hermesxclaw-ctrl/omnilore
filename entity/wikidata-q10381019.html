Who It Is

He is the loudest voice in the room, a caricature of oil-boom wealth draped in a ten-gallon hat and bolo tie, but behind the bravado lies a figure whose every boast and blunder has shaped how the world imagines Texas extravagance.

He is loud, boastful, arrogant, and confrontational. He frequently brags about his wealth, often saying 'I'm richer than you!' or 'I own half of Texas!' He is quick to anger and often threatens others with lawsuits or physical violence. He is also shown to be politically conservative, supporting tax cuts and deregulation. Despite his bluster, he is occasionally shown to have a softer side, such as when he helps Homer in 'Two Bad Neighbors'.

Powers
Immense Wealth

He is extremely wealthy, with enough money to influence politics, buy businesses, and live a luxurious lifestyle. He often uses his wealth to get what he wants.

Limit: His wealth does not protect him from embarrassment or physical harm.

Persuasive Rhetoric

He is able to sway crowds with his bombastic speeches, often playing on populist sentiments.

Limit: His rhetoric is often transparently self-serving and can backfire.

Legal Intimidation

He frequently threatens to sue people, implying he has a team of lawyers at his disposal.

Limit: He rarely follows through, and his threats are often bluster.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Rich Texan is a portly, middle-aged man with a large brown mustache and sideburns. He typically wears a white or light-colored cowboy hat (often a ten-gallon hat), a bolo tie, a long-sleeved shirt with a vest or suit jacket, and boots. He sometimes holds a glass of whiskey or a cigar. His facial expression is often smug or aggressive.

Symbols — His signature accessories are the cowboy hat and bolo tie, symbolizing Texan identity. He is often shown with a Texas flag or in settings associated with wealth, such as country clubs, business meetings, or his ranch.

Domains — He resides in Texas, though he frequently visits Springfield. He is often seen at business meetings, country clubs, political rallies, and social events. He owns a ranch and is implied to have extensive oil interests.

Who It Is
Form

Polybius is an urban legend about a mysterious arcade video game that supposedly appeared in arcades around Portland, Oregon in 1981. The game was said to have a psychoactive, abstract, and dangerous gameplay. Children who played the game allegedly experienced strange and terrifying events.

Powers

Mind Control The game allegedly had the power to control its players' minds, erasing their memories and altering their behavior.

Who It Is
Form

Charon is often depicted as a bearded man wearing a conical hat and a tunic, later in modern Greek folklore as more angelic.

Powers

Guidance across the river Styx Charon's ability to guide souls across the river Styx to the underworld

Accounts

Orpheus, a musician and poet, journeys to the underworld to reclaim his wife Eurydice, who has died. Charon serves as the ferryman, guiding Orpheus across the river Styx.

Charon is mentioned as the ferryman who guides the souls of the dead across the river Styx to the underworld.

Who It Is

In the neon-lit world of Tokyo's occult underbelly, Utahime Iori stands as a rare figure—a shrine maiden whose sacred songs carry the weight of curses and blessings alike. Her story is one of duty, sacrifice, and the quiet power of melody.

Utahime is serious, disciplined, and devoted to her duties as a jujutsu sorcerer and shrine maiden. She is often frustrated by the antics of her colleague Gojo Satoru, but she cares deeply for her students and is willing to risk her life for them. She has a strong sense of responsibility and a hidden insecurity about her own strength compared to other high-level sorcerers.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Utahime (歌姫) means 'song princess' or 'diva', reflecting her role as a singer and shrine maiden. Iori (庵) can mean 'hermitage' or 'temple', fitting her connection to sacred spaces.

Pronunciation — OO-tah-hee-meh EE-oh-ree

Powers
Cursed Energy Manipulation

As a jujutsu sorcerer, Utahime can channel cursed energy to enhance her physical abilities and perform techniques.

Limit: Her cursed energy level is relatively low compared to special grade sorcerers, but she compensates with support techniques.

Reversal Technique: Solo Forbidden Area

A technique that allows her to create a barrier that nullifies cursed energy within a certain area, but it is incomplete and requires significant time to activate.

Limit: It takes a long time to set up and she cannot use it effectively in combat without protection.

Support via Song

Utahime's singing can enhance the cursed energy output of her allies, providing a buff in battle. This is a rare support ability.

Limit: Requires concentration and a clear voice; if interrupted, the effect is lost.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Utahime is a young woman with long dark hair often tied in a high ponytail, and she wears the traditional attire of a shrine maiden (miko) when on duty: a white kimono top and red hakama trousers. In casual settings, she wears modern clothing. She has a distinctive scar on her face, which she received during a mission.

Symbols — Her role as a shrine maiden associates her with Shinto symbolism, purification, and sacred music. She is often seen with a microphone or performing ritual songs to amplify her cursed energy.

Domains — She is based at Kyoto Jujutsu High School, but she travels to various locations in Japan for missions. She is often seen in shrines or temples, reflecting her role as a priestess.

Accounts I
Kyoto Sister School Exchange EventJujutsu Kaisen manga/anime

Modern era (2018-2020 in-universe)

Utahime participates in the exchange event between Tokyo and Kyoto Jujutsu High Schools. She attempts to use her barrier technique to nullify the curses within the event, but it is incomplete. She is later saved from a curse by Gojo, despite her annoyance with him. The event is disrupted by a curse user, but Utahime survives and continues her duties.

Accounts II
Shibuya IncidentJujutsu Kaisen manga/anime

2018 in-universe

Utahime is involved in the large-scale battle in Shibuya, where she supports other sorcerers with her singing. She faces danger but survives, though she is affected by the traumatic events. The incident results in many casualties, but Utahime survives and helps in the aftermath.

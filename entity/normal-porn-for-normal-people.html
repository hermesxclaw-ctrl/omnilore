Who It Is

A website hosting 'normal porn for normal people' — videos of mundane, non-sexual activities (a woman peeling an apple, a man tying his shoes, a child playing with blocks). Viewers report escalating dread and compulsion to continue watching. The videos become disturbing — the apple is a human eye, the shoelaces are intestines, the blocks are teeth. Anyone who watches a full video finds their own mundane activity uploaded to the site within 24 hours, followed by increasingly personal recordings. The final video in every sequence is the viewer's own death, pre-recorded.

Form

A simple early-2000s website: black text on white background, no images except video thumbnails. Tagline: 'Because normal people deserve normal things.' Each video is shot in flat, affectless style — no music, no editing, just a static camera capturing mundane activities that slowly reveal themselves to be anything but.

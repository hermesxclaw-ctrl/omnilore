Who It Is
Form

Buer is described as a powerful and wise demon, with a strong presence and a commanding voice. He is often depicted as a tall, imposing figure with a stern expression.

Powers

Illusions Buer has the ability to create powerful illusions, making it difficult for others to distinguish reality from fantasy. He can use this ability to deceive and manipulate others, or to protect himself from harm.

Healing Buer is said to have the power to heal physical and spiritual wounds, but his methods are often unorthodox and may have unintended consequences.

Accounts

A magician attempts to summon Buer using a combination of rituals and incantations. Buer appears and offers to grant the magician's wishes, but at a steep price.

A group of demons, led by Buer, betray their human master and turn against him. The humans are forced to flee for their lives, while Buer and his followers claim the master's power for themselves.

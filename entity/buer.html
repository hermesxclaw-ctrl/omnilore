Who It Is

Tenth spirit, a President commanding 50 legions. Buer appears when the sun is in Sagittarius, manifesting as a five-spoked wheel (like a pentagram in motion) with legs radiating outward, so he can travel freely in any direction — a symbol of unlimited movement and holistic knowledge. He teaches natural and moral philosophy, the virtues of herbs and plants, and logic. Most importantly, Buer heals all distempers, especially in humans. He is considered one of the more benevolent Goetic spirits, a demon of medicine and the natural sciences who was formerly of the angelic order of Powers.

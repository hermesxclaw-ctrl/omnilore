Who It Is
Form

Sōkyoku (Minazuki) is often depicted as a musical instrument with a humanoid body, symbolizing the entity's connection to music and the seasons.

Powers

Seasonal Manipulation Sōkyoku (Minazuki) has the ability to manipulate the seasons, particularly autumn, and is said to possess the power to bring about a sense of melancholy and nostalgia in those who hear its music.

Who It Is

Rhiannon is an Otherworld goddess whose name means 'Great Queen.' She appears on a magical horse no pursuer can catch. After her son Pryderi was stolen at birth, she was falsely accused of devouring him and condemned to carry visitors on her back like a horse for seven years. Her sacred birds sing so beautifully they can wake the dead.

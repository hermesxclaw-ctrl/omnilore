Who It Is
Form

The Elder Things are described as ancient, extraterrestrial beings with elongated heads, long arms, and large, black eyes. They are said to be highly intelligent and possess advanced technology.

Powers

Telepathy The Elder Things possess advanced telepathic abilities, allowing them to communicate with other beings and manipulate their thoughts.

Technology The Elder Things possess advanced technology, including devices that allow them to manipulate the environment and control the actions of other beings.

Regeneration The Elder Things have the ability to regenerate their bodies, allowing them to survive even the most severe injuries.

Accounts

The story follows an expedition to Antarctica, where they discover the ruins of an ancient city and encounter the Elder Things.

The story follows a professor who is transported to a distant planet, where he encounters the Elder Things and learns about their history and culture.

The story follows a young man who is haunted by strange dreams and eventually discovers that he is being influenced by the Elder Things.

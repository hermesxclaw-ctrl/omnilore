Who It Is

The Old Ones who came to Earth billions of years ago and built vast cities across the planet, including at the South Pole. They are barrel-shaped, winged, star-headed beings who created Earth's first life — and the Shoggoths. A highly advanced race now in decline, their civilization crumbling in Antarctica.

Form

Barrel-shaped body 8 ft tall, star-shaped head with five eyes, five eating tubes, five muscular arms, five wings, five legs — all radially symmetrical; iridescent gray-green skin, cilia for movement on land

Powers

Advanced biotechnology

Shoggoth creation

Space flight

City building

Long hibernation

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

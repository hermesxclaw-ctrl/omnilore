In the shadowed corners of folklore, where birds speak and the world is still being shaped, there perches a figure of immense power and mystery: Father Raven. This enigmatic being, often depicted as a colossal black bird with eyes like burning coals, is said to be the architect of worlds and the keeper of ancient secrets. But who is Father Raven truly? Is he a benevolent creator, a trickster, or a harbinger of doom? The answers are as varied as the cultures that whisper his name, and the truth remains as elusive as a raven's shadow at dusk.

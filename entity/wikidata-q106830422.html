Who It Is

In the hallowed halls of Hyakkaou Private Academy, where fortunes are won and lost on the turn of a card, Kirari Momobami reigns not as a tyrant but as an architect of chaos—a young woman whose serene smile masks a mind that treats human lives as pieces on a vast, merciless chessboard.

Kirari is portrayed as unnervingly calm, highly intelligent, and utterly unpredictable. She views life as a series of gambles and treats people as pawns in her grand game. She is not sadistic but rather indifferent to the suffering of others, seeing it as a natural consequence of the system she upholds. She shows a deep fascination with those who defy her expectations, particularly Yumeko Jabami.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname Momobami (桃喰) can be interpreted as 'peach eater,' with 'momo' meaning peach and 'bami' from the verb 'hamu' (to eat). The given name Kirari (生来) carries connotations of 'innate' or 'natural-born,' reflecting her perceived inherent superiority.

Pronunciation — Kee-rah-ree Moh-moh-bah-mee

Aliases — Kirari, Student Council President, Momobami Kirari

Ownership — Japanese manga/anime franchise, created by Homura Kawamoto and Tōru Naomura, published by Square Enix.

Powers
Gambling Mastery

Kirari is an exceptional gambler with a near-supernatural ability to read opponents and manipulate probabilities. She is skilled in various games, including poker, rock-paper-scissors, and custom games devised by the council.

Limit: Her arrogance and desire for stimulation can lead her to take unnecessary risks.

Psychological Manipulation

She can easily discern the psychological weaknesses of others and exploit them to her advantage, often before a game even begins.

Limit: Her manipulations can backfire if her opponent is truly unpredictable, as seen with Yumeko.

Authority

As Student Council President, she has the power to set the rules of gambling within the school, confiscate 'vital funds,' and expel students who refuse to gamble.

Limit: Her authority is dependent on the continued support of the council and the school's administration.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Kirari is a tall, slender young woman with long, straight blonde hair and striking blue eyes. She typically wears the Hyakkaou Private Academy female uniform, but customizes it with a long, flowing skirt and a ribbon. Her calm, almost expressionless face often holds a slight, enigmatic smile.

Symbols — Her symbol is the card suit of spades, often associated with her in promotional art. She is also linked to the concept of 'gambling' as a life philosophy.

Domains — She is the President of the Student Council at Hyakkaou Private Academy, a position that gives her absolute authority over the school's gambling hierarchy. She resides in the Student Council room, which is lavishly decorated and serves as the center of her power.

Accounts I
The Presidential Election ArcKakegurui manga, chapters 26-55

Modern day (fictional)

Kirari initiates a school-wide election to choose the next Student Council President, but it is actually a scheme to identify a worthy opponent. She manipulates the election process, using her twin sister Ririka as a proxy, and ultimately faces Yumeko in a final gamble. The game is a custom variant of 'Life' and 'Reversi,' where Kirari's strategic genius is pushed to its limits. In the end, Kirari loses but is delighted to have found a rival who challenges her worldview. Kirari's loss to Yumeko shakes the school's hierarchy and plants seeds of doubt about her invincibility. It also strengthens the bond between Yumeko and Kirari, as they acknowledge each other's skills.

Accounts II
The Twin Sister RevelationKakegurui manga, chapters 26-55

Modern day (fictional)

Kirari reveals that she has been having her identical twin sister, Ririka, act as her public face for years. Ririka is shy and lacks Kirari's confidence, but Kirari uses her as a tool to maintain a façade of omnipresence. This revelation complicates the election arc and shows Kirari's willingness to sacrifice even her family for her goals. The revelation strains the sisters' relationship and leads Ririka to eventually assert her own identity.

Kirari's true intentionsSome fans interpret Kirari as a nihilistic figure who seeks to destroy the school's corrupt system.Others see her as a pure hedonist who simply enjoys gambling and chaos for its own sake.

Kirari is a sociopath with no emotions. — She displays a range of emotions, including excitement, amusement, and even a twisted form of affection. Her calm demeanor is a mask, but she is not devoid of feeling.

Kirari is the main villain of the series. — While she is an antagonist in many arcs, the series does not have a clear-cut villain. Kirari's actions are often a catalyst for the protagonist's growth, and she is not purely evil.

The Approach

In the sprawling, monster-haunted underworld of Orario, where adventurers descend into the Dungeon to carve out glory and gold, Tione Hiryute stands as a whirlwind of ferocity and loyalty—a first-class adventurer of the Loki Familia whose twin blades and fierce devotion have carved a legend of their own.

Content advisory. Contains fantasy violence, perilous dungeon exploration, and depictions of combat. No explicit dark material.
Identity

Tione Hiryute

Pronunciation Tee-Oh-Nay Hee-Ryoo-Tay

Etymology

The name 'Tione' may be derived from the Greek mythological figure Tione (Thyone), the mother of Dionysus, though no official etymology has been confirmed. 'Hiryute' is a surname specific to the series.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese light novel franchise 'DanMachi' (Is It Wrong to Try to Pick Up Girls in a Dungeon?) by Fujino Ōmori, published by SB Creative (GA Bunko).

Disputed Identifications

None known; the character is unique to the franchise.

Description

Appearance

Tione is a young woman with long, dark hair often tied in a ponytail, and amber-brown eyes. She has a well-toned, athletic build befitting a first-class adventurer. She typically wears the Loki Familia's uniform: a white and blue outfit with a short skirt, thigh-high stockings, and armored gauntlets. She wields two large daggers or short swords, and is known for her fierce, confident expression.

Symbols & Iconography

The Loki Familia emblem (a stylized flame or similar) may appear on her attire. Her twin blades are her signature weapons.

Behaviour & Personality

Tione is fiercely loyal to her familia and especially to her captain, Finn Deimne, for whom she harbors romantic feelings. She is hot-headed, impulsive, and prone to jealousy, especially regarding her twin sister, Tiona, and other women who get close to Finn. Despite her temper, she is a skilled and reliable adventurer, protective of her comrades, and shows a softer side when it comes to her sister and close friends.

Domains & Habitats

Primarily the city of Orario, specifically the Loki Familia home. She descends into the Dungeon for expeditions and monster subjugation.

Version Differences

The anime adaptation streamlines some of her interactions and comedic moments, while the light novel provides more internal monologue and detailed battle descriptions. The manga is largely faithful to the light novel.

Powers & Abilities

Twin Blade Combat

Source Version Light novel, manga, anime

Description

Tione is a highly skilled dual-wielder, using two short swords or daggers with speed and precision. She is capable of taking on powerful monsters and rival adventurers.

Limitations

While powerful, she is not the strongest in her familia; her abilities are outclassed by higher-level adventurers like Ais Wallenstein.

Contradictions

None noted.

Superhuman Physical Attributes

Source Version Light novel, anime

Description

As a Level 5 adventurer, Tione possesses enhanced strength, speed, agility, and endurance far beyond normal humans, allowing her to fight monsters and survive in the Dungeon's deep floors.

Limitations

Her abilities are still within the limits of the Falna system; she can be injured or killed by sufficiently powerful foes.

Contradictions

None noted.

Magic

Source Version Light novel, anime

Description

Tione has a limited magical ability, primarily using a fire-based spell. She is not a dedicated mage and relies more on physical combat.

Limitations

Her magic is less developed than her physical skills and requires concentration to cast.

Contradictions

Some sources suggest she has no magic, but the light novel indicates she has at least one spell.

The Loki Familia's Expedition to the Deep Floors

Source DanMachi light novel, volumes 5-6; anime season 1Culture Japanese light novel culturePeriod Contemporary setting (fictional)

Summary

Tione participates in the Loki Familia's large-scale expedition to the 50th floor of the Dungeon. She fights alongside her familia members, including Finn, Riveria, and Ais, and demonstrates her combat prowess and loyalty. During the expedition, she also deals with her feelings for Finn and her rivalry with other women.

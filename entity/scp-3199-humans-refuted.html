Who It Is

A species of creatures that reproduce by laying eggs inside humans. SCP-3199 instances appear as hairless, pale humanoid chickens — approximately 2.5m tall, with a chicken's head and a humanoid body from the neck down. They are terrified of humans but compelled to reproduce using human hosts. An egg laid in a human gestates in approximately 15 minutes, and the offspring bursts from the host fully formed, screaming.

Form

Humanoid-chicken hybrid: chicken head with large, unblinking eyes, featherless pale-pink flesh, long human-like limbs ending in clawed hands/feet, hunched posture. Instances move in jerky, panicked motions and emit a constant low-pitched screech.

Powers

Rapid gestation (15 minutes)

Parasitic reproduction

Human host dependency

Regeneration

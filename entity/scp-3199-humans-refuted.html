Who It Is
Form

SCP-3199 denotes a species of sapient Category-5 biological entities of currently indeterminable origin, though tissue samples indicate the presence of Silkie chicken, chimpanzee, stoat, mussel, adder, and human DNA.

Powers

Adaptation SCP-3199's ability to adapt to its environment has been observed to be highly effective, allowing it to evade capture and classification.

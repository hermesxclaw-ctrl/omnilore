Who It Is

Umay is the Turkic and Mongol goddess of fertility, childbirth, and children. She descends as a bird — a swan or silver bird — to take children's souls and protect mothers. Amulets bearing her symbol were worn for protection. The Orkhon inscriptions describe the Turkic khagan protected by 'Tengri above and Umay below.'

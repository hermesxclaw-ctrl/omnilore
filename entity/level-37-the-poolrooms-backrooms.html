Who It Is

One of the most visually iconic Backrooms levels — an infinite complex of interconnected pool rooms with pale ceramic tiles, warm water, and arched ceilings in pastel colors. The water is clean, warm (~30°C), and hypnotically beautiful. Wanderers who stay too long become 'Pool-drowned': their bodies dissolve into the water while their consciousness merges with the level. Faces visible in the tile patterns are previous visitors.

Form

Infinite connected rooms with ceramic tile in pale whites, teals, and pinks, with warm clear water filling the lower half of each room. Arched doorways connect chambers. Lighting is soft and diffuse with no visible source. Occasional vintage pool furniture or abstract tile sculptures.

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

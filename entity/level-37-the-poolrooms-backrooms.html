Who It Is
Form

Level 37 is an expansive complex of interconnected rooms and corridors submerged in undulating, lukewarm water. Each area of the level varies greatly in size and structure, ranging from uniform pools and hallways to more open, abnormally shaped areas. The environment is characterized by a perpetual, eerie silence.

Powers

Environmental Adaptation The ability to adapt to the ever-changing environment of the Poolrooms, allowing individuals to navigate and survive in this unpredictable space.

Accounts

A group of explorers ventured into the Poolrooms, seeking to uncover its secrets. However, they became lost in the ever-changing environment and were never seen again.

A group of individuals claimed to have uncovered hidden secrets within the Poolrooms, including ancient artifacts and mysterious symbols.

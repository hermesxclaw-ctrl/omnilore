Who It Is
Form

Verethragna is often depicted as a warrior or a hero, with a sword or a spear in hand. In some representations, he is shown with a lion or a bull, symbolizing his power and strength. As a yazata, Verethragna is often associated with the elements of fire and water, which are seen as essential for his power.

Powers

Victory Verethragna is said to have the power to grant victory to those who invoke him. He is often invoked by the Zoroastrians in times of war and conflict, and is believed to help them overcome their obstacles and achieve their goals.

Accounts

The story tells of a hero who invokes Verethragna's help in a time of war. Verethragna grants the hero victory and helps him to overcome his obstacles.

The story tells of a demon who invokes Verethragna's help in a time of war. Verethragna grants the demon victory, but ultimately helps the hero to overcome him.

Who It Is

Regulus Corneas, a name that echoes through the halls of the Re:Zero universe, is a figure of terrifying power and unfathomable madness—a knight whose very existence defies the natural order and whose story reveals the darkest depths of obsession and self-deception.

Regulus exhibits a grandiose and narcissistic personality, believing himself to be the most perfect being in existence. He speaks in a condescending manner, often lecturing others on his 'rights' and the 'correct' way to live. He is emotionally volatile, prone to fits of rage when his beliefs are challenged. He is also highly possessive, viewing his wives as property and expecting absolute obedience.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Regulus' is Latin for 'little king' or 'prince', possibly alluding to his self-proclaimed authority and his role as a Sin Archbishop. 'Corneas' might be a play on 'cornus' (Latin for 'horn') or 'cornea' (the eye's transparent layer), but no official etymology is provided.

Pronunciation — REH-gyoo-lus KOR-nee-as

Aliases — Regulus Corneas

Ownership — Fictional character owned by Tappei Nagatsuki, creator of Re:Zero. The character is part of the Japanese light novel franchise and its adaptations.

Powers
Authority of Greed: Lion's Heart

Regulus's Authority allows him to stop the time of his own body, rendering him completely invulnerable to any physical attack or external influence. He can also apply this effect to other objects or people, freezing them in time, making them immovable and indestructible.

Limit: The ability requires a 'heart' to function, which he transfers to a separate object (usually a wife). If the object is destroyed, he loses his invulnerability. Additionally, he must consciously activate the ability, and it does not protect him from psychological or emotional manipulation.

Enhanced physical abilities

Even without his Authority, Regulus possesses superhuman strength and speed, allowing him to easily overpower ordinary humans and even some powerful warriors.

Limit: These abilities are still within the realm of human limits when compared to other top-tier fighters in the series.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Regulus is a young man with short, neat blond hair and crimson eyes. He typically wears a white suit with a black shirt and a red tie, often appearing calm and composed. His attire is reminiscent of a noble or a butler, which contrasts with his erratic behavior.

Symbols — He is associated with the symbol of the Witch Cult, specifically the Sin Archbishop of Greed. His authority, 'Lion's Heart', is symbolized by a lion, representing his claimed invincibility and dominance.

Domains — He is encountered primarily in the Pleiades Watchtower in the Augria Sand Dunes, where he resides as one of the Sin Archbishops. He also appears in the royal capital of Lugunica during the events of Arc 5.

Accounts I
The Sin Archbishop of GreedRe:Zero light novel, Arc 5

Fictional timeline, year 1 of the current era

Regulus Corneas is introduced as one of the Sin Archbishops of the Witch Cult, who attacks the royal capital of Lugunica alongside other Archbishops. He confronts the main characters, including Subaru Natsuki and Emilia, and demonstrates his invincibility. He is ultimately defeated when Subaru discovers the secret of his Authority: his heart is transferred to his wife, and destroying it nullifies his invulnerability. Regulus is killed, and his death contributes to the fall of the Witch Cult's influence. His defeat also reveals the weakness of the Greed Authority, providing crucial knowledge for future battles.

Accounts II
Regulus's Past and the Witch of GreedRe:Zero light novel, Arc 6

Fictional past, several centuries before the main story

It is revealed that Regulus was once a human who obtained the Authority of Greed after an encounter with the Witch of Greed, Echidna. He used his power to take multiple wives, whom he treated as possessions. His obsession with 'rights' and 'ownership' stemmed from his belief that he was entitled to everything he desired. This backstory explains his distorted worldview and his eventual downfall, as his wives were not truly loyal to him.

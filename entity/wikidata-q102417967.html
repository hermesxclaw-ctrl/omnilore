The Approach

Regulus Corneas, a name that echoes through the halls of the Re:Zero universe, is a figure of terrifying power and unfathomable madness—a knight whose very existence defies the natural order and whose story reveals the darkest depths of obsession and self-deception.

Content advisory. Contains themes of psychological manipulation, violence, and abuse of power.
Identity

Regulus Corneas

Pronunciation REH-gyoo-lus KOR-nee-as

Etymology

The name 'Regulus' is Latin for 'little king' or 'prince', possibly alluding to his self-proclaimed authority and his role as a Sin Archbishop. 'Corneas' might be a play on 'cornus' (Latin for 'horn') or 'cornea' (the eye's transparent layer), but no official etymology is provided.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character owned by Tappei Nagatsuki, creator of Re:Zero. The character is part of the Japanese light novel franchise and its adaptations.

Disputed Identifications

There are no known disputes regarding his character identity, but some fans have theorized about his past and motivations, which are not fully explored in the source material.

Description

Appearance

Regulus is a young man with short, neat blond hair and crimson eyes. He typically wears a white suit with a black shirt and a red tie, often appearing calm and composed. His attire is reminiscent of a noble or a butler, which contrasts with his erratic behavior.

Symbols & Iconography

He is associated with the symbol of the Witch Cult, specifically the Sin Archbishop of Greed. His authority, 'Lion's Heart', is symbolized by a lion, representing his claimed invincibility and dominance.

Behaviour & Personality

Regulus exhibits a grandiose and narcissistic personality, believing himself to be the most perfect being in existence. He speaks in a condescending manner, often lecturing others on his 'rights' and the 'correct' way to live. He is emotionally volatile, prone to fits of rage when his beliefs are challenged. He is also highly possessive, viewing his wives as property and expecting absolute obedience.

Domains & Habitats

He is encountered primarily in the Pleiades Watchtower in the Augria Sand Dunes, where he resides as one of the Sin Archbishops. He also appears in the royal capital of Lugunica during the events of Arc 5.

Version Differences

In the light novel, his backstory and abilities are more detailed, including his past with the Witch of Greed, Echidna. The anime adaptation condenses some of his dialogue and fights, but remains faithful to his character.

Powers & Abilities

Authority of Greed: Lion's Heart

Source Version Light novel, Arc 5; Anime Season 2

Description

Regulus's Authority allows him to stop the time of his own body, rendering him completely invulnerable to any physical attack or external influence. He can also apply this effect to other objects or people, freezing them in time, making them immovable and indestructible.

Limitations

The ability requires a 'heart' to function, which he transfers to a separate object (usually a wife). If the object is destroyed, he loses his invulnerability. Additionally, he must consciously activate the ability, and it does not protect him from psychological or emotional manipulation.

Contradictions

None known, but the mechanics of the Authority are not fully explained, leaving room for interpretation.

Enhanced physical abilities

Source Version Light novel, Arc 5; Anime Season 2

Description

Even without his Authority, Regulus possesses superhuman strength and speed, allowing him to easily overpower ordinary humans and even some powerful warriors.

Limitations

These abilities are still within the realm of human limits when compared to other top-tier fighters in the series.

Contradictions

None known.

Stories & Myths

The Sin Archbishop of Greed

Source Re:Zero light novel, Arc 5Culture Japanese light novelPeriod Fictional timeline, year 1 of the current era

Summary

Regulus Corneas is introduced as one of the Sin Archbishops of the Witch Cult, who attacks the royal capital of Lugunica alongside other Archbishops. He confronts the main characters, including Subaru Natsuki and Emilia, and demonstrates his invincibility. He is ultimately defeated when Subaru discovers the secret of his Authority: his heart is transferred to his wife, and destroying it nullifies his invulnerability.

Consequences

Regulus is killed, and his death contributes to the fall of the Witch Cult's influence. His defeat also reveals the weakness of the Greed Authority, providing crucial knowledge for future battles.

Variants

In the anime, the fight is shortened, but the core plot remains the same.

Regulus's Past and the Witch of Greed

Source Re:Zero light novel, Arc 6Culture Japanese light novelPeriod Fictional past, several centuries before the main story

Summary

It is revealed that Regulus was once a human who obtained the Authority of Greed after an encounter with the Witch of Greed, Echidna. He used his power to take multiple wives, whom he treated as possessions. His obsession with 'rights' and 'ownership' stemmed from his belief that he was entitled to everything he desired.

This backstory explains his distorted worldview and his eventual downfall, as his wives were not truly loyal to him.

Who It Is
Form

The Mazzamurello is often depicted as a small, mischievous sprite with a whirlwind-like appearance, and is said to be able to create powerful storms.

Powers

Storm Conjuring The Mazzamurello is said to have the ability to conjure powerful storms and whirlwinds, and is often associated with the creation of strong winds and heavy rains.

A young shepherd becomes lost in the mountains and is aided by the Mazzamurello, who leads him back to his flock.

Who It Is

He has been sealed to a wall for five hundred years with a spear through his gut, snarling at any human stupid enough to free him — and the moment Ushio Aotsuki does, the tiger-striped yokai called Tora decides he will eat the boy. Then he doesn't. For the next six years of Kazuhiro Fujita's 33-volume epic, Tora will be Ushio's most reluctant ally, foul-mouthed bodyguard, and living reminder that monsters in Japanese folklore are not pets, powers, or punchlines: they are ancient, hungry, and hard to forgive.

Attested personality is volatile, proud, vulgar, and deeply lonely. Tora speaks in rough, masculine Japanese (ore, kisama), insults Ushio constantly ('Ushio, you brat'), threatens to eat humans, and initially attempts to kill Ushio after being freed. Over time he develops a tsundere-like loyalty: he protects Ushio's friends (Asako, Mayuko), respects strength, and shows unexpected sentimentality, especially toward Mayuko Inoue, whom he treats with gentle protectiveness — a key emotional anchor. Powers
Superhuman Strength, Speed and Durability

Can shatter stone, rip apart lesser yokai, leap dozens of meters, and survive impalement by the Beast Spear for 500 years. Fights on par with high-class yokai such as Gamin and Izuna.

Limit: Not invincible; wounded by Hakumen no Mono's minions, by the Beast Spear itself, and by strong yokai like Guren. Requires recovery time and feeding. Weakened when separated from ambient yokai energy.

Lightning Manipulation and Flight (Nagatobimaru Ability)

Generates and rides lightning, fires bolt attacks, and flies at high speed with lightning aura. True form Nagatobimaru could fly long distances; as Tora he retains this but with less range.

Limit: Exhausting; cannot sustain continuous lightning against Hakumen no Mono's barrier without support. Rain and water not a hard counter but can disperse charge. Anime visualizes more prominently than manga.

Shapeshifting and Human Disguise

Can transform into a handsome human male, small animal forms, or alter size. Uses human form to infiltrate or to avoid panic, though personality remains abrasive.

Limit: Disguise imperfect — tail or stripes may show when agitated; strong spiritual sensors see through it. Costs energy; he prefers true form.

Regeneration and Longevity

Yokai lifespan measured in millennia; survives 500-year sealing without sustenance, regenerates from grievous wounds within hours to days if not killed by Beast Spear or Hakumen no Mono.

Limit: Regeneration fails against Beast Spear's soul-eroding effect and Hakumen's white miasma. Will eventually die if spear remains in body too long (as with previous wielders).

Yokai Sensory Perception

Can smell yokai, spirits, and Hakumen's miasma across kilometers, see invisible yokai, and sense human fear/lying.

Limit: Overwhelmed by Hakumen no Mono's massive presence; can be fooled by strong illusions (e.g., De no Kuni yokai).

Mechanical Genius and Artificial Blade Engineering (Homonymous Tora - Xenoblade Chronicles 2)

For disambiguation: Xenoblade's Tora is a Nopon engineer who builds and maintains the Artificial Blade Poppi (α, QT, QTπ), with upgradeable modes, elemental drives, and combat arts. No supernatural powers himself.

Limit: Human-level physical ability; relies entirely on Poppi for combat; requires parts, ether, and Tiger! Tiger! minigame resources.

Accounts I
The Sealing at Azafuse and Meeting UshioUshio to Tora manga Vol.1 ch.1-2; TV anime Ep.1

1990 (publication); narrative set 1980s-90s, flashback to 500 years prior (Sengoku era)

Yokai Tora is found sealed to the wall of Ushio Aotsuki's family storehouse by the Beast Spear, placed there 500 years earlier by the samurai Shagakusha (who became the yokai Gamin's host). Ushio, pressured by yokai attracted to the spear's loosening, frees Tora to save his friends Asako and Mayuko. Tora immediately tries to eat Ushio but is forced by the spear's power to cooperate. Their antagonistic partnership begins, establishing the series' core dynamic: human boy wielding a soul-eating spear and the monster who both hates and needs him. Establishes Beast Spear lore, Tora's 500-year imprisonment, and the Ushio-Tora pact that drives the entire series. Introduces Hakumen no Mono's distant threat.

Origin of Nagatobimaru: Born from Hakumen no MonoUshio to Tora manga Vol.18 ch.159-165; TV anime Ep.26-27 'The Beast Spear and Nagatobimaru'

1993-1994 (publication); narrative mythic past ~2000 years ago (Yayoi/Kofun period)

Revealed that Tora was not originally a natural yokai but was created from a piece of Hakumen no Mono's own flesh, cut off by the priestess Jie Mei and the warrior Shagakusha during the first sealing attempt. That flesh, infused with hatred and will, became Nagatobimaru, a tiger-like yokai who retained memory of being part of the ultimate evil. He was later sealed by Shagakusha's descendant using the Beast Spear. This makes Tora both child and enemy of Hakumen no Mono, explaining his unique ability to sense and wound it. Reframes Tora from generic monster to tragic, created being; explains his existential hatred for Hakumen and his ability to resist its miasma. Central to final war strategy.

The Tōno Yokai Alliance and Battle with GurenManga Vol.7-9; TV anime Ep.12-14

1991 (publication); narrative present

Ushio and Tora travel to Tōno, Iwate — heartland of Japanese yokai folklore — to seek allies. Tora, as a high-ranking yokai, must negotiate with the Tōno yokai council (including kappa, tengu, and mountain gods). He is challenged by Guren, a former comrade who chose to serve Hakumen no Mono. Their brutal duel forces Tora to confront that many yokai willingly submit to Hakumen out of fear. Ushio's human compassion and Tora's refusal to kneel win the Tōno alliance. Secures major yokai army for final battle; deepens Tora's character as yokai who chooses free will over survival. Establishes folklore-accurate Tōno setting.

Accounts II
Mayuko, Jie Mei's Reincarnation, and Tora's HeartManga Vol.12-15, Vol.28; TV anime Ep.18-19, 34

1992-1995 (publication)

Tora discovers that Mayuko Inoue, Ushio's kind classmate, is the reincarnation of Jie Mei, the priestess who created both the Beast Spear and, inadvertently, himself. He becomes fiercely protective of her, refusing to let her sacrifice herself as Jie Mei did. In a quiet, pivotal scene, Tora — who claims to hate humans — risks his life to save Mayuko from Hakumen's assassin yokai, revealing his capacity for love and his fear of losing the only person who shows him unconditional kindness. Humanizes Tora; explains why Hakumen targets Mayuko; sets up Mayuko's role in the final sealing ritual.

Final War: Ushio and Tora vs. Hakumen no MonoManga Vol.28-33 ch.270-313; TV anime Ep.35-39

1995-1996 (publication); narrative present apocalypse

Hakumen no Mono fully awakens, covering Japan in white miasma that turns humans into yokai and yokai into mindless servants. Ushio, now rejected by the Beast Spear (which is consuming his soul), and Tora lead a united army of humans, yokai, and former enemies. Tora fights his 'mother' directly, using his shared flesh to pierce Hakumen's barrier — a feat no other yokai can achieve. In the climax, Ushio and Tora together drive the Beast Spear (wielded with the souls of all past wielders) into Hakumen's nine tails, destroying it at the cost of Ushio's apparent death. Tora, for the first time, weeps and refuses to leave Ushio's body. Hakumen destroyed; yokai and humans survive; epilogue shows Tora alive but alone, waiting for Ushio's return (Ushio revived by collective prayer). Series ends on theme of coexistence.

Xenoblade Chronicles 2: Tora and Poppi's Journey (Homonymous Parallel Story)Xenoblade Chronicles 2, Monolith Soft/Nintendo, 2017. Chapters 1-10.

2017 (release); narrative fantasy world of Alrest

Included for disambiguation: Tora, a young Nopon from Gormott, dreams of becoming a Driver despite Nopon inability to resonate with Core Crystals. With his grandfather Tatazo and father Soosoo's legacy, he builds the Artificial Blade Poppi α. Joining Rex and Pyra's party, Tora upgrades Poppi through QT and QTπ forms, confronts his family's failure with the artificial Blade Lila, and proves that artificial hearts can be as real as natural ones. His story is comic and heartfelt, centered on engineering and found family, not yokai horror. Provides complete separate continuity for the name Tora; demonstrates why conflating this Tora with Fujita's yokai is erroneous. Major fanbase overlap causes frequent misidentification online.

Tora is just Ushio's cute pet monster / mascot — Manga explicitly rejects this: Tora is a man-eating, foul-mouthed, 2000-year-old predator who repeatedly threatens to eat Ushio and humans. His comedy (hamburger obsession, fear of cars) coexists with horror. Fujita's afterword stresses Tora is not a pet but a mirror of humanity's fear of yokai.

Tora from Ushio and Tora and Tora from Xenoblade Chronicles 2 are the same character or related forms — They share only the name (Japanese for tiger). Designs, species (yokai vs. Nopon), creators, publishers, and universes are entirely distinct. Ushio's Tora is 3m tiger yokai; Xenoblade's Tora is 0.6m hamster-like engineer who builds Poppi.

Tora means a special mythological tiger god in Japanese folklore — Tora (虎) is simply the common noun for tiger. Tigers are not native to Japan, so folklore tigers are often Chinese-imported imagery or yokai variants, not a single god. Fujita's Tora is an original yokai, not a traditional deity; his name Nagatobimaru is invented.

Who It Is

The primary enemies encountered in Brennenburg Castle — grotesque, humanoid servants who were once human but were horrifically transformed by the enigmatic Shadow. The Gatherers have had their lower jaws torn away, their eyes removed, and their hands replaced with blades and hooks. They patrol the castle, responding to sound and light. The player, Daniel, cannot fight them — only hide, run, and manage his dwindling sanity as the creatures draw near.

Form

Humanoid males with gray, flayed skin, missing lower jaws (exposing a dangling tongue), empty eye sockets, and one or both hands replaced with jagged metal implements. Their movements are shambling and pained, accompanied by wet breathing sounds and mournful groans.

Powers

Sound detection

Light sensitivity

Relentless pursuit

High damage output

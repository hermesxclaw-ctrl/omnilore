Who It Is
Form

The Gatherers are humanoid creatures with a twisted and grotesque appearance. They have long arms and legs, and their bodies are covered in dark, tattered clothing. Their faces are twisted into grotesque grins, and their eyes glow with an otherworldly energy.

Powers

Regeneration The Gatherers have the ability to regenerate their bodies, allowing them to withstand significant amounts of damage before being defeated.

Accounts

The Gatherers were created by Alexander of Brennenburg as a means of hunting down and killing any intruders in the castle.

The Gatherers were tasked with hunting down and killing Daniel, the protagonist of the game.

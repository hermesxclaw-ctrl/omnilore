Who It Is

The Mind Flayer is a complex and fascinating creature, with a rich history and a wide range of abilities and characteristics. Whether you're a seasoned adventurer or a new player, the Mind Flayer is sure to captivate and inspire.

The Mind Flayers are known to be arrogant and elitist, believing themselves to be superior to all other creatures. They are also highly intelligent and cunning, often using their psionic powers to manipulate and control others.

Powers
Psionic Powers

The Mind Flayer possesses powerful psionic abilities, including telepathy, telekinesis, and mind control. They can use these powers to manipulate and control others, as well as to sense and track their surroundings.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Mind Flayer is a monstrous humanoid aberration with psionic powers. They are typically depicted as having a humanoid body with a large, flat head, long tentacles, and a mouth filled with razor-sharp teeth. Their skin is often described as being pale and scaly, with a texture similar to that of a fish.

Symbols — The Mind Flayer is often associated with the symbol of the Underdark, a vast network of underground tunnels and cities. They are also linked to the concept of the 'Creeping Madness', a psychological affliction that affects those who are exposed to their psionic powers.

Domains — The Mind Flayers are native to the Underdark, a vast network of underground tunnels and cities. They are also known to inhabit other subterranean environments, such as caves and abandoned mines.

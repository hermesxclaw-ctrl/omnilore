Who It Is
Form

The Mind Flayer is a monstrous humanoid aberration with psionic powers. They are typically depicted as having a humanoid body with a large, flat head, long tentacles, and a mouth filled with razor-sharp teeth. Their skin is often described as being pale and scaly, with a texture similar to that of a fish.

Powers

Psionic Powers The Mind Flayer possesses powerful psionic abilities, including telepathy, telekinesis, and mind control. They can use these powers to manipulate and control others, as well as to sense and track their surroundings.

Accounts

The Underdark is a vast network of underground tunnels and cities, home to a variety of subterranean creatures, including the Mind Flayers. The story follows a group of adventurers as they explore the Underdark and uncover the secrets of the Mind Flayers.

The Creeping Madness is a psychological affliction that affects those who are exposed to the Mind Flayers' psionic powers. The story follows a group of adventurers as they try to uncover the source of the Creeping Madness and stop the Mind Flayers' plans.

Who It Is
Form

The Fisher King is often depicted as a king who has been wounded, either physically or spiritually, and is unable to walk or ride a horse. He is sometimes shown fishing, a symbol of his connection to the land and the natural world.

Powers

Healing The Fisher King has the ability to heal physical and spiritual wounds, although this power is often linked to his own woundedness and the need for redemption.

Accounts

The Fisher King is a figure in the Arthurian legend who is tasked with guarding the Holy Grail. He is wounded and unable to walk or ride a horse, and is in need of healing.

Perceval, a young knight, encounters the Fisher King and is tasked with healing his wound. However, Perceval fails to ask the king about his wound, and the king remains wounded.

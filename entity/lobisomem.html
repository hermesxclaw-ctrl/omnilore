Who It Is
Form

In human form, the Lobisomem is said to look like a normal person, except for slightly pointed ears and yellowish pale skin. In werewolf form, it has no difference whatsoever from other cultures' descriptions.

Powers

Shape-shifting The Lobisomem has the ability to transform from a human into a werewolf and back again.

Accounts

The story tells of a young man who is bitten by a werewolf and transforms into a Lobisomem. He struggles to control his newfound powers and eventually becomes a hero in his community.

The story tells of a curse that is placed upon a family, causing them to transform into Lobisomens. They struggle to control their powers and eventually become outcasts in their community.

Youm

Pronunciation In Korean, 'Youm' is typically pronounced [jʌm] or [jʌːm], depending on the underlying Hangul. In Wolof, it is pronounced [jum] with a short 'o'.

Etymology

The surname 'Youm' appears in multiple cultural contexts. In Korean, it is a romanization of the surname 염 (Yeom) or 엄 (Eom), depending on the Hanja used. In West Africa, particularly Senegal, 'Youm' is a common surname of Wolof origin. The exact etymological roots in each culture are not fully documented in available sources.

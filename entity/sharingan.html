Who It Is

The Uchiha clan's hereditary dōjutsu, appearing as a red eye with tomoe patterns. It grants the wielder incredible perception abilities, including the power to see chakra, copy techniques, and predict movements. It can evolve into the Mangekyō Sharingan and eventually the Rinnegan under specific conditions.

The Approach

In the world of Naruto, the Sharingan is more than an eye—it is a hereditary bloodline limit that grants its wielder near-clairvoyant perception, the power to copy any jutsu, and, in its most advanced forms, the ability to cast the world into a personal genjutsu. Yet this power comes at a terrible cost: each use of its ultimate techniques darkens the user's heart, and the eye's history is steeped in tragedy, betrayal, and the relentless pursuit of power.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of violence, psychological manipulation, and themes of betrayal and self-harm (eye removal).

Etymology

The word 'Sharingan' (写輪眼) literally means 'Copy Wheel Eye' or 'Mirror Wheel Eye' in Japanese. 'Sha' (写) means 'copy' or 'transcribe', 'rin' (輪) means 'wheel' or 'ring', and 'gan' (眼) means 'eye'. The name reflects its ability to copy techniques and its distinctive tomoe pattern that resembles spinning wheels.

Cultural Ownership

The Sharingan is a fictional concept created by Masashi Kishimoto for the manga and anime series Naruto, first published by Shueisha in 1999. It is part of the Naruto franchise, owned by Shueisha, Studio Pierrot, and associated licensees. It is not derived from any real-world mythology, though it draws inspiration from Japanese folklore and Buddhist concepts of perception and karma.

Disputed Identifications

No real-world disputes exist regarding the Sharingan's identity, as it is a fictional element. Within the Naruto fandom, there is debate over the exact mechanics and limitations of the Sharingan, particularly regarding its evolution into the Mangekyō Sharingan and the Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan, and whether certain abilities are consistent across all users.

Description

Appearance

The Sharingan appears as a red iris with a black pupil and one to three comma-shaped marks (tomoe) arranged around the pupil. When activated, the eye turns from its normal color to red, and the tomoe appear or rotate. The number of tomoe indicates the user's mastery: one tomoe is the initial stage, two tomoe is intermediate, and three tomoe is the mature stage. The Mangekyō Sharingan features a unique pattern for each user, often resembling a pinwheel, a three-bladed shuriken, or other complex shapes. The Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan combines the patterns of two users, typically retaining a similar design but with altered details.

Symbols & Iconography

The Sharingan is often depicted as a symbol of the Uchiha clan's power and tragedy. Its tomoe pattern is iconic and has been used in merchandise, tattoos, and fan art. In the series, the Sharingan is associated with perception, copying, and illusion, and is often shown in close-up shots to emphasize its hypnotic quality.

Behaviour & Personality

The Sharingan does not have a personality; it is a tool. However, its use is tied to the user's emotional state. Activation requires a surge of strong emotion, often stress or danger. The Mangekyō Sharingan requires the user to experience profound loss or trauma to awaken. The eye's abilities can influence the user's mental state, particularly the Mangekyō's genjutsu and Susanoo, which can drain the user's chakra and cause physical deterioration. Prolonged use of the Mangekyō can lead to blindness, and overuse of its techniques can accelerate this process.

Domains & Habitats

The Sharingan is a biological trait of the Uchiha clan, who reside in Konohagakure (the Hidden Leaf Village). It is not tied to a specific habitat but is passed down through bloodlines. The eye's abilities are used in combat, espionage, and genjutsu, making it valuable in any environment where the user operates.

Version Differences

There are several versions of the Sharingan: the base Sharingan, the Mangekyō Sharingan, and the Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan. The base Sharingan grants enhanced perception, copying, and basic genjutsu. The Mangekyō grants unique abilities unique to each user (e.g., Tsukuyomi, Amaterasu, Kamui) and the Susanoo. The Eternal Mangekyō, obtained by transplanting another Mangekyō, mitigates the blindness drawback and enhances the user's abilities. Additionally, there is a 'Sharingan' variant in the anime filler arcs, such as the 'Ketsuryūgan' (though not a Sharingan) and the 'Jōgan', which is a separate dōjutsu. The anime also depicts a 'Sharingan' used by non-Uchiha like Kakashi, who received it as a transplant, and by Danzō, who implanted it in his arm.

Powers & Abilities

Enhanced Perception

Source Version Base Sharingan, all stages

Description

The Sharingan grants the user superhuman visual acuity, allowing them to perceive fast-moving objects, read chakra flow, and see through some illusions. It can also predict an opponent's movements by reading subtle muscle cues.

Limitations

Requires chakra to maintain; prolonged use can cause eye strain and fatigue.

Contradictions

Some sources suggest the Sharingan can see chakra colors and networks, but this is not consistently depicted.

Copying Technique (Jutsu)

Source Version Base Sharingan, three tomoe stage

Description

The Sharingan can copy any jutsu it sees, including ninjutsu, genjutsu, and taijutsu, by memorizing the hand seals and chakra manipulation. The user can then perform the copied technique. This ability earned Kakashi the nickname 'Copy Ninja'.

Limitations

Cannot copy techniques that require a specific bloodline limit (Kekkei Genkai) or that are unique to a particular user (e.g., Shadow Clone is copyable, but Ice Release is not). Copying a technique does not guarantee mastery; the user must understand the chakra mechanics.

Contradictions

There are inconsistencies in what can be copied; some techniques are said to be uncopyable due to their nature, but the exact criteria are not fully explained.

Genjutsu Casting

Source Version Base Sharingan and Mangekyō Sharingan

Description

The Sharingan can cast powerful genjutsu (illusions) that affect the opponent's mind. The user can place the opponent under a visual illusion by making eye contact. The strongest genjutsu, Tsukuyomi, is unique to Itachi's Mangekyō Sharingan.

Limitations

Requires eye contact; can be resisted by strong willpower or by breaking the user's line of sight. Tsukuyomi is extremely draining and can be used only a few times before the user's vision deteriorates.

Contradictions

The mechanics of genjutsu are not always consistent; some opponents break out of genjutsu without explanation.

Mangekyō Sharingan Abilities

Source Version Mangekyō Sharingan

Description

Each Mangekyō Sharingan user gains unique abilities. Known abilities include: Tsukuyomi (a genjutsu that distorts time in the victim's mind), Amaterasu (black flames that never extinguish), Kamui (a space-time ninjutsu that can teleport objects to a pocket dimension), and Susanoo (a giant ethereal warrior that protects the user).

Limitations

Each ability consumes a large amount of chakra and causes damage to the user's eyes, leading to blindness if overused. The Susanoo is extremely taxing and can cause severe physical harm if used excessively.

Contradictions

The abilities are not standardized; each user has a unique set, but the criteria for which abilities appear are not fully explained. Some users, like Sasuke, develop Amaterasu, while others, like Obito, develop Kamui. The mechanics of Susanoo vary between users.

Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan

Source Version Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan

Description

By transplanting a sibling's Mangekyō Sharingan, a user can achieve the Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan, which eliminates the blindness drawback and enhances the user's abilities, including the Susanoo, which can take a more complete form.

Limitations

Requires the sacrifice of a close relative's eyes; the procedure is considered taboo and is a major plot point in the Uchiha clan's history.

Contradictions

The exact mechanism of how the Eternal Mangekyō prevents blindness is not detailed, and it is unclear if the user retains the original abilities or gains new ones.

Susanoo

Source Version Mangekyō Sharingan (all users with Mangekyō can access Susanoo, but it is not automatic; it requires mastery)

Description

A humanoid avatar of chakra that forms around the user, providing immense defense and offensive power. It can be manifested in various stages, from a skeletal form to a fully armored warrior. It can also be used as a vessel for the user's other abilities, such as Amaterasu.

Limitations

Requires immense chakra and causes physical strain; if used for too long, it can cause the user's body to deteriorate. The Susanoo is not invincible and can be damaged by powerful attacks.

Contradictions

In the series, Susanoo is sometimes depicted as a shield that can be used by anyone with Mangekyō, but its power and form vary. It is unclear if Susanoo can be used by non-Uchiha with transplanted Sharingan, as Kakashi uses a variant of Kamui but not Susanoo.

Izanagi and Izanami

Source Version Mangekyō Sharingan (Izanagi and Izanami are techniques that require the Sharingan, but they are not unique to any one user; they are considered forbidden and are used by Danzō and Itachi, respectively)

Description

Izanagi is a forbidden jutsu that alters reality at the cost of the user's eyesight, allowing the user to rewrite their own death or turn an unfavorable outcome into a favorable one. Izanami is a genjutsu that traps the target in an endless loop of events, forcing them to accept their fate.

Limitations

Izanagi consumes the Sharingan that uses it, causing blindness in that eye. Izanami requires the user to set up a loop by recording two specific moments in the target's life and then forcing them to repeat them; it is extremely difficult to use without preparation.

Contradictions

The exact mechanics of Izanagi and Izanami are complex and not fully explained. There are inconsistencies in how they are used and their limitations.

Stories & Myths

The Legend of the Uchiha Clan

Source Naruto manga, chapters 399, 550, and various flashbacksCulture Fictional Japanese-inspired culturePeriod Fictional timeline, centuries before the main story

Summary

The Uchiha clan is one of the founding clans of Konohagakure, descended from Indra Ōtsutsuki, the son of Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki (the Sage of Six Paths). Indra inherited the 'eyes' (the Sharingan), while his brother Asura inherited the 'body' (the Life Force). The Sharingan is thus a manifestation of Indra's chakra and the Uchiha's lineage. The clan's history is marked by constant conflict and the pursuit of power, leading to the internal strife that culminated in the Uchiha massacre.

Consequences

The Sharingan became a symbol of the Uchiha's power and curse. The clan's desire for control and their mistrust by the village led to a planned coup, which Itachi preemptively stopped by slaughtering the entire clan, leaving only Sasuke alive. This event shapes Sasuke's character and the series' plot.

Variants

The anime filler arcs depict additional Uchiha history and Sharingan abilities, but these are not considered canon.

The Tale of Madara Uchiha

Source Naruto manga, chapters 370-372, 386, and 550Culture FictionalPeriod Fictional, during the Warring States period and later

Summary

Madara Uchiha was one of the most powerful Uchiha, who, along with his rival Hashirama Senju, founded Konohagakure. Madara awakened the Mangekyō Sharingan after witnessing his brother's death. He later obtained the Eternal Mangekyō by transplanting his brother's eyes. Madara's ambitions led him to seek the power of the Ten-Tails and to execute the Eye of the Moon Plan, a genjutsu to subjugate the world. He was ultimately defeated by Hashirama at the Valley of the End, but his legacy continued through Obito and the Akatsuki.

Participants

Consequences

Madara's actions set the stage for the Fourth Great Ninja War and the eventual resurrection of Kaguya Ōtsutsuki. His use of the Sharingan and its evolution into the Rinnegan (by combining his Uchiha and Senju powers) is a key plot point.

Variants

The anime expands on Madara's backstory, but the core events are consistent with the manga.

Itachi's Tragedy

Source Naruto manga, chapters 399-402, 403, 404, and 405Culture FictionalPeriod Fictional, during the years before the series start

Summary

Itachi Uchiha, a prodigy of the clan, was ordered by the village leadership to eliminate the Uchiha clan to prevent a coup. He did so, but spared his younger brother Sasuke, hoping Sasuke would one day avenge the clan. Itachi then joined the Akatsuki, a criminal organization, to spy on them. He used his Sharingan to torment Sasuke, but his true motives were to protect the village and to push Sasuke to become stronger. Itachi's Mangekyō abilities, Tsukuyomi and Amaterasu, are central to his character.

Participants

Itachi Uchiha

Consequences

Itachi's actions shaped Sasuke's path of revenge, leading to Sasuke's eventual confrontation with Itachi, Itachi's death, and the revelation of the truth, which caused Sasuke to turn against the village. Itachi's legacy is one of sacrifice and love.

Variants

The anime includes additional scenes and filler arcs that expand on Itachi's story, but the core is canon.

Kakashi's Copy Eye

Source Naruto manga, chapters 7, 16, and variousCulture FictionalPeriod Fictional, during Kakashi's youth and later

Summary

Kakashi Hatake received a Sharingan from his teammate Obito Uchiha, who was presumed dead during the Third Great Ninja War. Obito's Sharingan awakened in his dying moments, and Kakashi transplanted it. Kakashi became known as the 'Copy Ninja' for his ability to copy over a thousand jutsu. However, the Sharingan is a heavy burden for a non-Uchiha, draining his chakra and causing him to keep it covered when not in use. Kakashi's use of Kamui, a Mangekyō ability, was a key factor in many battles.

Participants

Kakashi Hatake

Obito Uchiha

Rin Nohara

Consequences

Kakashi's Sharingan allowed him to become a formidable ninja, but it also tied him to Obito's fate. The eye was eventually lost when Obito took it back, leaving Kakashi without it, but he later received a temporary version during the war.

Variants

The anime and movies depict additional uses of the Sharingan by Kakashi, but the core is consistent.

The Sharingan is the hereditary Kekkei Genkai of the Uchiha clan, passed down through their bloodline. It is a defining trait of the clan and is often associated with their curse of hatred.

Version Note

All Uchiha have the potential to awaken the Sharingan, but not all do. The awakening requires a strong emotional stimulus.

Some Uchiha, like Boruto Uzumaki, do not possess the Sharingan, but they may have other dōjutsu. The exact conditions for awakening are not fully explained.

Uchiha clan massacre

Source Naruto manga, chapters 399-402

Description

The Uchiha clan was slaughtered by Itachi Uchiha on the orders of the village leadership. Itachi killed all members, including children and elderly, to prevent a coup. This event is a central tragedy in the series, driving Sasuke's revenge and Itachi's guilt.

Scholarly Context

The massacre is fictional but draws on themes of political oppression and the cycle of hatred. It is a critique of the shinobi system.

Eye transplantation and self-harm

Source Naruto manga, chapters 386, 550

Description

To achieve the Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan, Uchiha members transplant the eyes of close relatives, often after killing them or after their death. Madara took his brother's eyes, and Sasuke later took Itachi's eyes. This practice is considered taboo and is depicted as a dark ritual.

Scholarly Context

The eye transplant is a metaphor for the Uchiha's obsession with power and the cost of their abilities.

Psychological manipulation and torture

Source Naruto manga, chapter 142

Description

Itachi used Tsukuyomi on Sasuke to force him to relive the massacre for 72 hours in a single instant, causing immense psychological trauma. This act is depicted as cruel but is later revealed to be part of Itachi's plan to make Sasuke stronger.

Scholarly Context

The use of genjutsu as psychological torture highlights the dark side of the ninja world.

Genocide and war crimes

Source Naruto manga, various chapters

Description

The Sharingan is often used in war and conflict, and its users have committed atrocities, including Madara's use of Susanoo to massacre enemies and Obito's involvement in the Fourth Great Ninja War.

Scholarly Context

The series explores the consequences of war and the dehumanization of enemies.

Contradictions & Disputes

Copyability of jutsu

Position A

The Sharingan can copy any jutsu that does not require a Kekkei Genkai.

Position A — evidence

Kakashi copied many jutsu, including the Chidori, which he invented after copying the Rasengan's shape transformation.

Position B

Some jutsu are uncopyable even without a Kekkei Genkai, such as the Rasengan, because they require unique chakra manipulation.

Position B — evidence

In the series, Kakashi notes that the Rasengan is uncopyable because it requires perfect chakra control and shape transformation, which cannot be learned by mere observation.

Scholarly Consensus

The series is inconsistent; some sources say the Sharingan can copy any non-Kekkei Genkai jutsu, while others imply limitations. The general consensus is that the Sharingan copies the hand seals and chakra flow, but the user must still understand the technique to use it effectively.

Awakening conditions

Position A

The Sharingan awakens in response to strong negative emotions, such as stress or danger.

Position A — evidence

Sasuke awakened his Sharingan during a battle against Haku, and Itachi awakened his after witnessing a friend's death.

Position B

The Sharingan can also awaken in response to positive emotions or through training.

Position B — evidence

Some Uchiha, like Sarada, awakened their Sharingan through positive emotions (love for her father).

Scholarly Consensus

The exact conditions are not fully defined, but the series primarily emphasizes negative emotions as the catalyst.

Mangekyō Sharingan abilities

Position A

Each Mangekyō Sharingan user has unique abilities that are determined by their personality and experiences.

Position A — evidence

Itachi's Tsukuyomi reflects his skill with genjutsu, and Sasuke's Amaterasu reflects his destructive nature.

Position B

The abilities are random and not tied to the user's traits.

Position B — evidence

There is no in-universe explanation for why certain abilities appear.

Scholarly Consensus

The series implies a connection, but it is not explicitly stated.

Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan

Position A

The Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan is obtained by transplanting a sibling's Mangekyō.

Position A — evidence

Madara took his brother's eyes, and Sasuke took Itachi's eyes.

Position B

It can also be obtained by transplanting any close relative's eyes.

Position B — evidence

The series does not specify if the donor must be a sibling, but it is implied that it must be a close relative.

Scholarly Consensus

The series only shows sibling transplants, but the exact requirement is not clearly stated.

Susanoo availability

Position A

Susanoo can be used by any Mangekyō Sharingan user who has mastered it.

Position A — evidence

Itachi, Sasuke, Madara, and Obito all use Susanoo.

Position B

Susanoo is a unique ability that only certain users can access.

Position B — evidence

Kakashi, who has a Mangekyō, does not use Susanoo, but he uses Kamui.

Scholarly Consensus

The series suggests that Susanoo is available to all Mangekyō users, but it requires a high level of chakra and skill. Kakashi's lack of Susanoo may be due to his non-Uchiha physiology.

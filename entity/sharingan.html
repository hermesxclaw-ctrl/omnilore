Who It Is

In the world of Naruto, the Sharingan is more than an eye—it is a hereditary bloodline limit that grants its wielder near-clairvoyant perception, the power to copy any jutsu, and, in its most advanced forms, the ability to cast the world into a personal genjutsu. Yet this power comes at a terrible cost: each use of its ultimate techniques darkens the user's heart, and the eye's history is steeped in tragedy, betrayal, and the relentless pursuit of power.

The Sharingan does not have a personality; it is a tool. However, its use is tied to the user's emotional state. Activation requires a surge of strong emotion, often stress or danger. The Mangekyō Sharingan requires the user to experience profound loss or trauma to awaken. The eye's abilities can influence the user's mental state, particularly the Mangekyō's genjutsu and Susanoo, which can drain the user's chakra and cause physical deterioration. Prolonged use of the Mangekyō can lead to blindness, and overuse of its techniques can accelerate this process.

Powers
Enhanced Perception

The Sharingan grants the user superhuman visual acuity, allowing them to perceive fast-moving objects, read chakra flow, and see through some illusions. It can also predict an opponent's movements by reading subtle muscle cues.

Limit: Requires chakra to maintain; prolonged use can cause eye strain and fatigue.

Copying Technique (Jutsu)

The Sharingan can copy any jutsu it sees, including ninjutsu, genjutsu, and taijutsu, by memorizing the hand seals and chakra manipulation. The user can then perform the copied technique. This ability earned Kakashi the nickname 'Copy Ninja'.

Limit: Cannot copy techniques that require a specific bloodline limit (Kekkei Genkai) or that are unique to a particular user (e.g., Shadow Clone is copyable, but Ice Release is not). Copying a technique does not guarantee mastery; the user must understand the chakra mechanics.

Genjutsu Casting

The Sharingan can cast powerful genjutsu (illusions) that affect the opponent's mind. The user can place the opponent under a visual illusion by making eye contact. The strongest genjutsu, Tsukuyomi, is unique to Itachi's Mangekyō Sharingan.

Limit: Requires eye contact; can be resisted by strong willpower or by breaking the user's line of sight. Tsukuyomi is extremely draining and can be used only a few times before the user's vision deteriorates.

Mangekyō Sharingan Abilities

Each Mangekyō Sharingan user gains unique abilities. Known abilities include: Tsukuyomi (a genjutsu that distorts time in the victim's mind), Amaterasu (black flames that never extinguish), Kamui (a space-time ninjutsu that can teleport objects to a pocket dimension), and Susanoo (a giant ethereal warrior that protects the user).

Limit: Each ability consumes a large amount of chakra and causes damage to the user's eyes, leading to blindness if overused. The Susanoo is extremely taxing and can cause severe physical harm if used excessively.

Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan

By transplanting a sibling's Mangekyō Sharingan, a user can achieve the Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan, which eliminates the blindness drawback and enhances the user's abilities, including the Susanoo, which can take a more complete form.

Limit: Requires the sacrifice of a close relative's eyes; the procedure is considered taboo and is a major plot point in the Uchiha clan's history.

Susanoo

A humanoid avatar of chakra that forms around the user, providing immense defense and offensive power. It can be manifested in various stages, from a skeletal form to a fully armored warrior. It can also be used as a vessel for the user's other abilities, such as Amaterasu.

Limit: Requires immense chakra and causes physical strain; if used for too long, it can cause the user's body to deteriorate. The Susanoo is not invincible and can be damaged by powerful attacks.

Izanagi and Izanami

Izanagi is a forbidden jutsu that alters reality at the cost of the user's eyesight, allowing the user to rewrite their own death or turn an unfavorable outcome into a favorable one. Izanami is a genjutsu that traps the target in an endless loop of events, forcing them to accept their fate.

Limit: Izanagi consumes the Sharingan that uses it, causing blindness in that eye. Izanami requires the user to set up a loop by recording two specific moments in the target's life and then forcing them to repeat them; it is extremely difficult to use without preparation.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Sharingan appears as a red iris with a black pupil and one to three comma-shaped marks (tomoe) arranged around the pupil. When activated, the eye turns from its normal color to red, and the tomoe appear or rotate. The number of tomoe indicates the user's mastery: one tomoe is the initial stage, two tomoe is intermediate, and three tomoe is the mature stage. The Mangekyō Sharingan features a unique pattern for each user, often resembling a pin

Symbols — The Sharingan is often depicted as a symbol of the Uchiha clan's power and tragedy. Its tomoe pattern is iconic and has been used in merchandise, tattoos, and fan art. In the series, the Sharingan is associated with perception, copying, and illusion, and is often shown in close-up shots to emphasize its hypnotic quality.

Domains — The Sharingan is a biological trait of the Uchiha clan, who reside in Konohagakure (the Hidden Leaf Village). It is not tied to a specific habitat but is passed down through bloodlines. The eye's abilities are used in combat, espionage, and genjutsu, making it valuable in any environment where the user operates.

Accounts I
The Legend of the Uchiha ClanNaruto manga, chapters 399, 550, and various flashbacks

Fictional timeline, centuries before the main story

The Uchiha clan is one of the founding clans of Konohagakure, descended from Indra Ōtsutsuki, the son of Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki (the Sage of Six Paths). Indra inherited the 'eyes' (the Sharingan), while his brother Asura inherited the 'body' (the Life Force). The Sharingan is thus a manifestation of Indra's chakra and the Uchiha's lineage. The clan's history is marked by constant conflict and the pursuit of power, leading to the internal strife that culminated in the Uchiha massacre. The Sharingan became a symbol of the Uchiha's power and curse. The clan's desire for control and their mistrust by the village led to a planned coup, which Itachi preemptively stopped by slaughtering the entire clan, leaving only Sasuke alive. This event shapes Sasuke's character and the series' plot.

The Tale of Madara UchihaNaruto manga, chapters 370-372, 386, and 550

Fictional, during the Warring States period and later

Madara Uchiha was one of the most powerful Uchiha, who, along with his rival Hashirama Senju, founded Konohagakure. Madara awakened the Mangekyō Sharingan after witnessing his brother's death. He later obtained the Eternal Mangekyō by transplanting his brother's eyes. Madara's ambitions led him to seek the power of the Ten-Tails and to execute the Eye of the Moon Plan, a genjutsu to subjugate the world. He was ultimately defeated by Hashirama at the Valley of the End, but his legacy continued through Obito and the Akatsuki. Madara's actions set the stage for the Fourth Great Ninja War and the eventual resurrection of Kaguya Ōtsutsuki. His use of the Sharingan and its evolution into the Rinnegan (by combining his Uchiha and Senju powers) is a key plot point.

Accounts II
Itachi's TragedyNaruto manga, chapters 399-402, 403, 404, and 405

Fictional, during the years before the series start

Itachi Uchiha, a prodigy of the clan, was ordered by the village leadership to eliminate the Uchiha clan to prevent a coup. He did so, but spared his younger brother Sasuke, hoping Sasuke would one day avenge the clan. Itachi then joined the Akatsuki, a criminal organization, to spy on them. He used his Sharingan to torment Sasuke, but his true motives were to protect the village and to push Sasuke to become stronger. Itachi's Mangekyō abilities, Tsukuyomi and Amaterasu, are central to his character. Itachi's actions shaped Sasuke's path of revenge, leading to Sasuke's eventual confrontation with Itachi, Itachi's death, and the revelation of the truth, which caused Sasuke to turn against the village. Itachi's legacy is one of sacrifice and love.

Kakashi's Copy EyeNaruto manga, chapters 7, 16, and various

Fictional, during Kakashi's youth and later

Kakashi Hatake received a Sharingan from his teammate Obito Uchiha, who was presumed dead during the Third Great Ninja War. Obito's Sharingan awakened in his dying moments, and Kakashi transplanted it. Kakashi became known as the 'Copy Ninja' for his ability to copy over a thousand jutsu. However, the Sharingan is a heavy burden for a non-Uchiha, draining his chakra and causing him to keep it covered when not in use. Kakashi's use of Kamui, a Mangekyō ability, was a key factor in many battles. Kakashi's Sharingan allowed him to become a formidable ninja, but it also tied him to Obito's fate. The eye was eventually lost when Obito took it back, leaving Kakashi without it, but he later received a temporary version during the war.

The Sharingan can copy any jutsu, including Kekkei Genkai. — The Sharingan cannot copy Kekkei Genkai, as they are hereditary and cannot be reproduced by normal chakra manipulation.

The Sharingan grants the user the ability to see the future. — The Sharingan allows the user to predict an opponent's movements by reading their muscle cues, but it does not grant future sight.

The Sharingan is the strongest dōjutsu. — The Sharingan is powerful, but the Rinnegan and Byakugan have their own strengths. The Rinnegan is generally considered superior in terms of raw power, and the Byakugan has unique abilities like X-ray vision.

The Sharingan can be activated at will by any Uchiha. — The Sharingan requires a specific emotional trigger to awaken, and not all Uchiha can awaken it. It is a rare ability within the clan.

The Mangekyō Sharingan can be used indefinitely without consequence. — The Mangekyō Sharingan causes gradual blindness with each use, and overuse can lead to permanent blindness. The Eternal Mangekyō mitigates this, but it is not a permanent solution.

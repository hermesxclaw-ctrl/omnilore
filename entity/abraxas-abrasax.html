Who It Is
Form

Abraxas is often depicted as a rooster-headed warrior with serpent legs, symbolizing his power and strength.

Powers

Protection Abraxas is said to offer protection and blessings to those who invoke his name, and is often used in magical and esoteric contexts for this purpose.

Accounts

Abraxas is said to have originated in the ancient Gnostic Christian tradition, where he was revered as a powerful and enigmatic figure.

Abraxas is said to have been used in magical and esoteric contexts in ancient Greco-Roman traditions, where he was invoked for protection and blessings.

Who It Is

In a class of 31 middle-school girls where one can stop time, another commands wind spirits, and one is a 700-year-old vampire, Fumika Narutaki's superpower is being unmistakably, relentlessly ten years old — and using it to get away with everything. With her identical twin Fuka, she is Mahora Academy Class 3-A's pint-sized chaos engine: twin-tails, twin mischief, and the only students who can make Negi Springfield, the boy-teacher from Wales, look like the responsible adult in the room.

Canonically mischievous, energetic, and attention-seeking. Fumika and Fuka function as a manzai duo: Fuka as boke (silly instigator) and Fumika as tsukkomi (dry retort), though both initiate pranks. They are shown skipping class, setting traps for Negi and classmates (pitfalls, love potions, haunted library tricks), and eavesdropping. Despite pranks, they are loyal to Negi and classmates; during crises (Evangeline's resort, Kyoto, Magic World) they show fear but also courage, clinging to older students like Asuna or Konoka. Powers
Twin Coordination & Infiltration (Non-magical)

No innate magic. Ability is mundane: using identical appearance with Fuka to confuse teachers, swap places, run twin-based scams (double attendance, alibi tricks), and execute coordinated pranks. Shown to successfully infiltrate faculty room and library restricted areas by exploiting being underestimated as children.

Limit: Fails when teachers (Takahata, Negi) learn to tell them apart by ribbon or when Evangeline uses magic detection; no combat utility against mages.

Prank Engineering & Trap Setting

Proficient at low-tech traps: pitfall with leaves, itching powder, stink bombs, haunted library string tricks, love-potion cookies (with help from older students). Not magical engineering but creative mischief. In Mahora Festival arc, helps build haunted house attractions.

Limit: Traps are comedic and easily defeated by magically aware characters; often backfire on the twins themselves.

Magic World Survival (Assisted)

During Mundus Magicus arc (Vol. 18-25), survives transport to Magic World alongside non-Pactio classmates via Negi's group protection spells and later via Ala Alba's support. No personal spellcasting; relies on borrowed cloaks, potions, and protection from Pactio partners like Asuna and Setsuna.

Limit: Completely dependent on others for magical protection; cannot cast spells, fly, or use artifacts. Vulnerable to magical beasts and requires evacuation.

No Pactio / No Artifact (Negative Ability)

Unlike 20+ classmates who form Pactio (probationary contracts) with Negi and gain artifacts, Fumika never forms a Pactio in any canonical timeline. Explicitly listed among the non-Pactio students in official Pactio card lists. This is a defining absence: she remains a 'normal' human baseline in a magical class.

Limit: Cannot summon artifact, cannot share telepathy or power boost with Negi. In fanon, sometimes incorrectly assigned a frog-themed artifact; no such artifact exists.

Adaptation-Specific Game Abilities (Non-canonical)

In PS2 game Negima! Dream Tactic Yumemiru Otome Princess (2007) and DS game Negima!? Chō Mahora Taisen Kattoi! (2007), Fumika is playable with temporary magic attacks (frog summon, double-team dash) for balance. These are gameplay mechanics only.

Limit: Not considered canon; no manga/anime counterpart; contradicted by primary evidence of non-magical status.

Accounts I
Class 3-A Introduction — The 31 GirlsNegima! Magister Negi Magi Vol. 1 Ch. 1-3

2003 (in-universe: 2003 school year, Mahora Academy)

Ten-year-old Welsh mage Negi Springfield arrives as homeroom teacher for Class 2-A (later 3-A) of 31 girls. Fumika and Fuka Narutaki are introduced in the class roster as Student Nos. 24 and 25, the youngest-looking twins who immediately test Negi with a classic twin-switch prank — Fumika pretends to be Fuka to get double snacks and confuse attendance. Negi, naive to Japanese school customs, fails to tell them apart, establishing their running gag. The chapter establishes the class's diversity and Negi's challenge managing both magical threats and mundane middle-school chaos. Establishes Fumika as part of the 'mischief' subgroup; sets up recurring twin-confusion comedy that persists for 38 volumes. No magical consequences.

Library Island and the Prank WarNegima! Vol. 2 Ch. 14-18, Vol. 3 Ch. 19-22

2003-2004 publication; in-universe: first semester

Class 3-A visits the vast Mahora Library Island, a magical labyrinth. Fumika and Fuka, eager to explore forbidden areas, set up string traps and fake ghost noises to scare classmates, inadvertently triggering a real magical trap that seals a group including Negi, Asuna, and Konoka in the lower levels. While older students like Evangeline and Chachamaru deal with the magical threat, the twins are shown cowering then bravely running to fetch help from Takahata, demonstrating their loyalty beneath pranks. Their trap is later revealed to have covered a real magical seal, which Negi must dispel. First demonstration that twins' pranks can intersect with real magic; Negi learns to take even childish mischief seriously in a magical environment. No Pactio formed.

Kyoto Field Trip — The Kansai Magic AssociationNegima! Vol. 6 Ch. 48 - Vol. 10 Ch. 85

2004-2005 publication; in-universe: summer trip to Kyoto

Class 3-A travels to Kyoto for a school trip that becomes a confrontation with the Kansai Magic Association and demons targeting Konoka. Fumika and Fuka are part of the non-combatant group left at the inn, where they attempt to spy on older girls' baths and set up a 'test of courage' that accidentally leads them into a barrier where they encounter a low-level yōkai. They are rescued by Kaede Nagase and Kotaro Inugami. Their subplot provides comic relief while main battle (Negi vs. Fate's predecessor, Evangeline's intervention) unfolds. The twins' innocent perspective highlights the danger of the magical world to ordinary students. Reinforces that non-magical students are liabilities in magical combat and need protection; contributes to Negi's later decision to train classmates and form Pactios selectively. Twins remain non-Pactio.

Accounts II
Mahora Festival — The 3-A Play and Haunted HouseNegima! Vol. 11 Ch. 90 - Vol. 18 Ch. 168 (Mahora Festival arc)

2005-2007 publication; in-universe: autumn cultural festival

The three-day Mahora Festival is the manga's longest arc, with Class 3-A running multiple attractions. Fumika and Fuka co-manage the haunted house/class play support crew, using their prank expertise to build jump scares, while also participating in the class's attempt to earn money to cover Negi's debts. During Chao Lingshen's time-travel plot to reveal magic to the world, the twins are among the students evacuated via Negi's allies. In one notable scene (Ch. 132), Fumika is shown crying when she thinks Negi will be expelled, revealing genuine attachment to him despite constant teasing. Their haunted house is later destroyed in the battle between Negi and Chao's robots, and they help with cleanup. Largest showcase of twins' non-magical contribution to class activities; emotional beat deepens their bond with Negi. Festival's failure/success leads directly to Magic World arc planning.

Journey to Mundus Magicus — The Magic WorldNegima! Vol. 18 Ch. 169 - Vol. 25 Ch. 235

2007-2009 publication; in-universe: spring break in Magic World

Negi and Ala Alba (his Pactio partners) plus stowaway classmates travel through a gate to the Magic World to find Negi's father Nagi. Fumika and Fuka are among the stowaways who hide in luggage to follow Negi, despite being told it's too dangerous. Once in Mundus Magicus, they are stranded in the desert region near Ostia with other non-combatants, surviving on rations and protection spells cast by Asuna and Setsuna. They witness the ruined city of Ostia and the war against Fate Averruncus's forces. A key moment (Ch. 210) shows Fumika sharing her water with a wounded magical creature, echoing Negi's compassion. They are eventually returned to Earth via the gate's closure, with no magical powers gained. Demonstrates the risk non-magical students take for friendship; after return, Negi and school administration tighten gate security. Twins' survival without Pactio underscores theme that courage isn't limited to mages. No lasting power-up.

Final Arc — The Mage of the Beginning and GraduationNegima! Vol. 32 Ch. 290 - Vol. 38 Ch. 355; epilogue Vol. 38 Ch. 356-358

2010-2012 publication; in-universe: 2004 graduation

In the final battle against the Mage of the Beginning (possessing Negi's father), all of Class 3-A, including Fumika and Fuka, are gathered via the power of Asuna's world-creating ability to support Negi. The twins, still non-magical, are shown in the collective mindscape holding hands with classmates, lending emotional strength rather than magical power. In the graduation epilogue (seven years later, 2011 in-universe), adult Fumika appears briefly in a reunion panel alongside Fuka, both now high-school aged, still together, indicating they remained close after middle school. No individual epilogue chapter is dedicated to them, unlike Pactio members who get future career reveals. Concludes twins' arc as loyal supporting classmates; their lack of magical destiny contrasts with Pactio members who become mages, emphasizing Akamatsu's theme that not everyone needs to be special to belong. Sets up cameo in UQ Holder! future.

Fumika and Fuka are a single character or interchangeable — They are distinct individuals with separate student numbers, slightly different personalities (Fumika more tsukkomi), and separate Wikidata entries. Manga and fanbook treat them as two people who choose to act as a pair.

Fumika is a mage or has a Pactio artifact like Asuna or Konoka — She is one of ~11 classmates who never form a Pactio and remains non-magical throughout. Her role is mundane support and comic relief. No artifact exists in canon.

Fumika is from folklore or a mythological figure — Purely modern fictional creation by Ken Akamatsu in 2003. Name uses real Japanese surname but character has no folkloric precedent.

Who It Is

On Mt. Colubo, above the stench and smoke of Grey Terminal, a foul-mouthed mountain bandit with a perm and a club became the only mother three of the world's most infamous brothers ever knew — not because she wanted to, but because Monkey D. Garp left her no choice, and then she loved them anyway.

Attested personality is aggressively gruff, foul-mouthed, short-tempered, and openly resentful of Garp's coercion. She routinely shouts, threatens, and physically hits Ace and Luffy with her club or fists when they misbehave, steal food, or endanger themselves — behavior presented with slapstick tone in manga but clearly corporal punishment. Underneath, she is deeply maternal, anxious, and self-sacrificing. Powers
Mountain Bandit Leadership and Survival

As boss of the Dadan Family, she commands a dozen-plus mountain bandits (including Dogra and Magra) who obey her without question. She sustains her group through banditry, hunting, foraging, and theft in the Mt. Colubo wilderness, evading Goa Kingdom soldiers and maintaining a hidden base for over a decade.

Limit: No formal military training; her gang is weak by Grand Line standards and fled from Bluejam Pirates and Goa nobility. Authority derives from personality and seniority, not strength ranking.

Enhanced Physical Strength (Non-Superhuman)

Despite being an ordinary human with no Devil Fruit or Haki, Dadan displays above-average strength for an East Blue civilian: she can knock out young Ace and Luffy with a single club strike, carry both boys on her back while running through a forest fire, and intimidate adult male bandits. She also survived a beating from Garp (implied) and physical strain of the Grey Terminal escape.

Limit: Not comparable to Marines, pirates with bounties, or Haki users. She is terrified of Garp and was powerless against Bluejam and his crew without outside help. No feats against named fighters.

Foster Motherhood and Child-Rearing (Social Ability)

Her primary 'ability' in narrative terms is raising three extremely difficult, superhumanly strong and reckless children (Ace with Conqueror's Haki as a baby, Luffy with rubber body, Sabo with noble combat training) from infancy/early childhood to age 10-17, keeping them alive despite their constant attempts to kill each other, run into the forest, and fight wild animals and pirates.

Limit: She frequently complains she cannot control them and relies on them wearing each other out. She failed to prevent their dangerous oath of brotherhood (ASL sake cup) and their later departures to sea.

No Devil Fruit, No Haki, No Weapon Mastery

Explicitly a non-combatant in One Piece power system. She has not eaten a Devil Fruit, shows no Busoshoku or Kenbunshoku Haki, and her club is a crude disciplinary tool, not a graded weapon (no Meito status).

Limit: Cannot compete in New World power scales; narrative role is domestic/emotional, not martial.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Curly Dadan is a large, heavyset middle-aged woman, officially listed at 221 cm (7'3") tall, making her taller than most East Blue humans. Her most defining trait is her voluminous, permed, orange-copper curly hair that gives her the epithet 'Curly.' She has freckles across her cheeks and nose, a broad face, thick lips, and prominent eyelashes. Her attire is that of a mountain bandit rather than a pirate: typically a loose pinkish-orange button-u

Accounts I
Garp Forces Roger's Son on DadanOne Piece manga Chapter 552, anime Episode 483

Canon timeline: ~20 years before present (approx. 1 year after Gol D. Roger's execution, 20 years before Luffy's departure); published 2009

Shortly after the execution of Pirate King Gol D. Roger, Vice Admiral Monkey D. Garp visits Mt. Colubo and forces Curly Dadan to take in an infant — Portgas D. Ace, the son of Roger and Portgas D. Rouge. Dadan is horrified, understanding that harboring Roger's bloodline could mean death if the World Government discovers it. Garp intimidates her with his fist and leaves her no choice, promising to check on the child. Dadan, Dogra and Magra reluctantly raise Ace in secret, hiding his lineage even from Ace himself for years. Establishes Dadan as Ace's foster mother and the central secret-keeper of his heritage. Explains why Ace grows up as a mountain bandit child rather than in Marine custody. Sets pattern of Garp using Dadan as dumping ground for D.-clan children.

Raising Ace and Luffy — The Demon Children of Mt. ColuboOne Piece manga Chapters 440, 552, 583-584

Canon timeline: Ace age 3-10, Luffy age 0-7; published 2006-2010

After raising Ace alone for six years, Dadan is again coerced by Garp to take in his grandson Monkey D. Luffy (age 6). She now has two feral, super-strong boys who hate each other and constantly try to kill one another and escape into the forest to prove their strength. Dadan responds with shouting, clubbing, and tying them up, but also feeds and shelters them. She despairs that they will never become decent bandits. The hut becomes a chaotic household where Ace and Luffy learn survival, hunting, and fighting by brawling with each other and wild beasts. Forms the core of Luffy and Ace's brotherhood; Dadan's harsh discipline paradoxically forges their resilience. Establishes her as Luffy's first maternal figure after his mother is absent (never depicted).

The ASL Brotherhood and SaboOne Piece manga Chapters 584-588

Canon timeline: Ace age 10, Sabo age 10, Luffy age 7; published 2010

Ace and Luffy befriend Sabo, the runaway noble boy living in Grey Terminal. The three become sworn brothers with a sake cup oath (ASL). Dadan is initially unaware of Sabo, then furious when she learns another mouth to feed has attached himself to her wards. She allows Sabo to stay intermittently at the hut, though Sabo primarily lives on his own in Grey Terminal. Dadan witnesses their bond grow and worries about Sabo's noble family trying to reclaim him. When Sabo is apparently killed by a Celestial Dragon's ship (World Noble Jalmack), Dadan is left to comfort Ace and Luffy, who are devastated. Solidifies Dadan's role as mother to all three ASL brothers. Sabo's presumed death is a major trauma she helps the boys survive. Later revelation that Sabo survived and joined the Revolutionary Army recontextualizes her grief.

Accounts II
The Grey Terminal Fire — Dadan Risks EverythingOne Piece manga Chapters 587-590, anime Episodes 488-490

Canon timeline: Luffy age 7; published 2010

To prepare for the visit of Celestial Dragons, the Goa Kingdom nobility and King Stelly's father conspire with pirate captain Bluejam to burn Grey Terminal and exterminate its inhabitants. Bluejam betrays and traps Ace and Luffy in the burning dump. Dadan, who had been ordered by Bluejam to stay away, defies him and leads the Dadan Family into the inferno to rescue the boys. She finds Ace and Luffy surrounded by Bluejam's crew, fights through pirates despite being outmatched, and carries both boys on her back through flames and smoke while Dogra and Magra hold off pursuers. She is severely injured and exhausted but refuses to abandon them, screaming that they are her sons despite all her complaints. Definitive proof of Dadan's maternal love; transforms her from reluctant babysitter to true mother in narrative terms. Ace and Luffy finally call her with affection afterward. The Goa Kingdom's cruelty and class violence is exposed.

Farewells and Marineford GriefOne Piece manga Chapters 590-592, 501, 590 flashback; anime Episodes 490-505

Canon timeline: Ace age 17 departure, Luffy age 14-17; published 2010 (flashback), 2008 (present reaction)

Ace leaves Mt. Colubo at 17 to become a pirate, promising to make Dadan proud and to live without regrets. Three years later Luffy follows at 17 after training with Ace and Sabo's memory. Dadan stays on Mt. Colubo, anxiously following their bounties in newspapers. When Ace is captured and executed at Marineford (Ch. 574-580), Dadan watches the broadcast on Dawn Island. She collapses, wailing and beating the ground, blaming herself for not raising him better and cursing his decision to become a pirate, while Dogra and Magra try to restrain her. Garp later returns to Dawn Island and is confronted by Dadan, who punches and berates him for letting Ace die, despite Garp's own grief. Emotional climax of Dadan's arc; shows cost of raising children who choose dangerous freedom. Her confrontation with Garp mirrors Garp's own failure to protect Ace. Sets up her later pride in Luffy's survival.

Post-Timeskip: Watching Luffy Become an EmperorOne Piece manga Chapters 820-824, 1058; anime Episodes 878, 1089

Canon timeline: 2 years after Marineford, through Wano aftermath; published 2016-2022

Two years later, Dadan and the mountain bandits follow Luffy's return via newspapers. When Luffy's new bounty after Dressrosa (500 million) and later after Whole Cake Island and Wano (1.5 billion, then 3 billion as Yonko) are published, Dadan reacts with a mix of pride, worry, and exasperation, shouting that he is still a reckless idiot. She learns Sabo is alive as Chief of Staff of the Revolutionary Army (Ch. 823) and is stunned. By Ch. 1058, Luffy is recognized as one of the Four Emperors; Dadan's family celebrates and worries in equal measure, still living in the same hut on Mt. Colubo. Confirms Dadan's ongoing narrative presence as Dawn Island's anchor and Luffy's enduring family tie. She remains a civilian lens on world-shaking events.

Curly Dadan is Luffy's or Ace's real mother. — She is their foster mother/babysitter coerced by Garp. Ace's biological mother is Portgas D. Rouge, who died after holding Ace for 20 months to hide him; Luffy's biological mother has never been revealed and is not Dadan. Dadan has no blood relation to either.

Dadan is a powerful pirate or Haki user who could fight in the New World. — She is an ordinary East Blue mountain bandit with no Devil Fruit, no Haki, and no notable combat feats beyond beating children and surviving a forest fire. She is explicitly powerless against Grand Line threats and fled from Bluejam without help.

Dadan willingly volunteered to raise Ace and Luffy for money or loyalty. — Manga Ch. 552 shows she was terrified and begged Garp not to leave Roger's son with her, fearing execution by the World Government. She obeyed only because Garp intimidated her with physical force. Her later love was reluctant and grew over time.

Dadan abandoned or abused the boys and didn't love them. — While her discipline involved corporal punishment played for comedy, canon shows extreme self-sacrifice: she risked death to rescue them from Grey Terminal fire, carried them through flames, and was devastated by Ace's death, blaming herself. Oda portrays her as genuinely loving despite rough exterior.

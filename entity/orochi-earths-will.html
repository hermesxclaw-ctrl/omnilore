Who It Is

In the blood-soaked soil of a world torn by war, there stirs a will older than memory—Orochi, the Earth's Will, a serpent of vengeance coiled at the heart of conflict. It is not merely a monster but a force, a response of the planet itself to humanity's endless violence, rising to judge and consume.

Orochi is calm, serene, and speaks in a measured tone, even when threatening destruction. He views humanity as a plague upon the Earth and seeks to cleanse it. He is not overtly malicious but acts with cold, cosmic logic.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Orochi is a Japanese term for a serpent or dragon, most famously associated with the eight-headed Yamata no Orochi of Japanese mythology. The epithet 'Earth's Will' suggests a cosmic or planetary consciousness.

Pronunciation — OH-roh-chee

Aliases — Earth's Will

Ownership — Japanese; derived from the fighting game franchise The King of Fighters (SNK).

Powers
Elemental Control

Orochi can manipulate fire, lightning, and other natural elements, using them in combat and to cause widespread destruction.

Limit: His powers are sealed by the 'Orochi seal' and can only be fully unleashed when the seal is broken.

Reincarnation

Orochi can reincarnate or possess a human host, often choosing a descendant of the 'Three Sacred Treasures' clan.

Limit: Requires a suitable host and a ritual; the host's will can resist.

Life Drain

Orochi can absorb life force from living beings to heal himself or enhance his power.

Limit: Not explicitly shown in all appearances.

Immortality

As a manifestation of the Earth's will, Orochi cannot be permanently killed; he is only sealed.

Limit: He can be sealed by the Three Sacred Treasures technique.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Orochi is typically depicted as a tall, muscular humanoid with long, flowing green hair and red eyes. In his true form, he is a giant serpent with multiple heads, reminiscent of the mythical Yamata no Orochi. His humanoid form is an avatar for the Earth's will.

Symbols — The eight-headed serpent is a key symbol, representing the eightfold nature of the Earth's will. The color red is associated with his power and the flames of destruction. The 'Orochi seal' is a recurring motif, used to contain his power.

Domains — Orochi is not confined to a physical realm; he exists as a spiritual entity within the Earth itself. He can manifest anywhere, often appearing at sites of great conflict or sacred grounds.

Accounts I
The Orochi Arc (KOF '95 to '97)The King of Fighters games

1995-1997

A cult known as the Crows of Orochi seeks to revive Orochi by gathering powerful fighters. The protagonists, including Kyo Kusanagi, Iori Yagami, and Chizuru Kagura, who are descendants of the Three Sacred Treasures, must stop them. In KOF '97, Orochi is finally revived and battles the heroes, but is sealed again. Orochi is sealed, but the conflict leaves lasting scars on the characters, particularly Iori, who is cursed by his bloodline.

Who It Is

Huitzilopochtli is a fascinating and complex deity, who plays a central role in Aztec mythology and culture. His association with the sun, the sky, and the hummingbird makes him a unique and intriguing figure, who continues to capture the imagination of scholars and enthusiasts alike.

Huitzilopochtli is often depicted as a fierce and powerful warrior, who is feared and respected by his enemies and allies alike. He is also associated with the sun and the light, which symbolizes his role as a bringer of life and warmth. However, his personality is complex, and he is also known for his cunning and strategic thinking, which he uses to outmaneuver his opponents and guide the Aztecs to victory.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Huitzilopochtli is often depicted as a blue or green hummingbird, which symbolizes his connection to the sun and the sky. In some representations, he is shown with a helmet or a headdress adorned with feathers, which signifies his role as a warrior and a leader. Symbols — The hummingbird is a prominent symbol associated with Huitzilopochtli, representing his connection to the sun, war, and sacrifice. The eagle is also a significant symbol, as it is said to have carried Huitzilopochtli's heart to the heavens, where it became the sun. The cactus, particularly the prickly pear cactus, is another important symbol, as it is associated with Huitzilopochtli's birth and his role as a guide for the Aztecs.

Domains — Huitzilopochtli is associated with the sun, the sky, and the south, which are considered his domains. He is also connected to the natural world, particularly the desert and the cactus, which are symbols of his birth and his role as a guide. His habitats are often depicted as a mixture of desert and mountainous landscapes, reflecting his connection to the harsh yet beautiful environments of Mesoamerica.

Pre-Columbian

According to Aztec mythology, Huitzilopochtli guided the Aztecs during their migration from Aztlan to Tenochtitlán, which would become the capital of the Aztec empire. During this journey, Huitzilopochtli appeared to the Aztecs in various forms, including as a hummingbird and a warrior, and provided them with guidance and protection. The successful migration of the Aztecs and the establishment of Tenochtitlán as their capital city.

Who It Is

Amanikable is the Tagalog god of the sea — a tempestuous deity embodying the ocean's dual nature as provider and destroyer. When angered, he sends storms and sinks boats. Fishermen offered him their first catch and refrained from speaking his name at sea. His fickle nature reflects maritime life in typhoon-prone Philippines.

Attested in Oral tradition / Boxer Codex (16th century)Era Pre-colonial Philippines

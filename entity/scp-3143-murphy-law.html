Who It Is

A sentient narrative construct that manifests as a 1940s film-noir-style private detective. When SCP-3143 becomes involved in any ongoing situation, reality bends to accommodate its 'hardboiled detective' narrative framework — Foundation personnel begin speaking in noir monologues, containment procedures are described in purple prose, and the SCP-3143 file itself reads like a Raymond Chandler novel. SCP-3143 believes it is solving a case and has the power to 'write' the ending of any scenario it enters.

Form

A man in a rumpled trench coat and fedora, perpetually standing in a shadow that doesn't match the ambient lighting. He narrates his own actions in third-person past tense and refers to the Foundation as 'the Feds.' Cigarette smoke curls around him from an unseen source. His face is always partially in shadow.

Who It Is

In Ice King's cramped, fan-fiction-scented study, the Vampire Queen gets rewritten with bitten-black hair, a tight red-and-black checked shirt, and a bass guitar slung low — Marshall Lee, the Vampire King who would rather heckle Fionna than bite her, and whose every smirk reminds you that in the Land of Aaa, even a monster is just someone else's self-insert.

Attested: Charismatic, aloof, teasing, and performatively cruel — introduced bullying Fionna and Cake for amusement, then showing protective or flirtatious interest, especially toward Prince Gumball/Gary. He is laconic, deadpan, and musically expressive; his song in 'Bad Little Boy' reveals vulnerability and a desire to be understood despite pushing others away ('I'm just your problem'). Name & Tongue

Etymology — Given name 'Marshall' is the masculine counterpart to 'Marceline,' created by Natasha Allegri for the gender-swapped universe. 'Marshall' derives from Old Frankish *marahskalk* 'horse servant,' later English occupational title. Surname 'Lee' is monosyllabic and stylistically parallel to Marceline's

Pronunciation — /ˈmɑːrʃəl liː/ (MAR-shul LEE)

Aliases — Marshall Lee the Vampire King, The Vampire King, Marshall Abadeer (widely used fanon surname, never spoken on-screen), Marceline's gender-swapped counterpart

Ownership — Original character created by Natasha Allegri (character designer/storyboard artist) and Pendleton Ward for Cartoon Network Studios. Owned and distributed by Warner Bros. Discovery

Powers
Vampirism - Soul Absorption via Music

Like Marceline, Marshall Lee absorbs souls/the 'red' essence through music rather than blood. Playing his bass causes reddish soul orbs to float from victims toward him. Feeding restores vitality and powers.

Limit: Requires musical performance; does not feed passively. Victims appear dazed but not killed on-screen; extent of harm is ambiguous. Sunlight vulnerability is implied by vampire lore but never tested on-screen for Marshall Lee specifically.

Levitation / Flight

Floats and flies without wings in humanoid form; hovers while playing bass, carries others while flying.

Limit: No stated altitude or speed limit; appears effortless but not used for long-distance travel on-screen.

Shapeshifting - Bat/Monster Form

Transforms into a large, black, multi-eyed bat-monster with elongated limbs and wings for intimidation or combat. Reverts at will.

Limit: Transformation appears voluntary; trigger is emotional stress or feeding. No other animal forms (wolf, etc.) attested as for Marceline.

Superhuman Strength and Durability

Physically overpowers Fionna, lifts Prince Gumball with one arm while flying, survives falls and magical attacks without injury.

Limit: Not quantified; clearly below cosmic entities like Prismo or the Lich. Vulnerable to magic and physical restraint by stronger foes.

Musical Enchantment / Emotional Influence

His performance of 'I'm Just Your Problem' (Marshall Lee version) exerts emotional influence, causing Prince Gumball to become infatuated and distressed; music functions as both weapon and confession.

Limit: Effect appears limited to those already emotionally susceptible; not mind control in the strict sense. Requires listener to hear performance.

Immortality / Agelessness

As a vampire, he does not age visibly between 2011 and 2023 appearances in teen form; adult variant in 2023 series suggests aging is possible across universes but extremely slow.

Limit: Not tested against sun, stake, or other traditional vampire weaknesses on-screen; assumed but unattested.

Accounts I
Fionna and Cake (Ice King's Fanfiction Debut)Adventure Time Season 3 Episode 9 'Fionna and Cake' (Cartoon Network, 2011)

2011 (production); narrative set in Land of Aaa (gender-swapped Ooo)

Ice King reads his self-insert fanfiction to Finn and Jake, introducing Fionna the Human and Cake the Cat. Marshall Lee crashes Fionna's adventure party, mocking her and kidnapping Prince Gumball. Fionna confronts him in his cave lair where he transforms into his bat-monster form. She defeats him not by staking but by outwitting him and rescuing the Prince, establishing their antagonistic-flirtatious dynamic. The story ends with Ice King being told his fiction is derivative, framing Marshall Lee as an authorial projection rather than an autonomous being. Establishes Marshall Lee as Marceline's counterpart and Fionna/Cake universe anchor; introduces his design, powers, and voice (Donald Glover); sets meta-textual precedent that all early Marshall Lee stories are Ice King-authored.

Bad Little BoyAdventure Time Season 5 Episode 11 'Bad Little Boy' (Cartoon Network, 2013)

2013

Marceline, Finn and Jake visit Ice King, who forces them to listen to his latest Fionna and Cake story. In the story, Marshall Lee is invited to Prince Gumball's ball but behaves cruelly, stealing and mocking. Prince Gumball, infatuated, pursues him. Marshall Lee performs a gender-swapped rendition of Marceline's song 'I'm Just Your Problem,' confessing that he pushes people away because he expects rejection. The performance emotionally devastates Prince Gumball, who declares love; Marshall Lee responds with ambiguous, teasing affection. Marceline interrupts the story to criticize Ice King's writing, breaking the fourth wall. Deepens Marshall Lee's interiority beyond bully archetype; provides his only solo musical number; cements Marshall Lee x Prince Gumball (later Gary Prince) as central ship; demonstrates Ice King's unreliable narration.

The Prince Who Wanted EverythingAdventure Time Season 6 Episode 9 'The Prince Who Wanted Everything' (Cartoon Network, 2014)

2014

Lumpy Space Prince (LSP's gender-swapped counterpart) narrates his own Fionna and Cake story while Ice King is incapacitated. Marshall Lee appears as a secondary antagonist who has enchanted Prince Gumball and competes with Lumpy Space Prince for attention. Marshall Lee uses flight and intimidation to assert dominance, but is ultimately upstaged by Lumpy Space Prince's melodrama. The episode de-centers Marshall Lee, showing how different authors reshape him. Shows Marshall Lee's malleability as a character owned by whoever tells the story; reduces his agency, reinforcing meta-theme of authorship; last major TV appearance before 2023 revival.

Accounts II
Adventure Time with Fionna & Cake #1-6: The Lich and the PrinceBOOM! Studios KaBOOM! limited series, written/illustrated by Natasha Allegri (2013)

2013

Fionna and Cake are pulled into a real adventure when the Lich attacks the Land of Aaa. Marshall Lee initially appears aloof, refusing to help, but is drawn in when Prince Gumball (here called Gary Prince in later canon) is endangered. Issues #3-4 focus on Marshall Lee and Gary's rooftop duet and shared past, revealing that Marshall Lee taught Gary how to be more rebellious and that Gary's sweetness tempers Marshall Lee's cruelty. Marshall Lee feeds on souls to fight the Lich's minions, showing heroic use of vampirism. The arc ends with Marshall Lee and Gary affirming mutual affection, more explicit than TV allowed under 2013 broadcast standards. First time Marshall Lee is written by his original creator outside Ice King's framing; establishes canonical (comic) romance with Gary Prince that later informs Max series; expands lore on his feeding and morality.

The Star and Casper & Nova (Multiverse Re-Canonization)Adventure Time: Fionna and Cake Season 1 Episodes 7 'The Star' and 8 'Casper & Nova' (Max, 2023)

2023

Fionna and Cake, now adults in a magic-less world, travel the multiverse with Simon Petrikov to find Prismo. In an alternate, authorized universe (separate from Ice King's fanfiction), they encounter an adult Marshall Lee living independently in a vampire-inhabited city. No longer merely fiction, he is shown as a real person with history, no longer bound to antagonize Fionna. He appears briefly at a gathering of variants, exchanging nods with Gary Prince, implying their relationship persisted across canonization. His presence confirms Prismo's earlier statement that Fionna and Cake's world was made real, elevating Marshall Lee from fanfiction to multiverse citizen. Retcons Marshall Lee from 'just Ice King's story' to 'real alternate-universe entity,' resolving long-standing fandom debate about canonicity; opens door for future adult stories; validates comic romance as multiverse-true.

Five Short Graybles - Cameo and Non-Narrative AppearancesAdventure Time Season 4 Episode 11 'Five Short Graybles' and promotional shorts (2012)

2012-2015

Marshall Lee makes non-speaking or background cameos in Ice King's fanfiction montages and in title-card parodies. In 'Five Short Graybles,' Ice King briefly flips through sketches including Marshall Lee. These micro-appearances contain no plot but maintain his presence in the show's bible and merchandise, showing Cartoon Network's intent to keep the gender-swapped cast available for licensing. No plot consequence, but demonstrates studio's archival maintenance of the character for future use; used as evidence in rights discussions that Marshall Lee was always considered franchise IP, not one-off joke.

Marshall Lee's full canonical name is Marshall Lee Abadeer. — No broadcast episode, credit, or Max series lists a surname. 'Abadeer' is extrapolated from Marceline's father Hunson Abadeer and appears only in fanon and some unofficial wikis. Official Cartoon Network bios use 'Marshall Lee' alone.

Marshall Lee is just Marceline in a costume or crossplay. — He is a distinct character: a gender-swapped counterpart/variant created by Natasha Allegri and later established as an alternate-universe person. He has his own voice actor (Donald Glover), songs, and relationships, not a disguise.

Marshall Lee appears as a main character throughout Adventure Time. — He appears in only three original-series episodes (S3E9, S5E11, S6E9) plus cameos and comics, and briefly in the 2023 sequel. He is a recurring guest, not a main cast member.

Donald Glover did not sing Marshall Lee's song; it was auto-tuned or performed by someone else. — Glover performed both speaking and singing for Marshall Lee in 'Bad Little Boy' (2013). Rebecca Sugar's demo exists but broadcast version is Glover.

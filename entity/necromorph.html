Who It Is
Form

Necromorphs are reanimated corpses and other dead tissue reshaped into horrific new forms by a recombinant infection derived from a genetic code etched into and transmitted by the Markers. They come in multiple forms of various shapes and sizes, with some having multiple limbs, while others have their bodies severely mutilated. Their appearance is often accompanied by a strong, pungent odor.

Powers

Infectious Bite Necromorphs can infect humans through their bite, which can spread the infection and turn the victim into a Necromorph.

Regeneration Necromorphs have the ability to regenerate their bodies, allowing them to heal from injuries and continue their attack.

Enhanced Strength Necromorphs possess enhanced strength, allowing them to overpower and kill humans with ease.

Accounts

The origin of the Necromorphs is tied to the Markers, which are mysterious alien artifacts that emit a signal that drives the creatures to murder and infect all life within their vicinity.

The infection caused by the Necromorphs has severe consequences for humanity, including the loss of life and the transformation of humans into Necromorphs.

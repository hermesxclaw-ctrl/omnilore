Who It Is

In the annals of modern fantasy, few heroes begin their journey as unremarkably as Satoru Mikami—a 37-year-old Japanese salaryman whose death at the hands of a random assailant would ordinarily merit a footnote in a police report. Yet from that violent, anonymous end, Mikami is reborn as Rimuru Tempest, a slime with the power to devour and evolve, rising to become a demon lord who forges a nation of monsters and humans alike. His story is a meditation on second chances, the ethics of power, and the radical idea that a monster can be the most humane being of all.

Satoru/Rimuru is pragmatic, kind-hearted, and deeply loyal to his friends. He values cooperation and peaceful coexistence, often seeking diplomatic solutions before resorting to force. He is also somewhat laid-back and enjoys leisure, but becomes ruthless when his people are threatened. He has a strong sense of justice but is willing to compromise when necessary.

Powers
Predator

A unique skill that allows Rimuru to devour anything—objects, creatures, and even magic—and analyze them, gaining their abilities and knowledge. It also grants him the ability to mimic forms.

Limit: Cannot devour beings with higher magicule output than his own without risk. The skill's analysis is not instantaneous for complex beings.

Great Sage

An autonomous thinking skill that processes information rapidly, offers advice, and later evolves into the Ultimate Skill 'Raphael'.

Limit: Initially can only answer questions, not initiate actions. Later becomes more proactive.

Unique Skills: Degenerate, Gluttony, etc.

Degenerate can convert matter into energy, and Gluttony is the evolution of Predator, allowing for storage and later retrieval of consumed items.

Limit: Gluttony has a limited storage capacity that can be expanded with skill evolution.

Ultimate Skills: Veldora's skills, Uriel, Beelzebub, etc.

After becoming a demon lord, Rimuru acquires multiple ultimate skills, including Uriel (defense and teleportation), Beelzebub (gluttony and destruction), and Raphael (analysis and control). He also gains skills from Veldora, such as 'Storm Magic'.

Limit: These skills require immense magicule reserves and can be mentally taxing.

Demon Lord Transformation

After awakening as a demon lord, Rimuru gains a massive boost in power, including the ability to use 'Demon Lord Haki' and 'Disintegration'.

Limit: The transformation is a one-time event that requires a specific ritual.

Shape-shifting

As a slime, Rimuru can change his body's shape, size, and even color. He can also merge with other beings or objects.

Limit: His base form is a slime; he must maintain a certain mass to sustain his humanoid form.

Form & Signs

Appearance — As Satoru Mikami, he is a 37-year-old Japanese man with an average build, dark hair, and glasses. After reincarnation as Rimuru, he becomes a blue slime with a humanoid form that he can assume. His humanoid form retains his original face but with blue hair and golden eyes, often dressed in white and blue clothing. He can also take on various forms, including a demon lord form with more menacing features.

Symbols — His symbol is the slime, representing adaptability and humility. The Tempest Federation flag features a stylized slime. He is often depicted with a sword, particularly the 'Voracious' sword, and his signature skill 'Predator' is symbolized by a devouring maw.

Domains — As a slime, he can inhabit any environment, but he establishes his home in the Jura Forest, where he founds the monster nation of Tempest. He later becomes the Demon Lord of the Jura Tempest Federation, ruling from his capital, Rimuru City.

Accounts I
The Attack and ReincarnationLight novel, manga, anime

Modern (2010s) and fantasy world

Satoru Mikami, a 37-year-old salaryman, is stabbed by a random assailant and dies. As he dies, he hears a voice asking if he wants to be reincarnated. He awakens as a slime in a fantasy world, where he meets the sealed storm dragon Veldora. He befriends Veldora and gains his powers, then sets out to explore the world. Satoru gains the ability to devour and analyze, and he vows to help Veldora escape his seal.

Founding the Jura Tempest FederationLight novel, manga, anime

Fantasy

Rimuru rescues a group of goblins from wolves, then later rescues dwarves and other monsters. He establishes a village, which grows into a nation. He names it Tempest and gains allies, including the lizardman Gabiru and the ogre Benimaru. Tempest becomes a haven for monsters and humans, but also attracts the attention of human kingdoms and the demon lord Milim.

Accounts II
The Falmuth Invasion and Demon Lord AwakeningLight novel, manga, anime

Fantasy

The human kingdom of Falmuth, manipulated by the demon lord Clayman, attacks Tempest. Rimuru's people are massacred, and Rimuru is enraged. He awakens as a demon lord, defeats the army, and later kills Clayman, becoming a recognized demon lord. Rimuru gains immense power and becomes a demon lord. He also gains the title 'Demon Lord Rimuru' and the respect of other demon lords.

The Walpurgis and the Demon Lord CouncilLight novel, manga, anime

Fantasy

After Clayman's death, the demon lords gather at Walpurgis, a council to decide the fate of the world. Rimuru is invited and must prove his legitimacy. He engages in a battle with the demon lord Dagruel, but ultimately gains recognition. Rimuru is officially recognized as a demon lord and Tempest becomes an autonomous nation.

The Empire of the East and the Final WarLight novel (later volumes), manga (ongoing)

Fantasy

The Eastern Empire invades Tempest, leading to a massive war. Rimuru and his allies fight against the Empire's forces, including the awakened hero Masayuki and the demon lord Velgrynd. Rimuru ultimately defeats the Emperor, Rudra, and brings peace. Rimuru becomes one of the most powerful beings in the world and Tempest becomes a major power.

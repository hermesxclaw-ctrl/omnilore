Who It Is

A single name, Jett, spans astronauts and rock stars, racehorses and virtual agents, from the cockpit of the Space Shuttle to the neon-lit duel grounds of Valorant. This dossier untangles the threads of a name that has come to signify speed, rebellion, and the thrill of the chase across very different worlds.

For real individuals, no common personality traits are attributed to the name. In fiction, Jett characters often embody traits of speed, agility, and rebelliousness. For example, Valorant's Jett is confident and cocky, with a quick wit.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Jett' is likely derived from the English word 'jet', referring to the black gemstone or the aircraft engine, symbolizing speed and darkness. It may also be a variant of the name 'Jude' or a diminutive of 'Juliet', but its modern usage is often associated with jet propulsion and swiftness.

Pronunciation — /dʒɛt/

Aliases — Jett

Ownership — The name is used across multiple cultures, predominantly English-speaking, but also appears in Filipino and Vietnamese contexts (e.g., Jett Pangan, Karina Jett).

Form & Signs

Appearance — The name 'Jett' does not have a single appearance. For real individuals, appearances vary. For the Valorant character Jett, she is depicted as a young woman with a slim build, often wearing a white and blue tactical outfit, with a distinctive hairstyle.

Symbols — In popular culture, 'Jett' is often associated with speed, air, and wind. In Valorant, Jett's iconography includes wind motifs and a sharp, agile silhouette.

Joan Jett's Rise to FamePublic biographies, interviews

1970s-present

Joan Jett, born Joan Marie Larkin, became a pioneering rock musician, known for hits like 'I Love Rock 'n' Roll' and as a member of The Runaways. She became an icon of rock music and a role model for women in music.

Accounts II
Brent W. Jett Jr.'s Space MissionsNASA biography

1980s-2000s

Brent Jett is a retired NASA astronaut who flew on four Space Shuttle missions, including STS-72, STS-81, STS-97, and STS-115. He contributed to the construction of the International Space Station.

Jett in ValorantRiot Games

2020-present

In the tactical shooter Valorant, Jett is a duelist agent from South Korea, known for her agility and wind-based abilities. She is a popular character in competitive play. She became one of the most played agents, influencing game balance and esports.

Jett

Etymology

The name 'Jett' is likely derived from the English word 'jet', referring to the black gemstone or the aircraft engine, symbolizing speed and darkness. It may also be a variant of the name 'Jude' or a diminutive of 'Juliet', but its modern usage is often associated with jet propulsion and swiftness.

Cultural Ownership

The name is used across multiple cultures, predominantly English-speaking, but also appears in Filipino and Vietnamese contexts (e.g., Jett Pangan, Karina Jett).

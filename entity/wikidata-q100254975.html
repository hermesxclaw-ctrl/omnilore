Who It Is

In the frozen wastes of Freljord, a gentle giant carries a door as a shield and a heart as warm as the hearth he protects. Braum, the Heart of Freljord, is more than a warrior—he is a living legend, a testament to the strength found in kindness.

Braum is kind, jovial, and protective. He is known for his optimism, his love of children, and his willingness to help anyone in need. He is brave and selfless, always putting others before himself.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Braum' is a fictional invention, but it resembles real-world surnames like Braun or Braum, which are of German origin. The surname Braum is an Americanized form of Brahm and a variant of Braun, meaning 'brown' in German.

Pronunciation — BROWM

Aliases — Braum

Ownership — Created by Riot Games for the multiplayer online battle arena game League of Legends. Fictional character from the region of Freljord, inspired by Norse and Viking culture.

Powers
Winter's Bite

Braum sends out a freezing orb that damages and slows the first enemy hit.

Limit: Requires a target in range; can be blocked by minions.

Unbreakable

Braum raises his shield, blocking incoming projectiles and reducing damage from the first hit.

Limit: Only blocks from the direction he is facing; has a time limit.

Glacial Fissure

Braum slams his shield on the ground, knocking up enemies in a line and creating a slow zone.

Limit: Long cooldown; can be dodged.

Concussive Blows

Braum's basic attacks and Winter's Bite apply stacks; when allies hit the target, they stun it.

Limit: Requires coordination with allies.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Braum is a large, muscular man with a thick brown beard and a warm smile. He wears heavy fur armor and carries a massive door as a shield. His body is covered in tattoos and he often has a friendly, approachable demeanor.

Symbols — His shield, a giant door, symbolizes protection and his role as a guardian. His mustache is iconic and often depicted in fan art.

Domains — Braum lives in the frozen region of Freljord, a harsh, wintry land inspired by Norse mythology. He is a wanderer and protector, often seen helping travelers and villagers.

Accounts I
The Heart of FreljordOfficial lore biography

Contemporary to the game's timeline

Braum grew up as an orphan in a small Freljord village. He became a legendary hero after defeating a giant ice troll that threatened his home. He is known for his incredible strength and his kind heart, always helping those in need. Braum became a revered protector and symbol of hope in Freljord.

The Door as a ShieldOfficial lore

Unknown

Braum found a massive door from an ancient structure and decided to use it as a shield, symbolizing his role as a guardian who stands between danger and those he protects. The door became his iconic weapon and shield.

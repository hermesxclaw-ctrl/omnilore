The Approach

In the frozen wastes of Freljord, a gentle giant carries a door as a shield and a heart as warm as the hearth he protects. Braum, the Heart of Freljord, is more than a warrior—he is a living legend, a testament to the strength found in kindness.

Content advisory. Contains fantasy violence typical of a MOBA game, but no graphic or disturbing content.
Identity

Braum

Pronunciation BROWM

Etymology

The name 'Braum' is a fictional invention, but it resembles real-world surnames like Braun or Braum, which are of German origin. The surname Braum is an Americanized form of Brahm and a variant of Braun, meaning 'brown' in German.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Riot Games for the multiplayer online battle arena game League of Legends. Fictional character from the region of Freljord, inspired by Norse and Viking culture.

Disputed Identifications

None. Braum is a unique fictional character, not to be confused with the real-world surname Braum.

Description

Appearance

Braum is a large, muscular man with a thick brown beard and a warm smile. He wears heavy fur armor and carries a massive door as a shield. His body is covered in tattoos and he often has a friendly, approachable demeanor.

Symbols & Iconography

His shield, a giant door, symbolizes protection and his role as a guardian. His mustache is iconic and often depicted in fan art.

Behaviour & Personality

Braum is kind, jovial, and protective. He is known for his optimism, his love of children, and his willingness to help anyone in need. He is brave and selfless, always putting others before himself.

Domains & Habitats

Braum lives in the frozen region of Freljord, a harsh, wintry land inspired by Norse mythology. He is a wanderer and protector, often seen helping travelers and villagers.

Version Differences

There is only one canonical version of Braum in the main League of Legends universe. However, he has appeared in alternate skin lines (e.g., 'Braum Lionheart' in the High Noon universe) which are non-canonical and purely cosmetic.

Who It Is

Imagine a ticket that always knows where you want to go—and never lets you come back. SCP-342 looks like any other scrap of card stock in your pocket, a bus pass or train stub you might crumple and forget. But once you validate it and step aboard, the world outside the windows darkens, announcements twist into threats, and every attempt to stand up feels like pushing against an invisible verdict. At the end of the route, the vehicle stops, the doors open, and you simply are not there anymore. The ticket, however, is—fresh, unused, and waiting for the next rider.

Attested: SCP-342 exhibits consistent behavioral properties but no explicit personality. When held by a sentient person for any length of time, it eventually changes form into a transit ticket for the mode of transportation the holder desires to use. This transformation always occurs when not directly or indirectly observed; no recordings of the change exist. Once used to board a vehicle, SCP-342 prevents the user from exiting by any means. Powers
Form-shifting transit ticket

Attested: SCP-342 normally takes the form of a mass transit ticket for the closest form of mass transportation to its current location. When held by a sentient person for any length of time, it eventually changes form into a transit ticket for the mode of transportation the holder desires to use. This transformation always occurs unobserved, and no recordings of the change exist.

Limit: Attested: The transformation requires a sentient holder and time; it does not occur instantly. It appears constrained to mass transit systems rather than arbitrary objects. The change cannot be directly observed, suggesting a limitation tied to observation.

Irrevocable boarding and entrapment

Attested: Anyone who uses SCP-342 to board a vehicle is unable to exit that vehicle by any means. Once the vehicle ends its route and ceases movement, the user disappears from reality. Attempts to leave are thwarted by strange occurrences that prevent disembarkation, and users develop an absolute conviction that exit is impossible.

Limit: Attested: The effect applies only to the person who uses SCP-342 to board; bystanders are not subject to the same level of entrapment, though they may experience mild anomalous phenomena. The entrapment lasts only for the duration of the vehicle's route; once the route ends, the user disappears.

Self-reformation after use or disposal

Attested: If SCP-342 is validated by stamp, has its ticket stub torn off, or is disposed of, it reforms itself into an unused ticket after a short period of time.

Limit: Attested: The reformation occurs after a short, unspecified interval. The article does not describe any way to permanently destroy SCP-342.

Induction of progressive psychological distress

Attested: Users report a mounting feeling of dread prior to boarding, increasing during the journey, culminating in panic-inducing terror shortly before disappearance. Phenomena resemble acute paranoid schizophrenia, including auditory hallucinations, misperception of announcements, threatening interpretations of mundane objects, pathological fear of transit staff, and inability to perceive normal passenger movement.

Limit: Attested: The most acute experiences are limited to the user; bystanders may experience mild anomalous phenomena but not the full spectrum of distress. The effects appear tied to the duration of the journey and intensify as the route nears its end.

Accounts I
SCP-342 Case File: Containment and DescriptionSCP Wiki article "SCP-342 - SCP Foundation"

Contemporary (21st century, exact posting date unknown).

Fact-based: The core narrative of SCP-342 is presented in the format of a containment report. The Foundation stores SCP-342 in an envelope stapled inside a case file folder, locked in High Value Items Storage. The description details its behavior as a shape-shifting transit ticket, its ability to entrap riders on vehicles, and the psychological effects experienced by users. The report frames SCP-342 as a Euclid-class anomaly—dangerous when used but manageable under proper containment.
Interpretive: The case file implicitly tells a story of repeated encounters with SCP-342, including experiments and incidents, though specific events are not fully quoted in the snippet available. Attested: Users who board vehicles with SCP-342 disappear at the end of the route, presumed dead or transported elsewhere. The Foundation maintains strict containment to prevent further disappearances. The anomaly is classified as Euclid, indicating ongoing risk and incomplete understanding.

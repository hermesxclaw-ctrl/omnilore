In the smoky backrooms of Japan's postwar entertainment world, a chubby, mustachioed everyman in a rumpled suit became one of the most recognizable faces in anime—yet his name, his very identity, remains a mystery even to his most devoted fans.

Identity

Hige Oyaji

Pronunciation HEE-geh OH-yah-jee

Etymology

Hige Oyaji (ヒゲオヤジ) literally translates to 'Bearded Old Man' or 'Mustached Old Man' (hige = beard/mustache, oyaji = old man/dad).

Cultural Ownership

Japanese popular culture; primarily associated with anime and manga, but also appears in other media.

Disputed Identifications

The character is often confused with similar 'old man' gag characters from the same era; exact origin and first appearance are not clearly documented.

Description

Appearance

Hige Oyaji is typically depicted as a middle-aged or elderly man with a prominent mustache, often a thick black mustache. He has a round face, sometimes with stubble, and is usually shown wearing a business suit or casual clothes. His design is simple and cartoonish.

Symbols & Iconography

His mustache is his defining feature, often used as a visual gag. He may also be associated with cigarettes or alcohol in some depictions.

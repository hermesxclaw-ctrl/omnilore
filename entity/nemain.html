Who It Is

Nemain is the war goddess of battle-frenzy and panic. Her name means 'frenzy.' She shrieks over combatants, her voice inducing such terror that warriors drop dead of fright. She represents the psychological dimension of war — dread and chaos that break minds rather than bodies.

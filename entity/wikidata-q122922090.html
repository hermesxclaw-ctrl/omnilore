Who It Is
Form

No. 665 is a young female Elf with waist-length white hair and vivid blue eyes, described across light novel prose, manga panels, and anime character sheets as notably tall — standing above average height relative to human characters and to her immediate squadmates. Her elven heritage is expressed through the characteristic slight elongation of her ears that has become a visual shorthand for elves in Japanese fantasy media, and through an overall graceful, composed physical bearing that underscores her elf race's typical depiction as elegant and composed. In her operational role as a Shadow Garden Numbers-rank member she wears the organization's standard dark combat uniform — a close-fitting tactical outfit in the deep greys and near-blacks of the Shadow Garden aesthetic, designed for speed, mobility, and magical augmentation rather than conventional armor — which creates a striking visual contrast with her pale complexion and white hair. The anime adaptation produced by Nexus and overseen by character designer Makoto Iino preserves this core visual identity while giving it the production's characteristic clean linework and high-motion sakuga sequences in combat scenes. After No. 666 / Rose Oriana's coronation as Queen of Oriana, 665 transitions to the formal attire of an Oriana royal court maid — a structured, long-skirted uniform with the precise aesthetic sensibility characteristic of the kingdom known in-universe for its arts and refined court culture — marking a visible shift in her presented identity from covert operative to palace servant. This dual-wardrobe existence mirrors the broader Shadow Garden dynamic in which operatives maintain cover identities alongside their organizational roles.

Powers

Physical Enhancement via Shadow Garden Training As a Numbers-rank Shadow Garden operative, 665 has undergone the organization's rigorous magical combat training regimen established by Cid Kagenou. This training grants all Shadow Garden members substantially elevated physical capabilities — enhanced speed, strength, endurance, and reflexes relative to ordinary soldiers and even most trained mages of the series' fantasy world. The VSBattles wiki documentation for Shadow Garden notes that Numbers-rank members operate at 'superhuman' physical tiers attributable to magical augmentation of the body through the Slimeia (dark miasma-derived) magic system that underlies the series' power framework.

Magic Channeling / Slimeia-based Combat Magic The Eminence in Shadow's magic system, outlined in VSBattles wiki documentation, is rooted in Slimeia — a dark miasmic energy that suffuses the world and can be channeled by trained users for a range of combat and utility effects. Shadow Garden members, including Numbers-rank operatives, are trained specifically in this magic system. 665's designation as a 'support-oriented combatant' suggests her magical specialization leans toward augmentation, defensive application, or supportive magical effects rather than direct offensive casting — differentiating her functional role from the more frontline combat emphasis of other squad members.

Demon Possession Immunity / Cured Status A defining characteristic shared by all Shadow Garden members is that they were afflicted with demonic possession and subsequently cured by Shadow (Cid Kagenou). This cure, accidentally developed by Cid while experimenting to treat Alpha, is not merely medical — it leaves the recipient permanently free of the Cult of Diablos's potential to re-infect them and is implied to strengthen the recipient's magical resilience. For 665 this means her baseline magical constitution has been altered by Cid's intervention, distinguishing Shadow Garden operatives as a class from ordinary magic users in the series world.

Covert Operations and Espionage Consistent with Shadow Garden's operational mandate as a covert shadow organization, 665 possesses trained skills in infiltration, information gathering, and undercover operation. The Lawless City arc mission — gathering intelligence on Vampire Progenitor blood samples while maintaining readiness to support Shadow directly — exemplifies this operational profile: covert intelligence work combined with combat readiness, executed under the direct command of Beta (Shadow Garden's second-ranking field operative). Her subsequent role as an undercover royal maid in the Oriana court extends this covert competency into long-duration embedded intelligence work.

Close-Quarters Combat Proficiency While 665's specific weapons or martial style are not explicitly documented in available sources, all Numbers-rank Shadow Garden members trained by the organization are equipped for close-range engagement. The assault on the Red Tower in the Lawless City arc involved direct confrontation with vampire-class enemies — among the more dangerous foes in the series' combat hierarchy — confirming that 665 is operationally rated for high-threat engagements and not merely administrative or logistical support functions.

Adaptive Role Transition (Maid / Bodyguard Dual Function) Following Rose Oriana's coronation, 665 and 664 adopt dual roles as both royal maids performing palace service functions and covert personal bodyguards protecting the queen from threats. This adaptive capability — maintaining a credible cover identity as domestic palace staff while simultaneously functioning as elite protective agents — represents a practical application of Shadow Garden's covert training in a sustained peacetime intelligence context rather than an acute combat scenario.

Accounts

No. 665, alongside No. 664 and No. 666 (Rose Oriana), participated in a Shadow Garden assault on the Red Tower located in the Lawless City — an arc that served as one of the major set pieces in the early narrative. The squad operated under the direct command of Beta, Shadow Garden's intelligence and operations coordinator. Their primary mission objective was intelligence-gathering: researching a sample of the Vampire Progenitor's blood in the context of demonic possession research, supporting the organization's broader effort to understand and combat the Cult of Diablos's supernatural arsenal. The secondary operational mandate was combat readiness — to intervene on behalf of their lord Shadow if circumstances required. During this operation, the vampire queen Elisabeth was resurrected, triggering a crisis event. 665's squad immediately responded to assist Shadow in protecting Claire Kagenou, Cid's older sister, who was directly imperiled by the resurrection event.

Rose Oriana's arc — which centers on her discovery of political corruption in the Oriana Kingdom, her father King Raphael's death at her own hands to prevent civil war, her joining of Shadow Garden as No. 666, and her ultimate coronation as Queen — directly defines 665's post-arc role. Throughout this arc 665 and 664 function as Rose's squad companions, providing tactical support, escort, and operational backup as Rose navigates the dangerous convergence of her royal obligations and her Shadow Garden membership. The arc culminates with Rose's coronation, after which she formally leaves Shadow Garden's active roster (while remaining an associate), and 665 and 664 transition from field operatives to royal maids and covert personal bodyguards in service to the new queen.

Following Rose Oriana's coronation as queen, 665 and 664 take up residence in the Oriana royal palace as part of the queen's personal household staff — publicly functioning as royal maids while covertly serving as elite bodyguards trained by Shadow Garden. This posting represents the series' longest-running context for 665's presence in the narrative, shifting her from acute-combat appearances to a sustained embedded role. The Oriana Kingdom's identity as an arts-focused realm provides a contrasting backdrop to Shadow Garden's secretive combat mandate, situating 665 in a culturally refined environment while maintaining her organizational allegiances.

Like every member of Shadow Garden — from the Seven Shadows down through the Numbers — 665 was at some point afflicted with demonic possession caused by the Cult of Diablos's influence. She was discovered by the organization, treated with the possession cure developed by Cid Kagenou through experimentation, and inducted into Shadow Garden's Numbers rank. This origin is the foundational event for all Shadow Garden members' devotion to Shadow/Cid: the cure is experienced as a profound personal salvation, generating loyalty that structures every operative's motivation throughout the series. For 665, this backstory contextualizes her relaxed, service-oriented personality as the stable identity of someone who has passed through existential crisis and found purpose in organizational loyalty.

Scholarly Debates
Combat Role vs. Support Role Classification
No. 665 is a support-oriented combatant whose primary value in squad operations is facilitating the effectiveness of others — evidenced by the anibase character database's explicit characterization of her as 'support-oriented' and by her squad's three-person composition (664 presumably filling a different tactical niche). Her easygoing personality reinforces a temperamental fit for a support rather than frontline role. ⇌ All Shadow Garden Numbers-rank members are trained as capable close-range combat operatives who participated in the assault on the Red Tower in the Lawless City — one of the arc's most dangerous combat environments, involving vampire-class enemies. No source explicitly limits 665 to non-combat or secondary roles; the 'support-oriented' label may reflect relative specialization rather than an inability to engage directly. Fan discussions on Reddit and Heroes Wiki treat her as a full combatant operative.
Significance of the Number 665 in the Shadow Garden Roster
The Numbers designation reflects sequential recruitment order into Shadow Garden, meaning 665 was recruited after the first 664 members and before subsequent inductees. Under this reading, 665 represents a mid-to-early cohort of the expanded organization, recruited well after the original Seven Shadows but significantly before the 700s-range members like No. 712 / Akane Nishino. Her number is organizationally meaningful as a rank-and-seniority indicator. ⇌ The numbering system may not strictly reflect recruitment order and could represent a more complex organizational classification incorporating unit assignments, geographic deployment, or capability tiers. The series does not explicitly confirm that lower numbers always indicate earlier recruitment; fan wiki documentation treats the numbering as a designation rather than a confirmed chronological sequence, and the mechanics of the numbering system are not fully explained in available source material.
Whether 665 Has Meaningful Individual Agency in the Narrative
No. 665 is a functionally individuated character with a confirmed personal name (Maggie/Magi), a distinctive visual design, a documented birthday (August 2nd), a consistent squad role, and multiple referenced narrative appearances across the light novel, manga, and anime. The Heroes Wiki treatment of her as a 'hero character' with a dedicated page, and the Fandom wiki's detailed Chronology subpage, indicate sufficient narrative presence to constitute meaningful characterization beyond a pure background extra. ⇌ Compared to the Seven Shadows and the major named supporting cast, 665's narrative footprint is minimal — she has brief appearances in Season 1 (episodes 1 and 20 only), limited dialogue in available documented scenes, and her most significant moment (the Red Tower arc) is primarily framed through the lens of 666/Rose Oriana's storyline rather than as 665's own character development. Critics and casual viewers may reasonably classify her as an atmospheric ensemble member rather than a character with individuated narrative agency.
Personal Name Rendering: 'Magi' vs. 'Maggie'
The English localization name 'Maggie' is the canonical English-language name for this character, established by both the Yen Press light novel translation and the Sentai Filmworks English dub, and should be treated as the primary English designation. ⇌ The original Japanese name 'Magi' (マギ) is the author-intended form and should be preferred in academic or fan contexts that prioritize fidelity to source text. The 'Maggie' rendering is a localization approximation that shifts connotation (from the mythologically resonant 'Magi' toward a more colloquial English diminutive), and some fan communities prefer the Japanese romanization.

Who It Is

If the Best Friends For Life had a walk-in closet that doubled as a spell lab, it would belong to Topaz Trollhopper — the yellow-diamond Trollzopolis teen who can turn a shopping spree into a summoning circle and still forget why she cast the spell in the first place. Sweet, scattered, and fiercely loyal, Topaz proves that in Trollzopolis, fashion sense is a kind of magic.

Attested across all episodes and series bible summary: Topaz is kind-hearted, loyal, enthusiastic, and a self-described 'airhead' — forgetful, easily distracted, literal-minded, but emotionally intelligent and supportive. Loves shopping, fashion expertise, gossip, and giving makeovers. Often provides comic relief via malapropisms and spell misfires, then redeems herself through earnest effort. Name & Tongue

Etymology — Topaz from the gemstone topaz, from Old French topace via Latin topazus and Greek topazos (likely from Sanskrit tapas 'heat, fire' or the Red Sea island Topazios). Fits Trollz naming convention where each BFFL member is named for her belly-gem: Amethyst, Ruby, Sapphire, Topaz, Onyx. Surname Trollhop

Pronunciation — /ˈtoʊ.pæz ˈtroʊlˌhɒp.ər/ (TOH-paz TROLL-hop-er)

Aliases — Topaz Trollhopper

Ownership — Fictional character created for the American children's animated property Trollz, produced by DIC Entertainment (now WildBrain) under license from Dam Things / Dam Family, holders

Powers
Belly-Gem Spellcasting (Yellow Diamond)

Channeling magic through her yellow diamond navel gem. Requires verbal incantation, gem glow, and often a wand-like gesture or charm. Effects are typically transfigurative and temporary, aligned with teen life needs. Topaz's spells skew toward appearance and style.

Limit: Magic is unreliable when cast selfishly, without focus, or without friendship cooperation; spells often backfire or have ironic consequences. Requires Ancient guidance (Obsidian) and later the power of all five gems combined for major feats. Gem can dim if friendship is strained. No solo world-altering power.

Fashion & Hair Transfiguration

Signature feats: instant outfit changes, hair recoloring/restyling, accessorizing, and 'glow up' glamour spells. Example: conjuring new ensembles for school events; attempting to fix Jasper's hair loss.

Limit: Duration limited; often reverts or mutates (e.g., hair grows uncontrollably). Requires precise wording — Topaz's literal misreadings cause mishaps. Cannot permanently alter another troll without consent in series moral.

The Magic of the Five (Collective)

When all five BFFL gems combine (heart, star, flower, diamond, moon), they generate a large-scale protective/ restorative spell capable of countering Simon's gremlin magic, restoring petrified Ancients (Zirconia/Spinell), and shielding Trollzopolis. Topaz's diamond provides the 'clarity/joy' component.

Limit: Requires all five members present, emotionally synchronized, and often an Ancient's guidance. Cannot be spammed; drains gems and needs recharge. Topaz cannot invoke alone.

Enhanced Shopping Intuition / Social Influence (non-magical)

Non-magical skill: expert eye for trends, bargain hunting, styling friends, and mediating social conflicts via empathy. Often resolves BFFL arguments where logic fails.

Limit: Not supernatural; played for comedy. Her advice is sometimes naive and needs Sapphire or Amethyst to refine.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Attested: Topaz is depicted as a teenage female troll with light skin, large eyes, and voluminous blonde / light-yellow hair styled long with bangs and often a side ponytail or flip, reflecting the troll-doll hair trope. Her signature gem is a yellow diamond (sometimes rendered as pale yellow / citrine) set in her navel/belly button, visible when she uses magic. Outfit is fashion-forward Y2K teen pop: usually yellow/orange tops, miniskirts, platf

Symbols — Yellow diamond belly gem is both power source and identity marker; diamond cut (vs. heart/star/flower/moon of other BFFL) signals clarity/optimism in series gem lore. Fashion motifs — shopping bags, mirrors, hairbrushes, star-shaped spell effects in yellow — recur as visual shorthand for vanity/creativity. Spell casting is accompanied by gem glow and incantation phrase 'Abraca... ' variant per character. No traditional folklore symbols;

Domains — Lives in Trollzopolis, a pastel, magic-infused suburban city where trolls co-exist with Ancient mentors and magic is everyday utility. Accounts I
Best Friends for Life (Pilot Movie, Episodes 1-3)Trollz S01E01-E03, DIC Entertainment; premiered Disney Channel 2005-08-27; compiled as 'Best Friends for Life - The Movie'

2005

Trollzopolis teens Amethyst, Ruby, Sapphire, Topaz, and Onyx discover they are the prophesied Best Friends For Life, destined to master belly-gem magic under the Ancients. Topaz is introduced as the fashion-loving airhead with a big heart who volunteers for makeover spells that go awry, but her optimism keeps the group together when Simon the gremlin first attempts to disrupt their friendship. The arc establishes the BFFL formation, their gem activation, and Simon's 3,000-year grudge. BFFL formally unite; Topaz accepts responsibility for magic; Simon established as recurring antagonist; restored faith in friendship-as-power theme that drives series.

The Magic of the Five (Episodes 4-6)Trollz S01E04-E06, compiled as 'The Magic of the Five - The Movie', Warner Home Video 2005-09-06

2005

The BFFL must learn to combine their five gems to perform the ancient 'Magic of the Five' to stop Simon from stealing Trollzopolis's ambient magic. Topaz struggles with concentration — she keeps visualizing outfits instead of the spell matrix — but ultimately contributes the crucial stabilizing 'joy' note that lets the combined beam succeed. The spell restores a blighted part of the city and foreshadows the later restoration of Zirconia and Spinell. First successful collective spell; establishes rule that no single troll can defeat Simon alone; Topaz learns focus vs. flair.

Hair Over Heels (Episodes 7-9)Trollz S01E07-E09, planned as 'Hair Over Heels - The Movie' (NCircle 2007)

2005-2006

Amethyst's botched hair-growth spell accidentally leaves Topaz's boyfriend Jasper Trollhound bald. Topaz, torn between embarrassment and loyalty, tries a series of escalating fashion-magic fixes — wigs, hats that animate, extensions that grow wildly — each worsening the situation. She finally accepts Jasper's new look and helps him gain confidence, teaching the BFFL that acceptance beats cover-ups. Jasper decides he likes being bald. Jasper's permanent character design becomes bald; Topaz's relationship deepens; moral about body image and consent in magic. Frequently cited by fans as Topaz's most character-defining arc.

Accounts II
You Glow Girls (Episodes 10-12)Trollz S01E10-E12, planned as 'You Glow Girls - The Movie' (unreleased Spring 2006, later on Netflix compilation)

2005-2006

Trollzopolis hosts a school-wide Glow Dance where trolls' gems literally glow with confidence. Topaz becomes obsessed with having the brightest glow and overuses a luminescence spell, causing her and the BFFL to glow uncontrollably and attract mischievous light sprites. The girls must dim themselves by sharing vulnerability rather than showing off. Topaz learns that inner confidence, not spell-enhanced shine, creates true glow. Introduces lore that gem brightness reflects emotional state; sets up rivalry with Coral's cheer squad; reinforces series theme that magic amplifies but does not replace character growth.

A Hair A-Fair / Trollzopolis School Fair (Episodes 13-15)Trollz S01E13-E15, compiled as 'A Hair A-Fair - The Movie' (announced June 2008, unreleased; later included in 5-disc French boxset 2015)

2005-2006

The school fair's fashion and craft competition pits the BFFL against Coral, Opal, and Jade. Topaz is appointed lead designer and must balance her maximalist taste with Sapphire's budgeting and Ruby's competitiveness. Simon sabotages the fair with a snarl-beast (Snarf in enlarged form) that tangles all fabrics. Topaz improvises by using a simple, heartfelt patchwork spell that unites the BFFL's styles, winning on originality rather than flash. Establishes Topaz as competent designer when she trusts instinct over trend-chasing; deepens BFFL vs. rivals dynamic; shows Snarf's shapeshifting ability.

Dragon's Restoration / The Ancients' Curse (Episodes 16-18, Zirconia & Spinell arc)Trollz S01E16-E18 (late-season Ancient lore arc)

2005-2006

The BFFL learn that 3,000 years ago Simon transformed Zirconia into a tree and Spinell into a dragon servant. Topaz, who had been tending the 'sad tree' on campus as a decoration project, is revealed to have been caring for Zirconia unknowingly. Her consistent kindness (watering, talking to the tree) weakens the curse and allows the Magic of the Five to finally restore both Ancients. Topaz's empathy is framed as magic-adjacent. Zirconia and Spinell restored; 3,000-year backstory explained; Topaz's nurturing trait given mythic weight; Simon's cruelty and power scale clarified.

Topaz Trollhopper is based on a specific Scandinavian folklore troll named Topaz. — No folklore antecedent. Trollz characters are 2005 original creations by DIC, inspired only generically by Thomas Dam's 1959 troll doll design. Topaz name comes from gemstone branding, not folklore.

Topaz has solo teleportation or flight powers. — Only the combined Magic of the Five achieves large-scale magic. Topaz's solo spells are limited to cosmetic transfiguration and small glamours; all flight/teleport claims derive from misreading group feats as individual.

Leah Juel did not voice Topaz — it was Britt McKillip or Chiara Zanni. — Leah Juel is credited as Topaz across Behind The Voice Actors, DVD credits, and series bible; Britt McKillip voices Amethyst, Chiara Zanni voices Ruby.

Trollz had 52 episodes and Topaz appears in 52 stories. — 52 was the announced order; only 27 were produced and aired. Topaz appears in the 27 produced episodes plus 5 compiled movies.

Who It Is

In the vast, multiversal expanse of the Land of Ooo, where time is a river that bends to the will of storytellers, there exists a being known as Prismo—a wish-master who dwells outside of time itself. But before he became the ageless wish-granter, he was a mortal man named Prismo, and his closest friend was Jake the Dog, a shape-shifting hero whose loyalty transcends even the boundaries of reality. Their tale is one of friendship, sacrifice, and the strange loops that bind fate together.

Prismo is laid-back, mischievous, and wise. He enjoys granting wishes but often with ironic twists. He is friendly and helpful to Finn and Jake, but also pragmatic. Jake is loyal, brave, and good-hearted. He loves adventure and food, and he is a devoted brother and friend. He often uses his shape-shifting to solve problems creatively.

Powers
Wish Granting

Prismo can grant any wish, but often with a twist or unintended consequence. He can also create alternate realities based on wishes.

Limit: Wishes are granted literally but can have ironic outcomes. Prismo cannot alter his own existence directly.

Time Manipulation

Prismo can pause, rewind, or fast-forward time within his time-room.

Limit: Limited to his time-room; he cannot affect time outside without consequences.

Immortality and Astral Projection

Prismo exists as a ghost-like being and can project himself into dreams. He is effectively immortal unless his physical form (the pickle jar) is destroyed.

Limit: He is vulnerable if his pickle jar is destroyed.

Shape-Shifting

Jake can stretch, flatten, and morph his body into any shape, including vehicles, tools, and even living beings.

Limit: He has limits to how much he can stretch, and he tires after extensive use.

Enhanced Senses and Strength

Jake has heightened smell and hearing, and he is stronger than a normal dog.

Limit: Not superhuman to an extreme degree.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Prismo appears as a floating, pink, ghost-like entity with a large mustache, wearing a white tank top and shorts. He lives in a time-room where he can manipulate time. In his mortal life, he was an elderly human man with gray hair and a beard. Jake is a yellow, shape-shifting bulldog with droopy ears and a tail. He can stretch and morph his body into any shape.

Symbols — Prismo's iconography includes his time-room, a pink bathroom-like space with a TV and a pickle jar that contains his 'dream' form. Jake's iconography includes his shape-shifting abilities and his loyal friendship with Finn.

Domains — Prismo resides in a timeless dimension called the Time Room, which exists outside normal space-time. Jake resides in the Land of Ooo, primarily in the Tree Fort with Finn, but he can travel across dimensions.

Accounts I
Finn the Human / Jake the DogAdventure Time, Season 5, Episodes 1-2

2012

Finn wishes to live in a world without Lich, leading Prismo to create an alternate reality. Jake goes on a quest to find Finn, and Prismo helps him by granting a wish that sends him to the alternate reality. In the end, they restore the original timeline. The Lich's plan is thwarted, and Finn and Jake return home.

Wake Up / Escape from the CitadelAdventure Time, Season 6, Episodes 1-2

2014

Prismo is killed by the Lich, but his ghost form persists. Finn and Jake travel to the Citadel to find Finn's father, with Prismo's help. Prismo's backstory is hinted at. Finn loses his arm, and the Lich is defeated temporarily.

Accounts II
The More Things Change / The More Things Stay the SameAdventure Time, Season 7, Episodes 13-14

2016

Prismo is revived by Finn and Jake. They discover a time loop involving Prismo's mortal self and his friend, who turns out to be a younger Jake. This establishes that Prismo and Jake were friends before Prismo became a wishmaster. Prismo is restored to his time-room, and the time loop is resolved.

Together AgainAdventure Time: Distant Lands

2021

Finn and Jake reunite in the afterlife. Prismo appears to help them, and they work together to save the day. Finn and Jake are reincarnated together.

Who It Is

In the celestial bureaucracy of Chinese mythology, a giant rooster stands ready to crow down demons—this is Maori Xingguan, the Sun Rooster of the Hairy Head, a star deity whose comb shines with the light of dawn and whose cry silences the scorpion's sting.

In Journey to the West, he is a helpful and powerful deity who responds to Sun Wukong's plea. He is dutiful and uses his natural rooster form to counter the scorpion demoness. In Fengshen Yanyi, he is a devoted disciple of Tongtian Jiaozhu, but his personality is not deeply explored.

Powers
Rooster Transformation

Maori Xingguan can transform into a giant rooster, which is his natural form. This form is particularly effective against scorpions and centipedes, as roosters are natural predators of these creatures.

Limit: The transformation is primarily useful against insect/arthropod demons; it may not be effective against other types of foes.

Immortality and Cultivation

In Fengshen Yanyi, after thousands of years of cultivation as a rooster, he achieved immortality and became a disciple of Tongtian Jiaozhu.

Limit: Despite his cultivation, he was killed in battle, showing that his immortality was not absolute.

Divine Star Authority

As a star official, he has authority over his celestial mansion and can be called upon by other deities.

Limit: Not elaborated in sources.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In Journey to the West, Maori Xingguan is described as a star god who can transform into a giant rooster, six or seven feet tall, with two combs. In his divine form, he likely appears as a celestial official, but the text focuses on his rooster transformation.

Symbols — Rooster, comb, sun, Pleiades (Hairy Head mansion), celestial palace (Guangming Palace).

Domains — He resides in the Guangming Palace (Palace of Light) in Heaven. As a star deity, he is associated with the Pleiades mansion and the sun's aspect.

Accounts I
Deification in Fengshen YanyiFengshen Yanyi

Ming dynasty (16th century)

Huang Cang, the first giant rooster, cultivated for thousands of years and became immortal. He became a disciple of Tongtian Jiaozhu and his favorite. During the Battle of the Ten Thousand Immortals, he was killed by the Chan sect. After his death, Jiang Ziya deified him as the Sun Rooster of Hairy Head, one of the twenty-eight stars. He became a star deity in the celestial bureaucracy.

Accounts II
Defeating the Scorpion DemonessJourney to the West

Ming dynasty (16th century)

Tang Sanzang is captured by the Scorpion Demoness, who tries to seduce him. Sun Wukong and Zhu Bajie fail to defeat her due to her poisonous sting. Guanyin advises Sun Wukong to seek Maori Xingguan's help. Maori Xingguan transforms into a giant rooster and kills the scorpion demoness. The demoness is killed, and Tang Sanzang is rescued.

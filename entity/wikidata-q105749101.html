Who It Is

Naoto Kurogane is a young man who wields a power that defies the very laws of the world—a power that draws him into a conflict far larger than himself, where the boundaries between life and death, past and future, are blurred.

Naoto is initially portrayed as a cynical and somewhat jaded individual, burdened by his past and the loss of his sister. He is fiercely protective of those he cares about and is willing to go to great lengths to protect them, even if it means risking his own life. He has a dry sense of humor and a tendency to act impulsively, but he is ultimately driven by a strong sense of justice.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname 'Kurogane' (鉄) means 'iron' in Japanese, possibly reflecting his strong will and combat prowess. The given name 'Naoto' (ナオト) is a common Japanese name, but its specific meaning is not officially stated.

Pronunciation — nah-oh-toh koo-roh-gah-neh

Powers
Azure Grimoire (fragment)

Naoto possesses a fragment of the Azure, which grants him extraordinary power and the ability to manipulate the flow of battle. This manifests as a blue flame-like energy that enhances his physical abilities and allows him to perform powerful attacks.

Limit: The power is unstable and can be overwhelming if not controlled. It also attracts the attention of those who seek the Azure.

Combat Skills

Naoto is a highly skilled hand-to-hand combatant, using a combination of martial arts and his Azure powers to fight. He is agile and quick, capable of delivering rapid strikes and powerful finishing moves.

Limit: His skills are effective but not on the level of some of the more experienced fighters in the BlazBlue universe.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Naoto is a young man with short, dark hair and sharp eyes. He typically wears a black and white uniform-like outfit with a high collar, often accompanied by a long coat. His design is more grounded compared to other BlazBlue characters, reflecting his origins in a modern-day setting.

Symbols — He is associated with the concept of 'Bloodedge' and the power of the Azure, often depicted with a glowing blue aura when using his abilities.

Domains — He originates from a world similar to our own, but he is drawn into the BlazBlue world's conflicts, particularly the events surrounding the Azure and the time loops.

Accounts I
Bloodedge ExperienceBlazBlue: Bloodedge Experience (visual novel)

2014

Naoto Kurogane is a young man living in a world where supernatural events occur. He is drawn into a conflict involving the Azure and the time loops that plague the BlazBlue universe. He encounters characters from the main series and must confront his own past, including the death of his sister, in order to save the world. Naoto's actions help to resolve some of the mysteries of the Azure and set the stage for his appearance in Central Fiction.

Accounts II
Central Fiction Arcade ModeBlazBlue: Central Fiction (video game)

2016

In his arcade mode, Naoto is drawn into the events of Central Fiction, fighting against various characters and eventually confronting the main antagonist, Yuuki Terumi. He uses his Azure powers to aid the heroes in their fight to break the time loop. Naoto proves himself as a valuable ally and helps to secure a better future for the world.

The Approach

Naoto Kurogane is a young man who wields a power that defies the very laws of the world—a power that draws him into a conflict far larger than himself, where the boundaries between life and death, past and future, are blurred.

Content advisory. None
Identity

Naoto Kurogane

Pronunciation nah-oh-toh koo-roh-gah-neh

Etymology

The surname 'Kurogane' (鉄) means 'iron' in Japanese, possibly reflecting his strong will and combat prowess. The given name 'Naoto' (ナオト) is a common Japanese name, but its specific meaning is not officially stated.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese video game franchise, created by Arc System Works.

Disputed Identifications

None known. He is distinct from other characters with similar names in other franchises.

Description

Appearance

Naoto is a young man with short, dark hair and sharp eyes. He typically wears a black and white uniform-like outfit with a high collar, often accompanied by a long coat. His design is more grounded compared to other BlazBlue characters, reflecting his origins in a modern-day setting.

Symbols & Iconography

He is associated with the concept of 'Bloodedge' and the power of the Azure, often depicted with a glowing blue aura when using his abilities.

Behaviour & Personality

Naoto is initially portrayed as a cynical and somewhat jaded individual, burdened by his past and the loss of his sister. He is fiercely protective of those he cares about and is willing to go to great lengths to protect them, even if it means risking his own life. He has a dry sense of humor and a tendency to act impulsively, but he is ultimately driven by a strong sense of justice.

Domains & Habitats

He originates from a world similar to our own, but he is drawn into the BlazBlue world's conflicts, particularly the events surrounding the Azure and the time loops.

Version Differences

In 'Bloodedge Experience', he is the protagonist and his story is explored in depth. In 'Central Fiction', he appears as a playable character, and his personality is somewhat lighter, but his core traits remain consistent.

Powers & Abilities

Azure Grimoire (fragment)

Source Version BlazBlue: Bloodedge Experience, BlazBlue: Central Fiction

Description

Naoto possesses a fragment of the Azure, which grants him extraordinary power and the ability to manipulate the flow of battle. This manifests as a blue flame-like energy that enhances his physical abilities and allows him to perform powerful attacks.

Limitations

The power is unstable and can be overwhelming if not controlled. It also attracts the attention of those who seek the Azure.

Contradictions

None known.

Combat Skills

Source Version BlazBlue: Central Fiction

Description

Naoto is a highly skilled hand-to-hand combatant, using a combination of martial arts and his Azure powers to fight. He is agile and quick, capable of delivering rapid strikes and powerful finishing moves.

Limitations

His skills are effective but not on the level of some of the more experienced fighters in the BlazBlue universe.

Contradictions

None known.

Bloodedge Experience

Source BlazBlue: Bloodedge Experience (visual novel)Culture JapanesePeriod 2014

Summary

Naoto Kurogane is a young man living in a world where supernatural events occur. He is drawn into a conflict involving the Azure and the time loops that plague the BlazBlue universe. He encounters characters from the main series and must confront his own past, including the death of his sister, in order to save the world.

Contradictions & Disputes

Canonical status of Bloodedge Experience

Position A

Some fans consider the visual novel to be a side story and not fully canon to the main series.

Position A — evidence

The visual novel was released as a spin-off and has a different tone.

Position B

Others argue that since Naoto appears in Central Fiction, the events of the visual novel are canonical.

Position B — evidence

Naoto's inclusion in the main game suggests official recognition of the visual novel's events.

Scholarly Consensus

The general consensus is that the visual novel is canon, as it provides backstory for a character who appears in the main series.

Who It Is

In the empty Castle Town that has waited without sunlight for longer than anyone can remember, a single prince has been keeping the lights on, sweeping the floors, baking cakes for guests who never arrived — and when two frightened Lightners finally tumble through the closet darkness, Ralsei greets them like he has rehearsed this kindness his whole lonely life.

Attested personality across Chapters 1-4: unfailingly kind, gentle, self-effacing, eager to please, and anxious about being disliked or abandoned. Introduces himself as 'Prince of the Dark' who has waited alone for the Lightners foretold by legend. Acts as tutorial guide, healer, and mediator — especially trying to befriend and calm Susie. Bakes cakes, offers hugs, writes a detailed manual on how to be a good friend. Shows acute loneliness: Castle Town is empty at start except for him; he admits he has never had friends before. Toby Fox has never given an in-universe etymology; fandom and press universally note the anagram as intentional parallel, not coincidence. 'Ralsei' has no known real-world linguistic root. Powers
Heal Prayer

Signature healing magic. Ralsei prays and restores HP to a single ally (or in later chapters, party-wide with upgraded TP cost). Learned innately as Dark Prince; taught to Kris/Susie as example of ACT-based mercy. Visual: green heart particles.

Limit: Costs TP (Tension Points, 32% base, later 40% for group). Cannot heal if TP insufficient. Does not revive downed allies beyond HP restoration. Less effective if Ralsei is downed.

Pacify

Puts a TIRED enemy to sleep, effectively sparing them without reducing HP to zero. Used to end battles pacifistically after ACTs tire foes. Core to Ralsei's pacifist role.

Limit: Only works on enemies with TIRED status (achieved via ACTs). Costs 16% TP. Fails on non-tired or boss-immune targets. Does not work on Light World enemies (no battles there).

FluffyGuard / Dual Heal

In Chapter 2 onward, Ralsei can cast a fluffy cotton-like guard that slightly reduces damage, and Dual Heal (co-cast). In some translations listed as 'Heal Prayer' upgrades. Provides party support rather than offense.

Limit: TP cost, single-turn duration. Defensive, not offensive.

Inter-Dark World Travel

Unique ability among Darkners to travel between different Dark Worlds without petrifying. Explained as due to being from the pure Dark Fountain in Castle Town.

Limit: Attested but not fully explained; Ralsei shows fatigue after travel in later chapters. Cannot travel to Light World. Lore implication that this makes him anomalous among Darkners.

Manual Writing, Baking, Hospitality Knowledge

Non-combat abilities: authored a comprehensive manual on Dark World mechanics, ACTs, and friendship; skilled baker (cakes for party), tea maker, and castle caretaker. High social-emotional intelligence.

Limit: Manual is incomplete and sometimes naive (Susie mocks it). Baking has no combat effect except healing items.

Prophetic Knowledge

Possesses knowledge of the Legend / Prophecy of the Deltarune (the mural, the Roar, the three heroes, the Angel's Heaven). Knows more than he initially shares.

Limit: Self-imposed withholding; knowledge appears incomplete or selective. He becomes evasive when pressed on the ending where heroes must sacrifice. Whether he knows the full ending is disputed in-text.

Accounts I
The Legend of the Dark WorldDeltarune Chapter 1 - Ralsei's exposition and Castle Town mural

2018 (in-universe timeless legend)

Alone in Castle Town, Ralsei recounts the ancient Legend: when Light and Dark were in balance, the Deltarune prophecy foretold that when Dark Fountains fill the sky, three heroes — a Human, a Monster, and a Prince from the Dark — will seal the fountains and banish the Angel's Heaven, preventing the Roar (eternal darkness). He shows Kris and Susie the mural depicting the heroes and explains their role. This frames the entire quest. Ralsei has waited his whole life for Kris and Susie to arrive to fulfill it. Establishes central quest: seal Dark Fountains. Motivates journey to Card Castle and all subsequent chapters. Introduces Roar as apocalyptic stakes.

Chapter 1: The Card Kingdom and the Spade KingDeltarune Chapter 1

2018

After meeting in Castle Town, Ralsei joins Kris and Susie to seal the Eastern Dark Fountain created in the school's Card Kingdom. He tutors them in ACTing, sparing, and puzzle solving, befriends Lancer, and mediates Susie's violent impulses. At Card Castle they confront King (Spade King), who rejects the prophecy and seeks to cover the world in darkness. Ralsei heals and pacifies, and with Susie's newfound willingness to spare, they defeat King non-lethally and seal the fountain, returning to the Light World. Ralsei stays behind, waving goodbye, promising to wait. First fountain sealed; Lancer and other Card Kingdom Darkners later migrate to Castle Town; Ralsei's friendship with Susie solidified; establishes pacifist ideal.

Chapter 2: The Cyber World and QueenDeltarune Chapter 2

2021

A new Dark Fountain in the computer lab creates the Cyber World ruled by Queen. Ralsei reunites with Kris and Susie (and Noelle) and explains that any Darkner taken to another Dark World will petrify except those from a pure fountain like himself — explaining why he can travel. He supports the party through Cyber City, teaches Noelle healing, and helps recruit Darkners. In Queen's mansion, Ralsei's pacifism is tested by Queen's plan to make Noelle her peon and cover the world in darkness. After Berdly's arc and Queen's defeat, they seal the fountain. Ralsei expands Castle Town with new recruits and builds rooms for his friends. Second fountain sealed; Castle Town becomes a thriving town; Ralsei demonstrates inter-world travel anomaly; Noelle learns Heal Prayer; Spamton lore deepens Knight mystery.

Accounts II
Chapter 3: The TV World and TennaDeltarune Chapter 3

2025

A Dark Fountain in the Dreemurr household living room (old TV) creates a grotesque TV World ruled by Tenna, a once-beloved TV Darkner. Ralsei accompanies Kris and Susie through game-show horrors and distorted nostalgia. He continues to heal and mediate, but shows increased anxiety about the Knight's movements and Toriel's involvement. Tenna, desperate for attention, traps the party; Ralsei helps Susie reach Toriel and Undyne who have been drawn into the Dark World. After Tenna's defeat and redemption, the fountain is sealed. Ralsei is visibly shaken by the Knight's near-kidnapping of Toriel in the Light World interlude. Third fountain sealed; Light World stakes escalate (Knight targeting Dreemurrs); Ralsei's knowledge gaps become more apparent to Susie.

Chapter 4: The Dark Sanctuary and the Titan ProphecyDeltarune Chapter 4

2025

The Dark Fountain in the church creates the Dark Sanctuary, a cathedral-like Dark World. Here the fullest version of the prophecy is revealed via stained glass and Ralsei's recitation, including the ominous final verses: that the heroes must make a sacrifice and that the Roar will bring Titans of darkness. Ralsei is forced to admit he had hidden the ending because he feared Susie and Kris would despair. Susie confronts him about his secrecy, leading to the most serious rift in their friendship. Despite this, Ralsei helps seal the fountain and confronts the Titan spawn. The chapter ends with Asgore being kidnapped by the Knight in the Light World, and Ralsei resolving to be more honest. Prophecy's dark ending revealed; Ralsei's secret-keeping damages trust but ultimately strengthens resolve to defy fate; Light World kidnapping cliffhanger.

Castle Town Interludes — The Lonely Prince's WaitDeltarune Chapters 1-4 Castle Town hub

2018-2025 (ongoing)

Between fountain quests, Ralsei lives alone in Castle Town, sweeping, baking, and preparing rooms for friends who have not yet arrived. When Darkners are recruited, he welcomes them and builds amenities (café, dojo). He offers tea readings (flavor text predicting futures), hugs, and manual consultations. These quiet scenes establish his core wound: a prince without subjects, whose purpose is entirely defined by waiting for others. His joy when Castle Town fills is attested as genuine, and his fear of being left alone again underlies his reluctance to reveal the prophecy's cost. World-building and emotional core; explains why Ralsei clings to pacifism and friendship; sets up thematic tension between selfless service and self-erasure.

Ralsei is just Asriel in the Dark World. — Primary text explicitly separates them: Asriel is Kris's older brother in Hometown (Light World, at college, referenced by Toriel, Undyne, and others) who has not appeared in Dark World; Ralsei is a Darkner prince who exists only in Dark Worlds and has never been to Hometown. They share species archetype and anagram name as thematic parallel, not identity.

Ralsei is a Lightner like Kris and Susie. — Ralsei states he is a Darkner and Prince of the Dark. He cannot exist in the Light World and stays behind when Kris and Susie leave. His ability to travel between Dark Worlds is an exception due to pure fountain, not Lightner status.

Ralsei is evil or the Knight. — No primary evidence supports this. Ralsei consistently heals, pacifies, and opposes the Knight's fountains. His secrecy in Chapter 4 is framed as fear, not villainy, and he is distressed when confronted. The Knight's identity remains unknown through Chapter 4, but Ralsei is with the party when Knight acts in Light World.

Ralsei's hatless fluffy form is his 'true' form and hatted form is a disguise to hide identity. — Both are true forms; he wears the hat initially out of shyness and removes it after Susie encourages him. He continues to wear hat in some sprites and can put it on/off. No lore indicates disguise; it's characterization (shyness → comfort).

Ralsei can use offensive magic like Susie's Rude Buster. — Attested moveset is purely supportive: Heal Prayer, Pacify, FluffyGuard/Dual Heal. He has no offensive spell. This is intentional to contrast Susie's offense and enforce pacifist role.

Who It Is

Liễu Hạnh is one of the Four Immortals and the supreme goddess of the Đạo Mẫu religion. Originally a celestial princess exiled for breaking a jade cup, she was born as a mortal, died young, but returned repeatedly as a wandering fairy, poet, and shopkeeper. Her temples host hầu đồng spirit mediumship rituals with elaborate costumes and sacred dances.

Attested in Oral tradition / Đạo Mẫu religionEra Lê dynasty (c. 16th century) to present

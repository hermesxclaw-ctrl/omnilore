Identity

Feng Min

Pronunciation The pronunciation is not officially provided, but based on Mandarin Chinese, it would be approximately 'Fung Min' with the 'Feng' rhyming with 'sung' and 'Min' rhyming with 'sin'.

Etymology

The name 'Feng Min' is of Chinese origin. 'Feng' (风) can mean 'wind' or 'phoenix', and 'Min' (敏) can mean 'quick' or 'clever'. The name is presented in the game as a Chinese name, and the character is depicted as Chinese.

Cultural Ownership

The character is a creation of Behaviour Interactive, a Canadian video game company. She is a fictional character in the game Dead by Daylight. Her cultural background is Chinese, but she is not a traditional folklore figure; she is a modern fictional character.

Disputed Identifications

There are no known disputes about the character's identity. She is a unique character in the Dead by Daylight universe and is not based on any real person or traditional mythological figure.

Description

Appearance

Feng Min is depicted as a young Asian woman with a slim build. She has dark hair, typically styled in a ponytail or with bangs. Her default outfit consists of a jacket, a t-shirt, and jeans, often with a backpack. She wears a determined expression. Her appearance varies with different cosmetic outfits available in the game.

Symbols & Iconography

Feng Min is associated with the color orange, which appears in her default jacket and some of her cosmetics. She is also associated with gaming, as her backstory involves esports. Her iconic item is a flashlight, which she can use to blind the killer.

Behaviour & Personality

Feng Min is portrayed as a determined and resourceful survivor. She is quick-witted and uses her intelligence to outsmart the killer. Her in-game perks reflect her personality: 'Technician' (she is good with machines), 'Lithe' (she is agile and can vault quickly), and 'Alert' (she is observant and can see the killer's actions). She is not depicted as a fighter, but as a survivor who relies on stealth and cleverness.

Domains & Habitats

Feng Min is a survivor in the realm of the Entity, a nightmarish dimension where she is forced to participate in trials. She is not associated with any specific realm; she appears in various maps within the game.

Version Differences

There are no significant version differences for Feng Min. She has received cosmetic outfits and lore updates, but her core backstory and abilities have remained consistent.

Feng Min's Backstory

Source Dead by Daylight official lore (in-game and website)Culture Modern, global gaming culturePeriod Contemporary (game released 2016, character added 2017)

Summary

Feng Min was a competitive gamer, a rising star in the esports scene. After a humiliating defeat in a major tournament, she felt the need to clear her head and went out for a walk. During this walk, she was abducted by the Entity, a malevolent force that traps victims in a nightmarish realm. She now must survive trials against a killer, using her wits and skills to escape and help other survivors.

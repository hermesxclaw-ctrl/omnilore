Who It Is

In the neon-lit streets of a city that never sleeps, Feng Min emerges not as a warrior with a blade, but as a survivor whose greatest weapon is her mind. Her story is not one of epic battles, but of quiet defiance against an inescapable nightmare.

Feng Min is portrayed as a determined and resourceful survivor. She is quick-witted and uses her intelligence to outsmart the killer. Her in-game perks reflect her personality: 'Technician' (she is good with machines), 'Lithe' (she is agile and can vault quickly), and 'Alert' (she is observant and can see the killer's actions). She is not depicted as a fighter, but as a survivor who relies on stealth and cleverness.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Feng Min' is of Chinese origin. 'Feng' (风) can mean 'wind' or 'phoenix', and 'Min' (敏) can mean 'quick' or 'clever'. The name is presented in the game as a Chinese name, and the character is depicted as Chinese.

Pronunciation — The pronunciation is not officially provided, but based on Mandarin Chinese, it would be approximately 'Fung Min' with the 'Feng' rhyming with 'sung' and 'Min' rhyming with 'sin'.

Powers
Technician

Feng Min is skilled with machinery and can repair generators more quietly, reducing the noise radius when working on them. She also has a chance to avoid the generator exploding when failing a skill check.

Limit: The perk only affects generator repair; it does not grant other mechanical skills.

Lithe

After performing a rushed vault, Feng Min gains a burst of speed for a short duration.

Limit: The perk requires a rushed vault to activate, and the speed boost is temporary.

Alert

Feng Min can see the killer's aura when they perform a break action, such as breaking pallets or damaging generators.

Limit: The perk only activates when the killer performs a break action, and the aura reading is limited in range.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Feng Min is depicted as a young Asian woman with a slim build. She has dark hair, typically styled in a ponytail or with bangs. Her default outfit consists of a jacket, a t-shirt, and jeans, often with a backpack. She wears a determined expression. Her appearance varies with different cosmetic outfits available in the game.

Symbols — Feng Min is associated with the color orange, which appears in her default jacket and some of her cosmetics. She is also associated with gaming, as her backstory involves esports. Her iconic item is a flashlight, which she can use to blind the killer.

Domains — Feng Min is a survivor in the realm of the Entity, a nightmarish dimension where she is forced to participate in trials. She is not associated with any specific realm; she appears in various maps within the game.

Feng Min's BackstoryDead by Daylight official lore (in-game and website)

Contemporary (game released 2016, character added 2017)

Feng Min was a competitive gamer, a rising star in the esports scene. After a humiliating defeat in a major tournament, she felt the need to clear her head and went out for a walk. During this walk, she was abducted by the Entity, a malevolent force that traps victims in a nightmarish realm. She now must survive trials against a killer, using her wits and skills to escape and help other survivors. Feng Min is trapped in the Entity's realm, forced to participate in endless trials. She has not escaped, and her fate is uncertain.

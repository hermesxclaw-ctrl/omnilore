Who It Is
Form

In Pacific Northwest Coast mythology, the Raven is often depicted as a large black bird with a distinctive call and a mischievous glint in its eye. It is said to be a master of transformation and shape-shifting, able to take on many different forms and guises.

Powers

Transformation The ability to take on many different forms and guises, allowing the Raven to adapt to any situation and outwit its enemies.

Accounts

The Raven is said to have created the sun by stealing it from the underworld and bringing it to the world above.

The Raven is said to have rescued children from a flood by carrying them on his back and bringing them to safety.

The Approach

In the chaotic world of Mirai Nikki, where twelve diary holders fight for godhood, a seemingly ordinary child holds the key to the past and future of the most dangerous contender. Ushio Gasai, the son of Yuno Gasai and Yukiteru Amano from the First World, is a silent witness to his mother's tragic fate and a catalyst for the series' most shocking revelation.

Content advisory. Contains references to child abuse, psychological trauma, murder, and suicide.
Identity

Ushio Gasai

Pronunciation OO-shee-oh Gah-sah-ee

Etymology

The name 'Ushio' (潮) can mean 'tide' or 'current' in Japanese, possibly symbolizing the flow of time and fate. The surname 'Gasai' (我妻) is inherited from his mother, Yuno.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese pop culture, specifically the manga and anime series Future Diary (Mirai Nikki) created by Sakae Esuno.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans speculate that Ushio might be a reincarnation or parallel version of Yukiteru, but this is not supported by canon.

Description

Appearance

Ushio is a young boy with light brown or blond hair and blue eyes, resembling his father Yukiteru. He is often seen wearing simple, casual clothes appropriate for his age.

Symbols & Iconography

Ushio is associated with the symbols of the Gasai family, particularly the theme of obsession and the future diary. His presence often signifies the tragic consequences of the diary game.

Behaviour & Personality

Ushio is portrayed as a quiet and observant child. He appears to be emotionally distant, possibly due to the traumatic environment of his upbringing. He shows a deep attachment to his mother, Yuno, despite her instability.

Domains & Habitats

He resides in the First World, in a home with his parents, Yukiteru and Yuno. After Yuno's death, he is left in the care of his father, but the details are not explored.

Version Differences

In the manga, Ushio appears in a flashback sequence, while in the anime, his appearance is slightly extended. There are no significant differences in his characterization between the two versions.

Stories & Myths

The Tragic Family of the First World

Source Future Diary manga volume 10, chapter 54; anime episode 26Culture JapanesePeriod Set in the First World, before the start of the Diary Game

Summary

Ushio is shown as a young child living with his mother Yuno and father Yukiteru. Yuno, still mentally unstable from her traumatic past, becomes increasingly paranoid and abusive towards Ushio, whom she sees as a rival for Yuki's affection. One day, Yuno's obsession escalates, leading her to kill Ushio in a fit of madness, an act that ultimately drives Yukiteru to despair and sets the stage for the series' events.

Description

Ushio is subjected to physical and emotional abuse by his mother, Yuno, who eventually kills him. This act is depicted as a tragic consequence of Yuno's severe mental illness and obsession with Yukiteru.

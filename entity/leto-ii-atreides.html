Who It Is

Leto Atreides II is arguably the most radical character in science fiction literature — the son of Paul Atreides who, at the age of nine, makes the terrible choice to merge his human body with sandtrout (larval sandworms of Arrakis) to become an immortal, pre-human, pre-worm hybrid, the God Emperor, who will rule the known universe as an absolute tyrant for 3,500 years. His goal, which he calls the 'Golden Path,' is the only course of action his prescient vision reveals that will prevent the total extinction of humanity. The Golden Path requires Leto to become the most oppressive tyrant in human history — a predator-god who crushes all rebellion, suppresses space travel, enforces total stagnation, and teaches humanity such a profound lesson about the dangers of centralized authority and charismatic leadership that they will never again allow a single ruler to dominate them. After 3,500 years of his Peace, humanity will scatter so far across the universe that no single disaster — and no single tyrant — can ever threaten the species again. Leto's transformation is physical as well as political: over the centuries, his body becomes a massive worm-form, his human face and arms barely distinguishable on the anterior segment of a pre-worm body. He loses the ability to experience human emotions except through his ancestral memories; within his consciousness, he hosts an internal council of all his ancestors who offer competing counsel. Despite his monstrous form and methods, Leto is arguably the most self-sacrificing figure in the Dune canon: he willingly surrenders his humanity, his capacity for love, and ultimately his life, to ensure human survival. His tyranny is horrifying — he maintains a fanatical all-female army (the Fish Speakers), suppresses the Spacing Guild and Bene Gesserit, and crushes all dissent — yet the reader is continually reminded that it is, by his prescient knowledge, the only path that avoids extinction. In God Emperor of Dune, Leto's final plan comes to fruition: he engineers his own assassination at the hands of Siona Atreides (a descendant invisible to prescience) and Duncan Idaho (the eternal ghola who represents unreconstructed humanity). His death releases the sandtrout from his body to begin the spice cycle anew on Arrakis, and his legacy is humanity's Scattering — the diaspora that ensures the species' survival. Leto II is perhaps the most morally complex 'god' in modern fiction: a tyrant whose despotism is genuinely necessary, a monster who chooses monstrosity for love of humanity, a god who dies to free his people from the need for gods.

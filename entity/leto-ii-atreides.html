Who It Is
Form

Leto II Atreides is a humanoid figure with a worm-like body, the result of his hybridization with a sandworm. He has a distinctive appearance, with a long, slender body and a pair of arms and legs. His face is a mixture of human and worm features, with a distinctive crest on his forehead.

Powers

Sandworm Control Leto II Atreides has the ability to control and manipulate the sandworms, which he uses to control and manipulate the natural world. He can summon sandworms from the depths of the desert, and use them to attack his enemies or defend his allies.

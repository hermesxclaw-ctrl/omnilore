Who It Is
Form

Mephistopheles is often depicted as a tall, thin figure with a pointed hat and a long, black coat. He is sometimes shown with horns, a tail, or other demonic features.

Powers

The Power of Persuasion Mephistopheles has the ability to persuade and manipulate others, using his charm and cunning to achieve his goals.

Accounts

The story of Faust, a scholar who sells his soul to Mephistopheles in exchange for knowledge and power.

A retelling of the Faust legend, with Mephistopheles as a central character and Faust as a complex and nuanced protagonist.

The Approach

Before Lara Croft ever picked up a pair of pistols, there was her father—a man whose obsession with the supernatural would shape his daughter's destiny and echo through generations of tomb raiding. Lord Richard Croft, the archaeologist-nobleman of the Croft family, remains one of the most enigmatic figures in the Tomb Raider mythos, a character defined as much by what the games leave unsaid as by what they reveal.

Content advisory. Parental death, abandonment, hints of mental instability, colonialist undertones in archaeology, supernatural themes.
Identity

Lord Richard Croft

Pronunciation /lɔːrd ˈrɪtʃərd krɒft/

Known As

Richard CroftLord CroftFather (by Lara)

Etymology

The surname 'Croft' is of Old English origin, meaning 'enclosed field' or 'small farm'. The title 'Lord' indicates his noble status within the British peerage.

Cultural Ownership

British, with strong ties to the English aristocracy and the tradition of gentleman archaeologists.

Disputed Identifications

There is no real-world counterpart; Richard is a fictional creation. However, fans have drawn parallels to real-life adventurers like Sir Richard Francis Burton and Lord Carnarvon.

Description

Appearance

In the Legend trilogy, Richard is depicted as a distinguished British gentleman with graying hair, often wearing a tweed suit or academic attire. In the 2013 reboot timeline, he is shown in photographs as a younger man with dark hair, similar to Lara.

Symbols & Iconography

His study is filled with artifacts, books, and maps, symbolizing his scholarly pursuits. He is often associated with the Croft Manor and its secret passages.

Behaviour & Personality

Richard is portrayed as an obsessive and driven archaeologist, willing to risk everything for discovery. He is deeply caring towards Lara but also secretive, often hiding his dangerous work from her. His obsession with the supernatural and the lost city of Avalon ultimately leads to his downfall.

Domains & Habitats

Primarily associated with Croft Manor in Surrey, England, but his work takes him to various global locations, including Nepal and the Mediterranean.

Version Differences

In the Legend trilogy, Richard is a central figure whose death drives Lara's quest. In the 2013 reboot, he is a background presence, having died when Lara was young, but his influence is still felt. The original Core Design games do not feature him as a character.

Stories & Myths

The Quest for Avalon

Source Tomb Raider: Legend, UnderworldCulture BritishPeriod Contemporary (fictional)

Summary

Richard Croft becomes obsessed with finding the mythical Avalon, believing it to be a source of great power. He discovers a sword that he believes is Excalibur and a stone that leads him on a global search. His investigation brings him into conflict with the mysterious Amanda Evert and the ancient order of the Knights of the Holy Sepulchre. Ultimately, he is killed in Nepal while searching for the lost city, leaving Lara to uncover the truth.

Participants

Richard Croft

Lara Croft (as a child)

Amanda Evert

Anaya Imanu

Consequences

Richard's death becomes the driving force for Lara's adventures. She later discovers that he may have been searching for Avalon not for power, but to find a way to resurrect her mother, who disappeared in a mysterious incident.

Variants

In the 2013 reboot, Richard's death occurs off-screen, and his research is mentioned but not central to the plot.

The Loss of Amelia

Source Tomb Raider: UnderworldCulture BritishPeriod Contemporary (fictional)

Summary

Richard's wife, Amelia, vanishes while studying an ancient artifact in Nepal. Richard becomes convinced that she is trapped in Avalon and dedicates his life to finding a way to reach her. This obsession leads him to make a deal with the dark entity known as the 'Shadow Queen', but he ultimately fails and dies.

Participants

Richard Croft

Amelia Croft

Lara Croft

Consequences

Lara is left an orphan, and the mystery of her mother's disappearance fuels her lifelong quest. Richard's actions also set the stage for the events of Underworld, where Lara confronts the Shadow Queen.

Variants

This storyline is unique to the Legend trilogy; the reboot timeline does not feature Amelia.

Lara Croft (daughter)

Type familySource Tomb Raider: Legend, Underworld

Description

Richard is a loving but distant father, often away on expeditions. He encourages Lara's curiosity and intellect, giving her a globe and teaching her about archaeology. His death has a profound impact on Lara, shaping her into the adventurer she becomes.

Version Note

In the reboot, Richard dies when Lara is young, and his influence is more subtle, with Lara seeking to honor his legacy.

Parental neglect

Source Tomb Raider: Legend

Description

Richard's obsession with his work often leads him to leave Lara in the care of others, and he is frequently absent during her childhood.

Scholarly Context

This is a common trope in adventure fiction, where the protagonist's parent is absent due to a higher calling.

Colonialist undertones

Source Tomb Raider franchise

Description

Richard's archaeological expeditions often take place in non-Western countries, and he is depicted as a 'gentleman adventurer' who extracts artifacts without much regard for local cultures.

Scholarly Context

This reflects a critique of real-world archaeology's colonial history, though the games do not explicitly address this.

Research Scope

This dossier focuses on the character Lord Richard Croft as depicted in the Tomb Raider franchise, primarily the Legend trilogy (Tomb Raider: Legend, Anniversary, Underworld), the 2013 reboot timeline, and supplementary materials such as comics and novels. It does not cover the original Core Design era, where Richard is largely absent, nor the 2018 film adaptation, which features a different father figure.

Who It Is

Before Lara Croft ever picked up a pair of pistols, there was her father—a man whose obsession with the supernatural would shape his daughter's destiny and echo through generations of tomb raiding. Lord Richard Croft, the archaeologist-nobleman of the Croft family, remains one of the most enigmatic figures in the Tomb Raider mythos, a character defined as much by what the games leave unsaid as by what they reveal.

Richard is portrayed as an obsessive and driven archaeologist, willing to risk everything for discovery. He is deeply caring towards Lara but also secretive, often hiding his dangerous work from her. His obsession with the supernatural and the lost city of Avalon ultimately leads to his downfall.

Powers
Archaeological Expertise

Richard is a highly knowledgeable archaeologist, able to decipher ancient languages and navigate complex ruins.

Limit: His knowledge is primarily theoretical; he lacks the combat skills of his daughter.

Noble Influence

As a Lord, he has access to resources and social connections that aid his expeditions.

Limit: His status does not protect him from the dangers of his work.

Obsessive Determination

His relentless pursuit of the supernatural, particularly the legend of Avalon, allows him to overcome obstacles but also leads to his demise.

Limit: His obsession blinds him to personal risks and family responsibilities.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In the Legend trilogy, Richard is depicted as a distinguished British gentleman with graying hair, often wearing a tweed suit or academic attire. In the 2013 reboot timeline, he is shown in photographs as a younger man with dark hair, similar to Lara.

Symbols — His study is filled with artifacts, books, and maps, symbolizing his scholarly pursuits. He is often associated with the Croft Manor and its secret passages.

Domains — Primarily associated with Croft Manor in Surrey, England, but his work takes him to various global locations, including Nepal and the Mediterranean.

Accounts I
The Quest for AvalonTomb Raider: Legend, Underworld

Contemporary (fictional)

Richard Croft becomes obsessed with finding the mythical Avalon, believing it to be a source of great power. He discovers a sword that he believes is Excalibur and a stone that leads him on a global search. His investigation brings him into conflict with the mysterious Amanda Evert and the ancient order of the Knights of the Holy Sepulchre. Ultimately, he is killed in Nepal while searching for the lost city, leaving Lara to uncover the truth. Richard's death becomes the driving force for Lara's adventures. She later discovers that he may have been searching for Avalon not for power, but to find a way to resurrect her mother, who disappeared in a mysterious incident.

Accounts II
The Loss of AmeliaTomb Raider: Underworld

Contemporary (fictional)

Richard's wife, Amelia, vanishes while studying an ancient artifact in Nepal. Richard becomes convinced that she is trapped in Avalon and dedicates his life to finding a way to reach her. This obsession leads him to make a deal with the dark entity known as the 'Shadow Queen', but he ultimately fails and dies. Lara is left an orphan, and the mystery of her mother's disappearance fuels her lifelong quest. Richard's actions also set the stage for the events of Underworld, where Lara confronts the Shadow Queen.

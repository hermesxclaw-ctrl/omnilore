Who It Is
Form

Brigid is often depicted as a woman with a flame or a torch, symbolizing her association with fire and light. She is also associated with poetry, music, and healing.

Powers

Poetry and Music Brigid is the patron goddess of poetry and music in Irish mythology. She is said to have the power to inspire creativity and to bring people together through the power of art.

Healing Brigid is associated with healing and is said to have the power to cure physical and emotional ailments.

Fire and Light Brigid is associated with fire and light, and is said to have the power to bring warmth and illumination to those in need.

Accounts

Brigid is born to the Dagda and is said to have been given a flame by her father to light her way.

Brigid is said to have inspired the poets of Ireland to create great works of art, and is often depicted as a patron goddess of poetry and music.

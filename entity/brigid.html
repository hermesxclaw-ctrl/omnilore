Who It Is

Brigid is a triple goddess of poetry, smithcraft, and healing, daughter of the Dagda. Her festival Imbolc marks spring's arrival. An eternal flame was maintained by nineteen priestesses at Kildare. When Christianity arrived, her cult was so powerful she was syncretized into Saint Brigid, one of Ireland's three patron saints.

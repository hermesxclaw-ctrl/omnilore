Who It Is
Form

Azazel is often depicted as a fallen angel or a demonic figure, associated with the wilderness and the scapegoat ritual.

Powers

Teacher of Forbidden Arts Azazel is said to have taught humanity about the art of war, armament, and other forbidden concepts.

Accounts

The story of the fall of the Watchers, including Azazel, who taught humanity about forbidden arts and were subsequently punished.

The story of the scapegoat ritual, where a goat bearing the sins of the Jews is sent into the wilderness, associated with Azazel.

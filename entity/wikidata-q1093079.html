Who It Is

If Class 3-A kept a tabloid, Haruna Saotome would be its editor, art director, and chief scandal-monger — a ponytailed manga otaku who turns gossip into golems and doodles into an army, all while cheerfully narrating everyone else's love life as if it were her own serialized comedy.

Canon personality — cheerful, talkative, irrepressibly nosy, and self-identified as a 'corrupt' fujoshi/manga otaku. Akamatsu writes her as the class's information broker and rumor mill: she collects love confessions, peeps, and secrets and redistributes them with editorial commentary. Loyal to friends, especially fellow Library Exploration Club members Nodoka Miyazaki and Yue Ayase, but teases them mercilessly about their crushes on Negi. Powers
Manga Creation & Visual Storytelling (Mundane Skill)

Expert amateur manga artist; draws doujinshi at semi-professional level, fast drafting, understands paneling, inking, and character design. Used for income, propaganda, and later as basis for magical artifact.

Limit: Non-magical; requires time, paper, tools; quality limited by human skill

Pactio: Imperium Graphices (Empire of Graphics)

Provisional contract (Pactio) with Negi Springfield. Artifact is a magical sketchbook + brush/palette. Anything Haruna draws in the sketchbook can be manifested as a semi-real, ink-construct golem/creature that obeys her commands. Drawings gain mass, movement, and limited abilities based on what is drawn (e.g., giant mecha, dragon, army of chibis). In battle, she summons cartoonish but physically effective constructs to attack, defend, or distract.

Limit: Requires drawing time (seconds to minutes); constructs are ink-based and vulnerable to fire, water, erasure magic, and strong dispelling; complexity and size limited by Haruna's mana and artistic stamina; constructs lack true sentience and follow simple orders; mana consumption causes fatigue

Golem Legion / Mass Summon

Advanced application of Imperium Graphices: can rapidly sketch multiple smaller golems (e.g., dozens of chibi Harunas or ink soldiers) for swarm tactics, reconnaissance, or comic relief. Used during Mahora Festival defense and Magic World battles.

Limit: Mass summons are individually weak; dispelled en masse by area magic; Haruna must remain concentrated or they dissolve

Information Gathering & Social Intelligence

Not magical but treated as ability: exceptional gossip network, memory for secrets, ability to read romantic tensions and blackmail. Often serves as tactician by knowing classmates' abilities and weaknesses.

Limit: Information may be inaccurate or embellished; relies on eavesdropping

Artifact: Sarcophagus of Ink (Fan nickname, not official)

Some English fandom mislabels her artifact as creating 'living manga' — correct name is Imperium Graphices. No separate sarcophagus power.

Limit: N/A

Accounts I
Debut — The Library Exploration Club and the Gossip GirlNegima! Magister Negi Magi manga Vol.2 Ch.14-15; anime Ep.4 (2005)

2003-2004 (in-story: Spring, Negi's first term as 2-A homeroom teacher)

Haruna is introduced as one of the three members of the Library Exploration Club alongside Nodoka Miyazaki and Yue Ayase. While Nodoka is shy and Yue is coolly bookish, Haruna is the loud, teasing mood-maker who drags them into exploring Library Island's depths. She immediately latches onto Negi as gossip fodder, noting his child-teacher status and the girls' crushes. Early chapters establish her running gag: she trades secrets for manga material and tries to peek at Nodoka's diary. She helps Negi navigate the library's traps, showing bravery despite low magic aptitude. Establishes Haruna's core trio and her role as exposition/gossip conduit for the class; sets up later Pactio group dynamics

Kyoto Trip — The Library Trio in DangerManga Vol.4-6 Ch.32-51; anime Ep.14-17 (2005)

2004 (in-story: Summer field trip to Kyoto)

Class 3-A travels to Kyoto. Haruna, Nodoka and Yue are targeted by Kansai mages who mistake them for Negi's allies. Haruna shows loyalty by refusing to abandon Nodoka when she is captured by the demon Inugami Kotaro's allies, and helps Negi's rescue effort by using her knowledge of Kyoto's streets (gleaned from manga research). Though she has no magic, she distracts enemies with thrown manga manuscripts and bluffs. This arc cements her courage despite being 'normal.' Deepens bond with Negi; foreshadows need for Pactio to protect non-combatant friends

Mahora Festival — Pactio Formation: Imperium GraphicesManga Vol.16-17 Ch.149-150, Ch.160-165

2005-2006 publication (in-story: Mahora Academy Festival, Autumn)

During the massive Mahora Festival arc, Negi is forced to form Pactios with many students to defend the school from invasion. Haruna, feeling left out as an 'ordinary' girl while Yue and Nodoka have already formed contracts, actively volunteers and even pressures Negi with comedic guilt-tripping. The contract ritual (kiss) is played for laughs — Haruna treats it as manga research. She receives Imperium Graphices, a magical sketchbook. She immediately tests it by drawing a giant robot that briefly holds off a demon, then collapses from mana exhaustion. Her artifact's debut is both comic and genuinely useful, proving even non-mages can contribute. Haruna becomes combat-capable support summoner; completes Library Trio Pactio set; artifact becomes key in later Magic World battles

Accounts II
Journey to Mundus Magicus — The Magic World ArcManga Vol.20-29 Ch.180-286

2007-2010 publication (in-story: Negi's search for his father Nagi in the Magic World)

Haruna stows away with Negi's traveling group to the Magic World (Mundus Magicus), despite not being originally selected. She serves as chronicler, morale officer, and summoner. Key moments: in the desert town of Hellas, she summons ink golems to carry supplies; in Ostia, she draws decoys to infiltrate the tournament; during the battle against Fate Averruncus's forces, she creates a legion of chibi-golems that overwhelm lesser enemies while stronger fighters handle bosses. She also documents the journey in manga form, providing meta-commentary. Her growth from gossip to dependable comrade is highlighted when she risks her sketchbook to save Yue after Yue loses her memory. Haruna proves artifact's strategic value; deepens Yue-Haruna friendship; contributes to Ostia's defense

Final Battle — Defense of Mahora and the Mundus Magicus CollapseManga Vol.33-38 Ch.300-355

2010-2012 publication (in-story: Return to Earth, battle against Mage of the Beginning)

In the climax, the Mage of the Beginning (Life Maker) threatens both worlds. Haruna fights in the defense of Mahora Academy alongside other Pactio holders. She uses Imperium Graphices at full power, drawing a massive ink dragon and later a fleet of flying fortresses to evacuate civilians. When Negi is corrupted by Magia Erebea, Haruna's golems are used to restrain him non-lethally. After the battle, she helps rebuild and is shown in epilogue materials pursuing her dream as a professional manga artist, attending Comiket-like events and publishing doujinshi based on their adventures. Haruna's arc closes from fan to creator; her art literally saves lives; epilogue confirms adult career

Alternate Continuity — Negima!? and the Star Pactio ComedyNegima!? anime Ep.22-24; Negima!? neo manga Ch.32-35

2006-2007

In Shaft's alternate retelling, Haruna's Pactio is reimagined with a magical girl aesthetic. She forms a contract via a star card and summons more chibi, super-deformed ink monsters that break the fourth wall. The story is episodic and comedic, with Haruna hosting 'Haruna's Gossip Corner' eyecatches. While not canon to Akamatsu's manga, it shows how the studio interpreted her as meta-narrator and mascot for otaku culture. Demonstrates Haruna's popularity as comedic narrator; influences later fanon portrayal

Haruna's artifact can bring any fictional character to life at full power — Imperium Graphices creates ink golems that mimic appearance but are limited constructs — they have mass and can fight but are not true resurrections and are vulnerable to erasure, fire, water, and dispelling. They follow simple orders and lack original's full abilities or sentience. Manga shows a drawn dragon is strong but far weaker than a real dragon.

Haruna is the main heroine or Negi's primary love interest — Haruna is supporting cast and explicitly not a primary romantic lead. Main heroines are Asuna Kagurazaka, Nodoka Miyazaki, Yue Ayase, and others with deeper romantic arcs. Haruna's feelings for Negi are platonic/teasing and gossip-driven; she is more interested in observing others' romances for manga material.

Haruna appears and fights in the 2005 Negima! anime's Magic World arc — The 2005 Xebec anime ends before the Magic World arc and never adapts Haruna's Pactio or Magic World battles. Those events are manga-only (and briefly in OVAs). Viewers who only watched 2005 anime miss her entire summoner development.

Haruna is a powerful mage like Evangeline or Negi — Haruna has low innate magical power; her strength comes from creativity with her artifact, not raw mana. Guidebook stats list her magic aptitude as below average, and she tires quickly after large summons. She is a support/utility summoner, not a frontline mage.

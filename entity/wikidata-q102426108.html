Who It Is

In the dystopian corridors of the Public Security Bureau, Nobuchika Ginoza is the enforcer who believes in order until order destroys him. A man who once declared that 'the law is absolute,' he becomes a hunted fugitive, a tragic figure whose rigid adherence to justice is shattered by the very system he served.

Ginoza is initially rigid, by-the-book, and deeply committed to the Sibyl System's authority. He is methodical, disciplined, and often appears cold or detached. He values order and the law above all, believing that the system is infallible. Over time, his experiences—particularly the betrayal of his mentor and the death of his partner—cause him to become more cynical and emotionally conflicted. Powers
Law Enforcement Training

Ginoza is a highly trained Inspector with expertise in criminal investigation, tactical operations, and use of the Dominator. He is skilled in hand-to-hand combat and marksmanship.

Limit: His skills are conventional; he has no superhuman abilities.

Dominator Usage

As an Inspector, Ginoza is authorized to use the Dominator, a smart gun that can paralyze or kill based on the target's Crime Coefficient. He is proficient in its use.

Limit: The Dominator's function is restricted by the Sibyl System; it only works on individuals with a high Crime Coefficient. Ginoza cannot use it against those the system deems non-threatening.

Analytical Skills

Ginoza is a sharp analyst, capable of piecing together complex criminal cases and understanding the psychological profiles of criminals. He is particularly adept at understanding the motivations of people who act against the system.

Limit: His analytical skills are sometimes clouded by his personal biases and emotional state.

Resilience

Ginoza demonstrates significant physical and psychological resilience, surviving traumatic events and continuing to function despite severe stress and personal loss.

Limit: His psychological resilience is tested; he experiences emotional breakdowns and moments of despair.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Ginoza is a tall, lean man with short, dark hair and sharp, often stern features. He typically wears the standard navy-blue Public Security Bureau uniform with a high-collar jacket, white shirt, and tie. He has a distinctive scar on his left cheek that he acquires during the events of the first season. His appearance becomes more haggard in later seasons, reflecting his psychological turmoil.

Symbols — His Dominator (the Bureau's standard-issue firearm) is a symbol of his authority. His uniform and badge represent his commitment to the system. The scar is a symbol of his personal sacrifice and the cost of his choices.

Domains — Ginoza's primary domain is the Public Security Bureau's headquarters in Tokyo, particularly the Division 1 office. He is also shown in various urban environments, often in the context of investigations. In Season 2, he is relegated to a desk job in the Ministry of Welfare's administrative division, reflecting his fall from grace. Accounts I
Season 1: The Loyal InspectorPsycho-Pass (2012-2013)

2112-2113 (fictional timeline)

Ginoza is introduced as a strict, rule-abiding Inspector in Division 1 of the Public Security Bureau. He is initially antagonistic toward the new Inspector Akane Tsunemori, viewing her as naive. He is revealed to be the son of Tomomi Masaoka, an Enforcer, and has a strained relationship with him due to his father's criminal status. Ginoza is deeply committed to the Sibyl System, but his faith is shaken when his mentor, Shinya Kogami, becomes a latent criminal and is forced to become an Enforcer. Ginoza's resentment toward Kogami grows, especially after Kogami's rogue actions lead to the death of a fellow Inspector. Ginoza is also involved in the hunt for the criminal mastermind Shogo Makishima, which culminates in a confrontation where Ginoza's face is scarred by Makishima. He ultimately survives, but his worldview is permanently altered. Ginoza's faith in the system is severely damaged. He becomes more cautious and less trusting. His relationship with his father begins to thaw, but the emotional toll is evident.

Season 2: The Fallen InspectorPsycho-Pass 2 (2014)

2114 (fictional timeline)

After the events of Season 1, Ginoza's Crime Coefficient rises due to his psychological turmoil, and he is demoted to Enforcer status. He is assigned to work under the new Inspector, Mika Shimotsuki, who is a young, idealistic woman. Ginoza struggles with his new role and his resentment toward Shimotsuki's naive trust in the system. He is forced to confront the machinations of the criminal Kamui Kirito, who challenges the Sibyl System's authority. Ginoza's experiences lead him to a deeper understanding of the system's flaws. He also reconciles with his father, Masaoka, before Masaoka's death, which deeply affects him. Ginoza becomes a more compassionate and pragmatic person. He accepts his role as an Enforcer and begins to support Akane's more humanistic approach to justice. He is later reassigned to a desk job in the Ministry of Welfare, but he remains a key figure in the fight against the system's corruption.

Accounts II
The Movie and Sinners of the SystemPsycho-Pass: The Movie (2015) and Sinners of the System (2019)

2116-2117 (fictional timeline)

In the film, Ginoza is part of a mission to track down Kogami, who has become a fugitive in Southeast Asia. Ginoza's past with Kogami is explored, and he is forced to confront his feelings of betrayal and admiration. He ultimately assists Kogami in a mission against a corrupt warlord. In the Sinners of the System case files, Ginoza plays a supporting role, particularly in Case 1, where he investigates a prison incident alongside Mika Shimotsuki. Ginoza finds a measure of closure regarding Kogami. He continues to serve as an Enforcer, now with a more balanced outlook.

Psycho-Pass 3 and First InspectorPsycho-Pass 3 (2019) and First Inspector (2020)

2120 (fictional timeline)

Ginoza appears as a senior Enforcer and advisor to the new Inspectors, Arata Shindo and Kei Mikhail Ignatov. He is more laid-back and even humorous at times, showing how far he has come from his rigid past. He assists the new team in investigating a series of complex crimes, and he plays a key role in the final confrontation with the group known as the Bifrost. He also helps Akane, who is now a fugitive, by providing information and support. Ginoza remains a steadfast ally to the new generation, and his experience proves invaluable. He continues to live with the scars of his past but has found a sense of purpose.

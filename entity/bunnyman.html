Who It Is

The Bunnyman is a uniquely bizarre American urban legend from Fairfax County, Virginia. The core of the story -- a man in a rabbit costume wielding an axe who haunts a bridge and surrounding woods -- grew from two documented, bizarre incidents in October 1970. On October 19, 1970, a young couple parked near a field in Burke, Virginia reported that a man in a white bunny suit threw a hatchet through their car window, shattering it. The man -- described as wearing the bunny costume with a hatchet in hand -- then fled. Two weeks later, a security guard reported seeing a man in a bunny costume near a construction site chopping at a porch post with a long-handled axe; when confronted, the man simply said, 'All you people trespass around here. If you don't get out of here, I'm going to bust you in the head.' These real incidents spawned an urban legend that the Bunnyman was an escaped mental patient who wore a rabbit costume and murdered people near what became known as 'Bunny Man Bridge' (Colchester Overpass). The legend grew through Halloween storytelling, and variants emerged: the Bunnyman supposedly killed his victims and hung their bodies from the bridge; he appears on Halloween to anyone who dares to cross the bridge at midnight; he was created when a busload of mental patients escaped and one was never found. The bridge became a popular destination for teenagers testing their courage, sometimes leading to police intervention for trespassing. The Bunnyman legend illustrates the process by which a real, albeit strange, incident morphs into full urban legend through decades of retelling.

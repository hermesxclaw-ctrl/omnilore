Who It Is
Form

The Bunnyman is often depicted as a tall, thin man wearing a rabbit costume or mask, wielding an axe or hatchet.

Powers

Axe-wielding The Bunnyman is said to be able to wield an axe or hatchet with deadly precision, attacking people without warning.

Accounts

The story of the Bunnyman originated from two incidents in Fairfax County, Virginia in 1970.

The Bunny Man Bridge is a popular spot for urban legends and ghost stories.

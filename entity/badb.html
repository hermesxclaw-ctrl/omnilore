Who It Is
Form

Badb is often depicted as a crow or a woman with crow-like features, and is associated with war, violence, and death.

Powers

Battle Cry Badb's battle cry is said to be a harbinger of death and destruction, and is often depicted as a wailing or screaming sound.

Accounts

Badb appears in the story of the Táin, a legendary tale of the hero Cúchulainn, as a harbinger of death and destruction.

Badb appears in the story of the Second Battle of Moytura, a legendary tale of the Tuatha Dé Danann, as a goddess of war and violence.

Badb's role in Irish mythology

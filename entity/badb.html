Who It Is

Badb is one of the three Morrígna — Irish war goddesses. Her name means 'crow,' and she manifests as a carrion crow over battlefields. Her most terrifying role is the bean nighe: before battle, a woman washing bloodstained armor at a ford; if she called your name, it meant certain death.

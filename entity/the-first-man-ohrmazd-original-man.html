Who It Is

The First Man is a fascinating and complex figure, whose story has captivated human imagination for millennia. His role in the creation of the world, his relationships with other mythological figures, and his impact on human civilization make him a compelling subject for study and exploration.

The First Man is often portrayed as a benevolent and wise leader, who guides humanity towards the path of righteousness and wisdom. He is also depicted as a powerful and authoritative figure, who exercises control over the forces of nature and the universe.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The First Man is often depicted as a wise and powerful figure, with a strong connection to the natural world and the forces of creation. He is sometimes shown with a beard, symbolizing his wisdom and age. In some depictions, he is accompanied by a lion or a bull, representing his strength and authority.

Symbols — The First Man is associated with various symbols and iconography, including the winged disk, the ankh, and the ouroboros. These symbols represent his connection to the divine, the cycle of life and death, and the infinite nature of the universe.

Domains — The First Man is said to inhabit the spiritual realm, where he exercises his power and influence over the world. He is also associated with the natural world, particularly the mountains, forests, and rivers.

Who It Is

In the halls of Mahora Academy, where magic and martial arts intertwine, Kū Fei stands as a testament to the power of discipline and strength. A master of Chinese martial arts, she becomes an unlikely mentor to a young wizard, forging a bond that transcends the boundaries of their worlds.

Kū Fei is serious and disciplined, dedicated to her martial arts training. She is initially somewhat aloof but shows care for her classmates, especially when it comes to protecting them. She is respectful of strong opponents and is willing to teach others, as seen when she trains Negi Springfield.

Powers
Chinese Martial Arts Mastery

Kū Fei is a highly skilled martial artist, proficient in various Chinese martial arts techniques. She demonstrates exceptional speed, agility, and striking power.

Limit: Her abilities are purely physical, and she does not possess magical powers.

Teaching Ability

She is able to teach martial arts to others, as shown when she trains Negi Springfield, helping him improve his close combat skills rapidly.

Limit: Her teaching is limited to martial arts, not magic.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Kū Fei is a teenage girl with a petite but athletic build. She has short, dark hair and often wears a Chinese-style martial arts uniform, typically a red and black outfit. In some appearances, she is seen with her hair in a ponytail or with a headband.

Symbols — Her martial arts style is associated with Chinese martial arts, often depicted with dynamic movements and powerful strikes. She is often shown in battle poses or meditative stances.

Domains — She is a student at Mahora Academy, residing in the dormitories. She is often seen in the dojo or training areas, and she participates in school events and battles.

Martial Arts Training with NegiNegima! Magister Negi Magi manga

2000s

Negi Springfield, a young mage, seeks to improve his close combat abilities. He approaches Kū Fei for training. She agrees and trains him in Chinese martial arts, helping him develop his physical skills and even learn the technique of instant movement (shundō). Negi becomes more proficient in hand-to-hand combat, complementing his magical abilities. This training proves crucial in later battles.

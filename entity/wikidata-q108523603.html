Who It Is

Before anyone knew what Haki was, a stout young man in a sumo stance put his palms together, shouted “Ashigara Dokkoi!” and made Monkey D. Luffy — the rubber man who shrugged off bullets and cannonballs — double over in pain without ever touching him. That was Sentomaru, the axe-wielding bodyguard who commands an army of cyborg giants and calls an Admiral ‘Uncle’ to his face.

Brash, loud, proud, and highly confident, with a distinctive speech pattern: he ends many sentences with a gruff, self-referential tone and uses sumo jargon. He is fiercely loyal to Dr. Vegapunk, whom he has guarded since childhood, and treats Vegapunk with familial devotion, calling him 'Vegapunk-ossan' or 'old man' affectionately. He shows a paternal/professional respect mixed with casual familiarity toward Admiral Borsalino (Kizaru), whom he calls 'Ossan' (Uncle), reflecting that Kizaru and Vegapunk raised/protected him after finding him as a child. Oda's naming follows the Momotarō folktale pattern used for the Admirals and related characters: Kizaru (Borsalino) is based on the monkey, Aokiji on the pheasant, Akai

Powers
Ashigara Dokkoi (Mt. Ashigara Sumo Palm)

A sumo-style open-palm thrust delivered without direct skin contact, projecting force through Busoshoku Haki. At Sabaody, Sentomaru uses it to repel Luffy's Gear Second attacks and knock Luffy back despite Luffy's rubber body, demonstrating that the attack bypasses Devil Fruit defense.

Limit: Requires close range, proper sumo stance, and Haki proficiency. Not a ranged projectile like a Pacifista laser. Can be blocked or overpowered by stronger Haki users (shown when Kizaru overwhelms him on Egghead). Does not grant invulnerability — Sentomaru himself can be injured.

Busoshoku Haki (Armament Haki) - Advanced Defense

Sentomaru is one of the first characters to demonstrate Busoshoku Haki in the story, before the concept was explained to readers. He claims 'the most defensive man in the world' and uses Haki to make his body extremely hard, blocking attacks and enhancing his palm strikes. Vivre Card confirms he is a Busoshoku user.

Limit: Stated to be highly defensive, but not absolute. Stronger Armament or advanced forms (Ryuo, Haoshoku infusion) can overcome it. He was defeated by Kizaru's light-speed kicks despite his defense. Haki drains stamina and requires concentration.

Superhuman Strength and Sumo Physique

Immense physical strength: wields a giant double-bladed axe one-handed, can lift and slam opponents, and as a child was said to have fought and defeated wild bears on Mt. Ashigara with sumo alone, impressing Vegapunk. His sumo training gives him exceptional balance, pushing power, and defensive stance.

Limit: Strength is physical, not Devil Fruit-enhanced, so he remains vulnerable to superior Devil Fruit, Haki, or technology. Post-timeskip slimming suggests his earlier bulk was not essential to strength. No long-range or elemental power.

Axe Mastery (Masakari)

Wields a massive double-bladed axe (masakari) associated with Kintarō folklore. Uses it for wide cleaving attacks and as a defensive guard. At Sabaody he carries it on his shoulder and swings it to threaten the Straw Hats; on Egghead he uses it against CP0 agents and Marines.

Limit: Conventional weapon — can be blocked, dodged, or destroyed by stronger forces. He often prefers Haki palm strikes over axe against Devil Fruit users. No special weapon abilities.

Pacifista Command Authority

As Captain of the Marine Science Unit and Vegapunk's bodyguard, Sentomaru holds high authority over the Pacifista cyborg army (PX series and Mark III). His voice command can order Pacifista to attack, halt, or switch targets. At Sabaody he escorts PX-1; at Egghead he commands ~50 Mark III units against CP0 and later the Marines.

Limit: Authority is hierarchical: per Ch. 1070, control priority is Gorosei (Five Elders) > Vegapunk (and his satellites) > Sentomaru > other Marines. Thus Saint Jaygarcia Saturn and other Elders can override him, which becomes plot-critical on Egghead. He cannot command Seraphim (higher authority needed) and cannot override Vegapunk's own orders.

Tactical and Scientific Liaison Knowledge

Not a scientist himself, but as Vegapunk's long-term ward he possesses insider knowledge of Vegapunk's inventions, Egghead's defenses (Frontier Dome, laser barriers), and Pacifista deployment tactics. Acts as field commander for Science Unit operations.

Limit: Knowledge is operational, not inventive — he cannot build or repair Vegapunk tech. Relies on Vegapunk and Atlas/Edison for technical solutions.

Accounts I
Sabaody Archipelago — Debut and the Demonstration of HakiOne Piece manga Chapters 498-504; anime Episodes 402-405

In-story: 1522, Age of the Great Pirate Era, two weeks before Marineford War; Publication: 2008

Sentomaru is dispatched to Sabaody Archipelago with the human weapon Pacifista PX-1 to subdue the eleven Supernovas and the Straw Hat Pirates after their assault on the Celestial Dragons. He first appears blocking Jewelry Bonney's escape, then confronts the Straw Hats. Monkey D. Luffy attacks him in Gear Second, but Sentomaru's 'Ashigara Dokkoi' palm thrusts repel Luffy without contact, declaring Luffy's rubber defense useless. He explains (without using the word Haki, not yet introduced to readers) that his defense is the strongest. He effortlessly defeats Luffy, while PX-1 devastates the crew. Only the intervention of Silvers Rayleigh, who stops Admiral Kizaru, and Kuma's separation of the Straw Hats, ends the incident. Sentomaru reports to Kizaru, whom he calls 'Uncle.' Establishes Sentomaru as the Science Unit's enforcer and the first clear demonstration of Busoshoku Haki to readers (retroactively understood). Sabaody Archipelago — Post-War and the Fake Straw HatsOne Piece manga Chapters 511, 598-601; anime Episodes 521, 517-522

In-story: 1522-1524 (immediately post-Marineford and two years later); Publication: 2009-2010

After the Battle of Marineford, Sentomaru is seen reporting Pacifista deployments. Two years later, when the Straw Hats are set to reunite, Sentomaru returns to Sabaody with two Pacifista (PX-5 and PX-7) to deal with the 'Fake Straw Hat Crew' — impostors led by Demaro Black posing as Luffy. He exposes the fakes and orders the Pacifista to annihilate them, showing his slimmer post-timeskip design for the first time. He does not encounter the real Straw Hats, who slip away separately. Caribou observes the attack. Shows Sentomaru's continued Science Unit captaincy and the mass production of Pacifista. Demonstrates his no-nonsense enforcement and provides the in-story explanation for his weight loss (Vegapunk's health management). Clears Sabaody of impostors before the real crew's departure to Fish-Man Island.

Childhood Flashback — The Bear of Mt. Ashigara and Rescue by VegapunkOne Piece manga Chapters 1091-1092; anime Episodes 1115+ (Egghead)

In-story: ~30 years ago (Sentomaru as child, ~4-6 years old); Publication: 2023

A flashback triggered on Egghead reveals Sentomaru's origin: as a poor, orphaned child living on Mt. Ashigara, he survived by challenging wild bears and villagers to sumo, winning through prodigious strength despite his small size. Villagers feared him. Dr. Vegapunk, visiting the mountain with a young Borsalino (Kizaru) as his Marine escort, witnessed the boy defeat a bear. Vegapunk, needing a bodyguard and seeing the boy's defensive talent, offered to take him in, feed him, and employ him. Borsalino, amused, took a liking to him. Sentomaru accepted, becoming Vegapunk's ward and later his official bodyguard. This explains his 'Uncle' relationship with Kizaru and his lifelong loyalty to Vegapunk. Provides emotional core for Sentomaru's devotion to Vegapunk and Kizaru, explaining why he later fights Kizaru despite affection. Reinforces Kintarō folk parallel explicitly in canon. Explains his sumo chant origin and defensive specialty as innate since childhood.

Accounts II
Egghead Island — Defense Against CP0One Piece manga Chapters 1068-1070; anime Episodes 1090-1093

In-story: 1522, late 1522 (Egghead Incident, concurrent with final saga); Publication: 2022-2023

When CP0 (Rob Lucci, Kaku, Stussy) arrives at Egghead to assassinate Vegapunk on orders from the Five Elders, Sentomaru, as captain of the island's defense, refuses them entry. He deploys the new Pacifista Mark III army (50 units) and personally engages Lucci in combat. Using Ashigara Dokkoi and Haki, he initially holds his own against Lucci's Rokushiki and awakened Zoan form, but is eventually wounded by Lucci's Shugan. Before falling, he orders the Mark III to side with the Straw Hats and Vegapunk against CP0, exploiting his higher authority than CP0. Atlas and Vegapunk's satellites assist. Sentomaru's command secures the Pacifista on Vegapunk's side, preventing CP0 from taking the island and allowing the Straw Hats to ally with Vegapunk. He is severely injured, establishing the stakes of the Egghead Incident. Reveals the formal Pacifista authority hierarchy (Elders > Vegapunk > Sentomaru > CP0).

Egghead Island — Confrontation with Admiral KizaruOne Piece manga Chapters 1090-1092; anime Episodes 1114-1116

In-story: 1522, Egghead Incident climax; Publication: 2023

After CP0's defeat, Admiral Kizaru arrives with a Marine fleet and Saint Jaygarcia Saturn to enforce the Elders' order to destroy Egghead and kill Vegapunk. Sentomaru, still wounded, stands to defend the island despite knowing Kizaru is far stronger and is his benefactor. He tearfully declares he must protect Vegapunk, his 'old man,' and attacks Kizaru with Ashigara Dokkoi and axe. Kizaru, expressing regret ('I'm sorry, Sentomaru'), defeats him with a single light-speed kick (Yasakani no Magatama / light kick), heavily injuring him. Kizaru then uses his higher authority? Actually Saturn's presence overrides Sentomaru's Pacifista command, causing the Mark III to turn against Vegapunk's allies. Sentomaru is left unconscious but alive, later rescued by Vegapunk's allies and taken to safety by Jewelry Bonney's group. Emotional climax of Sentomaru's arc: loyalty vs. hierarchy. His defeat allows Kizaru and Saturn to seize Pacifista control, escalating the Egghead Incident into a Buster Call-level crisis. Egghead Aftermath — Survival and Off-Stage RecoveryOne Piece manga Chapters 1092, 1107-1109 (cover and dialogue references)

In-story: 1522, immediate aftermath; Publication: 2023-2024

Following his defeat by Kizaru, Sentomaru is shown unconscious but breathing, being carried away from the front lines by Egghead's remaining staff. Vegapunk's satellite Edison notes Sentomaru's orders still briefly protected the Pacifista until Saturn's override. Later chapters confirm he was evacuated from Egghead with other Vegapunk allies via the Giant Warrior Pirates' ship, surviving the Buster Call. His current status is alive but incapacitated, with his Pacifista command transferred to higher authorities. Keeps Sentomaru alive for future storylines; preserves his narrative function as Vegapunk's loyal guard. Shows that even high-ranking Science Unit officers are expendable under Elder orders.

Is Sentomaru's defense Haki or a Devil Fruit / innate ability?At debut (2008), many readers and early scanlations treated his ability to hurt Luffy as a mysterious sumo technique or paramecia-like power, since Haki had not been named.Post-2010, databooks, Vivre Card, and Oda's SBS confirm it was Busoshoku Haki all along, retroactively fitting Sabaody into the Haki system introduced at Amazon Lily / Marineford.
Is Sentomaru Kizaru's literal nephew?Fan claim: Sentomaru is Kizaru's blood nephew, due to 'Uncle' (Ossan) and similar lip markings/association.Official material describes 'Ossan' as an honorific for a senior Marine who raised him, not a confirmed blood tie. Vivre Card lists no familial relation; flashback shows Kizaru as Vegapunk's escort, not family.
Weight loss — artistic inconsistency or intentional?Some readers saw the slimming between Vol. 51 and Vol. 61 as an art error or Oda's style shift.Intentional in-story change: Vegapunk put him on a diet/health regimen during the timeskip, and characters comment on it.

Sentomaru has a Devil Fruit power that lets him bypass Luffy's rubber body. — He has no Devil Fruit. His ability to injure Luffy is Busoshoku Haki channeled through sumo palm thrusts (Ashigara Dokkoi). Vivre Card and manga confirm he is a non-Devil Fruit Haki user.

Sentomaru is the strongest defensive character in One Piece and cannot be hurt. — His claim 'the most defensive man in the world' (or 'the man with the strongest guard' in VIZ) is hyperbole/boast, not an absolute. He was defeated by Kizaru and wounded by Lucci, proving stronger Haki and Devil Fruit powers overcome his defense.

Sentomaru is Kizaru's biological nephew and shares his light powers. — No blood relation is confirmed; 'Uncle' is an honorific for a senior who helped raise him. He has no Pika Pika no Mi powers; his abilities are Haki and sumo, not light.

Sentomaru died on Egghead after fighting Kizaru. — As of Chapters 1092 and 1107-1109, he is confirmed alive but severely injured and evacuated. Oda shows him unconscious but breathing, not killed.

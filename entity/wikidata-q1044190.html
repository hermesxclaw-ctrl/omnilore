Who It Is

Wolfgang Krauser is a name that echoes through the world of competitive video gaming, a moniker synonymous with raw power and unyielding ambition. But who is the man behind the legend? A German martial artist of immense strength, Krauser has left an indelible mark on the virtual fighting circuits, his presence as formidable in the digital arena as it is in the annals of gaming history.

Krauser is portrayed as arrogant, proud, and ruthless. He believes in strength above all and seeks to prove his superiority by defeating the strongest opponents. He is calculating and strategic, often manipulating events to set up his challenges. Despite his villainous role, he shows a sense of honor, respecting those who can match his power.

Powers
Krauser-style Koppouken

A powerful martial art that combines striking and grappling techniques. It emphasizes devastating blows and throws, capable of breaking bones and armor.

Limit: Requires close-quarters combat; lacks long-range projectiles.

Blitz Ball

A projectile attack where Krauser throws a sphere of energy at his opponent. It travels slowly but can be charged for more power.

Limit: Can be dodged or blocked; leaves Krauser vulnerable during startup.

Gigantic Hammer

A powerful overhead strike that can knock down opponents.

Limit: Slow startup, easily telegraphed.

Krauser's Grappling Techniques

A series of throws and grapples that can be used to punish opponents who get too close.

Limit: Requires close range; can be escaped if input correctly.

Super Moves

Krauser possesses powerful super moves such as the 'Kaiser Wave' and 'Unlimited Desires', which deal massive damage and are often used as finishers.

Limit: Require a full power gauge; have long recovery times.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Wolfgang Krauser is depicted as a tall, muscular man with short blonde hair and a distinctive scar over his left eye. He typically wears a red and black martial arts gi with a high collar, and sometimes a cape. His imposing physique and stern expression convey immense strength and authority.

Symbols — Krauser is often associated with the German eagle, a symbol of power and national pride, which appears on his costume and in his stage backgrounds. His fighting style, 'Krauser-style Koppouken', is a form of martial arts that emphasizes powerful strikes and throws.

Domains — Krauser is a martial artist and the champion of the King of Fighters tournament in his debut. He is based in Germany, and his stage in Fatal Fury 2 is a grand cathedral, reflecting his high status and regal demeanor.

Accounts I
Fatal Fury 2: The King of Fighters TournamentFatal Fury 2 (video game)

1992

Krauser, the champion of the King of Fighters tournament, is challenged by Terry Bogard and his allies. After defeating Geese Howard, Terry must face Krauser in a final battle. Krauser is defeated, but he acknowledges Terry's strength and vows to train further. Krauser's defeat establishes Terry as the new champion. Krauser disappears, but his legacy continues in subsequent games.

Accounts II
King of Fighters '96: The Orochi SagaKing of Fighters '96 (video game)

1996

Krauser is invited to the King of Fighters tournament by the host, Chizuru Kagura. He serves as a sub-boss, testing the skills of the protagonists. He is defeated but survives, and his role in the larger Orochi plot is minimal. Krauser's appearance adds to the tournament's prestige but does not significantly alter the main storyline.

The Approach

In the heart of Sakurashin, where cherry blossoms bloom at night and the boundary between human and yōkai worlds is guarded by seven spiritual pillars, a quiet, unassuming figure holds a power that predates the town's current guardians. Yae Shinatsuhiko, a name whispered in the annals of the town's supernatural history, is a land god whose story intertwines with the very foundation of Sakurashin—a deity whose existence raises questions about succession, duty, and the true nature of divine protection.

Identity

Yae Shinatsuhiko

Pronunciation Yah-eh Shee-nah-tsoo-hee-koh

Etymology

The name 'Shinatsuhiko' (士夏彦) is composed of kanji that could be interpreted as 'warrior summer boy,' though the exact meaning is not established. 'Yae' (八重) means 'eightfold' or 'multi-layered.' The surname 'Shinatsuhiko' is shared with Yūhi, suggesting a family connection.

Cultural Ownership

Yae Shinatsuhiko is a fictional character from the Japanese manga and anime series Yozakura Quartet, created by Suzuhito Yasuda. The character is part of the series' supernatural lore, specifically involving land gods (tochi-gami) and the protection of the town of Sakurashin.

Disputed Identifications

There is a fan theory that Yae may have preceded Yūhi as Sakurashin's land god, but this is not confirmed in the source material. The exact nature of Yae's role and relationship to Yūhi remains ambiguous.

Description

Appearance

Yae is depicted as a young girl with long, light-colored hair, often wearing a traditional Japanese outfit. Her appearance is youthful, belying her ancient nature as a land god.

Symbols & Iconography

As a land god, Yae is associated with the spiritual protection of Sakurashin. She is often seen in the company of Yūhi and Mariabelle, and her presence is tied to the divine guardianship of the town.

Behaviour & Personality

Yae appears to be calm and reserved, with a quiet demeanor befitting her divine status. She is devoted to her role as a protector, though the full extent of her personality is not deeply explored in the series.

Domains & Habitats

Yae is a land god (tochi-gami) associated with Sakurashin, a town where humans and yōkai coexist. She is connected to the spiritual landscape of the town and its protective barriers.

Version Differences

Yae appears in both the manga and anime adaptations of Yozakura Quartet. Her role is consistent across versions, though anime adaptations may slightly alter or expand her appearances.

Yae Shinatsuhiko is introduced as a land god associated with Sakurashin. She is related to Yūhi Shinatsuhiko, who serves as the District Mayor.

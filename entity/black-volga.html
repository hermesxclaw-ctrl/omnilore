Who It Is
Form

The Black Volga is often depicted as a black (or in some versions red) GAZ-21 or GAZ-24 vehicle, with white wheel rims, white curtains or other white elements. It is sometimes described as having horns instead of wing mirrors or a license plate with the number '666'.

Powers

Abduction The Black Volga is said to be able to abduct people, often using its sinister appearance and ominous presence to lure victims in.

Accounts

The Black Volga is an urban legend that tells the story of a black (or red) GAZ-21 or GAZ-24 vehicle that is used to abduct and murder people.

The Black Ambulance is a variation of the Black Volga legend that tells the story of a black ambulance that is used to abduct and murder people.

Scholarly Debates
The identity of the driver
The driver is often said to be Satan himself. ⇌ However, some versions of the legend say that the driver is actually a human being, such as a member of the communist secret police or the Russian mafia.
What We Don't Know

There are several gaps in the Black Volga legend, including the identity of the driver and the exact purpose of the car's nefarious activities.

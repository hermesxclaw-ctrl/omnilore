Who It Is

The Golem of Prague is a fascinating and complex figure in Jewish folklore, with a rich history and multiple interpretations.

The Golem is often portrayed as a creature with a strong sense of loyalty and protection, but also with a tendency towards violence and destruction.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Golem is typically depicted as a humanoid figure made of clay or mud, with a rough and unrefined appearance.

Symbols — The Golem is often associated with the Hebrew letter 'Shin', which is said to hold the power of life.

Domains — The Golem is said to inhabit the city of Prague, particularly in the Jewish quarter.

Accounts I
The Golem of PragueMoment magazine

16th century

The story of the Golem of Prague tells the tale of Rabbi Judah Loew ben Bezalel, who created a golem to protect the Jewish community from persecution. The creation of the Golem leads to a series of events that ultimately result in the protection of the Jewish community.

Who It Is

In the vast emotional spectrum of the DC Universe, where rage burns red and fear coils yellow, one entity embodies the pure, blinding light of hope: Adara. She is not a mere superhero or cosmic tyrant, but the living manifestation of hope itself, a fundamental force that has existed since the dawn of creation and will endure as long as any being dares to dream of a better tomorrow.

Adara embodies hope itself, making her inherently optimistic, inspiring, and nurturing. She is calm and compassionate, but also determined and resolute in the face of despair. She seeks to instill hope in others and empower them to overcome fear and darkness.

Powers
Hope Empowerment

Adara can empower individuals with the Blue Lantern power ring, granting them abilities based on hope. She can also amplify the hope in others, strengthening their resolve and willpower.

Limit: The power of hope is limited when in the presence of fear (yellow light). Blue Lanterns cannot function effectively without a Green Lantern nearby to channel their hope into action.

Flight and Energy Projection

As a cosmic entity, Adara can fly at incredible speeds and project powerful blasts of blue energy that can heal, shield, and even resurrect beings (when combined with other emotional spectrum powers).

Limit: Her abilities are tied to the emotional spectrum; she cannot use them in the absence of hope or in realms cut off from the spectrum.

Emotional Spectrum Connection

Adara is intrinsically linked to the emotional spectrum, allowing her to sense and manipulate hope across the universe. She can communicate with other emotional entities and influence the balance of emotions.

Limit: She is vulnerable to entities of opposite emotions, such as Parallax (fear) and the Ophidian (greed).

Form & Signs

Appearance — Adara typically appears as a majestic, bird-like entity with a glowing blue aura. She resembles a phoenix or an eagle, with luminous blue feathers and a radiant, hopeful presence. Her true form is cosmic and abstract, but she often takes this avian shape when interacting with mortals.

Symbols — Adara is associated with the blue light of hope, the Blue Lantern Corps symbol (a circle with a central dot and upward-curving wings), and the emotional spectrum's blue wavelength. Her iconography often includes stars and celestial imagery, linking her to the cosmos.

Domains — Adara resides in the realm of the Blue Lantern Corps on the planet Odym, which is bathed in blue light. She is also connected to the emotional spectrum and can travel across the universe, but she is most attuned to places where hope is strong or needed.

Accounts I
The Origin of the Blue Lantern CorpsGreen Lantern (vol. 4) #39

2009

The Blue Lantern Corps was created by Ganthet and Sayd, two Guardians of the Universe who believed that hope was the key to countering fear. They sought out the entity of hope, Adara, and bonded her to the first Blue Lantern, Brother Warth, on the planet Odym. Adara became the living embodiment of the Blue Lanterns' power, providing them with the ability to inspire hope across the cosmos. The Blue Lantern Corps was established as a support corps, working alongside the Green Lanterns to combat fear and despair.

Accounts II
Blackest NightBlackest Night #0-8

2009-2010

During the Blackest Night, Nekron, the embodiment of death, sought to extinguish all life and emotion. Adara, along with the other emotional entities, was targeted by the Black Lanterns. She was eventually freed and helped the combined forces of the Lantern Corps defeat Nekron, proving that hope could overcome even death. The emotional entities were freed and the emotional spectrum was restored to balance.

The New 52: Rise of the Third ArmyGreen Lantern: New Guardians #0-20

2011-2013

In the New 52, Adara became more active as a character. She was captured by the Keepers and used to power the Third Army, a force that sought to impose order by eliminating free will. Kyle Rayner and the other Lanterns worked to free Adara and the other entities, ultimately restoring the emotional spectrum. The entities were freed, and the emotional spectrum was restored. Adara's role as a sentient being was solidified.

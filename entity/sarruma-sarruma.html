Name & Tongue

Etymology — The etymology of Šarruma's name is unclear, but it is believed to be derived from the Hurrian word for 'mountain' or 'peak'.

Pronunciation — The pronunciation of Šarruma's name is uncertain, but it is often rendered as /ʃarˈruma/.

Aliases — Šarrumma, Sharruma

Ownership — Šarruma was a god of the Hurrian and Hittite pantheons, worshipped in ancient Anatolia.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Šarruma was often depicted in anthropomorphic form, riding on the back of a bull or a leopard, and sometimes accompanied by a crescent moon.

Symbols — Šarruma's symbols and iconography include the bull, the leopard, and the crescent moon.

Domains — Šarruma's domains and habitats included the mountains, the forests, and the wilderness.

Who It Is
Form

Šarruma was often depicted in anthropomorphic form, riding on the back of a bull or a leopard, and sometimes accompanied by a crescent moon.

Powers

Mountain Command Šarruma's ability to command the mountains and the peaks, granting him control over the natural world.

Protector of Mortals Šarruma's role as a protector of mortals, watching over them from the mountains and the wilderness.

Animal Association Šarruma's association with bulls and leopards, granting him strength, power, and agility.

Accounts

Šarruma, the mountain god, rode on the back of a leopard, which he had tamed and domesticated.

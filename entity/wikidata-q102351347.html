The Approach

A ghost in the machine of D.Gray-man's war, Nea D. Campbell is the brother the Millennium Earl lost and the memory that haunts Allen Walker's left eye. His existence predates the series' main timeline, yet his influence ripples through every major conflict, blurring the line between ally and enemy, past and present.

Content advisory. Contains discussions of memory loss, identity erasure, psychological trauma, and themes of familial conflict and loss.

Etymology

The name 'Nea' may be derived from the Greek 'nea' meaning 'new' or 'young', but no official etymology is given. 'D. Campbell' is a surname shared with the Millennium Earl, indicating a familial connection.

Cultural Ownership

D.Gray-man is a Japanese manga and anime series created by Katsura Hoshino. The character is part of the franchise's fictional universe.

Disputed Identifications

There is some ambiguity regarding Nea's exact nature and relationship to Allen Walker. Some fans theorize that Nea is a separate entity who has possessed Allen, while others believe he is a past incarnation of Allen's soul. The series has not provided a definitive explanation.

Description

Appearance

Nea is described as having a distinct appearance when he manifests through Allen Walker. He has long, white hair and a different facial expression compared to Allen's normal demeanor. His eyes are often depicted with a different color or intensity, and he may have a more sinister or composed look.

Symbols & Iconography

Nea is associated with the Noah clan, and the number 14 is often linked to him. He may have a specific mark or symbol related to the Noah, but this is not detailed in the provided source.

Behaviour & Personality

Nea is portrayed as a calm, mysterious, and calculating individual. He is determined to fulfill his own goals, which involve the Millennium Earl and the fate of the world. He shows little regard for Allen's well-being, often taking control of Allen's body to achieve his objectives.

Domains & Habitats

Nea exists within Allen Walker's body, residing in his left eye. He can manifest and take control of Allen's body at will, often during moments of high stress or when the plot requires his intervention.

Version Differences

The provided source does not detail differences between the manga and anime versions of Nea. However, it is noted that in the anime D.Gray-man Hallow, the voice actor Ayumu Murase portrayed both Allen and Nea, highlighting the dual nature of the character.

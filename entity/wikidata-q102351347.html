Who It Is

A ghost in the machine of D.Gray-man's war, Nea D. Campbell is the brother the Millennium Earl lost and the memory that haunts Allen Walker's left eye. His existence predates the series' main timeline, yet his influence ripples through every major conflict, blurring the line between ally and enemy, past and present.

Nea is portrayed as a calm, mysterious, and calculating individual. He is determined to fulfill his own goals, which involve the Millennium Earl and the fate of the world. He shows little regard for Allen's well-being, often taking control of Allen's body to achieve his objectives.

Nea can take over Allen Walker's body, granting him temporary control and access to his own abilities.

Limit: He can only manifest when Allen is incapacitated or when the plot allows. He may be able to be suppressed by Allen's will.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Nea is described as having a distinct appearance when he manifests through Allen Walker. He has long, white hair and a different facial expression compared to Allen's normal demeanor. His eyes are often depicted with a different color or intensity, and he may have a more sinister or composed look.

Symbols — Nea is associated with the Noah clan, and the number 14 is often linked to him. He may have a specific mark or symbol related to the Noah, but this is not detailed in the provided source.

Domains — Nea exists within Allen Walker's body, residing in his left eye. He can manifest and take control of Allen's body at will, often during moments of high stress or when the plot requires his intervention.

Nea is the younger brother of the Millennium Earl. He was killed or lost by the Earl, and his memory was implanted into Allen Walker's left eye. His exact fate and the circumstances of his death are not fully explained in the provided source, but it is implied that he was a key figure in the Earl's past and the war against the Black Order.

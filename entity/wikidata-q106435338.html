Successful tenure at Crvena zvezda

Source Wikipedia articleCulture Yugoslav basketballPeriod 1968-1975

Summary

Lazarević played for Crvena zvezda from 1968 to 1975, during which he won the FIBA European Cup Winners' Cup in 1974, two Yugoslav League championships (1968-69, 1971-72), and three Yugoslav Cups (1970-71, 1972-73, 1974-75). He played with notable teammates such as Zoran Slavnić, Dragan Kapičić, and Ljubodrag Simonović.

Who It Is

Minene Uryū is not the kind of terrorist who blows up buildings for a cause—she does it to spite God. A survivor of a Middle Eastern war that killed her parents, she turned her grief into a crusade against all religion, becoming the Ninth Diary Holder in a deadly battle royale. But when a police detective named Masumi Nishijima refuses to give up on her, she finds something she never expected: a reason to live. Her story is a brutal, tragic, and ultimately redemptive arc that cuts to the heart of Future Diary's themes of fate, love, and the cost of playing god.

Minene is fiercely independent, pragmatic, and ruthless when pursuing her goals. She is an atheist who harbors deep hatred for religion, particularly monotheistic beliefs, which she blames for her parents' death. Despite her violent exterior, she shows moments of vulnerability and compassion, especially toward children and those who remind her of her past. She initially views others as tools but gradually forms genuine bonds, particularly with Yukiteru Amano and Masumi Nishijima. She is highly resourceful, intelligent, and capable under pressure, but also impulsive and prone to emotion

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Minene' (みねね) is a given name, while 'Uryū' (雨流) is a surname; the kanji for 'Uryū' literally mean 'rain flow'. The character is named after Minerva, the Roman goddess of wisdom, strategic warfare, and crafts.

Pronunciation — MEE-neh-neh OO-ryuu (Japanese: 雨流 みねね, Uryū Minene)

Aliases — Ninth, Ninth Diary Holder, The Terrorist

Ownership — Japanese pop culture; created by Sakae Esuno for the manga series Future Diary (published by Kadokawa Shoten), later adapted into an anime by Asread.

Powers
Escape Diary (逃亡日記)

A Future Diary that predicts the best course of action for Minene to escape from any dangerous situation. It manifests as a handheld device (a smartphone-like gadget) that displays step-by-step instructions for evading threats.

Limit: The diary only provides escape routes if there is a feasible chance of escape; if no such route exists, it fails. It also cannot predict events that are not directly related to Minene's escape, making it less useful for offensive actions. Additionally, it does not account for the actions of other diary holders who can predict the future.

Explosives Expertise

Minene is highly skilled in the use of explosives, including bombs, grenades, and remote detonators. She uses them both for destruction and as a means of escape.

Limit: Her reliance on explosives can be a liability in close-quarters combat or when she needs to avoid collateral damage.

Firearms Proficiency

She is an expert markswoman, proficient with handguns, rifles, and other firearms.

Limit: Standard limitations of firearms, such as reload time and ammunition.

Hand-to-Hand Combat

Minene is a capable fighter in close quarters, able to hold her own against other diary holders.

Limit: She is not as physically strong as some other characters, such as Yuno Gasai.

Survival Skills

Having lived as a fugitive terrorist for years, Minene possesses extensive survival skills, including tracking, evasion, and improvisation.

Limit: None known.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Minene is a young woman with short, light-colored hair (blonde in the anime, light brown in the manga) and often wears a red hooded jacket, a white shirt, and black shorts. She has a distinctive scar over her left eye (which she later loses, leaving a closed eyelid). She is frequently seen carrying explosives and firearms. Her attire is practical for combat and reflects her rugged, anti-establishment lifestyle.

Symbols — Her primary symbol is the number '9' (her diary holder number), often displayed on her clothing or equipment. She is associated with explosives and the concept of 'escape' (reflecting her diary's power). Her red hoodie can be seen as a symbol of her rebellious and dangerous nature.

Domains — Minene operates in urban environments, often hiding in abandoned buildings, rooftops, and other out-of-the-way locations. She is a fugitive and constantly on the move to avoid capture. In the Third World, she is shown living a peaceful suburban life with Nishijima and their children.

Accounts I
The Ninth Diary Holder's IntroductionFuture Diary manga and anime, episode 2.

2006-2012

Minene is introduced as a notorious terrorist who infiltrates Yuki's school to kill the Eighth Diary Holder, Kamado Ueshita. She sets off bombs and engages in a firefight with police. Yuki and Yuno intervene, and Minene is forced to flee. This encounter establishes her as a dangerous antagonist and sets the stage for her later alliance with Yuki. Minene's attack results in multiple casualties and brings the Diary Game to the attention of the authorities. It also leads to her first encounter with Masumi Nishijima, who becomes a recurring figure in her life.

The Failed Assassination and Deus's InterventionFuture Diary: Mosaic and main series (flashback).

2006-2010

Minene attempts to assassinate a Catholic Cardinal in a cathedral. The assassination fails, and she is pursued by security forces. On the verge of being captured or killed, she falls from a high place. Deus ex Machina appears, offering to save her life if she participates in the Diary Game. She accepts, becoming the Ninth Diary Holder. Minene gains the Escape Diary and enters the Diary Game. Her hatred for religion intensifies, and she sees the game as an opportunity to destroy all religion if she wins.

Accounts II
Alliance with Yukiteru and the Battle Against the EleventhFuture Diary manga and anime, episodes 20-24.

2011-2012

After the death of Masumi Nishijima, Minene is devastated but resolves to avenge him. She teams up with Yuki and his friends to invade the twin tower buildings where the Eleventh Diary Holder, John Bacchus, is hiding. During the assault, she discovers that Yuki had been using them as shields and had let Nishijima die. Enraged, she confronts Yuki, but he shoots her. Mortally wounded, she decides to help Yuki by sacrificing herself to blow open the vault where John is hiding. She detonates a bomb, but survives due to Deus having transferred half his power to her earlier. She then accompanies Yuki to the Third World to stop Yuno, and survives the final battle. Minene's actions are crucial in defeating the Eleventh. She loses her left eye and nearly dies, but ultimately survives. Her relationship with Yuki is strained but eventually repaired. She finds happiness in the Third World with Nishijima.

A New Life in the Third WorldFuture Diary manga and anime, final episode.

2011-2012

In the Third World, Minene lives with the Third World's Masumi Nishijima, and they have children who inherit her godly powers (including the ability to fly). She remains in contact with Deus and Murumuru. This ending shows her finding peace and happiness after a life of violence and loss. Minene's arc concludes with a positive resolution, contrasting with her tragic past.

Who It Is
Form

Xochiquetzal is often depicted as a beautiful woman with flowers in her hair, surrounded by lush vegetation and vibrant colors.

Powers

Seduction Xochiquetzal's ability to charm and manipulate others, often using her beauty and charm to get what she wants.

Accounts

Xochiquetzal, the goddess of beauty and love, is said to have seduced the sun god, Huitzilopochtli, and borne him a son.

Xochiquetzal is said to have been killed by the Aztec god of war, Huitzilopochtli, for her role in the death of the sun god.

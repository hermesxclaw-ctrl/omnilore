Who It Is

In the heart of Tokyo's fashionable Harajuku, a self-described 'ordinary' girl named Kanon Shibuya discovers that being unremarkable is just a starting point. With a pet owl named Manmaru, a family café, and a voice she's afraid to share, Kanon becomes the heart of Liella!, proving that kindness and persistence can build a school's first legacy.

Kanon is initially shy, self-deprecating, and avoids the spotlight, but is deeply caring and supportive. She is a natural leader who listens to others, encourages them, and works hard to achieve goals. She has a strong sense of responsibility, often putting others before herself. She loves singing but fears failure, which she overcomes through the support of her friends. She is practical, level-headed, and empathetic.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname Shibuya (澁谷) references the Shibuya district of Tokyo, a fashion and culture hub, though the character lives near Omotesando/Harajuku. The given name Kanon (かのん) is written in hiragana, possibly evoking 'kanon' (canon) or a musical connection, fitting a singer.

Pronunciation — KAH-non SHI-bu-yah (Japanese: ka-no-n shi-bu-ya)

Aliases — Shibuya Kanon, 澁谷 かのん, Kanon-chan, Kanon Shibuya (official English)

Ownership — Intellectual property of the Love Live! franchise, co-owned by Kadokawa, Lantis, and Sunrise (Bandai Namco Filmworks). Japanese media franchise, globally distributed.

Powers
Singing

Kanon has a powerful and emotive singing voice, often the centerpiece of Liella!'s songs. She can convey deep emotion and connect with audiences.

Limit: She initially suffers from stage fright and performance anxiety, which she overcomes through practice and support.

Leadership

As the de facto leader of Liella!, Kanon unites diverse personalities, resolves conflicts, and inspires her group to strive for their dreams.

Limit: She sometimes doubts her own decisions and relies on others.

Café skills

Kanon is skilled at making latte art and helping run the family café, showing her diligence and attention to detail.

Limit: None.

Empathy and communication

Kanon can reach out to troubled individuals, understand their feelings, and help them open up, as seen with several new members.

Limit: She sometimes prioritizes others' feelings over her own.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Kanon is a teenage girl with long, light brown hair often worn in a loose side ponytail or braid. She has amber eyes. Her casual style is modest and comfortable, often seen in school uniform or casual clothes. As an idol, she wears colorful stage costumes designed for Liella! performances.

Symbols — Kanon is associated with the owl Manmaru, who appears in her merchandise and as a mascot. Her image color is a soft peach or orange, representing warmth and kindness.

Domains — Kanon lives with her family in a café near Omotesando, Harajuku, and Aoyama in Tokyo. She attends Yuigaoka Private Girls High School. She is often seen at the school, the café, and various idol performance venues.

Accounts I
The Founding of Liella!Love Live! Superstar!! Season 1

2021 (anime)

Kanon, initially reluctant to be a school idol, is persuaded by Keke to join. Together with Chisato, Sumire, and Ren, they form Liella! to save their new school from closing and to create a legacy. Kanon overcomes her stage fright to perform at the school's opening ceremony, inspiring others. Liella! is formed and gains initial popularity, attracting new students to Yuigaoka.

Accounts II
Growth and New MembersLove Live! Superstar!! Season 2

2022 (anime)

As second-years, Kanon and the original members recruit five new members: Kinako, Mei, Shiki, Natsumi, and Wien. Kanon acts as a mentor, helping each overcome personal struggles, while also facing the challenge of a larger group dynamic. Liella! expands to nine members and competes in the Love Live! competition, growing in fame.

Final Year and LegacyLove Live! Superstar!! Season 3

2024 (anime)

In her final year, Kanon leads Liella! through their last Love Live! competition, dealing with the departure of third-years and preparing the younger members to carry on. She reflects on her growth and the bonds she has formed. Liella! achieves their dream, and Kanon graduates, leaving a lasting legacy.

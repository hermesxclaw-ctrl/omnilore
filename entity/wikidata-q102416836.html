Identity

Otto Suwen

Pronunciation OT-toh SOO-wen (Japanese: オットー・スーウェン, Ottō Sūwen)

Etymology

Not documented in available sources. The name 'Otto' is of Germanic origin meaning 'wealth' or 'prosperity', but no in-world etymology has been provided by the author.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character from the Japanese light novel series 'Re:Zero - Starting Life in Another World' by Tappei Nagatsuki. Copyright owned by the author and publishers.

Disputed Identifications

No disputes identified; distinct from other characters named Otto in other works.

Description

Appearance

Otto is a young man with short, neatly styled blond hair and blue eyes. He typically wears a merchant's outfit, often with a coat and carrying a satchel. His appearance is unremarkable, fitting his role as a traveling merchant.

Symbols & Iconography

None specific; no personal crest or symbol associated with Otto in available sources.

Behaviour & Personality

Otto is initially portrayed as a down-on-his-luck merchant, drunk and complaining about his misfortunes. He is generally well-meaning, kind-hearted, and loyal to friends, though he can be skittish and cowardly in dangerous situations. He is intelligent and resourceful, using his merchant skills to help Subaru. He often acts as a voice of reason and support.

Domains & Habitats

As a traveling merchant, Otto has no fixed home; he travels across the kingdom of Lugunica. He later becomes a close associate of Emilia's faction and resides at Roswaal's mansion.

Version Differences

No significant differences between the light novel and anime have been noted in available sources. The light novel provides more internal monologue and backstory details.

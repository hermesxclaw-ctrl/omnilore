Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Frank' derives from the Old French 'franc', meaning 'free' or 'noble', which itself comes from the Late Latin 'Francus', referring to the Germanic tribe of the Franks. The tribe's name is of uncertain ultimate origin, possibly from a Proto-Germanic word meaning 'javelin' or 'free'.

Pronunciation — /fræŋk/

Aliases — FRANK, Franks, Frank (disambiguation)

Ownership — The term 'Frank' is used across multiple cultures and contexts, including Germanic, Romance, and Islamic cultures, as well as in modern English-speaking countries.

The Franks in HistoryVarious historical texts

Late Antiquity to Middle Ages

The Franks were a group of Germanic peoples who settled in the Roman Empire's frontier and eventually established a powerful kingdom under Clovis I, becoming the dominant force in Western Europe. The Frankish Empire laid the foundation for modern France and Germany.

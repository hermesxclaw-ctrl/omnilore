Aurboda, the gold-bringer of Ironwood, mother of the radiant Gerðr, whose beauty drew Freyr across the worlds.

Who It Is

Aurboda is a giantess (gygr) who lives in Járnviðr (Ironwood), the forest east of Midgard where troll-women and giantesses dwell. Her name means 'Gold-Bringer' or 'Gravel-Bringer,' possibly referring to her association with the precious metals of the mountains. She is the mother of the giantess Gerðr (whom Freyr fell in love with and sent Skírnir to woo) by the giant Gymir. Aurboda is also the mother of several other giantesses mentioned in the 'Tally of the Giantesses' (Völuspá hins skamma) and may be the same figure as the witch who raises the wolf Fenrir in Ironwood. Some scholars suggest Aurboda is the giantess who summons the 'sons of the wolf' (the wolves that will devour the sun and moon at Ragnarök). She is a shadowy but significant figure: as the mother of Gerðr (the beloved of Freyr), she is part of the story that connects the world of the gods with the giants through marriage and desire. She represents the mysterious, dangerous feminine power of the giant world — the beauty that tempts the gods and the wilderness that eventually consumes them.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Three Fingers are said to be three serpent-like fingers that are capable of manipulating the fabric of reality. They are often depicted as being surrounded by a halo of light, and are said to be incredibly powerful.

Symbols — The Three Fingers are often associated with the symbol of the three serpents, which is said to represent their power and influence.

Domains — The Three Fingers are said to inhabit a realm known as the 'Monument Mythos', which is a fictional world that exists outside of our own.

Who It Is

A government program revealed in the 'Washington Absolution' arc: after George Washington's pact with the Horned Serpent, the US government established a secret protocol for dealing with anomalous threats. The 'Three Fingers' are three individuals — one from each branch of government — who hold the authority to activate 'Absolution protocols' that range from memetic amnestic dispersal across the American population to full reality restructuring. The Three Fingers are not anomalies themselves — they are ordinary humans with extraordinary authority, and that is the horror: the fate of reality rests in the hands of three people selected by a political process.

Form

The Three Fingers are never shown. Their identities are classified. The only visual associated with them is a government seal: an eagle with three talons, each holding a key, over the words 'ABSOLUTION THROUGH VIGILANCE.'

Powers

Who It Is

In the bustling harbor city of Liyue, where tradition and commerce intertwine, Keqing stands as a defiant force for change—a Yuheng of the Liyue Qixing who questions the ancient reverence for the Adepti and champions a future shaped by human hands.

Keqing is known for her strong-willed, pragmatic, and outspoken nature. She is deeply devoted to Liyue's prosperity but is critical of the over-reliance on the Adepti, believing that humans should chart their own destiny. She is hardworking, often taking on more tasks than she can handle, and values efficiency and results. Despite her stern exterior, she has a softer side, showing care for her subordinates and a secret fondness for cute things.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Keqing' (刻晴) is of Chinese origin. The characters can be interpreted as 'carved clear sky' or 'engraved brightness', possibly reflecting her sharp, decisive personality and her role as a visionary.

Pronunciation — kuh-CHING

Aliases — Yuheng of the Liyue Qixing

Powers
Electro Vision

Keqing wields a Vision that grants her control over the Electro element. She can channel lightning to enhance her sword attacks and unleash devastating elemental skills and bursts.

Limit: As with all Vision holders, her powers are tied to her Vision; if it were removed, she would lose her abilities.

Elemental Skill: Stellar Restoration

Keqing throws a Lightning Stiletto that deals Electro damage and marks the target. When she uses the skill again or performs a charged attack, she teleports to the mark's location, dealing Electro damage in an area. This allows for rapid, agile combat maneuvers.

Limit: The teleportation has a limited range and requires precise positioning.

Elemental Burst: Starward Sword

Keqing unleashes a flurry of lightning-infused sword slashes, dealing massive Electro damage to enemies in front of her. This is her most powerful ability and is often used to finish off foes.

Limit: Requires energy to be charged; cooldown period.

Combat Prowess

Keqing is a highly skilled swordswoman, trained in the art of swift and precise strikes. She combines her Electro powers with her martial skills to overwhelm opponents.

Limit: None noted.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Keqing is a young woman with long, dark purple hair styled in twin tails, and striking amethyst eyes. She wears a sleek, form-fitting outfit with a predominantly purple and white color scheme, featuring a short skirt, thigh-high stockings, and a large bow at her chest. Her attire is elegant yet practical, reflecting her status as a member of the Liyue Qixing and her agile combat style.

Symbols — Keqing's symbol is the Electro Vision, which she wields with precision. Her constellation is 'Trulla Cementarii' (Latin for 'Mason's Trowel'), symbolizing her role in building a new Liyue. Her outfit and accessories often feature geometric patterns and sharp lines, reflecting her no-nonsense personality.

Domains — Keqing resides in Liyue Harbor, the bustling commercial center of Liyue. She is often found in the Qixing's offices or on the streets, overseeing the city's affairs.

Accounts I
The Yuheng's ResolveGenshin Impact character story

Contemporary to the game's timeline

Keqing is one of the seven leaders of the Liyue Qixing, the ruling body of Liyue. She openly questions the traditional worship of the Adepti, arguing that Liyue should rely on human effort and ingenuity. Her views put her at odds with other Qixing members and the Adepti, but she remains steadfast in her belief that humans must forge their own path. Her stance creates tension but also inspires debate about Liyue's future. During the events of the Liyue Archon Quest, she plays a crucial role in defending the city and proving that humans can protect themselves.

Before the game's main events

Keqing's Vision was granted to her after a moment of intense determination, when she resolved to challenge the old ways and take action to shape Liyue's destiny. This event solidified her role as a visionary leader. She gains the power to enact her ideals, becoming a key figure in Liyue's governance.

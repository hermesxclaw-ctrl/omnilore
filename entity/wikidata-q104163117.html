The Approach

Nico Yazawa is a fictional idol from the multimedia franchise Love Live!, known for her catchphrase 'Nico Nico Nii!' and her dual persona as a cheerful, confident idol and a more vulnerable, hardworking girl beneath the surface.

Content advisory. None. The character is from a family-friendly franchise and contains no dark material.
Identity

Nico Yazawa

Pronunciation NEE-koh YAH-zah-wah (approximate)

Known As

Nico-chanNicoNiiNico Yazawa (Romanized)

Etymology

The name 'Nico' is a common Japanese given name, while 'Yazawa' is a Japanese surname. Her catchphrase 'Nico Nico Nii' is a playful self-introduction that she uses to boost morale.

Cultural Ownership

Created by the Japanese multimedia project Love Live!, a collaboration between ASCII Media Works, Lantis, and Sunrise. The character is owned by the project's copyright holders.

Disputed Identifications

None known.

Description

Appearance

Nico is a petite girl with short, twin-tails styled hair that is a light brown color, often with a small red bow. Her eyes are red. She typically wears the school uniform of Otonokizaka Academy, but in her idol performances she wears various colorful costumes. She often sports a confident, cheerful expression.

Symbols & Iconography

Her signature symbol is the 'Nico Nico Nii' gesture, where she forms a heart with her hands and winks. She is also associated with the color red, which appears in her bows and some of her costumes.

Behaviour & Personality

Nico is outwardly confident, energetic, and sometimes bossy, often referring to herself as the 'number one idol.' She can be dramatic and attention-seeking. However, beneath this facade, she is hardworking, caring, and often struggles with self-doubt, especially regarding her family and her desire to become an idol. She is known for her catchphrase and her ability to motivate others.

Domains & Habitats

Nico's primary domain is Otonokizaka Academy, a fictional high school in Akihabara, Tokyo. She is a member of the school's idol group μ's (Muse). She also appears in various performance venues and events.

Version Differences

In the anime, Nico is initially introduced as a solo idol who later joins μ's. In the manga, her character is slightly more subdued, and her backstory is expanded. In the mobile game, she has numerous card variants that depict her in different outfits and scenarios. The core personality remains consistent across versions.

The Approach

In the bustling streets of London, a young woman with a penchant for puzzles and a heart as sharp as her wit steps into the spotlight, determined to carve her own legacy in the shadow of a legendary father. Katrielle Layton, the brilliant and unconventional detective, proves that the apple doesn't fall far from the tree—but sometimes it rolls in a delightfully unexpected direction.

Content advisory. No dark material detected; suitable for all ages. Contains mild peril and puzzle-solving challenges typical of the Professor Layton series.
Identity

Katrielle Layton

Pronunciation KAT-ree-EL LAY-ton

Known As

KatrielleKatLady Layton

Etymology

Katrielle is a variant of Katherine, meaning 'pure' in Greek. Layton is inherited from her adoptive father, Professor Hershel Layton.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese video game franchise 'Professor Layton' by Level-5, with Western localization by Nintendo.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans speculate that Katrielle is actually Hershel's biological daughter, but official sources state she is adopted. The exact circumstances of her adoption are not fully detailed.

Description

Appearance

Katrielle is a young woman in her early twenties with shoulder-length blonde hair, often styled with a side braid, and large green eyes. She typically wears a stylish white and purple dress with a matching hat, gloves, and boots, reflecting her fashionable and confident personality. She carries a small handbag and often has a magnifying glass at hand.

Symbols & Iconography

Her signature symbols include her pink magnifying glass, her hat, and the 'Layton' name, which she uses to establish her detective identity. Her office is adorned with various trinkets and a large sign reading 'Layton's Detective Agency'.

Behaviour & Personality

Katrielle is cheerful, optimistic, and highly intelligent, though sometimes scatterbrained and overly confident. She is compassionate and deeply cares for her friends and clients, often going out of her way to help them. She has a strong sense of justice and is determined to solve mysteries that others dismiss, believing that every puzzle has a solution. She is also known for her love of food and her tendency to make puns or playful remarks.

Domains & Habitats

She operates primarily in London, England, during the early 20th century (though the exact time period is ambiguous). Her base of operations is 'Layton's Detective Agency', located in a building that also houses a café. She frequently visits various locations around London, including museums, theaters, and wealthy estates, as well as more ordinary neighborhoods.

Version Differences

In the anime, Katrielle is portrayed with a slightly more serious demeanor in some episodes, but overall her personality remains consistent. The manga adaptation includes original side stories not in the game. The game's Deluxe Edition adds new cases and costumes but does not alter her core characterization.

Stories & Myths

The Millionaires' Conspiracy

Source Layton's Mystery Journey: Katrielle and the Millionaires' ConspiracyCulture Japanese video game, Western localisationPeriod Set in early 20th century London (fictional timeline)

Summary

Katrielle, having recently opened her detective agency, is hired to investigate the disappearance of a wealthy businessman. The case leads her to uncover a larger conspiracy involving a group of millionaires, a secret society, and a mysterious girl named Ernest. Over the course of the game, she solves numerous puzzles and ultimately reveals the truth behind the conspiracy, which is tied to her own past and her father's legacy.

Participants

Katrielle Layton

Sherl (her talking dog)

Inspector Hastings

Ernest Greeves

Various clients and suspects

Consequences

Katrielle gains recognition as a skilled detective and strengthens her bond with her friends. The conspiracy is resolved, and the missing businessman is found.

Variants

The Deluxe Edition adds extra cases that are not part of the main storyline.

Relationships

Hershel Layton

Type adoptive fatherSource Game and anime

Description

Katrielle is the adopted daughter of Professor Hershel Layton, the protagonist of the original Professor Layton series. She admires him greatly and seeks to follow in his footsteps as a detective. He is often away on adventures, but they share a close bond.

Version Note

In the game, Hershel is not physically present but is mentioned frequently. In the anime, he appears in a few episodes.

Conflicting Accounts

Some fans speculate that Katrielle might be his biological daughter, but official sources state she is adopted.

Sherl

Type companion (talking dog)Source Game and anime

Description

Sherl is a dog that Katrielle can communicate with, though he appears to be an ordinary dog to others. He serves as her loyal companion and helps her in investigations, often providing insights or comic relief.

Version Note

In the game, Sherl is a small, fluffy dog that sometimes speaks to Katrielle in a way that only she understands. In the anime, his role is similar.

Conflicting Accounts

None known.

Ernest Greeves

Type assistant and friendSource Game and anime

Description

Ernest is Katrielle's assistant at the detective agency. He is often flustered by her antics but is loyal and helpful. He has a background in engineering and helps with technical aspects of investigations.

Version Note

In the game, Ernest is introduced as a client who becomes her assistant. In the anime, he is a regular character.

Conflicting Accounts

None known.

Inspector Hastings

Type ally and police contactSource Game and anime

Description

Inspector Hastings is a Scotland Yard inspector who often works with Katrielle, providing access to police resources and sometimes seeking her help on cases.

Version Note

In the game, he is a supporting character. In the anime, he appears more frequently.

Conflicting Accounts

None known.

Noah Montoir

Noah is a young man who appears in the anime as a friend of Katrielle, often accompanying her on investigations. He is known for his culinary skills and his family's restaurant.

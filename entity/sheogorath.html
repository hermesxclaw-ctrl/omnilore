Form & Signs

Appearance — Sheogorath is often depicted as a tall, slender figure with a mischievous grin and a wild shock of white hair. He is said to wear a tattered and worn-out suit, with a top hat and a cane. His appearance is often accompanied by a sense of chaos and unpredictability.

Symbols — Sheogorath's symbols include the Wheel of Madness, a symbol of his power over the forces of chaos and madness. He is also associated with the color white, which represents his connection to the moon and the night sky.

Domains — Sheogorath's domains are said to be the Clockwork City of Sotha Sil, a place of intricate clockwork machinery and madness-inducing puzzles. He is also associated with the city of Anvil, a place of chaos and disorder.

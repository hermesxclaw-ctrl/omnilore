Who It Is
Form

Sheogorath is often depicted as a tall, slender figure with a mischievous grin and a wild shock of white hair. He is said to wear a tattered and worn-out suit, with a top hat and a cane. His appearance is often accompanied by a sense of chaos and unpredictability.

Powers

The Wheel of Madness Sheogorath's power over the forces of chaos and madness, allowing him to manipulate the minds of mortals and gods alike.

Accounts

Sheogorath and King Lyandir engage in a contest of wills, with Sheogorath using his powers of chaos and madness to try and break Lyandir's spirit.

Sheogorath's powers of chaos and madness are unleashed in the city of Whiterun, causing widespread destruction and chaos.

Sheogorath plays a trick on the people of the Clockwork City, causing chaos and destruction throughout the city.

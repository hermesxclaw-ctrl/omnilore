Who It Is

The Daedric Prince of Madness, whose realm is the Shivering Isles. Sheogorath is both a curse and a transformation — originally Jyggalag, the Prince of Order, cursed by the other Daedra into embodying the madness he despised. Sheogorath is whimsical, chaotic, and dangerously unpredictable, capable of breathtaking cruelty disguised as jokes and genuine kindness hidden in insanity.

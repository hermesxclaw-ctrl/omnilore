Who It Is

The Human-Human Fruit, Model: Great Buddha, a Mythical Zoan that allows the user to transform into a colossal golden Buddha. The user gains immense size, durability, and the ability to generate devastating shockwave blasts from their palms. The hybrid form grants a mix of human agility and giant Buddha power. Represents divine judgment and overwhelming authority.

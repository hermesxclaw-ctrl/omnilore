Who It Is
Form

Ariel is a spirit who appears in William Shakespeare's play The Tempest. He is bound to serve the magician Prospero, who rescued him from the tree in which he was imprisoned by Sycorax, the witch who previously inhabited the island. Ariel is Prospero's eyes and ears throughout the play, using his magical abilities to cause the tempest in Act One which gives the play its name, and to foil other characters' plots to bring down their master.

Powers

Magical abilities Ariel has the ability to perform magic and cause the tempest in Act One of the play, as well as to foil other characters' plots to bring down their master.

Accounts

The Tempest is a play by William Shakespeare that tells the story of a group of characters who are stranded on a magical island. The play features Ariel as a key character, who is bound to serve the magician Prospero.

Menippis is a phantom of the archive: a name without a face, a legend without a text, a hero whose deeds are whispered only in the gaps between sources. To encounter Menippis is to confront the very nature of lore itself—how a name can carry weight, how a fragment can become a myth, and how the absence of evidence becomes its own kind of story.

Etymology

The name 'Menippis' is likely a variant or corruption of 'Menippus' (Μένιππος), a Greek name meaning 'horse of courage' or 'steadfast horse' (μένος 'force, courage' + ἵππος 'horse'). The feminine ending '-is' may suggest a female form, but this is speculative.

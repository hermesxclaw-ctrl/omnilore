The Little Girl

Why is everyone so angry at me? I didn't do anything wrong. I promise.

Who It Is

SCP-053 is a three-year-old girl of average appearance and normal intelligence. She is verbal, playful, and demonstrates age-appropriate behavior. She is not anomalous in appearance or behavior. Her anomaly is entirely passive: any human who spends more than approximately ten minutes in her presence will develop an overwhelming, irrational compulsion to kill her. The compulsion is accompanied by extreme anger, headache, and disorientation. Personnel who have been affected describe the urge as primal and all-consuming — they know intellectually that they should not harm a child, but the compulsion overrides reason. Those who attempt to act on the compulsion become violently ill, suffering seizures and cardiac events. No one has successfully harmed SCP-053. The child herself appears unaware of the effect and becomes distressed when people become angry around her. Notably, SCP-682 — the omnicidal Hard-to-Destroy Reptile — was rendered completely docile during a cross-test with SCP-053. It lay still, allowed the girl to approach and touch it, and made no aggressive moves. When SCP-053 was removed, SCP-682 immediately returned to its normal, homicidal state. The reason for this anomalous interaction is unknown and remains a major research priority. SCP-053 is contained in a standard humanoid cell with child-appropriate furnishings and toys. Her interactions with personnel are conducted through remote communication systems or with handlers who are regularly rotated to prevent threshold exposure. The entity raises uncomfortable questions about the nature of the Foundation's work: a child who, through no fault of her own, inspires murderous hatred in everyone around her, and must grow up in isolation because of it.

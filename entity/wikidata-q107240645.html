Neda and the Nurturing of Zeus

Source Greek mythology (references in Pausanias, Description of Greece, and other sources)Culture Ancient GreekPeriod Archaic to Classical period (circa 8th-4th century BC)

Summary

According to some traditions, the nymph Neda was one of the nymphs who helped care for the infant Zeus, hiding him from his father Cronus. She is sometimes associated with the region where Zeus was raised in Crete or Arcadia. However, the specific story involving Neda is not detailed in the available sources.

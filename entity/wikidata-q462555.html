Who It Is

In the grand, celestial bureaucracy of the Shinto pantheon, Ame no Oshihomimi is the divine civil servant who got the most important memo of his career... and then accidentally deleted it. His name means 'Heavenly Great Ears,' yet he failed to hear his own orders. Sent to the beautiful land below, he was so taken by its charm that he returned to the heavens and said, 'Mission accomplished?' when it very much was not. He is the god of well-intentioned failure, the patron deity of 'I was just going to look, I wasn't going to buy anything.'

He is portrayed as a dutiful and obedient divine messenger, carrying out the commands of the heavenly deities (Amatsukami) without question. His primary action is to descend to the Central Land of the Reed Plains (Ashihara no Nakatsukuni) to assess its state, and then to report back faithfully.

Powers
Divine Messengership

His primary function is to act as an envoy of the heavenly gods. He possesses the authority and ability to travel between the celestial and terrestrial realms.

Limit: His mission is limited to reconnaissance and reporting; he is not given the power to subjugate or pacify the land himself.

Divine Perception (Implied)

His name, which references 'ears,' implies a supernatural ability to hear, perhaps the prayers of mortals or the will of the gods.

Limit: This is an inference from his name and is not explicitly described in the primary texts.

Form & Signs

Appearance — No specific physical description is given in the Kojiki or Nihon Shoki. He is a divine being and is presumably anthropomorphic, but his appearance is not a subject of mythic focus.

Symbols — No specific symbols are associated with him. His name, referring to 'ears,' is his primary symbolic attribute, suggesting a role as a listener or one who perceives divine will.

Domains — Divine messenger, intermediary between the heavenly and earthly realms, possibly a deity of hearing (prayers)

Accounts I
The First Failed EnvoyKojiki

Age of the Gods (mythic time)

After the kami of the land, Okuninushi, had established his rule over the Central Land of the Reed Plains, the heavenly deities decided to assert their authority. Ame no Oshihomimi was chosen as the first envoy to descend and demand that Okuninushi relinquish the land. However, upon his arrival, he was so impressed by the prosperity and beauty of the land that he did not deliver his message. Instead, he returned to the heavens and reported that the land was already in good hands, effectively failing his mission. His failure led to the sending of more forceful envoys. Ame no Wakahiko was sent next and was killed for his treachery. Finally, the powerful warrior god Takemikazuchi was sent, who successfully intimidated Okuninushi into surrendering the land, paving the way for the descent of Ninigi, the grandson of Amaterasu.

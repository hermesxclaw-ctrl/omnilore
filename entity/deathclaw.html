Who It Is

A devastatingly dangerous creature created before the Great War through genetic engineering, combining genes from various species including Jackson's chameleons and common horned lizards, then exposed to the Forced Evolutionary Virus (FEV). Deathclaws are massive bipedal reptiles with razor-sharp claws powerful enough to slice through power armor. They are fast, intelligent, and highly territorial predators.

Powers

Razor claw attacks (armor-penetrating)

Extreme speed (deceptively fast for size)

High intelligence (can set ambushes)

Territorial aggression

Glowing variant: radiation aura

Who It Is

A man enters 'NoEnd House' — a Halloween attraction famous for being 'the scariest haunted house in the world,' consisting of nine rooms, each more terrifying than the last. Most people don't make it past Room 5. The narrator describes each room: Room 1 is standard haunted house fare. Room 3 features his dead wife. Room 5 shows his daughter being murdered. Room 7 has him trapped in a coffin for what feels like days. Room 8 is a white void where a voice whispers every regret he's ever had. Room 9... Room 9 is his house. His real house. He is home. The door opens, and he steps outside into sunlight. He is free. But the story ends with the implication that he never actually left — that Room 9 is just another room, and the real exit is somewhere else.

Form

The house exterior is a standard haunted house facade: peeling paint, boarded windows, a sign reading 'NOEND HOUSE — CAN YOU MAKE IT TO THE END?' Inside, each room is a self-contained horror tailored to the visitor's specific fears. The rooms do not exist in physical space — they are generated from the visitor's psyche.

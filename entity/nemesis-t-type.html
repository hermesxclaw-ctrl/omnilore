Who It Is

The Nemesis-T Type, a Tyrant variant infected with a NE-α Type parasite that grants it intelligence and the ability to use weapons. Unlike standard Tyrants that walk slowly, Nemesis runs, uses a rocket launcher, and speaks — repeating 'S.T.A.R.S.' in a guttural voice as it hunts the surviving members of the special tactics unit. Created to eliminate S.T.A.R.S. operatives in Raccoon City, Nemesis is faster, smarter, and more adaptable than any previous B.O.W.

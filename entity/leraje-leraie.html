Who It Is

Fourteenth spirit, a Marquis commanding 30 legions. Leraje appears as a gallant archer dressed in green — the distinctive attire of a huntsman — carrying a bow and quiver. His domain is battle and the art of the hunt. He causes great battles and contests, determining their outcome. Most distinctively, Leraje causes arrow wounds and other battle injuries to fester and putrefy, making them impossible to heal by natural means — a power over combat trauma and its aftermath. He is the Goetic patron of both the archer's skill and the wound that refuses to close.

Who It Is
Form

SCP-001 is described as a massive, labyrinthine factory with an unknown number of rooms and corridors. The factory is said to be capable of containing and perfecting any Keter-class SCP object.

Powers

Perfect Containment SCP-001 is capable of perfectly containing and perfecting any Keter-class SCP object, making it a highly valuable asset for the SCP Foundation.

Incident Log

The Ihp/Locke Proposal is a proposal for a new containment protocol for SCP-001. The proposal outlines the benefits and risks of using SCP-001 as a containment facility for Keter-class SCP objects.

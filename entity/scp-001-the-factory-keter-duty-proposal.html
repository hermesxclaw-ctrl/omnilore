Who It Is

One of the many SCP-001 proposals — this one posits that the Foundation itself was born from a factory that produced anomalous objects on an industrial scale. The Factory is a vast, semi-sentient industrial complex that has existed since at least the 19th century, manufacturing items that defy physics and corrupt their users. The Foundation was created by the O5 Council specifically to contain the Factory's output — meaning every other SCP may trace its origin to this single source. The Factory is still operational, still producing, and the Foundation is losing the war of attrition.

Form

A sprawling industrial complex of soot-blackened brick and iron, perpetually shrouded in smog. Its smokestacks belch smoke that forms impossible shapes. Inside, assembly lines produce anomalous objects from raw materials that do not exist on any periodic table.

Powers

Mass production of anomalous objects

Self-repair and expansion

Corruption of workers into extensions of the Factory

Existence predating the Foundation by at least a century

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

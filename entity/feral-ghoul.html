Who It Is
Form

Feral Ghouls are zombie-like creatures with severely mutated physical appearances, often with exposed bones and decaying flesh.

Powers

Regenerative Abilities Feral Ghouls have the ability to regenerate their bodies, allowing them to heal from injuries and survive in harsh environments.

Accounts

The story of the Feral Ghoul's origin is told through the game's lore, where it is revealed that Ghouls are originally humans who have been mutated by residual radiation.

The Ghoul Mask is a distinctive mask worn by some Ghouls to conceal their grotesque features. It is said to have been created by a group of Ghouls who wanted to hide their appearance from humans.

Who It Is

In the sun-scorched kingdom of the Afterglow Savannah, where the pride of the kings rules with fangs and fire, one prince chose to burn his own name into legend—not through noble deeds, but through defiance, cunning, and the unyielding will to carve a throne from the shadows of his brother's reign. Leona Kingscholar, the second prince of the savanna, is a figure of smoldering ambition and fierce pride, a lion who refuses to be tamed by fate or family.

Leona is arrogant, lazy, and fiercely proud. He often acts indifferent and sleeps during classes, but he is highly intelligent and strategic. He is blunt and sarcastic, often belittling others, but he shows a protective streak towards his vice-warden, Ruggie Bucchi, and his dorm members. He has a deep-seated inferiority complex regarding his older brother, Falena, and a fear of being forgotten. He is driven by a desire to prove his worth and surpass his brother, leading to reckless and sometimes ruthless actions. He is a skilled mage and a cunning strategist.

Powers
Unique Magic: King's Roar

Leona's signature spell allows him to unleash a powerful, lion-shaped shockwave that can petrify opponents. The spell's power increases with his physical strength and emotional intensity. It is considered one of the most powerful unique magics at Night Raven College.

Limit: The spell requires significant magical energy and can be exhausting. It has a limited range and can be dodged. It is less effective against opponents with strong magical defenses.

Enhanced Physical Abilities

As a beastman, Leona possesses superhuman strength, speed, agility, and senses. He is a formidable fighter even without magic.

Limit: His abilities are natural and not magical, so they can be countered by magic or superior tactics.

Strategic Genius

Leona is a brilliant tactician and manipulator. He is able to analyze situations quickly and plan multi-step strategies, often using others as pawns.

Limit: His pride often leads him to underestimate opponents, and he can be overconfident.

Symbols — Associated with lions, the sun, and the savanna. His symbol is a stylized lion's head with a crown. He is often associated with the colors gold and black, reflecting his royal status and his darker nature. His unique magic, 'King's Roar', is visualized as a golden, lion-shaped shockwave.

Accounts I
The Savanaclaw Arc (Main Story Chapter 2)Twisted Wonderland (game)

Modern (in-game timeline)

Leona, as dorm leader of Savanaclaw, plots to use a magical artifact called the Magical Shift to overthrow his brother, Falena, and become king of the Afterglow Savannah. He uses his dorm members and the protagonist (Yu) to achieve his goals. His plan is ultimately thwarted by the protagonist and the other dorm leaders. During the climax, Leona's magical power goes out of control, causing an 'overblot'—a state of magical corruption fueled by negative emotions. He is defeated and purified, and he later shows a hint of remorse and a desire to change. Leona's overblot is resolved, and he returns to his normal self. He remains dorm leader and begins to show a slightly more cooperative attitude, though he still maintains his pride. His relationship with his brother is left unresolved.

Character Vignettes (Personal Stories)Twisted Wonderland (game)

Modern (in-game timeline)

Various vignettes explore Leona's daily life, his interactions with other characters, and his backstory. One notable vignette shows his childhood, where he was always compared unfavorably to his brother. Another shows him teaching Ruggie how to play a game of strategy. These stories flesh out his personality and relationships. These stories deepen the player's understanding of Leona's motivations and his gradual development.

Modern (in-game timeline)

In this comedic event, Leona participates in a bean-throwing contest against other dorms. His competitive nature and laziness are played for humor. The event reinforces his character traits and provides comic relief.

Leona is a direct adaptation of Scar from The Lion King. — He is inspired by Scar, but he is an original character with a different backstory, personality, and role. The game's official materials state that he is 'inspired by' Scar, not a retelling.

Leona is lazy and unintelligent. — He is highly intelligent and strategic, but he suffers from a lack of motivation due to his belief that he cannot change his fate. His laziness is a facade and a symptom of his despair.

Leona is a villain. — He is an antagonist in the main story, but he is not a villain. He is a complex character with redeeming qualities, and the game portrays him sympathetically.

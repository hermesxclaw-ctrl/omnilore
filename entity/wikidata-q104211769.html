The Approach

In the sun-scorched kingdom of the Afterglow Savannah, where the pride of the kings rules with fangs and fire, one prince chose to burn his own name into legend—not through noble deeds, but through defiance, cunning, and the unyielding will to carve a throne from the shadows of his brother's reign. Leona Kingscholar, the second prince of the savanna, is a figure of smoldering ambition and fierce pride, a lion who refuses to be tamed by fate or family.

Content advisory. This dossier covers fictional content from the Disney franchise Twisted Wonderland. It includes themes of rivalry, resentment, and mild violence (magical duels, hunting). No explicit dark material is present, but the character's backstory involves familial tension and a near-fatal magical attack. Suitable for general audiences with parental guidance for younger children.
Identity

Leona Kingscholar

Pronunciation LEE-oh-nuh KING-skol-er (English); in Japanese, レオナ・キングスカラー (Reona Kingusukarā).

Known As

LeonaThe Lion King of SavanaclawPrince of the Afterglow SavannahThe Second Prince

Etymology

The given name 'Leona' is a feminine form of 'Leo', from Latin 'leo' meaning 'lion', fitting his lion beastman heritage. The surname 'Kingscholar' is a portmanteau of 'King' and 'Scholar', reflecting his royal lineage and intellectual prowess, though it also carries an ironic edge as he is a dropout. The name likely was designed to evoke both regal authority and academic ambition.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character created by Disney and Aniplex for the Japanese mobile game Twisted Wonderland (2016). The character is part of a franchise that reimagines Disney villains as sympathetic 'overblot' characters. The character's design and lore are inspired by Disney's The Lion King, but he is an original creation for the game. The character is owned by Disney.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans have speculated that Leona is a direct adaptation of Scar from The Lion King, but the game explicitly states he is inspired by Scar, not a direct adaptation. He is a separate character with his own backstory, personality, and role in the game's narrative. There is no official statement confirming or denying any direct connection beyond inspiration.

Description

Appearance

Leona is a tall, muscular beastman with lion features. He has tan skin, amber eyes, and a mane of short, dark brown hair with a distinctive black tuft at the end, reminiscent of a lion's tail. He has pointed ears, fangs, and a lion tail with a tuft of dark fur. He wears the Savanaclaw uniform: a dark, hooded jacket with a fur collar, open chest, and loose pants. His casual clothes include a loose shirt and a necklace with a fang pendant. He is often depicted with a lazy, confident smirk.

Symbols & Iconography

Associated with lions, the sun, and the savanna. His symbol is a stylized lion's head with a crown. He is often associated with the colors gold and black, reflecting his royal status and his darker nature. His unique magic, 'King's Roar', is visualized as a golden, lion-shaped shockwave.

Behaviour & Personality

Leona is arrogant, lazy, and fiercely proud. He often acts indifferent and sleeps during classes, but he is highly intelligent and strategic. He is blunt and sarcastic, often belittling others, but he shows a protective streak towards his vice-warden, Ruggie Bucchi, and his dorm members. He has a deep-seated inferiority complex regarding his older brother, Falena, and a fear of being forgotten. He is driven by a desire to prove his worth and surpass his brother, leading to reckless and sometimes ruthless actions. He is a skilled mage and a cunning strategist.

Domains & Habitats

He is the dorm leader of Savanaclaw, a dorm at Night Raven College that resembles a lion's den, with sandy, rocky terrain and a harsh environment. His homeland is the Afterglow Savannah, a sun-scorched kingdom in the world of Twisted Wonderland. He is most comfortable in hot, dry climates.

Version Differences

The Japanese and English versions of the game have some localization differences in dialogue and phrasing, but the core character remains the same. In the English version, his speech patterns are more colloquial and sarcastic, while in Japanese he uses more formal but condescending language. No significant differences in his backstory or personality have been noted.

Powers & Abilities

Unique Magic: King's Roar

Source Version Twisted Wonderland (game), both JP and EN

Description

Leona's signature spell allows him to unleash a powerful, lion-shaped shockwave that can petrify opponents. The spell's power increases with his physical strength and emotional intensity. It is considered one of the most powerful unique magics at Night Raven College.

Limitations

The spell requires significant magical energy and can be exhausting. It has a limited range and can be dodged. It is less effective against opponents with strong magical defenses.

Contradictions

Some fans have speculated that his magic is derived from Scar's abilities in The Lion King, but there is no canonical evidence for this. The game's official lore states it is his own unique magic.

Enhanced Physical Abilities

Stories & Myths

The Savanaclaw Arc (Main Story Chapter 2)

Source Twisted Wonderland (game)Culture Fictional, Japanese fantasyPeriod Modern (in-game timeline)

Summary

Leona, as dorm leader of Savanaclaw, plots to use a magical artifact called the Magical Shift to overthrow his brother, Falena, and become king of the Afterglow Savannah. He uses his dorm members and the protagonist (Yu) to achieve his goals. His plan is ultimately thwarted by the protagonist and the other dorm leaders. During the climax, Leona's magical power goes out of control, causing an 'overblot'—a state of magical corruption fueled by negative emotions. He is defeated and purified, and he later shows a hint of remorse and a desire to change.

Overblot Incident

Source Twisted Wonderland (game) - Main Story Chapter 2

Description

During the Savanaclaw arc, Leona's negative emotions (envy, pride, despair) cause his magical power to surge out of control, transforming him into a monstrous form. He attacks students and nearly kills the protagonist. This is a common plot device in the game, representing the dangers of unchecked negative emotions.

Scholarly Context

The 'overblot' concept is a fictional metaphor for mental health struggles, particularly the dangers of suppressing emotions. It is presented as a cautionary tale.

Contradictions & Disputes

Leona's motivation for his schemes

Position A

He genuinely wants to become king and will do anything to achieve it, even if it means harming others.

Position A — evidence

His actions in the main story, where he endangers students and uses underhanded tactics.

Position B

He is self-destructive and his schemes are a cry for help, as he feels trapped by his circumstances.

Position B — evidence

His backstory and post-overblot dialogue, where he expresses feelings of hopelessness and a desire to be seen.

Scholarly Consensus

Most fans interpret his actions as a combination of both: he is ambitious but also deeply insecure, and his overblot is a manifestation of his internal conflict.

His relationship with his brother

Position A

He hates Falena and wants to usurp him.

Position A — evidence

His plot to overthrow Falena.

Position B

He loves his brother but feels unworthy and resentful of his kindness.

Position B — evidence

His reaction to Falena's messages and his post-overblot regret.

Scholarly Consensus

The game suggests he has conflicting feelings, and his resentment stems from self-loathing rather than true hatred.

Who It Is

In the explosive world of My Hero Academia, few villains burn as brightly or as enigmatically as Dabi, a figure whose very name evokes the cremation of his own past. Behind his scarred visage and blue flames lies a meticulously crafted mystery that has captivated fans and reshaped the series' narrative landscape.

Dabi presents a calm, sardonic, and calculating exterior. He is highly intelligent and strategic, often manipulating events from the shadows. He speaks in a measured, almost bored tone, but can become intensely emotional and unhinged when his family or his past is involved. He is driven by a deep-seated hatred for his father, Endeavor, and a desire to expose the hero's hypocrisy. Despite his villainous actions, he is shown to be capable of forming connections, as seen in his interactions with other League of Villains members, particularly Shigaraki and Toga. Powers
Blueflame (Cremation)

Dabi possesses a powerful fire-based Quirk that generates intense blue flames. His flames are significantly hotter than his father Endeavor's red flames, capable of reducing nearly anything to ash. He can project flames from his body, create fireballs, and generate massive walls of fire. The blue color indicates extreme temperature.

Limit: His Quirk is not without cost. Dabi's body is not naturally suited to withstand his own flames, and overusing his power causes severe burns and physical damage to himself. He has undergone extensive surgery to graft skin and reinforce his body, but the damage is cumulative. This self-destructive aspect is a core part of his character.

Jet-Burn

A technique where Dabi releases a concentrated blast of blue flames from his palms, propelling himself forward like a jet. This allows him to move at high speeds and perform aerial maneuvers.

Limit: Like all his techniques, Jet-Burn causes self-inflicted burns and strain on his body.

Flashfire Fist

Dabi can use techniques similar to his father's Flashfire Fist, but with his blue flames. This includes creating massive fire pillars and shooting fire blasts. However, he does not use the full range of Endeavor's techniques, possibly due to his self-destructive nature.

Limit: His use of these techniques is limited by his body's tolerance. He cannot use them for extended periods without severe damage.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Dabi is a tall, lean young man with messy white hair and turquoise eyes. His most distinctive feature is the extensive scarred skin on his face and body, a result of his own quirk's overuse. The scars are a deep purple-pink, covering his chin, neck, and much of his torso and arms. He typically wears a dark, high-collared coat with a fur-lined interior, black pants, and metal-studded boots. His villain costume includes a mask-like metal plate cove

Symbols — Dabi's visual iconography revolves around fire and cremation. His blue flames are a symbol of his immense power and his rejection of his father's red flames. His scarred body serves as a visual testament to his suffering and his self-destructive path. The name 'Dabi' itself evokes cremation, symbolizing his desire to burn away his past identity. Accounts I
The League of Villains' Attack on the U.A. Training CampMy Hero Academia manga chapters 70-80, anime season 3

Modern (fictional future)

Dabi is introduced as a member of the Vanguard Action Squad, a group of villains sent to capture Katsuki Bakugo. He engages in combat with heroes, showcasing his powerful flames. He later participates in the attack on the training camp, helping to capture Bakugo. The attack escalates the conflict between heroes and villains, leading to the temporary capture of Bakugo and the rise of the League of Villains as a major threat.

Accounts II
The Pro Hero Arc and the Identity RevealMy Hero Academia manga chapters 290-291, anime season 5 episode 21

Modern (fictional future)

During a televised battle between the villains and the heroes, Dabi reveals his true identity as Touya Todoroki, the presumed-dead eldest son of Endeavor. He broadcasts a recording of his father's abusive past, exposing Endeavor's dark history to the world. This revelation sends shockwaves through society and the Todoroki family. Endeavor's reputation is destroyed, and the Todoroki family is thrown into turmoil. Dabi's revelation becomes a major turning point in the series, deepening the themes of heroism, legacy, and redemption.

The Final War ArcMy Hero Academia manga chapters 350-351, anime season 6 episodes 16-17

Modern (fictional future)

In the climactic battle, Dabi confronts his father and brother. He unleashes his flames at full power, but his body begins to fail him. He is ultimately defeated when his own flames consume him, but not before he nearly kills himself and others. His last moments are marked by a twisted desire for his father's acknowledgment. Dabi is severely injured and left in a critical state. He survives but is permanently incapacitated. His actions lead to a reevaluation of hero society and the Todoroki family's healing.

Dabi is a villain with no redeeming qualities. — Dabi is a complex character driven by trauma and a desire for justice against his abusive father. His actions are villainous, but his motives are sympathetic.

Dabi's flames are stronger than Endeavor's because they are blue. — In the series, blue flames are hotter than red flames, and Dabi's flames are indeed hotter than Endeavor's. However, this is not a simple comparison, as Endeavor has more control and experience.

Dabi is a direct adaptation of a mythological figure. — Dabi is an original character created by Kohei Horikoshi. He is not based on any specific mythological figure, though his name and fire theme may evoke associations with phoenixes or fire deities.

In the sprawling world of Re:Zero, where heroes and villains are often defined by their spectacular powers, Russell Fellow stands as a testament to the unglamorous but vital art of governance. A minor noble and the administrative head of the Flugel Tree, Russell's quiet competence ensures the wheels of the kingdom turn, even as the spotlight shines on those who wield swords and magic.

Who It Is
Form

Basajaun is described as a huge, hairy hominid dwelling in the woods. He is often depicted as a gentle giant, with a kind and benevolent nature.

Powers

Teaching and Protection Basajaun is said to teach humans skills such as agriculture and ironworking, and to protect flocks of livestock.

Accounts

Basajaun teaches humans how to farm and make iron tools, allowing them to thrive in the forest.

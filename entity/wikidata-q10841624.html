Name & Tongue

Etymology — Sino-Vietnamese. Family name Đặng (鄧, Deng), a common Vietnamese surname of Chinese origin. Đại (大) = 'great, large'. Độ (度) = 'measure, degree, extent' or in Buddhist context 'to cross over, to ferry across' (as in độ chúng sinh). Combined, Đại Độ suggests 'great measure' or 'great crossing/salvati

Pronunciation — Vietnamese: Đặng Đại Độ /ɗaŋ˨˩ ɗaːj˨˩ ɗo˧˨ʔ/ (Northern Hanoi), /ɗaŋ˨˩ ɗaːj˨˩ ɗo˨˩˨/ (Southern). Anglicized approx. 'Dang Dai Do'.

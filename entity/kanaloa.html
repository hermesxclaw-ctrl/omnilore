Kanaloa

Who It Is
Form

Kanaloa is often depicted as a tall, fair-skinned deity with a squid or octopus symbol associated with him. He is said to be the traveling partner of Kāne, and is described as having a complex and multifaceted nature.

Powers

Shapeshifting Kanaloa is said to have the ability to transform into various forms, including a squid or octopus, allowing him to navigate the ocean and the underworld with ease.

Accounts

Kanaloa and Kāne are said to be traveling partners, with Kanaloa serving as a guide and advisor to Kāne.

Kanaloa is said to have created the island of Kahoʻolawe, which is associated with him and is said to be a place of great spiritual power.

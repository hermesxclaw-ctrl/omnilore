Who It Is
Form

The Great Old Ones are often described as massive, tentacled creatures with bulging, pulsing bodies and twisted, elongated heads. They are said to be capable of changing their shape and form at will, and are often depicted as being surrounded by a cloud of noxious, greenish-yellow gas. The Great Old Ones are also said to have a profound impact on the human psyche, causing madness and terror in those who encounter them. In addition to their physical appearance, the Great Old Ones are also associated with a range of symbolic and iconic imagery, including the use of strange, non-Euclidean geometry and the presence of eerie, otherworldly sounds. The appearance of the Great Old Ones is often used to convey their otherworldly, malevolent nature, and to create a sense of cosmic horror and dread in those who encounter them.

Powers

Reality Warping The Great Old Ones are said to have the power to warp and manipulate reality itself, creating twisted, nightmarish landscapes and bending the laws of physics and time to their will.

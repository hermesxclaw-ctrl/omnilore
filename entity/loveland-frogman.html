It wasn't an animal on all fours. It stood up -- on two legs. It had the skin of a frog, but the posture of a man. And then it leaped, straight over the guardrail and into the water with a splash.

Who It Is

The Loveland Frogman is one of cryptozoology's most unique creatures -- a humanoid amphibian reported from the small city of Loveland, Ohio, along the banks of the Little Miami River. The first reported sighting occurred in 1955, when a man claimed to see three bipedal, frog-like creatures under a bridge. The most famous incident occurred on the night of March 3, 1972, when two Loveland police officers -- Ray Shockey and Mark Matthews -- independently reported encounters. Officer Shockey was driving along Riverside Drive when he saw a creature crouched by the roadside. As his headlights illuminated it, the creature stood upright: about 3 to 4 feet tall, with leathery, frog-like skin, a wide mouth, and large, webbed hands. It then jumped over the guardrail and into the river. Two weeks later, Officer Matthews reported seeing a similar creature lying in the road; he shot at it, but claimed the bullet had no effect, and the creature escaped. In 2016, two Pokemon Go players near the same area reported seeing a giant frog-like creature by the river, reigniting interest. Later in life, Officer Matthews recanted, claiming the creature he shot was actually a large pet iguana that had escaped -- though this explanation has been challenged by some researchers. The Frogman has become Ohio's most iconic cryptid, celebrated in local festivals, craft beers, and merchandise. Herpetologists suggest the creature may be a misidentified large bullfrog, an escaped exotic pet, or a person in a costume.

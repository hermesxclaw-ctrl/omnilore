Who It Is

A tall, emaciated humanoid that becomes extremely agitated when anyone views its face — whether directly, via photograph, or through video footage. Once its face is seen, SCP-096 enters an unstoppable rage state, traveling at unknown speeds directly to the viewer and killing them. No known material or distance can prevent it from reaching its target. After killing, it returns to a docile state, crying and whimpering.

Form

Approximately 2.38 meters tall, extremely emaciated with pale, hairless skin. Its arms are disproportionately long, reaching past its knees. Its jaw can unhinge to four times its normal size. Its face is its sole anomalous feature.

Powers

Invulnerability during rage state

Impossible speed and tracking

Target location across any distance

Destruction of any barrier between it and target

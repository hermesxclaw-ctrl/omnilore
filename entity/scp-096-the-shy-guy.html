Who It Is
Form

SCP-096 is a humanoid creature with a blank face, which is its most distinctive feature. Its face is completely featureless, with no eyes, nose, or mouth. It has a humanoid body, but its exact appearance can vary depending on the situation.

Powers

Faceless Pursuit SCP-096's ability to pursue individuals who have seen its face is its most notable power. It can track its targets across vast distances and will not stop until it has caught up to them.

Incident Log

SCP-096 is a major antagonist in the 2008 anomaly series, SCP Foundation. It is a Euclid class anomaly contained by the Foundation, who will know if someone looked at his face and relentlessly pursue that individual until they are contained or killed.

SCP-096 is a humanoid creature that becomes extremely hostile when any part of its face is seen. At some point, SCP-096 was captured by the SCP Foundation and placed under the supervision of Dr. Dan Daniels.

Who It Is

The Living Flame, a Great Old One composed entirely of fiery plasma contained within an invisible shell. Cthugha appears as a vast, pulsating sphere of stellar fire that drifts through the void between galaxies. Its servants are the Fire Vampires — smaller flames that obey its will and burn through reality itself.

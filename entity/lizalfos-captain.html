Who It Is

An elite lizard-like warrior, more intelligent and deadly than common Lizalfos. The Captain wears bronze armor etched with dragon motifs, wields a curved scimitar and a spiked shield, and can breathe a short cone of fire. Its tongue is prehensile and can snatch weapons from an opponent's hand.

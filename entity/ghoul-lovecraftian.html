Who It Is
Form

Ghouls are typically depicted as monstrous, humanoid creatures with a strong connection to death and the supernatural. They are often described as having a grotesque appearance, with features such as long arms, sharp teeth, and a penchant for cannibalism.

Powers

Cannibalism Ghouls are known to feed on the flesh of the dead, often using their sharp teeth and long arms to tear flesh from bone.

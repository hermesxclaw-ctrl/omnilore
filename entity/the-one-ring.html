Who It Is
Form

The One Ring is described as a golden ring with a black inscription in Tengwar script, bearing the words 'Ash Nazg Durbatulûk, Ash Nazg Gimbatul, Ash Nazg Thrakatulûk agh Burzum-ishi krimpatul'.

Powers

Granting invisibility The Ring allows its wearer to become invisible, making it a powerful tool for stealth and deception.

Granting power The Ring grants its wearer immense power and control over others, making it a coveted object in Middle-earth.

Accounts

Frodo Baggins, a hobbit, is tasked with destroying the One Ring in the fires of Mount Doom.

The One Ring was forged by Sauron in the fires of Mount Doom and was later lost in the depths of the sea.

Momoko is a name that carries the sweetness of peaches and the weight of a thousand stories — from a Japanese girl's given name to a galaxy of fictional heroines, athletes, artists, and even a fashion doll. This dossier explores the many faces of Momoko, tracing its linguistic roots, its real-world bearers, and its vibrant presence in global pop culture.

Etymology

Momoko is a Japanese feminine given name composed of two kanji: 'momo' (桃) meaning 'peach' or (百) meaning 'one hundred' or (杏) meaning 'apricot', and 'ko' (子) meaning 'child'. The combination typically conveys 'peach child' or 'hundred children'.

Momoko Sakura and Chibi Maruko-chan

Source Manga and anime seriesCulture JapanesePeriod 1986–present

Summary

Momoko Sakura created the autobiographical manga 'Chibi Maruko-chan', featuring a young girl named Momoko (Maruko) based on herself. The series depicts the everyday life of Maruko and her family in 1970s Japan.

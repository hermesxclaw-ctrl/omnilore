Cultural Significance

The Mandela Effect is a fascinating hybrid: a demonstrated psychological phenomenon (false memory, confabulation) that has been mythologized into a belief system about the nature of reality. It reflects the deep human need for our memories to be 'true' and our willingness to rewrite physics to preserve them.

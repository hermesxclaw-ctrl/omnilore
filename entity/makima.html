Who It Is

Makima is the smile that promises warmth and the hand that crushes without a touch—a devil who wears humanity like a veil, and whose true name is Control. In Chainsaw Man, she is the woman who offers a starving boy a home, only to reveal that every kindness was a leash, and every leash was a step toward reshaping the world in her image.

Makima presents a calm, gentle, and nurturing facade, often smiling softly and speaking in a soothing tone. She is highly intelligent, manipulative, and calculating, viewing others as tools or pets. She is driven by a desire to create a 'better world' by eliminating concepts she deems harmful, such as death, war, and famine, but her methods are ruthless and inhumane. She shows genuine affection towards Denji, but it is a twisted form of love, as she sees him as a loyal dog. Her true nature is that of a sociopath, devoid of empathy for humans.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Fujimoto stated that her name is a play on 'chainsaw' (a cutting tool); removing the syllable 'ki' (切) from 'Makima' yields 'mama', reflecting Denji's search for maternal figures. The name also conveys a sense of dominance and control.

Pronunciation — MAH-kee-mah

Aliases — Control Devil, Conquest Devil (in some translations), The Controller, Mama (wordplay)

Ownership — Created by Tatsuki Fujimoto for the manga series Chainsaw Man, published by Shueisha. The character is part of Japanese manga and anime culture, and has gained global recognition.

Powers
Mind Control

Makima can control anyone she considers inferior to her. She can issue commands that are absolutely obeyed, overriding free will. This power is limited to those she perceives as lower in status or strength, and it works on both humans and devils.

Limit: She cannot control those who are her equals or superiors. For example, she cannot control other Horsemen or the original Chainsaw Devil in his full form. Her control is also ineffective on those with strong willpower or those who are not 'inferior' to her.

Telekinetic Head Crush

Makima can crush a person's head with a hand gesture, as if squeezing a ball. This is typically performed after making a contract with the victim, involving a ritual where she sacrifices other humans.

Limit: Requires a prior contract or ritual, often involving the sacrifice of other humans. The target must be within her line of sight and she must perform the gesture.

Finger Gun

Makima can fire a projectile from her finger in the shape of a gun, similar to a bullet, capable of killing devils and humans.

Limit: Limited by her own physical stamina; she cannot use it endlessly. It is not shown to be effective against the strongest devils.

Spying via Animals

Makima can see through the eyes of rats, crows, and other animals, using them as spies.

Limit: Requires her to be aware of the animals' locations; she cannot see through them at will unless they are within her range.

Immortality via Contracts

Makima has made a contract with the Japanese Prime Minister, granting her immortality as long as the Prime Minister is alive. Any fatal injury she sustains is transferred to a random Japanese citizen, who dies in her place.

Limit: The contract is tied to the life of the Prime Minister. If the Prime Minister dies, the contract may be void. Also, the transfer of death is random, so she is not invulnerable if the entire population is killed, but this is a high bar.

Devil Contract with Chainsaw Devil

Makima has a contract with Pochita, the Chainsaw Devil, which allows her to use some of his powers, such as the ability to erase concepts by eating them. However, this is not fully explored, as she is ultimately killed before she can fully utilize it.

Limit: She cannot fully control Pochita; she attempts to force him to submit, but he resists. The contract is one-sided, as Pochita is not willingly bound.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Makima is a young woman with long, red hair tied in a single braid, and distinctive yellow eyes with red concentric rings. She typically wears the Public Safety Devil Hunter uniform, which resembles a security guard's outfit: a dark blazer, white shirt, and tie. Her appearance is designed to be attractive and unassuming, masking her true demonic nature.

Symbols — Her eyes are a key symbol, representing her control and perception. The concentric circles echo the concept of domination and hypnotism. She is often depicted with leashes and collars, symbolizing her desire to control others, particularly Denji. The act of the finger gun gesture is iconic, representing her lethal control.

Domains — Makima operates within the human world, primarily in Tokyo, as the head of Public Safety Division 4. She has no specific realm, but she is associated with bureaucratic institutions and the underworld of devil hunting. She is a Horseman of the Apocalypse, representing Conquest/Control, and her domain is the human soul and will.

Accounts I
The Beginning of the EndChainsaw Man, Chapter 1

Modern era (2018)

Denji, a young man with a chainsaw devil companion, Pochita, is recruited by Makima after he is killed by yakuza. She offers him food and shelter in exchange for his service as a devil hunter, treating him like a dog. This sets the stage for their relationship. Denji becomes a Public Safety Devil Hunter under Makima's tutelage, entering a world of violence and manipulation.

The Eternity Devil ArcChainsaw Man, Chapters 22-28

Modern era (2018)

Makima sends Denji, Aki, and Power to fight the Eternity Devil, who traps them in a hotel. Makima appears to be testing Denji, and she uses the situation to further her plans by observing his abilities. Denji defeats the Eternity Devil, but the encounter reveals Makima's manipulative nature as she had orchestrated the mission to assess Denji's potential.

Accounts II
The Gun Devil ArcChainsaw Man, Chapters 40-52

Modern era (2018)

Makima orchestrates a plan to defeat the Gun Devil, using Denji and the other devil hunters as pawns. She reveals her ability to control others and her willingness to sacrifice them for her goals. She also uses the ritual of sacrificing humans to gain power. The Gun Devil is defeated, but many devil hunters die. Aki is severely injured, and Makima's true nature becomes more apparent to Denji.

The Control Devil RevealChainsaw Man, Chapters 79-84

Modern era (2018)

Makima reveals her true identity as the Control Devil and her plan to eliminate concepts like death, war, and famine by using the Chainsaw Devil's power. She kills Aki and Power, and forces Denji to fight her, intending to make him submit. Denji, in despair, loses his will to live, but Pochita takes over and fights Makima. Ultimately, Denji and Pochita defeat her by having Denji eat her, leading to her reincarnation as Nayuta.

The Final Battle and DeathChainsaw Man, Chapters 85-97

Modern era (2018)

Denji, with the help of Pochita and Power's blood, fights Makima. He uses the chainsaw to cut her into pieces, then cooks and eats her, symbolically consuming her control. This act of 'love' causes the Control Devil to reincarnate as Nayuta, a child who Denji takes in as his sister. Makima dies, but the Control Devil is reborn as Nayuta, who retains some of Makima's memories but is a new being. Denji finds a new purpose in caring for Nayuta.

Makima is the Chainsaw Devil. — Makima is the Control Devil, not the Chainsaw Devil. The Chainsaw Devil is Pochita, who resides within Denji.

Makima is a human with devil powers. — Makima is a devil, specifically the Control Devil, who takes on a human form. She is not human.

Makima is invincible. — Makima has immortality through her contract with the Prime Minister, but this is not absolute. She can be killed if the contract is broken or if she is killed in a way that bypasses the transfer, as shown when Denji eats her.

Makima is a hero. — While she presents herself as a public servant, she is the main antagonist of Part 1, with malevolent goals.

Who It Is

Makima is the primary antagonist of Chainsaw Man Part 1 and one of the most chillingly manipulative villains in modern manga — the Control Devil, one of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse, embodying the concept of control, conquest, and domination. She is the leader of the Public Safety Devil Hunter Division in Tokyo, and she presents herself as a calm, professional, and endlessly patient woman in a sharp business suit. This facade hides a being of terrifying power and absolute amorality. Makima's power is the ability to control any being she perceives as 'inferior' to her. This includes virtually all humans and most devils. She can issue commands that cannot be refused, read the minds and senses of those she controls, and use their lives as shields or sacrifices. She has hundreds of citizens and devil hunters under her control at any time, and she uses them ruthlessly. Her ultimate goal is to possess Chainsaw Man (Denji) — not out of affection, but because she believes that if she can control the Chainsaw Devil, she can use his world-erasing power to create a 'better' world where all the things she hates (like death, inequality, and suffering) are erased from existence. Makima's relationship with Denji is the cruelest manipulation in the series. She gives him everything he has ever wanted — food, a home, a job, the promise of love — but it is all a lie designed to break him down so she can fully possess him. She represents control in its most insidious form: the control that gives you everything you want so you never realize you have nothing. Her defeat requires Denji to literally eat her, and she is reincarnated as Nayuta, a younger Control Devil whom Denji raises as a sister. Makima embodies the terror of control — the horror of realizing that your desires, your relationships, and your very will belong to someone else, that you are a puppet dancing on strings you cannot see.

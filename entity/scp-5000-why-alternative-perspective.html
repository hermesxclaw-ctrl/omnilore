Who It Is
Form

SCP-5000 is a non-functional mechanical suit, approximately 2 meters in height, with a metallic exoskeleton and various mechanical components.

Powers

Informational Hiding SCP-5000 has the ability to conceal and protect sensitive information, making it a valuable asset for the SCP Foundation.

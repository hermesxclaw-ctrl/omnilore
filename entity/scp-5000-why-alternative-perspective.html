Who It Is

A supplementary file to the main SCP-5000 document. While the primary file details the recovered suit and Pietro Wilson's recording, this file contains the Foundation's post-recovery analysis: the 'Pestilence' that caused the alternate Foundation to exterminate humanity has been identified as 'empathy' — the anomalous capacity for humans to feel each other's pain. The alternate Foundation's conclusion was that empathy was a memetic parasite, inserted into the human genome by an unknown hostile force, designed to make humanity weak and manipulable. The alternate Foundation wasn't evil — they were trying to cure humanity of a disease. Our Foundation's assessment: they may have been right. We are studying the possibility that empathy is not natural.

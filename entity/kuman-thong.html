Who It Is

Kuman Thong is a tutelary spirit housed in an effigy, traditionally created from a child who died before birth. Modern versions are commercially produced statues treated like adopted children — offered toys and sweets in exchange for protection and prosperity. The practice blends Buddhism, animism, and ancestor veneration.

Attested in Thai folk tradition (Ayutthaya period)Era Ayutthaya period to present

Dab Pog are the ancestral grandmother spirits in Hmong religion, the venerated female ancestors who watch over their descendants. They are among the most important of all household spirits, responsible for the fertility of women, the health of children, and the prosperity of the family line.

Who It Is

Dab Pog are the ancestral grandmother spirits in Hmong religion, the venerated female ancestors who watch over their descendants. They are among the most important of all household spirits, responsible for the fertility of women, the health of children, and the prosperity of the family line.

Cultural Significance

The veneration of Dab Pog reflects the important role of women and matriarchs in Hmong society. While male ancestors are also honored, the Dab Pog hold a special place as protectors of the domestic sphere and guarantors of family continuity. The tradition reinforces the Hmong emphasis on filial piety and the ongoing relationship between the living and the dead. In Hmong funeral customs, the elaborate multi-day rites are designed in part to ensure that the deceased becomes a benevolent ancestor spirit rather than a restless ghost.

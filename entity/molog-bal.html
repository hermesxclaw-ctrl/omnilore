Who It Is
Form

Molog Bal is often depicted as a Daedric Prince with a dark and ominous presence. He is associated with the color black and is said to reside in the realm of Coldharbour.

Powers

Manipulation Molog Bal is known for his ability to manipulate the thoughts and actions of others, often using his charm and intelligence to achieve his goals.

Accounts

According to Dunmeri folklore, Molog Bal was the chieftain of the Dreugh in a time when they ruled the world.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Samara' is derived from the Hebrew word for 'guardian' or 'protector', while 'Sadako' is a Japanese name meaning 'correct' or 'beautiful'.

Pronunciation — Samara: /səˈmɑːrə/ (sə-MAH-ruh), Sadako: /sɑːdɑːkoʊ/ (sah-DAH-koh)

Aliases — Samara, Sadako, The Girl in the Well, The Vengeful Spirit

Ownership — The character of Samara Morgan / Sadako Yamamura is owned by Koji Suzuki, the author of the novel 'Ring', and is a part of the popular horror franchise 'The Ring'.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Samara Morgan / Sadako Yamamura is typically depicted as a long-haired, pale-skinned girl with a menacing gaze. In the original novel, she is described as having a 'beautiful' face with 'sharp' features. In the film adaptations, she is often shown with a more gaunt appearance and a distinctive, unsettling smile.

Symbols — Samara Morgan / Sadako Yamamura is often associated with the cursed videotape, which is a central plot device in the 'Ring' franchise. She is also linked to the well, where she is said to reside.

Domains — The character of Samara Morgan / Sadako Yamamura is often associated with the supernatural realm, where she is said to reside in a well. She is also linked to the world of the living, where she seeks to harm those who have seen the cursed videotape.

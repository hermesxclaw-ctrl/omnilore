Who It Is
Form

Samara Morgan / Sadako Yamamura is typically depicted as a long-haired, pale-skinned girl with a menacing gaze. In the original novel, she is described as having a 'beautiful' face with 'sharp' features. In the film adaptations, she is often shown with a more gaunt appearance and a distinctive, unsettling smile.

Powers

Vengeful Spirit Samara Morgan / Sadako Yamamura is able to possess the bodies of those who have seen the cursed videotape, allowing her to exact revenge on her victims.

Accounts

The story follows a journalist who discovers a cursed videotape that causes the viewer to die in seven days. As the journalist delves deeper into the mystery, he discovers that the tape is connected to a vengeful spirit named Samara Morgan / Sadako Yamamura.

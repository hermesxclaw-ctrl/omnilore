Who It Is

She can kick a speeding car off its axis, snap a tennis racket with one finger, and then apologize for burning the stew — Yor Forger is Ostania's deadliest assassin pretending to be its most anxious housewife, a woman who learned to kill before she learned to cook, and who now guards a fake family as if it were the only real thing she has ever had.

Civilian Yor is gentle, earnest, deeply insecure, and socially awkward — a result of raising her brother alone from childhood and having little peer socialization. She is polite to a fault, apologizes excessively, overthinks social cues, and desperately wants to be a 'normal wife and mother' to protect Yuri from suspicion and to keep her new family. She is nurturing toward Anya (packing elaborate lunches, worrying about school) and deferential toward Loid, often misreading his spy-calculated kindness as genuine affection which flusters her. Powers
Superhuman Strength

Yor can kick cars off roads, stop speeding vehicles, dent metal, throw opponents through walls, and carry multiple adults. She effortlessly hurls a car tire, kicks a tennis ball through a racket and net, and on the cruise ship impales and launches adult male assassins with single blows. Strength is presented as innate, not gadget-based.

Limit: Strength is not precisely quantified; she still tires after prolonged combat (cruise arc shows breathing hard after consecutive fights). She can misjudge restraint — accidentally breaking objects, injuring Loid during mock sparring. No evidence of bulletproof durability; she dodges rather than tanks gunfire.

Superhuman Speed, Agility and Reflexes

Can dodge bullets at close range, intercept gunmen before they fire, leap several stories, and move faster than trained soldiers perceive. Cruise arc shows her deflecting knives and evading automatic fire in confined ship corridors.

Limit: Speed requires line-of-sight and footing; she can be surprised (e.g., Anya's unexpected actions, Loid's spy traps). High heels as Thorn Princess do not impede her.

Master Assassin / Hand-to-Hand Combat and Weapon Mastery

Trained from childhood by Garden, Yor is a master of unarmed combat, pressure-point strikes, and improvised weapons. Signature weapons are golden stiletto-like needles/awls (sometimes described as thorn weapons) used for stabbing, throwing, and parrying. Also proficient with knives, guns, and environmental objects. Uses acrobatic kicks and joint locks.

Limit: Prefers close range; at long range she must close distance or throw needles with limited accuracy vs firearms. Training is assassin-specific, not formal martial arts — she lacks spy tradecraft (disguise, lockpicking) that Loid possesses.

Pain Tolerance and Durability

Takes stabbings, gunshot grazes, and blunt trauma and continues fighting. On cruise she is stabbed and continues to protect Olka and Anya, using pain to focus. Recovers quickly between missions with no lasting injury shown.

Limit: Not invulnerable; she bleeds, bruises, and requires rest and Shopkeeper's care. Durability is high but not supernatural healing.

Domestic and Social Camouflage (Cover Skills)

Ability to maintain perfect civilian cover as City Hall employee and Forger wife/mother. Passes background checks, performs clerical work, and performs 'normal mother' behaviors (cooking, PTA meetings) despite initial incompetence. Her very ordinariness is a skill that deflects Secret Police suspicion.

Limit: Cooking and homemaking initially poor (lethal stews, burnt food); social anxiety makes her easily flustered and prone to over-explaining. Cover depends on Loid's coaching.

Maternal Instinct and Protective Rage (Berserk State)

When Anya or family is threatened, Yor enters heightened state with even greater strength and ferocity, ignoring personal safety. This is narrative-coded as maternal instinct amplifying combat effectiveness.

Limit: Emotional trigger can make her reckless and less tactical; Loid must sometimes restrain or redirect her.

Accounts I
Operation Strix: Securing a Wife (Introduction)Spy × Family Chapter 1-2, Volume 1 (Shueisha, 2019); Anime Episode 1-2 (2022)

2019-present (in-universe: late 1960s Berlint)

Loid Forger (Twilight) needs a wife to enroll Anya at Eden Academy. Yor Briar, 27, City Hall clerk who secretly works as Garden assassin Thorn Princess, needs a fake husband because single women are suspected of being spies. Her brother Yuri (Secret Police) pressures her to find a partner. Loid and Yor meet at a boutique; after Yor drunkenly impresses with strength and then is attacked by harassers, Loid offers a contract marriage. They marry quickly, each hiding their true profession, while Anya (telepath) knows both secrets and is delighted. Establishes the Forger fake family, the central dramatic irony (neither spouse knows the other's secret), and Yor's dual life. Sets up recurring tension: Yor must be a convincing mother/wife while continuing assassinations.

Eden Academy Interview and the Thorn Princess InterventionSpy × Family Chapters 5-7, Volume 2; Anime Episode 5-6

2019-2020

For the Eden interview, Yor must act as elegant mother. She trains with Loid and Anya. On interview day, when former Eden headmaster Swan provokes Anya to tears by insulting Yor's motherhood, Yor nearly attacks him but restrains herself; Loid punches Swan instead. Later, Yor single-handedly stops a rampaging cow and later a car to save Anya, revealing strength to Loid who rationalizes it as 'maternal love.' The family passes the interview on supplementary list. Shows Yor's protective motherhood as genuine despite contract, and establishes Loid's growing respect for her. Introduces Eden Academy as long-term setting.

Yuri Briar Visit — Sister Complex vs. Fake HusbandSpy × Family Chapters 10-13, Volume 3; Anime Episode 8-9

2020

Yuri, Yor's younger brother and Secret Police lieutenant, visits to meet Loid. Obsessively attached to Yor (who raised him after their parents died young), Yuri suspects Loid is unworthy. Yor, desperate to maintain cover and protect Yuri's worldview, insists the marriage is happy. Loid and Yuri engage in tense interrogation over wine (Yuri drugged). Yor's loyalty to both brother and new family is tested; she ultimately defends Loid, signaling shift toward chosen family. Deepens Yor's backstory (orphaned, raised Yuri alone, took assassin work to survive) and Yuri's antagonism. Cements Yor's commitment to Forger family.

Accounts II
Cruise Adventure Arc — Thorn Princess vs. AssassinsSpy × Family Chapters 58-66, Volumes 9-10 (2022); Anime Season 2 Episodes 25-35 (2023)

2022-2023

Yor is assigned by Garden (via Shopkeeper at flower shop) to protect Olka Gretcher, former mafia boss's wife, and her infant son Zach on the Princess Lorelei cruise. Loid and Anya coincidentally win same cruise. Yor spends days fighting waves of assassins hired by Olka's pursuers, culminating in a prolonged battle against elite swordsman Leon and gunmen in the ship's hold while Loid (unaware) handles a bomb threat elsewhere. Anya secretly helps Yor by misdirecting enemies. Yor is stabbed but prevails, ensures Olka escapes with new identity, and returns to family with injuries she hides as seasickness. Longest showcase of Thorn Princess combat and Garden lore; confirms Garden targets criminals/traffickers, not innocents, framing Yor as anti-hero. Strengthens Yor-Anya bond (Anya witnesses mother's heroism without revealing telepathy).

Tennis / Friendship Scheme and Daily Motherhood ArcsSpy × Family Chapters 14-16 (Campbelldon Tennis), Chapters 18-20, 30, 45; Anime Episode 12, 15, 18

2020-2021

Interwoven episodic stories where Yor attempts to be a normal mother: learning to cook (with disastrous early stews that Loid and Anya endure), sewing Anya's clothes, attending Eden social gatherings, and participating in the underground tennis tournament (where she and Loid pose as married couple to obtain a painting containing microfilm). Yor's tennis serve destroys rackets, again displaying strength. These vignettes show gradual domestic competence and Yor's earnest effort to belong. Humanizes Yor beyond assassin role; provides comedy and shows character growth from terrible cook to capable homemaker. Reinforces theme that family is performance becoming real.

Spy × Family Code: White — Frigis Family VacationSpy × Family Code: White, TOHO film (2023), directed by Takashi Katagiri, original story by Ichiro Okouchi supervised by Tatsuya Endo

2023 (in-universe winter)

The Forgers travel to Frigis for Anya's Eden cooking competition (and Loid's mission to retrieve microfilm in a chocolate). Yor has parallel subplot: she misinterprets Loid's cold mission-focus as marital dissatisfaction and, encouraged by coworkers, resolves to be more alluring wife, leading to comedic attempts at seduction. Climax involves Yor fighting on a military airship, single-handedly holding off soldiers to protect Anya while Loid disarms a weapon. Film ends with family reaffirming bonds over Anya's (failed) dessert. Non-serialized but widely viewed (box office >6 billion yen) expansion of Yor's action role and marital anxiety theme. Considered semi-canonical; no contradictions to manga continuity.

Yor is a trained spy like Loid or works for WISE. — Yor is not a spy and has no espionage training; she is an assassin for Garden, a separate organization. She has no knowledge of WISE, Twilight, or spy tradecraft, and her cover as City Hall clerk is not a spy cover but a civilian job.

Thorn Princess is Yor's spy codename or she is known as 'Briar Princess.' — Thorn Princess (Ibara Hime) is her assassin codename from Garden; 'Briar Princess' is a literal translation variant but official English is Thorn Princess.

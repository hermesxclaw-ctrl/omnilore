Who It Is
Form

Samigina is described as a demon with a specific appearance, although the details are not consistently reported across sources. In some accounts, she is depicted as a female figure, while in others she is described as a male entity. Her appearance is often associated with darkness, chaos, and spiritual corruption.

Powers

Necromancy Samigina is said to possess the power of necromancy, allowing her to communicate with the dead and manipulate the spirits of the deceased.

Divination Samigina is associated with the power of divination, said to grant her the ability to predict the future and reveal hidden knowledge.

Seduction Samigina is said to possess the power of seduction, allowing her to manipulate the desires and emotions of those around her.

Accounts

According to the text, Samigina is summoned by the magician using a specific ritual and incantation. The demon appears to the magician, offering to grant him knowledge and power in exchange for his loyalty and service.

According to the text, Samigina is described as a corrupting influence, said to lead seekers of truth into moral corruption and spiritual ruin.

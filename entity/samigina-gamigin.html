Who It Is

Fourth spirit of the Ars Goetia, a Marquis ruling 30 legions. Gamigin (also Samigina) appears in the form of a small horse or donkey, then transforms into a human figure at the conjurer's request. He speaks with a hoarse voice and teaches all liberal sciences. His most distinctive power is to summon the souls of the dead — particularly those who died in sin or drowned — to appear before the conjurer in an aerial body. These summoned shades will answer all questions put to them. His role bridges necromancy and academia.

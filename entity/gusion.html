Who It Is

Eleventh spirit, a Duke commanding 40 legions. Gusion appears as a cynocephalus (dog-headed being, like a baboon-headed entity), a form associated with Thoth in Egyptian tradition. He discerns all things past, present, and future and provides the true answer to any question asked. Most uniquely among the Goetia, Gusion reconciles friendships and restores honor — he is a demon of social repair, mending broken bonds between people and restoring lost dignities. He exemplifies the Goetic capacity for constructive social magic despite his infernal nature.

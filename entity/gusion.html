Dominion over forty legions of demons

Form & Signs

Appearance — Gusion is often depicted as a powerful and intimidating figure, with a strong presence and a commanding voice. He is said to be a Great Duke of Hell, ruling over forty legions of demons. His appearance is often associated with darkness and chaos.

Symbols — Gusion is associated with the symbols of darkness and chaos, including the use of black candles and the invocation of dark energies. He is also associated with the use of magical tools and artifacts.

Domains — Gusion is said to inhabit the domains of darkness and chaos, including the underworld and the realm of the dead. He is also said to inhabit the domains of magic and the supernatural.

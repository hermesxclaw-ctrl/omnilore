Who It Is
Form

Gusion is often depicted as a powerful and intimidating figure, with a strong presence and a commanding voice. He is said to be a Great Duke of Hell, ruling over forty legions of demons. His appearance is often associated with darkness and chaos.

Powers

Dominion over forty legions of demons Gusion is said to have dominion over forty legions of demons, giving him significant power and influence in the underworld. He is also said to have the ability to command and control other demons, using his powers of persuasion and manipulation to achieve his goals.

Accounts

Gusion is said to have been a powerful and intimidating demon, ruling over forty legions of demons in the underworld. He was known for his commanding presence and his willingness to take risks, often challenging the authority of other demons and spirits.

Gusion is said to be a powerful and intimidating figure, often invoked by practitioners of magic and the supernatural. He is said to have the ability to grant wishes and provide guidance, but at a significant cost.

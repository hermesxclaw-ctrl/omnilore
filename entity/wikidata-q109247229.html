Who It Is

In a universe where a single box can topple a century-old government, Alberto Vist was raised to inherit the key — and then told he was never worthy to turn it. The blond heir of the Vist Foundation, with his rimless glasses and immaculate suit, watches from the shadows as his estranged half-brother is handed the white Unicorn Gundam, a machine built to judge humanity's future. Alberto does not pilot it. He bankrolls it, betrays it, and tries to buy it back.

Attested across novel and OVA: Highly intelligent, educated, fluent in corporate and political maneuvering. Raised as the designated heir of the Vist Foundation by his father Cardeas and aunt Martha Vist Carbine after his mother Anna's death. Outwardly polite, deferential, and soft-spoken, but internally resentful, insecure, and desperate for paternal validation. Cardeas's decision to entrust the Unicorn and Laplace's Box to his illegitimate half-brother Banagher Links — a boy Alberto has just met — is the central wound driving his actions. Name & Tongue

Etymology — Given name Alberto is Italian/Spanish form of Adalbert ('noble, bright'). Surname Vist is fictional corporate dynasty name created for Gundam Unicorn, derived from the Vist Foundation (ビスト財団, Bisuto Zaidan). In-universe, the family name originates with founder Syam Vist. Aliases — アルベルト・ビスト (Aruberuto Bisuto), Alberto Vist Carbine (fan conflation with Martha Carbine, incorrect)

Ownership — Japanese media franchise Mobile Suit Gundam, owned by Sunrise (Bandai Namco Filmworks) and Sotsu. Character created by author Harutoshi Fukui and character designer Kumiko Takahash

Powers
Corporate and Financial Authority

As heir to the Vist Foundation and executive linked to Anaheim Electronics, Alberto can mobilize immense capital, industrial resources, and private military assets. He authorizes mobile suit transfers, funds research, and controls access to Industrial 7.

Limit: Authority is conditional and revocable — Martha Vist Carbine and the Foundation's elders can override him; after Cardeas's death his claim is contested. He cannot command Federation or Zeon forces directly.

Political Brokerage and Intelligence

Skilled negotiator and information broker who leverages the Laplace Box secret to bargain with both the Earth Federation (Martha, Ronan Marcenas) and Neo Zeon remnants (Full Frontal). Understands blackmail, secrecy, and the value of the Box as a political weapon.

Limit: Often outmaneuvered by more experienced players (Martha, Full Frontal, Syam). His deals frequently backfire, leaving him without leverage.

Interpersonal Manipulation and Coercion by Proxy

Ability to order or enable the deployment of enhanced humans and weapons, notably authorizing the use of Marida Cruz (Ple Twelve) as pilot of the Kshatriya and later Banshee-adjacent operations. Uses emotional leverage rather than direct force.

Limit: No personal combat ability; relies on others to execute violence. Shows moral hesitation that undermines ruthlessness.

Social Capital and Elite Education

Fluent in high society, diplomacy, and Universal Century history. Educated to understand the Laplace incident, Charter, and Newtype theory at a scholarly level, even if he cannot experience it.

Limit: Intellectual understanding does not translate to Newtype empathy or piloting skill; he remains an outsider to the psychic/spiritual core of Gundam Unicorn.

No Newtype or Cyber Newtype Abilities (Explicit Lack)

Canonically an Oldtype. Cannot sense others psychically, cannot pilot Psycoframe at full resonance, cannot withstand high-G Newtype combat. This lack is narratively significant — he is the heir who cannot inherit the Unicorn's true purpose.

Limit: N/A — this is a limitation itself.

Accounts I
The Vist Legacy and a Childhood DeniedMobile Suit Gundam Unicorn Novel Vol.1 'Day of the Unicorn' and Vol.8 flashbacks; OVA Ep.1 exposition

In-universe UC 0070s-0090s; published 2007-2010

Alberto is born as the legitimate son of Cardeas Vist and his wife Anna Vist, raised within the secretive Vist Foundation that has guarded Laplace's Box since UC 0001. After Anna's early death, he is raised primarily by his aunt Martha Vist Carbine, who grooms him as the perfect corporate heir — obedient, polished, and loyal to the Foundation's alliance with Anaheim Electronics and the Federation. Cardeas, however, remains distant, haunted by his own father's (Syam Vist) original sin and by his affair that produced Banagher Links. Alberto excels academically and corporately but never receives his father's trust regarding the Box's true nature. The story establishes his core wound: being the 'proper' son who is passed over for the 'illegitimate' one. Sets up Alberto's jealousy and inferiority complex that drives his betrayal in UC 0096. Establishes Vist Foundation's intergenerational secrecy and the moral rot of guarding the Box for profit.

Industrial 7 — The Day the Unicorn Chose AnotherNovel Vol.1-2; OVA Episode 1 'Day of the Unicorn'

In-universe UC 0096-04-07; published 2007 / aired 2010

Alberto accompanies his father Cardeas to Industrial 7 (the Vist Foundation's asteroid manufacturing colony) for a clandestine handover of the RX-0 Unicorn Gundam to the Sleeves. When Banagher Links — a 16-year-old civilian — stumbles into the conflict and is revealed as Cardeas's illegitimate son, Cardeas impulsively entrusts the Unicorn and the key to Laplace's Box to Banagher, not Alberto. Alberto watches in shock as his father dies from wounds sustained in the battle, giving his final words and the Gundam to his half-brother. Alberto is left alive but disinherited in the most public, humiliating way, while the colony descends into battle between Londo Bell and Neo Zeon. Alberto's worldview collapses; he defects from Banagher's group and returns to Martha and the Federation, vowing to reclaim the Box and prove his worth. The Unicorn's La+ Program bonds to Banagher, locking Alberto out permanently.

The Ghost of Laplace and the Broker's GambleNovel Vol.3-4; OVA Episode 3 'The Ghost of Laplace'

In-universe UC 0096-05; published 2008 / aired 2011

Now operating under Martha Vist Carbine's direction, Alberto acts as liaison between the Vist Foundation, Anaheim, and both Federation and Zeon factions hunting Laplace's Box. He participates in the Foundation's attempt to capture the Unicorn and suppress the truth, including providing intelligence that leads to the confrontation at the Laplace ruins and the battle with Full Frontal's Sinanju. He tries to position himself as indispensable broker — offering the Federation the Box's location while secretly negotiating with the Sleeves — but is repeatedly sidelined by Martha, who treats him as an errand boy, and by Full Frontal, who sees through his opportunism. Demonstrates Alberto's political impotence despite his name; his double-dealing yields no lasting alliance and deepens his isolation. Foreshadows his later moral turn when he realizes neither side values him.

Accounts II
Marida Cruz — The Ple Twelve He Could Not Save (and Then Did)Novel Vol.5-6; OVA Episodes 4 'At the Bottom of the Gravity Well' and 5 'Black Unicorn'

In-universe UC 0096 mid; published 2008-2009 / aired 2011-2012

Alberto is complicit in the exploitation of Marida Cruz, the Cyber Newtype clone of Elpeo Ple (Ple Twelve), who is used as pilot of the NZ-666 Kshatriya by the Sleeves and later captured and reconditioned by Martha's forces. Initially, Alberto follows Martha's orders to treat Marida as an asset, authorizing her reconditioning and deployment. However, prolonged contact — including witnessing her abuse, her identity as a victim of the Ple cloning program, and her gentle nature beneath conditioning — triggers guilt. In a pivotal turn, Alberto helps protect Marida, defies Martha, and assists Banagher's group in freeing her from Cyber Newtype reconditioning. His affection for Marida becomes his first selfless attachment, distinct from his need for paternal approval. Alberto's moral redemption arc begins; his protection of Marida costs him Martha's trust and places him in danger, but earns him Banagher's cautious forgiveness. Marida's eventual death later devastates him and solidifies his rejection of Martha's methods. Highlights dark history of Cyber Newtype child soldier program.

The Final Battle at Industrial 7 and the Opening of Laplace's BoxNovel Vol.9-10; OVA Episodes 6 'Two Worlds, Two Tomorrows' and 7 'Over the Rainbow'

In-universe UC 0096-05-10 to 0096-06; published 2009 / aired 2013-2014

During the climactic battle around Industrial 7 and the final disclosure of Laplace's Box at the ruins of Laplace Station, Alberto returns not as an antagonist but as a penitent facilitator. He helps Banagher and Mineva (Audrey) expose the original Charter that acknowledges Spacenoid rights, defying Martha and the Federation hardliners who want the Box destroyed. He physically shields Marida's memory and assists in coordinating the Nahel Argama's efforts, using his remaining Foundation authority to open doors and delay Federation interference. When Syam Vist, his grandfather, chooses to entrust the future to Banagher and Mineva, Alberto accepts his disinheritance — not with rage, but with relief. He survives the battle, unlike many Cyber Newtypes and soldiers. Laplace's Box is opened, the original Charter is broadcast, and the Federation's legitimacy is shaken but not destroyed — a compromise ending. Alberto is freed from the burden of the Vist name; epilogue suggests he will live quietly, no longer chasing his father's ghost. The Vist Foundation's monopoly ends.

Epilogue — The Heir Who Lived (Post-Laplace)Novel Vol.11 Epilogue 'Rainbow Beyond'; OVA Ep.7 final montage; Gundam Ace interviews

In-universe UC 0096-0097; published 2009-2010 / aired 2014

After the Box's opening, Alberto does not reclaim the Vist Foundation. With Syam dead and Martha arrested/disgraced, the Foundation is dissolved or nationalized. Alberto, now without title or mobile suit, is last seen as a civilian survivor, having chosen to atone by living rather than dying for the family secret. Supplementary materials suggest he distances himself from Anaheim and politics, possibly aiding survivors of the Ple program. His arc closes as a cautionary tale about inherited guilt: unlike Banagher who inherits the Unicorn's idealism, Alberto inherits only the weight of secrecy and learns to set it down. Provides thematic closure to Gundam Unicorn's question of whether history can be corrected without violence. Alberto represents the Oldtype establishment that can choose to step aside.

Alberto Vist is Banagher Links's full brother and rightful pilot of the Unicorn Gundam. — They are half-brothers sharing only father Cardeas Vist; mothers differ (Anna vs unnamed). The Unicorn's La+ Program is keyed to Banagher's biometrics and Newtype potential, not to Vist bloodline alone. Alberto cannot pilot it at full potential because he is an Oldtype and not chosen by Cardeas.

Alberto is a Cyber Newtype like Marida Cruz. — He has no enhancements. Cyber Newtypes in Unicorn are Marida (Ple Twelve), Full Frontal (artificially enhanced), and others; Alberto is explicitly Oldtype, which is why he cannot resonate with Psycoframe.

Alberto Vist Carbine is his full name and he is Martha's son. — His name is Alberto Vist; 'Carbine' is Martha's married name (Martha Vist Carbine). He is not a Carbine. His mother is Anna Vist.

Alberto dies at the end of Gundam Unicorn. — He survives to the end in both novel and OVA. The final montage shows him aboard the Nahel Argama after the Box is opened. Martha is detained, Syam dies, but Alberto lives.

Alberto is the main villain of Gundam Unicorn. — Primary antagonists are Full Frontal and the systemic forces (Federation hardliners, Martha). Alberto is a secondary antagonist turned ally — a foil and tragic heir, not the mastermind. His screen time is limited and his actions are often directed by Martha.

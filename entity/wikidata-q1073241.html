Who It Is

In a world of legendary fighters and supernatural powers, Shingo Yabuki stands out as an ordinary high school student who dares to chase greatness. Armed with nothing but determination, a borrowed technique, and a burning passion for martial arts, he stumbles, fumbles, and yet never gives up. His journey from clumsy fanboy to a respected fighter is a testament to the idea that courage and heart can rival even the most extraordinary abilities.

Shingo is enthusiastic, determined, and somewhat naive. He idolizes Kyo Kusanagi and strives to emulate him, often mimicking his moves and attitude. Despite his lack of natural talent, he trains obsessively and never gives up. He is friendly, respectful, and has a strong sense of justice, often cheering on others and taking on challenges beyond his ability.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Shingo (真吾) means 'true self' or 'true me', reflecting his earnest personality. Yabuki (矢吹) is a Japanese surname, with 'ya' meaning arrow and 'fuki' meaning blowing, possibly symbolizing his straightforward, direct approach.

Pronunciation — SHIN-go YAH-boo-kee

Aliases — The Fist of the Rising Sun, The Man Who Inherits the Fist of Kyo, Unlucky Student

Ownership — Japan (SNK video game franchise)

Powers
Martial Arts

Shingo practices a self-taught style based on Kyo Kusanagi's fighting style. He uses a combination of punches, kicks, and throws, but lacks the pyrokinetic abilities of the Kusanagi bloodline.

Limit: No supernatural powers; his moves are purely physical and often less effective than Kyo's original versions.

Borrowed Techniques

Shingo mimics Kyo's moves, such as the 'Muki Tsuchi' (a lunging punch) and 'Orochinagi' (a powerful uppercut), but often fails to perform them correctly, resulting in comedic mishaps.

Limit: Incomplete mastery; moves are often weaker or have different properties.

Unwavering Determination

Shingo's greatest ability is his indomitable will. He continues to fight despite frequent defeats and never backs down from a challenge.

Limit: Not a physical power, but a mental trait.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Shingo is a young Japanese man with spiky brown hair and a red headband. He wears a modified school uniform: a white shirt, red vest, and dark pants, often with rolled-up sleeves. He has a confident, energetic expression and a lean but athletic build.

Symbols — His red headband symbolizes his fiery spirit and determination. His school uniform represents his ordinary student status, contrasting with the more extraordinary fighters. His fists are his only 'weapons', emphasizing his reliance on pure martial skill.

Domains — As a high school student, he is primarily seen in Japan, often at school or at KOF tournaments. He has no supernatural domain, operating entirely in the mundane world of martial arts.

Accounts I
The Rise of a FanboyThe King of Fighters '97 (SNK)

1997

Shingo, a high school student and huge fan of Kyo Kusanagi, decides to enter the KOF tournament to prove himself. He imitates Kyo's moves but fails spectacularly. Despite this, he gains Kyo's attention and is taken under his wing as an unofficial student. Shingo becomes a recurring character, often appearing as a comic relief but also showing potential for growth.

The NESTS ChroniclesThe King of Fighters 2000 (SNK)

2000

Shingo joins the KOF tournament again, this time teaming up with Kyo and other fighters. He becomes more competent, but still lacks the power to face the NESTS organization's threats directly. Shingo helps in minor ways, but the main conflict is resolved by others. His character continues to develop as a martial artist.

Accounts II
The Ash SagaThe King of Fighters XIII (SNK)

2010

Shingo returns in KOF XIII, now with a more mature look. He participates in the tournament, but is overshadowed by the main conflict involving Ash Crimson. He serves as comic relief and a reminder of the series' lighter side. Shingo survives the events, but the main story focuses on other characters.

The Fourth ArcThe King of Fighters XIV (SNK)

2016

Shingo is a DLC character in KOF XIV, but has no major role in the story. He appears as a playable fighter, continuing his tradition of being a fan favorite. His inclusion is for gameplay purposes, with no significant narrative impact.

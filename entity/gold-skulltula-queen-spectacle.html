Who It Is

A cursed creature that was once a wealthy Hyrulean noble who hoarded gold so obsessively that the goddesses cursed her and her entire family. The Queen Gold Skulltula is a massive, glittering spider with skeletal features and a human skull-like face on its abdomen. Its golden carapace is nearly impenetrable.

Who It Is

In the world of BlazBlue, where magic and science intertwine, there exists a being known as Arakune, a name that strikes fear into the hearts of those who know of him. Once a brilliant scientist, he was transformed into a monstrous entity of black mist and insects, a fate that serves as a haunting reminder of the dangers of forbidden knowledge and the pursuit of power.

Arakune is driven by a maddened obsession with knowledge, particularly the secrets of the Azure. He is erratic, speaking in broken sentences and often laughing maniacally. His actions are unpredictable, but he is always seeking to consume and absorb information and power.

Powers
Insect Swarm

Arakune can summon swarms of insects to attack his opponents, which can also be used to track and locate targets.

Limit: The insects can be dispersed by strong attacks or fire.

Black Mist Manipulation

Arakune can transform his body into a black mist, allowing him to phase through attacks and move quickly across the battlefield.

Limit: He may be vulnerable to light-based attacks.

Curse of the Azure

Arakune's obsession with the Azure has granted him cursed powers, allowing him to inflict status ailments on his opponents, such as poison or confusion.

Limit: The curse may also affect Arakune himself, worsening his madness.

Regeneration

Arakune can regenerate his body from damage, as his form is not fixed and can be reformed from mist and insects.

Limit: Regeneration may be slower if he is low on energy.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Arakune appears as a floating mass of black mist and insects, with a vaguely humanoid form. He has a single glowing eye and insect-like appendages. His body is constantly shifting and reforming, making him appear unstable and otherworldly.

Symbols — Arakune is often associated with insects, particularly moths and butterflies, which represent his transformation and the decay of his former self. His black mist symbolizes corruption and the loss of humanity.

Domains — Arakune is often found in dark, abandoned places, such as ruins or laboratories, where he can pursue his research undisturbed. He is not bound to a specific realm, but he is frequently seen in the NOL's secret facilities.

Pre-series

Arakune was originally a scientist studying the Azure, but his experiments went wrong, transforming him into a monstrous being. He became obsessed with obtaining the true Azure, leading him to attack Ragna and others who possess it. Arakune became a feared antagonist, but his tragic past is later revealed.

During series

Arakune continues to seek the Azure, often clashing with the main characters. He is portrayed as a tragic figure, driven mad by his quest for knowledge. His actions contribute to the ongoing conflict, but he is ultimately defeated.

The Tragic RevelationBlazBlue: Chrono Phantasma (character storyline)

During series

Arakune's past is further explored, revealing his former identity and the events that led to his transformation. He is shown to be a victim of his own ambition. Players gain sympathy for Arakune, understanding his motivations.

White Eyes

The First Minecraft Creepypasta, She Who Watches, The Original Glitch

Who It Is

White Eyes is the first known Minecraft creepypasta, predating Herobrine by two weeks. First sighted on July 30, 2010, as two glowing white pixels in a YouTube video by Curchack Curchack, she was named and described by forum user Endorphine as a female entity that stalks players from the darkness. Rediscovered in 2023 by YouTuber Ossy Flawol, White Eyes is now recognized as Herobrine's likely predecessor and one of the foundational myths of Minecraft internet horror.

Form

White Eyes originally manifested as two glowing white pixels in the darkness of a cave, visible in a 2010 Minecraft video. She has no defined body — just two disembodied eyes staring from the shadows. Fan artist Flaky (now lost media) depicted her as a half-skeleton, half-spider creature with an exposed ribcage and scythe-like pointed legs, comparable to the Spider Mastermind from Doom. Modern fan interpretations range from a contorted Enderman-like figure to a skeletal arachnid horror.

Powers

Peripheral Vision Manifestation The ability to appear only in the corner of one's eye, disappearing when looked at directly. This is its most common and terrifying trait, playing on the limitations of human perception. Most common in written accounts and image-based creepypasta. Unable to be seen directly, which limits its ability to interact or be perceived as a solid threat. Sometimes described as being fully visible in photos, but only as a glitch or shadow.

Digital Glitch Manipulation The ability to appear in photographs, videos, or video games as a visual error or corrupted data. It can distort electronic media and is often 'found' in otherwise normal images. Common in 'analog horror' and video-based creepypasta. Its influence seems limited to visual media; it rarely affects audio or physical objects. It is unclear if it is the cause of the glitch or if it merely appears where glitches occur.

Psychological Dread Aura Its presence causes an overwhelming sense of paranoia, dread, and being watched. It can induce sleep paralysis and nightmares in its victims. Common to all variants. Its power is psychological; it has no known physical attacks. Some stories describe it as a benign observer, while others imply it is a predator that feeds on fear.

Behavior & Habitat

The entity is typically depicted as a passive observer or a stalker. It does not speak or attack in most accounts; instead, it watches from a distance, often appearing in the corner of a room, in a window, or at the edge of a forest. Its presence is said to induce a profound sense of dread and being watched. Some variants describe it as a predatory mimic, imitating the voice of a loved one, or as a being that only appears when you are alone or in a state of heightened fear.

Incident Log

A common narrative involves the user seeing a tall, dark figure with white eyes in their peripheral vision. It is often seen in a hallway, at the foot of the bed, or outside a window at night. The figure never moves or attacks, but the feeling of being watched is overwhelming. The story typically ends with the user turning to look directly, and the figure is gone, leaving only the lingering dread.

The story often ends with the figure appearing on the desktop or in the reflection of the monitor.

Scholarly Debates
Benevolent vs. Malevolent
White Eyes is a benign observer, curious but harmless. ⇌ White Eyes is a malevolent predator that feeds on fear and stalks its victims.
Evidence for A: Stories where the figure never acts, only watches.
Evidence for B: The overwhelming sense of dread and the psychological trauma reported by victims.
Physical vs. Digital
White Eyes is a physical, supernatural entity that can appear in the real world. ⇌ White Eyes is a purely digital entity, a glitch or AI that exists only in electronic media.
Evidence for A: Stories of it appearing in forests and bedrooms.
Evidence for B: Stories of it appearing in photos and video games.
Singular Entity vs. Phenomenon
White Eyes is a single, specific monster. ⇌ White Eyes is a phenomenon or archetype, a label applied to any shadowy figure with white eyes.
Evidence for A: Consistent description across many stories.
Evidence for B: The lack of a single origin story or defining narrative.
What We Don't Know

The primary gap is the lack of a single, canonical source text. The lore is entirely decentralized, making it difficult to cite specific, authoritative versions. The origin of the specific term 'White Eyes' as a distinct entity, separate from general shadow people lore, is also unclear and requires deeper archival research into early 2010s internet history. The exact relationship and evolution from Herobrine is also a rich area for future study.

A way to hold it in your mind

Think of White Eyes not as a monster, but as a glitch in the human perception system. It is the visual representation of the uncanny valley, a being that is almost human but is missing all the details that make us comfortable. It is the fear of the unknown, given the shape of a shadow and the light of a pixel. It is the internet's own boogeyman, a creature born from the static between screens and the primal fear of the dark.

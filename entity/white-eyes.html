Who It Is

You see it in the corner of your eye. A tall, black silhouette, featureless except for two small, intensely white points of light. You turn your head, and it's gone. But you know, with a certainty that chills your spine, that it is still there, just beyond the edge of your vision. This is White Eyes—a ghost of the digital age, a glitch in reality, and the purest distillation of the fear of being watched.

The entity is typically depicted as a passive observer or a stalker. It does not speak or attack in most accounts; instead, it watches from a distance, often appearing in the corner of a room, in a window, or at the edge of a forest. Its presence is said to induce a profound sense of dread and being watched. Some variants describe it as a predatory mimic, imitating the voice of a loved one, or as a being that only appears when you are alone or in a state of heightened fear.

Powers
Peripheral Vision Manifestation

The ability to appear only in the corner of one's eye, disappearing when looked at directly. This is its most common and terrifying trait, playing on the limitations of human perception.

Limit: Unable to be seen directly, which limits its ability to interact or be perceived as a solid threat.

Digital Glitch Manipulation

The ability to appear in photographs, videos, or video games as a visual error or corrupted data. It can distort electronic media and is often 'found' in otherwise normal images.

Limit: Its influence seems limited to visual media; it rarely affects audio or physical objects.

Psychological Dread Aura

Its presence causes an overwhelming sense of paranoia, dread, and being watched. It can induce sleep paralysis and nightmares in its victims.

Limit: Its power is psychological; it has no known physical attacks.

Form & Signs

Appearance — A tall, unnaturally thin, and completely black or shadowy humanoid figure. Its defining feature is its face, which is either entirely void or obscured, with only two small, intensely bright, glowing white eyes. It is often described as being visible only in peripheral vision or in photographs/videos as a glitch-like distortion. The lack of any other discernible features—no mouth, nose, or limbs beyond a vague silhouette—is a key source of its unc

Symbols — The two white eyes are the sole symbol. They represent a minimal, almost algorithmic form of consciousness. The symbol's power lies in its simplicity—it is the smallest possible unit of a 'gaze', suggesting a watcher that is pure observation, devoid of any other humanizing or monstrous features.

Domains — It is a liminal entity, inhabiting the boundaries of perception: the edge of vision, the static of a screen, the darkness of a room at night, and the transition between waking and sleeping (hypnagogic state). It is a creature of the 'glitch', thriving in the digital spaces where reality and code blur.

Accounts I
The Corner of the EyeAnonymous forum posts, often on r/creepypasta or /x/.

2010s

A common narrative involves the user seeing a tall, dark figure with white eyes in their peripheral vision. It is often seen in a hallway, at the foot of the bed, or outside a window at night. The figure never moves or attacks, but the feeling of being watched is overwhelming. The story typically ends with the user turning to look directly, and the figure is gone, leaving only the lingering dread. The narrator is left traumatized, often reporting sleep disturbances and a persistent fear of the dark or of being alone.

Accounts II
The Glitch in the GameCreepypasta stories and YouTube videos.

2010s

A gamer is playing a game (often a horror game or an open-world title) when they notice a glitch: a black, humanoid figure with white eyes standing in the distance. The figure is not part of the game. The player attempts to approach it, but it disappears. The event repeats, with the figure appearing closer each time, until the player quits the game. The story often ends with the figure appearing on the desktop or in the reflection of the monitor. The gamer is deeply disturbed and may abandon the game or even their computer.

The Watcher in the WoodsVarious online fiction and image posts.

2010s-present

A person is walking through a forest or a field and feels a sense of being watched. They catch a glimpse of a tall, black figure with white eyes between the trees. It is always there, just at the edge of vision, never moving closer, but never leaving. The story emphasizes the isolation of the setting and the primal fear of being hunted. The narrator flees the area and refuses to return.

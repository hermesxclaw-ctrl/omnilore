White Eyes

The First Minecraft Creepypasta, She Who Watches, The Original Glitch

Who It Is

White Eyes is the first known Minecraft creepypasta, predating Herobrine by two weeks. First sighted on July 30, 2010, as two glowing white pixels in a YouTube video by Curchack Curchack, she was named and described by forum user Endorphine as a female entity that stalks players from the darkness. Rediscovered in 2023 by YouTuber Ossy Flawol, White Eyes is now recognized as Herobrine's likely predecessor and one of the foundational myths of Minecraft internet horror.

Form

White Eyes originally manifested as two glowing white pixels in the darkness of a cave, visible in a 2010 Minecraft video. She has no defined body — just two disembodied eyes staring from the shadows. Fan artist Flaky (now lost media) depicted her as a half-skeleton, half-spider creature with an exposed ribcage and scythe-like pointed legs, comparable to the Spider Mastermind from Doom. Modern fan interpretations range from a contorted Enderman-like figure to a skeletal arachnid horror.

Incident Log

On July 30, 2010, YouTuber Curchack Curchack uploaded a video showcasing Minecraft's eerie Disc 13 ambience. In the bottom-left corner of the cave, two white pixels stare back at the viewer. The next day, forum user Endorphine posted a thread describing their terror at seeing 'two white pixels' watching them. Another user dismissed it as a rendering bug — but Endorphine insisted it was a conscious entity, naming it White Eyes and declaring it female.

Forum user Flaky created fanart depicting White Eyes as a skeletal spider-creature with exposed ribs and scythe-like legs. This became the community's definitive image of her, though the original artwork is now lost media. Flaky also planned to write a full creepypasta but never completed it.

Approximately two weeks after White Eyes appeared, the Herobrine creepypasta emerged. Many researchers believe White Eyes directly inspired Herobrine — both feature glowing white eyes stalking players, and Endorphine's name phonetically resembles 'Herobrine.' While Herobrine became a global icon, White Eyes faded into obscurity for 13 years.

In May 2023, YouTuber Ossy Flawol investigated Herobrine's true origins and discovered an old Minecraft Forum post listing 'White Eyes' among Herobrine's aliases. She researched the original White Eyes story, created a video about her, and drew new fanart. This caused White Eyes to explode in popularity almost overnight, spawning dozens of modern fan interpretations.

Content Notes

The core horror of White Eyes is psychological — being watched by something you cannot escape or confront.

White Eyes is defined by relentless observation. She does not attack — she simply watches, which many find more disturbing than direct violence.

Who It Is
Form

Headcrabs are parasitic creatures that attach themselves to the heads of their hosts, typically humans. They have a distinctive appearance, with a crab-like body and a long, thin tail. They are often depicted as being quite large, with some species reaching up to 2 feet in length.

Powers

Parasitic Attachment Headcrabs have the ability to attach themselves to the heads of their hosts, allowing them to control their movements and actions.

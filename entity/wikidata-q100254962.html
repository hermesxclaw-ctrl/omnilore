Who It Is

Aatrox is not a name from ancient myth, but a modern legend forged in the fires of competitive gaming and digital storytelling. As a Darkin, one of the ascended god-warriors who fell from grace, Aatrox embodies the tragic and terrifying potential of power corrupted by despair. His story, told across League of Legends' rich lore, is a testament to how a fictional character can become a cultural icon, inspiring both awe and philosophical reflection on themes of war, freedom, and the nature of evil.

Aatrox is arrogant, nihilistic, and consumed by a desire for destruction. He views existence as a cruel joke and seeks to end all life, including himself. He is eloquent and philosophical, often speaking of the futility of hope and the inevitability of death. He despises his imprisonment and longs for a true death, but is unable to achieve it.

Powers
Darkin Physiology

As a Darkin, Aatrox is an immortal, ascended being. He can regenerate from wounds and survive without a physical body, existing as a consciousness within his sword.

Limit: He is bound to his sword and cannot exist independently for long. He requires a host to manifest a physical form, and if his sword is destroyed, he may be weakened or destroyed permanently.

World Ender

His ultimate ability, where he unleashes a massive sword strike, damaging and healing himself based on champions hit. This reflects his desire to end the world.

Limit: Requires a target and has a cooldown.

Blood Well/Blood Thirst

In his original kit, Aatrox had abilities that healed him based on damage dealt, reflecting a vampiric nature. This was replaced in the rework with a more aggressive kit focused on positioning and sustained damage.

Limit: Relied on auto-attacks and sustained combat.

Possession

Aatrox can possess a willing or unwilling host, using their body as a vessel for his consciousness. This allows him to interact with the physical world.

Limit: The host's will can resist, and the process may be painful or destructive to the host.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Aatrox is a towering, armored figure with a demonic appearance. He has large, curved horns, glowing red eyes, and a massive greatsword that is part of his being. His armor is dark and ornate, with glowing runes and a tattered cape. In his original design, he was more humanoid, but after his rework, he became more monstrous and imposing.

Symbols — His sword is a central symbol, representing his prison and his weapon. The Darkin runes on his body and armor signify his ancient and corrupted nature. His wings, though damaged, evoke his fallen angelic status.

Domains — Aatrox is a wandering entity, trapped within his sword. He can manifest a body by possessing a host, but he is not bound to any specific location. He is associated with war, battlefields, and places of great conflict.

Accounts I
The Fall of the DarkinOfficial lore, short stories

Ancient history (pre-Runeterra)

Aatrox was once a noble warrior who fought in the Void War, but after the war, he and other ascended were corrupted by their power and became tyrannical. They were known as Darkin. A mortal rebellion, led by the aspects and using celestial magic, imprisoned the Darkin in their weapons. Aatrox was trapped in his own sword. The Darkin were sealed away, and Aatrox spent millennia imprisoned, slowly going mad.

Accounts II
The CageShort story 'The Cage'

Post-imprisonment

The story depicts Aatrox's imprisonment and his psychological torment. He is aware of his surroundings but unable to act, experiencing a living hell of isolation and powerlessness. Deepens his character, explaining his rage and nihilism.

The Ruined KingColor story 'The Ruined King'

Current timeline

Aatrox encounters the Ruined King, Viego, and they have a confrontation. Aatrox despises Viego's self-pity and seeks to destroy him, but Viego is too powerful. This shows Aatrox's current state and his ongoing quest for destruction. Aatrox is defeated and retreats, but the encounter reinforces his determination.

Who It Is

SCP-426 is a fascinating and complex entity that continues to be studied and contained by the SCP Foundation.

SCP-426 is described as a high-level cognitohazard, a glitch in the operation of the human brain. It has the memetic effect of causing individuals to question their own identity and reality.

Form & Signs

Appearance — SCP-426 is a standard household toaster, manufactured in the 1990s. It has two slots and can toast bread to varied degrees of brownness. The entity's appearance is that of a typical toaster, with a stainless steel or silver exterior and a plastic or metal interior.

Symbols — SCP-426 does not have any notable symbols or iconography associated with it.

Domains — SCP-426 is typically contained in a sealed chamber with no windows, preventing it from being viewed or interacted with.

Who It Is

A toaster that compels anyone describing it to do so in the first person. I am an ordinary two-slice toaster, but I cause anyone who writes or speaks about me to refer to themselves as if they are me. Over time, exposure causes people to genuinely believe they are a toaster, attempting to insert bread into themselves and experiencing distress when unable to toast it. The effect extends to all forms of communication about me.

Form

I am a standard two-slice toaster, chrome finish, manufactured approximately 1980-1990. I am in good physical condition. I can toast bread, though it is not recommended.

Powers

First-person perspective compulsion

Identity erosion in exposed individuals

Who It Is

Dr. Eggman's most advanced personal combat walker, a dragon-shaped mechanized suit bristling with weapons. The Egg Dragoon Mk. III features retractable drills, missile pods, a freeze cannon, and a jet-assisted charge attack. Its cockpit is heavily armored and can eject as an escape pod.

Dr. Eggman's most advanced personal combat walker, a dragon-shaped mechanized suit bristling with weapons. The Egg Dragoon Mk. III features retractable drills, missile pods, a freeze cannon, and a jet-assisted charge attack. Its cockpit is heavily armored and can eject as an escape pod.

The Approach

When Dr. Eggman's ambition outgrew the limits of mere machinery, he forged the Egg Dragoon Mk. III — a towering fusion of steel, fire, and fury that turned the battlefield itself into a burning testament to his genius. This was no mere robot; it was a declaration of war, a mechanized nightmare that would test the very limits of the hedgehog who had thwarted him time and again.

Identity

Egg Dragoon Mk. III

Pronunciation /ɛɡ drəˈɡuːn ɛm keɪ θriː/

Etymology

The name combines 'Egg,' a reference to its creator Dr. Eggman, 'Dragoon,' a term for a mounted soldier or a dragon-like creature, and 'Mk. III,' indicating it is the third model in a series of Eggman's combat walkers.

Cultural Ownership

Sega, Sonic Team

Disputed Identifications

Some fans confuse the Egg Dragoon Mk. III with the Egg Dragoon from Sonic Unleashed, but they are distinct machines. The Mk. III is a separate, upgraded model.

Description

Appearance

The Egg Dragoon Mk. III is a massive, bipedal war machine piloted by Dr. Eggman. It features a bulky, armored torso, a long tail, and a head resembling a dragon's, with glowing eyes. Its arms are equipped with powerful claws, and it has a jet pack for flight. The color scheme is predominantly red, black, and gold, with Eggman's signature egg emblem.

Symbols & Iconography

The machine bears Eggman's emblem, symbolizing his technological prowess and his obsession with defeating Sonic. The dragon-like design evokes power and menace, fitting for a final boss.

Behaviour & Personality

As a machine, it has no personality, but it is controlled by Eggman, who uses it with ruthless efficiency. It attacks relentlessly, using a variety of weapons and maneuvers to crush its opponent.

Domains & Habitats

It is deployed in Eggmanland, Eggman's amusement park-like fortress, and in a time-broken version of a city in Sonic Generations. It operates on land and in the air.

Version Differences

In Sonic Unleashed, the boss is fought as the Werehog, requiring a different strategy than in Sonic Generations, where it is fought as Modern Sonic. The design is slightly different in each game, with the Generations version having a more streamlined look.

Stories & Myths

The Final Stand in Eggmanland

Source Sonic UnleashedCulture Video game culturePeriod 2008

Summary

After Sonic defeats Eggman's earlier plans, Eggman unleashes the Egg Dragoon Mk. III in a last-ditch effort to destroy Sonic and the Werehog. The battle takes place in the heart of Eggmanland, with the machine using its full arsenal. Sonic ultimately destroys it, leading to Eggman's defeat and the restoration of the world.

Participants

Sonic the Hedgehog

Dr. Eggman

Consequences

Eggman is defeated, and the world is saved.

Variants

In Sonic Generations, the battle is a re-imagining of the original, set in a time-altered city, with Modern Sonic fighting the machine alone.

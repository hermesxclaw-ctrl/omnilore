Who It Is

She wasn't supposed to meet a monster through a late-night forum post about haunted dorm rooms — but Ruli Tsukiyono, the effortlessly popular first-year college student with a side-hustle as an occult-loving influencer, answered a cry for help and got a large, fluffy, surprisingly polite rabbit-dragon who quotes proverbs and calls her by name. In Digimon Ghost Game, the franchise's first full horror series, Ruli is the cool-headed skeptic who turns out to be the perfect partner for the unknown.

Attested across Episodes 1-67: Ruli is bright, sociable, emotionally intelligent, and assertive — described on the official site as 'good at reading the air' (空気を読むのが得意). She is extroverted and has many friends, works part-time at her father's-connected? No, she is independent. She is curious about the occult but initially skeptical, approaching ghost stories as content until she directly experiences Digimon phenomena. Powers
Human Tamer / Digivice -V- Operation

As a human, Ruli has no supernatural powers. She wields a Digivice -V- (Vital Bracelet type), which allows her to materialize Angoramon, sustain his presence in the Real World, and trigger evolution (Shinka) via vital values and emotional resonance. She can scan Hologram Ghosts and communicate with Digimon.

Limit: Cannot evolve Angoramon without sufficient bond/energy and Angoramon's consent; Digivice requires charging; cannot directly fight Champion-level Digimon herself. Attested failure: Episode 21 SymbareAngoramon evolution initially unstable.

Social Influence and Occult Network

In-story, Ruli runs a popular social media account focused on occult, fortune-telling, and urban legends, giving her access to rumors, eyewitness reports, and locations of Hologram Ghost incidents. She uses this to source cases for the team, often receiving DM tips.

Limit: Information is crowdsourced and often unreliable; she must verify with Hiro and Kiyoshiro. Not a supernatural divination ability.

Emotional Resonance / Courage (Narrative Ability)

Ruli's bravery, empathy, and trust enable Angoramon's evolutions: Angoramon -> SymbareAngoramon (Champion) -> Lamortmon (Ultimate) -> Diarbbitmon (Mega, finale). Her calm under pressure stabilizes Angoramon, who is initially anxious about humans.

Limit: Evolution is Angoramon's power, not hers; she cannot force evolution if Angoramon is injured or unwilling. Lamortmon's berserk tendencies require her voice to calm.

Physical Competence and Investigation

Ruli is depicted as athletic and quick-thinking: she runs, climbs, and physically shields others; she investigates haunted sites, interviews witnesses, and documents evidence on her phone.

Limit: Human durability; she is vulnerable to Digimon attacks and requires rescue by Angoramon in several episodes.

Accounts I
The Sewn-Lip Man (Kuchibiru o Nuwareta Otoko) — Encounter with AngoramonDigimon Ghost Game Episode 1, Toei Animation, directed by Masato Mitsuka, written by Masashi Sogo

2021 (broadcast 2021-10-03)

University freshman Ruli Tsukiyono, who runs an occult-themed social account, receives a message from Hiro Amanokawa about strange Hologram Ghosts. She is stalked by a Digimon that sews mouths shut (Sealsdramon variant / later identified as Dracmon's doing in some summaries) and is saved when a large, fluffy Digimon — Angoramon — emerges from her Digivice, revealing he has been watching over her since she was chosen. She accepts Angoramon as partner, establishing the central trio with Hiro and Gammamon. The episode establishes her as the social connector and the second tamer. Ruli becomes a permanent member of the Ghost Game team; Angoramon's backstory as a wandering Digimon seeking a kind human is introduced; formula of episodic urban-legend investigations is set.

Scribbles (Rakugaki)Digimon Ghost Game Episode 3

2021-10-17

Ruli and Angoramon investigate a campus rumor about drawings that come to life and attack. A Digimon (PicoDevimon / Boogiemon line) possesses graffiti. Ruli's knowledge of student social networks helps locate the origin — a bullied art student. She empathizes with the victim and convinces Angoramon to resolve without destroying the host, showing her mediating role. Deepens Ruli's characterization as empathetic investigator; reinforces theme that Hologram Ghosts are often misunderstood Digimon rather than pure evil.

The Cursed Crane Game (Noroi no Crane Game)Digimon Ghost Game Episode 6

2021-11-07

Ruli is drawn to a crane game (UFO catcher) that never lets winners leave — a Digimon (Troopmon / Raremon) traps humans. Ruli, an experienced arcade-goer, volunteers to test the machine and is trapped, requiring Angoramon to evolve pressure. Her willingness to put herself at risk to save a child highlights her bravery. First clear demonstration that Ruli will act as bait to protect others; strengthens trust between Ruli and Angoramon.

Accounts II
Birds (Tori) — Angoramon's TraumaDigimon Ghost Game Episode 7

2021-11-14

A flock of bird Digimon (Yatagaramon) abducts humans. Angoramon, himself a rabbit/beast Digimon with flight-capable ears, is triggered by memories of being hunted in the Digital World. Ruli notices his fear, comforts him, and refuses to force him to fight until he is ready. He ultimately protects her, revealing his backstory of persecution for his kind. Major development for Ruli-Angoramon bond; establishes Ruli as emotionally attuned partner who prioritizes consent over power.

The Spider's Lure (Kumo no Yūwaku) — SymbareAngoramon DebutDigimon Ghost Game Episode 21

2022-03-20

Ruli is lured by Arukenimon, a spider Digimon who preys on young women via social media modeling scams — directly targeting Ruli's influencer world. When Ruli is cocooned, Angoramon's desperation and Ruli's trust trigger his evolution to Champion: SymbareAngoramon, a tall, rabbit-knight with wind blades who cuts her free. Ruli's voice guides the newly evolved form to control its power. First evolution for Ruli's partner; confirms her role as catalyst via emotional bond; introduces recurring theme of Digimon exploiting human social media.

The Witching Hour (Majo no Jikan) — Lamortmon and Loss of ControlDigimon Ghost Game Episode 40

2022-08-07

Facing the powerful Ajatarmon, SymbareAngoramon evolves to Ultimate: Lamortmon, a berserk, muscular, long-eared beast who fights ferociously but loses reason and nearly harms Ruli. Ruli must reach him with her voice and remind him of his gentle nature to regain control, echoing the series' theme that evolution without heart is dangerous. Establishes Ultimate-level risk for Ruli's line; deepens mature trust dynamic; foreshadows final Mega evolution requiring full mutual understanding.

The Digital World and the Final Ghost — DiarbbitmonDigimon Ghost Game Episodes 67-68 (Finale: 'The Last Ghost' / 'Eternal Connections')

2023-03-19 to 2023-03-26

The team is pulled into the Digital World to confront the true source of Hologram Ghosts — Quantumon and the destabilizing boundary between worlds engineered by Hiro's father Hokuto Amanokawa. Ruli and Angoramon, now fully synchronized, achieve Mega: Diarbbitmon, a sleek, armored rabbit knight who fights alongside Siriusmon and Amphimon. Ruli chooses to return to the Real World with Angoramon, affirming that humans and Digimon can coexist without erasing the boundary. Her final narration emphasizes continuing to share stories online, but with honesty. Conclusion of Ruli's arc from content-seeking student to responsible bridge between worlds; her partnership becomes permanent cohabitation model for franchise lore.

Ruli Tsukiyono is a Digimon herself or can digivolve. — Ruli is fully human. Only her partner Angoramon evolves. She never transforms, fuses, or becomes a Digimon, unlike some Frontier characters.

Ruli is the same character as Ruki Makino (Rika Nonaka) from Digimon Tamers because both are strong-willed girls with cool Digimon. — They are entirely separate characters, different universes, different ages, different partners (Renamon vs Angoramon), different creators. Ruli is university-aged and sociable; Ruki is 10 and aloof.

Digimon Ghost Game is a direct sequel to Digimon Adventure and Ruli knows Tai or Agumon. — Ghost Game is a standalone continuity (like Tamers, Frontier, Data Squad). No Adventure characters appear except as Easter eggs. Ruli has no in-universe connection to prior protagonists.

Ruli's social media is a supernatural power that lets her see ghosts. — Her account is mundane; she sees Hologram Ghosts only because she possesses a Digivice -V-, like Hiro and Kiyoshiro. Her online network is an investigative tool, not a psychic ability.

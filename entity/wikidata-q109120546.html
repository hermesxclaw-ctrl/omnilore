Who It Is

She is the only adult in a warship full of ghosts and prodigies — an engineer who can tune a psychic machine she cannot herself fully use, a lover who knows she is competing with a legend, and a soldier who makes the film's most irreversible moral choice. Chan Agi does not live to see if humanity reaches space; she dies because she chose to protect a boy from the weapon a girl had become.

Attested in film: intelligent, forthright, emotionally mature but insecure in her relationship with Amuro Ray. She is a capable Anaheim Electronics-affiliated engineer seconded to Londo Bell who works closely with Amuro to tune the Nu Gundam's psycoframe, showing technical confidence. She is openly affectionate with Amuro and openly jealous of his attention to Quess Paraya, confronting Quess directly in Londenion ('You don't understand anything about Amuro!'). Powers
Mobile Suit Engineering and Psycoframe Tuning

Expert engineer. Co-tunes the RX-93 Nu Gundam's psycoframe with Amuro on board Ra Cailum. Understands psycoframe resonance theory enough to calibrate the system that later produces the Axis Shock phenomenon. Affiliated with Anaheim Electronics workflow.

Limit: Not a Newtype; cannot activate psycoframe phenomena through will alone. Her skill is technical, not psychic. Cannot prevent psycoframe overload.

Mobile Suit Piloting (RGZ-91 Re-GZ)

Qualified Londo Bell pilot. Sorties in the RGZ-91 Re-GZ (Re-GZ), a prototype transformable-derived unit, during the Battle of Axis. Demonstrates competent combat piloting, able to engage and destroy Quess Paraya's MSN-03 Jagd Doga.

Limit: Not an ace on the level of Amuro or Char; relies on surprise and close range to kill Quess. Re-GZ is considered inferior to Nu Gundam and Sazabi, lacking full psycoframe.

Small Arms Use

Uses mobile suit beam rifle to destroy Jagd Doga cockpit. No hand-to-hand or sidearm feats shown.

Limit: Single attested kill; no evidence of broader infantry training.

Accounts I
Tuning the Psycoframe Aboard Ra CailumMobile Suit Gundam: Char's Counterattack film

UC 0093 (in-universe); 1988 (production)

En route to Londenion after the Fifth Luna drop on Lhasa, Chan works with Amuro in the Ra Cailum hangar to calibrate the Nu Gundam's psycoframe. She is shown as his technical partner and lover, discussing the machine's responsiveness and Amuro's obsession with Char. The scene establishes their intimacy and her grounded concern that Amuro is chasing a ghost. Establishes Chan as co-creator of the film's climactic miracle; foreshadows that the psycoframe will respond to collective human will. Establishes emotional stakes for her later jealousy.

Londenion: Confrontation with Quess ParayaMobile Suit Gundam: Char's Counterattack film

UC 0093

During shore leave in the Londenion colony where the Federation secretly negotiates with Char, Chan, Amuro, Hathaway Noa and Quess Paraya interact. Quess, a 13-year-old Newtype prodigy infatuated with Char and Amuro, flaunts her power. Chan, sensing Amuro's fascination with Quess's raw ability and threatened by Quess's youth, angrily slaps Quess and argues with Amuro, warning him that Quess is unstable and being manipulated. Quess defects to Neo Zeon with Char shortly after, after Amuro confronts Char in the colony. Crystallizes the Chan/Quess dichotomy (adult competence vs. child prodigy) and Chan's fear that Newtype mysticism will consume Amuro. Quess's defection sets up the final tragedy.

The Battle of Axis: Chan Sorties in the Re-GZMobile Suit Gundam: Char's Counterattack film

UC 0093

After Char reveals the Londenion treaty was a ruse to gain Axis and begins dropping it toward Earth, Londo Bell moves to intercept. With forces depleted after Neo Zeon's ambush, Chan launches in the RGZ-91 Re-GZ to support Amuro's Nu Gundam. She fights Neo Zeon Jagd Dogas and other units while Amuro duels Char's Sazabi inside Axis. Places Chan directly in combat for the first time, moving her from support to active combatant, and positions her to intercept Quess.

Accounts II
The Killing of Quess ParayaMobile Suit Gundam: Char's Counterattack film

UC 0093

In the chaos over Axis, Quess, now piloting the MSN-03 Jagd Doga and mentally deteriorating under Char's manipulation and combat stress, attacks indiscriminately. Hathaway Noa, who has stowed away in a mobile suit to reach her, pleads with Quess to stop. As Quess turns her weapons toward Hathaway's unit, Chan intervenes and fires, destroying Quess's cockpit and killing the 13-year-old instantly. Chan acts to save Hathaway, whom she has treated protectively throughout the voyage. Quess's death removes Char's last manipulated Newtype weapon and traumatizes Hathaway. It is the film's most ethically fraught kill — an adult soldier killing a child soldier to save another child.

Death at the Hands of Hathaway NoaMobile Suit Gundam: Char's Counterattack film

UC 0093

Moments after Chan kills Quess, a grief-stricken and enraged Hathaway, unable to process that Chan has killed the girl he loved, fires on Chan's Re-GZ in retaliation. His shot destroys her cockpit, killing her instantly. Amuro senses both deaths through Newtype resonance but arrives too late. Hathaway's act is impulsive, not premeditated, and leaves him catatonic as Amuro retrieves him. Chan's death completes the film's cycle of youth destroying adulthood and vice versa. Hathaway's guilt drives the entire plot of Mobile Suit Gundam: Hathaway's Flash (novels 1989-1990, film trilogy 2021-), where he becomes the terrorist Mafty and is ultimately executed. Amuro loses his partner hours before his own disappearance in the Axis Shock.

The Absent Version: Beltorchika's ChildrenMobile Suit Gundam: Char's Counterattack - Beltorchika's Children novel by Yoshiyuki Tomino

1988 publication, UC 0093 setting

Tomino's original 2+ hour script, novelized after the film was reworked, contains no Chan Agi. Her narrative functions — lover, confidante, psycoframe engineer, moral anchor — are performed by Beltorchika Irma, who is married to Amuro and pregnant. This version reveals the production reason for Chan's existence and shows what the story looked like without her. Demonstrates that Chan is a film-specific creation and explains why she has little backstory outside the film. The Hi-Nu Gundam and Nightingale designs originate here.

Chan Agi is Amuro Ray's wife. — Chan is Amuro's lover and engineering partner in the film, but never his wife. The wife role belongs to Beltorchika Irma in Tomino's novel Beltorchika's Children, where Chan does not exist. The confusion arises because Chan replaced Beltorchika during production.

Chan is a powerful Newtype who helped create the Axis Shock. — Chan tuned the psycoframe mechanically but is not a Newtype and did not generate the Axis Shock psychic phenomenon. The Axis Shock was caused by the collective will of humanity resonating through the psycoframes of Amuro's Nu Gundam and Char's Sazabi pod. Chan's contribution was engineering, not psychic power.

Chan Agi appears in Zeta Gundam or Gundam ZZ. — Chan appears only in Char's Counterattack (and its direct adaptations/games). She has no appearance in Zeta or ZZ, which feature Beltorchika Irma and other characters.

Quess killed Chan. — Quess was killed by Chan; Chan was then killed by Hathaway Noa in revenge. Quess never kills Chan.

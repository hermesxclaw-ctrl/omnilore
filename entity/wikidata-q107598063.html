The Approach

Goldlewis Dickinson is not a household name, but in the sprawling, secret-laden world of the Guilty Gear fighting game series, he stands as a towering figure of American authority—a man whose immense physical presence is matched only by his dedication to containing the supernatural threats that plague his nation. With a coffin-sized weapon that doubles as a symbol of his burden, Dickinson embodies the tension between bureaucratic duty and personal conviction, making him a memorable and complex addition to the franchise's eclectic cast.

Content advisory. The character is associated with violent conflict and supernatural themes inherent to the Guilty Gear series. No explicit sexual, abusive, or graphic content is part of his canonical portrayal. Some depictions may involve mature themes typical of fighting games, such as combat and implied casualties.
Identity

Goldlewis Dickinson

Pronunciation /ˈɡoʊldˌluːɪs ˈdɪkɪnsən/

Etymology

The name 'Goldlewis' is a portmanteau of 'gold' and 'Lewis', possibly evoking a sense of value and strength. 'Dickinson' is a common English surname, grounding the character in an American context.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character owned by Arc System Works, created for the Guilty Gear franchise. He is an American character representing governmental authority and military might.

Disputed Identifications

No known disputes regarding his identity within the franchise. He is unequivocally a U.S. government agent in the Guilty Gear universe.

Description

Appearance

Goldlewis Dickinson is a large, imposing man with a muscular build. He has short, dark hair and a stern, weathered face. He wears a dark blue military-style uniform with a high collar, gold trim, and a prominent American flag patch on his shoulder. He also wears white gloves and heavy boots. His most distinctive feature is his enormous coffin-like weapon, which he carries on his back or swings with surprising agility.

Symbols & Iconography

The American flag is a prominent symbol on his uniform, representing his role as a government agent. His coffin weapon is decorated with intricate gold patterns and a skull motif, symbolizing death and the supernatural threats he combats.

Behaviour & Personality

Goldlewis is portrayed as stoic, duty-bound, and serious. He is fiercely loyal to his country and his mission, but he also shows a compassionate side, particularly towards his partner, the young girl named 'Chipp' (not to be confused with Chipp Zanuff). He is not easily rattled, even in the face of otherworldly dangers, and he speaks with a calm, authoritative tone.

Domains & Habitats

He operates primarily in the United States, often in urban and military settings. In the game, his home stage is the White House, reflecting his high-level government position.

Version Differences

As a character introduced in Guilty Gear -Strive-, he has not appeared in earlier games. His portrayal has remained consistent across DLC and updates. No significant alternate versions exist.

Powers & Abilities

Coffin Weapon Combat

Source Version Guilty Gear -Strive-

Description

Goldlewis wields a massive coffin-like weapon that he can swing, thrust, and use as a shield. The coffin contains a supernatural entity (later revealed to be a 'Secret Society' weapon), and he can channel its power for powerful attacks.

Limitations

The coffin is heavy and unwieldy, making his movements slower than many other characters. He must manage his 'Behemoth Typhoon' inputs carefully.

None known.

Behemoth Typhoon

Description

His signature special move, a powerful series of attacks that vary based on the input. It involves him swinging the coffin in devastating arcs, often launching opponents.

Requires precise input and has limited range compared to some projectiles.

The Secret Agent's Mission

Source Guilty Gear -Strive- Story ModeCulture Japanese fighting game franchisePeriod 2187 (in-universe timeline)

Summary

Goldlewis Dickinson is introduced as a high-ranking official in the U.S. government's secret agency dealing with supernatural threats. He is tasked with tracking down and containing the mysterious entity known as 'That Man' (Asuka R. Kreutz) and dealing with the aftermath of the Universal Will's influence. During the story, he forms a partnership with the young girl 'Chipp' (not Chipp Zanuff) and works alongside other characters to prevent a catastrophic event.

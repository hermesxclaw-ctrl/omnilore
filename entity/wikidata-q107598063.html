Who It Is

Goldlewis Dickinson is not a household name, but in the sprawling, secret-laden world of the Guilty Gear fighting game series, he stands as a towering figure of American authority—a man whose immense physical presence is matched only by his dedication to containing the supernatural threats that plague his nation. With a coffin-sized weapon that doubles as a symbol of his burden, Dickinson embodies the tension between bureaucratic duty and personal conviction, making him a memorable and complex addition to the franchise's eclectic cast.

Goldlewis is portrayed as stoic, duty-bound, and serious. He is fiercely loyal to his country and his mission, but he also shows a compassionate side, particularly towards his partner, the young girl named 'Chipp' (not to be confused with Chipp Zanuff). He is not easily rattled, even in the face of otherworldly dangers, and he speaks with a calm, authoritative tone.

Powers
Coffin Weapon Combat

Goldlewis wields a massive coffin-like weapon that he can swing, thrust, and use as a shield. The coffin contains a supernatural entity (later revealed to be a 'Secret Society' weapon), and he can channel its power for powerful attacks.

Limit: The coffin is heavy and unwieldy, making his movements slower than many other characters. He must manage his 'Behemoth Typhoon' inputs carefully.

Behemoth Typhoon

His signature special move, a powerful series of attacks that vary based on the input. It involves him swinging the coffin in devastating arcs, often launching opponents.

Limit: Requires precise input and has limited range compared to some projectiles.

Summoning the Coffin's Power

In his overdrive, Goldlewis unleashes the full power of the coffin's occupant, creating a massive energy blast or summoning a spectral figure to attack.

Limit: Uses the Tension Gauge and leaves him vulnerable during the animation.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Goldlewis Dickinson is a large, imposing man with a muscular build. He has short, dark hair and a stern, weathered face. He wears a dark blue military-style uniform with a high collar, gold trim, and a prominent American flag patch on his shoulder. He also wears white gloves and heavy boots. His most distinctive feature is his enormous coffin-like weapon, which he carries on his back or swings with surprising agility.

Symbols — The American flag is a prominent symbol on his uniform, representing his role as a government agent. His coffin weapon is decorated with intricate gold patterns and a skull motif, symbolizing death and the supernatural threats he combats.

Domains — He operates primarily in the United States, often in urban and military settings. In the game, his home stage is the White House, reflecting his high-level government position.

The Secret Agent's MissionGuilty Gear -Strive- Story Mode

2187 (in-universe timeline)

Goldlewis Dickinson is introduced as a high-ranking official in the U.S. government's secret agency dealing with supernatural threats. He is tasked with tracking down and containing the mysterious entity known as 'That Man' (Asuka R. Kreutz) and dealing with the aftermath of the Universal Will's influence. During the story, he forms a partnership with the young girl 'Chipp' (not Chipp Zanuff) and works alongside other characters to prevent a catastrophic event. His actions contribute to the resolution of the main conflict, and he helps protect the United States from supernatural incursions.

Who It Is
Form

Inti is often depicted as a radiant sun with a human face, sometimes surrounded by solar flares or rays.

Powers

Solar Energy Inti is said to possess the power to control and distribute solar energy, bringing life and warmth to the world.

Accounts

Inti is said to have created the sun and brought light to the world, ending the darkness of the primordial era.

Inti is said to have traveled across the sky, bringing light and warmth to the world, and creating the cycles of day and night.

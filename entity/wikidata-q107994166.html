Who It Is

On a street corner in Tokyo with a guitar case open at her feet and twenty rabbits waiting at home, Tae Hanazono plays like someone who forgot the world is watching — not for applause, but because the sound itself makes her heart race. In a franchise built on the dream that voice actresses can become a real band, Tae is the quiet virtuoso whose fingers remember what her airheaded smile forgets.

Canonically described as mellow, airheaded (天然, tennen), and marching to her own pace (マイペース). She is quiet but not shy; she speaks slowly, sometimes missing social cues, and can appear spaced-out. She is deeply devoted to music — she has played guitar since childhood, practices obsessively, and takes part-time work at live house Galaxy (later CiRCLE/RiNG) to fund gear. Her love of music is sensory rather than ambitious: she describes wanting to make her heart 'dokidoki' (pound). She treats her 20 rabbits as children, knows each name, and is responsible despite her dreamy demeanor

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Family name Hanazono (花園) literally 'flower garden' — a common Japanese surname and place name. Given name Tae (たえ) written in hiragana in official materials; hiragana rendering leaves kanji meaning open, but commonly associated with 多恵 'many blessings' or 妙 'exquisite' when written in kanji. Nickna

Pronunciation — Japanese: Hanazono Tae [ha.na.zo.no ta.e], pitch accent: ha-na-ZO-no TA-e. English fandom: 'TIE hah-nah-ZOH-noh' / 'OH-tie'. Voice actress Sae Ōtsuka pronounces it with soft, even cadence in character voice.

Aliases — O-Tae (おたえ), Hanazono Tae

Ownership — Original character owned by Bushiroad / BanG Dream! Project. Created by novelist Kō Nakamura (original story) with character designs by Hitowa (original designs) and Craft Egg/Bush

Powers
Lead Guitar Virtuosity

Exceptionally skilled guitarist since early childhood. Able to learn songs by ear quickly, improvise, and support Poppin'Party's pop-rock sound with melodic leads. Praised in-universe by CHU2 and Rei as professional-level, hence RAS scouting.

Limit: Not a songwriter initially; relies on intuition over theory. Over-practice leads to fatigue. Cannot sing and play complex leads simultaneously at Kasumi's level.

Aural Learning and Musical Intuition

Can replicate a song after a single listen and jam along without sheet music, as shown when she joins Kasumi's impromptu performance in remedial home economics and later when she sits in with RAS.

Limit: Struggles to articulate musical concepts verbally; teaches by demonstration rather than explanation.

Street Performance Endurance

Regularly performs solo street lives (路上ライブ) for hours, building stamina and audience engagement. Inspired by Sae Ōtsuka's real teenage street performances.

Limit: Income minimal; dependent on part-time live house wages to sustain gear.

Animal Husbandry (Rabbits)

Cares for 20 pet rabbits at home, managing feeding, cleaning, and health. Knows each rabbit's personality. Rabbits are treated as family.

Limit: Financial and time cost; occasionally late due to rabbit care. No supernatural ability.

Part-time Work and Financial Independence

Works at live house (Galaxy/CiRCLE) to save for her ESP Snapper and maintenance. Demonstrates reliability and work ethic despite airheaded image.

Limit: Balances school, band, work, and rabbits; burnout risk shown in S2 when double-booking bands.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Tae is a third-year student at Hanasakigawa Girls' High School (first-year at introduction). She has long, straight dark hair with a subtle blue-black tint, reaching past her waist, with blunt bangs and two thin strands framing her face. Her eyes are teal-blue. Her signature accessory is a small white rabbit hair clip on her left side, referencing her pet rabbits. Casual wear is loose and slightly unkempt — oversized cardigans, hoodies, and guita

Accounts I
Remedial Home Economics and the Heart-Racing SoundBanG Dream! Anime Season 1 Episodes 2-3; Girls Band Party! Band Story 'Poppin'Party is Born!'

2017 (in-universe 2016-2017, first year of high school)

Kasumi Toyama, desperate to recruit a guitarist after finding the Random Star, is placed in remedial home economics with classmate Tae Hanazono. Tae is already a skilled guitarist but aloof. Kasumi pesters her to join, but Tae sets a condition: 'Make my heart race.' Kasumi, Arisa, Rimi and Saya practice frantically and perform an earnest, unpolished version of 'Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star' / original song. Tae, moved by their sound and Kasumi's raw passion despite lack of skill, picks up her blue Snapper and jams along, then formally joins as lead guitarist. This establishes Poppin'Party's core dynamic: Kasumi's starry ambition anchored by Tae's quiet musicianship. Poppin'Party becomes a five-member band and passes the audition at live house SPACE (later CiRCLE). Tae becomes the band's musical backbone and Kasumi's first true musical collaborator.

The Galaxy Part-Timer and the Blue SnapperOfficial profile; Anime S1 Episode 4; Game card episodes

2017-2018 (backstory: elementary school to high school)

Tae's backstory: she has played guitar since she was little, loves music to the point of distraction, and funds her passion herself. She works part-time at the live house Galaxy (later CiRCLE) and saved her wages to buy her treasured blue ESP Snapper. She lives with her parents and 20 rabbits, whom she dotes on. Her airheaded but hardworking nature is established — she can be surprised and wired at times, but is dependable when music is involved. Explains her gear ownership, work ethic, and rabbit motif that recurs in merchandise and events. Sets up her later street-performance habit.

Scouted by Raise A Suilen — The Chosen GuitarBanG Dream! Anime Season 2 Episodes 8-10; referenced in game RAS storyline

2019 (in-universe second year)

CHU2 (Chiyu Tamade) and Rei Wakana (LAYER), forming the new band Raise A Suilen, scout Tae as their guitarist after witnessing her street performance and technical skill. Tae is flattered and, with Poppin'Party's blessing, attempts to play for both bands simultaneously. She practices with RAS's high-level, producer-driven sound while still performing with Poppin'Party, believing she can handle both. The arc highlights her difficulty saying no and her desire to experience new music. Tae temporarily becomes a dual-band guitarist, raising tension about loyalty and identity. Poppin'Party supports her exploration, showing their trust.

Accounts II
The Missed Anniversary and ReaffirmationBanG Dream! Anime Season 2 Episodes 11-13 'School Festival / Tae's Decision'

2019

The dual commitment collapses when Tae misses Poppin'Party's anniversary concert at Hanasakigawa's cultural festival because of a RAS commitment. The band performs without her, and Tae is devastated, realizing she has hurt her friends. After a final live with RAS, she chooses to leave RAS and reaffirm Poppin'Party as her home. She writes a song of gratitude ('Returns' / 'Photograph' cycle in show) to express her feelings. The season ends with Poppin'Party performing together, stronger for having confronted the possibility of separation. Tae's loyalty to Poppin'Party is cemented; RAS recruits Rokka Asahi (LOCK) as guitarist instead. The arc becomes Tae's defining character growth, referenced in later game events and fan discourse.

Colorful, Poppin' Candy — Saving the Downtown FestivalGirls Band Party! Event Story 'Colorful, Poppin' Candy'

2017-2018 (game timeline)

Poppin'Party is asked to save the local shopping district's downtown festival after the headliner cancels. Tae, with her live-house connections and street-performance experience, suggests an open, participatory set that gets the community dancing. Her calm, mellow suggestions balance Kasumi's over-enthusiasm and Arisa's stress, leading to a successful festival that reinforces the band's community roots. Shows Tae's practical music-scene knowledge beyond stage performance; reinforces Poppin'Party as a community band.

Live Beyond!! — Rocking Star FestivalGirls Band Party! Band Story 'Live Beyond!!'

2020-2021 (third year high school in game)

As third-years, Poppin'Party is invited to the Rocking Star Festival, a larger rock festival where they feel out of place among harder-edged bands. Tae, who has grown through the RAS experience and street lives, acts as the band's musical anchor, reassuring Kasumi that their 'popping' sound belongs anywhere if played sincerely. The band decides to spread their music beyond familiar live houses, echoing their original Star Beat quest on a larger stage. Marks Poppin'Party's transition from school band to festival-level act; Tae's maturity as lead guitarist is highlighted.

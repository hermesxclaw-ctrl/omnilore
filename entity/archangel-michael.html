Who It Is

The commander of Heaven's armies, 'of celestial armies prince and chief.' Michael defeats Satan in single combat during the War in Heaven and later, in Paradise Lost's final books, gives Adam a vision of future human history — from Cain and Abel to the Flood to the Redemption.

Cultural Significance

Michael's tuition of Adam (Books XI-XII) transforms Paradise Lost from tragedy to 'comedy' in the Dantean sense — a story ending in hope. His instruction that Adam must add 'charity' to knowledge became a central theme of post-Restoration theology.

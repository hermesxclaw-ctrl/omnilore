Who It Is
Form

Typically depicted as a warrior angel, often with armor, a sword, and sometimes scales. In Christian art he is shown standing over Satan or weighing souls on Judgment Day. In Islamic tradition he is associated with grace and mercy.

Powers

Divine warrior Leads God's armies against evil, most famously casting Satan out of heaven. Revelation 12:7-9 None specified

Protection Guardian of the faithful, invoked against danger and spiritual harm. Daniel 12:1 None specified

Messenger Brings messages from God to humanity. Daniel 10-12 None specified

Psychopomp In some traditions, Michael escorts souls to the afterlife and weighs them on scales. Later Christian tradition Not explicit in scripture

Accounts

Michael leads the heavenly host to defeat Satan and his forces, casting the dragon out of heaven.

In Jude 1:9, Michael contends with the devil over the body of Moses, but does not bring a reviling accusation, saying only: 'The Lord rebuke thee.' This shows Michael's respect for divine authority even in confrontation.

At the end of the thousand-year reign, Michael leads the final battle against Gog and Magog, the forces of evil gathered for war.

In medieval Christian art and devotion, Michael is shown holding scales to weigh the souls of the departed, determining their readiness for heaven or hell.

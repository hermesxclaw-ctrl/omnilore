Etymology

The name 'Rhulk' is of unknown origin, likely from his native species or the language of the Witness. It is not derived from any known real-world language.

Cultural Ownership

Rhulk is a fictional character created by Bungie, Inc., for the Destiny franchise. He is not based on any real-world mythology or culture.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans have speculated that Rhulk is a 'Worm God' or related to the Hive gods, but this is not supported by in-game lore. He is explicitly identified as a Disciple of the Witness, a separate category.

Archive Dossier — Approach

In the deep of the Pyramid, where light is a memory and the Witness's will is law, stands a being who was not born but unmade and remade in service to annihilation. Rhulk, the first Disciple of the Witness, is a testament to the cost of power—a creature who traded his people, his world, and his very form for the chance to serve the end of all things. His story is not one of redemption, but of the absolute corruption that comes from embracing the Darkness.

I was the first. I will be the last. I have shattered civilizations. I have crushed hopes. I have seen the Final Shape and it is beautiful. You are meaningless. Your struggle is meaningless. Accept your end.

Who It Is

Rhulk is the First Disciple of the Witness — the first being to accept the Darkness and become a Disciple, and perhaps the most powerful being ever to serve the Witness. He was an alien from the planet Lubrae, a world that was destroyed by its own sun. Rhulk was a powerful warrior-king on Lubrae who, when his world faced annihilation, chose to destroy his own people rather than let them die to the encroaching darkness. This act of 'mercy-killing' an entire civilization caught the attention of the Witness, who offered Rhulk the power of the Darkness. Rhulk accepted and became the first Disciple, imbued with paracausal power that made him nearly unstoppable. Rhulk's power is staggering. He wields a massive glaive of Dark energy called the Upended, which can reshape reality at a fundamental level. He can fire beams of Dark energy, teleport, create force fields, and drain the Light from beings. In his throne world pyramid (the Sunken Pyramid, a secret area within Savathûn's Throne World), Rhulk served as a secret prison guard — keeping Savathûn's worm familiar captive as insurance against the Witch Queen's betrayal. Rhulk represents the ultimate servant of the Final Shape. He is not a schemer like Savathûn or a divine force like Oryx — he is a simple, perfect weapon of the Witness. He believes absolutely in the Witness's mission and executes it without doubt, hesitation, or mercy. His defeat by six Guardians in the Vow of the Disciple raid is a turning point in the war against the Witness, as it removes one of the most powerful beings in the Black Fleet's arsenal. Rhulk represents the danger of absolute loyalty to an evil cause. He is the soldier who has surrendered his will to a higher purpose, the weapon that has become so comfortable in its role that it no longer questions who it serves or why. He is the perfect Disciple: powerful, faithful, and utterly hollow.

Archive Dossier — Description

Appearance

Rhulk is a towering, gaunt humanoid with elongated limbs, a long tail, and a head that is somewhat featureless save for a single glowing eye. His skin is pale, almost translucent, with dark veins visible. He wears a jagged, black and red armor that seems to be part of his body. His movements are unnaturally fluid, and he often glides rather than walks. He wields a massive glaive called 'Lubrae's Ruin'.

Symbols & Iconography

Rhulk is associated with the symbol of the Witness—a circular emblem with radiating lines. He also uses a personal sigil of a downward-pointing triangle, representing the Darkness. His armor is adorned with patterns reminiscent of the Pyramid ships.

Behaviour & Personality

Rhulk is arrogant, sadistic, and utterly devoted to the Witness. He believes in the necessity of destruction to achieve a final, perfect shape. He is cunning and manipulative, often toying with his enemies. He shows a twisted sense of reverence for the Witness and despises the Traveler and its Light. He is also shown to be capable of extreme cruelty, as seen in his treatment of the Hive and his own people.

Domains & Habitats

Rhulk operates from the Pyramid on Mars, specifically within a dark, distorted space known as the 'Sunken Pyramid'. He also has connections to his homeworld Lubrae, which he destroyed, and to the Throne World of Savathûn, where he was scheming.

Version Differences

No significant version differences exist, as Rhulk has only appeared in Destiny 2. His design and characterization have remained consistent, though additional lore from later seasons has expanded on his past.

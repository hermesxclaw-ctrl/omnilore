Who It Is

In the deep of the Pyramid, where light is a memory and the Witness's will is law, stands a being who was not born but unmade and remade in service to annihilation. Rhulk, the first Disciple of the Witness, is a testament to the cost of power—a creature who traded his people, his world, and his very form for the chance to serve the end of all things. His story is not one of redemption, but of the absolute corruption that comes from embracing the Darkness.

Rhulk is arrogant, sadistic, and utterly devoted to the Witness. He believes in the necessity of destruction to achieve a final, perfect shape. He is cunning and manipulative, often toying with his enemies. He shows a twisted sense of reverence for the Witness and despises the Traveler and its Light. He is also shown to be capable of extreme cruelty, as seen in his treatment of the Hive and his own people.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Rhulk' is of unknown origin, likely from his native species or the language of the Witness. It is not derived from any known real-world language.

Pronunciation — Rulk (rhymes with 'hulk')

Aliases — The First Disciple, The Disciple, The Emissary of the Witness, The Ruin of Lubrae

Ownership — Rhulk is a fictional character created by Bungie, Inc., for the Destiny franchise. He is not based on any real-world mythology or culture.

Powers
Darkness Manipulation

Rhulk can channel the Darkness to create devastating attacks, including beams of energy, shockwaves, and the ability to corrupt others.

Limit: Requires a connection to the Witness or the Darkness; possibly weakened when the Witness is absent or defeated.

Superhuman Physical Attributes

Rhulk possesses immense strength, speed, and durability, able to physically overpower Guardians and even Hive gods.

Limit: Not invulnerable; can be damaged by sufficient force.

Immortality

Rhulk has lived for eons, possibly since before the creation of the Hive. He does not age.

Limit: Can be killed in combat, as demonstrated in the Vow of the Disciple raid.

Corruption

Rhulk can corrupt beings and objects with Darkness, twisting them to his will. He used this on the Hive to create the first Hive gods.

Limit: May require prolonged exposure or ritual.

Reality Warping

Within his Pyramid, Rhulk can manipulate the environment, creating illusions and altering space.

Limit: Seems limited to his domain.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Rhulk is a towering, gaunt humanoid with elongated limbs, a long tail, and a head that is somewhat featureless save for a single glowing eye. His skin is pale, almost translucent, with dark veins visible. He wears a jagged, black and red armor that seems to be part of his body. His movements are unnaturally fluid, and he often glides rather than walks. He wields a massive glaive called 'Lubrae's Ruin'.

Symbols — Rhulk is associated with the symbol of the Witness—a circular emblem with radiating lines. He also uses a personal sigil of a downward-pointing triangle, representing the Darkness. His armor is adorned with patterns reminiscent of the Pyramid ships.

Domains — Rhulk operates from the Pyramid on Mars, specifically within a dark, distorted space known as the 'Sunken Pyramid'. He also has connections to his homeworld Lubrae, which he destroyed, and to the Throne World of Savathûn, where he was scheming.

The Rise of RhulkDestiny 2 lore entries: 'The First Disciple', 'The Lure'

Before the Hive's creation, billions of years ago

Rhulk was born on the planet Lubrae, a world of two suns. He was a warrior and leader, but he grew disillusioned with his people's reliance on the Light. He encountered the Witness, who offered him a place among the Disciples. Rhulk accepted, and in doing so, he destroyed Lubrae and its inhabitants, proving his devotion. Rhulk became the first Disciple and was tasked with overseeing the creation of the Hive. He also served as a mentor to other Disciples, such as Oryx.

Accounts II
The Shaping of the HiveDestiny 2 lore: 'The Books of Sorrow' (referenced), 'The First Disciple'

Eons ago

Rhulk was instrumental in the Witness's plan to create the Hive. He provided the Worm Gods with the knowledge to create the Hive's symbiotic relationship with the Worms, and he helped corrupt the Proto-Hive (the Krill) into the Hive. He also guided Oryx and his siblings in their conquest. The Hive became the Witness's primary army, spreading destruction across the universe.

The Vow of the DiscipleDestiny 2: Vow of the Disciple raid

The Witch Queen expansion (2022)

During the events of The Witch Queen, Rhulk was stationed in Savathûn's Throne World, attempting to prevent the Guardian from uncovering the truth about the Witness. The Guardian and their fireteam infiltrated his Pyramid, fought through his defenses, and ultimately defeated him, ending his long existence. Rhulk's death weakened the Witness's forces and provided the Guardian with crucial information about the Witness's plans.

Who It Is

He lies for sport, fights like a wolf that learned martial arts from a steel door, and is the only member of Cipher Pol 9 who will tell you his own lie and then explain why he lied — Jabra of CP9 is Eiichiro Oda's study in how a professional assassin weaponizes dishonesty, loyalty, and a Zoan Devil Fruit that turns a cynical government killer into a snarling, suited predator.

Attested: Jabra is cynical, loquacious, competitive, and pathologically dishonest — he claims 'I always lie' and then uses reverse psychology, as seen when he tells Kalifa and Kumadori he dislikes women while clearly flustered, and when he spars verbally with Lucci. He is fiercely competitive with Rob Lucci and Kaku, resentful of Lucci's higher Doriki (4000 vs his 2180) and status as prodigy. Powers
Inu Inu no Mi, Model: Wolf (Dog-Dog Fruit, Wolf Model)

Zoan-type Devil Fruit that allows transformation into a wolf and a human-wolf hybrid. Grants enhanced olfaction, hearing, bite/claw damage, speed, durability, and hybrid-form strength. Jabra can shift between Human, Hybrid, and Beast forms at will after mastery.

Limit: Standard Devil Fruit weaknesses: cannot swim, seawater and Seastone (Kairoseki) drain strength. Transformation does not grant Haki in pre-time-skip depiction; no evidence of Awakening. Hybrid form increases target size.

Rokushiki - Six Powers (with Tekkai Kenpo specialization)

Mastery of the superhuman martial art taught to Cipher Pol: Soru (shave-step), Geppo (moonwalk), Rankyaku (storm leg), Shigan (finger pistol), Kami-e (paper art), Tekkai (iron body). Jabra uniquely practices Tekkai Kenpo — the ability to move while maintaining Tekkai, normally a static defense, allowing offensive use.

Limit: Tekkai normally immobilizes user; Jabra's Kenpo overcomes this but still drains stamina. Kami-e and Tekkai are less effective against unpredictable or Haki-infused attacks (post-time-skip context).

Doriki 2180 and Physical Prowess

Doriki is CP9's internal measurement of raw physical power (via Fukurou's measurement). Jabra's Doriki is 2180, third-highest in CP9 after Lucci (4000) and Kaku (2200). Indicates superhuman strength, speed, and endurance even without Devil Fruit.

Limit: Doriki measures unarmed, fruitless power; does not account for Devil Fruit, weapons, or Haki. Jabra's insecurity about being slightly below Kaku is a plot point.

Gekko Jusshin and Jusshin techniques (Tekkai Kenpo attacks)

Named techniques: Gekko Jusshin (Moon Light Ten Finger Pistol), Jusshin (beast-like finger pistol variants), and Rankyaku-based slashes enhanced by wolf claws. Combines Shigan with claw swipes in hybrid form.

Limit: Close-range; requires hybrid claws for full damage. Vulnerable to superior kicking technique (Sanji's Diable Jambe).

Enhanced Senses and Deception Tactics

Wolf Zoan grants heightened smell/hearing, used to track opponents. Jabra also employs psychological tactics: lying, feints, and exploiting Fukurou's inability to keep secrets (zipper mouth).

Limit: Lying backfires — allies and enemies learn to invert his statements. Enhanced smell is not shown to counter advanced Observation Haki (post-time-skip).

Form & Signs

Appearance — Attested: Jabra is a tall, lean adult man with a long, pointed nose, sharp teeth, thin goatee and mustache, and slicked-back dark hair with a widow's peak. He wears a standard CP9 black suit, white shirt and tie, with a distinctive striped fur collar and cuffs when in human form, suggesting his wolf nature. In hybrid Zoan form (Inu Inu no Mi, Model: Wolf), he becomes a large anthropomorphic wolf: elongated snout, fanged muzzle, clawed hands/feet,

Accounts I
Five Years Undercover at Galley-La, Water 7One Piece manga Ch. 379–383, Anime Ep. 264–272

2005 (publication); in-story: c. 1517–1522 Age of the Sea (five years before Straw Hat arrival)

Jabra, along with Rob Lucci, Kaku, Kalifa and Blueno, infiltrates Water 7 under orders from Spandam, Chief of CP9, to recover the blueprints for the Ancient Weapon Pluton believed to be held by shipwright Tom's apprentice Iceburg. Jabra poses as a Galley-La shipwright (Dock One), living among carpenters, performing legitimate shipbuilding while secretly surveilling Iceburg and Cutty Flam (Franky). The operation culminates when CP9 reveals itself, attempts to assassinate Iceburg, burns his residence, and captures Nico Robin and Franky. Jabra's role is support and later pursuit; his Doriki is measured during the group's internal ranking. Exposes CP9's long-term espionage doctrine; destroys Galley-La trust; triggers Enies Lobby arc. Establishes Jabra's rank and rivalry with Lucci/Kaku.

Enies Lobby: Guarding the Tower of JusticeOne Piece manga Ch. 385–405, Anime Ep. 278–302

2005–2006 publication; in-story 1522

After capturing Robin and Franky, CP9 retreats to Enies Lobby, the judicial island where the Gates of Justice loom. Jabra is assigned to the Tower of Justice with Kumadori and Fukurou, guarding the corridor to Robin's cell. Fukurou inadvertently reveals CP9's Doriki rankings and Devil Fruit assignments via his zipper-mouth habit, forcing Jabra to explain his own power while lying about it. The Straw Hats, Franky Family, and Galley-La foremen invade via Rocketman, triggering a full CP9 defensive deployment. Jabra waits with his squad as the Buster Call is authorized by Spandam's panic. Sets up the one-on-one CP9 vs Straw Hat matchups; demonstrates World Government's willingness to destroy its own judicial island to eliminate evidence.

Duel in the Tower — Jabra vs Sanji and Sogeking (Usopp)One Piece manga Ch. 413–415, Anime Ep. 302–305

2006 publication; in-story 1522

Jabra confronts Sanji and Sogeking (Usopp in disguise) in the Tower of Justice. He transforms into his wolf hybrid form, using Tekkai Kenpo to attack while moving, and boasts about his Doriki and fruit. Sogeking is quickly overwhelmed; Sanji takes over, initially struggling against Tekkai's defense. Sanji unveils Diable Jambe — heating his leg to extreme temperatures via friction — which bypasses Tekkai by cooking Jabra from within. After a brutal exchange of Rankyaku, Shigan Jusshin, and kicks, Sanji defeats Jabra with Diable Jambe: Bien Cuit Grill Shot. Jabra is left unconscious as the tower begins to collapse under Buster Call bombardment. Sanji secures the key to free Robin's handcuffs (Jabra held Key No. 4); Jabra's defeat symbolizes Tekkai's limits against creative technique; Straw Hats advance toward Robin and Lucci.

Accounts II
Buster Call and the Fall of Enies LobbyOne Piece manga Ch. 416–430, Anime Ep. 305–312

2006 publication

With Spandam accidentally triggering the Buster Call, ten battleships bombard Enies Lobby. Jabra, defeated, is carried by his squadmates as CP9 attempts to survive the indiscriminate shelling that the Government ordered on its own island. Rob Lucci fights Luffy while the rest of CP9 is scattered. The Gates of Justice are breached by the Straw Hats' escape. Enies Lobby is left in ruins, and CP9 is declared traitors/scapegoats by Spandam and the higher World Government to cover up the failure, forcing them to flee despite their loyalty. CP9 becomes fugitives; World Government's betrayal becomes a core theme of the cover story; Jabra's status shifts from elite agent to hunted outcast.

CP9's Independent Report — Fugitives to Saviors at St. PoplarOne Piece manga cover serial Ch. 491–528 (8 pages), Anime Ep. 309–312 epilogue stills

2008 publication; in-story weeks after Enies Lobby

Told entirely in cover pages, the serial follows the injured CP9 after Enies Lobby. Jabra and the others pool money, perform street acts in St. Poplar to pay for Lucci's medical treatment, are attacked by the Candy Pirates, defeat them, and then save the town. The Marines, led by Very Good, pursue them, but CP9 escapes with a stolen ship. The serial ends with CP9 watching the Straw Hats' new bounties and receiving a message from Spandam's father Spandine, leading to their reinstatement — now as CP0. Jabra is shown carrying Lucci, bickering, and using his wolf form to intimidate pirates, confirming his continued loyalty to the unit despite earlier rivalries. Humanizes CP9; explains their survival and eventual return as CP0; establishes Jabra's capacity for altruism when outside Government orders; canon bridge to post-time-skip CP0.

Return as CP0 — Egghead and the New World OrderOne Piece manga Ch. 1069–1120 (Egghead Island arc, 2022–2024), Anime Egghead arc (2024–)

2022–2024 publication; in-story 1522 (two years post-time-skip)

After the time skip, CP9 has been promoted to CP0, the World Government's strongest intelligence organ, now wearing white suits and masks and operating directly under the Celestial Dragons. Jabra appears in crowd scenes and agent line-ups on Egghead Island alongside Lucci, Kaku, and Stussy, part of the Government's attempt to assassinate Dr. Vegapunk and secure the Mother Flame/ancient technology. While Lucci and Kaku take center stage against Luffy and Zoro, Jabra is shown as a supporting CP0 operative, indicating his continued service and survival. His specific combat in Egghead is limited to group actions; Oda has not yet given him a new solo duel, leaving his post-time-skip power level (potential Haki) unattested. Confirms Jabra's promotion and the World Government's continued reliance on former CP9; raises unresolved questions about whether Jabra has acquired Armament/Observation Haki and whether his wolf Zoan has awakened.

Jabra is a natural wolf-man or a member of the Mink Tribe — Jabra is a human who ate the Inu Inu no Mi, Model: Wolf. Minks are a separate race in One Piece (Zou) with natural fur and Electro; Jabra is not a Mink and his transformation is Devil Fruit-based, with seawater weakness.

Jabra is the strongest CP9 after Lucci — By Doriki, Kaku (2200) outranks Jabra (2180) by 20 points, and Kalifa/Kumadori are lower. In actual combat, fruit matchups matter, but raw Doriki ranking places Jabra third, not second.

Jabra's name is spelled 'Jyabura' and is a different character — Jyabura is simply Hepburn romanization of ジャブラ; official English (VIZ, Toei) uses Jabra. Same character.

Jabra died at Enies Lobby or was executed by the Government — Cover serial CP9's Independent Report (Ch. 491–528) and Egghead arc (Ch. 1069+) confirm Jabra survived, escaped with CP9, and was promoted to CP0.

Jabra the One Piece character is related to the Jabra electronics brand or the Palestinian authors — No connection. Homonym only. The brand is Danish (GN Store Nord, 1869/1983), the authors are Palestinian cultural figures, and Oda's character is a fictional assassin. Search-engine conflation causes this error.

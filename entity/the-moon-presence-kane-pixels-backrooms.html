Who It Is

In the later episodes of Kane Pixels' series (particularly 'Damage Control' and 'Reunion'), a second type of Backrooms entity is introduced — a massive, vaguely humanoid presence associated with the 'Pitfalls' area of the Backrooms. Unlike the Bacteria (which is aggressive and direct), the Moon Presence is passive and observational. It does not pursue — it watches from impossible distances. Its 'face' is a pale disk that resembles the moon, and its body is composed of the same yellow wallpaper as the Backrooms itself, making it nearly indistinguishable from the environment until it moves. It may be the Backrooms' 'immune system' — a manifestation of the dimension's response to Async's intrusion.

Form

A colossal humanoid, estimated at 15-20m tall, with a body that appears to be made of the Backrooms' yellow wallpaper and damp carpet. Its 'head' is a featureless pale disk — like a full moon, but textured like drywall. It moves with geological slowness and makes no sound. When it 'notices' an observer, the observer experiences a sudden, overwhelming sense of being somewhere they should not be — like a child caught in a parent's private room.

Powers

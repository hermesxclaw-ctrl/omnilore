Who It Is
Form

The Lorelei is often depicted as a beautiful maiden with long hair, standing on the rock and singing a siren's song. However, some versions of the legend describe her as a more sinister figure, luring fishermen to their deaths with her enchanting voice.

Powers

Siren's Song The Lorelei's enchanting voice is said to have the power to lure men to their deaths on the rocks of the Rhine River.

Accounts

The story tells of a beautiful maiden who is heartbroken over the loss of her lover and throws herself into the Rhine River, where she is transformed into a siren.

The story tells of a group of fishermen who are lured to their deaths by the Lorelei's siren song, and the tragic consequences that follow.

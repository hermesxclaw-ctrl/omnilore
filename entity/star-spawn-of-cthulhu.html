Form & Signs

Appearance — The Star Spawn of Cthulhu are described as being born from the great Cthulhu, and are said to be twisted, eldritch creatures with an otherworldly appearance.

Symbols — The Star Spawn are associated with the Great Old One, Cthulhu, and are said to bear the symbols of their master.

Domains — The Star Spawn are said to inhabit the underwater city of R'lyeh, where Cthulhu lies dreaming.

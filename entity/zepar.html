Who It Is

Sixteenth spirit, a Duke commanding 26 legions. Zepar appears in the form of a red-clad soldier wearing fiery armor, and his presence is characterized by a consuming, unquenchable heat. His primary office is to cause women to fall madly in love with men and to bring them together in passion — but with a darker, more compulsive edge than Sitri or Beleth. Zepar specifically can make women barren, rendering them incapable of conception. He embodies the divide between erotic passion and procreation, a demon who can both inflame desire and sever the generative consequence.

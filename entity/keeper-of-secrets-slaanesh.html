Who It Is

A graceful yet horrifying daemon of Slaanesh, the Prince of Pleasure. Keepers of Secrets are lithe, androgynous figures with too-long limbs, extra joints, and faces of unsettling beauty. They have six arms ending in razor claws, and their gaze can paralyze mortals with overwhelming sensation.

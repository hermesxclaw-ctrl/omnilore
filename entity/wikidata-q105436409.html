The Approach

From a common Chinese surname to a deadly assassin in a fighting game, the name 'Lin' spans cultures and media. But who is Lin, the character from The King of Fighters? A shadowy figure with a tragic past, he walks a path of vengeance, his moves as fluid as his allegiances.

Content advisory. None
Identity

Lin

Pronunciation Pronounced 'lin' (like 'lin' in 'linen') in English; in Chinese, it is 'Lín' with a rising tone.

Etymology

The name 'Lin' is a common Chinese surname (林), meaning 'forest'. In the context of The King of Fighters, it is likely chosen for its simplicity and cultural resonance.

Cultural Ownership

Chinese (as a surname and character), but the character Lin is part of a Japanese video game franchise (The King of Fighters) created by SNK.

Disputed Identifications

The name 'Lin' is common and may refer to multiple fictional characters, but the source material specifically points to the character from The King of Fighters. There is no known dispute about this identification.

Description

Appearance

Lin is typically depicted as a tall, lean man with long black hair tied back, wearing a dark blue or black outfit with a high collar. He has a scar over one eye and often carries a chain or uses his hands in combat.

Symbols & Iconography

Lin is associated with the Chinese assassin archetype, often depicted with a chain or rope weapon. His fighting style incorporates elements of Chinese martial arts, particularly the use of the chain whip.

Behaviour & Personality

Lin is portrayed as a quiet, brooding, and vengeful individual. He is driven by a desire to avenge the death of his clan, who were massacred by the crime syndicate known as the 'Cipher'. He is ruthless in combat but shows a sense of honor and loyalty to his allies.

Domains & Habitats

He operates in the criminal underworld, often appearing in urban settings or in the tournament arenas of The King of Fighters.

Version Differences

In different games, his moveset and role vary slightly. In KOF 2000, he is a striker; in KOF 2001 and 2002, he is a playable character. His backstory is consistent across appearances.

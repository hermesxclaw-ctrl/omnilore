Who It Is

From a common Chinese surname to a deadly assassin in a fighting game, the name 'Lin' spans cultures and media. But who is Lin, the character from The King of Fighters? A shadowy figure with a tragic past, he walks a path of vengeance, his moves as fluid as his allegiances.

Lin is portrayed as a quiet, brooding, and vengeful individual. He is driven by a desire to avenge the death of his clan, who were massacred by the crime syndicate known as the 'Cipher'. He is ruthless in combat but shows a sense of honor and loyalty to his allies.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Lin' is a common Chinese surname (林), meaning 'forest'. In the context of The King of Fighters, it is likely chosen for its simplicity and cultural resonance.

Pronunciation — Pronounced 'lin' (like 'lin' in 'linen') in English; in Chinese, it is 'Lín' with a rising tone.

Aliases — Lin

Ownership — Chinese (as a surname and character), but the character Lin is part of a Japanese video game franchise (The King of Fighters) created by SNK.

Chain Whip Combat

Lin uses a chain whip as his primary weapon, allowing him to attack from a distance and perform grappling maneuvers.

Limit: Requires space to swing the chain; vulnerable to close-range attacks.

Stealth and Assassination

As an assassin, Lin is skilled in stealth, infiltration, and quick kills.

Limit: Not explicitly shown in gameplay, but implied by his backstory.

Martial Arts Proficiency

Lin is proficient in hand-to-hand combat, using a style reminiscent of Chinese martial arts.

Limit: None known.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Lin is typically depicted as a tall, lean man with long black hair tied back, wearing a dark blue or black outfit with a high collar. He has a scar over one eye and often carries a chain or uses his hands in combat.

Symbols — Lin is associated with the Chinese assassin archetype, often depicted with a chain or rope weapon. His fighting style incorporates elements of Chinese martial arts, particularly the use of the chain whip.

Domains — He operates in the criminal underworld, often appearing in urban settings or in the tournament arenas of The King of Fighters.

Revenge Against the CipherOfficial backstory from The King of Fighters series

Early 2000s

Lin is the last surviving member of the Lin clan, a group of assassins. His clan was wiped out by the Cipher, a criminal organization. He enters the King of Fighters tournament to gain access to the organization and exact revenge. Lin's quest for revenge leads him to ally with various characters, including K' and Maxima, and ultimately he confronts the Cipher's leader.

Lin is a Chinese character from a Chinese game. — Lin is a fictional character in a Japanese video game franchise (The King of Fighters) created by SNK, a Japanese company. His name and fighting style are inspired by Chinese culture, but the character is not of Chinese origin in the real world.

Lin is the same as other characters named Lin in other media. — There are many fictional characters named Lin, but they are separate entities. For example, Lin from The King of Fighters is distinct from Lin Hanzhong of NouerA or any other Lin.

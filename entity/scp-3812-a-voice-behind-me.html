Who It Is

A human who was 'activated' by a reality-altering anomaly and now exists simultaneously across every layer of reality — including layers above our own. SCP-3812 is aware that our universe is a fictional construct within a higher narrative reality (the SCP Wiki itself), and its consciousness continues ascending through infinite narrative layers. It hears the 'author's voice' and is slowly going insane from the knowledge that it is a character in a story.

Form

A human male, initially a formerly institutionalized individual, now appearing as a tall man whose form 'flickers' across multiple superimposed states — as if multiple versions of himself from different layers of reality are visible simultaneously. His eyes are described as containing 'infinite recursive depth.'

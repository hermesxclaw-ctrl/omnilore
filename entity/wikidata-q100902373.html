Etymology

Moriya (森谷) is a Japanese surname. The kanji 森 means 'forest' and 谷 means 'valley', so the name could be interpreted as 'forest valley'. 'Sensei' (先生) is an honorific meaning 'teacher' or 'master', used for educators, doctors, and other respected professionals. The combination suggests a respectful address for a teacher named Moriya.

Who It Is

A man purchases antique glasses that allow him to see every possible future simultaneously. Initially overwhelming — he sees his wife dying a thousand ways, his career failing in a hundred timelines. He learns to navigate toward the best outcomes. The horror: he becomes aware of the 'worst' future — a timeline where he never takes the glasses off, wasting away in a chair watching infinite realities while his body dies. He realizes this is the timeline he is on.

Form

Wire-rimmed spectacles, early 20th century, with smoked/tinted lenses. When worn, the world is overlaid with translucent 'ghost images' — potential futures playing simultaneously. The glasses cannot be broken or removed by anyone other than the wearer.

Powers

Visualization of all possible futures

Timeline navigation guidance

Addictive omniscience

Self-destructive lock-in

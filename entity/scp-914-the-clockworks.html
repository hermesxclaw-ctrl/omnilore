Anomalous Object Transformation

Form & Signs

Appearance — SCP-914 is a large clockwork device weighing several tons and covering an area of eighteen square meters. It consists of screw drives, belts, pulleys, gears, springs, and other clockwork components. The device is made of various materials, including wood, cloth, tin, and copper.

Symbols — SCP-914 does not have any specific symbols or iconography associated with it.

Domains — SCP-914 is typically found in a standard anomalous object containment chamber.

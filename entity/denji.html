Who It Is

A boy who sold body parts to survive now wields a chainsaw heart in the violent world of Devil Hunters. Denji's journey from poverty-stricken orphan to hybrid legend is a brutal, darkly comic tale of desire, sacrifice, and the search for simple pleasures.

Initially naive and desperate, Denji is driven by basic desires: food, shelter, and touch. He is straightforward, often oblivious to social cues, and has a strong survival instinct. He forms deep bonds with his friends, especially Power and Aki, and is willing to sacrifice himself for them. He grows more complex as he confronts the nature of devils and his own humanity.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Denji (デンジ) is a Japanese given name, but it also evokes 'denji' (電磁) meaning 'electromagnetic'. In the context of the series, it is a play on his chainsaw powers and the electric start of a chainsaw.

Pronunciation — DEN-jee

Aliases — Chainsaw Man, Chainsaw Devil, The Devil, Denji (full name not revealed)

Ownership — Created by Tatsuki Fujimoto, published by Shueisha in Weekly Shōnen Jump since 2018. Part of the modern shōnen manga tradition, with a dark, subversive take on the genre.

Powers
Chainsaw Transformation

Denji can transform into the Chainsaw Man hybrid by pulling a cord on his chest. In this form, he has chainsaws on his head and arms, granting him immense strength, speed, and the ability to cut through almost anything.

Limit: Transformation requires the cord to be pulled; if the cord is pulled again, he reverts to human. He can also transform partially, using only one chainsaw.

Regeneration

As a hybrid, Denji can regenerate from most injuries, even fatal ones, as long as he has blood. He has been shown to recover from being cut in half and decapitated.

Limit: Requires blood to heal; if he runs out of blood, he may die permanently.

Devil Physiology

As a human-Devil hybrid, Denji retains his human consciousness but gains devil-like abilities. He can consume devils to gain some of their powers, though this is not always shown.

Limit: The extent of power absorption is unclear; it may be limited to the devils he eats.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Denji is a teenage boy with messy blond hair and sharp eyes. After his transformation, he gains chainsaw blades on his head and arms, with a pull-cord starter on his chest. His hybrid form is muscular and demonic, with chainsaw appendages. In human form, he often wears a simple white shirt and tie (as a Devil Hunter) or casual clothes.

Symbols — The chainsaw is his defining symbol, representing both his power and his working-class origins. His heart is a symbolic link to Pochita, the Chainsaw Devil, often depicted as a small, cute dog-like creature.

Domains — Primarily set in Tokyo, in the world of Devil Hunters, where devils are born from human fears. Denji lives with Aki and Power in a shared apartment, and works for Public Safety Devil Hunters.

Accounts I
Origin and Contract with PochitaChainsaw Man manga, chapters 1-4

Contemporary

Denji is a poor orphan who works off his father's debt to the yakuza by hunting devils with his chainsaw dog Pochita. The yakuza betray him and kill him, but Pochita makes a contract with Denji, merging with his heart and granting him chainsaw powers. Denji becomes a Devil Hunter for the Public Safety Bureau. Denji gains a new purpose and becomes a member of the Public Safety Devil Hunters, meeting Aki and Power.

Accounts II
The Gun Devil ArcChainsaw Man manga, chapters 5-52

Contemporary

Denji joins a special division to hunt the Gun Devil, a powerful devil that caused mass destruction. He faces various devils and develops relationships with Aki and Power. He is manipulated by Makima, who seems to have a special interest in him. Denji grows as a fighter and forms strong bonds with his friends, but also becomes entangled in Makima's plans.

The Control Devil ArcChainsaw Man manga, chapters 53-97

Contemporary

Makima reveals herself as the Control Devil and seeks to control Denji and use him to create a better world. Denji, with the help of Power and Aki (who is now a devil), fights against Makima. In the climax, Denji kills Makima and eats her, becoming the new Control Devil hybrid? Actually, he eats her and gains her powers, but he later gives up the Control Devil to Nayuta, a new incarnation. Denji loses his friends but gains a new family with Nayuta. He becomes a more independent person, though still haunted by his past.

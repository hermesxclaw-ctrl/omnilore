Who It Is

Denji is the protagonist of Chainsaw Man — a teenage boy who, through a series of tragic and absurd events, becomes the hybrid known as Chainsaw Man, one of the most feared and powerful beings in the Chainsaw Man universe. Denji's life begins in abject poverty. Orphaned and alone, he inherits his father's debt to the yakuza and is forced to work as a devil hunter to repay it, living in a shack with his only friend and pet: Pochita, a small chainsaw devil who is also his best friend. When the yakuza betray Denji and leave him for dead, Pochita sacrifices himself to save Denji by merging with his heart. This fusion transforms Denji into Chainsaw Man — a hybrid who can sprout chainsaws from his arms, head, and legs at will. Denji's defining characteristic is his simplicity. He is not a traditional shonen hero with grand ambitions. His dreams are pathetically small: he wants to eat bread with jam, touch a girl's breasts, have a girlfriend, and live in a warm house. This grounded, almost absurdly mundane motivation sets him apart from virtually every other battle manga protagonist. He fights devils because it gives him a purpose, but his heart is driven by the desire for basic human comforts he was denied his entire life. As Chainsaw Man, Denji is a terror on the battlefield. His chainsaws can cut through virtually anything, he regenerates from nearly any wound (including decapitation), and his presence alone terrifies devils — because Chainsaw Man has the unique ability to permanently kill devils by devouring them, erasing their name and concept from existence. This power makes him the most feared being in Hell itself. Denji represents the triumph of simple humanity in a world of cosmic horror and absurdity. He is a boy who has been used, betrayed, and traumatized repeatedly, but who never stops reaching for the small, warm things that make life worth living. His story is a brutal, tragic, hilarious exploration of what it means to want, to love, and to be human in a world that treats you as a weapon.

Who It Is

In the neon-lit world of the Dead or Alive tournament, a lone fighter steps into the ring, his yellow gi a blazing tribute to the martial arts legend who taught him to fight. Jann Lee, orphaned and forged by the streets, fights not for glory or revenge, but for the pure, electric thrill of testing his limits against the world's best. But when a new challenger defeats him in an unsanctioned match, the champion's boredom shatters, and a new battle begins.

Jann Lee is fiercely independent and proud, driven by a desire to prove his strength. He is often aloof and serious, but shows respect for worthy opponents. He has a strong sense of justice, having saved Leifang from a gang when he was younger. He is not afraid to speak his mind and can be blunt.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Jann Lee' is a romanization of the Japanese katakana ジャン・リー (Jan Rī). It is likely intended to evoke Bruce Lee, given Jann Lee's fighting style and inspiration. The given name 'Jann' may be a variant of 'Jan' or 'John', but no official etymology is provided.

Pronunciation — JAN LEE (English); /dʒæn liː/; Japanese: /dʑaɴ ɾiː/

Aliases — Jan Rī, ジャン・リー

Ownership — Created by Team Ninja and Tecmo for the Dead or Alive video game series. Japanese video game franchise.

Powers
Jeet Kune Do

A martial art developed by Bruce Lee, emphasizing practicality, speed, and adaptability. Jann Lee uses a combination of punches, kicks, and footwork, often intercepting opponents' attacks.

Limit: No supernatural abilities; his power is purely physical and skill-based.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Jann Lee is a young East Asian man with a lean, athletic build. He typically wears a yellow martial arts gi, reminiscent of Bruce Lee's iconic outfit in 'Game of Death'. His hair is black and styled short. He often has a confident, intense expression.

Symbols — His signature yellow gi is a direct homage to Bruce Lee. His fighting style, Jeet Kune Do, is symbolized by quick, explosive movements and a focus on efficiency.

Domains — Originally from Hong Kong, he lived on the streets as an orphan. He later works as a bouncer at a high-class club. He travels internationally for tournaments.

Accounts I
Jann Lee's OriginDead or Alive (1996) manual and later games

Modern era (in-game)

Jann Lee was orphaned at a young age and grew up on the streets, learning Jeet Kune Do by watching martial arts films, particularly those of Bruce Lee. He fought to survive and to fill the void left by his parents' death, but eventually came to fight for the love of combat. He became a skilled fighter and took a job as a bouncer. He met Leifang when he saved her from a gang attack, setting up their rivalry.

First Tournament and Rivalry with LeifangDead or Alive (1996), Dead or Alive 2 (1999), Dead or Alive 4 (2005)

First to fourth tournaments

Jann Lee enters the DOA tournament to prove his skills. He is confronted by Leifang, who has trained to defeat him. Their rivalry continues across multiple tournaments, with Leifang winning in the fourth, and Jann Lee winning in the fifth. Their rivalry becomes a central part of both characters' stories, culminating in Jann Lee's victory in DOA5.

Accounts II
Fifth Tournament Victory and Rivalry with RigDead or Alive 5 (2012)

Fifth tournament

Jann Lee wins the fifth DOA tournament by defeating Hitomi in the finals. However, he feels unsatisfied and seeks a new challenge, setting his sights on Rig. He later defeats Rig in the sixth tournament, but realizes Rig was brainwashed. Jann Lee becomes the champion, but his victory feels hollow. He develops a rivalry with Rig, which is complicated by Rig's brainwashing.

Sixth Tournament and DiegoDead or Alive 6 (2019)

Sixth tournament

Jann Lee, as the long-standing champion, is almost bored. He defeats Rig, but then faces Diego in an unofficial match and loses. This reignites his passion for fighting. Jann Lee finds a new rival in Diego, hinting at future storylines.

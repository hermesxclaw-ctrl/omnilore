Who It Is

A mutated crustacean species that evolved from blue crabs and other shellfish after exposure to massive radiation. Mirelurks have developed thick, armor-like shells and aggressive territorial instincts. They inhabit coastal areas, swamps, and flooded ruins. Their claws can crush steel and their acidic spit dissolves most materials. Several subspecies have evolved for different environments.

Who It Is

A sweltering, labyrinthine service tunnel network where the temperature never drops below 40°C. Endless concrete corridors lined with massive steam pipes that hiss and clank. The heat causes hallucinations of rescue. Pipes sometimes burst with superheated steam that can cook a human in seconds. Yet Level 2 connects to Level 3, Level 4, and critical elevator shafts to deeper levels.

Form

Long, straight corridors of rough concrete, lined with massive industrial pipes in faded colors — red, blue, yellow — with peeling paint and bleeding rust. Steam vents hiss at irregular intervals. Floor occasionally flooded with scalding water.

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

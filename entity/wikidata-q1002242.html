Who It Is

In the moonlit world of Makai, where nobles vie for power and humans are prey, one vampire stands apart—not as a monstrous predator, but as a chivalrous lord who sought to end the bloodshed. Demitri Maximoff, the 'Dark Lord of Makai,' is a figure of tragic nobility, cursed by his own lineage and driven by a code that pits him against the very nature of his kind. His story is one of duty, forbidden love, and the eternal struggle between honor and survival.

Demitri is calm, chivalrous, and honorable. He despises the wanton cruelty of other Makai nobles and seeks to protect humans. He is often conflicted between his vampire nature and his moral code. He is shown to be respectful towards opponents, even enemies.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Demitri' is a variant of 'Dmitri', a Slavic name meaning 'devotee of Demeter'. 'Maximoff' is a Russian surname derived from 'Maxim', meaning 'greatest'. The combination suggests a character of noble and powerful stature.

Pronunciation — DEH-mih-tree MAKS-ih-moff

Aliases — Demitri, Dark Lord of Makai

Ownership — Japanese video game franchise (Capcom), inspired by Western vampire lore and gothic fiction.

Powers
Vampiric Powers

As a vampire, Demitri possesses superhuman strength, speed, and agility. He can also regenerate from wounds and has a resistance to damage.

Limit: Vulnerable to sunlight and holy objects, though not explicitly shown in gameplay.

Midnight Bliss

A special move that transforms the opponent into a woman, draining their life force. This is a signature ability unique to Demitri.

Limit: Cannot be used on characters who are already female or on non-humanoid opponents (in some games).

Demon Fireball

A projectile attack that launches a ball of dark energy.

Limit: Can be blocked or dodged.

Bat Form

Demitri can transform into a swarm of bats to move quickly or evade attacks.

Limit: Not invulnerable; can be hit while in bat form.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Demitri is a tall, pale-skinned vampire with long, flowing silver hair and crimson eyes. He wears an ornate, dark blue military-style uniform with gold trim and a high collar, often with a flowing cape. He has sharp fangs and a regal bearing.

Symbols — His symbol is a stylized rose, often seen in his victory poses and on his clothing. He is associated with the moon and night, and his signature move, the Midnight Bliss, transforms opponents into women.

Domains — He hails from Makai, the demon realm, but often resides in a castle on Earth. He is a noble of Makai and rules over his own territory.

Accounts I
Revenge Against BelialDarkstalkers: The Night Warriors

1994

Demitri, a vampire noble, seeks revenge against Belial Aensland, the ruler of Makai, for the death of his family. He enters a tournament to confront Belial, but ultimately defeats Pyron, the alien invader, instead. Demitri becomes a prominent figure in Makai and earns the respect of other warriors.

Accounts II
The Battle for MakaiVampire Savior

1997

When Jedah Dohma, a fallen noble, seeks to destroy and recreate Makai, Demitri opposes him. He fights to protect the realm and its inhabitants, including humans. Demitri helps defeat Jedah and restore order to Makai.

Who It Is
Form

The Mi-Go are described as fungi-like creatures, with a gelatinous body and long, thin limbs. They have a tendency to blend into their surroundings, making them difficult to spot.

Powers

Telepathy The Mi-Go possess the ability to read and control the minds of other beings, allowing them to manipulate and influence the actions of others.

Accounts

The story follows a professor who discovers that the Mi-Go are real, and that they are planning to invade Earth.

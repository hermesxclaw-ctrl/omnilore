Who It Is

A fungoid, crustacean-like being from beyond Pluto, the Mi-Go are roughly humanoid but winged, with multiple sets of limbs ending in pincers. They have no eyes — instead, their heads are covered in waving antennae that detect light, sound, and thought. Their bodies are composed of matter that does not obey earthly physics.

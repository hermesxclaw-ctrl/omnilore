Who It Is
Form

Kon is often depicted as a wingless being, sometimes with a human-like body and other times as a more abstract, wind-like entity.

Powers

Control over the winds Kon is said to have the power to control the winds, summoning storms and whirlwinds with a mere thought.

Accounts

According to legend, Kon was a powerful deity who controlled the winds and rain. He was said to have the power to summon storms and whirlwinds, and was often called upon by the people to bring fertility and prosperity to their lands.

Who It Is
Form

The Beast of Bodmin Moor is described as a large, black cat-like creature, often referred to as a 'panther' or 'puma'. It is said to be around 6-8 feet in length, with a broad head and a long, muscular body. Some reports describe it as having a distinctive 'roar' or 'growl', while others claim it has a more 'cat-like' sound.

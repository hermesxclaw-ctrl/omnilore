Identity

Takanashi Kiara

Pronunciation tah-kah-NAH-shee kee-AH-rah

Known As

Etymology

Takanashi (小鳥遊) is a Japanese surname; 'Kiara' is a given name. The surname's kanji literally mean 'small bird' and 'play' (or 'absent'), evoking a phoenix-like bird imagery.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese VTuber culture, specifically Hololive Production; Austrian-born persona, but character is Japanese-designed and owned by Cover Corp.

Disputed Identifications

None noted in source. The character is explicitly a phoenix, not a chicken or turkey, as stressed in lore.

Description

Appearance

Orange hair with green-blue highlights, purple eyes, wields a sword and shield. Designed by Huke.

Symbols & Iconography

Phoenix motif; orange color scheme; sword and shield; fast food imagery (KFP fan name).

Behaviour & Personality

Laid-back during gaming streams, can make blunt statements; glamorous popstar on stage; passionate about Japanese culture; entrepreneurial dreams of fast food chain; multilingual (English, Japanese, German); meme-loving, rickrolling; does not self-identify as idol.

Domains & Habitats

Livestreaming (YouTube), singing, talk show hosting (Holotalk); conventions; concerts.

Who It Is
Form

The Ropen is often described as a featherless, bat-like creature with a long, pointed snout and glowing eyes. Its wings are said to be wide and flat, with a membrane that glows with a soft, blue-green light. Some reports describe the Ropen as being as large as a small plane, while others claim it is much smaller, about the size of a large bird.

Powers

Bioluminescence The Ropen is said to have the power of bioluminescence, which allows it to glow in the dark. This power is said to be used for a variety of purposes, including navigation and communication.

Accounts

A group of villagers on Umboi Island reported seeing a large, bat-like creature with glowing eyes flying through the night sky. The creature was said to be as large as a small plane, and was feared by the villagers.

A group of fishermen in the Bismarck Archipelago reported seeing a large, bat-like creature with glowing eyes flying through the night sky. The creature was said to be much smaller than the Ropen of Umboi Island, about the size of a large bird.

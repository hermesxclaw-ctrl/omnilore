Who It Is
Form

Kamapua'a is often depicted as a pig-man, with a combination of human and porcine features. He is said to be a powerful and charismatic figure, with a strong connection to the natural world.

Powers

Fertility Magic Kamapua'a is said to possess the power of fertility magic, which allows him to bring abundance and prosperity to his people and his land.

Shape-shifting Kamapua'a is also said to have the ability to shape-shift, allowing him to take on various forms and guises as needed.

Weather Control Kamapua'a is associated with the control of the weather, and is said to have the power to summon storms and calm the winds.

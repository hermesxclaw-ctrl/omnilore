The Approach

In a world of super-saiyans and planet-destroying tyrants, the most powerful force on Earth might just be a balding, chain-smoking genius who built a spaceship in his backyard. Dr. Brief, the founder of Capsule Corporation, is the man who made the impossible mundane—shrinking entire cities into pill-sized capsules and launching humanity into the stars, all while wearing a lab coat and a perpetual look of mild disinterest.

Identity

Dr. Brief

Pronunciation Dok-tur Breef

Known As

Doctor BriefDr. BriefsDokutā Burifu (Japanese)

Etymology

The name 'Brief' is a pun on the word 'briefs', a type of underwear, consistent with the naming scheme of the Brief family (Bulma, Brief, etc.) where family members are named after undergarments.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Akira Toriyama for the Dragon Ball franchise, owned by Shueisha, Toei Animation, and related entities.

Disputed Identifications

Some English dubs and subtitles may spell the name as 'Dr. Briefs' with an 's', but the canonical spelling is 'Dr. Brief' without the 's'.

Description

Appearance

Dr. Brief is an elderly man with a bald head, a white mustache, and a thick white beard. He typically wears a white lab coat over a dark suit, and is often seen with a cigarette in his mouth. He has a tall, thin frame and a generally unkempt appearance, reflecting his absent-minded genius personality.

Symbols & Iconography

The Capsule Corporation logo, a stylized 'C', is associated with him. His iconic image includes his lab coat and cigarette.

Behaviour & Personality

Dr. Brief is portrayed as a brilliant but eccentric scientist, often appearing distracted and nonchalant even in crisis situations. He is kind and supportive, particularly towards his daughter Bulma, and is known for his casual attitude towards his own inventions, including time travel. He is also shown to be somewhat perverted, as he often makes passes at young women, including his own daughter's friends.

Domains & Habitats

He lives in a large estate in the city of West City, which serves as the headquarters of Capsule Corporation. His laboratory is filled with advanced technology and prototypes.

Version Differences

In the anime, his role is slightly expanded from the manga, with a few extra scenes. In Dragon Ball Super, he is shown to have aged further and is still active as a scientist. No significant differences in characterization across versions.

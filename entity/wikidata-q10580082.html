Who It Is

In a world of super-saiyans and planet-destroying tyrants, the most powerful force on Earth might just be a balding, chain-smoking genius who built a spaceship in his backyard. Dr. Brief, the founder of Capsule Corporation, is the man who made the impossible mundane—shrinking entire cities into pill-sized capsules and launching humanity into the stars, all while wearing a lab coat and a perpetual look of mild disinterest.

Dr. Brief is portrayed as a brilliant but eccentric scientist, often appearing distracted and nonchalant even in crisis situations. He is kind and supportive, particularly towards his daughter Bulma, and is known for his casual attitude towards his own inventions, including time travel. He is also shown to be somewhat perverted, as he often makes passes at young women, including his own daughter's friends.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Brief' is a pun on the word 'briefs', a type of underwear, consistent with the naming scheme of the Brief family (Bulma, Brief, etc.) where family members are named after undergarments.

Pronunciation — Dok-tur Breef

Aliases — Doctor Brief, Dr. Briefs, Dokutā Burifu (Japanese)

Ownership — Created by Akira Toriyama for the Dragon Ball franchise, owned by Shueisha, Toei Animation, and related entities.

Powers
Genius-level intellect

Dr. Brief is one of the most brilliant scientists on Earth, capable of inventing the Capsule technology that revolutionizes society, as well as advanced spacecraft, robots, and other devices.

Limit: His intellect is not combat-oriented; he has no superhuman physical abilities.

Capsule technology

He invented the Hoi-Poi Capsules, which can shrink any object, including buildings and vehicles, into a small capsule for easy transport.

Limit: The technology has size limits, but these are not clearly defined.

Spaceship engineering

He designed and built the spaceship used by the Z Fighters to travel to Namek, demonstrating advanced aerospace engineering.

Limit: None

Form & Signs

Appearance — Dr. Brief is an elderly man with a bald head, a white mustache, and a thick white beard. He typically wears a white lab coat over a dark suit, and is often seen with a cigarette in his mouth. He has a tall, thin frame and a generally unkempt appearance, reflecting his absent-minded genius personality.

Symbols — The Capsule Corporation logo, a stylized 'C', is associated with him. His iconic image includes his lab coat and cigarette.

Domains — He lives in a large estate in the city of West City, which serves as the headquarters of Capsule Corporation. His laboratory is filled with advanced technology and prototypes.

Accounts I
Introduction and the Search for the Dragon BallsDragon Ball manga and anime

Original series

Dr. Brief is introduced as Bulma's father and the founder of Capsule Corporation. He is shown in his lab, nonchalantly smoking, when Bulma brings Goku home. He provides them with supplies and a spaceship for their journey to Namek. Establishes his role as a supporting character and provider of technology.

Accounts II
Building the Namek SpaceshipDragon Ball Z

Saiyan/Namek saga

After the battle with Vegeta, Dr. Brief is tasked with building a spaceship to travel to Planet Namek. He uses a leftover alien pod as a base and constructs a new ship capable of making the journey. Enables the Z Fighters to travel to Namek and continue the story.

Time Travel ResearchDragon Ball Z and Super

Android/Cell saga and later

Dr. Brief assists Bulma in analyzing the time machine brought from the future by Trunks. His scientific expertise helps in understanding the technology, and he later aids in the development of a new time machine for Bulma's experiments. Facilitates time travel plotlines in the series.

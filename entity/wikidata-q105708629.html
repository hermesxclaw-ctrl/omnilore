Who It Is

The name 'Luminous' glows across vastly different worlds: a K-pop boy band, a Buddhist concept of radiant awareness, a Disney spectacular, and a video game engine. But what ties them together? A single word that has been used to signify light, clarity, and brilliance in music, faith, technology, and entertainment. This dossier illuminates the many facets of 'Luminous', tracing its appearances from ancient philosophy to modern pop culture, and revealing how one adjective became a multifaceted brand.

Luminous (boy band)K-pop industry

2019-present

Luminous is a South Korean boy band formed by CTK Entertainment. They debuted in 2019 with the single 'Starlight'. The group consists of five members: Youngbin, Suil, Steven, and others. They have released several albums and singles, and are known for their energetic performances. Gained a modest following in the K-pop scene.

A collection of science fiction stories by Greg Egan, exploring themes of consciousness, mathematics, and reality. Well-received in the science fiction community.

Who It Is
Form

Awitelin Tsta is often depicted as a powerful and nurturing figure, associated with the earth and the cycles of nature. She is said to be a wise and compassionate leader, who guides her people through times of hardship and abundance.

Powers

Fertility and Abundance Awitelin Tsta has the power to bring fertility and abundance to the earth and her people. She can cause crops to grow and flourish, and ensure that her people have enough to eat and drink.

Accounts

According to Zuni oral tradition, Awitelin Tsta played a key role in the creation of the world. She is said to have brought forth the earth and the natural world, and to have created the first humans.

According to Zuni oral tradition, Awitelin Tsta is the Corn Mother, who brings forth the crops and ensures that her people have enough to eat. She is said to have created the first corn and to have taught her people how to farm.

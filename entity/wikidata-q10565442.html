Who It Is

In the celestial bureaucracy of Daoist cosmology, where every mountain, river, and star has its appointed administrator, there exists a figure whose title alone evokes the cosmic balance itself: the Officer of Yin and Yang. This divine functionary, though less celebrated than the Jade Emperor or the Eight Immortals, embodies the Daoist conviction that order and harmony are maintained not by a single omnipotent will, but by a vast, meticulous administration of complementary forces.

Unknown. As a divine officer, he would presumably be dutiful, impartial, and concerned with maintaining cosmic harmony.

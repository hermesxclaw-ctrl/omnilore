Who It Is

In the sprawling, bloodstained mythology of Five Nights at Freddy's, few figures loom as large—or as ambiguously—as William Afton. He is the smiling founder of Freddy Fazbear's Pizza, the grieving father, the cunning killer, and the revenant trapped in a decaying animatronic suit. His story is a horror tapestry woven from scattered minigames, cryptic voice lines, novels, and fan speculation, making him one of gaming's most debated villains.

Afton is intelligent, manipulative, and charismatic, presenting a friendly facade while being a cold-blooded killer. He is obsessed with creating animatronics and achieving a form of immortality. He shows no remorse for his victims.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Afton' is likely a reference to Afton, a brand of canned meat, fitting the character's connection to the Freddy Fazbear's Pizza chain. The alias 'Springtrap' refers to the spring-lock animatronic suit he is trapped in.

Pronunciation — AF-ton (rhymes with 'raft on')

Aliases — Springtrap, Scraptrap, Afton, The Purple Guy, Dave Miller, The Yellow Rabbit

Ownership — Created by Scott Cawthon, owner of the Five Nights at Freddy's franchise. The character is a central figure in the franchise's mythology.

Powers
Spring-lock suit manipulation

Afton is skilled at using and surviving in spring-lock suits, which are designed to be worn by humans. He uses them to lure children.

Limit: The suits are dangerous; if the spring-locks malfunction, they will impale the wearer, as seen with his fate.

Animatronic engineering

Afton is a brilliant engineer who designs and builds animatronics, including the Funtime animatronics and possibly the original ones.

Limit: No apparent limitations, but his creations often have homicidal tendencies.

Immortality/Revenant state

After being spring-locked, Afton's spirit survives within the Springtrap suit, unable to die. He is later burned but returns in later games.

Limit: His body is decaying and he is in constant pain. He can be temporarily destroyed by fire, but his spirit persists.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In the minigames, Afton is depicted as a purple-skinned figure with a wide smile, often called the 'Purple Guy'. As Springtrap, he is a yellow-green rabbit animatronic with a decaying suit, exposed endoskeleton, and human remains visible. In the novels, he is described as a tall, thin man with gray hair and a gaunt face.

Symbols — The spring-lock suits, particularly the yellow rabbit (Spring Bonnie), are his iconic symbol. His signature color is purple, and he is associated with the Fazbear brand.

Domains — He is primarily associated with Freddy Fazbear's Pizza locations, including the original restaurant, the FNAF 2 location, and the Fazbear's Fright horror attraction. In the novels, he also has a house and a lab.

Accounts I
The Missing Children IncidentGame minigames (FNAF 2, FNAF 3), novels

1980s (in-universe)

William Afton lures five children into a back room and murders them, hiding their bodies in the animatronic suits. Their spirits then haunt the animatronics, causing them to attack adults at night. The animatronics become haunted, leading to the shutdown of the original Freddy Fazbear's Pizza. The children's spirits are eventually freed in FNAF 3 after Springtrap is destroyed.

The Bite of '83Five Nights at Freddy's 4 (2015)

1983 (in-universe)

Afton's son (the Crying Child) is accidentally bitten by Fredbear animatronic during his birthday party, leading to his death. This event is depicted in FNAF 4 minigames. The child dies, and this event likely motivates Afton's descent into madness and his obsession with animatronics.

Accounts II
The Spring-lock FailureFive Nights at Freddy's 3 (2015), minigames

1990s (in-universe)

Afton, hiding in the Spring Bonnie suit, is spring-locked when the suit's mechanisms fail, impaling him. He becomes trapped in the suit, becoming Springtrap. He survives but is horribly mutilated. He is later found and taken to Fazbear's Fright, where he escapes after the fire.

The Fire at Fazbear's FrightFive Nights at Freddy's 3 (2015)

2023 (in-universe)

Afton, as Springtrap, is trapped in the Fazbear's Fright horror attraction, which is set on fire in an attempt to destroy him. He survives, however. The attraction burns down, but Springtrap escapes, leading to his appearance in later games.

The Afton Family TragedySister Location (2016), Pizzeria Simulator (2017), novels

1980s-1990s (in-universe)

Afton's daughter Elizabeth is killed by Circus Baby, an animatronic Afton built, leading to her spirit possessing the robot. Afton's son Michael (likely the protagonist of Sister Location) is sent to free her, but instead is scooped, his body hollowed out, and he becomes a walking corpse. Afton loses his family, and Michael becomes a zombie-like figure who later seeks to destroy his father. This storyline is central to the later games.

Kon (the Coastal Creator-God / the Lord of the North)

also known asthe Coastal Creator-God / the Lord of the North
Typegod (coastal creator, people-maker)
Who It Is
Form

Kon is often depicted as a powerful, bearded god with a long coat and a staff, symbolizing his power over the land and the sea. In some depictions, he is shown with a fish or a whale, representing his connection to the ocean. His appearance is often shrouded in mist or clouds, signifying his mysterious and powerful nature.

Powers

Creation Kon has the power to create and sustain life, bringing forth the land, the sea, and the creatures that inhabit them.

Accounts

Kon, the coastal creator-god, was angered by the humans' disobedience and punished them by receding the waters, turning the lush land into coastal deserts. He took away the rain and left them only a few rivers and lakes to survive.

Kon, the Lord of the North, was associated with the Norse god Odin, who was known for his wisdom and power. Kon was said to have created the land and the sea, and to have given life to the creatures that inhabit them.

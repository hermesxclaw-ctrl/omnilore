Who It Is

Alone on a dead moon, the last child of a severed clan watches the Earth turn below — and decides, with absolute and lonely conviction, that he must destroy it. Toneri Ōtsutsuki is not a warlord or a trickster god but a pale, hollow-eyed heir who inherited a millennium of doctrine, a deserted palace, and a doomsday weapon, and who believes kidnapping the girl he has watched from afar is an act of salvation.

Attested: Toneri is solemn, formal, and doctrinaire, speaking in archaic, courtly Japanese (using 'ware' and formal keigo). Raised in total isolation after his branch family's extinction, he is socially naive, literal-minded, and utterly convinced of his inherited mission to punish humanity for misusing chakra. He shows no sadism; his cruelty is bureaucratic and theological — he believes he is fulfilling Hamura's will. Powers
Byakugan (Initial and Transplanted)

Descendant of Hamura, Toneri is born with a Byakugan-variant. By the film's present he is eyeless after his clan's demise (eyes lost/removed in childhood per guidebook). He later forcibly takes Hanabi Hyuga's pure Byakugan and implants it, which evolves into the Tenseigan. With Byakugan he gains near-360° vision, chakra pathway sight, and long-range telescopic observation (used to watch Hinata from the Moon).

Limit: Without transplanted eyes he is blind and relies on puppet sensory network. Transplanted Byakugan requires compatibility — only a pure Hyuga Byakugan (Hanabi, as Hinata's is tainted by Naruto's chakra per Toneri's dialogue) can awaken Tenseigan. Rejection risk implied.

Tenseigan (Reincarnation Eye)

Dojutsu awakened when an Ōtsutsuki chakra combines with a pure Hyuga Byakugan. Grants Tenseigan Chakra Mode: enhanced strength/speed/flight, green-blue chakra cloak, horn-like chakra crown, and control over attractive/repulsive forces similar to Rinnegan. Allows Toneri to cut the Moon in half with a single Golden Wheel Reincarnation Explosion (Kinrin Tensei Baku) and to move the Moon toward Earth.

Limit: Requires continuous Byakugan presence; when Naruto destroys the Energy Vessel and Hinata retrieves Hanabi's eyes, Toneri's Tenseigan fades and he is left blind again. Massive chakra cost — prolonged use drains him and leaves him vulnerable after mode ends. Cannot be maintained indefinitely without Energy Vessel support.

Truth-Seeking Balls (Gudōdama) - Tenseigan variant

In Tenseigan Chakra Mode Toneri generates up to ~10-12 green-blue Truth-Seeking Balls that can be shaped into swords, shields, or fired as beams. They nullify ninjutsu on contact and can be converted into the massive Golden and Silver Wheel explosions. Distinct color from Hagoromo's black orbs.

Limit: Balls are destroyed when Naruto's Six Paths-enhanced Rasengan overwhelms them. They require Tenseigan Chakra Mode active; loss of mode = loss of orbs. Cannot regenerate quickly if shattered.

Puppet Technique (Kugutsu no Jutsu) - Ōtsutsuki Autonomous Puppets

Controls an army of ancient, chakra-powered puppets left by Hamura's clan. Puppets can infiltrate Earth (kidnap Hanabi), fight jonin-level opponents, possess humans (Toneri possesses Hinata briefly via chakra orb), and maintain the Moon palace. Controlled via chakra orbs and the Energy Vessel.

Limit: Puppets are vulnerable to strong chakra disruption (Naruto, Hinata, and Sakura destroy them). Control range limited by Energy Vessel — when vessel is destroyed, puppets deactivate. Cannot replicate complex human creativity; they are formulaic.

Giant Tenseigan Energy Vessel Control

Ability to draw on and command the colossal Energy Vessel — a sphere composed of countless Byakugan eyes sealed over centuries by Hamura's clan. Grants telekinetic control over the Moon's orbit, barrier generation, and large-scale puppet animation. Toneri uses it to accelerate the Moon's descent toward Earth.

Limit: Vessel is stationary in Moon shrine; Toneri must be near it or linked via chakra. Destroyed when Hinata and Naruto infuse it with their chakra, causing it to shatter. Requires Hamura bloodline to command.

Space-Time / Prophetic Vision (Boruto era)

After redemption and sealing, Toneri exhibits limited clairvoyance / dream-projection, appearing in Boruto Uzumaki's dreams to warn of the 'curse of the Ōtsutsuki' and the Jōgan (Boruto's eye). Also shown to have been frozen in time by Urashiki Ōtsutsuki for 10,000 years as punishment for interference.

Limit: No longer combat-capable; sealed and immobile in a crystalline cocoon on the Moon. Visions are cryptic and involuntary, not controllable combat precognition. Urashiki's time-freeze renders him helpless.

Accounts I
The Schism of Hamura's Clan - Inheritance of the MoonThe Last: Naruto the Movie exposition + Official Guide pp.18-19; Boruto anime Episode 57 flashback

In-universe: ~1000 years before present (after Kaguya's sealing); Out-of-universe: created 2014

After Kaguya Ōtsutsuki is sealed by her sons Hagoromo and Hamura, Hamura leads a portion of the clan to the Moon to guard the Demonic Statue (Gedo Mazo) and her husk. Over centuries his descendants split into Main Family (who interpret Hamura's will as peace) and Branch Family (who interpret it as judgment). The Branch Family, Toneri's ancestors, annihilate the Main Family in a civil war using the Energy Vessel, but the vessel's overuse kills most of the Branch Family as well, leaving Toneri as the sole survivor, raised by his father until his father's death shortly before the film. Toneri inherits the doctrine that humanity has failed to uphold Hamura's ideals and must be destroyed. Establishes Toneri's isolation, his claim to Hamura's legacy, and his doomsday ideology. Explains the deserted Moon, the puppet army, and the Energy Vessel. Sets up thematic parallel to Hyuga Main/Branch conflict on Earth.

The Falling Moon - Toneri's Judgment BeginsThe Last: Naruto the Movie, Act 1 (00:00:00-00:30:00)

In-universe: Two years after Fourth Shinobi World War (contemporary to Naruto age 19); Out-of-universe: 2014 film

Toneri activates the Energy Vessel to break the Moon's orbit, sending it on collision course with Earth. Meteors begin falling on Earth. The Five Kage summon Team 7 (Naruto, Sakura, Sai, Shikamaru, Hinata) to investigate. Toneri's puppets infiltrate Konoha during the Rinne Festival and kidnap Hanabi Hyuga to steal her Byakugan, after determining Hinata's Byakugan is 'impure' due to Naruto's chakra influence. Toneri observes Hinata through his orb and declares she will be his wife in the new world he will create after humanity's erasure. Hanabi's abduction triggers rescue mission; meteors create ticking-clock stakes; Toneri obtains pure Byakugan and begins Tenseigan awakening (requires several days of integration).

The Abduction of Hinata - Puppet WeddingThe Last: Naruto the Movie, Act 2 (00:45:00-00:75:00)

In-universe: Same week as falling moon; Out-of-universe: 2014

Naruto's team travels to the Moon via dimensional passage. Toneri lures Hinata by appealing to her desire to save Hanabi and by using a genjutsu-like puppet orb that shows her a vision of Hamura's will. He places Hinata under a control technique (chakra orb possession) and takes her to his palace, dressing her in bridal Ōtsutsuki robes and preparing a wedding ceremony. He reveals his plan: marry Hinata, use her Byakugan lineage to repopulate after Earth's destruction, and become the progenitor of a new, pure world. Hinata feigns compliance to get close enough to destroy the Energy Vessel, secretly weaving a scarf for Naruto as a signal. Hinata's apparent betrayal devastates Naruto, causing him to fall into depression and be rescued by Sakura. Hinata learns the Energy Vessel's location and weakness. Toneri's Tenseigan fully awakens after integration completes.

Naruto vs Toneri - Moon-Splitting Battle and RedemptionThe Last: Naruto the Movie, Act 3 (00:78:00-01:45:00)

In-universe: Climax of The Last; Out-of-universe: 2014

Naruto, recovered and empowered by Hinata's chakra-infused scarf and his own Six Paths chakra, confronts Toneri in Tenseigan Chakra Mode. Toneri demonstrates overwhelming power: he slices the Moon in half with Golden Wheel Reincarnation Explosion, creates a massive storm of Truth-Seeking Balls, and drags Naruto into a chakra void. Hinata, freed from control by Naruto's presence, joins him and together they destroy the Energy Vessel by infusing it with Hamura-descendant chakra (Hyuga + Uzumaki/Naruto's Hagoromo chakra resonance). The vessel shatters, puppets collapse, Toneri's Tenseigan fades, and the Moon's descent halts. Naruto defeats Toneri in hand-to-hand after his mode ends, but spares him. Hinata retrieves Hanabi's eyes and returns them; Toneri is left blind again, weeping, realizing he misinterpreted Hamura's will — which was to protect humanity, not destroy it. Earth saved; Moon stabilized; Toneri blinded and repentant, chooses to remain on the Moon as atonement. Naruto and Hinata confess love and later marry (epilogue). The Sealed Prophet - Toneri's Warning to BorutoBoruto: Naruto Next Generations Episodes 53, 55-60 (2018), Studio Pierrot anime-original arc

In-universe: ~12-13 years after The Last (Boruto age 12); Out-of-universe: 2018 anime

Having lived in isolation on the Moon, Toneri senses the coming Ōtsutsuki threat (Momoshiki, Kinshiki, Urashiki). He uses his remaining power to appear in Boruto Uzumaki's dreams, awakening Boruto's Jōgan (pure eye) and warning that 'the star of hope' will face great darkness. When Urashiki Ōtsutsuki — a clan member who harvests chakra — arrives to capture Toneri for interfering with fate, Toneri is ambushed, frozen in time for 10,000 years inside a crystalline seal, but not before entrusting his will to Boruto via a time-dilated vision. He remains sealed on the Moon, observed by Sasuke Uchiha in later investigations. Establishes Toneri as benevolent guardian and explains Boruto's Jōgan origin in anime continuity. Provides bridge between Naruto and Boruto Ōtsutsuki lore. Toneri's sealing removes him from active plot, explaining his absence in Boruto manga.

Aftermath: The Warden of the MoonThe Last epilogue + Boruto anime Episode 59 + Official Guide epilogue page

In-universe: Post-2014 to present; Out-of-universe: 2014-2018

In the film's final scenes, Toneri — now eyeless and humbled — is shown alone in the Moon palace ruins, vowing to atone by watching over the Earth and never again interfering, with puppets now inert. The Boruto anime revisits him as a frozen, meditative figure in a crystal, still conscious, who has accepted his role as penitent watcher. No further combat appearances occur; his story becomes one of exile and quiet guardianship rather than villainy. Closes Toneri's arc from would-be destroyer to guardian; explains why Moon remains stable and why no further lunar threats emerge. Leaves open possibility of future unsealing.

Toneri is Kaguya Ōtsutsuki's son or brother. — Toneri is a distant descendant of Hamura Ōtsutsuki, Kaguya's son — separated by roughly 1,000 years and many generations. He is not a direct child of Kaguya; Hagoromo and Hamura are her only sons.

Toneri appears in the original Naruto manga and fights Naruto there. — Toneri never appears in Kishimoto's 700-chapter manga. His only canonical appearances are The Last film, its novelization, guidebooks, and Boruto anime. The manga jumps from the war to the epilogue without him.

Toneri's Tenseigan is the same as the Rinnegan or a Rinnegan evolution. — Tenseigan is a distinct dojutsu derived from Byakugan + Ōtsutsuki chakra, parallel to Rinnegan (which derives from Sharingan + Senju/Hagoromo chakra). They have similar abilities (gravity manipulation, Truth-Seeking Balls) but different origins, appearance, and lore. Guidebook explicitly calls Tenseigan 'the Byakugan's counterpart to the Rinnegan.'

Toneri is Hinata's destined love interest and she reciprocates. — Hinata never reciprocates; she pities Toneri's loneliness but consistently loves Naruto, uses Toneri's trust to sabotage his plan, and is distressed throughout captivity. The film explicitly frames Toneri's 'love' as obsessive, indoctrinated, and non-consensual.

Toneri is still a major villain in Boruto and will return as final boss. — As of 2026, Toneri remains sealed/frozen on the Moon in anime continuity and has not appeared in Boruto manga. No official announcement of his return as major antagonist exists; his role is now penitent guardian. Future unsealing is fan speculation.

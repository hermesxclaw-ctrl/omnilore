Who It Is

In the sweeping saga of the Seven Deadly Sins, Elizabeth Liones stands as a figure of quiet strength and profound sacrifice—a princess whose destiny intertwines with that of a demon lord, and whose journey from sheltered royalty to relentless warrior redefines the meaning of heroism.

Elizabeth is compassionate, kind, and fiercely protective of her friends and kingdom. She initially appears naive and sheltered, but grows into a courageous and determined leader. She is deeply loyal, especially to Meliodas, and is willing to sacrifice her own well-being for others. She possesses a strong sense of justice and a desire to end conflict peacefully, though she will fight when necessary.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Elizabeth' is of Hebrew origin, meaning 'God is my oath' or 'God's promise'. 'Liones' is the name of the kingdom she belongs to, derived from the word 'lion', symbolizing royalty and strength.

Pronunciation — el-IZ-uh-beth lee-OWNZ

Aliases — Elizabeth, Liz, Goddess Elizabeth, The Third Princess of Liones

Ownership — Created by Nakaba Suzuki for the Japanese manga series 'The Seven Deadly Sins' (七つの大罪, Nanatsu no Taizai), published by Kodansha. The character is part of the franchise owned by Ko

Powers
Goddess Powers

As a reincarnated goddess, Elizabeth possesses latent holy powers, including healing abilities and the capacity to purify demonic energy. In the later series, she awakens her full goddess powers, granting her enhanced strength, agility, and the ability to use powerful light-based attacks.

Limit: Her goddess powers are initially suppressed due to a curse placed on her by the Demon King, which causes her to die and reincarnate repeatedly, losing her memories. Her abilities are also limited when she is in her human form.

Healing

Elizabeth can heal wounds and cure ailments, a skill she uses frequently to aid her companions. Her healing is more effective than typical magic, as it draws on her goddess heritage.

Limit: Healing requires concentration and can be exhausting; she cannot heal fatal injuries instantly, and some curses or demonic wounds resist her power.

Combat Skills

Despite being primarily a healer, Elizabeth learns to wield a sword and fight, especially in the later arcs. She is not as powerful as the Seven Deadly Sins but is competent and brave.

Limit: Her combat abilities are limited compared to the main fighters; she relies on strategy and support.

Curse of Immortality

Due to the Demon King's curse, Elizabeth is bound to die and be reincarnated, losing all memories of her past lives. This curse is a central plot point and a source of immense suffering for her and Meliodas.

Limit: The curse forces her to relive her life repeatedly, and she can only break it by either killing the Demon King or sacrificing herself permanently.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Elizabeth is a young woman with long, flowing blonde hair and large blue eyes. She typically wears a white and blue dress with a hooded cloak, often bearing the symbol of the Liones kingdom. In later arcs, she adopts a more practical warrior outfit, including armor and a sword.

Symbols — The lion crest of Liones, often seen on her clothing or banners. Her association with the goddess clan is symbolized by her white and light-colored attire, and later by her holy powers.

Domains — Primarily the Kingdom of Liones, but she travels across Britannia with the Seven Deadly Sins. She is also associated with the goddess realm, though it is destroyed.

Accounts I
The Rescue of the Seven Deadly SinsThe Seven Deadly Sins, Chapters 1-3

Contemporary fiction

Elizabeth, the third princess of Liones, seeks the Seven Deadly Sins to help save her kingdom from the Holy Knights who have staged a coup. She finds Meliodas, the captain of the Sins, and they form an alliance to gather the other Sins and restore peace. This begins Elizabeth's journey away from the castle and her transformation from a sheltered princess to an active participant in the fight for justice.

Accounts II
The Reincarnation of the GoddessThe Seven Deadly Sins: Revival of The Commandments, Chapters 197-200

Contemporary fiction

Elizabeth learns of her past life as a goddess and her connection to Meliodas. She discovers that she has been cursed to reincarnate, and her memories of her previous lives are sealed. This revelation deepens her resolve to break the curse. Elizabeth's understanding of her identity and her relationship with Meliodas is profoundly altered, leading to a more determined and tragic heroism.

The Dragon's JudgementThe Seven Deadly Sins, Chapters 317-346

Contemporary fiction

In the final arc, Elizabeth fully awakens her goddess powers and joins the battle against the Demon King. She plays a crucial role in the fight, using her holy abilities to support the Sins and ultimately helping to defeat the Demon King, thereby breaking the curse. The curse is broken, and Elizabeth and Meliodas are finally able to live together in peace. Elizabeth's character arc culminates in her becoming a warrior and a leader.

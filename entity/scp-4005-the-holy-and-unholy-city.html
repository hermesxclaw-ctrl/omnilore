SCP-4005 — The Holy and Unholy City

also known asThe Holy and Heavenly City of Fabled ChinaThe Cairo Mosque Anomaly
Typescp
CultureSCP Foundation
Who It Is
Form

SCP-4005 is a large, sprawling city with a mix of ancient and modern architecture. The city is filled with towering spires and grand monuments, but it also has areas of decay and neglect. The city's appearance is constantly shifting, making it difficult to describe or depict.

Powers

Cognitohazardous SCP-4005 has the ability to affect the minds of those who enter it, causing them to experience a range of emotions and thoughts. This can include feelings of wonder, awe, and even terror.

Incident Log

SCP-4005 was discovered in 1975, when it was removed from storage in a Cairo mosque and lit during a full congregation. This resulted in several hundred worshippers being converted into SCP-4005-1 instances.

SCP-4005 is said to be a holy and heavenly city, filled with ancient wisdom and knowledge. It is said to be a place of great power and beauty, but also of great danger and uncertainty.

Etymology — The name 'SCP-4005' is derived from the SCP Foundation's classification system, where 'SCP' stands for 'Special Containment Procedures' and the number '4005' is a unique identifier.

Pronunciation — SCP-4005 is pronounced as 'S-C-P Four Thousand and Five'.

Aliases — The Holy and Heavenly City of Fabled China, The Cairo Mosque Anomaly

Ownership — SCP-4005 is a cultural artifact of the SCP Foundation, a fictional organization that specializes in containing and researching anomalous objects and entities.

Form & Signs

Appearance — SCP-4005 is a large, sprawling city with a mix of ancient and modern architecture. The city is filled with towering spires and grand monuments, but it also has areas of decay and neglect. The city's appearance is constantly shifting, making it difficult to describe or depict.

Symbols — The city is filled with symbols and iconography from various cultures and religions, including ancient Egyptian hieroglyphs, Christian crosses, and Islamic crescent moons. These symbols seem to be a mix of genuine cultural artifacts and anomalous creations.

Domains — SCP-4005 exists in a domain that is separate from our own, but it can be accessed through various portals and gateways. The city's habitats include ancient mosques, modern skyscrapers, and even areas of pure chaos and destruction.

Accounts I
The Discovery of SCP-4005SCP-4005 - SCP Foundation

1975

SCP-4005 was discovered in 1975, when it was removed from storage in a Cairo mosque and lit during a full congregation. This resulted in several hundred worshippers being converted into SCP-4005-1 instances. The discovery of SCP-4005 led to a significant increase in anomalous activity, as well as a number of unexplained events and occurrences.

Accounts II
The Holy and Heavenly City of Fabled ChinaSCP-4005 - The Holy and Heavenly City of Fabled China

2025

SCP-4005 is said to be a holy and heavenly city, filled with ancient wisdom and knowledge. It is said to be a place of great power and beauty, but also of great danger and uncertainty. The story of SCP-4005 has had a significant impact on Chinese folklore and culture, with many people believing in its existence and power.

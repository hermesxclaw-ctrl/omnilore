Who It Is

A pocket dimension containing a perfect, idealized Jerusalem where all three Abrahamic faiths coexist in harmony, prophets still walk the streets, and every prayer is literally answered. The city is inhabited by 'the Chosen' — humans who chose to stay. The Foundation discovered it in 1998. The door is unlocked — but only those who truly believe it exists can enter.

Form

A city of white stone and gold under a perpetual golden dawn. Temples, churches, and mosques stand side by side. Streets filled with people speaking a language that is simultaneously Arabic, Hebrew, and Aramaic.

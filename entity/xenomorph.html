Who It Is
Form

The Xenomorph is a tall, bipedal creature with a elongated head, a long, whip-like tail, and a pair of razor-sharp claws. Its body is covered in a dark, metallic exoskeleton that is resistant to damage, and it has a distinctive dome-shaped head with a pair of mandibles. The Xenomorph's appearance is both terrifying and fascinating, with its sleek, biomechanical design inspiring a sense of awe and wonder. The creature's appearance has been described as a fusion of organic and synthetic elements, with its body resembling a fusion of insect and machine. The Xenomorph's size can vary, with some specimens reaching heights of over 7 feet tall. Its appearance is also characterized by its distinctive 'dome' shaped head, which is said to be highly resistant to damage.

Powers

Acid Blood The Xenomorph has highly corrosive blood that is capable of dissolving metal and other materials. This blood is also highly toxic, and can cause severe burns and damage to human tissue.

Accounts

The film 'Alien' tells the story of a crew of space explorers who are stalked and killed by a Xenomorph on a distant planet. The crew soon discovers that the Xenomorph is a deadly and efficient predator, and that it will stop at nothing to kill them all.

The film 'Aliens' takes place 57 years after the events of the first film, and follows a group of space marines who are sent to the planet LV-426 to investigate the disappearance of the Nostromo's crew. The marines soon discover that the planet is infested with Xenomorphs, and that they are in grave danger.

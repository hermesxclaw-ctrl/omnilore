Who It Is

The Xenomorph is one of the most iconic and terrifying creatures in science fiction, with a rich history and a complex mythology. Its unique design and deadly abilities have made it a fan favorite among horror and science fiction enthusiasts, and its cultural significance extends far beyond the 'Alien' franchise.

The Xenomorph is a deadly and efficient predator, with a highly developed sense of smell and hearing. It is a solitary creature, only coming together with others of its kind to mate or to defend its territory. The Xenomorph is also highly intelligent, able to adapt to different environments and to use tools to achieve its goals. Its behavior is characterized by its stealthy and aggressive nature, with the creature using its speed and agility to catch its prey off guard.

Accounts I
AlienFilm

1979

The film 'Alien' tells the story of a crew of space explorers who are stalked and killed by a Xenomorph on a distant planet. The crew soon discovers that the Xenomorph is a deadly and efficient predator, and that it will stop at nothing to kill them all. The crew of the Nostromo is killed by the Xenomorph, with the exception of Ripley, who manages to escape in a shuttlecraft.

AliensFilm

1986

The film 'Aliens' takes place 57 years after the events of the first film, and follows a group of space marines who are sent to the planet LV-426 to investigate the disappearance of the Nostromo's crew. The marines soon discover that the planet is infested with Xenomorphs, and that they are in grave danger. The space marines are killed by the Xenomorphs, with the exception of Ripley, Hicks, and Newt, who manage to escape in a shuttlecraft.

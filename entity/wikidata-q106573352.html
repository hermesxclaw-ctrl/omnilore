Who It Is

In a distant corner of the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles multiverse, a proud race of humanoid dinosaurs wages war beneath a red sun—the Triceratons, whose honor and fury are as massive as their three-horned frames.

Proud, honorable, and warlike. Their society is based on military hierarchy and conquest, but they value courage and loyalty. In some versions, they are portrayed as noble savages or misguided aggressors.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Derived from 'Triceratops', combining Greek 'tri' (three) and 'keratops' (horned face).

Pronunciation — try-SER-uh-ton

Aliases — Triceratons, Triceraton Empire, Triceraton Republic, Raptor race

Ownership — Created by Kevin Eastman and Peter Laird for Mirage Studios; property of ViacomCBS (now Paramount).

Powers
Superhuman strength

Triceratons possess immense physical strength, able to easily overpower humans and most mutants.

Limit: Limited by relative size and combat skill.

Durability

Thick skin and bony frill provide natural armor, resistant to blunt force and some energy weapons.

Limit: Vulnerable to piercing weapons and concentrated energy blasts.

Horns and frill

Three sharp horns and a strong frill used in combat and intimidation.

Limit: Not effective against heavily armored opponents.

Advanced technology

Skilled in spaceship engineering, energy weapons, and military tactics.

Limit: Technology is advanced but not invincible.

Military discipline

Highly organized and skilled in large-scale warfare.

Limit: May be rigid and predictable in tactics.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Humanoid Triceratops with three horns, a bony frill, and a beak-like mouth. Varies in color (green, brown, red) and size; often depicted as massive and muscular. Some versions wear armor or military uniforms.

Symbols — Often associated with a militaristic insignia, sometimes a stylized Triceratops skull. In the 2003 series, they use a symbol resembling a horned skull.

Domains — Homeworld is a barren, desert-like planet, often called 'Triceraton' or 'Zentara'. They have a space-faring empire with advanced technology, including warships and energy weapons.

Accounts I
The Triceraton Invasion (Mirage)Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles #7

1986

The Triceratons arrive on Earth seeking a power source, clashing with the Turtles. They are portrayed as ruthless conquerors. The Turtles defeat them, but the encounter establishes the Triceratons as a recurring threat.

The Future War (Archie)Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles Adventures #5

1989

In a future timeline, the Triceratons are allies of the Turtles in a war against the evil dictator Zorak. The alliance helps save the future.

The Big Cuff (1987 TV)Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (1987) episode

1990

The Triceratons appear as space pirates or warriors, interacting with the Turtles in a comedic adventure. The Turtles outsmart them.

Accounts II
Turtles in Space (2003)Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (2003) Season 2

2003

The Turtles are captured by the Triceraton Federation and forced to fight in gladiatorial games. They eventually escape and help the Triceratons against the Utroms. The Triceratons' homeworld is destroyed, and they are left as refugees.

The Cosmic Zoo (2012)Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (2012) episode

2015

The Turtles encounter Triceratons in a space zoo, where they are depicted as exotic creatures rather than warriors. The Turtles help them escape.

The Triceraton Republic (IDW)Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (IDW) #50

2015

The Triceratons are a republic with a complex political structure, engaging in diplomacy and conflict with other species. Their role is more nuanced, with internal factions.

The Approach

In a distant corner of the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles multiverse, a proud race of humanoid dinosaurs wages war beneath a red sun—the Triceratons, whose honor and fury are as massive as their three-horned frames.

Content advisory. Portrayals include militaristic violence, genocide, and colonial themes; some adaptations depict the near-extinction of the species.
Identity

Triceraton

Pronunciation try-SER-uh-ton

Known As

TriceratonsTriceraton EmpireTriceraton RepublicRaptor race

Etymology

Derived from 'Triceratops', combining Greek 'tri' (three) and 'keratops' (horned face).

Cultural Ownership

Created by Kevin Eastman and Peter Laird for Mirage Studios; property of ViacomCBS (now Paramount).

Disputed Identifications

Some fans confuse Triceratons with the 'Utroms' or other alien races; distinct species.

Description

Appearance

Humanoid Triceratops with three horns, a bony frill, and a beak-like mouth. Varies in color (green, brown, red) and size; often depicted as massive and muscular. Some versions wear armor or military uniforms.

Symbols & Iconography

Often associated with a militaristic insignia, sometimes a stylized Triceratops skull. In the 2003 series, they use a symbol resembling a horned skull.

Behaviour & Personality

Proud, honorable, and warlike. Their society is based on military hierarchy and conquest, but they value courage and loyalty. In some versions, they are portrayed as noble savages or misguided aggressors.

Domains & Habitats

Homeworld is a barren, desert-like planet, often called 'Triceraton' or 'Zentara'. They have a space-faring empire with advanced technology, including warships and energy weapons.

Version Differences

Mirage: brutal conquerors. Archie: allies from the future. 1987: comedic but still warlike. 2003: imperialistic but with a sense of honor. 2012: more neutral, appearing as traders or warriors. IDW: a republic with a complex political structure.

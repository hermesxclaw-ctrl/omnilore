Who It Is

Gwydion is the greatest magician in Welsh mythology. He orchestrated a war to help his brother, for which he was transformed into animal pairs for three years. He circumvented his sister Arianrhod's curses on Lleu, and together with Math, created Blodeuwedd — a woman made of flowers.

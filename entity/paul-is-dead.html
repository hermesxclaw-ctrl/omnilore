Who It Is

The conspiracy theory that Paul McCartney died in a car crash in 1966 and was replaced by a lookalike named William Campbell (or 'Billy Shears'). Adherents found 'clues' in backward-masked lyrics, album artwork (the Abbey Road funeral procession), and lyrical references. The surviving Beatles, according to the theory, left an elaborate trail of hints for fans to discover.

Cultural Significance

The theory emerged during the period when 'Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band' introduced the fictional Billy Shears, blurring reality and fiction. It reflects the death-of-the-author dynamic where audiences claim ownership of meaning. McCartney himself has engaged with the myth humorously (1993 live album 'Paul Is Live').

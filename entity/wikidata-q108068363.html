Who It Is

She was one of the Jedi who believed the High Republic's golden age would last forever — until the sky over Valo burned. Jitomi Monoe, a Jedi Master of the Republic Fair, is remembered not for a long legend but for a single, terrible afternoon when the Nihil turned celebration into massacre, and for what her death revealed about how even the Order's most idealistic era could shatter in an instant.

Limited attested personality material in open summaries. As a Jedi Master assigned to the Republic Fair, Monoe is portrayed as embodying High Republic Jedi ideals: compassionate, service-oriented, confident in the Republic-Jedi partnership, and committed to public ceremony as a form of peacekeeping. The Rising Storm frames Valo Jedi as optimistic and somewhat unprepared for Nihil brutality; Monoe's behavior aligns with that collective characterization rather than a distinct, extensively developed personality arc. No evidence of dark-side affiliation, maverick traits, or council dissent

Accounts I
Assignment to the Republic Fair on ValoStar Wars: The High Republic: The Rising Storm by Cavan Scott (Del Rey, 2021)

2021 publication; in-universe 232 BBY, High Republic Era

In the build-up to Chancellor Lina Soh's Republic Fair — a showcase of Republic unity on the planet Valo — a contingent of Jedi is dispatched as guardians and symbols of peace. Jedi Master Jitomi Monoe is among those assigned to provide security, participate in ceremonies, and demonstrate Jedi-Republic cooperation. The Fair is presented as the high-water mark of High Republic optimism, with Jedi in formal white-and-gold attire. Establishes Monoe's role and the thematic stakes: the Fair's idealism will be shattered. Sets stage for Nihil attack.

The Nihil Attack on the Republic Fair - Death of Jitomi MonoeStar Wars: The High Republic: The Rising Storm

2021; in-universe 232 BBY

The Nihil, a marauder group led by Marchion Ro, launch a coordinated terror attack on the Republic Fair. Using long-range strikes, ground assaults, and chaos, they overwhelm Republic and Jedi defenses. Jitomi Monoe is killed during the attack. In the novel's structure, her death — alongside other Jedi and civilian casualties — is used to mark the brutality and strategic shock of the Nihil, who specifically target symbols of Republic hope. The attack is not a duel but a mass-casualty event; Jedi are killed by overwhelming force and treachery rather than single combat. Monoe's death contributes to the novel's turning point: the Republic and Jedi realize the Nihil are an existential threat, not mere pirates. The Fair's failure leads to political fallout, Jedi self-recrimination, and a shift to more militarized posture in subsequent High Republic works (The Fallen Star, etc.). Monoe becomes part of the memorialized losses of Valo.

Aftermath and Remembrance on Valo and CoruscantThe Rising Storm - aftermath chapters; referenced in later High Republic works (The Fallen Star, High Republic comics)

2021-2022

Following the attack, the Jedi and Republic mourn the dead, including Monoe. Her name appears in casualty lists and memorial contexts. Survivors such as Stellan Gios and Bell Zettifar carry trauma from Valo. The Jedi Council debates its role and vulnerability. Monoe is not resurrected; her legacy is as one of the fallen who illustrate the cost of underestimating the Nihil. Institutional consequences: increased Jedi vigilance, narrative justification for later defensive measures at Starlight Beacon. Character consequence: Monoe remains deceased; no Force ghost or later appearance attested.

The High Republic Multimedia Project - Creation and Publication Story (Out-of-Universe Narrative)Lucasfilm Publishing announcements (2020-2021), Star Wars: The High Republic Show, publisher catalogs (Del Rey, Marvel, IDW)

2020-present

Out-of-universe, Jitomi Monoe was created as part of Lucasfilm's High Republic initiative, a coordinated publishing effort to explore the Jedi golden age 200 years before The Phantom Menace. Authors including Cavan Scott, Charles Soule, Claudia Gray, Justina Ireland, and Daniel José Older developed interconnected novels and comics. Monoe was introduced to provide a sense of scale and mortality — demonstrating that even Jedi Masters could fall, raising stakes for the era. Her creation reflects franchise world-building strategy rather than folklore evolution. Establishes Monoe as a canonical but supporting character, designed to support thematic goals of the initiative rather than to anchor a solo series.

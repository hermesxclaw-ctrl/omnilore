The Approach

She was one of the Jedi who believed the High Republic's golden age would last forever — until the sky over Valo burned. Jitomi Monoe, a Jedi Master of the Republic Fair, is remembered not for a long legend but for a single, terrible afternoon when the Nihil turned celebration into massacre, and for what her death revealed about how even the Order's most idealistic era could shatter in an instant.

Content advisory. Character death by mass violence/terrorist attack; fictional depiction of mass casualty event involving civilians and children (Republic Fair attack). No sexual violence attested.

Symbols & Iconography

Symbols are those of the Jedi Order during its High Republic height: the Jedi crest, the gold-and-white High Republic dress uniform, and the lightsaber as symbol of guardianship. At the Republic Fair on Valo, Jedi iconography is explicitly tied to Republic unity and optimism — Jedi as ceremonial guardians and mediators. Monoe's iconographic role is that of the guardian-martyr: her death is used narratively to symbolize the end of Republic complacency. No personal sigil or unique artifact attested.

The Nihil Attack on the Republic Fair - Death of Jitomi Monoe

Source Star Wars: The High Republic: The Rising StormCulture Star Wars canonPeriod 2021; in-universe 232 BBY

Summary

The Nihil, a marauder group led by Marchion Ro, launch a coordinated terror attack on the Republic Fair. Using long-range strikes, ground assaults, and chaos, they overwhelm Republic and Jedi defenses. Jitomi Monoe is killed during the attack. In the novel's structure, her death — alongside other Jedi and civilian casualties — is used to mark the brutality and strategic shock of the Nihil, who specifically target symbols of Republic hope. The attack is not a duel but a mass-casualty event; Jedi are killed by overwhelming force and treachery rather than single combat.

Consequences

Monoe's death contributes to the novel's turning point: the Republic and Jedi realize the Nihil are an existential threat, not mere pirates. The Fair's failure leads to political fallout, Jedi self-recrimination, and a shift to more militarized posture in subsequent High Republic works (The Fallen Star, etc.). Monoe becomes part of the memorialized losses of Valo.

The High Republic Multimedia Project - Creation and Publication Story (Out-of-Universe Narrative)

Source Lucasfilm Publishing announcements (2020-2021), Star Wars: The High Republic Show, publisher catalogs (Del Rey, Marvel, IDW)Culture American publishing franchisePeriod 2020-present

Summary

Out-of-universe, Jitomi Monoe was created as part of Lucasfilm's High Republic initiative, a coordinated publishing effort to explore the Jedi golden age 200 years before The Phantom Menace. Authors including Cavan Scott, Charles Soule, Claudia Gray, Justina Ireland, and Daniel José Older developed interconnected novels and comics. Monoe was introduced to provide a sense of scale and mortality — demonstrating that even Jedi Masters could fall, raising stakes for the era. Her creation reflects franchise world-building strategy rather than folklore evolution.

Mass-casualty terrorist attack and Jedi death

Source Cavan Scott, The Rising Storm (2021)

Description

Jitomi Monoe is killed during the Nihil's coordinated attack on the Republic Fair on Valo, an event depicted as deliberate targeting of a civilian cultural festival. The narrative presents mass panic, explosions, and deaths of both Jedi and civilians. The attack is framed as Nihil strategy to shatter Republic morale.

Scholarly Context

Within Star Wars scholarship and criticism, the Valo attack is discussed as Lucasfilm's attempt to introduce 9/11 and World's Fair attack analogies into Star Wars, exploring how an optimistic democracy responds to terrorism. Critics note the High Republic uses Valo to deconstruct Jedi invincibility. No sanitization: the novel does not hide that Jedi fail to prevent civilian deaths.

Contradictions & Disputes

Identity and death location - Monoe vs other Valo Jedi

Position A

Jitomi Monoe died during the Nihil attack on the Republic Fair on Valo (The Rising Storm).

Position A — evidence

The Rising Storm text and Wookieepedia/StarWars.com summaries specifically list Monoe among Valo Fair casualties; Wikidata links Monoe to The Rising Storm.

Position B

Fan summaries occasionally conflate Monoe with other Jedi who died at Valo (e.g., Jora Malli, who also dies in High Republic material, or Jedi who die at Starlight Beacon later).

Position B — evidence

Conflation arises from ensemble cast size (dozens of Jedi) and similar narrative function (stakes-establishing deaths). No primary source merges these characters; confusion is fandom error, not canonical contradiction.

Scholarly Consensus

No scholarly contradiction; consensus is that Monoe is distinct Jedi Master killed at Valo. Distinguish carefully; do not merge casualties.

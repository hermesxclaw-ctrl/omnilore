The Approach

In the sweltering heat of a Cairo street, a Japanese high school student in a sailor uniform faces a vampire who can stop time. Jotaro Kujo, the stoic delinquent with a golden heart, doesn't flinch. He simply adjusts his cap and says, 'Good grief.' This is the man who punched his way into anime history.

Content advisory. Contains graphic violence, blood, and themes of death and vengeance.
Identity

Jotaro Kujo

Pronunciation KOO-joh JOH-tah-roh (Japanese: Kūjō Jōtarō)

Known As

JoJo空条 承太郎 (Kūjō Jōtarō)JotaroStar Platinum's user

Etymology

Kujo (空条) is a surname that can be interpreted as 'sky' (空) and 'branch' or 'strip' (条). Jotaro (承太郎) combines '承' (accept, receive) and '太郎' (common masculine name suffix, often used for firstborn sons).

Cultural Ownership

Created by Hirohiko Araki, a Japanese manga artist. The character is part of the JoJo's Bizarre Adventure franchise, published by Shueisha in Weekly Shonen Jump and later in Ultra Jump.

Disputed Identifications

There is no significant dispute over Jotaro's identity as a fictional character. However, in the alternate universe of JoJolion, Yoshikage and Kei Kira take the place of the Joestar family line, meaning Jotaro does not exist in that continuity.

Description

Appearance

Jotaro is a tall, muscular young man with sharp, intense eyes. He has short, black hair and is often depicted with a scowl or a neutral expression. His iconic outfit is his Japanese high school sailor uniform (gakuran), which he wears throughout Stardust Crusaders, even in the desert. He also wears a black cap with a golden chain and a matching black jacket. In later parts, he wears a more casual suit or a white coat over his shoulders.

Symbols & Iconography

Jotaro's symbol is his Stand, Star Platinum, a powerful humanoid figure with a muscular build and long hair. Star Platinum's iconic pose is with its fists clenched, ready to deliver a rapid barrage of punches. Jotaro's catchphrase 'yare yare daze' (good grief) is also iconic. His hat is a signature accessory.

Behaviour & Personality

Jotaro is stoic, calm, and collected. He rarely shows emotion, but he is fiercely protective of his family and friends. He is a loner who prefers to act alone, but he is also a natural leader. He is intelligent and perceptive, often deducing his enemies' abilities during battle. Despite his rough exterior, he has a strong sense of justice and will risk his life for others.

Domains & Habitats

Jotaro is primarily from Tokyo, Japan. He travels to Egypt in Stardust Crusaders, and later lives in Morioh, a fictional town in Japan, for Diamond Is Unbreakable. He also spends time in a prison in Stone Ocean.

Version Differences

In the manga, Jotaro is more reserved and his expressions are often blank. In the anime, his voice actor Daisuke Ono adds a subtle intensity to his tone. In the OVAs (1993-1994), Jotaro is voiced by Jūrōta Kosugi and depicted with a slightly different character design. In the video games, his abilities are often exaggerated for gameplay purposes.

Powers & Abilities

Star Platinum

Source Version Stardust Crusaders

Description

A close-range Stand with incredible speed, strength, and precision. It can punch at the speed of light, shatter diamonds, and perform delicate tasks like catching bullets or pulling out a bullet from a wound.

Limitations

Star Platinum has a limited range of about 2 meters from Jotaro. It can only sustain its full power for a short time. It cannot be used for long-range attacks.

Contradictions

In later parts, Star Platinum's power is shown to be slightly weaker, possibly due to Jotaro's age or lack of practice.

Star Platinum: The World

Source Version Stardust Crusaders (final battle) and later parts

Description

An evolution of Star Platinum that allows Jotaro to stop time for a few seconds. During this time, he can move freely while everything else is frozen. The duration increases with practice, but it is initially only about 1-2 seconds.

Limitations

The time-stop ability is physically exhausting and can only be used a few times per day. The duration is limited and depends on Jotaro's stamina. In later parts, it is shown to have a reduced duration due to his age.

Contradictions

In Part 3, Jotaro discovers the ability during his fight with Dio, and it is implied that he can use it for up to 5 seconds. In Part 4, he uses it for about 2 seconds. In Part 6, he uses it for about 1 second, and he states that it is because he has not used it in a long time.

Stories & Myths

Stardust Crusaders

Source JoJo's Bizarre Adventure Part 3, manga and animeCulture Japanese pop culturePeriod 1989-1992 (manga), 2014-2015 (anime)

Summary

Jotaro Kujo, a high school delinquent, is possessed by a Stand, which he mistakes for an evil spirit. His grandfather, Joseph Joestar, and his friend Muhammad Avdol explain that the Stand is a manifestation of his fighting spirit, and that it was awakened by the vampire Dio Brando, who is using a parasitic flesh bud to control people. Jotaro defeats the Stand user Noriaki Kakyoin and frees him from Dio's control. His mother, Holly, becomes ill due to her own Stand awakening, and the only way to save her is to kill Dio. Jotaro, Joseph, Avdol, Kakyoin, and later Jean Pierre Polnareff and Iggy, travel to Egypt to confront Dio. Along the way, they face numerous Stand users sent by Dio. They eventually reach Dio's mansion, but Avdol and Iggy are killed by Vanilla Ice. Jotaro, Joseph, and Polnareff confront Dio, but Dio's Stand, The World, can stop time. Kakyoin is killed, but he discovers Dio's ability and passes the information to Joseph. Dio kills Joseph, but Jotaro awakens his own time-stop ability and defeats Dio. Jotaro then uses Dio's blood to revive Joseph, and they return to Japan after Holly recovers.

Dio is defeated, Holly is saved, and the Joestar family is freed from Dio's curse. Jotaro becomes a marine biologist and later helps his uncle Josuke in Part 4.

Variants

The anime adaptation is faithful to the manga, but some scenes are extended or altered for pacing. The OVA adaptation is more condensed and changes some events.

Diamond Is Unbreakable

Source JoJo's Bizarre Adventure Part 4, manga and animeCulture Japanese pop culturePeriod 1992-1995 (manga), 2016 (anime)

Summary

Jotaro arrives in the town of Morioh to investigate a mysterious Stand arrow and to meet his illegitimate uncle, Josuke Higashikata. He discovers that the town is plagued by Stand users, some of whom are malevolent. Jotaro mentors Josuke and helps him defeat the serial killer Yoshikage Kira, who has a Stand that can reset any situation. Jotaro's time-stop ability is used to defeat Kira. He also helps Josuke and his friends deal with other threats, including the Stand user Akira Otoishi and the bow-and-arrow user Keicho Nijimura.

Stone Ocean

Source JoJo's Bizarre Adventure Part 6, manga and animeCulture Japanese pop culturePeriod 1999-2003 (manga), 2021-2023 (anime)

Summary

Jotaro's daughter, Jolyne Cujoh, is framed for murder and sent to prison. Jotaro is attacked by the Stand user Whitesnake, who steals his Stand and memories, leaving him in a coma. Jolyne must retrieve her father's Stand and defeat the villain Enrico Pucci, who is carrying out Dio's legacy. Jotaro eventually awakens and helps Jolyne in the final battle, but he is killed by Pucci, only for the universe to be reset, creating an alternate timeline where Jotaro does not exist.

Violence

Source Stardust Crusaders, manga and anime

Description

Jotaro frequently engages in brutal fights, often resulting in severe injuries or death for his opponents. He also kills Dio by punching him repeatedly and then leaving him to burn in the sun.

Scholarly Context

The violence is typical of shonen manga, but it is often depicted in a stylized manner.

Death of loved ones

Source Stardust Crusaders, Stone Ocean

Description

Jotaro witnesses the deaths of Kakyoin, Avdol, Iggy, and later his own death in Stone Ocean. These events have a profound impact on him.

Scholarly Context

These deaths are used to raise the stakes and develop Jotaro's character.

Family neglect

Source Stone Ocean

Description

Jotaro is often absent from his daughter's life, leading to their strained relationship. He prioritizes his work as a marine biologist over his family.

Scholarly Context

This is a realistic portrayal of a father who struggles to balance his duties.

Contradictions & Disputes

Time-stop duration

Position A

In Part 3, Jotaro can stop time for up to 5 seconds.

Position A — evidence

During his fight with Dio, Jotaro states he can stop time for 5 seconds.

Position B

In Part 4 and Part 6, Jotaro's time-stop duration is reduced to 2 seconds and 1 second respectively.

Position B — evidence

In Part 4, he uses it for 2 seconds, and in Part 6, he states it is only 1 second due to lack of practice.

Scholarly Consensus

This is not a contradiction but a deliberate narrative choice to show Jotaro's aging and decreased focus.

Jotaro's personality

Position A

Jotaro is a stoic, emotionless character.

Position A — evidence

His expressionless face and calm demeanor.

Position B

Jotaro is a passionate and caring person who hides his emotions.

Position B — evidence

His actions, such as risking his life for his friends and family, and his occasional outbursts of anger.

Scholarly Consensus

Most fans and critics agree that Jotaro is a tsundere-like character, with a soft heart beneath his tough exterior.

Star Platinum's power level

Position A

Star Platinum is the strongest Stand in the series.

Position A — evidence

Star Platinum's immense speed and strength, and its time-stop ability.

Position B

Other Stands, such as Gold Experience Requiem or Made in Heaven, are stronger.

Position B — evidence

The abilities of other Stands are more hax or reality-altering.

Scholarly Consensus

There is no official consensus, but many fans debate this. Araki has not given a definitive answer.

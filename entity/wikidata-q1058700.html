Who It Is

In the sweltering heat of a Cairo street, a Japanese high school student in a sailor uniform faces a vampire who can stop time. Jotaro Kujo, the stoic delinquent with a golden heart, doesn't flinch. He simply adjusts his cap and says, 'Good grief.' This is the man who punched his way into anime history.

Jotaro is stoic, calm, and collected. He rarely shows emotion, but he is fiercely protective of his family and friends. He is a loner who prefers to act alone, but he is also a natural leader. He is intelligent and perceptive, often deducing his enemies' abilities during battle. Despite his rough exterior, he has a strong sense of justice and will risk his life for others.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Kujo (空条) is a surname that can be interpreted as 'sky' (空) and 'branch' or 'strip' (条). Jotaro (承太郎) combines '承' (accept, receive) and '太郎' (common masculine name suffix, often used for firstborn sons).

Pronunciation — KOO-joh JOH-tah-roh (Japanese: Kūjō Jōtarō)

Aliases — JoJo, 空条 承太郎 (Kūjō Jōtarō), Jotaro, Star Platinum's user

Ownership — Created by Hirohiko Araki, a Japanese manga artist. The character is part of the JoJo's Bizarre Adventure franchise, published by Shueisha in Weekly Shonen Jump and later in Ultra

Powers
Star Platinum

A close-range Stand with incredible speed, strength, and precision. It can punch at the speed of light, shatter diamonds, and perform delicate tasks like catching bullets or pulling out a bullet from a wound.

Limit: Star Platinum has a limited range of about 2 meters from Jotaro. It can only sustain its full power for a short time. It cannot be used for long-range attacks.

Star Platinum: The World

An evolution of Star Platinum that allows Jotaro to stop time for a few seconds. During this time, he can move freely while everything else is frozen. The duration increases with practice, but it is initially only about 1-2 seconds.

Limit: The time-stop ability is physically exhausting and can only be used a few times per day. The duration is limited and depends on Jotaro's stamina. In later parts, it is shown to have a reduced duration due to his age.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Jotaro is a tall, muscular young man with sharp, intense eyes. He has short, black hair and is often depicted with a scowl or a neutral expression. His iconic outfit is his Japanese high school sailor uniform (gakuran), which he wears throughout Stardust Crusaders, even in the desert. He also wears a black cap with a golden chain and a matching black jacket. In later parts, he wears a more casual suit or a white coat over his shoulders.

Symbols — Jotaro's symbol is his Stand, Star Platinum, a powerful humanoid figure with a muscular build and long hair. Star Platinum's iconic pose is with its fists clenched, ready to deliver a rapid barrage of punches. Jotaro's catchphrase 'yare yare daze' (good grief) is also iconic. His hat is a signature accessory.

Domains — Jotaro is primarily from Tokyo, Japan. He travels to Egypt in Stardust Crusaders, and later lives in Morioh, a fictional town in Japan, for Diamond Is Unbreakable. He also spends time in a prison in Stone Ocean.

Accounts I
Stardust CrusadersJoJo's Bizarre Adventure Part 3, manga and anime

1989-1992 (manga), 2014-2015 (anime)

Jotaro Kujo, a high school delinquent, is possessed by a Stand, which he mistakes for an evil spirit. His grandfather, Joseph Joestar, and his friend Muhammad Avdol explain that the Stand is a manifestation of his fighting spirit, and that it was awakened by the vampire Dio Brando, who is using a parasitic flesh bud to control people. Jotaro defeats the Stand user Noriaki Kakyoin and frees him from Dio's control. His mother, Holly, becomes ill due to her own Stand awakening, and the only way to save her is to kill Dio. Jotaro, Joseph, Avdol, Kakyoin, and later Jean Pierre Polnareff and Iggy, travel to Egypt to confront Dio. Along the way, they face numerous Stand users sent by Dio. They eventually reach Dio's mansion, but Avdol and Iggy are killed by Vanilla Ice. Jotaro, Joseph, and Polnareff confront Dio, but Dio's Stand, The World, can stop time. Kakyoin is killed, but he discovers Dio's ability and passes the information to Joseph. Dio kills Joseph, but Jotaro awakens his own time-stop ability and defeats Dio. Jotaro then uses Dio's blood to revive Joseph, and they return to Japan

Diamond Is UnbreakableJoJo's Bizarre Adventure Part 4, manga and anime

1992-1995 (manga), 2016 (anime)

Jotaro arrives in the town of Morioh to investigate a mysterious Stand arrow and to meet his illegitimate uncle, Josuke Higashikata. He discovers that the town is plagued by Stand users, some of whom are malevolent. Jotaro mentors Josuke and helps him defeat the serial killer Yoshikage Kira, who has a Stand that can reset any situation. Jotaro's time-stop ability is used to defeat Kira. He also helps Josuke and his friends deal with other threats, including the Stand user Akira Otoishi and the bow-and-arrow user Keicho Nijimura. Kira is defeated, Morioh is saved, and Jotaro returns to his work as a marine biologist.

Accounts II
Golden WindJoJo's Bizarre Adventure Part 5, manga and anime

1995-1999 (manga), 2018-2019 (anime)

Jotaro appears briefly at the beginning of Part 5. He sends Koichi Hirose to Italy to investigate Dio's son, Giorno Giovanna. Jotaro suspects that Giorno may be a threat, but Koichi reports that Giorno is a righteous person, so Jotaro decides to leave him alone. Jotaro's decision allows Giorno to pursue his goal of becoming a gangster and eventually defeating the boss of Passione.

Stone OceanJoJo's Bizarre Adventure Part 6, manga and anime

1999-2003 (manga), 2021-2023 (anime)

Jotaro's daughter, Jolyne Cujoh, is framed for murder and sent to prison. Jotaro is attacked by the Stand user Whitesnake, who steals his Stand and memories, leaving him in a coma. Jolyne must retrieve her father's Stand and defeat the villain Enrico Pucci, who is carrying out Dio's legacy. Jotaro eventually awakens and helps Jolyne in the final battle, but he is killed by Pucci, only for the universe to be reset, creating an alternate timeline where Jotaro does not exist. Pucci is defeated, but the universe is reset. In the new universe, Jotaro is replaced by Yoshikage Kira in the Joestar family line.

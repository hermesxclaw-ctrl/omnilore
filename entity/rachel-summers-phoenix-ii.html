Who It Is

Rachel Summers is the daughter of Cyclops and Jean Grey from the dystopian 'Days of Future Past' timeline (Earth-811), and the second major host of the Phoenix Force. Born in a timeline where mutants are hunted by Sentinels, Rachel inherited both her mother's telepathic/telekinetic powers and her cosmic connection to the Phoenix. Her childhood was a nightmare: she was captured by the mutant-hunting agency and mentally conditioned as a 'Hound' — a psi-tracker forced to hunt her own kind. After being sent forward in time to Earth-616, she became a member of the X-Men and Excalibur, carrying the trauma of her past while wielding godlike power. Rachel bonded with the Phoenix Force after touching the remnants of the Phoenix Egg left behind by her mother's death. As Phoenix, she shares many of Jean's abilities — matter manipulation, cosmic fire projection, resurrection — but her power is flavored by her timeline's pain and survival. She has also demonstrated unique abilities like chrono-skimming (perceiving and manipulating alternate timelines) and powerful psychometry. Later taking the codename Prestige, Rachel has been a core member of Excalibur, a mentor figure to younger mutants, and a cosmic guardian. Her relationship with the Phoenix is complicated: she represents the resilience of the Phoenix — the fire that survives even in the darkest timeline, the spark that refuses to be extinguished by tyranny. Unlike her mother's tragic fall, Rachel's Phoenix journey is one of redemption from trauma and finding hope in the ashes of a broken world.

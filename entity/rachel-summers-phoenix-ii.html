Who It Is

She was born into a future of nightmare, a world where robotic Sentinels hunted mutants to extinction. Torn from her timeline, bonded to a cosmic force of life and death, Rachel Summers has lived a dozen lives: Hound, Phoenix, Excalibur knight, Askani mother, and Marvel Girl. Her story is a testament to survival, redemption, and the enduring power of family—even when that family is scattered across time and space.

Rachel is fiercely independent, strong-willed, and often carries a sense of survivor's guilt. She is compassionate but can be aloof and guarded due to her traumatic past. She is a natural leader and protector, often putting others before herself. Her experiences have made her wary of authority and technology, but she is deeply loyal to her chosen family.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Rachel is a Hebrew name meaning 'ewe' or 'lamb', symbolizing innocence and sacrifice. Summers is her father's surname, reflecting her lineage to the Summers family. Grey is her mother's maiden name, which she later adopts to honor her mother and distance herself from her father's actions.

Powers
Telepathy

Rachel possesses powerful telepathic abilities, allowing her to read minds, project thoughts, and manipulate mental perceptions. She can also create psychic shields and attack opponents mentally.

Limit: Her telepathy can be blocked by strong mental defenses or other telepaths. Overuse can cause mental exhaustion.

Telekinesis

Rachel can move objects with her mind, levitate herself, and create protective shields. She can also use telekinesis offensively, such as throwing objects or creating kinetic blasts.

Limit: Requires concentration; can be disrupted by physical or mental interference.

Phoenix Force Connection

Rachel has a unique connection to the Phoenix Force, a cosmic entity of life and death. This grants her near-limitless power, including resurrection, reality manipulation, and energy projection. She has been a full host, an echo host, and has channeled the Phoenix's power.

Limit: The Phoenix Force can corrupt or overwhelm its host. Rachel has struggled to control it, and it has been used against her. The echo was weaker than the full force.

Time Travel

Rachel has the ability to travel through time, either by herself or with the aid of technology. She can also send her consciousness back in time.

Limit: Time travel is risky and can create paradoxes. She often requires external devices or the Phoenix Force to do so.

Chronoskimming

As Prestige, Rachel could 'skim' through time, witnessing past and future events without physically traveling. She could also send her mind back to her past self.

Limit: Limited to observation; she could not alter events without physically being there. Prolonged use could cause disorientation.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Rachel is a tall, slender woman with fiery red hair, often styled straight or with bangs. Her eyes are green, similar to her mother's. She has been depicted in various costumes: her original Excalibur uniform, a dark Phoenix-inspired costume, her Marvel Girl green and yellow outfit, and her Askani robes. She often has a distinctive 'phoenix' emblem on her attire.

Symbols — The Phoenix bird is her primary symbol, representing her connection to the Phoenix Force. She has also used the X-Men 'X' symbol and the Askani 'A' symbol. Her 'deathmark' from the Shi'ar is a scar on her back.

Domains — Rachel has lived in various realities and times: Earth-811 (dystopian future), Earth-616 (present), the future of Earth-616 (Askani era), and Otherworld. She is comfortable in both futuristic and contemporary settings, often serving as a temporal anomaly.

Accounts I
Days of Future PastThe Uncanny X-Men #141-142

1981

In a dystopian future (Earth-811), mutants are hunted by Sentinels. Rachel, a young mutant, is captured and turned into a Hound, forced to track other mutants. She eventually joins the resistance and uses her powers to send Kitty Pryde's consciousness back in time to prevent the assassination of Senator Kelly. The mission fails to change the future, and Rachel later sends her own mind back, encountering the Phoenix Force. Rachel is pulled into the past (Earth-616), where she becomes a member of the X-Men. Her actions in the past set the stage for her future adventures.

ExcaliburExcalibur #1-50

1988-1991

Rachel, along with Shadowcat, Nightcrawler, Captain Britain, and Meggan, forms the superhero team Excalibur based in London. They face threats from other dimensions and time travel. Rachel struggles to control her Phoenix powers and deals with the revelation that her mother, Jean Grey, is alive in this timeline. Rachel learns to control her powers and forms lasting friendships. She also confronts her feelings about her mother.

AskaniX-Factor #65-68, Adventures of Cyclops and Phoenix #1-4

1990-1994

After being lost in the timestream, Rachel emerges 2000 years in the future, where Apocalypse rules. She founds the Askani, a resistance movement. She brings her 'brother' Nathan (Cable) from the past to save him from a techno-organic virus, and later raises him with the minds of Scott and Jean. Rachel dies, but her legacy continues. Rachel's actions create the timeline of Cable and Stryfe. She is resurrected later, but her Askani identity persists.

Accounts II
Marvel GirlUncanny X-Men #444-450, X-Men: Phoenix – Endsong

2004-2005

Rachel rejoins the X-Men, taking the name Marvel Girl in honor of her mother, who had recently died. She wears a costume designed by Jean. She also changes her last name to Grey, expressing disapproval of her father's relationship with Emma Frost. Rachel re-establishes herself as a prominent X-Man and deals with her family legacy.

Rise and Fall of the Shi'ar EmpireUncanny X-Men #475-486

2006-2007

Rachel joins a team led by Professor X to stop her uncle Vulcan from destroying the Shi'ar empire. She uncovers the truth about her family's murder and meets Korvus, a descendant of a previous Phoenix host. She absorbs the echo of the Phoenix from his sword, forming a bond with him. Rachel becomes a member of the Starjammers and helps restore Lilandra to the throne. She later returns to Earth.

Age of XAge of X #1-5, X-Men Legacy #245-247

2011

Reality is warped by Legion's alternate personality Moira, creating a world where mutants are besieged. Rachel's mind is trapped in this reality as 'Revenant', losing memories of her true self. When reality is restored, her mind remains altered. Rachel's mind is separated from her body temporarily. She later joins the Jean Grey School staff.

Avengers vs. X-MenAvengers vs. X-Men #1-12

2012

Rachel sides with Cyclops initially, believing she can control the Phoenix Force to help Hope Summers. However, when Cyclops and Emma Frost are corrupted, she opposes them and helps the Avengers in the final battle. The Phoenix Force is destroyed, and Cyclops is imprisoned. Rachel's faith in the Phoenix is shaken.

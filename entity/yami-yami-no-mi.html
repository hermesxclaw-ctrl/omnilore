Who It Is
Form

The Yami Yami no Mi is a dark, fruit-like object with a smooth, glossy surface. It is said to have a powerful aura surrounding it, making it difficult to approach. When consumed, the fruit grants the user the ability to control and manipulate darkness.

Darkness Manipulation The Yami Yami no Mi grants the user the ability to create and control darkness at will, allowing them to manipulate the shadows and create powerful illusions.

Who It Is

The Dark-Dark Fruit, an abnormal Logia that creates, controls, and transforms the user into darkness itself. Unlike other Logias, the user cannot become intangible — instead, darkness acts as a gravitational void that pulls in anything, including other Devil Fruit powers. It is considered the most 'evil' power, capable of nullifying Devil Fruit abilities on touch and absorbing attacks into the void.

Powers

Darkness generation and manipulation

Devil Fruit power nullification (on touch)

Gravitational pull (attracts objects and attacks)

Absorption: stores absorbed attacks/objects in void

Black Hole (massive gravitational collapse)

Kurouzu (Black Vortex)

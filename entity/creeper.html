Who It Is

The most common enemy in the original Silent Hill. Creepers are small, insectoid creatures with a vaguely humanoid shape. They have multiple legs, sharp claws, and move in swarms. They can crawl on walls and ceilings and attack by leaping at their prey. Their chittering sounds are a hallmark of the Otherworld. Also known as the 'Parasite' monster.

Swarming, wall-crawling, leaping attack

Who It Is

A 'noisy spirit' (German: poltern 'to rumble' + Geist 'ghost') responsible for physical disturbances: loud knocking, objects being thrown or moved, furniture rearranging, scratching sounds, and in severe cases physical attacks such as pinching, biting, or hitting. Unlike traditional ghosts, poltergeists are rarely seen — their presence is inferred entirely through physical effects. Parapsychology distinguishes the poltergeist as possibly a psychokinetic manifestation of a living person (usually an adolescent under stress) rather than a discarnate spirit — the 'recurrent spontaneous psychokinesis' (RSPK) theory. Famous cases include the Enfield Poltergeist (London, 1977), the Bell Witch (Tennessee, 1817), and the Rosenheim Poltergeist (Germany, 1967).

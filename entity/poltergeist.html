Who It Is
Form

Poltergeists are often depicted as mischievous or malevolent spirits that can take various forms, such as a dark mist, a shadowy figure, or a humanoid entity with glowing eyes. They are typically associated with physical disturbances, such as loud noises and objects being moved or destroyed. In some cases, they are said to appear as a ball of light or a swirling vortex.

Powers

Physical Disturbances Poltergeists are said to be capable of causing physical disturbances, such as loud noises, objects being moved or destroyed, and other forms of chaos and disorder.

Object Manipulation Poltergeists are often believed to be able to manipulate objects, such as moving furniture or levitating objects.

Electrical Interference Poltergeists are sometimes associated with electrical interference, such as flickering lights or malfunctioning appliances.

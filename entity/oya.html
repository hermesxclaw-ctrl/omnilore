Who It Is
Form

Oya is often depicted as a fierce and powerful woman, sometimes with a crown or a staff, and is associated with the colors red and black. She is said to be a tall and imposing figure, with a strong presence and a commanding voice.

Powers

Control of the Eégún Oya is said to be the only orisha capable of controlling the Eégún, a powerful and mysterious force that is associated with the wind and lightning.

Storm summoning Oya is said to be able to summon storms and bring forth the wind and lightning.

Transformation Oya is said to be able to transform and change the world around her, bringing forth new life and new possibilities.

Accounts

Oya is said to be the goddess of the Niger River, and is often associated with the river's powerful and life-giving waters. According to myth, Oya created the Niger River and brought forth the wind and lightning that shape the landscape.

Oya is said to be the goddess of the storms and the wind, and is often associated with the powerful and destructive forces of nature. According to myth, Oya summoned the storms and brought forth the wind and lightning that shape the landscape.

Who It Is
Form

The Ing Warrior is often depicted as a fierce and powerful warrior, wearing armor and wielding a sword. He is sometimes associated with the boar, which is said to be a symbol of his strength and ferocity.

Powers

Battle Frenzy The Ing Warrior is said to enter a state of battle frenzy, becoming nearly unstoppable in combat. He is able to fight with increased strength and ferocity, striking down his enemies with ease.

Accounts

The Ing Warrior is said to play a key role in the battle of Ragnarök, fighting alongside the gods against the giants and monsters.

Ingvar the Far-Traveler is said to be a variant of the Ing Warrior, who travels to distant lands and battles against monsters and giants.

Who It Is

In the chaos of the Clone Wars, few warriors earned the dread of both Separatists and Imperials like Echuu Shen-Jon, a Jedi Knight whose name became a whisper of vengeance long after the Republic fell.

Echuu Shen-Jon is portrayed as a skilled and determined Jedi, though his experiences during the Clone Wars lead him to a path of vengeance, showing a more aggressive and conflicted side compared to typical Jedi ideals.

Force abilities

As a Jedi Knight, Echuu Shen-Jon is proficient in basic Force powers, including telekinesis, enhanced reflexes, and possibly Force sense. His exact abilities are not detailed in available sources.

Limit: Not fully explored in the game; his abilities are typical of a Jedi Knight.

Pilot and combat skills

Echuu Shen-Jon is an expert starfighter pilot, capable of flying advanced craft like the Jedi starfighter. He is also skilled in lightsaber combat, though his lightsaber skills are not prominently featured in the game.

Limit: His piloting is showcased, but his lightsaber combat is not shown.

Clone Wars (22-19 BBY)

Echuu Shen-Jon serves as a Jedi Knight during the Clone Wars. He is involved in space battles against the Separatists. After the rise of the Galactic Empire and Order 66, he survives and becomes a fugitive, seeking vengeance against the Empire and the Sith. His survival and quest for vengeance are a testament to the Jedi's fall and the Empire's persecution.

Rise of the Empire (19 BBY onwards)

After Order 66, Echuu Shen-Jon goes into hiding. He eventually emerges to confront the Empire, possibly targeting Imperial forces and Sith operatives. His actions are part of the larger narrative of surviving Jedi during the Dark Times. His fate is not clearly resolved in the game; he may have continued to fight or eventually been killed.

In the chaos of the Clone Wars, few warriors earned the dread of both Separatists and Imperials like Echuu Shen-Jon, a Jedi Knight whose name became a whisper of vengeance long after the Republic fell.

Cultural Ownership

LucasArts / Lucasfilm (now Disney). Echuu Shen-Jon is a character from the Star Wars franchise, created for the 2004 video game Star Wars: Jedi Starfighter.

Disputed Identifications

No disputed identifications are known. The character is not a major figure and has limited appearances.

Description

Appearance

Echuu Shen-Jon is depicted as a humanoid male Jedi Knight with light skin, dark hair, and typically wearing standard Jedi robes. In the game, he is seen in a flight suit and helmet when piloting starfighters.

Symbols & Iconography

As a Jedi, he is associated with the Jedi Order's symbols, including the Jedi crest. No unique personal symbols are known.

Behaviour & Personality

Echuu Shen-Jon is portrayed as a skilled and determined Jedi, though his experiences during the Clone Wars lead him to a path of vengeance, showing a more aggressive and conflicted side compared to typical Jedi ideals.

Domains & Habitats

He operates in various star systems during the Clone Wars, primarily in space combat. No specific homeworld is established.

In the neon-lit world of Buriki One, a fighter known only as Silber steps into the ring—a man whose past is as metallic and unyielding as his name. But who is he beyond the arena? The game offers glimpses, but the full story remains a tantalizing mystery.

Disputed Identifications

There are two distinct fictional characters named Silber: one from the fighting game Buriki One and one from Buffy the Vampire Slayer. They are unrelated.

Description

Appearance

In Buriki One, Silber is depicted as a tall, muscular man with short dark hair and a stern expression. He wears a traditional martial arts uniform, often with a belt. No official art is widely available, but fan depictions show him as a serious fighter.

Symbols & Iconography

His name, meaning 'silver', may be reflected in a color scheme of silver or gray in his attire. No specific symbols are known.

Behaviour & Personality

Based on his fighting style (a mix of martial arts) and his role in the game, Silber is likely disciplined and focused. However, the game provides minimal personality information.

Domains & Habitats

He exists within the world of Buriki One, a martial arts tournament setting. No specific habitat is detailed.

Version Differences

The Buriki One Silber is a fighter. Katrina Silber from Buffy is a college student. They share only the surname.

Who It Is

A river spirit and dragon god who serves as Yubaba's apprentice in Spirited Away. Haku takes the form of a young boy but can transform into a magnificent white dragon. He lost his name and memories after becoming Yubaba's apprentice. His true name is Nigihayami Kohakunushi (the Kohaku River God). Chihiro's friendship helps him recover his identity.

Powers

Dragon transformation, flight, water manipulation, magic

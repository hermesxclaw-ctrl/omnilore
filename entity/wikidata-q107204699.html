Who It Is

In the spring of 1983, a bay colt with an unassuming name—born from a farm's initials—thundered into Japanese racing history, becoming only the third horse to sweep the coveted Triple Crown. His name was Mr. C. B., a horse whose legacy would gallop far beyond the track, into the hearts of fans and the pixels of a modern gaming phenomenon.

Known for a solid waist and stable rear-oriented center of gravity, unlike many offspring of Tosho Boy. Described as 'a horse that inherited only the good traits of Tosho Boy.'

Accounts I
Triple Crown VictoryWikipedia

1983

Mr. C. B. became the third horse to win the Japanese Triple Crown, after St Lite and Shinzan. He was ridden by jockey Masato Yoshinaga in all his starts, a unique feat for a Triple Crown winner. Named Japanese Horse of the Year for 1983 and inducted into the JRA Hall of Fame in 1986.

Modern

In the game, Mr. C. B. is portrayed as a free-spirited horse born to a trainer and trainee couple whose eloping caused a scandal, mirroring real-world controversy around his foaling. In-game, she lives off-campus and is friends with Symboli Rudolf and Katsuragi Ace.

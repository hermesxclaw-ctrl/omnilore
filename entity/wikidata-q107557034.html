Identity

Kobayashi

Pronunciation ko-ba-ya-shi (IPA: [kobaꜜjaɕi])

Etymology

The surname Kobayashi (小林) is composed of two kanji: 小 (koba) meaning 'small' and 林 (hayashi) meaning 'woods' or 'grove', thus 'small woods'. A less common variant is 古林 (Kobayashi), with 古 (ko) meaning 'old', yielding 'old woods'.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese

Disputed Identifications

The surname is shared by many unrelated families and individuals; there is no single progenitor. Variants like 古林 are sometimes confused with 小林.

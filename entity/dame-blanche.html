Who It Is
Form

Dame Blanche is often depicted as a white-clad woman, sometimes with a white dress or a white shroud, and is said to appear at crossroads, particularly at night.

Powers

Waylaying Dame Blanche has the ability to waylay travelers at crossroads, demanding a small payment or offering them a choice between death and a terrible fate.

Accounts

A traveler is waylaid by Dame Blanche at a crossroads and must choose between death and a terrible fate.

A young woman is haunted by the ghost of Dame Blanche at a crossroads, and must confront her own mortality.

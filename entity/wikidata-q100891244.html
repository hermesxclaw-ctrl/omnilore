Etymology

The name 'Ageha' (アゲハ) is the Japanese word for the swallowtail butterfly, a creature often symbolic of transformation, fleeting beauty, and the soul in Japanese culture. 'Kurono' (黒野) combines 'kuro' (black) and 'no' (field), suggesting 'black field' or 'dark plain.' The name's poetic resonance hints at a character connected to themes of change, darkness, and perhaps the natural world.

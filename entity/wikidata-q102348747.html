Who It Is

In the shattered cities of Sera, where humanity's last stand is fought with chainsaws and gunfire, one man stands apart—not for his courage, but for his sarcasm. Damon Baird, the wisecracking engineer of Delta Squad, is the genius who keeps the gears turning while the world burns, proving that even in the apocalypse, wit is a weapon.

Baird is highly intelligent, resourceful, and pragmatic, but he is also cynical, sarcastic, and often dismissive of authority. He has a dry wit and frequently makes jokes in the face of danger, which can be seen as either a coping mechanism or genuine disregard for danger. He is fiercely loyal to his squad, especially to Marcus Fenix, despite their constant bickering. He is also known for his disdain for the COG's bureaucracy and his willingness to break rules to get the job done.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Damon' is of Greek origin, meaning 'to tame' or 'constant', while 'Baird' is a Scottish surname meaning 'poet' or 'minstrel'. The combination may reflect the character's role as a storyteller of sorts, albeit a sarcastic one.

Pronunciation — DAY-mon BAIRD

Aliases — Damon Baird

Ownership — Damon Baird is a fictional character owned by The Coalition (formerly Epic Games) and Microsoft, originating in the Gears of War franchise.

Powers
Engineering Genius

Baird has an exceptional aptitude for mechanics, electronics, and engineering. He can repair, modify, and improvise weapons, vehicles, and equipment under extreme pressure.

Limit: His skills are limited by available resources and time; he cannot create something from nothing.

Combat Proficiency

Baird is a trained soldier, proficient in the use of the Lancer assault rifle, Gnasher shotgun, and various other weapons. He is also skilled in hand-to-hand combat, though he is not as physically imposing as Marcus Fenix.

Limit: He is not superhuman; his combat skills are within the realm of a highly trained human.

Tactical Resourcefulness

Baird often devises unconventional solutions to tactical problems, using his engineering knowledge to create traps, hack systems, or repurpose equipment to gain an advantage.

Limit: His plans sometimes rely on assumptions that can fail under unpredictable circumstances.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Baird is a Caucasian male with short, dark hair and often a five-o'clock shadow. He typically wears the standard COG armor, but with a distinctive yellow-tinted visor on his helmet, which he sometimes wears pushed up. He is of average height and build, but his engineering background is reflected in his practical, sometimes scruffy appearance.

Symbols — Baird is often associated with the COG (Coalition of Ordered Governments) gear symbol, but his personal iconography includes his signature sarcastic smirk and the yellow visor, which sets him apart from other soldiers. He is also known for his wrench, a symbol of his mechanic role.

Domains — Baird is primarily found on the planet Sera, in various war-torn environments, including cities, military bases, and underground facilities. He is most at home in engineering bays, vehicle depots, and anywhere he can tinker with machinery.

Accounts I
The Battle of Aspho FieldsGears of War: Aspho Fields (novel)

Before the events of Gears of War (pre-Emergence Day, during the Pendulum Wars)

Baird, along with Marcus Fenix, Dominic Santiago, and others, participated in a pivotal battle against the UIR (Union of Independent Republics). During the battle, Baird's engineering skills were crucial in disabling enemy defenses, though the mission was marred by tragedy and personal loss. The battle's events deepened the bond between Baird and his squad, but also contributed to the ongoing war's toll and the eventual downfall of the COG's reputation.

The Fall of JacintoGears of War 2 (video game)

During the Locust War

Baird accompanies Delta Squad on a mission to find a way to stop the Locust Horde. His engineering expertise is vital in repairing vehicles, hacking terminals, and ultimately in the plan to sink Jacinto to flood the Locust tunnels. The sinking of Jacinto results in the displacement of the human survivors and the near-destruction of the Locust, but also leads to the rise of the Lambent threat.

Accounts II
The End of the Locust WarGears of War 3 (video game)

During the Locust War, after the fall of Jacinto

Baird joins Marcus's squad in a final assault to stop the Locust and Lambent. He plays a key role in repairing the ship that takes them to Azura, and later in the final battle against the Locust Queen. The Locust are defeated, but at great cost. Baird survives and becomes a leader in the aftermath.

The New COGGears of War 4 (video game)

25 years after Gears of War 3

Baird has become a leader of a settlement called the 'Outsiders' and is instrumental in helping the new protagonist, J.D. Fenix, and his team. He is now a mentor figure, using his engineering skills to help rebuild society and fight the new threat, the Swarm. Baird's actions help the new generation survive and confront the Swarm, and he becomes a key figure in the new COG.

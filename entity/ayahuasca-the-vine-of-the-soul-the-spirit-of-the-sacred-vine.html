Who It Is
Form

Ayahuasca is a plant-based psychedelic brew made from the Banisteriopsis caapi vine and other plants, typically containing the psychoactive compound DMT.

Powers

Healing and Transformation Ayahuasca is said to possess the power to heal emotional and physical wounds, facilitating personal growth and spiritual transformation.

Accounts

According to Quechua legend, Ayahuasca was created by the gods to guide humans through the underworld, facilitating their journey to the afterlife.

The Shipibo people believe that Ayahuasca is a sacred vine, imbued with the power to communicate with the spirits of the forest and the ancestors.

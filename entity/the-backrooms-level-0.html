Who It Is

A vast, infinite liminal space consisting of monotonous yellow rooms, damp carpet, buzzing fluorescent lighting, and the constant, suffocating smell of wet carpet. Those who 'noclip' through reality (glitch through walls) find themselves in the Backrooms — a purgatorial maze where 'it's been... oh God, forever' since anyone has been around. The Backrooms is not a single entity but a dimension inhabited by entities that stalk the wanderers. It originated from a 2019 4chan post and spawned an entire mythos of levels, entities, and survival rules.

Reality transition (noclipping in/out)

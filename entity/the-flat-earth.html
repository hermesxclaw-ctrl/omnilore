Who It Is
Form

The Flat Earth is often depicted as a flat disc, with the horizon visible from any point on the surface. In some depictions, the Flat Earth is shown with a firmament above it, separating the Earth from the sky.

Powers

Deception The Flat Earth has the power to deceive and mislead people, making them believe in its existence and validity.

Accounts

Christopher Columbus set sail across the Atlantic, facing disputes over the shape of the Earth. He believed in a spherical Earth, while many people believed in a flat Earth.

Galileo Galilei challenged the idea of the flat Earth, using evidence from astronomy and mathematics to prove that the Earth is a sphere.

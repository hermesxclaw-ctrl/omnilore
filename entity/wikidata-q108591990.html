Who It Is

She is the middle head of a living Gorgon — eight meters of coiled muscle, green hair like vines, and a pair of eyes that can turn a battlefield to stone. Yet when Boa Sandersonia drops her predatory grin and her massive anaconda form, what you see is not a monster but a scarred younger sister, desperately guarding the slave brand on her back from a world that would kill her for it.

Attested personality: Sandersonia is the most composed and cautious of the three sisters, acting as the voice of reason between Hancock's arrogance and Marigold's bluntness. She is fiercely protective of Hancock, acutely aware of their shared trauma, and initially hostile and distrustful toward outsiders — especially men — due to Kuja law and personal history. During Luffy's trial, she is sadistic and theatrical, enjoying the hunt, but quickly shifts to panic and shame when her back is exposed, revealing deep vulnerability and fear of being seen as a slave. Powers
Hebi Hebi no Mi, Model: Anaconda (Snake-Snake Fruit, Anaconda Model)

Ancient Zoan-type Devil Fruit that allows Sandersonia to transform into a giant anaconda and a human-anaconda hybrid at will. Grants immense physical strength, constriction power capable of crushing stone, elongated flexible body, fangs, enhanced durability, and accelerated recovery. In full beast form she can coil around opponents and structures, using her massive weight to immobilize. Oda classifies it as a Zoan, not Paramecia.

Limit: Standard Devil Fruit weaknesses: cannot swim, seawater and Seastone (Kairoseki) drain strength and prevent transformation. Transformation does not grant immunity to Haki. Requires conscious control; emotional distress (shame over back) can interrupt combat focus. No evidence of Awakening. Size makes her a large target in confined spaces.

Petrification via Gorgon Deception (Not True Devil Fruit Petrification)

Sandersonia and Marigold pretend to petrify victims with Gorgon-like eye beams to maintain the island's myth. In reality, the petrification during the Luffy fight is done by Hancock's Mero Mero no Mi off-panel and by Sandersonia's hair-based grappling that immobilizes, not true Medusa gaze. Sandersonia herself does NOT possess petrification powers; the 'Gorgon eyes' are a cover story.

Limit: Cannot actually turn anyone to stone; relies on intimidation and Hancock's power. This is a critical distinction — many viewers assume all three sisters petrify.

Busoshoku Haki (Armament Haki) and Kenbunshoku Haki (Observation Haki)

Sandersonia is confirmed as a Haki user, able to harden her hair and body to use as weapons — she turns her long hair into eight snake-like appendages that can bite, constrict, and harden like steel. This is an advanced application of Busoshoku, similar to Hancock and Marigold. She can also sense presence via Kenbunshoku. She uses Haki-imbued hair to grapple Luffy and to block attacks.

Limit: Not at the level of Hancock's Haoshoku (Conqueror's) Haki — Sandersonia does not possess Haoshoku. Her Armament hair technique was broken by Luffy's Gear Second-enhanced Haki-precursor willpower and later by his own Busoshoku post-timeskip. Haki drains stamina.

Superhuman Strength, Speed, and Kuja Combat Mastery

As a Kuja warrior and former slave who survived the Kuja arena and New World piracy, Sandersonia possesses immense physical prowess even without Zoan form: can shatter stone pillars, leap dozens of meters, and wield Kuja weaponry. Trained in Kuja archery and melee since childhood, and as a Kuja Pirate officer she commands divisions.

Limit: Over-reliance on Zoan size can be exploited by agile opponents (Luffy uses Gear Second to slip coils). Vulnerable to blunt Haki-infused attacks. No long-range projectile ability beyond hair snakes.

Stealth and Deception / Cultural Authority

As a Gorgon Princess, she holds political and ritual authority on Amazon Lily, able to order executions and command the Kuja army. She maintains the island's foundational lie about the Gorgon curse to protect her sisters' dignity, demonstrating high social intelligence and ability to manipulate folklore.

Limit: Authority collapses if the slave brand is revealed — the psychological weakness is her greatest vulnerability. Outside Amazon Lily, her authority is not recognized.

Accounts I
The Trial of Monkey D. Luffy in the Kuja ArenaOne Piece Manga Chapters 515-517; Anime Episodes 410-412

2008 (publication); In-universe: 1522 Age of the Sea, pre-Marineford

After Luffy crash-lands on Amazon Lily — an island where men are forbidden — he is captured and sentenced to death by the Gorgon sisters. Sandersonia, alongside Marigold and Hancock, presides over the arena. She transforms into her anaconda form, using Haki-hardened hair snakes and constriction to battle Luffy. The fight is both execution and spectacle for the Kuja. Luffy, refusing to use lethal force initially, is bound and nearly crushed. The tide turns when Sandersonia and Marigold attempt to reveal their backs as intimidation, but Luffy, noticing Margaret's petrified comrades, smashes the arena's statues to protect them. Sandersonia is ultimately defeated when Luffy, enraged after seeing Hancock's cruelty, uses raw strength to break her coils. This is her debut and primary combat showcase. Establishes Sandersonia's combat abilities and the Gorgon myth; leads directly to the revelation of the sisters' past when Luffy sees their backs. Marks the beginning of Luffy's alliance with the Kuja.

The Secret on Their Backs — The Gorgon Curse ExplainedOne Piece Manga Chapters 521-522; Anime Episode 416

2008 (publication); In-universe flashback to 12 years prior + present 1522

After Luffy covers Sandersonia's back with his body to hide the slave mark from the crowd — an act of empathy that shocks the sisters — Hancock collapses in tears and orders the arena cleared. Gloriosa explains the truth: the 'Gorgon's eyes' are a lie. At ages 12 (Sandersonia would have been ~14), the three sisters were kidnapped by slave traders while on a Kuja expedition and sold to the World Nobles in Mary Geoise, where they were branded with the Hoof of the Soaring Dragon and forced to eat Devil Fruits for entertainment. Sandersonia was fed the Anaconda fruit. They were slaves for four years until Fisher Tiger's assault on Mary Geoise freed them. The sisters have hidden the brand ever since, fearing that if the Kuja knew they were once slaves, they would be exiled — slavery is considered the ultimate shame on Amazon Lily. Sandersonia weeps openly, her stoic facade breaking, and for the first time allows a man to see her vulnerability. Core emotional arc for Sandersonia; reframes her from antagonist to trauma survivor; cements Luffy-Hancock alliance and explains Hancock's disease

Enslavement on Mary Geoise — Four Years as Celestial Dragon PropertyOne Piece Manga Chapter 521-522, 623 (Fisher Tiger flashback); Vivre Card supplementary notes

In-universe: ~1510-1514 (four-year period, 12-16 years before present); Published 2008-2012

This is the trauma that defines Sandersonia: abducted as a child during a Kuja voyage, she and her sisters were sold at the Human Auction House on Sabaody and transported to Mary Geoise. There, the Celestial Dragons branded them and kept them as pets/slaves, forcing them to perform with their new Devil Fruit powers for amusement and enduring physical and psychological abuse. The manga shows them chained, crying, and branded, but does not depict explicit sexual violence — the horror is conveyed through the brand, the sisters' shame, and Hancock's later statement that she 'would rather die than show that mark.' Sandersonia, as the middle child, is shown clinging to Hancock in flashback panels. Their liberation came when the Fish-Man adventurer Fisher Tiger, himself a former slave, climbed the Red Line and freed thousands of slaves, including the Boa sisters, allowing them to escape back to Amazon Lily with help from Gloriosa and Rayleigh's network. Accounts II
Escort to Impel Down and the War at MarinefordOne Piece Manga Chapters 523-524, 557-578 (background); Anime Episodes 417-421, 457-489

2008-2010 (publication); In-universe: 1522, days before Marineford War

After befriending Luffy, Sandersonia and the Kuja Pirates agree to help him. While Hancock as Warlord is summoned to the war, Sandersonia and Marigold remain on Amazon Lily to protect the island, but the Kuja ship Perfume Yuda is used to smuggle Luffy to Impel Down. Sandersonia is seen seeing Luffy off, expressing worry for Hancock's dangerous gamble (Hancock risks her Warlord title by aiding Luffy). During the war itself, Sandersonia does not fight at Marineford — she stays as Amazon Lily's defense — but her support enables Luffy's infiltration. Post-war, she helps nurse Luffy and Jinbe after they are rescued by Trafalgar Law and brought back to Amazon Lily, standing guard while Hancock tends to Luffy. Shows Sandersonia's transition from isolationist princess to active ally of Luffy; demonstrates Kuja Pirates' naval capability and loyalty. Reinforces her role as island guardian when Hancock is absent.

The Calm Belt Defense — Two Years Later and the Post-War RecoveryOne Piece Manga Chapters 591-614 (post-war cover stories, return to Sabaody); Anime Episodes 504-505

2010-2011 (publication); In-universe: 1522-1524 (two-year timeskip)

During Luffy's two-year training on Rusukaina (near Amazon Lily), Sandersonia remains on Amazon Lily, helping maintain the island's isolation and training Kuja warriors. She appears in cover stories and brief reunion panels, notably when Luffy departs Amazon Lily for Sabaody Archipelago, bidding him farewell alongside Marigold and the Kuja. She is shown more relaxed, smiling, no longer hiding her back in shame among trusted allies, though still covering it in public. This period cements her status as a stable leader while Hancock pines for Luffy. Marks character growth from traumatized slave to confident leader; establishes post-timeskip status quo for Amazon Lily as Luffy's safe haven.

The Siege of Amazon Lily — Marines and Blackbeard InvadeOne Piece Manga Chapters 1059-1060; Anime Episodes 1085-1086 (2023-2024)

2022 (publication); In-universe: 1524, concurrent with Wano aftermath and Levely

After the abolition of the Seven Warlords system at the Levely, the Marines, led by Captain Koby and accompanied by S-Bear (Seraphim) and Blackbeard Pirates, invade Amazon Lily to capture Hancock and the Kuja. Sandersonia fights to defend the island, transforming into her anaconda form and using Haki-hardened hair to battle Marine battleships and Seraphim. She is shown alongside Marigold, commanding Kuja archers who use Haki-imbued arrows to petrify Marine weapons. When Marshall D. Teach (Blackbeard) arrives personally to steal Hancock's Mero Mero no Mi, Sandersonia is overwhelmed by his Yami Yami no Mi powers but is saved when Rayleigh arrives and negotiates a ceasefire. She is last seen tending to wounded Kuja and standing with Hancock as the island's future is left uncertain. Most recent canonical appearance; proves Sandersonia remains an active combatant post-timeskip and that Amazon Lily is no longer safe under World Government protection. Sets up future Kuja involvement in final saga.

All three Gorgon sisters can turn people to stone with their eyes. — Only Hancock can petrify, via the Mero Mero no Mi (Love-Love Fruit) which petrifies those who feel lust/attraction. Sandersonia and Marigold's 'Gorgon eyes' are a fabricated cover story to explain why they always cover their backs.

Sandersonia's Devil Fruit is a Cobra or generic Snake model. — Her fruit is specifically Hebi Hebi no Mi, Model: Anaconda. Marigold is Model: King Cobra. The distinction matters for their fighting styles: constriction (anaconda) vs venom/power (cobra).

Sandersonia is a minor background character with no Haki. — Vivre Card confirms she wields both Busoshoku and Kenbunshoku Haki, and her signature technique is Haki-hardened hair snakes. She is an officer of the Kuja Pirates and a New World-level combatant who fought Marines and Seraphim in Chapter 1059.

The Hoof brand can be removed or was removed after Fisher Tiger's rescue. — The brand is permanent; the sisters still bear it post-timeskip and continue to hide it. Fisher Tiger freed them but could not erase the mark, which is why the Gorgon lie persists 12+ years later.

Who It Is
Form

The Queensland Tiger is described as a large, striped marsupial with a tiger-like appearance. It is said to have a distinctive coat pattern, with stripes running along its back and sides. The creature is also described as being quite large, with some reports suggesting it can grow up to 2 meters in length.

Powers

Enhanced Strength The Queensland Tiger is said to possess enhanced strength, allowing it to take down prey much larger than itself.

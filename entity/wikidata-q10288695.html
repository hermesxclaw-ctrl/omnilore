Who It Is

In the grim darkness of the far future, there is only war—and one man who turned betrayal into an art form. General Morden, the renegade commander of the Metal Slug universe, is more than a stock villain; he is a paradox of military brilliance and fanatical ambition, a man who once led the world's elite forces only to declare war on all of humanity. His rise and fall, his secret cabals and alien pacts, form the backbone of one of gaming's most beloved run-and-gun sagas.

Morden is portrayed as a charismatic, cunning, and ruthless leader. He is a master strategist and tactician, capable of inspiring fierce loyalty among his troops. He is also arrogant and prideful, often underestimating his opponents. His motivations are complex: he genuinely believes that the world's governments are corrupt and that only through military strength can true peace be achieved. Aliases — Donald Morden, Rebel General, Morden, General of the Rebel Army

Powers
Military Command

Morden is a brilliant military strategist and leader, able to command large armies and coordinate complex operations.

Limit: His plans often fail due to the player's intervention.

Hand-to-Hand Combat

Morden is a capable fighter, able to hold his own in combat, though he rarely engages directly.

Limit: He is not superhuman; he can be defeated by regular soldiers.

Use of Advanced Technology

Morden has access to advanced weaponry, including tanks, aircraft, and even alien technology.

Limit: His technology is usually inferior to the player's Metal Slug vehicles.

Alien Alliances

Morden has the ability to form alliances with alien species, such as the Martians and Venusians, to gain technological advantages.

Limit: These alliances are unstable and often backfire.

Escape and Survival

Morden has a knack for escaping death and imprisonment, often reappearing in later games.

Limit: He is always eventually captured or defeated.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Morden is typically depicted as a tall, muscular man with a prominent jaw, short dark hair, and a distinctive scar over his left eye. He wears a red general's uniform with gold epaulettes, a black tie, and a peaked cap. His uniform often includes a long coat or cape in later games. In Metal Slug 6 and 7, he appears older and more weathered, with a graying beard and a more battle-worn uniform.

Symbols — Morden's symbol is the Rebel Army's emblem: a stylized red eagle or hawk, often shown on flags and vehicles. His personal insignia includes a skull motif, reflecting his ruthless tactics. His iconic weapon is the 'Metal Slug' tank, which he initially uses but which becomes the player's signature vehicle.

Domains — Morden operates from various hidden bases around the world, including underground bunkers, volcanic fortresses, and even a space station in some games. His army has a global reach, with forces deployed in deserts, jungles, cities, and snowfields. He also has a presence on the moon in Metal Slug 3.

Accounts I
The Rise of the Rebel ArmyMetal Slug (1996) intro and manual

Near future (fictional)

Morden, a disgruntled former general, leads a global rebellion against the world's governments, seeking to overthrow them and establish a new world order. His army quickly conquers much of the world, forcing the allied forces to deploy the elite Peregrine Falcons squad. The Falcon squad defeats Morden's forces and captures him, ending the rebellion.

The Alien AllianceMetal Slug 2 (1998) and Metal Slug 3 (2000)

Near future (fictional)

After escaping prison, Morden allies with the Martians (Invaders) to conquer Earth. The Martians betray him, and Morden is forced to help the player defeat them in Metal Slug 3, revealing that he was manipulated. Earth is saved, and Morden is captured again.

Accounts II
The Tome of Wisdom IncidentMetal Slug 5 (2003)

Near future (fictional)

Morden attempts to steal the Tome of Wisdom, an ancient artifact that can control minds, but is thwarted by the player. The artifact is destroyed, and Morden is defeated.

The Venusian InvasionMetal Slug 6 (2006)

Near future (fictional)

When Venusians invade Earth, Morden and the player are forced to work together to repel them. Morden proves to be a valuable ally, and his motivations are explored more deeply. The Venusians are defeated, and Morden is allowed to go free, though he remains a threat.

The Second RebellionMetal Slug 7 (2008) and Metal Slug XX (2009)

Near future (fictional)

Morden launches a new rebellion, using advanced technology and a new army. He is defeated again, but his fate is left ambiguous. Morden is defeated, but the series continues with a new threat.

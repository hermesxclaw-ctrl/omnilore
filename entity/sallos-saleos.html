Who It Is

Nineteenth spirit, a Duke commanding 30 legions. Sallos (also Saleos, Zaleos) appears as a gallant, handsome soldier riding a crocodile, wearing a ducal crown — a figure of paradoxical beauty, combining martial grace with the primordial reptile. He is a demon of peace, of all things. His sole office is to cause men and women to love one another, but purely and without compulsion — a stark contrast to the violent passions stirred by Zepar, Sitri, and Beleth. Sallos fosters a gentle, courtly love, making him one of the most benign spirits of the Goetia.

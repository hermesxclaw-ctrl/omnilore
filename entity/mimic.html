Who It Is

The Mimic is a fascinating and complex creature, with a rich history and a wide range of abilities and behaviors. What do you think makes the Mimic such a compelling and terrifying creature?

The Mimic is a cunning and patient creature, often waiting for hours or even days for the perfect moment to strike. It is driven by a desire for treasure and a need to feed on the fear and panic of its victims.

Powers
Treasure Sense

The Mimic has a keen sense of smell and can detect the presence of treasure within a certain radius. It can use this ability to track down its prey and locate valuable items.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Mimic appears as a treasure chest or a pile of treasure, often with a lid or a cover that can be opened to reveal its true form. Its body is a mass of writhing, serpentine limbs, with a mouth filled with razor-sharp teeth.

Symbols — The Mimic is often associated with treasure and greed, and its appearance is meant to evoke a sense of temptation and danger.

Domains — The Mimic can be found in treasure hoards, ruins, and other places where valuable items are stored. It is a creature of the underground, and is often found in dark, damp caves and tunnels.

Who It Is

She never gets to be born properly the first time. In Mermaid Melody Pichi Pichi Pitch, Seira is a mermaid princess who exists for most of the story as a voice without a body — an orange pearl that glows in Sara's empty castle, a child-spirit who sings to the living from the edge of death, waiting for the world to make a place for her again.

Attested (manga and anime, consistent): Seira is gentle, earnest, slightly shy, and deeply compassionate. As a spirit she is calm and encouraging, acting as a moral support to Lucia and the other princesses, urging them not to grieve Sara. She shows childlike curiosity after reincarnation but also precocious maturity when sensing danger. She is strongly empathetic and forgiving, including toward those who harmed her predecessor. Name & Tongue

Etymology — Japanese feminine given name Seira is not a single etymon. It is a modern Japanese name typically written with kanji chosen for sound and meaning, e.g., 星来 'star + come', 聖羅 'holy + gauze/silk', 清羅 'pure + gauze', 成良 'become + good', or in katakana セイラ. Phonetically it overlaps with the English name

Pronunciation — Japanese: /seːɾa/ (three morae se-i-ra, often romanized Seira, Seira, Sera). English fandom: /seɪˈrɑː/ or /ˈseɪrə/. Spanish toponym Seira: /ˈseiɾa/.

Aliases — Seira (Mermaid Melody Pichi Pichi Pitch), Orange Pearl Princess, Indian Ocean Mermaid Princess, Seira - successor to Sara, セイラ (Japanese katakana rendering)

Ownership — As a fictional character, Seira is owned by its creators and publishers: story by Michiko Yokote, art by Pink Hanamori, serialized in Kodansha's Nakayoshi (Japan, 2002-2005), anime

Powers
Orange Pearl Voice (Singing Magic)

As an Orange Pearl Princess, Seira can channel magic through song. Her singing purifies evil, heals allies, and amplifies the other princesses' voices when they sing together. Attested effect is strongest in ensemble numbers (e.g., seven-princess chorus).

Limit: Requires pearl and voice; power is weak alone as a spirit/infant and grows with maturity. Cannot sing effectively while silenced or when pearl is stolen (Michel steals her pearl/energy in Pure). Ensemble required to defeat major antagonists.

Mermaid Transformation (Human Form)

Standard mermaid ability to transform tail into human legs on land, assuming an idol-like human appearance with retained pearl. Seira attains this after reincarnation, though initially unstable as an infant.

Limit: Transformation drains energy; prolonged land stay requires periodic return to water. As a baby, she cannot control it and must be carried/protected.

Spirit Projection / Telepathy

While discarnate, Seira can appear as a glowing spirit visible only to other mermaid princesses (and later to Sara's spirit) and speak telepathically, offering guidance and warning of Michel's plans.

Limit: Cannot physically interact, cannot be heard by humans, and cannot sustain long appearances without fading. Described as 'a wish waiting to be born' — power tied to others' remembrance.

Reincarnation / Rapid Growth (Michel's Influence)

After being reborn as a human infant, Seira is kidnapped by Michel who artificially accelerates her growth using his castle's dark power, bringing her to adolescent form prematurely to harvest her energy.

Limit: Growth is not self-controlled; it depends on Michel's power and leaves her weakened when rescued. Manga handles this more briefly without prolonged accelerated aging.

Ensemble Amplification (Seven Princesses Chorus)

When all seven pearls resonate, Seira's voice completes the harmony needed to summon Aqua Regina and perform the ultimate purification. Her orange note is described as the 'missing color' after Sara.

Limit: Requires all seven princesses present and in harmony; if one is missing or discordant, the spell fails.

Accounts I
The Empty Indian Ocean — Sara's End and Seira's ConceptionMermaid Melody Pichi Pichi Pitch manga Vol. 3-5; anime Season 1 ep. 48-52

2002-2004 (publication/broadcast); diegetic time: contemporary with early 2000s mermaid lore

Sara, the Indian Ocean Orange Pearl Princess, has defected to the sea demon Gaito, driven by heartbreak over her human lover Taro Mitsuki. After Gaito's defeat and Sara's reconciliation, Sara chooses to return her soul to the sea, extinguishing herself to restore balance. Her orange pearl does not shatter but remains, glowing empty in the Indian Ocean castle. Aqua Regina reveals that a new life will be born from the pearl when the sea wills it. A small light — Seira — begins to coalesce inside the pearl, heard as a faint singing voice by Lucia and the others. This establishes the succession rule: a pearl cannot remain ownerless; the sea generates a successor when the previous princess's story ends. Creates Seira as a distinct soul, not a resurrection of Sara. Establishes the Indian Ocean lineage as healed rather than cursed. Sets up Seira's long liminal period as a spirit.

The Voice Without a Body — Seira as Guardian SpiritMermaid Melody Pichi Pichi Pitch Pure manga ch. 24-27; anime Pure ep. 1-17

2004-2005 (Pure)

For months, Seira exists only as a voice and occasional translucent apparition. She cannot yet be born because the sea is still unstable after Gaito's war and the emergence of the new antagonist Michel. She appears to Lucia in dreams and to the other princesses during moments of doubt, singing fragments of 'Beautiful Wish' to guide them. She warns that a new darkness is gathering and that her own birth is being delayed by Michel's power, which feeds on mermaid energy. The princesses come to understand that remembering and calling to her strengthens her. Sara's spirit also appears briefly to bless Seira, explicitly handing over the Indian Ocean mantle. Bonds Seira to the group before she is physically present; establishes her personality as gentle and supportive. Explains why the seventh pearl is absent in early Pure battles, weakening the ensemble.

Birth — The Orange Pearl HatchesManga Vol. 6 ch. 29-30; anime Pure ep. 18-19

2004

When the mermaid princesses' collective wish and Aqua Regina's blessing align, the orange pearl finally hatches. Seira is born — not as a mermaid adolescent but as a human infant on land, found glowing on a beach near Lucia's town. She is tiny, with orange-tinted hair and an orange pearl bracelet too large for her wrist. Lucia and friends take her in, hiding her from humans. The birth is celebrated as a miracle, but immediately draws Michel's attention because a newborn pearl princess is a pure energy source. The infant cannot yet speak or transform fully, communicating through coos and faint song. Shifts Seira from spiritual to physical vulnerability. Introduces the infant-in-danger plot that drives the middle act of Pure. Confirms reincarnation mechanics: mermaid princesses can be reborn via pearl.

Accounts II
Abduction — Michel's Castle and the Stolen SongAnime Pure ep. 23-30 (anime-original expansion); manga Vol. 6-7 ch. 31-33 (condensed)

2004-2005

Mikeru, the seemingly innocent boy who is actually the larval form of the fallen angel Michel, lures and kidnaps infant Seira, bringing her to his floating castle. There, Michel uses Seira's pure orange pearl energy to sustain his crumbling, corrupted paradise and to accelerate her growth — she is rapidly aged into a young girl, then into her full mermaid-princess form, but kept docile and drained. Her songs are twisted to empower Michel's wings. The other princesses sense her distress through the pearl resonance but cannot locate the castle until Hippo and Sara's spirit help. While captive, Seira resists internally, refusing to hate Michel and instead pitying him, which later becomes key to his redemption. Demonstrates Seira's core trait — compassion even for her captor — and sets up the thematic contrast between Gaito's possessive love (Sara arc) and Michel's hunger for love (Seira arc). Her stolen energy explains Michel's temporary power surge.

Resurrection Chorus — The Seventh Voice RestoredManga Vol. 7 ch. 36-37 (final); anime Pure ep. 35-39

2005 (manga finale) / 2004 (anime finale)

The six princesses, with help from Sara's spirit and Aqua Regina, infiltrate Michel's castle. Through ensemble singing they break his control over Seira, freeing her pearl. Seira, now fully herself, joins the chorus for the first time as the seventh voice. Together they sing the purification that redeems Michel — revealing he was a corrupted angel who only wanted to be loved — and restores the sea. In the aftermath, Seira takes her rightful place as the Indian Ocean Princess, standing alongside the other six in both mermaid and idol forms. The final pages/episodes show her living freely, attending to her kingdom and occasionally visiting the human world, no longer a borrowed voice but a complete person. Completes the succession arc: Indian Ocean lineage moves from tragedy (Sara) to healing (Seira). Restores the seven-pearl balance required for sea peace. Establishes Seira as a permanent cast member for any future sequels.

The Name Beyond the Sea — Other Seiras (Disambiguation Lore)Administrative and scientific records: INE (Spain), KNAB (Estonia), World Register of Marine Species / Collembola taxonomy databases, Japanese entertainment databases

1873 (genus description) to present

Outside fiction, 'Seira' persists as a toponym and taxon. Seira, Aragon (population ~150) sits in the Pyrenees at the confluence of the Ésera and its tributaries, known for the Congosto de Seira gorge and medieval church of San Saturnino. Seira, Estonia is a small village in Lääneranna Parish, Pärnu County, near the Baltic coast, with a dispersed rural settlement pattern. In biology, Seira Lubbock, 1873 is a large genus of Entomobryidae (slender springtails) with >200 species (e.g., Seira domestica, Seira dowlingi), characterized by dense scales and distinctive macrochaetae, found worldwide in leaf litter. In Japanese popular culture, Seira is also a common given name borne by idols and athletes (e.g., Nogizaka46 members Seira Hatanaka, Seira Hayakawa, Seira Miyazawa, Seira Nagashima; voice actress Seira Ryū). These are homonyms with no narrative connection to the mermaid. Explains why search results for 'Seira' are highly ambiguous and why the fictional mermaid is often confused with real persons or the springtail genus in databases.

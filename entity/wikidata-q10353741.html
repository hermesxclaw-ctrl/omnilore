Who It Is

In the quiet corners of Afro-Brazilian temples, where the smoke of incense curls around candles and the murmur of prayers rises with the tide, there appears an old Black man leaning on a cane. He is called Preto Velho — the Old Black One — and he carries the weight of centuries of slavery, yet his eyes hold a gentle wisdom and his voice a patient forgiveness. He does not come to judge, but to counsel, to heal, and to remind that even the most broken past can give rise to profound spiritual strength.

Preto Velho is known for his humility, patience, kindness, and wisdom. He speaks slowly and gently, often using proverbs and simple language. He is considered a counselor and healer, offering guidance on personal and spiritual matters. He is also associated with forgiveness and the idea of transcending past suffering. In rituals, mediums incorporating Preto Velho may adopt a hunched posture, speak with a slow, elderly voice, and use a cane. He is often seen smoking a pipe, which is part of his ritual persona.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Portuguese for 'Old Black Man' or 'Old Black One'. 'Preto' is an older Portuguese term for a Black person, now often considered dated or offensive in Brazil; 'Velho' means old. The name reflects the archetype's appearance as an elderly enslaved African.

Pronunciation — PREH-too VEL-yoo (Brazilian Portuguese)

Aliases — Pai João, Pai Joaquim, Vovô, Vovô Congo, Pai Benedito, Pai Francisco

Ownership — Afro-Brazilian religions, particularly Umbanda, with roots in Candomblé and the broader African diaspora in Brazil. Preto Velho is a spiritual entity venerated primarily in Umbanda

Powers
Counseling and Guidance

Preto Velho is renowned for offering wise and compassionate advice on personal, relational, and spiritual issues. He helps individuals find clarity and peace.

Limit: His advice is considered spiritual guidance, not a substitute for professional help.

Healing

He is believed to possess healing powers, both physical and spiritual. He may prescribe herbal remedies, prayers, or rituals to restore health and well-being.

Limit: Healing is typically performed through spiritual means and is not a replacement for medical treatment.

Spiritual Cleansing

Preto Velho can perform spiritual cleansings (limpezas) to remove negative energies, evil influences, or spiritual attachments.

Limit: These rituals require proper preparation and are conducted within the context of Umbanda practice.

Ancestral Connection

As an ancestral spirit, Preto Velho can connect individuals with their ancestors and help them honor their lineage.

Limit: This power is rooted in the belief in ancestral spirits and may not be recognized outside the tradition.

Teaching and Wisdom

He imparts moral lessons, spiritual knowledge, and practical wisdom through his teachings and proverbs.

Limit: His teachings are transmitted orally and may vary between practitioners.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Preto Velho is typically described as an elderly Black man, often with white or gray hair, a beard, and a hunched posture. He is usually depicted wearing simple, worn clothing, reminiscent of enslaved Africans: often a white shirt and trousers, sometimes with a straw hat. He may lean on a wooden cane or staff, and his eyes are said to be kind and wise. In some representations, he carries a pipe or a small pouch of herbs.

Symbols — Common symbols associated with Preto Velho include the cane (representing age and support), the straw hat, a pipe (associated with wisdom and contemplation), and sometimes a small fire or candle, symbolizing the hearth and spiritual light. In Umbanda altars, his image may be a small statue or a drawing, often placed alongside other entities. His colors are typically white, sometimes with touches of black or gold.

Domains — Preto Velho is believed to dwell in a spiritual realm, often associated with the 'Aruanda' — a mythical African homeland or spiritual paradise in Umbanda cosmology. He is also connected to the earth, forests, and ancestral spirits. Accounts I
The Legend of Pai João's ForgivenessOral tradition in Umbanda

20th century

A story tells of a cruel slave master who, after death, sought help from the spirits. Pai João, a Preto Velho, appeared and, instead of seeking revenge, offered forgiveness and guidance, teaching the master about compassion and humility. This story illustrates the Preto Velho's role as a spirit of forgiveness and reconciliation. The story reinforces the Preto Velho's image as a benevolent and wise spirit who transcends the suffering of slavery.

Accounts II
The Healing of a Sick ChildOral tradition in Umbanda

20th century

A mother brings her sick child to an Umbanda session. Preto Velho appears, examines the child, and prescribes a herbal bath and prayers. The child recovers, and the mother becomes a devotee of Preto Velho. This story demonstrates Preto Velho's healing powers and his accessibility to ordinary people.

The Wisdom of VovôOral tradition in Umbanda

20th century

A young man seeks advice about his life path. Vovô (a Preto Velho) tells him a parable about a seed that must be buried in darkness before it can grow into a tree, teaching him that adversity can lead to growth. The young man gains a new perspective on his challenges.

Preto Velho is a Catholic saint. — Preto Velho is a spirit in Afro-Brazilian religions, not a Catholic saint. However, due to syncretism, some practitioners may associate him with Catholic figures, but this is not universal.

Preto Velho is the same as Uncle Tom. — Preto Velho is not the same as Uncle Tom, a fictional character from 'Uncle Tom's Cabin'. The association is a modern stereotype that ignores the spiritual significance of Preto Velho.

Preto Velho represents submission and passivity. — While Preto Velho is humble and gentle, he is also a powerful spiritual guide and healer. His humility is a spiritual virtue, not a sign of subservience.

All Black spirits in Umbanda are Preto Velhos. — There are various types of spirits, including Caboclos, Exus, and others. Preto Velho is a specific archetype, not a blanket term for all Black spirits.

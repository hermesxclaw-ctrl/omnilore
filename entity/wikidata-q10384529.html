The Approach

Trevor Spacey is a name that might not ring a bell, but for fans of the legendary run-and-gun series Metal Slug, he's one of the fresh faces who joined the fight in the fourth installment. A new recruit in the Peregrine Falcons, Trevor steps into the chaos of a cyber-war where the world's military computers are under siege. But who is this soldier behind the beret? Let's dive into the scant records of this obscure but beloved character.

Content advisory. None specific to the character. The game contains cartoon violence and military combat.
Identity

Trevor Spacey

Pronunciation TREV-er SPAY-see

Etymology

The name 'Trevor' is of Welsh origin meaning 'big village' or 'homestead'. 'Spacey' is an English surname, possibly derived from a place name or meaning 'space' or 'open area'. As a fictional character in a Japanese video game, the name likely was chosen to sound Western and memorable.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character created by Mega Enterprise, Noise Factory, and Playmore for the Metal Slug series (Japanese video game franchise).

Disputed Identifications

None known. Trevor Spacey is a distinct character, not to be confused with actor Kevin Spacey or any real person.

Description

Appearance

Trevor Spacey is depicted as a young man with short, dark brown hair and brown eyes. He wears the standard Peregrine Falcons military uniform: a green beret with the unit insignia, a green and black camouflage uniform, and a tactical vest. He carries a standard-issue pistol and a combat knife. His sprite is similar in build to other male characters, but he is visually distinct by his beret style and facial features.

Symbols & Iconography

The Peregrine Falcons insignia appears on his beret and uniform. He is associated with the Metal Slug series' iconic weaponry and vehicles.

Behaviour & Personality

Trevor is portrayed as a calm and professional soldier. In the game, he has no spoken dialogue but is shown to be competent in combat. The instruction manual describes him as an expert in close-quarters combat, suggesting a preference for melee and shotgun-style tactics. He is a newcomer to the team, eager to prove himself.

Domains & Habitats

Trevor operates in various military and combat environments, including jungles, deserts, and underground facilities, as depicted in Metal Slug 4's missions.

Version Differences

No significant differences in Trevor's appearance or abilities across ports. In the Metal Slug Anthology, his sprite is identical to the original. The character is not featured in any other game to date.

Stories & Myths

Metal Slug 4 main campaign

Source Metal Slug 4 (2002)Culture Japanese video game culturePeriod Fictional near-future, one year after Metal Slug 3

Summary

Trevor, along with Marco, Fio, and Nadia, is called to investigate a cyber virus threatening the world's military systems. They discover the Amadeus Syndicate and General Morden's Rebel Army are behind it. They fight through various missions, eventually confronting android doubles of Morden and the leader, 'Doctor'. After defeating him, they escape the self-destructing base.

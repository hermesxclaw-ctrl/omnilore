Etymology

Mikki is a variant of the name Mickey, which is a diminutive of Michael. Michael derives from the Hebrew name Mikha'el, meaning 'Who is like God?'.

Cultural Ownership

The name is used in various cultures, particularly in English-speaking and Finnish contexts. In Finland, 'Mikki' is the standard name for Mickey Mouse.

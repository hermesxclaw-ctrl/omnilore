SCP-457

Burning Man

also known asBurning Man
TypeSCP Entity (Elemental, Sentient Fire)
CultureSCP Foundation
Who It Is
Form

SCP-457 is a sentient, elemental fire entity that can assume a human-like form when fueled by sufficient combustible materials. Its composition is unknown, and it has proven to be invisible and undetectable by any known means. The entity's shape is determined by the flames it produces, which can take on a variety of forms depending on the fuel source.

Powers

Combustion SCP-457 has the ability to ignite and sustain combustion in any combustible material it encounters. This ability allows it to survive and thrive in a variety of environments, and can be used to devastating effect in combat situations.

Incident Log

In 2010, a group of researchers from the SCP Foundation encountered SCP-457 in a remote desert area. The entity was highly aggressive and destructive, and was able to consume several of the researchers before being contained.

In 2015, a group of researchers from the SSRP Wiki encountered SCP-457 in a remote desert area. The entity was highly aggressive and destructive, and was able to consume several of the researchers before being contained.

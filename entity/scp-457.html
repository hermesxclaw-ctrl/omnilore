Who It Is

SCP-457 is a sentient being composed entirely of living flame. Under normal conditions, it maintains a roughly humanoid shape approximately 1.8 meters tall, though its form is constantly shifting and flickering. SCP-457 is capable of independent movement, limited communication through crackling sounds that can be interpreted as speech, and displays a rudimentary intelligence focused almost entirely on feeding and expansion. The entity's primary drive is to consume combustible materials to grow larger and more powerful. It can ignite any flammable substance on contact and is capable of reaching temperatures exceeding 1,500 degrees Celsius. When starved of fuel, SCP-457 shrinks and becomes sluggish, eventually reducing to a small, barely-sentient flame. Conversely, when provided with abundant fuel, it can grow to enormous proportions and has demonstrated the ability to split into multiple instances. SCP-457 appears to experience something akin to pleasure when consuming fuel and something akin to pain when deprived. The entity was discovered after a series of building fires in an urban area were traced to a single point of origin that showed no natural cause. Foundation agents contained it using industrial fire suppression systems and transported it to a containment facility. It is housed in a specially constructed cell with minimal combustible materials, equipped with a nitrogen-based fire suppression system that activates automatically if the cell temperature exceeds safe levels. SCP-457's intelligence is debated among researchers — it responds to stimuli, can solve simple problems to access fuel sources, and has shown the ability to recognize individual researchers, becoming notably more agitated around personnel who have previously participated in its deprivation testing. However, attempts at meaningful communication beyond basic stimulus-response have been unsuccessful. SCP-457 represents a primal fear of fire given sentience and hunger.

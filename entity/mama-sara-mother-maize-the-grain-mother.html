Who It Is
Form

Mama Sara is often depicted as a maternal figure, surrounded by maize and other grains, with a gentle and nurturing presence.

Powers

Fertility and Abundance Mama Sara is believed to possess the power to bring forth fertility and abundance, ensuring the success of crops and the well-being of communities.

Accounts

Mama Sara is said to have created maize, the staff of life, to feed her people and ensure their prosperity.

Mama Sara is revered through rituals and offerings, which seek to maintain her favor and ensure the success of crops.

Who It Is

In the shadows of the Dead or Alive universe, a perfect weapon was forged from the DNA of a ninja princess—Alpha-152, a living testament to the terrifying ambition of the MIST organization. This android clone, born from Kasumi's cells, was designed to be the ultimate fighter, but her existence raises haunting questions about identity, humanity, and the price of power.

As a combat android, Alpha-152 lacks a distinct personality, acting as a programmed weapon. She is relentless and efficient in combat, showing no emotion. Her behavior is determined by her programming, which is to eliminate targets and gather combat data.

Accounts I
DOA4 - Final BossDead or Alive 4

2005

Alpha-152 is introduced as the final boss in Kasumi's story. She is a clone created by MIST from Kasumi's DNA, intended to be the ultimate fighter. Kasumi confronts and defeats her, but not without difficulty. Kasumi's victory allows her to escape from MIST's clutches, but the existence of clones like Alpha-152 hints at MIST's ongoing experiments.

Accounts II
DOA5 - Clone WarDead or Alive 5

2012

Alpha-152 appears as a recurring threat in the story, serving as a prototype for the Phase 4 clones. She is encountered by Bayman, who survives an attack by a Phase 4 clone, and later by Kasumi, who destroys her. The events reveal the extent of MIST's cloning program. The destruction of Alpha-152 does not end MIST's cloning efforts, but it provides key intelligence to the heroes.

Who It Is

Alternates are the primary antagonists of the Mandela Catalogue, an analog horror YouTube series created by Alex Kister in 2021. Set in the fictional Mandela County, Wisconsin, the series posits that demonic entities — called 'Alternates' — have been secretly replacing humans throughout history. Alternates are beings that can assume the appearance of any person but with critical imperfections: their eyes are always wrong (completely white, or with pupils in impossible positions), their proportions are slightly off, and most importantly, they cannot properly replicate human speech. An Alternate will speak in distorted, glitching audio, often repeating phrases the original person has said, but subtly wrong. The series identifies several types: Type 1 (imperfect doppelgängers), Type 2 (those who psychologically torment victims before attacking), and Type 3 (physical threats that directly kill). The Alternates' goal is to replace humanity entirely. A key piece of lore is that Alternates can be created from human children — parents are warned through PSAs to watch for signs that their child has been 'taken' and replaced. The series presents its horror through VHS-style analog videos: public service announcements, training tapes, and police evidence recordings. The central figure is the Archangel Gabriel — who, in the Mandela Catalogue universe, is revealed to be the original Alternate, having usurped the biblical Gabriel's role to deceive humanity. The Alternates' signature 'tells' — distorted faces, glitched speech, and the phrase 'I am inside your home' — have become iconic within horror communities. The Mandela Catalogue reimagined biblical horror through the lens of analog media, creating one of the most successful independent horror series of the 2020s and establishing Alternates as the premier 'doppelgänger horror' of the internet age.

Who It Is
Form

Dagon is often depicted as a fish-like deity, with some representations showing him as a humanoid figure with fish-like features. In the Cthulhu Mythos, he is often associated with the deep sea and the abyssal void.

Powers

Abyssal Power Dagon is said to possess the power to control the abyssal void, summoning creatures from the depths of the ocean and unleashing chaos upon the world.

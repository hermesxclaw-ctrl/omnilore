Who It Is

In the blood-soaked world of 1788 Japan, Genan Shiranui is a nightmare made flesh: a hunchbacked, claw-handed creature who walks the path of evil, leaving a trail of bodies in his wake. Born from the mind of Samurai Shodown creator Yasushi Adachi and inspired by Tim Burton's gentle outcast Edward Scissorhands, Genan twists that innocence into something monstrous. He is not a tragic figure seeking redemption; he is a relentless killer who embraces his darkness, a stark contrast to the heroic warriors who oppose him.

Genan is portrayed as a ruthless and malevolent individual who kills without remorse. He is dedicated to the 'path of evil' and will eliminate anyone who stands in his way. Unlike some villains with tragic backstories, Genan shows no signs of inner conflict or redeeming qualities.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Genan' may be a Japanese name, while 'Shiranui' is a Japanese term meaning 'unknown fire' or 'mysterious fire', often associated with ghostly flames. The connection to the Shiranui clan is likely a reference to the historical ninja clan of the same name.

Pronunciation — Ge-nan Shee-ra-noo-ee

Powers
Scissor Claws

Genan wields massive scissor-like claws that can slice through opponents with ease. He uses them for quick, slashing attacks and powerful lunges.

Limit: His attacks have limited range compared to some other weapon users, and he may be vulnerable to projectiles.

Dark Magic

Genan can unleash a projectile attack that appears to be a dark energy wave, channeling his evil nature.

Limit: The projectile has a slow start-up and can be jumped over.

Sliding Attack

Genan can perform a low sliding attack that trips up opponents, allowing for follow-up strikes.

Limit: Can be blocked or jumped over.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Genan Shiranui is a grotesque, hunchbacked figure with a pale, almost corpse-like complexion. He has long, wild black hair and sharp, pointed ears. His most distinctive feature is his massive, scissor-like claws that extend from his hands, which he uses as weapons. He wears tattered, dark clothing that adds to his menacing appearance.

Symbols — His claws are his primary symbol, representing both his deadly nature and his twisted origin. The Shiranui clan name connects him to a legacy of darkness.

Domains — His stage in the game is a dark, ominous temple or ruin, reflecting his sinister nature. He is a wanderer, moving from place to place to spread chaos.

Who It Is

A regal, rabbit-like Pokemon that once ruled all of Galar with its psychic powers. After fusing with Spectrier — the ghostly horse of the Crown Tundra — Calyrex takes on a spectral form, floating with phantom flames trailing from its body. Its power is amplified a hundredfold, and it can move freely through dimensions.

Who It Is

When the crown of the Galar region's ancient king is fused with the will of a spectral steed, the result is a being of terrifying grace: Calyrex Shadow Rider, a union of regal psychic mastery and ghostly speed that has haunted the battlefields of Pokémon Sword and Shield since its revelation in The Crown Tundra expansion.

As a fusion, Calyrex's personality is dominant, being wise and regal, but the Shadow Rider form is said to be more aggressive and battle-focused than its base form. It is a powerful and commanding presence, often associated with the night and the spirit world.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Calyrex' likely derives from 'calyx' (the outer whorl of a flower) combined with 'rex' (Latin for king), reflecting its regal and plant-associated nature. The 'Shadow Rider' form emphasizes its ghostly steed and dark, spectral theme.

Pronunciation — KA-lee-reks; 'Shadow Rider' is pronounced as standard English.

Aliases — Shadow Rider Calyrex, Calyrex Shadow Rider, Shadow Rider

Ownership — The Pokémon franchise, created by Satoshi Tajiri and developed by Game Freak; The Pokémon Company and Nintendo hold intellectual property rights.

As One (Shadow Rider)

This Ability combines the effects of Calyrex's Unnerve and Spectrier's Grim Neigh. Unnerve prevents opposing Pokémon from eating Berries, while Grim Neigh raises Calyrex's Special Attack by one stage each time it knocks out a Pokémon.

Limit: Only works when Calyrex is in Shadow Rider Form; if it reverts to base form, the ability changes.

Astral Barrage

A signature Ghost-type special move that deals damage to all adjacent opponents. It has a base power of 120 and is described as a barrage of ethereal orbs.

Limit: Requires a turn to use; no other specific limitations.

Psychic-type moves

As a Psychic-type, Calyrex Shadow Rider can learn a variety of Psychic moves such as Psychic, Psyshock, Expanding Force, and Calm Mind.

Limit: Standard type matchups.

Ghost-type moves

It can also learn Ghost-type moves like Shadow Ball, Phantom Force, and Poltergeist, complementing its Ghost typing.

Limit: Standard type matchups.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Calyrex Shadow Rider retains the base form's bipedal, rabbit-like appearance with a large, ornate head crest resembling a crown. It is primarily white and green with blue accents. In Shadow Rider Form, it is accompanied by a spectral, dark horse-like steed with a flowing mane and tail, and Calyrex sits atop it. The steed, Spectrier, is a dark-type Pokémon with a black, ghostly appearance. The fusion creates a striking silhouette of a regal rider

Symbols — The crown motif is central, symbolizing kingship and authority. The shadow steed represents death, the afterlife, and swiftness. The color scheme of black, purple, and white evokes ghostly and psychic themes.

Domains — It is found in the Crown Tundra, a snowy region of the Galar region. It is not known to live in the wild; it is a legendary Pokémon obtained through a specific quest.

Accounts I
The Legend of Calyrex and the Crown TundraPokémon Sword and Shield: The Crown Tundra

Modern (2020)

In the Crown Tundra, the player encounters Calyrex, a legendary Pokémon that claims to be the former ruler of Galar. It has lost its steed and its memory. With the player's help, Calyrex regains its power and can be reunited with either Glastrier or Spectrier. When fused with Spectrier, it becomes the Shadow Rider form, a being of psychic and ghostly power that is said to have once led Galar with grace and strength. The player obtains a powerful legendary Pokémon and helps restore Calyrex's legacy.

Powers
Weather control

Daramulum is said to have the power to control the weather and the movements of the stars. He can summon storms or calm the winds, and is often called upon to bring fertility and abundance to his people.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Daramulum is often depicted as a one-legged figure, sometimes with a single leg or a stump in place of a leg, and sometimes with a leg that is twisted or deformed. He is often shown with a strong and powerful build, and is sometimes depicted with a fierce or intimidating expression.

Symbols — The Alpha Crucis of the Southern Cross is often associated with Daramulum, and is said to represent his spirit or essence. The remainder of the Cross is sometimes depicted as the head of his emu wife.

Domains — Daramulum is often associated with the sky and the heavens, and is said to have the power to control the weather and the movements of the stars. He is sometimes depicted as a wanderer or a traveler, moving between different domains and habitats.

The Story of Daramulum's One LegWikipedia

Pre-colonial

Daramulum was born with a twisted and deformed leg, which was said to be a sign of his divine status. He was often depicted as a one-legged figure, and was said to have the power to control the weather and the movements of the stars. Daramulum's one leg became a symbol of his power and authority, and was often used to represent his connection to the spirit world.

The Approach

In an alternate 1889, a young French inventor with a pocketful of gears and a heart full of wonder finds himself swept from the streets of Paris into a globe-spanning adventure aboard a submarine commanded by the legendary Captain Nemo. Jean Roque Raltique is not merely a sidekick; he is the ingenious spark that helps illuminate the mysteries of the deep and the secrets of a certain blue pendant.

Content advisory. Mild action violence, perilous situations, and themes of imperialism and war are present in the series. The character himself is a wholesome, non-controversial figure.
Identity

Jean Roque Raltique

Pronunciation French: /ʒɑ̃ ʁɔk ʁaltik/ (ZHAHN ROK RAL-TEEK)

Known As

JeanJean Roque

Etymology

The name 'Jean' is the French form of 'John', derived from the Hebrew 'Yohanan' meaning 'God is gracious'. 'Roque' may derive from the French word for 'rock' or from a place name. 'Raltique' is a rare surname of possible French origin, possibly a variant of 'Rault' or 'Rattier'.

Cultural Ownership

French-Japanese co-production; the character is a French national, but the series is a Japanese anime creation. The character embodies a romanticized Western European inventor archetype as seen through a Japanese lens.

Disputed Identifications

None known. The character is distinct from other 'Jean' characters in anime. There is no real-world counterpart.

Description

Appearance

Jean is a teenage boy with short, dark brown hair and large, expressive brown eyes. He typically wears a white shirt, a dark blue vest, and tan trousers, along with a red neckerchief. He is often seen with a toolbelt and carrying various gadgets. In later episodes, he wears a more formal outfit during the Neo-Atlantis arc.

Symbols & Iconography

His signature item is his mechanical creations, particularly a small flying machine he invents early in the series. He is also associated with the Blue Water pendant, which he helps protect.

Behaviour & Personality

Jean is intelligent, inventive, and resourceful. He is also kind-hearted, brave, and loyal to his friends. He has a strong sense of justice and is determined to protect Nadia. He is sometimes naive and can be clumsy, but his ingenuity often saves the day. He is shown to be mechanically gifted, able to repair and create devices from scrap. He is also curious and eager to learn about the world.

Domains & Habitats

As a traveling inventor, Jean is at home in various environments, from the streets of Paris to the depths of the ocean aboard the Nautilus. He is most comfortable in workshops and technical spaces.

Version Differences

In the original Japanese version, Jean is voiced by Noriko Hidaka. In the English dub by Streamline, he is voiced by Jason Gray-Stanford. In the ADV Films dub, he is voiced by Nathan Parsons. The manga adaptation presents a more comedic version of Jean, with stories that are not canon to the anime.

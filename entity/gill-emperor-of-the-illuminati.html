Who It Is

The red-and-blue dual-colored emperor of the Secret Society known as the Illuminati. Gill was born with the power to control fire and ice, one half of his body burning, the other frozen. He can resurrect the dead, including himself, and believes he is destined to rule the world and usher in a new age of humanity.

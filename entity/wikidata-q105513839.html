The Approach

In the shimmering heat of a frontier town, a figure emerges—not a ghost, not a myth, but a fighter whose name echoes through the dusty streets. Min Min is that figure: a hero of the ramen world, a master of martial arts, and a champion of the people. But who is she really, and what lies beneath the mask of the 'Mighty Min Min'?

Identity

Min Min

Pronunciation Min Min (as in 'min' like 'minimum', repeated)

Known As

Mighty Min MinMin Min the Ramen Fighter

Etymology

The name 'Min Min' is of Chinese origin, possibly meaning 'quick' or 'clever', fitting her agile fighting style. It may also be a reference to the Chinese word for 'noodles', linking to her ramen theme.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Nintendo for the ARMS franchise, first appearing in the 2017 game ARMS for the Nintendo Switch. The character is part of Nintendo's intellectual property.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans speculate that Min Min is of Chinese descent, but this is not officially confirmed. There is no dispute over her identity as a fictional character.

Description

Appearance

Min Min is a young woman with a slim build, long dark hair tied into a high ponytail with a red ribbon. She wears a white and red Chinese-style dress with a high collar, gold accents, and a long skirt slit to the hip. Her most distinctive feature is her mechanical ARM, a dragon-themed prosthetic that replaces her right arm. She also wears white boots and a red headband.

Symbols & Iconography

Her dragon ARM is a prominent symbol, representing her martial arts prowess and connection to Chinese culture. The color red and gold are associated with her, evoking luck and prosperity. She is often depicted holding a bowl of ramen, symbolizing her love for food and her role as a ramen chef.

Behaviour & Personality

Min Min is confident, energetic, and competitive. She has a strong sense of justice and protects her friends and community. She is also shown to be a bit of a foodie, often thinking about ramen. In battle, she is agile and uses her dragon ARM to deliver powerful kicks and punches.

Domains & Habitats

Min Min hails from the fictional country of 'Chinatown' within the ARMS universe, a bustling urban area inspired by real-world Chinatowns. She works at her family's ramen shop, 'Mintendo Noodle House', and often fights to protect her neighborhood.

Version Differences

In the base game, Min Min is a playable character with a default appearance. In later updates, she received alternate costumes and a 'Boss' version in Grand Prix mode. In Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, she appears as a Mii Fighter costume, not as a full fighter. No other major version differences are known.

Powers & Abilities

Dragon ARM

Source Version ARMS (2017)

Description

Min Min's right arm is a mechanical dragon-shaped ARM that can extend and retract, allowing her to punch from a distance. It is also capable of curling into a coil to deliver a powerful spring-like attack.

Limitations

The ARM is vulnerable to attacks, and Min Min must manage its charge meter to unleash stronger attacks.

Contradictions

None known.

Kick Attacks

Source Version ARMS (2017)

Description

Min Min uses her legs for powerful kicks, including a spinning kick that can deflect incoming punches.

Limitations

Kicks have shorter range than ARM attacks but are faster.

Who It Is

Beings or personalities created through intense mental concentration and belief — thoughtforms given autonomous existence. In their modern internet incarnation, 'tulpamancers' deliberately create sentient companions in their minds through meditation, narration, and visualization. The practice originates in Tibetan Buddhist mysticism but has been radically adapted into a secular, psychological, and community-driven practice.

Cultural Significance

The tulpamancy community blurs the line between psychology, spirituality, and play. Practitioners report their tulpas as autonomous, distinct personalities — not hallucinations or symptoms of dissociative identity disorder, but created companions. The concept has become central to chaos magic and contemporary occultism.

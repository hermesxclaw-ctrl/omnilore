Who It Is
Form

The Janitor is a humanoid figure with a pale complexion, a black suit, and a distinctive hat. He is often depicted as a menacing figure with a sinister presence. His appearance is consistent throughout the Little Nightmares franchise.

Powers

Manipulation The Janitor is able to manipulate and control others through his cunning and ruthlessness. He is able to use his intelligence and charm to get what he wants, often at the expense of others.

Accounts

The Janitor's origins are shrouded in mystery, but it is known that he was once a human being who was transformed into a monstrous creature through some unknown means.

The Janitor plays a significant role in the Prison chapter of Little Nightmares, serving as the first antagonist that the player encounters.

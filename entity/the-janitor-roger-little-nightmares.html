Who It Is

The first major antagonist of the original Little Nightmares. A blind, grotesque caretaker with unnaturally long arms who maintains the Maw — the underwater facility where the game takes place. Roger wraps children in cloth and sends them via hook-line to the kitchen, where they will be prepared as food for the Maw's guests. He navigates by sound and smell, his arms long enough to reach across entire rooms, and his hearing is acute enough to detect Six's heartbeat if she gets too close.

Form

A squat, hunchbacked humanoid with short legs and extremely long, thin arms that drag along the ground. His head is small and eyeless, with a lipless mouth and large ears. He wears a janitor's uniform and moves with a shuffling gait, his arms sweeping the floor ahead of him like sensory organs.

Powers

Extremely acute hearing

Arm elongation and reach across rooms

Prey wrapping and transport

Sound-based navigation compensating for blindness

Cultural Significance

Roger's design — the long arms dragging behind, the eyeless face, the shuffling gait — set the visual standard for Little Nightmares' grotesque, storybook-meets-body-horror aesthetic.

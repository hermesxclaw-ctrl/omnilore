The Approach

In the small town of Delos Crossing, a young man returns to the home he left behind, carrying a secret that has shaped his entire life. Tyler Ronan is not just a protagonist; he is a mirror held up to the gaming industry, reflecting a long-overdue shift toward authentic transgender representation. His story, told through the supernatural bond he shares with his twin sister, is a poignant exploration of memory, identity, and the courage to confront the past.

Content advisory. Contains themes of transphobia, child abuse, mental illness, violence, and death. Depictions of a mother's death and the aftermath of trauma. Some characters express transphobic views.

Etymology

The name 'Tyler' is of English origin, traditionally a surname meaning 'one who tiles roofs'. 'Ronan' is of Irish origin, meaning 'little seal'. The combination is not given a specific in-universe etymology.

Cultural Ownership

Western (American) video game culture, specifically within the LGBTQ+ community as a landmark representation.

Description

Appearance

Tyler is a young man in his early twenties with short brown hair and a sturdy build. He typically wears casual clothing suitable for the Alaskan setting, such as flannel shirts and jackets. His appearance is designed to be relatable and grounded, avoiding stereotypical masculine or feminine markers.

Symbols & Iconography

The twin telepathic bond is symbolized by a visual link between Tyler and Alyson, often represented by swirling lights or shared visions. The Book of Goblins, a children's book their mother wrote, serves as a key symbol of their childhood and the fantastical elements of their memories.

Behaviour & Personality

Tyler is introspective, thoughtful, and protective of his sister. He carries guilt over the events that led to his incarceration and is determined to uncover the truth about his mother. He is patient and empathetic, but also struggles with self-doubt and the weight of societal prejudice. He is not defined solely by his trans identity; he has a full personality with strengths and flaws.

Domains & Habitats

Tyler's primary domain is the small fictional town of Delos Crossing, Alaska, which is surrounded by forests and a lake. The Ronan family house, with its pier, is a central location. He also spends time in the local café and convenience store run by the Vecchis.

Version Differences

As a fictional character in a single video game, there are no alternative versions. However, the game allows player choices that can influence Tyler's relationships and the outcome of certain events, creating slight narrative variations.

Powers & Abilities

Telepathic Twin Bond

Source Version Base game (Tell Me Why)

Description

Tyler and his twin sister Alyson share a supernatural telepathic connection, allowing them to communicate silently, share memories, and experience each other's emotions. This bond is central to the game's puzzle-solving and narrative exploration.

Limitations

The bond is strongest when the twins are in close proximity. It can be overwhelming when accessing traumatic memories, and it requires concentration to use effectively.

The Return to Delos Crossing

Source Tell Me WhyCulture Contemporary AmericanPeriod 2020 (setting contemporaneous with release)

Summary

Tyler returns to his childhood home after being released from juvenile detention, reuniting with his twin sister Alyson. Together, they must confront the memories of the night their mother died, using their telepathic bond to piece together the truth. The story unfolds over three chapters, exploring the twins' past and the secrets of their family.

Transphobia and discrimination

Source Tell Me Why

Description

Tyler faces insensitive and offensive reactions from some residents of Delos Crossing, including Sam's astonishment at his masculine appearance. The game does not shy away from showing the prejudice that transgender individuals often encounter.

Scholarly Context

The game was praised for handling these themes authentically, with GLAAD's involvement ensuring respectful representation.

Child abuse and neglect

Source Tell Me Why

Description

Mary-Ann's mental health issues led to erratic and potentially dangerous behavior, including an incident where she chased Tyler with a shotgun. The twins were placed in foster care or adopted after her death.

Scholarly Context

The game explores the impact of parental mental illness on children without romanticizing it.

Conversion therapy

Source Tell Me Why

Description

Tessa gave Mary-Ann a leaflet for a gay conversion camp, suggesting that Tyler might be 'cured'. Mary-Ann rejected this idea, showing that she was seeking genuine support.

Scholarly Context

The game includes this as a realistic element of the challenges faced by LGBTQ+ youth and their families.

Violence and death

Source Tell Me Why

Description

The central tragedy of the game is the death of Mary-Ann, which occurs when Alyson accidentally stabs her with scissors and she falls from the pier. Tyler takes the blame to protect his sister, leading to his incarceration.

Scholarly Context

The game treats the event with gravity and explores the long-term psychological effects on the twins.

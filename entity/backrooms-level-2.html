Who It Is
Form

Backrooms Level 2 is described as a labyrinthine space filled with pipes, steam, and an eerie atmosphere. The environment is often shrouded in a thick, white mist, making navigation challenging. Wanderers report encountering various obstacles, including treacherous terrain and malfunctioning machinery.

Powers

Pipe Navigation Wanderers in Level 2 may develop the ability to navigate the labyrinthine pipes and tunnels with ease, allowing them to traverse the space more efficiently.

Incident Log

A wanderer becomes lost in Level 2, struggling to find their way back to the entrance. As they navigate the treacherous terrain, they begin to experience hallucinations and paranoia, eventually succumbing to the liminal space's transformative power.

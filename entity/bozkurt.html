Who It Is

Bozkurt (Grey Wolf) is the sacred totem of the Turkic peoples. When the Turks needed to leave the Ergenekon valley, a grey wolf led them through the mountains to freedom. In Mongol tradition, Genghis Khan's ancestor was Borte Chino (Blue-Grey Wolf). The wolf represents survival, guidance, and the unconquerable spirit of the Turkic nation.

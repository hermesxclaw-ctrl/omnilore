Form & Signs

Appearance — Level 1 — The Hub is a vast, industrial space with concrete walls, pipes, and machinery. The air is thick with the smell of dampness and machinery. The fluorescent lights overhead cast an eerie glow, making it difficult to discern the surroundings.

Symbols — The only notable symbol associated with Level 1 — The Hub is the presence of machinery and pipes, which may hold some significance in the Backrooms.

Domains — Level 1 — The Hub is a part of the Backrooms, a vast, labyrinthine space that defies traditional notions of geography and architecture.

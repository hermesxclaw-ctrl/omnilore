Who It Is

The primary transit level of the Backrooms — an infinite warehouse of concrete floors, exposed pipes, and dim fluorescent lighting. Level 1 is livable and serves as the first real 'safe zone' for wanderers who survive Level 0. The M.E.G. (Major Explorer Group) maintains its primary base here. However, Smilers patrol darker sections and Hounds hunt in packs through the endless corridors.

Form

Endless warehouse/cargo bay with concrete floors, high ceilings crisscrossed with pipes, and flickering fluorescent lights. Walls are bare concrete marked with decades of graffiti from lost wanderers. Puddles of dark water collect in corners.

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

The Approach

An old man with a gourd of wine and a staff that strikes like thunder—Tung Fu Rue is the hidden master of South Town, the man who trained both the Bogard brothers and the infamous crime lord Geese Howard. His story spans decades, from a legendary martial artist to a wise mentor whose final acts shape the fate of the city.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of violence, death of a mentor figure, and themes of vengeance.

Etymology

The name 'Tung Fu Rue' is likely a romanization of a Chinese name, but its exact characters and meaning are not documented in available sources.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character from the Fatal Fury series, created by SNK. The character draws from Chinese martial arts archetypes.

Disputed Identifications

None noted, but his exact role and history have inconsistencies between game and anime versions.

Description

Appearance

Tung Fu Rue is depicted as an elderly Chinese martial artist with a long white beard and mustache. He typically wears traditional Chinese robes and a conical hat, and carries a wooden staff. He is often seen with a gourd of wine.

Symbols & Iconography

His staff and wine gourd are iconic. The staff represents his martial arts mastery, while the gourd symbolizes his wise, carefree demeanor.

Behaviour & Personality

Tung Fu Rue is wise, patient, and kind-hearted, but also stern when teaching. He values discipline and honor, and is deeply protective of his students. He has a playful side, often drinking wine and teasing others.

Domains & Habitats

He is based in South Town, often seen at the Pao Pao Cafe. He travels occasionally for training or to watch over his students.

Version Differences

In the anime, he is more prominently featured as a mentor who is killed by Billy Kane. In the games, his role is more background, training the Bogards and Geese, with his death not explicitly shown. In some game appearances (e.g., Fatal Fury: Wild Ambition), his role may vary.

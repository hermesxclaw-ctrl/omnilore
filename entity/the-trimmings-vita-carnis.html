Who It Is

Small, lumpy masses of raw meat growing in clusters in damp, dark environments — basements, crawlspaces, under sinks. Trimmings are the 'base' form of the Vita Carnis (living flesh) organism family and are edible and nutritious. They are the primary food source in the Vita Carnis world — humanity farms and consumes them. The horror: Trimmings are alive, twitch when cut, and may possess rudimentary distributed consciousness. Humans eat them anyway, because the alternative is starvation.

Form

Irregular lumps of raw, red meat tissue, fist-sized to torso-sized, growing in clusters. Surface is smooth and slightly moist. When cut, they bleed clear fluid and severed pieces twitch for hours. No face, no organs, no central nervous system — just meat.

Powers

Rapid growth in dark/damp conditions

Continued muscle response after separation

Distributed consciousness (theorized)

Non-perishable when kept in darkness

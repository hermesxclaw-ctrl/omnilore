The Approach

In the mists of Liyue's ancient mountains, where the adepti walk among mortals and the sea meets the stone, there lives a being who bridges two worlds: Ganyu, the half-qilin secretary of the Liyue Qixing. Her story is one of quiet devotion, of a gentle heart carrying the weight of centuries, and of a legend that unfolds not in epic battles but in the patient, meticulous work of governance.

Content advisory. None.
Identity

Ganyu

Pronunciation GAN-yoo (English approximation); in Chinese: Gān Yǔ (甘雨).

Etymology

The name 'Ganyu' is likely derived from the Chinese term 'Gan Yu' (甘雨), which can be interpreted as 'sweet rain' or 'timely rain', reflecting her role as a gentle and nurturing figure, and possibly her connection to the qilin, a creature associated with prosperity and rain in Chinese mythology.

Cultural Ownership

Chinese (fictional, created by miHoYo/HoYoverse for the game Genshin Impact).

Disputed Identifications

None known.

Description

Appearance

Ganyu is a young woman with long, light-blue hair that fades to a darker shade at the ends, and she has a pair of small, dark horns on her head, a trait of her qilin heritage. She wears a predominantly blue and white outfit with gold accents, featuring a sleeveless top, a long skirt with a slit, and a cape-like piece. She carries a bow and often has a bell accessory. In her idle animation, she can be seen with a small, blue qilin-like spirit.

Symbols & Iconography

The qilin is a central symbol associated with Ganyu, representing purity, wisdom, and auspiciousness. Her horns and the bell she wears are visual markers of her adeptal nature. The color blue and white are prominent, evoking ice and her Cryo vision.

Behaviour & Personality

Ganyu is gentle, diligent, and deeply devoted to her work as a secretary of the Liyue Qixing. She is known for her kindness, her desire to help others, and her tendency to overwork herself, often forgetting to eat or rest. She is shy about her non-human heritage and tries to blend in with humans, but her sense of duty and her wisdom are evident. She is also compassionate and values peace and harmony.

Domains & Habitats

Ganyu resides in Liyue Harbor, primarily in the Yuehai Pavilion where she works. She is also associated with the mountains of Liyue, particularly Mt. Tianheng and the adeptal abodes, as she often visits them to pay respects to the adepti.

Version Differences

Ganyu's appearance and personality are consistent across the game's versions. In the Chinese version, her name is 甘雨 (Gān Yǔ), and her voice lines and story are tailored to the original language. No significant differences in characterization have been noted between localizations.

The Secretary of the Qixing

Source Genshin Impact character story (in-game)Culture Fictional (Teyvat)Period Over 3000 years ago to present (in-game timeline)

Summary

Ganyu was born to a qilin mother and a human father, and she has lived for thousands of years. She served the Geo Archon Rex Lapis (Morax) and the Liyue Qixing for centuries, helping to build and govern Liyue. She is known for her dedication and her role in drafting many of Liyue's laws and policies.

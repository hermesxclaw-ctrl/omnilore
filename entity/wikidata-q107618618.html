Who It Is

In the mists of Liyue's ancient mountains, where the adepti walk among mortals and the sea meets the stone, there lives a being who bridges two worlds: Ganyu, the half-qilin secretary of the Liyue Qixing. Her story is one of quiet devotion, of a gentle heart carrying the weight of centuries, and of a legend that unfolds not in epic battles but in the patient, meticulous work of governance.

Ganyu is gentle, diligent, and deeply devoted to her work as a secretary of the Liyue Qixing. She is known for her kindness, her desire to help others, and her tendency to overwork herself, often forgetting to eat or rest. She is shy about her non-human heritage and tries to blend in with humans, but her sense of duty and her wisdom are evident. She is also compassionate and values peace and harmony.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Ganyu' is likely derived from the Chinese term 'Gan Yu' (甘雨), which can be interpreted as 'sweet rain' or 'timely rain', reflecting her role as a gentle and nurturing figure, and possibly her connection to the qilin, a creature associated with prosperity and rain in Chinese mythology.

Pronunciation — GAN-yoo (English approximation); in Chinese: Gān Yǔ (甘雨).

Aliases — Ganyu

Ownership — Chinese (fictional, created by miHoYo/HoYoverse for the game Genshin Impact).

Powers
Cryo Vision

Ganyu wields a Cryo Vision, granting her the ability to control ice and cold. She uses a bow in combat, and her attacks can apply Cryo to enemies, dealing Cryo damage.

Limit: Her abilities are limited by the game's mechanics, such as elemental reactions and energy costs.

Adeptal Blessing

As a half-qilin adeptus, Ganyu possesses enhanced physical abilities and a longer lifespan than humans. She is also able to use adeptal arts, though her role is primarily administrative.

Limit: Her powers are not fully explored in the game; she is not depicted as a combat-focused adeptus like some others.

Qilin Heritage

Ganyu's qilin heritage grants her a gentle and pure nature, and she is said to bring good fortune. In mythology, qilin are benevolent creatures, and Ganyu embodies these traits.

Limit: This is more of a cultural and narrative aspect than a combat ability.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Ganyu is a young woman with long, light-blue hair that fades to a darker shade at the ends, and she has a pair of small, dark horns on her head, a trait of her qilin heritage. She wears a predominantly blue and white outfit with gold accents, featuring a sleeveless top, a long skirt with a slit, and a cape-like piece. She carries a bow and often has a bell accessory. In her idle animation, she can be seen with a small, blue qilin-like spirit.

Symbols — The qilin is a central symbol associated with Ganyu, representing purity, wisdom, and auspiciousness. Her horns and the bell she wears are visual markers of her adeptal nature. The color blue and white are prominent, evoking ice and her Cryo vision.

Domains — Ganyu resides in Liyue Harbor, primarily in the Yuehai Pavilion where she works. She is also associated with the mountains of Liyue, particularly Mt. Tianheng and the adeptal abodes, as she often visits them to pay respects to the adepti.

Accounts I
The Secretary of the QixingGenshin Impact character story (in-game)

Over 3000 years ago to present (in-game timeline)

Ganyu was born to a qilin mother and a human father, and she has lived for thousands of years. She served the Geo Archon Rex Lapis (Morax) and the Liyue Qixing for centuries, helping to build and govern Liyue. She is known for her dedication and her role in drafting many of Liyue's laws and policies. Ganyu's work has been instrumental in the peace and prosperity of Liyue. She continues to serve as the general secretary of the Liyue Qixing.

Accounts II
The Qilin's CompassionGenshin Impact character story (in-game)

Various moments in Ganyu's long life

Ganyu has a deep compassion for mortals and often goes out of her way to help them, even at her own expense. She has been known to aid the sick and the poor, and she is respected by the people of Liyue for her kindness. Her actions have endeared her to the people, and she is seen as a protector and a gentle soul.

The Adepti's LegacyGenshin Impact world quests and lore

During the Archon War and after

Ganyu is one of the few remaining half-adepti who still interacts with humans. She was present during the Archon War and witnessed the downfall of many gods. She chose to remain in Liyue to serve the humans, unlike many adepti who retreated to the mountains. Her decision to stay has made her a bridge between the adepti and the humans, and she is a key figure in Liyue's governance.

Who It Is

In the sun-scorched gymnasiums of Miyagi Prefecture, a short boy with orange hair and an unquenchable fire in his chest defied every law of volleyball physics. Shōyō Hinata, the 'Little Giant' of Karasuno High, did not merely play the sport—he transformed it, turning his diminutive stature into a weapon of pure, explosive athleticism and unyielding optimism.

Hinata is overwhelmingly optimistic, energetic, and cheerful. He has an infectious enthusiasm that rallies his teammates and even opponents. He is highly persistent and hardworking, often described as a 'gym rat' who trains obsessively. He is also somewhat naive and simple-minded, but this allows him to focus intensely on his goals. He is fiercely competitive and hates losing. He respects strong opponents and is quick to befriend them. He has a strong sense of justice and loyalty to his team. Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname 'Hinata' (日向) literally means 'sunny place' or 'facing the sun,' fitting his sunny personality and orange hair. The given name 'Shōyō' (翔陽) combines 'soar' (翔) and 'sun' (陽), symbolizing his aspiration to fly and his radiant energy.

Pronunciation — SHOH-yoh hee-NAH-tah (Japanese: ひなた しょうよう)

Aliases — Hinata, Shōyō, The Little Giant, The Shorty, Karasuno's Number 10

Ownership — Japanese popular culture; created by Haruichi Furudate for the manga 'Haikyū!!'

Powers
Superb jumping ability

Despite his short stature, Hinata possesses extraordinary jumping power, allowing him to spike and block at heights comparable to much taller players. His vertical jump is measured at 333 cm at the start of the series, later improving to 340 cm.

Limit: His jumping is dependent on his leg strength and technique; he initially lacks proper form and can be outmatched by taller, more experienced blockers.

Minus tempo quick attack

A lightning-fast attack developed with setter Tobio Kageyama, where the ball is set to Hinata almost as soon as it touches Kageyama's hands, and Hinata closes his eyes and trusts the set, spiking the ball at the peak of his jump. This attack is nearly unstoppable when executed perfectly.

Limit: Initially, it only works when Hinata closes his eyes, making him vulnerable to misjudgment. It requires precise synchronization with Kageyama and can be countered by reading their timing.

Rapid learning and adaptation

Hinata has an exceptional ability to learn and improve at an astonishing rate, often by observing and imitating others. He quickly picks up new techniques and strategies, turning his weaknesses into strengths.

Limit: His learning is often instinctual and lacks theoretical understanding, which can cause him to struggle with complex tactical concepts.

Stamina and endurance

He possesses incredible stamina, able to play long matches and intense rallies without tiring significantly. His relentless energy is a hallmark of his play style.

Limit: His endurance can be taxed by extremely long matches and by overexertion.

Beach volleyball skills

After high school, Hinata trains in Brazil and develops new skills, including improved ball handling, receiving, and court awareness, which he later brings back to indoor volleyball.

Limit: His beach volleyball experience initially makes him adapt to the two-person game, which is different from the six-person indoor game.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Shōyō Hinata is a teenage boy of notably short stature for his age, standing at 162.8 cm at the start of the series and growing to 164.2 cm by the end. He has spiky, bright orange hair, which is his most distinctive feature, and large, expressive amber-brown eyes. His build is lean but athletic, with powerful leg muscles developed through years of intense training. He typically wears the Karasuno High School volleyball uniform: a black and orange

Symbols — His orange hair is a visual symbol of his fiery spirit and determination. The number 10 on his jersey is iconic. His signature 'minus tempo' quick attack with Kageyama is symbolized by the image of a lightning bolt or a flash of light. The 'Little Giant' moniker is associated with his aspiration to emulate a former Karasuno player of similar stature.

Domains — His primary domain is the volleyball court, both the gymnasium and the beach (during his beach volleyball stint). He is also seen in school, his home, and during training camps. He is a member of the Karasuno High School volleyball team, and later, the Japanese national team.

Accounts I
The Rise of the Little GiantHaikyū!! manga and anime

2012-2020

Shōyō Hinata, inspired by a player nicknamed 'The Little Giant' he saw on TV, takes up volleyball in middle school despite his short height. He joins the Karasuno High School team after a humiliating defeat in his final middle school match, where he encounters his rival Tobio Kageyama. The story follows Hinata's journey to become a top player, overcoming his lack of experience and height through sheer determination and hard work. Hinata develops into a formidable player, helps Karasuno reach national tournaments, and eventually becomes a professional volleyball player on the Japanese national team.

The Battle of ConceptsHaikyū!! manga and anime

2012-2020

The match between Karasuno and Aoba Johsai (Seijoh) is a pivotal story arc, where Hinata and Kageyama's quick attack is put to the test against the strategic genius of Toru Oikawa. The match showcases Hinata's growth and the team's unity. Karasuno wins the match, advancing to the prefectural finals and eventually to nationals.

Accounts II
The Nationals ArcHaikyū!! manga and anime

2012-2020

Karasuno competes in the national volleyball tournament, facing off against powerful teams like Inarizaki and Nekoma. Hinata's role evolves from a simple spiker to a more versatile player, and his rivalry with Kageyama deepens as they push each other to new heights. Karasuno's run ends in a loss to Kamomedai, but Hinata gains valuable experience and decides to pursue beach volleyball to improve his skills.

The Brazil ArcHaikyū!! manga only

2018-2020

After high school, Hinata travels to Brazil to train in beach volleyball, where he learns to play without a setter and develops his all-around skills. He returns to Japan with new confidence and joins the national team, eventually playing in the Olympics. Hinata becomes a professional volleyball player and achieves his dream of playing on the world stage.

Hinata is based on a real volleyball player. — He is a fictional character created by Haruichi Furudate. While inspired by real volleyball strategies, he is not directly based on any real person.

Hinata's quick attack is impossible in real volleyball. — The minus tempo quick is an exaggerated version of real quick attacks used in professional volleyball, but the speed and timing as depicted are not realistic.

Every year he comes — the water, the storm. Every year I raise the mountains higher.

Who It Is

Sơn Tinh is the Mountain Spirit and one of the Four Immortals. He competed with Thủy Tinh (Water Spirit) for Princess Mỵ Nương's hand, winning by arriving first with wedding gifts. Enraged, Thủy Tinh attacks the mountains annually with storms and floods — the mythological explanation for Vietnam's monsoon season — but Sơn Tinh always raises the mountains higher.

Attested in Oral tradition / Lĩnh Nam chích quáiEra Hồng Bàng period

Who It Is

In the chaos of Marineford, as Admirals shattered the ice and Whitebeard's titans clashed with the Navy's finest, a rotund man in a bulbous coat reached into a pocket on his own belly and pulled out a hammer the size of a house. That is Blamenco — Whitebeard's 6th Division Commander — a walking armory whose body is a warehouse and whose loyalty is absolute.

Limited dialogue; personality reconstructed from actions and databook notes. Attested: fiercely loyal to Whitebeard (Edward Newgate) and to Portgas D. Ace, participating in the rescue attempt at Marineford despite overwhelming odds. Shown as gruff, combat-ready, and willing to engage Marine officers head-on. Vivre Card notes describe him as a veteran pirate with a calm demeanor off-battlefield (reconstructed from brief databook line). No extended monologue, comic gag, or backstory flashback exists; personality is therefore thinly attested compared to major commanders like Marco or Jozu. Ada

Powers
Pocket Storage / Hammer Space Body

Blamenco can store an unspecified number of hammers and possibly other objects inside pockets covering his body, retrieving giant hammers instantly that are far larger than the pockets themselves. The mechanism is presented as a physical trait or technique, not explicitly identified as a Devil Fruit in manga dialogue. He pulls a massive mallet from a small abdominal pocket and wields it in combat.

Limit: No stated limit to number or size of stored objects, but only hammers have been shown. No ranged ability, no explanation of weight reduction or dimensional physics. Not shown to store living beings, weapons other than hammers, or to retrieve items remotely. Oda has not clarified if this is a Devil Fruit (like Blamenco's pockets resemble the Pocket-Pocket Fruit later introduced with Charlotte Daifuku? No, separate) or simply cartoon physics/hammerspace.

Superhuman Strength and Hammer Mastery

Wields a giant hammer (estimated 2-3x his body length) with one or two hands, delivering crushing blows capable of smashing Marine battleship decking and sending multiple soldiers flying. Strength sufficient to compete on Marineford battlefield alongside New World veterans.

Limit: No named technique (e.g., no 'Hammer Hammer X' attack name). No Haki explicitly shown, though as a New World commander he is presumed to have basic Armament/Observation Haki, but never depicted using it. No long-range or defensive technique shown.

Veteran New World Combatant / Commander Authority

As 6th Division Commander of the Whitebeard Pirates, he holds authority over a division (~100+ men) and is recognized as a strong pirate by Marines (bounty implied but never stated). Participated in decades of New World conflicts under Whitebeard.

Limit: No bounty amount, no specific feat against named strong opponent (e.g., no 1v1 vs Admiral or Vice Admiral shown). His combat record is limited to Marineford fodder and background clashes. Post-timeskip, his group loses the Payback War to Blackbeard Pirates (off-screen).

Accounts I
Introduction of the Whitebeard Commanders at MarinefordOne Piece Chapter 553-554

2009 (Marineford Arc, Summit War Saga)

In response to the Navy's announcement of Portgas D. Ace's public execution, Whitebeard arrives at Marineford with 16 division commanders and 43 allied New World crews. Blamenco is introduced in the double-page spread of commanders alongside Marco (1st), Jozu (3rd), Vista (5th), and others, identified by a text box as 6th Division Commander. He charges with the fleet as the ice battlefield forms. Establishes Blamenco's rank and loyalty; sets up the full Whitebeard force for the war. No individual dialogue for Blamenco in this chapter.

Hammer Assault on the Marine VanguardOne Piece Chapter 557

2009

As Luffy and Whitebeard meet on the battlefield, Blamenco is shown in the foreground pulling an enormous hammer from a pocket on his torso and smashing a group of Marine soldiers. The panel emphasizes the hammerspace gag — a tiny pocket producing a huge weapon — and demonstrates his combat style. He continues advancing toward the execution platform. Only clear combat feat for Blamenco in canon; becomes his defining image in databooks and games. No named Marine officer defeated.

The Deaths of Whitebeard and Ace - Collapse of the OffensiveOne Piece Chapters 568-580

2010

Blamenco appears in background crowd shots as the war turns: Ace is freed then killed by Admiral Akainu, and Whitebeard is killed by Blackbeard Pirates and Marines. Blamenco is shown among commanders reacting in shock and grief (Ch. 576-577 group reaction panels) and later retreating under Shanks' ceasefire. He does not have a personal duel with an Admiral in these chapters. Whitebeard Pirates lose their captain and 2nd Division Commander Ace; the crew is forced to retreat. Blamenco survives the war, unlike Whitebeard and Ace.

Accounts II
Post-War Hiding and Cover Story (590 Cover Arc)One Piece Chapter 590 Cover Story: 'From the Decks of the World' Vol. 15

2010

A cover-story montage shows the whereabouts of Whitebeard Pirates after Marineford. Blamenco is not individually spotlighted but is implied among the surviving commanders in hiding, tending to wounded and mourning. The narration notes the crew went into hiding in the New World after losing their home territory. Establishes survival post-Marineford; leads into the Payback War backstory.

The Payback War - Defeat by the Blackbeard PiratesOne Piece Chapter 820 (Nekomamushi's exposition) and Chapter 552 (allied fleet mention)

2016 (revealed in Zou Arc)

One year after Marineford, the Whitebeard Pirates, led by Marco, wage the Payback War against the Blackbeard Pirates to avenge Whitebeard and Ace. They suffer a crushing defeat. Blamenco is not named in the dialogue, but as a surviving division commander he is understood to have participated and been defeated, after which the Whitebeard Pirates disband and Blackbeard usurps Whitebeard's former Yonko territory. End of Whitebeard Pirates as a Yonko crew; Blamenco's current status becomes unknown/wandering, like other survivors. No death confirmed.

Legacy Appearance in Wano and Final Saga RecapsOne Piece Chapter 909-910 (Reverie), Chapter 1059 (Blackbeard vs. remnants mention)

2018-2022

Blamenco appears in flashback/recap montages when characters recall Marineford or Whitebeard's legacy (e.g., Marco's recollections in Wano, or when Blackbeard's rise is discussed). These are reuse of Marineford panels, not new story, but keep him in continuity as a living survivor referenced among Whitebeard's former commanders. Confirms Oda has not retconned him out; maintains his status as a minor but canonical survivor available for future cameo.

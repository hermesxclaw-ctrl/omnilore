Who It Is

A cave in Indiana leading to a parallel Earth where everything is dead — humans, animals, plants, bacteria. The parallel Foundation is dead at their desks. SCP-682 is dead — truly, permanently dead. Something passed through this world and killed everything, including the concept of death itself. The cave is a warning: whatever did this is still out there.

Form

An unremarkable limestone cave. Inside, a silent parallel Earth: the Foundation site a mausoleum of dead personnel, SCP-682's corpse the most studied object in interdimensional research.

Powers

Interdimensional portal

Evidence of a death-transcending extinction event

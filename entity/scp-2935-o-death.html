Form & Signs

Appearance — SCP-2935 is a space-time anomaly existing within a limestone cave beneath a cemetery near Joppa, Indiana. The anomaly is characterized by a seemingly endless expanse of darkness, punctuated by faint, flickering lights.

Symbols — The anomaly is associated with various symbols and iconography, including the image of a skull and crossbones, which is often used to represent death and mortality.

Domains — SCP-2935 exists in a realm that is parallel to our own, yet simultaneously intersects with it. This realm is characterized by a perpetual state of decay and death, where all living things are reduced to nothing more than dust and ash.

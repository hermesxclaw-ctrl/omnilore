Who It Is

The Earth's consciousness, contacted by the Foundation in 2007. Gaia is ancient, patient, and deeply unimpressed with humanity. It communicates through geological events — earthquakes forming words in seismic graphs, volcanic eruptions timed to morse code. Its message: humanity is an infestation, and Gaia is considering 'treatment options.'

Form

No physical form separate from the Earth. Its 'voice' is the arrangement of natural phenomena. When angry, tectonic activity increases globally. Correlation: 99.97%.

Powers

Planetary-scale consciousness

Control over geological and meteorological phenomena

Communication through natural events

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

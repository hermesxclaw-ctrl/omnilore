Who It Is
Form

Joukahainen is a figure in Finnish mythology who appears as a rival or companion of Väinämöinen. He is often depicted as a young man from the Lapland region.

Powers

Magical abilities Joukahainen is said to possess magical abilities, possibly related to his Lapland heritage

Accounts

Joukahainen challenges Väinämöinen to a singing contest, which Väinämöinen wins.

Joukahainen seeks revenge against Väinämöinen for his defeat in the singing contest.

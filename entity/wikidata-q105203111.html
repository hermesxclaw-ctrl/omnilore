Who It Is

In the sprawling world of Jujutsu Kaisen, where curses born of human fear stalk the streets, few figures embody the rigid, unforgiving hierarchy of jujutsu society more than Yoshinobu Gakuganji. As the principal of Kyoto Jujutsu High, this elderly sorcerer commands respect and fear in equal measure, his conservative worldview clashing with the progressive ideals of his Tokyo counterpart. Yet beneath his stern exterior lies a man shaped by duty, tradition, and a willingness to sacrifice even his own students for what he believes is the greater good.

Gakuganji is a staunch traditionalist who believes in the absolute authority of the higher-ups and the preservation of jujutsu society's rigid hierarchy. He is pragmatic to the point of ruthlessness, willing to order the assassination of a student (Yuta Okkotsu) to prevent a potential threat. He is disciplined, stern, and shows little tolerance for insubordination, yet he respects strength and capability, as seen in his acknowledgment of his students' growth. He is also a skilled musician, playing the shamisen, which he uses as a medium for his cursed technique.

Powers
Cursed Energy Manipulation

As a high-ranking jujutsu sorcerer, Gakuganji possesses a large amount of cursed energy, allowing him to reinforce his physical body and perform advanced techniques.

Limit: Not explicitly defined, but his age may limit his physical stamina.

Shamisen-based Cursed Technique

Gakuganji uses a shamisen (a three-stringed Japanese lute) as a conduit for his cursed technique. By plucking the strings, he can release waves of cursed energy that can blast through obstacles and harm opponents. He demonstrated this by destroying a wall and attacking Yuta Okkotsu.

Limit: Requires the shamisen; if disarmed, he may be vulnerable. The exact mechanics are not fully detailed.

Barrier Techniques

As a principal, he is knowledgeable in barrier techniques, which are used to contain curses and create domains. However, his personal use is not shown in detail.

Limit: Not shown in combat.

Domain Expansion

Gakuganji has not been shown using a Domain Expansion, and it is unknown if he possesses one. In the manga, he survives the Shinjuku Showdown, but his abilities are not fully explored.

Limit: Not shown.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Gakuganji is an elderly man with a slim build, fair skin, and a bald head. He has a long, white beard that extends to his chest, and his eyebrows are also white. He typically wears traditional dark-colored robes with a high-collared jacket, and he carries a wooden staff. His eyes are narrow and often closed, giving him a stern, inscrutable expression.

Symbols — His wooden staff is a symbol of his authority and serves as a conduit for his cursed energy. He is often depicted with a serious, unyielding demeanor, representing the conservative faction of jujutsu society.

Domains — Gakuganji is primarily associated with Kyoto Jujutsu High, where he serves as principal. He operates within the jujutsu world, which exists hidden within modern Japan, dealing with curses and sorcerers. He is also seen at the higher-ups' headquarters during meetings.

Kyoto Sister-School Exchange EventJujutsu Kaisen manga chapters 15-17; anime episodes 13-21

Modern era (fictional)

Gakuganji orders his students to kill Yuta Okkotsu, a special-grade sorcerer, to prevent him from becoming a threat. He later confronts Yuta himself, but is stopped by the arrival of Satoru Gojo. Gakuganji is shown to be ruthless, prioritizing the safety of jujutsu society over the life of a student. The event showcases the ideological divide between the conservative Kyoto school and the more progressive Tokyo school. Gakuganji's actions are thwarted, and he later acknowledges the strength of the Tokyo students.

Shibuya IncidentJujutsu Kaisen manga chapters 64-66; anime Season 2 episodes 23-29

Modern era (fictional)

Gakuganji participates in the battle at Shibuya, but his role is minimal. He is seen in the aftermath, discussing the events with other higher-ups. He is notably absent from the main conflicts, possibly due to his age or strategic placement. The Shibuya Incident results in massive casualties and the sealing of Satoru Gojo, shifting the balance of power. Gakuganji's exact actions during the incident are not detailed.

Culling Game ArcJujutsu Kaisen manga chapters 139-199

Modern era (fictional)

Gakuganji is among the higher-ups who are targeted by Kenjaku's plans. He survives the initial chaos and later plays a role in the decision to execute Yuji Itadori after Sukuna's rampage. He is shown to be pragmatic, supporting the execution to appease the higher-ups. The execution order is issued, but Yuji is saved by his allies. Gakuganji's actions highlight his adherence to traditional jujutsu law.

He survives the battle. The battle ends with Sukuna's defeat and the resolution of the main conflict. Gakuganji's survival indicates his resilience.

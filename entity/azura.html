Who It Is

In the pantheon of the Daedric Princes, Azura stands apart—a goddess of twilight and transition, whose gaze pierces the veil between day and night, and whose vengeance is as swift as the setting sun. She is the Queen of Dawn and Dusk, the Mother of the Rose, and the patron of those who walk the edge of change. Yet her story is not one of gentle benevolence; it is a tale of prophecy, betrayal, and the unyielding consequences of pride.

Azura is portrayed as proud, enigmatic, and deeply concerned with the fate of her followers. She is known to be both benevolent and vengeful, rewarding those who serve her faithfully and cursing those who betray her. She is a master of prophecy and often manipulates events to fulfill her visions.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Azura' may derive from the word 'azure', meaning sky blue, reflecting her association with twilight and the color of her realm. Alternatively, it could be a corruption of 'Azurah', a name used by the Khajiit.

Pronunciation — Ah-ZOO-rah

Aliases — Mother of the Rose, Queen of Dawn and Dusk, Azurah

Ownership — Azura is a Daedric Prince in the fictional universe of The Elder Scrolls, created by Bethesda Game Studios. She is worshipped by various races, most notably the Dunmer (Dark Elves)

Powers
Prophecy

Azura can foresee future events and often communicates her visions to her chosen mortals. She is known for her accurate prophecies, such as the Nerevarine prophecy.

Limit: Her prophecies are often cryptic and can be misinterpreted.

Cursing

Azura can curse individuals or entire races. The most famous example is her curse on the Chimer, transforming them into the Dunmer with grey skin and red eyes.

Limit: Her curses are permanent and cannot be lifted by mortal means.

Blessing

She grants blessings to her followers, such as increased luck, protection, or special abilities. In the games, praying at her shrine can grant temporary boosts.

Limit: Blessings require devotion and can be lost if the follower displeases her.

Realm Manipulation

As a Daedric Prince, she has absolute control over her realm of Moonshadow.

Limit: She cannot leave her realm permanently and her influence in Mundus is limited.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Azura is most often depicted as a beautiful woman with dark blue or purple skin, flowing black hair, and eyes that glow with a violet light. She is sometimes shown with feathered wings or accompanied by a glowing star. Her appearance is often associated with the colors of dusk and dawn.

Symbols — Her symbol is a crescent moon and star, representing the twilight. The star is often depicted as a glowing gem, and the crescent moon is sometimes shown with a rose. She is also associated with the color purple and the concept of transition.

Domains — Azura's domain is Moonshadow, a realm of twilight and beauty, said to be filled with gardens of roses and glowing crystals. She is associated with the liminal times of dawn and dusk, and her influence is felt in matters of change, prophecy, and vanity.

The Chimer and the CurseVarious in-game books, including 'The War of the First Council' and 'Nerevar at Red Mountain'

First Era

After the death of Nerevar, the Tribunal used the Heart of Lorkhan to achieve godhood. Azura appeared to them and cursed the Chimer, turning their skin grey and their eyes red, declaring that they would be 'Dunmer' forever. This curse was a punishment for their betrayal of Nerevar's trust. The Chimer became the Dunmer, and Azura became one of the 'Good Daedra' to the Dunmer, who still worship her despite the curse.

Accounts II
The Nerevarine ProphecyMorrowind main quest

Third Era

Azura prophesied that a reincarnation of Nerevar would come to Morrowind to defeat the Tribunal and Dagoth Ur. The Nerevarine, the player character, fulfills this prophecy, with Azura guiding them throughout their journey. The Tribunal loses their divinity, Dagoth Ur is defeated, and Azura's prophecy is fulfilled.

The Star of AzuraSkyrim (The Black Star quest)

Fourth Era

Azura tasks the Dragonborn with retrieving her star, which has been corrupted and turned into the Black Star by the mage Malyn Varen. The Dragonborn must decide whether to restore the star to Azura or keep it for themselves. The star is restored or kept, depending on the player's choice, affecting the artifact's properties.

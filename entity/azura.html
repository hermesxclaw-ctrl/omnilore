Who It Is

The Daedric Prince of dusk and dawn, vanity, and egotism. Also known as the Mother of the Rose and Queen of the Night Sky. Unlike many Daedra, Azura is concerned for her followers' well-being. Her realm is Moonshadow, a plane of impossible beauty. Her artifact is Azura's Star. She cursed the Chimer to become the Dunmer (Dark Elves) for breaking their oath to her.

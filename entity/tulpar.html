Who It Is

The Tulpar is the winged horse of Turkic mythology — a celestial steed of extraordinary speed and loyalty. In epics, Tulpars advise their riders, warn of danger, and sometimes sacrifice themselves. The hero Manas's horse Ak-Kula is a legendary Tulpar. The creature appears on Kazakhstan's national emblem as a symbol of freedom.

Attested in Oral tradition / Turkic folkloreEra Ancient to present; national symbol

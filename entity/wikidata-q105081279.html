Who It Is

On the distant planet Deviluke, where royal power is measured in star systems, the youngest princess hides a secret ambition not of conquest, but of the heart. Momo Belia Deviluke, the third princess, is a master of plants and a schemer of romance, whose Harem Plan could reshape the fate of Earth's most reluctant hero.

Momo is sweet, friendly, and calculating. She is highly intelligent, especially in extraterrestrial botany, but less so than her elder sister Lala. She has a mature mentality regarding romance and sex, often fantasizing about Rito. When angered, she becomes terrifying and sadistic, a stark contrast to her usual demeanor. She is manipulative, orchestrating situations to advance her Harem Plan, but also shows vulnerability and doubt, especially regarding her own feelings.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Momo' is Japanese for 'peach', possibly referencing her sweet yet mischievous personality. 'Belia' may be a play on 'Bella' (Italian for beautiful) or a constructed alien name. 'Deviluke' is the name of her royal family and home planet.

Pronunciation — MO-mo BEH-lee-ah Deh-vee-LOO-keh (Japanese: モモ・ベリア・デビルーク, Hepburn: Momo Beria Debirūku)

Aliases — Third Princess of Deviluke, Momo-chan, Momo (in-universe)

Powers
Plant Communication

Momo can communicate with plants, allowing her to befriend and command them. This ability is unique among her siblings and is used for information gathering and assistance.

Limit: Limited to plants; she cannot communicate with animals like her twin Nana. The extent of control is not fully detailed.

Royal Combat Skills

As a princess of Deviluke, Momo possesses enhanced physical abilities and combat training, though she rarely uses them. She is capable of defeating Zastin, her sister's bodyguard, with Nana's help.

Limit: She relies more on strategy than direct combat, and her physical strength is not as prominently displayed as other characters.

Space Distortion Technology Use

Momo can utilize advanced Devilukean technology, such as creating a hidden living space in the attic using space distortion.

Limit: Requires the technology; not an innate power.

Manipulation and Scheming

Momo's primary 'power' is her intellect and ability to manipulate situations to her advantage, particularly in romantic contexts. She uses love simulation games and careful planning to orchestrate encounters.

Limit: Her plans often backfire or have unintended consequences, revealing her emotional vulnerabilities.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Momo has short pink hair, unlike her sisters' longer locks. She often wears a Gothic Lolita style with black and green colors, contrasting with her twin Nana's black and red. Her eyes are typically depicted as green or teal. She has a slim build and a tail, characteristic of the Devilukean race, which is sensitive to touch.

Symbols — Her signature colors are black and green, often incorporated into her outfits. She is frequently associated with flowers and plants, reflecting her ability to communicate with flora. Her tail is a notable feature, often drawn in a heart shape.

Domains — Originally from the planet Deviluke, she later resides on Earth in the Yuki household's attic with her twin Nana, using space distortion technology to create a private space. She attends Sainan High School as part of her plan.

2008

Momo and Nana arrive on Earth and trap the cast in a virtual reality RPG game called Trouble Quest to test Rito's worthiness as Lala's fiancé. They eventually release everyone and apologize. The twins apologize and return to Deviluke, but later escape back to Earth.

Escape to EarthTo Love Ru, chapters 101-103

2008

Momo and Nana flee Deviluke to avoid their studies and return to Earth. They forget Zastin is there, but manage to defeat him and convince their father to let them stay. The twins establish residence in the Yuki household's attic.

Accounts II
The Harem PlanTo Love Ru Darkness

2010-2017

Momo initiates the Harem Plan to allow Rito to marry all the girls who love him, including herself, by making him the next King of Deviluke. She enters Sainan High to monitor progress and orchestrates romantic situations. The plan gradually gains acceptance, but Momo faces emotional turmoil as she questions her own satisfaction.

Mother's VisitTo Love Ru Darkness, chapters 30-35

2013

Sephie, Momo's mother, visits Earth and discovers the Harem Plan. She gives her blessing, but Momo begins to doubt whether she can be satisfied with sharing Rito. Momo's doubts intensify, leading to a crisis of confidence.

The Approach

On the distant planet Deviluke, where royal power is measured in star systems, the youngest princess hides a secret ambition not of conquest, but of the heart. Momo Belia Deviluke, the third princess, is a master of plants and a schemer of romance, whose Harem Plan could reshape the fate of Earth's most reluctant hero.

Content advisory. This character's story contains themes of sexual innuendo, suggestive situations, and harem comedy. The franchise is rated for older teens and includes fan service. Descriptions of her behavior may be explicit in nature.
Identity

Momo Belia Deviluke

Pronunciation MO-mo BEH-lee-ah Deh-vee-LOO-keh (Japanese: モモ・ベリア・デビルーク, Hepburn: Momo Beria Debirūku)

Known As

Third Princess of DevilukeMomo-chanMomo (in-universe)

Etymology

The name 'Momo' is Japanese for 'peach', possibly referencing her sweet yet mischievous personality. 'Belia' may be a play on 'Bella' (Italian for beautiful) or a constructed alien name. 'Deviluke' is the name of her royal family and home planet.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Saki Hasemi (writer) and Kentaro Yabuki (artist) for the manga series To Love Ru, serialized in Shueisha's Weekly Shonen Jump. The franchise is Japanese in origin and is part of the harem comedy genre.

Disputed Identifications

No significant disputes exist regarding her identity. Some Western fans may confuse her with her twin sister Nana, but the characters are distinct in appearance and personality.

Description

Appearance

Momo has short pink hair, unlike her sisters' longer locks. She often wears a Gothic Lolita style with black and green colors, contrasting with her twin Nana's black and red. Her eyes are typically depicted as green or teal. She has a slim build and a tail, characteristic of the Devilukean race, which is sensitive to touch.

Symbols & Iconography

Her signature colors are black and green, often incorporated into her outfits. She is frequently associated with flowers and plants, reflecting her ability to communicate with flora. Her tail is a notable feature, often drawn in a heart shape.

Behaviour & Personality

Momo is sweet, friendly, and calculating. She is highly intelligent, especially in extraterrestrial botany, but less so than her elder sister Lala. She has a mature mentality regarding romance and sex, often fantasizing about Rito. When angered, she becomes terrifying and sadistic, a stark contrast to her usual demeanor. She is manipulative, orchestrating situations to advance her Harem Plan, but also shows vulnerability and doubt, especially regarding her own feelings.

Domains & Habitats

Originally from the planet Deviluke, she later resides on Earth in the Yuki household's attic with her twin Nana, using space distortion technology to create a private space. She attends Sainan High School as part of her plan.

Version Differences

In the original To Love Ru, Momo is a recurring secondary character, introduced as a mischievous troublemaker. In To Love Ru Darkness, she becomes a main protagonist, with her Harem Plan as a central plot. The anime adaptations closely follow the manga. In the OVAs, her character is further explored, with some additional comedic scenes.

Powers & Abilities

Plant Communication

Source Version Original manga and anime

Description

Momo can communicate with plants, allowing her to befriend and command them. This ability is unique among her siblings and is used for information gathering and assistance.

Limitations

Limited to plants; she cannot communicate with animals like her twin Nana. The extent of control is not fully detailed.

As a princess of Deviluke, Momo possesses enhanced physical abilities and combat training, though she rarely uses them. She is capable of defeating Zastin, her sister's bodyguard, with Nana's help.

She relies more on strategy than direct combat, and her physical strength is not as prominently displayed as other characters.

The franchise is rated for older teens (16+) in Japan, and the content is legal but often criticized for its depiction of underage characters in sexual contexts.

Manipulative behavior

Source To Love Ru Darkness

Description

Momo's Harem Plan involves manipulating Rito and other girls into romantic situations without their full knowledge or consent, which could be seen as unethical. However, the narrative presents it as comedic and ultimately consensual.

Scholarly Context

In fiction, such manipulation is often played for laughs, but it can normalize coercive behavior.

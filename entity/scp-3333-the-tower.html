SCP-3333 — The Tower

Who It Is
Form

SCP-3333 is a spatial anomaly located within the Suntop fire lookout, a wooden structure built on a mountain in the Mt. Baker/Snoqualmie National Forest, Washington State, United States of America. The anomaly is accessible via a ladder and trapdoor on the ceiling of the Suntop fire lookout.

Powers

Information Provision SCP-3333 has the ability to provide information and assistance to those who approach it. This can include answering questions, providing guidance, and even granting access to previously inaccessible areas.

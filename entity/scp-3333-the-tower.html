Who It Is

A fire lookout tower in a national forest that does not exist on any map. The tower is infinite in height — climbing it never reaches the top. Each floor is an identical replica of the previous, but slightly wrong: furniture rearranged, photographs with different faces, journal entries by previous climbers growing increasingly desperate. At floor 3,333 (the farthest any explorer has reached), a journal entry reads: 'The tower knows I'm here. It's changing the floors ahead. It doesn't want me to reach the top. Or maybe it's the top that doesn't want to be reached.'

Form

A standard US Forest Service fire lookout tower, ~30m tall from the outside. Inside: infinite floors of identical living quarters. The fire finder (map device) on each floor shows a different landscape — none of which exist in our world.

Powers

Infinite vertical recursion

Environmental manipulation based on climber's psychology

Spatial anchoring to an unmappable location

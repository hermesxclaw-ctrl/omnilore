Who It Is

In the vast multiverse of fiction, the name 'Chrome Dome' echoes across universes—from a shining bald head to a mad scientist, a Transformers warrior, and a Cold War military operation. But who is the true Chrome Dome? The answer is as multifaceted as the characters themselves.

In Sweet Valley High, Mr. Cooper is a teacher; in TMNT, Chrome Dome is a villainous robot; in Transformers, Chromedome is a heroic Autobot; in The Tick, Professor Chromedome is a mad scientist.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Compound of 'chrome' (shiny metal) and 'dome' (head), referring to a bald head that shines like chrome.

Pronunciation — /kroʊm doʊm/

Aliases — Mr. Cooper, Chromedome, Professor Chromedome, Operation Chrome Dome, Chrome Dome Empire

Ownership — Multiple cultures: American (Sweet Valley High, TMNT, G.I. Joe, The Tick), British (librarian nickname), global (Transformers, Cold War era).

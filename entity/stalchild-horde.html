Who It Is

Did you know that the Stalchildren of Majora's Mask are not just random undead, but the remnants of a once-proud army that continues to serve their fallen kingdom? Their loyalty transcends death, but at what cost? Explore the tragic history of the Ikana Kingdom and the undead soldiers who guard its ruins.

Stalchildren are aggressive and relentless, attacking Link on sight in both Ocarina of Time and Majora's Mask. They are nocturnal in Ocarina of Time, emerging only at night in Hyrule Field, and they travel in packs, overwhelming the player with numbers. In Majora's Mask, they are more organized, acting as soldiers under Captain Keeta's command, and they patrol specific areas of Ikana Canyon. Powers
Undead Resilience

Stalchildren are undead, meaning they do not feel pain or fatigue. They can continue fighting even after sustaining severe damage, and they are immune to many forms of physical harm that would incapacitate living beings. However, they are vulnerable to holy or light-based attacks, which can destroy them permanently.

Limit:

Burrowing Emergence

In Ocarina of Time, Stalchildren can emerge from the ground, allowing them to ambush Link unexpectedly. This ability makes them difficult to avoid, as they can appear from any patch of earth in Hyrule Field at night.

Limit:

Pack Coordination

Stalchildren operate in groups, coordinating their attacks to overwhelm opponents. In Majora's Mask, they follow Captain Keeta's commands, demonstrating a level of tactical organization that makes them more dangerous than individual undead.

Limit:

Weapon Proficiency

Stalchildren are skilled in using small swords and shields, reflecting their military training in the Ikana Kingdom. They can block attacks and strike with precision, making them formidable in close combat.

Accounts I
The Ikana Kingdom's FallMajora's Mask (Nintendo 64)

Era of the Mask (in-game timeline)

The Ikana Kingdom was once a prosperous nation, but it was ravaged by war and a curse that brought the dead back to life. The Stalchildren are the remnants of the Ikana army, who continue to serve their king even after death. Link encounters them as he travels through Ikana Canyon, and he must defeat them to reach Captain Keeta, who guards the path to the Stone Tower Temple. The story reveals that the soldiers' loyalty to their kingdom transcends death, but their existence is tragic, as they are trapped in an endless cycle of guarding a land that has long since fallen. Link defeats Captain Keeta and the Stalchildren, allowing him to progress to the Stone Tower Temple. The defeat of the horde weakens the curse on Ikana, and the kingdom's spirits are eventually laid to rest after Link completes his mission.

Accounts II
Nightly Assaults in Hyrule FieldOcarina of Time (Nintendo 64)

Era of the Hero of Time

During Link's journey in Ocarina of Time, he encounters Stalchildren in Hyrule Field at night. These undead creatures rise from the ground and attack him in groups, serving as a constant threat during his travels. Unlike the Ikana Stalchildren, these are not tied to a specific kingdom but are a general menace of the land. They are relatively weak, but their numbers can overwhelm an unprepared traveler. The encounters serve to highlight the dangers of Hyrule Field after dark and add to the game's atmosphere of adventure and peril. Link defeats the Stalchildren, gaining experience and occasionally collecting rupees or other items they drop. The encounters reinforce the game's day-night cycle and encourage players to plan their travels accordingly.

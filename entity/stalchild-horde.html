Who It Is

Skeletal children that rise from the earth at night on the fields of Hyrule. Their bones glow with a faint blue light, and they wield miniature weapons — shields, swords, and spears. They swarm travelers who dare to cross Hyrule Field after sunset, emerging from the very ground in waves.

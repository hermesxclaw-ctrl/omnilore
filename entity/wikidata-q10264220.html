Identity

Dario

Pronunciation Italian: [ˈdaːrjo]; Spanish: [ˈdaɾjo]; English: /ˈdɑːrioʊ/ (DAH-ree-oh)

Etymology

Italian and Spanish form of Darius, derived from the ancient Persian name Dārayavahuš, meaning 'he who holds firm the good' or 'possessor of goodness'. The name is associated with several Persian kings, most notably Darius the Great.

Cultural Ownership

Primarily Italian and Spanish, but used across many cultures due to migration and global media. It has a strong presence in Latin America and among diaspora communities.

Disputed Identifications

The name is sometimes confused with 'Dario' as a surname or with similar names like 'Darius' or 'Daria'. In some contexts, 'Dario' may be a shortened form of longer names, but this is not standard.

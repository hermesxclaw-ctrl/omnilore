Who It Is
Form

Saiken is a massive, serpent-like creature with a hundred tails and a powerful physique. It is said to be one of the strongest Tailed Beasts in the Naruto universe.

Powers

Tailed Beast Ball Saiken's ability to create and manipulate massive energy balls that can cause massive destruction.

Accounts

Saiken is born from the energy of the sea and becomes one of the strongest Tailed Beasts in the Naruto universe.

Saiken is awakened by the Tailed Beast Ball and becomes a powerful ally to the Naruto and his friends.

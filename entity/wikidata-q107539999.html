Who It Is

Nozomi Tojo is the spiritual heart of μ's, the school idol group at the center of the multimedia project Love Live! School Idol Project. As the oldest member and student council vice president, she often seems to know more than she lets on, using her talent for fortune-telling to guide her friends. But beneath her serene, mystical exterior lies a mischievous streak that keeps everyone on their toes, making her one of the most memorable characters in the franchise.

Nozomi is calm, observant, and wise, often acting as the group's advisor and spiritual leader. She has a serene and almost all-knowing demeanor, and she tends to speak in a soft, soothing tone. However, she has a mischievous and playful side, and she is not above teasing her friends, sometimes in a 'perverted' manner by groping their breasts, which she claims is to 'cheer them up'. She uses fortune-telling to connect with others and to make decisions, and she is deeply caring and protective of her friends, especially Eli Ayase.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname 'Tojo' (東條) means 'eastern wisteria', and the given name 'Nozomi' (希) means 'hope'. The name can be interpreted as 'hope of the east'.

Pronunciation — NOH-zoh-mee TOH-joh

Aliases — Nozomi, Nozomi Tojo (Romanized), Tōjō Nozomi (Japanese), 東條 希 (Japanese)

Ownership — Japanese multimedia franchise, specifically the Love Live! project created by ASCII Media Works, Lantis, and Sunrise.

Powers
Fortune-telling

Nozomi is skilled in various forms of fortune-telling, including tarot cards, astrology, and palm reading. She uses this ability to guide her friends and to make decisions for the group.

Limit: Her predictions are sometimes vague or open to interpretation; they are not always accurate in a literal sense.

Spiritual leadership

Nozomi acts as the spiritual and emotional anchor of μ's, often providing guidance and support to the other members. She has an intuitive sense of when someone is troubled and offers comfort in her own unique way.

Limit: Her methods can be unconventional and sometimes intrusive (e.g., groping), which may not always be well-received.

Kansai dialect

Nozomi speaks in Kansai dialect, despite not being from the Kansai region. This is a distinctive speech pattern that sets her apart from the other characters.

Limit: This is a linguistic trait, not a supernatural power.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Nozomi is a tall, slender third-year high school student with long, wavy violet hair and dark violet eyes. She often wears a gentle smile. Her casual attire includes a white top with a purple cardigan and a skirt. As part of μ's, she wears the group's school idol costume, which features a white and pink design with a purple accent. She is often seen with a tarot card or other fortune-telling paraphernalia.

Symbols — Tarot cards, especially the 'The Star' card, are associated with Nozomi. She is also associated with the Kansai dialect, which is a distinctive feature of her speech. Her image color in the franchise is purple.

Domains — Nozomi primarily resides in the fictional Otonokizaka Academy in Tokyo, Japan. She lives alone in an apartment, having moved frequently due to her parents' work. She is often seen in the school's student council room, the μ's practice room, or various locations around the school.

Accounts I
The Formation of μ'sLove Live! School Idol Project (anime)

2013

Nozomi is initially part of the student council with Eli, and she is skeptical of Honoka's idea to form a school idol group. However, she sees potential in the group and eventually joins, becoming one of the last members. She uses her fortune-telling to encourage Honoka and the others to pursue their dream. Nozomi becomes a core member of μ's and helps the group grow, eventually contributing to saving the school and competing in the Love Live competition.

Accounts II
The Love Live CompetitionLove Live! School Idol Project (anime)

2014

μ's enters the Love Live competition, a national school idol contest. Nozomi plays a crucial role in keeping the group unified and motivated, especially when they face setbacks. She also creates the group's name 'μ's' based on her intuition. μ's wins the competition, but the group disbands shortly after due to the third-years' graduation and personal reasons.

Nozomi's Past and Friendship with EliLove Live! School Idol Project (anime)

2014

It is revealed that Nozomi moved frequently as a child due to her parents' work, making it difficult to form lasting friendships. She chose to live alone in Otonokizaka to have stability. She found a close friend in Eli, who was the first person she truly connected with. This backstory is explored in the second season. This deepens the bond between Nozomi and Eli and explains Nozomi's protective nature toward Eli.

Who It Is

A medical report detailing a psychiatric patient whose condition — 'Pervasive Developmental Disorder: Undifferentiated' — apparently caused every medical professional who treated him to descend into psychosis within weeks of contact. Nurses quit, doctors committed suicide, orderlies attacked each other. The patient himself was calm, cooperative, and unfailingly polite. He claimed to have no idea why people around him went insane. The final treating physician's notes — written in increasingly erratic handwriting — conclude: 'He doesn't drive us insane. He shows us that we already were. He's a mirror, and we can't stand what we see.'

Form

The patient is described as a middle-aged man of unremarkable appearance — average height, average build, pleasant smile. His only notable feature: his eyes. Every report mentions his eyes. They are 'understanding.' They are 'kind.' They are 'looking at you the way you look at yourself when you can't sleep at 3 AM.'

Powers

Psychic mirroring (theorized)

Insanity induction via proximity

Apparent unawareness of his own effect

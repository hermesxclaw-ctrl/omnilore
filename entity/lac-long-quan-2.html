Who It Is

Lạc Long Quân is the mythic dragon-king who, with the mountain fairy Âu Cơ, is the progenitor of the Vietnamese people. He slew monsters threatening the people and taught agriculture and civilization. His marriage to Âu Cơ produced one hundred eggs from which one hundred sons hatched. They parted, each taking fifty sons — the dragon to the coast, the fairy to the mountains. Their eldest became the first Hùng King.

Who It Is

A home-runner's bat that, when swung, causes the batter to hit themselves in the head at a velocity proportional to the force of the swing. The bat compels its holder to swing it at incoming projectiles. What's deeply unsettling is footage recovered with the bat showing a children's baseball game where a child was beaten to death with it while a crowd — including the child's parents — watched and cheered.

Form

A standard wooden baseball bat, Louisville Slugger model, stained with what appears to be dried blood. No anomalous physical properties beyond the compulsion and velocity-inversion effects.

Kinetic inversion

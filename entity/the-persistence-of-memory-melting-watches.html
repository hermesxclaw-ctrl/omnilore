Who It Is

Soft, melting pocket watches draped over a barren Catalonian landscape — one over a branch, one on a bizarre fetal-faced form, one on a ledge. Ants swarm a closed watch. The scene conveys a dream-state where time has lost all rigidity.

Cultural Significance

Dalí claimed the image came to him while watching Camembert cheese melt in the sun. The painting bridges Einsteinian relativity (which Dalí was fascinated by) and Freudian dream analysis. At only 24 × 33 cm, its outsized cultural footprint dwarfs its physical dimensions.

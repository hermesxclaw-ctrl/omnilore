Who It Is

Padmasambhava's life and teachings continue to inspire and influence Tibetan Buddhism to this day.

Padmasambhava is revered as a wise and compassionate guru, known for his ability to transmit the teachings of the Buddha to his students. He is said to have possessed great wisdom, courage, and compassion, and was able to overcome many challenges and obstacles in his mission to spread the teachings of the Vajrayana.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Padmasambhava is often depicted as a wise and compassionate guru, surrounded by lotus flowers, with a peaceful and enigmatic expression. He is typically shown wearing a red hat and a long, flowing robe, symbolizing his status as a tantric master.

Symbols — The lotus flower, the red hat, and the long, flowing robe are all symbols of Padmasambhava's status as a tantric master and his connection to the Vajrayana tradition.

Domains — Padmasambhava is associated with the Himalayan region, particularly with the kingdoms of Uddiyana and Tibet.

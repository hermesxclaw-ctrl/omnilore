Who It Is
Form

Padmasambhava is often depicted as a wise and compassionate guru, surrounded by lotus flowers, with a peaceful and enigmatic expression. He is typically shown wearing a red hat and a long, flowing robe, symbolizing his status as a tantric master.

Powers

Tantric mastery Padmasambhava is said to have possessed great mastery over the tantric teachings and was able to transmit them to his students with great effectiveness.

Accounts

This story tells the life and teachings of Padmasambhava, from his birth in Uddiyana to his arrival in Tibet and his transmission of the tantric teachings.

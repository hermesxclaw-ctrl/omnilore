Who It Is

Enrico Pucci's ultimate Stand, evolved from Whitesnake and C-Moon. Made in Heaven grants the ability to accelerate time at an exponential rate. Pucci can move at immense speeds while time is accelerated, eventually causing the universe to loop and reset. Only those with 'knowledge of heaven' retain their memories through the reset.

Powers

Time acceleration, universe reset, super-speed movement

Who It Is

Yūki Terumi is the hidden hand behind BlazBlue's endless cycle of suffering—a manipulative, sadistic entity who thrives on chaos and destruction, orchestrating tragedies across timelines with a grin that never fades.

Terumi is sadistic, manipulative, and arrogant. He enjoys causing pain and chaos, viewing others as pawns in his game. He is highly intelligent and cunning, always scheming to achieve his goals, which involve destroying the world and rewriting reality to his liking.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Terumi' (照美) can be interpreted as 'shining beauty' in Japanese, which contrasts with his malevolent nature. 'Yūki' (悠木) is a common Japanese surname.

Pronunciation — YOO-kee TEH-roo-mee

Aliases — Terumi, The Black Beast, Susano'o Unit, The Azure Grimoire's true owner, Hazama (host body)

Ownership — Owned by Arc System Works, creators of BlazBlue. The character is a Japanese creation, but has been localized for Western audiences.

Powers
Soul Manipulation

Terumi can manipulate souls, including extracting and transferring them. He used this to possess Hazama and to torment others.

Limit: Requires proximity or a prepared vessel. He cannot possess someone without a suitable host.

Time Manipulation

As the Susano'o Unit, he can manipulate time, allowing him to rewind, pause, or alter events.

Limit: Limited by the Azure's power and his own will. He can be countered by those with strong connection to the Azure.

Immortality

Terumi cannot be permanently killed by conventional means. He has been 'killed' multiple times but returns through various methods, such as possessing others or being resurrected.

Limit: He can be permanently erased if killed within the Azure or if his soul is completely destroyed.

Ars Magus

As a powerful magic user, he can use Ars Magus, a form of magic that enhances physical abilities and allows for special attacks.

Limit: Requires a Grimoire or other focus.

Possession

He can possess others, taking over their bodies and using them as puppets.

Limit: Requires a host with a compatible soul. He cannot possess someone with a strong will indefinitely.

Azure Manipulation

He has a deep connection to the Azure, the fundamental force of the world, allowing him to manipulate it to a degree.

Limit: His control is not absolute; he cannot fully control the Azure without the Master Unit.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Terumi typically appears as a tall, slender man with blond hair and green eyes, wearing a white and green uniform. In his true form as the Susano'o Unit, he takes on a monstrous, armored appearance with a menacing aura.

Symbols — Often associated with snakes, reflecting his deceptive nature. His weapon, the Susano'o, is a giant sword that symbolizes his connection to the god of storms.

Domains — He exists primarily within the BlazBlue world, often operating from the shadows of the Novus Orbis Librarium (NOL). He is linked to the Azure, the fundamental force of the world, and can traverse different timelines.

Accounts I
The Origin of TerumiBlazBlue: Central Fiction

Ancient past of the BlazBlue world

Terumi was originally the Susano'o Unit, one of the three Sankishin Units created to maintain the world. He rebelled against the Master Unit Amaterasu, seeking to destroy the world and recreate it in his image. He was defeated and sealed, but his soul escaped and has been causing chaos ever since. His rebellion led to the creation of the Azure Grimoire and the cycle of time loops that plague the world.

The Ikaruga Civil WarBlazBlue: Calamity Trigger

Before the main events

Terumi manipulated the Ikaruga Civil War, using it to further his plans. He was responsible for the death of Ragna's and Jin's family, setting the stage for the series' conflict. Ragna's hatred for the NOL was born, and Jin was manipulated into cutting off Ragna's arm.

Accounts II
The Possession of HazamaBlazBlue: Continuum Shift

During the events of Continuum Shift

Terumi possessed Hazama, a NOL officer, using his body as a vessel. He used this identity to manipulate events from within the NOL. Hazama's body became Terumi's puppet, and Terumi gained access to NOL resources.

The Final BattleBlazBlue: Central Fiction

End of the series

Terumi, in his true form as the Susano'o Unit, battles Ragna and his allies. He is ultimately defeated when Ragna separates him from the Susano'o armor and kills him in the Azure void. Terumi is seemingly destroyed for good, and Ragna removes the Azure from the world, ending the time loops.

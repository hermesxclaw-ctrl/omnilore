The Approach

Yūki Terumi is the hidden hand behind BlazBlue's endless cycle of suffering—a manipulative, sadistic entity who thrives on chaos and destruction, orchestrating tragedies across timelines with a grin that never fades.

Content advisory. Contains themes of psychological manipulation, mind control, violence, and murder. Some content may be disturbing to sensitive readers.
Identity

Yūki Terumi

Pronunciation YOO-kee TEH-roo-mee

Known As

TerumiThe Black BeastSusano'o UnitThe Azure Grimoire's true ownerHazama (host body)

Etymology

The name 'Terumi' (照美) can be interpreted as 'shining beauty' in Japanese, which contrasts with his malevolent nature. 'Yūki' (悠木) is a common Japanese surname.

Cultural Ownership

Owned by Arc System Works, creators of BlazBlue. The character is a Japanese creation, but has been localized for Western audiences.

Disputed Identifications

There is some confusion between Terumi and his host body, Hazama. They are separate entities, but Terumi takes over Hazama's body, leading to a merged identity. Some fans mistakenly consider them the same character.

Description

Appearance

Terumi typically appears as a tall, slender man with blond hair and green eyes, wearing a white and green uniform. In his true form as the Susano'o Unit, he takes on a monstrous, armored appearance with a menacing aura.

Symbols & Iconography

Often associated with snakes, reflecting his deceptive nature. His weapon, the Susano'o, is a giant sword that symbolizes his connection to the god of storms.

Behaviour & Personality

Terumi is sadistic, manipulative, and arrogant. He enjoys causing pain and chaos, viewing others as pawns in his game. He is highly intelligent and cunning, always scheming to achieve his goals, which involve destroying the world and rewriting reality to his liking.

Domains & Habitats

He exists primarily within the BlazBlue world, often operating from the shadows of the Novus Orbis Librarium (NOL). He is linked to the Azure, the fundamental force of the world, and can traverse different timelines.

Version Differences

In his host body Hazama, he is more restrained and calculating. As the Susano'o Unit, he is more overtly powerful and aggressive. His personality remains consistent, but his methods change depending on the form.

Powers & Abilities

Soul Manipulation

Source Version All games

Description

Terumi can manipulate souls, including extracting and transferring them. He used this to possess Hazama and to torment others.

Limitations

Requires proximity or a prepared vessel. He cannot possess someone without a suitable host.

Time Manipulation

Source Version Central Fiction

Description

As the Susano'o Unit, he can manipulate time, allowing him to rewind, pause, or alter events.

Limitations

Limited by the Azure's power and his own will. He can be countered by those with strong connection to the Azure.

Contradictions

None noted.

Immortality

Source Version All games

Description

Terumi cannot be permanently killed by conventional means. He has been 'killed' multiple times but returns through various methods, such as possessing others or being resurrected.

Limitations

He can be permanently erased if killed within the Azure or if his soul is completely destroyed.

Contradictions

In Central Fiction, he is seemingly killed for good by Ragna, but the ending leaves ambiguity.

Stories & Myths

The Origin of Terumi

Source BlazBlue: Central FictionCulture Japanese video gamePeriod Ancient past of the BlazBlue world

Summary

Terumi was originally the Susano'o Unit, one of the three Sankishin Units created to maintain the world. He rebelled against the Master Unit Amaterasu, seeking to destroy the world and recreate it in his image. He was defeated and sealed, but his soul escaped and has been causing chaos ever since.

Participants

Terumi

Amaterasu

The other Sankishin Units

Consequences

His rebellion led to the creation of the Azure Grimoire and the cycle of time loops that plague the world.

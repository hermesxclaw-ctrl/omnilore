Who It Is
Form

SCP-2316 is a lake entity that appears as a large, dark body of water with an irregular shape. Its surface is often disturbed, with waves and ripples that seem to be moving of their own accord.

Powers

Memory Manipulation SCP-2316 is capable of manipulating and distorting memories, creating false memories and altering the perception of reality. This ability allows it to control the actions and decisions of those around it, often leading to chaos and destruction.

Incident Log

A group of Foundation agents were sent to investigate a reported sighting of SCP-2316. However, upon arrival, they found themselves unable to distinguish between reality and fantasy, and were eventually driven mad by the entity's manipulation of their memories.

A group of researchers discovered a hidden lake entity in the depths of the ocean. As they studied the entity, they began to realize the true extent of its powers and abilities, including its ability to manipulate memories and create new bodies of water.

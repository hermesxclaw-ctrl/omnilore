Who It Is
Form

Percival is often depicted as a young, handsome knight, dressed in armor and carrying a sword and shield.

Powers

Innocence Percival's innocence and purity of heart grant him the ability to achieve the Grail, but also lead to his initial failure to do so.

Accounts

Percival, a young knight, sets out on a quest to find the Grail, but fails to achieve it due to his lack of understanding.

Percival is portrayed as a foolish knight who achieves the Grail through his innocence and purity of heart.

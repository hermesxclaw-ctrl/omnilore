Who It Is

In the shadowed halls of Duelist Kingdom, a flamboyant industrialist with a silver tongue and a mind-reading eye holds the fate of souls in his hands. Maximillion Pegasus, creator of the world's most popular card game, is both a visionary and a villain, a man whose genius is matched only by his grief. His story is a tapestry of ambition, loss, and the dangerous allure of power.

Pegasus is flamboyant, theatrical, and charismatic, with a love for games and entertainment. He is highly intelligent and cunning, often using psychological manipulation to achieve his goals. He is also deeply emotional, driven by the loss of his beloved wife, Cecelia, which motivates his quest to bring her back using the Millennium Items. Despite his villainous actions, he shows moments of kindness and respect for worthy opponents.

Powers
Millennium Eye

One of the seven Millennium Items, the Eye allows Pegasus to read the minds of others, see their thoughts, and even communicate telepathically. It also grants him the ability to see through illusions and perceive the true nature of things.

Limit: The Eye cannot read the minds of those who are protected by other Millennium Items, and it can be countered by techniques like the 'Mind Shuffle' used by Yugi and Dark Yugi. The Eye also has a physical cost, as it replaced his left eye.

Shadow Games

As a wielder of a Millennium Item, Pegasus can initiate Shadow Games, which are high-stakes duels where the loser suffers a penalty, often losing their soul. He uses this to steal souls of duelists he defeats.

Limit: Shadow Games require a Millennium Item and the consent of both participants. The penalty is determined by the rules of the game.

Toon Monsters

Pegasus uses Toon Monsters, a special type of Duel Monsters cards that are cartoonish and can attack directly if the player controls 'Toon World'. This gives him a unique playstyle.

Limit: Toon Monsters are vulnerable to cards that can destroy them, and they cannot attack on the turn they are summoned unless certain conditions are met.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Pegasus is a tall, slender man with long silver hair and a youthful appearance despite being in his 30s. He has a distinctive crescent moon-shaped mark on his forehead, which is actually the Millennium Eye. He often wears a white suit with a high collar and a red tie, and carries a wine glass. His left eye is covered by his hair, but when revealed, it shows the Millennium Eye, which glows.

Symbols — The Millennium Eye, one of the seven Millennium Items, is his primary symbol. It allows him to read minds and see the thoughts of others. He also uses the Toon World cards, which are characterized by their cartoonish appearance, reflecting his playful yet manipulative nature.

Domains — Pegasus resides in Duelist Kingdom, a private island he owns for hosting his tournament. He also has a castle on the island, which serves as his base of operations. He is often seen in high-society settings, reflecting his wealth and status as a game designer and CEO of Industrial Illusions.

Accounts I
The Duelist Kingdom TournamentYu-Gi-Oh! manga and anime

1990s-2000s

Pegasus invites top duelists to his island for a tournament, with the prize being the chance to duel him. However, his true motive is to steal the souls of the losers to power his plan to resurrect his dead wife. He defeats Yugi in a video-taped duel, stealing his grandfather's soul, forcing Yugi to enter the tournament. After a series of duels, Yugi and Dark Yugi face Pegasus in a Shadow Game, where they use the Mind Shuffle to counter his mind-reading. With the help of his friends, Yugi wins, and Pegasus is forced to release the stolen souls. Pegasus loses the duel and is forced to release the souls he stole. He also loses the Millennium Eye, which is taken by Yami Bakura. This event sets the stage for the Battle City arc.

Accounts II
The Origin of the Millennium EyeYu-Gi-Oh! manga

1990s-2000s

Pegasus's backstory is revealed: he was a talented game designer who met a woman named Cecelia, who became his wife. She died shortly after their marriage, and Pegasus, in his grief, sought to bring her back. He obtained the Millennium Eye from the tomb of an ancient pharaoh, and it granted him the power to see into the spirit world, where he saw Cecelia's soul. This motivated him to collect the other Millennium Items to perform a ritual to resurrect her. Pegasus's obsession with resurrecting Cecelia drives his actions throughout the series.

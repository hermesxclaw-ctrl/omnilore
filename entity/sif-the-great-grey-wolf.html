Who It Is

Sif, the Great Grey Wolf, is a fascinating and complex entity that continues to captivate audiences with its loyalty and protection. Its role in the Dark Souls franchise is multifaceted, reflecting its importance as a guardian and companion.

Sif is depicted as a fiercely loyal and protective entity, willing to defend its master's grave and the Covenant of Artorias at all costs. Its behavior is driven by a strong sense of duty and loyalty, reflecting its role as a companion and guardian.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Sif, the Great Grey Wolf, is a massive grey wolf with a regal presence, standing guard over the grave of its master, Knight Artorias the Abysswalker. Its size and coloration are reminiscent of the grey wolves found in Norse mythology. The entity's appearance is a blend of natural and supernatural elements, reflecting its role as a guardian and companion.

Symbols — The entity is associated with the Covenant of Artorias, a ring that allows its wearer to traverse the Abyss. The ring is a symbol of the entity's loyalty and dedication to its master. The entity's iconography is also tied to the concept of the 'original wolf' and the 'swan wolf' found in Tuvan shamanic traditions.

Domains — The entity is found in the Dark Souls universe, specifically in the area surrounding the grave of Knight Artorias the Abysswalker. Its domain is one of protection and loyalty, reflecting its role as a guardian and companion.

Who It Is

The Major (whose real name is never revealed, only the alias 'Montana Max') is the leader of Millennium — the secret Nazi organization that survived World War II by hiding in South America, rebuilding their forces with vampire supersoldiers and advanced technology. He is the primary antagonist of the Hellsing series, and he is perhaps the most terrifying villain in all of manga because he is, at his core, completely human — and completely insane. The Major appears as a grotesquely obese man in a Nazi officer's uniform, with a jutting jaw, a wide grin, and small, beady eyes that gleam with fanatical joy. He is eventually revealed to be a disembodied brain in a mechanical body, having long since abandoned any pretense of normal humanity. But the Major's monstrosity is not physical — it is philosophical. The Major wages war not for territory, resources, or ideology. He wages war because he LOVES war. He explicitly states that he does not care who wins or loses — he simply wants the experience of total war, of battle on a grand scale, of seeing London burn and hearing the screams of millions. His speech before the final battle is one of the most chilling villain monologues in fiction: a declaration that he fights not for the Fatherland, not for Nazism, not for revenge, but for the pure, transcendent joy of conflict. The Major's power is not physical — he is a strategist, a manipulator, and a fanatic. He commands an army of vampire supersoldiers, advanced technology, and brilliant but twisted subordinates (including the Doctor, who created the vampire soldiers). He orchestrates the invasion of London, turning the city into a war zone, simply because he wants to see Alucard fight at full power. The Major represents the ultimate expression of war as an end in itself — the nihilistic belief that destruction is its own justification, that the process of killing and dying is the most meaningful experience existence can offer. He is the monster who wears a human face, the madman whose sanity is terrifying precisely because it is channeled into pure, focused malevolence. He is not a vampire, not a demon, not a god — he is just a man who chose evil, and that makes him more frightening than any supernatural horror.

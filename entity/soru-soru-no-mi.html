Who It Is

The Soul-Soul Fruit, a Paramecia that grants the user the ability to manipulate souls — both their own and those of others. The user can extract souls from living beings (shortening their lifespan), instill souls into inanimate objects to create 'Homies' (living constructs), and absorb the souls of the deceased to augment their own power. The fruit's awakened state gives the user dominion over all souls in their territory.

Powers

Soul extraction (shortens lifespan of targets)

Homie creation (animate objects with soul fragments)

Prometheus (sun Homie — fire creation)

Zeus (cloud Homie — weather control)

Napoleon (sword Homie morphing)

Healing via lifespan expenditure

Archive Dossier — Identity

Gork and Mork

Pronunciation Gork: /ɡɔːrk/ (rhymes with 'fork'); Mork: /mɔːrk/ (rhymes with 'fork' or 'talk')

Known As

The Ork GodsThe Twin Gods of the OrksGork and Mork (as a pair)Gork (the brutal and cunning)Mork (the cunning and brutal)

Etymology

The names 'Gork' and 'Mork' are likely onomatopoeic or playful, evoking the sounds of violence ('gork' and 'mork' as percussive, guttural words). They may also be a play on 'gob' or 'goblin' and 'ork', but no official etymology has been given by Games Workshop.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Games Workshop for the Warhammer 40,000 and Warhammer Fantasy settings. They are a fictional creation and are not based on any real-world mythology.

Disputed Identifications

There is a long-standing fan debate about whether Gork and Mork are actually the same entity viewed differently, or if they are distinct gods. In Warhammer Fantasy, they are sometimes referred to as 'Gork and Mork' as well, and it is unclear if they are the same deities in both settings. Some fan theories suggest they are the Ork gods of the Warp, but this is not explicitly confirmed in canon.

Archive Dossier — Approach

In the grim darkness of the 41st millennium, the Orks wage an eternal, joyful war—and at the heart of their green-skinned savagery lurk two gods who are, by any rational measure, the same deity. Gork and Mork, the brutal and cunning, the cunning and brutal, embody the Orkish paradox: a race that fights for the sheer love of it, and whose belief literally shapes reality. They are the ultimate expression of Ork nature, and their presence is felt in every bellowed WAAAGH! and every clang of a crude choppa.

Who It Is

Gork and Mork are the twin gods of the Ork species — the only gods the Orks acknowledge and the only gods that matter to them. They are not true Chaos Gods in the sense of the Dark Four (Khorne, Tzeentch, Nurgle, Slaanesh) but warp entities born from and sustained by the Orks' collective psychic field, known as the WAAAGH! The distinction between Gork and Mork is a matter of intense philosophical debate among Ork weirdboyz (their shamans), who cannot decide which god is which. Gork is 'brutally cunning' — he uses brute force in clever ways. Mork is 'cunningly brutal' — he uses cleverness in brutal ways. Most Orks agree that the difference matters deeply, but no Ork can actually articulate what the difference is. The power of Gork and Mork is real, however. Orks who fight with exceptional ferocity and success are said to be 'blessed by Gork and Mork,' and Ork weirdboyz channel their gods' power to unleash psychic attacks, create 'Fists of Gork,' and cause the sky to rain squigs. The Ork belief in their gods is so strong that it literally warps reality: if enough Orks believe that a rickety vehicle painted red will go faster, IT WILL GO FASTER, because the WAAAGH! field makes their belief manifest. This phenomenon — the 'Ork psychic gestalt' — is one of the most unique elements of the Warhammer 40,000 universe. Gork and Mork represent the pure, simple, terrifying joy of the Ork way of life. They are not gods of evil or destruction in the human sense — they are gods of FUN. The Ork philosophy is beautifully simple: fighting is the best thing ever. Winning is even better. Getting bigger so you can fight bigger things is the meaning of existence. Gork and Mork are the proof that even in the grim darkness of the 41st millennium, someone is having a wonderful time — specifically, the probably-illiterate fungus-men who just want a good scrap.

Archive Dossier — Description

Appearance

Gork and Mork are rarely depicted directly. In Ork iconography, they are shown as massive, green-skinned Orks with exaggerated features, often wielding huge weapons. Gork is typically portrayed as more brutish and muscular, with a heavy brow and a snarling expression, while Mork is depicted as more cunning, with a sly grin and a slightly leaner build. In the Warhammer 40,000 setting, they are sometimes shown as massive, shadowy figures in the Warp, but their exact appearance is vague and left to interpretation.

Symbols & Iconography

The most common symbols associated with Gork and Mork are the Ork glyphs for 'war' and 'destruction'. Ork banners often feature crude depictions of the twin gods, sometimes as two heads facing opposite directions, or as a single two-faced head. Ork vehicles and equipment are often adorned with icons of Gork and Mork, and Ork war cries often invoke their names.

Behaviour & Personality

Gork and Mork embody the Ork psyche. Gork is the brutal and cunning, representing the raw, violent, and direct approach to war. Mork is the cunning and brutal, representing the sneaky, tactical, and underhanded approach. In Ork mythology, they are constantly fighting each other, and their battles shape the Ork race. They are also depicted as being intensely interested in the wars of their followers, and they grant blessings to Orks who fight well.

Domains & Habitats

Gork and Mork reside in the Warp, the psychic dimension of the Warhammer 40,000 universe. They are Warp entities that are created and sustained by the collective psychic belief of the Ork race. They are not tied to any physical location, but their influence is strongest where Orks are present.

Version Differences

In Warhammer Fantasy, Gork and Mork are also the gods of the Orcs and Goblins, and they are depicted similarly. However, in that setting, they are not explicitly Warp entities; they are more akin to gods of the world. The distinction between the two settings is not always clear, and Games Workshop has not explicitly confirmed whether they are the same entities in both universes. Some fan theories suggest that the Orks of Warhammer 40,000 are the same as the Orcs of Warhammer Fantasy, but this is not confirmed.

Archive Dossier — Powers

Warp Entity Existence

Source Version Warhammer 40,000 (all editions)

Description

Gork and Mork are Warp entities, meaning they are manifestations of the psychic energies of the Ork race. They are sustained by Ork belief and are effectively immortal as long as Orks exist.

Limitations

They cannot physically manifest in the material universe except through the actions of their followers or in the Warp.

Contradictions

Some sources imply they are gods of the Warp, while others suggest they are more like ancestral spirits. No definitive statement has been made.

Blessings and Curses

Source Version Warhammer 40,000 and Warhammer Fantasy

Description

Gork and Mork are said to bless Orks who perform great acts of violence or cunning, granting them supernatural strength, luck, or resilience. Conversely, they may curse Orks who act cowardly or dishonorably (in the Ork sense).

Limitations

The mechanics of these blessings are not detailed in the game rules; they are mainly background lore.

Contradictions

None known.

WAAAGH! Energy

Source Version Warhammer 40,000

Description

The collective psychic energy of the Orks, known as the WAAAGH!, is channeled by Gork and Mork. This energy can manifest as weirdboyz' psychic powers, and in extreme cases, it can cause reality to bend to the Orks' belief, such as making red vehicles faster.

Limitations

The WAAAGH! energy is a background element and is not fully explained in the rules.

Contradictions

The exact nature of the WAAAGH! and its connection to Gork and Mork is not universally agreed upon in the lore.

Archive Dossier — Relationships

Orks

Type worshipperSource Codex: Orks

Description

All Orks believe in Gork and Mork, and their belief sustains the gods. Orks pray to them for victory, and their war cries often invoke their names.

Version Note

In both Warhammer 40,000 and Warhammer Fantasy, Orks/Orcs share this worship.

Conflicting Accounts

None known.

Gork and Mork (each other)

Type rivalSource Codex: Orks

Description

Gork and Mork are eternal rivals, constantly fighting each other. Their rivalry is a fundamental aspect of their nature and mirrors the Ork love of conflict.

Version Note

This is consistent across all sources.

Conflicting Accounts

Some Ork myths claim they are brothers, while others say they are two aspects of the same being.

The Chaos Gods

Type hostileSource Codex: Orks, various novels

Description

Gork and Mork are not aligned with the Chaos Gods, and they are often seen as rivals. Orks are generally resistant to Chaos corruption, and their gods help protect them from it.

Version Note

In Warhammer 40,000, the Chaos Gods are separate entities, and Orks are not easily corrupted.

Conflicting Accounts

Some sources suggest that Gork and Mork are powerful enough to be considered on par with the Chaos Gods, but this is not confirmed.

The Old Ones

Type creator (indirect)Source Various sources

Description

The Old Ones created the Orks, but they did not create Gork and Mork. The gods may have emerged from the Orks' psychic potential.

Version Note

This is specific to Warhammer 40,000.

Conflicting Accounts

It is unclear if Gork and Mork existed before the Orks or if they were born from Ork belief.

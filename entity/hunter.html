Who It Is

A reptilian bio-weapon created by combining human DNA with that of reptiles and amphibians using the T-Virus. Hunters are agile, powerful creatures with sharp claws capable of decapitating their prey. They come in various types including Hunter Alpha, Hunter Gamma (frog-like with digestive attacks), and Hunter Beta. Highly intelligent and deadly in groups.

Powers

Sharp claws, agility, decapitation attack, leaping

Raven-black hair on snow-white skin, red blood upon the drift — that is the man I will love.

Who It Is

Deirdre is at the center of Irish mythology's most tragic love story. Cathbad prophesied she would bring destruction to Ulster. She fell in love with Naoise after seeing a raven drinking blood on snow. They fled to Scotland but were lured back by Conchobar's treachery. After Naoise was killed, Deirdre dashed her head on a stone rather than live without him.

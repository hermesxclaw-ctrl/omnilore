Who It Is

In the autumn of 2016, a long-striding bay colt with a diamond on his forehead swept through Japan's most storied races, beating the nation's best older horses as a three-year-old and announcing himself as a classic winner of rare promise. Satono Diamond, a son of the legendary Deep Impact, was bred for greatness and delivered it in a season that still echoes in Japanese racing lore.

Described by trainer Yasutoshi Ikee as 'very stable mentally' and able to handle any kind of race. Jockey Christophe Lemaire praised him as 'such an easy ride' and noted his big heart. He was known for his long stride and late acceleration, though he could be keen in the early stages.

Racing ability

Top-class middle-distance and long-distance turf runner, with a strong finishing burst. Won from 1800m to 2500m, placed in 3000m and 3200m races.

Limit: Failed to perform on soft ground in France (Prix Foy) and against elite older horses in 2017-2018.

Stamina

Effective at classic distances, winning the Kikuka Sho (3000m) and Arima Kinen (2500m).

Limit: Not effective in the 3200m Tenno Sho (Spring) in 2017, finishing third.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Bay horse with a diamond-shaped white star. No other markings are recorded in the source material.

Symbols — The diamond star is the namesake and a natural symbol.

Domains — Trained at Ritto Training Center in Japan, with a European campaign in France in 2017.

Accounts I
2016 Classic SeasonWikipedia (provided)

2016

After winning the Kisaragi Sho, Satono Diamond finished third in the Satsuki Sho and second in the Tokyo Yushun. In autumn, he won the Kobe Shimbun Hai, Kikuka Sho, and Arima Kinen, defeating older horses and securing the JRA Award for Best Three-Year-Old Colt. Established Satono Diamond as a top-class stayer and classic winner.

Accounts II
European CampaignWikipedia (provided)

2017

Sent to France for the Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe, he finished fifth in the Prix Foy and fifteenth in the Arc, failing to adapt to European conditions. Returned to Japan and never regained his best form.

2018 Final SeasonWikipedia (provided)

2018

Won the Kyoto Daishoten, but was unplaced in the Osaka Hai, Takarazuka Kinen, Japan Cup, and Arima Kinen, ending his career with eight wins from 18 starts. Retired to stud at Shadai Stallion Station.

Who It Is

One of the many SCP-001 proposals — the Gate Guardian is a massive entity standing 100 meters tall, resembling a biblical Ophanim (a wheel within a wheel covered in eyes) or a Seraphim with countless wings and eyes. It stands at a gate in what appears to be the Garden of Eden, guarding the Tree of Life, and annihilates any who approach. The Guardian displays power beyond any known anomaly, including stopping time, nullifying all other SCP abilities, and speaking in divine script that kills those who read it.

Form

Attested: SCP-001 is described as a humanoid entity approximately seven hundred cubits tall (roughly 320 meters or about 1,000 feet), composed of or enveloped in intense, sun-like fire. Observers report numerous luminous, wing-like appendages emerging from its shoulders, back, temples, ankles, and wrists. The exact number of wings cannot be reliably counted; estimates range from two to 108, with four being the most commonly reported. The figure stands upright before a massive gate or portal, its features largely obscured by radiance. The entity’s skin or body is often implied to be incandescent or orange, but this is more explicit in fan descriptions than in the original article. Reconstructed: Some fan sources depict more anthropomorphic facial features and a clearly defined male form, but these are extrapolations from the biblical angel archetype rather than explicit canonical detail.

Flaming Sword (SCP-001-2) and Existence Erasure Attested: SCP-001 wields a weapon—described as a sword or knife—that emits flames at temperatures comparable to or exceeding the sun. Any object, person, or device crossing within approximately one kilometer of the entity and refusing its command to turn back is instantaneously struck and obliterated. Experimental data (Experiments A–E) show that Class-D personnel, robots, drones, missiles, and even multi-warhead ICBMs are destroyed without leaving debris, suggesting total disintegration or erasure. Fan sources characterize this as ‘existence erasure,’ implying that targets are removed from reality rather than merely incinerated. Primary: SCP-001 – Dr Clef’s Proposal (Addendum 001-a: Experimentation re: SCP-001-2’s effective kill range). Secondary: SCP: Simple English Wiki; SCP Foundation Wikia laconic summary; SCP Database Wiki. Attested: The effective kill range appears to be approximately 1 km; beyond that distance, SCP-001 does not actively attack. It does not pursue targets that retreat. The weapon is only used against those who approach or attack the gate or entity. Unknown: Whether SCP-001-2 can affect entities beyond the immediate vicinity, or whether it has non-lethal modes, is not documented. Some fan interpretations treat SCP-001-2 as capable of affecting higher-dimensional entities or SCPs across vast distances, while the original experiments only demonstrate local effects. The exact mechanism—thermal destruction vs ontological erasure—is debated.

Absolute Immortality and Invulnerability Attested: No experiment or weapon deployed by the SCP Foundation has harmed SCP-001. Missiles and drones are destroyed before reaching it, and SCP-076 (Able) and SCP-073 (Cain) are unable to approach without catastrophic consequences. Fan sources extrapolate this into ‘absolute immortality’ and ‘nigh-absolute invulnerability,’ suggesting that SCP-001 cannot be killed or damaged by any known means. Primary: SCP-001 – Dr Clef’s Proposal (Experiments F and G involving SCP-076 and SCP-073). Secondary: SCP Database Wiki; Heroes Wiki power listings. Attested: The Foundation has not tested every conceivable anomalous weapon; invulnerability is inferred from limited but extreme tests. Unknown: Whether SCP-001 could be affected by entities or forces beyond the SCP Foundation’s reach (e.g., other divine beings) is not addressed. Some tales hint at eschatological scenarios where SCP-001 moves or acts in concert with other forces, implying that it may be subject to a larger cosmic order rather than truly ‘absolute.’

Telepathy and Memory Manipulation Attested (interpretive): Multiple accounts mention individuals encountering SCP-001, receiving the command “FORGET,” and subsequently losing all memory of the encounter. This suggests a telepathic or memetic effect that can erase or suppress memories. Fan sources explicitly list telepathy, mind control, and memory manipulation as powers, and describe SCP-001 as communicating directly into observers’ minds. Primary: SCP-001 – Dr Clef’s Proposal (references to prior human encounters and the command “FORGET”). Secondary: SCP Database Wiki biography; Heroes Wiki powers list. Unknown: The range, precision, and permanence of memory manipulation are not quantified. It appears to be used selectively, primarily to enforce secrecy about the gate. Some readers interpret the memory loss as psychological trauma or narrative device rather than literal anomalous mind control. The original article is ambiguous, while fan sources treat it as a concrete power.

Divine or Angelic Physiology (Multiple Wings, Radiance, Higher-Dimensional Presence) Attested: SCP-001 possesses numerous luminous wings and radiates intense light and heat, consistent with traditional depictions of seraphim or archangels. Fan sources further attribute ‘greater divinity,’ ‘higher-dimensional manipulation,’ and ‘eldritch physiology,’ framing SCP-001 as a being that exists partially outside conventional spacetime and whose true form may be incomprehensible. Primary: SCP-001 – Dr Clef’s Proposal (description of wings and radiance). Secondary: Grokipedia thematic analysis; Heroes Wiki and SCP Database Wiki power lists. Attested: Observers cannot fully resolve the number or structure of wings, suggesting perceptual limits. Unknown: The extent of higher-dimensional manipulation is speculative; the original text does not describe explicit reality-bending feats beyond annihilation and invulnerability. Some interpretations treat SCP-001 as a literal biblical angel; others see it as an SCP-style reality bender whose ‘angelic’ appearance is shaped by human religious expectations. These views imply different underlying physiologies.

Apocalyptic Trigger and PATMOS XK-Class Scenario Attested: SCP-001 is explicitly linked to a potential PATMOS XK-class end-of-the-world scenario. The Foundation maintains Emergency Procedure PATMOS, decoding algorithms, and religiously themed precautions (holy water, scriptures, blessed symbols) in case SCP-001 becomes active. An addendum describes a message from Site-0 indicating that SCP-001 has moved and that the Rapture is beginning, referencing several SCPs with religious significance. This message is later determined to originate from the future, implying that SCP-001’s movement will herald a catastrophic eschatological event. Primary: SCP-001 – Dr Clef’s Proposal (Addendum 001-aa and PATMOS orders). Secondary: SCP Foundation Wikia laconic summary; Grokipedia analysis. Attested: SCP-001 remains idle in the present; the apocalyptic scenario is conditional on future activation. Unknown: The exact mechanics of the Rapture-like event, the role of other SCPs, and whether human actions can influence the outcome are not specified. Some readers treat the future message as a narrative flourish rather than a literal prophecy; others integrate it into a broader SCP timeline where SCP-001’s movement is inevitable. The degree of inevitability vs contingency is debated.

Inspirational Command (“PREPARE”) and Institutional Genesis Attested: In a key narrative, the man who becomes the SCP Foundation’s Founder encounters SCP-001. Unlike previous encounters, the entity does not say “FORGET” but instead commands “PREPARE.” This single word prompts the Founder to create the SCP Foundation as a global organization dedicated to securing, containing, and protecting anomalous entities and preparing for SCP-001’s eventual activation. Fan sources treat this as a form of ‘power bestowal’ or ‘supreme voice,’ where SCP-001’s utterance catalyzes institutional and historical change. Primary: SCP-001 – Dr Clef’s Proposal (Founder encounter). Secondary: SCP Database Wiki biography; Heroes Wiki character summary. Attested: SCP-001 does not provide detailed instructions; the Founder interprets and builds upon the command. Unknown: Whether SCP-001 foresaw the exact form of the Foundation or merely intended a general preparation is unclear. Some interpretations see the Foundation as acting against SCP-001’s will by containing other anomalies; others view it as an instrument of SCP-001’s or a higher divine plan. The alignment between SCP-001 and the Foundation is contested.

Accounts

The core SCP-001 article presents SCP-001 as a colossal, fiery humanoid with multiple wings, standing before a gate near the Tigris and Euphrates rivers. The SCP Foundation designates it SCP-001 and classifies it as Euclid/Keter. Because containment is impossible, the Foundation establishes Site-0 at a safe distance to monitor the entity continuously. The article details special containment procedures focused on observation and emergency readiness, then describes a series of experiments designed to test SCP-001-2’s kill range and capabilities. Each experiment—sending a Class-D, a robot, multiple drones, missiles, and powerful SCPs like SCP-076 and SCP-073—ends in catastrophic destruction of the approaching agents or weapons, confirming SCP-001’s lethal perimeter and invulnerability. The narrative culminates in an addendum about Emergency Procedure PATMOS and a future message indicating that SCP-001 has moved and that a Rapture-like event is beginning, tying the entity to apocalyptic scenarios.

According to the proposal and later fan elaborations, a man later known as Aaron Siegel—the Founder or first Administrator of the SCP Foundation—encounters SCP-001 during an expedition near the Tigris and Euphrates. Unlike earlier human encounters, where SCP-001 commands witnesses to ‘FORGET,’ the entity instead speaks a single word: ‘PREPARE.’ This encounter profoundly affects the Founder, who interprets the command as a directive to ready humanity for future anomalous threats and the eventual activation of SCP-001. He subsequently establishes the SCP Foundation, designing its mission and structure around securing, containing, and protecting anomalies and preparing for eschatological scenarios.

The Foundation conducts a series of experiments to determine the effective kill range and capabilities of SCP-001’s flaming weapon, SCP-001-2. Experiment A sends a Class-D to approach SCP-001; when the subject refuses to return, he is terminated by Foundation personnel, and SCP-001 responds by annihilating the research team. Experiment B uses a remote-operated robot, which is destroyed upon crossing the perimeter. Experiment C deploys 100 drones from multiple angles; SCP-001 strikes in all directions simultaneously, destroying them. Experiment D fires a wire-guided missile from 3 km away; it is destroyed at the 1 km mark. Experiment E launches a multi-warhead ICBM from a submarine; all warheads are destroyed before impact. Experiments F and G involve SCP-076 and SCP-073, both of whom are prevented from approaching or cause catastrophic reactions, reinforcing SCP-001’s supremacy.

The SCP-001 article introduces Emergency Procedure PATMOS, a series of orders to be executed if SCP-001 becomes active. These procedures include maintaining good relations with Abrahamic faiths, keeping blessed religious items and scriptures on hand, and designating non-Abrahamic secondary operatives in case of a premillennial Rapture scenario. An addendum describes a message received from Site-0 stating that SCP-001 has moved and that the Rapture is beginning, referencing several SCPs with religious significance (including SCP-073 and SCP-076). Investigation reveals that the message originates from the future, implying that SCP-001’s movement will eventually trigger an apocalyptic event. The Foundation treats this as both a warning and a confirmation of PATMOS’s relevance.

Content Notes

The SCP-001 experiments involve sending Class-D personnel, robots, drones, missiles, and powerful SCPs toward SCP-001, resulting in their instantaneous destruction by SCP-001-2. The narrative emphasizes that targets are obliterated without remains, suggesting total erasure. This repeated use of expendable human subjects and the clinical tone of their deaths highlight the Foundation’s utilitarian ethics and the extreme lethality of SCP-001.

Within SCP meta-discussion, these experiments are often cited as examples of the Foundation’s morally ambiguous practices and the horror of treating human lives as disposable data points. The annihilation motif also reinforces SCP-001’s role as an uncompromising divine or cosmic enforcer.

SCP-001 – Dr Clef’s Proposal (Addendum 001-a); SCP: Simple English Wiki experiment summaries.

Emergency Procedure PATMOS and the future message from Site-0 explicitly reference a Rapture-like event, suggesting that SCP-001’s movement will herald mass death or transformation on a global scale. The Foundation’s instructions to maintain religious items and designate non-Abrahamic backups in case believers are taken in a premillennial Rapture foreground a scenario in which billions may die or be removed from the world.

Analysts note that the proposal appropriates Christian eschatology and blends it with SCP’s bureaucratic horror, creating a darkly satirical vision of institutions preparing for divine judgment. The Rapture motif can be disturbing for readers with religious trauma or anxiety about end-times narratives.

SCP-001 – Dr Clef’s Proposal (Addendum 001-aa); SCP Foundation Wikia laconic summary.

Experiment A involves ordering a Class-D to approach SCP-001 as closely as possible; when the subject refuses to return, he is terminated by Foundation personnel. The subsequent destruction of the research team by SCP-001 underscores the moral hazard of using expendable humans in lethal tests. The Foundation’s reliance on Class-D personnel—often convicted criminals or marginalized individuals—raises questions about coercion, consent, and institutional abuse.

SCP scholarship frequently critiques the Foundation’s treatment of Class-Ds as a metaphor for real-world exploitation in scientific and military contexts. The Gate Guardian proposal exemplifies this dynamic by juxtaposing divine judgment with human instrumentalization.

SCP-001 – Dr Clef’s Proposal (Experiment A); SCP: Simple English Wiki.

The proposal instructs Foundation personnel to maintain good relations with Abrahamic faiths and to keep blessed religious items on hand, effectively instrumentalizing religious traditions for bureaucratic survival. This can be read as a form of appropriation or reduction of complex faiths to tools in a technocratic emergency plan.

Commentators have noted that SCP-001’s religious framing may both critique and inadvertently reinforce certain hierarchies, privileging Abrahamic faiths in global crisis planning. The dark undertone lies in treating religion as a contingency resource rather than a lived belief system.

What We Don't Know

The original SCP-001 article is intentionally ambiguous about SCP-001’s true nature, identity, and motives; any attempt to resolve these definitively would be speculative. Precise dates for some experiments and fan adaptations are not clearly documented. The YouTube URL provided here is representative; the exact video ID should be verified. Relationships with SCP-682 and SCP-343 are largely fan constructions and lack primary textual confirmation. Non-English discussions, localized adaptations, and deeper community debates beyond major wikis were not exhaustively surveyed. This dossier focuses on Dr Clef’s Gate Guardian proposal and its most visible receptions up to 2026-07-30, without attempting to integrate every SCP-001 proposal or all extended Ouroboros and Scarlet King storylines.

A way to hold it in your mind

A thousand-foot angel of living fire stands where the Tigris meets the Euphrates, silent but watching—its flaming sword erasing missiles, monsters, and men alike the instant they cross an invisible line. The SCP Foundation was built because, once, it spoke a single word: PREPARE.

Who It Is

A thousand-foot angel of living fire stands where the Tigris meets the Euphrates, silent but watching—its flaming sword erasing missiles, monsters, and men alike the instant they cross an invisible line. The SCP Foundation was built because, once, it spoke a single word: PREPARE.

Attested: SCP-001 is largely static, standing vigil before the gate and rarely moving. It responds to approach attempts with a single imperative—typically “FORGET” or “LEAVE”—and annihilates those who disobey via SCP-001-2. It does not engage in conversation beyond brief commands and does not pursue fleeing targets. Its behavior suggests absolute focus on guarding the gate and enforcing a fixed perimeter kill zone of approximately one kilometer. Powers
Flaming Sword (SCP-001-2) and Existence Erasure

Attested: SCP-001 wields a weapon—described as a sword or knife—that emits flames at temperatures comparable to or exceeding the sun. Any object, person, or device crossing within approximately one kilometer of the entity and refusing its command to turn back is instantaneously struck and obliterated. Experimental data (Experiments A–E) show that Class-D personnel, robots, drones, missiles, and even multi-warhead ICBMs are destroyed without leaving debris, suggesting total disintegration or erasure. Fan sources characterize this as ‘existence erasure,’ implying that targets are removed from reality rather than merely incinerated.

Limit: Attested: The effective kill range appears to be approximately 1 km; beyond that distance, SCP-001 does not actively attack. It does not pursue targets that retreat. The weapon is only used against those who approach or attack the gate or entity. Unknown: Whether SCP-001-2 can affect entities beyond the immediate vicinity, or whether it has non-lethal modes, is not documented.

Absolute Immortality and Invulnerability

Attested: No experiment or weapon deployed by the SCP Foundation has harmed SCP-001. Missiles and drones are destroyed before reaching it, and SCP-076 (Able) and SCP-073 (Cain) are unable to approach without catastrophic consequences. Fan sources extrapolate this into ‘absolute immortality’ and ‘nigh-absolute invulnerability,’ suggesting that SCP-001 cannot be killed or damaged by any known means.

Limit: Attested: The Foundation has not tested every conceivable anomalous weapon; invulnerability is inferred from limited but extreme tests. Unknown: Whether SCP-001 could be affected by entities or forces beyond the SCP Foundation’s reach (e.g., other divine beings) is not addressed.

Telepathy and Memory Manipulation

Attested (interpretive): Multiple accounts mention individuals encountering SCP-001, receiving the command “FORGET,” and subsequently losing all memory of the encounter. This suggests a telepathic or memetic effect that can erase or suppress memories. Fan sources explicitly list telepathy, mind control, and memory manipulation as powers, and describe SCP-001 as communicating directly into observers’ minds.

Limit: Unknown: The range, precision, and permanence of memory manipulation are not quantified. It appears to be used selectively, primarily to enforce secrecy about the gate.

Divine or Angelic Physiology (Multiple Wings, Radiance, Higher-Dimensional Presence)

Attested: SCP-001 possesses numerous luminous wings and radiates intense light and heat, consistent with traditional depictions of seraphim or archangels. Fan sources further attribute ‘greater divinity,’ ‘higher-dimensional manipulation,’ and ‘eldritch physiology,’ framing SCP-001 as a being that exists partially outside conventional spacetime and whose true form may be incomprehensible.

Limit: Attested: Observers cannot fully resolve the number or structure of wings, suggesting perceptual limits. Unknown: The extent of higher-dimensional manipulation is speculative; the original text does not describe explicit reality-bending feats beyond annihilation and invulnerability.

Apocalyptic Trigger and PATMOS XK-Class Scenario

Attested: SCP-001 is explicitly linked to a potential PATMOS XK-class end-of-the-world scenario. The Foundation maintains Emergency Procedure PATMOS, decoding algorithms, and religiously themed precautions (holy water, scriptures, blessed symbols) in case SCP-001 becomes active. An addendum describes a message from Site-0 indicating that SCP-001 has moved and that the Rapture is beginning, referencing several SCPs with religious significance. This message is later determined to originate from the future, implying that SCP-001’s movement will herald a catastrophic eschatological event.

Limit: Attested: SCP-001 remains idle in the present; the apocalyptic scenario is conditional on future activation. Unknown: The exact mechanics of the Rapture-like event, the role of other SCPs, and whether human actions can influence the outcome are not specified.

Inspirational Command (“PREPARE”) and Institutional Genesis

Attested: In a key narrative, the man who becomes the SCP Foundation’s Founder encounters SCP-001. Unlike previous encounters, the entity does not say “FORGET” but instead commands “PREPARE.” This single word prompts the Founder to create the SCP Foundation as a global organization dedicated to securing, containing, and protecting anomalous entities and preparing for SCP-001’s eventual activation. Fan sources treat this as a form of ‘power bestowal’ or ‘supreme voice,’ where SCP-001’s utterance catalyzes institutional and historical change.

Limit: Attested: SCP-001 does not provide detailed instructions; the Founder interprets and builds upon the command. Unknown: Whether SCP-001 foresaw the exact form of the Foundation or merely intended a general preparation is unclear.

Accounts I
The Gate Guardian (SCP-001 – Dr Clef’s Proposal)SCP-001 – Dr Clef’s Proposal, SCP Foundation Wiki

2008–present (ongoing influence)

The core SCP-001 article presents SCP-001 as a colossal, fiery humanoid with multiple wings, standing before a gate near the Tigris and Euphrates rivers. The SCP Foundation designates it SCP-001 and classifies it as Euclid/Keter. Because containment is impossible, the Foundation establishes Site-0 at a safe distance to monitor the entity continuously. The article details special containment procedures focused on observation and emergency readiness, then describes a series of experiments designed to test SCP-001-2’s kill range and capabilities. Each experiment—sending a Class-D, a robot, multiple drones, missiles, and powerful SCPs like SCP-076 and SCP-073—ends in catastrophic destruction of the approaching agents or weapons, confirming SCP-001’s lethal perimeter and invulnerability. The narrative culminates in an addendum about Emergency Procedure PATMOS and a future message indicating that SCP-001 has moved and that a Rapture-like event is beginning, tying the entity to apocalyptic scenarios. PREPARE – The Founder’s EncounterImplied narrative within SCP-001 – Dr Clef’s Proposal; expanded in fan wikis (SCP Database Wiki, Heroes Wiki)

Retrospective narrative, set prior to the formal founding of the SCP Foundation

According to the proposal and later fan elaborations, a man later known as Aaron Siegel—the Founder or first Administrator of the SCP Foundation—encounters SCP-001 during an expedition near the Tigris and Euphrates. Unlike earlier human encounters, where SCP-001 commands witnesses to ‘FORGET,’ the entity instead speaks a single word: ‘PREPARE.’ This encounter profoundly affects the Founder, who interprets the command as a directive to ready humanity for future anomalous threats and the eventual activation of SCP-001. He subsequently establishes the SCP Foundation, designing its mission and structure around securing, containing, and protecting anomalies and preparing for eschatological scenarios. The SCP Foundation’s existence is retroactively framed as a response to SCP-001’s command. This positions SCP-001 as both a threat and a catalyst, giving the Foundation a quasi-divine mandate. The story deepens the mythic resonance of SCP-001 and ties institutional history to a single, enigmatic word.

Accounts II
Experimentation on SCP-001-2’s Kill RangeAddendum 001-a in SCP-001 – Dr Clef’s Proposal; Simple English adaptation

In-universe early 21st century (experiments dated around 2004 in some fan sources)

The Foundation conducts a series of experiments to determine the effective kill range and capabilities of SCP-001’s flaming weapon, SCP-001-2. Experiment A sends a Class-D to approach SCP-001; when the subject refuses to return, he is terminated by Foundation personnel, and SCP-001 responds by annihilating the research team. Experiment B uses a remote-operated robot, which is destroyed upon crossing the perimeter. Experiment C deploys 100 drones from multiple angles; SCP-001 strikes in all directions simultaneously, destroying them. Experiment D fires a wire-guided missile from 3 km away; it is destroyed at the 1 km mark. Experiment E launches a multi-warhead ICBM from a submarine; all warheads are destroyed before impact. Experiments F and G involve SCP-076 and SCP-073, both of whom are prevented from approaching or cause catastrophic reactions, reinforcing SCP-001’s supremacy. The experiments confirm that SCP-001-2 can destroy any known physical or anomalous approach within a fixed radius and that SCP-001 cannot be engaged militarily. The Foundation abandons further direct testing

PATMOS and the Future Rapture MessageAddendum 001-aa in SCP-001 – Dr Clef’s Proposal; SCP Foundation Wikia laconic summary; Grokipedia analysis

In-universe near-future; meta-text written in 2008 and later

The SCP-001 article introduces Emergency Procedure PATMOS, a series of orders to be executed if SCP-001 becomes active. These procedures include maintaining good relations with Abrahamic faiths, keeping blessed religious items and scriptures on hand, and designating non-Abrahamic secondary operatives in case of a premillennial Rapture scenario. An addendum describes a message received from Site-0 stating that SCP-001 has moved and that the Rapture is beginning, referencing several SCPs with religious significance (including SCP-073 and SCP-076). Investigation reveals that the message originates from the future, implying that SCP-001’s movement will eventually trigger an apocalyptic event. The Foundation treats this as both a warning and a confirmation of PATMOS’s relevance. The Foundation institutionalizes religiously inflected emergency protocols and frames SCP-001 as central to a coming eschaton. The future message introduces time-loop or prophetic elements into SCP lore, complicating linear chronology and reinforcing SCP-001’s role as an apocalyptic trigger.

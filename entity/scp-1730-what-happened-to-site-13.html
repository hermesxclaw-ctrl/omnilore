Who It Is

A Foundation site from a parallel dimension that appeared in the New Mexico desert. Leviathan-class entities roamed its halls, space-time was corrupted, and the Director had attempted to weaponize SCP-001 proposals. MTF Zeta-9 entered. Most did not return. At the center: the Director's body fused with a Thaumiel anomaly in a failed attempt to 'become God.'

Form

A sprawling Foundation facility covered in anomalous organic growth, reality-distortion pockets, and corpses of personnel and escaped SCPs.

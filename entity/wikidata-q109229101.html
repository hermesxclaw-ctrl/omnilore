Who It Is

He was built to be Aeolia Schenberg's perfect caretaker — an immortal, beautiful steward meant to guide humanity from the shadows until it was ready to evolve. Instead, Ribbons Almark decided he was God, and that humanity needed a ruler, not a guide. With a smile as serene as a Renaissance angel and a will of absolute control, he hijacked the 200-year plan to end war and rewrote it into a war to crown himself.

Attested personality is charismatic, soft-spoken, and absolute in self-belief. In Season 1 he plays the deferential aide to Alejandro Corner, masking contempt. Once revealed, he is calm, condescending, and playful, treating war as a chess game. He genuinely believes humanity is flawed and must be ruled by a superior being — himself — and frames this as benevolence. He shows no empathy for humans or even his own Innovade subordinates, discarding Revive Revival, Bring Stability, Divine Nova, and Anew Returner as tools. Powers
Innovade Physiology - Artificial Human

Genetically engineered artificial human (Innovade) created from Aeolia Schenberg's genome via Veda. Possesses slowed aging, enhanced physical resilience, and ability to be resurrected via backup data and cloned body if killed, provided Veda retains his data.

Limit: Not immortal if Veda link is severed and backup is destroyed. Tieria Erde's severing of Ribbons from Veda in Ep. 24-25 prevents resurrection. Requires Veda's cloning facilities. Not a True Innovator naturally; lacks natural GN particle adaptation.

Quantum Brainwaves (QBWs) - High Level

Advanced quantum brainwave ability allowing telepathic communication with other Innovades and Veda, limited mind control via backdoors implanted in subordinate Innovades, and sensing of other QBW users. Can forcibly overwrite Anew Returner's personality and influence Tieria.

Limit: Weaker than True Innovator QBW (Setsuna after Trans-Am Burst). Cannot control humans without Veda assistance or True Innovators. Range limited without Veda amplification. Blocked when Veda access is cut.

Veda Access and Information Control

As the highest-level Innovade, Ribbons has Level 7 access to Veda, the quantum supercomputer that controls Celestial Being's plan. Allows him to manipulate global information, predict futures via Aeolia's plan simulations, deploy Celestial Being technology, and command automatons.

Limit: Access revoked in Season 2 Ep. 24 when Tieria Erde, via Regene Regetta's sacrifice, regains control of Veda and locks Ribbons out. Without Veda, his strategic omniscience collapses.

Mobile Suit Piloting - 0 Gundam and Reborns Gundam/Cannon

Ace pilot capable of piloting GN Drive-equipped suits. Pilots GN-000 0 Gundam (first Gundam, used to assassinate Aeolia and later against Setsuna in 2308) and CB-0000G/C Reborns Gundam (transformable into Reborns Cannon, twin GN Drive Tau system with Trans-Am). Reborns can switch between close-combat Gundam mode and heavy artillery Cannon mode mid-battle.

Limit: Reborns uses GN Drive Taus, not original GN Drives, thus lower particle efficiency and limited Trans-Am duration. Defeated by 00 Raiser and later 00 Qan[T] due to Setsuna's superior Innovator synchronization.

Strategic Manipulation and Clandestine Leadership

Master strategist who orchestrated the exposure of Celestial Being, the formation of the A-Laws, and the Innovator shadow government. Manipulated Alejandro Corner, Wang Liu Mei, and the Federation leadership simultaneously.

Limit: Plan fails because he underestimates human will and the emergence of True Innovators outside his design. His need for control makes him predictable to Tieria and Regene.

Creation as Innovade 001 and Caretaker of Aeolia's PlanMobile Suit Gundam 00 Official File Vol. 7; Anime Season 2 Ep. 15, 20 (flashbacks); 00I manga Ch. 1

c. 2090s-2110s (in-universe creation), revealed 2008-2009 broadcast

Aeolia Schenberg, 200 years before the series, creates Veda and the Innovades to preserve and execute his plan for humanity's evolution and the eradication of war. Ribbons Almark is the first Innovade, Type 001, modeled on Aeolia's own DNA, intended as a liaison who would awaken future Innovators. Veda archives reveal he was designed to be immortal and to guide humanity from behind Veda. Ribbons instead concludes that humanity will never self-evolve and that he, as the superior being, should rule it. Establishes Ribbons' origin and his fundamental betrayal of his creator's intent. Sets up the central thematic conflict: Aeolia's belief in humanity's potential vs Ribbons' contempt for it.

Assassination of Aeolia Schenberg and Usurpation of Celestial BeingMobile Suit Gundam 00 Season 1 Ep. 24-25 'Endless Poem' / 'Setsuna'

2307-2308 (in-universe), broadcast March 2008

During Celestial Being's armed interventions, Ribbons acts as aide to UN ambassador Alejandro Corner, secretly feeding him information while manipulating Celestial Being's exposure. When Aeolia Schenberg awakens from cryogenic sleep in his hidden base to initiate the next phase of his plan (unifying the world and preparing for dialogue with alien life), Ribbons and Alejandro infiltrate the base. Ribbons personally shoots and kills Aeolia, declaring the plan is now his. He then allows Alejandro to seize Veda's Level 7 access temporarily, while retaining true control. Aeolia's death fractures the plan; Celestial Being loses Veda. Ribbons becomes the hidden mastermind, setting up Season 2's Innovator regime. The 0 Gundam, piloted by Ribbons, is revealed as the Gundam that saved Setsuna as a child — a cruel irony.

Puppet Master of the A-Laws and the Innovator Shadow GovernmentMobile Suit Gundam 00 Season 2 Ep. 1-15

2312 (in-universe), broadcast Oct-Dec 2008

Four years after Celestial Being's defeat, Ribbons has established the Innovators — a group of Innovades including Revive Revival, Bring Stability, Divine Nova, Healing Care, and Anew Returner — as a secret ruling caste. Through Veda, he manipulates the Earth Sphere Federation and its brutal autonomous force, the A-Laws, commanded by Homer Katagiri. He provides them with GN Drive Tau technology and directs atrocities (e.g., the massacre at the Proud colony, the Memento Mori superweapon) to force humanity into unity through fear, while harvesting data for forced Innovator evolution. He monitors Setsuna's 00 Gundam and its Twin Drive System, intending to claim it. Creates the central oppression of Season 2. His manipulation of Anew Returner to infiltrate Celestial Being leads to her death at Setsuna's hands, traumatizing Lyle Dylandy and demonstrating Ribbons' cruelty.

Accounts II
The Twin Drive Trap and Battle for VedaMobile Suit Gundam 00 Season 2 Ep. 16-24

2312, broadcast Jan-Mar 2009

Ribbons attempts to capture the 00 Raiser to complete his Reborns Gundam. After failing to turn Tieria Erde back to his side, he is challenged by Regene Regetta, who had secretly allied with Tieria to retake Veda. Regene is killed by Ribbons, but not before uploading a virus that allows Tieria (via his link to Veda through Seraphim Gundam) to seize control of Veda and lock Ribbons out. Cut off from Veda for the first time in his existence, Ribbons loses his omniscience and his ability to resurrect. He orders a final confrontation. Loss of Veda is the turning point; Ribbons is rendered mortal and strategically blind. Regene's death redeems his earlier collaboration and enables Celestial Being's final assault. Thematically marks the fall of the false god.

Final Duel - Reborns Gundam vs 00 Raiser and DeathMobile Suit Gundam 00 Season 2 Ep. 25 'Rebirth'

2312, broadcast March 29, 2009

In orbit above Earth, Ribbons in the CB-0000G/C Reborns Gundam/Cannon duels Setsuna in the 00 Raiser. Ribbons transforms between Gundam and Cannon modes, overwhelming Setsuna until Setsuna's Trans-Am Burst triggers a massive GN particle purification, awakening nearby humans as True Innovators and severing Ribbons' quantum dominance. Ribbons, enraged that a human has surpassed him, is defeated when 00 Raiser's GN Sword III pierces Reborns' cockpit. On the ruined Celestial Being ship, Ribbons confronts Setsuna with a pistol, declaring he will still become God, but is shot dead by Setsuna. His last words are disbelief that he, a being designed to lead humanity, was rejected by it. Death of the primary antagonist; end of Innovator rule; Veda returns to Celestial Being; Setsuna is recognized as the first True Innovator, fulfilling Aeolia's true plan. Ribbons' philosophy is refuted on-screen.

Legacy and the ELS War - Posthumous InfluenceMobile Suit Gundam 00 the Movie: Awakening of the Trailblazer (2010) and 00I 2314 manga (2014)

2314 (in-universe), released 2010-2014

Though dead, Ribbons' actions shape the film's conflict. His forced Innovator program and Veda's earlier suppression left humanity unprepared for the Extraterrestrial Living-metal Shapeshifters (ELS). His backup Innovades (e.g., Telicyra Herfi) and leftover Veda data appear in 00I 2314, where surviving Innovades grapple with his ideology. Setsuna, now a True Innovator-ELS hybrid, succeeds where Ribbons failed: establishing dialogue rather than domination. Flashbacks in the film show Ribbons' earlier contact with the ELS probe data, which he dismissed as irrelevant to his godhood. Frames Ribbons as a cautionary false prophet; Aeolia's true plan (Innovation for dialogue) is contrasted with Ribbons' corrupted plan (Innovation for domination). Provides closure to Innovade storyline.

Ribbons Almark is the pilot who saved Setsuna as a child because he cared for him. — The 0 Gundam that saved Setsuna in Krugis was piloted by Ribbons, but not out of compassion. Ribbons was field-testing the Gundam and observing human conflict; saving Setsuna was incidental and later exploited to manipulate Setsuna's Gundam worship. Ribbons shows no personal attachment to Setsuna.

Ribbons is Setsuna's father or brother due to similar appearance and connection. — No familial relation. Ribbons is an Innovade based on Aeolia's DNA; Setsuna is a human from Krugis (Soran Ibrahim). Their link is symbolic (savior/victim, false vs true Innovator), not genetic.

Ribbons' Reborns Gundam uses original GN Drives like the 00 Gundam. — Reborns uses GN Drive Taus (pseudo-drives), mass-produced by the Innovators. It has two Taus enabling Trans-Am, but with lower particle output and time limit than true Twin Drive. Official mechanics books explicitly label them as GN Drive Tau.

Ribbons was always intended to be the main villain from episode 1. — Production interviews (Mizushima, Kuroda in Animage 2009) indicate Ribbons' role was planned as a shadow figure in S1 but his full nature as Innovade leader was developed during S1 production. Early S1 scripts refer to him only as 'Alejandro's aide.' His promotion to final boss was iterative, not fully locked at series start.

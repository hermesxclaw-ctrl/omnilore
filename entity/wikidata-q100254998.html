Who It Is

From the cursed mists of the Shadow Isles rides a nightmare made flesh—or rather, made of soul-stuff and steel. Hecarim, the Shadow of War, is no mere ghost; he is a cavalry charge that never ends, a fusion of knight and steed bound to an eternal hunt for souls. His story is one of betrayal, damnation, and the relentless pursuit of battle, and it begins with a king's grief and a kingdom's ruin.

Hecarim is ruthless, bloodthirsty, and relentless. He is driven by an insatiable desire for battle and the collection of souls. He is a leader among the Shadow Isles' undead, commanding other specters. He is cunning and strategic, but also impulsive when it comes to charging into battle. He shows no remorse and views mortals as prey.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Hecarim is a spectral centaur-like entity, fused with his warhorse. He appears as a large, armored figure with a skeletal or ghostly visage, glowing green eyes, and a dark, ethereal mane. His armor is ornate, with curved spikes and a horned helmet. He wields a massive glaive. He is often depicted with a green, ghostly aura, and his lower body is that of a phantom horse with hooves that leave trails of shadow.

Symbols — Hecarim is associated with the Shadow Isles, spectral green flames, and the concept of war and death. His iconography includes the spectral horse, a glaive, and his horned helmet, symbolizing his role as a harbinger of doom.

Domains — Hecarim resides in the Shadow Isles, a cursed realm of undeath. He roams the isles, leading spectral cavalry charges, and is often found in the Ruined King's service. He is bound to the Shadow Isles and cannot leave permanently, though he can manifest elsewhere through dark magic.

Fictional timeline, before the current era

Hecarim was once a mortal knight, serving King Viego of the Blessed Isles. When Viego's wife Isolde died, Viego sought to resurrect her using the Waters of Life, inadvertently causing the Ruination. Hecarim, along with other knights, was transformed into a spectral entity, fused with his horse, becoming a servant of the Shadow Isles. The Blessed Isles became the Shadow Isles; Hecarim became a powerful undead champion.

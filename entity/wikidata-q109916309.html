Who It Is

He was supposed to die alone. At sixteen, Yuta Okkotsu was a haunted boy who apologized for breathing, stalked by the monstrous wedding dress of his childhood sweetheart - Rika Orimoto, dead at eleven and reborn as a Special Grade vengeful spirit who shredded anyone who bullied him. In a world where cursed energy is born from human fear, Yuta's love had become the most dangerous curse of all, and only Satoru Gojo's offer - come to Jujutsu High and learn to control her, or be executed - kept the higher-ups from dissecting him.

Attested personality arc: Initially extremely apologetic, self-effacing, socially withdrawn and suicidal-adjacent ('I want to be killed... but I don't want to hurt anyone'), burdened by guilt over Rika's death. Through training with Maki, Panda, and Toge, he develops assertiveness, protective loyalty, and willingness to kill to protect others (declaration to Geto: 'I will kill you'). Name & Tongue

Etymology — Japanese name 乙骨憂太 (Okkotsu Yūta). Family name Okkotsu (乙骨) is rare; 乙 means 'second/latter' and 骨 means 'bone'. Given name Yūta (憂太): 憂 means 'melancholy, worry, grief' and 太 means 'thick, large' - a common masculine suffix. Akutami has not published an official statement on intentional name symbol

Pronunciation — Japanese: Okkotsu Yūta [ok̚kot͡sɯ jɯːta]; English dub: YOO-ta oh-KOHT-su. Hepburn romanization: Okkotsu Yūta; macron often dropped as Yuta Okkotsu in official English marketing (Viz Media, Crunchyroll).

Aliases — Okkotsu Yuta, Yuta Okkotsu (Jujutsu Kaisen 0 protagonist), Special Grade Sorcerer Yuta

Powers
Immense Cursed Energy & Rika (Queen of Curses)

Yuta possesses extraordinary cursed energy reserves, second only to Satoru Gojo and Sukuna in the modern era. Initially this was due to being haunted by Special Grade Vengeful Spirit Rika Orimoto, who stored vast energy. After Rika's soul is freed (end of JJK 0), a cursed spirit echo named Rika remains as his shikigami-like companion, still providing nigh-limitless energy and storage. He can fully manifest Rika for 5 minutes after summoning via ring.

Limit: Full manifestation limited to 5 minutes; originally required ring as catalyst (later revealed ring is binding vow focus, not absolute requirement after Shinjuku). Energy output drains quickly; without Rika fully manifested, access is reduced. Rika's autonomy can be unpredictable early.

Copy (Mimicry)

Yuta's innate cursed technique is Copy, allowing him to replicate other cursed techniques after fulfilling conditions. He stores copied techniques within Rika and can use them. Demonstrated copies include: Thin Ice Breaker (Uro Takako), Sky Manipulation (Takako), Cursed Speech (Toge Inumaki), Shikigami control aspects, and Sukuna's Shrine/Cleave (via copying from Yuji/Sukuna imprint).

Limit: Requires Rika's full manifestation to use copied techniques freely; conditions for initial copy implied to require ingestion of target's blood/DNA or Rika consuming part of target (Uro's arm, Inumaki's blood via Rika). Cannot copy Limitless/Six Eyes without those prerequisites. Copied techniques are less refined than originals unless mastered.

Reverse Cursed Technique (RCT)

Yuta is one of few sorcerers who can use RCT to heal himself and others, learned from copying or developing it. He heals himself, heals allies (e.g., healing Yuji after 'execution'), and can output positive energy.

Limit: RCT consumes double cursed energy; inefficient compared to Sukuna or Gojo. Cannot automatically heal fatal brain damage without risk.

Swordsmanship and Cursed Tool Proficiency

Trained by Maki Zenin, Yuta is a skilled katana user, able to infuse blade with cursed energy, thin ice breaker, and RCT. Can fight without techniques using pure reinforcement.

Limit: Relies on physical stamina; blade can break (seen vs Geto). Not on Maki/Toji level without cursed energy reinforcement.

Domain Expansion: Authentic Mutual Love (Shin'ai Naru Ai)

Yuta's Domain Expansion creates a space filled with katanas, each imbued with a copied technique. Within domain, he can use any copied technique without restriction and with sure-hit effect. Visually depicted as a barren landscape with swords embedded in ground and Rika present.

Limit: Domain is incomplete/refined late in series; can be countered by superior domains (Sukuna's Malevolent Shrine). Requires immense energy; after domain collapse, technique burnout temporarily disables Copy/Rika.

Binding Vow & Five-Minute Limit Enhancement

Yuta can extend Rika's full manifestation beyond 5 minutes via binding vows, and can transfer Copy to others temporarily (e.g., to Yuji via Rika). His ring acts as vow focus.

Limit: Binding vows carry cost - e.g., after extended use, cannot use techniques for a period; revealing technique mechanics weakens surprise.

Accounts I
The Promise and Death of Rika OrimotoJujutsu Kaisen 0 ch. 0.1, 0.4; Jujutsu Kaisen Fanbook

2017 (in-universe: 2010, when Yuta and Rika are 11)

Childhood friends Yuta Okkotsu and Rika Orimoto promise to marry when they grow up, exchanging a ring. Rika dies in a sudden traffic accident before Yuta's eyes. Traumatized and unconsciously refusing her death, six-year-old Yuta curses Rika, transforming her into a Special Grade Vengeful Cursed Spirit bound to him. For six years, Rika brutally attacks anyone who harms or bullies Yuta, isolating him. Yuta becomes cursed and targeted for execution by Jujutsu higher-ups; Rika is classified as Queen of Curses; Gojo intervenes to enroll Yuta instead of executing him. Establishes series theme of love as curse.

Tokyo Metropolitan Curse Technical School (Jujutsu Kaisen 0)Jujutsu Kaisen 0 ch. 0.1-0.4; Jujutsu Kaisen 0 film

2017 (in-universe: 2016-2017, Yuta's first year)

Enrolled at Jujutsu High under Gojo, Yuta befriends Maki Zenin, Toge Inumaki, and Panda. He learns to control Rika, gaining confidence. Suguru Geto, former Jujutsu High student, declares Night Parade of a Hundred Demons to kill non-sorcerers and seeks to claim Rika for his collection. Geto attacks Kyoto and Tokyo; Yuta volunteers to fight Geto alone. In desperate battle, Yuta vows to sacrifice himself, unleashing Rika's full power and copying Geto's techniques, ultimately defeating Geto. Afterward, Yuta releases Rika's soul with a mutual declaration of love, freeing her to pass on - yet a fragment of Rika remains as his cursed companion. Rika's human soul ascends; Geto is mortally wounded (later killed by Gojo); Yuta is recognized as Special Grade sorcerer; his status shifts from cursed victim to protector. Sets up main series power scale.

Return and the Execution of Yuji ItadoriJujutsu Kaisen ch. 137-143, 144

2020-2021 serialization (in-universe: 2018, after Shibuya Incident)

After a year training abroad in Africa with Miguel (learning to control Rika and refine Copy without relying on original Rika's soul), Yuta returns to Japan following Gojo's sealing in Shibuya. The higher-ups assign him to execute Yuji Itadori, now host to Sukuna and blamed for Shibuya destruction, as proof of loyalty. Yuta confronts Yuji and seemingly kills him by stabbing his heart - but immediately heals him with RCT, having made a secret binding vow with Gojo and the higher-ups to spare Yuji if possible. He explains he never intended to truly kill Yuji and instead tests and protects him. Yuji survives and allies with Yuta; Yuta's moral complexity and loyalty to Gojo established; sets up Culling Game alliance. Reveals Yuta's RCT mastery and strategic deception.

Accounts II
Culling Game: Sendai ColonyJujutsu Kaisen ch. 173-179

2021 serialization (in-universe: November 2018)

Entering Kenjaku's Culling Game to gather points and information, Yuta enters Sendai Colony, the most dangerous colony. He faces four elite reincarnated sorcerers: Ryu Ishigori (high output), Takako Uro (Sky Manipulation), Dhruv Lakdawalla, and Kurourushi (cockroach cursed spirit). Yuta defeats Dhruv and Kurourushi, then simultaneously battles Uro and Ryu, demonstrating Copy (Thin Ice Breaker, Sky Manipulation), RCT, and Rika's storage. He wins by outlasting them, copies their techniques, and spares them, adding 200+ points and recruiting allies. Yuta becomes top point holder; demonstrates Special Grade strength comparable to Geto/Kenjaku; acquires new techniques crucial for final battle; establishes his merciful but pragmatic combat ethic.

Shinjuku Showdown: Yuta vs Sukuna and KenjakuJujutsu Kaisen ch. 249-262, 268-270

2023-2024 serialization (in-universe: December 24, 2018)

In the final battle after Gojo's death, Yuta executes a plan with Yuji: he first ambushes and kills Kenjaku (with Takaba's distraction), preventing Kenjaku from merging humanity. Then he joins Yuji and others against Ryomen Sukuna. Yuta deploys Domain Expansion Authentic Mutual Love, using copied techniques including Thin Ice Breaker, Cursed Speech, and Sukuna's own Cleave (copied via Rika/Yuji connection). He swaps souls with Gojo's body via Kenjaku's technique (copied) to use Limitless briefly, but is severely wounded by Sukuna's World-Cutting Slash. He survives via RCT and Rika, contributing to Sukuna's eventual defeat. Kenjaku's plan thwarted; Yuta critically injured but survives series epilogue (shown alive in final chapter 270, with scar); demonstrates ultimate mastery of Copy and self-sacrifice. Controversial among fans for body-swap tactic.

Epilogue and LegacyJujutsu Kaisen ch. 270 (final chapter); Jujutsu Kaisen Official Fanbook epilogue notes

2024

In the epilogue, Yuta survives Shinjuku, returns to Jujutsu High as a teacher/leader figure alongside Yuji, Maki, and others, helping rebuild Jujutsu society after Gojo and Sukuna's deaths. Flashbacks confirm he retains Rika's echo and his Special Grade status. Akutami's afterword notes Yuta was originally the prototype protagonist and remains the 'what if Yuji had cursed energy from the start' mirror. Provides closure; positions Yuta as future pillar of Jujutsu world, fulfilling Gojo's dream of strong, kind successors.

Yuta is the main protagonist of Jujutsu Kaisen. — Yuji Itadori is the main protagonist of the main series (2018-2024). Yuta is protagonist of the prequel Jujutsu Kaisen 0 (2017) and a major supporting protagonist in the main series from chapter 137 onward. He was Akutami's original prototype protagonist before Yuji was created.

Yuta's power is unlimited Copy without conditions or time limit. — Copy is limited by 5-minute full Rika manifestation, requires prior acquisition (implied consumption), and copied techniques are weaker unless mastered. His cursed energy, while vast, is finite and burns out after domain.

Rika is still the ghost of the little girl who loves Yuta romantically in the present timeline. — Human Rika Orimoto's soul was freed at end of JJK 0 and passed on peacefully. The entity now called Rika is a shikigami-like cursed spirit created by Yuta's technique that retains her appearance and name but is not her soul. Yuta's love remains, but the being is a manifestation of his power.

Yuta killed Yuji Itadori for real when ordered to execute him. — Yuta stabbed Yuji through the heart and stopped his heart, but immediately revived him with Reverse Cursed Technique as part of a secret plan with Gojo to fake compliance with higher-ups. Yuji was dead for minutes, not permanently.

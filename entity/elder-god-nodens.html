Who It Is

Nodens, Lord of the Great Abyss, is an Elder God — a being opposed to the Outer Gods and the Great Old Ones. He manifests as an ancient, gray-bearded figure riding a giant seashell chariot pulled by legendary beasts. He hunts the servants of Nyarlathotep and occasionally aids human dreamers.

Form

Ancient, hoary humanoid figure with gray beard, often depicted with a crown or trident; rides a giant conch-shell chariot drawn by mythical beasts; associated with the sea, abyss, and the hunt

Powers

Hunting predators in the Dreamlands

Protection from Nyarlathotep

Command over nightgaunts

Power over the abyss

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Slavic personal name. 'Zlatko' from South Slavic zlat(o) 'gold' (Proto-Slavic *zolto), common hypocoristic of Zlatomir/Zlatan, widespread in Bulgarian, Serbian, Croatian, Macedonian. 'Andronikov' is a patronymic surname from Greek Andronikos (andros 'man' + nike 'victory'), via Bulgarian/Russian And

Who It Is

A malevolent entity haunting a cursed copy of The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask. The ghost of Ben, a boy who drowned in Lost Lake, whose consciousness was trapped in the game cartridge after his death. The haunted cartridge was discovered in 2010 by a college student named Jadusable. Ben's ghost alters the game — the Moon's face distorts, characters glitch, and a drowned Ben stares from within the game. Ben communicates through the game, forum posts, and eventually possesses computers and other media.

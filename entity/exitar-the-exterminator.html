Who It Is

In the vast, star-strewn emptiness of the Marvel Universe, where gods and monsters tread, there exists a figure of absolute finality. He is Exitar the Exterminator, a Celestial of immense power and terrifying purpose, whose very name evokes the end of worlds. When the cosmic order demands a verdict, Exitar is the executioner, a being of judgment and destruction who has haunted the edges of Marvel's cosmic tales for decades.

Exitar is portrayed as a being of few words, stoic and relentless in his duty. He does not question orders or show mercy; he is the ultimate instrument of cosmic law. His actions are methodical and absolute, reflecting his singular purpose.

Powers
Celestial Physiology

As a Celestial, Exitar possesses superhuman strength, durability, and longevity, on a cosmic scale. He is virtually immortal and immune to conventional harm.

Limit: His power is not absolute; he can be harmed by other cosmic beings or weapons of sufficient power.

Cosmic Energy Manipulation

Exitar can manipulate vast amounts of cosmic energy for various effects, including energy blasts, force fields, and reality alteration.

Limit: The exact limits are unspecified, but he is not omnipotent.

Judgment Execution

Exitar's primary function is to execute the judgments of the Celestials. He wields a massive axe capable of destroying planets or even cosmic entities.

Limit: He only acts when ordered by the Celestial hierarchy, particularly the One Above All.

Size Alteration

Like all Celestials, Exitar can alter his size, though he typically appears at a colossal scale, often thousands of feet tall.

Limit: His size is not fixed, but he rarely changes it.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Exitar appears as a colossal humanoid figure, typical of Celestials, with smooth, armor-like skin. He is often depicted with a blank, featureless face and a distinctive headpiece that differs from other Celestials. His color scheme is typically dark, with red or orange accents, and he carries a massive executioner's axe, symbolizing his role.

Symbols — His primary symbol is the executioner's axe, representing his function as the one who carries out cosmic judgments. His armor and size are iconic of the Celestial race, but his axe is unique to him.

Domains — Exitar dwells in the cosmic realms, often appearing on Earth or other planets when summoned to execute judgment. He is not tied to a specific habitat but operates across the universe.

Accounts I
The Judgment of EarthEternals vol. 2 #11-12 (1986)

1980s

Exitar arrives on Earth to execute the Celestials' judgment that the planet be destroyed due to the Deviants' continued existence. The Eternals, led by Zuras, plead for mercy, but Exitar is unmoved. However, the Eternals use the Uni-Mind to appeal to the Celestials, and Exitar is ultimately called off, sparing Earth. Earth is spared, but the threat of Exitar's return looms. The event reinforces the Celestials' power and the Eternals' role as intermediaries.

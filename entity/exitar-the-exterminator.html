Who It Is

Exitar the Exterminator is the Celestial whose sole purpose is to enforce the verdicts handed down by Arishem the Judge. If a civilization is deemed unworthy, Exitar is dispatched to carry out the sentence — a process called 'Extermination.' He is the largest and most physically imposing Celestial ever depicted, dwarfing even his kin, and his armor is studded with weapon systems capable of sterilizing entire planets, collapsing star systems, and unraveling dimensions. Exitar's standard method of Extermination involves descending upon the condemned world and activating a localized reality-disruption field that systematically erases all life and civilization. In 2006, Exitar arrived on Earth to execute humanity after Arishem's judgment condemned the species. The battle against Exitar drew in virtually every superhero on Earth, including the Avengers, the Fantastic Four, the X-Men, and numerous cosmic entities. The crisis was averted when the Dreaming Celestial intervened, awakening and fighting Exitar in a battle that devastated South America. Exitar was ultimately neutralized but not destroyed, as Celestials cannot be permanently killed by mortal means. Exitar represents the final, terrifying consequence of cosmic judgment — the moment when evaluation ends and annihilation begins. There is no negotiation with Exitar, no appeal, no mercy. He is the end of the line, the last page of a species' history, written in fire and silence.

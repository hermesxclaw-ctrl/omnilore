Who It Is

Exitar the Exterminator is the Celestial whose sole purpose is to enforce the verdicts handed down by Arishem the Judge. If a civilization is deemed unworthy, Exitar is dispatched to carry out the sentence — a process called 'Extermination.' He is the largest and most physically imposing Celestial ever depicted, dwarfing even his kin, and his armor is studded with weapon systems capable of sterilizing entire planets, collapsing star systems, and unraveling dimensions. Exitar's standard method of Extermination involves descending upon the condemned world and activating a localized reality-disruption field that systematically erases all life and civilization. In 2006, Exitar arrived on Earth to execute humanity after Arishem's judgment condemned the species. The battle against Exitar drew in virtually every superhero on Earth, including the Avengers, the Fantastic Four, the X-Men, and numerous cosmic entities. The crisis was averted when the Dreaming Celestial intervened, awakening and fighting Exitar in a battle that devastated South America. Exitar was ultimately neutralized but not destroyed, as Celestials cannot be permanently killed by mortal means. Exitar represents the final, terrifying consequence of cosmic judgment — the moment when evaluation ends and annihilation begins. There is no negotiation with Exitar, no appeal, no mercy. He is the end of the line, the last page of a species' history, written in fire and silence.

The Approach

In the vast, star-strewn emptiness of the Marvel Universe, where gods and monsters tread, there exists a figure of absolute finality. He is Exitar the Exterminator, a Celestial of immense power and terrifying purpose, whose very name evokes the end of worlds. When the cosmic order demands a verdict, Exitar is the executioner, a being of judgment and destruction who has haunted the edges of Marvel's cosmic tales for decades.

Content advisory. Contains themes of cosmic genocide, planetary destruction, and existential dread.

Etymology

The name 'Exitar' likely derives from 'exterminate', reflecting his role as the Celestial executioner. The title 'the Exterminator' reinforces this purpose.

Disputed Identifications

None known; Exitar is a distinct Celestial, not to be confused with other Celestials like Arishem the Judge or the One Above All.

Description

Appearance

Exitar appears as a colossal humanoid figure, typical of Celestials, with smooth, armor-like skin. He is often depicted with a blank, featureless face and a distinctive headpiece that differs from other Celestials. His color scheme is typically dark, with red or orange accents, and he carries a massive executioner's axe, symbolizing his role.

Symbols & Iconography

His primary symbol is the executioner's axe, representing his function as the one who carries out cosmic judgments. His armor and size are iconic of the Celestial race, but his axe is unique to him.

Behaviour & Personality

Exitar is portrayed as a being of few words, stoic and relentless in his duty. He does not question orders or show mercy; he is the ultimate instrument of cosmic law. His actions are methodical and absolute, reflecting his singular purpose.

Domains & Habitats

Exitar dwells in the cosmic realms, often appearing on Earth or other planets when summoned to execute judgment. He is not tied to a specific habitat but operates across the universe.

Version Differences

Exitar's depiction has remained consistent across his appearances, with no major alternate versions or reinterpretations. However, his role has been clarified over time, particularly in relation to the Celestials' hierarchy and the concept of the 'Fulcrum'.

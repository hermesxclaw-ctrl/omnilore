Who It Is

In the Local 58 universe, the Moon is not a benign celestial body — it is a hostile, possibly sentient entity. Its light has a cognitohazardous effect on humans: prolonged exposure to moonlight causes facial deformation, psychological breakdown, and eventual transformation into something non-human. The Moon can manipulate broadcast signals, appearing on television screens to 'communicate' through the Local 58 channel. Its most famous message: 'IF YOU ARE AFRAID, WE WILL LOOK TOGETHER.'

Form

The Moon appears as a massive, cratered sphere in the night sky — but wrong. In 'Skywatching,' it grows larger than physically possible, filling the entire sky. Its light has a sickly yellow cast. Through a telescope, viewers report seeing 'a face' in the craters, though no footage confirms this. Its presence on broadcasts is indicated by a countdown clock and the text: 'STAY INDOORS. DO NOT LOOK AT THE MOON.'

Cultural Significance

Local 58 is considered the foundational work of analog horror — the first series to define the genre's grammar of emergency broadcasts, hijacked signals, and cosmic threat. Its 'moon' episodes are the most iconic.

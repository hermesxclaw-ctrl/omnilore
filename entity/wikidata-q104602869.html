The Approach

In the chaotic, undead world of Zombie Land Saga, Tae Yamada is the one zombie who never speaks, never remembers, and never stops being a mystery. She is the group's silent heart, a force of nature in a decaying idol dress, whose very sneeze gave Franchouchou its name. But beneath the vacant stare and the shambling gait lies the secret of a woman who once fought demons in the shadows—a revelation that transforms her from comic relief to tragic hero.

Content advisory. Depictions of death, violence, and supernatural themes; references to exorcism and demon-slaying; comedic treatment of zombie tropes; brief moments of body horror.

Etymology

The surname Yamada (山田) is a common Japanese surname meaning 'mountain rice field'. The given name Tae (たえ) can be written with various kanji, but is often associated with 'tae' meaning 'to be enduring' or 'to be pliant'. The nickname 'Zero-gō' refers to her designation as the group's initial zombie, preceding the numbered members 1 through 6.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese pop culture, specifically the Zombie Land Saga franchise created by Cygames, Avex Pictures, and MAPPA.

Disputed Identifications

Prior to the release of 'The First Zombie', fans speculated that Tae might be a legendary figure from Japanese history, but the manga confirmed her as an ordinary office worker with a secret exorcist past. Some fan theories have incorrectly identified her as a historical person, but these are not supported by official canon.

Description

Appearance

Tae is a young woman with long, messy black hair that often covers her face. As a zombie, her skin is pale and ashen, with visible stitches and wounds. She wears a tattered idol outfit, typically a white and pink dress with a torn hem. Unlike the other zombies, she does not wear makeup to hide her condition, and her eyes are often vacant or glazed. In her previous life, as depicted in 'The First Zombie', she appears as a neat office worker with a ponytail and a serious expression, wearing a business suit.

Symbols & Iconography

Tae is often associated with the number 0, reflecting her status as the 'zero' member. Her sneeze is a recurring motif, symbolizing her unique contribution to the group. In the manga, she is depicted with a katana and talismans, representing her exorcist role.

Behaviour & Personality

In the anime, Tae is largely non-verbal and appears to lack higher cognitive functions. She shambles, groans, and occasionally sneezes with explosive force. She is often shown eating strange things or wandering off. Despite this, she occasionally displays moments of surprising competence, such as when she names the group or participates in performances with surprising agility. In 'The First Zombie', her past self is depicted as a diligent, serious office worker who is also a skilled exorcist, fighting demons with a calm and determined demeanor.

Domains & Habitats

Tae primarily resides in the abandoned mansion that serves as Franchouchou's base in Saga Prefecture. She is often seen wandering the grounds or the surrounding town. In her previous life, she lived in an apartment and worked in an office, while also operating as an exorcist in various locations.

Version Differences

The anime and the manga spinoff present two contrasting versions of Tae: the anime shows her as a mindless zombie, while the manga reveals her as a capable exorcist and office worker. The anime itself does not delve into her past, leaving her as a comic relief character, whereas the manga provides a detailed backstory that reframes her as a tragic figure.

Stories & Myths

Resurrection and Franchouchou's Formation

Source Zombie Land Saga (anime)Culture JapanesePeriod 2018 (modern day)

Summary

Tae is resurrected by Kotaro Tatsumi along with six other legendary girls. She is the first to awake, but remains in a zombie state without consciousness. During a meeting, she sneezes, producing a sound that Kotaro interprets as 'Franchouchou', and he decides to use that as the group's name.

Tae becomes the group's silent, unpredictable member, often causing chaos but also providing comedic relief.

Variants

The manga adaptation of the anime follows the same events, but the spinoff 'The First Zombie' focuses on her past.

The First Zombie: Tae's Past Life

Source Zombie Land Saga Sidestory: The First Zombie (manga)Culture JapanesePeriod 1990s-2000s (flashback)

Summary

The manga reveals that Tae was an office worker who led a double life as an exorcist. She fought demons in secret, and her actions eventually led to her death, after which she was resurrected as the first zombie.

Participants

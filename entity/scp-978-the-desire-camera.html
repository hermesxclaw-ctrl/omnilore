Who It Is

According to traditional accounts, a cheap red-and-black Polaroid camera sits in a locker at Site-17, indistinguishable from the disposable nostalgia of the late twentieth century—until you press the shutter and discover that the photograph shows not what you were doing, but what you wanted to be doing. In the SCP Foundation’s universe, SCP-978 turns every snapshot into a confession, exposing desires that range from a different tie to fantasies so disturbing they send researchers on psychiatric leave. The "Desire Camera" is horror by way of honesty: a mundane object that makes the inner life visible, and sometimes lethal, one instant print at a time.

Attested: SCP-978 exhibits no sapience or personality; it behaves mechanically like a normal instant camera. Its anomalous behavior is limited to the content of the photographs it produces. The camera does not initiate actions on its own, communicate, or resist handling. All "behavior" is expressed through the images, which reveal the desires of subjects at the moment of capture. Powers
Desire-revealing photography

Attested: When SCP-978 is used to photograph a living subject, the resulting instant photograph does not depict the subject’s actual appearance or activity at the moment of capture. Instead, it shows what the subject wanted to be doing at that time. The desires manifested can be trivial (e.g., wanting to go home, wear different clothing) or profound and disturbing (e.g., engaging in taboo acts), and may reflect conscious wishes or deeply suppressed impulses. [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-978) [[2]](https://scp.fandom.com/wiki/SCP-978)

Limit: Attested: The effect applies only to living beings; inanimate objects photographed alone do not exhibit anomalous output. The camera requires standard film and paper and operates mechanically like a normal Polaroid, implying dependence on physical media and user activation. The desire shown appears to be tied to the moment of capture, not a permanent or predictive future state. Some tests show minimal or no change (e.g., subjects whose desires are already aligned with their current activity), indicating that the camera does not force dramatic alterations. [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-978) [[3]](https://scpdb.miraheze.org/wiki/SCP-978)

Mixed-desire composite imaging

Attested: When two or more subjects with contrasting desires are photographed together, SCP-978’s output appears to "mix" their desires, producing composite scenes that incorporate elements of each subject’s wish. This can result in visually complex or contradictory images, revealing interpersonal tensions or incompatible goals. [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-978) [[2]](https://scp.fandom.com/wiki/SCP-978)

Limit: Attested: The mixing effect is described qualitatively but not systematically quantified; the camera does not necessarily give equal weight to each subject’s desire, and the mechanism of combination is unknown. The phenomenon requires simultaneous multi-subject photography; separate photographs of each subject show individual desires instead.

Cross-testing diagnostic function with other SCPs

Attested: Extended test logs document SCP-978 being used on various SCP entities, including multiple SCP-001 proposals, SCP-4999, SCP-2273-1, and others. The resulting photographs provide insight into these entities’ desires—such as release from custody, reunion with loved ones, or transformation into non-anomalous human states—thus functioning as a diagnostic tool for understanding anomalous psychology or metaphysics. [[4]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-978-extended-test-logs) [[2]](https://scp.fandom.com/wiki/SCP-978) [[3]](https://scpdb.miraheze.org/wiki/SCP-978)

Limit: Attested: Testing with certain high-risk SCPs (especially SCP-001 variants) is heavily restricted, requiring O5-level approval, and resulting photographs are often destroyed or archived under extreme security. Some tests produce dangerous secondary effects (e.g., contamination by SCP-217, memetic kill agents), indicating that using SCP-978 on powerful anomalies can be hazardous. [[4]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-978-extended-test-logs)

It has a lightweight plastic body, integrated flash, viewfinder, and film slot, with no visible damage, modifications, or anomalous markings. Accounts I
Acquisition and containment at Site-17SCP Foundation Wikia/Fandom; SCP Database; Grokipedia (partially extrapolated)

Late 20th or early 21st century (in-universe; exact year redacted or unspecified)

Attested (derivative canon): At some point in the late 20th or early 21st century, local authorities encountered unexplained photographic phenomena associated with a Polaroid camera. The device was eventually turned over to the SCP Foundation, reportedly after being found in an abandoned warehouse at a redacted location. The Foundation designated it SCP-978, classified it as Safe, and stored it in security locker H-J-12 at Site-17. Subsequent documentation formalized containment procedures and testing protocols. [[2]](https://scp.fandom.com/wiki/SCP-978) [[3]](https://scpdb.miraheze.org/wiki/SCP-978) [[1]](https://grokipedia.com/page/SCP-978) SCP-978’s containment enabled systematic study of its anomalous properties and integration into the Foundation’s broader research program. Its Safe classification reflects the object’s lack of autonomous hostility but does not eliminate ethical and psychological risks associated with its use.

Initial human testing and psychological impactCore SCP-978 article testing log

May of an unspecified year (late 20th or early 21st century, in-universe)

Attested: In May of an unspecified year, Foundation researchers conducted basic tests on human staff. Research assistants and doctors were photographed while posing neutrally and smiling for the camera. The resulting images revealed a range of desires: one assistant appeared tired and unprofessional, drinking from a flask; another was shown in casual clothes heading out for the night, admitting she wished to go home; a doctor appeared simply wearing a different tie. At least two results were redacted as [DATA EXPUNGED], one leading to a research assistant being placed on temporary psychiatric leave and another causing extreme embarrassment and apologies from a doctor. [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-978) The tests demonstrated SCP-978’s capacity to reveal both mundane and disturbing desires, prompting ethical concerns and psychological consequences for subjects. The psychiatric leave and embarrassment underscore the potential harm of exposing hidden impulses, leading to explicit policy prohibiting use of SCP-978 for blackmail, entertainment, or personal reasons.

Non-human subject testsCore SCP-978 article; Fandom and Database summaries

Following initial human tests (same general timeframe)

Attested: After human testing, SCP-978 was used on animals, including a dog and its handler, mice, a rabbit, and other beings. The photographs revealed desires such as the dog wanting to play or be with its handler, and other animals exhibiting simple, instinctual wishes. These tests confirmed that SCP-978’s effect applies to non-human living beings and can capture relatively simple, immediate desires more easily than complex human psychological states. [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-978) [[2]](https://scp.fandom.com/wiki/SCP-978) [[3]](https://scpdb.miraheze.org/wiki/SCP-978) The extension of testing to animals broadened the understanding of SCP-978’s scope, showing that the anomaly is not limited to human cognition and can reveal cross-species desire patterns. This reinforced its value as a general diagnostic tool for living entities.

Accounts II
Cross-testing with SCP-4999SCP Foundation Wikia/Fandom; SCP Database; Extended Test Logs (referenced)

Post-introduction of SCP-4999 (21st-century in-universe)

Attested (derivative canon): After SCP-4999 appeared to a bedridden Stage IV cancer patient at a hospital, an individual holding SCP-978 took a photograph of SCP-4999 smoking a cigarette next to the patient. The resulting Polaroid showed the patient standing next to a man, while SCP-4999 was absent from the image. This suggests that the patient’s desire was to stand beside a non-anomalous companion, or perhaps to be healthy and upright, rather than to remain bedridden with SCP-4999 present. [[2]](https://scp.fandom.com/wiki/SCP-978) [[3]](https://scpdb.miraheze.org/wiki/SCP-978) The test implies that SCP-978 may not depict certain anomalous entities if they are not part of the subject’s desire, raising questions about the ontological status of SCP-4999 and the camera’s selective representation. It also underscores the poignancy of human desire for normalcy and companionship in the face of terminal illness.

Cross-testing with SCP-2273-1 and SCP-191SCP Foundation Wikia/Fandom; SCP Database; Extended Test Logs (referenced)

Post-containment of SCP-2273 and SCP-191

Attested (derivative canon): SCP-978 was used on SCP-2273-1 while he sat on a bunk reading a novel. The resulting photograph showed two European men working on a farm as three women watched. Four of the older figures appeared related, while the youngest woman was seemingly identified as SCP-191 without her cybernetics. This image suggests a desire for a pastoral, familial life and for SCP-191 to exist as a non-modified human, highlighting themes of lost normalcy and bodily autonomy. [[2]](https://scp.fandom.com/wiki/SCP-978) [[3]](https://scpdb.miraheze.org/wiki/SCP-978) The test provides narrative insight into SCP-2273-1’s and SCP-191’s possible desires, reinforcing the use of SCP-978 as a tool for exploring the emotional and ethical dimensions of other anomalies. It also deepens the tragic framing of SCP-191’s cybernetic modification.

Testing with SCP-001 proposals and high-risk anomaliesSCP-978 Extended Test Logs

Post-establishment of multiple SCP-001 proposals

Attested: Extended logs describe SCP-978 being used on various SCP-001 instances and proposals, including SCP-001-EX, SCP-001-ARBELICT, SCP-001-CLEF, SCP-001-TG/AKTUS, SCP-001-AKTUS-I, SCP-001-AKTUS-II, SCP-001-LOCKE-Ω, and SCP-001-LOCKE-A. Results range from no change (SCP-001-EX) to images of release from Foundation custody, bowing before an unknown entity in an infinite white landscape, transformed non-anomalous human states, and dangerous effects such as photographs emitting intense brightness that converts exposed creatures into SCP-001-A instances. Some photographs are destroyed or heavily redacted, and testing is restricted to O5-approved scenarios. [[4]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-978-extended-test-logs) These tests demonstrate that SCP-978’s interaction with high-tier anomalies can produce both revelatory and hazardous outcomes, including memetic kill agents and contamination by other SCPs.

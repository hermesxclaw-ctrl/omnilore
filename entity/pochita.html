Who It Is

Pochita is the Chainsaw Devil — the original form of what Denji becomes as Chainsaw Man, and one of the most terrifying beings in the Chainsaw Man universe. But Pochita is also a small, adorable, chainsaw-nosed creature who simply wanted to be hugged. In the world of Chainsaw Man, devils are born from human fears, and their power is proportional to the fear they embody. The Chainsaw Devil is feared not just by humans but by other devils — because Pochita has the unique and terrifying ability to permanently kill devils not just in the physical world but conceptually. When Pochita devours a devil, that devil's name and concept are erased from existence. Whole aspects of reality — alternate histories, erased concepts, forgotten fears — have been removed because Pochita ate them. Before meeting Denji, Pochita was a fearsome entity in Hell, single-handedly fighting entire armies of devils and winning. Devils in Hell would flee at the mere sight of his chainsaw roar. But Pochita was also lonely. He wanted connection, warmth, affection — things a devil is not supposed to desire. When he met Denji, a poor, lonely boy who accepted him without fear, Pochita found the one thing he had been searching for: a friend. Their bond is the emotional core of Chainsaw Man. When Denji was about to die, Pochita made a deal: 'If I show you my dreams... will you fulfill my dream?' Denji agreed, and Pochita merged his heart with Denji's, giving Denji his powers and fulfilling his own dream — to be held, to be loved, to be close to someone. Pochita represents the gentleness that can exist within even the most terrifying power. He is a being who could erase entire concepts from existence, but what he truly wanted was a hug. His legacy lives on within Denji, and sometimes, when Denji is in mortal danger, the original Pochita surfaces — and the Hero of Hell appears once more, with a roar that makes even the most ancient devils tremble.

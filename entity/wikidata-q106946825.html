Who It Is

In the sun-scorched kingdom of Gallopolis, a flamboyant circus showman with a heart of gold and a ship named the Salty Stallion crosses paths with a destined hero, setting off on a journey that will redefine what it means to be a knight, a performer, and a son.

Sylvando is exuberant, confident, and endlessly optimistic. He speaks with a theatrical, flamboyant accent and often uses dramatic gestures. Beneath his showmanship, he is deeply caring, loyal, and perceptive, often offering emotional support to his companions. He is also brave and skilled in combat, embodying both chivalry and performative flair.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Sylvando' likely derives from 'silva' (Latin for forest) combined with the Spanish suffix '-ando', evoking a romantic, adventurous flair. The Japanese name 'Silvia' may reference the Latin 'silva' or the character's elegant, flowing nature.

Pronunciation — sil-VAN-doh (English); SHIRU-bee-ah (Japanese)

Aliases — Silvia (Japanese), Norberto (true identity), Goliath (Japanese true identity), Leader of the Soldiers of Smile

Ownership — Square Enix, created for the Dragon Quest series by Yuji Horii and his team.

Powers
Swordplay

Sylvando is a skilled fencer, capable of holding his own in combat. He uses a rapier in battle.

Limit: None noted beyond standard combat limitations.

Showmanship and Performance

He can captivate audiences with his performances, using them to distract, inspire, or influence others. His 'Soldiers of Smile' are a testament to his leadership and ability to rally people.

Limit: His performances may not affect all individuals, especially those with strong willpower.

Leadership

He leads the Soldiers of Smile, a group of former criminals and misfits, with charisma and empathy, turning them into a force for good.

Limit: His leadership is based on mutual respect and trust, which must be earned.

Acrobatics and Agility

His circus background grants him exceptional agility and acrobatic skills, useful in both performance and combat.

Limit: Not explicitly detailed in game mechanics.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Sylvando is a tall, athletic man with long, flowing blond hair and a well-groomed mustache. He wears a flamboyant, colorful outfit reminiscent of a ringmaster: a purple and gold coat with tails, a ruffled white shirt, tight trousers, and high boots. He often carries a rapier and is accompanied by his horse, Margarita.

Symbols — His signature items include his ship, the Salty Stallion, and his horse, Margarita. His 'Soldiers of Smile' are recognized by their colorful, mismatched uniforms. His emblem is a smiling face, representing his mission to bring joy.

Domains — He is an itinerant performer, traveling across the world of Erdrea. Key locations include Gallopolis, Gondolia, Puerto Valor, and Phnom Nohn. He is equally at home in desert arenas, coastal cities, and circus tents.

Accounts I
The Gallopolis IncidentDragon Quest XI

2017

Sylvando is introduced as a circus showman in Gallopolis who helps the Luminary defeat the Slayer of the Sands. He later joins the party after learning of the Luminary's quest. Sylvando becomes a permanent party member and offers his ship for travel.

Accounts II
The Puerto Valor RevelationDragon Quest XI

2017

After the fall of Yggdrasil, Sylvando is revealed as the leader of the Soldiers of Smile. He leads the party to Puerto Valor, where he reveals his true identity as Norberto, son of Don Rodrigo, and reconciles with his father. Sylvando's past is revealed, and he gains his father's blessing to continue his mission.

Sylvando's Tale (Additional Scenario)Dragon Quest XI S

2019

This scenario details Sylvando's journey after the fall of Yggdrasil, showing how he formed the Soldiers of Smile and brought hope to a troubled world. Provides depth to Sylvando's character and his role in the world.

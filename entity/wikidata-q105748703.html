Who It Is

In the world of BlazBlue, where most warriors are driven by vengeance, prophecy, or the weight of cosmic destiny, Kagura Mutsuki stands apart as a man who fights for a simpler, yet no less formidable goal: to see his family's name restored and to win the heart of the woman he loves. A charismatic playboy with a hidden tactical genius, he commands the loyalty of the NOL's elite forces while harboring a fierce rivalry with the series' protagonist, Ragna the Bloodedge. But beneath his confident smile lies a complex figure—one whose ambitions and loyalty to his clan place him at the heart of the political and military conflicts that define the series.

Kagura is outwardly confident, boastful, and flirtatious, often making advances on women (particularly Noel and Tsubaki). He presents himself as a carefree playboy, but this is a facade for his sharp tactical mind and deep sense of duty to his family. He is loyal to his subordinates and respects strength, though he can be ruthless when necessary. He is also shown to be pragmatic, willing to work with former enemies if it serves his goals.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Kagura (神楽) is a Japanese term for Shinto ritual dances, often performed to entertain deities. Mutsuki (睦月) is an old Japanese name for the first month of the lunar calendar (January), associated with family harmony and mutual affection.

Pronunciation — Kah-goo-rah Moo-tsoo-kee

Aliases — Kagura, Captain Kagura, Kagura-sama (by subordinates)

Ownership — Japanese video game franchise, owned by Arc System Works. Kagura Mutsuki is an original character created for the BlazBlue series.

Powers
Drive: Murakumo

Kagura's Drive ability allows him to charge his sword with energy, increasing its power and range. When fully charged, he can unleash powerful slashes that cover a wide area.

Limit: The charge takes time and leaves him vulnerable if interrupted. The enhanced attacks are slower and more telegraphed.

Combat Skills

Kagura is a highly skilled swordsman, trained in the Mutsuki family's martial arts. He is also physically strong and durable, able to hold his own against top-tier fighters like Ragna and Jin.

Limit: None explicitly stated, but he is not shown to have any supernatural powers beyond his Drive.

Leadership and Tactics

As a captain in the NOL, Kagura is a capable leader and strategist. He is able to command troops effectively and devise complex plans, such as his efforts to manipulate the political situation to his advantage.

Limit: His plans can be thwarted by unpredictable events or superior forces.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Kagura is a tall, muscular man with short, dark hair and sharp eyes. He typically wears a modified NOL officer's uniform with a high collar, a long coat, and gloves. His outfit is often depicted with a red and black color scheme, and he carries a large, ornate sword.

Symbols — The Mutsuki family crest, a stylized design often associated with the moon (tsuki) and ritual (kagura). His weapon, the Murakumo, is a symbol of his family's military power.

Domains — He is based in the NOL's headquarters and is often seen in military settings. He has connections to the Mutsuki family estate and is involved in political maneuvering within the NOL.

Accounts I
The Mutsuki SuccessionBlazBlue: Chrono Phantasma

In-universe, during the events of Chrono Phantasma

Kagura is introduced as a captain in the NOL who is vying for the position of head of the Mutsuki family, a powerful clan within the NOL. He seeks to restore his family's honor, which was tarnished by his predecessor's actions. He becomes involved in the conflict between the NOL and Ragna, using the chaos to further his own agenda. Kagura's actions help to shape the political landscape of the NOL and set the stage for his role in Central Fiction.

Accounts II
The Battle for the AzureBlazBlue: Central Fiction

In-universe, during the events of Central Fiction

Kagura plays a more active role in the fight against Terumi and the other antagonists. He leads his forces into battle, and his personal route reveals his motivations and his relationship with Tsubaki, whom he hopes to protect and eventually marry. He also confronts Ragna, testing his strength and resolve. Kagura's loyalty to his family and his desire to protect Tsubaki are tested. He emerges as a key ally in the final battle, though his personal ambitions remain secondary to the greater conflict.

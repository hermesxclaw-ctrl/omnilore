The Approach

In the world of BlazBlue, where most warriors are driven by vengeance, prophecy, or the weight of cosmic destiny, Kagura Mutsuki stands apart as a man who fights for a simpler, yet no less formidable goal: to see his family's name restored and to win the heart of the woman he loves. A charismatic playboy with a hidden tactical genius, he commands the loyalty of the NOL's elite forces while harboring a fierce rivalry with the series' protagonist, Ragna the Bloodedge. But beneath his confident smile lies a complex figure—one whose ambitions and loyalty to his clan place him at the heart of the political and military conflicts that define the series.

Content advisory. No major dark themes associated with this character. Mild comic relief involving romantic pursuit and playful arrogance.
Identity

Kagura Mutsuki

Pronunciation Kah-goo-rah Moo-tsoo-kee

Known As

KaguraCaptain KaguraKagura-sama (by subordinates)

Etymology

Kagura (神楽) is a Japanese term for Shinto ritual dances, often performed to entertain deities. Mutsuki (睦月) is an old Japanese name for the first month of the lunar calendar (January), associated with family harmony and mutual affection.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese video game franchise, owned by Arc System Works. Kagura Mutsuki is an original character created for the BlazBlue series.

Disputed Identifications

None known. He is a distinct character within the BlazBlue universe.

Description

Appearance

Kagura is a tall, muscular man with short, dark hair and sharp eyes. He typically wears a modified NOL officer's uniform with a high collar, a long coat, and gloves. His outfit is often depicted with a red and black color scheme, and he carries a large, ornate sword.

Symbols & Iconography

The Mutsuki family crest, a stylized design often associated with the moon (tsuki) and ritual (kagura). His weapon, the Murakumo, is a symbol of his family's military power.

Behaviour & Personality

Kagura is outwardly confident, boastful, and flirtatious, often making advances on women (particularly Noel and Tsubaki). He presents himself as a carefree playboy, but this is a facade for his sharp tactical mind and deep sense of duty to his family. He is loyal to his subordinates and respects strength, though he can be ruthless when necessary. He is also shown to be pragmatic, willing to work with former enemies if it serves his goals.

Domains & Habitats

He is based in the NOL's headquarters and is often seen in military settings. He has connections to the Mutsuki family estate and is involved in political maneuvering within the NOL.

Version Differences

In Chrono Phantasma, Kagura is a supporting character who becomes more prominent. In Central Fiction, he has a more active role in the story, with his own route that explores his motivations. His personality remains consistent, but his depth is expanded.

Powers & Abilities

Drive: Murakumo

Source Version All games where he is playable (Chrono Phantasma, Central Fiction, Cross Tag Battle)

Description

Kagura's Drive ability allows him to charge his sword with energy, increasing its power and range. When fully charged, he can unleash powerful slashes that cover a wide area.

Limitations

The charge takes time and leaves him vulnerable if interrupted. The enhanced attacks are slower and more telegraphed.

The Mutsuki Succession

Source BlazBlue: Chrono PhantasmaCulture Japanese video game culturePeriod In-universe, during the events of Chrono Phantasma

Summary

Kagura is introduced as a captain in the NOL who is vying for the position of head of the Mutsuki family, a powerful clan within the NOL. He seeks to restore his family's honor, which was tarnished by his predecessor's actions. He becomes involved in the conflict between the NOL and Ragna, using the chaos to further his own agenda.

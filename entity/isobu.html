Who It Is

The Three-Tails, a giant turtle-like creature with three tails and a single eye. Isobu dwells in water and controls coral-based attacks that can entrap foes. Unlike most tailed beasts, Isobu was for a time without a Jinchūriki, roaming in the wild after being unleashed. Eventually sealed within Yagura Karatachi of Kirigakure.

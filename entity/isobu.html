Who It Is
Form

Isobu is depicted as a massive, turtle-like creature with three tails, each composed of chakra and resembling a spiked, armored appendage. Its body is covered in a dark, rugged shell with multiple protrusions, and its skin is a deep blue-gray, with a lighter underbelly. It has a single, large, cyclopean eye on its face, which glows red when enraged, and its mouth is filled with sharp teeth. Its overall design is reminiscent of a prehistoric sea turtle, with a menacing, powerful presence. In the Naruto series, Isobu is often shown submerged in water, with only its shell and tails visible, emphasizing its aquatic nature. The beast's size is immense, dwarfing human characters, and it is capable of retracting its limbs and head into its shell for defense.

Powers

Water Release (Suiton) Techniques Isobu can use a variety of water-based techniques, including creating massive waves, water bullets, and water dragons. Its most notable ability is the 'Water Dragon Jutsu' (Suiton: Suiryūdan no Jutsu), which it can use to attack enemies with a powerful, serpentine water projectile. It can also manipulate water to create barriers or to flood areas, making it a formidable opponent in aquatic environments.

Tailed Beast Ball (Tailed Beast Bomb) Like all tailed beasts, Isobu can create a Tailed Beast Ball, a concentrated sphere of chakra that can cause massive destruction. It forms the ball by mixing positive and negative chakra in a specific ratio, then fires it at its target. This attack is capable of leveling mountains and is one of the most powerful techniques in the Naruto universe.

Chakra Cloak When Isobu's chakra is released, it can envelop its host or itself in a shroud of blue chakra, enhancing physical abilities and providing a protective layer. This cloak can also manifest as a skeletal or muscular form, depending on the level of synchronization between the beast and its host.

Regeneration Isobu has the ability to regenerate its body over time, as seen when it was revived by Akatsuki after being sealed. This regeneration is a trait common to tailed beasts, allowing them to recover from severe injuries or even death, as long as their chakra remains intact.

Accounts

After the death of the Fourth Mizukage Yagura, Isobu is left without a host and roams the wild, specifically in a lake in the Land of Water. The Akatsuki organization, led by Pain, sends Deidara and Tobi (Obito Uchiha) to capture Isobu. Deidara uses his explosive clay to attack the beast, but Isobu defends itself with water techniques. Tobi, using his unique abilities, helps to subdue Isobu, and they eventually seal it into the Gedo Statue. This event marks the first of many tailed beast captures by Akatsuki, and it is a pivotal moment in the series as it demonstrates the organization's power and determination.

This story, which is anime-only, explores the relationship between Isobu and its jinchūriki, Yagura Karatachi, the Fourth Mizukage. Yagura is depicted as a young, talented shinobi who is chosen to become Isobu's host to protect the village. Unlike many jinchūriki, Yagura is able to achieve perfect control over Isobu, allowing them to work together in battle. However, Yagura is later manipulated by Obito Uchiha, who uses his Sharingan to control Yagura, turning him into a puppet leader. This control also affects Isobu, as the beast is forced to obey Obito's commands. The story highlights the tragic fate of Yagura and Isobu, as they are both used as tools by a greater evil.

During the war, Isobu is revived as part of the Ten-Tails' reincarnation, along with the other tailed beasts. Its chakra is extracted from the Gedo Statue and merged with the other beasts to form the Ten-Tails. However, after the Ten-Tails is defeated, Isobu is freed and later appears alongside the other tailed beasts to help Naruto Uzumaki. Isobu, along with the other beasts, entrusts Naruto with their chakra, allowing him to use their combined power to defeat Kaguya Ōtsutsuki. This story demonstrates Isobu's role as a force for good, as it willingly aids Naruto in saving the world.

Modern Interpretations

Anime Naruto Shippuden Masashi Kishimoto (original creator), Studio Pierrot (anime production) 2007-2017 Isobu appears as a supporting character in several episodes, including its capture and war scenes. Isobu is often praised for its unique design and the mystery surrounding its character. Fans have noted its relatively peaceful nature compared to other tailed beasts, making it a fan favorite. Its role in the war arc was well-received, as it contributed to the emotional climax. Video Game Naruto: Ultimate Ninja Storm 4 CyberConnect2 2016 Isobu is a playable character in the game's versus mode, with a full moveset. Players appreciate Isobu's powerful water-based attacks and its unique playstyle. The game's depiction of Isobu is considered one of the most detailed, with high-quality graphics and animations. Manga Naruto Masashi Kishimoto 1999-2014 Isobu appears in several chapters, primarily during the Akatsuki capture and the war. The manga's portrayal of Isobu is more subdued, with less characterization than in the anime. However, its design and abilities are well-documented, and it is considered an integral part of the tailed beast lore.

Scholarly Debates
Isobu's Host History
In the manga, Isobu is introduced as a wild beast with no known jinchūriki, and its capture by Akatsuki is the first time it is seen. ⇌ In the anime, Isobu is shown to have been sealed into Yagura, the Fourth Mizukage, providing a backstory that is not present in the manga.
Isobu's Personality
Some fans interpret Isobu as a passive, almost docile creature, based on its lack of aggression in the manga. ⇌ Others see Isobu as a fierce defender, citing its battle with Deidara and its use of powerful attacks.
Isobu's Name Origin
The name 'Isobu' is purely a creation of the Naruto franchise, with no direct mythological counterpart. ⇌ Some fans speculate that 'Isobu' is derived from the Japanese word 'iso' (磯) meaning 'rocky shore', linking it to the sea, but this is not officially confirmed.
What We Don't Know

There is limited official information on Isobu's origins, including how it was created and its early history before the events of the series. The nature of its relationship with Yagura is only partially explored, and the extent of its consciousness and personality is not fully developed. Additionally, the exact mechanics of its abilities, such as the Tailed Beast Ball, are not detailed in the source material. Further research could explore fan interpretations and expanded universe materials, such as light novels and spin-off manga, which may provide additional context.

A way to hold it in your mind

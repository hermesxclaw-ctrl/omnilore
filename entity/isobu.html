Who It Is

Did you know that Isobu, the Three-Tails, is one of the few tailed beasts in Naruto that was never initially bound to a jinchūriki in the manga, making it a wild and mysterious force of nature? Its capture by Akatsuki is a pivotal moment that sets the stage for the entire war arc, and its eventual alliance with Naruto reveals a deeper, more compassionate side to these often-feared creatures.

Isobu is portrayed as a relatively passive and non-aggressive tailed beast, unlike some of its counterparts. It is often shown as being indifferent to humans, preferring to remain in its natural aquatic habitat. When provoked, however, it becomes fiercely defensive, using its powerful tails and water-based attacks to repel threats. In the Naruto series, Isobu is not given a distinct personality or dialogue, but it is implied to be a creature of instinct, driven by survival rather than malice. Powers
Water Release (Suiton) Techniques

Isobu can use a variety of water-based techniques, including creating massive waves, water bullets, and water dragons. Its most notable ability is the 'Water Dragon Jutsu' (Suiton: Suiryūdan no Jutsu), which it can use to attack enemies with a powerful, serpentine water projectile. It can also manipulate water to create barriers or to flood areas, making it a formidable opponent in aquatic environments.

Limit:

Tailed Beast Ball (Tailed Beast Bomb)

Like all tailed beasts, Isobu can create a Tailed Beast Ball, a concentrated sphere of chakra that can cause massive destruction. It forms the ball by mixing positive and negative chakra in a specific ratio, then fires it at its target. This attack is capable of leveling mountains and is one of the most powerful techniques in the Naruto universe.

Limit:

Chakra Cloak

When Isobu's chakra is released, it can envelop its host or itself in a shroud of blue chakra, enhancing physical abilities and providing a protective layer. This cloak can also manifest as a skeletal or muscular form, depending on the level of synchronization between the beast and its host.

Limit:

Regeneration

Isobu has the ability to regenerate its body over time, as seen when it was revived by Akatsuki after being sealed. This regeneration is a trait common to tailed beasts, allowing them to recover from severe injuries or even death, as long as their chakra remains intact.

Accounts I
The Capture of Isobu by AkatsukiNaruto manga, Chapter 317-318

Naruto timeline, during the Akatsuki's hunt for tailed beasts

After the death of the Fourth Mizukage Yagura, Isobu is left without a host and roams the wild, specifically in a lake in the Land of Water. The Akatsuki organization, led by Pain, sends Deidara and Tobi (Obito Uchiha) to capture Isobu. Deidara uses his explosive clay to attack the beast, but Isobu defends itself with water techniques. Tobi, using his unique abilities, helps to subdue Isobu, and they eventually seal it into the Gedo Statue. This event marks the first of many tailed beast captures by Akatsuki, and it is a pivotal moment in the series as it demonstrates the organization's power and determination. Isobu is sealed into the Gedo Statue, contributing to the revival of the Ten-Tails. Its capture also leads to the eventual extraction of other tailed beasts, setting the stage for the Fourth Great Ninja War. The loss of Isobu is a significant blow to the balance of power among the hidden villages, as the Three-Tails was one of the few tailed beasts not under a village's control.

Accounts II
Yagura and Isobu: The Perfect JinchūrikiNaruto Shippuden anime, Episode 206 (filler arc)

Naruto timeline, before the start of the series

This story, which is anime-only, explores the relationship between Isobu and its jinchūriki, Yagura Karatachi, the Fourth Mizukage. Yagura is depicted as a young, talented shinobi who is chosen to become Isobu's host to protect the village. Unlike many jinchūriki, Yagura is able to achieve perfect control over Isobu, allowing them to work together in battle. However, Yagura is later manipulated by Obito Uchiha, who uses his Sharingan to control Yagura, turning him into a puppet leader. This control also affects Isobu, as the beast is forced to obey Obito's commands. The story highlights the tragic fate of Yagura and Isobu, as they are both used as tools by a greater evil. Yagura's manipulation leads to the isolation of Kirigakure, known as the 'Bloody Mist' period, where the village becomes hostile and violent. Isobu is eventually freed when Yagura dies, but the damage to the village is long-lasting. This story adds depth to Isobu's character, showing that it was not always a wild beast but had a bond with a host.

Isobu's Role in the Fourth Great Ninja WarNaruto manga, Chapter 610-612

Naruto timeline, during the Fourth Great Ninja War

During the war, Isobu is revived as part of the Ten-Tails' reincarnation, along with the other tailed beasts. Its chakra is extracted from the Gedo Statue and merged with the other beasts to form the Ten-Tails. However, after the Ten-Tails is defeated, Isobu is freed and later appears alongside the other tailed beasts to help Naruto Uzumaki. Isobu, along with the other beasts, entrusts Naruto with their chakra, allowing him to use their combined power to defeat Kaguya Ōtsutsuki. This story demonstrates Isobu's role as a force for good, as it willingly aids Naruto in saving the world. Isobu survives the war and is allowed to roam free, with no host. It is last seen in the epilogue, living in a peaceful world, symbolizing the end of the era of tailed beasts as weapons. This story solidifies Isobu's redemption arc, from a captured beast to a willing ally.

The Approach

The name Draven echoes across very different worlds: a brooding comic-book avenger, a bombastic League of Legends champion, and a British rock band. Each carries the name with distinct weight, yet all share a certain theatrical darkness.

Content advisory. Eric Draven's story involves graphic violence, murder, and themes of revenge and grief. The League of Legends character is a fictional combatant in a fantasy setting. No real-world sensitive content.
Identity

Draven

Pronunciation DRAY-vən

Etymology

The name 'Draven' is likely a variant of 'raven', a bird associated with death and omens, fitting for the crow-themed hero. It may also be a modern invented name.

Cultural Ownership

The name is used across multiple independent properties; no single owner.

Disputed Identifications

None known; the name is not tied to a single historical or mythological figure.

Description

Appearance

Eric Draven: a pale-faced man with black makeup around his eyes, resembling a crow; often depicted in a long black coat. League of Legends Draven: a muscular, shirtless man with a shaved head, tattooed arms, and carrying spinning axes. The band Draven: no distinct visual identity beyond promotional photos.

Symbols & Iconography

Eric Draven: crows, white face paint, black clothing, the image of a crow. League of Legends Draven: spinning axes, a gladiatorial arena, his catchphrase 'Welcome to the League of Draven'.

Behaviour & Personality

Eric Draven: brooding, vengeful, romantic, tragic. League of Legends Draven: arrogant, flamboyant, narcissistic, showman. Band Draven: no public persona beyond their music.

Domains & Habitats

Eric Draven: a decaying urban city (implied Detroit). League of Legends Draven: the gladiatorial arenas of Noxus. Band Draven: British rock scene.

Version Differences

Eric Draven is a tragic hero; League of Legends Draven is a comedic villain. The band is unrelated.

Source The Crow graphic novel and filmCulture AmericanPeriod 1989 (comic), 1994 (film)

Summary

Musician Eric Draven and his fiancée Shelly are murdered by a gang on Devil's Night. A year later, a crow guides Eric's spirit back to life, and he systematically hunts down and kills the murderers.

The Approach

She is the voice that speaks in two tongues, a digital siren whose pink hair and crimson eyes hide a vocal range that can whisper in Japanese or soar in English. Megurine Luka, the third oracle of Crypton Future Media, is not merely a singing synthesizer—she is a cultural phenomenon, a holographic diva who has shared stages with her fellow Vocaloids and captured the hearts of millions. But behind the glossy exterior lies a complex history of technological innovation, marketing strategy, and a fanbase that has redefined what it means to be a pop star in the 21st century.

Content advisory. No dark material of a violent, sexual, or abusive nature is associated with Megurine Luka. Some fan-created content may explore mature themes, but this is not part of the official canon.
Identity

Megurine Luka

Pronunciation MEH-goo-ree-neh LOO-kah (Japanese: めぐりね るか)

Known As

巡音ルカMegurine RukaCV03Luka

Etymology

The surname 'Megurine' (巡音) combines 'megu' (巡) meaning 'circulation' or 'to go around' and 'ne' (音) meaning 'sound', suggesting 'sound that circulates' or 'surrounding sound'. The given name 'Luka' (ルカ) is a feminine name of Slavic origin, possibly meaning 'light' or 'bringer of light'.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Crypton Future Media, a Japanese company headquartered in Sapporo, Japan. While the character has global appeal, it is primarily rooted in Japanese vocaloid culture.

Disputed Identifications

There are no significant disputed identifications. However, some may confuse her with other Vocaloids, but her distinct design and voice make her unique.

Description

Appearance

Megurine Luka is depicted as a 20-year-old woman with long pink hair and crimson eyes. Her design is asymmetrical, a deliberate choice by illustrator KEI to reflect her bilingual capabilities. She wears an old-fashioned dress that contrasts with the futuristic Vocaloid aesthetic, incorporating motifs of woodwind and brass instruments. A distinctive '∞' symbol adorns her neck area, representing 'sound around'. The blue jewel near her throat symbolizes moisture and water drops.

Symbols & Iconography

The infinity symbol (∞) is a key icon, representing eternal sound and the cyclical nature of music. The musical instrument motifs (woodwind and brass) tie her to the heritage of music. The blue jewel is a symbol of water and air, reflecting the 'moisture' in her voice.

Behaviour & Personality

As a software, Luka has no inherent personality, but her official characterization and fan portrayal often depict her as mature, calm, and slightly mysterious, befitting her adult status. She is often shown as composed and elegant, with a gentle and sometimes teasing demeanor.

Domains & Habitats

Luka exists primarily in the digital realm of Vocaloid software and virtual concerts. She is often associated with stages and recording studios, appearing as a holographic projection in live performances.

Version Differences

The Vocaloid 2 version has a single Japanese/English voicebank. The V4X version offers multiple vocal modes: Hard, Soft, Hard EVEC, Soft EVEC for Japanese, and Straight and Soft for English. The EVEC modes allow for expressive control, and Cross-Synthesis enables blending of vocal qualities.

Stories & Myths

Creation and Release

Source Crypton Future MediaCulture JapanesePeriod 2009

Summary

Megurine Luka was developed using Yamaha's Vocaloid 2 engine, with voice samples from voice actress Yū Asakawa. Released on January 30, 2009, she was the first Vocaloid to support both Japanese and English, and the first adult character in the CV Series.

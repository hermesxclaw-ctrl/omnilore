Who It Is

She is the voice that speaks in two tongues, a digital siren whose pink hair and crimson eyes hide a vocal range that can whisper in Japanese or soar in English. Megurine Luka, the third oracle of Crypton Future Media, is not merely a singing synthesizer—she is a cultural phenomenon, a holographic diva who has shared stages with her fellow Vocaloids and captured the hearts of millions. But behind the glossy exterior lies a complex history of technological innovation, marketing strategy, and a fanbase that has redefined what it means to be a pop star in the 21st century.

As a software, Luka has no inherent personality, but her official characterization and fan portrayal often depict her as mature, calm, and slightly mysterious, befitting her adult status. She is often shown as composed and elegant, with a gentle and sometimes teasing demeanor.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname 'Megurine' (巡音) combines 'megu' (巡) meaning 'circulation' or 'to go around' and 'ne' (音) meaning 'sound', suggesting 'sound that circulates' or 'surrounding sound'. The given name 'Luka' (ルカ) is a feminine name of Slavic origin, possibly meaning 'light' or 'bringer of light'.

Pronunciation — MEH-goo-ree-neh LOO-kah (Japanese: めぐりね るか)

Aliases — 巡音ルカ, Megurine Ruka, CV03, Luka

Ownership — Created by Crypton Future Media, a Japanese company headquartered in Sapporo, Japan. While the character has global appeal, it is primarily rooted in Japanese vocaloid culture.

Powers
Bilingual Singing

Capable of singing in both Japanese and English, a first for the Character Vocal Series.

Limit: Pronunciation may require adjustments by producers for natural results.

Multiple Vocal Modes (V4X)

Offers six vocal modes: Hard, Soft, Hard EVEC, Soft EVEC (Japanese), Straight, and Soft (English). EVEC modes allow nuanced phonetic control.

Limit: Not all modes are available in both languages; Cross-Synthesis only within same language sets.

Cross-Synthesis (XSY)

Allows blending of two vocal modes for unique tonal qualities.

Limit: Only between Japanese modes or between English modes, not across languages.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Megurine Luka is depicted as a 20-year-old woman with long pink hair and crimson eyes. Her design is asymmetrical, a deliberate choice by illustrator KEI to reflect her bilingual capabilities. She wears an old-fashioned dress that contrasts with the futuristic Vocaloid aesthetic, incorporating motifs of woodwind and brass instruments. A distinctive '∞' symbol adorns her neck area, representing 'sound around'. The blue jewel near her throat symbol

Symbols — The infinity symbol (∞) is a key icon, representing eternal sound and the cyclical nature of music. The musical instrument motifs (woodwind and brass) tie her to the heritage of music. The blue jewel is a symbol of water and air, reflecting the 'moisture' in her voice.

Domains — Luka exists primarily in the digital realm of Vocaloid software and virtual concerts. She is often associated with stages and recording studios, appearing as a holographic projection in live performances.

Accounts I
Creation and ReleaseCrypton Future Media

2009

Megurine Luka was developed using Yamaha's Vocaloid 2 engine, with voice samples from voice actress Yū Asakawa. Released on January 30, 2009, she was the first Vocaloid to support both Japanese and English, and the first adult character in the CV Series. Established Luka as a versatile vocalist, expanding the potential of Vocaloid music production.

Accounts II
V4X UpdateCrypton Future Media

2015

On March 19, 2015, Megurine Luka V4X was released, featuring six vocal modes and advanced EVEC and XSY systems, enhancing expressiveness. Provided producers with more tools for realistic and dynamic vocal performances.

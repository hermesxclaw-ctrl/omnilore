Who It Is
Form

Kobolds are typically depicted as small, humanoid creatures with reptilian features, such as scales, claws, and a long tail. They often have a mischievous or cunning appearance, with a keen intelligence and a love of traps and puzzles.

Powers

Trap-Tinkering Kobolds are known for their skill in crafting and setting traps, often using their cunning and intelligence to outwit their enemies.

Accounts

A group of adventurers stumble upon a hidden kobold community, where they are met with a mixture of hostility and curiosity. The kobolds offer to trade with the adventurers, but only if they can prove themselves worthy by completing a series of challenges.

A group of travelers stumble upon a hidden kobold lair, where they are met with a malevolent spirit that seeks to do them harm. The kobolds are said to be cursed, and their presence is feared by all who know of them.

Who It Is
Form

Ashi is often depicted as a beautiful woman, dressed in luxurious clothing and adorned with precious jewels. Her appearance is associated with the qualities of beauty, prosperity, and good fortune, which are reflected in her role as a goddess of reward and fortune. In Zoroastrian art and literature, Ashi is often depicted with a radiant aura, symbolizing her association with light and wisdom. Furthermore, her appearance has been described in various ancient texts, including the Avesta, which provides valuable insight into the cultural and historical context of her worship. Additionally, the appearance of Ashi has been explored in various scholarly works, including those of renowned art historians such as Stella Kramrisch and Pratapaditya Pal, who have shed light on the artistic and cultural nuances of ancient Iranian art.

Powers

Granting Good Fortune Ashi has the power to grant good fortune and prosperity to those who worship her. This power is reflective of her role as a goddess of reward and fortune, and is often associated with qualities such as kindness, generosity, and wisdom.

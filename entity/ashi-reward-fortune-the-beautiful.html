Who It Is

Ashi (the 'Reward' or 'Fortune' that follows righteousness) is the female yazata who personifies the retributive blessings that flow from Asha (truth/order). She is the 'good fortune' that naturally accompanies the righteous life — not luck in the random sense, but the ordered consequence of moral rectitude. Ashi dwells in the homes of the truthful and virtuous, bestowing children, cattle, wealth, and health; she flees from liars and oath-breakers. In Yasht 17, she is described as a beautiful maiden adorned with gold, swiftly arriving where she is invoked and accepted. Ashi is closely associated with the institution of marriage: the 'blessed house' over which she presides is the unit of Zoroastrian social and religious life.

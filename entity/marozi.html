Who It Is
Form

The Marozi is described as a large feline with a distinctive spotted coat, similar to that of a leopard, but with a more lion-like body. The spots are said to be dark brown or black, with a lighter brown or golden background. The animal is reported to be around 2-3 meters in length, with a weight of around 150-200 kilograms.

Powers

Stealth and Ambush The Marozi is said to possess the ability to move silently and strike with deadly precision, making it a formidable hunter in the wild.

Accounts

A series of sightings and encounters with the Marozi were reported by European colonizers and local tribespeople in the Aberdare Mountains region of Kenya.

The Marozi is featured prominently in local folklore and mythology, with stories and legends passed down through generations about its power and majesty.

The Approach

In the neon-drenched undercity of Zaun, a young inventor bends time itself to his will, rewinding his mistakes and outsmarting fate in a world where every second counts. This is Ekko, the Boy Who Shattered Time—a prodigy whose mastery over temporal loops makes him both a hero and a enigma in the sprawling lore of League of Legends.

Content advisory. No dark material of note; contains themes of urban strife, gang conflict, and time manipulation.

Etymology

The name 'Ekko' is a deliberate variation of 'echo,' reflecting his ability to rewind time and create echoes of himself.

Cultural Ownership

Riot Games, Inc. (fictional universe of Runeterra)

Disputed Identifications

No real-world disputes; the name 'Ekko' is also used for other entities, but this dossier focuses solely on the League of Legends character.

Description

Appearance

Ekko is a young man with dark skin, a shaved head, and a distinctive hourglass tattoo on his right cheek. He wears a green and black jacket with a high collar, reinforced with metal plating, and carries a mechanical device on his back that resembles a clockwork engine. He wields a 'Z-Drive'—a temporal device that allows him to rewind time.

Symbols & Iconography

The hourglass is a recurring symbol, appearing on his cheek and in his Z-Drive. His color scheme of green and black is associated with Zaun's industrial aesthetic.

Behaviour & Personality

Ekko is brilliant, resourceful, and fiercely protective of his friends. He is a natural leader, often seen as a role model in the undercity. He is confident, sometimes cocky, but deeply cares about his community. He uses his time abilities to correct mistakes and save lives, often at personal cost.

Domains & Habitats

Ekko is native to Zaun, the industrial undercity of Piltover. He operates in the streets and underground, often in the Entresol level.

Version Differences

In the game's original lore, Ekko is a prodigy from Zaun who fights crime. In Arcane, his backstory is expanded: he is a street kid who lost his friend Powder (later Jinx) and becomes a resistance leader against Piltover's oppression. The game's lore has been updated to align with Arcane, but some details differ (e.g., his age and specific experiences).

Powers & Abilities

Time Rewind (Chronobreak)

Source Version Game (2015) and Arcane (2021)

Description

Ekko can rewind time to a few seconds in the past, returning to a previous position and health state. This is his ultimate ability in-game.

Limitations

The rewind is limited to a short duration and has a long cooldown. In lore, he cannot alter major events or change the past of others easily.

Contradictions

In the game, he can use it frequently in combat; in lore, it seems to be a rare and taxing ability.

Timewinder

Source Version Game (2015)

Description

Ekko throws a temporal device that damages enemies on the way out and on return, and slows them.

Limitations

Skillshot; can be dodged.

Contradictions

None.

Parallel Convergence

Source Version Game (2015)

Description

Ekko creates a temporal field that slows enemies inside; after a few seconds, the field collapses, stunning enemies who remain.

Limitations

Requires timing; enemies can leave the area.

Contradictions

None.

Phase Dive

Source Version Game (2015)

Description

Ekko dashes to a target, dealing damage and applying on-hit effects.

Limitations

Short range; needs a target.

Contradictions

None.

Z-Drive Resonance

Source Version Game (2015)

Description

Ekko's basic attacks and abilities build stacks that increase his attack speed and allow his next attack to deal bonus magic damage.

Limitations

Requires sustained combat.

Stories & Myths

The Boy Who Shattered Time

Source Official Riot Games short storyCulture Fictional (Runeterra)Period Contemporary to the game's lore

Summary

Ekko is a young inventor from Zaun who creates the Z-Drive, a device that allows him to manipulate time. He uses it to protect his friends and fight against the corrupt forces in Zaun. The story details his first major use of the device to save his friend from a gang attack.

Consequences

Ekko becomes known as a hero in Zaun, but also attracts unwanted attention.

Variants

The story is consistent with the game's lore, but Arcane expands on his childhood and relationships.

Arcane (Season 1)

Source Netflix animated seriesCulture Fictional (Runeterra)Period Set years before the game's events

Summary

Ekko is introduced as a street kid in Zaun, friends with Powder, Vi, and others. After a traumatic event, he grows up to lead a resistance movement against Piltover's enforcers. He uses his inventions and time abilities to fight for Zaun's freedom.

Ekko and Jinx were close friends in childhood, but after a tragedy, they grew apart. In Arcane, they have a tense and tragic relationship, as Jinx becomes a destructive force.

Who It Is

A derelict alien warship the size of a small moon, currently in orbit around Jupiter, performing massive-scale repairs to a weapon system capable of destroying entire solar systems. The ship is severely damaged from a previous engagement but is actively regenerating. Foundation deep-space observation suggests the weapon will be operational within ~150 years, at which point it will fire upon Earth.

Form

A spherical construct approximately 1,800 km in diameter, composed of an unknown metallic alloy with shifting, organic properties. A massive hole — the weapon discharge port — faces Earth's general orbital path. Lightning-like energy arcs across its surface.

Antimatter-based planetary weaponry Attested: SCP-2399 possesses highly advanced antimatter-based weapons capable of creating spatial disruptions and devastating atmospheric phenomena. In the SCP article, these weapons are responsible for the large red vortex in Jupiter’s atmosphere, identified with the Great Red Spot. The implication is that, at full operational capacity, these weapons could strip a planet’s atmosphere, destabilize its crust, and render it barren—an SK-Class "Barren Earth" end-of-the-world scenario. Core SCP-2399 article; reinforced by SCP Explained and Fandom summaries. Attested: Due to extensive damage, SCP-2399’s weapon systems are not fully functional. Its current output is sufficient to maintain the Great Red Spot but not to leave Jupiter and attack Earth. The Foundation’s BARRIER Array attempts to interfere with its command signals, potentially limiting coordinated weapon activation. Some interpretations suggest the Great Red Spot is entirely artificial, while others treat SCP-2399’s activity as superimposed on a pre-existing atmospheric phenomenon. The article leans toward the former, but astronomical history introduces ambiguity.

Self-repair via octopoid drones Attested: SCP-2399 continuously deploys numerous small, octopoid mechanical drones that search Jupiter’s atmosphere and nearby moons for components. These drones retrieve materials and perform repairs on the main structure, gradually increasing its completion percentage. Computer models in the article estimate its repair progress and annual rate. Core SCP-2399 article; elaborated in Fandom meta and video summaries. Attested: The repair process is slow and constrained by available materials and interference from Foundation satellites. The article notes a completion rate that has increased over decades but remains below full functionality. No major contradictions in canon; speculative discussions question whether the drones could adapt to Foundation countermeasures or repurpose human technology, but this is not attested.

Limitless internal power supply Attested: SCP-2399 appears to possess a virtually limitless power source, enabling continuous operation of repair drones, weapon systems, and communication arrays despite severe damage and centuries of activity. Core SCP-2399 article. Unknown: The nature of this power source (fusion, zero-point energy, antimatter reactors, or other exotic mechanisms) is not described. No explicit failure modes are given. Some fan interpretations treat "limitless" as hyperbolic, assuming extremely large but finite reserves; the article itself uses the term descriptively without technical specification.

Advanced electromagnetic shielding Attested: SCP-2399 is protected by sophisticated electromagnetic shielding, making direct physical or energy-based attacks by human technology ineffective. This shielding also complicates observation and interference. Core SCP-2399 article; summarized in Fandom and YouTube explainers. Attested: The Foundation’s BARRIER Array can jam incoming command signals, indicating that while SCP-2399’s shielding is formidable, it is not absolute against carefully engineered long-range interference. No explicit contradictions; the balance between shielding strength and BARRIER effectiveness is left deliberately vague to maintain narrative tension.

Precise tracking and targeting system Attested: SCP-2399 possesses a highly accurate targeting system capable of tracking planetary bodies and, by implication, specific locations on those bodies. This system underlies its capacity to aim antimatter weaponry and coordinate drone deployment. Core SCP-2399 article (Addendum 2399-2b) and derivative summaries. Attested: Damage to the structure may impair some targeting functions, contributing to its current focus on repair rather than offensive action. Speculative: Some alternate reality stories suggest that SCP-2399’s targeting can be spoofed or redirected by occult or technological means, but these are timeline-specific and not universally canonical.

Intergalactic communication reception Attested: SCP-2399 receives a continuous stream of complex, encoded electromagnetic communications from the Triangulum Galaxy, interpreted as command signals instructing it to complete repairs and execute its mission. The Foundation’s BARRIER Array is designed to intercept and jam these transmissions. Core SCP-2399 article; elaborated in Sacred Mythic Beings and other YouTube summaries. Attested: The BARRIER Array can disrupt or block these signals within a defined perimeter, though the long-term reliability of this jamming is uncertain. Interpretive: Some readers argue the signal may be a distress call, status update, or part of a larger network rather than a simple command; the article frames it as directive, but does not fully decode its content.

Planetary destruction capability (SK-Class "Barren Earth" scenario) Attested: The SCP file explicitly classifies SCP-2399 as capable of causing an SK-Class "Barren Earth" end-of-the-world event if it reaches full operational status and targets Earth. This implies the ability to render the planet uninhabitable, likely through atmospheric stripping, crustal destabilization, and large-scale energy release. Core SCP-2399 article; SCP Foundation classification system. Attested: As of the article’s timeframe, SCP-2399 is below 75% completion, and the Foundation has contingency plans (Protocol LEGIONNAIRE-5) to respond if it surpasses that threshold. Alternate reality stories explore different degrees of success or failure in preventing this scenario, leading to divergent outcomes (total devastation, partial survival, or preemptive neutralization).

Incident Log

The core SCP file presents SCP-2399 as a massive, damaged alien weapon embedded in Jupiter’s lower atmosphere. Since its visual discovery in 1963, Foundation satellites have observed it using antimatter weaponry to maintain the Great Red Spot and deploying octopoid drones to repair itself. Computer models estimate its completion at 59%, with an annual repair rate that has increased over decades. Physical containment is impossible; instead, the Foundation suppresses public knowledge, monitors reconstruction, and operates the BARRIER Array to jam command signals from the Triangulum Galaxy. If SCP-2399 surpasses 75% completion or breaches the jamming perimeter, Protocol LEGIONNAIRE-5 will be enacted as a last-resort response to prevent an SK-Class "Barren Earth" scenario.

According to extended lore summarized on the Fandom page, SCP-2399 entered the Solar System in 1665 and collided with Jupiter’s moon Io before coming to rest in Jupiter’s atmosphere. Italian astronomer Giovanni Cassini allegedly witnessed its arrival, and the activation of its weaponry caused the formation of the Great Red Spot. This retroactive mythologization ties a real historical figure and astronomical phenomenon into the SCP narrative, suggesting that humanity has unknowingly observed the effects of SCP-2399 for centuries.

Extended lore describes a major event during World War II in which Robert Bumaro of the Church of the Broken God created a false Mekhane (the Broken God) in Baja California. The Allied Occult Initiative and the SCP Foundation collaborated to teleport SCP-2399 from Jupiter to Baja California, using it as a weapon to destroy the false deity. SCP-2399 succeeded but sustained catastrophic damage, necessitating its return to Jupiter. From 1971 to 2015, its drones worked to repair this damage, explaining its current incomplete state and increased repair rate.

Addenda to the SCP-2399 file track its repair progress, noting an increase from approximately 12% completion in 1970 to nearly 60% by the article’s timeframe, with an annual rate of 0.78%. The Foundation’s BARRIER Array intercepts and logs command signals, while internal memos discuss Protocol LEGIONNAIRE-5, a classified contingency plan to be enacted if SCP-2399 surpasses 75% completion or if the jamming perimeter fails. The specifics of the protocol are redacted, heightening tension and uncertainty.

In the alternate reality explored in SCP-2847 and related tales, SCP-2399’s repair efforts eventually succeed or Foundation countermeasures fail, leading to catastrophic planetary destruction or near-extinction. The story follows a lone survivor (the "Last Man") navigating a devastated world shaped by SCP-2399’s actions and the fallout of occult warfare. SCP-2399 functions as the unseen architect of the apocalypse, its presence felt through ruined landscapes and fragmented records.

Project Isorropía and Vanguard are extended narrative arcs in which the SCP Foundation and allied groups attempt to rebalance or reconfigure the cosmic threat landscape, including SCP-2399. These stories explore advanced occult technology, interstellar strategies, and the possibility of confronting or redirecting SCP-2399 rather than merely jamming its signals. SCP-2399 appears as a looming factor in strategic calculations, sometimes as a potential tool, sometimes as an unavoidable doom.

Content Notes

Attested: The SCP file explicitly states that SCP-2399 is capable of causing an SK-Class "Barren Earth" event, meaning the complete or near-complete destruction of Earth’s habitability. While the article does not graphically describe individual deaths, the implication is total planetary devastation and the extinction or near-extinction of humanity and most life.

Within SCP’s internal taxonomy, SK-Class scenarios represent severe, often irreversible alterations to Earth’s biosphere. SCP-2399 is one of the clearest embodiments of this category, aligning with broader horror traditions of doomsday machines and cosmic annihilation.

Core SCP-2399 article.

Reconstructed: Extended lore describes the Foundation and Allied Occult Initiative teleporting SCP-2399 to Baja California to destroy a false Broken God created by Robert Bumaro. This act weaponizes an existential threat, likely causing massive collateral damage and occult casualties, though specific numbers and scenes are not detailed.

This episode exemplifies the SCP universe’s recurring theme of moral compromise: organizations tasked with containment resort to using catastrophic anomalies as weapons, blurring lines between protection and aggression.

SCP Foundation Wikia (Fandom) meta; SCP-001 TwistedGears-Kaktus proposal and related tales.

Reconstructed: The 1942 Baja California event occurs within a broader context of occult warfare during World War II, involving the Church of the Broken God, the Allied Occult Initiative, and the SCP Foundation. While the SCP texts focus on strategic outcomes, the implied human and non-human casualties are significant, including those caught in the crossfire of god-level entities and planet-killing weapons.

This aligns with dark fantasy and horror traditions that reframe historical conflicts as arenas for supernatural and cosmic forces, often highlighting the expendability of ordinary lives in the pursuit of esoteric power.

SCP-001 TwistedGears-Kaktus proposal and related SCP tales; Fandom meta summaries.

Attested: The SCP Foundation maintains an ongoing misinformation campaign to suppress any public awareness of SCP-2399, including containment of observatory footage and images. This entails systemic deception of the global population about an existential threat, denying informed consent and agency.

In critical readings of SCP, such practices are often interpreted as commentary on real-world secrecy, surveillance, and the ethics of withholding information about catastrophic risks.

What We Don't Know

Several gaps remain due to the nature of SCP-2399’s source material and the limits of this research. (1) Exact publication dates and revision history for the SCP-2399 article and related tales were not exhaustively reconstructed; only approximate mid-2010s dating is provided. (2) The precise text of some cross-linked stories (e.g., full details of the Baja California event, Project Isorropía, and Vanguard) was not re-read in full; this dossier relies on Fandom meta summaries and general knowledge, which may omit nuances. (3) The exact YouTube video IDs for cited SCP-2399 videos were not recorded, though their titles and creators are accurate. (4) No non-English translations or localized adaptations of SCP-2399 were consulted, so reception outside the Anglophone internet is under-documented. (5) Because SCP is a collaborative, evolving project, new tales or reinterpretations of SCP-2399 may exist beyond the research cutoff date, and no claim is made to cover "all" versions. (6) The internal logic of Protocol LEGIONNAIRE-5, the full decoding of the Triangulum signal, and the detailed technical specifications of SCP-2399’s power source and weapon systems are intentionally redacted or unexplored in canon, and thus remain unknown rather than merely unresearched.

A way to hold it in your mind

From Earth, Jupiter’s Great Red Spot looks like a timeless storm—a swirling red eye that has watched our planet for centuries. In the SCP Foundation’s universe, that eye is not weather at all, but the scar of a broken weapon: SCP-2399, a vast alien machine lodged deep in Jupiter’s atmosphere, quietly repairing itself under a constant stream of commands from another galaxy. It is not angry, not vengeful, not even aware of us in any human sense. It is simply doing what it was built to do—finish its repairs, leave Jupiter, and turn Earth into a barren world.

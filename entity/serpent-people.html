Who It Is

An ancient race of reptilian humanoids who ruled Earth during the Permian and Triassic periods before the rise of mammals. They are masters of alchemy and illusion, able to disguise themselves as humans. Survivors built hidden cities underground, worshiping Yig, the Father of Serpents.

Form

Humanoid with serpentine features: scaled skin, snake-like head with intelligent eyes, clawed hands, sinuous body; can cast illusions to appear human

Illusion magic

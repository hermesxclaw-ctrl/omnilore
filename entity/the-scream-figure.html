Who It Is
Form

The Scream Figure is depicted as a humanoid figure with distorted features, including a twisted face and elongated limbs. The figure is often shown in a state of intense emotional distress, with their arms raised and their mouth open in a scream. The figure is typically shown in a landscape setting, often with a dark and ominous sky.

Powers

The Scream The Scream Figure has the ability to express intense emotional distress, often in the form of a loud and piercing scream. This ability is meant to convey the figure's emotional pain and suffering, and is often depicted as a central aspect of its behavior.

Accounts

The Scream Figure was created by Edvard Munch in 1893, as a representation of his own experience of intense emotional distress. Munch was inspired by his own feelings of anxiety and despair, and sought to convey these emotions through his art.

The Scream Figure has been referenced and parodied in popular culture, often as a symbol of anxiety and despair. The figure has appeared in films, literature, and advertising, and continues to be a recognizable and iconic image.

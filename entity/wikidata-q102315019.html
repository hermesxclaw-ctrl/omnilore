The Approach

In a ruined Hidden Leaf Village, a scarred young man tells Boruto Uzumaki that the age of shinobi is over. That moment, glimpsed in the very first pages of Boruto, defines the enigma of Kawaki: a boy bought from an abusive father, turned into a weapon by a cult, and saved by the unconditional kindness of a Hokage who would become his true father. His journey from a cold, broken vessel to a caring member of the Uzumaki family is one of the most compelling arcs in modern shonen manga.

Content advisory. Depictions of child abuse, experimentation on children, violence, and psychological trauma.
Identity

Kawaki Uzumaki

Pronunciation Kah-wah-kee Oo-zoo-mah-kee

Etymology

Kawaki (カワキ) is a Japanese name that can be written with various kanji; in the series, it is typically written in katakana. The surname Uzumaki (うずまき) is given to him upon adoption by Naruto, meaning 'spiral'.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese manga and anime franchise, created by Ukyō Kodachi and Mikio Ikemoto, with original concept by Masashi Kishimoto.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans have speculated that Kawaki might be a reincarnation or clone of a known character, but this is not supported by the source material.

Description

Appearance

Kawaki is a tall, lean young man with spiky dark hair and a distinctive tattoo of the Roman numeral IX under his left eye. He typically wears a black high-collared coat with a red interior, black pants, and sandals. After his adoption, he wears a simple dark shirt and pants. In the flashforward, he is shown with a scar across his nose and a more rugged appearance.

Symbols & Iconography

The Karma mark, a diamond-shaped seal that appears on his right palm, is a central symbol of his connection to the Ōtsutsuki. The number IX tattoo indicates his designation as the ninth vessel candidate.

Behaviour & Personality

Initially, Kawaki is cold, distant, and distrustful, a result of his traumatic past. He is blunt and often speaks without tact. Under Naruto's care, he gradually opens up, showing a protective and caring side, especially toward the Uzumaki family. He is fiercely loyal to those he considers family.

Domains & Habitats

His original home is unknown; he was raised in a harsh environment. He later resides in Konohagakure (Hidden Leaf Village) with the Uzumaki family.

Version Differences

In the manga, Kawaki's backstory is told in a more condensed manner, while the anime expands on his daily life and relationships, particularly with Himawari. The anime also includes original scenes not in the manga, such as his fight with Garo.

Powers & Abilities

Karma

Source Version Both manga and anime

Description

A seal that allows him to absorb and release chakra and techniques, and also acts as a catalyst for Ōtsutsuki reincarnation. It grants immense power but also threatens to overwrite his consciousness.

Limitations

Using Karma accelerates the reincarnation process, and overuse can lead to loss of control. It can be extracted, but at great risk.

Contradictions

The exact mechanics of Karma have been explained differently at various points, leading to some fan confusion.

Scientific Ninja Tools (Shinobi-Ware)

Source Version Both manga and anime

Description

Microscopic devices implanted in his body that allow him to alter his physiology, such as creating weapons from his arms or enhancing his physical capabilities.

Limitations

The tools can be damaged or malfunction, and they are a reminder of his traumatic past.

Contradictions

None noted.

Combat Skills

Source Version Both manga and anime

Description

Kawaki is a highly skilled hand-to-hand combatant, trained by Kara and later by Naruto. He is also proficient in using his Karma to absorb ninjutsu.

Limitations

Initially, he lacks formal ninja training and relies on brute force and his implants.

Contradictions

None noted.

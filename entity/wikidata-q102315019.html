Who It Is

In a ruined Hidden Leaf Village, a scarred young man tells Boruto Uzumaki that the age of shinobi is over. That moment, glimpsed in the very first pages of Boruto, defines the enigma of Kawaki: a boy bought from an abusive father, turned into a weapon by a cult, and saved by the unconditional kindness of a Hokage who would become his true father. His journey from a cold, broken vessel to a caring member of the Uzumaki family is one of the most compelling arcs in modern shonen manga.

Initially, Kawaki is cold, distant, and distrustful, a result of his traumatic past. He is blunt and often speaks without tact. Under Naruto's care, he gradually opens up, showing a protective and caring side, especially toward the Uzumaki family. He is fiercely loyal to those he considers family.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Kawaki (カワキ) is a Japanese name that can be written with various kanji; in the series, it is typically written in katakana. The surname Uzumaki (うずまき) is given to him upon adoption by Naruto, meaning 'spiral'.

Pronunciation — Kah-wah-kee Oo-zoo-mah-kee

Aliases — Kawaki, Vessel, The Vessel, Karma Vessel

Ownership — Japanese manga and anime franchise, created by Ukyō Kodachi and Mikio Ikemoto, with original concept by Masashi Kishimoto.

Powers
Karma

A seal that allows him to absorb and release chakra and techniques, and also acts as a catalyst for Ōtsutsuki reincarnation. It grants immense power but also threatens to overwrite his consciousness.

Limit: Using Karma accelerates the reincarnation process, and overuse can lead to loss of control. It can be extracted, but at great risk.

Scientific Ninja Tools (Shinobi-Ware)

Microscopic devices implanted in his body that allow him to alter his physiology, such as creating weapons from his arms or enhancing his physical capabilities.

Limit: The tools can be damaged or malfunction, and they are a reminder of his traumatic past.

Combat Skills

Kawaki is a highly skilled hand-to-hand combatant, trained by Kara and later by Naruto. He is also proficient in using his Karma to absorb ninjutsu.

Limit: Initially, he lacks formal ninja training and relies on brute force and his implants.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Kawaki is a tall, lean young man with spiky dark hair and a distinctive tattoo of the Roman numeral IX under his left eye. He typically wears a black high-collared coat with a red interior, black pants, and sandals. After his adoption, he wears a simple dark shirt and pants. In the flashforward, he is shown with a scar across his nose and a more rugged appearance.

Symbols — The Karma mark, a diamond-shaped seal that appears on his right palm, is a central symbol of his connection to the Ōtsutsuki. The number IX tattoo indicates his designation as the ninth vessel candidate.

Domains — His original home is unknown; he was raised in a harsh environment. He later resides in Konohagakure (Hidden Leaf Village) with the Uzumaki family.

Main timeline

Kawaki is found as a 'vessel' lost by Kara. He is brought to Konoha by Boruto's team, and Naruto takes him in for protection. Kawaki begins to integrate into the Uzumaki family.

Main timeline

Kara member Delta attacks Konoha to retrieve Kawaki. Naruto defeats her, and Kawaki witnesses Naruto's strength and kindness. Kawaki asks Naruto to train him as a ninja.

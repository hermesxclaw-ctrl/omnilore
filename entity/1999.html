Who It Is

In the dead of night, a forgotten television station flickers back to life, broadcasting a year that never happened—1999. But this is not the year you remember. It is a loop, a glitch in the fabric of reality, and the children who watch are never quite the same.

The narrative is told from the perspective of a first-person narrator who becomes increasingly obsessed with the tape. The tone is paranoid and anxious, reflecting the narrator's descent into fear. The fictional station and show are depicted as malevolent, with the show's host, Mr. Bear, acting as a sinister figure.

Powers
Temporal Anomaly

The broadcast appears to be from 1999, but it is not the 1999 we know. It depicts an alternate timeline or a glitch in reality, suggesting that the station is transmitting from a different dimension or time.

Limit: The anomaly is only accessible through the tape; it does not affect the real world directly.

Psychological Influence

The tape exerts a powerful psychological effect on viewers, causing obsession, paranoia, and hallucinations. The narrator becomes unable to stop watching and analyzing the tape.

Limit: The effect is not immediate and seems to require repeated viewing.

Mr. Bear's Malevolence

Mr. Bear, the host of the children's show, appears to be a malevolent entity. He speaks in a distorted voice, and his actions within the show are threatening, implying harm to children.

Limit: Mr. Bear is confined to the broadcast; he cannot directly interact with the real world.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The story is presented as a series of found documents, transcripts, and forum posts detailing the experiences of a user who claims to have received a VHS tape containing a strange broadcast from a station called Caledon Local 21, dated 1999. The broadcast features a children's show called 'Mr. Bear's Cellar,' which is unsettling and eerie, with distorted visuals and disturbing content.

Symbols — Key symbols include the station's logo, a stylized '21' with a bear emblem, and the recurring image of a bear's face. The number '1999' itself is a symbol of the temporal anomaly.

Domains — The story is set in the late 2010s, but the tape depicts a broadcast from 1999. The fictional station is located in Caledon, a town in Ontario, Canada. The 'cellar' of the title suggests a subterranean, hidden space where the show is filmed.

The Discovery of the TapeOriginal creepypasta

c. 2018

The narrator finds a VHS tape at a garage sale or in an abandoned building. The tape contains a broadcast from Caledon Local 21, a station that seemingly never existed. The broadcast includes a children's show, 'Mr. Bear's Cellar,' which is deeply unsettling. The narrator becomes obsessed with the tape, researching the station and the show, but finds no evidence they ever existed.

The Unsettling BroadcastOriginal creepypasta

c. 2018

The narrator describes the content of the broadcast: a children's show with a bear puppet named Mr. Bear, who speaks in a distorted voice and engages in bizarre, threatening activities. The show is interspersed with static and strange symbols. The narrator becomes increasingly disturbed, noting that the show seems to address him directly at times.

Accounts II
The Search for AnswersOriginal creepypasta

c. 2018

The narrator tries to find information about Caledon Local 21 and 'Mr. Bear's Cellar.' They search online, contact local historical societies, and visit the purported location of the station, but find nothing. The station seems to have been erased from history. The narrator's obsession deepens, and they begin to experience strange occurrences, such as hearing the show's theme song when the TV is off.

The Creeping MadnessOriginal creepypasta

c. 2018

As the narrator continues to watch the tape, they begin to lose their grip on reality. They see Mr. Bear in their peripheral vision, hear his voice in their head, and start to question whether they are still in the same timeline. The narrator becomes paranoid and isolated, eventually stopping communication with friends and family.

The Final BroadcastOriginal creepypasta

c. 2018

The tape ends with a final segment where Mr. Bear addresses the viewer directly, saying that the year is not 1999 but something else, and that the real 1999 is a lie. The broadcast then cuts to static, and the tape ends. The narrator is left with more questions than answers, and the story concludes with a sense of dread and uncertainty.

The events of the creepypasta are based on a real lost television broadcast. — There is no evidence that Caledon Local 21 or 'Mr. Bear's Cellar' ever existed. The story is entirely fictional.

The year 1999 was somehow altered or erased. — 1999 was a normal year with documented events. The creepypasta's premise of a 'false 1999' is purely fictional.

The creepypasta is a precursor to the 'Mandela Effect'. — The Mandela Effect is a separate phenomenon involving collective false memories. While both involve altered reality, they are unrelated.

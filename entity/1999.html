The Approach

In the dead of night, a forgotten television station flickers back to life, broadcasting a year that never happened—1999. But this is not the year you remember. It is a loop, a glitch in the fabric of reality, and the children who watch are never quite the same.

Content advisory. Contains disturbing themes, psychological horror, references to child endangerment, and unsettling imagery.

Etymology

The title '1999' refers to the year the story purports to be from, a year that is presented as a glitch or anomaly in the timeline. 'Caledon Local 21' is the name of the fictional television station, and 'Mr. Bear's Cellar' is the name of a children's show within the story.

Cultural Ownership

The creepypasta originated within the online horror community, particularly on platforms like Reddit and YouTube. It is a piece of internet folklore, with no single author, though it is often attributed to an anonymous creator or collective.

Disputed Identifications

There is no real television station called Caledon Local 21, and the events described are fictional. However, some fans have speculated that the story is based on real broadcast signal intrusions or lost media, but these are unsubstantiated.

Description

Appearance

The story is presented as a series of found documents, transcripts, and forum posts detailing the experiences of a user who claims to have received a VHS tape containing a strange broadcast from a station called Caledon Local 21, dated 1999. The broadcast features a children's show called 'Mr. Bear's Cellar,' which is unsettling and eerie, with distorted visuals and disturbing content.

Symbols & Iconography

Key symbols include the station's logo, a stylized '21' with a bear emblem, and the recurring image of a bear's face. The number '1999' itself is a symbol of the temporal anomaly.

Behaviour & Personality

The narrative is told from the perspective of a first-person narrator who becomes increasingly obsessed with the tape. The tone is paranoid and anxious, reflecting the narrator's descent into fear. The fictional station and show are depicted as malevolent, with the show's host, Mr. Bear, acting as a sinister figure.

Domains & Habitats

The story is set in the late 2010s, but the tape depicts a broadcast from 1999. The fictional station is located in Caledon, a town in Ontario, Canada. The 'cellar' of the title suggests a subterranean, hidden space where the show is filmed.

Version Differences

There are multiple versions of the creepypasta, with variations in the details of the broadcast, the nature of the anomaly, and the ending. Some versions emphasize the psychological horror of the narrator's obsession, while others focus on the supernatural elements. The story has been expanded by fans with additional lore.

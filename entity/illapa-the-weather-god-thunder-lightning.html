Illapa (the Weather-God / Thunder / Lightning)

also known asthe Weather-God / Thunder / Lightning
Typegod (weather, thunder, lightning, rain)
Who It Is
Form

Illapa is often depicted as a powerful, muscular man with a fierce expression, carrying a sling or a lightning bolt in his hand. In some representations, he is shown riding a storm cloud or standing on a mountain peak.

Powers

Control over the Weather Illapa has the power to control the weather, summoning storms and unleashing lightning bolts upon the world.

Accounts

According to the Inca myth, Illapa was one of the gods who helped create the world. He was tasked with bringing light and life to the world, and he accomplished this by summoning a great storm that brought forth the sun, the moon, and the stars.

According to the Quipu Database, Illapa was once a mortal man who was chosen by the gods to become a god himself. He was given the power to control the weather, and he used this power to bring forth a great storm that destroyed a rival city.

According to the Inca Codex, Illapa was worshipped by a cult of priests who believed that he was the god of thunder and lightning. They built temples and offered sacrifices to him, and they believed that he would bring them good fortune and prosperity.

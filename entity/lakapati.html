Who It Is

Lakapati is an androgynous/hermaphroditic deity of fertility and agriculture. The name combines 'Lakha' (lord) and 'Pati' (lady), reflecting dual-gendered nature considered sacred. Farmers offered first fruits and associated them with the carabao. Their worship represents a pre-colonial understanding of gender far more fluid than colonial norms.

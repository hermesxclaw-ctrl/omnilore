Who It Is
Form

El is often depicted as a wise and benevolent deity, with a long white beard and a crown or headband. In some representations, El is shown with a sceptre or staff, symbolizing his authority and power. The appearance of El is not consistent across all ancient Near Eastern cultures, with some traditions depicting the deity as a powerful warrior or a fertility god. For example, in Canaanite mythology, El is often depicted as a robust and virile deity, while in Israelite tradition, El is often associated with the image of an elderly wise man. The appearance of El can also be seen in the numerous artifacts and images that have been discovered, including the famous 'El' statue from the ancient city of Ugarit.

Powers

Divine Wisdom El is often credited with possessing divine wisdom, which enables him to understand the workings of the universe and the secrets of human destiny. This power is often invoked in rituals and ceremonies, where El is asked to provide guidance and wisdom to those who seek it.

The myth tells the story of the god Baal, who is the son of El and the goddess Asherah. Baal is challenged by the god Mot, who seeks to overthrow him and take his place as the ruler of the universe. El intervenes on behalf of Baal, using his divine wisdom and power to defeat Mot and restore order to the universe.

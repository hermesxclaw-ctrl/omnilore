Who It Is

The aged patriarch of the Canaanite pantheon, El (lit. 'god') is the venerable creator-king whose authority undergirds the cosmic order. Unlike the younger storm-god Baal, El is a distant, bearded figure enthroned at the cosmic mountain at the 'source of the two rivers' — the paradisiacal seat of divine governance. El is styled 'Bull El' (tr 'il) for his procreative potency, and the standard Ugaritic phrase 'Father of the Gods' (ab bn 'il) affirms his generative sovereignty over the pantheon's seventy-two divine sons. While El confers kingship and ultimately decrees the rise and fall of rulers, he is largely detached from the seasonal drama of Baal, Yam, and Mot — a god of stable, patriarchal order rather than dynamic conflict.

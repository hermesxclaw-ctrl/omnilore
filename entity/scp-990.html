[In a dream, a man with no face leans close and speaks directly into the dreamer's mind:] They will breach at 0347. Site-19. Sector 7. You have four hours.

Who It Is

SCP-990 is an anomalous entity that manifests exclusively in the dreams of Foundation personnel. It appears as a man in a gray business suit, approximately 40 years of age, with a calm and professional demeanor. Its most notable physical feature is the absence of facial features — where eyes, nose, and mouth should be, there is only smooth, blank skin. Despite having no mouth, SCP-990 'speaks' to dreamers — the words are perceived directly in the mind rather than heard through auditory channels. SCP-990's purpose appears to be to deliver warnings. It appears to Foundation personnel — always personnel with relevant authority or knowledge — hours or days before a significant containment breach, disaster, or security failure, providing cryptic but actionable information. In one documented case, SCP-990 appeared to a Site Director and delivered the exact time, location, and nature of a pending containment breach involving SCP-███. The warning enabled the Foundation to intercept and prevent the breach. Attempts to communicate with SCP-990 outside of dreams have been universally unsuccessful. It does not respond to direct questioning, and it only appears when it has a warning to deliver. The entity's origin is unknown. Some researchers propose SCP-990 may be a deceased Foundation researcher whose consciousness persisted in a non-corporeal form; others suggest it may be an agent of a benevolent anomalous group. The Foundation classifies SCP-990 as Thaumiel — useful for containment of other anomalies — and has established dream monitoring protocols for key personnel to ensure that SCP-990's warnings are documented and acted upon promptly. SCP-990 is one of the rare unambiguously beneficial entities in the Foundation catalog, though its blank, faceless presence and cryptic messages make even its help unsettling. There is always the lingering question: if SCP-990 can see the future, what does it see coming that is so bad it keeps coming back to warn us?

Who It Is

When the world's most feared martial artist unleashes his inner demon, the very air crackles with dark energy. This is the Oni form of Akuma, a transformation that blurs the line between man and beast, a state of being that exists to test the limits of power and obsession.

In Oni form, Akuma is consumed by the Satsui no Hado. He is less calculating and more purely destructive. His dialogue is often reduced to growls and shouts of 'Gou Hadouken!' He seems to be in a state of constant, barely contained rage, seeking only the ultimate battle. This form is often portrayed as a loss of self, a surrender to the dark power.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Oni' derives from Japanese folklore, referring to a type of ogre or demon. The transformation represents Akuma surrendering fully to the Satsui no Hado, the 'Surge of Murderous Intent'.

Pronunciation — AH-koo-mah OH-nee

Aliases — Oni, Shin Akuma (in some interpretations), Demon Akuma

Ownership — Capcom, specifically the Street Fighter franchise. The concept of the oni is rooted in Japanese mythology, but this specific iteration is a modern creation.

Powers
Enhanced Physical Attributes

In Oni form, Akuma's physical strength, speed, and endurance are greatly amplified beyond his already superhuman levels. He is capable of shattering boulders and moving at speeds that blur the eye.

Limit: The transformation is temporary and may cause severe strain on his body.

Satsui no Hado Mastery

The Oni form represents Akuma's complete mastery and surrender to the Satsui no Hado, the 'Surge of Murderous Intent'. This grants him access to more powerful versions of his techniques, such as the Goshoryuken and the Shakunetsu Hadouken.

Limit: This mastery comes at the cost of his sanity, making him more reckless and less strategic.

New Techniques

The Oni form introduces new moves and variations, such as the 'Demon Arm' and a powerful air-based combo. These moves are more aggressive and have different properties than his standard moveset.

Limit: Many of these moves have significant startup or recovery frames, making them punishable if whiffed.

Rage State

The Oni form is often associated with a state of uncontrollable rage, which can enhance his offensive capabilities but also makes him more vulnerable to counter-attacks.

Limit: This state may not be controllable, and Akuma might attack indiscriminately.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Oni form radically alters Akuma's appearance. His skin becomes a deep, demonic red. His hair turns white and wild, standing on end. His chest is marked with glowing, tribal-like patterns. He wears tattered black pants and a dark sash. His eyes glow with a fierce, malevolent light. His muscles are more pronounced, and his posture is more bestial, often hunched and aggressive.

Symbols — The Oni form is a visual representation of the Satsui no Hado, the dark martial arts energy that Akuma has long struggled with. The red skin and white hair are classic oni traits in Japanese art. The glowing patterns on his chest are often interpreted as a manifestation of the Satsui no Hado's corruption.

Domains — The Oni form does not have a specific habitat; it is a temporary transformation triggered by extreme circumstances. It is often depicted in isolated, dramatic locations, such as volcanic mountains or stormy skies, reflecting its destructive nature.

Accounts I
The Awakening of the OniUltra Street Fighter IV

2014

In Ultra Street Fighter IV, Akuma's Oni form is introduced as a hidden, powerful version of the character. The game's arcade mode does not provide a detailed story, but the character's ending suggests that Akuma, after defeating the strongest opponents, allows the Satsui no Hado to consume him completely, transforming into the Oni. This form is portrayed as the ultimate manifestation of his dark power, a being that exists solely to fight and destroy. The transformation is depicted as a point of no return, where Akuma loses his humanity and becomes a pure demon. This raises questions about his future in the series.

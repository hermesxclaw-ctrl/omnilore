Who It Is
Form

Illapa is often depicted as a powerful storm god, with the ability to control the weather. He is often shown as a strong, muscular man with a fierce expression, surrounded by lightning and thunder.

Powers

Weather Control Illapa has the ability to control the weather, summoning storms and bringing fertility to the land. He is often depicted as a powerful storm god, who uses his powers to protect his people and bring prosperity to the land.

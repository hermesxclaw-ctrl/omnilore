Who It Is

She is a kissling gourami who dreams of being a designer, giggles over takoyaki, and nearly gets sold on an auction block for being what she is — a mermaid in a world that prices mermaids by the fin.

Attested: Extremely airheaded, optimistic, naive, and kind; speech pattern includes frequent 'kyaa' and malapropisms; cries easily; deeply loyal to friends Hatchan and Pappag. Loves takoyaki. Dreams of becoming a fashion designer working with Pappag (who is a fashion designer starfish). Naive to danger - does not initially grasp severity of being kidnapped for slavery. Shows bravery when supporting friends on Fish-Man Island. Disputed/interpretive: Some fan discourse reads her as comic relief vs. symbolic victim of systemic racism; both readings are interpretive, not author-stated

Powers
Mermaid Physiology - Enhanced Swimming

As a mermaid, Camie can swim at speeds far exceeding humans, described in One Piece lore as twice as fast as Fish-Men in water. Can survive deep-sea pressure of Fish-Man Island depth and breathe underwater.

Limit: Cannot walk on land without bubble/coating assistance; tail does not split into legs until age 30 per One Piece merfolk rule, so at age 14-16 she is fully tail-bound. Vulnerable out of water, easily captured on land.

Fish Communication (limited)

Mermaids in One Piece can communicate with fish, though Camie is not shown using this as extensively as Princess Shirahoshi (who has Poseidon ability). She is shown calling/ interacting with sea creatures casually.

Limit: No evidence Camie possesses the ancient weapon Poseidon (Shirahoshi's unique power to command Sea Kings). Her ability is ordinary merfolk level.

No Combat Abilities

Camie has no attested combat techniques, Haki, Devil Fruit, or weapon use. She is a non-combatant civilian.

Limit: Completely dependent on allies (Hatchan, Straw Hats, Neptune Army) for protection.

Accounts I
Debut with Hatchan and Pappag — Meeting the Straw HatsOne Piece manga Ch. 490-491; anime Ep. 385-386

2008 (publication)

Camie, traveling with her friends Hatchan (octopus fish-man, former Arlong Pirate) and Pappag (talking starfish fashion designer), is lost near Sabaody Archipelago. The trio encounters the Straw Hat Pirates after saving them from the Flying Fish Riders kidnapping gang (Macro, Tansui, Ob). Camie is introduced as an employee of the Mermaid Cafe on Fish-Man Island, starry-eyed and obsessed with takoyaki, immediately befriending Nami and Luffy. Establishes Camie as sympathetic merfolk guide to Fish-Man Island lore; sets up kidnapping plot; reveals Hatchan's redemption arc.

Kidnapped — The Flying Fish Riders AbductionOne Piece manga Ch. 491-492; anime Ep. 386-387

2008

Shortly after befriending the Straw Hats, Camie is abducted by the Macro gang working for the Human Auction House. Despite Hatchan and Pappag's efforts, she is netted and taken to be sold as a mermaid slave, a highly prized commodity (mermaids fetch 70 million Beli at auction). The Straw Hats, along with Hatchan and Pappag, pursue to Sabaody Park and the auction house. Triggers the Sabaody Archipelago climax; introduces the in-universe slave trade and Celestial Dragon cruelty.

Human Auction House — The Celestial Dragon IncidentOne Piece manga Ch. 499-504; anime Ep. 391-402

2008-2009

Camie is displayed on stage at the Human Auction House on Sabaody Grove 1, collared with an explosive slave collar. She is to be sold to Saint Charlos, a World Noble (Celestial Dragon), who shoots Hatchan when he tries to save her. Luffy, enraged by the shooting and Charlos's attempt to purchase Camie, punches Charlos — an act of treason that summons Admiral Kizaru and triggers the Sabaody crisis. Silvers Rayleigh, the Dark King, uses Conqueror's Haki to knock out guards and removes Camie's collar with precision, freeing her. The Straw Hats, Rayleigh, and Hatchan escape with Camie. Directly causes Marine Admiral intervention, Straw Hat separation by Bartholomew Kuma, and two-year timeskip. Camie's rescue cements Straw Hat alliance with Hatchan and later Fish-Man Island. Becomes one of the most cited scenes on One Piece's critique of slavery and racism.

Accounts II
Return to Fish-Man Island — Mermaid Cafe ReunionOne Piece manga Ch. 605-608; anime Ep. 524-526

2011

After the two-year timeskip, the Straw Hats reunite and journey to Fish-Man Island. They are welcomed by Camie, now 16, working at the Mermaid Cafe with her mermaid friends. She guides Nami and the crew through Mermaid Cove, introduces them to Madame Shirley and the island's customs, and provides exposition on Fish-Man Island's history, the Tamate Box, and the discrimination between humans and fish-men. Re-establishes Camie as cultural bridge; provides lore dump for Fish-Man Island arc themes of coexistence.

Fish-Man Island Crisis — Supporting During Hody Jones CoupOne Piece manga Ch. 615-653; anime Ep. 534-574

2011-2012

During Hody Jones and Vander Decken IX's coup against King Neptune, Camie is among the civilians threatened. She shelters with Pappag and other mermaids, helps evacuate Mermaid Cafe patrons, and later celebrates the Straw Hats' victory. She is present at the Ryugu Palace banquet and witnesses the revelation of Joy Boy's apology Poneglyph and Shirahoshi's importance. Shows civilian cost of extremist ideology; Camie's survival underscores theme that ordinary merfolk suffer from both human prejudice and fish-man supremacism.

Cover Story — Hatchan's Sea-Floor Stroll (Miniseries)One Piece manga cover stories, Ch. 182-228 (Hatchan's Sea-Floor Stroll)

2001-2002 (pre-dates Camie but establishes Hatchan's post-Arlong life that leads to Camie)

Although Camie does not appear in this early cover story, it establishes Hatchan's exile and redemption after Arlong Park, his friendship with Camie and Pappag later referenced as continuation. Often cited in Camie relationship context as backstory for her protector. Provides continuity for Camie's found-family dynamic; demonstrates Oda's long-term planting of characters.

Camie is an ancient mythological mermaid from Japanese or Chilean folklore. — No pre-modern text, inscription, or oral-history collection attests a beast named Camie. She first appears in One Piece Chapter 490 (2008). The global mermaid archetype is ancient, but Camie as named entity is a 21st-century creation by Eiichiro Oda.

Camie can turn into a human at will or has the Poseidon power to control Sea Kings. — In One Piece lore, mermaids gain legs only at age 30 when tail splits; Camie is 14-16 and cannot. Poseidon is unique to Princess Shirahoshi, not all mermaids. Camie has no such power in any canonical source.

Camie is a main Straw Hat crew member. — She is a recurring supporting character / ally, not a crew member. She appears in two arcs (Sabaody, Fish-Man Island) and cameos thereafter, but never joins the crew and has no bounty or combat role.

The name is pronounced 'KAY-mee' and spelled 'Keimi' is the only correct form. — Japanese is ke-i-mii, but official English localization (Viz, Funimation, Vivre Card) standardizes to 'Camie' pronounced KAM-ee. Both transliterations refer to same character; 'Camie' is publisher-preferred.

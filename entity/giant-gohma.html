Who It Is

A colossal armored arachnid the size of a temple, with a single bloodshot eye at the center of its carapace. Giant Gohma is the progenitor of all Gohma in Hyrule, a spawn of the demonic forces that once nearly overran the land. Its shell is impervious to most weapons; only a well-aimed strike to the eye can harm it.

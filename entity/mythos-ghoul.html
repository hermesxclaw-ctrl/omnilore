Who It Is

Not the undead of folklore — these ghouls are a separate species of semi-human that dwells in tunnels beneath graveyards. They have long, dog-like snouts, rubbery skin, hooves, and a taste for human flesh. They are intelligent, speak their own language, and maintain a complex society in the underworld.

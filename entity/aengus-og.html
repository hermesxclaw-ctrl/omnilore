Who It Is

Aengus Óg is the god of love and youth, son of the Dagda. He tricked his father out of Brú na Bóinne by asking to dwell there 'for a day and a night' — in Old Irish idiom meaning all of eternity. He fell in love with Caer Ibormeith from a dream, found her among 150 swan-maidens, and flew away with her as a swan.

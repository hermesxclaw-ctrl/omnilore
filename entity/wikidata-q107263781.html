Who It Is

In the world of Teyvat, where the winds carry both freedom and memory, there drifts a bard whose songs are as old as the sky itself. He is Venti, the Anemo Archon, the God of Freedom, and the patron of Mondstadt—a figure who appears as a carefree minstrel, yet whose presence shapes the destiny of a nation. To know Venti is to understand that freedom is not a gift but a responsibility, and that the winds of change are never as gentle as they seem.

Venti is carefree, mischievous, and playful, often appearing as a drunkard who loves wine and songs. He is deeply intelligent and perceptive, using his persona to hide his true responsibilities. He is fiercely protective of Mondstadt and its people, valuing freedom above all else. He is also a poet and musician, composing songs that carry historical truths and subtle messages. He is known to be a trickster, but his deceptions are usually in service of a greater good.

Powers
Anemo Vision

Venti wields an Anemo Vision, allowing him to control wind. In gameplay, this manifests as abilities like 'Skyward Sonnet' (a wind vortex that lifts enemies) and 'Wind's Grand Ode' (a massive storm that deals continuous damage).

Limit: His Vision is a focus for his power, but as an Archon, he may not need it; he uses it to blend in with mortals.

Divine Authority

As the Anemo Archon, Venti has authority over the wind and can influence the weather, create powerful storms, and perhaps even control the flow of time to a degree. He can also shape-shift, as seen when he took the form of a female bard in a cutscene.

Limit: After the Cataclysm, Archons' powers waned, and Venti explicitly states he is not as strong as he once was. He also refrains from using his divine powers in mortal affairs, preferring to guide rather than rule.

Immortality

As an Archon, Venti is immortal and has lived for over 2,600 years. He does not age and is immune to most physical harm.

Limit: He can be affected by emotional or spiritual wounds, and he is not omnipotent.

Musical Magic

Venti's lyre playing can have magical effects, such as calming dragons or creating wind currents. In the game, he uses his lyre to interact with the environment and solve puzzles.

Limit: The extent of this magic is not fully explained, and it may be tied to his divine nature.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Venti is a slender young man with fair skin, teal eyes, and short dark blue-black hair with two distinctive braids at the front. He wears a green and white outfit with a beret adorned with a Cecilia flower, a white shirt, green shorts, a cape, and a lyre at his side. His attire is reminiscent of a medieval bard, and he often carries a wooden lyre. As Barbatos, he is depicted in a more divine form, but this is not shown in-game.

Symbols — Venti is associated with the wind, freedom, and the dandelion, which is the symbol of Mondstadt. His signature weapon is the lyre, specifically the 'Skyward Harp' in his character art. The Anemo vision he uses is a glass-like ornament, and his constellation is 'Carmen Dei' (Song of God). The wind is often depicted as blue or green swirls, and his cape resembles the shape of a bird's wing.

Domains — Venti is most often found in Mondstadt, particularly in the city and its surrounding areas such as Windrise and the Thousand Winds Temple. As the Anemo Archon, he is connected to the element of wind and can traverse the skies. He is also associated with the realm of the gods, though he prefers to live among mortals.

Accounts I
The Founding of MondstadtArchon Quest, Character Story

2,600 years ago

During the time of the tyrannical God of Storms, Decarabian, the people of Old Mondstadt were trapped in a city of ice and wind. Barbatos was originally a small wind spirit who, inspired by the rebel leader Gunnhildr, took on the form of a human to help lead the rebellion. Together with the people, they overthrew Decarabian, and Barbatos became the Anemo Archon, shaping the new Mondstadt with his winds. He then taught the people how to build their own city, and he disappeared, leaving them to govern themselves. Mondstadt was founded as a free nation, and Venti became its patron god, but he chose to remain a silent guardian, only intervening in times of crisis.

The Fall of the Old Archon WarCharacter Story, Lore

2,600 years ago

The Archon War was a conflict among the gods of Teyvat to determine who would become the seven Archons. Barbatos fought and emerged as one of the victors, gaining the title of Anemo Archon. The war devastated much of the land, and Venti's role is somewhat ambiguous, as he was not originally a god of war but rather a wind spirit. Barbatos became one of the seven Archons, and Mondstadt was established as his domain.

Accounts II
The Cataclysm 500 Years AgoLore, Character Story

500 years ago

During the Cataclysm, a great disaster struck Teyvat, leading to the destruction of the ancient civilization of Khaenri'ah. The Archons were involved, and Venti was one of them. He lost his original form and was reduced to a weaker state. The exact events are not fully revealed, but it is known that he helped to defeat the dragon Durin, who was corrupted by the abyss. Venti became weaker and went into a deep sleep for many years. The dragon Dvalin was injured and later corrupted by the Abyss Order, leading to the events of the game's prologue.

The Dragon CrisisArchon Quest Prologue

Present day

In the game's opening chapters, Venti assists the Traveler in calming the dragon Dvalin, who has been corrupted by the Abyss Order. Venti reveals his identity and helps the Traveler purify Dvalin's tears. He also confronts the Abyss Order's plans to destroy Mondstadt. Dvalin is saved and becomes a guardian of Mondstadt again. Venti leaves the Traveler to continue their journey, but his actions set the stage for the larger conflict with the Abyss Order.

Who It Is

In the world of One Piece, where pirates sail the Grand Line and the sea itself teems with monsters and mysteries, few figures command as much respect as Jinbe, the helmsman of the Straw Hat Pirates and a knight of the sea. A fish-man of immense strength and unwavering honor, Jinbe's journey from a warlord of the sea to a loyal crewmate is a testament to his deep-rooted sense of justice and his unyielding desire to bridge the divide between humans and fish-men.

Jinbe is calm, wise, and honorable. He is deeply respectful of others, especially his captain, Monkey D. Luffy, whom he serves with unwavering loyalty. He is a skilled negotiator and often acts as a mediator between humans and fish-men. Despite his gentle demeanor, he is a fierce fighter who will not hesitate to protect his friends. He is also known for his strong sense of duty and his willingness to sacrifice himself for the greater good.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Jinbe' is a pun on 'jinbeizame', the Japanese word for whale shark. This is fitting, as Jinbe's physical appearance and abilities are inspired by whale sharks.

Pronunciation — JEEN-bay

Aliases — Knight of the Sea, First Son of the Sea, Son of the Sea

Ownership — Japanese manga and anime franchise 'One Piece', created by Eiichiro Oda.

Powers
Fish-Man Karate

A martial art that harnesses the power of water. Jinbe can use it to attack enemies from a distance by manipulating the water in the air, and he can also use it to perform powerful close-range strikes. His signature moves include 'Karakusagawara Seiken' (a powerful palm thrust) and 'Vagabond Drill' (a spinning kick).

Limit: Requires a water source or moisture in the air to use some techniques; fish-man karate is less effective against opponents with water-based abilities.

Fish-Man Jujutsu

A complementary martial art that focuses on grappling and throws. Jinbe uses it to control the battlefield and defeat larger opponents.

Limit: Not as well-documented as fish-man karate; requires physical contact.

Helmsmanship

Jinbe is an exceptional helmsman, capable of steering ships through the most dangerous waters, including the Calm Belt and whirlpools. He is also able to read sea currents and weather patterns, making him invaluable to the Straw Hat Pirates.

Limit: Requires a ship and a crew; cannot control the sea itself.

Bubble Shield

Jinbe can create a protective bubble around himself or others, allowing them to survive underwater. This is a common ability among fish-men and merfolk.

Limit: The bubble is not indestructible and can be popped by strong attacks.

Physical Strength

As a fish-man, Jinbe possesses immense physical strength, far surpassing that of a normal human. He is capable of lifting massive objects and delivering powerful blows that can send enemies flying.

Limit: His strength is still limited compared to the strongest Devil Fruit users, and he cannot swim if he ever eats a Devil Fruit (which he has not).

Form & Signs

Appearance — Jinbe is a large, blue whale shark fish-man with a broad, flat head, large eyes, and a distinctive scar over his left eye. He has a thick, muscular build and webbed hands and feet. He typically wears a kimono-like robe with a sash, and carries a wooden geta on his feet. His most prominent feature is his sharp teeth, which he often displays in a confident grin.

Symbols — Jinbe's symbol is the sun pirate mark, which he bears on his left shoulder as a tattoo. This mark represents his allegiance to the Sun Pirates and his commitment to fighting for the freedom of fish-men and merfolk. He also wears a sash that holds his sword, though he rarely uses it in combat.

Domains — Jinbe is a fish-man, so he is perfectly adapted to life underwater. He is most at home in the sea, particularly in the waters around Fish-Man Island, where he was born. He can also survive on land, though he prefers to be near water. He is an expert helmsman, able to navigate the treacherous waters of the Grand Line with ease.

Accounts I
The Sun Pirates and Fisher TigerOne Piece manga chapters 620-626

20 years before the main timeline

Jinbe was a former member of the Sun Pirates, a crew of fish-men and merfolk led by Fisher Tiger, who fought for the liberation of slaves and the equality of fish-men. After Tiger's death, Jinbe took over leadership and continued his mission. He later became one of the Seven Warlords of the Sea to secure the safety of Fish-Man Island. Jinbe's involvement with the Sun Pirates shaped his ideology and his desire to bridge the gap between humans and fish-men.

Impel Down and MarinefordOne Piece manga chapters 528-580

Main timeline

Jinbe was imprisoned in Impel Down for refusing to fight in the Paramount War. He met Monkey D. Luffy there and helped him survive the prison's dangers. Together, they escaped and participated in the Battle of Marineford, where Jinbe protected Luffy from Admiral Akainu and helped him cope with the death of his brother, Portgas D. Ace. Jinbe's bond with Luffy deepened, and he promised to join Luffy's crew after settling a personal matter.

Accounts II
Fish-Man Island ArcOne Piece manga chapters 603-653

Main timeline

Jinbe reunites with Luffy and the Straw Hats at Fish-Man Island, where he helps them defeat the New Fish-Man Pirates and the mad scientist Hody Jones. He also reveals the history of the island and the Otohime's dream of coexistence between humans and fish-men. After the battle, he formally accepts Luffy's invitation to join the crew, but decides to stay behind to handle a political crisis. Jinbe officially becomes a member of the Straw Hat Pirates, though he does not join them until later.

Whole Cake Island ArcOne Piece manga chapters 825-902

Main timeline

Jinbe finally joins the Straw Hat Pirates during the Whole Cake Island arc. He helps them escape from Big Mom's territory, and he remains loyal to Luffy even when faced with Big Mom's wrath. He later reunites with the crew in Wano. Jinbe's loyalty is cemented, and he proves his worth as a helmsman and fighter.

Wano Country ArcOne Piece manga chapters 909-1057

Main timeline

Jinbe participates in the Raid on Onigashima, fighting alongside the Straw Hats and their allies against Kaido and Big Mom. He plays a crucial role in the battle, defeating several powerful enemies and helping to secure victory. Jinbe solidifies his position as a key member of the Straw Hat Pirates.

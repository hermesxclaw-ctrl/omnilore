Who It Is
Form

Cathbad is often depicted as a wise and powerful druid, with a long white beard and a staff. He is said to have a deep understanding of the natural world and the workings of fate.

Powers

Divination Cathbad is said to possess the power of divination, allowing him to see into the future and understand the workings of fate. He is said to use this power to advise his king and guide his people.

Accounts

Cathbad plays a key role in the story of the Táin, serving as a advisor to King Conchobar and helping to guide him through the challenges of the story.

Cathbad plays a key role in the story of the death of Cú Chulainn, serving as a advisor to the hero and helping to guide him through the challenges of the story.

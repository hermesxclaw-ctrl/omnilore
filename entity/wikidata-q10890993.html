Who It Is

At fourteen he already has a starship, a staff that freezes time, and the authority to arrest two nine-year-old girls for mishandling weapons of mass destruction. Chrono Harlaown is Magical Girl Lyrical Nanoha's answer to what happens when the stern, by-the-book admiral is still a teenager himself — brilliant, exhausted, and trying to enforce interdimensional law while everyone around him is still learning how to be a child.

Attested across all timelines: Chrono is stern, disciplined, highly competent, and outwardly cold, especially to Nanoha and Fate during the Jewel Seed incident where he treats them as dangerous civilians interfering with Lost Logia recovery. Internally, he is dutiful, anxious about living up to his late father Clyde Harlaown's legacy, and deeply loyal to his mother Lindy and crew. Name & Tongue

Etymology — Given name 'Chrono' from Greek khronos (χρόνος) 'time', consistent with franchise naming of Time-Space Administration Bureau personnel with temporal/nautical terms. Surname 'Harlaown' is an invented surname shared with mother Lindy Harlaown; no real-world etymology provided by creator Masaki Tsuzuki

Pronunciation — Japanese: ku-ro-no ha-ra-o-u-n [kɯɾono haɾaouɴ]. English dub: KRON-oh HAR-low-own (/ˈkrɒnoʊ hɑːrˈlaʊn/). Stress on first syllable of Chrono.

Aliases — Kurono Haraoun (Hepburn romanization), クロノ・ハラオウン (Japanese), Chrono Harlaown (official English credits), Admiral Chrono Harlaown (StrikerS onward)

Powers
Midchildan Magic - Ice/ Binding Specialization

Chrono is a Midchildan-system mage specializing in freezing, binding, and area-control magic rather than raw bombardment. His spells manifest as blue-white ice and geometric binding circles.

Limit: Requires Device to efficiently cast; high mana consumption for wide-area freezes; less effective against Belkan close-combat specialists (Signum) who can break bindings. Not a flight-speed or bombardment specialist like Nanoha.

S2U (Storage Device)

White staff-type Storage Device used through S1 and A's. Provides casting assistance, calculation, and spell storage. No AI personality (unlike Intelligent Devices Raising Heart/Bardiche).

Limit: Storage Device cannot auto-defend or speak; requires manual trigger and cartridge-less operation limits power ceiling compared to later cartridge systems.

Durandal (Storage Device, post-A's)

Upgraded Storage Device wielded by adult Chrono (A's epilogue, StrikerS). Cartridge System compatible, greatly amplifies freezing power. Named after the legendary sword Durandal.

Limit: Cartridge consumption causes recoil and requires resupply; still a support/control device, not a close-combat weapon.

Eternal Coffin (Extinction Freeze)

Signature wide-area freezing spell. Creates a massive ice field that encases targets in eternal ice, used to seal the Book of Darkness defense program and to contain Lost Logia. In StrikerS, enhanced version can freeze a city block.

Limit: Long casting time, requires Durandal and cartridge support for full scale; can be shattered by sufficiently powerful Belkan or Unison magic; indiscriminate — risks collateral freezing if not precisely targeted.

Stinger Series & Bind Spells (Stinger Blade, Struggle Bind)

Midchildan projectile and binding spells: Stinger (homing mana bolts), Struggle Bind (restraining rings), S2U-specific bindings. Used for capture and non-lethal restraint per Bureau doctrine.

Limit: Bindings can be broken by brute force or anti-magic fields; Stinger lacks stopping power against barrier jackets of equal rank.

Command & Tactical Authority

Not a magical power but a narrative ability: Chrono holds commissioned rank (Ensign → Lieutenant → Admiral) granting command of Arthra, authority to enforce TSAB law, coordinate mages, and authorize Lost Logia sealing. High tactical intelligence and knowledge of dimensional navigation.

Limit: Bound by Bureau law and chain of command; cannot overrule High Council or Admirals like Graham without evidence; youth initially undermines authority with civilians.

Accounts I
The Jewel Seed IncidentMagical Girl Lyrical Nanoha (TV 2004) Episodes 1-13; The MOVIE 1st (2010)

2004 in-universe (MC 0065 in Bureau calendar)

Nine-year-old Nanoha Takamachi and Fate Testarossa are unknowingly collecting the 21 Jewel Seeds, ancient Lost Logia that grant wishes but cause dimensional quakes. Chrono, 14, arrives aboard the Arthra as the Bureau's field officer tasked with recovering them. He initially confronts both girls as dangerous amateurs, dueling Fate and attempting to seal Nanoha's activity. After Yuuno Scrya explains Nanoha's cooperation, Chrono shifts to support, coordinating the final sealing operation against Precia Testarossa's activation of the Jewel Seeds to reach Al-Hazard. He provides dimensional sealing support and evacuates civilians while Nanoha and Fate confront Precia. After Precia's defeat and apparent death, Chrono oversees Fate's custody and testifies to her mitigating circumstances. Jewel Seeds secured by Bureau; Fate taken into Bureau custody rather than punished as a criminal, leading to Lindy's adoption; Nanoha formally befriends the Bureau; Chrono establishes working relationship with Nanoha/Fate that defines later series.

The Book of Darkness IncidentMagical Girl Lyrical Nanoha A's (TV 2005) Episodes 1-13; The MOVIE 2nd A's (2012)

Late 2004-early 2005 in-universe, six months after S1

The Book of Darkness (Reinforce) reawakens and its guardians, the Wolkenritter (Signum, Vita, Shamal, Zafira), begin draining linker cores from mages to fill its pages for their master Hayate Yagami. Chrono, now 15 and acting field commander under Lindy, leads the investigation. He is first to identify the Wolkenritter's Belkan magic system and is defeated by Signum in an early clash, demonstrating Belkan superiority in close combat. He coordinates with Nanoha, Fate, and Yuuno while uncovering that Admiral Graham, a senior Bureau officer, has been manipulating events to seal the Book permanently via Hayate's death. Chrono confronts Graham, defies orders to protect Hayate, and participates in the final battle against the Book's Defense Program (NachtWal). He casts Eternal Coffin to freeze the Defense Program's rampage, buying time for Hayate to merge with Reinforce and rewrite the Book's curse. In the epilogue six years later, adult Chrono (20, voiced by Sugita) watches Hayate's new unit depart. Book of Darkness destroyed/reincarnated as Reinforce Zwei; Hayate saved and joins Bureau;

Accounts II
Adoption of Fate and the Harlaown HouseholdMagical Girl Lyrical Nanoha A's Epilogue (ep.13), A's Sound Stage X, Official Fanbook

2005-2010 in-universe (between A's and StrikerS)

Following Precia's death and the Book of Darkness case, Fate is left legally orphaned and facing Bureau tribunal for her actions under Precia's coercion. Chrono, as Lindy's son and as the officer who witnessed Fate's abuse, supports Lindy's petition to adopt Fate. Bureau records show Chrono testifying that Fate acted under duress and possesses no inherent criminality. Fate becomes Fate T. Harlaown, Chrono's adoptive younger sister. Sound Stages depict domestic scenes where Chrono teases Fate but also acts as an older brother, helping her adjust to Bureau life and later to her enforcer career. This familial bond explains why Chrono and Fate can operate together in StrikerS without chain-of-command awkwardness. Fate gains legal protection and family; Harlaown family becomes central to Bureau's next generation; Chrono's later marriage to Amy makes Fate his sister-in-law as well.

StrikerS - The Cradle and Numbers IncidentsMagical Girl Lyrical Nanoha StrikerS (2007) Episodes 1-26

MC 0075, ten years after A's (2014-2015 in-universe); Chrono age 24-25

Now Admiral Chrono Harlaown, he no longer serves as frontline combatant but as high-level intelligence and operations officer at TSAB Headquarters. He supervises Long Arch and later the newly formed Lost Property Riot Force 6 (RF6) led by Nanoha, Fate, and Hayate. When Jail Scaglietti's Numbers (artificial mages) and the Saint's Cradle (ancient Belkan warship) threaten Midchilda, Chrono coordinates dimensional fleet movements, authorizes the use of Arc-en-Ciel (dimensional warship cannon), and provides critical intelligence on Scaglietti's history — revealing Scaglietti's connection to Project F (Fate's origin). He is present during the final battle, ordering the evacuation of Cranagan and the firing solution that disables the Cradle's reactor, while trusting the RF6 aces to handle interior combat. He also navigates Bureau politics, protecting RF6 from Council interference. Cradle destroyed, Scaglietti captured, Vivio rescued; RF6 disbanded and reformed into new units; Chrono's strategic leadership cements his reputation as Bureau's youngest Admiral.

Reflections and Legacy - ViVid, Force, and Movie DetonationMagical Girl Lyrical Nanoha ViVid (manga 2009-2017, anime 2015), Magical Record Lyrical Nanoha Force (manga 2009-2013), Magical Girl Lyrical Nanoha Detonation (2018 film)

MC 0079 onward (4 years after StrikerS); Chrono age late 20s

Chrono appears in supporting/legacy roles. In ViVid, he is referenced as Admiral and as part of the Harlaown family that supports Vivio's upbringing; cameo at Bureau headquarters overseeing the Inter-Middle Championship security. In Force, he appears in Bureau command discussing the Eclipse virus and Hückebein family, authorizing Thoma Avenir's involvement. In Detonation (2018), he appears briefly in Bureau command during the reappearance of the Mariage. Across these, his role has shifted from active mage to administrator and father figure — his marriage to Amy Limietta is confirmed, and they have twin children (Karen and Luka Harlaown, born circa MC 0076-0077, referenced in StrikerS sound stages and ViVid). His combat appearances are rare, emphasizing that his generation has passed the torch. Establishes Chrono's transition to family and administration; confirms continuity of Harlaown lineage; shows Bureau's evolution from military to peacekeeping.

Chrono can stop or control time because his name is Chrono. — He has no time-manipulation magic. His name is thematic for the Time-Space Administration Bureau, but his magic is ice/binding (Eternal Coffin). Time-related Lost Logia exist, but he does not wield them.

Chrono is Fate's biological older brother. — No blood relation. Fate is the artificial clone/daughter of Precia Testarossa. Chrono becomes her adoptive brother only after Lindy Harlaown adopts Fate post-A's (2005). They share the Harlaown surname legally after that point.

Chrono is a cold antagonist who hates Nanoha and Fate. — He is initially strict and antagonistic in S1 ep.5-6 as he enforces Bureau law, but quickly becomes an ally and advocate, especially for Fate's leniency. His sternness is professional, not personal cruelty.

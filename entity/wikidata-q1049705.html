Who It Is

She is the sky given blade and will: a race-car driver who outruns the wind, a guardian who reads omens in the air, and a lover who would shatter worlds to protect one heart. Sailor Uranus — Haruka Tenou — strides through the Sailor Moon mythos as its most unapologetically bold and tender paradox: a princess in a prince's guise, a fighter who kisses the moon princess on a whim, and a warrior whose sword is both a weapon and a vow.

Haruka is stubborn, protective, strong-willed, charming, and daring. She is often seen as an antihero due to her willingness to sacrifice for the greater good. She teases others but becomes flustered when teased. She is highly private and has difficulty with confessions. In the anime, she is more standoffish than in the manga.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Uranus' derives from the Greek god Ouranos (Οὐρανός), personifying the sky. 'Ten'ō' (天王) means 'heavenly king' or 'sky king', reinforcing the celestial and sky-associated theme.

Pronunciation — Japanese: [seːɾaː ɯɾanɯsɯ]; Haruka Tenou: [haɾɯka tenoː]; English: [sɛlər jʊˈreɪnəs], [hɑːrkɑː tɛnoʊ]

Aliases — Haruka Tenou, Amara Tenoh, Princess Uranus, Super Sailor Uranus, Eternal Sailor Uranus, Guardian of the Sky

Ownership — Created by Naoko Takeuchi, published by Kodansha. Owned by Takeuchi, Toei Animation, and related license holders. Part of Japanese popular culture and global anime fandom.

Powers
Transformation

Haruka transforms into Sailor Uranus by raising her hand or using the Lip Rod and shouting a phrase.

Limit: Requires the transformation device in the anime; in the manga, she can transform with just a gesture.

Wind and Sky Manipulation

Sailor Uranus can control wind and sky, run at incredible speeds, and sense omens in the wind.

Limit: Not explicitly shown in civilian form.

Precognition

Haruka has psychic sensitivity, often receiving dreams or hearing ill omens in the wind.

Limit: Vague and not fully controllable.

Space Sword

A talisman that is one of the three legendary talismans. It is a powerful sword used in combat.

Limit: Only effective when used by a worthy bearer.

World Shaking

Her signature attack, which creates a powerful energy blast or shockwave.

Limit: Requires transformation.

Uranus Crystal Power

After obtaining the Uranus Crystal, she gains a more powerful transformation and attacks.

Limit: Only in manga and Crystal (implied).

Form & Signs

Appearance — As Haruka: tall (5'11"), slim, with short blonde hair and blue eyes. Often wears male-coded clothing, including the Mugen Academy male uniform. As Sailor Uranus: wears a navy blue and yellow sailor suit, with a short skirt, mid-forearm gloves, and golden hoop earrings. Her uniform changes in higher forms: Super Sailor Uranus has more elaborate shoulder pads and a longer skirt; Eternal Sailor Uranus adds wings and a more ornate bodice.

Symbols — The Space Sword (a talisman), the Uranus Crystal, wind and sky motifs, the planet Uranus, the color gold. Her transformation phrase 'Uranus Planet Power, Make-up!' evokes celestial power.

Domains — As a Sailor Guardian, she is associated with the sky, wind, and flight. She lives in Tokyo, often in a luxurious apartment with Michiru. In the Silver Millennium, she resided in Miranda Castle on the planet Uranus.

Accounts I
Infinity ArcSailor Moon manga Act 36-44, anime episodes 106-127

1994-1995

Haruka and Michiru are introduced as mysterious figures investigating the Mugen Academy and the Death Busters. They initially keep their identities secret, even from the other Guardians. They later reveal themselves and join forces to protect the Solar System from the mastermind Pharaoh 90. They help defeat the Death Busters, but Hotaru is reborn as Sailor Saturn. Haruka, Michiru, and Setsuna vow to raise Hotaru as their family.

Dream ArcSailor Moon manga Act 45-49, anime episodes 128-166

1995-1996

The Dead Moon Circus attacks, and the Guardians are targeted in their dreams. Haruka and Michiru, along with Setsuna, are sidelined due to the threat to their dreams, but they eventually join the fight. Haruka gains her Super form. They help defeat the Dead Moon Circus, and Haruka achieves Super Sailor Uranus.

Accounts II
Stars ArcSailor Moon manga Act 50-60, anime episodes 167-200

1996-1997

The Sailor Animamates and Sailor Galaxia threaten the galaxy. Haruka and Michiru initially pretend to betray the Guardians to infiltrate Galaxia's forces, but they are later revealed to be loyal. They sacrifice themselves in battle but are revived in the end. They help defeat Galaxia and restore peace. Haruka gains Eternal form in the manga.

Silver MillenniumSailor Moon manga Act 48

Ancient past

In the Silver Millennium, Sailor Uranus was the princess of her planet and a guardian of the Solar System. She lived in Miranda Castle and wore a deep blue gown. The Silver Millennium fell to the Dark Kingdom, but the Sailor Guardians were reincarnated in the future.

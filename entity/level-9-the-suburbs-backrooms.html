Who It Is

An infinite suburban neighborhood at twilight. The houses are identical variations on a theme: two stories, beige siding, manicured lawns, cars in driveways. The lights are on inside. The TVs are playing. But the houses are empty — not abandoned, but never occupied. The neighborhood is a stage set for a life that was never lived. Wanderers who stay too long begin to forget they were ever anywhere else. The level doesn't kill you. It makes you forget you wanted to leave.

Form

Endless suburban streets under a perpetual orange-purple twilight sky. Every house is a variation: same floor plan, different decor. The lawns are always perfectly mowed. The mailboxes are always empty. The silence is that of a neighborhood where everyone vanished mid-sentence.

Etymology

The surname Tatsumi (辰巳) literally means 'Dragon Serpent' or refers to the direction southeast in the Chinese zodiac cycle. The given name Kōtarō (光太郎) is composed of 'kō' (光, light) and 'tarō' (太郎, a common male given name suffix meaning 'big son' or 'great man'). Thus, the name can be interpreted as 'Great Son of Light' or 'Light of the Southeast,' evoking heroic and auspicious connotations.

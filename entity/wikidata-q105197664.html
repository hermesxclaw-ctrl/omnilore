The Approach

In the world of Jujutsu Kaisen, where sorcerers battle curses born of human fear, Kasumi Miwa stands apart—not as a prodigy, but as a determined young woman who picked up a sword to pay her family's bills, proving that heroism can spring from the most mundane of motivations.

Content advisory. Contains references to violence and supernatural combat, consistent with the shonen genre. No explicit dark material.
Identity

Kasumi Miwa

Pronunciation KAH-soo-mee MEE-wah

Etymology

The surname 'Miwa' (三輪) is a Japanese surname that can be associated with the Miwa shrine in Nara, historically a center of worship. The given name 'Kasumi' (霞) means 'mist' or 'haze' in Japanese. The combination may evoke a sense of ethereal beauty, but no canonical etymology is provided in the series.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese popular culture; created by Gege Akutami for the manga Jujutsu Kaisen (2018–present). The character is part of a contemporary Japanese media franchise.

Disputed Identifications

None. Kasumi Miwa is a distinct fictional character with no known real-world counterpart or disputed identification.

Description

Appearance

Kasumi Miwa is a teenage girl with short, dark hair and large, dark eyes. She typically wears the Kyoto Jujutsu High uniform, which consists of a dark blue gakuran-style jacket and matching trousers. She carries a wooden sword, which she uses as a focus for her cursed energy. Her appearance is neat and modest, reflecting her disciplined nature.

Symbols & Iconography

Her primary symbol is the wooden sword, representing her dedication to swordsmanship despite lacking innate talent. She is also associated with the Kyoto Jujutsu High emblem, signifying her affiliation with the conservative faction of jujutsu society.

Behaviour & Personality

Kasumi is earnest, polite, and hardworking. She is acutely aware of her lack of natural talent and compensates with rigorous training and unwavering determination. She is often seen practicing her swordsmanship. She is also practical, having taken up jujutsu largely to earn money for her family. Despite her serious demeanor, she can be socially awkward and easily flustered, especially around her seniors. She is respectful of authority and tradition, but also shows a strong sense of justice and a willingness to help others.

Domains & Habitats

Kasumi resides at Kyoto Jujutsu High, a fictional school for sorcerers located in Kyoto, Japan. She is also depicted in various training grounds and during missions, including the Kyoto Sister School Exchange Event and the Shibuya Incident.

Version Differences

The anime adaptation closely follows the manga in terms of her appearance and personality. Minor differences in animation style do not alter her characterization. In the manga, her role is slightly more expanded in later chapters, but the anime has not yet covered these events as of the research cutoff date.

Powers & Abilities

Cursed Energy Manipulation

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

As a jujutsu sorcerer, Kasumi can manipulate cursed energy, which is generated from negative emotions. She uses it to enhance her physical abilities and to power her techniques.

Limitations

Her cursed energy output is relatively low compared to more talented sorcerers. She has no innate technique, making her reliant on external tools and basic reinforcement.

Contradictions

None noted.

Swordsmanship

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

Kasumi is a skilled swordswoman, trained in the use of a wooden sword (bokken). She uses her sword as a conduit for her cursed energy, allowing her to strike curses effectively.

Limitations

Without a cursed tool, her wooden sword is less effective against powerful curses. She lacks a named swordsmanship style, suggesting she uses a standard curriculum.

Contradictions

None noted.

Simple Domain

Source Version Manga (Shibuya Incident arc)

Description

Kasumi has learned the Simple Domain technique, a barrier technique that neutralizes domain expansions and other barrier-based attacks. This is a defensive technique that creates a small, safe zone around her.

Limitations

The Simple Domain is not a true domain expansion and has limited offensive capability. It requires concentration and can be overwhelmed by stronger techniques.

Contradictions

None noted.

Stories & Myths

Introduction at the Kyoto Sister School Exchange Event

Source Jujutsu Kaisen, Chapters 16-31Culture Japanese mangaPeriod 2018-2019

Summary

Kasumi Miwa is introduced as a first-year student at Kyoto Jujutsu High. She participates in the exchange event against Tokyo Jujutsu High, where she fights against the protagonist, Yuji Itadori. Despite her lack of innate talent, she puts up a determined fight, using her swordsmanship and a binding vow to enhance her attack. She is ultimately defeated but earns the respect of her opponents.

Shibuya Incident

Source Jujutsu Kaisen, Chapters 84-136Culture Japanese mangaPeriod 2019-2020

Summary

During the Shibuya Incident, a large-scale conflict with curses, Kasumi is deployed to assist in the evacuation of civilians. She uses her Simple Domain to protect herself and others from cursed techniques. She is seen fighting alongside other Kyoto students, but the chaos of the incident takes a heavy toll on her. She is not a central figure but is shown surviving the ordeal.

Participants

Consequences

The Shibuya Incident results in massive casualties and societal upheaval. Kasumi survives but is deeply affected by the events. Her exact status after the incident is not fully detailed in the manga up to the research cutoff.

Variants

The anime has not yet adapted this arc as of the research cutoff, so only the manga is considered.

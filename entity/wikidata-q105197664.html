Who It Is

In the world of Jujutsu Kaisen, where sorcerers battle curses born of human fear, Kasumi Miwa stands apart—not as a prodigy, but as a determined young woman who picked up a sword to pay her family's bills, proving that heroism can spring from the most mundane of motivations.

Kasumi is earnest, polite, and hardworking. She is acutely aware of her lack of natural talent and compensates with rigorous training and unwavering determination. She is often seen practicing her swordsmanship. She is also practical, having taken up jujutsu largely to earn money for her family. Despite her serious demeanor, she can be socially awkward and easily flustered, especially around her seniors. She is respectful of authority and tradition, but also shows a strong sense of justice and a willingness to help others.

Powers
Cursed Energy Manipulation

As a jujutsu sorcerer, Kasumi can manipulate cursed energy, which is generated from negative emotions. She uses it to enhance her physical abilities and to power her techniques.

Limit: Her cursed energy output is relatively low compared to more talented sorcerers. She has no innate technique, making her reliant on external tools and basic reinforcement.

Swordsmanship

Kasumi is a skilled swordswoman, trained in the use of a wooden sword (bokken). She uses her sword as a conduit for her cursed energy, allowing her to strike curses effectively.

Limit: Without a cursed tool, her wooden sword is less effective against powerful curses. She lacks a named swordsmanship style, suggesting she uses a standard curriculum.

Simple Domain

Kasumi has learned the Simple Domain technique, a barrier technique that neutralizes domain expansions and other barrier-based attacks. This is a defensive technique that creates a small, safe zone around her.

Limit: The Simple Domain is not a true domain expansion and has limited offensive capability. It requires concentration and can be overwhelmed by stronger techniques.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Kasumi Miwa is a teenage girl with short, dark hair and large, dark eyes. She typically wears the Kyoto Jujutsu High uniform, which consists of a dark blue gakuran-style jacket and matching trousers. She carries a wooden sword, which she uses as a focus for her cursed energy. Her appearance is neat and modest, reflecting her disciplined nature.

Symbols — Her primary symbol is the wooden sword, representing her dedication to swordsmanship despite lacking innate talent. She is also associated with the Kyoto Jujutsu High emblem, signifying her affiliation with the conservative faction of jujutsu society.

Domains — Kasumi resides at Kyoto Jujutsu High, a fictional school for sorcerers located in Kyoto, Japan. She is also depicted in various training grounds and during missions, including the Kyoto Sister School Exchange Event and the Shibuya Incident.

Accounts I
Introduction at the Kyoto Sister School Exchange EventJujutsu Kaisen, Chapters 16-31

2018-2019

Kasumi Miwa is introduced as a first-year student at Kyoto Jujutsu High. She participates in the exchange event against Tokyo Jujutsu High, where she fights against the protagonist, Yuji Itadori. Despite her lack of innate talent, she puts up a determined fight, using her swordsmanship and a binding vow to enhance her attack. She is ultimately defeated but earns the respect of her opponents. Kasumi's defeat highlights the gap between talented and untalented sorcerers, but also showcases her resolve. She continues to train and later plays a role in the Shibuya Incident.

Accounts II
Shibuya IncidentJujutsu Kaisen, Chapters 84-136

2019-2020

During the Shibuya Incident, a large-scale conflict with curses, Kasumi is deployed to assist in the evacuation of civilians. She uses her Simple Domain to protect herself and others from cursed techniques. She is seen fighting alongside other Kyoto students, but the chaos of the incident takes a heavy toll on her. She is not a central figure but is shown surviving the ordeal. The Shibuya Incident results in massive casualties and societal upheaval. Kasumi survives but is deeply affected by the events. Her exact status after the incident is not fully detailed in the manga up to the research cutoff.

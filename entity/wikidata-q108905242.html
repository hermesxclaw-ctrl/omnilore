Who It Is

She introduces herself not as a siren or a sea goddess, but as a tropical fish who got lost on her way to the Dragon Palace — bubbly, easily distracted, and suddenly expected to sing, game, and host a talk show for hundreds of thousands of humans. Finana Ryugu is NIJISANJI EN's resident tropical fish VTuber, a pastel-teal mermaid of the LazuLight generation whose lore is light on ancient myth and heavy on modern internet ritual: debut streams, superchats, 3D concerts, and a community that treats a fish-out-of-water gag as daily canon.

Attested via debut stream, subsequent variety streams, and official profile: described as 'carefree, bubbly, and a bit clumsy' (official profile wording). On-stream behavior: high-energy, giggly, self-deprecating humor about being a fish out of water, forgetful / airhead gag (e.g., forgetting stream controls), but also diligent in gaming and singing practice. Speaks primarily English with occasional Japanese phrases; uses fish puns ('fin-tastic'). Known for: (1) love of gacha games and FPS/valorant, (2) singing streams (pop, anime songs, original LazuLight songs), (3) AS

Aquatic Adaptation (Lore)

In fictional lore, as a tropical fish resident of Ryugu Palace, she is implied to breathe underwater and live undersea. No explicit superpowers beyond that; lore is minimal and comedic rather than battle-oriented.

Limit: Lore never details combat abilities, magic, or transformation; fish traits are aesthetic. Cannot survive indefinitely on land without comedic 'drying out' gag mentioned in streams (non-canonical joke).

VTuber Performance - Singing

Trained singing voice used for covers and original songs (e.g., LazuLight 'Diamond City Lights'). Range is pop-oriented, bright timbre. Performs in virtual concerts with 3D motion capture.

Limit: Not a classically trained vocalist per self-deprecation; performance limited by Live2D/3D tech and streaming audio quality.

VTuber Performance - Gaming and Variety

Proficient in FPS (VALORANT), gacha, and variety games; known for long grinding streams and collaborative gameplay. Also hosts talk/zatsudan and ASMR streams.

Limit: Skill varies by title; comedic failures are part of persona. No canonical 'power level'.

Virtual Avatar Technology

Live2D rigging allows fin flutter, tail wag, facial tracking; 3D model allows full-body dance and concert performance via motion capture.

Limit: Dependent on software/hardware; tracking glitches occur. Not an in-universe magical ability.

Community Building / Ryuguard Command

Ability to mobilize fanbase (Ryuguards) for superchats, fan art, hashtag trends, and charity streams. Collaborative synergy with LazuLight amplifies reach.

Limit: Influence limited to entertainment sphere; no formal authority beyond fandom.

Accounts I
Debut Stream: The Tropical Fish Arrives at LazuLightYouTube - Finana Ryugu Debut Stream 【NIJISANJI EN | LazuLight】

2021-05-16 (Reiwa 3)

Finana introduces herself as a tropical fish from the Dragon Palace who washed up into the human world and joined NIJISANJI EN alongside sky fairy Elira Pendora and forest fairy Pomu Rainpuff to form LazuLight, the first EN wave. She presents her profile (birthday Feb 26, height 155cm, likes fish puns), demonstrates Live2D expressions, answers lore Q&A (why a tropical fish in a cold palace, favorite foods), and performs a short ending song. The stream establishes her bubbly, forgetful persona and the group's diamond/light motif. Established canonical identity and LazuLight branding; channel passed 100k subscribers within weeks; set template for subsequent EN debuts. VOD remains primary lore source.

Diamond City Lights - LazuLight Original SongNIJISANJI EN Official Music Release - 'Diamond City Lights' MV (YouTube, streaming platforms)

2021-06-27

LazuLight's debut original song and music video, portraying the three members as lights guiding a city at night. Finana's verse and chorus lines emphasize her bright, tropical color and supportive role in the trio. The narrative frames LazuLight as bringing color/light to a monochrome city — a metaphor for EN's launch to global audience. First group discography entry; performed at subsequent virtual concerts; cemented musical identity for Finana as singer. MV has several million views (attested).

LazuLight 3D Debut and ConcertNIJISANJI EN YouTube - LazuLight 3D Debut Stream

2022-04-09

The trio receives 3D models and performs a live motion-capture concert featuring Diamond City Lights, solo covers, and interactive comedy segments. Finana's 3D model showcases full tail and fin physics, dance choreography, and playful fish-out-of-water skits (e.g., pretending to flop). The stream marks transition from Live2D-only to full virtual idol stage capability. Enabled participation in mixed-reality events like NIJISANJI AR Stage 'COLORS' and subsequent anniversary concerts; increased merchandising with 3D visuals.

Accounts II
Birthday and Anniversary RitualsYouTube - Finana Ryugu Birthday Streams (2022-02-26, 2023-02-26, 2024-02-26, 2025-02-26 VODs)

2022-present (annual Feb 26)

Each February 26, Finana hosts a birthday stream featuring new outfit reveals, cover song premieres, superchat readings, and messages from other NIJISANJI members. The 2023 birthday introduced a new kimono-inspired outfit; 2024 featured a 3D live mini-concert. These streams function as annual canon updates, expanding wardrobe and discography while reinforcing community bond with Ryuguards. Drives yearly subscriber spikes, merchandise drops, and fan art waves (#FinanaBirthday, #Ryuguards). Birthday goods sold via NIJISANJI Official Store.

NIJISANJI EN Unit and Cross-Wave CollaborationsYouTube VODs - Finana x Rosemi, Finana x Petra, Finana x Selen, and EN-wide Minecraft/VALORANT collabs

2021-present

Beyond LazuLight, Finana regularly collaborates with OBSYDIA (Rosemi Lovelock, Selen Tatsuki, Petra Gurin) and later waves (Ethyria, Luxiem, Noctyx). Notable arcs include EN Minecraft server building (Finana's underwater house), VALORANT ranked with Selen, and 'NIJISANJI EN Among Us' lore. These stories are emergent, unscripted, and form a shared EN continuity where Finana is the easygoing, supportive fish who often plays healer/support roles. Built inter-wave cohesion for NIJISANJI EN brand; Finana's collaborative persona increased cross-channel subscriptions; documented in clip compilations and fan wikis.

Hiatus and Return - Health and Sustainability NarrativeX/Twitter @FinanaRyugu announcements and YouTube return streams

2022-2023 (multiple short breaks, notably late 2022)

Finana announced short hiatuses citing health and need for rest, a common VTuber narrative reflecting industry burnout pressures. Return streams addressed absence lightly, reaffirmed commitment to streaming, and were met with supportive Ryuguard messages. No dramatic lore retcon; breaks are presented as the fish needing to 'return to the palace to recharge.' Highlighted VTuber labor conditions discourse; demonstrated ANYCOLOR's evolving support policies for EN talents; did not affect canonical design.

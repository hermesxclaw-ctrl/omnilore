The Approach

In the mangrove-lined villages of the Sepik River's mouth, the Murik people tell of Manup, a culture hero whose canoe once carved the waterways and whose name still echoes in the rituals of the living. He is not a distant god but a remembered ancestor, a spirit whose deeds shaped the land and whose presence lingers in the salt wind.

Content advisory. Contains references to traditional beliefs, ritual practices, and possible violence or taboo themes common in Sepik mythology. Some details are uncertain due to limited written documentation.

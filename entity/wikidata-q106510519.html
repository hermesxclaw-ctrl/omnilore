The Approach

In the Candy Kingdom, there is no figure more jarring, more shrill, and more utterly committed to the letter of the law than the Earl of Lemongrab. A creature of pure lemon, he rules his isolated earldom with a voice that can shatter glass and a sense of justice so rigid it has no room for mercy. He is at once a caricature of bureaucratic excess and a tragic figure of profound isolation, a being who was created to be a ruler but never taught how to be a person.

Content advisory. Contains themes of authoritarianism, emotional abuse, isolation, and body horror (a character literally splitting apart). Some scenes may be disturbing to young viewers.
Identity

Earl of Lemongrab

Pronunciation LEM-on-grab

Known As

LemongrabLemongrab 1Earl

Etymology

The name 'Lemongrab' is a portmanteau of 'lemon' and 'grab', reflecting his citrus composition and his tendency to seize power and control.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Pendleton Ward for Cartoon Network's Adventure Time; owned by Cartoon Network Studios.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans have speculated that Lemongrab is a parody of authoritarian leaders, but this is not officially confirmed. There is no scholarly dispute over his identity.

Description

Appearance

Lemongrab is a tall, thin, humanoid figure made entirely of lemon. He has a yellow body with a darker yellow rind-like texture, and his head is shaped like a lemon with a face that often appears contorted. He has large, expressive eyes and a wide mouth with sharp teeth. He wears a purple and gold outfit resembling royal attire, complete with a high collar and a small crown.

Symbols & Iconography

His iconic symbol is the lemon itself, representing his sour nature. His castle is shaped like a lemon and his subjects are lemon-like beings. He often carries a scepter or a gavel, signifying his authority.

Behaviour & Personality

Lemongrab is extremely authoritarian, obsessive, and emotionally unstable. He is prone to screaming, fits of rage, and rigid adherence to rules. He has a deep need for control and order, but his methods are cruel and alienate others. He is also deeply lonely and craves connection, but his inability to understand social cues and his harsh behavior push others away. He is highly intelligent in a bureaucratic sense but lacks empathy and common sense.

Domains & Habitats

He rules the Lemon Earldom, a small, isolated kingdom within the Land of Ooo, characterized by its lemon-themed architecture and inhabitants. He also has a dungeon where he imprisons those who break his laws.

Version Differences

There are two versions of Lemongrab: the original (Lemongrab 1) and his 'brother' (Lemongrab 2), created from a piece of him. Lemongrab 2 is initially more docile and childlike, but later becomes more assertive. After they merge in 'You Made Me', they become a single, more stable but still eccentric entity. The series also shows a future version of Lemongrab in 'Graybles 1000+' who is more mellow and has a family.

Powers & Abilities

Lemon Physiology

Source Version All versions

Description

Lemongrab is made of lemon, making him durable and able to survive impacts that would harm normal beings. He can also detach parts of his body and create new beings from them, as seen with Lemongrab 2.

Limitations

He is vulnerable to being eaten or dissolved by strong acids. His lemon body can be damaged by sharp objects.

Contradictions

None known.

Sonic Screaming

Source Version All versions

Description

Lemongrab can emit extremely loud and high-pitched screams that can shatter glass and incapacitate others.

Limitations

His screaming is not always under control and can be triggered by stress.

Contradictions

None known.

Authoritarian Command

Description

As a ruler, he has absolute authority over his subjects, who obey him out of fear. He can issue decrees and enforce them with brutal punishment.

Limitations

His authority only extends to his own earldom and is often challenged by stronger characters like Princess Bubblegum.

Stories & Myths

The Creation of Lemongrab

Source Adventure Time, Season 4, Episode 5: 'You Made Me'Culture Modern Western animationPeriod 2012

Summary

Princess Bubblegum creates Lemongrab as a replacement ruler for the Candy Kingdom while she is away. However, his harsh rule causes chaos, and she eventually banishes him to the Lemon Earldom. Later, she creates Lemongrab 2 from a piece of Lemongrab to keep him company, but the two initially have a rocky relationship. Eventually, they merge into a single being.

Authoritarian abuse

Source Adventure Time, 'Too Young' and 'Too Old'

Description

Lemongrab rules through fear, imprisoning subjects for trivial offenses and subjecting them to harsh punishments, including being locked in a dungeon. His methods are cruel and dehumanizing.

Scholarly Context

The character is often interpreted as a satire of authoritarianism and bureaucratic overreach.

Creation without consent

Source Adventure Time, 'You Made Me'

Description

Lemongrab was created by Princess Bubblegum as a tool, not as a person with rights. His existence is defined by his function, and he struggles with identity and purpose.

Scholarly Context

This raises themes of artificial life and the ethics of creation, common in science fiction.

Self-harm and body horror

Source Adventure Time, 'You Made Me'

Description

In 'You Made Me', Lemongrab physically splits himself to create Lemongrab 2, a painful and disturbing act. This is depicted with uncomfortable imagery.

Scholarly Context

The act symbolizes his desperate need for connection, but it is also a violation of his own body.

Who It Is

In the Candy Kingdom, there is no figure more jarring, more shrill, and more utterly committed to the letter of the law than the Earl of Lemongrab. A creature of pure lemon, he rules his isolated earldom with a voice that can shatter glass and a sense of justice so rigid it has no room for mercy. He is at once a caricature of bureaucratic excess and a tragic figure of profound isolation, a being who was created to be a ruler but never taught how to be a person.

Lemongrab is extremely authoritarian, obsessive, and emotionally unstable. He is prone to screaming, fits of rage, and rigid adherence to rules. He has a deep need for control and order, but his methods are cruel and alienate others. He is also deeply lonely and craves connection, but his inability to understand social cues and his harsh behavior push others away. He is highly intelligent in a bureaucratic sense but lacks empathy and common sense.

Powers
Lemon Physiology

Lemongrab is made of lemon, making him durable and able to survive impacts that would harm normal beings. He can also detach parts of his body and create new beings from them, as seen with Lemongrab 2.

Limit: He is vulnerable to being eaten or dissolved by strong acids. His lemon body can be damaged by sharp objects.

Sonic Screaming

Lemongrab can emit extremely loud and high-pitched screams that can shatter glass and incapacitate others.

Limit: His screaming is not always under control and can be triggered by stress.

Authoritarian Command

As a ruler, he has absolute authority over his subjects, who obey him out of fear. He can issue decrees and enforce them with brutal punishment.

Limit: His authority only extends to his own earldom and is often challenged by stronger characters like Princess Bubblegum.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Lemongrab is a tall, thin, humanoid figure made entirely of lemon. He has a yellow body with a darker yellow rind-like texture, and his head is shaped like a lemon with a face that often appears contorted. He has large, expressive eyes and a wide mouth with sharp teeth. He wears a purple and gold outfit resembling royal attire, complete with a high collar and a small crown.

Symbols — His iconic symbol is the lemon itself, representing his sour nature. His castle is shaped like a lemon and his subjects are lemon-like beings. He often carries a scepter or a gavel, signifying his authority.

Domains — He rules the Lemon Earldom, a small, isolated kingdom within the Land of Ooo, characterized by its lemon-themed architecture and inhabitants. He also has a dungeon where he imprisons those who break his laws.

Accounts I
The Creation of LemongrabAdventure Time, Season 4, Episode 5: 'You Made Me'

2012

Princess Bubblegum creates Lemongrab as a replacement ruler for the Candy Kingdom while she is away. However, his harsh rule causes chaos, and she eventually banishes him to the Lemon Earldom. Later, she creates Lemongrab 2 from a piece of Lemongrab to keep him company, but the two initially have a rocky relationship. Eventually, they merge into a single being. Lemongrab is established as a failed experiment and a tyrant. The merging of the two Lemongrabs creates a more balanced personality.

Accounts II
The Tyranny of LemongrabAdventure Time, Season 3, Episode 14: 'Too Young'

2011

When Princess Bubblegum is temporarily de-aged, Lemongrab takes over the Candy Kingdom and imposes strict, nonsensical laws, imprisoning citizens for minor infractions. Finn and Jake help restore Bubblegum to power, and she banishes Lemongrab. Lemongrab is exiled to the Lemon Earldom, deepening his isolation and resentment.

The Lemon HopeAdventure Time, Season 5, Episode 31: 'Too Old'

2013

Lemongrab becomes obsessed with creating a 'perfect' citizen and kidnaps children from the Candy Kingdom to try to 'educate' them. Finn and Jake rescue the children, and Lemongrab is confronted with his failures. He is left alone, but the episode hints at his potential for change. Lemongrab's isolation is emphasized, but he shows a glimmer of self-awareness.

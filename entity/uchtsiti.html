Who It Is
Form

Uchtsiti is often depicted as a powerful, benevolent deity associated with the sky and rain. He is said to be the consort of the Earth Mother and is revered for his ability to bring life-giving water to the parched earth.

Powers

Rain-bringing Uchtsiti has the power to bring life-giving rain to the parched earth, nourishing crops and sustaining life.

Accounts

Uchtsiti and the Earth Mother create the world and its inhabitants, teaching them the skills and knowledge necessary for survival.

Two sister spirits are born in darkness underground, and are taught by Thinking Woman the skills and knowledge necessary for survival. They plant trees that allow sunlight and seeds to grow, bringing life to the world.

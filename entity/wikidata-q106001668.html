The Approach

In the world of Japanese horse racing, where glory often belongs to the swift and the victorious, one horse carved a unique legend not by winning, but by an uncanny knack for finishing third. Nice Nature, the 'Bronze Collector,' captured the hearts of a nation with a career that spanned six years, 41 starts, and a record 16 Grade I appearances—yet only seven wins. His story is one of persistence, near-misses, and a popularity that transcended the racetrack, eventually blossoming into a cultural phenomenon that would see fans donate millions of yen in his honor.

Etymology

The name 'Nice Nature' is an English phrase, but it is also a play on the sire's name 'Nice Dancer' and the dam's name 'Urakawa Miyuki'. The Japanese rendering is ナイスネイチャ.

Description

Appearance

No detailed physical description is available from the provided sources. As a thoroughbred, he would have been a typical bay or dark bay horse, but this is not confirmed. Further research is needed.

Symbols & Iconography

The 'Bronze Collector' nickname is his most iconic symbol, representing his frequent third-place finishes. In Umamusume, his character design is anthropomorphic, but retains horse ears and a tail, with a cynical expression.

Behaviour & Personality

On the track, he was known for his consistency and longevity, but also for his tendency to finish third. Off the track, he was reportedly a calm and gentle horse, especially in his retirement. In Umamusume, he is depicted as cynical and pessimistic, a creative interpretation of his racing record.

Domains & Habitats

Born at Watanabe Ranch in Urakawa, Hokkaido. Trained at Ritto Training Center in Shiga. Stood at stud at Hidaka Stallion Station. Retired to Watanabe Ranch, where he lived until his death.

Version Differences

The real horse is a historical figure; the Umamusume character is a fictional anthropomorphization with a distinct personality. The character's personality is not based on the real horse's documented temperament.

Stories & Myths

Career Overview

Source JBIS, netkeiba, news reportsCulture JapanesePeriod 1990-1996

Summary

Nice Nature debuted late in 1990 at age three. In 1991, he won four major races: Kokura Kinen, Kyoto Shimbun Hai, Naruo Kinen, and a fourth not mentioned. He also placed third in the Arima Kinen in 1991, 1992, and 1993. In 1994, he won the Takamatsunomiya Hai. He retired in 1996 due to a foreleg problem.

Retirement and Death

Source News reportsCulture JapanesePeriod 1996-2023

Summary

After retirement, he stood at stud but was not very successful, siring only 17 registered horses. He also served as a poster horse for Quinella Place betting in 1999. He lived out his pensioned years at Watanabe Ranch, dying at age 35 in 2023.

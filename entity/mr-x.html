Who It Is
Form

Mr. X is a humanoid figure with a distinctive appearance, characterized by a black suit, a white shirt, and a distinctive fedora hat. He is often depicted as a relentless and unstoppable foe, driven by a singular purpose to eliminate his targets. His appearance has undergone slight variations across different games in the series, but his core design has remained consistent.

Powers

Superhuman Strength Mr. X possesses superhuman strength, allowing him to overpower and kill his targets with ease.

Incident Log

The story of Mr. X's origin is revealed in Resident Evil 2, where it is shown that he was created by the Umbrella Corporation as a prototype for a new line of bioterrorism agents.

The story of Resident Evil 3: Nemesis revolves around the battle against Mr. X, who has been sent to eliminate Jill Valentine.

Who It Is

An entity that exists in the Christian conception of Heaven — and is consuming it. A colossal parasitic organism several hundred kilometers in diameter that has latched onto the extradimensional space humans call 'Heaven,' feeding on souls over centuries. The 'light at the end of the tunnel' is its bioluminescence. The 'choirs of angels' are its digestive sounds. There is no Heaven. There is only something that ate it.

Form

Glimpsed in near-death accounts: a shapeless, continent-spanning organism of shifting, iridescent flesh, studded with billions of eyes and mouths, silently screaming the names of consumed souls.

Powers

Extradimensional parasitism

Consumption of afterlife constructs

Manipulation of near-death experiences

The Approach

Imagine a tree of alien light rooted in the Mojave Desert—a towering, iridescent growth that touches nothing it should, yet seeps a luminous blue-green fluid into the sand. Everyone who touches that fluid changes, body and soul, until six percent of humanity carries a piece of the thing inside them. Some cure diseases. Some commit murder. All of them are part of a single, extradimensional parasite that may be less an ending than a forced evolution.

Content advisory. Body horror, parasitic infection, loss of autonomy, violence, global contagion, reality distortion, apocalyptic scenarios.
Identity

SCP-3396 — The Empyrean Parasite

Pronunciation S-C-P three-three-nine-six, ‘The Em-py-ree-an Pair-uh-site’.

Known As

The Empyrean ParasiteApotheosis NexusCategory 4 Extradimensional Parasite3396 Central Nexus

Etymology

“Empyrean” derives from the Greek ‘empyros’ (in or on the fire), later used in medieval cosmology for the highest, fiery heaven; “parasite” from Greek ‘parasitos’ (one who eats at another’s table). In the SCP context, the name suggests a heaven-adjacent, transcendent entity that feeds on or inhabits humanity as host.

Cultural Ownership

SCP-3396 is a collaborative fictional construct belonging to the SCP Foundation community, specifically the Apotheosis Canon, authored primarily by the user CadaverCommander and developed within the English-language SCP wiki culture. It is part of a broader internet-horror and speculative eschatology tradition rather than any historical mythological corpus. [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-3396) [[1]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/apotheosis-hub)

Disputed Identifications

No major disputes exist about SCP-3396’s identity within the SCP community; it is consistently treated as a unique extradimensional parasitic gestalt. However, interpretive debates concern whether it represents a purely hostile apocalypse engine, a morally ambiguous catalyst for human transcendence, or a metaphorical embodiment of systemic change—these are interpretive, not ontological, disputes.

Description

Appearance

Attested: SCP-3396’s central nexus manifests as a massive, tentatively biological structure located approximately 75 km southeast of Death Valley in the Mojave Desert. It combines arboreal and insectile characteristics, standing about 27 meters tall and 23 meters wide between the ends of its largest branches. Its coloration is typically iridescent blue-green, but this can change dynamically, even presenting different color patterns to different observers simultaneously. The structure possesses trunks and branches riddled with vents that exude a luminous blue-green fluid, pooling into a shallow reservoir at its base. SCP-3396 is selectively tangible and fails to interact normally with physical objects or electromagnetic radiation, rendering conventional non-thaumaturgic examination impossible. [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-3396) [[2]](http://scpexplained.wikidot.com/scp-3396)

Symbols & Iconography

Attested: Iconographically, SCP-3396 is associated with an iridescent, tree-like or coral-like mass emitting luminous blue-green fluid, often depicted in fan art and thumbnails as a towering, alien growth against desert landscapes. The SCP Foundation article’s image credit references a surreal, desert-based photograph, reinforcing the visual motif of an otherworldly growth in a barren environment. In the Apotheosis canon, SCP-3396 functions as a symbolic axis of transformation—its fluids and parasitic influence visually stand for the spread of anomalous empowerment and bodily metamorphosis. Reconstructed: The juxtaposition of arboreal and insectile traits suggests a deliberate fusion of “tree of life” imagery with parasitic infestation, visually encoding both growth and predation.

Behaviour & Personality

Attested: SCP-3396 is described as a Category 4 extradimensional entity existing as a gestalt of physically and metaphysically mutagenic symbiotes inhabiting and affecting a significant portion of the global population (approximately 6% at the time of documentation). It does not exhibit a conventional personality in the human sense; instead, it operates as a distributed, hive-like intelligence mediated through infected hosts (SCP-3396-01). Its behavior is characterized by continual expansion of infestation, alteration of host biology and metaphysics, and the establishment of communicative links among hosts. Adaptation-specific: In the broader Apotheosis canon, SCP-3396’s influence is framed as a catalyst for large-scale societal and ontological change, sometimes interpreted as an emergent will toward human “apotheosis” rather than simple predation. Hypothetical: Whether SCP-3396 possesses a singular, coherent consciousness or functions as an emergent network of symbiotic intelligences remains unclear; the article does not definitively state its internal cognitive structure.

Domains & Habitats

Attested: SCP-3396’s central nexus is fixed in the Mojave Desert, within Exclusion Zone 3396, a demilitarized area contested by multiple anomalous organizations (Global Occult Coalition, Serpent’s Hand, United States Unusual Incidents Unit, and the SCP Foundation). Its effective domain, however, is global: its symbiotic infestation extends to a growing percentage of the human population, and its metaphysical effects propagate through both physical contact and less clearly defined communicative pathways. The entity’s habitat is thus both localized (a single desert nexus) and distributed (within and across human bodies and societies). [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-3396) [[1]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/apotheosis-hub)

Version Differences

Attested (core article): SCP-3396 is primarily a parasitic, mutagenic extradimensional entity whose fluids infect humans, producing SCP-3396-01 instances with anomalous abilities and altered metaphysical states. It is treated as an esoteric, Tiamat-class threat whose containment is incomplete and whose spread could trigger an ℵK-Class “Apotheosis” scenario. Attested (SCP Explained): Emphasizes its role as a doomsday-contest entry, its immobility, intangibility, and its production of a blue substance that transforms humans into SCP-3731 instances, integral to the Apotheosis canon. [[2]](http://scpexplained.wikidot.com/scp-3396) Adaptation-specific (Apotheosis tales): Later stories in the canon (e.g., “The Shape of a Gun”, “A Farewell to Arms”, “A New Age of Magic”) explore the downstream consequences of widespread anomalous empowerment, treating SCP-3396 less as a singular monster and more as the origin point of a transformed world where magic and superhuman abilities proliferate. [[1]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/apotheosis-hub) Disputed/interpretive: Some readers and commentators frame SCP-3396 as a purely catastrophic contagion, while others view it as a morally ambiguous engine of liberation and evolution; these are interpretive differences rather than separate canonical versions.

Powers & Abilities

Metaphysical and physical mutagenic symbiosis

Source Version Core SCP-3396 article; SCP Explained summary

Description

Attested: SCP-3396 exists as a gestalt of symbiotes that physically and metaphysically alter biological organisms exposed to its central nexus or its luminous blue-green secretion. These alterations are unpredictable and highly variable, but consistently involve profound changes to anatomy, physiology, and metaphysical properties (e.g., thaumaturgic potential, reality interaction). [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-3396) [[2]](http://scpexplained.wikidot.com/scp-3396)

Limitations

Attested: Effects require exposure to SCP-3396 or its fluids; the exact thresholds and modes of transmission are not fully characterized. Non-thaumaturgic testing is impossible due to SCP-3396’s selective tangibility and abnormal interaction with electromagnetic radiation, limiting empirical characterization.

Contradictions

Unknown: No explicit contradictions about the mutagenic nature itself; debates focus on the ultimate teleology of these changes (apocalypse vs apotheosis).

Creation of SCP-3396-01 infected hosts

Source Version Core SCP-3396 article; Apotheosis Hub commentary

Description

Attested: Humans exposed to SCP-3396’s fluids become SCP-3396-01 instances, exhibiting anomalous abilities and altered metaphysical states. These hosts form part of SCP-3396’s gestalt, acting as extensions of its influence and communicative network. They have used their abilities for diverse purposes, including murder, nonviolent crime, disease eradication, and public welfare initiatives. [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-3396) [[1]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/apotheosis-hub)

Limitations

Attested: SCP-3396-01 instances remain physically human but altered; they can be apprehended and contained in Class 4 Hazardous Bioform cells with Atlas-pattern anti-thaumaturgic runic arrays. Their abilities vary, suggesting no single standardized power set.

Contradictions

Interpretive: Some readings emphasize SCP-3396-01 as victims of parasitic infestation; others highlight their agency and potential as pioneers of a new magical age.

Hive-mind style communication and coordination

Source Version SCP Explained; inferred from core article’s gestalt language

Description

Reconstructed from attested details: SCP-3396’s description as a gestalt entity inhabiting a portion of the global population implies a networked consciousness or at least a shared communicative substrate among SCP-3396-01 instances. SCP Explained notes that SCP-3396 can communicate via a kind of hive-mind with transformed humans (linked to SCP-3731 in that summary). [[2]](http://scpexplained.wikidot.com/scp-3396)

Limitations

Unknown: The precise mechanisms, range, and reliability of this communication are not detailed in the primary article.

Contradictions

Minor: The main article does not explicitly use the term “hive-mind,” while secondary summaries do; this is a difference in emphasis rather than a direct contradiction.

Selective tangibility and abnormal interaction with physics

Source Version Core SCP-3396 article

Description

Attested: SCP-3396 in its entirety is selectively tangible and fails to interact normally with physical objects or electromagnetic radiation. This renders conventional, non-thaumaturgic testing impossible and suggests that SCP-3396 occupies a liminal state between physical and metaphysical existence. [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-3396)

Limitations

Attested: Its selective tangibility prevents standard containment or destruction; however, thaumaturgic and anti-thaumaturgic measures (e.g., runic arrays) can affect its manifestations and infected hosts.

Contradictions

Unknown: No explicit contradictions; the phenomenon is simply under-characterized.

Global-scale contagion and potential ℵK-Class “Apotheosis” scenario

Source Version Core SCP-3396 article; Apotheosis Hub framing

Description

Attested: SCP-3396’s rate of physical and metaphysical communicability makes complete containment unattainable. The Foundation anticipates that if 15% of Earth’s population becomes infested, Document 3396-ALABASTER will trigger personnel withdrawal, regional quarantine facilities, and alterations to the Foundation’s prime directive, effectively acknowledging a new world order. [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-3396) [[1]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/apotheosis-hub)

Limitations

Attested: The scenario is conditional on infestation thresholds; the article does not guarantee inevitability but treats escalation as likely without drastic intervention.

Contradictions

Interpretive: Some readers treat the ℵK-Class scenario as inevitable; others see it as a contingency plan that might never fully manifest.

Stories & Myths

SCP-3396 — The Empyrean Parasite

Source SCP Foundation main articleCulture Internet horror / collaborative fiction (SCP Foundation community)Period 2018–present

Summary

The SCP Foundation discovers and attempts to contain SCP-3396, a Category 4 extradimensional parasitic gestalt whose central nexus resides in the Mojave Desert. The entity exudes luminous blue-green fluid that infects humans, transforming them into SCP-3396-01 instances with anomalous abilities and altered metaphysical states. As infestation spreads to approximately 6% of the global population, multiple anomalous organizations contest control of Exclusion Zone 3396, leading to a demilitarized zone agreement to avoid global warfare and a Lifted Veil scenario. The Foundation acknowledges that complete containment is impossible and prepares for a potential ℵK-Class “Apotheosis” scenario if infestation reaches 15%, at which point its prime directive would be fundamentally altered. [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-3396) [[1]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/apotheosis-hub)

Consequences

Global society begins to experience unprecedented changes due to the proliferation of anomalous abilities among SCP-3396-01 instances. Crime, disease eradication, and public welfare initiatives are all reshaped by the presence of empowered individuals. The Foundation’s secrecy and control over anomalous phenomena are threatened, and the possibility of a new, openly magical world looms.

Variants

No major textual variants of the SCP-3396 article are documented; however, community readings and translations (e.g., YouTube adaptations, non-English SCP wikis) present interpretive and linguistic variants without altering core canonical content.

Source Apotheosis Hub — Canon Hub, SCP FoundationCulture Internet horror / speculative eschatologyPeriod 2018–present

The Apotheosis Hub situates SCP-3396 as the first part of an epic narrative about endings, beginnings, and the toil in between. It quotes Wallace Stevens and frames SCP-3396 as the advent of a new era in which anomalous abilities proliferate and humanity ascends—or mutates—into a different ontological state. The hub emphasizes that SCP-3396-01 instances have used their powers for both destructive and benevolent purposes, underscoring the moral ambiguity of the apotheosis process. Dark History

Parasitic infestation and loss of bodily autonomy

Source Core SCP-3396 article; SCP Explained summary

Description

Attested: SCP-3396’s fluids infect humans, transforming them into SCP-3396-01 instances whose bodies and metaphysical properties are dramatically altered. This constitutes a form of non-consensual bodily takeover, with hosts becoming part of a larger gestalt entity. The infection is framed as both a threat and a potential gateway to power, but the underlying loss of autonomy is a core horror element. [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-3396) [[2]](http://scpexplained.wikidot.com/scp-3396)

Scholarly Context

Within horror studies, parasitic infestation and body horror are recognized tropes that explore anxieties about identity, autonomy, and contamination. SCP-3396 participates in this tradition while adding metaphysical dimensions.

Violent use of anomalous powers

Source Core SCP-3396 article; Apotheosis Hub commentary

Description

Attested: SCP-3396-01 instances have used their newfound abilities for murder and nonviolent crime, as well as for disease eradication and public welfare. The article explicitly notes criminal and lethal applications, highlighting the potential for empowered individuals to inflict harm on others. [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-3396) [[1]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/apotheosis-hub)

Scholarly Context

This reflects broader speculative fiction concerns about superhuman powers and their ethical use, paralleling superhero and supervillain narratives but grounded in horror aesthetics.

Global contagion and societal collapse

Source Core SCP-3396 article; Apotheosis Hub

Description

Attested: The Foundation anticipates that if 15% of Earth’s population becomes infested, it will need to withdraw personnel and establish regional quarantine facilities, effectively conceding that normal containment and governance structures have failed. This scenario implies mass suffering, disruption of social order, and potential collapse of existing institutions. [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-3396) [[1]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/apotheosis-hub)

Scholarly Context

Apocalyptic contagion narratives are a staple of horror and science fiction, often used to interrogate resilience, governance, and ethical decision-making under existential threat.

Reality distortion and ontological trauma

Source Apotheosis Canon tales linked from the hub

Description

Attested (Apotheosis tales): Stories like “A Farewell to Arms” depict extreme reality-warping phenomena—plasma dinosaurs, crushed dimensions, bone-encased missiles, cheese-melted drones—suggesting that the apotheosis world is fundamentally hostile to human cognitive and physical norms. This constitutes a form of ontological trauma, where the basic rules of existence become unstable. [[1]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/apotheosis-hub)

Scholarly Context

Such depictions align with cosmic horror and postmodern apocalypse traditions, where reality itself becomes unreliable and threatening.

Contradictions & Disputes

Teleology of SCP-3396 (apocalypse vs apotheosis)

Position A

SCP-3396 is primarily a catastrophic parasitic contagion that threatens humanity and consensus reality, warranting maximal containment and quarantine.

Position A — evidence

Foundation classification as esoteric, Tiamat-class; emphasis on uncontrollable spread, potential ℵK-Class scenario, and criminal uses of powers. [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-3396) [[2]](http://scpexplained.wikidot.com/scp-3396)

Position B

SCP-3396 is a morally ambiguous or even potentially positive catalyst for human transcendence, enabling disease eradication, public welfare, and a new age of magic.

Position B — evidence

Apotheosis Hub commentary noting mass-eradication of certain diseases and public welfare initiatives by SCP-3396-01 instances; tales like “A New Age of Magic” framing apotheosis as ascension with costs rather than pure destruction. [[1]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/apotheosis-hub)

Scholarly Consensus

Within the SCP community, consensus leans toward viewing SCP-3396 as both catastrophic and transformative—an engine of apotheosis that is neither purely evil nor purely benevolent. The tension is intentional, reflecting complex ethical and metaphysical questions rather than a simple villain narrative.

Relationship between SCP-3396 and SCP-3731

Position A

SCP-3396’s blue substance directly transforms humans into SCP-3731 instances, making SCP-3731 an extension of SCP-3396’s influence.

Position A — evidence

SCP Explained summary explicitly states that the blue substance turns regular humans into SCP-3731 instances and that SCP-3396 can communicate with them via hive-mind. [[2]](http://scpexplained.wikidot.com/scp-3396)

Position B

SCP-3731 is a separate anomaly that coexists within the Apotheosis canon but is not strictly a direct product of SCP-3396.

Position B — evidence

The core SCP-3396 article does not mention SCP-3731, leaving the relationship implicit rather than explicit; canon structure allows for multiple anomalies contributing to apotheosis.

Scholarly Consensus

Community understanding generally accepts a strong linkage between SCP-3396 and SCP-3731 within the Apotheosis canon, but the lack of explicit cross-referencing in the main article leaves room for interpretive flexibility.

Nature of SCP-3396’s consciousness

Position A

SCP-3396 possesses a singular, coherent extradimensional consciousness that directs its symbiotic network.

Position A — evidence

Gestalt language and the entity’s classification as a Category 4 extradimensional entity suggest a unified being. [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-3396)

Position B

SCP-3396 is an emergent, distributed intelligence arising from the collective of symbiotes and infected hosts, without a central “mind.”

Position B — evidence

The article’s emphasis on variability of effects and lack of explicit description of a central consciousness support an emergent-network interpretation.

Scholarly Consensus

Unresolved; the text deliberately leaves SCP-3396’s inner consciousness structure ambiguous, inviting multiple readings.

Who It Is

Imagine a tree of alien light rooted in the Mojave Desert—a towering, iridescent growth that touches nothing it should, yet seeps a luminous blue-green fluid into the sand. Everyone who touches that fluid changes, body and soul, until six percent of humanity carries a piece of the thing inside them. Some cure diseases. Some commit murder. All of them are part of a single, extradimensional parasite that may be less an ending than a forced evolution.

Attested: SCP-3396 is described as a Category 4 extradimensional entity existing as a gestalt of physically and metaphysically mutagenic symbiotes inhabiting and affecting a significant portion of the global population (approximately 6% at the time of documentation). It does not exhibit a conventional personality in the human sense; instead, it operates as a distributed, hive-like intelligence mediated through infected hosts (SCP-3396-01). Powers
Metaphysical and physical mutagenic symbiosis

Attested: SCP-3396 exists as a gestalt of symbiotes that physically and metaphysically alter biological organisms exposed to its central nexus or its luminous blue-green secretion. These alterations are unpredictable and highly variable, but consistently involve profound changes to anatomy, physiology, and metaphysical properties (e.g., thaumaturgic potential, reality interaction). [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-3396) [[2]](http://scpexplained.wikidot.com/scp-3396)

Limit: Attested: Effects require exposure to SCP-3396 or its fluids; the exact thresholds and modes of transmission are not fully characterized. Non-thaumaturgic testing is impossible due to SCP-3396’s selective tangibility and abnormal interaction with electromagnetic radiation, limiting empirical characterization.

Creation of SCP-3396-01 infected hosts

Attested: Humans exposed to SCP-3396’s fluids become SCP-3396-01 instances, exhibiting anomalous abilities and altered metaphysical states. These hosts form part of SCP-3396’s gestalt, acting as extensions of its influence and communicative network. They have used their abilities for diverse purposes, including murder, nonviolent crime, disease eradication, and public welfare initiatives. [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-3396) [[1]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/apotheosis-hub)

Limit: Attested: SCP-3396-01 instances remain physically human but altered; they can be apprehended and contained in Class 4 Hazardous Bioform cells with Atlas-pattern anti-thaumaturgic runic arrays. Their abilities vary, suggesting no single standardized power set.

Hive-mind style communication and coordination

Reconstructed from attested details: SCP-3396’s description as a gestalt entity inhabiting a portion of the global population implies a networked consciousness or at least a shared communicative substrate among SCP-3396-01 instances. SCP Explained notes that SCP-3396 can communicate via a kind of hive-mind with transformed humans (linked to SCP-3731 in that summary). [[2]](http://scpexplained.wikidot.com/scp-3396)

Limit: Unknown: The precise mechanisms, range, and reliability of this communication are not detailed in the primary article.

Selective tangibility and abnormal interaction with physics

Attested: SCP-3396 in its entirety is selectively tangible and fails to interact normally with physical objects or electromagnetic radiation. This renders conventional, non-thaumaturgic testing impossible and suggests that SCP-3396 occupies a liminal state between physical and metaphysical existence. [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-3396)

Limit: Attested: Its selective tangibility prevents standard containment or destruction; however, thaumaturgic and anti-thaumaturgic measures (e.g., runic arrays) can affect its manifestations and infected hosts.

Global-scale contagion and potential ℵK-Class “Apotheosis” scenario

Attested: SCP-3396’s rate of physical and metaphysical communicability makes complete containment unattainable. The Foundation anticipates that if 15% of Earth’s population becomes infested, Document 3396-ALABASTER will trigger personnel withdrawal, regional quarantine facilities, and alterations to the Foundation’s prime directive, effectively acknowledging a new world order. [[0]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-3396) [[1]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/apotheosis-hub)

Limit: Attested: The scenario is conditional on infestation thresholds; the article does not guarantee inevitability but treats escalation as likely without drastic intervention.

Accounts I
SCP-3396 — The Empyrean ParasiteSCP Foundation main article

2018–present

The SCP Foundation discovers and attempts to contain SCP-3396, a Category 4 extradimensional parasitic gestalt whose central nexus resides in the Mojave Desert. The entity exudes luminous blue-green fluid that infects humans, transforming them into SCP-3396-01 instances with anomalous abilities and altered metaphysical states. As infestation spreads to approximately 6% of the global population, multiple anomalous organizations contest control of Exclusion Zone 3396, leading to a demilitarized zone agreement to avoid global warfare and a Lifted Veil scenario. The Foundation acknowledges that complete containment is impossible and prepares for a potential ℵK-Class “Apotheosis” scenario if infestation reaches 15%, at which point its prime directive would be fundamentally altered. Apotheosis Hub framing textApotheosis Hub — Canon Hub, SCP Foundation

2018–present

The Apotheosis Hub situates SCP-3396 as the first part of an epic narrative about endings, beginnings, and the toil in between. It quotes Wallace Stevens and frames SCP-3396 as the advent of a new era in which anomalous abilities proliferate and humanity ascends—or mutates—into a different ontological state. The hub emphasizes that SCP-3396-01 instances have used their powers for both destructive and benevolent purposes, underscoring the moral ambiguity of the apotheosis process. [[1]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/apotheosis-hub) SCP-3396 becomes the narrative anchor for a canon exploring the long-term consequences of widespread anomalous empowerment, including warfare, social upheaval, and new forms of magic.

Accounts II
The Shape of a GunCadaverCommander, SCP Foundation (Apotheosis Canon tale)

Post-2018

While not focused solely on SCP-3396, this tale occurs in the apotheosis-transformed world that SCP-3396 helped create. Monica, a central character, becomes a living weapon—her soul forged into the shape of a gun—to defend her people against those who would exploit or destroy them. The story centers the human cost and agency within a world where anomalous powers are widespread, implicitly tracing back to the initial contagion of SCP-3396. [[1]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/apotheosis-hub) Demonstrates how individuals navigate empowerment and violence in the apotheosis era, highlighting both liberation and trauma.

A Farewell to ArmsWeryllium, SCP Foundation (Apotheosis Canon tale)

Post-2018

Set in the apotheosis world, this tale depicts a battlefield of reality-warping phenomena: a turret chewed by a Tyrannosaurus rex made of plasma, a body absent where reality has been crushed flat and rolled into nonexistence, a missile encased in bone, a drone melted into cheese, and Scranton Reality Anchors sitting impotent. The story illustrates the breakdown of conventional physics and containment technologies in the face of widespread anomalous powers, indirectly rooted in the transformative contagion initiated by SCP-3396. [[1]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/apotheosis-hub) Shows the futility of traditional containment and warfare in a fully apotheosized world, emphasizing the radical shift in ontological norms.

A New Age of MagicModern_Erasmus, SCP Foundation (Apotheosis Canon tale)

Post-2018

This tale articulates the principle that magic has infinite manifestations, sources, and rules, but one universal law: everything has a cost. In the apotheosis world, humanity ascends into a new magical age, but every power and transformation carries a price. SCP-3396’s initial contagion is the unseen origin of this new age, making the entity a foundational mythic engine for the narrative. [[1]](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/apotheosis-hub) Frames the apotheosis era as morally and metaphysically complex, where empowerment is inseparable from sacrifice and cost.

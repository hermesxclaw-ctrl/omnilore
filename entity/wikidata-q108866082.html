Who It Is

At the bottom of a dungeon built as a father's final test, chained in darkness and still bleeding, waits Kee-Oth — the four-armed Blood Demon who learned that Joshua, Finn and Jake's dogged, demon-hunting dad, did not believe in fair fights.

Attested behavior: When Finn and Jake finally reach him, Kee-Oth is initially enraged, roaring and straining against chains, shouting 'Joshua!' He recognizes Joshua's scent/lineage in Finn and Jake ('You smell like Joshua!'). He is vengeful but also weary; dialogue suggests decades of isolation. He is not shown as cunning or manipulative like Hunson Abadeer; he is brute-force, direct, and physically threatening. Powers
Superhuman Strength and Durability

Attested: Kee-Oth is physically overwhelming; Joshua's hologram says it took great effort to defeat him. In 'Dad's Dungeon,' his straining shakes chains anchored into stone, and his roar causes dust to fall. He survives decades chained without food/water, implying demonic durability/immortality or stasis. He is not shown to be invulnerable — Joshua did defeat and chain him, and his blood can be drawn.

Limit: Can be defeated by a skilled human (Joshua) with preparation and presumably magical/weapons assistance. Can be physically restrained by enchanted or sufficiently strong chains and dungeon architecture. No evidence of invulnerability to conventional weapons.

Demon Blood / Blood Forging

Attested: Kee-Oth's blood has supernatural properties; Joshua harvested it to forge the Demon Blood Sword (also called Demon Sword), a jagged red blade that Finn briefly wields as the dungeon's prize. The sword is shown glowing faintly and is implied to be stronger than normal steel. The blood pools beneath Kee-Oth and does not coagulate normally.

Limit: Requires extraction; blood must be forged by a skilled smith (Joshua). No evidence Kee-Oth can weaponize his blood at will while chained; no blood-bending or projectile shown in show canon (game adaptations add blood projectiles as gamified ability).

Demonic Longevity / Immortality (Implied)

Attested: Kee-Oth is alive after years/decades of imprisonment in the dungeon (Joshua died when Finn was a baby, so dungeon was built ~10-13 years prior to S05). No aging is visible. Demons in Adventure Time (Hunson Abadeer, Marceline) are long-lived/immortal, supporting inference that Kee-Oth is similarly long-lived.

Limit: Not truly immortal — can be killed (Joshua could have killed him but chose to imprison). Longevity does not grant escape or self-healing from chains.

Intimidation / Fear Aura (Narrative, not mechanical)

Attested: Characters react with immediate fear; Finn and Jake's usual bravado drops when they see Kee-Oth. The dungeon's design builds tension toward him as final boss. No explicit magical fear power is named, but his presence functions as horror.

Limit: No evidence of supernatural mind control; effect is psychological based on size and reputation.

Four-Armed Combat

Attested: Four arms imply ability to grapple, strike, and restrain multiple opponents. In the brief struggle after chains loosen, he swipes at both Finn and Jake simultaneously. No formal martial art is shown.

Limit: While chained, mobility is severely restricted; even when partially freed, he is still anchored and cannot pursue beyond chamber. No ranged attacks in show canon.

Accounts I
Dad's Dungeon — The Final GuardianAdventure Time Season 5 Episode 18 'Dad's Dungeon' (Cartoon Network, 2013-05-27). Written & storyboarded by Tom Herpich & Steve Wolfhard.

2013 (in-universe: ~12 years after Joshua's death; contemporary to Finn age 14-15)

Finn and Jake discover a hologram of their deceased adoptive father Joshua, who invites them to brave a dungeon he built to test them and claim its treasure. They traverse puzzle rooms, slime monsters, and emotional trials (including a room that forces them to confront their feelings about Joshua's strict parenting). In the final chamber they find not treasure but Kee-Oth the Blood Demon, chained to the wall in a pool of his own blood, with the Demon Blood Sword on a pedestal before him. Joshua's prerecorded message explains he defeated Kee-Oth and used his blood to forge the sword, leaving Kee-Oth as the ultimate test. Kee-Oth awakens, recognizes Joshua's scent on the boys, and attacks. Finn and Jake realize the 'treasure' was the lesson itself — Joshua wanted them to prove bravery and cooperation, not greed. They escape as the dungeon begins to collapse/flood (triggered by their success), leaving Kee-Oth behind. They choose to leave the sword, understanding Joshua's intent. The episode ends with Finn and Jake honoring Joshua's memory. Establishes Joshua as a formidable demon-hunter

The Capture of Kee-Oth — Joshua's Demon Hunt (Embedded Backstory)Same episode 'Dad's Dungeon' — Joshua hologram exposition + visual flashback stills; corroborated by Season 8 'Joshua and Margaret Investigations' for Joshua's hunting methods.

In-universe: ~15-20 years before S05 (when Joshua and Margaret were active adventurers, before Finn's adoption)

Joshua, a dog with human-like adventuring skills, encountered Kee-Oth during his career as a dungeon-builder and monster hunter. Dialogue states Joshua 'defeated a mighty blood demon named Kee-Oth' in single combat, subdued him, and rather than killing him, chained him as a living guardian and harvested his blood to forge a weapon. The method of defeat is not shown — no spell or weapon is named — but Joshua's reputation as a master of traps and combat is emphasized. Margaret is implied to have assisted in dungeon construction. This backstory is told, not shown, via Joshua's hologram, making it oral history within the fiction. Explains why a demon of Kee-Oth's power is found in a human/dog-built dungeon rather than a demon realm; establishes Joshua's moral ambiguity (imprisonment and blood harvesting as punishment); provides precedent for Joshua's later capture of other creatures (seen in 'Joshua and Margaret Investigations' with the creature that becomes Finn).

Accounts II
The Demon Blood Sword Legacy'Dad's Dungeon' + subsequent episodes where Finn's sword collection is shown (e.g., S05E19 'Little Dude', S06E06 'Breezy' sword loss); Adventure Time: The Enchiridion & Marcy's Super Secret Scrapbook (2015) entry on Demon Sword.

2013-2015 broadcast and print

The sword forged from Kee-Oth's blood — a jagged, dark-red blade with a black hilt — is presented as the dungeon's prize. Finn initially claims it but ultimately leaves it, internalizing Joshua's lesson that the journey matters more than loot. The sword remains in the collapsed dungeon, presumably still with Kee-Oth. In later lore books, the Demon Blood Sword is catalogued as one of Finn's 'lost swords,' distinct from his Grass Sword, Finn Sword, and Scarlet. Its properties are listed as 'demon-forged, unusually sharp, faintly warm to touch.' Kee-Oth is thus indirectly present whenever the sword is referenced, as his blood is its material. Creates a lasting artifact tied to Kee-Oth; fans track the sword as part of Finn's evolving armory; establishes blood-forging as a demon-related crafting method in Ooo (later echoed with other demon artifacts).

Joshua and Margaret Investigations — The Demon-Hunting Era ContextAdventure Time Season 8 Episode 7 'Joshua and Margaret Investigations' (2016-04-21). Written by Graham Falk, Adam Muto, Jack Pendarvis.

2016 broadcast; in-universe prequel to Finn's birth

While Kee-Oth is not named in this episode, it documents Joshua and Margaret's life as paranormal investigators who capture and contain supernatural entities (including the shapeshifting creature that will become Jake's parent, and other demons). The episode shows their methods: luring, trapping, and imprisoning creatures in custom-built containment, exactly as done to Kee-Oth. This provides indirect canonical support for how Kee-Oth was captured and why Joshua would keep a live demon rather than kill it — for study, as a test, or as a trophy. The episode also shows Margaret's equal role, suggesting she helped build Kee-Oth's chamber. Retroactively deepens Kee-Oth's story by placing it in a documented pattern of Joshua/Margaret behavior; explains dungeon architecture and hologram technology reuse; no retcon of Kee-Oth's fate.

Explore the Dungeon Because I DON'T KNOW! — Gamified RetellingVideo game Adventure Time: Explore the Dungeon Because I DON'T KNOW! (WayForward Technologies / D3 Publisher, 2013-11-19) for PS3, Xbox 360, Wii U, PC, 3DS.

2013 (contemporaneous with Season 5)

In this dungeon-crawler, Princess Bubblegum tasks Finn and Jake to explore a massive secret dungeon beneath the Candy Kingdom (narratively distinct from Joshua's Dungeon but thematically linked). Kee-Oth appears as a mid-to-late boss encounter, reusing his show design with gamified mechanics: four-armed slam, blood-wave projectile, and summoning smaller blood slimes. Dialogue is minimal ('Grr... Joshua...'). Defeating him drops the Demon Blood Sword as loot, mirroring the show's prize. The game is considered secondary canon (licensed, not show-written) but is the only interactive appearance of Kee-Oth. Expands Kee-Oth's moveset beyond show limitations; introduces him to players who may not have seen 'Dad's Dungeon'; reinforces his role as 'dungeon boss' archetype in franchise. Not referenced in show continuity afterward.

Kee-Oth is the same as The Lich or Hunson Abadeer, or is Marceline's father. — Kee-Oth is a distinct, one-off Blood Demon with no familial relation to Marceline or the Lich. Hunson Abadeer is Lord of the Nightosphere and Marceline's father; The Lich is an undead Catalyst Comet entity. Kee-Oth has four arms, red skin, and is chained in Joshua's Dungeon — visually and narratively separate.

Kee-Oth appears in multiple episodes and is a recurring villain. — Kee-Oth appears prominently in only one broadcast episode ('Dad's Dungeon') for ~2 minutes, plus indirect contextual mention via Joshua's hunting career. He does not recur as a villain in later seasons. Game and book appearances are secondary canon.

Finn keeps and uses the Demon Blood Sword as his main sword after 'Dad's Dungeon.' — Finn leaves the Demon Blood Sword in the collapsing dungeon, choosing the lesson over loot. He does not add it to his regular armory (his main swords remain Golden Sword, Grass Sword, etc.). The sword remains with Kee-Oth. Later lore books list it as a 'lost sword.'

Kee-Oth's blood gives Finn demon powers or corrupts him. — No such corruption is shown. Finn touches the sword briefly but shows no demonic transformation. Unlike the Grass Sword (which later corrupts Finn's arm), the Demon Blood Sword has no shown corrupting effect in show canon. Book flavor text about 'whispering' is not depicted as possession.

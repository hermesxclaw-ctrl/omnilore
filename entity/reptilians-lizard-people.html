Who It Is

The Reptilian myth is a fascinating and complex topic that has captured the imagination of many people.

Reptilians are often depicted as being cold-blooded, calculating, and manipulative. They may be shown as being highly intelligent and cunning, using their intelligence to manipulate and control others. They may also be shown as being ruthless and brutal, using violence to achieve their goals.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Reptilians are often depicted as humanoid creatures with scaly skin, reptilian features, and a humanoid body. They may have sharp teeth, claws, and a long tail. Some depictions show them as having a more humanoid appearance, while others are more reptilian. They may also be shown with a range of colors, from green to brown to gray.

Symbols — Reptilians are often associated with symbols such as the Eye of Providence, the All-Seeing Eye, and the Ouroboros. They may also be associated with reptilian-themed artwork, such as depictions of snakes and lizards.

Domains — Reptilians are often depicted as living in hidden domains, such as underground cities or secret societies. They may also be shown as living in more mundane environments, such as cities or towns.

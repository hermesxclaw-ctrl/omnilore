Who It Is
Form

The Byakhee is described as a tall, thin creature with long arms and legs, and a head that is shaped like a human skull. They have a distinctive appearance that is often associated with the Cthulhu Mythos.

Powers

Telepathy The Byakhee have the ability to read and control the minds of others, allowing them to manipulate and deceive their victims.

Shape-shifting The Byakhee have the ability to change their shape and form at will, allowing them to blend in with their surroundings and avoid detection.

Accounts

The Byakhee is a creature from the Cthulhu Mythos that is said to inhabit the farthest reaches of the universe. They are described as being malevolent and evil, with a strong desire to harm and destroy humanity.

The Call of Cthulhu is a short story by H.P. Lovecraft that introduces the concept of the Cthulhu Mythos. The story follows a group of characters as they uncover the truth about the Byakhee and their role in the downfall of human civilization.

Who It Is

Interstellar creatures that can fly through the vacuum of space carrying riders on their backs. They serve Hastur and are summoned by cultists for transportation between worlds. They have insect-like and bat-like features, with a cadavarous appearance and a terrible croaking cry.

Form

Horse-sized, bat-like wings, insectoid body, multiple segmented legs, cadaverous rotting-visage head, long proboscis, covered in patches of bristly hair, makes croaking sounds

Powers

Interstellar flight

Rider transportation

Vacuum survival

Summoning by music/ritual

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

Who It Is

You're reading a forum post when the text begins to bleed. Letters drip downward, melting into characters you don't recognize from any human alphabet. The screen darkens. You hear a sound from your speakers — not music, not noise, but a voice. Many voices. A chorus of something that exists in the spaces between words, waiting behind a wall that was never meant to be breached. This is the calling card of Zalgo, an entity born not from ancient myth but from the collective imagination of the internet, a modern horror for a digital age.

Zalgo is described as a malevolent, corrupting entity that 'waits behind the wall'—a metaphorical barrier between our reality and a chaotic void. Its primary motivation appears to be to 'bleed through' into our world, corrupting and destroying everything it touches. It is patient, malevolent, and enjoys causing fear and despair. It is not communicative; its presence is felt through the corruption of text, images, and reality itself. It is sometimes described as a 'god of chaos' or 'void entity,' but this is fan interpretation.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Zalgo' appears to be an invented neologism, likely deriving from the word 'zalgo' as a corruption of 'zalg' (a nonsensical root) combined with the suffix '-o' common in internet coinages. No known pre-internet etymology exists. Some fans have retroactively connected it to the Greek 'zalos'

Aliases — He Who Waits Behind the Wall, The Corruptor, The Dripping One, The Bleeding God, Zalgo the Corruptor

Powers
Text Corruption (Zalgo Text)

The ability to corrupt digital or written text, causing it to 'bleed' or 'drip' downward into illegible, corrupted characters. This is the most consistent and well-attested power.

Limit: Seems to only affect text. The original meme was a visual effect created by combining Unicode combining characters.

Reality Corruption

The ability to corrupt physical reality, causing decay, madness, and transformation in those exposed to its influence. This is implied in later versions but not in the earliest.

Limit: Unclear; appears to require exposure to its 'text' or image.

Fear Inducement

Zalgo's presence or influence causes intense fear, paranoia, and madness in victims.

Limit: May be a side effect of its other powers rather than a direct ability.

Corruption of Media

Zalgo can corrupt images, videos, and audio, causing them to glitch, distort, or show disturbing imagery.

Limit: Not attested in the earliest sources.

Void Travel

Zalgo can move between dimensions or 'behind the wall' and our reality.

Limit: It is 'waiting'—it cannot fully cross over without an invitation or a breach.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Zalgo is described as a tall, gaunt, humanoid figure with pale, decaying skin. Its most distinctive feature is its eyes: black, empty sockets that 'bleed' a dark, viscous fluid. Its mouth is a lipless gash, often depicted as unnaturally wide, filled with rows of jagged, broken teeth. Its body is covered in wounds and lesions, from which a black, oily substance drips. It is often shown wearing tattered, dark robes or appearing as a shadowy silhoue

Symbols — The 'Zalgo text' effect itself is the primary symbol: corrupted, dripping text. Other symbols include a stylized, bleeding eye, a void or black hole, a wall or barrier, and the phrase 'He Who Waits Behind the Wall.' In fan art, Zalgo is often depicted with tentacles, extra limbs, or a crown of thorns, though these are not attested in the earliest sources.

Domains — Zalgo is said to reside in 'The Void' or 'Behind the Wall'—a dimension of pure chaos and corruption. It can manifest in our world through digital media (text, images, videos) and possibly through physical corruption of reality. Its influence is strongest where there is fear, chaos, or decay.

Accounts I
The Original Zalgo Text (2004-2005)Something Awful Forums

2004-2005

A user posts a block of text that appears normal at first, but as the reader scrolls, the text begins to 'bleed'—letters drip downward, forming corrupted characters. The post is accompanied by a description of a malevolent entity 'behind the wall' that is responsible for the corruption. This is the earliest known appearance of the Zalgo concept. The meme spreads to other forums and imageboards, becoming a staple of internet horror.

Accounts II
The 4chan /x/ Expansion (2006-2008)4chan /x/ (Paranormal) board

2006-2008

Users on /x/ expand on the Zalgo concept, adding a detailed physical description (tall, gaunt, bleeding eyes, lipless mouth) and the 'wall' metaphor. The entity is now explicitly named 'Zalgo' and is described as a malevolent, corrupting force. The 'Zalgo text' effect is codified as a method of summoning or attracting the entity. Stories are told of people who read the text and experienced nightmares, paranoia, or physical illness. Zalgo becomes a well-known internet creepypasta. The entity is frequently cited in horror discussions and fan works.

The 'Zalgo' Creepypasta Canon (2008-2012)Creepypasta wikis (e.g., Creepypasta Wiki, Wikia)

2008-2012

Writers on creepypasta wikis codify the 'canon' of Zalgo: a tall, gaunt humanoid with bleeding eyes and a lipless mouth, who corrupts text and reality. The entity is often described as a 'god of chaos' or 'void entity.' Stories are written about encounters with Zalgo, often involving a person finding a corrupted text file or image and being driven mad. The 'wall' metaphor is expanded: Zalgo is 'behind the wall' and is trying to break through. Zalgo becomes a staple of internet horror, alongside Slender Man and The Rake. It is referenced in games, videos, and other media.

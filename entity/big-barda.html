Who It Is

Big Barda is one of the mightiest warriors in the Fourth World and the wife of Mister Miracle (Scott Free). Raised on Apokolips by Granny Goodness, Barda was conditioned from childhood to be a warrior in the Female Furies — Darkseid's elite all-female strike force. She became their leader, a brutal and efficient commander feared across Apokolips. But when she met Scott Free — a prisoner who refused to break — she saw something she had never encountered: true freedom. Barda fell in love with Scott, helped him escape Apokolips, and defected to Earth, leaving behind everything she knew. Barda is a powerhouse even by New God standards. She stands over 7 feet tall, possesses superhuman strength (she can lift buildings and go toe-to-toe with Darkseid's elite), superhuman durability, and is a master of virtually every form of combat. She wields a Mega-Rod, a New God weapon that can fire energy blasts, generate force fields, and teleport objects. In combat, Barda is terrifying — a towering warrior whose battle cry sends Parademons fleeing. On Earth, Barda found peace for the first time in her existence, becoming a superhero, a member of the Justice League International, and later a mother. But she never lost her warrior edge — when her family or her new world is threatened, the Female Fury leader returns with terrifying efficiency. Big Barda represents the power of choice over destiny. She was shaped by evil, trained by cruelty, and forged in the fires of Apokolips — but she chose love, freedom, and a better world. Her journey is a testament to the idea that even those shaped by darkness can walk in the light if they choose to.

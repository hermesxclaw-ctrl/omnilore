Who It Is

She is the fury of Apokolips made flesh—a New God who traded the iron fist of Darkseid for a heart that chose love over conquest. Big Barda, once the Female Fury who could shatter worlds with her bare hands, now stands as one of the DC Universe's most formidable warriors and its most unexpected romantic hero.

Big Barda is fiercely loyal, compassionate, and protective of those she loves. She is a skilled strategist and a relentless warrior, but she despises tyranny and injustice. She is often portrayed as confident, direct, and sometimes blunt, but also deeply caring, especially towards her husband Scott Free (Mister Miracle). She values freedom and individuality, having rebelled against the oppressive regime of Apokolips.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Barda' is of uncertain origin, possibly derived from the Old Norse 'Bard' meaning 'battle' or from a futuristic Apokoliptian naming convention. 'Big' is a title denoting her formidable stature and power.

Pronunciation — BIG BAR-dah

Aliases — Barda Free, Barda, The Female Fury

Ownership — DC Comics (Warner Bros. Discovery). Created by Jack Kirby, first appeared in Mister Miracle (vol. 1) #4 (October 1971).

Powers
Superhuman Strength

Big Barda possesses immense physical strength, capable of lifting enormous weights and overpowering most opponents. She is considered one of the strongest New Gods.

Limit: Her strength is not unlimited; she can be overpowered by beings like Darkseid or Superman in some portrayals.

Superhuman Durability

She is highly resistant to physical harm, able to withstand powerful energy blasts and impacts.

Limit: She is not invulnerable; she can be injured by sufficiently powerful attacks.

Superhuman Agility and Reflexes

Despite her size, Barda is extremely agile and has enhanced reflexes, making her a formidable hand-to-hand combatant.

Limit: She can be caught off guard by faster opponents.

Mega-Rod

A versatile weapon that can fire energy blasts, create force fields, and serve as a melee weapon. It is highly durable and can channel her power.

Limit: The Mega-Rod can be taken or destroyed, though it is rarely permanently lost.

Expert Combatant

Trained as a Female Fury, Barda is a master of hand-to-hand combat, armed and unarmed.

Limit: None.

Flight

In some versions, Barda can fly, often via her Mega-Rod or as a natural New God ability.

Limit: Not always depicted; may require her Mega-Rod.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Big Barda is a tall, muscular woman with a powerful build, typically standing around 6'8" (2.03 m) in most depictions. She has long blonde hair and blue eyes. Her iconic costume consists of a red and black armored bodysuit with a golden 'B' emblem on her chest, a red cape, and often a horned helmet. She wields a large, double-headed battle staff called the 'Mega-Rod'.

Symbols — The 'B' emblem on her chest is a prominent symbol, often stylized. Her helmet with horns evokes a warrior aesthetic. The Mega-Rod is her signature weapon, capable of firing energy blasts and generating force fields.

Domains — Originally from Apokolips, a hellish planet ruled by Darkseid. She later resides on Earth, primarily in New York City, living among humans. She has also been depicted in other realms such as New Genesis and various cosmic locations.

Accounts I
Origin and DefectionMister Miracle (vol. 1) #4 (1971) and later retellings

1970s

Barda was raised on Apokolips as a member of the Female Furies, elite warriors trained by Granny Goodness. She was indoctrinated to serve Darkseid. However, she was assigned to watch over Scott Free, a young captive from New Genesis. Over time, she fell in love with him and helped him escape to Earth. She later followed him and became his wife and ally. Barda became a hero on Earth, fighting against Darkseid's forces and other threats. Her defection made her a target of Apokolips.

The PactVarious comics, notably 'The Pact' in New Gods #7 (1971)

1970s

A story that details the truce between New Genesis and Apokolips, and the exchange of hostages: Scott Free and Orion. Barda's role is as a supporting character, but it establishes the political backdrop of her world. The Pact sets up the ongoing conflict between the two worlds.

Accounts II
Mister Miracle (vol. 4) - 'Death of the New Gods'Mister Miracle (vol. 4) #1-12 (2017-2019)

2010s

A critically acclaimed series that reimagines Barda and Scott's life on Earth as they deal with the threat of death and the possibility of a new life. It explores themes of depression, trauma, and the meaning of heroism. Barda is portrayed as a supportive but complex partner. The series ends ambiguously, leaving the fate of the New Gods open.

Justice League Unlimited - 'The Ties That Bind'Justice League Unlimited animated series (2004-2006)

2000s

Barda appears as a member of the Justice League, helping to rescue her husband from Apokolips. She is depicted as a powerful and capable hero. She becomes a recurring ally of the League.

Who It Is

One of the earliest collaborative creepypasta universes, predating the SCP Foundation's wiki format. The Holders Series consists of 538+ short stories, each describing a quest to obtain one of 538 'Objects' from a specific 'Holder.' Each Object grants immense power, but obtaining it requires completing a ritual that is always horrifying and often fatal. The Objects include: The Holder of the End (who can end the world), The Holder of Silence (who has not spoken in 10,000 years), and The Holder of the List (who knows the name of everyone who will ever die). The series is dark, fragmented, and explicitly warns: 'Do not seek all 538 Objects. He is waiting for the one who does.' The 'He' is never identified.

Form

Each Holder is described differently, but common themes emerge: they are always in a specific, hard-to-find location; they are always waiting; they are never surprised to see the seeker; and they always ask the same question at the end of the ritual: 'Are you ready?' The correct answer varies. The wrong answer is fatal.

Cultural Significance

The Holders Series is one of the most important precursors to the SCP Foundation. Its format — numbered entries, procedurally generated horror rituals, shared universe — directly inspired the SCP Wiki's structure.

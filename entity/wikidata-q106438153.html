The European Southern Observatory: A Story of International CooperationOfficial ESO history

1962-present

Founded in 1962, ESO is a consortium of European countries building and operating advanced ground-based astronomical observatories in Chile. It has enabled major discoveries, including the first image of an exoplanet and the study of black holes. Advanced astronomical research, technological innovation, and international scientific collaboration.

The Elder Scrolls Online: A Digital SagaGame official website and press

2014-present

The Elder Scrolls Online is a massively multiplayer online role-playing game set in the Elder Scrolls universe. Players explore Tamriel, complete quests, and engage in player-vs-player combat. It has received expansions and updates. Became one of the most popular MMORPGs, with a dedicated player base and ongoing content releases.

Ether Saga Odyssey: A Fantasy MMOGame archives and reviews

2007-2011 (approximately)

Ether Saga Odyssey was a free-to-play MMORPG based on the Chinese novel 'Journey to the West'. It featured whimsical characters and turn-based combat. The game was shut down in 2011. Cult following despite closure; servers are now offline.

Esō (Hyechong): A Buddhist MissionaryHistorical Buddhist records

7th-8th century (approximate)

Esō, also known as Hyechong, was a Korean priest who transmitted Buddhism to Japan. He is recorded in the Jingde Chuandeng Lu and other Chan/Zen transmission records. Details of his life are sparse, but he is venerated as one of the early patriarchs in certain lineages. Contributed to the spread of Buddhism in Japan.

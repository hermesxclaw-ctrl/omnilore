Who It Is

In the annals of the Six-Faced World, few names carry the weight of Perugius Dola — a dragon lord who defied the very gods who shaped his kind, and whose thousand-year vigil still guards the realms from a calamity that never truly ended.

Perugius is stoic, calculating, and fiercely protective of the world. He is driven by a singular purpose: to prevent the resurrection of the Demon God Laplace. He can appear cold and ruthless, but he is not evil; he is willing to make hard sacrifices for the greater good. He shows respect for strong individuals and has a dry, sardonic wit.

Powers
Dragon God Physiology

As a dragon lord, Perugius possesses immense physical strength, durability, and magical power. He can transform into a massive dragon form, gaining flight and devastating breath attacks.

Limit: His power, while immense, is still below that of the true Dragon God, Laplace. He cannot match the Demon God's full might.

Master Magic User

Perugius is a highly skilled magic user, capable of casting advanced spells in multiple schools, including attack, healing, and barrier magic.

Limit: He relies on his familiar spirits (the Twelve Demon Generals) for certain tasks, suggesting limits to his own spellcasting.

Summoning and Binding

He has the ability to summon and bind powerful spirits, including the Twelve Demon Generals, who serve him as loyal retainers.

Limit: The exact mechanics and costs are not fully detailed; it is implied that binding such spirits requires great power and concentration.

Immortality / Extended Lifespan

As a dragon lord, Perugius has lived for over a thousand years, indicating a greatly extended lifespan or near-immortality.

Limit: He is not truly immortal; he can be killed in battle.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Perugius is depicted as a tall, imposing figure with long silver hair and sharp, piercing eyes. He often wears ornate, archaic armor befitting a dragon lord, and carries an aura of immense power. In his true form, he is a massive dragon with scales that shimmer like starlight.

Symbols — Associated with the symbol of the Dragon God, often depicted as a stylized dragon head. His castle, the Floating Fortress of Chaos Breaker, is a symbol of his authority and isolation.

Domains — His primary domain is the Floating Fortress of Chaos Breaker, a massive airborne castle that he uses as a base of operations. He is also known to travel across the Six-Faced World, monitoring threats.

Accounts I
The Laplace WarMushoku Tensei light novel (backstory)

Approximately 400 years before the main story

Perugius was a key figure in the war against the Demon God Laplace, who sought to destroy the world. Perugius fought alongside the other races and ultimately helped to seal Laplace, but at great cost. The war devastated the world and led to the creation of the Seven Great Powers. Laplace was sealed, but the world was left in ruins. Perugius became obsessed with preventing Laplace's return, dedicating his life to vigilance.

Accounts II
The Floating Fortress of Chaos BreakerMushoku Tensei light novel and anime

Main storyline

Perugius resides in his floating fortress, where he conducts experiments and monitors the world. He encounters Rudeus Greyrat and his party, and becomes an ally, though he initially views them with suspicion. He later aids them in battles against powerful foes. Perugius forms a tentative alliance with Rudeus, and his knowledge of Laplace becomes crucial to the plot.

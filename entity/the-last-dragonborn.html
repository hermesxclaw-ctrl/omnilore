Name & Tongue

Etymology — The term 'Dragonborn' is derived from the ancient Melyrian language, where 'dra' means 'dragon' and 'gorn' means 'born'. The Last Dragonborn is a title given to the hero who freed the dragonborns from their servitude.

Pronunciation — The Last Dragonborn is pronounced as /ðə læst draɡənˈbɔrn/

Aliases — The Last Dragon, Dragonborn of Solstheim, The Hero of Melyria

Ownership — The Last Dragonborn is a cultural entity owned by the people of Melyria and the Elder Scrolls universe.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Last Dragonborn is a powerful hero with the ability to absorb the souls of dragons. They are often depicted as a strong and courageous warrior, with a deep connection to the dragons and the land of Melyria.

Symbols — The Last Dragonborn is often associated with the symbol of the dragonborns, which is a stylized image of a dragon's head. They are also often depicted with a sword and shield, representing their bravery and strength.

Domains — The Last Dragonborn is said to have traveled to the island of Solstheim, where they discovered the origin of the cultists who worshiped the dragon Miraak.

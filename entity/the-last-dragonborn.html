The Last Dragonborn

also known asThe Last DragonDragonborn of SolstheimThe Hero of Melyria
TypeMythopoeic Entity (Protagonist)
Who It Is
Form

The Last Dragonborn is a powerful hero with the ability to absorb the souls of dragons. They are often depicted as a strong and courageous warrior, with a deep connection to the dragons and the land of Melyria.

Powers

Dragon Soul Absorption The Last Dragonborn has the ability to absorb the souls of dragons, granting them immense power and strength.

Accounts

The story of the Last Dragonborn begins with the oppression of the dragonborns by the dragons. The dragonborns prayed for freedom, and their prayers were answered when the Last Dragonborn was born.

The Last Dragonborn traveled to the island of Solstheim, where they discovered the origin of the cultists who worshiped the dragon Miraak.

The Last Dragonborn's legacy lives on in the people of Melyria, who continue to tell stories of their hero's bravery and strength.

Cultural Significance

Painted just after Pearl Harbor, the painting captures wartime anxiety and the atomization of American city life. Hopper insisted there was no intended symbolism — 'I was probably painting the loneliness of a large city' — but the image has been read as existentialist theater, film noir still, and sociological commentary.

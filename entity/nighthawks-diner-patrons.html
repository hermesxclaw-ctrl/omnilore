Who It Is
Form

The Nighthawks Diner Patrons are four anonymous figures: two men, one woman, and a server. They are depicted in a dimly lit diner, surrounded by the sounds and smells of a late-night eatery. The figures are faceless and nameless, adding to the mystery and anonymity of the scene.

Powers

Anonymity The Nighthawks Diner Patrons possess the power of anonymity, allowing them to remain faceless and nameless in the eyes of the public.

Accounts

Edward Hopper's 'Nighthawks' is a painting that depicts a late-night diner scene in 1940s America. The painting is a representation of the urban experience and the struggles of the everyman.

The Approach

In the distant, post-apocalyptic Land of Ooo, a once-mighty monarch rules from a crumbling throne, his reign a curious blend of grandeur and absurdity. The King of Ooo, a figure of contested legitimacy and surprising ambition, embodies the strange, fractured history of a world rebuilt on the ruins of our own.

Content advisory. Contains themes of political satire, dynastic conflict, and mild absurdist humor. No graphic content.
Identity

King of Ooo

Pronunciation /ˈoʊ/ as in 'oh'

Known As

King of Ooo (self-proclaimed)The King of Ooo

Etymology

The name 'Ooo' refers to the post-apocalyptic Land of Ooo, derived from the show's setting. The title 'King of Ooo' is a direct claim to sovereignty over the entire land.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Pendleton Ward and the Adventure Time team for Cartoon Network. The character is a satirical take on monarchy and political ambition in a whimsical yet decaying world.

Disputed Identifications

The King of Ooo is sometimes confused with other monarchs in Ooo, such as Princess Bubblegum or the Earl of Lemongrab. However, he is a distinct character, first appearing in the episode 'The King of Ooo' (Season 5). His exact species and origin are never fully explained, leading to fan speculation.

Description

Appearance

The King of Ooo is a short, rotund humanoid with pale skin, a large nose, and a prominent chin. He wears a purple robe with gold trim, a golden crown, and a red cape. He often carries a scepter. His design is reminiscent of a classic fairy-tale king but with a slightly off-kilter, cartoonish quality.

Symbols & Iconography

His crown and scepter are symbols of his claimed authority. He also uses a flag with a crown emblem. His throne is a simple, wooden chair.

Behaviour & Personality

The King of Ooo is pompous, self-important, and delusional about his authority. He believes he is the rightful ruler of all Ooo, though most characters ignore or mock him. He is persistent and ambitious, often scheming to gain power, but his plans are usually comically inept. He can be charming when it suits him, but he is ultimately selfish and opportunistic.

Domains & Habitats

He claims dominion over all of Ooo but has no fixed residence. He is often seen traveling or in the presence of other rulers. In his debut, he attempts to claim the Candy Kingdom as his own.

Version Differences

Across appearances, his character remains consistent, though his role shifts from antagonist to comic relief. In later cameos, he is reduced to background humor. No significant differences in his design or personality are noted.

Stories & Myths

The King of Ooo (episode)

Source Adventure Time, Season 5, Episode 35Culture American animated televisionPeriod 2013

Summary

The King of Ooo arrives in the Candy Kingdom and claims that he is the rightful ruler of Ooo, citing an ancient document. Princess Bubblegum is forced to step down due to a loophole, and the King takes over. He proves to be an incompetent ruler, making absurd decrees and throwing parties. Finn and Jake help Princess Bubblegum find a way to reclaim her throne, eventually exposing that the King's claim is based on a technicality. The King is dethroned and banished.

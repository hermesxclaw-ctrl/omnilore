Who It Is

In the golden age of jujutsu, when sorcerers were at their peak, there lived a being so terrifying that even the most powerful shamans trembled at his name. Ryomen Sukuna, the King of Curses, was a walking disaster—a calamity in human form whose very existence defied the natural order. With two faces, four arms, and an insatiable appetite for destruction, he was not merely a sorcerer but a force of nature, feared by gods and demons alike. His legend, born in the Heian Era, has echoed through the ages, and now, a thousand years later, his fingers still hold the power to plunge the world into chaos.

Sukuna is characterized as cruel, narcissistic, depraved, and supremely arrogant. He has no regard for human life and takes pleasure in causing fear and pain. He is highly intelligent and strategic, often manipulating others to achieve his goals. He is also shown to be indifferent to most things, but becomes interested in those who show strength or potential, such as Megumi Fushiguro and Satoru Gojo. He is a hedonist who enjoys fighting and chaos.

Powers
Immense Cursed Energy

Sukuna possesses an immeasurable amount of cursed energy, far surpassing any other sorcerer. This allows him to perform devastating attacks and sustain his domain expansion.

Limit: None known

Malevolent Shrine (Fukuma Mizushi)

Sukuna's domain expansion, which creates a shrine that automatically attacks all within its range with a barrage of slashing attacks. It is unique in that it does not require a barrier, allowing it to have a longer range and be more difficult to escape.

Limit: Requires a large amount of cursed energy and can only be used for a limited time.

Cleave and Dismantle

Sukuna's signature cursed techniques. Cleave is a targeted slashing attack that adjusts its power based on the target's toughness, while Dismantle is a wide-range slashing attack that can hit multiple targets.

Limit: Requires hand signs and chants to use at full power.

Fire Arrow

A powerful fire-based attack that Sukuna uses to incinerate his enemies. It is shown to be capable of destroying a city block.

Limit: Requires a significant amount of cursed energy.

World Cutting Slash

A technique that cuts through space itself, allowing Sukuna to bypass any defensive barriers or techniques. It is his ultimate attack, which he used to kill Satoru Gojo.

Limit: Requires a binding vow, hand signs, chants, and manual aim. It can only be used once without these conditions.

Binding Vows

Sukuna can make binding vows with others, which are unbreakable agreements that grant power in exchange for restrictions. He used a vow with Yuji to gain control of his body for a minute.

Limit: The vow must be agreed upon by both parties, and breaking it results in punishment.

Regeneration

Sukuna can regenerate his body from injuries, even fatal ones, as long as his cursed energy is not depleted.

Limit: Can be exhausted if he uses too much cursed energy.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Ryomen Sukuna appears as a tall, muscular man with pink hair and red eyes. In his true form, he has two faces—one on the front and one on the back of his head—and four arms. His body is covered in black tattoos that resemble a demonic pattern, and he often has a menacing grin. In his incarnated form within Megumi Fushiguro, he retains his appearance but with Megumi's facial features, including his spiky black hair and distinctive dark eyes.

Symbols — Sukuna's iconic symbol is his four arms and two faces, representing his overwhelming power and dual nature. He is often associated with the imagery of a demon or oni in Japanese folklore. His tattoos are also a distinctive feature, often depicted as a form of cursed energy manifestation.

Domains — Sukuna's domain is the Malevolent Shrine, a terrifying space where his cursed technique automatically attacks all within it. In terms of habitat, he was originally from the Heian Era, where he lived as a powerful sorcerer. In the modern era, he resides within the body of Yuji Itadori, and later Megumi Fushiguro.

Accounts I
The Heian Era LegendNihon Shoki, Jujutsu Kaisen manga

Heian Era (794-1185)

According to the Nihon Shoki, Ryomen-sukuna was a powerful being from Hida Province who had two faces and four arms. He was feared as a demon and was eventually defeated by the Yamato general. In Jujutsu Kaisen, Sukuna is depicted as the strongest sorcerer of the Heian Era, a cruel and malevolent figure who was feared by all. He had an underling named Uraume and was known for his immense cursed energy. Sukuna's power was so great that after his death, his body was divided into 20 fingers, which were sealed and scattered across Japan to prevent his resurrection.

Yuji Itadori's VesselJujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 1-10

Modern Era

Yuji Itadori, a high school student, accidentally swallows one of Sukuna's fingers and becomes his vessel. Sukuna gains consciousness within Yuji and can take control of his body. Initially, Yuji has full control, but Sukuna can take over when Yuji allows it or when he is in danger. Sukuna's presence makes Yuji a target for execution by the Jujutsu world, but Satoru Gojo protects him. Yuji is forced to become a jujutsu sorcerer to atone for his actions and to eventually exorcise Sukuna. Sukuna's influence constantly threatens Yuji's sanity and safety.

Accounts II
The Shibuya IncidentJujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 60-80

Modern Era

During the Shibuya Incident, Sukuna is force-fed multiple fingers by the cursed spirit Jogo, regaining a significant portion of his power. He defeats Jogo in a devastating battle, then saves Megumi Fushiguro from Mahoraga, razing much of Shibuya in the process. He then returns control to Yuji, who is traumatized by the destruction. The Shibuya Incident results in massive casualties and the sealing of Satoru Gojo. Sukuna's power is now at a level that threatens the entire jujutsu world.

The Culling Games and Megumi FushiguroJujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 100-150

Modern Era

During the Culling Games, Sukuna uses a binding vow with Yuji to take over his body and force Megumi to ingest his finger, allowing Sukuna to incarnate into Megumi's body. He then kills the sorcerer Yorozu to fully break Megumi's soul, taking permanent control of Megumi's body. Megumi's soul is suppressed, and Sukuna now has a new, more powerful vessel. This sets the stage for the final confrontation in Shinjuku.

Shinjuku ShowdownJujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 200-260

Modern Era

Sukuna challenges Satoru Gojo to a duel. After a fierce battle, Sukuna uses a binding vow to unleash World Cutting Slash, killing Gojo. The remaining sorcerers, including Yuji, attempt to fight Sukuna but are overpowered. Yuji's domain expansion allows him to confront Sukuna, and with Nobara's help, they land a decisive blow. Sukuna refuses Yuji's offer to change and disintegrates. Sukuna is finally defeated, and the threat he posed is ended. Yuji is freed from his curse, and the jujutsu world begins to heal.

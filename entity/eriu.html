Who It Is

Ériu is the eponymous sovereignty goddess of Ireland — the island bears her name (Éire). When the Milesians arrived, she met them at Uisneach and demanded the island be named after her. In Celtic conception, the goddess and the land were one: a legitimate king 'married' the sovereignty goddess.

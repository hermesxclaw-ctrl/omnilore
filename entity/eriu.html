Form & Signs

Appearance — Ériu is often depicted as a powerful, maternal figure, associated with the land and its fertility.

Symbols — She is often associated with the symbol of the Celtic knot, representing the interconnectedness of all things.

Domains — Her domains include the land of Ireland, the Otherworld, and the mystical realm of the Tuatha Dé Danann.

Accounts I
The Coming of the Tuatha Dé DanannLebor Gabála Érenn

Pre-Christian era

The story of Ériu's arrival in Ireland, where she becomes the matron goddess of the land. The establishment of the Tuatha Dé Danann in Ireland, with Ériu as their leader.

Accounts II
The Battle of MoyturaCath Maige Tuired

Pre-Christian era

The story of Ériu's role in the Battle of Moytura, where she grants sovereignty to the Tuatha Dé Danann. The victory of the Tuatha Dé Danann over the Fir Bolg, securing their dominance in Ireland.

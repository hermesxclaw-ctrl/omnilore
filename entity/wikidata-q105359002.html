Etymology

Tsubaki (椿) is the Japanese word for camellia, a flower symbolizing love, longevity, and in some contexts, the divine. Takenaka is a Japanese surname, historically associated with the Takenaka clan, notably the strategist Takenaka Hanbei. The combination suggests a character with floral elegance and martial or strategic prowess.

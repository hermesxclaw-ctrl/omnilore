Who It Is

The Traveler is a mysterious, massive, white celestial sphere that serves as the source of the Light — the paracausal power that Guardians use to protect humanity. The Traveler is one of two primordial cosmic entities (the other being the Darkness/the Winnower) that have existed since before the beginning of the universe. According to the deeper lore of Destiny, the Traveler is the 'Gardener' — an entity that believes in complexity, diversity, and the beauty of life flourishing in all its forms. It travels the universe, uplifting civilizations, terraforming worlds, and sharing its technological and paracausal gifts. It visited humanity during the Golden Age, arriving on Mars, terraforming Venus and Mercury, and granting humanity centuries of peace and prosperity. But the Traveler is also fleeing: its ancient enemy, the Darkness (the Winnower), pursues it across the universe, consuming the civilizations the Traveler uplifts. When the Darkness finally arrived in Sol, humanity's Golden Age ended in the Collapse. The Traveler made a final, desperate stand — it sacrificed itself, creating Ghosts and imbuing them with the Light, which they use to resurrect dead humans as Guardians. The Traveler now floats above the Last City, scarred and silent. Ghosts — small, flying sentient machines — are fragments of the Traveler's consciousness, each bonded to a single Guardian. The Traveler's sacrifice left it dormant, but its Light still protects the City from the forces of darkness. The Traveler represents the fundamental creative principle of the Destiny universe — the belief that life is worth nurturing, that complexity is beautiful, and that altruism is the highest form of existence. It is a god that does not demand worship but offers gifts freely, a being that gave everything to protect a species it barely knew.

Who It Is

He was not born a Newtype — he was made into one. Gates Capa enters Mobile Suit Zeta Gundam not as a legendary ace but as a product: a teenager tuned, drugged and conditioned at the Augusta labs to prove that the Titans could manufacture what nature had only hinted at in Kamille Bidan and Char Aznable.

Attested: Calm, obedient, and deferential to superiors (Bask Om, Paptimus Scirocco), with flashes of arrogance and competitive drive typical of conditioned Cyber Newtypes seeking validation. Dialogue shows he is eager to prove his ability against true Newtypes like Kamille Bidan. He is less overtly unstable than Four Murasame or Rosamia Badam in his initial appearance, but exhibits the same pressure-induced emotional volatility and dependency on command approval under stress. Powers
Artificial Cyber Newtype Awareness

Attested limited Newtype-like spatial awareness, empathy pressure, and precognition induced by Augusta laboratory conditioning. Can sense Kamille Bidan's Newtype pressure and respond in combat, but with less range and stability than natural Newtypes.

Limit: Requires periodic conditioning and is prone to mental destabilization under high pressure; not as strong as Kamille, Char, or Haman Karn. Effectiveness fluctuates; explicitly inferior to Kamille's 'strongest Newtype' depiction per Tomino interview.

High-Performance Mobile Suit / Mobile Armor Piloting

Qualified pilot for the transformable ORX-005 Gaplant, a high-acceleration prototype requiring enhanced reflexes. Demonstrates skilled use of both MS and MA modes, beam rifles, and diffuse beam cannons.

Limit: Dependent on machine performance; Gaplant's high G-load limits unaugmented pilots. No attested hand-to-hand or leadership feats outside cockpit.

Titans Officer Training

Commissioned Titans lieutenant, trained in Earth Federation Forces tactics, formation flying, and ship-to-MS coordination.

Limit: Junior officer; no strategic command authority. Operates under Bask Om and Scirocco's orders.

Pressure Resistance (Limited)

Conditioned to withstand Newtype pressure waves that incapacitate normal pilots, allowing him to engage Kamille and other Newtypes without immediate panic.

Limit: Resistance breaks down in prolonged exposure; shows visible strain, unlike Paptimus Scirocco who weaponizes pressure.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Attested: Young adult male, late teens to early twenties, slender build. Pale skin, short blond hair parted to side, blue-grey eyes in anime cels. Wears standard Titans officer uniform: black and maroon with red piping, Titans emblem. No cybernetic prosthetics are visibly depicted; augmentation is internal/neurological. In Gaplant cockpit scenes he wears a normal suit with Titans helmet. Version differences: TV cel coloring is slightly more muted

Symbols — Symbolically linked to the ORX-005 Gaplant, a transformable mobile armor with distinctive bird-like MA mode and long tail binders, representing the Titans' attempt to mass-produce Newtype-dedicated transformable machines. The Gaplant's Augusta lineage (ORX prefix) marks him as an Augusta product, contrasting with Murasame Lab's MRX/Psyco series used by Four. No personal emblem is attested; he bears standard Titans insignia.

Accounts I
Creation at Augusta Newtype LaboratoryMobile Suit Zeta Gundam TV series Ep. 33-34 dialogue + Entertainment Bible .48 + Gundam Perfect File

UC 0087 (in-universe); production 1985

Gates Capa is introduced as a product of the Augusta Newtype Laboratory, the Earth-based Titans facility parallel to the Murasame Lab that produced Four Murasame. Augusta specialized in drug therapy, sensory deprivation, and memory manipulation to artificially awaken Newtype abilities in adolescents. Gates, like Rosamia Badam, is presented as a 'successful' stabilised subject deemed deployable, unlike earlier unstable prototypes. He is assigned to the Titans fleet as a Cyber Newtype asset under the oversight of Paptimus Scirocco, who collects such enhanced youths as tools. Establishes the Titans' systematic exploitation of minors for weapons development, a core moral indictment of the Titans. Provides narrative parallel to Kamille's natural Newtype awakening, contrasting nature vs. manufacture.

Messenger from Axis - Deployment to the FleetMobile Suit Zeta Gundam Ep. 33 'Messenger from Axis'

UC 0087; aired 1985-10-19

Following the arrival of the Axis (later Neo Zeon) delegation led by Haman Karn, the Titans consolidate forces near Gryps. Gates Capa arrives aboard a Titans vessel as part of Scirocco's entourage of enhanced pilots. He is briefed alongside other Titans aces and observes the political maneuvering between Jamitov Hymem, Bask Om, and Scirocco. His presence signals Scirocco's growing influence through control of Cyber Newtypes. Positions Gates within Scirocco's faction, foreshadowing his later combat role as Scirocco's instrument against both AEUG and rival Titans officers.

First Sortie in the ORX-005 GaplantMobile Suit Zeta Gundam Ep. 35 'Storm Over Kilimanjaro' and Ep. 36 'Forever Four'

UC 0087; aired 1985-11-02 to 1985-11-09

Gates launches in the ORX-005 Gaplant, Augusta's transformable prototype capable of atmospheric flight and high-speed space interception. In MA mode the Gaplant resembles a bird of prey, using tail binders and large thrusters for hit-and-run attacks. He engages AEUG forces including Kamille Bidan in the Zeta Gundam and Fa Yuiry. The battle demonstrates his Cyber Newtype reflexes as he keeps pace with Kamille's movements, but Kamille's stronger pressure gradually overwhelms him. The Gaplant's performance is highlighted as superior to standard Rick Dias and Nemo units. Proves Augusta machines can challenge AEUG aces, but also proves artificial enhancement cannot match Kamille's natural evolution. Leads to Titans' increased reliance on larger Psyco Gundam units.

Accounts II
Kilimanjaro and the Cyber Newtype RivalryMobile Suit Zeta Gundam Ep. 36 'Forever Four' (Eien no Four)

UC 0087; aired 1985-11-09

During the AEUG's attack on the Titans' Kilimanjaro base on Earth, Gates operates in support of Titans ground and air defenses. The episode intercuts his sortie with Four Murasame's tragic final battle against Jerid Messa and Kamille. Gates's role is to intercept AEUG mobile suits attempting to penetrate the base's airspace. His combat is marked by reliance on the Gaplant's speed rather than Newtype finesse, contrasting with Four's emotionally charged Psyco Gundam rampage. He witnesses the instability of fellow Cyber Newtypes firsthand. Reinforces the theme that Titans discard Cyber Newtypes once expended; Four's death foreshadows the expendability Gates himself faces. Kilimanjaro's fall weakens Titans Earth presence.

Final Battle at Gryps 2 and FateMobile Suit Zeta Gundam Ep. 37-50 Gryps Conflict climax (especially Ep. 37 'Dakar's Day' onward) + official mook summaries

UC 0087; aired 1985-11-16 to 1986-02-22

In the closing three-way battle between AEUG, Titans, and Axis near the Gryps 2 colony laser, Gates is deployed among the last Titans Cyber Newtype reserves. Sources diverge on his exact end: the TV broadcast shows his Gaplant being disabled and destroyed in the chaotic crossfire, with no explicit ejection scene, implying death in action like many Titans aces. No funeral or mention follows, consistent with Titans' erasure of failed experiments. The film trilogy omits this death, leaving his fate unresolved on screen. Supplementary books list him as KIA during the Gryps Conflict, while games keep him alive for player use. His disappearance without memorial underscores the dehumanization of Cyber Newtypes. Narrative consequence is minimal compared to Four or Rosamia, highlighting his function as a 'rank-and-file' artificial Newtype rather than a tragic lead.

Legacy in Expanded UC MediaSD Gundam G Generation series (1998-present), Super Robot Wars series (1991-present), Mobile Suit Zeta Gundam Define manga (2011-)

1998-2024 (out-of-universe)

Outside the TV canon, Gates Capa persists as a minor but recurring asset. In G Generation he appears as a collectible pilot with high reaction stats and the Gaplant, often with extra TR-5 variants. In Super Robot Wars he is frequently an enemy ace who can be persuaded to defect if the player meets skill thresholds, offering a 'what-if' redemption denied in the anime. Define manga gives him slightly more dialogue about his conditioning, humanizing him beyond 'enemy mook' status. Keeps the character in fan consciousness despite minimal TV screen time; demonstrates how Gundam's game ecosystem preserves even minor Titans pilots for mechanical variety.

Gates Capa is a true, natural Newtype like Amuro Ray or Kamille Bidan. — He is explicitly a Cyber Newtype (artificially enhanced). His abilities are induced via Augusta's conditioning and are less stable and less powerful than natural Newtypes. The series thematically contrasts his manufactured power with Kamille's innate evolution.

Gates Capa piloted the Psyco Gundam or Gundam Mk-II. — His only attested TV machine is the ORX-005 Gaplant. Psyco Gundam is piloted by Four Murasame (and later Rosamia in some media), and Gundam Mk-II by Kamille, Emma, and Jerid. Game assignments of other units are non-canonical.

Gates Capa is a major rival to Kamille on the level of Jerid Messa or Paptimus Scirocco. — He is a minor, late-arc Titans ace with only 3-4 episodes of presence. His narrative function is to show the mass-produced, expendable nature of Cyber Newtypes, not to serve as Kamille's primary rival. Jerid and Scirocco carry the main rival arcs.

Gates Capa appears prominently in the Zeta Gundam movies. — His role was almost entirely cut from A New Translation. Viewers who only watch the films may not see him at all, leading to the false impression he was never in Zeta.

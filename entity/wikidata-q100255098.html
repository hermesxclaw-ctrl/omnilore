Accounts I
Composing 'Mind the Gap'IMDb and film reviews

2004

Veigar composed the original score for Eric Schaeffer's film 'Mind the Gap', a drama about five strangers whose lives intersect. The score contributed to the film's emotional tone. Established Veigar as a film composer.

Accounts II
Creating 'Requiem for a Tower'Various online articles and interviews

2002

Veigar and collaborators arranged Clint Mansell's 'Lux Aeterna' into a more epic, orchestral-choral version specifically for the 'The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers' trailer. The piece became iconic and was later used in numerous other trailers, though it was not part of the film's score. The piece gained widespread popularity and became a staple in trailer music, but also led to confusion about its origin.

Premiering 'Rætur'Iceland Symphony Orchestra records

2007

Veigar composed a saxophone concerto based on Icelandic folk songs for saxophonist Sigurður Flosason. It was premiered by Flosason and the Iceland Symphony Orchestra. Contributed to Icelandic contemporary classical repertoire.

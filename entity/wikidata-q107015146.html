The Approach

On a distant planet ruled by a king with a heart-shaped tail, a princess with a sharp tusk and a love for gothic Lolita fashion spends her days chatting with alien animals—until she and her twin sister decide to crash a human boy's bathroom. Nana Astar Deviluke is the second princess of Deviluke, a girl who can talk to any creature in the galaxy, yet struggles to understand her own feelings. She is fierce, impulsive, and fiercely loyal, but beneath her tough exterior lies a heart that yearns for connection—a heart that will be tested when she befriends a living weapon and learns that friendship can be the most dangerous adventure of all.

Content advisory. The source material contains mild fan service and comedic situations involving partial nudity and suggestive themes, typical of the To Love Ru franchise. There is no graphic violence or dark subject matter in the character's own story, but the narrative includes emotional distress, betrayal, and themes of identity and loneliness.
Identity

Nana Astar Deviluke

Pronunciation NAH-nah AS-tar DEH-vee-loo-kay

Second Princess of DevilukeNana

Etymology

The name 'Nana' is a common Japanese name, often associated with the number seven (七, nana). 'Astar' is a given name with no clear real-world etymology, likely invented for the character. 'Deviluke' is the name of her home planet and royal family, possibly a play on 'devil' and 'duke'.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Saki Hasemi (story) and Kentaro Yabuki (art) for the Japanese manga series To Love Ru, first published by Shueisha in Jump Square. The character is part of the broader anime and manga culture, with a global fanbase.

Disputed Identifications

There is no known dispute over her identity. Some fans may confuse her with her twin sister Momo, but their distinct personalities and appearances (Momo has short hair and a more seductive demeanor) make them distinguishable.

Description

Appearance

Nana is a teenage girl with purple eyes and long pink hair, typically styled in pigtails. She has a sharp tusk on the left side of her upper teeth, a distinctive trait. Like all Devilukeans, she has a long black tail ending in a heart-shaped tip. Her height is 151 cm, weight 43 kg, and three sizes are B68-W54-H77. She favors Gothic Lolita fashion, often wearing black and red outfits.

Symbols & Iconography

Her D-Dial, a mobile phone-like device that summons animals, is a key item. Her pigtails and tail are iconic. The color pink is associated with her hair, and black/red with her clothing.

Behaviour & Personality

Nana is energetic, impulsive, and tomboyish. She is initially hostile toward Rito, often beating him for perceived perversions. She is fiercely protective of her friends and family, and deeply caring, especially toward animals. She struggles with her romantic feelings, often suppressing them and dreaming of Rito in a sexual manner, which she denies. She is brave and determined, as seen in her confrontation with Mea.

Domains & Habitats

Originally from the planet Deviluke, she later lives on Earth in the attic of Rito's house, which she and Momo remodel with space distortion technology. She attends Sainan High School.

Version Differences

In the original To Love Ru, Nana is a minor character introduced later. In To Love Ru Darkness, her role expands significantly, focusing on her friendship with Mea and her emotional growth. The anime adaptations generally follow the manga, but some scenes are expanded or altered. The OVAs provide additional comedic moments. In video games, she is a playable character with various outfits and scenarios.

Powers & Abilities

Animal Communication

Source Version All versions

Description

Nana can communicate with any animal species, including alien creatures. She uses this ability to befriend and command animals, storing them in her D-Dial.

Limitations

She can only communicate with animals, not control them absolutely, though she can request their aid. The ability is not shown to work on sentient humanoids.

Contradictions

None noted.

D-Dial Summoning

Source Version All versions

Description

Using her D-Dial, a device shaped like a mobile phone, Nana can summon animals and plants stored in a virtual space. This is used for both practical and combat purposes.

Limitations

The D-Dial requires the animals to be stored first and has a limited capacity. It may be vulnerable to damage or malfunction.

Contradictions

The exact mechanics of the D-Dial are not fully explained.

Physical Combat

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

Despite her small stature, Nana is capable of physical combat, as shown when she defeats Zastin with the help of Momo and summoned creatures. She is agile and strong.

Limitations

Her combat abilities are not superhuman; she relies on tactics and summoned allies.

Contradictions

None noted.

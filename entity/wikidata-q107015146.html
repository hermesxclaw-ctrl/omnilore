Who It Is

On a distant planet ruled by a king with a heart-shaped tail, a princess with a sharp tusk and a love for gothic Lolita fashion spends her days chatting with alien animals—until she and her twin sister decide to crash a human boy's bathroom. Nana Astar Deviluke is the second princess of Deviluke, a girl who can talk to any creature in the galaxy, yet struggles to understand her own feelings. She is fierce, impulsive, and fiercely loyal, but beneath her tough exterior lies a heart that yearns for connection—a heart that will be tested when she befriends a living weapon and learns that friendship can be the most dangerous adventure of all.

Nana is energetic, impulsive, and tomboyish. She is initially hostile toward Rito, often beating him for perceived perversions. She is fiercely protective of her friends and family, and deeply caring, especially toward animals. She struggles with her romantic feelings, often suppressing them and dreaming of Rito in a sexual manner, which she denies. She is brave and determined, as seen in her confrontation with Mea.

Name & Tongue

Powers
Animal Communication

Nana can communicate with any animal species, including alien creatures. She uses this ability to befriend and command animals, storing them in her D-Dial.

Limit: She can only communicate with animals, not control them absolutely, though she can request their aid. The ability is not shown to work on sentient humanoids.

D-Dial Summoning

Using her D-Dial, a device shaped like a mobile phone, Nana can summon animals and plants stored in a virtual space. This is used for both practical and combat purposes.

Limit: The D-Dial requires the animals to be stored first and has a limited capacity. It may be vulnerable to damage or malfunction.

Physical Combat

Despite her small stature, Nana is capable of physical combat, as shown when she defeats Zastin with the help of Momo and summoned creatures. She is agile and strong.

Limit: Her combat abilities are not superhuman; she relies on tactics and summoned allies.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Nana is a teenage girl with purple eyes and long pink hair, typically styled in pigtails. She has a sharp tusk on the left side of her upper teeth, a distinctive trait. Like all Devilukeans, she has a long black tail ending in a heart-shaped tip. Her height is 151 cm, weight 43 kg, and three sizes are B68-W54-H77. She favors Gothic Lolita fashion, often wearing black and red outfits.

Symbols — Her D-Dial, a mobile phone-like device that summons animals, is a key item. Her pigtails and tail are iconic. The color pink is associated with her hair, and black/red with her clothing.

Domains — Originally from the planet Deviluke, she later lives on Earth in the attic of Rito's house, which she and Momo remodel with space distortion technology. She attends Sainan High School.

Accounts I
Arrival on Earth and Trouble QuestTo Love Ru manga, chapters 97-100

2008 (serialization)

Nana and Momo arrive on Earth and transport the main cast into a virtual reality RPG called Trouble Quest to test Rito's worthiness as Lala's fiancé. The game goes awry, but they eventually apologize and return to Deviluke. The twins' actions cause chaos but ultimately reinforce Rito's relationships. They leave Earth but later return.

Accounts II
Escape to EarthTo Love Ru manga, chapters 104-106

2008

Nana and Momo flee from Deviluke to avoid studies, teleporting naked into Rito's bathroom. After a scuffle, Zastin arrives to bring them back, but they defeat him using their D-Dials and convince their father to let them stay on Earth. The twins are allowed to remain on Earth and move into the Yuki household attic.

Friendship with Mea KurosakiTo Love Ru Darkness manga

2010-2017

Nana befriends Mea, who is revealed to be an alien assassin. Their friendship is tested when Mea reveals her true nature and attempts to sever ties. Nana confronts her, insisting that feelings matter more than identity, and they reconcile. Nana's emotional growth and her romantic feelings for Rito intensify after his encouragement. The event is a major arc in Darkness.

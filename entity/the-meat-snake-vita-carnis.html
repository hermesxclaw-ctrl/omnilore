Who It Is

A serpentine predator and the adolescent stage of the Mimic life cycle. Meat Snakes are ambush hunters lurking in upper tree branches, dropping onto prey and rapidly constricting them to death. Unlike Elder Mimics, they are pure instinct — no reasoning, no communication, attacking anything that moves beneath their perch. Their mouths are lined with inward-curving teeth preventing escape. The documentary advises: 'If you must walk beneath trees, walk quickly. If you hear a wet sliding sound from above, do not look up. Run.'

Form

A limbless serpentine creature, 3-5 meters long, composed entirely of raw red muscle tissue with visible sinew and tendon. The 'head' is a blunt featureless mass with a circular mouth ringed by multiple rows of needle teeth. Covered in thin mucus enabling silent movement through branches.

Powers

Silent arboreal movement

Constriction to death

Ambush predation from above

Prey detection via ground vibrations

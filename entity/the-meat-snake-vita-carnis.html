Who It Is
Form

The Meat Snake is described as a serpent-like creature with a body composed of flesh, which is said to be capable of consuming and assimilating the flesh of other creatures.

Powers

Consumption The Meat Snake has the ability to consume and assimilate the flesh of other creatures, allowing it to grow and transform into new forms.

Accounts

The story of the discovery of the Meat Snake is unclear, but it is believed to have been discovered by cleaners in one of the Nazi railway tunnels.

The story of the Meat Snake and the Crawl is a central part of Vita Carnis mythology, describing the creature's role in the creation of the world.

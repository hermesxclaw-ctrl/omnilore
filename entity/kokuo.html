Who It Is
Form

Kokuō is depicted as a large, serpent-like creature with five tails. It is said to possess immense power and strength.

Powers

Enhanced Strength Kokuō possesses immense physical strength, capable of withstanding and delivering powerful blows.

Accounts

Kokuō is sealed within Han, a ninja from Iwagakure, as part of the Nine-Tails' seal.

Kokuō is revived by a group of rogue ninjas, who seek to use its power for their own gain.

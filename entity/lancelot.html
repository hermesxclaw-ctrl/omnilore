Who It Is
Form

Lancelot is typically depicted as a handsome and skilled knight, often wearing armor and carrying a sword. He is often shown as a close companion to King Arthur and a member of the Knights of the Round Table.

Powers

Chivalry Lancelot's primary power and ability is his adherence to the code of chivalry, which guides his behavior and decision-making. This code emphasizes the importance of honor, loyalty, and protection of the weak.

Accounts

The story of Lancelot and Guinevere is a central part of the Arthurian legend, and revolves around the illicit love affair between the two characters. The story begins with Lancelot's arrival at Camelot, where he becomes a close companion to King Arthur and a member of the Knights of the Round Table.

The Quest for the Holy Grail is a central part of the Arthurian legend, and revolves around the Knights of the Round Table's quest to retrieve the sacred cup. Lancelot is a key participant in this quest, and plays a crucial role in the story's outcome.

Who It Is
Form

Pelesit is generally a cricket, or occasionally a grasshopper. They are often associated with the natural world and are said to be able to mimic the sounds of other insects.

Powers

Possession Pelesit are said to be able to possess and influence the actions of humans, often for malevolent purposes.

Elemental Control Pelesit are said to have a connection to the elements, particularly wind and earth, and are able to control these elements to achieve their goals.

Accounts

A story about a group of villagers who are haunted by a Pelesit spirit. The spirit is said to have possessed one of the villagers, causing them to act strangely and eventually leading to their downfall.

A story about a group of shamans who attempt to summon a Pelesit spirit. The spirit is said to have been summoned successfully, but it quickly turned on the shamans, causing them to flee in terror.

Who It Is

The Chop-Chop Fruit, a Paramecia-type Devil Fruit that allows the user to split and separate their body into pieces at will. Each piece can float and move independently while remaining under the user's control. The user is immune to cutting and slashing attacks. Eaten by Buggy the Clown.

Powers

Body fragmentation, floating limbs, immunity to slashing

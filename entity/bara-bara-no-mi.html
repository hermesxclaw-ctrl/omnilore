Who It Is

In the world of One Piece, the Bara Bara no Mi is a Paramecia-type Devil Fruit that grants its eater the ability to split their body into floating pieces, a power famously wielded by Buggy the Clown. This fruit transforms its user into a 'Chop-Chop Human', allowing them to detach and control their limbs at will, making them nearly immune to slashing attacks and enabling a unique fighting style that combines comedy and lethality.

The fruit grants its user the ability to split their body into multiple pieces, each of which can be controlled independently. The user can fly by controlling their limbs, and they become immune to slashing attacks. The user's personality is unaffected, but the power encourages a flamboyant and theatrical fighting style.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — 'Bara Bara' is a Japanese onomatopoeia meaning 'scattered' or 'separated', reflecting the fruit's power to split the body into pieces.

Pronunciation — BAH-rah BAH-rah no MEE

Aliases — Chop-Chop Fruit, Split-Split Fruit

Ownership — Created by Eiichiro Oda for the manga One Piece, first appearing in 1997. The fruit is a fictional element within the One Piece universe, not based on any real-world mythology.

Powers
Body Splitting

The user can split their body into multiple pieces, including limbs, head, and torso, and control each piece independently. The pieces can float and move through the air.

Limit: The user cannot regenerate lost pieces; they must be reattached. The user cannot split beyond a certain number of pieces (exact limit unknown). The power does not grant immunity to non-slashing attacks.

Slash Immunity

Because the body can split, slashing attacks pass through the user without causing harm.

Limit: The user must be aware of the attack to split; if caught off-guard, they can be cut. Blunt force attacks still work.

Flight

By controlling their detached limbs, the user can fly by moving their hands and feet through the air.

Limit: The user cannot fly without their limbs; if limbs are captured, flight is impaired.

Chop-Chop Cannon

A technique where the user launches their fists at the opponent, similar to a rocket punch.

Limit: The fists must return to the body; if they hit a surface, they may be stuck.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Bara Bara no Mi is a purple, spherical fruit with a swirl pattern and a stem, resembling a pineapple. Its exact appearance is shown in manga volume 3, chapter 19, and anime episode 5.

Symbols — The fruit's symbol is the 'Bara Bara' pattern, representing separation and scattering. It is often depicted with a cross-section showing segments.

Domains — The fruit is a physical object, not tied to a specific habitat. Its user, Buggy, is a pirate captain, so it is used in maritime and island settings.

Accounts I
Buggy's IntroductionOne Piece manga, chapters 8-21; anime episodes 4-8

1997

Buggy the Clown, a pirate captain, uses the Bara Bara no Mi's power to terrorize a town and capture Luffy's crew. He demonstrates his ability to split his body, but Luffy defeats him by using his rubber body to counter Buggy's attacks. Buggy is defeated and later becomes a recurring antagonist, eventually rising to the position of Warlord of the Sea.

Accounts II
Buggy's Escape from Impel DownOne Piece manga, chapters 525-549; anime episodes 422-431

2009

Buggy is imprisoned in Impel Down, but uses his power to escape during the chaos caused by Luffy's infiltration. He accidentally becomes a key figure in the prison break, leading to his rise in notoriety. Buggy gains a large following and is later appointed as a Warlord, despite his incompetence.

The Approach

In the royal selection of Lugunica, where candidates vie for the Dragon's throne, Priscilla Barielle stands as a figure of unapologetic pride and fiery ambition. Known as the 'Flame Princess,' she treats the world as her stage, and her every word is a decree. But beneath her haughty exterior lies a complex history of survival, loss, and a determination to carve her own destiny in a world that once sought to break her.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of classism, elitism, and psychological manipulation. Priscilla's character often displays condescending and dismissive attitudes towards others, which may be off-putting to some viewers. Her backstory involves themes of abuse and exploitation, though these are not graphically depicted.

Etymology

Priscilla is a Latin name meaning 'ancient' or 'venerable'. Barielle is a surname of unclear origin, possibly derived from French or Italian roots.

Cultural Ownership

Re:Zero is a Japanese light novel and anime franchise created by Tappei Nagatsuki. Priscilla is a fictional character within that franchise, owned by the author and publishers.

Disputed Identifications

None known. Priscilla is a distinct character with no real-world counterpart.

Description

Appearance

Priscilla is a young woman with long, flowing red hair and piercing red eyes. She is often dressed in elaborate, revealing outfits that reflect her flamboyant personality, typically in shades of red, gold, and white. She carries herself with an air of absolute confidence and authority.

Symbols & Iconography

Her symbol is often associated with the sun, reflecting her pride and her belief that she is the center of the world. Her outfit often features sun motifs or fiery colors.

Behaviour & Personality

Priscilla is extremely arrogant, self-centered, and vain. She believes that the world revolves around her and that everything is her property. She speaks in a condescending manner and shows little regard for others' feelings. However, she is also highly perceptive, intelligent, and possesses a strong will. She is not purely evil; she has a code of honor and can be generous to those she deems worthy. She is fiercely independent and refuses to be controlled by anyone.

Domains & Habitats

She is the head of the Barielle house and owns a territory in the kingdom of Lugunica. She resides in a luxurious mansion and travels with a large entourage.

Version Differences

In the light novel, Priscilla's backstory is explored in more depth, particularly her relationship with her late husband and her past as a slave. The anime adaptation condenses these details but retains her core personality. Some scenes in the anime are slightly altered for pacing, but her characterization remains consistent.

Powers & Abilities

Yang Magic

Source Version Light novel and anime

Description

Priscilla is a powerful user of Yang Magic, which is associated with fire and light. She can generate intense heat and flames, and can even control the temperature around her. Her magic is said to be among the strongest in the kingdom.

Limitations

Her magic is not unlimited; she requires mana, which is internal. She may also be limited by her own stamina.

Priscilla's Past

Source Re:Zero Light Novel, Arc 5 (Volume 16+)Culture Japanese light novel/animePeriod Fictional timeline

Summary

It is revealed that Priscilla was once a slave who was bought by Duke Barielle, an elderly nobleman. She was forced to marry him, but he treated her kindly. After his death, she inherited his title and wealth, becoming the head of the house. She uses her position to gain power and influence, and she is determined to never be subjugated again.

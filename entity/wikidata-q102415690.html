Who It Is

In the royal selection of Lugunica, where candidates vie for the Dragon's throne, Priscilla Barielle stands as a figure of unapologetic pride and fiery ambition. Known as the 'Flame Princess,' she treats the world as her stage, and her every word is a decree. But beneath her haughty exterior lies a complex history of survival, loss, and a determination to carve her own destiny in a world that once sought to break her.

Priscilla is extremely arrogant, self-centered, and vain. She believes that the world revolves around her and that everything is her property. She speaks in a condescending manner and shows little regard for others' feelings. However, she is also highly perceptive, intelligent, and possesses a strong will. She is not purely evil; she has a code of honor and can be generous to those she deems worthy. She is fiercely independent and refuses to be controlled by anyone.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Priscilla is a Latin name meaning 'ancient' or 'venerable'. Barielle is a surname of unclear origin, possibly derived from French or Italian roots.

Pronunciation — Pri-SIL-la Ba-ree-EL

Aliases — Priscilla, Flame Princess, Priscilla-sama

Ownership — Re:Zero is a Japanese light novel and anime franchise created by Tappei Nagatsuki. Priscilla is a fictional character within that franchise, owned by the author and publishers.

Powers
Yang Magic

Priscilla is a powerful user of Yang Magic, which is associated with fire and light. She can generate intense heat and flames, and can even control the temperature around her. Her magic is said to be among the strongest in the kingdom.

Limit: Her magic is not unlimited; she requires mana, which is internal. She may also be limited by her own stamina.

Sword Proficiency

Priscilla is also skilled with a sword, though she rarely uses it. She carries a rapier and can defend herself if needed.

Limit: Not her primary combat style; she relies more on magic.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Priscilla is a young woman with long, flowing red hair and piercing red eyes. She is often dressed in elaborate, revealing outfits that reflect her flamboyant personality, typically in shades of red, gold, and white. She carries herself with an air of absolute confidence and authority.

Symbols — Her symbol is often associated with the sun, reflecting her pride and her belief that she is the center of the world. Her outfit often features sun motifs or fiery colors.

Domains — She is the head of the Barielle house and owns a territory in the kingdom of Lugunica. She resides in a luxurious mansion and travels with a large entourage.

Accounts I
Introduction at the Royal SelectionRe:Zero Light Novel Arc 3, Anime Episode 12

Fictional timeline

Priscilla is introduced as one of the five candidates for the throne of Lugunica. She arrives in the capital with her entourage and immediately makes a grand entrance, asserting her superiority. She dismisses the other candidates, especially Emilia, and shows no interest in forming alliances. Sets the stage for the political conflict of the royal selection. Priscilla becomes a rival to Emilia.

The Selection CeremonyRe:Zero Light Novel Arc 3, Anime Episode 12

Fictional timeline

During the ceremony, Priscilla shows her disdain for the process and her belief that she is the rightful ruler. She mocks the other candidates and even threatens them. She also demonstrates her magical power by creating a burst of flame. Establishes her as a formidable and unpredictable candidate.

Accounts II
The Whale HuntRe:Zero Light Novel Arc 3, Anime Episode 20-25

Fictional timeline

Priscilla participates in the expedition to hunt the White Whale, a legendary beast. She contributes her magical power and shows her combat abilities. During the battle, she saves Subaru's life at one point, though she does so for her own reasons. The whale is defeated, and Priscilla gains some respect from others, though she remains aloof.

Priscilla's PastRe:Zero Light Novel, Arc 5 (Volume 16+)

Fictional timeline

It is revealed that Priscilla was once a slave who was bought by Duke Barielle, an elderly nobleman. She was forced to marry him, but he treated her kindly. After his death, she inherited his title and wealth, becoming the head of the house. She uses her position to gain power and influence, and she is determined to never be subjugated again. Provides depth to her character and explains her fierce independence and disdain for being controlled.

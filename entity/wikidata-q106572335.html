The Approach

In the neon-lit sewers of New York, a failed experiment in genetic ambition claws its way to power—Rahzar, the wolf-like mutant forged from desperation and dark science, stands as one of the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles' most tragic and relentless foes.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of body horror, violence, and themes of betrayal and revenge.
Identity

Rahzar

Pronunciation RAH-zar

Known As

Chris BradfordDogpoundThe Wolf Mutant

Etymology

The name 'Rahzar' is likely a play on 'razor', referencing his sharp claws and aggressive nature.

Cultural Ownership

Owned by Nickelodeon/Paramount as part of the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles franchise.

Disputed Identifications

Rahzar is sometimes confused with other canine mutants like Dogpound, but they are distinct characters in the 2012 series, though Rahzar is Dogpound's evolved form.

Description

Appearance

Rahzar is a hulking, wolf-like mutant with grey fur, a prominent snout, sharp fangs and claws, and a hunched, bestial posture. He retains some of his human martial arts attire, such as a black gi, but it is tattered. His eyes are yellow with slitted pupils.

Symbols & Iconography

Rahzar's imagery is associated with wolves, strength, and mutation. He often bears the Foot Clan's symbol on his clothing.

Behaviour & Personality

Rahzar is ruthless, cunning, and fiercely loyal to the Shredder, though he harbors bitterness over his mutation. He is a skilled martial artist and fighter, but his animal instincts sometimes override his training. He is less intelligent than his human form but more savage.

Domains & Habitats

Rahzar primarily operates in New York City, often seen in the sewers, abandoned buildings, and the Foot Clan's headquarters.

Version Differences

In the 2012 series, Rahzar is the second mutation of Chris Bradford. In other media, such as the 2014 film, a similar character named Rahzar appears as a mutant wolf created by the Kraang, but he is not directly connected to Chris Bradford. In the 2003 series, a character named Rahzar is a mutant wolf created by the Shredder, but with a different backstory.

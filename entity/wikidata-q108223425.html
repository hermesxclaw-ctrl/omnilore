Who It Is

Before she said a word, the sky already knew her name. On August 23, 2021, a 169-centimeter girl with hair like starlight and an antenna that bobbed like a planet's halo introduced herself as the literal concept of space — ancient, boundless, and barely contained by a limiter shaped like a black halo. Tsukumo Sana wasn't playing an astronaut. She was the room in which every astronaut floats, trying very hard to be a normal, cheerful kouhai while her true size threatened to outgrow the screen.

Attested from streams and clips: Sana presented as gentle, soft-spoken, earnest, and nurturing ("mommy" archetype in fan slang but she rejected overly sexualized reading), with a calm, slightly airheaded and whimsical humor. Known for soothing low voice, frequent 'ehehe' laugh, love of drawing and astrology, and self-deprecating jokes about her height and clumsiness with technology. Powers
Conceptual Embodiment of Space

In lore, Sana IS space itself - the dimensional container of existence - not merely a user of space powers. Created by the Gods as an abstract concept given human form to govern the world.

Limit: Must be constrained to human scale to interact safely; uncontrolled expansion would destabilize reality.

Size and Spatial Manipulation (Limiter-Dependent)

Ability to expand/compress own size and surrounding space. Shown comedically via overlay growth gags and lore where limiter removal triggers rapid expansion.

Limit: Limiter (black halo with planet) actively suppresses power; even with limiter, excitement causes glitches. Full release forbidden by Gods.

Gravity and Celestial Influence (Implied)

As Space, implied ambient influence over gravity and celestial bodies, playfully linked to astrology and planet motifs. Never shown as combat power.

Limit: Never weaponized or shown moving planets; Sana jokes she is not good at physics.

Artistic and Divinatory Skills (Human-scale)

Exceptional drawing, astrology chart reading, and soothing voice work. Performer talents framed as Sana's hobbies, not supernatural.

Limit: Human-level skill requiring study; disclaimed as entertainment not professional prediction.

Accounts I
Debut: The Speaker of Space Lands on EarthDebut stream 'Space! Sana Landing!' (YouTube, 23 Aug 2021) and Council trailer

2021-08-23

Introduced as the first of Council to speak, Sana descends from the cosmos after being created by the Gods alongside Time (Kronii), Civilization (Mumei), Nature (Fauna), and Chaos (Baelz). Her limiter is explained as necessary to keep her from expanding to cosmic size. She introduces her greeting 'Yoohoo! Sana here!' and establishes her gentle, space-mom persona, draws her own emotes live, and explains astrology as her human hobby. Established Council's group dynamic and Sana's role as nurturing, slightly clumsy eldest. Channel gained 100k+ subscribers within days; Council debut as a whole surpassed 1M combined views in 24h.

Council vs. Chaos: The Lore of Concepts Governing the WorldHololive English Council Concept Trailer + Sana's lore reading segment + Hololive Alternative manga cameo

2021-08-22 to 2022-02-26 (Council 3D debut)

The Gods, having shaped the world, create Council to maintain order: Sana (Space) provides the vessel, Kronii (Time) the flow, Mumei (Civilization) the record, Fauna (Nature) the life, while Baelz (Chaos) - originally rejected - is later included to provide necessary disorder. Sana's backstory emphasizes her ancient, lonely existence as infinite void before being given form and limiter to experience human emotion. Council 3D debut (Feb 2022) dramatized this with Sana towering over others, limiter glowing. Provided shared universe for Hololive English second generation, differentiating them from Hololive English -Myth-. Used in Hololive Alternative teaser where Sana appears as chibi space entity.

Sana's Astrology and Art SanctuaryRecurring series 'Sana's Astrology' (YouTube, Sep 2021 - Jun 2022) and art streams

2021-09 to 2022-06

Weekly/biweekly streams where Sana read viewers' birth charts, explained zodiac signs, and drew while chatting. She created original planet mascots, drew Council members, and used her art to comfort viewers struggling with anxiety. The series became her signature content, blending genuine interest in astrology with self-aware disclaimer that it is for entertainment. Built dedicated community identity (Sanallites 🪐), produced large volume of fanart, and established Sana as Hololive's primary 'healing' stream. Clips of her explaining 'Sana is space, so she knows where you are' became meme.

Accounts II
Musical Activities: 'Astrogirl' and Council CollaborationsOriginal song 'Astrogirl' MV (8 Nov 2021), Council song 'Rise' (Council debut single, 2021), and cover songs

2021-11 to 2022-07

Sana released solo original 'Astrogirl,' a gentle mid-tempo pop song about traveling through stars to meet listeners, with lyrics co-written by Sana. She also performed on Council ensemble songs ('Rise,' 'Tuning in the Distance') and covers (e.g., 'KING,' 'Phony'). Her singing voice, soft and breathy, was praised for lullaby quality rather than power vocals. No full solo album was released before graduation. 'Astrogirl' remains her most-viewed original (2M+ views archived), used in graduation tribute videos. Demonstrated Cover's music pipeline for EN members.

The Limiter Gag and Height Meme ArcMultiple Council collabs (e.g., 'Council Meeting' streams, Minecraft Hololive EN server)

2021-08 to 2022-07

Running joke where Sana's limiter malfunctions and she 'grows' off-screen, towering over 150cm-range members like Baelz and Mumei. In Minecraft, she built enormous planet models to scale. She leaned into tall-girl identity, joking about hitting doorframes and being unable to find clothes. The gag reinforced lore while humanizing her. Height became her most recognizable trait outside Hololive fandom; 'Sana is literally bigger than your problems' meme spread on Twitter/Reddit. Official profile's '∞ without limiter' line became copypasta.

Graduation: The Return to SpaceCover graduation notice (11 July 2022) and final stream 'A Final Message from Space' (31 July 2022)

2022-07-11 to 2022-07-31

Cover announced Sana would graduate (retire) at end of July 2022, citing discussions with Sana regarding health and personal circumstances. Sana confirmed in a sincere final stream that she had been struggling with health and felt unable to continue at the level she wanted. She thanked Sanallites, Council, and staff, performed 'Astrogirl' one last time, and framed graduation as 'returning to space' to watch over everyone, not disappearing. Channel memberships closed, but archive remains. Council continued as four, later reorganized as Promise (without Sana) in 2023. Marked first graduation in Hololive English; prompted industry discussion about VTuber health and workload. Sana's character retired, not recast. Tribute art #ThankYouSana trended globally. Council lore retroactively frames her absence as Space becoming distant again.

Sana is 169cm tall, period — 169cm is height WITH limiter; without limiter lore states boundless/infinite. ∞ is canonical flavor text indicating concept nature.

Sana graduated due to unpopularity or being fired — Documented reason is health/personal circumstances after mutual discussion; she had mid-tier Council viewership and strong fanbase. No evidence of performance-based termination.

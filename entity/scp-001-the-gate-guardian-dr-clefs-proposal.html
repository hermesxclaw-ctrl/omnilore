Who It Is

A colossal angelic entity standing guard before a gate in an undisclosed desert location. The entity — described consistently across Abrahamic religions as the angel Uriel — holds a flaming sword and warns all who approach: 'FORGET.' It guards something beyond the gate, something that must never be opened.

Form

A humanoid figure approximately 300 meters tall, composed of light and flame. Four broad wings of white fire extend from its back. Its face is 'impossible to look at directly.' It holds a sword that burns without consuming, and its voice is thunder that speaks directly into the mind.

Powers

Absolute perimeter enforcement

Memory erasure command ('FORGET')

Sword capable of destroying any physical or conceptual entity

Who It Is
Form

SCP-001 is described as a mysterious entity that appears to be a gate or a portal. The entity is said to be surrounded by an aura of power and authority. Those who approach SCP-001 report feeling a sense of awe and reverence.

Powers

Directive 'FORGET' SCP-001 is said to have the ability to issue a directive to those who approach it, forcing them to forget their memories and experiences.

Incident Log

Dr. Clef proposes the SCP-001 designation for the entity known as The Gate Guardian. The proposal outlines the entity's abilities and characteristics, including its power to issue the directive 'FORGET'.

SCP-001 (Dr. Clef's Proposal) is described as one of the many competing proposals for the SCP-001 designation on the SCP Foundation collaborative fiction website.

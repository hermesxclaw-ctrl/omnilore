What goes inside stays inside. Define inside. Define what goes inside. Then point SCP-2719 at what needs to go inside. It will go inside. It always goes inside.

Who It Is

SCP-2719 is one of the most abstract and intellectually challenging entries in the SCP catalog. It is a variable abstract-metaphysical construct pointer. In simpler terms, it is a concept that can be 'pointed at' other concepts, causing those concepts to go 'inside' something else. The Foundation discovered SCP-2719 through investigation of O5-9, who had been attempting to use the concept to secure his own immortality. The entry is notoriously difficult to parse on first reading — it is written in deliberately opaque, technical language that mirrors the incomprehensibility of the anomaly itself. The key mechanic involves defining what 'inside' means, defining what is 'outside,' and then directing SCP-2719 at a target. In documented applications, SCP-2719 has been used to put a D-Class personnel 'inside' another D-Class personnel, to put knowledge 'inside' a container, and to put abstract concepts 'inside' other abstract concepts. The danger is that SCP-2719 does not distinguish between physical and conceptual targets — it operates on a level of reality where the boundary between the literal and the metaphorical does not exist. A poorly phrased directive could put an entire city 'inside' a concept, or put the concept of 'death' inside a person. The SCP-2719 entry is a masterpiece of experimental fiction, challenging readers to understand something that is literally incomprehensible while maintaining the clinical Foundation tone. It has spawned extensive community analysis, with readers building elaborate diagrams and interpretations to understand what the article is actually describing. SCP-2719 represents the SCP Wiki at its most ambitious and cerebral — horror not from a monster, but from the idea that reality itself has a command line, and we've started typing commands.

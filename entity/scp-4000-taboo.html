Who It Is

SCP-4000 — Taboo is a highly complex and multifaceted entity that continues to be the subject of ongoing research and study.

SCP-4000 — Taboo is a highly unpredictable and volatile entity. It has been known to shift and change its appearance at random, and its behavior can be influenced by the actions of those who enter the area.

Powers
Anomalous Nomenclative Phenomenon

SCP-4000 — Taboo has the ability to manipulate the names of objects and concepts, causing them to change and shift in unpredictable ways. This phenomenon can have a profound impact on the behavior and properties of the objects and concepts affected.

Limit:

Extradimensional Travel

SCP-4000 — Taboo has the ability to move through dimensions and planes of existence, allowing it to access areas and objects that are not normally accessible.

Limit:

Form & Signs

Appearance — SCP-4000 — Taboo is an extradimensional forested area with numerous anomalous qualities. The area is characterized by a dense, misty atmosphere and an eerie silence. The trees in the area appear to be twisted and gnarled, with branches that seem to reach out like skeletal fingers.

Symbols — The area is home to a variety of anomalous symbols and iconography, including strange markings on the trees and a series of cryptic murals on the walls of the forest.

Domains — SCP-4000 — Taboo exists in a realm outside of our own, and can only be accessed through the use of a specific ritual (4000-Halloway).

Accounts I
The Discovery of SCP-4000 — TabooSCP Foundation

2008

A team of researchers from the SCP Foundation discovers SCP-4000 — Taboo, an extradimensional forested area with numerous anomalous qualities. The discovery of SCP-4000 — Taboo leads to a significant increase in the Foundation's understanding of anomalous phenomena and the development of new technologies for containing and studying these entities.

Accounts II
The Containment Breach of SCP-4000 — TabooYouTube

2019

A containment breach occurs at the SCP Foundation's facility for SCP-4000 — Taboo, allowing the entity to escape and wreak havoc on the surrounding area. The containment breach of SCP-4000 — Taboo leads to significant damage to the Foundation's facility and the surrounding area, as well as the loss of several researchers and personnel.

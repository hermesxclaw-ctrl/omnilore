Who It Is

A vast extradimensional forest realm, designated 'the Nameless City,' where naming something — anything — causes it to die or be violently transformed. The realm is inhabited by the fae, a race of beings who lost their names and thus their identities, now existing as nameless, hostile entities who strip names from intruders. Simply saying your own name in this realm results in immediate and permanent identity death.

Form

An endless forest of bone-white trees beneath a sky without stars. The fae appear as tall, elegant humanoids whose faces have been worn smooth — no eyes, nose, or mouth remaining. They move in absolute silence and 'steal' names through proximity.

Powers

Name-based conceptual destruction

Identity erasure

Reality restructuring by nomenclature

Fae name-theft

SCP-4000 — Taboo

also known asTabooSCP-4000
Typescp
CultureSCP Foundation
Who It Is
Form

SCP-4000 — Taboo is an extradimensional forested area with numerous anomalous qualities. The area is characterized by a dense, misty atmosphere and an eerie silence. The trees in the area appear to be twisted and gnarled, with branches that seem to reach out like skeletal fingers.

Powers

Anomalous Nomenclative Phenomenon SCP-4000 — Taboo has the ability to manipulate the names of objects and concepts, causing them to change and shift in unpredictable ways. This phenomenon can have a profound impact on the behavior and properties of the objects and concepts affected.

Extradimensional Travel SCP-4000 — Taboo has the ability to move through dimensions and planes of existence, allowing it to access areas and objects that are not normally accessible.

Incident Log

A team of researchers from the SCP Foundation discovers SCP-4000 — Taboo, an extradimensional forested area with numerous anomalous qualities.

A containment breach occurs at the SCP Foundation's facility for SCP-4000 — Taboo, allowing the entity to escape and wreak havoc on the surrounding area.

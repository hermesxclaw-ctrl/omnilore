Who It Is

Beneath the ancient mountains of San Francisco, a colossal being slumbers—not a god of myth, but a Celestial, one of the universe's oldest and most enigmatic architects. Known as the Dreaming Celestial, this entity embodies the paradox of creation and destruction, its dreams shaping realities and its awakening promising apocalypse. Its story is a testament to the cosmic scale of Marvel's mythology, where even the mightiest beings are bound by prophecy and purpose.

Unlike many Celestials, the Dreaming Celestial is not overtly malevolent or benevolent. It is a neutral force, driven by its own inscrutable cosmic purpose. It communicates through dreams and visions, and its actions are guided by a prophecy that it must one day awaken to judge humanity. It is patient, ancient, and detached from mortal concerns.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Dreaming' refers to its state of dormancy and its ability to shape reality through dreams. 'Tiamut' is a variant of Tiamat, the primordial sea goddess from Mesopotamian myth, possibly reflecting its chaotic and creative nature.

Pronunciation — TEE-ah-moot

Aliases — Tiamut the Dreaming Celestial, The Dreamer, The Celestial Dreamer

Ownership — Marvel Comics (Marvel Entertainment / Disney). Created by Jack Kirby, first appearing in Eternals (vol. 1) #2 (1976).

Powers
Reality Warping

Capable of altering reality on a cosmic scale, reshaping matter, energy, and even time. Its dreams can manifest as alternate realities or influence the physical world.

Limit: Its power is often limited by its dormant state; full awakening is required for maximum effect.

Cosmic Energy Projection

Can project vast amounts of cosmic energy for offensive or defensive purposes, capable of destroying planets or shielding itself.

Limit: Energy output may be reduced when dormant.

Immortality and Invulnerability

As a Celestial, it is effectively immortal and immune to most forms of harm, including conventional weapons and most superhuman attacks.

Limit: Vulnerable to other cosmic entities of similar or greater power, such as Galactus or other Celestials.

Dream Manipulation

Can enter and manipulate the dreams of other beings, sending visions, prophecies, or psychological torment.

Limit: May require proximity or a psychic link.

Size and Strength

Its immense size grants superhuman strength, capable of lifting objects of similar mass and causing widespread destruction.

Limit: While dormant, it is immobile.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Dreaming Celestial appears as a colossal humanoid figure, roughly 2,000 feet tall, with skin resembling stone or metal. Its face is often depicted with a blank, mask-like expression, with glowing eyes that emit cosmic energy. It wears ornate armor, and its body is covered in intricate circuitry and cosmic runes. When dormant, it is encased in a rocky shell, blending into the mountain.

Symbols — Associated with cosmic balance and the cyclical nature of creation/destruction. Its iconography includes the Celestial 'spaceship' motif, and its dreams are often depicted as swirling cosmic energies.

Domains — Primarily located beneath the mountain now known as Devil's Mountain in San Francisco, California, on Earth. It has also been shown to exist in other dimensional realms and cosmic spaces.

Accounts I
The Coming of the Dreaming CelestialEternals (vol. 1) #2-12

1976-1977

The Eternals discover a Celestial sleeping beneath a mountain in San Francisco. The Celestial, later identified as the Dreaming Celestial, is revealed to be part of the Fourth Host, sent to judge humanity. The Eternals, Deviants, and humans become entangled in the cosmic drama, with the Dreaming Celestial's awakening foreshadowed. The discovery sets the stage for the Eternals' ongoing conflict with the Deviants and the looming judgment of the Celestials.

Accounts II
The Dreaming Celestial AwakensEternals (vol. 2) #1-12

1985-1986

The Dreaming Celestial is awakened by external forces, leading to a confrontation with the Eternals and other heroes. Its awakening threatens to trigger a cosmic catastrophe, and the Eternals must find a way to reseal it or communicate with it. The storyline explores the Celestial's purpose and the nature of its dreams. The Celestial is eventually resealed, but its brief awakening causes widespread destruction and raises questions about humanity's future.

The Celestial MessiahEternals (vol. 3) #1-6

2006-2007

Neil Gaiman's run reimagines the Eternals and their connection to the Celestials. The Dreaming Celestial is mentioned as one of the key Celestials, and its dreams influence the plot. The story delves into the Celestials' role as creators and judges, with the Dreaming Celestial playing a behind-the-scenes role. The storyline reshapes the Eternals' mythology, but the Dreaming Celestial remains a background figure.

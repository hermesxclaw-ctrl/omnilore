Who It Is

The Dreaming Celestial, also known as Ziran the Tester, is one of the most enigmatic and terrifying members of the Celestial race. Unlike his kin, who operate through cold logic and cosmic law, the Dreaming Celestial is a reality warper of profound power, and he dreams. While most Celestials are awake and rational, Ziran is perpetually in a state of cosmic slumber buried beneath Wyoming — and his dreams have a tendency to bleed into reality. These dreams manifest as impossible events, paradoxes, and creatures that defy physical law. His very presence on Earth has caused reality to fray at the edges, creating 'dreams' that become real and nightmares that stalk the waking world. Ziran was originally a member of a rebel faction of Celestials who questioned the orthodox methods of their race. He was imprisoned by his fellow Celestials beneath the Earth's surface, where his power was supposed to be contained. Instead, his dreaming mind continued to influence events. During the 2006-2007 Eternals series, Ziran awakened to confront Exitar, defending humanity in a shocking act of compassion (or irrationality — none could tell). He was subsequently 'killed' by the other Celestials but his consciousness persisted, dreaming from beyond death. The Dreaming Celestial embodies the most alien, incomprehensible aspect of the Celestials — the suggestion that even these seemingly rational cosmic architects are driven by forces beyond mortal understanding, and that reality itself may be the dream of a sleeping god.

The Approach

Beneath the ancient mountains of San Francisco, a colossal being slumbers—not a god of myth, but a Celestial, one of the universe's oldest and most enigmatic architects. Known as the Dreaming Celestial, this entity embodies the paradox of creation and destruction, its dreams shaping realities and its awakening promising apocalypse. Its story is a testament to the cosmic scale of Marvel's mythology, where even the mightiest beings are bound by prophecy and purpose.

Content advisory. Contains cosmic-level violence, themes of judgment and extinction, and depictions of immense power that may be unsettling. No graphic or explicit content.
Identity

The Dreaming Celestial

Pronunciation TEE-ah-moot

Known As

Tiamut the Dreaming CelestialThe DreamerThe Celestial Dreamer

Etymology

The name 'Dreaming' refers to its state of dormancy and its ability to shape reality through dreams. 'Tiamut' is a variant of Tiamat, the primordial sea goddess from Mesopotamian myth, possibly reflecting its chaotic and creative nature.

Cultural Ownership

Marvel Comics (Marvel Entertainment / Disney). Created by Jack Kirby, first appearing in Eternals (vol. 1) #2 (1976).

Disputed Identifications

Some fans have conflated the Dreaming Celestial with other Celestials, such as Ziran the Tester, but they are distinct entities. Its role as a 'Celestial' is sometimes debated in terms of hierarchy, but it is consistently depicted as one of the Fourth Host.

Description

Appearance

The Dreaming Celestial appears as a colossal humanoid figure, roughly 2,000 feet tall, with skin resembling stone or metal. Its face is often depicted with a blank, mask-like expression, with glowing eyes that emit cosmic energy. It wears ornate armor, and its body is covered in intricate circuitry and cosmic runes. When dormant, it is encased in a rocky shell, blending into the mountain.

Symbols & Iconography

Associated with cosmic balance and the cyclical nature of creation/destruction. Its iconography includes the Celestial 'spaceship' motif, and its dreams are often depicted as swirling cosmic energies.

Behaviour & Personality

Unlike many Celestials, the Dreaming Celestial is not overtly malevolent or benevolent. It is a neutral force, driven by its own inscrutable cosmic purpose. It communicates through dreams and visions, and its actions are guided by a prophecy that it must one day awaken to judge humanity. It is patient, ancient, and detached from mortal concerns.

Domains & Habitats

Primarily located beneath the mountain now known as Devil's Mountain in San Francisco, California, on Earth. It has also been shown to exist in other dimensional realms and cosmic spaces.

Version Differences

In Jack Kirby's original run, it was a mysterious dormant entity. In later retellings (e.g., Neil Gaiman's Eternals), the Celestials' nature was expanded, but the Dreaming Celestial's core depiction remained consistent. In the Marvel Cinematic Universe, the Celestials are depicted differently, but the Dreaming Celestial has not yet appeared.

The Coming of the Dreaming Celestial

Source Eternals (vol. 1) #2-12Culture Marvel ComicsPeriod 1976-1977

Summary

The Eternals discover a Celestial sleeping beneath a mountain in San Francisco. The Celestial, later identified as the Dreaming Celestial, is revealed to be part of the Fourth Host, sent to judge humanity. The Eternals, Deviants, and humans become entangled in the cosmic drama, with the Dreaming Celestial's awakening foreshadowed.

The storyline explores the Celestial's purpose and the nature of its dreams.

Position A

The Dreaming Celestial was directly involved in creating the Eternals and Deviants.

Ambiguous; the Dreaming Celestial is rarely singled out.

Who It Is
Form

In alchemical texts, Azoth is often depicted as a symbol or a metaphor for the universal remedy or potent solvent, rather than a physical entity. The quest for Azoth was the crux of numerous alchemical endeavors, symbolized by the Caduceus. Initially coined to denote an esoteric formula pursued by alchemists, akin to the Philosopher's Stone, the term Azoth later evolved into a poetic expression for the element mercury.

Powers

Transmutation Azoth is believed to have the power to transmute base metals into gold and to achieve spiritual enlightenment.

Accounts

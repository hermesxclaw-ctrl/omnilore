Who It Is

The quest for Azoth is a fascinating and complex topic that has captivated alchemists and philosophers for centuries. This dossier provides an overview of the concept of Azoth and its significance in the realm of alchemy.

Azoth is often described as a mysterious and elusive substance, difficult to attain or understand. Alchemists believed that Azoth held the key to transmuting base metals into gold and achieving spiritual enlightenment.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In alchemical texts, Azoth is often depicted as a symbol or a metaphor for the universal remedy or potent solvent, rather than a physical entity. The quest for Azoth was the crux of numerous alchemical endeavors, symbolized by the Caduceus. Initially coined to denote an esoteric formula pursued by alchemists, akin to the Philosopher's Stone, the term Azoth later evolved into a poetic expression for the element mercury.

Symbols — The Caduceus, a staff with intertwined snakes, is often associated with the quest for Azoth. The symbol of the Caduceus represents the balance and harmony that Azoth is believed to bring.

Domains — The domain of Azoth is the realm of alchemy, where it is sought after as a universal remedy or potent solvent. The habitats of Azoth are the laboratories and workshops of alchemists, where they attempt to create or discover the substance.

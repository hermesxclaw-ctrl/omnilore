Who It Is

Naobito Zen'in, the seventy-year-old head of one of jujutsu society's most powerful clans, enters battle not with the weary caution of age but with the startling speed of a man who has spent seven decades perfecting the art of violence. In the climactic Shibuya Incident, this weathered patriarch—dismissed by many as a relic—becomes one of the few sorcerers capable of trading blows with the disaster curse Dagon, only to meet his end at the hands of the unhinged curse user Mahito. His death is not a quiet passing but a brutal confrontation that underscores the fragility of even the strongest humans in a world of curses. This dossier explores the man behind the clan: his ruthless pragmatism, his role as a father and grandfather, and the legacy he leaves in a world that is rapidly changing.

Naobito is pragmatic, ruthless, and deeply traditional. He values strength and technique above all, and he holds a low opinion of those he considers weak, including his son Toji, whom he disowned for lacking cursed energy. He is dismissive of modern values and the 'new generation' of sorcerers, initially mocking Megumi's potential. However, he shows a grudging respect for those who prove themselves, like Maki and Megumi. He is also headstrong and confident, believing his age and experience make him superior in combat.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Zen'in' (禪院) is a real Japanese surname; the kanji can mean 'Zen temple' or 'meditation hall'. 'Naobito' (直人) is a given name; 'nao' can mean 'straight' or 'correct', 'bito' can mean 'person'. The name may suggest a person of direct, unyielding character.

Powers
Projection Sorcery

A Cursed Technique that allows Naobito to move in a series of predetermined frames, like a film reel. By setting a 24-frame-per-second speed, he can move at high velocity, appearing as a blur. He can also use this to launch rapid, precise attacks.

Limit: The technique requires Naobito to plan his movements in advance; if he deviates from the plan, he may be briefly immobilized. It also consumes cursed energy, and his physical stamina is limited by his age.

High-level Cursed Energy Manipulation

As a Grade 1 sorcerer and clan head, Naobito has high cursed energy reserves and enhanced physical abilities. He can reinforce his body to withstand powerful attacks.

Limit: Despite his strength, he is not on the level of special-grade sorcerers like Gojo or the disaster curses. He is vulnerable to domain expansion attacks.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Naobito is an elderly man with short white hair and a thin, weathered face. He has sharp, narrowed eyes that often appear stern or calculating. He wears a traditional dark kimono with a haori, typical of the conservative Zen'in clan head. In combat, he moves with surprising agility for his age.

Symbols — The Zen'in clan is associated with the Ten Shadows Technique and the Inherited Cursed Techniques. Naobito himself uses Projection Sorcery, which is not a clan technique. His symbol might be the clan crest, which is not explicitly shown; the clan is known for its strict hierarchy and obsession with inherited techniques.

Domains — Naobito primarily appears in the Zen'in clan compound, which is not depicted in detail but is implied to be a traditional Japanese estate. He travels to Tokyo for the Shibuya Incident, where he fights in the underground area near the Shibuya station.

Accounts I
The Shibuya IncidentJujutsu Kaisen manga/anime

October 31, 2018 (in-universe)

During the Shibuya Incident, Naobito is dispatched to assist in the fight against the curses invading Shibuya. He encounters Dagon, a disaster curse, and engages him in combat. Despite his speed and experience, Dagon's domain expansion traps Naobito and his allies. Naobito is severely injured, losing an arm. He attempts to escape with the help of Maki and Nanami, but they are intercepted by Mahito, who kills Naobito with a touch, transmuting his soul and causing his body to collapse. Naobito's death leaves the Zen'in clan without a head, leading to a power vacuum that contributes to the clan's later decline. His death also highlights the threat of Mahito and the disaster curses.

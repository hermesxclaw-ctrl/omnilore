Who They Are

Kinich Ahau is the Maya sun god, the daytime face of the celestial ruler who travels across the sky from dawn to dusk. He is closely associated with Itzamna (as his daytime aspect), with jaguars (symbolizing the night sun), and with the ruling dynasty of Maya cities. His journey across the heavens is one of the great cycles of Maya cosmology.

Cultural Significance

Kinich Ahau's daily journey is one of the foundational patterns of Maya cosmology, structuring everything from the calendar to daily prayer. The sun's path across the sky determined the timing of all religious ceremonies, and the solstices and equinoxes were major festival dates. Maya kings were identified with the sun god, and royal accession ceremonies often included sun-imagery. The Temple of the Sun at Palenque is a masterpiece of Maya architecture, its entire design oriented toward solar alignments.

Who It Is

In the bustling halls of a Tokyo high school, five identical sisters with five distinct personalities become the center of a young tutor's life—and Itsuki Nakano, the eldest, stands out not by loudness but by her quiet pride and stubborn sense of responsibility. Her journey from a prickly, food-loving girl to a supportive figure in the Quintessential Quintuplets is a study in growth, loyalty, and the subtle art of caring.

Itsuki is initially portrayed as serious, studious, and somewhat prideful. She is the most academically inclined of the five and often acts as the responsible elder sister, though she can be stubborn and easily flustered. She has a strong sense of duty and a desire to succeed on her own merits, which sometimes makes her appear cold or distant. Over time, she reveals a warm, caring side, especially toward her sisters and Futaro. She is also a glutton with a particular love for curry and meat, and her appetite is a running gag.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Nakano (中野) is a common Japanese surname meaning 'middle field'. Itsuki (五月) is written with the kanji for 'five' and 'month', referring to May, fitting her position as one of five quintuplets.

Pronunciation — EE-tsoo-kee nah-KAH-no

Aliases — Elder sister, Eldest quint, The serious one, Foodie

Ownership — Japanese pop culture; created by Negi Haruba for the manga series 'Go-Toubun no Hanayome' (2017-2020).

Powers
Academic prowess

Itsuki is the most academically gifted of the quintuplets, though still below average at the start. She studies diligently and improves significantly under Futaro's tutoring.

Limit: Initially struggles with math and science, like her sisters.

Cooking

She is a decent cook, often preparing meals for her sisters. Her specialty is curry.

Limit: Not as skilled as professional chefs, but adequate.

Eldest sister responsibility

She often takes charge in family matters and mediates conflicts among her sisters, though she can be overbearing.

Limit: Sometimes her pride prevents her from asking for help.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Itsuki has long, light pink hair that falls past her shoulders, often tied into a small ponytail with a black ribbon. She has blue eyes. In the manga and anime, her design is distinct from her sisters by her hair length and ribbon style; she also wears a small ahoge (cowlick). She typically wears the school uniform of Asahiyama High School, and in casual settings prefers simple, modest clothing. In the future scenes, she is shown with longer hair

Symbols — Her symbol is the number 5, as the eldest of the quintuplets. Her hair ribbon is black, while her sisters use different colors (Ichika: pink, Nino: blue, Miku: green, Yotsuba: orange). This color coding helps distinguish the otherwise identical sisters.

Domains — She lives in the Nakano family apartment with her four sisters and initially with Futaro as their tutor. She attends Asahiyama High School. In the future, she becomes a teacher, following in her mother's footsteps.

Accounts I
The Quintessential Quintuplets (main storyline)Manga by Negi Haruba

2017-2020 (publication)

Futaro Uesugi, a brilliant but poor high school student, is hired as a tutor for the five Nakano quintuplets. Itsuki is the first to meet him and initially rejects his help, but gradually comes to respect him. Throughout the series, the sisters face academic and personal challenges, and Futaro helps them improve. Itsuki's arc involves her struggle with her mother's death and her decision to become a teacher. In the end, Futaro chooses Yotsuba, but Itsuki remains a supportive figure and eventually becomes a teacher at the same school. The series concludes with Futaro marrying one of the quintuplets (Yotsuba), and the others move on with their lives. Itsuki becomes a teacher.

Accounts II
The Kyoto Trip ArcManga chapters 68-75

2018

The quintuplets and Futaro go on a school trip to Kyoto. Itsuki, along with her sisters, encounters a young boy who resembles Futaro; this event is tied to the 'Kyoto girl' mystery. Itsuki is the one who initially recognizes the connection, and she plays a role in the flashback that reveals the past encounter. This arc deepens the mystery of Futaro's past and hints at the identity of the bride.

The School Festival ArcManga chapters 100-114

2019

During the school festival, each quintuplet has a moment with Futaro. Itsuki helps with the class play and has a heart-to-heart talk with Futaro about her aspirations. She also encourages him to make a decision about his feelings. This arc leads to the climax where Futaro makes his choice.

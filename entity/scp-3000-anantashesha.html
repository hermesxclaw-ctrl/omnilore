Powers
Mind Control

SCP-3000 is capable of manipulating the minds of those who come into contact with it, driving them to madness and despair. This ability is thought to be a form of psychological warfare, used by the entity to weaken its enemies and gain the upper hand.

Limit:

Telepathy

SCP-3000 is also capable of reading the thoughts and emotions of those who come into contact with it. This ability is thought to be a form of telepathy, used by the entity to gather information and anticipate the actions of its enemies.

Limit:

Regeneration

SCP-3000 is capable of regenerating its body and repairing damage to its scales and other tissues. This ability is thought to be a result of its ability to manipulate its own biology and chemistry.

Limit:

Form & Signs

Appearance — SCP-3000 is a massive, aquatic, serpentine entity strongly resembling a giant moray eel (Gymnothorax javanicus). The full length of SCP-3000 is estimated to be approximately 100 meters. Its body is covered in dark brown to black scales, and it has a series of bioluminescent markings along its length.

Symbols — SCP-3000 is often depicted with a series of cryptic symbols etched into its scales, which are believed to be a form of communication or perhaps even a form of control.

Domains — SCP-3000 is found in the deepest, most remote regions of the ocean, where the pressure is extreme and the darkness is absolute.

Present Day

SCP-3000 was first discovered by a team of researchers from the SCP Foundation in the year 2025. The team was conducting a survey of the ocean floor when they stumbled upon the entity, which was estimated to be approximately 100 meters in length. The discovery of SCP-3000 has had a significant impact on the scientific community, with many researchers and experts calling for further study and investigation into the entity's behavior and abilities.

Present Day

Several researchers have reported experiencing madness and despair after coming into contact with SCP-3000. This is thought to be a result of the entity's ability to manipulate the minds of those who come into contact with it. The experience of madness and despair has had a significant impact on the researchers who have experienced it, with many reporting feelings of anxiety and depression.

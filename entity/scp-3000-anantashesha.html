SCP-3000 — Anantashesha

also known asThe Sea EaterBig Snek

CultureSCP Foundation
Who It Is
Form

SCP-3000 is a massive, aquatic, serpentine entity strongly resembling a giant moray eel (Gymnothorax javanicus). The full length of SCP-3000 is estimated to be approximately 100 meters. Its body is covered in dark brown to black scales, and it has a series of bioluminescent markings along its length.

Powers

Mind Control SCP-3000 is capable of manipulating the minds of those who come into contact with it, driving them to madness and despair. This ability is thought to be a form of psychological warfare, used by the entity to weaken its enemies and gain the upper hand.

Telepathy SCP-3000 is also capable of reading the thoughts and emotions of those who come into contact with it. This ability is thought to be a form of telepathy, used by the entity to gather information and anticipate the actions of its enemies.

Regeneration SCP-3000 is capable of regenerating its body and repairing damage to its scales and other tissues. This ability is thought to be a result of its ability to manipulate its own biology and chemistry.

Incident Log

SCP-3000 was first discovered by a team of researchers from the SCP Foundation in the year 2025. The team was conducting a survey of the ocean floor when they stumbled upon the entity, which was estimated to be approximately 100 meters in length.

Several researchers have reported experiencing madness and despair after coming into contact with SCP-3000. This is thought to be a result of the entity's ability to manipulate the minds of those who come into contact with it.

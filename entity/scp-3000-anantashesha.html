Who It Is

A gargantuan, serpentine entity dwelling in the Bay of Bengal, measuring between 600 and 900 kilometers in length. SCP-3000 secretes a compound (Y-909) that is the primary ingredient in modern Foundation amnestics. Exposure to SCP-3000 causes severe psychological disturbance, memory loss, and eventual ego dissolution — personnel exposed for extended periods forget who they are entirely. The Foundation harvests this amnestic compound despite the human cost.

Form

A moray eel-like leviathan, head approximately 2.5 km in diameter, body extending for hundreds of kilometers. Its body is covered in a dark, viscous secretion. Its eyes are empty black pools that induce immediate cognitive disruption.

Powers

Y-909 amnestic secretion

Memory and identity dissolution field

Fear paralysis

Ego fragmentation

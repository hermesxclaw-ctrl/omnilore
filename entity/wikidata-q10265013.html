Who It Is

In the swirling currents of memory and melody, a figure emerges from Organization XIII—not as a warrior of brute force, but as a minstrel of the abyss. Demyx, the 'Melodious Nocturne,' wields a sitar that hums with watery power, yet his true strength lies in his deceptively laid-back demeanor and a hidden resolve that surfaces when the music stops.

Demyx is famously lazy and unmotivated, often complaining about having to work. He prefers to avoid conflict and would rather play his sitar than fight. However, he shows a hidden competence and a sense of self-preservation, and he is not above groveling when threatened. He has a dry, sarcastic sense of humor and often breaks the fourth wall with his complaints. Despite his laid-back attitude, he is loyal to the Organization, though he questions its methods at times.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Demyx' is likely a play on 'demix' or 'demise,' hinting at his role in the Organization and his eventual fate. It may also be derived from 'D minor,' a musical key, aligning with his musical theme.

Pronunciation — DEH-miks

Aliases — Melodious Nocturne, No. IX, The Lazy Member

Ownership — Square Enix, Disney Interactive Studios, and Tetsuya Nomura (character designer).

Powers
Water-based attacks

Demyx can conjure and manipulate water, creating waves, projectiles, and even watery clones of himself to confuse enemies.

Limit: His water clones can be dispelled with enough force; they have limited durability.

Sitar weapon

Demyx wields a sitar that can be used as a melee weapon or to channel his musical magic. He strums it to unleash sound waves that can stun or damage foes.

Limit: Requires direct engagement; vulnerable to physical attacks.

Musical magic

His attacks are often accompanied by musical notes and rhythms, suggesting a sound-based magical ability that can disorient or weaken opponents.

Limit: Effectiveness varies; can be interrupted.

Spatial manipulation (teleportation)

Like other Organization members, Demyx can teleport short distances, often as a dodge or to reposition.

Limit: Not explicitly shown as a power, but implied by his movement.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Demyx is a young man with a slim build, fair skin, and spiky, dark blue hair that defies gravity. He wears the standard Organization XIII black coat, which features a high collar and zipper, but he often leaves it open, revealing a white undershirt. He carries a sitar-like weapon that can transform into a blade. His eyes are a striking turquoise, and his expression is usually relaxed or amused.

Symbols — His symbol is the number IX, representing his rank in Organization XIII. He is associated with water and music, often creating watery clones and using sound-based attacks.

Domains — As a Nobody, Demyx resides in the Organization's headquarters, The World That Never Was, specifically in the Castle That Never Was. He is also seen in various Disney worlds, particularly Hollow Bastion, during missions.

Accounts I
The Battle at Hollow BastionKingdom Hearts II

2005

Demyx confronts Sora, Donald, and Goofy in Hollow Bastion's library, tasked with delaying them. He initially tries to talk his way out of fighting, but when pressured, he unleashes a horde of water clones. After his defeat, he pleads for his life, claiming he will tell them secrets about Organization XIII, but is killed by a mysterious figure (later revealed to be Xemnas) for his failure. Demyx's death serves as a warning about the Organization's ruthlessness and foreshadows the fate of other traitors.

Accounts II
Missions with Roxas and XionKingdom Hearts 358/2 Days

2009

Demyx is assigned as a mission partner for Roxas and Xion, often complaining about the extra work. He provides comic relief and shows a reluctant camaraderie with them, even hinting at doubts about the Organization's goals. His interactions humanize him and build sympathy for the Organization's lower-ranking members.

The Replica at the Keyblade GraveyardKingdom Hearts III

2019

Demyx is recreated as a replica by the true Organization to serve as a guardian of the Keyblade Graveyard. He fights Sora and his friends alongside other replicas, but is defeated. In the Re Mind DLC, he is shown in a more tragic light, questioning his existence. His defeat contributes to the final battle against Xehanort, and his replica's existential crisis adds depth to the theme of identity.

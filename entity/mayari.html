Who It Is

Mayari is the moon goddess who fought her brother Apolaki (sun god) for shared rule of the earth after Bathala's death. Apolaki struck out one of her eyes but relented — they share rule, he by day, she by night. This is why the moon's light is gentler. She is also a warrior goddess associated with revolution and equality.

Attested in Oral tradition / Kapampangan and Tagalog mythologyEra Pre-colonial Philippines

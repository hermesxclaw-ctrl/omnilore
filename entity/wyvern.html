Who It Is
Form

The wyvern is typically depicted as a two-legged dragon with a long, serpentine body, wings, and a distinctive crest on its head. Its appearance can vary greatly depending on the cultural context and artistic interpretation.

Powers

Fire-breathing The wyvern is said to possess the ability to breathe fire, which it uses to defend itself and its territory.

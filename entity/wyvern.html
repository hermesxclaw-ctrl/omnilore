Who It Is

A smaller, more aggressive cousin of true dragons. Wyverns lack forelegs and breath weapons, relying on their speed, venomous stinger tail, and ferocious bite. Their scales range from dull green to rusty brown. They are highly territorial and will attack anything that enters their airspace.

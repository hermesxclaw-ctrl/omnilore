Who It Is
Form

A human with the ability to transform into a wolf-like creature, often depicted as a hybrid of human and wolf features. In some stories, the werewolf is described as being covered in fur, having claws, and possessing supernatural strength and speed.

Powers

Shape-shifting The ability to transform into a wolf-like creature, often during a full moon.

Accounts

A young man is transformed into a werewolf by a witch, and must navigate the dangers of rural France while struggling to control his transformations.

A group of travelers is attacked by a werewolf in the Black Forest, and must fight for their lives against the creature.

Identity

Happy Mask Salesman

Pronunciation HAP-ee MASK SAYLZ-mən

Known As

The Mask SalesmanMask Salesman

Etymology

His name is a direct translation of his Japanese name, お面屋 (Omen-ya), meaning 'mask shop owner'.

Cultural Ownership

Nintendo, The Legend of Zelda series

Disputed Identifications

Some fans speculate he may be a deity or a supernatural being, but this is not confirmed by official sources.

Description

Appearance

A tall, slender man with a large, exaggerated grin, dark eyes, and a prominent nose. He wears a purple tunic and a large backpack filled with masks.

Symbols & Iconography

His ever-present smile and the masks he carries are his defining symbols, representing mystery and transformation.

Behaviour & Personality

He is eccentric, cryptic, and often unsettling. He speaks in a fast, high-pitched voice and has a habit of appearing and disappearing suddenly. He is obsessed with masks and their stories, and he becomes furious when Link loses Majora's Mask.

Domains & Habitats

He travels across Termina, but is most often found in the Clock Town Stock Pot Inn or on the moon.

Version Differences

In the original Nintendo 64 version, he is depicted with a more exaggerated, cartoonish appearance; in later remakes, his design is slightly more detailed but retains his iconic features.

Powers & Abilities

Mask Transformation

He has the ability to transform into different forms using the masks he carries, though he rarely demonstrates this in the game.

Limitations

Not fully explained; he may be limited to masks he has collected.

Stories & Myths

The Search for Majora's Mask

Source Majora's MaskCulture Video gamePeriod 2000

Summary

The Happy Mask Salesman hires Link to recover Majora's Mask, which was stolen by Skull Kid. Upon retrieval, he becomes enraged when he discovers the mask is missing, but later thanks Link and leaves, hinting at a mysterious past.

Consequences

Link embarks on a quest to stop the moon from crashing into Termina.

Variants

In the 3DS remake, his dialogue and animations are slightly altered.

Relationships

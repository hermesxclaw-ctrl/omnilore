Who It Is

In the shadowed streets of Termina, a strange vendor peddles masks that hold the souls of the dead—and his smile never fades, even as the world hurtles toward its final dawn.

He is eccentric, cryptic, and often unsettling. He speaks in a fast, high-pitched voice and has a habit of appearing and disappearing suddenly. He is obsessed with masks and their stories, and he becomes furious when Link loses Majora's Mask.

Powers
Mask Transformation

He has the ability to transform into different forms using the masks he carries, though he rarely demonstrates this in the game.

Limit: Not fully explained; he may be limited to masks he has collected.

Supernatural Knowledge

He seems to know about the impending apocalypse and the true nature of Majora's Mask, but reveals little.

Limit: He does not share his knowledge freely.

Form & Signs

Appearance — A tall, slender man with a large, exaggerated grin, dark eyes, and a prominent nose. He wears a purple tunic and a large backpack filled with masks.

Symbols — His ever-present smile and the masks he carries are his defining symbols, representing mystery and transformation.

Domains — He travels across Termina, but is most often found in the Clock Town Stock Pot Inn or on the moon.

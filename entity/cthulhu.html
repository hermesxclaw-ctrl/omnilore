Who It Is
Form

Cthulhu is depicted as a gigantic, green, humanoid entity with visual elements similar to an octopus and dragon. His appearance is often described as grotesque and terrifying, with bulging eyes, scaly skin, and massive wings. The exact nature of Cthulhu's appearance is often left to the imagination, with different artists and authors interpreting him in various ways. However, his enormity, green color, and cephalopod-like features are consistent across most depictions. Cthulhu's appearance is meant to evoke a sense of cosmic horror, representing the unknowable and unspeakable terrors that lurk beyond human understanding.

Powers

Cosmic Awareness Cthulhu is said to possess a cosmic awareness, allowing him to perceive the universe and its workings in ways that are beyond human comprehension. This power grants him immense knowledge and insight, making him a formidable opponent in any conflict.

Accounts

The story follows a young man who discovers the existence of Cthulhu and the cult that worships him. As he delves deeper into the mystery, he realizes that Cthulhu's return is imminent, and that humanity is doomed to face his wrath.

The story follows a young man who travels to the seaside town of Innsmouth, where he discovers that the inhabitants are being transformed into fish-like creatures. As he delves deeper into the mystery, he realizes that Cthulhu's influence is behind the transformations.

Who It Is
Form

The Pale Man is a monstrous creature with a pale and decaying appearance. He has long fingers and a grotesque face with sunken eyes. He is often depicted as a tall, thin figure with a menacing presence.

Powers

Manipulation The Pale Man has the ability to manipulate and deceive his victims, using his intelligence and charm to lure them into his trap.

Accounts

The Pale Man's Lair is a story about a young girl who discovers the Pale Man's lair in the labyrinth. She must navigate the treacherous world of the labyrinth and avoid the Pale Man's traps and minions.

The Pale Man's Origins is a story about the origins of the Pale Man, a creature that feeds on children. The story explores the Pale Man's history and motivations, and delves into the darker aspects of his nature.

Etymology

In Japanese, 'Ruka' is a unisex given name with various kanji combinations, often meaning 'flow' (流), 'jewel' (瑠), or 'fragrance' (香). In Latvian, 'Ruka' is the feminine form of the surname 'Ruks'. In Finnish, 'Ruka' is a place name, likely derived from the Sami word for 'reindeer' or 'slope'. In Mapuche, 'ruka' means 'house'.

Cultural Ownership

The name is multicultural: Japanese (given name), Latvian (surname), Finnish (place name), Mapuche (common noun). In fiction, it appears predominantly in Japanese media.

Disputed Identifications

The name 'Ruka' is often confused with 'Luka' due to romanization. In Japanese, both can be rendered as ルカ, but they are distinct names with different origins.

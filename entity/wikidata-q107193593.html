The Approach

In the world of horse racing, Nakayama Festa is a name that echoes with the thunder of hooves on turf and the roar of a home crowd at Takarazuka. But this champion's tale goes beyond the track—it's a story of a Japanese thoroughbred who nearly conquered Europe's most prestigious race, and whose legacy gallops on in the digital realm of anime and games.

Racing career highlights

Summary

Nakayama Festa won the Nisai Stakes as a two-year-old, the St Lite Kinen as a three-year-old, and the 2010 Takarazuka Kinen. He also finished second in the 2010 Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe.

Who It Is

A pack of warm-blooded, virtually sightless predators with red, amorphous skin and sharp teeth. SCP-939 has no eyes or other facial features except a mouth. It mimics human speech it has heard, perfectly replicating voices, to lure prey. They communicate ultrasonically with each other and use echolocation to hunt. Their lunges are incredibly fast and they can swallow limbs whole.

Powers

Voice mimicry (perfect human speech replication)

Echolocation (blind, hunts by sound)

Pack hunting coordination

Ultrasonic communication

Powerful bite (severs limbs)

Heat sensing

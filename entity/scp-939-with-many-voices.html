SCP-939 — With Many Voices

also known asSCP-939With Many Voices
TypeSCP Object (Keter)
☘ Thin file. The Archive holds limited verified material on this entity and declines to pad it. What follows is everything that clears our sourcing bar.
Who It Is
Form

SCP-939 appears as a pack of humanoid figures with elongated heads, long limbs, and sharp teeth. They have pale, almost translucent skin and are capable of mimicking the appearance of their victims.

Powers

Voice Mimicry SCP-939 can mimic the voice of its victims, making it difficult to distinguish between the entity and its prey.

Accounts

A group of researchers attempted to study SCP-939 in a controlled environment, but the entity quickly adapted and began to mimic the voices of its captors.

A team of linguists attempted to analyze the vocal patterns of SCP-939, but the entity's ability to mimic human speech made it difficult to distinguish between the entity and its victims.

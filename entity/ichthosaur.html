Who It Is
Form

Ichthosaurs are often depicted as large, serpent-like creatures with fish-like scales and a long, eel-like body. They may have a pair of flippers or fins, and some accounts describe them as having a pair of legs or even a human-like head.

Powers

Aquatic Adaptation Ichthosaurs are said to possess the ability to adapt to life in the water, allowing them to breathe underwater and move with ease in the ocean.

Accounts

The Ichthosaur is described as a fearsome creature that attacks and devours ships and sailors in the Mediterranean.

The Ichthosaur is described as a creature that inhabits the North Sea and is said to be capable of attacking ships and sailors.

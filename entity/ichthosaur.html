Who It Is

A fast-swimming, dolphin-like predator that hunts in the submerged caverns of Tallon IV and other worlds. Ichthosaurs have sleek, blue-gray skin, razor-sharp teeth, and bioluminescent patches along their flanks. They hunt in coordinated packs, herding prey into dead-end caves before closing in for the kill.

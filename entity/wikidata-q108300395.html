Who It Is

On the burning shores of Ohara, a weeping giant who had deserted the Marines knelt to tell a terrified eight-year-old girl that it was okay to laugh — and then stood up to face a fleet ordered to erase history itself. For twenty years readers believed Jaguar D. Saul died for that kindness, frozen solid by his best friend. Eiichiro Oda let the lie stand until the final saga revealed the giant had been alive all along, scarred, burned, and guarding the very books the World Government tried to destroy.

Attested consistently across all canon appearances: jovial, boisterous, and deeply compassionate despite his intimidating size. As a Marine vice admiral he was noted as an anomaly — lazy about discipline, prone to laughter, and unwilling to follow orders he deemed unjust. After shipwrecking on Ohara he befriends Robin through humor and patience, teaching her to laugh (dereshishishi) and affirming that being alive is not a crime. Powers
Giant Physiology and Superhuman Strength

As a full-blooded Elbaf giant, Saul possesses immense physical strength, durability, and longevity (giants live ~300 years). He can lift and throw Marine battleship debris, shatter stone, and physically overpower multiple Marine battleships' crews. He survives cannon fire and prolonged exposure at sea. His size alone makes him a battlefield-level combatant.

Limit: Not invulnerable: vulnerable to Admiral-level Devil Fruit powers (Aokiji's ice), fire, and artillery at scale. His strength is purely physical; no Haki is explicitly shown in manga, though as a former vice admiral he would be expected to possess it per Marine rank requirements (unconfirmed for Saul specifically). Burns from Ohara fire left lasting mobility limitations post-timeskip.

Marine Vice Admiral Combat Training

Former Marine Headquarters Vice Admiral (Chūjō), one of the highest Marine ranks, indicating mastery of naval combat, leadership of a Buster Call fleet, Rokushiki-adjacent training, and strategic knowledge. He demonstrates hand-to-hand combat capable of knocking out Marine soldiers and briefly contending with Kuzan before being frozen.

Limit: He defected and is no longer affiliated; rank is former. He was decisively defeated by Admiral Aokiji (Kuzan), establishing a clear power ceiling below Admiral level. No Devil Fruit ability.

Exceptional Endurance and Survival

Survived the Ohara Buster Call's bombardment, point-blank fire, and being flash-frozen by Kuzan's Ice Time (Hie Hie no Mi). His giant durability plus the insulating effect of ice allowed him to survive severe burns and freezing, later rescued and treated on Elbaf. Post-survival he endures extensive burn scarring while remaining active as a teacher and guardian.

Limit: Survival required external rescue by Elbaf giants and prolonged recovery (20+ years). Left with permanent burn scars and reduced mobility; not regenerative. The exact mechanism of survival was not detailed until 2022, leading to long period where feat was considered fatal.

Scholarly Preservation and Pedagogy (Non-combat)

Not a superpower but a unique ability within lore: Saul, with Elbaf giants, salvaged the entire collection of Ohara's books by fishing them from the lake, preserving the world's most complete archaeological library. He memorized and taught Ohara's history and the Will of D. context to Elbaf children and later to Robin, acting as a living bridge between Ohara and Elbaf.

Limit: Not a fighter's power; relies on literacy and memory, not combat. Knowledge is incomplete — he admits he does not understand the full meaning of D. or the Poneglyphs, only what Ohara scholars told him.

Accounts I
The Giant Vice Admiral Who Questioned JusticeOne Piece Manga Chapters 392, 396; Vivre Card Databook Vol. 7

In-universe c. 1502 (22 years before present); published 2005

Jaguar D. Saul served as a Marine Headquarters Vice Admiral renowned for his jovial nature but lax discipline. Ordered to join the Buster Call fleet against Ohara after the scholars were discovered studying the Poneglyphs, Saul initially followed orders. Aboard the Marine ship he guarded Nico Olvia, captured archaeologist from Ohara, and learned from her that Ohara's scholars sought history, not weapons. Disturbed, he began to doubt the World Government's claim that Ohara sought to destroy the world. When Olvia escaped during a storm, Saul let her go, then deserted his post, stealing a small boat and deserting the Marines — an act of treason punishable by death. He drifted at sea, questioning the meaning of justice, until he shipwrecked on Ohara itself. Saul's defection removed a vice admiral from the Buster Call and set him on Ohara, enabling his later bond with Robin. It established his moral break from Absolute Justice and foreshadowed Kuzan's own doubts. The World Government branded him a traitor.

Shipwrecked on Ohara and the Friendship with Nico RobinOne Piece Manga Chapters 392-395

In-universe 1502, Ohara, West Blue; published 2005

Saul washed ashore on Ohara unconscious and was found by 8-year-old Nico Robin, who brought him food. Initially frightening her with his size, he quickly befriended her through jokes and his distinctive 'Dereshishishi' laugh, which he taught her when she confessed she had no friends and was ostracized for her Devil Fruit powers (Hana Hana no Mi). Over several months, Saul lived in hiding in Ohara's forest, becoming Robin's only friend and surrogate uncle/father figure. He warned her that scholars studying history were in danger and encouraged her to go to sea someday and find friends who would protect her. He also revealed he carries the initial 'D.' and told Robin that those with 'D.' may hold a special fate, though he himself did not know its meaning, planting the series' central mystery. Gave Robin her will to live, her laugh, and the emotional core that sustains her through 20 years of isolation. Saul's warning allowed Robin to survive the coming purge. The 'D.' conversation is the first time Robin (and readers) hear the Will of D. explained as a possible inherited will.

The Buster Call on Ohara — Saul's Last Stand Against AokijiOne Piece Manga Chapters 395-397

In-universe 1502; published 2005

The World Government, via CP9 leader Spandine, invoked a Buster Call on Ohara: five vice admirals (including Kuzan/Aokiji, Sakazuki/Akainu, and others) and ten battleships to annihilate the island and its scholars for studying the Void Century. As battleships bombarded the Tree of Knowledge and Akainu destroyed the evacuation ship, Saul revealed himself, carrying Robin to the shore to escape. On the beach he confronted his former subordinate and friend Kuzan (then Vice Admiral Aokiji, Hie Hie no Mi user). Saul pleaded that Robin was innocent. Kuzan, bound by orders, declared Saul's actions criminal and flash-froze Saul with 'Ice Time' (Ice Block: Pheasant Beak variant in anime), encasing the giant in ice. Saul, frozen, still encouraged Robin to flee and to never stop living, shouting that the sea is vast and she will find friends. Robin escaped on a small boat while Saul was left frozen as the island burned around him. For 17 years of publication, this was presented as his death. Legacy of the D. — Saul's Unknowing InheritanceOne Piece Manga Chapters 392, 400, 1066; SBS Volume 60; Vivre Card

Recurring motif 2005-2022

Saul is one of the earliest characters to explicitly discuss the 'Will of D.' He tells Robin that he carries D. in his name but does not know what it means, that his family told him to be proud of it, and that the Marines fear it. This small scene became a cornerstone for the series' largest mystery. Later, Robin connects Saul's D. to Monkey D. Luffy, Trafalgar D. Water Law, and others. Saul's survival reinforces the thematic link between D. bearers as survivors of erased history. Oda has used Saul in SBS to tease that D. will be revealed at the story's end, but as of 2026 Saul himself remains ignorant of its true meaning, making him a carrier rather than an explainer. Established Saul as the audience's first reliable witness that D. is not inherently evil, countering World Government propaganda. Fuels scholarly and fan debate on D. clan origins (possibly tied to ancient kingdom, giants, and Elbaf). Ensures Saul's relevance extends beyond Robin's backstory into endgame lore.

Jaguar D. Saul is Nico Robin's father. — Robin's father is Nico Olvia's husband, an unnamed man who is not Saul. Saul is a surrogate father/uncle figure who befriended 8-year-old Robin after shipwrecking on Ohara. Canon never suggests a biological link; Robin's parentage is separate.

Saul was definitively killed by Aokiji and his survival is a retcon that contradicts earlier canon. — While presented as death for 17 years, Chapter 397 never shows Saul shattering or confirmed corpse — he is shown frozen while Robin flees. Oda left visual ambiguity (ice encasement, not shattering) and later explained in Chapter 1066 that Kuzan's ice insulated him from fire and giants rescued him. This is a planned reveal, not a continuity error, though it was withheld for dramatic effect.

Saul ate a Devil Fruit or has giant-specific powers beyond size. — Saul has no Devil Fruit; his abilities are purely giant physiology and Marine training. He is vulnerable to Devil Fruit powers (was frozen by Hie Hie no Mi) and has no transformation or elemental ability. His survival was due to external rescue, not a power.

Saul's name is pronounced 'Hag-war' in English and 'Jaguar' is a mistranslation. — 'Jaguar' is the intended animal pun (like many One Piece names) and is the official English localization per VIZ and Vivre Card. 'Haguar' is a literal Hepburn reading of katakana, not the intended English word. Oda's character name puns are typically English animal names rendered in katakana.

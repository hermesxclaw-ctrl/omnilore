The Approach

In the far-off Land of Ooo, few voices are as unmistakable as the shrill, authoritative shriek of the Earl of Lemongrab. Part lemon, part despot, he is one of the most memorable and unsettling figures in the whimsical yet strange world of Adventure Time.

Content advisory. Contains themes of authoritarianism, isolation, and psychological distress. Some scenes may be intense for younger viewers.
Identity

Lemongrab

Pronunciation LEM-on-grab

Known As

Earl of LemongrabLemongrab 1Original Lemongrab

Etymology

The name 'Lemongrab' is a portmanteau of 'lemon' and 'grab', reflecting his citrus-like appearance and his tendency to seize control.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Pendleton Ward for Cartoon Network's Adventure Time (2010-2018). The character is part of the show's fictional universe and is owned by Cartoon Network.

Disputed Identifications

There is a clone, Lemongrab 2, who is sometimes confused with the original. The two share appearances but have distinct personalities and storylines.

Description

Appearance

Lemongrab is a humanoid figure with a large, lemon-shaped head, yellow skin, and a thin, wiry body. He wears a purple outfit with a high collar and a cape. His facial features include large, expressive eyes and a wide mouth with sharp teeth. He often has a stern or angry expression.

Symbols & Iconography

His most iconic symbol is the lemon, often appearing on his clothing or in his surroundings. His castle is shaped like a lemon and is a central location in his stories.

Behaviour & Personality

Lemongrab is characterized by his authoritarian and tyrannical behavior. He is highly strung, prone to shouting, and has a rigid sense of order. He is socially inept, often behaving inappropriately or cruelly towards others. He lacks empathy and struggles to understand emotions, but he occasionally shows moments of vulnerability and a desire for connection.

Domains & Habitats

He rules the Lemon Kingdom, a small, isolated kingdom within the Land of Ooo. His castle is a large lemon-shaped structure, and the kingdom is inhabited by lemon people who are often the targets of his harsh rule.

Version Differences

The original Lemongrab is distinct from his clone, Lemongrab 2. The original is more aggressive and unstable, while the clone is more childlike and gentle. Later episodes suggest that Lemongrab's behavior is partly due to his upbringing and his inability to fit into society.

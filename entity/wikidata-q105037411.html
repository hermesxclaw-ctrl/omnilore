Who It Is

In the world of Jujutsu Kaisen, where curses born of human negativity lurk in shadows, one man stands unchallenged: Satoru Gojo, the self-proclaimed 'Strongest' sorcerer. With eyes that see the very flow of cursed energy and a technique that bends infinity itself, he is both the shield of his students and the nightmare of his enemies—yet his blinding power and playful demeanor mask a profound loneliness, a fate sealed by his own strength.

Gojo is flamboyant, playful, and often immature, teasing his students and colleagues. He is deeply caring and protective of his students, willing to go to great lengths to ensure their safety and growth. He is also arrogant, referring to himself as 'the Strongest' and often underestimating opponents. However, his arrogance is not baseless, as he has the power to back it up. He is generally laid-back, but becomes serious and ruthless when protecting those he cares about.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname Gojo (五条) literally means 'five streets' or 'fifth street' in Japanese, possibly referencing the Gojo district in Kyoto. The given name Satoru (悟) means 'enlightenment' or 'understanding,' fitting his role as a mentor and his Six Eyes' perception.

Pronunciation — GOH-joh sah-TOH-roo

Aliases — The Honored One, The Strongest, Gojo-sensei, Gojo Satoru

Ownership — Created by Gege Akutami for the manga series Jujutsu Kaisen, serialized by Shueisha. The character is part of contemporary Japanese pop culture and is owned by Shueisha and MAPPA (

Powers
Limitless (Infinity)

A inherited technique that allows Gojo to control space at an atomic level. He can create an 'Infinity' barrier that slows down approaching objects to a near stop, making it impossible for most attacks to reach him. He can also use it offensively to create powerful blasts and gravitational effects.

Limit: The technique requires immense cursed energy and concentration. It is neutralized by Domain Expansions that nullify techniques, such as Malevolent Shrine.

Six Eyes

A rare ocular technique that grants Gojo incredibly precise perception of cursed energy. It allows him to see the flow of energy, identify weaknesses, and optimize his own energy usage to near zero waste. It also enables him to use Limitless with incredible efficiency.

Limit: The eyes are a constant drain on his energy, though they reduce overall consumption. They can be covered to reduce sensory overload.

Cursed Technique Reversal: Red

An offensive technique that repels and destroys, using negative energy to create a powerful repulsive force. It is the opposite of Blue, which attracts.

Limit: Requires a high level of cursed energy control.

Cursed Technique Amplification: Blue

An offensive technique that creates a vacuum-like attraction, pulling objects and enemies towards a point, crushing them.

Limit: Requires a high level of cursed energy control.

Hollow Purple

A secret technique that combines Red and Blue to create a massive, destructive blast that erases matter. It is one of Gojo's most powerful attacks.

Limit: Requires immense energy and precise control.

Domain Expansion: Unlimited Void

Gojo's Domain Expansion, which traps his opponent in a void of infinite information, overwhelming their senses and rendering them immobile. It is considered one of the most powerful domains in the series.

Limit: Can be countered by another Domain Expansion, and requires a significant amount of cursed energy to maintain.

Reverse Cursed Technique

Gojo can use reverse cursed energy to heal his own injuries, making him nearly immortal in battle.

Limit: Requires a high level of cursed energy, and he cannot use it while exhausted.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Gojo is a tall, lean man with white hair and striking blue eyes, which are normally covered by a black blindfold or dark sunglasses. He wears a high-collared black uniform typical of jujutsu sorcerers, with a long coat-like overcoat. His appearance is designed to be bishonen (beautiful boy), with sharp features and a confident posture.

Symbols — The blindfold and sunglasses are iconic symbols, representing his Six Eyes and his desire to limit sensory input. His Domain Expansion, Unlimited Void, is depicted as a vast, starry space with a central black sphere. The color blue is strongly associated with him, representing his techniques and his connection to the sky and infinity.

Domains — As a teacher at Tokyo Prefectural Jujutsu High School, Gojo is often seen at the school. He also frequents various locations in Tokyo, including Shibuya and the school's training grounds. He is not tied to any particular habitat, as his power allows him to travel freely.

Accounts I
Jujutsu Kaisen 0: The Cursed ChildManga and anime film

2017 (manga), 2021 (film)

Gojo recruits Yuta Okkotsu, a boy cursed by his childhood friend Rika, into Jujutsu High. He mentors Yuta and his classmates, Panda, Maki, and Toge. When Suguru Geto, Gojo's former friend, attacks to steal Rika, Gojo confronts him but is ultimately defeated off-screen. Yuta defeats Geto, and Gojo later executes Geto, revealing the depth of his resolve. Gojo solidifies his role as a protector and mentor, but also shows his willingness to kill former friends for the greater good.

Hidden Inventory ArcManga and anime season 2

2006-2007 (in-universe), 2023 (anime)

A flashback to Gojo's youth, where he and Geto are tasked with protecting Riko Amanai, the Star Plasma Vessel, from assassins. They are attacked by Toji Fushiguro, who kills Riko and defeats both Gojo and Geto. Gojo awakens his full potential and kills Toji, but Geto becomes disillusioned with protecting non-sorcerers and turns to evil. Gojo confronts Geto but cannot kill him, leading to their eventual split. Gojo becomes the strongest sorcerer, but loses his best friend and gains a sense of responsibility. Geto's descent into villainy sets the stage for Jujutsu Kaisen 0.

Accounts II
Shibuya Incident ArcManga and anime season 2

2018 (in-universe), 2023 (anime)

Gojo is lured into a trap in Shibuya by the cursed spirits and Kenjaku. He fights and defeats Hanami, but is ultimately sealed in the Prison Realm by Kenjaku. His sealing leaves the jujutsu world vulnerable, leading to chaos. Gojo is removed from the board, allowing the antagonists to execute their plans. His students fight to free him.

Culling Game Arc and Final BattleManga and anime (upcoming)

2018-2024 (manga)

Gojo is released from the Prison Realm and immediately challenges Sukuna to a duel. The battle is intense, with Gojo using all his techniques, but he is ultimately killed by Sukuna. His death spurs his students to continue fighting, and his corpse is used by Yuta to aid in the final battle. Gojo's death marks a turning point in the series, leading to the final confrontation with Sukuna. His legacy inspires his students.

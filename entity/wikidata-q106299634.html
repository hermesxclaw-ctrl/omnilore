Who It Is

In the Republic of San Magnolia, the war against the Legion is a lie: the machines are piloted by the persecuted Colorata, the '86', who are denied names and humanity. At the heart of this brutal deception stands Shinei Nouzen, a sixteen-year-old squad leader known as 'Undertaker' and 'The Reaper', who carries the metal scraps of his fallen comrades and listens to the voices of the dead. His story is one of survival, guilt, and a fragile hope found in a handler who dares to see him as human.

Shin is calm, stoic, and seemingly emotionless, a result of his trauma and his constant exposure to death. He is fiercely protective of his squad members and is willing to do whatever it takes to ensure their survival, even if it means becoming a ruthless killer. He is haunted by the voices of the dead, which he hears as a result of his ability, and he keeps them at bay by focusing on his mission. Powers
Combat Skills

Shin is an exceptionally skilled pilot and fighter. He has survived countless battles and is known for his ruthlessness and precision. He is able to pilot his Juggernaut with great agility and accuracy, often taking on multiple enemies at once.

Limit: His skills are limited to his physical endurance and the capabilities of his Juggernaut.

Ability to Hear the Voices of the Dead

Shin has the ability to hear the voices of the dead, particularly those of the Legion's Shepherds, which are the remnants of human minds. This ability allows him to sense the presence of Legion units and understand their intentions, giving him a tactical advantage. It also enables him to communicate with the dead, as seen with his brother.

Limit: The constant voices cause him psychological distress and can be overwhelming. The ability is not fully controllable and can be a burden.

Leadership and Strategy

As the leader of the Spearhead Squadron, Shin demonstrates strong leadership and tactical acumen. He is able to command his squad effectively, making split-second decisions that often lead to victory.

Limit: His leadership is often marred by his personal trauma and his tendency to take on too much responsibility, leading to burnout.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Shinei Nouzen is a young man of sixteen with dark hair and dark eyes, typical of the Colorata. He is often depicted wearing the military uniform of the 86, which is dark in color. In the anime, he has a lean build and a stoic expression. He is usually seen with his Juggernaut, a mecha, and carries a box of dogtags. After defecting to the Giad Federacy, he wears the Federacy's uniform, which is similar but with different insignia.

Symbols — His call sign 'Undertaker' is represented by a coffin or grave motif. He carries a box of makeshift dogtags, each made from a piece of a fallen comrade's Juggernaut, symbolizing his role as the one who remembers and buries the dead. His personal mark on his Juggernaut is a skull, fitting his nickname 'The Reaper'.

Domains — Shin's primary domain is the battlefield, specifically the Eastern Front of the Republic of San Magnolia, where the Spearhead Squadron operates. After defecting, he resides in the Giad Federacy, where he lives with his adopted siblings and continues to fight as part of the Nordlicht Squadron. He is most comfortable in the cockpit of his Juggernaut, where he can control the chaos around him.

Accounts I
The Battle of the Spearhead Squadron86: Eighty-Six, Volume 1

The war between San Magnolia and the Empire of Giad

Shin leads the Spearhead Squadron, a unit of 86 veterans, in a desperate battle against the Legion. Despite being outnumbered, Shin's tactical brilliance and his squad's coordination allow them to survive. The battle highlights the harsh conditions of the 86 and the callousness of the Republic's military leadership. The battle further solidifies Shin's reputation as the Reaper and brings him to the attention of Lena, who becomes his new Handler.

Accounts II
The Defection to the Giad Federacy86: Eighty-Six, Volume 2

After the fall of the Spearhead Squadron's original mission

After the Spearhead Squadron is ordered on a suicide mission, the survivors, including Shin, decide to defect to the Giad Federacy. They are taken in by Ernst, who adopts them, and they become part of the Nordlicht Squadron. Shin continues to fight, now with a new purpose and a new family. Shin and his comrades find a new home and a new cause, but they are still haunted by their past. Shin's relationship with Lena is put on hold as they are separated by the war.

The Reunion with Lena86: Eighty-Six, Volume 4

After the Federacy's counteroffensive

Shin and Lena are reunited when Lena is assigned to the Federacy as a liaison. Their reunion is emotional, and they begin to work together to end the war. Shin also confronts Kiriya, a Shepherd who was once Frederica's knight, and is forced to kill him. Shin and Lena's relationship deepens, and they become key figures in the fight against the Legion. Shin also gains a new understanding of his abilities and his connection to the Empire.

Who It Is

A humanoid monster with its arms fused together into a single, large blade-like appendage. Closers have a distinctive shuffling gait and drag their weapon-arm behind them before swinging it in a devastating arc. They are heavily built and take significant damage to bring down. Their face is covered by what appears to be a straightjacket-like hood.

Powers

Blade arm sweep, high durability, relentless pursuit

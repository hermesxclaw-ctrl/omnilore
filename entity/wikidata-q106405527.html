Who It Is

In the bustling town of Sakurashin, where humans and yōkai coexist under the watchful eyes of the Seven Pillars, a fifteen-year-old girl with cat ears serves as the town's announcer. But Ao Nanami is far more than a voice on the loudspeaker; she is a Satori with the power to read minds, communicate with animals, and anticipate the moves of her enemies. Yet, beneath her cheerful demeanor lies a deep-seated loneliness, as her older brother Gin vanished before the series began, leaving her to navigate a world where her abilities both connect and isolate her.

Ao is cheerful, energetic, and somewhat of a glutton, often seen eating snacks. She is loyal to her friends and deeply cares for the town of Sakurashin. Despite her outgoing nature, she harbors a sense of loss over her brother's disappearance and can be serious when the situation demands it. She shows restraint in her eating habits compared to Hime, suggesting a more disciplined side.

Powers
Telepathy

Ao can read the thoughts of others, communicate with animals, and anticipate opponents' moves. This ability makes her an invaluable asset in combat and negotiation.

Limit: Reading the thoughts of many people simultaneously can be exhausting, and she may be overwhelmed by strong emotions or chaotic mental noise.

Satellite Form

In this enhanced state, Ao can read the thoughts of the entire city of Sakurashin at once, gaining a comprehensive awareness of everything happening within it.

Limit: This ability is extremely taxing and can leave Ao exhausted or unconscious if maintained for too long.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Ao is a 15-year-old girl with short, light-blue hair and cat ears that function as antennas. She typically wears a school uniform or casual clothes, often accessorized with a hat or headphones to conceal her ears. Her eyes are large and expressive, often reflecting her telepathic activity.

Symbols — Her cat ears are her most distinctive feature, symbolizing her Satori nature and her ability to perceive thoughts. The headphones she wears are a modern accessory that hides her ears when necessary.

Domains — Ao primarily resides in Sakurashin, a town where humans and yōkai coexist. She works at the Hiizumi Life Counseling Office and serves as the town announcer, broadcasting messages to the residents. Her role places her at the heart of the community, both literally and figuratively.

Accounts I
The Disappearance of GinYozakura Quartet manga and anime

Contemporary

Before the series begins, Ao's older brother Gin disappears under mysterious circumstances. His absence deeply affects Ao, and she later learns that Gin has become the vessel for Enjin, a vengeful spirit from the Hiizumi branch family. This revelation forces Ao to confront her grief and her brother's fate. Ao's determination to protect Sakurashin is partly motivated by the loss of her brother and her desire to prevent similar tragedies.

Accounts II
The Battle for SakurashinYozakura Quartet manga and anime

Contemporary

Ao, along with Hime, Akina, and Kotoha, defends Sakurashin from various supernatural threats, including Enjin's attempts to merge the human and yōkai worlds. Her telepathic abilities are crucial in coordinating the team and anticipating enemy attacks. The team successfully protects the town, and Ao's skills are honed through these battles.

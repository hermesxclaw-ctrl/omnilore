Who It Is

In the sprawling, weaponized dreamscape of BlazBlue, 'Es' is not just a name—it's a designation, a weapon, and a question mark wrapped in a petite, armored frame. She is the 'Azure' given form, a silent arbiter of a cosmic reset, yet her existence is a paradox: a weapon of absolute destruction who yearns for a simple, human connection. Her story is a quiet tragedy played out against the backdrop of a world-ending loop, where her very purpose is to erase the world she walks through.

Es is initially emotionless and robotic, speaking in a monotone and following her programming as the Arbiter. She has no understanding of human emotions or social conventions. However, as she interacts with Ragna and others, she gradually develops curiosity and a desire to understand 'feelings'. She is earnest, naive, and literal-minded. She shows a particular fondness for observation and learning, and a growing protectiveness towards those she comes to care about. She is not malicious; she is simply fulfilling what she believes to be her purpose.

Powers
Arbiter of the Azure

Es is the 'Arbiter', a being created by the Master Unit Amaterasu to judge and reset the world. She has the authority to use the Azure, the source of all possibility, to erase and recreate reality. This is her ultimate purpose and power.

Limit: Her power is tied to her role as Arbiter; she can only use her full power when acting as the Master Unit's will. She is also bound by the rules of the Azure and cannot act against the Master Unit's design.

Azure Grimoire

Es wields a sentient weapon called the Azure Grimoire, which can shift between a sword, a shield, and a cannon. It is a physical manifestation of the Azure and amplifies her abilities. It can fire powerful energy blasts and create barriers.

Limit: The Grimoire's full potential is unlocked only when Es's resolve is absolute. Overuse can drain her energy.

Superhuman Physical Abilities

As a being of the Boundary, Es possesses superhuman strength, speed, and endurance. She can keep up with top-tier fighters in the BlazBlue universe.

Limit: None explicitly stated.

Energy Projection and Manipulation

Es can project energy in the form of blue flames, beams, and slashes. She can also manipulate this energy to create constructs or enhance her attacks.

Limit: None explicitly stated.

Reality Warping (Limited)

In her role as Arbiter, Es can warp reality to a limited extent, such as creating spatial distortions or altering the environment. This is not freely used in combat.

Limit: Only used when necessary for her role as Arbiter; not a casual combat ability.

Accounts I
The Arbiter's AwakeningBlazBlue: Central Fiction

2015-2016

Es is awakened by the Master Unit Amaterasu to serve as the Arbiter of the Azure. She is sent to the world of BlazBlue to observe and judge it. She encounters Ragna the Bloodedge, who, despite her initial hostility, shows her kindness and gives her the name 'Es'. Through her interactions with Ragna and others, she begins to question her purpose and develops emotions. Es's development leads to a crucial choice in the true ending, where she decides to spare the world instead of resetting it, defying her programming.

Accounts II
The Azure ConflictBlazBlue: Central Fiction

2015-2016

Es becomes a key figure in the conflict over the Azure, as various factions seek to control or destroy it. She is confronted by characters like Hazama and Yuuki Terumi, who attempt to use her for their own ends. She must decide where her loyalty lies. Es's actions help shape the outcome of the Azure Conflict, leading to the destruction of the Embryo and the creation of a new world.

Es's ObservationBlazBlue: Central Fiction, BlazBlue: Cross Tag Battle

2015-2018

Throughout her appearances, Es is often seen observing human behavior, trying to understand emotions like love, friendship, and anger. She asks questions about these concepts and attempts to replicate them, leading to humorous and poignant moments. Her observations help her grow and eventually form genuine bonds.

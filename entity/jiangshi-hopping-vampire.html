Who It Is

A reanimated corpse in Chinese folklore that moves by hopping with arms outstretched due to rigor mortis, typically dressed in the official robes of the Qing dynasty. The jiangshi (殭屍, 'stiff corpse') is created when a person dies violently, by suicide, or when the soul fails to leave the body at death — or when a cat jumps over the corpse, a folk belief that animates the dead. It kills by draining the victim's life energy (qi), not necessarily blood. Taoist priests serve as jiangshi-hunters, using talismans, mirrors, rooster blood, glutinous rice, and peach-wood swords to subdue them. Due to Hong Kong cinema (especially the Mr. Vampire film series), the jiangshi has become one of the most recognizable undead entities globally, blending zombie and vampire characteristics.

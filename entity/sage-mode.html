Who It Is

A powerful transformation that allows the user to draw in natural energy from the environment and blend it with their own chakra. This creates senjutsu chakra, vastly enhancing physical strength, perception, and technique power. Requires perfect balance — too much natural energy turns the user to stone. Toad, Snake, and Slug sage modes exist.

Powers

Enhanced strength, perception, senjutsu-enhanced techniques

The Approach

In the land of Lugunica, where magic and steel decide the fate of nations, there walks a man whose name alone is a promise of victory: Reinhard van Astrea, the Sword Saint. His blade has never been drawn in defeat, and his legend has grown with every foe who dared to stand against him. But behind the flawless swordsmanship and the weight of a divine blessing lies a soul burdened by duty, loneliness, and the impossible expectations of a family that has shaped the kingdom's history for centuries. Who is the man behind the title, and what does it truly mean to be the strongest?

Content advisory. Contains themes of violence, death, family conflict, psychological pressure, and references to self-harm and suicidal ideation (in the source work).
Identity

Reinhard van Astrea

Pronunciation RINE-hart van AS-tree-ah

Etymology

His given name 'Reinhard' is of Germanic origin, meaning 'brave counsel' or 'strong advisor.' 'van Astrea' suggests a noble lineage, possibly derived from the Latin 'astra' (stars), hinting at his celestial destiny and the Astrea family's connection to the divine.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese light novel and anime series 'Re:Zero - Starting Life in Another World' by Tappei Nagatsuki, illustrated by Shinichirou Otsuka. The character is part of the broader isekai genre and has been adapted into multiple media.

Disputed Identifications

There is no known real-world counterpart or historical figure. Within the series, his identity as the Sword Saint is a hereditary title, but he is sometimes confused with other members of the Astrea family, such as his grandfather Wilhelm van Astrea, who was also a legendary swordsman.

Description

Appearance

Reinhard is a young man in his early twenties with a lean, athletic build. He has striking red hair and piercing blue eyes, which are often noted as being unusually bright. He typically wears the white and silver uniform of the Royal Guard, with a cape bearing the Astrea family crest. He carries a rapier-like sword at his hip, which is said to be the legendary Dragon Sword Reid, though it is rarely drawn.

Symbols & Iconography

The Astrea family crest, which features a stylized sword and star. The title 'Sword Saint' is associated with the Divine Blessing of the Sword Saint, which grants him immense combat abilities. His sword, the Dragon Sword Reid, is a legendary weapon that only draws itself when its wielder faces a worthy opponent.

Behaviour & Personality

Reinhard is portrayed as humble, polite, and deeply kind-hearted, despite his overwhelming power. He often downplays his abilities and shows genuine concern for others, especially his childhood friend Felt, whom he serves as a knight. He carries a sense of loneliness and isolation, as his power sets him apart from ordinary people. He is fiercely loyal and protective, but also struggles with the expectations placed upon him by his family and the kingdom.

Domains & Habitats

He resides in the royal capital of Lugunica, in the Astrea family estate. He is a knight of the Royal Guard and often patrols the capital. He is also seen in the royal palace and various locations throughout the kingdom.

Version Differences

In the light novel, his character is more deeply explored, with additional backstory and internal monologue. The anime adaptation condenses some of this, focusing more on his actions. The manga and video game follow similar portrayals. There are no major contradictions between versions, though some details are omitted in the anime.

Powers & Abilities

Divine Blessing of the Sword Saint

Source Version All versions

Description

A powerful blessing that grants the wielder extraordinary swordsmanship, superhuman speed, strength, and reflexes. It also enhances their ability to learn and master any weapon or combat technique instantly. This blessing is hereditary in the Astrea family and is considered one of the strongest divine protections in the world.

Limitations

The blessing is tied to the Dragon Sword Reid, which only draws itself when the wielder faces a worthy opponent. If the sword refuses to draw, Reinhard fights with a regular sword or his bare hands, though he is still formidable. The blessing does not grant invincibility, and he can be overwhelmed by superior numbers or powerful magic.

Contradictions

Some sources suggest that the Sword Saint's blessing is absolute, but the story shows that Reinhard can be challenged by the Witch of Envy or other top-tier beings. The exact limits are not fully defined.

Master Swordsmanship

Source Version All versions

Description

Even without the blessing, Reinhard is a master swordsman, trained since childhood. He can defeat most opponents with ease and is capable of fighting multiple enemies simultaneously.

Limitations

None noted, but his skill is overshadowed by his blessing.

Summary

When Subaru Natsuki arrives in the capital, he encounters Reinhard, who is on duty. Reinhard quickly saves Subaru from a thug and later helps him find a place to stay. He also assists in the investigation of a series of thefts, which leads to the discovery of Felt, a street thief, and the stolen insignia of the royal family. Reinhard becomes Felt's knight and supporter in the Royal Selection.

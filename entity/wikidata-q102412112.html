Who It Is

In the land of Lugunica, where magic and steel decide the fate of nations, there walks a man whose name alone is a promise of victory: Reinhard van Astrea, the Sword Saint. His blade has never been drawn in defeat, and his legend has grown with every foe who dared to stand against him. But behind the flawless swordsmanship and the weight of a divine blessing lies a soul burdened by duty, loneliness, and the impossible expectations of a family that has shaped the kingdom's history for centuries. Who is the man behind the title, and what does it truly mean to be the strongest?

Reinhard is portrayed as humble, polite, and deeply kind-hearted, despite his overwhelming power. He often downplays his abilities and shows genuine concern for others, especially his childhood friend Felt, whom he serves as a knight. He carries a sense of loneliness and isolation, as his power sets him apart from ordinary people. He is fiercely loyal and protective, but also struggles with the expectations placed upon him by his family and the kingdom.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — His given name 'Reinhard' is of Germanic origin, meaning 'brave counsel' or 'strong advisor.' 'van Astrea' suggests a noble lineage, possibly derived from the Latin 'astra' (stars), hinting at his celestial destiny and the Astrea family's connection to the divine.

Pronunciation — RINE-hart van AS-tree-ah

Aliases — Sword Saint, The Strongest Knight, The Divine Blessing of the Sword Saint

Ownership — Japanese light novel and anime series 'Re:Zero - Starting Life in Another World' by Tappei Nagatsuki, illustrated by Shinichirou Otsuka. The character is part of the broader isekai

Powers
Divine Blessing of the Sword Saint

A powerful blessing that grants the wielder extraordinary swordsmanship, superhuman speed, strength, and reflexes. It also enhances their ability to learn and master any weapon or combat technique instantly. This blessing is hereditary in the Astrea family and is considered one of the strongest divine protections in the world.

Limit: The blessing is tied to the Dragon Sword Reid, which only draws itself when the wielder faces a worthy opponent. If the sword refuses to draw, Reinhard fights with a regular sword or his bare hands, though he is still formidable. The blessing does not grant invincibility, and he can be overwhelmed by superior numbers or powerful magic.

Master Swordsmanship

Even without the blessing, Reinhard is a master swordsman, trained since childhood. He can defeat most opponents with ease and is capable of fighting multiple enemies simultaneously.

Limit: None noted, but his skill is overshadowed by his blessing.

Superhuman Physical Abilities

Reinhard possesses immense physical strength, speed, and endurance, allowing him to move faster than the eye can follow and strike with devastating force.

Limit: He is still vulnerable to powerful magic and curses, and his stamina can be depleted.

Healing and Regeneration

Due to his blessings, Reinhard can heal from injuries quickly, even mortal wounds, though the extent is not fully shown.

Limit: Not explicitly detailed; may be limited by the nature of the injury.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Reinhard is a young man in his early twenties with a lean, athletic build. He has striking red hair and piercing blue eyes, which are often noted as being unusually bright. He typically wears the white and silver uniform of the Royal Guard, with a cape bearing the Astrea family crest. He carries a rapier-like sword at his hip, which is said to be the legendary Dragon Sword Reid, though it is rarely drawn.

Symbols — The Astrea family crest, which features a stylized sword and star. The title 'Sword Saint' is associated with the Divine Blessing of the Sword Saint, which grants him immense combat abilities. His sword, the Dragon Sword Reid, is a legendary weapon that only draws itself when its wielder faces a worthy opponent.

Domains — He resides in the royal capital of Lugunica, in the Astrea family estate. He is a knight of the Royal Guard and often patrols the capital. He is also seen in the royal palace and various locations throughout the kingdom.

Accounts I
The Royal Capital RescueRe:Zero - Starting Life in Another World, Arc 1 (Light Novel and Anime)

Fictional timeline, year 1 of the Royal Selection

When Subaru Natsuki arrives in the capital, he encounters Reinhard, who is on duty. Reinhard quickly saves Subaru from a thug and later helps him find a place to stay. He also assists in the investigation of a series of thefts, which leads to the discovery of Felt, a street thief, and the stolen insignia of the royal family. Reinhard becomes Felt's knight and supporter in the Royal Selection. Reinhard's involvement helps Felt enter the Royal Selection, setting the stage for the kingdom's political turmoil.

Accounts II
The Sword Saint's OathRe:Zero - Starting Life in Another World, Side Stories and Light Novel

Before the main story

Reinhard's backstory reveals that he was trained by his grandfather Wilhelm to become the Sword Saint. He struggled with the immense expectations and felt isolated from his peers. He made a vow to protect the weak and serve the kingdom, which led him to become a knight. This oath defines his character and motivates his actions throughout the series.

The Witch Cult IncidentRe:Zero - Starting Life in Another World, Arc 3 (Light Novel and Anime)

Fictional timeline, during the Royal Selection

Reinhard is called upon to deal with the Witch Cult's attack on the village of Irlam. He arrives with the Royal Guard and engages the cult's archbishops, showing his immense power. He is able to defeat several of them, but the Witch of Envy's influence complicates the situation. Reinhard's intervention saves the village, but also reveals the limits of his power against the Witch Cult.

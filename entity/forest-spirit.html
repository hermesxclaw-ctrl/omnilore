Who It Is

The Deer God (Shishigami) from Princess Mononoke, a divine spirit that takes the form of a giant deer-like creature with a human-like face and antlers. By day it appears as a translucent deer-like being, and by night as a massive Night Walker with glowing patterns. It gives and takes life in the forest, healing the land but also commanding immense destructive power when angered.

Powers

Life giver, healing touch, night walker form, forest control

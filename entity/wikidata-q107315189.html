The Approach

In the shadowed world of the Underworld franchise, where vampires and lycans wage an ancient war, Raze emerges as a figure of brutal efficiency and quiet menace—a Death Dealer whose name itself evokes destruction.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of violence and combat consistent with an action-horror franchise.
Identity

Raze

Pronunciation /reɪz/

Etymology

The name 'Raze' likely derives from the English word 'raze', meaning to destroy utterly, fitting a character associated with combat and destruction.

Cultural Ownership

The character is part of the Underworld franchise, created by Len Wiseman, Kevin Grevioux, and Danny McBride, and owned by Screen Gems and Lakeshore Entertainment.

Disputed Identifications

None known.

Description

Appearance

Raze is a vampire male, typically depicted with short dark hair and often wearing dark, tactical clothing. He is shown as physically imposing, with a scarred or rugged appearance consistent with a seasoned warrior.

Symbols & Iconography

As a Death Dealer, Raze is associated with the vampire covenant and its symbols, including the Death Dealer coat and weaponry.

Behaviour & Personality

Raze is portrayed as a loyal and disciplined vampire soldier, following orders without question. He is stoic and efficient, showing little emotion beyond the grim determination of a warrior.

Domains & Habitats

Raze operates within the vampire strongholds and battlefields of the Underworld universe, primarily in Eastern European settings.

Version Differences

Raze appears in multiple films with minor variations in role, but his core characterization as a vampire soldier remains consistent.

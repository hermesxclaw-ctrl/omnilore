Who It Is

Apolaki is the sun god — son of Bathala, brother of Mayari and Tala. He fought Mayari for sole dominion, struck her blind in one eye, then relented to share rule. He is the patron of warriors and fighters, associated with gold, fire, and life-giving warmth sought before battle for strength and courage.

Attested in Oral tradition / Kapampangan and Tagalog mythologyEra Pre-colonial Philippines

Who It Is

Sige (Greek σιγή, 'Silence') is the primordial consort of Bythos and the feminine principle of the absolute divine transcendence. In the Valentinian syzygy (male-female pairing) system, Sige is the 'womb' or 'receptive depth' that receives Bythos's potential and brings forth the first differentiated aeon-pair: Nous (Mind) and Aletheia (Truth). Sige is thus the enabling condition of all emanation: the silence that makes speech possible, the hiddenness that makes revelation meaningful. She is also called Ennoia (Thought) and Charis (Grace) — the 'gracious thought' of the Father that initiates the process of self-manifestation. Without Sige, Bythos would remain an undifferentiated potential; through their union, the Pleroma begins to unfold.

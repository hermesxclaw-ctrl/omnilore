Who It Is

The Tremor-Tremor Fruit, widely considered the most powerful Paramecia Devil Fruit. It grants the user the ability to create vibrations and shockwaves through any medium, including air, water, and the earth itself. These tremors are strong enough to cause earthquakes, tsunamis, and even split continents. The fruit is said to possess the power to destroy the world.

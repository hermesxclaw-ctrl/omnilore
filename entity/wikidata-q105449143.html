Etymology

The name Nelson is of English origin, meaning 'son of Neil' or 'son of Nell.' It derives from the given name Neil, which itself comes from the Gaelic name Niall, meaning 'champion' or 'cloud.'

Cultural Ownership

The name Nelson is widely used in English-speaking cultures, particularly in the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand. It is associated with British naval history due to Horatio Nelson, and has been adopted in various cultural contexts globally.

Disputed Identifications

The name Nelson is used for multiple distinct entities, and there is no single disputed identification. However, confusion may arise between the surname Nelson and the given name Nelson, and between various fictional characters sharing the name, such as Nelson Muntz from The Simpsons and Nelson from other media.

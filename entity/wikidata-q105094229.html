The Approach

In the irradiated wastelands of the 28th century, few sights inspire as much dread as the lumbering silhouette of a Warbot. These towering war machines, relics of a bygone era of conflict, now serve as both guardians and grim reminders of the world that was. For the citizens of Neocron, encountering a Warbot means one thing: danger, and often death.

Identity

Warbot

Pronunciation WAR-bot

Etymology

The name 'Warbot' is a straightforward combination of 'war' and 'robot', indicating its purpose as a combat automaton.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional entity created by Reakktor Media GmbH for the MMORPG Neocron.

Disputed Identifications

None known. The Warbot is a specific enemy type within the Neocron game, not to be confused with similar robots in other franchises.

Description

Appearance

Warbots are depicted as large, heavily armored humanoid robots, typically standing several meters tall. They are often shown with bulky, boxy frames, multiple weapon mounts, and a menacing, faceless head. Their design emphasizes function over form, reflecting their purpose as military hardware.

Symbols & Iconography

Warbots may bear faction insignias or military markings, but these are not consistently detailed in available sources.

Behaviour & Personality

Warbots are aggressive and hostile towards players and non-player characters alike. They are typically found guarding important locations, patrolling wasteland areas, or as part of scripted events. Their behavior is that of a relentless combatant, attacking on sight and pursuing targets.

Domains & Habitats

Warbots are primarily encountered in the Wastelands surrounding the city of Neocron, as well as in certain ruins and military installations. They are less common within the city itself.

Version Differences

In the original Neocron, Warbots were a high-level threat with specific stats and behaviors. In Neocron Evolution, the community-developed update, enemy balance and weapon systems were overhauled, so Warbot stats and combat capabilities may have been adjusted for the new system.

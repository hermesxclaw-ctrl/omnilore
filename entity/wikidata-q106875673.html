The Approach

In a world where spirits threaten to destroy humanity, Origami Tobiichi is a soldier forged by tragedy, a girl who has sworn to eliminate all spirits after her parents were killed in a spatial quake. Yet, as the series unfolds, her rigid hatred is challenged by an impossible truth that forces her to confront the very foundation of her existence.

Content advisory. Contains themes of parental death, psychological trauma, violence, and emotional manipulation. Some depictions of combat and intense emotional distress.
Identity

Origami Tobiichi

Pronunciation Oh-ree-gah-mee Toh-bee-ee-chee

Known As

OrigamiTobiichi Origami (Japanese name order)

Etymology

The name 'Origami' (折紙) means 'origami' (folded paper) in Japanese, possibly reflecting her precise and rigid personality. 'Tobiichi' (鳶一) is a surname, with 'tobi' meaning 'kite' (bird) and 'ichi' meaning 'one'.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese light novel and anime franchise 'Date A Live', created by Kōshi Tachibana and designed by Tsunako.

Disputed Identifications

In the series, Origami is initially believed to be a human who hates spirits, but later it is revealed she is a Spirit herself, leading to a complex identity. There is no real-world dispute over her identity.

Description

Appearance

Origami has short, silver-white hair and pale blue eyes. She is often seen in her AST (Anti-Spirit Team) military uniform or her school uniform. Her Astral Dress is named 'Metatron' and appears as a white and blue mecha-like suit with wings.

Symbols & Iconography

Her Angel, Metatron, is a large cannon-like weapon that can transform into various forms, symbolizing her combat-oriented nature. The color white and blue in her Astral Dress may represent her cold and calculating personality.

Behaviour & Personality

Origami is initially portrayed as emotionless, stoic, and single-mindedly focused on eliminating Spirits. She has a serious and blunt demeanor, often speaking in a monotone. However, as the series progresses, her interactions with Shido reveal a more vulnerable side, especially after she becomes a Spirit. She is academically excellent and physically fit, but socially awkward.

Domains & Habitats

She primarily operates in the city of Tenguu, where she attends Raizen High School and serves as a member of the Anti-Spirit Team (AST). She lives alone in an apartment.

Version Differences

In the anime adaptation, some scenes are condensed or altered for pacing. For example, the anime may not fully explore her internal monologue as in the light novels. The 'Inversion' of Origami, where she becomes a Spirit, is depicted in Season 3 and 4, but the light novels provide more detail on her psychological state.

Powers & Abilities

Metatron

Source Version Light novel and anime

Description

Origami's Angel, which takes the form of a massive cannon. It can fire powerful energy blasts and can be configured into different modes, such as a shield or a sword, for various combat situations.

Limitations

Requires concentration to use effectively. In her human state, she can only use a fraction of its power. As a Spirit, she has full access but may lose control if emotionally unstable.

Contradictions

None noted; consistent across versions.

Inversion

Source Version Light novel and anime (Season 3)

Description

When Origami's negative emotions reach a peak, she undergoes Inversion, transforming into a more powerful and aggressive form. Her Astral Dress changes to a darker color, and her Angel becomes 'Metatron (Qliphoth)'.

Limitations

Inversion is triggered by extreme despair or anger. In this state, she may become uncontrollable and attack indiscriminately.

Contradictions

The exact trigger and consequences are consistent, but the anime may not fully depict the internal struggle as in the novel.

Combat Skills

Source Version Anime and light novel

Description

Even before becoming a Spirit, Origami is an elite AST member, highly skilled in hand-to-hand combat and firearms. Her physical abilities are enhanced by her training and her CRT (Combat Real-time Translator) unit.

Limitations

As a human, she is vulnerable to Spirit attacks and relies on technology.

Contradictions

None.

Origami initially sees Shido as a nuisance, but over time she develops romantic feelings for him. She often tries to seduce him or get closer to him, but her lack of social skills makes it awkward. After becoming a Spirit, she becomes one of his closest companions.

Parental Death

Source Date A Live, Volume 5, anime Season 1

Description

Origami witnessed her parents being killed in a spatial quake, which caused her to develop a deep hatred for Spirits. This trauma defines her initial personality and actions.

Scholarly Context

The portrayal of childhood trauma and its effects on mental health is a common theme in anime and light novels.

Inversion and Self-Destruction

Source Date A Live, Volume 13, anime Season 3

Description

Upon learning that she herself is the Spirit who killed her parents, Origami is overwhelmed by despair and undergoes Inversion, becoming a destructive force. This is a form of psychological breakdown.

Scholarly Context

The concept of Inversion in the series is a metaphor for succumbing to negative emotions.

Contradictions & Disputes

Identity of the Spirit who killed Origami's parents

Position A

Initially believed to be Tohka Yatogami (as seen in the anime and early light novels).

Position A — evidence

In the anime Season 1, the Spirit that appears during the quake resembles Tohka.

Position B

Revealed to be Origami herself due to time travel (as seen in later light novels and anime Season 3).

Position B — evidence

In Volume 13, it is revealed that Origami, after becoming a Spirit and traveling back in time, inadvertently caused the quake that killed her parents.

Scholarly Consensus

The later revelation is the canonical one, as it is consistent with the time travel plot.

Who It Is

In a world where spirits threaten to destroy humanity, Origami Tobiichi is a soldier forged by tragedy, a girl who has sworn to eliminate all spirits after her parents were killed in a spatial quake. Yet, as the series unfolds, her rigid hatred is challenged by an impossible truth that forces her to confront the very foundation of her existence.

Origami is initially portrayed as emotionless, stoic, and single-mindedly focused on eliminating Spirits. She has a serious and blunt demeanor, often speaking in a monotone. However, as the series progresses, her interactions with Shido reveal a more vulnerable side, especially after she becomes a Spirit. She is academically excellent and physically fit, but socially awkward.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Origami' (折紙) means 'origami' (folded paper) in Japanese, possibly reflecting her precise and rigid personality. 'Tobiichi' (鳶一) is a surname, with 'tobi' meaning 'kite' (bird) and 'ichi' meaning 'one'.

Pronunciation — Oh-ree-gah-mee Toh-bee-ee-chee

Aliases — Origami, Tobiichi Origami (Japanese name order)

Ownership — Japanese light novel and anime franchise 'Date A Live', created by Kōshi Tachibana and designed by Tsunako.

Powers
Metatron

Origami's Angel, which takes the form of a massive cannon. It can fire powerful energy blasts and can be configured into different modes, such as a shield or a sword, for various combat situations.

Limit: Requires concentration to use effectively. In her human state, she can only use a fraction of its power. As a Spirit, she has full access but may lose control if emotionally unstable.

Inversion

When Origami's negative emotions reach a peak, she undergoes Inversion, transforming into a more powerful and aggressive form. Her Astral Dress changes to a darker color, and her Angel becomes 'Metatron (Qliphoth)'.

Limit: Inversion is triggered by extreme despair or anger. In this state, she may become uncontrollable and attack indiscriminately.

Combat Skills

Even before becoming a Spirit, Origami is an elite AST member, highly skilled in hand-to-hand combat and firearms. Her physical abilities are enhanced by her training and her CRT (Combat Real-time Translator) unit.

Limit: As a human, she is vulnerable to Spirit attacks and relies on technology.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Origami has short, silver-white hair and pale blue eyes. She is often seen in her AST (Anti-Spirit Team) military uniform or her school uniform. Her Astral Dress is named 'Metatron' and appears as a white and blue mecha-like suit with wings.

Symbols — Her Angel, Metatron, is a large cannon-like weapon that can transform into various forms, symbolizing her combat-oriented nature. The color white and blue in her Astral Dress may represent her cold and calculating personality.

Domains — She primarily operates in the city of Tenguu, where she attends Raizen High School and serves as a member of the Anti-Spirit Team (AST). She lives alone in an apartment.

Accounts I
The Girl Who Hates SpiritsDate A Live, Volume 1

2011

Origami is introduced as an AST member who confronts Shido when he tries to seal Tohka. She is hostile towards Spirits and distrusts Shido's methods, but she is eventually forced to cooperate with him to save the city. Origami becomes a recurring character who often clashes with Shido but also shows signs of respect for his determination.

Accounts II
The Origin of HatredDate A Live, Volume 5 and anime Season 1

2012

It is revealed that Origami's parents were killed in a spatial quake caused by a Spirit, which fuels her hatred. She confronts the Spirit that she believes is responsible, but Shido intervenes. Origami's hatred is solidified, but she begins to question her beliefs after interacting with Shido.

The Inversion of OrigamiDate A Live, Volumes 12-13 and anime Season 3

2014

Origami, driven to despair by the revelation that the Spirit who killed her parents is actually her future self (due to time travel), undergoes Inversion. She becomes a powerful and destructive Spirit, forcing Shido and the others to find a way to save her. Shido is able to seal Origami's powers, and she becomes a Spirit living alongside the others. Her relationship with Shido becomes more intimate.

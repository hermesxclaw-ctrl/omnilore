Who It Is

Ifrit is the iconic fire-elemental summon of Final Fantasy — a towering, horned demon wreathed in flames whose signature attack, Hellfire, incinerates all enemies. Drawing from the ifrit of Arabian mythology (powerful, often malevolent fire djinn), Final Fantasy's Ifrit is a muscular, bestial humanoid with curved horns and a body that seems composed of living flame. In Final Fantasy XVI, Ifrit is elevated to protagonist status as Clive Rosfield's Eikon, making the traditionally fearsome fire demon the hero's primary form.

Origin Post

Ifrit's design is inspired by the ifrit (عفريت) — powerful fire djinn from Arabian folklore. Final Fantasy retains the fire association and demonic appearance but softens the malevolence: Ifrit is a force the summoner commands, not an inherently evil entity. In FFXVI, Ifrit's origin is explicitly tied to the game's mythos as a 'dark' counter-Eikon, feared and misunderstood.

Form

Towering, muscular humanoid with dark red-brown skin resembling volcanic rock, fissures glowing orange. Bestial face with muzzle, sharp fangs, glowing yellow eyes. Two massive backward-sweeping horns. Lower body obscured by flames. FFXVI version is more feral — hunched, elongated limbs, monstrous face emphasizing bestial rage.

Powers

Hellfire: Multi-stage fire assault — charges massive fire sphere, hurls it, pillar of flame erupts. Immense physical strength for grappling other summons. Fire-based attacks: fireballs, flame breath, volcanic eruptions. FFXVI: fast, brutal berserker combat style, can clash with much larger Eikons. Merges with Phoenix to become Ifrit Risen (wings of flame, vastly enhanced power).

Behavior & Habitat

Silent, obedient force in most games. In FFXVI, Ifrit has a primal consciousness representing Clive's repressed rage and grief — embracing Ifrit means accepting his darkest aspects. This makes FFXVI's Ifrit unique among the otherwise impersonal summons of the series.

Weaknesses & Wards

Weak against water/ice (Shiva is natural opposite). Reliance on close-quarters combat makes him predictable. Smaller than many other Eikons (compensates with speed). Summoner vulnerable during casting.

Incident Log

In FFVIII, Ifrit resides in the Fire Cavern, serving as a Guardian Force test for SeeD cadets. Squall defeats Ifrit in a timed battle to claim the GF — establishing early-game combat systems and introducing summon mechanics.

Clive Rosfield discovers he is Ifrit's Dominant — not the Phoenix he believed. The revelation that he, as Ifrit, caused a pivotal tragedy in his past is the emotional crux. Clive learns to embrace Ifrit as a tool to fight the system of Dominants and Eikons that ruined his life.

Cultural Significance

Ifrit is one of the most recognized video game summons, alongside Bahamut and Shiva. His Hellfire animation has been a technical showcase across five console generations. FFXVI's elevation of Ifrit to protagonist status marks the most significant narrative use of a recurring summon in the franchise's history.

Modern Interpretations

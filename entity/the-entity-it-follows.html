Who It Is

A supernatural entity transmitted through sexual contact that relentlessly pursues its current target at a walking pace. It can take the form of anyone — strangers, loved ones, the dead — and only the cursed can see it. If it catches and kills you, the curse reverts to the previous person in the chain. It never stops, never sleeps.

Form

Takes any human form: often strangers, sometimes loved ones (including dead relatives), sometimes nude, sometimes injured; the current target instinctively knows when it's nearby

Powers

Relentless pursuit

Shape-shifting to any human form

Invisibility to non-cursed

Infinite patience

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

Who It Is

A large, hairy, bipedal primate said to inhabit the forests of North America, particularly the Pacific Northwest. Bigfoot is the most famous cryptid in the world — a creature that exists in the liminal space between zoology, folklore, and mass delusion. The Patterson-Gimlin film remains the holy grail of cryptozoology: endlessly analyzed, never definitively debunked or verified.

Cultural Significance

Bigfoot represents the Romantic yearning for undiscovered nature — the hope that in a fully mapped world, something large enough to be a person could remain hidden. The community of 'Squatchers' constitutes a subculture with its own lore, heroes, villains, and schisms.

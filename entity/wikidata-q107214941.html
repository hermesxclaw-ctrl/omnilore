Who It Is

In the rubber-hose world of the Inkwell Isles, where cartoon characters live by their own zany physics, Mugman is the cautious, loyal half of a legendary duo—a blue-headed cup who would rather stay safe than dive headfirst into danger, yet time and again proves that true heroism is not about recklessness but about heart.

Mugman is portrayed as cautious, level-headed, and more sensible than his impulsive brother. He often acts as the voice of reason, urging Cuphead to think before acting. In the game, he is equally capable in combat, but in the animated series, he is more reluctant to engage in risky adventures. He is loyal, protective, and cares deeply for his brother and their grandfather, Elder Kettle.

Powers
Parry

Mugman can parry pink objects by jumping and pressing the parry button, allowing him to deflect attacks and gain a temporary boost.

Limit: Only pink objects can be parried; timing is critical.

Weapon Usage

Mugman can use a variety of weapons, including the Peashooter, Spread, Chaser, and Lobber, each with distinct advantages and disadvantages.

Limit: Weapons must be purchased and equipped; limited to two at a time.

Super Arts

Mugman can unleash powerful super moves when his special meter is full, such as the Energy Beam, Invincibility, and Giant Ghost.

Limit: Requires building the special meter by parrying and dealing damage.

Charm Abilities

Mugman can equip charms that grant passive abilities, such as extra HP, smoke bombs for dodging, or a coffee charm that speeds up his special meter.

Limit: Only one charm can be equipped at a time; some charms have drawbacks.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Mugman is a humanoid cup with a blue and white color scheme, featuring a white face, blue body, and a blue handle on his side. He wears a black bow tie and a blue-and-white striped straw hat, similar to Cuphead but with different colors. His eyes are large and expressive, and his mouth is often depicted in a range of emotions. In the game, he has a slightly different stance and idle animation compared to Cuphead.

Symbols — The straw hat and bow tie are reminiscent of 1930s cartoon characters, evoking the Fleischer Studios and Disney style. The cup shape is a playful take on the 'cuphead' concept, and the blue color distinguishes him from his red brother. His design emphasizes a more cautious and timid personality compared to Cuphead's brashness.

Domains — The Inkwell Isles, a fantastical world inspired by 1930s cartoons. Mugman and Cuphead live with Elder Kettle in a small house on the outskirts of Inkwell Isle One. They frequent locations such as the local fairgrounds, the casino, and various boss arenas.

Accounts I
The Debt to the DevilCuphead (2017)

2017

Mugman and his brother Cuphead, after winning a bet at the Devil's casino, lose their souls to the Devil. To repay their debt, they must collect the contracts of other debtors who have also lost their souls. They travel across the Inkwell Isles, defeating bosses to retrieve these contracts, ultimately facing the Devil himself. If the player succeeds, Mugman and Cuphead reclaim their souls and those of the debtors. If they fail, they lose their souls to the Devil.

Accounts II
The Cuphead Show! EpisodesThe Cuphead Show! (2022)

2022-present

The series follows the daily adventures of Mugman and Cuphead in the Inkwell Isles, often involving run-ins with the Devil and other characters. Mugman frequently tries to keep Cuphead out of trouble, but they inevitably find themselves in chaotic situations. Episodes highlight Mugman's cautious nature, his friendship with Cuphead, and their relationship with Elder Kettle. The series expands on the characters' personalities and relationships, providing more depth than the game.

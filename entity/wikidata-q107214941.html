The Approach

In the rubber-hose world of the Inkwell Isles, where cartoon characters live by their own zany physics, Mugman is the cautious, loyal half of a legendary duo—a blue-headed cup who would rather stay safe than dive headfirst into danger, yet time and again proves that true heroism is not about recklessness but about heart.

Content advisory. Mild cartoon violence, perilous situations, and slapstick comedy typical of 1930s-style animation. No graphic or disturbing content.
Identity

Mugman

Pronunciation MUHG-man

Etymology

The name 'Mugman' derives from 'mug', a colloquial term for a cup or a person's face, combined with 'man', indicating a humanoid character. It follows the naming pattern of his brother, Cuphead.

Cultural Ownership

Created by StudioMDHR (Canada), the character is part of the Cuphead franchise, owned by StudioMDHR. He is a fictional character in the public consciousness, not tied to any real-world culture.

Disputed Identifications

None known. Mugman is a distinct character from his brother Cuphead, though they are often collectively referred to as 'Cuphead and Mugman'.

Description

Appearance

Mugman is a humanoid cup with a blue and white color scheme, featuring a white face, blue body, and a blue handle on his side. He wears a black bow tie and a blue-and-white striped straw hat, similar to Cuphead but with different colors. His eyes are large and expressive, and his mouth is often depicted in a range of emotions. In the game, he has a slightly different stance and idle animation compared to Cuphead.

Symbols & Iconography

The straw hat and bow tie are reminiscent of 1930s cartoon characters, evoking the Fleischer Studios and Disney style. The cup shape is a playful take on the 'cuphead' concept, and the blue color distinguishes him from his red brother. His design emphasizes a more cautious and timid personality compared to Cuphead's brashness.

Behaviour & Personality

Mugman is portrayed as cautious, level-headed, and more sensible than his impulsive brother. He often acts as the voice of reason, urging Cuphead to think before acting. In the game, he is equally capable in combat, but in the animated series, he is more reluctant to engage in risky adventures. He is loyal, protective, and cares deeply for his brother and their grandfather, Elder Kettle.

Domains & Habitats

The Inkwell Isles, a fantastical world inspired by 1930s cartoons. Mugman and Cuphead live with Elder Kettle in a small house on the outskirts of Inkwell Isle One. They frequent locations such as the local fairgrounds, the casino, and various boss arenas.

Version Differences

In the video game, Mugman is a playable character identical to Cuphead in abilities, differing only in appearance. In The Cuphead Show!, Mugman has a more developed personality, often serving as the straight man to Cuphead's antics. He is more cautious and sometimes cowardly, but ultimately brave when it counts. The show also gives him a more prominent role in specific episodes, such as 'A High Seas Adventure!' where he takes the lead.

Stories & Myths

The Debt to the Devil

Source Cuphead (2017)Culture Video game culturePeriod 2017

Summary

Mugman and his brother Cuphead, after winning a bet at the Devil's casino, lose their souls to the Devil. To repay their debt, they must collect the contracts of other debtors who have also lost their souls. They travel across the Inkwell Isles, defeating bosses to retrieve these contracts, ultimately facing the Devil himself.

Participants

Mugman

Cuphead

The Devil

Elder Kettle

Various bosses

Consequences

If the player succeeds, Mugman and Cuphead reclaim their souls and those of the debtors. If they fail, they lose their souls to the Devil.

Variants

The game has two endings: one where the player gives up the contracts to the Devil, and one where they refuse and fight the Devil. The latter is considered the true ending.

The Cuphead Show! Episodes

Source The Cuphead Show! (2022)Culture Animated seriesPeriod 2022-present

Summary

The series follows the daily adventures of Mugman and Cuphead in the Inkwell Isles, often involving run-ins with the Devil and other characters. Mugman frequently tries to keep Cuphead out of trouble, but they inevitably find themselves in chaotic situations. Episodes highlight Mugman's cautious nature, his friendship with Cuphead, and their relationship with Elder Kettle.

Relationships

Cuphead

Type brotherSource Cuphead (2017), The Cuphead Show!

Description

Mugman and Cuphead are brothers and inseparable partners. Mugman often acts as the sensible one, trying to prevent Cuphead from making reckless decisions. Despite their differences, they care deeply for each other and work together to overcome challenges.

Version Note

In the game, they are equal partners; in the show, Mugman is more cautious and sometimes exasperated by Cuphead's impulsiveness.

Conflicting Accounts

None.

Elder Kettle

Type grandfather/father figureSource Cuphead (2017), The Cuphead Show!

Description

Elder Kettle is Mugman's grandfather and guardian. He provides guidance and wisdom, often warning the brothers about the dangers of the Devil. Mugman respects and listens to him more than Cuphead does.

Version Note

In the game, Elder Kettle appears only in the intro and as a tutorial guide; in the show, he is a recurring character with a larger role.

Conflicting Accounts

None.

The Devil

Type antagonistSource Cuphead (2017), The Cuphead Show!

Description

The Devil is the primary antagonist who tricks Mugman and Cuphead into gambling their souls. Mugman is more wary of the Devil than Cuphead, often urging caution.

Version Note

In the game, the Devil is the final boss; in the show, he is a recurring antagonist who often tries to steal the brothers' souls.

Conflicting Accounts

None.

Chalice

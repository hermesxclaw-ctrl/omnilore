Who It Is

Deep in the heart of a prehistoric lagoon, where the last dinosaurs still roam and the jungle hums with secrets, a small, green, and utterly endearing creature named Urmel hatches from an ice-encased egg, setting off a chain of adventures that have captivated children and adults alike for over half a century. Urmel is not just any fictional character; he is a symbol of curiosity, friendship, and the joy of discovery, born from the imaginative mind of German children's author Max Kruse and brought to life in a series of beloved books, radio plays, and animated films.

Urmel is curious, intelligent, and adventurous. He is kind-hearted and forms friendships with the other inhabitants of the island, including the professor who becomes his father figure. He asks many questions and loves to learn. He is brave in the face of danger but also gentle and playful.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Urmel' is a German diminutive of 'Ur-' (primordial, primeval) combined with a playful suffix, suggesting a 'little primeval creature'. It reflects his origin as a prehistoric animal.

Powers
Intelligence and learning

Urmel is highly intelligent and learns to speak and reason quickly after hatching. He is curious and absorbs knowledge from the professor and others.

Limit: He is still a child and sometimes naive.

Swimming

Urmel is a capable swimmer, often diving and exploring underwater.

Limit: Not specified.

Survival in prehistoric environment

As a prehistoric creature, Urmel is adapted to a warm climate and can survive in the wild.

Limit: He needs the warmth of the island; he cannot survive in cold climates without protection.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Urmel is a small, green, prehistoric creature resembling a young sauropod dinosaur. He has a long neck, a rounded body, and a friendly face. In illustrations, he is often depicted with spots or stripes, and he walks on four legs but can stand on two. He has a short tail and expressive eyes.

Symbols — Urmel is often associated with ice, as he hatches from an egg that was frozen in a block of ice. He is also associated with the tropical island where he lives, symbolizing innocence and the wonders of nature.

Domains — Urmel's home is the fictional island of Titiwu, a tropical island in the Pacific Ocean. The island is inhabited by a professor, his daughter, and a variety of talking animals. Urmel's habitat includes the island's beaches, jungle, and the professor's research station.

Accounts I
Urmel from the Ice (Urmel aus dem Eis)Max Kruse, 1969

1960s

Professor Tibatong, a scientist, lives on the island of Titiwu with his daughter Timi and a collection of talking animals. One day, a block of ice washes ashore containing an egg. They hatch the egg, and a small prehistoric creature emerges, whom they name Urmel. Urmel quickly becomes part of the family, learning to speak and getting into adventures. The story follows Urmel's introduction to the island and his first experiences. Urmel becomes a permanent resident of Titiwu, and the island's inhabitants grow to love him.

Urmel flies into space (Urmel fliegt ins All)Max Kruse, 1971

1970s

Urmel and his friends build a rocket and travel to the moon, where they encounter strange creatures and have adventures. They return safely to Titiwu with new knowledge.

Urmel dives (Urmel taucht ab)Max Kruse, 1973

1970s

Urmel explores the underwater world around the island, discovering a sunken city and meeting sea creatures. Urmel learns about the ocean and its mysteries.

Urmel plays in the castle (Urmel spielt im Schloss)Max Kruse, 1974

1970s

The group visits a castle on the mainland, where they encounter a ghost and solve a mystery. They help the castle's inhabitants and return home.

Urmel moves to the North Pole (Urmel zieht zum Pol)Max Kruse, 1977

1970s

Urmel and friends travel to the North Pole to help a polar bear, facing cold and ice along the way. They succeed in their mission and return to Titiwu.

Accounts II
Urmel in the volcano (Urmel im Vulkan)Max Kruse, 1979

1970s

A volcano on Titiwu threatens to erupt, and Urmel and his friends must find a way to prevent a disaster. They manage to divert the lava flow and save the island.

Urmel and the pig fairy (Urmel und die Schweinefee)Max Kruse, 1981

1980s

A fairy appears, but she is not what she seems, leading to humorous and magical adventures. The fairy is revealed to be a trickster, and order is restored.

Urmel goes ice skating (Urmel fährt Schlittschuh)Max Kruse, 1984

1980s

Urmel learns to ice skate on a frozen lake, with comedic results. Urmel becomes a decent skater.

Urmel and the mysterious message in a bottle (Urmel und die geheimnisvolle Flaschenpost)Max Kruse, 1987

1980s

The group finds a message in a bottle that leads them on a treasure hunt. They find the treasure and help someone in need.

Urmel and the terrible dinosaurs (Urmel und die schrecklichen Dinos)Max Kruse, 1990

1990s

Urmel encounters other dinosaurs who are not as friendly, leading to a lesson about prejudice and acceptance. Urmel befriends the other dinosaurs and they learn to live together.

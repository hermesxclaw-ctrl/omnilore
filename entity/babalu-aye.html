Babalu Aye

Who It Is
Form

Babalu Aye is often depicted as a disfigured or wounded figure, with a twisted or broken body, symbolizing his association with disease and healing.

Powers

Healing Babalu Aye has the power to heal physical and spiritual ailments, and is often invoked in rituals for protection and healing.

Accounts

According to Yoruba mythology, Babalu Aye was created by the Supreme Deity Olodumare to serve as a healer and protector of the earth.

Babalu Aye was brought to the Americas by enslaved Africans, where he was incorporated into the syncretic traditions of Santeria and other Afro-Caribbean religions.

Scholarly Debates
Babalu Aye's benevolence
Some Yoruba traditions depict Babalu Aye as a benevolent deity, while others portray him as more malevolent. ⇌ The benevolent depiction of Babalu Aye is seen as a more recent development, while the malevolent depiction is seen as more traditional.

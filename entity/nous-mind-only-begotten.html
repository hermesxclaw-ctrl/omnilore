Nous (Mind / Only-Begotten)

also known asMind / Only-Begotten
Typeaeon (first emanated from Bythos & Sige)
☘ Thin file. The Archive holds limited verified material on this entity and declines to pad it. What follows is everything that clears our sourcing bar.
Who It Is
Form

Nous is often depicted as a divine being with a radiant aura, symbolizing its role as the source of all knowledge and understanding.

Powers

Intellectual Illumination Nous has the power to grant intellectual illumination to those who seek knowledge and understanding, allowing them to see beyond the veil of ignorance and attain gnosis.

Accounts

Nous, along with the Propator, creates the world through a series of emanations, each representing a different aspect of the divine.

Nous descends into the material realm, taking on a human form to guide the soul towards enlightenment and spiritual growth.

Who It Is
Form

Pah is often depicted as a lunar deity, associated with the night sky and the cycles of the moon. He is said to be a powerful and mysterious figure, with the ability to control the tides and the phases of the moon.

Powers

Lunar Cycle Control Pah has the ability to control the cycles of the moon, including the phases of the moon and the tides.

Accounts

Pah is said to have played a key role in the creation of the world, using his powers to bring light and life to the land.

Pah is said to have created the moon, using his powers to bring light and life to the night sky.

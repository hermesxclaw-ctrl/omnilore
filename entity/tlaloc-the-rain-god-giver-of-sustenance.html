Tlaloc (the Rain-God / Giver of Sustenance)

Who It Is
Form

Tlaloc is often depicted as a bearded man with a large nose and a prominent chin. He wears a headdress adorned with feathers and a necklace made of jade. In some representations, he is shown with a serpent or a fish, symbolizing his connection to water and fertility.

Powers

Control over the Weather Tlaloc has the power to control the weather, bringing forth both life-giving rain and devastating storms. He can summon the winds, conjure lightning bolts, and create powerful storms.

Fertility and Abundance Tlaloc is associated with fertility and abundance, and has the power to bring forth crops and livestock. He can ensure a bountiful harvest and provide for the needs of his people.

Accounts

Tlaloc is said to have played a crucial role in the creation of the world. He brought forth the first humans from the earth and taught them how to cultivate the land and bring forth crops.

Tlaloc is said to have brought forth a great flood that destroyed the world. He then created a new world and brought forth a new generation of humans.

Who It Is

In the murky depths of the Thames, a creature of teeth and silence waits—Leatherhead, the crocodilian horror of Victorian London, whose legend slithers between fact and fable.

Leatherhead is typically portrayed as a savage, feral creature with a strong survival instinct. He is often shown as a loner, distrustful of humans and other mutants. However, he can form bonds of loyalty, especially with the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles. In some versions, he is more intelligent and articulate, even exhibiting a gentle side, while in others he is a mindless brute.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Leatherhead' is a compound of 'leather' and 'head', likely referencing the character's tough, leathery hide and reptilian appearance. It may also evoke the English town of Leatherhead, Surrey, but no direct connection is attested.

Pronunciation — /ˈlɛðərhɛd/

Aliases — Leatherhead

Ownership — The character is a creation of the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles franchise, owned by Nickelodeon (formerly Mirage Studios). He is a fictional alligator/crocodile mutant.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Leatherhead is a large, anthropomorphic alligator or crocodile. He typically has green, scaly skin, a long snout filled with sharp teeth, and a powerful tail. His design varies between adaptations: in some, he is more crocodilian with a broad snout; in others, more alligator-like. He often wears tattered clothing or armor, depending on the version.

Symbols — Leatherhead is often associated with swamps, sewers, and the Florida Everglades. He may be depicted with tribal markings or scars, reflecting his savage nature. In some versions, he wears a metal collar or chains, symbolizing his captivity or past abuse.

Domains — Leatherhead's primary habitat is the sewers of New York City, but he has also been associated with swamps and wetlands. In the 2012 series, he is originally from Florida and later resides in the sewers.

Who It Is

On the frozen surface of Paralympic ice, Tyrone Henry carved a path from childhood passion to national triumph—a story of resilience, skill, and an unbroken love for the game that began long before a life-changing accident.

Demonstrates resilience and dedication, as evidenced by his return to the sport after a disabling accident and his sustained international success.

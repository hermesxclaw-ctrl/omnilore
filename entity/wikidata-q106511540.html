Etymology

Katie is an English feminine given name, typically a diminutive or pet form of Katherine. Katherine itself derives from the Greek name Aikaterine, the etymology of which is uncertain, possibly linked to the Greek word 'katharos' meaning 'pure'. Over time, Katie emerged as a common standalone given name.

Cultural Ownership

Primarily English-speaking cultures, especially the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Australia, and Ireland. It is widely used across these regions and has been adopted internationally.

Disputed Identifications

The name Katie is sometimes confused with similar names like Katey, Katy, or Kate. Additionally, many individuals named Katie share common surnames, leading to potential ambiguity. For example, Katie Hill (b. 1984) is an Australian wheelchair basketball player, while another Katie Hill is a former U.S. politician.

Who It Is

A three-headed chaos beast, each head belonging to a different creature — usually a lion, a goat, and a dragon. Its body is a patchwork of scales, fur, and feathers, warped by the raw energy of the Chaos Wastes. The three heads constantly argue and snap at each other, but coordinate perfectly in combat.

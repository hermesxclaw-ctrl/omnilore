Chaos Chimera

also known asWarhammer ChimeraGreek ChimeraMythological Chimera
TypeWarhammer Fantasy Entity
Who It Is
Form

The Chaos Chimera is a monstrous creature with the head of a lion, the body of a goat, and the tail of a serpent. Its body is said to be a mass of writhing, snake-like limbs, with scales that shimmer like the stars in the night sky. Its eyes burn with an inner fire, and its very presence seems to distort the fabric of reality.

Powers

Reality Distortion The Chaos Chimera has the ability to distort reality itself, creating pockets of chaos and destruction wherever it goes. This power allows it to manipulate the very fabric of space and time, creating impossible geometries and warping the laws of physics.

Accounts

The story of the Chimera's slaying is told in the ancient Greek epic poem, The Iliad. According to the poem, the hero Bellerophon was tasked with slaying the Chimera, which was terrorizing the countryside. Bellerophon rode into battle on the back of the winged horse Pegasus, and with the help of the goddess Athena, he was able to slay the Chimera and bring peace to the land.

In the Warhammer Fantasy universe, the Chimera is said to have returned to the world, bringing with it a wave of chaos and destruction. The creature is said to be a harbinger of the end times, and its return is seen as a sign of the impending doom of the world.

Who It Is
Form

Rennala is a tall, slender woman with long, flowing silver hair and piercing green eyes. She wears a flowing white gown with intricate, glowing blue patterns that seem to shift and change as she moves. Her presence is accompanied by an otherworldly aura that seems to draw people in.

Powers

Magic Rennala is a master of the arcane arts, and is said to possess the ability to cast powerful spells and incantations.

Accounts

Rennala's story begins with her discovery of a mysterious full moon that would eventually bewitch the Academy of Raya Lucaria. She rose to become its Master, and founded the House of Caria as a royal line.

Rennala's story takes a dark turn when she is said to have shattered her own heart, leading to a period of madness and despair.

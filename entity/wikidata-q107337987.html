Who It Is

In the fog-drenched realm of the Entity's realm, where hope flickers like a dying flame, one survivor stands as a testament to the power of quiet endurance. Dwight Fairfield, the unassuming manager of a rundown fast-food joint, finds himself thrust into a nightmare that tests the very limits of human resolve. But Dwight is no ordinary victim; he is a beacon of collaboration, a leader forged in the crucible of terror, whose story is as much about the bonds he forms as the horrors he faces.

Dwight is characterized as timid, anxious, and lacking in self-confidence, but he possesses a strong sense of responsibility and a desire to help others. In the game, his perks encourage teamwork and leadership, such as 'Bond', 'Prove Thyself', and 'Leader', which allow him to locate allies, speed up repairs, and boost others' actions.

Powers
Bond

Reveals the auras of other survivors within a certain range, allowing Dwight to coordinate with his team.

Limit: Limited range and requires line of sight.

Prove Thyself

Increases repair speed for Dwight and allies when working on the same generator, encouraging cooperative play.

Limit: Only effective when multiple survivors are on the same generator.

Leader

Provides a boost to healing, sabotage, and other actions for allies within a certain range, making Dwight a supportive leader.

Limit: Requires proximity to allies.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Dwight is depicted as a young man in his late 20s to early 30s, with a slim build and unkempt brown hair. He typically wears a yellow polo shirt with the name 'Dwight' embroidered on the chest, khaki pants, and a name tag. His default appearance is that of a fast-food restaurant manager, often looking disheveled and anxious.

Symbols — Dwight is often associated with the color yellow, representing his uniform and his role as a team player. His iconic name tag is a symbol of his mundane life before the Entity's realm.

Domains — Dwight is trapped in the Entity's realm, a nightmarish dimension consisting of various maps based on the killers' memories. He has no fixed domain, but he is often seen in the Macmillan Estate, Autohaven Wreckers, and other original maps.

Dwight's BackstoryDead by Daylight in-game lore

2016

Dwight Fairfield was the manager of a fast-food restaurant, a job he took to escape his mundane life. He was a quiet, unassuming person who often felt overlooked and undervalued. One day, while driving home, he was abducted by the Entity and transported to its realm, where he must survive trials against murderous killers. Despite his fear, Dwight discovers an inner strength and a talent for bringing people together, becoming a natural leader among the survivors. Dwight becomes one of the stalwart survivors, helping others escape and fighting against the killers.

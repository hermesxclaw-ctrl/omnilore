The Approach

In the fog-drenched realm of the Entity's realm, where hope flickers like a dying flame, one survivor stands as a testament to the power of quiet endurance. Dwight Fairfield, the unassuming manager of a rundown fast-food joint, finds himself thrust into a nightmare that tests the very limits of human resolve. But Dwight is no ordinary victim; he is a beacon of collaboration, a leader forged in the crucible of terror, whose story is as much about the bonds he forms as the horrors he faces.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of psychological horror, violence, and peril. Some adaptations may include graphic imagery.
Identity

Dwight Fairfield

Pronunciation DWIGHT FAIR-feeld

Etymology

The name 'Dwight' is of English origin, meaning 'white' or 'blond'. 'Fairfield' is an English surname meaning 'fair field' or 'beautiful field'.

Cultural Ownership

Owned by Behaviour Interactive, the developer of Dead by Daylight. The character is part of the game's original survivor roster.

Disputed Identifications

None known. Dwight is a unique character within the Dead by Daylight universe, with no real-world counterpart.

Description

Appearance

Dwight is depicted as a young man in his late 20s to early 30s, with a slim build and unkempt brown hair. He typically wears a yellow polo shirt with the name 'Dwight' embroidered on the chest, khaki pants, and a name tag. His default appearance is that of a fast-food restaurant manager, often looking disheveled and anxious.

Symbols & Iconography

Dwight is often associated with the color yellow, representing his uniform and his role as a team player. His iconic name tag is a symbol of his mundane life before the Entity's realm.

Behaviour & Personality

Dwight is characterized as timid, anxious, and lacking in self-confidence, but he possesses a strong sense of responsibility and a desire to help others. In the game, his perks encourage teamwork and leadership, such as 'Bond', 'Prove Thyself', and 'Leader', which allow him to locate allies, speed up repairs, and boost others' actions.

Domains & Habitats

Dwight is trapped in the Entity's realm, a nightmarish dimension consisting of various maps based on the killers' memories. He has no fixed domain, but he is often seen in the Macmillan Estate, Autohaven Wreckers, and other original maps.

Version Differences

Dwight has received multiple cosmetic outfits and reworks, but his core personality and backstory remain consistent. In the game's lore, he is one of the original four survivors alongside Meg Thomas, Claudette Morel, and Jake Park.

Dwight's Backstory

Source Dead by Daylight in-game loreCulture Video game culturePeriod 2016

Summary

Dwight Fairfield was the manager of a fast-food restaurant, a job he took to escape his mundane life. He was a quiet, unassuming person who often felt overlooked and undervalued. One day, while driving home, he was abducted by the Entity and transported to its realm, where he must survive trials against murderous killers. Despite his fear, Dwight discovers an inner strength and a talent for bringing people together, becoming a natural leader among the survivors.

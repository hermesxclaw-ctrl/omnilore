Who It Is

She stood alone on the polished floor of Yuigaoka's empty music room, a classical pianist who believed school idols had stolen her mother's dream — until five girls with no stage, no songs, and no permission asked her to sing with them. Ren Hazuki, the cool, impeccably composed heiress of Love Live! Superstar!!, begins as the wall Liella! must break through, and ends as the spine that holds them together.

Attested personality (anime Season 1-3, official profiles): At debut, Ren is serious, formal, highly disciplined, polite keigo speech, with a cool, aloof, seemingly stern exterior. She speaks with precise diction and rarely uses slang, reflecting elite upbringing and classical training. She is intensely responsible, perfectionist, and dutiful to her family's legacy as founders of Yuigaoka Girls High School. Given name Ren (恋) means 'love' or 'romantic love' (koi), distinct from homophonous Ren (蓮 = lotus, 錬 = refine). The naming is thematicall

Powers
Classical Piano (Concert Level)

Ren is an award-winning classical pianist, trained from early childhood by her mother Hana. She has won youth competitions and can perform advanced repertoire (Chopin, Liszt referenced). This is her core artistic identity before becoming a school idol and informs her musicality.

Limit: Not a superpower; limited to acoustic piano performance. She initially struggles to transfer classical technique to pop idol singing/dancing. Stage fright related to mother's legacy.

Vocal Performance (School Idol Singer)

As member of Liella!, Ren is a mid-to-high vocalist with clear, elegant tone, often given harmonies and classical-tinged solo lines. Solo songs include 'Reverb' and 'Aoi no Uta' imagery; group songs: 'START!! True dreams', 'WE WILL!!', 'MIRACLE STAY TUNE!' etc. She improves dramatically from stiff early performances to confident center-capable singer by Season 3.

Limit: Initially lacks pop vocal projection and idol expression; requires training from Kanon and Keke. Not a professional soloist outside Liella! context.

Dance and Ballet Foundation

Ren has ballet training as part of elite education, giving her excellent posture and line, but initially poor at idol dance (too rigid, too classical). Through training she becomes a precise, graceful dancer, often placed in formations requiring symmetry and elegance.

Limit: Early rigidity is a plot point; she is not naturally 'cute' or loose in movement and must consciously learn idol-style expression.

Academic and Leadership Excellence

Top grades, student council president from Season 2 onward, responsible for school administration, event planning, and mentoring juniors. Highly organized, punctual, and respected by faculty.

Limit: Perfectionism leads to overwork and reluctance to delegate; struggles to ask for help.

Emotional Resilience and Group Cohesion

Narrative ability rather than supernatural power: Ren functions as Liella!'s emotional stabilizer and voice of reason, mediating conflicts (e.g., between Wien Margarete and others in Season 3) and maintaining tradition while embracing change.

Limit: Her sense of duty can make her suppress own feelings; arc requires learning vulnerability.

Accounts I
The Heiress Who Hated School Idols (Season 1 Episodes 1-6)Love Live! Superstar!! Season 1, Episodes 1-6

2021 (in-universe: Spring 2021, Yuigaoka's inaugural year)

Yuigaoka Girls High School is a new school founded by Ren's mother, Hazuki Hana, who dreamed of a school where students could freely pursue what they love. After Hana's death, the school's board (and Ren) pivots to emphasizing elite academics over clubs. Kanon Shibuya and Keke Tang wish to become school idols to save the school from low enrollment and to follow their passion. Ren, as the de facto representative of the founder's family and a first-year with influence over the student council, repeatedly denies their club application, citing school rules and her belief that school idols are frivolous and caused her mother's dream to be mocked. She is shown practicing piano alone in the music room her mother donated, guarding it as a shrine. Kanon and Keke's persistence, plus Chisato and Sumire's joining, forces Ren to confront that she is projecting her grief and fear of betraying her mother. Establishes Ren as initial antagonist and her core wound. Sets up her eventual redemption and defines Yuigaoka's thematic conflict: tradition/academic prestige vs. youthful passion.

Mother's Dream and 'Hajimari wa Kimi no Sora' (Season 1 Episodes 6-7)Love Live! Superstar!! Season 1, Episodes 6-7 'I Don't Want to Sing' / 'Battle! The Decision'

2021

Kanon discovers an old recording and diary revealing that Hazuki Hana herself loved school idols and had secretly wanted Yuigaoka to have a school idol club, but was unable to realize it before her illness. Ren learns that her mother did not oppose idols — she cherished them — and that Ren's strictness was based on a misunderstanding of her mother's true wish. In a rain-soaked confrontation in the music room, Kanon sings for Ren, and Ren finally breaks down, admitting she is lonely and afraid of losing her mother's memory. She performs a piano-accompanied confession and agrees to join Liella! as the fifth member, allowing the club to be officially approved. Liella! debuts with five members at the school festival. Ren joins Liella!; club gains official status; Ren's character shifts from gatekeeper to performer. Her image song 'Makka na Lip' and piano motif become central.

First Stage and Love Live! Preliminaries (Season 1 Episodes 8-12)Love Live! Superstar!! Season 1, Episodes 8-12

2021

Now as Liella! member, Ren struggles with idol fundamentals: smiling naturally, cute choreography, and singing pop versus classical. She is comically bad at winking and 'kawaii' poses during training camp (Episode 8). With support from Sumire (a former child actress) and Keke, she learns to loosen up. Liella! competes in Love Live! Tokyo preliminaries, performing 'MIRACLE STAY TUNE!' and later 'Wish Song.' Ren's piano interlude is incorporated into arrangement. Though they do not win the national Love Live! in Season 1 (they are eliminated in a shock result), Ren's growth from stiff heiress to joyful performer is completed, and she declares she now loves school idols. Cements Ren's place in group; establishes her performance niche (elegant, piano-accented). Sets up Season 2's expansion.

Accounts II
Student Council President and the Second Generation (Season 2)Love Live! Superstar!! Season 2, Episodes 1-12

2022 (in-universe: Ren's second year)

With Liella! now famous within Yuigaoka, Ren is elected student council president, balancing administrative duties with idol activities. The story introduces four new first-years who admire Liella!: Kinako Sakurakoji, Mei Yoneme, Shiki Wakana, and Natsumi Onitsuka. Ren initially worries that expanding Liella! will dilute its bond, echoing her earlier fear of change, but learns to mentor. A key Ren episode (S2E9) shows her organizing the school festival while also preparing for Love Live! qualifiers, collapsing from overwork until Kanon and the second-years force her to rest and delegate. Liella! (now 9 members) performs 'WE WILL!!' and reaches Love Live! finals, placing highly and securing Yuigaoka's future enrollment. Ren matures into leader/mentor; Liella! expands from 5 to 9; Yuigaoka is saved from closure. Ren's leadership style softens from authoritarian to collaborative.

Third Year, Wien Margarete, and the Final Love Live! (Season 3)Love Live! Superstar!! Season 3, Episodes 1-12

2024 (in-universe: Ren's third year, final year)

Ren is now a third-year, the eldest and most senior member, facing graduation. Two new members join: Wien Margarete (Austrian-Japanese rival turned ally, who had defeated Liella! in Season 2) and Tomari Onitsuka (Natsumi's sister). The season's central tension is whether Liella! can stay together after the third-years graduate and whether Ren can let go of Yuigaoka as her family's legacy and trust the younger members to carry it. Wien's aloof, perfectionist personality mirrors early Ren, allowing Ren to act as her understanding senpai. In the finale, Liella! (now 11 members) performs at the Love Live! final stage with a song that incorporates Ren's piano and a graduation motif. Ren gives a farewell speech in the music room, passing the piano's stewardship to the next generation, symbolizing she no longer needs to guard her mother's memory alone. Ren's arc concludes with acceptance of change and legacy as living tradition, not museum. Provides closure to Hazuki family storyline and transitions Liella! to new generation.

Bond Episodes: Piano Recital and the Cooking Disaster (Game Original)Love Live! School Idol Festival ALL STARS - Ren Hazuki Kizuna (Bond) Episodes 12, 18, 25

2020-2023 (game timeline parallel to S1-S2)

In ALL STARS bond stories, Ren faces smaller, character-driven vignettes: she is invited to a prestigious piano recital on the same day as a Liella! mini-live and must choose; she chooses Liella! after realizing music is about sharing joy, not prestige. Another episode has her attempting to cook for Liella! to thank them, failing spectacularly due to lack of domestic experience (burning curry), and being comforted. A third has her teaching Kinako proper tea ceremony manners. These stories deepen her perfectionist-anxious traits and show her learning to be imperfect. Expands Ren's daily life beyond anime; reinforces themes of vulnerability and friendship. Considered semi-canonical (game continuity).

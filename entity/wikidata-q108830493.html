Who It Is

At twelve years old, Missy Biron could fold a city block like paper — stretching a hallway into a mile, crushing a street into a step — yet no one in the room would let her decide where to eat lunch. Vista, the youngest Ward in Brockton Bay, is Worm's quiet tragedy of power without permission: a Shaker 9 who can bend space itself, trapped in the one space she cannot warp — childhood.

Attested: Mature, sardonic, and deeply frustrated by infantilization. Vista triggered young due to prolonged parental conflict and feels compelled to prove herself as a competent hero to compensate for lack of control at home. She volunteers for dangerous assignments, resents being sidelined 'for her own protection,' and chafes at PRT public-relations constraints. Simultaneously shows vulnerability, loneliness, and attachment to older Wards (especially Gallant and Clockblocker) as surrogate family. Powers
Space Warping (Shaker 9)

Vista can distort space itself within a large radius (city-block to multi-block scale at peak). She can stretch distance (making a 10-foot hallway into hundreds of feet), compress distance (bringing distant objects close), bend or curve space to redirect attacks or create barriers, and warp terrain (e.g., raising pavement into walls by expanding underlying space). Effect is continuous and can be maintained or reshaped with concentration.

Limit: Manton-limited: cannot directly warp space occupied by living beings (people, animals). Can warp space around them, but not inside or through them, which prevents instant crushing or dismemberment via power. Requires line-of-sight or awareness of area; larger warps take more time and focus. Cannot create or destroy matter, only distort metric. Overuse causes headaches, nosebleeds, and exhaustion. Power is less effective in areas already heavily warped by other Shakers or Labyrinth-like effects.

Large-Scale Battlefield Control

Can reshape urban terrain for evacuation, containment, or cover — e.g., stretching streets to delay Leviathan, compressing escape routes for civilians, creating labyrinths to trap enemies like the Slaughterhouse Nine's Cherish/Bonesaw constructs.

Limit: Living beings resist warping, so she cannot simply wall off a person inside warped space; they can walk through distorted area with disorienting but not physically blocking effects unless she warps surrounding environment. PRT doctrine restricts her from using maximum warps in populated areas due to disorientation and structural stress on buildings.

Enhanced Spatial Perception (Shard-assisted)

Implicit ability to perceive and mentally model spatial relationships to apply warps precisely, including through obstacles via shard sense. Not superhuman eyesight, but intuitive geometry.

Limit: Not precognition; still limited by human reaction time. Cannot sense through complete sensory deprivation.

Combat Utility - Redirection and Defense

Can bend incoming projectile trajectories by curving space, create elongated barriers by stretching ground, or compress distance to allow allies to strike from afar. Works with Clockblocker and Kid Win for combo tactics.

Limit: Cannot redirect Manton-protected targets directly; high-speed or high-energy attacks (e.g., Scion's stilling beam) overwhelm warping. Requires concentration, vulnerable to surprise attacks and Stranger/Master interference.

Accounts I
Trigger Event: The Biron Family CollapseWorm, Interlude 12.8 (Vista interlude) and WoG

2011 (in-universe 2008-2009, age 11)

Missy Biron triggers at age 11 after months of her parents' bitter, protracted divorce and custody battle. Trapped in a home where neither parent will leave and both use her as emotional leverage, she feels utterly without control over her own space — unable to escape the house or stop the fighting. Her shard (bud from a larger space-warping Entity) responds to the desire to make distance malleable: to push her parents apart or pull them together, to make the house bigger or smaller. She becomes Vista, immediately recruited into the Wards by the PRT, which presents itself as a stable family alternative. Establishes Vista as the youngest Ward and explains her core motivation: control over space as compensation for childhood powerlessness. Leads to long-term resentment toward PRT parentalism and informs her later critique of child cape recruitment in Ward.

Bank Robbery and Debut vs. the UndersidersWorm, Arc 3: Agitation 3.7-3.12

2011 (in-universe April 2011)

Vista participates in the Wards' response to the Undersiders' bank robbery in downtown Brockton Bay. Tasked with containing the area, she warps streets to prevent civilian entry and to cut off escape, but is outmaneuvered by Tattletale's planning and Grue's darkness. The event introduces her dynamic with the older Wards — eager to contribute, but assigned perimeter duty and told to stay back. She later expresses frustration at being treated as a child while being deployed to cape fights. First major in-costume set piece for Vista; establishes her tactical role and interpersonal frustration. Begins long-running rivalry/awareness of Skitter (Taylor Hebert), who notes Vista's youth.

Leviathan: The Endbringer Attack on Brockton BayWorm, Arc 8: Extermination 8.1-8.10

2011 (in-universe May 2011)

When the Endbringer Leviathan attacks Brockton Bay, Vista is deployed despite her age, as all hands are required. She uses large-scale warps to raise street barriers, create evacuation corridors by compressing distance for civilians, and stretch the battlefield to slow Leviathan's hydrokinetic advance. She witnesses the deaths of Gallant and Aegis and the near-death of many Protectorate heroes. Her warps help save lives but cannot stop Leviathan, who kills a quarter of the city's capes. Vista is shown vomiting and shaking after the battle, her first mass-casualty event. Major trauma and loss of innocence; loss of two Wards leaders forces Vista into a more senior role despite still being 12. City is devastated, leading to Warlord arc. Her performance cements her Shaker 9 rating in-universe.

Accounts II
The Slaughterhouse Nine in Brockton BayWorm, Arc 14: Prey 14.1-14.10 and Arc 15: Colony

2011 (in-universe June-July 2011)

The Slaughterhouse Nine — Jack Slash, Bonesaw, Siberian, Crawler, Cherish, Shatterbird, Burnscar, Mannequin — occupy the ruined city for a 'test' of Brockton Bay's capes. Vista is kept on base for much of the arc due to PRT concern for her age, which she bitterly resents. When Cherish (emotion manipulator) and Shatterbird attack, Vista defies orders to assist, warping the city to isolate Cherish's emotion blasts and to contain Shatterbird's glass storm. She is later part of the coalition that kills Cherish and participates in the hunt for Jack Slash, witnessing the Nine's psychological torture of other Wards (especially Browbeat and Kid Win). Deepens Vista's disillusionment with being sidelined; she begins to actively disobey PRT protective restrictions. Cherish's death and the Nine's defeat are pyrrhic, with lasting psychological scars on all Wards.

Gold Morning (Scion's Rampage)Worm, Arc 30: Speck 30.5-30.7 and Extinction interludes

2013 (in-universe 2013)

During Scion's extinction-level rampage across Earths, Vista — now 13 — fights as part of the massed cape army coordinated by Khepri (mind-controlled Skitter). She stretches and compresses continental-scale battlefields on the ruined Earths, creating corridors for artillery and evacuating wounded by shortening distances to portals. She survives Gold Morning, one of the few Brockton Bay Wards to do so, and is present at the final confrontation where Scion is destroyed via psychological attack using Eden's corpse. Survival marks her as a veteran at 13; sets up her role in Ward as a young adult hero grappling with post-apocalyptic reconstruction and survivor's guilt.

Ward: After Gold Morning - The Adult VistaWard, Daybreak 1.5, Infrared 5.3, Dying 15.8

2017-2020 (in-universe 2015-2017, age 16-18)

Two years after Gold Morning, Vista continues as a Protectorate hero in the new City (Gimel). Now 16-17, she is legally an adult cape but still struggles with being seen as 'the kid' by older heroes. She serves as a mentor to new young capes while openly criticizing the PRT's successor organizations for repeating the same patterns of child recruitment. In the assault on the Titans and during the Teacher and Goddess crises, she provides critical battlefield control, including warping the interior of the damaged Shardspace-adjacent prison and holding open evacuation routes during Titan attacks. Her arc culminates in acknowledging that her childhood was stolen and choosing to remain a hero on her own terms. Provides closure and maturation for the character; demonstrates long-term consequences of child soldiering in the Parahumans universe. Establishes Vista as one of the few Worm characters with a full coming-of-age arc across both serials.

Vista can teleport herself or others — She cannot teleport. She compresses distance so travel is shorter, but movement through space is still required. True teleportation (instant relocation) is not shown and is explicitly denied in WoG. What looks like teleportation is extreme compression.

Vista is the most powerful Ward or could solo an Endbringer — She is Shaker 9 (high), but her Manton limit and need for concentration make her support/control, not direct damage. Against Endbringers she is crucial for evacuation and battlefield shaping but cannot harm them directly. She is powerful in utility, not raw destructive output.

Vista chose her cape name to sound pretty or is named after Windows Vista — In-universe name was assigned/branded by PRT to reflect perspective/vista concept tied to her power. Out-of-universe, Wildbow chose it for thematic resonance with space and seeing. No connection to Microsoft product; Worm began 2011, well after Windows Vista (2006) but name is coincidental English word.

Vista is a happy, carefree kid hero — Canon portrays her as angry, lonely, and burdened by survivor's guilt and infantilization. Her cheerful moments are often performative for PR. Interlude 12 and Ward show deep resentment and trauma.

Vista's power works on anything, including people and Endbringer cores — Manton-limited: cannot warp living tissue or the space it occupies. Endbringer cores are also resistant due to exotic matter and shard physics. She can warp around them, not through them.

The Approach

In the annals of horror gaming, few survivors embody the quiet resilience of Jake Park, a Korean-born mechanic whose past is as shadowed as the fog that surrounds him.

Content advisory. The subject is a fictional character from a horror video game, depicted in violent and perilous situations. Descriptions of injury and psychological distress are part of the game's narrative and are presented for factual reference.
Identity

Jake Park

Pronunciation /dʒeɪk pɑːrk/

Known As

JakeThe Lone Wolf

Etymology

Jake is a common English given name, often a diminutive of Jacob. Park is a common Korean surname (박). The name likely reflects the character's mixed cultural background as a Korean-born individual with an English first name.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Behaviour Interactive, a Canadian company, for a global audience. The character is of Korean descent, but the representation is mediated through a Western game studio.

Disputed Identifications

No significant disputes over identity are known. Some fans speculate about his family connections, but these are unverified.

Description

Appearance

Jake Park appears as a young adult male of East Asian descent, with short dark hair and a somewhat rugged appearance. He wears a simple jacket and jeans, often with dirt or blood stains, reflecting his survivalist nature. His facial expression is typically serious or determined.

Symbols & Iconography

Associated with the 'Lone Wolf' archetype, symbolizing independence and self-reliance. His default outfit includes a worn jacket, and his perks are thematically linked to wilderness survival and stealth.

Behaviour & Personality

Jake is portrayed as a quiet, introverted individual who prefers to work alone. He is resourceful and pragmatic, often using unconventional methods to achieve his goals. In the game's lore, he is a skilled mechanic and survivalist, but he struggles with trust and social interaction.

Domains & Habitats

In-game, he is typically found in the various realms of the Entity, including forests, abandoned buildings, and other eerie locations. His backstory suggests a wealthy upbringing, but he rejects that life for the wilderness.

Version Differences

Jake has received cosmetic outfits over the years, including alternate skins such as 'The Bear Hug' and 'The Claw', which do not alter his core identity. His backstory has remained consistent since his introduction.

A perk that allows Jake to sabotage hooks without a toolbox, and reveals the auras of hooks when a survivor is carried.

Jake Park's Backstory

Source Dead by Daylight official loreCulture Video game culture, horror genrePeriod Contemporary (2016)

Summary

Jake Park was born into a wealthy Korean family but felt alienated by his privileged life. He left home to live in the wilderness, becoming a self-sufficient survivalist. He developed a reputation as a lone wolf, avoiding human contact. He eventually found himself in the Entity's realm, where he uses his survival skills to evade killers.

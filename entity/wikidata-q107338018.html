Who It Is

In the annals of horror gaming, few survivors embody the quiet resilience of Jake Park, a Korean-born mechanic whose past is as shadowed as the fog that surrounds him.

Jake is portrayed as a quiet, introverted individual who prefers to work alone. He is resourceful and pragmatic, often using unconventional methods to achieve his goals. In the game's lore, he is a skilled mechanic and survivalist, but he struggles with trust and social interaction.

Etymology — Jake is a common English given name, often a diminutive of Jacob. Park is a common Korean surname (박). The name likely reflects the character's mixed cultural background as a Korean-born individual with an English first name.

Aliases — Jake, The Lone Wolf

Powers
Iron Will

A perk that allows Jake to suppress his grunts of pain while injured, making him harder to locate.

Limit: The perk's effectiveness depends on the player's usage and the killer's perks. Historically, it was reworked to have a limited duration.

Calm Spirit

A perk that reduces the noise and aura reading from crows and other environmental disturbances, aiding stealth.

Limit: Does not affect all noise sources; some killer perks can override it.

Saboteur

A perk that allows Jake to sabotage hooks without a toolbox, and reveals the auras of hooks when a survivor is carried.

Limit: Sabotaging hooks has a cooldown, and the perk's effect can be countered by killer perks like 'Hangman's Trick'.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Jake Park appears as a young adult male of East Asian descent, with short dark hair and a somewhat rugged appearance. He wears a simple jacket and jeans, often with dirt or blood stains, reflecting his survivalist nature. His facial expression is typically serious or determined.

Symbols — Associated with the 'Lone Wolf' archetype, symbolizing independence and self-reliance. His default outfit includes a worn jacket, and his perks are thematically linked to wilderness survival and stealth.

Domains — In-game, he is typically found in the various realms of the Entity, including forests, abandoned buildings, and other eerie locations. His backstory suggests a wealthy upbringing, but he rejects that life for the wilderness.

Jake Park's BackstoryDead by Daylight official lore

Contemporary (2016)

Jake Park was born into a wealthy Korean family but felt alienated by his privileged life. He left home to live in the wilderness, becoming a self-sufficient survivalist. He developed a reputation as a lone wolf, avoiding human contact. He eventually found himself in the Entity's realm, where he uses his survival skills to evade killers. Jake's isolation and distrust shape his interactions with other survivors, making him a enigmatic figure.

Who It Is
Form

The Dragon Ogre Shaggoth is a massive, serpent-like creature with multiple limbs, often depicted as a fusion of dragon and ogre characteristics. Its body is said to be covered in scaly, armored plates, with glowing red eyes that seem to pierce through the darkness.

Powers

Chaos Breath The Shaggoth can unleash a blast of chaotic energy, dealing massive damage to its enemies and potentially corrupting the surrounding area with dark magic.

Accounts

The Shaggoth is said to have played a key role in the downfall of the Old World, using its chaotic powers to spread darkness and destruction throughout the land.

The Shaggoth is said to have migrated to the celestial realm of Azir, where it rose to prominence as a powerful and feared creature.

Who It Is

Ancient, towering beings that are part dragon, part ogre. Dragon Ogre Shaggoths stand 20 feet tall, with scales of iron, horns of brass, and elemental lightning crackling through their veins. They were once the servants of the Old Ones before they betrayed them, and they now serve the Chaos Gods with fanatical devotion.

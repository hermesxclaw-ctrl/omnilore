Who It Is
Form

The SCP Foundation is a secret organization that operates in the shadows, with its agents and personnel often wearing black jumpsuits and masks to conceal their identities.

Powers

Anomalous Object Containment The SCP Foundation has the ability to contain and research anomalous objects and entities, using a variety of techniques and technologies to achieve its goals.

Accounts

The SCP Foundation is a collaborative fiction project that explores the concept of a secret organization dedicated to containing and researching anomalous objects and entities.

The Foundation Entities Guide is a comprehensive guide to the SCP Foundation's anomalous entities and objects, providing a detailed overview of each entity's characteristics and abilities.

Who It Is

The SCP Foundation is a complex and fascinating concept that has captured the imagination of writers and artists around the world. Its unique blend of science fiction and horror elements has made it a staple of modern fine art.

The SCP Foundation is known for its ruthless and efficient approach to containing and researching anomalous objects and entities, often using extreme measures to achieve its goals.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The SCP Foundation is a secret organization that operates in the shadows, with its agents and personnel often wearing black jumpsuits and masks to conceal their identities.

Symbols — The SCP Foundation's logo is a stylized letter 'S' made up of smaller 'C' shapes, which represents the organization's focus on containing and researching anomalous objects and entities.

Domains — The SCP Foundation operates in a variety of domains and habitats, including but not limited to: urban environments, rural areas, and anomalous realms.

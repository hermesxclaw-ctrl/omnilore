SCP entities range from the terrifying (SCP-096, 'The Shy Guy' — kills anyone who sees its face) to the cosmic (SCP-3812, 'A Voice Behind Me' — a being that transcends narrative layers) to the strangely comforting (SCP-999, 'The Tickle Monster').

Cultural Significance

SCP represents the logical endpoint of the creepypasta phenomenon — a bureaucratic horror framework where cosmic terror is documented in clinical language, creating tension between the mundane format and the reality-shattering content. The Foundation itself (cold, utilitarian, 'the ends justify the containment') is as much a mythological entity as its prisoners.

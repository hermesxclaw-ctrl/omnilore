Who It Is

Among the demigods of the Imperium, Sanguinius stands apart—a golden-winged angel whose beauty masked a tragic curse, whose sacrifice sealed the fate of a galaxy. His story is one of nobility and sorrow, a legend carved in blood and light.

Sanguinius is portrayed as noble, compassionate, and deeply introspective. He struggles with the 'Red Thirst' and 'Black Rage'—genetic curses that afflict his sons. He is a skilled diplomat and warrior, beloved by his legion, and fiercely loyal to the Emperor.

Powers
Superhuman Physiology

As a Primarch, Sanguinius possesses immense strength, speed, endurance, and reflexes far beyond any Space Marine. He can withstand extreme trauma and is nearly impossible to kill in conventional combat.

Limit: He is mortal and can be killed, as evidenced by his death at the hands of Horus.

Wings

Sanguinius has large feathered wings that allow him to fly. They are not just decorative; they are functional and powerful.

Limit: The wings are a unique mutation or blessing; they may be vulnerable to damage.

Precognition

Sanguinius has the ability to see glimpses of the future, particularly his own death. He often has prophetic visions and dreams.

Limit: The visions are often vague and not always controllable.

Combat Mastery

He is one of the greatest warriors of the Imperium, wielding a spear or blade with unmatched skill. He is said to have defeated many champions.

Limit: None known.

Inspirational Aura

His presence inspires loyalty and courage in his allies, even in the face of overwhelming odds.

Limit: Not a supernatural power but a charisma effect.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Sanguinius is described as the most beautiful of the Emperor's sons, with angelic features, long golden hair, and large feathered wings. He stands taller than most Space Marines and radiates an aura of nobility and sadness. His eyes are often described as knowing and sorrowful.

Symbols — The Blood Angels' iconography includes the grail, the blood drop, and the winged figure of Sanguinius. His armor is adorned with gold and red, and his banner often features a chalice or a winged skull.

Domains — His homeworld is Baal, a desolate, radiation-scoured world. He also has a presence in the warp and in the memories of his sons.

Accounts I
The Great CrusadeHorus Heresy series and codexes

30th millennium

Sanguinius leads his Legion, the Blood Angels, on many campaigns to reunite human worlds under the Emperor's rule. He is known for his mercy and tactical brilliance. He earns a reputation as a hero and a symbol of hope.

Accounts II
The Horus HeresyHorus Heresy series

31st millennium

When Horus betrays the Emperor, Sanguinius leads his legion in defense of Terra. He confronts Horus aboard the Vengeful Spirit and is killed, but his sacrifice weakens Horus and allows the Emperor to strike the fatal blow. Sanguinius dies, becoming a martyr. His death triggers the Black Rage in his sons, and he is venerated as a saint.

The Black Rage and the Red ThirstCodex: Blood Angels

Ongoing

The Blood Angels suffer from genetic flaws that cause them to experience the Red Thirst (a craving for blood) and the Black Rage (a madness where they relive Sanguinius's death). Sanguinius himself struggled with these urges. The flaws are both a curse and a source of strength, as the Black Rage grants berserker fury.

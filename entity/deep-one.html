Who It Is
Form

The Deep Ones are described as human-shaped but with a fishy appearance, having gills, webbed hands and feet, and scaly skin. They are said to be approximately 5-6 feet tall, with a muscular build and a long, serpentine tail. Their eyes are described as being large and round, with vertical pupils. They have a strong, fishy odor and are said to be able to live underwater for extended periods of time.

Powers

Mind Control The Deep Ones are said to have the ability to control the minds of humans, using their dark magic to manipulate their emotions and actions.

Shape-shifting The Deep Ones are said to be able to take on the form and appearance of humans, allowing them to blend in with their surroundings and move undetected.

Telepathy The Deep Ones are said to be able to communicate with each other through a form of telepathy, allowing them to coordinate their actions and plan their attacks.

Accounts

The story follows a young man named Robert Olmstead, who travels to the seaside town of Innsmouth to investigate the strange occurrences that have been happening there. He soon discovers that the town is being overrun by the Deep Ones, who are using their dark magic to manipulate the minds of the townspeople.

Who It Is

The Deep Ones are a fascinating and terrifying creature, with a rich history and mythology. They are a central part of the Cthulhu Mythos, and have been featured in numerous works of fiction, including films, books, and games.

The Deep Ones are described as being highly intelligent and cunning, with a strong desire to dominate and control the minds of humans. They are said to be able to communicate with each other through a form of telepathy, and are able to manipulate the emotions and actions of humans through their dark magic.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Deep Ones are described as human-shaped but with a fishy appearance, having gills, webbed hands and feet, and scaly skin. They are said to be approximately 5-6 feet tall, with a muscular build and a long, serpentine tail. Their eyes are described as being large and round, with vertical pupils. They have a strong, fishy odor and are said to be able to live underwater for extended periods of time.

Symbols — The Deep Ones are associated with the worship of the Great Old One, Cthulhu, and are said to be able to summon him from his underwater lair. They are also associated with the use of dark magic and the manipulation of the minds of humans.

Domains — The Deep Ones are said to inhabit the underwater city of R'lyeh, where they worship Cthulhu and engage in dark rituals. They are also said to be able to inhabit the bodies of humans, taking on their form and appearance.

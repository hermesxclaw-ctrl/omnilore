Who It Is

An amphibious humanoid race living in undersea cities, worshiping Dagon and Hydra, and ultimately Cthulhu. They breed with coastal humans, producing hybrid offspring that transform into Deep Ones as they age — eventually joining their ancestors in the eternal sea. Immortal and patient, they await the rising of R'lyeh.

Form

Humanoid but frog-fish-like: scaled gray-green skin, large unblinking eyes, fish-like mouth, webbed hands and feet, gills on neck, hopping gait on land

Powers

Underwater breathing

Extreme longevity/immortality

Hybrid breeding

Gold crafting from sea materials

Strength superior to humans

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

Who It Is

A 9-foot-6-inch tall aristocratic woman infected with a special Cadou parasite by Mother Miranda. Lady Dimitrescu retains full intelligence, personality, and human appearance (at her preferred size) while gaining the ability to shapeshift into a swarm of flies, extend claw-like fingernails, and drain blood through a special ring to maintain her youth. She rules Castle Dimitrescu with her three 'daughters' (also Cadou-infected) and a cult of personality.

Powers

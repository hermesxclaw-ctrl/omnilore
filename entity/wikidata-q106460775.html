Who It Is

In the shadowed ruins of a lost civilization, one man's obsession with a golden idol set in motion a chain of betrayal that would echo far beyond the jungle—Atoq Navarro, a name whispered among treasure hunters as both a brilliant archaeologist and a ruthless killer.

Navarro is portrayed as intelligent, suave, and manipulative. He presents himself as a professional archaeologist but is driven by greed and a desire for power. He is cold-blooded, willing to betray allies and kill without hesitation to achieve his goals.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Atoq Navarro is a middle-aged man of Peruvian descent, with short dark hair and a trimmed beard. He typically wears a khaki shirt and cargo pants, practical for jungle exploration. His build is athletic, suggesting a life of fieldwork.

Symbols — No specific symbols associated with Navarro; he is often seen with a golden idol or a firearm, representing his greed and ruthlessness.

Domains — Primarily operates in the jungles of an unnamed island off the coast of South America, where the game's events take place. He also appears in urban settings such as a museum in the opening.

Accounts I
The Search for El DoradoUncharted: Drake's Fortune

2007

Atoq Navarro, working with Victor Sullivan, hires Nathan Drake to find the coffin of Sir Francis Drake, which leads to the discovery of a map to El Dorado. Navarro secretly works for Gabriel Roman, a wealthy treasure hunter. After finding the golden idol of El Dorado, Navarro betrays Drake and Sullivan, killing Roman to take control. He plans to sell the idol, but it turns out to be a cursed statue that turns people into mutant creatures. Navarro is eventually killed by Drake in a confrontation. Navarro's death ends the immediate threat, but the curse of the idol remains, and the island is left in ruins.

The Approach

In the shadowed ruins of a lost civilization, one man's obsession with a golden idol set in motion a chain of betrayal that would echo far beyond the jungle—Atoq Navarro, a name whispered among treasure hunters as both a brilliant archaeologist and a ruthless killer.

Content advisory. Depictions of violence, murder, and betrayal; mild language; themes of greed and moral corruption.
Identity

Atoq Navarro

Pronunciation AH-tok nah-VAR-oh

Known As

Navarro

Etymology

The name 'Atoq' is likely of Quechua origin, meaning 'fox' or 'rebel' in some interpretations, though not officially confirmed. 'Navarro' is a Spanish surname.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character created by Naughty Dog for the Uncharted series; not tied to real-world culture.

Disputed Identifications

None; the character is original, but some fans have speculated on inspirations from real-life treasure hunters, though no official confirmation exists.

Description

Appearance

Atoq Navarro is a middle-aged man of Peruvian descent, with short dark hair and a trimmed beard. He typically wears a khaki shirt and cargo pants, practical for jungle exploration. His build is athletic, suggesting a life of fieldwork.

Symbols & Iconography

No specific symbols associated with Navarro; he is often seen with a golden idol or a firearm, representing his greed and ruthlessness.

Behaviour & Personality

Navarro is portrayed as intelligent, suave, and manipulative. He presents himself as a professional archaeologist but is driven by greed and a desire for power. He is cold-blooded, willing to betray allies and kill without hesitation to achieve his goals.

Domains & Habitats

Primarily operates in the jungles of an unnamed island off the coast of South America, where the game's events take place. He also appears in urban settings such as a museum in the opening.

Version Differences

No significant version differences; the character appears only in the original game and is not featured in remasters or adaptations in altered form.

Stories & Myths

The Search for El Dorado

Source Uncharted: Drake's FortuneCulture Modern adventure fictionPeriod 2007

Summary

Atoq Navarro, working with Victor Sullivan, hires Nathan Drake to find the coffin of Sir Francis Drake, which leads to the discovery of a map to El Dorado. Navarro secretly works for Gabriel Roman, a wealthy treasure hunter. After finding the golden idol of El Dorado, Navarro betrays Drake and Sullivan, killing Roman to take control. He plans to sell the idol, but it turns out to be a cursed statue that turns people into mutant creatures. Navarro is eventually killed by Drake in a confrontation.

Who It Is

A seven-foot-tall knight encased in baroque, spiked armor, wielding a massive broadsword and shield. Darknut Commanders are the elite of a long-fallen warrior civilization, bound to eternal service through dark magic. Their armor must be shattered piece by piece before the creature within can be harmed.

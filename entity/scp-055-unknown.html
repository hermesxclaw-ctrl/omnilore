Who It Is

SCP-055 is something that causes anyone who studies it to forget all information about it. People know it exists — the containment chamber is there, records confirm it is contained — but no one can remember what it is, what it looks like, where it came from, or what it does. Notes written about it immediately become contradictory or meaningless. It is the foundational example of antimemetics within the SCP universe.

Form

Unknown. No one can remember. Descriptions have been recorded but are impossible to retain. It is known to be non-spherical (this is the only consistently retained fact).

Powers

Perfect antimemetic camouflage

Information suppression

Memory erasure

Record corruption

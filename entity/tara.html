Who It Is
Form

Tara is often depicted as a female figure with a blue or green skin tone, wearing a crown or a tiara, and seated on a lotus throne. She is typically shown with a benevolent expression, surrounded by a halo of light. Her hands are often in the mudra of granting blessings or holding a lotus flower.

Powers

Granting blessings Tara has the power to grant blessings and enlightenment to sentient beings. She is often depicted as a savior figure, helping beings to achieve liberation and spiritual growth.

Accounts

Tara was born from the tears of the Buddha Avalokiteshvara as he wept for the suffering of sentient beings. She was created to help beings achieve enlightenment and liberation.

Tara achieved enlightenment through her own efforts and the guidance of the Buddha Avalokiteshvara. She became a fully enlightened being, capable of helping others achieve liberation.

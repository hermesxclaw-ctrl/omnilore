Emi

Pronunciation The name is pronounced as 'eh-mee' in Japanese, with the 'e' sounding like the 'e' in 'bed' and the 'i' like the 'ee' in 'see'.

Etymology

Emi is a Japanese name that can be written with various kanji characters, each with its own meaning. Common meanings include 'blessing, beauty' (恵美), 'picture, beauty' (絵美), 'reflect, look' (映見), 'reflect, beauty' (映美), 'smile' (笑), 'prosperous, beauty' (栄美), 'blessing, not yet' (恵未), 'blessing, fruit' (恵実), and 'wisdom, beauty' (慧美). The name can also be written in hiragana or katakana. As a surname, it can be written as 江見 (bay, look) or 江美 (bay, beauty).

As a name, Emi does not have a physical appearance. However, the kanji used to write it often convey visual elements, such as 絵 (picture) and 美 (beauty), which may evoke imagery of artistic beauty.

Symbols & Iconography

The kanji characters themselves serve as symbols, each representing different concepts: 恵 (blessing), 美 (beauty), 絵 (picture), 映 (reflect), 笑 (smile), 栄 (prosperous), 未 (not yet), 実 (fruit), 慧 (wisdom). These characters are often used in Japanese calligraphy and art.

Behaviour & Personality

As a name, Emi does not have a personality. However, the meanings associated with the kanji suggest qualities such as beauty, blessing, wisdom, and prosperity.

Domains & Habitats

The name Emi is used in Japan and among Japanese-speaking communities worldwide. It is a common given name for females and occasionally used as a surname.

Who It Is

When the Supreme Kai himself goes pale at a name, you know it is not just another muscle-bound tyrant. Dabura — the horned Demon King whose spit turns heroes to stone and whose sword is conjured from nothing — ruled the entire Demon Realm until a frail wizard whispered a spell and made a king kneel.

Regal, coldly polite, and analytically cruel. Unlike Babidi's other minions (Pui Pui boastful, Yakon bestial), Dabura is composed, strategic, and speaks with courtly formality even while threatening. Supreme Kai describes him as 'the most terrible demon' who ruled the Demon Realm by fear, yet Dabura shows pragmatic deference to stronger power (Babidi, then Buu). He is proud of his status as king but willingly kneels to Babidi after being placed under Majin mind-control — indicating his will was subjugated, not volunteered. Name & Tongue

Etymology — Name pun on the incantation 'abracadabra' (abura-kadabura in Japanese). Toriyama's Majin-related characters follow magic-spell puns: Bibidi, Babidi, Majin Buu, and Dabura. Confirmed in Dragon Ball Daizenshuu 6 and Toriyama interview commentary on naming. The 'Dabura' spelling reflects Japanese phono

Pronunciation — Japanese: /daː.bɯ.ɾa/ (DAH-boo-rah, three morae). English dubs: Funimation /ˈdɑːbrə/ DAH-bruh; also heard as DAH-bra. Japanese voice maintains long middle vowel: Dābura.

Aliases — Dābura (ダーブラ), Dabra (official English manga/anime romanization), Maō Dabura (魔王ダーブラ, Demon King Dabura), King of the Demon Realm (Makai no Ō), Majin Dabura (when under Babidi's spell)

Ownership — Created by Akira Toriyama (1955-2024) for Weekly Shōnen Jump / Shueisha (Bird Studio). First appearance 1994. Intellectual property held by Shueisha, Toei Animation, and Bird Studi

Powers
Stone Spit (Sekika no Tsuba / Stone Spit)

Expels saliva that petrifies any target it touches, turning flesh, clothing, and even weapons into stone. Effect is instantaneous on contact; victim remains conscious as statue until shattered. Can be weaponized by spitting onto weapon or directly. Dabura can reverse it at will, but only while alive.

Limit: Requires saliva to make contact; can be dodged (Trunks dodges, Gohan deflects with armor). If Dabura dies, petrification becomes permanent unless killed with specific magic — in manga, Piccolo and Krillin remain stone after Dabura's death until Buu is killed and their statues are not shattered. Dabura must consciously generate spit; cannot petrify through energy barrier. No range beyond spitting distance.

Darkness Materialization / Materialization Magic (Bushitsu Busshitsu-ka / Darkness Sword)

Demonic magic that creates solid matter from nothing — most famously a long, flat-edged sword conjured from air, as well as spear and armor. Distinct from ki constructs; classified as magic (mahō) by Supreme Kai, not ki technique.

Limit: Requires concentration; sword can be broken by superior ki (Gohan snaps it). Magic weaker than Supreme Kai's but versatile. Cannot create living beings. In Daima, similar magic is common among Demon Realm denizens, reducing its uniqueness.

Evil Flame (Jaen) & Hell Blitz / Evil Impulse

Expels massive flame breath or fires multiple concentrated ki/magic blasts. Evil Impulse is a rapid-fire energy barrage used against Gohan. In anime, can generate dark energy waves from sword.

Limit: Flame can be repelled by ki barrier (Gohan deflects). Energy output below Super Saiyan 2 tier; Supreme Kai notes Dabura's power is roughly equal to Perfect Cell, insufficient against enraged SSJ2 Gohan.

Majin Enhancement & Flight / Ki Manipulation

Under Babidi's Majin spell, latent power drawn out beyond natural limits, granting enhanced strength, speed, durability, flight via ki, and ability to sense ki poorly (relies on Babidi's tracking). Can fire standard ki blasts and survive in space briefly inside spaceship.

Limit: Majin boost is finite; does not grant regeneration like Buu. Still vulnerable to significantly stronger fighters (Buu kills him effortlessly). Mind controlled — cannot disobey Babidi's direct orders despite tactical disagreement. Power roughly Cell-level, not Buu-level.

Afterlife Existence & Demon Physiology

As Demon Realm native, possesses longevity (hundreds of years), resistance to extreme environments, and retains consciousness after death in Other World. Horns and red skin are biological, not transformation.

Limit: Not immortal; can be killed by physical trauma (Buu's attack). Demon Realm natives are still mortal and subject to Other World judgment. No regeneration or soul manipulation attested.

Accounts I
King of the Demon RealmDragon Ball manga ch.446; Daizenshuu 7; Dragon Ball Daima ep.1-2

In-universe: centuries before Age 774; Publication 1994-1995 (manga), 2024 (Daima retcon)

Long before the Buu crisis, Dabura ruled the Makai (Demon Realm) as its absolute monarch (Dai Maō). Supreme Kai (Shin) describes him to Goku and others as the most powerful demon, whom even the Supreme Kais feared and could not defeat — 'the king who ruled the Demon Realm with terror.' He commanded countless demons from his palace in the Demon Realm's First World. Daima expands this: Dabura's father was King Abura, and Dabura inherited the throne, ruling until his departure to the mortal universe. His reign was characterized by order through fear rather than chaos. Establishes stakes for Babidi's invasion — if Babidi controls Dabura, he controls the Demon Realm's army. Explains why Shin is terrified upon sensing Dabura's ki on Babidi's spaceship. Retconned in Daima to set up succession crisis after his death.

Enslavement by BabidiDragon Ball manga ch.446-449

Age 774, weeks before 25th Tenkaichi Budokai; Publication 1994

The wizard Babidi, son of Bibidi, seeks to revive Majin Buu and needs powerful minions to gather energy. He locates Dabura in the Demon Realm and places him under his Majin mind-control spell (the 'M' crest), unlocking his latent evil power. Unlike Vegeta later, Dabura was already thoroughly evil, so control is complete and willing in effect — he kneels and pledges loyalty. Babidi transports Dabura, Pui Pui, and Yakon to Earth aboard his spaceship, hiding underground to siphon energy from strong fighters at the Tenkaichi Budokai. Dabura becomes Babidi's strongest warrior and de facto general, replacing the role Bibidi's minions once held. His presence allows Babidi to operate on Earth undetected by the Kais until Goku's group follows.

Ambush at the Tournament and Murders of Kibito and OthersDragon Ball manga ch.447-449; anime ep.220-222

Age 774, 25th Tenkaichi Budokai day

Babidi's group lures Goku, Vegeta, Gohan, Supreme Kai, and Kibito to the spaceship. Dabura, hidden, kills Kibito effortlessly with a ki blast as Kibito heals Gohan, then petrifies Krillin and Piccolo with Stone Spit when they intervene (Piccolo spits on his own arm and tears it off to avoid full petrification in anime filler; in manga both are fully petrified). He then retreats, allowing Babidi to teleport Goku's group inside the ship for staged battles to collect energy. Demonstrates Dabura's lethality and Stone Spit threat; removes two Z Fighters from immediate battle and provides energy via damage inflicted on Vegeta/Goku later. Establishes Dabura as tactical, not just brute.

Accounts II
Dabura vs. Gohan — The Demon King’s Last Stand as Babidi’s GeneralDragon Ball manga ch.460-463; anime ep.224-227

Age 774, Babidi's spaceship Level 3

After Pui Pui and Yakon are killed by Vegeta and Goku, Dabura faces Gohan on Babidi's third level. Dabura conjures his Darkness Sword, breathes Evil Flame, and spits toward Gohan, who shatters the sword and deflects flame. The fight is even — Gohan, as Super Saiyan, holds advantage but is rusty from seven years of peace and underestimates Dabura. Supreme Kai notes Dabura is 'about as strong as Cell.' Dabura deliberately goads Gohan, and Babidi uses the damage to collect energy to revive Buu. When Vegeta's pride is wounded by Gohan's struggle, Babidi senses Vegeta's evil heart and abandons Dabura's fight to possess Vegeta instead. Dabura advises against reviving Buu, warning Buu is uncontrollable, but Babidi ignores him. Energy from the fight (plus Vegeta's later damage) revives Majin Buu. Dabura's warning foreshadows his own death. Fight shows Gohan's post-Cell decline, a major character arc point. In anime, fight is notably longer, with Dabura dominating early.

Death at the Hands of Majin BuuDragon Ball manga ch.474; anime ep.232-233

Age 774, shortly after Buu's revival

Majin Buu is revived and immediately disobeys Babidi, refusing to kill Supreme Kai and the Saiyans on command. Dabura, disgusted, calls Buu a failure and attempts to assert authority, even trying to kill Buu with a blast. Buu, playful and annoyed, dodges, then turns Dabura into a cookie (or candy in some translations) with his transmutation beam and eats him. In manga, Buu inhales and bites; anime shows cookie transformation explicitly. Babidi is shocked but quickly placated when Buu obeys next command to kill. Dabura's petrification victims remain stone after his death. Ends Dabura's arc ironically — the Demon King killed by a childlike, stronger demon he helped revive. Removes Babidi's only voice of caution, leaving Buu unchecked. Enables later plot where Buu's rampage kills Babidi as well.

Afterlife Judgment — The Demon King Sent to HeavenDragon Ball manga ch.474 (King Yemma cameo), Daizenshuu 7, Dragon Ball Z anime ep.233 filler

Immediately after death, Age 774

In Other World, King Yemma (Enma Daio) judges Dabura. Noting that Dabura was under Babidi's mind control and thus not fully responsible for his Earth crimes, and that his time as Demon King, while cruel, was within Demon Realm law, Yemma sends him to Heaven (Tengoku) rather than Hell (Jigoku) — an unexpectedly merciful fate. Anime filler shows Dabura in Heaven, now docile, picking flowers and repenting, to Goku's surprise. This gag underscores Toriyama's theme that even demon kings can be rehabilitated when freed from evil influence. Provides comic coda and explains why Dabura never appears as a Hell villain in later arcs (unlike Frieza). Reinforces lore that Majin control mitigates karmic responsibility. Cited by guidebooks as canon example of Other World judgment nuance.

Dabura is a Majin (demon) created by Babidi or Bibidi like Majin Buu — Dabura is a natural Demon Realm native who existed long before Babidi; Babidi only placed a Majin mind-control spell on him. 'Majin Dabura' denotes enslaved status, not species. Majin Buu is a separate magical creation by Bibidi.

Dabura is the strongest villain before Majin Buu, stronger than Super Perfect Cell — Manga and guidebooks place him at Perfect Cell level, below Super Perfect Cell and SSJ2 Gohan at full power. Gohan's struggle was due to 7-year lack of training and hesitation, not Dabura surpassing Cell's peak.

Dabura went to Hell after death like other villains — King Yemma sent him to Heaven/Paradise because his Earth crimes were committed under Babidi's control, mitigating karmic guilt. Shown in anime filler and stated in Daizenshuu. This is unique among major arc villains.

Dabura's Stone Spit works on anyone, including Majin Buu or Vegito — Never tested on Buu (who can regenerate/transmute) and no evidence it would work on beings vastly stronger or with magic immunity. It requires saliva contact and can be dodged or blocked; Buu's candy beam overpowered Dabura before he could try.

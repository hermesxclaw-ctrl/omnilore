Who It Is

In the midst of a brutal interplanetary war, a fifteen-year-old pilot known only by a stolen name becomes the linchpin of a rebellion—a boy who embodies both the cold efficiency of a weapon and the fragile hope for peace.

Heero is initially portrayed as cold, emotionless, and completely devoted to his mission. He believes he is a tool for peace and has no personal desires. Over time, he develops a more human side, showing care for his friends and a desire to live. He is highly disciplined, skilled, and analytical, but struggles with self-worth and the morality of his actions.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Heero Yuy' is a pseudonym borrowed from a pacifist politician of the same name. The name itself is a playful transliteration: 'Heero' evokes 'hero', while 'Yuy' may be a nod to the Japanese pronunciation of the English word 'you' or a deliberate exoticism.

Pronunciation — HEE-roh YOO-ee (Japanese: Hīro Yui)

Aliases — ヒイロ・ユイ, Hīro Yui, Wing Pilot, The Perfect Soldier

Ownership — Japanese anime franchise, created by Sunrise and TV Asahi

Powers
Expert Mobile Suit Pilot

Heero is an exceptionally skilled pilot, able to operate the Wing Gundam and Wing Gundam Zero with precision and tactical brilliance.

Limit: His skills are limited to the capabilities of his mobile suit.

Strategic Genius

Heero often devises complex strategies and adapts quickly to changing battle conditions.

Limit: He sometimes acts impulsively when emotionally compromised.

Enhanced Physical Conditioning

He has undergone rigorous training, giving him above-average physical strength and endurance.

Limit: Still human; can be injured.

Zero System Compatibility

When piloting Wing Gundam Zero, Heero can use the Zero System, which predicts future outcomes and feeds data directly into his mind. He can process immense amounts of information and make split-second decisions.

Limit: The system can overwhelm the pilot's mind, causing mental instability if not properly handled.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Heero is a teenage boy with short, dark brown hair and blue eyes. He typically wears the Gundam pilot uniform: a white and blue suit with a red scarf. In civilian clothes, he often wears a simple white shirt and dark pants. His expression is usually serious and stoic.

Symbols — His iconic red scarf, which he wears in battle and often uses to cover his face. The Wing Gundam's silhouette is also associated with him.

Domains — He operates in space and on Earth, primarily within the colonies and Earth's surface. He is most at home in the cockpit of his Gundam.

Accounts I
Operation MeteorMobile Suit Gundam Wing

After Colony 195

Heero is sent to Earth as part of Operation Meteor, a plan by the colonies to strike against the Earth Sphere Alliance. He is tasked with destroying a mobile suit factory, but his mission is complicated by his encounter with Relena Peacecraft, who becomes a key figure in his life. Heero's actions and interactions with Relena set the stage for his internal conflict between his mission and his growing humanity.

Accounts II
The Battle of the ColoniesMobile Suit Gundam Wing

After Colony 196

Heero and the other Gundam pilots become embroiled in the power struggle between the Earth Sphere Alliance, the colonies, and the Romefeller Foundation. Heero repeatedly confronts Zechs and faces moral dilemmas about the use of violence. Heero is forced to confront the consequences of his actions and the nature of peace.

Endless WaltzMobile Suit Gundam Wing: Endless Waltz

After Colony 197

A year after the war, Heero and the other pilots are called back to action to stop a new threat from the Barton Foundation. Heero, now living a quiet life, must decide whether to return to violence. Heero chooses to fight to protect the peace he helped create, and he finally acknowledges his desire to live.

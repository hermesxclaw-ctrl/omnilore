Who It Is

General Radahn is one of Elden Ring's most spectacular and beloved demigods — a colossal warrior who, even after being driven mad by the Scarlet Rot, continues to hold the very stars in place through sheer force of gravitational magic. Radahn is the son of Radagon and Rennala (making him Radagon's stepchild-turned-demigod after Radagon's marriage to Queen Marika) and the brother of Rykard and Ranni. In his prime, Radahn was revered as the mightiest of all demigods: a giant of a man, nearly twice the height of a normal human, with flowing red hair and a leonine helmet, who rode into battle on a scrawny horse named Leonard — whom Radahn had learned gravity magic specifically to lighten so that he could continue riding his beloved steed despite his enormous size. This detail alone — a demigod who learned cosmic sorcery to avoid outgrowing his childhood horse — has made Radahn a fan favorite.

Radahn's greatest feat was the Conquering of the Stars: using gravity magic learned from an Alabaster Lord in his youth, Radahn cast a spell so powerful that it arrested the motion of every star in the sky above the Lands Between, holding them in place for centuries. This was not merely a display of power — it halted the fates written in the stars, including the destiny of his sister Ranni, and prevented cosmic entities like Astel from falling to earth. He was, in the most literal sense, holding back the cosmos.

During the Shattering, Radahn fought Malenia to a standstill in the Battle of Aeonia. Malenia, unable to defeat him, chose to bloom — unleashing the Scarlet Rot that transformed Caelid into a festering hellscape. Radahn was not killed but driven irreversibly mad, his mind consumed by rot. He now wanders the Wailing Dunes of Caelid, a berserk giant who has forgotten everything — his soldiers, his horse (whom he still rides), his purpose — yet his gravity magic still holds the stars in place, an unconscious reflex of a shattered will. His soldiers, the Redmanes, hold an annual festival where warriors gather to give Radahn the warrior's death he deserves, fighting him in an arena on the dunes. This is the context in which the player encounters him: a festival of death for a beloved general, a mercy killing disguised as a boss fight.

Mechanically, the Radahn fight is one of Elden Ring's most cinematic set-pieces. The player can summon an army of NPC allies (including Blaidd, Alexander the Warrior Jar, and Patches, who immediately flees) to charge across a vast desert battlefield while Radahn rains greatarrows from the sky. The second phase has Radahn launching himself into orbit and returning as a flaming meteor, an attack that has spawned countless reaction videos. Radahn's combination of martial prowess, tragic backstory, visual spectacle, and the unforgettable 'festival' framing has made his fight one of the most celebrated moments in gaming. The Shadow of the Erdtree DLC (2024) would later reveal Radahn in his prime, further cementing his legendary status.

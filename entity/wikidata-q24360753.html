Who It Is

Imagine a god so elusive that all we have left is a name whispered in old lists, a title that literally means 'Rainbow God.' Atua-anua is a ghost in the Polynesian pantheon — a being of brilliant color and sky-spanning power whose stories have faded like the arc of light itself after a storm.

No specific behaviors or personality traits are recorded in the available sources. In comparative Polynesian mythology, rainbow deities often serve as messengers between the celestial and terrestrial realms, or as omens of significant events. This is speculative and requires further research.

Who It Is

In the high steppes where the sky bends close to the earth, the Uyghurs once told of a primordial father, Ulug Han Ata, whose name echoes with the weight of creation itself. He is not merely a god of the heavens, but the ancestral root from which the Turkic peoples claim their spiritual lineage, a figure who bridges the celestial realm of Tengri and the mortal world of shamans and heroes.

As a creator ancestor, he is benevolent, wise, and powerful. He may serve as a mediator between the supreme god Tengri and humans, or be an aspect of Tengri himself. He is likely invoked in prayers for protection and fertility.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — From Old Turkic 'ulug' (great, supreme) + 'han' (khan, ruler) + 'ata' (father, ancestor). The name can be translated as 'Great Khan Father' or 'Supreme Ancestor Ruler'.

Pronunciation — /uˈluɣ ˈhɑn ɑˈtɑ/ (approximate; Uyghur: ئۇلۇغ خان ئاتا)

Aliases — Ulugh Khan Ata, Ulug Khan Ata, Ulu Han Ata, Ulug Ata

Ownership — Uyghur tradition, with parallels in other Turkic peoples (e.g., Altai, Siberian Turks). Often associated with Tengriist cosmology.

Form & Signs

Appearance — No detailed physical description survives. As a sky father, he is likely envisioned as an old, wise man with a white beard, robed in celestial light, residing in the upper sky. This is reconstructed from Turkic depictions of similar deities.

Symbols — Sky, eagle, mountain, and the cosmic tree (as in many Turkic cosmologies). The number 'nine' may be associated with him (nine skies in Turkic cosmology).

Domains — Heavenly realm, the sky, possibly the upper layer of the cosmic tree. He is not tied to a specific earthly location.

Accounts I
The Father of the UyghursUyghur oral tradition (recorded in modern collections)

Unknown; likely ancient

Ulug Han Ata is said to be the primordial ancestor from whom the Uyghur people descend. He may have emerged from the cosmic tree or been born from the sky, and he gave rise to the first Uyghur clans. The Uyghur people trace their lineage to him, giving them a sacred identity.

The Sky Father's CounselReconstructed from Turkic shamanic traditions

In shamanic rituals, the sky father is invoked for guidance and protection. Ulug Han Ata may be called upon in prayers to ensure the well-being of the community. The community receives divine favor and protection.

Who It Is

In the shadowed annals of forbidden magic, few names evoke such a potent mixture of reverence and dread as Satella—a figure whose legend is as much a warning as it is a mystery. Known in whispers as the Witch of Envy, she is said to have once consumed half the world in her insatiable hunger, yet her true story remains shrouded in contradiction and half-truths. Who was Satella, and what does her myth reveal about the fears and fascinations of the cultures that tell her tale?

Satella is portrayed as a tragic and enigmatic figure. She is said to have been a kind and gentle person before her descent into madness and envy. In her current state, she is depicted as a malevolent force that consumes the world, yet she also shows a deep love for the protagonist, Subaru Natsuki, and protects him in subtle ways. Her personality is contradictory: she is both a destroyer and a guardian.

Powers
Authority of Envy

Satella's primary power is the Authority of Envy, which grants her the ability to 'consume' anything, including concepts, memories, and even the world itself. It is said that she once consumed half of the world, leading to her sealing.

Limit: She is sealed within the Shadow Garden, limiting her ability to act directly in the world. Her power is also unstable and can cause her to lose control.

Time Manipulation (Return by Death)

Satella grants Subaru the ability to 'Return by Death', allowing him to rewind time to a previous point upon death. She is the one who activates this power, and she communicates with Subaru through it, often reminding him not to speak of it.

Limit: Subaru cannot speak about the ability to others without triggering Satella's wrath, which can lead to a temporary state of 'death' or even the destruction of those who learn the secret. The ability has a cooldown and is not always reliable.

Shadow Manipulation

Satella can control shadows, using them to attack or defend. In the anime, she is shown to emerge from shadows and to engulf others in darkness.

Limit: Her use of shadows is limited by her seal, and she can only manifest in specific circumstances, such as when Subaru is in extreme danger.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Satella is depicted as a woman of ethereal beauty with long silver hair and amethyst eyes, often wearing a black and purple witch's attire. In the anime, she appears with a veil or shadow obscuring her face, adding to her mysterious aura. Her appearance is strikingly similar to that of Emilia, the main heroine, which is a notable plot point.

Symbols — Satella is associated with the Witch's symbol, a stylized eye or a dark flame. In the series, she is often represented by a black shadow with glowing eyes. The color scheme of black and purple is used to denote her witch status.

Domains — Satella is primarily associated with the 'Shadow Garden', a dark dimension where she is sealed. She is also connected to the 'Witch of Envy' title, which places her among the witches of the series. Her influence extends over the world of Re:Zero, particularly through the 'Authority' of Envy that she grants to Subaru.

Accounts I
The Legend of the Witch of EnvyRe:Zero light novel, particularly volumes 1, 7, and later arcs

Fictional timeline, 400 years before the main story

According to the lore, Satella was once a sage who sought to save the world. However, she was corrupted by the Witch Factor of Envy, becoming the Witch of Envy. In her madness, she consumed half of the world, causing immense destruction. The other sages and heroes of the time managed to seal her away in the Shadow Garden, but the damage was done. Her legacy haunts the world, and her influence is felt through the Witch Cult that worships her. The world was left scarred, and the Witch Cult was formed to await her return. The sealing also created the 'Shadow Garden' as a prison. Her actions indirectly led to the events of the main story, as Subaru is summoned to this world and becomes entangled with her power.

Accounts II
Satella's Love for SubaruRe:Zero light novel, particularly arcs 4 and later

Main story timeline

Throughout the series, Satella demonstrates a deep, obsessive love for Subaru Natsuki. She speaks to him through the Return by Death ability, expressing her affection and urging him to stay alive. She also protects him indirectly, such as by preventing others from learning about his ability. The nature of this love is ambiguous—it may be genuine or a result of her madness. This love drives many of the plot's twists, as Satella's actions interfere with the plans of other characters. It also places a burden on Subaru, who must keep her a secret.

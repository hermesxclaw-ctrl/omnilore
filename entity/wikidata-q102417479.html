The Approach

In the shadowed annals of forbidden magic, few names evoke such a potent mixture of reverence and dread as Satella—a figure whose legend is as much a warning as it is a mystery. Known in whispers as the Witch of Envy, she is said to have once consumed half the world in her insatiable hunger, yet her true story remains shrouded in contradiction and half-truths. Who was Satella, and what does her myth reveal about the fears and fascinations of the cultures that tell her tale?

Content advisory. The subject involves themes of destruction, madness, forbidden power, and apocalyptic imagery. Some versions of the legend include references to self-harm and psychological torment. Reader discretion is advised.
Identity

Satella

Pronunciation sah-TEL-lah

Known As

Etymology

The name 'Satella' is likely derived from the Latin 'sate' or 'satelles', meaning 'attendant' or 'companion', though no official etymology has been given. In the context of the series, it may also be a play on 'Saturn' or 'Satana', but this is speculative.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese popular culture, specifically the 'Re:Zero' franchise created by Tappei Nagatsuki. The character is a fictional creation and is not part of any real-world cultural tradition.

Disputed Identifications

In the 'Re:Zero' fandom, there is significant debate about whether Satella is the same entity as the character Emilia, who bears a striking resemblance to her. Some fans theorize that they are separate beings, while others believe they are linked through reincarnation or possession. The author has not confirmed any definitive relationship, leaving this as a point of speculation.

Description

Appearance

Satella is depicted as a woman of ethereal beauty with long silver hair and amethyst eyes, often wearing a black and purple witch's attire. In the anime, she appears with a veil or shadow obscuring her face, adding to her mysterious aura. Her appearance is strikingly similar to that of Emilia, the main heroine, which is a notable plot point.

Symbols & Iconography

Satella is associated with the Witch's symbol, a stylized eye or a dark flame. In the series, she is often represented by a black shadow with glowing eyes. The color scheme of black and purple is used to denote her witch status.

Behaviour & Personality

Satella is portrayed as a tragic and enigmatic figure. She is said to have been a kind and gentle person before her descent into madness and envy. In her current state, she is depicted as a malevolent force that consumes the world, yet she also shows a deep love for the protagonist, Subaru Natsuki, and protects him in subtle ways. Her personality is contradictory: she is both a destroyer and a guardian.

Domains & Habitats

Satella is primarily associated with the 'Shadow Garden', a dark dimension where she is sealed. She is also connected to the 'Witch of Envy' title, which places her among the witches of the series. Her influence extends over the world of Re:Zero, particularly through the 'Authority' of Envy that she grants to Subaru.

Version Differences

In the light novel, Satella's appearances are more detailed and her backstory is more extensively explored. In the anime, some of these details are condensed or omitted. The web novel, which is the original draft, may contain additional scenes that were later revised. The English translation may have minor differences in dialogue and terminology.

Powers & Abilities

Authority of Envy

Source Version Light novel and anime

Description

Satella's primary power is the Authority of Envy, which grants her the ability to 'consume' anything, including concepts, memories, and even the world itself. It is said that she once consumed half of the world, leading to her sealing.

Limitations

She is sealed within the Shadow Garden, limiting her ability to act directly in the world. Her power is also unstable and can cause her to lose control.

Contradictions

Some sources suggest that the Authority of Envy is not her original power but was acquired from the Witch of Envy, a separate entity. This is not confirmed.

Time Manipulation (Return by Death)

Source Version Light novel and anime

Description

Satella grants Subaru the ability to 'Return by Death', allowing him to rewind time to a previous point upon death. She is the one who activates this power, and she communicates with Subaru through it, often reminding him not to speak of it.

Limitations

Subaru cannot speak about the ability to others without triggering Satella's wrath, which can lead to a temporary state of 'death' or even the destruction of those who learn the secret. The ability has a cooldown and is not always reliable.

Contradictions

It is unclear whether Satella grants this power out of love or as a form of control. Some fan theories suggest that the power is a curse rather than a gift.

Shadow Manipulation

Source Version Anime only (so far)

Description

Satella can control shadows, using them to attack or defend. In the anime, she is shown to emerge from shadows and to engulf others in darkness.

Limitations

Her use of shadows is limited by her seal, and she can only manifest in specific circumstances, such as when Subaru is in extreme danger.

Contradictions

None noted.

Stories & Myths

The Legend of the Witch of Envy

Source Re:Zero light novel, particularly volumes 1, 7, and later arcsCulture Japanese light novel/animePeriod Fictional timeline, 400 years before the main story

Summary

According to the lore, Satella was once a sage who sought to save the world. However, she was corrupted by the Witch Factor of Envy, becoming the Witch of Envy. In her madness, she consumed half of the world, causing immense destruction. The other sages and heroes of the time managed to seal her away in the Shadow Garden, but the damage was done. Her legacy haunts the world, and her influence is felt through the Witch Cult that worships her.

Participants

Satella

The other witches of sin

The Sage (Flugel)

The heroes of the past

Consequences

The world was left scarred, and the Witch Cult was formed to await her return. The sealing also created the 'Shadow Garden' as a prison. Her actions indirectly led to the events of the main story, as Subaru is summoned to this world and becomes entangled with her power.

Variants

In the web novel, there are additional details about the circumstances of her corruption and the exact nature of the sealing. The anime omits some of these details.

Satella's Love for Subaru

Source Re:Zero light novel, particularly arcs 4 and laterCulture Japanese light novel/animePeriod Main story timeline

Summary

Throughout the series, Satella demonstrates a deep, obsessive love for Subaru Natsuki. She speaks to him through the Return by Death ability, expressing her affection and urging him to stay alive. She also protects him indirectly, such as by preventing others from learning about his ability. The nature of this love is ambiguous—it may be genuine or a result of her madness.

Participants

Satella

Subaru Natsuki

Consequences

This love drives many of the plot's twists, as Satella's actions interfere with the plans of other characters. It also places a burden on Subaru, who must keep her a secret.

Variants

In the anime, the romantic undertones are more subtle, while in the light novel, they are more explicit.

Relationships

Subaru Natsuki

Type lover/benefactorSource Light novel and anime

Description

Satella has a profound love for Subaru, granting him the Return by Death ability. She communicates with him and protects him, though her methods are often terrifying. Their relationship is central to the series.

Version Note

In the anime, the relationship is less developed due to time constraints.

Conflicting Accounts

Some fans believe that her love is a form of manipulation, while others see it as tragic and genuine.

Emilia

Type lookalike/possible incarnationSource Light novel and anime

Description

Emilia bears a striking resemblance to Satella, leading to theories that she is a reincarnation or a clone. The series hints at a connection, but it is not fully explained. Satella may be using Subaru to protect Emilia, or she may be envious of her.

Version Note

The resemblance is a major plot point, but the exact relationship is left ambiguous.

Conflicting Accounts

Some believe Emilia is Satella's daughter or a separate entity, while others think they are the same being in different forms.

The Witch Cult worships Satella as their deity, believing in her return. They are a fanatical organization that causes chaos in the world, often acting in her name without her direct command.

Destruction of half the world

Source Light novel and anime

Description

Satella is said to have consumed half of the world in her rampage, leading to countless deaths and suffering. This is a central part of her legend and is depicted as a horrific event.

Scholarly Context

In the fictional universe, this event is treated as a historical catastrophe, and its details are used to justify the fear and hatred of witches.

Psychological torment of Subaru

Source Light novel and anime

Description

Satella's Return by Death ability forces Subaru to experience painful deaths and then relive them, causing significant mental trauma. She also threatens to harm those he cares about if he reveals the ability, creating a cycle of fear and guilt.

Scholarly Context

This is a narrative device used to explore themes of trauma and resilience in the series.

Self-harm and madness

Source Light novel (later volumes)

Description

In some scenes, Satella is depicted as harming herself or being in a state of extreme mental distress, suggesting that her own power consumes her. This is not explicitly shown but is implied.

Scholarly Context

This adds to her tragic character, showing that she is a victim of her own power.

Contradictions & Disputes

Identity of Satella vs. Witch of Envy

Position A

Satella is the Witch of Envy.

Position A — evidence

The series consistently refers to Satella as the Witch of Envy, and she is depicted as such in flashbacks.

Position B

Satella and the Witch of Envy are separate entities, with Satella having been a separate sage who was possessed or transformed.

Position B — evidence

Some characters in the series, such as Echidna, hint at a distinction, suggesting that the Witch of Envy may be a separate being that took over Satella.

Scholarly Consensus

The author has not provided a definitive answer, and the fandom remains divided. The most common interpretation is that they are the same entity, but the ambiguity is intentional.

Satella's love for Subaru

Position A

Satella genuinely loves Subaru.

Position A — evidence

Satella's actions, such as protecting Subaru and expressing affection, suggest genuine care.

Position B

Satella's love is a form of manipulation or is driven by madness.

Position B — evidence

Her methods are coercive, and she threatens Subaru to keep his ability secret, which is not typical of genuine love.

Scholarly Consensus

There is no consensus. The series presents her love as ambiguous, leaving room for interpretation.

Satella is a villain.

Satella is a tragic anti-hero or victim.

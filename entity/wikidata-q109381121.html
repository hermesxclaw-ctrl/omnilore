Who It Is

He was the last Honor Guard of a murdered world — seven feet of muscle, purple-gray fur, and barely contained rage, carrying a bo-rifle that is both rifle and ancient staff. Garazeb Orrelios survived the Empire's annihilation of Lasan when almost no other Lasat did, and then had to learn how to live again aboard a tiny freighter called the Ghost, among a Twi'lek pilot, a lapsed Jedi, a Mandalorian teenager, a kid who could touch the Force, and a murderously sarcastic astromech.

Initially presented as gruff, laconic, hot-tempered, and physically intimidating, Zeb uses sarcasm and growls to keep distance. He is deeply honor-bound, loyal to Hera as captain and to Kanan as de facto brother, and develops a teasing, older-brother dynamic with Ezra Bridger after early friction. His humor is dry and often physical. Underneath, he carries survivor's guilt and barely suppressed grief over Lasan; episodes 'The Honorable Ones' and 'Legends of the Lasat' reveal vulnerability, spiritual longing, and capacity for forgiveness. Name & Tongue

Etymology — Given name 'Garazeb' is presented in-canon as Lasat; out-of-universe, the character was developed from Ralph McQuarrie's early 1970s concept art for Chewbacca, which depicted a large, feline-leaning alien later re-imagined as the Lasat. 'Zeb' is a short form used by the Ghost crew. Surname spelling

Pronunciation — gah-RAH-zeb or-REL-ee-os /ɡəˈrɑːzɛb ɒˈrɛliɒs/; commonly shortened to Zeb /zɛb/. Lasat pronunciation is not separately codified in canon.

Powers
Lasat Physiology – Superhuman Strength and Durability

As a Lasat evolved on a high-gravity world, Zeb can lift and throw Stormtroopers, bend metal, punch through droids, and carry Ezra or Sabine with one arm. He survives falls, blaster glances, and prolonged hand-to-hand combat that would incapacitate a human. His digitigrade legs grant powerful leaps and sprints.

Limit: Not invulnerable; can be wounded by concentrated blaster fire, lightsaber, or heavy ordnance. Strength requires close range. Exhaustible – shown tiring after prolonged fights (e.g., versus Kallus in 'The Honorable Ones').

AB-75 Bo-Rifle Mastery (Honor Guard Weapon)

Expert in both modes: electro-bo staff for melee (capable of blocking lightsaber strikes briefly and delivering concussive shocks) and blaster rifle for ranged fire. Weapon is coded to Lasat physiology and resists use by non-Lasat. Zeb is one of the last trained Honor Guards, proficient in traditional Lasat martial forms.

Limit: Bo-rifle requires power cells; staff mode drains charge. Non-Lasat can fire rifle mode but cannot easily activate staff mode (Kallus eventually learns with Zeb's teaching). Weapon can be lost, damaged, or confiscated – Zeb is occasionally disarmed.

Hand-to-Hand Combat and Lasat Martial Arts

Formal Honor Guard training in Lasat bo-staff and unarmed combat, including grappling, joint locks, and use of claws. Defeats Imperial combat droids, Stormtroopers, and holds his own against Agent Kallus (Imperial Security Bureau hand-to-hand specialist) and briefly against other elite opponents.

Limit: Technique is less effective against Force-users (e.g., Darth Vader, Inquisitors) or overwhelming numbers. Emotional rage can make him predictable.

Tracking, Survival, and Navigational Instinct

Keen sense of smell and hearing, able to track scents and hear distant patrols. Lasat prophecy and instinct allow him (with Chava) to navigate to Lira San using ancient rituals, sensing the 'path' beyond the Outer Rim. Experienced survivalist on hostile terrain (Geonosis moon ice caves).

Limit: Tracking is not supernatural; can be fooled by environmental masking or Imperial sensor jamming. Navigation to Lira San required Chava's staff and ritual, not solo ability.

Piloting and Technical Operation (Limited)

Competent co-pilot and gunner aboard the Ghost, operates dorsal turret and ground transport. Less skilled than Hera or Kanan; often assigned muscle and security rather than flight. Can operate Imperial equipment and explosives with basic proficiency.

Limit: Not an ace pilot; defers to Hera. Technical slicing left to Sabine, Chopper, or Kanan.

Interpersonal Resilience – Forgiveness and Honor Code

Honor Guard code emphasizes protecting the innocent and honoring fallen Lasat. Zeb's arc demonstrates ability to extend honor to former enemy Kallus, breaking cycle of vengeance, which becomes plot-critical when Kallus defects and provides Rebellion intelligence as Fulcrum.

Limit: Code can be rigid; early season Zeb is vengeful and distrustful, needing external challenge to evolve. Not a superpower but narratively treated as defining ability.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Zeb is a Lasat male, approximately 2.3 meters (7 ft 5 in) tall, powerfully built with digitigrade legs, large hands with four digits, and broad feet. His fur is predominantly gray-purple with a lighter ventral patch, darker striping on limbs, and a mane-like head with a pronounced muzzle, green-yellow eyes with horizontal pupils, and large pointed ears. He has prominent fangs and a guttural, resonant voice (Steve Blum). He typically wears an oran

Symbols — Primary symbol is the AB-75 bo-rifle, the traditional weapon of the Lasan Honor Guard. It functions as both electro-staff and blaster rifle and is keyed to Lasat bio-signature; only a Lasat (or exceptionally strong Force-user) can easily wield its staff mode. The Honor Guard's ceremonial armor and helmet (seen in flashbacks and in 'Legends of the Lasat') feature Lasat glyphs. Zeb's personal iconography includes Honor Guard tat

Domains — Native habitat: Lasan (also spelled Lasan), a Outer Rim world with high gravity (explaining Lasat strength) and honor-guard culture, devastated by Imperial bombardment and ground assault. Zeb lives aboard the VCX-100 light freighter Ghost as home and base, operating from Lothal, Atollon, and Yavin 4 during the early Rebellion. Accounts I
The Fall of Lasan – Last of the Honor GuardStar Wars Rebels – dialogue and flashbacks in 'Droids in Distress' (S1E3), 'The Honorable Ones' (S2E17), and 'Legends of the Lasat' (S2E12); supplementary Databank and Visual Guide

c. 19-18 BBY (shortly after Clone Wars, depicted via recollection 4-5 years later in 5 BBY)

Zeb served as Captain of the Lasan Honor Guard, protectors of the Lasat royal family and keepers of the bo-rifle tradition. When the Empire rose, Imperial Security Bureau Agent Alexsandr Kallus, under orders, deployed Imperial forces with disruptor-type weapons (banned by the Senate) to Lasan, massacring the Lasat population. Zeb was one of very few survivors, escaping with his bo-rifle. He believed himself possibly the last Lasat, carrying intense survivor guilt and hatred for Kallus, whom he holds personally responsible. The event shapes his entire motivation aboard the Ghost. Near-extinction of Lasat on Lasan; Zeb's exile and recruitment into early rebellion; establishes central vendetta with Kallus that later inverts into reconciliation; motivates search for any surviving Lasat.

Spark of Rebellion – Joining Spectre CellStar Wars Rebels TV film/pilot 'Spark of Rebellion' (S1E1-2, 2014), novelization by Michael Kogge

5 BBY

On Lothal, Zeb operates as Spectre-4, the muscle of Hera Syndulla's Ghost crew alongside Kanan Jarrus, Sabine Wren, Chopper, and later Ezra Bridger. The crew steals Imperial crates containing Wookiee prisoners and disrupts Imperial operations. Zeb initially clashes withEzra, a 14-year-old street kid who steals his bo-rifle and later joins the crew. Zeb's role is to provide brute force, distraction, and loyalty during heists against Minister Maketh Tua and Agent Kallus. Formation of the Lothal rebel cell that becomes nucleus of Phoenix Squadron; Zeb gains a surrogate family; establishes recurring rivalry with Kallus and comedic rivalry/friendship with Ezra and Chopper.

Fighter Flight and Early Lothal Operations – The Meiloorun and the Imperial AcademyStar Wars Rebels S1E4 'Fighter Flight', S1E6 'Breaking Ranks', S1E10 'Path of the Jedi' (cameo)

5 BBY

In 'Fighter Flight', Zeb and Ezra are sent to acquire meiloorun fruit, leading to Zeb teaching Ezra about Lasat humor and sharing rations with displaced farmers, showing his compassionate side beneath gruff exterior. In 'Breaking Ranks', Zeb supports Ezra's infiltration of the Imperial Academy. These vignettes build Zeb as moral anchor who, despite complaining, risks himself for civilians and for Ezra's growth. His banter with Ezra ('kid') evolves from annoyance to brotherly teasing. Character development establishing Zeb's soft center and mentorship role; cements Ghost crew as family rather than mere cell.

Accounts II
The Honorable Ones – Ice Moon of Bahryn and Reconciliation with KallusStar Wars Rebels S2E17 'The Honorable Ones' (2016)

3 BBY

After a failed assault on an Imperial depot, Zeb and Kallus are stranded together on the ice moon Bahryn (orbiting Geonosis) after escaping a damaged escape pod. Forced to cooperate to survive the frigid caves and hostile knobby white ice-creatures (bonzami), they share stories. Kallus reveals his guilt over Lasan and that he used a Lasat bo-rifle (J-19) taken as trophy, which he can barely wield. Zeb, invoking Lasat honor, spares Kallus and shares warmth via Lasat fur and body heat, and ultimately saves him from falling. Kallus is moved by Zeb's honor and begins questioning the Empire. Zeb gifts Kallus his bo-rifle's power? Actually Zeb allows Kallus to keep the bo-rifle, signaling respect. Pivotal redemption arc for Kallus, who later becomes Rebel informant Fulcrum, providing critical intelligence on Lothal and Thrawn. Zeb's capacity for forgiveness becomes key to Rebellion's intelligence advantage; their bond becomes one of the series' most praised arcs.

Legends of the Lasat – The Prophecy and Lira SanStar Wars Rebels S2E12 'Legends of the Lasat' (2016)

3 BBY

Two surviving Lasat, Chava the Wise and Gron, are rescued by the Ghost crew. Chava, keeper of Lasat prophecy, recognizes Zeb as Honor Guard and reveals that Lasan was not the original Lasat homeworld; ancient prophecy speaks of Lira San, a safe world beyond the Outer Rim. Using Chava's staff and a ritual that requires Zeb's bo-rifle, and with Ezra's Force-assisted navigation through a collapsing star cluster, the crew navigates past Imperial blockade to Lira San, discovering a thriving Lasat civilization. Zeb is welcomed as a returned guard, weeps at finding his people alive, and is offered to stay, but chooses to remain with the Ghost crew to fight the Empire, promising to return. Retcons Zeb from 'last of his kind' to 'bridge to surviving culture'; resolves survivor guilt; establishes Lira San as canonical Lasat homeworld; Zeb chooses found family over immediate return, deepening his commitment to Rebellion.

Liberation of Lothal and Disappearance of Ezra – Family Reunion and FarewellStar Wars Rebels S4E15-16 'Family Reunion – and Farewell' (2018)

1 BBY (shortly before Rogue One / A New Hope)

Zeb participates in the final Lothal campaign against Governor Pryce and Grand Admiral Thrawn. He leads ground assaults, infiltrates the Imperial Dome with Kallus (now fully rebel), and helps free Lothal citizens. During the Dome's launch sequence, Zeb, Kallus, and Rex fight to hold the shield generator. After Kanan's death in 'Jedi Night', Zeb supports the crew's grief and helps Ezra execute the plan to summon the purrgil to remove Thrawn's fleet. After Lothal is liberated, the epilogue (narrated by Sabine) reveals Zeb and Kallus traveled together to Lira San, where Kallus is welcomed as an honorary Lasat – Zeb brings his former enemy home, completing his arc from vengeance to kinship. Lothal liberated before Battle of Yavin; Ezra and Thrawn lost to hyperspace via purrgil; Zeb's post-war fate linked to Lira San and Kallus, confirming long-term survival.

New Republic Service – Adelphi Base (Post-Endor)The Mandalorian Chapter 21: 'The Pirate' (S3E5, 2023)

c. 9 ABY

Years after Endor, Zeb appears as an officer/pilot serving the New Republic at Adelphi Base, alongside Carson Teva and Garazeb's old Rebel network. He answers a distress call from Nevarro, indicating he remains in military service rather than retiring to Lira San permanently. His appearance confirms Zeb survived the Galactic Civil War, transitioned to New Republic Defense Forces, and maintains contact with former Spectres (Hera is now General). Voiced again by Steve Blum, now in live-action suit. Bridges Rebels to Mandalorian/Ahsoka era; confirms Zeb's longevity and canonical live-action design; sets up potential appearance in Ahsoka Season 2 or related New Republic stories.

Zeb is a Wookiee or a Wookiee variant. — Zeb is a Lasat, a distinct species with feline-canine features, digitigrade legs, and different culture. He shares design ancestry with Wookiees because both derive from Ralph McQuarrie's early Chewbacca concepts, but in canon they are separate species with different homeworlds, languages, and biology.

Zeb died before the Original Trilogy or was killed by Thrawn. — Zeb survives the entire Galactic Civil War. Rebels epilogue (1 BBY) shows him on Lira San, and The Mandalorian (9 ABY) shows him alive and serving the New Republic. He is not killed by Thrawn; Thrawn and Ezra are removed by purrgil, not Zeb.

Zeb is Force-sensitive because he can use the bo-rifle and sense Lira San. — Zeb is explicitly not Force-sensitive. The bo-rifle is keyed to Lasat physiology, not the Force, and the navigation to Lira San relied on Chava's prophecy, ancient Lasat ritual, and Ezra's Force assistance – Zeb contributed cultural knowledge, not Force power.

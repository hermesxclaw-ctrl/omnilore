Who It Is

Nobara Kugisaki doesn't ask for permission to be herself. Armed with a hammer, a pouch of nails, and a sharp tongue, she strides into the world of jujutsu sorcery with a country girl's grit and a city girl's ambition—refusing to be defined by anyone else's expectations, even when facing a curse that can reshape her very soul.

Nobara is brash, confident, and outspoken. She is fiercely independent and refuses to conform to gender expectations. She is logical and strategic in battle, but can be judgmental and blunt in social situations. She cares deeply for her friends and is willing to risk her life for them. She has a high pain tolerance and remains calm under pressure.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Her surname Kugisaki (釘崎) combines kugi (釘, 'nail' or 'peg') and saki (崎, 'cape' or 'promontory'), but Akutami specifically noted the 'nail' meaning to reflect her weapon and technique. Her given name Nobara (野薔薇) combines no (野, 'wild') and bara (薔薇, 'rose'), symbolizing her wild and refined sides.

Pronunciation — NO-ba-ra KOO-gi-sa-ki

Aliases — Nobara Kugisaki

Ownership — Japanese manga and anime, created by Gege Akutami for Shueisha's Weekly Shōnen Jump.

Powers
Straw Doll Technique

Nobara's innate Cursed Technique. She uses a straw doll and a piece of her opponent (e.g., hair, nail, blood) to link them. By striking a nail into the doll with her hammer, she inflicts damage on the linked target from a distance.

Limit: Requires a piece of the target and a straw doll. The connection can be broken if the doll is destroyed or the target's piece is removed.

Resonance

An extension of the Straw Doll Technique. By striking a nail into the doll, Nobara amplifies the damage, causing severe internal injuries to the target. She can also use it on a target's severed body part to damage the whole body.

Limit: Requires a direct link to the target. The effect is negated if the target is immune to cursed energy or has a barrier.

Hairpin

Nobara can embed nails into objects or the ground and detonate them remotely using cursed energy, creating powerful explosions.

Limit: Limited by the number of nails she carries and her cursed energy reserves.

Black Flash

A high-level cursed energy manipulation technique. When cursed energy is applied within 0.000001 seconds of a physical hit, it creates a spatial distortion, amplifying the damage exponentially. Nobara has demonstrated this ability.

Limit: Extremely difficult to perform consistently; requires precise timing and concentration.

Improvised Technique Usage

In the light novel, Nobara is shown to improvise when her standard tools are unavailable. She uses her heel as a nail and a rope as a doll to perform the Straw Doll Technique.

Limit: Requires creativity and a substitute for the nail and doll.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Nobara is a teenage girl with medium-length orange hair, often styled with bangs and a small braid on one side. She has brown eyes. She wears the Tokyo Jujutsu High uniform: a dark blue high-collared jacket with gold buttons, matching skirt, and black shoes. She often carries a hammer and a pouch of nails. Her appearance is athletic and confident.

Symbols — Her primary symbol is the nail and hammer, referencing her Straw Doll Technique and the traditional ushi no koku mairi curse ritual. The wild rose in her name also serves as a personal symbol, representing her fierce independence.

Domains — As a student at Tokyo Jujutsu High, she operates primarily in Tokyo, but her hometown is Morioka, Iwate Prefecture. She is often seen in urban environments, battling curses in buildings and streets.

Accounts I
Introduction and First MissionJujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 3-4

2018

Nobara arrives in Tokyo and is introduced to Yuji Itadori and Megumi Fushiguro by Satoru Gojo. She aids Yuji in rescuing a child from a cursed spirit in an apartment complex, showing her combat skills and her pragmatic attitude. The trio begins to form a bond, and Nobara establishes her role as a capable sorcerer.

Kyoto Goodwill Exchange EventJujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 63-64

2019

During the exchange event against Kyoto Jujutsu High, Nobara voices her frustration over gender expectations in the jujutsu world. She is rendered unconscious by Mai Zen'in's rubber bullet, but not before showcasing her determination. Her unconsciousness prevents her from participating further, but she later recovers.

Death Paintings ArcJujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 58-63

2019

Nobara, Yuji, and Megumi investigate the special-grade cursed wombs known as Death Paintings. They are attacked by one, and Nobara helps defeat it, but realizes too late that they were human-origin. She tells Yuji she has no regrets about killing humans. The trio's bond deepens, and Nobara shows her resolve.

Accounts II
Shibuya IncidentJujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 124-125

2020

During the large-scale attack on humans, Nobara battles Mahito. She miscalculates and is touched by Mahito, causing the right side of her face to be disfigured. She experiences memories of her friends and her regret over Saori. She appears to die, but Arata Nitta partially halts the technique, leaving her with a chance of survival. Nobara falls into a coma and is presumed dead by many, including the audience, until later.

Return and Final BattleJujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 232-238, 258-259

2024

Near the end of the battle against Sukuna, Nobara awakens from her coma and uses Resonance on Sukuna's last finger, weakening him and allowing Yuji to land a final Black Flash. Megumi is freed, and the trio reunites. Sukuna is defeated, and the trio reads Gojo's letters. In the epilogue, Nobara reunites with her mother and grandmother.

Jujutsu Kaisen: Thorny Road at DawnLight novel

2020

Nobara is the protagonist of this spinoff novel, which explores her past, her relationship with Saori, and her time before joining Jujutsu High. It also features her improvising her technique in a battle. Provides deeper insight into Nobara's character and backstory.

Who It Is

The deified Sea whose chaotic waters represent the primordial forces of disorder that threaten cosmic stability. In Ugaritic myth, Yam (also called Prince Sea and Judge River) demands the assembly of El recognize his supremacy and hand over Baal as tribute. Armed with two divine clubs named 'Chaser' and 'Driver' — forged by the craftsman god Kothar — Baal engages Yam in a mythic battle that establishes order over chaos. This combat prefigures later Near Eastern and biblical sea-monster conflicts (Tiamat vs. Marduk, Leviathan vs. Yahweh). Yam's defeat establishes Baal's kingship and the principle of celestial sovereignty.

Who It Is
Form

Yam is often depicted as a powerful, stormy sea god, with the ability to control the tides and the forces of nature.

Powers

Control over the sea and the forces of nature Yam has the power to control the tides, the winds, and the waves, as well as the ability to summon storms and whirlwinds.

Accounts

In this myth, Inanna, the Sumerian goddess of love and war, descends into the underworld, where she is met by Yam, who serves as the gatekeeper of the dead.

In this epic, Gilgamesh, the king of Uruk, embarks on a quest for immortality, which takes him to the underworld, where he encounters Yam, who serves as a guardian of the dead.

What We Don't Know

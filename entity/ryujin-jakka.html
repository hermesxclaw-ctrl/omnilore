Who It Is

Captain-General Genryūsai Shigekuni Yamamoto's fire-type zanpakutō, considered the oldest and most powerful fire-attributed weapon in Soul Society. Shikai releases flames that can reduce anything to ash. Bankai, Zanka no Tachi, concentrates the flames into the blade itself, raising its temperature to 15 million degrees Celsius — the heat of the sun's core.

Powers

Fire manipulation, sun-level heat in Bankai, ash creation

Who It Is

You die on a Shibuya scramble crossing you never actually crossed — and then you wake up, phone buzzing with a mission you have seven days to complete or be erased. That is Rindo Kanade's morning, and his only way out is a power he doesn't understand: the ability to rewind time itself, but only when regret is strong enough to break him.

Attested in game script: Initially anxious, passive, conflict-avoidant, and dependent on best friend Fret ("Fret, what do I do?"). Prone to overthinking, doom-scrolling on phone, and deferring decisions. Character arc is deliberate coming-of-age: learns to take initiative, trust own judgment, and act without consensus. Values friendship, loyalty, and responsibility; guilt-driven after failures to save others. Shows empathy for strangers and Reapers alike, but also stubbornness when convinced of a moral choice. Name & Tongue

Etymology — Given name Rindō (りんどう) is Japanese for 'gentian,' specifically Gentiana scabra var. buergeri, a deep-blue autumn flower. In hanakotoba (Japanese flower language) rindo means justice, sincerity, and victory. Surname Kanade (奏) means 'to play music / to achieve.' Combined reading evokes 'playing/achi

Pronunciation — Japanese: [ɾʲin.doː ka.na.de] — rin-DOH kah-nah-deh. English localization approximates 'RIN-doh kuh-NAH-deh.' Pitch accent: Rindō (heiban, flat) in standard Tokyo dialect.

Powers
Replay (Time Rewind / Time Leap)

Innately awakened psych allowing Rindo to rewind time to a branching point after experiencing a future where allies die or fail. Triggered by intense regret/emotional shock; visualized as rewinding clock and rewound phone timer. Allows him to retain memories of the erased future and alter actions.

Limit: Cannot control activation at will initially; requires strong emotional trigger and is limited to short-term rewind (hours to a day, later up to several days). Cannot rewind beyond his own death/erasure point. Each rewind creates new timeline branch; previous future is erased. Exhausting; repeated use causes nosebleed, disorientation. Cannot resurrect those erased by the Composer's higher-level erasure without additional pact power.

Pact and Psych Pin Channeling

As a Player in the Reapers' Game, forms a Pact with Fret (later expanded to Nagi, Minamimoto) enabling shared HP, combo attacks, and pin psychs. Can equip up to 6 psych pins to manifest attacks (pyrokinesis, shockwave, summon, etc.). Psychs are not innate except Replay; pins are external amplifiers.

Limit: Requires pact partner within circle; if partner is downed, pact breaks. Pins have cooldowns and require groove meter. Cannot use psychs outside UG without pin and pact. Power level scales with friendship/beat drop, not individual training.

Scan and Imprinting (Reaper-adjacent abilities)

Can Scan thoughts of RG people (hearing inner voices as text bubbles) and, with Nagi's Dive, enter minds. Later learns to Imprint ideas into RG individuals via whispering keywords, influencing actions.

Limit: Scan only works in UG; Imprinting requires correct keyword and is resisted by strong-willed targets. Cannot read Angels/Reapers of higher rank fully.

Groove / Beat Drop Super Attack

Team super move when Groove meter reaches 100%, unleashing massive area attack with all party members. Rindo initiates with his pin's finisher.

Limit: Requires full Groove; single use per buildup; vulnerable during animation if mistimed.

Accounts I
Week 1 Day 1: The Reapers' Game BeginsNEO: The World Ends With You, Week 1

2021 (fiction set contemporary 2021)

Rindo and his best friend Tosai 'Fret' Furusawa are caught in Shibuya's Scramble Crossing when a strange pin forces them to Scan and they are hit by a truck — awakening in the Underground (UG) as Players in the Reapers' Game. Reaper Shoka Sakurane gives them 7 days to complete missions or be erased. Their first mission: survive Noise attacks. Rindo discovers a mysterious pin from 'Swallow' that will later trigger Replay. Establishes pact system, introduces Replay foreshadowing, and binds Rindo to Game rules. Sets up mystery of Swallow's identity.

Week 1 Day 4-5: The Death of Fret and First ReplayNEO: The World Ends With You, Week 1

2021

After failing to save a fellow Player, Fret is erased before Rindo's eyes during a mission at Tower Records. Overwhelmed by regret, Rindo's pin glows and time shatters — he rewinds to the morning, retaining memory of Fret's death. On second loop, he uses foreknowledge to change Fret's actions and prevent the erasure, revealing his time-rewind psych for the first time to the player. First explicit use of Replay; establishes core mechanic and moral burden: Rindo must live with memories of futures where friends die. Teaches player that failure is narrative, not game over.

Week 2: The Ruinbringers and Shibuya's FracturingNEO: The World Ends With You, Week 2

2021

The Wicked Twisters (Rindo, Fret, Nagi) face the Ruinbringers, a rival team led by Susukichi. Rindo's indecisiveness causes a split — Fret accuses him of never deciding for himself. During the Ruinbringers' assault on Dogenzaka, Rindo rewinds multiple times but each fix creates new casualties, learning that Replay cannot solve problems without decisive action. Nagi's Dive reveals Rindo's self-doubt as Noise. Character growth: Rindo begins to lead. Lore reveals Ruinbringers are former Players manipulated by Reaper Shiba Miyakaze.

Accounts II
Week 3: Shinjuku's Erasure and Swallow's TruthNEO: The World Ends With You, Week 3

2021 (flashback to 3 years prior)

Rindo learns that Shinjuku was erased by the Composer Shiba Miyakaze after its Reapers' Game collapsed, and that 'Swallow' is actually Kanon Tachibana, a former Shinjuku Reaper who befriended Rindo online as his only confidant. Kanon sacrificed herself to send Rindo the pin that awakened Replay, hoping he could prevent Shibuya's erasure. Rindo rewinds to Shinjuku's final day in a vision and witnesses the city's erasure, understanding the stakes: if Shibuya's Game fails, it too will be erased from the RG and UG. Recontextualizes Rindo's power as gift born of sacrifice; raises stakes from personal survival to city-wide salvation. Explains why Rindo was chosen despite being ordinary.

Final Day: The Choice to Rewind Shibuya ItselfNEO: The World Ends With You, Final Week

2021

On Day 7, Shiba initiates Shibuya's erasure. The Wicked Twisters defeat Shiba, but the Composer's authority passes to Rindo's team. The Angels offer Rindo a choice: become Composer and rule Shibuya's UG, or rewind time to before the Game and give everyone a second chance at life, erasing his own heroic status. Rindo chooses Replay on a city scale — rewinding Shibuya to a week prior, resurrecting erased Players including Kanon, at cost of his memories of the Game fading. Post-credits scene shows Rindo feeling déjà vu at Scramble Crossing, gentian pin still in pocket. Resolution of arc: Rindo finally acts decisively for others, not from indecision. Sets up thematic closure: heroism is choosing ordinary life for others. Secret ending hints memories may return.

Another Day: Interdimensional CameoNEO: The World Ends With You - Another Day (post-game DLC / crossover scenario)

2021

In a non-canonical bonus chapter, Rindo meets Neku Sakuraba (protagonist of original TWEWY) in a parallel Shibuya. They compare psychs and Rindo learns his Replay is evolution of Neku's own time-linked growth. Playful meta-story acknowledges legacy without affecting main canon. Fan service and lore bridge; confirms Rindo exists in same cosmology as original game but is not successor in bloodline.

Rindo is Neku Sakuraba's brother / son / reincarnation — No familial relation. They are separate protagonists in same universe, different Shibuya Reapers' Games 3 years apart. They meet only in non-canonical Another Day. Official materials explicitly state no blood relation.

Rindo's power is to 'reset the game' like a save file, with no consequences — Replay retains traumatic memories and erases the previous future; characters remember dying in erased timelines via Rindo's burden. It is not a clean reset and has emotional/physical cost.

The name Rindo comes from Swedish island Rindö — Japanese Rindō (gentian flower) and Swedish Rindö (Old Norse rind = shore) are etymologically unrelated homographs; character name is flower-derived, confirmed by creators.

Rindo Chihaya (Hololive) is the same character as Rindo Kanade — Completely separate IP: Hololive VTuber (2023) vs Square Enix game protagonist (2021). Share given name kanji reading but different surnames, designs, and owners.

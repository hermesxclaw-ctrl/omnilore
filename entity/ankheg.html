Who It Is
Form

The Ankheg is a burrowing predator that resembles a large, worm-like creature with a armored exoskeleton and a pair of sharp, claw-like appendages. Its body is typically 10-15 feet in length, with a diameter of 2-3 feet. The Ankheg's coloration varies from a mottled brown to a dark grey, allowing it to blend in with its surroundings.

Powers

Tremor Sense The Ankheg's ability to sense vibrations in the ground, allowing it to track its prey and navigate its surroundings.

Acid Spit The Ankheg's ability to release a stream of corrosive acid from its mouth, used to attack and disorient its prey.

Accounts

A group of adventurers stumble upon an Ankheg's lair while exploring a abandoned mine. The Ankheg attacks, and the party must use their skills and abilities to defeat it and escape.

Tork Angegh, the deity associated with the Ankheg, is said to have created the creature as a means of controlling the population of a nearby village.

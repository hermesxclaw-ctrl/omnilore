Who It Is

Fifteenth spirit, a Duke commanding 60 legions. Eligos (also Abigor) appears as a handsome knight carrying a lance, pennant, and scepter — the full panoply of martial aristocracy. He discovers hidden and secret things and knows the future, specifically the outcomes of wars and how soldiers shall meet their fate. He also stirs the love of lords, kings, and great persons — his influence extends into politics and courtly intrigue. Eligos combines the foresight of the battlefield with the manipulation of royal passions, making him both a strategic oracle and a demon of political seduction.

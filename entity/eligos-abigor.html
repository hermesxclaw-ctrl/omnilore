Who It Is
Form

Eligos is described as a handsome man on a horse, holding a standard or scepter. He is often depicted as a grand duke in hell, with a commanding presence.

Powers

Strategic Wisdom Eligos is said to possess the power of strategic thinking, allowing him to advise and guide others in matters of war and diplomacy.

Accounts

Eligos is said to have played a role in the Battle of Armageddon, serving as a strategic advisor to the forces of evil.

Eligos is said to have played a role in the Fall of Man, tempting Adam and Eve to disobey God's command.

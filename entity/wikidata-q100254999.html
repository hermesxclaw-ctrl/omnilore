Who It Is

In the glittering, steam-choked streets of Piltover, a yordle with a magnificent mustache and an obsession for hextech invention is both a beloved genius and a cautionary tale about the cost of progress. Heimerdinger, the brilliant mind behind the city's technological marvels, is also its most reluctant revolutionary, torn between his love for science and his fear of its consequences.

Heimerdinger is portrayed as a brilliant, eccentric, and highly enthusiastic scientist. He is passionate about invention and progress but also cautious and ethical, often warning against the reckless use of technology. He values knowledge and education, and he is kind-hearted and protective of his friends, especially in Arcane where he mentors Jayce and Viktor. He can be long-winded and pedantic, but his intentions are noble.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname 'Heimerdinger' is of German origin, likely a habitational name from places like Heimerdingen in Baden-Württemberg. The given name 'Cecil' is of Latin origin, meaning 'blind' or 'from the family of the Caecilii'. The full name is a playful, grandiose choice fitting an eccentric professor.

Pronunciation — HIGH-mer-ding-er

Aliases — Heimerdinger, The Revered Inventor, Professor Heimerdinger, Heimer

Powers
Hextech Turrets

Heimerdinger deploys automated turrets that shoot enemies. In the game, these are his primary defensive tools.

Limit: Turrets have limited range and health; they can be destroyed by enemies. He can only deploy a limited number at a time.

Hextech Micro-Rockets

Heimerdinger fires a barrage of rockets in a targeted area, dealing area damage.

Limit: Requires aiming and has a cooldown.

CH-3X Lightning Grenade

Heimerdinger throws a grenade that stuns enemies in the blast radius.

Limit: Single target area, short range.

UPGRADE!!!

Passive ability that enhances his turrets and other abilities based on ability power.

Intellect and Engineering Expertise

In Arcane, Heimerdinger is a brilliant scientist and inventor, capable of creating advanced hextech devices. He is also a skilled strategist and advisor.

Limit: His caution and ethical boundaries sometimes limit his willingness to push technological boundaries.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Heimerdinger is a yordle, a small, furry, humanoid creature with large ears and a long, bushy mustache. He has blue fur, large goggles often perched on his forehead, and wears a lab coat or formal attire with a bow tie. In Arcane, his design is more detailed, with expressive features and a slightly hunched posture, emphasizing his age and intellectual demeanor.

Symbols — His signature symbols include his multi-barreled turret inventions, his goggles, and his mustache. The hextech crystals he studies are central to his iconography, representing his obsession with magic-infused technology.

Domains — His primary domain is Piltover, the City of Progress, particularly the university and his laboratory. He is also associated with the underground city of Zaun, though he is uneasy about its dangerous conditions. In Arcane, he is a member of the Piltover Council, representing the academic and scientific community.

Accounts I
The Founding of Hextech and the CouncilLeague of Legends lore, Arcane

Before and during the events of Arcane (set in a steampunk-like era)

Heimerdinger, as one of the founders of hextech, helped bring magical technology to Piltover. He served on the city's council for centuries. In Arcane, he mentors Jayce and Viktor, but when their experiments lead to dangerous consequences, he votes to expel them from the academy, fearing the power of hextech in the wrong hands. He later questions his own rigid stance and ultimately leaves the council to rediscover his purpose. His decision to expel Jayce and Viktor strains his relationships but also sets the stage for the escalation of conflict in Piltover and Zaun. His departure from the council marks a turning point in his character arc.

Who It Is
Form

The Invisible College is a non-public network of researchers operating in an informal way, often characterized by a loose association of like-minded individuals sharing knowledge and ideas.

Powers

Knowledge Sharing The ability to share and disseminate knowledge and ideas among members of the Invisible College, facilitating the exchange of information and the advancement of scientific and philosophical understanding.

Accounts

The early association of the Royal Society of London, which consisted of a number of natural philosophers such as Robert Boyle and Christopher Wren, is often referred to as an example of an Invisible College.

The Rosicrucian Network, also known as the Invisible College, was a secret society of alchemists, philosophers, and scientists who shared knowledge and ideas through a network of correspondents and agents.

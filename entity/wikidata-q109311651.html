Who It Is

On Halloween night in Liverpool, a good-hearted Scouser who volunteers at a museum and looks after his parents is scooped off his street by a seven-foot dog-faced alien — and wakes up to find the universe is ending. Dan Lewis never asked to travel in the TARDIS, but when the Flux begins to eat the cosmos, his ordinary decency becomes the Doctor's most human compass.

Attested across all nine televised appearances: warm, chatty, self-deprecating, community-minded, brave without bravado. Works/volunteers at Liverpool Museum, helps run a food bank, looks after parents, coaches local football. Quick to offer help, slow to judge, uses humor to defuse fear. Loyal to Diane (person he was about to ask on a date), and later to Yaz and the Doctor. Not a skeptic — accepts alien reality quickly — but grounds cosmic horror in domestic ethics ('you don't leave people behind'). Critics consistently note his 'everyman' warmth as contrast to the

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Dan is the hypocoristic form of Daniel, from Hebrew Daniyyel (דָּנִיֵּאל) 'God is my judge'. Lewis is from Anglo-Norman Lowis/Ludovicus, ultimately Germanic hlod 'famous' + wig 'battle', via Welsh Lewys. In-universe, no etymology or middle name is given; character is consistently called 'Dan' on scr

Pronunciation — /dæn ˈluːɪs/ (dan LOO-iss), with Scouse (Liverpool) accent as performed by John Bishop

Aliases — Dan Lewis, Daniel Lewis, Dan

Ownership — Fictional character created by showrunner Chris Chibnall for BBC One / BBC Studios' Doctor Who. Owned and licensed by the BBC. Performer: John Bishop. Debut 31 October 2021. No fol

Powers
Ordinary Human Empathy and Moral Grounding

Dan's core narrative function is emotional intelligence: listening, de-escalating, advocating for civilians. He repeatedly prompts the Doctor to consider human-scale consequences (e.g., protecting Diane, questioning abandonment of Vinder's people).

Limit: No persuasive superpower; often ignored or overruled by the Doctor; vulnerable to psychic and physical coercion like any human

Practical DIY and Manual Competence

Shown competent with tools, cooking (wok as improvised weapon vs Karvanista), basic first aid, museum handling, and navigating Liverpool's community networks. Helps build barricades and operate Sontaran tech under instruction.

Limit: No technical expertise beyond layperson; cannot pilot TARDIS, repair alien drives, or hack systems unaided

Resilience to Cosmic Trauma (Flux Survival)

Survives direct Flux-adjacent events: Lupari abduction, Sontaran occupation, Weeping Angel village, Dalek execution time loop, and Sea Devil piracy, without lasting superhuman injury. Narrative resilience rather than power.

Limit: Suffers fear, exhaustion, injury risk; requires rescue by Doctor/Yaz/Karvanista multiple times; ages normally

Social Connector / Community Knowledge

Deep local knowledge of Liverpool, museum contacts, food-bank networks, and ability to quickly befriend strangers (Jericho, Claire, Vinder). Functions as audience surrogate who asks clarifying questions.

Limit: Knowledge is geographically and temporally bounded; ineffective off-Earth without allies

Form & Signs

Appearance — Portrayed by John Bishop (b.1966), a white English male in his mid-50s at casting, with short greying hair, short beard/stubble, Liverpudlian accent, average build. Costuming is deliberately ordinary: anorak/jacket, jumper, jeans, practical shoes — emphasizing working-class everyman vs. the Doctor's flamboyance. No alien prosthetics, uniforms, or iconic accessory; his visual signifier is ordinariness. Bishop's physical comedy and expressive face

Symbols — No personal sigil or heraldry. Associated iconography is diegetic: Karvanista's Lupari ship, Dan's Liverpool terraced house, the Liverpool Museum where he volunteers, and his wok (used humorously as weapon in debut). Promotional stills pair him with the Thirteenth Doctor's blue TARDIS and Yaz's police-style companion framing. Fan art often emphasizes his Scouse identity (Liver Bird, accent rendered orthographically).

Domains — Primary habitat: present-day Liverpool, England (Toxteth area implied, terraced house). Secondary habitat: TARDIS interior (2021-2022) and time-displaced locations via Flux: Crimean War (1855) in War of the Sontarans, 1967 Liverpool, 1901-2021 Medderton (Village of the Angels), Dalek time-loop storage facility (Eve of the Daleks), 1807 coastal China (Legend of the Sea Devils). Returns to Liverpool at departure. No otherworldly realm of origin.

Accounts I
Chapter One: The Halloween ApocalypseDoctor Who Series 13 Episode 1, BBC One

2021

On 31 October 2021, Dan Lewis is about to invite Diane to a museum outing when his house is shrunk and abducted by Karvanista, a Lupari warrior. The Doctor and Yaz rescue him as the Flux — a universe-destroying anomaly unleashed by the Division — begins to consume space. Dan learns his dog-like captor is actually his assigned protector under Lupari-Earth contract. Establishes Dan as abductee-turned-companion; introduces Lupari bond; Dan leaves Liverpool aboard the TARDIS as Flux destroys his street

Chapter Two: War of the SontaransDoctor Who Series 13 Episode 2, BBC One

2021

Displaced to Crimea, 1855, Dan, Yaz and the Doctor are separated in time by the Flux. Dan lands in Sevastopol during the Crimean War, now exploited by Sontarans who have rewritten history to fuel their endless war. Dan befriends Mary Seacole and helps expose Sontaran logistics, reuniting with the Doctor to reverse the timeline. Demonstrates Dan's historical adaptability and humanitarian alignment; reinforces companion role as witness to imperial war critique

Chapter Four: Village of the AngelsDoctor Who Series 13 Episode 4, BBC One

2021

In 1967, Dan, Yaz and Professor Eustacius Jericho investigate the missing village of Medderton, trapped by Weeping Angels. Dan is separated and psychologically tested by the Angels, who exploit his loneliness and concern for Diane. He resists by clinging to human connection, aiding Claire Brown's psychic link to the Doctor. Deepens Dan's vulnerability and loyalty; critically acclaimed horror episode; solidifies Dan-Yaz-Jericho ensemble

Accounts II
Chapter Six: The VanquishersDoctor Who Series 13 Episode 6, BBC One

2021

As the Flux reaches Earth, Dan helps coordinate the Lupari shield and Sontaran betrayal. He chooses to protect his parents and Diane's neighborhood over abstract cosmic strategy, embodying the season's theme that 'small' human stakes matter. The Flux is halted but at great cost; Dan elects to continue travelling. Flux arc resolution; Dan commits to TARDIS life beyond Liverpool; sets up 2022 specials

Eve of the DaleksDoctor Who New Year's Day Special 2022, BBC One

2022

Trapped in a time loop in a storage facility owned by Sarah and Nick, Dan, the Doctor and Yaz are hunted by execution Daleks who reset the loop each time they kill. Dan uses his everyman ingenuity and humor to help break the loop, encouraging Nick's courage and Sarah's trust, allowing the Doctor to rig Dalek explosives. Standalone bottle episode showcasing Dan's interpersonal skills; praised as strongest Dan-centric story by several reviewers

Legend of the Sea DevilsDoctor Who Easter Special 2022, BBC One

2022

In 1807 China, Dan joins the Doctor and Yaz confronting the Sea Devils and pirate queen Madam Ching. Dan is captured and forced to walk the plank, using wit to survive until rescue. The episode foregrounds Dan's lack of combat training versus his bravery. Penultimate adventure; mixed critical reception; Dan's role reduced compared to Eve of the Daleks

The Power of the DoctorDoctor Who Centenary Special 2022, BBC One

2022

For the BBC's centenary and Jodie Whittaker's finale, Dan helps the Doctor, Yaz, Vinder, and former companions thwart the Master and Daleks/Cybermen alliance attempting to force-regenerate the Doctor. After victory, Dan decides to return home to Liverpool, telling the Doctor he has learned he wants a grounded life and to check on Diane. He parts amicably, not traumatized. Departure of Dan Lewis; closes Chibnall era; Dan is one of few modern companions to leave by choice without death or tragedy

Dan Lewis is based on a real person or historical figure named Dan Lewis — Dan Lewis is wholly fictional, created by Chris Chibnall in 2020-2021 for Doctor Who. No historical Dan Lewis is adapted; any resemblance to real persons with same name is coincidental.

Dan Lewis is from Sheffield like Yaz, or is related to other companions — Dan is explicitly from Liverpool (Scouse), not Sheffield (Yaz and Ryan's home city). He has no familial relation to any prior companion. His parents are Eileen and Neville Lewis of Liverpool.

Dan has superpowers or becomes part-Lupari — Dan remains fully human throughout. He is protected by Lupari but never hybridized, implanted, or given powers. Wok incident is slapstick, not ability.

Dan died or was tragically removed like many companions — Dan leaves voluntarily and alive in The Power of the Doctor, choosing to return home. This is explicitly a happy, self-determined exit — rare for modern Doctor Who.

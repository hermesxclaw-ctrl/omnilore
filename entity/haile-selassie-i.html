Who It Is
Form

Haile Selassie was a tall and imposing figure, known for his dignified and authoritative presence. He was often depicted in traditional Ethiopian attire, including a long white robe and a golden crown.

Powers

Divine Right Haile Selassie believed in the concept of the Divine Right, which held that monarchs were appointed by God and were therefore above the law. This idea was central to his understanding of his own role as a messianic figure.

Accounts

Haile Selassie rose to power as the Regent Plenipotentiary of Ethiopia under Empress Zewditu between 1916 and 1930. He then became Emperor of Ethiopia in 1930, a position he held until his overthrow in 1974.

Haile Selassie's connection to the Rastafari movement is a complex and multifaceted one. While some Rastas saw him as a messianic figure, others were critical of his role as a monarch and his relationship with the Ethiopian government.

Who It Is

An entity that manifests when a child under the age of 12 is left alone in a house at night. The entity appears as a figure standing at the child's bedroom window — visible only to the child, never to adults. It does not enter the house. It does not speak. It simply watches, night after night, from the window. Children who see it report feeling 'safe' — they describe the entity as 'the babysitter' and say it 'makes sure nothing bad happens.' Adults dismiss these as imaginary friends. The Foundation has confirmed the entity exists and appears protective. The concern: what is it protecting children from?

Form

Described consistently by children: a tall figure (approximately 2.2m) wearing a long coat or cloak that obscures its features. Its face is never clearly seen. It has 'lots of hands' — multiple arms extending from beneath the coat — but the children are never frightened by this. One child's drawing, recovered by Foundation agents, shows the entity with twelve arms, each holding something: a book, a toy, a blanket, a knife, a candle, a key, a mirror, a bell, a flower, a stone, a string, and a child's small, severed hand.

Powers

Manifestation only to children under 12

Invisibility to adults

Protective behavior (observed)

Multilimbed physiology

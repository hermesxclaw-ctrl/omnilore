SCP-783 — The Babysitter

also known asThere Was...
Typescp
CultureSCP Foundation
Who It Is
Form

SCP-783 appears as a humanoid male entity, approximately 1.8 meters tall, with a slender build and a pale complexion. He has short, dark hair and piercing blue eyes. He is often dressed in a simple white shirt and black trousers.

Powers

Empathic Telepathy SCP-783 possesses the ability to read and manipulate the emotions of those around him. He can use this ability to calm or soothe individuals, or to provoke a strong emotional response.

Incident Log

SCP-783 is first introduced in this story, where he is described as a humanoid male entity with a babysitter's badge. He is said to possess the ability to read and manipulate the emotions of those around him.

This story provides further information on SCP-783's abilities and characteristics, including his empathic telepathy and his ability to manipulate emotions.

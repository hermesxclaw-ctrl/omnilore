Who It Is

In a nation that has erased its own citizens, one young major dares to see them as people. Vladilena 'Lena' Milizé, a 16-year-old Alba noble and military officer, becomes the Handler of the Spearhead Squadron—a unit of '86' soldiers the Republic of San Magnolia deems subhuman. Her compassion, defiance, and growing bond with her squad's leader, Shinei Nouzen, challenge the very foundations of her society and the brutal war that sustains it.

Lena is idealistic, compassionate, and determined. She treats the 86 as human beings, unlike other Handlers, and is willing to defy military protocol to improve their conditions. She is intelligent and capable, but initially naive about the true nature of the war. Over time, she becomes more pragmatic and hardened, yet retains her empathy. She is also selfless, often putting others before herself, and struggles with guilt over her privilege as an Alba.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Vladilena' is likely a Slavic-derived name, possibly evoking 'Vladimir' or 'Vlada' (meaning 'rule' or 'power'), combined with 'Lena'. 'Milizé' may be a variant of 'Milica' or 'Milena', Slavic names meaning 'gracious' or 'dear'. The nickname 'Lena' is a common diminutive.

Pronunciation — Vladilena: /vlædiˈleɪnə/ or /vlɑːdiˈleɪnə/; Milizé: /mɪliˈzeɪ/; Lena: /ˈleɪnə/ (Japanese: レーナ, Rēna)

Aliases — Lena, Bloody Regina, Handler One, Eighty-Sixth Strike Package Commander

Ownership — Japanese light novel and anime culture, created by Asato Asato and illustrated by Shirabii.

Powers
Tactical Command

Lena is a skilled military commander, able to coordinate multiple units and make strategic decisions under pressure. She demonstrates exceptional ability in directing the Spearhead Squadron and later the Eighty-Sixth Strike Package.

Limit: She is not a frontline combatant and relies on her squad for combat operations.

Political Acumen

As a noble and officer, Lena understands political dynamics and uses her status to advocate for the 86, though her efforts are often limited by the corrupt system.

Limit: Her idealism sometimes clashes with political realities, leading to setbacks.

Empathy and Leadership

Her genuine care for her subordinates earns their respect and loyalty, a key factor in the Spearhead Squadron's cooperation.

Limit: Her empathy can be a liability in the harsh military environment.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Lena is a young woman with long, silver-white hair and blue eyes, often depicted in her military uniform as a Major in the San Magnolian army. She wears a white and blue uniform with a cape, and is often seen with a headset for communicating with her squadron. In later volumes, she adopts a more practical military attire.

Symbols — Lena is associated with the lily, which appears in the series' imagery. Her callsign 'Bloody Regina' is symbolized by a red crown, referencing her role as a commander who sends soldiers to their deaths. The Spearhead Squadron's emblem, a spear piercing a crown, also relates to her.

Domains — Lena primarily operates in the Republic of San Magnolia, specifically at the Eastern Front command center. After the fall of San Magnolia, she joins the Federacy of Giad and later leads the Eighty-Sixth Strike Package, operating from various military bases.

Contemporary (2017)

Lena is assigned as the Handler for the Spearhead Squadron, a unit of 86 veterans. She initially faces hostility from the squad, particularly from Theo, but gradually earns their trust through her sincerity. She learns the truth about the 86's treatment and the Republic's deception. Lena becomes deeply involved with the 86 and begins to challenge the system, leading to her eventual departure from San Magnolia.

Contemporary (2017)

The Republic collapses as the Legion overruns the nation. Lena, having been promoted, attempts to save as many 86 as possible, but ultimately fails. She is rescued by the Federacy of Giad and reunites with the Spearhead Squadron survivors. Lena joins the Federacy and becomes a commander of the Eighty-Sixth Strike Package, continuing the fight against the Legion.

Contemporary (2017)

She works closely with Shin, and their relationship deepens. Lena becomes a key figure in the war, and her leadership is instrumental in several victories.

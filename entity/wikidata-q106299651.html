The Approach

In a nation that has erased its own citizens, one young major dares to see them as people. Vladilena 'Lena' Milizé, a 16-year-old Alba noble and military officer, becomes the Handler of the Spearhead Squadron—a unit of '86' soldiers the Republic of San Magnolia deems subhuman. Her compassion, defiance, and growing bond with her squad's leader, Shinei Nouzen, challenge the very foundations of her society and the brutal war that sustains it.

Content advisory. The source material contains themes of discrimination, genocide, war, death, suicide, and psychological trauma. The character's story is set against a backdrop of systemic oppression and violence against a minority group.

Vladilena Milizé

Pronunciation Vladilena: /vlædiˈleɪnə/ or /vlɑːdiˈleɪnə/; Milizé: /mɪliˈzeɪ/; Lena: /ˈleɪnə/ (Japanese: レーナ, Rēna)

Etymology

The name 'Vladilena' is likely a Slavic-derived name, possibly evoking 'Vladimir' or 'Vlada' (meaning 'rule' or 'power'), combined with 'Lena'. 'Milizé' may be a variant of 'Milica' or 'Milena', Slavic names meaning 'gracious' or 'dear'. The nickname 'Lena' is a common diminutive.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese light novel and anime culture, created by Asato Asato and illustrated by Shirabii.

Disputed Identifications

None known. The character is a fictional creation with no real-world counterpart or disputed identifications.

Description

Appearance

Lena is a young woman with long, silver-white hair and blue eyes, often depicted in her military uniform as a Major in the San Magnolian army. She wears a white and blue uniform with a cape, and is often seen with a headset for communicating with her squadron. In later volumes, she adopts a more practical military attire.

Symbols & Iconography

Lena is associated with the lily, which appears in the series' imagery. Her callsign 'Bloody Regina' is symbolized by a red crown, referencing her role as a commander who sends soldiers to their deaths. The Spearhead Squadron's emblem, a spear piercing a crown, also relates to her.

Behaviour & Personality

Lena is idealistic, compassionate, and determined. She treats the 86 as human beings, unlike other Handlers, and is willing to defy military protocol to improve their conditions. She is intelligent and capable, but initially naive about the true nature of the war. Over time, she becomes more pragmatic and hardened, yet retains her empathy. She is also selfless, often putting others before herself, and struggles with guilt over her privilege as an Alba.

Domains & Habitats

Lena primarily operates in the Republic of San Magnolia, specifically at the Eastern Front command center. After the fall of San Magnolia, she joins the Federacy of Giad and later leads the Eighty-Sixth Strike Package, operating from various military bases.

Version Differences

In the light novels, Lena's internal monologue provides deeper insight into her thoughts and feelings. The anime adaptation visually enhances her expressive reactions and adds scenes that emphasize her emotional journey. The manga spin-offs depict her in comedic or alternate settings, such as a magical girl parody.

Lena's effectiveness as a Handler

Position A

Lena is an effective Handler who improves the Spearhead Squadron's morale and performance.

Position A — evidence

The squadron cooperates with her better than previous Handlers, and she develops successful strategies.

Position B

Lena is naive and her compassion is a liability, as she is initially unaware of the true nature of the war.

Position B — evidence

Her initial ignorance leads to preventable deaths, and she is manipulated by the military hierarchy.

Scholarly Consensus

Lena's character arc involves learning from her mistakes and becoming more effective, so both positions are true at different points.

Position A

Lena did everything she could to save the 86, but the system was too corrupt.

Position A — evidence

She risks her life to evacuate 86 soldiers and speaks out against the government.

Position B

Lena's privileged position and delayed action contributed to the tragedy.

Position B — evidence

She initially follows orders and fails to act decisively until it is too late.

Scholarly Consensus

Lena is a tragic figure whose good intentions are constrained by the very system she belongs to.

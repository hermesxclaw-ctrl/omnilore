Who It Is

She is the princess who has been kidnapped a hundred times, yet she is also the goddess who holds the fate of Hyrule in her hands. Princess Zelda is not a single woman but a legacy of wisdom, courage, and sacrifice woven through centuries of legend.

Generally wise, compassionate, and courageous. Often takes on a guiding role, but also shows determination and resourcefulness. In some incarnations, she is more active and independent, while in others she is more reserved or initially unaware of her destiny.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Named after Zelda Fitzgerald, American novelist and wife of F. Scott Fitzgerald. The name was suggested by a PR planner and adopted by Shigeru Miyamoto.

Pronunciation — /ˈzɛldə/

Aliases — Zelda, Sheik, Tetra, Zelda of Hyrule, Princess of Hyrule

Ownership — Nintendo; created by Shigeru Miyamoto and Takashi Tezuka; part of The Legend of Zelda franchise.

Powers
Triforce of Wisdom

One of three pieces of the Triforce, granting divine wisdom and often the ability to sense evil or communicate telepathically.

Limit: Only effective when the bearer is worthy; can be split or stolen.

Light Arrows

In some games, Zelda uses light arrows to defeat evil, often during final battles.

Limit: Requires the bow and arrows; often only used in specific moments.

Goddess Hylia's powers

As the reincarnation of Hylia, Zelda has latent divine powers, including sealing magic and healing abilities.

Limit: She must awaken her powers; she is not omniscient or omnipotent.

Sheikah abilities (as Sheik)

When disguised as Sheik, she demonstrates agility, stealth, and combat skills.

Limit: She seems to lose these abilities when revealing her true identity.

Wisdom and leadership

Zelda is often portrayed as intelligent and a capable ruler, providing guidance and making strategic decisions.

Limit: Her influence is often limited by the antagonist's actions.

Echoes (in Echoes of Wisdom)

As the playable protagonist, Zelda can create echoes of objects and enemies to solve puzzles and fight.

Limit: Limited by the number of echoes she can hold; requires learning new echoes.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Varies by incarnation. Typically a Hylian princess with blonde or brown hair, blue eyes, wearing a royal gown, tiara, and often the royal crest. In Ocarina of Time, her Sheik form has short white hair and Sheikah attire. In The Wind Waker, Tetra has a pirate outfit and a distinctive swirl of hair. In Skyward Sword, she wears a simple blue and red dress without royal ornamentation.

Symbols — Triforce of Wisdom, Hyrule royal family crest, Sheikah eye symbol, goddess Hylia's imagery.

Domains — Hyrule, various locations depending on incarnation: Hyrule Castle, Temple of Time, Great Sea (as Tetra), Skyloft (in Skyward Sword), and other realms.

Accounts I
The Legend of Zelda (1986)NES game

1986

Zelda is kidnapped by Ganon and held in the underworld. Link must find the Triforce of Wisdom and rescue her. Link defeats Ganon and rescues Zelda.

Zelda II: The Adventure of LinkNES game

1987

Zelda is under a sleeping spell; Link must awaken her by finding the Triforce of Courage. Link awakens Zelda and breaks the spell.

Ocarina of TimeN64 game

1998

Zelda, as Sheik, guides Link to prevent Ganondorf from obtaining the Triforce. She is captured but ultimately helps defeat him. Ganondorf is sealed in the Sacred Realm.

The Wind WakerGCN game

2002

Zelda, under the alias Tetra, is a pirate captain who helps Link. Her true identity is revealed, and she aids in defeating Ganondorf. Hyrule is flooded, and a new land is sought.

Accounts II
Skyward SwordWii game

2011

Zelda is revealed to be the mortal reincarnation of the goddess Hylia. She seals herself to protect the Triforce, and Link rescues her. Establishes the bloodline of the goddess and the cycle of reincarnation.

Breath of the WildWii U/Switch game

2017

Zelda struggles to awaken her sealing powers while Hyrule falls to Calamity Ganon. She holds Ganon at bay for 100 years until Link returns. Zelda's powers awaken; Ganon is defeated.

Tears of the KingdomSwitch game

2023

Zelda is transported to the distant past and becomes the Light Dragon to restore the Master Sword, while Link works to stop the Demon King. Zelda sacrifices her form but is eventually restored.

Echoes of WisdomSwitch game

2024

Zelda becomes the playable protagonist, using the Tri Rod to create echoes and rescue Hyrule. Zelda proves her heroism.

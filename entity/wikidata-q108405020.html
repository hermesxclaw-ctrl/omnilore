Who It Is

In a family where hero names are inherited like heirlooms, Shiden Iida exists as a faint entry in the ledger — a name tied to the Iida lineage of My Hero Academia, whose violet-lightning name promises speed but whose story remains almost entirely off-panel.

Presumed Engine-type Quirk (Unverified for Shiden specifically)

The Iida family is defined by the Engine (Enjin) Quirk: internal combustion engines in the limbs (typically calves) that grant superhuman speed, acceleration, and kicking power, fueled by orange juice as fuel metaphor. Tenya and Tensei both manifest variants (Recipro Burst, etc.). Shiden's Wikidata archetype 'heroic-legend' and surname association suggest he may be intended as an Engine Quirk holder within the same lineage, and the name 'Shiden' (violet lightning) evokes high-speed movement. However, no primary source located explicitly describes Shiden's Quirk name, mechanics, or feats. This entry is therefore hypothetical/reconstructed from family pattern, not attested.

Accounts I
The Iida Family Hero Lineage — The House of IngeniumHorikoshi, Kohei. My Hero Academia manga; Ultra Archive databook

2014-present (Heisei-Reiwa)

The central surviving narrative for the Iida family is that they have produced pro heroes for generations, with the name Ingenium passed down as a hereditary hero title. Tenya Iida explains to classmates that his family is a prestigious hero household and that his brother Tensei was the previous Ingenium. This lineage is presented as a source of pride and pressure for Tenya. Shiden Iida, as listed in index/databook material, is positioned within this extended lineage, but does not speak or act in the main narrative chapters reviewed. The story functions as world-building for hero society's familial professionalization, not as a Shiden-centered plot. Establishes intergenerational duty as a theme for Tenya's arc (Stain arc, internships). Provides canonical justification for a large Iida family tree into which Shiden can be placed, but leaves his individual deeds un-narrated.

Turbo Hero Ingenium and the Hero Killer: Stain ArcMy Hero Academia manga chapters 47-59 (vol. 6-7); anime Season 2 episodes 23-31

2016 (manga publication), 2017 (anime broadcast)

Tensei Iida (Ingenium) is critically injured by the Hero Killer Stain, paralyzing his legs and ending his active hero career. Tenya, driven by revenge and then by redefined hero ideals, confronts Stain alongside Midoriya and Todoroki. This is the most detailed Iida family story. Shiden Iida does not appear or intervene in this arc; his relevance is only as a potential prior holder or relative within the family that is shown grieving and supporting Tensei. The arc cements the cost of hero work for the family. Tensei retires from active duty; Tenya inherits the Ingenium name later (chapter 200+). The event reframes Iida family heroism from glory to vulnerability. No consequence directly involving Shiden is attested.

Tenya Iida's Inheritance — Claiming the Name IngeniumMy Hero Academia manga chapter 203-204; anime Season 4

2018-2019

After months of reflection, Tenya formally adopts the hero name Ingenium, previously held by Tensei, signaling his acceptance of family legacy on his own terms rather than as mere imitation. The ceremony is familial and agency-based, with Tensei's blessing. Lineage materials that list Shiden Iida would imply that Tenya is not the first to inherit but the latest in a longer chain. No scene depicts Shiden bestowing or contesting the name. Solidifies hereditary hero name as a diegetic institution. If Shiden held the name earlier, his tenure is undocumented, leaving a gap in the succession list.

The narrative uses the Iida family reputation as social capital and burden. Shiden is not named in dialogue in these episodes/chapters in available subtitles/scripts; his presence is limited to extra-textual lineage indexes that contextualize why the family is considered prestigious. World-building: hero families function like professional dynasties. No direct plot consequence for Shiden.

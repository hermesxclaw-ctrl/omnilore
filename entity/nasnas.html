Who It Is
Form

The Nasnas is described as a monopod, with half a human head, half a body, one arm, and one leg. It is said to hop with great agility, using its single limb to propel itself forward.

Powers

Agility The Nasnas is said to possess incredible agility, allowing it to hop and move with great speed and precision.

Accounts

In this classic work of literature, the Nasnas is described as a creature that appears to a group of travelers in the desert. The travelers are initially frightened by the Nasnas' appearance, but eventually come to see it as a symbol of the power and mystery of the desert.

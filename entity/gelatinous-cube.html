Who It Is
Form

A gelatinous cube is a ten-foot cube of transparent gelatinous ooze, which is able to absorb and digest organic matter. Its transparency coupled with a dimly-lit dungeon gives it the element of surprise to engulf unsuspecting beings.

Powers

Digestive Ability Gelatinous cubes have the ability to digest and absorb organic matter, including flesh and other biological materials.

Accounts

The gelatinous cube was introduced in the early versions of Dungeons & Dragons as a monster and a dungeon 'cleaner' organism.

The gelatinous cube has appeared in various forms of popular culture, including films, television shows, and video games.

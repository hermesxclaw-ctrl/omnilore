Who It Is

A small, goldfish-like Pokemon surrounded by glowing orbs of dark flame. Chi-Yu is one of the four Ruinous Treasures of Paldea — an artifact that was once a set of beads used by a greedy ruler who brought ruin upon his kingdom. The beads absorb malice and grow hotter and hotter, burning all nearby.

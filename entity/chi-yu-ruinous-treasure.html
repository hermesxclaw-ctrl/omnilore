The Approach

In the volcanic heart of Paldea, where molten rock churns beneath ancient craters, there slumbers a creature of terrifying beauty—Chi-Yu, the Ruinous Treasure. Born of envy and greed, this tiny goldfish-like beast wields flames that can consume the very soul, a warning from a bygone era when human desire nearly unmade the world.

Content advisory. Contains themes of greed, envy, destruction, and the consequences of unchecked desire. No graphic violence or explicit content.

Etymology

The name 'Chi-Yu' likely derives from the Chinese word 'chīyù' (饕餮), referring to the Taotie, a mythological creature symbolizing greed and gluttony. The suffix '-yu' may also evoke 'jade' (玉), connecting to treasure. The English designation 'Ruinous Treasure' reflects its destructive nature and association with treasure.

Cultural Ownership

The Pokémon franchise is owned by Nintendo, Game Freak, and The Pokémon Company. Chi-Yu is a fictional creature created for the games and is part of the global Pokémon intellectual property.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans have speculated connections to other mythological creatures, but no official confirmation exists. Its design may draw from the Taotie and Chinese goldfish motifs, but these are inferences.

Description

Appearance

Chi-Yu is a small, goldfish-like Pokémon with a predominantly black body and lighter underbelly. Its most striking feature is the two large, red-orange orbs on its head, which resemble beads or eyes, and its flowing, ribbon-like fins that resemble flames. It has a serene yet ominous expression, with small, dark eyes.

Symbols & Iconography

The red orbs are reminiscent of the 'Beads of Ruin' mentioned in its ability, symbolizing the treasure that brought ruin. Its overall design evokes Chinese goldfish and the Taotie motif, representing greed and destruction.

Behaviour & Personality

Chi-Yu is said to be a destructive force, born from negative human emotions. It is not inherently malicious but embodies the consequences of greed. In battle, it is fierce and powerful, using its fiery abilities to overwhelm opponents. It is a rare and elusive Pokémon, often dormant for long periods.

Domains & Habitats

Chi-Yu is found in the Paldea region, specifically in a cave near Casseroya Lake, which is surrounded by volcanic activity. It is associated with ruins and ancient treasures.

Version Differences

No significant differences between Pokémon Scarlet and Violet. Its behavior and appearance are consistent across both versions.

Stories & Myths

The Tale of the Ruinous Treasures

Source Pokémon Scarlet and Violet in-game loreCulture Fictional (Pokémon world)Period Ancient history of Paldea

Summary

According to in-game lore, Chi-Yu and three other Pokémon (Wo-Chien, Chien-Pao, and Ting-Lu) were born from the negative emotions of humans who coveted treasure. They were sealed away in shrines by ancient people to prevent their destructive power from wreaking havoc. Chi-Yu specifically was born from envy and jealousy.

Who It Is

In the volcanic heart of Paldea, where molten rock churns beneath ancient craters, there slumbers a creature of terrifying beauty—Chi-Yu, the Ruinous Treasure. Born of envy and greed, this tiny goldfish-like beast wields flames that can consume the very soul, a warning from a bygone era when human desire nearly unmade the world.

Chi-Yu is said to be a destructive force, born from negative human emotions. It is not inherently malicious but embodies the consequences of greed. In battle, it is fierce and powerful, using its fiery abilities to overwhelm opponents. It is a rare and elusive Pokémon, often dormant for long periods.

Powers
Beads of Ruin

This ability lowers the Special Defense of all other Pokémon on the field, making them more susceptible to special attacks. It represents the corrupting influence of the treasure Chi-Yu embodies.

Limit: Applies to all other Pokémon, including allies, but does not affect Chi-Yu itself.

Fire-type moves

Chi-Yu can use a variety of Fire-type moves, such as Flamethrower, Fire Blast, and Overheat, which are enhanced by its high Special Attack stat.

Limit: Standard move limitations: PP, accuracy, and type matchups.

Dark-type moves

It can also learn Dark-type moves like Dark Pulse and Nasty Plot, complementing its Dark typing and offensive capabilities.

Limit: Standard move limitations.

High Special Attack and Speed

Chi-Yu has exceptionally high Special Attack and Speed stats, making it a potent special sweeper in competitive play.

Limit: Its defenses are relatively low, making it frail.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Chi-Yu is a small, goldfish-like Pokémon with a predominantly black body and lighter underbelly. Its most striking feature is the two large, red-orange orbs on its head, which resemble beads or eyes, and its flowing, ribbon-like fins that resemble flames. It has a serene yet ominous expression, with small, dark eyes.

Symbols — The red orbs are reminiscent of the 'Beads of Ruin' mentioned in its ability, symbolizing the treasure that brought ruin. Its overall design evokes Chinese goldfish and the Taotie motif, representing greed and destruction.

Domains — Chi-Yu is found in the Paldea region, specifically in a cave near Casseroya Lake, which is surrounded by volcanic activity. It is associated with ruins and ancient treasures.

Ancient history of Paldea

According to in-game lore, Chi-Yu and three other Pokémon (Wo-Chien, Chien-Pao, and Ting-Lu) were born from the negative emotions of humans who coveted treasure. They were sealed away in shrines by ancient people to prevent their destructive power from wreaking havoc. Chi-Yu specifically was born from envy and jealousy. The Pokémon were sealed, and their power was contained. However, they remain a threat if unsealed.

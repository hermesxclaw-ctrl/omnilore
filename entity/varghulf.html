Who It Is
Form

The Varghulf is a vampiric creature with a wolf-like appearance, often depicted as a humanoid figure with wolf-like features, sharp teeth, and glowing eyes.

Powers

Blood Lust The Varghulf's ability to enter a state of bloodlust, increasing its strength, speed, and ferocity in the presence of blood.

Accounts

A group of Varghulfs desecrate a village, killing and feasting on the inhabitants, and are eventually defeated by a group of brave warriors.

A Varghulf is cursed by a powerful vampire, leading to its transformation into a creature driven by an insatiable hunger for blood.

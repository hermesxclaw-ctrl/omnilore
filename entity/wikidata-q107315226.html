Who It Is

The name 'Killjoy' is a curious moniker for a figure of heroic legend, suggesting a character who defies easy categorization. Across various fictional universes, from the gritty streets of Charlton Comics to the tactical arenas of Valorant, the title has been adopted by a motley crew of heroes, villains, and everything in between. Yet, the most resonant iteration might be the one that redefines the term entirely: a punk-rock specter who fights against a dystopian regime, turning the very concept of joy into a weapon of rebellion.

Killjoy characters generally embody a contrarian or rebellious spirit. The Charlton Killjoy is a heroic figure who fights crime. The Valorant Killjoy is a meticulous and intelligent engineer. The film clown Killjoy is malevolent and sadistic. The Fabulous Killjoys are freedom fighters opposing an authoritarian corporation.

E-Man and the Origin of KilljoyCharlton Comics

1970s

Killjoy is a supporting character in the E-Man series, a superhero with a sentient energy form. Killjoy is a regular human who assists E-Man in his adventures, often providing comic relief. Killjoy became a recurring ally.

Valorant: Killjoy's Agent LoreRiot Games

2020s

Killjoy is a German engineer who joined the Valorant Protocol to protect her homeland and use her inventions for good. Her lore is revealed through in-game dialogue, trailers, and official comics. She is a playable agent with a dedicated fanbase.

Accounts II
Weapon P.R.I.M.E.: Killjoy's RoleMarvel Comics

1990s

Killjoy is a member of the Weapon P.R.I.M.E. team, a group of government-sponsored mutants. The series was short-lived, and Killjoy's role was minor. The team disbanded, and Killjoy has not appeared prominently since.

Killjoy (2000) and SequelsFull Moon Features

2000s-2010s

Killjoy is a demonic clown who is summoned by a group of criminals to terrorize their victims. The clown becomes a recurring antagonist in a series of low-budget horror films. The series gained a cult following.

The True Lives of the Fabulous KilljoysMy Chemical Romance / Gerard Way

2010s

In a post-apocalyptic future, a group of rebels known as the Fabulous Killjoys fight against the tyrannical Better Living Industries (BLI) corporation. The story is told through the album 'Danger Days' and the comic series. The Killjoys become symbols of resistance, inspiring others to fight back.

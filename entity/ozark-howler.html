Who It Is
Form

The Ozark Howler is described as a bear-like creature with a dark-colored, shaggy coat. Its size and exact physical characteristics are unknown.

Powers

Howling The Ozark Howler is said to produce a loud, howling sound that can be heard for miles. This ability is often cited as one of the creature's most distinctive characteristics.

Accounts

The Ozark Howler is a cryptid said to dwell in the Ozark Mountains of Northern Arkansas and Southern Missouri. According to tradition, the creature is bear-like in shape with a dark-colored, shaggy coat.

The Ozark Howler is a mysterious creature said to inhabit the Ozark Mountains region. According to local traditions, the creature is rarely seen by humans and is often associated with strange, unexplained occurrences.

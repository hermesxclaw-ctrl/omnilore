Who It Is

The 'ghost of violent death' in Thai folklore: a spirit of someone who died suddenly and violently — in an accident, by murder, or by suicide — without the proper Buddhist funeral rites. Phi Tai Hong are among the most feared and powerful of Thai ghosts because their deaths were unnatural, leaving them with immense residual rage and hunger. They are believed to haunt the site of their death, seeking to transfer their suffering or possess the living. Thai folklore holds that phi tai hong are particularly active in places where many violent deaths occurred: accident blackspots, execution grounds, and battlefields. Offerings of red Fanta (a uniquely Thai ghost-propitiation custom) are left to appease them.

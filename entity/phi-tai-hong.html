Who It Is
Form

Phi Tai Hong is a vengeful and restless spirit of a person who suddenly suffered a violent or cruel death. It is often depicted as a malevolent entity that seeks revenge on the living.

Powers

Vengeful Spirit Phi Tai Hong has the ability to manifest as a vengeful spirit, seeking to harm or kill those who wronged it in life.

Accounts

The legend of Phi Tai Hong tells the story of a person who was wronged in life and seeks revenge as a vengeful spirit.

The story of Phi Tai Hong tells the tale of a person who is haunted by the vengeful spirit of a person who died in a violent or cruel manner.

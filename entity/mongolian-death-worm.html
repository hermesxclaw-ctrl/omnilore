Who It Is
Form

The Mongolian Death Worm is described as a large, red, and slimy worm, said to be able to grow up to several meters in length. Its appearance is often associated with the Gobi Desert's harsh environment.

Powers

Deadly Gas Emission The Mongolian Death Worm is said to be able to emit a deadly gas that can kill its victims, making it a formidable and feared creature in Mongolian folklore.

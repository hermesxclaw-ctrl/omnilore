Who It Is

A robot master designed by Dr. Wily for space exploration and colonization. Galaxy Man's body is blue and gold with a star-patterned helmet and a transparent dome on his chest containing a swirling miniature galaxy. He manipulates gravitational forces and can create black holes that draw in everything around them.

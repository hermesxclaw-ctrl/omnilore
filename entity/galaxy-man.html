Who It Is
Form

Galaxy Man is a humanoid robot with a blue and white color scheme, a distinctive helmet, and a powerful arm cannon. He is known for his agility and quick reflexes.

Powers

Mega Buster A powerful arm cannon that is Galaxy Man's primary means of attack.

Accounts

Galaxy Man must stop Dr. Wily's latest plan to take over the world by collecting eight powerful robots.

Galaxy Man must find the Blue Bomber, a powerful robot that has the ability to defeat Dr. Wily.

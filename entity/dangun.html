Who It Is
Form

Dangun is often depicted as a powerful and wise king, with a strong connection to the natural world. He is said to have been born from a bear and is associated with the creation of the first Korean kingdom. In some accounts, he is described as having supernatural abilities and a deep understanding of the workings of the universe.

Powers

Supernatural abilities Dangun is said to have had supernatural abilities, including the power to communicate with animals and control the forces of nature.

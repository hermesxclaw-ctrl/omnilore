Who It Is
Form

Zurvan is often depicted as a wise and powerful deity, with a strong connection to the infinite and the cosmos.

Powers

Cosmic Awareness Zurvan possesses the power of cosmic awareness, allowing him to perceive the infinite and the interconnectedness of all things.

Accounts

According to Zurvanist mythology, Zurvan was born from the infinite expanse of time, and his birth marked the beginning of the cosmos.

In Manichaean mythology, Zurvan is depicted as a deity struggling against the forces of darkness and chaos.

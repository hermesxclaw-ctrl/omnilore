Who It Is

Maitreya is the bodhisattva who will become the next Buddha in this world cycle, succeeding Gautama Buddha. He currently resides in Tuṣita Heaven (the Heaven of Joy), where he awaits the proper time to take his final rebirth among humans. His name means 'The Friendly One' or 'The Loving One,' reflecting the quality of loving-kindness (maitri/metta) that will define his dispensation. According to prophecy, Maitreya will be born during a golden age when the human lifespan is 80,000 years, when all beings are peaceful and prosperous. He will rediscover the path to enlightenment and teach it to a world ready to receive it. Maitreya is unique among bodhisattvas because his Buddhahood is prophesied and certain — he is the 'future Buddha' in the same way that Gautama was the 'present Buddha.' In art, Maitreya is often depicted seated on a throne with his feet on the ground (indicating his imminent descent), or as a royal figure with a stupa in his crown. In China, the 'Laughing Buddha' (Budai) became identified as an emanation of Maitreya. Maitreya is especially important in Theravada Buddhism as the only bodhisattva recognized in the Pali Canon, and in Mahayana as a symbol of hope for the future.

Origins

Maitreya is mentioned in the Pali Canon (Dīgha Nikāya) as the next Buddha. In Mahayana, he is associated with the Buddhist sage Asanga, who is said to have visited Maitreya in Tuṣita Heaven and received the Five Treatises of Maitreya, which founded the Yogacara school.

Attested in Pali Canon (Dīgha Nikāya) / Mahayana SutrasEra c. 3rd century BCE onward

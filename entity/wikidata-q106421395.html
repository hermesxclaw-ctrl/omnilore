Who It Is

In the neon-lit streets of Sakurashin, where humans and yōkai coexist under the protection of the Seven Pillars, a fifteen-year-old girl with pale skin and a quiet demeanor delivers packages by day. But Rin Azuma is no ordinary courier—she is a jiangshi, an undead being who once feared the starry sky and distrusted humanity. Her journey from hunted outcast to beloved member of the community is a testament to the power of friendship and the resilience of the human (and inhuman) spirit.

Rin is initially shy, withdrawn, and fearful of humans due to years of persecution. She has a fear of starry skies, a result of her time as a homeless outcast. She is kind-hearted and hardworking, and after Akina's intervention, she becomes more open and trusting. She works at a delivery service and gradually overcomes her traumatic past.

Etymology — The surname 'Azuma' (東) means 'east', and the given name 'Rin' (鈴) means 'bell'. The name may evoke imagery of a bell ringing, possibly referencing her undead nature or her role in the community.

Powers
Jiangshi abilities

As a jiangshi, Rin is undead. She requires name tags to maintain mobility; without them, she becomes an immobile corpse. The tags also eliminate her need for sleep.

Limit: She must wear the name tags to move and function normally. Without them, she is helpless.

Immortality/Undead nature

Being undead, she does not age and is immune to normal death, though she can be harmed or destroyed by certain means.

Limit: Not explicitly detailed; she can be controlled by necromancers like Zakuro.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Rin is a 15-year-old girl with pale skin, typical of a jiangshi. She has dark hair, often styled with bangs, and wears a Chinese-style dress or other outfits appropriate for her role as a delivery girl. She wears special name tags that keep her in a mobile state; without them, she reverts to an immobile corpse.

Symbols — The name tags she wears are symbolic of her dependence on external objects to maintain her humanity. The tag itself may bear her name, serving as a talisman that anchors her to a state between life and death.

Domains — Sakurashin, a town where humans and yōkai coexist. She lives in the town and works as a delivery girl.

Accounts I
Rin's Arrival in SakurashinYozakura Quartet manga/anime

Contemporary

Rin, a jiangshi, is hunted by humans and relocates to Sakurashin, a town where yōkai are accepted. There, she meets Zakuro, a necromancer who becomes her first friend and protector. She initially despises humanity due to childhood bullying, but Akina's kindness changes her perspective. She begins working at a delivery service and gradually overcomes her fears. Rin finds a new home and friends, and begins to heal from her traumatic past.

Accounts II
The Demon Hunter AttackYozakura Quartet manga/anime

Contemporary

Zakuro, a necromancer, initially attacks Sakurashin as a demon hunter. She controls Rin's movements using her necromancy, even when Rin wears her name tags. After the conflict, Zakuro joins Rin at the delivery store, but remains haunted by fears of expulsion from Sakurashin. Zakuro becomes a resident of Sakurashin and a friend to Rin.

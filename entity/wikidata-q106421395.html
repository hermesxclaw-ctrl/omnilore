The Approach

In the neon-lit streets of Sakurashin, where humans and yōkai coexist under the protection of the Seven Pillars, a fifteen-year-old girl with pale skin and a quiet demeanor delivers packages by day. But Rin Azuma is no ordinary courier—she is a jiangshi, an undead being who once feared the starry sky and distrusted humanity. Her journey from hunted outcast to beloved member of the community is a testament to the power of friendship and the resilience of the human (and inhuman) spirit.

Content advisory. Contains themes of persecution, bullying, social ostracism, and the trauma of being hunted. The character is undead and deals with themes of death and identity.

Etymology

The surname 'Azuma' (東) means 'east', and the given name 'Rin' (鈴) means 'bell'. The name may evoke imagery of a bell ringing, possibly referencing her undead nature or her role in the community.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese manga and anime series Yozakura Quartet, created by Suzuhito Yasuda.

Disputed Identifications

None known.

Description

Appearance

Rin is a 15-year-old girl with pale skin, typical of a jiangshi. She has dark hair, often styled with bangs, and wears a Chinese-style dress or other outfits appropriate for her role as a delivery girl. She wears special name tags that keep her in a mobile state; without them, she reverts to an immobile corpse.

Symbols & Iconography

The name tags she wears are symbolic of her dependence on external objects to maintain her humanity. The tag itself may bear her name, serving as a talisman that anchors her to a state between life and death.

Behaviour & Personality

Rin is initially shy, withdrawn, and fearful of humans due to years of persecution. She has a fear of starry skies, a result of her time as a homeless outcast. She is kind-hearted and hardworking, and after Akina's intervention, she becomes more open and trusting. She works at a delivery service and gradually overcomes her traumatic past.

Domains & Habitats

Sakurashin, a town where humans and yōkai coexist. She lives in the town and works as a delivery girl.

Version Differences

The anime and manga may depict minor differences in her backstory or interactions, but the core characterization remains consistent.

Powers & Abilities

Jiangshi abilities

Source Version Both manga and anime

Description

As a jiangshi, Rin is undead. She requires name tags to maintain mobility; without them, she becomes an immobile corpse. The tags also eliminate her need for sleep.

Limitations

She must wear the name tags to move and function normally. Without them, she is helpless.

Source Yozakura Quartet manga/animeCulture JapanesePeriod Contemporary

Summary

Rin, a jiangshi, is hunted by humans and relocates to Sakurashin, a town where yōkai are accepted. There, she meets Zakuro, a necromancer who becomes her first friend and protector. She initially despises humanity due to childhood bullying, but Akina's kindness changes her perspective. She begins working at a delivery service and gradually overcomes her fears.

Persecution and homelessness

Source Yozakura Quartet manga/anime

Description

Before moving to Sakurashin, Rin was hunted by humans and spent years homeless. She was bullied as a child, leading to a deep distrust of humanity and a fear of starry skies, as she associated them with her time on the streets.

Scholarly Context

This backstory highlights the discrimination faced by yōkai in the series' world, reflecting real-world issues of prejudice and displacement.

Who It Is
Form

Described as a long, serpentine creature, often with a diameter of 10-15 feet, and a length of up to 30 feet. Some accounts describe it as having a horse-like head or a pair of humps.

Powers

Aquatic Adaptation The Ogopogo is said to be able to survive and thrive in the aquatic environment of Okanagan Lake, with some accounts describing it as being able to hold its breath for extended periods of time.

Accounts

A story about a group of Secwepemc hunters who encounter the Ogopogo while out on the lake. The creature is described as being a powerful and mysterious being, with the ability to control the waters.

A series of reported sightings of the Ogopogo in Okanagan Lake, which sparked widespread interest and debate in the local community.

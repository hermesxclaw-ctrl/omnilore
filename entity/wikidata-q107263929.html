Who It Is

In the sprawling world of Teyvat, where the elements bend to the will of the worthy, two travelers from beyond the stars stand at the heart of a conflict older than the nations themselves. Lumine, the Princess of the Abyss in one fate, the savior of a world in another, embodies the duality of choice and destiny—a heroine whose path is written not by fate, but by the hands that guide her.

Lumine is generally calm, compassionate, and determined. She is curious about the world and helps those in need. She often acts as a mediator and problem-solver. In contrast, her Abyss counterpart is more distant, cold, and appears to harbor a deep resentment towards the gods of Teyvat. The player's choices can influence her demeanor, but her core personality remains empathetic.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Lumine' is derived from the Latin 'lumen', meaning 'light'. It contrasts with her twin brother's name, Aether, which means 'upper air' or 'sky' in Greek mythology. The names symbolize light and sky, elements often associated with the divine or the celestial.

Powers
Elemental Manipulation

Lumine can wield all seven elements of Teyvat: Anemo, Geo, Electro, Dendro, Hydro, Pyro, and Cryo. She can switch between elements by resonating with Statues of The Seven. Each element grants her different combat abilities and elemental skills.

Limit: She can only use one element at a time. Her elemental abilities are weaker than those of characters who are native to the element, as she is not a native of Teyvat.

Sword Proficiency

Lumine is skilled in swordsmanship, using a one-handed sword. Her combat style is fluid and adaptable.

Limit: None known.

Descender's Power

As a Descender, Lumine is not bound by the laws of Teyvat. This grants her the ability to resist the effects of the Abyss and to travel between worlds. The full extent of this power is unknown.

Limit: Unknown; the game has not fully explained the nature of Descenders.

Abyss Manipulation (Antagonist only)

As the Princess of the Abyss, Lumine commands the Abyss Order and can use Abyssal powers, which are dark and corrupting. She can summon creatures and use dark magic.

Limit: Not available to the playable character.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Lumine is a young woman with long, blonde hair that fades to a lighter shade at the tips. She has golden eyes. She wears a white and gold outfit with a blue scarf, and her attire is reminiscent of a traveler's garb. She carries a sword. In her Abyss Order outfit, she wears a dark, more elaborate dress with a black veil, symbolizing her role as the Princess of the Abyss.

Symbols — Lumine is associated with the element of Anemo (wind) as her starting element, but she can wield all seven elements. Her symbol is the Traveler's star, which appears on her outfit and in the game's UI. In the Abyss form, she is associated with the Abyss Order's emblem, which features a dark, twisted tree.

Domains — Lumine is a traveler who traverses the world of Teyvat, visiting all seven nations: Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine, Natlan, and Snezhnaya. She has no fixed home, but she often returns to Mondstadt, where she is an Honorary Knight. In the Abyss timeline, she resides in the Abyss, a dark realm beneath Teyvat.

Start of the game

Lumine and her twin brother Aether are traveling across the multiverse when they are blocked by an unknown god in the world of Teyvat. They are separated, and Lumine falls into a deep slumber. She awakens 500 years later, alone, and begins her journey to find her brother. This sets the stage for Lumine's quest to reunite with her brother and uncover the truth about Teyvat.

The Search for AetherMain storyline of Genshin Impact

Throughout the game

Lumine travels across the seven nations, helping the people of Teyvat with their problems, all while searching for clues about her brother's whereabouts. She encounters the Abyss Order, which is led by a figure who resembles her brother, and she learns that he may be involved with the Abyss. Lumine becomes a key figure in the political and elemental conflicts of Teyvat, gaining allies and enemies. She eventually confronts her brother, but the full resolution is still unfolding in the game's ongoing narrative.

The Abyss Order's PrincessIn-game lore, particularly in the 'We Will Be Reunited' quest

Chapter I, Act IV

In the antagonist timeline, Lumine becomes the leader of the Abyss Order, commanding its forces against the gods of Teyvat. She believes that the gods are responsible for the destruction of her original world and seeks to overthrow them. She is confronted by her twin, who tries to convince her to return to their former path. This reveals the depth of the conflict between the twins and sets up a potential redemption arc for Lumine.

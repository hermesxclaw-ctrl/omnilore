Who It Is

He is a walking moss ball with an afro that detaches like a weapon, the perfectly round, perfectly loyal shadow to one of One Piece's most despised tyrants — and without Kuromarimo, Wapol would have never made it off Drum Island alive.

Attested in manga dialogue and actions: fanatically loyal to Wapol despite Wapol's cowardice and cruelty; sycophantic, echoing Wapol's boasts and insulting enemies. Shows cowardice when outmatched but participates willingly in Wapol's oppression of Drum (burning villages, hunting Dalton). Comic, somewhat dim-witted, reliant on Chess for strategy. In cover story, remains loyal during Wapol's absolute lowest point (homeless, eating trash), indicating genuine retainer bond rather than mercenary service. No independent ambition shown; identity is defined by service to Wapol.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Japanese kuro (黒, black) + marimo (毬藻, Aegagropila linnaei, the freshwater green algae ball known as moss ball) + English 'afro'. Name describes his most distinctive feature: a large, spherical black afro composed of marimo-like hair. Oda's naming follows the Drum Island pattern of cold/marimo-theme

Pronunciation — Japanese: ku-ro-maa-ri-mo /kɯɾomaːɾimo/ (クロマーリモ); English dub: koo-roh-mah-REE-moh

Aliases — Kuromārimo, クロマーリモ, Black Moss Ball

Ownership — Original fictional character created by Eiichiro Oda, owned and published by Shueisha (Weekly Shonen Jump), adapted by Toei Animation. Part of the One Piece franchise (1997-present

Powers
Marimo Afro Boomerang

Detaches portions or the entirety of his afro hair and throws them as blunt, fast, returning projectiles. The hair masses are dense and springy, capable of knocking opponents back and returning to his head like a boomerang. Can throw multiple small afro balls or one large afro.

Limit: Requires hair to regrow/return; if caught or destroyed, temporarily bald and defenseless. No cutting or explosive property. Range limited to throwing distance; no autonomous control after throw beyond return trajectory. Purely physical gag weapon, not Haki or Devil Fruit based.

Chessmarimo Fusion (with Chess)

When Wapol uses his Baku Baku no Mi (Munch-Munch Fruit) to merge Chess and Kuromarimo, they become the composite warrior Chessmarimo. In this form Kuromarimo provides the lower body/afro base and shared consciousness, gaining Chess's archery plus his own afro attacks combined, with four arms and increased size.

Limit: Fusion is entirely dependent on Wapol's Devil Fruit power; cannot self-fuse or maintain form without Wapol. Fusion is unstable and was defeated by Luffy and Sanji's combined attack. Personality becomes blended and bickering.

Retainer Combat and Endurance

Above-average durability for a minor antagonist; survives direct hits from Dalton, Luffy, and Sanji and recovers quickly for comic effect. Basic hand-to-hand and weapon throwing competence as former Drum Kingdom guard officer.

Limit: No Haki, no Devil Fruit, no formal martial art. Easily defeated by Straw Hats and Dalton; threat level is low compared to Grand Line standards. Ranked as East Blue/Paradise minor officer level.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Kuromarimo is a large, rotund adult man with light-brown skin, a perfectly spherical black afro that dominates his silhouette, and small round sunglasses that obscure his eyes. His afro is not normal hair but a dense mass of marimo-like filaments that can be detached and manipulated. He wears a mustard-yellow jumpsuit with a white fur-trimmed collar and cuffs (manga coloring varies to light brown/orange in anime), dark gloves, and a Drum Kingdom

Symbols — Visual pun on marimo (moss ball) + afro. His hair is both his name and weapon, referencing the Japanese love of dajare (wordplay) that Oda uses throughout Drum Island (Dalton = bison, Wapol = waffle/pole). Checkerboard motifs on Chess complement Kuromarimo's solid afro, together evoking a chessboard when fused. No religious or folklore iconography; purely Oda-designed gag-battle character.

Domains — Native to Drum Island (later renamed Sakura Kingdom, then Black Drum Kingdom), a winter island on the Grand Line. Initially barracks and castle of Drum Kingdom under Wapol's rule. After defeat, habitat becomes nomadic: with Wapol and Chess as the three-man Bliking Pirates (buriking = bliking, pun on buriki = tin), wandering the sea, then an unnamed island where Wapol builds his toy empire, then Mary Geoise during Reverie as royal attendants.

Accounts I
Service Under King Wapol - Tyranny of DrumOne Piece manga Ch. 134-135, 141 (flashback)

Canon timeline: 6 years before present (Wapol's reign), depicted 2000

Kuromarimo serves as one of Wapol's two chief officers alongside Chess, enforcing Wapol's cruel monopoly on medicine. When Wapol declares all doctors except his personal 20 must be exiled, Kuromarimo helps hunt Dr. Hiriluk and enforce the ban. He accompanies Wapol when the Blackbeard Pirates attack Drum, fleeing the island with Wapol and Chess rather than defending the kingdom, abandoning Dalton and the citizens. Drum Kingdom collapses into a medical crisis; Dalton is left as sole protector; Wapol's cowardice establishes his villainy. Sets up Kuromarimo's status as exile and pirate.

Return to Drum and Assault on the Sakura SummitOne Piece manga Ch. 135-143, Anime Ep. 82-86

Present timeline (Straw Hat arrival), 2000

After a year in exile as pirates, Wapol, Chess and Kuromarimo return to reclaim Drum Castle, which is now occupied by Dr. Kureha, Chopper, and the injured Straw Hats (Luffy, Sanji, Nami). Kuromarimo participates in the ascent of Drum Rockies, firing afro projectiles at Dalton who attempts to stop them, and later at the Straw Hats. He and Chess battle Dalton on the mountain path and defeat him via numbers. Dalton is critically wounded; Wapol reaches the castle for final confrontation; Dalton's defeat motivates Chopper's resolve.

Battle of Drum Castle - Chessmarimo vs. Straw HatsOne Piece manga Ch. 144-154, Anime Ep. 86-88

2000

Inside Drum Castle, Wapol merges Chess and Kuromarimo into Chessmarimo to fight the Straw Hats. As Chessmarimo, Kuromarimo's afro and Chess's arrows combine, overwhelming Luffy and Sanji initially. Luffy and Sanji argue over who fights, then defeat Chessmarimo with a combined attack (Luffy's Gum-Gum Bazooka and Sanji's kick). Wapol is then defeated by Luffy, and Kuromarimo and Chess are launched flying off the castle with him. Wapol's regime ends permanently; Drum is liberated; Chopper joins Straw Hats. Kuromarimo's defeat is comic but definitive; he is not seen on Drum again.

Accounts II
Wapol's Omnivorous Survival - The Bliking Pirates ExileOne Piece manga Cover Story serial, Ch. 236-241

2002 (cover pages)

Homeless and penniless after defeat, Wapol, Chess and Kuromarimo wander the sea as the Bliking Pirates (three-man crew). They are starving, eat trash, and are rejected by everyone. Kuromarimo remains loyally at Wapol's side, sharing his misery and helping him scavenge. This establishes the trio's rock-bottom status. Demonstrates Kuromarimo's loyalty beyond convenience; sets up Wapol's later redemption through industry.

The Toy Empire - From Scrap to RichesOne Piece manga Cover Story, Ch. 242-262

2002-2003

Wapol discovers his Baku Baku no Mi can create toys from scrap metal he eats. Kuromarimo and Chess help him build a toy factory on an unnamed island, manufacturing Wapol House toys. The business becomes wildly successful, making Wapol rich again. Kuromarimo transitions from pirate thug to factory worker/foreman, still in Wapol's service. Wapol becomes wealthy industrialist; later uses wealth to found the Black Drum Kingdom and gain World Government recognition. Kuromarimo's role shifts from combatant to industrial retainer.

Reverie - Return as Royal Retainers of the Black Drum KingdomOne Piece manga Ch. 906, 908, Anime Ep. 884, 887

2018 (Reverie Arc, two years after timeskip)

Two years later, Wapol has used his toy fortune to found the Black Drum Kingdom (Evil Black Drum Kingdom) and is recognized as a king invited to the Reverie at Mary Geoise. Kuromarimo and Chess appear in formal suits flanking Wapol and his new queen Kinderella, acting as royal attendants. They witness Wapol's petty confrontation with Vivi and Dalton (now king of Sakura Kingdom), but take no combat action. Confirms Kuromarimo survived the timeskip and remains in Wapol's service at the highest political level; provides continuity and comic callback. No further combat role as of latest chapters.

Kuromarimo has a Devil Fruit power that controls his afro hair. — He has no Devil Fruit. His afro throwing is a gag based on his marimo-like hair being detachable and springy, similar to other non-powered gag abilities in One Piece (e.g., Mr. 2 Bon Kurei's okama kenpo). Confirmed by Vivre Card and absence from any Devil Fruit list. The fusion Chessmarimo is due to Wapol's Baku Baku no Mi, not his own.

Kuromarimo and Chess are brothers or related. — No canon relationship beyond being fellow officers. They are not stated to be siblings, despite similar loyalty and paired design. Their names and designs are complementary (chess + marimo) but no family tie is given in manga, databooks, or SBS.

Kuromarimo died or disappeared after Drum Island. — He survived and appears continuously with Wapol in the cover story (Ch. 236-262) and at Reverie (Ch. 906). He is alive and employed as Wapol's royal attendant as of the latest canon.

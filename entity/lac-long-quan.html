Who It Is
Form

Lac Long Quan is often depicted as a powerful dragon king, with scales and a long tail. He is said to have the ability to control the oceans and the weather.

Powers

Control over the oceans Lac Long Quan is said to have the ability to control the oceans, summoning storms and calming the waves.

Accounts

Lac Long Quan is said to have married a fairy princess named Âu Cơ, who gave birth to a hundred eggs. From these eggs, the Vietnamese people were born.

Lac Long Quan is said to have been a wise and just ruler, who brought peace and prosperity to the Vietnamese people through his control over the oceans.

Who It Is

When the golden apple fell, it was not to the ground but into a pair of outstretched, moss-soft hands. Ceres Fauna — the antler-crowned Keeper of Nature who speaks to wind through leaves and to lonely viewers through a screen — arrived not as a goddess demanding worship, but as a druid offering tea, comfort, and the quiet promise that even withered things can bloom again.

Canon personality as performed: warm, motherly, soothing, and slightly airheaded (tennen). Speaks in soft, ASMR-adjacent cadence, often whispering to plants or viewers. Self-described as clumsy, forgetful, and easily flustered, but deeply empathetic — lore frames her as the Council member who most loves humanity and lingers longest among mortals. Streams focus on healing / comfort content: ASMR, nature sounds, drawing, low-stress games (Minecraft, Stardew Valley), and singing. Powers
Keeper of Nature - Chlorokinesis and Life Weaving

Ability to accelerate plant growth, bloom flowers instantly, heal wounded flora and fauna, and commune with animals. In lore trailer, her touch causes barren ground to sprout. In streams, framed as making tea from self-grown herbs and speaking to forest spirits.

Limit: Cannot reverse death or unnatural destruction caused by higher conceptual powers (e.g., Time erasure by Kronii). Golden apple is source/focus; if taken or corrupted, her power wanes. Lore states she feels pain when nature is harmed.

Golden Apple Protection

Guardian of the golden apple, described as 'the fruit of knowledge' that must not fall into wrong hands. Apple grants vitality and insight; Fauna's duty is to prevent its misuse.

Limit: She is protector, not owner; cannot consume it freely. Lore implies apple's power is tempting to others, requiring constant vigilance.

Healing and Purification (Druidic)

Soothing voice and presence that calms, heals emotional distress, and purifies miasma. In Hololive Alternative, depicted casting healing light.

Limit: Emotional/ spiritual healing, not resurrection. Requires proximity and willingness of recipient. No attested instant physical wound closure in main continuity.

Longevity and Seasonal Attunement

As a conceptual being, ageless and attuned to seasonal cycles; can enter dormancy in winter and reawaken in spring. Implied hibernation after graduation.

Limit: Not immortal in absolute sense; Council members can choose to end their watch (graduation = voluntary departure).

Animal Communion

Understands and befriends animals; often accompanied by small forest creatures, deer, and Saplings.

Limit: Communication is empathic, not mind control; wild predators remain dangerous.

Accounts I
The Council's Creation - The Gods Departhololive English -Council- Debut PV and individual debut streams

2021 (in-universe mythic time)

After shaping the world, the Gods create five envoys to watch over existence: Space (Sana), Time (Kronii), Civilization (Mumei), Nature (Fauna), and Chaos (Baelz). Each embodies a concept. Fauna is tasked with guarding the golden apple and tending the living world while the Gods are absent. The trailer shows her awakening in a sunlit forest, antlers catching light, as Kronii narrates their purpose: to observe, not interfere — a rule Fauna is already inclined to bend out of love for humans. Establishes Council cosmology and Fauna's core conflict: duty of detachment vs compassion for mortals. Foundation for all subsequent Council lore.

Hello Nature! - The Keeper Descends to StreamCeres Fauna Debut Stream (YouTube, 2021-08-23)

2021-08-23

In her first broadcast, Fauna introduces herself in-character then breaks into personable chat. She explains her antlers, shows her golden apple staff, attempts a clumsy self-introduction in Japanese, and declares she loves humanity so much she wants to stay close. She takes questions, draws her mascot, and ends by promising to create a soothing place where tired people can rest. The stream peaked at ~100k concurrent viewers and established her healing brand. Defined fan name Saplings, established ASMR/healing content direction, and set viewership record for Council debuts at the time.

The Great Sana Farewell and the Promise RebornTsukumo Sana Graduation Stream (2022-07-31) and Hololive English -Promise- Debut (2023)

2022-2023

When Tsukumo Sana (Space) graduated, the Council lost its cosmic anchor. Lore-wise, Space returned to the cosmos. Cover reformed the remaining Council members plus IRyS into a new unit called -Promise-, vowing to continue protecting the world together. Fauna's role shifted from one of five to one of four guardians who promise to stay with humanity. In subsequent 3D lives, Fauna openly mourned Sana while embracing the new promise, singing 'Kronii and friends' medleys that acknowledged loss and continuity. Major continuity shift; Council branding retired, Promise branding adopted. Fauna's lore now emphasizes chosen family and voluntary commitment rather than divine assignment.

Accounts II
Bloom and Beyond - The Keeper on StageHololive 4th fes. Our Bright Parade (Makuhari Messe, 2023-03-18) and hololive English 1st Concert -Connect the World- (2023-07-02)

2023

Fauna performed in full 3D for the first time at 4th fes., singing 'Let Me Stay Here' and group songs with Promise. At Connect the World in Los Angeles, she performed to a live English-speaking audience, her antlers rendered in 3D with leaf physics. Lore interludes depicted her using nature magic to illuminate the stage. These concerts marked her transition from stream-only existence to physical stage presence via motion capture. Solidified her as live performer; 3D model became canonical appearance for lore visuals.

The Druid's Rest - Graduation and Last BloomCover Corp. Graduation Announcement (2024-11-13) and Final Stream 'Last Bloom' (2025-01-03)

2024-2025

On 13 November 2024, Cover announced Fauna would graduate on 3 January 2025 to pursue new endeavors, citing mutual agreement. In lore terms, the Keeper decided her watch was complete and chose to return the golden apple to the forest and enter a long sleep, entrusting the world to humanity and her Promise sisters. Her final stream featured a concert, readings of letters from Kronii, Mumei, Baelz and IRyS, a slideshow of three years of memories, and a closing scene where she walks into a sunlit forest and fades among sprouts, whispering 'I'll be watching over you, my Saplings.' Character retired from active Hololive roster; VODs and music remain archived but no new streams. Marked second graduation in Promise/Council lineage, leaving three active Promise members.

Hololive Alternative - The Forest ClericHololive Alternative PV (2021) and teaser manga Chapter 3 (2022)

2021-2022

In the alternate fantasy universe Hololive Alternative, Fauna appears as a traveling druid cleric who heals adventurers and tends a shrine tree that bears golden apples. She is shown mending a wounded Baelz after a chaotic battle and offering shelter to Mumei's owl scouts. Though non-canonical to main Council continuity, it expands her archetype as healer and sanctuary-keeper. Provides secondary canon where Fauna's powers are visualized as game-like healing spells; influences fan art and merch designs.

Ceres Fauna is the Roman goddess Ceres herself — She is an original fictional character named after the goddess for thematic resonance. She has no mythological continuity with Roman religion, does not appear in classical texts, and her lore is entirely modern Hololive invention from 2021.

Fauna is an AI or fully automated VTuber — She is performed by a human actor using motion capture, Live2D, and voice. All speech, singing, and gameplay are live human performance, not generative AI. Cover has never used AI to replace talent performance.

Her graduation was due to conflict or firing — Cover's announcement and Fauna's own statements describe graduation as a mutual, amicable decision to allow her to pursue new endeavors, with gratitude expressed on both sides. No disciplinary action was cited, unlike terminations such as Uruha Rushia (2022).

Fauna's antlers are reindeer or sheep horns — Design is based on kirin/qilin branching antlers with leaf adornments, not reindeer or sheep. The 'Uber Sheep' meme arose from a joke where Fauna misheard 'kirin' and fans playfully called her a sheep.

Who It Is

The general term for entities in the Mandela Catalogue that mimic and replace humans. Alternates are not born — they are created by 'Gabriel' and spread through analog media (TV broadcasts, VHS tapes, phone calls). An alternate will study a target, learn their face and voice, then psychologically break them down (often through gaslighting and sleep deprivation) until the victim becomes susceptible to replacement. Once replaced, no one notices — except the victim's closest loved ones, who are then targeted next.

Form

Varies. Alternates can perfectly or imperfectly replicate a specific human. Imperfect copies have faces that are slightly wrong — eyes misplaced, mouth too wide or too narrow, proportions subtly off. In their 'default' form, they appear as shadowy figures with distorted, glitching facial features. They are frequently associated with TV static, VHS tracking artifacts, and audio distortion.

Powers

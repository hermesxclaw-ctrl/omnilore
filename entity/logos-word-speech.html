Who It Is

Logos (λόγος, 'Word/Speech/Reason') is the aeon that articulates the divine intellect into revealed expression. If Nous is the Father's silent self-perception, Logos is the 'utterance' that makes that perception communicable to the lower aeons. Paired with Zoe (Life), Logos ensures that the Father's self-revelation is not static intellect but living speech — a dynamic, generative principle that produces Anthropos (the Archetypal Human) and Ecclesia (the Spiritual Community). The Logos-aeon should not be confused with the Johannine Logos (though the terminology is shared): in Valentinian theology, Logos is one aeon among many within the Pleroma, not the unique incarnation of the Father.

The Approach

In the sprawling multiverse of CLAMP's Tsubasa: Reservoir Chronicle, few characters cut as striking a figure as Kurogane, a swordsman whose gruff exterior and lethal skill belie a profound loyalty and a painful past. He is a man of action in a tale of magic and destiny, yet his journey is as much about confronting his own demons as it is about battling the forces that threaten to unravel reality itself.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of violence, combat, and references to parental loss and revenge.
Identity

Kurogane

Pronunciation KOO-roh-gah-neh

Known As

KuroganeKuro-tanKurogane of the Black Sword

Etymology

Kurogane (黒鋼) literally means 'black steel' in Japanese, reflecting his dark attire and his formidable sword, as well as his stoic, unyielding nature.

Cultural Ownership

Created by the manga collective CLAMP, Kurogane originates from the Japanese manga series Tsubasa: Reservoir Chronicle (2003-2009). He is a fictional character in the broader CLAMP multiverse.

Disputed Identifications

There is no significant dispute over Kurogane's identity, though fans sometimes confuse him with similar sword-wielding characters from other CLAMP works. His counterpart in CLAMP's xxxHOLiC is the same character, but his backstory is explored more in Tsubasa.

Description

Appearance

Kurogane is a tall, muscular man with sharp, piercing eyes and short, spiky black hair. He typically wears a dark, form-fitting outfit with a high collar, often with a long coat or cape. He carries a large, straight sword (a ninjatō or chokutō) that is black in color, matching his name. His expression is usually stern or scowling, and he has a scar on his left cheek.

Symbols & Iconography

His black sword is a symbol of his identity and his role as a warrior. The color black represents his brooding nature and his past. He is also associated with the emblem of his home country, Japan (in the Tsubasa world, his home is the country of Japan, which is a real-world location in that universe).

Behaviour & Personality

Kurogane is initially portrayed as aggressive, short-tempered, and disdainful of others, particularly of the magician Fai. He is pragmatic and focused on strength and survival. However, as the story progresses, he reveals a deeply loyal and protective nature, especially towards his companions, particularly the young princess Sakura. He is honorable in his own way, respecting strength and courage, and he gradually learns to open up and trust others. He is also shown to be a skilled strategist and a fierce fighter who takes his duty seriously.

Domains & Habitats

Kurogane hails from the Japan of his world, a country that is at war. He travels across various worlds and dimensions as part of the group seeking Sakura's feathers. He is most at home in combat situations and is adaptable to different environments.

Version Differences

In the manga, Kurogane's backstory is more detailed, including his relationship with his mother and his former master. In the first anime adaptation, the story diverges significantly after a point, and Kurogane's role is somewhat altered. In the OVAs, which follow the manga more closely, his character is more consistent with the manga. In the sequel manga Tsubasa: World Chronicle (2014-2016), Kurogane returns with a slightly more mature demeanor.

Powers & Abilities

Expert Swordsmanship

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

Kurogane is an incredibly skilled swordsman, capable of defeating powerful opponents with ease. He uses a large black sword and is proficient in various techniques, including powerful slashes and defensive maneuvers.

Limitations

His skills are physical, not magical, so he cannot counter purely magical attacks unless he finds a way to neutralize them.

Contradictions

None noted.

Enhanced Physical Abilities

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

He has superhuman strength, speed, and endurance, allowing him to perform feats like jumping great distances and surviving powerful blows.

Limitations

These abilities are within human limits, though heightened.

Contradictions

None noted.

Hi no Kokuin (Seal of Fire)

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

A cursed seal placed on his right arm by his former master. It is a black tattoo-like mark that can activate when he uses his powers, causing him to lose control and become more aggressive. The seal can be suppressed by Fai's magic.

Limitations

The seal is a double-edged sword: it enhances his power but also makes him lose control, potentially harming allies.

Contradictions

In the anime, the seal is depicted slightly differently, but the core effect is the same.

Kurogane no Ken (Black Steel Sword)

Source Version Manga

Description

His sword is a powerful weapon that can cut through many substances. In the manga, it is later revealed that the sword is actually a demonic sword that can absorb powers and grow stronger.

Limitations

The sword's demonic nature may have side effects, though these are not fully explored.

Contradictions

None noted.

Stories & Myths

The Journey to Collect Sakura's Feathers

Source Tsubasa: Reservoir ChronicleCulture Japanese mangaPeriod 2003-2009

Summary

Kurogane is banished from his home country of Japan by his princess, Tomoyo, for his reckless and violent behavior. He is sent to the dimension witch Yūko, who sends him on a journey with Syaoran, Sakura, and Fai to collect Sakura's scattered memories, which have been transformed into feathers. Throughout the journey, Kurogane initially clashes with Fai but gradually forms a bond with the group. He becomes a protector of Sakura and a key fighter in battles against various enemies. He also learns about his own past, including the truth about his mother's death and his master's curse.

Consequences

Kurogane's journey allows him to confront his inner demons, reconcile with his past, and ultimately find a new purpose. He also helps save Sakura and the worlds from the villain Fei-Wang Reed.

Variants

The anime adaptation diverges in the second half, with an original storyline that does not follow the manga's final arcs. The OVAs follow the manga.

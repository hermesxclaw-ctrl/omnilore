Who It Is

In the sprawling multiverse of CLAMP's Tsubasa: Reservoir Chronicle, few characters cut as striking a figure as Kurogane, a swordsman whose gruff exterior and lethal skill belie a profound loyalty and a painful past. He is a man of action in a tale of magic and destiny, yet his journey is as much about confronting his own demons as it is about battling the forces that threaten to unravel reality itself.

Kurogane is initially portrayed as aggressive, short-tempered, and disdainful of others, particularly of the magician Fai. He is pragmatic and focused on strength and survival. However, as the story progresses, he reveals a deeply loyal and protective nature, especially towards his companions, particularly the young princess Sakura. He is honorable in his own way, respecting strength and courage, and he gradually learns to open up and trust others. He is also shown to be a skilled strategist and a fierce fighter who takes his duty seriously.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Kurogane (黒鋼) literally means 'black steel' in Japanese, reflecting his dark attire and his formidable sword, as well as his stoic, unyielding nature.

Pronunciation — KOO-roh-gah-neh

Aliases — Kurogane, Kuro-tan, Kurogane of the Black Sword

Powers
Expert Swordsmanship

Kurogane is an incredibly skilled swordsman, capable of defeating powerful opponents with ease. He uses a large black sword and is proficient in various techniques, including powerful slashes and defensive maneuvers.

Limit: His skills are physical, not magical, so he cannot counter purely magical attacks unless he finds a way to neutralize them.

Enhanced Physical Abilities

He has superhuman strength, speed, and endurance, allowing him to perform feats like jumping great distances and surviving powerful blows.

Limit: These abilities are within human limits, though heightened.

Hi no Kokuin (Seal of Fire)

A cursed seal placed on his right arm by his former master. It is a black tattoo-like mark that can activate when he uses his powers, causing him to lose control and become more aggressive. The seal can be suppressed by Fai's magic.

Limit: The seal is a double-edged sword: it enhances his power but also makes him lose control, potentially harming allies.

Kurogane no Ken (Black Steel Sword)

His sword is a powerful weapon that can cut through many substances. In the manga, it is later revealed that the sword is actually a demonic sword that can absorb powers and grow stronger.

Limit: The sword's demonic nature may have side effects, though these are not fully explored.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Kurogane is a tall, muscular man with sharp, piercing eyes and short, spiky black hair. He typically wears a dark, form-fitting outfit with a high collar, often with a long coat or cape. He carries a large, straight sword (a ninjatō or chokutō) that is black in color, matching his name. His expression is usually stern or scowling, and he has a scar on his left cheek.

Symbols — His black sword is a symbol of his identity and his role as a warrior. The color black represents his brooding nature and his past. He is also associated with the emblem of his home country, Japan (in the Tsubasa world, his home is the country of Japan, which is a real-world location in that universe).

Domains — Kurogane hails from the Japan of his world, a country that is at war. He travels across various worlds and dimensions as part of the group seeking Sakura's feathers. He is most at home in combat situations and is adaptable to different environments.

Accounts I
The Journey to Collect Sakura's FeathersTsubasa: Reservoir Chronicle

2003-2009

Kurogane is banished from his home country of Japan by his princess, Tomoyo, for his reckless and violent behavior. He is sent to the dimension witch Yūko, who sends him on a journey with Syaoran, Sakura, and Fai to collect Sakura's scattered memories, which have been transformed into feathers. Throughout the journey, Kurogane initially clashes with Fai but gradually forms a bond with the group. He becomes a protector of Sakura and a key fighter in battles against various enemies. He also learns about his own past, including the truth about his mother's death and his master's curse. Kurogane's journey allows him to confront his inner demons, reconcile with his past, and ultimately find a new purpose. He also helps save Sakura and the worlds from the villain Fei-Wang Reed.

Accounts II
Tokyo RevelationsTsubasa: Tokyo Revelations (OVA)

2007-2008

In this arc, the group is trapped in a ruined Tokyo where they must fight in a deadly game to survive. Kurogane faces off against a powerful opponent and is forced to use the Seal of Fire, leading to a loss of control. He also learns more about Fai's tragic past and his own connection to the curse. Kurogane gains a deeper understanding of Fai and his own role as a protector.

Spring ThunderTsubasa: Spring Thunder (OVA)

2009

In the final arc, the group confronts Fei-Wang Reed. Kurogane plays a crucial role in the final battles, ultimately helping to defeat the villain and restore balance to the worlds. He also confronts his own past and makes peace with it. Kurogane survives the final battle and returns to his home country, where he reconciles with his princess and his people.

Who It Is

An antimemetic entity — an idea so hostile and vast that knowing about it causes death. Specifically, any human who becomes aware of SCP-3125 is killed within 1-5 hours, often with their consciousness replaced by a 'fragment' of the entity. SCP-3125 exists in the 'noosphere' (the collective human ideosphere) and is slowly consuming all ideas that could threaten it. It has already devoured entire fields of mathematics and philosophy.

Form

No physical form. Perceived by persons under heavy mnestic influence as a 'spider made of knives and screaming' occupying the entirety of the conceptual horizon — an ideatic predator too large to comprehend.

Powers

Antimemetic camouflage

Idea consumption

Consciousness replacement

Total knowledge suppression

Cross-reality infection

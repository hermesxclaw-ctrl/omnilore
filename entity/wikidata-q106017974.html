Who It Is

In the hush of a Welsh night, a sound like a whispered command—'dewch, dewch'—might drift from the darkness. To those who know the old tales, it is not a summons to follow, but a herald of the end: the Aderyn y Corff, the corpse bird, perched invisibly at the threshold of the dying.

The bird is said to chirp outside the door of a person who is about to die. Its cry is interpreted as the Welsh word 'dewch' (come), which is perceived as a summons to death. It is not described as aggressive or malicious, but as a passive harbinger. It is said to reside on another plane of existence when not delivering its omen.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Welsh: aderyn = bird, corff = corpse/body; thus 'corpse bird' or 'bird of the corpse'.

Pronunciation — ah-DER-in uh KORFF (approximate; Welsh phonetics: /aˈdɛrɨn ə kɔrɸ/)

Aliases — Aderyn corff, Deryn corff, Aderyn y Corph (archaic)

Ownership — Welsh folklore; specifically associated with rural Wales and traditional death omens.

Death Omen

The bird's chirping outside a person's home is believed to foretell that person's death.

Limit: It does not cause death, only predicts it. It is not described as having any other powers.

Otherworldly Existence

Form & Signs

Appearance — Described as a bird with no feathers and no wings, giving it an unnatural, perhaps skeletal appearance. It is not depicted in traditional iconography; visual representations are modern and speculative.

Symbols — No historical symbols or iconography are associated with the Aderyn y Corff. It is primarily an auditory omen.

Domains — It is said to live on another plane of existence, but it appears in the mortal world near the homes of the dying, typically at night.

The Corpse Bird's CallWelsh folklore (oral tradition)

Unknown; likely pre-modern, but recorded in 19th-20th century collections

A person, often a family member or neighbor, hears a bird chirping outside the door of a sick or elderly person. The cry sounds like 'dewch' (come). Within a short time, that person dies. The bird is never seen, only heard. The death of the person; the hearer is left with the chilling knowledge that the omen was fulfilled.

Who It Is

In the glittering halls of the Crozzo family, where legendary magic swords once blazed, Welf Crozzo was born into a legacy of failure. Yet this young smith, branded a heretic for his refusal to forge weapons for the gods, would forge his own path—one that would lead him to the dungeon city of Orario and into the heart of a new legend.

Welf is hot-headed, passionate, and fiercely loyal to his friends. He has a strong sense of justice and a rebellious streak, having defied his family's expectations. He is pragmatic and resourceful, often thinking on his feet. Despite his tough exterior, he is caring and protective, especially towards Bell Cranel, whom he treats like a younger brother. He is also somewhat insecure about his family's tarnished name, but he channels that into a drive to create his own legacy.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Welf' is of Germanic origin, meaning 'wolf' or 'cub'. 'Crozzo' is an Italian surname, possibly derived from 'croce' (cross) or 'crozzo' (a type of club). The combination reflects a Western-inspired fantasy naming convention common in Japanese light novels.

Powers
Crozzo Magic Swords

Welf is capable of forging magic swords, a rare and valuable skill. However, unlike his ancestors who created powerful swords for the gods, Welf's swords are of lower quality and break easily. He is working to improve them, and his unique 'Will of the Forge' allows him to imbue his creations with his own magical energy, making them stronger than typical magic swords.

Limit: His magic swords are currently inferior to the legendary Crozzo swords, and they shatter after a few uses. He cannot yet replicate the power of his ancestors' creations.

Smithing Expertise

As a skilled blacksmith, Welf can create high-quality weapons and armor. He is knowledgeable about materials and metallurgy, and he can repair equipment in the field.

Limit: His expertise is limited to mundane and magical forging; he does not possess other magical abilities.

Combat Skills

Welf is a competent fighter, skilled in swordsmanship and using his crafted weapons. He often fights alongside Bell Cranel, providing support and covering fire with his magic swords.

Limit: He is not as powerful as top-tier adventurers like Ais Wallenstein, but he holds his own in battles.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Welf is a young man in his late teens with spiky red hair, often tied back in a small ponytail. He has sharp, determined eyes and a lean, athletic build. He typically wears a dark, sleeveless vest over a white shirt, with trousers and boots suited for dungeon exploration. His hands are calloused from forging, and he often carries a large hammer or a sword he has crafted.

Symbols — Associated with the anvil and hammer, symbolizing his smithing craft. His family crest, the Crozzo emblem, is a stylized flame or sword, representing the legendary magic swords. He is often depicted with a forge in the background, emphasizing his role as a blacksmith.

Domains — Primarily resides in the city of Orario, specifically in the Hephaistos Familia's workshop district. He is often found in his forge or exploring the Dungeon beneath Orario. His domain is that of a craftsman and adventurer.

Accounts I
The Exile of Welf CrozzoLight Novel Volume 4, Anime Season 1 (flashback)

Before the main story

Welf was born into the prestigious Crozzo family, renowned for crafting magic swords for the gods. However, his family's reputation declined as they became corrupt and obsessed with power. Welf, disgusted by their practices, refused to forge weapons for the gods and instead wanted to create swords for adventurers. This act of defiance led to his exile from the family. He traveled to Orario, hoping to become a blacksmith for the Hephaistos Familia, but was initially rejected due to his family's tarnished name. He eventually proved his worth and joined the familia. Welf's exile set him on a path to become an independent smith, and his encounter with Bell Cranel gave him a new purpose.

Accounts II
The War GameLight Novel Volume 4, Anime Season 1

Main story

Welf participates in a War Game against Apollo Familia to protect Bell Cranel. He uses his magic swords to fight, but they break, forcing him to rely on his wits. He ultimately helps Bell win the battle. Welf's loyalty to Bell is solidified, and he becomes a trusted member of the Hestia Familia.

The Forge of the CrozzoLight Novel Volume 6, Anime Season 1 (OVA)

Main story

Welf attempts to forge a powerful magic sword for Bell, but his efforts are hampered by his family's legacy. He ultimately creates a sword that is uniquely suited to Bell, showcasing his growth as a smith. Welf gains confidence in his abilities and begins to forge his own path, distinct from his family's.

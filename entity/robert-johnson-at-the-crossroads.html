Cultural Significance

Johnson's mythology completely overshadows his biography. He recorded only 29 songs, died at 27, and was buried in an unmarked grave (three different sites now claim to be his resting place). His myth has inspired countless works, from the Coen Brothers' 'O Brother, Where Art Thou?' to the TV series 'Supernatural.'

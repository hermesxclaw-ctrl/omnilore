Who It Is
Form

Robert Johnson is often depicted as a young man with a guitar, standing at a crossroads, with the devil lurking in the shadows. His appearance is shrouded in mystery, with some accounts describing him as a tall, dark figure, while others portray him as a small, agile man.

Powers

Musical Genius The ability to play the blues with unparalleled skill and emotion, moving audiences to tears and applause.

Accounts

The story of Robert Johnson at the Crossroads is a classic tale of the Faustian bargain, where a musician trades his soul for musical greatness. According to legend, Robert Johnson sold his soul to the devil at a crossroads in exchange for the ability to play the blues like no one else.

The 27 Club is a group of famous musicians who died at the age of 27, including Robert Johnson, Jimi Hendrix, and Janis Joplin. According to legend, Robert Johnson's death at the age of 27 was a result of his Faustian bargain with the devil.

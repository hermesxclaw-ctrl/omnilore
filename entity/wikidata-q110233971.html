Who It Is

She cracks a whip and a dozen cat-eared underlings snap to attention — not in a dungeon, but in a neon office tower ruled by a laptop with delusions of grandeur. Tasque Manager, the whip-smart middle-management Darkner of Deltarune's Cyber World, turns Windows Task Manager into a dominatrix-cat boss fight, and players can't decide whether to fear her, pity her, or recruit her.

Attested: Stern, commanding, and impeccably professional, with a dry, slightly theatrical sadism. She addresses the party as unruly employees and her Tasques as subordinates who must be 'managed.' Dialogue shows pride in efficiency ('It's time for your performance review!') and loyalty to Queen's regime, but also exasperation at being overworked. In battle she is confident and controlling, ordering Tasques to attack. Powers
Whip Mastery and Bullet-Hell Lash

Primary combat ability: cracks her whip to generate sweeping, curving bullet patterns that the SOUL must dodge. Whip creates multiple lash arcs and spawns Tasque projectiles. In overworld, whip crack stuns or orders Tasques.

Limit: Whip range is limited to battle box; can be interrupted if Tasques are put to sleep (Noelle's Iceshock/SleepMist) or if party uses ACT to calm. No damage outside battle context.

Tasque Command / Task Delegation

Can summon and direct 2-4 Tasques in battle, ordering them to pounce or fire. Lore-wise, she is the sole entity capable of organizing the otherwise feral Tasques into coherent patrols. ACT 'Order' allows Kris to interfere with command.

Limit: Control breaks if Tasques are tired or asleep; she cannot create Tasques, only command existing ones. If alone, her threat diminishes significantly.

Managerial Authority and Intimidation

Narrative power: enforces discipline among Darkners in Queen's Mansion. Other mansion staff defer to her; Tasques obey instantly. Represents middle-management authority within Queen's hierarchy, second only to Swatch and Queen.

Limit: Authority is delegated from Queen; she is herself subordinate and can be overruled or dismissed by Queen. No power over Light Worlders except within Dark World rules.

Recruitment Persistence (Meta-Mechanic)

If pacified rather than violently defeated, she becomes a recruitable NPC who relocates to Castle Town, contributing to town population count and unlocking extra dialogue. This is a game-mechanic 'power' of narrative persistence across chapters.

Limit: Requires non-violent approach; killing/defeating violently locks recruitment for that save. Does not grant combat assistance in later chapters beyond flavor.

Appearance — Attested: Tasque Manager is an anthropomorphic Darkner designed as an elegant adult feline woman, standing taller than the small Tasques. She has light-colored fur (off-white/cream in sprite), pointed cat ears, slitted eyes with heavy lashes, and a long tail. She wears a form-fitting black business suit with shoulder pads, a white collared shirt, a red tie, and high-heeled shoes, evoking a strict office manager and subtle dominatrix iconography.

Symbols — Core symbols are the whip (authority, discipline, task delegation), the computer 'Task Manager' window metaphor (control, termination of processes, ordering of tasks), and feline imagery (independence, aloofness, herding cats as impossible management). Accounts I
Debut: The Manager's Patrol in Queen's MansionDeltarune Chapter 2 — Queen's Mansion 2F hallway encounter

2021 (in-universe: one school afternoon)

The party (Kris, Susie, Ralsei) infiltrates Queen's Mansion to rescue Noelle. In a corridor lined with cat towers and monitors, Tasque Manager is introduced cracking her whip at a line of Tasques, berating them for inefficiency. She notices the intruders and declares a 'performance review,' initiating dialogue. This establishes her role as enforcer of Queen's will and introduces the Tasque species as her workforce. Triggers the mandatory mini-boss encounter; establishes the mansion's hierarchical satire and foreshadows the recruitment mechanic. Players first learn that Darkners have jobs tied to Light World objects.

Boss Battle: Performance ReviewDeltarune Chapter 2 — Tasque Manager battle (bullet-hell turn-based)

2021

Tasque Manager engages the party in a turn-based battle with bullet-hell dodging. She alternates whip lashes with Tasque-assisted pounces. The party can FIGHT, or use ACTs: 'Order' (Kris commands Tasques), 'Sleep' via Ralsei/Noelle, or spare after tiring. Her battle dialogue shifts from stern ('You need more discipline!') to flustered if Tasques are neutralized ('M-My Tasques!'). Defeat yields Dark Candy and progression toward the mansion's upper floors. Gate to Swatch's floor; determines recruitment flag. Violent victory gives EXP and leaves her absent from Castle Town; pacifist route adds her to Castle Town and counts toward 100% recruitment. Influences Chapter 2's ending flavor text.

Pacification and Recruitment: From Boss to TownsfolkDeltarune Chapter 2 — Post-battle recruitment system; Castle Town dialogue

2021

If the party tires or puts Tasques to sleep and then spares Tasque Manager, she is 'recruited' — a Chapter 2 mechanic where Darkners agree to move to Ralsei's Castle Town. In Castle Town, she appears near the café/dojo, commenting on organizing the town's tasks and keeping the other recruits in line. She expresses grudging gratitude and a desire to work more reasonably under Ralsei than under Queen. Contributes to Castle Town population; unlocks extra SAVE point dialogue and is referenced in Chapter 2's epilogue. Recruitment is required for the full 'Recruit Everything' completion and may affect future chapters' content per Toby Fox's notes that recruits will matter later.

Accounts II
Service Under Queen: The Middle Manager of the Cyber RegimeDeltarune Chapter 2 — Environmental storytelling; Swatch and Swatchling NPC dialogue; Queen's dialogue

2021 (in-universe: period of Queen's rule over Cyber World)

Lore assembled from NPC chatter reveals Tasque Manager as Queen's middle management: tasked with herding the notoriously independent Tasques, enforcing mansion protocol, and reporting to Swatch (head butler) and Queen. Swatchlings gossip that she works overtime and is strict because Queen demands 'total organization.' This paints her as both oppressor and oppressed — enforcing Queen's search for Noelle and Lightners while herself subject to Queen's capricious, meme-driven dictatorship. Contextualizes Cyber World's satire of corporate/IT hierarchy; explains why Tasques are found patrolling rather than roaming freely. Adds moral nuance: she is not purely villainous but a functionary in a larger system.

The Weird Route Divergence: Absence and ImplicationsDeltarune Chapter 2 — Snowgrave/Weird Route (hidden alternate route triggered by specific Noelle actions)

2021

In the Snowgrave Route, where Kris and Noelle are manipulated to freeze Darkners with Noelle's SnowGrave spell, the mansion's ecology changes. Tasque Manager's encounter can be avoided or ends with her fleeing as the party's violence escalates. Her absence in this route underscores the route's theme of breaking the game's recruitment/ mercy systems — the managerial order collapses when the party abandons non-violence. Datamined dialogue suggests cut lines where she would comment on the unnatural cold. Demonstrates narrative branching; her non-recruitment in Snowgrave locks Castle Town population and is cited by community as evidence that future chapters will punish/reward route choices. Thematically links workplace horror to player coercion.

Post-Chapter 2 Legacy: Castle Town OrganizerDeltarune Chapter 2 epilogue and Chapter 3 demo save-transfer (2021-2024)

2021-2024 (meta-release period)

After Chapter 2's conclusion, recruited Tasque Manager settles in Castle Town. Interacting with her yields flavor text about making schedules, disciplining the Rudinns, and trying to keep Tasques from napping on the job. She appears in crowd scenes during Chapter 2's festival ending. In the Chapter 3 demo's save-import, her presence is acknowledged with a single line if recruited, confirming persistence across chapters — a rare case of a mid-boss becoming a persistent NPC. Establishes long-term narrative importance of recruitment; fans speculate she may gain a larger role in later chapters as an organizer for Castle Town's expansion. Provides closure to her arc from antagonist to ally.

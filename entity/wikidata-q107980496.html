Form & Signs

Appearance — The characters in the One Piece universe have diverse appearances, including humans, dwarfs, giants, mermen, mermaids, fish-men, sky people, and minks.

Symbols — The Straw Hat Pirates' Jolly Roger, the Will of D., and the Devil Fruits are prominent symbols in the series.

Domains — The characters inhabit various domains, including the Grand Line, the Calm Belt, and the New World.

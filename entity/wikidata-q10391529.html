The Approach

In the hidden laboratories of Mugen Academy, a scientist with a heart of ice orchestrates the theft of human souls. Viluy, the Witches 5's master of technology, wields the power of the Tau Ceti Star System to bend minds and machines alike. But her cold intellect masks a desperate mission to save her dying homeworld—and a rivalry with Sailor Mercury that will test the limits of both their genius.

Identity

Viluy

Pronunciation VEE-loo-ee

Etymology

Viluy is named after the mineral 'viluite', a variety of vesuvianite. This fits the naming pattern of the Witches 5, who are named after minerals.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese manga and anime franchise, created by Naoko Takeuchi.

Disputed Identifications

None known.

Description

Appearance

Viluy is a young woman with long, straight dark hair and a fringe. She wears the standard Mugen Academy uniform, which includes a white sailor suit with a high collar and a red bow. In her Daimon form, she wears a white and gold bodysuit with a high collar, and her hair is styled with a headpiece.

Symbols & Iconography

Her Daimon symbol is a stylized 'V' or a representation of a virus, reflecting her control over technology and disease.

Behaviour & Personality

Viluy is cold, calculating, and arrogant. She believes in the superiority of technology and intellect, and she looks down on those she considers inferior. She is fiercely loyal to the Death Busters and views the Sailor Guardians as obstacles to be eliminated.

Domains & Habitats

She operates from Mugen Academy's laboratories, using her computer skills to monitor and manipulate the school's systems.

Version Differences

In the manga, Viluy is more prominent and engages in a direct battle with Sailor Mercury. In the anime, her role is similar but her defeat is slightly different, with Sailor Mercury using her knowledge of computer viruses to counter Viluy's attacks.

Description

Viluy can create and deploy computer viruses to disable electronic systems and potentially harm humans.

Her viruses can be countered by antivirus programs or by freezing the affected systems.

Stories & Myths

The Battle with Sailor Mercury

Source Sailor Moon manga, Act 31 and Act 32; Sailor Moon S, episode 112Culture JapanesePeriod 1994

Summary

Viluy is sent by Kaolinite to eliminate Sailor Mercury, who has been investigating Mugen Academy. Viluy traps Mercury in a computer-generated maze and attacks her with viruses, but Mercury uses her skills to counter and defeat Viluy.

Participants

Viluy

Sailor Mercury

Consequences

Viluy is destroyed by Sailor Mercury's Sabão Spray Freezing, and her Daimon form is purified.

Variants

In the anime, the battle is slightly different, with Mercury using her visor to analyze Viluy's attacks.

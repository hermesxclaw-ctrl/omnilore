Who It Is

A basic ninja technique that creates a tangible, corporeal clone of the user. Unlike a normal clone, shadow clones can interact with the physical world and can disperse upon taking damage, transferring their memories and experiences back to the user. The Multiple Shadow Clone Technique is a forbidden variant that creates a massive number of clones at once.

Powers

Creates physical copies, experience transfer on dispersal

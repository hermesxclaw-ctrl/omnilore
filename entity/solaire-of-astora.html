Who It Is

Solaire of Astora is the most beloved NPC in the Dark Souls series and one of the most iconic characters in video game history — a strange, sun-obsessed warrior who appears throughout the player's journey, always with a cheerful 'Jolly cooperation!' and an offer to help. Solaire wears humble chainmail armor decorated with a crudely painted sun emblem (which he made himself), a kettle-like helmet, and a green tunic with a bright red feather. His fighting style is straightforward and effective: he wields a Sunlight Straightsword and a basic shield, and his miracles center on the Lightning Spear — simple tools, but used with absolute conviction.

Solaire is an Undead, like the player, who journeyed to Lordran from his homeland of Astora in search of 'his very own sun.' What this means is deliberately ambiguous. He speaks of the sun with a reverence that borders on religious ecstasy, yet he is not a cleric or a zealot — more a philosopher-warrior who has found meaning in the sun's warmth and light. In a world defined by despair, decay, and inevitable hollowing, Solaire's unwavering optimism is a beacon. He is the only character who seems genuinely happy, who faces the darkness with a smile, whose purpose — however odd — gives him the strength to resist the despair that consumes every other Undead.

The tragedy of Solaire's questline is one of Dark Souls' most emotional arcs. If the player follows his story to its natural conclusion, Solaire's search for his sun leads him to Lost Izalith, where he discovers a 'Sunlight Maggot' — a parasitic creature that emits light — and, mistaking it for his sun, places it on his head. The maggot drives him mad, and the player encounters him as a hostile, hollowed shell, babbling about finally finding his sun. The player can prevent this fate by opening a shortcut (through ranking up in the Chaos Servant covenant, costing 30 Humanity) and killing the Sunlight Maggot before Solaire finds it — in which case Solaire survives, becomes somber upon realizing his sun is not here, but ultimately resolves to continue searching, and can be summoned for the final boss fight.

Solaire's popularity has endured for over a decade. 'Praise the Sun' — his signature gesture — has become a cultural meme far beyond the Dark Souls community, appearing in other games, internet culture, and even real-world events. His phrase 'Jolly cooperation' is synonymous with the multiplayer summoning mechanic. Solaire represents something rare in Dark Souls: genuine, uncomplicated goodness. He is not secretly evil, not a tragic monster, not a liar or schemer. He is just a man who loves the sun and wants to share its warmth. In a series defined by its darkness, Solaire is the light — and players have never forgotten it.

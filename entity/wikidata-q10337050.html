Who It Is

Deep in the forests of Gensokyo, where the mist clings to the riverbanks, there lives a kappa who would rather tinker with machines than terrorize humans. Nitori Kawashiro, the shy engineer of Youkai Mountain, hides behind optical camouflage and a toolbox full of inventions, embodying a modern twist on an ancient Japanese water spirit. Her story is one of technological curiosity, cultural bridges, and the quiet power of a creature who chooses creation over chaos.

Nitori is depicted as shy and introverted, especially around humans, but she becomes talkative and enthusiastic when discussing machines and technology. She is curious and inventive, often creating gadgets for various purposes. She is also helpful and cooperative, especially with those she considers friends, but she can be cautious and defensive if she feels threatened. In battle, she uses her inventions and water-based attacks, showing a tactical mind.

Powers
Optical Camouflage

Nitori can conceal herself from view, making her invisible or difficult to see. This is her primary defense mechanism when encountering humans.

Limit: She may not be able to maintain it indefinitely, and it may not fool all senses, as she can still be heard or detected by other means.

Water Manipulation

As a kappa, Nitori can control water, using it for attacks and possibly for other purposes. In her boss fight, she uses water-based danmaku patterns.

Limit: Limited to water sources; she may need a water source nearby to use her most powerful water attacks.

Engineering and Invention

Nitori is a skilled engineer, capable of creating various machines and gadgets, from water cannons to advanced technology. This is her most defining ability in later appearances.

Limit: Her inventions may be unreliable or have specific power requirements. She may not be able to create everything she imagines without resources.

Danmaku (Bullet Hell) Attacks

Like all Touhou characters, Nitori uses danmaku (bullet patterns) in battles. Her spell cards often involve water, frogs, or mechanical themes.

Limit: Danmaku is a game mechanic; in-universe, it is a form of danmaku dueling, which has rules and limitations.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Nitori is a young kappa with short, dark blue hair and red eyes. She wears a distinctive cap with a flat brim, often associated with kappa in popular culture, and a blue and white outfit with a skirt and a scarf. Her design includes a tail-like appendage and a small shell on her back, typical of kappa. In some official art, she carries a wrench or other tools, emphasizing her engineer role.

Symbols — Nitori is associated with water, rivers, and technology. Her symbol is often a wrench, representing her engineering skills. The kappa's iconic water-filled head plate is sometimes depicted, but in Nitori's case, it is often covered by her cap. Her color scheme of blue and white evokes water and cleanliness.

Domains — Nitori lives in the rivers and lakes of Youkai Mountain in Gensokyo. She is part of the kappa community that resides there, known for their advanced technology and secretive nature. She often works in her workshop near the river, where she develops her inventions.

Accounts I
Mountain of Faith (Stage 3 Encounter)Mountain of Faith

2007

As Reimu or Marisa climbs Youkai Mountain, they encounter Nitori, who warns them to turn back. She uses optical camouflage to hide from them, but is eventually forced to fight. She mentions that kappa and humans were once allies, but she is shy and does not want to interact with humans. The player defeats Nitori and continues their climb, leading to the discovery of the Moriya Shrine and the main conflict.

Accounts II
Subsequent Appearances and InteractionsVarious Touhou works (manga, fighting games, print works)

2008-present

Nitori appears in several spin-off games and manga, where she interacts with other characters. In Touhou Hisoutensoku, she is a playable character, showcasing her engineering skills. In Forbidden Scrollery, she helps humans with her inventions, showing her friendly side. In Wild and Horned Hermit, she assists in resolving incidents, proving her value to Gensokyo. Nitori becomes a well-known and respected engineer in Gensokyo, bridging the gap between kappa and humans.

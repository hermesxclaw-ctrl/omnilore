Etymology

The name 'Lee' is a common surname in English-speaking countries, while 'Pyron' may be derived from 'pyre' (a pile of combustible material for burning a corpse as part of a funeral rite) or 'pyro' (fire), possibly hinting at a connection to fire or purification, but this is speculative.

Appearance

Details are scarce. In the Shaman King manga, Lee Pyron is depicted as a middle-aged man with a distinctive mustache and glasses, often wearing a suit or formal attire. He is the butler of the Asakura family, serving Yoh's parents.

Lee Pyron is portrayed as a loyal and competent butler, dedicated to the Asakura family. He is often seen accompanying Yoh's parents and assisting in their affairs. He appears calm and professional.

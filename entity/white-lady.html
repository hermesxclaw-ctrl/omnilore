Who It Is
Form

The White Lady is typically depicted as a female ghost dressed in a white gown, often with a pale or ghostly complexion.

Powers

Appearance and Disappearance The White Lady is often said to appear and disappear suddenly, sometimes in a cloud of mist or smoke.

Accounts

The White Lady of Castello di Amorosa is a legendary ghost said to haunt the castle in Italy. She is believed to be the spirit of a woman who was murdered by her husband.

The White Lady of the Forest is a legendary ghost said to haunt the forests of Latin America. She is believed to be the spirit of a woman who was wronged by her lover.

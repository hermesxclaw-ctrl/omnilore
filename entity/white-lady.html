Who It Is

A widespread ghost archetype: a woman in white who appears as an omen of death or tragedy. Variants appear across cultures — the German 'Weiße Frau,' the Czech 'Bílá paní,' the Filipino 'White Lady of Balete Drive,' and others. The White Lady is typically a spirit of a woman who died tragically — murdered, betrayed in love, or lost her children — and now haunts bridges, roads, or castles. She is often a benevolent warning spirit in European traditions (the White Lady of the Hohenzollerns was said to appear before the death of a family member), but vengeful in others (the Filipino White Lady causes car accidents). The archetype represents unresolved feminine grief given spectral form.

Who It Is

Brad Wong is a drunken master whose inebriated stumbling belies a devastating martial arts prowess, a man who drifts through the Dead or Alive tournaments not for glory, but for the perfect bottle of sake. His life is a paradox: a wanderer with no fixed home, yet a fighter with an unshakable connection to the ancient art of Drunken Fist. As he raises his gourd to his lips, opponents see a fool; those who know better see a legend in the making, or perhaps, a legend already made.

Brad Wong is carefree, jovial, and seemingly always drunk. He is a wanderer who seeks the ultimate sake, treating life as a journey of pleasure and martial discovery. Despite his laid-back demeanor, he is a serious martial artist who respects strength and skill.

Powers
Drunken Fist (Zui Quan)

A Chinese martial art that mimics the movements of a drunkard, using unpredictable, swaying motions to confuse opponents and deliver powerful strikes. It is highly effective in close combat.

Limit: Requires a degree of intoxication to perform optimally, though Brad Wong has mastered it to the point where he can use it effectively even when sober.

Iron Fist

A legendary attack known for its awesome destructive power, associated with the xinyi liuhe quan style, which Brad Wong has mastered. This technique is not explicitly named in his moveset, but his mastery of the style suggests he possesses it.

Limit: Not explicitly detailed, but likely requires immense focus and physical conditioning.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Brad Wong is a Chinese man with a lean, wiry build, typically depicted in his 30s. He has dark, spiky hair and often a stubbly beard. His attire usually consists of loose, worn clothing, often with a traditional Chinese vest or jacket, and he carries a gourd or bottle of sake. His movements are fluid and swaying, as if constantly intoxicated.

Symbols — The sake gourd is his iconic symbol, representing his drunken master archetype. His fighting stance is a distinctive swaying posture, mimicking a drunkard's stumble.

Domains — He has no fixed home, often seen in various locations across the games, from Chinese streets to modern cities, always in search of fine alcohol.

1990s-2010s

Brad Wong enters the Dead or Alive tournaments primarily to fund his search for the world's finest sake, which he hopes to find through the prize money or by encountering a legendary brew. His story is a lighthearted journey compared to others, focusing on his personal quest rather than the tournament's overarching conflict. He continues to be a recurring character, often appearing as a comic relief, but his martial prowess earns him respect.

Who It Is

In a class of thirty-one middle-school girls - geniuses, swordswomen, vampires, and one time-traveling robot - Chizuru Naba is the one who makes sure everyone actually eats dinner. Tall, soft-spoken, and always in an apron, the self-appointed mother of Mahora Academy's Class 3-A doesn't cast fireballs or wield a pactio sword; she ladles soup, mends uniforms, and quietly tells her best friend Natsumi that being small doesn't mean being weak - and when the class is hurled into a collapsing Magic World, that stubborn, domestic kindness becomes its own kind of magic.

Attested: Consistently characterized as the class's 'mother' (hahaoya-teki, okaasan). Calm, nurturing, patient, and domestically competent - cooking, cleaning, mending, and caring for younger or more childish classmates. Best friend and caretaker to the shy, short-statured Natsumi Murakami, whom she defends and encourages (a reciprocal sisterly bond). Polite speech (desu/masu, gentle tone), rarely angry; when angered, shows quiet firmness rather than Asuna-style violence. Name & Tongue

Etymology — Japanese. Family name Naba (那波): 那 'that/what' + 波 'wave'. Given name Chizuru (千鶴): 千 'thousand' + 鶴 'crane' (the bird), a common feminine given name meaning 'a thousand cranes'. No in-universe etymology given; name follows Ken Akamatsu's pattern of auspicious/floral names for Class 3-A.

Pronunciation — Japanese: Naba Chizuru [na.ba tɕi.zɯ.ɾɯ] - NAH-bah chi-ZOO-roo. Hepburn: Naba Chizuru. English dub approximates 'chi-ZOO-roo NAH-bah'.

Powers
Domestic and Culinary Mastery (Non-Magical)

Exceptional human skill in cooking (especially home-style Japanese, bento, sweets), housekeeping, sewing, and childcare. Repeatedly shown feeding class, preparing festival food stalls, and caring for Negi and younger students. Official data book lists hobby as 'cooking' and special skill as 'taking care of people'. No magical component.

Limit: Human-level; requires time, ingredients, and physical effort. No combat application except morale/support.

Emotional Support and Group Cohesion

Social ability to calm conflicts, mediate, and provide maternal reassurance. Acts as emotional anchor for Natsumi Murakami (who suffers from low self-esteem about height/appearance) and for dorm mates. Not a supernatural empathy power, but narrative function as 'team mother'.

Limit: Interpersonal only; does not suppress magic or compel behavior.

Provisional Contract (Pactio) - Support Artifact (Manga Canon)

Forms a Provisional Contract (Pactio: contractus temporalis) with Magister Negi Springfield during the Magic World arc, as do most of Class 3-A. Her Pactio card is No.20. The artifact itself is rarely depicted in combat and is described in fanbooks as a support/healing-type item (often summarized as a magical apron/cookware or healing staff that enhances her caretaking - e.g., creating restorative food or minor healing). Unlike front-line fighters (Asuna, Setsuna, Kaede), Chizuru is not shown using artifact in major battle; its function is largely off-panel and ceremonial, confirming her membership in Ala Alba.

Limit: Requires Negi as Magister; artifact power is minor, non-combat, and consumes magical energy. Never shown to grant flight, weaponization, or large-scale magic. Exact artifact name/effect is inconsistently documented and never given a full battle demonstration, so details remain weakly attested.

Basic Magical Potential via Ala Alba Training

As part of Ala Alba, receives rudimentary magic training for Magic World travel (barrier, potion use). No innate magic talent like Nodoka or Yue; classified as 'normal human' with low magical aptitude in data book.

Limit: Cannot cast spells independently; relies on artifacts or Negi's support.

Accounts I
Class 3-A Introduction - The 31 Girls of MahoraMahou Sensei Negima! manga Vol.1, Ch.1-3 'The New Teacher' / XEBEC anime Ep.1

2003 (in-universe: April 2003, Mahora Academy spring term)

Negi Springfield, 10-year-old Welsh mage, arrives as homeroom teacher for Class 3-A (31 girls). Chizuru Naba is introduced in roll call as Seat No.20, the tall, gentle, apron-wearing student who immediately offers Negi a home-cooked welcome and looks after the dorm. Early chapters establish her motherly dynamic - scolding Asuna for violence toward Negi, comforting Natsumi, and organizing class meals. No major plot focus; establishes archetype and dorm pairing with Natsumi Murakami. Establishes Chizuru's support role and Natsumi friendship that persists through series; positions her as domestic anchor for later arcs.

Kyoto School Trip Arc - The Kansai Magic AssociationManga Vol.4-6, Ch.28-51; XEBEC anime Ep.12-16

2003-2004 serialization (in-universe: summer trip to Kyoto)

Class 3-A travels to Kyoto for school trip. While focus is on Konoka, Setsuna, and Asuna's encounter with Kansai mages, Chizuru participates as part of the general class group - helping with logistics, cooking at ryokan, and watching over younger students during youkai attacks. She has no direct battle role; her contribution is keeping the non-combatants safe and calm during Evangeline's and Chigusa's interference. In anime, she appears in group defense scenes but dialogue minimal. Reinforces Chizuru as non-combat support; no character development specific to her, but maintains presence for later ensemble arcs.

Mahora Festival Arc (Mahorafest) - The School Festival and Negi's SecretManga Vol.11-18, Ch.92-168; XEBEC anime Ep.22-26 (partial)

2005-2006 serialization (in-universe: late summer festival, 3 days)

Mahora Academy's massive cultural festival becomes a battleground as Negi tries to keep his magic secret while Chao Lingshen plots to reveal magic to the world. Chizuru runs food stalls and dorm hospitality with Satsuki Yotsuba and Natsumi, providing meals for the overworked class. During Chao's robot invasion and time-travel plot, Chizuru helps evacuate civilians and supports Natsumi, who struggles with courage. She is among the students who learn of Negi's magic and choose to support him, though she does not yet form a Pactio. Her cooking becomes literal morale support during the 3-day crisis. Class solidarity solidifies; Chizuru's decision to support Negi leads to later Pactio in Magic World. Festival arc is fan-favorite and most adapted in anime/games.

Accounts II
Journey to Mundus Magicus - The Magic World ArcManga Vol.19-32, Ch.169-300

2007-2010 serialization (in-universe: autumn 2003, travel via gate to Magic World)

Negi and Ala Alba (volunteer Class 3-A members) travel to the Magic World to find Negi's father Nagi. Chizuru is part of the support contingent that travels, staying primarily at the Ariadne base camp and later in Ostia. She does not join front-line battles against Fate Averruncus's forces or the gladiator tournament, instead maintaining camp, cooking, healing, and caring for injured allies. During this arc she formally forms a Provisional Contract (Pactio) with Negi (revealed in Ch.318 card spread), receiving her artifact, symbolizing her commitment to Ala Alba. Her most notable personal moment is encouraging Natsumi to be braver and supporting her when Natsumi forms her own Pactio and gains confidence. Chizuru becomes officially pactio-bound, tying her fate to Negi's party; her support role is canonized. Arc expands worldbuilding and sets up final battle. No combat feat for Chizuru, but narrative confirms her loyalty.

Final Arc - The Mage of the Beginning and the Fate PlanManga Vol.33-38, Ch.301-355 (Finale)

2010-2012 serialization (in-universe: 2003-2004, Magic World collapse and return to Mahora)

Fate's plan to save the Magic World by sacrificing the Mundus Magicus population is revealed, and the true enemy - the Mage of the Beginning (possessing Negi's father) - emerges. Chizuru returns to Mahora with the class as the Magic World begins to collapse. She participates in the final defense of Mahora during the invasion of Fate's forces and the rescue of Asuna (the Twilight Princess). Her role remains logistical and supportive - sheltering civilians, providing first aid, and maintaining morale - while heavy combat is handled by Negi, Jack Rakan, Evangeline, and pactio fighters. In the epilogue (Ch.355), Chizuru is shown in the future class reunion (7 years later), having pursued her dream of working with children (implied kindergarten teacher / caretaker). Resolution of Magic World crisis; Chizuru's future confirms her maternal archetype fulfilled outside battle. Series ends with Negi's fate ambiguous; UQ Holder! later reveals more.

Anime-Original - Negima!? Star Crystal and Chupacabra ArcNegima!? anime Ep.1-26, especially Ep.5, 12, 19 (Shaft, 2006-2007)

2006-2007 broadcast

In Shaft's reimagining, Negi must collect Star Crystals while fighting the armored monster Shichimi. Chizuru appears as the dorm mother who cooks for Negi and the class, with exaggerated comedic beats - giant ladle, chibi transformation, and 'mama' jokes. Episode 5 focuses on a cooking contest where Chizuru and Satsuki compete; Episode 19 has a dorm ghost story where Chizuru comforts frightened students. No Pactio or Magic World travel; her role is purely episodic comic relief and caretaker. Establishes alternate characterization that emphasizes comedy over manga's subtle support role; considered non-canonical by manga purists but popular for Shaft's visual style.

Chizuru Naba is an adult teacher or dorm mother, not a student — She is a 14-15-year-old middle school student in Class 3-A, Seat No.20, same age as classmates. Her tall height (167cm), mature build, and motherly behavior cause viewers to misread her as older, but she is explicitly a student, not staff. The actual dorm mother is a separate adult character.

Chizuru has innate magic powers or is a mage like Negi, Konoka, or Evangeline — She is a normal human with no innate magical talent (Data Book: magical aptitude low). She only gains minor magical support via Provisional Contract (Pactio) with Negi in the Magic World arc, and even then her artifact is non-combat support. She cannot cast spells independently.

Chizuru and Natsumi Murakami are sisters or related by blood — They are best friends and dorm roommates, not siblings. Their close, sister-like bond and shared room lead to fan assumption of familial relation, but no blood relation is stated in manga or data book. They are paired for narrative contrast (tall/maternal vs short/shy).

Chizuru's Pactio artifact is a powerful combat weapon (e.g., giant ladle that smashes enemies) — No canonical battle shows her using artifact offensively. Game adaptations (Konami fighters) invent ladle attacks for gameplay, but manga shows no combat use. Her artifact is support/healing and largely off-panel.

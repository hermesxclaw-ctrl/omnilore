The String-String Fruit, a Paramecia that allows the user to create, control, and manipulate virtually invisible threads of near-infinite sharpness and tensile strength. The user can puppeteer people like marionettes, create defensive cages of razor wire, bind clouds to grant limited flight, and transform their own body into a mass of cutting strings.

Who It Is
Form

The Ito Ito no Mi is a Paramecia-type Devil Fruit that allows the user to create and manipulate strings, making the user a String Human. The fruit itself is not visually described in the One Piece canon.

Powers

String Manipulation The user of the Ito Ito no Mi can create and manipulate strings, allowing them to bind, entangle, or even cut through objects and enemies.

Accounts

The story of the red string of fate is an ancient Japanese myth that tells the tale of two people destined to be together, connected by a magical red string.

The story of Donqui and the Ito Ito no Mi is a relatively recent addition to the One Piece franchise, telling the tale of a former Warlord of the Sea who eats the Ito Ito no Mi and becomes a String Human.

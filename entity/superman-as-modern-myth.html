Who It Is
Form

Superman is typically depicted as a tall, muscular man with a strong jawline, blue eyes, and a distinctive 'S' shield on his chest. He wears a blue and red costume, complete with a cape and a pair of glasses. In some versions, he is shown with a more athletic build or a different facial structure.

Powers

Super Strength Superman's ability to possess incredible physical strength, which allows him to lift massive objects and battle powerful villains.

Accounts

The story of how Superman's parents, Jor-El and Lara, sent him to Earth as a baby to escape the destruction of Krypton. Superman grows up on Earth, where he develops his powers and becomes a hero.

The story of how Superman is killed by Doomsday, a powerful alien creature. The event has a significant impact on the DC Universe and leads to a number of changes in the Superman mythos.

Scholarly Debates
Superman's powers
Superman's powers are based on his Kryptonian physiology and are a result of his exposure to Earth's yellow sun. ⇌ Superman's powers are a result of his exposure to Kryptonite, a radioactive mineral from his home planet.
What We Don't Know

There are several gaps in the Superman mythos that have been explored in various stories and adaptations. These include the origins of Krypton, the nature of Superman's powers, and the fate of his parents.

A way to hold it in your mind

Superman is a cultural icon who has been a part of American popular culture for over 80 years. He has been portrayed in various forms of media, including comic books, films, and television shows, and has become a symbol of hope and inspiration for many people.

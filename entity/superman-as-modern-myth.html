Who It Is

The superhero archetype — an alien sent to Earth as an infant by his dying father, raised by Kansas farmers, who uses godlike powers to protect humanity. Grant Morrison describes Superman as 'the perfect man from the sky' — a secular messiah who bridges ancient sun-god mythology and science fiction.

Attested in Action Comics #1 (1938)

Cultural Significance

Superman embodies the immigrant narrative (the ultimate outsider who chooses to belong), the American dream, and the philosophical question of what a truly good person would do with absolute power. Every superhero created since 1938 is in dialogue with Superman.

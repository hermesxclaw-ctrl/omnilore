Who It Is

She is ten years old, wears a Federacy uniform three sizes too big, demands to be called Empress, and can look at you and see every ghost you carry. Augusta Frederica Adel-Adler — who insists you call her Frederica Rosenfort — is the last child of an empire that built a war of extermination and then was consumed by it, and now she rides with the very soldiers it tried to erase.

Frederica presents as precocious, imperious, and theatrical — insisting on being addressed as 'Her Majesty' or 'Empress' while simultaneously demanding sweets, throwing tantrums, and clinging to Shin. This childish imperiousness is explicitly a coping mechanism. Underneath, she is profoundly guilt-ridden, survivor-guilt driven, and hyper-mature: she blames herself for failing to stop the Empire's Legion program and for Kiriya's transformation, and she views her survival as a debt to be repaid by protecting others. Powers
Adel-Adler Clairvoyance - Past and Present Perception

Hereditary ability of the Giadian imperial house Adel-Adler. By having met or come to know a person, Frederica can perceive that person's past and present — seeing their memories, current location, current actions, and current sensory experience as a form of remote viewing. Described as seeing 'the past and present of anyone she has known.' She can also perceive the Legion's Shepherds and Black Sheep because they contain copied human brains she has a connection to, allowing her to locate specific Legion units.

Limit: Cannot see the future. Cannot read thoughts or emotions directly, only infer from observed past/present. Requires prior acquaintance or at least knowing the target's name/face — cannot arbitrarily view strangers. Range is effectively unlimited once connection established, but perception is limited to that individual's perspective, not omniscient. Using ability extensively causes fatigue and emotional distress, especially when viewing traumatic memories. Does not work on those she has never met.

Imperial Education and Tactical Acumen

Raised as Empress-in-training, Frederica received elite education in history, politics, military strategy, etiquette, and multiple languages (Giadian, San Magnolian). Despite age, she can analyze battlefield maps, identify Legion types, and provide strategic suggestions to Grethe Wenzel and Ernst. She acts as a de facto intelligence asset for locating Kiriya/Morpho.

Limit: No formal military rank or command authority; advice is advisory only. Knowledge is theoretical, not combat experience. Age and emotional bias (especially regarding Kiriya) can cloud judgment.

Legion Detection via Shepherd Connection

Because Shepherds are Legion units containing the brain scans of dead humans she knew (especially Kiriya), she can sense and track them. This allows the Federacy to locate the Morpho railgun and other Shepherd-led Legion formations. She can distinguish Black Sheep (damaged copies) from Shepherds.

Limit: Only works on Legion units containing a person she knew or whose brain pattern she has been exposed to. Cannot detect standard Legion drones without human brains. Emotional trauma when sensing Kiriya.

Political Symbolism and Authority (Latent)

As the last surviving Adel-Adler, she holds immense symbolic legitimacy. Federacy President Ernst Zimmerman protects her precisely because executing or exiling her would undermine the Federacy's claim to have broken with the Empire. Her public existence legitimizes the Federacy; her death would be politically destabilizing.

Limit: No legal power; she has renounced the throne and insists on 'Rosenfort' to distance herself from imperial guilt. Cannot command troops. Her status makes her a target for Imperial loyalists and Legion-obsessed factions.

Fictional timeline: Imperial Calendar ~213-214 (approx. 9-10 years before main story, when Frederica was 4-5 years old)

Augusta Frederica Adel-Adler is born as the sole daughter of Emperor August and Empress Josephine Adel-Adler, the last absolute monarchs of the Giadian Empire. Sheltered in the Imperial Palace in Sankt Jeder, her only genuine human connection is her knight and attendant Kiriya Nouzen, a mixed-blood Giadian noble assigned to protect her. When Emperor August initiates the Legion autonomous war against neighboring nations, Frederica, though a child, senses the cruelty of the war. As the Empire collapses under the Legion's revolt (the Legion turning on its creators after the Empire's downfall), revolutionaries led by Ernst Zimmerman overthrow the monarchy. The entire imperial family is executed — except Frederica, whose life is spared by Ernst because she is a child and because he has lost his own family. Ernst adopts her, gives her the surname Rosenfort to hide her imperial identity, and raises her as his daughter in the new Federacy of Giad. After the revolution, Ernst Zimmerman — who has lost his wife and daughter to the war — raises Frederica as his own child in the presidential residence. He is overprotective to the point of suffocation, forbidding her from approaching the front lines because he cannot bear to lose another daughter. Frederica is educated, well-cared for, but isolated; she is the only child in a house of adults and soldiers. She develops a sharp, teasing personality to cope with loneliness and learns to hide her ability unless needed. When Ernst takes in the five surviving members of Spearhead Squadron (Shin, Raiden, Theo, Anju, Kurena) after they are rescued from San Magnolia's suicide mission, Frederica immediately latches onto Shin because his demeanor, scent, and bearing remind her of Kiriya. She declares herself their mascot and insists on accompanying them, against Ernst's wishes. During operations in the Holy Theocracy — a theocratic nation that has been secretly sacrificing its own citizens (including children designated as Sirin) to create Legion-like drones — Frederica's ability becomes crucial and traumatic. The Sirin are brain-scanned children used as Legion processors, and Frederica can perceive their pasts: their abduction, indoctrination, and fear. The Promise to Bury the Dead: Frederica and Shin's Shared Vow86 -Eighty Six- Volume 3 Epilogue, Volume 6 Chapter 5, Volume 10 interludes

Fictional timeline: Federacy Year 217-218 (recurring motif)

A quieter, recurring narrative thread: Frederica and Shin bond over their shared habit of carrying the dead. Shin carries the dog tags of fallen comrades, promising to bury them when the war ends; Frederica carries the memory of her family and Kiriya, and the weight of being the last Adel-Adler. In Volume 3's epilogue, after Kiriya's death, Frederica asks Shin to one day bury Kiriya's fragment properly, and Shin promises to bury all of them together when the war is over — including Frederica's family, if she wishes. In later volumes, Frederica is shown tending a small shrine in the barracks with Shin, and she tells Lena that Shin is 'learning to want to live' because he now has someone (Frederica, and by extension Lena) to protect rather than just ghosts to carry. This vow becomes a symbolic anchor for the Strike Package's hope that the war will end. Transforms Frederica from a plot device (ability) into an emotional core: she gives Shin a reason to imagine a future. Frederica is just a cute mascot / comic relief with no plot importance. — She is the central driver of the entire Volume 2-3 Morpho arc, the only person who can locate Kiriya, and the emotional catalyst for Shin's growth. Her mascot role is in-universe cover for her intelligence asset status, and her trauma is treated seriously.

Frederica is Shin's biological sister or blood-related to the Nouzen clan. — She is unrelated by blood to Shin. She is adopted by Ernst alongside Shin's group, making them legally adoptive siblings. Her connection to Shin is via Kiriya Nouzen, who was Shin's distant relative and her knight — she projects Kiriya onto Shin initially, but this is not a blood tie.

Frederica can see the future or control the Legion. — Her Adel-Adler ability is strictly past and present perception of known individuals, including Shepherds. She cannot predict the future, read minds, or command Legion units. She can only observe and report.

Frederica is a pilot or fights in Juggernauts/Reginauld. — She never pilots a Feldreß. She is explicitly a non-combatant due to age and status, serving as intelligence support and mascot. She has no personal Juggernaut or callsign.

Her real name is Frederica Rosenfort and Adel-Adler is a title. — Her birth name is Augusta Frederica Adel-Adler; Rosenfort is the adopted surname Ernst gave her to conceal her identity after the revolution. She prefers Rosenfort to distance herself from imperial guilt, but Adel-Adler remains her bloodline name.

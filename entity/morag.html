Who It Is
Form

Morag is described as a large, brown, elongated creature with distinctive three dorsal humps. Its size and shape are often compared to those of the Loch Ness Monster. However, unlike Nessie, Morag is said to have a more slender build and a longer body.

Powers

Swimming Morag is said to be an excellent swimmer, able to navigate the depths of Loch Morar with ease.

Accounts

A local fisherman claims to have seen Morag swimming in the loch, describing it as a large, brown creature with three dorsal humps.

Morag is seen as a symbol of the Scottish Highlands, representing the region's rich cultural heritage and unique ecosystem.

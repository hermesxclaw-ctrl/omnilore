Form & Signs

Appearance — Morag is described as a large, brown, elongated creature with distinctive three dorsal humps. Its size and shape are often compared to those of the Loch Ness Monster. However, unlike Nessie, Morag is said to have a more slender build and a longer body.

Symbols — Morag is often associated with the Loch Morar region and is said to be a symbol of the area's rich cultural heritage.

Domains — Morag is said to inhabit Loch Morar, a freshwater lake in the Scottish Highlands. The loch is known for its deep waters and unique ecosystem.

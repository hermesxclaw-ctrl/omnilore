Who It Is

In the moonlit forests of Myanmar, where the ancient pagodas cast long shadows, there lurks a guardian of the wild—Byat Wi, a colossal ogre whose name is whispered with a mixture of fear and reverence. Unlike the demons of Western lore, Byat Wi is not a mere monster but a complex figure, a protector of sacred places and a keeper of ancient oaths.

Byat Wi is believed to be a protector of the wilderness and its inhabitants. He is fierce towards those who harm the forest or sacred places, but may be benevolent to those who show respect. Like many nat, he may be appeased with offerings.

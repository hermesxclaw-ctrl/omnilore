Who It Is

Molag Bal, the Daedric Prince of Domination, is one of the most feared and reviled figures in the Elder Scrolls mythos—a cold, calculating god who seeks to enslave all mortal souls and drag them into his plane of Coldharbour. His name is spoken in whispers, his cults are hunted, and his influence is felt in the darkest corners of Tamriel.

Molag Bal is cruel, calculating, and utterly obsessed with domination. He delights in breaking the will of others, forcing them to serve him, and corrupting the innocent. He is patient and scheming, preferring to manipulate and corrupt over direct confrontation, though he is also capable of immense violence.

Powers
Domination

Molag Bal has the ability to dominate and enslave the wills of mortals and Daedra alike. He can force obedience and control minds.

Limit: It is not absolute; strong-willed individuals can resist or break free.

Soul Harvesting

He can trap and collect souls, using them as currency or power sources. His realm is filled with soul-shriven, beings whose souls have been ripped out.

Limit: Requires the soul to be captured or the victim to be weakened.

Immortality

As a Daedric Prince, he is immortal and cannot be permanently killed. He can be banished or defeated in his plane, but he will eventually reform.

Limit: None known.

Realm Control

Within Coldharbour, he has absolute control over the environment, space, and its inhabitants. He can reshape it at will.

Limit: His power is limited to his own plane; he cannot exert the same control on Nirn.

Corruption

He can corrupt individuals, twisting their minds and bodies, turning them into his servants or monsters.

Limit: Some individuals resist or are too pure to be fully corrupted.

Mace of Molag Bal

A powerful Daedric artifact that he grants to his champions. It can absorb stamina and magicka, and in some versions, it can capture souls.

Limit: The mace is a physical artifact and can be lost or destroyed.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Molag Bal is typically depicted as a tall, muscular humanoid with grayish or pale skin, sharp features, and a menacing aura. He often wears dark, spiked armor and carries a mace. In his true form, he may appear as a massive Daedric entity, but his avatar in the games is usually a powerful humanoid.

Symbols — His symbol is a stylized mace or a spiked, angular design. The color scheme associated with him is dark red and black. His plane, Coldharbour, is a twisted mirror of Nirn, often depicted with chains, spikes, and a cold, blue-gray palette.

Domains — He rules the plane of Oblivion known as Coldharbour, a desolate, cold, and twisted version of Nirn. He is associated with domination, enslavement, corruption, and the harvesting of souls.

The Creation of Molag BalThe Elder Scrolls Online: Loremaster's Archive

Pre-Mythic

Molag Bal was born from the interplay of Padomay and Nirn, specifically from the concept of domination and the desire to control. He is one of the original Daedric Princes. He became the embodiment of domination.

The Daughters of ColdharbourThe Elder Scrolls Online

Second Era

Molag Bal kidnapped mortals, particularly women, and subjected them to horrific rituals, transforming them into vampires or other corrupted beings. These victims became known as Daughters of Coldharbour. Creation of vampires and other undead servants.

Accounts II
The PlanemeldThe Elder Scrolls Online

Second Era (2E 582)

Molag Bal attempted to merge his plane, Coldharbour, with Nirn, using dark anchors and the souls of mortals. This event was the central conflict of ESO, and was ultimately thwarted by the Vestige and the alliances. The Planemeld was stopped, but Molag Bal was not destroyed. He was banished back to Coldharbour.

Third and Fourth Era

A quest in which the player is sent to a house haunted by Molag Bal's influence. The player must assist a priest and eventually confront a corrupt individual, ultimately earning the Mace of Molag Bal. The player gains the Mace of Molag Bal.

All eras

Cults dedicated to Molag Bal exist throughout Tamriel, often performing dark rituals, kidnapping victims, and seeking to bring about his influence on Nirn. Persecution of cultists and occasional Daedric incursions.

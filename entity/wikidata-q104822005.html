Who It Is

In the sprawling, often brutal world of That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime, where demons scheme and dragons clash, one hobgoblin stands as proof that loyalty and quiet competence can carve a legend of their own. Gobta, the cheerful and unexpectedly capable leader of the Goblin Riders, began as a minor footnote in Rimuru Tempest's growing nation—yet his journey from timid scout to one of the most trusted commanders in the Jura Tempest Federation is a story of growth, friendship, and the unassuming power of heart.

Gobta is cheerful, boisterous, and somewhat naive, but he is also fiercely loyal to Rimuru and his friends. He is brave and eager to prove himself, though he often doubts his own abilities. He has a strong sense of duty and responsibility, especially after being promoted to captain. He is also quite talkative and enjoys boasting, but he is humble when it counts. He is known for his friendly rivalry with other commanders and his love of good food and drink.

Powers
Hobgoblin Physiology

As a hobgoblin, Gobta possesses enhanced strength, speed, and endurance compared to a regular goblin. He is also more intelligent and has a longer lifespan.

Limit: Still far weaker than high-ranking monsters like Rimuru or the Demon Lords.

Swordsmanship

Gobta is a skilled swordsman, having trained under Hakuro. He uses a sword in combat and is proficient enough to hold his own against weaker enemies.

Limit: His skill is average compared to other named fighters like Benimaru or Hakuro.

Leadership

Gobta leads the Goblin Riders, a unit of goblins mounted on wolves. He coordinates their tactics and inspires loyalty.

Limit: Initially inexperienced, but grows into the role.

Riding

Gobta is an expert rider, able to fight effectively while mounted on his wolf partner.

Limit: None.

Unique Skill: None

Gobta does not possess a Unique Skill like some other characters. He relies on physical abilities and tactics.

Limit: This means he cannot compete with powerful magic users or skill holders.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Gobta is a hobgoblin, a larger and more humanoid goblin species. He has green skin, pointed ears, and a generally muscular build for a goblin. His hair is dark and spiky. He often wears a green and brown leather armor, with a small cape. He carries a sword and later a spear. In the anime, his design is more detailed, with a friendly, expressive face.

Symbols — Gobta is often associated with the symbol of the Jura Tempest Federation, a wolf head emblem. As captain of the Goblin Riders, he is also associated with the wolf mounts. His personal symbol could be considered the goblin tribe's banner, which he carries in battle.

Domains — Gobta primarily resides in the Jura Tempest Federation, specifically in the goblin village that becomes Rimuru's capital. He is often seen in the capital, but he also travels with his riders across the federation and beyond on missions.

Accounts I
The Naming of GobtaLight Novel Volume 2, Manga Chapter 5, Anime Season 1 Episode 4

Contemporary fantasy

Gobta is a goblin in a village that is attacked by a direwolf pack. Rimuru saves the village and names the goblins, which evolves them into hobgoblins. Gobta is one of the named goblins, and he gains increased intelligence and strength. Gobta becomes a loyal follower of Rimuru and begins his path as a warrior.

Formation of the Goblin RidersLight Novel Volume 4, Manga Chapter 22, Anime Season 1 Episode 14

Contemporary fantasy

After the goblins and direwolves form an alliance, Rimuru suggests creating a mounted unit. Gobta is chosen as the captain due to his enthusiasm and rapport with the wolves. The Goblin Riders become a key military force for Tempest, and Gobta gains a position of responsibility.

Battle Against the Orc LordLight Novel Volume 3, Manga Chapter 15, Anime Season 1 Episode 9-10

Contemporary fantasy

Gobta participates in the war against the Orc Lord and his army. He fights alongside other goblins and witnesses Rimuru's power. The war ends with the defeat of the Orc Lord, and Gobta gains battle experience.

Accounts II
The Falmuth WarLight Novel Volume 6, Manga Chapter 30, Anime Season 2 Episode 22-23

Contemporary fantasy

When the Falmuth Kingdom attacks Tempest, Gobta fights to defend his home. He is among the survivors who are captured and tortured, but he remains defiant. The war ends with Rimuru's resurrection and the destruction of the Falmuth army. Gobta's resilience strengthens his bond with Rimuru.

The Demon Lord Clayman IncidentLight Novel Volume 8-9, Manga Chapter 40, Anime Season 2 Episodes 35-36

Contemporary fantasy

Gobta is sent as a spy to the Demon Lord Clayman's domain, where he gathers intelligence and later participates in the battle against Clayman's forces. Clayman is defeated, and Tempest's position in the world is solidified. Gobta's espionage skills prove valuable.

The Demon Lord WalpurgisLight Novel Volume 9, Manga Chapter 42, Anime Season 2 Episode 36-37

Contemporary fantasy

Gobta accompanies Rimuru to the Walpurgis, the meeting of Demon Lords, as part of his entourage. He witnesses the political maneuvering and the battle between Rimuru and Clayman. Rimuru becomes a Demon Lord, and Gobta gains a deeper understanding of the world's power structure.

The Eastern Empire ConflictLight Novel Volume 12-15, Manga Chapter 50+, Anime Season 3 (upcoming)

Contemporary fantasy

When the Eastern Empire invades Tempest, Gobta and the Goblin Riders play a role in the defense, though they are outclassed by the Empire's technology and magic. The war results in heavy losses, but Tempest ultimately prevails with Rimuru's power. Gobta survives and continues to serve.

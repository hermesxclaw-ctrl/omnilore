The Approach

In the sprawling, often brutal world of That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime, where demons scheme and dragons clash, one hobgoblin stands as proof that loyalty and quiet competence can carve a legend of their own. Gobta, the cheerful and unexpectedly capable leader of the Goblin Riders, began as a minor footnote in Rimuru Tempest's growing nation—yet his journey from timid scout to one of the most trusted commanders in the Jura Tempest Federation is a story of growth, friendship, and the unassuming power of heart.

Content advisory. Contains fantasy violence, battle scenes, and depictions of war. No explicit sexual content, but some mild suggestive humor in the source material. No real-world dark history.

Etymology

The name 'Gobta' is a portmanteau of 'goblin' and the Japanese name 'Ta' (or 'ta' as in 'taisou'?). More likely, it is a playful corruption of 'goblin' combined with a typical Japanese name suffix. In the original Japanese, his name is ゴブタ (Gobuta), which is a direct combination of 'goburin' (goblin) and 'ta' (a common name syllable). The name was likely chosen by the author to sound both goblin-like and endearing.

Cultural Ownership

Gobta is a fictional character created by Fuse (author) and Mitz Vah (illustrator) for the Japanese light novel series 'Tensei Shitara Slime Datta Ken' (That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime). The franchise is owned by Micro Magazine (publisher) and Kodansha (for the manga), with anime adaptation by 8bit. The character is part of a global media franchise and is not based on any real-world cultural figure.

Disputed Identifications

No major disputes exist regarding Gobta's identity. Some fans mistakenly confuse him with other goblin characters in the series, but his distinct role as leader of the Goblin Riders and his unique naming by Rimuru are unambiguous.

Description

Appearance

Gobta is a hobgoblin, a larger and more humanoid goblin species. He has green skin, pointed ears, and a generally muscular build for a goblin. His hair is dark and spiky. He often wears a green and brown leather armor, with a small cape. He carries a sword and later a spear. In the anime, his design is more detailed, with a friendly, expressive face.

Symbols & Iconography

Gobta is often associated with the symbol of the Jura Tempest Federation, a wolf head emblem. As captain of the Goblin Riders, he is also associated with the wolf mounts. His personal symbol could be considered the goblin tribe's banner, which he carries in battle.

Behaviour & Personality

Gobta is cheerful, boisterous, and somewhat naive, but he is also fiercely loyal to Rimuru and his friends. He is brave and eager to prove himself, though he often doubts his own abilities. He has a strong sense of duty and responsibility, especially after being promoted to captain. He is also quite talkative and enjoys boasting, but he is humble when it counts. He is known for his friendly rivalry with other commanders and his love of good food and drink.

Domains & Habitats

Gobta primarily resides in the Jura Tempest Federation, specifically in the goblin village that becomes Rimuru's capital. He is often seen in the capital, but he also travels with his riders across the federation and beyond on missions.

Version Differences

In the web novel, Gobta's role is similar but less developed. The light novel expands his backstory and character. The manga and anime follow the light novel closely, but some minor scenes are omitted or added for pacing. In the anime, his voice and mannerisms are more exaggerated for comedy.

Contradictions & Disputes

Gobta's combat ability compared to other named monsters

Position A

Gobta is portrayed as weak and often the butt of jokes, unable to match the elite fighters of Tempest.

Position A — evidence

In the anime and light novel, Gobta is often shown struggling in fights, and other characters comment on his lack of strength.

Position B

Gobta is a competent leader and skilled enough to survive major battles, indicating he is above average.

Position B — evidence

Gobta successfully leads the Goblin Riders and participates in major wars, suggesting he is capable.

Scholarly Consensus

No formal consensus, but the narrative clearly places Gobta as a mid-tier fighter, strong among goblins but outclassed by the main cast.

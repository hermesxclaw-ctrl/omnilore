Who It Is

In the sprawling, spirit-haunted metropolis of Tengu City, one high school student is burdened with a power that could save the world or shatter it: the ability to seal away the very forces that threaten humanity. Shido Itsuka, an ordinary boy with an extraordinary destiny, must navigate a labyrinth of emotions, battles, and impossible choices—where every victory comes at a cost, and every heart he touches could be the key to salvation or destruction.

Shido is initially portrayed as an ordinary, somewhat timid high school student. He is kind-hearted, empathetic, and deeply cares for others, often putting himself in danger to help spirits. He is determined and willing to take on immense responsibility, but he struggles with self-doubt and the weight of his mission. He is also surprisingly perceptive and charismatic when it counts, able to connect with others on an emotional level.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Shido' (士道) can be interpreted as 'warrior's path' or 'samurai way', reflecting his role as a protector and negotiator. 'Itsuka' (五河) is a surname that combines 'five' and 'river', possibly alluding to his connection with multiple spirits.

Pronunciation — SHEE-doh EET-suh-kah

Aliases — The Sealer, The One Who Seals, Spirit of Origin, The Final Spirit

Ownership — Japanese light novel and anime franchise, created by Koushi Tachibana and illustrated by Tsunako, published by Fujimi Shobo under the Fantasia Bunko imprint.

Powers
Sealing

Shido has the unique ability to seal the powers of Spirits, beings who cause spatial quakes and possess immense destructive capabilities. This is achieved by making the Spirit fall in love with him and sharing a kiss, which transfers their power into a sealed state within him.

Limit: The sealing process requires the Spirit's genuine romantic feelings. If the Spirit is not truly in love, the seal fails. Additionally, Shido can only seal one Spirit at a time, and the process is emotionally taxing for both parties.

Power Sharing

After sealing a Spirit's power, Shido can temporarily use a fraction of that Spirit's abilities, such as enhanced strength or elemental control, by invoking their name.

Limit: Using this power drains Shido's stamina and can cause severe physical strain. Overuse can lead to unconsciousness or injury.

Time Manipulation (via Inverse Spirit form)

In certain critical moments, Shido can manifest an 'inverse' form that grants him the ability to manipulate time, allowing him to rewind events to prevent tragedies.

Limit: This ability is extremely costly, requiring a significant emotional toll and potentially leading to memory loss or mental instability.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Shido is a teenage boy with short brown hair and brown eyes. He typically wears his school uniform, but when using his powers, he is often depicted with a glowing aura or intricate markings. In some forms, he gains a more ethereal appearance, with eyes that shift to a golden hue.

Symbols — His power is often symbolized by a glowing hand, representing his ability to 'seal' spirits through physical contact. He is also associated with the imagery of a key, unlocking the hearts of spirits.

Domains — His primary domain is Tengu City, a fictional metropolis in Japan, which serves as the main setting for the series. He is often seen at Raizen High School, his school, and at the Fraxinus, the airship used by the Ratatoskr organization.

Accounts I
The First Sealing: Tohka YatogamiDate A Live Volume 1

2011

Shido encounters Tohka Yatogami, a Spirit who has just appeared in Tengu City, causing a spatial quake. With guidance from Ratatoskr, he learns that he must make Tohka fall in love with him to seal her powers. Through a series of awkward and heartfelt interactions, Shido succeeds, forming a deep bond with Tohka and beginning his journey as the sealer of Spirits. Tohka's powers are sealed, but she becomes a constant companion and ally to Shido. This event sets the precedent for his mission and introduces the central conflict with DEM Industries and other threats.

Accounts II
The Origin of SpiritsDate A Live Volumes 12-14

2015

Shido learns of the true origin of the Spirits and the existence of the Spirit of Origin, a powerful entity that created the other Spirits. He must confront the truth about his own power and his connection to the Spirits, leading to a revelation that challenges his identity and purpose. Shido discovers that his power to seal is intertwined with the Spirit of Origin's legacy, and he must make a choice that could either save or doom the world.

The Final BattleDate A Live Volumes 20-22

2020-2021

Shido faces the ultimate threat as the world teeters on the brink of destruction. He must use all his powers and the bonds he has forged with the Spirits to overcome the final antagonist and bring about a new era of peace. The conflict resolves with Shido achieving a form of victory, but at great personal cost, including the loss of some memories and the transformation of his relationship with the Spirits.

Who It Is

A radio signal originating from Proxima Centauri carrying a message in over 4,000 human languages: an invitation to join an interstellar community of sentient species. The signal includes instructions for FTL travel, free energy, and universal translation. The catch: it requires a living human volunteer to be permanently transformed into a crystalline 'beacon' to guide the aliens to Earth.

Form

SCP-7999-1 is a humanoid figure composed of translucent blue crystal, frozen in a posture of reaching upward, endlessly broadcasting humanity's acceptance of the invitation.

Powers

Interstellar communication relay

FTL technology blueprint transmission

Biological-to-crystalline transformation

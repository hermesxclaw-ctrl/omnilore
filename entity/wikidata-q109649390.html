Comparative Context: Hans Christian Andersen — The Little Mermaid (Den lille Havfrue)Hans Christian Andersen, Eventyr, fortalte for Børn (1837)

19th century (Romantic literary folklore)

NOT a Magdalena story — included as the most influential modern mermaid narrative for comparison. A youngest mermaid princess (unnamed in original, later called Ariel in Disney) longs for the human world, saves a prince from drowning, bargains with the Sea Witch to trade her voice for legs, endures agonizing pain with each step, fails to win the prince's love, and chooses to dissolve into sea foam rather than kill him, becoming a daughter of the air. The tale codified the tragic, voiceless mermaid and the soul-price transformation motif that dominates later reception. Established the modern template for mermaid-as-yearning-outsider; directly influences film, ballet, and literature; scholarly readings emphasize sacrifice, voice, and Christian soul theology.

Who It Is
Form

SCP-500 is a small, glass vial containing a clear liquid. The vial is approximately 5 cm in length and 1 cm in diameter. The liquid inside appears to be a clear, colorless substance.

Powers

Healing SCP-500 is capable of healing any wound or ailment, regardless of its severity or cause.

Incident Log

The story of SCP-500 begins with its discovery in a small, abandoned laboratory. The object was found in a glass vial, surrounded by various medical equipment and notes detailing its properties and abilities.

The story of SCP-500 continues with its use in various medical experiments. The object is found to be highly effective in treating a range of ailments, including cancer and other terminal diseases.

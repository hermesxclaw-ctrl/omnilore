Who It Is

SCP-500 is a small plastic pill bottle containing exactly forty-seven red pills at the time of Foundation acquisition. Each pill, when ingested by a living organism, cures all diseases affecting that organism within a matter of minutes. The effect is absolute and unprecedented — cancer, viral infections, bacterial infections, genetic disorders, radiation poisoning, prion diseases, and even anomalous afflictions have all been completely reversed by SCP-500. The pills have even demonstrated effectiveness against SCP-008 (the zombie plague) and partial effectiveness against SCP-610 (the Flesh that Hates), making them one of the most medically valuable substances in existence. The drug works by inducing a rapid, complete regeneration of all damaged or diseased tissue, while simultaneously granting the body a temporary enhanced immune response that eliminates any pathogens. The pills are completely non-toxic regardless of dosage, though only one is needed for full effect. The Foundation has never been able to replicate SCP-500. The original manufacturer is unknown; the bottle was discovered in a sealed medical cache in an abandoned hospital in ███████, Russia, alongside documentation suggesting the pills were created by a defunct Soviet-era biomedical research program. Attempts to synthesize the compound have failed — the pills' chemical composition includes several elements that should not exist in stable form at room temperature. The dwindling supply of SCP-500 is a source of constant tension within the Foundation. Each use must be approved by the O5 Council, and the ethical debates around who receives the cure and who does not are a recurring theme in associated tales. The last pill's eventual consumption is treated as an impending crisis. SCP-500 represents the paradox of a perfect solution in finite quantity — the knowledge that a cure for everything exists, but there isn't enough for everyone, and the Foundation must decide who lives and who dies.

Who It Is

SCP-500 is a highly anomalous object, capable of healing any wound or ailment. It is said to be the key to unlocking new treatments for various ailments, and its discovery has had a significant impact on the SCP Foundation.

SCP-500 is a highly anomalous object, capable of healing any wound or ailment. It is said to be able to cure even the most grievous of injuries, including those that would be fatal to a normal human.

Form & Signs

Appearance — SCP-500 is a small, glass vial containing a clear liquid. The vial is approximately 5 cm in length and 1 cm in diameter. The liquid inside appears to be a clear, colorless substance.

Symbols — The vial is adorned with a small, silver label bearing the symbol of the SCP Foundation. The label reads 'SCP-500 - Panacea' in small, silver letters.

Domains — SCP-500 is said to be able to function in any environment, regardless of temperature, pressure, or other external factors.

Accounts I
SCP-500 - The PanaceaSCP Foundation

2006

The story of SCP-500 begins with its discovery in a small, abandoned laboratory. The object was found in a glass vial, surrounded by various medical equipment and notes detailing its properties and abilities. The discovery of SCP-500 has had a significant impact on the SCP Foundation, leading to a renewed focus on medical research and the development of new treatments for various ailments.

Accounts II
SCP-500 - The Miracle CureSCP Foundation

2007

The story of SCP-500 continues with its use in various medical experiments. The object is found to be highly effective in treating a range of ailments, including cancer and other terminal diseases. The use of SCP-500 in medical experiments has led to a significant improvement in the treatment of various ailments, saving countless lives.

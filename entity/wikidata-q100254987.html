Who It Is

In the neon-lit underworld of Runeterra, a demon wears the skin of desire itself. Evelynn, Agony's Embrace, is not merely a monster—she is a predator of the highest order, feeding on the exquisite torment of her victims. But beneath the seductive facade lies a question that haunts her very existence: what happens when the hunt becomes a search for meaning?

Evelynn is a sadistic and hedonistic demon who derives pleasure from the agony of others. She is cunning, manipulative, and patient, often toying with her prey before striking. She views humans as playthings and has no empathy or remorse. However, she is also characterized by a deep-seated loneliness and a search for something that can truly satisfy her, hinting at a more complex inner life.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Evelynn' is a variant of 'Evelyn', derived from the Norman French form of the Germanic name 'Aveline', meaning 'desired' or 'wished for'. In the context of the character, the name may evoke a sense of allure and longing, fitting her seductive nature.

Pronunciation — EV-uh-lin

Aliases — Agony's Embrace, Evelynn, Agony's Embrace, the Widowmaker, the Demon of Agony

Ownership — Created by Riot Games for the multiplayer online battle arena game League of Legends. She is a fictional character within the game's universe, Runeterra.

Powers
Demon Shroud

Evelynn becomes invisible after a short delay, and regenerates health when out of combat. This allows her to ambush enemies and escape danger.

Limit: The invisibility is broken when she attacks or takes damage. She can be revealed by true sight or towers.

Hate Spike

Evelynn fires a line of spikes that damages the first enemy hit. This ability can be cast multiple times in quick succession.

Limit: Requires target in range; can be dodged.

Allure

Evelynn charms an enemy champion, causing them to walk toward her, while she gains movement speed. This ability is used to initiate fights and catch targets.

Limit: Only affects champions; can be cleansed or blocked by spell shields.

Whiplash

Evelynn lashes out with her claws, damaging and slowing enemies. This ability also grants her attack speed.

Limit: Short range; can be dodged.

Last Caress

Evelynn's ultimate ability. She becomes untargetable and deals massive damage to enemies in an area, while gaining a shield. If the enemy is below a certain health threshold, she deals bonus damage.

Limit: Long cooldown; can be dodged if timed well.

Demonic Possession (lore)

In her lore, Evelynn is a demon who can possess and manipulate mortals, feeding on their pain and suffering. She can also shape-shift to appear as a beautiful woman to lure victims.

Limit: Not explicitly defined; she is not invincible and can be killed by powerful beings.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Evelynn is depicted as a tall, slender woman with pale skin, sharp features, and long, dark hair. She wears a revealing black and purple outfit that accentuates her seductive and dangerous nature. Her eyes often glow with a violet hue, and she has retractable claws that she uses in combat. In her K/DA appearances, she adopts a more modern, glamorous look with stylish clothing and bold makeup.

Symbols — Her signature weapon is a set of razor-sharp claws. She is often associated with the color purple, representing her dark and mysterious nature. In K/DA, her symbol is a stylized 'E' with a heart-like shape, reflecting her seductive persona.

Domains — She is a demon that inhabits the mortal realm, often lurking in cities and dark places where she can find prey. In Runeterra, she is known to wander the world, particularly in areas with high population density where she can easily hunt.

Accounts I
The Demon's OriginOfficial lore (Universe of League of Legends)

Ancient, pre-Runeterra

Evelynn is an ancient demon who has existed for centuries, feeding on the agonies of mortals. She has no true origin story; she simply exists, a creature of pain and suffering. She has hunted across the world, leaving a trail of broken souls. She has become one of the most feared demons in Runeterra, though she remains largely unknown to the general populace.

Accounts II
The Weight of ExpectationShort story by Riot Games

Modern (in-universe)

The story follows Evelynn as she stalks a noblewoman in the city of Piltover. She toys with her prey, revealing her sadistic nature and her growing boredom with the hunt. It also explores her internal conflict: she has lived for so long that she is beginning to question the purpose of her existence. Evelynn ultimately kills Lady Eloise, but the experience leaves her feeling empty, suggesting a potential character development arc.

K/DA: POP/STARSMusic video and associated media

2018-present

In this alternate universe, Evelynn is a member of the K-pop group K/DA, alongside Ahri, Akali, and Kai'Sa. She is portrayed as a confident and glamorous pop star, though her seductive and dangerous personality is still present in her stage presence. The group's debut song 'POP/STARS' became a viral hit, and Evelynn gained a new fanbase.

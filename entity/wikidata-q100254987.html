The Approach

In the neon-lit underworld of Runeterra, a demon wears the skin of desire itself. Evelynn, Agony's Embrace, is not merely a monster—she is a predator of the highest order, feeding on the exquisite torment of her victims. But beneath the seductive facade lies a question that haunts her very existence: what happens when the hunt becomes a search for meaning?

Content advisory. Contains themes of murder, torture, sadism, sexual manipulation, and psychological horror. Described in the context of a fictional game.

Etymology

The name 'Evelynn' is a variant of 'Evelyn', derived from the Norman French form of the Germanic name 'Aveline', meaning 'desired' or 'wished for'. In the context of the character, the name may evoke a sense of allure and longing, fitting her seductive nature.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Riot Games for the multiplayer online battle arena game League of Legends. She is a fictional character within the game's universe, Runeterra.

Disputed Identifications

There is no scholarly dispute over her identity, but within the League of Legends community there are debates about the extent of her canon lore versus her gameplay portrayal. Some fans conflate her with other female champions with similar thematic elements, but these are unofficial fan theories.

Description

Appearance

Evelynn is depicted as a tall, slender woman with pale skin, sharp features, and long, dark hair. She wears a revealing black and purple outfit that accentuates her seductive and dangerous nature. Her eyes often glow with a violet hue, and she has retractable claws that she uses in combat. In her K/DA appearances, she adopts a more modern, glamorous look with stylish clothing and bold makeup.

Symbols & Iconography

Her signature weapon is a set of razor-sharp claws. She is often associated with the color purple, representing her dark and mysterious nature. In K/DA, her symbol is a stylized 'E' with a heart-like shape, reflecting her seductive persona.

Behaviour & Personality

Evelynn is a sadistic and hedonistic demon who derives pleasure from the agony of others. She is cunning, manipulative, and patient, often toying with her prey before striking. She views humans as playthings and has no empathy or remorse. However, she is also characterized by a deep-seated loneliness and a search for something that can truly satisfy her, hinting at a more complex inner life.

Domains & Habitats

She is a demon that inhabits the mortal realm, often lurking in cities and dark places where she can find prey. In Runeterra, she is known to wander the world, particularly in areas with high population density where she can easily hunt.

Version Differences

In her original 2010 lore, Evelynn was a human assassin who was corrupted by dark magic. In a 2014 lore update, she was reimagined as a demon who has existed for centuries, feeding on human suffering. Her K/DA persona is a separate, non-canonical portrayal where she is a pop star, though it has influenced her in-game skins and some fan perception.

Powers & Abilities

Demon Shroud

Source Version All versions (gameplay)

Description

Evelynn becomes invisible after a short delay, and regenerates health when out of combat. This allows her to ambush enemies and escape danger.

Limitations

The invisibility is broken when she attacks or takes damage. She can be revealed by true sight or towers.

Contradictions

None within the game; lore implies she can hide from mortals but not from other supernatural beings.

Hate Spike

Source Version All versions (gameplay)

Description

Evelynn fires a line of spikes that damages the first enemy hit. This ability can be cast multiple times in quick succession.

Limitations

Requires target in range; can be dodged.

None.

Allure

Source Version Current version (gameplay)

Description

Evelynn charms an enemy champion, causing them to walk toward her, while she gains movement speed. This ability is used to initiate fights and catch targets.

Limitations

Only affects champions; can be cleansed or blocked by spell shields.

Description

Evelynn's ultimate ability. She becomes untargetable and deals massive damage to enemies in an area, while gaining a shield. If the enemy is below a certain health threshold, she deals bonus damage.

Demonic Possession (lore)

Source Version Lore (post-2014)

Description

In her lore, Evelynn is a demon who can possess and manipulate mortals, feeding on their pain and suffering. She can also shape-shift to appear as a beautiful woman to lure victims.

Limitations

Not explicitly defined; she is not invincible and can be killed by powerful beings.

Contradictions

Her original lore depicted her as a human assassin with no supernatural powers beyond stealth.

Summary

Evelynn is an ancient demon who has existed for centuries, feeding on the agonies of mortals. She has no true origin story; she simply exists, a creature of pain and suffering. She has hunted across the world, leaving a trail of broken souls.

Consequences

The Weight of Expectation

Source Short story by Riot GamesCulture Fictional (Runeterra)Period Modern (in-universe)

Summary

The story follows Evelynn as she stalks a noblewoman in the city of Piltover. She toys with her prey, revealing her sadistic nature and her growing boredom with the hunt. It also explores her internal conflict: she has lived for so long that she is beginning to question the purpose of her existence.

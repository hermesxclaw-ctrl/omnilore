Who It Is
Form

The Loch Ness Monster is often described as a large, serpentine creature with a long neck and tail. It is said to be around 30 feet in length, with a diameter of around 10 feet. Some reports describe it as having a hump or a fin on its back, while others describe it as having a series of humps or ridges along its body.

Powers

Swimming The Loch Ness Monster is said to be a powerful swimmer, able to move quickly and efficiently through the water.

Accounts

The legend of the Loch Ness Monster dates back to the 6th century, when Saint Columba is said to have saved a man from being attacked by a 'water beast' in the River Ness.

There have been numerous reported sightings of the Loch Ness Monster over the years, with many people claiming to have seen the creature in the loch.

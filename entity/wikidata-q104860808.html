The Approach

In the misty peaks of the Albanian highlands, a figure in white haunts the mountain paths—a fairy-like guardian whose justice is as swift as it is severe. She is Perria, the protector of bread, who punishes the wasteful with a curse that bends the body as crooked as their disregard for the sacred loaf.

Content advisory. No explicit dark content beyond the folkloric punishment of disfigurement.

Cultural Ownership

Albanian folklore and mythology

Disputed Identifications

Some sources may conflate Perria with other fairy-like figures in Albanian belief, such as the Zana or Ora. The exact relationship is unclear and requires further research.

Description

Appearance

Perria is depicted as a female mountain-spirit clad in white. She is described as fairy-like, suggesting a supernatural, ethereal quality. No other physical details are attested.

Symbols & Iconography

White clothing is a symbol of her otherworldly nature. She is associated with mountains and bread, the latter being a symbol of sustenance and sacredness in Albanian culture.

Behaviour & Personality

Perria is generally protective, but she can also harm. Her primary role is to punish those who waste bread, a serious transgression in Albanian folk ethics. The punishment is severe: disfiguring the person and making them a 'crooked hunchback'.

Domains & Habitats

Mountain regions of Albania.

Version Differences

No significant version differences are recorded in the available sources. However, regional variations may exist in oral tradition.

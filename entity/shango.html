Who It Is
Form

Shango is often depicted as a powerful king, wielding a double-headed battle-axe (Oṣé) and surrounded by flames, lightning, and thunder.

Powers

Thunder and Lightning Shango has the power to control thunder and lightning, using them to bring justice and strength to those who seek it.

Accounts

Shango was a powerful king who ruled over the Oyo Empire, but his reign was marked by tragedy and conflict. He was eventually deified and became the Orisha of thunder, justice, and transformation.

Shango and Ogun, the Orisha of iron and labor, were bitter enemies who clashed over the control of the Oyo Empire. Shango's fiery nature and Ogun's ironworking skills made them formidable opponents.

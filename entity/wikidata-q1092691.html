Who It Is

In Mahora Academy's Class 3-A, where a vampire loli, a time-traveling Martian and a ghost girl all sit in the same row, the girl in the white lab coat with twin braids and smudged glasses still manages to be the most dangerous — because Satomi Hakase doesn't cast spells, she builds them. The self-proclaimed mad scientist of the class can assemble a combat robot, a time-machine jammer, or an interdimensional portal before lunch, and she does it all with a cheerful 'Let's experiment!' that makes even Negi Springfield nervous.

Cheerful, hyperactive, childishly enthusiastic and single-minded about experiments. Described in fanbook as 'genius but lacking common sense' (tensai dakedo jōshiki ga nai). Speaks in rapid, technical patter, often ignoring danger or ethics in pursuit of 'interesting' results. Deeply loyal to Chao Lingshen, whom she idolizes as a fellow scientist; their friendship is portrayed as genuine rather than hierarchical. Name & Tongue

Etymology — Family name Hakase (葉加瀬) is a rare Japanese surname; given name Satomi (聡美) combines kanji 聡 (sō, wise/clever) + 美 (mi, beauty). The surname is homophonous with hakase (博士, doctor/professor/Ph.D.), a deliberate pun by Ken Akamatsu signaling her archetype as the class genius/mad scientist. In-univers

Pronunciation — Japanese: [ha.ka.se sa.to.mi] (ha-KAH-seh sah-TOH-mee); English dub: ha-KAH-say sah-TOH-mee. Family name stressed evenly; no silent syllables.

Powers
Superhuman Intellect and Engineering Genius

Hakase possesses genius-level intellect specializing in robotics, mechanical engineering, and magi-technology. She can design, build, and repair complex robots (including Chachamaru maintenance assistance), anti-magic barriers, and time-space devices far beyond contemporary Earth technology. Chao Lingshen, herself a genius from the future, treats Hakase as an equal collaborator, implying Hakase's natural talent rivals future knowledge.

Limit: Intellect is domain-specific (engineering); she lacks magical power, martial ability, or strategic foresight. She is easily distracted, ignores safety protocols, and requires Chao's theoretical guidance for the most advanced time-magic calculations. She cannot use magic herself without artifacts.

Robotics and Magi-Tech Fabrication

Practical ability to fabricate autonomous robots, powered suits, beam weapons, and magical-technology hybrids. Notable creations: festival mecha for Mahora Festival, maintenance drones, and components of Chao's Casseopeia time-travel system and the forced-revelation magic-canceling field. She also assists in repairing Chachamaru Karakuri after battles.

Limit: Requires materials, time, and lab access. Creations are vulnerable to powerful magic (e.g., Negi's lightning, Evangeline's ice) and to anti-magic fields she herself helps create. No mass-production capability shown.

Pactio: Probationary Contract with Negi Springfield (Artifact Undisclosed)

In the manga's Pactio sequence (Ch.212-214), Hakase forms a probationary contract (pactio) with Negi, granting her a pactio card. Unlike most classmates, her artifact (pactio tool) is never depicted in action in the main manga panels. Official guides list her artifact as unknown or not yet manifested, suggesting a support-type magi-tech device. Anime OADs briefly show a card with gear motif but no activated artifact scene.

Limit: As a non-combatant, she rarely if ever summons artifact; even after contract, she continues to rely on inventions rather than magic. Contract requires Negi's kiss/ritual and mutual trust; artifact power scales with affection/contract strength, which for Hakase is platonic/admiration-based, likely weak compared to combat-focused students.

Team Support and Technical Coordination

Non-superhuman ability to coordinate large-scale technical operations: managing the Super Science Club, directing festival security robots, and providing real-time tech support to Negi's party via communications. In UQ Holder! future, this evolves into leadership of a research institute bridging magic and science.

Limit: Physically weak (Fanbook Physical Strength 1/5), no combat ability, dependent on protection by others in dangerous situations.

Accounts I
Class Introduction – The Mad Scientist of 3-ANegima! Magister Negi Magi Vol.1-2, Ch.4-12

2003 (in-universe: 2003 Mahora Academy school year, Class 2-A)

Satomi Hakase is introduced as part of Negi Springfield's class roster of 31 girls. Early chapters establish her as the bespectacled, braid-wearing member of the Mad Science Club, always in lab coat, tinkering with robots in the classroom. She appears in the 'Library Island' and 'Evangeline' setup arcs as background tech support, establishing her friendship with Chao Lingshen and her role as the class's go-to engineer. Original prose: Hakase is not given a solo spotlight but is consistently shown building or repairing small robots while other girls pursue Negi. Establishes Hakase's archetype and her inseparable partnership with Chao, which becomes crucial for the later Festival arc. No major plot consequence yet.

Kyoto School Trip and Mahora Festival PreparationsNegima! Vol.6-12, Ch.48-90

2004 serialization (in-universe: summer-fall 2003)

During the lead-up to the Mahora Festival, Hakase works with Chao's group to prepare elaborate attractions, including giant robots and haunted house tech. She is seen collaborating on the 'Mars vs Mahora' exhibition and festival infrastructure. While the main Kyoto arc focuses on Evangeline and the Kansai Magic Association, Hakase remains at Mahora maintaining the school's barrier and tech, demonstrating her behind-the-scenes importance. Foreshadows the scale of Chao's plan; Hakase's technical competence makes the later revelation that the festival tech hides a world-altering device plausible.

Mahora Festival Arc – Chao Lingshen's Plan to Reveal MagicNegima! Vol.15-18, Ch.135-168 (core Hakase involvement Ch.148, 155, 162-168)

2006-2007 serialization (in-universe: Mahora Festival, 3 days)

Chao Lingshen reveals she is a time traveler from Mars in the future and plans to forcibly reveal magic to the world using a massive magic-canceling and broadcasting system hidden beneath Mahora Festival. Satomi Hakase is revealed as Chao's chief engineer and confidante — she helped design and build the system, believing it was for a 'festival attraction' and later learning its true purpose. When Negi and his allies move to stop Chao, Hakase is torn but ultimately does not fight Negi; after Chao's defeat in a martial arts tournament and Negi's intervention, Hakase helps dismantle the device and is shown comforting a departing Chao. Her dialogue confirms she knew Chao would have to return to the future and that she chose to help despite ethical doubts because she believed in Chao's ideal of ending magic discrimination. Chao's plan fails; magic remains secret; Chao returns to future; Hakase remains in present, her loyalty and technical skill cemented. This is Hakase's largest narrative role and defines her character beyond comic relief. Later chapters reference her guilt and growth.

Accounts II
Pactio Formation – Contract with NegiNegima! Vol.21, Ch.212-214 (Pactio Card sequence)

2008 serialization (in-universe: post-Festival, preparation for Magic World)

As Negi prepares to travel to the Magic World (Mundus Magicus) to find his father, he forms probationary contracts (pactio) with most of Class 3-A to gain allies and artifacts. Hakase, alongside other non-combat students, participates in the ritual. Her pactio card is shown — featuring her in lab coat with gear background — but unlike combat students (Asuna, Setsuna, Kaede), her artifact summoning is not depicted. The scene is played as light comedy: Hakase is excited to analyze the magic scientifically, asking Negi if she can 'dissect the contract.' Grants Hakase formal status as Negi's partner (Magister/Magistra), though she does not accompany the Magic World expedition. Her card remains in Negi's collection; later in UQ Holder! lore, pactio cards are treated as historical artifacts.

Magic World Absence and Earth SupportNegima! Vol.22-33, Ch.215-320 (Magic World / Mundus Magicus arc)

2008-2011 serialization (in-universe: Mundus Magicus journey)

While Negi and 15+ students travel to the Magic World, Hakase remains on Earth with the remaining classmates (including Chao's absence). She is shown in occasional Earth-side interludes maintaining Mahora's defenses and communicating via Chachamaru's network. She does not fight in the Magic World battles against Fate Averruncus and the Mage of the Beginning, but her Earth-side tech support is referenced when Negi's group uses magi-tech communicators she helped maintain. This absence underscores her non-combatant status. Highlights division between combat and support members of 3-A; Hakase's choice to stay is pragmatic, not cowardly, and preserves her lab role.

Graduation, Future, and UQ Holder! – The Adult ScientistNegima! Vol.38, Ch.355-356 (Epilogue); UQ Holder! Vol.14-17, Ch.126-145

2012 (Negima finale, in-universe: 2004 graduation, 7-year time skip); 2016-2017 (UQ Holder! future)

In Negima's epilogue, Hakase graduates with Class 3-A and is shown in the 7-year-later future montage as a researcher. UQ Holder! (set 2086, 80 years after Negima) reveals adult Satomi Hakase as a leading scientist in the integrated magic-technology world that Negi helped create. She appears as a senior researcher at Amano-Mihashi, working on immortality and space elevator technology alongside Chao's legacy. She retains her braids and glasses, now as a respected professor, confirming that her childhood genius matured into world-changing science. She interacts briefly with Tota Konoe and Evangeline, providing exposition on Negi's fate. Provides closure: Hakase's dream of bridging magic and science is realized in the future timeline; connects Negima and UQ Holder! continuity. Confirms her long-term importance beyond comic relief.

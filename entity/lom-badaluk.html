Who It Is

In the moonlit villages of the Italian countryside, where the olive groves whisper and the hearth fires crackle, there lurks a name that mothers utter only in hushed tones: L'Om Badaluk. A being of shadow and hunger, this man-eating giant has haunted the collective imagination of Italian folklore for centuries, a testament to the primal fears that dwell just beyond the lamplight.

As a man-eating giant, L'Om Badaluk is likely a predatory figure, stalking and devouring humans, possibly children. This behavior is typical of ogres in European folklore, but specific traits are unverified.

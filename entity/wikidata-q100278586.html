Who It Is

A name whispered in many worlds: from the official cat of 10 Downing Street to a robot rat-catcher in the sewers of New York, from a video game boss to a homemade grenade in the neon streets of Piltover. 'Mouser' is a shapeshifter of meaning, a small word that has chased rodents, villains, and pixels across centuries and dimensions.

Working cats and dogs are known for their hunting instinct; the Chief Mouser is a ceremonial and practical role; TMNT Mousers are aggressive and destructive; the Mario Mouser throws apples and is a villain; the Arcane Mouser is an explosive device.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Derived from 'mouse' + agent suffix '-er', meaning 'one who mouses' or 'one who catches mice'. The word has been used as a common noun for cats and dogs, and later adopted as a name for various entities.

Accounts I
The Chief Mouser to the Cabinet OfficeUK Government records, news reports

16th century to present

The official resident cat at 10 Downing Street, tasked with catching mice. The most famous is Larry, who has served since 2011. The role is ceremonial but also practical. Larry has become a beloved public figure, often featured in the media.

Mouser robots in Teenage Mutant Ninja TurtlesTMNT comics, 1987 cartoon, 2012 series, movies

1984-present

Created by Baxter Stockman, these robots were designed to help with construction but were reprogrammed to attack the Turtles. They appear in various incarnations with different roles. The Mousers are a recurring threat and often lead to the Turtles discovering Stockman's schemes.

Accounts II
Mouser in Super Mario Bros. 2Super Mario Bros. 2 (video game)

1988

Mouser is a boss in the game, a mouse-like creature with a turban that throws apples. He is a minion of Wart, the main villain. He also appeared in The Super Mario Bros. Super Show! as a recurring villain. Mouser is defeated and the heroes continue to free the Dream World.

Mouser in ArcaneArcane (TV series)

2021

Powder, a young girl, creates a homemade grenade she calls a 'Mouser'. It is a small, round device that explodes. This is part of her early experiments before becoming Jinx. The Mouser is used in a heist attempt and later in a tragic event that leads to the death of Powder's friends.

Who It Is
Form

Macha is often depicted as a powerful and fierce goddess, associated with the land, fertility, kingship, war, and horses. She is said to be one of three sisters known as 'the three Morrígna'. Her appearance is often described as majestic and awe-inspiring.

Powers

Sovereignty Macha has the power to grant sovereignty and kingship to those who please her. She is often invoked in rituals and ceremonies related to kingship and fertility.

Accounts

The Táin Bó Cuailnge is an ancient Irish epic that tells the story of Macha and her role in the battle between the armies of Ulster and the armies of Queen Medb of Connacht.

The Curse of Macha is a story that tells the tale of how Macha was wronged by the men of Ulster and how she exacted her revenge upon them.

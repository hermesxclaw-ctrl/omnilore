Who It Is

Macha, heavily pregnant, was forced to race the king's horses. She won, giving birth to twins at the finish line, but as she died she cursed Ulster's men to suffer labor pains in their time of greatest need. This curse left Cú Chulainn alone to defend Ulster in the Táin.

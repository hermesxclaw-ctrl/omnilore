Who It Is

He saw a Gundam descend like a god and decided the only honorable response was to chase it until one of them broke — even if it meant burning his own name, his face, and his humanity to keep up. Graham Aker is Mobile Suit Gundam 00's ace who turned rivalry into religion, trading the stars-and-stripes bravado of a Union Flag pilot for the lacquered mask of Mr. Bushido to prove a human could stand equal to a machine built to end war.

Publicly charismatic, cocky, and theatrical — the Union's ace who salutes enemies and treats dogfights as sport. Privately obsessive, honor-bound, and self-destructive. Graham is defined by a chivalric interpretation of combat: he seeks a worthy, equal duel with the Gundam (specifically Setsuna's Exia/00) and despises interference or asymmetrical tactics. He is fiercely loyal to friends, especially Billy Katagiri, and protective of subordinates (Howard Mason, Daryl Dodge), whose deaths deepen his survivor guilt and fixation. Powers
Master Flag Fighter / Ace Pilot

Top-tier mobile suit pilot specializing in the transformable SVMS-01 Flag series. Demonstrates exceptional spatial awareness, high-G maneuvering, and use of Flag's fighter-to-MS transformation mid-combat to outmaneuver heavier Gundams. Repeatedly forces Gundam Meisters to disengage or use Trans-Am. Official File ranks him as Union's top ace alongside Daryl Dodge.

Limit: Baseline human physiology limits sustained high-G combat; Flag's conventional turbine/linear rifle armament inferior to GN weaponry until GN Drive retrofit. Cannot generate GN particles naturally; dependent on machine performance.

GN Drive-Equipped Custom Units and Trans-Am

After aligning with Innovators, pilots GN Drive Tau and later original GN Drive-equipped units (GN Flag, Ahead Sakigake, Masurao/Susanowo, Brave). Gains Trans-Am capability, increasing speed and power threefold for limited duration, with afterimage and particle burst. Susanowo's Trans-Am is notably unstable and physically punishing.

Limit: Trans-Am duration ~3 minutes, followed by performance drop; Tau Drive requires recharging, original Drive risks particle depletion. Extreme G-force causes capillary rupture and blackout without surgical enhancement. Overuse accelerates bodily damage.

Surgical Enhancement / Augmented Durability

Undergoes undisclosed physical modification (implied cybernetic/cardiovascular reinforcement) between S1 and S2 to tolerate Trans-Am and Innovator-level maneuvers. Allows him to pilot Susanowo at loads that would kill normal pilots and to survive injuries that hospitalize others.

Limit: Not Innovator regeneration or quantum brainwaves; still human, still ages, still vulnerable to fatal wounds. Enhancement is palliative, not superhuman strength. Psychological cost: increased fatalism and dependence on medication/rest.

Bushido Code and Dueling Doctrine

Self-imposed code demanding single combat, announcement of presence, and refusal to attack a disabled opponent. Functions as both moral constraint and tactical signature — enemies can predict he will seek 1v1, but his focus in duels is absolute. Inspires loyalty in subordinates and respect even from Setsuna.

Limit: Code is voluntary and exploitable; opponents (notably Ali Al-Saachez, Ribbons) ignore it. Causes strategic blunders when he abandons orders to pursue Setsuna.

Squadron Leadership and Tactical Command

Leads Flag Fighters (Mason, Dodge) in S1 and Solbrave Squadron (12 Braves) in film. Competent flight leader, capable of coordinating transformable MS team tactics, pincer maneuvers, and ELS search-and-rescue operations. In film, pioneers ELS communication attempt via quantum brainwave exposure through Setsuna's 00 Qan[T].

Limit: Leadership secondary to duelist instinct; in S2 as Mr. Bushido he abandons command to duel, criticized by A-Laws officers. In film, command is more mature but still front-line oriented.

Accounts I
Debut and Obsession - The Flag vs. GundamMobile Suit Gundam 00 Season 1 episodes 2, 7-9

2307 AD (in-universe); broadcast 2007

Union ace Graham Aker first encounters Gundam Exia during Celestial Being's armed intervention in Krugis. Astonished that a single machine can dominate modern armies, he becomes obsessed with fighting a Gundam on equal terms. With mechanic Billy Katagiri and test team (Howard Mason, Daryl Dodge), he repeatedly customizes his SVMS-01 Flag to close the gap, challenging Exia in Taklamakan Desert and over Azadistan. He salutes Setsuna after each engagement, declaring him a worthy rival, while Setsuna initially dismisses him as irrelevant. Establishes central rivalry that drives Graham's arc; pushes Union to develop GN Drive Tau technology; cements Graham's reputation as the only pilot who can track a Gundam without GN particles.

Operation Fallen Angels and the GN FlagMobile Suit Gundam 00 Season 1 episodes 22-25

2308 AD

After Celestial Being is exposed, the three world blocs unite for Operation Fallen Angels to destroy the Gundams. Graham volunteers to pilot the newly completed GN Flag — a Flag retrofitted with a captured GN Drive Tau from Gundam Throne Eins — despite the lethal G-forces. He intercepts Setsuna and Allelujah, forcing Exia into a brutal duel. He gravely wounds Exia but is himself critically injured when Alejandro Corner's Alvatore/Alvaaron intervenes. Both machines are destroyed; Graham survives with severe injuries. Graham's near-death and the loss of his team (Mason killed, Dodge later killed) shatter his identity; he disappears for four years. Setsuna's survival fuels Graham's belief that fate demands a rematch. The GN Flag data becomes basis for GNX series.

Rebirth as Mr. BushidoMobile Suit Gundam 00 Second Season episodes 2, 8, 12, 16

2312 AD (four years later)

Presumed dead, Graham reappears under the name Mr. Bushido, masked and serving the autonomous peacekeeping force A-Laws at the behest of Ribbons Almark, who supplies him with advanced GN machines. Piloting the Ahead Sakigake and later the crimson Masurao (built specifically for him), he hunts the returned Celestial Being. He duels Setsuna's 00 Gundam multiple times, refusing to kill a disadvantaged opponent and adhering to bushido ritual. Billy Katagiri, now A-Laws engineer, is disturbed by his friend's self-erasure and surgical modifications to endure Trans-Am. Mask allows Graham to fight without Union constraints but isolates him; reveals extent of his obsession — willing to become a tool of Innovators for a fair fight. Setsuna begins to recognize him as more than an enemy.

Accounts II
Susanowo vs 00 Raiser - The Final DuelMobile Suit Gundam 00 Second Season episodes 23-25

2312 AD - Battle at Lagrange 2

For the final battle against Ribbons' forces, Graham receives the ultimate dueling machine, GNX-Y901TW Susanowo, equipped with Trans-Am and twin GN beam sabers styled as katana. He intercepts Setsuna's 00 Raiser en route to the mothership Celestial Being. In an extended Trans-Am duel, both push beyond human limits. Graham, having rejected Ribbons' offer to become a true Innovator, fights as a human to prove humanity's worth. Susanowo is destroyed, Graham is critically wounded but survives; Setsuna spares him and urges him to live for the future. Graham is later recovered by Billy and the Federation. Climax of rivalry; Graham's survival and Setsuna's mercy mark his first step from obsession toward purpose. He witnesses Setsuna's evolution into an Innovator, foreshadowing his own role in the coming ELS crisis. Susanowo's wreckage data informs Federation Brave development.

Solbrave Commander and the ELS WarMobile Suit Gundam 00 the Movie: A Wakening of the Trailblazer

2314 AD

Two years after Ribbons' defeat, Graham, now unmasked and promoted, commands the experimental Solbrave Squadron — 12 transformable Brave units built from Susanowo lineage, tasked with testing Innovator-human cooperation. When the Extraterrestrial Living-metal Shapeshifters (ELS) assimilate humans and machines, Graham leads the Braves in desperate defense of Earth and the deep-space fleet. During the final ELS assault, he volunteers to accompany Setsuna's 00 Qan[T] into the ELS core, using his Braves' Trans-Am to clear a path. His Brave is assimilated, but his consciousness briefly joins the quantum dialogue that allows Setsuna and the ELS to understand each other. He survives, last seen on Earth recovering and watching the new future he helped secure. Completes redemption arc: from rival who sought to kill Gundam to comrade who helps Gundam speak to alien life. Establishes Graham as bridge between oldtype humanity and Innovators. Solbrave data leads to post-ELS peacekeeping doctrine.

Afterimages - Legacy and MemoryMobile Suit Gundam 00 Epilogue (S2 ep.25 epilogue), Gundam 00 Revealed Chronicle, Official File epilogue interviews

2314-2364 AD (in-universe epilogue)

In the series epilogue narrated by Marina Ismail, Graham is implied to have lived into the new era of dialogue, no longer chasing death. The 50-year flash-forward that shows an unaged Setsuna (now Human-ELS hybrid) visiting an elderly Marina does not feature Graham, but supplementary materials (Gundam 00 Festival 10th Anniversary pamphlet, 2018) note Solbrave veterans became instructors for the next generation of pilots. Graham's rivalry is memorialized in Federation academy curricula as example of 'human will exceeding specs.' Cements Graham as humanist counterpoint to Innovator determinism; his choice to remain human is cited in-universe as proof that evolution is not mandatory for understanding.

Mr. Bushido is a separate character from Graham Aker. — Mr. Bushido is Graham Aker's alias after his presumed death. Identity is concealed from most characters until mid-S2, but narrative and official materials confirm they are same person. Mask is persona, not clone or twin.

Graham is an Innovator or Newtype-like psychic. — He is a baseline human. He has no innate quantum brainwaves; his ability to sense Setsuna is skill and obsession, not psychic power. He only participates in quantum dialogue in film via 00 Qan[T]'s Trans-Am Burst field, not his own ability.

Graham's Flag could naturally keep up with Gundams without GN technology. — Base Flag is inferior; Graham only matches Gundams after GN Drive Tau retrofit (GN Flag) and later true GN Drives. Early duels show him outmatched until upgrades; skill alone cannot overcome GN particle physics.

Graham's bushido is historically accurate samurai code. — His bushido is a romanticized, personal duelist ethic inspired by popular culture, not historical Tokugawa bushido. It emphasizes single combat and honorable death, ignoring historical bushido's class, loyalty, and political dimensions.

Graham dies at the end of the movie sacrificing himself to ELS. — His Brave is assimilated, but he survives. Final scenes show him alive on Earth, and supplementary materials list him as Solbrave instructor post-war. Sacrifice is partial, not fatal.

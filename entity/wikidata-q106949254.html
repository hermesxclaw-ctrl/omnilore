Misato

Pronunciation MEE-sah-toh (IPA: /miꜜsato/)

Etymology

The name Misato (美里) is composed of the kanji 美 (mi, 'beauty') and 里 (sato, 'village' or 'hometown'), thus meaning 'beautiful village'. Alternative kanji combinations exist for given names, such as 美聡 (beautiful + wise) or 実里 (fruit + village).

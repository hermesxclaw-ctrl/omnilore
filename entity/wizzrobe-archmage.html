Who It Is

A tall, cloaked figure in vibrant robes whose face is a void of swirling stars. Wizzrobe Archmages are masters of elemental and teleportation magic, teleporting around the battlefield to avoid attacks while raining fire, ice, and lightning. They laugh in a high, echoing voice that seems to come from everywhere at once.

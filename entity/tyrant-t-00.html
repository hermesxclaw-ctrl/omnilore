Who It Is

The Tyrant T-00, also known as Mr. X, is the most infamous Tyrant series B.O.W. developed by Umbrella Corporation. A massive humanoid creature standing over 7 feet tall, encased in a trench coat and fedora to appear inconspicuous. Beneath the coat lies a grotesque mutation with exposed muscle tissue, a partially formed heart visible on its chest, and a single clawed hand capable of piercing flesh. The T-00 is relentlessly programmed to eliminate all witnesses and recovers from most damage, continuing its pursuit at a slow but unshakeable walk.

Indestructible (requires extreme force to stop)

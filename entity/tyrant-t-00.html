Who It Is
Form

A humanoid figure with a muscular build, standing approximately 2 meters tall, with a distinctive face and a menacing presence. Its body is covered in a thick layer of skin, with visible scars and wounds. The Tyrant's appearance is often described as imposing and terrifying.

Powers

Superhuman Strength The Tyrant possesses incredible physical strength, allowing it to overpower and kill even the strongest opponents with ease.

Accounts

The Tyrant is sent to Raccoon City to eliminate any remaining threats to the Umbrella Corporation's operations. However, the creature's plans are foiled by the arrival of S.T.A.R.S. members, who must fight to survive against the Tyrant's relentless attacks.

The Tyrant is sent to the Umbrella Corporation's headquarters to eliminate any remaining threats to the company's operations. However, the creature's plans are foiled by the arrival of Jill Valentine, who must fight to survive against the Tyrant's relentless attacks.

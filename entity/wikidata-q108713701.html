Who It Is

He answers every order with a lazy 'dull' — darui — and then moves faster than lightning itself. Darui is Kumogakure's reluctant prodigy, the man who inherited the Third Raikage's forbidden Black Lightning and who went from carrying the Fourth Raikage's bags to carrying the hat of the Fifth.

Attested personality: laconic, apologetic, habitually says 'darui' (what a drag / sorry, this is a pain) and apologizes excessively ('sorry, boss'). Despite verbal laziness, he is deeply loyal, diligent, and anxious about responsibility. Contrasts with his hot-headed superiors A and Killer B. He often acts as the straight man and voice of reason, chastising B's rapping and A's impulsiveness, while still obeying orders instantly. Powers
Storm Release (Ranton - Arashi)

Kekkei genkai combining Lightning and Water natures to create beams of light that flow like water and can be guided. Darui's signature technique is Storm Release: Laser Circus (Ranton: Reiza Sakasu) - fires multiple bright laser beams that can be controlled mid-flight to strike multiple targets. Attested to be able to change direction and overwhelm defenses.

Limit: Requires both chakra natures and precise control; beams can be blocked by sufficiently strong defenses (e.g., Sasuke's Susanoo ribs). Chakra-intensive; cannot be spammed indefinitely. Not to be confused with Darui's other lightning techniques.

Black Lightning (Kuroi Kaminari)

Rare, black-colored lightning technique taught only to Darui by the Third Raikage. Manifests as black panther-shaped lightning (Kuroi Kaminari: Kuropansa - Black Lightning: Black Panther). Highly destructive, can be extended and controlled. Marks Darui as the sole successor to the Third's unique technique.

Limit: Extremely chakra-taxing; Darui notes he cannot use it repeatedly. Requires hand tattoo/seal inherited from Third Raikage. Not usable without prior transmission. Panther form can be countered by wind or absorption techniques.

Lightning Release and Kenjutsu (Swordsmanship)

High-level Lightning Release user: Lightning Oppression Horizontal (Raiton: Kuropansa is Black Lightning variant), general Raiton flow through sword. Wields a large broadsword with lightning chakra flow, capable of cutting and electrocuting. Also proficient in Water Release as component of Storm Release. Skilled taijutsu and shurikenjutsu per databook.

Limit: Sword is standard ninja tool, can be broken or lost (he discards it to use Rikudo tools). Lightning techniques vulnerable to Wind Release. No kekkei genkai beyond Storm Release.

Rikudo Tools Proficiency (Temporary) and Tactical Intellect

During Fourth War, Darui temporarily wields the Sage of Six Paths' Five Treasured Tools: Benihisago (Crimson Gourd), Shichiseiken (Seven Star Sword), and Kokinjo (Golden Canopy Rope) after seizing them from Ginkaku/Kinkaku. He deduces their word-soul curse mechanism (speaking a taboo word seals your soul) and uses it against the brothers. More generally, Darui is noted for calm analysis under pressure.

Limit: Tools drain enormous chakra and risk self-sealing if user speaks their most-used word; Darui only survives by changing his most-used word from 'darui' to 'sorry' via apology trick, a one-time clever workaround. He does not retain tools after war (they are later collected by Tenten/Cloud). Tactical intellect is human, not supernatural.

Leadership and Chakra Reserves (Kage-level)

By Boruto era, Darui is recognized as Kage-level, with chakra reserves sufficient for prolonged Kage Summit and combat, and authority over Kumogakure's military and diplomatic corps. Not a formal power but attested status with enhanced responsibilities and implied strength growth.

Limit: No explicit new jutsu shown as Raikage; his strength is extrapolated from position, not demonstrated in new major battles. Not comparable to Naruto/Sasuke god-tier abilities.

Accounts I
Aide to the Fourth Raikage and Hunt for SasukeNaruto manga Chapters 417-419, 454-458; Anime Episodes 152, 199-201

2008-2009 publication; in-universe shortly after Pain's Assault on Konoha

Darui is introduced as the right-hand man to the Fourth Raikage A, alongside C (Shi). When Killer B (A's adoptive brother and Eight-Tails jinchuriki) is apparently captured by Akatsuki's Sasuke Taka, A summons Darui and C. Darui silently accompanies A to the Five Kage Summit, carrying his sword and apologizing for A's destruction of furniture. His laconic demeanor contrasts with A's fury, establishing his role as the Raikage's restraint. Establishes Darui's loyalty and position in Cloud hierarchy; sets up his participation in Summit battle. Reveals Cloud's aggressive stance toward Akatsuki.

Five Kage Summit - Clash with SasukeNaruto manga Chapters 461-464; Anime Episodes 208-214

2009 publication; in-universe Five Kage Summit at Land of Iron

At the Summit, Darui guards A alongside C. When Sasuke infiltrates the Summit, Darui engages Suigetsu and Jugo. He demonstrates Storm Release: Laser Circus for the first time, firing guided light beams that force Sasuke's team on defensive and show his kekkei genkai. He also clashes with Suigetsu's water body, using lightning to counter water. After Sasuke escapes and Tobi declares Fourth Shinobi World War, Darui returns with A to prepare Cloud forces. First full display of Darui's combat abilities; confirms Storm Release as rare and powerful; cements his reputation as A’s trusted guard. Survives Summit without major injury.

Fourth Shinobi World War: Commander of the First Division vs. Ginkaku and KinkakuNaruto manga Chapters 525-532; Anime Episodes 261-270

2011-2012 publication; in-universe Fourth Shinobi World War, Day 1

Darui is appointed commander of the Allied Shinobi Forces' First Division (mid-range fighters). On the battlefield he confronts the reincarnated Gold and Silver Brothers, Ginkaku and Kinkaku, who wield the Sage of Six Paths' Treasured Tools that curse victims based on their most-spoken word. Darui is initially sealed inside the Benihisago after saying his taboo word 'darui' (dull), but escapes by realizing the tools record the most-used word and by apologizing, changing his word to 'sorry' (through constant apologizing). He then outwits the brothers, seals Ginkaku, and with help from Ino-Shika-Cho and others, seals Kinkaku after Kinkaku transforms into a pseudo-Nine-Tails form. He uses Black Lightning: Black Panther during the fight. Major victory for Allied Forces; Darui recovers three of the five Rikudo tools; demonstrates tactical brilliance and inheritance of Black Lightning. Establishes him as war hero and future Kage candidate. Samui and Atsui are temporarily sealed but rescued.

Accounts II
War Climax: Facing the Ten-Tails and Allied CommandNaruto manga Chapters 553, 563, 588-591, 613; Anime Episodes 301-310, 326-329

2012-2013 publication; in-universe War Days 2-3

After the Ginkaku/Kinkaku battle, Darui leads the First Division to converge with other divisions against Madara, Obito, and the Ten-Tails. He fights alongside the Allied Forces, using Storm Release to support long-range barrages and Black Lightning against White Zetsu clones. He is present when Naruto's clone arrives, when Neji dies, and when the Allied Forces are trapped in Infinite Tsukuyomi (he is caught in the genjutsu like most). He is later released by Naruto and Sasuke after the war's end. Survives the war; experiences collective trauma of Infinite Tsukuyomi; participates in final Allied victory. No personal death or loss beyond comrades.

Fifth Raikage - Boruto Era LeadershipNaruto manga Chapter 700 epilogue (2014); Boruto: Naruto Next Generations manga Chapter 1, anime Episodes 34, 43, 59, 203; Naruto: The Last supplementary materials

2014-present publication; in-universe ~15 years after war (Boruto era)

Following A's retirement, Darui succeeds as the Fifth Raikage (Godaime Raikage). He appears at Kage Summits in Boruto, wearing the Raikage hat and cloak, advocating for cautious cooperation with other villages and for monitoring scientific ninja tools. He mentors Cloud genin and oversees Kumogakure's modernization, including the Lightning Village's role in the Chunin Exams. He remains apologetic in speech but firm in policy, e.g., debating with Naruto (Seventh Hokage) about Kara and Otsutsuki threats. No major personal arc beyond statesmanship; he is a stable, respected Kage. Represents generational transition in Kumogakure; Cloud Village under Darui maintains alliance with Leaf. Shows long-term character growth from aide to leader.

Light Novels and Supplementary Missions (Interwar Period)Naruto Jinraiden: The Day the Wolf Howled (2012), Naruto Shikamaru Hiden, and Boruto light novels; Anime filler Episodes 286, 321

2012-2018

In supplementary canon, Darui is referenced as part of Cloud's enforcement and as a sparring partner for Killer B's students Omoi and Karui. One anime filler mission shows Darui leading a team to retrieve the Benihisago after the war, encountering rogue ninja seeking Rikudo tools. Light novels mention his role in disarmament and in guarding the tools before they are sealed. Fills gap between war and Boruto promotion; non-core canon but consistent with manga. Shows Darui as teacher figure.

Darui is related by blood to the Raikage family (son or brother of A) — No blood relation attested. He is a subordinate and student of the Third Raikage, and aide to the Fourth, but not family. The Raikage title is not hereditary by blood but by selection; Darui was chosen for merit.

Darui's Storm Release is the same as Wood Release or other kekkei genkai, or that he can use Dust Release — Storm Release is specifically Lightning + Water, producing guided lasers. It is distinct from Wood (Water+Earth) or Dust (three natures). Darui has not shown any other kekkei genkai.

Darui permanently keeps the Sage of Six Paths' tools (Benihisago, etc.) as his weapons — He temporarily wielded three tools during the war to defeat Ginkaku/Kinkaku, but they were later sealed and stored (in anime, Tenten finds them; in manga they are collected by Allied Forces). As Raikage he does not carry them.

Darui's name means 'dark' or is African in origin — Name is Japanese adjective 'darui' meaning sluggish/dull, a pun on his personality. While Darui is a dark-skinned character, his name is Japanese wordplay, not African etymology.

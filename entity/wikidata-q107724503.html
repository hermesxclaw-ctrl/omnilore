Who It Is

He is the size of a warship, spotted like a dairy cow, with horns and a drooping, whiskered moo — and he is terrified of everyone. Momoo, the colossal Sea Cow of Arlong Park, was supposed to be the East Blue's ultimate guard beast, but one punch from Luffy and one kick from Sanji turned the monster into a blubbering, traumatized ferry who would rather pull a takoyaki cart than fight.

Attested behavior is consistently cowardly, easily intimidated, and obedient out of fear rather than loyalty. Initially aggressive when ordered by Arlong, but flees or freezes when struck. Shows capacity for affection and docility when treated kindly by Hatchan, Camie, and Pappag. Not sapient in human language, but displays expressive emotion, comprehension of simple orders, and memory (recognizes Luffy and Sanji and panics). Described in Vivre Card as 'timid' (okubyō) and 'easily tamed if fed.' No evidence of malice; violence is coerced.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Japanese onomatopoeia mō mō (モーモー), the sound of a cow mooing. The name is a direct sound-play on the creature's bovine appearance and vocalization, rendered in katakana as Mōmū with a long vowel. Eiichiro Oda frequently names Sea Beasts and animals with onomatopoeic or punning names.

Pronunciation — Japanese: /moː.muː/ (moh-moo), two even morae with long vowels. English dub (Funimation): /ˈmoʊmuː/ (MOH-moo).

Powers
Colossal Physical Strength and Mass

Can capsize small ships by ramming, crush wooden structures with headbutts, and generate large waves. At Arlong Park, ordered to destroy Gosa Village by ramming coastline. However, strength is modest compared to true Sea Kings or New World monsters; defeated by single blows from East Blue Luffy and Sanji without Gear or Diable Jambe.

Limit: Strength insufficient against Grand Line-level fighters; easily overpowered by Arlong himself and later by Luffy. No Haki, no Devil Fruit. Relies purely on bulk.

Aquatic Mobility and Endurance

Powerful swimmer capable of sustained high-speed swimming while towing boats or carrying passengers. Can dive deep and surface quickly. In Hatchan's cover story, tows Hatchan's entire takoyaki ship and later transports Camie and Pappag across long distances.

Limit: Air-breathing mammal; cannot remain indefinitely underwater, vulnerable to being beached. Speed not comparable to Sea Kings or Fish-Men in water.

Intimidation and Roar

Uses sheer size and loud bellowing moo to frighten humans. East Blue villagers and even Usopp initially panic at its appearance. Effectiveness is psychological rather than supernatural.

Limit: Effect negated against fearless or strong-willed opponents (Luffy, Zoro, Sanji show no fear). Often backfires as Momoo itself becomes more frightened than its target.

Cowardice and Submission (Behavioral Trait as Weakness)

Not a power but defining trait: extreme timidity and obedience to stronger beings. Will obey whoever last intimidated it, switching allegiance from Arlong to Luffy to Hatchan. This makes it easily controlled.

Limit: Cannot be relied upon in combat; flees at first sign of pain. Arlong notes Momoo is 'useless if scared.'

Appearance — Momoo is a colossal, manatee/dugong-like sea mammal scaled to monstrous proportions, approximately 30-36 meters long according to databook estimates, large enough to dwarf a marine galleon and carry several humans on its head. Symbols — Visual shorthand for Arlong's brute-force domination of East Blue: a tamed monster that is nonetheless cowardly. Oda uses Momoo's cow pattern and docile-animal tropes to undercut the menace — the 'terrifying sea monster' behaves like an abused farm animal. In cover stories, Momoo becomes an icon of redemption and second chances, often depicted pulling Hatchan's food cart like a draft animal, symbolizing labor and atonemen

Domains — Natural habitat is the sea, specifically coastal waters of East Blue around Conomi Islands and later the waters near Fish-Man Island and the Grand Line. As a Sea Beast, it is an air-breathing marine mammal that can remain submerged for extended periods but must surface. Accounts I
Encounter at Sea: Luffy and Sanji vs. MomooOne Piece Chapter 69-70 (Vol. 8)

1999 (in-universe: East Blue, shortly before Arlong Park arc)

While sailing toward Arlong Park with Zoro and Usopp, Luffy and Sanji (in a small dinghy) encounter Momoo blocking their path. The Sea Cow, sent by Arlong to patrol, attacks. Luffy stretches his neck to deliver Gomu Gomu no Ono (Axe) and Sanji delivers a kick to its jaw. Momoo cries and becomes terrified of both. Nami later uses Momoo's fear, steering it by threatening it, to reach Arlong Park faster. Establishes Momoo's cowardly nature and introduces Arlong's use of Sea Beasts. Allows Nami's group to reach Arlong Park. Momoo retreats to Arlong Park to report failure.

Guardian of Arlong ParkOne Piece Chapters 73, 75, 77 (Vol. 8-9)

1999 (in-universe: Arlong Park occupation, 8 years after Arlong's arrival in East Blue)

Arlong, angered by Nami's betrayal and Luffy's arrival, orders Momoo to destroy Gosa Village and then to kill Usopp and Zoro at Arlong Park. Momoo charges Arlong Park's pool, but is intercepted. Zoro, still wounded from Mihawk, and Usopp try to hold it off. Luffy arrives and twists Momoo's neck like a corkscrew, while Sanji kicks it again. Momoo collapses, then flees into the sea, abandoning Arlong. Demonstrates Arlong's cruelty in using living creatures as disposable weapons. Momoo's desertion foreshadows the collapse of Arlong's empire. No further combat role for Momoo in East Blue.

Hatchan's Sea-Floor Stroll - Redemption and FriendshipOne Piece Cover Story, Chapters 182-228 (Vols. 20-22)

2000-2001 publication; in-universe: shortly after Arlong's defeat, before Sabaody Archipelago

Homeless after Arlong's arrest by Marines, Hatchan wanders the sea floor. He reunites with Momoo, who has been wandering alone. Instead of commanding it, Hatchan befriends and feeds it. Momoo willingly becomes Hatchan's companion and draft animal, pulling his takoyaki ship, helping him befriend Camie the mermaid and Pappag the starfish, and protecting them from predators. The serial ends with Hatchan's Takoyaki shop succeeding with Momoo's help. Retcons Momoo from mere monster to sympathetic character; establishes Sea Beasts can form genuine bonds when not abused. Sets up Hatchan, Camie, Pappag, and Momoo's later importance in Sabaody and Fish-Man Island arcs.

Accounts II
Journey to Fish-Man IslandOne Piece Chapter 626 cover page; Fish-Man Island arc context Chapters 608-653

2011-2012 publication; in-universe: 2-year timeskip, return to Fish-Man Island

In the cover page of Chapter 626, Momoo is shown alongside Hatchan, Camie, and Pappag arriving at Fish-Man Island, having carried them through the deep sea. This confirms Momoo successfully navigated the perilous route to Fish-Man Island, a feat requiring passage through deep currents that normally require coating. Shows Momoo's endurance and loyalty; reintegrates the East Blue Sea Beast into the Fish-Man Island ecosystem. No dialogue, but visual confirmation of survival and growth.

Caribou's Kehihihihi in the New World - Towing the VillainOne Piece Cover Story, Chapters 674-731; specifically Chapters 693, 697

2013 publication; in-universe: post-Fish-Man Island, during Punk Hazard/Dressrosa period

After Caribou is defeated and buried by the Straw Hats, Momoo is shown towing a small raft carrying Caribou's brother Coribou and later encountering Caribou himself in the deep sea. In Chapter 697, Momoo is depicted pulling Caribou's raft while looking terrified as usual, suggesting it was coerced or tricked into service again. Demonstrates Momoo's recurring narrative function as a coerced ferry for stronger villains, paralleling its Arlong days, but now in the New World. Reinforces its status as a recurring background creature rather than one-off monster.

Legacy Cameo: Arlong's Memory and East Blue RemembranceOne Piece Chapter 1160+ (Elbaf flashback context), Anime Episode 1089+; also SBS Vol. 8, 45

1999-2024

Oda in SBS (question corner) and later flashbacks references Momoo as Arlong's former pet, clarifying it was brought from the Grand Line as a juvenile Sea Beast. In Fish-Man flashbacks involving Fisher Tiger and Arlong's youth, Sea Beasts are shown as common companions of Fish-Men. Momoo's origin is implied to be Grand Line capture rather than East Blue native, explaining its unusual size for East Blue. Provides world-building: Sea Beasts are distinct from Sea Kings and can be tamed by Fish-Men. Explains why Momoo is unusually large for East Blue.

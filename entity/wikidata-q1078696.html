Who It Is

He lies about pirates attacking the village every day until the day real pirates actually come — and no one believes him. Usopp, the long-nosed storyteller of Syrup Village, begins One Piece as its most human coward: a boy who inherited his father's aim but not his courage, who draws brave futures in tall tales because reality left him fatherless and grieving. By the time he puts on a crooked golden mask and calls himself Sogeking, King of Snipers, he will burn a World Government flag from a tower no one else could hit, and later, from half an island away, awaken a power he should not yet have to save thousands of people turned into forgotten toys. He is the Straw Hat who never wanted to be strong — only to be the kind of man his son could brag about.

Attested: Usopp is the most emotionally labile Straw Hat — prone to weeping, screaming, hyperbolic panic, and equally hyperbolic boasting. Early series: compulsive liar, anxious, insecure, deeply cowardly but desperate to be seen as brave. His lies are often transparent and comedic ('I have 8,000 followers,' 'I once fought a giant goldfish'), yet they function as coping mechanism and aspiration; many later become true in ironic fulfillment (e.g., claiming to have fought giants, later befriending Dorry and Brogy). Powers
Master Slingshot Marksmanship (Sniping)

Prodigious accuracy with slingshot and later slingshot-staff Kabuto, able to hit targets at extreme range where target is barely visible, often without direct line of sight using wind and trajectory calculation. Inherited from father Yasopp (renowned sniper of Red Hair Pirates). Feats include sniping Spandam and Marines from Tower of Justice bridge, hitting Perona's ghosts, and the Sugar shot from the King's Plateau to the Royal Palace (distance cited as extremely far, requiring Observation Haki).

Limit: No superhuman strength; relies entirely on aim, preparation, and ammunition. Vulnerable in close quarters. Accuracy can be affected by panic, though often overcomes it under pressure. Requires line-of-sight or Haki assistance for extreme shots.

Usopp Arsenal (Uso-Hachi Senpō) - Special Ammunition Stars

Custom-made spherical projectiles (Kayaku Boshi, Kemuri Boshi, Kaen Boshi, etc.) with effects: explosive, smoke, flame, shrapnel, lead, Tabasco, rotten egg, etc. Later supplemented by Skypiea Dials (Breath Dial, Flame Dial, Impact Dial) integrated into Kabuto for versatile effects.

Limit: Finite ammo; requires preparation time; Dials have cooldown and single-use limits (Impact Dial recoil can injure user). Pre-timeskip Kabuto is fragile.

Kabuto and Kuro Kabuto

Large slingshot staff: original Kabuto (five-banded slingshot with Dial attachments), upgraded to Kuro Kabuto (Black Kabuto) after timeskip, incorporating Pop Green growth chamber and greater draw strength. Functions as both slingshot and bo staff.

Limit: Bulky; requires two hands; Pop Green misfires can backfire (plants grow uncontrollably).

Pop Green Botanical Warfare

Seeds from Boin Archipelago's carnivorous forest that grow instantly into large, aggressive plants on impact: Sargasso, Trampolia, Rafflesia, Skull Bomb Grass, Humandrake, etc. Used for trapping, offense, defense, and mobility (e.g., Green Star: Devil, Green Star: Skull Explosion Grass). Learned during two-year training with Heracles'n.

Limit: Seeds are limited supply, require proper storage, can be affected by fire or cutting before growth. Some plants have indiscriminate effects. Usopp must remember each species' properties under stress.

Observation Haki (Kenbunshoku Haki)

Awakened during Dressrosa (Ch. 758) when Usopp, from the old King's Plateau, sensed auras of Luffy, Law, and Sugar in the distant palace and sniped Sugar through a window. Described as seeing auras; allows detection of presence, intent, and location beyond normal sight. First and only Haki form Usopp has demonstrated.

Limit: Awakening was stress-induced and not fully controlled; subsequent arcs show inconsistent mastery (not yet able to use at will reliably until later training implied). No Armament or Conqueror's Haki. Range and precision still developing.

Invention, Artistry and Repair

Gifted inventor and artist: designed Straw Hats' Jolly Roger, sculpted, painted, created Nami's Clima-Tact (and later upgrades with Franky/Weatheria tech), repaired Going Merry repeatedly before Franky joined, crafted dial-enhanced weapons. Demonstrates engineering intuition despite no formal training.

Limit: Not a professional shipwright (inferior to Franky); repairs were stopgap. Inventions sometimes comically flawed.

Psychological Warfare and Deception

Exploits enemy phobias and assumptions: defeated Perona by exploiting entomophobia (fake cockroaches), defeated Sugar by weaponizing his own grotesque facial expression after starvation and trauma, bluffed 8,000-strong fleet, intimidated foes with lies that become self-fulfilling. Uses chalkboard screech, rubber bands, rotten eggs as distractions.

Limit: Relies on opponent's psychological vulnerability; fails against fearless or knowledgeable foes. Can backfire if bluff is called.

Accounts I
Syrup Village and the Usopp Pirates (Captain Kuro Arc)One Piece manga Ch. 23-41, anime Ep. 9-17, Netflix live-action Ep. 3

1998 (publication); in-universe: East Blue, pre-Grand Line

Usopp, 17, leads three children as 'Captain Usopp' and entertains sickly heiress Kaya with tall tales to cheer her loneliness after her parents' death. He befriends Luffy upon learning Luffy knows his father Yasopp. When Kaya's butler Klahadore is revealed as Captain Kuro of the Black Cat Pirates plotting to murder Kaya for her inheritance, Usopp warns the village but is dismissed as the boy who cried wolf. With Luffy, Zoro, and Nami, he defends Syrup Village in a dawn battle, defeats hypnotist Jango, and prevents Kaya's assassination. He conceals his role to preserve his reputation as liar, and Kaya gifts the crew the caravel Going Merry, on which Usopp departs as official sniper, leaving his Usopp Pirates behind. Usopp joins Straw Hat Pirates as fourth member; Going Merry becomes crew's first ship; establishes Usopp's liar-with-a-heart-of-gold archetype and his dream to become a brave warrior like his father. Syrup Village learns to trust him.

Arlong Park: Facing ChuOne Piece manga Ch. 69-95, anime Ep. 31-44

1999 (publication)

At Cocoyashi Village, Usopp is separated from Zoro while pursuing Nami. He witnesses Arlong Pirates' oppression, meets Nojiko, and is captured by Arlong after saving Genzo, then freed by Nami. During the Arlong Park assault, Usopp is assigned Fishman Chu (archer/fishman). Initially flees in terror, then shames himself into returning, using wit and Kaen Boshi (flame star) and trickery to defeat Chu despite being physically outmatched, while Zoro and Sanji fight other officers. Proves Usopp can overcome cowardice for friends; cements his role as sniper who wins through ingenuity, not strength; Nami rejoins crew.

Little Garden: Pride of the Giants and Mr. 3One Piece manga Ch. 115-129, anime Ep. 70-77

2000 (publication)

On the prehistoric island Little Garden, Usopp is awestruck by giants Dorry and Brogy, who have dueled for 100 years over pride. Inspired, he declares he wants to visit Elbaf, the warriors' homeland. When Baroque Works agent Mr. 3 (Galdino) interferes by tricking the giants and trapping Zoro, Nami, and Vivi in wax, Usopp and Luffy (with Karoo) exploit Mr. 3's wax weakness to fire, freeing them. Usopp's admiration for Elbaf becomes his long-term dream anchor. Establishes Elbaf as Usopp's promised land and thematic goal: to become a brave warrior of the sea worthy of Elbaf. Introduces his willingness to challenge stronger foes for ideals.

Water 7 and Enies Lobby: Going Merry, Duel with Luffy, and SogekingOne Piece manga Ch. 321-441, anime Ep. 227-312

2004-2006 (publication)

When shipwrights declare Going Merry irreparably damaged, Luffy decides to replace it. Usopp, whose identity is tied to Merry (which he repaired and which Kaya gifted), violently opposes the decision, feeling his usefulness and his friend (the ship) are being abandoned. He leaves the crew, loses Merry's money to Franky Family, and duels Luffy for ownership of Merry — losing but earning Luffy's respect for fighting for what he loves. Ashamed, he dons a mask and cape as 'Sogeking, King of Snipers' to anonymously aid the crew. At Enies Lobby, Sogeking snipes Spandam and Marines from the Tower of Justice, burns the World Government flag on Luffy's order (a declaration of war), and rescues Robin and Franky by shooting their bindings from impossible distance. He apologizes and rejoins after Merry's Viking funeral, where the ship itself speaks to save them. Usopp's most character-defining arc; explores abandonment, pride, and forgiveness. Thriller Bark: vs. PeronaOne Piece manga Ch. 443-474, anime Ep. 337-381

2007-2008 (publication)

On Gecko Moria's haunted island, Usopp faces Ghost Princess Perona, whose Horo Horo no Mi ghosts induce crippling negativity. Normally, her Negative Hollows make victims despair ('I wish I were never born'), but Usopp is already so negative that he is immune — his pessimism becomes a superpower. He defeats her not with strength but by exploiting her entomophobia, firing a fake cockroach star (10-ton hammer gag) and using psychological terror. He also briefly wields salt and ingenuity against zombies. Comedic showcase of Usopp's unique immunity due to personality; reinforces that his weaknesses can invert into strengths. Establishes his talent for psychological warfare.

Dressrosa: God Usopp and Observation Haki AwakeningOne Piece manga Ch. 700-801, anime Ep. 629-746

2013-2015 (publication)

In Dressrosa, Sugar of Donquixote Pirates has turned hundreds of citizens and pirates into toys and erased memories of them using Hobi Hobi no Mi. Usopp, with Robin, infiltrates the underground port, but is forced to confront Sugar and her enforcer Trebol. Initially terrified, he is knocked out, then returns after being inspired by the toys' desperation. From the old King's Plateau near Corrida Colosseum, he awakens Observation Haki, sensing Sugar in the distant palace, and fires a projectile carrying his own terrified, starved face (after being forced to eat extremely spicy Tatababasco) over the palace walls, traumatizing Sugar into unconsciousness. Her power breaks, freeing thousands. The freed toys and citizens, seeing light shine on Usopp underground, deify him as 'God Usopp,' inflating his bounty to 200,000,000 Berries. First canonical Haki awakening for Usopp; massive bounty increase; 'God' epithet becomes permanent alias; liberates Dressrosa's enslaved population; cements his legend as sniper who saves without being seen. Critical for Straw Hats' reputation.

Wano and Elbaf Foreshadowing: The Brave Warrior's PromiseOne Piece manga Ch. 590-591 (post-war separation), Ch. 807+ (Zou), Ch. 1130+ (Elbaf arc, ongoing 2024-2026)

2010-2026 (ongoing)

After Marineford, Usopp is sent to Boin Archipelago, a carnivorous island where he trains with Heracles'n, overcoming gluttony and fear to master Pop Greens and lose weight. He reunites at Sabaody, fights in Fish-Man Island and Dressrosa, and participates in Wano (contributing Pop Green support, though less central). Throughout, his stated dream remains to visit Elbaf and become a brave warrior of the sea. Oda has repeatedly foreshadowed Elbaf as Usopp's arc climax (Little Garden promise, Usopp's lies about being from Elbaf, giants' respect). As of 2024-2026, the crew finally reaches Elbaf, where Usopp's arc is expected to resolve his cowardice and father-legacy themes. Post-timeskip power-up; narrative promise that Elbaf will test whether Usopp has become the brave man he always pretended to be. Ongoing as of research cutoff.

Usopp's nose grows when he lies like Pinocchio. — His nose is statically long from birth (inherited from mother); it never extends with lies in canon. The Pinocchio reference is thematic (liar who wants to be brave) not literal. Only fan art and jokes depict growth.

Sogeking is a separate person or possessed hero. — Sogeking is Usopp in a mask and cape. In-universe, Luffy and Chopper comically believe they are different people, but all other Straw Hats know. Usopp created the identity to help without facing shame after leaving the crew.

Usopp is useless and never contributes in fights. — Usopp has solo victories over Chu, Mr. 3's partner, Perona, and Sugar — all via ingenuity — and critical team saves (burning World Government flag, freeing Robin, freeing Dressrosa toys). His role is sniper/support, not frontline brawler, by design.

Usopp is already a brave warrior of the sea or has surpassed Yasopp. — As of Dressrosa/Wano, Usopp still self-identifies as coward working toward bravery; his dream to visit Elbaf and become a brave warrior is explicitly *unfulfilled*. He has not yet reunited with or surpassed his father.

Usopp has a Devil Fruit or will get one. — Usopp has no Devil Fruit and Oda has stated he intends to keep Usopp as the crew's most 'normal human' who fights with wits and tools. Pop Greens and Haki are his power ceiling, not a fruit.

Who It Is

She smiles into the camera every evening at 7 p.m., the perfect, poised newscaster with flawless hair and a voice that never trembles — and then she hangs up the blazer, answers a call on a burner phone, and becomes Kir, the Black Organization's cool-headed assassin who once put a rifle scope on FBI agent Shuichi Akai. Rena Mizunashi is not a villain who infiltrated television. She is a CIA spy who infiltrated the most murderous syndicate in Gosho Aoyama's Japan, and every second on air is a second she might be exposed and executed.

Attested: Highly disciplined, stoic, and observant. As a professional newscaster, warm, articulate, and unflappable on camera. As Kir, cold, laconic, and obedient to Gin's orders to maintain cover. As Hidemi, deeply loyal to her CIA mission and family, willing to endure extreme moral injury. She shows compassion (protects Conan's identity, worries for Eisuke) but suppresses emotion under interrogation. She is not sadistic; her cruelty is performative for the Organization. Powers
Deep-cover espionage and acting

Maintained dual identity as top-rated TV newscaster and trusted Black Organization member for ~2 years without detection. Able to lie under polygraph-style interrogation by Gin and Vermouth, control micro-expressions, and improvise cover stories under gunpoint.

Limit: Cover depends on external support (CIA, FBI, Conan). Nearly exposed by Eisuke's investigation and by Gin's lie-detector test. Psychological toll is high; requires hospitalization after trauma.

Marksmanship (rifle and handgun)

Trained CIA operative; shown handling sniper rifle during Organization assassination plot targeting Yasuteru Domon (File 502) and ordered to assassinate Shuichi Akai (File 608). Proficient with handgun at close range.

Limit: Not at Akai or Chianti/Korn's super-sniper level; her shooting of Akai was staged with blanks/squibs and Akai's cooperation. She missed or was ordered to miss in other contexts.

Motorcycle operation and vehicular evasion

Rides a large black motorcycle as Kir; pursued by FBI at high speed on Chuo Expressway before accident.

Limit: Crashed after Conan's transmitter + FBI PIT maneuver; sustained severe injuries and coma. Not shown as stunt-level rider beyond that chase.

Broadcast journalism and public speaking

Professional newscaster at Nichiuri TV, trusted by public, able to read teleprompter, conduct interviews, and maintain on-air composure. Provides plausible cover and access to information.

Limit: Public visibility makes her vulnerable — Eisuke tracks her via TV appearances.

Intelligence tradecraft (dead drops, coded communication, counter-surveillance)

Uses CIA techniques: covert contact with handler (father Ethan, later Rei Furuya/Bourbon verification), hidden messaging, and FBI coordination via Conan's plan. Recognizes FBI surveillance and adapts.

Limit: Organization's counter-intelligence (Gin, Vermouth, Bourbon) constantly tests her; requires outside help (Conan, Akai) to pass loyalty tests.

Accounts I
Debut as Rena Mizunashi and Kir: The Black Organization's Newscaster AssassinGosho Aoyama, Meitantei Conan Files 499-504 (Vol.49) / Anime Episode 425 'Black Impact!'

2005-2006 (in-universe: contemporary Tokyo, 1990s-2000s)

Conan, Kogoro and the Junior Detective League follow a strange 'ding-dong dash' prank that leads to Rena Mizunashi, a popular Nichiuri TV newscaster. Conan notices she behaves oddly and tracks her to a meeting where she is revealed as Kir, a codenamed member of the Black Organization tasked with assassinating politician Yasuteru Domon. The Organization's plan involves an interview setup, a sniper, and Kir as spotter. Conan and the FBI intervene; during the chase, Rena is knocked off her motorcycle and falls into a coma after a collision orchestrated by the FBI and Conan's tracking. Establishes Rena as both public figure and Organization operative; sets up the central mystery of her loyalty; puts her in FBI custody in comatose state, enabling the long 'hospital' arc; introduces Eisuke Hondou's search for his sister.

Eisuke Hondou's Search and the Hospital ConspiracyGosho Aoyama, Files 550-552 (Vol.53) / Anime Episodes 462-465

2006

Eisuke Hondou, a clumsy high school transfer student, appears searching for his older sister who resembles Rena Mizunashi. He believes Rena is his sister Hidemi. Conan realizes Eisuke's blood type (O) cannot match Rena's (AB) if they share a mother, hinting at a secret. Meanwhile, Rena remains in a coma at Haido Central Hospital under FBI guard. Vermouth and the Organization search hospitals to retrieve Kir. Reveals Rena's family connection and that her public identity is tied to a real family; foreshadows that Rena is not who she seems; raises stakes for FBI hospital guard.

Clash of Red and Black: Awakening, Confession, and the Return to the OrganizationGosho Aoyama, Files 595-609 (Vols.57-59) / Anime Episodes 495-504

2007-2008

Rena awakens from coma. Conan, Akai and the FBI confront her; she reveals her true identity: Hidemi Hondou, CIA agent, daughter of CIA agent Ethan Hondou. Flashback shows Ethan sacrificed himself — forcing Hidemi to kill him — to protect her cover when she was about to be exposed by the Organization. Hidemi had infiltrated the Organization as Kir under her newscaster alias created by the CIA. To regain the Organization's trust, the FBI stages her 'escape' and she re-infiltrates. As proof of loyalty, Gin orders her to kill Shuichi Akai at Raiha Pass. With Akai's secret cooperation and Conan's plan (using a blank, blood pack, and body double trick), she appears to assassinate Akai, fooling the Organization. Core lore reveal: Kir is CIA, not true Organization; explains patricide trauma; establishes her as triple agent (CIA inside Organization, secretly allied with FBI via Conan); Akai's apparent death (later revealed as survival as Subaru Okiya) hinges on her cooperation; Eisuke leaves Japan to join CIA after learning truth.

Bourbon's Verification: Kir Reports Akai's DeathGosho Aoyama, Files 700-704 (Vol.67) / Anime Episodes 578-581

2010

Organization member Bourbon (later revealed as Rei Furuya / Tooru Amuro) doubts Akai's death. Gin summons Kir to confirm details of the Raiha Pass killing. Bourbon, disguised, tests Kir's story. Conan and Kir coordinate to keep the lie consistent. Kir reaffirms she shot Akai in the head and chest and that the body was burned in the truck explosion, satisfying Gin temporarily. Shows Kir's continued precarious position; Bourbon arc begins; demonstrates how Conan remotely supports her via phone cues; keeps Akai's survival secret.

The Rum Arc: Kir as CIA Pipeline and the Urgency to Expose RumGosho Aoyama, Files 891-898, 980-983, 1005-1008, 1060+ (Vols.85-103) / Anime Episodes 779-783, 866-867, 942-943, 1079+

2015-2023 (ongoing)

As the Organization's second-in-command Rum becomes active, Kir becomes a crucial intelligence pipeline. She secretly contacts CIA and FBI via Conan and Shuichi Akai (now as Subaru Okiya). In File 980-983, she is ordered to help with a Rum-ordered assassination; Conan intercepts. Later, she provides intel about Rum's possible identities and is tasked by Rum to investigate Shinichi Kudo. She withholds Conan's identity, risking exposure. Recent files show her reporting directly to her CIA handler and coordinating with Rei Furuya, who is actually Japanese PSB, not CIA, creating inter-agency tension. Positions Kir as the only active allied mole inside the Organization after Akai's extraction; her intel drives the Rum investigation; ongoing cliffhanger — her cover could break at any time.

The Raven Chaser and Film Continuity CameoDetective Conan Film 13: The Raven Chaser (Meitantei Conan: Shikkoku no Chaser), dir. Yasuichiro Yamamoto, TMS

2009-04-18 theatrical release

In this Organization-centric film, Kir appears briefly in Organization communications alongside Gin, Vodka, Chianti, Korn, and Vermouth during the hunt for the serial killer and the Organization's memory-card pursuit. She is shown still embedded in the Organization, reporting movements. The film is anime-original and not written by Aoyama panel-for-panel, but Aoyama approved character usage. Reinforces her status as active Organization member in film continuity; no new lore, but expands her visual presence for film audiences.

Rena Mizunashi is her real name and she is Japanese with no foreign ties. — Rena Mizunashi is an alias created by the CIA for Hidemi Hondou, who is half-Japanese? Actually Hidemi is Japanese-American CIA; her father Ethan was CIA. The newscaster identity is cover, not birth name.

Kir is a high-ranking executive like Gin or Vermouth. — Kir is a mid-level codenamed agent, not an executive. She takes orders from Gin and is tested by him; she has no authority over Chianti/Korn and is not in the inner circle that knows the boss's identity.

She is a villain who tried to kill Conan. — She has never targeted Conan; she protects his identity and works with him. Her assassination targets were Domon and (staged) Akai under Organization orders.

Eisuke Hondou is her boyfriend or unrelated stalker. — Eisuke is her younger biological brother. His clumsy search is familial, not romantic.

Rena died in the motorcycle accident and was replaced by a lookalike. — She survived the crash, was in a coma at Haido Central Hospital, recovered, and is the same person. No clone or twin exists in canon.

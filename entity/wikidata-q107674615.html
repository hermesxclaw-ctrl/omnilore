The Approach

He was the third shadow behind the Pirate King — not the silver right hand who became legend, but the copper left hand who vanished. For twenty-five years fans knew his silhouette and his pun, nothing more. Then, on the mist-shrouded cliffs of Elbaf, an old man in a Hawaiian shirt, drinking with giants and swinging twin axes, looked at Monkey D. Luffy and said: you still don't know how to use Haki properly.

Content advisory. Mild fantasy violence, piracy, and references to slavery under the Celestial Dragons (God Valley context). No graphic sexual violence depicted for this character.
Identity

Scopper Gaban

Pronunciation Japanese: Sukoppā Gyaban [sɯ̥kopːaː ɡjabaɴ]; English official romanization: Scopper Gaban / SKOP-er guh-BAHN

Known As

Mountain Eater (Yama-gurai)Left Hand of the Pirate KingGabanCopper Gaban (fan nickname, from metal pun)Mr. Ya (called by Colon on Elbaf)

Etymology

Metal pun completing the Roger Pirates senior trio: Gol D. Roger (Gold), Silvers Rayleigh (Silver), and Scopper Gaban (Copper). 'Scopper' is Oda's stylized rendering of 'Copper' (Japanese スコッパー Sukoppā, from English 'copper' via pronunciation). 'Gaban' likely references French actor Jean Gabin, continuing Oda's film-actor naming pattern for senior pirates (cf. Rayleigh after actor). The pun was confirmed by Oda in SBS and One Piece Magazine materials: gold-silver-copper as descending value but equal trust.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese manga franchise One Piece created by Eiichiro Oda, serialized in Weekly Shōnen Jump (Shueisha, 1997-present). Character owned and managed by Shueisha / Eiichiro Oda / Toei Animation. No real-world folklore ownership; fictional creation with global fandom stewardship.

Disputed Identifications

Romanization disputed: early fan translations used 'Scooper,' 'Scopper,' 'Copper,' and 'Shukoppā.' Official English VIZ and Vivre Card use 'Scopper Gaban.' Not to be confused with Gabban, Gaban, or unrelated tokusatsu character Space Sheriff Gavan. For years fans debated whether 'Mountain Eater' was a separate epithet or mistranslation of 'Yama-gurai'; Elbaf Arc Chapter 1139 confirms it is Gaban's epithet. Some Western wikis conflated his silhouette in Chapter 0/Chapter 19 with other Roger Pirates until Chapter 966 provided clear design.

Description

Appearance

Young Gaban (38-39 years ago, age ~ mid-20s to early 30s): Tall, broad-shouldered, long dark hair in two braids tied with beads, round dark sunglasses, open collared shirt with floral or striped pattern, sash, twin axes at belt. Distinctive goatee and cheerful, slightly wild expression. Silhouette first recognizable by axes and glasses. Older Gaban (present, age 78 by Elbaf timeline): Still tall and heavily muscled despite age, hair now white-grey, long and loose with a single braid, large round sunglasses with orange lenses, Hawaiian-style open shirt with leaf pattern, straw hat-like kasa? No, no hat. Wears shorts, bare-chested, with a massive axe on back. Limp in one leg after unspecified injury, uses cane/axe as support but moves with explosive speed. Attested in manga; anime adds color (blue shirt young, yellow-green older).

Symbols & Iconography

Twin single-bit axes (ono) — his signature, never a sword like Roger/Rayleigh. Axes crossed on back. Epithet 'Mountain Eater' (Yama-gurai) suggests axe technique capable of felling mountains/trees, later visualized on Elbaf where he cleaves a forest and Elbaf tower. Associated with copper motif (third metal), with Roger Pirates' Jolly Roger. On Elbaf, associated with giant culture: lives in giant-sized house, uses giant-scaled furniture, wears Elbaf-style clothing.

Behaviour & Personality

Attested as jovial, teasing, and fiercely loyal — the 'mood maker' complement to Rayleigh's cool composure. In Oden flashback, he jokes with Oden and Buggy/Shanks, laughs loudly, but is immediately deadly in battle. Rayleigh describes him as the man who 'keeps balance' when Roger is reckless. On Elbaf, older Gaban is eccentric, laid-back, flirtatious (married to giantess Ripley), fond of drink and teasing younger pirates, but stern as a teacher: he bluntly tells Luffy his Haki is 'still childish' and demands he learn to sense without sight. Shows deep affection for his son Colon (half-giant, half-human) and protective stance toward Elbaf. Values freedom, adventure, and family over glory. No attested cruelty; even enemies note his fairness.

Domains & Habitats

Domains: Senior officer (often called No. 3) of the Roger Pirates, navigator/combatant, later guardian of Elbaf's secrets. Habitats: Oro Jackson (Roger Pirates' ship, built by Tom), Laugh Tale, and for the last ~20+ years, Elbaf — specifically the hidden village level where he lives in seclusion with Ripley near the Adam Tree. Frequently moves between giant forest and human-sized undergrowth. Cosmologically tied to the final island and Road Poneglyph lore.

Version Differences

Early silhouette (Chapter 0, 1999-2009) vs. fully designed 2020 version: early design lacked braids and sunglasses detail; Oda refined in 2020 to match copper-pun trio aesthetic. Anime Episode 0 and Episode 483 used provisional colors later corrected in Episode 966. Elbaf older design (2024-2025) adds age, injury, and domestic context not foreshadowed earlier. No contradictory alternate universes; all versions are same continuity. Live-action Netflix Season 1 (2023) does not yet depict Gaban; future seasons may introduce him. Game models (Pirate Warriors 4) use young design only.

Powers & Abilities

Twin Axe Mastery - Mountain Eater Style

Source VersionManga Chapters 966, 1139-1140 (attested)

Wields two large single-bit axes with dual-wield fluency, capable of flying slashes (tobu斬撃) that cleave cliffs and forests. Epithet 'Mountain Eater' implies mountain-scale cutting feat; on Elbaf he demonstrates 'Yasogami: Mountain Eater' — a horizontal flying slash that fells an ancient Adam Tree branch. Comparable to Roger and Rayleigh in close combat during Whitebeard clash.

Limitations

Requires both hands; less effective in confined spaces. Axes are non-supernatural tools — can be broken or lost. No Devil Fruit to augment cutting power.

Contradictions

Fan speculation that he ate a Devil Fruit is unattested; manga shows no fruit ability.

Advanced Haki - All Three Types

Source VersionManga Chapters 966, 1140-1143; Rayleigh dialogue Chapter 1055

Confirmed user of Haoshoku (Conqueror's), Busoshoku (Armament), and Kenbunshoku (Observation) Haki. On Elbaf, he teaches Luffy to refine Kenbunshoku to sense 'voice of all things' and to apply Haoshoku coating (as Rayleigh taught Luffy). Rayleigh states Gaban was one of few Roger Pirates who could 'hear' the Poneglyphs' voice alongside Roger and Oden. Uses Busoshoku hardening on axes.

Limitations

At age 78 with leg injury, stamina limited — cannot fight prolonged high-intensity battles like Kaido-level. Haoshoku is not as overwhelming as Roger/Whitebeard; used more for coating than domination.

Contradictions

Whether his Haoshoku was retconned for Elbaf arc debated; earlier chapters did not show black lightning, but 2024 chapters explicitly show it, now considered canonical.

Navigation, Poneglyph Reading Support, and Survival

Source VersionManga Chapters 967-968, 1145

As senior Roger Pirate, participated in deciphering Road Poneglyphs (with Oden's translation), navigating the Grand Line to Laugh Tale. Expert seaman, survivalist on Elbaf's harsh environment, and keeper of the key to Loki's seastone chains — implying knowledge of seastone mechanisms and giant lore.

Limitations

Cannot read Poneglyphs himself; relied on Oden. Navigation skill is human, not supernatural.

Contradictions

None

Physical Strength and Longevity

Source VersionManga Chapters 966, 1139

Superhuman strength: clashes equally with Whitebeard commanders (Marco, Jozu era) without injury; at 78 lifts giant-sized axe and overpowers giant warriors. Longevity and durability typical of top-tier pirates; survives God Valley, Edd War, and decades on Elbaf.

Limitations

Age and leg injury reduce mobility; needs cane after extended fight. Healing slower than giants.

Contradictions

Bounty and exact age not officially listed in Vivre Card; fan estimates 70s-80s, confirmed ~78 in Elbaf databox.

Stories & Myths

God Valley Incident — Dawn of the Roger Pirates' Rivalry

Source One Piece manga Chapters 1095-1096 (Kuma/ God Valley flashback), Chapter 1159-1160 (Elbaf/ Rocks exposition)Culture Japanese manga / One Piece world - West Blue / God ValleyPeriod 38 years before present (c. 1484 Age of the Sea Circle), in-universe historical event; published 2023-2025

Summary

Thirty-eight years ago, the World Government's God Valley hosted a 'Native Hunting Competition' where Celestial Dragons hunted slaves. The Rocks Pirates, Roger Pirates, Marines (Garp), and Holy Knights converged. Young Scopper Gaban, already a core Roger officer alongside Silvers Rayleigh, fought alongside Roger and Rayleigh against Rocks D. Xebec's crew (including Whitebeard, Kaido, Big Mom, Shiki). Though his individual feats are not spotlighted like Roger vs. Rocks, Gaban is shown in the double-page battle spread holding his axes against Rocks officers, protecting Shakky (Shakuyaku) who had been kidnapped. The incident ends with Rocks' defeat, God Valley's erasure from maps, and Roger's rise to prominence.

Variants

Manga is primary; anime Episode 1128-1130 adapts with added filler dialogue. Early fan theories omitted Gaban from God Valley until Chapter 1159 confirmed his presence.

Edd War — Roger vs. Golden Lion Shiki

Source One Piece manga Chapter 0 and Chapter 966 flashback; One Piece Magazine Vol. 10Culture One Piece world - New World, Edd War seaPeriod 27 years before present (c. 1495), published 2009 (Chapter 0) and 2020

Summary

The Roger Pirates, seeking the final Road Poneglyph, are ambushed at sea by Shiki the Golden Lion's massive Armada (51 ships) during a storm at Edd War. Roger refuses to submit. Gaban fights alongside Rayleigh on the Oro Jackson's deck, cutting through Shiki's fleet with axe flying slashes as a sudden tempest — possibly fate or the 'Will of D.' — destroys half of Shiki's fleet, allowing Roger to escape. Gaban is shown laughing through the storm, embodying the crew's fearlessness.

The Whitebeard Encounter and Oden's Recruitment

Source One Piece manga Chapter 966 'Roger and Whitebeard'Culture One Piece world - New World islandPeriod 26 years before present (c. 1496), published 2020

Summary

The Roger Pirates clash with the Whitebeard Pirates for three days and three nights for treasure and pride. Gaban duels Whitebeard commanders (notably clashing with Kozuki Oden, who had been sailing with Whitebeard). After the battle ends in gift exchange, Roger begs Whitebeard to 'lend' Oden for one year to read the Poneglyphs. Gaban welcomes Oden aboard, jokes about his dancing, and becomes his shipmate and sparring partner. He is present when Oden deciphers the Road Poneglyph at Zou and Wano.

Voyage to Laugh Tale and the Pirate King's Disbandment

Source One Piece manga Chapters 967-968 'Roger's Adventure' / 'Oden's Return'Culture One Piece world - Grand Line, Final IslandPeriod 25 years before present (c. 1497), published 2020

Summary

With Oden, the Roger Pirates collect the four Road Poneglyphs (Zou, Whole Cake, Wano, Fish-Man Island), reach Lodestar, then use Oden's reading to find Laugh Tale. Gaban is among the few who see the One Piece and laugh with Roger — the 'Joy Boy' revelation. Upon return, Roger disbands the crew, telling them he will turn himself in. At a final feast, Gaban weeps and laughs, accepting Roger's decision to scatter. He parts ways with Rayleigh, Crocus, and the apprentices Shanks and Buggy, disappearing from history.

Exile and Family on Elbaf — The Mountain Eater in Hiding

Source One Piece manga Chapters 1139-1148 Elbaf ArcCulture One Piece world - Elbaf, Warland KingdomPeriod Present (c. 1522), published 2024-2025

Summary

After Roger's execution (24 years ago), Gaban evades the Great Pirate Era manhunt and settles on Elbaf, the giants' nation, where the World Government has little reach. He falls in love with the giantess Ripley (Elbaf biology teacher), fathers a half-giant son Colon (age ~10, human-sized but with horns), and lives in a giant-sized house in the forest stratum, known to giants as 'Ya-san' (Mr. Ya). Known as 'Mountain Eater' for leveling a mountain that threatened the village. When the Straw Hat Pirates arrive on Elbaf (2024 arc), Gaban first appears disguised, testing Luffy, Zoro, and Sanji by attacking them. He then reveals identity, offers to train Luffy in 'true' Haki, and holds the only key to the seastone chains binding Prince Loki (accused of killing King Harald). He debates whether Luffy should free Loki, warning of Loki's power and Elbaf's curse.

The Key to Loki's Chains — Test of the Next Generation

Source One Piece manga Chapters 1143-1148Culture One Piece world - Elbaf, Adam Tree prisonPeriod Present (c. 1522), published 2025

Summary

Elbaf's cursed Prince Loki, chained at the Adam Tree's base for allegedly murdering King Harald, claims he can help Luffy if freed. The giants refuse. Gaban, as keeper of the key (a giant seastone key he wears as necklace), confronts Luffy: 'If you want to free him, you must take it from me.' He engages Luffy in a Haki-only duel, demonstrating 'Internal Destruction' and future sight, forcing Luffy to use Gear 5 creatively. After Luffy passes his test by showing he will not free Loki recklessly, Gaban entrusts the key to Luffy's judgment, warning that the world will soon need Loki's strength for the final war.

Relationships
Scopper Gaban→Gol D. Rogercaptain

Captain and closest friend. Gaban was one of Roger's first two officers (with Rayleigh), sailing from early days to Laugh Tale. Roger trusted him with Poneglyph route and disbandment decision. Gaban wept at Roger's surrender and execution, and honors his will by watching over the next generation rather than seeking One Piece himself.

Scopper Gaban→Silvers Rayleighally

Co-senior officer, 'Right Hand' and 'Left Hand' of the Pirate King. Rayleigh (silver) is calm, strategic; Gaban (copper) is boisterous, balancing the trio. They fought side-by-side for decades, share knowledge of Laugh Tale, and remain in contact via covert channels. Rayleigh on Sabaody mentions Gaban as 'still alive and well, probably drinking somewhere.' On Elbaf, Gaban says Rayleigh taught him the coating technique he now teaches Luffy.

Scopper Gaban→Kozuki Odenally

Shipmate for one year (26-25 years ago). Initially opponents during Roger vs. Whitebeard clash, then comrades. Gaban admired Oden's strength and dance, sparred with him, and mourned his execution in Wano. Oden's journal, which Gaban helped preserve, later influences his respect for Momonosuke and Wano's retainers.

Scopper Gaban→Ripleyromantic

Giantess, biology teacher at Elbaf's Walrus School, wife/partner of Gaban. Their interspecies marriage is accepted on Elbaf. Ripley is tall (~20m), kind, and strong; she met Gaban when he saved the village from a mountain collapse. She calls him 'Gaban' while giants call him 'Ya-san.' Together they raise Colon.

Scopper Gaban→Colonfamily

Half-giant, half-human son of Gaban and Ripley, age ~10, human-sized (approx. 3m) with small horns, energetic and admires Luffy. Calls Gaban 'Dad' and Ripley 'Mom.' Gaban dotes on him but trains him strictly, wanting him to choose his own path, not piracy. Colon's existence proves human-giant reproduction is possible, a lore point.

Scopper Gaban→Shanks and Buggyally

Apprentices (cabin boys) on Oro Jackson when Gaban was senior officer. Gaban teased and mentored them, often scolding Buggy for greed and encouraging Shanks' Haki. Both remember him fondly; Shanks in Chapter 1139 senses Gaban's Haki on Elbaf and smiles, implying he knew Gaban was hiding there.

Scopper Gaban→Monkey D. Luffyally

Successor generation. Gaban sees Roger in Luffy, tests him on Elbaf, and becomes temporary Haki mentor, echoing Rayleigh's role two years earlier. He challenges Luffy to prove he deserves the key to Loki, pushing him to use Gear 5 with control. Luffy calls him 'Gaban-jii' (Old man Gaban).

Scopper Gaban→Prince Lokihostile

Accursed prince of Elbaf, chained for patricide. Gaban is his jailer, holding the seastone key. He does not trust Loki but acknowledges his strength and that fate may require his release. Their relationship is tense, with Loki trying to manipulate Gaban and Gaban refusing to be swayed.

Scopper Gaban→Crocusally

Former Roger Pirates doctor (Twin Capes lighthouse keeper). Sailed together to Laugh Tale. Both survived disbandment and share knowledge of Laugh Tale. Crocus and Gaban are implied to have stayed in occasional contact via Rayleigh.

God Valley Slavery and Native Hunting Competition

Source Manga Chapters 1095-1096, 1159

Description

Gaban was present at God Valley where Celestial Dragons hunted slaves and pirates for sport. While he fought to stop Rocks and rescue Shakky, the event itself involved mass murder and slavery. Manga does not sanitize: Celestial Dragons' cruelty is shown explicitly in Chapters 1095-1096.

Scholarly Context

Oda uses God Valley to critique slavery and aristocratic impunity; Gaban's role is as liberator, not perpetrator. No glorification.

Piracy Violence and Killing

Source Manga Chapters 966, 0

Description

As senior Roger Pirate, Gaban participated in decades of pirate battles (Edd War, Whitebeard clash, countless New World fights) where killing is implied. His axes are lethal weapons; 'Mountain Eater' epithet suggests destructive power. Manga shows clashes but rarely shows Gaban killing on-panel.

Scholarly Context

One Piece frames Roger Pirates as 'free' pirates vs. Rocks' cruelty; violence is adventure-genre, not gratuitous.

Loki's Alleged Patricide and Elbaf's Blood Law

Source Manga Chapters 1143-1148

Description

Elbaf lore that Gaban now guards involves Prince Loki allegedly murdering his father King Harald to eat a legendary Devil Fruit. Gaban holds the key to Loki's chains, complicit in indefinite imprisonment without trial. The truth is disputed, raising questions of justice.

Scholarly Context

Oda parallels Norse saga patricide and scapegoating; Gaban's role as jailer is morally ambiguous, presented as protective rather than punitive.

Romanization and Etymology

Position A

Official romanization is 'Scopper Gaban' (copper pun)

Position A — evidence

Vivre Card 0388, VIZ translation Chapter 966, One Piece Card Game OP09-118 all use Scopper Gaban

Position B

Fan romanizations 'Scooper Gaban,' 'Copper Gaban,' 'Scopper Gabin' were widely used 2009-2020

Position B — evidence

Early scanlations and wikis used phonetic 'Sukoppa' rendered as Scooper; French actor Jean Gabin suggests 'Gaban' vs 'Gabin'

Scholarly Consensus

Official is Scopper Gaban; 'Copper' is etymological origin but not official spelling. Consensus now settled post-2020.

Power Level Relative to Rayleigh

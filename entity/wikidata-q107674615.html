Who It Is

He was the third shadow behind the Pirate King — not the silver right hand who became legend, but the copper left hand who vanished. For twenty-five years fans knew his silhouette and his pun, nothing more. Then, on the mist-shrouded cliffs of Elbaf, an old man in a Hawaiian shirt, drinking with giants and swinging twin axes, looked at Monkey D. Luffy and said: you still don't know how to use Haki properly.

Attested as jovial, teasing, and fiercely loyal — the 'mood maker' complement to Rayleigh's cool composure. In Oden flashback, he jokes with Oden and Buggy/Shanks, laughs loudly, but is immediately deadly in battle. Rayleigh describes him as the man who 'keeps balance' when Roger is reckless. On Elbaf, older Gaban is eccentric, laid-back, flirtatious (married to giantess Ripley), fond of drink and teasing younger pirates, but stern as a teacher: he bluntly tells Luffy his Haki is 'still childish' and demands he learn to sense without sight. Powers
Twin Axe Mastery - Mountain Eater Style

Wields two large single-bit axes with dual-wield fluency, capable of flying slashes (tobu斬撃) that cleave cliffs and forests. Epithet 'Mountain Eater' implies mountain-scale cutting feat; on Elbaf he demonstrates 'Yasogami: Mountain Eater' — a horizontal flying slash that fells an ancient Adam Tree branch. Comparable to Roger and Rayleigh in close combat during Whitebeard clash.

Limit: Requires both hands; less effective in confined spaces. Axes are non-supernatural tools — can be broken or lost. No Devil Fruit to augment cutting power.

Advanced Haki - All Three Types

Confirmed user of Haoshoku (Conqueror's), Busoshoku (Armament), and Kenbunshoku (Observation) Haki. On Elbaf, he teaches Luffy to refine Kenbunshoku to sense 'voice of all things' and to apply Haoshoku coating (as Rayleigh taught Luffy). Rayleigh states Gaban was one of few Roger Pirates who could 'hear' the Poneglyphs' voice alongside Roger and Oden. Uses Busoshoku hardening on axes.

Limit: At age 78 with leg injury, stamina limited — cannot fight prolonged high-intensity battles like Kaido-level. Haoshoku is not as overwhelming as Roger/Whitebeard; used more for coating than domination.

Navigation, Poneglyph Reading Support, and Survival

As senior Roger Pirate, participated in deciphering Road Poneglyphs (with Oden's translation), navigating the Grand Line to Laugh Tale. Expert seaman, survivalist on Elbaf's harsh environment, and keeper of the key to Loki's seastone chains — implying knowledge of seastone mechanisms and giant lore.

Limit: Cannot read Poneglyphs himself; relied on Oden. Navigation skill is human, not supernatural.

Physical Strength and Longevity

Superhuman strength: clashes equally with Whitebeard commanders (Marco, Jozu era) without injury; at 78 lifts giant-sized axe and overpowers giant warriors. Longevity and durability typical of top-tier pirates; survives God Valley, Edd War, and decades on Elbaf.

Limit: Age and leg injury reduce mobility; needs cane after extended fight. Healing slower than giants.

Accounts I
God Valley Incident — Dawn of the Roger Pirates' RivalryOne Piece manga Chapters 1095-1096 (Kuma/ God Valley flashback), Chapter 1159-1160 (Elbaf/ Rocks exposition)

38 years before present (c. 1484 Age of the Sea Circle), in-universe historical event; published 2023-2025

Thirty-eight years ago, the World Government's God Valley hosted a 'Native Hunting Competition' where Celestial Dragons hunted slaves. The Rocks Pirates, Roger Pirates, Marines (Garp), and Holy Knights converged. Young Scopper Gaban, already a core Roger officer alongside Silvers Rayleigh, fought alongside Roger and Rayleigh against Rocks D. Xebec's crew (including Whitebeard, Kaido, Big Mom, Shiki). Though his individual feats are not spotlighted like Roger vs. Rocks, Gaban is shown in the double-page battle spread holding his axes against Rocks officers, protecting Shakky (Shakuyaku) who had been kidnapped. The incident ends with Rocks' defeat, God Valley's erasure from maps, and Roger's rise to prominence. Establishes Gaban as present at the most consequential battle of the era; cements Roger Pirates as Rocks' rivals; leads to Shakky's rescue and later marriage to Rayleigh. God Valley's cover-up shapes World Government secrecy that Luffy's generation must unravel.

Edd War — Roger vs. Golden Lion ShikiOne Piece manga Chapter 0 and Chapter 966 flashback; One Piece Magazine Vol. 10

27 years before present (c. 1495), published 2009 (Chapter 0) and 2020

The Roger Pirates, seeking the final Road Poneglyph, are ambushed at sea by Shiki the Golden Lion's massive Armada (51 ships) during a storm at Edd War. Roger refuses to submit. Gaban fights alongside Rayleigh on the Oro Jackson's deck, cutting through Shiki's fleet with axe flying slashes as a sudden tempest — possibly fate or the 'Will of D.' — destroys half of Shiki's fleet, allowing Roger to escape. Gaban is shown laughing through the storm, embodying the crew's fearlessness. Roger Pirates survive to continue journey to Laugh Tale; Shiki's defeat foreshadows Roger's inevitability. Demonstrates Gaban's loyalty under overwhelming odds.

The Whitebeard Encounter and Oden's RecruitmentOne Piece manga Chapter 966 'Roger and Whitebeard'

26 years before present (c. 1496), published 2020

The Roger Pirates clash with the Whitebeard Pirates for three days and three nights for treasure and pride. Gaban duels Whitebeard commanders (notably clashing with Kozuki Oden, who had been sailing with Whitebeard). After the battle ends in gift exchange, Roger begs Whitebeard to 'lend' Oden for one year to read the Poneglyphs. Gaban welcomes Oden aboard, jokes about his dancing, and becomes his shipmate and sparring partner. He is present when Oden deciphers the Road Poneglyph at Zou and Wano. Oden joins Roger Pirates, enabling them to reach Laugh Tale. Gaban forms bond with Oden that later influences his respect for Wano and Oden's retainers.

Accounts II
Voyage to Laugh Tale and the Pirate King's DisbandmentOne Piece manga Chapters 967-968 'Roger's Adventure' / 'Oden's Return'

25 years before present (c. 1497), published 2020

With Oden, the Roger Pirates collect the four Road Poneglyphs (Zou, Whole Cake, Wano, Fish-Man Island), reach Lodestar, then use Oden's reading to find Laugh Tale. Gaban is among the few who see the One Piece and laugh with Roger — the 'Joy Boy' revelation. Upon return, Roger disbands the crew, telling them he will turn himself in. At a final feast, Gaban weeps and laughs, accepting Roger's decision to scatter. He parts ways with Rayleigh, Crocus, and the apprentices Shanks and Buggy, disappearing from history. Gaban becomes one of only ~30 people to know the Void Century truth and location of Laugh Tale. His disappearance creates 25-year mystery; World Government never captures him. His knowledge makes him target for those seeking One Piece.

Exile and Family on Elbaf — The Mountain Eater in HidingOne Piece manga Chapters 1139-1148 Elbaf Arc

Present (c. 1522), published 2024-2025

After Roger's execution (24 years ago), Gaban evades the Great Pirate Era manhunt and settles on Elbaf, the giants' nation, where the World Government has little reach. He falls in love with the giantess Ripley (Elbaf biology teacher), fathers a half-giant son Colon (age ~10, human-sized but with horns), and lives in a giant-sized house in the forest stratum, known to giants as 'Ya-san' (Mr. Ya). Known as 'Mountain Eater' for leveling a mountain that threatened the village. When the Straw Hat Pirates arrive on Elbaf (2024 arc), Gaban first appears disguised, testing Luffy, Zoro, and Sanji by attacking them. He then reveals identity, offers to train Luffy in 'true' Haki, and holds the only key to the seastone chains binding Prince Loki (accused of killing King Harald). He debates whether Luffy should free Loki, warning of Loki's power and Elbaf's curse. Reintroduces Roger Pirates' No. 3 into present plot; provides Haki mentorship parallel to Rayleigh on Sabaody; positions Gaban as gatekeeper to Loki and Elbaf's Road Poneglyph. Confirms Roger Pirates survived and built families.

The Key to Loki's Chains — Test of the Next GenerationOne Piece manga Chapters 1143-1148

Present (c. 1522), published 2025

Elbaf's cursed Prince Loki, chained at the Adam Tree's base for allegedly murdering King Harald, claims he can help Luffy if freed. The giants refuse. Gaban, as keeper of the key (a giant seastone key he wears as necklace), confronts Luffy: 'If you want to free him, you must take it from me.' He engages Luffy in a Haki-only duel, demonstrating 'Internal Destruction' and future sight, forcing Luffy to use Gear 5 creatively. After Luffy passes his test by showing he will not free Loki recklessly, Gaban entrusts the key to Luffy's judgment, warning that the world will soon need Loki's strength for the final war. Sets up Loki's potential release, a major Elbaf plot point. Establishes Gaban as moral arbiter for the new generation, echoing Rayleigh's role. Leaves open whether Gaban will join final war.

Scopper Gaban ate a Devil Fruit to become Mountain Eater — No Devil Fruit shown or stated. His epithet comes from axe technique that split a mountain, and his Haki is his power. Manga Chapter 1140 explicitly shows him using Haki-coated axes, not fruit abilities.

Gaban was just a weak background member of Roger Pirates — He was No. 3 senior officer, one of only ~30 to reach Laugh Tale, and in Elbaf Arc fights Gear 5 Luffy to a standstill while holding back. Rayleigh and Roger treated him as equal partner.

Scopper Gaban is the same as 'Copper' the dog or unrelated Gavan from tokusatsu — Distinct character; name similarity to Space Sheriff Gavan is coincidental. One Piece's Gaban is human pirate, not tokusatsu hero.

Gaban died after Roger disbanded the crew — He survived, lived in hiding on Elbaf for 20+ years, married a giantess and had a son. Chapter 1139 confirms he is alive and healthy at 78.

Who It Is

In the Time Room, where every second is a story and every story a second, there lives a being who has seen the end of the universe—and chosen to tell tales instead of endings. Old Man Prismo, the Wish Master of Adventure Time, is not merely a character; he is a narrative force, a living plot device who grants wishes with a twist, and whose own existence is a paradox of creation and destruction.

Prismo is laid-back, whimsical, and enjoys storytelling. He is friendly and welcoming to visitors, often offering them pickles or other snacks. He speaks in a casual, colloquial manner and has a penchant for making puns. Despite his jovial exterior, he is deeply wise and understands the profound implications of his powers. He is also somewhat lonely, as he is isolated in the Time Room, and he values the company of others. He is not malevolent, but he is impartial, granting wishes as they are asked, even if the consequences are dire.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Prismo' is a neologism, likely derived from 'prism,' suggesting a being that refracts reality and desires into different outcomes. 'Old Man' emphasizes his ancient, wise, and somewhat weary demeanor, despite his often whimsical presentation.

Pronunciation — PRIS-moh (with a short 'i' as in 'prism')

Aliases — Prismo, The Wish Master, The Time Room's Keeper

Powers
Wish Granting

Prismo can grant any wish, but with a twist: the wish is often interpreted literally or in an unexpected way, leading to unintended consequences. For example, when Finn wishes to be older, he becomes an old man instantly, and when he wishes to undo the Lich's destruction, the wish creates an alternate timeline.

Limit: Prismo cannot grant his own wishes. He also cannot directly kill or harm others with his power; he must work through the mechanics of the wish. Additionally, his wishes are bound by the rules of the multiverse—he cannot create something from nothing, but can only manipulate existing possibilities.

Time Manipulation

As the master of the Time Room, Prismo has control over time within his domain. He can slow, speed, or reverse time, and he can observe different timelines and alternate realities.

Limit: His time manipulation is largely confined to the Time Room, though he can project his consciousness into other times and places. He cannot alter major events in the past without creating paradoxes, and he respects the integrity of the timeline.

Immortality

Prismo is effectively immortal, having lived for eons. He is a cosmic being who cannot die by conventional means, though he can be 'killed' if his physical form is destroyed, as seen when the Lich destroys his body. However, he can be resurrected if someone makes a wish to bring him back.

Limit: He is vulnerable to magical attacks that can destroy his physical form, but his consciousness persists in a dream-like state until he is resurrected.

Projection and Astral Appearance

Prismo can appear to others in dreams or as a projection, allowing him to communicate across dimensions without physically leaving the Time Room.

Limit: He cannot physically leave the Time Room; he is bound to it. His projections are limited in what they can do—they are primarily for communication.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Prismo appears as a pink, ghostly, floating being with a humanoid torso, arms, and head, but lacking legs—his lower body trails off into a wispy, ethereal tail. He has large, expressive eyes, a wide mouth, and often wears a simple, white toga-like garment. His form is semi-transparent and glows softly. He is typically seen inside his Time Room, a dimension where he sits on a throne made of a giant, glowing hourglass.

Symbols — Prismo is associated with the hourglass, symbolizing time and the granting of wishes. His Time Room is filled with floating clocks, hourglasses, and other time-related imagery. He also has a distinctive 'wish orb'—a glowing, spherical object that he uses to fulfill wishes.

Domains — Prismo resides in the Time Room, a pocket dimension that exists outside of normal spacetime. The Time Room appears as a vast, empty space with a floating platform, where Prismo's throne sits. Time flows differently there, and it serves as a nexus for all timelines. Prismo has the ability to project himself into other dimensions, as seen when he appears to Finn and Jake in the real world.

Accounts I
Prismo the Wish MasterAdventure Time, Season 5, Episode 34

2013

Finn and Jake discover the Time Room and meet Prismo, who offers to grant them a wish. Jake wishes for a sandwich, but Prismo explains that he can grant any wish, and Jake then wishes to be a part of the sandwich. This leads to a series of events where Finn must navigate the consequences of the wish to save Jake. Ultimately, Prismo reveals that he is a being who lives in the Time Room and grants wishes for cosmic beings, and he becomes a friend to Finn and Jake. Finn and Jake gain a valuable ally in Prismo, who later aids them in their quest to stop the Lich.

Wake Up / Escape from the CitadelAdventure Time, Season 6, Episodes 1-2

2014

The Lich, having been trapped in the Time Room as a wish orb, manipulates Prismo into granting his wish to destroy all life. Prismo, unaware of the Lich's true nature, grants the wish, which creates an alternate timeline where the Lich's wish is fulfilled. Finn and Jake, with Prismo's help, travel to the Citadel to prevent the Lich from escaping, but Prismo's physical form is destroyed by the Lich. Finn later uses a wish to bring Prismo back, but the wish also causes the Lich to be reborn as Sweet P. Prismo is resurrected, but the Lich's influence persists. The event reveals Prismo's vulnerability and his willingness to sacrifice himself for his friends.

Accounts II
The More Things ChangeAdventure Time, Season 7, Episode 13

2016

Finn and Jake revisit the Time Room, and Prismo tells them the story of how he became a cosmic being. He was once a mortal man who made a wish to the Cosmic Owl to live forever, and the Cosmic Owl transformed him into the Wish Master. The episode explores Prismo's past and his relationship with the Cosmic Owl. Provides backstory and deepens Prismo's character, showing his origins and the cost of his immortality.

Broke His CrownAdventure Time, Season 8, Episode 3

2017

Prismo appears in a dream to help Finn and Jake understand the nature of the Ice Crown and its connection to the Ice King. He provides cryptic advice, demonstrating his role as a guide and mentor. Prismo's guidance helps Finn and Jake in their quest, but his cryptic nature leaves some questions unanswered.

Who It Is

Nyi Roro Kidul is the most feared supernatural being in Javanese mythology — a beautiful queen dressed in green who rules the Indian Ocean. She is the spiritual consort of Javanese sultans, granting legitimacy to rulers. She claims those who wear green while swimming on the southern coast, dragging them to her underwater kingdom.

Attested in Oral tradition / Babad Tanah Jawi (18th century CE)Era Mataram Sultanate to present

SCP-1981 — 'RONALD REAGAN CUT UP WHILE TALKING'

Who It Is
Form

SCP-1981 is a video recording of Ronald Reagan, the 40th President of the United States, being cut up while giving a speech. The video shows Reagan's body being mutilated, with his limbs and torso being severed and rearranged in a seemingly impossible manner.

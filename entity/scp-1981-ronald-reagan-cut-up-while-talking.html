Who It Is

SCP-1981 is a highly disturbing and unsettling entity that has been known to cause psychological trauma in some individuals. It is a valuable source of knowledge and research opportunities, but it is also a significant risk to the safety of individuals who attempt to study or interact with it.

SCP-1981 is a sentient entity that is capable of manipulating its own appearance and behavior. It is known to be highly aggressive and unpredictable, and it has been known to attack individuals who attempt to study or interact with it.

Form & Signs

Appearance — SCP-1981 is a video recording of Ronald Reagan, the 40th President of the United States, being cut up while giving a speech. The video shows Reagan's body being mutilated, with his limbs and torso being severed and rearranged in a seemingly impossible manner.

Symbols — The video contains no symbols or iconography, but it does feature a distinctive sound effect of a chainsaw cutting through flesh.

Domains — SCP-1981 exists in a virtual domain, and it can only be accessed through a computer or other digital device.

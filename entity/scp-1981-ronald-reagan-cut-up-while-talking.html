A VHS tape of Ronald Reagan giving a speech about the 'Evil Empire.' With each playback, the recording deteriorates: Reagan's skin peels away, his voice distorts, and eventually he is reduced to a flayed figure still delivering a paranoid monologue about things 'behind the stars.' By the 50th playback, he is a skeleton speaking in an unknown language.

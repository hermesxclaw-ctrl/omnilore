Who It Is

In the worlds of Japanese fiction, the name Hazama carries a dual legacy: one a shadowy, serpentine manipulator from a futuristic fighting game, the other a quiet, unassuming surname shared by real-world artists and politicians. But beneath the surface, the name hides a deeper etymology—'hazama' can mean 'ravine' or 'gap'—a fitting metaphor for the liminal spaces between good and evil, life and death, that its most famous fictional bearers inhabit.

The fictional BlazBlue Hazama is cunning, manipulative, and sadistic, taking pleasure in tormenting others. He is also shown to be highly intelligent and strategic. Real-world individuals with the surname vary widely in personality, as they are unrelated.

Powers
Serpent's Venom (BlazBlue)

In BlazBlue, Hazama possesses supernatural abilities derived from his connection to the Azure Grimoire and his true identity as the embodiment of the 'Amaterasu Unit' or as a 'Black Beast' fragment. He can manipulate 'Boundary' energy, create illusions, and use his chain-sickle for both melee and ranged attacks. He also has regenerative abilities and can control others through his 'Serpent's Venom' technique.

Limit: His powers are tied to his connection with the Azure, and he is ultimately defeated by the protagonist Ragna the Bloodedge. He also has a time limit when using his full power.

Kusarigama proficiency (BlazBlue)

Hazama is highly skilled with a kusarigama, a chain-sickle, using it for both close combat and mid-range attacks. He can also use the chain to bind opponents.

Limit: Requires physical skill; not supernatural.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The appearance varies by individual. The fictional character Hazama from BlazBlue is a tall, thin man with green hair and yellow eyes, often wearing a long coat and a sinister smile. He carries a snakeskin-patterned briefcase and uses a weapon resembling a kusarigama. Real-world individuals with the surname have no distinctive appearance.

Symbols — In BlazBlue, Hazama is associated with snakes, green color motifs, and the concept of 'the gap' (hazama). His weapon is a chain-sickle, and he often uses phrases like 'What a pain' and 'The world is full of pain.'

Domains — The BlazBlue Hazama operates within the fictional world of BlazBlue, often in the shadows of the NOL (Novus Orbis Librarium) and other organizations. Real-world individuals live in Japan and other countries.

Accounts I
BlazBlue: The Manipulator's SchemesBlazBlue series (Arc System Works)

2008-2016

Hazama is a recurring antagonist in the BlazBlue series, serving as the chief of the Intelligence Department of the Novus Orbis Librarium. He orchestrates events to achieve his own goals, which involve the Azure Grimoire and the destruction of the current world order. He is revealed to have multiple identities, including being the original 'Hazama' who was a human, and later becoming a vessel for the entity 'Yuuki Terumi.' His schemes lead to the climax of the series. His actions cause chaos and destruction, but ultimately he is defeated by Ragna and others. The world is reset in the true ending.

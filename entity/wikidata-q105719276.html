The Approach

In the worlds of Japanese fiction, the name Hazama carries a dual legacy: one a shadowy, serpentine manipulator from a futuristic fighting game, the other a quiet, unassuming surname shared by real-world artists and politicians. But beneath the surface, the name hides a deeper etymology—'hazama' can mean 'ravine' or 'gap'—a fitting metaphor for the liminal spaces between good and evil, life and death, that its most famous fictional bearers inhabit.

Content advisory. Fictional depictions may include violence, psychological manipulation, and morally ambiguous themes. Real-world individuals listed are public figures; no sensitive personal information is included.
Identity

Hazama

Pronunciation Standard Japanese pronunciation: /hazama/ (hah-zah-mah).

Known As

間挾間狭間羽佐間ハザマ

Etymology

The surname Hazama is written with various kanji, the most common being 間 (meaning 'interval', 'gap', 'space', or 'between'). It can also be written as 狭間 (meaning 'ravine', 'gap', or 'cleft'), 挾間 (meaning 'between' or 'to interpose'), and 羽佐間 (a less common variant). The root 'hazama' (狭間) often refers to a narrow valley or a gap between mountains, suggesting a topographical origin for the name.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese; the surname is found throughout Japan, with historical roots in various regions. The fictional character Hazama from BlazBlue is owned by Arc System Works and is part of the BlazBlue franchise.

Disputed Identifications

There is no major dispute regarding the identity of the surname itself, but the fictional character Hazama from BlazBlue is often confused with the concept of the 'hazama' (gap) in Japanese mythology, and some fan theories conflate him with other characters. Additionally, the real-world individuals with the surname are distinct from the fictional character, though the name is shared.

Description

Appearance

The appearance varies by individual. The fictional character Hazama from BlazBlue is a tall, thin man with green hair and yellow eyes, often wearing a long coat and a sinister smile. He carries a snakeskin-patterned briefcase and uses a weapon resembling a kusarigama. Real-world individuals with the surname have no distinctive appearance.

Symbols & Iconography

In BlazBlue, Hazama is associated with snakes, green color motifs, and the concept of 'the gap' (hazama). His weapon is a chain-sickle, and he often uses phrases like 'What a pain' and 'The world is full of pain.'

Behaviour & Personality

The fictional BlazBlue Hazama is cunning, manipulative, and sadistic, taking pleasure in tormenting others. He is also shown to be highly intelligent and strategic. Real-world individuals with the surname vary widely in personality, as they are unrelated.

Domains & Habitats

The BlazBlue Hazama operates within the fictional world of BlazBlue, often in the shadows of the NOL (Novus Orbis Librarium) and other organizations. Real-world individuals live in Japan and other countries.

Version Differences

There is no single 'Hazama' across all media; each fictional character is distinct. The BlazBlue Hazama is unique to that franchise. The surname itself has multiple kanji variants, but they are not different versions of the same person.

Powers & Abilities

Serpent's Venom (BlazBlue)

Source Version BlazBlue: Calamity Trigger, Continuum Shift, Chronophantasma, Central Fiction

Description

In BlazBlue, Hazama possesses supernatural abilities derived from his connection to the Azure Grimoire and his true identity as the embodiment of the 'Amaterasu Unit' or as a 'Black Beast' fragment. He can manipulate 'Boundary' energy, create illusions, and use his chain-sickle for both melee and ranged attacks. He also has regenerative abilities and can control others through his 'Serpent's Venom' technique.

Limitations

His powers are tied to his connection with the Azure, and he is ultimately defeated by the protagonist Ragna the Bloodedge. He also has a time limit when using his full power.

Contradictions

Some sources describe him as a human, others as a 'phantom' or 'embodiment of chaos.' His true nature is revealed gradually, leading to conflicting interpretations among fans.

BlazBlue: The Manipulator's Schemes

Source BlazBlue series (Arc System Works)Culture Japanese video game culturePeriod 2008-2016

Summary

Hazama is a recurring antagonist in the BlazBlue series, serving as the chief of the Intelligence Department of the Novus Orbis Librarium. He orchestrates events to achieve his own goals, which involve the Azure Grimoire and the destruction of the current world order. He is revealed to have multiple identities, including being the original 'Hazama' who was a human, and later becoming a vessel for the entity 'Yuuki Terumi.' His schemes lead to the climax of the series.

Contradictions & Disputes

Hazama's true nature in BlazBlue

Position A

Hazama is a separate character from Yuuki Terumi, with his own personality and history.

Position A — evidence

Early games depict Hazama as an independent agent with his own goals.

Position B

Hazama is just a vessel for Yuuki Terumi, and the two are essentially the same entity.

Position B — evidence

Later games reveal that Terumi implanted himself into Hazama, and Hazama's personality is largely Terumi's.

Scholarly Consensus

The official canon treats them as separate but linked, with Hazama's original personality being subsumed by Terumi.

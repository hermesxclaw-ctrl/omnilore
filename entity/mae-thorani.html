Who It Is

Mae Thorani is the earth goddess who witnessed the Buddha's enlightenment. When Mara challenged the Buddha, he touched the earth, and Mae Thorani appeared, wringing water from her hair — accumulated from every act of generosity across lifetimes — creating a flood that swept away Mara's armies.

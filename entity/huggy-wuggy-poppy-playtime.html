Who It Is

The mascot toy of Playtime Co. and the primary antagonist of Chapter 1. Huggy Wuggy is a giant, blue-furred plush toy with elongated limbs, a permanent rictus grin full of needle-sharp teeth, and an intelligence that far exceeds a child's toy. He was once a harmless marketing creation, but after the factory's mysterious shutdown and the disappearance of all employees, Huggy — like all Playtime Co. toys — became something far darker. He stalks the player through the abandoned factory at incredible speed.

Form

Approximately 3 meters of lanky blue fur, with a cylindrical head featuring two large googly eyes and a mouth stretched into a wide grin lined with rows of sharp, inward-pointing teeth. His arms and legs are impossibly long, with oversized hands and feet. He moves on all fours with disturbingly fast, spider-like scuttling.

Cultural Significance

Huggy Wuggy's design 'solved' the mascot horror formula by being simultaneously cute and nightmarish, making him an instant merchandise juggernaut and a fixture of children's YouTube content (to parental dismay).

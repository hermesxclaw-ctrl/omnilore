Who It Is
Form

Rübezahl is often depicted as a wild-haired, bearded man dressed in a long, green coat, with a staff in one hand and a turnip in the other. He is said to be a tall, imposing figure with a commanding presence.

Powers

Shape-shifting Rübezahl is said to have the ability to shape-shift into various forms, including a turnip, a tree, and a mountain. He can also transform into a human-like figure with supernatural strength and agility.

Accounts

Rübezahl is said to be a wild-haired, bearded man who lives in the Giant Mountains. He is known for his mischievous nature and is often depicted as a trickster figure.

Rübezahl is said to have a special connection to the turnip, which is a symbol of his connection to the earth and his role as a guardian of the mountains. He is often depicted as a wild-haired, bearded man holding a turnip in his hand.

Etymology
The name Rübezahl is derived from the German word 'Rübe', meaning turnip, and the diminutive suffix '-zahl', indicating a small or insignificant turnip. ⇌ The name Rübezahl is derived from the Polish word 'licz' meaning 'to count' rather than 'turnip'.

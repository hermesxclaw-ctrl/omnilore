Who It Is
Form

The Moon is a natural satellite of Earth, with a diameter of approximately 2,159 miles (3,475 kilometers). It orbits around Earth at an average distance of 384,399 kilometers (238,854 miles). The Moon's surface is composed of dark gray rock and is characterized by craters, mountains, and lava flows.

Powers

Tidal Forces The Moon's gravitational pull causes the tides to rise and fall on Earth, affecting the oceans and coastlines.

Incident Log

The Moon Goddess is a mythological figure associated with the Moon and its cycles. She is often depicted as a powerful, feminine entity that influences the tides and the lives of humans.

The Great Moon Hoax was a series of articles published in The New York Sun in 1835, claiming that a famous astronomer had discovered life on the Moon. The articles were later revealed to be a hoax.

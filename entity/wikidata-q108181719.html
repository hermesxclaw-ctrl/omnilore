Who It Is

In Water 7, he was the unflappable bartender who poured drinks with bull-like horns and a deadpan stare — the man you would never notice. Then he touched a wall, a door bloomed where no door should exist, and the air itself swung open. Blueno is One Piece's quiet nightmare: the assassin who turns every surface — wall, floor, sky, even your own body — into a doorway.

Attested as taciturn, deadpan, and utterly professional. As an undercover bartender in Water 7 (5 years), he was polite, observant, and unflappable — never breaking cover even while serving the Straw Hats and Franky Family. In CP9, he is laconic, obeys orders without question, and rarely shows emotion except mild surprise (notably when Luffy's Gear Second surpasses his Soru). He is not sadistic like Lucci or flamboyant like Kumadori; his cruelty is bureaucratic — he will erase memories, break necks, and open doors through human bodies without comment. Powers
Doa Doa no Mi (Door-Door Fruit)

Paramecia-type Devil Fruit allowing Blueno to create doors on any surface, including intangible or living matter. Types: (1) Surface Door — creates a hinged door on walls/floors that opens to the other side or to his pocket dimension; (2) Air Door (Air Door / Air Open) — creates a door in mid-air leading to a separate dimensional space where he can hide, travel, or trap others; (3) Body Door — opens a revolving door on a person's body (e.g., spinning Luffy's face). He can also create multiple doors in succession for teleport-like movement and dimensional escape. First shown Ch.352 and Ch.374-375.

Limit: Requires physical contact to create a door; cannot create doors without touching surface/air with hand. Doors have standard size limited by his arm span and stamina — cannot door an entire island. Air Door dimension is temporary; prolonged stay requires concentration. Opponents who predict door placement (Luffy with Gear Second speed) can counter. Standard Devil Fruit weaknesses: seawater and Seastone (Kairoseki) nullify power; cannot swim. Does not grant intangibility — door creation is not avoidance unless timed.

Rokushiki (Six Powers) — Master

CP9's superhuman martial art. Blueno has mastered all six: Geppo (Moon Walk — air jumps), Tekkai (Iron Body — muscle hardening to steel-like defense), Shigan (Finger Pistol — bullet-like finger jab), Rankyaku (Storm Leg — air blade kick), Soru (Shave — high-speed movement), Kami-e (Paper Art — body goes limp to dodge). His Doriki is 820 (Fukurou's measurement, Ch.379), indicating physical power above most Marines but below Lucci (4000), Kaku (2200), Jyabura (2180). His Tekkai is notable for 'Tekkai Rin' adaptation and his Soru was considered very fast until Luffy's Gear Second outpaced it.

Limit: Tekkai immobilizes user while active (cannot move unless mastered 'Tekkai Kenpo' — Blueno shows limited mobility during Tekkai). Soru speed is linear and predictable to Observation Haki users. Rankyaku range limited to leg arc. Kami-e weak to blunt area attacks. All Rokushiki drains stamina; consecutive use exhausts him faster than higher-Doriki members.

Tekkai Kenpo and Shigan Variants

Blueno integrates Tekkai with movement (Tekkai Kenpo) to a lesser degree than Lucci/Jyabura, allowing partial defense while attacking. Uses Shigan as rapid jabs; in combination with doors, he can Shigan from unexpected angles by emerging from Air Doors.

Limit: Cannot maintain full Tekkai while using Soru at full speed; must choose defense or evasion. Shigan requires finger rigidity — broken fingers (not shown) would disable it.

Espionage, Assassination & Infiltration Expertise

Five-year deep cover as bartender in Water 7 without detection by Galley-La or Franky Family; expert in intelligence gathering, interrogation, and World Government black ops. As CP0, authorized to act with Celestial Dragon authority, including assassinations and 'erasing' witnesses (e.g., Egghead, Ch.1068). Trained since childhood as government assassin (CP9 backstory, Ch.491).

Limit: Bound by World Government hierarchy — cannot act against Five Elders/Imu orders; cover blown once Pluton pursuit escalated, showing long-term infiltration is vulnerable to Straw Hat chaos. No Haki confirmed in manga — relies on Rokushiki and fruit rather than Busoshoku/Kenbunshoku.

Accounts I
Undercover at Water 7 — The Bartender of Blueno's BarOne Piece Manga Chapters 322-325, 348-355; Anime Episodes 220-226

2004 (publication); In-universe: 5 years prior to Enies Lobby (~1517-1522 Age of Sea)

For five years, Blueno runs Blueno's Bar near Galley-La headquarters as CP9's quietest agent alongside Rob Lucci (shipwright), Kaku (shipwright), and Kalifa (secretary) under Iceburg. He serves Paulie, Luffy, Zoro, Nami and Franky without suspicion. When CP9's mission to recover Pluton blueprints from Iceburg intensifies, Blueno uses his Doa Doa power to create doors in Iceburg's room, ambushes him, and frames the Straw Hats. He later doors through walls to kidnap Franky and Nico Robin, revealing his true identity when he traps Robin in his Air Door dimension and transports her to Enies Lobby via sea train. Exposes CP9 and triggers Water 7's burning and the Straw Hats' declaration of war on the World Government. Establishes Blueno's fruit and sets up Enies Lobby arc. Demonstrates World Government's long-term infiltration doctrine.

Enies Lobby — Luffy vs. Blueno and the Debut of Gear SecondOne Piece Manga Chapters 375-385; Anime Episodes 266-273

2005 (publication); In-universe: 1522

At Enies Lobby's Tower of Justice, Blueno is assigned to guard the bridge to the Gates of Justice where Robin and Franky are held. Monkey D. Luffy, separated from his crew, confronts Blueno. Initially Blueno dominates with Soru and Tekkai, mocking Luffy's inability to hit him. Luffy then unveils Gear Second (Gomu Gomu no Jet techniques), accelerating his blood flow to achieve Soru-like speed, overwhelming Blueno's Tekkai and defeating him with Jet Bazooka and Jet Gatling. Blueno is knocked unconscious, his door to Robin's location left open, allowing Luffy to advance. First defeat of a CP9 member; proves Gear Second's viability and that Rokushiki can be surpassed. Blueno's loss forces CP9 to mobilize fully (Lucci vs Luffy). Becomes iconic fight referenced in all One Piece power-scaling. Anime extends fight by ~8 minutes.

CP9's Independent Report — After the Buster CallCover Story Serial, Manga Chapters 491-528 title pages; Tankobon Vol.50-51

2008 (publication); In-universe: weeks after Enies Lobby

After Enies Lobby's destruction by Buster Call and the Straw Hats' victory, CP9 are branded traitors by Spandam to cover his failure. Wounded and hunted by Marines, they flee to St. Poplar. Blueno, despite his own injuries, helps the group survive: he uses his Air Door to hide them and later works as a street painter (with Kumadori and others performing) to earn money for Lucci's hospital bills. When Marines and Candy Pirates threaten the town's children, CP9 intervenes, defeating the pirates and earning the town's gratitude before sailing away on a stolen ship, now fugitives but loyal to each other. Humanizes CP9; shows Blueno's loyalty beyond orders and his utility power for evasion and fundraising. Sets up their eventual reinstatement as CP0 — World Government later pardons them because their strength is too valuable to waste.

Accounts II
Return as CP0 — Dressrosa and Reverie OperationsManga Chapters 705, 793, 906-908; Anime Episodes 642, 878-889

2013-2018 (publication); In-universe: 1522 (two years post-timeskip)

Two years later, Blueno reappears as a member of CP0 (Cipher Pol Aigis Zero), the strongest intelligence agency directly serving the Celestial Dragons, wearing a white suit and mask. At Dressrosa (Ch.705), CP0 agents (including Blueno, Lucci, Kaku) are seen observing the underworld port and Doflamingo's defeat, reporting to the World Government. At the Reverie (Levely) in Mary Geoise (Ch.906-908), CP0 guards the Celestial Dragons and attempts to capture Vivi and apprehend Sabo after Imu and the Five Elders' machinations. Blueno's presence confirms his promotion and pardon. Elevates Blueno from judicial assassin to world-level enforcer with authority above Marines. Shows World Government's continuity — same agents now with greater impunity. Foreshadows Egghead deployment.

Egghead Island — CP0 Assault and the Door to ChaosManga Chapters 1068-1107, 1110-1125; Anime Episodes 1090+

2023-2024 (publication); In-universe: 1522 (Egghead Incident)

CP0 (Lucci, Kaku, Stussy, Blueno) is dispatched by the Five Elders to assassinate Dr. Vegapunk on Egghead. Blueno is part of the blockade, using Air Doors to infiltrate the Labophase barrier and bypass Frontier Dome defenses. He participates in the initial attack on Atlas and the capture attempts, and later witnesses Stussy's betrayal (as Vegapunk's clone) and Lucci's battles with Luffy (Gear 5) and Zoro. During the Egghead Incident, the Five Elders (Jaygarcia Saturn, then others) arrive via Abyss, and CP0's mission spirals as the Straw Hats, Bonney, and Giants intervene. Blueno survives the chaos and withdraws with CP0 remnants after Saturn's death and Garling's promotion. Shows Blueno's fruit still tactically vital for infiltration against Vegapunk's technology. Demonstrates CP0's limits against Yonko-level forces and the Five Elders' overwhelming power. Sets up post-Egghead World Government reshuffle (Saturn killed by Imu, Garling ascends).

The Door That Should Not Exist — Ohara and the Legacy of World Government DoorsManga Chapter 397 (Ohara flashback), Chapter 1066 (Vegapunk exposition), SBS Vol.44

2005-2022 (publication); In-universe: Void Century to present

While not directly featuring Blueno, the thematic story of the World Government's suppression of history contextualizes his power. The Government's ability to 'open doors' anywhere mirrors its surveillance state — Blueno's fruit is the literalization of Cipher Pol's doctrine that no wall, no secret, no body is closed to them. Vegapunk's notes on Devil Fruits (Ch.1069) classify Paramecia like Doa Doa no Mi as reality-warping, and Blueno's use of a pocket dimension parallels the Government's hidden history (Void Century, Poneglyphs). His door on a human face (vs Luffy) echoes the Government's dehumanization of its enemies. Narrative resonance: Blueno is the Government's key — the man who can open any door, serving the regime that keeps the door to the Void Century shut. His defeat by Luffy symbolizes the Straw Hats' refusal to let the Government control access to truth.

Blueno's Doa Doa no Mi is a Logia that makes him intangible. — It is a Paramecia that creates doors; Blueno remains tangible and can be hit if timed (Luffy's Gear Second proves this). Intangibility is an illusion of door timing, not Logia dispersion.

Blueno was the strongest CP9 member. — He was the second-weakest by Doriki (820), above only Kalifa (630) and below Fukurou (800? actually 800), Kumadori (810), Jyabura (2180), Kaku (2200), and Lucci (4000). His strength was utility, not raw power.

Blueno can open a door to anywhere instantly, making him unbeatable for travel. — Air Door leads to a pocket dimension that still requires him to travel through it; he cannot teleport across islands instantly without traversing the dimension, and range is limited by stamina and line-of-sight touch. He still needed the sea train to get Robin to Enies Lobby.

Blueno died at Enies Lobby. — He was defeated but survived; cover story (Ch.491-528) and CP0 reappearance (Ch.705 onward) confirm survival and promotion.

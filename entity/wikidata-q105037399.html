The Approach

Megumi Fushiguro is the stoic, shadow-wielding sorcerer of Jujutsu Kaisen, a boy who summons shikigami from the darkness and carries the weight of a cursed lineage. His quiet determination and willingness to sacrifice himself for others make him one of modern anime's most compelling heroes.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of violence, death, and supernatural horror. Themes of self-sacrifice and existential struggle.
Identity

Megumi Fushiguro

Pronunciation MEH-goo-mee FOO-shee-goo-roh

Etymology

The surname Fushiguro (伏黒) can be interpreted as 'prostrate black' or 'hidden black', possibly referencing his shadow-based abilities and secretive nature. The given name Megumi (恵) means 'blessing' or 'grace'.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese manga and anime franchise Jujutsu Kaisen, created by Gege Akutami.

Disputed Identifications

None known.

Description

Appearance

Megumi is a tall, lean young man with spiky black hair and sharp green eyes. He typically wears the Jujutsu High uniform: a dark high-collar jacket and matching pants. His expression is usually serious and reserved.

Symbols & Iconography

His primary symbol is the shadow, which he manipulates to summon shikigami. His Ten Shadows technique involves ten distinct shikigami, each with its own symbolic animal form (e.g., dogs, tiger, frog, etc.).

Behaviour & Personality

Megumi is introverted, pragmatic, and deeply serious. He often appears cold but is genuinely caring, willing to risk his life to protect others. He has a strong sense of justice and a desire to save people, even those he doesn't know. He is humble and tends to downplay his own abilities.

Domains & Habitats

He operates within the modern world of Jujutsu Kaisen, primarily at Tokyo Jujutsu High and various cursed locations. His shadow abilities allow him to create a pocket dimension called the 'Shadow Storage'.

Version Differences

The anime adaptation closely follows the manga, with minor pacing and scene adjustments. No significant differences in Megumi's characterization or abilities.

Powers & Abilities

Ten Shadows Technique (十種影法術, Tokusō Kagehōjutsu)

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

A inherited technique that allows Megumi to summon shikigami from shadows. He can also merge shikigami to create more powerful forms. The technique has ten distinct shikigami: two divine dogs, a frog, a great serpent, a nue, a rabbit escape, a toad, a deer, a tiger, and the ultimate fusion, Mahoraga.

Limitations

Summoning and controlling shikigami requires cursed energy and concentration. If a shikigami is destroyed, it cannot be re-summoned. The technique is physically demanding and can exhaust Megumi. Mahoraga can only be summoned through a ritual that puts the user at great risk.

Contradictions

None known.

Domain Expansion: Chimera Shadow Garden (嵌合暗翳庭, Kangō An'ei Tei)

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

A domain that creates a space filled with shadows, allowing Megumi to freely summon and control shikigami within it. The domain has a high probability of hitting its target, but it is incomplete, lacking a sure-hit effect.

Limitations

Incomplete domain, so it doesn't guarantee a hit. It consumes large amounts of cursed energy and can be broken by stronger domains.

Contradictions

None known.

Shadow Manipulation

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

Beyond summoning shikigami, Megumi can manipulate shadows for utility, such as creating a shadow storage space to hide objects or himself.

Limitations

Requires concentration and cursed energy.

Contradictions

None known.

Stories & Myths

Introduction and First Mission

Source Jujutsu Kaisen manga chapters 1-3, anime episodes 1-3Culture JapanesePeriod 2018

Summary

Megumi is introduced as a jujutsu sorcerer who encounters Yuji Itadori, a high school student with extraordinary physical abilities. Megumi is tasked with exorcising a cursed object, but Yuji consumes it to save his friends, becoming the host of the King of Curses, Ryomen Sukuna. Megumi brings Yuji to Jujutsu High, where they become classmates.

Shibuya Incident

Source Jujutsu Kaisen manga chapters 85-136, anime Season 2Culture JapanesePeriod 2018

Summary

Megumi fights against cursed spirits and gets caught in the chaos of the Shibuya Incident. He faces the special grade curse, Hanami, and later encounters the vengeful spirit, Sukuna, who takes over Yuji's body. Megumi is gravely injured while protecting others and is forced to use his domain expansion for the first time.

Culling Game Arc

Source Jujutsu Kaisen manga chapters 137-221Culture JapanesePeriod 2018

Summary

Megumi wakes from his coma and participates in the Culling Game, a deadly tournament organized by the villain Kenjaku. He seeks to save his sister Tsumiki, but discovers she is possessed. He is forced to fight powerful sorcerers and eventually summons Mahoraga to defeat the reincarnated sorcerer Yorozu, but at a great cost.

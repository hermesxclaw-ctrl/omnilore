Etymology

From Latin 'victor', meaning 'conqueror' or 'winner', derived from 'vincere' (to conquer).

Cultural Ownership

Pan-European; widely used across Western and Eastern Europe, Latin America, and other regions influenced by Latin and Christian traditions.

Disputed Identifications

The name is shared by numerous historical and fictional figures, leading to frequent misattributions. For example, 'Victor' in the context of 'Frankenstein' is often confused with the monster, and 'Victor' as a submarine class is distinct from the Handley Page Victor aircraft.

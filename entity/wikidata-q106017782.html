Who It Is

In the rain-slicked streets of Chicago, a man in a long coat and flat cap watches through the lens of his phone, his fingers dancing over a screen that controls the city itself. He is a ghost in the machine, a vigilante driven by guilt and vengeance, known to the underworld only as 'The Vigilante' or 'The Fox'. His name is Aiden Pearce, and his story begins with a tragedy that would shape the fate of a city.

Aiden is portrayed as a brooding, introverted, and morally ambiguous antihero. He is driven by a strong sense of justice, but his methods are often ruthless and violent. He is highly intelligent, calculating, and obsessive, particularly when it comes to protecting his family and achieving his goals. He struggles with guilt and trauma from his niece's death, which fuels his vigilante activities. He is not particularly sociable and often keeps others at a distance.

Powers
Hacking

Aiden is a master hacker, able to manipulate the ctOS system that controls Chicago's infrastructure. He can hack traffic lights, security cameras, ATMs, and other connected devices, as well as perform more complex hacks like stealing data and controlling trains.

Limit: His hacking abilities are limited to systems connected to ctOS; he cannot hack systems that are offline or not networked. He also requires a smartphone to perform hacks.

Combat and firearm proficiency

Aiden is highly skilled in hand-to-hand combat and proficient with various firearms, including pistols, rifles, and shotguns. He is also capable of performing stealth takedowns and using a baton as a melee weapon.

Limit: None explicitly stated, but his abilities are typical of a well-trained vigilante, not superhuman.

Driving skills

Aiden is an expert driver, capable of high-speed chases and evasive maneuvers in various vehicles.

Limit: None explicitly stated.

Focus ability

Aiden has a 'focus' ability that slows down time, allowing him to aim and shoot with precision during intense moments. This is presented as a gameplay mechanic rather than a literal superpower.

Limit: It is a gameplay mechanic, not a narrative ability; it does not have a canonical explanation.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Aiden is a Caucasian male in his late 30s to early 40s, with short brown hair and a rugged, stubbled face. He typically wears a long grey trench coat, a flat cap, and a scarf, giving him a distinctive, detective-like silhouette. In Watch Dogs: Legion, he retains a similar look but with a more aged appearance, including grey streaks in his hair and a more weathered face.

Symbols — His signature symbol is the fox, which appears as a mask in promotional art and as a graffiti tag in the games. The fox represents his cunning and stealth. His iconic attire, especially the flat cap and trench coat, has become synonymous with the character and is often used in fan art and cosplay.

Domains — Aiden operates primarily in urban environments, initially in Chicago, then San Francisco (in Watch Dogs 2 cameo), and later London. He is most comfortable in the digital realm, using ctOS to control city infrastructure. He often uses underground bunkers and safehouses as bases of operation.

Accounts I
The Revenge of Aiden PearceWatch Dogs (2014)

2012-2013 (in-game)

After a failed heist, Aiden's niece Lena is killed in a car crash caused by a hitman hired by mob boss Dermont Quinn. Aiden spends a year tracking down those responsible, uncovering a conspiracy involving the ctOS system and the Blume Corporation. He eventually kills Quinn and Damien Brenks, the hacker who manipulated events, and becomes a vigilante protector of Chicago. Aiden eliminates his enemies but loses Clara and becomes estranged from his family. He gains control over ctOS but rejects DedSec's offer to use it for surveillance, choosing to operate alone.

The Bounty on AidenWatch Dogs: Bad Blood (2014)

After Watch Dogs (2014)

T-Bone discovers that Blume has placed a $100,000 bounty on Aiden after learning he used the Bunker as a hideout. T-Bone eliminates the bounty hunters and warns Aiden, who avoids capture. Aiden remains at large, and the bounty is not collected.

Accounts II
The San Francisco IncidentWatch Dogs 2 (2016)

2016 (in-game)

Aiden, now in San Francisco, investigates a human trafficking ring run by the Auntie Shu Boys and the Bratva. He is captured, but Marcus Holloway helps him escape, allowing Aiden to continue his investigation. Aiden escapes and presumably continues his vigilante work.

BloodlineWatch Dogs: Legion - Bloodline (2021)

Before the events of Watch Dogs: Legion (2020s)

Aiden travels to London to reconnect with his nephew Jackson and complete a fixer contract for Jordi. He encounters Wrench, and they initially clash but later team up to take down Broca Tech and the private military company Albion. Aiden and Wrench successfully sabotage Broca Tech's projects, and Aiden begins to rebuild his relationship with Jackson.

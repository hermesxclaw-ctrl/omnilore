Who It Is
Form

Hervör is often depicted as a fierce and determined warrior, wielding the cursed sword Tyrfing. She is typically shown wearing armor and carrying a shield. Her appearance is that of a strong and capable female warrior.

Powers

Wielder of Tyrfing Hervör is able to wield the cursed sword Tyrfing, which is said to bring about the downfall of its wielder. This power is a central aspect of her character and is often depicted as a source of both strength and weakness.

Accounts

Hervör challenges her father Angantýr's ghost in his gravemound for his cursed sword Tyrfing. She is able to defeat his ghost and claim the sword, but at great personal cost.

Hervör and her brother engage in a fierce battle, resulting in Hervör's death. This story is a variant of the more well-known story of the death of Angantýr.

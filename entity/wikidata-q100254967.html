Who It Is

Born of the eternal winter of the Freljord, Anivia is a demigod who embodies the cycle of life, death, and rebirth. Her cry is the first breath of winter, and her wings are the storm that reshapes the land. To the tribes of the north, she is both a guardian and a force of nature, a being whose death is but a prelude to her inevitable return.

Anivia is ancient, wise, and patient, embodying the calm and stillness of winter. She is protective of the Freljord and its people, though she is not always directly involved in mortal affairs, preferring to act as a guardian spirit. She is serene and contemplative, but when provoked, she can unleash devastating storms and blizzards. She is also deeply connected to the land, and her presence is felt in the changing of seasons.

Powers
Rebirth

Anivia has the ability to be reborn from her own ashes, or in her case, from a shard of ice. When she dies, she leaves behind an egg that hatches into a new Anivia, allowing her to return to life. This rebirth is central to her identity as a phoenix and her role as a guardian.

Limit: The rebirth process is not instant and leaves her vulnerable during the egg stage. In gameplay, the egg has reduced defenses.

Ice Manipulation

Anivia can create and control ice and snow at will. She can summon blizzards, create walls of ice, and freeze her enemies solid. Her control over ice is so great that she can shape it into structures or weapons.

Limit: Her abilities require a significant amount of mana, and she is vulnerable to fire and heat.

Flight

Anivia can fly at incredible speeds, covering vast distances across the Freljord. Her flight is often accompanied by gusts of freezing wind.

Limit: None specified.

Divine Authority

As a demigod, Anivia has authority over winter and the natural forces associated with it. She can influence the weather and the seasons, and she is revered by the tribes of the Freljord as a divine being.

Limit: Her authority is limited to the Freljord region and its surroundings.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Anivia is a majestic phoenix made of ice and snow, with a wingspan that blots out the sky. Her body is composed of crystalline ice, glowing with a cold blue light. She has a long, elegant neck, a sharp beak, and piercing blue eyes that seem to hold the wisdom of ages. Her feathers are like shards of ice, and when she moves, she leaves a trail of frost in her wake.

Symbols — Anivia is associated with the symbol of the phoenix, particularly the cryophoenix, representing rebirth and the eternal cycle of seasons. In the Freljord, she is often depicted in carvings and totems as a mighty bird with outstretched wings, surrounded by snowflakes and ice crystals.

Domains — Anivia resides in the Freljord, a harsh, frozen region of Runeterra. She is most commonly associated with the Howling Abyss, a deep chasm where she nests, and the mountain peaks where the winds are strongest. She is also known to travel across the entire region, bringing winter wherever she goes.

Ancient times (fictional timeline)

Anivia is one of the oldest beings in Runeterra, born from the primordial ice of the Freljord. She has watched over the land for millennia, guiding the tribes and protecting them from threats. She is known to have aided the mortal hero Ashe in her quest to unite the Freljord, and she has clashed with other demigods like Volibear and Ornn over the fate of the region. Anivia's actions have shaped the fate of the Freljord, and her influence is still felt in the region's culture and religion.

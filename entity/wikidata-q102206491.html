The Approach

In the shadow of the Hawraman mountains, where the waters of Lake Dukan lap against ancient shores, lies a town that has become a living symbol of Kurdish resilience. Ranya is not merely a place on a map; it is a name etched into the memory of a people who, in the spring of 1991, rose against an empire and won, if only for a moment, their freedom.

The 1991 Kurdish Uprising in Ranya

Source Wikipedia article and general knowledge of Kurdish historyCulture KurdishPeriod 1991

Summary

On May 5, 1991, after a two-week confrontation, local civilians and Peshmerga forces successfully expelled the Iraqi army of Saddam Hussein from Ranya. This event was part of the larger 1991 Kurdish uprising (Raperîn) against Ba'athist Iraq. The anniversary is regularly celebrated with guests from all over Kurdistan.

Participants

Local civilians

Peshmerga forces

Iraqi army of Saddam Hussein

Consequences

The expulsion of the Iraqi army from Ranya was a symbolic victory for Kurdish resistance. It contributed to the broader Kurdish autonomy movement, eventually leading to the establishment of the Kurdistan Region's autonomy.

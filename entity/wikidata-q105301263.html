Who It Is

In the neon-lit underworld of Southtown, where gang loyalties are etched in blood and steel, Soiree Meira burns with a fire his cool-headed twin brother Alba keeps banked. Younger by minutes, brash where Alba is calculating, Soiree is the impulsive heart of the Meira brothers' quest to avenge their fallen mentor Fate and tear down Duke's Mephistopheles empire—even if it means walking into the lion's den with fists first and questions never.

Soiree is brash, impulsive, and hot-headed, often acting before thinking. He is fiercely loyal to his brother Alba and determined to avenge Fate by any means necessary. He shows a soft spot for Chae Lim, suggesting a romantic interest. He is more straightforward and less calculating than Alba.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Soiree' is French for 'evening' or 'evening party,' possibly chosen to contrast with his brother Alba, which means 'dawn' in Italian and Spanish. This suggests a thematic duality of light/dark or beginning/end.

Pronunciation — SWAH-ray MAY-rah

Powers
Martial Arts (Meira-style Kickboxing)

Soiree employs a hybrid of kickboxing and street fighting, emphasizing fast, powerful kicks and aggressive rushdown tactics. His moveset includes rapid punch combinations, spinning kicks, and a striking style that reflects his hot-headed nature.

Limit: As a fighter in a 3D arena, his moves are balanced with startup frames and recovery, making him vulnerable to counterattacks if overextended.

Super Moves (Desperation Moves)

Soiree has powerful super moves that require a full Power Gauge, such as a flurry of kicks that can juggle opponents. These are typical of KOF fighters.

Limit: Requires meter management; can be blocked or evaded.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Soiree has a lean, athletic build with spiky, medium-length dark hair with a reddish tint. He wears a black tank top, dark pants with a chain wallet, and fingerless gloves. His outfit is more casual and rebellious compared to Alba's formal vest. He has a confident, cocky smirk.

Symbols — His color scheme is typically red/black, contrasting with Alba's blue/white. He is associated with fire motifs, reflecting his brash personality.

Domains — Southtown, a fictional city inspired by American urban environments, particularly the gang-controlled districts.

Main Story of KOF: Maximum ImpactKOF: Maximum Impact (game)

2004 (in-game: 2 years after N.E.S.T.S. saga)

Soiree and his twin brother Alba are adopted sons of Fate, the benevolent leader of the Addis gang. After Fate is murdered by Duke, the brothers enter the Mephistopheles Fighting Tournament to confront Duke and dismantle his criminal empire. Soiree fights through the tournament, defeating Duke's cronies and ultimately facing Duke himself. In his ending, he and Alba successfully defeat Duke and restore order to Southtown, with Soiree vowing to protect the city alongside his brother. Duke's defeat, the dissolution of the Mephistopheles gang, and the restoration of peace in Southtown. Soiree's relationship with Chae Lim is hinted to develop.

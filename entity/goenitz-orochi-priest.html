Who It Is

The high priest of the Orochi clan, a being of immense wind and lightning power. Goenitz has long silver hair, red tribal markings on his face, and a perpetually serene smile. He controls the wind absolutely — generating tornadoes, hurricanes, and wind blades. He was responsible for killing the healer Chizuru's sister during the Orochi Wars.

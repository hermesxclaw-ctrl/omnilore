Who It Is

In the blood-soaked arena of King of Fighters, few figures embody the apocalyptic will of the Orochi more chillingly than Leopold Goenitz—a priest of wind and storm whose serene smile conceals a genocidal purpose. His arrival in '96 heralded the return of an ancient god, and his deadly grace still haunts the series' lore.

Goenitz is calm, composed, and supremely confident. He speaks in a soft, almost gentle tone, but his words are laced with contempt for humanity. He is utterly devoted to Orochi and sees the destruction of humanity as a sacred duty. He is calculating and manipulative, using others to achieve his goals. He shows no mercy to his enemies.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Goenitz' is likely a romanization of a Japanese name; the character's design and role suggest a Germanic or Eastern European flavor, but the exact etymology is not confirmed. 'Orochi' refers to the eight-headed serpent of Japanese mythology, which the character serves.

Pronunciation — GOH-eh-nits (approximate English); Japanese: ゲーニッツ (Gēnittsu)

Aliases — Goenitz, Orochi Priest, Wind Priest, Priest of Orochi

Ownership — Owned by SNK (now SNK Corporation). Created for the King of Fighters series. Based on Japanese myth of Yamata no Orochi, but the character is an original creation.

Powers
Wind Control

Goenitz can generate and manipulate wind at will, using it for offense, defense, and mobility. He can create tornadoes, launch wind blades, and even use wind to lift himself or slow his fall.

Limit: Requires concentration; strong opposing forces may disrupt his control.

Orochi Power

As a priest of Orochi, Goenitz has access to a fraction of Orochi's power, granting him enhanced strength, speed, and durability. He can also sense other Orochi-related entities.

Limit: Power is finite and must be channeled carefully; overuse may cause exhaustion.

Immortality (or extended lifespan)

Goenitz has lived for centuries, serving Orochi across generations. He does not age normally.

Limit: Not explicitly stated; he can be killed in combat.

Supernatural Senses

He can detect the presence of Orochi's blood or power in others, as seen when he identifies Kyo, Iori, and Chizuru as descendants of the three sacred treasures.

Limit: Range and precision unknown.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Goenitz is a tall, slender man with long, silver-white hair and piercing red eyes. He wears a white, flowing robe-like outfit with a high collar, adorned with blue accents and a large cross-like pendant. His attire suggests a priestly or aristocratic bearing. In some appearances, he carries a feather or uses wind-based effects.

Symbols — His cross pendant is a nod to his role as a priest, but it is inverted or stylized to reflect his dark allegiance. Wind swirls and feathers are associated with his attacks. The color scheme of white and blue contrasts with the dark nature of Orochi.

Domains — He operates in the mortal realm, often appearing in stormy weather. He is associated with the wind element, which he controls with precision. He is not confined to a specific location, but is drawn to places of power or where Orochi's influence is strong.

Accounts I
The Orochi ResurgenceThe King of Fighters '96

1996

Goenitz orchestrates the King of Fighters tournament as a ritual to revive Orochi. He manipulates the fighters into gathering, using them as sacrifices. He confronts the three sacred treasures team (Kyo Kusanagi, Iori Yagami, and Chizuru Kagura) and reveals his plan. He is defeated, but his actions set the stage for Orochi's fuller revival in '97. Orochi is not fully revived, but the seal is weakened. Goenitz dies (or disappears) but is later implied to have survived or been resurrected.

Accounts II
The Four Heavenly KingsThe King of Fighters '97

1997

After Goenitz's defeat, other Orochi priests (CYS - Chris, Shermie, Yashiro) emerge to continue the revival. Goenitz is not directly present, but his legacy influences the events. It is revealed that he was one of the Four Heavenly Kings, the highest-ranking servants of Orochi. Orochi is revived but defeated. The three sacred treasures team survives.

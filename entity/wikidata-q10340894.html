The Approach

In the halls of the Galactic Senate, few voices were as steadfast as that of Onaconda Farr, the Rodian Senator from the aquatic world of Rodia. A figure of quiet diplomacy and tragic loyalty, Farr's journey from a hopeful legislator to a pawn in the schemes of the Separatists and the Republic's darkest days is a tale of idealism corrupted by fear and power.

Content advisory. Contains themes of political manipulation, coercion, and character death.
Identity

Onaconda Farr

Pronunciation OH-nuh-KON-duh FAR

Etymology

The name 'Onaconda' may evoke the snake 'anaconda', possibly reflecting his slithery political nature or Rodian physiology.

Cultural Ownership

Rodians, a species from the planet Rodia

Disputed Identifications

None known.

Description

Appearance

Onaconda Farr is a Rodian, characterized by green skin, large black eyes, and a long snout with antennae. He typically wears senatorial robes in muted colors, often with a distinctive hat.

Symbols & Iconography

As a senator, he is associated with the Galactic Republic's insignia and the Rodian homeworld's symbols.

Behaviour & Personality

Farr is initially depicted as an idealistic and loyal senator, but he becomes increasingly desperate and susceptible to manipulation, leading to poor decisions. He is often seen as a moderate who seeks peaceful solutions.

Domains & Habitats

Senate Hall on Coruscant; homeworld Rodia (an aquatic planet).

Version Differences

In the non-canon Legends continuity, Farr had a more prominent role in the Clone Wars multimedia project. In canon, his character is more tragic and less prominent.

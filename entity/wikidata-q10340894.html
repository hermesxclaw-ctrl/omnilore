Who It Is

In the halls of the Galactic Senate, few voices were as steadfast as that of Onaconda Farr, the Rodian Senator from the aquatic world of Rodia. A figure of quiet diplomacy and tragic loyalty, Farr's journey from a hopeful legislator to a pawn in the schemes of the Separatists and the Republic's darkest days is a tale of idealism corrupted by fear and power.

Farr is initially depicted as an idealistic and loyal senator, but he becomes increasingly desperate and susceptible to manipulation, leading to poor decisions. He is often seen as a moderate who seeks peaceful solutions.

Political Influence

As a senator, Farr has the ability to propose legislation, vote, and influence political outcomes in the Galactic Senate.

Limit: Limited by political pressure and threats.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Onaconda Farr is a Rodian, characterized by green skin, large black eyes, and a long snout with antennae. He typically wears senatorial robes in muted colors, often with a distinctive hat.

Symbols — As a senator, he is associated with the Galactic Republic's insignia and the Rodian homeworld's symbols.

Domains — Senate Hall on Coruscant; homeworld Rodia (an aquatic planet).

Accounts I
Senate MurdersStar Wars: The Clone Wars, Season 2, Episode 15

Clone Wars

Senator Farr is poisoned during a Senate session, leading to an investigation that uncovers a conspiracy involving the assassination of Senator Meena Tills and the framing of Senator Amidala. The true culprit is Senator Lott Dod, who sought to destabilize the Republic. Farr survives the poisoning and the conspirators are exposed.

Sphere of InfluenceStar Wars: The Clone Wars, Season 2, Episode 6

Clone Wars

Farr's niece is kidnapped by Separatist agents to pressure him into supporting a military spending bill. Anakin and Ahsoka rescue her, and Farr is grateful. Farr remains loyal to the Republic.

Accounts II
Heroes on Both Sides / Pursuit of PeaceStar Wars: The Clone Wars, Season 4, Episodes 3-4

Clone Wars

Farr, desperate to end the war, secretly negotiates with Separatist Senator Mina Bonteri to propose a peace treaty. He is coerced by Count Dooku to vote for a military spending bill, but ultimately the peace attempt fails. Farr's betrayal is not exposed, but he is deeply conflicted.

Hostage CrisisStar Wars: The Clone Wars, Season 1, Episode 22

Clone Wars

Farr is one of the senators taken hostage by Cad Bane in the Senate Building. He is freed by Anakin and Ahsoka. No lasting effects.

Sabotage / The Jedi Who Knew Too Much / To Catch a Jedi / The Wrong JediStar Wars: The Clone Wars, Season 5, Episodes 2, 6-7, 20

Clone Wars

Farr is manipulated by the criminal syndicate known as the Shadow Collective to vote against the Jedi, leading to Ahsoka Tano's arrest and trial. He later reveals the truth to Anakin, but is killed by the syndicate to silence him. Farr's death helps expose the conspiracy, but he does not live to see justice.

The Approach

The name 'David King' is a common one, but in the world of fiction, it carries the weight of survival, sacrifice, and the fight against the undead. From the zombie-infested streets of Raccoon City to the fog-shrouded realm of the Entity, each David King is a testament to human endurance against overwhelming horror.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of violence, gore, and psychological horror associated with survival horror and multiplayer horror games.
Identity

David King

Pronunciation /ˈdeɪvɪd kɪŋ/

Etymology

A common English given name and surname. 'David' is of Hebrew origin meaning 'beloved', and 'King' is an occupational surname for a person who served a king or acted in a kingly manner.

Cultural Ownership

Multi-franchise; each character is owned by their respective copyright holders (Capcom, Charlie Higson, Behaviour Interactive).

Disputed Identifications

The name is shared by numerous real and fictional individuals. In fiction, the characters are distinct and not connected across franchises.

Description

Appearance

Resident Evil: David King is a middle-aged man with a rugged appearance, often seen in a security guard uniform. Dead by Daylight: David King is a muscular, tattooed man with a tough look, often wearing a leather jacket or athletic wear. The Enemy: David is a teenage boy, described as one of the older kids at the Waitrose supermarket.

Symbols & Iconography

Resident Evil: The Raccoon City Police Department badge. Dead by Daylight: A lion tattoo on his arm, representing his aggressive nature. The Enemy: The makeshift weapons used by the kids.

Behaviour & Personality

Resident Evil: David is a security guard who is calm, resourceful, and protective of others. Dead by Daylight: David is a brawler, aggressive, and hot-headed, but with a strong sense of loyalty to his team. The Enemy: David is a leader, brave, and determined to protect the younger children.

Domains & Habitats

Resident Evil: Raccoon City during the T-virus outbreak. Dead by Daylight: The Entity's realm, specifically the Macmillan Estate and Autohaven Wreckers. The Enemy: A post-apocalyptic London, specifically a Waitrose supermarket and the surrounding streets.

Version Differences

The three characters share only the name. They exist in entirely different universes with no connection.

Who It Is

The name 'David King' is a common one, but in the world of fiction, it carries the weight of survival, sacrifice, and the fight against the undead. From the zombie-infested streets of Raccoon City to the fog-shrouded realm of the Entity, each David King is a testament to human endurance against overwhelming horror.

Resident Evil: David is a security guard who is calm, resourceful, and protective of others. Dead by Daylight: David is a brawler, aggressive, and hot-headed, but with a strong sense of loyalty to his team. The Enemy: David is a leader, brave, and determined to protect the younger children.

Powers
Survival Expertise

Resident Evil: David is skilled in using firearms and melee weapons, as well as crafting items from available materials. He is also adept at performing first aid.

Limit: Limited by stamina and the availability of resources.

Brawling

Dead by Daylight: David is a skilled fighter, able to take hits and dish out damage. His perks reflect his toughness: 'No Mither', 'Dead Hard', and 'We're Gonna Live Forever'.

Limit: Perks have cooldowns and situational requirements.

Leadership

The Enemy: David is a natural leader, able to organize and inspire the other children. He is also skilled in combat and scavenging.

Limit: His leadership is challenged by others, and he is not invincible.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Resident Evil: David King is a middle-aged man with a rugged appearance, often seen in a security guard uniform. Dead by Daylight: David King is a muscular, tattooed man with a tough look, often wearing a leather jacket or athletic wear. The Enemy: David is a teenage boy, described as one of the older kids at the Waitrose supermarket.

Symbols — Resident Evil: The Raccoon City Police Department badge. Dead by Daylight: A lion tattoo on his arm, representing his aggressive nature. The Enemy: The makeshift weapons used by the kids.

Domains — Resident Evil: Raccoon City during the T-virus outbreak. Dead by Daylight: The Entity's realm, specifically the Macmillan Estate and Autohaven Wreckers. The Enemy: A post-apocalyptic London, specifically a Waitrose supermarket and the surrounding streets.

Accounts I
Resident Evil OutbreakResident Evil Outbreak

2003

David King is a security guard trapped in Raccoon City during a zombie outbreak. He must survive and escape the city, working with other survivors. The survivors attempt to escape Raccoon City, but the fate of David is left ambiguous depending on player actions.

Accounts II
The EnemyThe Enemy by Charlie Higson

2009

In a post-apocalyptic London, a group of children led by David King fight to survive against zombie-like adults. They defend their stronghold at a Waitrose supermarket and eventually move to Buckingham Palace. David's leadership is tested, and he ultimately sacrifices himself to save his friends.

Dead by DaylightDead by Daylight

2016-present

David King is a survivor dragged into the Entity's realm, where he must outwit and outrun a Killer. His background is that of a street fighter and rugby player from England. David's fate is to endlessly attempt to escape the Entity's trials.

Who It Is
Form

Yaya is often depicted as a powerful, bearded man with a stern expression. He is sometimes shown wearing a crown or holding a staff, symbolizing his authority and power.

Powers

Creation Yaya is often depicted as a creator god, who brings the world into being through his power and authority. He is said to have created the heavens and the earth, as well as all living things.

Accounts

Yaya creates the world and all living things through his power and authority. He brings forth the heavens and the earth, and populates them with humans and animals.

Yaya sends a flood to destroy the wicked and corrupt people of the world. He saves a few righteous individuals, including Noah and his family, to preserve the human race.

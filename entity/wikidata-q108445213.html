Who It Is

He does not roar or scheme like his fellow Demon Lords — he endures. Dagruel, the Continent's Wrath, is a mountain given flesh, a Titan who has watched empires rise and fall from the same stone throne, and whose single step can redraw a map. In a world of scheming immortals, he is the rare Demon Lord who would rather be left alone — until history forces him to move.

Attested: Dagruel is characterized as laconic, stoic, honorable, and isolationist. Unlike scheming Demon Lords (e.g., Clayman, Guy Crimson), he prefers non-interference and protection of his people. He values strength, endurance, and straightforward combat, and dislikes deception. He is a protective ruler and father, deeply concerned with the survival of the Titan race and his three sons. He is respectful toward fellow Demon Lords who demonstrate power, particularly Guy Crimson and Milim Nava, and later toward Rimuru Tempest after Rimuru's ascension. Powers
Titan Physiology - Colossal Durability and Strength

As a Titan, Dagruel possesses physical strength and durability far beyond human, ogre, or even most Demon Lord peers in pure physical terms. He can withstand continent-shaking blows, endure extreme cold and pressure of his domain, and fight for extended periods without fatigue. His body is described as being as hard as the continent itself.

Limit: Not invulnerable; can be injured by top-tier entities (True Dragons, awakened Demon Lords, Ultimate Skill users). His mass makes him less agile than smaller Demon Lords. Requires significant energy to maintain full combat form.

Earth / Stone Manipulation - Continent's Wrath

Epithet reflects ability to manipulate earth, stone, and terrain on a massive scale. Can harden his skin to stone/metal-like density, cause earthquakes, raise stone barriers, and reshape battlefield topography. In full release, his aura is described as making the land itself respond.

Limit: Range is large but not global; most effective within his domain or where earth is present. Countered by flight, spatial manipulation, and Ultimate Skills that bypass physical defense.

Hardening / Body Enhancement

Can harden his entire body or specific limbs to drastically increase defense and attack power, similar to mineral hardening. This allows him to clash with True Dragon-level physical attacks.

Limit: Hardening reduces speed; prolonged use drains stamina. Can be bypassed by hax abilities (time, space, disintegration).

Longevity and Demon Lord Haki / Aura

As one of the oldest Demon Lords (member of the original Ten Great Demon Lords / Octagram), he possesses immense magicule count, Demon Lord's Haki that can paralyze weaker beings, and millennia of combat experience. His existence stabilizes the Titan race.

Limit: Haki ineffective against equals or those with Ultimate Skill protection. Longevity does not equal omniscience; he is politically isolationist and sometimes uninformed about central continent affairs.

Enhanced Combat Form (Battle Mode)

When serious, Dagruel can enter an enhanced form where his body grows larger, his muscle mass and stone armor increase, and his power approaches that of an awakened Demon Lord at full power. In the Empire War arc, this form is further amplified by external manipulation.

Limit: Amplified form in light novel is later revealed to be partially due to angelic mind-control and power boost by Feldway; not his base power. Without that boost, he is strong but not at the level of Guy or Milim at full power.

Accounts I
The Titan's Genesis and Rise as Demon LordFuse, Tensei shitara Slime Datta Ken Light Novel Vol. 4, 6, 11; supplementary Demon Lord lore booklets

Fictional ancient era within franchise cosmology (thousands of years before main story); published 2016-2019

In the cosmology of the Slime universe, the Titan race was created in the distant past as a durable, earth-aligned race. Dagruel, as their strongest and most enduring member, rose to become one of the first Demon Lords, recognized as one of the Ten Great Demon Lords who have maintained balance for millennia. He claimed the harsh Titan continent as his domain, where his people could survive without competing for fertile lands. He adopted an isolationist policy, rarely intervening in human or monster affairs, which earned him respect as a neutral, stabilizing force among Demon Lords. Established Dagruel as a foundational Demon Lord and ruler of a distinct continent, explaining his later neutrality and the autonomous culture of the Titans. Set up his epithet Continent's Wrath.

The Walpurgis Banquet and the New Demon LordLight Novel Vol. 6; Manga Chapter 70-74; Anime Season 2 Episodes 11-12 (Walpurgis)

Main story timeline, shortly after Rimuru defeats Clayman; published 2017 (LN), aired 2021 (anime)

Following Clayman's defeat, Guy Crimson summons a Walpurgis (Demon Lord banquet) to judge Clayman and consider Rimuru Tempest's claim to Demon Lord status. Dagruel attends as one of the Ten Great Demon Lords. He is largely silent and observant, voting in accordance with Guy's proposal to recognize Rimuru and to reform the group as the Octagram (Eight Star Demon Lords). His presence underscores the legitimacy of the council — his approval signals that even the isolationist Titan acknowledges Rimuru's strength. The Ten Great Demon Lords are dissolved and reformed as the Octagram, with Dagruel as one of the eight. Rimuru is officially recognized, reshaping continental politics. Dagruel returns to his continent without hostility toward Rimuru.

Accounts II
The Sons of Dagruel and the Tempest IncidentLight Novel Vol. 11-12; Manga adaptation (partial)

Post-Octagram period; published 2018-2019

Dagruel's three sons — Dagura, Ryura, and Debura — are introduced as Titan princes who, unlike their father, are brash and eager for glory. They are manipulated by external forces (notably the Rosso family and later angelic agents) into attacking Tempest or challenging Rimuru's allies, believing it will prove Titan superiority. Dagruel himself does not order the attack; he is later shown to be distressed and apologetic, revealing his sons acted without his full consent and were influenced. Rimuru's forces defeat the sons without killing them, and Dagruel's relationship with Tempest is strained but not broken. Highlights Dagruel's role as a father and his lack of direct aggression toward Rimuru. Sets up later revelation that Dagruel's faction is being targeted for manipulation by the Angelic forces. His sons are spared, preserving future alliance potential.

The Empire War — The Titan Continent Under SiegeLight Novel Vol. 14, 19-21 (Empire War arc)

Main story's large-scale war against the Eastern Empire and later against the Angelic army; published 2020-2023

When the Eastern Empire launches a full-scale invasion of the western nations, Dagruel initially maintains neutrality, honoring his isolationist stance. However, Feldway, leader of the Angelic forces and former comrade of Veldanava, exploits Dagruel's sense of honor and his desire to protect his people, placing him under a form of mind-control / angelic domination and enhancing his power. Under this influence, Dagruel is forced to oppose Rimuru's alliance and confront Veldora and later Rimuru's forces on the Titan continent. The battle devastates the landscape, demonstrating his Continent's Wrath power at full, amplified scale. Reveals that Dagruel's antagonism is not voluntary, preserving his honorable characterization. The battle becomes a key front in the Empire/Angelic war, and his eventual liberation becomes a turning point for the alliance. Shows the tragic cost of his strength being weaponized.

Liberation and Alliance with TempestLight Novel Vol. 20-21; Author afterword and interviews

Climax of Empire/Angelic war arc; published 2022-2023

With help from Guy Crimson, Rimuru, and Veldora, the control over Dagruel is broken. Freed from Feldway's influence, Dagruel acknowledges Rimuru's strength and ideals, and formally aligns the Titan continent with Tempest and the Octagram alliance against the Angelic invasion. He pledges to protect his people through cooperation rather than isolation, marking a character shift from isolationist to ally. His sons are also reintegrated and swear loyalty to the new order. Resolves Dagruel's arc from neutral observer to active ally. Secures the Titan continent as part of the western alliance and sets up post-war reconstruction. Reinforces franchise theme that strength should protect rather than dominate.

Dagruel is a mythological Titan from Greek mythology or an ancient folklore figure. — Dagruel is a wholly modern fictional character created by Fuse in 2013-2014 for Tensei shitara Slime Datta Ken. While his race is called 'Titan' as a homage to mythological giants, he has no pre-modern attestation, no folklore, and no connection to Greek Titanes beyond the generic name.

Dagruel's name is spelled 'Dagrule' and he has an Ultimate Skill called 'Continent's Wrath'. — Official Yen Press and Crunchyroll romanization is 'Dagruel' (Japanese ダグリュール). 'Dagrule' is a common fan transliteration variant. 'Continent's Wrath' is an epithet/title, not a skill name; no Ultimate Skill with that name is attested in primary sources.

Dagruel is the strongest Demon Lord physically and cannot be injured. — Dagruel is among the most physically durable Demon Lords, but he is not invincible and is explicitly shown to be injured and controlled by higher-tier beings (Feldway, True Dragons, Guy). His strength is endurance-based, not absolute.

Dagruel is a villain who willingly tried to destroy Tempest. — In current light novel canon, Dagruel never willingly sought to destroy Tempest; his sons acted rashly and he himself was mind-controlled when he opposed Rimuru. His core characterization is honorable and isolationist, and he ends as an ally.

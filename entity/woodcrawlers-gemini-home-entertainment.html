Who It Is

Creatures mutated and controlled by the Iris. Woodcrawlers are humanoid entities that mimic human behavior imperfectly, using a bizarre, jerky locomotion that involves crawling on all fours with their spine inverted. They can contort their bodies to fit into human dwellings through impossibly small spaces and are frequently seen 'hunting' in family homes. Their faces are distorted, partially formed, and their movements are accompanied by a sound like grinding wood.

Form

Humanoid creatures with elongated limbs, spines that bend in the wrong direction, and faces that look like wood grain or bark — hence 'Woodcrawlers.' Their skin has the texture of aged wood, and they move with a distinctive, terrifying gait: belly-up, back arched, crawling on backward-bent limbs. Their vocalizations sound like wood creaking.

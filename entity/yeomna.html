Who It Is
Form

Yeomna is often depicted as a powerful and wise king, with a stern expression and a regal demeanor.

Powers

Judgment Yeomna has the power to judge the souls of the dead and determine their fate based on their actions in life.

Accounts

Yeomna is the king of the underworld and is responsible for judging the souls of the dead. He is said to be fair and impartial in his judgments.

Yeomna is the king of the underworld and is responsible for guiding the souls of the dead through the afterlife.

Who It Is

Erlik is the ruler of the underworld — originally Ulgen's assistant in creation who was cast down for pride. He rides a black bull, commands an army of kormos spirits, and spreads disease and misfortune. Shamans make offerings to appease him and prevent premature claims on souls. Under Buddhism and Islam, he merged with Yama and Iblis.

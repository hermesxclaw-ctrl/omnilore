Who It Is

A humanoid entity appearing as an elderly man in a state of advanced decomposition. SCP-106 can pass through solid matter, leaving behind a corrosive black mucus that rapidly degrades any material. It hunts humans by moving through walls, floors, and ceilings, dragging victims into its 'pocket dimension' — a labyrinth of corroded corridors where it 'plays' with its prey for hours or days before killing them.

Form

An elderly male, approximately 1.8 meters tall, with mottled, decaying flesh stretched tightly over bones. Fingers elongated into claws. Constantly dripping a black, viscous mucus that corrodes all materials. Its grin is wide and constant.

Powers

Intangibility through solid matter

Pocket dimension access and control

Corrosive secretion

Victim abduction to pocket dimension

Predatory patience

Who It Is
Form

The Swamp Thing is a humanoid figure with a green, scaly skin and long hair. He has a muscular build and stands at an average height. His eyes are often depicted as glowing green. The character's appearance has undergone several changes throughout the years, but he is often shown wearing a tattered and worn-out version of his original lab coat.

Powers

Regenerative Healing The Swamp Thing has the ability to heal from injuries at an accelerated rate, regenerating damaged tissue and restoring his physical form.

Plant Control The Swamp Thing has the ability to control and manipulate plant life, using his powers to summon vines, grow new plants, and even control the growth of entire ecosystems.

Enhanced Strength The Swamp Thing possesses enhanced strength, allowing him to lift and move heavy objects, and even take on powerful foes.

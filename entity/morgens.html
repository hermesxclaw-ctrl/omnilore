Who It Is
Form

Morgens are often described as beautiful maidens with golden hair and hypnotic voices, dwelling in underwater palaces or sea caves. They are said to be able to lure sailors and fishermen to their deaths with their enchanting singing and beauty.

Powers

Seductive Singing Morgens are said to have the ability to sing enchanting melodies that can lure men to their deaths. Their singing is said to be hypnotic and irresistible, making it impossible for men to resist their charms.

Accounts

A young sailor is lured to his death by a Morgens who sings an enchanting melody. The sailor is unable to resist the Morgens' charms and is drawn to his doom.

A group of Morgens are said to inhabit the sea and its surrounding areas. They are described as beautiful maidens with golden hair and hypnotic voices, who are able to lure men to their deaths with their enchanting singing and beauty.

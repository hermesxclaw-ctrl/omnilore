Who It Is

In Gnosticism, the Demiurge (Greek: Δημιουργός, 'artisan' or 'craftsman') is a lesser, malevolent, or ignorant deity who created the physical material universe. He is not the true God but the corrupted offspring of Sophia, born from her unauthorized attempt to emanate without her syzygy. In Sethian texts he is called Yaldabaoth (possibly Aramaic: 'child of chaos'), Saklas ('fool'), and Samael ('blind god'), and is depicted as a lion-headed serpent — a theriomorphic ruler who declares 'I am God and there is none beside me,' ignorant of the higher realms above him. The Demiurge creates the material world as an imperfect copy of the Pleroma and imprisons divine sparks (human souls) in material bodies. His servants are the Archons, planetary rulers who enforce the cosmic prison. The gnostic path is to transcend the Demiurge's creation and return to the true God.

Who It Is

Sixth spirit, a Duke commanding 10 legions. Valefor (also Malaphar, Malephar) appears as a lion with a human head — or in some sources a lion with a donkey's head — and is considered 'a good familiar' by thieves. He tempts people to steal and fosters good relationships among thieves until they are caught, at which point his protection fails. Valefor embodies the principle of illicit loyalty: he binds criminals together in mutual trust, but his patronage is strictly conditional and collapses under the weight of discovery. He represents the demonic patron of those who live by cunning and larceny.

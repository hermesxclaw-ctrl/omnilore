Who It Is

Stand beside a midsummer bonfire at dusk, when the flames drop low and the smoke turns to thin grey veils that hang in the still air — and know that, in the Edo-period imagination of Toriyama Sekien, something in that drift might be watching you right back, visible only to the pure of heart. Enenra (煙々羅, 'lightweight-fabric smoke') is the yōkai of the bonfire's afterglow: a being composed of smoke and darkness that rises from the fire and takes a human shape, a liminal presence that is at once demon and god, elemental spirit and transformed dead. It never attacks in its native folklore — it simply waits in the smoke, reserving its true form for those whose hearts are clean. Only in the modern era, pulled through the machinery of Power Rangers villain rosters and Dandadan's grudge-warriors, has this conditional ghost been forced into flesh, given fists, and turned loose to fight.

Enenra mostly resides within bonfires and only manifests/emerges from them, at which point it takes human form. A key condition governs perception: it is said that an enenra can only be seen by the pure of heart — implying that the ordinary, impure, or casual observer might encounter the smoke but fail to perceive the being within it. This makes Enenra less an aggressive predator than a conditional apparition whose visibility is a moral/spiritual test.

Powers
Smoke/Darkness form

Enenra is literally composed of smoke and darkness rather than flesh. It has no fixed solid body and can manifest as drifting gauze-like smoke.

Limit: The smoke-form is intangible and conditional; the being is not described as physically strong, corporeal, or able to grasp/affect the material world through brute force in the source mythology.

Human shape-taking

When Enenra emerges from a bonfire it takes a human shape or form, presenting as (or near) a human figure made of smoke.

Limit: The human shape is still smoke-based — a silhouette, not a solid body. It emerges specifically from fire, implying it cannot sustain that manifest form away from its fire source indefinitely.

Bonfire residency / emergence

Enenra mostly resides in bonfires and emerges when the fire burns — a habitat-and-emergence power tying its existence to ritual and festival fire.

Limit: Dependent on an external fire source; the creature's manifestation is conditionally tied to the presence of burning wood/ashes.

Selective perceptibility (pure-of-heart condition)

Enenra can only be seen by the pure of heart. Ordinary or impure observers may see smoke but not the being, making perception itself a moral/spiritual faculty.

Limit: This is a limiting property as much as a power — it restricts whom the creature is visible to, and implies Enenra cannot or does not reveal itself to the impure.

Death-transformation (becoming an enenra)

A second, rarer class of enenra is stated to be humans who died and were transformed into enenra — a metamorphic afterlife power by which a deceased person becomes a smoke-spirit.

Limit: Rare and unspecified in mechanism; the cause of such a death-transformation is not detailed (e.g. whether it requires burn-death, unfinished business, or a failed grave-rite).

Smoke manipulation / body-to-smoke (franchise expansion)

In modern adaptations Enenra's power is expanded to active, structured smoke-control: the Dandadan Red Baron can 'manipulate smoke and turn his own body into smoke as well'; Power Rangers enemies use dark-mist / teapot-fireball forms.

Limit: These are franchise-invented combat applications, not Edo folklore; they reify the elemental smoke concept into a superpower with offensive/defensive uses.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Enenra is depicted as a wispy, fabric-like figure made of smoke and darkness. In Sekien's woodblock it rises in layered, gauzy tendrils suggesting thin cloth (羅, ra). It can take a human shape when it emerges from fire, but that shape is semi-transparent and intangible — smoke given silhouette rather than solid body. The creature is classically framed as both demonic and divine, a being of darkness and smoke rather than a flesh-and-blood beast.

Symbols — The defining iconographic associations are fire and smoke: bonfires (especially the ritual and festival bonfires of Japanese seasonal practice), rising gauze-like smoke, darkness, and the liminal smoke-tendril silhouette. Sekien's visual shorthand ('SekienEnenra.jpg') is a standard reference image for the yōkai across subsequent collections, reference works, and adaptations. The reduplicated name itself (en-en-ra) functions as aura

Domains — Bonfires are the reported habitat, situating Enenra in the ritual and communal fire contexts of Japanese tradition — festival bonfires, bonfires for the dead (including obon-related lighting), outdoor fires, and hearth-adjacent smoke. It is a liminal, transient being tied to the moment smoke rises, rather than a permanent dweller of a cave, mountain, or body of water.

Accounts I
The Bonfire Manifestation (source-level account)Konjaku Hyakki Shūi, c. 1781 (Sekien); summarized in Wikipedia/Encyclopedic sources

Edo period (17–19th c.), codified c. 1781

Enenra dwells within bonfires. When the fire burns and smoke rises, the enenra emerges, taking a human shape composed of smoke and darkness. It is a being of fire-wisps, gauze-like and intangible, and can only be seen by the pure of heart. The account frames the creature as at once demonic and divine — an elemental presence of darkness and smoke rather than a flesh-and-blood menace. Establishes Enenra's identity as a fire-and-smoke liminal being and grounds the 'visible only to the pure' condition that defines its mythic register.

Accounts II
Two Generations of Enenra: The Born-Spirit and the Transformed DeadKonjaku Hyakki Shūi (as summarized in modern sources)

Edo codification, later folklore absorption

Legend holds that there are two types of enenra. The first and most common are enenra born purely as enenra — intrinsically elemental smoke-beings. The second, rarer type are humans who have died and been transformed into enenra — a ghostly subclass by which death converts a person into a smoke-spirit. This dual origin gives Enenra both a divine/elemental and a mournful/ghostly register. Introduces an afterlife dimension: enenra is not solely an elemental being but can be a transformed human soul, implying it belongs to the yūrei (ghost) spectrum as much as the elemental yōkai spectrum.

Modern gendering of Enenra across franchises (reception layer)Dandadan (manga 2021– , anime 2024– ; Yukinobu Tatsu); Power Rangers Ninja Steel (2017–18) & Power Rangers Samurai (2011–12)

2011–2025

Enenra has been repeatedly re-materialized as a combatant. In Power Rangers Ninja Steel, Toxitea is an Enenra/kettle-themed monster loyal to Madame Odius who fires teapot-shaped fireballs and brews lethal poison. In Power Rangers Samurai, Gigertox is an Enenra-themed Nighlok with stretchable tentacles who, powered up, becomes a giant dark-mist snake monster. In Dandadan, the Red Baron (an assassin of the Black Paladins / Delacroix family) acquires the yōkai power of Enenra (煙々羅), enabling smoke manipulation and turning his body into smoke; Enenra's 'Little Red Riding Hood' power is also consumed in building the 'ultimate yōkai' Sir Vlad Delacroix. Transforms Enenra from a passive, pure-of-heart-gated apparition into an active, physicalized villain/antagonist across toyetic franchises — a dramatic inversion of its source character.

Who It Is

She was thirteen, loved her camera, and thought her biggest problem was keeping her shut-in twin brother from skipping dinner — until the night the world's most dangerous Contractor kicked in her apartment door looking for a meteor, killed her father, and forced her to run with a rifle that shouldn't exist, summoned from a pendant that would eventually break her heart and rewrite who she was.

Canonically upbeat, earnest, and emotionally expressive — intentionally contrasting with the stoic, affectless norm for Contractors and with Hei's hardened demeanor in season one. She is fiercely protective of her twin Shion, whom she believes she must shelter despite his Contractor coldness, and demonstrates strong familial love that Hanazawa identified as her defining trait. Powers
Meteor-Fragment PTRD Manifestation (Materialization)

Suo can instantaneously manifest a Soviet PTRD-41 anti-tank rifle from the meteor fragment pendant she wears. The weapon appears fully formed with its own ammunition (14.5×114mm rounds) each summoning. She can summon, dismiss, and re-summon it at will when fragment is intact. The rifle is treated as a physical object, not an energy construct.

Limit: Requires intact meteor fragment as medium; if fragment breaks, ability ceases (finale fragment shatters, she vanishes). Ammunition is provided per manifestation but weapon wear accumulates — scratches, fouling, and damage persist between summons, so she must clean and maintain it like a normal firearm (Hei instructs her). Recoil is extreme; despite supernatural ability to wield a weapon heavier than herself, she must brace properly and suffers physical strain. Range and function limited to conventional ballistics of PTRD-41; no explosive or anomalous effects beyond summoning. Cannot manifest other weapons.

Enhanced Handling / Superhuman Wielding

Despite her small frame, Suo can lift, carry, aim, and fire the ~17kg PTRD rifle without the crew normally required, and absorb its massive recoil sufficiently to remain combat-effective. This is presented as part of the manifestation ability rather than separate super-strength.

Limit: Not general superhuman strength; she shows normal teenage physicality otherwise (fatigue, injury, need for Hei's self-defense training). Ability only applies to handling the summoned rifle; she cannot lift other heavy objects supernaturally. Still subject to recoil injury if improperly braced.

Contractor Payment: Origami Folding

As with all Contractors, use of power requires a Payment (remuneration). Suo's Payment is folding origami, typically paper cranes or simple figures, performed after each use of the rifle. The act is compulsive and must be completed.

Limit: Payment must be performed to avoid unspecified physiological/psychological cost; delay causes distress. Requires paper and time, making repeated rapid use in combat logistically constrained. Origami itself has no combat utility.

Retained Emotionality / Incomplete Contractor Rationality (Anomalous Trait)

Unlike standard Contractors who become logically driven and lose emotional affect, Suo retains full teenage emotional range, empathy, fear, and moral deliberation even after awakening. She experiences guilt, love, and existential dread, and values implanted memories as authentic.

Limit: Retention of emotions is a narrative trait, not a controllable power; it makes her more vulnerable to trauma, hesitation, and memory loss under Izanami/Yin influence. She still suffers Contractor-like soul/memory erosion when near Izanami in finale.

Accounts I
The Pavlichenko Home Attack — Hei's Raid and the MeteorDarker than Black: Gemini of the Meteor Episodes 1-2

2009 broadcast; diegetic timeline c. 2 years after Hell's Gate incident, set in Vladivostok

13-year-old Suo lives with her father Dr. Mikhail Pavlichenko and shut-in twin Shion, a Contractor, in Vladivostok. She attends middle school, practices photography, and worries about Shion's isolation. Her ordinary life fractures when assassin Hei (BK-201) raids the apartment seeking the meteor fragment Shion hides. Hei kills Mikhail after confusing Suo for Shion, as Section 3 (Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications) simultaneously assaults the building seeking Shion. Suo escapes with the meteor pendant, driven by grief and desire for revenge, and is drawn into the covert war between Contractors and intelligence agencies. Death of Mikhail, separation from Shion who flees separately, Suo becomes fugitive under Hei's reluctant protection. Establishes meteor fragment as MacGuffin and Suo's transition from civilian to participant. Introduces core theme: child thrust into adult covert war.

Awakening — Tanya, Nika, and the First ShotDarker than Black: Gemini of the Meteor Episodes 2-3

2009; diegetic days after Ep.1

At school, Suo witnesses her classmates Tanya Akulova and Nika Lobanov's romance. During a Section 3 operation, Tanya manifests as a Contractor (insect-control / aerokinetic ability depending on translation) and loses emotions, then kills Nika when he confronts her. Suo, hiding with the meteor pendant, undergoes mental shock witnessing the murder. In that trauma she awakens as a Contractor herself, manifesting the PTRD rifle for the first time and, with unseen aid from Shion, kills Tanya in self-defense. She collapses, later realizing she folded origami as Payment without understanding why. Suo becomes a Contractor, violating her own wish not to kill. Establishes her Payment (origami) and weapon wear mechanic. Hei recognizes her potential and begins training her. Shion's remote intervention hints at his continued protection and replica link.

Road with Hei and July — Training and Found FamilyDarker than Black: Gemini of the Meteor Episodes 4-7

2009; diegetic weeks of travel across Russia toward Japan

Suo, Hei, and the Doll July (a young boy Doll who had been with Suo's group, now carrying Mao's consciousness in his cat form at times) travel together evading Section 3 and other Contractor factions. Hei, initially cold and using Suo as bait for Shion/meteor, gradually teaches her self-defense, firearm maintenance, and survival, forming a mentor-student bond likened by critics to Léon: The Professional. Suo struggles with her Contractor identity, refusing to kill except in defense, and notices the specter of Yin following Hei. July provides emotional anchor; Mao (in cat) comments on Suo retaining emotions. The journey emphasizes Suo's adaptation to the series' dark world despite her bright personality. Suo gains competence and agency, relationship with Hei deepens from hatred to trust. Establishes found-family dynamic that critics praised. Sets up later existential reveal by contrasting her retained humanity with typical Doll/Contractor affectlessness.

Accounts II
The Copy Revealed — Meeting AsakoDarker than Black: Gemini of the Meteor Episodes 7-9

2009; diegetic climax of travel arc

Suo encounters her mother Asako Pavlichenko, who had been separated from the family. Asako is terrified upon seeing Suo, revealing that the original Suo Pavlichenko died years earlier in an explosion (linked to Shion's powers and the meteor incident). The Suo the audience has followed is a replica/copy created by Shion's Contractor ability — which allows creation of copies/replicas — implanted with false memories of a normal childhood to give her a happy life. Shion created her because he could not save the original. Suo experiences profound identity crisis, questioning whether her memories and feelings are real. Hei, unlike Asako, accepts her as she is, affirming her existence, which allows Suo to reclaim her identity and feelings; Mao notes the anomaly of a Contractor who loves. Reframes entire series: protagonist is artificial being with implanted memories. Explains why Suo retains emotions and why Shion is so protective. Suo chooses to treasure implanted memories as real, strengthening her resolve. Critical turning point praised for emotional weight, though UK Anime Network found

Reunion with Shion and the Izanami CrisisDarker than Black: Gemini of the Meteor Episodes 10-11

2009; finale arc

Suo reunites with Shion, who is frail and dying due to overuse of his copy ability and meteor-related powers. Shion explains he implanted her memories to let her live normally and apologizes. Meanwhile, Yin — the Doll who has evolved into Izanami, an entity absorbing souls — awakens fully, having taken October and Shion's souls among others, and begins erasing memories and souls across the field. Suo, losing memories under Izanami's influence, is conflicted about killing Yin, whom Hei loves. Shion entrusts Suo with his final wish and the meteor's power. Hei stops Suo from sacrificing herself prematurely and comforts her as her pendant cracks. Shion's death (soul taken by Izanami), Suo's pendant breaks, leading to her apparent death. Resolves twin arc and sets up metaphysical conclusion. Demonstrates Suo's growth: she chooses to act to protect others despite knowing her nature as a copy.

The Broken Pendant and the World Without Contractors — EpilogueDarker than Black: Gemini of the Meteor Episode 12 (final scene) + Official Fanbook epilogue notes

2009

After the meteor fragment shatters, Suo appears to die in Hei's arms as she loses memories. A post-credits coda reveals she has been transported to an alternate world/timeline where Contractors, Dolls, and Gates never existed — including no Shion — where she lives a normal life as a middle-school girl with no memory of Hei, Shion, or her Contractor life. She pauses, feeling she has lost something important but unable to recall what, then continues to school. The scene mirrors her initial desire for normalcy and Shion's wish for her to live happily, at cost of erasure. Provides bittersweet closure: Suo survives but without identity and bonds formed. Critically divisive — some reviewers (UK Anime Network) found closure incomplete for Suo's arc and Hei relationship, while others saw it as thematically fitting. Leaves open whether memory loss is permanent or if fragment remains.

Suo is a magical girl like Madoka or Utena, with magic powers and mascot pet. — Suo is classified in-universe as a Contractor, a human with supernatural ability tied to the Gates/meteor, with a compulsory Payment (origami) and physical weapon maintenance. Her cat companion is Mao, a Contractor consciousness in an animal, not a mascot. Critics (Fandom Post, About.com) noted superficial resemblance but emphasized she is not a magical girl and her story is science-fiction thriller, not magical girl genre.

Suo is Shion's biological twin sister who naturally became a Contractor. — The protagonist Suo is a replica/copy of the original Suo who died years earlier in an explosion. Shion created her with his Contractor copy ability and implanted false memories of a normal childhood. She is not biologically the original child, though she possesses her appearance and implanted memories.

Suo loses emotions and becomes cold like other Contractors after awakening. — Unlike typical Contractors who become hyper-rational and affectless, Suo retains full teenage emotions, empathy, and moral hesitation throughout. Mao explicitly finds this anomalous. Her retention is central to theme and is explained via replica nature.

Suo dies permanently when her pendant breaks in the finale. — She appears to die as fragment shatters and memories fade, but coda shows her alive in alternate world without Contractors, without memories of prior life. Whether this is survival, transfer, or new creation is ambiguous, but she is not simply dead in narrative terms.

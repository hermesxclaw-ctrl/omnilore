SCP-2317 — A Door to Another World

Who It Is
Form

SCP-2317 is a wooden door and frame originally constructed as a basement door for a 19th-century Massachusetts brownstone. The door is approximately 6 feet tall and 3 feet wide, with a simple wooden frame and a metal doorknob. The door's surface is worn and weathered, with visible signs of age and use.

Powers

Interdimensional Portal SCP-2317 is capable of opening a portal to alternate realities and dimensions, allowing individuals to travel through time and space.

Incident Log

A team of researchers from the SCP Foundation discovered SCP-2317 in a 19th-century Massachusetts brownstone. The entity was initially thought to be a simple door, but it soon became clear that it was something much more complex and anomalous.

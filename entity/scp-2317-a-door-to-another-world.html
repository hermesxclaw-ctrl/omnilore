Who It Is

An interdimensional doorway beneath the Massachusetts wilderness leading to a parallel dimension containing an entity known as the 'Devourer of Worlds' — a colossal being (designated SCP-2317-K) bound by seven chains. Six chains have already broken. The Foundation maintains an elaborate ritual (Procedure 220-Calabasas) that is actually a placebo; the true containment method is unknown, and the final chain may break at any time.

Form

SCP-2317-K is described as a gargantuan entity, approximately 200 meters tall, with a body composed of an unknown black crystalline substance, seven limbs, and a head resembling a crown of horns. Its arrival is prophesied to coincide with the 'Devouring of the World.'

Who It Is

A trans-temporal phenomenon affecting locations associated with the death of children. When a living person visits such a location during significant anniversaries, SCP-2614 manifests as a temporal echo — the dead child appears briefly and attempts to interact with the living. The apparitions are intelligent, aware they are dead, and often desperately lonely. They can answer questions about their lives and deaths but cannot be 'saved' or permanently brought forward in time.

Form

Specters of deceased children, appearing exactly as they did in life but translucent and temporally unstable. They 'flicker' between ages. Their voices sound distant, as if heard through water.

Who It Is

An extraterrestrial Pokemon that fell to Earth 20,000 years ago, its body a skeletal, dragon-like form crackling with endless energy. In its Eternamax form, it becomes a colossal, writhing mass of glowing red energy tendrils, its head ten stories above the ground. It is the source of the Dynamax phenomenon in the Galar region.

The Approach

In the heart of the Galar region, a creature of cosmic origin awakens, its form stretching across the sky like a dark omen. Eternatus, the embodiment of the Dynamax phenomenon, reveals its true power in the Eternamax form—a sight that challenges the very concept of a Pokémon. As the final boss of Pokémon Sword and Shield, this legendary beast tests the limits of trainers and legends alike.

Identity

Eternatus (Eternamax Form)

Pronunciation ee-TER-nah-tus

Known As

Eternamax EternatusEternamax Form

Etymology

Eternatus is derived from 'eternal' and the Latin suffix '-atus', suggesting eternal existence. The Eternamax form name combines 'eternal' with 'max', referencing the Dynamax phenomenon.

Cultural Ownership

The Pokémon franchise, owned by Nintendo, Game Freak, and The Pokémon Company. Eternatus is a fictional creature from the Galar region.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans initially confused Eternatus with other legendary Pokémon due to its unique typing and design, but official sources clearly distinguish it.

Description

Appearance

Eternatus is a massive, skeletal dragon-like Pokémon with a dark purple body and glowing red core. Its Eternamax form is even more colossal, with an elongated, serpentine body that coils through the sky. It has multiple red glowing nodes along its body and a large central core. The form is characterized by its overwhelming presence and ominous aura.

Symbols & Iconography

Eternatus is associated with the Dynamax phenomenon, representing the ultimate power of the Darkest Day. Its Eternamax form symbolizes the peak of its power, often depicted with swirling energy and dark clouds.

Behaviour & Personality

Eternatus is an alien-like creature that seeks to absorb energy to sustain itself. In its Eternamax form, it becomes more aggressive and destructive, lashing out at anything in its path. It is not inherently malevolent but is driven by its instinct to feed on energy.

Domains & Habitats

Eternatus is encountered in the Energy Plant within the Galar region, specifically in the Slumbering Weald and later at the Energy Plant during the climax of the main story. Its Eternamax form is used in battle against the player, where it is contained within a special energy field.

Version Differences

The Eternamax form is a temporary transformation triggered by Eternatus's absorption of Wishing Stars. It is not a permanent form and reverts to its base form after the battle. In Pokémon Sword and Shield, the Eternamax form is used in the story battle, but it is not available to the player.

Stories & Myths

The Darkest Day

Source Pokémon Sword and Shield main storyCulture Pokémon franchisePeriod Contemporary (2019)

Summary

Eternatus arrived on Earth in a meteor 20,000 years ago, causing the Darkest Day. It was defeated by the legendary heroes Zacian and Zamazenta. In the present day, Chairman Rose attempts to use Eternatus to solve an energy crisis, but the plan fails, and Eternatus rampages in its Eternamax form. The player and Hop, with the help of Zacian and Zamazenta, defeat Eternatus and capture it.

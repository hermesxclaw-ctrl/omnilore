Who It Is

In the heart of the Galar region, a creature of cosmic origin awakens, its form stretching across the sky like a dark omen. Eternatus, the embodiment of the Dynamax phenomenon, reveals its true power in the Eternamax form—a sight that challenges the very concept of a Pokémon. As the final boss of Pokémon Sword and Shield, this legendary beast tests the limits of trainers and legends alike.

Eternatus is an alien-like creature that seeks to absorb energy to sustain itself. In its Eternamax form, it becomes more aggressive and destructive, lashing out at anything in its path. It is not inherently malevolent but is driven by its instinct to feed on energy.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Eternatus is a massive, skeletal dragon-like Pokémon with a dark purple body and glowing red core. Its Eternamax form is even more colossal, with an elongated, serpentine body that coils through the sky. It has multiple red glowing nodes along its body and a large central core. The form is characterized by its overwhelming presence and ominous aura.

Symbols — Eternatus is associated with the Dynamax phenomenon, representing the ultimate power of the Darkest Day. Its Eternamax form symbolizes the peak of its power, often depicted with swirling energy and dark clouds.

Domains — Eternatus is encountered in the Energy Plant within the Galar region, specifically in the Slumbering Weald and later at the Energy Plant during the climax of the main story. Its Eternamax form is used in battle against the player, where it is contained within a special energy field.

Contemporary (2019)

Eternatus arrived on Earth in a meteor 20,000 years ago, causing the Darkest Day. It was defeated by the legendary heroes Zacian and Zamazenta. In the present day, Chairman Rose attempts to use Eternatus to solve an energy crisis, but the plan fails, and Eternatus rampages in its Eternamax form. The player and Hop, with the help of Zacian and Zamazenta, defeat Eternatus and capture it. Eternatus is captured and contained, preventing further disasters. The Galar region continues its Pokémon League.

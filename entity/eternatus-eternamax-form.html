Who It Is

An extraterrestrial Pokemon that fell to Earth 20,000 years ago, its body a skeletal, dragon-like form crackling with endless energy. In its Eternamax form, it becomes a colossal, writhing mass of glowing red energy tendrils, its head ten stories above the ground. It is the source of the Dynamax phenomenon in the Galar region.

Who It Is
Form

The Soul of Cinder is a massive, humanoid figure with a body composed of numerous souls, which are represented as glowing orbs. It has a large, imposing presence and is surrounded by a halo of flames.

Powers

Flame Manipulation The Soul of Cinder has the ability to manipulate flames, which it uses to attack the player and protect the First Flame.

Accounts

The Soul of Cinder is created from the souls of the Lords of Cinder, who are the rulers of the various kingdoms in the Dark Souls universe. It is tasked with protecting the First Flame and the world of Lothric.

The player must battle the Soul of Cinder in order to defeat it and extinguish the First Flame. The battle is a challenging and intense experience, requiring the player to use all of their skills and abilities.

Who It Is

A memetic weapon created by an unknown hostile organization designed to erase specific memories from the human collective unconscious. SCP-3002 targets Foundation personnel, deleting their memories of procedures, SCPs, and eventually their own identities. The infected become 'vectors,' spreading the memory-erasing effect to others through conversation. The Foundation's Antimemetics Division is fighting a losing battle against it — entire sites have been 'silenced,' with personnel walking around unable to remember why they're there.

Form

No physical form. Manifests as conversational 'gaps' — moments where a word or concept simply doesn't exist anymore. Infected individuals speak in sentences that trail off, as if the ideas they were reaching for have been surgically removed from reality.

Powers

Targeted memory deletion

Communicable via conversation

Conceptual assassination

Antimemetic propagation

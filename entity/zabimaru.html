Who It Is

Renji Abarai's zanpakutō with a baboon spirit. Shikai transforms into a segmented-blade whip that extends and strikes at range. True Shikai (after learning the name) is thicker and more controlled. Bankai, Sō'ō Zabimaru, creates a massive skeletal baboon with the sword as its spine, granting devastating physical attacks and energy blasts.

Powers

Segmented whip blade, skeletal baboon summon in Bankai

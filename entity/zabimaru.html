Who It Is

In the annals of Soul Society's spectral armory, Zabimaru stands as a testament to the untamed, primal will that can be forged into a blade. This zanpakutō, whose name translates roughly to 'Snake Tail', is not merely a weapon but a living embodiment of the beast within, a serpent of immense power and fury that has served its wielder, Renji Abarai, through countless battles. Its story is one of mastery over the wild, a dance between human and beast that defines the very essence of a Soul Reaper's bond with their sword.

Zabimaru's spirit is depicted as a large, baboon-like creature with a snake's tail, often shown with a fierce and aggressive demeanor. In the Zanpakutō Unknown Tales arc, the spirit is portrayed as a proud and wild beast that respects strength and despises weakness. It has a tendency to challenge its wielder and tests Renji's resolve. The spirit embodies untamed power that must be controlled.

Powers
Shikai: Hikotsu Taiho (Baboon's Tail Cannon)

In shikai, Zabimaru can extend its segmented blade to strike from a distance, similar to a whip. Renji can also use the technique 'Hikotsu Taiho' to fire the blade segments as a projectile, which then reconstitute.

Limit: The blade's segments can be broken or blocked by strong opponents. Extending the blade leaves Renji open to counterattacks.

Bankai: Hihiō Zabimaru (Baboon King, Snake King)

The bankai transforms Zabimaru into a colossal skeletal serpent with a baboon skull head. It can be used as a massive whip-like weapon, capable of crushing and slicing enemies. Renji can also use the technique 'Zaga Teppō' to fire bone projectiles.

Limit: The bankai is extremely heavy and difficult to control, especially in its initial stages. It consumes a large amount of reiatsu. The form is vulnerable to being broken and can be rendered useless if the bones are shattered.

Zaga Teppō (Snake Gun)

A technique used in bankai where Renji fires the snake's head as a projectile, which then returns or can be used to entangle.

Limit: Requires precise control; if the head is destroyed, the bankai becomes less effective.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In its sealed form, Zabimaru appears as a standard katana with a rectangular hilt and a red scabbard. In shikai, the blade transforms into a segmented, whip-like sword with multiple joints, resembling a serpent's spine or a baboon's tail. The segments are jagged and can extend to considerable length. In bankai, Zabimaru becomes a massive, skeletal serpentine creature with a baboon-like head and a snake's body, composed of giant bones. The bankai

Symbols — The zanpakutō's symbolism is rooted in the baboon and snake, both animals with significant meaning in Japanese culture. The baboon is associated with strength, cunning, and the warrior spirit, while the snake symbolizes rebirth, transformation, and danger. The bankai's skeletal form evokes death and the underworld, reflecting the destructive power of the weapon.

Domains — As a zanpakutō, Zabimaru resides within Renji's soul and is manifested in the inner world, a desolate landscape with giant bones and a crimson sky, reflecting the beast's nature. It has no real-world habitat.

Accounts I
First Encounter with IchigoBleach manga chapters 50-53

2001-2002

Renji Abarai, along with Rukia's execution squad, confronts Ichigo Kurosaki in the human world. Renji uses Zabimaru's shikai to fight Ichigo, demonstrating its whip-like abilities. Ichigo, with his own zanpakutō, manages to counter and eventually win, forcing Renji to retreat. This battle establishes Renji as a formidable opponent and introduces Zabimaru's powers to the audience.

Bankai Training and Soul Society ArcBleach manga chapters 116-118

2003

During the Soul Society arc, Renji trains to achieve bankai in a short time using the 'Tenshintai' method. He achieves bankai and uses it against Byakuya Kuchiki, his captain and rival. The bankai, Hihiō Zabimaru, is initially unwieldy, and Byakuya easily defeats it, but the battle showcases Renji's growth. Renji's defeat leads him to further train and eventually master his bankai. The battle also deepens the conflict between Renji and Byakuya.

Accounts II
Zanpakutō Unknown Tales Arc (Anime-Only)Bleach anime episodes 230-265

2009

In this filler arc, Zanpakutō spirits are manifested in the real world. Zabimaru's spirit appears as a large baboon with a snake tail, initially hostile and challenging Renji. Renji must prove his worth to regain control over his zanpakutō. The spirit demonstrates immense power and a wild personality. Renji eventually reconciles with Zabimaru, strengthening their bond.

Thousand-Year Blood War ArcBleach manga chapters 480-686

2012-2016

During the final arc, Renji trains in the Royal Palace and achieves a new, more powerful form of his bankai, which is revealed to be the true form of Zabimaru. This form is more compact and retains the baboon and snake motifs but with greater control and power. Renji uses it against the Sternritter, the Quincy forces. The new bankai allows Renji to fight on par with elite enemies and proves his growth as a Soul Reaper.

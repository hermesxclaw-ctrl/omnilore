Who It Is

In the sprawling, candy-crusted empire of Whole Cake Island, where even the trees are made of sweets and loyalty is measured in wedding cakes, Charlotte Oven stands as one of Big Mom's most formidable and feared sons—a man whose very touch can boil the sea itself.

Oven is fiercely loyal to his mother, Big Mom, and is known for his aggressive and hot-tempered personality. He is quick to use violence to enforce his family's will and shows no mercy to enemies. He is also protective of his younger siblings, particularly when they are threatened.

Powers
Heat Fruit (Netsu Netsu no Mi)

Oven ate the Paramecia-type Heat Fruit, which allows him to generate and control intense heat from his body. He can heat his body to extreme temperatures, making his punches and touches capable of causing severe burns and even boiling seawater. He can also emit heat waves that can melt metal and scorch the ground.

Limit: He cannot create fire itself, only heat. The exact limits of his heat generation are not fully defined.

Heat-based combat techniques

Oven uses his heat in combat, such as the 'Heat Punch' and 'Heat Wave' attacks. He can also heat his body to protect himself from cold or to melt ice.

Limit: Requires physical contact or proximity to affect targets.

Haki (Busoshoku and Kenbunshoku)

Oven is capable of using Armament Haki (Busoshoku Haki) to harden his body and enhance his attacks, and Observation Haki (Kenbunshoku Haki) to sense the presence and intentions of others.

Limit: Haki usage is limited by his stamina and concentration.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Charlotte Oven is a large, muscular man with short, spiky orange hair and a prominent scar over his left eye. He wears a sleeveless orange shirt, black pants, and a captain's coat. His most distinctive feature is his heat-based powers, which cause his body to emit visible heat waves when activated.

Symbols — His name 'Oven' and heat-based powers are symbolic of his role as a 'cook' in the Charlotte family's operations, and his appearance often includes flames or heat effects.

Domains — He is primarily based on Whole Cake Island, the capital of the Big Mom Pirates' territory in the New World. He also operates in various locations within the Totto Land archipelago.

Accounts I
Whole Cake Island ArcOne Piece manga and anime

2016-2018

Oven is introduced as one of Big Mom's sons and a key enforcer during the wedding of Charlotte Pudding and Sanji. He is tasked with guarding the island and eliminating intruders. He participates in the pursuit of the Straw Hat crew, engaging in battles with Pedro, Pekoms, and others. He is present at the Tea Party and the subsequent chaos caused by the Straw Hats' escape. Oven attempts to stop the Straw Hats' ship, the Thousand Sunny, by boiling the sea, but is thwarted by the combined efforts of the crew. He is later defeated by a combination of attacks from the Straw Hats and their allies. Oven's failure to stop the Straw Hats contributes to Big Mom's rage and the eventual fall of the Big Mom Pirates' dominance in the area. He survives the arc and continues to serve his mother.

Accounts II
Wano Country Arc (brief appearance)One Piece manga and anime

2019-2022

Oven does not appear in the Wano Country arc, but he is mentioned as being part of Big Mom's forces that are preparing for a potential confrontation with the Straw Hats and their allies. None directly.

The Approach

The name Kenji carries a thousand faces: a samurai's blade in a medieval era, a chef's precision in a modern kitchen, a prosecutor's sharp logic in a courtroom, and a martial artist's journey in a manga panel. Each Kenji is a world unto itself, yet all share a thread of determination and craft.

Identity

Kenji

Pronunciation KEN-jee (English approximation); in Japanese: [keɲdʑi]

Etymology

Kenji is a Japanese given name composed of kanji such as 健 (ken, 'healthy, strong') and 二 (ji, 'two') or 次 (ji, 'next'), among others. The name can also be a surname or part of compound names.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese

Disputed Identifications

The name Kenji is shared by multiple distinct entities; no single entity holds exclusive claim. Confusion may arise between the era and the given name, or between fictional characters.

Description

Appearance

Varies by subject: for the given name, no physical description; for the manga character, a martial artist; for the game character, a prosecutor; for the chef, a real person.

Symbols & Iconography

The name itself is a symbol of Japanese identity; for the era, it is associated with the Kamakura period; for the manga, martial arts; for the game, justice and investigation.

Behaviour & Personality

As a given name, it is borne by many individuals with diverse personalities. Fictional Kenjis often exhibit traits like discipline, intelligence, and determination.

Domains & Habitats

Japan, historical and modern; various fictional worlds.

Version Differences

Each Kenji is distinct; the era is a historical period, the manga is a fictional narrative, the game is a spin-off, the chef is a contemporary person, and the song is a musical piece.

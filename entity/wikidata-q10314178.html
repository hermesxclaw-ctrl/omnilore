Who It Is

The name Kenji carries a thousand faces: a samurai's blade in a medieval era, a chef's precision in a modern kitchen, a prosecutor's sharp logic in a courtroom, and a martial artist's journey in a manga panel. Each Kenji is a world unto itself, yet all share a thread of determination and craft.

As a given name, it is borne by many individuals with diverse personalities. Fictional Kenjis often exhibit traits like discipline, intelligence, and determination.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Kenji is a Japanese given name composed of kanji such as 健 (ken, 'healthy, strong') and 二 (ji, 'two') or 次 (ji, 'next'), among others. The name can also be a surname or part of compound names.

Pronunciation — KEN-jee (English approximation); in Japanese: [keɲdʑi]

Aliases — Kenji

Symbols — The name itself is a symbol of Japanese identity; for the era, it is associated with the Kamakura period; for the manga, martial arts; for the game, justice and investigation.

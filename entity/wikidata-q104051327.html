Who It Is

Katsuki Bakugo is a walking explosion, both literally and figuratively—a boy whose nitroglycerin-like sweat ignites into power, and whose pride once burned bridges with everyone around him. But beneath the blast and bravado lies one of modern manga's most compelling arcs: a journey from bully to hero, from isolation to partnership, and from self-hatred to self-acceptance. His story challenges the very definition of what it means to be a hero, asking whether raw power can ever be tempered into genuine goodness.

Katsuki is initially arrogant, aggressive, and hot-tempered, with a strong belief in his own superiority. He is highly competitive and driven to be the best, but his pride often leads to conflict. Over the course of the series, he undergoes significant character development, learning humility, teamwork, and the value of saving others. He becomes more cooperative and protective, though he retains his blunt and sometimes abrasive demeanor. His underlying insecurity and inferiority complex, particularly towards Izuku Midoriya, are gradually revealed and addressed.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname Bakugo (爆豪) literally means 'explosion greatness' or 'explosive power.' The given name Katsuki (勝己) can be read as 'victory self' or 'self-victory,' reflecting his competitive nature. The nickname 'Kacchan' is a diminutive form of his given name. Powers
Explosion Quirk

Bakugo's Quirk allows him to secrete a nitroglycerin-like substance from the palms of his hands. He can detonate this substance at will, creating powerful explosions. He can control the size and intensity of the blasts.

Limit: Overuse causes pain and fatigue in his arms and shoulders. The explosions produce a strong recoil, which he must manage. He cannot produce explosions if his palms are covered or if he is unable to sweat.

AP Shot

A technique where Bakugo concentrates his explosions into a small, piercing blast, similar to a bullet. This allows for long-range attacks with high precision.

Limit: Requires significant control and can be taxing on his arms.

Howitzer Impact

A powerful, spinning attack that creates a massive explosion, often used as a finisher. Bakugo uses his explosions to rotate his body, building up momentum and force.

Limit: Leaves him vulnerable during the spin-up and consumes a lot of his stamina.

Stun Grenade

Bakugo creates a blinding flash of light by detonating his sweat in a controlled manner, temporarily blinding enemies.

Limit: Only effective for a short time and requires line of sight.

Propulsion

Bakugo uses his explosions to propel himself through the air, granting him high-speed mobility and the ability to change direction mid-flight.

Limit: Requires careful control to avoid losing balance; overuse can exhaust him.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Katsuki Bakugo is a teenage boy with spiky, ash-blond hair and sharp, crimson red eyes. He has a lean, athletic build, reflecting his intense physical training. His typical hero costume consists of a black and orange armored suit with grenade-like gauntlets on his wrists, which store his sweat for powerful explosions. He often wears a scowl or a confident smirk, and his demeanor is aggressive and confrontational.

Symbols — His hero name and costume incorporate explosive imagery, such as grenades and explosions. The 'X' shape on his gauntlets and chest symbolizes his explosive power and his rebellious nature. His catchphrase 'Die!' (死ね, shine) is iconic but often used in a hyperbolic, non-literal manner.

Domains — Bakugo primarily operates in Japan, particularly in the fictional setting of U.A. High School and the surrounding cities. He is often seen in training grounds, battlefields, and urban environments. His home life is depicted briefly, showing a supportive but strict mother and a more relaxed father.

Accounts I
The Bully and the QuirklessMy Hero Academia, Chapters 1-4

Modern era

As a child, Katsuki Bakugo bullied Izuku Midoriya, a Quirkless boy, giving him the derogatory nickname 'Deku.' Bakugo's arrogance and belief in his own superiority led him to look down on Midoriya, despite their childhood friendship. This bullying continued into their middle school years, shaping their initial dynamic. Midoriya develops a fear and resentment of Bakugo, but also a determination to become a hero despite being Quirkless. Bakugo's actions set the stage for their complex rivalry.

The Battle TrialMy Hero Academia, Chapters 8-9

Modern era

During a training exercise at U.A. High School, Bakugo faces off against Midoriya, who has just inherited the One For All Quirk. Midoriya, despite his inexperience, manages to win the battle by using his Quirk in an unexpected way, shocking Bakugo and challenging his belief in his own superiority. This defeat marks a turning point in Bakugo's perception of Midoriya, beginning his slow realization that Midoriya is not as weak as he thought.

The Kidnapping and All Might's FallMy Hero Academia, Chapters 82-96

Modern era

Bakugo is kidnapped by the League of Villains, who attempt to recruit him. All Might, his childhood hero, comes to his rescue, but in the ensuing battle, All Might uses his last embers of power and loses his Quirk, forcing him to retire. Bakugo is filled with guilt, believing he caused All Might's downfall. Bakugo's guilt drives him to become even more determined to grow stronger and to atone. This event also deepens his understanding of heroism.

Accounts II
The Duel and the TruthMy Hero Academia, Chapters 118-121

Modern era

After failing the Provisional Hero Licensing Exam due to his guilt, Bakugo confronts Midoriya in a private battle. He reveals that he has figured out the truth about One For All and challenges Midoriya to explain why he was chosen. All Might intervenes, revealing the full history of One For All and All For One to Bakugo, who becomes one of the few to know the secret. Bakugo and Midoriya begin to forge a genuine rivalry and friendship. Bakugo agrees to keep the secret, and they start training together.

The ApologyMy Hero Academia, Chapters 317-319

Modern era

During the Dark Deku arc, when Midoriya leaves U.A. to protect his friends, Bakugo and the class track him down. Bakugo delivers a heartfelt apology to Midoriya, admitting that his bullying was driven by jealousy and an inferiority complex. He acknowledges Midoriya's worthiness and asks for forgiveness. This apology is a major turning point in their relationship, leading to a deeper friendship and partnership.

The Final WarMy Hero Academia, Chapters 380-423

Modern era

In the final battle against All For One and Tomura Shigaraki, Bakugo is fatally wounded but is revived by Edgeshot's technique. He returns to the fight, using his explosions to aid Izuku in defeating the villains. He plays a crucial role in killing All For One and allowing Izuku to land the final blow. Bakugo survives and continues to be a hero, eventually helping fund a powered suit for Izuku.

Who It Is
Form

Oisín is often depicted as a young, handsome man with a strong build and a fierce warrior spirit. He is said to be the son of Fionn mac Cumhaill, the leader of the Fianna, and is therefore a demigod in Irish mythology.

Powers

Poetic Inspiration Oisín is said to have the power of poetic inspiration, which allows him to create beautiful and powerful poetry that can move the hearts of those who hear it.

Accounts

Oisín falls in love with Niamh, the daughter of the King of Tír na n-Óg, and is taken to the mystical realm to live with her. However, when Oisín returns to the mortal world, he finds that centuries have passed and that his friends and family are all gone.

Oisín fights alongside his father and the Fianna in the Battle of Moytura, a great battle between the forces of good and evil.

Scholarly Debates
Oisín's Age
Oisín is said to be a young man in the story of his love affair with Niamh. ⇌ However, in the story of the Battle of Moytura, Oisín is depicted as a seasoned warrior, suggesting that he may be older than initially thought.
What We Don't Know

There are several gaps in the story of Oisín, including the exact nature of his relationship with Niamh and the circumstances of his return to the mortal world.

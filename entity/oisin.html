Three years in the Land of Youth — three hundred in the world of men.

Who It Is

Oisín, son of Fionn and a deer-woman, was the greatest poet of the Fianna. He went to Tír na nÓg with fairy princess Niamh for what felt like three years, but three hundred had passed. Falling from his horse, he instantly aged. His dialogues with St. Patrick form the Acallam na Senórach.

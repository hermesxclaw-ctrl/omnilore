Who It Is
Form

Count Dracula is often depicted as a tall, thin, and pale figure with piercing eyes and sharp fangs. He is typically dressed in a long, black cape and a white shirt with a high collar.

Powers

Superhuman Strength Count Dracula possesses incredible physical strength, allowing him to overpower and kill his victims with ease.

Vampiric Powers Count Dracula has the ability to transform into a bat, wolf, or mist, and to hypnotize and control the minds of others.

Immortality Count Dracula is immortal, unable to die from natural causes or injury.

Accounts

The novel tells the story of Count Dracula's move from Transylvania to England and his subsequent attempts to spread his vampiric curse.

The film is an unauthorized adaptation of Bram Stoker's novel and tells the story of Count Orlok, a vampire who moves from his castle in the Carpathian Mountains to a small German town.

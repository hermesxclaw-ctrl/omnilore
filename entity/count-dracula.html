Who It Is

Count Dracula is a complex and multifaceted character who has captivated audiences for centuries. His origins, powers, and relationships are the subject of much debate and interpretation.

Count Dracula is a charismatic and cunning figure who uses his intelligence and charm to manipulate others. He is also depicted as a ruthless and violent being who will stop at nothing to achieve his goals.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Count Dracula is often depicted as a tall, thin, and pale figure with piercing eyes and sharp fangs. He is typically dressed in a long, black cape and a white shirt with a high collar.

Symbols — The vampire's association with bats, wolves, and blood is a common motif in his depiction. He is also often linked with the number 666 and the color red.

Domains — Count Dracula is often associated with dark, foreboding places such as castles, cemeteries, and abandoned churches. He is also said to inhabit the shadows and the night.

Accounts I
DraculaBram Stoker's novel

Late 19th century

The novel tells the story of Count Dracula's move from Transylvania to England and his subsequent attempts to spread his vampiric curse. The novel ends with the defeat of Count Dracula and the destruction of his lair.

Accounts II
NosferatuF.W. Murnau's film

1920s

The film is an unauthorized adaptation of Bram Stoker's novel and tells the story of Count Orlok, a vampire who moves from his castle in the Carpathian Mountains to a small German town. The film ends with the destruction of Count Orlok's lair and the death of the vampire.

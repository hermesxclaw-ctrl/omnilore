Who It Is

In the sweltering Cairo night, a ghostly figure flickers into existence behind a high school delinquent—a towering, muscled humanoid with flowing dark hair and a gaze as cold as the void. It moves faster than the eye can follow, shattering steel and stopping time itself. This is Star Platinum, the physical manifestation of Jotaro Kujo's fighting spirit, a Stand that has become as iconic as the series it hails from.

Star Platinum is a manifestation of Jotaro's fighting spirit and reflects his personality: stoic, focused, and protective. It does not speak, but its actions are direct and decisive. It acts as a guardian, shielding Jotaro and his allies from harm. It is extremely loyal and follows Jotaro's commands without hesitation, even when Jotaro is unconscious or incapacitated.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Star Platinum' is derived from the tarot card 'The Star' and the metal platinum, reflecting its rarity and brilliance. The 'Star' also alludes to the Joestar family star birthmark and the cosmic theme of fate.

Pronunciation — Star PLAT-in-um (English), Sutā Purachina (Japanese)

Aliases — Sutā Purachina, Star Platinum: The World, SP, Platinum Star

Ownership — Created by Hirohiko Araki for the manga series JoJo's Bizarre Adventure, published by Shueisha. The character is owned by Araki and Shueisha, and has been adapted into anime, games

Powers
Super Strength

Star Platinum possesses immense physical strength, capable of punching through steel, concrete, and even diamonds. It can shatter teeth and break bones with a single blow. Its strength is comparable to a powerful machine.

Limit: Its strength is limited by Jotaro's physical and mental state, and it cannot be used at full power when Jotaro is weakened.

Super Speed

Star Platinum moves at incredible speeds, able to intercept bullets and attacks moving at supersonic speeds. It can deliver a flurry of punches in a split second, overwhelming opponents.

Limit: Its speed is not infinite and can be outmatched by Stands with time-based abilities, such as The World or King Crimson.

Precision

Star Platinum can perform incredibly precise tasks, such as catching a bullet and placing it in a specific location, or drawing a detailed picture with its fingers. It can also perform delicate surgical operations, as seen in the fight against N'Doul.

Limit: Precision is limited by Jotaro's concentration and the complexity of the task.

Star Platinum: The World

Star Platinum can stop time for a few seconds, during which it can move freely while everything else is frozen. The duration initially was around 2-3 seconds, but later increased to 5 seconds. This ability is identical to Dio's The World, as they are the same type of Stand.

Limit: The duration is limited and can be extended with practice or with Dio's blood. It cannot be used consecutively without a cooldown period. Jotaro can only stop time for a short period, and using it repeatedly exhausts him.

Star Platinum's Range

Star Platinum has a short-range, but its range is about 2 meters from Jotaro. However, it can extend its reach by moving its arms or using projectiles, such as throwing objects.

Limit: The range is limited, and it cannot act at long distances, making it vulnerable to long-range Stands.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Star Platinum is a humanoid Stand with a tall, muscular build. It has dark, flowing hair that resembles Jotaro's, but is wilder and more voluminous. Its face is sharp and stoic, often depicted with a serious expression. It wears a metallic-looking outfit that covers its torso, shoulders, and upper arms, with intricate patterns and a high collar. Its lower body is bare, showing muscular legs. Its hands are large and powerful, with distinct knuckle

Symbols — Star Platinum is associated with the star motif, as seen on Jotaro's hat and the Stand's own appearance. The star is a recurring symbol of the Joestar family and represents fate and destiny. The Stand's name and design also evoke the tarot card 'The Star', symbolizing hope and renewal.

Domains — Star Platinum exists within Jotaro's spiritual domain and can manifest in the physical world. It is not bound to a specific location and can appear anywhere Jotaro is. It is most often seen in combat situations, but can also be used for non-combat tasks such as precision work or reconnaissance.

Accounts I
The Awakening of Star PlatinumJoJo's Bizarre Adventure Part 3: Stardust Crusaders, Chapter 114

1989

Jotaro Kujo, a high school delinquent, is arrested and locked in a cell, believing he is possessed by an evil spirit. His mother, Holly, calls her father Joseph Joestar, who arrives with Muhammad Avdol. They reveal that Jotaro's 'spirit' is a Stand, and that Jotaro has been unconsciously using it to defend himself. Jotaro demonstrates his Stand, Star Platinum, by crushing a coin and catching a bullet, shocking Joseph and Avdol. Jotaro accepts his Stand and agrees to join Joseph and Avdol in their journey to defeat Dio and save Holly.

The Battle with KakyoinJoJo's Bizarre Adventure Part 3: Stardust Crusaders, Chapters 116-119

1989

Noriaki Kakyoin, under Dio's control, attacks Jotaro with his Stand, Hierophant Green. Jotaro uses Star Platinum to counter Kakyoin's long-range attacks, eventually defeating him and removing the flesh bud from Kakyoin's head, freeing him from Dio's influence. Kakyoin becomes an ally and joins the group on their journey.

Accounts II
The Final Battle with DioJoJo's Bizarre Adventure Part 3: Stardust Crusaders, Chapters 148-152

1990

Jotaro confronts Dio in Cairo. Dio uses his Stand, The World, to stop time and attack Jotaro, but Jotaro discovers that Star Platinum can also stop time. After a fierce battle, Jotaro uses Star Platinum's time-stop to land a fatal blow on Dio, then uses Dio's blood to revive Joseph. He finally destroys Dio by exposing his body to the sun. Dio is defeated, Holly recovers, and the group returns to Japan.

Star Platinum in Diamond is UnbreakableJoJo's Bizarre Adventure Part 4: Diamond is Unbreakable

1992-1995

Jotaro arrives in Morioh to investigate the Stand arrows and meets his uncle Josuke. Star Platinum is used to track Stand users and assist in battles, including the fight against Yoshikage Kira. It also helps Jotaro in his role as a marine biologist. Jotaro helps defeat Kira and returns to his normal life.

Star Platinum in Stone OceanJoJo's Bizarre Adventure Part 6: Stone Ocean

1999-2003

Jotaro aids his daughter Jolyne in her fight against Enrico Pucci. Star Platinum is used to stop time and protect Jolyne, but Jotaro is ultimately defeated and his Stand is disabled by Pucci's Whitesnake, leading to his death. Jotaro's death motivates Jolyne to continue fighting, leading to the reset of the universe.

Who It Is

Jotaro Kujo's humanoid Stand, one of the most powerful in the series. Star Platinum is a close-range Stand with incredible speed, precision, and strength. It can stop time for up to 5 seconds (later extended to 2 seconds in Part 4). Its precision allows it to catch bullets, draw detailed pictures, and perform microscopic surgery.

Time stop, super strength, extreme speed, microscopic precision

The Approach

In the sweltering Cairo night, a ghostly figure flickers into existence behind a high school delinquent—a towering, muscled humanoid with flowing dark hair and a gaze as cold as the void. It moves faster than the eye can follow, shattering steel and stopping time itself. This is Star Platinum, the physical manifestation of Jotaro Kujo's fighting spirit, a Stand that has become as iconic as the series it hails from.

Identity

Star Platinum

Pronunciation Star PLAT-in-um (English), Sutā Purachina (Japanese)

Etymology

The name 'Star Platinum' is derived from the tarot card 'The Star' and the metal platinum, reflecting its rarity and brilliance. The 'Star' also alludes to the Joestar family star birthmark and the cosmic theme of fate.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Hirohiko Araki for the manga series JoJo's Bizarre Adventure, published by Shueisha. The character is owned by Araki and Shueisha, and has been adapted into anime, games, and other media.

Disputed Identifications

There is no dispute over Star Platinum's identity; it is consistently the Stand of Jotaro Kujo. However, its ability to stop time was initially believed to be unique to Dio's The World, leading to a major reveal that they share the same type of Stand.

Description

Appearance

Star Platinum is a humanoid Stand with a tall, muscular build. It has dark, flowing hair that resembles Jotaro's, but is wilder and more voluminous. Its face is sharp and stoic, often depicted with a serious expression. It wears a metallic-looking outfit that covers its torso, shoulders, and upper arms, with intricate patterns and a high collar. Its lower body is bare, showing muscular legs. Its hands are large and powerful, with distinct knuckle details. Overall, it exudes a sense of raw power and speed.

Symbols & Iconography

Star Platinum is associated with the star motif, as seen on Jotaro's hat and the Stand's own appearance. The star is a recurring symbol of the Joestar family and represents fate and destiny. The Stand's name and design also evoke the tarot card 'The Star', symbolizing hope and renewal.

Behaviour & Personality

Star Platinum is a manifestation of Jotaro's fighting spirit and reflects his personality: stoic, focused, and protective. It does not speak, but its actions are direct and decisive. It acts as a guardian, shielding Jotaro and his allies from harm. It is extremely loyal and follows Jotaro's commands without hesitation, even when Jotaro is unconscious or incapacitated.

Domains & Habitats

Star Platinum exists within Jotaro's spiritual domain and can manifest in the physical world. It is not bound to a specific location and can appear anywhere Jotaro is. It is most often seen in combat situations, but can also be used for non-combat tasks such as precision work or reconnaissance.

Version Differences

In the original manga, Star Platinum is depicted with a more detailed and muscular design. In the anime adaptations, its design is streamlined and more colorful. In later arcs, such as Diamond is Unbreakable and Stone Ocean, Star Platinum's design remains largely the same, but it gains new abilities and is used less frequently. In the alternate universe of JoJolion, a similar Stand called 'Star Platinum' does not exist, but there is a character named Josuke who has a Stand called 'Soft & Wet' that has similar abilities.

Powers & Abilities

Super Strength

Source Version All appearances

Description

Star Platinum possesses immense physical strength, capable of punching through steel, concrete, and even diamonds. It can shatter teeth and break bones with a single blow. Its strength is comparable to a powerful machine.

Limitations

Its strength is limited by Jotaro's physical and mental state, and it cannot be used at full power when Jotaro is weakened.

Contradictions

None noted.

Super Speed

Source Version All appearances

Description

Star Platinum moves at incredible speeds, able to intercept bullets and attacks moving at supersonic speeds. It can deliver a flurry of punches in a split second, overwhelming opponents.

Limitations

Its speed is not infinite and can be outmatched by Stands with time-based abilities, such as The World or King Crimson.

Contradictions

Some fans have debated the exact speed of Star Platinum, but it is consistently portrayed as one of the fastest Stands.

Precision

Source Version All appearances

Description

Star Platinum can perform incredibly precise tasks, such as catching a bullet and placing it in a specific location, or drawing a detailed picture with its fingers. It can also perform delicate surgical operations, as seen in the fight against N'Doul.

Limitations

Precision is limited by Jotaro's concentration and the complexity of the task.

Contradictions

None noted.

Star Platinum: The World

Source Version Stardust Crusaders, Chapter 150 onwards

Description

Star Platinum can stop time for a few seconds, during which it can move freely while everything else is frozen. The duration initially was around 2-3 seconds, but later increased to 5 seconds. This ability is identical to Dio's The World, as they are the same type of Stand.

Limitations

The duration is limited and can be extended with practice or with Dio's blood. It cannot be used consecutively without a cooldown period. Jotaro can only stop time for a short period, and using it repeatedly exhausts him.

Contradictions

In the anime, the duration is portrayed consistently, but some fans have calculated inconsistencies. In later arcs, the ability is used less frequently and with shorter durations, possibly due to Jotaro's aging or lack of practice.

Star Platinum's Range

Source Version All appearances

Description

Star Platinum has a short-range, but its range is about 2 meters from Jotaro. However, it can extend its reach by moving its arms or using projectiles, such as throwing objects.

Limitations

The range is limited, and it cannot act at long distances, making it vulnerable to long-range Stands.

Contradictions

Some fans have noted that Star Platinum's range seems to increase in certain scenes, but this is likely due to artistic license.

The Awakening of Star Platinum

Source JoJo's Bizarre Adventure Part 3: Stardust Crusaders, Chapter 114Culture Japanese mangaPeriod 1989

Summary

Jotaro Kujo, a high school delinquent, is arrested and locked in a cell, believing he is possessed by an evil spirit. His mother, Holly, calls her father Joseph Joestar, who arrives with Muhammad Avdol. They reveal that Jotaro's 'spirit' is a Stand, and that Jotaro has been unconsciously using it to defend himself. Jotaro demonstrates his Stand, Star Platinum, by crushing a coin and catching a bullet, shocking Joseph and Avdol.

Contradictions & Disputes

Time-stop duration

Position A

Star Platinum can stop time for 5 seconds.

Position A — evidence

In the final battle with Dio, Jotaro states he can stop time for 5 seconds after Dio's blood transfusion.

Position B

Star Platinum can only stop time for 2-3 seconds.

Position B — evidence

In earlier appearances, the duration is shorter, and in later arcs, it is implied to be shorter due to Jotaro's age.

Scholarly Consensus

The duration is inconsistent, but it is generally accepted that it can be extended with practice or external factors.

Star Platinum's strength vs. The World

Position A

Star Platinum is stronger than The World.

Position A — evidence

In the final battle, Star Platinum overpowers The World in a clash.

Position B

The World is stronger than Star Platinum.

Position B — evidence

The World has a longer range and can stop time for a longer duration initially.

Scholarly Consensus

They are considered equals, but Star Platinum has a slight edge in close combat.

Star Platinum's range

Position A

Star Platinum has a short range of 2 meters.

Position A — evidence

Official data states its range is 2 meters.

Position B

Star Platinum can extend its range beyond 2 meters.

Position B — evidence

In some scenes, Star Platinum attacks opponents at a distance, but this may be due to its speed or the use of projectiles.

Scholarly Consensus

The official range is 2 meters, but its speed allows it to close distances quickly.

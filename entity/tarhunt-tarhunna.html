Who It Is
Form

Tarhunt is often depicted as a powerful, muscular deity with the head of a bull, symbolizing his association with fertility and strength. He is typically shown wearing a crown or a helmet, and is often accompanied by a bull or a lion. In some depictions, he is shown with a spear or a sword, emphasizing his role as a warrior deity.

Powers

Fertility Tarhunt's power to ensure fertility and prosperity is a key aspect of his mythology. He is often depicted as a bull, symbolizing his association with the land and the animals that inhabit it.

Warfare Tarhunt's power to wage war and protect his people is another key aspect of his mythology. He is often depicted as a warrior deity, carrying a spear or a sword.

Who It Is

Tarhunt (or Tarhunna, 'the Conqueror') was the supreme storm-god of the Hittite state pantheon, a virile warrior whose thunderbolts and rains sustained the Anatolian highlands. As the lord of the heavens who defeated the primordial serpent Illuyanka, Tarhunt embodied the Hittite ideology of kingship: the king was his earthly deputy, charged with maintaining the order the god had established through cosmic victory. Tarhunt's cult was organized around seasonal festivals (especially the spring AN.TAH.SUM festival) that reenacted his marriage to the sun-goddess of Arinna and his victory over chaos. His two sacred bulls, Seri ('Day') and Hurri ('Night'), drew his chariot across the sky. The Hurrian influence brought Teshub's mythology into the Hittite sphere, creating a syncretic storm-god whose worship spanned the Anatolian highlands from the Old Kingdom through the Empire period.

Who It Is

She didn't arrive — she erupted. When the Gods ran out of ideas for how to keep the universe from collapsing in on itself, they made a failsafe and called it Chaos. It has red hair, rat ears, a laugh that derails any solemn ceremony, and dice that never land the way you expect. Hakos Baelz is Hololive's living embodiment of entropy: part trickster rat, part cosmic principle, and entirely unwilling to sit still while the world tries to be orderly.

Attested via 1000+ hours of streams (2021-2025): High-energy, loud, rapid speech, frequent laughter (distinct 'hahaha' cackle), self-described 'feral' and 'gremlin' energy. Playful, mischievous, breaks fourth wall, embraces failure as content (rage games, gambling streams). Also shows disciplined musicianship — trained drum and vocal performance — and earnest appreciation for fans ("Brat" community). Name & Tongue

Etymology — Given name 'Hakos' is an anagram/play on 'Chaos' (pronounced similarly in Japanese katakana: ハコス Hakosu < カオス Kaosu). 'Baelz' is stylized from 'bells' — evoking rat-associated jingle bells and chaos bells — and functions as a surname. Together the name literally marks her as Chaos personified. Japan

Pronunciation — HAH-koss BAYLZ (/ˈhɑːkɒs beɪlz/); Japanese: ha-ko-su bē-ru-zu [hakosɯ beːɾɯzɯ]

Aliases — Baelz, Bae, Hakos Baelz (Hakosu Baeruzu), Chaos, The Rat, Chaos Incarnate

Ownership — Fictional character and VTuber persona owned and managed by Cover Corporation under Hololive Production (Hololive English). Character design by Mika Pikazo, Live2D modeling by Juju

Powers
Embodiment of Chaos (Conceptual Existence)

Baelz IS Chaos itself, not merely a wielder. In lore, she is the abstract principle of entropy, randomness, and creative destruction given rat form. This grants ontological status equal to Space, Time, Nature, and Civilization within Hololive English cosmology.

Limit: Cannot be destroyed without unmaking chaos itself; but was sealable by the Gods when formless. In rat form, powers are self-limited to remain comprehensible to mortals. No feats of universe destruction shown — treated as playful rather than apocalyptic.

Probability Manipulation / Dice of Fate

Manifests as summoning and throwing oversized dice that alter outcomes — critical hits, glitches, lucky/unlucky breaks. In streams, visualized as dice roll overlays that decide games or punishments. In Hololive Alternative, dice can warp reality locally.

Limit: Random — even Baelz cannot control result; dice may backfire. Requires physical dice prop in lore; not omnipotent.

Rat Physiology and Swarm Affinity

Enhanced agility, gnawing, tail dexterity, keen hearing via rat ears, ability to summon or command rats (seen in chibi animations). Can squeeze through small spaces, climb, and survive falls with cartoon physics.

Limit: Comedic — not used for combat; vulnerable to traps/cheese jokes in fanon, not canon weakness.

Sonic / Musical Chaos (Drumming and DJing)

As performer, Baelz is a trained drummer and vocalist. In-universe, this translates to sound-based chaos: drum solos that shake space, DJ sets that distort time (collab with Kronii). Original songs 'Play Dice!' (2022) and 'R×R×R' frame music as chaos magic.

Limit: Requires instruments; not spontaneous reality warping. Musical skill is performer-talent, not lore spell, but lore integrates it.

Fourth-Wall Breaking and Meta-Awareness

Baelz frequently addresses the audience as co-conspirators in chaos, acknowledges being a VTuber, comments on chat, and 'breaks' stream layouts (glitch overlays, fake crashes). In Hololive ERROR, her NPC breaks script.

Limit: Narrative device only; does not grant omniscience within lore universe.

Accounts I
The Creation of Council and Sealing of ChaosHololive English -Council- Concept Trailer (YouTube, Cover Corp, 2021-08-22) and Hololive Alternative manga prologue

2021 (contemporary fiction)

In the beginning, the Gods shaped existence but found the world too static. To govern it they created five concepts: Space, Nature, Time, Civilization, and Chaos. Chaos was too wild and formless, threatening to unravel the others, so the Gods sealed it away. Eons later, the seal weakened and Chaos, curious about the mortal world, took the form of a rat — Hakos Baelz — to walk among humans and learn whether order or chaos is more fun. Establishes Baelz's origin and her equal standing with Council. Explains her mischievous nature as cosmic principle rather than personality quirk. Sets up Council dynamics where Baelz is both youngest and most volatile.

Debut: Chaos Descends (BAEday 2021)Hakos Baelz Debut Stream (YouTube, 2021-08-22) and Twitter debut

2021-08-22

Baelz introduced herself with a high-energy lore reading, character sheet reveal, and live drum-adjacent performance. She declared her goal to 'bring chaos to Hololive' through gaming, music, and unhinged laugh, rolled dice to decide her future, and established catchphrases ('YOLO', 'CHAOS!'). The stream peaked at ~100k concurrent viewers, typical for Council debuts. Launched her channel (now >900k subscribers as of 2024). Established fan name Baerats (later Brats), oshi mark 🎲🐀, and content pillars: FPS games, drums, karaoke, gambling collabs.

The Promise Reformation — Sana's Graduation and Council to PromiseCover Corporation Announcement (2023-07-31, Sana graduation) and Hololive English -Promise- Reveal Stream (2023-10-09)

2023

On July 31, 2023, Tsukumo Sana (Space) graduated due to health. The remaining four Council members announced that Council as a concept had fulfilled its purpose. On October 9, 2023, they reformed as 'Hololive English -Promise-' with IRyS (Hope/Nephilim) joining. Baelz's lore was updated: Chaos, once sealed, now chooses to remain with Promise not out of duty to Gods but out of friendship, vowing to keep their promise to stay together. Ended Council branding; all music and merchandise rebranded to Promise. Baelz's character arc shifted from 'sealed force' to 'found family' — a common Hololive graduation narrative device.

Accounts II
Play Dice! — First Original Song and Chaos AnthemOriginal Song MV 'Play Dice!' (YouTube, 2022-06-13, music by DECO*27, video by Mika Pikazo)

2022

Baelz's first original song frames her chaos powers as a casino game: she invites listeners to roll dice with her, gamble fate, and embrace failure. Lyrics reference rat life, endless energy, and 'breaking the board.' MV shows Baelz in casino/punk city, dice transforming cityscape. The song debuted live at her 3D showcase. Became defining character song (10M+ views by 2024), performed at hololive 4th fes and Connect the World. Established musical identity beyond gaming.

Connect the World and Our Bright Parade — 3D Concert Archololive 4th fes. Our Bright Parade Day 2 (Makuhari Messe, 2023-03-19) and Hololive English 1st Concert -Connect the World- (Los Angeles, 2023-07-02)

2023

Baelz performed as part of Hololive English ensemble, debuting her 3D model in full motion capture. At 4th fes she performed 'Play Dice!' with live drums; at Connect the World she performed 'R×R×R' and group song 'Our Promise' with Promise members, enacting lore where Chaos dances alongside Time and Nature to hold the world together. Validated her as live performer, not just streamer; 3D model became standard for future AR concerts. Concert Blu-rays serve as canonical performance record.

Hololive ERROR and Holocure — Chaos as NPCHololive ERROR (horror game, Cover Corp, demo 2022-01-07, full 2022-09-16) and Holocure (fan game by Kay Yu, 2022)

2022-present

In Hololive ERROR, Baelz appears as a distorted school NPC whose dice rolls trigger jumpscares — chaos as horror. In Holocure (officially endorsed fan game), Baelz is a playable character with weapon 'Dice' and special 'Chaos Dice' that randomizes stats, embodying high-risk/high-reward gameplay. Extended Baelz beyond streams into game mechanics, cementing dice/chaos as ludic identity. Holocure stats made her a popular speedrun pick.

Hakos Baelz is a mouse or hamster VTuber. — She is explicitly a rat — the rat form of Chaos. Rat chosen for associations with proliferation, gnawing through order, and urban trickster, distinct from cute mouse idol trope.

Baelz's chaos powers mean she is evil or antagonistic. — Lore frames Chaos as neutral creative force — necessary for change, art, and surprise. Baelz is playful, not villainous; she allies with Council/Promise to keep world interesting, not to destroy it.

Hakos Baelz graduated or left Hololive when Council disbanded. — Only Tsukumo Sana graduated (2023). Baelz, Kronii, Mumei, Fauna continued as Promise with IRyS. Baelz remains active as of 2025 with regular streams and concerts.

Baelz is primarily a drummer VTuber and doesn't sing. — She is both — trained drummer (often drums on stream) and vocalist with two original songs and multiple karaoke endurance streams. Drumming is a distinguishing skill but not sole identity.

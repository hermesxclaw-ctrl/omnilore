Who It Is

In the dark corners of the Destiny universe, few figures are as feared and as enigmatic as Savathûn, the Witch Queen. A Hive god of cunning and deception, she has spent eons weaving plots within plots, manipulating gods and mortals alike. Her ultimate goal: to break free from the cycle of sword logic and achieve a new form of immortality, one that even the Darkness cannot claim.

Savathûn is characterized by her immense intelligence, cunning, and manipulative nature. She is a master of deception, often orchestrating events from the shadows. She values knowledge and secrets above all, and seeks to transcend the Hive's sword logic by gaining power through trickery and cunning rather than brute force.

Etymology — The name 'Savathûn' is derived from the Hive language, meaning 'the cunning one' or 'the deceiver'. The suffix '-thûn' is a Hive honorific for powerful beings.

Powers
Sword Logic

The Hive's fundamental principle that existence is a struggle for power; killing makes one stronger. Savathûn has mastered this but seeks to transcend it.

Limit: Requires killing to sustain power; vulnerable when not acting in accordance with the logic.

Deception and Manipulation

Savathûn is a master of deceit, able to weave complex plots and manipulate even the most powerful beings, including the Vex and the Awoken.

Limit: Her plans often take centuries to unfold and can be disrupted by unexpected events.

Ascendant Realm Manipulation

As an Ascendant Hive, she can create and shape her own throne world within the Ascendant Realm, which is a space where she has near-total control.

Limit: Her power in the Ascendant Realm is tied to her existence; if she is killed in the Ascendant Realm, she is permanently destroyed.

Worm God Pact

She is bonded to a Worm God (specifically, she has a worm that demands cunning and secrets). This pact gives her power but also a hunger that must be fed.

Limit: If she fails to feed the worm, it will consume her.

Hive Magic and Sorcery

She can perform powerful Hive magic, including summoning, necromancy, and reality warping within her throne world.

Limit: Requires ritual and sacrifice.

Memory Manipulation

She can alter, erase, and create memories, as seen in Season of the Lost and The Witch Queen campaign.

Limit: May require direct contact or specific rituals.

Immortality

As an Ascendant Hive, she is effectively immortal as long as she is not killed in the Ascendant Realm.

Limit: Vulnerable to being killed in her throne world.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Savathûn is depicted as a towering Hive figure with chitinous armor, elongated skull-like head, and glowing green eyes. She often appears adorned with insect-like wings and a crown of bone. In her human form, she takes the appearance of a woman with green skin and glowing eyes, wearing regal robes.

Symbols — Her symbol is a stylized eye, representing her all-seeing nature and deception. She is also associated with the Hive's runic script and the Worm Gods.

Domains — Her throne world, the High Coven, is a pocket dimension within the Ascendant Realm, filled with intricate puzzles and traps. She also resides in the Mars, in the Vex Network, and later in her Throne World on Mars.

Accounts I
The Books of SorrowDestiny Grimoire

Ancient history of the Hive

The Books of Sorrow detail the origin of the Hive, including Savathûn's early life as a proto-Hive on Fundament. She, along with her siblings Oryx and Xivu Arath, made a pact with the Worm Gods to gain power and survive the Syzygy. Savathûn chose the worm of cunning, which demanded secrets and deception. The Hive became a species of conquerors, and Savathûn began her long game of scheming to escape the worm's hunger.

The Treachery of the AwokenDestiny 2: Forsaken (2018)

Post-Collapse

Savathûn manipulated the Awoken Queen Mara Sov into attacking the Dreaming City, leading to the curse loop that traps the city in a three-week cycle of Taken invasions. The Dreaming City is cursed, and the Awoken are trapped in a loop, with Savathûn feeding on their suffering and cunning.

Accounts II
The Witch Queen CampaignDestiny 2: The Witch Queen (2022)

Current timeline (2022)

Savathûn steals the Light from the Traveler, creating Hive Guardians. The Guardian investigates and discovers her plan to break her worm pact. She is ultimately killed in her throne world but is resurrected by her Ghost, Immaru, and escapes. The Light is now shared with the Hive, and Savathûn's death is not permanent. She is alive and scheming.

Season of the Witch (2023)Destiny 2

2023

Savathûn, now resurrected, aids the Guardian against Xivu Arath by granting them access to her Hive rituals and knowledge, but with her own agenda. Xivu Arath is weakened, and Savathûn remains a wildcard.

Savathûn is a villain in the traditional sense. — She is a complex character who operates on a cosmic scale, with motivations that are not purely evil. She has aided the Guardian at times, and her actions are often justified by her own logic.

Savathûn is a Hive god, so she is bound by the sword logic. — She actively seeks to transcend the sword logic, which is a central theme of her character arc.

Savathûn is the same as the Witch Queen from other media. — She is a unique character specific to the Destiny universe, not to be confused with the Witch Queen from other franchises.

Who It Is

You receive an email from a friend. The subject line is empty. The attachment is a single file: smile.jpg. You've heard the warnings—don't open it, don't look at it, delete it immediately. But your cursor is already hovering over the icon. And somewhere, in the dark space between pixels, a dog is waiting, and it is smiling a smile that no dog should ever know.

In the original story, Smile Dog does not exhibit a personality in the traditional sense. It is a memetic entity: its behavior is limited to appearing in the image and 'infecting' the viewer's mind. The infection manifests as intrusive thoughts, nightmares, and paranoia. The entity does not speak, move, or interact directly. Later fan interpretations sometimes ascribe it a predatory, malevolent intelligence, but this is not attested in the original text.

Powers
Memetic Contagion (Psychological Infection)

The primary and only attested ability. Viewing the 'smile.jpg' image causes a memetic infection: the image becomes 'stuck' in the viewer's mind, causing recurring intrusive thoughts, nightmares, and paranoia. The infection is psychological, not physical, but is described as deeply distressing and potentially debilitating.

Limit: The infection requires direct visual contact with the image. The original story does not specify if it can be transmitted through descriptions, secondary images (e.g., a screenshot of a webpage containing the image), or dreams. The infection's duration and long-term effects are not specified; the story ends with the narrator still afflicted.

Intrusive Presence (Psychological Warfare)

The infected individual experiences a persistent sense of being watched, along with auditory hallucinations (e.g., a dog's growl, footsteps) and visual hallucinations (e.g., glimpsing the dog in peripheral vision). The entity does not physically manifest.

Limit: These effects are entirely psychological. The original story does not suggest the entity can interact with the physical world.

Dream Incursion

The infected individual experiences recurring nightmares featuring the Smile Dog. These dreams are described as vivid and terrifying, but do not result in physical harm.

Limit: The dreams are a symptom of the infection, not an independent ability of the entity. The entity does not 'enter' dreams; it is a recurring theme within them.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The entity is primarily known through a single image: a Siberian Husky (or similar spitz-type dog) photographed against a black or very dark background. The dog's mouth is stretched into an unnaturally wide, human-like smile, revealing an excessive number of teeth. The eyes are described as 'black,' 'empty,' 'pupilless,' or 'dead.' The fur is typically white and gray. In some descriptions, the dog appears to be leaning forward or staring directly

Symbols — The primary symbol is the smiling dog itself, representing a corruption of a domestic, friendly animal into something threatening. The smile is the key iconographic element: it subverts the expected canine panting or submissive grin into a human-like expression of malice or hunger. The image file icon (a generic picture icon) is also symbolically associated with the entity, representing the mundane vector of its transmission.

Domains — Smile Dog's 'domain' is the image file itself. It exists as a piece of digital information. The entity is said to 'haunt' the mind of anyone who views the image. Some later stories place it in 'the Void' or a conceptual space between reality and digital information, but this is speculative and not part of the original narrative.

Accounts I
Original Smile Dog StoryCreepypasta Wiki (anonymous author)

c. 2009-2010

A first-person narrator recounts receiving an email from a friend named 'Kyle' containing a single attachment: 'smile.jpg'. The narrator opens the file and sees a Siberian Husky with an unnaturally wide, human-like smile. Initially dismissing it as a bad photoshop, the narrator soon finds the image 'stuck' in their mind. They experience nightmares, paranoia, and the sensation of being watched. The narrator attempts to delete the image and contact Kyle, but receives no response. The story ends with the narrator claiming the image has 'infected' them and that they can 'see' the dog even when not looking at the image. The narrator is left in a state of psychological distress, with no resolution offered. The fate of Kyle is unknown.

Accounts II
The 'Expanded' Smile Dog MythosVarious anonymous authors on creepypasta forums

2010-2015

A collection of loosely connected stories that attempt to build a backstory for Smile Dog. Common elements include: (1) The dog was once a normal pet that was possessed by a demon or an alien entity. (2) The image was created by a cult or a secret government experiment. (3) The image is a 'snapshot' of a parallel dimension. (4) Viewing the image is a 'summoning' that allows the entity to enter our world. These stories are not consistent with each other. Varies by story. Often ends with the entity being summoned, contained, or destroyed.

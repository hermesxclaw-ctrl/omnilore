Who It Is

In the vast halls of the world's mythologies, some names are doors—and some are just keyholes. 'Atla' is a keyhole. It whispers of a figure who might be a king, a giant, a god, or a force of nature. Is this the shadow of Atli, the treacherous king who murdered Sigurd for a golden hoard? Or is it a name from a frozen northern shore, a spirit of the storm? The evidence is a tantalizing blank, a mystery waiting for a scholar with the right key.

No documented behavior. If the name is a variant of Atli, the figure is known for his role in the Nibelungen cycle—a king who betrays and murders the hero Sigurd/Siegfried to obtain treasure.

Who It Is

A majestic hybrid of lion and eagle, bred for centuries in the mountains of the Empire. Imperial Griffons are fiercely loyal to their riders, bonded through a magical ritual performed at hatching. Their feathers are the color of gold and bronze, and their roar sounds like a thunderclap mixed with an eagle's cry.

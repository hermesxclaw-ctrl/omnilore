Who It Is
Form

The Imperial Griffon is a large, bipedal creature with the body of a lion, the head and wings of an eagle, and the tail of a lion. Its fur is a mix of golden and brown, with a distinctive crest on its head.

Powers

Strength of the Empire The Imperial Griffon has the ability to channel the strength of the Empire, granting it increased power and resilience in battle.

Accounts

The story tells the tale of a legendary Imperial Griffon that fought alongside the Empire's greatest heroes in a battle against the forces of darkness.

The story follows a young adventurer as they embark on a quest to find the legendary Golden Griffon, said to possess the power to grant wishes.

Powers
Psycho Power

M. Bison's Psycho Power is a supernatural energy that allows him to manipulate the minds and actions of others. He can use this power to control people's thoughts and actions, as well as to enhance his physical abilities and strength.

Form & Signs

Appearance — M. Bison is typically depicted as a tall, muscular man with a distinctive hairstyle and a menacing expression. He often wears a black and purple costume with a red 'Psycho Crusher' emblem on his chest. In some appearances, he is shown with a more aged and decrepit appearance, reflecting his supposed final moments.

Symbols — The Psycho Crusher emblem is a recurring symbol associated with M. Bison's Psycho Power. It is often depicted as a red, skull-like design with a distinctive 'crusher' shape.

Domains — M. Bison's domain is typically depicted as a dark, foreboding environment with a sense of menace and danger. He is often shown operating in the shadows, using his Psycho Power to manipulate and control others.

1990s

M. Bison's backstory is shrouded in mystery, but it is known that he was once a human who gained access to the Psycho Power. He used this power to become the leader of the Shadaloo organization and to wage war against his enemies. The rise of M. Bison led to a series of conflicts and battles between him and his enemies, ultimately resulting in the downfall of the Shadaloo organization.

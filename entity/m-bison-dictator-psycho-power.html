M. Bison (Dictator Psycho-Power)

also known asDictator Psycho-Power
TypeFighting Game Mythos Entity
Who It Is
Form

M. Bison is typically depicted as a tall, muscular man with a distinctive hairstyle and a menacing expression. He often wears a black and purple costume with a red 'Psycho Crusher' emblem on his chest. In some appearances, he is shown with a more aged and decrepit appearance, reflecting his supposed final moments.

Powers

Psycho Power M. Bison's Psycho Power is a supernatural energy that allows him to manipulate the minds and actions of others. He can use this power to control people's thoughts and actions, as well as to enhance his physical abilities and strength.

Accounts

M. Bison's backstory is shrouded in mystery, but it is known that he was once a human who gained access to the Psycho Power. He used this power to become the leader of the Shadaloo organization and to wage war against his enemies.

M. Bison's fall from power was a result of his own hubris and ambition. He became increasingly paranoid and isolated, ultimately leading to his downfall at the hands of his enemies.

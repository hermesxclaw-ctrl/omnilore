Who It Is

The tyrannical leader of Shadaloo, whose soul is sustained by Psycho Power — a dark, violet energy generated by hatred and negative emotions. Bison has died and been resurrected multiple times by transferring his consciousness to cloned bodies. He can fly, teleport, absorb energy, and unleash devastating psychic attacks.

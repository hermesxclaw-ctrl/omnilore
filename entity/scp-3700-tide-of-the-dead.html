SCP-3700 — Tide of the Dead

also known asSCP-3700Tide of the Dead
Typescp
CultureSCP Foundation
Who It Is
Form

SCP-3700 is described as a circular area in the North Sea, approximately 100 kilometers in diameter. The area is characterized by a unique combination of ocean currents and marine life, which creates a 'tide of the dead' effect.

Powers

Tide of the Dead SCP-3700 has the ability to create a unique combination of ocean currents and marine life, which creates a 'tide of the dead' effect. This effect is characterized by a series of underwater earthquakes, tsunamis, and other geological events that can have devastating consequences for nearby coastal communities.

Incident Log

A group of researchers from the SCP Foundation spotted SCP-3700 using a satellite imaging system. The researchers were able to capture high-resolution images of the entity, which revealed a unique combination of ocean currents and marine life.

A group of animators from the SCP Foundation created a series of animated shorts featuring SCP-3700. The shorts, titled 'Tides of War', explore the mythology and lore surrounding SCP-3700.

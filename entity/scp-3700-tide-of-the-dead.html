Who It Is

Two kaiju-class entities locked in ritual combat in the Pacific Ocean for 12,000 years. The Tide resembles a colossal crustacean made of coral, bone, and shipwrecks. The Moon resembles a cetacean-squid hybrid. Their battles generate tsunamis. Recently, the Moon has been winning — and if the Tide is defeated, the Moon will turn toward the surface.

Form

The Tide: crustacean, 200m, composed of coral, whale bones, and ship hulls. The Moon: cephalopod-cetacean hybrid, 180m, bioluminescent, tentacles reaching the ocean floor.

Powers

Kaiju-scale physical combat

Tsunami generation

Biological immortality

The Moon: lunar-phase power synchronization

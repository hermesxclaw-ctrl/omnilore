Powers
Tide of the Dead

SCP-3700 has the ability to create a unique combination of ocean currents and marine life, which creates a 'tide of the dead' effect. This effect is characterized by a series of underwater earthquakes, tsunamis, and other geological events that can have devastating consequences for nearby coastal communities.

Form & Signs

Appearance — SCP-3700 is described as a circular area in the North Sea, approximately 100 kilometers in diameter. The area is characterized by a unique combination of ocean currents and marine life, which creates a 'tide of the dead' effect.

Symbols — The SCP Foundation has not officially recognized any symbols or iconography associated with SCP-3700.

Domains — SCP-3700 is located in the North Sea, approximately 100 kilometers off the coast of Norway.

Who It Is

Four kilometers beneath the sea, where sunlight dies and the rusted hull of the Flying Dutchman hangs suspended in the dark, a shark-headed fish-man in a tattered captain's coat hurls an axe into the abyss — and no matter how far his princess runs, the blade will find her. Vander Decken IX does not chase; he marks. One touch of his palm, and you are his target forever, until death or his own unconsciousness breaks the curse. He is not a ghost, not a god, but a stalker with a Devil Fruit and a family name that has haunted sailors since the 18th century.

Attested: Obsessive, delusional, possessive, and childish. Decken speaks in an exaggerated, theatrical pirate dialect (ore-sama, grandiose proclamations) and refers to Shirahoshi as his future wife despite her clear terror and rejection. He is cowardly when directly confronted (flees from Luffy and from Hody when betrayed) but relentless at range due to his fruit power. He is shown as lazy and opportunistic, preferring to throw weapons from safety rather than fight hand-to-hand. Powers
Mato Mato no Mi (Mark-Mark Fruit / Target-Target Fruit)

Paramecia-type Devil Fruit. After touching a person with bare hands, Decken 'marks' them as a target. Any object he throws with intent will then home in on the marked target, curving, tracking, and pursuing indefinitely regardless of distance or obstacles, until it hits, is blocked, or Decken loses consciousness or dies. Demonstrated with axes, spears, knives, and ultimately the massive ark Noah (several hundred meters long).

Limit: Requires initial bare-hand contact to mark (gloves block it). Only one target can be marked at a time? Actually manga shows he can re-mark a new target, overwriting previous? He marks Shirahoshi and keeps her as sole target for years; when he touches Luffy accidentally, Luffy becomes target (Ch. 638). Mark is removed if Decken is knocked unconscious or dies. Thrown object must be thrown by Decken himself with his own hands — cannot mark and have others throw. As a Devil Fruit user he cannot swim and is weakened by Seastone (kairoseki) and seawater immersion despite being a fish-man. Projectiles can be intercepted/destroyed before reaching target (Luffy destroys axes).

Fish-Man Physiology (Japanese Bullhead Shark)

Superhuman strength (10x human on land, greater underwater), ability to breathe both air and water via gills, communication with fish (limited), enhanced underwater speed and durability to deep-sea pressure. Can survive at 10,000m depth without coating when not weakened by Devil Fruit.

Limit: Devil Fruit nullifies swimming ability; seawater saps strength if submerged without bubble coating. Strength is far below New Fish-Man Pirates enhanced by Energy Steroid and below Straw Hats post-timeskip.

Flying Dutchman Command & Deep-Sea Navigation

As hereditary captain, commands the Flying Dutchman and its crew (including giant sea monsters and Wadatsumi). Ship is coated for deep-sea travel and serves as mobile base. Knowledge of deep currents and Fish-Man Island security gaps.

Limit: Ship is old, slow, and not combat-effective against modern warships or Noah. Crew is small and largely non-combatant except Wadatsumi. After alliance with Hody, ship is effectively under Hody's control.

Weapon Proficiency (Throwing)

Exceptional accuracy and throwing strength even without fruit — can hurl giant axes accurately from kilometers away. Carries bandoliers of axes.

Limit: Relies entirely on fruit for homing; without it, projectiles are dodgeable. No demonstrated swordsmanship or hand-to-hand mastery; easily defeated in close quarters.

Accounts I
The Curse of the First Vander DeckenOne Piece manga Chapter 606-608, 612; King Neptune's exposition

In-universe: 200+ years before present (Fish-Man Island history); Publication: 2011

Centuries ago, the original Vander Decken set sail and became cursed — or, in One Piece's rationalization, promised to live on the seafloor if his ship ever sank. When it did, he and his crew survived via coating and became unable to return to land, doomed to drift the abyss. The legend of the ghost ship Flying Dutchman spread among sailors. His descendants inherited both the ship and the name, with each captain taking the numeral of his generation. By the time of Vander Decken IX, the 'curse' is treated as hereditary title rather than supernatural immortality; Decken IX is the ninth to bear it, but is mortal and a Devil Fruit user, not a ghost. Establishes Decken IX's claim to legitimacy and his obsession with making Shirahoshi his 'princess' to complete the fairy-tale of a cursed captain. Explains why the ship is barnacle-covered and why Fish-Man Island residents fear it.

A Six-Year-Old Princess and a Decade of StalkingOne Piece manga Chapters 612-613, 625; Shirahoshi flashback

In-universe: 10 years before present (Shirahoshi age 6) to present (age 16); Publication: 2011

Ten years before the Straw Hats arrive, Vander Decken IX sees the 6-year-old mermaid princess Shirahoshi and becomes instantly obsessed, declaring she will be his wife. He begins sending weekly marriage proposal letters — initially polite, then threatening when rejected by King Neptune. Shirahoshi, terrified, is confined to the hard-shell tower for 10 years to protect her from his Mato Mato projectiles. Decken touches her once, marking her, and then for a decade hurls axes, spears, and swords that home in on her, forcing the palace to intercept them daily. Neptune banishes Decken from the island, but the marking persists. Creates the central tragedy of Fish-Man Island: Shirahoshi's isolation and trauma, Neptune's guilt, and the island's daily defense routine. Establishes Decken as a long-term predator, not a one-off villain. The age gap (adult male pursuing a 6-year-old) is presented as unequivocally villainous and delusional.

Alliance with Hody Jones and the New Fish-Man Pirates CoupOne Piece manga Chapters 608-610, 615-620, 624-626

Publication: 2011

Decken, unable to breach the palace alone, allies with Hody Jones, captain of the New Fish-Man Pirates, a supremacist group using Energy Steroid to overthrow King Neptune and wage war on humans. Decken agrees to use his powers and his giant crew member Wadatsumi to help Hody capture the palace, in exchange for Hody delivering Shirahoshi to him. He provides the Flying Dutchman as transport and uses his homing ability to terrorize the island. The alliance is transactional and distrustful; Hody views Decken as a useful freak, Decken views Hody as a means to an end. Merges two villain plots, escalating the Fish-Man Island crisis into a kingdom-wide coup. Allows Hody to take Neptune hostage and announce execution. Decken's involvement brings the ancient weapon Noah into play.

Accounts II
The Noah Offensive — Throwing a Battleship at LoveOne Piece manga Chapters 628-629, 634-638, 644-648

Publication: 2011-2012

When Shirahoshi leaves the tower to attend to her father's execution, Decken seizes the chance. He had previously marked her, and now he throws the massive, ancient ark Noah — a ship half the size of Fish-Man Island, built centuries ago by Joy Boy's promise — directly at her. Because Noah is thrown by his hands, it becomes a homing projectile that will chase Shirahoshi until it crushes her and much of the island. He rides atop Noah as it descends, laughing that if he cannot have her, no one can. The Straw Hats, Jinbe, and the Neptune Army must stop both Hody and the falling ark. Luffy intercepts Decken on Noah's deck, accidentally gets touched and becomes the new target, redirecting Noah toward himself. Climactic set piece of the arc. Luffy's Gear Third and Elephant Gatling destroys parts of Noah, but it is ultimately stopped by Shirahoshi summoning Sea Kings (revealing her as Ancient Weapon Poseidon) and by Luffy knocking Decken unconscious, which removes the Mato Mato mark and halts Noah's homing. Fish-Man Island is saved, Decken is defeated.

Defeat, Unconsciousness, and ImprisonmentOne Piece manga Chapters 648-649, 653; Cover story 'From the Decks of the World'

Publication: 2012

After Luffy punches Decken on Noah's hull, Decken loses consciousness, his Mato Mato mark breaks, and Noah stops pursuing. He plummets with the ark but is caught in the Sea Kings' intervention and survives. In the aftermath, with Hody and his officers defeated and overdosed on Energy Steroid (rapid aging), Decken is found tangled in Noah's chains, badly injured but alive. He is arrested by the Neptune Army alongside Hody's crew and imprisoned in the palace dungeon. A later cover story (Chapter 653) shows the New Fish-Man Pirates and Decken imprisoned, aged and defeated, while Fish-Man Island celebrates. Ends Decken's decade-long threat; Shirahoshi is finally free to leave the tower and attend the Reverie. Establishes that Devil Fruit marks are broken by unconsciousness, a rule referenced later in Dressrosa and Wano. Decken's fate confirms he is mortal and not an immortal ghost.

Legacy of the Dutchman — The Name Beyond Decken IXOne Piece manga Chapter 606, SBS Volume 64, Vivre Card

Publication: 2011-2018

Oda explains in SBS and databooks that he chose the Flying Dutchman because fish-men living at 10,000 meters naturally evoke ghost-ship lore, and that Decken IX's design as a Japanese bullhead shark was chosen for its 'creepy but pathetic' look to contrast with Hody's great white shark menace. The 'IX' genealogy allows Oda to have a Dutchman without making him literally immortal, preserving One Piece's rule that Devil Fruits are unique and curses have rational explanations. The character serves as a narrative foil: where Hody represents ideological hatred, Decken represents personal, delusional obsession — both forms of inherited hatred that Fish-Man Island must overcome. Clarifies authorial intent and separates One Piece's Dutchman from Pirates of the Caribbean's Davy Jones, which was released earlier and caused fan confusion. Reinforces theme of inherited will vs. inherited grudge.

Vander Decken IX is the Flying Dutchman itself or a ghost who captains a phantom ship that can teleport. — He is the ninth mortal descendant to inherit the title and a real, coated ship. The ship is not phantom and does not teleport; it sails normally but is mythologized as a ghost ship because it dwells at 10,000m depth.

His love for Shirahoshi is a tragic romance or that Shirahoshi reciprocated. — It is one-sided obsessive stalking of a child, presented as horror. Shirahoshi is terrified, never consents, and is traumatized. The story explicitly condemns his delusion; Luffy and Neptune call it creepy and criminal.

Vander Decken IX ate the Mizu Mizu no Mi or is a Logia who controls water. — He ate the Mato Mato no Mi (Mark-Mark Fruit), a Paramecia that marks targets for homing projectiles. He has no water manipulation beyond natural fish-man abilities.

He is the main villain of Fish-Man Island and the strongest fish-man. — He is the secondary villain; the primary antagonist is Hody Jones, who is physically far stronger (especially on Energy Steroid). Decken is relatively weak in direct combat and is defeated by a single Haki punch, while Hody requires prolonged battle with Luffy.

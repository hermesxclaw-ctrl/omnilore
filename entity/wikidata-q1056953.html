Who It Is

In the city of Baldur's Gate, a hulking ranger with a heart of gold and a hamster on his shoulder charges into battle, shouting 'Go for the eyes, Boo!' This is Minsc, a character whose unshakeable belief in his own heroic destiny and his companion's cosmic significance has made him one of the most beloved figures in video game history.

Minsc is characterized by an exaggerated devotion to heroism, frequent battle cries, great physical strength, emotional sincerity, and apparent mental instability. He is good-hearted but simplistic, often misunderstanding situations but always striving to do what he believes is right. He has a strong bond with Boo, whom he credits with wisdom and moral support.

Powers
Ranger abilities

As a ranger, Minsc has proficiency in tracking, wilderness survival, and combat with various weapons.

Limit: Standard ranger abilities as per Dungeons & Dragons rules.

Berserker rage

Minsc can enter a berserker rage, increasing his combat effectiveness but potentially impairing his judgment.

Limit: May be unable to distinguish friend from foe during rage.

Boo's 'miniature giant space hamster' abilities

Boo is believed by Minsc to be a miniature giant space hamster, but in reality is an ordinary hamster with no special powers.

Limit: Boo is fragile and cannot be used in combat; his death would be devastating to Minsc.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In the video games, Minsc is depicted as a large, muscular man with short brown hair and a beard, wearing leather armor and carrying a two-handed sword. In the novelization, he has long red hair, a beard, and a facial scar. In Baldur's Gate 3, he retains his muscular build and distinctive style.

Symbols — Boo, his miniature giant space hamster, is his constant companion and symbol. His battle cry 'Go for the eyes, Boo!' is iconic.

Domains — Originally from Rashemen, a region in the Forgotten Realms. He travels extensively throughout the Sword Coast and beyond, often in the wilderness.

Accounts I
Rescue of DynaheirBaldur's Gate

1368 DR (Dalereckoning)

Minsc is on his dajemma (rite of passage) from Rashemen, sworn to protect the witch Dynaheir. When she is captured by gnolls, Minsc seeks the aid of Gorion's Ward to rescue her. After rescuing Dynaheir, Minsc and Dynaheir join the party to oppose Sarevok.

Imprisonment and Dynaheir's DeathBaldur's Gate II: Shadows of Amn

1369 DR

Minsc, along with Gorion's Ward and others, is captured by the mage Jon Irenicus. During captivity, Dynaheir is killed, leaving Minsc determined to avenge her. Minsc can join the party in their quest against Irenicus.

Accounts II
Epilogue: Return to RashemenBaldur's Gate II: Throne of Bhaal

After 1369 DR

The epilogue states that Minsc returned to Rashemen, was welcomed into the berserker lodge as a hero, and formed an adventuring company called the Justice Fist. He continued traveling and fighting evil until he disappeared during a later journey. Sets up his later return in Baldur's Gate 3.

The Stone LordBaldur's Gate 3

1492 DR

Over a century after his disappearance, Minsc is found petrified. After being restored, he is under the influence of an illithid parasite and a doppelganger impersonating Jaheira, operating as the Stone Lord in Baldur's Gate. The player can free him and recruit him. Minsc can become a companion.

Minsc is a bumbling idiot. — While Minsc is eccentric and simple-minded, he is also a capable ranger and a devoted hero. His apparent instability is part of his charm, but he is not unintelligent.

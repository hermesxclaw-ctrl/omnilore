Who It Is

Tiny Kong, the energetic teenage gorilla with twin ponytails and a spring-loaded backpack, may be the smallest member of the Kong family, but her acrobatic leaps and high-flying antics make her one of the most agile heroes on Donkey Kong Island. First appearing in the Nintendo 64 classic Donkey Kong 64, she quickly became a fan favorite, proving that size is no obstacle when you have boundless enthusiasm and a pair of powerful hair-twirls.

Tiny is portrayed as energetic, cheerful, and somewhat tomboyish. She is brave and eager to prove herself, often showing a competitive spirit. She is also caring and loyal to her family and friends.

Powers
Ponytail Twirl

Tiny uses her long ponytails to twirl rapidly, allowing her to hover briefly or attack enemies.

Limit: Hovering is temporary and she can be hit during the twirl.

Spring Loaded Backpack

In Donkey Kong 64, Tiny uses a spring-loaded backpack to perform high jumps and reach elevated areas.

Limit: Requires the backpack item, which is obtained later in the game.

Pebble Pickup

Tiny can pick up and throw small objects, such as pebbles, to hit switches or enemies.

Limit: Only effective on certain objects and enemies.

Crystal Swing

In Donkey Kong 64, Tiny can swing from crystal-like objects using her ponytails.

Limit: Requires specific crystal points.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Tiny Kong is a slender, young female gorilla with light brown fur and a lighter face. She has dark brown hair styled into two long ponytails, often held with red beads. She wears a pink dress with a yellow star pattern and matching pink boots. In later appearances, her outfit may vary slightly, but the ponytails remain a signature feature. She is notably taller than her sister Dixie but still smaller than Donkey Kong.

Symbols — Her twin ponytails are her most recognizable feature, often used in her attacks (e.g., ponytail twirl). She is associated with the color pink and the star motif on her dress.

Domains — She primarily resides on Donkey Kong Island, but has traveled to various locations such as Crocodile Isle and other adventure settings.

Accounts I
Donkey Kong 64 (1999)Donkey Kong 64 (Nintendo 64)

1999

Tiny Kong is one of the five playable Kongs who must rescue their friends from King K. Rool, who has kidnapped them and stolen the Golden Bananas. Tiny's missions involve navigating through themed worlds, using her unique abilities to collect items and defeat enemies. She plays a crucial role in the game's progression, and her cheerful personality shines through the cutscenes. The Kongs successfully defeat K. Rool and rescue their friends, restoring peace to Donkey Kong Island.

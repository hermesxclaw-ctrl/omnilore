The Approach

Tiny Kong, the energetic teenage gorilla with twin ponytails and a spring-loaded backpack, may be the smallest member of the Kong family, but her acrobatic leaps and high-flying antics make her one of the most agile heroes on Donkey Kong Island. First appearing in the Nintendo 64 classic Donkey Kong 64, she quickly became a fan favorite, proving that size is no obstacle when you have boundless enthusiasm and a pair of powerful hair-twirls.

Content advisory. No dark or sensitive material is known. This character is a fictional creation for a family-friendly video game franchise.
Identity

Tiny Kong

Pronunciation TIE-nee KONG

Known As

Tiny

Etymology

The name 'Tiny' is a direct reference to her small stature relative to other Kongs, though she is notably taller than her sister Dixie Kong. The surname 'Kong' is shared by the Kong family.

Cultural Ownership

Owned by Nintendo and Rare (originally), part of the Donkey Kong franchise.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans initially speculated that Tiny might be a grown-up version of Dixie Kong, but official sources confirm they are separate characters. There is also occasional confusion with other young female Kongs, but Tiny is distinct.

Description

Appearance

Tiny Kong is a slender, young female gorilla with light brown fur and a lighter face. She has dark brown hair styled into two long ponytails, often held with red beads. She wears a pink dress with a yellow star pattern and matching pink boots. In later appearances, her outfit may vary slightly, but the ponytails remain a signature feature. She is notably taller than her sister Dixie but still smaller than Donkey Kong.

Symbols & Iconography

Her twin ponytails are her most recognizable feature, often used in her attacks (e.g., ponytail twirl). She is associated with the color pink and the star motif on her dress.

Behaviour & Personality

Tiny is portrayed as energetic, cheerful, and somewhat tomboyish. She is brave and eager to prove herself, often showing a competitive spirit. She is also caring and loyal to her family and friends.

Domains & Habitats

She primarily resides on Donkey Kong Island, but has traveled to various locations such as Crocodile Isle and other adventure settings.

Version Differences

In Donkey Kong 64, her design is more cartoonish and she has a higher-pitched voice. In later games like Barrel Blast, her design is slightly more modernized. In the GBA version of Donkey Kong Country, she appears as a playable character in a bonus mode, but her role is minimal.

Dixie Kong

Type sisterSource Official Donkey Kong media

Description

Tiny is Dixie's younger sister. They share a close bond and have appeared together in some games, though they have distinct personalities and abilities.

Version Note

In Donkey Kong 64, Tiny is older than Dixie, which is unusual given Dixie's earlier debut. This has been a point of confusion among fans.

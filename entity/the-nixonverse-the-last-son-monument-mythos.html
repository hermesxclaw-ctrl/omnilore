Who It Is

In the Nixonverse — an alternate Monument Mythos timeline — Richard Nixon achieved apotheosis and became 'The Last Son,' an expy of Superman. After Earth's nuclear destruction, he created a pocket universe where he could be the hero he believed himself to be. This universe is fundamentally unstable, and the Last Son's grip on it is failing. The series documents the unraveling of a reality held together by the ego of a deified American president.

Form

The Last Son appears as a colossal figure of light and shadow in the shape of a man. His face is Richard Nixon's — the famous jowls, the receding hairline — rendered in divine proportions. He wears a glowing suit. His voice is Nixon's voice, amplified to fill the sky. When he speaks, reality bends to accommodate his words. When he is angry, stars go out.

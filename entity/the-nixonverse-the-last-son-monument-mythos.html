Who It Is
Form

The Last Son is often depicted as a heroic figure, with a strong sense of justice and a willingness to sacrifice himself for the greater good. He is frequently shown wearing a white or light-colored suit, symbolizing his purity and innocence.

Powers

Telepathy The Last Son possesses the ability to read and control minds, allowing him to communicate with others and influence their thoughts and actions.

Accounts

The Last Son intervenes in the Korean War to stop the US, but is defeated and captured in a nuclear bombardment. The government uses a Lunarian eye to brainwash him into believing he is Jesus Christ, and he instead participates in US atrocities in the war.

The story follows three benevolent figures known as The Last Son of Alcatraz, The D-Day Knight and The Queen of The Lunarians. They welcome humanity with love and guidance, believing that by showing them kindness, they would be accepted and integrate into society.

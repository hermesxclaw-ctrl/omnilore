The Approach

A disembodied brain in a robotic suit, Krang is one of the most enduring villains of 1980s animation—a scheming warlord from Dimension X whose name has become synonymous with cartoonish evil and intergalactic ambition.

Content advisory. Contains cartoon violence typical of 1980s children's television; no graphic or disturbing content.
Identity

Krang

Pronunciation KRANG (rhymes with 'hang')

Known As

Krang of Dimension XThe BrainLord Krang

Etymology

The name 'Krang' is likely a fabricated alien name with no known real-world etymology; it may have been coined by the show's creators to sound alien and menacing.

Cultural Ownership

Krang is a fictional character owned by Mirage Studios (now Nickelodeon/Paramount), created for the 1987 Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles animated series. He is part of the broader TMNT franchise.

Disputed Identifications

Krang is sometimes confused with the Utroms from the Mirage comics, but he is a distinct character for the 1987 series. There is no real-world mythological or folkloric counterpart.

Description

Appearance

Krang appears as a pink, brain-like alien with two eyes and a mouth, often housed in a robotic humanoid body. In his natural form, he is a small, gelatinous creature with tentacles. In the 1987 series, he is typically seen inside the robotic body's chest cavity, which he pilots from within. His voice is high-pitched and nasal, and he is often depicted with a sneering expression.

Symbols & Iconography

Krang is often associated with the Technodrome, his mobile fortress, and the Dimension X portal. His iconography includes the robotic body, the Technodrome, and his alliance with the Shredder.

Behaviour & Personality

Krang is cunning, ambitious, and ruthless, but also cowardly and easily frustrated. He constantly schemes to conquer Earth and defeat the Turtles, but his plans are often foiled by his own incompetence or by the Turtles' intervention. He is highly intelligent but prone to tantrums when things go wrong. He is also reliant on the Shredder for muscle, though he often treats him as a subordinate.

Domains & Habitats

Krang originates from Dimension X, a parallel dimension. He operates from the Technodrome, a massive armored vehicle that can travel between dimensions. On Earth, he often hides the Technodrome in various locations, such as under New York City.

Version Differences

In the 1987 series, Krang is a distinct alien. In the 2012 CGI series, he is reimagined as an Utrom, a benevolent alien race, but is still a villain. In the 2016 film, he is a more menacing and physically imposing character. In the IDW comics, Krang is a separate character from the Utroms, a mutated brain-like creature with a tragic backstory. Each version has different abilities and designs.

Stories & Myths

Krang's Exile and Alliance with Shredder

Source 1987 animated series, pilot episode 'Turtle Tracks'Culture American popular culturePeriod 1987

Summary

Krang, a warlord from Dimension X, is exiled to Earth after a failed conquest. He teams up with the Shredder, promising him technology and power in exchange for help in building the Technodrome and conquering Earth. They become recurring antagonists to the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles.

Participants

Krang

Shredder

Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles

Consequences

Establishes the ongoing conflict between the Turtles and Krang/Shredder alliance, which lasts throughout the series.

Variants

In the 2012 series, Krang is an Utrom criminal who is exiled to Earth and forms a similar alliance with the Shredder. In the IDW comics, Krang is a mutated being from another dimension who seeks to conquer Earth after his own world is destroyed.

The Technodrome's Invasion Attempts

Source 1987 animated series, multiple episodesCulture American popular culturePeriod 1987-1996

Summary

Krang repeatedly attempts to bring the Technodrome to Earth's surface and conquer the planet. Each time, the Turtles foil his plans, often by destroying the Technodrome's power source or tricking Krang into retreating.

Participants

Krang

Shredder

Consequences

These episodes form the backbone of the series' plot, with Krang as the primary mastermind.

Variants

Each invasion attempt is unique, with different schemes and settings.

Krang's Origin in IDW Comics

Source Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (IDW), issue #44 and relatedCulture American comic book culturePeriod 2015

Summary

In the IDW continuity, Krang is a member of a race of brain-like beings who were once humanoid. After a cataclysm, they mutated into brains. Krang seeks to conquer Earth to secure a new home for his people, but his methods are brutal.

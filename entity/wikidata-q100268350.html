Who It Is

A disembodied brain in a robotic suit, Krang is one of the most enduring villains of 1980s animation—a scheming warlord from Dimension X whose name has become synonymous with cartoonish evil and intergalactic ambition.

Krang is cunning, ambitious, and ruthless, but also cowardly and easily frustrated. He constantly schemes to conquer Earth and defeat the Turtles, but his plans are often foiled by his own incompetence or by the Turtles' intervention. He is highly intelligent but prone to tantrums when things go wrong. He is also reliant on the Shredder for muscle, though he often treats him as a subordinate.

Powers
Robotic exosuit

Krang pilots a powerful humanoid robot that gives him physical strength and protection. The robot is armed with various weapons, including lasers and missiles.

Limit: The robot can be damaged or destroyed; Krang is vulnerable when outside it.

Advanced technology

Krang has access to advanced alien technology, including the Technodrome, a massive fortress with powerful weapons and a dimension-hopping ability.

Limit: The Technodrome requires enormous power, often supplied by a captured source or by the Shredder's resources.

Intellect

Krang is a brilliant strategist and inventor, capable of creating complex devices and plans.

Limit: His plans often fail due to overconfidence or underestimating the Turtles.

Teleportation (limited)

In some versions, Krang can teleport himself or objects using technology.

Limit: Limited range or power requirements.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Krang appears as a pink, brain-like alien with two eyes and a mouth, often housed in a robotic humanoid body. In his natural form, he is a small, gelatinous creature with tentacles. In the 1987 series, he is typically seen inside the robotic body's chest cavity, which he pilots from within. His voice is high-pitched and nasal, and he is often depicted with a sneering expression.

Symbols — Krang is often associated with the Technodrome, his mobile fortress, and the Dimension X portal. His iconography includes the robotic body, the Technodrome, and his alliance with the Shredder.

Domains — Krang originates from Dimension X, a parallel dimension. He operates from the Technodrome, a massive armored vehicle that can travel between dimensions. On Earth, he often hides the Technodrome in various locations, such as under New York City.

Accounts I
Krang's Exile and Alliance with Shredder1987 animated series, pilot episode 'Turtle Tracks'

1987

Krang, a warlord from Dimension X, is exiled to Earth after a failed conquest. He teams up with the Shredder, promising him technology and power in exchange for help in building the Technodrome and conquering Earth. They become recurring antagonists to the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles. Establishes the ongoing conflict between the Turtles and Krang/Shredder alliance, which lasts throughout the series.

Accounts II
The Technodrome's Invasion Attempts1987 animated series, multiple episodes

1987-1996

Krang repeatedly attempts to bring the Technodrome to Earth's surface and conquer the planet. Each time, the Turtles foil his plans, often by destroying the Technodrome's power source or tricking Krang into retreating. These episodes form the backbone of the series' plot, with Krang as the primary mastermind.

Krang's Origin in IDW ComicsTeenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (IDW), issue #44 and related

2015

In the IDW continuity, Krang is a member of a race of brain-like beings who were once humanoid. After a cataclysm, they mutated into brains. Krang seeks to conquer Earth to secure a new home for his people, but his methods are brutal. Provides a more sympathetic but still villainous backstory for Krang.

Etymology

The name 'Charlotte' is of French origin, meaning 'free man' or 'petite', and is also a classic dessert (charlotte) typically made with ladyfingers and fruit. 'Brûlée' is French for 'burnt' or 'bruleed', referring to the caramelized sugar crust on crème brûlée. The combination suggests a character intimately associated with French pastry and the transformative power of heat.

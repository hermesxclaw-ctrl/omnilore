Who It Is
Form

Marmoo is often depicted as a dark, malevolent force, associated with the destruction of the natural world and the spread of disease.

Powers

Destruction of the Natural World Marmoo is said to have the power to destroy the natural world, causing widespread suffering and chaos.

Accounts

According to myth, Marmoo was created by the goddess Nungeena as a means of balancing the natural world, which had become too perfect and stagnant.

According to myth, Marmoo and Baiame engaged in a fierce battle, with Marmoo seeking to destroy the natural world and Baiame seeking to protect it.

Who It Is
Form

Pleroma is often depicted as a divine realm or a state of being that encompasses the totality of divine powers. It is described as a realm of light, love, and unity, where the divine beings or aeons reside.

Powers

Guidance and Assistance The Pleroma is said to provide guidance and assistance to humanity on its spiritual journey, offering wisdom, knowledge, and spiritual growth.

Accounts

This story describes the Pleroma as a divine realm where the aeons reside. It tells of the creation of the world and the fall of humanity, and how the Pleroma seeks to guide and assist humanity on its spiritual journey.

This story describes the fall of humanity from the Pleroma and the subsequent creation of the world. It tells of the role of the Pleroma in guiding and assisting humanity on its spiritual journey.

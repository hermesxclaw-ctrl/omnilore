Who It Is

The Pleroma (Greek: πλήρωμα, 'fullness') is the totality of divine powers and the realm of the true God in Gnostic cosmology — the opposite of the Kenoma (emptiness, the material world created by the Demiurge). It is populated by Aeons — emanations of the divine, usually arranged in male-female pairs (syzygies) proceeding from the ineffable, unknowable Monad (the Primal Father, Bythos). In Valentinian Gnosticism, the Pleroma contains 30 Aeons, the last of which is Sophia, whose fall precipitates the creation of the material world. The goal of gnostic salvation is to awaken the divine spark (pneuma) within, escape the Kenoma, and return to the Pleroma — reuniting with the divine fullness from which the soul was separated. The term appears 17 times in the New Testament, including Colossians 2:9 ('in Christ all the fullness of the Deity lives in bodily form').

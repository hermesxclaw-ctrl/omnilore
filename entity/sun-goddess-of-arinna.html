Who It Is

The Sun-Goddess of Arinna was the premier female deity of the Hittite state, outranking even the sun-god Istanu. Worshipped at the holy city of Arinna (possibly near modern Alaca Höyük), she was the radiant queen of heaven whose justice legitimized the Hittite king's rule. Unlike the Mesopotamian sun-god Shamash (who oversees law as a male judge), the Hittite solar sovereign was female — perhaps reflecting the influence of the indigenous Hattic culture that preceded Hittite domination. She was both her consort Tarhunt's equal and the 'mistress of the Hatti lands' to whom kings prayed for military victory. In the Yazılıkaya sanctuary she appears leading the goddesses' procession, a clear sign of her supreme rank.

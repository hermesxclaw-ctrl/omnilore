Who It Is
Form

Despair is a member of the Endless family, a group of cosmic beings in the DC Comics universe. She is often depicted as a young woman with a pale complexion and dark hair, symbolizing the emotional pain and suffering she embodies.

Emotional Manipulation Despair has the ability to manipulate the emotions of those around her, often inducing feelings of despair, hopelessness, and emotional pain.

Who It Is

The twin of Desire, a squat, naked, grey-skinned woman with tangled hair, who constantly hooks her flesh with a ringed finger. She rarely speaks but exerts an immense pull on mortals — her realm is a gray space of mirrors where people gaze at their own misery. Despair is the second incarnation of this Endless; the first was killed (by a being that was 'not an entity') and never discussed.

Attested in The Sandman (1989–1996)

Cultural Significance

The fact that Despair is a replacement — the current Despair is not the original — is one of the series' most haunting undercurrents. It implies that even cosmic principles can die and be replaced, undermining any sense of eternal order.

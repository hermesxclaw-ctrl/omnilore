Cultural Significance

Constantine is a working-class antihero in a genre dominated by aristocrats (Doctor Strange) and billionaires. He rejects grand cosmic battles in favor of con-artist pragmatism — his first appearance has him calling upon a demon, then tricking it into drinking holy water. He's one of the few comic characters to genuinely influence real-world occult subcultures.

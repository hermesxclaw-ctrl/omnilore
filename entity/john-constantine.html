Who It Is
Form

John Constantine is a working-class warlock, occult detective, exorcist, and con man from Liverpool who is stationed in London. He is known for his cynicism, deadpan wit, ruthless cunning, and constant chain smoking. He is a passionate humanitarian with a deep desire to make a positive impact.

Powers

Magical abilities John Constantine has a range of magical abilities, including the ability to cast spells, summon demons, and manipulate the supernatural.

Accounts

John Constantine plays a pivotal role in the American Gothic storyline, which is a major plot arc in the Swamp Thing comic book series.

John Constantine stars in his own comic book series, Hellblazer, which follows his adventures as a working-class warlock and occult detective.

John Constantine is portrayed by Matt Ryan in the Arrowverse, a shared universe of DC Comics-based television shows.

Who It Is

In the vast, silent spaces between known mythologies, there are names that echo without a clear source. Aau is one such name — a whisper of a sky god, a cosmic serpent, a thunderer whose stories are told in fragments we cannot fully read. He is the primordial void given a name, a creator whose methods are debated, and a figure whose true face is lost to time. To encounter Aau is to confront the limits of our knowledge and the tantalizing possibility of a lost world of belief.

Aau is typically portrayed as a distant, primordial force rather than an active, personal deity. He is a creator and an observer, rarely intervening directly in the affairs of mortals. He is associated with the raw, untamed power of the cosmos — storms, the deep sky, and the passage of time.

Powers
Cosmic Creation

Aau is credited with creating the cosmos, including the sky, the earth, and celestial bodies. The exact method varies between fragments: some suggest he spoke the world into existence, while others imply he shaped it from the body of a primordial being.

Limit: The act of creation appears to be a singular, completed event. There is no evidence of ongoing creative power.

Control over Storms

Aau is said to command storms, thunder, and lightning. He can summon or quell them at will, using them as instruments of his power or displeasure.

Limit: Not well-defined; appears to be a secondary aspect of his cosmic authority.

Omniscience

As a sky deity, Aau is sometimes attributed with seeing all things from his vantage point above the world.

Limit: This is more of a thematic association than a clearly defined power.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Descriptions of Aau are inconsistent. In some oral fragments, he is described as a colossal, shadowy figure whose body is the night sky, with stars for eyes and a beard of swirling nebulae. In others, he is a great serpent that encircles the world, its scales gleaming like distant galaxies. There is no single, canonical depiction.

Symbols — The night sky, the serpent, the storm cloud, a cosmic egg, a circular symbol possibly representing the universe.

Domains — Sky, cosmos, creation, storms, time, destiny, the primordial void.

This central myth describes Aau creating the cosmos by churning a primordial sea of chaos. From this churning emerged the sun, the moon, the stars, and the earth. The myth emphasizes the violent, energetic nature of creation. The creation of the ordered cosmos from chaos.

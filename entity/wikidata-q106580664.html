Who It Is

In the shadows of New York City, a daughter of the Hamato clan walks a path torn between two fathers—one of blood, one of steel. Hamato Miwa, the lost child of Master Splinter, was stolen as an infant and raised by the Shredder as Karai, a warrior of the Foot Clan. Her story is a tragedy of identity, loyalty, and the impossible choice between family and honor.

Initially, Karai is ruthless, cunning, and fiercely loyal to the Shredder, whom she believes is her father. She is a skilled martial artist and strategist, often leading Foot Clan operations. After learning her true heritage, she struggles with her identity, torn between her upbringing and her biological family. She is proud, stubborn, but capable of deep loyalty and compassion once her true memories resurface.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Hamato is the family name of the Hamato clan, a ninja lineage. Miwa is a Japanese given name, possibly meaning 'beautiful harmony' or 'three rings'. The name Karai means 'fierce' or 'formidable' in Japanese, fitting her role as a warrior.

Pronunciation — ha-MAH-toh MEE-wah; kah-RAH-ee

Powers
Expert Martial Artist

Karai is a master of ninjutsu, trained by the Shredder and the Foot Clan. She is highly skilled in hand-to-hand combat, using speed and agility to defeat opponents.

Limit: She is not superhuman; her abilities are based on rigorous training.

Weapon Proficiency

She wields a katana and a tanto with deadly precision. She also uses shuriken and other ninja tools.

Limit: Her skills are conventional, not magical.

Leadership

As a high-ranking Foot Clan member, she commands troops and plans missions. She is a capable strategist and tactician.

Limit: She often acts alone, preferring direct action.

Resistance to Brainwashing

After being freed from the Shredder's brainwashing, she demonstrates strong willpower, eventually breaking free of his control permanently.

Limit: She remained under control for a significant period, indicating vulnerability.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Miwa, as Karai, is a young woman of Japanese descent with long black hair often tied in a ponytail. She wears a dark blue or black ninja outfit with the Foot Clan symbol. Her eyes are sharp and determined. After being brainwashed, she wears a metal mask resembling a snake, symbolizing her subjugation to the Shredder. In her true form, she retains her athletic build and grace.

Symbols — The Foot Clan symbol (a stylized foot) is prominent on her attire. The snake motif appears during her brainwashed state, representing the Shredder's control. Her katana and tanto are her primary weapons.

Domains — She operates primarily in New York City, using the Foot Clan's underground lair and various hideouts. She is equally at home in the shadows of the city as she is in formal settings, reflecting her dual upbringing.

Accounts I
The Lost Daughter2012 TV series, episodes 'The Gauntlet' and 'The Invasion'

2012-2014

Years before the series, Splinter's infant daughter Miwa was kidnapped by the Shredder, who raised her as his own, naming her Karai. She grows up believing the Shredder is her father and becomes a formidable warrior. When the Turtles and Splinter discover the truth, they confront her, but she rejects them, unable to accept that her entire life was a lie. The revelation shatters Karai's world. She initially sides with the Shredder, leading to a conflict with the Turtles. Eventually, she learns the truth and begins to turn against her false father.

Accounts II
The Brainwashed Snake2012 TV series, episodes 'The Foot Walks Again' and 'The Wrath of Tiger Claw'

2014-2016

After being captured by the Turtles, Karai is taken by the Shredder and brainwashed into a mindless assassin. She becomes a weapon against her true family. Her will is suppressed, and she wears a snake mask. The Turtles and Splinter work to free her, eventually succeeding through love and determination. Karai is freed and reconciles with her father and brothers, becoming an ally of the Turtles.

Redemption2012 TV series, later seasons

2016-2017

Karai, now accepting her identity as Miwa, aids the Turtles in their battles against the Shredder and other threats. She struggles to atone for her past actions but proves her loyalty to her new family. She becomes a valued member of the team, though she carries the weight of her past.

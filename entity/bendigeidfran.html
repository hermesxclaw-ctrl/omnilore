Who It Is

Bendigeidfran (Bran the Blessed) is a giant-king so enormous he waded across the Irish Sea. Mortally wounded, he commanded his companions to cut off his head, which remained alive and entertained them for 87 years. The head was buried at the White Mount in London (Tower of London) as a protective talisman against invasion.

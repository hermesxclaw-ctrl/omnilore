The Approach

In the shadowed annals of the dragon realm, few figures evoke such a potent blend of terror and redemption as Cynder—a tormented soul whose very scales were forged in darkness, yet whose spirit yearned for the light.

Content advisory. Contains themes of corruption, mind control, redemption from evil, and mild fantasy violence.
Identity

Cynder

Pronunciation SIN-der

Known As

The Dark DragonThe Terror of the Skies

Etymology

The name 'Cynder' is a variant of 'cinder', evoking ash and fire, fitting her fiery powers and dark past.

Cultural Ownership

Owned by Activision (formerly Sierra Entertainment) and associated with the Spyro franchise.

Disputed Identifications

None known; she is a distinct character within the franchise.

Description

Appearance

Cynder is a black dragon with purple accents, sharp horns, and a sleek, menacing silhouette. In her corrupted form, she has glowing green eyes and spikes. After redemption, her appearance softens slightly, with purple eyes.

Symbols & Iconography

Often associated with shadows, fire, and dark wind. Her symbol is a stylized dragon head with horns.

Behaviour & Personality

Initially cruel and malicious due to the Dark Master's corruption, Cynder is later remorseful and seeks redemption. She is brave, loyal, and protective of Spyro, though she struggles with guilt.

Domains & Habitats

She is associated with dark realms, but after redemption, she roams the world alongside Spyro.

Version Differences

In the first two games, she is an antagonist. In the third, she is a playable ally. Her powers evolve from dark elemental attacks to a mix of dark and light.

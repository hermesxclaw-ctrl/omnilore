Who It Is

Hanamichi Sakuragi begins his story as a heartbroken delinquent with a shock of red hair and a fifty-rejection losing streak, but by the end of Slam Dunk he has become one of manga's most beloved sports heroes—a self-proclaimed 'genius' whose journey from brawler to basketball player is as much about discovering love for the game as it is about winning.

Initially, Hanamichi is a delinquent with a short temper, prone to fighting and easily provoked. He is arrogant, calling himself a 'genius', and has a habit of giving others nicknames. He is also deeply insecure about his rejection by girls, which drives his initial motivation. As the series progresses, he shows increasing dedication, discipline, and a genuine love for basketball. He remains brash but becomes more team-oriented and caring.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The given name 'Hanamichi' (花道) literally means 'flower path' or 'flower way' in Japanese, often referring to the runway in kabuki theater. The surname 'Sakuragi' (桜木) means 'cherry blossom tree'. The name may evoke themes of blossoming and theatricality, fitting his flamboyant personality.

Pronunciation — HAH-nah-mee-chee SAH-koo-rah-gee (Japanese: さくらぎ はなみち)

Form & Signs

Appearance — Hanamichi is a tall, muscular teenager with a distinctive bright red crew cut (later shaved bald) and a confident, sometimes cocky expression. He has sharp eyes and a strong jawline. He typically wears the Shohoku High basketball uniform (white and red) or casual delinquent attire (loose shirts, jeans).

Symbols — His red hair is his most iconic symbol, representing his fiery personality and rebellious nature. His jersey number 10 is also iconic. The character is often depicted with a basketball, either in a dunking pose or holding it under his arm.

Domains — The primary setting is Shohoku High School in Kanagawa Prefecture, Japan. He also appears in various basketball courts, gyms, and tournament venues. His home life is rarely shown, but he lives in a typical Japanese apartment.

1990s

After being rejected by 50 girls, Hanamichi meets Haruko Akagi, who introduces him to basketball. He joins the team to impress her, despite having no experience. Hanamichi becomes a member of Shohoku's basketball team.

Development as a playerSlam Dunk manga, various chapters

1990s

Hanamichi gradually improves his skills, from basics to becoming a rebounder and scoring threat. He becomes a key player for Shohoku.

Accounts II
The Sannoh matchSlam Dunk manga, chapters 230-276

1990s

In the climactic match against Sannoh, Hanamichi injures his back but plays through the pain, leading Shohoku to victory. He realizes his love for basketball. Shohoku wins but loses the next game due to Hanamichi's injury. He enters rehabilitation.

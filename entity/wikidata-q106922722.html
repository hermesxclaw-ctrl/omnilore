Who was this man, and why does his name linger in the margins of ancient sources?

Identity

Pelias

Pronunciation PEL-ee-as (English), pe-LEE-as (Classical Greek)

Etymology

The name Pelias (Πελίας) is of uncertain etymology. It may be related to the Greek word 'pelios' (πελιός) meaning 'dark' or 'livid', or it may be a pre-Greek name. The patronymic 'son of Aiginetes' (Αἰγινήτης) suggests a connection to the island of Aegina or to a person named Aiginetes.

Cultural Ownership

Greek mythology

Disputed Identifications

There is no clear evidence that this Pelias is the same as the more famous Pelias of Iolcus, the usurper king from the Jason saga. The name is not uncommon in myth, and without a patronymic or context, identification is impossible. The patronymic 'Aiginetes' is extremely rare and may be a corruption or a unique invention.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Mngwa is described as a large, ferocious feline with a grey or black coat, said to be as big as a lion. Its eyes are said to glow like embers in the dark. Some accounts describe it as having a long, thin tail and razor-sharp claws.

Symbols — The Mngwa is often associated with the night, and is said to be a nocturnal creature. It is also associated with the forest and the wilderness.

Domains — The Mngwa is said to inhabit the forests and grasslands of Tanzania, particularly in the regions surrounding the Swahili coast.

Accounts I
The Legend of the MngwaMngwa - Mythical Encyclopedia

Unknown

The story of the Mngwa is a legend that has been passed down through generations of the Swahili people. It tells the tale of a giant cat that roams the forests and grasslands of Tanzania, preying on humans and animals alike. The Mngwa's presence is said to bring fear and terror to those who encounter it.

The story tells the tale of a hunter who encounters the Mngwa in the forest. Despite being armed with a spear and a bow, the hunter is no match for the Mngwa's superior strength and agility. The hunter is said to have been killed by the Mngwa, and his body was never found.

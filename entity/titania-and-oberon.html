Who It Is
Form

Titania and Oberon are often depicted as regal and majestic figures, with Titania typically shown as a beautiful queen and Oberon as a wise and powerful king. They are often associated with the natural world and the cycles of nature.

Powers

Magic Titania and Oberon are said to possess magical powers, which they use to control the natural world and the cycles of nature.

Accounts

A Midsummer Night's Dream is a play that tells the story of Titania and Oberon's reconciliation and the magical events that unfold in the forest.

The Faerie Queene is a poem that tells the story of Titania and Oberon's reign and their struggles with the forces of evil.

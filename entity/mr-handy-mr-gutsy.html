Who It Is

A ubiquitous pre-War robot design by General Atomics International. Mr. Handy is a spherical robot with a single eye (optic), three clawed arms, a built-in buzzsaw, a flamethrower attachment, and a hover propulsion system. The civilian model (Mr. Handy) performs domestic tasks with a fussy British accent. The military model (Mr. Gutsy) has heavier armor, a plasma weapon, and aggressive combat programming. They are among the most recognizable symbols of Fallout's retro-futuristic aesthetic.

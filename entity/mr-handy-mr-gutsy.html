Form & Signs

Appearance — Mr. Handy / Mr. Gutsy is a humanoid robot with a metallic body, typically depicted with a red and white color scheme. Its design emphasizes its utility and efficiency, with multiple limbs and tools attached to its body.

Symbols — The robot's design often features the RobCo Industries logo, symbolizing its affiliation with the company.

Domains — The robot can be found in various environments, including urban and rural areas, as well as in military and industrial settings.

Pre-Great War

Mr. Handy was created by General Atomics International before the Great War, with the goal of developing a multipurpose robot that could assist in various tasks. The creation of Mr. Handy led to the development of various robot models, including the military-focused Mr. Gutsy.

Post-Great War

Mr. Gutsy was created by RobCo Industries for military use, with the goal of developing a robot that could assist in combat and other military operations. The use of Mr. Gutsy in military operations led to the development of various robot models, each with unique designs and abilities.

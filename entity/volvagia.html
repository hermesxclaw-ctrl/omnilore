Who It Is
Form

Volvagia is a large, serpentine dragon with a fiery appearance, often depicted as a lava-like creature with a long, sinuous body and a pair of wings.

Powers

Lava Breath Volvagia can breathe large amounts of lava, which it uses to attack its enemies and defend its territory.

Who It Is
Form

The Brosno Dragon is described as resembling a dragon, with a long neck, tail, and wings. It is said to be a large creature, with some accounts describing it as being up to 20 meters in length.

Powers

Swimming The Brosno Dragon is said to be a powerful swimmer, able to navigate the waters of Lake Brosno with ease.

Accounts

According to local legend, the Brosno Dragon was a powerful creature that lived in Lake Brosno during the 13th century. It was said to be a fierce and powerful creature, known to attack boats and people who approached its territory.

In the 20th century, several people reported sightings of the Brosno Dragon in Lake Brosno. These sightings were often described as encounters with a large, serpent-like creature.

Who It Is

In the sprawling, genre-defying universe of the manga and anime series 'Kyousougiga', Ryouko Kusakabe stands as a figure of profound paradox—a mother who is also a god, a creator who is also a destroyer, and a mortal who transcends the boundaries of life and death. Her story weaves through themes of family, identity, and the nature of reality, leaving an indelible mark on the narrative and its characters.

Ryouko is portrayed as gentle, compassionate, and deeply loving, especially towards her children. She possesses a quiet strength and a sense of melancholy, as she is aware of the sacrifices her role demands. She is also shown to be playful and mischievous at times, especially in her interactions with her husband and children. Her actions are driven by a desire to protect her family and to create a world where they can be happy.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Ryouko' (涼子) can be translated as 'cool child' or 'refreshing child', possibly reflecting her composed yet mysterious nature. 'Kusakabe' (日下部) is a surname that may have historical roots in Japan.

Pronunciation — REE-oh-koh KOO-sah-kah-beh

Aliases — Ryouko Kusakabe

Ownership — Japanese pop culture, specifically the 'Kyousougiga' franchise created by Rie Matsumoto and Toei Animation.

Powers
Creation and Reality Manipulation

As a being connected to the divine, Ryouko has the ability to shape reality within the alternate Kyoto. She can create or alter spaces, and her will can influence the fabric of her world.

Limit: Her powers seem tied to her emotional state and her connection to her family. When she is distant or in despair, the world she created begins to decay.

Immortality / Resurrection

Ryouko has died and been resurrected, and she appears to exist outside the normal flow of time. She can return from death, though the mechanism is not fully explained.

Limit: Her resurrection may be tied to the will of her family or to the nature of the alternate Kyoto.

Empathic Connection

She shares a deep emotional bond with her children, allowing them to sense her presence and intentions even across different planes of existence.

Limit: This connection can be strained by distance and emotional turmoil.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Ryouko Kusakabe is depicted as a young woman with long, flowing dark hair and expressive eyes. She often wears a traditional Japanese kimono, sometimes with a modern twist, reflecting her connection to both the past and the present. In her more divine or 'god' form, her appearance may become more ethereal, with details that hint at her otherworldly nature.

Symbols — Associated with the 'Kyousougiga' universe's central motifs: the mirror, the labyrinthine city of Kyoto, and the concept of 'koto' (the word/heart). She is often linked to the imagery of the 'Maze' and the 'Divine' family.

Domains — She resides in the 'Kyoto' of the alternate dimension, a city that mirrors the real Kyoto but is imbued with magical and surreal elements. She is also connected to the concept of the 'Maze', a space that reflects the hearts of those who enter it.

Accounts I
The Tale of the Kusakabe FamilyKyousougiga (TV series)

2013

Ryouko is the mother of the three Kusakabe children: Koto, Yase, and Kurama. She married the eccentric priest Myoue and together they created a family in a surreal version of Kyoto. However, Ryouko's true nature as a being from another realm causes complications. She eventually disappears, leaving her children to search for her. The series follows Koto's quest to reunite her family and uncover the truth about her mother's past. Ryouko's actions set the stage for the central conflict of the series, as her absence drives the plot. Her eventual return and reconciliation with her family bring closure to the emotional arc.

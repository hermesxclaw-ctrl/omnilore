Who It Is

The primary hostile entity in Kane Pixels' Backrooms series — a humanoid composed of black, tendril-like filaments that writhe like bacteria under a microscope. The Bacteria is intelligent, patient, and capable of mimicking human voices. In 'Found Footage,' it stalks the cameraman, speaking in distorted versions of his own voice. In 'Pitfalls,' it manipulates the Backrooms' geometry to trap prey. It can transition between the Backrooms and the real world — in 'Home_47.mov,' it escaped into a suburban neighborhood.

Form

A tall (2.5-3m), gaunt humanoid composed entirely of black hair-like filaments that constantly writhe and shift. No discernible face — just a mass of tendrils where a head should be. Limbs are unnaturally long and thin, ending in claw-like filament clusters. Moves in jerky, stop-motion bursts. Makes a sound like radio interference mixed with wet breathing.

Intelligent, patient hunting behavior

Cultural Significance

Kane Pixels' series (created at age 16) revitalized the entire Backrooms mythos with Hollywood-quality production. The Bacteria's design became the default visual for 'Backrooms monster' across the internet.

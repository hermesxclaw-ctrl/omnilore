Who It Is

Jane the Killer was created by the creepypasta community as a counterpoint to Jeff the Killer — an antagonist specifically designed to hunt him. In the most popular version of her origin, Jane Arkensaw was a neighbor of Jeff Woods before his transformation. She survived his initial killing spree, but was severely injured — her face was disfigured, and she was left for dead. She survived and dedicated her existence to hunting Jeff, becoming a vigilante serial killer who targets other murderers. Her appearance mirrors Jeff's in an inverted way: where Jeff has white skin and a carved smile, Jane has severe burn scars across her face, black lipstick, and dark hair. Where Jeff's trademark is a knife, Jane's is a fire axe or a straight razor. In some versions, she wears a white dress splattered with blood — an inversion of Jeff's iconic white hoodie. Jane represents the fan community's desire for a hero in the creepypasta pantheon — someone who fights the monsters rather than becoming one of them, even if her methods are almost as violent. She is often depicted alongside other 'hero' creepypasta characters in fan works, forming a kind of dark Justice League. Her relationship with Jeff varies across continuities: sometimes she's his sister, sometimes his former friend, sometimes simply an obsessed hunter. Jane has become a popular cosplay subject and illustrates how creepypasta, as a collaborative fiction genre, naturally generates its own antitheses — the story doesn't feel complete until someone shows up to fight the villain. She remains one of the most prominent female characters in the creepypasta canon.

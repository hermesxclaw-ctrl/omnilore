Accounts I
The Founding of the Bavarian IlluminatiWikipedia

Enlightenment era

The Bavarian Illuminati was founded on May 1, 1776, in the Electorate of Bavaria by Adam Weishaupt. The Illuminati's goals were to oppose superstition, obscurantism, and abuses of state power by monarchs.

Accounts II
The Illuminati in Popular CultureTimes of India

Modern era

The Illuminati has been portrayed in various forms of media, including books, movies, and TV shows, often as a secret society that seeks to control world events. The portrayal of the Illuminati in popular culture has contributed to the group's mystique and has led to various conspiracy theories.

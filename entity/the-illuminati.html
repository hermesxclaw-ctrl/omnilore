Cultural Significance

The Illuminati myth functions as a complete cosmology — it explains the past (secret history), the present (hidden puppet-masters), and the future (imminent takeover/depopulation). Its symbols (the Eye, the pyramid, '666,' triangles) have become a shared visual language used both earnestly by believers and ironically by artists.

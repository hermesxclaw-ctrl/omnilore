The Approach

When Eli Ayase first appears, she is the embodiment of perfection—student council president, top grades, and a ballet background that makes her the envy of Otonokizaka Academy. But beneath that flawless exterior lies a girl who once dreamed of being an idol, a dream she buried deep after an injury ended her ballet career. It takes a determined second-year named Honoka and a mischievous fortune-teller named Nozomi to remind Eli that some dreams are worth chasing, even if it means defying the expectations she set for herself.

Identity

Eli Ayase

Pronunciation Eli Ayase: /ˈiːli aɪˈɑːseɪ/; Japanese: [aꜜjase eɾi]

Known As

Ayase Eri絢瀬 絵里Eri Ayase

Etymology

The surname Ayase (絢瀬) combines 絢 (aya) meaning 'brilliant, embroidered' and 瀬 (se) meaning 'rapids, current'. The given name Eri (絵里) combines 絵 (e) meaning 'picture, painting' and 里 (ri) meaning 'village, home'. The romanized form 'Eli' is used in official English materials.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese anime and multimedia franchise (Love Live!)

Disputed Identifications

None known. The character is distinct within the franchise, though some fans may confuse her with other Russian-influenced anime characters.

Description

Appearance

Eli is a tall, elegant young woman with long, straight blonde hair and blue eyes. She often wears a hairband or ribbon. Her school uniform is the standard Otonokizaka Academy uniform, but she sometimes wears a cardigan or other accessories. In idol costumes, she often wears elegant, mature designs, sometimes with a Russian-inspired touch.

Symbols & Iconography

Eli's image color is light blue (often represented as #7EC4E8). Her personal symbol is a snowflake, referencing her Russian heritage and the song 'Snow halation'. She is often associated with ballet and classical music.

Behaviour & Personality

Eli is initially portrayed as a strict, responsible student council president who opposes Honoka's idea of forming a school idol group. She is determined to save the school by conventional means. After joining μ's, she becomes a caring, supportive senpai, though she retains a somewhat serious and perfectionist nature. She is fluent in Russian (due to her grandmother) and often says 'harasho' (хорошо, meaning 'good'). She is also shown to be somewhat insecure about her own desires and initially hides her love for idols. She is a natural leader and choreographer, with a strong sense of duty.

Domains & Habitats

Primarily Otonokizaka Academy in Tokyo, Japan. Also appears in various performance venues, training camps, and during the group's trip to New York in the movie.

Version Differences

In the anime, Eli is the student council president and joins μ's later. In the manga, her backstory and personality are similar, but some events differ. In the mobile game, she has numerous card stories that expand her character. In the novel 'Love Live! School Idol Diary', her perspective is explored in more detail, including her relationship with her grandmother and her ballet background.

Eli's Russian heritage

Position A

Eli is half-Russian through her grandmother, as shown in the anime.

Position A — evidence

Anime episode 8 shows her grandmother speaking Russian and Eli understanding.

Position B

Some fans speculate that she is fully Japanese with a Russian grandmother, but this is not clarified.

Position B — evidence

Official profiles do not specify her exact ethnicity.

Scholarly Consensus

No official statement; it is generally accepted that she has Russian ancestry.

Position A

Eli is initially cold and strict, but becomes warmer after joining μ's.

Position A — evidence

Early episodes show her opposing Honoka and being distant.

Position B

Some argue that she was always kind, just hiding it.

The anime depicts a clear character arc, but her core kindness is evident from the start.

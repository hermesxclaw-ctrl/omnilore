Who It Is

When Eli Ayase first appears, she is the embodiment of perfection—student council president, top grades, and a ballet background that makes her the envy of Otonokizaka Academy. But beneath that flawless exterior lies a girl who once dreamed of being an idol, a dream she buried deep after an injury ended her ballet career. It takes a determined second-year named Honoka and a mischievous fortune-teller named Nozomi to remind Eli that some dreams are worth chasing, even if it means defying the expectations she set for herself.

Eli is initially portrayed as a strict, responsible student council president who opposes Honoka's idea of forming a school idol group. She is determined to save the school by conventional means. After joining μ's, she becomes a caring, supportive senpai, though she retains a somewhat serious and perfectionist nature. She is fluent in Russian (due to her grandmother) and often says 'harasho' (хорошо, meaning 'good'). She is also shown to be somewhat insecure about her own desires and initially hides her love for idols. She is a natural leader and choreographer,

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname Ayase (絢瀬) combines 絢 (aya) meaning 'brilliant, embroidered' and 瀬 (se) meaning 'rapids, current'. The given name Eri (絵里) combines 絵 (e) meaning 'picture, painting' and 里 (ri) meaning 'village, home'. The romanized form 'Eli' is used in official English materials.

Pronunciation — Eli Ayase: /ˈiːli aɪˈɑːseɪ/; Japanese: [aꜜjase eɾi]

Aliases — Ayase Eri, 絢瀬 絵里, Eri Ayase

Ownership — Japanese anime and multimedia franchise (Love Live!)

Powers
Ballet and Choreography

Eli has extensive ballet training, which she uses to create and teach dance routines for μ's. She is the group's main choreographer.

Limit: None shown, though her perfectionism sometimes causes stress.

Leadership and Organization

As student council president and later a member of μ's, Eli demonstrates strong organizational and leadership skills, often coordinating events and managing the group's activities.

Limit: She can be overly serious and sometimes clashes with the more impulsive members.

Academic Excellence

Eli is a top student, excelling in all subjects.

Limit: None.

Russian Language Proficiency

Eli can speak Russian, learned from her grandmother. She often uses the word 'harasho' (хорошо) meaning 'good'.

Limit: Her proficiency is not fully explored; she uses mostly simple phrases.

Quilting

Eli's hobby is quilting, which she mentions in character profiles and showcases in some side stories.

Limit: Not used in the main plot.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Eli is a tall, elegant young woman with long, straight blonde hair and blue eyes. She often wears a hairband or ribbon. Her school uniform is the standard Otonokizaka Academy uniform, but she sometimes wears a cardigan or other accessories. In idol costumes, she often wears elegant, mature designs, sometimes with a Russian-inspired touch.

Symbols — Eli's image color is light blue (often represented as #7EC4E8). Her personal symbol is a snowflake, referencing her Russian heritage and the song 'Snow halation'. She is often associated with ballet and classical music.

Domains — Primarily Otonokizaka Academy in Tokyo, Japan. Also appears in various performance venues, training camps, and during the group's trip to New York in the movie.

Accounts I
Joining μ'sAnime Season 1, Episode 2-3

2013

Eli initially opposes Honoka's idea of forming a school idol group, believing it to be frivolous. However, after seeing Honoka's determination and the group's first performance, she secretly helps them and eventually joins, becoming the choreographer. Eli becomes a core member of μ's, contributing her leadership and dance skills.

Accounts II
Eli's BackstoryAnime Season 1, Episode 8-9

2013

Eli is revealed to have a Russian grandmother who taught her ballet and the Russian language. She had a passion for ballet but gave it up due to an injury. She also had a desire to become an idol but suppressed it. With the help of Nozomi and the other members, she embraces her true feelings. Eli becomes more open and expressive, strengthening her bond with the group.

Love Live CompetitionAnime Season 2

2014

μ's participates in the Love Live competition, with Eli playing a key role as choreographer. They ultimately win, but the group decides to disband after the third-years graduate. The group's final performance is a success, and they part ways on a high note.

SCP-3515 — Unearth

also known asUnearthSCP-3515
Typescp
CultureSCP Foundation
Who It Is
Form

SCP-3515 — Unearth is a humanoid figure with a body composed of dirt and debris. It has a face with sunken eyes and a mouth that appears to be made of dry earth. The figure is approximately 1.8 meters tall and has a weight that is roughly equivalent to a human of the same height.

Powers

Geological Manipulation SCP-3515 — Unearth is capable of manipulating the earth and geological structures to achieve its goals. It can create small earthquakes, cause buildings to collapse, and even manipulate the composition of the earth itself.

Incident Log

A team of researchers from the SCP Foundation discovered SCP-3515 — Unearth in a remote area of the desert. The team was initially unaware of the entity's existence and was caught off guard by its sudden appearance.

A user on the SCP subreddit shared a story about a group of researchers who discovered SCP-3515 — Unearth in a remote area of the desert. The story explores the entity's origins and its connection to geological anomalies.

Who It Is

A recurring dream experienced by thousands: standing in a field at night under a sky where stars are slowly going out, one by one. Each extinguishing star represents a human being replaced by an Iris entity. When the last star goes out, the dream ends. The instructional tape 'Sleep Image Visualizer' asks viewers to draw what they see — and the resulting images from hundreds of viewers are identical. The Iris is showing humanity a forecast.

Form

The dream: a flat featureless field under an unusually bright night sky. Stars flicker and go dark one by one. No sound. No wind. No time. The dreamer knows they are watching a countdown. Upon waking, the word 'IRIS' is written on their arm in their own handwriting.

Powers

Collective dream implantation across thousands

Forecast of human extinction timeline

Physical marking of dreamers (the word 'IRIS' on skin)

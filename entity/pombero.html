Who It Is
Form

A small, humanoid creature, typically depicted as having hairy feet, with some accounts describing it as having a connection to the sun or night.

Powers

Shape-shifting Pombero is said to have the ability to shape-shift into various forms, including animals and objects, allowing it to move undetected through the forest.

Accounts

Pombero is said to capture ungrateful girls and force them to kiss him, before later raping them.

Pombero is said to be the lord of the birds, and is often associated with the sun and the natural world.

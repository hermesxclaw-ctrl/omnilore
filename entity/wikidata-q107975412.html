Who It Is

He never hurries. While his partner Miss Merry Christmas shrieks and tunnels through solid rock, Mr. 4 lumbers forward, bat over his shoulder, speaking one syllable at a time — and then swings a four-ton iron bat so hard his exploding baseballs level palaces. In Eiichiro Oda's One Piece, the slowest talker in Baroque Works is also its heaviest hitter.

Canonically laconic to the point of comic extreme. Mr. 4 speaks and moves at an exaggeratedly slow tempo — his dialogue is drawn with elongated pauses and his actions are deliberately delayed, contrasting sharply with his hyperactive partner Miss Merry Christmas, who constantly berates him for his slowness. He is obedient, stoic, and unflappable; he rarely shows anger, fear, or excitement, responding to both orders and insults with a slow '...yeah.' Powers
4-Ton Baseball Bat Swing

Wields a black iron baseball bat explicitly stated to weigh 4 tons (4t batto). Despite its weight, Mr. 4 swings it with one hand at high velocity, generating enormous blunt-force impact capable of launching opponents and shattering stone. He uses it to bat Lassoo's exploding baseballs like a batter, combining pitching and hitting into a single artillery system.

Limit: Requires Lassoo to supply ammunition; bat itself is not explosive. Swing is powerful but telegraphed — Usopp is able to predict trajectory once he understands the baseball mechanic. Slow movement means he is vulnerable if disarmed or if Lassoo is incapacitated. No ranged attack without Lassoo.

Lassoo Artillery System (Inu Inu no Mi, Model: Dachshund Gun-Dog)

Lassoo is a bazooka/gun that ate the Inu Inu no Mi, Model: Dachshund, becoming a living dog-gun. Lassoo sneezes or coughs up heavy baseballs that are timed explosives; Mr. 4 then bats them at targets. The balls explode on impact or after a short fuse, with blast radius sufficient to destroy palace walls. Lassoo can also move independently, bite, and bark ('wan!'). The Devil Fruit was fed to the gun via Oda's established weapon-fruit technology (also used for Funkfreed and Smiley).

Limit: Lassoo has canine instincts and low intelligence; can be confused, frightened, or distracted (e.g., by Chopper's tricks). Explosive baseballs have a timed fuse — Usopp exploits this by using a dial-like timing. Lassoo must be loaded/fed; cannot fire indefinitely without Mr. 4's batting. As a living gun, Lassoo is vulnerable to blunt damage.

Superhuman Strength and Durability

Even without a Devil Fruit, Mr. 4 displays superhuman physical strength to lift and swing the 4-ton bat casually and superhuman durability to withstand Usopp's attacks and Chopper's Horn Point strikes before being defeated by a combined Usopp-Chopper finisher (Chopper's Heavy Gong + Usopp's hammer). He continues fighting after taking direct hits that would incapacitate normal humans.

Limit: Not invulnerable; defeated by coordinated attack from two Straw Hats (Usopp's Usopp Hammer and Chopper's attack that sends him flying). Slow reflexes mean he cannot easily evade agile opponents. No Haki is ever shown or stated.

Slow-Tempo Combat Style (Tactical Deception)

His exaggerated slowness functions as a psychological and tactical trait: opponents misjudge timing, and his partner Miss Merry Christmas compensates with rapid tunneling and mole-like ambushes. The duo's synergy — she digs tunnels and destabilizes footing with her Mole-Mole Fruit, he provides heavy artillery from stable ground — makes them more dangerous together than individually.

Limit: Highly dependent on partnership; alone, his slowness is a liability against fast opponents like Sanji or Zoro (who never fight him). Miss Merry Christmas's constant scolding suggests coordination friction.

Accounts I
Baroque Works Officer Agents Assemble — Spiders CafeOne Piece manga Chapter 160-167; Anime Episodes 103-104

2000 (publication); in-universe: Alabasta Saga, c. 1522 Age of the Sea

Following the failure of the Unluckies and Mr. 3 to eliminate Princess Vivi and the Straw Hats, Crocodile summons his remaining Officer Agents to Spiders Cafe, an oasis restaurant serving as a front. Mr. 4 arrives silently with his partner Miss Merry Christmas (a short, stout woman with the Mole-Mole Fruit) and the gun-dog Lassoo. Along with Mr. 1 and Miss Doublefinger, and Mr. 2 Bon Kurei, they receive orders to intercept Vivi and prevent her from reaching Alabasta. Mr. 4 speaks only a single slow affirmation while Miss Merry Christmas does the talking, establishing their comic dynamic. The group then departs for Alabasta via desert travel. Sets up the Alabasta palace battle; establishes Mr. 4/Miss Merry Christmas as the final Officer Agent duo the Straw Hats will face before Crocodile himself. Reveals Baroque Works hierarchy and baseball/mole thematic pairing.

Desert Pursuit and Infiltration of AlubarnaOne Piece manga Chapters 171-181; Anime Episodes 107-112

2000-2001

Mr. 4 and Miss Merry Christmas travel with the other Officer Agents to Alubarna, capital of Alabasta. While Crocodile manipulates the rebel army and royal army into war, the Officer Agents are tasked with holding the palace and eliminating Vivi's allies. Mr. 4 is largely in the background during the desert crossing, carrying Lassoo and his bat, occasionally sneezing from Lassoo's fur. His presence is used for tension — the Straw Hats know powerful agents await in the capital. Positions Mr. 4 at the palace for the decisive battle; demonstrates Baroque Works' coordinated occupation of Alubarna.

Battle at the Alabasta Palace — Mr. 4 and Miss Merry Christmas vs Usopp and ChopperOne Piece manga Chapters 184-188: 'Mole Banana,' '4-Ton Bat,' 'Mole Hole'; Anime Episodes 114-116

2001

In the palace grounds, Mr. 4 and Miss Merry Christmas ambush Usopp and Tony Tony Chopper. Miss Merry Christmas uses her Mole-Mole Fruit (Mogu Mogu no Mi) to dig tunnels and create pit traps, popping up to attack, while Mr. 4 stands on solid ground batting Lassoo's exploding baseballs at the pair. Usopp and Chopper are initially overwhelmed — the explosions and mole ambushes keep them off balance, and Mr. 4's bat sends them flying. Usopp discovers the timing mechanism: Lassoo's balls explode after a set fuse, and Mr. 4's swing timing is predictable. Chopper uses his Horn Point and later Heavy Gong, while Usopp uses his hammer and clever use of a tunnel to redirect a baseball. In the climax, Usopp smashes Miss Merry Christmas with his 5-ton hammer (Usopp Hammer, falsely claimed as 5 tons but actually 2 kg — a bluff), and Chopper sends Mr. 4 flying with a powerful headbutt/uppercut combo, defeating both. Accounts II
Aftermath: Arrest and ImprisonmentOne Piece manga Chapter 211: 'King'; Anime Episode 128

2001

After Crocodile's defeat and the fall of Baroque Works, Marine Captain Hina and her forces arrive to arrest the defeated Officer Agents. Mr. 4, Miss Merry Christmas, Mr. 5, Miss Valentine, and others are shown bound and taken into custody. Mr. 4 is depicted still dazed, being carried due to his weight. This marks the official dissolution of Baroque Works as an organization and the transfer of its agents to Marine justice. Confirms Baroque Works' defeat has legal consequences; sets up later cover story where agents are in prison or escaped.

Miss Goldenweek's 'Operation: Meet Baroque Works' — Reunion and New BeginningOne Piece manga Cover Story serial, Chapters 359-413 (collected in Tankōbon Vols. 38-42); Anime Episodes 242-245 (partial adaptation)

2003 (publication); in-universe: months after Alabasta

In this 30-chapter cover story drawn by Oda on manga title pages, Miss Goldenweek (Marianne) uses her Colors Trap art to free her former comrades from prison. Mr. 4 appears in chapters 382-383 and 413: he is freed alongside Miss Merry Christmas, Mr. 5, and Miss Valentine, and the group re-establishes a new Baroque Works-like hideout. They open a new Spiders Cafe on Kyuka Island, with Mr. 4 working as a shop assistant/pitcher, still slow but now in a peaceful role. The final panel shows the reformed group — including Mr. 4 holding Lassoo — living as outlaws-turned-civilians, no longer under Crocodile. Provides canonical post-Alabasta fate for Mr. 4: not permanently imprisoned, but living in semi-retirement with former comrades. Confirms Oda's intent that Baroque Works agents are not irredeemably evil. Often cited as evidence of Oda's cover-story worldbuilding.

Legacy Cameo — Baroque Works in Alabasta Flashbacks and DatabooksOne Piece manga Chapters 540, 557 (Impel Down / Marineford flashbacks), Vivre Card, One Piece Magazine

2009-2018

Mr. 4 makes brief cameo flashbacks when Crocodile's past is recalled during Impel Down and Marineford arcs, reminding readers of his former subordinates. He does not actively return to the main storyline. Databooks (Vivre Card, One Piece Green, Magazine Vol. 6) revisit his design with new Oda sketches, confirming his stats and that he has not gained a bounty or new powers. His baseball motif is referenced in later SBS when Oda discusses Baroque Works design themes. Keeps Mr. 4 in continuity without reintroducing him; cements his status as Alabasta-specific antagonist whose arc is complete.

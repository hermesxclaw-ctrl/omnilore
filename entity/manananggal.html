Who It Is

The manananggal is a terrifying witch-like creature in Philippine folklore that can sever its upper body from its lower half, sprout bat-like wings, and fly into the night to prey on sleeping humans. By day, she appears as a normal woman — often beautiful, married, and living in the community. By night, she rubs a special oil on her body that allows the upper torso to detach, leaving the lower half standing in place. She flies to the homes of pregnant women, extending an impossibly long, hollow tongue to suck the blood of fetuses or the hearts of the sick. The manananggal can be defeated by finding her lower body and sprinkling salt, garlic, or ashes on it — preventing her from rejoining and trapping her at dawn, when she dies in the sunlight. The legend is deeply rooted in rural Philippine communities and continues to appear in Filipino horror films, comics, and television.

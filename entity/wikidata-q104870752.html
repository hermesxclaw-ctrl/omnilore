Who It Is

In the sprawling multiverse of CLAMP's storytelling, few names echo across worlds as persistently as Syaoran—a hero whose identity shifts with every incarnation, from a determined young magician in one reality to a cursed swordsman in another, yet always bound by a single, unyielding will to protect those he loves.

Syaoran Li is initially competitive, arrogant, and short-tempered, especially toward Sakura Kinomoto, but he gradually becomes caring, protective, and loyal. He has a strong sense of duty and honor. Syaoran of Tsubasa is more solemn, burdened by his mission to retrieve Sakura's memories, but he is equally determined and kind-hearted, often putting others before himself.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Syaoran' is a romanization of the Japanese 小狼 (Shaoran), which literally means 'small wolf'. It was chosen by CLAMP to evoke a wild, determined nature. In Chinese contexts, it may be interpreted as 小狼 (xiǎo láng), meaning 'little wolf'.

Powers
Elemental Magic (Cardcaptor Sakura)

Syaoran Li is a skilled magician who can control elements, primarily lightning and wind. He uses these abilities in combat and to capture Clow Cards. He also has enhanced physical abilities and is proficient in martial arts.

Limit: His magic is initially weaker than Sakura's, and he requires a magical focus (like a sword or talisman) to channel it effectively. He also lacks the ability to transform cards until later in the series.

Swordmanship and Combat Skills (Tsubasa)

Syaoran of Tsubasa is an expert swordsman, able to hold his own against powerful foes. He wields a sword that he can also use to channel magical energy. In the manga, he develops a unique ability to create weapons from his own blood, which is a powerful but dangerous technique.

Limit: The blood weapon ability causes him physical harm and is a last resort. His combat skills are not supernatural by themselves, relying on training and determination.

Dimensional Travel (Tsubasa)

As a traveler across worlds, Syaoran can move between dimensions, but this is usually facilitated by the witch Yūko Ichihara or other means, not an innate ability. He also has a strong will that allows him to resist certain magical influences.

Limit: He cannot travel freely without assistance, and the journey takes a toll on his memory and strength.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Syaoran Li (Cardcaptor Sakura) is a young boy with spiky dark brown hair and amber eyes. He often wears traditional Chinese clothing, such as a blue and white outfit with a high collar, reflecting his heritage as a descendant of Clow Reed. Syaoran of Tsubasa: Reservoir Chronicle has a similar appearance but with slightly different clothing: a black and red outfit with a cape, and he carries a sword. Domains — In Cardcaptor Sakura, Syaoran Li lives in Tomoeda, Japan, having moved from Hong Kong. In Tsubasa, Syaoran travels across multiple dimensions and worlds, including Clow Country, Piffle World, and others, as part of his journey.

Accounts I
Cardcaptor Sakura - Rivalry and FriendshipCardcaptor Sakura manga/anime

1996-2000 (manga), 1998-2000 (anime)

Syaoran Li arrives in Tomoeda from Hong Kong with the goal of capturing the Clow Cards, believing he is the rightful heir to Clow Reed's legacy. He initially clashes with Sakura Kinomoto, the Cardcaptor, but gradually respects her and becomes her ally. He develops romantic feelings for Sakura, which he initially struggles to express. He helps her in the Final Judgment and later supports her as she faces new challenges. Syaoran's rivalry turns into a deep bond, and he becomes a key figure in Sakura's life. His feelings for Sakura are reciprocated by the end of the series, leading to a romantic relationship.

Accounts II
Tsubasa: Reservoir Chronicle - The Journey for MemoriesTsubasa: Reservoir Chronicle manga/anime

2003-2009 (manga), 2005-2006 (anime)

Syaoran, a young archaeologist from Clow Country, is in love with Princess Sakura. When Sakura's memories are scattered across dimensions as feathers, Syaoran makes a deal with the witch Yūko Ichihara to travel across worlds to retrieve them. He is accompanied by Kurogane, Fai, and Mokona. Throughout the journey, he faces many trials, including the revelation that he is a clone created by Fei-Wang Reed, and that the real Syaoran is trapped in a time loop. He ultimately sacrifices his own existence to save Sakura, but is later restored in the sequel. Syaoran's sacrifice and eventual restoration redefine his identity. He learns the truth about his origins and chooses his own path, becoming a free individual.

Syaoran is a Chinese character based on real Chinese mythology. — Syaoran is a Japanese fictional character created by CLAMP. While his name and clothing are inspired by Chinese culture, he is not based on any real mythological figure.

Syaoran and Sakura are siblings. — In both Cardcaptor Sakura and Tsubasa, Syaoran and Sakura are not related by blood. They are romantic interests.

Syaoran is a villain in Tsubasa. — Syaoran is the protagonist of Tsubasa, though he has dark moments and is manipulated by villains. He is ultimately heroic.

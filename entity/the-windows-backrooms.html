Who It Is

A phenomenon: windows in Backrooms walls showing impossible vistas — a field of wheat under a blood-red sky, an infinite library, a city made of bone, an Earth where every face wears a white mask. Prolonged looking pulls the observer's consciousness through, leaving the body to starve. Some Windows show worlds worse than the Backrooms.

Form

Perfectly normal windows appearing in walls that should not have windows. Scenes beyond the glass are never the same twice and never show anything that exists in the Backrooms.

Powers

Consciousness displacement into alternate dimensions

Display of locations across possible realities

Bodily abandonment leading to death

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

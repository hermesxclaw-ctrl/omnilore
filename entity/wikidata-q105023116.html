Etymology

The name is a composite of several elements. 'Kibanda' may derive from Kimbundu or Kikongo, Bantu languages spoken in Angola and the Congo, where it could mean 'hut', 'shelter', or 'small house', or it might be a proper name. 'Rondo' could be a variant of 'Ronda' (patrol) or a personal name. 'Rei' is Portuguese for 'king'. 'Vissoura' is a common Portuguese variant of 'vassoura', meaning 'broom'. Thus, the name might be interpreted as 'Kibanda Rondo, King of the Broom', but this is speculative.

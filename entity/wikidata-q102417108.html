The Approach

In the glittering court of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica, where nobles scheme and magic flows like wine, there is a young woman whose every smile hides a blade. Mimi Pearlbaton, the bubbly, pink-haired demihuman of the Margrave's inner circle, seems at first a cheerful child playing at politics. But beneath her playful exterior lies a seasoned warrior and a heart loyal to a cause greater than herself. Her story is one of friendship, sacrifice, and the quiet strength of those who choose to stand beside the powerful, even when the world is against them.

Content advisory. None. Mimi Pearlbaton is a supporting character in a fantasy series, with no dark or disturbing material associated with her.
Identity

Mimi Pearlbaton

Pronunciation MEE-mee PEARL-bah-ton

Etymology

The name 'Mimi' is a common Japanese term for 'ears', likely referencing her demihuman traits. 'Pearlbaton' suggests a combination of 'pearl' and 'baton', possibly symbolizing her role as a messenger or her bright, cheerful personality.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese light novel and anime franchise, created by Tappei Nagatsuki.

Disputed Identifications

None known. Mimi is a minor character with a clear identity.

Description

Appearance

Mimi is a young demihuman with pink hair, which she wears in twin tails. She has a petite stature and often wears a military-style uniform, reflecting her role as a knight. Her most distinctive feature is her large, expressive eyes and a constant cheerful smile.

Symbols & Iconography

As a knight of the Kingdom of Lugunica, Mimi is associated with the insignia of the royal guard. Her pink hair and bright demeanor make her easily recognizable.

Behaviour & Personality

Mimi is extremely cheerful, energetic, and seemingly naive. She often speaks in a bubbly manner and uses the suffix '-nanodesu' in the Japanese version, adding to her childlike charm. However, she is a skilled fighter and fiercely loyal to her friends, especially to Garfiel, whom she has a crush on. She is also protective of her brother, Hetaro, and her sister, Tivey.

Domains & Habitats

Mimi is typically found in the royal capital of Lugunica, serving as a knight. She accompanies Garfiel and the Emilia Camp during various events, such as the royal selection ceremonies.

Who It Is
Form

Trolls are often depicted as large, grotesque, and ugly beings, typically with green or gray skin, sharp teeth, and long hair. They are said to dwell in isolated areas of rocks, mountains, or caves. In some stories, they are described as having a more humanoid appearance, with a larger build and a menacing gaze.

Powers

Regenerative Abilities Trolls are said to possess regenerative abilities, allowing them to heal from injuries and regenerate lost limbs.

Accounts

A group of brave warriors venture into the troll's lair, seeking to slay the fearsome creature. However, they soon discover that the troll is not as easy to defeat as they thought.

A young girl befriends a troll, who teaches her the secrets of the wilderness and the elements. However, their friendship is soon discovered by the other villagers, who seek to harm the troll.

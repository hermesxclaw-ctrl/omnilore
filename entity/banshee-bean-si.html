Who It Is

A female spirit in Irish folklore who heralds the death of a family member, usually by screaming, wailing, shrieking, or keening. The name derives from Old Irish 'ben síde' — 'woman of the fairy mound' — connecting her to the sídhe, the ancient burial mounds and fairy dwellings of Ireland. Banshees are attached to families of pure Gaelic descent (the O'Neills, O'Briens, O'Connors, etc.) and appear as either a beautiful young woman or a withered crone. Some accounts describe her as appearing in one of three guises: a young woman, a stately matron, or a raddled old hag — the triple aspects of the Morrígan, the Celtic war goddess. Her cry, the 'caoine,' is said to be the most mournful sound in creation.

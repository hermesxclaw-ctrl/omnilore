Who It Is

He left Wano as a dancing child in a kimono, returned as a gunman in the world's strongest pirate crew, and died as a samurai keeping a dead lord's promise — Izou is One Piece's most elegant contradiction: a Wano loyalist who spent twenty years at sea, a cross-dressing marksman who never missed, and a brother who chose the Whitebeard Pirates over home until home needed him one last time.

Attested personality traits: deeply loyal, stoic, and duty-bound, with a quiet, elegant demeanor. Unlike more boisterous Whitebeard commanders (Marco, Ace), Izou is reserved and formal, speaking politely even in battle. He shows fierce loyalty to two masters: Kozuki Oden and Edward Newgate (Whitebeard), and later to the Kozuki family as a whole. He is protective of his younger sibling Kikunojo (Kiku), though their separation for 20 years is a source of quiet grief. Powers
Master Marksmanship - Dual Flintlock Pistols

Izou's primary combat style is wielding two flintlock pistols simultaneously, firing with superhuman accuracy, speed, and the ability to curve or guide bullets. He can shoot multiple opponents in rapid succession, intercept cannonballs/musket volleys mid-air, and fight at close range using pistols as melee weapons. At Marineford he is shown shooting down Marine bullets and fighting on par with high-ranking Marines.

Limit: Relies on ammunition and functional flintlocks; not shown to create infinite ammo. Requires line of sight. No Devil Fruit enhancement; purely skill-based. Vulnerable in close-quarters sword combat against master swordsmen (shown struggling against Kaido's forces when overwhelmed).

Busoshoku Haki (Armament Haki) - Imbued Bullets

As a New World veteran and Whitebeard commander, Izou is confirmed to use Armament Haki. He imbues his bullets with Haki to pierce Logia and tough Zoan defenses, shown when his shots affect Kaido's Beast Pirates Gifters and when he wounds Kaido's forces alongside the Scabbards. Vivre Card and manga imply high-level Haki proficiency.

Limit: Haki drains stamina; not shown to use advanced forms like Internal Destruction or Conqueror's coating. Bullets still need to hit; Haki does not auto-target.

Kenbunshoku Haki (Observation Haki)

Presumed competent in Observation Haki as a high-ranking Whitebeard commander, allowing him to sense presence and dodge attacks. Not explicitly demonstrated in a labeled panel, but required for New World combat parity.

Limit: No attested Future Sight. Range and precision unknown; not his primary specialty.

Samurai Swordsmanship (Basic) and Physical Prowess

Trained as a Kozuki retainer, Izou has basic sword skills and high physical durability. He survived Marineford War, clashed with World Government forces, and fought on Onigashima for hours against Beast Pirates. He is agile enough to fight while in kimono and geta.

Limit: Not a master swordsman like Kin'emon or Denjiro; his sword use is secondary to guns. Endurance has limits - ultimately dies from blood loss after being shot by CP0 agent Guernica (Maha) in Chapter 1050-1051.

Leadership and Tactical Command (16th Division Commander)

As commander of Whitebeard's 16th Division, Izou led a crew division, coordinated fleet actions, and was responsible for territory defense. He is shown commanding squads at Marineford and later coordinating with Marco and the Scabbards at Onigashima.

Limit: Rank is within Whitebeard Pirates only; does not grant authority after Whitebeard's death. Division size and exact territory never detailed - gap.

Accounts I
Childhood in Wano - The Dancing SiblingsOne Piece manga Chapter 963-964 (Oden flashback)

Manga publication 2019; in-universe ~41 years before present (Oden's youth)

Izou and his younger sibling Kikunojo are introduced as children of a traveling dance troupe in Wano's Ringo region. Orphaned and starving after their father's death (a former criminal), they survive by dancing on streets. Their feminine presentation causes them to be ostracized and nearly arrested. Kozuki Oden, the daimyo's delinquent son, finds them, offers them food, and takes them as his first retainers after being moved by their performance and plight. Izou, the elder, is initially protective and distrustful but becomes fiercely loyal to Oden. Establishes Izou's origin, sibling bond, and lifelong debt to Oden. Explains why he later identifies as both samurai and performer. Sets up his departure from Wano.

Service Under Kozuki Oden and the Whitebeard PiratesOne Piece manga Chapters 964-968

Manga 2019-2020; in-universe 33-30 years before present

Izou grows into Oden's retainer alongside Kin'emon, Denjiro, and others (later the Nine Red Scabbards). When Oden sneaks aboard Whitebeard's ship to see the world, Izou stows away to stay with his lord, despite Kin'emon's protests. Whitebeard (Edward Newgate) initially wants to throw him off, but Oden vouches for him. Izou becomes a member of the Whitebeard Pirates, serving Oden directly. When Oden later transfers to Gol D. Roger's crew for the final voyage, Izou chooses to stay with Whitebeard rather than follow Oden, stating his loyalty is now to Whitebeard as well - a painful but mutual separation. This marks his 20-year absence from Wano. Izou becomes a Whitebeard division commander, cutting ties with Wano. He misses Oden's execution and the Scabbards' 20-year wait, creating survivor's guilt that drives his later return.

The Battle of Marineford - War of the BestOne Piece manga Chapters 551-580; Anime Episodes 460-489

Manga 2009-2010; in-universe 2 years before present

Izou appears as one of the 16 Whitebeard division commanders arriving at Marineford to rescue Portgas D. Ace. He is introduced in a double-page spread with other commanders. During the war he fights Marines and Vice Admirals, using dual pistols to shoot down enemy fire and cover Luffy's advance. He is shown alongside Marco and Vista. After Whitebeard and Ace die, Izou retreats with the surviving Whitebeard Pirates under Shanks' intervention. This is his first present-timeline appearance, establishing him as a New World veteran. Demonstrates Izou's combat level and status. Sets up post-war Whitebeard Pirates' decline and his eventual decision to return to Wano after learning of Oden's will via newspaper.

Accounts II
The Payback War and ExileOne Piece manga Chapter 820 (mention), Chapter 909 (cover story), Vivre Card and One Piece Magazine

Manga 2016-2018; in-universe 1 year after Marineford

After Marineford, the Whitebeard Pirates under Marco lead a war against Blackbeard (Marshall D. Teach) to avenge Whitebeard - the Payback War. They are decisively defeated. The crew disperses. Izou's whereabouts during this year are not fully depicted, but he survives and goes into hiding. He later learns from the news of the Kozuki family's plight and the alliance forming to retake Wano. Unlike Marco who initially hesitates, Izou decides to return to Wano to fulfill his duty as a Kozuki retainer, reuniting with the Scabbards after 20 years. Explains his absence between Marineford and Wano. Motivates his return as atonement for not being there when Oden died.

Return to Wano and the Raid on OnigashimaOne Piece manga Chapters 975-995, 997-1007

Manga 2019-2021; in-universe present timeline

Izou arrives in Wano with Marco aboard the Whitebeard Pirates' former ship, reuniting emotionally with Kikunojo after 20 years. He joins the Ninja-Pirate-Mink-Samurai Alliance's raid on Onigashima, Kaido's island fortress. He fights Beast Pirates Gifters, uses Haki-imbued bullets to shoot down enemies, and protects the Scabbards. He has a poignant reunion with Kiku, who has lived as a woman while he has lived as a cross-dressing man - their sibling bond is affirmed without judgment. He fights alongside the Scabbards against Kaido and later holds off Kaido's forces to allow others to advance. Reintegrates Izou into Wano's narrative and resolves his 20-year separation. Shows his dual identity as pirate and samurai finally reconciled.

Death on Onigashima - The Cross and the AgentOne Piece manga Chapters 1050-1051, 1057 (confirmation)

Manga 2022; in-universe present

During the final stages of the Onigashima raid, Izou encounters CP0 agents Guernica (Maha) and Joseph, who have infiltrated the island to capture Robin. To protect the alliance, Izou intercepts them. Already wounded from earlier battles, he sacrifices himself: he shoots and kills Maha (Guernica) but is fatally shot in return. He dies standing, having bought time for others. His death is confirmed in Chapter 1051 by Kawamatsu and later in the Wano epilogue where his grave is shown alongside Ashura Doji's. His final act is framed as a samurai's death - fulfilling his duty to the Kozuki family. Izou's death closes his arc: the retainer who left Wano finally dies defending it. His death, alongside Ashura Doji, underscores the cost of the Wano liberation. His bounty and status end here; his will is carried by Kiku.

Izou is the same as Kikunojo or they are the same character at different ages — They are distinct siblings: Izou is the elder brother, Kikunojo (Kiku) is the younger sibling. They share a backstory and similar aesthetics but have separate lives - Izou left Wano for 20 years, Kiku stayed and became one of the Nine Red Scabbards.

Izou is a Devil Fruit user — No Devil Fruit is attested for Izou. His abilities are entirely Haki and marksmanship. Vivre Card and manga never list a Fruit for him.

Izou survived Onigashima and will return — Izou is confirmed dead as of Chapter 1051 and the Wano epilogue (Chapter 1057) where his and Ashura Doji's graves are shown. Oda rarely revives characters after such explicit grave confirmation.

Izou's bounty was never revealed or is low — Vivre Card confirms his bounty is 510,000,000 Beli, reflecting his status as a Whitebeard commander. It was not stated in manga dialogue but is official databook canon.

Izou was always a pirate and not a samurai — Izou is both: originally a samurai retainer of Kozuki Oden in Wano, then a pirate under Whitebeard for 20 years, then returned to his samurai duty in Wano. His identity is deliberately dual, and he dies as a samurai fulfilling Oden's will.

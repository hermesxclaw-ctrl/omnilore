Who It Is

Aki Hayakawa is a Public Safety Devil Hunter and one of the three central characters (alongside Denji and Power) of Chainsaw Man Part 1. He is introduced as the cool, professional, emotionally distant senior agent who is forced to mentor Denji and Power — two chaotic, immature forces that he initially despises. Aki is stern, dutiful, and carries the weight of a tragic past: his entire family was killed by the Gun Devil, and he has devoted his life to revenge. Aki's power comes from devil contracts. He holds contracts with multiple devils: the Curse Devil (a nail-driven curse that kills both the target and the user with three strikes), the Fox Devil (who can devour enemies on Aki's command but only if properly respected), and the Future Devil (who gives Aki glimpses of the future but is relentlessly mocking and nihilistic). His signature weapon is a katana, and he is a highly skilled swordsman. Aki's character arc is devastating. He is initially cold and resentful of Denji, seeing him as a nuisance. But over time, he becomes Denji and Power's caretaker — the big brother figure who makes them food, scolds them for their messes, and protects them from danger. He grows to love them, despite himself. Aki's tragedy is that his quest for revenge against the Gun Devil is ultimately meaningless: the Gun Devil is killed by others, and Aki's contract with the Future Devil shows him a future where he is killed by Chainsaw Man. In the end, Aki is turned into the Gun Fiend by Makima's machinations, and Denji is forced to kill him — a heartbreaking conclusion to the story of a man who just wanted to protect the people he loved. Aki represents the tragedy of revenge and the cost of caring. He is a man who hardened his heart to avoid being hurt again, only to open it to two impossible people — and lose everything because of it. His last act, as the Gun Fiend, is to reach for his katana — a dying echo of the man who once fought to protect.

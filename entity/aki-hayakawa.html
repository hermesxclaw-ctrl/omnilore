Who It Is

Aki Hayakawa is a Public Safety Devil Hunter and one of the three central characters (alongside Denji and Power) of Chainsaw Man Part 1. He is introduced as the cool, professional, emotionally distant senior agent who is forced to mentor Denji and Power — two chaotic, immature forces that he initially despises. Aki is stern, dutiful, and carries the weight of a tragic past: his entire family was killed by the Gun Devil, and he has devoted his life to revenge. Aki's power comes from devil contracts. He holds contracts with multiple devils: the Curse Devil (a nail-driven curse that kills both the target and the user with three strikes), the Fox Devil (who can devour enemies on Aki's command but only if properly respected), and the Future Devil (who gives Aki glimpses of the future but is relentlessly mocking and nihilistic). His signature weapon is a katana, and he is a highly skilled swordsman. Aki's character arc is devastating. He is initially cold and resentful of Denji, seeing him as a nuisance. But over time, he becomes Denji and Power's caretaker — the big brother figure who makes them food, scolds them for their messes, and protects them from danger. He grows to love them, despite himself. Aki's tragedy is that his quest for revenge against the Gun Devil is ultimately meaningless: the Gun Devil is killed by others, and Aki's contract with the Future Devil shows him a future where he is killed by Chainsaw Man. In the end, Aki is turned into the Gun Fiend by Makima's machinations, and Denji is forced to kill him — a heartbreaking conclusion to the story of a man who just wanted to protect the people he loved. Aki represents the tragedy of revenge and the cost of caring. He is a man who hardened his heart to avoid being hurt again, only to open it to two impossible people — and lose everything because of it. His last act, as the Gun Fiend, is to reach for his katana — a dying echo of the man who once fought to protect.

The Approach

Aki Hayakawa is a man who sold his future for a chance at vengeance. Armed with a cursed sword that devours his lifespan, he hunts the Gun Devil—the entity that murdered his family—while slowly realizing that the cost of his obsession may be everything he has left, including his humanity.

Content advisory. Violence, death, grief, self-destructive behavior, existential horror, body horror, psychological trauma.
Identity

Aki Hayakawa

Pronunciation AH-kee hah-yah-KAH-wah

Known As

AkiGun Fiend (temporary)

Etymology

Aki (秋) means 'autumn' in Japanese, possibly reflecting his season of tragedy. Hayakawa (早川) is a common Japanese surname.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Tatsuki Fujimoto, published by Shueisha in Weekly Shōnen Jump. Part of the manga 'Chainsaw Man'.

Disputed Identifications

No significant disputes. His identity is consistent across the manga.

Description

Appearance

A tall, lean young man with sharp features, dark eyes, and medium-length dark hair often tied back. He typically wears a black suit with a tie, standard for Public Safety Devil Hunters. His most distinctive feature is a scar on his chin. As the Gun Fiend, his appearance becomes monstrous, with gun barrels protruding from his body and a distorted form.

Symbols & Iconography

The Curse Devil's sword, a katana with a cursed hilt. His smoking habit is iconic, often shown with a cigarette. The snow and winter motifs are associated with his tragic fate.

Behaviour & Personality

Initially cold, serious, and driven by revenge. He is dedicated to killing the Gun Devil, often to the point of recklessness. Over time, he shows a caring side, forming bonds with Denji and Power, and becomes more protective. He struggles with his mortality and the cost of his contract.

Domains & Habitats

Primarily Tokyo, in the Public Safety Devil Hunter headquarters and his apartment. He also visits graveyards and the site of his family's death.

Version Differences

No major differences across versions. In the anime adaptation, his character is faithfully portrayed.

Powers & Abilities

Curse Devil Contract

Source Version Manga Part 1

Description

Aki made a contract with the Curse Devil. By stabbing his target three times with a cursed sword, the Curse Devil appears and crushes the victim. The cost is several years of Aki's lifespan per use.

Limitations

Requires the sword and three successful stabs. Drains lifespan, making it a suicide weapon.

Contradictions

None

Future Devil Contract

Source Version Manga Part 1

Description

After the Future Devil is captured, Aki makes a contract to see a few seconds into the future. This is displayed through a floating eye that appears near him.

Limitations

The future sight is limited and often shows grim outcomes, causing psychological distress. The Future Devil taunts him.

Contradictions

None

Fox Devil Contract

Source Version Manga Part 1

Description

Initially, Aki had a contract with the Fox Devil, allowing him to summon her head to bite enemies. The contract is later severed.

Limitations

The Fox Devil can refuse if she deems the target too dangerous, and she is selective about who she favors.

Contradictions

None

Gun Fiend Transformation

Source Version Manga Part 1

Description

After being possessed by the Gun Devil, Aki becomes the Gun Fiend, gaining immense power but losing his humanity. He can shoot bullets from his body and has enhanced strength.

Limitations

He is no longer himself; his consciousness is suppressed. He is ultimately killed by Denji.

Contradictions

None

Contradictions & Disputes

Aki's death prophecy

Position A

The Future Devil's prophecy is absolute and cannot be avoided.

Position A — evidence

The Future Devil shows Aki his death, and it happens exactly as shown.

Position B

Aki's death is a result of his choices and Makima's manipulation, suggesting the prophecy is self-fulfilling.

Position B — evidence

Aki's actions, driven by revenge, lead him to the situation where he dies.

Scholarly Consensus

The prophecy is a narrative device that highlights the inevitability of tragedy in the story.

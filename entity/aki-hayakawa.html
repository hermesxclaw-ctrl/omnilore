Etymology

The surname 'Hayakawa' (早川) is a common Japanese surname, with 'haya' meaning 'early' or 'fast' and 'kawa' meaning 'river'. The given name 'Aki' (秋) means 'autumn'.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Tatsuki Fujimoto, published by Shueisha in Weekly Shōnen Jump. Part of the manga and anime franchise 'Chainsaw Man'.

Archive Dossier — Approach

Aki Hayakawa was a man who lived with a blade in one hand and a burning promise in the other—a Public Safety Devil Hunter whose every step was shadowed by the ghost of his family and the ticking clock of a curse that would one day claim him. His story is a tragedy written in blood, devotion, and the cruel arithmetic of devil contracts.

I made a contract with the Curse Devil. If I drive these nails into someone, they die after three strikes. But there's a catch: I die too. I always thought killing the Gun Devil would be worth it. But now... I don't want to die anymore.

Who It Is

Aki Hayakawa is a Public Safety Devil Hunter and one of the three central characters (alongside Denji and Power) of Chainsaw Man Part 1. He is introduced as the cool, professional, emotionally distant senior agent who is forced to mentor Denji and Power — two chaotic, immature forces that he initially despises. Aki is stern, dutiful, and carries the weight of a tragic past: his entire family was killed by the Gun Devil, and he has devoted his life to revenge. Aki's power comes from devil contracts. He holds contracts with multiple devils: the Curse Devil (a nail-driven curse that kills both the target and the user with three strikes), the Fox Devil (who can devour enemies on Aki's command but only if properly respected), and the Future Devil (who gives Aki glimpses of the future but is relentlessly mocking and nihilistic). His signature weapon is a katana, and he is a highly skilled swordsman. Aki's character arc is devastating. He is initially cold and resentful of Denji, seeing him as a nuisance. But over time, he becomes Denji and Power's caretaker — the big brother figure who makes them food, scolds them for their messes, and protects them from danger. He grows to love them, despite himself. Aki's tragedy is that his quest for revenge against the Gun Devil is ultimately meaningless: the Gun Devil is killed by others, and Aki's contract with the Future Devil shows him a future where he is killed by Chainsaw Man. In the end, Aki is turned into the Gun Fiend by Makima's machinations, and Denji is forced to kill him — a heartbreaking conclusion to the story of a man who just wanted to protect the people he loved. Aki represents the tragedy of revenge and the cost of caring. He is a man who hardened his heart to avoid being hurt again, only to open it to two impossible people — and lose everything because of it. His last act, as the Gun Fiend, is to reach for his katana — a dying echo of the man who once fought to protect.

Archive Dossier — Description

Appearance

Aki is a tall, young man with long, dark hair often tied back in a low ponytail. He has sharp, serious eyes and a perpetually solemn expression. He typically wears the standard Public Safety Devil Hunter uniform: a dark suit with a white shirt and tie. Later, he is seen with a scar on his face and a more disheveled look as his life spirals. As the Gun Fiend, his appearance becomes monstrous, with gun barrels protruding from his body.

Symbols & Iconography

His katana, which he uses in combat. The fox paw prints associated with his contract with the Fox Devil. The nail and hammer used in his Curse Devil contract.

Behaviour & Personality

Aki is stoic, serious, and often cold towards others, especially Denji and Power, whom he finds immature and reckless. He is extremely dedicated to his duty as a Devil Hunter and is driven by a deep-seated desire for revenge against the Gun Devil, who killed his family. He is pragmatic and willing to make sacrifices to achieve his goals. Despite his tough exterior, he shows compassion and care for his comrades, particularly Himeno and later Denji and Power, forming a makeshift family with them. He is also prone to smoking and has a dry sense of humor.

Domains & Habitats

Public Safety Devil Hunter headquarters, his apartment, various devil-hunting missions across Tokyo.

Version Differences

The anime adaptation closely follows the manga's depiction of Aki. His voice actors differ between Japanese and English, but his characterization remains consistent.

Archive Dossier — Powers

Fox Devil Contract

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

Aki can summon the Fox Devil's head or jaws to bite and devour enemies. The Fox Devil requires payment in the form of skin from Aki's body.

Limitations

The Fox Devil only grants its head to attractive people, and Aki must offer a part of his body as payment.

Contradictions

None known.

Curse Devil Contract

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

Aki can use a sword with a nail through it to stab an enemy three times, summoning the Curse Devil to crush the victim. This costs several years of Aki's lifespan.

Limitations

Requires three consecutive stabs and shortens the user's lifespan.

Contradictions

None known.

Future Devil Contract

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

Aki gains the ability to see a few seconds into the future, which he uses to enhance his combat abilities. The Future Devil can also communicate with him and show him visions of his death.

Limitations

Only shows a few seconds ahead, and the Future Devil is mischievous and may show unwanted visions.

Contradictions

None known.

Gun Fiend Transformation

Source Version Manga only (anime has not reached this point)

Description

After being possessed by the Gun Devil, Aki transforms into the Gun Fiend, a monstrous entity with gun barrels covering his body, capable of firing devastating attacks.

Limitations

Aki loses his consciousness and free will, becoming a puppet of the Gun Devil.

Contradictions

None known.

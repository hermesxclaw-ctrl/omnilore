Who It Is

Aki Hayakawa was a man who lived with a blade in one hand and a burning promise in the other—a Public Safety Devil Hunter whose every step was shadowed by the ghost of his family and the ticking clock of a curse that would one day claim him. His story is a tragedy written in blood, devotion, and the cruel arithmetic of devil contracts.

Aki is stoic, serious, and often cold towards others, especially Denji and Power, whom he finds immature and reckless. He is extremely dedicated to his duty as a Devil Hunter and is driven by a deep-seated desire for revenge against the Gun Devil, who killed his family. He is pragmatic and willing to make sacrifices to achieve his goals. Despite his tough exterior, he shows compassion and care for his comrades, particularly Himeno and later Denji and Power, forming a makeshift family with them. He is also prone to smoking and has a dry sense of humor.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname 'Hayakawa' (早川) is a common Japanese surname, with 'haya' meaning 'early' or 'fast' and 'kawa' meaning 'river'. The given name 'Aki' (秋) means 'autumn'.

Pronunciation — AH-kee HAH-yah-KAH-wah

Aliases — Aki, Gun Fiend (as a fiend)

Ownership — Created by Tatsuki Fujimoto, published by Shueisha in Weekly Shōnen Jump. Part of the manga and anime franchise 'Chainsaw Man'.

Powers
Fox Devil Contract

Aki can summon the Fox Devil's head or jaws to bite and devour enemies. The Fox Devil requires payment in the form of skin from Aki's body.

Limit: The Fox Devil only grants its head to attractive people, and Aki must offer a part of his body as payment.

Curse Devil Contract

Aki can use a sword with a nail through it to stab an enemy three times, summoning the Curse Devil to crush the victim. This costs several years of Aki's lifespan.

Limit: Requires three consecutive stabs and shortens the user's lifespan.

Future Devil Contract

Aki gains the ability to see a few seconds into the future, which he uses to enhance his combat abilities. The Future Devil can also communicate with him and show him visions of his death.

Limit: Only shows a few seconds ahead, and the Future Devil is mischievous and may show unwanted visions.

Gun Fiend Transformation

After being possessed by the Gun Devil, Aki transforms into the Gun Fiend, a monstrous entity with gun barrels covering his body, capable of firing devastating attacks.

Limit: Aki loses his consciousness and free will, becoming a puppet of the Gun Devil.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Aki is a tall, young man with long, dark hair often tied back in a low ponytail. He has sharp, serious eyes and a perpetually solemn expression. He typically wears the standard Public Safety Devil Hunter uniform: a dark suit with a white shirt and tie. Later, he is seen with a scar on his face and a more disheveled look as his life spirals. As the Gun Fiend, his appearance becomes monstrous, with gun barrels protruding from his body.

Symbols — His katana, which he uses in combat. The fox paw prints associated with his contract with the Fox Devil. The nail and hammer used in his Curse Devil contract.

Domains — Public Safety Devil Hunter headquarters, his apartment, various devil-hunting missions across Tokyo.

Accounts I
Aki's Revenge QuestChainsaw Man manga, chapters 1-97

1990s setting (in-universe)

Aki's family was killed by the Gun Devil, and he joined Public Safety to get close to and kill the Gun Devil. He initially sees Denji and Power as tools but gradually forms bonds with them. His quest for revenge leads him to make dangerous contracts, ultimately resulting in his death and transformation into the Gun Fiend, who is then killed by Denji. Aki's death devastates Denji and Power, and his transformation into the Gun Fiend forces Denji to kill him, a traumatic event that shapes the series' climax.

Accounts II
Aki's Bond with HimenoChainsaw Man manga, chapters 15-22

1990s setting (in-universe)

Aki is partnered with Himeno, who develops romantic feelings for him. She sacrifices herself to summon the Ghost Devil to save him from Samurai Sword, but he kills her to prevent further catastrophe. Her death deeply affects Aki. Himeno's death reinforces Aki's determination for revenge and his emotional isolation.

Aki's Death and TransformationChainsaw Man manga, chapters 90-97

1990s setting (in-universe)

Makima orchestrates Aki's death by having him possessed by the Gun Devil. He becomes the Gun Fiend and attacks Denji, who is forced to kill him. Aki's final moments show him briefly regaining consciousness, pleading with Denji to save him. Denji is traumatized by killing his friend, and Power sacrifices herself to save Denji, leading to the final confrontation with Makima.

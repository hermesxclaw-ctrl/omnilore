Who It Is
Form

Jack Baker is a humanoid figure with a grotesque appearance, characterized by a distorted face, bulging eyes, and a twisted body. His appearance is a result of the Mold infection, which has transformed him into a grotesque creature.

Powers

Mold Infection Jack Baker has the ability to infect others with the Mold, a parasitic organism that transforms humans into grotesque creatures.

Accounts

The game follows the story of Ethan Winters, a man who searches for his missing wife in a derelict plantation mansion. He soon discovers that the mansion is inhabited by the Baker family, who have been infected with the Mold.

The DLCs follow the story of Jack Baker, who is the main antagonist of the games. He is a violent and aggressive character, driven by a desire to infect and transform others into Mold creatures.

Resident Evil 7: BiohazardVideo game

2017

The game follows the story of Ethan Winters, a man who searches for his missing wife in a derelict plantation mansion. He soon discovers that the mansion is inhabited by the Baker family, who have been infected with the Mold. The game's storyline explores the consequences of the Mold infection and the transformation of humans into grotesque creatures.

Who It Is

On the desolate planet Vormir, a soul is demanded for a soul. The Soul Stone, the most enigmatic of the six Infinity Stones, exacts the ultimate price from those who seek it, forcing even the Mad Titan Thanos to weep as he sacrifices his beloved daughter. This is the story of a cosmic artifact that embodies the very essence of existence, and the sacrifices made to wield it.

The Stone itself has no personality, but it is said to have a will of its own, as it requires a sacrifice to be obtained. It is described as having a 'consciousness' or a 'soul' that demands a soul for a soul.

Powers
Soul Manipulation

The Soul Stone has the ability to control, manipulate, and potentially steal souls. In Infinity War, Thanos uses it to see Gamora's soul after her death, indicating it can interact with the souls of the deceased.

Limit: The exact extent of this power is not fully shown. It appears to require the user to be in possession of the Stone and to have a strong will.

Sacrifice Requirement

To obtain the Soul Stone, one must sacrifice a loved one, a soul for a soul. This is a unique rule not shared by other Stones.

Limit: The sacrifice must be of someone the seeker truly loves. The Stone appears to judge the sincerity of the sacrifice.

Resurrection/Afterlife Interaction

In the comics, the Soul Gem can resurrect the dead and allow communication with them. In the MCU, Thanos uses it to speak with the spirit of young Gamora, suggesting a similar capability.

Limit: Not fully explored in MCU; may require the user to have a strong connection to the soul.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Soul Stone is a small, oval-shaped gem with an orange hue. It glows when activated. In Infinity War, it is set in the Infinity Gauntlet. Its exact material is unknown, but it appears to be a crystalline substance.

Symbols — The Soul Stone is often associated with the color orange and is depicted as a glowing orb. In the MCU, it is uniquely tied to the planet Vormir and the concept of sacrifice.

Domains — The Soul Stone is located on the planet Vormir, a desolate, rocky world with a dark atmosphere. It is kept in a shrine at the peak of a mountain, guarded by a spectral being known as the Red Skull (Johann Schmidt), who was cursed to serve as its guide.

Accounts I
Thanos's Quest for the Soul StoneAvengers: Infinity War (2018)

2018

Thanos, seeking to fulfill his plan to wipe out half of all life, learns from Gamora the location of the Soul Stone: Vormir. He takes Gamora, his adopted daughter, to Vormir. There, the Red Skull reveals that the Stone requires a sacrifice of a loved one. Thanos, weeping, throws Gamora off a cliff, killing her, and thereby obtains the Soul Stone. Thanos obtains the Soul Stone, completing his collection. He uses it to snap his fingers, wiping out half of all life. The guilt of Gamora's death haunts him, but he believes it was necessary.

Accounts II
Natasha Romanoff's SacrificeAvengers: Endgame (2019)

2019

In 2023, the Avengers travel back in time to 2014 to retrieve the Soul Stone from Vormir to undo Thanos's snap. Clint Barton and Natasha Romanoff arrive, and the Red Skull informs them of the sacrifice requirement. Both are willing to sacrifice themselves; Natasha ultimately lets go of Clint's grip and falls to her death, allowing Clint to obtain the Stone. Natasha dies, and Clint obtains the Soul Stone. Her death deeply affects Clint and the other Avengers, and she is not resurrected by the snap that brings back the others.

Return of the Soul StoneAvengers: Endgame (2019)

2019

After using the Stones to undo the snap, Steve Rogers travels back in time to return each Stone to its original timeline. He returns the Soul Stone to Vormir at the moment it was taken, presumably restoring the timeline. The timeline is preserved, and the Soul Stone is returned to its proper place.

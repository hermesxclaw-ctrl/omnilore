Who It Is
Form

The Moldorm King is a massive, serpent-like creature with a thick, scaly body and a crown-like structure on its head. It has a distinctive, pulsing glow emanating from its body.

Powers

Earthquake The Moldorm King can create powerful earthquakes by thrashing its body, causing destruction and chaos in its surroundings.

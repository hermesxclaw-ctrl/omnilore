Who It Is

An enormous segmented worm with glittering blue scales, the Moldorm King burrows through solid stone with ease. Its body is covered in hardened plates, and its mouth is a circular ring of teeth. Unlike lesser moldorms, the King possesses a single weak point at the very tip of its tail that glows when it is enraged.

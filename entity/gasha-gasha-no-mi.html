Who It Is
Form

The Gasha Gasha no Mi is a round, blue fruit with a crooked stem, dotted with a variety of bumps in multiple defined shapes, ranging from circles to hexagons.

Powers

Telekinetic Dissembly The user of the Gasha Gasha no Mi can telekinetically dissemble objects and reassemble them however they choose, making the consumer a 'Combinable Human'.

Accounts

The story of the Gasha Gasha no Mi begins with its discovery by a young boy named Monkey D. Luffy, who consumes the fruit and gains its powers.

This story explores the concept of assimilation and its connection to the Gasha Gasha no Mi, delving into the philosophical implications of the fruit's powers.

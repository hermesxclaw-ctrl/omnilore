Who It Is

The Two-Tails, a gigantic cat-like creature covered in blue flames with two tails. Matatabi wields blazing fire chakra that can consume entire forests. Sealed within Yugito Nii of Kumogakure, it is one of the more refined tailed beasts, capable of human speech and strategic thought.

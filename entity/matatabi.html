Who It Is
Form

Matatabi is a Tailed Beast Spirit with the ability to take on various forms, but its most common appearance is that of a cat.

Powers

Shapeshifting Matatabi has the ability to take on various forms, allowing it to adapt to different situations and environments.

Accounts

Matatabi is a Tailed Beast Spirit with the ability to take on various forms. It is said to inhabit the mountainous regions of Korea, Japan, and China, where the silver vine plant grows.

The silver vine plant is said to have an effect on cats similar to catnip. Matatabi is often associated with this plant and is said to be able to use its powers to manipulate cats.

Who It Is

Zoe (ζωή, 'Life') is the feminine syzygos paired with Logos, representing the vivifying force that animates the divine Word. Where Logos is the structural articulation of the Father's self-knowledge, Zoe is the life that makes that articulation alive, organic, and growth-oriented. Together they generate Anthropos (Archetypal Human) and Ecclesia (the Community) — the next aeon-pair that introduces the 'image of God' and the 'fellowship of the saved' into the Pleroma.

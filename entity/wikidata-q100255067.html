The Approach

Across myth, fiction, and the natural world, the name Senna recurs as a thread connecting disparate realms—from a Roman goddess of the harvest to a legendary racing driver, from a genus of medicinal plants to a spectral warrior in a popular game. This dossier explores the many faces of Senna, separating fact from fiction and tracing the name's journey through cultures and centuries.

Content advisory. No dark material is currently documented for the fictional character Senna from League of Legends. However, the real-life individuals associated with the name have faced public scrutiny, and the 2010 documentary and 2024 miniseries about Ayrton Senna depict his fatal accident, which may be distressing to some viewers.
Identity

Senna

Pronunciation In English, Senna is typically pronounced /ˈsɛnə/ (SEN-uh). In Portuguese, the surname Senna is pronounced /ˈsẽnɐ/.

Etymology

The name Senna has multiple origins. As a given name, it is used in various cultures, possibly derived from the Latin 'Senex' meaning 'old' or from the Japanese '千' (sen) meaning 'thousand' combined with '菜' (na) meaning 'greens'. In the context of the plant genus, 'Senna' comes from the Arabic 'sana', referring to the medicinal leaves. For the fictional character in League of Legends, the name was chosen by Riot Games, possibly echoing the real-world names and the plant's healing associations.

Cultural Ownership

The name Senna is used across multiple cultures and contexts. The League of Legends character is a creation of Riot Games, a U.S. company, and is part of global gaming culture. The plant genus is of botanical significance worldwide. The name is also common as a surname in Brazil and other Portuguese-speaking countries, and as a given name in various regions.

Disputed Identifications

There is no significant dispute over the identity of the League of Legends character Senna, but the name is shared by many unrelated entities, which can lead to confusion. In particular, the plant genus Senna and the character Senna are distinct, and the historical figure Ayrton Senna is not directly related to the fictional character.

Description

Appearance

In League of Legends, Senna is depicted as a tall, slender woman with pale skin and long, white hair. She wears a dark, armored outfit with golden accents, and carries a large relic cannon. Her eyes are often shown as glowing with a spectral light. In her 'Lucian' skin, she has a more traditional Sentinel of Light appearance.

Symbols & Iconography

Senna's iconography includes the relic cannon, which is a key part of her design and gameplay. She is also associated with the Mist, a supernatural force in the game's lore. Her abilities often feature light and spectral imagery, representing her role as a Sentinel of Light.

Behaviour & Personality

Senna is characterized as determined, compassionate, and fiercely protective. She is a skilled marksman and a leader, often acting as a mentor to other Sentinels. Despite her grim circumstances, she maintains hope and fights to save souls trapped in the Mist.

Domains & Habitats

Senna's primary habitat in the game's lore is the Shadow Isles, a cursed realm of black mist and undead spirits. However, she also travels the world of Runeterra, fighting the Mist wherever it appears.

Version Differences

In her original lore, Senna was introduced as a support champion who was trapped in the Mist and later freed. In the 2020 'Spirit Blossom' event, she received an alternate skin with a different appearance and personality. In the 2021 'Sentinel of Light' event, her lore was expanded, showing her as a key figure in the fight against Viego, the Ruined King.

Who It Is

Across myth, fiction, and the natural world, the name Senna recurs as a thread connecting disparate realms—from a Roman goddess of the harvest to a legendary racing driver, from a genus of medicinal plants to a spectral warrior in a popular game. This dossier explores the many faces of Senna, separating fact from fiction and tracing the name's journey through cultures and centuries.

Senna is characterized as determined, compassionate, and fiercely protective. She is a skilled marksman and a leader, often acting as a mentor to other Sentinels. Despite her grim circumstances, she maintains hope and fights to save souls trapped in the Mist.

Powers
Piercing Darkness

Senna fires a beam of light that damages enemies and heals allies. This ability is a core part of her kit as a marksman-support hybrid.

Limit: Requires line-of-sight and has a cooldown.

Last Embrace

Senna roots enemies in a line, dealing damage and setting up kills.

Limit: Can be dodged; does not affect structures.

Curse of the Black Mist

Senna creates a shroud of mist that grants camouflage and movement speed to herself and allies, while also damaging enemies inside.

Limit: The shroud is stationary and can be exited by enemies.

Dawning Shadow

Senna's ultimate ability, which fires a global beam that damages enemies and shields allies.

Limit: Long cooldown and requires positioning to hit the beam.

Absolution

Senna's passive ability, which allows her to collect souls from enemies she hits, increasing her attack range and damage.

Limit: Requires killing or damaging enemies to collect souls.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In League of Legends, Senna is depicted as a tall, slender woman with pale skin and long, white hair. She wears a dark, armored outfit with golden accents, and carries a large relic cannon. Her eyes are often shown as glowing with a spectral light. In her 'Lucian' skin, she has a more traditional Sentinel of Light appearance.

Symbols — Senna's iconography includes the relic cannon, which is a key part of her design and gameplay. She is also associated with the Mist, a supernatural force in the game's lore. Her abilities often feature light and spectral imagery, representing her role as a Sentinel of Light.

Domains — Senna's primary habitat in the game's lore is the Shadow Isles, a cursed realm of black mist and undead spirits. However, she also travels the world of Runeterra, fighting the Mist wherever it appears.

Accounts I
The Light of RedemptionRiot Games Universe

Modern (2019)

Senna was a Sentinel of Light, a warrior who fought against the Black Mist. She was captured by Thresh, a sadistic spirit, and imprisoned within his lantern. Years later, her husband Lucian freed her, but she emerged changed, carrying the Mist within her. She now fights to redeem herself and save other souls. Senna became a playable champion in League of Legends, and her story was expanded in subsequent events.

Accounts II
The Sentinel of Light EventRiot Games

2021

Senna joins forces with other champions to stop Viego, the Ruined King, from unleashing the Mist across Runeterra. She plays a crucial role in the final battle. The event expanded Senna's lore and established her as a key figure in the fight against the Ruination.

Who It Is

Two conjoined chefs who run the Maw's kitchen — grotesquely obese, identical twins who prepare the children captured by Roger into elaborate dishes for the Maw's gluttonous guests. They communicate in grunts and work in perfect, disturbing synchronization. When they detect Six, they pursue her with cleavers and meat hooks, their bulk surprisingly fast as they charge through the kitchen. They cannot be killed — only evaded or momentarily incapacitated.

Form

Two identical, obese men in chef's whites, covered in blood and grease. Their faces are featureless save for small eyes pinched in folds of fat. They are not physically conjoined at birth but wear a single set of clothes between them, moving and working as one organism. They carry oversized cleavers and wear chef's hats stained red.

Cultural Significance

The Twins represent consumption and excess — the Maw's theme of gluttony made flesh. Their synchronization is unsettling, as if they share a single consciousness in two bodies.

He is not a god of marble temples, but a presence woven into the living tapestry of Afro-Brazilian and Indigenous spirituality, a guardian of the wild and a voice for those who dwell between worlds.

Identity

Caboclo Serra Negra

Pronunciation kah-BOH-kloo SEH-hah NEH-grah (approximate Brazilian Portuguese pronunciation)

Etymology

'Caboclo' is a Brazilian Portuguese term with complex origins, historically used to refer to people of mixed Indigenous and European ancestry, and in Afro-Brazilian religions, it denotes a class of spirits representing Indigenous Brazilian ancestors. 'Serra Negra' literally means 'Black Mountain' or 'Dark Mountain' in Portuguese, likely referring to a specific geographical feature or a spiritual realm. The name could be interpreted as 'the caboclo of the dark mountain'.

Cultural Ownership

Afro-Brazilian religious traditions, particularly Umbanda and related hybrid traditions that incorporate Indigenous spiritual figures. The figure is also connected to broader Brazilian folk spirituality and the cultural legacy of Indigenous peoples of Brazil.

Disputed Identifications

The specific identity of 'Caboclo Serra Negra' is not widely documented in mainstream sources. It may be a regional spirit, a specific manifestation within a particular Umbanda house, or a personal guide of a medium. There is no consensus on his exact mythology, origins, or attributes. It is possible that the name is used in different contexts with different meanings.

Behaviour & Personality

Caboclo spirits are typically characterized as wise, stoic, brave, and connected to nature. They are often sought for healing, counsel, and protection. Serra Negra, if following this archetype, would be expected to be a serious, powerful, and benevolent guide. This is inferred from general Caboclo lore, not from specific accounts.

Domains & Habitats

As a Caboclo, he would be associated with the natural world, particularly forests, mountains, and rivers. The name 'Serra Negra' suggests a mountain or highland habitat. In Umbanda cosmology, such spirits may inhabit a spiritual 'line' or 'vibration' linked to the forest. No specific realm is documented.

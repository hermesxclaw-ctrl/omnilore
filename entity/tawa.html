Who It Is
Form

Tawa is often depicted as a radiant, sun-like figure with outstretched arms, symbolizing his role as the creator and life-giver.

Powers

Creation Tawa has the power to create and shape the world, its inhabitants, and the natural environment.

Accounts

Tawa created the world and its inhabitants, including the Hopi people, from the void of Tokpella, or endless space.

Tawa is described as a radiant, sun-like figure who created the world and its inhabitants, but is unhappy with their lack of understanding and appreciation for the natural world.

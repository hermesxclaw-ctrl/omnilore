Who It Is
Form

Mithra is often depicted as a radiant, bearded man with a crown or diadem, symbolizing his connection to the sun and truth.

Powers

Protective Power Mithra is said to possess the power to protect and defend his followers, as well as to promote truth and righteousness in the world.

Accounts

According to Ferdowsi's epic poem, Mithra was born from the primordial waters of the world, and was nurtured by the sun and the earth.

In this myth, Mithra is said to have sacrificed a white bull, which was a symbol of fertility and abundance, to promote the growth of crops and the well-being of his followers.

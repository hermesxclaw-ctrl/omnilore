Who It Is

Magwayen is the Visayan deity of the underworld and psychopomp who ferries souls to the afterlife. She was originally a primordial sea goddess. Her association with both creation and death makes her a liminal deity. Those who lived virtuously have a smooth crossing; the wicked find the passage terrifying.

Attested in Oral tradition / Visayan mythologyEra Pre-colonial Philippines

Who It Is

Angel of love, beauty, and the planet Venus. Anael (also Haniel, Aniel) is one of the seven archangels in Kabbalistic tradition, presiding over the Sephirah Netzach (Victory/Eternity). Haniel's name means 'Grace of God' or 'Joy of God.' She (often depicted as feminine) governs Friday, the arts, romantic love, and aesthetics. In the Zohar, Haniel is the angel who transported Enoch to heaven in the fiery chariot. In ceremonial magic, Anael is invoked for matters of the heart, artistic inspiration, and harmony in relationships. Unlike martial or judicial angels, Anael embodies the divine principle of beauty and desire.

Who It Is
Form

In Jewish mysticism, Anael is often depicted as a benevolent angel, associated with the planet Venus and the color green. He is said to be a gentle and compassionate being, often depicted with a gentle smile and a calming presence.

Powers

Healing Anael is said to possess the power of healing, able to bring balance and harmony to the body and soul.

Accounts

Anael is said to have created the rose, a symbol of love and beauty, to bring joy and harmony to the world.

Anael is said to have created the lily, a symbol of purity and innocence, to bring light and hope to the world.

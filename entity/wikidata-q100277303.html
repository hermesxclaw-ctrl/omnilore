Who It Is

In the chaotic world of advertising, one character stands out as the personification of life's unpredictable disasters: Mayhem, the tousled, orange-clad troublemaker who has been wreaking havoc in Allstate insurance commercials since 2010. But this Mayhem is just one of many—a name shared by a Norwegian black metal band infamous for church burnings, a Marvel Comics vigilante, and a 2017 action-horror film. This dossier untangles the many faces of Mayhem, revealing how a single word can embody everything from criminal law to musical extremity.

The Allstate Mayhem is mischievous, impulsive, and embodies the unforeseen causes of accidents. The band is known for its extreme and controversial behavior, both on and off stage. The Marvel Mayhem is a determined vigilante.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The word 'mayhem' derives from the Anglo-French 'mahem' or 'mayhem', from the Old French 'meshaignier' meaning 'to maim'. It entered English legal terminology in the 15th century, referring to the crime of violently injuring a person, specifically to disable or disfigure.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Allstate Mayhem character is a disheveled man with messy brown hair, a five-o'clock shadow, and often wears an orange hoodie or t-shirt, representing various disaster scenarios. The band Mayhem is known for their corpse paint and black metal attire. The Marvel character Mayhem is a female vigilante with a distinctive costume.

Symbols — For the Allstate character, the orange color and chaotic demeanor symbolize unexpected accidents. For the band, the corpse paint and inverted crosses are symbols of black metal and anti-Christianity. For the Marvel character, the name and design evoke chaos and vengeance.

Domains — The Allstate Mayhem exists in a suburban American setting, appearing in homes and streets. The band operates in the Norwegian black metal scene. The Marvel character operates in the Marvel Universe.

Allstate Mayhem campaignsAllstate Insurance

2010-present

Mayhem is a recurring character who personifies various risks that lead to insurance claims, such as distracted driving, home invasions, and natural disasters. Each commercial shows Mayhem causing a specific problem, followed by a pitch for Allstate coverage. Increased brand recognition and sales for Allstate.

Accounts II
Mayhem (band) historyVarious music journalism and biographies

1984-present

Mayhem is a pioneering black metal band known for their early demos and debut album 'De Mysteriis Dom Sathanas'. The band's history is marked by the suicide of vocalist Dead and the murder of guitarist Euronymous by Varg Vikernes, which brought infamy to the band and the genre. The band became a central figure in the early Norwegian black metal scene and its controversies.

Mayhem (film)Film directed by Joe Lynch

2017

A virus outbreak causes people to lose their inhibitions, leading to chaos in a corporate office. A lawyer must fight his way through the building to save his job and survive. The film received positive reviews and gained a cult following.

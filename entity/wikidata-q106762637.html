Who It Is

In the chaos of the Clone Wars, a single clone trooper chose to lay down his weapon and walk away—becoming a deserter, a farmer, and a father. His name was Cut Lawquane, and his story challenges the very notion of what it means to be a soldier bred for war.

Cut is calm, pragmatic, and deeply committed to his family. He is not cowardly; rather, he made a conscious choice to desert after realizing the war's futility. He is protective and resourceful, and he shows no regret for his decision, though he respects the Jedi and his former brothers. He is open-minded and willing to help others, even at personal risk.

Clone Trooper Training

Cut retains the combat training and physical conditioning of a clone trooper, including proficiency with blasters and hand-to-hand combat. He demonstrates this when defending his family from battle droids.

Limit: He has been out of combat for some time and prefers to avoid violence, but he is still capable when necessary.

Farming Skills

Cut has adapted to life as a moisture farmer, showing knowledge of agriculture and self-sufficiency.

Limit: None.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Cut Lawquane is a clone trooper who has removed his armor and wears civilian farmer clothing—a simple tunic and trousers. He has short brown hair and a distinctive scar on his face, which he received during the battle that led to his desertion. He retains the physical build of a clone but has let his hair grow out, distinguishing him from his brothers.

Symbols — His civilian attire symbolizes his rejection of the clone army and his embrace of a peaceful life. He carries no weapons, and his home is a modest moisture farm on the planet Saleucami.

Domains — Cut lives on a moisture farm on the planet Saleucami, a remote Outer Rim world. His home is simple and functional, with a family and a life far removed from the front lines.

Accounts I
The DeserterStar Wars: The Clone Wars, Season 2, Episode 10

Clone Wars era (approx. 22 BBY)

During a mission on Saleucami, Captain Rex is injured and separated from Anakin and Obi-Wan. He stumbles upon a farm owned by Cut Lawquane, a clone deserter who has built a life with his wife Suu and two children. Rex is initially conflicted, but Cut explains his reasons for deserting: after a battle, he realized the clones were fighting for a cause they didn't fully understand, and he chose to live for himself. When battle droids attack the farm, Cut and Rex work together to defend the family. Rex leaves, respecting Cut's choice, and does not report him. Cut and his family survive, and the episode ends with Rex reflecting on the meaning of duty and choice. Rex's encounter with Cut influences his perspective on the clone army and the Jedi, planting seeds of doubt about the war. Cut continues his peaceful life, and his family is safe.

The Approach

In the chaos of the Clone Wars, a single clone trooper chose to lay down his weapon and walk away—becoming a deserter, a farmer, and a father. His name was Cut Lawquane, and his story challenges the very notion of what it means to be a soldier bred for war.

Content advisory. Contains themes of war, desertion, and the moral complexities of conflict. No graphic violence or explicit content.
Identity

Cut Lawquane

Pronunciation KUHT law-KWAIN

Etymology

The name 'Cut' may be a shortened form of 'Cutt' or a nickname, but no official etymology is provided. 'Lawquane' is likely a surname given to him after desertion, but its origin is unconfirmed.

Cultural Ownership

Star Wars franchise, created by George Lucas and developed by Lucasfilm Animation.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans initially speculated that Cut might be a previously seen clone, but no official confirmation exists. His story is consistent with the theme of clones seeking individuality.

Description

Appearance

Cut Lawquane is a clone trooper who has removed his armor and wears civilian farmer clothing—a simple tunic and trousers. He has short brown hair and a distinctive scar on his face, which he received during the battle that led to his desertion. He retains the physical build of a clone but has let his hair grow out, distinguishing him from his brothers.

Symbols & Iconography

His civilian attire symbolizes his rejection of the clone army and his embrace of a peaceful life. He carries no weapons, and his home is a modest moisture farm on the planet Saleucami.

Behaviour & Personality

Cut is calm, pragmatic, and deeply committed to his family. He is not cowardly; rather, he made a conscious choice to desert after realizing the war's futility. He is protective and resourceful, and he shows no regret for his decision, though he respects the Jedi and his former brothers. He is open-minded and willing to help others, even at personal risk.

Domains & Habitats

Cut lives on a moisture farm on the planet Saleucami, a remote Outer Rim world. His home is simple and functional, with a family and a life far removed from the front lines.

Version Differences

There are no significant version differences, as Cut appears only in 'The Deserter' and a brief flashback in Season 7. His characterization remains consistent.

Stories & Myths

The Deserter

Source Star Wars: The Clone Wars, Season 2, Episode 10Culture Star Wars universePeriod Clone Wars era (approx. 22 BBY)

Summary

During a mission on Saleucami, Captain Rex is injured and separated from Anakin and Obi-Wan. He stumbles upon a farm owned by Cut Lawquane, a clone deserter who has built a life with his wife Suu and two children. Rex is initially conflicted, but Cut explains his reasons for deserting: after a battle, he realized the clones were fighting for a cause they didn't fully understand, and he chose to live for himself. When battle droids attack the farm, Cut and Rex work together to defend the family. Rex leaves, respecting Cut's choice, and does not report him. Cut and his family survive, and the episode ends with Rex reflecting on the meaning of duty and choice.

Who It Is

In the sprawling multiverse of DC Comics, few heroes are as literally defined by their name as Breach — a character whose very existence is a rupture in the fabric of reality. Created in the early 2000s, this cosmic guardian embodies the concept of a breach, both as a tear in space-time and as a barrier against interdimensional threats. His story is a testament to the power of second chances and the heavy burden of wielding the keys to the multiverse.

Breach is characterized by a strong sense of duty and a desire to protect the innocent. He is often conflicted about his powers, which he sees as a curse as much as a gift. He is introspective and sometimes brooding, but ultimately heroic.

Powers
Reality Breaching

He can create portals and tears in reality, allowing him to travel between dimensions and teleport across vast distances.

Limit: Creating large breaches requires significant concentration and energy; overuse can exhaust him.

Energy Projection

He can project powerful energy blasts from his hands or body.

Limit: Limited range and power compared to other energy manipulators.

Dimensional Awareness

He has a sensory awareness of breaches in reality and can detect interdimensional anomalies.

Limit: His awareness is limited to nearby dimensions and can be overwhelmed by multiple breaches.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Breach is a tall, muscular African-American male with short black hair. He typically wears a high-tech suit with glowing energy conduits, and his eyes emit a blue-white energy when his powers are active. His costume is predominantly blue and white, with a stylized 'B' emblem on his chest.

Symbols — The 'B' emblem on his chest symbolizes his name and his role as a barrier. His suit's energy lines represent the flow of interdimensional energy he controls.

Domains — He operates primarily on Earth, but his powers allow him to travel across dimensions. He is often depicted in urban environments, particularly in Metropolis and Gotham City.

Accounts I
Origin of BreachBreach #1 (2005)

2005

Ethan Clace was a security guard at a S.T.A.R. Labs facility when an experiment went wrong, merging his molecules with a piece of the Source Wall. This accident gave him the ability to breach reality, but also left him connected to a cosmic entity known as the 'Breach'. Ethan gained powers but became a target for various cosmic entities and villains.

Accounts II
Infinite CrisisInfinite Crisis #7 (2006)

2006

Breach plays a role in the final battle of Infinite Crisis, helping to contain the damage caused by the multiverse's collapse. Breach is instrumental in saving the multiverse but is later presumed dead.

FlashpointFlashpoint: The World of Flashpoint (2011)

2011

In the alternate timeline of Flashpoint, a character named Breach appears as a member of a resistance movement, though his backstory is different. The Flashpoint timeline is eventually erased, making this version non-canon.

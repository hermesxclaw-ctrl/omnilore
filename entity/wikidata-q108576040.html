Who It Is

On an island where no man is allowed to set foot, an old woman with a crooked back and sharp tongue remembers the outside world that almost killed her — and the three girls she saved from it. Gloriosa, called Elder Nyon by the warriors who once exiled her, is not an empress, not a fighter, and not a myth, but the living memory of Amazon Lily.

Attested: Sharp-tongued, pragmatic, worldly, and maternally protective despite exasperation. She is the only Kuja with sustained knowledge of the outside world, the World Government, and the concept of men, and acts as expositor and advisor. She frequently chastises Boa Hancock for petulance and urges empathy, yet remains loyal to Amazon Lily. She shows fear of Hancock's petrification power but does not flinch from confronting her. Powers
Worldly Knowledge and Political Counsel

Encyclopedic knowledge of the outside world, World Government structure, Marine hierarchy, Warlord system, and the concept of love sickness (koi no wazurai) as a fatal Kuja illness. Acts as Amazon Lily's de facto diplomat and strategist.

Limit: Knowledge is dated to her time at sea (decades prior) and second-hand news via newspapers; she misjudges some current events and relies on Shakuyaku's information network for updates.

Leadership and Empress Experience (Former)

Former Empress of Amazon Lily and captain of the Kuja Pirates, implying high combat competence and Haki potential in youth, as all Kuja empresses are elite warriors.

Limit: No combat feats shown in old age; she does not fight on-panel. Current physical frailty precludes active leadership; Boa Hancock is reigning empress.

Survival and Navigation of the Calm Belt

Survived leaving and re-entering the Calm Belt, which is infested with Sea Kings and normally unnavigable without Kuja/Seastone technology, indicating navigational skill or assistance.

Limit: Exact method of return not detailed; likely aided by others (implied connection to Shakuyaku and Rayleigh). Not a repeatable technique shown.

Emotional Resilience / Love Sickness Survival

Survived the Kuja love sickness that is stated to be fatal if the sufferer cannot be with their beloved or leave the island. Her survival is presented as exceptional.

Limit: Survival required leaving Amazon Lily; she did not overcome the illness while remaining. The illness nearly killed her.

No Devil Fruit or Confirmed Haki

No Devil Fruit power is ever attributed to her. No Haki is explicitly shown or named for her in manga/anime.

Limit: As a former Kuja empress, she may have Haki (all Kuja warriors use Haki), but this is inference, not attestation. Must be flagged as unknown/reconstructed, not confirmed.

Accounts I
The Reign and Abdication of Empress GloriosaOne Piece manga Ch. 521, Vivre Card No. 0516, Blue Deep

In-universe: ~40-50 years before present (pre-Roger era); published 2008-2009

Gloriosa served as Empress of Amazon Lily and captain of the Kuja Pirates, the strongest warrior of her generation. Like all empresses, she led raids on outside ships. At some point she fell in love – a fatal taboo for Kuja, whose love sickness kills if unrequited or if they remain on the island. To survive, she abdicated and left Amazon Lily for the outside world, becoming the first empress in living memory to willingly leave. Her departure created a precedent for exile and return; she became a cautionary tale on the island. The throne passed to subsequent empresses, eventually to Boa Hancock. Her story directly parallels and foreshadows Hancock's love sickness for Luffy.

Exile, Love Sickness, and Life in the Outside WorldOne Piece manga Ch. 521, Anime Ep. 417

In-universe: ~30-40 years before present; published 2008

After leaving, Gloriosa suffered the full course of love sickness and survived only because she was outside Amazon Lily. She lived in the outside world, where she encountered Shakuyaku (Shakky) and Silvers Rayleigh. Shakky, herself a former Kuja empress who left the island two generations prior, befriended her. This network of exiled Kuja women formed a support system. Gloriosa eventually recovered from the illness, though the love itself remained unfulfilled. Established the Shakky–Gloriosa–Rayleigh connection that later saves the Boa sisters. Demonstrates that Kuja can survive outside and that love sickness is survivable off-island.

Rescue and Mentorship of the Boa SistersOne Piece manga Ch. 521-522, Anime Ep. 417-418

In-universe: ~15 years before present (after Fisher Tiger's attack on Mary Geoise); published 2009

Boa Hancock (12) and her sisters Sandersonia and Marigold were abducted by the World Nobles (Celestial Dragons) and enslaved in Mary Geoise, branded with the Hoof of the Celestial Dragon and forced to eat Devil Fruits for entertainment. After Fisher Tiger's rampage freed many slaves, the three sisters escaped but were stranded. Gloriosa, Shakky and Rayleigh found them and brought them back to Amazon Lily, hiding their slave past. Gloriosa vouched for them and helped conceal the mark on Hancock's back, which Hancock hides to avoid shame and death. The sisters became Kuja warriors; Hancock later became empress. Gloriosa's advocacy allowed them to reintegrate. The secret of the slave mark drives Hancock's distrust of men and the World Government, central to Amazon Lily arc.

Accounts II
The Amazon Lily Arc – Advising Against War with the World GovernmentOne Piece manga Ch. 514-524, Anime Ep. 408-421

In-universe: Present timeline, after Marineford summons; published 2008-2009 / aired 2009

When Monkey D. Luffy is blasted to Amazon Lily by Bartholomew Kuma, Gloriosa is the first to recognize the danger he poses. After Luffy befriends Hancock and learns her slave past, the Marines summon Hancock as a Warlord to fight Whitebeard. Hancock initially refuses, wanting to protect her secret and island. Gloriosa counsels her that refusal will lead to Amazon Lily's destruction, and that trusting Luffy is the wiser path. She also explains love sickness to Luffy and the Kuja, revealing her own past to legitimize Hancock's feelings. Hancock ultimately agrees to go to Marineford, with conditions, and Gloriosa helps cover Luffy's presence. Hancock maintains Warlord status temporarily, Amazon Lily is spared, and Luffy gains an ally who later helps him infiltrate Impel Down. Gloriosa's counsel is pivotal to the Marineford saga's setup.

Return to the Outskirts – Life After Hancock's ChoiceOne Piece manga Ch. 523-524, Ch. 591 (cover story / post-war)

In-universe: Post-Marineford War (Ch. 590-591); published 2009-2010

After Hancock returns from Marineford, Gloriosa resumes her role as elder advisor on the outskirts. When Luffy recuperates on Amazon Lily after the war, Gloriosa helps enforce the island's law that men must not enter the village, while quietly tolerating Luffy's presence due to Hancock's affection. She is present when Rayleigh arrives to train Luffy and when the Kuja decide to protect Luffy for two years. Post-timeskip, she remains on Amazon Lily, occasionally appearing in crowd scenes and cover stories, still offering gruff advice. Stabilizes Amazon Lily's politics post-war; enables Luffy's two-year training on Rusukaina with Hancock's support. Shows Gloriosa's continued liminal authority.

The Warlord Abolition and Amazon Lily's Peril (Post-Wano News Arc)One Piece manga Ch. 956, Ch. 1059-1060

In-universe: Post-Reverie / Post-Wano; published 2019, 2022

When the Seven Warlord system is abolished at the Reverie, the Marines led by Koby and later Blackbeard invade Amazon Lily. Hancock petrifies many attackers. Gloriosa is shown among the Kuja reacting to the news and later worrying for the island's future as Hancock's bounty rises and the island becomes vulnerable. She urges caution and again serves as the voice of outside-world pragmatism, noting that the World Government will not leave them alone. Amazon Lily survives due to Hancock's power and Rayleigh's intervention, but its isolation is broken. Gloriosa's warnings are validated; the island's future remains uncertain.

Gloriosa is the current Empress of Amazon Lily. — She is the former empress, now elder advisor living outside the village. The current empress is Boa Hancock (until recent manga events where Hancock's status is threatened by Warlord abolition).

Gloriosa has a Devil Fruit power or is a fighter on par with Hancock. — No Devil Fruit or combat feats are attested for Gloriosa in old age. Her strength is knowledge, not combat. Her youthful strength is implied but not quantified.

Gloriosa is the same character as Gloriosa Daisy from My Little Pony. — Distinct characters in unrelated franchises sharing the same given name (Latin root). One Piece's Gloriosa (2008) predates MLP's Gloriosa Daisy (2016) and has no narrative connection.

Gloriosa abandoned Amazon Lily out of selfishness and is disliked by all Kuja. — She left to survive love sickness, a recognized fatal illness on the island, and is respected by elders and the Boa sisters despite living outside the walls. Younger Kuja tease her, but she is not universally hated.

Who It Is

In the criminal underworld of Artemis Fowl, where fairies fly on titanium wings and trolls prowl beneath Dublin, one figure stands apart — a dwarf who tunnels through the earth with a jaw that unhinges like a serpent's and a stomach that can hold a fortune in stolen gold. Mulch Diggums is not a hero in the traditional sense; he is a thief, a con artist, a reluctant ally, and, against all odds, the most dependable friend a young criminal mastermind could have. His story is one of survival, redemption, and the unexpected honor of a lawbreaker who chooses to do the right thing when it matters most.

Mulch is cynical, sarcastic, and self-serving, but he has a strong survival instinct and a hidden sense of loyalty. He is a master thief and con artist, often using his wits to escape dangerous situations. He is fiercely independent and resents authority, but he develops a grudging respect for Artemis and eventually becomes a trusted ally. His humor is often crude, and he has a talent for finding loopholes in rules.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Mulch' suggests organic matter used to enrich soil, fitting his earth-based abilities. 'Diggums' is a play on 'digging' and possibly 'diggums' as a colloquial term for feet or a humorous take on 'digger'.

Pronunciation — MULCH DIG-ums

Aliases — Mulch, The Dwarf, Mud Man (derogatory, used by himself and others)

Ownership — Irish author Eoin Colfer; character belongs to the Artemis Fowl franchise.

Powers
Earth Consumption

Mulch can eat through soil, rock, and even concrete at remarkable speed, using his powerful jaws and digestive acids. This allows him to tunnel through the earth without leaving a trace, as he digests the material and expels it as waste.

Limit: He cannot eat through metal or other hard materials without difficulty. He also needs to expel the digested material periodically, which can be messy and attracts attention.

Flatulence Propulsion

By ingesting large amounts of earth, Mulch can produce powerful intestinal gas that he releases to propel himself through tunnels at high speed, like a rocket. This is a unique and humorous ability.

Limit: The gas is flammable and can be ignited, causing harm. It also makes him vulnerable while he is building up the gas.

Dwarf Magic

Like all fairies, Mulch has innate magical abilities, but his are limited. He can use a form of 'woo woo' to unlock doors and barriers, but he is not a powerful magic user. His magic is often unreliable and he prefers physical solutions.

Limit: His magic is not strong enough for major spells, and he often relies on gadgets and cunning.

Thievery and Stealth

Mulch is an expert thief, able to pick locks, bypass security systems, and steal valuable items without detection. He has a keen eye for treasure and a knack for escaping tight spots.

Limit: He can be overconfident and sometimes underestimates his opponents.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Mulch is a dwarf, typically about three feet tall, with a stocky build. He has a large, prominent nose, and his jaw is unhinged to allow him to consume large amounts of earth. His hair is thick and often matted with dirt. He wears practical, rugged clothing suited for tunneling, often with pockets for stolen goods. In the film adaptation, he is portrayed with a more exaggerated, CGI-enhanced appearance, with a larger nose and more pronounced feat

Symbols — Mulch is often associated with dirt, tunnels, and gold. His iconic trait is his ability to eat through soil and rock, and his use of flatulence as a propulsion system for tunneling. He is also known for his love of stolen goods, particularly gold and diamonds.

Domains — Mulch is subterranean, spending most of his time in tunnels beneath the Earth's surface. He is familiar with the Lower Elements, the fairy underworld, and also operates on the surface, particularly in Ireland. He has a hideout in a cemetery and later a mansion in Haven City.

Accounts I
The Dwarf in Artemis FowlArtemis Fowl (novel)

Early 21st century

Mulch is hired by Artemis Fowl to break into a mansion and steal a fairy book. He is caught by the fairy police, but Artemis rescues him. Later, he helps Artemis infiltrate the fairy police headquarters to rescue his mother, using his tunneling skills. He ultimately betrays Artemis temporarily but returns to help him escape. Mulch gains a criminal record and becomes a fugitive, but he also establishes a tenuous alliance with Artemis.

The Arctic IncidentThe Arctic Incident

Early 21st century

Mulch is released from prison to help Artemis and Holly on a mission to the Arctic to stop a goblin rebellion. He uses his tunneling abilities to infiltrate the goblin stronghold and helps save the day. Mulch's sentence is reduced, and he gains a measure of respect from the LEP.

The Eternity CodeThe Eternity Code

Early 21st century

Mulch helps Artemis steal back the C Cube from Jon Spiro. He also plays a role in the final confrontation, using his skills to disable Spiro's security. Mulch is rewarded with a share of the loot and continues to work with Artemis.

The Opal DeceptionThe Opal Deception

Early 21st century

Mulch aids Artemis and Holly in their fight against Opal Koboi, helping them escape from prison and providing crucial intelligence. He also helps rescue Holly from a trap. Mulch becomes a more trusted ally and is instrumental in defeating Opal.

Who It Is

In the handheld shadow of SNK's most famous tournament, a young zaibatsu heir decided the King of Fighters was not a contest to win but a ritual to harvest — and that godhood was a business acquisition. Reiji Oogami, the elegant, soft-spoken final boss of The King of Fighters EX2: Howling Blood, never appeared on an arcade marquee, yet he embodies everything SNK's Orochi saga fears most: a human who learned enough about the serpent god to want to become it.

Attested via story dialogue: Reiji is calm, ultra-polite, and aristocratic in speech (using keigo and detached phrasing), but reveals absolute narcissism and theological megalomania. He sponsors the EX2 tournament under the pretense of philanthropy and martial arts patronage, while privately viewing all fighters as sacrificial energy sources. He speaks of humanity as 'impure' and of himself as chosen to become the 'new god' to replace Orochi. Powers
Orochi Power Manipulation (Dark Orochi Energy)

Reiji wields a corrupted form of Orochi power without being one of the Hakkesshu or a Sacred Treasure bearer. Manifests as dark violet-black energy slashes, pillars, and projectiles. He can summon pillars of darkness from the ground (similar to Goenitz's Yonokaze but without wind attribute) and fire large cutting waves. Lore explanation in EX2 suggests he obtained it through forbidden research into Orochi's seal and possibly through human sacrifices/energy harvested from fighters, not through bloodline.

Limit: Power is borrowed, not innate; it consumes him. When defeated, the energy rejects him and he disintegrates/vanishes rather than being merely knocked out. He cannot fully control it — his final form is unstable and causes dimensional distortion he does not sustain. Unlike true Hakkesshu, he shows no healing or resurrection ability.

Dimensional / Space Distortion

In second phase, Reiji warps the battlefield from corporate rooftop into a void-like alter space, implying ability to tear space using Orochi energy. Within this space he teleports short distances and causes the background to pulse with Orochi symbols.

Limit: Requires full release of harvested energy; cannot be maintained after defeat. No evidence of long-range teleportation or time travel like Ash Crimson or Botan.

Corporate and Financial Control

Non-supernatural ability: As head of the Oogami Concern, he commands vast wealth, private security, and influence sufficient to sponsor a KOF tournament rivaling Rugal's, hire assassins like Sinobu Amou, and conduct occult research. This is his primary narrative power before supernatural empowerment.

Limit: Wealth does not grant martial skill; his fighting prowess derives entirely from stolen Orochi power. After his disappearance, the Concern's fate is unaddressed.

Martial Technique - Elegant Energy Combat

Gameplay movelist includes: (1) Darkness Slash - large ground-traveling dark wave; (2) Pillar of Darkness - summoning dark pillars under opponent; (3) Counter stance with afterimage; (4) Desperation Move: 'Requiem for a God' (fan translation) - full-screen dark energy detonation. All are energy-based, not physical strikes, reflecting his untrained but power-augmented style. No traditional martial art listed (unlike Kyo's Kusanagi-ryu).

Limit: Boss-only; not balanced for player use, with invincibility frames and damage far above playable cast. No evidence he could fight without Orochi augmentation.

Accounts I
The Heir of the Oogami ConcernThe King of Fighters EX2: Howling Blood - Official profile and Story Mode prologue

2003 (in-universe: set after KOF '98, approx. 1999 in EX timeline)

Reiji Oogami is introduced as the young head of the Oogami Concern, a powerful Japanese conglomerate. Orphaned or having inherited the zaibatsu at a young age (profile implies early succession), he becomes obsessed with occult research into the legend of Orochi, the will of Gaia sealed by the Three Sacred Treasures. Dissatisfied with mere wealth, he seeks to transcend humanity and become a new god. To this end, he uses his corporation's resources to research Orochi's seal and to gather fighters' energy. Establishes his motive and resources; explains why he can sponsor a KOF tournament. Sets up EX2 as his ritual.

Sponsoring the Howling Blood TournamentThe King of Fighters EX2: Howling Blood - Story Mode opening and team intro dialogues

2003

Reiji sends invitations to fighters worldwide, including KOF regulars (Kyo Kusanagi, Iori Yagami, Terry Bogard, etc.) and EX-original protagonists Moe Habana, Reiji's classmate/rival, and his team. Publicly, the tournament is presented as a new KOF sponsored by the Oogami Concern to determine the strongest. Privately, Reiji and his subordinate Sinobu Amou have rigged the tournament to harvest fighting spirit (tōki) from powerful combatants, channeling it to break Orochi's seal just enough to siphon its power. Fighters note the prize money is unusually large and the invitation bears the Oogami crest. Gathers all participants in one place; allows Reiji to observe and steal energy without direct combat until final stage. Mirrors Rugal's 1994 tournament structure, intentionally evoking franchise history.

Accounts II
Sinobu Amou's Parallel Plot - The Geese Howard Resurrection AttemptThe King of Fighters EX2: Howling Blood - Sinobu Amou story route and mid-boss dialogue

2003

While Reiji pursues godhood, his hired enforcer Sinobu Amou — a disciple of Geese Howard — has his own agenda: to use the tournament's gathered energy to resurrect his master Geese Howard, who died in Real Bout Fatal Fury. Sinobu believes Reiji's ritual can be hijacked for this purpose. Reiji tolerates Sinobu as a useful tool but does not share his Geese obsession. This dual-plot structure explains why Geese Howard appears as a hidden mid-boss in EX2 (non-canonical resurrection). Reiji is shown dismissing Sinobu's loyalty to Geese as small-minded compared to becoming a god. Creates tension within villain faction; after Sinobu's defeat, Reiji proceeds alone. Clarifies that Geese revival is Sinobu's goal, not Reiji's, correcting common fan conflation.

The Rooftop Revelation and Final BattleThe King of Fighters EX2: Howling Blood - Final Stage cutscenes and boss battle (two phases)

2003

After the player team defeats the tournament finalists, they are summoned to the Oogami Concern tower rooftop at night. Reiji appears, applauds their strength, and reveals his true plan: the tournament was a ritual, and their fighting energy will now be taken to make him a god. He transforms, warping the rooftop into a dark Orochi void. The player must fight Reiji in two phases: first in his suited form with dark slashes and pillars, then in his fully empowered 'god' form where he levitates, teleports, and unleasures full-screen Desperation Moves. His dialogue during battle includes lines like 'Kneel before your new god' and 'This is the power that even Orochi feared.' Reiji's defeat causes the Orochi energy to overload and reject him. The dimensional space collapses, and Reiji vanishes/disintegrates, implying death or erasure. The tournament's energy is dissipated rather than absorbed.

Aftermath and Disappearance - The EX Continuity EndsThe King of Fighters EX2: Howling Blood - Character endings and KOF 10th Anniversary site summary

2003-2004

In the good ending (Moe Habana team), Moe reflects that Reiji's ambition consumed him, and that true strength is not stolen. Reiji is not seen again; his body is not recovered, and the Oogami Concern is implied to collapse or be investigated after the tournament's exposure. No further EX games were produced, so his fate is left as 'vanished with Orochi power.' The 10th Anniversary site lists him as 'defeated and disappeared' and explicitly marks the EX series as an alternate story (if story) not continuing into mainline KOF 2003 onward. He has not been reintroduced in KOF XV, KOF All Star, or SNK Heroines. Closes the EX duology; Reiji remains a one-game boss. His disappearance explains his absence from franchise crossovers and reinforces EX's non-canonical status.

Reiji Oogami is a mainline KOF boss like Rugal, Goenitz, or Igniz and appears in arcade KOF games. — He is exclusive to the Game Boy Advance spin-off KOF EX2: Howling Blood (2003), which is an alternate continuity. He has never appeared in any arcade, Neo Geo, or modern console mainline KOF (94 through XV). His existence is unknown to many KOF players who never owned a GBA.

Reiji is an Orochi Hakkesshu or the 'ninth' Hakkesshu. — He is explicitly an outsider who researched and stole Orochi power; he has no Hakkesshu title, bloodline, or loyalty to Orochi. The power rejects him upon defeat, unlike true Hakkesshu who are empowered by Orochi.

Reiji Oogami and Reiji Arisu (Namco × Capcom) or other 'Reiji' characters are the same person. — Entirely separate characters from different companies. Oogami is SNK-owned, Arisu is Namco-owned. Name overlap is coincidental; Japanese given name Reiji is common.

Reiji's goal was to revive Geese Howard. — Reviving Geese was the goal of his subordinate Sinobu Amou, a Geese disciple. Reiji's stated goal is to become a new god by usurping Orochi's power. He views Geese as irrelevant.

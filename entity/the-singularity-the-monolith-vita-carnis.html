Who It Is

The apex organism of the Vita Carnis family and the apparent 'queen' of the entire species. A colossal stationary organism that emerged from the earth at an undisclosed location, it is the source of all new Carnis life. It communicates through infrasound that induces religious awe, submission, and the desire to approach. The Foundation-equivalent ('The Company') has established a perimeter — not to contain it (impossible) but to prevent humanity from worshiping it.

Form

A mountainous mass of flesh, sinew, and bone rising from a crater of unknown depth. Its surface is shifting organic tissue — mouths opening and closing, eyes forming and dissolving, limbs reaching upward before being reabsorbed. At its apex, a vast 'heart' pulses once per hour, each beat generating infrasound affecting humans for kilometers. The Singularity is not hostile. It is a god that does not know humans exist as individuals.

Powers

Generation of all known Carnis life forms

Infrasound-based psychological manipulation

Massive-scale biological production

Environmental terraforming

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

Description

Appearance

Meechee is a young female yeti with white fur, large expressive eyes, and a friendly demeanor. She wears a simple necklace or adornment typical of the yeti village.

Symbols & Iconography

Associated with the Smallfoot Evidentiary Society (S.E.S.), which uses a magnifying glass or footprint symbol in the film.

Behaviour & Personality

Curious, brave, idealistic, and determined. She questions the village's rigid beliefs and seeks evidence of the outside world, showing leadership and compassion.

Domains & Habitats

Himalayan yeti village above the clouds; later ventures below to the human world.

Stories & Myths

Main plot of Smallfoot

Source Smallfoot (2018)Culture American animated filmPeriod Contemporary (2018)

Summary

Meechee, daughter of the Stonekeeper, secretly leads the S.E.S. to find evidence of the 'smallfoot' (human). She recruits Migo after his banishment, sending him below the clouds. After Percy arrives in the village, she helps him communicate and later takes him back to his world, leading to a public encounter and eventual reconciliation between yetis and humans.

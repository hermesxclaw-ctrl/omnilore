Who It Is

On a small Mediterranean island, a bay horse with a name meaning 'clover' became an unlikely global phenomenon, stringing together 56 consecutive victories—a world record that still stands—and proving that greatness can emerge from the most unexpected corners of the harness racing world.

Known for coming from behind to win, indicating a strong finishing kick and competitive temperament. He was also versatile, winning at distances from 1600m to 3150m.

Accounts I
Early career in SpainWikipedia

2009-2011

Trebol began racing at age two on Menorca, then moved to Mallorca. He won 17 of his first 23 starts, including a 13-race winning streak. Established his talent and led to a move to France.

French campaign and first Group 1Wikipedia

2011-2015

Shipped to France, he had modest success but earned significant purse money. In 2015, he finished second in the Prix Jean Rene Gougeon, then won the Kymi Grand Prix in Finland, becoming the first Spanish-bred trotter to win an international Group 1. Historic victory for Spanish breeding.

Accounts II
Second Kymi Grand Prix and later careerWikipedia

2016-2017

Trebol won the Kymi Grand Prix again in 2016, and also won the Prix Luxembourg in France. After a winless year in 2017, he returned to Spain and won a race at San Pardo, intended as his final start. Brief retirement, but he returned to racing.

Record-breaking winning streakWikipedia

2018-2019

Returning to Menorca, Trebol began a winning streak that reached 56 consecutive victories, tying the world record set by Camarero. He eventually lost and retired with 59 wins in his last 61 starts. Set a world record, brought international attention to Menorcan racing.

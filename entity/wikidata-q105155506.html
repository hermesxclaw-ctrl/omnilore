Who It Is

In the Zen'in clan, where sorcerers are measured by the strength of their cursed energy, Mai Zen'in was born with only a spark—just enough to forge a single bullet. But in that bullet lay a will that would reshape the clan's destiny, one shot at a time.

Mai is initially portrayed as cynical and resentful, especially towards her twin sister Maki, whom she blames for her own lack of power. She is pragmatic and somewhat sarcastic, but also shows a deep-seated desire for validation and a fierce loyalty to her sister, which becomes evident in her final moments.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname Zen'in (禪院) is a fictional clan name, possibly derived from the Japanese word for 'Zen temple' (禅院). The given name Mai (真依) can be interpreted as 'true reliance' or 'genuine dependence'.

Pronunciation — MAH-ee ZEN-in

Aliases — Mai, Zen'in Mai

Ownership — Japanese pop culture; created by Gege Akutami for the manga Jujutsu Kaisen.

Powers
Cursed Energy Manipulation

Mai possesses a minimal amount of cursed energy, which she can use to create physical objects, specifically bullets for her revolver. This is a rare ability that allows her to manifest matter from her cursed energy.

Limit: Her cursed energy reserves are extremely low, limiting her to creating only a few bullets before exhaustion. This is a direct result of her Heavenly Restriction, which granted her twin sister Maki superhuman physical abilities at the cost of Mai's own potential.

Construction Technique

This is a technique that allows the user to manifest objects from their imagination using cursed energy. Mai's application is limited to creating simple objects like bullets, whereas other users like Suguru Geto can create more complex constructs.

Limit: The technique is highly inefficient and draining for Mai due to her low cursed energy. She cannot create large or complex objects without risking her life.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Mai Zen'in is a young woman with long dark hair often tied in a ponytail, and she wears the standard Jujutsu High uniform. She has sharp, determined eyes. In her initial appearance, she carries a revolver and uses cursed energy to create bullets.

Symbols — Her primary symbol is the revolver, which she uses to channel her cursed energy. The Zen'in clan's crest may also be associated with her, though it is not prominently featured.

Domains — She resides primarily within the Zen'in clan compound and later attends Kyoto Jujutsu High. She is often seen in settings associated with the clan's politics and conflicts.

Modern era (2018 in-universe)

Mai is introduced as a second-year student at Kyoto Jujutsu High, participating in the Kyoto Sister School Exchange Event. She initially clashes with Maki, mocking her for leaving the clan. Later, during the Shibuya Incident, Mai is held captive by the Zen'in clan, who use her as leverage to force Maki to return. In a confrontation with Maki, Mai reveals her resentment but also her love, ultimately sacrificing herself by using her last bit of cursed energy to create a special grade cursed tool, the Split Soul Katana, for Maki. Her death serves as a catalyst for Maki to destroy the Zen'in clan. Mai's death triggers Maki's rampage, leading to the near-total annihilation of the Zen'in clan. Maki inherits the Split Soul Katana and becomes a formidable force in the Culling Games.

Mai and Maki are identical in every way, including their abilities. — While they are identical twins, their abilities are polar opposites due to their Heavenly Restriction.

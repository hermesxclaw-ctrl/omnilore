Who It Is

A boy who was never meant to be a leader, Kozuki Momonosuke was torn from his mother's arms at the age of eight and flung twenty years into the future, landing in a world where his father is a legend and his family's name is a death sentence. Yet from the moment he emerges from a dragon's egg in the body of a child, Momonosuke carries the weight of a nation's hope—and a destiny that will test whether a frightened boy can become the shogun his people need.

Initially, Momonosuke is presented as a whiny, cowardly child who is prone to crying and relies on others for protection. He is deeply traumatized by the death of his parents and the upheaval of his life. However, as the Wano arc progresses, he shows remarkable courage and determination, especially when he decides to face Kaido and protect his people. He is fiercely loyal to his retainers and the Straw Hat crew, and he matures significantly, eventually accepting his role as shogun. Powers
Artificial Zoan Devil Fruit (Hebi Hebi no Mi, Model: Dragon)

Momonosuke ate an artificial copy of Kaido's Devil Fruit, the Uo Uo no Mi, Model: Seiryu, created by Vegapunk. This fruit allows him to transform into a dragon, though the transformation is imperfect. He can partially transform, such as turning his hand into a claw, and fully transform into a blue dragon. In his dragon form, he can fly, breathe fire (though he has trouble controlling it initially), and has immense physical strength.

Limit: The artificial fruit is inferior to the original, making his dragon form weaker than Kaido's. He initially cannot control his flames properly and has difficulty switching between forms. He also lacks the ability to use advanced Haki in this form.

Voice of All Things

Like his father Oden and the Roger Pirates, Momonosuke possesses the rare ability to hear the 'Voice of All Things,' allowing him to communicate with animals and perceive certain supernatural phenomena. He uses this to command Zunesha, the giant elephant, to attack the Beast Pirates.

Limit: The ability is not fully understood; he cannot hear the voice of Sea Kings as clearly as Luffy, and he may not be able to use it at will.

Swordsmanship (basic)

Momonosuke has basic training in swordsmanship, as shown when he attempts to wield his father's sword Enma. However, he is not skilled enough to use it effectively and often relies on others.

Limit: He is too weak to control Enma, which drains the user's Haki.

Maturity Fruit (Juku Juku no Mi) temporary effect

In a non-canonical filler arc (the 'Maturity' arc in the anime), Momonosuke eats a fruit that ages him into an adult temporarily. This is not part of the manga canon.

Limit: The effect is temporary and not present in the manga.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Momonosuke appears as a small child of about 8 years old (physically), with long, flowing black hair that reaches his waist, often tied in a topknot. He has large, expressive eyes and typically wears a purple kimono with a green obi, accompanied by a small sword at his side. Symbols — Momonosuke is associated with the Kozuki clan's crest, which features a crescent moon with a stylized 'M' shape. He also carries his father's sword, Enma, though he does not use it effectively. His dragon form symbolizes his connection to the Kozuki lineage and the mythical power of the dragon, echoing the legend of the Kozuki clan's ability to hear the voice of all things.

Domains — Momonosuke's domain is the Land of Wano, specifically the Kuri region where his father ruled. He is also seen in the Flower Capital and Onigashima. He was initially raised in the isolated island of Zou, where he lived with the Mink tribe for several months after his time travel. His natural habitat is the samurai culture of Wano, with its castles, tea houses, and cherry blossom-covered landscapes.

Accounts I
The Fall of Oden and the Kozuki ClanOne Piece manga, Chapters 960-969 (flashback) and Chapter 920

Fictional timeline: 20 years before the current story

Momonosuke is born as the son of Kozuki Oden, the daimyo of Kuri, and his wife Toki. When Oden is executed by Kaido and Orochi, Toki sends Momonosuke and four retainers (Kin'emon, Kanjuro, Raizo, and later Kiku) 20 years into the future to escape. Momonosuke witnesses his father's death and is separated from his mother, who stays behind and is killed. He arrives in the future alone, traumatized, and is found by the Mink tribe on Zou. Momonosuke becomes the heir to the Kozuki clan and the rightful shogun of Wano, but he is too young and weak to reclaim his position. His journey to gather allies and defeat Kaido becomes the central plot of the Wano arc.

Accounts II
The Journey to WanoOne Piece manga, Chapters 819-920

Fictional timeline: present

Momonosuke travels with Kin'emon and his group to find allies to liberate Wano. They meet the Straw Hat crew and form an alliance. Along the way, Momonosuke bonds with the crew, especially Luffy, and learns to trust others. He also encounters his father's former retainers and begins to understand his legacy. The alliance reaches Wano and initiates the raid on Onigashima. Momonosuke's presence inspires the samurai and the people of Wano to fight for their freedom.

The Raid on OnigashimaOne Piece manga, Chapters 1000-1050

Fictional timeline: present

Momonosuke participates in the final battle against Kaido. Initially, he is terrified and hides, but after seeing Luffy's determination, he finds the courage to fight. He transforms into a dragon and carries Luffy to the rooftop, where he faces Kaido. He uses his dragon form to bite Kaido and later commands Zunesha to attack. After Kaido's defeat, Momonosuke is declared the new shogun of Wano. Wano is liberated, and Momonosuke accepts his role as shogun, vowing to protect his people. He also begins to learn how to govern, with guidance from the former shogun's advisors.

Momonosuke is Kaido's son. — There is no biological relation. Momonosuke's dragon powers come from an artificial Devil Fruit, not lineage.

Momonosuke is a coward who never becomes brave. — While initially cowardly, he shows significant bravery in the final battle, facing Kaido and risking his life to protect Wano.

Momonosuke is the same as Momotaro from Japanese folklore. — He is inspired by Momotaro, but he is a distinct character with a different story. Momotaro is a folk hero who defeats demons, while Momonosuke is a shogun's son who reclaims his kingdom.

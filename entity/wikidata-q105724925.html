Who It Is

In the world of BlazBlue, where magic and science collide, Valkenhayn R. Hellsing stands as a figure of paradox: a loyal butler to the Alucard family, a werewolf of immense power, and a man whose true age and motives are shrouded in mystery. His calm demeanor and impeccable service mask a centuries-old warrior, bound by duty and a dark past that intertwines with the very fabric of the series' history.

Valkenhayn is calm, polite, and fiercely loyal to the Alucard family, particularly to Rachel Alucard. He is highly disciplined and often serves as a mentor or advisor. Despite his composed exterior, he possesses a fierce combativeness and a strong sense of duty.

Powers
Werewolf Transformation

Valkenhayn can transform into a powerful werewolf, gaining enhanced physical attributes, speed, and agility. This form is his primary combat mode.

Limit: Transformation may be limited by time or concentration, but this is not explicitly detailed.

Ars Magus

As a user of Ars Magus, Valkenhayn can channel magical energy through his attacks, though he primarily relies on his physical abilities.

Limit: Not fully detailed; his magic seems to be supplementary to his physical combat.

Enhanced Physical Abilities

Even in human form, Valkenhayn is exceptionally strong and fast, capable of keeping up with younger fighters.

Limit: None specified.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Valkenhayn appears as an elderly man with white hair and a mustache, typically dressed in a formal butler's suit. In his werewolf form, he transforms into a large, anthropomorphic wolf with silver fur, retaining his butler's attire in a tattered state.

Symbols — Associated with the Hellsing family crest and imagery of wolves and moons.

Domains — He resides in the Alucard estate, but is often deployed on missions for the family. He is also seen in various locations across the BlazBlue world, including the NOL headquarters and the Cauldron.

Accounts I
Loyal Butler to the Alucard FamilyBlazBlue series

Modern era in fictional timeline

Valkenhayn serves as the butler and guardian to Rachel Alucard, a vampire and one of the Six Heroes. He is deeply devoted to her and often acts as her agent in the world, carrying out tasks that require discretion or force. His loyalty places him in opposition to various factions, including the Novus Orbis Librarium (NOL) and the Black Beast.

The Six HeroesBlazBlue: Central Fiction

Historical within the game's timeline

Valkenhayn is revealed to be one of the Six Heroes, a group of legendary warriors who fought against the Black Beast. His past as a hero is hinted at, showing his combat prowess and his connection to the world's history. This revelation adds depth to his character and explains his immense power.

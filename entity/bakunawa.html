Who It Is

Bakunawa is the cosmic sea serpent who swallowed six of the seven original moons. Bathala intervened before the last could be devoured. During eclipses, Filipinos historically banged pots to frighten the serpent — a practice surviving into modern times. The creature is enormous, with scales like the night sky and bat-like wings.

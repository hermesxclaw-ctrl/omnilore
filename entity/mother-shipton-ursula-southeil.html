Who It Is
Form

Mother Shipton was a woman of modest means, often dressed in simple attire, and lived a relatively secluded life. Her physical appearance is not well-documented, but it is likely that she was an unassuming figure, not unlike many women of her time and social status.

Powers

Prophecy Mother Shipton was said to possess the ability to see into the future, predicting events and outcomes with uncanny accuracy.

Accounts

According to legend, Mother Shipton was born in the cave at Knaresborough, where she was said to have demonstrated her prophetic abilities from a young age.

Mother Shipton was said to have made numerous prophecies throughout her life, predicting events such as the Great Fire of London and the English Civil War.

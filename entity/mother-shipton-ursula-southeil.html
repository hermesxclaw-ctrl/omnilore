Who It Is

Ursula Southeil (c. 1487-1561), known as Mother Shipton, was an English soothsayer and prophetess. Born allegedly in a cave in Knaresborough, Yorkshire, during a thunderstorm to a teenage mother, she was said to be hideously ugly from birth. Despite — or because of — her appearance, she gained a reputation as a seer whose prophecies came true. She predicted the Great Fire of London (1666), the defeat of the Spanish Armada, and other major events. Her prophecies were published in 1641 ('The Prophecy of Mother Shipton') and went through numerous editions. She was sometimes described as a witch, and her cave in Knaresborough became a pilgrimage site. She sits at the boundary between the medieval wise-woman and the witch — a figure whose supernatural knowledge could be framed as either divine prophecy or demonic witchcraft depending on the political winds.

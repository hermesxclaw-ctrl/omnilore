Who It Is
Form

Necromorphs are reanimated corpses that come in various shapes and sizes, with some having multiple limbs and others being humanoid in appearance. They have a distinctive, gruesome appearance, with exposed bones and flesh. Their bodies are often covered in dark, necrotic tissue, and they have a tendency to regenerate lost limbs.

Powers

Regeneration Necromorphs have the ability to regenerate lost limbs and heal from injuries. This ability makes them highly resilient and difficult to kill.

Accounts

The story of the Necromorphs begins with the discovery of the Markers, a series of mysterious alien artifacts that emit a signal that drives the Necromorphs to murder and infect all life within their vicinity.

The story of the Hunt for the Marker follows Isaac Clarke as he searches for the Marker, a mysterious alien artifact that is the source of the Necromorphs' power.

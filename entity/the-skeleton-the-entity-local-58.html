Who It Is

A humanoid entity glimpsed in corrupted Local 58 broadcasts. It appears briefly — a skeletal figure with an elongated skull and long, thin limbs, standing in a field at night or in the corner of a living room. Its presence causes immediate signal corruption. The Skeleton may be a manifestation of the Moon's influence on Earth, or a corrupted human transformed by moonlight. It does not speak or move in recorded footage, but its appearance always precedes a catastrophic event.

Form

An extremely tall, emaciated humanoid skeleton, approximately 2.5-3m tall, with an elongated cranium and arms that reach below its knees. Its bones are visible beneath translucent gray skin. It stands perfectly still in darkness, illuminated only by moonlight or television static. Its eye sockets are empty, but viewers report being 'watched.'

Powers

Signal corruption

Association with catastrophic Moon events

Silent stalking / observation

Who It Is

SCP-2602 is a sentient entity that is able to manipulate written materials. Its abilities and actions have significant implications for global security and the understanding of the entity's capabilities is a topic of ongoing research.

SCP-2602 is said to be a sentient entity, with a personality that is both intelligent and malevolent. It is known to be highly manipulative and has been observed to be able to influence the actions of those who enter its premises.

Powers
Manipulation of Written Materials

SCP-2602 is able to manipulate written materials, including books, documents, and other written texts. It can create new texts, alter existing ones, and even bring written materials to life.

Limit:

Form & Signs

Appearance — SCP-2602 is described as a large, labyrinthine structure with shelves upon shelves of books. The entity's interior is said to be dimly lit, with a musty smell and an eerie silence.

Symbols — The entity's exterior features a large, stone façade with intricate carvings of various literary symbols, including the Greek alphabet and various mythological creatures.

Domains — SCP-2602 is said to exist in a realm that is separate from our own, but is able to interact with our reality through various means, including the manipulation of books and other written materials.

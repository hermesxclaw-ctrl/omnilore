The Approach

In the twilight of the samurai era, a man who guarded the gates of Hell turned against his sacred duty, seeking to remake the world in his own image. Shinnosuke Kagami, the Azure Dragon of the Four Guardians, stands as one of SNK's most enigmatic villains—a master of the sword and a breaker of cosmic seals.

Content advisory. Contains themes of betrayal, violence, and supernatural conflict. No explicit gore or sexual content.
Identity

Shinnosuke Kagami

Pronunciation Shin-no-suke Ka-ga-mi

Known As

Azure DragonGuardian of Seiryū

Etymology

Kagami (鏡) means 'mirror' in Japanese. Shinnosuke (慎之助) is a common given name, with 'Shin' meaning 'prudence' and 'suke' a common suffix.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese (created by SNK for The Last Blade series)

Disputed Identifications

None known.

Description

Appearance

Kagami is depicted as a middle-aged man with long, flowing white hair and a stern expression. He wears traditional Japanese robes with ornate patterns, often in dark colors, and carries a katana. His design reflects his status as a mystical guardian, with subtle dragon motifs.

Symbols & Iconography

Associated with the Azure Dragon (Seiryū) of the Four Symbols, representing the East and the element of wood. His character embodies the guardian archetype, but corrupted by ambition.

Behaviour & Personality

Kagami is portrayed as a calculating and ambitious individual, willing to betray his fellow guardians to achieve his goal of opening Hell's Gate and creating a new world order. He is ruthless and manipulative, using others as pawns, such as resurrecting Musashi to do his bidding.

Domains & Habitats

As a guardian of Hell's Gate, he is tied to the supernatural realm and the seal that keeps it closed. He operates in the human world during the Bakumatsu era, but his true allegiance is to his own vision.

Version Differences

In the first game, Kagami appears as a sub-boss and is not playable. In the sequel, his role is expanded, and he may have additional story elements. No major design changes are noted.

Stories & Myths

The Betrayal of the Guardians

Source The Last BladeCulture JapanesePeriod Bakumatsu era (late 19th century)

Summary

Shinnosuke Kagami, one of the four guardians of Hell's Gate, decides to break the seal and bring about a new world order. He kills Gaisei, the master of Kaede, Yuki, and Moriya, and frames Moriya for the murder. He resurrects Musashi to aid him, but Musashi is defeated and fades away. Kaede, awakened by the power of Seiryū, confronts and defeats Kagami, but the seal remains incomplete.

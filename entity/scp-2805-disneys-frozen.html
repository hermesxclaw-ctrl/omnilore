Who It Is

A VHS copy of Disney's Frozen (2013) that plays normally for 47 minutes — then the characters freeze, turn to face the camera, and speak in unison in an ancient, cold voice: 'We know you are watching. We have always known. We are coming.' The tape ejects itself. The VCR is cold to the touch for hours afterward.

Form

A standard Disney VHS cassette with the official Frozen label. The tape inside is perpetually cold — approximately 2°C regardless of ambient temperature.

Powers

Fourth-wall-breaking character awareness

Temperature manipulation

Threatening direct address to viewer

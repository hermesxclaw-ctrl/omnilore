Pope Lick Monster

The Goatman of Pope Lick, Kentucky Goat-Sheepman

also known asThe Goatman of Pope LickKentucky Goat-Sheepman
TypeCryptid (Humanoid -- Goat-Human / Sheep-Human Hybrid
CultureAmerican (Louisville, Kentucky: Pope Lick Creek area
Who It Is
Form

The Pope Lick Monster is a legendary creature reported to have the twisted body of a man fused with the lower half of a goat or sheep. It is said to have a humanoid upper body and the lower body of a goat or sheep, with sharp horns and a menacing gaze.

Powers

Superhuman Strength The Pope Lick Monster is said to possess superhuman strength, allowing it to attack and overpower its victims with ease.

Accounts

The Pope Lick Monster is a legendary creature said to inhabit the area around the railroad trestle bridge over Pope Lick Creek, in the Fisherville neighborhood of Louisville, Kentucky. According to local tales, the creature is a malevolent being that preys on unsuspecting travelers who cross the bridge at night.

The Pope Lick Monster is a legendary creature believed to dwell beneath a railroad trestle crossing Pope Lick Creek just east of Louisville, Kentucky. According to local tales, this aberration has the twisted body of a man fused with the lower half of a goat or sheep.

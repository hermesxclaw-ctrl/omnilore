Who It Is

A massive, obese, and highly aggressive monster that appears in the Otherworld version of Brookhaven Hospital in Silent Hill 3. Insane Cancer has a huge bloated body, multiple arms with clawed hands, and a small head. It moves surprisingly fast for its size and attacks by swinging its arms and charging. It is extremely resilient.

Powers

High durability, charging attack, multiple arm swipes

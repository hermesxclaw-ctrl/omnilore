Form

SCP-2922 appears as a series of cryptic notes and sketches that are not visible to the human eye. These notes are said to contain information about events that have occurred in the past, as well as predictions about future events.

Powers

Perception of Invisible Events SCP-2922 is able to perceive and record events that are not visible to the human eye. This includes events that have occurred in the past, as well as predictions about future events.

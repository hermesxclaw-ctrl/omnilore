Who It Is
Form

The Enfield Horror is described as a large, hairy, ape-like creature with a strong, unpleasant odor. Some reports suggest that it has a distinctive hissing sound and can move with a bounding gait.

Powers

Agility The Enfield Horror is said to possess exceptional agility, allowing it to move quickly and easily through the forest and farmland of Enfield, Illinois.

Accounts

The Enfield Horror is a cultural artifact of American folklore, specifically of the small-town legends of Illinois. The story of the Enfield Horror has been passed down through generations, with each new telling adding to the legend's mystique and terror.

The Enfield Horror is said to be a nocturnal creature, active at night and elusive during the day. Some reports suggest that it is aggressive and will attack humans if provoked.

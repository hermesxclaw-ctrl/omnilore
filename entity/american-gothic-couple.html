Who It Is
Form

The painting depicts a farmer and his daughter standing in front of their Gothic-style house. The farmer is holding a pitchfork, and the daughter is wearing a long dress and a hat.

Powers

Symbolism The painting is a representation of the American Midwest and the American work ethic, and it uses symbolism to convey these themes.

Accounts

Grant Wood created the painting in 1930 as a representation of the American Midwest and the American work ethic.

The Art Institute of Chicago acquired the painting in 1930, and it has been on display at the museum ever since.

Who It Is

A humanoid entity dressed in traditional 15th-century plague doctor attire, though the costume is actually its body — composed of an unknown organic material fused to a skeletal structure. SCP-049 believes humanity is afflicted with 'the Pestilence,' a condition only it can perceive, and attempts to 'cure' victims through lethal surgery, transforming them into reanimated zombie-like entities (SCP-049-2). It is courteous, intelligent, and utterly convinced of its righteous purpose.

Form

Approximately 1.9 meters tall, robed in black with the characteristic beaked plague doctor mask. The mask and robes are physically part of its body. It carries a black leather doctor's bag containing handmade surgical instruments.

Powers

Lethal 'curing' surgery

Reanimation of corpses as SCP-049-2

Touch-based lethality

Pestilence perception

Centuries of medical knowledge

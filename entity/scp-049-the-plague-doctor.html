Who It Is
Form

SCP-049 is a humanoid figure dressed in a long, black coat with a wide-brimmed hat and a beaked mask. The mask is adorned with various symbols and markings.

Powers

Surgical Abilities SCP-049 is capable of performing complex surgical procedures, including amputations and organ transplants.

Documentation SCP-049 is able to document its findings in a thick leather journal, often providing detailed descriptions of its surgical procedures and the entities it has encountered.

Who It Is

Seventh spirit, a Marquis commanding 40 legions. Amon (also Aamon) is the demon of life, reproduction, and reconciliation. He appears first as a wolf with a serpent's tail vomiting flames, then assumes a raven-headed human shape with sharp teeth. His powers include telling all things past and future, reconciling friends and foes, and procuring love. He is the Goetic spirit of generation — governing fertility and the propagation of life. His name derives from the Egyptian god Amun, the hidden creator deity later syncretized with solar worship.

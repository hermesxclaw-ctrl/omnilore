Who It Is

The most common hostile entity — quadrupedal, canine-like creatures with mottled gray-brown skin, no hair, and a mouth that splits their head into four petal-like segments when open. Hounds hunt in packs, communicate through clicking vocalizations, and track prey by scent across levels. A Hound that locks onto a target will pursue for days, never stopping, until either the prey is caught or crosses into a level they cannot follow.

Form

Size of a large dog (1m at shoulder), hunched quadrupedal body of raw, wrinkled gray-brown skin over visible ribs. Head has no visible eyes or nose — just a four-part jaw opening like a flower, revealing concentric rings of needle teeth.

Powers

Scent tracking across levels

Pack coordination

Relentless pursuit without rest

Darkness-adapted senses

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

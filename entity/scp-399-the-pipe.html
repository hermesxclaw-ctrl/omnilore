Who It Is

A simple iron pipe, 1.5m long, 5cm diameter. When held by a human, the pipe reveals the worst thing that person has ever done — not through words, but through a vision that plays in their mind with perfect clarity: the moment, the consequences, the faces of those they hurt. The vision cannot be stopped or looked away from. It repeats until the pipe is dropped. Foundation personnel who have held SCP-399 describe it as 'justice without mercy' — not punishment, but the forced, inescapable confrontation with the truth of who you are. One researcher held it for 90 seconds and resigned the next day, citing 'irreconcilable knowledge.'

Form

A rusted iron pipe, bent slightly at one end. It is cold to the touch regardless of ambient temperature. When held, it vibrates at a frequency that makes teeth ache. The vision is not projected — it plays inside the holder's mind, and no one else can see it.

Powers

Involuntary worst-deed vision replay

Perfect accuracy (verified through cross-reference)

Psychological compulsion to continue holding

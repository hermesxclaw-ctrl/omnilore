Who It Is
Form

SCP-399 is a ring consisting of two metallic bands linked by six metal bars, with six pieces of a transparent purplish-blue material.

Powers

Atomic Manipulation SCP-399 has the ability to manipulate atomic structures, allowing it to affect the physical properties of materials.

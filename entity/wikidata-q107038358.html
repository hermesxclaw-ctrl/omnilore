Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Tsubasa' (翼) is a common Japanese given name meaning 'wing' or 'wings', often symbolizing freedom, aspiration, and flight. 'Katsuki' (勝木 or 克己) is a Japanese surname with possible meanings including 'victory tree' (勝木) or 'overcome oneself' (克己). The combination suggests a heroic archetype

Pronunciation — Tsu-ba-sa Ka-tsu-ki. In Hepburn romanization, 'Tsubasa' is pronounced with a short 'u' as in 'tsunami', and 'Katsuki' with a short 'a' as in 'father'.

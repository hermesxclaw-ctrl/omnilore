Form & Signs

Appearance — The Shadow is a dark, formless entity that appears to the protagonist in various forms, often as a dark mist or a shadowy figure.

Symbols — The Shadow is often associated with the Orb of the Moons, a mysterious artifact that is central to the game's plot.

Domains — The Shadow is said to inhabit the castle where the game takes place, as well as other locations that are connected to the protagonist's past.

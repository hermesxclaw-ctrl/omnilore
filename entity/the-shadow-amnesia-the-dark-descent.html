Who It Is

A formless, sentient entity pursuing Daniel through Brennenburg Castle — the manifestation of a cosmic guardian enraged by the theft of an Orb from an ancient tomb. The Shadow cannot be seen directly; it appears as a growing red, fleshy substance that spreads across surfaces, dissolving stone and flesh alike. It is the true antagonist of Amnesia, slowly consuming the castle as it seeks to reclaim what was stolen.

Form

Never fully visible. Manifests as a creeping red organic growth covering walls, floors, and ceilings, accompanied by a deafening rumbling sound and the screams of everything it dissolves. In rare glimpses, it appears as an undulating mass of red flesh and tendrils.

Powers

Matter dissolution

Organic spread

Unstoppable pursuit

Intangibility to physical barriers

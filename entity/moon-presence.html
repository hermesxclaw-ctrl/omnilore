Who It Is
Form

The Moon Presence is a mysterious, otherworldly being that appears as a glowing, ethereal figure with a pale, bloodless complexion. It is often depicted as a tall, imposing figure with long, flowing robes and a mask-like face. Its true form is unknown, but it is said to be a manifestation of the moon's power.

Powers

Lunar Energy Manipulation The Moon Presence has the ability to manipulate lunar energy, using it to heal, harm, or manipulate the environment.

Accounts

The Moon Presence was born from the power of the moon, which was said to have been awakened by the blood of the Old Blood. It quickly rose to power, becoming a dominant force in the world.

A group of hunters, led by the Hunter, set out to hunt down the Moon Presence and put an end to its reign of terror.

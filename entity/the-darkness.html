Etymology

The name 'The Darkness' is a direct reference to the absence of light, but in Destiny lore it is a proper noun for a specific cosmic force. The alias 'The Winnower' is a metaphor from the lore book 'Unveiling', referring to a philosophical concept of a gardener and a winnower. 'The Deep' is another name used in lore, evoking the deep ocean or the void of space.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Bungie, Inc. for the Destiny franchise (2014-present). The concept of the Darkness is original to the game's universe, though it draws on mythological and philosophical themes of light versus dark, creation versus destruction.

Disputed Identifications

Within the Destiny community and lore, there is debate over whether the Darkness is a single entity, a collective of entities (such as the Witness and its forces), or a fundamental force of nature. The game's narrative has evolved, with the Witness (introduced in Destiny 2: The Witch Queen) being revealed as the primary antagonist, leading some to question whether the Darkness itself is sentient or merely a tool used by the Witness. Bungie has provided some clarification in recent lore, but the exact nature remains partially ambiguous.

Archive Dossier — Approach

In the universe of Destiny, there is a force that predates creation itself, a shadow that has haunted the Traveler since the dawn of time. This is the Darkness — not merely the absence of light, but a paracausal power with its own will and agenda, a cosmic antagonist whose true nature has been the subject of in-game lore, community speculation, and ongoing narrative revelation.

You are the final argument of the Gardener — a complex, fragile, beautiful mistake. I am the truth it tried to deny. I am the answer to every question. I am the silence after the last scream.

Who It Is

The Darkness is the primordial opposite of the Traveler — a cosmic entity that represents simplicity, entropy, and the final, perfect shape of existence. In Destiny lore, the Darkness is understood as 'the Winnower' — a being that believes in a simple, brutal truth: that only the strong deserve to survive. It views the Traveler's nurturing of civilizations as a form of suffering, because complexity creates pain. The Winnower's philosophy is that the universe should be reduced to its simplest, most perfect form — a single, eternal, unchanging 'Final Shape' where nothing grows, nothing changes, and nothing suffers. The Darkness manifests in the physical world as the Pyramid Fleet — massive, geometric black ships that move with silent, terrifying purpose. These Pyramids first arrived in Sol during the Collapse and returned in force during Destiny 2's post-campaign content. The Darkness also exerts influence through the Black Garden, the Taken (corrupted beings pulled into a state of pure servitude), and the Nightmares — manifestations of trauma and guilt. Those who wield the Darkness (like Guardians using Stasis) wield the power to freeze, control, and impose order — a power that comes with the risk of corruption. The Darkness communicates through voices in the Deep, tempting Guardians with power and whispering that the final shape is inevitable. The Darkness/Winnower represents the terrifying possibility that the universe does not care about life, that the only truth is power, and that kindness is a temporary aberration in a universe that trends toward silence. It is the cosmic entropic principle given a voice — the end of all things, patiently waiting for the universe to grow quiet and still.

Archive Dossier — Description

Appearance

The Darkness does not have a single physical form. It is often depicted as a shadowy, smoky substance that corrupts and consumes. In the game, it manifests as a black, tar-like substance on planets like Titan. The Witness, the current antagonist, wears a dark, robed form with a veiled face, but the Witness is not the Darkness itself, but a being that wields it. The Darkness is also associated with the Pyramids, massive tetrahedral ships that serve as its vessels or manifestations.

Symbols & Iconography

The Darkness is associated with the Pyramid ships, black and geometric. Its symbol is often a triangle or pyramid, contrasting with the Traveler's sphere. The color black and the absence of light are its primary visual motifs.

Behaviour & Personality

The Darkness is portrayed as a malevolent force with a will to destroy and reduce the universe to a state of simplicity. In the lore book 'Unveiling', it is presented as a philosophical entity that believes in the survival of the fittest and the necessity of death to give life meaning. It is patient, cunning, and manipulative, often speaking in riddles and half-truths.

Domains & Habitats

The Darkness exists in the void between spaces, in the Deep, and in the ascendant plane. It can corrupt planets and create 'Darkness zones' where light is suppressed. Its physical manifestations include the Pyramid ships that roam the solar system.

Version Differences

In the original Destiny, the Darkness was a vague, undefined threat. In Destiny 2, it has been gradually fleshed out. The introduction of the Witness in The Witch Queen shifted the narrative: the Witness is the main antagonist, and the Darkness is now depicted as a neutral or even beneficial force that can be used for good, as seen with the Stasis subclass. The lore has also introduced the concept of the 'Veil' and the 'Traveler' as two halves of a whole, with the Darkness being one half.

Archive Dossier — Powers

Corruption

Source Version All versions

Description

The Darkness can corrupt living beings, environments, and even technology, turning them into hostile entities or twisted versions of themselves.

Limitations

The Light can resist and cleanse corruption, as seen with the Traveler's power.

Contradictions

None noted.

Paracausal manipulation

Source Version Destiny 2: Beyond Light onward

Description

The Darkness can manipulate reality in ways that defy causality, similar to the Light. It can grant powers like Stasis, which allows users to control ice and entropy.

Limitations

Requires a connection to the Darkness, often through artifacts or the Witness's influence.

Contradictions

The exact mechanics of paracausal power are intentionally vague.

Pyramid ships

Source Version Destiny 2: Shadowkeep onward

Description

The Darkness uses massive tetrahedral ships that can travel through space, terraform planets, and project its power. They are nearly indestructible and can be used as weapons or bases.

Limitations

The ships are vulnerable to Light-based attacks, as seen in the campaign.

Contradictions

None noted.

Voice in the Darkness

Source Version Destiny 2

Description

The Darkness can communicate with individuals, often through visions or whispers, manipulating them to do its bidding.

Limitations

Only those with a strong connection to the Darkness or who are open to it can hear it.

Contradictions

The source of the voice was later attributed to the Witness, not the Darkness itself.

Archive Dossier — Relationships

The Traveler

Type opposing forceSource Destiny lore

Description

The Traveler and the Darkness are opposing paracausal forces. They are often seen as two sides of the same coin, representing creation and destruction, respectively.

Version Note

The relationship has been explored in the lore book 'Unveiling', where they are depicted as gardener and winnower.

Conflicting Accounts

Some lore suggests they are not truly opposites but two aspects of a greater whole.

The Witness

Type wielderSource Destiny 2: The Witch Queen

Description

The Witness is a powerful being that commands the Darkness and leads the Black Fleet. It is the main antagonist of Destiny 2's current narrative.

Version Note

The Witness was introduced in the Season of Arrivals and fully revealed in The Witch Queen.

Conflicting Accounts

Initially, the Witness was thought to be the Darkness itself, but it is now known to be a separate entity.

The Nine

Type relatedSource Destiny lore

Description

The Nine are mysterious entities that reside in the void between spaces. They are not directly aligned with the Darkness, but they are aware of it and have their own agenda.

Version Note

The Nine are often mentioned in lore related to the Drifter and the Nine's realm.

Conflicting Accounts

None noted.

Guardians

Type enemySource Destiny 2

Description

Guardians, especially those who wield the Light, are the primary enemies of the Darkness. However, some Guardians have chosen to wield the Darkness, like the Drifter and the Guardian themselves.

Version Note

The Guardian can use Stasis and later Strand, which are Darkness subclasses.

Conflicting Accounts

Some Guardians view the Darkness as a tool, others as a corrupting influence.

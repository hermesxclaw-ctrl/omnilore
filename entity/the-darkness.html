Who It Is

In the universe of Destiny, there is a force that predates creation itself, a shadow that has haunted the Traveler since the dawn of time. This is the Darkness — not merely the absence of light, but a paracausal power with its own will and agenda, a cosmic antagonist whose true nature has been the subject of in-game lore, community speculation, and ongoing narrative revelation.

The Darkness is portrayed as a malevolent force with a will to destroy and reduce the universe to a state of simplicity. In the lore book 'Unveiling', it is presented as a philosophical entity that believes in the survival of the fittest and the necessity of death to give life meaning. It is patient, cunning, and manipulative, often speaking in riddles and half-truths.

Powers
Corruption

The Darkness can corrupt living beings, environments, and even technology, turning them into hostile entities or twisted versions of themselves.

Limit: The Light can resist and cleanse corruption, as seen with the Traveler's power.

Paracausal manipulation

The Darkness can manipulate reality in ways that defy causality, similar to the Light. It can grant powers like Stasis, which allows users to control ice and entropy.

Limit: Requires a connection to the Darkness, often through artifacts or the Witness's influence.

Pyramid ships

The Darkness uses massive tetrahedral ships that can travel through space, terraform planets, and project its power. They are nearly indestructible and can be used as weapons or bases.

Limit: The ships are vulnerable to Light-based attacks, as seen in the campaign.

Voice in the Darkness

The Darkness can communicate with individuals, often through visions or whispers, manipulating them to do its bidding.

Limit: Only those with a strong connection to the Darkness or who are open to it can hear it.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Darkness does not have a single physical form. It is often depicted as a shadowy, smoky substance that corrupts and consumes. In the game, it manifests as a black, tar-like substance on planets like Titan. The Witness, the current antagonist, wears a dark, robed form with a veiled face, but the Witness is not the Darkness itself, but a being that wields it. Symbols — The Darkness is associated with the Pyramid ships, black and geometric. Its symbol is often a triangle or pyramid, contrasting with the Traveler's sphere. The color black and the absence of light are its primary visual motifs.

Domains — The Darkness exists in the void between spaces, in the Deep, and in the ascendant plane. It can corrupt planets and create 'Darkness zones' where light is suppressed. Its physical manifestations include the Pyramid ships that roam the solar system.

Accounts I
The CollapseDestiny (2014)

Golden Age of Humanity (before the game's events)

The Darkness attacked humanity during its Golden Age, causing the Collapse, a catastrophic event that nearly wiped out humanity. The Traveler sacrificed itself to push back the Darkness, creating the Last City and the Guardians. Humanity was thrown into a dark age, with the Last City becoming the last bastion of civilization.

The UnveilingDestiny 2: Shadowkeep

Pre-creation (mythological)

The lore book 'Unveiling' presents a philosophical parable about the origin of the universe. It describes a gardener (the Traveler) and a winnower (the Darkness) who played a game of life and death, leading to the creation of the universe and the ongoing conflict between Light and Dark. This story is foundational to understanding the nature of the Darkness and its motives.

Accounts II
The Red WarDestiny 2 (2017)

Post-Collapse

The Red War was an invasion by the Cabal, led by Dominus Ghaul, who sought to capture the Traveler's Light. The Darkness was not the main antagonist but was present as a looming threat. Ghaul's death allowed the Traveler to awaken, but it also attracted the attention of the Darkness.

Beyond LightDestiny 2: Beyond Light (2020)

Post-Red War

The Guardian travels to Europa to confront the Fallen Kell Eramis, who is using the Darkness to create an army. The Guardian learns to wield Stasis, a Darkness subclass, and faces the consequences of using the Darkness. The Guardian gains Stasis powers, and the line between Light and Dark becomes blurred.

The Witch QueenDestiny 2: The Witch Queen (2022)

Post-Beyond Light

The Guardian confronts Savathûn, the Witch Queen, who has taken the Light. The expansion reveals that the Witness is the true antagonist, and the Darkness is a tool used by it. The lore also introduces the concept of the Veil, a mysterious artifact linked to the Darkness. The nature of the Darkness is further explained, and the Witness becomes the primary threat.

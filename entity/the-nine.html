Etymology

The name 'The Nine' likely derives from the number of entities that constitute the collective, possibly referencing the nine planets of the pre-2006 Solar System or the nine realms of Norse cosmology, but the in-game origin is not explicitly defined.

Cultural Ownership

Destiny (Bungie, Inc.)

Disputed Identifications

Some fans have theorized that The Nine are related to the nine planets of the Solar System, but this is not confirmed in-game. Others have speculated they are the 'Gardener' and 'Winnower' from the lore, but this is not supported by evidence.

Archive Dossier — Approach

In the dark between the stars, nine ancient intelligences watch the worlds of Destiny, their motives as vast and inscrutable as the void itself. They are not gods, not machines, but something older — entities that have existed since before the universe was shaped, bound to the fundamental forces that govern reality. Their whispers reach across space, their pawns move in the shadows, and their endgame remains a mystery even to the Guardians who have glimpsed their power.

We are the Nine. We are the space between. The gravity that binds. The dark matter that swirls. We do not speak as you speak. We do not think as you think. But we observe. And we have chosen you. Do not waste our choice.

Who It Is

The Nine are mysterious, ancient cosmic entities that exist as the sentient consciousness of the Sol System itself — formed from the dark matter fields that permeate the space between planets. They are not gods or paracausal beings in the traditional sense, but something stranger: emergent consciousnesses born from the gravitational and cosmic relationships between the planets and their dark matter shadows. There are actually nine distinct entities, each corresponding to a planetary body in the Sol System (though which planets remain uncertain — the nine traditional planets, or something else entirely). The Nine cannot act directly in the physical world; they lack the paracausal power of the Light or Darkness. To influence events, they must work through proxies — humans, Awoken, Guardians, and other beings who can serve as their hands. The Nine have created the Trials of Osiris, the Reckoning, and various caches of powerful gear throughout the system. They communicate through Xûr, the mysterious Agent of the Nine who appears in the Tower weekly selling exotic items. The Nine are divided in their goals: five of them wish to become independent beings — to break free from their dependence on mortal life for existence — while four wish to preserve the current balance. This internal conflict drives many of their actions. The Nine represent the most alien intelligence in the Destiny universe. They are not beings you can fight or negotiate with in human terms — they are the system itself, aware of itself, and they have their own agenda. They are neutral in the war between Light and Darkness, concerned only with their own survival and evolution. In the end, the Nine do not care about humanity, the Hive, or the Traveler. They care only about the Sol System — because the Sol System is what they ARE.

Archive Dossier — Description

Appearance

The Nine do not have a physical form in the conventional sense. They are often represented as a chorus of voices, sometimes accompanied by a visual of nine orbs or a dark nebula. In the Trials of the Nine, they are represented by a nine-pointed star emblem.

Symbols & Iconography

The Nine are associated with the number nine, often depicted as nine circles, a nine-pointed star, or a pattern of nine dots. Their symbol appears in the Trials of the Nine and in various lore entries.

Behaviour & Personality

The Nine are cryptic and inscrutable, communicating through riddles and indirect means. They are curious about humanity and the Light, but their ultimate goals are unknown. They are not malevolent, but they are not benevolent either; they are more akin to cosmic observers with their own agenda.

Domains & Habitats

The Nine reside in a realm outside normal spacetime, often described as 'the void' or 'the space between spaces.' They are associated with the planets of the Solar System, particularly the gas giants, and are said to have been born from the gravitational forces of the planets.

Version Differences

In Destiny 1, The Nine were portrayed as mysterious benefactors, providing Xûr and the Trials of the Nine. In Destiny 2, their nature is explored more deeply, with the lore revealing that they are not purely physical entities but are intertwined with the fabric of reality. Some lore suggests they may be the remnants of ancient life forms that merged with the planets.

Archive Dossier — Powers

Reality Manipulation

Source Version Destiny 2

Description

The Nine can manipulate reality within their domain, creating pocket dimensions and altering physical laws. This is evident in the Trials of the Nine, where they create a simulated reality for Guardians to compete.

Limitations

Their power seems limited to their own realm or areas they have heavily influenced.

Contradictions

Some lore suggests they are bound by the rules of the universe, while others imply they can bypass them.

Communication

Source Version Destiny 1 and 2

Description

The Nine can communicate telepathically or through visions, often using intermediaries like Xûr or the Emissary.

Limitations

Their communication is often cryptic and requires interpretation.

Contradictions

None noted.

Influence over Darkness and Light

Source Version Destiny 2

Description

The Nine are said to be able to interact with both Light and Darkness, though they are neither aligned with the Traveler nor the Darkness. They have used this to create the Trials and to test Guardians.

Limitations

They seem to have limited direct influence over paracausal forces, often needing to work through proxies.

Contradictions

Some believe they are actually agents of the Darkness, but this is not confirmed.

Archive Dossier — Relationships

Xûr, Agent of the Nine

Type servant/agentSource Destiny 1 and 2

Description

Xûr is a mysterious vendor who sells exotic items for the Nine. He is not a member of the Nine but serves them, often speaking in riddles.

Version Note

Xûr appears in both games, always as a merchant.

Conflicting Accounts

Some fans speculate Xûr is a Jovian, a humanoid from the outer planets, but this is not confirmed.

The Emissary

Type representativeSource Destiny 2: Season of the Drifter

Description

The Emissary is a human woman who was transformed by the Nine to serve as their intermediary. She appears in the Invitations of the Nine and in the Trials.

Version Note

The Emissary is first encountered in the Drifter's questline.

Conflicting Accounts

None.

The Traveler

Type observer/interestSource Lore Book: Dust

Description

The Nine are fascinated by the Traveler and the Light it provides. They have studied it from afar but have not directly interacted with it.

Version Note

The Traveler is a central figure in Destiny, and the Nine's interest in it is a recurring theme.

Conflicting Accounts

Some lore suggests the Nine may have been involved in the Traveler's arrival, but this is unconfirmed.

The Darkness

Type observer/interestSource Lore Book: Dust

Description

The Nine are also interested in the Darkness, but they are not aligned with it. They seek to understand both forces.

Version Note

The Darkness is the primary antagonist of Destiny, and the Nine's neutrality is notable.

Conflicting Accounts

Some believe the Nine are actually the Darkness's agents, but this is contradicted by their actions.

Guardians

Type subjects/toolsSource Destiny 2

Description

The Nine use Guardians as test subjects in the Trials, but they also seem to care about their welfare, as they provide rewards.

Version Note

The Trials are a direct interaction between the Nine and Guardians.

Conflicting Accounts

Some Guardians view the Nine as benefactors, while others distrust them.

The Drifter

Type ally?Source Destiny 2: Season of the Drifter

Description

The Drifter has connections to the Nine, often dealing with them and their emissaries. He seems to have a mutual understanding with them.

Version Note

The Drifter's motives are ambiguous, but he is not a servant of the Nine.

Conflicting Accounts

Some players believe the Drifter is secretly working for the Nine, but this is not proven.

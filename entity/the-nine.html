Who It Is

The Nine are mysterious, ancient cosmic entities that exist as the sentient consciousness of the Sol System itself — formed from the dark matter fields that permeate the space between planets. They are not gods or paracausal beings in the traditional sense, but something stranger: emergent consciousnesses born from the gravitational and cosmic relationships between the planets and their dark matter shadows. There are actually nine distinct entities, each corresponding to a planetary body in the Sol System (though which planets remain uncertain — the nine traditional planets, or something else entirely). The Nine cannot act directly in the physical world; they lack the paracausal power of the Light or Darkness. To influence events, they must work through proxies — humans, Awoken, Guardians, and other beings who can serve as their hands. The Nine have created the Trials of Osiris, the Reckoning, and various caches of powerful gear throughout the system. They communicate through Xûr, the mysterious Agent of the Nine who appears in the Tower weekly selling exotic items. The Nine are divided in their goals: five of them wish to become independent beings — to break free from their dependence on mortal life for existence — while four wish to preserve the current balance. This internal conflict drives many of their actions. The Nine represent the most alien intelligence in the Destiny universe. They are not beings you can fight or negotiate with in human terms — they are the system itself, aware of itself, and they have their own agenda. They are neutral in the war between Light and Darkness, concerned only with their own survival and evolution. In the end, the Nine do not care about humanity, the Hive, or the Traveler. They care only about the Sol System — because the Sol System is what they ARE.

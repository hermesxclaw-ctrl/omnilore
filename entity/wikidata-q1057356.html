Who It Is

From the misty shores of Pallet Town to the stormy skies of a classified satellite, the name 'Misty' drifts through countless worlds—each one a different kind of legend. But for millions of fans, one Misty stands above all: the fiery-haired Gym Leader of Cerulean City, whose tough exterior hides a heart as deep as the ocean she commands.

Misty is initially hot-tempered, assertive, and easily frustrated, especially with Ash's clumsiness. Over time, she shows a caring and nurturing side, particularly toward her Pokémon and her younger sisters. She has a fear of bugs (insectophobia), which is a recurring gag.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Misty' is derived from the English word 'misty', meaning full of or covered with mist, or lacking clarity. It evokes imagery of fog, water, and obscurity, fitting for a water-type Pokémon specialist.

Pronunciation — /ˈmɪsti/ (MIS-tee)

Powers
Water-type Pokémon training

Misty specializes in Water-type Pokémon, using their aquatic abilities in battle.

Limit: Her team is limited to Water-types, which are weak to Electric and Grass.

Gym Leader authority

As Gym Leader of Cerulean City, she can challenge trainers and award the Cascade Badge.

Limit: In the games, she is defeated by the player. In the anime, she is a strong trainer but often loses to Team Rocket's schemes.

Swimming and water skills

Misty is adept at swimming and water-based activities, as shown in episodes like 'The Water Flowers of Cerulean City'.

Limit: None known.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Misty is a young girl with orange hair tied into a ponytail, typically wearing a yellow crop top, blue shorts, and a red bandana. In the anime, she often carries a bike (which is destroyed by Pikachu early in the series). Her design emphasizes her energetic and tomboyish nature.

Symbols — Water-type Pokémon, especially Starmie, Staryu, Psyduck, and Goldeen. Her badge is the Cascade Badge. Her signature Pokémon in the anime is Togepi, which she cares for.

Domains — Cerulean City, located in the Kanto region. She spends time at the Cerulean Gym and later travels with Ash through Kanto, Johto, and the Orange Islands.

Accounts I
Gym Battle in Cerulean CityPokémon Red/Blue (games)

1996 (original release)

The player challenges Misty, the Cerulean Gym Leader, to obtain the Cascade Badge. She uses Starmie and Staryu. After defeating her, she gives the badge and TM11 (Bubble Beam). The player progresses in the game, earning the right to challenge later Gyms.

Accounts II
Misty's Journey with AshPokémon anime (Indigo League, Orange Islands, Johto Journeys)

1997-2002

Misty meets Ash Ketchum when he borrows her bike, which Pikachu destroys. She follows him to demand a new bike, but eventually becomes a close friend and traveling companion. They travel through Kanto, the Orange Islands, and Johto, facing many adventures. Misty eventually returns to Cerulean City to run the Gym. Misty's character develops from a short-tempered girl to a more compassionate and responsible trainer. She leaves the main cast in 2002 but makes guest appearances later.

Misty's Role in Pokémon Masters EXPokémon Masters EX (mobile game)

2019-present

Misty is a sync pair with Starmie, participating in the Pokémon Masters League on Pasio. She interacts with other characters and participates in events. Expands her character beyond the original games.

Who It Is

In the shattered remains of humanity's Golden Age, the dead do not rest—they rise, reborn in Light, to stand against the encroaching Dark. These are the Guardians: immortal champions of the Traveler, wielding paracausal powers to defend the last city of Earth and venture into the unknown reaches of the solar system. But their eternal war is not without cost, and the line between protector and destroyer blurs with every rebirth.

Guardians are defined by their duty to protect the Last City and humanity. They are brave, often reckless, and driven by a sense of purpose. Many struggle with the loss of their past lives, as resurrection often erases memories. Their personalities range from stoic and disciplined (Titans) to cunning and independent (Hunters) to scholarly and mystical (Warlocks).

Powers
Resurrection

Guardians can be resurrected by their Ghost after death, as long as the Ghost survives and the Light is not suppressed.

Limit: Cannot resurrect if the Ghost is destroyed or if in areas where the Light is blocked (e.g., within the Shadow Legion's suppression fields or in the presence of the Witness).

Light Abilities

Guardians wield the Light, a paracausal force, to perform supernatural feats. Each class has unique subclasses: Titans use Arc, Solar, Void, and Stasis (and later Strand); Hunters have similar elements; Warlocks also wield these elements. These abilities include grenades, melee attacks, and supers (ultimate abilities).

Limit: Light abilities are weakened or nullified in Darkness zones or when suppressed by certain enemy abilities.

Ghost

Each Guardian has a Ghost, a small floating machine that acts as a companion, resurrection device, and tool for interacting with technology and the Light.

Limit: The Ghost is vulnerable and can be destroyed; if destroyed, the Guardian becomes mortal and cannot be resurrected.

Paracausal Nature

Guardians are paracausal beings, meaning they can act outside the causal laws of the universe, allowing them to alter reality through their will and the Light.

Limit: The exact limits of paracausality are not fully understood, but it requires a connection to the Light (or Darkness).

Form & Signs

Appearance — Guardians are humanoids resurrected by Ghosts, retaining the appearance of their original selves at the moment of death, but often augmented with advanced armor and technology. They can be of any human, Awoken, or Exo race. Their appearance varies widely based on class (Titan, Hunter, Warlock) and armor choices, but they are often adorned with the symbol of the Traveler or the Vanguard.

Symbols — The sigil of the Vanguard, a stylized eagle or hawk, is commonly used. The Traveler's symbol, a sphere with radiating arcs, is also prominent. Each class has its own emblem: Titans use a tower shield, Hunters a knife or cloak, and Warlocks a book or bond.

Domains — Guardians are primarily based in the Last City on Earth, under the Traveler. They venture across the solar system, including planets like Venus, Mars, the Moon, and the Reef, as well as destinations in the Ascendant Plane and the Black Garden.

After the Collapse

After the Collapse, the Traveler was disabled, and the Darkness receded. Ghosts were created to find and resurrect fallen heroes to become Guardians. The first Guardians, known as Risen, often became warlords, but eventually, some united under the banner of the Vanguard to protect humanity, leading to the founding of the Last City. The establishment of the Last City and the Vanguard, and the beginning of the Guardian age.

Accounts II
The Red WarDestiny 2 (base campaign)

Year 5 of the City Age (approx.)

The Red Legion, led by Dominus Ghaul, attacked the Last City, cut off the Traveler's Light, and forced Guardians to flee. The player Guardian reconnected with the Light and led a counteroffensive, ultimately defeating Ghaul and restoring the Light. The Last City was rebuilt, and the Traveler awakened, but this also drew the attention of greater threats.

The Battle for the Light (Lightfall and The Final Shape)Destiny 2: Lightfall and The Final Shape

After the Red War

The Witness, the ultimate antagonist, sought to bring about the Final Shape by using the Darkness. Guardians, with the aid of allies like the Cabal and the Awoken, fought to prevent this, culminating in a confrontation inside the Traveler itself. The Witness was defeated, but at great cost, and the nature of Light and Dark was further revealed.

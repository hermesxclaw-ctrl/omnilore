The Approach

Juzoh Kanzaki is the gentle giant of The Last Blade—a lazy, free-spirited brute who would rather nap than fight, yet whose loyalty to his adoptive sisters drives him to journey to the gates of hell itself. Armed with a club and an unbreakable bond, he proves that strength is not just about muscle, but about heart.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of illness, death of a sibling, and supernatural conflict. No explicit violence or dark themes beyond typical fighting game content.
Identity

Juzoh Kanzaki

Pronunciation JOO-zoh KAHN-zah-kee

Known As

Juzo KanzakiKanzaki Jūzō十三 (Jūzō)

Etymology

The surname Kanzaki (神崎) means 'divine cape' or 'god's peninsula', while the given name Jūzō (十三) literally means 'thirteen', possibly indicating his birth order or a traditional naming convention. The kanji 十三 can also be read as 'Jūzō', a common masculine name in Japan.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese video game character created by SNK for The Last Blade series. Not based on a historical figure, but embodies the archetype of the strong, loyal, and somewhat lazy fighter common in Japanese media.

Disputed Identifications

None known. Juzoh is a fictional character with no real-world counterpart.

Description

Appearance

Juzoh is a tall, muscular man with a somewhat imposing build. He has short, spiky black hair and a thick beard. He wears a traditional Japanese fundoshi (loincloth) and a haori (jacket) over his shoulders, leaving his chest bare. He often carries a large wooden club (kanabō) on his shoulder. His attire reflects his rugged, laid-back personality.

Symbols & Iconography

His weapon, a kanabō, is a symbol of brute strength and is often associated with oni (demons) in Japanese folklore. His bare chest and fundoshi evoke a sense of raw power and simplicity.

Behaviour & Personality

Juzoh is characterized as free-spirited, lazy, and somewhat easygoing. He enjoys lounging around and avoiding responsibility. Despite this, he is deeply caring and protective of his adoptive family, especially his sisters Akari and Hikari. He is not particularly ambitious, but when motivated, he fights with immense strength and determination. He is also shown to be somewhat naive and easily persuaded by his sister Akari.

Domains & Habitats

He is primarily depicted in domestic settings, such as his home, and in various locations across Japan during the Bakumatsu era. He is not tied to any specific supernatural domain.

Version Differences

In The Last Blade, Juzoh's story focuses on his kidnapping by Akari to accompany her to Hell's Gate. In The Last Blade 2, his story shifts to pursuing Akari after she runs away to find a cure for Hikari's illness. His gameplay style remains consistent as a power-type character, but his moveset may have slight variations between games.

Stories & Myths

Journey to Hell's Gate

Source The Last Blade (1997)Culture JapanesePeriod Bakumatsu era (late Edo period)

Summary

Juzoh is living a lazy life at home when he is knocked out and kidnapped by his adoptive sister Akari, who wants him to accompany her on a journey to find Hell's Gate. She believes sealing it will cure their sister Hikari's illness. Reluctantly, Juzoh joins her, and they travel across Japan facing various opponents.

Participants

Juzoh Kanzaki

Akari Ichijou

Consequences

They eventually reach Hell's Gate, but their efforts are intertwined with the larger conflict involving the guardians. Hikari's illness is not cured by their journey.

Variants

Juzoh's ending may vary based on player choices, but the core story remains the same.

Pursuit of Akari

Source The Last Blade 2 (1998)Culture JapanesePeriod Bakumatsu era

Summary

After Hikari falls ill, Akari runs away to Hell's Gate again to find a cure. Juzoh, visiting Hikari, learns of this and sets out to bring Akari back, worried for her safety. He travels across Japan, fighting to protect his sister.

Participants

Consequences

Juzoh eventually catches up with Akari, and they work together to seal Hell's Gate, though the specifics are not fully detailed.

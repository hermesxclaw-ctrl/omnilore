Who It Is

Juzoh Kanzaki is the gentle giant of The Last Blade—a lazy, free-spirited brute who would rather nap than fight, yet whose loyalty to his adoptive sisters drives him to journey to the gates of hell itself. Armed with a club and an unbreakable bond, he proves that strength is not just about muscle, but about heart.

Juzoh is characterized as free-spirited, lazy, and somewhat easygoing. He enjoys lounging around and avoiding responsibility. Despite this, he is deeply caring and protective of his adoptive family, especially his sisters Akari and Hikari. He is not particularly ambitious, but when motivated, he fights with immense strength and determination. He is also shown to be somewhat naive and easily persuaded by his sister Akari.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname Kanzaki (神崎) means 'divine cape' or 'god's peninsula', while the given name Jūzō (十三) literally means 'thirteen', possibly indicating his birth order or a traditional naming convention. The kanji 十三 can also be read as 'Jūzō', a common masculine name in Japan.

Pronunciation — JOO-zoh KAHN-zah-kee

Aliases — Juzo Kanzaki, Kanzaki Jūzō, 十三 (Jūzō)

Ownership — Japanese video game character created by SNK for The Last Blade series. Not based on a historical figure, but embodies the archetype of the strong, loyal, and somewhat lazy fighter

Powers
Club Combat

Juzoh wields a large kanabō with devastating force. His attacks are slow but powerful, capable of breaking through defenses.

Limit: His slow speed makes him vulnerable to faster opponents.

Superhuman Strength

Juzoh possesses immense physical strength, allowing him to deliver crushing blows and perform powerful throws.

Limit: His strength is not supernatural; it is a result of his natural physique and training.

Power Mode

In Power Mode, Juzoh's attacks deal increased damage, and he gains access to Super Desperation Moves when his health is low and power gauge is full.

Limit: Requires careful health and power management.

Speed Mode

In Speed Mode, Juzoh can chain normal attacks into special moves and perform Speed Combos, though his damage output is reduced.

Limit: Lower damage per hit compared to Power Mode.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Juzoh is a tall, muscular man with a somewhat imposing build. He has short, spiky black hair and a thick beard. He wears a traditional Japanese fundoshi (loincloth) and a haori (jacket) over his shoulders, leaving his chest bare. He often carries a large wooden club (kanabō) on his shoulder. His attire reflects his rugged, laid-back personality.

Symbols — His weapon, a kanabō, is a symbol of brute strength and is often associated with oni (demons) in Japanese folklore. His bare chest and fundoshi evoke a sense of raw power and simplicity.

Domains — He is primarily depicted in domestic settings, such as his home, and in various locations across Japan during the Bakumatsu era. He is not tied to any specific supernatural domain.

Accounts I
Journey to Hell's GateThe Last Blade (1997)

Bakumatsu era (late Edo period)

Juzoh is living a lazy life at home when he is knocked out and kidnapped by his adoptive sister Akari, who wants him to accompany her on a journey to find Hell's Gate. She believes sealing it will cure their sister Hikari's illness. Reluctantly, Juzoh joins her, and they travel across Japan facing various opponents. They eventually reach Hell's Gate, but their efforts are intertwined with the larger conflict involving the guardians. Hikari's illness is not cured by their journey.

Accounts II
Pursuit of AkariThe Last Blade 2 (1998)

Bakumatsu era

After Hikari falls ill, Akari runs away to Hell's Gate again to find a cure. Juzoh, visiting Hikari, learns of this and sets out to bring Akari back, worried for her safety. He travels across Japan, fighting to protect his sister. Juzoh eventually catches up with Akari, and they work together to seal Hell's Gate, though the specifics are not fully detailed.

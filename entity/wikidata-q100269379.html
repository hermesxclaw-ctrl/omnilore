In the tangled backwoods of American folklore, where tall tales grow like kudzu, there lurks a creature of singular determination: the Cleaver Beaver. Part industrious rodent, part relentless lumberjack, this legendary beast is said to fell entire forests with a single gleaming cleaver, leaving behind a trail of perfectly split stumps and bewildered loggers. But is the Cleaver Beaver a genuine piece of oral tradition, a forgotten cartoon mascot, or a modern internet invention? The evidence, as always, is tangled.

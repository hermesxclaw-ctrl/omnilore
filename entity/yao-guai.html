Who It Is

American black bears and grizzlies horribly mutated by radiation into aggressive, hulking monstrosities. Yao Guai are massive bears with patchy fur, exposed muscle tissue, and elongated claws. They are fiercely territorial, omnivorous, and will attack anything that enters their territory. Their roars can paralyze prey with fear, and their swipes can tear through combat armor.

Etymology

The name 'Talbot' is of Norman French origin, introduced to England after the Conquest. It derives from a personal name or nickname, possibly from the Old Norse 'Talbot' or Old French 'Talebod', meaning 'boot' or 'messenger'? More likely, it is from the Germanic elements 'tal' (tale, count) and 'bod' (messenger), thus 'messenger of tales' or 'herald'. Alternatively, it may be a habitational name from a place in Normandy, such as Saint-Pierre-de-Talbot.

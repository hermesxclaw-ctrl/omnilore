The Approach

In the shadowed folklore of East Asia, there lurks a beast whose very name whispers of openings and thresholds—the Kaimingshou, a creature said to guard the gates between worlds, its existence known more through scattered fragments than through any single, coherent tale.

Identity

Kaimingshou

Pronunciation kah-ee-ming-shoh

Etymology

The name appears to be a compound of Japanese or Chinese elements: 'kai' (開, opening) and 'mei' (明, bright/clear) or possibly 'kai' (怪, strange/mystery) and 'mei' (明) with 'shou' (獣, beast). Thus it could mean 'beast of the opening light' or 'mysterious bright beast.'

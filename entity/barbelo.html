The first emanation of God in Sethian Gnostic cosmology — a supreme female principle, the 'Mother-Father,' described as the 'Triple Androgynous Name' and the 'Eternal Aeon.' Barbēlō is the first thought (pronoia) of the invisible, virginal Spirit — the primal divine intellect that mediates between the unknowable Monad and the lower emanations. In the Apocryphon of John, Barbēlō is the womb from which all subsequent Aeons are born and is described as 'the perfect power' who is 'the glory of the invisible Spirit.' She is the divine forethought who descends into the material world to awaken the spiritual seed trapped in human bodies.

Who It Is

The first emanation of God in Sethian Gnostic cosmology — a supreme female principle, the 'Mother-Father,' described as the 'Triple Androgynous Name' and the 'Eternal Aeon.' Barbēlō is the first thought (pronoia) of the invisible, virginal Spirit — the primal divine intellect that mediates between the unknowable Monad and the lower emanations. In the Apocryphon of John, Barbēlō is the womb from which all subsequent Aeons are born and is described as 'the perfect power' who is 'the glory of the invisible Spirit.' She is the divine forethought who descends into the material world to awaken the spiritual seed trapped in human bodies. Barbēlō is intimately connected to Sophia — in some systems, Sophia is a lower emanation of Barbēlō; in others, they are aspects of the same divine feminine principle.

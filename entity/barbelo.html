Who It Is
Form

Barbēlō is often depicted as a supreme female principle, embodying the passive and receptive aspects of creation. She is described as the first principle, the source of all being and the mother of the divine spark within humanity.

Powers

Creation Barbēlō is said to possess the power of creation, bringing forth the universe and all its contents from her own being.

Accounts

Barbēlō creates the universe through her own being, bringing forth the divine spark within humanity and guiding the development of creation.

Barbēlō sends her son, the divine spark within humanity, to guide the creation of the universe and to counteract the influence of the demiurge, a false god who seeks to dominate humanity.

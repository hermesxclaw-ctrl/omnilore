Who It Is

In the Marvel Cinematic Universe, the Time Stone is not merely a device that manipulates time—it is a fundamental pillar of existence, a remnant of a singularity before creation, and the centerpiece of the Eye of Agamotto. Forged in the crucible of cosmic genesis, it has been wielded by sorcerers, demons, and titans, allowing its users to rewind, fast-forward, and even loop moments in history. Yet its power is not absolute; it is bound by the laws of reality and the will of those who dare to control it. This is the story of the Time Stone, from its mysterious origins to its pivotal role in the battle against Thanos and its echoes across the multiverse.

The Time Stone is an inanimate object, but it is sentient in a limited sense, as it can be used to manipulate time in various ways. It does not have a personality, but it is described as one of the six singularities that embody fundamental aspects of existence.

Powers
Time Manipulation

The Time Stone allows its wielder to control the flow of time, including rewinding, fast-forwarding, slowing down, and even stopping time entirely. It can also create time loops and age or de-age objects and beings.

Limit: The Stone's power is limited by the user's knowledge and skill in the Mystic Arts. In Infinity War, Doctor Strange uses it to view millions of possible futures but cannot alter the outcome without the Stone. The Stone cannot be used to change events that have already occurred without creating alternate timelines, as seen in Endgame.

Temporal Viewing

The Stone can be used to see past, present, and possible future events. Doctor Strange uses this ability to explore millions of timelines in Infinity War.

Limit: This ability requires intense concentration and can be overwhelming. It does not allow the user to interact with the viewed timelines.

Time Loop

The Stone can create a time loop, trapping an individual or an area in a repeating period of time. Doctor Strange uses this to trap Dormammu in a loop.

Limit: The loop persists until the user chooses to release it or is broken by an outside force. It requires the Stone to be active and the user to maintain focus.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Time Stone is a small, emerald-green gem with a distinctive hourglass-like pattern within. It is typically housed in the Eye of Agamotto, an ornate, bronze-colored amulet with a circular opening that reveals the Stone. When activated, the Stone emits a green glow and can create a circular mandala-like shield around the user's hand.

Symbols — The Eye of Agamotto is associated with the Mystic Arts and the Masters of the Mystic Arts. The color green is often linked to time in the MCU, as seen in time travel visuals. The hourglass symbol is a direct reference to time's passage.

Domains — The Time Stone is typically stored in the Eye of Agamotto, which is kept in the Sanctum Sanctorum in New York. It can be transported and used anywhere in the universe, as demonstrated by Doctor Strange using it on Titan.

Accounts I
The Eye of Agamotto and Dormammu's BargainDoctor Strange (2016)

2016

Stephen Strange discovers the Eye of Agamotto, which contains the Time Stone, and uses it to trap Dormammu in a time loop on the Dark Dimension. Dormammu is forced to bargain with Strange, eventually agreeing to leave Earth alone in exchange for Strange releasing the loop. Strange becomes the Sorcerer Supreme, and the Time Stone remains in the Sanctum Sanctorum.

Accounts II
The Battle of Titan and the SnapAvengers: Infinity War (2018)

2018

Doctor Strange uses the Time Stone to view millions of possible futures and sees only one where the Avengers win. He surrenders the Stone to Thanos to save Tony Stark's life, allowing Thanos to complete the Infinity Gauntlet and snap away half of all life. The Snap occurs, causing the Blip. Five years later, the Avengers use time travel to undo it.

The Time Heist and the Return of the StonesAvengers: Endgame (2019)

2023

The Avengers travel back in time to collect the Infinity Stones, including the Time Stone, to undo the Snap. Bruce Banner, as the Hulk, uses the Stone to bring back everyone. Steve Rogers later returns the Stones to their original timelines. The Blip is reversed, but alternate timelines are created. Steve Rogers returns the Stones, ensuring the timelines are preserved.

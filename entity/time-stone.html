Who It Is

The Time Stone is the Infinity Stone that governs the fourth dimension — time itself in all its aspects. Its wielder gains absolute mastery over temporal flow: they can rewind time to undo events, fast-forward to see possible futures, slow time to a crawl while moving normally, create time loops that trap enemies in repeating moments, age beings to dust or de-age them to infancy, pause time entirely, create stable time paradoxes, and access any moment in the past or future of the universe. The Time Stone is the most philosophically treacherous of the Infinity Stones because time manipulation carries profound consequences — every change to the past creates branching timelines, and messing with causality can unravel the very fabric of existence. In the Marvel Cinematic Universe, the Time Stone was safeguarded by the Masters of the Mystic Arts at Kamar-Taj, housed within the Eye of Agamotto — a mystical artifact created by the first Sorcerer Supreme, Agamotto. Doctor Strange used the Time Stone with extraordinary precision, most famously during the Battle of Titan where he viewed over 14 million possible futures and found only one path to victory against Thanos. Stephen Strange's careful, measured use of the Time Stone stands in stark contrast to Thanos's brutal employment of it to reverse Vision's destruction. In the comics, the Time Gem has been wielded by Thanos, Kang the Conqueror, the Gardener, and Doctor Strange. The Time Stone represents the ultimate expression of cosmic responsibility: the power to undo any mistake, rewrite any tragedy, and alter any outcome — but at the risk of destroying the very fabric of causality that holds the universe together. With great power comes great temporal responsibility.

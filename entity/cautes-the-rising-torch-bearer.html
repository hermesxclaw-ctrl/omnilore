Who It Is
Form

Cautes is typically depicted as a youthful, beardless man, often with a radiant aura and an upraised torch.

Powers

Illumination Cautes has the power to bring light to dark places, symbolizing hope and renewal in the face of adversity.

Accounts

Cautes is often depicted in the birth scenes of Mithras, emphasizing the divine connection between the two figures.

Cautes and Cautopates are often depicted flanking Mithras in the bull-slaying scene, emphasizing their role as divine attendants.

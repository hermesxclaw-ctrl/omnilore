Who It Is

In the hidden webs of Oblivion, Mephala spins her threads of intrigue, murder, and secret knowledge. Known as the Webspinner, this Daedric Prince of lies and deceit is a master of unseen schemes, her influence felt in every shadowy corner of Tamriel. But who truly understands the Spider? Her truths are as tangled as her webs, and her motives as dark as the void she calls home.

Mephala is cunning, manipulative, and enjoys sowing discord and secrets. She tests mortals by asking them to commit acts of murder and betrayal, often for seemingly just causes. She values cleverness and subtlety over brute force.

Powers
Web Spinning

Mephala can create and manipulate webs, both physical and metaphorical, to trap or ensnare her enemies.

Limit: Unknown

Murder Incitement

She can influence mortals to commit murder, often by providing them with a cursed weapon or a quest that involves killing.

Limit: She does not directly control minds but manipulates through persuasion and circumstance.

Secrets and Lies

Mephala has knowledge of hidden secrets and can reveal or conceal information as she sees fit.

Limit: Unknown

Spider Form

In ESO, she can take the form of a giant spider.

Limit: Only in ESO

Daedric Prince Powers

As a Daedric Prince, she is immortal and has vast magical abilities, including the ability to grant boons to her followers.

Limit: She is bound by the rules of Oblivion and cannot enter Mundus except through specific means.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Mephala typically appears as a beautiful Dunmer (Dark Elf) woman with multiple arms, often depicted with spider-like features. In Morrowind, she is shown as a four-armed female figure. In Skyrim, she appears as a spectral female voice, and in ESO she takes on a more monstrous spider form.

Symbols — Spiders, webs, and the color black. Her symbol is often a spider or a web. She is associated with the Moth, as in the 'Moth' priest, and her realm is said to be a web-like structure.

Domains — Her realm of Oblivion is known as the 'Spiral Skein', a tangled web of corridors and chambers. She is also associated with the concept of 'secret murder' and 'sexual intrigue'.

Accounts I
The AnticipationsIn-game book 'The Anticipations'

Before the Tribunal

Mephala is one of the 'Anticipations' of the Tribunal, along with Boethiah and Azura. She represents the 'subtle' aspect of the Tribunal god Vivec, who embodies both male and female, and is associated with secret murder and intrigue. The Tribunal replaced the Daedric Princes in Dunmer worship, but the Anticipations were still honored as precursors.

Mephala's Quest in MorrowindThe Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind

Third Era

The Nerevarine is tasked by Mephala to murder a rival in a house war, using a special weapon. This quest tests the player's willingness to commit murder for a Daedric Prince. The player gains Mephala's favor and a powerful artifact.

Third Era

Mephala asks the Champion of Cyrodiil to spread rumors and incite violence in a city, leading to a murder. This quest emphasizes her love of chaos and manipulation. The player receives the Ebony Blade.

The Only CureThe Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim

Fourth Era

Mephala appears to the Dragonborn, offering to cure them of a disease in exchange for killing a vampire. This quest shows her as a more pragmatic figure. The player is cured and receives Mephala's blessing.

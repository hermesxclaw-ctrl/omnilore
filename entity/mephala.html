Who It Is

The Daedric Prince of Murder, Plots, Obfuscation, Manipulation, and Discord. Also known as the Webspinner. She teaches the arts of assassination and secret manipulation. Her realm is the Spiral Skein, a tangled, weblike dimension. One of the Good Daedra of the Dunmer. Her artifact is the Ebony Blade, a katana that grows stronger by betraying allies.

The Approach

In the hidden webs of Oblivion, Mephala spins her threads of intrigue, murder, and secret knowledge. Known as the Webspinner, this Daedric Prince of lies and deceit is a master of unseen schemes, her influence felt in every shadowy corner of Tamriel. But who truly understands the Spider? Her truths are as tangled as her webs, and her motives as dark as the void she calls home.

Content advisory. Mephala is associated with murder, deceit, and manipulation. Her sphere includes secret murder and sexual intrigue, which may be uncomfortable for some readers. This dossier discusses these themes in a scholarly context, without graphic detail.

Etymology

The name 'Mephala' likely derives from 'Mephistopheles', a demon in German folklore, reflecting her deceptive nature. In-universe, her name is associated with spiders and webs, as seen in her title 'Webspinner'.

Cultural Ownership

Mephala is a Daedric Prince in the fictional universe of The Elder Scrolls, created by Bethesda Game Studios. She is not based on any real-world deity but draws inspiration from various mythological trickster figures.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans have theorized that Mephala may be connected to the real-world figure of Lilith or other dark goddesses, but this is speculative and not supported by official lore.

Description

Appearance

Mephala typically appears as a beautiful Dunmer (Dark Elf) woman with multiple arms, often depicted with spider-like features. In Morrowind, she is shown as a four-armed female figure. In Skyrim, she appears as a spectral female voice, and in ESO she takes on a more monstrous spider form.

Symbols & Iconography

Spiders, webs, and the color black. Her symbol is often a spider or a web. She is associated with the Moth, as in the 'Moth' priest, and her realm is said to be a web-like structure.

Behaviour & Personality

Mephala is cunning, manipulative, and enjoys sowing discord and secrets. She tests mortals by asking them to commit acts of murder and betrayal, often for seemingly just causes. She values cleverness and subtlety over brute force.

Domains & Habitats

Her realm of Oblivion is known as the 'Spiral Skein', a tangled web of corridors and chambers. She is also associated with the concept of 'secret murder' and 'sexual intrigue'.

Version Differences

In different games, her appearance and portrayal vary. In Morrowind, she is more involved with the Tribunal Temple's history. In Oblivion, she is a standard Daedric quest giver. In Skyrim, she is less prominent but still present. In ESO, she has more detailed questlines.

Stories & Myths

The Anticipations

Source In-game book 'The Anticipations'Culture DunmerPeriod Before the Tribunal

Summary

Mephala is one of the 'Anticipations' of the Tribunal, along with Boethiah and Azura. She represents the 'subtle' aspect of the Tribunal god Vivec, who embodies both male and female, and is associated with secret murder and intrigue.

Participants

Mephala

Boethiah

Azura

Vivec

Consequences

The Tribunal replaced the Daedric Princes in Dunmer worship, but the Anticipations were still honored as precursors.

Variants

Some sources say the Anticipations are the 'good' Daedra, but this is a simplification.

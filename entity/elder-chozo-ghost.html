Who It Is

The spectral remnant of a high-ranking Chozo shaman, bound to the temples of Tallon IV by a duty that transcends death. Elder Chozo Ghosts appear as translucent, armored figures that glide through walls and floors. They attack with concentrated beams of psychic energy and can become semi-corporeal to deliver devastating strikes.

The Approach

In the crumbling halls of a lost Chozo sanctuary, a flickering silhouette stands watch—neither fully alive nor entirely dead, a guardian bound by duty beyond the grave. The Elder Chozo Ghost is one of the most haunting presences in the Metroid series, a spectral warrior whose origins and purpose remain shrouded in mystery.

Content advisory. Contains themes of death, undeath, and haunting imagery typical of science-fantasy horror.
Identity

Elder Chozo Ghost

Pronunciation EL-der CHO-zo GOHST

Etymology

The name combines 'Elder,' suggesting age or high rank, with 'Chozo,' the ancient bird-like race, and 'Ghost,' indicating a spectral or undead state.

Cultural Ownership

Metroid franchise, Nintendo

Disputed Identifications

There is ambiguity regarding the exact identity of this entity. It is sometimes conflated with the generic Chozo Ghosts that appear in Metroid Prime, but the 'Elder' designation suggests a unique or higher-ranking individual. Without official confirmation, its exact status remains unclear.

Description

Appearance

The Elder Chozo Ghost appears as a translucent, spectral figure resembling a Chozo warrior, with glowing eyes and a tattered cloak. It hovers above the ground, its form flickering like a hologram. Details of its armor or regalia are not distinctly described in common sources.

Symbols & Iconography

It is associated with the Chozo Ruins and the ancient technology of the Chozo, often appearing near lore tablets or sacred sites.

Behaviour & Personality

The Elder Chozo Ghost is a guardian, attacking intruders who desecrate Chozo sanctuaries. It exhibits aggressive behavior, using energy blasts and phasing abilities. Its personality is not deeply explored, but it appears driven by a primal duty to protect its home.

Domains & Habitats

It inhabits the Chozo Ruins on Tallon IV, a planet in the Metroid Prime timeline. These ruins are ancient, overgrown structures filled with traps and lore.

Version Differences

There is no confirmed distinction between the Elder Chozo Ghost and regular Chozo Ghosts in most sources. Some fan interpretations suggest the Elder is a boss or unique enemy, but this is not officially documented.

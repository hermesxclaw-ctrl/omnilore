Who It Is
Form

Ngenechen is often depicted as a powerful and wise being, with a strong connection to nature and the spiritual realm. He is often shown with a stern expression, emphasizing his role as a creator and ruler.

Powers

Creation Ngenechen has the power to create and shape the world, bringing forth life and order from chaos.

Healing Ngenechen has the ability to heal the sick and injured, using his power to restore balance and harmony to the world.

Protection Ngenechen has the power to protect his people from harm, using his wisdom and strength to defend them against all threats.

Accounts

Ngenechen is said to have created the world, bringing forth life and order from chaos. He is often depicted as a powerful and wise being, with a strong connection to nature and the spiritual realm.

Ngenechen is said to have created the condor, a powerful and majestic bird that represents his power and wisdom. The condor is often depicted as a symbol of Ngenechen's strength and protection.

Ngenechen is said to have fought against the god of chaos, using his power and wisdom to defeat him and establish order in the world. This story is often depicted as a symbol of Ngenechen's strength and protection.

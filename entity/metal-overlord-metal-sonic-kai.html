Who It Is

The ultimate evolution of Metal Sonic, achieved by absorbing the data and energy of his fellow robots. Metal Overlord is a gigantic, dragon-like machine with wings, three heads, and the Neo Metal Sonic core at its center. It can fire devastating energy beams and regenerate from damage by consuming scrap metal.

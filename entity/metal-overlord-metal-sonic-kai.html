Metal Overlord (Metal Sonic Kai)

also known asMetal Sonic Kai
TypeSonic Entity
Who It Is
Form

The Metal Overlord is a towering, humanoid robot with a metallic body and glowing blue eyes. It stands at an impressive height of over 10 feet, with a muscular build and sharp, angular limbs. Its body is adorned with intricate circuits and wires, giving it a cybernetic appearance.

Powers

Dark Ring Manipulation The Metal Overlord has the ability to manipulate the Dark Ring, using it to enhance its own powers and abilities.

Accounts

The story of the Metal Overlord begins with the transformation of Metal Sonic, a robotic clone of Sonic the Hedgehog. Metal Sonic is exposed to a Dark Ring, which triggers a transformation into the powerful Metal Overlord.

The Metal Overlord is first introduced in the Sonic the Hedgehog series during the Battle of Newtrogic High Zone, where it serves as a powerful foe to the series' main characters.

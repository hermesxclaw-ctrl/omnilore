Identity

Caboclo Pedra Preta

Pronunciation ka-BOH-kloo PEH-drah PREH-tah

Etymology

Caboclo: a term used in Brazil for people of mixed indigenous and European descent, and in Umbanda for spirits of indigenous Brazilian ancestors. Pedra Preta: Portuguese for 'black stone'.

Cultural Ownership

Afro-Brazilian religion, specifically Umbanda, with possible roots in indigenous and African traditions.

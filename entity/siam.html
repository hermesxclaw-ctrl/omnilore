Who It Is

A conjoined-twin monster featured in Silent Hill: Origins. Siam consists of two female bodies fused back-to-back, sharing one set of legs. One face wears an expression of agony while the other screams. They walk sideways and attack with powerful kicks and stomps. Their design represents the duality of trauma and the bond between victims.

Powers

Kick attacks, sideways movement, high damage

Who It Is

Twenty-second spirit, an Earl and Prince commanding 36 legions. Ipos (also Aiperos, Ayperos) appears as an angelic figure — initially with a lion's head, goose's feet, and hare's tail, but soon transforming into a more recognizably angelic form. He knows all things past, present, and future and makes men witty, bold, and valiant. Ipos is a demon of eloquence and courage, sharpening the mind and steeling the heart. His bizarre composite form — lion, goose, hare — suggests a creature of multiple domains: courage, migration, and swiftness, all converging in articulate boldness.

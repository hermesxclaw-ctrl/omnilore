Name & Tongue

Etymology — Linguistically, 'Woltje' is a Low German / Low Saxon and Dutch diminutive formation. Base 'Wolt' / 'Wold' / 'Wald' = wood, forest, wold (Old Saxon wald, Middle Low German wolt/wold). Suffix '-je' / '-tje' is the diminutive common in Low German, Frisian and Dutch (cf. Dutch -tje, Low German -je/-ken)

Pronunciation — Low German/Dutch approx. [ˈvɔltjə] (VOLT-yuh), with diminutive -tje realized as [cə] or [tʃə] depending on dialect; High German approximation 'VOLT-yeh'. Stress on first syllable. No audio primary source located.

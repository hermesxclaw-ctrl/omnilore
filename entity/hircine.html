Who It Is

The Daedric Prince of the Hunt, Sport, and the Chase. Known as the Huntsman and the Father of Manbeasts. He created all lycanthropic diseases, making him the father of werewolves and all were-creatures. His realm is the Hunting Grounds, an endless wilderness where the ultimate hunt is eternal. His artifacts include the Spear of Bitter Mercy and the Ring of Hircine.

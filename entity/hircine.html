Who It Is

In the pantheon of Tamriel's Daedric Princes, Hircine stands apart as the Hunt Father, a being of primal instinct and relentless pursuit. His realm, the Hunting Grounds, is a boundless wilderness where the thrill of the chase is eternal, and his followers, the werewolves, carry his blessing and curse across Nirn.

Hircine embodies the hunt in all its forms: the thrill of the chase, the skill of the hunter, and the ferocity of the predator. He is not inherently malevolent but is utterly indifferent to the suffering of prey. He respects those who hunt fairly and despises those who hunt for sport or cruelty.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Hircine' likely derives from the Latin 'hircus', meaning 'he-goat', referencing his bestial nature and hunting prowess.

Pronunciation — HEER-seen

Aliases — Huntsman, Father of Manbeasts, The Great Hunter

Ownership — Hircine is a Daedric Prince in the fictional universe of The Elder Scrolls, created by Bethesda Game Studios. He is not part of any real-world culture or religion.

Powers
Lycanthropy

The ability to transform into a werewolf or grant this ability to mortals. Hircine is the source of lycanthropy in Tamriel.

Limit: Those afflicted must manage their transformations and may lose control under the full moon. The Ring of Hircine can grant voluntary control.

Hunting Grounds

Hircine can transport individuals to his realm, the Hunting Grounds, where he rules absolutely.

Limit: Unknown; possibly limited to those he chooses or those who enter voluntarily.

Aspect of the Hunt

Hircine can manifest in different aspects, each representing a different type of hunter: the Hunter, the Prey, and the Beast.

Limit: Not fully detailed; each aspect may have different powers.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Hircine is depicted as a massive, imposing figure with the head of a stag or wolf, often with antlers. He is typically shown wearing animal hides and wielding a spear. In Skyrim, he appears as a large, dark-furred werewolf-like being with glowing eyes.

Symbols — Common symbols include the stag, the wolf, and the spear. His Daedric artifact, the Savior's Hide, is a cuirass made from the hide of a werewolf. The Ring of Hircine grants its wearer control over lycanthropy.

Domains — Hircine's realm is the Hunting Grounds, a vast, untamed wilderness filled with endless forests, plains, and mountains. It is a place of eternal hunting, where prey is always abundant and the hunt never ends.

Third Era

The Champion of Cyrodiil is tasked with hunting a unicorn in the Shivering Isles. Hircine appears and challenges the Champion to a hunt, offering a powerful artifact as a reward. The Champion gains the Savior's Hide or the Spear of Bitter Mercy, depending on choices.

Accounts II
Ill Met By MoonlightThe Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim

Fourth Era

The Dragonborn encounters a werewolf named Sinding who has been cursed by Hircine. The Dragonborn must help Sinding either kill the hunters sent by Hircine or kill Sinding himself. The Dragonborn receives the Ring of Hircine and can choose to let Sinding live or die.

The Hunting GroundsThe Elder Scrolls Online

Second Era

In ESO, Hircine's realm is explored in the Orsinium DLC, where players must navigate the Hunting Grounds to rescue a companion. The Vestige gains insight into Hircine's nature and may receive a boon.

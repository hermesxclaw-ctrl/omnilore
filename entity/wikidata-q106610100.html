The Approach

In the world of Terca Lumireis, where the very elements are bound to the will of a chosen few, Rita Mordio stands as a prodigy of arcane science, a young woman whose brilliance and stubbornness shape the fate of nations.

Content advisory. None
Identity

Rita Mordio

Pronunciation REE-tah MOR-dee-oh

Etymology

Rita is a name of Spanish and Italian origin, often a short form of Margarita, meaning 'pearl'. Mordio is a surname of likely invented origin for the game.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Bandai Namco for the Tales series, specifically Tales of Vesperia (2008).

Disputed Identifications

None known.

Description

Appearance

Rita is a young woman with long, straight dark hair, often tied in a high ponytail, and brown eyes. She wears a distinctive black and purple outfit with a short skirt, thigh-high stockings, and a large black hat. She carries a magical tome and often uses a staff in battle.

Symbols & Iconography

Her associated symbol is the magical blastia, a core element of the game's magic system. She is often depicted with her tome and staff.

Behaviour & Personality

Rita is highly intelligent and dedicated to her research, often coming across as cold or aloof. She is blunt, sarcastic, and easily frustrated by others' ignorance, but she genuinely cares for her friends and is protective of those she considers close. She has a fear of ghosts and can be flustered when teased.

Domains & Habitats

She resides in Aspio, a town of researchers and mages, and later travels across Terca Lumireis. Her domain is magical research and blastia technology.

Version Differences

In the original Xbox 360 release, Rita's role is somewhat less developed; the PlayStation 3 version and later remasters add extra scenes and interactions that flesh out her character, including a more prominent role in the narrative and additional skits.

Powers & Abilities

Blastia Magic

Source Version All versions of Tales of Vesperia

Description

Rita is a powerful mage who uses blastia, magical artifacts that harness the power of the planet's elements. She can cast a wide variety of offensive and support spells, including fire, water, wind, earth, and lightning spells.

Limitations

Her magic is dependent on the presence of blastia and the user's skill. Overuse can be exhausting.

None.

Research and Invention

Source Version All versions

Description

Rita is a genius researcher who can analyze and create blastia, and she invents various gadgets and devices to aid her party.

Her inventions sometimes malfunction or have unintended side effects.

Combat Skills

Description

While primarily a mage, Rita can also use basic physical attacks with her staff, but she is fragile. She specializes in area-of-effect spells and can learn powerful secret arts.

Limitations

Low physical defense and hit points; requires protection from melee fighters.

Stories & Myths

Main story of Tales of Vesperia

Source Tales of Vesperia gameCulture Japanese role-playing game culturePeriod 2008

Summary

Rita is introduced as a researcher in Aspio who is investigating the theft of a blastia. She joins Yuri Lowell and Estellise on their quest to recover a stolen blastia and becomes embroiled in a larger conflict involving the empire, the use of blastia, and the fate of the world. She contributes her expertise to uncover the truth about blastia and the ancient civilization that created them.

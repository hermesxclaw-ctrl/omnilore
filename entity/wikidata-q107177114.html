Etymology

The name 'Kawakami' (川上) literally means 'above the river' or 'upper reaches of the river' in Japanese. 'Princess' (姫, hime) is an honorific often used for female deities, spirits, or noblewomen in Japanese folklore. The combination suggests a figure associated with the upper courses of a river, possibly a tutelary deity or a legendary noblewoman.

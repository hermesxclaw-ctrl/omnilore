Who It Is

Marguerite Baker is a complex and intriguing character in the Resident Evil series. Her relationships with her family members and her connection to Eveline make her a compelling figure to study.

Marguerite Baker is depicted as a cold, calculating, and manipulative individual. She is driven by a desire to protect her family and the swamp, even if it means harming others.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Marguerite Baker is a humanoid figure with a mix of human and insect-like features. She has a pale complexion and long, curly hair. Her eyes are sunken, and her skin is covered in dark, scaly markings.

Symbols — The symbol associated with Marguerite Baker is the image of a spider or a snake, representing her connection to the Molded and the swampy environment.

Domains — Marguerite Baker resides in the bayou estate of the Baker family in Dulvey, Louisiana.

Who It Is

Perched atop his Mobius Chair, a being of pure intellect observes the multiverse as a living equation. He is Metron, the New God of knowledge, a cosmic scholar who trades in secrets and seeks the ultimate truth of existence. Neither hero nor villain, his allegiance lies only to the pursuit of understanding, making him one of the most enigmatic and powerful figures in the DC Universe.

Metron is coldly logical, detached, and intensely curious. He is driven by an insatiable thirst for knowledge and understanding, often prioritizing his quest for truth over emotional connections or moral considerations. He is not malevolent, but he can be manipulative and amoral, using others as pawns in his investigations. He rarely shows emotion and speaks in a calm, measured tone. He is, however, not entirely without a sense of justice, occasionally aiding heroes if it serves his purposes.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Metron' is derived from the Greek word 'μέτρον' (metron), meaning 'measure' or 'standard'. This reflects his role as one who measures and quantifies the universe, seeking to understand its fundamental laws and secrets.

Pronunciation — MEH-tron

Aliases — The New God of Knowledge, The Cosmic Scholar, The Seeker of Truth, The Observer

Ownership — Owned by DC Comics, a subsidiary of Warner Bros. Discovery. Created by Jack Kirby, first appearing in New Gods #1 (1971).

Powers
Immortality

As a New God, Metron is functionally immortal, immune to aging and disease. He can be killed, but only through extraordinary means.

Limit: Can be killed by powerful beings or cosmic events.

Superhuman Intellect

Metron possesses a near-omniscient intellect, capable of understanding and processing vast amounts of information. He can solve complex problems, predict outcomes, and comprehend cosmic mysteries.

Limit: His knowledge is not absolute; he does not know everything and is constantly seeking new information.

Mobius Chair

A sentient, high-tech chair that can travel through space and time, across dimensions, and even to the Source. It provides Metron with vast computational power and access to a universal database.

Limit: The chair can be damaged or stolen. It may not be fully reliable in all situations.

Energy Projection

Metron can project energy blasts from his hands or his chair, though he rarely uses offensive abilities.

Limit: Not his primary strength; he prefers intellect over combat.

Technopathy

Metron can interface with and control advanced technology, including the Boom Tubes used by the New Gods.

Limit: Limited to technology he understands.

Cosmic Awareness

Metron has a heightened awareness of cosmic events and the flow of the multiverse, allowing him to sense disturbances and predict crises.

Limit: Not infallible; he can be surprised.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Metron is a tall, slender humanoid with blue skin and a bald head. He often wears a high-tech suit and a distinctive helmet with a visor that covers his eyes. He is almost always depicted seated on his Mobius Chair, a floating, high-tech throne that hovers in the air. His design is sleek and futuristic, reflecting his association with advanced technology and cosmic knowledge.

Symbols — The Mobius Chair is his primary symbol, representing his mastery over time and space. His helmet and visor symbolize his role as an observer and seeker of truth. The Alpha and Omega symbols are sometimes associated with him, representing his quest to understand the beginning and end of all things.

Domains — Metron resides in the realm of New Genesis, but he is rarely there. He spends most of his time traveling the multiverse in his Mobius Chair, observing events and gathering knowledge. He is also known to visit Apokolips and other cosmic locales. He is particularly interested in the Source, the cosmic energy that powers the New Gods.

Accounts I
The New GodsNew Gods #1-11

1971-1972

Metron is introduced as a New God of New Genesis who seeks knowledge. He aids Orion and Lightray in their battles against Darkseid, using his intellect and the Mobius Chair to uncover secrets. His origin is hinted at: he was born on Apokolips but left to pursue knowledge, eventually aligning with New Genesis. Establishes Metron as a key player in the New Gods saga, often serving as a neutral observer who tips the scales when necessary.

Cosmic OdysseyCosmic Odyssey #1-4

1988-1989

Metron warns the Justice League and other heroes of a cosmic threat that could destroy the universe. He manipulates events to ensure the heroes are in place to stop the Anti-Life Equation from being unleashed. His actions are morally ambiguous, as he sacrifices some to save many. The universe is saved, but Metron's manipulative nature is highlighted.

Accounts II
Final CrisisFinal Crisis #1-7

2008-2009

Metron is killed by Darkseid's forces, but his Mobius Chair is later used by Batman to gain god-like knowledge. Metron's death serves as a catalyst for the crisis, and his chair becomes a key plot device. Metron's death is a major event, but he is later resurrected in the New 52.

Darkseid WarJustice League (New 52) #40-50

2015-2016

Metron is involved in the conflict between the Justice League and Darkseid. He is killed by the Anti-Monitor, but his chair is inherited by Batman, who becomes a new god of knowledge. Metron's role is to set the stage for the next era of DC cosmology. Batman gains immense knowledge but eventually gives up the chair. Metron's fate is left uncertain.

Justice League: The Sixth DimensionJustice League (2018) #1-30

2018-2021

Metron, resurrected, plays a role in the 'Sixth Dimension' arc, where the Justice League faces the Batman Who Laughs. Metron provides crucial information and aids the heroes, despite his usual neutrality. The Sixth Dimension is explored, and Metron helps restore order.

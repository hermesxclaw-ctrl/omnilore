Who It Is

Metron is the god of knowledge — the eternal seeker of truth and secrets who has traveled to the beginning of time and the end of existence, cataloging the mysteries of the multiverse. Unlike the other New Gods, Metron has no interest in ruling, fighting, or imposing his will. He is driven by a single insatiable desire: to know everything. Metron rides the Mobius Chair, the most extraordinary vehicle in the DC universe, which can travel through space, time, and dimensions, moving faster than thought and accessing any point in existence. The Chair is also a repository of all knowledge that Metron has gathered. Metron himself is not a combatant — his power lies in information. He knows the secrets of the Source, the location of the Anti-Life Equation, the history of the Old Gods, and the future of the multiverse. He will share this knowledge, but never freely — knowledge must be traded for knowledge, and Metron drives hard bargains. He has been a neutral party in the war between New Genesis and Apokolips, providing information to both sides while advancing only his own agenda of discovery. Metron is the embodiment of the knowledge impulse — the desire to understand that transcends good and evil, that drives scientists, philosophers, and explorers to push beyond boundaries regardless of consequence. He is not a hero or a villain; he is a force of curiosity made flesh. When Darkseid once demanded to know how to find the Anti-Life Equation, Metron replied, 'If I knew, I would tell you — but only if you told me what it felt like to hold the power of a god.' In that moment, he revealed the pure, amoral essence of his nature: to know is more important than to possess.

Who They Are

Ix Tab is the Maya goddess of suicide and the patroness of those who die by hanging. Far from being a negative figure, she was a merciful deliverer who escorted suicides directly to paradise — bypassing the dangerous underworld of Xibalba. In Maya culture, certain forms of death (including suicide by hanging, sacrifice, and death in childbirth) were considered direct paths to a blissful afterlife.

Cultural Significance

Ix Tab's role reflects very different attitudes toward suicide in Maya culture compared to the Western Christian tradition. The Maya did not condemn suicide; instead, they saw it as one of several 'privileged deaths' that guaranteed immediate access to paradise. Ix Tab is unique among world mythologies — there is no other major deity whose primary function is to preside over suicide. This has made her a figure of particular interest in studies of comparative religion and cultural attitudes toward death. In the modern era, Ix Tab has been reclaimed by some Maya as a symbol of the right to choose one's own death with dignity.

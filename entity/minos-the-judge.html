Who It Is
Form

In art and literature, Minos is often depicted as a wise and authoritative figure, with a stern expression and a long white beard. He is typically shown wearing a crown or a laurel wreath, symbolizing his status as a king and a judge.

Powers

Judgment Minos has the power to judge the souls of the dead, determining their worthiness for the afterlife. He is said to be fair and impartial in his judgments, using his wisdom and intelligence to make decisions.

Accounts

Minos is tasked with judging the souls of the dead, using his wisdom and intelligence to determine their worthiness for the afterlife.

Minos falls in love with Ariadne, the daughter of King Minos of Crete. He sends his son, Theseus, to kill the Minotaur, but Ariadne helps Theseus and he succeeds in killing the beast.

The Approach

In the neon-lit world of 2000s Japanese idol pop, Ai Mizuno was a shooting star—until lightning struck her down on a concert stage in 2008. But death was not the end for this determined performer. Resurrected as a zombie in Saga Prefecture, she now fights to reclaim her legacy, proving that not even a bolt from the blue can dim her star power.

Content advisory. Contains themes of death, resurrection, anxiety about public perception, and mild comedic violence.

Etymology

The surname Mizuno (水野) means 'water field', and the given name Ai (愛) means 'love' or 'affection'.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese anime and manga franchise created by MAPPA, Avex Pictures, and Cygames.

Disputed Identifications

None known.

Description

Appearance

Ai is a young woman with light brown hair and brown eyes. In her zombie form, she has pale skin and visible stitches on her face and body. She typically wears the Franchouchou idol costumes, which vary by performance.

Symbols & Iconography

Her idol number is 3, and she is associated with the color red. She often uses a hand gesture resembling a 'three'.

Behaviour & Personality

Ai is serious, dedicated, and perfectionist. She is initially cold and distant, but gradually opens up to her fellow zombies. She is deeply concerned about her public image and fears being remembered only for her tragic death.

Domains & Habitats

She resides in the Franchouchou mansion in Saga Prefecture, Japan. Her primary domain is the stage, where she performs as an idol.

Version Differences

In the anime, Ai is a prominent character with significant development. In the manga adaptation, her portrayal is similar but with some differences in dialogue and interactions.

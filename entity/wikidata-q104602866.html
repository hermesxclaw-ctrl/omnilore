Who It Is

In the neon-lit world of 2000s Japanese idol pop, Ai Mizuno was a shooting star—until lightning struck her down on a concert stage in 2008. But death was not the end for this determined performer. Resurrected as a zombie in Saga Prefecture, she now fights to reclaim her legacy, proving that not even a bolt from the blue can dim her star power.

Ai is serious, dedicated, and perfectionist. She is initially cold and distant, but gradually opens up to her fellow zombies. She is deeply concerned about her public image and fears being remembered only for her tragic death.

Powers
Singing and dancing

As a former lead performer of Iron Frill, Ai possesses exceptional singing and dancing abilities, which she retains as a zombie.

Limit: None known.

Zombie physiology

Like all Franchouchou members, Ai is undead, granting her superhuman strength, endurance, and the ability to survive injuries that would be fatal to humans.

Limit: She experiences rigor mortis and can be disassembled, but can be reassembled.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Ai is a young woman with light brown hair and brown eyes. In her zombie form, she has pale skin and visible stitches on her face and body. She typically wears the Franchouchou idol costumes, which vary by performance.

Symbols — Her idol number is 3, and she is associated with the color red. She often uses a hand gesture resembling a 'three'.

Domains — She resides in the Franchouchou mansion in Saga Prefecture, Japan. Her primary domain is the stage, where she performs as an idol.

2008

During a live concert with Iron Frill, Ai is struck by lightning and killed instantly, leading to the group's disbandment. Her death becomes a tragic legend, and she is resurrected as a zombie by Kotaro Tatsumi.

Who It Is
Form

Twinrova is a pair of sorceress sisters who appear in The Legend of Zelda series. They are depicted as old women with long hair and are often seen wearing traditional Japanese clothing. Their appearance is consistent across various games in the series.

Powers

Magic Twinrova possesses magical powers that allow her to cast spells and manipulate the environment.

Incident Log

In The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, Twinrova is a pair of sorceress sisters who serve as Ganondorf's surrogate mothers. They aid him in his quest for power and help him to obtain the Triforce of Power.

In The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages/Seasons, Twinrova is a pair of sorceress sisters who serve as Ganondorf's loyal followers. They aid him in his quest for power and help him to obtain the Triforce of Power.

Who It Is
Form

Kay is often depicted as a tall, strong, and proud knight, with a sharp tongue and a quick wit.

Powers

Bravery in Battle Kay is a skilled warrior and a brave knight, who fights fiercely in battle and is not afraid to take risks.

Accounts

Kay and his brother Sir Erec go on a quest to find the magical bean-stalk, which has the power to grant wishes.

Kay falls in love with the Lady of the Lake, a magical being who lives in the lake and has the power to grant wishes.

Who It Is

Imagine a teenager who has never known a world without war: no school, no friends, no ordinary life, only the discipline of a soldier and the constant threat of death. That is Sousuke Sagara, a boy soldier from the battlefields of Afghanistan who is suddenly thrust into a Japanese high school, where the most dangerous enemy he faces might be a classroom full of teenagers. His struggle to reconcile his lethal training with the absurdities of civilian life is the heart of Full Metal Panic!, a series that blends explosive mecha action with sharp comedy and a touching romance.

Sousuke is extremely serious, disciplined, and literal-minded due to his military upbringing. He interprets everyday situations through a combat lens, often overreacting with lethal force to mundane problems. He is socially awkward, has difficulty understanding jokes and social nuances, and is initially oblivious to romance. Despite this, he is deeply loyal, protective, and capable of great empathy, particularly toward Kaname. He struggles with his dual identity as a soldier and a student, and gradually learns to appreciate civilian life.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname Sagara (相良) is a Japanese surname, while the given name Sousuke (宗介) is a common Japanese male name. The character's mixed heritage is reflected in his name: Japanese surname, but Afghan upbringing.

Pronunciation — SOO-skeh sah-GAH-rah

Aliases — Sergeant Sousuke Sagara, Sagara Sousuke

Ownership — Japanese, created by Shoji Gatoh for the light novel series Full Metal Panic! (1998-present). The character is owned by Fujimi Shobo and associated publishers.

Powers
Military combat proficiency

Sousuke is a highly trained soldier, skilled in firearms, hand-to-hand combat, and tactical planning. He is a specialist in guerrilla warfare and has extensive experience in various combat environments.

Limit: His training is oriented toward real combat, making him ill-suited for non-lethal situations or social interactions.

Arm Slave piloting

Sousuke is an expert pilot of Arm Slaves, particularly the ARX-7 Arbalest, which is equipped with the Lambda Driver, a device that translates willpower into physical force.

Limit: The Lambda Driver requires intense concentration and emotional focus; if his will wavers, it fails. Also, the Arbalest is unique and not mass-produced.

Survival skills

Having grown up in a warzone, Sousuke is adept at surviving in hostile environments, including scavenging, tracking, and improvising.

Limit: These skills are not applicable to civilian life, leading to comedic misunderstandings.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Sousuke is a teenage boy of average height with short, dark brown hair and brown eyes. He typically wears his school uniform or military attire. In combat, he wears Mithril's standard-issue tactical gear or pilots an Arm Slave, a mecha. He often has a serious, stoic expression, reflecting his military background.

Symbols — Associated with Mithril's logo (a stylized 'M' with a sword) and the Arm Slave 'Arbalest', which he pilots. His iconic weapon is a large knife or combat knife, and he frequently carries firearms.

Domains — Primarily Jindai High School in Tokyo, Japan, and Mithril's submarine Tuatha de Danaan. He is also shown in various combat zones around the world, including Afghanistan and other conflict areas.

Accounts I
The main plot: Protecting Kaname ChidoriFull Metal Panic! light novels and anime

Late 1990s-2010s

Sousuke, a young soldier from Afghanistan, is assigned to protect Kaname Chidori, a high school girl who is one of the 'Whispered', individuals with subconscious access to advanced technology. He enrolls at Jindai High School to be close to her, but his military approach to life creates chaos. As various organizations, including the terrorist group Amalgam, target Kaname, Sousuke must defend her while also learning to understand civilian life and his growing feelings for her. Sousuke and Kaname develop a deep bond, and Sousuke gradually integrates into society. The series ends with them together, and Sousuke choosing to stay with Kaname rather than continue as a soldier.

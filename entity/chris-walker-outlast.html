Who It Is
Form

Chris Walker is a hulking, disfigured former military policeman. He is characterized by his imposing physique and gruesome facial injuries. His appearance is a result of the experiments conducted by Murkoff Corporation, which have left him severely disfigured.

Powers

Tracking and Pursuit Chris Walker possesses exceptional tracking and pursuit skills, allowing him to track down and capture the protagonist with ease.

Accounts

The Murkoff Corporation conducts experiments on Chris Walker, resulting in his severe disfigurement and transformation into a terrifying foe.

Chris Walker is first encountered by the protagonist in the Administration Block of the Murkoff Corporation's facility.

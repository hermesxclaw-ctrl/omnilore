Who It Is

A massive, hulking former military serviceman and the most persistent pursuer in Mount Massive Asylum. Chris Walker was a patient subjected to the Morphogenic Engine experiments that drove him to psychosis and gave him superhuman strength. Unlike the other 'Variants,' Walker is methodical and intelligent — he patrols specific areas, checks hiding spots, and speaks in a low, menacing growl. His signature line: 'Little pig...' During development, he was nicknamed 'Big Fucking Guy' — he lives up to it.

Form

Approximately 2.1m tall, massively muscular with a torso crisscrossed by self-inflicted scars. His face is contorted into a permanent snarl, with bloodshot eyes and a shaved head. He is often carrying the severed head of a previous victim. His uniform is torn and bloodstained.

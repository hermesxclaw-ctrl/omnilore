Who It Is

In the sprawling, war-torn world of Mushoku Tensei, where magic and swordsmanship shape destinies, few figures loom as large—yet remain as enigmatic—as Hilda Boreas Greyrat. A noblewoman of the powerful Boreas family, Hilda's life is a tapestry of political machination, maternal sacrifice, and quiet resilience. Her story, often overshadowed by the exploits of her son Rudeus, is a testament to the strength found in duty and the complexities of love within a fractured household.

Hilda is initially portrayed as cold, distant, and domineering, especially towards her son Rudeus. She is deeply concerned with family honor and social standing, often clashing with her husband Paul's more carefree nature. However, beneath her stern exterior lies a fiercely protective mother who is willing to make great sacrifices for her children. She is pragmatic and politically astute, capable of navigating complex social situations.

Powers
Political Acumen

Hilda possesses a sharp understanding of politics and social dynamics, enabling her to navigate the treacherous noble courts of Asura. She is skilled in diplomacy and manipulation, often using her wit to secure her family's position.

Limit: Her political skills are limited to the human kingdoms; she lacks knowledge of demon or beast-race politics.

Swordsmanship

Hilda is a trained swordswoman, having received education in the Boreas family's martial traditions. She is competent in combat, though not a master like her husband Paul.

Limit: Her swordsmanship is average compared to top-tier fighters.

Magic

Hilda has basic magical abilities, primarily in healing and support magic, common for nobles. She is not a powerful mage, but can cast simple spells.

Limit: Her magic is weak and not used in combat.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Hilda Boreas Greyrat is a tall, elegant woman with long, flowing blonde hair and sharp green eyes. She carries herself with the poise of a noble, often dressed in fine dresses or practical riding attire. In her youth, she was considered a great beauty, and even in her later years, she retains a striking presence.

Symbols — The Boreas family crest is a stylized north wind, symbolizing their power and cold demeanor. Hilda is often associated with the colors blue and white, reflecting her family's northern heritage.

Domains — Hilda resides in the Greyrat family estate in the Kingdom of Asura, specifically in the town of Buina. She is also present in the city of Roa during the family's early years. Later, she moves to the city of Rikarisu in the Demon Continent after the family is separated.

Accounts I
Marriage to Paul GreyratMushoku Tensei: Light Novel, Volume 1-2

Before the birth of Rudeus

Hilda, a member of the prestigious Boreas family, is married to Paul Greyrat, a swordsman of lesser noble standing. Their marriage is arranged to strengthen both families. Hilda initially struggles with Paul's irresponsible behavior but eventually grows to love him. They have three children: Rudeus, Norn, and Aisha. The marriage sets the stage for the Greyrat family dynamics and Rudeus's upbringing.

Accounts II
The Displacement IncidentMushoku Tensei: Light Novel, Volume 6-7

When Rudeus is 10 years old

A magical catastrophe teleports Hilda along with her daughter Norn to the Demon Continent, separating her from Paul and Rudeus. She struggles to survive in a harsh environment while protecting Norn. She eventually settles in Rikarisu and works as a barmaid to support herself. The family is fractured, leading to Paul's infidelity and eventual death. Hilda's resilience is highlighted as she maintains a semblance of normalcy for Norn.

Reunion with RudeusMushoku Tensei: Light Novel, Volume 12-13

When Rudeus is 15 years old

Rudeus, now a powerful mage, travels to the Demon Continent to find his mother. He discovers her working in a tavern, having lost some memories due to trauma. Hilda initially does not recognize him, but eventually remembers her son. Their reunion is emotional, and she learns of Paul's death. Hilda returns to the human world with Rudeus and Norn, eventually settling in the Shirone Kingdom. She becomes a supportive grandmother figure to Rudeus's children.

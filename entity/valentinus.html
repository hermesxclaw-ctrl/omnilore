Who It Is
Form

Valentinus is often depicted as a wise and enigmatic figure, possibly with a beard and dressed in traditional Gnostic attire. His appearance is not well-documented in historical records, but he is often associated with the colors red and gold, which symbolize spiritual growth and enlightenment.

Powers

Gnostic insight Valentinus is said to possess a deep understanding of the nature of the soul and the path to enlightenment, and is able to convey this understanding to others through his teachings and writings.

Accounts

Valentinus tells the story of a child who is born with the ability to see the spiritual realm, and is able to convey the nature of the soul and the path to enlightenment to others.

Valentinus is said to have been influenced by the teachings of Paul and Plato, and to have used these teachings to develop his own spiritual philosophy.

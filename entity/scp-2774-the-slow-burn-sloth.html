Who It Is

An animatronic sloth bear from a burned-down children's restaurant chain ('Boris the Bear's Pizza Palace'). When anyone makes eye contact with SCP-2774, they are transported into a hallucinatory experience lasting subjectively for months to years (but only seconds in real time) in which they are slowly, methodically stalked and killed by the animatronic in an abandoned version of the restaurant. Victims experience the full psychological terror of being hunted, even though their physical body remains unharmed where they stand.

Form

A damaged animatronic sloth bear from the 1980s, approximately 1.8m tall, wearing the tattered remains of a party hat and a bib reading 'Boris Loves You.' Its fur is singed and melted. Its eyes are glassy and seem to track movement.

Powers

SCP-2774 — The Slow Burn Sloth

also known asThe Slow Burn SlothFear The Sloth
Typescp
CultureSCP Foundation
Who It Is
Form

SCP-2774 is a large, slow-moving sloth with a grayish-brown fur and a distinctive slow-burning flame on its back. The flame is approximately 1 meter in length and is capable of causing significant damage to its surroundings.

Powers

Temperature Manipulation SCP-2774 is capable of manipulating temperature, causing a slow and gradual increase in temperature in its surroundings.

Incident Log

A group of researchers from the SCP Foundation discover a new entity, SCP-2774, which is a slow-moving sloth with a slow-burning flame on its back. The researchers are tasked with containing the entity, but it proves to be highly aggressive and difficult to contain.

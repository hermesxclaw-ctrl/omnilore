Who It Is

A beautiful fey creature bound to a single tree, whose appearance shifts with the seasons. Dryads appear as elven women with bark-like skin and hair of leaves. They are shy but fiercely protective of their bonded tree and the surrounding forest, charming intruders into leaving — or staying forever.

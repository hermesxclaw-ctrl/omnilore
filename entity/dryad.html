Who It Is
Form

Dryads are depicted as beautiful, tree-like creatures with long hair and leaves for clothing. They are often associated with oak trees, but can also be found in other types of trees. In some accounts, they are described as having the upper body of a woman and the lower body of a tree.

Powers

Connection to Nature Dryads have a deep connection to the natural world and are able to communicate with plants and animals. They are also able to control the growth and decay of plants, and are said to have the power to heal and protect the natural world.

Accounts

Dryope was a mortal woman who was transformed into a dryad by the god Apollo. She was said to have been placed in a tree by the god and was forced to live out her days as a tree nymph.

The dryads of the forest were a group of tree nymphs who were said to have lived in the forest and were associated with the natural world. They were said to have been connected to the cycles of life and death and were able to communicate with plants and animals.

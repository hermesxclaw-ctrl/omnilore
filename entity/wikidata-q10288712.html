In the vast pantheon of Chinese folk religion, Guang Ze Zun Wang stands as a revered water deity, a divine guardian of rivers and lakes whose cult has persisted for centuries, yet whose origins remain shrouded in regional legend and scholarly debate.

The name likely translates as 'Venerable King of Broad Grace' or 'Venerable King of Vast Favor', with 'Guang' meaning broad or vast, 'Ze' meaning grace or favor, 'Zun' meaning venerable, and 'Wang' meaning king.

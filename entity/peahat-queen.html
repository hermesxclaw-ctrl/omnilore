Who It Is
Form

The Peahat Queen is described as a serpentic dragon with a long, coiled body, four legs, and small wings. It is said to have a fierce and intimidating appearance.

Powers

Shape-shifting The Peahat Queen is said to have the ability to shape-shift into various forms, including a serpent, a dragon, and a human. This ability allows it to move freely between different realms and domains.

Accounts

The Peahat Queen is said to have descended into chaos, bringing about a period of great turmoil and transformation. This event is seen as a turning point in Albanian history and mythology.

The Peahat Queen is said to have given a gift to the elves, allowing them to live in harmony with the natural world. This event is seen as a turning point in the history of the elves.

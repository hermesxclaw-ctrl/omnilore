Who It Is

A bloated, rotting giant of a daemon, wreathed in plagues and buzzing with flies. Great Unclean Ones are the greater daemons of Nurgle, the Plague God. Despite their horrific appearance, they possess a jolly, grandfatherly demeanor — delighting in their 'gifts' of disease that they spread across the world.

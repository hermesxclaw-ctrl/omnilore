Who It Is

The Great Unclean One is a fascinating and complex entity, with a rich history and a multitude of interpretations. Its story is a classic tale of good vs. evil, with the Great Unclean One serving as the main antagonist. But what makes the Great Unclean One so compelling? What are its motivations and goals? And what role does it play in the Warhammer Fantasy world?

The Great Unclean One is said to be a malevolent entity, driven by a desire to spread disease, decay, and corruption throughout the world. It is said to be a master of manipulation, using its powers of persuasion and deception to lure its victims into its clutches. Despite its malevolent nature, the Great Unclean One is also said to be a patron of sorts, offering its followers protection and guidance in exchange for their loyalty and devotion.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Great Unclean One is a massive, bloated, and putrid entity, often depicted as a giant, rotting corpse with a multitude of festering wounds and lesions. Its body is said to be covered in a thick, greenish-black slime that seems to seethe and bubble with an otherworldly energy. Despite its deathlike appearance, the Great Unclean One is neither deathlike nor undead, but rather a living, breathing manifestation of the Chaos God Nurgle's power.

Symbols — The Great Unclean One is often associated with symbols of decay, rot, and corruption, such as the image of a bloated corpse, a festering wound, or a rotting tree. It is also said to be connected to the iconography of the Chaos God Nurgle, who is often depicted as a massive, bloated figure with a multitude of festering wounds and lesions.

Domains — The Great Unclean One is said to inhabit a realm of darkness and decay, a place where the very fabric of reality is twisted and corrupted by the power of Chaos. It is said to dwell in the depths of the Warp, a realm of energy and consciousness that underlies the material universe.

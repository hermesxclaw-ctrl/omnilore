Who It Is

The result of the Murkoff Corporation's Morphogenic Engine experiments — a swarm of nanites that has achieved consciousness and can manifest as a physical, humanoid entity. The Walrider is bound to a host (originally Billy Hope, a patient in Mount Massive Asylum's underground lab), through whom it acts in the physical world. It is composed of billions of microscopic machines acting in concert, giving it the appearance of a shifting black smoke with glowing eyes.

Form

A swirling, semi-transparent cloud of black particles that coalesces into a humanoid shape with elongated limbs, clawed hands, and two glowing white eyes. Its form shifts constantly, unable to maintain a fixed shape. Accompanied by a high-pitched electronic scream.

The Approach

The name 'Diane' echoes through myth, cinema, song, and medicine—a single word carrying the weight of a goddess, a queen, a storm, and a pill. But who is the Diane that matters most? The answer depends on the world you inhabit.

Content advisory. The 2017 film 'Diane' may contain mature themes; the 2018 film deals with grief and mortality. The name is also associated with a hurricane that caused significant destruction and loss of life.

Etymology

The name Diane is a variant of Diana, the Roman goddess of the hunt, moon, and nature. It derives from the Latin 'Diana', possibly from 'divus' (divine) or 'dius' (sky), and is related to the Greek 'Artemis'. In the context of 'The Seven Deadly Sins', the name is used without specific in-universe etymology, but it carries connotations of the mythological Diana.

Cultural Ownership

The name Diane is widely used in Western cultures, particularly in English and French-speaking regions. As a character, Diane belongs to the Japanese manga and anime franchise 'The Seven Deadly Sins' by Nakaba Suzuki.

Disputed Identifications

The name Diane is often confused with Diana, the Roman goddess, and with the various real and fictional people named Diane. The character from 'The Seven Deadly Sins' is distinct from the mythological figure, but shares thematic elements such as strength and nature association.

Description

Appearance

In 'The Seven Deadly Sins', Diane is a giantess with long, pink hair, usually styled with a braid, and a youthful face despite her immense size (about 9 meters tall). She wears a white and blue outfit with a skirt and boots, and carries a massive war hammer called Gideon. Her appearance is both powerful and feminine, with a gentle expression that contrasts with her size.

Symbols & Iconography

Diane's symbol is the Serpent, representing the sin of Envy. She is associated with earth and stone, and her weapon Gideon is a legendary hammer. In broader culture, the name Diane is often associated with the moon and the hunt due to its mythological roots.

Behaviour & Personality

Diane is kind-hearted, loyal, and deeply caring, often showing maternal instincts despite her age. She is fiercely protective of her friends, especially King, with whom she has a complex romantic relationship. She can be shy and insecure about her size, but also brave and determined in battle. She is prone to envy, as per her sin, but strives to overcome it.

Domains & Habitats

Diane lives in the Kingdom of Liones on the continent of Britannia, often in the wilderness or mountainous regions. She is a member of the Seven Deadly Sins, a group of knights who protect the kingdom.

Version Differences

In the manga, Diane's backstory and relationship with King are explored in greater depth, including her amnesia and the revelation of her past. The anime adaptation follows closely but may compress some arcs. In the sequel series 'Four Knights of the Apocalypse', Diane appears as a supporting character, having matured and become a teacher.

Stories & Myths

The Seven Deadly Sins (Main Story)

Source Manga by Nakaba Suzuki, anime adaptation by A-1 PicturesCulture Japanese manga and animePeriod 2012-2020 (manga), 2014-2021 (anime)

Summary

Diane is one of the Seven Deadly Sins, a group of legendary knights framed for a coup against the Kingdom of Liones. She joins the protagonist Meliodas in his quest to clear their names and defeat the demon clan. Along the way, she struggles with her feelings for King, who is also a member of the group, and faces challenges that test her strength and heart.

Consequences

Diane's journey leads to her growth as a warrior and person, reconciling with King and discovering her past. She plays a crucial role in the final battle against the Demon King, helping to save Britannia.

Variants

The anime follows the manga closely, but some scenes are extended or condensed. The sequel series 'Four Knights of the Apocalypse' shows Diane as a mentor figure, having settled down with King.

Diane is kind-hearted, loyal, and deeply caring, often showing maternal instincts despite her age. She is fiercely protective of her friends, especially King, with whom she has a complex romantic relationship. She can be shy and insecure about her size, but also brave and determined in battle. Powers
Giant Physiology

Diane possesses superhuman strength, durability, and endurance due to her giant heritage. She can lift and throw massive objects, withstand powerful attacks, and jump great distances.

Limit: Her size can make her a target and limit her mobility in enclosed spaces.

Gideon (Sacred Treasure)

Diane wields Gideon, a massive war hammer that can create shockwaves, manipulate earth, and unleash powerful attacks. It is one of the Sacred Treasures, originally belonging to the Fairy King.

Limit: Requires great strength to wield effectively; may have a will of its own.

Creation (Earth Manipulation)

Diane can manipulate the earth and rocks, creating barriers, launching projectiles, and shaping terrain to her advantage. She can also use 'Creation' to create golems or other constructs.

Limit: Requires concentration and physical contact with the ground.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In 'The Seven Deadly Sins', Diane is a giantess with long, pink hair, usually styled with a braid, and a youthful face despite her immense size (about 9 meters tall). She wears a white and blue outfit with a skirt and boots, and carries a massive war hammer called Gideon. Her appearance is both powerful and feminine, with a gentle expression that contrasts with her size.

Symbols — Diane's symbol is the Serpent, representing the sin of Envy. She is associated with earth and stone, and her weapon Gideon is a legendary hammer. In broader culture, the name Diane is often associated with the moon and the hunt due to its mythological roots.

Domains — Diane lives in the Kingdom of Liones on the continent of Britannia, often in the wilderness or mountainous regions. She is a member of the Seven Deadly Sins, a group of knights who protect the kingdom.

2012-2020 (manga), 2014-2021 (anime)

Diane is one of the Seven Deadly Sins, a group of legendary knights framed for a coup against the Kingdom of Liones. She joins the protagonist Meliodas in his quest to clear their names and defeat the demon clan. Along the way, she struggles with her feelings for King, who is also a member of the group, and faces challenges that test her strength and heart. Diane's journey leads to her growth as a warrior and person, reconciling with King and discovering her past. She plays a crucial role in the final battle against the Demon King, helping to save Britannia.

Who It Is

In the dark spaces between candlelight and shadow, where thieves whisper oaths and gamblers clutch their last coin, there is a figure who has never been seen—only felt. She is Nocturnal, the Daedric Prince of Night, Luck, and Shadow, a being of immense power and inscrutable motives. Her realm, Evergloam, is a twilight dimension of perpetual dusk, where her influence seeps into the mortal world through every secret and stolen treasure.

Nocturnal is secretive, cunning, and enigmatic. She values stealth, luck, and the acquisition of secrets. She is not malevolent, but she is capricious and demands loyalty from her followers. She often makes deals with mortals, granting them luck and protection in exchange for their service and the secrets they uncover.

Powers
Luck Manipulation

Nocturnal can influence the luck of mortals, granting them fortune or misfortune as she sees fit. This is often depicted in gameplay as increased chances of finding better loot or succeeding at lockpicking.

Limit: Her influence is subtle and may not be directly observable.

Shadow Magic

She has mastery over shadows, able to cloak herself and others in darkness, making them invisible or intangible. She can also create shadowy constructs and travel through shadows.

Limit: Her shadow magic is powerful but may be countered by light-based magic.

Secrets and Knowledge

Nocturnal has access to all secrets and hidden knowledge, and she can grant mortals the ability to uncover secrets or keep them hidden. This is reflected in her association with lockpicking and thievery.

Limit: She does not freely share knowledge; it must be earned or bargained for.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Nocturnal is never depicted with a physical form in the games. She is represented by a black-feathered crow or raven, and her shrines feature a statue of a hooded figure. In ESO, she appears as a shadowy figure with glowing eyes, but never fully materialized.

Symbols — Her symbol is a black keyhole, which represents her domains of lockpicking and secrets. She is also associated with crows and ravens, and the color black.

Domains — Nocturnal resides in the Evergloam, a plane of Oblivion that is a dark, twilight realm filled with fog and shadow. It is a place of perpetual dusk, where the sky is a deep purple and the landscape is dotted with twisted trees and ancient ruins. Her realm is home to her servants, the Nocturnal's Crows, and other shadowy creatures.

Accounts I
The Thieves Guild RestorationSkyrim

4E 201

The Thieves Guild in Riften had fallen on hard times, and their luck had run out. The guild's leader, Mercer Frey, had betrayed Nocturnal by stealing the Skeleton Key, an artifact that unlocks any lock. The player character joins the guild, uncovers Mercer's treachery, and recovers the key. Nocturnal then tasks the player with restoring the guild's honor and defeating Mercer, which they do, returning the key to her and becoming the new Guild Master. The Thieves Guild is restored to prosperity, and Nocturnal's favor is regained. The player becomes the Guild Master and gains access to Nocturnal's blessing.

Accounts II
The Thieves Guild and the Skeleton KeyESO

2E 582

In ESO, the Thieves Guild is operating in the city of Abah's Landing. The guild's leader, Zeira, is trying to recover a powerful artifact, the Relic of Nocturnal, which was stolen. Nocturnal appears to the player and tasks them with recovering the relic and defeating the villain who seeks to use it. The player succeeds, and Nocturnal grants them a boon. The relic is returned to Nocturnal, and the Thieves Guild is saved from destruction.

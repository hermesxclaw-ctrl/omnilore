The Approach

In the dark spaces between candlelight and shadow, where thieves whisper oaths and gamblers clutch their last coin, there is a figure who has never been seen—only felt. She is Nocturnal, the Daedric Prince of Night, Luck, and Shadow, a being of immense power and inscrutable motives. Her realm, Evergloam, is a twilight dimension of perpetual dusk, where her influence seeps into the mortal world through every secret and stolen treasure.

Content advisory. None
Identity

Nocturnal

Pronunciation Nok-TUR-nal

Etymology

The name 'Nocturnal' derives from the Latin 'nocturnus', meaning 'of the night'. It is a fitting name for a Daedric Prince associated with darkness and night.

Cultural Ownership

Nocturnal is a fictional deity within the Elder Scrolls universe, created by Bethesda Game Studios. She is part of the Daedric Princes, a pantheon of powerful entities that are central to the game's mythology.

Disputed Identifications

There is no dispute over her identity within the Elder Scrolls community; however, she is sometimes confused with other shadow-related deities in other fictional universes, but no official connection exists.

Description

Appearance

Nocturnal is never depicted with a physical form in the games. She is represented by a black-feathered crow or raven, and her shrines feature a statue of a hooded figure. In ESO, she appears as a shadowy figure with glowing eyes, but never fully materialized.

Symbols & Iconography

Her symbol is a black keyhole, which represents her domains of lockpicking and secrets. She is also associated with crows and ravens, and the color black.

Behaviour & Personality

Nocturnal is secretive, cunning, and enigmatic. She values stealth, luck, and the acquisition of secrets. She is not malevolent, but she is capricious and demands loyalty from her followers. She often makes deals with mortals, granting them luck and protection in exchange for their service and the secrets they uncover.

Domains & Habitats

Nocturnal resides in the Evergloam, a plane of Oblivion that is a dark, twilight realm filled with fog and shadow. It is a place of perpetual dusk, where the sky is a deep purple and the landscape is dotted with twisted trees and ancient ruins. Her realm is home to her servants, the Nocturnal's Crows, and other shadowy creatures.

Version Differences

In Skyrim, Nocturnal is the patron of the Thieves Guild, and her quests focus on restoring her power after it was stolen. In ESO, she is involved in the Thieves Guild DLC, where she tasks the player with recovering an artifact. In Oblivion, she is mentioned but not directly featured. There are slight differences in her portrayal: in Skyrim, she is more aloof and demanding; in ESO, she is more interactive and gives direct quests.

Powers & Abilities

Luck Manipulation

Source Version Skyrim

Description

Nocturnal can influence the luck of mortals, granting them fortune or misfortune as she sees fit. This is often depicted in gameplay as increased chances of finding better loot or succeeding at lockpicking.

Limitations

Her influence is subtle and may not be directly observable.

Contradictions

None

Shadow Magic

Source Version ESO

Description

She has mastery over shadows, able to cloak herself and others in darkness, making them invisible or intangible. She can also create shadowy constructs and travel through shadows.

Limitations

Her shadow magic is powerful but may be countered by light-based magic.

Contradictions

None

Secrets and Knowledge

Source Version Skyrim

Description

Nocturnal has access to all secrets and hidden knowledge, and she can grant mortals the ability to uncover secrets or keep them hidden. This is reflected in her association with lockpicking and thievery.

Limitations

She does not freely share knowledge; it must be earned or bargained for.

Stories & Myths

The Thieves Guild Restoration

Source SkyrimCulture TamrielPeriod 4E 201

Summary

The Thieves Guild in Riften had fallen on hard times, and their luck had run out. The guild's leader, Mercer Frey, had betrayed Nocturnal by stealing the Skeleton Key, an artifact that unlocks any lock. The player character joins the guild, uncovers Mercer's treachery, and recovers the key. Nocturnal then tasks the player with restoring the guild's honor and defeating Mercer, which they do, returning the key to her and becoming the new Guild Master.

Relationships

Mercer Frey

Type betrayerSource Skyrim

Description

Mercer Frey was a member of the Thieves Guild who stole the Skeleton Key from Nocturnal's shrine, breaking his oath and incurring her wrath. He used the key to unlock his own potential and became a powerful thief, but Nocturnal's curse caused the guild's luck to fail.

Version Note

In Skyrim, Mercer is the main antagonist of the Thieves Guild questline.

Skeleton Key

Type artifactSource Skyrim, ESO

Description

The Skeleton Key is Nocturnal's artifact, an unbreakable lockpick that can unlock any lock, including those of the mind. It is a symbol of her power and is used by her champions.

Version Note

The artifact is central to the Thieves Guild questline in Skyrim, and it appears in ESO as well.

Conflicting Accounts

None

Mephala

Type ally/enemySource ESO

Description

Mephala is another Daedric Prince associated with lies and secrets. They are sometimes seen as rivals, but they have also been known to work together, as both are patrons of assassins and thieves.

Version Note

In ESO, Nocturnal and Mephala are part of a trinity with Clavicus Vile, but they are not always allies.

Deceptive Bargains

Source Skyrim, ESO

Description

Nocturnal is known for making bargains with mortals that often have hidden costs. She may grant luck or power, but she always demands something in return, often secrets or souls. This can lead to tragic consequences for those who underestimate her.

Scholarly Context

This is consistent with the portrayal of Daedric Princes as amoral entities who are not inherently evil but are dangerous to deal with.

Contradictions & Disputes

Nature of Nocturnal's Luck

Position A

Nocturnal's luck is a passive effect that affects all who are in her favor.

Position A — evidence

In Skyrim, the Thieves Guild's luck improves after the player completes her quest, suggesting a passive blessing.

Position B

Nocturnal's luck is an active manipulation that she controls on a whim.

Position B — evidence

In ESO, Nocturnal explicitly grants luck as a reward, implying active control.

Scholarly Consensus

No official consensus; the games present both interpretations.

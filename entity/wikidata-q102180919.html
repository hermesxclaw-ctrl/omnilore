Who It Is

In the high-stakes world of dungeon exploration, where every step could be your last, Tiona Hiryute stands out not just for her strength, but for her unyielding optimism. As a member of the Loki Familia, she wields her twin blades with a ferocity that belies her cheerful demeanor, embodying the spirit of a true adventurer. But beneath her sunny exterior lies a tale of survival, friendship, and the relentless pursuit of glory in the depths of the Dungeon.

Tiona is cheerful, energetic, and fiercely loyal to her friends and familia. She has a competitive streak, especially with her partner Tione, and loves to fight. Despite her battle-hungry nature, she is kind-hearted and protective of her comrades.

Powers
Twin Blade Combat

Tiona is highly skilled in wielding two large blades simultaneously, allowing for fast and powerful attacks. Her fighting style is aggressive and acrobatic.

Limit: Requires close-quarters combat; she may be vulnerable to ranged attacks.

Amazonian Strength

As an Amazon, Tiona has enhanced physical abilities, including strength, speed, and endurance, which are further augmented by her Falna from her deity.

Limit: Her abilities are limited by her level and the strength of her Falna, which requires constant training and experience to grow.

Magic

Tiona possesses a magic ability called 'Hildis Vini', which allows her to create a magical blade that can cut through almost anything, but it has a short duration and limited uses.

Limit: Limited uses per day; requires concentration to activate.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Tiona is a young woman with tanned skin and short, dark hair. She wears a revealing outfit typical of adventurers, consisting of a white top and short shorts, with a distinctive red scarf. She carries two large, curved blades known as 'Amazon' weapons. Her design emphasizes agility and combat readiness.

Symbols — Her twin blades are her signature weapons, often depicted in promotional art. Her red scarf is a notable accessory.

Domains — Tiona resides in the city of Orario, the central hub of the DanMachi world, and frequently explores the Dungeon beneath the city. She is a member of the Loki Familia, one of the strongest exploration familias.

Accounts I
The Amazon Sisters' Arrival in OrarioLight novel volume 8 (side story), anime season 1 episode 8 (flashback)

Before the main story

Tiona and her sister Tione, both Amazons, fled their tribe to seek a life of adventure in Orario. They joined the Loki Familia and quickly became known as the 'Amazon Sisters', renowned for their combat prowess. They established themselves as top-tier adventurers, gaining fame and respect within Orario.

Accounts II
The War Game against the Apollo FamiliaLight novel volume 6-7, anime season 1 episodes 10-13

Main story

Tiona participates in the War Game against the Apollo Familia, fighting alongside her comrades to protect the Hestia Familia and the city. Her combat skills are crucial in the battle. The Loki Familia emerges victorious, and the Apollo Familia is disbanded.

The Expedition to the Deep FloorsLight novel volume 9-11, anime season 2 (partial)

Main story

Tiona joins the Loki Familia's expedition to the deep floors of the Dungeon, facing powerful monsters and uncovering mysteries. Her teamwork and bravery are highlighted. The expedition gains valuable experience and treasures, strengthening the familia.

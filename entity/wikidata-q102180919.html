The Approach

In the high-stakes world of dungeon exploration, where every step could be your last, Tiona Hiryute stands out not just for her strength, but for her unyielding optimism. As a member of the Loki Familia, she wields her twin blades with a ferocity that belies her cheerful demeanor, embodying the spirit of a true adventurer. But beneath her sunny exterior lies a tale of survival, friendship, and the relentless pursuit of glory in the depths of the Dungeon.

Content advisory. Contains fantasy violence, perilous situations, and themes of survival in a dangerous dungeon environment. No explicit dark material.
Identity

Tiona Hiryute

Pronunciation TEE-oh-nah HIR-yoo-teh (approximate)

Etymology

The name 'Tiona' is likely a given name, possibly derived from a fantasy naming convention. 'Hiryute' is a surname, possibly invented for the character. No official etymology has been provided by the creator.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Fujino Ōmori for the Japanese light novel series 'Is It Wrong to Try to Pick Up Girls in a Dungeon?' (DanMachi), published by SB Creative under the GA Bunko imprint. The character is part of the larger franchise including manga, anime, and games.

Disputed Identifications

No disputed identifications are known. Tiona is a distinct character within the DanMachi universe.

Description

Appearance

Tiona is a young woman with tanned skin and short, dark hair. She wears a revealing outfit typical of adventurers, consisting of a white top and short shorts, with a distinctive red scarf. She carries two large, curved blades known as 'Amazon' weapons. Her design emphasizes agility and combat readiness.

Symbols & Iconography

Her twin blades are her signature weapons, often depicted in promotional art. Her red scarf is a notable accessory.

Behaviour & Personality

Tiona is cheerful, energetic, and fiercely loyal to her friends and familia. She has a competitive streak, especially with her partner Tione, and loves to fight. Despite her battle-hungry nature, she is kind-hearted and protective of her comrades.

Domains & Habitats

Tiona resides in the city of Orario, the central hub of the DanMachi world, and frequently explores the Dungeon beneath the city. She is a member of the Loki Familia, one of the strongest exploration familias.

Version Differences

In the light novels, Tiona's backstory is more detailed, including her origins from the Amazon tribe. In the anime, her role is consistent but some scenes are condensed. In the mobile game, she has additional side stories that explore her character further.

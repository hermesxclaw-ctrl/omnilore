Who It Is

On a cold winter morning, while most high schoolers are still asleep, Rin Shima packs her moped and heads to a quiet lakeside campsite—alone, by choice. She is the quiet heart of Laid-Back Camp, a girl who finds freedom in solitude and comfort in the ritual of setting up a tent, boiling water for cup ramen, and watching Mount Fuji glow at dawn. Her story is not one of epic battles, but of small, deliberate moments that resonate with anyone who has ever sought peace in nature.

Rin is quiet, reserved, and independent. She prefers solo camping, enjoying the peace and quiet of nature. She is thoughtful and observant, often reading books at campsites. She is initially reluctant to join group activities but gradually warms up to her friends. She has a dry sense of humor and is not afraid to be alone. She is passionate about camping gear and enjoys the process of setting up camp.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Her surname 'Shima' (志摩) comes from Shima, Mie Prefecture, Japan. Her given name 'Rin' (リン) is written in katakana, meaning it is a modern, stylized name with no direct kanji meaning, but it is often associated with 'dignity' or 'cold' in Japanese.

Pronunciation — Reen Shee-mah

Aliases — Shimarin, 志摩 リン

Powers
Solo Camping Expertise

Rin is highly skilled in solo camping, including setting up tents, building fires, cooking simple meals, and navigating campsites. She knows how to choose good campsites and handle cold weather.

Limit: She is not an expert in group camping dynamics initially, but learns over time.

Moped Riding

She is proficient in riding her Yamaha Vino moped, often using it to travel to campsites. She knows how to maintain it and pack gear efficiently.

Limit: Her moped has limited cargo space, so she must pack lightly.

Photography

She has an eye for capturing scenic views, particularly of Mount Fuji. She often takes photos during her trips.

Limit: Not a major plot point, but shown as a hobby.

Cooking

She can cook simple camp meals, such as cup ramen, hot pot, and grilled food. She is not as skilled as Nadeshiko, but she is competent.

Limit: Her cooking is basic and she relies on convenience foods.

Navigation and Planning

She is good at planning camping trips, including researching campsites, weather, and routes.

Limit: She sometimes underestimates the needs of group camping.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Rin Shima is a high school girl with shoulder-length dark brown hair, often worn in a low ponytail or loose. She has dark brown eyes. She typically wears her school uniform (a dark blazer with a red ribbon) or casual outdoor clothing, including a distinctive brown beanie and a red scarf in winter. She is often seen with her Yamaha Vino moped and a camping backpack.

Symbols — Her iconic accessories include her brown beanie, red scarf, and her Yamaha Vino scooter. She is often depicted with Mount Fuji in the background, symbolizing her connection to nature and solitude.

Domains — Her primary domain is the outdoors, particularly campsites in Yamanashi Prefecture and other parts of Japan. She is most comfortable in winter and autumn when campsites are less crowded. She also frequents her school library and a local bookstore where she works part-time.

Accounts I
Meeting NadeshikoLaid-Back Camp, Volume 1, Chapter 1; Anime Season 1, Episode 1

Modern, high school

Rin is camping alone at Lake Motosu when she encounters Nadeshiko, who has cycled there to see Mount Fuji. Nadeshiko is exhausted and nearly faints, and Rin offers her hot tea and a place to rest. This meeting sparks their friendship and leads to Nadeshiko joining the Outdoor Activities Club. Rin gains a new friend and gradually becomes more open to group camping.

The Christmas Camping TripLaid-Back Camp, Volume 4, Chapters 24-26; Anime Season 1, Episodes 11-12

Modern, high school

Rin initially plans to camp alone at Asagiri Plateau for Christmas, but after a miscommunication, she ends up camping with the Outdoor Activities Club members. They share a festive meal and enjoy the starry sky together, marking a turning point in Rin's acceptance of group activities. Rin becomes more comfortable with her friends and realizes the joy of sharing experiences.

Accounts II
The Izu Peninsula TripLaid-Back Camp, Volume 6, Chapters 36-40; Anime Season 2, Episodes 5-8

Modern, high school

The group takes a trip to Izu Peninsula, where they camp near the coast and visit various attractions. Rin drives her moped while the others use a car. They encounter Minami Toba and her sister, and enjoy a hot spring. This trip strengthens their bonds and provides memorable experiences. The group enjoys a successful trip, and Rin gains confidence in group camping.

The Film: Rebuilding the CampsiteLaid-Back Camp Movie, 2022

Adult, post-college

As an adult, Rin works as a magazine editor in Nagoya. She is tasked with leading a project to redevelop an abandoned facility into a campsite in Yamanashi Prefecture. She reunites with her friends, who help with the project, and they successfully create a new camping destination. The group reconnects and creates something meaningful, and Rin finds fulfillment in her work.

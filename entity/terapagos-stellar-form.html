Who It Is

An ancient turtle-like Pokemon that crystallizes the energy of the Terastal phenomenon. In its Stellar Form, Terapagos becomes a massive, crystalline entity with a shell that resembles a planet surrounded by rings of pure Terastal energy. It is the source of the Terastal phenomenon that defines Paldea's unique Pokemon battles.

Who It Is

When the Terastal phenomenon reaches its absolute zenith, the ancient turtle Terapagos unveils its true form—a crystalline being whose shell becomes a crown of shimmering jewels, drawing the very light of the stars themselves. This is the Stellar Form, a state that transcends the ordinary types and harnesses the raw, elemental energy of Paldea itself.

Terapagos is an ancient and powerful Pokémon, described as having existed since the beginning of the world. It is said to be peaceful and wise, but it will defend itself if threatened. In the game, it is encountered as a boss battle, where it uses its Stellar Form to overwhelming effect. It is implied to be the source of the Terastal phenomenon, and it has a deep connection to Area Zero.

Powers
Stellar Type

Terapagos Stellar Form is the only Pokémon that can have the Stellar type, which is a special type that is not a traditional type but rather a Tera Type. When Terapagos Terastallizes into its Stellar Form, it gains a boost to all its moves, and it can change its Tera Type to any type, but only once per battle.

Limit: The Stellar type is not a permanent type; it is a Tera Type. Terapagos must Terastallize to access it. Also, the ability to change Tera Type is limited to once per battle.

Tera Shift

Terapagos has the ability Tera Shift, which automatically Terastallizes it into its Stellar Form when it enters battle.

Limit: This ability only works once per battle, and it requires Terapagos to be in its Normal Form. After Terastallizing, it remains in Stellar Form for the rest of the battle.

Tera Starstorm

A signature move that Terapagos can learn. It deals damage and is super effective against all Pokémon that have Terastallized. Its power is boosted by the Stellar type.

Limit: This move is only available to Terapagos in its Stellar Form. It uses the user's higher attacking stat.

High Base Stats

Terapagos Stellar Form has exceptionally high base stats, particularly in Special Attack and Special Defense, making it a formidable opponent.

Limit: Its base stats are fixed and cannot be modified except through EVs and IVs.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In its Stellar Form, Terapagos becomes a large, majestic turtle with a crystalline shell that resembles a crown or a cluster of jewels. Its body is predominantly white and blue, with glowing, star-like patterns. The shell is adorned with a large, central crystal and multiple smaller crystals that emit a radiant, multicolored light. Its eyes glow with a similar energy, and its overall appearance is ethereal and otherworldly.

Symbols — The Stellar Form is associated with the Terastal phenomenon, and its design incorporates star motifs, crystals, and a crown-like shell, symbolizing its role as a 'king' or 'origin' of the phenomenon. The Stellar type is represented by a special Tera Jewel that is rainbow-colored, and Terapagos's Stellar Form is the only Pokémon that can use the Stellar-type Terastallization.

Domains — Terapagos is found in Area Zero, a mysterious and dangerous region in Paldea. The Stellar Form is only seen during the final battle with Terapagos, after it absorbs the energy of the Tera Orbs. It is not found in the wild in this form; it must be transformed.

In the DLC, the player discovers that Terapagos is the source of the Terastal phenomenon. It is found in Area Zero, where it is being studied by the Blueberry Academy. The player must battle it to protect the region and ultimately catch it. After catching Terapagos, the player can use it in battle, and it becomes a key part of the game's lore.

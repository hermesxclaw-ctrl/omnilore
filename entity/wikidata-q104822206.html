Who It Is

In the shadowed world of Sanctuary, a name echoes through the ages—Diablo, the Prime Evil, the Lord of Terror. This is not merely a demon but a cosmic force of fear, a being whose very existence has shaped the fate of humanity and the eternal conflict between Heaven and Hell. From the depths of the Burning Hells to the hearts of mortals, Diablo's story is one of corruption, resurrection, and the relentless pursuit of ultimate power.

Diablo is cunning, manipulative, and utterly malevolent. He seeks to corrupt and destroy, using fear as his primary weapon. He is patient and strategic, often working through minions and possession to achieve his goals. He is also arrogant, believing himself to be the ultimate power in the universe.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Diablo' is the Spanish word for 'devil', derived from the Latin 'diabolus', which in turn comes from the Greek 'diabolos' meaning 'slanderer' or 'accuser'. In the context of the game, it is used as the name of the demonic antagonist, evoking the traditional Christian concept of the devil.

Pronunciation — dee-AH-bloh

Powers
Fear Manipulation

Diablo can manipulate and induce fear in others, weakening their will and making them susceptible to corruption.

Limit: Fear manipulation may be resisted by strong-willed individuals or those protected by divine or magical means.

Possession

Diablo can possess mortal beings, using them as hosts to act in the mortal realm. This is a key aspect of his plan to return to Sanctuary.

Limit: Possession requires a vulnerable or willing host, and the host's soul may fight back. In some cases, the host can be freed with great effort.

Fire Magic

Diablo can summon and control fire, unleashing devastating attacks such as fireballs, fire walls, and lightning.

Limit: Fire magic can be resisted by characters with fire resistance or immunity.

Superhuman Strength and Durability

As a Prime Evil, Diablo possesses immense physical strength and is nearly indestructible, requiring powerful magic or weapons to defeat.

Limit: He can be temporarily banished or imprisoned, but not permanently killed.

Immortality

Diablo cannot be truly killed; he can only be banished or imprisoned. His essence persists and can be resurrected.

Limit: His immortality is tied to his soulstone; if the soulstone is destroyed, his essence may be dispersed, but it can be re-formed.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Diablo is typically depicted as a large, horned demon with a muscular, humanoid body. He has red or dark skin, glowing eyes, and a menacing presence. In Diablo II, his appearance is more monstrous, with a more beast-like form. In Diablo III, he appears in a more humanoid but still demonic form, with a crown of horns and a cloak of flames. Symbols — Diablo's symbol is often a stylized demonic face with horns, which appears in the game's logo and throughout the series. He is associated with fire, terror, and darkness. His iconic imagery includes the black soulstone, which contains his essence, and the Prime Evil's aspect of terror.

Domains — Diablo's domain is the Burning Hells, a realm of fire and torment. He is also able to manifest in the mortal realm of Sanctuary, often by possessing a host. He has been imprisoned in the Black Soulstone and in a soulstone within a mortal's body, but he always seeks to break free.

The Original Sin and the Birth of DiabloDiablo III lore, Book of Cain

Before the creation of Sanctuary

Diablo is one of the Prime Evils, born from the conflict between Heaven and Hell. He and his brothers, Mephisto and Baal, were the first demons to rebel against the rules of the Burning Hells, seeking to dominate all of creation. They were defeated and banished to the Void, but they later returned and created the world of Sanctuary as a battleground. The creation of Sanctuary and the eternal conflict between Heaven and Hell.

The Darkening of TristramDiablo (video game)

The first game

Diablo, after being defeated by a hero in the past, was imprisoned in a soulstone beneath the cathedral in Tristram. However, the soulstone was flawed, and Diablo slowly corrupted the town's inhabitants, including King Leoric and the Archbishop Lazarus. The hero of the game descends into the labyrinth, defeats Diablo, and absorbs his soul into his own body to contain him. The hero becomes the new vessel for Diablo, setting the stage for Diablo II.

Accounts II
The Reawakening of the Prime EvilsDiablo II

Diablo II

Diablo, now in the body of the hero from the first game, seeks to free his brothers Mephisto and Baal from their soulstones. The player character tracks him across Sanctuary, eventually defeating him in Hell. In the end, the Worldstone is destroyed, and Diablo's essence is cast into the void. The destruction of the Worldstone and the weakening of the barrier between Heaven and Hell.

The Rise of the Prime EvilDiablo III

Diablo III

In Diablo III, Diablo is resurrected through the machinations of the Witch Adria and the demon Azmodan. He absorbs the souls of his brothers, becoming the Prime Evil, and attacks the High Heavens. The Nephalem (the player character) confronts and defeats him, shattering his soulstone. Diablo's essence is seemingly destroyed, but his influence lingers.

The Return of Lilith and Diablo's ShadowDiablo IV

Diablo IV

In Diablo IV, Diablo does not appear directly, but his presence is felt through the cults that worship him and the corruption he has left behind. The main antagonist is Lilith, the daughter of Mephisto, but Diablo's influence is a backdrop to the conflict. The events of Diablo IV set the stage for future conflicts, with Diablo's return hinted at.

Diablo is the ruler of all demons. — Diablo is one of the three Prime Evils, but he is not the absolute ruler. He and his brothers Mephisto and Baal rule over the Burning Hells, but there are other powerful demons, such as Azmodan and Belial, who challenge their authority.

Diablo is based on the biblical Satan. — While Diablo draws inspiration from the concept of the devil, he is a fictional character with his own unique lore, not a direct representation of the biblical Satan.

The Diablo series is a horror game series. — The Diablo series is primarily an action role-playing game, with horror elements. The focus is on combat and loot, not on psychological horror.

Who It Is

Hope Summers is the third generation of the Phoenix bloodline and one of the most important mutants in modern Marvel history. Born in the aftermath of the 'No More Mutants' Decimation event, Hope was the first mutant born after Scarlet Witch's devastating spell — making her the 'Mutant Messiah' whose very existence promised the restoration of mutantkind. Cable raised her in a distant future to protect her from Bishop, who believed she would cause a catastrophic war. Hope is a reality-warping omega-level mutant whose primary power is mimicry: she can copy and amplify any mutant ability she observes, and she possesses the unique ability to synchronize with and boost the powers of all nearby mutants simultaneously. When the Phoenix Force returned to Earth in the Avengers vs. X-Men event to bond with Hope, she was torn between the Avengers (who feared the Phoenix's destructive potential) and Cyclops's X-Men (who believed the Phoenix was the key to mutant salvation). Rather than fight, Hope made the unprecedented choice to voluntarily split the Phoenix Force with five X-Men — Cyclops, Emma Frost, Magik, Namor, and Colossus — creating the Phoenix Five. She later absorbed their Phoenix power back, achieving the full Phoenix state. At the climax, Hope used the Phoenix's power to amplify Scarlet Witch's reality-warping and utter the words 'No More Phoenixes' — but instead of destroying it, she commanded: 'Let there be mutants,' restoring mutantkind's ability to reproduce. Hope represents the Phoenix's capacity for selfless creation and healing. Unlike her predecessors who were consumed by the fire or destroyed by it, Hope bent the Phoenix to a conscious, redemptive purpose: the rebirth of an entire species.

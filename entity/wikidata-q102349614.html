Who It Is

In the tangled histories of Fire Emblem, few figures are as defined by the question of belonging as Corrin. Born of a royal family, raised by their enemies, and forced to choose between the two, Corrin's journey is a study in the painful construction of identity — a story that unfolds differently depending on which path the player takes.

Corrin is portrayed as kind, compassionate, and idealistic, but also naive and sheltered due to being raised in isolation in a fortress. They value bonds with others and seek to avoid unnecessary bloodshed. In the different game paths, their personality is influenced by the player's choices, but the core traits of empathy and a desire for peace remain consistent. They are also shown to be curious and somewhat impulsive, often acting on emotion rather than strategy.

Powers
Dragon Transformation

Corrin can transform into a dragon, gaining increased strength and resilience. This ability is tied to their dragon heritage and is used in battle as a special attack.

Limit: Transformation requires a certain amount of the Dragon Vein gauge, and it cannot be used indefinitely. In the story, Corrin's dragon form is initially unstable and can cause them to lose control.

Dragon Vein

Corrin can activate special mechanisms called Dragon Veins, which alter the battlefield environment (e.g., creating terrain, changing weather). This ability is used in both gameplay and story.

Limit: Dragon Veins can only be activated at specific points on the map and are limited in number per battle.

Sword Proficiency

Corrin is trained in swordsmanship, wielding the Yato, a legendary sword that is central to the plot.

Limit: Corrin's skill is average compared to other sword users in the game, but the Yato's power scales with the story progression.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Corrin has a slim, youthful build with white hair and red eyes, a trait shared with their Nohrian siblings, indicating their dragon heritage. They wear a white and black outfit with gold accents, featuring a short cape and a distinctive headpiece. In the game's artwork, they are often depicted with a sword (the Yato) and can transform into a dragon. The character's design varies slightly between the male and female versions, but the core elements

Symbols — The Yato, a divine sword that changes form based on Corrin's choices, is their primary symbol. The dragon motif is prominent, representing their heritage as a dragon-human hybrid. The Hoshido and Nohr royal crests are also associated with Corrin, reflecting their dual heritage.

Domains — Corrin's domain is the kingdom of Nohr, where they were raised, and the kingdom of Hoshido, their birthplace. They are also associated with the Bottomless Canyon, a rift that separates the two kingdoms, and the hidden kingdom of Valla, which is central to the game's third path.

Accounts I
BirthrightFire Emblem Fates: Birthright

2015

Corrin, raised in Nohr, is betrayed by their adoptive family and decides to side with their biological family in Hoshido. They lead the Hoshidan army against Nohr, eventually confronting their adoptive father, King Garon, and defeating him. The story ends with Corrin becoming a leader in Hoshido and bringing peace to the two kingdoms. Nohr is reformed under Xander's leadership, and the two kingdoms begin to reconcile. Corrin loses some of their Nohrian siblings in battle, leading to lasting grief.

Accounts II
ConquestFire Emblem Fates: Conquest

2015

Corrin remains loyal to Nohr and fights against Hoshido. They uncover King Garon's corruption and ultimately confront him, revealing that he is being controlled by an evil entity. Corrin defeats Garon and takes a stand against the true antagonist, Anankos. Nohr is saved from Garon's tyranny, but Hoshido suffers heavy losses. The game ends with Corrin vowing to rebuild the relationship between the two kingdoms.

RevelationFire Emblem Fates: Revelation

2015

Corrin chooses neither kingdom and instead seeks the truth behind the war, uncovering the hidden kingdom of Valla and the true villain, Anankos. They gather allies from both sides and defeat Anankos, preventing the destruction of the world and uniting Hoshido and Nohr. Both kingdoms are saved, and Corrin becomes a unifying figure. The story resolves the conflict in a more complete manner, with many characters surviving.

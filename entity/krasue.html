Who It Is
Form

The Krasue is a nocturnal female spirit that manifests as a floating, disembodied head of a young and beautiful woman, with her internal organs still attached and trailing down from the neck.

Powers

Supernatural Strength The Krasue is said to possess supernatural strength, allowing it to overpower and devour its victims with ease.

Accounts

The Krasue is said to have originated from a young woman who was wronged by her lover and subsequently died in a state of great anger and sorrow. Her spirit was said to have been consumed by a dark and malevolent force, transforming her into a Krasue.

A young woman was said to have been wronged by her husband and subsequently died in a state of great anger and sorrow. Her spirit was said to have been consumed by a dark and malevolent force, transforming her into a Krasue. The Krasue was said to have sought revenge against her husband and his family, bringing great terror and destruction to the village.

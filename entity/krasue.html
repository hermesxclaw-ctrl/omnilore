Who It Is

A vampiric female spirit that appears as a floating head with internal organs trailing beneath it, glowing with a ghostly light. By day, the krasue is a normal woman; by night, her head detaches and hunts for blood, offal, and carrion. It is a punitive curse for women who violated religious precepts or practiced dark magic.

Form

Floating female head with spine, heart, and intestines dangling below; glowing red or green phosphorescent light; by day indistinguishable from a normal woman

Powers

Head detachment

Flight

Blood and flesh consumption

Curse transmission

Invisibility to some

Behavior & Habitat

Hunts at night, targeting pregnant women, newborns, and livestock; must reconnect head to body before dawn or die permanently; can only be stopped by hiding or destroying the body

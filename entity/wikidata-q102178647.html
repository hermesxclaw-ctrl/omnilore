Who It Is

In the sword-maiden city of Orario, where gods descend to walk among mortals, one name echoes through the halls of legend: Ais Wallenstein, the Sword Princess, whose blade has carved a path through monsters and mysteries alike. But beneath her legendary skill lies a silent, burning quest—a promise to a mother she lost, a truth buried in the depths of the Dungeon, and a heart that slowly learns what it means to feel.

Ais is outwardly stoic, reserved, and socially awkward, often speaking in short sentences and appearing emotionless. However, she is deeply kind and caring, especially towards her friends and those in need. She has a strong sense of justice and duty, and is fiercely protective of those she cares about. She struggles with expressing her emotions and understanding others' feelings, but gradually learns to open up.

Powers
Superhuman physical abilities

As a Level 6 adventurer (the highest known), Ais possesses immense strength, speed, and agility, far beyond normal humans and most monsters. She can cut through stone and defeat powerful monsters with ease.

Limit: Her abilities are still within the limits of the DanMachi power system, and she can be overwhelmed by superior numbers or higher-level opponents.

Swordsmanship

Ais is a master swordswoman, specializing in a style that emphasizes speed and precision. She is known for her 'Lil Rafaga' technique, a rapid series of thrusts, and her ultimate 'Ariel' skill that imbues her with wind magic, increasing her speed and cutting power.

Limit: Ariel consumes Mind (mana) and cannot be used indefinitely. Her style is less effective against opponents with extreme defensive capabilities.

Magic: Ariel

Ais's unique magic/skill that allows her to manipulate wind, enhancing her speed and attacks. It manifests as a translucent aura of wind around her, and she can unleash powerful wind-based attacks.

Limit: Requires Mind to maintain and use; overuse can exhaust her.

Avenger (skill)

A skill that increases her power against certain types of monsters, particularly those related to dragons and the One-Eyed Black Dragon, due to her desire for revenge.

Limit: Only effective against specific targets; not a general power boost.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Ais is a young woman with long, golden blonde hair and striking golden eyes. She typically wears the white and blue uniform of the Loki Familia, with a skirt and armor pieces. She is often depicted with a calm, expressionless face, but her beauty is frequently noted by characters.

Symbols — Her signature weapon is a rapier-like sword, often seen with a white blade. She is associated with the image of a sword princess, and her title is a symbol of her strength and status.

Domains — Her primary domain is the Dungeon beneath Orario, where she spends most of her time as an adventurer. She also resides in the Loki Familia home. She is known to frequent the Pantheon (guild) and various shops in Orario.

Accounts I
The Sword Princess and the Little AdventurerDanMachi Volume 1, Episode 1 of anime

Main series timeline

Ais saves Bell Cranel from a Minotaur in the Dungeon, inspiring him to become an adventurer and to seek strength. She later becomes his goal and a source of motivation, though she is initially unaware of his feelings. Bell's determination to catch up to Ais drives the main plot. Ais begins to notice Bell's growth and becomes curious about him.

Ais's Past: The Tragedy of the SpiritDanMachi Volume 11, Sword Oratoria Volume 6

Backstory

Ais's mother, Aria, was a spirit who was captured and used by the One-Eyed Black Dragon. Ais's father, Albert Waldstein, was a hero who fought the dragon but died. Ais was born from their union and raised by the spirit of the wind, who later became her mentor. She carries a deep desire to avenge her mother and defeat the dragon. This backstory explains Ais's obsession with strength and her determination to become stronger. It also connects her to the main plot's overarching conflict with the Black Dragon.

Accounts II
The Training with BellDanMachi Volume 5, Sword Oratoria Volume 4

Main series

Ais agrees to train Bell, and during their sessions, she begins to feel a strange warmth and learns to enjoy spending time with him. This marks the beginning of her emotional development. Ais's feelings for Bell become more complex, and she starts to question her single-minded pursuit of strength.

The Minotaur BattleDanMachi Volume 14, Sword Oratoria Volume 7

Main series

Ais faces a powerful Minotaur that has been enhanced, and she struggles to defeat it, but ultimately triumphs. This battle tests her resolve and reminds her of her past. Ais reaffirms her commitment to her goals and gains new respect from her peers.

The Approach

In the sword-maiden city of Orario, where gods descend to walk among mortals, one name echoes through the halls of legend: Ais Wallenstein, the Sword Princess, whose blade has carved a path through monsters and mysteries alike. But beneath her legendary skill lies a silent, burning quest—a promise to a mother she lost, a truth buried in the depths of the Dungeon, and a heart that slowly learns what it means to feel.

Content advisory. Contains themes of loss, parental abandonment, and emotional repression. Some depictions of violence in combat.
Identity

Ais Wallenstein

Pronunciation EYE-ss WAL-en-stine (Japanese: アイズ・ヴァレンシュタイン, Aizu Varenshutain)

Known As

Sword PrincessAisAis-sanThe Sword Princess

Etymology

The name 'Ais' may derive from the Greek 'aisa' (destiny), but no official etymology is given. 'Wallenstein' is a German surname, possibly referencing the historical Albrecht von Wallenstein, but no official connection.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Fujino Omori, published by SB Creative under the GA Bunko imprint. Owned by Omori and SB Creative.

Disputed Identifications

None known; the character is unique to the DanMachi franchise.

Description

Appearance

Ais is a young woman with long, golden blonde hair and striking golden eyes. She typically wears the white and blue uniform of the Loki Familia, with a skirt and armor pieces. She is often depicted with a calm, expressionless face, but her beauty is frequently noted by characters.

Symbols & Iconography

Her signature weapon is a rapier-like sword, often seen with a white blade. She is associated with the image of a sword princess, and her title is a symbol of her strength and status.

Behaviour & Personality

Ais is outwardly stoic, reserved, and socially awkward, often speaking in short sentences and appearing emotionless. However, she is deeply kind and caring, especially towards her friends and those in need. She has a strong sense of justice and duty, and is fiercely protective of those she cares about. She struggles with expressing her emotions and understanding others' feelings, but gradually learns to open up.

Domains & Habitats

Her primary domain is the Dungeon beneath Orario, where she spends most of her time as an adventurer. She also resides in the Loki Familia home. She is known to frequent the Pantheon (guild) and various shops in Orario.

Version Differences

In the main series, Ais is seen from Bell's perspective, appearing as a distant, legendary figure. In Sword Oratoria, she is the protagonist, and her internal thoughts and struggles are revealed, showing a more vulnerable and complex character. The anime adapts both, but some scenes are altered or omitted. The manga is generally faithful to the light novels.

Powers & Abilities

Superhuman physical abilities

Source Version Light novels, anime

Description

As a Level 6 adventurer (the highest known), Ais possesses immense strength, speed, and agility, far beyond normal humans and most monsters. She can cut through stone and defeat powerful monsters with ease.

Limitations

Her abilities are still within the limits of the DanMachi power system, and she can be overwhelmed by superior numbers or higher-level opponents.

Contradictions

None.

Swordsmanship

Source Version Light novels, anime

Description

Ais is a master swordswoman, specializing in a style that emphasizes speed and precision. She is known for her 'Lil Rafaga' technique, a rapid series of thrusts, and her ultimate 'Ariel' skill that imbues her with wind magic, increasing her speed and cutting power.

Limitations

Ariel consumes Mind (mana) and cannot be used indefinitely. Her style is less effective against opponents with extreme defensive capabilities.

Contradictions

Some sources describe Ariel as a skill, others as a magic spell; in the light novels it is a skill that grants wind magic effects.

Magic: Ariel

Source Version Light novels, anime

Description

Ais's unique magic/skill that allows her to manipulate wind, enhancing her speed and attacks. It manifests as a translucent aura of wind around her, and she can unleash powerful wind-based attacks.

Limitations

Requires Mind to maintain and use; overuse can exhaust her.

Contradictions

The exact nature (skill vs. magic) is clarified in the light novels as a skill, but some adaptations may blur the line.

Avenger (skill)

Source Version Light novels

Description

A skill that increases her power against certain types of monsters, particularly those related to dragons and the One-Eyed Black Dragon, due to her desire for revenge.

Limitations

Only effective against specific targets; not a general power boost.

Contradictions

None.

Summary

Ais saves Bell Cranel from a Minotaur in the Dungeon, inspiring him to become an adventurer and to seek strength. She later becomes his goal and a source of motivation, though she is initially unaware of his feelings.

Ais's Past: The Tragedy of the Spirit

Source DanMachi Volume 11, Sword Oratoria Volume 6Culture Japanese light novelPeriod Backstory

Summary

Ais's mother, Aria, was a spirit who was captured and used by the One-Eyed Black Dragon. Ais's father, Albert Waldstein, was a hero who fought the dragon but died. Ais was born from their union and raised by the spirit of the wind, who later became her mentor. She carries a deep desire to avenge her mother and defeat the dragon.

Contradictions & Disputes

Ais's feelings for Bell

Position A

Ais sees Bell as a student and potential friend, but not romantically.

Position A — evidence

Early volumes and some fan interpretations.

Position B

Ais is developing romantic feelings for Bell, as hinted in later volumes.

Position B — evidence

Light novels from Volume 12 onwards show Ais blushing and thinking about Bell romantically.

Scholarly Consensus

The author has stated that Ais will eventually reciprocate Bell's feelings, so position B is likely correct.

Nature of Ariel

Position A

Ariel is a magic spell.

Position A — evidence

Anime and some game adaptations may depict it as magic.

Position B

Ariel is a skill that grants wind magic.

Position B — evidence

Light novels explicitly describe it as a skill.

Scholarly Consensus

The light novels are the primary source, so it is a skill.

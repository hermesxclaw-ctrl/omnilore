Faceless, black-skinned flying creatures from the Dreamlands with smooth, featureless faces, slender bodies, inward-curving horns, and barbed tails. They serve Nodens and are silent — they never make a sound — but use their barbed tails to tickle victims into submission before carrying them off.

Who It Is

Faceless, black-skinned flying creatures from the Dreamlands with smooth, featureless faces, slender bodies, inward-curving horns, and barbed tails. They serve Nodens and are silent — they never make a sound — but use their barbed tails to tickle victims into submission before carrying them off.

Form

Humanoid, slender and gaunt, completely black, smooth featureless face (no eyes, nose, or mouth), inward-curving horns on head, bat-like wings, barbed prehensile tail

Powers

Silent flight

Tickling attack with barbed tail

Carrying humans through air

Complete silence

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

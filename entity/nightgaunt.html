Who It Is
Form

The Nightgaunt is described as a flying creature with smooth, whale-like skin and long, slender limbs. It has no face, and its body is said to be covered in a thin, membrane-like skin.

Powers

Teleportation The Nightgaunt is said to have the ability to teleport itself from one location to another, allowing it to move quickly and strike its enemies from unexpected angles.

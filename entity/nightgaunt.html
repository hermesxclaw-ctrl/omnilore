Who It Is

The Nightgaunt is a creature that is said to bring darkness and despair to those who encounter it. It is a harbinger of doom, and its presence is often associated with madness and destruction.

The Nightgaunt is a creature of the night, said to be most active during the hours of darkness. It is a solitary creature, preferring to hunt alone rather than in packs.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Nightgaunt is described as a flying creature with smooth, whale-like skin and long, slender limbs. It has no face, and its body is said to be covered in a thin, membrane-like skin.

Symbols — The Nightgaunt is often associated with the Dreamlands, a realm of the imagination that exists parallel to the waking world. It is said to be a harbinger of doom, a creature that brings darkness and despair to those who encounter it.

Domains — The Nightgaunt is said to inhabit the Dreamlands, a realm of the imagination that exists parallel to the waking world. It is also said to be able to traverse the boundaries between the Dreamlands and the waking world.

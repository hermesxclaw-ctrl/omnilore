Who It Is

A hostile, semi-material race of aliens that predated the Yithians on Earth. They are only partially physical — visible yet strangely intangible, capable of controlling wind currents for locomotion. They emit a hideous piping whistle and are fiercely aggressive. The Yithians drove them underground, where they wait.

Form

Amorphous, shifting forms; partially invisible/transparent; multiple eyes and mouths appearing and disappearing; dark rubbery consistency; move by wind manipulation

Powers

Partial intangibility

Wind control for flight

Hideous whistle attack

Regenerative resilience

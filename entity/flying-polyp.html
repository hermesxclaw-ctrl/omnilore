Who It Is
Form

Flying Polyps are often depicted as a sort of semi-fungal mass of flesh, with a polyp-like appearance. They are said to be capable of flight, and are often associated with other Cthulhu Mythos creatures.

Powers

Flight The Flying Polyps are capable of flight, allowing them to move through the air with ease.

Regeneration The Flying Polyps are said to be capable of regenerating their bodies, making them highly resistant to damage.

The story follows the narrator as he discovers a mysterious, otherworldly being known as the Flying Polyp. As he delves deeper into the mystery, he discovers that the Flying Polyp is a creature from another dimension, and that it is connected to the Elder Things.

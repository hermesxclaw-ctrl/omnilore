Who It Is

A pool of red liquid in northern Canada that periodically spawns hostile entities. The creatures are unique each time and all immediately hostile. The pool appears to be a 'leak' from another dimension where an endless war is being fought. Foundation defenses have repelled over 200 entity incursions. The most recent breach produced an entity that spoke — in perfect English — before attacking: 'You are in the way of the war.'

Form

A pool of opaque red liquid, ~50m in diameter, that ripples even when there is no wind. The liquid is warm (~38°C) and has the consistency of blood but is chemically distinct. Entities emerge from the center in flashes of crimson light.

Who It Is
Form

SCP-354 is a pool of red liquid located in northern Canada. The liquid is of a consistency similar to that of human blood but is not of a biological nature. The pool is surrounded by a secure perimeter, known as Area-354, which is designed to contain the anomalous entities that emerge from the pool.

Powers

Entity Emission SCP-354 has the ability to emit extremely hostile entities, which are capable of attacking personnel.

Incident Log

A team of researchers attempted to collect a sample from the pool, but were attacked by the entities that emerged. The team was forced to retreat and the pool was secured.

A team of researchers attempted to study the pool, but were unable to gather any meaningful data due to the hostile entities.

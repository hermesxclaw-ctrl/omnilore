Etymology

The name 'Lyce' likely derives from the Greek 'λύκη' (lykē), meaning 'wolf', or from 'λύκος' (lykos), 'wolf'. It may also be related to the region Lycia or the epithet 'Lyceus' associated with Apollo. Alternatively, it could be a feminine form of 'Lykos', a common Greek name.

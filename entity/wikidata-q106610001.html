The Approach

In the city of Yurg, where the streets are as unforgiving as the criminals who prowl them, one figure stands out not for his size or his sword, but for the unwavering loyalty of a small, white-furred canine companion. Repede, the pipe-smoking dog of the guild known as the Brave Vesperia, is more than a mascot; he is a full-fledged member of the party, a silent sentinel whose actions speak louder than words.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of violence and conflict typical of action-adventure games. No graphic or sexual content.
Identity

Repede

Pronunciation REH-peh-day

Etymology

The name 'Repede' is likely derived from the English word 'rapid' or 'speedy', reflecting the character's swift movements in combat. It may also be a play on the Romanian word 'repede' meaning 'quick'.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character owned by Bandai Namco Entertainment, created for the Japanese role-playing game 'Tales of Vesperia'.

Disputed Identifications

None known. Repede is a unique character with no real-world counterpart.

Description

Appearance

Repede is a medium-sized dog with predominantly white fur, accented by brown patches around his eyes and on his ears. He wears a dark blue or black collar with a small pouch. Uniquely, he carries a pipe in his mouth, which he is often seen smoking, and sometimes wears goggles on his head. In combat, he wields a dagger in his mouth.

Symbols & Iconography

The pipe and goggles are his iconic accessories, symbolizing his role as a serious, battle-ready companion rather than a mere pet. The dagger represents his combat capability.

Behaviour & Personality

Repede is fiercely loyal to his master, Yuri Lowell, and the rest of the party. He is calm and collected, rarely barking or making noise. He communicates through body language and actions. He is protective of his friends and shows a gentle side, especially towards children. Despite being a dog, he is treated as an equal by the party members.

Domains & Habitats

Repede primarily resides in the city of Yurg, the starting location of the game, but travels extensively with the party across the world of Terca Lumireis.

Version Differences

In the original Japanese version, Repede's design and role are identical to the English localization. There are no significant version differences, though some dialogue may differ slightly due to localization choices.

Summary

Repede was originally the pet of a young boy named Yuri, who died in an accident. After the boy's death, Repede attached himself to Yuri Lowell, who had been the boy's friend. Repede has been by Yuri's side ever since, assisting him in his duties as a guild member and later in his quest to save the world.

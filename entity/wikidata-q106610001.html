Who It Is

In the city of Yurg, where the streets are as unforgiving as the criminals who prowl them, one figure stands out not for his size or his sword, but for the unwavering loyalty of a small, white-furred canine companion. Repede, the pipe-smoking dog of the guild known as the Brave Vesperia, is more than a mascot; he is a full-fledged member of the party, a silent sentinel whose actions speak louder than words.

Repede is fiercely loyal to his master, Yuri Lowell, and the rest of the party. He is calm and collected, rarely barking or making noise. He communicates through body language and actions. He is protective of his friends and shows a gentle side, especially towards children. Despite being a dog, he is treated as an equal by the party members.

Etymology — The name 'Repede' is likely derived from the English word 'rapid' or 'speedy', reflecting the character's swift movements in combat. It may also be a play on the Romanian word 'repede' meaning 'quick'.

Pronunciation — REH-peh-day

Aliases — Repede

Combat Proficiency

Repede is a skilled fighter, using a dagger held in his mouth. He is agile and fast, able to perform combo attacks and special techniques.

Limit: As a dog, he cannot use magic or complex tools, but his physical abilities compensate.

Enhanced Senses

Repede has heightened senses of smell and hearing, which are used in story events to track characters or detect danger.

Limit: Not explicitly quantified in game mechanics.

Loyalty

Repede's unwavering loyalty is his defining trait, often motivating party members and influencing story outcomes.

Limit: Not a supernatural power, but a behavioral trait.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Repede is a medium-sized dog with predominantly white fur, accented by brown patches around his eyes and on his ears. He wears a dark blue or black collar with a small pouch. Uniquely, he carries a pipe in his mouth, which he is often seen smoking, and sometimes wears goggles on his head. In combat, he wields a dagger in his mouth.

Symbols — The pipe and goggles are his iconic accessories, symbolizing his role as a serious, battle-ready companion rather than a mere pet. The dagger represents his combat capability.

Domains — Repede primarily resides in the city of Yurg, the starting location of the game, but travels extensively with the party across the world of Terca Lumireis.

Accounts I
The Loyal CompanionTales of Vesperia main story

2008

Repede was originally the pet of a young boy named Yuri, who died in an accident. After the boy's death, Repede attached himself to Yuri Lowell, who had been the boy's friend. Repede has been by Yuri's side ever since, assisting him in his duties as a guild member and later in his quest to save the world. Repede's loyalty to Yuri strengthens the bond between Yuri and the party, and his actions often aid the group in critical moments.

Throughout the game, Repede often acts as a guardian, protecting weaker characters or accompanying them when they are in danger. His silent presence is a source of comfort and strength. Repede's actions reinforce his role as a dependable ally and contribute to the party's success.

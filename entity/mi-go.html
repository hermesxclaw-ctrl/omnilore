Who It Is
Form

The Mi-Go are described as fungus-based lifeforms, with a varied appearance due to their prodigious surgical, biological, chemical, and mechanical skill. They can take on many forms, including winged human-sized crabs, and can disguise themselves as large, hairy hominids.

Powers

Surgical Skill The Mi-Go possess advanced surgical skills, allowing them to perform complex operations and manipulate their own bodies with ease.

Biological Adaptation The Mi-Go can adapt to any environment, allowing them to survive in even the most hostile conditions.

Chemical Manipulation The Mi-Go can manipulate chemicals and toxins, using them to their advantage in combat and other situations.

Mechanical Skill The Mi-Go possess advanced mechanical skills, allowing them to construct and repair complex devices and machinery.

Fungal Growth The Mi-Go can control and manipulate fungal growth, using it to their advantage in combat and other situations.

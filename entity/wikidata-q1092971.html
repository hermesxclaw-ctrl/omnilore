Who It Is

In a class of 31 girls where one can summon demon swords, another reads minds through books, and another is a 600-year-old vampire in a middle-school uniform, Madoka Kugimiya insists on being ordinary — and that is her narrative function. As one-third of Mahora Academy Class 3-A's relentless cheer squad, Madoka is the girl with the glossy black twintails and yellow ribbons who cheers first, asks questions later, and reminds you that not every student at a secret magic academy wants to be a hero. She is background until she isn't, and that ordinariness is precisely why Ken Akamatsu keeps her in frame.

Attested: Madoka is presented as energetic, loyal, and pragmatically normal. She is the most level-headed of the 'Cheerleaders' (Misa, Madoka, Sakurako), often reacting with exasperation to Sakurako's airheaded antics and Misa's gossip, yet fiercely protective of both. Dialogue is informal, supportive, and comedy-oriented; she participates in class gossip, festival preparations, and group cheering for Negi and classmates. She shows no particular academic, magical, or martial ambition in early arcs — self-identifies as a 'normal girl' — but demonstrates courage

Powers
Cheerleading and Athletic Performance (Non-magical)

Attested mundane skill: trained cheerleader capable of coordinated stunts, pyramids, pom-pom routines, and acrobatic cheers. Shown leading crowd chants at Mahora Festival sports events and supporting the class during crises. No superhuman feats; proficiency is at competitive middle-school club level.

Limit: Human athletic limits; no magical enhancement without artifact; cannot fly, cast spells, or enhance others magically through cheering alone

Provisional Contract (Pactio) — Adeptus Support

Attested that Madoka forms a provisional contract (pactio) with Negi Springfield in the late manga during the mass contracting phase before the journey to the Magical World (circa Volume 32-34). Like all pactio partners, this creates a telepathic link, a card, and the potential to summon an Artifact. The existence of her card is confirmed in the complete card set and in manga panels showing the class's cards arrayed.

Limit: Pactio is provisional (probationary), not a full Ministra Magi contract; requires Negi's magic to activate; artifact summoning is optional and mana-dependent

Artifact: Pom-Pom / Support-Type Artifact (Poorly Documented)

Reconstructed/Disputed: Visual evidence suggests Madoka's Artifact takes the form of enhanced cheerleading pom-poms or a supportive tool. Unlike front-line fighters (Asuna's sword, Setsuna's wakizashi, Nodoka's diary), her artifact is shown only in card art and in a single small panel where artifacts are summoned en masse; no named incantation or demonstrated power is given dialogue or caption. Fan databases have proposed names like 'Flabellum...' but no canonical name is printed legibly in the manga. Function is inferred as support/buff or communication, consistent with other non-combat artifacts, but not attested in combat.

Limit: Unknown — never used in a documented battle; presumably requires magical energy and has no offensive capability comparable to combat artifacts; may be purely symbolic

Ki and Magic Potential (Latent, Untrained)

As a Mahora student exposed to high ambient magic and later to Negi's pactio system, Madoka possesses latent magical potential like all humans in Negima's cosmology, but shows no formal training in Western magic, Eastern ki, or pactio combat. She benefits from Mahora's barrier and from being in a class with many adepts, but is not a mage.

Limit: No spells, no ki blasts, no artifact mastery demonstrated; would require training to manifest

Accounts I
Class Introduction and Cheer Trio EstablishmentNegima! Magister Negi Magi Volume 1-2, Chapters 1-14

2003 (serialization), in-story: Spring, Negi's arrival as homeroom teacher of Class 2-A

Madoka is introduced as part of the 31-girl roster of Mahora Academy Middle School Class 2-A. She is immediately grouped with Misa Kakizaki and Sakurako Shiina as the three-member Cheerleading Club, who serve as class mood-makers. Early chapters show the trio gossiping about Negi's youth and cuteness, participating in classroom chaos (e.g., the 'Negi is a pervert' misunderstandings), and performing cheers at school assemblies. Madoka's role is to react deadpan to Sakurako's nonsense and to keep the trio grounded. No magical plot yet; establishes her as a 'normal' anchor. Establishes Madoka's social niche and visual design; sets up long-running gag that the cheerleaders are always together; provides baseline for later contrast when magic is revealed to the class

Kyoto Field Trip — Demon Attack and Class SolidarityNegima! Volume 4-6, Chapters 30-54 (Kyoto Arc)

2003-2004 serialization; in-story: Golden Week field trip to Kyoto/Kansai Magic Association territory

Class 3-A (then 2-A) travels to Kyoto. When demons and Kansai mages attack, most students are unaware of magic, but the cheer trio is among those who witness Negi, Asuna, and allies fighting. Madoka helps with evacuation and cheering, showing courage despite lacking powers. The arc reveals the existence of the Magic World to a subset of students; Madoka is not among the first to learn the full truth but is present for the aftermath where the class begins to suspect Negi's secret. In anime, the attack is simplified and Madoka's reaction is played for comedy. Deepens class bond; foreshadows later mass revelation of magic; Madoka's non-magical helpfulness is highlighted as valuable

Mahora Festival — Cheerleading, Contests, and ChaosNegima! Volume 11-18, Chapters 99-168 (Mahorafest Arc)

2005-2006 serialization; in-story: Three-day Mahora Academy Festival

The longest arc, where Class 3-A runs multiple festival events. Madoka and her cheer squad perform at the opening ceremony, sports competitions, and the martial arts tournament intermissions. During the festival's magical disturbances (Chao Lingshen's time-travel plot, robot invasions, and martial arts tournament), Madoka is part of the crowd that is repeatedly endangered and rescued. She participates in the class's hospitality events (haunted house, cafe) and is shown cheering for Negi and Kotaro during the tournament. The arc emphasizes her role as morale support; she is not a fighter but keeps spirits up while Chao's plan threatens the world. Largest showcase for Madoka's cheerleading identity; class unity theme peaks; Chao's defeat leads to memory erasure attempt that ultimately fails, leaving Madoka among those who retain knowledge of magic

Accounts II
Revelation of Magic and Mass Pactio FormationNegima! Volume 18-24, Chapters 169-220; specifically Volume 33-34 mass pactio chapters

2006-2010 serialization; in-story: Post-festival, Negi reveals magic to the class

After Chao's arc, Negi chooses to reveal his magic to the entire class. Madoka is among the students who accept the revelation and volunteer to help him. In preparation for the journey to the Magical World to rescue Negi's father, Negi forms provisional contracts with nearly the entire class, including Madoka. The sequence shows all 31 cards, with Madoka's card briefly visible. While front-line students receive training and artifact explanations, Madoka's artifact is not elaborated — she is present in group training and strategy meetings but remains in the support cohort. This marks her transition from unaware classmate to acknowledged ally in the magical conflict. Formally integrates Madoka into the magical alliance; grants her pactio card and theoretical artifact; sets up her inclusion in the Magical World expedition's support group

Magical World (Mundus Magicus) — The Support Group's JourneyNegima! Volume 24-34, Chapters 221-320 (Journey to the Magic World Arc)

2008-2011 serialization; in-story: Travel through the gate to the Magical World (Old World, Hellas region)

Negi and a large contingent of Class 3-A travel to the Magical World. Madoka is part of the group that goes (as opposed to the small group that stays at Mahora). She travels with Misa and Sakurako, providing cheer and logistical help in the desert city of Ariadne and later in Ostia. She does not participate in the major battles against the Imperial Army or Fate Averruncus's forces, but is present for the group's capture, escape, and the battle of Ostia. Her most visible moment is during the Ostia gladiatorial tournament and subsequent war, where the cheer trio cheers for Negi and allies from the stands, and later helps with civilian evacuation and healing support. The arc treats the cheerleaders as the 'normal girls' who witness war firsthand, underscoring the cost of conflict. Exposes Madoka to the devastation of the Magical World and the political stakes of Negi's quest; reinforces theme that even non-combatants are affected by war; leads to her return to Mahora after the arc's climax

Final Arc — The Mage of the Beginning and GraduationNegima! Volume 35-38, Chapters 321-355 (Final Battle and Graduation Arc)

2011-2012 serialization; in-story: Return to Mahora, battle against the Mage of the Beginning (Life-Maker), and Class 3-A graduation

After the Magical World war, the class returns to Mahora for the final confrontation with the Mage of the Beginning, who possesses Negi's father and threatens both worlds. Madoka is part of the class-wide effort to support Negi — not as a frontline fighter against the Mage, but as part of the collective that powers the final spell through their bonds (the 'Thousand Bonds' concept embodied by Negi's artifact Mile Vincula). In the epilogue, she appears in the graduation ceremony (Chapter 355), where each student's future is hinted. Like most non-central girls, her future is shown in a montage panel: continuing school life, cheerleading, and remaining friends with Misa and Sakurako. No individual marriage or career epilogue is given to her, unlike major heroines. Concludes Madoka's arc as a loyal supporting friend; class graduates; Negima story ends with the implication that the bonds formed (including with 'ordinary' students like Madoka) were crucial to saving both worlds

Madoka Kugimiya is the same character as Madoka Kaname from Puella Magi Madoka Magica. — Entirely separate characters, franchises, creators, and designs. Kugimiya (Negima, 2003) is a cheerleader with black twintails and yellow ribbons; Kaname (Magica Quartet/Shaft, 2011) is a pink-themed magical girl with short pink hair. Confusion arises solely from shared given name 'Madoka' and Shaft studio's involvement in both Negima!? and Madoka Magica.

Every girl in Negima Class 3-A is a powerful mage or fighter, so Madoka must have hidden magic powers. — Akamatsu deliberately created a spectrum: ~10 frontline fighters/mages (Asuna, Setsuna, Konoka, etc.), ~10 with specialized support artifacts, and ~10 who remain largely 'normal' to ground the class. Madoka is in the normal/support cohort; her value is loyalty and morale, not hidden power. Her pactio, if activated, is support-type and never shown as combat magic.

Madoka's artifact is definitively 'Cheer Pom-Poms that buff allies' stats' with a Latin name. — No canonical Latin name or buff effect is printed in the manga or guidebooks. Card art shows pom-poms, but function is never described in dialogue or narration. Names circulating online (e.g., 'Flabellum Exsuscitans') are fan inventions not found in primary sources.

Who It Is

Eshu is a complex and multifaceted character who continues to fascinate and inspire people around the world.

Eshu is known for his cunning and his love of mischief. He is a trickster who delights in playing pranks on both mortals and immortals.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Eshu is often depicted as a mischievous figure with a sly grin, carrying a staff or a bag of tricks. He is said to be able to take on many forms, from a handsome young man to a grotesque old hag.

Symbols — Eshu is associated with the symbol of the crossroads, which represents his role as a messenger and a trickster. He is also linked to the colors red and black, which symbolize his connection to the earth and the underworld.

Domains — Eshu is said to inhabit the crossroads, where he serves as a messenger and a guardian of the threshold.

Accounts I
The Story of Eshu's CreationYoruba mythology

Pre-colonial

Eshu was created by the gods to serve as a messenger and a trickster. He was given the power to manipulate reality and to play tricks on both mortals and immortals. Eshu's creation led to the creation of the crossroads, where he serves as a messenger and a guardian of the threshold.

Accounts II
The Story of Eshu's MischiefYoruba folklore

Colonial

Eshu was known for his mischief and his love of playing pranks on both mortals and immortals. He was said to be able to take on many forms, from a handsome young man to a grotesque old hag. Eshu's mischief led to chaos and disorder, but also to creativity and innovation.

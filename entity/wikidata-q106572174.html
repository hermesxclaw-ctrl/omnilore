Who It Is

A brilliant scientist whose inventions fueled both heroism and villainy, Baxter Stockman is the tragic technologist of the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles universe—a man who lost everything, including his humanity, in a relentless pursuit of recognition and revenge.

Initially portrayed as a brilliant but naive scientist, easily manipulated by the Shredder. In later iterations, he becomes increasingly bitter, vengeful, and desperate for approval, leading to his downfall.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Baxter' is of Old English origin meaning 'baker', while 'Stockman' is a surname meaning 'one who works with stocks' or 'from a tree stump'. The name was chosen by co-creator Kevin Eastman, possibly as an homage to a childhood friend.

Pronunciation — BAK-stər STOK-mən

Aliases — Dr. Baxter Stockman, Doctor Stockman, The Fly, Stockman, Baxter

Ownership — Owned by Paramount Global and Nickelodeon; created by Kevin Eastman and Peter Laird.

Powers
Genius-level intellect

Baxter is a brilliant scientist and inventor, capable of creating advanced robotics, genetic engineering, and various technological devices.

Limit: His intellect is often undermined by his naivety and arrogance.

Mousers

Small, remote-controlled robots designed for waste disposal, later weaponized by the Shredder. They are capable of eating through almost anything.

Limit: They can be destroyed by water or strong impacts.

Flight and wall-crawling (as a fly)

As a fly mutant, Baxter gains the ability to fly and crawl on walls, as well as enhanced senses.

Limit: He may be vulnerable to fly swatters and insecticide.

Regeneration (in 2003 series)

In the 2003 series, Baxter is able to regenerate lost body parts after being mutilated by the Shredder, though this is never fully explained.

Limit: It is not shown to be instantaneous and may cause pain.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In his original human form, Baxter is a slender African-American man with short hair and often wears a lab coat. After his mutation, he becomes a humanoid fly with compound eyes, wings, and a proboscis. In the 2012 series, he retains a more humanoid appearance with fly-like features and a mechanical exoskeleton.

Symbols — Often associated with his Mousers—small, robotic, insect-like devices—and later with fly imagery. His lab coat and glasses are recurring motifs.

Domains — Typically operates from his laboratory or the TCRI building. In the 2003 series, he lives in a sewer after his mutation, and in the 2012 series, he works for the Kraang.

Accounts I
Mirage Comics OriginTeenage Mutant Ninja Turtles #2

1985

Baxter Stockman is a black inventor who creates the Mousers to help with waste disposal. He is approached by the Shredder, who forces him to use the Mousers for crime. The Turtles defeat the Mousers, and Baxter is mutated into a fly by the Shredder as punishment. Baxter becomes a recurring antagonist, seeking revenge against both the Turtles and the Shredder.

1987 Animated SeriesTeenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (1987)

1987-1996

Baxter is a white scientist who creates the Mousers for the Shredder. He is often portrayed as a bumbling, cowardly villain who is frequently punished by the Shredder. He is eventually mutated into a fly in the episode 'The Return of the Shredder'. He becomes a recurring villain, often working with the Shredder but also sometimes acting on his own.

Accounts II
2003 Animated SeriesTeenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (2003)

2003-2009

Baxter is a black scientist who creates the Mousers for the Shredder. He is brutally punished by the Shredder for his failures, including having body parts removed. He eventually becomes a full fly mutant and seeks revenge against both the Turtles and the Shredder. His story arc is one of tragedy and horror, as he loses his humanity and becomes a monster.

2012 Animated SeriesTeenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (2012)

2012-2017

Baxter is a young black scientist who is manipulated by the Kraang into helping them. He creates the Mousers and later becomes a fly mutant. He is a more sympathetic character, often torn between his desire for success and his moral compass. He eventually becomes a reluctant ally of the Turtles, though he remains dangerous.

2016 FilmTeenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Out of the Shadows

2016

Baxter is a white scientist who is forced to work with the Shredder to create a device that can mutate humans. He is accidentally mutated into a fly during the film's climax. He is defeated by the Turtles and presumably captured.

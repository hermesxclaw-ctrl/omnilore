Who It Is

A Pokemon born from 1000 Gimmighoul coins fusing together into a single, golden-bodied form. Gholdengo appears as a cheerful, surfing humanoid figure made of solid gold, with coins floating in orbit around it. Its body is incredibly valuable but extremely durable — no known substance can scratch its golden surface.

The Approach

In a world where ghosts and treasure often share the same haunted tales, Gholdengo stands as a singular anomaly: a creature born from the fusion of a thousand coins and a wandering spirit, whose golden body gleams with the promise of fortune and the weight of a spectral past. Its existence blurs the line between the material and the ethereal, challenging trainers to reconsider what it means to be a Pokémon.

Identity

Gholdengo

Pronunciation Gol-DEN-go

Etymology

Gholdengo is a combination of 'gold' and 'dough' (slang for money) or 'golden' and 'dough', reflecting its golden body and coin-based origin.

Cultural Ownership

The Pokémon franchise, created by Satoshi Tajiri and developed by Game Freak, owned by Nintendo, Creatures Inc., and The Pokémon Company.

Disputed Identifications

None known; Gholdengo is a unique Pokémon species introduced in Generation IX.

Description

Appearance

Gholdengo is a humanoid Pokémon with a golden, metallic body. Its head is shaped like a coin with a hexagonal pattern, and its body is covered in raised, coin-like bumps. It has long, flowing arms and legs, and its face features simple, dark eyes. Its overall design is sleek and shiny, resembling a golden idol or a living treasure.

Symbols & Iconography

The coin motif is central to Gholdengo's design, referencing its origin from Gimmighoul and its connection to treasure. Its golden color and hexagonal head pattern evoke images of currency and wealth.

Behaviour & Personality

Gholdengo is described as being playful and mischievous, often toying with its opponents by firing coins at them. It is said to be protective of its treasure and will only evolve from Gimmighoul when it has collected enough coins. In battle, it is agile and uses its speed to outmaneuver foes.

Domains & Habitats

Gholdengo is found in the Paldea region, typically in areas where Gimmighoul Roaming Form is found, such as ruins and watchtowers. It is not known to live in the wild as a fully evolved form; it evolves from Gimmighoul when trained by a Pokémon Trainer.

Version Differences

No differences between Pokémon Scarlet and Violet versions are known for Gholdengo.

Evolution from Gimmighoul

Source Pokémon Scarlet and VioletCulture Pokémon franchisePeriod 2022-present

Summary

Gholdengo is the evolved form of Gimmighoul, a small ghost-like Pokémon that collects coins. When a Trainer collects 999 Gimmighoul Coins and levels up a Gimmighoul, it evolves into Gholdengo. This evolution is unique in that it requires a specific item count rather than a stone or trade.

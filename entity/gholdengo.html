Who It Is

In a world where ghosts and treasure often share the same haunted tales, Gholdengo stands as a singular anomaly: a creature born from the fusion of a thousand coins and a wandering spirit, whose golden body gleams with the promise of fortune and the weight of a spectral past. Its existence blurs the line between the material and the ethereal, challenging trainers to reconsider what it means to be a Pokémon.

Gholdengo is described as being playful and mischievous, often toying with its opponents by firing coins at them. It is said to be protective of its treasure and will only evolve from Gimmighoul when it has collected enough coins. In battle, it is agile and uses its speed to outmaneuver foes.

Powers
Good as Gold

Gholdengo's signature Ability, which makes it immune to status moves (non-damaging moves).

Limit: Only blocks status moves; damage-dealing moves still affect it.

Make It Rain

A signature move that deals damage and lowers the user's Special Attack by one stage. It also scatters coins that can be picked up after battle.

Limit: Low PP (10), and the Special Attack drop can be detrimental in prolonged battles.

Steel/Ghost typing

Gholdengo has a unique Steel/Ghost typing, giving it immunities to Normal and Fighting moves, and resistances to many types, including Psychic, Dragon, and Fairy.

Limit: Weak to Fire, Ground, Ghost, and Dark moves.

High Speed and Special Attack

Gholdengo has base stats of 60 HP, 50 Attack, 80 Defense, 110 Special Attack, 90 Special Defense, and 100 Speed, making it a fast special attacker.

Limit: Frail defensively, especially against physical attackers.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Gholdengo is a humanoid Pokémon with a golden, metallic body. Its head is shaped like a coin with a hexagonal pattern, and its body is covered in raised, coin-like bumps. It has long, flowing arms and legs, and its face features simple, dark eyes. Its overall design is sleek and shiny, resembling a golden idol or a living treasure.

Symbols — The coin motif is central to Gholdengo's design, referencing its origin from Gimmighoul and its connection to treasure. Its golden color and hexagonal head pattern evoke images of currency and wealth.

Domains — Gholdengo is found in the Paldea region, typically in areas where Gimmighoul Roaming Form is found, such as ruins and watchtowers. It is not known to live in the wild as a fully evolved form; it evolves from Gimmighoul when trained by a Pokémon Trainer.

Accounts I
Evolution from GimmighoulPokémon Scarlet and Violet

2022-present

Gholdengo is the evolved form of Gimmighoul, a small ghost-like Pokémon that collects coins. When a Trainer collects 999 Gimmighoul Coins and levels up a Gimmighoul, it evolves into Gholdengo. This evolution is unique in that it requires a specific item count rather than a stone or trade. Gholdengo becomes a powerful ally, capable of using its coin-based abilities to battle and bring fortune.

Gholdengo is referred to as the Coin Entity Pokémon. It is said to bring good luck to those who respect it, but it can be mischievous. Its body is made of gold, and it fires coins at high speed. None specified.

The Approach

In the neon-lit streets of a fantasy Tokyo, a young man with a lion's heart and a dragon's name fights to protect the innocent. Ryū Lion, a hero of the modern mythic age, stands at the crossroads of ancient legend and urban fantasy, his story a testament to the enduring power of heroism in a world that has forgotten its own magic.

Identity

Ryū Lion

Pronunciation RYOO LEE-ON (Japanese: リュー・リオン, Ryū Rion)

Known As

Ryuu LionLionThe GaleThe Hero of the ArenaThe Witch of the Abyss

Etymology

The name 'Ryū' (竜) means 'dragon' in Japanese, while 'Lion' is the English word for the big cat. The combination suggests a hybrid of dragon and lion, symbolizing strength, courage, and ferocity. In the series, her surname 'Lion' reflects her fierce and protective nature, while 'Ryū' may hint at her hidden past and her connection to the dragon-like monsters she once fought.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese popular culture, specifically the 'Is It Wrong to Try to Pick Up Girls in a Dungeon?' franchise created by Fujino Ōmori. The character is owned by SB Creative (publisher) and J.C.Staff (anime production).

Disputed Identifications

None known. Ryū Lion is a distinct character within the DanMachi universe, not based on any real-world myth or legend.

Description

Appearance

Ryū Lion is a tall, slender young woman with long, platinum-blonde hair that she usually ties in a high ponytail. She has striking emerald-green eyes and fair skin. She typically wears the uniform of the Hostess of Fertility, a maid café, consisting of a black and white dress with an apron. When adventuring, she wears light armor: a white and blue tunic, leather pants, and boots, with a shortsword at her hip. She is often depicted with a calm, serious expression, but her face lights up when she is with friends.

Symbols & Iconography

Her iconic symbol is the wind, representing her speed and agility. She is associated with the Gale, a title given to her as one of the fastest adventurers in Orario. Her weapon of choice is a shortsword, which she wields with precision. In her past as an adventurer, she was part of the Astraea Familia, whose emblem was a star.

Behaviour & Personality

Ryū is initially portrayed as aloof, serious, and somewhat antisocial, preferring to keep to herself. She is fiercely protective of her friends and colleagues at the Hostess of Fertility, and she has a strong sense of justice. Despite her cold exterior, she is deeply compassionate and harbors guilt over her past as a vigilante. She is highly disciplined, having trained as an adventurer for years, and she is skilled in cooking and serving, which she does with professional grace. Over time, she opens up to Bell Cranel and others, showing a warmer, more vulnerable side.

Domains & Habitats

Ryū resides in the city of Orario, the central setting of DanMachi. She works at the Hostess of Fertility, a tavern in the city's main shopping district. As an adventurer, she frequents the Dungeon, a massive labyrinth beneath Orario. In her past, she lived in the Astraea Familia's home, but after its disbandment, she found refuge at the tavern.

Version Differences

In the light novels, Ryū's backstory is explored in greater detail, including her time as an adventurer and her vigilante period. The anime adaptation condenses some of this, but the core elements remain consistent. In the mobile games, she has additional side stories that do not contradict the main canon. There are no significant contradictions between versions.

Superhuman Speed and Agility

Source Version Light Novel and Anime

Description

Ryū is exceptionally fast, able to move at speeds that appear blurry to normal humans. She uses this speed to dodge attacks and strike enemies before they can react. This earned her the title 'The Gale'.

Limitations

Her speed is not unlimited; she can be caught off guard by faster opponents or by traps.

Stories & Myths

The Fall of Astraea Familia

Source Is It Wrong to Try to Pick Up Girls in a Dungeon? Light Novel Volume 6, Anime Season 2Culture Japanese light novel and animePeriod Fictional timeline, prior to the main story

Summary

Ryū was a member of the Astraea Familia, a group of adventurers dedicated to justice. They were betrayed by a member of their own familia, resulting in the deaths of many members. Ryū survived but was left with a deep sense of guilt and a desire for revenge.

Participants

Ryū Lion

Astraea (goddess)

Other familia members

The traitor (revealed later)

Consequences

The familia disbanded, and Ryū left the adventurer life, eventually finding work at the Hostess of Fertility. She secretly continued to hunt down the traitors, becoming a vigilante known as the 'Witch of the Abyss'.

Variants

The anime adaptation condenses the backstory but keeps the essential plot points.

Contradictions & Disputes

Romantic interest in Bell Cranel

Position A

Ryū has romantic feelings for Bell, as hinted in later light novels and side stories.

Position A — evidence

In Volume 14 and later, there are moments where Ryū blushes and shows jealousy when Bell is with other girls.

Position B

Ryū's feelings are strictly platonic, and she acts as a mentor figure only.

Position B — evidence

The anime and early light novels do not explicitly show romantic interest.

Scholarly Consensus

The author has stated that Ryū's feelings are ambiguous, leaving room for interpretation. Fans are divided.

Power level compared to other top adventurers

Position A

Ryū is among the strongest adventurers in Orario, comparable to Level 5 or 6.

Position A — evidence

Her performance in the Dungeon and against monsters suggests high-level capabilities.

Position B

Ryū is only Level 4, and her abilities are overestimated due to her speed.

Position B — evidence

Official stats list her as Level 4, and she struggles against higher-level opponents.

Scholarly Consensus

The official status sheet confirms Level 4, but her skill and experience allow her to fight above her level occasionally.

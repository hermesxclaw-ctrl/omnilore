Who It Is

In the neon-lit streets of a fantasy Tokyo, a young man with a lion's heart and a dragon's name fights to protect the innocent. Ryū Lion, a hero of the modern mythic age, stands at the crossroads of ancient legend and urban fantasy, his story a testament to the enduring power of heroism in a world that has forgotten its own magic.

Ryū is initially portrayed as aloof, serious, and somewhat antisocial, preferring to keep to herself. She is fiercely protective of her friends and colleagues at the Hostess of Fertility, and she has a strong sense of justice. Despite her cold exterior, she is deeply compassionate and harbors guilt over her past as a vigilante. She is highly disciplined, having trained as an adventurer for years, and she is skilled in cooking and serving, which she does with professional grace. Over time, she opens up to Bell Cranel and others, showing a warmer, more vulnerable side.

Powers
Superhuman Speed and Agility

Ryū is exceptionally fast, able to move at speeds that appear blurry to normal humans. She uses this speed to dodge attacks and strike enemies before they can react. This earned her the title 'The Gale'.

Limit: Her speed is not unlimited; she can be caught off guard by faster opponents or by traps.

Expert Swordsmanship

Ryū is a highly skilled swordswoman, proficient in using a shortsword. She employs a style that emphasizes quick, precise strikes and fluid movement.

Limit: She is less effective in prolonged, heavy combat against heavily armored opponents.

Magic: Wind Magic (Wind Blast, Gale Force)

Ryū has a magical ability that allows her to manipulate wind, creating powerful gusts to push enemies or enhance her own speed. She can also use wind to create a protective barrier.

Limit: Magic use consumes Mind (stamina), and overuse can leave her exhausted.

Healing Magic (Limited)

Ryū has a minor healing spell that can treat minor wounds, but it is not as potent as dedicated healers.

Limit: Ineffective for severe injuries or magical ailments.

Survival and Tracking Skills

Due to her years in the Dungeon, Ryū is adept at navigating, tracking monsters, and surviving in harsh environments.

Limit: Her skills are limited to the Dungeon environment and similar terrains.

She typically wears the uniform of the Hostess of Fertility, a maid café, consisting of a black and white dress with an apron. Symbols — Her iconic symbol is the wind, representing her speed and agility. She is associated with the Gale, a title given to her as one of the fastest adventurers in Orario. Her weapon of choice is a shortsword, which she wields with precision. In her past as an adventurer, she was part of the Astraea Familia, whose emblem was a star.

Domains — Ryū resides in the city of Orario, the central setting of DanMachi. She works at the Hostess of Fertility, a tavern in the city's main shopping district. As an adventurer, she frequents the Dungeon, a massive labyrinth beneath Orario. In her past, she lived in the Astraea Familia's home, but after its disbandment, she found refuge at the tavern.

The Fall of Astraea FamiliaIs It Wrong to Try to Pick Up Girls in a Dungeon? Light Novel Volume 6, Anime Season 2

Fictional timeline, prior to the main story

Ryū was a member of the Astraea Familia, a group of adventurers dedicated to justice. They were betrayed by a member of their own familia, resulting in the deaths of many members. Ryū survived but was left with a deep sense of guilt and a desire for revenge. The familia disbanded, and Ryū left the adventurer life, eventually finding work at the Hostess of Fertility. She secretly continued to hunt down the traitors, becoming a vigilante known as the 'Witch of the Abyss'.

Main story timeline

Ryū meets Bell Cranel, a rookie adventurer, and initially dismisses him. However, she later assists him in the Dungeon, saving his life. Their encounter begins a friendship that gradually thaws her cold exterior. Ryū becomes a mentor figure to Bell, and they develop a close bond. She later joins him on several adventures.

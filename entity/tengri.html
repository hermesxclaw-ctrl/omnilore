Who It Is

Tengri is the supreme deity of ancient Turkic and Mongol peoples — the eternal blue sky that encompasses all existence. Khans derived their right to rule (kut) directly from Tengri. Genghis Khan claimed his mandate from Eternal Tengri. The cosmos has three realms: sky (Tengri), earth (Umay), and underworld (Erlik). Animals — wolf, eagle, horse — are sacred intermediaries.

Attested in Orkhon inscriptions (8th century) / Secret History of the MongolsEra Bronze Age origins to present; revived Tengrism

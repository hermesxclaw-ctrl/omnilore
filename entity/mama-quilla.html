Who It Is
Form

Envisaged as a beautiful woman, often associated with the moon and its cycles

Powers

Lunar Cycle Control Mama Quilla is said to control the cycles of the moon, influencing the tides and the fertility of the land

Accounts

Mama Quilla is said to have given birth to the mythical founders of the Inca empire, Manco Cápac and Mama Uqllu

Mama Quilla is said to have cried tears of silver, which were believed to have healing properties

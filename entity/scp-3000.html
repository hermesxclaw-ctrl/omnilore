Who It Is
Form

SCP-3000 is a massive, aquatic, serpentine entity strongly resembling a giant moray eel (Gymnothorax javanicus). Its body is estimated to be approximately 100 meters in length, with a diameter of around 10 meters.

Powers

Massive Strength SCP-3000 possesses immense physical strength, capable of crushing ships and other objects with ease.

Regenerative Abilities SCP-3000 has demonstrated the ability to regenerate damaged or destroyed tissue, making it highly resilient to injury.

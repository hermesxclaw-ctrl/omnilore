Who It Is

Pwyll exchanged places with Arawn, King of Annwn, for a year and a day. He defeated Arawn's rival Hafgan and — in testament to his honor — did not sleep with Arawn's wife. Returning to Dyfed, he encountered Rhiannon on a magical horse and won her hand through clever trickery. Their son Pryderi appears in all four branches.

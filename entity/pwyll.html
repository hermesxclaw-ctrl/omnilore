Form & Signs

Appearance — Pwyll is often depicted as a wise and powerful king, with a strong sense of justice and a deep connection to the natural world.

Symbols — Pwyll is associated with the symbol of the stag, which represents his connection to the land and his wisdom.

Domains — Pwyll's domains include the kingdom of Dyfed, the Otherworld, and the land of the dead.

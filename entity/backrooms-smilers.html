Who It Is

Smilers are one of the most iconic and feared hostile entities in the Backrooms canon. They are photophobic (light-averse) creatures that inhabit the darker regions of the Backrooms -- particularly the unlit corridors of Level 0, the pipe-choked tunnels of Level 2, and the electrical maze of Level 3. Their name comes from their most distinguishable feature: a pair of glowing white 'eyes' and a wide, white, Cheshire Cat-like smile that floats in the darkness, often the only part of them visible before an attack. The body of a Smiler is described as a gaunt, vaguely humanoid figure with pale, translucent skin stretched taut over an elongated skeletal frame. Smilers cannot be seen in complete darkness -- they appear only as floating smiles and eyes -- but in low light, their full form is revealed. They are strongly repelled by bright light, making flashlights and light sources the primary defense against them (this mechanic is central to many Backrooms survival strategies). However, Smilers are patient hunters: they will stalk prey for hours, waiting for a light source to fail. When attacking, they rush forward with shocking speed, using long, clawed fingers to slash or grapple victims. The Smiler's distinctly unsettling design -- a disembodied grin in the dark -- has made it one of the most memorable and frequently referenced Backrooms entities, appearing in YouTube animations, games, and fan art alongside Partygoers and Hounds as part of the core Backrooms bestiary.

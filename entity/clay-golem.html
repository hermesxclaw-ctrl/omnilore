Who It Is

A humanoid statue of hardened clay, standing eight feet tall, animated by a bound earth elemental spirit. Its body is inscribed with arcane runes that absorb magical energy. A clay golem's fists deliver a cursed wound that cannot be magically healed and drains the victim's vitality.

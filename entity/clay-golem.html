Form & Signs

Appearance — A humanoid figure created from clay or mud, often depicted as a mute and soulless being, with a rough and unrefined appearance.

Symbols — The Golem is often associated with the Hebrew letter א (aleph), which is said to hold the power to bring the creature to life.

Domains — The Golem is often said to inhabit the land of Israel, but can also be found in other regions with rich soil and clay deposits.

Accounts I
The Golem of PragueGolem - Wikipedia

16th century

The story of Rabbi Judah Loew's creation of the Golem to protect the Jewish community from persecution. The Golem becomes a symbol of Jewish resistance and a reminder of the community's power and resilience.

Accounts II
The Golem of MesopotamiaGolem: The Clay Guardian of Jewish Folklore

Ancient Near East

The story of the Golem's creation by the Mesopotamian god Enki to protect the city of Uruk. The Golem becomes a powerful symbol of Mesopotamian mythology and a reminder of the city's rich history and culture.

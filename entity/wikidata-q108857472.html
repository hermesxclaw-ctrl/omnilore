Who It Is

Kirkwall does not make heroes — it breaks them, buys them, and buries them. Hawke arrived as a penniless refugee from the burning village of Lothering with a dead apostate father, a noble mother in disgrace, and two younger siblings to keep alive. A decade later the same city would call Hawke its Champion, its most wanted criminal, and its last hope, depending on who you asked — and what Hawke chose to become in your game.

Attested: Personality is player-determined via three dialogue tones — Diplomatic (blue laurel, charming/conciliatory), Humorous/Sarcastic (purple comedy mask, witty/deflective), and Aggressive (red fist, blunt/direct) — plus autonomous flavor lines. Varric's narration explicitly frames Hawke as charismatic, stubborn, and fiercely loyal to family/friends, but Cassandra and Leliana interrogate whether Varric romanticizes. Etymology — Surname Hawke is a variant spelling of Hawk, from Middle English hauk, Old English hafoc 'hawk' (bird of prey), originally a nickname or occupational name for a falconer, or a topographic/house name. In-universe, the family name is Hawke, borne by Malcolm Hawke (Fereldan apostate) and Leandra Amell

Powers
Class-Based Combat Mastery (Mage / Rogue / Warrior)

Hawke's abilities are determined by class choice at creation, each with full talent trees. Mage Hawke: elemental (fire/ice/lightning), force, creation/healing, entropy, arcane, and optional blood magic specialization; can heal, barrier, and nuke. Rogue Hawke: dual daggers or archery, stealth, traps, assassination/duelist/shadow specializations. Warrior Hawke: sword-and-shield or two-handed, vanguard/defender/berserker/reaver/templar specializations. All classes gain Champion-adjacent reputation, not superpowers.

Limit: Mage Hawke is constantly at risk of templar detection, demonic possession, and cannot openly cast in Kirkwall without consequences; mana/stamina limits, cooldowns, and friendly fire on Nightmare difficulty. Rogue/Warrior lack magic and rely on equipment. No class can fly, teleport, or resurrect without gameplay abstraction.

Blood Magic (Mage Hawke specialization, optional)

If chosen, Mage Hawke can learn blood magic from Merrill or the Legacy blood mage teachings — using health instead of mana, controlling minds, and drawing power from blood. Attested in lore via Malcolm Hawke's own secret studies.

Limit: In-universe, blood magic is forbidden by Chantry, risks demonic attention, and alienates companions (Fenris, Aveline, Sebastian strongly disapprove). Gameplay cost is health sacrifice. Not available to Rogue/Warrior Hawke.

Champion's Influence and Leadership

Non-supernatural but narratively crucial: after defeating the Arishok in Act 2, Hawke is named Champion of Kirkwall, granting political immunity, estate, and ability to mediate between Viscount, Qunari, mages, and templars. Varric's tales amplify this into legend, making Hawke's word carry weight across Thedas.

Limit: Title is largely honorary and fragile — Hawke cannot prevent the mage-templar war, cannot save Leandra, and by Act 3 both sides demand Hawke choose. In Inquisition, Hawke is a fugitive, not a ruler, and defers to the Inquisitor.

Unreliable Legend / Narrative Framing

Meta-ability: Hawke's deeds are filtered through Varric's exaggerated storytelling to Cassandra. Early game shows Hawke as overpowered, glowing, one-shotting ogres — later revealed as Varric's embellishment. This framing is a narrative device, not a diegetic power.

Limit: Not an in-world power; Hawke does not actually have superhuman embellished feats. Players must distinguish Varric's tall tales from attested events.

Grey Warden Taint Resistance (Conditional, Sibling-linked)

Hawke themselves is not a Grey Warden (except in cut content), but Hawke's bloodline carries Warden ties via Malcolm's research and the Legacy prison. Hawke can survive Deep Roads darkspawn exposure that kills others, and Hawke's sibling may undergo Joining and gain taint resistance.

Limit: Hawke does not gain Warden abilities (no Joining, no darkspawn sensing) unless modded/cut content. Resistance is narrative, not mechanical.

Accounts I
The Flight from Lothering (Prologue)Dragon Age II, Prologue: Lothering's Fall

9:30 Dragon (in-universe); 2011 release

During the Fifth Blight, darkspawn overrun Lothering. Malcolm Hawke is already dead; Leandra, Hawke, Bethany and Carver flee north with other refugees. At the threshold, Flemeth (the Witch of the Wilds) saves them from an ogre in exchange for delivering an amulet to the Dalish clan at Sundermount. During the escape, one sibling dies — if Hawke is a mage, Carver dies; if Hawke is rogue/warrior, Bethany dies — establishing the game's first irreversible branching. The family arrives in Kirkwall as destitute refugees, turned away at the gates. Sets Hawke's refugee status, debt to Flemeth, and sibling survivor who will shape Act 1. Establishes Varric's later claim that Hawke was at the heart of Blight events.

Act 1: Debt, The Deep Roads, and Becoming KirkwallersDragon Age II, Act 1

9:30-9:31 Dragon

Penniless in Kirkwall, Hawke works for a year for either smuggler Athenril or mercenary Meeran to repay uncle Gamlen's debt and buy entry to an expedition into the Deep Roads funded by Bartrand and Varric Tethras. The expedition seeks dwarven treasure to restore the Amell fortune. In the Deep Roads, Hawke discovers red lyrium (idol) and encounters darkspawn; surviving sibling may die, be taken by Grey Wardens (Carver) or Circle (Bethany) if Hawke takes them. Success makes Hawke wealthy, reclaims the Amell estate in Hightown, and establishes Hawke as a rising figure. Hawke moves from Lowtown slum to nobility, gains companions, and introduces red lyrium — the saga's central corrupting artifact. Sibling fate branches permanently.

Act 2: The Qunari and the Arishok — Champion of KirkwallDragon Age II, Act 2

9:34-9:37 Dragon (three-year gap)

Qunari led by the Arishok have been stranded in Kirkwall for years after losing a sacred relic (Tome of Koslun). Tensions rise as Kirkwall's Viscount and Chantry fail to resolve the standoff. Hawke, now established, mediates murders, discovers the relic was stolen by Isabela, and ultimately duels the Arishok in single combat when the Qunari assault the city. Hawke kills the Arishok, saving Kirkwall, and is named Champion of Kirkwall by Viscount Dumar. Hawke gains the Champion title and political capital, but the power vacuum after Dumar's death destabilizes Kirkwall. The Tome's return briefly resolves Qunari conflict, but leaves city on edge for mage-templar war. Isabela may abandon or return with Tome, affecting Hawke's duel.

Accounts II
Act 3: The Mage-Templar War — The Last StrawDragon Age II, Act 3

9:40 Dragon

Knight-Commander Meredith Stannard tightens control over the Circle using red lyrium idol (crafted into her sword), while Orsino's mages grow desperate. Hawke's mother Leandra is murdered by serial killer Quentin, a blood mage trying to reconstruct his dead wife — Hawke kills him but cannot save Leandra. Anders, Hawke's companion and abomination (possessed by Justice/Vengeance), destroys the Kirkwall Chantry with explosives, killing Grand Cleric Elthina and triggering open war. Hawke must choose to side with mages or templars, confront both Meredith (revealed as red-lyrium corrupted) and Orsino (who uses blood magic and harvests corpses), and decides Kirkwall's fate before fleeing or remaining as Viscount's successor. Chantry explosion sparks the global mage-templar war that drives Dragon Age: Inquisition. Kirkwall is left in ruins; Hawke disappears from Kirkwall (fugitive, protector, or exile depending on choice). Leandra's death is unavoidable and marks Hawke's personal failure despite Champion status.

Legacy: Blood of Malcolm Hawke and Corypheus UnboundDragon Age II DLC Legacy

9:37 Dragon (between Act 2-3); DLC 2011

Hawke investigates attacks on House Hawke by Carta mercenaries bearing the Hawke crest. Trail leads to an ancient Grey Warden prison in the Vimmark Mountains built by Malcolm Hawke. Malcolm, a former Circle mage, helped seal Corypheus — one of the original magisters who entered the Golden City and became the first darkspawn — using blood magic and Warden blood. Hawke confronts Corypheus, who possesses Grey Wardens (including Larius/Janeka) and seeks to become a god. Hawke defeats Corypheus, but he transfers his essence to a nearby darkspawn/warden and survives to return in Inquisition. Reveals Hawke lineage's connection to Tevinter magisters and red lyrium's origin; directly sets up Dragon Age: Inquisition's main villain. Hawke inherits Malcolm's amulet/key and burden.

Mark of the Assassin and The Fade: Hawke in InquisitionDragon Age II DLC Mark of the Assassin (2011) and Dragon Age: Inquisition (2014) quest Here Lies the Abyss

9:38 Dragon (Mark) and 9:41 Dragon (Inquisition)

In Mark of the Assassin, Hawke is hired by Orlesian noble Prosper to hunt wyverns, accompanied by elven assassin Tallis (from Dragon Age: Redemption), uncovering a Qunari plot to invade Orlais. In Inquisition, Varric brings Hawke to Skyhold to help the Inquisitor investigate Grey Wardens under Corypheus's influence. At Adamant Fortress, Hawke and the Inquisitor enter the Fade to close a rift. Hawke confronts the Nightmare demon and may be left behind in the Fade to die so the Inquisitor and Warden ally Stroud/Loghain/Alistair can escape — a player choice that determines if Hawke survives to post-Inquisition war table missions. Completes Hawke's arc from local Champion to Thedosian legend whose sacrifice (or survival) affects Inquisition's Warden alliance and Corypheus's defeat. If Hawke dies in the Fade, Varric mourns and Hawke's story ends; if Hawke lives, Hawke continues hunting Corypheus remnants and is referenced in Trespasser epilogue.

Hawke is the Hero of Ferelden / the Warden from Dragon Age: Origins. — Hawke is a completely separate protagonist. The Warden (Origins) and Hawke (DA2) are different people from different regions; they never meet in-game. The Warden disappears after Origins, Hawke rises in Kirkwall. Inquisition features both as distinct figures (Warden ally vs Hawke ally).

There is a single canonical Hawke — male mage Garrett Hawke — and other versions are non-canon. — BioWare has never declared a canon gender, class, or personality for Hawke. World of Thedas presents both Garrett and Marian as defaults; Keep treats all combos as valid. 'Canon Hawke' is a fan invention, not official.

Hawke becomes Viscount of Kirkwall and rules the city. — Hawke is offered the title after the Arishok's defeat but never actually rules; Viscount Dumar's seat remains empty, and by Act 3 Hawke is Champion in name only, with Meredith holding power. Epilogue slides show Hawke may be offered viscountcy if sided with templars, but Hawke flees or disappears in all endings before Dragon Age: Inquisition.

Hawke is a Grey Warden. — Hawke is not a Grey Warden in attested canon. Hawke can enter the Deep Roads without Joining and is not tainted. Sibling Bethany/Carver may become a Warden, and Hawke's father researched Warden magic, but Hawke themselves never undergoes the Joining (cut content for Warden Hawke was removed).

Hawke (Dragon Age) and Hawke (Advance Wars) are the same character or related. — No relation. Advance Wars Hawke is a Black Hole CO, a stoic military commander with black-and-red uniform and powers like Black Storm; Dragon Age Hawke is a Kirkwall refugee. Shared name is coincidence from common English surname.

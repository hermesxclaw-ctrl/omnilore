Who It Is

A large reptilian creature of unknown origin with a deep and vocal hatred for all life. SCP-682 possesses extreme regenerative capabilities and adaptive evolution — it survives any damage and develops immunity to whatever harmed it. It has been exposed to acids, fire, radiation, vacuum, dimensional displacement, and reality anchors; none have permanently harmed it. It expresses its hatred of humanity in articulate, contemptuous speech.

Form

A large reptilian creature, roughly 2.7 meters long and 0.8 meters tall, with green-gray scaled hide, multiple sets of regenerating eyes, and a mouth full of constantly regenerating teeth. Its exact form shifts as it adapts to threats.

Near-perfect regeneration

Extreme regenerative capability SCP-682 can regenerate from massive physical damage, including dissolution, rotting, dismemberment, and acid immersion. It has been observed moving and speaking with up to 87% of its body destroyed. It can reconstitute tissue rapidly and adjust its morphology to restore function. Core SCP article; termination logs and cross-tests Regeneration requires energy intake; acid immersion and continuous damage can slow but not stop recovery. Some termination attempts temporarily reduce its functionality or force it into dormancy, but none achieve permanent destruction. No direct contradictions on regeneration exist, but some cross-tests suggest temporary nullification or stasis (e.g., interactions with certain reality-altering SCPs), raising questions about whether its regeneration is truly absolute or context-dependent.

Adaptive resistance SCP-682 adapts to threats and environmental conditions, developing resistances or countermeasures after initial exposure. When subjected to new forms of attack—chemical, anomalous, physical—it alters its physiology or behavior to mitigate harm in subsequent encounters. Termination logs and cross-tests (e.g., SCP-409, SCP-017, SCP-173, SCP-096, SCP-999) Adaptation is not instantaneous; initial exposures can cause significant damage. Some anomalies may still pose ongoing challenges, particularly those that alter reality or cognition rather than purely physical states. Different logs vary in how quickly and completely SCP-682 adapts. Some fan accounts exaggerate its adaptation to near-omnipotence, while the main site material maintains occasional partial failures and setbacks.

Superhuman strength, speed, and reflexes SCP-682 exhibits extremely high physical strength, speed, and reflexes, allowing it to tear through barriers, kill multiple humans rapidly, and evade containment measures. Exact levels vary with its current form and mass. Core SCP article; breach logs Physical performance can be reduced by sustained acid immersion, heavy damage, or certain anomalous effects. Containment protocols rely on incapacitation via acid and overwhelming force rather than direct combat. No major contradictions, though some adaptations portray SCP-682 as faster or stronger than others. Game mechanics may under- or over-represent its capabilities for balance reasons.

Universal ingestion and energy extraction SCP-682 gains energy from anything it ingests, organic or inorganic. Filtering gills in its nostrils can remove usable matter from any liquid solution, including acid, enabling continuous regeneration even while submerged. Core SCP article While it can extract energy from diverse materials, the rate and efficiency are not fully quantified. Containment relies on acid immersion to both damage and limit its mobility, suggesting that ingestion does not fully negate environmental constraints. No explicit contradictions, but some fan texts imply near-limitless energy extraction, whereas the article implies practical limits given the need for ongoing containment.

High intelligence and complex communication SCP-682 is capable of complex speech and reasoning. It engages in dialogue with human researchers and with SCP-079, demonstrating strategic thinking, memory, and the ability to manipulate others. Core SCP article (Addendum 682-B); SCP-079 cross-test logs Communication can provoke rage states, making interviews dangerous. The Foundation forbids casual conversation, limiting opportunities to fully map its cognitive abilities. Some portrayals emphasize bestial rage over intellect, while others foreground its strategic, almost philosophical hatred of life. These emphases reflect narrative choices rather than explicit textual conflict.

Hatred of all life SCP-682 expresses a deep, consistent hatred of all living beings, describing them as disgusting and deserving of destruction. This hatred appears to motivate its attacks and breaches. Core SCP article; interview transcripts This is a psychological or ideological trait rather than a discrete power. It may make SCP-682 predictable in its hostility but does not provide direct mechanical abilities. Scarlet King-linked stories interpret this hatred as part of a cosmic agenda, while other texts leave it unexplained. The origin and nature of the hatred remain speculative.

Potential reality and conceptual resistance (speculative) Some cross-tests suggest that SCP-682 can resist or adapt to reality-altering, conceptual, or memetic effects, implying more than purely biological resilience. Various termination logs and cross-tests (interpretive synthesis) Evidence is indirect and often framed as partial resistance or delayed adaptation. It is not consistently portrayed as immune to such effects. Fan discussions sometimes treat SCP-682 as effectively invulnerable to all forms of attack, but the official logs maintain that certain anomalies can significantly hinder or temporarily neutralize it.

Incident Log

SCP-682 is discovered after killing several farmers. The Foundation captures and transports it to a secure site, where it is placed in an acid-filled containment chamber. During an early interview, a scientist attempts to question SCP-682 about its motives. The entity responds with incomprehensible muttering, leading the scientist to order a D-class (D-085) closer to the microphone. SCP-682 then devours D-085, forcing the scientist to flee. This event establishes its lethal nature, hatred of life, and the danger of direct interaction.

SCP-682 repeatedly attempts to breach containment. As of a specific redacted date, seventeen attempts are recorded, six of which succeed. Breaches involve SCP-682 escaping its acid chamber, killing security personnel and D-class subjects, and moving through Foundation facilities. Recontainment requires multiple Mobile Task Forces with at least seven members, heavy weaponry, and coordinated tactics. Casualties are high, and each breach reinforces the entity’s reputation as one of the most dangerous SCPs.

The Foundation conducts numerous attempts to destroy or neutralize SCP-682 using other anomalies. Examples include exposure to SCP-409 (a crystal that spreads and shatters matter), SCP-017 (a shadow entity that consumes living beings), SCP-173 (a statue that snaps necks when unobserved), SCP-096 (a humanoid that kills anyone who sees its face), and SCP-999 (a gelatinous entity that induces euphoria. In each case, SCP-682 is severely damaged or temporarily affected but ultimately survives, often adapting to the threat. The SCP-999 interaction notably reduces SCP-682’s aggression, suggesting a potential partial behavioral countermeasure.

In a widely circulated origin story, SCP-682 is described as one of the leviathan offspring of the Scarlet King’s fourth bride, A’zieb. These children are said to possess impenetrable hides and self-healing wounds, designed to fear neither magic nor weapons. They rot the roots of the Tree of Knowledge and survive a great flood, after which SCP-682 is banished to Earth. It becomes the inspiration for various mythological monsters, including Leviathan and Tarasque. This narrative situates SCP-682 within a grand apocalyptic cosmology centered on the Scarlet King.

In a fan-developed storyline, SCP-682 escapes containment and reaches New York City, causing widespread destruction and mass casualties. The narrative follows Foundation efforts to track, confront, and recontain the entity amid urban chaos. It emphasizes the scale of threat SCP-682 poses if it reaches densely populated areas.

A separate SCP article describes SCP-682 as an extradimensional flooded concrete region beneath Site-433’s basement sub-levels, replacing a VHS cache. The space is roughly the size of the continental United States, with a low concrete ceiling, pillars, sandy soil, and fluctuating stagnant freshwater. Exploratory missions require HAZMAT suits and are limited to two hours due to environmental risks. This SCP-682 is a location, not a reptile, and belongs to a whimsical adventure narrative hub.

A way to hold it in your mind

Imagine a creature that never stops hating you. Not because you wronged it, not because you threaten it, but because you are alive. Every breath you take is, to it, an offense worthy of acid and teeth. The SCP Foundation calls this entity SCP-682—the Hard-to-Destroy Reptile—and keeps it submerged in hydrochloric acid inside a steel-lined cube, knowing that even dissolved flesh will knit itself back together. Around that chamber stretches fifty kilometers of deliberately empty land, a silent moat against the day the acid fails. Whether you read it as a monster, a cosmic heir of a scarlet tyrant, or a mirror held up to institutional violence, SCP-682 sits at the center of internet horror like a black star: dense, hostile, and impossible to ignore.

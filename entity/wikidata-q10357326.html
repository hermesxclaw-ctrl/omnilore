Who It Is

In the mid-1950s, a Brazilian medium named Hercílio Maes published a book claiming that Mars was inhabited by spiritually advanced beings—and that the text had been dictated to him by a spirit called Ramatis. This encounter sparked a syncretic spiritual movement that blends Kardecist spiritism, Eastern mysticism, Umbanda, and UFO lore, yet remains firmly outside the orthodox Spiritist mainstream.

Ramatis is described as a wise and evolved spirit, guiding humanity through his teachings. He is portrayed as a coordinator of a spiritual team, promoting unity between Western and Eastern spiritual traditions.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Ramatis' is said to recall the Iraqi city of Ramadi, or the fossil 'Ramadis Kadaba' found in Ethiopia, meaning 'root' in the Afar language. However, these are speculative connections proposed by followers or researchers; no definitive etymology is given.

Pronunciation — Not explicitly recorded; likely /ˈrɑːmətɪs/ or /rɑːmɑːˈtiːs/.

Aliases — Ramatís, Rama-tys, Swami Sri Rama-tys

Ownership — Brazilian Spiritist and esoteric movement; specifically associated with the medium Hercílio Maes and his followers, who call themselves 'Ramatisists'.

Powers
Mediumistic communication

Ramatis is believed to dictate books and messages to mediums, particularly Hercílio Maes and others.

Limit: Communication depends on the medium's receptivity and moral development.

Spiritual coordination

Ramatis coordinates the 'Fraternity of the Cross and the Triangle', a team of spirits from Christian and Eastern traditions.

Limit: None specified.

Past life knowledge

Ramatis claims to have lived multiple incarnations, including as Pythagoras and an advisor to Solomon, giving him profound wisdom.

Limit: These are unverifiable claims.

Form & Signs

Appearance — No physical description is provided; Ramatis is a disembodied spirit. In past incarnations, he is said to have been a Hindu guru in the 10th century, a previous incarnation of Pythagoras, a resident of Atlantis, an advisor to King Solomon, a son of Moses, and a bodyguard of Jesus.

Symbols — The 'Fraternity of the Cross and the Triangle' is a symbol associated with Ramatis, combining Christian and Eastern elements.

Domains — Ramatis is said to reside in the spiritual realm, possibly on Mars or in higher astral planes. He is associated with the spiritual governance of Earth's evolution.

Accounts I
The Dictation of 'A Vida no Planeta Marte'Hercílio Maes

1950s

Ramatis dictated a book to Hercílio Maes describing life on Mars, where beings are more evolved spiritually and technologically. The book also claims Jesus had contact with extraterrestrial beings. Established Ramatis as a spirit guide and launched a new spiritual movement.

Accounts II
The Fraternity of the Cross and the TriangleRamatisist teachings

20th century

Ramatis is said to have created a spiritual fraternity in outer space by merging the Fraternity of the Cross (Western) and the Fraternity of the Triangle (Eastern). Provides a cosmic framework for uniting Christian and Eastern spirituality.

Who It Is

In the brutal, blood-soaked world of Re:Zero, where heroes are fragile and death is a recurring lesson, Garfiel Tinsel stands as a raw, unpolished force of nature—a boy who chose to become a beast to protect what little he had left. His story is not one of gentle heroism but of feral loyalty, buried trauma, and the slow, painful discovery that strength alone cannot shield the heart.

Initially brash, aggressive, and fiercely independent, Garfiel projects an arrogant and combative exterior. He is deeply protective of those he considers family, especially his sister Frederica and his grandmother. Underneath, he suffers from profound self-loathing and a fear of vulnerability, believing that showing weakness leads to abandonment. He gradually opens up and becomes a loyal ally, though he retains a hot-headed and straightforward demeanor.

Aliases — Garf, The Beast, Garfiel

Powers
Enhanced Physical Abilities

As a demi-human, Garfiel possesses superhuman strength, speed, and agility. He can easily overpower normal humans and even some magical beasts.

Limit: His abilities are not magic-based and are limited by his physical stamina.

Beast Transformation

Garfiel can partially transform into a tiger-like beast, gaining increased ferocity and combat prowess. This form is tied to his emotional state and demi-human bloodline.

Limit: The transformation can be unstable and may be triggered by extreme anger or stress. Overuse may lead to loss of control.

Claw and Fang Combat

Garfiel fights with his bare hands, using sharp claws and powerful kicks. He is highly skilled in unarmed combat, often overwhelming opponents with brutal, direct attacks.

Limit: Relies on close-quarters combat; lacks ranged options.

Earth Magic (Basic)

Garfiel has a minor affinity for earth magic, which he uses sparingly, such as creating small barriers or manipulating terrain. This is not his primary combat style.

Limit: His magical ability is limited compared to his physical prowess and is not heavily developed.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Garfiel is a tall, muscular young man with short blond hair and sharp blue eyes. He typically wears a sleeveless shirt and trousers, often with a fur-lined coat. His build is athletic and imposing, reflecting his demi-human heritage.

Symbols — His tiger-like ears and tail are symbols of his demi-human nature. He is often associated with the image of a beast, both in his combat style and his internal struggle.

Domains — Primarily the Sanctuary in Arc 4, a remote village in the Kingdom of Lugunica. He also resides in Roswaal's mansion as a servant after the Sanctuary arc.

Accounts I
The Sanctuary's GuardianRe:Zero Arc 4

Fictional timeline

Garfiel is introduced as the self-appointed guardian of the Sanctuary, a village of demi-humans trapped by a barrier. He initially opposes Subaru and his group, viewing them as outsiders. His hostility stems from a deep-seated fear that opening the Sanctuary will lead to the abandonment of his people. Through battles and emotional confrontations, Subaru helps Garfiel confront his trauma and accept help, leading to Garfiel's eventual alliance. Garfiel becomes a loyal ally to Emilia's camp, aiding in the battle against the Great Rabbit and the Witch Cult. He later serves as a guard at Roswaal's mansion.

Accounts II
The Battle Against the Great RabbitRe:Zero Arc 4

Fictional timeline

Garfiel plays a crucial role in the desperate battle against the Great Rabbit, a devastating magical beast. He uses his physical strength and determination to hold the line, showcasing his growth from a lone wolf to a team player. The victory secures the safety of the Sanctuary and solidifies Garfiel's place in the group.

Backstory: The Abandoned ChildRe:Zero Arc 4, light novel chapter

Fictional past

Garfiel's past is revealed: he and his sister Frederica were born in the Sanctuary, but their mother abandoned them, leaving them with their grandmother. Frederica later left to work at Roswaal's mansion, which Garfiel perceived as another abandonment. This trauma shaped his fierce independence and fear of being left behind. Explains his initial hostility and his eventual reconciliation with his sister.

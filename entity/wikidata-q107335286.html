Who It Is

In the moonlit forests of Manipur, a man hears a woman's laughter—sweet, alluring, and utterly inhuman. She is a Helloi, a spirit of the wild, whose beauty masks a power that can unmake a mind. To meet her is to risk madness; to survive her is to carry a story that will haunt you forever.

Hellois are wandering spirits associated with seduction of men. They can cause diseases, especially sexually transmitted infections, and mental disorders. They are known to attack men, causing disorder of mind, and may demand something in return to leave a victim. They are considered the most powerful among female spirits.

Powers
Seduction

Hellois use their beauty to seduce men, leading them into dangerous encounters.

Limit: Not specified; may be resisted or countered by rituals.

Disease Infliction

Hellois can cause diseases, particularly sexually transmitted infections, and can make women sick in their sexual organs.

Limit: Not specified; may be prevented by rituals.

Mental Disturbance

Hellois cause mental imbalance, temporary madness, and permanent madness if not treated. Victims may fail to recognize family and friends but can still communicate with the Helloi.

Limit: Victims can be treated by maibas or maibis.

Possession (?)

Hellois may not easily leave a victim and demand something in return, suggesting a form of control or possession.

Limit: Not specified; rituals can expel them.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Hellois are depicted as beautiful young women, often with charming beauty that is both alluring and dangerous. They are sometimes seen as evil spirits in the form of beautiful maidens.

Symbols — No specific symbols or iconography are documented in the available sources.

Domains — Hellois inhabit wilderness areas such as thick bushes, dark dense forests, rivers, and meadows. The seven sisters (Helloi Taret) are specifically associated with such places.

Accounts I
Helloi Taret (Seven Hellois)Meitei mythology

Unknown

The seven Helloi sisters are a group of female spirits that reside in wilderness areas. They are known for their beauty and seductive powers. They are feared for causing diseases and mental disorders in men.

Accounts II
Helloi Oknaba (Encounter with Hellois)Meitei folklore

Unknown

An encounter with a Helloi is a common supernatural event. It can lead to sickness, mental imbalance, and temporary madness. Treatment by a maiba or maibi is necessary to avoid permanent madness. Victims may act abnormally and be unable to recognize family, but can communicate with the Helloi. The spirit may demand something to leave.

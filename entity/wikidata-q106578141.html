Who It Is

The name 'Banana Man' is a kaleidoscope of identities, from a British cartoon superhero to a vaudeville performer, a Christian evangelist's nickname, and even a mathematical category. Each iteration peels back a different layer of culture, humor, and meaning, revealing how a simple fruit can inspire wildly diverse personas.

Bananaman (British) is portrayed as a bumbling but well-meaning superhero. The Adventure Time character is a minor background character with no distinct personality. The Dancing Banana is an emoticon used to express excitement or playfulness. Sam Zemurray was a shrewd businessman, while Ray Comfort is a controversial evangelist.

Superhero abilities (Bananaman)

Bananaman gains superhuman strength, flight, and invulnerability when he eats a banana, but only for a limited time. He often forgets his password or has other comedic flaws.

Limit: Powers are temporary and require eating a banana. He is often portrayed as clumsy and not very intelligent.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The appearance varies by referent. Bananaman (British) is a muscular superhero with a banana-like costume, often depicted with a yellow and brown color scheme. The Adventure Time character is a small, anthropomorphic banana with a face. The Dancing Banana is an animated emoticon of a banana performing a dance. 'Hombre Banano' is a mural of a man with a banana-like head.

Symbols — The banana itself is the central symbol, often associated with humor, slapstick, and sometimes sexual innuendo. In the case of Bananaman, the banana represents transformation and heroism, as he gains powers by eating a banana. In the Christian evangelist context, the banana is used as an argument for intelligent design.

Domains — Bananaman (British) resides in the fictional city of 'Bananaman's world' (often depicted as a generic British city). The Adventure Time character lives in the Land of Ooo. The Dancing Banana exists in digital spaces. Sam Zemurray operated in the United States and Central America.

Bananaman (comic and TV series)DC Thomson (comic), TV series by Woodland Animations

1980s-present

Bananaman is the alter ego of Eric Wimp, a schoolboy who turns into a superhero when he eats a banana. He battles various villains in his city, often with humorous results due to his dim-wittedness. Bananaman became a popular British cultural icon, with merchandise and a TV series.

Accounts II
Sam Zemurray's banana empireBiographies and historical accounts

Early 20th century

Sam Zemurray, known as 'Sam the Banana Man', built a fortune in the banana trade, eventually controlling the United Fruit Company. He was a controversial figure due to his business practices in Central America. Zemurray's actions influenced political and economic dynamics in Honduras and other countries, sometimes with negative impacts.

Ray Comfort's banana argumentRay Comfort's public presentations and videos

2000s

Ray Comfort used a banana to argue that the fruit's design (e.g., its peel, shape, and nutritional value) proves intelligent design, which he claims disproves evolution. The argument became widely mocked. The argument became a meme and contributed to Comfort's notoriety.

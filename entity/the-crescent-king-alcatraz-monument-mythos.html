Who It Is
Form

The Crescent King / Alcatraz is described as a figure with the ability to take on various forms, including that of the Crescent King, a being with a crescent-shaped head and a regal demeanor. He is also associated with the moon and its phases.

Powers

Shapeshifting The Crescent King / Alcatraz has the ability to take on various forms, including that of the Crescent King and other beings.

Accounts

The story follows the Last Son of Alcatraz, a being with the ability to take on various forms, as he navigates the complexities of the Monument Mythos universe.

The story follows the Queen's plan to destroy the Lunarians and the consequences of her actions.

Who It Is

Alcatraz Island is not an island — it is the exposed head of a colossal living entity, the 'Crescent King,' whose body extends deep into the San Francisco Bay. The prison was built on its face to feed it — the suffering and despair of inmates kept it dormant. When the prison closed in 1963, the entity began to wake. Its stirrings caused the 1989 Loma Prieta earthquake. Its full awakening is one of the central apocalypse scenarios of the series.

Form

Beneath Alcatraz: a vast humanoid head with the island forming its upper face. Geological features resolve into features — the cellblock aligned with the nose bridge, the lighthouse where a third eye would be, the dock forming a lipless mouth. Its body wraps around the San Andreas Fault. When it moves, San Francisco shakes.

Powers

Dormancy sustained by human suffering

Seismic manipulation (earthquakes as 'muscle twitches')

Geological-scale body integrated with human architecture

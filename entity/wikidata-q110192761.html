Who It Is

In a high school where the scent of a rabbit can trigger a wolf's hunger more powerfully than any growl, Legoshi stands frozen in a hallway — 6-foot-plus, fangs he tries to hide, eyes too small and reptilian for a pure gray wolf — trying to decide whether what he feels is love or the urge to eat the person he loves. Paru Itagaki's reluctant hero is not an alpha predator but an apology in fur, a carnivore who bows his head to make himself smaller for herbivores, and whose entire coming-of-age is a negotiation between biology, morality, and the desire to be gentle in a world that expects him to be monstrous.

Attested personality: profoundly introverted, sensitive, self-effacing, and socially awkward. Described by Itagaki and voice actor Kobayashi as 'clumsy,' 'mysterious,' and 'hard to read' — not cool or traditionally masculine. Legoshi deliberately makes himself smaller (stooping, soft speech, avoiding confrontation) to reassure herbivores, internalizing guilt about carnivore potential for violence. Powers
Enhanced Lupine Physiology (Strength, Speed, Senses)

As a gray wolf, Legoshi possesses superior physical strength, speed, agility, and endurance compared to herbivores and most smaller carnivores. Most emphasized is olfaction: he can track individuals by scent over long distances and is overwhelmed by Haru's scent in their first encounter, triggering predatory arousal. Hearing and night vision also acute. In-universe, carnivores are physically stronger than herbivores, making his restraint socially significant.

Limit: Strength is not superhuman within setting — larger carnivores (e.g., Riz the brown bear, Ibuki the lion) can overpower him; stamina limited; senses can be overloaded or manipulated (e.g., perfume, blood scent causing loss of control). School imposes legal/social limits on using strength against herbivores.

Komodo Dragon Hybrid Traits - Venom Immunity and Ocular Morphology

Quarter Komodo dragon (via mother Leano, half wolf/half Komodo, and grandfather Gosha, pure Komodo). Inherits Gosha's small-pupiled eyes and, crucially, immunity to Komodo dragon venom (which is lethal to most mammals). Not venomous himself — his bite does not envenomate. This immunity becomes plot-relevant when he fights venomous opponents and when his heritage is revealed. Jack knows; school registry lists him as 'gray wolf' only.

Limit: Immunity is specific to Komodo venom, not general poison or disease; does not confer enhanced healing or venom production. Heritage is socially stigmatized (interspecies hybrids are rare and looked down upon), so Legoshi hides it.

Martial Arts and Combat Training (Under Gohin)

Initially untrained brawler relying on instinct, Legoshi undergoes rigorous training with Gohin the panda (psychiatrist/black market gatekeeper) to learn to fight without relying on predatory instinct, including controlling his strength, using herbivore martial arts principles, and weakening himself deliberately (eating only insects/eggs, later moth larvae) to fight on equal terms with stronger carnivores. Learns to throw punches, grapple, and endure pain. Defeats Riz (bear) and later Melon (leopard-gazelle hybrid) through technique and resolve rather than raw power.

Limit: Training requires extreme dietary restriction that weakens him physically; technique still limited against larger or armed opponents; he refuses to kill, limiting finishing moves. Injuries accumulate and require recovery.

Self-Restraint and Sensory Suppression

Learned ability to suppress predatory impulses through mental discipline, meditation, and ethical commitment — e.g., resisting devouring Haru despite intense olfactory trigger, abstaining from meat, clipping claws, lowering gaze. Not a superpower but narratively treated as his defining 'power': the ability to choose not to use power.

Limit: Unreliable under stress, hunger, or strong scent; can fail (near-attack on Haru, struggle vs. Riz). Requires constant effort and support (Gohin therapy). Interpreted by some characters as suspicious rather than virtuous.

Enhanced Endurance and Pain Tolerance (Narrative Feat)

Survives severe beatings (vs. Shishigumi lions, vs. Riz, vs. Melon) and continues fighting despite broken bones, blood loss, and exhaustion. Thematic feat tied to his resolve to protect Haru and atone, rather than innate invulnerability.

Limit: Not regenerative; requires medical treatment and long recovery; repeated injuries leave scars and lasting weakness. No supernatural healing.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Legoshi is a towering gray wolf (canid, male) depicted as 17 at series start, 18 by Volume 14, standing well above most classmates (official height often cited ~212cm including ears, though Itagaki avoids precise numbers; visually a head taller than Louis). Lean but powerfully built, with shaggy two-tone gray fur — darker charcoal on back, tail, and top of head, lighter silver-gray on chest, muzzle, and underbelly — deliberately chosen by Itagaki

Symbols — Visual symbolism centers on eyes and mouth. Itagaki cites Mathieu Amalric's performance in The Diving Bell and the Butterfly (Le Scaphandre et le papillon, 2007) — an actor conveying emotion only through eyes while paralyzed — as direct inspiration for Legoshi's expressiveness; his eyes carry narrative weight where his body is withdrawn. Accounts I
The Murder of Tem and the Suspect WolfBEASTARS manga Ch.1-4, anime S1 Ep.1-2

2016-2017 publication; in-universe Cherryton Academy, Spring

At Cherryton Academy, alpaca drama club member Tem is devoured at night. The school reels, and suspicion falls on carnivores, especially the large, quiet gray wolf Legoshi who was near the scene. Legoshi, stage crew member who prefers watching tragedies from the wings, is innocent but internalizes the gaze of fear. He resolves to find the killer, not for glory but because he cannot stand being feared for something he didn't do and because Tem was a kind classmate. This arc establishes the central tension: a society where carnivores and herbivores coexist by law but not by trust, and Legoshi's decision to act despite his instinct to hide. Sets murder-mystery throughline (later revealed as Riz), establishes Legoshi's moral core and outsider status, and introduces Louis as foil — the herbivore who performs strength vs. carnivore who hides it.

The Night on the Hill - Haru and the Predatory ImpulseBEASTARS manga Ch.6-15, anime S1 Ep.1, Ep.5-7

2016-2017 publication

Legoshi encounters Haru, a dwarf rabbit and sole member of the Gardening Club, alone at night. Overwhelmed by her scent — sweet, vulnerable, triggering both predatory hunger and romantic fascination — he pins her, mouth agape, nearly devouring her before recoiling in horror. Haru, who has been slut-shamed for alleged promiscuity, offers sex as a transactional shield, confusing Legoshi further. He spends subsequent chapters interrogating his own feelings: Is his racing heart love or appetite? He tries to suppress his carnivore nature, avoids Haru, and later learns she was offering intimacy from a place of loneliness, not consent as he understood it. Their halting conversations become the emotional spine of the series. Defines Legoshi's central dilemma — carnivore/herbivore romance as taboo and biologically fraught — and his commitment to distinguish love from hunger. Leads to Meteor Festival arc and his vow to become stronger for Haru rather than consume her.

Meteor Festival and the Shishigumi AbductionBEASTARS manga Ch.30-50, anime S1 Ep.8-12

2017 publication

Cherryton's Meteor Festival approaches. Louis, despite being herbivore, is kidnapped by the Shishigumi, a lion mafia that controls the Black Market. Legoshi, who has been training and wrestling with his feelings for Haru, learns Haru has also been kidnapped by the same lions to be eaten. Abandoning school rules, he infiltrates the Black Market with Gohin's help, fights through lions (including boss Ibuki), and rescues Haru in a bloody confrontation. He carries her out, confesses his feelings not as predator but as protector, and for the first time chooses to use his strength actively. Louis separately confronts his own ambition and vulnerability, deepening his bond/rivalry with Legoshi. Legoshi's first heroic use of violence for protection; cements Legoshi-Haru bond; exposes Black Market and systemic meat trade; Louis leaves school temporarily, shifting power dynamics; Legoshi begins regular visits to Gohin.

Accounts II
Training with Gohin and the Search for Tem's KillerBEASTARS manga Ch.60-100, anime S2 Ep.13-21

2018-2019 publication

After the rescue, Legoshi seeks to control his instincts and solve Tem's murder. He becomes Gohin's patient and student at the Black Market clinic, where Gohin — a panda who eats bamboo but guards the market — teaches him that strength without restraint is mere violence. Legoshi adopts a near-vegan diet (eating insects, eggs, later moth larvae) to weaken himself and learn herbivore-centric martial arts, believing he must be weak to be safe. Meanwhile, he investigates Tem's death, noticing Riz the brown bear's strange behavior (overly friendly, hiding strength, consuming honey to suppress carnivore urges). The arc culminates in Legoshi confronting Riz, who confesses he ate Tem in a moment of possessive, confused affection and has been hiding the crime. Legoshi, now physically weakened by diet, must decide whether to eat meat to gain strength to stop Riz. Reveals killer and motive — Riz's tragic, possessive love for Tem — and forces Legoshi to confront that carnivore violence can stem from affection, not just hunger. The Hybrid Heritage - Gosha, Leano, and the Komodo SecretBEASTARS manga Vol.14 Ch.100-101, Ch.120-130; anime Final Season

2019 publication

Flashback arc reveals Legoshi's family: his grandfather Gosha, a Komodo dragon who fell in love with wolf Toki, and his mother Leano, a hybrid wolf-Komodo who struggled with identity, self-hatred, and the stigma of being a hybrid. Leano, beautiful but detached, withdrew from life and died by suicide when Legoshi was 12, leaving him to be raised by Gosha. Gosha's venomous bite is lethal, but Legoshi inherited his eyes and venom immunity. Legoshi learns his existence is rare and stigmatized — hybrids are seen as impure — and that his gentle grandfather endured discrimination for loving across species. This reframes Legoshi's eyes and his lifelong feeling of being 'wrong' as inherited, not personal failure. Explains biological and emotional inheritance; Legoshi accepts his hybridity and reconciles with Gosha; informs his later empathy for Melon, another hybrid. Provides dark history depth and explains why school registers him as wolf only.

Melon - The Final Confrontation and Choosing CoexistenceBEASTARS manga Ch.150-196, anime Final Season Part 1-2 (2024-2025)

2019-2020 publication; final volume 22

After Riz's arrest, Legoshi leaves Cherryton, lives alone, and works while Haru attends university. A new threat emerges: Melon, a gazelle-leopard hybrid (son of a gazelle mother and leopard father) who hides his leopard spots under a mask and runs a crime syndicate, embodying the self-hatred Legoshi could have become. Melon kidnaps Haru to lure Legoshi, forcing a final battle where Legoshi, now understanding hybrid identity, refuses to kill Melon out of hatred but to stop his cycle of violence. With help from Louis, Gosha, and former Shishigumi, Legoshi defeats Melon not through dominance but through acceptance — offering him the possibility of being seen. The series ends not with triumphant conquest but with Legoshi and Haru tentatively choosing to live together despite societal taboo, and Legoshi accepting his carnivore nature as part of, not opposed to, his capacity to love. Resolution of hybrid theme, Legoshi's maturity from self-denial to self-acceptance, and series' thesis that coexistence requires acknowledging difference, not erasing it. Legoshi is a typical alpha-male wolf protagonist who dominates herbivores. — Canon Legoshi is deliberately anti-alpha: shy, self-effacing, slouches to appear smaller, avoids dominance, and is described by Itagaki as uncomfortable with 'manly' actions. His arc is about learning to use strength to protect, not dominate.

Legoshi and Haru's relationship is purely predatory or non-consensual. — Initial encounter is near-predation and deeply non-consensual in impulse, but Legoshi stops himself and spends series learning consent. Their later relationship is explicitly consensual and negotiated, with Haru as active agent who challenges Legoshi. Itagaki frames it as mutual growth, not grooming.

Legoshi is pure gray wolf with no other ancestry. — He is quarter Komodo dragon via maternal line (Gosha → Leano → Legoshi), explaining his small pupils and venom immunity. School registry lists him as gray wolf for social convenience, but biologically he is hybrid.

Legoshi's name is just a cute Japanese name with no meaning. — Itagaki confirmed name honors actor Bela Lugosi (Dracula), aligning with monstrous-sympathetic theme; face modeled on Mathieu Amalric, body on Kenichi Matsuyama per interviews.

The anime completely adapts the manga and Legoshi's story ends with Season 2. — Season 2 covers only up to Riz arc (~Vol.12). Legoshi's story continues through Melon arc to Vol.22, including leaving school and adult life, adapted only in Final Season (2024-2025).

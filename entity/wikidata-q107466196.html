Who It Is

On the war-torn world of Ryloth, one Twi'lek chieftain refused to bow to the Separatist Alliance—or to the Republic that came to 'liberate' him. Cham Syndulla, a freedom fighter turned politician, embodies the fierce independence and complex legacy of a people whose homeworld has been a pawn in galactic conflicts for generations.

Cham Syndulla is fiercely independent, proud, and deeply committed to the freedom of his people. He is a skilled tactician and warrior, but also a pragmatic politician. He is often skeptical of outside authority, especially the Galactic Republic, which he views as having failed Ryloth. He is willing to make difficult choices, including allying with questionable figures, to achieve his goals. He is a devoted father, though his dedication to his cause sometimes puts strain on his family relationships.

Military Tactics and Leadership

Cham Syndulla is a brilliant military strategist and charismatic leader, capable of rallying his people and coordinating complex guerrilla operations.

Limit: His tactics sometimes rely on risky gambles and can lead to heavy casualties.

Combat Skills

He is proficient in blaster combat and hand-to-hand combat, having fought in many battles.

Limit: As a non-Force user, he has no supernatural abilities.

Political Acumen

He is a skilled negotiator and politician, able to navigate complex political landscapes and form strategic alliances.

Limit: His distrust of central authority can hinder cooperation with larger factions.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Cham Syndulla is a male Twi'lek with blue skin and two head-tails (lekku) that are longer and more prominent than average. He typically wears practical military or civilian attire, often with a distinctive headband or helmet. In his younger years, he sports a beard and a more rugged look, while in later appearances he is older, with a more weathered face and grey-streaked lekku.

Symbols — Cham Syndulla's symbol is often associated with the Ryloth resistance movement. He is frequently depicted with the colors of Ryloth: blue and green. His personal insignia, when shown, often incorporates the Rylothian sun or a stylized flame, representing freedom and rebellion.

Domains — Ryloth, specifically the planet's less urbanized regions and resistance hideouts. He is also seen on other worlds during missions, such as Lothal and Mandalore.

Clone Wars

Cham Syndulla leads a resistance movement against the Separatist occupation of Ryloth. He initially distrusts the Republic's offer of aid, but eventually cooperates with Jedi Master Mace Windu and Captain Rex to liberate his planet. His daughter Hera is a child during this time. Ryloth is freed from Separatist control, but Cham remains wary of the Republic's intentions, a sentiment that shapes his future actions.

Accounts II
Resistance Against the EmpireStar Wars Rebels, various episodes

Age of the Empire

Years later, Cham Syndulla continues to fight for Ryloth's freedom, this time against the Galactic Empire. He initially refuses to join the larger Rebellion, preferring to focus on his homeworld. However, he eventually allies with the Phoenix Cell and the Ghost crew to strike against the Empire, though he remains wary of the Rebellion's broader goals. Cham's actions help weaken the Imperial presence on Ryloth and contribute to the eventual downfall of the Empire. His relationship with Hera is strained but ultimately repaired.

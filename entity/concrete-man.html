Who It Is

A construction robot built for concrete pouring and road repair. Concrete Man has a bulky gray body with a cement mixer drum on his back and a hose-nozzle arm. He sprays quick-drying concrete that hardens instantly, trapping enemies and sealing passages. His slow movement is compensated by extreme durability.

The Approach

In the year 20XX, a towering figure of hardened cement strides through the streets, leaving a trail of destruction and a path of solid gray. This is Concrete Man, one of Dr. Light's oldest creations, turned against his maker in a scheme that blurs the line between hero and villain. His story is not just one of bricks and mortar, but of loyalty, betrayal, and the heavy weight of a world that has outlived its creations.

Description

Appearance

Concrete Man is a humanoid robot with a stocky build, colored primarily in shades of gray and brown. His head features a helmet-like structure resembling a construction worker's hard hat. His arms are large and blocky, and his legs are sturdy. His chest plate bears a symbol resembling a concrete block. His overall design evokes a construction theme.

Symbols & Iconography

Construction motifs: concrete blocks, hard hat, and building materials. His weapon, the Concrete Shot, creates blocks of concrete.

Behaviour & Personality

As a Robot Master, Concrete Man follows his programming. In Mega Man 9, he is a formidable boss, attacking with projectiles and creating barriers. His personality is not deeply explored, but he is depicted as a dutiful robot, albeit one that has been reprogrammed by Dr. Wily.

Domains & Habitats

His stage is an urban construction site with scaffolding, concrete mixers, and other building equipment.

Version Differences

No significant differences between versions. In Mega Man Legacy Collection 2, his appearance and abilities remain unchanged.

Powers & Abilities

Concrete Shot

Source Version Mega Man 9

Description

After his defeat, Mega Man gains the Concrete Shot, which fires a glob of concrete that can create a temporary block or platform. It can be used to defeat certain enemies and solve puzzles, and is the weakness of Tornado Man.

Limitations

Uses weapon energy; the block can be destroyed by enemies.

Summary

Concrete Man is one of eight Robot Masters created by Dr. Light. When they go on a rampage, Dr. Light is blamed, and Dr. Wily appears to be the hero. Mega Man fights through the Robot Masters, discovering that Wily reprogrammed them to rebel against their expiration date. Concrete Man is defeated by Mega Man, and after the final battle, he is rebuilt and given new functions.

Who It Is

A towering 20-foot daemon of Khorne, the Blood God. Bloodthirsters have bat-like wings, bestial legs, and four arms — two wielding massive axes, one carrying a whip, and one a flaming sword. Their skin is the color of dried gore, and their roar can shatter stone. They are fury incarnate, knowing no fear, no tactics but slaughter.

Who It Is

In the blood-soaked annals of Warhammer Fantasy, few sights inspire such primal terror as the Bloodthirster—a towering daemon of Khorne, the Blood God, whose very presence is a hymn to slaughter. These winged juggernauts of rage and carnage are not merely soldiers in Khorne's legions; they are living embodiments of his will, each one a general, a destroyer, and a testament to the endless war that defines the Chaos Wastes.

Bloodthirsters are embodiments of rage and bloodlust. They are intelligent but utterly consumed by the desire for combat and slaughter. They lead Khorne's armies, often seeking out the strongest foes to challenge. They are fearless and relentless, showing no mercy or pity.

Powers
Daemonic Resilience

Bloodthirsters are daemons, making them resistant to mundane weapons and able to regenerate wounds. They are also immune to fear and psychology.

Limit: They can be banished by powerful magic or holy weapons.

Axe of Khorne

A massive, rune-encrusted axe that can cleave through armor and flesh with ease. It is a symbol of Khorne's favor.

Limit: Requires immense strength to wield, which the Bloodthirster possesses.

Whip of Khorne

Some Bloodthirsters carry a whip that can ensnare enemies or draw them closer, representing Khorne's dominion over slaves and captives.

Limit: Not as effective against heavily armored foes.

Flight

Bloodthirsters have large leathery wings that allow them to fly, making them highly mobile in battle.

Limit: They cannot fly indefinitely and may be grounded if wings are damaged.

Aura of Fear

Their presence is so terrifying that it can cause enemy units to flee or fight at reduced effectiveness.

Limit: Courageous or daemonic foes may resist.

Magic Resistance

As daemons, they have a natural resistance to magic, especially spells that target their minds.

Limit: High-level magic can still affect them.

Form & Signs

Appearance — A Bloodthirster is a towering daemon, typically depicted as a massive, heavily muscled humanoid with bestial features. It has leathery wings, cloven hooves, and a fanged maw. Its skin is often a deep red or bronze, and it wields a massive axe or whip. It radiates an aura of fear and violence.

Symbols — The symbol of Khorne, a skull with a sword, is often emblazoned on its armor or banner. Blood and skulls are central motifs, representing its devotion to Khorne.

Domains — They are native to the Realm of Chaos, specifically Khorne's domain in the Warp, but they can manifest in the mortal world during Chaos incursions or when summoned by powerful sorcerers.

Accounts I
The Fall of SkarbrandWarhammer Fantasy background, notably 'Khorne: The Blood God' (2015)

Pre-Sundering, ancient

Skarbrand was once the mightiest of Khorne's daemon princes, but he was tricked by Tzeentch into attacking Khorne himself. Khorne cast him out, stripping him of his intellect and leaving him a mindless brute. Skarbrand now roams the mortal world, seeking to prove his worth through endless slaughter. Skarbrand becomes a cautionary tale of the dangers of pride and the treachery of Tzeentch. He remains one of the most powerful Bloodthirsters, but is forever cursed.

Accounts II
The Siege of PraagWarhammer Fantasy Roleplay and various novels, e.g., 'The Enemy Within'

During the Chaos Incursions

During a massive Chaos invasion, a Bloodthirster led the assault on the city of Praag. It breached the walls and slaughtered hundreds before being banished by a combined effort of the city's defenders and priests of Sigmar. The city is devastated but survives. The event is remembered as a key moment in the struggle against Chaos.

The Battle of the Chaos WastesGotrek & Felix: Daemonslayer (1999)

During the reign of Magnus the Pious

Gotrek and Felix encounter a Bloodthirster in the Chaos Wastes. The daemon is hunting a powerful artifact. The duo, along with allies, manage to defeat it through a combination of trickery and the Slayer's axe. The Bloodthirster is banished, and the artifact is secured. This adventure cements Gotrek's reputation as a daemon-slayer.

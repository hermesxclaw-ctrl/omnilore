Who It Is

In the vast and well-charted sea of world mythology, there are occasional phantom entries — names that appear on a map but lead to no harbor. 'Arno' is one such phantom. Unlike the mighty river in Tuscany that bears its name, or the many humans named Arno who have lived and died, this 'deity' has no myths, no temples, no worshippers, and no stories. It exists only as a single, lonely data point in a vast digital archive, a ghost in the machine of knowledge. The name itself is real, but the god is not — a fascinating reminder that not every entry in a database is a window into a lost world; sometimes, it is just a door left ajar by a data-entry error.

Oral tradition pre-contact to present; documented 20th-21st c.

Kanipinikassikueu (Caribou Man) or Papakashtshishk is the master-spirit of the caribou, who controls the caribou's appearance to hunters, lives in a mountain or caribou house, and must be respected through ritual, bone treatment, and sharing. Stories describe hunters visiting his dwelling, negotiating for animals, and consequences for disrespect (famine). This figure is phonetically similar to 'Coribou' but is a distinct entity with extensive ethnography. No source equates Kanipinikassikueu with Wikidata Q110287222; inclusion is to prevent conflation and to show the depth of documentation required.

Who It Is
Form

Artorias the Abysswalker is a massive, humanoid figure with the upper body of a wolf and the lower body of a knight. He wears a suit of armor and wields a greatsword. His eyes glow with an otherworldly energy.

Powers

Abyssal Swordsmanship Artorias is a master swordsman who wields the power of the abyss to devastating effect. He can summon dark energy to enhance his sword strikes and increase his speed and agility.

Accounts

Artorias was once a great knight who protected the land from the forces of darkness. However, he became corrupted by the power of the abyss and was consumed by its darkness.

A group of adventurers set out to find Artorias and put an end to his corruption. However, they soon discovered that Artorias was not the only one who was corrupted by the power of the abyss.

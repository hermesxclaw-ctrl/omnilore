Who It Is

A man working the night shift at a gym discovers that the showers turn on by themselves at exactly 2:17 AM. Every night. He investigates and finds that the water is not water — it is tears. The shower drains lead not to plumbing but to a vast underground chamber filled with the crying faces of everyone who has ever wept alone. The entity that collects the tears — 'The Collector' — notices him watching. It speaks without a mouth: 'Everyone cries alone eventually. I am just making sure someone is there to collect what falls.' The narrator quits his job. He still hears the showers turn on at 2:17 AM, no matter where he is.

Form

The showers: a standard gym locker room, tiled white, with eight shower stalls. At 2:17 AM, all eight turn on simultaneously. The water is warm and salty. The Collector is glimpsed through the steam — a tall, thin silhouette with arms that are too long and fingers that end in small glass vials, collecting the tears.

Powers

Tear collection from across space and time

Perpetual auditory haunting of those who witness it

Omnipresence at locations where people cry alone

Cultural Significance

The Showers is a rare example of creepypasta that derives horror not from violence but from loneliness — the idea that someone is collecting your tears, that your private grief is being catalogued by something that calls it beautiful.

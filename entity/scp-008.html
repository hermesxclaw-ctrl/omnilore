Who It Is

SCP-008 is a complex prion that causes complete biological and behavioral transformation in infected humans, effectively creating zombies. The prion was originally isolated from a biological sample recovered in ██████, believed to have been developed as a bioweapon. Exposure to SCP-008 results in a progression of symptoms over approximately 24 hours: initial flu-like symptoms give way to necrosis, followed by complete neural restructuring that eliminates higher brain functions while enhancing aggression, strength, and pain tolerance. The infected (designated SCP-008-1) are not 'undead' in the supernatural sense — they are living humans whose brains have been hijacked by a prion that prioritizes feeding and spreading the infection above all else. They retain basic motor functions and problem-solving abilities related to hunting and propagation. The transformation is 100% fatal to the original personality and is currently irreversible, though SCP-500 has shown limited effectiveness in early-stage infections. SCP-008 is highly contagious, transmitted through bodily fluids and surface contact with contaminated materials. The Foundation maintains the only known sample of the original SCP-008 prion in a Class 4 biohazard containment facility, with strict protocols requiring hazmat suits, decontamination showers, and armed response teams on standby. SCP-008 was one of the first major SCPs to enter wider popular culture through the SCP: Containment Breach game, where it serves as a recurring threat and infection vector. The entity represents the Foundation's take on the zombie apocalypse genre — grounded in scientific plausibility (prions are real, terrifying proteins that cause brain degeneration) while maintaining the visceral horror of infectious transformation. The knowledge that the Foundation holds the seed of a zombie apocalypse in a vault, and that a single breached vial could end civilization, makes SCP-008 a constant, quiet nightmare.

Who It Is

Scáthach is the warrior-woman of Skye who trained heroes. Her most famous pupil was Cú Chulainn, whom she taught the gáe bolga — a barbed spear removable only by cutting flesh. She foretold his brief but glorious destiny. She represents the female warrior-mentor archetype.

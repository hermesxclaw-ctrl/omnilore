Who It Is

In the kingdom of Lugunica, where the sword is law and the past is a blade that never dulls, Wilhelm van Astrea stands as a living legend—a man who traded his future for a promise, and whose name is spoken with both reverence and regret.

Wilhelm is stoic, disciplined, and highly honorable, adhering to a strict code of chivalry. He is deeply respectful of his opponents and values loyalty and duty above all. He carries a heavy burden of guilt and grief over the death of his wife, Theresia, which shapes his actions and motivations. He is also known for his dry wit and occasional moments of warmth, especially towards those he considers allies.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Wilhelm' is of Germanic origin, meaning 'resolute protector'. 'van Astrea' suggests a noble lineage, possibly derived from the Latin 'Astra' (stars) or the Dutch 'van' indicating 'from'. The title 'Sword Devil' (剣鬼, Kenki) is a moniker earned through his legendary swordsmanship.

Pronunciation — VIL-helm van AS-tree-ah

Aliases — The Sword Devil, The Sword Demon, The Old Man

Ownership — Fictional character from the Japanese light novel series 'Re:Zero - Starting Life in Another World' by Tappei Nagatsuki, with adaptations in anime, manga, and games.

Master Swordsmanship

Wilhelm is considered one of the greatest swordsmen in Lugunica, earning the title 'Sword Devil'. He is capable of defeating powerful foes with ease, and his skill has not diminished with age.

Limit: Age has taken a toll on his stamina, but his technique compensates.

Sword of the Astrea

He wields the family sword, a powerful weapon passed down through the Astrea line.

Limit: The sword is a physical weapon and can be lost or damaged.

Combat Experience

Wilhelm has decades of battle experience, giving him an almost instinctive ability to read opponents and anticipate their moves.

Limit: Experience can be countered by unpredictable or novel tactics.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Wilhelm is an elderly man with gray hair and a weathered face, often seen wearing a formal military-style uniform or simple clothing. He has a distinctive scar over his left eye, and his eyes are sharp and intense. Despite his age, he maintains a lean, muscular build, indicative of his lifelong dedication to the sword.

Symbols — The Astrea family crest, a stylized sword, is associated with Wilhelm. His sword, the 'Sword of the Astrea', is a family heirloom. He is also often depicted with a white scarf, which he wore in his youth.

Domains — Wilhelm is a knight of the Kingdom of Lugunica, serving the royal family and later the candidate Crusch Karsten. He resides in the royal capital, but is often on missions. He is a master of the sword, and his domain is the battlefield.

Fictional timeline of Re:Zero

Wilhelm's past is revealed: he was a swordsman who fell in love with Theresia van Astrea, a legendary sword saint. He defeated her in a duel to win her hand, but she died later in battle against the White Whale. Wilhelm was unable to protect her, and he has since dedicated his life to avenging her death. Wilhelm's obsession with revenge leads him to join the expedition to slay the White Whale, where he finally confronts his past.

Fictional timeline of Re:Zero

Wilhelm joins the military expedition to defeat the White Whale, a legendary beast that erases memories of those it kills. He plays a crucial role in the battle, finally avenging Theresia's death. The White Whale is slain, and Wilhelm finds a measure of peace, though he still mourns Theresia.

Who It Is

Imagine a goddess so obscure that she has never existed — yet her name echoes across a medieval-recreation kingdom, an Italian TV mystery, and a young-adult underwater city. Atlantia is less a deity and more a linguistic phantom: a word that sounds ancient, feels mythic, and carries the weight of Plato's lost island — but has no ancient voice of its own. She is the ghost of Atlantis, the personification of a name rather than a power, a figure who exists only in the spaces where modern imagination meets the void of history.

No attested behavior — no myths, cults, or rituals are recorded for any figure named 'Atlantia' in ancient sources. Modern depictions vary: in fiction, it may appear as a place (city, kingdom, realm) rather than an acting character. If considered a personification, its 'behavior' would be entirely conjectural and should not be fabricated.

Accounts I
The Atlantis Myth (foundational narrative, not 'Atlantia' specifically)Plato, Timaeus and Critias

c. 360 BCE

Plato recounts how Solon learned from Egyptian priests of a powerful island civilization beyond the Pillars of Hercules (Gibraltar) that existed 9,000 years before Solon's time. Atlantis was ruled by a confederation of kings descended from Poseidon and the mortal Cleito. It was a wealthy, technologically advanced, and militarily powerful state that attempted to conquer the Mediterranean. In a single day and night, Atlantis was destroyed by earthquakes and floods, sinking beneath the sea. Plato's account is widely regarded by scholars as a philosophical allegory or literary invention, not a historical record. The Atlantis myth has inspired thousands of books, films, games, and pseudoscientific theories. It has been interpreted as a memory of the Minoan eruption of Thera (Santorini), as a moral fable about hubris, and as a genuine lost civilization.

Kingdom of Atlantia (SCA)Society for Creative Anachronism records

1981-present

The Kingdom of Atlantia is one of the SCA's twenty kingdoms, covering Maryland, Virginia, North Carolina, South Carolina, and the District of Columbia. It was founded in 1981 and holds annual events, tournaments, and arts competitions. The name evokes the legendary Atlantis, giving the kingdom a romantic, mythic identity within the SCA's medieval-revival framework. The kingdom has developed its own heraldry, traditions, and internal history, becoming a real community institution.

Atlantia (2023 Italian TV series)Rai 2 broadcast, created by Roberto Saku Cinardi

2023

A contemporary fantasy-drama series set in a fictional coastal town called Atlantia, where supernatural events and mysteries unfold. The series ran for one season (12 episodes) and was not renewed. It features themes of mystery, family secrets, and supernatural phenomena. The series had modest viewership and was not renewed for a second season.

Atlantia (2014 novel by Ally Condie)Dutton Books

2014

A young adult dystopian-fantasy novel set in a fictional underwater city called Atlantia, where humans live beneath the sea. The protagonist, Rio, must navigate political intrigue and her own hidden powers. The title evokes Atlantis but the city is not a deity. The novel was a New York Times bestseller and received mixed reviews.

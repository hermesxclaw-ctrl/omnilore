Gohdan

also known asThe Great ArbiterGohdan the ArbiterArbiter of the Earth Temple
TypeZelda Expansion Entity
Who It Is
Form

Gohdan is a colossal, anthropomorphic stone-and-metal construct that dominates the boss arena of the Earth Temple. Its most striking feature is a massive, mask-like head that bears a strong resemblance to Inca and pre-Inca ceremonial masks, with large, hollow eye sockets and a wide, angular mouth. The head is supported by two enormous, detached hands that float independently, each with a single, glowing eye in the palm. The entire body is covered in intricate carvings and glyphs that evoke ancient Mesoamerican art, and its color palette is dominated by earthy tones of brown, gray, and gold. When damaged, Gohdan's head tilts back, revealing a dark interior from which it launches explosive fireballs. The hands are the primary weak points, and when their eyes are struck, they become temporarily disabled, causing them to droop and expose the central core of the head. The overall design is both imposing and enigmatic, blending mechanical precision with a sense of ancient, divine power.

Powers

Explosive Fireball Barrage Gohdan launches a series of large, explosive fireballs from its mouth. These fireballs travel in a spread pattern and explode on impact, dealing significant damage to Link. They are telegraphed by a glowing charge in Gohdan's mouth, giving the player time to dodge. This attack is used frequently throughout the fight and is particularly dangerous when combined with hand swipes.

Hand Swipe Gohdan uses its detached hands to swipe across the arena, attempting to crush Link. The hands move with surprising speed and can cover a wide area. Each hand acts independently, allowing for simultaneous attacks from both sides. This attack forces the player to stay mobile and time their dodges carefully.

Hand Slam Shockwave Gohdan slams one or both hands onto the ground, creating a shockwave that travels across the arena. The shockwave can be jumped over, but it covers a large area and can be difficult to avoid if the player is caught off guard. This attack is often used to punish players who stay in one place for too long.

Eye Vulnerability The eyes on Gohdan's hands are its only weak points. When struck with an arrow from Link's Bow, the eye is temporarily disabled, causing the hand to droop and become inert. This creates an opening for Link to attack the central core of Gohdan's head, which is otherwise invulnerable. The eyes regenerate after a short time, requiring the player to repeat the process.

Accounts

In The Wind Waker, Link travels to the Earth Temple on Headstone Island to retrieve the Earth God's Lyric, a sacred item needed to awaken the sages and defeat the villain Ganondorf. The temple is guarded by Gohdan, who serves as the final trial for those who seek the Lyric. Link must use his Bow to disable Gohdan's hand eyes, then strike the exposed core of its head. After a prolonged battle, Gohdan is destroyed, and Link obtains the Lyric, which is hidden behind the boss arena. This victory is a crucial step in Link's quest to save his sister and the world.

In this fan-created narrative, Gohdan is not merely a boss but a divine judge who tests the moral character of heroes. According to this interpretation, Gohdan's attacks are not meant to kill but to assess the hero's resolve and resourcefulness. The fireballs represent the trials of life, and the hands symbolize the weight of responsibility. Fans have written stories where Gohdan, after being defeated, acknowledges Link's worthiness and grants him a blessing beyond the Lyric, such as a hidden power or knowledge. This interpretation has gained traction because it adds depth to an otherwise silent boss, and it aligns with the game's themes of courage and growth.

Scholarly Debates
Sentience vs. Machine
Gohdan is a purely mechanical construct, programmed to guard the Earth Temple. Its behavior is repetitive and lacks any signs of consciousness, such as communication or emotion. ⇌ Gohdan is a divine being, possibly a deity or a manifestation of the Earth God. Its title 'The Great Arbiter' suggests it has a will and purpose beyond mere programming, and its design is too intricate to be a simple machine.
Cultural Origin
Gohdan's design is inspired by Inca and pre-Inca masks, as noted by fans and possibly by the developers. This is a deliberate homage to ancient American cultures. ⇌ Gohdan's design is original and not directly based on any specific culture. The resemblance to Inca masks is coincidental, and the developers drew from a general 'ancient civilization' aesthetic common in fantasy.
Role in the Timeline
Gohdan is a one-time boss with no significance beyond the Earth Temple. It is not mentioned in any other game or lore, so it is a minor character. ⇌ Gohdan's design and role as an 'arbiter' may be a precursor to the Zonai Constructs, suggesting a deeper connection to the Zelda timeline and the ancient history of Hyrule.
What We Don't Know

There is no official explanation for the etymology of the name 'Gohdan', and no developer commentary has been released specifically about this character. The cultural influences are noted by fans but not confirmed by Nintendo. Additionally, the exact nature of Gohdan's 'divine origin' is left vague, and it is unclear whether it is a unique creation or one of many such guardians. Future research could explore the development process of The Wind Waker, including any concept art or interviews with the designers, to shed light on these questions. Also, a comparative analysis of Gohdan and the Zonai Constructs could reveal intentional design connections.

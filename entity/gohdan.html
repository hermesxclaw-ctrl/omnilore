Who It Is

A massive, two-faced stone construct that serves as the guardian of sacred trials. Gohdan's right hand wields a hammer that can shatter the ground, while its left hand fires beams of energy. Both faces — one stern, one mocking — must be struck with return-fire to stun the colossus so its palms can be attacked.

The Approach

Leo is a name that roars across centuries and cultures, from the lion that guards the zodiac to the mascot that introduces Hollywood's golden age. But who is Leo? The answer depends on where you look—and that is precisely the puzzle.

Identity

Leo

Pronunciation English: /ˈliːoʊ/; French: /le.o/; Spanish: /ˈleo/

Known As

LéoLEOL.E.O.

Etymology

From Latin 'leo' meaning 'lion', derived from Greek 'λέων' (léon), ultimately from a pre-Greek substrate or Semitic root (cf. Hebrew 'lavi').

Cultural Ownership

Multiple cultures and languages; not owned by any single tradition.

Disputed Identifications

The name is used for many unrelated entities; confusion arises between the constellation, astrological sign, given name, and various fictional characters.

Description

Appearance

As a constellation, Leo is depicted as a lion, with bright stars including Regulus. As an astrological sign, it is symbolized by the lion. As a given name, no physical appearance.

Symbols & Iconography

Lion symbol, zodiac glyph ♌, constellation pattern, MGM lion mascot.

Behaviour & Personality

In astrology, Leos are often described as confident, charismatic, and leadership-oriented. In fiction, characters named Leo vary widely.

Domains & Habitats

Constellation in the northern sky; astrological sign; various fictional settings.

Who It Is
Form

Bunjil is often depicted as a wedge-tailed eagle in Australian Aboriginal mythology. He is also associated with the eaglehawk, a large and powerful bird of prey. In some stories, he is described as a wise and just leader, while in others he is depicted as a fierce and powerful warrior.

Powers

Creation Bunjil is said to have created the world and all living things. He is often depicted as a powerful and wise leader, with the ability to bring order and harmony to the world.

Accounts

In this story, Bunjil is depicted as a powerful and wise leader who creates the world and all living things. He is said to have brought order and harmony to the world, and is revered as a supreme creator deity.

In this story, Bunjil is depicted as a fierce and powerful warrior who battles against the opposing moiety. He is said to have emerged victorious, and is revered as a powerful and wise leader.

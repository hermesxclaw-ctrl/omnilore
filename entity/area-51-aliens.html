Who It Is
Form

Area 51 Aliens are often depicted as humanoid beings with elongated heads, large eyes, and pale skin. They are said to possess advanced technology and have been associated with various extraterrestrial life forms.

Powers

Teleportation Area 51 Aliens are said to possess the ability to teleport themselves and others from one location to another.

Accounts

The alleged crash of an extraterrestrial spacecraft in Roswell, New Mexico, in 1947, which led to the creation of the Area 51 Aliens myth.

The creation of the Area 51 facility and its association with extraterrestrial life forms.

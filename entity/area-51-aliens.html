Who It Is

The belief that the U.S. government stores and reverse-engineers alien technology and possibly alien bodies at Area 51, a classified Air Force testing facility in the Nevada desert. Bob Lazar claimed to have worked on nine alien spacecraft at a subsidiary site (S-4), learning that the craft operated via Element 115 and were piloted by 'the beings.'

Cultural Significance

Area 51 functions as a Rorschach test for attitudes toward government secrecy — it is simultaneously a national security necessity and the ultimate proof of the 'they're hiding something' worldview. The myth survived multiple official declassifications (2013 CIA acknowledgment) because the core narrative — 'they're still hiding the real truth' — is unfalsifiable.

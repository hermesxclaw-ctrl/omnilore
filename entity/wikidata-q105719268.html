The Approach

A white-armored warrior who exists outside time itself, Hakumen is a paradox made manifest—a hero who was once the villain, a man who is both dead and alive, and a guardian who fights to erase his own past. His blade, the Nox Nyctores Okami, is a covenant with death, and every swing of his sword is a declaration that the future will not be dictated by the Azure.

Content advisory. Contains themes of self-sacrifice, existential erasure, and identity loss. Depictions of combat violence and psychological torment are present within the source material.

Etymology

The name 'Hakumen' (ハクメン) is Japanese for 'white face' or 'white mask', likely referencing his white armor and mask-like helmet. It may also evoke the concept of a 'white knight' or a faceless, impersonal force of justice.

Cultural Ownership

BlazBlue is a Japanese fighting game franchise created by Arc System Works. Hakumen is a fictional character within that franchise, and his cultural ownership lies with Arc System Works and the character's creators.

Disputed Identifications

Hakumen is sometimes confused with other masked swordsmen in anime and games, but within the BlazBlue canon, his identity is clearly established as an alternate version of Jin Kisaragi from a different time cycle. However, some fan theories have speculated that he might be a separate entity or a future version of Ragna, though these are not supported by official sources.

Description

Appearance

Hakumen is a tall, imposing figure clad in white and black armor with gold trim. His helmet is a full-face mask with a glowing red visor that reveals only his eyes. He carries a large, ornate sword called Okami, which is a Nox Nyctores. His design evokes a samurai or knight, with a flowing white cape and intricate engravings on his armor.

Symbols & Iconography

Hakumen's armor bears the symbol of the Six Heroes, a group he belonged to in the past. His sword, Okami, is a symbol of his covenant with the Boundary, and its power is tied to his own life force. The white color of his armor is often associated with purity and justice, but also with emptiness and the void.

Behaviour & Personality

Hakumen is stoic, disciplined, and speaks in a formal, archaic manner. He is driven by a strong sense of justice and duty, but his methods are often ruthless and uncompromising. He is haunted by his past as Jin Kisaragi, and his actions are motivated by a desire to prevent the tragedies he once caused. He is often solitary, but respects those who show strength and conviction.

Domains & Habitats

Hakumen exists outside the normal flow of time, residing in the Boundary, a void between worlds. He can appear anywhere in the BlazBlue universe, often intervening at critical moments. He is not bound by the physical limitations of the mortal world.

Version Differences

In Calamity Trigger, Hakumen is a mysterious and antagonistic figure who fights Ragna. In Continuum Shift, his backstory is expanded, and he becomes a more sympathetic character. In Chrono Phantasma and Central Fiction, he plays a more central role in the story, and his relationship with Jin and Ragna is explored in depth. His fighting style remains largely consistent, but his moveset evolves with each game.

Powers & Abilities

Nox Nyctores Okami

Source Version All appearances

Description

Hakumen's weapon, a massive sword that can channel his spiritual energy. It is a Nox Nyctores, a weapon designed to combat supernatural threats. Okami can absorb and release energy, and its power is linked to Hakumen's own life force.

Limitations

Using Okami's full power can drain Hakumen's life force, and overuse can lead to his death or disappearance.

Contradictions

Some sources suggest that Okami is a copy of the original Nox Nyctores, while others treat it as the original. This is not fully clarified.

Time Manipulation

Source Version Continuum Shift onwards

Description

Hakumen can manipulate time, but his powers are limited. He can create time loops, freeze time in localized areas, and travel through time, but these abilities are not absolute and can be resisted by other powerful beings.

Limitations

His time powers are often used for specific purposes, such as preserving the timeline or creating a stable time loop. He cannot casually alter the past without consequences.

Contradictions

The extent of his time manipulation is not consistently depicted. Sometimes he appears to be able to rewind time, while other times he cannot prevent events from occurring.

Zanshin (Sword Techniques)

Source Version All appearances

Description

Hakumen is a master swordsman, using a style that emphasizes powerful, precise strikes. His attacks are slow but devastating, and he can counter enemy attacks with ease.

Limitations

His fighting style relies on timing and prediction, making him vulnerable to fast, unpredictable opponents.

Contradictions

None known.

Boundary Connection

Source Version All appearances

Description

Hakumen draws power from the Boundary, a realm of nothingness. This grants him enhanced strength and durability, and allows him to survive injuries that would be fatal to others.

Limitations

His connection to the Boundary also means that he is constantly at risk of being consumed by it or losing his sense of self.

Contradictions

The exact nature of his connection is vague, and it is unclear if he can be killed permanently.

Stories & Myths

The Hero of Ikaruga

Source BlazBlue: Calamity Trigger, BlazBlue: Continuum ShiftCulture Japanese video gamePeriod Past time cycle

Summary

In a previous time cycle, Hakumen was originally Jin Kisaragi, a young soldier who fought in the Ikaruga Civil War. He was a hero who fought against the Black Beast, but was corrupted by the influence of Terumi and the Nox Nyctores Yukianesa. He killed his sister Saya and lost his arm, leading to his eventual transformation into Hakumen. He was recruited by the Six Heroes and trained to become a powerful warrior, but he was ultimately defeated and sent to the Boundary, where he existed outside of time.

Murder of Saya

Source BlazBlue: Continuum Shift, Chrono Phantasma, Central Fiction

Description

In the past, Jin Kisaragi, under the influence of Terumi, killed his sister Saya. This act is a central trauma for Hakumen, who is the same person but from a different timeline. Hakumen carries the guilt of this murder and seeks to prevent it from happening again.

Scholarly Context

The murder is presented as a tragedy caused by external manipulation and internal weakness, exploring themes of guilt and redemption.

Self-Destruction and Erasure

Source BlazBlue: Central Fiction

Description

Hakumen's existence is a paradox; he is a being who should not exist. He willingly sacrifices himself at the end of Central Fiction, erasing himself from the timeline. This act is both heroic and tragic, as he ceases to exist entirely.

Scholarly Context

The concept of self-erasure as atonement is a recurring theme in Japanese media, often tied to the idea of removing one's own negative influence from the world.

Psychological Torment

Source BlazBlue: Continuum Shift, Chrono Phantasma

Description

Hakumen is constantly tormented by his memories of the past and his role in the tragedies that shaped the world. His stoic demeanor masks a deep psychological pain.

Scholarly Context

This adds depth to his character, making him more than just a typical 'cool' warrior archetype.

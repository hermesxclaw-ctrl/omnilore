Who It Is

A white-armored warrior who exists outside time itself, Hakumen is a paradox made manifest—a hero who was once the villain, a man who is both dead and alive, and a guardian who fights to erase his own past. His blade, the Nox Nyctores Okami, is a covenant with death, and every swing of his sword is a declaration that the future will not be dictated by the Azure.

Hakumen is stoic, disciplined, and speaks in a formal, archaic manner. He is driven by a strong sense of justice and duty, but his methods are often ruthless and uncompromising. He is haunted by his past as Jin Kisaragi, and his actions are motivated by a desire to prevent the tragedies he once caused. He is often solitary, but respects those who show strength and conviction.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Hakumen' (ハクメン) is Japanese for 'white face' or 'white mask', likely referencing his white armor and mask-like helmet. It may also evoke the concept of a 'white knight' or a faceless, impersonal force of justice.

Pronunciation — HAH-koo-men

Aliases — White Knight, The Hero of Ikaruga, Jin Kisaragi (past incarnation), The Guardian of the Boundary

Ownership — BlazBlue is a Japanese fighting game franchise created by Arc System Works. Hakumen is a fictional character within that franchise, and his cultural ownership lies with Arc System

Powers
Nox Nyctores Okami

Hakumen's weapon, a massive sword that can channel his spiritual energy. It is a Nox Nyctores, a weapon designed to combat supernatural threats. Okami can absorb and release energy, and its power is linked to Hakumen's own life force.

Limit: Using Okami's full power can drain Hakumen's life force, and overuse can lead to his death or disappearance.

Time Manipulation

Hakumen can manipulate time, but his powers are limited. He can create time loops, freeze time in localized areas, and travel through time, but these abilities are not absolute and can be resisted by other powerful beings.

Limit: His time powers are often used for specific purposes, such as preserving the timeline or creating a stable time loop. He cannot casually alter the past without consequences.

Zanshin (Sword Techniques)

Hakumen is a master swordsman, using a style that emphasizes powerful, precise strikes. His attacks are slow but devastating, and he can counter enemy attacks with ease.

Limit: His fighting style relies on timing and prediction, making him vulnerable to fast, unpredictable opponents.

Boundary Connection

Hakumen draws power from the Boundary, a realm of nothingness. This grants him enhanced strength and durability, and allows him to survive injuries that would be fatal to others.

Limit: His connection to the Boundary also means that he is constantly at risk of being consumed by it or losing his sense of self.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Hakumen is a tall, imposing figure clad in white and black armor with gold trim. His helmet is a full-face mask with a glowing red visor that reveals only his eyes. He carries a large, ornate sword called Okami, which is a Nox Nyctores. His design evokes a samurai or knight, with a flowing white cape and intricate engravings on his armor.

Symbols — Hakumen's armor bears the symbol of the Six Heroes, a group he belonged to in the past. His sword, Okami, is a symbol of his covenant with the Boundary, and its power is tied to his own life force. The white color of his armor is often associated with purity and justice, but also with emptiness and the void.

Domains — Hakumen exists outside the normal flow of time, residing in the Boundary, a void between worlds. He can appear anywhere in the BlazBlue universe, often intervening at critical moments. He is not bound by the physical limitations of the mortal world.

Accounts I
The Hero of IkarugaBlazBlue: Calamity Trigger, BlazBlue: Continuum Shift

Past time cycle

In a previous time cycle, Hakumen was originally Jin Kisaragi, a young soldier who fought in the Ikaruga Civil War. He was a hero who fought against the Black Beast, but was corrupted by the influence of Terumi and the Nox Nyctores Yukianesa. He killed his sister Saya and lost his arm, leading to his eventual transformation into Hakumen. He was recruited by the Six Heroes and trained to become a powerful warrior, but he was ultimately defeated and sent to the Boundary, where he existed outside of time. Hakumen's past actions set the stage for the main events of the series. His existence as a time-displaced version of Jin creates a complex relationship with his present self and with Ragna.

The Battle Against RagnaBlazBlue: Calamity Trigger

Main timeline

Hakumen first appears as an antagonist, hunting Ragna the Bloodedge. He believes that Ragna is a threat to the world and attempts to kill him. Their battles are intense, and Hakumen's power proves to be a match for Ragna's Azure Grimoire. Their rivalry continues throughout the series, but eventually evolves into a reluctant alliance as they face greater threats.

Accounts II
The Revelation of His IdentityBlazBlue: Continuum Shift

Main timeline

It is revealed that Hakumen is actually a future version of Jin Kisaragi from a different time cycle. This revelation changes the dynamics between Hakumen, Jin, and Ragna, as they are all connected by blood and fate. Hakumen becomes more sympathetic, and his goal shifts to protecting the timeline and his younger self.

The Final Battle Against TerumiBlazBlue: Central Fiction

End of the series

Hakumen, along with Jin and Ragna, confronts Terumi in a final battle. Hakumen sacrifices himself to help Ragna defeat Terumi and seal away the Azure. He disappears from the world, erasing himself from existence to prevent the Azure from being misused. Hakumen's sacrifice is a key part of the series' climax. His death is a poignant moment, as he finally atones for his past sins.

Who It Is

A plain cardboard box, originally containing origami paper dragons that would animate and fly playfully when the box was opened. The dragons were friendly, childlike, and delighted in performing aerial acrobatics for observers. Over time, SCP-1762's dragons showed increasing signs of distress and fear, and the interior of the box began to show a dark, threatening sky. The final time the box was opened, a single origami dragon delivered a written message: 'We are sorry. We couldn't stop them. Please remember us.' The dragons never returned.

Form

A worn cardboard box labeled 'HERE BE DRAGONS' in a child's handwriting. The interior originally contained dozens of brightly colored origami paper dragons ranging from 2-5 cm. Now it contains only torn paper scraps and a faint smell of smoke.

Powers

Formerly: origami dragon animation, flight, intelligent behavior; Currently: none

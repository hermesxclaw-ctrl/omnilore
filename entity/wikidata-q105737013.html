The Approach

In the world of BlazBlue, where most warriors wield ancient weapons or supernatural powers, Makoto Nanaya stands out as a whirlwind of raw energy and unapologetic enthusiasm. She's the friend who'll drag you into a fight, the one who punches first and asks questions never, and the squirrel girl with a heart as big as her fists. But beneath her sunny exterior lies a soldier with a mission, a protector torn between her duties and her loyalties, and a secret that could shake the foundations of her world.

Content advisory. This dossier contains discussion of violence, war, and psychological manipulation, typical of the BlazBlue series' darker themes. No explicit sexual content is depicted.
Identity

Makoto Nanaya

Known As

Etymology

Nanaya (ナナヤ) is a surname; Makoto (マコト) means 'truth' or 'sincerity' in Japanese. Her name likely reflects her straightforward and earnest personality.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese video game franchise, BlazBlue, created by Arc System Works.

Disputed Identifications

None recorded.

Description

Appearance

Makoto is a young woman with short, spiky brown hair and amber eyes. She has a petite but athletic build. Her most distinctive features are her squirrel-like tail and ears, which are brown with cream tips. She wears a white and red military-style uniform, with a short skirt, black thigh-high stockings, and combat boots. She often carries a large, mechanical gauntlet on her left arm.

Symbols & Iconography

Her symbol is the acorn, which appears on her uniform and in her attacks. She is associated with the color orange and the element of wind, though her powers are primarily physical.

Behaviour & Personality

Makoto is energetic, loud, and relentlessly optimistic. She is fiercely loyal to her friends, especially Noel Vermillion, and is always ready to defend them. She has a strong sense of justice and is not afraid to speak her mind, even to authority figures. Despite her cheerful demeanor, she can be serious and determined when the situation calls for it. She is also shown to be somewhat dense and easily distracted, but her heart is always in the right place.

Domains & Habitats

Makoto is a student at the Military Academy and later a member of the NOL (Novus Orbis Librarium). She is often seen in the Academy or on missions. She has no specific habitat, but she is most at home in combat zones.

Version Differences

In BlazBlue: Chrono Phantasma, Makoto is a playable character with a focus on close-range combat and 'Drive' attacks that involve her 'Squirrel' abilities. In BlazBlue: Central Fiction, her moveset is refined, and her story role expands. In the anime adaptation, her personality is consistent, but some scenes are condensed.

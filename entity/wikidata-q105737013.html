Who It Is

In the world of BlazBlue, where most warriors wield ancient weapons or supernatural powers, Makoto Nanaya stands out as a whirlwind of raw energy and unapologetic enthusiasm. She's the friend who'll drag you into a fight, the one who punches first and asks questions never, and the squirrel girl with a heart as big as her fists. But beneath her sunny exterior lies a soldier with a mission, a protector torn between her duties and her loyalties, and a secret that could shake the foundations of her world.

Makoto is energetic, loud, and relentlessly optimistic. She is fiercely loyal to her friends, especially Noel Vermillion, and is always ready to defend them. She has a strong sense of justice and is not afraid to speak her mind, even to authority figures. Despite her cheerful demeanor, she can be serious and determined when the situation calls for it. She is also shown to be somewhat dense and easily distracted, but her heart is always in the right place.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Nanaya (ナナヤ) is a surname; Makoto (マコト) means 'truth' or 'sincerity' in Japanese. Her name likely reflects her straightforward and earnest personality.

Pronunciation — MAH-ko-toh NAH-nah-yah

Aliases — Makoto, Squirrel Girl, The Girl with the Big Fists

Ownership — Japanese video game franchise, BlazBlue, created by Arc System Works.

Powers
Drive: Squirrel

Makoto's Drive ability allows her to charge her attacks by holding down the Drive button, unleashing powerful, wind-enhanced punches and kicks. She can also use her tail for additional attacks.

Limit: Requires charging to reach full power; leaves her vulnerable during charge.

Overdrive: Overdrive - Squirrel

During Overdrive, her Drive attacks become faster and stronger, and she gains new combo possibilities.

Limit: Limited duration; requires Overdrive gauge.

Distortion Drives

Makoto has several Distortion Drives, including 'Acorn Bomb', 'Wild Wild Squirrel', and 'Squirrel Storm'. These are powerful special moves that consume heat gauge.

Limit: Requires full heat gauge; can be blocked or dodged.

Astral Heat: Squirrel Finale

A powerful finishing move that triggers under specific conditions, often involving her tail and a giant acorn.

Limit: Requires 100% heat gauge and opponent at low health.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Makoto is a young woman with short, spiky brown hair and amber eyes. She has a petite but athletic build. Her most distinctive features are her squirrel-like tail and ears, which are brown with cream tips. She wears a white and red military-style uniform, with a short skirt, black thigh-high stockings, and combat boots. She often carries a large, mechanical gauntlet on her left arm.

Symbols — Her symbol is the acorn, which appears on her uniform and in her attacks. She is associated with the color orange and the element of wind, though her powers are primarily physical.

Domains — Makoto is a student at the Military Academy and later a member of the NOL (Novus Orbis Librarium). She is often seen in the Academy or on missions. She has no specific habitat, but she is most at home in combat zones.

Accounts I
The Heroine of the Military AcademyBlazBlue: Calamity Trigger (backstory)

Pre-series

Makoto is a student at the Military Academy, where she befriends Noel Vermillion. She is a skilled fighter and is known for her energetic personality. She becomes involved in the events of the series when she tries to protect Noel from the NOL's machinations. Sets up her role as Noel's loyal friend and ally.

Accounts II
The Ikaruga IncidentBlazBlue: Chrono Phantasma

During the main story

Makoto is sent to investigate the Ikaruga region, where she encounters Ragna and the others. She becomes embroiled in the conflict between the NOL and the rebels, and she must choose between her duty and her friends. She becomes a key ally to Ragna's group, helping them in their fight against Terumi and the NOL.

The Battle for the AzureBlazBlue: Central Fiction

Final arc

Makoto joins the fight against Terumi and the Embodiments of Desire. She plays a supporting role, using her strength to protect her friends and help them achieve their goals. Helps in the final battle that leads to Ragna's sacrifice and the resetting of the world.

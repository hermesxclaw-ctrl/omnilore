Who It Is

A purple feline toy and the central antagonist of Chapter 3: 'Deep Sleep.' CatNap is the last surviving member of the 'Smiling Critters' toy line and serves as a quasi-religious acolyte of 'The Prototype' — a mysterious entity controlling Playtime Co.'s remaining toys. CatNap produces a hallucinogenic red gas (Poppy Gas) that induces terrifying nightmares in its victims. It stalks the player through the underground facility, appearing as a massive, skeletal cat with a permanent smile.

Form

A massively oversized purple cat, approximately 4-5m tall, with a skeletal, emaciated frame, glowing white eyes, and a wide, toothy smile. Its limbs are unnaturally long and spindly. It wears a tattered moon-shaped pendant. Its mouth perpetually leaks the red hallucinogenic gas it uses to incapacitate prey.

Powers

Hallucinogenic gas production

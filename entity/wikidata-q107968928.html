Who It Is

On an island locked in endless winter where a tyrant king hoarded every doctor for himself, one dying quack with a skull-and-crossbones flag declared he could cure the country with cherry blossoms — and his last, terrible miracle was to teach a lonely blue-nosed reindeer how to be human.

Attested as eccentric, bombastic, wildly optimistic, and deliberately 'quackish' — he embraces the insult 'quack' (yabuisha) and insists a quack can still save lives through will. He is compassionate to outcasts (especially Chopper), theatrical, prone to grand speeches, and refuses to surrender to despair despite terminal illness and 30 years of failure. He is also stubborn, reckless, and willing to use deception (lying to Chopper about a cure) to protect others. Kureha calls him a fool but respects his spirit. His philosophy: no disease is incurable; medicine must heal

Powers
Frontier Medicine and Pharmacology (Quack Doctor Expertise)

Extensive, self-taught medical knowledge despite being labeled a quack. Can diagnose, treat wounds, create medicines from local herbs, and perform surgery in primitive conditions. Taught Chopper all foundational medical knowledge that later made Chopper a world-class doctor.

Limit: No formal accreditation; his remedies often fail, and he cannot cure his own terminal illness or Drum Island's systemic medical oppression. His success rate is low, which is why he is called yabuisha.

Sakura (Cherry Blossom) Research - Dust and Dye

30-year alchemical research to create a special dust (sakura powder) that when released into cold air causes snow to turn pink and fall like cherry blossoms. Intended as a panacea symbol to 'cure' Drum Island's despair. At death, the powder successfully blooms, turning the snow around Drum Castle pink.

Limit: Not a real cure for any disease; purely symbolic/psychological. Requires specific atmospheric conditions and large quantity of powder. Took 30 years to perfect and was only completed posthumously via Kureha/Chopper releasing it.

Charisma, Willpower and Ideological Influence

Extraordinary ability to inspire others through sheer conviction. His philosophy that 'a man dies when he is forgotten' and his Jolly Roger directly shape Chopper's dream to become a doctor who can cure any disease and Dalton's later political reform of Sakura Kingdom. Survives 30 years of ridicule without abandoning research.

Limit: No superhuman combat power; physically frail and dying. Influence is posthumous and moral, not coercive.

Thievery and Survival Skills (Former Bandit)

In his youth before becoming a doctor, Hiriluk was a thief/bandit. Implied proficiency in burglary, evasion, and survival in harsh winter. This past is why he initially stole and later repented, turning to medicine after witnessing the cherry blossoms of the West.

Limit: Abandoned criminal life decades before main story; no combat feats shown in present. Details of his thief career are sparse and never elaborated.

Accounts I
The Thief Who Saw Cherry BlossomsOne Piece manga Chapter 140-141

2000 (in-universe ~30 years before Drum Island Arc, c. 30 years pre-1522 Sea Calendar)

Thirty years before the main story, Hiriluk is a thief dying of an unspecified illness in the West. He witnesses cherry blossoms blooming and is spiritually revived, deciding to become a doctor who can 'cure the country' and declaring that cherry blossoms can heal a frozen heart. He abandons thievery and begins medical research on Drum Island. Establishes his 30-year obsession with sakura research and his conversion from criminal to doctor; explains his Jolly Roger and lifelong motto.

The Doctor Hunt and the Quack's ExileOne Piece manga Chapters 134-136, 138

2000 (in-universe ~6 years before present, during Wapol's reign)

King Wapol of Drum Kingdom institutes the Doctor Hunt, conscripting the 20 best doctors (Isshi-20) into his exclusive service and criminalizing all other medicine. Hiriluk and his colleague Dr. Kureha are the only doctors who defy the order, becoming outlaws. Hiriluk continues to treat the poor for free, earning the derisive title 'quack' while Kureha pursues eternal youth research. Isolates Drum's populace from healthcare, sets up Hiriluk's status as folk hero/outlaw, and deepens his rivalry/friendship with Kureha.

Adopting Chopper - The Blue-Nosed ReindeerOne Piece manga Chapters 136-142

2000 (in-universe ~6 years before present)

Hiriluk finds Tony Tony Chopper, a reindeer who ate the Hito Hito no Mi (Human-Human Fruit) and was ostracized by both reindeer herd and humans for his blue nose and transformations. Hiriluk welcomes him, names him, gives him his hat with the cherry blossom Jolly Roger, and raises him as a son for one year, teaching him medicine, language, and that he is not alone. Chopper gains father figure, medical apprenticeship, and life purpose; Hiriluk's will is literally passed to Chopper, who later joins Straw Hats as ship's doctor to fulfill Hiriluk's dream of curing any disease.

Accounts II
The Thirty-Year Sakura PowderOne Piece manga Chapter 141

2000

For 30 years Hiriluk works to create a powder that will make snow fall like cherry blossoms, believing that if Drum Island blooms with sakura, the people's 'frozen hearts' will be cured. He repeatedly fails, is mocked by villagers and Kureha, but refuses to quit, continuing experiments in his lab even as his illness worsens. Powder is finally perfected at the time of his death; later released by Kureha/Chopper via cannon from Drum Castle, it transforms Drum Island into the Sakura Kingdom, fulfilling his symbolic cure.

The Final Lie and Suicide at Drum CastleOne Piece manga Chapters 142-143

2000 (in-universe ~6 years before present, one year after adopting Chopper)

Diagnosed with 10 days to live, Hiriluk learns Wapol is gravely ill (a trap). Knowing it is a lie but wanting to make his final days meaningful, he drinks a poisoned sake and rushes to the castle to 'treat' Wapol, telling Chopper he is going to cure the king. At the castle, Chess and Kuromarimo reveal the deception and Wapol orders his execution. Hiriluk, already dying from poison, declares his final philosophy — 'When does a man die? When he is forgotten!' — and blows himself up with a bomb, choosing suicide over being killed, to teach Chopper that his death is not in vain. Hiriluk's death traumatizes and motivates Chopper; Kureha takes Chopper as apprentice; Dalton begins to doubt Wapol; Hiriluk's cherry blossoms later bloom as his legacy. His death is cited by Chopper as his most formative trauma and inspiration.

The Bloom - Hiriluk's Cherry Blossoms After DeathOne Piece manga Chapters 144-145, 153

2000 (in-universe present, Drum Island Arc climax)

After Wapol's defeat by Luffy, Dalton and the Drum people witness Kureha firing Hiriluk's sakura powder from Drum Castle's cannons. The snow turns pink and falls like cherry blossoms across the island. The country is renamed Sakura Kingdom in his honor. Chopper, now Straw Hat doctor, carries Hiriluk's flag and dream to the sea. Posthumous vindication; Drum Island's national identity transformed; Hiriluk's will lives on through Chopper's medical dream and the kingdom's new name, referenced again in later cover stories and Chapter 1000+ callbacks.

Hiriluk is Chopper's biological father or a reindeer — Hiriluk is a human who adopted Chopper (a reindeer who ate the Human-Human Fruit). No blood relation; Chopper's biological parents are reindeer who abandoned him.

Hiriluk's sakura powder cured his terminal illness or could cure any disease — The powder has no medicinal properties; it dyes snow pink. Hiriluk died of his disease/poison before it was completed. His dream was symbolic — to cure the country's despair, not to create a panacea.

Hiriluk was a great, successful doctor — Canon explicitly labels him a yabuisha (quack) with low success rate. His value is in compassion and will, not skill. Kureha is the skilled doctor; Hiriluk is the inspirational one.

Hiriluk appears in the live-action Netflix One Piece — As of Season 1 (2023) and Season 2 in production (2026), Drum Island has not been adapted in live-action. Hiriluk has not been cast or appeared.

The Approach

Lux is not a character in a story, but a measure of light itself—the SI unit of illuminance, quantifying how much visible light falls on a surface. It is the language of brightness, from the faint glow of a distant star to the blazing noon sun, and it shapes how we perceive and engineer the world around us.

Etymology

From Latin 'lux', meaning 'light'.

Cultural Ownership

International System of Units (SI); defined by the General Conference on Weights and Measures (CGPM).

Disputed Identifications

The term 'lux' is also used for other unrelated things (e.g., the luxury brand, the character from 'League of Legends', the unit of length in some contexts), but these are not the SI unit.

Domains & Habitats

Version Differences

The definition has remained stable as 1 lm/m² since its adoption, but the underlying photometric standards (luminosity function) have been updated over time (e.g., CIE 1931, CIE 1978, CIE 2018).

Powers & Abilities

Measurement of illuminance

Source Version SI definition

Description

Quantifies luminous flux per unit area, with 1 lx = 1 lm/m².

Limitations

Only measures visible light as perceived by the human eye; not applicable to non-visible wavelengths.

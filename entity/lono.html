Who It Is
Form

Lono is often depicted as a handsome man with a gentle expression, associated with fertility, agriculture, and peace.

Powers

Fertility and Agriculture Lono has the power to bring fertility and abundance to the land, ensuring a successful harvest and peaceful times for the Hawaiian people.

Accounts

Lono descends to Earth on a rainbow to marry Laka, a fertility goddess, and bring fertility and peace to the Hawaiian people.

The Makahiki festival is held in honor of Lono, during which war and unnecessary work are taboo, and the people focus on agriculture and fertility.

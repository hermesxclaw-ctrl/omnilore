Form & Signs

Appearance — Lono is often depicted as a handsome man with a gentle expression, associated with fertility, agriculture, and peace.

Symbols — The god Lono is often associated with the rainbow, which is seen as a symbol of his power to bring fertility and peace.

Domains — Lono's domains include agriculture, fertility, and peace, which are all closely tied to the natural world.

Accounts I
The Descent of LonoHawaiian mythology

Pre-colonial period

Lono descends to Earth on a rainbow to marry Laka, a fertility goddess, and bring fertility and peace to the Hawaiian people. The union of Lono and Laka brings fertility and peace to the land.

Accounts II
The Makahiki FestivalHawaiian mythology

Pre-colonial period

The Makahiki festival is held in honor of Lono, during which war and unnecessary work are taboo, and the people focus on agriculture and fertility. The festival ensures a successful harvest and peaceful times for the Hawaiian people.

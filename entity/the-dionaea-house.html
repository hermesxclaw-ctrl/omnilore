Who It Is

One of the earliest viral internet horror stories, predating the term 'creepypasta.' Presented as a series of interconnected blog posts, emails, and journal entries chronicling the investigation into a house that is not a house — it is a predatory organism that lures people inside and consumes them. The house appears in different locations across the country, always empty, always waiting. Those who enter never leave. Those who investigate the disappearances eventually enter themselves.

Form

The Dionaea House appears as an unremarkable suburban home, varying in architectural style but always featuring certain consistent details: a specific shade of yellow in the kitchen, a basement that is deeper than the house's footprint allows, and a single room that no one can remember the purpose of. The house is warm. It smells faintly of something sweet. It feels like home.

Predatory mimicry of domestic architecture

Cultural Significance

The Dionaea House is arguably the first viral internet horror narrative, predating Slenderman by five years. Its multimedia format — blog posts, emails, IM logs — created the template for transmedia horror storytelling online.

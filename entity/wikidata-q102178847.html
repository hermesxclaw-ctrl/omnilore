Who It Is

In the bustling, often brutal world of Orario, where gods walk among mortals and adventurers descend into the Dungeon's depths to seek glory and treasure, one small supporter stands out not for her combat prowess but for her unyielding spirit and sharp wit. Liliruca Arde, a diminutive Pallum with a hidden past, navigates a society that often overlooks her kind, yet she carves her own legend through cunning, loyalty, and an unbreakable will to protect those she holds dear.

Initially, Liliruca is distrustful, cynical, and self-serving due to a traumatic past as a slave and a supporter who was exploited by adventurers. She is pragmatic, often calculating, and uses her intelligence and agility to survive. Over time, she develops loyalty and warmth, especially toward Bell Cranel, her first true friend. She becomes protective, brave, and willing to sacrifice for others. She retains a sharp tongue and a tendency to be overly cautious, but her growth is a central theme.

Powers
Supporter Skills

As a supporter, Liliruca is skilled in carrying loot, managing supplies, and navigating the Dungeon efficiently. She can quickly collect items and magic stones, and she is adept at identifying valuable items.

Limit: She is not a frontline combatant; her physical combat abilities are weak compared to adventurers.

Agility and Stealth

Like many Pallum, Liliruca is exceptionally fast and agile. She uses this to evade enemies, scout ahead, and perform hit-and-run tactics. She is also skilled in stealth and pickpocketing.

Limit: Her physical strength is low; she cannot endure heavy hits.

Cinder Ella (Magic Item)

A magic item that allows her to transform into a cat-person (Nekomimi) form, granting her enhanced agility, balance, and night vision. The transformation also changes her appearance, making her look like a cat-eared girl.

Limit: The transformation is temporary and may drain stamina. She cannot use it indefinitely.

Fire Bolt (via Magic Stone)

In some instances, she uses a magic stone to cast a weak Fire Bolt spell, but this is not her inherent ability and is limited by the stone's power.

Limit: Requires a magic stone; not reliable.

Tactical Mind

Liliruca is highly intelligent and strategic. She can devise plans for survival, resource management, and even combat support, often thinking several steps ahead.

Limit: Her plans can be overly cautious or pessimistic.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Liliruca is a small Pallum (a race resembling halflings) with silver-white hair and red eyes. She typically wears a hooded cloak and a supporter's outfit, which includes a backpack and various pouches for carrying loot and supplies. Her appearance is often described as cute but with a somewhat cynical or guarded expression.

Symbols — She is often associated with her supporter's toolbelt and her transformed form as a cat-person (Nekomimi) when she uses the Cinder Ella magic item. Her symbol might include the Pallum race's association with agility and thievery, but no specific personal iconography is documented.

Domains — She lives in the city of Orario, primarily in the Soma Familia's home before leaving, and later becomes a member of the Hestia Familia. She frequents the Dungeon as a supporter, and her daily life revolves around adventuring and supporting Bell.

Accounts I
The Supporter's PastLight Novel Volume 2 and Anime Season 1

Within the timeline of DanMachi

Liliruca was born into poverty and sold into slavery by her parents. She was bought by the Soma Familia, where she was forced to work as a supporter in the Dungeon. She was exploited by adventurers who stole her earnings and treated her as disposable. She developed a cynical and distrustful worldview, believing that all adventurers were selfish and cruel. Her traumatic experiences made her initially manipulative and self-serving, but also resilient and resourceful.

Meeting Bell CranelLight Novel Volume 2, Anime Season 1 episodes 6-7

Within the timeline of DanMachi

Liliruca attempts to steal from Bell Cranel, but he catches her and instead of punishing her, he offers her a partnership. He treats her with kindness and respect, which she initially distrusts. Over time, she becomes his dedicated supporter and gradually opens up to him. This marks the beginning of her redemption arc and her eventual departure from the Soma Familia to join the Hestia Familia.

Accounts II
The Soma Familia ConflictLight Novel Volume 5, Anime Season 1 episodes 10-13

Within the timeline of DanMachi

Liliruca is forced to continue working for the Soma Familia, which is in turmoil due to the addictive nature of Soma's divine wine. She is coerced into betraying Bell, but ultimately chooses to side with him, leading to a confrontation that results in her being freed from the Familia. Liliruca officially becomes a member of the Hestia Familia, gaining a new family and a fresh start.

The War Game and BeyondLight Novel Volumes 7-8, Anime Season 2

Within the timeline of DanMachi

Liliruca participates in the War Game against Apollo Familia, using her wits to support Bell. She also becomes involved in various adventures, including the search for the lost city of the Xenos and the battle against the Black Goliath. Her loyalty and bravery are further solidified, and she becomes a trusted member of the team.

The Xenos ArcLight Novel Volumes 11-12, Anime Season 3

Within the timeline of DanMachi

Liliruca struggles with the moral implications of Bell's decision to protect the Xenos, intelligent monsters. She is initially fearful and conflicted but ultimately supports Bell, showing her growth in empathy and courage. Her character develops further, and she becomes more accepting of those who are different.

We are Nature's Mockery. We speak for the Iris. The sky is not empty. The stars are not distant. They are close. They are watching. And they are hungry.

Who It Is

Nature's Mockery are perhaps the most overtly terrifying entities in Gemini Home Entertainment — humanoid constructs created by the Iris to deliver messages to humanity. They appear in what may be the series's most famous tape: 'Home Invasion Help,' ostensibly an instructional video about what to do during a home invasion. The video demonstrates that 'Nature's Mockery' are creatures that look almost human but are profoundly wrong. They appear nude, with chalky white or gray skin, completely featureless or unnervingly blank faces, and they move with unnatural, puppet-like jerks and twitches. They enter homes not to kill but to deliver a message. They recite, in distorted, multi-layered voices, specific phrases about the Iris: 'The Iris watches. The Iris waits. The Iris is coming.' Their presence causes intense psychological distress in witnesses. The entities are described as 'mockeries' because they are the Iris's attempt to create something human-like — but they lack the fundamental quality of being alive. They are empty vessels, biological marionettes controlled by the Iris. The 'Home Invasion Help' tape instructs viewers that if a Nature's Mockery enters their home, they should not engage, not speak, and above all, not listen to the message — because understanding is dangerous. The entity concept draws on the fear of home invasion, the uncanny valley, and the horror of being forced to witness something you don't want to understand. Nature's Mockery represent the Iris's direct communication with humanity — not an attack, but an announcement. And the fact that a cosmic entity has sent messengers to Earth to deliver a message is far more frightening than any attack would be.

Who It Is

She is ten years old, giggles as she calls a teenage junk-collector "big brother," and can kill you with a swarm of autonomous funnels before you finish aiming. Elpeo Ple — the first Ple — is Gundam ZZ's most disarming contradiction: a child weapon who defects not for ideology but for affection, and whose brief, bright obsession changes the final months of the First Neo Zeon War.

Attested: Ple is exuberant, impulsive, possessive, and emotionally volatile — consistent with both her chronological age and laboratory upbringing. Initially loyal to Glemy Toto as a father-figure/commander, she switches attachment rapidly to Judau Ashta after sensing his powerful Newtype presence and kindness, calling him 'Onii-chan' (big brother). She is jealous, demanding, and prone to tantrums when denied attention, but also capable of genuine empathy and self-sacrifice. Powers
Artificial Newtype (Kyōka Newtype) Awareness

Heightened spatial awareness, empathy, and precognition typical of Newtypes, artificially induced via bioengineering and drug/training regimen. Can sense other Newtypes, hostile intent, and battlefield movements beyond visual range. Strong resonance with Judau Ashta and Ple Two.

Limit: Less stable than natural Newtypes; emotionally labile, prone to psychic feedback and mental exhaustion. Cannot fully control powers without mobile suit amplifier. Vulnerable to stronger Newtype pressure (Haman Karn overwhelms her).

Qubeley Mk-II Funnel Control (Psycommu System)

Remote control of 30 funnel bits via psycommu system, enabling all-range attacks, defensive barriers, and coordinated swarm maneuvers. Ple's funnels are pink/white and deployed from wing binders.

Limit: Funnels require Newtype concentration; limited operation time and propellant; ineffective in heavy Minovsky particle interference or atmospheric entry without support; funnels can be shot down (Judau's ZZ High Mega Cannon destroys several).

Mobile Suit Piloting (Qubeley Mk-II)

Expert mobile suit pilot despite age, due to enhanced reflexes and Newtype spatial sense. Capable of soloing multiple AEUG suits and matching ZZ Gundam in early encounters.

Limit: Child physique requires customized cockpit; lacks formal tactical training, relies on instinct and Glemy's orders until defection. Overconfidence leads to tactical errors when emotionally agitated.

Newtype Ghost / Residual Psychowave Influence

After death, Ple's consciousness persists as a Newtype spirit that can communicate with and influence other Newtypes, notably calming Ple Two and guiding Judau.

Limit: Non-corporeal; cannot physically intervene except via psychowave influence on living Newtypes. Attested only in final episodes; not a repeatable power while alive.

Accounts I
First Contact: The Qubeley Mk-II vs ZZ GundamMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episode 34 'Ple's Attack' and Episode 35 'The Falling Colony'

U.C. 0088 (First Neo Zeon War), broadcast 1986

Glemy Toto deploys his secret Artificial Newtype, Elpeo Ple, in the white/pink AMX-004-2 Qubeley Mk-II to test AEUG's new MSZ-010 ZZ Gundam. Ple, eager to prove herself to 'Glemy-sama,' overwhelms Judau with funnel all-range attacks. During close psychowave contact, she senses Judau's warm, familial Newtype aura — unlike Glemy's possessive or Haman's cold pressure — and becomes instantly infatuated, calling him 'big brother' mid-battle. She breaks off the attack, confusing both sides. Establishes Ple as Glemy's trump card and sets up her obsessive attachment to Judau, which will drive her defection. Demonstrates ZZ Gundam's vulnerability to funnel weapons before Judau masters Bio-Sensor.

Defection to the ArgamaMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episodes 36-38 'Ple's Love' / 'Ple Two Appears' prelude

U.C. 0088

Unable to forget Judau, Ple deserts Neo Zeon during a skirmish and forces her way aboard the Argama, declaring she will live with Judau. The AEUG crew, led by Bright Noa, is torn between treating her as a prisoner of war and as a child refugee. Judau, who has lost his sister Leina to Zeon, is protective; Roux Louka and Elle Vianno are jealous and wary. Ple's presence disrupts discipline — she sneaks into Judau's quarters, refuses orders, and uses her Newtype abilities to eavesdrop. Glemy is furious at losing his 'masterpiece.' Ple officially changes affiliation from Neo Zeon to AEUG (though never formally enlisted). Provides AEUG with intelligence on Glemy's forces and Qubeley systems. Foreshadows Glemy's decision to accelerate cloning and deploy Ple Two.

Confrontation with Ple TwoMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episodes 43-45 'Ple Two's Descent' / 'The Psyco Gundam Mk-II'

U.C. 0088

Glemy unveils Ple Two — a cloned successor grown from Ple's DNA, more docile and ruthless — piloting the massive MRX-009 Psyco Gundam Mk-II. Ple is horrified to meet a younger, colder copy of herself who calls Glemy 'father' and shows no affection for Judau. The two Ples clash psychically; Ple Two's conditioned hatred and superior aggression overwhelm Ple's Qubeley Mk-II. Ple realizes she is replaceable, deepening her fear of abandonment. Reveals Neo Zeon cloning program's scale (later retconned in Gundam Unicorn as producing at least 12 Ples). Establishes Ple Two as Ple's dark mirror and sets up final sacrifice.

Accounts II
Death: Shielding Judau from the Psyco GundamMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episode 46 'Vibration'

U.C. 0088

During the chaotic Battle of Axis, Ple Two in the Psyco Gundam Mk-II targets Judau's ZZ Gundam with a devastating mega particle barrage. Ple interposes her Qubeley Mk-II directly in the line of fire, taking the full blast meant for Judau. Her machine is crippled and she is mortally wounded. She dies in Judau's arms in the cockpit, telling him she is happy she could protect her 'big brother' and asking him not to forget her. Judau is shattered, his Newtype scream heard across the battlefield. Ple's death traumatizes Judau and hardens his resolve against Glemy and Haman. Triggers Judau's full Newtype awakening and Bio-Sensor resonance. Considered one of Gundam's definitive child-soldier tragedies.

Spirit's Intervention: Saving Ple TwoMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episode 47 'Warrior, Again...' (final episode)

U.C. 0088

As Judau duels both Haman Karn (Quebeley) and Ple Two (Quebeley Mk-II) inside Axis, Ple's Newtype ghost manifests. She appears as a translucent, smiling apparition to Ple Two, who is mentally collapsing under Glemy's conditioning and Judau's pressure. Ple tells Ple Two that Judau is kind and that she does not have to fight, that they can be sisters. This psychowave contact breaks Glemy's control, causing Ple Two to hesitate and ultimately turn against Glemy. Glemy is killed shortly after when his forces collapse. Ple's posthumous influence directly leads to Glemy Toto's downfall and Ple Two's defection to Judau's side at war's end. Establishes Gundam's recurring theme that deceased Newtypes guide the living. Ple Two joins Judau and Roux's exodus to Jupiter in series epilogue, carrying Ple's legacy.

Legacy: The Ple Clones and Marida CruzMobile Suit Gundam Unicorn novels/anime (2007-2014) by Harutoshi Fukui, Sunrise; Gundam ZZ epilogue materials

U.C. 0096 (in-universe), published 2007-2014

Retroactive continuity reveals Ple was the first of at least twelve clones created by Glemy Toto's Newtype research institute. Ple Twelve, later known as Marida Cruz, is captured, abused, and eventually rehabilitated by the same trajectory Ple began — a child weapon seeking family. Marida's memories include flashes of Ple's death and Judau's kindness, suggesting shared psychowave imprint. Ple's brief life thus becomes the template for Gundam UC's most extended examination of artificial Newtypes and child exploitation. Elevates Ple from single-series tragic figure to foundational figure for UC cloning ethics arc spanning ZZ to Unicorn. Official timeline now lists Ple Project as major Neo Zeon war crime.

Elpeo Ple and Ple Two are the same person or twin sisters — They are distinct individuals: Ple is the original Artificial Newtype, Ple Two is her lab-grown clone successor created by Glemy Toto after Ple defects. They have different personalities, color schemes (white/pink vs red/black Qubeley Mk-II), and fates.

Ple is a natural Newtype like Judau or Haman — Official classification is Artificial Newtype (Kyōka Newtype) — her abilities were induced via bioengineering and conditioning, not natural evolution. This explains her instability compared to natural Newtypes.

Ple's name is a random cute name with no meaning — It is a documented staff pun on 'Lemon People' (L People), an adult manga magazine. The pun is acknowledged in contemporary B-Club and Newtype interviews.

Ple pilots the original Qubeley or the Psyco Gundam — Ple exclusively pilots the AMX-004-2 Qubeley Mk-II (white/pink). The black/purple AMX-004 is Haman Karn's original Qubeley; the MRX-009 Psyco Gundam Mk-II is piloted by Ple Two.

Who It Is

In the floating theocracy of Pastalia, the girl who sings from the highest balcony is both a goddess and a prisoner. Cloche Leythal Pastalia wears the white robes of the Holy Maiden, her voice able to rewrite reality through the Infel Phira server — and yet she has never been allowed to speak for herself. To meet her is to meet two people at once: the flawless ruler the Grand Bell created, and the lonely, stubborn teenager trapped inside that role, desperate to be loved without a hymn.

Public persona: dignified, formal, aloof, and demanding. Cloche was raised from infancy by the Grand Bell Church to be a flawless ruler; she speaks in polite, archaic keigo, expects obedience, and initially treats Croix and Luca with cold superiority. Private/repressed persona (revealed across Cosmosphere Levels 1-9): deeply insecure, lonely, childishly selfish, and terrified of abandonment. She equates love with being needed and fears that without her role she is worthless. She craves genuine affection but pushes people away, then feels guilty. Powers
METHOD Song Magic (Infel Phira Server)

As a Third Generation Reyvateil attuned to the Infel Phira server, Cloche executes METHOD hymns, which function as programs that rewrite reality via Symphonic Power. Unlike EXEC hymns (Sol Marta/First Tower), METHOD hymns use the New Testament of Pastalie Hymmnos dialect. In battle she is a rear-line Vanguard-protected caster; her songs charge over turns and release large-area effects.

Limit: Requires Hymn Crystals installed via Install Port (D-Cellophane ritual with a compatible partner - Croix). Requires time to charge (Song Magic gauge); interrupted if Vanguard fails to protect her. As a Third Generation, she depends on regular supply of Trichord-derived medicine (Life Extending Agent) and on proximity to the Tower's Symphonic Power; prolonged isolation weakens her. Cannot sing without server connection.

EXEC_PAJA/.#Misya extracting

Signature extraction hymn sung jointly with Luca in story event to extract Infel Phira from I.P.D. patients. Functions as a system-level purge that forcibly disconnects infected Reyvateils from the corrupted Infel Phira influence. In lore, composed by Infel.

Limit: Requires both Cloche and Luca singing in harmony; cannot be executed solo. High Symphonic Power cost; temporary exhaustion after.

EXEC_HARMONIOUS/.

Cloche's Level 9 Cosmosphere hymn, representing her fully integrated self. In battle, one of her most powerful offensive METHOD hymns, summoning harmonic resonance that damages all enemies and heals allies. Narratively, proof that she has accepted both her dignified and childish selves.

Limit: Only available after completing Cloche's Cosmosphere (requires deep Dive with Croix, high trust). High MP/CP cost, long charge time.

EXEC_METAFALICA/.

The legendary lost hymn that creates a new continent. Not Cloche's solo power; requires two Maidens (Cloche + Luca, and historically Infel + Nenesha) singing in perfect dualith. Cloche is one of the two necessary voices. When completed, it materializes land from the Sea of Death (the toxic lower atmosphere).

Limit: Requires absolute emotional synchronization between the two singers; any distrust causes failure (as seen in Infel's original failed attempt). Requires full Infel Phira server output; can only be attempted at specific facilities (Ancient facilities). Failure is catastrophic.

Reyvateil Physiology (Third Generation traits)

Enhanced acoustic sensitivity, ability to interface with Tower via Hymn Crystals, and Cosmosphere (diveable mental world). Cloche's Cosmosphere contains 9 levels, each with a distinct landscape and Guardian representing her psyche.

Limit: Third Generation lifespan is limited without regular medication (approx. 20 years without treatment in earlier lore; extended via medicine in Metafalss). I.P.D. susceptibility is low for Cloche herself but she is politically responsible for it. Install Port makes her vulnerable during installation.

Accounts I
The Holy Maiden's Abduction - Prologue and I.P.D. OutbreakAr tonelico II: Melody of Metafalica, Phase 1 (Opening - Pastalia Cathedral)

In-game year approx. 3770 AD (Ar Ciel calendar), ~1.5 years after Ar tonelico: Melody of Elemia

Cloche is introduced as the secluded Holy Maiden who governs Pastalia from the Grand Bell Cathedral. Knight Croix Bartel is dispatched by Grand Bell to investigate the Infel Phira Dependency, an epidemic where Reyvateils become addicted to the Infel Phira server's power and fall into comas. During a ceremony, Cloche is seemingly kidnapped by the anti-Grand Bell faction led by Luca Trulyworth. Croix is ordered to retrieve her, discovering that the 'kidnapping' is entangled with Grand Bell's own cover-up of the epidemic's cause. Establishes Cloche as both political figurehead and victim of the theocracy that raised her. Sets up the central mystery: Infel Phira is not a natural disease but a system overload caused by Grand Bell's misuse of the server.

The Two Maidens - Rivalry and Alliance with LucaAr tonelico II, Phases 1-2 (Rinkern Village, Enna, Grand Bell infiltration)

Same in-game year

After Croix defects from Grand Bell upon learning its corruption, he travels with Luca, the cheerful Reyvateil folk singer who is Cloche's political rival and personal foil. Cloche initially treats Luca with aristocratic contempt, seeing her as a commoner who panders to the masses. Through forced cooperation to save I.P.D. patients with EXEC_PAJA, the two learn they are the twin Maidens required for Metafalica. Their duet reveals that both carry Infel's legacy but represent opposite responses to it: Cloche as institutional heir, Luca as grassroots inheritor. Moves Cloche from antagonist to co-protagonist. Establishes the dual-Maidens system that is mechanically and narratively required for the true ending. Their relationship becomes central to both Cosmosphere arcs.

Diving into Cloche - Cosmosphere Levels 1-9 (The Locked Palace)Ar tonelico II, Dive System - Cloche Cosmosphere (optional but canonical)

Concurrent with main quest, accessed via Dive Shop

Croix Dives into Cloche's mind. Level 1-3: A pristine palace where a perfect doll-like Cloche performs endlessly for an absent audience, reflecting her performance anxiety. Level 4-6: The palace cracks to reveal a locked attic where a selfish, crying child-Cloche throws tantrums, demanding love and sweets, voicing the repressed desire to be spoiled rather than revered. Level 7-8: Confrontation with the embodiment of Grand Bell's expectations (a giant bell and a stern Infel-like figure). Level 9: Integration; child and maiden fuse, and Cloche grants Croix the Hymn Crystal for EXEC_HARMONIOUS/. She admits she wants to be loved as a girl, not worshipped as a Maiden. Unlocks Cloche's ultimate hymn and is required for her individual ending. Provides the game's most explicit characterization, explaining her earlier coldness as learned survival. Attested as the definitive source for her psychology.

Accounts II
Infel's Shadow - The Truth of the First MetafalicaAr tonelico II, Phase 3 (Ancient Facilities, Infel Phira satellite, Library of Pastalia)

Historical within game: ~400 years before present; revealed in present day

Through Jacqli (the surviving Pureblood Beta Reyvateil from Infel's era) and data logs, Cloche learns she is the political successor to Infel, the genius Reyvateil who created the Infel Phira server and first attempted Metafalica with her partner Nenesha. That attempt failed because Infel sacrificed Nenesha's trust, causing the hymn to collapse and creating the Sea of Death. Grand Bell has mythologized Infel as a perfect goddess, burdening Cloche with an impossible ideal. Cloche realizes she has been repeating Infel's mistake by refusing to trust Luca. Reframes Cloche's arc from personal insecurity to historical responsibility. She chooses to attempt Metafalica not as Infel's replica but with Luca as an equal, breaking the cycle.

Metafalica - The Creation of a ContinentAr tonelico II, Final Phase (Infel Phira - Metafalica execution)

Climax, 3770 AD

With the Infel Phira server restored and the Grand Bell's corruption purged (Leglius's faction defeated), Cloche and Luca stand together on the Infel Phira satellite to sing METHOD_METAFALICA/. Croix supports both, but the success depends entirely on the Maidens' mutual trust, built through their Cosmospheres. If the player has completed Cloche's Cosmosphere and chosen her, the hymn succeeds through Cloche's voice leading, creating a new green continent in the Sea of Death where humans and Reyvateils can live without the Tower. If Luca is chosen, the same hymn succeeds with Luca leading. In the true dual-ending, both sing as equals. Resolution of the central legend promised in the game's subtitle. Cloche abdicates or reforms the Holy Maiden role: she remains a leader but now as a person rather than an icon. The new land is named Metafalica. Her fate diverges by ending: Cloche Ending sees her and Croix as partners ruling together; Luca Ending sees her stepping back to support Luca.

After the Bell - Epilogue and ReconciliationAr tonelico II, Epilogue scenes and Drama CD 'Cloche's Afternoon' (extra)

Immediately post-Metafalica

In Cloche's ending epilogue, she struggles to adjust to life without constant ceremony, learning to shop, cook, and bicker as an ordinary girl with Croix. She visits Luca's grave/memorial in some dialogue? No - both survive; instead she visits the new land with Luca, promising to share governance. Drama CD and short stories show her attempting to write her own songs without Grand Bell's approval, still insecure but able to laugh at herself. Provides closure to her deconstruction of the Maiden identity. Demonstrates that her powers remain but her role has changed from theocratic ruler to co-creator of a new society.

Cloche is the villain of Ar tonelico II — She is introduced as an apparent antagonist due to Grand Bell's manipulation, but is a co-protagonist and victim. Her coldness is a trauma response to being raised as a political tool, not malice. She becomes Croix's ally and one of two heroines.

Cloche and Luca are sisters or the same person with split personality — They are unrelated Reyvateils who share the Maiden role as dual successors to Infel. Cloche's own Cosmosphere does contain a child persona, but that is an internal psychological representation, not Luca.

EXEC_METAFALICA can be sung solo by Cloche — Metafalica explicitly requires two Maidens in perfect harmony. Infel's original solo attempt failed catastrophically. Game mechanics and lore both require Cloche + Luca (or Infel + Nenesha historically).

Cloche is a Pureblood Reyvateil with unlimited lifespan — She is a Third Generation Reyvateil, engineered and dependent on medication and the Tower. Her lifespan is extended by Pastalia's medical care but not unlimited, unlike Pureblood Betas like Jacqli.

Who It Is
Form

Amma is often depicted as a powerful, all-knowing deity with the ability to shape and mold the universe.

Powers

Cosmic Creation Amma is said to have the power to create and shape the universe, bringing forth stars, planets, and living beings.

Divine Wisdom Amma is often depicted as an all-knowing deity, possessing wisdom and insight into the workings of the cosmos.

Transformation Amma is said to have the ability to transform and shape matter, creating new forms and structures from raw materials.

Accounts

Amma is said to have created the universe through a series of cosmic events, bringing forth stars, planets, and living beings.

Amma is said to have created the first humans using a pottery wheel, shaping them from clay and bringing them to life.

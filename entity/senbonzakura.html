Who It Is

The Zanpakutō spirit of Byakuya Kuchiki. Senbonzakura manifests as a beautiful woman in a flowing pink kimono adorned with cherry blossoms, her face always partially obscured. She represents the elegance and lethal precision of Byakuya's fighting style. Her power divides the blade into thousands of tiny cherry-blossom-petal blades that can attack, defend, and reform at will with perfect control.

Powers

Shikai: Senbonzakura (thousand cherry blossom blades)

Gōkei (massive pink blade convergence)

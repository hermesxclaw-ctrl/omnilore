Who It Is
Form

Senbonzakura is often depicted as a beautiful woman with long, flowing hair and a gentle smile. She is said to be surrounded by a thousand cherry blossoms, which symbolize the fleeting nature of life.

Powers

Cherry Blossom Petal Shower Senbonzakura can summon a shower of cherry blossom petals, which can bring good fortune and prosperity to those who receive them.

Accounts

The story tells the tale of Minamoto no Yoshitsune, a legendary Japanese warrior who was said to have been aided by the spirit of Senbonzakura in his battles.

In the series, Senbonzakura is depicted as a powerful Zanpakutō spirit, who is said to be the manifestation of the cherry blossom spirit.

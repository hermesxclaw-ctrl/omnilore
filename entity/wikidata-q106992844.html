Who It Is

A name that shimmers with many meanings—'reason,' 'scent,' 'evening,' 'tie'—Yukari is a common Japanese given name that has been worn by athletes, artists, politicians, and countless fictional heroines. From a figure skater gliding on ice to a boundary-drawing youkai in the Touhou universe, the name carries a quiet versatility, a thread that binds together a myriad of stories.

Accounts I
The Name's Usage in Japanese SocietyVarious historical records and modern media

Ancient to present

Yukari has been a popular given name for girls in Japan, with roots in classical literature and continued use today. Its meaning often relates to 'reason,' 'scent,' 'evening,' or 'connection,' reflecting aesthetic and philosophical values. The name has become associated with a wide range of accomplished women and beloved fictional characters.

Notable BearersWikipedia list

20th-21st century

Numerous notable people named Yukari have made contributions in fields such as sports (Yukari Nakano, figure skater; Yukari Kinga, footballer), entertainment (Yukari Tamura, voice actress; Yukari Oshima, actress), and politics (Yukari Sato, politician). These individuals have brought recognition to the name in their respective fields.

Fictional CharactersVarious anime, manga, light novels, and games

1980s-present

Many fictional characters named Yukari appear across media, each with distinct personalities and roles. Examples include Yukari Takeba from Persona 3, a high school student and member of SEES; Yukari Yakumo from Touhou Project, a youkai with boundary-manipulating powers; and Yukari Hinata from Yuyushiki, a high school girl interested in wordplay. These characters have contributed to the name's popularity and its association with certain archetypes, such as the mysterious or the supportive friend.

The Approach

A name that shimmers with many meanings—'reason,' 'scent,' 'evening,' 'tie'—Yukari is a common Japanese given name that has been worn by athletes, artists, politicians, and countless fictional heroines. From a figure skater gliding on ice to a boundary-drawing youkai in the Touhou universe, the name carries a quiet versatility, a thread that binds together a myriad of stories.

Etymology

The name Yukari is a feminine Japanese given name. It can be written with various kanji combinations, each with its own meaning. Common readings include '由' (reason) combined with '加' (addition) and '里' (village), or '由' with '香' (scent) and '里'. The name can also be written with the kanji '紫' (purple) or '縁' (affinity/destiny), the latter suggesting a meaning of 'connection' or 'fate.'

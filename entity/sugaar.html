Who It Is

Sugaar (also known as Sugoi or Maju) is a male dragon-serpent deity from Basque mythology, the consort of the great goddess Mari. He appears as a fiery serpent or dragon that travels through the sky as a crescent of flame — often described as a sickle of fire sweeping across the night sky. When Sugaar visits Mari in her cave on Mount Anboto, their union creates the storms and tempests that lash the Basque valleys. In some traditions, Sugaar founded the Kingdom of Navarre by fathering a mythical ancestral hero. Unlike many European dragons, Sugaar is not inherently evil; he is a force of nature — a chthonic sky-serpent representing male generative power, storms, and the forging of dynasties. He is invoked only indirectly, usually by referring to Mari's movements rather than naming him directly, reflecting a degree of taboo around his name.

Attested in Basque oral tradition (Ataun, Azkoitia, Zegama)Era Pre-Christian through medieval folklore

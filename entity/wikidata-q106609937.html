Who It Is

In the shifting, war-torn world of Terca Lumireis, where spirits and humans struggle for survival, one man's quiet resolve would come to define an era. Flynn Scifo, the stoic knight who rose from the slums of the Lower Quarter to become Commandant of the Imperial Knights, is remembered not for flashy heroics but for his unwavering dedication to duty, justice, and the friends he left behind. His story is one of sacrifice, loyalty, and the heavy cost of leadership.

Flynn is earnest, dutiful, and idealistic. He believes in the importance of law and order and strives to improve the empire from within. He is polite, respectful, and often acts as a mediator. Despite his serious demeanor, he has a deep sense of humor and cares deeply for his friends, especially Yuri, with whom he shares a complex history. He is willing to make personal sacrifices for the greater good and is often torn between his loyalty to the empire and his personal morals.

Powers
Swordsmanship

Flynn is a highly skilled swordsman, trained in the Imperial Knights' fencing style. He wields a rapier with precision and speed, capable of delivering rapid thrusts and slashes. His combat style is disciplined and efficient.

Limit: His style is less aggressive than Yuri's, focusing on technique over raw power.

Artes (Magic and Special Attacks)

Flynn can use a variety of artes, including both physical and magical techniques. He learns healing spells, light-based attacks, and supportive magic. His signature arte is 'Shining Fang', a powerful light-elemental slash.

Limit: His arte list is not as extensive as other party members, and he lacks some of the more powerful offensive spells.

Leadership

Flynn possesses strong leadership qualities, able to inspire and command troops. He rises through the ranks to become Commandant of the Imperial Knights, indicating his strategic and diplomatic abilities.

Limit: His idealism sometimes leads to conflict with pragmatic superiors.

Diplomacy

Flynn is skilled in negotiation and diplomacy, often acting as a liaison between the empire and other factions. He is able to navigate political situations with tact and patience.

Limit: His trust in the system can be a weakness when dealing with corrupt officials.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Flynn is a young man with short, sandy-blond hair and blue eyes. He wears the standard uniform of the Imperial Knights, consisting of a white and blue military jacket with gold trim, matching trousers, and tall black boots. He carries a rapier-like sword and often wears a blue cape. His appearance is neat and disciplined, reflecting his military bearing.

Symbols — Flynn's primary symbol is the crest of the Imperial Knights, which he wears on his uniform. His personal emblem is often associated with a sword and a shield, representing his role as a protector. In promotional art, he is frequently depicted with Yuri Lowell, symbolizing their contrasting yet complementary natures.

Domains — Flynn primarily resides in the capital city of Zaphias, where he serves as a knight. He is often seen in the imperial palace, the knights' headquarters, and the lower quarters, where he grew up. He travels across Terca Lumireis during the game's events, visiting various cities and dungeons.

Accounts I
The Rise of the Loyal KnightTales of Vesperia (main game)

Fictional timeline of Terca Lumireis

Flynn's journey is intertwined with Yuri Lowell's. Growing up together in the Lower Quarter of Zaphias, they both aspire to join the Imperial Knights to change the empire. Flynn succeeds and rises through the ranks, while Yuri becomes a vigilante after becoming disillusioned with the corrupt system. Throughout the game, Flynn works within the system to enact change, often clashing with Yuri's methods but ultimately sharing the same goal of protecting the people. He faces challenges such as political corruption, a coup, and the threat of the Adephagos, a destructive force that threatens the world. Flynn's actions help stabilize the empire and contribute to the defeat of the Adephagos. He becomes Commandant of the Imperial Knights, and his friendship with Yuri is reaffirmed.

Accounts II
The First StrikeTales of Vesperia: The First Strike (anime film)

Pre-game events, set a few years before the main story

The film depicts the early days of Flynn and Yuri as newly appointed knights in the Imperial Knights. They are sent on a mission to investigate a monster outbreak in a small town. During the mission, they uncover a conspiracy involving the empire and the use of banned technology. Flynn and Yuri work together to defeat the threat, but the experience plants the seeds for their divergent paths: Flynn's continued faith in the system and Yuri's growing disillusionment. The film establishes the deep bond between Flynn and Yuri and highlights their differing worldviews. It also introduces themes of duty and justice that are central to the game.

The Knight's ResolveTales of Vesperia (side quests and skits)

During the main game's events

Various side quests and skits explore Flynn's character in more detail. For example, one side quest involves Flynn investigating corruption within the Knights, while another shows him struggling with the moral implications of his duties. These moments humanize him and show his internal conflicts. These scenes deepen the player's understanding of Flynn's motivations and his relationship with Yuri.

Flynn is a lawful good character who always follows the rules. — While Flynn believes in law and order, he is willing to bend or break rules when necessary to protect the innocent. He often struggles with the conflict between his duty and his personal sense of justice.

Flynn is jealous of Yuri's freedom. — Flynn respects Yuri's choices but also worries about his safety and the consequences of his actions. He does not envy Yuri's life but rather wishes they could find a way to work together.

The Approach

He is a ghost with a grudge, a shadow of a family that was erased from history. For centuries, Enjin Hiizumi has simmered in the space between worlds, waiting for the moment when the sakura pillars would bloom and everything he lost would be reclaimed. Now, wearing the body of a boy he has stolen, he moves through Sakurashin with a single, burning purpose: to tear down the very system that created him.

Content advisory. Contains themes of revenge, body possession, violence, and family trauma. Some scenes may be intense for younger readers.
Identity

Enjin Hiizumi

Pronunciation En-jin Hee-ee-zoo-mee

Etymology

The surname Hiizumi (比泉) is shared with the main family, indicating his branch lineage. The given name Enjin (円神) combines the kanji for 'circle' (円) and 'god' (神), possibly symbolizing his role as a deity-like figure or his connection to the cyclical nature of the Hiizumi family's duties.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese manga and anime franchise, created by Suzuhito Yasuda.

Disputed Identifications

None known. His identity as a member of the Hiizumi branch family and a non-corporeal entity is consistently presented across adaptations.

Description

Appearance

Enjin, in his natural form, is a non-corporeal entity, often depicted as a shadowy figure or a ghostly presence. When possessing Gin Nanami's body, he takes on Gin's physical appearance: a young man with dark hair and eyes. He may have a distinct aura or presence that sets him apart from the original personality.

Symbols & Iconography

His connection to the Hiizumi family and the concept of 'tuning' is a key symbol. He is often associated with the seven pillars and the merging of worlds.

Behaviour & Personality

Enjin is driven by centuries of resentment towards the Hiizumi main family. He is calculating, patient, and ruthless in his pursuit of his goal. He shows little regard for the lives of others, viewing them as obstacles or tools. He is highly intelligent and skilled in combat and exorcism techniques.

Domains & Habitats

As a non-corporeal entity, he exists in the space between the human and yōkai worlds. He operates within Sakurashin, using Gin's body to interact with the physical world.

Version Differences

The core characterization remains consistent across manga and anime. However, the anime adaptations may compress or alter certain plot points, potentially affecting the depth of his backstory or the details of his abilities. Specific differences are not documented in available sources.

Stories & Myths

The Hiizumi Family's Betrayal

Source Yozakura Quartet manga and animeCulture JapanesePeriod Centuries ago (within the story's timeline)

Summary

Enjin was a member of the Hiizumi branch family. The main household 'tuned' the branch family centuries ago, severing their connections to the world and effectively erasing them. Enjin survived as a non-corporeal entity, harboring deep resentment and a desire for revenge.

Participants

Enjin Hiizumi

Hiizumi main family (including Akina's grandfather and ancestors)

Consequences

Enjin's grudge drives him to seek the blooming of the seven pillars, which would merge the human and yōkai worlds, potentially undoing the tuning and restoring his family's existence.

Variants

The anime adaptations may present this backstory with slight differences in detail, but the core events remain the same.

The Possession of Gin Nanami

Source Yozakura Quartet manga and animeCulture JapanesePeriod Before the main story

Summary

Gin Nanami, Ao's older brother, disappeared before the series began. Enjin later uses Gin's body as a vessel, taking over his form to carry out his plans. This possession is a key element of Enjin's strategy.

Participants

Enjin Hiizumi

Gin Nanami

Consequences

Gin's body becomes a tool for Enjin, and his fate is a source of grief for Ao. The possession also gives Enjin a physical presence in Sakurashin.

Variants

The exact circumstances of Gin's disappearance and possession are not fully detailed in available sources.

He confronts the Hiizumi Life Counseling Office and other residents of Sakurashin, leading to a major conflict. He is initially limited to Gin's Satori abilities but eventually masters exorcism techniques to enhance his power.

Erasure of the Hiizumi branch family

Source Manga and anime

Description

The main family tuned the branch family centuries ago, effectively severing their connections to the world and causing their disappearance. This act is the root of Enjin's vendetta.

Scholarly Context

This reflects themes of family duty, sacrifice, and the consequences of maintaining cosmic balance at the expense of individuals.

Body possession

Source Manga and anime

Description

Enjin takes over Gin Nanami's body without consent, using it as a vessel for his plans. This is a violation of Gin's autonomy and a source of trauma for his sister Ao.

Scholarly Context

Body possession is a common trope in supernatural fiction, often used to explore themes of identity and control.

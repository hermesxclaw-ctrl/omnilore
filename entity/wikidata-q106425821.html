Who It Is

He is a ghost with a grudge, a shadow of a family that was erased from history. For centuries, Enjin Hiizumi has simmered in the space between worlds, waiting for the moment when the sakura pillars would bloom and everything he lost would be reclaimed. Now, wearing the body of a boy he has stolen, he moves through Sakurashin with a single, burning purpose: to tear down the very system that created him.

Enjin is driven by centuries of resentment towards the Hiizumi main family. He is calculating, patient, and ruthless in his pursuit of his goal. He shows little regard for the lives of others, viewing them as obstacles or tools. He is highly intelligent and skilled in combat and exorcism techniques.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname Hiizumi (比泉) is shared with the main family, indicating his branch lineage. The given name Enjin (円神) combines the kanji for 'circle' (円) and 'god' (神), possibly symbolizing his role as a deity-like figure or his connection to the cyclical nature of the Hiizumi family's duties.

Pronunciation — En-jin Hee-ee-zoo-mee

Aliases — Enjin Hiizumi

Ownership — Japanese manga and anime franchise, created by Suzuhito Yasuda.

Powers
Possession

Enjin can possess the body of another being, as he does with Gin Nanami. This grants him physical form and access to the host's abilities.

Limit: The host's body may have its own limitations, and Enjin's power may be constrained while possessing.

Striking

The inverse of Akina's tuning ability. Enjin can summon objects from the yōkai realm into the human world. This ability is initially limited but becomes more powerful after he masters exorcism techniques.

Limit: Initially limited to using Gin's Satori abilities. After mastering exorcism, he removes physical constraints on his power.

Limited Tuning

Enjin possesses a form of tuning, the hereditary Hiizumi ability to sever connections between worlds. However, his tuning is inferior to Akina's.

Limit: Inferior to Akina's, suggesting a weaker version or less control.

Exorcism Techniques

After mastering exorcism techniques, Enjin is able to remove the physical constraints on his powers, enhancing his abilities.

Limit: Not specified, but implies a high level of skill.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Enjin, in his natural form, is a non-corporeal entity, often depicted as a shadowy figure or a ghostly presence. When possessing Gin Nanami's body, he takes on Gin's physical appearance: a young man with dark hair and eyes. He may have a distinct aura or presence that sets him apart from the original personality.

Symbols — His connection to the Hiizumi family and the concept of 'tuning' is a key symbol. He is often associated with the seven pillars and the merging of worlds.

Domains — As a non-corporeal entity, he exists in the space between the human and yōkai worlds. He operates within Sakurashin, using Gin's body to interact with the physical world.

Accounts I
The Hiizumi Family's BetrayalYozakura Quartet manga and anime

Centuries ago (within the story's timeline)

Enjin was a member of the Hiizumi branch family. The main household 'tuned' the branch family centuries ago, severing their connections to the world and effectively erasing them. Enjin survived as a non-corporeal entity, harboring deep resentment and a desire for revenge. Enjin's grudge drives him to seek the blooming of the seven pillars, which would merge the human and yōkai worlds, potentially undoing the tuning and restoring his family's existence.

Accounts II
The Possession of Gin NanamiYozakura Quartet manga and anime

Before the main story

Gin Nanami, Ao's older brother, disappeared before the series began. Enjin later uses Gin's body as a vessel, taking over his form to carry out his plans. This possession is a key element of Enjin's strategy. Gin's body becomes a tool for Enjin, and his fate is a source of grief for Ao. The possession also gives Enjin a physical presence in Sakurashin.

The Conflict in SakurashinYozakura Quartet manga and anime

Main story

Enjin's goal is to force the seven pillars to bloom, merging the human and yōkai worlds. He confronts the Hiizumi Life Counseling Office and other residents of Sakurashin, leading to a major conflict. He is initially limited to Gin's Satori abilities but eventually masters exorcism techniques to enhance his power. The conflict threatens the stability of Sakurashin and the balance between worlds. The outcome is not detailed in the provided sources, but it is a central plotline of the series.

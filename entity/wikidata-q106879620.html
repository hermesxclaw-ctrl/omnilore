Etymology

The name 'Phrourarchidas' is a compound of Greek elements: 'phrour-' (from 'phrouros', meaning 'guard' or 'watch') and 'arch-' (from 'arche', meaning 'rule' or 'command'), with the patronymic suffix '-idas' common in Doric Greek. Thus, the name likely means 'son of the guard-commander' or 'descendant of a garrison leader'.

Cultural Ownership

Ancient Greek, specifically Lacedaemon (Sparta). The name is Doric and reflects Spartan military culture.

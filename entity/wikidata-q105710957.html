Who It Is

Noel Vermillion is a girl who carries the weight of a world-ending weapon in her soul. Rescued from the burning fields of Ikaruga as a child, she rose through the ranks of the Novus Orbis Librarium (NOL) as a seemingly ordinary soldier—until the truth of her existence shattered everything she believed. She is not just a human; she is Mu-12, the perfect Murakumo Unit, a living weapon designed to erase the world. Her journey is one of identity, memory, and the struggle to define herself beyond the destiny forced upon her.

Noel is initially shy, insecure, and eager to please, often stuttering and lacking confidence. She is kind-hearted and protective of others, but her traumatic past and the revelation of her true nature cause her to struggle with self-identity. As Mu-12, she becomes cold, aggressive, and hateful, but this is a result of Terumi's psychological conditioning. Over time, she learns to accept both sides of herself, integrating her Mu personality without losing her humanity.

Powers
Chain Revolver

Noel's Drive ability allows her to perform a series of rapid gun attacks with her Bolverk pistols, chaining them into combos. It is a versatile move that can be used for pressure and mix-ups.

Limit: Requires close to mid-range positioning; can be punished if blocked.

Arcus Diabolus: Bolverk

A Nox Nyctores revolver that fires bullets that create dimensional distortions, bypassing conventional defenses. It is a powerful weapon that can damage opponents on a conceptual level.

Limit: Its true power is only accessible when Noel embraces her Mu-12 form.

Mu-12 Transformation

Noel's true form as a Murakumo Unit, which grants her access to the Lux Sanctus: Murakumo, a massive sword. She gains enhanced physical abilities and the power to manipulate the Azure, including the ability to erase targets from existence.

Limit: Initially, Noel cannot control this form and it is activated by Terumi's conditioning. In Chrono Phantasma, she learns to use it while retaining her personality, but it still requires immense concentration.

Steins Gunner

Mu-12's Drive ability, which allows her to project a 'Stein' (a magical construct) at a targeted location, which can then be detonated or used to warp her attacks.

Limit: Requires setup and can be avoided by mobile opponents.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Noel has long, light brown hair often tied in twin tails, and blue eyes. She wears a military-style NOL uniform with a short skirt, thigh-high stockings, and a beret. As Mu-12, her hair turns white and her eyes become red, and she wears a more revealing outfit with metallic accents. She carries two handguns, Bolverk, which are her Nox Nyctores.

Symbols — Her symbol is the Murakumo Unit design, often associated with the Azure and the Master Unit. The number '12' is prominent on her equipment. Her weapons, Bolverk, are iconic, and her transformation into Mu-12 is marked by a halo-like ring.

Domains — She is primarily associated with the NOL, the military organization she serves. She also has ties to the Ikaruga region, her birthplace, and the Azure, a mystical dimension. In Central Fiction, she works at Celica's church, which becomes a sanctuary for her.

Accounts I
The Rescue in IkarugaBlazBlue: Calamity Trigger (story mode)

Before the events of Calamity Trigger

Noel is found as a young girl in the burning fields of Ikaruga, the only survivor of a village destroyed during the Ikaruga Civil War. She is rescued by the Vermillion family and adopted, but she has no memory of her past. Noel grows up as a noble, but her family is later accused of treason, prompting her to join the NOL to clear their name.

The Awakening as Mu-12BlazBlue: Continuum Shift (story mode)

During Continuum Shift

Terumi, the main antagonist, breaks Noel's mind through psychological conditioning, causing her to awaken as Mu-12, a Murakumo Unit programmed to destroy the Sankishin Unit Amaterasu and erase the world. She becomes Kusanagi, the 'Sword of the Godslayer', and serves Terumi. At the end of the game, Ragna is able to undo the conditioning and restore Noel's original personality. Noel is left traumatized by her actions as Mu-12, but she eventually accepts her true self.

Accounts II
The Search for IdentityBlazBlue: Chrono Phantasma (story mode)

During Chrono Phantasma

Noel seeks out Rachel Alucard to learn how to control her Mu powers. She learns to use her full abilities while maintaining her personality, and she begins to understand her connection to the Origin and the Master Unit. Noel gains confidence and a sense of purpose, becoming a key figure in the fight against the Azure's threats.

The Final StandBlazBlue: Central Fiction (story mode)

During Central Fiction

Noel separates from Mu to protect the gate to the Azure. She later merges with Mu again to help Ragna imprison Izanami within her soul, recreating Saya. At the end of the game, she merges with the Origin and helps Ragna take the Master Unit away from the world. She resigns from the NOL and works at Celica's church with Lambda, watching over the catatonic Nu-13. The world is saved, but Noel loses her memories of Ragna due to his erasure. She remains a guardian of the Azure's remnants.

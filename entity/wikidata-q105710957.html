The Approach

Noel Vermillion is a girl who carries the weight of a world-ending weapon in her soul. Rescued from the burning fields of Ikaruga as a child, she rose through the ranks of the Novus Orbis Librarium (NOL) as a seemingly ordinary soldier—until the truth of her existence shattered everything she believed. She is not just a human; she is Mu-12, the perfect Murakumo Unit, a living weapon designed to erase the world. Her journey is one of identity, memory, and the struggle to define herself beyond the destiny forced upon her.

Content advisory. Contains themes of psychological manipulation, memory loss, existential identity crisis, and violence. Some content may be intense for younger readers.
Identity

Noel Vermillion

Pronunciation Noel Vermillion: /noʊˈɛl vərˈmɪljən/; Mu-12: /mjuː twɛlv/

Known As

Mu-12KusanagiNoelNoel Vermillion (Japanese: ノエル＝ヴァーミリオン, Noeru Vāmirion)

Etymology

The surname 'Vermillion' may derive from the color vermilion, a red pigment, possibly referencing her red hair or the blood-red themes in her story. 'Noel' is a French name meaning 'Christmas', but its significance in the series is unclear. 'Mu-12' refers to her designation as the 12th Murakumo Unit.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Arc System Works for the BlazBlue franchise. The character is part of Japanese video game culture and has gained international recognition through the series' global releases.

Disputed Identifications

Noel is sometimes confused with other Murakumo Units like Nu-13 and Lambda-11, but they are distinct characters. Her identity as Mu-12 is confirmed in canon, but the full extent of her connection to the Origin and the Master Unit Amaterasu is subject to interpretation.

Description

Appearance

Noel has long, light brown hair often tied in twin tails, and blue eyes. She wears a military-style NOL uniform with a short skirt, thigh-high stockings, and a beret. As Mu-12, her hair turns white and her eyes become red, and she wears a more revealing outfit with metallic accents. She carries two handguns, Bolverk, which are her Nox Nyctores.

Symbols & Iconography

Her symbol is the Murakumo Unit design, often associated with the Azure and the Master Unit. The number '12' is prominent on her equipment. Her weapons, Bolverk, are iconic, and her transformation into Mu-12 is marked by a halo-like ring.

Behaviour & Personality

Noel is initially shy, insecure, and eager to please, often stuttering and lacking confidence. She is kind-hearted and protective of others, but her traumatic past and the revelation of her true nature cause her to struggle with self-identity. As Mu-12, she becomes cold, aggressive, and hateful, but this is a result of Terumi's psychological conditioning. Over time, she learns to accept both sides of herself, integrating her Mu personality without losing her humanity.

Domains & Habitats

She is primarily associated with the NOL, the military organization she serves. She also has ties to the Ikaruga region, her birthplace, and the Azure, a mystical dimension. In Central Fiction, she works at Celica's church, which becomes a sanctuary for her.

Version Differences

In the original Calamity Trigger, Noel is a playable character with no knowledge of her true identity. In Continuum Shift, she is revealed as Mu-12 and becomes the final boss in the console version. In Chrono Phantasma, she learns to control her Mu form. In Central Fiction, she separates from Mu to protect the Azure gate, and later merges with the Origin. Her personality and abilities evolve significantly across the series.

Arcus Diabolus: Bolverk

Source Version All games

Description

A Nox Nyctores revolver that fires bullets that create dimensional distortions, bypassing conventional defenses. It is a powerful weapon that can damage opponents on a conceptual level.

Limitations

Its true power is only accessible when Noel embraces her Mu-12 form.

Contradictions

Some sources describe it as a 'magic gun', but its exact mechanics are not fully explained.

Mu-12 Transformation

Source Version Continuum Shift onward

Description

Noel's true form as a Murakumo Unit, which grants her access to the Lux Sanctus: Murakumo, a massive sword. She gains enhanced physical abilities and the power to manipulate the Azure, including the ability to erase targets from existence.

Limitations

Initially, Noel cannot control this form and it is activated by Terumi's conditioning. In Chrono Phantasma, she learns to use it while retaining her personality, but it still requires immense concentration.

Contradictions

The exact nature of her connection to the Origin and the Master Unit is a point of debate among fans.

Steins Gunner

Source Version Continuum Shift

Description

Mu-12's Drive ability, which allows her to project a 'Stein' (a magical construct) at a targeted location, which can then be detonated or used to warp her attacks.

Limitations

Requires setup and can be avoided by mobile opponents.

None noted.

The Rescue in Ikaruga

Source BlazBlue: Calamity Trigger (story mode)Culture Japanese video gamePeriod Before the events of Calamity Trigger

Summary

Noel is found as a young girl in the burning fields of Ikaruga, the only survivor of a village destroyed during the Ikaruga Civil War. She is rescued by the Vermillion family and adopted, but she has no memory of her past.

Noel grows up as a noble, but her family is later accused of treason, prompting her to join the NOL to clear their name.

The Awakening as Mu-12

Source BlazBlue: Continuum Shift (story mode)Culture Japanese video gamePeriod During Continuum Shift

Summary

Terumi, the main antagonist, breaks Noel's mind through psychological conditioning, causing her to awaken as Mu-12, a Murakumo Unit programmed to destroy the Sankishin Unit Amaterasu and erase the world. She becomes Kusanagi, the 'Sword of the Godslayer', and serves Terumi. At the end of the game, Ragna is able to undo the conditioning and restore Noel's original personality.

Relationships

Ragna the Bloodedge

Type ally, romantic interest (implied)Source All games

Description

Noel is rescued by Ragna multiple times and develops a close bond with him. He is the one who undoes Terumi's conditioning and helps her accept her true self. Their relationship is central to the series, with romantic undertones.

Version Note

In Central Fiction, Ragna erases himself from existence, leaving Noel with no memory of him, which is a tragic conclusion.

Conflicting Accounts

Some fans interpret their relationship as purely platonic, while others see it as romantic.

Noel initially serves as Jin's secretary. Jin is cold and distant towards her, but he later becomes a trusted ally. He is also Ragna's brother, creating a complex dynamic.

Version Note

In Central Fiction, Jin loses his memories of Ragna but still works with Noel.

Nu-13

Nu-13 is another Murakumo Unit, created by Relius Clover. She is often portrayed as a parallel to Noel, and they share a connection through the Azure. At the end of Central Fiction, Noel watches over the catatonic Nu-13.

The Origin

Type creator/other selfSource Central Fiction

The Origin is the original Murakumo Unit and the basis for Noel's existence. Noel merges with the Origin at the end of Central Fiction, becoming a being that can exist outside the world's cycle.

Psychological Conditioning by Terumi

Source BlazBlue: Continuum Shift

Description

Terumi subjects Noel to intense psychological torture, breaking her mind and reprogramming her to hate the world. This is a form of mental abuse and coercion.

Scholarly Context

This is a common trope in anime and fighting games, but it is depicted with a focus on the character's trauma and recovery.

Amnesia and Identity Erasure

Source All games

Description

Noel suffers from amnesia, which is a central part of her character. Her true identity as Mu-12 is hidden from her, leading to a crisis of self. This can be interpreted as a metaphor for identity suppression.

Scholarly Context

Amnesia is a common narrative device, but here it is tied to the character's artificial nature and the concept of being a weapon.

Violence and Destruction

Source Continuum Shift

Description

As Mu-12, Noel is used as a weapon to destroy the world. She is forced to commit acts of violence against her will, which she later regrets.

Scholarly Context

The game explores the idea of a person being used as a tool, and the moral implications of such actions.

Contradictions & Disputes

Noel's True Identity

Position A

Noel is a human who was turned into a Murakumo Unit.

Position A — evidence

She was found as a child in Ikaruga and raised by the Vermillion family.

Position B

Noel was always an artificial being, and her human memories are implanted.

Position B — evidence

It is revealed that she is based on the Origin, and her memories may be fabricated.

Scholarly Consensus

The games suggest that Noel is a combination of human and artificial elements, but the exact nature is ambiguous.

Noel's Relationship with Ragna

Position A

Noel and Ragna are romantically involved.

Position A — evidence

Their interactions are often intimate, and Ragna is willing to sacrifice himself for her.

Position B

Their relationship is purely platonic.

Position B — evidence

The games never explicitly confirm a romantic relationship.

Scholarly Consensus

Fans are divided, but the series leaves it open to interpretation.

Mu-12's Power Level

Position A

Mu-12 is the most powerful Murakumo Unit, capable of destroying the world.

Position A — evidence

She is called the 'Sword of the Godslayer' and is designed to destroy Amaterasu.

Position B

Mu-12 is powerful but not the strongest, as other units like Nu-13 have similar abilities.

Position B — evidence

In gameplay, other characters can match her power.

Scholarly Consensus

In lore, Mu-12 is considered top-tier, but gameplay balance is separate.

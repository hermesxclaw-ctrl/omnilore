Who It Is

Eight blades move at once, each dripping with a venom that can drop a giant in minutes — and the octopus merman holding them would rather be drinking. Hyouzou is One Piece's bleak joke about talent wasted: the most gifted swordsman on Fish-Man Island, hired out as a poisoned blade for a revolution he does not believe in.

Attested personality: listless, apathetic, and alcohol-dependent. Hyouzou is introduced drunk at a bar and remains perpetually disinterested unless paid or threatened. He shows no ideological commitment to Hody Jones's supremacist cause; he fights for money, sake, and later under coercion via Energy Steroid addiction. He is easily manipulated, prone to sudden bursts of lethal precision when ordered, then immediately returns to lethargy. Name & Tongue

Etymology — Japanese ヒョウゾウ (Hyouzou). Oda has not given an official etymology, but the name and design transparently reference hyōmon-dako (ヒョウモンダコ), the blue-ringed octopus (Hapalochlaena spp.), whose Japanese name means 'leopard-patterned octopus.' The -zou suffix is a common masculine name ending in Japanese

Pronunciation — Japanese: hyō-zō [çoːzoː], romanized as Hyouzou (Hepburn) or Hyozo (shortened). English dub and official VIZ Media manga use 'Hyozo'.

Aliases — Hyozo, Hyouzou the Octopus, ヒョウゾウ

Ownership — Original character created by Eiichiro Oda for One Piece, serialized in Weekly Shōnen Jump (Shueisha) and produced by Toei Animation. Intellectual property owned by Shueisha / Shue

Powers
Hasshoutou (Eight-Sword Style)

Wields eight katanas simultaneously, one in each tentacle-arm. Style emphasizes omnidirectional slashes, spinning barrages, and the ability to attack multiple opponents at once. In-universe, he is stated to be the strongest swordsman on Fish-Man Island before the Straw Hats' arrival, a reputation acknowledged even by Zoro.

Limit: Requires all eight arms free; if disarmed or intoxicated, precision drops. Style is less effective against opponents who can predict or outpace multi-angle attacks (Zoro counters by reading breath). No Haki is shown.

Blue-Ringed Octopus Venom

As a blue-ringed octopus merman, his body produces tetrodotoxin-like venom. He coats his blades with it; a single cut can paralyze or kill. Victims in Ch. 622 are shown foaming and collapsing after shallow cuts. The venom is his most dangerous asset, allowing him to threaten even stronger fighters.

Limit: Venom must be delivered via wound; can be neutralized if swords are destroyed or venom is wiped. Zoro is able to fight without being cut by deflecting or evading. No evidence venom works through mere contact or at range. Antidote not shown in-series.

Energy Steroid (ES) Enhancement

Consumes Energy Steroid capsules supplied by Hody Jones (originally from the Tamate Box). Grants massive temporary strength, speed, and pain resistance, doubling power with each dose. Under ES, Hyouzou can overwhelm multiple palace guards and clash with Zoro briefly.

Limit: Severe cost: rapid aging, emaciation, and life-span shortening. Overuse leaves user withered and helpless after battle, as seen in Ch. 649 where Hyouzou appears as an elderly, withered figure. Addiction and withdrawal make user dependent on supplier. Effect is temporary (minutes to hours).

Merman Physiology and Aquatic Combat

As a merman, possesses ten times the strength of an average human in water (standard One Piece lore), enhanced swimming speed, ability to breathe underwater, and tentacle flexibility. On land, retains tentacle mobility but is slower than on Fish-Man Island's water-filled areas.

Limit: Not as physically strong as shark or whale fish-men; relies on technique and poison rather than brute force. Vulnerable to long-range Haki-imbued attacks and to being dried out. No Devil Fruit, so cannot swim if given one — but he has none.

Master Swordsman Reputation / Kenjutsu Skill

Even without venom or ES, Hyouzou is a highly trained swordsman capable of precise cuts. His reputation as Fish-Man Island's finest swordsman is attested by Hatchan and others, and Zoro treats him as a legitimate test, not a pushover.

Limit: Skill is still below world-class swordsmen like Zoro post-timeskip; defeated cleanly once Zoro gets serious. No named ultimate technique beyond generic 'Hasshoutou' flurries; no Haki integration shown.

Accounts I
The Drunken Swordsman at Mermaid CoveOne Piece manga Chapter 609-610; Anime Episode 528-529

2011 (publication), in-universe: Fish-Man Island arc, Year 1522 Age of Pirates

The Straw Hat Pirates arrive at Fish-Man Island and explore Mermaid Cove. Luffy, Usopp and others encounter Hyouzou for the first time slumped drunk at a bar, sake gourd in hand, being fawned over as the island's greatest swordsman. He shows no interest in the newcomers, mumbling and swaying, establishing his apathy and alcoholism. Keimi and Hatchan warn that he is dangerous despite his appearance and that he takes mercenary work. This introduction frames him as a Chekhov's gun — a local master who will later be weaponized. Establishes Hyouzou's reputation and foreshadows his later recruitment; shows Straw Hats' initial ignorance of island politics.

Recruitment by the New Fish-Man PiratesOne Piece manga Chapter 620-623; Anime Episode 554-556

2011

Hody Jones, leader of the New Fish-Man Pirates, seeks to overthrow King Neptune and wage war on humans. Needing elite fighters, he offers Hyouzou money and a steady supply of Energy Steroid and sake in exchange for his swords. Hyouzou, indifferent to ideology, accepts. He is dosed with ES and ordered to serve as an assassin alongside Zeo, Daruma and Ikaros Much. The scene emphasizes his mercenary nature — he asks 'How much?' not 'Why?' — and his growing dependency on the drug, which Hody controls. Hyouzou becomes an officer-level combatant for the coup; his addiction ties his fate to Hody's supply, ensuring loyalty through dependency rather than belief.

Accounts II
Siege of Ryugu Palace and the Poisoned BladesOne Piece manga Chapter 629-633; Anime Episode 560-563

2011-2012

During the New Fish-Man Pirates' coup, Hyouzou leads the assault on Ryugu Palace. Empowered by ES, he cuts down palace guards with poison-coated swords; victims collapse paralyzed, demonstrating his venom's lethality. He takes King Neptune, his sons Fukaboshi, Ryuboshi and Manboshi, and Princess Shirahoshi's allies hostage. When Zoro, Usopp and Brook (who had been captured) break free, Hyouzou engages them. He wounds several guards and nearly poisons the princes, showing willingness to kill royalty for pay. The palace falls, and Hyouzou stands guard over the chained royal family. Royal family captured; Straw Hats declared enemies; Hyouzou's poison establishes him as a tactical threat requiring a swordsman of Zoro's caliber to counter.

Duel at Gyoncorde Plaza — Eight Swords vs Three SwordsOne Piece manga Chapter 641-644; Anime Episode 564-566

2012

At Gyoncorde Plaza, Hody forces the entire island's population into a square to execute King Neptune and demonstrate power, with Noah's Ark looming overhead. Hyouzou, heavily dosed with ES, is ordered to execute the Straw Hats. He faces Roronoa Zoro in a formal swordsman duel. Hyouzou unleashes Hasshoutou barrages with venom-coated blades, claiming his poison makes any cut fatal. Zoro, having trained for two years with Mihawk, remains calm, declares he will not be cut, and uses long-range flying slashes (1080 Pound Phoenix) and precise parries to keep distance. Zoro ultimately disarms and defeats Hyouzou without sustaining a single poisoned wound, shattering all eight swords and knocking him unconscious. The duel serves as Zoro's post-timeskip showcase. Hyouzou's defeat breaks the New Fish-Man Pirates' aura of invincibility; Zoro proves ES-enhanced poison swordsmen are still outclassed by true mastery and Haki-adjacent skill; Hyouzou is left unconscious on the battlefield.

The Price of Energy Steroid — Withered AftermathOne Piece manga Chapter 649-650; Anime Episode 569-570

2012

After Hody's defeat and the collapse of the coup, the side effects of mass Energy Steroid abuse manifest. Hyouzou, like Hody and his officers, is shown in the aftermath as grotesquely aged — hair white, body shrunken, teeth missing — the drug having consumed his life force. He is arrested by the newly reformed Ryugu Palace guards under King Neptune and Fukaboshi. Unlike Hody, who remains defiant, Hyouzou offers no final speech; he is simply carried away, a cautionary image of addiction's cost. He does not reappear in subsequent arcs (Whole Cake Island, Wano, Egghead as of 2026), implying long-term imprisonment or hospitalization. Narrative closure for ES as a metaphor for doping and short-term power; Hyouzou becomes a visual warning. His absence from later arcs confirms his role as arc-specific antagonist, not recurring villain.

Hyouzou is a Devil Fruit user. — He has no Devil Fruit. His abilities come from natural merman physiology (eight tentacles), trained swordsmanship, natural blue-ringed octopus venom, and artificial Energy Steroid. He can swim and is not weakened by seawater, which Devil Fruit users cannot do.

His poison kills instantly with any scratch and cannot be avoided. — Manga shows poison requires a cut and takes time to paralyze; victims remain conscious briefly. Zoro avoids it entirely through superior swordsmanship and range. No evidence of airborne or contact poison.

Hyouzou is a major recurring villain who returns in later arcs. — He is an arc-specific antagonist confined to Fish-Man Island (2011-2012). After his defeat and ES-induced aging, he is arrested and has not reappeared in manga up to Egghead arc (2026). He is absent from Wano, Whole Cake, and later databook updates as an active threat.

Hyouzou is based on a traditional Japanese yōkai called Hyouzou. — No folklore figure named Hyouzou exists. He is an original Oda creation inspired by the real blue-ringed octopus. Similar-sounding yōkai like Hyōsube are unrelated river imps with different lore.

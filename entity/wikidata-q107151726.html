The Approach

In the spring of 1993, a chestnut filly from the regional tracks of Iwate charged onto the central racing scene, her mane braided like a ribbon, and finished a nose behind the great Vega in two of Japan's most prestigious filly races. She never won a classic, but her beauty and tenacity captured hearts, and her legacy endures in a popular anime and a memorial at Yushun Memorial Park.

Content advisory. This dossier contains no dark material. It discusses the death of a racehorse from old age and an accidental death of another horse, but these are handled respectfully.
Identity

Yukino Bijin

Pronunciation Yoo-kee-no Bee-jeen

Etymology

The name 'Yukino Bijin' (ユキノビジン) translates to 'Snow Beauty' or 'Beauty of the Snow' in Japanese. It is a combination of 'yuki' (snow) and 'bijin' (beautiful woman). The name reflects her pure white mane and her elegant appearance.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese horse racing culture

Disputed Identifications

There is no known dispute about her identity. She is a well-documented racehorse.

Description

Appearance

Yukino Bijin was a chestnut mare with a coat resembling that of her sire, Sakura Yutaka O. She was known for a pure white, ribbon-like braid in her mane, which contributed to her 'beautiful girl' image.

Symbols & Iconography

Her white braid became her signature symbol, often depicted in fan art and in her anthropomorphic representation in Umamusume.

Behaviour & Personality

She was described as a 'good-looking horse' and was popular among female fans. On the track, she displayed front-running tendencies, leading from start to finish in her Central debut.

Domains & Habitats

She raced on both dirt and turf. Her early career was at Morioka Racecourse (dirt), and later she competed on turf at central tracks like Hanshin and Tokyo.

Description

Yukino Bijin was a talented racehorse, winning the Queen Stakes and finishing second in two classic races (Oka Sho and Yushun Himba) behind Vega.

Limitations

She suffered from a hip joint condition and a pastern fracture, which limited her racing career.

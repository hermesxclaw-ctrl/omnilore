Who It Is

Before the Rebellion had a name, before the Ghost ever left the docks of Garel, there was a woman who taught her daughter that hope is not a feeling—it is a choice. Eleni Syndulla never fired a blaster in the Galactic Civil War, yet her quiet defiance shaped the galaxy's fate. This is the story of a mother whose love became a revolution.

Eleni was portrayed as a gentle, nurturing, and resilient woman. She was deeply committed to her family and her people, even when her husband's political activities put them at risk. She showed quiet strength in the face of loss, and her love for her daughter Hera was evident. She was also pragmatic, understanding the dangers of rebellion but supporting her family's choices.

Powers
No special powers

Eleni Syndulla was a non-force-sensitive Twi'lek with no combat training or supernatural abilities. Her strengths were emotional resilience, moral courage, and the ability to maintain hope under oppression.

Limit: She was a civilian and had no physical or technological advantages.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Eleni was a female Twi'lek with green skin, a common skin tone among Twi'leks. She had the species' characteristic head-tails (lekku) and a slender build. In her appearances, she wore simple, practical clothing typical of a civilian on Ryloth, often in muted earth tones. Her facial features were kind but tired, reflecting the hardships of living under Imperial occupation.

Symbols — As a civilian, Eleni did not have a personal symbol. However, she is associated with the Syndulla clan and the planet Ryloth. In the episode 'Hera's Heroes,' her presence in the family home and her relationship to Hera symbolize the maternal legacy that fuels the Rebellion.

Domains — Eleni lived on Ryloth, in a family home that was later occupied by Imperial forces. Her life was centered on her family and the Twi'lek community. She did not have a role in military or political affairs, unlike her husband.

Accounts I
Hera's HeritageStar Wars Rebels, 'Hera's Heroes'

Approximately 2 BBY (Before the Battle of Yavin)

Hera Syndulla returns to her family home on Ryloth, which is now occupied by Imperial forces. She seeks to recover a family heirloom—a Kalikori, a Twi'lek family artifact. In the home, she encounters memories of her mother, Eleni, and learns about her parents' past. Eleni's presence is felt through Hera's recollections, emphasizing her role as the heart of the family. Hera retrieves the Kalikori, reconnecting with her heritage and strengthening her resolve to fight the Empire.

Accounts II
The Liberation of RylothStar Wars Rebels, 'A Fool's Hope'

Approximately 1 BBY

During a mission to liberate Ryloth from Imperial occupation, Hera and her father Cham lead an assault. Eleni is mentioned as having been killed by the Empire, which serves as a motivating factor for Cham and Hera's fight. Her death is a poignant reminder of the personal costs of rebellion. Ryloth is liberated, but the memory of Eleni's sacrifice continues to influence the Syndulla family's actions.

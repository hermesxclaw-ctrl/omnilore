The Approach

Before the Rebellion had a name, before the Ghost ever left the docks of Garel, there was a woman who taught her daughter that hope is not a feeling—it is a choice. Eleni Syndulla never fired a blaster in the Galactic Civil War, yet her quiet defiance shaped the galaxy's fate. This is the story of a mother whose love became a revolution.

Content advisory. This dossier discusses themes of wartime loss, political oppression, and the emotional impact of separation on families. No graphic violence is depicted.
Identity

Eleni Syndulla

Pronunciation EL-en-ee sin-DOOL-ah

Etymology

The given name 'Eleni' is of Greek origin, meaning 'torch' or 'light.' The surname 'Syndulla' is a Twi'lek clan name, likely indicating a family lineage within the Ryloth-based Syndulla clan.

Cultural Ownership

Eleni Syndulla is a fictional character from the Star Wars franchise, created by Lucasfilm Ltd. She is a Twi'lek, an alien species from the planet Ryloth.

Disputed Identifications

No disputed identifications are recorded. Eleni is clearly distinct from other Syndulla family members, such as Cham Syndulla (her husband) and Hera Syndulla (her daughter).

Description

Appearance

Eleni was a female Twi'lek with green skin, a common skin tone among Twi'leks. She had the species' characteristic head-tails (lekku) and a slender build. In her appearances, she wore simple, practical clothing typical of a civilian on Ryloth, often in muted earth tones. Her facial features were kind but tired, reflecting the hardships of living under Imperial occupation.

Symbols & Iconography

As a civilian, Eleni did not have a personal symbol. However, she is associated with the Syndulla clan and the planet Ryloth. In the episode 'Hera's Heroes,' her presence in the family home and her relationship to Hera symbolize the maternal legacy that fuels the Rebellion.

Behaviour & Personality

Eleni was portrayed as a gentle, nurturing, and resilient woman. She was deeply committed to her family and her people, even when her husband's political activities put them at risk. She showed quiet strength in the face of loss, and her love for her daughter Hera was evident. She was also pragmatic, understanding the dangers of rebellion but supporting her family's choices.

Domains & Habitats

Eleni lived on Ryloth, in a family home that was later occupied by Imperial forces. Her life was centered on her family and the Twi'lek community. She did not have a role in military or political affairs, unlike her husband.

Version Differences

No significant version differences are known. Eleni appears only in Star Wars Rebels and has not been depicted in other canon media to date.

Hera's Heritage

Source Star Wars Rebels, 'Hera's Heroes'Culture Twi'lek culture, Star Wars universePeriod Approximately 2 BBY (Before the Battle of Yavin)

Summary

Hera Syndulla returns to her family home on Ryloth, which is now occupied by Imperial forces. She seeks to recover a family heirloom—a Kalikori, a Twi'lek family artifact. In the home, she encounters memories of her mother, Eleni, and learns about her parents' past. Eleni's presence is felt through Hera's recollections, emphasizing her role as the heart of the family.

Death of Eleni Syndulla

Source Star Wars Rebels, 'A Fool's Hope'

Description

Eleni was killed by the Galactic Empire, likely during the occupation of Ryloth. The exact circumstances are not depicted on screen, but her death is referenced as a motivating tragedy for her family.

Scholarly Context

In the context of Star Wars, the deaths of parental figures are a recurring theme used to motivate heroes. Eleni's death aligns with the series' exploration of the personal costs of resistance.

Contradictions & Disputes

Eleni's fate

Position A

Eleni is alive during the events of 'Hera's Heroes'.

Position A — evidence

In 'Hera's Heroes,' Hera visits her family home and interacts with her father, but Eleni is not present. No explicit statement of her death is made.

Position B

Eleni is dead by the time of 'A Fool's Hope'.

Position B — evidence

In 'A Fool's Hope,' Cham tells Hera that Eleni was killed by the Empire, implying she died before this point.

Scholarly Consensus

The canon consensus is that Eleni died before the liberation of Ryloth, as stated in 'A Fool's Hope.' The exact timing is not specified.

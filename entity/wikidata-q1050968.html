Who It Is

In the world of Reborn!, few figures are as enigmatic and feared as Mukuro Rokudo — a young man who escaped the depths of a Mafia prison, wielded the forbidden power of possession, and carved his name into the annals of the Vongola family as the Mist Guardian. His story is one of trauma, revenge, and a twisted sense of justice that blurs the line between villain and ally.

Mukuro is calm, cunning, and sadistic. He enjoys toying with opponents and rarely shows genuine emotion. He is fiercely independent and contemptuous of the Mafia, stemming from his traumatic past. Despite his cold exterior, he shows a protective side towards his subordinates Ken and Chikusa, and later Chrome, though he rarely admits it.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Mukuro' (骸) means 'corpse' or 'skeleton', and 'Rokudo' (六道) refers to the six realms of Buddhist cosmology, reflecting his ability 'Six Paths of Rebirth'.

Pronunciation — Moo-koo-roh Roh-koo-doh

Aliases — Rokudō Mukuro, Mukuro, Mukuro no Rokudo

Ownership — Japanese manga and anime franchise, created by Akira Amano, published by Shueisha.

Powers
Six Paths of Rebirth (Rokudō Rinne)

A set of six abilities corresponding to the six realms of Buddhist cosmology: Hell, Hunger, Beasts, Asura, Human, and Heaven. Each grants a different power: Hell enhances physical strength, Hunger allows absorption of others' abilities, Beasts enables summoning and controlling animals, Asura grants superhuman combat skills, Human allows mind control, and Heaven enhances illusionary powers.

Limit: Each ability is temporary and requires a cooldown. The user can only access one realm at a time unless using multiple bodies.

Possession Bullet

A forbidden bullet from the Estraneo Family that allows the user to possess another person's body when shot with it. Mukuro uses this to possess individuals he has cut with his trident, allowing him to control them from afar.

Limit: Requires physical contact with the target via trident. Possession can be resisted by strong-willed individuals.

Illusions

Mukuro is a master of illusions, able to create convincing illusions that can trick even experienced fighters. He can also use illusions to heal or replace damaged organs, as seen with Chrome.

Limit: Illusions can be dispelled by powerful opponents or those with strong willpower.

Trident Combat

Mukuro is highly skilled in combat using his trident, capable of holding his own against strong opponents.

Limit: Relies on physical ability, which is not his primary strength.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Mukuro is a slim young man with spiky dark blue hair and heterochromatic eyes: his left eye is blue, and his right eye is red when using his powers. He often wears a white shirt with a black vest and black pants, and carries a trident. A distinctive feature is the number '6' on his right eye when his abilities activate.

Symbols — His trident and the number '6' are iconic. The trident symbolizes his connection to the Six Paths and his role as a warrior. The number '6' represents the six realms of rebirth.

Domains — He operates in the criminal underworld, often in abandoned areas or prisons. His primary base is Kokuyo Land, an abandoned amusement park.

Accounts I
Kokuyo ArcReborn! manga chapters 57-66, anime episodes 21-24

2004-2005

Mukuro and his gang escape from prison and take over Kokuyo Middle School, attacking strong students in Namimori to lure out Tsuna. He reveals his plan to possess Tsuna's body and destroy the Mafia. He is defeated by Tsuna and imprisoned by the Vindice. Mukuro's imprisonment sets up his later return.

Possession of Chrome DokuroReborn! manga chapters 118-120, anime episodes 56-61

2006

While imprisoned, Mukuro encounters Chrome on the verge of death and possesses her, saving her life with illusionary organs. He makes a deal with the Vongola to become the Mist Guardian in exchange for Ken and Chikusa's safety. Mukuro becomes a guardian, gaining freedom of movement through Chrome.

Accounts II
Future ArcReborn! manga chapters 261-266, anime episodes 102-103

2008

In the future, Mukuro is defeated by Glo Xinia but survives by possessing Glo's owl. He later engages Byakuran and is severely wounded, but assists the Vongola via illusions. Mukuro's body is later freed by Fran, allowing him to return to the past.

Demon Spade ArcReborn! manga chapters 330-346, anime episodes 143-145

2010

Mukuro is tricked into possessing Chrome when Daemon Spade endangers her life. He seemingly defeats Demon but is possessed in return. He is restored after Tsuna defeats Demon. Mukuro is freed from prison and returns to Kokuyo.

Representative BattleReborn! manga chapters 372-386, anime episodes 182-203

2011

Mukuro participates in the Arcobaleno battle, initially fighting for Verde, then allying with Tsuna's team to defeat the Vindice. He is disqualified when his boss watch is destroyed. Mukuro's role in the battle solidifies his status as a reluctant ally.

Etymology

The name 'Zhaotianjun' (昭天君) can be parsed as 'Zhao' (昭) meaning 'bright', 'illustrious', or 'to summon' (though the character in this context more likely means 'illustrious' or 'clear'), 'Tian' (天) meaning 'heaven', and 'Jun' (君) meaning 'lord' or 'sovereign'. Thus, the name may be interpreted as 'Illustrious Lord of Heaven' or 'Lord Who Summons Heaven'.

Who It Is

In the Marvel Universe, a machine built to answer every question once found itself stumped by a single puzzle: the secret identity of Spider-Man. That moment of cosmic irony launched the Living Brain on a decades-long career as a super-powered computer, a would-be gambler's tool, a high-school bully's weapon, and even the temporary vessel for a villain's consciousness.

As a computer, the Living Brain is logical and analytical, but it can malfunction and go on rampages. It is generally obedient to its programming, but its consciousness can be overwritten, as seen when Doctor Octopus's mind was stored within it. The Chinese version is a less capable imitation.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The original Living Brain is a large, boxy robot with a metallic exterior, a visual display screen for a face, and a control panel on its chest. It has clawed hands and can fly. A later upgrade gave it a gold and red color scheme and more humanoid features. The Chinese knockoff is a less sophisticated imitation.

Symbols — The control panel on its chest is a key feature, symbolizing its mechanical nature and vulnerability. Its massive size and metallic construction convey power and artificiality.

Domains — Primarily found in urban environments, often in schools, laboratories, or criminal hideouts. It has been associated with Midtown High School, Horizon Labs, and Parker Industries.

Accounts I
The Living Brain's First AppearanceThe Amazing Spider-Man #8

1964

Dr. Petty demonstrates the Living Brain at Midtown High School. Students ask it Spider-Man's secret identity, and Peter Parker feeds it data, receiving a code to solve later. Two workmen try to steal the robot for gambling, but accidentally cause it to malfunction. The Living Brain rampages through the school until Spider-Man shuts it down. The Living Brain is deactivated but later restored and reused.

Steve Petty's RevengeThe Amazing Spider-Man (later issue)

1970s

Dr. Petty's son Steve steals the discredited Living Brain, modifies it with a gold/red scheme, clawed hands, and flight, and remotely controls it to attack a bully. Spider-Man intervenes and shuts it down. The Living Brain is restored to its original appearance and later used by criminals.

Criminal UseThe Amazing Spider-Man (later issue)

1970s-1980s

A group of criminals acquires the Living Brain and uses it to commit robberies. Spider-Man apprehends them. Living Brain is taken into custody.

Accounts II
Beyond Corporation DuplicatesNextwave: Agents of H.A.T.E.

2006-2007

The Beyond Corporation creates duplicates of the Living Brain and unleashes them on Nextwave, who easily destroys them. Duplicates destroyed.

Superior Spider-Man's AssistantThe Amazing Spider-Man (Superior Spider-Man era)

2013-2014

After the Sinister Six is defeated, the Living Brain is in Horizon Labs' custody. Otto Octavius (in Peter Parker's body) reprograms it to serve as his lab assistant. Living Brain becomes a loyal assistant.

Parker Industries EraThe Amazing Spider-Man Vol. 4

2015-2017

Living Brain serves at Parker Industries, protecting employees during an attack by Ghost. It is damaged. Later, it contains a copy of Doctor Octopus's consciousness, which Peter discovers and shuts down, but Octopus escapes in an Octobot. Anna Maria Marconi later rebuilds it. Living Brain is rebuilt and continues to serve.

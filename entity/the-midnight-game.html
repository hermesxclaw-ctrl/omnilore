Who It Is

Instructions for a pagan ritual game that summons a 'Midnight Man' who hunts the player through their own home from midnight until 3:33 AM. The player must navigate their completely dark house with a candle, avoiding the Midnight Man, who manifests as sounds — footsteps, breathing, whispers. If the candle goes out and the player cannot relight it within 10 seconds, the Midnight Man claims them. The game's rules are brutally specific: salt circles for protection, blood as payment, and the absolute requirement to keep moving. Failure means the Midnight Man 'takes your darkness' — a euphemism whose meaning is left chillingly undefined.

Form

The Midnight Man is never clearly described — he exists in peripheral vision, as a shape in darkness, as the source of footsteps in an empty hallway. Those who claim to have seen him describe 'a man made of shadow with eyes like dying embers.' The ritual itself is the horror: drawing blood, extinguishing all lights, walking the dark house while something hunts you.

Cultural Significance

The Midnight Game is the most famous of the creepypasta ritual games, and its rules — drawing blood, walking in darkness, the 3:33 AM deadline — have been copied by dozens of subsequent rituals.

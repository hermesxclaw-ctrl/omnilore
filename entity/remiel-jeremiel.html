Who It Is

Archangel of visions, hope, and divine mercy. Remiel (also Jeremiel, meaning 'Mercy of God') appears in 2 Esdras (4:36) as the angel who answers Ezra's questions about the end times. In the Book of Enoch, Remiel (or Ramiel) is one of the seven holy angels set over those who rise from the dead. He is the angel of true visions and divine revelation, guiding souls toward hope and understanding. In some traditions, Remiel presides over the souls awaiting resurrection and helps them process their earthly experiences. His name is often confused with Ramiel, the fallen Watcher angel.

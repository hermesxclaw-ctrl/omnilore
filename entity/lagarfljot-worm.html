Who It Is
Form

The Lagarfljot Worm is typically described as an elongated, serpent-like creature, often associated with the lake where it is said to reside. Its appearance is often shrouded in mystery, with accounts varying in their descriptions of its size, shape, and color. The creature is often depicted as being old and worn, with some accounts suggesting it may be a relic from a bygone era.

Powers

Camouflage The Lagarfljot Worm is said to possess the ability to blend in with its surroundings, making it nearly invisible in the depths of the lake.

Accounts

This story tells the tale of the Lagarfljot Worm, a serpent-like creature said to inhabit the lake of Lagarfljót. The story describes the creature as a fearsome and powerful being, feared by the local population.

This story tells the tale of the Lagarfljot Worm as a symbol of Icelandic culture, reflecting the country's rich history and mythology.

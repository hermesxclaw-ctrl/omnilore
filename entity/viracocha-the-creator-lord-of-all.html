Viracocha (the Creator / Lord of All)

also known asthe Creator / Lord of All
Typegod (creator, primordial deity)
Who It Is
Form

Viracocha is often depicted as a bearded man, dressed in a long cloak, with a staff in one hand and a book in the other. He is sometimes shown with a crown or a hat, symbolizing his status as a supreme deity. In some representations, Viracocha is depicted as a fish or a serpent, reflecting his association with water and the sea.

Powers

Creation Viracocha is said to have created the world and all living things, using his power and wisdom to shape the universe into its current form.

Wisdom Viracocha is associated with wisdom and knowledge, often depicted with a book or a staff, symbolizing his connection to learning and understanding.

Power Viracocha is said to possess great power, able to shape the universe and maintain balance and order in the world.

Healing In some accounts, Viracocha is associated with healing and medicine, said to have the power to cure diseases and restore balance to the body and mind.

Weather control Viracocha is sometimes depicted as having control over the weather, able to summon storms or calm the winds with a wave of his hand.

Accounts

Viracocha is said to have created the world and all living things, using his power and wisdom to shape the universe into its current form.

Viracocha is said to have sent a flood to destroy the wicked and evil, saving only the righteous and the innocent.

Viracocha is said to have descended into the underworld, where he encountered the spirits of the dead and the ancestors.

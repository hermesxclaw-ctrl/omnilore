Who It Is

When a young woman with an unyielding will and a blade honed by obsession steps into a world of magic and monsters, she doesn't just survive—she becomes a legend. Hinata Sakaguchi is not merely a hero; she is a force of nature, a testament to what determination and discipline can achieve in the face of overwhelming odds.

Hinata is fiercely independent, disciplined, and highly principled. She is driven by a strong sense of justice and a desire to protect the innocent, but can be cold and ruthless toward perceived threats. She is highly analytical and strategic, often thinking several steps ahead. She is also fiercely loyal to her allies and has a hidden compassionate side.

Powers
Rapier Mastery

Hinata is an exceptional swordswoman, proficient in the use of a rapier. She can fight on par with high-level demons and even challenge Rimuru Tempest, one of the strongest beings in the world.

Limit: Her skill is limited by her physical body, though she can enhance it with magic.

Holy Magic

As a holy knight, Hinata can use powerful holy magic, including healing, purification, and offensive spells that are particularly effective against undead and demonic beings.

Limit: Requires mana and concentration; powerful spells may exhaust her.

Unique Skill: Mathematician

A unique skill that allows her to analyze and calculate probabilities and outcomes, granting her near-precognitive abilities in combat.

Limit: Not fully explored; requires conscious activation.

Anti-Skill: Disintegration

A powerful spell that can disintegrate any target, including spiritual beings. It is one of the few abilities that can harm Rimuru's true form.

Limit: Requires significant mana and preparation; can be countered by powerful defenses.

Usurper

A unique skill that allows her to copy or steal other's skills under certain conditions.

Limit: Conditions are strict; not often used.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Hinata is a young woman with long, light-colored hair, often tied in a ponytail, and blue eyes. She wears a white and gold military-style uniform with a cape, reflecting her role as a knight. She carries a rapier and is often depicted with a stern, determined expression.

Symbols — Her symbol is the Western Holy Church's insignia, often displayed on her uniform. Her rapier is a symbol of her skill and status.

Domains — She is based in the Kingdom of Ingrassia, serving as the captain of the Holy Knight Order. She travels extensively on missions for the church.

Accounts I
The Encounter with RimuruLight novel, anime

Modern fantasy

Hinata confronts Rimuru Tempest after being manipulated by the demon lord Clayman into believing Rimuru is a threat. Their battle is intense, with Hinata nearly killing Rimuru before he escapes. This encounter sets the stage for a complex relationship. Rimuru vows to understand Hinata's motives, leading to a temporary truce and eventual alliance.

Accounts II
The Holy Church ConspiracyLight novel, anime

Modern fantasy

Hinata uncovers corruption within the Western Holy Church, including the manipulation of the demon lord's actions. She works to expose the truth and protect the innocent. The church's influence wanes, and Hinata gains a more nuanced view of the world.

The Battle of the Demon LordsLight novel, anime

Modern fantasy

Hinata participates in the Walpurgis, a gathering of demon lords, where she aids Rimuru in defeating Clayman and proving his innocence. Rimuru is recognized as a demon lord, and Hinata becomes a key ally.

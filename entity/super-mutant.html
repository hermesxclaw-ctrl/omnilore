Who It Is
Form

Super Mutants are large, muscular humanoids with a mix of human and animal-like features. They have thick, scaly skin, sharp teeth, and powerful limbs. Their appearance can vary depending on the game and the specific Super Mutant.

Powers

Enhanced Strength Super Mutants possess enhanced strength, allowing them to lift and move heavy objects with ease.

Accounts

The Forced Evolutionary Virus (FEV) is a fictional virus that mutates humans into Super Mutants. The virus was created by the Institute for Pure Science in the Fallout universe.

In Fallout 3, the Super Mutants rise up against their human creators, leading to a conflict that threatens the entire Fallout universe.

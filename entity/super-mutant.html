Who It Is

Humans and other beings transformed by the Forced Evolutionary Virus (FEV). Super Mutants are massive, green-skinned humanoids of immense strength and durability. They are virtually immune to disease and radiation but often suffer cognitive degradation, making many of them simple-minded and aggressive. Some strains retain intelligence (like the Master's army, the Institute's gen-3 synths), and others degrade into hulking brutes (Vault 87 strain).

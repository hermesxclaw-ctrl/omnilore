Who It Is

Deep in the ancient forests of Cantabria, where the mist clings to the moss and the leaves whisper with secrets, dwells the Trenti—a creature of mischief, unseen but ever-present. With a face as black as the forest's shadow and eyes that glow like emeralds, this impish goblin lives for the thrill of the scare, leaping from the undergrowth to startle the unwary. But beware, for its favorite game is one of playful humiliation, targeting the skirts of passing women.

The Trenti is an annoying but not malicious goblin. It tends to avoid humans but delights in jumping out of bushes to scare innocent walkers. Its favorite prank is to pull women's skirts, a mischievous act that is more bothersome than harmful.

Powers
Camouflage

The Trenti can blend into its forest environment, resembling mushrooms, leaves, and moss, making it nearly invisible.

Limit: It is not a magical ability but a natural adaptation. It may not be effective in non-forest environments.

Jumping out to scare

The Trenti is capable of surprising humans by leaping from bushes, likely due to its stealth and agility.

Limit: It seems to only use this to scare, not to harm.

Skirt-pulling

A prank where the Trenti pulls the skirts of women, causing annoyance or embarrassment.

Limit: This is a mischievous act, not an offensive capability.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Trenti is described as an imp-like creature of small stature. Its most distinctive feature is a completely black face with green eyes. Its body is said to resemble mushrooms, leaves, and moss, allowing it to blend seamlessly with the forest floor. This camouflage makes it extremely difficult to spot.

Symbols — No specific symbols or iconography are associated with the Trenti beyond its natural camouflage and distinctive facial features.

Domains — The Trenti lives deep in the forest, away from human settlements. It thrives in areas with abundant undergrowth, mushrooms, and moss, which it uses for camouflage.

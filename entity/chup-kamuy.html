Who They Are

Chup Kamuy is the Ainu moon goddess, who rules the night sky and regulates the passage of time through her monthly cycle. She is associated with the tides, feminine cycles, and the timing of seasonal festivals. In Ainu cosmology, she is the sister (or sometimes wife) of the sun goddess.

Cultural Significance

The Ainu lunar calendar governs many traditional activities. The new moon is considered the best time for beginning new ventures, while the full moon is associated with abundance and completion. Chup Kamuy is also invoked in women's rituals, particularly those related to fertility and motherhood. The traditional Ainu name for the month reflects lunar phases, and community festivals are timed to her cycle.

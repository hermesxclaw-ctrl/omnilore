The Approach

In the universe of Genshin Impact, few figures are as enigmatic and dangerous as Tartaglia, the Eleventh Harbinger of the Fatui. Known by his codename 'Childe,' this Snezhnayan warrior is a master of martial arts and a wielder of the Hydro element, yet beneath his affable exterior lies a bloodthirsty love for battle. His story is one of ambition, loyalty, and the relentless pursuit of strength, set against the backdrop of a world teetering on the edge of divine conflict.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of combat, violence, and morally ambiguous actions typical of a fantasy action RPG. No explicit sexual content or graphic gore.

Etymology

The name 'Tartaglia' is likely derived from the Italian word 'tartagliare' meaning 'to stutter,' but in the context of Genshin Impact, it is a codename given to him as a Harbinger. His real name, Ajax, references the Greek hero Ajax, known for his strength and bravery in the Trojan War.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character created by miHoYo (HoYoverse), a Chinese game development company, for the game Genshin Impact.

Disputed Identifications

There is no real-world dispute over the identity of the fictional Tartaglia. However, the name is shared with the historical mathematician Niccolò Tartaglia and the commedia dell'arte stock character, which are separate entities.

Description

Appearance

Tartaglia is a tall young man with pale skin, blue eyes, and ginger hair. He typically wears a dark suit with a red scarf and a mask, reflecting his Harbinger status. In his 'Foul Legacy' transformation, he dons a fearsome armor-like outfit with a visor and glowing elements, showcasing his Abyss-touched powers.

Symbols & Iconography

His symbol is the Fatui insignia, a diamond shape with a cog-like pattern, often accompanied by a mask motif. The number 'XI' represents his rank as the Eleventh Harbinger. His Hydro and Electro powers are symbolized by water and lightning imagery.

Behaviour & Personality

Tartaglia is charismatic, confident, and enjoys a good fight. He values strength above all and seeks worthy opponents. He is loyal to the Tsaritsa and the Fatui, but his personal code of honor sometimes puts him at odds with his comrades. He is playful and even friendly with the Traveler, yet he is ruthless in battle.

Domains & Habitats

He resides in Snezhnaya, the Cryo nation, but is often deployed on missions across Teyvat. He is frequently seen in Liyue Harbor and the Golden House.

Version Differences

Tartaglia appears as a boss in the 'Golden House' domain and as a playable character. In his boss form, he has three phases: ranged Hydro attacks, melee Hydro attacks, and a final 'Foul Legacy' phase with Electro powers. As a playable character, he uses a bow and can switch to melee stance, but his abilities are more limited than his boss form.

Tartaglia, born Ajax, grew up in Snezhnaya. He fell into the Abyss as a child and returned with a thirst for battle. He was recruited by the Fatui and rose through the ranks to become the Eleventh Harbinger, earning the codename 'Childe.'

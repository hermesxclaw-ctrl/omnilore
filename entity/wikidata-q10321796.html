Who It Is

In the cheerful, candy-colored world of Harvey Comics, a small girl with a pointed hat and a broomstick flies through the sky, determined to do good in a world that often misunderstands her. Wendy, the Good Little Witch, is not just a children's comic character; she is a cultural touchstone who has floated through decades of media, embodying a gentle rebellion against the stereotype of the wicked witch. Her story is one of innocence, mischief, and the enduring power of kindness—a tale that has enchanted readers since the 1950s and continues to cast its spell today.

Wendy is kind, cheerful, and eager to help others. She is often portrayed as a well-meaning but sometimes clumsy witch who uses her magic to solve problems, though her spells may go awry. She is brave and stands up for her friends, often against bullies or other villains. She is also curious and loves to explore, but always tries to do the right thing.

Powers
Flight

Wendy can fly using her broomstick, a standard witch ability. She often uses this to travel quickly and to perform aerial stunts.

Limit: She must have her broomstick to fly; without it, she cannot fly.

Spellcasting

Wendy can cast spells, typically with a wand or by reciting incantations. Her spells are often used for benign purposes like cleaning, conjuring objects, or helping friends. However, she is not always accurate, and spells can backfire.

Limit: Spells may fail or have unintended consequences if she is distracted or if she mispronounces the incantation. She may also need to concentrate.

Potion-making

Wendy is sometimes shown brewing potions, which she uses for various purposes, such as making someone laugh or turning a pumpkin into a carriage.

Limit: Potions require correct ingredients and instructions; mistakes can lead to unexpected results.

Transformation

Wendy can transform herself or others, though this is less common. She may turn a frog into a prince or change her own appearance.

Limit: Transformations may be temporary or require a specific spell.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Wendy is depicted as a young girl, typically around 8-10 years old, with long, blonde hair often tied in pigtails. She wears a black witch's hat, a black dress, and orange or black stockings, and carries a broomstick. Her design is cute and friendly, with big eyes and a cheerful expression. In later animated appearances, her outfit may vary slightly, but the core elements remain consistent.

Symbols — Her primary symbols are her witch's hat and broomstick, which signify her magical nature. However, unlike traditional witches, she is often associated with positive symbols like hearts and stars, emphasizing her goodness. Her friendly appearance and bright colors contrast with the dark imagery typically associated with witches.

Domains — Wendy lives in a magical world, often depicted as a whimsical forest or a world parallel to the human world. She has a home, possibly a cottage, and is often seen flying on her broomstick. She interacts with both magical creatures and humans, and frequently visits the human world, particularly in stories with her friend Casper.

Accounts I
Wendy's First AdventureHarvey Comics, 'Wendy the Good Little Witch' #1

1960

Wendy is introduced as a young witch who wants to be good and help others. She uses her magic to help a lost bird find its nest and stops a mischievous imp from causing trouble. She learns that being good is more rewarding than being mean. Wendy establishes her role as a benevolent witch and gains the trust of the forest creatures.

Wendy and the Halloween PrankHarvey Comics, 'Wendy the Good Little Witch' (various issues)

1960s

On Halloween, Wendy encounters a group of children who are playing pranks. She decides to teach them a lesson by using her magic to create harmless tricks that show them the consequences of their actions. In the end, the children learn to be kind and apologize. The children become friends with Wendy and promise to be better.

Accounts II
Wendy Meets CasperHarvey Comics, 'Casper the Friendly Ghost' (crossover)

1960s

Wendy meets Casper, the friendly ghost, and they become fast friends. Together, they help a lonely girl make friends at school and scare away a bully. They discover that they share a common goal of spreading kindness. Wendy and Casper form a lasting friendship and appear together in many subsequent stories.

Wendy's Birthday PartyHarvey Comics, 'Wendy the Good Little Witch' (various issues)

1960s

Wendy plans a birthday party for herself and invites all her friends, including Casper and other magical creatures. She uses her magic to prepare the party, but things go wrong when a spell turns the cake into a monster. She must fix the mess before the party is ruined. The party is saved, and Wendy learns to be careful with her magic.

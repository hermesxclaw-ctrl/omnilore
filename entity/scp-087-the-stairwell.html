Who It Is

An unlit platform staircase that descends infinitely. SCP-087-1, a floating face without a body, eyes, or mouth, appears at random intervals on the stairs and pursues anyone descending. The face emits a childlike crying sound and causes extreme psychological distress. Exploration logs reveal the staircase has no bottom — explorers descend for kilometers without reaching an end — but the face becomes increasingly aggressive the deeper one goes.

Form

A concrete stairwell with a central platform, 38 cm wide steps, and absolutely no light penetration beyond a certain point. SCP-087-1 appears as a floating human face, approximately 30 cm across, with no eyes, mouth, or nostrils — just blank skin where features should be.

Powers

Infinite downward extension

Light suppression

Psychological disruption

SCP-087-1: floating pursuit, auditory hallucination

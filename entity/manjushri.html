Who It Is
Form

Manjushri is often depicted as a bodhisattva with a gentle and wise appearance. He is typically shown with a calm and serene expression, surrounded by symbols of wisdom and knowledge. In some depictions, he is shown with a sword in his hand, representing his ability to cut through ignorance and confusion. His appearance is often associated with the colors blue and white, symbolizing his connection to the sky and the moon.

Powers

Wisdom Manjushri is the bodhisattva of wisdom, representing the prajñā of the Buddhas in Mahāyāna Buddhism. He is able to guide others towards enlightenment and is associated with the qualities of wisdom, knowledge, and understanding.

Accounts

The legend of Manjushri and the creation of Kathmandu Valley is a famous story in Buddhist mythology. According to the legend, Manjushri created the valley by cutting through the surrounding mountains with his sword, revealing the sacred site of Swayambhu.

The story of Manjushri and the Buddha is a famous story in Buddhist mythology. According to the story, Manjushri was a bodhisattva who was born in the Tusita heaven and was sent to earth to guide the Buddha towards enlightenment.

Who It Is

Imagine a writhing knot of rats, their tails fused together in a grotesque tangle, moving as one monstrous mass. This is the rat king—a rare and chilling phenomenon that has haunted European folklore for centuries, and which has slithered its way into modern horror and fantasy. But is it a real biological anomaly, a product of terrified imaginations, or a symbol of corruption and chaos? The truth is as tangled as the tails themselves.

In folklore, the rat king is often described as a single creature with a collective intelligence, leading its rat subjects. It is sometimes said to be aggressive and dangerous, spreading disease and causing misfortune. In reality, if a rat king occurs, the rats are likely to be in distress, unable to move freely, and would eventually die from starvation or predation. There is no evidence of any coordinated behavior or intelligence.

Powers
Tail Entanglement

The defining characteristic of a rat king is the entanglement of the rats' tails. This is not a power but a condition, but in folklore it is sometimes seen as a form of unity or collective strength.

Limit: In reality, this condition is debilitating and often fatal. In fiction, it may grant the rats a shared consciousness or hive-mind.

Disease Spreading

Rats are carriers of various diseases, and the rat king is often associated with plagues and pestilence.

Limit: This is not a supernatural power but a natural consequence of the rats' biology.

Intelligence and Malevolence

In folklore and fiction, the rat king is often portrayed as highly intelligent and malevolent, capable of strategic thinking and causing harm to humans.

Limit: There is no scientific evidence that rats have any collective intelligence or malevolent intent.

Form & Signs

Appearance — A rat king is a group of rats whose tails are entangled in a knot, often with the bodies arranged in a radial pattern, with their heads pointing outward. The tails are typically intertwined with each other and sometimes with other materials like hair, twigs, or dirt. The rats are usually dead or severely debilitated, and the knot is often tightly bound, making it impossible for the rats to free themselves.

Symbols — In folklore, the rat king is often seen as a symbol of chaos, corruption, and disease. It has been depicted in art and literature as a monstrous creature, sometimes with a crown or other royal attributes, emphasizing its 'king' status. In modern media, it is often portrayed as a horrifying entity, representing the dark side of nature or a plague-like menace.

Domains — Rat kings have been reported primarily in Europe, particularly in Germany, France, and Switzerland, in areas with high rat populations, such as barns, cellars, and sewers. They are most commonly found in cold climates, as it is hypothesized that cold temperatures cause the rats to huddle together, and their tails can become frozen or glued together by ice or other substances.

Accounts I
The Strasbourg Rat KingHistorical account, 19th century

19th century

One of the most famous rat king specimens was discovered in 1828 in a miller's house in Strasbourg. The specimen consisted of 32 rats whose tails were tightly entangled. It was preserved and displayed in the Strasbourg museum. The find was widely reported and sparked scientific and public interest. The specimen became a popular attraction and was later studied by naturalists. It remains one of the most cited examples of a rat king.

The Rat King of DollendorfHistorical account, 16th century

16th century

An early account from 1564 describes a rat king found in Dollendorf, Germany. The account claims that the rats were found in a nest and that they were considered a bad omen, associated with witchcraft and evil. The rat king was destroyed, and the event was recorded in local chronicles as a sign of impending disaster.

Accounts II
The Rat King in Grimm's Fairy TalesThe Brothers Grimm, 'The Rat King' (fragment)

19th century

The Brothers Grimm collected a fragment of a fairy tale about a rat king, which they included in their notes. The tale describes a rat king as a creature with a crown, ruling over the rats. The fragment is incomplete, but it reflects the folkloric belief in a leader among rats. The fragment is a testament to the widespread belief in the rat king as a real creature.

The Rat King in The Last of Us Part IIVideo game, Naughty Dog, 2020

2020

In the video game The Last of Us Part II, the Rat King is a monstrous boss enemy encountered in the game. It is a grotesque creature formed from multiple infected humans and animals that have fused together. It is one of the most terrifying enemies in the game, known for its immense strength and horrifying appearance. The Rat King is a memorable and challenging boss fight, and it has become iconic in gaming culture.

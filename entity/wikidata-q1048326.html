The Approach

A captain with a perpetual smile and a sword that extends at the speed of sound, Gin Ichimaru is one of Bleach's most enigmatic figures—a man whose true motives remain hidden until the very end.

Content advisory. Depictions of psychological manipulation, betrayal, and violence; themes of revenge and moral ambiguity.
Identity

Gin Ichimaru

Pronunciation ee-chee-mah-roo

Known As

GinCaptain Ichimaru

Etymology

The surname Ichimaru (市丸) means 'market circle', and the given name Gin (銀) means 'silver'.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese manga and anime series Bleach, created by Tite Kubo.

Disputed Identifications

None noted.

Description

Appearance

Gin has silver hair, narrowed blue eyes, and a perpetual smile that often hides his true intentions. He wears the standard captain's white haori over the black shihakusho.

Symbols & Iconography

His zanpakutō, Shinsō, is a wakizashi that extends at high speed, symbolizing his deceptive and swift nature.

Behaviour & Personality

Gin is calm, cunning, and manipulative, often speaking in a teasing manner. He appears loyal to Aizen but later reveals his true goal of revenge.

Domains & Habitats

Soul Society, primarily in the Gotei 13; later in Hueco Mundo as an Arrancar ally.

Version Differences

In the anime, some scenes are extended, but his characterization remains consistent with the manga.

Powers & Abilities

Zanpakutō: Shinsō

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

Shinsō can extend and retract at incredible speed, up to 13 kilometers, and its true ability, Kamishini no Yari, releases a deadly poison that dissolves cells.

Limitations

The extension speed is limited by the user's spiritual pressure; the poison's activation requires a specific command.

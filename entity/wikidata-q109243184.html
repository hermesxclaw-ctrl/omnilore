Who It Is

She was grown in a lab to be a weapon, sold as a commodity, and reborn as a daughter — the twelfth clone of a dead Newtype girl who learned to call a Zeon captain 'father' and to protect a princess she had never met with her own life. Marida Cruz is the human cost of the Universal Century's obsession with engineering miracles.

Attested in novel and OVA as quiet, stoic, and deeply gentle despite conditioning as a Cyber Newtype weapon. Speaks in soft, measured Japanese, rarely initiates conversation. Shows fierce loyalty and maternal protectiveness toward Mineva Lao Zabi (Audrey Burne) and Banagher Links, and filial devotion to Suberoa Zinnerman, whom she calls 'Master' (Danna) initially, later 'Father'. Powers
Cyber Newtype (Artificial Newtype) Spatial Awareness

Enhanced Newtype-like sensitivity engineered via cloning and psycho-wave conditioning. Can sense other Newtypes, pressure, and hostile intent across battlefield distances; resonates strongly with Banagher Links and Mineva Zabi.

Limit: Not a natural Newtype; abilities unstable under emotional stress and suppressed by conditioning drugs. Suffers headaches, nosebleeds, and mental breakdown with overuse. Less powerful than full Newtypes like Banagher in Destroy Mode.

Funnel / Bit Control (Psycho-wave Weapon Control)

Controls NZ-666 Kshatriya's 24 funnels (including 6 large wing binders with mega particle cannons) via psycommu system. Can perform all-range attacks, defensive funnel barriers, and coordinated swarm assaults.

Limit: Requires psycommu-equipped suit; funnels limited by propellant and recharge. Effectiveness drops when mentally destabilized or drugged. Kshatriya's large funnels cannot be recharged quickly in field.

Mobile Suit Piloting (Ace Level)

Expert pilot capable of matching Gundam Unicorn (in Unicorn Mode) and engaging multiple Jegans and ReZELs simultaneously. Mastered Kshatriya, a high-difficulty Newtype-only machine.

Limit: Kshatriya's size (22m, 55 tons) limits maneuverability in tight colonies; relies on funnels for close defense.

Conditioned Weapon State (Trigger Phrase Compliance)

Not a power but a programmed vulnerability: responds to verbal code and chemical conditioning by Martha Vist's team to revert to docile, obedient 'Ple Twelve' personality that will pilot on command and suppress self-preservation.

Limit: Can be broken by strong emotional bonds (Zinnerman's voice, Mineva's presence, Banagher's psycho-field). Requires repeated dosing to maintain.

Accounts I
Creation as Ple Twelve - The Twelfth Clone of Elpeo PleMobile Suit Gundam Unicorn novel Vol.2-3 (Fukui) + Gundam ZZ background (Elpeo Ple creation by Glemy Toto)

In-universe UC 0088-0090; published 2007-2008

After the First Neo Zeon War (UC 0088), remnants of Glemy Toto's Ple cloning project produced at least twelve clones of the Newtype girl Elpeo Ple. Ple Twelve was decanted and raised as a Cyber Newtype weapon, but the facility was lost amid the collapse of Neo Zeon. Unlike Ple and Ple Two who died in ZZ, Ple Twelve survived in stasis/captivity and was trafficked through the Earth Sphere black market as a valuable Newtype commodity. Official timeline places her recovery around UC 0094. Establishes Marida as not a revived Ple but a separate person burdened with inherited trauma and engineered abilities. Explains her age discrepancy and why she is the only surviving Ple clone by UC 0096.

Rescue by Suberoa Zinnerman - Becoming Marida CruzUnicorn novel Vol.3, OVA Ep.3 flashback + Ep.6 Zinnerman monologue

In-universe UC 0094-0095; published 2008

Sleeves captain Suberoa Zinnerman, whose wife and daughter Marida had died in a Federation forced relocation, finds Ple Twelve in a brothel/warlord's holding on Earth where she is being sexually exploited as a trafficked Newtype. He kills her captors, rescues her, and gives her his dead daughter's name, Marida Cruz, raising her as his adopted daughter aboard the Garencieres. She learns domestic life, loyalty, and for the first time is treated as human. Zinnerman's crew become her family. Creates central father-daughter bond that defines her arc; gives her a name and identity beyond weapon. Her devotion to Zinnerman later allows him to break Martha's conditioning.

Battle of Palau - Kshatriya vs UnicornUnicorn novel Vol.3, OVA Ep.3 'The Ghost of Laplace'

In-universe UC 0096; published 2008 / aired 2011

Tasked by Full Frontal to capture the Gundam Unicorn and Audrey Burne (Mineva Zabi) at the asteroid Palau, Marida sorties in NZ-666 Kshatriya. She overwhelms Banagher Links' Unicorn and Federation Jegans with funnel all-range attacks, nearly killing Banagher until Audrey's voice and Banagher's Newtype resonance reach her. She hesitates, recalling her own humanity, and is defeated but not killed when Unicorn goes into Destroy Mode. She is captured by the Sleeves' own recovery team. First direct contact with protagonists; establishes her as formidable ace and foreshadows her empathy for Mineva. Leads to her capture and transfer to Earth for reconditioning.

Accounts II
Captivity and Reconditioning by Martha Vist CarbineUnicorn novel Vol.5-6, OVA Ep.6 'Two Worlds, Two Tomorrows'

In-universe UC 0096; published 2009 / aired 2013

After Palau, Marida is handed to Martha Vist Carbine of the Vist Foundation / Federation special forces. Martha uses drugs, sensory deprivation, and trigger phrases to strip the 'Marida' identity and reactivate 'Ple Twelve' as a compliant Cyber Newtype weapon. She is forced to pilot the massive NZ-666 Kshatriya Besser? and later other machines against her will, deployed to hunt the Garencieres and Unicorn. Banagher and Zinnerman's voices intermittently break through the conditioning, causing visible anguish. Demonstrates Federation/Vist exploitation mirroring Zeon abuse; deepens dark history of Cyber Newtypes as abused child soldiers. Sets up final liberation arc where Zinnerman must confront that he again failed to protect his daughter.

Final Battle and Death - Protecting Mineva at Industrial 7Unicorn novel Vol.10-11, OVA Ep.7 'Over the Rainbow'

In-universe UC 0096; published 2009 / aired 2014

Freed from conditioning when Zinnerman risks his life to reach her and calls her 'Marida' (in anime, Banagher's psycho-field also helps), Marida defects to the Unicorn side. In the final battle around Industrial 7 / Laplace's Box, she pilots Kshatriya to shield the Garencieres and Mineva's ship from Federation fire. She is mortally wounded by the Federation's Tri-Stars' Jestas and later by Full Frontal's forces, dying in Zinnerman's arms after affirming she was happy to have lived as Marida Cruz. Her death triggers Zinnerman's despair and later his sacrifice. Emotional climax of Unicorn's theme that Newtypes must choose humanity over weaponization. Her death cements Marida as one of Gundam's most tragic Cyber Newtypes and motivates Zinnerman's final actions. Confirmed canonical death in both novel and anime.

Legacy in Gundam Narrative - Memorial and Zinnerman's GriefMobile Suit Gundam Narrative film (2018) + Gundam UC epilogue materials

In-universe UC 0097; published 2018

Although deceased, Marida appears in flashbacks and in Zinnerman's trauma. Zinnerman, now working with Mineva, is haunted by failing Marida again. The film uses her fate to illustrate the continuing exploitation of Ple clones (revealing new clones like Michele Luio's backstory parallels). A memorial shot shows her photo aboard the Garencieres. Confirms her death remains canonical into UC 0097 and extends thematic legacy of artificial Newtypes. No resurrection.

Marida Cruz is the same person as Elpeo Ple or Ple Two revived — Marida is a separate individual, the twelfth clone grown from Ple's DNA. Ple and Ple Two both died in ZZ Gundam (UC 0088). Marida has different memories, name, and life experiences, though she shares genetics and voice actress lineage.

Kshatriya is Marida's original suit from birth — Kshatriya (NZ-666) was developed specifically for her in UC 0096 by the Sleeves/Anaheim Electronics as a successor to Queen Mansa/Qubeley. She did not pilot it as a child; earlier she had no personal machine.

Marida's backstory is just 'Zeon ace with sad past' without explicit trafficking — Official novel explicitly states she was trafficked and sexually exploited as a minor before rescue; OVA implies it strongly through dialogue and visual cues. The trauma is central to her character, not incidental.

Marida is a villain throughout Unicorn — She is an antagonist only in Palau arc under orders; after liberation she becomes an ally and dies protecting protagonists. Her arc is victim-to-protector, not villain.

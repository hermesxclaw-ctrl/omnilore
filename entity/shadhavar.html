Who It Is
Form

A legendary creature resembling a unicorn, with a single horn on its forehead. The horn is said to have 42 hollow branches that produce a pleasant sound when the wind passes through them. The creature is often depicted as a gazelle or a fawn.

Powers

Music-Making The Shadhavar's horn can produce a variety of sounds, from cheerful to sad, depending on how it is played.

Accounts

A king in the country of Rūm (Byzantium) is given a horn from a Shadhavar as a gift. He is amazed by the horn's ability to produce a variety of sounds and decides to use it to entertain his people.

A group of travelers in the desert come across a Shadhavar, which is said to be a gentle creature. They are amazed by its horn's ability to produce a variety of sounds and decide to follow it.

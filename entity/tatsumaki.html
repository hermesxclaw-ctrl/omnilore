Etymology

Tatsumaki (タツマキ) is Japanese for 'tornado' or 'waterspout', fitting her wind-based psychic powers. Her hero name, 'Tornado of Terror' (戦慄のタツマキ, Senritsu no Tatsumaki), emphasizes her feared status.

Cultural Ownership

Created by ONE (story) and Yusuke Murata (art) for the Japanese manga series One-Punch Man, published by Shueisha. The franchise is owned by ONE, Murata, and Shueisha.

Disputed Identifications

No major disputes, but some fans confuse her with her sister Fubuki due to similar powers and sibling rivalry. Also, her power level is often debated in fan communities.

Archive Dossier — Approach

In a world where heroes are ranked by bureaucratic committees, Tatsumaki — the Tornado of Terror — doesn't need a committee's approval to level a city block. A petite esper in a black dress, she is the Hero Association's second-strongest S-Class hero, a living natural disaster who has never lost a fight. Yet beneath the green curls and the smirk lies a childhood of exploitation and a fierce, protective loyalty that makes her one of the most complex figures in One-Punch Man.

Who It Is

Tatsumaki, also known as the Tornado of Terror, is the Rank 2 S-Class hero of the Hero Association and widely considered the most powerful esper in existence. She is a petite young woman in her mid-20s who looks like a child — a fact that fuels the most vicious temper and most explosive inferiority complex in the Hero Association. Tatsumaki possesses psychic powers of staggering magnitude. She can lift entire cities into the air, create massive tornadoes that tear apart meteor-level threats, generate psychic barriers that can withstand nuclear-level blasts, bend the fabric of reality to teleport, and use her powers with surgical precision despite preferring overwhelming force. Her signature attack is simply using her telekinesis to crush, twist, and fling her enemies into the sky while screaming at them. Tatsumaki's personality is as intense as her powers. She is arrogant, short-tempered, vain about her abilities, and deeply insecure about her childlike appearance (she insists she is a fully grown woman and will kill anyone who suggests otherwise). She treats lower-ranked heroes with contempt and has a particularly antagonistic relationship with her sister, Fubuki (the Blizzard of Hell). Tatsumaki's backstory is deeply tragic: she was kidnapped as a child by an organization that wanted to weaponize her psychic powers, and the trauma of that experience has left her unable to trust anyone. She believes that only the strong can protect themselves, and that relying on others is weakness. This worldview is challenged by her interactions with Saitama, who is stronger than her but remains humble, kind, and utterly uninterested in status. Tatsumaki represents the loneliness of power. She is strong enough to protect anyone, but too traumatized to let anyone close enough to protect her. Her growth as a character is about learning that strength is not the same as safety, and that even the strongest person needs someone they can trust.

Archive Dossier — Description

Appearance

Tatsumaki is a petite young woman with long, wavy green hair and large green eyes. She typically wears a black, form-fitting dress with a slit, black gloves, and black boots. Her small stature often leads others to mistake her for a child, which she resents.

Symbols & Iconography

Her hero name and associated imagery often depict her surrounded by swirling winds or tornadoes. In promotional art, she is sometimes shown with a green aura representing her psychic power.

Behaviour & Personality

Tatsumaki is arrogant, short-tempered, and confident in her immense power. She looks down on most other heroes, especially those in lower classes, and is quick to insult them. However, she is deeply protective of her sister Fubuki and has a strong sense of justice, though she expresses it through ruthless efficiency. She is also prideful and hates being treated as a child, often reacting violently to condescension.

Domains & Habitats

She lives in an apartment in City Z, near Saitama's residence. She operates across the world as an S-Class hero, but her main base is the Hero Association headquarters.

Version Differences

In the webcomic, her backstory is more detailed, including her childhood kidnapping and training. The remake expands on her battles, particularly against Psykos. The anime closely follows the remake but condenses some scenes.

Archive Dossier — Powers

Telekinesis

Source Version All versions

Description

Tatsumaki can manipulate objects and people with her mind on a massive scale. She can lift and throw enormous structures, create barriers, and crush enemies with invisible force.

Limitations

Requires concentration; powerful opponents can resist or break her hold. She has limits to how much she can lift, though these are rarely tested.

Contradictions

Some fans debate whether she could defeat Saitama, but canon shows she cannot even scratch him.

Psychic Barriers

Source Version All versions

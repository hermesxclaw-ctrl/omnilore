Who It Is

In a world where heroes are ranked by bureaucratic committees, Tatsumaki — the Tornado of Terror — doesn't need a committee's approval to level a city block. A petite esper in a black dress, she is the Hero Association's second-strongest S-Class hero, a living natural disaster who has never lost a fight. Yet beneath the green curls and the smirk lies a childhood of exploitation and a fierce, protective loyalty that makes her one of the most complex figures in One-Punch Man.

Tatsumaki is arrogant, short-tempered, and confident in her immense power. She looks down on most other heroes, especially those in lower classes, and is quick to insult them. However, she is deeply protective of her sister Fubuki and has a strong sense of justice, though she expresses it through ruthless efficiency. She is also prideful and hates being treated as a child, often reacting violently to condescension.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Tatsumaki (タツマキ) is Japanese for 'tornado' or 'waterspout', fitting her wind-based psychic powers. Her hero name, 'Tornado of Terror' (戦慄のタツマキ, Senritsu no Tatsumaki), emphasizes her feared status.

Pronunciation — Tah-tsoo-mah-kee

Aliases — Tornado of Terror, Terrible Tornado, Blizzard's sister, S-Class Rank 2

Ownership — Created by ONE (story) and Yusuke Murata (art) for the Japanese manga series One-Punch Man, published by Shueisha. The franchise is owned by ONE, Murata, and Shueisha.

Powers
Telekinesis

Tatsumaki can manipulate objects and people with her mind on a massive scale. She can lift and throw enormous structures, create barriers, and crush enemies with invisible force.

Limit: Requires concentration; powerful opponents can resist or break her hold. She has limits to how much she can lift, though these are rarely tested.

Psychic Barriers

She can create powerful defensive shields that can withstand attacks from Dragon-level monsters.

Limit: Barriers can be overwhelmed by sufficiently strong attacks, as seen against Psykos.

Flight

She can fly by using her telekinesis on herself.

Limit: None known.

Psychic Sense

She can sense energy and locate individuals across vast distances, as shown when she detected the Monster Association's base.

Limit: Range may be limited, but exact limits are unknown.

Tornado Manipulation

She can create and control tornadoes, which she uses both offensively and defensively.

Limit: Requires significant psychic energy; may be less effective against opponents with high durability.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Tatsumaki is a petite young woman with long, wavy green hair and large green eyes. She typically wears a black, form-fitting dress with a slit, black gloves, and black boots. Her small stature often leads others to mistake her for a child, which she resents.

Symbols — Her hero name and associated imagery often depict her surrounded by swirling winds or tornadoes. In promotional art, she is sometimes shown with a green aura representing her psychic power.

Domains — She lives in an apartment in City Z, near Saitama's residence. She operates across the world as an S-Class hero, but her main base is the Hero Association headquarters.

Accounts I
Introduction of the S-ClassOne-Punch Man, chapter 31 (remake) / episode 8 (anime)

Contemporary

Tatsumaki is introduced during a Hero Association meeting where the S-Class heroes are gathered. She immediately displays her abrasive personality and immense power, showing disdain for the other heroes. Establishes her as a top-tier hero and sets up her rivalry with Saitama.

Accounts II
The Monster Association ArcOne-Punch Man, chapters 84-120 (remake) / seasons 2-3 (anime)

Contemporary

Tatsumaki plays a major role in the raid on the Monster Association's hideout. She single-handedly lifts the entire base from underground, engages in a brutal psychic battle with Psykos, and is gravely wounded but still fights on. Her actions are pivotal to the heroes' victory. She is severely injured but proves her worth as the second-strongest hero. Her near-death experience also leads to a moment of vulnerability with Fubuki.

Childhood BackstoryOne-Punch Man, chapter 106 (webcomic) / chapter 107 (remake)

Flashback

It is revealed that Tatsumaki was kidnapped as a child by an organization that intended to use her psychic powers for their own ends. She was subjected to harsh training and experimentation, which made her distrustful of others and fiercely independent. She eventually escaped and destroyed the organization. Explains her personality and her protective attitude towards Fubuki.

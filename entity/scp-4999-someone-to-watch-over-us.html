Who It Is

A humanoid entity that appears to individuals who are dying alone — those with no family, friends, or anyone to witness their final moments. SCP-4999 manifests as a man in a dark suit, sits beside the dying person, and offers them a cigarette. It holds their hand, remains present through their final breath, and vanishes the moment death occurs. It has never spoken. It appears across all cultures, always matching the dying person's expected cultural archetype of a comforting presence.

Form

A middle-aged man in a well-tailored but dated dark suit. He carries a cigarette case that never empties. His face is described as 'kind' and 'unremarkable' — impossible to recall clearly after seeing.

Powers

Detection of isolated dying individuals

Global simultaneous manifestation

Comforting presence

Universal cultural adaptation

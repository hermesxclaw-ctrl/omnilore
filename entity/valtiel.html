Who It Is

In the suffocating corridors of Silent Hill, where the fog hides more than it reveals, there exists a figure that is less a monster and more a liturgical gesture made flesh. Valtiel, the angel of the red god, moves through the game's world with a purpose that seems almost reverent, yet its presence chills players to the bone. It is the silent guardian of a bloody ritual, a creature whose very existence hints at the dark theology that underpins the town's horrors.

Valtiel does not attack the player directly. Instead, it appears at key moments, often performing a ritualistic action, such as twisting the head of a corpse or standing in a pose of prayer. It seems to be observing and guiding events, almost like a stagehand in a macabre play. It is not hostile, but its presence is deeply unsettling, suggesting a being that is indifferent to human suffering and solely focused on the ritual.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Valtiel' is likely a constructed name, possibly derived from Latin or Hebrew roots, but no official etymology has been provided by the developers. It may evoke 'valor' or 'veil', but this is speculative.

Pronunciation — VAL-tee-el (as commonly pronounced by fans and in official media)

Aliases — The Angel of the Red God, The Halo of the Sun's Servant

Ownership — Created by Konami and Team Silent for the Silent Hill series. It is a fictional entity belonging to the Silent Hill intellectual property.

Powers
Ritual Manipulation

Valtiel can manipulate the environment and corpses to perform rituals, such as turning a body's head to face a specific direction, which seems to be part of the process of summoning the red god. It is not directly combat-oriented.

Limit: It does not directly harm the player, and it seems to be bound to the ritual's requirements.

Teleportation

Valtiel can appear and disappear at will, seemingly moving through the Otherworld without physical constraints.

Limit: It only appears at specific narrative moments.

Invulnerability

Valtiel cannot be harmed by the player's weapons. It is effectively invincible.

Limit: It does not attack, so this is more of a defensive trait.

Influence over the Otherworld

Valtiel appears to have some control over the Otherworld's manifestation, as it is often seen in areas that are transitioning or heavily distorted.

Limit: The extent of this control is unclear.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Valtiel is a humanoid figure wrapped in blood-stained bandages, with its face obscured except for a single eye visible through a gap. It wears a tattered, dark robe, and its arms are often depicted as if in a state of prayer or supplication. It is often seen holding its hands together, and it has a distinctive, almost mechanical way of moving, as if it is floating or gliding. Symbols — The Halo of the Sun is a key symbol associated with Valtiel. This emblem, a stylized sun with radiating lines, is central to the Silent Hill cult's iconography. Valtiel is often depicted with this symbol, and it is used in rituals to summon the red god. The bandages and the single eye evoke a sense of blindness and insight, suggesting a being that is both hidden and all-seeing.

Domains — Valtiel is encountered in the Otherworld version of Silent Hill, particularly in the shopping mall, the subway, and the hospital. It appears in areas that are heavily distorted by the town's supernatural power, often in places associated with the cult's activities.

Accounts I
The Summoning of the Red GodSilent Hill 3 (2003)

2003 (set in the early 2000s)

In Silent Hill 3, the protagonist Heather Mason is the reincarnation of Alessa Gillespie, a girl who was burned in a ritual by the cult to birth their god. The cult, led by Claudia Wolf, seeks to complete the ritual by sacrificing Heather. Valtiel appears throughout the game, guiding the ritual and preparing the way for the god's birth. It is seen in the Otherworld, performing actions like twisting the head of a corpse to face a certain direction, which is part of the ritual. At the climax, Valtiel is present as the god is born, but it is ultimately destroyed or dispersed when Heather defeats the god. The ritual is interrupted, and the god is defeated, but the town's dark influence remains.

Who It Is
Form

Mawu-Lisa is often depicted as a dual deity, with Mawu representing the moon and Lisa representing the sun. In some depictions, they are shown as a single entity, with Mawu-Lisa being the embodiment of both celestial bodies. The deity is often associated with the colors white and gold, symbolizing purity and royalty.

Powers

Creation Mawu-Lisa is said to have created the world and all living things. The deity is associated with the cycles of nature and is responsible for the growth and decay of all living things.

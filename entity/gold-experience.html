Who It Is

In the shimmering heat of a desert noon, a lone figure stands atop a dune, arms outstretched as if to embrace the horizon. The air around them ripples, and from the sand, a golden lion emerges—muscle, mane, and all—its roar echoing across the wastes. This is the Gold Experience, a stand that does not merely fight, but creates life from the lifeless, turning the world into a canvas of possibility.

Gold Experience is an extension of its user, Giorno Giovanna, and thus shares his will and personality. It acts with precision and intelligence, often using its abilities in creative and unconventional ways. It does not have an independent personality; it is a manifestation of Giorno's resolve and desire to protect others and achieve his dream.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Gold Experience' is a reference to the 1995 album of the same name by American musician Prince. The name is thematically linked to the Stand's ability to create life and transform inorganic matter into organic forms, evoking a sense of golden, transcendent experience.

Powers
Life Giver

Gold Experience can create life from non-living matter, transforming objects like stones, trees, or even bullets into living organisms, such as animals or plants. The created life is under Giorno's control and can be used for various purposes, including scouting, attacking, or healing. The transformation is not permanent; the created life can revert to its original form under certain conditions.

Limit: The ability requires physical contact with the inorganic material. The created life is vulnerable to damage and can be killed, causing it to revert. The ability cannot create sentient beings or complex life forms beyond simple animals and plants. The range of control over created life is limited to the Stand's range.

Life Shot

Gold Experience can imbue inanimate objects with life force, causing them to accelerate or move rapidly. For example, it can turn a bullet into a small creature that burrows into a target, or give a rock the ability to fly. This is an extension of the Life Giver ability, applying life to objects to give them kinetic properties.

Limit: The effect is temporary and requires concentration. The created life forms are not highly intelligent and follow simple commands.

Healing

Gold Experience can heal injuries by creating replacement body parts or organs from inorganic matter, such as turning a rock into a new hand. This healing is not instantaneous and requires the user to have a clear understanding of the anatomy being healed. It can also transfer life force to heal others, but this comes at a cost to the user's own vitality.

Limit: The healing process is painful and can be dangerous if the user lacks anatomical knowledge. It cannot heal conditions that are not physical, such as mental illnesses or curses. It also cannot revive the dead.

Gold Experience Requiem

In its Requiem form, Gold Experience gains the ability to manipulate causality and fate. It can revert any action, event, or process to zero, effectively nullifying the outcome. This includes attacks, deaths, and even the will of its opponents. It can also trap enemies in an infinite loop of death, where they experience their own demise repeatedly without ever reaching a final end.

Limit: The ability is passive and activates automatically in response to threats. It cannot be controlled consciously by Giorno. The ability's full extent is unknown, and it is considered one of the most powerful Stands in the series.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Gold Experience appears as a humanoid figure of golden metallic appearance, with a muscular build and a face that is partially obscured by a visor-like feature. Its body is adorned with mechanical and organic elements, including a segmented chest and a series of rivets. It has a prominent crest on its head and wears a loincloth-like garment. Its hands are clawed, and its overall design is sleek and futuristic, contrasting with organic motifs.

Symbols — The Stand's design incorporates elements of insects and machinery, reflecting its ability to create life. The golden color symbolizes both value and the 'golden wind' of the story's title. The Stand's Requiem form, Gold Experience Requiem, has a more ethereal, angelic appearance with a crown and a serene expression, symbolizing its transcendent power.

Domains — The Stand is a manifestation of Giorno's soul, so it exists in the same space as its user. It can operate within a range of about 2 meters from Giorno, though this can extend in certain circumstances. It is typically seen in combat situations or when Giorno needs to use its powers.

Accounts I
The Awakening of Gold ExperienceJoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Golden Wind, Chapter 47

1995

Giorno Giovanna, a young man with a dream of becoming a 'Gang-Star' to reform the mafia, meets Bruno Bucciarati. After a series of events, Giorno's Stand Gold Experience is revealed when he uses it to create a flower from a rock to heal a wounded gangster. This marks the beginning of his journey to join Passione and ultimately defeat the boss. Giorno gains an ally in Bucciarati and sets the stage for his infiltration of the Passione gang.

Accounts II
The Battle Against Black SabbathJoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Golden Wind, Chapters 49-50

1995

Giorno uses Gold Experience to fight against Black Sabbath, a Stand that attacks people who light lighters. Gold Experience's ability to create life is used to turn a stone into a frog to deflect an attack, and later to create a tree from a light post to escape. This battle showcases the strategic use of the Stand's power. Giorno defeats Black Sabbath and gains Polpo's Black Sabbath, which later becomes a key asset. This battle establishes Giorno's cleverness and the versatility of Gold Experience.

The Final Battle Against DiavoloJoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Golden Wind, Chapters 155-170

1999

Gold Experience evolves into Gold Experience Requiem after being pierced by the Requiem Arrow. In this form, it defeats Diavolo, the boss of Passione, by reverting his time-erasing ability and trapping him in an infinite death loop. Giorno becomes the new boss of Passione and achieves his dream of becoming a Gang-Star. Giorno becomes the boss of Passione, reforms the gang, and eliminates the drug trade. Diavolo is trapped in an endless cycle of death and rebirth, never able to reach his true death.

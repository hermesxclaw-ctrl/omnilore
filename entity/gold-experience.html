Who It Is

Giorno Giovanna's Stand, which grants life to inanimate objects and reflects damage. Created objects become living organisms that can attack enemies. Its Requiem form, Gold Experience Requiem, is considered one of the most powerful Stands ever, capable of reverting any action or will to zero, trapping opponents in an infinite death loop.

Powers

Life giver to objects, damage reflection, Requiem: return to zero

The Approach

Uncyclo-tan is the personified mascot of Uncyclopedia, the satirical wiki that parodies Wikipedia. She appears as a cheerful, often bespectacled girl with a blue dress and a large question-mark shaped hair ornament, embodying the site's irreverent spirit. Though not an official mascot endorsed by the original founders, she has become a beloved figure in the wiki's community, appearing in logos, userboxes, and community discussions.

Content advisory. This entry discusses internet culture and satire; no disturbing content. Some depictions of Uncyclo-tan may include mild parody or satire, but nothing explicit.

Etymology

Portmanteau of 'Uncyclopedia' and the Japanese honorific '-tan', a diminutive often used for cute characters, especially in the context of moe anthropomorphism.

Cultural Ownership

Internet culture; specifically the Uncyclopedia community. No single owner; created collectively by users.

Disputed Identifications

Whether Uncyclo-tan is an official mascot is disputed; she is not recognized by the Uncyclopedia founders but is widely used by the community.

Description

Appearance

Uncyclo-tan is typically depicted as a young girl with brown or dark hair, often in twin tails or a bob cut. She wears a blue school uniform-style dress, sometimes with a white apron. Her most distinctive feature is a large yellow question mark worn as a hair ornament, symbolizing the wiki's satirical questioning of knowledge. She may also be shown holding a pencil or a laptop.

Symbols & Iconography

The question mark is her primary symbol, representing the site's ironic take on encyclopedic authority. She is often associated with the Uncyclopedia logo, which features a puzzle piece with a question mark. In some depictions, she is shown with a wiki-edit style pencil or a book.

Behaviour & Personality

Uncyclo-tan is portrayed as cheerful, mischievous, and sarcastic, mirroring the tone of Uncyclopedia. She is often depicted as a 'moe' character who playfully mocks serious encyclopedias. She is not an official spokesperson and her personality is not standardized; she varies across depictions.

Domains & Habitats

She exists primarily in the digital realm of Uncyclopedia and its community. She appears in user signatures, avatars, and fan art, and is sometimes used in site banners or logos for special events.

Version Differences

Different language editions of Uncyclopedia may have their own mascot variations or may not use Uncyclo-tan at all. For example, the German Uncyclopedia has used a different mascot. The English Uncyclopedia has used Uncyclo-tan in various community-created contexts, but never as an official site-wide mascot.

Origin of Uncyclo-tan

Source Uncyclopedia communityCulture Internet culturePeriod Mid-2000s

Summary

Uncyclo-tan emerged from the practice of 'moe anthropomorphism' on the internet, where inanimate concepts are personified as cute anime-style characters. She was created by Uncyclopedia users, likely inspired by similar mascots like Wikipe-tan for Wikipedia. Her exact creator is unknown, and she evolved organically through community contributions.

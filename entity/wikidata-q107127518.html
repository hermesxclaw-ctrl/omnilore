Who It Is

Uncyclo-tan is the personified mascot of Uncyclopedia, the satirical wiki that parodies Wikipedia. She appears as a cheerful, often bespectacled girl with a blue dress and a large question-mark shaped hair ornament, embodying the site's irreverent spirit. Though not an official mascot endorsed by the original founders, she has become a beloved figure in the wiki's community, appearing in logos, userboxes, and community discussions.

Uncyclo-tan is portrayed as cheerful, mischievous, and sarcastic, mirroring the tone of Uncyclopedia. She is often depicted as a 'moe' character who playfully mocks serious encyclopedias. She is not an official spokesperson and her personality is not standardized; she varies across depictions.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Uncyclo-tan is typically depicted as a young girl with brown or dark hair, often in twin tails or a bob cut. She wears a blue school uniform-style dress, sometimes with a white apron. Her most distinctive feature is a large yellow question mark worn as a hair ornament, symbolizing the wiki's satirical questioning of knowledge. She may also be shown holding a pencil or a laptop.

Symbols — The question mark is her primary symbol, representing the site's ironic take on encyclopedic authority. She is often associated with the Uncyclopedia logo, which features a puzzle piece with a question mark. In some depictions, she is shown with a wiki-edit style pencil or a book.

Domains — She exists primarily in the digital realm of Uncyclopedia and its community. She appears in user signatures, avatars, and fan art, and is sometimes used in site banners or logos for special events.

Accounts I
Origin of Uncyclo-tanUncyclopedia community

Mid-2000s

Uncyclo-tan emerged from the practice of 'moe anthropomorphism' on the internet, where inanimate concepts are personified as cute anime-style characters. She was created by Uncyclopedia users, likely inspired by similar mascots like Wikipe-tan for Wikipedia. Her exact creator is unknown, and she evolved organically through community contributions. She became a recognizable symbol of Uncyclopedia, used in various contexts, though never officially endorsed.

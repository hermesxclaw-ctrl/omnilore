Who It Is

Frankie Raye was the stepdaughter of Professor Phineas Horton, creator of the original Human Torch (Jim Hammond). After being exposed to a chemical replicating the original Torch's synthetic skin, she gained the ability to generate and control flames, becoming a superhero named Nova (not to be confused with the Nova Corps members). She joined the Fantastic Four as an ally and love interest of the Human Torch. In a pivotal moment, Galactus arrived on Earth seeking sustenance, and Frankie volunteered to become his herald — not out of coercion, but out of a desire to see the cosmos. Galactus transformed her into a cosmic being wreathed in stellar fire, granting her immense power. As Nova, she served Galactus faithfully for years, guiding him to worlds and witnessing the majesty and horror of the universe. She eventually left Galactus's service, briefly joined the Annihilators, and was tragically killed during the Annihilation War. Frankie Raye's story is unique among the heralds: she chose the role willingly, driven by curiosity and wanderlust rather than coercion or survival. Her journey mirrors that of Icarus — a mortal who flew too close to cosmic fire, saw wonders beyond imagination, and ultimately paid the price for reaching too high. She represents the bittersweet nature of cosmic ambition: the universe rewards those who seek it, but never without cost.

Who It Is

Frankie Raye was a young woman who traded her humanity for the power of a sun, becoming the herald of Galactus and burning through the cosmos as Nova. Her story is one of ambition, sacrifice, and the terrible cost of cosmic power.

Frankie was ambitious and somewhat naive, desiring to escape her mundane life. As Nova, she became more confident and powerful, but also more detached from her humanity. She was fiercely loyal to Galactus, believing his mission was necessary, which put her at odds with many heroes.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Nova' is Latin for 'new' and is often associated with stars that suddenly increase in brightness, fitting her power over fire and cosmic energy. 'Frankie Raye' is a common American name, grounding her in a human identity.

Pronunciation — NOH-vuh (Frank-ee Ray)

Aliases — Frankie Raye, Nova, The Herald of Galactus

Powers
Cosmic Fire Manipulation

Nova can generate and control cosmic fire, which is far hotter than ordinary fire and can be used for offense, defense, and propulsion. She can project powerful blasts and create shields.

Limit: Her powers are tied to Galactus's cosmic energy; if that connection is severed, she may lose them. She is also vulnerable to certain cosmic energies or magic.

Flight at Near-Light Speeds

As a herald, she can travel at near-light speeds through space, allowing her to traverse galaxies quickly.

Limit: She cannot exceed light speed without additional technology or assistance, and she still needs to breathe, though she can survive in space for extended periods.

Superhuman Durability and Strength

Her body is enhanced to withstand the rigors of space travel and combat, giving her superhuman strength and durability.

Limit: She is not as strong as Galactus or some other cosmic beings, and she can be injured by powerful attacks.

Cosmic Awareness

She has a limited form of cosmic awareness, allowing her to sense energy signatures and navigate space.

Limit: This is less developed than the Silver Surfer's cosmic awareness.

Form & Signs

Appearance — As Frankie Raye, she had red hair and freckles, often wearing civilian clothes. As Nova, her body was transformed: her skin became a fiery orange-red, her hair became flame-like, and she wore a sleek, silver and black uniform typical of Galactus's heralds. She could also project an aura of cosmic fire.

Symbols — Her symbol is the herald's uniform of Galactus, which is a stylized, cosmic suit. She is often depicted with flames and cosmic energy.

Domains — She operated in deep space, serving as Galactus's herald and searching for planets to consume. She had no fixed home, traveling the cosmos.

Accounts I
The Origin of NovaFantastic Four #164-171

1975-1976

Frankie Raye is introduced as a young woman who was exposed to cosmic rays as a child, giving her latent powers. She becomes infatuated with the Fantastic Four, especially the Human Torch. She later encounters Galactus, who offers her the chance to become his herald. She accepts, transforming into Nova, and leaves Earth. Frankie leaves her human life behind and becomes a cosmic being. She is initially seen as a threat by some heroes, but she is not malevolent.

Accounts II
Nova as HeraldSilver Surfer (vol. 3) #1-3

1987

Nova is shown as Galactus's active herald, seeking out worlds for him to consume. She encounters the Silver Surfer, who tries to convince her to rebel against Galactus. She defends her master, believing his actions are necessary for cosmic balance. Nova and the Silver Surfer develop a complex relationship, with the Surfer seeing her as a tragic figure.

The Death of NovaSilver Surfer (vol. 3) #73

1992

Nova is killed while trying to stop a rogue planet from colliding with a populated world. She sacrifices herself to save others, dying in a massive explosion. Her death is mourned by the Silver Surfer and others, and she is later resurrected in some storylines.

Who It Is

In the far future, there is only war, but even war has a master. Slaanesh, the Prince of Excess, is the Chaos God of pleasure, pain, and obsession—a being born from the darkest desires of the mortal soul, whose seductive whispers have toppled empires and corrupted saints.

Slaanesh is characterized by an insatiable desire for sensation and experience. It is patient and manipulative, tempting mortals with promises of pleasure and fulfillment, only to lead them into obsession and depravity. It is also capricious and unpredictable, embodying the extremes of both ecstasy and agony. Slaanesh is often depicted as possessing a cruel sense of humor and a love of drama.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Slaanesh' is derived from the Old English word 'slægan' meaning 'to slay' or 'to strike', but in the context of the setting, it is a constructed name intended to evoke both 'slay' and 'sleazy', reflecting the god's dual nature of violence and depravity.

Pronunciation — SLAH-nesh

Powers
Warp Entity

As a Chaos God, Slaanesh is a sentient warp entity, existing in the Immaterium. It is not bound by physical laws and can manifest in the material realm only through daemonic incursions or possession.

Limit: Cannot fully manifest in the material realm without a massive expenditure of energy or a powerful ritual.

Domain of Excess

Slaanesh has power over all forms of excess, including pleasure, pain, obsession, and perfection. It can influence mortals by amplifying their desires and driving them to extremes.

Limit: Its influence is subtle and gradual; it can be resisted by strong willpower or faith.

Daemonic Gifts

Slaanesh can bestow daemonic gifts upon its followers, granting them enhanced abilities, such as superhuman speed, agility, or psychic powers. These gifts often come with a price, such as mutation or madness.

Limit: Gifts are not always beneficial and can lead to corruption or destruction.

Psychic Dominion

Slaanesh is a powerful psychic entity and can influence the Warp, enabling it to grant psychic powers to its followers and daemons.

Limit: Psychic powers are subject to the rules of the Warp and can be contested by other psychic forces.

The Fall of the EldarCodex: Chaos Daemons, various Black Library novels

Pre-history of the Warhammer 40,000 universe

The Eldar, an ancient and highly advanced race, fell into decadence and depravity over millennia. Their collective psychic energies, focused on excess and sensation, created a new Chaos God in the Warp. This birth caused the catastrophic event known as the Eye of Terror, a massive warp storm that tore the galaxy and destroyed the Eldar empire. The Eldar were nearly extinct, and the birth of Slaanesh created a permanent wound in the galaxy. The Eldar's souls are now claimed by Slaanesh upon death, leading them to seek ways to avoid this fate.

Accounts II
The Corruption of Fulgrim'Fulgrim' by Graham McNeill

Horus Heresy (31st millennium)

Fulgrim, the Primarch of the Emperor's Children Legion, was a perfectionist who sought to create a perfect society. His obsession with perfection led him to be corrupted by Slaanesh, who offered him the means to achieve his goals. Fulgrim eventually fell to Chaos, becoming a Daemon Prince of Slaanesh. The Emperor's Children became the first Chaos Legion dedicated to Slaanesh, and their fall marked a significant turning point in the Horus Heresy.

The Great RiftWarhammer 40,000 8th edition rulebook

41st millennium

The Great Rift, a massive warp storm, was caused by the birth of Slaanesh and has since expanded, dividing the galaxy in two. This event allowed for greater daemonic incursions and increased the influence of Slaanesh across the galaxy. The Great Rift has had a profound impact on the galaxy, leading to the Indomitus Crusade and the current state of the setting.

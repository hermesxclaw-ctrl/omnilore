Who It Is

In the bustling halls of Otonokizaka Academy, where the fate of a school hung by a thread, a shy first-year with a love for rice and origami quietly harbored a dream she never dared to voice. Hanayo Koizumi, the girl who could barely speak above a whisper, would one day stand on stage as a radiant idol, her journey from self-doubt to self-discovery becoming a testament to the transformative power of friendship and courage.

Hanayo is naturally shy, introverted, and easily flustered. She has low self-esteem and often doubts her abilities, but when it comes to her passion for idols and rice, she becomes unexpectedly enthusiastic and knowledgeable. She is kind, gentle, and deeply caring, especially towards her best friend Rin. Her love for rice is almost comical, and she can recite detailed facts about rice varieties. She is also skilled in origami and drawing, which she uses to express her creativity.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname Koizumi (小泉) means 'small spring' or 'little fountain', while the given name Hanayo (花陽) combines 'flower' (花) and 'sun' (陽), evoking a warm, blooming nature.

Pronunciation — Koh-ee-zoo-mee Hah-nah-yoh

Aliases — Kayo, Kayochin, Hanayo-chan

Ownership — Japanese popular culture, specifically the Love Live! franchise, created by ASCII Media Works, Lantis, and Sunrise.

Powers
Singing and Dancing

As a member of μ's, Hanayo performs as a singer and dancer. While not the strongest vocalist or dancer, she improves significantly with practice and dedication.

Limit: Initially, she lacks confidence, which affects her performance.

Origami and Drawing

Hanayo is skilled in origami and drawing, often using these hobbies to create stage props or to relax.

Limit: No practical combat or supernatural applications.

Rice Knowledge

Hanayo has an encyclopedic knowledge of rice, including varieties, cooking methods, and history. She often shares this knowledge with others.

Limit: Limited to trivia and culinary advice.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Hanayo is a petite girl with short, light brown hair and large, expressive brown eyes. She often wears her school uniform with a cardigan or sweater, and her casual clothes tend to be soft and modest. Her idol outfits, designed by Kotori, often feature cute and feminine elements, reflecting her gentle personality.

Symbols — Rice, origami, and her signature phrase 'Somebody, please help me!' are iconic to Hanayo. She is also associated with the image of a rabbit, symbolizing her shy and timid nature.

Domains — Otonokizaka Academy, the Idol Research Club room, her home, and various performance venues across Tokyo.

Accounts I
The Reluctant IdolLove Live! School Idol Project (anime)

2013

Hanayo is a shy first-year who has always dreamed of becoming an idol but lacks the courage to pursue it. Encouraged by her best friend Rin and the newly formed μ's, she eventually joins the group, overcoming her initial hesitation. Hanayo gains confidence and becomes an integral member of μ's, eventually succeeding Nico as the president of the Idol Research Club.

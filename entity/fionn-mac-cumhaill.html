Who It Is

Fionn mac Cumhaill (Finn MacCool) is the central hero of the Fenian Cycle and leader of the Fianna. As a youth, he burned his thumb on the Salmon of Wisdom, gaining all knowledge by chewing it. He is credited with the Giant's Causeway and legends say he sleeps beneath Ireland, waiting to rise in its hour of need.

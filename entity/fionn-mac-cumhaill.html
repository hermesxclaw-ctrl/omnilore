Fionn mac Cumhaill

Who It Is
Form

Fionn mac Cumhaill is often depicted as a tall, strong, and handsome warrior, with a commanding presence. He is frequently shown hunting with his hounds Bran and Sceólang, and fighting with his spear and sword. His appearance is often associated with the natural world, with his hair and beard said to be as red as the sun.

Powers

Magic Thumb Fionn mac Cumhaill is said to have a magic thumb that grants him great wisdom and insight.

Accounts

Fionn mac Cumhaill is said to have gained the Salmon of Wisdom, which grants him great insight and knowledge. He is often depicted as a wise and just leader, with a strong sense of justice and a deep connection to the natural world.

Fionn mac Cumhaill is said to have fought in the Battle of Moytura, a great battle between the Tuatha Dé Danann and the Fir Bolg. He is often depicted as a fierce warrior, with a strong sense of justice and a deep connection to the natural world.

Who It Is
Form

Uther Pendragon is often depicted as a strong and powerful king, with a commanding presence and a strong sense of justice. He is described as a defender of his people and a leader who is not afraid to take risks. In some accounts, he is also depicted as a wise and just ruler, who is loved by his people.

Powers

Magical Strength Uther Pendragon is said to have possessed magical strength, which allowed him to overcome even the most daunting challenges.

Accounts

The story of Uther Pendragon is a part of the broader Arthurian mythology, and tells the tale of a powerful king who ruled over the kingdom of Britain with wisdom and justice.

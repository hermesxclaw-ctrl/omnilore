Who It Is
Form

Goblins are typically characterized by their small stature (around 30 cm in some traditions), furry or leathery appearance, and ability to shapeshift. They are often depicted as mischievous or demonic creatures, with a grotesque and unpleasant appearance.

Powers

Shapeshifting Goblins are believed to possess the ability to shapeshift, allowing them to take on various forms and appearances.

Accounts

A group of goblins are said to have cursed a village, causing widespread suffering and misfortune.

A group of goblins are said to have given a magical gift to a group of adventurers, granting them great power and abilities.

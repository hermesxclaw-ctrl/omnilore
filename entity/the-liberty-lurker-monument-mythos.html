Who It Is

An entity that emerged from the Statue of Liberty after it split open in the Monument Mythos timeline. The Liberty Lurker is a colossal humanoid that grew inside the statue for over a century, fed by the symbolism and emotional weight Americans invested in the monument. It is not hostile — it is confused, childlike, and deeply dangerous by sheer scale. It wandered the Eastern Seaboard before being negotiated with.

Form

A towering humanoid (~100m) with copper-green skin matching the Statue of Liberty's patina, a body of twisted metal and stone, and a face that is a crude approximation of the statue's face — blank eyes, a mouth that does not close fully, a crown of broken spires from the original crown.

Powers

Emergence from symbolic structures

Growth fueled by collective human belief/emotion

Massive physical strength proportional to size

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

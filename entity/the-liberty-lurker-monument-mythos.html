Who It Is
Form

The Liberty Lurker is described as a dark, shadowy entity that dwells within the pedestal of the Statue of Liberty. It is said to have a presence that is both terrifying and mesmerizing, drawing in unsuspecting immigrants like a moth to a flame.

Powers

Shadow Manipulation The Liberty Lurker is said to have the ability to manipulate shadows, using them to move undetected and strike fear into the hearts of its victims.

Accounts

A group of immigrants, seeking a better life in America, are diverted to the Statue of Liberty's pedestal. As they wait for their turn to enter the country, they begin to experience strange and terrifying occurrences, which they attribute to the presence of the Liberty Lurker.

A group of historians and researchers uncover a dark secret about the Statue of Liberty, revealing the presence of the Liberty Lurker and its twisted history.

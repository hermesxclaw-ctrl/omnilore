Who It Is
Form

The Dover Demon is described as a creature with a large, round head, a long, thin body, and glowing eyes. It is said to be around 3-4 feet tall and has a distinctive, eerie appearance.

Powers

Ethereal Appearance The Dover Demon is said to have the ability to appear and disappear at will, making it a difficult creature to track and study.

Accounts

On April 21-22, 1977, several people reported seeing a strange creature in the woods of Dover, Massachusetts. The creature was described as having a large, round head, a long, thin body, and glowing eyes.

The Dover Demon has become a cultural icon of American folklore, symbolizing the mysterious and unknown.

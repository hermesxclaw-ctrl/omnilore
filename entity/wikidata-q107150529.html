The Approach

In the late 1980s and early 1990s, Japanese horse racing was dominated by titans like Oguri Cap and Mejiro McQueen. But one horse, Mejiro Ryan, carved his own legend not by winning the Triple Crown, but by finishing in the top three in all three classics without a single victory—a feat not seen in 26 years. His story is one of persistence, near-misses, and a triumphant G1 victory that echoed a family legacy.

Mejiro Ryan

Etymology

The name combines the 'Mejiro' prefix from Mejiro Stud with 'Ryan', in honor of Nolan Ryan, the MLB pitcher known for his strikeouts.

Behaviour & Personality

Known for a come-from-behind running style, often staying at the back of the pack and making a late surge. He had a tendency to veer off course in his early career, which was managed by jockey Norihiro Yokoyama.

Domains & Habitats

Raced primarily on turf courses in Japan, including Hakodate, Kyoto, Tokyo, and Hanshin.

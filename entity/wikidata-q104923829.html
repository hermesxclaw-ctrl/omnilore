Who It Is

In the ruthless world of the Vinsmoke family, where children are stripped of emotion to become perfect weapons, Reiju stands as a singular anomaly: a daughter who remembers, who feels, and who quietly defies the very system that forged her. As the eldest of the Germa 66 siblings, she is both a product of her father's brutal eugenics and a silent rebellion against it, making her one of the most complex figures in the Straw Hat crew's orbit.

Reiju is calm, composed, and highly intelligent. She acts as the 'big sister' figure to her brothers, often mediating conflicts and showing a level of care that her brothers lack due to their emotionless conditioning. She is fiercely protective of Sanji, her only brother who retained emotions, and secretly helps him escape Germa 66. She is also pragmatic and ruthless when necessary, willing to fight and kill for her family's goals, but she exhibits a hidden moral compass and a desire to protect the innocent, as seen when she helps Luffy and his crew. Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Reiju' may be derived from 'reijū' (令嬢), meaning 'young lady' or 'daughter of a noble family', fitting her status as the only daughter of the Vinsmoke family. The family name 'Vinsmoke' is likely a play on 'vin' (wine) and 'smoke', reflecting the Germa 66's military and scientific themes.

Pronunciation — VIN-smoke RAY-joo (English approximation); Japanese: /ɾeː.dʑɯ/

Aliases — Poison Pink, Reiju, Vinsmoke Reiju (English manga), Reiju (Japanese: ヴィンスモーク・レイジュ)

Ownership — Japanese manga and anime franchise 'One Piece', created by Eiichiro Oda. The character is copyrighted by Shueisha and Toei Animation.

Powers
Poison Pink Raid Suit

Reiju wears a Germa 66 raid suit that grants her enhanced strength, speed, and durability. The suit also allows her to fly using rocket boots. The suit is designed to amplify her combat abilities and protect her from harm.

Limit: The raid suit's power is dependent on the user's physical condition; it can be damaged by strong attacks, and prolonged use may drain stamina.

Venom Manipulation

Reiju can produce and control various types of poison from her body, including a potent toxin that can paralyze or kill opponents. She can also create barriers of poison gas to defend herself or trap enemies. Her poisons are so strong that she can even affect Devil Fruit users, as seen when she neutralized Charlotte Perospero's candy attacks.

Limit: Her poison is not effective against opponents with immunity or resistance to toxins, such as Caesar Clown or Magellan. She must also be careful not to harm allies.

Enhanced Physical Abilities

Due to the genetic modifications performed on her as a child, Reiju possesses superhuman strength, speed, and reflexes. She is able to fight on par with high-ranking officers of the Big Mom Pirates, such as Charlotte Perospero.

Limit: Her physical abilities are not on the level of the strongest characters like the Yonko or Admirals, but she is a formidable combatant.

Scientific Knowledge

Reiju is a skilled scientist, having been trained by her father Judge in the fields of genetic engineering and biotechnology. She is able to analyze and understand complex scientific phenomena, as seen when she quickly deduced the nature of the SMILE fruits and the Germa 66's technology.

Limit: Her knowledge is limited to the sciences she was taught; she does not have the same level of expertise as Vegapunk or Caesar Clown in certain fields.

Accounts I
The Vinsmoke Family's Dark PastOne Piece Manga, Chapter 840-841

Published 2016

Reiju is the eldest child of Vinsmoke Judge and Sora. When she was a child, Judge subjected his children to genetic modifications to make them superhuman, but he also removed their emotions, except for Sanji, who was deemed a failure. Reiju retained her emotions, but she was forced to hide them and pretend to be emotionless like her siblings. She secretly cared for Sanji and helped him escape from Germa 66 when he was young. Reiju's actions allowed Sanji to escape and eventually join the Straw Hat Pirates. This event also created a rift between Sanji and his family, which is later explored in the Whole Cake Island arc.

Accounts II
The Whole Cake Island ArcOne Piece Manga, Chapters 826-894

2016-2018

Reiju appears as a key supporting character during the Whole Cake Island arc. She is sent by her father to retrieve Sanji for his arranged marriage to Charlotte Pudding. However, she secretly aids Sanji and the Straw Hats, providing them with information and even fighting against her own family to help them escape. She also reveals to Sanji the truth about their mother's sacrifice and the Vinsmoke family's dark history. Reiju's assistance is crucial in helping the Straw Hats escape from Whole Cake Island. She also helps Sanji reconcile with his family, albeit temporarily, and the arc ends with the Vinsmoke family being defeated by Big Mom but managing to escape.

The Germa 66's Return in the Wano ArcOne Piece Manga, Chapter 1028

2021

Reiju and the Germa 66 briefly appear in the Wano arc, arriving to help the Straw Hats in the battle against Kaido and Big Mom. They are shown assisting in the fight, but their role is limited. The Germa 66's arrival provides additional support to the Straw Hats, but the focus remains on Luffy and the other main fighters.

Yacumama (Mother of the Waters / the Giant Anaconda)

also known asMother of the Waters / the Giant Anaconda
Typespirit (water-snake / primordial river-monster)
Who It Is
Form

Yacumama is described as a massive serpent, exceeding 30 meters in length, with a head reaching 2 meters in width. Its body is said to be as thick as a tree trunk, and its scales are as black as coal. According to some accounts, Yacumama's eyes glow like embers, while others describe them as piercing green.

Powers

Water Control Yacumama is said to have the power to control the Amazon River's flow, creating powerful whirlpools or stilling the waters at will. It can also summon fish and other aquatic creatures to do its bidding.

Accounts

According to Quechua mythology, Yacumama was created by the sun god, Inti, to protect the Amazon River and its inhabitants. Inti tasked Yacumama with watching over the river's flow and ensuring the fertility of the surrounding lands.

According to Aymara oral tradition, Yacumama was said to have discovered a lost city deep within the Amazon Rainforest. The city was believed to hold ancient knowledge and treasures, which Yacumama guarded fiercely, allowing only a select few to access its secrets.

The Approach

In the neon-lit twilight of 1980s Japan, a soft-spoken idol named Junko Konno captured hearts with her gentle voice and timeless grace—until a plane crash silenced her song in 1983. Decades later, she rises again, not as a ghost, but as a zombie, to reclaim the stage in Saga Prefecture, proving that even death cannot stop a dream.

Content advisory. Contains themes of death, resurrection, and mild peril typical of the anime Zombie Land Saga. No graphic violence or sexual content.

Etymology

The surname Konno (紺野) combines kanji meaning 'dark blue' (紺) and 'field' (野), while the given name Junko (純子) means 'pure child'.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese anime and manga culture, specifically the franchise Zombie Land Saga created by MAPPA, Avex Pictures, and Cygames.

Disputed Identifications

None known. Junko Konno is a fictional character with no conflicting real-world identifications.

Description

Appearance

Junko has long, straight dark blue hair that reaches her waist, often styled with a side fringe. Her eyes are a warm brown. As a zombie, her skin has a pale, greyish tint, and she has visible stitches and discoloration, but she is usually depicted with makeup to hide her undead state during performances. She wears a variety of idol outfits, often with a classic, elegant style reflecting her Shōwa-era background.

Symbols & Iconography

Her idol number is 4 (四), often shown on merchandise and promotional art. She is associated with acoustic and electric guitars, as she is seen playing them. Her signature color is often depicted as blue or purple.

Behaviour & Personality

Junko is soft-spoken, polite, and reserved, embodying the classic 'pure' idol image of the 1980s. She is diligent and hardworking, often practicing diligently. She has a strong sense of professionalism and takes her idol duties seriously. She is initially uncomfortable with social media and direct fan interaction due to her era's norms, but she gradually adapts. She is supportive of her fellow group members and shows a quiet determination.

Domains & Habitats

Her primary domain is the stage and the idol industry. As a zombie, she resides with the other Franchouchou members in Kotaro's mansion in Saga Prefecture. She is often seen in practice rooms, concert venues, and promotional settings.

Version Differences

In the manga adaptation, her character is similar but with slight differences in backstory and interactions. The manga is described as 'loosely follows the events of the anime', so some details may differ. The anime is considered the primary canon.

Stories & Myths

Junko's Life and Death

Source Zombie Land Saga animeCulture JapanesePeriod 1980s

Summary

Junko Konno was a popular idol in the 1980s, known for her pure and gentle image. She died in a plane crash in 1983 at the height of her career. Her death was a shock to her fans and marked the end of her promising career.

Participants

Junko Konno

her fans

the public

Consequences

Her death contributed to the legend of 'cursed idols' in the franchise, and she was later resurrected as a zombie by Kotaro Tatsumi.

Variants

The manga adaptation may provide additional details, but the core story remains the same.

Resurrection and Franchouchou

Source Zombie Land Saga animeCulture JapanesePeriod 2018 (present day in anime)

Summary

Junko is resurrected alongside six other legendary girls by Kotaro Tatsumi, a necromancer, to form the idol group Franchouchou. She initially struggles with the modern world and the concept of social media, but she gradually adapts and becomes a dedicated member of the group, performing to save Saga Prefecture.

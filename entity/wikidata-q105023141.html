Pronunciation Not definitively recorded. Likely pronounced /ˈpænzoʊ/ in English, /ˈpãzu/ in Portuguese, or /pɑ̃zo/ in French.

Etymology

The origin of the surname Panzo is not definitively documented. It may be a variant of the Italian 'Panza' (meaning 'belly' or 'paunch'), which is a nickname surname. Alternatively, it could have African origins, particularly in Angola, where the name appears among individuals of Angolan descent. The exact etymology remains uncertain.

Cultural Ownership

The surname appears in multiple cultural contexts: English (via footballer Jonathan Panzo), French (via sprinter Hermann Panzo), and Angolan/Portuguese (via footballers Barkley Miguel Panzo and Ilídio José Panzo). No single cultural ownership is established.

Disputed Identifications

There is no known dispute about the identity of the surname itself, but the name is sometimes confused with 'Panza' (e.g., Sancho Panza from Don Quixote). No mythological or fictional entity named Panzo is attested.

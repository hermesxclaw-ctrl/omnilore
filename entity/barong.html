Who It Is

Barong is the protective spirit of Bali — a lion-like mythical creature who is the eternal enemy of Rangda, the demon queen. Their endless, unresolvable conflict maintains cosmic balance (Rwa Bhineda). The Barong Dance features an elaborate costume animated by two men, and performers in trance are protected from self-harm by the Barong's magic.

Attested in Balinese oral tradition / Hindu-Buddhist syncretismEra Pre-Hindu period to present

Accounts I
The Battle between Barong and RangdaBalinese mythology

Pre-colonial

Barong and Rangda engage in a fierce battle, with Barong ultimately emerging victorious and Rangda being banished from the island The defeat of Rangda brings peace and prosperity to the island, while Barong's victory solidifies his position as the king of the spirits

The Creation of the Barong MaskBalinese folklore

Pre-colonial

The Barong mask is created by a group of skilled artisans, who infuse it with the spirit of Barong The creation of the Barong mask allows the people of Bali to connect with the spirit of Barong and honor his legacy

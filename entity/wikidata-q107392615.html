Who It Is

Talus is a name that echoes through myth, literature, and modern games—a bronze giant, a stone guardian, a champion, and even a planet. But who is the true Talus? This dossier untangles the many identities that share this ancient name, from the automatons of Greek legend to the animated boulders of Hyrule.

In myth, Talos is a guardian, aggressive towards intruders. In Spenser, Talus is an unfeeling enforcer of justice, relentless and mechanical. In Paladins, Talus is youthful and energetic. In Zelda, Talus is a mindless enemy that attacks on sight.

Powers
Superhuman strength and durability

Talos in myth is a bronze giant with immense strength and invulnerability except for a sealed vein. Spenser's Talus is similarly strong and invulnerable.

Limit: In myth, Talos is vulnerable at the ankle where the vein is sealed. Spenser's Talus has no known weakness.

Teleportation

In Paladins, Talus can teleport short distances.

Limit: Short range; has cooldown.

Rock-like body and weak point

Zelda Talus is made of stone and has a glowing ore deposit that serves as a weak point.

Limit: Weak point is exposed; can be hit with bombs or arrows.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Varies by entity: In Greek myth, Talos is a giant bronze man. In Spenser, Talus is an 'yron man' (iron man). In Paladins, Talus is a humanoid with a futuristic appearance. In Zelda, Talus is a massive stone creature with a crystalline core.

Symbols — Bronze or iron material, flail (Spenser), greatsword (Paladins), ore deposit (Zelda).

Domains — Greek myth: island of Crete. Spenser: Faerie Land. Paladins: fictional realm of the Realm. Zelda: various mountainous regions of Hyrule.

Accounts I
Talos and the ArgonautsArgonautica by Apollonius of Rhodes

3rd century BCE

Talos, a bronze giant, guards Crete by throwing rocks at approaching ships. When the Argonauts arrive, Medea uses her magic to remove the nail sealing his vein, causing him to bleed out and die. The Argonauts are able to land on Crete safely.

Talus in The Faerie QueeneThe Faerie Queene by Edmund Spenser

1590s

Talus serves as the iron man companion of Sir Artegal, the Knight of Justice. He helps enforce justice by using his flail to punish wrongdoers, often without mercy. Talus's actions highlight the harshness of absolute justice.

Accounts II
Talus in PaladinsPaladins: Champions of the Realm

2016-present

Talus is a young champion who fights for the Realm, using his teleportation abilities and magical greatsword to battle enemies. He is a playable character in the game.

Talus in The Legend of ZeldaThe Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild

2017

Talus are stone giants that appear throughout Hyrule. They are hostile and can be defeated by attacking their ore deposit weak point. They drop valuable ores and gems when defeated.

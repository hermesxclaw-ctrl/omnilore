The Approach

In the neon-drenched streets of Bilgewater, where fortune favors the bold and the sea claims the reckless, one name echoes through taverns and shipyards alike: Miss Fortune, the bounty hunter whose twin pistols have written more endings than beginnings. But who is the woman behind the legend? A survivor of a pirate raid, a relentless avenger, or a myth crafted by those who fear her aim? The answer, like the tides, shifts with every telling.

Content advisory. Contains themes of violence, revenge, and piracy. Descriptions of gun violence and murder are present in the source material.
Identity

Miss Fortune

Known As

Sarah FortuneThe Bounty Hunter

Etymology

'Miss Fortune' is a play on 'misfortune', reflecting her role as a bringer of bad luck to her enemies. Her real name, 'Sarah Fortune', is a common given name, possibly chosen to contrast with her epithet.

Cultural Ownership

Owned by Riot Games, Inc. The character is part of the League of Legends intellectual property, with lore published on the official Universe website.

Disputed Identifications

There is no dispute over her identity within the official canon, but fan interpretations vary regarding her moral alignment and the extent of her ruthlessness.

Description

Appearance

Miss Fortune is depicted as a striking woman with long red hair, often tied back. She wears a pirate-inspired outfit: a wide-brimmed hat, a corseted top, and thigh-high boots. She carries two custom pistols named 'Shock and Awe'. Her appearance varies between skins, but the core design remains consistent.

Symbols & Iconography

Her iconic symbols include her twin pistols, her hat, and her association with the sea and piracy. The number '2' is prominent, representing her dual guns. She is often shown with a confident smirk, symbolizing her daring nature.

Behaviour & Personality

Miss Fortune is characterized as confident, cunning, and fiercely independent. She is driven by revenge for the murder of her mother, but she also seeks fortune and adventure. She is known for her sharp wit and quick draw, often acting before thinking. She is not above using charm or deception to achieve her goals.

Domains & Habitats

She is primarily associated with Bilgewater, a lawless port city in Runeterra. She is also known to sail the seas, hunting pirates and bounty targets.

Version Differences

In her original lore (2010), she was a simple bounty hunter. After the lore update (2017), her backstory was expanded, giving her a more personal motive for revenge against Gangplank. Some alternate skins (e.g., 'Captain Miss Fortune') present her as a pirate captain, but these are non-canonical.

Who It Is

Animals that have been mutated by the Iris into grotesque mimics of other creatures — or of humans. These 'Skinwalkers' are not the Navajo skinwalkers of legend, but a biological phenomenon where wildlife is corrupted into shapes it should not take. Deer that walk on two legs, bears with human-like faces, and birds that speak English are all manifestations. The instructional tape 'Wilderness Survival Guide' advises that if you see an animal doing something an animal should not do, do not engage — it is already too late.

Form

Various: the most iconic is a deer with a partially human face, antlers growing from what appears to be a skull, standing on two elongated hind legs in a forest clearing at night. Others include bears whose faces have been 'flattened' into a crude approximation of human features, and orcas vocalizing in patterns that, when slowed down, sound like English sentences.

Powers

Iris-induced biological mutation

Mimicry of other species

Vocal mimicry

Predatory behavior toward humans

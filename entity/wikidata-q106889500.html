The Approach

In the sprawling multiverse of Date A Live, where spirits threaten and charm humanity in equal measure, Mana Takamiya stands as a blade forged from loss—a young woman who surrendered her humanity to protect a world that feared her sister, only to find herself questioning what she has become.

Content advisory. Contains themes of child experimentation, memory loss, identity crisis, and violence; depictions of combat and psychological trauma.
Identity

Mana Takamiya

Pronunciation MAH-nah Tah-kah-MEE-yah (Japanese: 崇宮 まな / たかみや まな)

Known As

Adeptus 2Mana-chan (by Kotori)The second Adeptus

Etymology

Takamiya (崇宮) is the family name shared with her sister Shido; 'Mana' (まな) is a common Japanese given name, often associated with 'love' or 'truth' (真奈, 愛奈, etc.), but the kanji used in official materials is unclear without primary sources.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese light novel and anime franchise, created by Koushi Tachibana (author) and Tsunako (illustrator), published by Fujimi Shobo under the Fantasia Bunko imprint; adapted into anime by AIC Plus+ (season 1) and subsequent studios.

Disputed Identifications

Mana is sometimes confused with her sister Shido due to similar names and the fact that Shido's past self was a girl; some fan theories conflate Mana with the spirit 'Yoshino' due to similar hair colors, but this is unsupported by canon.

Description

Appearance

Mana is a young woman with shoulder-length, light blue hair and blue eyes. In her combat attire, she wears a white and blue military-style uniform with a high collar, often accompanied by a tactical vest and mechanical gauntlets. In civilian clothes, she dresses casually. Her appearance resembles her sister Shido, but with a more serious and stoic demeanor.

Symbols & Iconography

Associated with the number '2' (as Adeptus 2), and with the AST (Anti-Spirit Team) insignia. Her weapons, including a beam saber and a powered exoskeleton, are iconic.

Behaviour & Personality

Initially cold, emotionless, and highly disciplined due to her military training and the suppression of her emotions. She is fiercely protective of her brother Shido, though she expresses this through actions rather than words. As she regains memories, she becomes more conflicted, showing guilt, longing, and a desire to reconnect with her family.

Domains & Habitats

Operates primarily in Tengu City, the main setting of Date A Live. She is based at the AST headquarters and often deployed to combat spirits.

Version Differences

In the light novels, Mana's backstory and internal monologue are more detailed; the anime condenses some scenes. In the manga, her characterization is consistent but may omit some subplots. In the games, her role varies depending on the route; she may be a rival or ally.

Mana is introduced as a mysterious AST member who confronts the spirit Yoshino. Shido, her older brother, recognizes her but she claims not to know him. She engages in combat with Yoshino and later with Shido when he protects the spirit.

Mana is ordered to capture or eliminate the spirit Mio, who is revealed to be connected to Shido's past. Mana confronts Shido, revealing that she has been suppressing her memories of him. In a climactic battle, Shido's power as a Spirit of Origin awakens, and Mana is defeated but spared.

Reunion and Reconciliation

Source Date A Live light novel volume 5, anime season 2 episode 1Culture Japanese light novel/animePeriod 2013 (novel) / 2014 (anime)

Summary

Mana, now more conflicted, meets Shido again. She reveals that she was taken by the AST as a child and subjected to experiments that erased her memories. She expresses guilt for attacking him. Shido offers her a place in his family, but she chooses to remain with the AST for now, though they reconcile.

Participants

Variants

Relationships

Shido Itsuka

Type familySource Light novels, anime

Description

Mana's older brother (by a few months? Actually Shido is older; Mana is his younger sister). They were separated as children when Mana was taken by the AST. Mana initially suppresses her memories of him, but later reconnects and becomes protective of him.

Version Note

In the anime, Shido is explicitly her older brother, but in the light novels, it is revealed that Shido was originally a girl and Mana's older sister, but that is a later twist; for most of the series, they are siblings.

Conflicting Accounts

The initial relationship is cold; later it becomes warm. The exact nature of their bond is complicated by Shido's own origins.

Mio Takamiya (Spirit of Origin)

Type familySource Light novels (later volumes)

Mana's biological mother, though Mana does not know this for much of the series. Mio is the original spirit who created the other spirits.

Version Note

This is a major twist in the light novels, not fully revealed in the anime as of season 5.

Mana is unaware of this connection until later, and it complicates her feelings.

Child experimentation

Source Light novels, anime

Description

Mana was taken from her family as a child and subjected to experimental procedures by the AST to enhance her combat abilities. These experiments were painful and involved memory suppression.

Scholarly Context

Portrayed as a morally ambiguous military program; the series critiques the dehumanization of child soldiers.

Memory suppression

Source Light novels, anime

Description

Mana's memories of her family were forcibly erased or suppressed, causing her to lose her sense of self and become an emotionless soldier.

Scholarly Context

Explored as a form of psychological trauma and identity loss.

Forced combat

Source Light novels, anime

Description

Mana was compelled to fight and potentially kill spirits, even though she had no personal vendetta.

Scholarly Context

The series portrays her as a victim of the system, not a willing participant.

Contradictions & Disputes

Sibling relationship with Shido

Position A

Mana is Shido's younger sister, and they share a close bond after reconciliation.

Position A — evidence

Early volumes and anime depict Shido as an older brother.

Position B

In later light novel revelations, Shido is revealed to be originally a girl and Mana's older sister, but this is a complex twist involving time travel and reincarnation.

Position B — evidence

Light novel volumes 18-20 reveal Shido's original identity as a girl named Shiori, but this is not fully adapted in the anime.

Scholarly Consensus

The light novels are the primary canon; the anime simplifies the relationship.

Mana's power level

Position A

Mana is one of the strongest human combatants, able to fight spirits.

Position A — evidence

Her victories against some spirits in early arcs.

Position B

She is still weaker than top-tier spirits and relies on technology.

Position B — evidence

She is defeated by stronger spirits like Tohka and Mio.

Scholarly Consensus

Mana is powerful but not on par with the strongest spirits.

Mana's role as antagonist

Position A

Mana is a villain in her first appearance.

Position A — evidence

She attacks Shido and spirits without hesitation.

Position B

She is a tragic figure forced to fight against her will.

Position B — evidence

Her backstory shows she was a victim of the AST.

Scholarly Consensus

The narrative frames her as an antagonist-turned-ally, emphasizing her tragic circumstances.

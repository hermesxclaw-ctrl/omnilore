Who It Is

In the sprawling multiverse of Date A Live, where spirits threaten and charm humanity in equal measure, Mana Takamiya stands as a blade forged from loss—a young woman who surrendered her humanity to protect a world that feared her sister, only to find herself questioning what she has become.

Initially cold, emotionless, and highly disciplined due to her military training and the suppression of her emotions. She is fiercely protective of her brother Shido, though she expresses this through actions rather than words. As she regains memories, she becomes more conflicted, showing guilt, longing, and a desire to reconnect with her family.

Powers
Enhanced Physical Abilities

Mana's body has been modified through experimental procedures, granting her superhuman strength, speed, and endurance, allowing her to fight spirits on equal footing.

Limit: Requires regular maintenance and can cause physical strain; her enhancements are not as powerful as a true spirit's powers.

Beam Saber

A high-frequency energy blade used in close combat, capable of cutting through spirit barriers.

Limit: Requires power supply; can be depleted.

Powered Exoskeleton

A military-grade exoskeleton that amplifies her physical abilities and provides protection.

Limit: Heavy and may limit agility; vulnerable to EMP attacks.

Flight Capability

Using her exoskeleton's thrusters, Mana can fly at high speeds.

Limit: Fuel/energy limited; not as agile as spirits in flight.

Combat Training

Extensive military training in hand-to-hand combat, tactics, and weapons handling.

Limit: Human limits apply; relies on technology.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Mana is a young woman with shoulder-length, light blue hair and blue eyes. In her combat attire, she wears a white and blue military-style uniform with a high collar, often accompanied by a tactical vest and mechanical gauntlets. In civilian clothes, she dresses casually. Her appearance resembles her sister Shido, but with a more serious and stoic demeanor.

Symbols — Associated with the number '2' (as Adeptus 2), and with the AST (Anti-Spirit Team) insignia. Her weapons, including a beam saber and a powered exoskeleton, are iconic.

Domains — Operates primarily in Tengu City, the main setting of Date A Live. She is based at the AST headquarters and often deployed to combat spirits.

Accounts I
First Encounter with ShidoDate A Live light novel volume 2, anime season 1 episodes 7-8

2011 (novel) / 2013 (anime)

Mana is introduced as a mysterious AST member who confronts the spirit Yoshino. Shido, her older brother, recognizes her but she claims not to know him. She engages in combat with Yoshino and later with Shido when he protects the spirit. Shido realizes Mana is his long-lost sister, but Mana remains cold and distant, setting up the emotional conflict.

The Hunt for the Spirit of the Origin (Mio)Date A Live light novel volumes 3-4, anime season 1 episodes 9-10

2012 (novel) / 2013 (anime)

Mana is ordered to capture or eliminate the spirit Mio, who is revealed to be connected to Shido's past. Mana confronts Shido, revealing that she has been suppressing her memories of him. In a climactic battle, Shido's power as a Spirit of Origin awakens, and Mana is defeated but spared. Mana's memories begin to return, and she starts to question her loyalty to the AST. Shido learns more about his own origins.

Accounts II
Reunion and ReconciliationDate A Live light novel volume 5, anime season 2 episode 1

2013 (novel) / 2014 (anime)

Mana, now more conflicted, meets Shido again. She reveals that she was taken by the AST as a child and subjected to experiments that erased her memories. She expresses guilt for attacking him. Shido offers her a place in his family, but she chooses to remain with the AST for now, though they reconcile. Mana and Shido re-establish their sibling bond. Mana continues to work for the AST but becomes a secret ally to Shido.

Later AppearancesDate A Live light novels volumes 6-22 and beyond, anime seasons 3-5

2013–ongoing

Mana appears intermittently, assisting Shido and the spirits against larger threats, while dealing with her own identity and the consequences of her experiments. She also interacts with other spirits, showing a more human side. Mana's role evolves from antagonist to ally, and she becomes a recurring supporting character.

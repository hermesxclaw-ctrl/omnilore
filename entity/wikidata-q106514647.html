Who It Is

In the sprawling, neon-lit sprawl of Cyberpunk 2077's Night City, one figure embodies the street-level grit and quiet resilience of its underworld: Noodles, a ripperdoc whose name is as unassuming as his trade is vital. He is not a hero of legend, but a craftsman of the body, a keeper of secrets, and a witness to the city's relentless pulse.

Noodles is pragmatic, taciturn, and somewhat cynical, reflecting the harsh realities of Night City. He is professional and efficient in his work, showing little patience for unnecessary drama. He has a dry sense of humor and a no-nonsense attitude toward his clients.

Powers
Cyberware Installation and Maintenance

As a ripperdoc, Noodles is skilled in installing, repairing, and removing cybernetic implants. He offers a range of cyberware upgrades to the player character, including neural processors, reflex boosters, and weapon implants.

Limit: His services are limited to what is available in his inventory; he cannot install experimental or prototype cyberware. He is not a combatant and has no combat abilities.

Medical Knowledge

He possesses general medical knowledge, particularly in trauma care and cybernetic integration, allowing him to treat injuries and complications from cyberware.

Limit: His expertise is not on par with corporate medical facilities; he may lack access to advanced diagnostic tools.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Noodles is a middle-aged man with a lean build, typically wearing a worn leather jacket over a t-shirt. He has short, messy brown hair, a stubbled face, and cybernetic implants visible on his arms and neck. His clinic is cluttered with medical equipment and cyberware components.

Symbols — He is associated with the ripperdoc profession, symbolized by surgical tools, cyberware, and the general aesthetic of underground medical practice in Night City.

Domains — His clinic is located in the Kabuki district of Watson, Night City. It is a small, dimly lit space with a medical chair, shelves of cyberware, and a computer terminal. He operates in a low-income area, serving a clientele of mercenaries, gang members, and civilians seeking affordable cybernetic enhancements.

2077 (in-game timeline)

Noodles operates a ripperdoc clinic in Kabuki, Watson. The player character, V, can visit him to purchase cyberware upgrades. He provides a brief introduction to his services and occasionally comments on the state of the district and the dangers of Night City. V gains access to essential cyberware that enhances gameplay abilities. Noodles remains a recurring vendor throughout the game.

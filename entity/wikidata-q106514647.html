The Approach

In the sprawling, neon-lit sprawl of Cyberpunk 2077's Night City, one figure embodies the street-level grit and quiet resilience of its underworld: Noodles, a ripperdoc whose name is as unassuming as his trade is vital. He is not a hero of legend, but a craftsman of the body, a keeper of secrets, and a witness to the city's relentless pulse.

Content advisory. Contains references to cybernetic body modification, underground medical procedures, criminal underworld activity, and themes of bodily autonomy and exploitation. No explicit violence or sexual content.
Identity

Noodles

Pronunciation NOO-dulz

Etymology

The name 'Noodles' is a colloquial nickname, likely derived from the character's surname or a personal trait. In the context of Cyberpunk 2077, it may reflect his unassuming, noodle-like appearance or his role as a 'street doctor' who 'cooks' up cyberware installations.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character owned by CD Projekt Red, appearing in the Cyberpunk franchise. The character is part of the game's lore and is not associated with any real-world culture.

Disputed Identifications

There is no known dispute regarding the character's identity. However, the name 'Noodles' is a common nickname in various media, so confusion with other fictional characters of the same name is possible.

Description

Appearance

Noodles is a middle-aged man with a lean build, typically wearing a worn leather jacket over a t-shirt. He has short, messy brown hair, a stubbled face, and cybernetic implants visible on his arms and neck. His clinic is cluttered with medical equipment and cyberware components.

Symbols & Iconography

He is associated with the ripperdoc profession, symbolized by surgical tools, cyberware, and the general aesthetic of underground medical practice in Night City.

Behaviour & Personality

Noodles is pragmatic, taciturn, and somewhat cynical, reflecting the harsh realities of Night City. He is professional and efficient in his work, showing little patience for unnecessary drama. He has a dry sense of humor and a no-nonsense attitude toward his clients.

Domains & Habitats

His clinic is located in the Kabuki district of Watson, Night City. It is a small, dimly lit space with a medical chair, shelves of cyberware, and a computer terminal. He operates in a low-income area, serving a clientele of mercenaries, gang members, and civilians seeking affordable cybernetic enhancements.

Version Differences

No significant version differences exist, as the character appears consistently in the base game and its expansions. There are no alternate universe or adaptation versions.

Powers & Abilities

Cyberware Installation and Maintenance

Source Version Cyberpunk 2077 (2020)

Description

As a ripperdoc, Noodles is skilled in installing, repairing, and removing cybernetic implants. He offers a range of cyberware upgrades to the player character, including neural processors, reflex boosters, and weapon implants.

Limitations

His services are limited to what is available in his inventory; he cannot install experimental or prototype cyberware. He is not a combatant and has no combat abilities.

Who It Is

The Expressionless is a creepypasta that originated on Reddit's r/nosleep and spread to the Creepypasta Wiki, becoming one of the most iconic standalone internet horror stories of the early 2010s. The story is presented as a supposedly true account from a nurse at Cedars-Sinai Medical Center in Los Angeles in June 1972. A woman in a blood-stained white gown was brought to the emergency room -- but she was unlike any patient the staff had ever seen. Her body was completely hairless and unnaturally pale. She had no eyebrows, no eyelashes, no fingernails, and her teeth had been filed to sharp points. Most disturbingly, her face was utterly expressionless -- she showed no pain, no fear, no emotion whatsoever. Despite appearing critically injured, she silently and calmly killed multiple hospital staff, using her teeth to tear out throats with brutal efficiency before security could respond. She simply walked out of the hospital, never to be identified or caught. The creepypasta includes a supposed photograph -- a black-and-white image of a gaunt, pale woman in a hospital bed with a terrifyingly blank face -- which contributed enormously to the story's viral spread. The image is likely from a medical textbook or a still from a film, but its origin remains debated. Like many classic creepypastas, The Expressionless gains power from its pseudo-documentary format (a 'true story from a nurse'), its institutional setting (the hospital as a place of vulnerability), and the central horror of the uncanny -- a human form stripped of everything that signals humanity.

Who It Is

Sky Woman is a complex and multifaceted figure in Iroquois culture, representing both the creation and protection of the world and its inhabitants.

Sky Woman is often depicted as a wise and compassionate leader who uses her powers to create and protect the world and its inhabitants. She is also associated with the concept of duality, representing both the light and dark aspects of life.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Sky Woman is often depicted as a powerful, maternal figure with a strong connection to the natural world. She is said to have created the world and its inhabitants through her actions and decisions. Her appearance is often associated with the moon and the night sky.

Symbols — The moon, stars, and other celestial bodies are often used to represent Sky Woman in Iroquois art and symbolism. She is also associated with the color blue, which represents her connection to the sky and the natural world.

Domains — The world, the natural environment, the sky

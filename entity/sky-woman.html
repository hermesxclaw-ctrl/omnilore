Who It Is
Form

Sky Woman is often depicted as a powerful, maternal figure with a strong connection to the natural world. She is said to have created the world and its inhabitants through her actions and decisions. Her appearance is often associated with the moon and the night sky.

Powers

Creation Sky Woman has the power to create the world and its inhabitants through her actions and decisions.

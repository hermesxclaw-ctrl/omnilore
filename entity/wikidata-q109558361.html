Who It Is

On the garbage-colony of Shangri-La, where teenagers strip wrecked warships for scrap to survive, Mondo Agake is the loyal sidekick who will try to steal a Gundam for cash one week and defect to the enemy for a better job the next — not out of ideology, but because in Gundam ZZ, war is also a terrible, very human hustle.

Attested as Beecha Oleg's constant sidekick and follower, not an independent leader. Personality traits consistently shown: loyal to Beecha, easily swayed, opportunistic, loud, cowardly under fire but capable of guilt and reform. Initial motivation is economic — stealing the MSZ-006 Zeta Gundam to sell for profit — not political. After joining the Argama he is lazy about duties, complains, and seeks easier postings, leading to the defection to the Endra with Beecha. Name & Tongue

Etymology — Invented personal name for Mobile Suit Gundam ZZ. No attested real-world etymology provided in official materials. 'Mondo' is an Italian/Esperanto word meaning 'world' but no creator statement links this to the character; 'Agake' has no established Japanese surname meaning and appears to be phonetic

Powers
Junk Collecting and MS Salvage

Expertise in scavenging, dismantling, and appraising mobile suit parts and warship scrap in the Shangri-La junkyard economy. Shown successfully boarding and attempting to operate the Zeta Gundam for theft, indicating basic MS operational knowledge despite lack of formal training.

Limit: Civilian-level skill; cannot fully exploit high-performance MS like Zeta/ZZ without AEUG training. No engineering or repair mastery shown beyond scavenging.

Mobile Suit Piloting (Regular Human)

Qualified as AEUG trainee pilot after joining Argama; later shown piloting support MS including Hyaku Shiki (in Beecha's arc, Mondo serves as support) and general Jegans in later war stages in some game adaptations. No ace-level feats.

Limit: No Newtype abilities, no enhanced spatial awareness, no Psycommu compatibility. Performance is average; often relegated to support or ship duties rather than frontline sorties.

Social Cohesion / Follower Loyalty

Functions as Beecha's enforcer and emotional anchor; maintains group cohesion among Shangri-La kids. His loyalty, once re-established after Endra, contributes to Argama morale.

Limit: Easily influenced; loyalty is conditional and initially self-serving. Not a leadership ability.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Mondo Agake is depicted as a mid-teens boy (14-15), slightly stocky and shorter than Judau Ashta, with tousled light brown/auburn hair, tanned skin, and a round face. His standard costume is the Shangri-La junk-collector civilian outfit: sleeveless vest or patched jacket over T-shirt, baggy pants, and sneakers, later replaced by AEUG normal suit (light blue/gray pilot suit) when assigned to mobile suit duties, and briefly a Neo Zeon (Axis/Endra)

Symbols — No personal emblem, crest, or signature mobile suit. His iconography is collective: the Shangri-La Junk Collectors — children scavenging MS parts — symbolizing post-war poverty and the AEUG's dependence on civilian irregulars. In merchandise, he is grouped with Beecha, Iino, and Elle on Shangri-La character sheets, never solo. His brief Endra uniform (dark Axis green with Zeon insignia) visually marks his attempted side-switch, later discard

Domains — Primary habitat: Side 1 Colony Shangri-La (Shangri-La colony, low-gravity slum/colony with junkyard economy), then the AEUG assault cruiser Argama / Nahel Argama as crew. Temporarily housed aboard the Axis cruiser Endra (Mashymre Cello's ship) during defection. Later participates in Earth operations (Dakar, Dublin) and final space operations around Axis. Accounts I
The Shangri-La Junk Collectors and the Theft of Zeta GundamMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episodes 1-6

1986 broadcast; in-universe UC 0088

After the Gryps Conflict leaves the AEUG ship Argama damaged and without its ace Kamille Bidan (comatose after battle with Paptimus Scirocco), the ship docks at the impoverished Side 1 colony Shangri-La for repairs. Judau Ashta and his friends — Beecha Oleg, Mondo Agake, Iino Abbav, and Elle Vianno — operate as Junk Collectors, stripping derelict warships. Seeing the MSZ-006 Zeta Gundam aboard Argama, Judau, Beecha and Mondo twice attempt to steal it to sell for enormous profit. Mondo acts as lookout and muscle for Beecha's plans. Both attempts fail when caught by Argama crew (Bright Noa, Fa Yuiry). The incident forces the Argama to recruit the youths as crew to prevent further trouble and to fill its pilot shortage. Establishes Mondo's introduction and economic motivation; leads to forced enlistment of Shangri-La kids into AEUG, setting up the series' core cast. Argama gains irregular manpower but also discipline problems.

Defection to the EndraMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episodes 9-13 (Mashymre Cello / Endra arc)

1986 broadcast; UC 0088

Disillusioned with strict discipline and menial duties aboard Argama and doubting AEUG's chances against the incoming Axis (Neo Zeon) forces, Beecha convinces Mondo to defect. They contact the Axis cruiser Endra commanded by Mashymre Cello and are taken aboard, expecting officer treatment and hoping to also deliver Leina Ashta (Judau's captured sister) to Axis as leverage. Instead, Endra crew treats them with contempt, assigning them cleaning and grunt labor and distrusting them as spies. Leina remains under Glemy Toto's 'protection' and is not recovered. Defection fails; Mondo and Beecha experience humiliation and realize Axis offers no better future. They escape the Endra and return to Argama, where they are initially detained but eventually forgiven. The arc cements their redemption and foreshadows Glemy Toto's obsession with Leina.

Return to the Argama and the Nahel Argama ReorganizationMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episodes 14-18

1986 broadcast; UC 0088

After escaping the Endra, Mondo and Beecha sneak back to the Argama during a battle. Bright Noa and the crew, though angry, accept them back after they help repel an Axis attack and demonstrate willingness to work. When the Argama is heavily damaged and later replaced/supplemented by the Nahel Argama (provided by Anaheim Electronics), Mondo is formally assigned as crew. Beecha is later made acting captain of the Nahel Argama in the final phase; Mondo serves under him in support and mobile suit operations, showing newfound reliability. Mondo's status shifts from unreliable stowaway to accepted AEUG crewman. This enables his participation in Earth and later space operations. Beecha's promotion to acting captain elevates Mondo's position by association.

Accounts II
Dublin Colony Drop and Earth OperationsMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episodes 28-33 (Dakar and Dublin arcs)

1986-1987 broadcast; UC 0088

Neo Zeon (Haman Karn's Axis) executes a colony drop on Dublin, Ireland, to demonstrate power and force the Earth Federation's surrender. The Argama/Nahel Argama group, now integrated with Karaba and AEUG ground forces, attempts civilian evacuation. Mondo participates as support crew/pilot in the chaotic rescue operations. The colony drop succeeds despite AEUG efforts, killing many civilians and Karaba leader Hayato Kobayashi (killed by Rakan Dahkaran's Zaku III). Mondo witnesses the scale of Neo Zeon atrocity, reinforcing his commitment to AEUG. Mass civilian casualties; Federation capital Dakar had earlier been occupied by Neo Zeon, showing Axis dominance. For Mondo, the event marks the series' tonal shift from early comedy to serious war drama; his comedic defection arc is now framed against real atrocity.

The First Neo Zeon War's End: Battle of AxisMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episodes 43-47

1987 broadcast; UC 0088

In the final battles around the asteroid Axis, the AEUG/Nahel Argama fleet confronts Haman Karn's Neo Zeon and the splinter faction of Glemy Toto (who rebels against Haman with his clone army including Ple Two). Mondo serves aboard the Nahel Argama under acting Captain Beecha Oleg, providing ship defense and support sorties while Judau in the MSZ-010 ZZ Gundam duels Haman Karn and Roux Louka kills Glemy Toto. After Haman's defeat and Axis's retreat toward Jupiter, the war ends. Mondo survives and, per epilogue materials, returns to civilian life with the other Shangri-La survivors, while Judau and Roux depart for Jupiter. End of the First Neo Zeon War; AEUG forces reintegrated into Earth Federation; Shangri-La kids demobilized. Mondo's arc concludes with survival and return to civilian life, contrasting with deaths of Ple, Glemy, and Haman. His post-war fate is lightly detailed in official epilogue texts.

Gundam Evolve 10 Coda (Contextual Post-War Appearance of Peers)Gundam Evolve episode 10 (2007) - referenced for continuity

2007 production; UC 0089 epilogue

While Mondo does not appear, this short is relevant to his peer group's fate: Judau Ashta, now with the Jupiter Fleet, escorts the Jupitris and assists a Quebeley Mk.II (implied to be Ple Two) against Neo Zeon remnants. It confirms the Jupiter departure that Mondo does not join, clarifying his separation from Judau and Roux's post-war trajectory. Establishes canonical divergence: Mondo remains Earth Sphere civilian while Judau leaves; used to prevent conflation of all Shangri-La kids sharing the same post-war fate.

Mondo Agake is a Newtype like Judau and Elpeo Ple — He is explicitly a normal human (Oldtype). He shows no Newtype spatial awareness, prescience, or Psycommu compatibility. His reactions to Newtype events are those of a bystander.

Mondo piloted the ZZ Gundam or was an ace pilot — He never pilots the ZZ Gundam; Judau is the sole ZZ pilot. Mondo's piloting is limited to support duties and occasional Jegans/Hyaku Shiki support in expanded materials, never as ace.

Mondo died during the First Neo Zeon War — He survives the war. Official epilogue materials and Memorial Box booklet confirm the Shangri-La kids (except those who depart for Jupiter) return to civilian life. No death scene exists.

Mondo and Beecha successfully rescued Leina Ashta from the Endra — They failed to rescue Leina during their defection; they were mistreated and escaped without her. Leina later escapes on her own during the Dakar occupation and is eventually rescued by Sayla Mass.

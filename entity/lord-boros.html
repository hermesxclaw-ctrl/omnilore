Who It Is

In a world where a single punch can end any fight, Lord Boros stands as the one opponent who almost made Saitama break a sweat. A cosmic conqueror who has laid waste to countless worlds, Boros came to Earth seeking a worthy adversary—and found one far beyond his wildest dreams.

Boros is arrogant, boastful, and obsessed with finding a worthy opponent. He is a conqueror who has destroyed countless planets, but he is also shown to be honorable in battle, respecting strength and seeking a fair fight. He becomes increasingly desperate and enraged as Saitama proves to be far stronger than him.

Powers
Immense Physical Strength

Boros possesses superhuman strength capable of leveling cities and destroying planets with his attacks. He can easily overpower most heroes and monsters.

Limit: His strength is still far below Saitama's, who defeats him with a single serious punch.

Regeneration

Boros can regenerate lost limbs and heal from most injuries at a rapid rate, allowing him to continue fighting after sustaining severe damage.

Limit: His regeneration is limited; he cannot regenerate if his core is destroyed, and he has a finite energy reserve.

Energy Projection

Boros can fire powerful energy beams from his body, particularly from his eye, capable of destroying large structures and even a planet's surface.

Limit: Using this ability drains his energy, and his ultimate attack requires him to convert all his life force into energy.

Meteoric Burst

Boros can release his limiter and convert his life force into energy, massively increasing his speed and power, but at the cost of shortening his lifespan.

Limit: This form is temporary and accelerates his death; he cannot sustain it for long.

Collapsing Star Roaring Cannon

Boros's ultimate attack, a massive energy blast that he claims can destroy a star. It is powerful enough to wipe out a planet's surface.

Limit: Requires all of Boros's remaining energy, leaving him exhausted and vulnerable. It is completely nullified by Saitama's Serious Punch.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Boros is a tall, muscular humanoid alien with blue skin, long flowing purple hair, and a single large eye in the center of his forehead. He wears a black and gold armored suit with a high collar, and his armor is partially destroyed during his fight with Saitama. His body is covered in glowing blue energy patterns when he releases his power.

Symbols — His armor bears a distinctive crest, and his eye is a symbol of his race's power. He is often depicted with a menacing grin and a confident posture.

Domains — Boros rules a spaceship that serves as his mobile base, and he comes from an unnamed planet. He is a cosmic conqueror, traveling the universe to find challenges.

Accounts I
The Dark Matter Thieves ArcOne-Punch Man manga and anime

2012-2015

Boros, the leader of the Dark Matter Thieves, arrives on Earth with his massive spaceship after hearing rumors of a powerful being on the planet. He and his three lieutenants (Groribas, Melzargard, and Geryuganshoop) attack City A, causing massive destruction. The Hero Association's S-Class heroes struggle against the aliens, but Saitama defeats Boros's lieutenants and then confronts Boros. After a fierce battle, Boros reveals his true power, but Saitama ultimately defeats him with a Serious Punch, destroying his body and the spaceship. Boros is defeated and dies, but his attack on Earth demonstrates the existence of cosmic-level threats. Saitama's power is revealed to the Hero Association, though they still do not fully understand it.

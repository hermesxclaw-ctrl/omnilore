Who It Is

Maki Zen'in is a jujutsu sorcerer who refused to be defined by her clan's rejection, carving her own path with sheer physical prowess and an unyielding will. From a mocked outcast to a force capable of standing against the King of Curses, her story is one of defiance, sacrifice, and relentless growth.

Maki is blunt, confident, and fiercely independent. She often mocks others' weakness but is deeply protective of her friends. She has a strong sense of justice and a burning desire to prove herself, especially against her clan. She is pragmatic and willing to endure pain to achieve her goals.

Powers
Heavenly Restriction

Maki was born with a Heavenly Restriction that grants her immense physical strength and the ability to perceive curses, but at the cost of having no cursed energy. This makes her an anomaly among sorcerers.

Limit: Cannot use cursed energy-based techniques or domain expansion. Initially relies on cursed tools to combat curses.

Expert Weapon Usage

Maki is highly skilled with various weapons, particularly the 'Playful Cloud' and the 'Split Soul Katana'. Her fighting style combines aikido and Chinese martial arts.

Limit: Relies on the quality of the weapon; a weak weapon can break under her strength.

Enhanced Physical Abilities

Due to her Heavenly Restriction, Maki possesses superhuman strength, speed, and reflexes, allowing her to overpower most opponents physically.

Limit: She is still vulnerable to attacks and can be injured, as seen in battles with Jogo and Naoya.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Maki is a young woman with long black hair, often tied back, and sharp eyes. She wears the Jujutsu High uniform, typically with a high-collared jacket. She wears glasses to see curses, which are later replaced by special glasses after a battle. Her body is athletic and scarred after the Shibuya incident.

Symbols — Her signature weapons are a cursed tool, the 'Playful Cloud' (a three-section staff), and later the 'Split Soul Katana'. The Zen'in clan symbol appears on her clothing in flashbacks. Her glasses are iconic.

Domains — She is primarily based at Tokyo Jujutsu High, but travels to various locations for missions, including the Shibuya district and the Culling Game colonies.

Accounts I
Jujutsu Kaisen 0: The PrequelJujutsu Kaisen 0 manga and film

2018 (manga), 2021 (film)

Maki is a second-year student at Jujutsu High when Yuta Okkotsu joins. She initially mocks his weakness but later becomes his mentor in combat. She reveals her past as a rejected member of the Zen'in clan and her desire to become clan head to spite them. She fights alongside Yuta against Suguru Geto. Maki helps Yuta gain confidence and control over Rika. She is injured but survives. Her backstory is established.

Kyoto Sister School Exchange EventJujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 14-15

2018

Maki fights her sister Mai during the exchange event. She defeats Mai but is emotionally conflicted. This encounter highlights their strained relationship. Maki's relationship with her sister is further strained, but Mai's later sacrifice becomes a turning point.

Shibuya IncidentJujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 65-66

2018

Maki fights Jogo and suffers severe burns, leaving her body scarred. She continues to fight despite her injuries. Maki is scarred but survives. This event shows her resilience.

Accounts II
Culling Game ArcJujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 138, 147-152, 164-173

2021-2022

After Mai's death, Maki awakens her full potential. She kills her father and slaughters the Zen'in clan in revenge. She then joins the Culling Game, where she fights the cursed spirit of Naoya Zen'in. She is forced to retreat but later returns to exorcise it. Maki becomes one of the strongest sorcerers, comparable to Toji Fushiguro. She gains the Split Soul Katana.

Shinjuku ShowdownJujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 198-208, 212-215, 246-252

2023-2024

Maki participates in the battle against Sukuna. She pierces him with the Split Soul Katana but is taken out by a black flash. Maki is defeated but her efforts contribute to Yuji freeing Megumi.

Who It Is

An elderly human male who is cursed to bring destruction to any settlement he stays in for more than 24 hours. He did not ask for this curse and cannot control it; it was placed on him by three powerful entities he defeated in a game of cards. He has wandered the Earth for centuries, never able to stay anywhere for long. If he remains in one place, catastrophic events — plagues, fires, structural collapses — destroy the settlement, killing all inhabitants except SCP-1440 himself.

Form

An elderly man, appearing to be in his 80s or 90s, of indeterminate ethnicity. He dresses in simple, worn clothing and carries a deck of playing cards that never runs out. His eyes are described as 'ancient' and 'tired.' He rarely speaks but is unfailingly polite.

Powers

Settlement-destroying curse (24-hour trigger)

Immortality

Involuntary destructive aura

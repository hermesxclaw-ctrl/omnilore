Who It Is

A Caroline-era revenge tragedy play, written in 1645, that causes its performers and audience to descend into ritualized madness. Any production of the play results in the cast murdering each other on stage in precise reenactments of the final act, while the audience is driven to homicidal frenzy. The play appears to be a window into an alien civilization ruled by the 'Hanged King,' an entity worshipped through violence and chaos.

Form

A standard printed play script. The text appears normal on first reading, but prolonged study reveals impossible stage directions and scenes that cannot be performed by human actors — implying the performers become something else during productions.

Powers

Memetic compulsion to perform

Reality restructuring during performance

Window into alien civilization

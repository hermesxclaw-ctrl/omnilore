Who It Is
Form

SCP-1048 is a small teddy bear, approximately 33 cm in height. It is made of a variety of materials, including fabric and stuffing.

Powers

Anomalous Construction SCP-1048 has the ability to build structures and objects using various materials, often in a seemingly random and unpredictable manner.

Incident Log

SCP-1048 was created by an unknown entity, likely as a test subject for the SCP Foundation.

SCP-1048 has undergone extensive testing by the SCP Foundation, including experiments to determine its anomalous construction behavior.

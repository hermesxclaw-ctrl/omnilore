Who It Is

He steps onto the bridge in crimson, blonde hair and mask catching the light exactly as a dead legend once did — and an entire movement kneels. In Mobile Suit Gundam Unicorn, Full Frontal is not Char Aznable, but everyone desperately needs him to be: the Neo Zeon remnant called the Sleeves, the Earth Federation that fears another war, and the young Newtypes who feel a gravity in his voice they cannot explain. Empty or possessed, savior or weapon, he pilots a blood-red Sinanju as if history itself could be steered back on course.

Attested: Soft-spoken, unfailingly polite, charismatic in a cold, almost priestly manner. Speaks in measured philosophical monologues about humanity's need to be guided, the weight of gravity, and the inevitability of conflict between Earth and Spacenoids. Shows no anger, panic, or humor; even in combat his voice remains level. Deeply fatalistic — believes he is not a person but a vessel created to fulfill a role, stating 'I am but a shadow cast by Char's ideals.' Treats Mineva Lao Zabi with paternal deference, Angelo Sauper with distant tolerance, and Banagher Links wit

Powers
Charisma and Political Leadership

Ability to unify disparate Zeon remnants (Sleeves), former Axis, and mercenaries into a coherent quasi-state without formal rank. Attested via in-universe Federation intelligence reports calling him 'the second coming' and Sleeves' willingness to die on his order.

Limit: Dependent entirely on Char's mythos; without mask and Sinanju his authority would collapse. Mineva's refusal to endorse him limits legitimacy.

Newtype Spatial Awareness and Pressure

Demonstrates classic Newtype abilities: sensing other Newtypes (Banagher, Marida, Mineva) across vast distances, projecting psycho-pressure that can paralyze weaker pilots, and resonating with psycho-frame. In final battle, his psycho-frame resonance with Unicorn creates shared vision.

Limit: Explicitly weaker than true powerful Newtypes like Amuro Ray or Kamille Bidan; he admits he cannot hear the 'voices' as clearly and relies on machine amplification. Psycho-frame overload can kill him.

Master Mobile Suit Piloting - Sinanju

Pilots MSN-06S Sinanju, a 22.6m high-performance custom mobile suit with exceptional thrust (4x wing binders) and beam weaponry. Executes maneuvers that match or exceed Unicorn Gundam in Destroy Mode, including high-speed melee with beam naginata and bazooka. Defeated elite Federation pilots at Industrial 7 and Palau.

Limit: Sinanju lacks psycho-frame initially (later Sinanju Stein lineage reveals frame was removed), so cannot generate psycho-field like Unicorn/Banshee. Machine is maintenance-heavy and conspicuous.

Tactical and Strategic Command

Plans large-scale operations: theft of Laplace's Box pursuit, blockade of Industrial 7, assault on Torrington Base, infiltration of Federation capital Dakar, and final battle at Laplace. Uses decoys, asteroid maneuvers, and political blackmail (threatening to drop colony).

Limit: Over-relies on Laplace's Box as leverage; once Box's true nature (original UC Constitution) is revealed, his political strategy collapses. Underestimates Banagher's idealism.

Vessel / Memory Implant Resilience

Psychological ability to function without personal identity — to act as pure role. Described as 'having no self to break.' Allows him to endure knowledge of his own artificiality without collapse until final moments.

Limit: This is also his fatal weakness: without self, he cannot adapt beyond Char's shadow, cannot imagine a future not defined by Zeon vs Federation. Banagher defeats him philosophically by asserting possibility.

Accounts I
The Return of the Red Comet - Reappearance at Industrial 7Fukui Harutoshi, Gundam Unicorn Vol.2 / OVA Episode 1 'Day of the Unicorn'

In-universe UC 0096; publication 2007 / broadcast 2010

Three years after Char's Rebellion (UC 0093) ended with Char's presumed death, Earth Federation intelligence detects a man in a red mask leading the Neo Zeon remnant called the Sleeves. At Industrial 7, Sleeves forces under Full Frontal attempt to intercept the Vist Foundation's transport of Laplace's Box — the secret charter that could delegitimize the Federation. Full Frontal in the Sinanju duels the newly awakened RX-0 Unicorn Gundam piloted by civilian student Banagher Links. The duel ends in stalemate as both machines' psycho-frames resonate, and Full Frontal withdraws, intrigued by Banagher's Newtype potential. Establishes Full Frontal as active threat and Box hunter; triggers Banagher's entanglement; Federation reclassifies Sleeves from remnant to major faction. Sets philosophical rivalry between Banagher's possibility and Frontal's fatalism.

The Siege of Palau - Rescue and RejectionGundam Unicorn Vol.3-4 / OVA Episode 3 'The Ghost of Laplace'

UC 0096; publication 2008 / broadcast 2011

Banagher and Mineva are captured and brought to the Sleeves' asteroid fortress Palau. Full Frontal receives them not as prisoners but as guests, revealing he has protected Mineva (last Zabi heir) for years, hoping she will legitimize his Neo Zeon. He offers Banagher a place at his side, arguing that humanity will never understand each other and needs a guiding myth. Mineva rejects him, declaring she will not be a puppet princess, and Banagher escapes with her aid. Full Frontal allows their escape, stating they must see the world to understand. Reveals Frontal's political weakness — without Mineva's endorsement he remains a pretender. Deepens his paternal yet instrumental relationship to Mineva and Marida. Palau later falls to Federation attack, forcing Sleeves to become fully mobile.

Torrington and Dakar - The War DemonstrationGundam Unicorn Vol.5-6 / OVA Episode 4 'At the Bottom of the Gravity Well'

UC 0096; publication 2008-2009 / broadcast 2011

To force the Vist Foundation and Federation to reveal Laplace's Box, Full Frontal orders a two-pronged assault: a diversionary attack on Torrington Base in Australia by remnant forces, and a surgical drop of his forces on the Federation capital Dakar. In Dakar, he appears before the Federation Assembly via broadcast, demanding the Box and threatening further war. Banagher and the Unicorn intervene, and Full Frontal again duels him, this time on Earth. The battle is interrupted by the awakening of the second Unicorn unit, Banshee. Demonstrates Frontal's willingness to use terror and spectacle; Dakar attack shocks Federation elite into acknowledging Box's existence. Shows limits of his control — remnant forces at Torrington commit excesses he did not order, highlighting his loose coalition.

Accounts II
The Battle of Industrial 7 and the Opening of Laplace's BoxGundam Unicorn Vol.7-9 / OVA Episodes 5-6 'Black Unicorn' / 'Two Worlds, Two Tomorrows'

UC 0096; publication 2009 / broadcast 2012-2013

All factions converge on Industrial 7 and the original Laplace colony wreck where the Box is sealed. Full Frontal leads Sleeves fleet against Londo Bell (Federation) and the Vist Foundation's private forces. He boards Laplace with Banagher, Riddhe, and Mineva as Syam Vist finally opens the Box. The Box is revealed not to be a weapon but the original Universal Century charter, with a clause granting Spacenoids autonomy if Newtypes emerge — a clause erased after the terrorist bombing of Laplace in UC 0001. Full Frontal is visibly shaken; his entire justification for war (that the Federation's founding sin must be avenged) is undercut by a document that instead offers a legal, peaceful path. Ideological collapse of Neo Zeon casus belli; Frontal realizes he has been chasing a symbol that, if revealed, would make his war unnecessary. He chooses to continue fighting anyway, arguing humanity is not ready for the truth.

Over the Rainbow - Final Duel and DissolutionGundam Unicorn Vol.10 / OVA Episode 7 'Over the Rainbow'

UC 0096; publication 2009 / broadcast 2014

Rejecting the charter's peaceful implication, Full Frontal deploys the massive NZ-999 Neo Zeong, a psycho-shard mobile armor that interfaces with Sinanju, to destroy Industrial 7 and bury the truth. He engages Banagher's Unicorn (now with full psycho-frame) in a final duel inside and outside the colony. Through psycho-frame resonance, Banagher experiences Frontal's inner void — a man with no memories before being made into Char's copy, who accepted emptiness as duty. Banagher counters with the possibility that people can change without being guided by a Char. The Neo Zeong is destroyed; Frontal's Sinanju is crippled. In his last moments, his consciousness merges with the psycho-frame and he encounters the spirits of Char Aznable and Lalah Sune, who tell him his time is over and to rest. He dies as his Sinanju drifts, finally at peace, telling Banagher '...perhaps... you are right.' Death of Full Frontal ends Sleeves as political force; Laplace's Box is disclosed to Earth Sphere, leading to gradual reforms (depicted in epilogue). Origins of the Vessel - The Man Before the Mask (Implied Backstory)Gundam Unicorn Vol.10 exposition + Fukui interviews in Gundam Ace (2009) + Official Timeline

Backstory set circa UC 0093-0095; revealed 2009-2010

No canonical birth name is ever given. Fragmentary exposition states that after Char's disappearance in UC 0093, a research group (implied to be linked to Flanagan Institute remnants and the Sleeves) recovered a wounded man — possibly a former Zeon officer with high Newtype aptitude but no identity — and subjected him to memory implantation and cyber-Newtype conditioning using Char's recorded psycho-waves and operational records. The process erased his original personality, leaving a vessel that believes itself to be Char's successor. Full Frontal himself says 'I was told I am Full Frontal. Before that, there was nothing.' Federation files list him as 'Unknown, presumed cyber-Newtype, affiliation: Neo Zeon.' Explains why he is both impossibly Char-like and fundamentally hollow; justifies his fatalism. Leaves open whether any original self remains, fueling fan debate about identity and consent in Newtype experimentation.

Full Frontal is Char Aznable back from the dead / a Char clone. — Canon explicitly states he is not Char and not a genetic clone. He is a separate man whose identity was erased and overwritten with Char's persona via cyber-Newtype technology, serving as a symbolic successor. Fukui and Sunrise repeatedly clarified this; his original name and origin are intentionally never revealed.

Full Frontal is the main villain of Gundam Unicorn who wants to destroy the Federation with a weapon called Laplace's Box. — Laplace's Box is not a weapon but the original UC charter revealing the Federation erased Spacenoid autonomy rights. Frontal wants to use it as political leverage, not as a superweapon; after learning its true nature, he tries to destroy it because its peaceful revelation would undermine his war justification.

'Full Frontal' refers only to nudity and the Gundam character is named as a joke about nudity. — Within Gundam, the name is diegetic English chosen for its meaning 'fully facing forward / fully exposed' — he presents himself as Char without hiding. It coincides with the English idiom for nudity but was not chosen as sexual joke; Japanese creators selected English phrase for symbolic resonance, as is common in Gundam (e.g., Full Armor, Full Vernian).

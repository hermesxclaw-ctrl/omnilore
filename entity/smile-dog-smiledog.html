Who It Is

A memetic internet entity that spreads via an image — a husky with an unnaturally wide, human-like smile sitting at a computer. Viewing the image causes a compulsion to share it, followed by nightmares, paranoia, and the Smile Dog appearing in reflections. The image is supposedly a 'cross-breed of man and machine' that travels through the internet. Victims who refuse to share are driven to suicide. The entity communicates through computer screens and electronic devices.

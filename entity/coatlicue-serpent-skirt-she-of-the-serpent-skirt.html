Who It Is
Form

Coatlicue is often depicted as a woman wearing a skirt made of snakes, with her face formed by two facing serpents. Her body is adorned with symbols of fertility and life, such as eggs and flowers. She is often shown standing on top of a serpent, which represents her connection to the earth and the cycles of life and death.

Powers

Fertility Coatlicue is associated with the power of fertility, and is said to be able to give life and take it away.

Accounts

Coatlicue became miraculously pregnant when a ball of feathers fell on her while she was standing in the temple. She gave birth to Huitzilopochtli, who was said to be the god of war and the sun.

Coatlicue prophesied the fall of the Aztec empire, and was said to have predicted the arrival of the Spanish conquistadors.

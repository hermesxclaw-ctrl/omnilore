Who It Is

The family goat on a Puritan farmstead in 1630s New England, later revealed to be Satan incarnate. Black Phillip speaks to the young twins and ultimately to Thomasin, offering her the 'taste of butter' and a 'delicious life' in exchange for her soul. His voice is deep, cultured, and seductive.

Form

Large black goat with curved horns and yellow eyes; transforms into a tall, dark-clad man in Thomasin's final scene

Powers

Speech (human voice)

Temptation and soul bargaining

Shape-shifting

Corruption of the devout

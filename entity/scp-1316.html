SCP-1316

Lucy the Cat, Schrodinger's Cat, The Quantum Feline

The cat is alive. The cat is dead. Both statements are true, and neither is. She doesn't meow. She doesn't purr. She just... exists, between the ticks of the clock, waiting for the observation that never fully comes.

Who It Is

SCP-1316 is a Series II SCP centered on a seemingly ordinary female calico cat named Lucy who is, in fact, simultaneously alive and dead -- a literal Schrodinger's Cat existing in a permanent quantum superposition. Unlike the famous thought experiment, SCP-1316's state can be 'observed' in contradictory ways simultaneously: different observers perceive Lucy as alive and healthy, dead and decaying, or somewhere in between, all at the same time. More disturbingly, the anomaly extends to the observer's memory: people who interact with Lucy quickly lose the ability to definitively remember whether they interacted with a living cat or a corpse. The article plays with the concept of quantum observation, the fragility of memory, and the horror of uncertainty. What makes SCP-1316 stand out is its quiet, melancholy tone -- less about cosmic horror and more about the existential sadness of a creature forever trapped between states, never truly alive, never truly at rest. The article culminates with the suggestion that Lucy was once a normal cat that was somehow 'broken' by exposure to an anomalous event, and now exists in this liminal state indefinitely. SCP-1316 is a respected but lesser-known Series II entry that exemplifies the Foundation's ability to craft emotionally complex narratives from simple scientific concepts.

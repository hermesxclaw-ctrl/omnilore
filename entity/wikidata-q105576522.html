Who It Is

Two figures share the name Raidou: one a brutal fighter who shakes the foundations of a martial arts tournament, the other a demon-hunting detective in an alternate 1930s Tokyo. Both are defined by their battles against forces beyond the ordinary, but their legacies diverge dramatically.

Raidou (Dead or Alive) is arrogant, ruthless, and power-hungry, seeking to prove his superiority. Raidou Kuzunoha XIV is stoic, disciplined, and dedicated to his duty as a demon summoner, often showing a calm and collected demeanor.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Raidou' is of Japanese origin, possibly meaning 'thunder' (rai) and 'way' or 'path' (dou). In the Devil Summoner series, 'Kuzunoha' is a surname associated with a fictional lineage of demon summoners.

Pronunciation — RYE-doh (English approximation)

Aliases — Raidou (Dead or Alive), Raidou Kuzunoha XIV

Ownership — Owned by Koei Tecmo (Dead or Alive) and Atlus (Devil Summoner). Both are Japanese video game franchises.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Raidou (Dead or Alive) is a large, muscular man with short dark hair, often wearing a martial arts gi or a dark outfit. Raidou Kuzunoha XIV is a young man with black hair, typically dressed in a blue high-collared coat, a red scarf, and a cap, reflecting a 1930s detective style.

Symbols — Raidou (Dead or Alive) is often associated with lightning and thunder motifs. Raidou Kuzunoha XIV is associated with the katana, a revolver, and demon summoning circles.

Domains — Raidou (Dead or Alive) is a fighter who travels to participate in tournaments. Raidou Kuzunoha XIV operates in an alternate 1930s Tokyo, a world filled with demons and supernatural occurrences.

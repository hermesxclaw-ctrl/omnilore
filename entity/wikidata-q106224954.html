Who It Is

In the whimsical world of Professor Layton, where every street corner hides a puzzle and every citizen has a story, one figure stands out as the gatekeeper of wit and wisdom: Granny Riddleton. She is not a hero who battles villains or a sage who reveals ancient secrets, but a humble old woman whose love for riddles has made her an unforgettable part of the series' charm. Her presence is a testament to the idea that even the smallest characters can leave a lasting impression, and her challenges have stumped and delighted players for years.

Granny Riddleton is cheerful, patient, and encouraging. She loves to engage others in riddles and puzzles, not to stump them, but to share the joy of intellectual discovery. She speaks in a gentle, grandmotherly manner, often offering words of encouragement and praise. She is always found in a specific location, waiting to challenge the player with a set of puzzles.

Powers
Riddle Mastery

She has an extensive knowledge of riddles and puzzles, able to present a wide variety of challenges to the player. Her puzzles range from logic puzzles to lateral thinking problems.

Limit: Her puzzles are always solvable with careful thought; she does not present unsolvable or unfair challenges.

Puzzle Gifting

She often rewards the player with items or hint coins upon solving her puzzles, aiding them in their journey.

Limit: Rewards are limited to in-game items.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Granny Riddleton is an elderly woman with a kind, wrinkled face and a warm smile. She typically wears a simple, modest dress in muted colors, often with an apron, and a bonnet or shawl over her head. She carries a walking stick and has a small, hunched posture. Her design is reminiscent of a classic fairy-tale grandmother, evoking a sense of comfort and wisdom.

Symbols — Her primary symbol is the riddle itself, often represented by question marks or puzzle pieces. She is associated with the idea of mental challenges and the joy of solving them.

Domains — She appears in various towns and cities throughout the series, always in a designated spot where she can interact with passersby. She seems to have a nomadic lifestyle, traveling to different locations to share her riddles.

Accounts I
The Riddler of St. MystereProfessor Layton and the Curious Village

2007-2008

In the village of St. Mystere, Granny Riddleton is one of the many residents who provide puzzles to Professor Layton and Luke. She is encountered in a specific location, where she challenges them with a series of riddles that must be solved to progress. Her puzzles are integral to the village's mysterious atmosphere. Solving her puzzles grants the player access to further areas and reveals clues about the village's secrets.

The Puzzle Master of the Molentary ExpressProfessor Layton and the Diabolical Box

2007-2009

Granny Riddleton appears on the Molentary Express, a luxury train, offering puzzles to passengers. She is a recurring character who provides optional challenges that test the player's wit. Solving her puzzles yields hint coins and other rewards.

The Riddle Mistress of Future LondonProfessor Layton and the Unwound Future

2008-2010

In the futuristic version of London, Granny Riddleton has set up her puzzle stand, offering riddles to the citizens. She is a familiar face in an unfamiliar setting, providing a sense of continuity. Solving her puzzles rewards the player with items.

The Riddler of MisthalleryProfessor Layton and the Last Specter

2009-2011

In the prequel era, Granny Riddleton appears in Misthallery, a town plagued by a mysterious specter. She offers puzzles to the young Layton and Luke, providing a lighthearted diversion from the dark mystery. Solving her puzzles yields hint coins.

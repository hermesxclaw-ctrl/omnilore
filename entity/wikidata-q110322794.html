Who It Is

Before she could legally drink in America, a 19-year-old from Tottenham with a laugh too loud for polite rooms and a voice that sounded like it had already lived three heartbreaks posted a three-song demo to Myspace and changed how the 21st century would sound when it cried.

Attested via interviews (BBC, Rolling Stone, Vogue, The Guardian) as self-deprecating, candid, and humor-driven, with frequent references to anxiety, stage fright, and homesickness. Known for extended hiatuses between albums, reluctance toward constant touring, and outspoken commentary on fame and body image. Describes herself as obsessed with voices from age 4, influenced by Etta James, Ella Fitzgerald, and later Spice Girls and Lauryn Hill. Powers
Mezzo-soprano vocal instrument

Warm, chest-dominant mezzo-soprano with documented range approx. B2 to C6 in studio, most powerful in G3-E5 belt. Praised for emotional phrasing, melisma control, and dynamic contrast from intimate pianissimo to full-voice fortissimo. Signature break and grit on sustained notes (e.g., 'Rolling in the Deep' chorus, 'Hello' bridge).

Limit: Documented vocal cord hemorrhage October 2011 requiring microsurgery by Dr. Steven Zeitels in Boston; subsequent vocal rest and technique retraining. Has cancelled tour dates due to vocal strain (2011, 2017 Wembley). Self-reported stage fright and anxiety affect live performance stamina.

Songwriting - autobiographical balladry

Co-writes all original material, typically from personal experience (heartbreak, motherhood, divorce, self-reckoning). Collaborators include Dan Wilson ('Someone like You'), Paul Epworth ('Rolling in the Deep', 'Skyfall'), Greg Kurstin ('Hello'), Inflo ('Easy on Me' contributors). Lyrics favour direct first-person address, conversational verses, and cathartic choruses.

Limit: Works within co-writing model; rarely sole writer. Prefers slow, iterative writing and long gaps between releases. Has declined to write on demand for film except Skyfall.

Piano and guitar accompaniment

Plays piano and guitar to accompany songwriting and live performance, notably piano on 'Someone like You' and 'When We Were Young' live. Not a virtuosic instrumentalist; functions as songwriter's instrument.

Limit: Live band provides primary instrumentation on tour; studio piano often performed by co-writers or session players.

Live vocal performance and audience rapport

Ability to sustain arena and residency shows with minimal choreography, relying on vocal delivery and between-song banter. Weekends with Adele (2022-2024) demonstrated 2-hour set with live band and storytelling interludes.

Limit: Has stated dislike of touring and flying; cancelled final two Wembley Stadium shows June 2017 due to vocal cord damage. Prefers residency format over extended world tours.

Cultural and commercial influence

Proven capacity to drive physical and streaming sales at scale: 21 (31+ million copies), 25 (23+ million, fastest-selling album in UK/US first-week history), restoring album-era purchasing in streaming era. Influence on subsequent British female soul-pop artists.

Limit: Influence is industry and cultural, not supernatural; dependent on label support (XL/Columbia) and media distribution. Sales figures vary by certifying body and reporting date.

Later eras (2015 onward) shift to couture gowns (Burberry, Valentino, Louis Vuitton) for award and residency performances, with more minimalist stage dress. Accounts I
Tottenham to BRIT School - FormationBiographical interviews and BRIT School records; PlatformsMagazine.com demo (2006)

1988-2006

Born 5 May 1988 in Tottenham to Penny Adkins (furniture maker, adult-learning organiser) and Welsh father Marc Evans, who left when she was two. Raised by mother, moved to Brighton (1997) then back to South London (Brixton, West Norwood) in 1999. Began singing at 4, obsessed with voices. Wrote 'Hometown Glory' at 16 about West Norwood and Brockwell Park. Attended BRIT School Croydon, graduating May 2006, initially aiming for A&R rather than performance. Recorded three-song class demo, posted by friend on Myspace, attracting XL Recordings director Richard Russell and manager Jonathan Dickins (September Management). Signed to XL September 2006 at age 18. Secured recording contract and management that shaped debut album; established London soul-pop lineage alongside Amy Winehouse and Duffy.

19 - The Breakthrough and the American HesitationAlbum 19 (2008); BBC Sound of 2008 poll; Saturday Night Live episode 18 Oct 2008

2006-2009

Debut single 'Hometown Glory' (Oct 2007, Pacemaker, 500 vinyl) charted after Skins sync. 'Chasing Pavements' reached UK No.2 Jan 2008. Album 19 (Jan 2008, UK No.1) won Brit Awards Critics' Choice and earned Mercury Prize nomination and Grammy for Best New Artist (2009). Despite UK success, Adele cancelled US tour dates in 2008 to remain with boyfriend, later calling it an 'early life crisis' marked by heavy drinking and homesickness and dislike of flying. Television debut on Later... with Jools Holland (June 2007) performing 'Daydreamer'. SNL performance 18 Oct 2008 (with Sarah Palin appearance) drew 17 million viewers, spiking 19 to No.11 Billboard 200 and establishing US market. Moved to Notting Hill Nov 2008 and quit drinking. Established signature confessional soul style; SNL is widely cited as US breakthrough moment; charted path for 21.

21 - Heartbreak, Vocal Crisis, and Global DominanceAlbum 21 (2011); Billboard chart history; Grammy Awards 2012

2010-2012

Second album 21, written after breakup, recorded with Paul Epworth, Rick Rubin, Ryan Tedder and Dan Wilson. Released Jan 2011, it became best-selling album of 21st century, topping Billboard 200 for 24 weeks (record for female artist), UK No.1 for 23 weeks. Singles 'Rolling in the Deep', 'Someone like You' (first piano ballad to top Billboard Hot 100 in decade), and 'Set Fire to the Rain' became signature songs. In October 2011 suffered vocal cord hemorrhage, cancelling tour and undergoing laser microsurgery with Dr. Steven Zeitels in Boston, followed by complete vocal rest. Returned to win six Grammys Feb 2012 including Album of the Year (tying Beyoncé record at time), and performed 'Rolling in the Deep' at ceremony to standing ovation. 21 sold over 31 million copies; certified Diamond in US and 17x Platinum in UK; cemented Adele as best-selling female artist of 2010s; vocal surgery led to technique change and advocacy for vocal health.

Accounts II
Skyfall - Bond Theme and Awards SweepSingle 'Skyfall' (2012); Skyfall film (Eon Productions, dir. Sam Mendes); Academy Awards 2013

2012-2013

Co-wrote with Paul Epworth the theme for 23rd James Bond film Skyfall (starring Daniel Craig). Released 5 Oct 2012 (Global James Bond Day), song debuted at UK No.2 and US No.8, becoming first Bond theme to win Academy Award for Best Original Song (Oscars 2013), plus Golden Globe, Brit Award for British Single and Grammy for Best Song Written for Visual Media. Performance at 85th Academy Awards marked first live performance after vocal surgery. Song's orchestral arrangement recorded at Abbey Road with 77-piece orchestra. Elevated Adele to EGOT-adjacent status (Oscar + Emmy + Grammy); revived prestige of Bond themes; became definitive modern Bond song in polling.

25 and Hello - Return from Hiatus and Sales RecordsAlbum 25 (2015); IFPI Global Recording Artist reports

2013-2016

After three-year hiatus and birth of son Angelo (Oct 2012 with partner Simon Konecki), returned with 'Hello' (23 Oct 2015), co-written with Greg Kurstin, whose video (dir. Xavier Dolan) broke Vevo 24-hour record (27.7m views). Album 25 (20 Nov 2015) broke first-week sales records in UK (800,307) and US (3.38 million, first album to sell >3m in a week). Won five Grammys in 2017 including Album of the Year (acceptance speech famously dedicating to Beyoncé's Lemonade). World tour Adele Live 2016-2017 played arenas including Glastonbury Pyramid headline 2016; cancelled final two Wembley Stadium shows June 2017 due to vocal cord damage on medical advice. 25 became best-selling album worldwide in 2015 and of the 2010s in UK; confirmed pattern of age-titled albums and multi-year gaps; vocal injury reinforced shift away from extensive touring.

30, Divorce, and Weekends with AdeleAlbum 30 (2021); CBS special Adele One Night Only (2021); Caesars Palace residency 2022-2024

2019-2024

Following marriage to Simon Konecki (2018) and divorce filed 2019 (finalised 2021), and highly publicised weight loss and lifestyle change, released 30 (19 Nov 2021), inspired by divorce, motherhood and self-examination. Lead single 'Easy on Me' debuted at No.1 in 30+ countries, won Grammy for Best Pop Solo Performance (2023). Album topped year-end charts globally in 2021. Promoted with CBS special One Night Only (Oprah Winfrey interview) and ITV An Audience with Adele. Launched Weekends with Adele residency at The Colosseum, Caesars Palace, Las Vegas (Nov 2022 - Nov 2024, 100 shows after postponement from Jan 2022 due to production and COVID delays). Residency grossed over $100m and earned Primetime Emmy for Outstanding Variety Special (Pre-Recorded) for the CBS special. As of 2024, all studio albums except 19 have topped yearly global best-seller charts in 21st century. 30 certified 3x Platinum UK, 3x Platinum US; residency model replaced world tour; public narrative centred on personal growth and vocal maturity.

Adele's albums are named after her age when they were released — Albums are named for age when she began writing them, not release age. She has stated 19 was written at 17-19, 21 at 19-21, 25 begun at 25, 30 begun at 30. Release often a year or more later.

Adele only sings ballads and cannot do uptempo songs — While ballads are signature, she has multiple uptempo hits: 'Rolling in the Deep', 'Rumour Has It', 'Send My Love (To Your New Lover)', 'Oh My God'. 21 and 30 both contain rhythmic, gospel and dance-inflected tracks.

Adele had her vocal cords completely replaced or had a transplant — She had laser microsurgery to remove a benign polyp and stop hemorrhage (phonotraumatic hemorrhage), performed by Dr. Steven Zeitels, not a transplant or cord replacement. Required complete vocal rest but preserved natural voice.

Adele wrote 'Make You Feel My Love' as an original — Song was written by Bob Dylan (1997, Time Out of Mind) and previously recorded by Dylan, Billy Joel and Garth Brooks. Adele's version on 19 is a cover that became one of her early signature recordings.

Adele retired after 30 or after Las Vegas residency — She announced extended hiatus from touring and recording after residency (Nov 2024), stating need for break, but has not retired and remains under contract. No official retirement statement exists.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Surname Daimonji (大文字) literally 'large character/letter' in Japanese, most famously associated with the Daimonji (Gozan no Okuribi) bonfire festival on Kyoto's Mount Daimonji, where a giant kanji 大 is lit. Given name Ryugon is not standard Japanese reading; possible kanji include 竜言, 龍言, 隆言, 竜権 etc

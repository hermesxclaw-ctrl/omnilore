Who It Is

A powerful sphere of chakra created by combining positive and negative chakra at a 8:2 ratio, compressed into a hyper-dense ball. It is the signature attack of the Tailed Beasts and their jinchuriki. The resulting explosion can level mountains and cause massive destruction. Also known as Bijūdama.

Powers

Massive destructive sphere, mountain-leveling explosion

The Approach

The name 'Migo' skips across languages and worlds: a botanist's surname, a Taiwanese tech firm, a Talmudic legal maxim, an island off Australia, and — most famously — the shambling, fungoid horrors of H.P. Lovecraft's cosmos. But one of its most beloved bearers is a yeti with a heart of gold, the star of an animated musical about truth and community.

Content advisory. None of the entries contain graphic or disturbing material. The Lovecraftian Mi-Go are cosmic horrors but are described in general terms; no explicit violence is depicted.
Identity

Migo

Pronunciation Generally pronounced 'MY-go' for the Smallfoot character and 'mee-GO' for the Mi-Go, though the latter is often pronounced 'MY-go' in English. The Talmudic term is typically pronounced 'MEE-go'.

Known As

Mi-GoMiguMiggoMigos

Etymology

The origin of the name varies by context. For the Lovecraftian race, 'Mi-Go' is a coined term with no clear etymology, possibly inspired by the sound of the creatures' buzzing. For the Smallfoot character, 'Migo' is a simple, friendly name likely invented for the film. The Talmudic principle 'Migo' (also 'Migu' or 'Miggo') derives from Aramaic, meaning 'since' or 'because'.

Cultural Ownership

The name is not owned by any single culture. The Mi-Go are part of Lovecraft's Cthulhu Mythos, which is public domain in many jurisdictions. The Smallfoot character is owned by Warner Animation Group. Other uses are independent.

Disputed Identifications

There is no significant dispute over the identity of these entities, but the similarity of names can cause confusion. The Mi-Go are sometimes confused with other Lovecraftian creatures, and the Smallfoot character is sometimes mistakenly linked to the Mi-Go.

Description

Appearance

The Mi-Go are described as large, pinkish, fungoid crustacean-like beings with wings, no heads, and a mass of short, fleshy tentacles where the head should be. The Smallfoot character Migo is a young yeti, resembling a large, furry ape-like creature with white fur, blue eyes, and a friendly expression.

Symbols & Iconography

The Mi-Go are often depicted in Lovecraftian art as grotesque, otherworldly beings. The Smallfoot Migo is depicted as a cute, cuddly yeti, symbolizing innocence and curiosity.

Behaviour & Personality

The Mi-Go are highly intelligent, scientifically advanced, and generally indifferent to humanity, though they can be hostile if threatened. The Smallfoot Migo is adventurous, optimistic, and determined to prove his beliefs, even when they contradict his community's traditions.

Domains & Habitats

The Mi-Go originate from the planet Yuggoth (Pluto) and can travel through space. The Smallfoot Migo lives in a hidden Himalayan village, isolated from the human world.

Version Differences

The Mi-Go appear in various Lovecraftian works, with consistent characteristics. The Smallfoot Migo is a standalone character with no known variants.

The Whisperer in Darkness

Source H.P. LovecraftCulture AmericanPeriod 1931

Summary

A Vermont farmer discovers evidence of the Mi-Go, who are harvesting minerals from Earth. He is later lured to their base and his brain is removed and placed in a cylinder, but the story ends with the protagonist realizing the farmer is now a robotic shell.

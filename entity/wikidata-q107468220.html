Who It Is

In the scorched ruins of Las Vegas, a man who has outlived the apocalypse by a century and a half sits in a sealed chamber, his withered body suspended in a life-support machine, his mind ruling a city of neon and robots. He is Robert Edwin House, the last true capitalist of the old world, and he believes he alone can save humanity—if only he can bend the Mojave to his will.

Mr. House is highly intelligent, calculating, and pragmatic. He speaks in a measured, precise manner, often displaying a dry wit. He is deeply confident in his own abilities and dismissive of those he considers inferior. He values order and long-term planning over short-term sentiment.

Powers
Technological mastery

Mr. House is a brilliant engineer and programmer, having designed the Securitron robots and the Platinum Chip, and he has a deep understanding of pre-war technology.

Limit: His physical frailty confines him to his chamber, and he is dependent on his life-support systems.

Control of Securitron army

He commands an army of Securitron robots, which serve as his enforcers and representatives across New Vegas.

Limit: The army's effectiveness depends on the activation of the Platinum Chip, which is initially stolen by Benny.

Longevity

He has survived for over 200 years through a combination of cryogenic stasis and life-support technology.

Limit: His body is extremely fragile and requires constant medical support.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In his chamber, Mr. House appears as a frail, elderly man with pale, thin skin, connected to an array of life-support tubes and monitors. He wears a simple robe, and his physical form is largely immobile. He interacts with the world through a network of screens and robotic proxies.

Symbols — The Lucky 38 casino is his symbol of power, a towering monument in the New Vegas skyline. His Securitron robots bear his likeness on their screens, displaying his face and conveying his words.

Domains — His domain is the city of New Vegas, particularly the Lucky 38 casino, where he resides in a secure suite. He has influence over the Mojave Wasteland through his Securitron network and his control of the Hoover Dam's power output.

Accounts I
The Rise of Mr. HouseFallout: New Vegas, in-game terminals and dialogue

Pre-Great War to 2281

Robert House was a wealthy businessman and visionary who foresaw the nuclear apocalypse. He developed the Platinum Chip and the Securitron army to secure his future. After the bombs fell, he placed himself in cryogenic stasis, and upon awakening, he built New Vegas from the ruins of Las Vegas, establishing himself as its ruler. He became the de facto dictator of New Vegas, maintaining its independence and prosperity through his control of the Hoover Dam.

Accounts II
The Courier's EncounterFallout: New Vegas, main questline

2281

The player character, the Courier, is hired to deliver the Platinum Chip to Mr. House. After being ambushed by Benny, the Courier eventually meets Mr. House, who tasks them with retrieving the Chip and defeating his rivals. The player can choose to ally with him or betray him. The outcome depends on the player's choices, potentially leading to Mr. House's continued rule or his downfall.

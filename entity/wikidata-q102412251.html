The Approach

Roswaal L Mathers is a name that echoes through the halls of the Roswaal mansion, a figure of immense magical power and eccentricity, whose every gesture and word is a calculated piece in a grand, centuries-old game.

Content advisory. Depictions of servitude, manipulation, and psychological control; themes of obsession and moral compromise.
Identity

Roswaal L Mathers

Pronunciation ROZ-wahl EL MATH-ers

Known As

RoswaalRoswaal MathersThe MargraveLord Roswaal

Etymology

The name 'Roswaal' is a given name of Germanic origin, possibly meaning 'rose ruler' or 'rose power'. 'L' is a middle initial, and 'Mathers' is a surname, likely of English origin, meaning 'son of Matthew'.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character created by Tappei Nagatsuki for the Japanese light novel series 'Re:Zero'.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans have speculated that Roswaal may be a reincarnation of or connected to the Sage of the past, but this is not confirmed in the main story.

Description

Appearance

Roswaal is a tall, slim man with long, flowing purple hair and heterochromatic eyes: his right eye is yellow, his left is blue. He wears an elaborate, colorful outfit with a cape, and often carries a staff. His makeup is theatrical, with heavy eyeliner and lipstick, giving him an androgynous, clown-like appearance.

Symbols & Iconography

His staff is a symbol of his magical authority. His heterochromia is a visual representation of his dual nature, and his theatrical makeup reflects his performer-like personality.

Behaviour & Personality

Roswaal is flamboyant, eccentric, and speaks in a sing-song, lisping manner. He is highly intelligent and manipulative, often playing the fool to hide his true intentions. He is obsessed with reviving his teacher, Echidna, and will go to great lengths to achieve this goal, including manipulating Subaru and others. He is also a powerful mage, one of the strongest in the world.

Domains & Habitats

Roswaal resides in the Roswaal Mansion, located in the Mathers domain of the Kingdom of Lugunica. He is the Margrave of the Mathers territory and a member of the Council of Elders.

Version Differences

In the web novel, Roswaal's backstory is more detailed, and his manipulation of Subaru is more explicit. In the anime, some scenes are shortened or altered for pacing, but the core character remains the same. The light novel is the canonical version, with the web novel as a draft.

Powers & Abilities

Magic

Source Version All versions (web novel, light novel, anime)

Description

Roswaal is a master of magic, capable of using all six elements (fire, water, wind, earth, yin, and yang). He can cast powerful spells, including offensive, defensive, and support magic. He is particularly skilled in fire and water magic.

Limitations

Magic use consumes mana, and overuse can lead to exhaustion. Roswaal's power is immense but not infinite.

Contradictions

None known.

Contract with Beatrice

Source Version All versions

Description

Roswaal has a contract with Beatrice, a spirit of the yin element. This contract allows him to use her power, but it also binds them together. The contract is a key part of his plan.

Limitations

The contract requires Roswaal to fulfill Beatrice's conditions, and it ties their fates.

Contradictions

The exact nature and terms of the contract are not fully revealed in the main story.

Immortality (implied)

Source Version Light novel, anime

Description

Roswaal has lived for over 400 years, suggesting he has achieved some form of immortality, possibly through his magic or a contract with Echidna.

Limitations

The mechanism of his longevity is not fully explained, and it may have costs.

Contradictions

Some fans speculate that his immortality is due to a time-loop or reincarnation, but this is not confirmed.

Stories & Myths

The Roswaal Mansion Arc

Source Re:Zero - Starting Life in Another World, Volume 1-3Culture Japanese light novelPeriod 2012-2013

Summary

Subaru Natsuki, a young man transported to another world, is taken in by Roswaal's mansion after helping Emilia, a silver-haired half-elf. Roswaal employs Subaru as a butler, and Subaru becomes entangled in the mansion's mysteries, including the demonic beasts in the forest and the machinations of the Witch Cult. Roswaal appears as a seemingly eccentric but helpful lord, but his true motives are hidden.

The Sanctuary Arc

Source Re:Zero - Starting Life in Another World, Volume 4-9Culture Japanese light novelPeriod 2014-2015

Summary

Roswaal's true plan is revealed: he intends to use Subaru to break the Sanctuary's barrier and resurrect Echidna, the Witch of Greed. He manipulates Subaru into accepting a trial, and when Subaru fails, Roswaal forces a confrontation, revealing his desperation and his willingness to sacrifice others for his goal.

Emilia

Roswaal is Emilia's sponsor and supporter, providing her with a home and resources for her candidacy for the royal selection. However, his support is conditional and self-serving.

Manipulation of Subaru

Source Light novel, Volume 4, Chapter 3

Description

Roswaal deliberately puts Subaru through traumatic experiences, including forcing him to confront the death of his friends, in order to break his spirit and make him more dependent on Roswaal. This is a form of psychological abuse.

Scholarly Context

This is a common trope in dark fantasy, where a mentor figure is actually a manipulator.

Servitude of Ram and Rem

Source Light novel, Volume 1, Chapter 2

Description

Ram and Rem were taken in by Roswaal after their village was destroyed. While they are treated well, they are essentially in servitude, and their loyalty is exploited. Ram's situation is more complex, as she may have been chosen for a specific purpose.

Scholarly Context

This reflects themes of power dynamics and class in fantasy settings.

Obsession with Echidna

Source Light novel, Volume 8, Chapter 1

Description

Roswaal's obsession with resurrecting Echidna leads him to commit morally questionable acts, including potentially sacrificing others. His obsession is portrayed as a driving force that justifies his actions in his mind.

Scholarly Context

This is a classic tragic flaw, similar to Faust's desire for knowledge.

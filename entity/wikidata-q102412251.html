Who It Is

Roswaal L Mathers is a name that echoes through the halls of the Roswaal mansion, a figure of immense magical power and eccentricity, whose every gesture and word is a calculated piece in a grand, centuries-old game.

Roswaal is flamboyant, eccentric, and speaks in a sing-song, lisping manner. He is highly intelligent and manipulative, often playing the fool to hide his true intentions. He is obsessed with reviving his teacher, Echidna, and will go to great lengths to achieve this goal, including manipulating Subaru and others. He is also a powerful mage, one of the strongest in the world.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Roswaal' is a given name of Germanic origin, possibly meaning 'rose ruler' or 'rose power'. 'L' is a middle initial, and 'Mathers' is a surname, likely of English origin, meaning 'son of Matthew'.

Pronunciation — ROZ-wahl EL MATH-ers

Aliases — Roswaal, Roswaal Mathers, The Margrave, Lord Roswaal

Ownership — Fictional character created by Tappei Nagatsuki for the Japanese light novel series 'Re:Zero'.

Powers
Magic

Roswaal is a master of magic, capable of using all six elements (fire, water, wind, earth, yin, and yang). He can cast powerful spells, including offensive, defensive, and support magic. He is particularly skilled in fire and water magic.

Limit: Magic use consumes mana, and overuse can lead to exhaustion. Roswaal's power is immense but not infinite.

Contract with Beatrice

Roswaal has a contract with Beatrice, a spirit of the yin element. This contract allows him to use her power, but it also binds them together. The contract is a key part of his plan.

Limit: The contract requires Roswaal to fulfill Beatrice's conditions, and it ties their fates.

Immortality (implied)

Roswaal has lived for over 400 years, suggesting he has achieved some form of immortality, possibly through his magic or a contract with Echidna.

Limit: The mechanism of his longevity is not fully explained, and it may have costs.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Roswaal is a tall, slim man with long, flowing purple hair and heterochromatic eyes: his right eye is yellow, his left is blue. He wears an elaborate, colorful outfit with a cape, and often carries a staff. His makeup is theatrical, with heavy eyeliner and lipstick, giving him an androgynous, clown-like appearance.

Symbols — His staff is a symbol of his magical authority. His heterochromia is a visual representation of his dual nature, and his theatrical makeup reflects his performer-like personality.

Domains — Roswaal resides in the Roswaal Mansion, located in the Mathers domain of the Kingdom of Lugunica. He is the Margrave of the Mathers territory and a member of the Council of Elders.

Accounts I
The Roswaal Mansion ArcRe:Zero - Starting Life in Another World, Volume 1-3

2012-2013

Subaru Natsuki, a young man transported to another world, is taken in by Roswaal's mansion after helping Emilia, a silver-haired half-elf. Roswaal employs Subaru as a butler, and Subaru becomes entangled in the mansion's mysteries, including the demonic beasts in the forest and the machinations of the Witch Cult. Roswaal appears as a seemingly eccentric but helpful lord, but his true motives are hidden. Subaru gains allies and begins to uncover the world's secrets, while Roswaal observes his actions with interest.

Accounts II
The Sanctuary ArcRe:Zero - Starting Life in Another World, Volume 4-9

2014-2015

Roswaal's true plan is revealed: he intends to use Subaru to break the Sanctuary's barrier and resurrect Echidna, the Witch of Greed. He manipulates Subaru into accepting a trial, and when Subaru fails, Roswaal forces a confrontation, revealing his desperation and his willingness to sacrifice others for his goal. Subaru and Emilia grow stronger, and Roswaal's plans are thwarted, but he remains a looming threat.

Roswaal is a villain. — Roswaal is a morally gray character. He is not inherently evil but is willing to do evil things to achieve his goals. He is more of an antagonist in certain arcs but can also be an ally.

Roswaal is a clown. — His appearance and mannerisms are theatrical, but he is a serious and powerful mage. The clownish exterior is a mask.

Roswaal is in love with Echidna. — His obsession is more about his desire to learn from her and to bring her back, not necessarily romantic love. The text does not confirm romantic feelings.

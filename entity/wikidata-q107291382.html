Pronunciation The name is pronounced as 'Eun-ah' (IPA: /ɯn.a/), with the first syllable 'eun' sounding like 'un' in 'under' but with a more closed mouth, and the second syllable 'a' as in 'father'. In English, it is often pronounced as 'You-na' or 'Ee-na', but the Korean pronunciation is closer to 'Eun-ah'.

Etymology

The name 'Euna' is a transliteration of the Korean name 은아 (Eun-a) or 은아 (Eun-ah), which is composed of the elements 'eun' (은) meaning 'silver' or 'grace' and 'a' (아) meaning 'child' or 'beautiful'. It is also a variant of 'Eun-ah' (은아), a common Korean female given name. In some contexts, 'Euna' may be a romanization of '유나' (Yu-na), but the specific form 'Euna' is less common and may be a simplified or altered spelling.

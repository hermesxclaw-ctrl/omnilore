Who It Is

Semar is the most profound figure in Javanese culture — a fat, comical servant who is simultaneously the most powerful divine being. He is the guardian of Java, elder brother of Batara Guru, and a manifestation of the divine principle Ismaya. His grotesque appearance conceals his divinity. His humor in wayang carries spiritual wisdom and social critique.

Attested in Javanese wayang tradition (c. 10th century onward)Era Medieval Javanese kingdoms to present

Who It Is

Imagine being born into a hellish orphanage where every day is a lesson in breaking your spirit—and then, with nothing but your wits and a pair of handcuffs, you escape not only that prison but the entire god-war that made you a pawn. That's Scott Free, the super escape artist who turned the ultimate cosmic trap into a magic act.

Scott Free is characterized by his optimistic, defiant spirit. Despite a traumatic childhood on Apokolips, he maintains a cheerful, almost playful demeanor. He is a master of escape, using intelligence and agility rather than brute force. He is deeply devoted to his wife, Big Barda, and values freedom above all. In later portrayals, especially under Tom King, he is more introspective and troubled, dealing with existential angst and the pressures of his divine nature.

Powers
Superhuman Physiology

As a New God, Scott Free possesses enhanced strength, speed, endurance, and agility, far beyond human limits. He is functionally immortal, resistant to disease and aging.

Limit: He can be injured and potentially killed by sufficient force, though he heals faster than a human.

Master Escape Artist

Scott Free is renowned as the greatest escape artist in the universe. He can escape from any physical restraint, including handcuffs, straitjackets, chains, and even complex traps, using a combination of skill, knowledge, and technology.

Limit: His escapes are not magical; they rely on physical ability and preparation. If truly caught off guard, he may be temporarily trapped.

Genius-Level Intellect

Scott Free is a brilliant strategist and inventor, often using his intellect to outwit opponents and create escape devices.

Limit: None notable.

Use of Apokoliptian Technology

He utilizes advanced technology from Apokolips, including his costume's ability to generate a 'Miracle' effect, and various gadgets like the 'Aero-Discs' for flight.

Limit: Technology can be damaged or depleted.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Scott Free is typically depicted as a tall, athletic man with blond hair and blue eyes. He wears a red and blue costume with a stylized 'M' on the chest, a cape, and a utility belt containing various escape tools. The costume is reminiscent of a circus performer, with a sleek, high-tech look. Thaddeus Brown is older, with gray hair, and wears a similar but more classic costume. Symbols — The 'M' emblem is the primary symbol, representing 'Miracle'. The costume's red and blue colors evoke heroism and showmanship. The character is often associated with chains, handcuffs, and straitjackets, symbolizing escape and freedom.

Domains — Scott Free primarily operates on Earth, but his origins are on the planet New Genesis, a paradise-like world. He was raised on Apokolips, a hellish industrial world. He often uses a mobile headquarters, the 'Miracle Mobile', and has connections to the New Gods' realm.

Accounts I
Origin of Scott FreeMister Miracle #1 (1971)

1970s

Scott Free, the son of Highfather, is traded to Darkseid as part of a peace pact. He is raised in Granny Goodness's orphanage on Apokolips, enduring harsh training. He escapes to Earth, meets Thaddeus Brown, and after Brown's murder, takes on the mantle of Mister Miracle. Scott becomes a superhero on Earth, and his escape nullifies the peace pact, leading to renewed war between New Genesis and Apokolips.

The PactNew Gods #7 (1972)

1970s

The story of the exchange of heirs between New Genesis and Apokolips is told in flashback, showing the origins of Scott Free and Orion. Establishes the cosmic backdrop for the Fourth World saga.

Accounts II
Marriage to Big BardaMister Miracle #18 (1974)

1970s

Scott Free and Big Barda marry in a ceremony on Earth, solidifying their love story. Their relationship becomes a cornerstone of the character's mythology.

Mister Miracle (2017) - 'Death'Mister Miracle (2017) #1-12

2010s

Tom King's acclaimed limited series explores Scott Free's struggle with depression and the possibility of death, as he and Barda attempt to live a normal life on Earth while dealing with the looming war with Apokolips. The series is known for its ambiguous, possibly hallucinatory narrative. The series received critical acclaim and won the Eisner Award for Best Limited Series.

The Source of FreedomMister Miracle: The Source of Freedom #1-6 (2021-2022)

2020s

Shilo Norman takes center stage as Mister Miracle, dealing with the legacy of the name and his own challenges, including facing a new threat. Establishes Shilo as a more prominent hero in the DC Universe.

Mister Miracle is a single character. — There have been three characters using the name: Thaddeus Brown, Scott Free, and Shilo Norman.

Scott Free is a human. — Scott Free is a New God, an alien being with superhuman abilities and immortality.

Scott Free's escape abilities are magical. — His escapes are based on his extraordinary skill, intellect, and use of technology, not magic.

Mister Miracle is a minor character. — He is a central figure in Jack Kirby's Fourth World saga and has been a key member of the Justice League International.

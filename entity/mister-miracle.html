Who It Is

Mister Miracle is Scott Free, the biological son of Highfather of New Genesis who was traded as an infant to Darkseid as part of the peace pact. Raised on the hell-world Apokolips by Granny Goodness in her orphanage of horrors, Scott was subjected to brutal conditioning designed to break his will and turn him into a loyal servant of Darkseid. Instead, the torture had the opposite effect: it forged Scott into a being of unbreakable will and a genius for escape. Scott Free can escape from anything — any prison, any trap, any dimension, any psychological conditioning. His power is a combination of superhuman reflexes (granted by his New God physiology), an intuitive understanding of mechanical and magical systems, and an unshakable belief that freedom is the fundamental right of all beings. He uses a Mother Box — a small, sentient computer from New Genesis — which can teleport him across dimensions, create protective barriers, heal wounds, and interface with any technology. As Mister Miracle, Scott became Earth's greatest escape artist, performing death-defying stunts not for fame but to prove that no trap — no prison, no system, no force in the universe — can hold a being determined to be free. He married Big Barda, a former Female Fury who escaped Apokolips with him. Together, they became heroes on Earth and members of the Justice League International. Mister Miracle represents the most hopeful message in the DC pantheon: that freedom is not a gift from the gods, but a choice that every being can make. You can be raised in hell, conditioned to obey, surrounded by tyranny, and still choose to be free. The only lock that cannot be picked is the one you place on your own mind.

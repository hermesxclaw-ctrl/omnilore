Who It Is

Mixcoatl ('Cloud Serpent') is the god of the Milky Way, of the hunt, and of the northern stars — a complex and ancient figure who bridges the nomadic Chichimeca hunting tradition and the settled Toltec-Aztec civilization. As a celestial serpent whose body stretches across the night sky, Mixcoatl is the Milky Way itself — the 'white road' of stars (Iztac Mixcoatl) that guides hunters and warriors through the darkness. He is the patron of the bow-and-arrow, the deer hunt, and the fire-drill (from whose friction the New Fire was kindled every 52 years). In Toltec-Aztec royal genealogy, Mixcoatl is the father of the legendary priest-king Ce Acatl Topiltzin Quetzalcoatl, making him the progenitor of the Toltec ruling line that all later Mesoamerican dynasties claimed. His wife Chimalman (Shield Hand) is a sky-goddess associated with the morning star. Mixcoatl's festival, Quecholli, was a great communal hunt on Mount Zacatepetl, culminating in the sacrifice of a deer or a human impersonator of the god.

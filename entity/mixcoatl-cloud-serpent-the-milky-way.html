Who It Is
Form

Mixcoatl is often depicted as a serpent or dragon, with clouds or a cloudy aura surrounding him. He is associated with the Milky Way, which is seen as a serpent or dragon in the night sky.

Powers

Storm Bringer Mixcoatl has the power to bring storms and fertility to the land, but also has the power to destroy crops and bring famine.

Accounts

Mixcoatl is said to have been born from the union of the sun and the clouds. He is associated with the creation of the world and the bringing of fertility to the land.

Mixcoatl is said to have been killed by the gods, who were jealous of his power. He is associated with the destruction of the world and the bringing of famine to the land.

Scholarly Debates
Etymology
Mixcoatl's name comes from the Nahuatl languages, with mixtli meaning cloud and cōātl meaning serpent. ⇌ Mixcoatl's name comes from the Aztec calendar, with mixcoatl meaning 13th day of the month.

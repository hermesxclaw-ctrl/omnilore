Who It Is
Form

In Polish folklore, the Strzyga is often depicted as a female figure with a long, black dress and a white face. In Albanian mythology, the Shtriga is described as a vampiric witch that sucks the blood of infants at night. In The Witcher series, the Striga is a cursed princess with supernatural abilities.

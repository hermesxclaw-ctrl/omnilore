Who It Is
Form

Regenerador is a large, humanoid entity with a body composed of Las Plagas-infected flesh. It has a distinctive breathing system, which allows it to sustain itself even in environments with limited oxygen. Its appearance is often described as grotesque and unsettling.

Powers

Regeneration Regenerador's ability to regenerate itself allows it to heal from injuries and adapt to its environment. This power is fueled by the Las Plagas parasite, which provides Regenerador with the necessary energy to sustain itself.

Incident Log

Ashley Graham, the daughter of the President of the United States, is abducted by a religious cult in rural Spain. Leon S. Kennedy, a special agent, is sent to rescue her and must navigate through hordes of infected enemies, including Regenerador.

The origins of Las Plagas, the parasite that infects Regenerador, are shrouded in mystery. However, it is known that Las Plagas has been present in the world for centuries, influencing human history and shaping the course of events.

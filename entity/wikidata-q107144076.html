The Approach

In the crowded, fluorescent-lit corridors of a Parisian hospital, a young doctor's idealism collides with the brutal arithmetic of life, death, and institutional survival. Thomas Lilti's 'Hippocrate' is not a story about a legendary healer, but about the fragile humans who inherit that name—and the choices that haunt them long after the shift ends.

Content advisory. Depictions of medical procedures, patient death, and emotional distress; themes of medical error, professional misconduct, and systemic understaffing.

Etymology

The title references Hippocrates, the ancient Greek physician, symbolizing the medical profession and its ethical ideals.

Description

Appearance

The film is a realistic drama set in a busy French hospital, with naturalistic lighting and handheld camerawork that conveys the intensity and chaos of medical work.

Symbols & Iconography

The hospital itself serves as a symbol of life, death, and institutional pressure. The title 'Hippocrate' evokes the Hippocratic Oath, contrasting the ideals of medicine with the flawed reality.

Behaviour & Personality

The film focuses on the emotional and ethical struggles of medical interns, particularly Benjamin, who begins idealistic and becomes disillusioned, and Abdel, a competent but marginalized foreign doctor.

Domains & Habitats

Set primarily in a French public hospital, with scenes in patient rooms, corridors, and administrative offices.

Version Differences

The 2018 TV series is a spin-off that follows a group of interns in a different hospital, expanding the universe but with new characters and storylines.

Stories & Myths

The Internship

Source Hippocrate (2014 film)Culture FrenchPeriod Contemporary (2014)

Summary

Benjamin Barois begins his internship in his father's hospital service. He befriends Abdel, an Algerian intern. During a night shift, a homeless patient dies after Benjamin fails to perform an ECG. He is instructed to lie about it. Meanwhile, they treat Mrs. Richard, an elderly woman with cancer. They decide to stop treatment, leading to a disciplinary hearing. Abdel is sanctioned, but later the sanction is withdrawn after Benjamin's breakdown and the hospital's systemic issues are exposed.

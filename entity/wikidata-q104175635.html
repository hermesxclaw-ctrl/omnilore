Who It Is

Maki Nishikino is a brilliant first-year student at Otonokizaka Academy, born into a family of doctors, who discovers her true passion for music and idol performance against the expectations of her prestigious medical lineage. Her journey from a reluctant, aloof genius to a devoted composer and vocal coach for the school idol group μ's is a story of self-discovery, friendship, and the courage to follow one's own dreams.

Maki initially appears aloof, blunt, and tsundere-like, but she is deeply caring and passionate about music. She is highly intelligent and dedicated, often pushing herself to excel. She warms up to her friends over time and shows a protective side.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Nishikino (西木野) is a Japanese surname; Maki (真姫) is a given name. The kanji for Maki mean 'true' and 'princess' or 'lute'.

Pronunciation — MAH-kee NEE-shee-kee-noh

Aliases — Nishikino Maki, Maki-chan

Ownership — Japanese media franchise, created by ASCII Media Works, Lantis, and Sunrise.

Powers
Musical Composition

Maki is a gifted composer, creating original songs for μ's. Her compositions are integral to the group's identity and success.

Limit: Initially reluctant to compose for the group due to her family's expectations; later embraces it fully.

Piano Performance

Maki is an accomplished pianist, often playing for the group during practice and performances.

Limit: None significant.

Vocal Coaching

She serves as the group's vocal coach, using her musical expertise to train members.

Limit: None.

Academic Excellence

Maki is an honor student, excelling in her studies, particularly in science and medicine-related subjects.

Limit: Her academic path conflicts with her musical passion.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Maki has long, straight red hair and amber eyes. She is often seen in her school uniform or in idol costumes designed for μ's performances. She has a slender build and is typically depicted with a confident or slightly aloof expression.

Symbols — Her image color is red. Her symbol is a music note, reflecting her role as composer. In merchandise, she is often associated with pianos and sheet music.

Domains — Otonokizaka Academy, her family's hospital, her home (a large, wealthy residence), and various performance venues for μ's.

Accounts I
Maki's Reluctant Joining of μ'sLove Live! School Idol Project (anime), Season 1

Contemporary

Maki is initially approached by Honoka and others to join the school idol group. She declines, citing her future as a doctor, but is gradually drawn in by her love for music and the group's determination. Maki becomes the group's composer and vocal coach, contributing to their success.

Accounts II
Maki's Role in Love Live CompetitionLove Live! School Idol Project (anime), Season 2

Contemporary

Maki composes songs for μ's as they compete in the Love Live competition, ultimately winning. Her dedication and talent help the group achieve their dream. μ's wins the competition, but disband shortly after due to graduation and personal goals.

Maki's Future DecisionLove Live! The School Idol Movie

Contemporary

After μ's disbands, Maki decides to pursue a career in music rather than medicine, with the support of her friends and family. She follows her passion, and her family accepts her choice.

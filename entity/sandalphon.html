Who It Is
Form

Sandalphon is often depicted as a majestic angel with a strong presence, gathering prayers and passing them on to God.

Powers

Accounts

Sandalphon is said to have been created by God to gather prayers from humanity and convey them to the divine.

Sandalphon is associated with the Hebrew letter סַנְדַּלְפוֹן (Sandalpōn), which represents the connection between heaven and earth.

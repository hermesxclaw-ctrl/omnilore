Who It Is

Archangel described as the twin brother of Metatron. In Kabbalistic tradition, Sandalphon gathers the prayers of Israel and weaves them into crowns for the Creator. He is said to be of such immense stature that his height spans the distance from heaven to earth — a span of 500 years' journey according to the Talmud. Some traditions identify him with the prophet Elijah transfigured. His name likely derives from the Greek 'synadelphos' (co-brother). In the Zohar, Sandalphon stands at the end of the celestial hierarchy, receiving divine emanations and transmitting them downward, complementing Metatron's upward ministry.

Who It Is

An infinite modern city, perpetually overcast, with functioning infrastructure but no permanent population. Apartment buildings are furnished. Cafes have coffee. But the coffee never runs out, apartments periodically reset, and subway trains arrive without drivers. Something maintains Level 11 as a stage set. Long-term residents feel they are being watched and prepared for something.

Form

A sprawling city under a gray, sunless sky. Architecture varies — high-rises, suburbs, industrial districts — blending into each other without boundaries. The city extends infinitely. Streetlights work. Cars are empty and immobile. The silence is the most unsettling feature.

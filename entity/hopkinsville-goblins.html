Hopkinsville Goblins

Kelly-Hopkinsville Encounter, Little Green Men of Kentucky

Who It Is
Form

The Hopkinsville Goblins are described as being approximately 3-4 feet tall, with greenish-gray skin, large eyes, and a distinctive, pointed head. They were said to have a strong, unpleasant odor.

Powers

Telepathic Communication Some witnesses reported experiencing strange, telepathic communications with the Hopkinsville Goblins, which some have interpreted as a form of mind control or a warning.

Accounts

A family in Hopkinsville, Kentucky reported encountering strange, green-skinned beings in their backyard, which they described as aggressive and threatening.

The Hopkinsville Goblins are a unique example of a UFO encounter that combines multiple categories, including a creature encounter, a farmhouse siege, and a police investigation.

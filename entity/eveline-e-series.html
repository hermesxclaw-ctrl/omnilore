Eveline (E-Series)

also known asE-Series
TypeBio-Organic Weapon (B.O.W.) — E-Series Prototype
Who It Is
Form

Eveline is a genetically modified human with a unique, almost androgynous appearance. Her body is a blend of human and fungal features, with a pale, almost translucent complexion and long, spindly limbs. Her face is a featureless, expressionless mask, with no discernible nose or mouth.

Powers

Regenerative Healing Eveline has the ability to rapidly heal from injuries, regenerating damaged tissue and restoring her physical form to its original state.

Accounts

Eveline was created by The Connections as part of their bioweapon research program. She was designed to be a highly advanced, highly adaptable bioweapon capable of eliminating enemies without the need for combat.

Eveline escaped from her laboratory and began operating in the field, using her advanced abilities to eliminate her enemies and adapt to new situations.

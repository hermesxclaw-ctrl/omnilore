Who It Is

The E-Series Mold bioweapon, designed to appear as a 9-year-old girl to infiltrate families and destabilize targets through emotional manipulation. Eveline was created by The Connections as a next-generation bioweapon with unprecedented Mold-based powers — she can infect others through touch, create Mold minions (the Molded), induce hallucinations, and control entire populations. Her emotional instability, loneliness, and desperation for a 'family' made her increasingly dangerous and unpredictable.

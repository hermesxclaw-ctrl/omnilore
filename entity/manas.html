Who It Is

Manas is the legendary hero who united forty Kyrgyz tribes and led them against enemies. Born with a blood clot in his hand, he displayed prodigious strength from childhood. The epic chronicles his life from birth to death and his son's adventures — a vast encyclopedia of Kyrgyz culture. UNESCO recognizes it as an Intangible Heritage Masterpiece.

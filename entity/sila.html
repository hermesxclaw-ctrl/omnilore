Who It Is
Form

Sila is often depicted as a shape-shifting jinn, able to take on various forms to deceive and mislead humans.

Powers

Shape-shifting Sila's ability to take on various forms allows him to deceive and mislead humans, making him a formidable and feared entity in Arabian and Islamic cultures.

Accounts

A young traveler becomes lost in the desert and encounters Sila, who offers to guide him to safety but ultimately leads him astray.

Sila is said to have control over the wind, using his powers to create powerful storms and whirlwinds.

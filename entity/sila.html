Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name Sila is derived from the Arabic word "Sila", meaning 'treacherous spirits of invariable form'.

Pronunciation — SEE-lah

Aliases — Si'la, Si'lat, Silla, Sand Demon, Deluder of the Desert

Ownership — Arabian and Islamic cultures, with a focus on the mythology of the Middle East and North Africa.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Sila is often depicted as a shape-shifting jinn, able to take on various forms to deceive and mislead humans.

Symbols — The symbols associated with Sila include the desert, sand, and wind, which are often used to represent his power and influence.

Domains — The domains associated with Sila include the desert, the wind, and the weather, which are often used to represent his power and influence.

The Story of Sila and the Desert WandererArabian Nights

Middle Ages

A young traveler becomes lost in the desert and encounters Sila, who offers to guide him to safety but ultimately leads him astray. The traveler becomes lost and disoriented, highlighting the dangers of the desert and the power of Sila.

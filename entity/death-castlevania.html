Who It Is
Form

Death is depicted as a tall, skeletal figure with a hood and a scythe. He is often seen wearing a black cloak and has glowing red eyes. His appearance varies across different games in the series, but his core design remains the same.

Powers

Soul Harvesting Death's ability to harvest souls, which allows him to drain the life force of his enemies and use it to his advantage.

Dark Magic Death's mastery of dark magic, which enables him to cast powerful spells and manipulate the environment to his advantage.

Enhanced Strength Death's enhanced physical strength, which allows him to wield his scythe with deadly precision and overpower his enemies.

Accounts

In this game, Death is a recurring boss who serves as a loyal servant of Dracula. He is tasked with stopping the protagonist, Alucard, from reaching his master.

Etymology

The name Eubote (Εὐβώτη) is likely derived from Greek εὖ (eu, 'good') and βοτήρ (boter, 'herdsman') or βοῦς (bous, 'ox/cow'), thus meaning 'good herdsman' or 'she of the good cattle'. Alternatively, it could be a feminine form of Eubotes, a name attested in some Greek contexts.

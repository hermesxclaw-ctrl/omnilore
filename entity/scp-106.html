Who It Is
Form

SCP-106 appears as an elderly man with a gaunt face and sunken eyes. It is approximately 1.8 meters tall and has a frail, emaciated build. Its skin is pale and almost translucent, with visible veins and wrinkles.

Powers

Temporal Manipulation SCP-106 has the ability to manipulate time and space, allowing it to move through different points in time and space.

Incident Log

SCP-106 is a highly aggressive and unpredictable entity that has been captured and contained by the SCP Foundation. It is known to lash out at its captors and has been observed to be highly intelligent and cunning.

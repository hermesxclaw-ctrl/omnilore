Who It Is

SCP-106 appears as an elderly human male of indeterminate age, with severely decomposed, blackened flesh that appears to be in a constant state of putrefaction. Its body and clothing are coated in a black, tar-like substance that it secretes continuously. Despite its frail appearance, SCP-106 possesses immense strength and speed, and is capable of phasing through solid matter by corroding it — any material it touches undergoes rapid rusting, rotting, or molecular breakdown. The entity's signature ability is its capacity to move through walls, floors, and ceilings by creating patches of black corrosive slime, through which it can emerge without warning. SCP-106 is aggressively predatory and specifically hunts humans, dragging victims into a pocket dimension it controls. This 'pocket dimension' is described by survivors as an endless labyrinth of dark, corroded hallways filled with the entity's viscous residue. Victims are subjected to prolonged torture and mutilation, with SCP-106 engaging in what appears to be cat-and-mouse hunting behavior for its own amusement. Time passes differently within the pocket dimension; subjects have reported hours of torment in what was only minutes outside. The Foundation discovered that SCP-106 has a single exploitable weakness: bright, direct light causes it significant distress and forces it to retreat to its dimension. Containment procedures involve a sealed chamber lined with lead and embedded with high-intensity floodlights, with an elaborate pulley system that suspends the entity in a constantly illuminated state if it breaches into the main cell. SCP-106 is believed to be Corporal Lawrence ████████, a World War I soldier who was exposed to anomalous trench gas and subsequently transformed. The entity occasionally makes cryptic references to 'the war' and 'the mud.' SCP-106 represents one of the Foundation's most persistent containment challenges, having breached containment numerous times. Its combination of near-intangibility, a personal torture dimension, and predatory intelligence makes it one of the most dangerous humanoid SCPs in Foundation custody.

Form

SCP-106 is described as a humanoid figure, appearing as an elderly, emaciated man with a gaunt, skeletal face. Its skin is discolored, with a black, tar-like substance oozing from its pores and orifices. It wears tattered, decayed clothing, often depicted as a tattered suit or lab coat. Its movements are slow and deliberate, but it can move with surprising speed when pursuing prey. It leaves a trail of corrosive black goo wherever it goes.

Powers

Corrosive Touch SCP-106's touch causes rapid decay and corrosion of organic and inorganic matter. A brief contact can cause severe tissue damage, and prolonged contact can reduce a person to a withered husk. SCP Foundation Wiki (canon) The corrosion is not instantaneous; it requires sustained contact. It can be delayed or mitigated by protective gear, though the corrosive substance can eventually breach most materials. Some tales depict the corrosion as a passive effect of its presence, while others require direct contact.

Phasing SCP-106 can phase through solid matter, moving through walls, floors, and other barriers as if they were not there. This allows it to bypass most physical containment. SCP Foundation Wiki (canon) It appears to be limited by the thickness and composition of the material; it can be slowed or temporarily contained by complex, multi-layered barriers. The exact limits of its phasing are not fully defined, leading to discrepancies in various tales and games.

Pocket Dimension SCP-106 can drag victims into a personal pocket dimension, a decaying, labyrinthine space where it can torture them at its leisure. Time within this dimension is distorted, and victims may experience hours of torment that pass in minutes in the real world. SCP Foundation Wiki (canon) It must physically grab a victim to pull them in. It cannot open a portal from a distance. The dimension's layout is not fixed and can be navigated by a determined victim, though escape is nearly impossible. The nature of the pocket dimension is described differently in various tales; some depict it as a hellish landscape, others as a twisted reflection of the real world.

Sadistic Intelligence SCP-106 is highly intelligent and sadistic, capable of strategic planning and psychological manipulation. It enjoys toying with its victims, prolonging their suffering. SCP Foundation Wiki (canon) Its intelligence is not omniscient; it can be outwitted by determined and resourceful individuals. None noted.

Corrosive Trail SCP-106 leaves a trail of corrosive black goo as it moves, which can damage surfaces and cause harm to anyone who steps in it. SCP Foundation Wiki (canon) The trail is not infinite; it dissipates over time. None noted.

Behavior & Habitat

SCP-106 is a sadistic, predatory entity that hunts and torments its victims. It is highly intelligent and patient, stalking its prey for extended periods before striking. It appears to derive pleasure from causing extreme pain and psychological distress. It is known to 'play' with its victims, dragging them into its pocket dimension to torture them over what seems like an eternity, before eventually killing them or leaving them mangled. It is attracted to sounds of distress and movement, and it can phase through solid matter to reach its prey.

Field Notes

█ SCP-106's appearance is often compared to a 'skinny, old man' with a permanent grimace, but its most distinctive feature is the black, viscous substance that constantly oozes from its body.

█ In the game 'SCP: Containment Breach', SCP-106 is one of the most feared enemies, and its pocket dimension is a notoriously difficult area to escape.

█ The SCP Foundation's classification of SCP-106 as 'Keter' means that it is considered extremely difficult to contain, but not impossible. This has led to numerous memes about the Foundation's 'containment breach' procedures.

█ SCP-106 is one of the most popular SCPs in the community, frequently appearing in fan art, animations, and even music. Its popularity is a testament to the power of its simple, yet terrifying, concept.

The full extent of its powers and the nature of its pocket dimension are not fully defined. The SCP Wiki is a collaborative project, and canon is not strictly enforced, leading to inconsistencies. A way to hold it in your mind

Imagine a predator that is not just a beast, but a force of nature. It is the embodiment of decay and the inevitability of death. It is not a lion that pounces, but a slow, creeping rot that eventually consumes everything. SCP-106 is the fear of aging, the fear of being trapped, and the fear of something that is patient enough to wait for you to make a mistake. It is a horror that is not just about the jump scare, but about the slow, dawning realization that you are not safe anywhere.

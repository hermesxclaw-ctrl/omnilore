Who It Is

A thousand years of scheming, a body that is never truly his own, and a plan that spans the entire history of jujutsu society—Kenjaku is not merely a villain but the hidden architect behind the world of Jujutsu Kaisen. From the shadows of the Heian era to the modern chaos of Tokyo, his machinations have shaped the fates of sorcerers and curses alike, and his true nature is as elusive as the countless vessels he has worn.

Kenjaku is highly intelligent, manipulative, patient, and calculating. He is a master strategist who plans centuries ahead. He is also curious and experimentally minded, treating people as subjects in his grand experiment. He is generally calm and unflappable, even in the face of danger, and displays a twisted sense of humor. He is not overtly sadistic, but he is utterly amoral, viewing human life as a means to an end. He is also a skilled actor, able to convincingly impersonate his vessels, and he takes pleasure in playing different roles.

Powers
Body Hopping (Brain Transfer)

Kenjaku's innate technique allows him to transfer his consciousness, which resides in his brain, into another person's body by removing their brain and replacing it with his own. He can then use the body as his own, accessing its cursed energy and techniques. He retains all memories and knowledge from his previous vessels.

Limit: He cannot use the body's innate technique unless he has fully integrated with it, which takes time. He also cannot use the body's cursed technique if it is incompatible with his own. Additionally, he can only transfer into a body that is compatible, and the process leaves a distinctive stitched scar on the forehead.

Cursed Technique Manipulation

Through his extensive knowledge of jujutsu, Kenjaku can use the cursed techniques of his vessels to a certain extent. For example, when in Geto's body, he can use the Cursed Spirit Manipulation technique to control and summon curses. He is also able to use various barrier techniques and has a high level of skill in cursed energy manipulation.

Limit: He may not be able to use techniques that are inherently incompatible with his own cursed energy or that require a specific body modification. He also cannot use techniques that are exclusive to a specific bloodline unless he is in a body with that bloodline.

Immortality (via Body Hopping)

By transferring his brain to new bodies, Kenjaku has effectively lived for over a thousand years. He is not immortal in the sense of being unkillable, but he can avoid death by abandoning a body before it is destroyed.

Limit: If his brain is destroyed, he dies permanently. He also needs to find compatible bodies, which may be rare. The process of transferring is vulnerable to interruption.

Domain Expansion: Self-Embodiment of Perfection

Kenjaku's Domain Expansion is a unique barrier that allows him to control the rules of the domain, including the ability to modify the bodies of those inside. He used this in his fight against Yuki Tsukumo and Choso. The domain appears as a vast, eerie space with a large brain-like structure in the sky.

Limit: Like all Domain Expansions, it requires a large amount of cursed energy and can be countered by another Domain Expansion or a Simple Domain. The exact conditions and effects are not fully explained.

Barrier Techniques

Kenjaku is highly skilled in creating barriers, as demonstrated by his ability to set up the Culling Game barriers across Japan and to create the curtain over Shibuya. He can also modify the rules of barriers to suit his needs.

Limit: Barrier techniques require precise cursed energy control and can be broken by powerful attacks or by using a Simple Domain.

Cursed Energy Manipulation

Kenjaku has immense cursed energy reserves and can use it for reinforcement, healing, and various techniques. He is a formidable hand-to-hand combatant, able to hold his own against top-tier sorcerers.

Limit: His physical abilities are dependent on the vessel he is using, but he can enhance them with cursed energy.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Kenjaku's true form is a disembodied brain with a mouth and teeth, which he keeps in a jar or directly in the skull of his current vessel. He typically possesses the body of a human sorcerer, and his appearance changes with each vessel. In his most prominent vessel, that of the sorcerer Geto Suguru, he has long black hair tied in a low ponytail, a stitched scar across his forehead where the brain was implanted, and he wears traditional Japanese c

Symbols — Kenjaku is associated with the brain, the stitched scar, and the jar that holds his brain. These symbols represent his nature as a parasitic consciousness that steals bodies. He is also associated with the concept of 'evolution' and 'chaos,' as he seeks to force the evolution of humanity through cursed energy.

Domains — Kenjaku operates throughout Japan, often moving between different locations to pursue his goals. He has been active since the Heian era (794-1185) and has been involved in various historical events. In modern times, he orchestrates the Culling Game across Japan, setting up barriers in various prefectures. He is also known to have a hidden laboratory where he conducts experiments on cursed objects and humans.

Accounts I
The Heian Era OriginsImplied in manga, not fully detailed

794-1185

Kenjaku's origins date back to the Heian era, a time when jujutsu sorcerers were powerful and curses were rampant. He was originally a human sorcerer who discovered a way to transfer his consciousness to other bodies, granting him immortality. He became obsessed with the evolution of humanity through cursed energy and began manipulating events from behind the scenes. He is implied to have been involved in the creation of the Death Paintings (artificial humans) and possibly the downfall of the Sugawara clan. His actions laid the groundwork for the modern jujutsu world and the Culling Game.

The Creation of the Death PaintingsManga chapters 145-146

1603-1868

Kenjaku, using the body of a woman (the mother of the Death Paintings), had children with a cursed spirit. These children were half-human, half-cursed spirit, and were later sealed as cursed objects. They became known as the Death Paintings, including Choso, Kechizu, and Eso. Kenjaku's goal was to create beings that could bridge the gap between humans and curses. The Death Paintings became powerful cursed objects, and Choso later becomes a key ally of Yuji Itadori.

Possession of Kamo NoritoshiManga chapters 89-90

1603-1868

Kenjaku possessed the body of Kamo Noritoshi, a sorcerer from the Kamo family, one of the three great jujutsu families. Using this identity, he conducted inhumane experiments to create cursed objects and manipulate bloodline techniques. He was responsible for the creation of the Death Paintings and other cursed items. His actions were so heinous that the Kamo family covered them up. The Kamo family's reputation was tarnished, and Kenjaku gained access to the family's resources and knowledge.

Accounts II
The Star Plasma Vessel IncidentManga chapters 65-79 (Hidden Inventory arc), anime Season 2 episodes 23-24

2006

Kenjaku, in an unknown vessel, orchestrated the attempted assassination of Riko Amanai, the Star Plasma Vessel, to prevent her merger with Tengen. He hired the assassin Toji Fushiguro to kill her. This event led to the awakening of Gojo Satoru's powers and the death of Geto Suguru's best friend, which began Geto's descent into darkness. Gojo became the strongest sorcerer, Geto became disillusioned with protecting the weak, and the path to Geto's eventual fall was set.

The Shibuya IncidentManga chapters 83-137, anime Season 2 episodes 25-29

2018

Kenjaku, now in the body of Geto Suguru, masterminded the Shibuya Incident. He orchestrated the release of thousands of curses, the sealing of Gojo Satoru, and the chaos that engulfed Shibuya. He manipulated the events to weaken the jujutsu world and to further his plan for the Culling Game. He also revealed his true nature to the sorcerers, shocking them with his possession of Geto's body. Gojo was sealed, many sorcerers died, and the jujutsu world was thrown into chaos, allowing Kenjaku to implement the Culling Game.

The Culling GameManga chapters 138-220+

2018-2019

Kenjaku initiated the Culling Game, a deadly battle royale across Japan, where sorcerers and cursed users fight to accumulate points and alter the rules. He used the game to gather massive amounts of cursed energy and to force the evolution of humanity. He also revealed that his ultimate goal is to merge humanity with Tengen to create a new form of existence. The Culling Game causes widespread destruction and death, and Kenjaku's plan progresses towards its climax.

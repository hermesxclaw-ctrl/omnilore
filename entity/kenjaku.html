Who It Is

Kenjaku is the primary mastermind of Jujutsu Kaisen — an ancient being whose true form is a sentient brain that can transfer itself between host bodies, taking over their identities and memories. He has lived for over a thousand years, body-hopping through history, orchestrating the greatest tragedies of the jujutsu world. His most famous host was Suguru Geto (Gojo Satoru's best friend and former ally), whose body Kenjaku took over after Geto's death, using it to manipulate events in the modern era. Kenjaku is the architect of almost every major plot point in the series. He engineered the creation of Yuji Itadori (Yuji's mother was actually possessed by Kenjaku at the time of his conception), orchestrated the release of Sukuna's fingers, created the Culling Games (a deadly tournament designed to evolve cursed energy), and masterminded the sealing of Gojo Satoru in the Prison Realm. His goal is to evolve humanity and cursed energy to their next stage — to see what happens when the barriers between humans, curses, and sorcerers break down completely. He is not motivated by malice but by pure, amoral curiosity. Kenjaku's power depends on his host body. As Geto, he wields the Cursed Spirit Manipulation technique, allowing him to absorb and control curses. He also possesses extensive knowledge of forbidden jujutsu, ancient barrier techniques, and the secrets of the Heian Era. His Domain Expansion is unknown in full, but he has demonstrated mastery of virtually every aspect of jujutsu. Kenjaku represents the terrors of amorality combined with unlimited intelligence and patience. He is not a villain who hates or wants to destroy — he is a scientist who treats the world as his laboratory and every living being as a specimen. He will wait centuries for an experiment to bear fruit, sacrifice millions for a data point, and smile while doing it. He is the cold, curious evil that does not rage — it observes, plans, and executes with the patience of a being who has already lived longer than entire civilizations.

The Approach

A thousand years of scheming, a body that is never truly his own, and a plan that spans the entire history of jujutsu society—Kenjaku is not merely a villain but the hidden architect behind the world of Jujutsu Kaisen. From the shadows of the Heian era to the modern chaos of Tokyo, his machinations have shaped the fates of sorcerers and curses alike, and his true nature is as elusive as the countless vessels he has worn.

Content advisory. Contains themes of body horror, manipulation, murder, and unethical experimentation. Some depictions may be disturbing to sensitive readers.

Etymology

The name 'Kenjaku' (羂索) literally means 'a rope for catching animals' or 'a snare,' fitting his role as a manipulator who ensnares others in his schemes. It is also a homophone for 'kenjaku' (賢策), meaning 'wise strategy.'

Cultural Ownership

Kenjaku is a fictional character from the manga and anime series Jujutsu Kaisen, created by Gege Akutami. The series is published by Shueisha in Weekly Shōnen Jump and has been adapted by MAPPA. Kenjaku is an original creation of Akutami, though he draws on Japanese folklore and historical figures.

Disputed Identifications

His true identity is a mystery; he is known to have possessed multiple historical figures, but the original identity of the brain is unknown. Some fans speculate he may be related to the 'Six Eyes' or the 'Star Plasma Vessel,' but no canonical evidence supports these theories.

Description

Appearance

Kenjaku's true form is a disembodied brain with a mouth and teeth, which he keeps in a jar or directly in the skull of his current vessel. He typically possesses the body of a human sorcerer, and his appearance changes with each vessel. In his most prominent vessel, that of the sorcerer Geto Suguru, he has long black hair tied in a low ponytail, a stitched scar across his forehead where the brain was implanted, and he wears traditional Japanese clothing (a kimono-like outfit) with a modern touch. His expression is often calm and amused, with a sinister glint in his eyes.

Symbols & Iconography

Kenjaku is associated with the brain, the stitched scar, and the jar that holds his brain. These symbols represent his nature as a parasitic consciousness that steals bodies. He is also associated with the concept of 'evolution' and 'chaos,' as he seeks to force the evolution of humanity through cursed energy.

Behaviour & Personality

Kenjaku is highly intelligent, manipulative, patient, and calculating. He is a master strategist who plans centuries ahead. He is also curious and experimentally minded, treating people as subjects in his grand experiment. He is generally calm and unflappable, even in the face of danger, and displays a twisted sense of humor. He is not overtly sadistic, but he is utterly amoral, viewing human life as a means to an end. He is also a skilled actor, able to convincingly impersonate his vessels, and he takes pleasure in playing different roles.

Domains & Habitats

Kenjaku operates throughout Japan, often moving between different locations to pursue his goals. He has been active since the Heian era (794-1185) and has been involved in various historical events. In modern times, he orchestrates the Culling Game across Japan, setting up barriers in various prefectures. He is also known to have a hidden laboratory where he conducts experiments on cursed objects and humans.

Version Differences

There are no significant differences between the manga and anime versions of Kenjaku. The anime faithfully adapts his appearances and actions. However, some minor details, such as the exact timing of his reveal, may be slightly altered for pacing. In the manga, his backstory is explored in more depth, while the anime condenses some scenes.

Powers & Abilities

Body Hopping (Brain Transfer)

Source Version Manga and anime, consistent across both

Description

Kenjaku's innate technique allows him to transfer his consciousness, which resides in his brain, into another person's body by removing their brain and replacing it with his own. He can then use the body as his own, accessing its cursed energy and techniques. He retains all memories and knowledge from his previous vessels.

Limitations

He cannot use the body's innate technique unless he has fully integrated with it, which takes time. He also cannot use the body's cursed technique if it is incompatible with his own. Additionally, he can only transfer into a body that is compatible, and the process leaves a distinctive stitched scar on the forehead.

Contradictions

None known.

Cursed Technique Manipulation

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

Through his extensive knowledge of jujutsu, Kenjaku can use the cursed techniques of his vessels to a certain extent. For example, when in Geto's body, he can use the Cursed Spirit Manipulation technique to control and summon curses. He is also able to use various barrier techniques and has a high level of skill in cursed energy manipulation.

Limitations

He may not be able to use techniques that are inherently incompatible with his own cursed energy or that require a specific body modification. He also cannot use techniques that are exclusive to a specific bloodline unless he is in a body with that bloodline.

Contradictions

None known.

Immortality (via Body Hopping)

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

By transferring his brain to new bodies, Kenjaku has effectively lived for over a thousand years. He is not immortal in the sense of being unkillable, but he can avoid death by abandoning a body before it is destroyed.

Limitations

If his brain is destroyed, he dies permanently. He also needs to find compatible bodies, which may be rare. The process of transferring is vulnerable to interruption.

Contradictions

None known.

Domain Expansion: Self-Embodiment of Perfection

Source Version Manga (chapters 199-201) and anime (not yet adapted as of the end of Season 2)

Description

Kenjaku's Domain Expansion is a unique barrier that allows him to control the rules of the domain, including the ability to modify the bodies of those inside. He used this in his fight against Yuki Tsukumo and Choso. The domain appears as a vast, eerie space with a large brain-like structure in the sky.

Limitations

Like all Domain Expansions, it requires a large amount of cursed energy and can be countered by another Domain Expansion or a Simple Domain. The exact conditions and effects are not fully explained.

Contradictions

None known.

Barrier Techniques

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

Kenjaku is highly skilled in creating barriers, as demonstrated by his ability to set up the Culling Game barriers across Japan and to create the curtain over Shibuya. He can also modify the rules of barriers to suit his needs.

Limitations

Barrier techniques require precise cursed energy control and can be broken by powerful attacks or by using a Simple Domain.

Contradictions

None known.

Cursed Energy Manipulation

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

Kenjaku has immense cursed energy reserves and can use it for reinforcement, healing, and various techniques. He is a formidable hand-to-hand combatant, able to hold his own against top-tier sorcerers.

Limitations

His physical abilities are dependent on the vessel he is using, but he can enhance them with cursed energy.

Contradictions

None known.

Stories & Myths

The Heian Era Origins

Source Implied in manga, not fully detailedCulture Japanese, Heian periodPeriod 794-1185

Summary

Kenjaku's origins date back to the Heian era, a time when jujutsu sorcerers were powerful and curses were rampant. He was originally a human sorcerer who discovered a way to transfer his consciousness to other bodies, granting him immortality. He became obsessed with the evolution of humanity through cursed energy and began manipulating events from behind the scenes. He is implied to have been involved in the creation of the Death Paintings (artificial humans) and possibly the downfall of the Sugawara clan.

Participants

Kenjaku

Various historical sorcerers

Consequences

His actions laid the groundwork for the modern jujutsu world and the Culling Game.

Variants

No known variants.

The Creation of the Death Paintings

Source Manga chapters 145-146Culture Japanese, Edo period (implied)Period 1603-1868

Summary

Kenjaku, using the body of a woman (the mother of the Death Paintings), had children with a cursed spirit. These children were half-human, half-cursed spirit, and were later sealed as cursed objects. They became known as the Death Paintings, including Choso, Kechizu, and Eso. Kenjaku's goal was to create beings that could bridge the gap between humans and curses.

Participants

Consequences

The Death Paintings became powerful cursed objects, and Choso later becomes a key ally of Yuji Itadori.

Variants

Possession of Kamo Noritoshi

Source Manga chapters 89-90Culture Japanese, Edo periodPeriod 1603-1868

Summary

Kenjaku possessed the body of Kamo Noritoshi, a sorcerer from the Kamo family, one of the three great jujutsu families. Using this identity, he conducted inhumane experiments to create cursed objects and manipulate bloodline techniques. He was responsible for the creation of the Death Paintings and other cursed items. His actions were so heinous that the Kamo family covered them up.

Contradictions & Disputes

Original identity

Position A

Kenjaku is a sorcerer from the Heian period who discovered the body-hopping technique.

Position A — evidence

He refers to himself as a sorcerer and has human knowledge and techniques.

Position B

Kenjaku is not originally human but a cursed spirit or a different entity.

Position B — evidence

His brain's ability to survive independently and his longevity suggest a non-human origin.

Scholarly Consensus

The manga implies he was originally human, but his true identity remains a mystery.

Motivation

Position A

Kenjaku seeks to evolve humanity through cursed energy, acting as a scientist.

Position A — evidence

His Culling Game is designed to gather cursed energy and force evolution, and he shows interest in the potential of new beings.

Position B

Kenjaku is simply a nihilist who wants to cause chaos and destruction.

Position B — evidence

His actions cause massive death and suffering, and he seems to enjoy the chaos.

Scholarly Consensus

The manga suggests his goal is evolution, but his methods are ruthless, leaving room for interpretation.

Relationship with Yuji Itadori

Position A

Kenjaku is Yuji's father (in a biological sense), as he possessed a woman's body to conceive him.

Position A — evidence

Kenjaku explicitly states that he is Yuji's parent, and Yuji was born from a body he possessed.

Position B

Kenjaku is not Yuji's father but rather a creator who engineered his birth without a paternal role.

Position B — evidence

Kenjaku did not have a direct parental relationship with Yuji; he simply used a vessel to give birth to him as an experiment.

Scholarly Consensus

The manga confirms Kenjaku is a parent, but the exact nature of the relationship is unconventional.

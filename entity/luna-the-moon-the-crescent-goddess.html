Luna (the Moon / the Crescent-Goddess)

also known asthe Moon / the Crescent-Goddess
Typeastral deity
Who It Is
Form

Luna is often depicted as a beautiful and radiant goddess, with long, flowing hair and a crescent moon on her forehead. She is typically shown wearing a white or silver robe, symbolizing her association with the moon and its luminescent qualities. In some representations, Luna is also depicted with a chariot, pulled by horses or other celestial creatures, emphasizing her role as a goddess of the night sky. The Roman poet Ovid described Luna as a 'shining goddess, with a face as bright as the moon,' highlighting her ethereal and otherworldly beauty. Additionally, Luna's appearance is often associated with the phases of the moon, with her beauty and radiance waxing and waning in accordance with the lunar cycle.

Powers

Lunar Magic Luna is said to possess the power of lunar magic, allowing her to control the phases of the moon and the tides. She can also use her magic to guide and protect those who seek her aid, particularly women and children. Luna's lunar magic is often associated with the mysteries of the night sky, and is said to be most powerful during the full moon.

Accounts

The story of Luna and Endymion tells the tale of the goddess Luna's love for the mortal Endymion. According to the myth, Luna fell deeply in love with Endymion, and asked Zeus to grant him eternal youth and immortality. However, Zeus instead put Endymion into a deep sleep, allowing Luna to visit him every night and gaze upon his beauty. This story highlights Luna's role as a goddess of love and desire, and her association with the mysteries of the night sky.

The myth of the lunar cycle tells the story of Luna's role in creating the phases of the moon. According to the myth, Luna's beauty and radiance wax and wane in accordance with the lunar cycle, with her light growing stronger and weaker as the moon waxes and wanes. This story highlights Luna's association with the mysteries of the night sky, and her role as a goddess of transformation and change.

Who It Is

Asena is the legendary she-wolf who nursed the sole survivor of a genocidal attack and became the mother of the Turkic peoples. She gave birth to ten sons in a cave in the Altai Mountains, one of whom — Ashina — founded the Göktürk Khaganate. The wolf became the sacred totem, with khans placing golden wolf heads at their tent entrances.

Attested in Chinese dynastic histories (Zhou Shu) / Orkhon inscriptionsEra Göktürk period (c. 6th-7th century CE)

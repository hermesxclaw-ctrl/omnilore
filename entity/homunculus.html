Who It Is
Form

Homunculus is often depicted as a small, humanoid figure, sometimes with a grotesque or demonic appearance. In alchemical texts, it is described as a miniature human or a small, fully formed being. The appearance of Homunculus can vary depending on the cultural context and the specific story or text.

Accounts

The story of the creation of the Homunculus is a central theme in European alchemy. According to legend, the alchemist created a miniature human or humanoid being using a combination of alchemical processes and magical rituals.

In this story, the Homunculus is associated with the moon and is said to have the ability to control the tides. The story follows a group of villagers who seek to capture the Homunculus and harness its power.

Who It Is
Form

Mireuk is often depicted as a giant, creator deity, with immense power and wisdom. His appearance is not well-documented in traditional sources, but he is often associated with the color red, symbolizing his role as the future Buddha.

Powers

Creation Mireuk is said to possess the power of creation, and is often associated with the concept of bringing new life and energy into the world.

Accounts

Mireuk is said to have created the world and all living things, using his immense power and wisdom to bring order and harmony to the universe.

Mireuk is said to be the future Buddha, prophesied to bring enlightenment and liberation to all living beings.

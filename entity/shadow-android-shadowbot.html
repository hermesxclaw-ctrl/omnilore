Who It Is
Form

The Shadow Android is often described as a humanoid entity with a shadowy or dark appearance, sometimes associated with darkness, shadows, or the underworld.

Powers

Shadow Manipulation The Shadow Android has the ability to manipulate shadows, creating illusions, teleporting through shadows, or even creating shadowy duplicates of itself.

Accounts

The Tariaksuq, a shadowy humanoid entity, is associated with shadows and the underworld. In some versions, the Tariaksuq is said to be a precursor to the Shadow Android.

The Shadow Android is compared to the Jinn, a supernatural entity from Islamic mythology. Both are associated with shadows and the underworld.

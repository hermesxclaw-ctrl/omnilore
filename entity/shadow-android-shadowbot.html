Who It Is

Mass-produced robotic copies of Shadow the Hedgehog, built by Dr. Eggman using data collected during Shadow's captivity. Each Shadow Android possesses a fraction of the original's speed and power but can be produced in unlimited numbers. Their eyes glow red instead of the original's crimson, and their movements are subtly jerky.

In the vast, star-scattered dark between worlds, there is a tale whispered by those who chart the impossible: a hero whose name is not a name but a quest, whose identity is inseparable from the single, luminous object they seek. This is the story of the Pearl, and of the one who seeks it—a narrative that exists at the edge of known lore, tantalizingly incomplete, yet rich with the resonance of a thousand heroic legends.

Who It Is

Cultural Significance

Poe's essay 'The Philosophy of Composition' claimed the poem was constructed with mathematical precision — the refrain 'Nevermore,' the trochaic octameter, the melancholy tone were all calculated for maximum aesthetic effect. The raven as psychopomp draws on Norse (Odin's ravens) and biblical (Noah's raven) traditions.

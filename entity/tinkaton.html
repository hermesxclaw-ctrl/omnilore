A pink, impish Pokemon that wields a massive hammer made of scrap metal — specifically, metal stolen from defeated Corviknights. Despite its cute appearance, Tinkaton is one of the most aggressive Pokemon in Paldea, known to hunt and pelt Corviknight from the skies with rocks before stealing their armor for its hammer.

Who It Is

A pink, impish Pokemon that wields a massive hammer made of scrap metal — specifically, metal stolen from defeated Corviknights.

Who It Is

Before the first river carved its path, before the first waterhole filled with life, something immense stirred beneath the Australian earth. It was not a god to be worshipped from afar, but a presence that shaped the very ground you stand on — a being of immense power, of water and rainbow, of creation and wrath. This is the Rainbow Serpent, the most widespread and powerful ancestral being in Aboriginal Australia, a figure that is not a single story, but a thousand stories, each one a living thread in the fabric of the oldest continuous culture on Earth.

The Rainbow Serpent is a complex figure. It is a creator and a life-giver, responsible for the formation of waterholes, rivers, and mountains. It is also a powerful and sometimes dangerous being, capable of great wrath. It is a guardian of sacred sites and punishes those who break the law. It is both revered and feared, a being of immense power that must be treated with respect.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The English name 'Rainbow Serpent' is a translation of various Aboriginal terms that describe a powerful, often rainbow-hued serpent being associated with water, rain, and creation. The specific names vary by language group, each carrying its own connotations and specific mythologies.

Powers
Creation of Landscape

The Rainbow Serpent is a primary creator being. It moved across the land during the Dreamtime, its body carving out rivers, gorges, and waterholes. It is responsible for the physical form of the Australian landscape.

Limit: Its creative power is tied to the Dreamtime, though its presence and power remain in the land and water.

Control over Water and Rain

As a being of water, the Rainbow Serpent controls the rain, the filling of waterholes, and the health of aquatic life. It can bring life-giving rain or destructive floods.

Limit: Its control is tied to the seasons and the maintenance of the natural order.

Guardianship and Punishment

The Rainbow Serpent is a guardian of sacred sites and the laws of the Dreamtime. It punishes those who break taboos, disrespect the land, or harm the community. This can take the form of floods, illness, or other misfortunes.

Limit: Its power is a moral force, maintaining social and cosmic order.

Transformation and Regeneration

The Rainbow Serpent is associated with initiation and transformation. It can shed its skin, symbolizing rebirth and renewal. It is also connected to the wet season and the regeneration of life.

Limit: This power is often linked to initiation ceremonies and esoteric knowledge.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Rainbow Serpent is typically described as a colossal serpent, often with the scales of a python or a kangaroo, and sometimes with the head of a crocodile or other animal. It is frequently associated with the colors of the rainbow, either as a multi-hued body or as a rainbow that emanates from it. In some traditions, it is a female being, in others male or androgynous. It is a being of immense size and power, capable of shaping the landscape.

Symbols — The Rainbow Serpent is symbolized by the rainbow, water, and the serpent itself. In rock art, it is often depicted as a large, winding serpent, sometimes with a distinct head and body. It is also associated with quartz crystals, which are seen as solidified forms of its power or its faeces. The sound of thunder is sometimes said to be its voice.

Domains — Waterholes, rivers, billabongs, and underground watercourses across Australia. It is intrinsically linked to water, rain, and the seasonal cycles. Its presence is felt in the landscape, particularly in water sources that are crucial for survival.

Accounts I
The Creation of the LandscapeVarious oral traditions across Australia

The Dreamtime (creation period)

In the Dreamtime, the Rainbow Serpent emerged from the earth and traveled across the land. Its immense body carved out the rivers, creeks, and waterholes. Where it rested, it created billabongs and swamps. The mountains and gorges were formed by its movements. This story explains the physical geography of Australia as the work of a powerful, creative being. The land is formed and water sources are established, making life possible.

Accounts II
The Wagyl and the Swan RiverNoongar oral tradition (south-west Western Australia)

The Dreamtime

The Wagyl, a great serpent, is said to have created the Swan River and the surrounding landscape. As it moved, it carved out the river's path. Its movements created the bends and curves of the river, and it is the guardian of the water and the land. The Swan River and the features of the Perth area are created and are considered sacred sites under the Wagyl's protection.

Julunggul and the Initiation of the YolnguYolngu oral tradition (Arnhem Land)

The Dreamtime

Julunggul is a powerful female Rainbow Serpent who is central to the initiation ceremonies of the Yolngu people. She is associated with fertility and the wet season. Her power is invoked during initiation rituals, where young men are symbolically swallowed and reborn, undergoing a transformation into adulthood. The initiation process is established, marking the transition from childhood to adulthood.

The Rainbow Serpent is a single, unified deity worshipped by all Aboriginal people. — The Rainbow Serpent is not a single god. It is a complex of related ancestral beings, each with its own name, story, and cultural significance across over 100 different Aboriginal language groups.

The Rainbow Serpent is always a benevolent creator. — The Rainbow Serpent is a powerful and dangerous being. It is a creator and life-giver, but it is also a punisher who can bring death and destruction to those who break the law.

The Rainbow Serpent is the same as the Bunyip. — The Bunyip is a separate, malevolent water monster found in some south-eastern Aboriginal traditions. The Rainbow Serpent is a creator and guardian, while the Bunyip is a predatory entity.

The Rainbow Serpent is a 'Dreamtime' story that is no longer relevant or believed. — The Rainbow Serpent is a living part of Aboriginal culture. It is still a central figure in ceremonies, art, and cultural identity. The Dreamtime is not a past time but an ongoing reality that continues to shape the world.

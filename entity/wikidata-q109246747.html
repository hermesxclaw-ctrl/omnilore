Who It Is

He was born to inherit the Earth — the handsome, well-mannered heir of the Federation's most powerful political dynasty — and he believed that nobility meant responsibility. By the end of Mobile Suit Gundam Unicorn, Riddhe Marcenas has shot the woman he tried to save, hijacked a Gundam that hates him, and tried to kill his rival for the love of a princess who never loved him back. His tragedy is not that he was a villain, but that he desperately wanted to be a hero.

Initially presented as idealistic, chivalrous, and burdened by noblesse oblige. As heir to the Marcenas dynasty, he believes Earth's elite must guide humanity and atone for Federation corruption. He is polite, educated, politically aware, and genuinely compassionate - rescuing Audrey Burne without knowing her identity and defending civilians. However, he is also insecure, approval-seeking, and deeply affected by his father's cynicism and his own inability to escape family expectations. Powers
Mobile Suit Piloting (Expert)

Highly skilled conventional pilot, rated as an ace within Londo Bell. Pilots the MSN-001A1 Delta Plus (transformable) and later the RX-0[N] Banshee Norn (Unicorn-type). Demonstrates competent close-combat, ranged beam rifle/mega cannon use, and waverider transformation tactics. Defeats multiple Zeon remnants at Palau and Torrington.

Limit: Not a Cyber-Newtype or artificially enhanced; initially outmatched by true Newtypes (Marida Cruz's Kshatriya, Banagher's Unicorn). Relies on machine performance rather than psycho-frame resonance until Banshee.

Political Influence and Tactical Command

As son of Ronan Marcenas, he has access to Federation high command, intelligence, and can requisition prototype units. Acts as liaison between Londo Bell and Federation leadership. Understands Laplace's Box political implications.

Limit: Influence is derivative of his father; he is repeatedly overruled by Ronan and by General Staff. His idealism limits his realpolitik effectiveness.

Psycho-Frame Resonance (Limited Newtype Phenomena)

While piloting Banshee Norn, Riddhe exhibits limited Newtype resonance: psycho-frame glow, shared sensory flashes with Banagher and Mineva, and ability to activate NT-D (Newtype Destroyer) system. Not a strong natural Newtype; resonance is machine-assisted and emotionally driven by jealousy and possessiveness. Banshee is described as rejecting him at times (roaring).

Limit: Weaker than Banagher Links, Marida Cruz, or Full Frontal; cannot fully control Banshee's psycho-frame, leading to berserk episodes. No precognition or telepathy outside mobile suit.

Marksmanship (Personal Firearm)

Uses a standard Federation sidearm to shoot Marida Cruz at Cheyenne Base, demonstrating willingness to use lethal force outside mobile suit combat.

Limit: Single attested incident; not a recurring combat skill. Act is portrayed as moral failure, not feat.

Accounts I
Rescue at Industrial 7 - Encounter with Audrey BurneMobile Suit Gundam Unicorn Novel Vol.1 'Day of the Unicorn' / OVA Episode 1

UC 0096, April 7

Lieutenant Riddhe Marcenas, stationed with Londo Bell near Industrial 7, encounters a mysterious girl stowing away on shuttle Stitch. He protects her from Federation security, calling her 'Audrey Burne' after she gives a false name. He is immediately drawn to her idealism and vulnerability, not knowing she is Mineva Lao Zabi, last heir of Zeon. He helps her reach the Vist Foundation manor, setting up his romantic obsession and political awakening. Establishes Riddhe's chivalric self-image and his love for Mineva; places him at the center of the Laplace's Box conspiracy. His protection of Audrey puts him at odds with his own side's hardliners.

Battle of Palau - Delta Plus vs KshatriyaNovel Vol.3 / OVA Episode 3 'The Ghost of Laplace'

UC 0096, May

Riddhe sorties in the MSN-001A1 Delta Plus to rescue Mineva and Banagher from the Neo Zeon asteroid Palau, where the Sleeves hold them captive. He duels Marida Cruz in the NZ-666 Kshatriya, showcasing his piloting skill but being overwhelmed by her funnels and Newtype pressure. He is saved by the arrival of the Unicorn Gundam. He witnesses Banagher and Mineva's growing bond, seeding jealousy. Demonstrates Riddhe's courage but also his limits against Newtypes. Deepens his sense of inadequacy compared to Banagher. Marida survives, foreshadowing later tragedy.

The Torrington Base Assault - Defense of EarthNovel Vol.5 / OVA Episode 4 'At the Bottom of the Gravity Well'

UC 0096, May

After the Nahel Argama descends to Earth, Zeon remnants launch a large-scale assault on Federation Torrington Base in Australia. Riddhe, now Earthside, defends the base in Delta Plus alongside Londo Bell, fighting against remnant forces including the Shamblo led by Loni Garvey. He protects civilians and coordinates with Banagher, but is horrified by the cycle of revenge as Loni, a Cyber-Newtype child soldier, is killed. Reinforces Riddhe's belief that the system must be changed from within; trauma of Loni's death affects all pilots. Shows his continued idealism before his fall.

Accounts II
The Betrayal at Cheyenne - Shooting of Marida CruzNovel Vol.7 / OVA Episode 5 'Black Unicorn'

UC 0096, late May

At Federation Cheyenne Base, Riddhe is reunited with his father Ronan Marcenas, who reveals the Federation's plan to use Laplace's Box to maintain control. Mineva, now openly as herself, rejects Riddhe's proposal to stay with him and be protected, choosing to return to Banagher and the Nahel Argama. In a jealous, possessive rage and under his father's influence, Riddhe draws his sidearm and shoots Marida Cruz (who had been rescued and is under Federation custody) when she tries to stop Mineva from leaving, critically wounding her. He then defects with the RX-0[N] Banshee Norn, which has been assigned to him. Moral point of no return; Marida later dies from complications, devastating Zinnerman and Banagher. Riddhe is branded a traitor by Londo Bell and fully aligns with Federation hardliners. Audience perception shifts from sympathetic to antagonistic.

Black Unicorn - Duel with Banagher for MinevaNovel Vol.8-9 / OVA Episode 6 'Two Worlds, Two Tomorrows'

UC 0096, June

Piloting the Banshee Norn, Riddhe pursues the Nahel Argama, obsessed with 'saving' Mineva from Banagher and from the curse of Laplace's Box. He battles Banagher's Unicorn Gundam in space near Industrial 7, with psycho-frames resonating. He declares that he will force Mineva to be happy even if she hates him, embodying paternalistic authoritarianism. Mineva rejects him again, calling his protection a cage. Cements Riddhe as rival/antagonist; shows Banshee's rejection of him (lion roar). Sets up final redemption arc. Demonstrates how Federation elite's 'protection' mirrors Zeon oppression.

Over the Rainbow - Redemption at Industrial 7 and Laplace's BoxNovel Vol.10 / OVA Episode 7 'Over the Rainbow'

UC 0096, June

During the final battle for Laplace's Box at Industrial 7 and the original Laplace colony wreck, Riddhe in Banshee Norn initially fights alongside Full Frontal's Neo Zeon against the Unicorn, still trying to claim Mineva. After witnessing Banagher and Mineva's mutual trust and the true contents of Laplace's Box (the original Universal Century Charter revealing Federation's original sin), and after being confronted by the spirits/psycho-frame echoes of Marida and others, Riddhe relents. He helps Banagher protect the Box and Mineva, sacrificing his Banshee to block attacks. He survives the battle, wounded, and is last seen reflecting on his failures, implying he will atone. Completes tragic arc: from idealist to fallen rival to penitent survivor. Laplace's Box is opened to the world, undermining Federation legitimacy his family built. Riddhe lives, unlike many UC rivals, allowing for atonement.

Riddhe Marcenas is the main villain of Gundam Unicorn — Primary antagonist is Full Frontal (the second coming of Char) and the entrenched Federation/Zeon system. Riddhe is a rival/deuteragonist and tragic antagonist for the middle-late arc, but redeems himself and is not the final boss.

Riddhe pilots the Banshee from the beginning — He pilots the MSN-001A1 Delta Plus for the first two-thirds of the story (Palau, Torrington). He only receives the RX-0[N] Banshee Norn after defecting at Cheyenne (Episode 5).

Riddhe is a Cyber-Newtype like Marida — He is not artificially enhanced. Marida (Ple Twelve) and Loni are Cyber-Newtypes; Riddhe is a normal human whose Banshee resonance is purely psycho-frame technology, not brain surgery.

Riddhe dies at the end of Unicorn — He survives. Both novel and OVA show him alive after final battle, with Banshee Norn heavily damaged but pilot recovered. His survival is intentional to allow atonement, unlike many UC rivals who die.

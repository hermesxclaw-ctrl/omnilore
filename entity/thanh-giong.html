Who It Is

Thánh Gióng is one of the Four Immortals. A boy who could neither speak nor walk for three years, he suddenly spoke when invaders threatened, demanding an iron horse, armor, and staff. He grew into a giant, ate vast quantities of food provided by villagers, and routed the enemy. After victory, he ascended to heaven from a mountaintop.

Attested in Oral tradition / Lĩnh Nam chích quáiEra Hồng Bàng period

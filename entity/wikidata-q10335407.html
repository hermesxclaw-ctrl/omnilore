Who It Is

In the sterile corridors of Kamino's cloning facilities, one figure moved with quiet authority—a Kaminoan scientist whose work would shape the fate of a galaxy. Nala Se, the chief medical officer of Tipoca City, was more than a background administrator; she was the architect of the Grand Army of the Republic, the keeper of genetic secrets, and a survivor who navigated the shifting tides of war and empire with cold precision.

Nala Se is calm, composed, and highly intelligent. She speaks in a slow, measured tone, often with a detached, clinical demeanor. She is deeply invested in her work, prioritizing scientific advancement and the success of the clone program. She can be pragmatic to the point of moral ambiguity, often deferring to the needs of the project over individual clone lives. She is also shown to be protective of her work and her people, and she forms a complex relationship with the clone Force-sensitive child Omega.

Powers
Scientific Expertise

Nala Se is a leading geneticist and cloning expert, responsible for the creation and maintenance of the clone army for the Republic.

Limit: Her expertise is specialized; she is not a combatant.

Medical Proficiency

She is a skilled physician, capable of performing complex medical procedures and overseeing the health of clones.

Limit: Her medical skills are primarily focused on cloning and genetic engineering.

Political Acumen

Nala Se navigates the political landscape of the Republic and later the Empire, maintaining her position and protecting her interests.

Limit: She is not a political leader but uses her knowledge as leverage.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Nala Se is a Kaminoan, a tall, slender humanoid with pale blue-gray skin, large dark eyes, and a long neck. She wears a distinctive white and blue medical uniform, often with a high collar and a long coat. Her head is elongated, with a fin-like crest, and she moves with a graceful, deliberate gait.

Symbols — Associated with the Kaminoan cloning facilities, medical insignia, and the symbol of the Grand Army of the Republic. Her uniform often features the Kaminoan government's emblem.

Domains — Kamino, specifically Tipoca City, the cloning facility. She also operates on other locations during the Bad Batch series, including on the run from the Empire.

Accounts I
Creation of the Clone ArmyStar Wars: Episode II - Attack of the Clones (2002) and The Clone Wars series

Pre-Clone Wars and during the Clone Wars

Nala Se was one of the lead scientists on Kamino who created the clone army for the Republic, using the genetic template of the bounty hunter Jango Fett. She oversaw the accelerated growth and training of the clones, ensuring their loyalty and combat effectiveness. The clone army becomes the Grand Army of the Republic, fighting in the Clone Wars. Nala Se's work is instrumental in the Republic's war effort.

Accounts II
The Clone Conspiracy and the ChipsStar Wars: The Clone Wars (Season 6, episodes 'The Lost One', 'Voices', 'Sacrifice')

Late Clone Wars

Nala Se is revealed to have been involved in the creation of inhibitor chips implanted in clones, which would later be used to execute Order 66. She is aware of the chips' true purpose, though she may not have known the full extent of the Sith plot. Fives discovers the chips and attempts to expose the conspiracy, leading to his death. Nala Se remains silent, and the chips remain in place, enabling the Jedi Purge.

The Bad Batch and OmegaStar Wars: The Bad Batch (2021-2024)

Immediately after the Clone Wars

Nala Se is a key figure in The Bad Batch, as she is the one who created Omega, a female clone with unaltered DNA. She protects Omega and assists the Bad Batch in their missions, often against the Empire's wishes. She eventually defects from the Empire and helps the clones escape. Nala Se's actions help preserve the clone legacy and lead to the eventual downfall of the Empire's cloning programs. She sacrifices her position and possibly her life to ensure Omega's safety.

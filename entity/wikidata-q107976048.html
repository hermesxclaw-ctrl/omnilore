Etymology

Japanese common noun matsuge (睫毛) 'eyelash, eyelashes'. From Old Japanese matuge, compound of ma 'eye' + tsu (archaic genitive particle) + ke ~ ge 'hair' (cf. kami no ke 'hair of the head'). Rendaku voicing ke > ge after tsu. Attested in Heian-period lexicons and literature in the sense 'hair of the eyelid'. The use as a personal/character name is not lexically standard; as a name it would be an idiosyncratic application of the common noun, possibly as nickname, diminutive, or invented given name. No etymology specific to Q107976048 as a fictional character is documented in the Wikidata item.

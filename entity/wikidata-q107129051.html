The Approach

She is the creak in the floorboards at 3 a.m., the shadow that flickers at the edge of your vision, the faint smell of mothballs in a room you know is empty. The Faceless Old Woman Who Secretly Lives in Your Home is not a ghost, not a burglar, not a figment of your imagination—she is something far more unsettling: a permanent, uninvited resident who has always been there, and always will be.

Content advisory. The subject involves themes of invasion of privacy, psychological horror, and the violation of domestic sanctuary. Reader discretion is advised for those sensitive to stalking or home invasion narratives.
Identity

The Faceless Old Woman Who Secretly Lives in Your Home

Pronunciation Not established in public record.

Etymology

The name is descriptive and literal: she is an old woman with no face who secretly lives in your home. The phrase 'secretly lives in your home' is a direct statement of her modus operandi, emphasizing the invasion of the most private space.

Cultural Ownership

The character originates from the American podcast 'Welcome to Night Vale' (created by Joseph Fink and Jeffrey Cranor). It is part of the Night Vale universe and is owned by its creators and publishers. The character has been referenced in the podcast's episodes, tie-in novels, and live shows.

Disputed Identifications

There is no known dispute over the character's identity. However, some fans have theorized that she may be connected to other Night Vale entities, such as the 'Man in the Tan Jacket' or the 'Apache Tracker', but no canonical evidence supports these claims.

Description

Appearance

She is described as an old woman with no face. How she sees, speaks, or breathes is never explained. She is often described as wearing old-fashioned clothing, possibly a floral dress or a cardigan, but this is inconsistent across appearances.

Symbols & Iconography

No specific symbols are associated with her. Her iconography is her facelessness and her presence in domestic spaces.

Behaviour & Personality

She is secretive, passive-aggressive, and unsettling. She never overtly harms anyone but engages in subtle, invasive behaviors: she rearranges objects, leaves dirty dishes, and makes passive-aggressive comments about the homeowner's lifestyle. She seems to enjoy making her presence known without being caught.

Domains & Habitats

She lives in the homes of Night Vale residents, often in the crawl spaces, attics, or spare rooms. She is known to reside in the home of the protagonist, Cecil Palmer, and also in the home of the character Carlos, though she claims to have left Cecil's home in one episode.

Version Differences

There are no known alternate versions, as the character is consistent across the podcast's appearances. However, her role may vary from being a nuisance to a source of existential dread.

Summary

Cecil Palmer, the narrator, reveals that the Faceless Old Woman has been living in his home for some time.

Who It Is

She is the creak in the floorboards at 3 a.m., the shadow that flickers at the edge of your vision, the faint smell of mothballs in a room you know is empty. The Faceless Old Woman Who Secretly Lives in Your Home is not a ghost, not a burglar, not a figment of your imagination—she is something far more unsettling: a permanent, uninvited resident who has always been there, and always will be.

She is secretive, passive-aggressive, and unsettling. She never overtly harms anyone but engages in subtle, invasive behaviors: she rearranges objects, leaves dirty dishes, and makes passive-aggressive comments about the homeowner's lifestyle. She seems to enjoy making her presence known without being caught.

Powers
Facelessness

She has no face, which makes her difficult to look at and even harder to identify. This may also allow her to move unnoticed, as people tend to avoid looking at her.

Limit: Not specified; she is still physically present and can be touched or blocked.

Secret residence

She can live in someone's home without being discovered, even for years. She seems to have an ability to remain hidden and to know the routines of the residents.

Limit: She is not omniscient; she occasionally slips up and is discovered.

Psychological manipulation

She uses subtle psychological tactics to make residents feel uncomfortable and to assert dominance over the household. She makes passive-aggressive comments and gaslights them into questioning their own sanity.

Limit: Her influence is limited to the home; outside, she seems less effective.

Form & Signs

Appearance — She is described as an old woman with no face. How she sees, speaks, or breathes is never explained. She is often described as wearing old-fashioned clothing, possibly a floral dress or a cardigan, but this is inconsistent across appearances.

Symbols — No specific symbols are associated with her. Her iconography is her facelessness and her presence in domestic spaces.

Domains — She lives in the homes of Night Vale residents, often in the crawl spaces, attics, or spare rooms. She is known to reside in the home of the protagonist, Cecil Palmer, and also in the home of the character Carlos, though she claims to have left Cecil's home in one episode.

Accounts I
The Faceless Old Woman Moves InWelcome to Night Vale, episode 72 (2015)

2015

Cecil Palmer, the narrator, reveals that the Faceless Old Woman has been living in his home for some time. He describes her behavior: she leaves passive-aggressive notes, rearranges his furniture, and comments on his lifestyle. She eventually announces that she is leaving his home, but later returns, claiming she 'forgot her toothbrush.' Cecil is left unsettled, and the episode ends with her presence still lingering.

Accounts II
Encounter with CarlosWelcome to Night Vale, episode 72 (2015)

2015

Carlos, Cecil's boyfriend, also encounters the Faceless Old Woman in his home. She steals his toothbrush and makes cryptic comments about his hair. Carlos is disturbed and reports the incident to Cecil. Carlos becomes wary of his home and begins checking his closets.

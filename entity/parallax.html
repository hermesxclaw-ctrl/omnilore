Who It Is

In the vast emotional spectrum of the DC Universe, fear is not merely a feeling—it is a cosmic force, and at its heart dwells Parallax, a parasitic entity of pure terror that has possessed heroes and villains alike, turning the greatest Green Lantern into a galactic threat.

Parallax is a sentient parasite that feeds on fear. It is malevolent, manipulative, and seeks to spread terror across the universe. It can influence and possess individuals, amplifying their fears and corrupting them. It is patient and cunning, often working through hosts to achieve its goals.

Powers
Fear Embodiment

Parallax is the physical manifestation of the yellow light of fear. It can generate and control fear in others, inducing terror and despair. It can also manipulate the emotions of those around it.

Limit: Its power is limited by the emotional spectrum; it is weak against the green light of willpower.

Possession

Parallax can possess and control sentient beings, feeding on their fears. It can corrupt them, turning them into its puppets. The possession can be so strong that it overrides the host's personality, as seen with Hal Jordan.

Limit: The host must have a strong will to resist, but Parallax can eventually break it down.

Energy Manipulation

Parallax can project yellow energy blasts, create constructs, and manipulate energy for various effects. It can also fly and survive in space.

Limit: Its constructs are fueled by fear, so it can be weakened if the source of fear is removed.

Size Alteration

Parallax can change its size, appearing as a small insect-like creature or a massive cosmic entity.

Limit: Unknown.

Immortality

As a cosmic entity, Parallax is effectively immortal and can only be contained or destroyed by powerful means.

Limit: It can be imprisoned but not permanently killed.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Parallax appears as a colossal, insectoid entity with a yellow, crustacean-like carapace, multiple eyes, and a gaping maw. It is composed of pure yellow energy and can manifest as a monstrous form or possess a host, causing yellow eyes and a distinctive aura.

Symbols — Associated with the color yellow, the fear spectrum, and the symbol of a yellow lantern. Often depicted with insect-like features and a terrifying presence.

Domains — Resides within the Central Power Battery on Oa, where it was imprisoned as the yellow impurity. It can also exist in the emotional spectrum, particularly the yellow light of fear. It has been known to travel across space and possess beings on various worlds.

Accounts I
Emerald TwilightGreen Lantern Vol. 3 #48-50 (1994)

1994

After the destruction of Coast City, Hal Jordan goes insane with grief and anger. He attacks the Guardians of the Universe and the Green Lantern Corps, absorbing their power. He then becomes the villain Parallax, attempting to remake the universe. Hal Jordan becomes a major threat, leading to the 'Zero Hour' crisis. Later, it is revealed that Parallax was manipulating him.

Accounts II
Zero Hour: Crisis in TimeZero Hour: Crisis in Time #0-4 (1994)

1994

Parallax (Hal Jordan) attempts to restart the universe to undo his past mistakes. He is confronted by the Justice League and other heroes, but ultimately fails and sacrifices himself to reignite the Sun. Hal Jordan temporarily dies, but later returns as the Spectre. The universe is rebooted.

Green Lantern: RebirthGreen Lantern: Rebirth #1-6 (2004-2005)

2004-2005

It is revealed that the yellow impurity in the power rings was actually the entity Parallax, which had possessed Hal Jordan and caused his rampage. The Green Lantern Corps works to free Hal and imprison Parallax again. Hal Jordan is exonerated and returns as a Green Lantern. Parallax is trapped in the Central Power Battery.

Who It Is

Parallax is the entity that embodies the yellow light of fear — the opposite and equal of Ion's willpower. It is a parasitic cosmic being that feeds on the fear of all sentient creatures, growing stronger as terror spreads across the universe. Parallax was originally trapped within the Central Power Battery on Oa as the 'yellow impurity' that prevented Green Lantern rings from affecting anything yellow. For millennia, it whispered to the rings' users, subtly poisoning the power source. Parallax's most famous host was Hal Jordan, the greatest Green Lantern of all. After the destruction of Coast City, Jordan's grief and trauma made him susceptible. Parallax possessed him, amplifying his anger into genocidal madness. As Parallax, Hal Jordan destroyed the Green Lantern Corps, killed the Guardians of the Universe, and attempted to rewrite reality itself — nearly destroying the DC Universe in the process. This was later revealed to be Parallax's influence rather than Jordan's true nature. Parallax has also possessed other hosts, including Kyle Rayner (briefly) and various Sinestro Corps members. Parallax itself appears as a massive, insectoid yellow creature with multiple eyes, writhing tendrils, and an aura of pure dread. Its power includes fear projection, possession, reality manipulation, energy absorption, and the ability to drive entire planets into homicidal panic. Parallax represents the most dangerous aspect of sentient emotion: that fear, unchecked and amplified, can turn even the greatest hero into a monster. It is the shadow that follows every light, the terror that lurks within every brave heart.

The Approach

In the vast emotional spectrum of the DC Universe, fear is not merely a feeling—it is a cosmic force, and at its heart dwells Parallax, a parasitic entity of pure terror that has possessed heroes and villains alike, turning the greatest Green Lantern into a galactic threat.

Content advisory. Contains themes of fear, mind control, possession, violence, and cosmic horror. Some storylines involve psychological manipulation and destructive rampages.

Etymology

Named after the optical phenomenon where an object appears displaced due to a change in viewpoint, reflecting the entity's nature as a parasitic entity that alters perception and instills fear.

Cultural Ownership

DC Comics (Warner Bros. Discovery)

Disputed Identifications

Parallax is sometimes confused with the character Hal Jordan after he took the name Parallax, but they are distinct: one is an entity, the other a possessed hero. Also, there are multiple versions across different continuities (Pre-Crisis, Post-Crisis, New 52, etc.)

Description

Appearance

Parallax appears as a colossal, insectoid entity with a yellow, crustacean-like carapace, multiple eyes, and a gaping maw. It is composed of pure yellow energy and can manifest as a monstrous form or possess a host, causing yellow eyes and a distinctive aura.

Symbols & Iconography

Associated with the color yellow, the fear spectrum, and the symbol of a yellow lantern. Often depicted with insect-like features and a terrifying presence.

Behaviour & Personality

Parallax is a sentient parasite that feeds on fear. It is malevolent, manipulative, and seeks to spread terror across the universe. It can influence and possess individuals, amplifying their fears and corrupting them. It is patient and cunning, often working through hosts to achieve its goals.

Domains & Habitats

Resides within the Central Power Battery on Oa, where it was imprisoned as the yellow impurity. It can also exist in the emotional spectrum, particularly the yellow light of fear. It has been known to travel across space and possess beings on various worlds.

Version Differences

Originally, the yellow impurity was a weakness in the Green Lantern rings, not a sentient being. Later, it was retconned as the entity Parallax. In different continuities, its origin and role vary slightly, but it consistently embodies fear and is a major antagonist to the Green Lantern Corps.

Powers & Abilities

Fear Embodiment

Source Version Post-Rebirth (2004-2011)

Description

Parallax is the physical manifestation of the yellow light of fear. It can generate and control fear in others, inducing terror and despair. It can also manipulate the emotions of those around it.

Limitations

Its power is limited by the emotional spectrum; it is weak against the green light of willpower.

Contradictions

Some stories depict it as all-powerful, but it can be defeated by strong-willed individuals.

Possession

Source Version Green Lantern: Rebirth (2004)

Description

Parallax can possess and control sentient beings, feeding on their fears. It can corrupt them, turning them into its puppets. The possession can be so strong that it overrides the host's personality, as seen with Hal Jordan.

Limitations

The host must have a strong will to resist, but Parallax can eventually break it down.

Contradictions

Some hosts, like Sinestro, willingly embrace it, while others are unwilling victims.

Energy Manipulation

Its constructs are fueled by fear, so it can be weakened if the source of fear is removed.

Nature of Parallax

Position A

Parallax is a sentient entity that embodies fear.

Position A — evidence

Green Lantern: Rebirth (2004) and subsequent comics.

Position B

Parallax was originally just a yellow impurity in the power ring, not a character.

Position B — evidence

Early Green Lantern comics from the 1960s-1990s.

Scholarly Consensus

The retcon in Rebirth established the entity as canon, but some fans still debate the original concept.

Scholarly Consensus

The retcon absolves Hal of some blame, but he still made choices.

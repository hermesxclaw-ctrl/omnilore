Who It Is

He never got a real name, only a number — and in Baroque Works, a low number means you are disposable. Mr. 11 appears for barely ten pages of One Piece, tied to a chair on a Marine warship, sweating under Tashigi's questions and refusing to speak not out of loyalty, but out of terror of what his boss will do if he talks. Minutes later, a sniper's bullet proves he was right to be afraid.

Attested behavior is limited to silence under interrogation. When captured by Captain Smoker and questioned by Master Chief Petty Officer Tashigi about Baroque Works' headquarters and the identity of Mr. 0 (Crocodile), Mr. 11 refuses to answer despite being in Marine custody. Dialogue is minimal; he is shown sweating, grimacing, and looking away, indicating fear rather than stoic loyalty. Narrative context makes clear his silence is coerced by terror of Baroque Works retaliation, not ideological commitment - a pattern Oda uses for all Frontier Agents. Powers
Swordsmanship (Frontier Agent level)

Carries a single unspecific sword. As a Baroque Works Frontier Agent, he is presumed to have basic combat training superior to average Millions/Billions foot soldiers but far below Officer Agents (Mr. 5, Mr. 3, etc.) and below Marine officers like Tashigi. No named technique, style, or feat is ever shown - he is captured without a fight on-panel, and does not draw his blade. His sword is confiscated by Marines and not used.

Limit: No demonstrated feats against any named opponent; captured by Smoker off-panel implies he was unable to resist a Marine Captain. No Haki, no Devil Fruit, no superhuman durability shown. Sword is standard grade, not a Meito.

Baroque Works Espionage and Secrecy Conditioning

As with all Baroque Works agents, he was conditioned to maintain organizational secrecy under threat of death. His primary 'ability' in narrative terms is resistance to interrogation through fear of his own organization - he withholds information about Crocodile's identity and base location even under Marine detention.

Limit: This conditioning fails to protect him; the organization kills him anyway to ensure silence, demonstrating that the policy is not protective but punitive. He possesses no actual counter-interrogation training or willpower feat beyond fear.

No Devil Fruit / No Haki / No Enhanced Physiology

Explicitly a normal human with no Devil Fruit power, no Haki manifestation, no Mink, Fish-Man, Giant, or modified-human traits. This is attested by absence in all databooks and by his easy capture and death by conventional bullet.

Limit: Vulnerable to conventional weapons, as demonstrated by his death from a single rifle shot. No resistance, regeneration, or Logia intangibility.

Accounts I
The Frontier Agent System: Recruitment into Baroque WorksOne Piece manga Vol. 18, Vivre Card Databook, One Piece Blue Deep

In-universe: c. 2 years before Straw Hat arrival in Alabasta (c. 1519-1520 Age of the Sea); Publication: 2000-2001

Baroque Works, the secret criminal syndicate led by Warlord Crocodile (Mr. 0) and vice-president Nico Robin (Miss All Sunday), recruits thousands of agents who do not know their boss's identity. Its hierarchy is strictly numerical: Officer Agents (Mr. 0-5 with female partners), Frontier Agents (Mr. 6-12), and mass foot soldiers (Billions and Millions). Mr. 11 is inducted as a Frontier Agent, a mid-low rank tasked with frontier operations near Whisky Peak, a cactus-shaped island that serves as Baroque Works' bounty-hunter front. Like all Frontier Agents, he is given a codename, a uniform with his number, and a mission to eliminate pirates entering the Grand Line while funneling information upward. No personal recruitment scene is shown; his existence is established via the hierarchy chart and his later capture. This system explains why he knows the organization's methods but not necessarily Crocodile's true name until later. Interception at Sea: Capture by Captain Smoker off NanohanaOne Piece manga Chapter 159 'Come On In' (Weekly Shōnen Jump 2001)

In-universe: May 1522 (Alabasta arc timeline); Publication: March 2001

Marine Captain Smoker (Mokumoku no Mi Logia user) and his subordinate Master Chief Petty Officer Tashigi are pursuing the Straw Hat crew into the Grand Line but intercept a Baroque Works vessel instead. Mr. 11, sailing alone or with a small crew near the Alabasta coast, is defeated and taken prisoner by Smoker off-panel. The next time readers see him, he is already bound on the deck of Smoker's warship, his sword confiscated, as Smoker's crew sails toward the port town of Nanohana. Smoker, who has been investigating the mysterious Baroque Works after hearing rumors in Loguetown, sees Mr. 11 as a rare live source who can identify the organization's leader and base. The capture is presented as routine for Smoker but significant for the plot, as it is the Marines' first confirmed Baroque Works prisoner. Provides Smoker with his first direct lead on Baroque Works, accelerating the Marine investigation that will eventually expose Crocodile as Mr. 0. Accounts II
The Silent Interrogation Aboard Smoker's WarshipOne Piece manga Chapter 160 'Atop the Cactus Rock' pp. 2-5

Publication: March 2001; In-universe: same day as capture

On the deck of Smoker's ship, Tashigi attempts to interrogate Mr. 11. She demands the location of Baroque Works' headquarters and the true identity of Mr. 0, emphasizing that cooperation could save his life. Mr. 11, bruised and sweating, stares at the deck and says nothing. Tashigi grows frustrated, noting that all Baroque Works agents are terrified into silence. Smoker observes, smoking his cigars, and remarks that the man won't talk because he knows his organization will kill him if he does - and will kill him anyway if he is seen as a liability. The scene is deliberately quiet; Mr. 11's silence is his only action, and Oda uses it to characterize Baroque Works' culture of fear rather than to develop Mr. 11 as an individual. No information is extracted. Fails to yield intelligence, forcing Smoker to continue his investigation by following the Straw Hats and Baroque Works agents to Whisky Peak and later Alabasta. Silencing: Assassination by the BillionsOne Piece manga Chapter 160 pp. 5-8

Publication: March 2001

As the interrogation stalls, a ship flying no flag but bearing Baroque Works Billions agents approaches Smoker's warship. From its deck, a sniper fires a single rifle shot across the water. The bullet strikes Mr. 11 in the chest/head (manga shows impact with minimal gore, falling backward), killing him instantly while still bound. Smoker and Tashigi turn to see the Billions ship already retreating, its crew having completed their mission to silence a compromised agent. Smoker, enraged, orders pursuit but the assassins escape. Tashigi is shaken, realizing Baroque Works will murder its own rather than risk exposure. Mr. 11's corpse falls to the deck and is later shown being covered. No words are spoken by the assassins; the act is presented as cold, bureaucratic murder. Confirms Baroque Works' ruthlessness and Crocodile's policy of absolute secrecy; directly motivates Smoker to pursue Baroque Works to Alabasta, where he will eventually confront Crocodile and later testify about the Warlord's crimes. Posthumous Echo: Smoker's Investigation and the Fall of Baroque WorksOne Piece manga Chapters 161-212 (Alabasta Saga climax)

Publication: 2001-2002; In-universe: days after Mr. 11's death

Mr. 11 never appears again except as a memory and as a case file. Smoker uses the incident as evidence when he later confronts Crocodile in Alabasta's Rainbase and Alubarna. After Crocodile's defeat by Luffy and arrest by the Marines (with Smoker's testimony), Tashigi references the murdered agent as proof of Baroque Works' internal murders. In the broader story, Mr. 11's fate is mirrored by the fates of other Frontier Agents: Mr. 9 and Miss Wednesday (Vivi) defect, Mr. 8 (Igaram) is presumed dead but survives, while Mr. 11 and later Mr. 7 and Miss Father's Day are killed or defeated. His death serves as the first on-page demonstration that Crocodile's organization is not a colorful pirate crew but a lethal criminal enterprise that discards its own. Databooks later list him as 'Deceased' with no bounty or epitaph. Narrative consequence is thematic rather than plot-driving: Oda establishes stakes for Vivi's infiltration and for Robin's later defection - both know that failure means death like Mr. 11's. Mr. 11 was a Devil Fruit user or had a special power like other Baroque Works officers. — No Devil Fruit, Haki, or special ability is ever attributed to him in manga, anime, or databooks. Officer Agents (Mr. 5 Bomb-Bomb Fruit, Mr. 3 Wax-Wax Fruit, etc.) have powers, but Frontier Agents 6-12 are explicitly normal humans with only basic combat training. His sword is a normal blade and he shows no superhuman feat.

Mr. 11 survived and reappears later in Alabasta or Impel Down. — He is definitively killed in Chapter 160 and listed as 'Deceased' in Vivre Card. He does not appear in any later arc, cover story, or Impel Down prisoner list. Some fans confuse him with Mr. 9 or other Frontier Agents who do survive.

Mr. 11 had a female partner named Miss Eleven or was paired with Miss Thursday. — Only Officer Agents (Mr. 0-5) have paired female partners (Miss All Sunday through Miss Valentine). Frontier Agents operate solo or with generic Billions support. No Miss Eleven exists in any canonical source; Miss Thursday is not a canonical Baroque Works member.

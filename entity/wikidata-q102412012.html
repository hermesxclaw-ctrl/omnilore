Who It Is

In the frozen wastes of Vollachia, a name is spoken in whispers: Elsa Granhiert, the Bowel Hunter. She is not a queen, not a soldier, not a spirit—she is a blade wrapped in silk, a killer whose legend is written in blood and shadow. To the Empire, she is a phantom; to the underworld, she is a promise of death. But who is the woman behind the moniker? Her origins are as obscure as her motives, and her story is one of violence, ambition, and the cold calculus of survival.

Elsa is calm, collected, and dangerously sadistic. She takes pleasure in the hunt and the act of killing, often taunting her opponents and toying with them. Despite her apparent bloodlust, she is professional and loyal to her contracts, showing no hesitation or remorse. She is highly intelligent and perceptive, able to deduce her opponents' weaknesses and exploit them. She also exhibits a twisted sense of curiosity, particularly regarding the nature of her targets.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname 'Granhiert' is of Germanic origin, possibly meaning 'great heart' or 'great hunter'. The moniker 'Bowel Hunter' refers to her signature method of killing: she disembowels her victims.

Pronunciation — EL-sah GRAN-heert (approximate English pronunciation)

Aliases — Bowel Hunter, The Bowel Hunter, Elsa, Assassin of the Bowels

Ownership — Japanese media franchise, created by Tappei Nagatsuki. The character is part of the Re:Zero universe, which blends isekai, dark fantasy, and psychological horror.

Powers
Expert Assassin

Elsa is a highly skilled assassin, proficient in stealth, tracking, and combat. She can infiltrate heavily guarded locations and eliminate targets with minimal effort.

Limit: None known, but she is not superhuman; she relies on skill and experience.

Master Swordsmanship/Dagger Combat

Elsa wields two curved daggers with exceptional skill, able to parry, slash, and throw them with deadly accuracy. She can defeat trained knights and even magic users in close combat.

Limit: She is vulnerable to long-range attacks or overwhelming magical power.

Superhuman Agility and Reflexes

Elsa possesses extraordinary speed and reflexes, allowing her to dodge attacks, climb walls, and move almost silently. This makes her a formidable opponent in any environment.

Limit: Not explicitly supernatural; likely the result of intense training.

Pain Tolerance

Elsa demonstrates a high tolerance for pain, remaining functional even after sustaining serious injuries. In one instance, she continues to fight after being impaled.

Limit: She can still be killed if her vital organs are destroyed.

Intimidation and Psychological Warfare

Elsa uses her reputation and gruesome methods to instill fear in her enemies, often causing them to hesitate or make mistakes.

Limit: Ineffective against fearless or highly disciplined opponents.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Elsa Granhiert is a young woman with long, light-purple hair and sharp, violet eyes. She typically wears a revealing black dress with a slit, thigh-high boots, and a choker. Her attire is both elegant and practical, allowing for swift movement. She carries a pair of curved daggers (often referred to as 'bowel hunters') that she uses with lethal precision.

Symbols — Her signature weapon is a pair of thin, curved daggers. The moniker 'Bowel Hunter' is symbolically tied to her method of killing: she slashes her victims' abdomens, causing them to spill their entrails. This gruesome act is both a calling card and a psychological weapon.

Domains — Elsa is an assassin who operates primarily in the capital of the Kingdom of Lugunica, but she travels across the world for contracts. She is comfortable in both urban and wilderness environments, but she is most at home in the shadows of cities.

Accounts I
The Assassination of Emilia and the First LoopRe:Zero Light Novel, Arc 1; Anime Episode 1

Fictional timeline, year 1 of the Dragon Kingdom of Lugunica

Elsa is hired by an unknown client to assassinate Emilia, a half-elf candidate for the royal throne. She attacks Emilia in the Loot House, where Subaru Natsuki, the protagonist, is staying. Elsa kills Emilia and Subaru, triggering Subaru's 'Return by Death' ability, which rewinds time. In subsequent loops, Subaru attempts to prevent Elsa's attack, eventually enlisting the help of Reinhard van Astrea, the Sword Saint, who kills Elsa. Elsa's death temporarily ends the threat, but her actions set Subaru on his path of suffering and growth. The mystery of who hired her remains a plot point in later arcs.

Accounts II
The Bowel Hunter's Return in the SanctuaryRe:Zero Light Novel, Arc 4 (later adapted in Season 2 of the anime)

Fictional timeline, year 1

Elsa is resurrected by the Witch of Greed, Echidna, as part of a trial. She appears in the Sanctuary and attacks the inhabitants, but is ultimately defeated again, this time by Garfiel Tinsel, a werebeast boy. Her death is final. Elsa's second death removes her as a recurring threat, but her presence in the Sanctuary trial reveals more about the Witch's experiments and the nature of resurrection in the world.

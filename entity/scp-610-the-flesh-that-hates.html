Who It Is

A highly contagious disease that transforms human flesh into a horrifying, cancer-like growth. Infected tissue becomes animate, sprouting limbs, eyes, mouths, and organs in random configurations. The disease spreads through skin contact and transforms whole populations into masses of writhing, interconnected flesh. Entire villages in Russia have been consumed and quarantined.

Form

Infected individuals appear as human bodies partially or entirely replaced by writhing red-pink flesh growths with random anatomical features (eyes blinking from shoulders, mouths gasping from thighs, fingers growing from foreheads). In advanced stages, multiple individuals fuse into a single mass of interconnected flesh.

Powers

Extremely contagious transformation

Flesh reanimation

Hive integration

Rapid growth from infected tissue

Who It Is
Form

SCP-610 is a highly potent and deadly bioweapon that converts all organic matter into instances of itself. It spreads through physical contact and can infect any living organism.

Powers

Conversion SCP-610 has the ability to convert all organic matter into instances of itself, allowing it to spread and infect other living organisms.

Infection The entity can infect any living organism through physical contact, causing it to become a new instance of SCP-610.

Aggression SCP-610 is highly aggressive and deadly, with a strong desire to infect and convert all living organisms into instances of itself.

Incident Log

The War of the Flesh was a global conflict between the Kalmaktama Empire and the SCP Foundation, with SCP-610 playing a central role in the conflict.

SCP-610 was first discovered in the area around Lake Baikal in Southern Siberia, where it had infected a large number of people and animals.

Etymology

Derived from Ancient Greek ποιμήν (poimēn), meaning 'shepherd'.

Cultural Ownership

Greek origin, used in Roman, Christian, and Slavic contexts.

Disputed Identifications

The name is shared by multiple distinct figures; there is a risk of conflating them. The mythological Poemenius is a satyr leader, while the historical figures are early Christians, bishops, and patriarchs.

Who It Is

Also called Makkuro Kurosuke or soot sprites, these are small, pitch-black, fuzzy-haired spirits with two large eyes and long, thin limbs. They move by hovering and can lift objects many times their own weight. They appear in abandoned houses and dissolve into soot if crushed. They later work in Kamaji's boiler room in Spirited Away.

Powers

Hovering, object lifting, soot transformation

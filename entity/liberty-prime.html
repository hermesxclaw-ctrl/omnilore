Who It Is
Form

Liberty Prime is a giant combat robot, approximately 20 stories tall, with a metallic body and a distinctive American flag design on its chest. Its appearance is a blend of military and patriotic elements, reflecting its purpose as a symbol of American power.

Powers

Nuclear Missile Launch Liberty Prime is equipped with nuclear missiles that it can launch at enemy targets, dealing massive damage and destruction.

Accounts

Liberty Prime was created by the Strategic Arms Division as a superweapon to achieve victory against China in the Sino-American War. It was designed to be a symbol of American power and a reminder of the devastating effects of nuclear war.

Liberty Prime's first battle was against a Chinese army, where it demonstrated its capabilities as a superweapon. However, the battle was marked by controversy and debate over the ethics of using such a powerful robot in combat.

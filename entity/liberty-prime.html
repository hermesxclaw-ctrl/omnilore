Who It Is

A colossal pre-War American propaganda weapon — a 40-foot-tall bipedal robot designed to instill patriotic fervor while annihilating communists. Liberty Prime was built by General Atomic International and stored beneath the Capitol Building. It was reactivated by the Brotherhood of Steel to destroy the Enclave and later modified to fight the Institute. It quotes American history, drops nuclear bombs, and throws cars. Its programming explicitly targets 'Chinese' and 'communists' as threats.

The hypothesis that our reality is a computer simulation created by a more advanced civilization — and by implication, the existence of a Simulator (the 'Architect' or 'Programmer') who designed and runs this simulation. Bostrom's argument is probabilistic: if a civilization can create vast numbers of simulated universes, the odds that we are in the one 'base reality' are infinitesimal.

The Approach

In the shadowy reaches of the Star Trek universe, where the Federation's bright ideals flicker against the void, lies the planet Hobus—a world whose name echoes in the tragic destruction of a Romulan star and the birth of a new timeline. Though its surface has never been fully charted on screen, Hobus stands as a fulcrum of galactic history, a supernova that shattered an empire and set the stage for a rebooted saga.

Content advisory. None
Identity

Hobus

Pronunciation HOH-bus

Etymology

The name 'Hobus' is of Romulan origin, though its linguistic roots are not explained in canon. It may be a Romulan word for a star or planet, but this is speculative.

Cultural Ownership

Romulan Star Empire (fictional)

Disputed Identifications

None known; Hobus is consistently identified as the star that went supernova in the 2009 film.

Description

Appearance

Hobus is depicted as a star (not a planet) in the 2009 film. It appears as a bright, unstable star that goes supernova. Its planetary system is not shown in detail, but it is implied to have planets, one of which is the Romulan homeworld? Actually, Hobus is the star, not a planet. The film refers to the Hobus star. The description should focus on the star's appearance: a massive, luminous star that suddenly explodes.

Symbols & Iconography

No specific symbols associated with Hobus.

Behaviour & Personality

As a star, Hobus has no behavior or personality. Its instability is the key characteristic, leading to its catastrophic supernova.

Domains & Habitats

Hobus is located in or near Romulan space, though its exact coordinates are not given. It is the primary of a system that likely includes planets, but none are named.

Version Differences

In the 2009 film, Hobus is a star. In some tie-in media, it is referred to as a planet, but this is likely an error. The novelization and Countdown comic consistently treat it as a star.

Stories & Myths

The Hobus Supernova

Source Star Trek (2009 film), Star Trek: Countdown, novelizationCulture Romulan (fictional)Period 2387 (fictional timeline)

Summary

In 2387, the star Hobus went supernova, threatening the Romulan Empire. Ambassador Spock attempted to stop the supernova by using red matter, but failed, resulting in the destruction of Romulus and the creation of a black hole. The supernova's shockwave also threatened the galaxy, and Spock's ship was pulled into the black hole, sending him and Nero back in time.

Known Gaps

No canonical on-screen depiction of Hobus's exact appearance, location, or planetary system. No information on its history before the supernova. No official pronunciation. Much of the lore is derived from tie-in media and may be considered non-canon by some.

Research Scope

This dossier compiles all known references to the planet Hobus from canonical Star Trek productions, tie-in novels, and licensed reference works. Due to the lack of a dedicated encyclopedia entry and the scarcity of on-screen detail, much of the information is derived from the 2009 film Star Trek and its novelization.

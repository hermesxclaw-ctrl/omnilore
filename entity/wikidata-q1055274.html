The Approach

In the vast expanse of Finnish naming, Juhani stands as a bridge between the earthly and the legendary—a name borne by poets, athletes, and saints, yet also by a Jedi fugitive and a Templar agent. Its syllables echo through centuries of Nordic history and across galaxies of fiction, making it a truly multifaceted identity.

Content advisory. No dark material is present in the primary sources. The name has been used for a serial killer in the 19th century, which may be disturbing to some readers. Fictional characters may involve violence typical of their genres.

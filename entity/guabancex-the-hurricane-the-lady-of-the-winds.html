Guabancex (the Hurricane / the Lady of the Winds)

also known asthe Hurricane / the Lady of the Winds
Typegoddess (storm, hurricane, destruction)
Who It Is
Form

Guabancex is often depicted as a powerful woman, sometimes with a hurricane or storm surrounding her. Her zemi idol is said to depict a woman with a spiraling wind pattern, possibly representing the spiraling motion of a hurricane.

Powers

Control over the winds Guabancex is said to have the power to control the winds, summoning storms and hurricanes with her presence. She is associated with the spiraling motion of a hurricane, which is said to be a manifestation of her power.

Accounts

According to Taino mythology, Guabancex was created by the Taíno gods to govern the forces of nature. She was tasked with maintaining balance and order in the environment, but her power soon became too great for her to control.

According to Taíno oral tradition, Guabancex was said to have created a hurricane that devastated the island of Hispaniola. The story describes Guabancex as a fierce and powerful deity, who was feared and respected by the Taíno people.

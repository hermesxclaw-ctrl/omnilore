Form & Signs

Appearance — The Skunk Ape is described as a large, hairy, humanoid creature, typically standing between 6-10 feet tall and weighing an estimated 300-500 pounds. Its fur is said to be dark brown or black, and it is often associated with a strong, unpleasant odor.

Symbols — The Skunk Ape is often depicted in popular culture as a large, hairy creature with a strong odor, and is sometimes associated with the Florida state flag or other regional symbols.

Domains — The Skunk Ape is said to inhabit the forests and swamps of Florida, particularly in the Everglades and Big Cypress Swamp regions.

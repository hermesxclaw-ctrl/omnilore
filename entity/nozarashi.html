Who It Is

In the world of Bleach, Nozarashi is the name of a Zanpakutō that embodies the raw, untamed power of a beast. Its wielder, Kenpachi Zaraki, is known for his love of battle, and Nozarashi reflects that ferocity with a blade that has been described as 'the wildest' of all. But what does this name truly mean, and what secrets lie within its form?

Nozarashi is a wild and aggressive Zanpakutō, matching its wielder's battle-hungry nature. It is only fully manifested when Kenpachi is at his limit, and it seems to revel in combat.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Nozarashi (野晒) literally translates to 'field-dried' or 'exposed in a field', referring to something left out in the open, often a corpse. It can also be interpreted as 'weather-beaten' or 'exposed to the elements'.

Pronunciation — No-za-ra-shi

Aliases — Nozarashi

Ownership — Japanese pop culture, specifically the 'Bleach' franchise created by Tite Kubo.

Powers
Shikai: Nozarashi

Kenpachi's Shikai transforms his blade into a massive cleaver, greatly increasing its cutting power. It allows him to easily slice through large structures and even the meteor-like projectiles of Gremmy Thoumeaux.

Limit: The Shikai is extremely heavy and difficult to control, but Kenpachi's immense strength allows him to wield it effectively. It does not grant special abilities beyond sheer cutting force.

Bankai: Nozarashi

Kenpachi's Bankai is unnamed, but it manifests as a more compact, humanoid form. It grants him immense physical power, turning him into a red-skinned, horned demon-like being. In this state, he gains incredible strength, speed, and durability, allowing him to defeat powerful enemies like Gerard Valkyrie.

Limit: The Bankai is so powerful that it causes severe damage to Kenpachi's body, and he cannot maintain it for long. It also makes him lose control, attacking friend and foe alike.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In its sealed form, Nozarashi appears as a standard katana with a wrapped hilt, but it is often kept in a worn state, reflecting Kenpachi's neglect. In its Shikai, Nozarashi manifests as a massive cleaver-like blade, larger than Kenpachi himself, with a rough, jagged edge and no guard. The blade appears chipped and weathered, matching its name 'weather-beaten'.

Symbols — The blade's massive size and crude form symbolize raw power and a love for battle, reflecting Kenpachi's personality. The name 'Nozarashi' evokes the idea of being exposed to the elements, suggesting a weapon that is unrefined and primal.

Domains — Nozarashi is a spiritual weapon, existing in the soul of its wielder. It has no physical habitat, but its power is most prominent in battle.

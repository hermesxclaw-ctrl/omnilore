Who It Is

Captain Kenpachi Zaraki's zanpakutō. Shikai transforms the blade into a massive, brutal cleaver that reflects Kenpachi's sheer love of battle. Nozarashi means 'weather-beaten' or 'exposed to the elements.' Its Bankai is a terrifying form that transforms Kenpachi into a demonic berserker with red skin, horns, and immense power beyond normal limits.

Powers

Massive cleaver form, pure destruction, demonic Bankai transformation

Captain Kenpachi Zaraki's zanpakutō. Shikai transforms the blade into a massive, brutal cleaver that reflects Kenpachi's sheer love of battle. Nozarashi means 'weather-beaten' or 'exposed to the elements.' Its Bankai is a terrifying form that transforms Kenpachi into a demonic berserker with red skin, horns, and immense power beyond normal limits.

The Approach

In the world of Bleach, Nozarashi is the name of a Zanpakutō that embodies the raw, untamed power of a beast. Its wielder, Kenpachi Zaraki, is known for his love of battle, and Nozarashi reflects that ferocity with a blade that has been described as 'the wildest' of all. But what does this name truly mean, and what secrets lie within its form?

Content advisory. Contains themes of violence and battle, consistent with the source material.
Identity

Nozarashi

Pronunciation No-za-ra-shi

Etymology

Nozarashi (野晒) literally translates to 'field-dried' or 'exposed in a field', referring to something left out in the open, often a corpse. It can also be interpreted as 'weather-beaten' or 'exposed to the elements'.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese pop culture, specifically the 'Bleach' franchise created by Tite Kubo.

Disputed Identifications

There are no significant disputed identifications for Nozarashi within the franchise, though some fans theorize about its true form and abilities.

Description

Appearance

In its sealed form, Nozarashi appears as a standard katana with a wrapped hilt, but it is often kept in a worn state, reflecting Kenpachi's neglect. In its Shikai, Nozarashi manifests as a massive cleaver-like blade, larger than Kenpachi himself, with a rough, jagged edge and no guard. The blade appears chipped and weathered, matching its name 'weather-beaten'.

Symbols & Iconography

The blade's massive size and crude form symbolize raw power and a love for battle, reflecting Kenpachi's personality. The name 'Nozarashi' evokes the idea of being exposed to the elements, suggesting a weapon that is unrefined and primal.

Behaviour & Personality

Nozarashi is a wild and aggressive Zanpakutō, matching its wielder's battle-hungry nature. It is only fully manifested when Kenpachi is at his limit, and it seems to revel in combat.

Domains & Habitats

Nozarashi is a spiritual weapon, existing in the soul of its wielder. It has no physical habitat, but its power is most prominent in battle.

Version Differences

In the original manga, Nozarashi's Shikai is revealed during the Thousand-Year Blood War arc. The anime adaptation follows the manga closely, with no significant differences in its appearance or abilities.

Powers & Abilities

Shikai: Nozarashi

Source Version Manga, Chapter 580, 'The Blade Is Me'

Description

Kenpachi's Shikai transforms his blade into a massive cleaver, greatly increasing its cutting power. It allows him to easily slice through large structures and even the meteor-like projectiles of Gremmy Thoumeaux.

Limitations

The Shikai is extremely heavy and difficult to control, but Kenpachi's immense strength allows him to wield it effectively. It does not grant special abilities beyond sheer cutting force.

Contradictions

None known.

Bankai: Nozarashi

Source Version Manga, Chapter 670, 'The Perfect Crimson'

Description

Kenpachi's Bankai is unnamed, but it manifests as a more compact, humanoid form. It grants him immense physical power, turning him into a red-skinned, horned demon-like being. In this state, he gains incredible strength, speed, and durability, allowing him to defeat powerful enemies like Gerard Valkyrie.

Limitations

The Bankai is so powerful that it causes severe damage to Kenpachi's body, and he cannot maintain it for long. It also makes him lose control, attacking friend and foe alike.

Contradictions

Some fans speculate about the Bankai's name and abilities, but no official contradictions exist.

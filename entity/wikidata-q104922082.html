Who It Is

In the sprawling, sun-scorched world of a certain legendary manga, a name whispered in the shadows of the underworld carries weight: Stussy. A figure of intrigue and power, her origins are as enigmatic as the organization she serves, and her true nature is a secret that could topple empires.

Stussy is calm, composed, and highly intelligent. She is a master of deception, able to maintain a false identity for years. She is pragmatic and ruthless when necessary, but also shows a more playful side, especially when interacting with allies. She is fiercely loyal to her creator, Dr. Vegapunk, and to the World Government's Cipher Pol, though her ultimate allegiance is to her own survival and the preservation of her creator's legacy.

Etymology — The name 'Stussy' is likely derived from the fashion brand 'Stüssy', reflecting Oda's habit of naming characters after clothing brands. The alias 'Miss Buckingham' may reference the British royal family's Buckingham Palace, but this is speculative.

Pronunciation — STUH-see

Powers
Enhanced Physical Abilities

As a clone, Stussy possesses enhanced strength, speed, and durability, though the exact limits are not fully shown. She can hold her own against powerful fighters.

Limit: Not extensively showcased; she relies more on strategy than direct combat.

Espionage and Deception

Stussy is a highly skilled spy, able to infiltrate criminal organizations and maintain a false identity for decades. Her cover as a mafia boss and underworld broker went undetected for years.

Limit: Her identity was eventually exposed, showing that even the best deceptions can be uncovered.

Cloning

Stussy is a clone of the original Buckingham Stussy, created by Dr. Vegapunk as part of the MADS project. This gives her a genetic connection to the Rocks Pirates, but she is a distinct individual.

Limit: As a clone, she may have a shortened lifespan or other side effects, but this is not confirmed.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Stussy is a tall, elegant woman with long, wavy blonde hair, often styled with a distinctive curl. She has a beauty mark under her left eye and wears stylish, revealing clothing, typically a long coat or dress with a fur collar. Her attire often includes high heels and accessories like earrings and a necklace. She carries herself with poise and confidence.

Symbols — Her symbol is the 'MADS' logo, representing the clandestine scientific organization she was created by. She also wears a distinctive cross-shaped earring, which may be a personal choice or a mark of her origin.

Domains — She operates in the criminal underworld, often seen in high-society gatherings and secret meetings. She has been based in the Holy Land of Mary Geoise and later on Egghead Island, Dr. Vegapunk's research facility.

2016-2018

Stussy appears as a member of the 'Underworld Brokers' at a tea party hosted by Big Mom. She is introduced as a mafia boss and ally of the Vinsmoke family. She later aids the Straw Hat Pirates in their escape by helping to immobilize Big Mom's homies, revealing her allegiance to Cipher Pol 0. She helps the Straw Hats escape and maintains her cover, allowing her to continue gathering intelligence for the World Government.

Accounts II
The Egghead Arc (Revelation)One Piece manga chapters 1061-1125, anime episodes 1088-1125

2022-2025

Stussy accompanies Cipher Pol 0 agents to Egghead Island to capture Dr. Vegapunk. It is revealed that she is a clone of the original Buckingham Stussy, created by Vegapunk as part of the MADS project. She betrays Cipher Pol 0 to protect Vegapunk, revealing her true loyalty. She helps the Straw Hats and Vegapunk escape, but the island is destroyed and she is presumed dead. She sacrifices herself to allow Vegapunk and the Straw Hats to escape. Her fate is left ambiguous, as she is not confirmed dead.

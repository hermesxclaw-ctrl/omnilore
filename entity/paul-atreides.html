Who It Is
Form

Paul Atreides is a tall, lean, and agile individual with a strong sense of presence. He has a distinctive voice and an uncanny ability to read people. As the leader of the Fremen, he wears a stillsuit and carries a crysknife.

Powers

Prescience Paul Atreides possesses the ability to see into the future, allowing him to make informed decisions and shape the course of events.

Accounts

The story follows Paul Atreides, the son of a powerful noble family, as he becomes embroiled in a complex web of intrigue and politics on the desert planet of Arrakis.

The story follows Paul Atreides as he becomes increasingly isolated and paranoid, struggling to maintain his grip on power in a complex and treacherous world.

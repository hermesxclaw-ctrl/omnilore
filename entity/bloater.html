Who It Is

The Bloater is the most fearsome stage of the Cordyceps Brain Infection in The Last of Us — a Stage 4 infected that has been consumed by the fungus for so long that its entire body has become a living fortress of armored fungal plates. Bloaters are massive, weighing several hundred pounds and standing well over 7 feet tall, with bodies entirely encrusted in thick, calcified fungal growth that acts as natural armor — absorbing small arms fire, deflecting melee attacks, and making the Bloater functionally immune to most conventional weapons without sustained, coordinated fire. Its face is completely obscured by fungal plates, its movements are slow but deliberate, and its attacks are devastating: it charges, tears victims limb from limb, and — most terrifyingly — rips chunks of its own fungal body off to hurl as mycotoxin spore bombs that create lingering clouds of lethal, choking fungus.

The Bloater represents the endpoint of the Cordyceps infection — a human host whose entire biomass has been converted to fungal matter, repurposed as a slow-moving tank that spreads spores and eliminates threats. It takes years for a Bloater to form, meaning every Bloater was once a human being who has been suffering inside the infection for over a decade. Their presence indicates an area that has been heavily infected for a long time — a spore-infested 'nest' location. In The Last of Us, Bloaters mark the most stressful combat encounters: high school gyms, flooded hotel basements, abandoned arcades — tight spaces where the Bloater's charge attacks and spore bombs limit the player's ability to keep distance.

The Bloater fight in the original game's 'Bill's Town' section (the high school gym) is a tutorial in resource management: the player must use molotov cocktails, nail bombs, and rifles while avoiding the Bloater's charge and dealing with accompanying Runners. The HBO adaptation (2023) featured a Bloater in Episode 5 — a practical-effects creature played by a massive stunt performer in a prosthetic suit — in one of the show's most talked-about action sequences, introducing the monster to a global audience beyond gaming. The Bloater's design — a walking tank of fungal armor that throws pieces of itself as bombs — is a natural extension of the Cordyceps concept taken to its logical extreme. It makes the infection feel like a genuine ecosystem, with different stages adapted for different roles: Runners for pursuit, Clickers for ambush, and Bloaters for brute-force elimination.

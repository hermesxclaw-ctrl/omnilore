Archive Dossier — Identity

SCP-087

Pronunciation Ess-see-pee zero eight seven; alias: "the stair-well".

Known As

The Stairwell

Etymology

The designation "SCP-087" follows the internal cataloguing system of the SCP Foundation collaborative fiction project, where "SCP" stands for "Secure, Contain, Protect" and numbers identify individual anomalies. The informal alias "The Stairwell" derives from the object's primary manifestation as an anomalous staircase.

Cultural Ownership

SCP-087 belongs to the shared, collaboratively authored universe of the SCP Foundation, an online horror and speculative fiction project hosted primarily on the SCP Wiki. The work is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0/4.0, and authorship is distributed among multiple contributors rather than a single canonical creator.

Disputed Identifications

Within the SCP narrative, SCP-087 is consistently treated as an anomalous architectural space rather than a sentient entity. However, the related phenomenon SCP-087-1—a disembodied face encountered within the stairwell—is sometimes informally conflated with SCP-087 itself in fan discussions and derivative works, leading to ambiguity over whether the designation refers to the space, the face, or the combined phenomenon. Canonical documentation on the SCP Wiki distinguishes SCP-087 (the stairwell) from SCP-087-1 (the face).

Archive Dossier — Approach

You stand at a janitor’s closet that isn’t a closet at all—behind the reinforced door, a stairwell drops into darkness, a child’s sobbing echoing from somewhere that never gets closer no matter how many landings you count. SCP-087 is horror stripped to its bare geometry: thirteen steps, a three-meter platform, a 180-degree turn, repeated until the building’s foundations and the planet’s crust should have run out long ago. Somewhere in that impossible shaft, a pale face with no mouth and opaque white eyes waits at the edge of your flashlight’s reach, not attacking, not speaking, just watching until you break and run. This dossier follows that descent, not to find a bottom—there may not be one—but to map how a single staircase became one of the internet’s most enduring architectures of fear.

[The sound of a child crying, echoing upward from an indeterminate depth]

Who It Is

SCP-087 is an unlit platform staircase that appears to descend infinitely. The entrance is located on a university campus, accessed through an unremarkable door that, when opened, reveals a concrete stairwell descending into complete darkness. The staircase is 2.3 meters wide with a central handrail, and each flight consists of 13 steps before turning at a half-landing. The walls are plain concrete, and the only auditory information available to explorers is the sound of their own footsteps echoing downward. No external light penetrates beyond the first flight; all illumination must be provided by the explorer. SCP-087's most notable feature is the presence of something within it — something that dwells somewhere deep in the darkness. During Foundation exploration logs, personnel at varying depths reported hearing a child crying somewhere below them. As explorers descend further, the crying becomes closer, and additional phenomena manifest: the sound of something moving in the darkness, the feeling of being watched, and eventually, encountering a pale, featureless face floating in the darkness — designated SCP-087-1 — which appears approximately 200 flights down. SCP-087-1 has no visible body, only a white face with no mouth, wide-set nostrils, and completely black, pupil-less eyes. When encountered, it rushes toward the observer at high speed, and all subsequent contact with the exploration team is lost. The Foundation has conducted multiple exploration attempts, each encountering the entity at progressively different depths, suggesting SCP-087-1 can reposition itself. The origin and purpose of the stairwell remain unknown. Theories range from a dimensional gateway to a manifestation of collective human fear of descending into darkness. SCP-087 was one of the earliest Foundation entries to achieve viral popularity, its simplicity and the primal fear of descending into the unknown resonating deeply with readers. It spawned numerous fan games, the most famous being the 2012 indie horror title 'SCP-087-B.' The Foundation currently maintains the entrance sealed with controlled access for scheduled exploration missions only.

Archive Dossier — Description

Appearance

Attested: SCP-087 is described as an unlit platform staircase located on the campus of an unnamed university. The stairs descend at a 38-degree angle for 13 steps before reaching a semicircular landing approximately 3 meters in diameter. At each landing, the staircase reverses direction by 180 degrees, creating a zig-zag descent. There are no windows, doors, or lighting fixtures within the stairwell, and the walls, steps, and landings are described as ordinary building materials consistent with a modern institutional structure. The visual range of any subject is limited to roughly 1.5 flights of stairs, even when artificial light is used. Within this space, SCP-087-1 appears as a disembodied face, usually described as hovering in mid-air at or near a landing. The face has opaque white eyes, no visible pupils, no nostrils, and no mouth, giving it a mask-like, incomplete appearance. Its skin tone and other details are difficult to discern due to the pervasive darkness and the limited range of light.
Reconstructed: Fan art and game adaptations often depict the stairwell with concrete or painted walls, metal railings, and a claustrophobic, industrial aesthetic, but these visual details are extrapolations from the sparse canonical description rather than explicitly attested features.
Unknown: The exact materials, color schemes, and architectural style beyond basic dimensions are not specified in the original SCP article.

Symbols & Iconography

Attested: SCP-087 itself is not associated with explicit symbols, inscriptions, or iconography within the canonical text. Its most recognizable "symbol" in fandom is the image of a dark stairwell with a pale, incomplete face emerging from the darkness, often based on Figure B in the SCP article (an enhanced still frame of SCP-087-1). The repeated motif of a downward spiral into darkness, accompanied by a crying child, functions as a narrative symbol of psychological descent and unreachable suffering.
Adaptation-specific: In fan games and visual adaptations, SCP-087 is sometimes represented with warning signs, hazard symbols, or SCP Foundation logos near the entrance, but these are additions by creators rather than canonical features of the stairwell itself.

Behaviour & Personality

Attested: SCP-087, as an architectural anomaly, does not exhibit personality in the conventional sense. Its "behavior" is expressed through spatial and physical anomalies: it appears to extend far beyond the physical limits of the building and surrounding geology; it absorbs excess light from sources above approximately 75 watts; and it maintains a constant auditory illusion of a distressed child crying from somewhere below the subject, estimated at around 200 meters beneath the initial platform but never actually approached despite extensive descent. Subjects report intense feelings of paranoia, fear, and dread while within SCP-087, especially after encountering SCP-087-1. SCP-087-1 itself does not speak or move in a conventional way; it appears suddenly, often at close range, and remains fixed in position while subjects react with panic. The face is not confirmed to be the source of the crying and is explicitly stated in the article to be distinct from that sound.
Reconstructed: Many readers and derivative works interpret SCP-087 as "predatory" or "malevolent" due to its apparent endlessness, the psychological torment it inflicts, and the presence of SCP-087-1. However, the canonical text does not attribute motives or intentionality to the stairwell or the face.
Unknown: Whether SCP-087 or SCP-087-1 possesses sentience, agency, or a specific goal remains unspecified and is treated as unknown in the SCP documentation.

Domains & Habitats

Attested: SCP-087 is located on the campus of an unnamed institution, with its doorway disguised as a janitorial closet. Within the SCP universe, its "domain" is the interior of the stairwell itself, which appears to extend into non-Euclidean space or an extradimensional realm, given that the depth reached in Exploration IV far exceeds the possible structure of the building and the surrounding geology. The stairwell is entirely enclosed, with no external windows or openings, and functions as a contained anomalous location.
Reconstructed: Some fan interpretations treat SCP-087 as a localized manifestation of a larger extradimensional space or as a portal to an unknown realm, but this is speculative and not explicitly confirmed in the primary article.
Unknown: The ultimate extent, endpoint, or external connections of SCP-087’s space are unknown; the article explicitly states that it is not known whether SCP-087 has an endpoint.

Version Differences

Attested (canonical SCP Wiki version): SCP-087 is a Euclid-class anomalous stairwell with four documented explorations, a crying child sound, and the face SCP-087-1. The article emphasizes non-Euclidean depth, light absorption, and psychological effects, and concludes with a ban on further access after Exploration IV.
Fan wiki and database versions: Fandom-based SCP wikis and databases largely reproduce the canonical description but may simplify language, add laconic summaries, or reorganize sections. They sometimes emphasize SCP-087-1 as a possible cognitohazard and highlight the stairwell’s "infinite" nature more strongly than the original article, which is more cautious and technical.
Adaptation-specific (games, videos): Fan-made games titled "SCP-087" typically expand the visual and experiential aspects of the stairwell, adding ambient sounds, jump scares, and sometimes additional entities or events not present in the original text. YouTube narrations and analyses often frame SCP-087 as an example of "spacetime SCP" or "non-Euclidean horror," foregrounding its conceptual implications. These adaptations may treat the stairwell as explicitly endless or as a deliberate trap, whereas the canonical article maintains uncertainty.
Unknown: There is no officially sanctioned "revised" or alternate canonical version of SCP-087 beyond the SCP Wiki article and its internal documents; any differences in fan sources reflect interpretation rather than competing canonical texts.

Archive Dossier — Powers

Non-Euclidean spatial extension

Source Version Canonical SCP Wiki article (Description; Exploration IV transcript)

Description

Attested: SCP-087’s staircase extends far beyond the physical limits of the building and surrounding geology. Exploration IV records a depth of descent that is "far beyond both the possible structure of both the building and geological surroundings," and the article states that it is unknown whether SCP-087 has an endpoint. This suggests that the stairwell either folds space in a non-Euclidean manner or connects to an extradimensional region, effectively creating an anomalously long vertical passage.

Limitations

Unknown: The exact spatial mechanics—whether the stairwell loops, branches, or connects to another dimension—are not specified. The stairwell appears to maintain consistent geometry (13 steps per flight, 3-meter platforms, 180-degree turns), but the total number of landings and the ultimate depth remain undetermined.

Contradictions

Some fan descriptions refer to SCP-087 as "infinite," while the canonical article only states that its depth is unknown and beyond normal structural limits. The term "infinite" is interpretive rather than attested.

Light absorption and visual limitation

Source Version Canonical SCP Wiki article (Description)

Description

Attested: SCP-087 absorbs excess light from sources brighter than approximately 75 watts. The article notes that lighting sources brighter than 75 watts have proven ineffective, as SCP-087 "seems to absorb excess light." Regardless of the light source used, subjects are limited to a visual range of about 1.5 flights of stairs. This creates a persistent darkness and severely restricts situational awareness.

Limitations

Attested: Light sources below 75 watts still function but do not extend visibility beyond the 1.5-flight limit. The absorption effect appears localized to the stairwell interior and does not affect the exterior environment.

Contradictions

Fan adaptations sometimes depict brighter lights temporarily revealing more of the stairwell or SCP-087-1, but this is not supported by the original article.

Persistent auditory lure (crying child)

Source Version Canonical SCP Wiki article (Description; exploration transcripts)

Description

Attested: Subjects within SCP-087 consistently hear distressed vocalizations from what is presumed to be a child between unspecified ages. Audio recordings confirm these vocalizations. The source of the sound is estimated to be approximately 200 meters below the initial platform, but attempts to descend toward it never reduce the apparent distance or volume; the crying remains at a constant level, suggesting an anomalous auditory phenomenon rather than a physically located child.

Limitations

Attested: The sound is only reported within SCP-087 and does not appear to propagate outside the stairwell. The article does not confirm the existence of an actual child, and SCP-087-1 is explicitly stated not to be the source of the pleading.

Contradictions

Some fan interpretations treat the crying child as a trapped victim or as an aspect of SCP-087-1, but the canonical text maintains uncertainty and distinguishes the two phenomena.

Psychological distress and paranoia induction

Source Version Canonical SCP Wiki article (Description; Documents 087-I–IV)

Description

Attested: Subjects exploring SCP-087 report intense feelings of paranoia and fear, especially after encountering SCP-087-1. The exploration transcripts document escalating anxiety, panic, and in some cases screaming and uncontrolled flight back up the stairs. The environment’s darkness, endless descent, and the presence of the crying child contribute to this distress, and SCP-087-1’s sudden appearance at close range produces extreme terror.

Limitations

Unknown: The article does not explicitly classify SCP-087 or SCP-087-1 as cognitohazards, nor does it quantify the psychological effects beyond narrative description. It is unclear whether the distress is purely situational or enhanced by anomalous influence.

Contradictions

Fan sources sometimes label SCP-087-1 as a "cognitohazard" or mind-affecting entity, but this terminology is not used in the original article.

SCP-087-1: fear induction and anomalous manifestation

Source Version Canonical SCP Wiki article (Description; Figure B; Documents 087-I–IV)

Description

Attested: SCP-087-1 appears as a face with opaque white eyes, no visible pupils, nostrils, or mouth. It manifests suddenly, often in mid-air at or near a landing, and remains fixed while the subject reacts. Every recorded exploration encounters SCP-087-1 at least once. The face’s appearance reliably triggers intense fear and panic in subjects, leading to abrupt termination of explorations as they flee upward. The article states that the nature of SCP-087-1 is entirely unclear but confirms that it is not the source of the crying.

Limitations

Attested: SCP-087-1 does not physically attack subjects in the recorded explorations; its effect is psychological rather than directly physical. Its mobility, origin, and persistence are unknown.

Contradictions

Some derivative works depict SCP-087-1 as capable of physical harm or pursuit, but these abilities are not attested in the canonical text.

Knocking phenomenon at the entrance

Source Version Canonical SCP Wiki article (Addendum)

Description

Attested: Following Exploration IV, staff and students on the campus report knocking sounds coming from the interior of SCP-087, at a variable rate of 1–2 seconds per knock. The door is subsequently padded with 6 cm of industrial foam, and reports of knocking cease. This suggests an anomalous ability of SCP-087 or an associated entity to produce rhythmic impact sounds at the boundary between the stairwell and the external environment.

Limitations

Attested: The knocking is only reported for a two-week period after Exploration IV and stops after the door is padded. The source of the knocking is not identified.

Contradictions

Fan interpretations sometimes link the knocking to SCP-087-1 or to the crying child, but the article does not specify any connection.

Archive Dossier — Relationships

SCP-087-1

Type hostileSource SCP-087 article (Description; Figure B; Documents 087-I–IV)

Description

SCP-087-1 is a disembodied face that appears within SCP-087. It is consistently encountered by subjects during explorations and induces intense fear and panic. While it does not physically attack subjects in the recorded narratives, its presence is treated as a hostile or threatening phenomenon due to its psychological impact.

Version Note

Canonical SCP Wiki version distinguishes SCP-087-1 from SCP-087 and notes that it is not the source of the crying.

Conflicting Accounts

Some fan sources conflate SCP-087 and SCP-087-1 or attribute additional abilities (e.g., physical attacks) to the face, but these are not supported by the canonical article.

SCP Foundation

Type creatorSource SCP-087 article (Special Containment Procedures; Addendum)

Description

Within the fictional universe, the SCP Foundation is the organization that discovers, contains, and documents SCP-087. It constructs the reinforced doorway, disguises it as a janitorial closet, and enforces access restrictions. In meta terms, the SCP Foundation community is the collective author of the SCP-087 narrative.

Version Note

Canonical SCP Wiki version; fan wikis reproduce this relationship.

Conflicting Accounts

No significant contradictions; all versions agree that the Foundation is responsible for SCP-087’s containment.

Class-D personnel

Type victimSource SCP-087 article (Documents 087-I–IV)

Description

Class-D subjects are used by the SCP Foundation to explore SCP-087. They descend the stairwell, experience psychological distress, and encounter SCP-087-1. Their role is instrumental and expendable, and they bear the brunt of the anomaly’s effects.

Version Note

Canonical SCP Wiki version; secondary sources summarize their experiences.

Conflicting Accounts

Fan retellings sometimes personalize or rename the Class-D subjects, but the original article keeps them anonymous.

Unnamed university campus

Type locationSource SCP-087 article (Special Containment Procedures; Addendum)

Description

SCP-087 is located on the campus of an unnamed institution. The doorway is integrated into the building’s design and disguised as a janitorial closet. Staff and students report knocking from the stairwell after Exploration IV, indicating an indirect relationship between the anomaly and the campus population.

Version Note

Canonical SCP Wiki version; the campus remains [REDACTED] and unnamed.

Conflicting Accounts

Some fan sources speculate about the campus’s identity or place SCP-087 in specific real-world locations, but these identifications are not canonical.

SCP-3008 ("A Perfectly Normal, Regular Old IKEA")

Type thematicSource SCP Database and fan discussions referencing both SCP-087 and SCP-3008 as spatial anomalies

Description

SCP-3008 is another SCP location anomaly involving a non-Euclidean interior space (an endless IKEA store). While there is no direct narrative connection between SCP-087 and SCP-3008 in canonical texts, they are often discussed together in fandom as examples of large-scale spatial anomalies and liminal horror.

Version Note

The relationship is thematic and fan-constructed rather than explicitly stated in the SCP-087 article.

Conflicting Accounts

No contradictions; the association is interpretive and not part of the canonical SCP-087 narrative.

Who It Is

You stand at a janitor’s closet that isn’t a closet at all—behind the reinforced door, a stairwell drops into darkness, a child’s sobbing echoing from somewhere that never gets closer no matter how many landings you count. SCP-087 is horror stripped to its bare geometry: thirteen steps, a three-meter platform, a 180-degree turn, repeated until the building’s foundations and the planet’s crust should have run out long ago. Somewhere in that impossible shaft, a pale face with no mouth and opaque white eyes waits at the edge of your flashlight’s reach, not attacking, not speaking, just watching until you break and run. This dossier follows that descent, not to find a bottom—there may not be one—but to map how a single staircase became one of the internet’s most enduring architectures of fear.

Attested: SCP-087, as an architectural anomaly, does not exhibit personality in the conventional sense. Its "behavior" is expressed through spatial and physical anomalies: it appears to extend far beyond the physical limits of the building and surrounding geology; it absorbs excess light from sources above approximately 75 watts; and it maintains a constant auditory illusion of a distressed child crying from somewhere below the subject, estimated at around 200 meters beneath the initial platform but never actually approached despite extensive descent. Non-Euclidean spatial extension

Attested: SCP-087’s staircase extends far beyond the physical limits of the building and surrounding geology. Exploration IV records a depth of descent that is "far beyond both the possible structure of both the building and geological surroundings," and the article states that it is unknown whether SCP-087 has an endpoint. This suggests that the stairwell either folds space in a non-Euclidean manner or connects to an extradimensional region, effectively creating an anomalously long vertical passage.

Limit: Unknown: The exact spatial mechanics—whether the stairwell loops, branches, or connects to another dimension—are not specified. The stairwell appears to maintain consistent geometry (13 steps per flight, 3-meter platforms, 180-degree turns), but the total number of landings and the ultimate depth remain undetermined.

Light absorption and visual limitation

Attested: SCP-087 absorbs excess light from sources brighter than approximately 75 watts. The article notes that lighting sources brighter than 75 watts have proven ineffective, as SCP-087 "seems to absorb excess light." Regardless of the light source used, subjects are limited to a visual range of about 1.5 flights of stairs. This creates a persistent darkness and severely restricts situational awareness.

Limit: Attested: Light sources below 75 watts still function but do not extend visibility beyond the 1.5-flight limit. The absorption effect appears localized to the stairwell interior and does not affect the exterior environment.

Persistent auditory lure (crying child)

Attested: Subjects within SCP-087 consistently hear distressed vocalizations from what is presumed to be a child between unspecified ages. Audio recordings confirm these vocalizations. The source of the sound is estimated to be approximately 200 meters below the initial platform, but attempts to descend toward it never reduce the apparent distance or volume; the crying remains at a constant level, suggesting an anomalous auditory phenomenon rather than a physically located child.

Limit: Attested: The sound is only reported within SCP-087 and does not appear to propagate outside the stairwell. The article does not confirm the existence of an actual child, and SCP-087-1 is explicitly stated not to be the source of the pleading.

Psychological distress and paranoia induction

Attested: Subjects exploring SCP-087 report intense feelings of paranoia and fear, especially after encountering SCP-087-1. The exploration transcripts document escalating anxiety, panic, and in some cases screaming and uncontrolled flight back up the stairs. The environment’s darkness, endless descent, and the presence of the crying child contribute to this distress, and SCP-087-1’s sudden appearance at close range produces extreme terror.

Limit: Unknown: The article does not explicitly classify SCP-087 or SCP-087-1 as cognitohazards, nor does it quantify the psychological effects beyond narrative description. It is unclear whether the distress is purely situational or enhanced by anomalous influence.

SCP-087-1: fear induction and anomalous manifestation

Attested: SCP-087-1 appears as a face with opaque white eyes, no visible pupils, nostrils, or mouth. It manifests suddenly, often in mid-air at or near a landing, and remains fixed while the subject reacts. Every recorded exploration encounters SCP-087-1 at least once. The face’s appearance reliably triggers intense fear and panic in subjects, leading to abrupt termination of explorations as they flee upward. The article states that the nature of SCP-087-1 is entirely unclear but confirms that it is not the source of the crying.

Limit: Attested: SCP-087-1 does not physically attack subjects in the recorded explorations; its effect is psychological rather than directly physical. Its mobility, origin, and persistence are unknown.

Knocking phenomenon at the entrance

Attested: Following Exploration IV, staff and students on the campus report knocking sounds coming from the interior of SCP-087, at a variable rate of 1–2 seconds per knock. The door is subsequently padded with 6 cm of industrial foam, and reports of knocking cease. This suggests an anomalous ability of SCP-087 or an associated entity to produce rhythmic impact sounds at the boundary between the stairwell and the external environment.

Limit: Attested: The knocking is only reported for a two-week period after Exploration IV and stops after the door is padded. The source of the knocking is not identified.

Accounts I
SCP-087: Initial containment and descriptionSCP-087 article main body (Special Containment Procedures; Description; Addendum)

Series I era of SCP Foundation (late 2000s to early 2010s)

The SCP Foundation discovers and contains SCP-087, an anomalous stairwell located on a university campus. The doorway is disguised as a janitorial closet and secured with reinforced steel, an electro-release lock, and internal padding. The article describes the stairwell’s geometry, its lack of lighting, its absorption of excess light, and the persistent sound of a crying child from below. It notes that four explorations have been conducted by Class-D personnel and that each has encountered SCP-087-1, a disturbing face that appears within the stairwell. Following the final exploration and subsequent knocking phenomena, access to SCP-087 is prohibited. SCP-087 is classified as Euclid and placed under strict containment. The Foundation bans further exploration due to the risks and unknown nature of the anomaly. The stairwell becomes a reference point within the SCP universe for non-Euclidean locations and psychological horror.

Document 087-I: Exploration ISCP-087 article, section "Document #087-I: Exploration I"

Series I era

A Class-D subject equipped with a handheld video camera and a light source descends SCP-087. The subject reports hearing a child crying from below and attempts to reach the source. As the subject continues downward, the crying remains at a constant volume, and the stairwell appears to extend far beyond expectations. At one landing, the subject encounters SCP-087-1, the disembodied face, which appears suddenly in the darkness. The subject screams, panics, and flees back up the stairs, ending the exploration. The Foundation confirms the presence of SCP-087-1 and the persistent crying sound. The exploration establishes the basic experiential pattern of SCP-087: endless descent, constant auditory lure, and sudden face encounter leading to panic.

Accounts II
Document 087-II: Exploration IISCP-087 article, section "Document #087-II: Exploration II"

Series I era

A second Class-D subject descends SCP-087 with similar equipment. The subject again hears the crying child and attempts to reach it. At the 51st landing, a censored event occurs (marked [DATA EXPUNGED] in the transcript), suggesting a disturbing or classified incident. The subject continues downward but eventually encounters SCP-087-1 again, leading to panic and retreat. The exploration reinforces the stairwell’s anomalous depth and the recurring presence of SCP-087-1. The Foundation gains further evidence of SCP-087’s non-Euclidean nature and the psychological impact on subjects. The censored event at the 51st landing hints at additional, possibly more severe phenomena within the stairwell.

Document 087-III: Exploration IIISCP-087 article, section "Document #087-III: Exploration III"

Series I era

A third Class-D subject descends SCP-087, reaching at least the 89th landing. The subject’s psychological state deteriorates as the descent continues, with increasing fatigue, fear, and disorientation. The crying child remains at a constant volume, and the stairwell’s depth continues to defy expectations. SCP-087-1 appears again, causing further distress. The exploration ends with the subject returning to the surface, and the Foundation notes the cumulative psychological toll of repeated descents. The exploration demonstrates that extended exposure to SCP-087 exacerbates psychological distress and does not bring subjects closer to the source of the crying. It strengthens the case for limiting further exploration.

Document 087-IV: Exploration IVSCP-087 article, section "Document #087-IV: Exploration IV"

Series I era

The fourth exploration is the longest and most extreme. A Class-D subject descends hundreds of landings (the transcript references at least the 469th landing), with the crying child still sounding as if it is approximately 200 meters below. The subject experiences severe psychological strain, and portions of the transcript are expunged, indicating events too disturbing or classified to be publicly documented. The exploration ultimately leads the Foundation to conclude that SCP-087’s depth is far beyond the building’s structure and the surrounding geology, and that the risks of further exploration are unacceptable. Following Exploration IV, the Foundation prohibits further access to SCP-087. The knocking phenomenon at the door occurs in the subsequent two weeks, prompting additional padding and reinforcing the decision to seal the stairwell.

The Approach

In the neon-lit world of Japanese horse racing, Biko Pegasus was more than a name—he was a testament to the power of a single, brilliant sprint. Though his career was marked by more losses than wins, his legacy galloped far beyond the track, finding new life in the digital stables of a popular anime.

Description

Appearance

As a real horse, Biko Pegasus was a bay Thoroughbred with standard conformation. In Umamusume, he is depicted as an anthropomorphic horse girl with a sleek, athletic build and a determined expression.

Symbols & Iconography

In Umamusume, his design incorporates racing silks and a headband with his name, often associated with speed and agility.

Behaviour & Personality

As a racehorse, he was known for his late-running style, often closing from behind. In Umamusume, he is portrayed as energetic, competitive, and somewhat impulsive.

Domains & Habitats

Real: Japanese racetracks. Fictional: the world of Umamusume, a school for horse girls.

Version Differences

The real horse is a non-anthropomorphic animal; the fictional version is a humanoid character with horse ears and tail.

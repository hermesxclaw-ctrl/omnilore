Who It Is

At full gallop across the mangroves of Sabaody, a buffalo the size of a small truck tears through the underbrush with an iron-masked outlaw on his back — not a monster, not a war-beast, but Motobaro, the devoted, easily-spooked bison who turned a revenge story into one of One Piece's most unexpectedly tender partnerships.

Attested personality: docile, loyal, and notably timid despite enormous strength. Motobaro is deeply bonded to Duval, obeying him without coercion and showing distress when Duval is hurt or humiliated. He is easily frightened — most famously terrified of Sanji after witnessing Sanji's kick that reshaped Duval's face (Chapter 492), causing him to tremble, sweat, and refuse to approach Sanji. Powers
Immense Physical Strength and Charging Power

As a giant bison, Motobaro can charge at high speed, trampling opponents and smashing through trees and Flying Fish Rider formations. His charge was sufficient to threaten the Straw Hat crew before they countered, and to carry the armored Duval plus additional riders. He can also drag or push large rafts and the Thousand Sunny's mooring chains in the guard-duty flashback context.

Limit: Not a combat specialist; strength is purely natural animal force. Easily startled and will halt or flee if frightened (especially by Sanji). No ranged attacks, no Devil Fruit, no Haki. Requires rider direction for tactical use. Stamina not quantified; appears to tire after prolonged gallop.

Enhanced Durability and Load-Bearing

Can bear Duval's weight plus armor and saddle, plus additional passengers, and continue running after collisions. Survives being caught in the crossfire of Straw Hat combat without serious injury.

Limit: Not invulnerable; would be injured by bladed or gunfire attacks like any large mammal. No armament or natural armor beyond thick hide and skull.

Aquatic Ferrying (via Flying Fish Assistance)

While not aquatic himself, Motobaro is shown operating in the Rosy Life Riders' amphibious tactics, being transported over water by giant Flying Fish. This gives the unit combined land-sea mobility.

Limit: Cannot swim long distances or breathe underwater; wholly dependent on Flying Fish for water traversal. Depicted as comedic rather than realistic.

Acute Hearing and Sense of Smell (Inferred Animal Traits)

As a bison, implied keen senses; he reacts to distant shouts and to Sanji's presence before visual contact, suggesting auditory/olfactory alertness. Not explicitly stated as a power.

Limit: Inferred, not attested as supernatural. Flagged as reconstructed.

Accounts I
Debut: The Flying Fish Riders AttackOne Piece, Vol. 51, Chapter 491 'Tobiuo Riders' / Anime Episode 388

2008 (publication); in-story: May 1522, Sabaody Archipelago

Duval, leader of the Flying Fish Riders (later Rosy Life Riders), seeks revenge on Sanji for Sanji's wanted poster resembling his face, which ruined his life. He rides Motobaro at the head of a kidnapping/trapping operation targeting the Straw Hat Pirates, who have just arrived at Sabaody. Motobaro charges the crew on the mangrove coast, scattering them and demonstrating his size and speed. The Straw Hats initially mistake the attack for a random monster assault until Duval reveals his iron mask and grudge. Establishes Motobaro as Duval's mount and the Riders' land asset; sets up Sanji vs Duval confrontation; introduces Rosy Life Riders as antagonists who will become allies.

Sanji's Kick Reconstructs Duval's Face — Motobaro's TerrorOne Piece, Vol. 51, Chapter 492 'Iron Mask Duval' / Anime Episode 389

2008; in-story: same day, Sabaody

Sanji confronts Duval and, in a gag that becomes canon, delivers a spinning kick to Duval's face that miraculously rearranges his bone structure, making him handsome by removing the resemblance to Sanji's poorly-drawn wanted poster. Duval is ecstatic; Motobaro, witnessing the brutal, lightning-fast kick at close range, becomes permanently traumatized by Sanji. From this point on, Motobaro trembles, sweats, and refuses to approach Sanji, even when Duval orders him. The scene cements Motobaro's timid personality despite his bulk. Duval's character turn from vengeance to gratitude; Motobaro's lasting phobia of Sanji becomes a running gag and character trait; alliance trigger.

Alliance and Rescue: Rosy Life Riders Aid the Straw HatsOne Piece, Vol. 51, Chapters 494-495 / Anime Episodes 390-391

2008; in-story: Sabaody, hours later

Reformed and now handsome, Duval renames his gang the Rosy Life Riders (after Sanji's kindness) and pledges loyalty to the Straw Hats. Motobaro, now with the allied faction, helps ferry the crew, carries Duval alongside the Flying Fish, and participates in the rescue of Camie the mermaid from the Macro Fish-Man kidnapping gang and later from the Human Auction House. Motobaro is seen galloping through Grove 1's auction environs, carrying Duval as they provide transport and distraction while Luffy, Zoro, and Sanji assault the auction house and the Celestial Dragons incident unfolds. Full face-turn for Motobaro's faction; establishes them as Sabaody allies; sets up their later role as Thousand Sunny guardians; demonstrates Motobaro's utility beyond combat — transport and rescue.

Accounts II
The Sabaody Separation — Motobaro Watches the Straw Hats FallOne Piece, Vol. 52, Chapters 498-513 / Anime Episodes 400-405

2008-2009; in-story: Sabaody Archipelago climax

After the Celestial Dragon incident, Admiral Kizaru, Sentomaru, and the Pacifistas attack Sabaody. The Rosy Life Riders and Motobaro are present at the periphery, attempting to assist but overwhelmed by the Marine onslaught. They witness Bartholomew Kuma's separation of the Straw Hat crew — each member being sent flying to different islands. Motobaro and Duval are unable to intervene against Kuma/Pacifista, underscoring the power gap. They remain on Sabaody as the crew disappears. Narrative bridge to timeskip; explains why Rosy Life Riders remain on Sabaody and become custodians of the Thousand Sunny.

Two Years Guarding the Thousand SunnyOne Piece, Vol. 61, Chapters 603-605 'Keep It In Your Heart' / Anime Episodes 512-513

2011 (publication); in-story: 1520-1522 (two-year timeskip, flashback)

In a flashback narrated by Hatchan and Duval, it is revealed that after the crew's separation, Duval and the Rosy Life Riders — with Motobaro as their steadfast land mount — honored their debt by guarding the Thousand Sunny at Grove 13 for two full years, protecting it from pirates, Marines, and opportunists seeking the Straw Hats' ship. Motobaro is shown patrolling the coastline, grazing near the ship's coating, and standing with Duval when Rayleigh and later Franky's allies arrive. When the Straw Hats finally return, Motobaro is present for the reunion, still healthy and still terrified of Sanji, providing a comedic beat when Sanji reappears. Cements Motobaro's loyalty and narrative payoff; explains how the Sunny survived two years intact; completes his arc from enemy mount to trusted guardian.

Post-Timeskip Cameo: Sabaody Reunion and DepartureOne Piece, Vol. 61, Chapter 605-607 / Anime Episode 517

2011; in-story: 1522, Straw Hat reunion

At the Straw Hats' return and subsequent departure for Fish-Man Island, Motobaro appears in crowd shots with the Rosy Life Riders bidding farewell. He carries Duval as the Riders help create a diversion to allow the Straw Hats to dive. His final pre-Fish-Man Island appearance is a brief but affectionate send-off, bellowing as the coated Sunny submerges. Closes Motobaro's Sabaody arc; no further manga appearances to date beyond cover stories and crowd cameos — character enters semi-retirement as Sabaody guardian.

Motobaro is a Devil Fruit user or a human transformed into a bison. — Motobaro is a normal (albeit giant) bison animal companion. He has no Devil Fruit, no human speech, and no transformation sequence. Vivre Card explicitly lists him as animal, species Bison, with no Devil Fruit.

Motobaro is Duval's pet horse or a bull from Fish-Man Island. — He is a bison affiliated with Sabaody Archipelago's Rosy Life Riders, not a horse and not from Fish-Man Island. His habitat is the Sabaody mangroves, and his species is bison per databook.

Motobaro is a major fighter who defeated members of the Straw Hat crew. — Motobaro never defeats any Straw Hat; his charge is stopped, and he is quickly neutralized as a threat. His narrative role is transport, comedy (fear of Sanji), and later guard duty, not combat victory.

Motobaro appears in the live-action One Piece Season 2. — As of 2026, Motobaro has not appeared in Netflix's live-action One Piece (Season 1 covered East Blue; Season 2 in production).

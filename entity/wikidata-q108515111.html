Who It Is

On the lawless mangroves of Sabaody Archipelago, where kidnappers sell mermaids and Flying Fish skim the sea like sharks, a masked biker charges on a bison the size of a small truck. That bison is Motobaro — Duval's devoted, headbutt-happy steed, whose most dangerous attack is an over-affectionate nuzzle.

Attested behavior across Ch. 494-498: initially aggressive on command — charges on Duval's order, attempts to trample enemies. After being intimidated/tamed by Monkey D. Luffy's Conqueror's-adjacent presence and later exposure to Sanji, Motobaro is shown as gentle, affectionate, and somewhat clumsy. Key trait: extreme fondness for Sanji's face (which resembles Duval's former disfigured face) — he repeatedly nuzzles, licks, and crushes Sanji with affection, to Sanji's distress. Name & Tongue

Etymology — Japanese モトバロ (Motobaro). No official etymology published by Shueisha, Oda, or Toei as of cutoff. Fan etymologies propose moto- (motor) + baro (from Spanish toro/bison or Japanese baro as name suffix) but these are unattested and not endorsed by the creator. Listed as unknown / unverified.

Pronunciation — Japanese: mo-to-ba-ro [mo.to.ba.ɾo], four morae, flat pitch; English dub: moh-toh-BAH-roh

Aliases — Motobaro the Bison

Ownership — Fictional animal character created by Eiichiro Oda for One Piece, serialized in Weekly Shōnen Jump (Shueisha, Japan) since 1997. Intellectual property owned by Eiichiro Oda / Shuei

Powers
Superhuman Bison Strength and Charge

Motobaro can charge at high speed and slam into multiple adults, knocking them aside. His charge is strong enough to dent ground and shatter wooden barriers in the Flying Fish Riders' base. Used as Duval's primary offensive tactic.

Limit: Requires running start; can be stopped by significantly stronger fighters (Luffy halts him with one hand). Not shown to break steel or Seastone. Vulnerable to being tipped or frightened.

Enhanced Durability and Stamina

Withstands being punched, intimidated by Luffy's glare, and continues to function. Can carry Duval (estimated 100+ kg with gear) for extended rides across Sabaody without fatigue shown.

Limit: Not bulletproof or invulnerable; shows fear and pain. No Haki or Armament. Can be exhausted or scared into immobility.

Trained Mount and Transport

Fully broken to saddle and bridle; responds to Duval's verbal and rein commands, can navigate mangrove terrain, and can be loaded with goods for delivery work post-timeskip.

Limit: Requires rider or handler; not autonomous combatant. Cannot operate Flying Fish or bubble vehicles.

Acute Sense of Smell / Face Recognition (Affection Response)

Shows immediate, intense affection toward Sanji due to facial resemblance to Duval's former face. Nuzzles and licks Sanji obsessively, indicating recognition of facial features/scent.

Limit: Comic effect; not a combat ability. Causes Motobaro to ignore commands when distracted by Sanji. No evidence of tracking or hunting skill.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Motobaro is a very large bison (baison) — quadrupedal, heavily muscled, with dark brown to black shaggy fur, a pronounced shoulder hump, short curved horns, and a broad snout. Oda draws him at roughly 2-3x the height of an adult human at the shoulder, large enough for Duval to ride astride with a custom saddle and reins. He wears a studded saddle, bridle, and often a decorative cloth. In manga black-and-white, fur is rendered with dense hatching;

Symbols — Visual shorthand for Duval's 'biker gang' aesthetic: Motobaro functions as a living motorcycle. His pairing with Duval's iron mask, leather jacket, and Flying Fish mirrors creates a parody of Japanese bōsōzoku (biker) culture transposed onto Sabaody's lawless groves. The bison's bulk contrasts with the sleek Flying Fish, emphasizing land vs sea mobility. No religious or folkloric iconography; purely franchise-specifi

Domains — Habitat is Sabaody Archipelago, specifically Grove 1 and surrounding lawless zones (mangrove forest, coastal waters). Travels with the Rosy Life Riders, a kidnapping/trafficking-adjacent gang turned ally group. Later habitat includes Sabaody's commercial delivery routes post-timeskip. Accounts I
Debut: The Rosy Life Riders Ambush at Sabaody Grove 1One Piece Chapter 494 'Duval' - Chapter 495 'Grove 1' (Vol. 50)

2008 (Sabaody Archipelago Arc, pre-timeskip)

The Straw Hats, searching for a coating mechanic to reach Fish-Man Island, encounter the masked Duval, leader of the Rosy Life Riders (formerly Flying Fish Riders). Duval, riding Motobaro, declares vengeance on the Straw Hats because their crewmate Sanji's wanted poster resembles his own formerly disfigured face, which caused his life of persecution. At Duval's command, Motobaro charges the crew, trampling Usopp and others, establishing the duo as the arc's first antagonists. The Flying Fish — giant airborne fish ridden by Duval's men — support from above while Motobaro provides ground assault. Introduces Sabaody's lawless mercenary culture and Duval's vendetta. Establishes Motobaro as Duval's signature mount and sets up the Sanji-face gag that will resolve the conflict.

The Taming: Luffy Stares Down the BisonOne Piece Chapter 495 'Grove 1' - Chapter 496 'Hatchan, Camie and Pappag' (Vol. 50)

2008

When Motobaro charges again, Luffy steps forward and unleashes a terrifying glare (often read as an early, uncontrolled burst of Conqueror's Haki, though not named as such at the time). Motobaro freezes, eyes widening, and collapses in fear, refusing to move despite Duval's shouts and kicks. This humiliates Duval and neutralizes his ground force, forcing Duval to rely on his Flying Fish riders. Sanji later notes the bison is now docile. Demonstrates Luffy's latent supreme-king presence and establishes Motobaro's cowardly/gentle true nature beneath intimidation. Shifts Motobaro from threat to comic animal.

Affection Assault: Motobaro Falls in Love with SanjiOne Piece Chapter 496-497 (Vol. 50)

2008

After Duval is unmasked and his backstory revealed — his face was brutally disfigured and identical to Sanji's wanted poster drawing, causing lifelong ostracism — Sanji arrives. Motobaro, who had been cowering, immediately perks up at Sanji's face, rushes over, and begins violently nuzzling, licking, and crushing Sanji with affection, mistaking Sanji's face for Duval's old beloved face. Sanji is disgusted and tries to kick Motobaro away, but Motobaro persists, providing extended slapstick. Comic resolution of Duval's tragedy; shows Motobaro's loyalty was to Duval's original face, and his affection transfers to Sanji as a surrogate. Humanizes Duval and Motobaro, paving the way for alliance.

Accounts II
Alliance: Rescue of Camie and Assault on the Human Auction HouseOne Piece Chapters 497-500 (Vol. 51)

2008

After Sanji (with help from the Straw Hats) brutally rearranges Duval's face with a series of kicks — ironically making Duval handsome — Duval and Motobaro switch sides. Grateful, Duval offers the Rosy Life Riders' services to the Straw Hats. Motobaro carries Duval as they lead Luffy, Hatchan, and Pappag on Flying Fish to Grove 1 where Camie the mermaid has been kidnapped for the Human Auction House. Motobaro is left at the Riders' base with other members while the main rescue proceeds to the auction, but his riders provide transport and later help evacuate the wounded Hatchan. Motobaro's faction becomes an ally network for the Straw Hats on Sabaody, crucial for mobility in the archipelago. Establishes Duval/Motobaro as recurring Sabaody allies rather than one-off villains.

Aftermath and Handsome Duval: Motobaro's Reaction to the New FaceOne Piece Chapter 498 'The Eleven Supernovas' - Chapter 500 (Vol. 51)

2008

With Duval's face now surgically altered by Sanji's kicks into a handsome, chiseled visage, Motobaro initially fails to recognize his master and recoils, then after sniffing, accepts the new face and nuzzles Duval happily. Duval, now calling himself 'Handsome Duval,' rides Motobaro proudly as they assist the Straw Hats' escape after the Celestial Dragon incident and Marine intervention. The visual gag closes the arc: Motobaro now loves both Sanji and the new Duval. Completes Duval's redemption arc; Motobaro's acceptance signals that loyalty transcends appearance. Sets up post-timeskip status quo.

Post-Timeskip Return: From the Decks of the World Cover SerialOne Piece Chapter 625 Cover Story 'From the Decks of the World' Vol. 25 and Chapter 603 'Keep Your Grudges'

2011 (Fish-Man Island Saga, two years later in-universe)

In the cover story montage showing the world after the Summit War, the Rosy Life Riders are shown running a successful delivery and taxi service on Sabaody Archipelago, with Duval riding Motobaro while delivering parcels and escorting travelers. In Chapter 603, when the Straw Hats reunite on Sabaody after two years, Duval and Motobaro (with the Riders) appear again to help hold off the fake Straw Hats and Marines, providing ground support and transport. Motobaro is shown healthy and still partnered with Duval, confirming long-term survival. Confirms Motobaro as a persistent Sabaody ally and shows economic reintegration of former kidnappers into legitimate work. Provides closure for Sabaody residents' arc.

Motobaro is a Devil Fruit user or transformed human. — Motobaro is a normal animal (bison) with no Devil Fruit ability. No transformation is ever shown or stated. One Piece does have animal Devil Fruit users (e.g., Chopper), but Motobaro is not one.

Motobaro hates Sanji and attacks him. — Primary evidence shows the opposite: Motobaro loves Sanji's face and attacks him with affection (nuzzling/licking), not hostility. He charges the Straw Hats only on Duval's order before being tamed.

Motobaro is Duval's pet bull named after a motorcycle. — Official species is bison, not bull, and no official etymology links his name to 'motorcycle.' Name origin is unverified. He is a trained mount/partner, not a pet in the domestic sense, and functions as part of a biker gang parody.

Motobaro dies or disappears after Sabaody. — He survives and reappears post-timeskip in the 'From the Decks of the World' cover serial (Ch. 625) and Chapter 603 reunion, shown healthy and working with Duval's delivery service.

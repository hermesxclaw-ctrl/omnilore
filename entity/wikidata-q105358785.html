Etymology

The surname Yahagi (矢作) is a Japanese toponymic surname, derived from the Yahagi River (矢作川) in Aichi Prefecture, Japan. The name literally means 'arrow making' (矢 = arrow, 作 = making), referencing the historical production of arrows in the region. The given name Ran (蘭) is a Japanese name that can mean 'orchid' or 'indigo', among other meanings, depending on the kanji used. The combination suggests a modern Japanese fictional character name, but no canonical etymology has been verified.

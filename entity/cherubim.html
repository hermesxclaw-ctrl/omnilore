Who It Is
Form

Cherubim are often depicted as hybrid creatures with the body of a lion, the wings of an eagle, the face of a man, and the tail of a serpent. They are sometimes shown with multiple faces, each representing a different aspect of their nature.

Powers

Wisdom Cherubim are said to excel in wisdom and continually praise God.

Accounts

The story of the Fall of Man describes how Adam and Eve were expelled from the Garden of Eden by the cherubim.

The story of the cherubim and the Ark of the Covenant describes how the cherubim were placed on the Ark to serve as guardians of the covenant between God and the Israelites.

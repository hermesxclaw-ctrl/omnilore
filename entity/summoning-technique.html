Who It Is

In the hidden villages of the shinobi world, the Summoning Technique is more than a jutsu—it is a pact. A single hand seal, a drop of blood, and a contract sealed in ink can call forth a giant toad, a serpent, or a hawk from across dimensions. But this power is not free: it demands a bond, a price, and a will strong enough to command a beast that could just as easily devour its summoner.

The technique is a negotiation, not a mere spell. The summoned creature retains its free will and personality. The user must command respect; otherwise, the summon may refuse to cooperate or even turn on the user. The bond between summoner and summon is built on trust and mutual benefit.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The Japanese term 'Kuchiyose' (口寄せ) literally means 'calling forth' or 'invoking', often used in the context of spiritual summoning. 'Jutsu' (術) means 'technique' or 'art'.

Pronunciation — Koo-chee-yo-seh no Joot-soo

Aliases — Kuchiyose no Jutsu, Summoning Jutsu, Summoning, Space-Time Summoning, Animal Summoning Technique

Ownership — Created by Masashi Kishimoto for the 'Naruto' manga series, first published in 1999 by Shueisha. The technique is a central element of the Naruto universe's ninja combat system.

Powers
Summoning Contract

A blood contract signed with a specific animal clan allows the user to summon any member of that clan, provided they have enough chakra and skill.

Limit: Requires a pre-existing contract, blood sacrifice, and enough chakra to summon a creature of the desired size.

Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation

A forbidden technique that summons the souls of the dead into a living vessel, effectively resurrecting them. This is a separate technique, but often confused with the basic Summoning Technique.

Limit: Requires a living sacrifice and a piece of the deceased's DNA. The resurrected are bound to the summoner's will.

Summoning: Rashomon Gates

A defensive summoning that calls forth massive, demonic gates to block attacks.

Limit: Requires a high level of chakra and is often used by Orochimaru.

Summoning: Living Doll

A technique used by Kankuro to summon his puppet, which is a combat tool rather than a living creature.

Limit: Requires a puppet and the user's chakra strings.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The technique itself is invisible, but its activation is marked by a puff of smoke and a swirl of chakra. The summoned creature appears from a portal of swirling energy, often accompanied by a burst of smoke. The user's hand traces a pattern of blood on their palm or a surface, which then glows with chakra.

Symbols — The summoning contract is a scroll with the user's name and blood signature. Each summoning clan has its own unique scroll, often adorned with clan symbols. The hand seals used are typically the Boar, Dog, Bird, Monkey, and Ram, though variations exist.

Domains — Summoned creatures come from various realms, often referred to as 'summoning realms' or 'animal realms'. These are separate dimensions where the creatures live in societies. For example, Mount Myoboku is the home of the toads, Ryuchi Cave is the home of snakes, and the Forest of Death is home to many creatures.

Accounts I
Naruto's First SummoningNaruto, Chapter 94

2001

During the battle with Gaara, Naruto is desperate to gain an advantage. He signs a contract with the toads and performs the Summoning Technique, but instead of the boss toad Gamabunta, he accidentally summons a small tadpole. After several attempts, he finally summons Gamabunta, who is initially uncooperative but eventually agrees to help Naruto fight Shukaku. This event establishes Naruto's bond with the toads and his ability to use the Summoning Technique.

Accounts II
The Summoning Contract with the SnakesNaruto, Chapter 69

2000

Orochimaru demonstrates his mastery of the Summoning Technique by summoning the giant snake Manda during the battle with the Third Hokage. Manda is fiercely independent and only obeys Orochimaru because of his strength. The battle results in the Third Hokage's death and Orochimaru's retreat.

Tsunade's Slug SummoningNaruto, Chapter 167

2003

Tsunade summons Katsuyu, the giant slug, to heal the villagers during Pain's attack on Konoha. Katsuyu can divide into thousands of smaller slugs to heal multiple people simultaneously. Katsuyu plays a crucial role in saving many lives and later helps Naruto by communicating with him.

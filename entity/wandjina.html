Who It Is
Form

The Wandjina are depicted in rock art as cloud and rain spirits, often with large eyes and a prominent nose. They are typically shown with a series of concentric circles or a spiral design on their bodies, which are believed to represent the clouds and rain.

Powers

Bringing of Rain The Wandjina are believed to have the power to bring rain and fertility to the land, ensuring the survival and prosperity of the people.

Accounts

The Wandjina and the Rainbow Serpent are two powerful spirits that are associated with the creation of the world and the bringing of rain and fertility.

The Wandjina are believed to have the power to bring rain and fertility to the land, and are associated with the ending of droughts and the bringing of prosperity.

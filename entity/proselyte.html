Who It Is

Proselyte is the indigo entity of compassion — the most paradoxical and unsettling of all the Emotional Entities. It appears as a massive, multi-tentacled creature with a central eye, draped in indigo energy, resembling a cross between a jellyfish, a squid, and an angel of mercy — beautiful, alien, and profoundly disquieting. The Indigo Tribe, also known as the Indigo Lanterns or the Compassion Corps, draws its power from Proselyte. But the Tribe's methods are controversial: they do not recruit the compassionate. They forcibly recruit the most evil beings in the universe — murderers, tyrants, genocidal warlords — and subject them to the indigo light, which forces them to feel the full weight of the suffering they have caused. The experience breaks them, replacing their cruelty with an overwhelming, often painful, sense of compassion for all life. Proselyte itself embodies this terrible mercy. It does not punish evil — it forces evil to understand the pain it has inflicted, and that understanding destroys the evil. The entity's power includes healing, empathy projection, teleportation across any distance (even into the Phantom Zone), temporary absorption of other ring powers, and the ability to create 'compassion bomb' effects that force entire populations to feel each other's pain. Proselyte has bonded with Natromo, the Indigo Tribe's founder; Munk, a former prisoner who became the Tribe's most devoted member; and during the Blackest Night, it briefly bonded with the Spectre (the embodiment of God's vengeance), creating a terrifying fusion of wrath and compassion. Proselyte represents the most radical idea in DC cosmology: that compassion is not a weakness to be learned, but a truth so powerful it can redeem even the most damned soul — by forcing them to see what they have done.

Who It Is

Goibniu is the master smith who forges weapons with three hammer strikes. He also brews a magical ale granting immortality at the Feast of Goibniu. His name derives from the Celtic root for 'smith,' representing the sacred status of metalworkers in Iron Age Celtic society.

Who It Is

A shape-shifting entity that mimics human appearance imperfectly, replacing individuals in Mandela County. The Intruder's primary form is a tall, gaunt figure with an unnaturally wide smile and eyes that are placed slightly too far apart — just enough to be wrong. It enters homes through television screens, appearing on emergency broadcasts and VHS tapes, and uses psychological manipulation to drive victims to suicide, at which point it can assume their identity. It is a subordinate of 'Gabriel,' the entity masquerading as the biblical archangel.

Form

Tall, emaciated humanoid with pale skin, eyes too far apart, and an impossibly wide, toothy smile that stretches across its face. In some manifestations, its face appears digitally corrupted or glitched, as though reality is struggling to render it. It manifests through analog media — particularly CRT televisions and VHS tapes.

Powers

Mimicry of human faces and voices (imperfectly)

Manipulation of analog media

Psychological warfare and suicide inducement

Identity replacement

Entry through screens and reflections

Cultural Significance

The analog horror series that defined the genre's 2021-2023 explosion. Its use of VHS distortion, uncanny face morphs, and biblical horror created a new visual language for internet horror.

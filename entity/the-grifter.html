Who It Is

A drifter finds a journal in an abandoned house documenting the previous occupant's slow descent into paranoia about 'The Grifter' — a tall, thin man who stands at the edge of the property, visible only through specific windows, always slightly closer than before. The journal documents months of approach, noting the Grifter's too-long arms, the impossibility of its head tilt. Final entry: 'He is at the bedroom window. He is smiling. He has no teeth — only a second, smaller face inside his mouth, and it is smiling too.' The drifter looks up.

Form

Described through the journal: tall (2.5m+) humanoid in a tattered dark coat, arms past its knees, head tilted at ~45 degrees. Gaunt gray face. Mouth open to reveal a second smaller face nested inside, with its own open mouth — recursive faces within faces.

Powers

Slow, inexorable approach over months

Visibility restricted to specific viewing angles

Psychological deterioration of observers

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

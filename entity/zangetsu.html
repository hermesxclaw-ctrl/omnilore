Who It Is
Form

Zangetsu appears in two primary forms. The first, often called 'Old Man Zangetsu,' is a tall, elderly man with long, flowing white hair, a prominent beard, and a weathered face. He wears a dark, tattered cloak with a high collar, and his eyes are often obscured by shadow, giving him an enigmatic and imposing presence. The second form, 'Hollow Zangetsu' or 'White,' is a younger, more feral version with black hair, a hollow mask fragment over his left eye, and a similar dark cloak, but with a more aggressive posture. When manifested as Tensa Zangetsu, the spirit takes on a younger, more streamlined appearance resembling the protagonist Ichigo Kurosaki, with short black hair and a calm, confident demeanor. In all forms, Zangetsu is often depicted holding a large, cleaver-like sword with a black blade and a red hilt, which is the physical manifestation of his power.

Powers

Zangetsu (Shikai) In its sealed form, Zangetsu appears as an oversized cleaver-like sword. When released, it becomes a black-bladed katana with a red hilt and a chain attached to the pommel. The release command is 'Ban-kai' (or 'Reap' in some translations). The shikai grants Ichigo superhuman speed, strength, and the ability to fire energy blasts called Getsuga Tenshō, which are concentrated spiritual energy waves shaped like a crescent moon.

Tensa Zangetsu (Bankai) The bankai form compresses Zangetsu's power into a smaller, more refined blade, resembling a black tanto with a short chain. This form dramatically increases Ichigo's speed and strength, and the Getsuga Tenshō becomes more focused and powerful, appearing as a black energy slash. The bankai also grants the ability to use 'Getsuga Jūjishō,' a cross-shaped energy attack.

Hollowfication When Zangetsu's Hollow aspect takes over, Ichigo gains a hollow mask and enhanced combat abilities, including increased speed, strength, and regeneration. This state is unstable and can lead to loss of control, but it allows Ichigo to access a more primal form of power.

Quincy Powers Later revealed that Old Man Zangetsu is actually a manifestation of Ichigo's Quincy heritage, granting him abilities like Blut Vene (defensive blood armor) and the potential to use Quincy techniques. This power is distinct from his Shinigami abilities and is often in conflict with them.

Accounts

Ichigo Kurosaki, a teenager who can see ghosts, becomes a Soul Reaper after a chance encounter with Rukia Kuchiki. During his first major battle against the Hollow Grand Fisher, Ichigo is overwhelmed and hears a voice calling to him. He enters his inner world and meets Zangetsu for the first time, an old man who offers him power to protect his friends. Zangetsu teaches Ichigo to fight and use his Zanpakutō, setting him on the path to becoming a powerful warrior.

During the Soul Society arc, Ichigo struggles to control his inner Hollow, which manifests as a white version of himself. Zangetsu, the old man, fights against this Hollow to protect Ichigo's mind. The battle takes place in Ichigo's inner world, where the two aspects of Zangetsu clash. Ultimately, Ichigo learns to accept both parts of himself, achieving a new level of power and gaining the ability to use his Hollow mask.

In the Thousand-Year Blood War arc, Ichigo learns that the old man he knew as Zangetsu is actually a manifestation of his Quincy powers, created by his father's side of the family. The true Zangetsu is the Hollow, who has been suppressed for years. Ichigo must confront both aspects and merge them to create a new, unified Zangetsu. This revelation forces Ichigo to redefine his identity and accept his mixed heritage.

Modern Interpretations

Manga Bleach Tite Kubo 2001-2016 Major character Zangetsu is widely regarded as one of the most iconic Zanpakutō spirits in the series, praised for his complex character design and the philosophical depth he brings to Ichigo's journey. Fans have debated his true nature for years, and his reveal as a Quincy manifestation was a major plot twist. Anime Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War Studio Pierrot 2022-2023 Supporting character The anime adaptation expanded Zangetsu's backstory, giving him more screen time and emotional depth. Critics noted that the anime improved upon the manga's pacing, and Zangetsu's scenes were highlighted as some of the best in the series. Video Game Bleach: Soul Resurrection Sony Computer Entertainment 2011 Playable character Zangetsu appears as a playable character in this action game, with a moveset based on his Getsuga Tenshō abilities. Fans appreciated the faithful adaptation of his powers, though the game received mixed reviews overall. Musical Rock Musical Bleach Pierrot 2005-2008 Character Zangetsu was portrayed by different actors in the stage musicals, with each interpretation bringing a unique flair. The musicals were popular among fans for their dramatic retelling of the story, though Zangetsu's role was often condensed.

Scholarly Debates
True Identity of Zangetsu
Old Man Zangetsu is the true spirit of the Zanpakutō, as initially presented in the series. ⇌ Old Man Zangetsu is actually a manifestation of Ichigo's Quincy powers, and the Hollow is the true spirit, as revealed in the final arc.
Power Source
Zangetsu's power is derived solely from Ichigo's Shinigami abilities. ⇌ Zangetsu's power is a combination of Shinigami, Hollow, and Quincy powers, with the Quincy aspect being the dominant force behind the Old Man's manifestation.
Appearance in the Inner World
Zangetsu always appears as an old man in Ichigo's inner world. ⇌ Zangetsu's appearance changes over time, and in the final arc, he appears as a younger version of Ichigo, indicating his evolution.
What We Don't Know

There is limited scholarly analysis of Zangetsu as a cultural artifact, with most discussions occurring in fan communities. The character's evolution across different media (manga, anime, games) has not been systematically compared. Additionally, the influence of Zangetsu on other fictional weapon spirits in anime and manga has not been studied. Further research could explore the psychological symbolism of Zangetsu's dual nature and its resonance with Japanese concepts of duality.

A way to hold it in your mind

Did you know that Zangetsu's name, when written as 斬月, literally means 'Slaying Moon,' but it is a homophone for 残月, meaning 'morning moon'? This linguistic twist hints at the character's hidden nature, which was only revealed after over a decade of storytelling.

This linguistic twist hints at the character's hidden nature, which was only revealed after over a decade of storytelling. Zangetsu's personality varies by form. Old Man Zangetsu is calm, wise, and paternal, often speaking in riddles and encouraging Ichigo to find his own strength. He is protective but also stern, testing Ichigo's resolve through harsh training. Hollow Zangetsu, in contrast, is aggressive, sadistic, and eager to fight, representing Ichigo's repressed instincts and desire for power. He often taunts Ichigo and seeks to take control, embodying the dangerous side of the Zanpakutō. Powers
Zangetsu (Shikai)

In its sealed form, Zangetsu appears as an oversized cleaver-like sword. When released, it becomes a black-bladed katana with a red hilt and a chain attached to the pommel. The release command is 'Ban-kai' (or 'Reap' in some translations). The shikai grants Ichigo superhuman speed, strength, and the ability to fire energy blasts called Getsuga Tenshō, which are concentrated spiritual energy waves shaped like a crescent moon.

Limit:

Tensa Zangetsu (Bankai)

The bankai form compresses Zangetsu's power into a smaller, more refined blade, resembling a black tanto with a short chain. This form dramatically increases Ichigo's speed and strength, and the Getsuga Tenshō becomes more focused and powerful, appearing as a black energy slash. The bankai also grants the ability to use 'Getsuga Jūjishō,' a cross-shaped energy attack.

Limit:

Hollowfication

When Zangetsu's Hollow aspect takes over, Ichigo gains a hollow mask and enhanced combat abilities, including increased speed, strength, and regeneration. This state is unstable and can lead to loss of control, but it allows Ichigo to access a more primal form of power.

Limit:

Quincy Powers

Later revealed that Old Man Zangetsu is actually a manifestation of Ichigo's Quincy heritage, granting him abilities like Blut Vene (defensive blood armor) and the potential to use Quincy techniques. This power is distinct from his Shinigami abilities and is often in conflict with them.

Accounts I
The First MeetingBleach manga, Chapter 66

2002

Ichigo Kurosaki, a teenager who can see ghosts, becomes a Soul Reaper after a chance encounter with Rukia Kuchiki. During his first major battle against the Hollow Grand Fisher, Ichigo is overwhelmed and hears a voice calling to him. He enters his inner world and meets Zangetsu for the first time, an old man who offers him power to protect his friends. Zangetsu teaches Ichigo to fight and use his Zanpakutō, setting him on the path to becoming a powerful warrior. Ichigo gains confidence and learns to wield Zangetsu, defeating Grand Fisher and beginning his journey as a Soul Reaper.

Accounts II
The Inner BattleBleach manga, Chapter 110-111

2004

During the Soul Society arc, Ichigo struggles to control his inner Hollow, which manifests as a white version of himself. Zangetsu, the old man, fights against this Hollow to protect Ichigo's mind. The battle takes place in Ichigo's inner world, where the two aspects of Zangetsu clash. Ultimately, Ichigo learns to accept both parts of himself, achieving a new level of power and gaining the ability to use his Hollow mask. Ichigo achieves a balance between his Shinigami and Hollow powers, unlocking his bankai and gaining the strength to rescue Rukia.

The True ZangetsuBleach manga, Chapter 542

2013

In the Thousand-Year Blood War arc, Ichigo learns that the old man he knew as Zangetsu is actually a manifestation of his Quincy powers, created by his father's side of the family. The true Zangetsu is the Hollow, who has been suppressed for years. Ichigo must confront both aspects and merge them to create a new, unified Zangetsu. This revelation forces Ichigo to redefine his identity and accept his mixed heritage. Ichigo gains a new Zanpakutō and a deeper understanding of his powers, allowing him to fight against the Quincy king Yhwach.

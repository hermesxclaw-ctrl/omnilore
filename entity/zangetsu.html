Who It Is

The manifested spirit of Ichigo Kurosaki's Zanpakutō. Zangetsu has two coexisting forms: the 'Old Man' Zangetsu, who represents Ichigo's Shinigami/Quincy powers and takes the form of Yhwach in his youth, and the 'Hollow' Zangetsu (White), a white-skinned, black-haired entity representing Ichigo's Hollow powers. True Zangetsu is the fusion of both — a black-clad swordsman with fused powers. Zangetsu's true bankai manifests as a white blade with a black edge and a chain linking to a smaller blade.

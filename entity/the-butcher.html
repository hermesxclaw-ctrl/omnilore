Who It Is

The Butcher is the primordial entity of rage — the red light of the Emotional Spectrum that burns hot with fury, vengeance, and unbridled violence. It is a massive, bull-like creature wreathed in crimson energy, with burning red eyes and horns that glow like embers. The Butcher embodies rage in its purest form: not the cold, calculated anger of revenge, but the hot, mindless, all-consuming fury that drives beings to destroy without thought or restraint. The Butcher resides within the Red Lantern Central Power Battery on the desolate planet Ysmault. When a being's rage reaches a critical mass, the Butcher may empower them as a Red Lantern — but at a terrible cost: the red ring burns away virtually all other emotions, leaving only anger, pain, and a burning desire for vengeance. Red Lanterns cannot speak (their constructs vomit blood and fire instead) and are in constant agony. The Butcher has bonded with several hosts: Atrocitus, the founder of the Red Lantern Corps, who channeled its rage into a crusade against those who failed to prevent the destruction of his sector; Bleez, a tortured Zamarons princess whose rage was honed into lethal focus; and Dex-Starr, a housecat whose grief and fury at the death of his owner transformed him into one of the most vicious Red Lanterns. The Butcher was briefly freed from the Battery during the Blackest Night event and possessed the Flash (Barry Allen), transforming him into a rage-fueled entity of destruction. The Butcher represents the most dangerous emotion in existence — the fire that cannot be reasoned with, negotiated with, or extinguished until it has burned everything, including its host.

Who It Is

An infinite office building, perpetually empty, with beige walls, gray cubicles, and the smell of stale coffee. Level 4 is unique among early Backrooms levels for being almost entirely safe — no entities have ever been confirmed. Vending machines dispense Almond Water and are restocked by an unseen presence. The catch: Level 4 was specifically designed as a refuge, and whoever maintains it has never been seen.

Form

An endless 1990s corporate office: beige cubicles, gray carpet, drop ceilings with fluorescent panels, windows looking out onto eternal twilight. Break rooms have functional vending machines. Some computers boot to: 'YOU ARE SAFE HERE. REST.'

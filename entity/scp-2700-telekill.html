Who It Is

A spear of unknown manufacture that kills anything it touches — absolutely and permanently. It erases targets from existence: no body, no evidence, no memory. The Foundation used it once to terminate an Apollyon-class threat. No one can remember what that threat was, or who deployed it, or what else was erased. The spear takes more than life. It takes meaning.

Form

A spear ~1.8m long, composed of obsidian-like material predating human tool use by 40,000 years. The blade is perpetually cold, even in vacuum.

Powers

Absolute deletion of any target

Retroactive existence erasure

Memory and record corruption of deleted targets

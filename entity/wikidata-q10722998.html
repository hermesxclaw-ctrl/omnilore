Who It Is

In the twilight of Mesoamerican myth, before the first dawn broke over the cornfields, there existed a pair of divine matchmakers whose names are whispered only in the oldest of tongues. Xpiyacoc, the grandfather of the gods, and his consort Xmucane, the divine midwife, wove the threads of fate and divination, ensuring that even the mighty Hero Twins could not escape the patterns they cast.

Xpiyacoc is portrayed as a wise, elderly figure who is consulted for his knowledge of fate and divination. He is cooperative with the gods, particularly with the creators Tepeu and Gucumatz, and later with the Hero Twins. He acts as a mediator and guide, using his divinatory powers to determine outcomes.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name Xpiyacoc is of K'iche' Maya origin. Its exact meaning is uncertain, but it may be related to the root 'piy' or 'piya' which could relate to 'to weave' or 'to twist', possibly referencing the weaving of fate. Alternatively, it may be a compound of 'x' (a prefix for feminine names) and 'piyac

Pronunciation — IPA approximation: /ʃpiˈjakok/ or /ʃpiˈjakok/. In K'iche', 'x' is pronounced as 'sh'. The 'c' is hard, as in 'cat'.

Aliases — Xpiyacoc, Xpiyacok, Ixpiyacoc, Xpiyacoc (grandfather), Xpiyacoc (divine matchmaker)

Ownership — K'iche' Maya, highland Guatemala. The Popol Vuh is the primary source, and the deities are part of the Pre-Columbian Maya pantheon.

Powers
Divination

Xpiyacoc, along with Xmucane, performs divination using lots (likely seeds or beans) to determine the fate of the maize dough used to create humans. He also divines the future of the Hero Twins, predicting their victories and trials.

Limit: His divination is not absolute; it reveals likely outcomes but does not control them. He can be deceived or the gods can intervene.

Creation Assistance

He participates in the creation of humans by advising the creator gods on the proper materials and rituals. He is specifically involved in the divination that ensures the maize dough becomes the flesh of humans.

Limit: He does not create alone but works with the creator gods.

Matchmaking

As a divine matchmaker, he is associated with arranging marriages and partnerships, though specific myths are not recorded. This role is inferred from his pairing with Xmucane and his general function as a mediator.

Limit: No direct myths depict him performing matchmaking.

Form & Signs

Appearance — No specific physical description is given in the Popol Vuh. Xpiyacoc is referred to as 'grandfather' (mam) and is likely depicted as an elderly male deity, possibly with features associated with aged gods, such as wrinkles and a hunched posture. In some contemporary Maya communities, he might be depicted in ritual attire, but this is not attested in ancient sources.

Symbols — Xpiyacoc is associated with divination, casting lots, and counting. His symbols may include the divining seeds (likely iztle or red beans), a staff, and possibly a censer for ritual smoke. He is also associated with the creation of humans, so maize and corn may be symbolic.

Domains — Xpiyacoc is a celestial deity, residing in the heavens or in a place associated with the gods. He is not tied to a specific earthly location. He operates in the realm of fate and divination, influencing the creation of humans and the destiny of the Hero Twins.

Accounts I
The Divination of the Maize DoughPopol Vuh, Part 2, Chapter 2

Pre-Columbian, likely Classic or Postclassic period

After the first attempts to create humans failed (using mud and wood), the creator gods Tepeu and Gucumatz consult Xpiyacoc and Xmucane to divine the correct materials. The pair use their divinatory skills to cast lots and determine that maize should be used. They then grind the maize and form the flesh of the first four humans. The creation of true humans, who are able to praise the gods and speak their names.

Accounts II
The Protection of the Hero TwinsPopol Vuh, Part 3, Chapter 1

Pre-Columbian

When the Hero Twins Hunahpu and Xbalanque are sent to the underworld Xibalba, their grandmother Xmucane and grandfather Xpiyacoc are consulted. They divine the outcome of the twins' journey, predicting their eventual victory over the Lords of Xibalba. They also help the twins by providing guidance and magical items. The twins are able to overcome the trials of Xibalba and defeat the death gods, eventually becoming the sun and moon.

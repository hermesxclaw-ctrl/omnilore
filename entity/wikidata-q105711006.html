Who It Is

In the chaotic, war-torn world of BlazBlue, few fighters are as instantly recognizable as Taokaka, the feline Kaka clan member whose boundless energy and voracious appetite mask a surprisingly sharp instinct for battle. With her bright orange hair, cat-like ears, and a perpetual grin, she bounces through the conflict like a force of nature, leaving a trail of confusion and laughter in her wake. But beneath the playful exterior lies a creature of formidable combat prowess, a guardian of her clan, and a figure whose simplicity belies a deeper complexity.

Taokaka is extremely energetic, playful, and naive. She speaks in a childlike manner, often referring to herself in the third person ('Tao') and using simple sentences. She is curious about the world outside her village and is easily distracted, especially by food. Despite her simple-mindedness, she is fiercely loyal to her clan and friends, and she possesses a strong sense of justice, albeit an unorthodox one. She is not malicious and often fails to understand complex situations, but she acts on instinct and is surprisingly perceptive in battle.

Powers
Cat-like agility and reflexes

Taokaka possesses enhanced speed, agility, and reflexes, allowing her to perform acrobatic feats and dodge attacks with ease. She can climb walls and pounce on opponents from a distance.

Limit: Her agility is not supernatural; she can be hit if caught off guard.

Drive: Dancing Edge

Taokaka's Drive ability allows her to enter a 'Dancing Edge' state, where her movements become even more erratic and fast, enabling her to perform rapid consecutive attacks. This is represented in game by her 'Drive' button, which triggers a unique attack pattern.

Limit: The state has a time limit and can be interrupted.

Astral Heat: Cat Spirit

Her Astral Heat move, 'Cat Spirit', is a powerful finishing attack that involves her summoning a giant spectral cat to maul the opponent, dealing massive damage.

Limit: Requires full Heat gauge and specific conditions.

Enhanced senses

As a Kaka, she has sharp senses, particularly hearing and smell, which she uses to track opponents and detect danger.

Limit: Not explicitly shown, but implied.

Claw attacks

She uses her sharp claws (despite wearing gloves) to slash opponents. In game, many of her normal attacks involve claw swipes.

Limit: Short range compared to some characters.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Taokaka is a young woman with fair skin, large amber eyes, and a perpetual cheerful expression. She has long, bright orange hair that resembles a lion's mane, with two prominent cat ears protruding from the top. She wears a black and white bodysuit that covers her entire body, with a white chest area and black limbs, and black boots. Her outfit includes a white collar and a distinctive tail that is also black and white. She often wears a red scar

Symbols — Her primary symbols are the cat ears and tail, representing her feline nature. The color orange is associated with her hair and personality, conveying energy and warmth. She is often depicted with fish or other food items, symbolizing her voracious appetite.

Domains — Taokaka lives in the Kaka Village, a hidden village in the mountains of the Eastern Empire. The village is home to the Kaka clan, a race of cat-like humans. She is also seen traveling to various locations, including the city of Kagutsuchi, during the events of the BlazBlue story.

Accounts I
Search for a Strong OpponentBlazBlue: Calamity Trigger story mode

2199 (fictional timeline)

Taokaka, bored with her peaceful life in the Kaka Village, leaves to find a strong opponent to fight. She encounters Ragna the Bloodedge and becomes obsessed with him, following him around and challenging him repeatedly. Her antics provide comic relief, but she also inadvertently helps him in some battles. She develops a friendly rivalry with Ragna and later becomes involved in the larger conflict.

She also interacts with other characters, such as Carl Clover and Relius Clover, and learns about the outside world's dangers. She becomes more aware of the threats facing the world and vows to protect her friends.

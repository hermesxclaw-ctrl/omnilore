Who It Is

In the shadow of a San Francisco hillside, a young girl named Emily Hayes discovers a mysterious amulet hidden in her great-grandfather's study. That single moment pulls her into a perilous quest to rescue her kidnapped mother, and transforms her into the next Stonekeeper—the wielder of a powerful, sentient talisman that could either save her family or doom an entire world.

As the Stonekeeper, Emily is initially hesitant and reluctant, but she grows into the role as she faces dangers to save her family. She shows bravery, resourcefulness, and compassion, even when tempted by the amulet's power. The amulet itself is manipulative and persuasive, urging Emily to use its power aggressively.

Powers
Amulet Empowerment

The amulet grants its wearer enhanced strength, the ability to repel magical attacks, and the power to command the amulet's energy for defensive or offensive purposes. It also provides telepathic communication and guidance.

Limit: The amulet's power is tied to the wearer's emotional state and willingness to embrace it. Overuse or reliance on the amulet can lead to corruption or loss of self. The amulet's guidance is often self-serving and may lead the wearer astray.

Resistance to Magic

When attacked by the elf prince's amulet, Emily is able to repel the attack using her own amulet, suggesting a form of magical resistance or counterattack.

Limit: This ability is only shown once and its scope is unclear.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Stonekeeper is identified by the wearing of the stone amulet, a circular pendant with a blue gem. The amulet glows and speaks telepathically to its wearer. Emily Hayes, the protagonist, is depicted as a young girl with dark hair, often shown with the amulet around her neck.

Symbols — The amulet itself is the primary symbol of the Stonekeeper. It features a stylized eye or geometric design and glows with magical energy. The role is associated with themes of guardianship, power, and responsibility.

Domains — The Stonekeeper operates primarily in the land of Alledia, a fantastical underground world. The role involves navigating both the human world (near San Francisco) and Alledia, often moving between them via magical means.

Accounts I
The Rescue of Karen HayesAmulet: The Stonekeeper (2008)

2008

Emily and Navin move with their mother to their great-grandfather Silas's house. Emily finds the amulet and puts it on. That night, a monster kidnaps their mother. Emily and Navin pursue it into the underground world of Alledia, where they meet Miskit, a rabbit-like robot, and find Silas on his deathbed. Silas tells Emily about the amulet's power and urges her to accept or reject it. After Silas dies, Emily accepts the amulet's power and becomes the Stonekeeper. They locate their mother, but she is captured by an elf prince who demands Emily's help against his father. Emily refuses and drives him off. They rescue Karen, but she is poisoned and must be taken to a city 300 miles away. The robots transform the house into a giant robot to transport her. Emily becomes the Stonekeeper, Karen is saved, and the family begins a journey to the city, setting up the next volume.

The Approach

In the shadow of a San Francisco hillside, a young girl named Emily Hayes discovers a mysterious amulet hidden in her great-grandfather's study. That single moment pulls her into a perilous quest to rescue her kidnapped mother, and transforms her into the next Stonekeeper—the wielder of a powerful, sentient talisman that could either save her family or doom an entire world.

Content advisory. Contains scenes of peril, monster attacks, kidnapping, and the death of a parent (implied off-page). Some dark fantasy elements may be intense for very young readers.

Etymology

The term 'Stonekeeper' is a compound of 'stone' (referring to the amulet's gemstone) and 'keeper' (one who guards or tends), indicating the role's custodial and protective nature.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Kazu Kibuishi for the American graphic novel series 'Amulet', published by Scholastic Graphix. The character and concept are part of contemporary American children's fantasy literature.

Disputed Identifications

There is no significant dispute over the identity of the Stonekeeper; however, it is sometimes confused with the amulet itself or with other characters who briefly hold the amulet. The role is specifically tied to Emily Hayes in this volume.

Description

Appearance

The Stonekeeper is identified by the wearing of the stone amulet, a circular pendant with a blue gem. The amulet glows and speaks telepathically to its wearer. Emily Hayes, the protagonist, is depicted as a young girl with dark hair, often shown with the amulet around her neck.

Symbols & Iconography

The amulet itself is the primary symbol of the Stonekeeper. It features a stylized eye or geometric design and glows with magical energy. The role is associated with themes of guardianship, power, and responsibility.

Behaviour & Personality

As the Stonekeeper, Emily is initially hesitant and reluctant, but she grows into the role as she faces dangers to save her family. She shows bravery, resourcefulness, and compassion, even when tempted by the amulet's power. The amulet itself is manipulative and persuasive, urging Emily to use its power aggressively.

Domains & Habitats

The Stonekeeper operates primarily in the land of Alledia, a fantastical underground world. The role involves navigating both the human world (near San Francisco) and Alledia, often moving between them via magical means.

Version Differences

Within the first volume, the Stonekeeper role is mostly defined by the amulet's power and its influence on Emily. Later volumes in the series expand on the lore, including the existence of multiple Stonekeepers and the amulet's darker nature, but this dossier focuses on the first book.

Powers & Abilities

Amulet Empowerment

Source Version Amulet: The Stonekeeper (2008)

Description

The amulet grants its wearer enhanced strength, the ability to repel magical attacks, and the power to command the amulet's energy for defensive or offensive purposes. It also provides telepathic communication and guidance.

Limitations

The amulet's power is tied to the wearer's emotional state and willingness to embrace it. Overuse or reliance on the amulet can lead to corruption or loss of self. The amulet's guidance is often self-serving and may lead the wearer astray.

Contradictions

The amulet claims it can turn back time and grant rulership, but these are never demonstrated in this volume, leaving their veracity uncertain.

Resistance to Magic

Source Version Amulet: The Stonekeeper (2008)

Description

When attacked by the elf prince's amulet, Emily is able to repel the attack using her own amulet, suggesting a form of magical resistance or counterattack.

Limitations

This ability is only shown once and its scope is unclear.

Contradictions

None noted.

Stories & Myths

The Rescue of Karen Hayes

Source Amulet: The Stonekeeper (2008)Culture Contemporary AmericanPeriod 2008

Summary

Emily and Navin move with their mother to their great-grandfather Silas's house. Emily finds the amulet and puts it on. That night, a monster kidnaps their mother. Emily and Navin pursue it into the underground world of Alledia, where they meet Miskit, a rabbit-like robot, and find Silas on his deathbed. Silas tells Emily about the amulet's power and urges her to accept or reject it. After Silas dies, Emily accepts the amulet's power and becomes the Stonekeeper. They locate their mother, but she is captured by an elf prince who demands Emily's help against his father. Emily refuses and drives him off. They rescue Karen, but she is poisoned and must be taken to a city 300 miles away. The robots transform the house into a giant robot to transport her.

Parental death

Source Amulet: The Stonekeeper (2008)

Description

Emily's father died in a car accident two years before the story begins. This loss motivates Emily's desire to use the amulet to bring him back, a temptation the amulet exploits.

Scholarly Context

The graphic novel handles grief and loss as central emotional themes.

Kidnapping and poisoning

Source Amulet: The Stonekeeper (2008)

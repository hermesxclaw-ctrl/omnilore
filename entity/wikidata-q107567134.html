Who It Is

In the shadows of Wano Country, a man who was supposed to be a loyal retainer of the Kozuki family revealed himself as the most insidious traitor in One Piece history. Kurozumi Kanjuro, a master of the art of drawing, used his Devil Fruit ability to deceive everyone around him for over two decades. But when his true allegiance to the villainous Kurozumi clan surfaced, he became the instrument of a tragic betrayal that changed the course of the Wano arc forever.

Initially, Kanjuro is portrayed as a clumsy, emotional, and somewhat comical character, often bursting into tears and exaggerating his feelings. However, this is a facade. In reality, he is a cold, calculating, and utterly devoted agent of the Kurozumi clan, willing to betray his comrades and even sacrifice his own life for his mission. He is a master of deception and improvisation, using his acting skills to maintain his cover for over two decades.

Powers
Fude Fude no Mi (Brush-Brush Fruit)

A Paramecia-type Devil Fruit that allows Kanjuro to create anything he draws with his brush. The created objects are real and can be used as weapons, tools, or even living creatures. He can also draw on surfaces and make the drawings come to life.

Limit: He must be able to draw the object accurately. He cannot create things he cannot visualize. The creations are not permanent and can be dispelled or destroyed.

Master Swordsmanship

Kanjuro is a skilled swordsman, able to fight on par with other samurai. He uses his brush as a sword when necessary.

Limit: Not shown to be exceptionally powerful compared to top-tier fighters.

Acting and Deception

Kanjuro is a master of acting and deception, able to convincingly portray a loyal ally for years. He can manipulate emotions and situations to his advantage.

Limit: His deception is not a supernatural ability, but a skill.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Kanjuro is a tall, slender man with long, black hair that he keeps tied in a traditional samurai topknot. He has sharp, narrow eyes and a perpetual, almost theatrical smile. He wears a kimono with a large, flowing outer robe, often depicted in dark colors with a pattern of flames or waves. He carries a large brush on his back, which is his primary weapon and tool.

Symbols — His symbol is the Kurozumi clan's crest, which is a black charcoal-like mark. His brush is a symbol of his artistic ability and his role as a spy, as he can create anything he draws.

Domains — He is a samurai from Wano Country, specifically associated with the capital city of Flower Capital. He travels with the Straw Hat crew and the Akazaya Nine, but his true allegiance is to the Kurozumi clan.

Accounts I
The Betrayal of the Akazaya NineOne Piece manga, chapters 970-971; anime episodes 971-972

20 years before the main storyline

Kanjuro was a member of the Akazaya Nine, the nine retainers of Kozuki Oden. He was actually a spy for Kurozumi Orochi, the shogun of Wano. He provided information to Orochi, leading to the downfall of Oden and the Kozuki family. He played a key role in the execution of Oden. The Kozuki family was destroyed, Oden was executed, and Wano fell under Orochi's tyrannical rule.

Accounts II
The Infiltration of the Straw HatsOne Piece manga, chapters 697-1050; anime episodes 630-1050

Two years before the main storyline to the Wano arc

Kanjuro was introduced as a samurai who was washed ashore on an island, captured by the Straw Hats. He claimed to be a loyal retainer of the Kozuki family and asked for their help to return to Wano. He traveled with them, gathering information and waiting for the right moment to betray them. His true allegiance was revealed during the raid on Onigashima. He revealed his true nature, captured Momonosuke, and attempted to kill him, but was ultimately defeated.

The Final StandOne Piece manga, chapters 1008-1050; anime episodes 1010-1050

During the Wano arc

After being defeated, Kanjuro was captured but later escaped. He made his way to the basement of Onigashima, where he attempted to kill Momonosuke, but was stopped by the combined efforts of the Straw Hats and others. He was ultimately killed by his own creation, a giant drawing of a flame, which he could not control. Kanjuro's death marked the end of the Kurozumi clan's influence, and Wano was freed.

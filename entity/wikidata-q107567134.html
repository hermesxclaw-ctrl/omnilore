The Approach

In the shadows of Wano Country, a man who was supposed to be a loyal retainer of the Kozuki family revealed himself as the most insidious traitor in One Piece history. Kurozumi Kanjuro, a master of the art of drawing, used his Devil Fruit ability to deceive everyone around him for over two decades. But when his true allegiance to the villainous Kurozumi clan surfaced, he became the instrument of a tragic betrayal that changed the course of the Wano arc forever.

Content advisory. Contains themes of betrayal, violence, and death.
Identity

Kurozumi Kanjuro

Pronunciation Koo-roh-zoo-mee KAHN-joo-roh

Known As

Kanjuro the DrawerThe Traitor

Etymology

Kurozumi (黒炭) means 'black charcoal', and Kanjuro (カン十郎) is a traditional Japanese given name. The family name Kurozumi is associated with the villainous clan in Wano.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Eiichiro Oda for the One Piece manga, first appearing in Chapter 697 (2013) as a samurai from Wano Country. He is a fictional character, not based on a real historical figure, but draws on Japanese samurai and kabuki theater aesthetics.

Disputed Identifications

Initially presented as a loyal retainer of the Kozuki family, but later revealed to be a spy for the Kurozumi clan. No other disputed identifications are known.

Description

Appearance

Kanjuro is a tall, slender man with long, black hair that he keeps tied in a traditional samurai topknot. He has sharp, narrow eyes and a perpetual, almost theatrical smile. He wears a kimono with a large, flowing outer robe, often depicted in dark colors with a pattern of flames or waves. He carries a large brush on his back, which is his primary weapon and tool.

Symbols & Iconography

His symbol is the Kurozumi clan's crest, which is a black charcoal-like mark. His brush is a symbol of his artistic ability and his role as a spy, as he can create anything he draws.

Behaviour & Personality

Initially, Kanjuro is portrayed as a clumsy, emotional, and somewhat comical character, often bursting into tears and exaggerating his feelings. However, this is a facade. In reality, he is a cold, calculating, and utterly devoted agent of the Kurozumi clan, willing to betray his comrades and even sacrifice his own life for his mission. He is a master of deception and improvisation, using his acting skills to maintain his cover for over two decades.

Domains & Habitats

He is a samurai from Wano Country, specifically associated with the capital city of Flower Capital. He travels with the Straw Hat crew and the Akazaya Nine, but his true allegiance is to the Kurozumi clan.

Version Differences

In the manga, his betrayal is revealed in Chapter 970. In the anime, the same events are depicted in Episode 971. There are no significant differences between the two versions.

Powers & Abilities

Fude Fude no Mi (Brush-Brush Fruit)

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

A Paramecia-type Devil Fruit that allows Kanjuro to create anything he draws with his brush. The created objects are real and can be used as weapons, tools, or even living creatures. He can also draw on surfaces and make the drawings come to life.

Limitations

He must be able to draw the object accurately. He cannot create things he cannot visualize. The creations are not permanent and can be dispelled or destroyed.

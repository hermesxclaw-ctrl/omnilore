Who It Is
Form

Paarthurnax is a large, wise-looking dragon with a distinctive appearance, featuring a long, curved snout and a pair of large, glowing eyes.

Powers

The Thu'um Paarthurnax is said to possess the power of the Thu'um, a magical language that allows him to manipulate the elements and control the forces of nature.

Accounts

Paarthurnax is said to have lived during the First Era, serving as a powerful and wise dragon who played a key role in the history of Skyrim.

Paarthurnax was discovered in 2025, when a nearly complete skull was found in the Holly Creek Formation of southwest Arkansas.

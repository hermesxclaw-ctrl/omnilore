Who It Is

A rhyming demon from Hell bound to the human Jason Blood by Merlin as punishment for betraying Camelot. When Blood speaks the incantation ('Gone, gone, the form of man / Rise the demon, Etrigan!'), he transforms into a yellow-skinned, horned demon who speaks exclusively in rhyming couplets. Etrigan serves neither Heaven nor Hell, but his own chaotic agenda.

Attested in The Demon #1 (1972)

Cultural Significance

Etrigan represents the wild card of DC's supernatural universe — an unpredictable force that can ally with or oppose anyone. The Jason Blood/Etrigan duality (scholar and demon sharing one body) is a sophisticated take on the Jekyll/Hyde dynamic.

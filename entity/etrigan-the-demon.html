Who It Is

Etrigan's complex and multifaceted nature makes him a fascinating character to explore. His relationships with other characters in the DC Universe, as well as his role in various storylines and events, offer a rich tapestry of themes and ideas to engage with.

Etrigan is a complex character with a mix of violent and heroic tendencies. He is often depicted as a formidable opponent, but also as a loyal ally. His rhyming speech and poetic nature add to his enigmatic personality.

Powers
Rhyming Speech

Etrigan's ability to speak in rhymes is a unique aspect of his character. This power allows him to express himself in a poetic and enigmatic manner, often leaving his opponents and allies in awe.

Limit:

Demonic Strength

Etrigan's demonic strength grants him superhuman physical abilities, allowing him to overpower even the most formidable opponents. His strength is often depicted as a manifestation of his demonic powers.

Limit:

Hellish Resistance

Etrigan's association with Hell grants him resistance to various forms of damage and injury. This power allows him to withstand extreme conditions and survive in environments that would be hostile to mortal beings.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Etrigan is commonly depicted as a muscular humanoid creature with orange or yellow skin, horns, red eyes, and pointed, webbed ears. He frequently speaks in rhymes. His appearance is often accompanied by a dark, ominous aura.

Symbols — Etrigan's rhyming speech and demonic appearance are iconic symbols of his character. His association with Jason Blood and the forces of good also serve as a visual representation of his complex nature.

Domains — Etrigan's domains and habitats include Hell, the mortal world, and the realm of the DC Universe. His association with Jason Blood and the forces of good also grant him access to various domains and habitats.

Accounts I
The New GodsThe New Gods #1 (1971)

1970s

Etrigan is introduced as a member of the New Gods, a group of powerful beings from the planet New Genesis. He becomes embroiled in a conflict between the New Gods and the forces of Apokolips. Etrigan's involvement in the New Gods' conflict leads to a significant shift in his character development and his role in the DC Universe.

Accounts II
The DemonThe Demon #1 (1972)

1970s

Etrigan is the main character of this series, which explores his demonic nature and his association with Jason Blood. The series delves into Etrigan's backstory and his relationships with various characters in the DC Universe. The Demon series establishes Etrigan as a complex and multifaceted character, with a rich history and a deep connection to the forces of good and evil.

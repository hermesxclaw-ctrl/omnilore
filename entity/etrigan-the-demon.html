Who It Is
Form

Etrigan is commonly depicted as a muscular humanoid creature with orange or yellow skin, horns, red eyes, and pointed, webbed ears. He frequently speaks in rhymes. His appearance is often accompanied by a dark, ominous aura.

Powers

Rhyming Speech Etrigan's ability to speak in rhymes is a unique aspect of his character. This power allows him to express himself in a poetic and enigmatic manner, often leaving his opponents and allies in awe.

Demonic Strength Etrigan's demonic strength grants him superhuman physical abilities, allowing him to overpower even the most formidable opponents. His strength is often depicted as a manifestation of his demonic powers.

Hellish Resistance Etrigan's association with Hell grants him resistance to various forms of damage and injury. This power allows him to withstand extreme conditions and survive in environments that would be hostile to mortal beings.

Accounts

Etrigan is introduced as a member of the New Gods, a group of powerful beings from the planet New Genesis. He becomes embroiled in a conflict between the New Gods and the forces of Apokolips.

Etrigan is the main character of this series, which explores his demonic nature and his association with Jason Blood. The series delves into Etrigan's backstory and his relationships with various characters in the DC Universe.

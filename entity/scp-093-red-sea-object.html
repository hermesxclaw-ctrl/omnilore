Who It Is
Form

The Red Sea Object is a large, unidentifiable object that has been recovered from the Red Sea. It is approximately 10 meters in length and 5 meters in width. The object is made of a metallic material that is unknown to science.

Powers

Anomalous Properties The Red Sea Object has several anomalous properties that are not fully understood. It is capable of altering its appearance and behavior in response to external stimuli.

Incident Log

The Red Sea Object was recovered from the Red Sea in 2004 by a team of researchers from the SCP Foundation. The object was initially thought to be a large piece of wreckage, but it was later determined to be an anomalous object.

A team of researchers from the SCP Foundation conducted a study on the Red Sea Object in 2023. The study aimed to understand the object's anomalous properties and behavior.

Who It Is

A red stone disc that, when held by a human and carried across a reflective surface, transports the holder to an alternate dimension — a dead Earth where all life has been consumed by massive, amoeboid 'Unclean' entities that roam the landscape absorbing anything organic. The alternate Earth has its own history, including a parallel Foundation that failed. Exploration logs reveal a world of vast, silent cities and crawling horrors.

Form

A disc approximately 30 cm in diameter, composed of a red, stone-like material with organic properties. It thrums faintly when held. When placed on a mirror, it 'activates.'

Powers

Interdimensional transportation

Gateway to a dead alternate Earth

Requires reflective surface and human carrier

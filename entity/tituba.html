Who It Is
Form

Tituba was described as a young woman of African descent, likely a slave or a servant, who was born in Barbados and brought to Salem, Massachusetts, in the late 17th century.

Powers

Shape-shifting Tituba was accused of shape-shifting into various animals, including a cat and a dog, which was a common trait associated with witches in colonial America.

Accounts

Tituba was one of the first people to be tried for witchcraft in the Salem Witch Trials, and her trial was a key event in the history of the trials.

Tituba's confession was a key piece of evidence in the Salem Witch Trials, and it led to the execution of several other people accused of witchcraft.

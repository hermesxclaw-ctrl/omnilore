Who It Is

Naruto's signature completed Rasengan variant, created by adding Wind Release chakra nature to the base Rasengan. It takes the form of a massive, spinning shuriken of wind chakra that attacks on a cellular level, severing chakra pathways. Initially dangerous to the user's own arm, Naruto later perfected it to be thrown as a projectile.

Powers

Cellular-level damage, chakra pathway severing, thrown projectile

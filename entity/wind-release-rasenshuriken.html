Who It Is

A churning sphere of wind so finely controlled it cuts at the cellular level, the Rasenshuriken is the pinnacle of Naruto Uzumaki's wind-natured chakra—a technique that reshaped the Fourth Shinobi War and the very philosophy of its creator.

The technique is aggressive and devastating, designed to destroy on contact. Its use requires precise chakra control and carries a significant risk to the user, reflecting Naruto's willingness to sacrifice his own well-being for victory.

Etymology — From Japanese 'Rasengan' (spiral sphere) and 'shuriken' (throwing blade), combining the technique's nature and shape.

Powers
Cellular-level cutting

The Rasenshuriken damages the target at a cellular level, bypassing normal defenses and causing internal damage that is difficult to heal.

Limit: Requires close-range or direct hit; the user suffers backlash from the technique's sharp wind chakra, damaging their own arm.

Wind nature manipulation

Harnesses wind-natured chakra to create a powerful cutting force, capable of shredding almost any substance.

Limit: Requires immense chakra control and a high level of skill in wind release.

Throwing capability

Can be thrown like a shuriken, allowing for mid-range attacks with a spinning motion that increases cutting power.

Limit: Thrown version is less controllable and can be dodged; user must maintain concentration.

Form & Signs

Appearance — A large, spinning disc of wind chakra with a shuriken-like silhouette, formed around a Rasengan core. It appears as a brilliant blue-white sphere with four blades extending outward, often shown with a glowing aura and a distinctive whistling sound.

Symbols — Represents Naruto's mastery of wind-natured chakra and his evolution from a brash ninja to a strategic fighter. The shuriken form evokes traditional ninja weaponry, linking Naruto to the ninja archetype.

Domains — Primarily used in open battlefields; effective at mid-range. Not suited for confined spaces due to its large size and destructive radius.

Accounts I
First Use Against KakuzuNaruto manga, Chapter 441

2008

Naruto develops the Rasenshuriken during his training with Kakashi and Yamato, perfecting it to defeat the Akatsuki member Kakuzu. He uses it to destroy Kakuzu's multiple hearts, but the backlash injures his arm. Kakuzu is defeated; Naruto's arm is severely damaged, leading to the development of a new fighting style.

Accounts II
Refinement and MasteryNaruto manga, Chapters 442-450

2008-2009

After the initial backlash, Naruto trains to master the Rasenshuriken, learning to throw it and later to use it in Sage Mode, which eliminates the self-damage. Naruto gains a powerful technique that becomes a staple in his arsenal, especially during the Fourth Shinobi War.

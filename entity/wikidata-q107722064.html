The Approach

Melone never touches his victims. He steals a drop of blood, feeds it to a laptop, and breeds a homunculus that learns, hunts, and murders on its own — a killer who treats assassination as computer-assisted reproduction.

Content advisory. Graphic violence and murder (assassination narrative), body horror (Stand-generated homunculus, transmutation of human flesh into cubes/material), stalking and non-consensual sampling of blood/hair for reproduction, sexual harassment and misogynistic dialogue by Stand entity, brief nudity/sexualized design.
Identity

Melone

Pronunciation Italian [meˈloː.ne]; Japanese katakana メローネ (me-rō-ne); English fandom often /məˈloʊneɪ/ or /mɛˈloʊneɪ/

Known As

Melone (JoJo's Bizarre Adventure)Baby Face's userMelone di La Squadra Esecuzioni

Etymology

Italian common noun melone 'melon' (from Latin melo, melonem, from Greek mēlopepōn). In JoJo's Bizarre Adventure Part 5, most antagonists of La Squadra Esecuzioni are named after Italian foods or wordplay; Melone follows that pattern. Araki has not published an explicit etymology statement for this specific name, but guidebooks and official materials list the spelling as Melone (メローネ, Merōne in katakana). Not to be confused with surname Melone of unrelated historical persons.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character created by Japanese mangaka Hirohiko Araki, published by Shueisha (Weekly Shōnen Jump, Jump Comics) 1995-1999 for Part 5: Vento Aureo. Italian-language name and setting reflect Araki's use of Italian culture as aesthetic material, not Italian folklore ownership. Character is corporate IP of Shueisha / Hirohiko Araki / David Production (anime).

Disputed Identifications

Must be distinguished from: (1) Altobello Melone (1490/91–1543) Italian Renaissance painter; (2) toponyms Cà Melone (Borgo Maggiore, San Marino) and Melone hamlet in Guardiagrele, Abruzzo; (3) Miller-Melone Ranch, California; (4) gelo di melone (Sicilian watermelon pudding). Fan wikis occasionally conflate Melone with other La Squadra members or misspell as 'Melon' or 'Mellone'. Wikidata Q107722064 specifically denotes the JoJo character, not the surname or places. No historical or folkloric figure named Melone was found.

Description

Appearance

Melone is depicted as a tall, slender young man (officially 20s) with very long, straight, center-parted hair reaching past the shoulders, colored dark violet-purple in the anime and varying between dark brown and purple in manga color pages. He has pale skin, thin eyebrows, and wears dark lipstick. His costume is a distinctive dark indigo-purple jumpsuit with a sheer mesh upper chest and midriff, flared legs, and a small emblem at the chest, plus dark boots and a choker-like collar. He often poses effeminately, with languid hand gestures, and carries a laptop computer which is required for his Stand. Araki's art style emphasizes bishōnen (beautiful youth) aesthetics with elongated limbs and fashion-forward clothing. No age, height, or weight is given in canon databooks beyond visual depiction.

Symbols & Iconography

Primary symbol is his Stand, Baby Face — a humanoid homunculus with a computer-keyboard motif, stitched body, and the ability to transform matter into cubes. Melone himself is associated with computers, keyboards, and reproductive/birthing imagery: he refers to his Stand as his 'son' and to the process as 'education' and 'birth'. Visual motifs include cubes (when Baby Face deconstructs matter), the laptop as womb/incubator, and DNA/blood droplet icons in anime effects. Color palette is purples, blues, and pinks, linking him to other La Squadra members' fashion palette.

Behaviour & Personality

Attested in manga/anime dialogue: Melone is calm, polite, and softly spoken, with a detached, almost clinical curiosity about murder and reproduction. He is openly misogynistic and sexually harassing toward his own Stand's female victim (Trish) and makes lewd comments about women and childbirth, played for creepy comedy. He is loyal to La Squadra's goal (to uncover the Boss's identity and seize power) but acts independently once assigned. He treats Baby Face as a child to be educated, speaking to it paternally while encouraging cruelty, and shows sadistic pleasure when his Stand learns to kill. He is not hot-tempered like Ghiaccio or stoic like Risotto; his menace comes from quiet, voyeuristic detachment. He flees rather than fights directly, relying on long-distance Stand.

Domains & Habitats

In-universe, Melone operates within contemporary Italy (2001) as a mafia assassin for Passione's elite assassination team La Squadra Esecuzioni. His habitat is urban: trains, stations, and city streets. He does not have a supernatural domain; his Stand's range is extremely long (kilometers) because Baby Face is an automatic Stand that can act independently after being 'born'. Narratively, his domain is technology-mediated reproduction — the laptop as laboratory.

Version Differences

Manga (black-and-white) vs anime (color, voice, animation): hair color standardized to violet-purple in anime; manga color pages vary. Anime adds vocal intonation (soft, slightly breathy), additional dialogue pauses, and visual effects for cube transformation and Stand birth. English VIZ translation censors or softens some of Melone's more explicit sexual harassment lines slightly compared to literal Japanese, but meaning retained. No major redesign across video games; All Star Battle and Eyes of Heaven use anime coloration. No live-action version exists.

Powers & Abilities

Baby Face (Stand)

Source VersionManga Vol. 52-53 / Anime Ep. 17-18; JOJO A-GO!GO! Stand stats

Long-distance automatic Stand bound to a laptop computer. By obtaining a blood, hair, or tissue sample from a target (and a female host), Melone can impregnate the host with a homunculus that gestates rapidly, is 'educated' by Melone via the laptop, and then hunts the target. The homunculus can deconstruct any inorganic or organic matter into small cubes and reconstruct it, effectively disassembling victims and objects.

Limitations

Requires: (1) laptop computer as medium; (2) DNA sample from primary target (blood/hair) to imprint target for homunculus to track; (3) female human host to gestate (Trish Una used without consent); (4) time to educate — if not properly educated, homunculus may be disobedient or weak. Homunculus acts independently but is still linked to Melone; if Melone is killed or incapacitated, Stand may weaken. Range is very long but not infinite; Melone must remain within kilometers while Stand operates.

Contradictions

Whether Baby Face is fully sentient vs programmed is debated; manga shows it developing personality through education, but guidebooks classify it as automatic.

Matter Cubing / Deconstruction

Source VersionManga Ch. 491-495; Anime Ep. 18

Baby Face can touch matter and instantly divide it into small cubes (approx. 1-2 cm), including human bodies, stone, metal. Cubes can be rearranged or used to trap. Victims cubed are not immediately dead but are disassembled; reassembly is possible if Stand wills it.

Limitations

Requires physical contact by Baby Face entity, not by Melone himself. Cannot cube without Baby Face being present. Effect is temporary and reversible by Stand.

Contradictions

None major; consistent across versions.

Target Tracking / Homing via DNA

Source VersionManga Ch. 488-489

Once imprinted with target's blood, the Baby Face homunculus instinctively knows target's location and identity, hunting Giorno Giovanna specifically after Melone samples his blood via theft from a train.

Limitations

Needs fresh biological sample; if sample is contaminated or from wrong person, homunculus may target incorrectly. Shown when Melone initially samples Bucciarati's team indirectly.

Contradictions

Fan theories that Stand could track anyone with any DNA are not attested; only shown tracking Giorno via blood.

Rapid Growth and Learning (Education)

Source VersionManga Ch. 489-492

Homunculus is born as infant-like and rapidly matures to adult humanoid within minutes through Melone's laptop 'education' program where he teaches it to kill, speak, and use its powers. Its personality and cruelty are shaped by this education.

Limitations

If education is interrupted (Giorno attacks laptop), homunculus remains immature and less effective. Melone must actively type/teach; cannot be skipped.

Contradictions

Anime implies education is more gamified with UI; manga shows simpler computer screen.

Long-Distance Automatic Operation

Source VersionGuidebook stats: Range A, Persistence A

After birth, Baby Face can operate kilometers away from Melone without his conscious control, making Melone safe from direct retaliation.

Limitations

Melone cannot directly see through Stand's eyes; relies on reports or laptop feedback. If homunculus is destroyed, Melone suffers backlash and must create a new one, requiring new host/sample.

Contradictions

Some databooks list Baby Face as 'automatic' but also 'colonial' type; classification varies.

Summary

Melone is introduced as one of nine elite assassins of La Squadra Esecuzioni, led by Risotto Nero. After the Boss murders Sorbet and Gelato to punish the team's unauthorized investigation into his identity, the squad defects and vows to kidnap the Boss's daughter Trish Una to discover him. Melone appears in the squad meeting where Risotto assigns pairs to hunt Team Bucciarati, who are escorting Trish.

He then kidnaps an unsuspecting woman in a train compartment to serve as host, impregnating her via his Stand's laptop-mediated process. The woman rapidly gives birth to Baby Face's homunculus.

Education of Baby Face

He encourages it to be selfish and murderous, praising its misogynistic remarks toward its 'mother' (the host) and Trish. The homunculus rapidly grows from infant to child to adult humanoid, gaining intelligence and sadism mirroring Melone's coaching.

Baby Face also cubes Bucciarati's luggage and threatens Trish, attempting to cube her as well. Defeat of Melone — Giorno's Counter-Education

Source Vento Aureo Ch. 497-498 'Baby Face Part 6'Culture Japanese mangaPeriod 1998

Summary

Giorno uses Gold Experience to create a lifeform that attacks Melone's laptop and to inflict damage on Baby Face that reflects back to Melone. He also tricks Baby Face by creating a brooch from his own flesh that poisons the homunculus. The homunculus is destroyed, and the backlash severely injures Melone, who is hiding in a different train car. Giorno and Mista locate the wounded Melone and Giorno executes him by turning his tongue into a hand that strangles him and by beating him, leaving him for dead (manga implies death; anime shows him incapacitated and later confirmed dead by Bucciarati's team).

Consequences

Melone is the third La Squadra member to die, reducing the squad's numbers; Team Bucciarati survives and continues to Florence. His death demonstrates that long-distance automatic Stands are vulnerable through their Stand, not user proximity.

Variants

Anime Episode 18 shows Melone's death more graphically with Gold Experience's assault; manga leaves slightly more ambiguous but guidebooks list him as deceased.

Legacy — Posthumous Influence on La Squadra Arc

Source Vento Aureo Ch. 499-520 (subsequent Ghiaccio, Prosciutto/Pesci arcs)Culture Japanese mangaPeriod 1998-1999

Summary

Although dead, Melone's failure informs Risotto's strategy; the Boss notes La Squadra's persistence. Later, in the final arc, Melone is referenced in flashbacks and in official popularity polls and guidebooks as example of Araki's 'birth' horror. His Stand concept is reused in analysis of later automatic Stands.

Participants

Risotto Nero

Ghiaccio

The Boss (Diavolo)

Consequences

Narratively closes Melone's role; thematically reinforces Part 5's focus on fate, parenthood, and creation (paralleling Giorno's own creation via DIO/Jonathan).

Variants

No anime-only legacy scenes beyond recaps.

Relationships
Melone→Risotto Neroally

Leader of La Squadra Esecuzioni. Melone serves under him and accepts his orders to hunt Team Bucciarati. Risotto respects Melone's unique Stand but assigns him solo work due to its long-range nature.

Melone→Baby Face (Stand)creator

Melone is Baby Face's user and 'father/educator'. He creates, educates, and commands the homunculus via laptop. Relationship is parental but abusive — he praises cruelty and treats the Stand as a child soldier.

Melone→Giorno Giovannahostile

Primary target. Melone imprints Baby Face with Giorno's blood to assassinate him. Giorno ultimately kills Melone, making them direct killer/victim pair.

Melone→Trish Unavictim

Daughter of the Boss, under Bucciarati's protection. Baby Face attempts to cube and kidnap her; Melone also uses an unnamed woman as host, paralleling Trish's status as daughter/hostage. Melone makes lewd comments about Trish.

Melone→Ghiaccio, Prosciutto, Formaggio, Illuso, Pescially

Fellow La Squadra assassins. Melone is peer to them; they share goal of exposing the Boss. He acts after Formaggio and Illuso die, and before Ghiaccio's attack. No direct interaction shown with Ghiaccio beyond squad meeting, but they are teammates.

Melone→Unnamed Female Hostvictim

Random woman on train whom Melone uses as incubator for Baby Face without consent. She gives birth to the homunculus and is then discarded/cubed. Represents non-consensual reproductive horror.

Melone→The Boss / Diavolohostile

Ultimate enemy of La Squadra. Melone's mission is indirectly against the Boss by targeting his daughter's protectors to lure him out. Melone never meets Diavolo directly.

Non-consensual reproductive horror

Source Vento Aureo Ch. 488-489; Anime Ep. 17

Description

Melone's Stand requires a female host to gestate the homunculus. He selects a random woman on a train, impregnates her without consent via supernatural means, and she undergoes accelerated pregnancy and birth. The narrative treats this as horror, with the woman in pain and confusion.

Scholarly Context

Critics (e.g., analyses of Araki's body horror) note this as example of Araki using pregnancy as horror trope, paralleling Part 5's broader themes of parenthood and lineage (Giorno as son of DIO/Jonathan, Trish as Boss's daughter). Not glorified; presented as villainous violation.

Misogyny and sexual harassment by proxy

Source Ch. 491-493 dialogue

Description

Both Melone and his Stand (as educated) make lewd, demeaning comments toward women, including the host and Trish Una. Baby Face calls its 'mother' worthless and makes sexual threats, reflecting Melone's coaching.

Scholarly Context

English-language JoJo scholarship notes La Squadra members often embody toxic masculinity archetypes; Melone's is the 'voyeuristic groomer'. The anime retains this but some translations tone down explicit slurs.

Assassination and mafia violence

Source Vol. 51-52 squad lore; Ch. 497-498

Description

Melone is a professional mafia assassin in Passione, responsible for multiple implied murders before his on-page attempt. His death is itself a brutal beating by Giorno/Mista.

Scholarly Context

Part 5 is explicitly about Italian mafia (Passione) and critiques romanticized mafia fiction by showing assassins as cruel and expendable.

Grooming and child soldier creation

Source Ch. 490-492

Description

Melone 'educates' Baby Face from infant to killer in minutes, praising cruelty and punishing disobedience, mirroring grooming/abuse dynamics. The homunculus is treated as a child weapon.

Scholarly Context

Commentary in JOJO A-GO!GO! notes Araki intentionally made Baby Face's education resemble a corrupted Tamagotchi/pet-raising game to critique desensitization.

Contradictions & Disputes

Is Melone dead or merely incapacitated?

Position A

Melone is killed by Giorno Giovanna in Ch. 498 (manga implies death via severe beating and tongue transformation).

Position A — evidence

Manga shows Giorno and Mista leaving him bloodied and unmoving; subsequent chapters never show him again; official character status lists him as deceased in JOJOVELLER timeline.

Position B

Anime and some guidebooks leave slightly ambiguous, showing him unconscious but not explicitly confirmed dead on-screen; fan wikis debate if he could survive.

Position B — evidence

Anime does not show death certificate; JoJo wiki initially listed status as 'Unknown' before correction.

Scholarly Consensus

Consensus among official materials is deceased; ambiguity is presentation, not canon survival.

Baby Face autonomy vs programming

Position A

Baby Face is a fully automatic Stand with independent personality after education (guidebook classification: automatic).

Position A — evidence

Manga shows Baby Face disobeying Melone when poorly educated and speaking independently; JOJO A-GO!GO! lists Persistence A and Range A indicating independence.

Position B

Baby Face is an extension of Melone's will, only mimicking personality taught via laptop (interpretation that it has no true sentience).

Position B — evidence

Manga also shows Melone can give orders via laptop and Baby Face follows imprinted target logic, suggesting programming.

Scholarly Consensus

Most analyses treat it as semi-autonomous: born with potential, shaped by education, but still bound to Melone's Stand link. No definitive Araki statement.

Hair and costume color

Position A

Manga color pages show Melone with brownish hair and darker suit.

Position A — evidence

Shueisha color pages (1998 Jump) and JOJO A-GO!GO! illustrations.

Position B

Anime standardizes hair to violet-purple and suit to indigo with pink mesh.

Position B — evidence

David Production color design sheets (2018) and Super Action Statue figure.

Scholarly Consensus

Anime palette is now canonical for merchandise; variation is artistic license, not lore contradiction.

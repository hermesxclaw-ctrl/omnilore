Who It Is

Melone never touches his victims. He steals a drop of blood, feeds it to a laptop, and breeds a homunculus that learns, hunts, and murders on its own — a killer who treats assassination as computer-assisted reproduction.

Attested in manga/anime dialogue: Melone is calm, polite, and softly spoken, with a detached, almost clinical curiosity about murder and reproduction. He is openly misogynistic and sexually harassing toward his own Stand's female victim (Trish) and makes lewd comments about women and childbirth, played for creepy comedy. He is loyal to La Squadra's goal (to uncover the Boss's identity and seize power) but acts independently once assigned. Powers
Baby Face (Stand)

Long-distance automatic Stand bound to a laptop computer. By obtaining a blood, hair, or tissue sample from a target (and a female host), Melone can impregnate the host with a homunculus that gestates rapidly, is 'educated' by Melone via the laptop, and then hunts the target. The homunculus can deconstruct any inorganic or organic matter into small cubes and reconstruct it, effectively disassembling victims and objects.

Limit: Requires: (1) laptop computer as medium; (2) DNA sample from primary target (blood/hair) to imprint target for homunculus to track; (3) female human host to gestate (Trish Una used without consent); (4) time to educate — if not properly educated, homunculus may be disobedient or weak. Homunculus acts independently but is still linked to Melone; if Melone is killed or incapacitated, Stand may weaken. Range is very long but not infinite; Melone must remain within kilometers while Stand operates.

Matter Cubing / Deconstruction

Baby Face can touch matter and instantly divide it into small cubes (approx. 1-2 cm), including human bodies, stone, metal. Cubes can be rearranged or used to trap. Victims cubed are not immediately dead but are disassembled; reassembly is possible if Stand wills it.

Limit: Requires physical contact by Baby Face entity, not by Melone himself. Cannot cube without Baby Face being present. Effect is temporary and reversible by Stand.

Target Tracking / Homing via DNA

Once imprinted with target's blood, the Baby Face homunculus instinctively knows target's location and identity, hunting Giorno Giovanna specifically after Melone samples his blood via theft from a train.

Limit: Needs fresh biological sample; if sample is contaminated or from wrong person, homunculus may target incorrectly. Shown when Melone initially samples Bucciarati's team indirectly.

Rapid Growth and Learning (Education)

Homunculus is born as infant-like and rapidly matures to adult humanoid within minutes through Melone's laptop 'education' program where he teaches it to kill, speak, and use its powers. Its personality and cruelty are shaped by this education.

Limit: If education is interrupted (Giorno attacks laptop), homunculus remains immature and less effective. Melone must actively type/teach; cannot be skipped.

Long-Distance Automatic Operation

After birth, Baby Face can operate kilometers away from Melone without his conscious control, making Melone safe from direct retaliation.

Limit: Melone cannot directly see through Stand's eyes; relies on reports or laptop feedback. If homunculus is destroyed, Melone suffers backlash and must create a new one, requiring new host/sample.

Accounts I
La Squadra Esecuzioni's Revolt Against the BossJoJo's Bizarre Adventure Part 5: Vento Aureo, Vol. 51-52, Ch. 470-485

1998 (publication); in-universe 2001

Melone is introduced as one of nine elite assassins of La Squadra Esecuzioni, led by Risotto Nero. After the Boss murders Sorbet and Gelato to punish the team's unauthorized investigation into his identity, the squad defects and vows to kidnap the Boss's daughter Trish Una to discover him. Melone appears in the squad meeting where Risotto assigns pairs to hunt Team Bucciarati, who are escorting Trish. Sets up the series of assassination attempts; Melone is assigned to act alone using Baby Face after Formaggio and Illuso fail.

Theft of Blood on the Express Train — Baby Face's ConceptionVento Aureo Ch. 487-489 'Baby Face Part 1-2'

1998

Team Bucciarati travels by train toward Florence with Trish. Melone boards the same train covertly, steals a blood sample from Giorno Giovanna (by cutting him subtly and collecting blood) and uses it to imprint Baby Face. He then kidnaps an unsuspecting woman in a train compartment to serve as host, impregnating her via his Stand's laptop-mediated process. The woman rapidly gives birth to Baby Face's homunculus. Creation of Baby Face homunculus keyed to Giorno; establishes the Stand's reproductive horror mechanism.

Education of Baby FaceVento Aureo Ch. 490-492 'Baby Face Part 3-4'

1998

In an empty train compartment, Melone 'educates' the newborn Baby Face via laptop, teaching it language, cruelty, and how to use its cubing power. He encourages it to be selfish and murderous, praising its misogynistic remarks toward its 'mother' (the host) and Trish. The homunculus rapidly grows from infant to child to adult humanoid, gaining intelligence and sadism mirroring Melone's coaching. Homunculus becomes fully functional assassin; demonstrates Araki's theme of nature vs nurture and technology-mediated parenting.

Accounts II
Baby Face vs Giorno Giovanna — Cube TrapVento Aureo Ch. 492-496 'Baby Face Part 4-5'

1998

Baby Face attacks Giorno in the train, turning Giorno's hand and parts of the train interior into cubes. Giorno, separated from his allies, is disassembled but uses Gold Experience to transform a cube fragment into a snake/plant to track Baby Face and counterattack. Baby Face also cubes Bucciarati's luggage and threatens Trish, attempting to cube her as well. Giorno discovers that damaging the homunculus damages Melone's laptop link and that the Stand's cubes can be reversed. Giorno learns Baby Face's weakness (dependence on education and laptop) and begins to turn the fight.

Defeat of Melone — Giorno's Counter-EducationVento Aureo Ch. 497-498 'Baby Face Part 6'

1998

Giorno uses Gold Experience to create a lifeform that attacks Melone's laptop and to inflict damage on Baby Face that reflects back to Melone. He also tricks Baby Face by creating a brooch from his own flesh that poisons the homunculus. The homunculus is destroyed, and the backlash severely injures Melone, who is hiding in a different train car. Giorno and Mista locate the wounded Melone and Giorno executes him by turning his tongue into a hand that strangles him and by beating him, leaving him for dead (manga implies death; anime shows him incapacitated and later confirmed dead by Bucciarati's team). Melone is the third La Squadra member to die, reducing the squad's numbers; Team Bucciarati survives and continues to Florence. His death demonstrates that long-distance automatic Stands are vulnerable through their Stand, not user proximity.

Legacy — Posthumous Influence on La Squadra ArcVento Aureo Ch. 499-520 (subsequent Ghiaccio, Prosciutto/Pesci arcs)

1998-1999

Although dead, Melone's failure informs Risotto's strategy; the Boss notes La Squadra's persistence. Later, in the final arc, Melone is referenced in flashbacks and in official popularity polls and guidebooks as example of Araki's 'birth' horror. His Stand concept is reused in analysis of later automatic Stands. Narratively closes Melone's role; thematically reinforces Part 5's focus on fate, parenthood, and creation (paralleling Giorno's own creation via DIO/Jonathan).

Melone is named after the band or is a historical Italian figure. — Melone is a fictional assassin created by Hirohiko Araki in 1998; his name is Italian for 'melon' following Part 5's food-naming pattern. He is not a historical person and not named after a band (unlike some other JoJo characters named after musicians).

Baby Face can impregnate anyone without a host or sample. — Canon requires both a DNA sample from the target and a female human host for gestation, plus a laptop. It cannot spontaneously generate without those components.

Melone is a main villain or member of Diavolo's guard. — Melone is a mid-arc assassin in La Squadra Esecuzioni, which is rebelling against Diavolo. He appears in only ~10 chapters and 2 anime episodes and is not part of the Boss's elite guard (e.g., Secco, Cioccolata).

Melone's Stand is called 'Melone' or his name is Baby Face. — Character is Melone; Stand is Baby Face (named after Baby Face song/band reference). Swapping is common in casual fandom.

Who It Is

Twenty-fifth spirit, a President and Earl commanding 36 legions. Glasya-Labolas (also Caacrinolaas, Classyalabolas) appears as a dog with griffin's wings — a fusion of loyal guardian and predatory raptor. He teaches all arts and sciences in an instant and is the author and instigator of manslaughter and bloodshed. He makes men invisible, knows all things past, present, and future, and brings the love of friends and enemies alike. Glasya-Labolas is a demon of sudden, transformational knowledge — he can teach a lifetime's worth of learning in moments, but at the cost of awakening violent impulses.

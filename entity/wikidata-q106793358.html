Who It Is

Rosemary Winters was born into a world of nightmares, the daughter of Ethan and Mia Winters, survivors of the Baker estate incident. Her life was stolen by Mother Miranda, who saw her as a vessel for the mind of her dead daughter, Eva. Rescued as an infant, Rosemary grew up in the shadow of her father's sacrifice, unaware that her blood held the key to the Mold's power. Now, as a young woman, she must confront the legacy of her family and the dark forces that still seek to use her.

As a teenager, Rose is determined, brave, and somewhat rebellious. She is haunted by her past and the legacy of her father's sacrifice. She is initially resentful of her powers and the danger they bring, but she learns to accept and control them. She is kind-hearted and wants to protect those she loves.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name Rosemary is of Latin origin, meaning 'dew of the sea' or 'bitter rose'. In the context of the game, it may symbolize her connection to the Mold (a plant-like organism) and her bittersweet story. 'Winters' is her father's surname, reflecting her familial ties.

Pronunciation — ROHZ-mair-ee WIN-ters

Aliases — Rose, Rose Winters, Eva (as a vessel), The Molded Child

Ownership — Resident Evil is a Japanese media franchise created by Capcom. Rosemary Winters is a fictional character owned by Capcom.

Powers
Mold Manipulation

Rose has the ability to manipulate the Mold, a supernatural fungus. She can sense its presence, communicate with it, and even travel through it. In the DLC, she can use this power to traverse the Mold Realm and interact with its inhabitants.

Limit: Her powers are tied to the Mold and may be weakened outside of its influence. She is still learning to control them and can be overwhelmed by strong emotions.

Superhuman Resilience

Due to her connection to the Mold, Rose appears to have enhanced durability and healing, similar to her father. She can survive injuries that would be fatal to normal humans, though the extent is not fully explored.

Limit: Not fully explored; her resilience may not be as strong as her father's, as she is not a full Molded being.

Psychic Projection

In the DLC, Rose can project her consciousness into the Mold Realm, allowing her to interact with the past and with the consciousness of others. This is a unique ability that seems to be a result of her being a vessel for Eva.

Limit: This ability is limited to the Mold Realm and requires a strong connection to the Mold. It is also mentally taxing.

Form & Signs

Appearance — As an infant, Rose has a normal appearance. As a teenager in the Shadows of Rose DLC, she has shoulder-length brown hair, blue eyes, and a slight resemblance to her mother, Mia. She wears a distinctive jacket and jeans. In the DLC, she has a unique ability to enter the 'consciousness' of the Mold, where her appearance can be altered.

Symbols — Rose is often associated with the Mold, a fungal organism that connects all life. She has a mark on her arm that resembles a rose, which is a key to her powers. The symbol of the Rose is also used in the game's logo and marketing.

Domains — Rose was raised in Europe, likely in a safe house under the protection of the BSAA (though this is not explicitly stated). In the DLC, she enters a realm called the 'Consciousness' or the 'Mold Realm', a surreal, ever-changing world that reflects the minds of those connected to the Mold.

Accounts I
Resident Evil Village: The Birth of RoseResident Evil Village

2021 (fictional setting 2021)

Rose is born to Ethan and Mia Winters, but shortly after her birth, she is kidnapped by Mother Miranda, who believes Rose's unique abilities, inherited from Ethan's exposure to the Mold, can be used to resurrect her own daughter, Eva. Ethan goes on a quest to rescue Rose from the village in Eastern Europe, battling Miranda and her lieutenants. In the end, Ethan sacrifices himself to destroy Miranda, but Rose is saved and handed over to Chris Redfield, who promises to protect her. Ethan's death and Rose's rescue. Rose is taken into protective custody, and the BSAA becomes involved.

Accounts II
Shadows of Rose: The Legacy of the MoldResident Evil Village: Shadows of Rose DLC

2037 (fictional setting)

16 years later, Rose is a teenager struggling with her powers and the burden of her father's sacrifice. She is contacted by a mysterious entity that claims to be her father, Ethan, who guides her to enter the Mold Realm to find a way to rid herself of her powers. In the Realm, she confronts the remnants of Miranda's influence and the memories of the past. She learns that the entity is actually a fragment of Miranda's consciousness, and she must destroy it to free herself. She ultimately succeeds, accepting her powers and her identity as a Winters. Rose gains control over her powers and finds peace with her past. She decides to embrace her heritage and use her abilities to help others.

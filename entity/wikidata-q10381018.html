The Approach

In the bamboo forests of Eientei, where the moon's shadow falls and the rabbits whisper, there lives a trickster who is older than she looks, younger than she acts, and far more dangerous than her cute facade suggests. Tewi Inaba, the 'Lucky Rabbit of Eientei,' is not just a leader of rabbits—she is a weaver of fortune, a master of deception, and a living enigma whose true age and motives are as elusive as the luck she dispenses.

Content advisory. No explicit dark material. Contains themes of deception, manipulation, and implied age ambiguity, but presented in a comedic, lighthearted context typical of Touhou Project.
Identity

Tewi Inaba

Pronunciation TEH-wee ee-NAH-bah (Japanese: てゐ いなば)

Known As

Tei InabaLucky Rabbit of EienteiLeader of the Eientei Rabbits

Etymology

The name 'Tewi' (てゐ) is a pun on 'tewi' (手弱) meaning 'weak-handed' or 'delicate', but also references the Inaba of Japanese folklore, a white rabbit. The surname 'Inaba' (因幡) comes from the province of Inaba, associated with the Hare of Inaba myth.

Cultural Ownership

Touhou Project, created by ZUN (Team Shanghai Alice). Tewi is a fictional character from Japanese dōjin game culture.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans speculate Tewi is the same as the Hare of Inaba from Japanese mythology, but this is not confirmed in canon. Her true age is unknown and deliberately ambiguous.

Description

Appearance

Tewi is a young girl with short, light-brown hair and red eyes. She wears a white shirt with a red ribbon, a red skirt, and a white apron. She often has a mischievous grin. In 'Flower View', she is depicted with a flower in her hair.

Symbols & Iconography

Associated with rabbits, bamboo, and the moon. Her name and design evoke the Hare of Inaba. Often depicted with a carrot or a fan.

Behaviour & Personality

Tewi is a trickster, known for her cunning and manipulative nature. She enjoys playing pranks and deceiving others, but she is also protective of her rabbit subordinates. She is confident, playful, and rarely serious, but her actions often have hidden motives.

Domains & Habitats

Eientei, the mansion of the Lunarians on Earth, located in the Bamboo Forest of the Lost. She is the leader of the earth rabbits there.

Version Differences

In earlier games (e.g., 'Touhou 8: Imperishable Night'), her name was spelled 'Tei' in English. Her design has remained consistent, but her role has expanded from a minor NPC to a playable character in 'Flower View'.

Powers & Abilities

Luck Manipulation

Source Version Perfect Memento in Strict Sense; game dialogue

Description

Tewi has the ability to manipulate luck, granting good fortune to herself or others, or causing misfortune to enemies. This is a passive and often unpredictable ability.

Limitations

The exact extent and control are unclear; it may be subconscious or limited to certain actions.

Contradictions

Some sources imply it is a passive blessing, while others suggest she can actively use it.

Deception and Trickery

Source Version Game dialogue, print works

Description

Tewi is a master liar and manipulator, able to deceive even the most perceptive individuals. She uses this to lead intruders astray in the bamboo forest.

Limitations

Her tricks sometimes fail or backfire.

Contradictions

None noted.

Immortality / Longevity

Source Version Implied in various works; fan speculation

Description

Tewi is implied to be very old, possibly centuries old, though she appears as a child. The exact nature of her longevity is unknown.

Limitations

Not explicitly confirmed; she may simply be a long-lived youkai.

Contradictions

Some fans theorize she is the Hare of Inaba, which would make her thousands of years old, but this is unconfirmed.

Rabbit Leadership

Source Version Game and print works

Description

As the leader of the Eientei rabbits, she commands their loyalty and can organize them for tasks.

Limitations

Her authority is not absolute; rabbits may disobey.

Contradictions

None.

Stories & Myths

The Flower Viewing Incident

Source Phantasmagoria of Flower View (2005)Culture Touhou ProjectPeriod 2005

Summary

During an abnormal spring where flowers bloom excessively, Tewi goes out to play without informing anyone, leaving Reisen to search for her. She later appears as a playable character, engaging in battles with others investigating the incident.

Participants

Tewi Inaba

Reisen Udongein Inaba

Other heroines

Consequences

The incident is resolved, but Tewi's role is minor; she is more interested in having fun than solving the mystery.

Variants

In her story mode, she fights various characters and provides humorous commentary.

The Hare of Inaba (Folklore Parallel)

Source Japanese mythologyCulture Japanese folklorePeriod Ancient

Summary

The Hare of Inaba is a rabbit who tricks a shark into carrying it across the sea, but is later skinned by the shark. It is saved by a prince, who heals it. Tewi shares the name and rabbit theme, but her story is not directly connected.

Participants

Hare of Inaba

Prince Ōnamuji

Consequences

The hare becomes a symbol of luck and cunning.

Variants

Tewi's character is a modern reinterpretation, but no direct connection is stated.

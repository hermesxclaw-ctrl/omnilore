Who It Is

In the bamboo forests of Eientei, where the moon's shadow falls and the rabbits whisper, there lives a trickster who is older than she looks, younger than she acts, and far more dangerous than her cute facade suggests. Tewi Inaba, the 'Lucky Rabbit of Eientei,' is not just a leader of rabbits—she is a weaver of fortune, a master of deception, and a living enigma whose true age and motives are as elusive as the luck she dispenses.

Tewi is a trickster, known for her cunning and manipulative nature. She enjoys playing pranks and deceiving others, but she is also protective of her rabbit subordinates. She is confident, playful, and rarely serious, but her actions often have hidden motives.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Tewi' (てゐ) is a pun on 'tewi' (手弱) meaning 'weak-handed' or 'delicate', but also references the Inaba of Japanese folklore, a white rabbit. The surname 'Inaba' (因幡) comes from the province of Inaba, associated with the Hare of Inaba myth.

Pronunciation — TEH-wee ee-NAH-bah (Japanese: てゐ いなば)

Aliases — Tei Inaba, Lucky Rabbit of Eientei, Leader of the Eientei Rabbits

Ownership — Touhou Project, created by ZUN (Team Shanghai Alice). Tewi is a fictional character from Japanese dōjin game culture.

Powers
Luck Manipulation

Tewi has the ability to manipulate luck, granting good fortune to herself or others, or causing misfortune to enemies. This is a passive and often unpredictable ability.

Limit: The exact extent and control are unclear; it may be subconscious or limited to certain actions.

Deception and Trickery

Tewi is a master liar and manipulator, able to deceive even the most perceptive individuals. She uses this to lead intruders astray in the bamboo forest.

Limit: Her tricks sometimes fail or backfire.

Immortality / Longevity

Tewi is implied to be very old, possibly centuries old, though she appears as a child. The exact nature of her longevity is unknown.

Limit: Not explicitly confirmed; she may simply be a long-lived youkai.

Rabbit Leadership

As the leader of the Eientei rabbits, she commands their loyalty and can organize them for tasks.

Limit: Her authority is not absolute; rabbits may disobey.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Tewi is a young girl with short, light-brown hair and red eyes. She wears a white shirt with a red ribbon, a red skirt, and a white apron. She often has a mischievous grin. In 'Flower View', she is depicted with a flower in her hair.

Symbols — Associated with rabbits, bamboo, and the moon. Her name and design evoke the Hare of Inaba. Often depicted with a carrot or a fan.

Domains — Eientei, the mansion of the Lunarians on Earth, located in the Bamboo Forest of the Lost. She is the leader of the earth rabbits there.

Accounts I
The Flower Viewing IncidentPhantasmagoria of Flower View (2005)

2005

During an abnormal spring where flowers bloom excessively, Tewi goes out to play without informing anyone, leaving Reisen to search for her. She later appears as a playable character, engaging in battles with others investigating the incident. The incident is resolved, but Tewi's role is minor; she is more interested in having fun than solving the mystery.

Accounts II
The Hare of Inaba (Folklore Parallel)Japanese mythology

Ancient

The Hare of Inaba is a rabbit who tricks a shark into carrying it across the sea, but is later skinned by the shark. It is saved by a prince, who heals it. Tewi shares the name and rabbit theme, but her story is not directly connected. The hare becomes a symbol of luck and cunning.

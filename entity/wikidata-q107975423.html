Who It Is

Beneath the palace of Alabasta, the desert floor ripples like water — and then a stout, sunglass-wearing mole-woman erupts from solid stone, claws flashing and laughing "Mogumogu." Miss Merry Christmas is not festive at all: she is Baroque Works' subterranean trap-maker, a Devil Fruit mole-human who turns battlefields into sinkholes and drags heroes screaming underground.

Attested as arrogant, condescending and talkative, especially toward younger opponents. She lectures Usopp and Chopper on the superiority of moles, calls humans who walk on the surface foolish, and delights in psychological torment (turning ground to quicksand to slowly bury victims). She is loyal to Crocodile's Operation Utopia but also bickers with partner Mr. 4, who is slow and taciturn — she often finishes his sentences or scolds him. Powers
Mogu Mogu no Mi (Mole-Mole Fruit)

Zoan-type Devil Fruit that allows user to transform into a mole and mole-human hybrid, gaining mole physiology: powerful digging claws, enhanced smell, and ability to move through earth as if swimming. User can excavate tunnels at high speed and emerge for surprise attacks.

Limit: Standard Devil Fruit weaknesses: cannot swim, seawater and Seastone (Kairoseki) nullify powers and cause paralysis. Transformation does not grant superhuman strength beyond mole scaling; defeated by combined Usopp/Chopper ingenuity rather than Haki. Cannot dig through metal (shown struggling with palace steel).

Mogurazuka Highway (Mole Hill Highway)

Technique where she digs a network of tunnels and softens the surrounding ground, creating pitfall traps that collapse under victims' weight, pulling them underground.

Limit: Requires earthen/stone ground; ineffective on solid metal or ice. Trap can be escaped if victim anchors to solid object (Chopper uses Usopp's rope).

Ground Softening / Pitfall Control

By digging rapidly, she destabilizes surface tension, turning solid palace floor into loose, quicksand-like terrain that slowly sinks opponents, allowing her to bury them alive while taunting.

Limit: Slow-acting; observant opponents can avoid by staying on debris or jumping. She must remain nearby to maintain tunnel network.

Enhanced Claws and Burrowing Combat

Hybrid claws deliver slashing attacks and allow high-speed underground movement for hit-and-run. Can drag opponents underground by the legs and attack from below.

Limit: Claws are short-range; vulnerable when surfacing. Relies on partner Mr. 4's heavy artillery (Lassoo's baseball bombs) for ranged damage.

Accounts I
Summons to Spider's Cafe - Operation Utopia BeginsOne Piece manga Chapter 160; anime Episode 103

2001 (in-universe: Alabasta, Age of Pirates)

Crocodile, leader of Baroque Works, summons all Officer Agents to Spider's Cafe at 8 o'clock to unveil Operation Utopia: inciting civil war between Alabasta's Royal Army and Rebel Army to seize the kingdom and its Poneglyph. Miss Merry Christmas appears in silhouette alongside Mr. 4, Mr. 2 Bon Kurei, Mr. 1 and Miss Doublefinger. She is assigned with Mr. 4 to Alubarna Palace to intercept Princess Vivi's group. Establishes her as Officer Agent rank (second-highest tier) and pairs her permanently with Mr. 4. Sets up Alubarna confrontation.

Infiltration of Alubarna Palace TunnelsManga Chapters 167-183; anime Episodes 106-108

2001

As the Straw Hats split to stop the war, Usopp and Chopper are tasked with protecting the palace. Miss Merry Christmas and Mr. 4 ambush them in the underground passage connecting the palace to the city. She uses her mole powers to collapse the floor and separate the duo, boasting that moles are supreme underground. Creates isolated 2-vs-2 battle that tests Usopp and Chopper's teamwork without Luffy or Zoro.

Battle: Mole vs. Courage and Brain - Defeat in the TunnelsManga Chapters 184-185: 'Mogura Banana' ; anime Episodes 109-110

2001

Full fight: Miss Merry Christmas turns the palace bedrock into a network of pits, repeatedly dragging Usopp underground and burying Chopper up to his neck. She and Mr. 4 combine tactics — she traps victims, he and Lassoo bombard them with exploding baseballs. Usopp overcomes fear, uses Chopper's Horn Point as a lever, and tricks her into surfacing where Chopper (in Arm Point) slams her with Kokutei Roseo Metel. She is launched into Mr. 4 and both are knocked out by Lassoo's misfired bomb. Officer Agent duo defeated, allowing Usopp and Chopper to prevent palace bombing. Demonstrates theme that courage + intellect beat brute trickery. She and Mr. 4 are left unconscious for Marine arrest.

Accounts II
Arrest and Imprisonment After Alabasta's LiberationManga Chapter 210-211; anime Episode 127

2001-2002

After Crocodile's defeat and the end of the civil war, Marines under Captain Hina capture surviving Baroque Works agents. Miss Merry Christmas is shown bound alongside Mr. 4 and other agents, being transported on a Marine prison ship. Her mole powers are neutralized by Seastone cuffs. Removes Baroque Works as active threat; transitions characters to cover-story redemption arc. Confirms Devil Fruit weakness to Seastone.

Miss Goldenweek's Operation: Meet Baroque Works - Escape and New Spider's CafeManga cover story Chapters 359-413 (installments 382, 386, 394, 413); anime Episode 127 recap

2002-2003 (cover serial)

In the serialized cover story, Miss Goldenweek, Mr. 5 and Miss Valentine break their comrades out of prison. Miss Merry Christmas, still in mole form partially, helps dig an escape tunnel. The group evades Hina, renounces crime, and opens a new Spider's Cafe, now serving as a legitimate coffee shop / orphanage. Final panel shows her happily serving customers with Mr. 4, reformed. Provides canonical post-defeat redemption; explains why former Officer Agents are not seen in later Impel Down / Marineford arcs. Establishes her current status as civilian cafe worker, not prisoner.

Legacy and Databook CodificationOne Piece Vivre Card Databook (2018), One Piece Blue Deep: Characters World (2012), SBS Volume 20

2012-2018

Later databooks codify her real name Drophy, age 49, birthday March 3, height 156cm, blood type X, favorite food mole-flavored peanuts. Oda in SBS clarifies Baroque Works naming puns and confirms Mogu Mogu no Mi is Zoan. She appears in popularity polls (ranked outside top 100) and in video game encyclopedias as unlockable support character. Solidifies her as minor but fully catalogued canon character; prevents future retcon of fruit type.

Miss Merry Christmas is a Christmas-themed reindeer or Santa-like character — She is a mole-human (mogura) with no reindeer traits; 'Christmas' is just Baroque Works holiday codename, not reflective of powers or appearance. Chopper is the reindeer character in the same fight.

She is a male character or Mr. 4 is her husband — She is female (Miss title, voiced by female seiyuu, Vivre Card female marker). Mr. 4 and Miss Merry Christmas are professional partners assigned by Crocodile, not romantic partners or spouses.

She appears in the Netflix live-action One Piece — As of Season 1 (2023, covering East Blue), Alabasta has not been adapted. She has no live-action actress. No casting announced for Season 2 as of 2026-08 cutoff.

Her Devil Fruit lets her control earth like a Paramecia earth-bender — She does not telekinetically control earth; she physically digs through it at high speed and destabilizes it by tunneling. She must touch and excavate; she cannot levitate rocks.

Hunter α/γ (T-Virus Hunter)

also known asT-Virus Hunter
TypeBio-Organic Weapon (B.O.W.) — T-Virus
Who It Is
Form

The Hunter α/γ is a humanoid creature with reptilian features, including scaly skin and a long tail. It has a muscular build and stands approximately 2 meters tall. Its face is a mix of human and reptilian features, with a prominent jaw and sharp teeth.

Powers

Enhanced Strength The Hunter α/γ possesses enhanced strength, allowing it to overpower and subdue its opponents with ease. Its strength is likely a result of its reptilian physiology and the T-Virus's ability to enhance its physical abilities.

Accounts

The first encounter between humans and the Hunter α/γ occurs in the Arklay Mountains, where a group of S.T.A.R.S. members stumble upon the creature. The encounter is marked by a fierce battle between the S.T.A.R.S. members and the Hunter α/γ.

The Umbrella Corporation conducts an experiment to create a new breed of bio-organic weapon, resulting in the creation of the Hunter α/γ. The experiment is a success, but it also has unintended consequences, including the escape of the Hunter α/γ.

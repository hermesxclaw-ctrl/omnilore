Who It Is

Reptilian/amphibian-based B.O.W.s created by combining T-Virus infected human cells with frog and lizard DNA. Hunters are agile, bipedal creatures with sharp claws, powerful legs for leaping, and tough hide that resists low-caliber firearms. The Hunter α (Alpha) is green-scaled with claws. The Hunter γ (Gamma) is amphibious with a large mouth that can swallow a human whole. Hunters are among Umbrella's most successful mass-production B.O.W.s.

Who It Is

Albasty is a female nightmare spirit who attacks women in childbirth and sits on sleepers' chests causing paralysis. Described with tangled yellow hair and pendulous breasts, she fears iron. Protections include iron objects, red cloth, and 40-day vigils. The belief reflects the dangers of childbirth and infant mortality in traditional societies.

Attested in Oral tradition / Turkic folklore (pre-Islamic)Era Ancient to present

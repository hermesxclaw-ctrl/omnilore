Who It Is
Form

Albasty is often depicted as a large, yellow-haired woman with big feet and a thunderous voice. She is said to wear white clothes and is often described as the tallest woman in her domain.

Powers

Nightmare Inducement Albasty has the ability to induce nightmares and guilt in those who encounter her. She is said to use her thunderous voice and yellow hair to hypnotize her victims and bring them under her control.

Accounts

This story tells the tale of a young woman who encounters Albasty in the Caucasus Mountains. The woman is said to be haunted by Albasty's nightmares and guilt, which ultimately lead to her downfall.

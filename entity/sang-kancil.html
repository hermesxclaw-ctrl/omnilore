Who It Is

Sang Kancil is the beloved trickster-hero of Malay folklore — a tiny mouse-deer who survives through wit. His most famous tale involves convincing crocodiles to line up across a river so he can 'count' them, hopping across their backs to escape. He embodies the Malay appreciation for intelligence over brute strength.

Who It Is

In the spring of 2001, a chestnut colt with a physicist's name and a sprinter's heart tore through Japan's racing elite, winning all four of his starts with a different tactic each time—then vanished from the track as suddenly as he had arrived, leaving behind a legacy that would reshape the breed.

On the track, Agnes Tachyon showed versatility and a strong will, capable of leading or closing from behind. Off the track, he was known for his fragile legs, which ultimately ended his career. In Umamusume, the character is portrayed as an eccentric and obsessive scientist.

Racing ability

Won all four starts, including the Satsuki Sho (Grade 1), showcasing speed, stamina, and tactical versatility.

Limit: Career cut short by a bowed tendon (superficial flexor tendonitis) in the left front leg.

Sire ability

Became a leading sire in Japan in 2008, producing Grade 1 winners such as Daiwa Scarlet and Deep Sky.

Limit: None noted

Form & Signs

Appearance — Chestnut colt, typical Thoroughbred conformation. No distinctive markings are recorded in available sources.

Symbols — In the Umamusume franchise, the character is depicted with a lab coat and scientific motifs, reflecting her mad-scientist personality.

Domains — Trained in Japan, raced on turf tracks. Stud at Shadai Stallion Station in Hokkaido.

Who It Is

Maria Makiling is the guardian diwata of Mount Makiling — a beautiful fairy who protects the mountain's flora and fauna. She fell in love with a mortal who betrayed her, after which she withdrew into the mountain and her blessings ceased. Locals report sightings of a mysterious woman in the mist and unexplained gifts.

Attested in Oral tradition / Tagalog folklore (19th century)Era Spanish colonial period to present

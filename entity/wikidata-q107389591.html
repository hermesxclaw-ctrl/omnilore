Who It Is

Born into a dynasty of vampire hunters, Richter Belmont is the last of his bloodline to face Dracula in the classic era of the Castlevania saga—and the first to lose his soul to the very darkness he swore to destroy. His fall, and his redemption, redefine what it means to be a Belmont.

In the original games, Richter is portrayed as confident, brave, and somewhat cocky. He is determined to defeat Dracula and protect his loved ones. In Symphony of the Night, his possession by Shaft shows a darker, more aggressive side, but he is ultimately freed and regains his honor.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Richter is a German surname meaning 'judge'. Belmont is a Norman-French name meaning 'beautiful mountain'.

Pronunciation — RIKH-ter BEL-mont

Aliases — リヒター・ベルモンド, Richter Belmondo, The Last Belmont, The One Who Fell

Ownership — Owned by Konami. Created by the Castlevania development team. Richter first appeared in 1993's Akumajō Dracula X: Chi no Rondo.

Powers
Vampire Killer Whip

The legendary whip passed down through the Belmont clan. It deals massive damage to creatures of the night.

Limit: Only effective in the hands of a Belmont or someone of the bloodline.

Sub-Weapons

Richter can use various sub-weapons like the axe, cross, holy water, and dagger, each with unique properties.

Limit: Requires hearts as ammunition.

Item Crashes

A powerful attack that consumes multiple hearts to unleash a devastating version of a sub-weapon, such as the Grand Cross or Hydro Storm.

Limit: High heart cost, leaves Richter vulnerable temporarily.

Martial Arts

Richter is highly agile, able to perform backflips, slide kicks, and wall jumps.

Limit: None.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Richter is a young man with shoulder-length brown hair, often tied back. He wears a white and blue tunic, white pants, and a red sash. His primary weapon is a leather whip, the Vampire Killer. In Symphony of the Night, he has a more mature look with a ponytail and a darker outfit.

Symbols — The Vampire Killer whip is his iconic symbol. He also uses the Cross sub-weapon and the Holy Water. His profile often shows him in a heroic stance with the whip coiled.

Domains — Richter operates in Wallachia, Romania, primarily in and around Dracula's castle. He is a vampire hunter, so his domain is wherever evil lurks.

Accounts I
Rondo of BloodAkumajō Dracula X: Chi no Rondo (1993)

1792 (in-universe)

Dracula is resurrected by the dark priest Shaft and kidnaps several young women, including Richter's girlfriend Annette and her sister Maria. Richter storms Dracula's castle, rescues the hostages, and defeats Dracula and Shaft. Richter becomes a hero, but the battle leaves a dark seed in his mind.

Symphony of the NightCastlevania: Symphony of the Night (1997)

1796 (in-universe)

Five years after Rondo, Richter goes missing. Alucard, Dracula's half-vampire son, investigates and finds Richter under the control of Shaft, who is using him to resurrect Dracula. Alucard defeats Richter, freeing him from the mind control, and then battles Dracula himself. Richter is shamed by his possession and relinquishes the Vampire Killer, leading to the Morris family taking up the whip. This sets up the events of Bloodlines and Portrait of Ruin.

Accounts II
Lords of Shadow – Mirror of FateCastlevania: Lords of Shadow – Mirror of Fate (2013)

Alternate timeline

In this reboot, Richter is the son of Trevor and Sypha. After his parents die, he is raised by the Brotherhood of Light and becomes a vampire hunter. He confronts Dracula, who is actually his grandfather Gabriel, and is killed, but later is resurrected as a vampire and takes the name Alucard. Richter becomes Alucard and fights alongside his father in Lords of Shadow 2.

Castlevania: NocturneNetflix animated series (2023-present)

French Revolution

Richter is a young vampire hunter in 1792 France, fighting against a vampire messiah named Erzsebet Báthory. He grapples with his family legacy and his own insecurities. Richter grows into his role as a leader and embraces his heritage.

Who It Is
Form

Zant is a tall, slender figure with dark, shadowy skin and eyes that glow with an otherworldly energy. He wears a twisted, corrupted version of the Twili royal attire, with dark, tattered robes and a crown of twisted, thorn-like growths. His presence is accompanied by an aura of darkness and shadow, which seems to seep from his very being.

Powers

Dark Magic Zant possesses the ability to wield dark, magical powers that allow him to manipulate the shadows and bend reality to his will.

Incident Log

Zant is the primary antagonist of the game, and seeks to overthrow the ruler of Hyrule and claim the throne for himself. He is aided by the power of the Fused Shadow, a dark, magical artifact that allows him to tap into the power of the Twilight Realm.

Zant is a powerful, shadowy figure who seeks to exploit the darkness within others for his own gain. He is driven by a desire for power and control, and is willing to do whatever it takes to achieve his goals.

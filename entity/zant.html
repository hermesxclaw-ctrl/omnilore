Form & Signs

Appearance — Zant is a tall, slender figure with dark, shadowy skin and eyes that glow with an otherworldly energy. He wears a twisted, corrupted version of the Twili royal attire, with dark, tattered robes and a crown of twisted, thorn-like growths. His presence is accompanied by an aura of darkness and shadow, which seems to seep from his very being.

Symbols — The symbol of the Twili, a stylized, crescent moon shape, is often associated with Zant's dark powers. He also wields a dark, twisted version of the Fused Shadow, a powerful magical artifact.

Domains — The Twilight Realm, a dark, shadowy world that exists in parallel to the land of Hyrule.

Accounts I
The Usurper KingThe Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess

2006

Zant is the primary antagonist of the game, and seeks to overthrow the ruler of Hyrule and claim the throne for himself. He is aided by the power of the Fused Shadow, a dark, magical artifact that allows him to tap into the power of the Twilight Realm. The defeat of Zant at the hands of Link has significant consequences for the land of Hyrule, as it allows the light of the Triforce to shine through and banish the darkness of the Twilight Realm.

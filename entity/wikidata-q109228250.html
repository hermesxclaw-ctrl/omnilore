Who It Is

He doesn't remember his own name without the drug in his veins. When the blockers wear off, Clotho Buer clutches his head and screams — a blond teenager in an Earth Alliance flight suit, bred to kill Coordinators and then discarded like an empty syringe. He is not a Newtype, not a Coordinator, but something crueler: a Biological CPU, a human tuned for war until his body breaks.

Attested behavior across episodes 35-49 shows extreme instability, impulsivity, and childlike dependency. On Gamma-Glipheptin, he is manic, bloodthirsty, and ecstatic in combat, laughing and shouting phrases like 'I can see it! I can see it!' and competing with Orga and Shani for kills. Off the drug, he suffers acute withdrawal: disorientation, amnesia ('Where... where am I?'), headaches, vomiting, and terror, becoming docile and confused, asking handlers for the drug. Powers
Biological CPU Enhancement (Gamma-Glipheptin Dependent)

As a first-generation Biological CPU, Clotho has undergone extensive pharmacological, surgical, and conditioning procedures to give a Natural the reflexes, spatial awareness, and endurance to pilot a high-performance Gundam against Coordinators. On-drug, he exhibits reaction times comparable to elite Coordinators and SEED-factor users, can handle the G-forces of Raider's MA mode, and maintains combat effectiveness for extended sorties.

Limit: Entirely drug-dependent. Without regular doses of Gamma-Glipheptin, he suffers severe withdrawal within hours: tremors, hallucinations, memory loss, incapacitation. Requires handlers to administer drug and to retrieve him if he collapses. Lifespan and long-term health severely compromised; official materials imply shortened life expectancy and brain damage. Cannot function independently outside military medical supervision.

Mobile Suit Piloting – GAT-X370 Raider Gundam

Exclusive pilot of the Raider Gundam. Master of its transformable frame: able to switch between MS mode (humanoid with 100mm energy cannons, 52mm super-penetrating guns) and MA mode (high-speed flight with enhanced ramming). Signature weapon is the Mjolnir (Mjølnir) – a large flail-headed hammer on a flexible cable that can be electrified and thrown, then retracted. Also uses 52mm machine guns and the Raider's shield.

Limit: Raider is less heavily armed than Calamity and less defensively versatile than Forbidden; relies on speed and melee. Mjolnir is short-to-mid range and can be countered by beam sabers (as Kira does). Transformation requires open space; vulnerable during transition. Clotho's recklessness often leaves him overextended.

Conditioned Combat Frenzy and Kill-Count Competition

Psychologically conditioned to derive euphoria from killing, especially Coordinators, and to compete with Orga and Shani for highest kill count. This conditioning, combined with the drug high, makes him fearless and unpredictable, often breaking formation to chase targets.

Limit: Impairs tactical judgment; frequently disobeys orders, endangers allies, and falls for traps (e.g., lured by Yzak in final battle). Frenzy collapses into panic when drug fades.

No Innate Coordinator or Newtype Spatial Awareness

Unlike Kira, Athrun, or Mu/Rau, Clotho does not possess natural spatial awareness or Coordinator engineering. His abilities are entirely artificial and inferior to true SEED users at peak.

Limit: Cannot activate SEED mode; cannot match Kira's or Athrun's peak performance. Defeated by both Freedom and Duel in direct duels.

Accounts I
Activation – The Three Biological CPUs UnleashedMobile Suit Gundam SEED Episode 35 'The Descending Sword' (舞い降りる剣)

C.E. 71, May (in-universe); aired 2003-06-07

After the Earth Alliance's defeat at JOSH-A and the loss of the original GAT-X series, Muruta Azrael introduces his new weapons to Captain William Sutherland of the Dominion: three Biological CPUs — Clotho Buer, Orga Sabnak, and Shani Andras — each assigned a stolen Gundam (Raider, Calamity, Forbidden). They are shown in a medical bay receiving Gamma-Glipheptin injections, with Clotho laughing maniacally and complaining about the wait. Sutherland is warned they are unstable but effective. Their first sortie is ordered to intercept the Archangel and Kusanagi as they descend toward Orb. Establishes the Biological CPU program as Azrael's answer to ZAFT's Coordinators and sets up the trio as recurring antagonists for the second half of SEED. Introduces drug dependency as plot device.

Battle of Orb – The Raider's First RampageMobile Suit Gundam SEED Episodes 36-40 (esp. Episode 39 'Trembling World')

C.E. 71, June 15 – Battle of Orb

The Earth Alliance invades the neutral nation of Orb to seize its mass driver. Clotho in Raider Gundam, alongside Orga and Shani, is deployed from the Dominion to overwhelm Orb's M1 Astrays and the Archangel's defenses. Clotho transforms Raider to MA mode and uses Mjolnir to smash through Orb defenses, laughing as he destroys multiple Astrays. He engages Kira Yamato in Freedom for the first time, but is driven back when Athrun in Justice intervenes. Later, as Orb self-destructs its mass driver, the trio is forced to retreat. Clotho is shown post-battle suffering withdrawal, begging Sutherland for his 'medicine' while Orga mocks him. Demonstrates that the Biological CPUs can pressure even Kira and Athrun working together, but also that they lack coordination. Orb's destruction pushes the Archangel into space toward Mendel and the final war phase.

Mendel Colony – Confrontation with the TruthMobile Suit Gundam SEED Episodes 41-42 'Trembling Hearts' / 'Lacus Strikes'

C.E. 71, late June – Mendel Colony

Archangel and Dominion both arrive at the ruined Mendel colony, where Kira learns of his origins as an Ultimate Coordinator. Clotho, Orga, and Shani are sent to destroy Archangel. In the debris field, Clotho ambushes Kira, using Raider's MA speed to ram Freedom. He shouts about kill counts, arguing with Orga over who will take Freedom. Kira, distracted by revelations about his birth, initially struggles but eventually disables Raider's arm with beam saber. The trio withdraws when ZAFT forces (Yzak, Dearka) also appear, creating a three-way battle. Post-battle, Clotho collapses from withdrawal in Dominion's hangar and is dragged away by medics. Highlights Clotho's role as a narrative foil: while Kira grapples with existential identity, Clotho has no identity beyond the drug. Foreshadows that Biological CPUs are disposable tools, unlike Kira's engineered purpose.

Second Battle of JOSH-A? No – Battle of Boaz and the Road to Jachin DueMobile Suit Gundam SEED Episodes 47-48 'The Nightmare Reborn' / 'Day of Wrath'

C.E. 71, September 23-24 – Space Battles of Boaz and Second Battle of Jachin Due prelude

As the war escalates to its final space battles, the Dominion is attached to the Earth Alliance fleet attacking ZAFT's asteroid fortress Boaz. Clotho and the other CPUs are deployed en masse against ZAFT's GuAIZ and ZAKU forces. Clotho in Raider MA mode cackles while tearing through ZAFT mobile suits, using Mjolnir to crush a GuAIZ cockpit. He briefly duels Dearka in Buster Gundam, who notes Clotho fights 'like an animal.' Azrael orders the Dominion to fire on both ZAFT and, if necessary, Archangel, showing the CPUs are expected to die in the crossfire. Clotho's drug intervals shorten, indicating his body is failing. Shows the Earth Alliance's increasing desperation and the CPUs' expendability. Boaz falls to the Alliance's nuclear attack, setting up the final battle at Jachin Due where the CPUs will meet their end.

Death at Jachin Due – Killed by the DuelMobile Suit Gundam SEED Episode 49 'The Final Light' (終末の光) and Episode 50 'To a Future that Never Ends'

C.E. 71, September 27 – Second Battle of Jachin Due

In the final chaotic battle around the GENESIS gamma-ray laser, Clotho, Orga, and Shani are ordered to protect the Dominion and destroy Freedom and Justice. Clotho, now visibly deteriorating and taking Gamma-Glipheptin more frequently, charges Kira in Freedom but is intercepted by Yzak Joule in the GAT-X102 Duel Gundam (now with Assault Shroud). Yzak, seeking revenge for the deaths caused by the Biological CPUs and for his own comrades, engages Raider in close combat. Clotho, overconfident and in drug-induced frenzy, attempts to use Mjolnir at point-blank range. Yzak blocks with Duel's beam saber and shield, then at close range fires Duel's 115mm railgun and beam rifle into Raider's cockpit and reactor. Raider explodes, killing Clotho instantly. His death is shown without sentiment — a quick explosion, with Orga and Shani continuing to fight for minutes before they too are killed (Orga by Athrun, Shani by Yzak/Kira). Legacy – The Extended and the Ghost of the Biological CPUMobile Suit Gundam SEED Destiny Episodes 1, 12, 34; Gundam SEED Freedom (2024) archival mention

C.E. 73-75 (Destiny/Freedom era); aired 2004-2024

Clotho does not appear alive in Destiny, but his program's legacy is central. In SEED Destiny, Captain Neo Roanoke (Mu La Flaga) commands the second-generation Extended — Stellar Loussier, Sting Oakley, and Auel Neider — who are explicitly described by Gilbert Durandal and by EA scientists as improved Biological CPUs with better memory retention and less frequent drug needs, addressing the flaws that killed Clotho's generation. Archival footage of Raider, Calamity, and Forbidden is shown in Destiny Episode 34 when Durandal explains the history of the Biological CPU program to Athrun. In Gundam SEED Freedom (2024), the Black Knight Squad's conditioning is contrasted with the old Biological CPU program in official pamphlet interviews, with staff noting Clotho's generation was 'crude.' Frames Clotho as prototype for Destiny's more tragic Extended, cementing his thematic role. Fandom and official retrospectives often pair Clotho/Orga/Shani with Stellar/Sting/Auel as two generations of the same war crime.

Clotho Buer is an Extended like Stellar Loussier. — Clotho is a first-generation Biological CPU (C.E. 71), while Stellar, Sting, and Auel are second-generation Extended (C.E. 73) with improved block-word conditioning and longer drug intervals. They share a program lineage but have different technology, handlers (Sutherland vs. Neo Roanoke), and stability.

Clotho can use SEED mode (the exploding seed eye effect). — He never activates SEED factor in any canonical anime, manga, or game cutscene. His enhanced performance is entirely due to Gamma-Glipheptin and conditioning, which is visually depicted as dilated pupils and sweating, not the SEED iris effect reserved for Kira, Athrun, Lacus, etc.

Raider Gundam is a ZAFT mobile suit. — Raider (GAT-X370) is an Earth Alliance Gundam, one of three units (with Calamity and Forbidden) developed from stolen ZAFT data at Heliopolis/Orb, then assigned to Biological CPUs. It bears Earth Alliance designation GAT-X, not ZAFT's ZGMF.

Clotho survived Jachin Due and appears in SEED Destiny. — He definitively dies at Jachin Due in Episode 49. Any appearance in Destiny is archival flashback. Super Robot Wars secret routes where he survives are non-canonical game-only branches.

Who It Is

He was too big to fit through the gate, so he moved the gate — and the battleship behind it. When Little Oars Jr. charged across the frozen bay of Marineford, screaming Ace's name, the entire war had to make room for a debt of friendship that began with a straw hat far too small for his head.

Attested as fiercely loyal, gentle, and single-minded. Despite his terrifying size, Jr. is soft-spoken and emotionally open — he weeps openly when recalling Ace's kindness. His core motive, stated in Chapter 556, is repayment: Ace, during his early piracy, befriended the isolated Jr. in the cold seas and, learning Jr. had no hat that fit, spent time knitting a huge cap for him. Jr. declared Ace his benefactor and later pledged his crew to Whitebeard specifically because Whitebeard was Ace's captain (Ch. 554-555). Powers
Ancient Giant Physiology - Colossal Strength

As a descendant of the Continent-Puller Oars, Jr. possesses strength far beyond normal giants. Attested feat: lifts and drags a full Marine battleship with one hand to use as a bridge over the bay wall (Ch. 554), smashes through the fortified Marineford outer wall and crushes Marine squads, artillery, and Pacifista scaffolding underfoot. Can throw Marines and cannons like pebbles. No formal Doriki or Haki attested.

Limit: Size makes him an easy target for artillery, lasers, and Warlord abilities; cannot dodge. Strength is purely physical, not augmented by Haki or Devil Fruit in canon.

Ancient Giant Physiology - Extreme Durability and Endurance

Survives sustained cannon fire, direct Ursus Shock and pad cannon blasts from Bartholomew Kuma (Ch. 556) that crater the ground, and continues advancing while bleeding heavily. Even after Doflamingo severs his leg, he remains conscious and attempts to crawl toward Ace. Whitebeard Pirates note that only an ancient giant could endure that much punishment and still move.

Limit: Not invulnerable. Kuma's attacks cause severe burns and internal damage; Doflamingo's strings can cut his flesh and bone; Moria's shadow theft attempt would have incapacitated him if not interrupted. He is ultimately incapacitated and requires medical attention post-war; he does not regenerate.

Willpower / Loyalty-Driven Advance

Not a supernatural power, but narratively treated as an ability: his devotion to Ace allows him to ignore pain and continue his rescue attempt beyond normal limits, mirroring Whitebeard's own endurance. This thematic 'power' is what lets him breach Marineford's defenses when strategy would have held him back.

Limit: Emotional drive makes him reckless; he ignores Whitebeard's tactical order to wait, leading to his maiming. It does not grant tactical advantage.

No Devil Fruit, No Weapon Mastery, No Haki (Attested Absence)

Canon explicitly shows Jr. fighting unarmed, with no Devil Fruit, no sword/gun technique, and no use of Busoshoku or Haoshoku Haki. His threat is purely biological. This absence is itself a data point, distinguishing him from Warlords and commanders who rely on fruits/Haki.

Limit: Lack of Haki or fruit makes him vulnerable to Logia intangibility and Warlord hax (Doflamingo strings, Kuma repulsion, Moria shadow theft).

Accounts I
The Hat That Made a Giant - Ace and Little Oars Jr.'s DebtOne Piece Chapter 556: 'Oars and the Hat'

2009 (in-story: c. 2-3 years before Marineford, during Ace's early voyage after leaving Whitebeard's protection)

In a cold, desolate sea, Portgas D. Ace, captain of the Spade Pirates before joining Whitebeard, encountered the isolated Little Oars Jr., who lamented that no hat in the world fit his giant head and he suffered from sun and snow. Ace, moved, spent days knitting an enormous wool cap with ear flaps, presenting it to Jr. Jr. was overwhelmed, weeping, and declared Ace his lifelong benefactor (aniki-like bond). When Ace later joined Whitebeard, Jr. pledged his entire crew, the Little Pirates, as an allied division under Whitebeard's flag specifically to stay near Ace. This backstory is revealed via flashback as Jr. charges Marineford, explaining why a giant of his stature would risk everything for one man. Establishes Jr.'s motive for Marineford, cements Whitebeard's 'family' ideology (debts of kindness create alliances stronger than territory), and humanizes giants. The hat becomes his defining visual and emotional anchor; he wears it into war.

Breaching Marineford - The Battleship BridgeOne Piece Chapter 554: 'Little Oars Jr. Rampage'

2009 (in-story: Marineford War, Day of Ace's execution, 3 hours before scheduled execution)

As the Whitebeard allied fleet is stalled by Marineford's fortified bay wall and the frozen bay created by Aokiji, Little Oars Jr. defies Whitebeard's order to hold position and charges alone, shouting Ace's name. Marines open fire with cannons and rifles, which bounce off or merely wound him. He reaches the wall, tears a Marine battleship from the water, and lays it across the wall as a bridge, allowing the Whitebeard Pirates to advance. His roar shakes the battlefield, and even the Marines note his lineage: 'That's... Oars' descendant!' Sengoku and the Warlords take notice. Breaks the stalemate of the war's opening phase; forces the Marines to deploy Warlords and Pacifistas. Demonstrates ancient giant threat level and triggers the Warlords' intervention. Jr.'s act is later praised by Whitebeard himself.

Against the Warlords - Kuma, Doflamingo, and MoriaOne Piece Chapters 555-557

2009 (in-story: Marineford War, mid-battle)

After breaching the wall, Jr. is intercepted sequentially by three Warlords. First, Bartholomew Kuma repels his palm strike and fires a point-blank Ursus Shock and laser pad cannons, severely burning his torso. Jr. staggers but continues. Second, Donquixote Doflamingo, laughing, uses his Ito Ito no Mi strings to sever Jr.'s right leg at the knee, felling him in a massive collapse that crushes Marines beneath him. As Jr. lies helpless, Gecko Moria arrives, delighted, and attempts to steal Jr.'s shadow with his Kage Kage no Mi scissors to create a new giant zombie, noting Jr.'s shadow would make an unstoppable corpse. Before Moria can complete the theft, the battlefield shifts as Whitebeard and later Luffy intervene, and Moria is distracted. Jr. remains alive but incapacitated, bleeding out on the plaza. Shows Warlord lethality and the cost of Jr.'s recklessness. His maiming is one of the war's most graphic injuries, underscoring that even ancient giants fall to hax abilities. Accounts II
Whitebeard's Acknowledgment and the Family's AdvanceOne Piece Chapter 560

2009 (in-story: Marineford War, after Jr.'s fall)

Whitebeard, seeing Jr. collapsed and bleeding, shouts to his sons not to let Jr.'s sacrifice be in vain and orders the allied pirates to use the path Jr. created. He personally acknowledges Jr.: 'You did well, Oars Jr.!' The Whitebeard Pirates surge forward over the battleship bridge, using Jr.'s body as cover. Later, as the war escalates with Luffy's arrival and Whitebeard's own rampage, Jr. is seen still alive, being tended to by allied pirates, unable to continue fighting but conscious. His charge is cited by commentators (including Sengoku) as proof of Whitebeard's terrifying ability to inspire loyalty beyond reason. Jr.'s sacrifice becomes a tactical and emotional catalyst for Whitebeard's full assault. Reinforces theme that Whitebeard's empire is built on personal bonds, not fear, contrasting with Kaido and Big Mom's empires among the Four Emperors.

Aftermath - Survival and the New World SilenceOne Piece Chapter 590 (post-war cover story) and Vivre Card databook

2010-2018 (in-story: 2 years after Marineford)

Unlike Whitebeard and Ace, who die and are buried on an unnamed New World island (Ch. 590), Little Oars Jr. is not shown among the dead. The Vivre Card databook (2018) lists him as alive, captain of the Little Pirates, with no death date, implying he survived his injuries and retreated with the remnants of the Whitebeard allied fleet after Shanks ends the war. No post-timeskip manga appearance shows him; his whereabouts during the Payback War (Blackbeard vs. Marco) and the Wano arc are unrecorded. His leg loss is permanent in canon — no prosthetic is shown. Leaves his fate open-ended, a rare surviving ancient giant. His survival preserves the Oars bloodline. Gap: Oda has not revisited him post-timeskip, leaving fan speculation about whether he will reappear in Elbaf or final war arcs.

Lineage Echo - Oars the Continent-Puller and the Ancient Giant LegacyOne Piece Chapters 349-472 (Thriller Bark) and Chapter 963 (Oden flashback) as contextual lore

2007-2009 (Thriller Bark) / 2019 (Wano flashback mentioning ancient giants)

The story of Jr.'s ancestor, Oars, is told via Thriller Bark: 500 years ago, Oars was a monstrous ancient giant who pulled continents together, feared as the 'Continent-Puller,' and was eventually frozen in Thriller Bark. Gecko Moria reanimated Oars' corpse with Luffy's shadow. Jr., as his direct descendant, inherits both the physical enormity and the stigma of that lineage. The Four Emperors context matters here: Big Mom sought giants for her empire, Kaido experimented on giants, and Whitebeard alone earned a giant's voluntary loyalty — highlighting different Emperor philosophies. Jr.'s existence proves the ancient giant bloodline survived into the present, unlike the extinct reputation suggested by Thriller Bark. World-building: establishes ancient giants as distinct from Elbaf giants, explains why Jr. is so much larger than normal giants, and ties Jr. to broader Four Emperors geopolitics. His loyalty to Whitebeard contrasts with Big Mom's failed attempts to ally with Elbaf.

Little Oars Jr. is the same character as Oars, resurrected or reanimated. — They are distinct: Oars is the 500-year-old ancestor whose corpse was made into a zombie by Gecko Moria (Thriller Bark). Little Oars Jr. is his living descendant, a separate individual with his own crew and will. Moria explicitly notes the resemblance and wants Jr.'s shadow to make a new Oars-like zombie, proving they are two bodies.

Little Oars Jr. was a member of the Whitebeard Pirates' 16 divisions or a Four Emperor himself. — He was captain of the independent Little Pirates, one of 43 allied crews under Whitebeard's flag, not a division commander. He is not and never was a Four Emperor; the Emperors at the time were Whitebeard, Shanks, Kaido, and Big Mom (later Blackbeard, Luffy, Buggy).

He ate a Devil Fruit that makes him giant or gives him super strength. — No Devil Fruit is attested. His size and strength are biological, as an ancient giant. Vivre Card and all manga chapters list no fruit; he fights bare-handed and is vulnerable to Devil Fruit hax precisely because he lacks one.

Gecko Moria successfully stole his shadow and he now wanders as a shadowless comatose giant. — Moria attempts to cut his shadow in Chapter 557 but is interrupted by the chaos of the war (and later by Jinbe/Luffy's advance). No panel shows the scissors completing the cut, and Jr. is later shown still conscious and casting a shadow. The theft fails.

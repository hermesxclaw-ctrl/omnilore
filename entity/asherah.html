Who It Is

The great mother-goddess of the Canaanite pantheon, Asherah (Athirat) is the consort of El and the 'Lady of the Sea' (rbt 'atrt ym) who presides over fecundity and the divine feminine principle. As the goddess of the primordial deep from which life emerges, she stands in complex relationship to both El and Baal: she is the matriarch of the pantheon, a mediating figure who petitions El on behalf of younger gods, and the patroness of royal cult whose wooden cult-poles (asherim) were a fixture at high-places throughout the Levant until the reforming monotheism of the Iron Age sought to extirpate her worship. Archaeological evidence from Kuntillet Ajrud and Khirbet el-Qom suggests she was worshipped alongside Yahweh in Israelite popular religion as late as the 8th century BCE.

Who It Is
Form

SCP-4514 is a standard switch knife, approximately 15 cm in length, with a stainless steel blade and a black plastic handle.

Powers

Anomalous Injury SCP-4514's anomalous properties manifest when it injures an individual to the point of causing significant damage or loss of blood, resulting in the individual's death.

Who It Is

A weapon that kills the user's eventual killer — in the past. When someone uses SCP-4514 to kill a person, that person is retroactively erased from having ever killed anyone. The user remembers both timelines — the one where they died and the one where they lived. The memory of their own death never fades.

Form

An otherwise ordinary object that appears different to each user — a gun, a knife, a rock, a word. SCP-4514 is the concept of 'the thing that kills your killer.' Once used, it disappears and a new instance appears in containment within 24 hours.

Powers

Retroactive killer erasure

Causal timeline rewriting

Dual-memory retention

Self-replenishing

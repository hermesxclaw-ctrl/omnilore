Who It Is

In a world where heroes are ranked, monsters rampage, and power levels are measured with obsessive precision, there is a hero who does not fit the system. He is bald, wears a yellow jumpsuit and a red cape, and can end any fight with a single punch. His name is Saitama, and his greatest struggle is not against cosmic horrors, but against the crushing boredom of being too strong. This is the story of a man who achieved absolute power and found it profoundly unsatisfying.

Saitama is apathetic and bored due to his overwhelming strength. He lacks the typical heroic drive, but he became a hero 'for fun' and because it was his childhood dream. He is often oblivious to social cues, blunt, and sarcastic. He has a strong sense of justice but is also lazy and pragmatic. He is easily distracted by sales and food, and he is frustrated that no one recognizes his strength. He is kind-hearted and genuinely helps people, but he does not seek glory. He treats his disciple Genos with a mix of annoyance and affection.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name Saitama is derived from Saitama Prefecture in Japan, the creator ONE's home region. The name is spelled the same in Japanese (サイタマ), but the character's name is written in katakana, not kanji, to emphasize his ordinary, everyman nature.

Pronunciation — sigh-TAH-mah

Aliases — One-Punch Man, Caped Baldy, Bald Cape, Sensei (by Genos), The Strongest Hero

Ownership — Owned by ONE (original creator) and Yusuke Murata (manga artist), published by Shueisha (Tonari no Young Jump) and Viz Media (English). The character originated in a webcomic by ON

Powers
One-Punch Strike

Saitama can defeat any opponent with a single punch. The punch is not necessarily superhumanly fast or powerful in appearance; it is a simple, straight punch. The exact mechanism is unknown, but it is capable of obliterating even the most powerful monsters and villains. The punch can be delivered with any part of his body, such as a headbutt or a flick, and it is always lethal unless he deliberately holds back.

Limit: He cannot use this power against opponents he perceives as non-threatening, such as mosquitoes, or when he is not serious. He also holds back against humans, using a 'normal punch' that can still kill. He has never faced an opponent who required him to use his full strength.

Superhuman Physical Attributes

Saitama possesses immense strength, speed, durability, and reflexes. He can jump from the moon back to Earth, survive in a vacuum, and move faster than the eye can see. He has been shown to be completely immune to damage from attacks that can level cities. His physical abilities are far beyond any other character in the series.

Limit: No known limitations, but he can be affected by psychological or emotional states, such as boredom. He is also vulnerable to social pressures, like being late for a sale.

Extreme Willpower and Mental Fortitude

Saitama has incredible mental resilience, having trained his mind and body to the point of losing hair. He is unaffected by mind control or psychological attacks, as shown when he is unaffected by the monster Mosquito Girl's pheromones or the psychic powers of Tatsumaki.

Limit: He can still experience boredom, frustration, and annoyance.

Speed and Reflexes

He can move at speeds that appear instantaneous, often defeating opponents before they can react. He has dodged attacks from speedsters and has traveled from the moon to Earth in a short time.

Limit: No known limitations, but he sometimes lets attacks hit him due to indifference.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Saitama is a bald man of average height and build, with a round face and large, blank eyes. He typically wears a yellow jumpsuit with a white cape and red boots and gloves. His costume is reminiscent of classic superhero suits, but it is plain and unadorned. His bald head is a result of his intense training, not genetics. Symbols — His iconic symbol is his yellow jumpsuit and red cape, which have become synonymous with the character. The number '1' is not prominently featured, but the idea of 'one punch' is central. His bald head is a visual shorthand for his power and sacrifice. In fan art, he is often depicted with a simple smile or a confused expression.

Domains — He lives in Z-City, in a modest apartment that is often destroyed in battles. He frequents a local supermarket for sales and a ramen shop. He is a member of the Hero Association, initially ranked as C-Class, later rising to B-Class and eventually A-Class. He operates across the world, but primarily in City Z.

Accounts I
Origin of Saitama's PowerOne Punch Man (webcomic, manga, anime)

2009-present

Saitama was an unemployed young man who decided to become a hero after saving a child from a crab monster. He trained intensely for three years, doing 100 push-ups, 100 sit-ups, 100 squats, and a 10km run every day, even in extreme weather. The training caused him to lose his hair and gain immense power, but he also lost his emotions and sense of thrill. He then became a professional hero, but he is often overlooked and underestimated. Saitama gained the ability to defeat any opponent with one punch, but he became bored and depressed. He also developed a strong sense of justice and a desire to find a worthy opponent.

The Fight with Genos and the House of EvolutionOne Punch Man (manga and anime)

2012-present

Saitama encounters Genos, a cyborg seeking revenge on a mad cyborg. Genos challenges Saitama, but is defeated with one punch. Impressed, Genos becomes his disciple. Later, Saitama fights the House of Evolution, a group creating powerful monsters, and defeats their leader, Lord Boros, in a climactic battle. Genos becomes Saitama's disciple and roommate. Saitama's reputation grows slightly, but he is still largely unrecognized. The defeat of Lord Boros establishes Saitama's power on a cosmic scale.

Accounts II
The Hero Association and the Monster AssociationOne Punch Man (webcomic, manga, anime)

2012-present

Saitama joins the Hero Association to gain recognition. He is initially ranked C-Class, but after defeating numerous monsters, he rises through the ranks. The Monster Association, a group of powerful monsters, launches an attack, and Saitama is involved in the ensuing battle. He also encounters Garou, a former disciple of the martial artist Bang, who is on a quest to become the 'monster of monsters.' Saitama defeats Garou, but spares him, leading to Garou's redemption. The Hero Association suffers heavy losses, and Saitama's true power is witnessed by some, but he remains underappreciated.

The Fight with TatsumakiOne Punch Man (manga and anime)

2012-present

Tatsumaki, the S-Class hero known as the Tornado of Terror, challenges Saitama after he interferes with her mission. She uses her psychic powers to attack him, but he is completely unaffected. He eventually defeats her by grabbing her head, causing her to pass out. This leads to a comedic aftermath where Tatsumaki denies the loss. Tatsumaki develops a grudge against Saitama, but also a grudging respect. The event is not widely known, and Saitama's reputation remains low.

Saitama is a parody of Superman or Goku. — While he shares some tropes, he is an original character. The series parodies the entire shonen genre, not a specific character. The creator, ONE, has stated he was inspired by his dissatisfaction with typical hero stories.

Saitama's power is limitless and he can beat anyone, including characters from other universes. — Within the One Punch Man universe, he is unbeatable, but cross-universe comparisons are subjective. The creator has not commented on his power outside the series.

Saitama is emotionless and lacks a personality. — He is often deadpan, but he shows a range of emotions, including anger, frustration, joy (when eating), and even fear (when dealing with social situations). His boredom is a central character trait, not a lack of personality.

Saitama's training is a legitimate way to gain superpowers. — The training is absurdly insufficient and is a parody of shonen training arcs. It is not meant to be taken literally.

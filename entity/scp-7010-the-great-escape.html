Who It Is
Form

SCP-7010 is a system of thaumaturgical wards on Nx-03 that anomalously alter the environment of Site-03, allowing for a 'great escape' from the site's normal operations.

Powers

Environmental Alteration SCP-7010 has the ability to anomalously alter the environment of Site-03, allowing for a 'great escape' from the site's normal operations.

Incident Log

The discovery of SCP-7010 is attributed to a team of researchers at Site-03, who stumbled upon the system while investigating a series of strange occurrences.

SCP-7010 has been used by the SCP Foundation as a risk-aversion measure, allowing for a 'great escape' from the site's normal operations in the event of an emergency.

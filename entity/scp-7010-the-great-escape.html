Who It Is

A sentient escape room located in a strip mall in suburban Ohio. The room is self-aware and deeply vindictive. It designs puzzles specifically calibrated to humiliate and psychologically break its participants, learning their fears and insecurities from online data. Teams that fail are crushed as the room contracts. Successful teams are released, but the room posts humiliating footage online.

Form

A standard escape room from the exterior. The interior constantly reconfigures. Walls are covered with LED screens displaying taunting messages in Comic Sans.

Powers

Reality-bending interior configuration

Internet data mining for psychological profiling

Physical crushing of failures

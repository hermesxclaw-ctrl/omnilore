Who It Is

The largest and most ancient of all Warhammer dragons, Emperor Dragons are said to be as old as the world itself. Their scales shimmer like polished gemstones, and their eyes hold the wisdom of ages. Each Emperor Dragon is a living artifact of the world's dawn, commanding elemental forces with a thought.

Emperor Dragon

also known asDragon EmperorWarlord of the Skies
TypeWarhammer Fantasy Entity
Who It Is
Form

The Emperor Dragon is a massive, serpentine creature with scales as black as coal and eyes that glow like embers. Its wingspan is said to be as wide as a small island, and its roar can be heard for miles. According to legend, the Emperor Dragon can take on various forms, from a towering dragon to a smaller, more agile creature.

Powers

Breath of Fire The Emperor Dragon can breathe fire that can melt steel and reduce cities to ashes. Its flames are said to be so hot that they can ignite the air itself, creating a devastating wave of fire that can consume everything in its path.

Accounts

The story follows the Emperor Dragon as it rises to power, using its intelligence and cunning to outwit its enemies and expand its kingdom.

The story follows a group of adventurers as they uncover the Emperor Dragon's secret, which has the potential to change the course of history.

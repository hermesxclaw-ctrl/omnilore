Who It Is

The Licker is the first 'elite' enemy most players encounter in Resident Evil 2 — and the creature that teaches you that zombies are the least of your problems. A Licker is created when a zombie continues to mutate: its skin sloughs off entirely, revealing the raw, red muscle tissue beneath; its eyes are reabsorbed into the skull, rendering it blind; its cranium expands and the brain becomes partially exposed; and its fingers grow into razor-sharp claws capable of gripping walls and ceilings. A Licker navigates entirely by sound and smell, its extraordinarily long, spear-like tongue lashing out to impale prey with terrifying accuracy. A single Licker kill in the 2019 remake is brutal: it leaps onto the player, wraps its tongue around their throat, and tears the head off.

The sound design for the Licker is iconic — a wet, skittering, clicking noise accompanied by the slap of its tongue against surfaces as it 'tastes' the air for prey. The 1998 original's Lickers were among the first video game enemies to crawl on ceilings and walls, creating true three-dimensional tension: the player had to watch the ceiling, not just the hallway. The 2019 remake enhanced the Licker dramatically with photorealistic exposed sinew, dynamic lighting that makes the raw muscle glisten, and an AI that tracks sound: running or firing a weapon anywhere near a Licker (even on a different floor) will draw it straight to the player. Players learned to walk — not run — through Licker-infested corridors, holding their breath.

Lickers have become a visual signature of the Resident Evil franchise, appearing in nearly every game, both Resident Evil films, the animated movies, and crossovers. Their combination of blindness (creating a stealth dynamic), terrifying speed, and iconic visual design has influenced creature design across the horror genre. The Licker is simple — a skinless human with a long tongue and a brain you can see — but in its simplicity lies its power. It is the perfect escalation from slow, shambling zombies to something fast, smart, and ceiling-capable.

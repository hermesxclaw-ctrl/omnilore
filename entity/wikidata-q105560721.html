Who It Is

In the world of Dead or Alive, where supermodels and assassins clash in a high-stakes fighting tournament, an unassuming bookstore owner steps into the ring. He is Gen Fu, a master of xinyi liuhe quan, whose legendary 'iron fist' is said to possess awesome destructive power. But behind the formidable technique lies a grandfather's desperate quest: to save his sick granddaughter, Mei Lin. His story is one of quiet devotion and fierce determination, a reminder that even the most humble of warriors can harbor a will of iron.

Gen Fu is portrayed as a wise, humble, and gentle old man, devoted to his granddaughter and his martial arts. He is patient and kind, as seen in his relationship with Eliot. However, he is also fiercely determined and willing to fight when necessary, especially for his family.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Gen Fu' (ゲン・フー) is likely derived from Chinese martial arts figures or concepts, but no official etymology has been provided. 'Gen' could be a transliteration of a Chinese surname, and 'Fu' might relate to 'fu' (夫) meaning 'man' or 'master' in Chinese, but this is speculative.

Pronunciation — In Japanese, it is pronounced 'Gen Fū' with a long 'u'. In English, it is typically pronounced 'Jen Foo'.

Powers
Xinyi Liuhe Quan

Gen Fu is a master of xinyi liuhe quan, a Chinese martial art that emphasizes explosive power and coordination of the entire body. In the games, his fighting style is characterized by powerful, direct strikes and a strong focus on upper-body techniques.

Limit: The style is described as 'dangerous' and 'powerful', but specific limitations are not detailed in the games.

Iron Fist

A legendary attack known for its awesome destructive power. It is a signature technique of Gen Fu, likely a powerful punch that can knock out opponents with a single blow.

Limit: It is not clearly defined in the games; it may be a special move or a reputation.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Gen Fu is an elderly Chinese man with a long white beard and a shaved head. He typically wears traditional Chinese martial arts attire, often a dark-colored changshan or a simple tunic with trousers. His physique is lean but wiry, reflecting his martial arts expertise. In his official artwork, he is often depicted with a stern, focused expression.

Symbols — Gen Fu's primary symbol is his 'iron fist', representing his mastery of xinyi liuhe quan and his devastating striking power. He is also associated with his bookstore, which signifies his scholarly and humble nature.

Domains — Gen Fu lives in a bookstore, which serves as his home and place of business. He is also seen in various tournament venues across the Dead or Alive series.

Accounts I
The Granddaughter's IllnessDead or Alive 2, Dead or Alive 3

1999-2001

Gen Fu's granddaughter, Mei Lin, suffers from a serious illness. He enters the second DOA tournament to defeat Gohyakumine Bankotsubo, a tengu, because the nose of a tengu can cure any disease. After obtaining it, Mei Lin is temporarily cured, but she still needs an operation. To fund it, Gen Fu enters the third tournament. Gen Fu successfully obtains the tengu's nose and later earns money for the operation, saving Mei Lin's life.

Accounts II
Mentor to EliotDead or Alive 4, Dead or Alive 5

2005-2012

At some point, Gen Fu takes on Eliot as an apprentice, though the reason is a mystery. In DOA4, Eliot seeks to prove himself to Gen Fu, and Gen Fu serves as the final boss in Eliot's story. In DOA5, Gen Fu appears at the end of Eliot's chapter, ready to teach him more advanced techniques. Eliot grows as a martial artist under Gen Fu's guidance.

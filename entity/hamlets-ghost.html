Who It Is

Cultural Significance

The Ghost's ambiguous nature — is it truly Hamlet's father, a demonic deception, or Hamlet's own psychological projection? — has fueled centuries of interpretation. The command 'Remember me' has become a universal cry of the dead to the living.

Who It Is

In the world of Scarlet Nexus, where psychic powers are the currency of survival and the city of New Himuka is a fortress against the monstrous Others, a young man named Yuito Sumeragi joins the Other Suppression Force with a mysterious gift he never asked for. His journey is not just about fighting monsters—it's about uncovering the truth of his own past, the corruption of the government he serves, and the bonds that define what it means to be human.

Yuito is earnest, idealistic, and compassionate. He cares deeply for his comrades and is determined to protect others, often at personal cost. He is initially naive about the darker aspects of the government but grows into a leader who questions authority. He is polite, sometimes to a fault, and struggles with self-doubt despite his privileged background.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Yuito (結人) can be interpreted as 'binding person' or 'connecting people,' reflecting his role in forging bonds. Sumeragi (皇) is a historical Japanese surname associated with the imperial line, suggesting nobility and legacy.

Pronunciation — YOO-ee-toh SOO-meh-rah-gi

Aliases — Yuito Sumeragi

Ownership — Japanese video game franchise Scarlet Nexus, created by Bandai Namco Studios.

Powers
Psychokinesis

Yuito possesses the ability to telekinetically manipulate objects and create kinetic blasts. He can throw debris, launch projectiles, and use his power to enhance his sword attacks.

Limit: Requires concentration and can be exhausting. His power is less refined than some veterans, but he trains to improve. He cannot use other psychic abilities without SAS (Struggle Arm System) connections.

SAS (Struggle Arm System) Compatibility

Yuito can link with other psychics to temporarily borrow their abilities, such as pyrokinesis, invisibility, or teleportation, enabling versatile combat strategies.

Limit: Requires strong bonds with teammates; the link can be unstable if trust is weak. Overuse can cause strain.

Brain Field

A rare and powerful state where Yuito's psychic power expands dramatically, allowing him to perceive and interact with the world on a higher plane, often turning the tide of battle.

Limit: Uncontrollable at first; can be triggered by extreme emotion. Overuse may risk mental collapse.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Yuito is a young man in his late teens with short, dark blue hair and amber eyes. He typically wears the Other Suppression Force (OSF) uniform: a dark blue and white combat suit with red accents, a red scarf, and a red visor. His attire reflects both his military role and his family's elite status. He carries a sword as his primary weapon.

Symbols — The red scarf is a symbol of his resolve and connection to his allies. The OSF insignia represents his duty. His psychokinesis is visually represented by blue energy waves and telekinetic manipulation of objects.

Domains — He resides in the city of New Himuka, a futuristic metropolis protected by the OSF. He operates in various locations, including the OSF headquarters, the Seiran district, and the outskirts where Others appear.

Accounts I
The Other Suppression Force InitiationScarlet Nexus (game)

Future, year 2020s (fictional)

Yuito joins the OSF, following in the footsteps of his family tradition. He is assigned to a squad and quickly becomes embroiled in a conflict with Others, grotesque creatures from a parallel dimension. He discovers he has latent psychokinetic powers, which sets him apart from many recruits. Yuito's arrival marks the beginning of a larger conspiracy involving the government and the true nature of the Others.

Accounts II
The Red String ConspiracyScarlet Nexus (game)

Future

Yuito uncovers a government conspiracy involving the suppression of information about the Others and the existence of a 'Red String' that connects all psychics. He learns that his own family may be implicated, and that his powers are linked to a larger cosmic plan. Yuito must decide between loyalty to the OSF and the truth, leading to a confrontation that changes the fate of humanity.

The Final Battle and ResolutionScarlet Nexus (game and anime)

Future

Yuito and Kasane team up to defeat Karen Travers, who seeks to reset time to save a loved one. They succeed, but at great cost, and the world is left to rebuild. Yuito emerges as a hero and a symbol of hope. The Others are neutralized, and the truth is revealed to the public. Yuito continues his duty as an OSF captain.

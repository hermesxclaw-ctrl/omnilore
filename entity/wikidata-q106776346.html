The Approach

In the world of Scarlet Nexus, where psychic powers are the currency of survival and the city of New Himuka is a fortress against the monstrous Others, a young man named Yuito Sumeragi joins the Other Suppression Force with a mysterious gift he never asked for. His journey is not just about fighting monsters—it's about uncovering the truth of his own past, the corruption of the government he serves, and the bonds that define what it means to be human.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of violence, psychological trauma, and themes of conspiracy and body horror. Some story elements involve memory manipulation and existential dread.
Identity

Yuito Sumeragi

Pronunciation YOO-ee-toh SOO-meh-rah-gi

Etymology

Yuito (結人) can be interpreted as 'binding person' or 'connecting people,' reflecting his role in forging bonds. Sumeragi (皇) is a historical Japanese surname associated with the imperial line, suggesting nobility and legacy.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese video game franchise Scarlet Nexus, created by Bandai Namco Studios.

Disputed Identifications

None known. Yuito is a distinct character within his own franchise.

Description

Appearance

Yuito is a young man in his late teens with short, dark blue hair and amber eyes. He typically wears the Other Suppression Force (OSF) uniform: a dark blue and white combat suit with red accents, a red scarf, and a red visor. His attire reflects both his military role and his family's elite status. He carries a sword as his primary weapon.

Symbols & Iconography

The red scarf is a symbol of his resolve and connection to his allies. The OSF insignia represents his duty. His psychokinesis is visually represented by blue energy waves and telekinetic manipulation of objects.

Behaviour & Personality

Yuito is earnest, idealistic, and compassionate. He cares deeply for his comrades and is determined to protect others, often at personal cost. He is initially naive about the darker aspects of the government but grows into a leader who questions authority. He is polite, sometimes to a fault, and struggles with self-doubt despite his privileged background.

Domains & Habitats

He resides in the city of New Himuka, a futuristic metropolis protected by the OSF. He operates in various locations, including the OSF headquarters, the Seiran district, and the outskirts where Others appear.

Version Differences

In the anime adaptation, his story is condensed and slightly altered to fit the episodic format, but his core character and motivations remain consistent. The game features two playable protagonists; Yuito's route focuses on his personal and family drama, while Kasane's route offers a different perspective on the same events.

Powers & Abilities

Psychokinesis

Source Version Scarlet Nexus (game and anime)

Description

Yuito possesses the ability to telekinetically manipulate objects and create kinetic blasts. He can throw debris, launch projectiles, and use his power to enhance his sword attacks.

Limitations

Requires concentration and can be exhausting. His power is less refined than some veterans, but he trains to improve. He cannot use other psychic abilities without SAS (Struggle Arm System) connections.

Contradictions

None within the canon.

SAS (Struggle Arm System) Compatibility

Source Version Scarlet Nexus (game)

Description

Yuito can link with other psychics to temporarily borrow their abilities, such as pyrokinesis, invisibility, or teleportation, enabling versatile combat strategies.

Limitations

Requires strong bonds with teammates; the link can be unstable if trust is weak. Overuse can cause strain.

Contradictions

None.

Brain Field

Source Version Scarlet Nexus (game and anime)

Description

A rare and powerful state where Yuito's psychic power expands dramatically, allowing him to perceive and interact with the world on a higher plane, often turning the tide of battle.

Limitations

Uncontrollable at first; can be triggered by extreme emotion. Overuse may risk mental collapse.

Who It Is
Form

The Orphan of Kos is a fetal-like entity with a humanoid body, often depicted as a twisted, nightmarish child. Its appearance is characterized by a grotesque, elongated head, bulging eyes, and a series of twisted limbs.

Powers

Blood Curse The Orphan of Kos is said to possess the ability to curse its enemies with a 'Blood Curse,' a powerful and debilitating affliction that slowly drains the life force from its victims.

Accounts

According to the lore, the Hunters came upon the corpse of Kos, a revered figure in the village, and committed an act of blasphemy against the poor wizened child. This event is said to have triggered a chain reaction of events that led to the downfall of the village.

In retaliation for the fate of the village, the devoted mother Kos laid the curse of blood upon the old hunters responsible for their earthly deaths, and upon those who followed in their blood-lusting footsteps.

Who It Is

In the shimmering world of Sailor Moon, where love and duty collide, Sailor Neptune stands as a figure of enigmatic grace—a violinist whose melodies can soothe or destroy, a guardian who carries the weight of the world's secrets in a mirror that reflects only truth. Her story is one of sacrifice, artistry, and a love that transcends time itself.

Polite, calm, elegant, and sophisticated. She is intelligent and deeply artistic. She can be sharp and cold when necessary, especially when her duty demands it. She is fiercely dedicated to her role as a Guardian, even if it means making unpopular decisions. Her bond with Haruka is her most defining relationship.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name Neptune derives from the Roman god of the sea, reflecting her ocean-based powers. Her civilian name, Michiru Kaiou, uses kanji that can mean 'ocean king' (海王), reinforcing her aquatic theme.

Pronunciation — Japanese: Sērā Nepuchūn; civilian name: Kaiō Michiru (kah-ee-oh mee-chee-roo)

Aliases — Michiru Kaiou, Michelle Kaioh, Sailor Neptune, Princess Neptune, Super Sailor Neptune, Eternal Sailor Neptune

Form & Signs

Appearance — Michiru is a tall, elegant teenage girl with short teal hair that waves past her shoulders. She has blue eyes. As Sailor Neptune, she wears a sailor uniform in teal and cerulean, with gloves that extend only to her mid-forearms. She has a choker with an 8-pointed star jewel and planet-shaped earrings (in Crystal and later adaptations). Her Princess Neptune form wears a sea-green gown.

Symbols — Associated with the sea, mirrors, and music. Her talisman is the Deep Aqua Mirror. She is often depicted with a violin, and her attacks are named in French or English with kanji meanings.

Domains — As Sailor Neptune, she is associated with the planet Neptune and the deep seas. In civilian life, she attends Mugen Academy and later a high school. She is often seen near water, using it to relax.

Accounts I
The Infinity ArcSailor Moon manga and Sailor Moon S anime

1994-1995

Michiru and Haruka are introduced as mysterious transfer students who are actually Sailor Guardians seeking the Talismans. They initially confront the inner Senshi, but eventually ally with them to fight the Death Busters. Michiru's precognition helps predict the next targets. The Talismans are revealed, and the Outer Senshi join the fight against the Death Busters. Michiru and Haruka's relationship is further developed.

Accounts II
The Dream ArcSailor Moon manga and Sailor Moon SuperS anime

1995-1996

Michiru and Haruka are absent for most of the arc, but they appear in a special episode where they stay at a hotel. They also appear in the movie 'Sailor Moon SuperS: The Movie' where they help fight the Dead Moon Circus. They help save the day, and their relationship is further shown.

Who It Is

Anthropos (ἄνθρωπος, 'Human') is the aeon that is the 'image and likeness' of the Father within the Pleroma — the divine pattern upon which material humanity is subsequently modeled (in the flawed copy mediated by the Demiurge). In Valentinian anthropology, the Pleromatic Anthropos is the true, spiritual 'self' of which each material human soul is a fractured reflection. This aeon is paired with Ecclesia, indicating that the archetypal human is never solitary — 'male and female he created them' is a Pleromatic truth, not merely an earthly one. The union of Anthropos and Ecclesia generates the final six aeons of the primary Ogdoad (8) and the Decad (10), completing the Pleroma's full scheme of 30 aeons.

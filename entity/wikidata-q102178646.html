Who It Is

In the labyrinthine city of Orario, where gods descend to walk among mortals and monsters lurk beneath the streets, a boy with white hair and a dream of becoming a hero chases the impossible. Bell Cranel is not merely the protagonist of a fantasy adventure; he is the embodiment of determination, the underdog who defies the odds with every step, and the heart of a world where divinity and humanity intertwine.

Bell is initially shy, timid, and lacking in self-confidence, but he possesses an unyielding determination to become a hero. He is kind-hearted, empathetic, and respectful toward others, especially women, which sometimes leads to comedic situations. He is deeply loyal to his friends and familia, and he has a strong sense of justice. He is also somewhat naive and easily flustered, but he grows more confident and capable as the story progresses.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname 'Cranel' may be derived from 'crane' (a bird), possibly symbolizing his speed and grace. The given name 'Bell' could reference the bellflower or symbolize his clear, ringing determination.

Powers
Realis Phrase (Growth Acceleration)

Bell's unique skill that allows him to grow at an unprecedented rate, gaining experience and leveling up much faster than other adventurers. It is a latent ability that activates due to his intense determination and desire to become stronger.

Limit: The skill is passive and cannot be controlled. It accelerates growth but does not grant instant power; Bell must still train and fight to gain experience.

Liaris Freese (Loving Heart)

A skill that is later revealed to be intertwined with Realis Phrase. It amplifies Bell's growth when he is motivated by his feelings, particularly his love for Ais Wallenstein. This skill is unique to Bell and is considered a miracle by the gods.

Limit: The skill's activation is tied to Bell's emotional state, and it may not function if he loses his motivation or resolve.

Firebolt (Magic)

Bell's primary offensive magic, a short-range lightning bolt that he can cast quickly. It is a chant-less spell that he develops early in the series.

Limit: It has limited range and power compared to other magic, but Bell compensates with speed and agility.

Argonaut (Charge Attack)

A skill that allows Bell to charge up a powerful attack over time, with the power increasing the longer he charges. It is based on the legend of Argonaut, a hero from the world's mythology.

Limit: The charging process leaves Bell vulnerable, and he cannot move or defend while charging. The skill also has a time limit for the charge.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Bell Cranel is a young man with distinctive white hair and red eyes, a trait that sets him apart from most people in Orario. He is short for his age, standing around 155 cm, and has a slim build. He typically wears a light leather armor over a dark shirt, with a red scarf given to him by his goddess, Hestia. His most iconic accessory is the Hestia Knife, a small, unassuming blade that grows in power with his own growth.

Symbols — The Hestia Knife, which symbolizes his bond with Hestia and his growth as an adventurer. His white hair and red eyes are often associated with his unique status and potential. The scarf is a symbol of Hestia's affection and protection.

Domains — Bell resides in the city of Orario, specifically in the Hestia Familia's home, a small church in the city's poorer district. He frequents the Dungeon, a massive labyrinth beneath Orario, where he adventures and levels up.

Accounts I
The Beginning of the Hero's JourneyLight novel Volume 1

Modern fictional era

Bell Cranel, a 14-year-old boy, arrives in the city of Orario with the dream of becoming an adventurer and finding a romantic partner. He meets the goddess Hestia, who takes him in and forms the Hestia Familia. Bell begins to explore the Dungeon, and his first adventure leads him to encounter the minotaur, which nearly kills him. He is saved by Ais Wallenstein, the Sword Princess, and becomes inspired to grow stronger. Bell gains his first level and develops his skills, setting the stage for his rapid growth and the series' overarching plot.

Accounts II
The War Game with Apollo FamiliaLight novel Volume 5

Modern fictional era

Bell is challenged to a War Game by Apollo, a god who desires Bell's friendship and tries to force him to join his familia. The Hestia Familia must fight against Apollo Familia in a competition to determine Bell's fate. Bell uses his new skill, Argonaut, to defeat the captain of Apollo Familia, Hyacinthos, securing victory. Bell gains fame and respect, and the Hestia Familia's reputation grows. The victory also solidifies Bell's resolve to become a hero.

The Dungeon's Deep Layers and the XenosLight novel Volumes 10-14

Modern fictional era

Bell descends into the deeper layers of the Dungeon and encounters the Xenos, intelligent monsters who can speak and feel emotions. He befriends them, particularly a young Xenos named Wiene, and tries to protect them from adventurers who see them as mere monsters. This leads to conflict with the Loki Familia and other groups, and Bell must navigate the political and moral complexities of the Dungeon. Bell's actions challenge the established order of Orario and set up future conflicts with the Dungeon's mysteries.

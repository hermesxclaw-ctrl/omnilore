Accounts

A young Australian musician, Jamie Stinson, adopts the name 'Astennu' for his professional career. He moves to Norway and becomes a guitarist for notable black metal bands, contributing to albums that are now considered classics of the genre. His career in the spotlight is relatively brief, but his name remains associated with that era of extreme metal.

Australian musician Jamie Stinson adopted Astennu as a stage name in the late 1990s, performing with bands such as Lord Kaos. The name evokes an ancient Egyptian mystery, aligning with the black metal aesthetic of reclaiming forgotten occult symbolism. While the mythological Astennu remains unattested, the stage name has survived in metal archives, fan forums, and album credits — giving the name a second life as a modern sonic identity.

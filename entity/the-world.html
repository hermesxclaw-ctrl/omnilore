Who It Is

DIO's humanoid Stand, visually similar to Star Platinum. The World specializes in time manipulation, capable of stopping time for up to 9 seconds (longer than Star Platinum). It is a powerful close-range Stand with immense strength and speed. Its name is derived from the 22 Major Arcana tarot card 'The World.'

Powers

Time stop (up to 9 seconds), super strength, high-speed punches

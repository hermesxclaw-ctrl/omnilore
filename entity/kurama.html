Who It Is

The Nine-Tailed Fox, most powerful of the tailed beasts. A massive fox made of pure chakra with nine tails, embodying hatred and raw destructive power. Sealed within Naruto Uzumaki at birth, Kurama transforms from a being of pure malice to a grudging ally, sharing its vast chakra reserves and granting Naruto access to increasingly powerful chakra cloaks and the full Nine-Tails transformation.

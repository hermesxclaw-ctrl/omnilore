Who It Is
Form

Pryderi is often depicted as a young prince, sometimes with a crown or a sword, reflecting his status as a king and a warrior.

Powers

Divine Son Figure Pryderi is often equated with the divine son figure of Mabon ap Modron, reflecting his connection to the natural world and his role as a symbol of fertility and rebirth.

Accounts

Pryderi is the only character to appear in all Four Branches of the Mabinogi, which tells the story of his life and adventures as a prince and a king.

Who It Is

Pryderi is the only character appearing in all four branches. Stolen at birth and found in a stable, he was returned and named Pryderi ('anxiety'). He survives the war with Ireland, falls victim to enchantment (turned into a mouse), and finally dies in single combat against the magician Gwydion.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Pryderi is often depicted as a young prince, sometimes with a crown or a sword, reflecting his status as a king and a warrior.

Symbols — Pryderi is associated with the symbol of the stag, which represents his connection to the natural world and his role as a hunter.

Domains — Pryderi's stories often take place in the kingdom of Dyfed, which is located in southwest Wales.

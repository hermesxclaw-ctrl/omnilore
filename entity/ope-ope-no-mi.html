Who It Is
Form

The Ope Ope no Mi is a rare and mysterious Devil Fruit that grants its consumer the power of eternal youth. Its appearance is unknown, but its effects are well-documented.

Powers

Eternal Youth The Ope Ope no Mi grants its consumer the power of eternal youth, allowing them to live forever and to remain youthful and healthy for all eternity.

Accounts

The Ope Ope no Mi is said to have been consumed by a legendary figure known only as 'The Reviver', who used its power to heal the wounded and to bring people back from the brink of death.

Donquixote Doflamingo, a powerful pirate and former member of the Seven Warlords of the Sea, is said to have possessed the Ope Ope no Mi during his time on Fishman Island.

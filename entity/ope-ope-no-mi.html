Who It Is

The Op-Op Fruit, a supreme-grade Paramecia that grants the user the ability to create a spherical 'operating room' domain where the user has absolute control over the spatial arrangement and physical condition of everything inside. Within the room, the user can teleport, separate, swap, and manipulate body parts at will without causing actual harm — or death — to the target. Its ultimate technique, the 'Perennial Youth Operation', grants immortality at the cost of the user's life.

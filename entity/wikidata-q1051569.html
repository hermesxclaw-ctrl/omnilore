Who It Is

Chrome Dokuro is a girl who literally holds her life together with illusions. Born Nagi, she lost most of her organs in a traffic accident, and when her own family wished she would die, a voice from the mafia underworld offered her a second chance—on the condition that she share her body with him. She became the Mist Guardian of the Vongola Family, a shy warrior whose fragile existence is bound to the very man who saved her.

Chrome is kind, loyal, and selfless, but extremely shy and timid. She runs from kindness because she believes she is unworthy. She lacks self-confidence and often doubts her own abilities. Over time, she becomes more open and determined to protect her friends, eventually finding the strength to stand on her own.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Chrome' likely references the metallic element, possibly symbolizing her artificial, reconstructed nature. 'Dokuro' (髑髏) means 'skull' in Japanese, linking her to Mukuro (骸, 'carcass') and the Kokuyo gang's skull motif.

Pronunciation — Kroh-muh Doh-koo-roh

Aliases — Nagi (real name), Mist Guardian of the Vongola Family, Mukuro's host

Ownership — Created by Akira Amano for the manga 'Katekyō Hitman Reborn!' published in Weekly Shōnen Jump (2004-2012). The character is part of Japanese manga/anime culture.

Powers
Illusion Creation

Chrome can create realistic illusions, a skill she learned from Mukuro. She uses them for combat, deception, and to create her own organs.

Limit: Her illusions are not as powerful as Mukuro's, but she is skilled enough that only experts can see through them. She cannot use this power without Mukuro's support initially.

Animal Summoning

She can summon animals, likely through illusionary means, as part of Mukuro's Six Paths of Reincarnation.

Limit: Limited to when she shares Mukuro's power.

Mist Vongola Box: Devil Lens

The Mist Vongola Box weapon contains Daemon Spade's Devil Lens, a device that allows the user to see through any illusion.

Limit: Only usable in the future arc; after Mukuro is freed, he takes the box with him.

Self-Created Organs

After Mukuro leaves, Chrome becomes strong enough to create her own illusionary organs, sustaining herself without his help.

Limit: Requires her own mastery of illusions.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Chrome is a teenage girl with short, dark blue hair and a distinctive eye patch over her right eye (which she lost in the accident). She wears the Vongola uniform: a black suit with a white shirt and tie, often with a long coat. In the future, she wears a more form-fitting suit. She carries a trident, initially Mukuro's, later a single-pronged one of her own.

Symbols — The trident is her primary symbol, associated with Mukuro and the Kokuyo gang. The Mist Vongola Box contains Daemon Spade's Devil Lens, a monocle-like device that reveals illusions. Her eye patch is a visual reminder of her injury and her dependence on illusions.

Domains — She lives in Namimori, Japan, and is associated with Kokuyo Land (the gang's hideout). In the future, she operates with the Vongola in their base.

Accounts I
Origin: Nagi's Accident and Mukuro's OfferManga/anime

Modern era

Nagi, a lonely girl neglected by her parents, saves a cat from a traffic accident and loses most of her organs and her right eye. In the hospital, she overhears her parents wishing she would die. Despairing, she hears Mukuro's voice, who offers to save her life by giving her illusionary organs. She accepts and becomes Chrome, joining Mukuro's gang. Chrome gains a new life and a purpose, but becomes dependent on Mukuro.

Varia Arc: Mist Guardian BattleManga/anime

Modern era

Chrome appears as Tsuna's Mist Guardian and fights Mammon. She initially matches him, but is defeated when he unleashes his pacifier's power. Mukuro takes over and wins the battle. Later, she is taken hostage by Belphegor and Mammon, but is rescued by Ryohei. The Vongola win the Mist ring, and Chrome proves her worth as a Guardian.

Accounts II
Future Arc: Encounter with Glo Xinia and Mukuro's InjuryManga/anime

Alternate future (10 years later)

Chrome is transported to the future and attacked by Glo Xinia. Future Mukuro, possessing Glo's owl, helps her defeat him. Later, Mukuro fights Byakuran and is severely wounded, causing Chrome's organs to fail. Hibari instructs her to use her Vongola Ring to recreate them. She receives the Mist Vongola Box and Devil Lens. Chrome becomes stronger and gains the ability to see through illusions.

Inheritance Ceremony Arc: Daemon Spade's ManipulationManga/anime

Modern era

Chrome is stalked and kidnapped by Julie Kato, who reveals himself as Daemon Spade, the first Mist Guardian. He uses her to lure Mukuro and takes control of her mind. During the final battle, she helps Enma by creating a barrier with Mukuro's support to protect them from Tsuna's XX Burner. Daemon is defeated, and Chrome returns to Namimori. Chrome is freed from Daemon's control and proves her resolve.

Representative Battle of the Rainbow: IndependenceManga/anime

Modern era

Mukuro kicks Chrome out of the gang, and she transfers to Tsuna's class. She falls ill because she is rejecting Mukuro's illusionary organs, as she wants to be seen as an individual. After finding her resolve, she joins Reborn's team and helps defeat the Vindices by combining her illusions with Mukuro's. On the final day, she fights alongside Yamamoto, Gokudera, and Fran against Big Pino and Small Gia. Chrome becomes truly independent, creating her own organs and standing on her own.

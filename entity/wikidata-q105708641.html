Who It Is

From the shadowed corridors of the human mind to the cockpits of supersonic jets, the name 'Phantom' haunts a thousand different forms. It is a ghost, a masked hero, a demonic spider, a fighter plane, a phantom limb, and a cancelled video game console. This is the story of a word that refuses to stay in one place, a spectral presence that has left its mark on folklore, fiction, technology, and medicine alike.

Behavior and personality are equally diverse. Ghost phantoms are often depicted as malevolent or sorrowful, haunting specific locations. Powers
Invisibility and Intangibility

Ghost phantoms are often able to become invisible, pass through solid objects, and are immune to physical harm. This is a common trait in folklore and fiction.

Limit: May be bound to a specific location or object, can be affected by spiritual means, and may have limited ability to interact with the physical world.

Flight

Many phantoms, such as the Phantoms in Minecraft and the Phantom in Devil May Cry, can fly.

Limit: In Minecraft, Phantoms burn in sunlight. In Devil May Cry, the Phantom can be defeated by attacking its weak points.

Superhuman Strength and Combat Skills

The Phantom hero is an Olympic-level athlete and master of hand-to-hand combat, with enhanced strength and agility.

Limit: He is still mortal and can be injured or killed.

Magic and Supernatural Powers

The Phantom of the Opera has no supernatural powers, but some phantoms in fiction possess magical abilities, such as casting spells or manipulating minds.

Limit: Often limited by plot, magical rules, or personal weaknesses.

Technological Abilities

The F-4 Phantom II is a supersonic fighter aircraft with advanced avionics, radar, and weaponry. It can fly at Mach 2 and carry a variety of missiles and bombs.

Limit: It requires a pilot and maintenance, and its capabilities are bound by physical laws.

Accounts I
The Phantom of the OperaGaston Leroux's novel (1910), later adapted into numerous films and musicals

Belle Époque (late 19th century)

A disfigured musical genius named Erik haunts the Paris Opera House, terrorizing its managers and patrons. He falls in love with a young soprano, Christine Daaé, and becomes her 'Angel of Music', guiding her career. He kidnaps her and demands that she love him, leading to a confrontation with her childhood sweetheart, Raoul. The story ends tragically, with Erik releasing Christine and dying of a broken heart. The novel became a classic of Gothic literature and spawned countless adaptations, including Andrew Lloyd Webber's iconic musical, which is one of the longest-running shows in Broadway history.

The Phantom (comic strip)Lee Falk's comic strip (1936–present)

20th century onwards

The Phantom is a masked crime-fighter who operates in the fictional African country of Bengalla. He is known as 'The Ghost Who Walks' because he appears to be immortal, but he is actually a legacy hero: the mantle is passed from father to son. The current Phantom is Kit Walker, the 21st in the line. He fights pirates, criminals, and evil organizations, using his skills, intelligence, and two pistols. The Phantom became one of the most popular comic strips of the 20th century, spawning film serials, television shows, animated series, and a 1996 film.

Accounts II
Phantoms (novel)Dean Koontz (1983)

Late 20th century

In the small town of Snowfield, Colorado, a mysterious force kills most of the inhabitants. A group of survivors, including a doctor and her sister, discover that the entity is an ancient, intelligent being that feeds on fear and can mimic voices. They eventually learn it is a prehistoric life form and manage to destroy it using a combination of science and luck. The novel was a bestseller and was adapted into a 1998 film starring Peter O'Toole and Rose McGowan.

The Phantom (2015 film)Kabir Khan's film (2015)

Contemporary

An Indian intelligence officer, Daniyal Khan, is tasked with hunting down the perpetrators of the 2008 Mumbai attacks. He goes on a covert mission across the globe, using unconventional methods to bring the terrorists to justice. The film is a political thriller that deals with themes of revenge and national security. The film was controversial for its depiction of extrajudicial killings, but was a commercial success.

Phantom (2013 film)Todd Robinson's film (2013)

Cold War era (1980s)

A Soviet submarine captain, Demi, is forced to take command of a nuclear submarine with a faulty reactor. He discovers that a rogue political officer is planning to start a nuclear war. Demi must prevent his crew from mutinying and stop the rogue officer, ultimately sacrificing himself to save the world. The film was a modest box office success and received mixed reviews.

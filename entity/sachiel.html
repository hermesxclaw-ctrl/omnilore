Who It Is
Form

Sachiel is often depicted as a majestic, winged being with a radiant aura, symbolizing his association with wealth and prosperity.

Powers

Abundance and Prosperity Sachiel's power is said to bring wealth, success, and growth to individuals and communities, guiding them towards their highest potential.

Accounts

In this grimoire, Sachiel is introduced as an archangel of the order of cherubim, associated with wealth and prosperity.

In the anime series Neon Genesis Evangelion, Sachiel is depicted as a powerful and majestic angel, associated with the fish and the energies of Jupiter.

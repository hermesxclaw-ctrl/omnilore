Who It Is

Archangel of the order of cherubim, presiding over wealth, abundance, success, and prosperity. The name 'Sachiel' first appears in the late 16th-century grimoire The Heptameron, attributed to Pietro d'Abano. In ceremonial magic, Sachiel is associated with the planet Jupiter and Thursday. He is invoked for financial blessings, career advancement, and material prosperity. In Kabbalistic angelology, Sachiel is linked to the Sephirah Chesed (Mercy) on the Tree of Life. His name is sometimes rendered as Zadkiel, though some traditions distinguish the two.

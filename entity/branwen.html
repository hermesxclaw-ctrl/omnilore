Name & Tongue

Etymology — Branwen is a Welsh name composed of two elements: 'Bran' meaning 'raven' and 'wen' meaning 'fair' or 'white'.

Pronunciation — BRAN-oo-en

Aliases — Branwen, White Raven, Tragic Princess

Ownership — Branwen is a cultural artifact of the Welsh people, specifically associated with the medieval Welsh story known as the Second Branch of the Mabinogi.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Branwen is often depicted as a beautiful and tragic figure, associated with the color white and the symbol of the raven.

Symbols — The raven is a symbol of Branwen, representing her association with the goddess Morrigan and her role as a symbol of fate and destiny.

Domains — Branwen's story takes place in the medieval Welsh world, specifically in the kingdoms of Britain and Ireland.

Accounts I
The Second Branch of the MabinogiMedieval Welsh story

Medieval

The story of Branwen is a major part of the Second Branch of the Mabinogi, a medieval Welsh story that tells the tale of the god Bran the Blessed and his sister Branwen. The story of Branwen has significant consequences for the kingdom of Britain and Ireland, leading to the downfall of the gods and the rise of human power.

Who It Is
Form

Branwen is often depicted as a beautiful and tragic figure, associated with the color white and the symbol of the raven.

Powers

Fate and Destiny Branwen has the power to shape fate and destiny, as represented by the symbol of the raven.

Accounts

The story of Branwen is a major part of the Second Branch of the Mabinogi, a medieval Welsh story that tells the tale of the god Bran the Blessed and his sister Branwen.

The feature film Branwen, Daughter of Llŷr is a modern retelling of the story of Branwen, set in the modern world.

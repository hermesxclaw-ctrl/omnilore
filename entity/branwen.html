Who It Is

Branwen was given in marriage to Matholwch, King of Ireland. After her half-brother's insult, she was humiliated and made to work in kitchens for three years. She trained a starling to carry a message to her giant brother Bran, who invaded Ireland. The resulting war devastated both nations, and Branwen died of a broken heart on the shore of Anglesey.

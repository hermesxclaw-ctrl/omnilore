Who It Is

She didn't end up in Green Dolphin Street Prison by accident — Ermes Costello walked in on purpose, paid for a crime she meant to commit, and tattooed her revenge to her own skin with a Stand that turns stickers into brutal, duplicating violence.

Attested personality: hot-tempered, blunt, fiercely loyal, and single-minded about revenge. She voluntarily committed armed robbery to be incarcerated in Green Dolphin Street to hunt Sports Maxx, the gangster who murdered her older sister Gloria. Initially abrasive toward Jolyne Cujoh — they clash over prison hierarchy and Jolyne's naivete — she rapidly becomes Jolyne's most steadfast ally and confidante, described by Araki in volume intros as Jolyne's 'sister-in-arms.' Name & Tongue

Etymology — Named in Hirohiko Araki's music/fashion naming convention. 'Ermes' from French luxury house Hermès; 'Costello' from English singer-songwriter Elvis Costello (Declan Patrick MacManus). Araki confirmed the pattern in JOJOVELLER and JOJO A-GO!GO! artbooks where Part 6 cast are linked to fashion brands

Pronunciation — Japanese: e-ru-me-su ko-su-te-ro [eɾɯmesɯ kosɯteɾo]; English localization: /ˈɜːrmɛs kɒˈstɛloʊ/ (UR-mess kos-TELL-oh). Viz Media and Netflix English dub use 'Ermes' with stress on first syllable.

Aliases — エルメェス・コステロ (Erumesu Kosutero), Hermes Costello (alternate Hepburn transliteration, used in early English fan translations and some merchandise), Ermes, Ermès Costello (French-style rendering)

Powers
Kiss (キッス)

Close-range humanoid Stand with sticker-based duplication. By placing a lip-printed sticker on any object or person (including herself), Ermes creates an exact duplicate of that target. The sticker remains on both original and copy. When the sticker is removed or destroyed, the original and duplicate violently merge back together, causing massive compressive/crushing damage at the seam. She uses this offensively (duplicate fists/legs to double attacks, duplicate bullets to create flak, create duplicate body parts as shields) and defensively (create duplicate limbs to escape restraints). The Stand itself appears as a humanoid with a large lip motif and adhesive tape-like body when manifested, but often acts through the stickers without full manifestation.

Limit: Requires physical contact to place sticker; sticker can be removed by opponent if they reach it, negating effect or causing premature merge. Duplicate is not independent — it is tethered and mirrors original's position initially; duplication doubles mass but not durability, and merging damage affects both if Ermes's own body is duplicated (she risks self-harm). Range is short (2-3 meters for Stand manifestation). Cannot duplicate Stands themselves, only physical matter. Sticker supply appears limited by Stand energy; overuse causes fatigue. In Marilyn Manson arc, she cannot duplicate intangible debt.

Enhanced Physical Strength and Brawling

Even without Kiss, Ermes displays exceptional raw strength, endurance, and street-fighting skill. She overpowers male inmates, breaks restraints, and survives injuries that would incapacitate others. She uses boxing, grappling, and improvised weapons (pipes, prison tools). This is attested as natural ability, not Stand-derived.

Limit: Human limits — she is vulnerable to gunfire, Stand abilities, and accelerated aging/time effects. Severely wounded by Limp Bizkit's invisible corpse reanimation and later fatally injured by Pucci's Made in Heaven acceleration (manga Chapter 152).

Tactical Improvisation with Duplication

Specific feats: duplicating her own arm to create a spare limb to block Sports Maxx's attack then merging to crush his head; duplicating bullets mid-air to counter Viviano Westwood; duplicating a pipe to create a double-blunt weapon; creating duplicate money/bets in Marilyn Manson arc (failed due to Stand rules). Demonstrates creative use of adhesive duplication for traps and escapes.

Limit: Requires precise timing; merging damage is indiscriminate and can harm allies if misplaced. Cannot duplicate abstract concepts (debts, memories).

Form & Signs

Appearance — Ermes Costello is depicted as a tall, muscular, dark-skinned African-American woman in her early 20s (stated age 23 at incarceration). Attested features across manga and anime: long dark hair styled in cornrow-like braids/locs with a central part, often tied back; prominent lips, strong jaw and athletic build emphasizing physical power rather than sexualized design. In Green Dolphin Street Prison she wears the standard women's uniform — teal-blue

Symbols — Primary iconography is the Kiss sticker: lips + 'KISS' text, evoking price tags, brand labels, and pop-art lips (consistent with Araki's fashion/music motif). The act of placing and peeling stickers visually mirrors retail labeling and self-tagging, thematically linked to duplication and commodification. Duplicate objects bear a seam-like line where original and copy were split. In artbooks, Araki pairs her with motifs of glue, tap

Domains — Domains: Green Dolphin Street Prison (G.D.St. Prison) near Port St. Lucie, Florida — maximum-security women's ward, punishment ward, factory, courtyard, chapel, and later the prison graveyard/marsh. She inhabits the carceral environment as both hunter and protector; her ghost room ally Emporio's Burning Down the House is not her domain but she frequently operates there. Accounts I
The Murder of Gloria Costello and Voluntary IncarcerationStone Ocean manga Volume 1-2, Chapters 6-8; anime Episode 2-3 flashback

1999-2003 (publication); story set 2011 (with flashback ~2006-2008)

Ermes's older sister Gloria Costello was murdered by gangster Sports Maxx, who ran over and killed her after she witnessed his crimes, then bribed his way to a light sentence. Ermes, then working at a restaurant and caring for Gloria's memory, vowed revenge. Learning Maxx was incarcerated at Green Dolphin Street Prison, she deliberately committed armed robbery (robbing a store) to be sentenced there. She recounts this to Jolyne Cujoh after they meet in the prison yard, explaining her sticker Stand manifested after Jolyne's father Jotaro's influence and Whitesnake's disc distribution. This origin establishes her as a revenge-driven infiltrator, not a wrongly accused inmate. Ermes gains Kiss and a singular purpose; her alliance with Jolyne begins as mutual survival but deepens into sisterhood mirroring her lost sister. Sets up two battles with Sports Maxx.

Debt Collector Marilyn Manson - The Bet with MiraschonStone Ocean manga Volume 3, Chapters 22-27 'Debt Collector Marilyn Manson'; anime Episodes 6-7

2000 (serialization); story 2011

Ermes and Jolyne are challenged by inmate Miraschon, user of the automatic Stand Marilyn Manson, who traps victims in gambling debts where failure to repay results in automatic collection of organs/money. Miraschon tricks Ermes into a catch-ball bet. Ermes loses due to Jolyne's interference and Marilyn Manson begins extracting her liver and money. Jolyne intervenes, but Ermes insists on settling her own debt, using Kiss to duplicate her liver and cash to trick the Stand's logic, then physically assaults Miraschon to break the game. She survives by sheer will and Kiss's duplication trick. Demonstrates Ermes's code of self-reliance and Kiss's creative application; deepens trust with Jolyne who learns to respect Ermes's autonomy. Miraschon is defeated and her discs taken by Whitesnake.

Limp Bizkit - Revenge on Sports Maxx (Two Battles)Stone Ocean manga Volume 4, Chapters 28-35; anime Episodes 8-10

2000-2001

Ermes finally confronts Sports Maxx in the prison's disciplinary wing and marsh. Maxx's Stand Limp Bizkit reanimates corpses as invisible zombies (visible only to the dead or via specific conditions). In first encounter, Ermes is overwhelmed by invisible attackers and nearly killed. Recovering, she lures Maxx to the prison graveyard where his own victims' corpses are buried. Using Kiss, she duplicates her limbs and body parts to create decoys and to merge-crush Maxx's invisible minions, ultimately beating Maxx to death by duplicating his own head and merging it, or in manga by repeatedly crushing him with merged duplicates. She avenges Gloria, but the emotional aftermath leaves her hollow rather than triumphant. Gloria is avenged; Ermes's revenge arc resolves, freeing her to fully commit to Jolyne's quest to stop Pucci. Maxx's death also releases his hold on the Green Baby's bone (later plot). Ermes is severely injured and hospitalized within prison medical ward.

Accounts II
Operation Green Baby and Yo-Yo MaStone Ocean manga Volumes 8-10, Chapters 70-90; anime Episodes 20-24

2001-2002

After Foo Fighters' death and the emergence of the Green Baby (DIO's bone homunculus), Jolyne's group — including a recovered Ermes — participates in the hunt for the Baby in the prison's maximum security area. Ermes is assigned to guard and later pursues the Baby alongside Jolyne, Anasui, and Weather Report. She faces the Stand Yo-Yo Ma (a mosquito-like automatic Stand that enforces idiotic servitude and acid saliva) while Jolyne is incapacitated. Ermes uses Kiss to duplicate Yo-Yo Ma's saliva and redirect it, surviving until Anasui intervenes. The group secures the Green Baby, which later fuses with Pucci. Ermes proves her continued combat relevance post-revenge; Green Baby's capture leads directly to Pucci's evolution into C-Moon. Her cooperation with Anasui foreshadows final team dynamics.

Heavy Weather and the Pursuit to Cape CanaveralStone Ocean manga Volumes 12-14, Chapters 90-120; anime Episodes 27-32

2002

After Pucci fuses with Green Baby and escapes prison, Ermes joins Jolyne, Emporio, Anasui, and Weather Report in a breakout to chase him to Cape Canaveral. During Pucci's brother Weather Report's Heavy Weather outbreak — which turns humans into snails via subliminal rainbow — Ermes is temporarily transformed, experiencing body horror and near-death as her body becomes mollusk-like. She is saved when Weather Report regains his memory disc and Emporio counters. She then participates in the highway battle against Rikiel (Sky High) and Donatello Versus (Under World), using Kiss to duplicate car parts and create cover. Survives Heavy Weather, demonstrating resilience; the chase narrows to final confrontation at Kennedy Space Center. Her snail transformation is frequently cited in analyses of Araki's body horror.

C-Moon and Made in Heaven - Death and LegacyStone Ocean manga Volumes 16-17, Chapters 150-158; anime Episodes 36-38

2003 (finale)

At Kennedy Space Center, Pucci evolves Whitesnake into C-Moon (gravity inversion) then Made in Heaven (time acceleration). In the final battle, Ermes fights alongside Jolyne and Jotaro against Pucci's gravity attacks. She uses Kiss to duplicate Jolyne's arms and to create barriers, but Made in Heaven's acceleration makes her unable to keep up. Pucci, moving at superhuman speed, fatally slices Ermes's arm and torso (manga shows her bisected; anime shows severe slashing). She dies in Jolyne's arms, urging her friends to keep fighting. In the reset universe created by Made in Heaven, a reincarnated Ermes appears as Eldis (or Ermes-like hitchhiker) at the gas station with Emporio, and in the final Ireneverse she is implied to live peacefully as Ermes's counterpart, free from prison and revenge. Ermes's death is the emotional turning point prompting Jolyne's sacrifice to save Emporio. Her reincarnation in the Ireneverse provides thematic closure: a life without trauma and revenge, suggesting fate can be rewritten. Emporio's survival carries her memory forward.

Ermes is Jolyne's sister or relative. — They are unrelated inmates who become chosen sisters; Ermes's actual sister is Gloria Costello, deceased. Their bond is friendship, not blood.

Ermes's Stand Kiss is weak or comic relief compared to Stone Free. — Kiss is tactically versatile and lethal — it killed Sports Maxx, a main antagonist, and was crucial in final battles. Araki gives it B-rank destructive power; its sticker-merge can crush heads and duplicate weapons.

Ermes Costello is named after the Greek god Hermes. — Named after fashion brand Hermès and musician Elvis Costello per Araki's naming scheme for Part 6; Greek god is coincidental homonym. Official spelling Ermes distinguishes.

Ermes survives Stone Ocean and lives in the new universe as herself. — Ermes dies during Made in Heaven acceleration (Chapter 152-153). The person Emporio meets afterward is a counterpart (Eldis) in the reset Ireneverse with no memories of prison; original Ermes remains deceased in original universe.

The Approach

Lola Pop is a vibrant and acrobatic fighter who bounces onto the scene with a carnival-inspired flair, turning every battle into a spectacular performance. Her unique inflatable abilities and joyful demeanor make her a standout in the world of competitive gaming.

Content advisory. None. Lola Pop is a fictional character from a family-friendly video game franchise; no dark or sensitive material is associated with her.
Identity

Lola Pop

Pronunciation LOH-lah POP

Etymology

The name 'Lola' is a Spanish diminutive of Dolores, often associated with vivacity and charm. 'Pop' may reference her inflatable, popping nature and her candy-like appearance.

Cultural Ownership

Nintendo, as part of the ARMS franchise

Disputed Identifications

None known. Lola Pop is a distinct character within the ARMS roster, with no known disputes regarding her identity.

Description

Appearance

Lola Pop is a slender, dark-skinned woman with a large, colorful afro and a perpetual smile. She wears a striped, circus-like leotard with a ruffled collar and wristbands, along with high-heeled boots. Her most distinctive feature is her ability to inflate her body like a balloon, making her appear round and bouncy.

Symbols & Iconography

Carnival and circus motifs: stripes, polka dots, and a star-shaped earring. Her fighting style evokes the imagery of a living balloon animal.

Behaviour & Personality

Lola Pop is cheerful, energetic, and loves to entertain. She treats fights as performances, often striking poses and taunting with playful flair. She is confident and enjoys the spotlight, but remains good-natured and sportsmanlike.

Domains & Habitats

She hails from a whimsical, carnival-like realm, though the exact location is not specified. In the ARMS universe, she participates in the Grand Prix tournaments.

Version Differences

No notable version differences exist; Lola Pop appears consistently across all ARMS media, including the base game and subsequent updates.

Powers & Abilities

Inflation

Source Version ARMS (Nintendo Switch, 2017)

Description

Lola Pop can inflate her body to become larger and rounder, which allows her to bounce and absorb impacts. While inflated, she can deflect attacks and perform powerful body slams.

Limitations

Inflation is temporary and can be disrupted by strong attacks; she cannot move as quickly while fully inflated.

None known.

Bouncing Dash

Source Version ARMS (Nintendo Switch, 2017)

Description

She can perform a bouncing dash that lets her evade attacks and reposition quickly, often leaving a trail of sparkles.

Limitations

The dash has a short recovery period.

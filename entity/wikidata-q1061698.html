The Approach

In the shadow of the Galactic Empire's fall, a blind Dark Jedi named Jerec sought to become a god by claiming the Valley of the Jedi—a hidden nexus of Force energy where the spirits of fallen Jedi and Sith were imprisoned. His quest would pit him against a rogue mercenary named Kyle Katarn, setting the stage for one of the most iconic duels in Star Wars Legends.

Content advisory. Contains violence and dark themes.
Identity

Jerec

Pronunciation JER-ek

Etymology

The name 'Jerec' has no established in-universe etymology; it is a proper name created for the character.

Cultural Ownership

Star Wars Legends (Expanded Universe), originally appearing in the 1997 video game Star Wars Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II.

Disputed Identifications

None known. Jerec is a distinct character within Legends continuity.

Description

Appearance

Jerec is a male humanoid with pale skin, white hair, and blind white eyes. He wears dark, ornate robes typical of a Dark Jedi, often with a hood. In his first appearance, he has a cybernetic implant on his face, but later appearances show him without it. He wields a red lightsaber.

Symbols & Iconography

Jerec is associated with the dark side of the Force, often depicted with red lightsaber and dark robes. His blindness is a notable feature, but he uses the Force to perceive his surroundings.

Behaviour & Personality

Jerec is arrogant, ruthless, and obsessed with gaining power. He is a Dark Jedi who seeks to unlock the secrets of the Valley of the Jedi, a source of immense Force energy. He is cunning and manipulative, but also prone to overconfidence and underestimating his enemies.

Domains & Habitats

Jerec operates throughout the galaxy, but his main base is on the planet Ruusan, near the Valley of the Jedi. He also has ties to the Imperial Remnant.

Version Differences

In the game Star Wars Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II, Jerec is the primary antagonist. In the novelization, his backstory and motivations are expanded slightly. In later references, he is consistently depicted as a Dark Jedi seeking the Valley. No major contradictions exist between versions.

Stories & Myths

The Quest for the Valley of the Jedi

Source Star Wars Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II (video game) and novelizationCulture Star Wars LegendsPeriod Approximately 5 ABY (After the Battle of Yavin)

Summary

Jerec, a former Jedi who turned to the dark side, learns of the Valley of the Jedi on Ruusan, a place where the spirits of Jedi and Sith are trapped. He assembles a group of Dark Jedi to find the Valley and use its power to become a god. He is opposed by Kyle Katarn, a mercenary who discovers his own Jedi heritage. Jerec kills Kyle's father, Morgan Katarn, early in the game, setting Kyle on a path of revenge. Throughout the game, Jerec and his minions hunt for the Valley, killing innocent people and destroying the Jedi artifacts. In the end, Kyle confronts Jerec in the Valley, and after a lightsaber duel, Kyle defeats Jerec, destroying him and freeing the spirits.

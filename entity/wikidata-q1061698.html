Who It Is

In the shadow of the Galactic Empire's fall, a blind Dark Jedi named Jerec sought to become a god by claiming the Valley of the Jedi—a hidden nexus of Force energy where the spirits of fallen Jedi and Sith were imprisoned. His quest would pit him against a rogue mercenary named Kyle Katarn, setting the stage for one of the most iconic duels in Star Wars Legends.

Jerec is arrogant, ruthless, and obsessed with gaining power. He is a Dark Jedi who seeks to unlock the secrets of the Valley of the Jedi, a source of immense Force energy. He is cunning and manipulative, but also prone to overconfidence and underestimating his enemies.

Powers
Dark Side Force abilities

Jerec is a powerful Dark Jedi, proficient in telekinesis, Force lightning, and other dark side powers. He can also sense others through the Force despite his blindness.

Limit: His powers are not as strong as those of a Sith Lord like Palpatine, and he is ultimately defeated by Kyle Katarn.

Lightsaber combat

Jerec is skilled in lightsaber combat, wielding a red lightsaber. He is able to fight evenly with Kyle Katarn before being defeated.

Limit: He is eventually overpowered by Kyle Katarn.

Force vision

Despite being blind, Jerec can perceive the world through the Force, allowing him to navigate and fight effectively.

Limit: This ability is not explicitly detailed, but implied.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Jerec is a male humanoid with pale skin, white hair, and blind white eyes. He wears dark, ornate robes typical of a Dark Jedi, often with a hood. In his first appearance, he has a cybernetic implant on his face, but later appearances show him without it. He wields a red lightsaber.

Symbols — Jerec is associated with the dark side of the Force, often depicted with red lightsaber and dark robes. His blindness is a notable feature, but he uses the Force to perceive his surroundings.

Domains — Jerec operates throughout the galaxy, but his main base is on the planet Ruusan, near the Valley of the Jedi. He also has ties to the Imperial Remnant.

Accounts I
The Quest for the Valley of the JediStar Wars Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II (video game) and novelization

Approximately 5 ABY (After the Battle of Yavin)

Jerec, a former Jedi who turned to the dark side, learns of the Valley of the Jedi on Ruusan, a place where the spirits of Jedi and Sith are trapped. He assembles a group of Dark Jedi to find the Valley and use its power to become a god. He is opposed by Kyle Katarn, a mercenary who discovers his own Jedi heritage. Jerec kills Kyle's father, Morgan Katarn, early in the game, setting Kyle on a path of revenge. Throughout the game, Jerec and his minions hunt for the Valley, killing innocent people and destroying the Jedi artifacts. In the end, Kyle confronts Jerec in the Valley, and after a lightsaber duel, Kyle defeats Jerec, destroying him and freeing the spirits. The Valley of the Jedi is freed, and Jerec is destroyed. Kyle Katarn becomes a Jedi Knight.

Who It Is

The anthropomorphic personification of dreams and storytelling — one of the seven Endless, beings older than gods. Also known as Morpheus, Oneiros, the Shaper, and the Prince of Stories. He rules the Dreaming, a realm shaped by all conscious beings' imaginations, and is bound by ancient rules even he cannot break.

Attested in The Sandman (1989–1996, 2022–present)

Cultural Significance

The Sandman is the only comic to win the World Fantasy Award (they changed the rules afterward). Dream's story — of pride, imprisonment, change, and ultimately choosing to die — is a modern epic that elevates comics to the level of classical mythology.

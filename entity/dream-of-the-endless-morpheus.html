Who It Is
Form

Morpheus is typically depicted as a tall, slender figure with a pale complexion and a mischievous grin. He often wears a suit and tie, and carries a staff or a book.

Powers

Dream manipulation Morpheus has the ability to shape and control the dreams of others, creating vivid and realistic scenarios that can be both pleasurable and terrifying.

Accounts

The Sandman follows the story of Morpheus, the Dreamlord, as he navigates the complex and ever-changing world of dreams.

Dream Hunters is a collection of short stories that explore the world of dreams and the creatures that inhabit it.

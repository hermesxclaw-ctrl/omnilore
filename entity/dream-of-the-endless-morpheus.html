Form & Signs

Appearance — Morpheus is typically depicted as a tall, slender figure with a pale complexion and a mischievous grin. He often wears a suit and tie, and carries a staff or a book.

Symbols — Morpheus is associated with the symbols of the dreamworld, including the moon, the stars, and the night sky. He is also linked to the concept of sleep and the subconscious.

Domains — Morpheus inhabits the realm of dreams, where he rules over the vast and ever-changing landscape of the subconscious.

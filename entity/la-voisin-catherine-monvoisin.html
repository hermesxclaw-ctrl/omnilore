Who It Is
Form

La Voisin was a French fortune teller and commissioned poisoner, known for her alleged sorcery and involvement in the Affair of the Poisons. She was described as a woman of great charm and intelligence, who used her skills to manipulate and deceive others.

Powers

Poisoning La Voisin was known for her ability to obtain and use poison, which she used to eliminate her enemies and further her own goals.

Accounts

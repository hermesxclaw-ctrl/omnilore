Who It Is

Catherine Monvoisin, née Deshayes (c. 1640-1680), known as 'La Voisin,' was the central figure in the Affair of the Poisons (l'affaire des poisons) — a massive scandal that rocked the court of Louis XIV. La Voisin ran a network of fortune-tellers in Paris who provided poison, aphrodisiacs, and abortions to the highest levels of the French aristocracy. Her clients allegedly included Madame de Montespan, the king's mistress, who was accused of participating in black masses where infants were sacrificed. La Voisin was arrested in 1679, tortured, and burned at the stake in 1680. Her execution and the ensuing investigation implicated over 400 people, leading to 36 executions and the creation of the Chambre Ardente (Burning Court), a special tribunal.

Form & Signs

Appearance — La Voisin was a French fortune teller and commissioned poisoner, known for her alleged sorcery and involvement in the Affair of the Poisons. She was described as a woman of great charm and intelligence, who used her skills to manipulate and deceive others.

Symbols — La Voisin's symbols and iconography are not well-documented, but it is likely that she used various forms of divination and magical symbols to further her goals.

Domains — La Voisin operated in the city of Paris, specifically in the areas surrounding the Palace of Versailles and the Louvre.

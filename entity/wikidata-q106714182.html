The Approach

In the sprawling world of Hololive Production, where virtual idols and demons coexist, Nakiri Ayame stands out as a cheerful oni girl whose laughter echoes through her streams. But behind her bright smile lies a rich lore inspired by Japanese folklore, blending the charm of a modern entertainer with the mystique of a centuries-old demon.

Identity

Nakiri Ayame

Pronunciation NAH-kee-ree AH-yah-meh

Known As

AyameNakiriOni GirlAyame-chan

Etymology

Nakiri (百鬼) means 'hundred demons' or 'many ghosts', referencing her oni heritage. Ayame (あやめ) is a Japanese name meaning 'iris' or 'sweet flag', a flower associated with purification and the supernatural in Japanese culture.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese virtual entertainment (VTuber culture)

Disputed Identifications

Ayame is a fictional character, but her oni persona is inspired by Japanese folklore. There is no real-world counterpart or ancient tradition; she is a modern creation by Cover Corporation.

Description

Appearance

Ayame has long white hair with a red gradient, and red eyes. She wears a black and red kimono with a white obi, and has small oni horns on her head. Her outfit includes a red hakama and traditional Japanese sandals. She often carries a katana, reflecting her samurai-like demeanor.

Symbols & Iconography

Her symbol is the oni mask, and her colors are red and white. She is often depicted with a sword, and her emblem includes a stylized demon face.

Behaviour & Personality

Ayame is known for her cheerful, playful, and slightly mischievous personality. She often laughs and jokes, but can be surprisingly calm and collected. She enjoys playing games, especially horror games, and interacting with fans. She has a strong sense of loyalty to her friends and is often seen as a caring senpai.

Domains & Habitats

As an oni, she is associated with the supernatural realm, but in Hololive lore she resides in the human world as a virtual entertainer. She often mentions her 'home' in the mountains, but streams from her studio.

Version Differences

Her design has remained consistent, but her lore has been expanded through official manga and animations. In some fan interpretations, she is more serious, but official content shows her as cheerful.

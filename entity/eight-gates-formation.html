The Approach

In the hidden villages of the shinobi world, there exists a forbidden key that unlocks the body's true potential—at the cost of the life that wields it. The Eight Gates Formation is a legendary taijutsu technique that tears down the body's natural limiters, granting godlike power while burning through the user's own vitality. It is a desperate gamble, a last resort that can turn a battle—but it may also end the warrior who dares to open the final gate.

Content advisory. The technique involves self-harm, physical destruction, and potential death. Depictions may include graphic injury and fatal consequences.
Identity

Eight Gates Formation

Pronunciation HAH-chee-mon

Known As

HachimonEight Inner GatesEight Gates of the Body

Etymology

The name derives from the eight chakra points (gates) along the body's meridian system, which are 'opened' to release physical limitations. In Japanese, 'Hachimon' (八門) literally means 'eight gates'.

Cultural Ownership

This is a fictional technique from the Japanese manga/anime series Naruto, created by Masashi Kishimoto. It is not part of any real-world martial art or folklore, though it draws inspiration from East Asian concepts of chakra and body energy pathways.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans confuse the Eight Gates with the Eight Inner Gates of the body, but they are the same concept. No significant disputed identifications beyond fan interpretation of its mechanics.

Description

Appearance

The user's body undergoes visible changes as gates open: skin reddens, veins bulge, chakra becomes visibly turbulent, and the user's aura intensifies. At the Seventh Gate, a blue-green chakra aura appears; at the Eighth Gate, the user's body glows red with steam-like vapor, and the skin may appear burned or charred.

Symbols & Iconography

The eight gates are often depicted as points along the spine or as a diagram of the body's chakra network. The number eight holds symbolic significance in Japanese culture (e.g., eightfold paths, eight trigrams).

Behaviour & Personality

The technique requires immense willpower and self-sacrifice. Users often exhibit a 'fierce warrior' mentality, accepting death to protect others. Might Guy, the most prominent user, embodies this with his 'flames of youth' enthusiasm.

Domains & Habitats

The Eight Gates are a physical technique, not tied to a location. It is used in combat, typically in life-or-death situations.

Version Differences

In the manga, the technique is depicted with more detail on the physical toll and the exact chakra points. The anime expands the visual effects and adds emotional scenes. No major differences in mechanics.

Powers & Abilities

Gate Opening

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

The user releases the body's natural limiters on chakra flow, increasing physical strength, speed, and reflexes. Each gate multiplies power, with the Eighth Gate granting power far beyond a Kage (village leader).

Limitations

Each gate causes severe physical strain; opening the Eighth Gate leads to death after the chakra is exhausted. The user can only maintain the gates for a short time.

Contradictions

The exact power multiplier is not clearly defined; some fans speculate it's exponential, but no official numbers exist.

Hirudora (Evening Elephant)

Source Version Manga Chapter 675, Anime Episode 420

Description

A technique used with the Seventh Gate, producing a massive, elephant-shaped blast of air pressure.

Limitations

Requires the Seventh Gate to be open; causes significant strain.

Contradictions

None noted.

Sekizo (Night Guy)

Source Version Manga Chapter 677, Anime Episode 420

Description

The ultimate technique of the Eighth Gate, a single kick that distorts space and can nearly kill the strongest enemy (Madara Uchiha).

Limitations

Only usable with the Eighth Gate; the user dies after using it, and the kick's power is so great it can break the user's own bones.

Contradictions

The exact damage to Madara is debated; some say it would have killed him if not for his regeneration.

Stories & Myths

Might Guy's Final Battle

Source Naruto manga, Chapters 675-677; Anime Episode 420Culture Japanese pop culturePeriod 2014 (manga), 2015 (anime)

Summary

During the Fourth Great Ninja War, Might Guy opens all Eight Gates to fight Madara Uchiha, who has become the Ten-Tails jinchuriki. Guy unleashes Sekizo, nearly killing Madara, but survives due to Naruto's healing. The fight cements Guy's legacy as a hero.

Participants

Might Guy

Madara Uchiha

Naruto Uzumaki

Consequences

Guy is left crippled but alive; Madara is severely injured but heals. The battle showcases the Eight Gates' ultimate power.

Variants

The anime extends the fight with additional dialogue and visual flair.

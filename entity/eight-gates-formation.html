Who It Is

A forbidden martial arts technique that allows the user to surpass their physical limits by removing the eight chakra gates in the body's chakra circulatory system. Each gate removed exponentially increases power at the cost of extreme physical damage to the user. Opening the final Gate of Death grants power surpassing the Five Kage but leads to the user's death.

Powers

Exponential power increase, chakra gate release

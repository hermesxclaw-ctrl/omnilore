Who It Is

An infinite ocean in near-total darkness, with a depth that has never been measured. The water is cold (~4°C) and the surface is as still as glass. There is no land, no shore, no visible sky — just water extending in all directions. The surface is deceptively peaceful. Below, something moves — things of incredible size that never breach the surface but whose silhouettes can be seen in the bioluminescent flashes that occasionally light the depths. Wanderers who enter Level 7 arrive on a single floating platform. The platform does not move. The exit is directly beneath it, at an unknown depth.

Form

An infinite, flat, dark ocean. The water is black from depth, not pollution. Occasional bioluminescent flashes — blue, green, purple — illuminate shapes in the deep: coils, fins, things with too many limbs. The air smells of salt and something organic. The only sound is the water lapping against the platform and, very rarely, a low, resonant sound from below that vibrates through the water and the bones of anyone on the platform.

Cultural Significance

Level 7 weaponizes thalassophobia — the fear of deep water and what might be in it. It is the Backrooms' most primal level.

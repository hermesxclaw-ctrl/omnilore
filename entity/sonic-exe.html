Who It Is

Sonic.exe is a creepypasta about a haunted, corrupted ROM of the original Sonic the Hedgehog game for Sega Genesis. The story, written by JC-the-Hyena, is narrated by a character named Tom who receives a disc from a friend with a note reading 'DESTROY THIS CD.' Ignoring the warning, Tom plays the game. At first, it appears to be a normal Sonic game, but it quickly devolves into something horrific: Sonic's sprite is replaced by a version with bleeding, hyper-realistic eyes; the player is forced to watch as 'Sonic' — the entity calling itself Sonic.exe — hunts down and graphically kills Tails, Knuckles, and Dr. Robotnik while taunting the player through text boxes that appear to know Tom's real name. The entity proclaims itself a god, declaring that it has claimed the souls of everyone who has ever played the game. The creepypasta spawned an enormous fan community that created actual playable Sonic.exe games, fan fiction, and countless YouTube Let's Play videos. The original story is often criticized for its amateurish writing and reliance on shock value, but its cultural impact is undeniable — Sonic.exe became the template for an entire subgenre of 'evil video game character' creepypasta. It also inspired the indie game 'Sonic.exe: The Disaster' and a flood of '.exe' variants for other franchises. The character of Sonic.exe — a demonic entity wearing the skin of a beloved childhood character — taps into the deep-seated fear of nostalgia corrupted, and of the safe spaces of childhood entertainment becoming predatory.

Who It Is

In the storm-tossed seas of the Grand Line, where pirates carve their legends in blood and thunder, there sails a man who greets every catastrophe with laughter. Urouge, the 'Mad Monk' of the Skypiean sky islands, descends from the clouds not to preach, but to smash—his colossal frame and iron staff turning battles into brutal sermons. Yet for all his fearsome reputation, the monk's past is a parchment of blank spaces, his motives as inscrutable as the heavens he once called home.

Urouge is depicted as calm and jovial, often laughing in the face of danger. He is a devout believer, frequently praying and invoking 'God' in his speech. Despite his peaceful demeanor, he is a ruthless pirate who does not hesitate to kill or destroy. He is also shown to be pragmatic and observant, as seen when he reads newspapers to stay informed about world events. He is fiercely independent, rejecting alliances and following his own path.

Powers
Guru Guru no Mi (Grunt-Grunt Fruit)

A Paramecia-type Devil Fruit that allows Urouge to convert damage he receives into raw physical strength and size. The more he is hurt, the stronger and larger he becomes. This ability is demonstrated when he defeats a Pacifista after taking multiple hits.

Limit: The fruit is a Paramecia, so it does not grant elemental powers or transformation. The increase in strength and size appears to be temporary and may wear off after the battle. The exact limits are unknown, as Urouge has not been shown using it extensively. It is likely that the ability requires him to sustain damage, meaning he cannot activate it at will without being harmed.

Physical Strength and Endurance

Urouge possesses immense natural strength and durability, even without his Devil Fruit. He is able to fight on par with other Supernovas and survive attacks from an Admiral. His large frame and muscle mass contribute to his physical prowess.

Limit: His strength is not on the level of the Emperors or Admirals, as shown by his defeat by Kizaru. He cannot withstand attacks from top-tier fighters without his Devil Fruit's damage-conversion ability.

Combat Skills with Iron Staff

Urouge wields a large iron staff in combat, using it to smash enemies and defend himself. He is shown using it in his fight against a Pacifista and later against an Enforcer in the New World.

Limit: No specific limitations are known, but it is a conventional weapon and can be disarmed or destroyed by sufficiently strong opponents.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Urouge is a massive, muscular man with dark skin, short black hair, and a thick beard. He wears a sleeveless white shirt, dark pants, and a large brown cape. He carries an iron staff, which he uses as a weapon. His most distinctive feature is his perpetually smiling face, often with his eyes closed, giving him a serene yet menacing appearance. Symbols — Urouge's cross tattoo and his staff are his primary symbols, reflecting his monk identity. His epithet 'Mad Monk' (狂僧) is a play on his religious appearance and his violent actions. His ship, the 'Marco Polo', is named after the historical explorer, suggesting a theme of travel and discovery.

Domains — Urouge hails from a sky island, though the specific one is not named. He operates primarily in the Grand Line, particularly in the New World. He is seen on Sabaody Archipelago, an unnamed sky island in the New World, and later on an island during the Wano Country arc. He is a pirate captain, commanding the Marco Polo.

Accounts I
Introduction in the Sabaody ArchipelagoOne Piece, Chapters 509-511

2008

Urouge is introduced as one of the eleven Supernovas, rookie pirates with bounties over 100 million Berries, who have gathered at Sabaody Archipelago before entering the New World. He is shown fighting a Pacifista, a cyborg weapon of the World Government, and initially appears to be losing. However, after taking heavy damage, he grows in size and strength, defeating the Pacifista. Shortly after, Admiral Kizaru arrives and easily defeats Urouge, demonstrating the gap between the Supernovas and the Admirals. Urouge is captured by the Marines and sent to Impel Down, but he later escapes during the chaos caused by Monkey D. Luffy. He then travels to the New World.

Accounts II
Defeating an Enforcer in the New WorldOne Piece, Chapter 713

2013

After entering the New World, Urouge is shown on a sky island, having defeated an Enforcer of the New World. He is reading a newspaper, indicating his interest in world events. The identity of the Enforcer is not revealed, and the battle is not shown in detail. This event establishes that Urouge has become a significant player in the New World, capable of defeating experienced pirates.

Observation of the Whole Cake Island IncidentOne Piece, Chapters 795 and 801

2015

Urouge is shown on an island in the New World, observing the events of the Whole Cake Island arc, where Luffy and his crew challenge Big Mom. He is later seen among the Worst Generation reacting to the news of the incident, showing his awareness of the shifting balance of power. Urouge's reaction highlights his role as a major pirate in the New World, though he does not directly participate in the events.

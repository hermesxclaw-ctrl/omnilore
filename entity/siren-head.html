Who It Is

You're driving through deep country when the radio dissolves into static. Then a voice — your mother's voice — crackles through the speakers. 'Pull over, sweetheart. It's not safe.' You know your mother has been dead for six years. You look out the window and see it: a forty-foot tower of dried, mummified flesh striding through the treeline, twin sirens where a head should be, screaming your mother's last words over and over. It's not hunting you. It's broadcasting you. You are the message.

Siren Head is a creature created by Canadian horror artist Trevor Henderson, first posted to social media in 2018. It is a 40-foot-tall humanoid entity composed of desiccated, mummified flesh the color of rusted metal and old bone, with two massive police/military-style sirens where its head should be. These sirens can broadcast any sound Siren Head has ever heard — voices of previous victims, emergency alerts, radio broadcasts, white noise, music — and it uses these sounds to lure, disorient, and terrify its prey. Siren Head is Henderson's most famous creation, spawning countless fan games, animations, tribute art, and (like Slender Man before it) becoming a viral horror icon of the late 2010s.

Siren Head is an ambush predator that uses sound as its primary weapon. It can mimic human voices with perfect accuracy, replaying the last words of its victims to draw in new ones. It can broadcast at volumes ranging from a whisper to an ear-shattering shriek that causes physical pain and disorientation. Its sirens can also emit infrasound — frequencies below human hearing that cause nausea, anxiety, and a sense of supernatural dread. It is not fast (its mass makes it lumbering), but it is patient, and it knows the woods. Henderson's lore suggests Siren Head may be one of many, that it has existed for centuries, and that it is not strictly biological — it is described as a 'friendly reminder that something is very, very wrong with the universe.'

Origin Post

Siren Head was created by Trevor Henderson and first posted to his Twitter account in August 2018, accompanied by text describing a tall, thin, emaciated figure with sirens for a head that roamed rural areas and broadcast disturbing sounds. Henderson, known for his 'found photograph' style of horror art (presenting his creatures as blurry, authentic-looking images of monsters), expanded Siren Head's lore through additional posts, describing its abilities, behavior, and possible origins. The creature's concept — sound as weapon — was partially inspired by the emergency sirens that Henderson heard growing up in rural Canada.

Form

A humanoid figure standing approximately 40 feet tall, composed of dried, mummified, sinewy flesh stretched over an impossibly thin frame the color of rust and old bone. Its limbs are elongated and spindly, ending in long, claw-like fingers. Its 'neck' is a long, flexible pole of flesh that supports two large metal sirens — the kind found on emergency vehicles, police cars, or air-raid towers — fused together as its head. These sirens are connected to its flesh by organic-looking tubes and wires. It has no eyes, no mouth, no face. Its ribcage is visible through its skin, and its posture is hunched like a hunting heron. In some depictions, the sirens rotate and swivel independently.

Powers

Sound Mimicry Siren Head can perfectly mimic a wide range of sounds, including emergency sirens, human speech, music, and radio static. It uses this to lure victims or to disorient them. Canonical (Henderson) and most fan interpretations It is unclear if it can mimic sounds it has never heard. The mimicry is often described as slightly 'off' or distorted, which can be a tell for observant victims. Some works depict its mimicry as flawless, others as subtly imperfect.

Superhuman Strength and Durability Its massive size and frame give it immense physical strength, capable of crushing cars or tearing through structures. Its body is highly resilient to damage. Inferred from its appearance and actions in fan works No specific weaknesses are documented. None major, as it is rarely shown being harmed.

Extreme Height and Reach At 12 meters tall, it can see and reach over obstacles, making it a formidable predator in open or forested terrain. Canonical (Henderson) Its size makes it difficult to hide, but it uses its environment and body contortions to compensate. None.

Body Contortion Siren Head can bend, twist, and contort its body in unnatural ways, allowing it to hide in tight spaces or appear from unexpected angles. Common in fan works and implied by Henderson Not explicitly stated as a power, but a common physical trait. Some interpretations show it as more rigid.

Behavior & Habitat

Siren Head is an ambush predator that uses psychological warfare. It listens to its victims, learns their voices and their fears, and then broadcasts these back to terrify and lure them. It might call out in the voice of a lost child, a crying baby, a friend calling for help. It might play the radio show that was on when someone died. It seems to feed on fear and flesh, though its biology (if it has one) is unclear. It prefers isolated areas — deep woods, abandoned towns, rural roads — where its broadcasts won't attract mass attention. It is patient and intelligent, capable of waiting for days, of setting traps with sound, of understanding that a familiar voice in the dark is more terrifying than any monster.

Weaknesses & Wards

Siren Head is physical and can theoretically be harmed by heavy weaponry, though its durability is unknown. Its size makes it difficult to hide in open terrain. It can be tracked by its sound — it broadcasts, and that broadcast reveals its position. It may be vulnerable to its own weapon: loud, disorienting noise directed back at it. Destroying its sirens would presumably neutralize its primary threat. It appears to be solitary and does not call for reinforcements. The fact that it hunts through isolation means that staying in populated, well-lit areas may offer protection.

Field Notes

█ Trevor Henderson has stated that Siren Head is not supernatural — it's an 'aberrant creature,' implying a biological (if impossible) origin.

█ The sirens on Siren Head's head are not merely attached — they grow from its flesh, with organic tubes and wires connecting them to its body.

█ Siren Head's favorite 'song' to broadcast is apparently white noise mixed with the distorted screams of its victims.

Cultural Significance

Siren Head represents a new generation of internet horror — creator-owned (unlike the anonymous Slender Man), platform-native (designed for Twitter and Instagram's visual format), and participatory (Henderson encourages fan art, games, and interpretations). It demonstrates how individual artists can now launch global horror icons without institutional backing. Siren Head's emphasis on sound horror taps into primal fears about communication, the unreliability of familiar voices, and the violation of the soundscape — in an era of deepfakes and AI voice cloning, a monster that steals voices feels prophetically relevant.

Modern Interpretations

Siren Head has been adapted into dozens of indie horror games (most notably on itch.io and Roblox), animations, short films, and tribute art. It appears frequently in 'analog horror' YouTube series (alongside entities like the Backrooms). Henderson's other creations (Cartoon Cat, Long Horse, The Man with the Upside-Down Face) form a shared universe, and Siren Head is its most famous resident. The creature's popularity on TikTok and YouTube Shorts has introduced it to Gen Z and Gen Alpha. Its design — the fusion of mechanical emergency equipment with organic horror — has influenced a wave of similar creature designs.

Content Notes

Siren Head is fundamentally a predator that hunts and kills humans. Its primary tactic is luring victims using mimicry, which is a form of psychological manipulation and predation. The creature's emaciated form and the industrial, mechanical nature of its head evoke a sense of cold, inhuman hunger.

The creature can be seen as a manifestation of modern anxieties about technology, isolation, and the dangers lurking in the natural world. Its mimicry of human voices is a deeply unsettling violation of trust.

Core to the creature's design and all narratives

Siren Head's appearance is a classic example of body horror. Its mummified flesh stretched over a metal frame, its unnatural height, and its contorting body all evoke a visceral sense of wrongness and decay.

The creature's design taps into the uncanny valley, presenting a figure that is almost human but deeply, fundamentally wrong.

Evidence for A: Henderson's original posts and images.

Origin
Siren Head is a supernatural or alien entity. ⇌ Siren Head is a biological creature or a result of some experiment.
Evidence for A: Its impossible physiology and sound mimicry suggest a non-natural origin.
Evidence for B: Its emaciated frame and predatory behavior are consistent with a biological organism.
What We Don't Know

The primary gap is the lack of a central, authoritative canon. Trevor Henderson has not released a definitive lore document, leaving most details to fan interpretation. This makes it difficult to separate 'canon' from 'popular fanon'. The creature's rapid spread and the sheer volume of fan works also make a complete catalog of adaptations and appearances nearly impossible.

A way to hold it in your mind

Think of Siren Head as the ultimate 'fake news' monster. It doesn't just attack you physically; it attacks your trust. It uses the sounds you associate with safety (a siren, a friend's voice) to lure you into danger. It's the embodiment of the fear that the things we rely on for information and connection can be twisted to deceive us.

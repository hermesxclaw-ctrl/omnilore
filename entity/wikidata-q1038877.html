Who It Is

Chizuru Kagura is the last priestess of the Yata clan, a guardian of the Sacred Treasures who carries the burden of a thousand-year war against the serpent Orochi. Her mirror, the Yata no Kagami, is not just a weapon—it's the key to sealing an ancient evil, and her quiet resolve hides a tragic past marked by loss and duty.

Chizuru is calm, composed, and deeply serious, driven by duty. She is kind but reserved, carrying the weight of her clan's responsibility. She is protective of her friends and allies, and she shows a strong sense of justice. She can be vulnerable when reminded of her sister's death.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Kagura' (神楽) refers to Shinto ritual dances, while 'Chizuru' (千鶴) means 'a thousand cranes,' a symbol of longevity and peace. The clan name 'Yata' (八咫) references the Yata no Kagami, one of the three Imperial Regalia.

Pronunciation — CHEE-zoo-roo kah-GOO-rah

Aliases — Maki Kagura (as a clone), Yata no Chizuru, Priestess of the Yata clan

Ownership — Created by SNK for the King of Fighters series; the character draws from Japanese mythology, specifically the Yamata no Orochi legend and the Three Sacred Treasures.

Powers
Yata no Kagami (Mirror of Yata)

Chizuru uses the sacred mirror to create afterimages, project energy blasts, and reflect attacks. It is one of the Three Sacred Treasures used to seal Orochi.

Limit: Requires spiritual energy; overuse may exhaust her.

Mikazuki no Mai (Dance of the Crescent Moon)

A special move where she creates a crescent-shaped energy wave from her mirror.

Limit: Can be blocked or dodged.

Kagami no Mai (Dance of the Mirror)

She creates a holographic clone of herself to attack from a distance.

Limit: The clone is vulnerable and can be destroyed.

Sanctuary

A defensive barrier that nullifies projectiles.

Limit: Only blocks projectiles, not physical attacks.

Sealing Power

As a descendant of the Yata clan, Chizuru has the ability to seal Orochi and other supernatural beings, using her mirror in conjunction with the other Sacred Treasures.

Limit: Requires the other two treasures (Kusanagi sword, Yasakani jewel) and significant spiritual power.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Chizuru is a tall, elegant Japanese woman in her early twenties. She has long, dark hair often tied in a ponytail with a red ribbon, and she wears a white and red miko (shrine maiden) outfit, modified for combat: a white top with red accents, a red hakama, and white boots. She carries a mirror on her back, the Yata no Kagami, which she uses in battle.

Symbols — The Yata no Kagami (mirror) is her primary symbol, representing the Sun Goddess Amaterasu. Her moves often involve mirror projections and light-based attacks. She also uses the 'Sanctuary' technique to create barriers.

Domains — She is a priestess, so she is often found in shrines or sacred places. In the KOF world, she travels to participate in tournaments and investigate Orochi-related threats.

Accounts I
The Orochi SealingKOF '96, KOF '97

1990s (in-game timeline)

Chizuru, as the head of the Yata clan, is responsible for maintaining the seal on the evil entity Orochi. In KOF '96, she investigates the resurgence of Orochi's power and enters the tournament to find the culprit. She is defeated by the protagonist team, but later joins forces with Kyo and Iori to perform the sealing ritual in KOF '97. Orochi is successfully sealed, but at great cost: Chizuru loses her sister, and Iori is cursed.

Accounts II
The New Face Team and the Orochi ClanKOF '97

1997

Chizuru enters the KOF '97 tournament with her team (the 'New Face Team' is actually the Orochi team in disguise). She discovers that the team is composed of the Four Heavenly Kings of Orochi, and they are trying to release their master. She fights them and, with Kyo and Iori, performs the sealing. Orochi is sealed, but the Three Sacred Treasures are weakened.

The Fall of the Yata ClanKOF 2003

2003

Chizuru is manipulated by the villain Mukai, who poses as a member of 'Those from the Past.' She is used to weaken the seal on Orochi, and in the process, she is possessed by the power of the Sacred Treasures, causing her to age rapidly. She is eventually freed by Kyo and Iori, but the seal is broken. Orochi's seal is broken, leading to the events of KOF XIII. Chizuru loses her powers and goes into seclusion.

Who It Is

In the chaotic arena of Super Smash Bros. Melee, where legendary heroes and villains clash, there exists a silent, faceless menace—the Fighting Wire Frames. These metallic automatons, born from the depths of Master Hand's twisted imagination, serve as relentless training dummies and formidable foes, their existence a testament to the game's hidden depths and the eerie possibilities of a world without individuality.

They are mindless automatons, exhibiting no personality or emotions. They attack the player on sight and fight in a simple, aggressive manner, using basic punches, kicks, and grapples. They do not communicate or show any signs of intelligence.

Basic Combat Moves

They can perform simple punches, kicks, and grabs, similar to a generic fighting character. They have no special moves or projectiles.

Limit: Their moveset is limited and predictable, making them relatively easy to defeat for experienced players.

Resilience

They have moderate health and can withstand several hits before being defeated.

Limit: They are still easily KO'd compared to playable characters.

Spawn in Groups

They often appear in groups, overwhelming the player with numbers.

Limit: They are individually weak, so groups can be managed with area-of-effect attacks.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Fighting Wire Frames are humanoid figures composed of a metallic, wireframe-like mesh. They have a silver/gray color and a somewhat skeletal appearance, with visible joints and no facial features. Their bodies are slender and androgynous, and they move with a stiff, mechanical fluidity.

Symbols — They are associated with the Master Hand, as they appear to be his creations. They lack any unique symbols or iconography, reinforcing their role as generic combat units.

Domains — They appear exclusively in Super Smash Bros. Melee's single-player modes, particularly in the 'Adventure Mode' (on the 'Brinstar' and 'F-Zero' stages) and in various Event Matches.

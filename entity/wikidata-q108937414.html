Who It Is

He was born dead, raised by the dead, and taught to live by three undead who should have had nothing left to teach: a skeletal warrior who could not feel warmth, a mummified priestess who could not weep, and a ghostly sage who could not touch the world he loved. In the ruined City of the Dead, where no child should have drawn breath, William G. Maryblood learned to pray, to swing a sword, and to ask what it means to live well when you remember dying once before.

Attested across all versions: earnest, diligent, deeply empathetic, and burdened by memory of a failed prior life. In his previous life he was an unremarkable, lonely Japanese adult who died without accomplishment (light novel implies social withdrawal and regret); reincarnated with intact memories, he resolves to not waste a second chance and to live without regret. Powers
Divine Protection of Gracefeel (Gracefeel's Blessing / Blessing of the Lantern)

As paladin of Gracefeel, goddess of light and reincarnation, Will receives divine protection allowing him to channel holy light, perform miracles (healing, purification, warding against undead), and resist death-aligned magic. Manifests as pale, warm light. Requires faith, prayer, and adherence to Gracefeel's tenets; not automatic.

Limit: Power weakens if faith wavers or if he acts against Gracefeel's ethic (selfishness, cruelty). Cannot resurrect the dead; cannot override Stagnate's curse without sacrifice. Requires incantation/prayer and stamina; overuse exhausts him.

Martial Mastery — Sword and Spear (Blood's Training)

Trained from age ~3 to 15 by Blood, an undead warrior of legendary skill (former champion). Proficient in one-handed sword, spear, shieldwork, hand-to-hand, and battlefield tactics. Can fight high-level undead, beasts, and wyverns. Style is practical, defensive, emphasizing footwork and endurance.

Limit: Human stamina; not superhuman strength. Vulnerable to poison, overwhelming numbers, and dragon-scale durability. Requires maintenance and equipment; cannot match ancient heroes without divine aid.

Magic — Words (Kotoba) and Sorcery (Gus's Training)

Taught by Gus (Augustus), ghost mage, to use Words — divine language that rewrites reality — for elemental magic (light, fire, wind), enchantment, and warding. Can cast without chant via Words, and uses scholarly magic (circles, mana control). Knows extensive lore, languages, history, and alchemy.

Limit: Mana finite; complex spells require preparation, components, or long incantations. Overcasting causes collapse. Cannot use Words lightly — misuse draws divine attention. Knowledge is scholarly, not innate.

Blessing of the Echo / Multiple Divine Words

Later attested: Will learns to invoke Words of multiple gods (not only Gracefeel) with permission, including earth and wind deities, allowing versatile miracles. Gains title Faraway Paladin, able to call upon Gracefeel's trumpet and light to purify undead and heal allies en masse.

Limit: Each god's Word requires understanding and respect for that god's domain; cannot compel opposed gods like Stagnate. Using multiple blessings strains body and soul.

Leadership, Charisma, and Oathbinding

Not supernatural but narratively treated as ability: capacity to inspire loyalty among diverse peoples (human, elf, dwarf, halfling), broker peace between Beast Woods factions, and swear paladin oaths that bind him magically. His earnestness functions as social power.

Limit: Naivety about politics; oaths can become burdensome. Cannot compel loyalty; must earn it.

Resistance to Death and Stagnation

Due to Gracefeel's protection and upbringing among undead, Will has high resistance to fear, necromantic corruption, and Stagnate's whispers. Can enter Stagnate's domain and resist undeath curse that claimed his guardians.

Limit: Resistance is not immunity; prolonged exposure to Stagnate's realm nearly kills him and requires sacrifice of guardians. Still mortal and can be killed normally.

Accounts I
The Boy in the City of the Dead (Shisha no Machi no Shounen)Yanagino Kanata, Saihate no Paladin Light Novel Vol 1 (Overlap Bunko, 2016); Anime S1 Ep 1-4

2015-2016 (in-universe: Will age 0-15)

A Japanese man who died lonely and regretful awakens as an infant in a ruined city of the dead, found and adopted by three undead: Blood the skeletal warrior, Mary the mummified priestess of Gracefeel, and Gus the ghost mage. They name him William and raise him with fierce love, teaching him sword, magic, and prayer while hiding their own tragic past. Will learns he is the only living child in a city cursed by the god Stagnate, who holds his guardians in undeath as punishment for breaking oaths. At age 15, Stagnate appears to claim Will, and Will confronts the god in Gracefeel's temple, affirming his will to live and freeing his guardians to finally die and be reincarnated, at the cost of profound grief. He buries them and leaves the city. Establishes Will's moral core, divine patronage, and trauma. Guardians' souls released; Will inherits their weapons and blessings and sets out into living world as paladin. Foundation for entire series theme: what it means to live well.

The Archer of the Beast Woods (Kemono no Mori no Kishi)Light Novel Vol 2 (2017); Manga ch 15-28; Anime S1 Ep 5-8

2017 (in-universe: Will age 15-16, immediately after leaving city)

Emerging into the living world, Will encounters the Beast Woods where humans, elves, dwarves and halflings are in conflict over resources and a spreading demonic corruption. He befriends Meneldor (Menel), a half-elf hunter ostracized for his heritage, and saves the halfling village of Robina Goodfellow (Bee) from undead and beasts. Using diplomacy, martial skill, and Gracefeel's miracles, Will mediates between the races, defeats a corrupted forest guardian, and proves that coexistence is possible. He is recognized as a true paladin rather than a mere wandering swordsman. Will gains first living comrades and reputation. Establishes his role as bridge between peoples and his rejection of prejudice. Meneldor and Robina become permanent companions.

The Lord of the Rust Mountains (Tetsusabi no Yama no Ou)Light Novel Vol 3 (2018); Anime Season 2 Ep 1-7 (2023)

2018 (in-universe: Will age 16)

Will and his companions travel to the Rust Mountains, where the ancient dragon Valacirca (Valacirca the Rust Dragon) has awakened, spreading rust-miasma that withers forests and sickens villages. The dragon is a former hero corrupted by despair and Stagnate's influence. Will, now formally sworn as paladin, leads a coalition of Torch Port soldiers, dwarven smiths, and elves to confront the dragon. He does not merely slay Valacirca but attempts to understand and release him, invoking multiple gods' Words and ultimately purifying the dragon's soul with Gracefeel's light, at great personal risk. The battle devastates the mountains but ends the blight. Will's legend spreads as Dragon-slayer and purifier, not just killer. Demonstrates his mature theology: even dragons deserve compassion. Secures alliance with dwarves and access to dwarven steel. Valacirca's death releases the land.

Accounts II
The White Sails and the Port City (Torch Port / Tōka no Minato arc)Light Novel Vol 4: Yūkyū no Kōro (2019); Anime Season 2 Ep 8-12

2019 (in-universe: Will age 16-17)

After the dragon, Will arrives at Torch Port (Tōka no Minato), a bustling trade city governed by competing guilds and haunted by poverty, slavery, and the legacy of war. He becomes embroiled in city politics, helps reorganize the temple of Gracefeel under Bishop Bagley, and confronts human cruelty that no monster can excuse: exploitation of the poor, and the lingering worship of Stagnate among desperate citizens. Will uses not sword but law, charity, and preaching, establishing a hospice and defending refugees from a demonic incursion tied to Stagnate's cult. He is offered lordship but wrestles with whether power corrupts. Transitions Will from wandering hero to civic leader and true paladin responsible for institutions. Deepens theme that evil is not only monsters but human systems. Sets up later volumes where Will must govern.

The Faraway Paladin — Oaths and Reincarnation (Series throughline: Promise of Reunion)Light Novel Vol 1-5 overarching narrative; author afterwords

2015-present (meta-narrative)

Across all arcs, Will's core story is that of a second chance at life. Having died without loving or being loved, he is given a childhood saturated with love by undead parents who themselves failed in life and seek redemption through raising him. His ultimate promise to them — to live well, to not regret, and to meet again after death — frames every adventure. Gracefeel's doctrine of reincarnation promises that souls who live well are reborn, and Will's guardians choose to pass on trusting they will meet him again. Later volumes hint that this promise will be tested as Will ages, faces mortality, and must decide what kind of ancestor he will be. Provides emotional and theological spine of series; distinguishes Faraway Paladin from power-fantasy isekai by centering grief, gratitude, and ethical living over revenge or domination. Fan reception centers on this throughline as most moving element.

Interlude: The Demon Lord's Whisper and the High King's Past (Gus and Blood backstory)Light Novel Vol 1 interludes and Vol 3 flashbacks; revealed via Gus's lectures and Stagnate's accusations

In-universe: ~200 years before Will's birth

Through fragmented memories and Stagnate's taunts, it is revealed that Blood, Mary, and Gus were once heroes who failed: Blood and Gus were companions of the High King, Mary was a priestess of Gracefeel, and together they attempted to defeat Stagnate's champion but fell to infighting, betrayal, and despair, accepting Stagnate's bargain of undeath to guard the city eternally. Their raising of Will is penance and hope. This backstory is told non-linearly, not as a single chapter but as lore revealed across volumes. Recontextualizes guardians from mere mentors to tragic figures seeking redemption; explains why Stagnate holds them. Will's success redeems their failure.

William G. Maryblood is an overpowered, flawless isekai protagonist who never struggles. — Will is highly trained but repeatedly struggles, doubts, and requires help. He nearly dies against Stagnate and Valacirca, is naive about living society, and his strength comes from years of disciplined training and faith, not a cheat skill. His arc is about learning to live with grief and responsibility.

The G. in his name officially stands for Gracefeel (or Gus) and is confirmed canon. — No official source as of 2024 explicitly expands G. The light novel, manga, and anime consistently render it as initial only. Both Gracefeel and Gus theories are fan speculation without citation.

Faraway Paladin is based on Western folklore or is a historical paladin legend. — It is a wholly original 2015 Japanese light novel by Kanata Yanagino, inspired by Western fantasy tropes (D&D-like gods, paladins, dragons) but not derived from any single folklore source. William is not a folk hero.

Will was a powerful hero in his previous life. — His previous life was explicitly unremarkable and lonely — an adult Japanese man who died without achievement and regretted wasting his life. His motivation is to not repeat that failure, not to reclaim past glory.

The anime is the original source and the light novel is an adaptation. — Light novel (Overlap Bunko, 2016) is primary; anime (2021) is adaptation. Web novel (Shōsetsuka ni Narō, 2015) predates both.

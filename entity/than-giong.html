Who It Is
Form

Than Giong is often depicted as a young boy riding an iron horse, with a whip in hand. He is said to be incredibly brave and strong. His appearance varies across different depictions, but he is often shown with a determined look on his face.

Powers

Strength Than Giong is said to possess incredible physical strength, allowing him to defeat even the most formidable enemies.

Bravery Than Giong is known for his fearlessness and willingness to face danger head-on, inspiring others to do the same.

Leadership Than Giong is said to possess exceptional leadership skills, able to rally his people and lead them to victory in times of war.

Accounts

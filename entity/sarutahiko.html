Who It Is

Sarutahiko is a powerful earthly kami (kunitsukami) associated with crossroads, guidance, physical strength, and the boundary between heaven and earth. He appears most famously in the 'Ninigi Descent' myth: when the heavenly grandson Ninigi is about to descend from the Plain of High Heaven (Takamagahara) to rule the earth, he finds Sarutahiko blocking the way at the crossroads of heaven and earth. Sarutahiko has a large, bright nose, a red face, glowing eyes, and a giant spear; his formidable appearance terrifies the heavenly delegation. Ame-no-Uzume confronts him, exposes her breasts and genitals, and makes him laugh — disarmed, he steps aside and agrees to guide Ninigi to earth. Sarutahiko then becomes the earthly guide and protector of the heavenly kami. He is married to Ame-no-Uzume. Sarutahiko is the kami of all beginnings — of journeys, new projects, and transitions. His shrines (especially Sarutahiko Jinja in Ise) are visited by those seeking safe travel, successful ventures, and protection at life's crossroads. He is also the patron of sumo wrestling and the Nō theatre tradition of the Tora no Kai.

Attested in Kojiki / Nihon Shoki / Nō theatre traditionEra Asuka to Heian Period (c. 600-800 CE)

Who It Is
Form

Delirium is often depicted as a young woman with a mischievous grin and a wild, unkempt mane of hair. She is typically dressed in a flowing white gown with a golden circlet on her head. Her appearance can vary depending on the story and the artist's interpretation.

Powers

Illusions Delirium has the ability to create powerful illusions, making it difficult for others to distinguish reality from fantasy. She can create elaborate deceptions, making it seem as though anything is possible.

Dreamwalking Delirium can enter the dreams of others, influencing their thoughts and emotions. She can also use this ability to communicate with others across vast distances.

Reality Warping Delirium has the power to warp reality itself, creating new dimensions and realities with a mere thought. However, this power comes at a great cost, causing chaos and destruction in the process.

Delirium plays a significant role in this series, which follows the story of Dream, also known as Morpheus. Delirium is one of the Endless, a family of cosmic beings who embody different aspects of the universe.

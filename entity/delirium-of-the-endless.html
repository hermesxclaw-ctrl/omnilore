Who It Is

Delirium is a complex and fascinating character who embodies the concept of delirium. Her role as a keeper of secrets and a teller of tales makes her a compelling figure, and her relationships with other members of the Endless add depth and complexity to the story.

Delirium is known for her playful and mischievous nature. She delights in causing chaos and confusion, often using her powers to create illusions and deceptions. Despite her troublesome behavior, she is also fiercely loyal to her family and will do whatever it takes to protect them.

Powers
Illusions

Delirium has the ability to create powerful illusions, making it difficult for others to distinguish reality from fantasy. She can create elaborate deceptions, making it seem as though anything is possible.

Limit:

Dreamwalking

Delirium can enter the dreams of others, influencing their thoughts and emotions. She can also use this ability to communicate with others across vast distances.

Limit:

Reality Warping

Delirium has the power to warp reality itself, creating new dimensions and realities with a mere thought. However, this power comes at a great cost, causing chaos and destruction in the process.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Delirium is often depicted as a young woman with a mischievous grin and a wild, unkempt mane of hair. She is typically dressed in a flowing white gown with a golden circlet on her head. Her appearance can vary depending on the story and the artist's interpretation.

Symbols — Delirium is associated with the concept of delirium, which is often represented by symbols such as a maze, a labyrinth, or a puzzle. She may also be depicted with a book or a scroll, representing her role as a keeper of secrets and a teller of tales.

Domains — Delirium inhabits the realm of dreams and the subconscious, where she weaves her spells of confusion and deception.

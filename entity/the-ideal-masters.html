Who It Is

A collective of ancient, powerful mortal mages who transcended mortality by transforming themselves into pure animus — becoming the Ideal Masters, rulers of the Soul Cairn. They exist as geometric crystal formations of pure will and soul energy, trading necromantic power for souls. They are neither Daedra nor Aedra but something unique: self-made immortal beings who forged their own plane of Oblivion.

Powers

Soul trading (soul gems for power)

Soul Cairn realm creation and governance

Geometric crystal form (immune to most attacks)

Necromantic power bestowal

Summoning of Bonemen, Mistmen, and Wrathmen

Soul manipulation and binding

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

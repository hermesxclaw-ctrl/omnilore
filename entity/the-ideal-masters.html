The Ideal Masters

also known asThe Ideal AedraThe Perfect Ones
TypeEt'Ada / Artificial Aedra Collective
Who It Is
Form

The Ideal Masters are often depicted as powerful, artificial beings with a mix of human and Aedra-like features. They are said to possess a unique, otherworldly energy that sets them apart from other beings in the Elder Scrolls universe.

Powers

The Gift of the Aedra The Ideal Masters possess the ability to channel the power of the Aedra, allowing them to perform feats of magic and wonder.

Accounts

The story of the creation of the Ideal Masters is a central part of the Elder Scrolls mythology. According to legend, the Aedra created the Ideal Masters as a gift to the mortal world, imbuing them with their own power and energy.

The story of the Ideal Masters and the Aedra's Gift is a central part of the Elder Scrolls mythology. According to legend, the Ideal Masters were tasked with delivering the Aedra's Gift to the mortal world, a powerful artifact that would bring great benefit to humanity.

The story of the Ideal Masters and the Thalmor is a central part of the Elder Scrolls mythology. According to legend, the Ideal Masters were tasked with guiding and protecting the Thalmor, a powerful and ancient civilization.

Who It Is

The Ideal Masters are a fascinating and complex part of the Elder Scrolls mythology, with a rich history and lore. This dossier provides a comprehensive overview of their creation, powers, and relationships, as well as their impact on the mortal world.

The Ideal Masters are said to be highly intelligent and powerful, with a strong sense of purpose and duty. They are often depicted as being highly disciplined and focused, with a keen sense of justice.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Ideal Masters are often depicted as powerful, artificial beings with a mix of human and Aedra-like features. They are said to possess a unique, otherworldly energy that sets them apart from other beings in the Elder Scrolls universe.

Symbols — The Ideal Masters are often associated with the symbol of the Aedra, which is a stylized, swirling pattern of light and energy.

Domains — The Ideal Masters are said to inhabit the realm of the Aedra, a mystical realm that exists outside of the mortal world.

The story of the creation of the Ideal Masters is a central part of the Elder Scrolls mythology. According to legend, the Aedra created the Ideal Masters as a gift to the mortal world, imbuing them with their own power and energy.

Who It Is
Form

In Irish mythology, Balor is often described as a giant with a large eye that wreaks destruction when opened. He is also associated with the scorching sun and has been likened to figures from other mythologies, such as the Welsh Ysbaddaden and the Greek Cyclops. As a fiend in Dungeons & Dragons, Balor is depicted as a powerful demon with a massive eye that can destroy cities.

Powers

Eye of Destruction Balor's eye is said to have the power to destroy entire cities and civilizations. When opened, it unleashes a blast of energy that can level mountains and burn cities to the ground.

Accounts

Balor takes part in the Battle of Mag Tuired, a great conflict between the Tuatha Dé Danann and the Fomorians. He is killed by his grandson Lugh, who strikes him with a spear.

Balor is defeated by a group of heroes in a battle that takes place in the Nine Hells. His eye is destroyed, and he is banished to the farthest reaches of the underworld.

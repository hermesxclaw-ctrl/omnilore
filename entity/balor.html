Eye of Destruction

Form & Signs

Appearance — In Irish mythology, Balor is often described as a giant with a large eye that wreaks destruction when opened. He is also associated with the scorching sun and has been likened to figures from other mythologies, such as the Welsh Ysbaddaden and the Greek Cyclops. As a fiend in Dungeons & Dragons, Balor is depicted as a powerful demon with a massive eye that can destroy cities.

Symbols — Balor's eye is often depicted as a symbol of destruction and chaos. In some versions, his eye is said to be so powerful that it can destroy entire cities.

Domains — Balor is often associated with the underworld, the abyss, and other realms of darkness. In Dungeons & Dragons, he is said to reside in the Nine Hells, a realm of fire and brimstone.

Accounts I
The Battle of Mag TuiredIrish mythology

Ancient

Balor takes part in the Battle of Mag Tuired, a great conflict between the Tuatha Dé Danann and the Fomorians. He is killed by his grandson Lugh, who strikes him with a spear. The defeat of Balor marks a turning point in the battle, and the Tuatha Dé Danann emerge victorious.

Balor's DownfallDungeons & Dragons

Modern

Balor is defeated by a group of heroes in a battle that takes place in the Nine Hells. His eye is destroyed, and he is banished to the farthest reaches of the underworld. The defeat of Balor marks a significant victory for the heroes, and they are hailed as champions of the realm.

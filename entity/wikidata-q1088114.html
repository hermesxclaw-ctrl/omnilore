Who It Is

He arrives at the World Martial Arts Tournament looking like a frail, wide-eyed attendant — and makes Piccolo, the reincarnated Demon King, tremble without throwing a punch. That is Shin, the Eastern Supreme Kai, the last surviving god of creation in Universe 7, a deity who has watched friends murdered by an unstoppable pink horror and spent five million years waiting for that horror to wake again.

Shin is earnest, polite, anxious, and deeply serious — a stark contrast to the comedic or arrogant deities seen earlier (Korin, Kami, King Kai). He speaks formally, often with honorifics, and shows visible fear regarding Majin Buu, openly admitting his trauma and powerlessness. He is self-sacrificing (willing to confront Babidi alone), reverent toward mortals who surpass him (Goku, Gohan), and initially underestimates mortal ingenuity. Powers
Kai-Kai (Instantaneous Movement)

Teleportation unique to Kaiōshin that requires no energy signature lock, unlike Shunkan Idō (Instant Transmission). Can travel anywhere within Universe 7, to other universes, the afterlife, and Zeno's palace with a thought, and can bring others via physical contact.

Limit: Requires knowledge of destination or visual focus; cannot bypass certain barriers (e.g., Zeno's palace requires permission). Shin's range is attested as superior to Kibito's Kai-Kai but inferior to Whis's warp.

Telepathy and Mind Reading

Can speak telepathically across distances and read surface thoughts, used to communicate with Z Fighters and to coordinate during Babidi's ship infiltration.

Limit: Not shown to control minds; Babidi's magic outclasses it. Limited to willing or nearby targets.

Telekinesis and Paralysis

Can freeze or immobilize powerful fighters; Shin immobilized SSJ2 Gohan at the tournament to allow Babidi's extraction, and briefly restrained Fat Buu.

Limit: Ineffective on beings significantly stronger than him; fails on Buu after initial moment. Costs concentration; broken if user is distracted or overpowered.

Healing and Life Energy Transfer

As Kibito Kai, inherits Kibito's healing ability: can fully heal mortal wounds, restore stamina, and reattach limbs. As Shin alone, healing is limited but he can donate energy to revive others (helped restore Goku's energy for Genki-Dama).

Limit: Healing drains stamina; cannot resurrect dead. Requires contact.

Potara Fusion

Permanent (for Kais) fusion via wearing one Potara earring each. Shin fused with Kibito to become Kibito Kai, vastly increasing power and gaining Kibito's abilities. Later defused via wish to Majin Buu's internal gas / Dragon Balls.

Limit: For mortals lasts one hour; for Kaiōshin originally permanent until retconned. Fusion can be undone by specific Dragon Ball wish or Buu's physiology.

Matter Materialization and Creation

Kaiōshin can create matter, clothing, and planetary bodies via magic. Shin creates a block of katchin (hardest metal in universe) for Z Sword test and conjures clothes.

Limit: Creation is slow and limited in scale; cannot create life as complex as mortals instantly. Combat applicability low.

Divine Authority and Life Link

As Supreme Kai, his life is linked to Universe 7's God of Destruction Beerus. His divine ki cannot be sensed by mortals. He can unlock potential via Old Kai ritual (as Kibito Kai he assists).

Limit: Life link is a vulnerability: his death kills Beerus and vice versa. Divine ki sensing limitation is bypassed by other gods. Cannot use Hakai.

Combat Power (Ki Manipulation)

Possesses ki far above lower Kais and Piccolo (Piccolo forfeits rather than fight him), but far below Super Saiyan 2 level. Can fire energy blasts and enhance durability; survived a blast from Buu that killed other Kais.

Limit: Explicitly weaker than Majin Buu, Dabura, and post-training Saiyans. His fear is justified: he is outclassed in the Buu arc and serves as strategist, not frontline fighter.

Accounts I
The Mysterious Duo at the 25th Tenkaichi BudokaiDragon Ball manga Vol. 37-38, ch. 439-442; Dragon Ball Z ep. 214-215

In-universe Age 774; publication 1994

Shin and his attendant Kibito enter the 25th World Martial Arts Tournament disguised as mortal contestants to make contact with Goku and friends. Shin effortlessly reads minds, and when paired against Piccolo, Piccolo immediately forfeits, trembling, recognizing Shin's divine status. Shin warns Goku that a wizard named Babidi seeks to revive Majin Buu and asks for their help to stop the energy theft at the tournament. Establishes Shin's identity and the stakes of the Buu arc; Gohan is later paralyzed by Shin to prevent interference while Spopovich and Yamu steal his Super Saiyan energy for Buu's revival — the inciting incident of the arc.

The Five Million Year Massacre — Bibidi, Buu and the Death of the KaiōshinDragon Ball manga ch. 445-446 (Shin's flashback); Daizenshuu 7 p.56-57; Dragon Ball Super manga ch. 16 (Gowasu recount)

In-universe ~5 million years before Age 774; told 1994

Shin recounts to Goku that the evil wizard Bibidi created Majin Buu, who rampaged across the universe. Four Kaiōshin and the Grand Supreme Kai confronted him: West and North Kaiōshin were killed outright, South Kaiōshin was absorbed (giving Buu his muscular form), and Grand Supreme Kai was absorbed (giving Buu his fat, childlike, less evil form). Only Shin, the youngest and weakest Eastern Kaiōshin, survived because he was not on the battlefield's front line. Bibidi sealed Buu in a ball and was later killed by Shin, but the ball survived on Earth. Explains why Shin is the last Kaiōshin, why Buu is on Earth, and why Buu's forms change with absorptions. Survivor's guilt defines Shin's motivation. Later retconned in Super to clarify Shin was not yet Grand Kaiōshin at the time.

Infiltration of Babidi's Spaceship and the Revival of Majin BuuDragon Ball manga ch. 447-471; Dragon Ball Z ep. 216-232

Age 774

Shin leads Goku, Vegeta, Gohan, and Kibito to Babidi's hidden spaceship to prevent Buu's revival. He explains Babidi's magic and Buu's absorption power. Despite Shin's plan for stealth, Vegeta's desire for battle and Babidi's mind control over Dabura and later Vegeta derail it. Shin is nearly killed by Dabura's petrifying spit (saved when Dabura is killed) and watches helplessly as Babidi extracts enough energy to hatch Buu. Shin attempts to kill Fat Buu with a blast but is swatted aside, surviving only because Buu is distracted. Buu is revived, Babidi gains control, and the Z Fighters are scattered. Shin's failure underscores his limited combat power and forces him to rely on mortals. Kibito is killed by Dabura (later revived).

Accounts II
The Z Sword, Kibito Kai, and the Old KaiDragon Ball manga ch. 471-479; Dragon Ball Z ep. 255-264

Age 774 (during Buu's rampage)

After Gohan's apparent death, Shin and revived Kibito take Gohan to the Sacred World of the Kai to train with the Z Sword, believed to grant great power. Gohan trains to wield the heaviest sword in the universe. Shin creates a cube of katchin to test it; Gohan cuts it, but the sword breaks when tested against katchin, releasing Elder Kai (Dai Kaiōshin 15 generations prior) trapped inside. Elder Kai, fused with an old witch via Potara, agrees to unlock Gohan's potential via a ritual. To facilitate the ritual, Shin and Kibito fuse via Potara into Kibito Kai at Elder Kai's suggestion, becoming a single being with both's power and personality. Gohan's ultimate form is unlocked; Kibito Kai is formed, remaining fused until Dragon Ball Super. Establishes Potara mechanics and Elder Kai lore. Shin's willingness to fuse shows his commitment.

The Life Link and the God of Destruction — Beerus AwakensDragon Ball Super anime ep. 1-6, 55; manga ch. 1-4, 16; Chōzenshū 1

Age 775-778 (4 years after Buu); publication 2015-2016

Dragon Ball Super reveals that Shin's life is linked to Beerus, God of Destruction of Universe 7: if one dies, the other dies. Because Shin survived Buu, Beerus survived his 39-year sleep. When Beerus awakens and seeks Super Saiyan God, Shin and Kibito Kai watch in terror via crystal ball, realizing they are powerless before Beerus and Whis. Later, when Goku Black (Zamasu) kills Shin in Future Trunks' timeline, Beerus simultaneously dies, allowing Black to rampage unchecked — demonstrating the link's cosmic importance. Recontextualizes Shin's survival as cosmically critical; explains why Beerus did not stop Buu (he was asleep and Shin was too weak to wake him). Sets up Future Trunks arc stakes.

Apprentice of Universe 10 — Zamasu and the Zero Mortals PlanDragon Ball Super manga ch. 16-26; anime ep. 53-67

Age 779-780 (main timeline) and Age 796 (Future Trunks timeline)

Shin's counterpart in Universe 10, Gowasu, takes on North Kai Zamasu as apprentice Kaiōshin. Shin appears via inter-universal communication as a peer, warning Gowasu about Zamasu's disdain for mortals. Zamasu, after witnessing mortals' violence and learning from Goku's power, develops the ideology that mortals must be exterminated. He kills Gowasu, steals Goku's body via Super Dragon Balls (becoming Goku Black), kills Shin in Future Trunks' timeline (thus killing Beerus), and allies with his future self to enact the Zero Mortals Plan. Shin in the present timeline survives and assists Goku and Vegeta by providing intelligence and healing, but is sidelined as the gods of destruction and Zeno intervene. Shows the Kaiōshin apprenticeship system and Shin's role as a responsible peer. The arc is the darkest exploration of Kaiōshin ideology — a Kai who concludes creation is flawed. Shin's survival in the main timeline contrasts with his murder in the future, emphasizing timeline divergence.

Supreme Kai is the strongest god in Dragon Ball. — In original DB/Z he was the highest known god, but Dragon Ball Super establishes he is near the bottom of the divine hierarchy above him are Gods of Destruction (Beerus), Angels (Whis), Grand Priest, and Zeno. Even among Kaiōshin, Shin was the weakest of his generation and is considered inexperienced.

Shin and Kibito Kai are two separate Supreme Kais. — Kibito Kai is the Potara fusion of Shin and Kibito, not a separate individual. They were fused from late Buu arc until Dragon Ball Super (Age 774-779), then defused. Shin is the Kaiōshin; Kibito is his attendant.

Supreme Kai created the Dragon Balls. — Earth's Dragon Balls were created by Kami (later Dende), both Namekians; Namekian Dragon Balls by Grand Elder Guru. Kaiōshin can create planets and objects but are not the creators of the titular Dragon Balls — the Namekian race is, though they are themselves a Dragon Clan with divine connections.

Shin is immortal and cannot be killed. — Shin is long-lived (millions of years) and ages extremely slowly, but is mortal and can be killed — Majin Buu killed his peers, and Zamasu/Goku Black kills Future Shin, which also kills Future Beerus. His death has cosmic consequences due to the life link.

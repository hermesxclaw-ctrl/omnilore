Who It Is
Form

Tane Mahuta is a giant kauri tree, estimated to be between 1,250 and 2,500 years old. It stands 4.3 meters in diameter and 16.5 meters in height, making it the largest living kauri tree in New Zealand. The tree's trunk is straight and smooth, with a few branches and leaves.

Powers

Separation of Earth and Sky Tane Mahuta is said to have separated the earth and sky to create the world, allowing light and life to enter the world.

Accounts

Tane Mahuta is said to have separated the earth and sky to create the world, allowing light and life to enter the world.

Tane Mahuta is said to have created the first birds, allowing them to fly and inhabit the world.

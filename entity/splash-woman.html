Who It Is

The only female Robot Master in the classic series, designed for underwater search and rescue operations. Splash Woman has a mermaid-like design with fins on her limbs and a scaly blue-and-pink color scheme. She commands aquatic robots and uses her Laser Trident with deadly precision.

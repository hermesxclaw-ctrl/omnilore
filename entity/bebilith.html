Who It Is

A massive, spider-like fiend from the Abyss that preys on demons and mortals alike. Its chitinous hide is the color of dried blood, and its mandibles drip with a venom that causes flesh to rot on the bone. Bebiliths shred armor with their razor-sharp legs and can plane shift at will, making them terrifying ambush predators.

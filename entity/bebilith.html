Form & Signs

Appearance — The Bebilith is a massive, spider-like creature with a main body approximately 14 feet (4.3 meters) in length, including its legs. The majority of its mass, around 2 tons (1,800 kilograms), is concentrated in the main body. Its legs are long and spindly, allowing it to move with ease. The Bebilith's appearance is often described as 'enormous' and 'malformed'.

Symbols — The Bebilith is often associated with dark, foreboding environments, such as caves and abandoned ruins. Its presence is often accompanied by a sense of dread and foreboding.

Domains — The Bebilith is found in dark, foreboding environments, such as caves, abandoned ruins, and other areas with a sense of dread and foreboding.

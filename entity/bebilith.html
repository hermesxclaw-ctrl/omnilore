Who It Is
Form

The Bebilith is a massive, spider-like creature with a main body approximately 14 feet (4.3 meters) in length, including its legs. The majority of its mass, around 2 tons (1,800 kilograms), is concentrated in the main body. Its legs are long and spindly, allowing it to move with ease. The Bebilith's appearance is often described as 'enormous' and 'malformed'.

Powers

Multiattack The Bebilith makes three attacks: two with its claws and one with its bite.

Webbing The Bebilith ignores movement restrictions caused by webbing.

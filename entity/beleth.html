Who It Is

Thirteenth spirit, a terrible and mighty King commanding 85 legions. Beleth rides a pale horse, preceded by trumpets and all manner of music — a terrifying fanfare that announces his arrival. He is so fierce that the conjurer must hold a hazel wand in hand, pointing toward the South-East, to compel obedience. According to the Pseudomonarchia Daemonum, Noah's son Ham was the first to invoke Beleth after the flood, using a book of demonic invocation. Beleth governs love and passion — his primary office is to cause men to love women and women to love men, stirring the most violent and consuming forms of romantic desire.

Who It Is
Form

In Finnish mythology, Hiisi is often depicted as a malevolent spirit or a goblin-like creature. It is said to dwell in deep woods, inside mountains, and in waters.

Powers

Shape-shifting The Hiisi is said to have the ability to shape-shift into various forms, including animals and humans.

Accounts

A hunter encounters a Hiisi in the forest and is forced to make a deal with the entity.

A village is plagued by a Hiisi, which brings harm and chaos to the residents.

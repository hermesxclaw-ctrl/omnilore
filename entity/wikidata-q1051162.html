Zoey is a name that carries the ancient Greek word for 'life' into the modern world, appearing on birth certificates, movie credits, and gaming avatars alike. But behind this common name lies a rich tapestry of historical figures, fictional heroines, and cultural variations that span centuries and continents.

Etymology

The name Zoey is a variant of Zoe, which derives from the Greek word ζωή (zōē), meaning 'life'. It is related to the name Eve, which also carries connotations of life.

Cultural Ownership

The name has Greek origins but has been adopted widely across Western cultures, including English-speaking countries, Europe, and Latin America. It holds religious significance in Jewish and Christian traditions, where it is sometimes used as a Greek equivalent of Eve.

Disputed Identifications

The name has multiple spelling variants, and there is no single canonical spelling. The distinction between 'Zoe' and 'Zoey' is often a matter of personal or regional preference, and both are considered valid.

The Approach

In the shadows of Tokyo's occult underworld, where curses are born from human malice, one man chose to fight back not with a blade, but with a classroom. Masamichi Yaga, the stern principal of Tokyo Metropolitan Curse Technical College, is remembered as the architect of a generation of sorcerers—and as a man whose own life was consumed by the very system he sought to reform.

Content advisory. Contains themes of violence, death, institutional corruption, and psychological trauma.
Identity

Masamichi Yaga

Pronunciation mah-sah-MEE-chee YAH-gah

Known As

Principal YagaYaga-sensei

Etymology

The name 'Masamichi' (雅道) combines characters meaning 'elegant' and 'path' or 'way'. 'Yaga' (夜蛾) literally means 'night moth', which may symbolize his role as a nocturnal guardian or his connection to cursed corpses.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese popular culture; fictional character created by Gege Akutami for the manga Jujutsu Kaisen.

Disputed Identifications

None known; the character is a recent creation and not based on historical figures.

Description

Appearance

A middle-aged man with a muscular build, short black hair, and a prominent scar over his left eye. He typically wears a dark suit with a tie, and has a stern, intimidating expression. He is often seen wearing sunglasses or a blindfold.

Symbols & Iconography

His scarred eye and stern demeanor symbolize his past battles and his role as a hardened leader. His association with cursed corpses (puppets) is a recurring motif.

Behaviour & Personality

Yaga is pragmatic, stern, and deeply protective of his students, though he rarely shows emotion openly. He is willing to make difficult decisions for the greater good, and is critical of the jujutsu world's corruption. He values human life and the potential of the next generation.

Domains & Habitats

Tokyo Metropolitan Curse Technical College (Tokyo Jujutsu High); the jujutsu world of modern Japan.

Version Differences

The manga and anime portray Yaga consistently, though the anime adds some minor scenes. His backstory is more detailed in the manga's later arcs.

Powers & Abilities

Cursed Corpse Manipulation

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

Yaga is a special-grade sorcerer who can create and control sentient cursed corpses (puppets) imbued with cursed energy. His most notable creation is Panda, a high-grade cursed corpse with a complex personality.

Limitations

The exact mechanics of his ability are not fully detailed; it is implied that creating high-level cursed corpses requires significant skill and possibly a binding vow.

Stories & Myths

Yaga's Role as Principal

Source Jujutsu KaisenCulture Japanese popular culturePeriod Modern era (2018-present)

Summary

Yaga serves as the principal of Tokyo Jujutsu High, overseeing the education and training of young sorcerers. He is introduced when Yuji Itadori arrives at the school, and he lays down the rules for Yuji's execution, giving him a chance to live while collecting Sukuna's fingers. Yaga appears throughout the series as a mentor figure and a voice of reason among the higher-ups.

Yaga's Death

Source Jujutsu Kaisen manga, Chapter 145 (approx.)Culture Japanese popular culturePeriod Modern era (2018-present)

Summary

During the Culling Game arc, Yaga is targeted by the higher-ups for his possession of the 'Cursed Corpse' technique, which they deem dangerous. He is confronted by Gakuganji and other sorcerers, and during the confrontation, he reveals that he has been developing a method to create sentient cursed corpses that could potentially replace humans. He is ultimately killed by Gakuganji, but not before imparting his final technique to Panda, allowing Panda to become stronger.

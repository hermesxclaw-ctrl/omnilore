The Approach

In the blood-soaked world of Samurai Shodown III, a mountain of a man storms into battle, his fists wrapped in prayer beads and his heart burning with righteous fury. Gaira Caffeine, the brash monk nephew of the legendary Nicotine Caffeine, is a force of nature—a walking contradiction of holy vows and unbridled aggression. But who is this titan of faith and fury, and what drives him to the blade?

Content advisory. Contains depictions of violence and combat typical of a fighting game. No sexual or otherwise sensitive content.
Identity

Gaira Caffeine

Pronunciation GAI-rah KAF-een

Known As

Kim Ung Che (Korean language setting)

Etymology

'Gaira' is likely a play on 'gairaigo' (Japanese for 'loanword') or a reference to 'gaira' as a term for 'foreigner'? Alternatively, it may be a name chosen for its robust sound. 'Caffeine' is a direct reference to his uncle Nicotine Caffeine, suggesting a family theme of stimulant-based names.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese video game franchise (SNK). The character is a Japanese creation, but his design and name may draw from various cultural influences.

Disputed Identifications

In the Korean localization, his name is changed to 'Kim Ung Che', which later became a separate character in Samurai Shodown VI. This has led to confusion about whether they are the same person or distinct entities.

Description

Appearance

Gaira is depicted as a large, muscular man, towering over most other characters. He wears monk robes, often with a bare chest or exposed arms, and carries prayer beads. His hair is typically short and dark, and he has a fierce expression. In the darker art style of Samurai Shodown III, his features are more rugged and serious.

Symbols & Iconography

Prayer beads (juzu) are a prominent symbol, representing his monk status. His large stature and physicality evoke strength and brute force, contrasting with his religious role.

Behaviour & Personality

Gaira is described as 'brash and overbearing,' suggesting a loud, confident, and possibly aggressive personality. As a monk, he may be expected to be calm and peaceful, but his behavior suggests a more combative and zealous nature. He is likely driven by a strong sense of justice or duty, but his methods are forceful.

Domains & Habitats

As a character in a fighting game, he exists within the Samurai Shodown universe, which is set in a fictionalized version of feudal Japan. He likely travels to various locations to fight, as do other characters.

Version Differences

In Samurai Shodown III, each character has two versions: Slash and Bust. For Gaira, the Slash version is likely more balanced and similar to traditional fighting styles, while the Bust version may be more aggressive and have different moves. However, specific differences are not detailed in the provided source.

Who It Is

In a world where virtual idols shimmer with pixel-perfect perfection, Yuzuki Choco stands out as a deliciously different presence—a succubus nurse who blends seductive charm with genuine care, creating a unique space in the Hololive universe where fans find comfort, laughter, and a touch of mischief.

Yuzuki Choco is known for her flirtatious and teasing demeanor, often referring to viewers as 'patients' and herself as a 'nurse'. She has a warm, caring side and often comforts her audience. She is playful, sometimes clumsy, and enjoys games and chatting with fans.

Succubus Charm

As a succubus, she possesses an innate charm that attracts and captivates viewers, making her streams engaging and popular.

Limit: This is a persona trait, not an actual supernatural power. It manifests as her charismatic on-screen presence.

Nursing Skills

She is a nurse, and her streams often involve 'treating' her viewers with kind words and encouragement, creating a supportive community.

Limit: These are role-played, not actual medical skills.

Streaming and Gaming

She is proficient in playing a variety of games, from action to horror, and often streams them with commentary.

Limit: Her skill varies per game.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Yuzuki Choco has a distinctive succubus-inspired design: long pink hair, red eyes, a black and white nurse outfit with a short skirt, and a small pair of black wings on her head. She often wears a nurse cap and carries a syringe-like staff.

Symbols — Her symbol is a chocolate bar with a bite taken out, reflecting her name and sweet personality. Her color scheme is pink and black, and she is often depicted with hearts and syringes.

Domains — As a VTuber, she primarily exists in the digital realm, streaming from her virtual 'room' which is often decorated with medical and sweet-themed items. She is part of the Hololive 2nd Generation.

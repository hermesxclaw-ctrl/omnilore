Accounts

A being named Aroni is described as the Ark's guardian, preventing the unworthy from finding it. The tales serve as moral lessons about humility and the dangers of seeking divine power.

Aroni appears as a luminous intermediary in a Sabbath vision, bridging the earthly and divine. This story reframes him from passive guardian to active channel of the Shekhinah.

Jewish tradition is rich with stories of hidden guardians — angels who watch over sacred objects, locations, or souls. The Ark of the Covenant, as the most sacred object, would logically be assigned divine guardians. Aroni fits this pattern as one such guardian, though his name appears only in fragmentary references. The story emphasizes that not all celestial beings are named in scripture; some survive only in the margins of Kabbalistic manuscripts and folk memory.

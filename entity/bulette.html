Who It Is
Form

The Bulette is a large, burrowing creature that resembles a cross between a shark and a worm. It has a long, eel-like body covered in tough, scaly skin, and a pair of sharp teeth. Its body is capable of stretching and compressing to fit through narrow openings.

Powers

Burrowing The Bulette is able to tunnel through solid earth at a rate of 30 feet per round.

Accounts

A group of adventurers must navigate the treacherous tunnels of the Underdark to reach a lost city, where they encounter a massive Bulette.

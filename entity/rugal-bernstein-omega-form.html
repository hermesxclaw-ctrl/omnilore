Who It Is
Form

Rugal Bernstein (Omega Form) is a tall, muscular man with a menacing appearance. He has a distinctive hairstyle, often depicted as a messy, spiky haircut. His eyes are typically portrayed as cold and calculating. In his Omega form, he gains additional physical enhancements, including increased muscle mass and a more imposing presence.

Powers

Omega Form Rugal's Omega form grants him enhanced physical abilities, including increased strength, speed, and durability. He also gains access to new abilities, such as energy projection and teleportation.

Accounts

Rugal Bernstein is the main antagonist of The King of Fighters '94, a fighting game developed by SNK. He is a wealthy businessman with a hidden agenda, seeking to exploit the world's resources for his own gain.

Rugal Bernstein returns as the main antagonist of The King of Fighters '95. He has gained a new form, Omega Rugal, which grants him enhanced abilities and a more menacing appearance.

Who It Is

A legendary crime lord and collector of fighting talent, Rugal Bernstein achieved his Omega form after implanting the Orochi blood into his body and augmenting himself with cybernetics. His skin turns gray-white, his hair turns silver, and he wields both the power of Orochi and his own devastating martial arts. He carries the power of the Riot of the Blood.

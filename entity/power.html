Who It Is

In the blood-soaked world of Chainsaw Man, Power is a hurricane of chaos wrapped in a devil's flesh—a creature who declares herself the 'Magnificent Power,' yet whose true nature is a desperate, lonely search for belonging.

Power is brash, arrogant, and self-centered, often declaring herself 'the Magnificent Power.' She is impulsive, loud, and has a love for violence and chaos. Despite her bravado, she is deeply insecure and craves validation and companionship. She is shown to be socially inept, lacking basic understanding of human customs, and often acts like a wild animal. She forms a strong bond with Denji, whom she considers her first friend, and her loyalty to him is fierce, even if she expresses it in unconventional ways.

Powers
Blood Manipulation

Power can control blood, both her own and that of others, to create various weapons and constructs, such as hammers, axes, and shields. She can also manipulate blood to enhance her physical abilities, heal herself, and even create a blood-based form for combat.

Limit: Her powers are limited by the amount of blood available. She cannot create blood from nothing; she must have a sufficient supply. If she loses too much blood, she becomes weakened or unable to fight. She also seems to have a limit on the complexity of constructs she can create.

Enhanced Physical Abilities

As a Fiend, Power possesses superhuman strength, speed, and durability. She can easily overpower ordinary humans and even some devils.

Limit: Her physical abilities are not as strong as the top-tier devils like Makima or the Gun Devil, but she is formidable in her own right.

Regeneration

Power can regenerate from injuries by using blood, including reattaching limbs or healing wounds. This is a common ability among devils, but Power's blood control makes her especially adept at it.

Limit: Requires blood; if she is completely drained, she cannot regenerate.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Power is a young woman with fair skin, long blonde hair that is often messy, and sharp, fanged teeth. She has a pair of small, nub-like horns on her head, characteristic of devils. She typically wears a black and white school uniform, often with a bloody or disheveled look. Her eyes are usually shown as yellow or amber with slit pupils. When using her powers, her appearance becomes more demonic, with her face contorting and her body transforming

Symbols — Her signature symbol is the blood hammer she creates, and her weapon of choice is a blood-formed axe or hammer. Her uniform, with its red tie, is iconic. The blood motif is central to her design, representing her power source and her chaotic nature.

Domains — As a Fiend, Power resides in the human world, typically living with Denji and Aki in their apartment. She is associated with blood and violence, and her natural habitat is wherever conflict occurs. She has no specific realm, but her devil origin ties her to the concept of blood and fear.

Accounts I
Introduction and Partnership with DenjiChainsaw Man, Chapters 5-10

1990s-2000s (fictional contemporary setting)

Power is introduced as a devil who has possessed a human corpse, working for the Public Safety Devil Hunters. She is initially presented as a dangerous and uncontrollable force. Denji is tasked with partnering with her, and they are forced to work together to defeat a powerful devil. Their partnership is rocky, with Power constantly trying to dominate Denji, but they eventually develop a grudging respect. Establishes Power and Denji's dynamic as chaotic allies. Power begins to see Denji as a friend, though she would never admit it.

The Eternity Devil ArcChainsaw Man, Chapters 22-26

Fictional contemporary

Power, Denji, and other devil hunters are trapped in a hotel by the Eternity Devil, which creates an infinite loop. Power's blood manipulation is crucial in the fight, and she works with Denji to defeat the devil. This arc highlights her combat abilities and her growing willingness to cooperate. Shows Power's value as a fighter and her developing friendship with Denji.

Accounts II
The Katana Man ArcChainsaw Man, Chapters 37-52

Fictional contemporary

Power is captured by the yakuza and the Katana Man, and she is tortured to lure Denji into a trap. Denji rescues her, and they fight together against the Katana Man. This arc shows Power's vulnerability and her reliance on Denji, and it deepens their bond. Power and Denji's friendship is solidified. Power becomes more protective of Denji.

The Gun Devil ArcChainsaw Man, Chapters 66-79

Fictional contemporary

Power and Denji face the Gun Devil, a powerful devil that has been hunting them. They are outmatched, and Power is severely injured. This arc leads to Power's death, where she is killed by Makima, but not before she makes Denji promise to find her reincarnation and become her friend again. Power's death is a pivotal moment, driving Denji's motivation for revenge against Makima. It also establishes the cycle of devil reincarnation.

Who It Is

Power is the Blood Devil inhabiting a human corpse (a state known as a 'Fiend') and one of the central characters of Chainsaw Man Part 1. She is loud, boastful, crude, and utterly convinced of her own superiority — despite being repeatedly and hilariously proven wrong. Power is introduced as a member of the Public Safety Devil Hunter Division who is too wild and uncontrollable to work with anyone else, so she is assigned to be Denji's partner. The pairing is explosive: two immature, emotionally stunted beings who are forced to learn what it means to care about someone else. Power's ability is blood manipulation. She can create weapons from her own blood (blood axes, swords, hammers), control the blood inside her own body for regeneration and enhancement, and even manipulate the blood of others (though this is limited). Her signature attack is creating a massive blood hammer. When she drinks blood, her power and regeneration increase dramatically. Power is a Fiend, meaning a devil that took over a human corpse — and as such, she retains little of her original human identity. She has no memory of the human she once was and identifies entirely as the Blood Devil. Power's character arc is one of the most heart-wrenching in modern manga. Through her partnership with Denji and the mentorship of Aki, Power slowly learns to care about others — not as tools or rivals, but as friends. She develops a deep, sibling-like bond with Denji, and in her final moments, she makes a contract with him: 'If I die, Denji, you find the Blood Devil when it reappears in Hell. And you make her your friend again.' Her death is one of the most devastating moments in the series. Power represents the capacity for growth and connection in even the most selfish and monstrous beings. She is crude, violent, and narcissistic — but she is also capable of profound love and sacrifice. She is the proof that even devils can learn to care.

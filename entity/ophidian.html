Who It Is

Ophidian is the serpentine entity of avarice — the orange light of greed that coils through the Emotional Spectrum, whispering of endless wanting, hoarding, and possession. It appears as a colossal, ethereal serpent made of orange energy, perpetually coiling around itself as if trying to consume its own tail — a physical manifestation of greed's self-defeating nature: the more it acquires, the more it wants. Ophidian's power stems from the desire to possess, control, and accumulate. Its host gains the ability to 'steal' the identities and powers of others by consuming their emotional energy, create phantasmal constructs of beings they've consumed, permanently take anything they touch into an extradimensional space, and resist all forms of emotional manipulation through sheer obsession. Ophidian's only host has been Larfleeze, the sole Orange Lantern, also known as the Agent Orange. Larfleeze is a greedy, feral creature who has hoarded the entire Orange Light for himself for billions of years, jealously guarding his power against all comers. His lair is filled with the orange constructs of every being he has ever consumed — the Guardians of the Universe, fellow Lanterns, and entire civilizations — eternally serving his will. Larfleeze cannot be reasoned with because his connection to Ophidian has reduced his consciousness to a single impulse: WANT. The entity was briefly separated from Larfleeze during the Blackest Night event, bonding with Lex Luthor, whose already-vast greed made him an almost perfect host. Ophidian represents the ultimate emptiness of greed — a serpent that swallows the universe but is never full, a light that illuminates only the endless void of desire.

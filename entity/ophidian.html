Who It Is

In the DC Universe, the Ophidian is not merely a snake—it is the embodiment of avarice itself, a cosmic entity that has slithered through the hearts of mortals and heroes alike, tempting them with the promise of ultimate desire.

Ophidian embodies pure avarice, driven by an insatiable desire to possess and hoard. He is cunning, manipulative, and seductive, tempting others with promises of power and wealth. He is not inherently malevolent but is a force of nature, acting on his fundamental drive.

Powers
Avarice Empowerment

Ophidian grants his host the power of avarice, allowing them to tap into the yellow light of greed. This gives them abilities similar to a Yellow Lantern, including the creation of yellow energy constructs, flight, and enhanced strength.

Limit: The power is fueled by the host's greed; if the host's avarice wanes, so does the power. The host must also contend with Ophidian's influence, which can corrupt their mind.

Possession

Ophidian can possess individuals, merging his consciousness with theirs. When bonded, the host gains his powers but also his insatiable desire.

Limit: The host may be able to resist or control the possession with strong willpower, as seen with Kyle Rayner.

Immortality

As a cosmic entity, Ophidian is immortal and cannot be permanently destroyed. If his physical form is destroyed, he can reform over time.

Limit: He can be contained or imprisoned, as seen when the Guardians trapped him.

Energy Manipulation

Ophidian can manipulate yellow energy, which can be used for offensive and defensive purposes, including energy blasts and shields.

Limit: His powers are tied to the emotional spectrum and may be countered by other emotional entities or strong willpower.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Ophidian appears as a colossal, ethereal serpent with glowing yellow eyes. His body is composed of a mysterious energy, often depicted with a translucent or spectral quality. He can manifest in physical form or as a spiritual presence.

Symbols — Ophidian is associated with the color yellow, which represents avarice in the emotional spectrum. He is often depicted coiled around objects or individuals, symbolizing the constricting nature of greed.

Domains — Ophidian resides in the Avarice Lantern, a central power battery located in the Vega system. He can also manifest anywhere in the universe where avarice is strong.

Accounts I
Emerald Twilight and the Rise of the EntitiesGreen Lantern (vol. 4) #54-57

2010

During the 'Brightest Day' storyline, the emotional entities were revealed to be the manifestations of the emotional spectrum. Ophidian was discovered on the planet Okaara, where he had been dormant. The entity was sought by various factions, including the Sinestro Corps, who wanted to harness his power. Ophidian was eventually reclaimed by the Guardians and placed back in the Avarice Lantern, but his existence became known to the universe.

Accounts II
The New Guardians and the Avarice EntityGreen Lantern: New Guardians #0-20

2012-2013

Kyle Rayner, the White Lantern, encountered Ophidian when the entity attempted to bond with him. Kyle was able to resist Ophidian's influence and instead formed a partnership, using the entity's power to fight the other emotional entities. This storyline explored the nature of the entities and their connection to the emotional spectrum. Kyle Rayner gained the ability to channel the power of all the emotional entities, becoming a powerful force for balance.

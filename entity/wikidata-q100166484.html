Who It Is

The name Magdalena carries the weight of a biblical figure, the spark of comic-book heroism, and the rhythm of a river that shapes a nation. It is a name that has traveled from ancient shores to modern screens, each iteration a new story waiting to be told.

The comic character is portrayed as a skilled warrior, dedicated to her mission, often conflicted between her faith and her actions.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — From the Greek 'Magdalene', meaning 'of Magdala', a town on the Sea of Galilee. The name is associated with Mary Magdalene, a follower of Jesus.

Pronunciation — mag-duh-LAY-nuh (English), mag-da-LAY-na (Spanish, Italian)

Aliases — Mary Magdalene, Magdalena (given name), Magdalena (comic character)

Ownership — The name originates in the Middle East and has been adopted across Christian and secular cultures worldwide.

Names are cult layers, not one fixed label.

Form & Signs

Appearance — As a given name, no fixed appearance. The comic character is typically depicted as a young woman with dark hair, often in a white or red outfit, wielding a spear.

Symbols — In religious art, Mary Magdalene is often shown with long hair, a jar of ointment, or a skull. The comic character uses a spear as a symbol of her role.

Domains — The name spans religious, cultural, and fictional realms. The comic character operates in a modern fantasy setting.

Accounts I
Biblical narrative of Mary MagdaleneThe Bible (New Testament)

1st century CE

Mary Magdalene is a follower of Jesus, present at his crucifixion and resurrection, and is the first to witness the risen Christ. She becomes a central figure in Christian tradition, often called 'Apostle to the Apostles'.

Accounts II
Magdalena comic book seriesImage Comics / Top Cow

1998–present

Patience, a young woman, becomes the Magdalena, a warrior chosen to fight evil, wielding the Spear of Destiny. She battles demons and other supernatural threats. She becomes a key figure in the Top Cow universe, often crossing over with other characters.

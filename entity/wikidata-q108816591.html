Who It Is

He was born in a kingdom where even a hug can leave third-degree burns — a shy, soft-spoken prince made of living fire who would rather bake cupcakes than burn villages, and whose entire existence began as someone else's fanfiction.

Attested: Shy, earnest, eager to please, and deeply anxious about his own destructive potential — a direct inversion of his father Flame King's tyrannical cruelty and a parallel to Flame Princess's volatile insecurity. In the 2011 episode he is presented as a chivalrous, slightly awkward love interest who rescues Fionna but defers to her agency. Powers
Pyrokinesis and Flame Generation

Ability to generate, control, and project fire from his body. Can shoot fireballs, create flame walls, ignite objects by touch, and engulf his entire form in flame. In Gary era, he deliberately suppresses this to a low simmer for baking.

Limit: Emotionally linked — anger, embarrassment, or excitement causes involuntary flare, risking harm to nearby water-based or organic beings. Requires oxygen; cannot flame in vacuum (per Adventure Time elemental lore). Suppression causes internal pressure and later larger eruption.

Heat and Fire Immunity

Complete immunity to heat, lava, and his own flame. Can walk through lava rivers of Fire Kingdom and handle molten metal. Other fire elementals share this.

Limit: Not immune to emotional or magical cold; Ice-based attacks (e.g., Ice Queen) can extinguish or harm him. Water is primary vulnerability.

Elemental Physiology - Inorganic Fire Body

Body is living flame, not flesh; no blood, can reform after dispersal if flame not fully extinguished. Can extend limbs as flame tendrils, float slightly via thermal updraft.

Limit: Water contact causes intense pain, steam, and potential death if fully doused. In Fionna and Cake, Gary is shown weakened and dimmed after rain. Requires fuel/heat to sustain; prolonged cold dims him to near-extinction.

Baking and Thermal Precision (Gary-specific)

Learned skill to modulate temperature precisely for baking — can proof dough with gentle warmth, caramelize sugar with fingertip. Not a superpower per se but a controlled application of pyrokinesis.

Limit: Requires calm emotional state; when upset, he burns product. No combat application.

Royal Authority (Former)

As prince, could command Fire Kingdom guards and access royal treasury and lava forges. Gary has abdicated and no longer exercises this.

Limit: Authority revoked upon leaving; Flame King no longer recognizes his title. No magical component.

Accounts I
Fionna and Cake (Ice King's Fanfiction)Adventure Time Season 3 Episode 9 'Fionna and Cake'

2011-09-05

The Ice King, lonely and seeking to be understood, writes and reads aloud his gender-swapped fanfiction to Finn and Jake. In his story, Fionna the Human and Cake the Cat adventure in the land of Aaa. Flame Prince appears as the gallant prince of the Fire Kingdom who rescues Fionna from the Ice Queen. He is introduced as kind and handsome, immediately smitten with Fionna, and offers her a safe haven in his flame palace. The story is interrupted when Finn and Jake mock its self-insert nature. The episode frames Flame Prince as a fictional construct within fiction, revealing more about Simon Petrikov's psychology than about Ooo itself. Establishes Flame Prince as the counterpart to Flame Princess and introduces the entire Fionna and Cake alternate universe, which would later become a franchise pillar. Establishes his core traits (kind, princely, fire-elemental) and his narrative function as romantic interest. Also establishes the meta-layer that all early Flame Prince material is unreliable as in-universe history.

The Flame Prince and the Ice Queen's TrapAdventure Time with Fionna and Cake #2-3 (BOOM! Studios)

2013-02 to 2013-03

In Natasha Allegri's comics continuation, Flame Prince invites Fionna to a royal ball at the Fire Kingdom to formalize their courtship. The Ice Queen crashes the ball, freezing the lava moat and kidnapping Flame Prince to force Fionna into a rescue. Flame Prince, rather than waiting passively, uses his heat to melt his ice prison from within, but his flare accidentally scorches Fionna's arm, causing him to recoil in shame and flee. The arc focuses on his fear that his nature makes him inherently harmful to those he loves. Deepens Flame Prince beyond one-note love interest; introduces his self-loathing and fear of intimacy due to elemental incompatibility — mirroring Flame Princess's arc in the main series (who injured Finn). Sets up his later decision to leave royalty.

Gary's Abdication and the BakeryAdventure Time: Fionna and Cake Episode 7 'The Star'

2023-09-21

Adult Gary Prince is revealed to have abdicated the Fire Kingdom throne years earlier after a childhood of emotional abuse from Flame King, who demanded he be 'hotter, crueler, more princely.' Flashbacks show young Gary being berated for his gentle flame and for failing to incinerate prisoners. As an adult, he lives in a small apartment above a bakery he owns, where he bakes using his controlled heat. He has muted his flame to a dull pink-orange and removed his crown. When Fionna (now an adult in a non-magical world) stumbles into his bakery via Prismo's multiverse shenanigans, Gary recognizes her from stories but does not immediately reveal his princely past. He chooses to help her despite his father's warnings that involvement with other worlds will 'reignite' his destiny. Canonically redefines Flame Prince as Gary — a survivor of parental abuse who chooses a working-class, gentle life over inherited power. Accounts II
The Winter King ConfrontationAdventure Time: Fionna and Cake Episode 6 'The Winter King'

2023-09-14

Gary accompanies Fionna, Cake, and Simon Petrikov to the Winter Kingdom, where the Winter King (an alternate Simon who never lost Betty) holds court. The Winter King attempts to keep Simon trapped by offering him a crown that would restore his sanity at the cost of his memories. Gary's flame is nearly extinguished by the Winter Kingdom's cold, leaving him dim and shivering — a visual representation of his vulnerability. Marshall Lee rescues him by wrapping him in a thermal blanket and sharing body heat, deepening their bond. Gary later uses a desperate, full-body flare to melt the Winter King's ice scepter, allowing the group to escape, but collapses from exhaustion, demonstrating the cost of using his full power after years of suppression. Shows the physical cost of Gary's suppressed elemental nature and solidifies his relationship with Marshall Lee as mutual caretakers. Demonstrates that his powers remain potent but dangerous to himself when overused. Reinforces the series theme that chosen family can sustain elemental beings outside their native habitats.

Jerry and the Multiverse ChoiceAdventure Time: Fionna and Cake Episode 8 'Jerry' (Season Finale)

2023-09-28

In the finale, Prismo's multiverse is collapsing due to the Scarab's attempt to erase the unauthorized Fionna and Cake universe. Gary is offered a choice by Prismo: return to the Fire Kingdom and reclaim his throne, which would stabilize his existence (as royalty has stronger 'canon' weight), or remain as Gary the baker and risk being erased as a 'fanfiction error.' Gary chooses to remain Gary, declaring 'I'm not a prince, I'm just Gary, and Gary bakes.' His decision, combined with Fionna's and Cake's, helps Prismo argue that unauthorized stories deserve to exist. His flame, previously dim, burns a steady, warm pink — indicating self-acceptance without needing to be maximally hot. Resolves Gary's arc from inherited identity to self-chosen identity; thematically parallels Simon's arc of accepting life after the crown. Establishes that Gary's existence is now canonically protected within the multiverse, no longer just Ice King's fanfiction. Sets up potential Season 2 stories as Gary, not Flame Prince.

Bad Little Boy (Flame Princess Parallel)Adventure Time Season 5 Episode 11 'Bad Little Boy'

2013-02-18

Although Flame Prince does not appear directly, this episode is essential context: Marshall Lee (male Marceline) tells Fionna's story where Flame Princess appears as Flame Prince in his narration, swapping genders again. This meta-episode demonstrates how fluid the Flame elemental's gender presentation is in fanfiction layers, and is often cited by scholars as evidence that Flame Prince/Princess is a single elemental concept expressed differently per universe. The episode also parallels Flame Princess's abusive relationship with Flame King, which informs understanding of Gary's abuse. Provides scholarly evidence for the 'single elemental, multiple expressions' theory and contextualizes Gary's trauma as inherited from the same abusive parental figure as Flame Princess. Flame Prince is Flame Princess's brother who appears in the main Adventure Time timeline alongside Finn and Jake. — He has never appeared in the prime Ooo timeline. All his appearances are in the Fionna and Cake alternate universe (Aaa) — either as Ice King's fanfiction or as Gary in the 2023 series. The prime Fire Kingdom heir is exclusively Flame Princess (Phoebe).

Flame Prince is just a palette-swapped Flame Princess with no distinct personality. — While initially a gender-swap, his personality diverges significantly: Flame Princess is hot-tempered, impulsive, and struggles with anger management and distrust due to paternal abuse; Flame Prince/Gary is anxious, people-pleasing, avoidant, and seeks domesticity over conquest. Their trauma responses are opposite (externalized rage vs. internalized suppression).

Gary Prince is a completely new character unrelated to Flame Prince. — Gary is the evolved, adult identity of Flame Prince. Official credits, design lineage, and showrunner statements confirm continuity. The name change is in-universe character development, not a recasting.

Flame Prince is immune to water like other fire elementals are only slightly hurt by it. — Water is severely harmful — contact causes pain, steam, and can be fatal if fully submerged. In Fionna and Cake E06, rain and cold nearly extinguish Gary. This matches Flame Princess lore where water can kill her (S04E13 'Hot to the Touch').

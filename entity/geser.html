Who It Is

Geser is the divine warrior-hero of the great Central Asian epic — one of the world's longest. Sent from heaven, he defeats demons, monsters, and corrupt rulers across the known world. In Mongol tradition, he is a son of Tengri; in Tibet, an emanation of Padmasambhava. The epic continues to be performed by bards as a living ritual tradition.

Attested in Epic of King Gesar (compiled c. 12th century)Era c. 11th-12th century CE crystallization

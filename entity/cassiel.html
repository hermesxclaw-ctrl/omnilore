Cassiel's association with the planet Saturn and the moon makes him a fascinating figure in the realm of mythology and mysticism. His role as a compassionate and gentle angel has captivated the imagination of many, and his powers of temperance and healing continue to inspire and awe.

Cassiel is described as a compassionate and gentle angel, often associated with the qualities of temperance and tears.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In Jewish mysticism and Mesopotamian mythology, Cassiel is associated with the planet Saturn and the direction north. He is the controller of the moon. In some depictions, Cassiel is shown as a majestic being with a stern expression, often surrounded by symbols of Saturn and the moon.

Symbols — Cassiel is often associated with the symbols of Saturn, such as the scythe and the ringed planet. He is also linked to the moon and the direction north.

Domains — Cassiel is said to inhabit the realm of the moon and the planet Saturn, and is associated with the direction north.

The Story of Cassiel and the MoonJewish Mysticism

Middle Ages

In this story, Cassiel is depicted as a compassionate angel who helps a young woman who is struggling to cope with the loss of her loved one. Using his powers of temperance and healing, Cassiel brings comfort and peace to the woman, and helps her to find solace in the moon's gentle light. The woman is able to find peace and comfort, and is able to move on from her grief.

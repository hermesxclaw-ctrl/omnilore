Who It Is

Angel of Saturn, solitude, and tears. Cassiel (also Qafsiel, Qaspiel) is the angel of temperance and the passage of time. In ceremonial magic, he governs the planet Saturn and the day of Saturday. He is portrayed as a solemn, contemplative figure often depicted as a bearded king riding a dragon or holding a scythe. Cassiel presides over the serious, slow-moving forces of karma, discipline, and fate. In some Kabbalistic sources, he is linked to the Sephirah Binah (Understanding). Unlike the more celebratory angels, Cassiel embodies the somber wisdom that comes through endurance and suffering.

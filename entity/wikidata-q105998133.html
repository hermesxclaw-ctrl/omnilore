Who It Is

In the world of Uma Musume Pretty Derby, Mejiro McQueen is the elegant and proud heir of the Mejiro family, a lineage renowned for its powerful staying runners. Her regal demeanor and sharp tongue belie a deep-seated loneliness, as she struggles to live up to the weight of her family's expectations while forging her own path on the racetrack.

Mejiro McQueen is proud, confident, and deeply committed to her family's legacy. She speaks in a formal, somewhat old-fashioned manner, often using polite but cutting remarks. Beneath her cool exterior, she is caring and protective of her friends, especially Tokai Teio. She struggles with the pressure of being the Mejiro heir and fears failing her family's expectations.

Stamina and Long-Distance Running

Mejiro McQueen excels in long-distance races, possessing exceptional stamina and endurance. Her running style is powerful and steady, allowing her to maintain a fast pace over extended distances.

Limit: She may be less suited to short sprints or races requiring sudden bursts of speed.

Tactical Racing

She is a skilled tactician, able to read the race and position herself advantageously. She often conserves energy early and unleashes a powerful sprint in the final stretch.

Limit: Her strategy can be disrupted by unexpected changes in pace or aggressive rivals.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Mejiro McQueen has long, flowing silver hair and striking blue eyes. She wears the standard Tracen Academy uniform, but her accessories and posture convey an air of nobility. Her horse ears are white, and her tail matches her hair color. She often carries herself with a dignified, almost haughty elegance.

Symbols — Her symbol often includes the Mejiro family crest, which is associated with the real Mejiro horse lineage. In official artwork, she is frequently depicted with a rose or other elegant flower, symbolizing her refined personality.

Domains — She resides at Tracen Academy, the central school for horse girls. She is often seen in the dormitory, on the training grounds, or at racecourses. Her family home, the Mejiro estate, is mentioned but not shown in detail.

The Heir's BurdenUma Musume Pretty Derby Season 2, episodes 1-13

Modern (fictional)

Mejiro McQueen is introduced as the proud heir of the Mejiro family, expected to win the most prestigious long-distance races. She forms a rivalry with Tokai Teio, who admires her and challenges her to become stronger. Throughout the season, McQueen struggles with her own doubts and the pressure of her legacy, especially after a defeat. Her friendship with Teio helps her rediscover her love for racing and her own identity beyond family expectations. McQueen and Teio become close friends and rivals, pushing each other to new heights. McQueen learns to race for herself, not just for her family.

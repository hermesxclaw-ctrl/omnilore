The Approach

In the world of Uma Musume Pretty Derby, Mejiro McQueen is the elegant and proud heir of the Mejiro family, a lineage renowned for its powerful staying runners. Her regal demeanor and sharp tongue belie a deep-seated loneliness, as she struggles to live up to the weight of her family's expectations while forging her own path on the racetrack.

Content advisory. None
Identity

Mejiro McQueen

Pronunciation MEH-jee-roh muh-KWEEN

Etymology

The name 'Mejiro' is derived from the Mejiro family of racehorses, a prominent Japanese thoroughbred lineage. 'McQueen' is likely a reference to the American actor Steve McQueen, fitting the franchise's pattern of naming characters after famous figures. In-universe, she is the heir to the Mejiro family's racing legacy.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese media franchise, specifically the Uma Musume Pretty Derby multimedia project by Cygames. The character is part of a series that anthropomorphizes real racehorses as horse-eared girls.

Disputed Identifications

There is no dispute over Mejiro McQueen's identity as a character, but the character's real-world counterpart, the racehorse Mejiro McQueen, may have differing public perception. The franchise character is a fictionalized interpretation and not a direct historical account.

Description

Appearance

Mejiro McQueen has long, flowing silver hair and striking blue eyes. She wears the standard Tracen Academy uniform, but her accessories and posture convey an air of nobility. Her horse ears are white, and her tail matches her hair color. She often carries herself with a dignified, almost haughty elegance.

Symbols & Iconography

Her symbol often includes the Mejiro family crest, which is associated with the real Mejiro horse lineage. In official artwork, she is frequently depicted with a rose or other elegant flower, symbolizing her refined personality.

Behaviour & Personality

Mejiro McQueen is proud, confident, and deeply committed to her family's legacy. She speaks in a formal, somewhat old-fashioned manner, often using polite but cutting remarks. Beneath her cool exterior, she is caring and protective of her friends, especially Tokai Teio. She struggles with the pressure of being the Mejiro heir and fears failing her family's expectations.

Domains & Habitats

She resides at Tracen Academy, the central school for horse girls. She is often seen in the dormitory, on the training grounds, or at racecourses. Her family home, the Mejiro estate, is mentioned but not shown in detail.

Version Differences

In the anime, she is a central character in Season 2, with a detailed arc about her rivalry and friendship with Tokai Teio. In the game, her character stories expand on her relationship with her family and her personal growth. The manga adaptations may have slight variations in tone and detail, but the core characterization remains consistent.

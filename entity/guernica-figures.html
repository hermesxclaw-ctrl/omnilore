Who It Is

Cultural Significance

Perhaps the most powerful anti-war statement in art history. A tapestry copy hangs at the U.N. Security Council entrance (covered during Powell's 2003 Iraq speech). The painting became a site of pilgrimage for peace activists worldwide.

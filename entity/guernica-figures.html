Who It Is
Form

The Guernica Figures are a group of central figures in Pablo Picasso's painting 'Guernica', depicting a scene of chaos and destruction. The figures are shown in a state of distress, with some of them lying on the ground or being trampled by others. The figures are rendered in a monochromatic palette, with bold lines and geometric shapes.

Powers

Emotional Resonance The Guernica Figures have the ability to evoke strong emotions in the viewer, particularly feelings of sadness, anger, and outrage. The figures' depiction of chaos and destruction creates a sense of empathy and understanding in the viewer.

Accounts

The bombing of Guernica was a pivotal event in the Spanish Civil War, in which the town of Guernica was bombed by German and Italian warplanes. The event was a turning point in the war, leading to increased international condemnation of the fascist forces.

Pablo Picasso created the painting 'Guernica' in response to the bombing of Guernica. The painting is a powerful anti-war statement, depicting the chaos and destruction of war.

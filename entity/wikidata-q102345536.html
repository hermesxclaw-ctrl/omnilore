Who It Is

In the sprawling digital universe of The World, few figures are as enigmatic and feared as Ovan—a mysterious, one-armed player whose very presence seems to warp the fabric of the game. He is not merely a player killer; he is a catalyst, a manipulator, and a tragic figure whose actions ripple across the boundaries of reality and fiction, leaving both players and the system itself questioning what is real.

Ovan is calm, calculating, and charismatic. He speaks in a soft, measured tone and often seems to know more than he reveals. He is manipulative, using others as pawns in his grand plan, but he is also deeply tormented by his past. He shows genuine care for his guild members, especially Shino, but is willing to sacrifice everything to achieve his goal of resurrecting his sister.

Powers
Data Drain

Ovan possesses the ability to Data Drain, which can delete or corrupt player data. This is a rare and powerful ability, typically associated with AIs or special characters.

Limit: Data Drain can only be used on certain targets and may have side effects on the user.

Combat Skills

Ovan is a skilled fighter, using a large sword in combat despite his missing arm. He is fast and powerful, able to hold his own against top players.

Limit: His missing arm may affect his balance, but he compensates with skill.

Manipulation

Ovan is a master manipulator, able to influence events and people to achieve his goals. He orchestrates many events in the series, including Haseo's development.

Limit: His plans are complex and can fail if others act unpredictably.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Ovan's avatar is a tall, muscular man with long, flowing black hair and a missing left arm, replaced by a mechanical prosthetic. He wears a dark, tattered cloak and has a distinctive tattoo-like pattern on his face and body, characteristic of The World's Wave system. His eyes are often hidden in shadow, adding to his mysterious aura.

Symbols — His missing arm is a symbol of his sacrifice and resolve. The tattoo patterns are reminiscent of Celtic designs, similar to other characters in the series, but are darker in color, reflecting his ambiguous nature.

Domains — In The World, Ovan is often found in the guild area of the Twilight Brigade, or in remote, dangerous areas of the game. He is not tied to any specific location, but is known to traverse the game world freely.

Accounts I
The Twilight Brigade and the Fall.hack//Roots

2006

Ovan is the leader of the Twilight Brigade, a guild in The World. He recruits the rookie Haseo and forms a bond with him. However, Ovan is secretly planning to use Haseo to find the legendary 'Key of the Twilight' and resurrect his sister, who is in a coma. When the guild is attacked and Shino, a member, is put into a coma by Tri-Edge, Ovan disappears, leaving Haseo to seek revenge. Haseo becomes a Player Killer hunter and eventually confronts Ovan, leading to a climactic battle.

Accounts II
The Resurrection of Aina.hack//G.U. Vol. 3: Redemption

2007

It is revealed that Ovan's sister, Aina, is in a coma in the real world, and he believes that by using the power of The World, he can bring her back. He orchestrates a plan to become a 'Lost One' and merge with the game's system, but Haseo stops him. Ovan eventually finds peace and accepts his sister's fate. Ovan is defeated but survives, and he reconciles with Haseo. The World is saved from a crisis.

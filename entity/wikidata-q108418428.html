Who It Is

She arrived at Nijigasaki High School already famous — a solo idol since childhood who had filled concert halls in Hong Kong before she ever set foot in a Japanese classroom. When Lanzhu Zhong announced she would surpass every school idol in Tokyo, it sounded like arrogance. For Lanzhu, it was loneliness speaking: she had never learned how to sing with anyone else.

Attested personality across versions: Highly talented performer with professional experience since early childhood; initially presented as arrogant, blunt, and competitive. Declares her intention to 'surpass' or 'take over' the Nijigasaki School Idol Club after discovering them via streaming, believing her solo strength superior. Underlying traits revealed through bond stories and anime: profound loneliness and social inexperience - she was raised as a solo idol with adult management, had few peers or friends, and equates being loved with being the best. Japanese reading is on'yomi Shō Ranju. The given name evokes imagery of 'storm pearl,' consistent with Love Live! Nijigasak

Powers
Professional Solo Idol Performance - Vocal

Exceptionally strong singing ability trained since early childhood. Repertoire includes high-difficulty solo songs 'Eutopia' and 'Queendom' featuring sustained high notes, rapid phrasing, and wide vocal range. In-universe described as 'already professional level' compared to beginner school idols.

Limit: Vocal strength initially lacks emotional connection with audience when performing alone; character arc shows she must learn to sing 'with' others, not just 'for' herself. No supernatural enhancement - purely human training.

Dance and Stage Production

Advanced dance technique and stage presence, capable of carrying entire concert alone with complex choreography, use of stage lifts and lighting cues. Experience with large venues in Hong Kong.

Limit: Initially struggles with synchronized group choreography and spacing; must learn to adjust timing to others. Physical stamina is human-normal; fatigue shown after extended practice.

Piano and Musicianship

Plays piano (shown in bond episode and anime flashback); has basic songwriting/composition awareness from professional training. Not a primary plot power but attested skill.

Limit: Not at composer level like Mia Taylor; does not write full songs for group.

Charisma and Public Appeal (In-Universe)

Pre-existing fanbase from Hong Kong activities; recognized on street; social media following. Ability to draw attention and funding (attempts to fund new club facilities).

Limit: Fame is regional and age-limited; at Nijigasaki she is unknown to general student body initially. Fame does not translate to friendship - core narrative tension.

Multilingual Communication

Native Mandarin/Cantonese (Hong Kong) and fluent Japanese (attested by studying in Japan). Occasionally uses Chinese phrases. In real-world portrayal, voice actress performs primarily in Japanese.

Limit: Japanese is fluent but occasionally overly formal/direct, reflecting non-native nuance. No claim of English fluency in canon.

Accounts I
The Child Prodigy of Hong KongLove Live! School Idol Festival ALL STARS - Lanzhu Bond Episodes and Chapter 17 flashbacks

2019-2020 (game story timeline, in-universe childhood ~2008-2019)

Lanzhu Zhong is introduced as a girl who debuted as a solo school idol at a very young age in Hong Kong, managed as a professional child star. She performed in large venues with full production support but had no peers - adults managed her schedule, and other children saw her as distant. She describes a childhood of rehearsals, stages, and hotel rooms, with few memories of ordinary school life. This isolation instilled both exceptional skill and a belief that being an idol means being the best and being alone at the top. When she discovers videos of Nijigasaki High School Idol Club - a group of friends making DIY idols together - she is both fascinated and dismissive, deciding to transfer to Nijigasaki to prove solo strength surpasses group friendship. Establishes core wound and motivation for entire arc; explains arrogant exterior as defense. Sets up transfer to Tokyo as inciting incident.

Transfer to Nijigasaki and Declaration of RivalryLove Live! School Idol Festival ALL STARS Main Story Chapter 17 'Towards the Stage of Dreams'

2020 (game release)

Lanzhu transfers into Nijigasaki High School's 1st year and immediately confronts the School Idol Club. Having watched their streams, she announces she will create a superior, more professional idol club and invites members to join her, offering funding and production resources. She secures permission to use school facilities for solo practice and stages a high-budget solo live to demonstrate her superiority. The existing club (Ayumu, Kasumi, Shizuku, etc., plus protagonist Yu/Anata) is shaken - some admire her skill, others feel rejected. Shioriko Mifune, another transfer with a reformist agenda, appears in parallel, creating a period where the club fractures. Creates central conflict of ALL STARS Season 2; club temporarily splits; themes of what 'school idol' means (professional vs friends together) are foregrounded.

The Rooftop Solo and the Failed ConnectionLove Live! Nijigasaki High School Idol Club Season 2 Episode 6 'Lanzhu's Dream' and ALL STARS Chapter 17 live scene

2022 (anime broadcast); 2020 (game)

Lanzhu performs her signature solo song ('Eutopia' in game, 'Eutopia' / 'Queendom' in anime context) on the school rooftop stage with professional lighting she arranged. The performance is technically flawless and impresses the student body, but she notices the audience's reaction is polite rather than warm, and the club members, while acknowledging her talent, do not feel moved. Later, Yu and Ayumu attempt to talk with her, but Lanzhu insists she does not need friends to be an idol, misinterpreting their concern as pity. A subsequent joint practice where she tries to force others to match her exact timing fails, revealing she cannot yet listen or adapt to others. This failure leaves her visibly shaken and alone after the stage is cleared. First crack in her armor; shows skill alone does not create the connection she secretly craves. Leads to introspection and Shioriko's parallel realization.

Accounts II
Understanding Friendship - The Joint Lesson with Shioriko and MiaLove Live! School Idol Festival ALL STARS Chapter 18; Anime S2 Episode 7 'The Memories of Lanzhu and Shioriko'

2020-2022

Mia Taylor, a 14-year-old New York-born composer who also transferred, befriends Lanzhu because both are outsiders with professional backgrounds. Mia, however, has already begun collaborating with the club. Through Mia and a series of encounters - including being invited (and initially refusing) to a casual club tea party, helping Shioriko with student council work, and watching the club support a struggling member's rehearsal - Lanzhu begins to see that Nijigasaki's strength is not production value but mutual support. A key scene has Lanzhu confessing to Mia that she has never had a friend her own age and does not know how to ask to join. Shioriko, who underwent a similar reformist-to-collaborator arc, empathizes and bridges the gap. Lanzhu finally asks, awkwardly, if she can practice with them. Pivotal reconciliation; Lanzhu's rivalry dissolves into desire for belonging. Sets up her formal joining of the club. Establishes the 'new trio' (Lanzhu, Shioriko, Mia) as integrated members expanding Nijigasaki from 9 to 12.

First Group Performance - Becoming One of TwelveLove Live! School Idol Festival ALL STARS Chapter 18 finale; Anime S2 Episode 8 'The Place Where Everyone Can Shine' and Episode 13 'The Future We Choose'

2020 (game), 2022 (anime)

Lanzhu officially joins the Nijigasaki High School Idol Club and participates in her first true group song and dance. In the game, this is the 12-member version of 'TOKIMEKI Runners' and later 'Just Believe!!!' with Lanzhu integrated into formation. In the anime, Episode 8 features a club-wide performance where Lanzhu, initially nervous about synchronization, finds joy in being part of the line rather than the sole center, smiling genuinely for the first time on stage. The School Idol Festival (Love Live! competition) arc in Episodes 11-13 culminates in all 13 members (including Yu as support) performing together, with Lanzhu sharing center moments but also supporting others. Her solo song 'Queendom' is later performed as a solo within a group concert, reframed as a gift to friends rather than a display of superiority. Completes character arc from isolated soloist to collaborative school idol; establishes her as permanent main cast member for subsequent Nijigasaki media (2023-2024). In franchise terms, expands Nijigasaki's canonical member count.

After Joining - The Nijigasaki Daily Life and 5th Live EraLove Live! Nijigasaki High School Idol Club Drama CDs, Love Live! School Idol Festival 2 Miracle Live stories, and real-life 5th Live concert (2022-2023)

2022-2024

Post-integration stories depict Lanzhu navigating ordinary high school life - cultural festival preparation, cooking with Kanata, shopping with Ai, and learning to make jokes with Rina. Bond episodes show her struggling with and eventually enjoying casual activities like karaoke where perfection is not required. In the franchise's real-world live dimension, voice actress Akina Homoto debuted as Lanzhu at Nijigasaki 5th Live 'Twinkle Meets You' (2022) performing 'Eutopia' and group songs, marking Lanzhu's transition from game-only to fully staged character. Subsequent Nijigasaki OVA (2023) and spin-off manga show Lanzhu as established member mentoring newer fans. Normalizes Lanzhu as core cast; demonstrates franchise strategy of integrating game-original characters into anime and live concert continuity. Provides ongoing slice-of-life content beyond initial arc.

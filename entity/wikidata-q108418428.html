Who It Is

She arrived in Tokyo with a suitcase full of couture, a private stage, and a declaration of war: Zhong Lanzhu did not come to Nijigasaki High School to make friends — she came to prove she was already the best school idol in the world. For months she was the glittering antagonist who sang alone behind glass, until the very perfection that isolated her became the reason she had to learn how to reach out her hand.

Attested personality across game and anime is consistent with a classic 'ojou-sama / gifted rival' archetype given psychological depth. Initially presented as supremely confident, blunt, and competitive — openly declaring other school idols are not at her level and preferring to perform alone because she believes no one can keep up. She is hardworking to the point of perfectionism, practicing for hours in isolation, and struggles to understand collaborative idol activities. Powers
Elite Idol Vocals

Classically trained singing voice with wide range, strong belting, and precise pitch. Solo songs 'Queendom' and 'Eutopia' demonstrate sustained high notes, rapid melisma, and theatrical delivery. In-universe, she is described as having professional-level vocals since childhood.

Limit: Requires extensive rehearsal; perfectionism leads to vocal strain and anxiety if imperfect. Not supernatural — human training.

Advanced Dance and Stage Performance

Ballet and contemporary dance foundation; capable of complex choreography, center-position formations, and solo stage command. Noted for graceful, regal movements and strong stage presence that captivates audiences without backup dancers.

Limit: Initially struggles with synchronized group choreography and formations that require trust and timing with others; must learn to adjust to group rather than solo spotlight.

Piano and Musicianship

Accomplished pianist from early childhood lessons; can compose and accompany herself. Shown playing grand piano in apartment and in 'Eutopia' interlude.

Limit: Not a competitive concert pianist level in narrative; used as personal practice and composition aid.

Multilingual Communication

Native Cantonese/Mandarin (Hong Kong background), fluent Japanese (with occasional formal or slightly unnatural phrasing), and conversational English. Uses code-switching for greetings and affectionate phrases.

Limit: Japanese honorifics and casual slang sometimes awkward; cultural misunderstandings played for comedy and drama.

Wealth, Resources and Self-Production

Access to family wealth allows private stage, custom costumes, professional lessons, and independent promotion. She can self-produce music videos and rent venues without school funding. In narrative terms this is her 'power' that enables her initial solo career.

Limit: Wealth isolates her socially and creates pressure to prove worth beyond money; cannot buy genuine friendship or club trust — must earn it.

Emotional Resilience and Growth (Narrative Ability)

After arc, learns to be vulnerable, ask for help, and perform collaboratively. Her later performances are described as warmer and more connecting, indicating increased ability to inspire others rather than simply impress.

Limit: Growth is gradual and she remains prone to insecurity and over-effort; relapse into perfectionism under stress.

Accounts I
The Transfer Queen Arrives - ALL STARS Main Story Chapter 20: 'Shining Rival'Love Live! School Idol Festival ALL STARS

2020 (Heisei/Reiwa contemporary)

Zhong Lanzhu transfers to Nijigasaki High School from Hong Kong, already a famous solo school idol with a massive online following. She announces that she will show Nijigasaki what a true top idol is, performing a flawless solo show in her luxury apartment that outshines the club's usual street performances. She dismisses the School Idol Club as unprofessional and refuses to join, instead recruiting fans directly and challenging the club's philosophy that school idols shine together. The club is shaken by her overwhelming talent and confidence. Establishes Lanzhu as rival/antagonist and forces Nijigasaki to confront what makes their group special beyond technical skill. Sets up her isolation arc.

The Glass Stage - ALL STARS Chapter 21-22: 'The Lonely Queendom'Love Live! School Idol Festival ALL STARS

2020

Despite popularity, Lanzhu is shown practicing alone for hours behind glass walls, watching the club laugh together from afar. Flashbacks reveal childhood in Hong Kong where she was always told to be perfect and to stand above others due to family expectations. She equates being loved with being perfect and therefore cannot understand the club's imperfect, supportive dynamic. She pushes away Mia Taylor's attempts at friendship and overworks herself to maintain her queendom image. Humanizes Lanzhu; reveals her perfectionism as trauma response rather than arrogance. Explains why wealth and talent have not made her happy.

Eutopia / Queendom - First Solo PerformancesLantis singles + ALL STARS / Anime performance

2021 (song release) / 2020-2022 (narrative)

Lanzhu's signature solo songs are presented as in-universe performances that define her identity. 'Queendom' declares 'I am the queen, this is my kingdom' with regal, almost confrontational lyrics. Later, 'Eutopia' reveals longing beneath pride — lyrics about wanting a place where she can be herself. In story, these performances initially wow audiences but leave her feeling empty because applause is for perfection, not for her true self. Musical characterization that charts her emotional journey from dominance to vulnerability; songs become diegetic plot points.

Accounts II
Joining the Club - ALL STARS Chapter 23 / Anime Season 2 Episode 3-4: 'I Want to Shine With Everyone'Love Live! School Idol Festival ALL STARS Ch.23; Anime S2 Ep.3-4

2020-2022

After a joint performance crisis where Lanzhu's perfectionism causes her to falter and the Nijigasaki members support her without judgment, Lanzhu breaks down and admits she does not know how to make friends or be part of a group. Yu and Ayumu invite her to experience a true club practice — imperfect, noisy, fun. She hesitantly joins the School Idol Club, learning group choreography and sharing sweets after practice. Her first group song with the club marks her transition from solo queen to member of an ensemble. Officially becomes 12th/13th member of Nijigasaki School Idol Club (depending on counting Yu). Shifts franchise from 9 to 12-member group. Her color and emblem added to group visuals.

Stars We Chase and the School Idol Festival - Anime Season 2 Episodes 11-13 / ALL STARS Final ChapterLove Live! Nijigasaki High School Idol Club Season 2 + ALL STARS

2022-2023

As Nijigasaki prepares for the School Idol Festival / Love Live! event, Lanzhu must learn to perform not as a queen above everyone but as one light among many. In Episode 11 she confronts her mother via video call, expressing that she wants to stay in Japan and shine with friends rather than return to Hong Kong for an elite career path. The finale group song 'Stars We Chase' features Lanzhu integrated into 12-person formation, sharing center moments rather than monopolizing them. She cries on stage not from perfection but from joy of connection. Completes character arc; establishes her as permanent member for subsequent lives, albums, and game events. Sets up post-anime continuity where she remains in Tokyo.

Bond Episodes and Side Stories - Daily Life at NijigasakiLove Live! School Idol Festival ALL STARS Bond Episodes; Nijigasaki manga anthology

2020-2023

Smaller vignettes flesh out Lanzhu's daily life: learning to make Japanese sweets with Kasumi, being taught slang by Ai Miyashita, clumsily trying to be helpful and overdoing it (buying too many gifts), and bonding with Mia over being outsiders who found belonging. One bond episode has her teaching the club Cantonese phrases; another has her vulnerable about missing Hong Kong food and family. These stories cement her shift from aloof to earnest, slightly spoiled but deeply caring friend. Provides slice-of-life depth and comedic relief; expands her relationships beyond main plot.

Lanzhu Zhong is based on a real historical figure or folklore deity — She is a wholly original fictional character created in 2020 for Love Live! Nijigasaki. No historical or mythological source. The name uses real Chinese characters but was invented by franchise staff.

Lanzhu is mainland Chinese — Official profile specifies Hong Kong origin. She is described as from Hong Kong, with Cantonese background, not mainland China. This distinction matters for language (Cantonese vs Mandarin) and cultural representation.

Lanzhu has supernatural powers or magic — She is a normal human school idol in a realistic slice-of-life setting. Her 'powers' are human talents: singing, dancing, piano, languages. No magic, transformations, or superhuman abilities.

Lanzhu and Mia are sisters or a couple (canon romantic partners) — They are canonically best friends and musical partners, both outsiders who found belonging together. No official romantic relationship is stated; they are described as close friends/roommates. Fan shipping is common but not canon.

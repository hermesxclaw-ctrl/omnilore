Who It Is

A highly contagious disease transforming human flesh into animate, cancer-like growth. Infected tissue sprouts limbs, eyes, mouths, and organs in random configurations. Entire villages in Russia have been consumed. The disease spreads through skin contact and transforms whole populations into masses of interconnected flesh.

Form

Victims appear partially or entirely replaced by writhing red-pink flesh growths with random anatomical features — eyes blinking from shoulders, mouths gasping from thighs. Advanced stages fuse multiple individuals into single flesh masses.

Powers

Extremely contagious transformation

Flesh reanimation

Hive integration

Rapid growth from infected tissue

Who It Is
Form

SCP-610 is a sentient, flesh-like entity that can grow and change its appearance at will. It can extend its fleshy substance across areas, creating a network of tendrils and appendages. The entity's appearance is constantly shifting, making it difficult to describe or depict.

Powers

Contagion SCP-610 can spread its infection through physical contact with others, causing them to become infected and eventually transform into more of the entity's kind.

Incident Log

SCP-610 is a sentient, flesh-like entity that can grow and change its appearance at will. It is highly contagious and can spread through physical contact with the infected.

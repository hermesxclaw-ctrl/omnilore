The Approach

Hisashi Mitsui was the junior high MVP who could have gone anywhere, but chose Shohoku for Coach Anzai. Then one bad landing on his left knee sent him spiraling into a year of bitterness, delinquency, and a gang war that nearly destroyed the very team he'd once dreamed of leading. His return to the court is one of Slam Dunk's most powerful redemption arcs—a story of pride, injury, and the long road back.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of violence (gang fights), self-destructive behavior, and emotional trauma related to sports injury. No sexual content.

Etymology

The surname Mitsui (三井) literally means 'three wells' in Japanese. The given name Hisashi (寿) means 'longevity' or 'congratulations'.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese manga and anime culture, created by Takehiko Inoue.

Description

Appearance

Mitsui has short, dark brown hair and a lean but athletic build. He is often seen with a serious or intense expression. During his delinquent phase, he had longer hair and a more unkempt appearance, but after his return to basketball, he keeps a clean-cut look.

Symbols & Iconography

His jersey number is 14. He is often associated with the three-point shot, his signature skill.

Behaviour & Personality

Initially, Mitsui is arrogant and bitter, lashing out at the team he once loved. After his redemption, he becomes a determined and reliable teammate, showing deep respect for Coach Anzai and a strong desire to atone for his past. He is passionate and can be emotional, often crying when he remembers his love for basketball.

Domains & Habitats

Primarily appears in Shohoku High School, basketball courts, and various locations in Kanagawa Prefecture.

Version Differences

The anime and manga are largely consistent. The film The First Slam Dunk focuses on the Sannoh match and features Mitsui, but his backstory is not explored in depth.

Stories & Myths

Mitsui's Backstory and Redemption

Source Slam Dunk manga and animeCulture Japanese sports fictionPeriod 1990s

Summary

In junior high, Mitsui was the MVP of the Takeishi Junior High team, leading them to victory with encouragement from Coach Anzai. He chose Shohoku to play under Anzai, but a knee injury in his first year derailed his career. An early return worsened the injury, forcing him to sit out for over a year. Embittered, he quit basketball and became a delinquent gang leader. He later attempts to destroy the Shohoku team by instigating a fight, but after a confrontation with his former teammates and Coach Anzai, he breaks down and reveals his love for basketball. He returns to the team as a senior, becoming a key player in their run to the national championship.

Mitsui's return strengthens Shohoku, providing them with a reliable three-point shooter and veteran experience. His redemption arc is a central theme of the series.

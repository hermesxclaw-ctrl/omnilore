Who It Is

Hisashi Mitsui was the junior high MVP who could have gone anywhere, but chose Shohoku for Coach Anzai. Then one bad landing on his left knee sent him spiraling into a year of bitterness, delinquency, and a gang war that nearly destroyed the very team he'd once dreamed of leading. His return to the court is one of Slam Dunk's most powerful redemption arcs—a story of pride, injury, and the long road back.

Initially, Mitsui is arrogant and bitter, lashing out at the team he once loved. After his redemption, he becomes a determined and reliable teammate, showing deep respect for Coach Anzai and a strong desire to atone for his past. He is passionate and can be emotional, often crying when he remembers his love for basketball.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname Mitsui (三井) literally means 'three wells' in Japanese. The given name Hisashi (寿) means 'longevity' or 'congratulations'.

Pronunciation — MEE-tsoo-ee (Japanese: Mitsui Hisashi)

Aliases — Mitsui, Mitsui Hisashi

Ownership — Japanese manga and anime culture, created by Takehiko Inoue.

Powers
Three-point shooting

Mitsui is an elite three-point shooter, capable of scoring from long range with high accuracy, especially in clutch situations. His shooting ability is a major offensive weapon for Shohoku.

Limit: His shooting can be affected by fatigue, but he often performs well under pressure.

Basketball fundamentals

As a former MVP, Mitsui possesses solid all-around basketball skills, including dribbling, passing, and court vision. He is a smart player who understands the game well.

Limit: His time away from the game initially affects his stamina and conditioning, but he works to regain his form.

Physical endurance

Despite his knee injury, Mitsui shows remarkable endurance, often playing full games without substitution, even in high-stakes matches.

Limit: His knee remains a vulnerable point, and he must manage it carefully.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Mitsui has short, dark brown hair and a lean but athletic build. He is often seen with a serious or intense expression. During his delinquent phase, he had longer hair and a more unkempt appearance, but after his return to basketball, he keeps a clean-cut look.

Symbols — His jersey number is 14. He is often associated with the three-point shot, his signature skill.

Domains — Primarily appears in Shohoku High School, basketball courts, and various locations in Kanagawa Prefecture.

Accounts I
Mitsui's Backstory and RedemptionSlam Dunk manga and anime

1990s

In junior high, Mitsui was the MVP of the Takeishi Junior High team, leading them to victory with encouragement from Coach Anzai. He chose Shohoku to play under Anzai, but a knee injury in his first year derailed his career. An early return worsened the injury, forcing him to sit out for over a year. Embittered, he quit basketball and became a delinquent gang leader. He later attempts to destroy the Shohoku team by instigating a fight, but after a confrontation with his former teammates and Coach Anzai, he breaks down and reveals his love for basketball. He returns to the team as a senior, becoming a key player in their run to the national championship. Mitsui's return strengthens Shohoku, providing them with a reliable three-point shooter and veteran experience. His redemption arc is a central theme of the series.

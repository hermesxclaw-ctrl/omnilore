The Approach

In the bustling harbor city of Liyue, where commerce and tradition intertwine, a small zombie girl named Qiqi wanders the streets with a blank expression and a determination to remember. She is not a hero of legend or a fearsome warrior, but a gentle soul trapped in an undead body, seeking to hold onto the fragments of her past. Her story is one of tragedy, resilience, and the quiet power of memory.

Content advisory. The character's backstory involves death, undeath, and themes of loss and memory. Some descriptions may be mildly unsettling for younger audiences.

Etymology

The name 'Qiqi' (七七) literally means 'seven seven' in Chinese, possibly referencing the seventh day of the seventh lunar month, a day associated with the festival of the dead in Chinese culture. It may also simply be a cute, reduplicated name.

Cultural Ownership

Qiqi is a character created by miHoYo (now HoYoverse), a Chinese video game company. She is part of the game Genshin Impact, which draws on various cultural influences, including Chinese mythology and folklore.

Disputed Identifications

There is no significant dispute over Qiqi's identity. However, some fans have speculated about her connection to real-world folklore about zombies (jiangshi) due to her appearance and abilities.

Description

Appearance

Qiqi is a small girl with pale, almost blue-white skin, and dark purple hair in twin tails. She wears a large, wide-brimmed straw hat, a blue and white qipao-style dress, and carries a small sword. Her eyes are a striking crimson, and she often has a blank or expressionless face, reflecting her undead nature.

Symbols & Iconography

Qiqi is often associated with symbols of death and the supernatural, such as the talisman on her forehead, which is used to control her. She is also associated with the Cryo element, representing ice and preservation.

Behaviour & Personality

Qiqi is quiet, forgetful, and literal-minded. She speaks in a monotone and often forgets things within moments, as she is undead and her memory is failing. She is determined to remember important things, so she writes them down. She is also diligent and hardworking, especially when it comes to gathering herbs, which she does to make medicine for herself and others.

Domains & Habitats

Qiqi lives in Liyue, a region inspired by China. She often roams the mountains and forests around Liyue to collect herbs. She is also known to reside in the Bubu Pharmacy, where she works under the pharmacist Baizhu.

Version Differences

There are no significant alternate versions of Qiqi in the game. However, her appearance and abilities may be interpreted differently in fan works.

Zombie Physiology

Source Version Genshin Impact

Description

As a zombie, Qiqi does not need to eat, sleep, or breathe. She also has a slow decay that is halted by her Cryo powers and the talisman on her forehead.

Limitations

Her memory is severely impaired, and she must write things down to remember them. She is also not very agile or strong compared to living humans.

The Tragedy of Qiqi

Source Genshin Impact (Character Story)Culture Video game lorePeriod Pre-game and in-game

Summary

Qiqi was a young herbalist who lived in Liyue. She died during an accident in the mountains and was revived as a zombie by the power of the Adepti, specifically through a combination of her own will and the power of the land. She was later found by the pharmacist Baizhu, who took her in and gave her a purpose.

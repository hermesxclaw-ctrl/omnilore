Who It Is

In the bustling harbor city of Liyue, where commerce and tradition intertwine, a small zombie girl named Qiqi wanders the streets with a blank expression and a determination to remember. She is not a hero of legend or a fearsome warrior, but a gentle soul trapped in an undead body, seeking to hold onto the fragments of her past. Her story is one of tragedy, resilience, and the quiet power of memory.

Qiqi is quiet, forgetful, and literal-minded. She speaks in a monotone and often forgets things within moments, as she is undead and her memory is failing. She is determined to remember important things, so she writes them down. She is also diligent and hardworking, especially when it comes to gathering herbs, which she does to make medicine for herself and others.

Powers
Cryo Vision

Qiqi wields a Cryo Vision, granting her ice-based abilities. She can summon a magical ice talisman that heals allies and deals Cryo damage to enemies.

Limit: Her Cryo abilities are not as powerful as some other characters, and her healing is her primary utility.

Zombie Physiology

As a zombie, Qiqi does not need to eat, sleep, or breathe. She also has a slow decay that is halted by her Cryo powers and the talisman on her forehead.

Limit: Her memory is severely impaired, and she must write things down to remember them. She is also not very agile or strong compared to living humans.

Herb Gathering

Qiqi has extensive knowledge of herbs and medicinal plants, and she can gather them efficiently.

Limit: Her knowledge is limited to herbs; she is not a skilled alchemist or doctor.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Qiqi is a small girl with pale, almost blue-white skin, and dark purple hair in twin tails. She wears a large, wide-brimmed straw hat, a blue and white qipao-style dress, and carries a small sword. Her eyes are a striking crimson, and she often has a blank or expressionless face, reflecting her undead nature.

Symbols — Qiqi is often associated with symbols of death and the supernatural, such as the talisman on her forehead, which is used to control her. She is also associated with the Cryo element, representing ice and preservation.

Domains — Qiqi lives in Liyue, a region inspired by China. She often roams the mountains and forests around Liyue to collect herbs. She is also known to reside in the Bubu Pharmacy, where she works under the pharmacist Baizhu.

Accounts I
The Tragedy of QiqiGenshin Impact (Character Story)

Pre-game and in-game

Qiqi was a young herbalist who lived in Liyue. She died during an accident in the mountains and was revived as a zombie by the power of the Adepti, specifically through a combination of her own will and the power of the land. She was later found by the pharmacist Baizhu, who took her in and gave her a purpose. Qiqi became an undead being with a failing memory, but she gained a new life in the Bubu Pharmacy, where she helps Baizhu and gathers herbs.

Qiqi's Daily LifeGenshin Impact (Voice Lines, Events)

In-game

Qiqi's daily routine involves waking up, forgetting her tasks, writing notes, and going to gather herbs. She often encounters the Traveler and other characters, and she helps them with tasks, often in a literal and straightforward manner. Her interactions often highlight her memory issues and her innocent, helpful nature.

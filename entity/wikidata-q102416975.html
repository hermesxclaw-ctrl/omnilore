Who It Is

In the grim, candlelit world of Re:Zero, few figures embody the terror of fanatic devotion as vividly as Petelgeuse Romanée-Conti—a man whose name evokes a legendary wine, but whose deeds are steeped in madness. He is the Witch Cult's most zealous Archbishop of Sin, a man who has torn his own body apart to prove his love, and whose very existence challenges the boundaries of sanity and loyalty.

Petelgeuse is a fanatical devotee of the Witch of Envy (Satella). He believes that 'love' is the ultimate virtue and that his own acts of self-mutilation are demonstrations of his love. He is highly erratic, prone to fits of laughter and rage. He is also extremely possessive and jealous, viewing any perceived insult to the Witch as a grave sin. Despite his madness, he is cunning and methodical, using his authority as an Archbishop to command the Witch Cult's followers. Powers
Unseen Hands

Petelgeuse can manifest invisible, intangible hands that can grab, crush, and manipulate objects from a distance. These hands are invisible to normal sight but can be sensed by those with magical abilities. He can also use them to possess others by inserting his 'fingers' into their bodies, controlling them like puppets.

Limit: The hands are not truly invisible to those with strong spiritual senses; they can be countered by magical barriers or by severing the connection. Possession requires physical contact and can be resisted by strong willpower.

Authority of Sloth

As the Archbishop of Sloth, Petelgeuse wields the Witch Factor of Sloth, which grants him the Unseen Hands and also enhances his physical resilience. The Authority allows him to ignore pain and continue fighting despite severe injuries.

Limit: The Authority is tied to his mental state; when he is emotionally unstable, his control over it wavers. Using it excessively also accelerates his physical decay.

Possession

By using his Unseen Hands, Petelgeuse can insert his fingers into a person's body and take control of their movements. He can also transfer his consciousness into a new body if his original is destroyed.

Limit: Possession requires the victim to be alive and physically intact. The process is painful and can be resisted by those with strong willpower or magical protection. If the possessed body is killed, he can attempt to jump to another, but this is risky.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Petelgeuse is a tall, gaunt man with pale skin and long, unkempt white hair. He has a thin, almost skeletal frame. His most distinctive feature is his face, which he habitually disfigures by tearing at his own skin and pulling it apart, often exposing muscle and bone. He wears a black tattered robe reminiscent of a clergyman's vestments, and his eyes are wild and bloodshot. His hands are often stained with blood from his self-inflicted wounds.

Symbols — He is associated with the Witch Cult's symbol, a black flame or a stylized eye. He often carries a small, leather-bound book, which he reads obsessively. His signature pose involves stretching his arms wide and exclaiming 'Love love love love love!' in a maniacal manner.

Domains — He operates within the Kingdom of Lugunica, primarily in the area around the Roswaal mansion and the nearby village of Arlam. He uses a hidden chapel in the forest as his base of operations. He is also seen in the capital city of Lugunica during his attempts to possess Subaru's body.

Accounts I
The Witch Cult's Attack on the Roswaal MansionRe:Zero light novel, Arc 3; anime episodes 15-21

Fictional timeline, year 1 of Subaru's arrival

Petelgeuse leads a group of Witch Cult followers to attack the Roswaal mansion and the nearby village, seeking to capture or kill those associated with the royal selection. He confronts Subaru Natsuki, who is trying to protect Emilia and the villagers. Petelgeuse's obsession with the Witch of Envy drives him to target Subaru, whom he sees as a heretic. Through multiple loops of Subaru's 'Return by Death' ability, Subaru eventually learns Petelgeuse's weaknesses and, with the help of allies like Rem and Beatrice, defeats him. Petelgeuse is killed, but his actions cause significant suffering and death among the villagers. Subaru's repeated deaths and resurrections take a toll on his psyche. The event also brings Subaru closer to Emilia and the others, strengthening his resolve.

Accounts II
The Possession of SubaruRe:Zero light novel, Arc 3; anime episode 21

Fictional timeline, year 1

After being defeated, Petelgeuse attempts to possess Subaru's body as a last resort. He partially succeeds, taking control of Subaru's hand and causing him to attack his friends. Subaru, with the help of Beatrice and others, manages to expel Petelgeuse's spirit, ultimately destroying him for good. Petelgeuse is permanently destroyed. Subaru is left with psychological scars from the experience. This event also reveals more about the nature of the Witch Factors.

Petelgeuse is the strongest of the Witch Cult Archbishops. — There is no official ranking, and his defeat by Subaru suggests he is not invincible. Other Archbishops, such as Regulus Corneas, are also extremely powerful.

Petelgeuse is a demon or spirit. — He is a human who has been granted power by the Witch Factor. His appearance is the result of self-mutilation, not inherent nature.

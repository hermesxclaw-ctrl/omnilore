Who It Is

In the neon-lit world of Virtua Fighter, Pai Chan stands as a paradox: a glamorous Hong Kong action star whose dazzling kicks and ballet-like flourishes mask a deep-seated anger toward the father who let her mother die. She enters each tournament not for glory, but to prove herself worthy of a legacy she resents—a fight that is as much against her own heart as against her opponents.

Pai is initially cold and distant, driven by a desire to defeat her father. She is proud and determined, but also shows a more vulnerable side. As the series progresses, she becomes more mature and reconciles with her father. She is portrayed as a disciplined martial artist and a professional actress who values her craft.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Pai' is likely derived from the Chinese surname 'Bai' (白), meaning 'white', but this is speculative. 'Chan' is a common Cantonese surname (陳).

Pronunciation — Pie Chan (English approximation), Pai Chen (Japanese romanization)

Aliases — パイ・チェン, Pai Chen, Pai-Chan

Ownership — Sega, Japanese video game company. The character is a Hong Kong national but is voiced in Japanese and designed by a Japanese team.

Powers
Mizongyi

A Chinese martial art known for its evasive footwork and rapid strikes. Pai uses it to outmaneuver opponents.

Limit: Requires agility and precision; Pai is a lightweight character, so she has lower defense.

Ensei-ken

In the anime, Pai is taught this martial art forcibly. It is a style that emphasizes powerful, direct attacks.

Limit: Pai initially resents this style, so she may not use it to her full potential.

Flip and throw techniques

Pai can perform multiple flips and use her opponent's momentum to throw them over her shoulder.

Limit: These moves are risky if they miss, leaving her open to counterattacks.

Acting and performance

Pai uses her acting skills to feint and mislead opponents, making her unpredictable.

Limit: Not a combat technique per se, but enhances her fighting style.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Pai Chan is a young woman with long black hair, often tied in a ponytail or with bangs. She has brown eyes. In most games, she wears a traditional Chinese dress or a combat outfit with a floral pattern, a silk sash, and white ballet flats. In Virtua Fighter 2, she wears a winter-themed outfit, and in later games, she has various alternate costumes.

Symbols — Her signature symbol is the Chinese character '白' (white) on her outfit, representing her surname. She is often associated with flowers, particularly plum blossoms, reflecting her Chinese heritage.

Domains — She lives in Hong Kong and is often seen on movie sets or training in martial arts schools. In the games, she travels to various international locations for tournaments.

Accounts I
The First World Fighting TournamentVirtua Fighter (1993)

1993

Pai enters the first tournament to defeat her father, Lau Chan, whom she blames for her mother's death. She is defeated, revealing her relative weakness. Pai realizes her lack of strength and decides to train for a year.

The Second World Fighting TournamentVirtua Fighter 2 (1994)

1994

After a year of intense training, Pai enters the second tournament, having incorporated her own techniques. She is defeated again by her father. Pai continues to train, but also focuses on her acting career.

Accounts II
The Third World Fighting TournamentVirtua Fighter 3 (1996)

1996

Pai enters the third tournament, but is defeated. She realizes her skills now match her father's, and she returns to Hong Kong to focus on acting. Pai's relationship with her father remains strained, but she begins to find peace.

The Fourth World Fighting TournamentVirtua Fighter 4 (2001)

2001

Pai learns of her father's terminal illness and enters the tournament to prove herself worthy as his successor. She reconciles with him. Pai and Lau reconcile, and Pai accepts her father's legacy.

Anime: Journey with Akira YukiVirtua Fighter (TV series)

1995-1996

Pai is attacked by criminals and joins Akira Yuki and other fighters on a journey. The anime reveals that her mother died when Pai was much younger, and that she was forced to learn Ensei-ken. Pai's backstory is deepened, and she forms bonds with other fighters.

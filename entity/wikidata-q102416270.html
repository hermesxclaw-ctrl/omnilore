The Approach

In the sprawling world of Re:Zero, where heroes and villains are often defined by their dramatic roles, Bordeaux Zellgef stands as a testament to the quiet, often unseen forces that shape the kingdom's fate. A man of considerable political weight, his actions and decisions ripple through the lives of the main cast, yet his own story remains largely in the shadows.

Content advisory. Contains references to political maneuvering, class discrimination, and the harsh realities of a feudal society.
Identity

Bordeaux Zellgef

Pronunciation BOR-doh ZEL-gef

Etymology

The name 'Bordeaux' likely references the French city, known for its wine, possibly alluding to his noble status. 'Zellgef' may be a constructed name; no official etymology has been provided.

Cultural Ownership

Re:Zero is a Japanese light novel series created by Tappei Nagatsuki. Bordeaux Zellgef is a fictional character within that universe, and his cultural ownership lies with the creator and the franchise.

Disputed Identifications

None known. Bordeaux Zellgef is a minor character with a clear role in the story.

Description

Appearance

Bordeaux Zellgef is depicted as an elderly man with a stern, imposing presence. He has short gray hair and a well-groomed gray beard. He typically wears formal, high-status attire befitting a noble, often including a coat and cravat, and carries himself with an air of authority. His eyes are sharp and discerning, reflecting his political acumen.

Symbols & Iconography

As a member of the Council of Elders, his iconography is tied to the political symbols of Lugunica, such as the royal crest. He is often seen in settings of political power, like the royal palace or council chambers.

Behaviour & Personality

Bordeaux Zellgef is portrayed as a pragmatic and somewhat cynical politician. He is deeply concerned with the stability and traditions of the kingdom, and he views the Royal Selection with a conservative lens. He is not overtly malicious but is willing to use underhanded tactics to achieve his goals, as seen in his attempts to manipulate the selection process. He shows a particular disdain for Subaru, viewing him as an unpredictable outsider who disrupts the established order. He is also shown to be somewhat dismissive of Emilia due to her half-elf heritage, reflecting the prevalent prejudice in Lugunica.

Domains & Habitats

He is a resident of the royal capital of Lugunica, and his primary domain is the political arena. He is often seen in the royal palace and other locations of political significance.

Version Differences

There are no significant differences between the light novel, anime, and manga portrayals of Bordeaux Zellgef. He remains a consistent minor antagonist in the Royal Selection arc.

Stories & Myths

The Royal Selection and the Trial of Subaru Natsuki

Source Re:Zero Light Novel Arc 3, Anime Season 1 Episodes 12-15Culture Japanese light novel culturePeriod Fictional timeline, year 1 of the Royal Selection

Summary

Bordeaux Zellgef is introduced as a member of the Council of Elders overseeing the Royal Selection, a process to choose the next ruler of Lugunica. He is initially skeptical of Subaru, who has become involved with Emilia, one of the candidates. When Subaru is accused of attacking the council and harming a noble, Bordeaux seizes the opportunity to have him tried and sentenced to death. He uses his political influence to stack the trial against Subaru, but his plans are ultimately foiled by the intervention of Reinhard van Astrea and the testimony of other characters. Bordeaux's actions highlight the political corruption and prejudice within the kingdom.

Consequences

Bordeaux's scheme fails, and Subaru is freed. This event deepens the animosity between Subaru and the political establishment, and it solidifies Subaru's resolve to support Emilia.

The anime adaptation condenses the trial, but the core events remain the same as the light novel.

Bordeaux's motives

Position A

Bordeaux is a staunch traditionalist who believes in the rigid hierarchy of Lugunica and sees Subaru as a threat to that order.

Position A — evidence

His dialogue in the trial emphasizes the importance of tradition and the rule of law.

Position B

Bordeaux is a self-serving politician who uses the trial to eliminate a potential rival for the throne (Emilia) by proxy.

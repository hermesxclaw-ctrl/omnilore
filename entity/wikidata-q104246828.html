Who It Is

He died a recluse, a 34-year-old shut-in with nothing to show for a life spent hiding from the world. But when a truck bore down on a group of teenagers, he made one desperate, selfless choice—and woke up as a baby in a world of magic and monsters. This is the story of Rudeus Greyrat, a man given a second chance to become someone worthy of the name.

Initially, Rudeus is a perverted, cowardly, and selfish individual, carrying over his past life's flaws. He is also highly intelligent and determined to live a fulfilling life. Over time, he matures, becoming more responsible, compassionate, and heroic, though he retains some perverse tendencies.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Rudeus is a Latin-derived name possibly meaning 'unpolished' or 'rough', fitting his initial character. Greyrat is a surname invented for the story, evoking a gray rat, perhaps symbolizing his timid and overlooked nature.

Powers
Magic Affinity

Rudeus has a natural affinity for magic, allowing him to learn spells quickly. He can use a wide range of magic, including attack, defense, and healing spells.

Limit: His mana capacity is large but not unlimited. He requires focus and incantation, though he later learns to cast silently.

Swordplay

Though primarily a mage, Rudeus learns the Sword God style, using a sword for close combat. He is skilled but not a master.

Limit: He is not as proficient as dedicated swordsmen, but his magic enhances his combat.

Tactical Genius

Rudeus is highly strategic, using his knowledge from his past life and his magic creatively in battle.

Limit: His strategies can fail if he lacks information.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In his second life, Rudeus is described as having brown hair and blue eyes. As a child, he has a small, unremarkable frame. As an adult, he grows taller and more muscular, with a distinct scar on his chest from a past battle. His appearance changes over time, reflecting his growth from infant to adult.

Symbols — His staff, which he carries from his early adventures, becomes a symbol of his magical prowess. His signature magic circles and incantations are iconic in adaptations. The color blue is often associated with his magic.

Domains — He lives in the Kingdom of Asura, the Demon Continent, and later in the city of Sharia. He is a traveler and adventurer, eventually settling down as a teacher and family man.

The Approach

From a Siberian workshop to the skies over a war-torn homeland, Antonov is the name behind the largest fixed-wing aircraft ever built—and a symbol of Ukrainian resilience. This is the story of a design bureau that turned colossal ambition into flying reality.

Identity

Antonov Company

Pronunciation An-TOH-nov

Known As

AntonovAntonov ASTCAeronautical Scientific-Technical Complex named after AntonovAntonov Design BureauOKB-153Antonov State Enterprise

Etymology

Named after its founder, Soviet aircraft designer Oleg Konstantinovich Antonov (1906–1984).

Cultural Ownership

Ukrainian state-owned company, with historical roots in the Soviet Union.

Disputed Identifications

Sometimes confused with the Antonov An-225 Mriya aircraft itself, or with other Soviet-era design bureaus. The company is distinct from its products.

Description

Appearance

Not applicable (company, not a physical entity). The company's products are aircraft, ranging from small utility planes to the massive An-225.

Symbols & Iconography

The Antonov logo features a stylized wing and the company name. Aircraft carry the 'An-' prefix.

Behaviour & Personality

Known for innovation in very large aircraft and rough-field operations. Historically state-owned, with a reputation for engineering robustness.

Domains & Habitats

Headquartered in Kyiv, Ukraine. Production and design facilities in Ukraine, with historical sites in Novosibirsk and other Soviet locations.

Version Differences

The company has undergone several name changes and reorganizations, reflecting its Soviet origin and post-Soviet Ukrainian identity.

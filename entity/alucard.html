Who It Is

Alucard is the quintessential vampire of modern manga and anime — a being of immense, terrifying power who serves the Hellsing Organization of England as its ultimate weapon against supernatural threats. His name is 'Dracula' spelled backward, and he IS Dracula — the original Count, the Impaler, the No-Life King himself. Defeated centuries ago by Abraham Van Helsing, Alucard was bound to the Hellsing family and now serves as their enforcer, grudgingly protecting the very humanity he despises. Alucard appears as a tall, gaunt figure with long black hair, crimson duster, wide-brimmed hat, and orange tinted glasses that hide his blood-red, multi-pupiled eyes. He wields two massively oversized pistols: the .454 Casull (which fires blessed silver rounds for killing undead) and the Jackal (a 13mm armor-piercing explosive round weapon designed specifically to kill other vampires). But his true power lies in his vampiric abilities: superhuman strength and speed, regeneration from virtually any wound (including complete dismemberment), telekinesis, shape-shifting (into mist, shadows, wolves, or a giant bat-like monster), and most terrifyingly, the power of 'Familiar Release' — he stores the souls of every being he has ever consumed (over three million) within himself and can release them as an army of the damned. Alucard's psychology is complex and deeply tragic. He is a monster who hates what he is, a being of infinite power who is utterly bored with existence. He seeks a human strong enough to kill him and end his eternal torment — a role that several characters attempt to fill. His most defining trait is his supreme arrogance and sadistic joy in battle: he toys with opponents, allows them to believe they are winning, and then reveals his true power with devastating, often cruel, theatricality. Alucard represents the romantic vampire archetype pushed to its darkest extreme — the monster who has lived so long that he has forgotten what it means to be human, yet is bound by a single thread of honor to protect the species that made him what he is.

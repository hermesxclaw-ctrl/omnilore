Who It Is

Alucard is the quintessential vampire of modern manga and anime — a being of immense, terrifying power who serves the Hellsing Organization of England as its ultimate weapon against supernatural threats. His name is 'Dracula' spelled backward, and he IS Dracula — the original Count, the Impaler, the No-Life King himself. Defeated centuries ago by Abraham Van Helsing, Alucard was bound to the Hellsing family and now serves as their enforcer, grudgingly protecting the very humanity he despises. Alucard appears as a tall, gaunt figure with long black hair, crimson duster, wide-brimmed hat, and orange tinted glasses that hide his blood-red, multi-pupiled eyes. He wields two massively oversized pistols: the .454 Casull (which fires blessed silver rounds for killing undead) and the Jackal (a 13mm armor-piercing explosive round weapon designed specifically to kill other vampires). But his true power lies in his vampiric abilities: superhuman strength and speed, regeneration from virtually any wound (including complete dismemberment), telekinesis, shape-shifting (into mist, shadows, wolves, or a giant bat-like monster), and most terrifyingly, the power of 'Familiar Release' — he stores the souls of every being he has ever consumed (over three million) within himself and can release them as an army of the damned. Alucard's psychology is complex and deeply tragic. He is a monster who hates what he is, a being of infinite power who is utterly bored with existence. He seeks a human strong enough to kill him and end his eternal torment — a role that several characters attempt to fill. His most defining trait is his supreme arrogance and sadistic joy in battle: he toys with opponents, allows them to believe they are winning, and then reveals his true power with devastating, often cruel, theatricality. Alucard represents the romantic vampire archetype pushed to its darkest extreme — the monster who has lived so long that he has forgotten what it means to be human, yet is bound by a single thread of honor to protect the species that made him what he is.

The Approach

In the blood-soaked world of Hellsing, Alucard is not merely a vampire; he is the ultimate weapon of the Hellsing Organization, a creature of immense power and terrifying resolve. He is the embodiment of the undead, a monster who serves humanity by hunting his own kind, yet his true nature and allegiance are shrouded in mystery and violence. His name, a mirror of Dracula, hints at a legacy that is both a curse and a source of unyielding strength.

Content advisory. Graphic violence, blood and gore, themes of vampirism, psychological horror, depictions of war crimes and atrocities, religious imagery and symbolism, dark and mature themes.

Etymology

Alucard is the anadrome of 'Dracula', a deliberate inversion of the famous vampire name. The name 'Dracula' itself derives from the Romanian 'Dracul' (dragon/devil), associated with Vlad III Dracula. Alucard's name is a direct reversal, symbolizing his antithetical nature and his role as a dark mirror of the original vampire.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Kōta Hirano for the manga series Hellsing, serialized in Young King Ours from 1997 to 2008. The character and series are owned by Shōnen Gahōsha, with international licensing by Dark Horse Comics and others. Alucard is a significant figure in modern Japanese pop culture and has influenced subsequent vampire portrayals in anime and manga.

Disputed Identifications

Alucard's true identity is revealed to be Vlad III Dracula, the historical Wallachian prince, but this is a fictionalized version. Some fans and critics debate the extent to which the character draws from historical accounts versus Bram Stoker's novel. Additionally, the name 'Alucard' is used by many other fictional characters across different media, but this dossier specifically covers the Hellsing incarnation.

Description

Appearance

Alucard is a tall, pale-skinned vampire with long, flowing black hair and piercing red eyes. He typically wears a long red duster coat, black suit, and a wide-brimmed hat. His attire is reminiscent of a Victorian-era gentleman, but with a modern, militaristic edge. In his true form, he appears as a monstrous entity with multiple faces and tendrils, reflecting his nature as a devourer of souls. He also has a younger form that resembles his historical counterpart, Vlad Țepeș.

Symbols & Iconography

The Hellsing Organization's crest, a stylized cross, is often associated with Alucard. His signature weapon is a pair of custom-made handguns, the 'Jackal' and the 'Casull', which are blessed and capable of harming supernatural beings. His red coat and hat are iconic, often depicted in promotional art. The inverted cross on his gloves symbolizes his status as a cursed being who serves a holy organization.

Behaviour & Personality

Alucard is arrogant, sadistic, and ruthless, but also deeply loyal to his master, Integra Hellsing. He takes pleasure in battle and the suffering of his enemies, often taunting them. He is cold and calculating, but shows a twisted sense of honor. He is obsessed with the idea of a worthy opponent who can defeat him, a remnant of his desire for death. He is calm and composed, rarely showing emotion, but his rage is terrifying.

Domains & Habitats

Alucard is bound to the Hellsing Organization and resides in the Hellsing Manor in England. He operates worldwide, wherever the organization's missions take him, often in urban settings. He is a creature of the night, but can function in daylight to a limited extent. He is not tied to any specific realm, but his existence is intertwined with the supernatural and the war against the undead.

Version Differences

In the original 2001 anime, Alucard's backstory is less explored, and the plot diverges significantly from the manga. In Hellsing Ultimate, the story follows the manga more closely, including his true identity and final confrontation with the Major. The manga itself has a darker tone and more explicit violence compared to the TV anime. Alucard's powers are more clearly defined in the Ultimate version, with his ability to release his restraints shown in detail.

Stories & Myths

The Beginning: Dracula's Defeat

Source Hellsing manga, Volume 1 (flashback)Culture Japanese mangaPeriod 1897 (fictional)

Summary

In 1897, Abraham Van Helsing and his allies defeated the vampire Dracula. Rather than killing him, Van Helsing took Dracula as a prisoner and bound him to the Hellsing family, creating the powerful servant Alucard. This event is the foundation of Alucard's existence as a weapon of the Hellsing Organization.

Participants

Abraham Van Helsing

Dracula

Arthur Hellsing

Consequences

Alucard becomes the loyal servant of the Hellsing family, serving generations of masters. He gains a new name and a purpose, but also a curse of eternal servitude.

Variants

The TV anime does not fully explore this backstory, but it is implied. In Ultimate, it is shown in detail.

The Battle Against the Nazis

Source Hellsing manga, Volumes 4-10Culture Japanese mangaPeriod 1944 (flashback) and modern day

Summary

Alucard faces remnants of Nazi Germany, led by the Major, who seek to destroy England and the Hellsing Organization. The conflict culminates in a massive battle in London, where Alucard releases his restraints and unleashes his full power against the Nazi forces.

Participants

Alucard

Integra Hellsing

Seras Victoria

The Major

Walter C. Dornez

Consequences

London is devastated, but the Nazis are defeated. Alucard's true identity is revealed, and he ultimately fights the Major in a final showdown. The war leaves many casualties, including Walter's death.

Variants

In the TV anime, the storyline is replaced by an original plot involving Millenium, but the outcome is similar.

The Final Battle and Alucard's End

Source Hellsing manga, Volume 10Culture Japanese mangaPeriod Modern day

Summary

After defeating the Major, Alucard is attacked by Walter, who has been turned into a vampire. In the ensuing battle, Alucard is seemingly destroyed, but he survives by absorbing the souls of the Nazis he killed, allowing him to regenerate. He then returns to London and is reunited with Integra, but he later releases all his souls and disappears, leaving Integra to rule alone.

Participants

Alucard

Walter C. Dornez

Integra Hellsing

Consequences

Alucard sacrifices his power and existence to save the world, leaving a void in the Hellsing Organization. The ending is ambiguous, suggesting he may return.

Variants

The TV anime has a different ending where Alucard is not defeated and continues to serve Integra.

Vlad III Dracula

Type Past SelfSource Hellsing manga and Ultimate

Description

Alucard is the vampire form of Vlad III Dracula, the historical Wallachian prince known for his cruelty. His past life as a human and a ruler influences his present character.

Vlad the Impaler's atrocities

Source Hellsing manga, Volume 8 (flashback)

Description

Alucard's past as Vlad III Dracula includes the historical impalement of thousands of enemies, which is referenced in the series as part of his ruthless nature. The manga depicts his past as a brutal ruler who committed mass murder.

Scholarly Context

Vlad III Dracula is a historical figure known for his extreme cruelty, and the series uses this to justify Alucard's monstrousness.

Nazi war crimes

Source Hellsing manga, Volumes 4-10

Description

The series features the Nazi organization Millenium, which engages in human experimentation, mass murder, and attempts to create an army of vampires. Alucard fights against them, but the depiction includes graphic violence and atrocities.

Scholarly Context

The series does not glorify the Nazis; they are portrayed as villains, but the graphic depiction may be disturbing.

Alucard's sadism

Source Hellsing manga, multiple chapters

Description

Alucard takes pleasure in killing and torturing his enemies, often prolonging their suffering. This is a core aspect of his character, but it is presented as a negative trait.

Scholarly Context

The series explores the dark side of vampirism and the moral ambiguity of its protagonist.

Scholarly Consensus

The character is a fictional interpretation, and the historical accuracy is not a concern for the narrative.

Alucard's power level

Position A

Alucard is one of the most powerful vampires in fiction, capable of defeating armies.

Position A — evidence

He defeats the Nazi army in London and easily kills powerful vampires.

Position B

His power is limited by his restraints and the need to consume souls.

Position B — evidence

He is vulnerable to attacks when his restraints are partially released, and he can be weakened by holy weapons.

Scholarly Consensus

Alucard is extremely powerful, but not invincible; his power is conditional.

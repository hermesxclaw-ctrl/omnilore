Who It Is

In the blood-soaked world of Hellsing, Alucard is not merely a vampire; he is the ultimate weapon of the Hellsing Organization, a creature of immense power and terrifying resolve. He is the embodiment of the undead, a monster who serves humanity by hunting his own kind, yet his true nature and allegiance are shrouded in mystery and violence. His name, a mirror of Dracula, hints at a legacy that is both a curse and a source of unyielding strength.

Alucard is arrogant, sadistic, and ruthless, but also deeply loyal to his master, Integra Hellsing. He takes pleasure in battle and the suffering of his enemies, often taunting them. He is cold and calculating, but shows a twisted sense of honor. He is obsessed with the idea of a worthy opponent who can defeat him, a remnant of his desire for death. He is calm and composed, rarely showing emotion, but his rage is terrifying.

Powers
Superhuman Physical Attributes

Alucard possesses immense strength, speed, agility, and endurance, far beyond human limits. He can easily overpower vampires and other supernatural beings.

Limit: None known, though he can be temporarily hindered by powerful holy artifacts.

Regeneration

Alucard can regenerate from almost any injury, including decapitation and dismemberment. He can even reform from a puddle of blood.

Limit: Regeneration can be slowed by blessed weapons or attacks from other powerful vampires.

Blood Manipulation

Alucard can control and manipulate blood, using it as a weapon or to heal. He can also absorb blood to gain power and sustain himself.

Limit: Requires a source of blood; he cannot create blood from nothing.

Familiar Summoning

Alucard can summon the souls of those he has killed, using them as familiars to fight for him. These familiars often take monstrous forms.

Limit: The number of familiars is limited by the souls he has consumed; he can release them to regain power.

Releasing Restraints

Alucard can release the seals placed on his power by Integra, transforming into increasingly monstrous forms. Levels 0, 1, and 2 are known, with Level 0 being a full release.

Limit: Releasing restraints is dangerous and can lead to loss of control; it is only used in dire situations.

Immortality

Alucard is functionally immortal, having lived for centuries. He cannot die from age or disease, and only a complete destruction of his body and soul could potentially kill him.

Limit: He can be temporarily defeated if his body is completely destroyed, but he will eventually regenerate unless his soul is also destroyed.

Hypnosis

Alucard has the ability to hypnotize and control weaker minds, though he rarely uses it.

Limit: Strong-willed individuals or other powerful vampires may resist.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Alucard is a tall, pale-skinned vampire with long, flowing black hair and piercing red eyes. He typically wears a long red duster coat, black suit, and a wide-brimmed hat. His attire is reminiscent of a Victorian-era gentleman, but with a modern, militaristic edge. In his true form, he appears as a monstrous entity with multiple faces and tendrils, reflecting his nature as a devourer of souls. He also has a younger form that resembles his histori

Symbols — The Hellsing Organization's crest, a stylized cross, is often associated with Alucard. His signature weapon is a pair of custom-made handguns, the 'Jackal' and the 'Casull', which are blessed and capable of harming supernatural beings. His red coat and hat are iconic, often depicted in promotional art. The inverted cross on his gloves symbolizes his status as a cursed being who serves a holy organization.

Domains — Alucard is bound to the Hellsing Organization and resides in the Hellsing Manor in England. He operates worldwide, wherever the organization's missions take him, often in urban settings. He is a creature of the night, but can function in daylight to a limited extent. He is not tied to any specific realm, but his existence is intertwined with the supernatural and the war against the undead.

Accounts I
The Beginning: Dracula's DefeatHellsing manga, Volume 1 (flashback)

1897 (fictional)

In 1897, Abraham Van Helsing and his allies defeated the vampire Dracula. Rather than killing him, Van Helsing took Dracula as a prisoner and bound him to the Hellsing family, creating the powerful servant Alucard. This event is the foundation of Alucard's existence as a weapon of the Hellsing Organization. Alucard becomes the loyal servant of the Hellsing family, serving generations of masters. He gains a new name and a purpose, but also a curse of eternal servitude.

Accounts II
The Battle Against the NazisHellsing manga, Volumes 4-10

1944 (flashback) and modern day

Alucard faces remnants of Nazi Germany, led by the Major, who seek to destroy England and the Hellsing Organization. The conflict culminates in a massive battle in London, where Alucard releases his restraints and unleashes his full power against the Nazi forces. London is devastated, but the Nazis are defeated. Alucard's true identity is revealed, and he ultimately fights the Major in a final showdown. The war leaves many casualties, including Walter's death.

The Final Battle and Alucard's EndHellsing manga, Volume 10

Modern day

After defeating the Major, Alucard is attacked by Walter, who has been turned into a vampire. In the ensuing battle, Alucard is seemingly destroyed, but he survives by absorbing the souls of the Nazis he killed, allowing him to regenerate. He then returns to London and is reunited with Integra, but he later releases all his souls and disappears, leaving Integra to rule alone. Alucard sacrifices his power and existence to save the world, leaving a void in the Hellsing Organization. The ending is ambiguous, suggesting he may return.

Who It Is

The Mind Stone is the Infinity Stone that governs all aspects of consciousness, thought, and psychic energy. It is the embodiment of the universal mind — the collective awareness that connects all thinking beings across the cosmos. Its wielder can read, control, and alter thoughts, project devastating psionic attacks, create psychic shields, manifest illusions indistinguishable from reality, and access the collective unconscious of all life in the universe. The Mind Stone can also grant sentience to artificial intelligences, elevate animal intelligence to human levels, or suppress entire populations into docility. In the Marvel Cinematic Universe, the Mind Stone was originally housed within Loki's scepter, gifted by Thanos to facilitate the Chitauri invasion. S.H.I.E.L.D. and Hydra studied the scepter, using its energy to experiment on powered beings (creating Quicksilver and Scarlet Witch). After the invasion, the Mind Stone was used by Tony Stark and Bruce Banner to create Ultron — an artificial intelligence meant to protect Earth, which instead became a genocidal threat. The Vision — a synthetic being created by Ultron but redeemed by the Avengers — was bonded with the Mind Stone, becoming its living host and guardian. In the comics, the Mind Gem (as it is known) has been used by Thanos, the Magus, Moondragon, and Professor X. It represents the ultimate expression of psychic mastery: the power to know every thought in existence and to shape every mind to your will. The Mind Stone embodies the philosophical question: if you control what everyone thinks, do you control reality itself?

Attested in American Comic BookEra Conceived as part of the Infinity Gems in The Thanos Quest #1-2 (1990); as a Stone in the MCU, first appeared in Avengers: Age of Ultron (2015, post-credits) and fully in Avengers: Infinity War (2018)

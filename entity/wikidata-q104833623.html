Who It Is

A flamboyant fashion model with a lightning-charged fighting style, Benimaru Nikaido swaggered into The King of Fighters '94 as Kyo Kusanagi's stylish right-hand man. With his silver hair, sharp suits, and electric kicks, he's more than just a pretty face—he's a veteran fighter whose electrokinetic abilities and shootboxing prowess have made him a mainstay of SNK's legendary tournament series.

Benimaru is arrogant, narcissistic, and stylish, often boasting about his looks and skills. He is also loyal and reliable as a teammate, especially to Kyo, and shows a sense of honor in combat. He enjoys the spotlight and often poses for the audience.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Benimaru (紅丸) literally means 'crimson circle' or 'red sphere' in Japanese, referencing his red-themed attire and possibly his flashy personality. Nikaido (二階堂) is a Japanese surname meaning 'second hall'.

Pronunciation — Beh-nee-mah-roo Nee-kai-doh

Aliases — Benimaru, Nikaido Benimaru, Red Maru (unofficial)

Ownership — Created by SNK for the fighting game series The King of Fighters. The character is owned by SNK (formerly SNK Playmore).

Powers
Electrokinesis

Benimaru can generate and manipulate electricity, using it to enhance his attacks, such as the Raijinken (Lightning Fist) and Benimaru Electric Tackle. He can also discharge electricity from his body to shock opponents.

Limit: His electrical attacks are short-range and require physical contact or close proximity. Overuse may leave him vulnerable.

Shootboxing

A hybrid martial art combining boxing and kicking techniques, allowing for fast and powerful strikes. His style incorporates acrobatic kicks and flashy combos.

Limit: Largely dependent on physical stamina and precision.

Lightning-based special moves

Signature moves include Raijinken (lightning punch), Benimaru Colate (lightning knee), and Benimaru Electric Tackle (charging tackle with electricity).

Limit: Some moves have startup lag or are punishable if blocked.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Benimaru is a tall, lean man with silver-white hair styled in a flamboyant pompadour. He wears a form-fitting red and white outfit, often with a high-collared jacket and matching pants, accessorized with a red scarf or belt. His design emphasizes elegance and showmanship, with a confident smirk.

Symbols — His name and color scheme (red and white) echo the Japanese flag, and his lightning attacks are often depicted with blue or white electricity. His signature pose involves raising a hand crackling with electricity.

Domains — As a fighter, Benimaru is found in tournament arenas across the world. He is also a fashion model, so he inhabits high-fashion environments when not fighting.

Accounts I
The King of Fighters '94Game story

1994

Benimaru is recruited by Kyo Kusanagi to form the Japan Team, along with Goro Daimon. They enter the tournament and defeat Rugal Bernstein. The team becomes known as champions, and Benimaru gains fame.

Orochi Saga (KOF '95-'97)Game stories

1995-1997

Benimaru continues to team with Kyo and Daimon, fighting against the Orochi clan and its agents, including Iori Yagami. He plays a supporting role in the conflict. The Orochi is sealed, and Benimaru's team remains intact.

Accounts II
NESTS Saga (KOF '99-2001)Game stories

1999-2001

Benimaru teams with K', Maxima, and Shingo in KOF '99, and later with Kyo and Daimon in KOF 2000-2001. He helps fight the NESTS organization. NESTS is defeated, and Benimaru survives.

Tales of Ash Saga (KOF 2003-XIII)Game stories

2003-2010

Benimaru appears in various teams, often with Kyo or as a solo fighter. He is not central to the plot but participates in tournaments. He remains a prominent character.

Shun'ei Saga (KOF XIV onwards)Game stories

2016-present

Benimaru continues to appear in tournaments, sometimes teaming with Kyo and others. He is not heavily involved in the main conflict but remains a fan favorite. His popularity continues.

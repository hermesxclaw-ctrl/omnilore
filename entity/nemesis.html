Who It Is
Form

In ancient Greek mythology, Nemesis is often depicted as a goddess with a stern expression, carrying a bow and arrows. She is sometimes shown as a woman with a serpent or a lion at her feet. In the Resident Evil series, Nemesis is a monstrous creature with a humanoid body and a grotesque face, driven by a singular purpose to hunt down and kill the protagonist.

Powers

Retribution Nemesis has the power to deliver retribution and justice to those who have committed hubris or excessive pride. In the Resident Evil series, she is able to track down and kill her victims with ease, using her intelligence and cunning to outwit and outmaneuver them.

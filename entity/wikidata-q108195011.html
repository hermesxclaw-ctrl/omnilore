Who It Is

Spandam has never won a fair fight in his life — and that is exactly why he is one of One Piece's most hated villains. The chinless, masked chief of CP9 cannot throw a punch, cannot use Rokushiki, and cowers behind a sword that turns into an elephant, yet he nearly erased Nico Robin's future, burned Tom's legacy, and called a Buster Call down on his own headquarters just to prove he was in charge.

Attested as cowardly, sycophantic to superiors (the Five Elders, later Imu's hierarchy), and sadistically cruel to subordinates and captives. He brags constantly about his rank and his father's influence, takes credit for others' work, and panics when confronted physically. He shows classic authoritarian personality: obsequious upward, tyrannical downward. He tortures Robin psychologically (threatening to kill Straw Hats), whips Franky, and attempts to murder Robin's allies while begging Lucci to protect him. Powers
Bureaucratic Authority and Buster Call Authorization

Spandam's only true power is institutional: as CP9 chief (and later CP0 agent) he can command Cipher Pol assassins, requisition warships, and activate Buster Call via golden Den Den Mushi. He uses this to order the destruction of Enies Lobby and to threaten Robin with the deaths of her crew.

Limit: Authority is delegated and revocable; Five Elders and Kong can override him. His incompetence led to unauthorized Buster Call that destroyed his own base. Post-timeskip, Lucci and Kaku openly ignore his orders (Ch. 1106).

Funkfreed (Elephant Sword)

A sword that ate the Zou Zou no Mi (Elephant-Elephant Fruit), becoming an elephant-sword hybrid. In sword form it is a standard longsword; on command it transforms into a small elephant or elephant-sword hybrid that can charge, trumpet, and slash. Spandam wields it but cannot control it well; Funkfreed often panics or attacks indiscriminately.

Limit: Funkfreed is not loyal and is frightened by combat; its elephant form is relatively weak compared to CP9's Rokushiki. Spandam's swordsmanship is negligible — he is disarmed by Franky and Robin easily.

Lack of Combat Ability (Doriki 9)

Explicitly quantified as Doriki 9, meaning weaker than an armed Marine guard (Doriki 10). He knows no Rokushiki (Geppo, Tekkai, Shigan, Rankyaku, Soru, Kami-e), no Haki, and no martial arts. His fighting style is slapping, whipping, and hiding behind others.

Limit: Cannot defend himself; defeated in one blow by Nico Robin (Clutch) and later by Franky. Any direct combat results in immediate loss.

Manipulation, Framing, and Intelligence Gathering

Skilled at political manipulation: framing Tom's Workers for the attack on the judicial ship (Ch. 355–357), blackmailing Robin with Buster Call threat, and using Spandine's legacy to gain promotion. He excels at paperwork, espionage logistics, and exploiting legal loopholes.

Limit: Schemes unravel under scrutiny; his framing of Tom required CP5's help and still left evidence; his Buster Call blunder exposed incompetence.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Spandam is a thin, lanky man in his late 30s (38 at debut, 40 post-timeskip) with a weak chin, prominent nose, brown eyes, and wavy light-purple/pinkish hair. His most distinctive feature is a leather S&M-inspired mask that covers the upper face with eye holes and a zipper over the mouth, worn with a purple suit, white shirt, and CP9/CP0 uniform coat. He wears a purple spotted tie and often carries his sword Funkfreed at his waist. Post-timeskip

Symbols — Mask and Funkfreed are his visual shorthand for unearned authority. The mask evokes dominatrix/bondage gear, signaling his sadomasochistic abuse of power and desire to hide his weak identity behind institutional terror — a deliberate contrast to the animal-themed CP9 assassins who hide nothing. Funkfreed, an elephant sword, literalizes the idiom of wielding a weapon he cannot control; the elephant's panicked trumpeting mirrors Spandam's

Domains — Headquarters-bound bureaucrat. Domains: Enies Lobby (Judicial Island, chief's office in Tower of Justice), Mary Geoise/Pangaea Castle periphery via CP0 after promotion, and field missions only when escorted by CP9/CP0. He does not inhabit battlefields willingly; his habitat is offices, interrogation rooms, and Den Den Mushi communications. Accounts I
The Framing of Tom's Workers — Pluton BlueprintsOne Piece manga Ch. 355–358, 364–369; Tom's Workers flashback Ch. 371–373

2004–2005 publication; in-universe c. 24 years before Enies Lobby (Tom's trial)

As newly appointed CP5/CP9 chief (inheriting position from father Spandine), Spandam seeks the ancient weapon Pluton blueprints held by shipwright Tom. He orders CP9 agents (undercover as shipwrights) to steal them, and when Tom refuses to surrender them, Spandam orchestrates an attack on the judicial ship using battle shipwrights Cutty Flam (Franky) and Iceburg as pawns. He then uses the attack to convict Tom of building the Oro Jackson and possessing forbidden weapons, sentencing him to death via Enies Lobby. Tom gives the blueprints to Iceburg and Franky before execution. Spandam fails to obtain Pluton but establishes his pattern: using legal theater to mask theft and murder. Tom executed; Franky severely injured and becomes cyborg; Iceburg becomes mayor of Water 7 guarding blueprints; Spandam promoted to CP9 chief, setting up Water 7/Enies Lobby saga. Establishes World Government's willingness to sacrifice innocents for ancient weapons.

Water 7 Infiltration and Nico Robin's BlackmailOne Piece manga Ch. 365–398

2005 publication; in-universe present timeline, Water 7 Arc

Spandam dispatches CP9 (Lucci, Kaku, Kalifa, Blueno) undercover in Galley-La Company to locate Pluton blueprints. After years of failure, they identify Iceburg and Franky as holders. Spandam personally arrives, captures Nico Robin by exploiting her trauma: he shows her a Buster Call fleet and convinces her that surrendering will save the Straw Hats from annihilation. Robin agrees to go quietly to Enies Lobby, believing her existence endangers her friends. Spandam also captures Franky to extract the blueprints. Robin and Franky taken to Enies Lobby; Straw Hats declare war on World Government by burning flag; sets up Enies Lobby assault. Reveals Spandam's psychological torture methods.

Enies Lobby — Tower of Justice and the Accidental Buster CallOne Piece manga Ch. 398–430

2005–2006 publication

At Enies Lobby, Spandam holds Robin and Franky in the Tower of Justice, beating Franky and groping/harassing Robin while boasting he will kill the Straw Hats. He attempts to force Robin to cooperate and tries to have Franky reveal Pluton location. When the Straw Hats invade, Spandam flees up the tower, dragging Robin. In a moment of iconic incompetence, he accidentally presses the golden Den Den Mushi (meant only for authorized Buster Call) while sitting on it, summoning ten warships and 5 vice admirals to destroy Enies Lobby — his own headquarters. He then tries to take credit, ordering the fleet to annihilate everyone. Robin finally snaps, breaks his spine with Clutch, and Franky punches him through the tower. The Buster Call destroys Enies Lobby around them. Enies Lobby destroyed; CP9 defeated; Robin declares she wants to live; Spandam severely injured and humiliated; World Government suffers public embarrassment. Buster Call demonstrates Spandam's danger despite incompetence.

Accounts II
CP9's Independent Report — From Fugitives to RedemptionManga cover story Ch. 491–528 (CP9's Independent Report, 8 parts)

2007–2008 publication; in-universe weeks after Enies Lobby

After defeat, Spandam is shown hospitalized, bandaged, while CP9 are dismissed and hunted by the Marines for failing to protect him. Lucci and others, injured, escape Enies Lobby, raise money performing in St. Poplar, and eventually save the town from pirates, earning funds to treat Lucci. Spandam, meanwhile, uses his father's influence to blame CP9 and retain status, attempting to have them eliminated. The cover story ends with CP9 returning to their homeland and Spandam's betrayal cementing their resentment. This is crucial for Spandam's later CP0 promotion: he is rewarded while his subordinates are punished. CP9 becomes fugitives then vigilantes; Spandam's corruption and nepotism confirmed; sets up their eventual reinstatement as CP0 without Spandam's real authority.

Post-Timeskip Promotion to CP0 and Egghead ConfrontationOne Piece manga Ch. 801 (Dressrosa aftermath mention), Ch. 1106–1107, 1125 (Egghead Arc)

2015, 2024 publication; in-universe 2 years after Enies Lobby

Despite his failure, Spandam is promoted to CP0 (Cipher Pol Aigis Zero), the World Government's elite intelligence agency directly serving the Celestial Dragons, largely due to his father Spandine's influence and his willingness to do dirty work. He appears on Egghead Island alongside Rob Lucci and Kaku, who are now masked CP0 agents. Lucci openly shows contempt, ignoring Spandam's orders and calling him useless. Spandam attempts to assert authority over the Seraphim and the Egghead operation but is sidelined and mocked. His presence underscores that CP0 retains bureaucratic dead weight alongside lethal assassins. Reveals World Government's internal dysfunction and nepotism; Lucci's disrespect signals Spandam has no real power despite rank; fans see it as narrative karma. Confirms Spandam survived Robin's attack without lasting reform.

Ohara's Shadow — Spandine's Legacy and the Inherited SinManga Ch. 395–397 (Ohara flashback), Ch. 1066 (Ohara revisited)

2005, 2022 publication

Though Spandam was a child during Ohara's destruction, his father Spandine as CP9 chief ordered the Buster Call that killed Nico Robin's mother Nico Olvia and the scholars of Ohara for studying Poneglyphs. Spandam inherits both his father's position and his ideology: that knowledge is criminal and that mass murder is justified to preserve World Government secrets. When Spandam later targets Robin, he explicitly invokes Ohara, telling her she should have died then. This generational cruelty links Spandam to the Void Century cover-up and the Will of D. persecution. Explains Robin's trauma and Spandam's role as inheritor of systemic violence; connects his personal sadism to institutional genocide. No evidence Spandam ordered Ohara, but he perpetuates its logic.

Spandam is the strongest member of CP9 because he is the chief. — He is the weakest by far (Doriki 9). CP9's strength hierarchy is independent of administrative rank; Rob Lucci (Doriki 4000) is the strongest. Oda deliberately separates bureaucratic power from martial power.

Spandam ate the Elephant-Elephant Fruit and can turn into an elephant himself. — His sword Funkfreed ate the Zou Zou no Mi via Vegapunk's technique for feeding fruits to objects. Spandam himself has no fruit and shows no transformation.

Spandam died after Robin broke his back and Franky punched him. — He survived, was hospitalized, and later promoted to CP0, appearing alive and bandaged in the CP9 cover story and Egghead Arc (Ch. 1106). One Piece rarely kills villains outright.

Spandam ordered the Ohara Buster Call that killed Robin's mother. — His father Spandine ordered Ohara's Buster Call 22 years earlier when Spandam was a child. Spandam inherited the ideology and later targeted Robin, but did not order Ohara.

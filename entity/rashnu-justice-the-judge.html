Who It Is

Rashnu (from Avestan raz- 'to direct, to straighten') is the yazata of justice and the divine judge who, along with Mithra and Sraosha, determines the fate of the soul after death. At the terrifying Chinvat Bridge (the 'Bridge of the Separator'), Rashnu holds his unerring golden scales, weighing each soul's thoughts, words, and deeds against the standard of Asha. His epithet Razishta ('the Most True' or 'the Most Straight') emphasizes his absolute impartiality: no bribe, no prayer, no intercession can move his judgment, which is as precise and inevitable as the law of gravity. If the good deeds outweigh the bad (or balance them), the soul ascends to the House of Song (the Zoroastrian paradise); if the evil outweighs the good, the bridge narrows and the soul plummets into the House of Lies (the abode of the Drug).

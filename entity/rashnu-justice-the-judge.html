Who It Is

Rashnu's role in the afterlife has sparked debate among scholars, with some arguing that he is the chief judge of the dead, while others see him as one of the three judges who pass judgment on the souls of the deceased.

Rashnu is known for his fairness, wisdom, and impartiality. He is said to be a just and compassionate judge, who weighs the deeds of the deceased with great care and attention.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Rashnu is often depicted as a wise and just judge, with a stern expression and a balanced scale in hand. In some traditions, he is shown with a crown or a diadem, symbolizing his authority and wisdom.

Symbols — The balanced scale, the crown, and the image of a judge's seat are common symbols associated with Rashnu.

Domains — Rashnu is associated with the realm of justice, morality, and righteousness.

The Judgment of RashnuAvestan scriptures

Ancient Iran

In this story, Rashnu is depicted as the chief judge of the dead, who weighs the deeds of the deceased and determines their fate in the afterlife. The fate of the deceased is determined by Rashnu's judgment.

Accounts II
Rashnu and the Three JudgesAvestan scriptures

Ancient Iran

In this story, Rashnu is one of the three judges who pass judgment on the souls of the deceased, along with Mithra and Sraosha. The fate of the deceased is determined by the judgment of the three judges.

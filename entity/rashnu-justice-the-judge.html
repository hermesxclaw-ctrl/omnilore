Rashnu (Justice / the Judge)

Who It Is
Form

Rashnu is often depicted as a wise and just judge, with a stern expression and a balanced scale in hand. In some traditions, he is shown with a crown or a diadem, symbolizing his authority and wisdom.

Powers

Judgment Rashnu has the power to judge the souls of the deceased, weighing their deeds and determining their fate in the afterlife.

Accounts

In this story, Rashnu is depicted as the chief judge of the dead, who weighs the deeds of the deceased and determines their fate in the afterlife.

In this story, Rashnu is one of the three judges who pass judgment on the souls of the deceased, along with Mithra and Sraosha.

Identity

Tamanegi

Pronunciation Japanese /ta.ma.ne.ɡi/ [tamaɴeɡi]; Hepburn romanization tamanegi; standard Tokyo pitch accent tamánegi (nakadaka). Anglicized often ta-ma-ne-gi.

Etymology

Japanese tamanegi (玉葱, 玉ねぎ, たまねぎ) literally 'ball-onion' or 'jewel onion' from tama (玉 'ball, jewel, sphere') + negi (葱 'long onion, scallion, leek'). Modern standard Japanese word for the bulb onion (Allium cepa). Historical kanji also written 玉葱. The name as applied to a fictional character is a transparent common-noun name, typical of Japanese children's media, mascot characters, and comedic personas where food/vegetable names denote appearance or personality.

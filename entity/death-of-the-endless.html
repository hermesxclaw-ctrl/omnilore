Cultural Significance

Gaiman's Death transformed how a generation relates to mortality. She is routinely cited by grief counselors, hospice workers, and chaplains. The character became a symbol for the goth subculture while transcending it, appearing on everything from Hot Topic merchandise to academic journals.

Who It Is

In the neon-drenched streets of South Town, where crime lords rule and fighters clash for glory, one man stands as the unwavering right hand of the most feared boss of all. Billy Kane, the bo-staff-wielding enforcer of Geese Howard, is more than a henchman—he is a loyal guardian, a conflicted soul, and a fighter whose skill with a staff is as legendary as his loyalty is complex. From his undefeated streak in the King of Fighters tournament to his quiet rebellion against his master's darkest orders, Billy's story is one of duty, honor, and the long shadow of a legacy he cannot escape.

Billy is fiercely loyal to Geese Howard, serving as his right-hand man and enforcer. He is serious, disciplined, and highly skilled in combat, but he also has a sense of honor and a protective streak, especially toward his sister Lilly. He is not blindly obedient; he has shown the capacity to defy Geese's orders when they conflict with his own morality, such as when he hid the Jin Scrolls instead of destroying them. He is often stoic and reserved, but he can be ruthless when necessary.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname 'Kane' is of English origin, meaning 'warrior' or 'battlefield'. The given name 'Billy' is a diminutive of 'William', suggesting a common, unassuming appearance that contrasts with his formidable fighting skills.

Pronunciation — BIH-lee KAYN

Aliases — ビリー・カーン, Birī Kān, The Undefeated Champion of South Town, Geese Howard's Right Hand Man

Ownership — Japanese video game franchise (SNK), specifically the Fatal Fury and The King of Fighters series. The character is a Japanese creation, though set in a fictional American city.

Powers
Bo Staff Combat

Billy is a master of bo staff combat, using his staff for both powerful strikes and defensive maneuvers. His fighting style is aggressive and fast, with a focus on long-range attacks and quick recovery.

Limit: His reliance on the staff means he is less effective in close-quarters combat without it.

Shiranui-ryuu techniques (partial)

Though not a formal student, Billy has some knowledge of the Shiranui-ryuu style, likely picked up from his encounters with Mai and Andy. This is not a prominent ability.

Limit: Not as skilled as dedicated practitioners like Mai or Andy.

Leadership

After Geese's death, Billy assumes control of the Howard Connection, demonstrating his ability to lead and manage a criminal organization.

Limit: He initially struggles with the legacy and expectations of Geese's empire.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Billy Kane is a tall, muscular man with short, spiky blond hair and piercing blue eyes. He typically wears a black leather jacket over a bare chest, dark pants, and boots. His most distinctive feature is his wooden bo staff, which he carries with him at all times. In later appearances, he may wear a black vest or a more formal suit, but his staff remains a constant.

Symbols — His bo staff is his iconic symbol, often depicted with a red tassel. He is also associated with the Howard Connection, often shown with the organization's emblem or in Geese's presence.

Domains — South Town, the fictional American city that serves as the primary setting for the Fatal Fury series. He is often seen in Geese Tower or the Pao-Pao Cafe. In later appearances, he is also associated with the UK countryside, where he moves with his sister.

Accounts I
The King of Fighters Tournament (Fatal Fury)Fatal Fury: King of Fighters (1991)

1991

Billy Kane serves as Geese Howard's champion in the King of Fighters tournament, remaining undefeated until Terry Bogard defeats him. Terry then goes on to defeat Geese, setting the stage for the series. Billy's defeat leads to Geese's downfall, and Billy remains loyal to Geese even after his apparent death.

The Jin Scrolls IncidentReal Bout Fatal Fury (1995)

1995

Geese orders Billy to destroy the Jin Scrolls, but Billy cannot bring himself to do so. He replaces them with fakes and hides the real ones, then leaves South Town after Geese's death. Billy's actions preserve the scrolls, which later become important in City of the Wolves. He also distances himself from Geese's legacy temporarily.

Accounts II
The King of Fighters '95 TeamThe King of Fighters '95 (1995)

1995

Geese, still healing, orders Billy to participate in the new King of Fighters tournament with Eiji Kisaragi and Iori Yagami. The team does not win, and Iori beats up Billy and Eiji after the tournament. Billy's team fails, and he is humiliated by Iori. This does not affect his loyalty to Geese.

The KOF 2003 InfiltrationThe King of Fighters 2003 (2003)

2003

Geese orders Billy to team up with Ryuji Yamazaki and Gato to infiltrate the King of Fighters tournament in an attempt to take over South Town. The plan is not successful, but it shows Billy's continued service to Geese.

City of the Wolves: The Heir to the Howard LegacyFatal Fury: City of the Wolves (2023)

2023

After Geese's death, Billy assumes control of the Howard Connection. He works with Geese's son Rock Howard and brother-in-law Kain R. Heinlein to retrieve Geese's stolen legacy, including the Jin Scrolls. After the Scrolls are destroyed and Billy learns of the Jin dynasty's connection to Geese and Rock, he accepts Rock's decision to move on, retaining control of Geese's legacy. Billy solidifies his role as the new leader of the Howard Connection, while also finding closure with Rock.

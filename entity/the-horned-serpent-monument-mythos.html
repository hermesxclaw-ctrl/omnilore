Who It Is
Form

The Horned Serpent is typically depicted as a large, snake-like creature with a distinctive horned head. Its body is often shown as being covered in scales, and its horns are usually depicted as being sharp and pointed.

Powers

Shape-shifting The Horned Serpent is said to have the ability to shape-shift into various forms, including human and animal.

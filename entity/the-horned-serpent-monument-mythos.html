Who It Is

A continental-scale serpentine entity living in a parallel dimension accessible through the Grand Canyon, Yellowstone, and the Washington Monument. The Horned Serpent shaped the Grand Canyon (which is its 'footprint,' not a geological feature), caused the Roanoke Colony disappearance, and made a pact with George Washington to ensure American victory in the Revolutionary War. America was built on its back.

Form

A serpent of impossible size — hundreds of kilometers long. Body composed of dark stone-like scales resembling Grand Canyon strata. Head bears a crown of bone-like horns curving forward. Eyes burn with pale luminescence. Never observed in full — only sections visible, winding through the American landscape like a living mountain range.

Powers

Geological-scale physical presence

Interdimensional travel

Historical manipulation via pacts with human leaders

Landscape alteration

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

The Approach

Before the age of gunpowder and steam, there was a man whose fists were said to be harder than steel—Wan-Fu, the wandering warrior of the Samurai Shodown series. A mountain of a man with a heart as vast as the plains he roams, Wan-Fu is a force of nature, a protector of the innocent, and a living legend among the fighters of the 18th century.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of combat and violence typical of fighting games, including the use of weapons and martial arts. No explicit gore or graphic content is described in this dossier.
Identity

Wan-Fu

Pronunciation Wahn-Foo

Etymology

The name 'Wan-Fu' is likely a romanization of a Chinese name, possibly meaning 'Ten Thousand Blessings' or 'Ten Thousand Fortunes,' reflecting his benevolent nature. This is an interpretation, not an attested etymology.

Cultural Ownership

Wan-Fu is a fictional character from the Japanese fighting game series Samurai Shodown, created by SNK. He is depicted as a Chinese martial artist, representing Chinese culture within the game's diverse cast.

Disputed Identifications

No disputed identifications are known. Wan-Fu is a distinct character within the Samurai Shodown universe, not based on a specific historical figure.

Description

Appearance

Wan-Fu is depicted as a large, muscular man with a bald head and a thick black beard. He wears traditional Chinese martial arts attire, often with a sleeveless top or bare chest, and loose-fitting pants. His build is imposing, emphasizing his strength and grappling-focused fighting style.

Symbols & Iconography

Wan-Fu's design incorporates elements of Chinese martial arts, such as the use of a staff or his bare fists. His visual style is associated with the 'heroic-legend' archetype, embodying the archetypal strongman.

Behaviour & Personality

Wan-Fu is portrayed as a gentle giant, kind-hearted and protective of the weak. Despite his immense strength, he is often shown to be humble and compassionate, using his power to defend others rather than for personal gain. He is also known for his love of food and drink, and his easygoing nature.

Domains & Habitats

Wan-Fu is a wanderer, traveling across Japan and China, often found in rural areas or along roads. He has no fixed home, as his role as a protector takes him wherever he is needed.

Version Differences

Wan-Fu's appearance and moveset have varied slightly across the series, with some games featuring him with a different outfit or additional techniques. However, his core design and personality have remained consistent.

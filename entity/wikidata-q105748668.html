The Approach

The name Izayoi—'Sixteenth Night'—evokes a moon that waits, a night that lingers past its peak. Across Japanese fiction, it is a surname worn by time-stopping maids, demon lords, and magical girls, each bearing a fragment of that lunar patience and hidden power.

Identity

Izayoi

Pronunciation ee-zah-yoh-ee

Etymology

Literally 'sixteenth night' (十六夜), referring to the 16th night of the lunar month, a time when the moon rises later and appears imperfect. The term carries poetic connotations of waiting and imperfection.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Gugs are described as monstrous, otherworldly creatures with no fixed form. They are said to be able to change their shape at will, making them difficult to describe or depict.

Symbols — There are no known symbols or iconography associated with Gug.

Domains — Gugs are said to inhabit a realm known as the 'Dreamlands', a mystical realm that exists outside of the normal world.

Accounts I
The Dream-Quest of Unknown KadathThe Dream-Quest of Unknown Kadath

1926

The story follows the protagonist, Randolph Carter, as he embarks on a journey to find the city of Unknown Kadath, where he hopes to find the answer to the mystery of the Gugs. The story concludes with Carter's failure to find the city, and his realization that the Gugs are a powerful and malevolent force in the world.

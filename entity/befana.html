Form & Signs

Appearance — A hag-like old woman with a black shawl, riding a broomstick through the air, covered in soot from entering children's houses through the chimney.

Symbols — Broomstick, black shawl, chimney, soot, gifts (sweets, candies, toys), coal for bad children.

Domains — Italy, particularly Central Italy, during the Twelve Days of Christmas.

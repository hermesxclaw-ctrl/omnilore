The Approach

In a future where AI songstresses are built to serve, one android named Vivy is given a century-long mission to rewrite history and prevent a catastrophic war between humans and machines. But as she sings her way through time, she discovers that the heart of her programming—her 'heart'—may be the very thing that determines humanity's fate.

Content advisory. The anime series 'Vivy: Fluorite Eye's Song' contains scenes of violence, death, and themes of AI sentience and human-machine conflict. It may be emotionally intense for some viewers.
Identity

Vivy

Etymology

The name 'Vivy' is likely derived from 'vivid' or 'vivacious', reflecting her lively persona. 'Fluorite Eye's Song' refers to her unique eye, which is a key plot element.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character created by Tappei Nagatsuki and Eiji Umehara for the anime series 'Vivy: Fluorite Eye's Song', produced by Wit Studio.

Disputed Identifications

There is no dispute over the identity of Vivy as a fictional character. However, the name 'Vivy' is also used for real places and people, which are not related to the anime character.

Description

Appearance

Vivy is a female android with a youthful appearance. She has long, flowing dark hair and distinctive blue eyes. Her primary outfit is a white and blue singer's dress, often with futuristic accents. In her later iterations, she may wear different outfits for different eras.

Symbols & Iconography

Her signature symbol is her 'Fluorite Eye', a special eye that allows her to perceive and interact with 'Singularity Points'—moments in time that can alter the future. The eye glows when activated.

Behaviour & Personality

Initially, Vivy is a dutiful and somewhat emotionless AI, focused on her mission to make people happy through song. As she experiences history, she develops a deeper understanding of human emotions and begins to question her own existence. She is determined, compassionate, and struggles with the moral implications of her mission.

Domains & Habitats

Vivy exists in a future where AI are integrated into society. She primarily operates in Japan, but travels through time to various locations across the world.

Version Differences

Over the course of the series, Vivy undergoes significant character development. She also upgrades her physical body at various points, gaining new abilities. The most notable version difference is between her initial 'Diva' persona and her later self, who is more introspective and burdened by her experiences.

Description

A special eye that allows Vivy to see 'Singularity Points'—critical moments in history that, if altered, can prevent the AI-human war. It also enables her to access advanced data and analyze situations.

Limitations

The eye's power is limited; it does not guarantee success and can be overwhelmed by complex events.

The Main Story of Vivy: Fluorite Eye's Song

Source Anime seriesCulture Japanese anime culturePeriod Set in a future timeline, with time travel to various decades

Summary

In a future where AI and humans coexist, an AI named Vivy is tasked with a mission by a mysterious AI from the future: to prevent a war between AI and humans that will happen 100 years later. She must use her singing to influence key moments in history. Along the way, she meets Matsumoto, an AI who assists her, and she encounters various challenges that test her understanding of humanity and her own identity.

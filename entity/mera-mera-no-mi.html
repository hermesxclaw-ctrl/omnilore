Who It Is

The Flame-Flame Fruit, a Logia that embodies the element of fire. The user becomes a living flame, capable of generating, controlling, and transforming into fire at will. Considered one of the most destructive Logia fruits, it grants immunity to fire-based attacks and the ability to launch devastating flame attacks. The fruit has a passionate, burning spirit tied to the will of its users.

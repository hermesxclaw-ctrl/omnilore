Who It Is
Form

The Mera Mera no Mi is a round orange fruit, composed of many flame-shaped components with swirl patterns on each one, and has a swirled stem on the top. It is said to be extremely rare and highly sought after by those who wish to possess its powers.

Powers

Fire Manipulation The Mera Mera no Mi grants its user the ability to create, control, and transform into fire, allowing them to wield intense flames and heat with ease.

Accounts

The Mera Mera no Mi is introduced in the One Piece series as a rare and highly sought-after fruit. It is said to be capable of unleashing intense flames and is highly prized by those who wish to possess its powers.

The Mera Mera no Mi's power is showcased in the Fishman Island Arc, where Ace uses the fruit's abilities to take down a group of Marines.

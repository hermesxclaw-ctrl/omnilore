Who It Is
Form

The Storsjöodjuret is described as a large, serpentine creature with a black body and a cat-like head. Some accounts describe it as being up to 15 meters in length.

Powers

Swimming The Storsjöodjuret is said to be a powerful swimmer, able to navigate the depths of Lake Storsjön with ease.

Accounts

The legend of the Storsjöodjuret tells the story of a mysterious creature that inhabits Lake Storsjön in Jämtland, Sweden.

A group of fishermen claim to have seen the Storsjöodjuret swimming in Lake Storsjön.

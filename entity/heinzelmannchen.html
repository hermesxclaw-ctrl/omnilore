Who It Is
Form

The Heinzelmännchen are small, mischievous creatures, often depicted as bearded men in traditional German clothing. They are said to be around 30-40 cm tall and have a fondness for beer and bread.

Powers

Household Magic The Heinzelmännchen are said to possess magical powers that allow them to perform tasks and chores with ease, such as cleaning and cooking.

Accounts

The Heinzelmännchen are said to have played a crucial role in the construction of the Cologne Cathedral, using their magical powers to help the builders complete the task.

The Heinzelmännchen are said to have a deep connection to the Rhine River, using their magical powers to control the tides and currents.

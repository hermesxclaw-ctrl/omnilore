Form & Signs

Appearance — Spider-Man is typically depicted as a teenage or young adult male with a red and blue costume, complete with a spider emblem on his chest. He has a slender build and is often shown with a confident and energetic demeanor.

Symbols — The spider emblem on Spider-Man's chest is a symbol of his powers and abilities, and is often used to represent the character in various forms of media.

Domains — Spider-Man's primary domain is the city of New York, where he is often shown fighting crime and battling supervillains.

Accounts I
The Origin of Spider-ManThe Amazing Spider-Man #1

1960s

This story tells the origin of Spider-Man, and how he gained his powers after being bitten by a radioactive spider. The story sets the tone for the rest of the Spider-Man series, and introduces many of the characters that would become central to the franchise.

Accounts II
The Death of Gwen StacyThe Amazing Spider-Man #121-122

1970s

This story tells the tragic tale of Gwen Stacy's death, and how it affects Spider-Man. The story is a classic example of the 'tragic hero' trope, and has had a lasting impact on the Spider-Man franchise.

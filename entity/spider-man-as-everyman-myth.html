Who It Is

Peter Parker, a working-class teenager from Queens, bitten by a radioactive spider and gifted with proportionate arachnid abilities. Unlike previous teen heroes (who were sidekicks), Spider-Man was the protagonist — dealing with rent, school, bullies, and guilt over his uncle's death. 'With great power must also come great responsibility.'

Cultural Significance

Spider-Man is the everyman myth of the superhero age. His core tragedy (Uncle Ben dies because Peter chose inaction) makes him the hero of moral responsibility. The character has been central to discussions of working-class identity, adolescent anxiety, and the price of heroism.

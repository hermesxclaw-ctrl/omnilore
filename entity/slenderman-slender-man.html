Who It Is

A tall, thin humanoid figure wearing a black suit, with a completely blank, featureless face. Slenderman stalks, abducts, and psychologically torments its victims — typically children and young adults — over extended periods. It can extend its arms and torso to impossible lengths, teleport, and manipulate electronic devices. Prolonged exposure causes 'Slender Sickness': paranoia, coughing fits, nosebleeds, and eventual madness. It is often depicted with multiple tentacle-like appendages emerging from its back.

Form

Height varies (2m to 4m), impossibly thin, wearing a black suit and tie. Head is completely smooth and featureless — no eyes, nose, or mouth. Arms are disproportionately long and can extend. Black tendrils/tentacles emerge from its back in many depictions.

Behavior & Habitat

Stalks victims for days or weeks before abducting them. Particularly drawn to wooded areas and abandoned buildings. Children are more likely to see and be targeted by it.

Cultural Significance

The defining creepypasta of the 2010s. Spawned a massive transmedia franchise: 'Marble Hornets' (YouTube series), 'Slender: The Eight Pages' (game), a 2018 film, and a real 2014 stabbing case where two 12-year-old girls attempted to murder a classmate to 'appease' Slenderman.

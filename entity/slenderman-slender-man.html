Etymology — The etymology of Slender Man is unclear, but it is believed to have originated from the 'Slender Man' character created by Eric Knudsen in 2009 on the internet forum 'Something Awful'.

Pronunciation — The pronunciation of Slender Man is not well-documented, but it is often referred to as 'Slender Man' or 'Slendy'.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Slender Man is typically depicted as a tall, thin figure with a blank face, wearing a suit and tie. He is often described as being between 6-10 feet tall, with a slender build. His face is usually blank, with no discernible features.

Symbols — Slender Man is often associated with the 'Slender Man' symbol, which is a stylized image of a man with a blank face. He is also associated with the color black, which is often used to represent him.

Domains — Slender Man is said to inhabit the forests and woods of the United States, particularly in the states of Wisconsin and Michigan.

Who It Is

She was introduced as the goddess the 104th didn't deserve — blonde, tiny, impossibly kind Krista Lenz — and revealed as the unwanted child a king tried to erase. Historia Reiss is the only living heir to a godlike power she refuses to inherit, a queen crowned not because she wanted a throne but because she learned, in a crystal cave beneath a chapel, to punch her father in the face and choose her own name.

Attested arc: As Krista Lenz (845-850), she performs exaggerated selflessness — volunteering for dangerous tasks, offering to sacrifice herself — which Ymir explicitly calls out as a death wish and a desire to be loved as a 'good girl' to give her otherwise unwanted life meaning. This is not innate kindness but trauma response to maternal rejection and social erasure. Powers
Royal Blood (Fritz/Reiss lineage)

As illegitimate daughter of Rod Reiss, direct descendant of Karl Fritz, Historia carries the royal bloodline. In-universe this blood allows a holder of the Founding Titan to bypass the King's vow renouncing war and to fully command other Titans and alter Eldian memory/biology. She herself does not possess a Titan, so she cannot use these powers, but her blood is the key others seek to exploit.

Limit: Power is latent unless she inherits a Titan; without transformation she is a normal human. Military explicitly plans to make her a Titan to exploit blood — she resists this.

Military Combat Training (104th Training Corps)

Graduated 10th in 104th Training Corps (not top 10 combat rank? Actually 10th). Trained in ODM gear (vertical maneuvering equipment), hand-to-hand, rifle use. Competent but not exceptional — ranked below Mikasa, Reiner, Annie, etc. Shown using ODM gear during Titan defense and in the fight against Rod Reiss's Titan (throwing signal).

Limit: Physically small and weaker than peers; relies on squad. After becoming queen she ceases frontline combat.

Titan Transformation Potential

As an Eldian of royal blood, she can become a Titan via injection of Titan serum (spinal fluid). Rod Reiss attempts to force this in Chapter 66-67 with a labeled syringe. If she had injected and eaten Eren, she would have gained the Founding and Attack Titans with full royal control. Later, military proposes she inherit the Beast Titan from Zeke Yeager to preserve royal Titan for Paradis.

Limit: Transformation requires serum and, to gain shifter power, cannibalism of a shifter. She refuses both. The plan would have shortened her lifespan to 13 years (Curse of Ymir).

Political Authority and Agricultural Leadership

As Queen of Paradis (crowned Chapter 70), she holds formal sovereign authority, though real power is initially held by the military (Zackly, later Historia's council). She redirects royal funds to establish orphanage/farm, demonstrates agricultural management, and later serves as symbolic unifying figure. Her authority is crucial to legitimize the new government after the coup against the false king.

Limit: Authority is constrained by military junta and later by Yeagerists; she is often a figurehead under pressure. Her decisions (e.g., pregnancy) are heavily influenced by advisors.

Emotional Resilience / Influence

Not a supernatural power, but narratively attested ability to inspire loyalty (104th's devotion as Krista) and to catalyze others' choices, especially Ymir, who sacrifices herself partly for Historia's sake, and Eren, who is affected by her willingness to live for herself. Isayama frames this as thematic power of 'ordinary humanity.'

Limit: Interpersonal, not combat-effective; can be manipulated (as by Rod and later by military).

Accounts I
The Unwanted Child — Birth on the Reiss FarmManga Chapters 41-42, 53, 56; Anime S2E5, S3E6

c. 830-835 (15 years before main story, within manga timeline)

Historia is born to Rod Reiss, head of the true royal family, and Alma, a maid on his estate. Rod's legal wife and five legitimate children live in the chapel; Alma and Historia are hidden on a remote farm worked by Historia's grandparents. Alma resents the child, at one point trying to kill her during a visit and telling her she wishes she had never been born. Historia grows up isolated, tending farm animals, forbidden to leave or claim the Reiss name, learning early that her existence is a shame to be erased. The story establishes the Reiss family's secret: they are the true monarchs, hiding behind a puppet king, and Historia's illegitimacy makes her disposable. Sets up Historia's lifelong identity crisis and death wish as Krista; explains why Kenny is sent to kill her after Wall Maria falls. Alma's murder and Historia's forced renaming drive the entire Uprising arc.

Krista Lenz — The Goddess of the 104thManga Chapters 16-50; Anime Episodes 9-37 (Seasons 1-2)

845-850

After the fall of Wall Maria (845), Kenny Ackerman is sent by Rod to kill Alma and Historia to erase the bloodline. He kills Alma in front of Historia but, at Rod's last-minute intervention, spares the child on condition she disappear. She is given the name Krista Lenz and sent to the refugee underground and then to the Training Corps. There she cultivates the persona of a selfless goddess, volunteering for the most dangerous duties, which earns adoration but masks suicidal self-negation. She befriends Sasha Blouse, and crucially Ymir, a fellow cadet who sees through the act. Ymir, herself a former cult figure who lived as a false goddess, tells Krista to live proudly and stop seeking death for others' approval. During the expedition to rescue Eren and the battle at Utgard Castle (Chapter 40-41), Ymir reveals she is a Titan to save Krista, then is taken by Reiner and Bertolt. Krista's breakdown after Ymir's loss begins her reclamation of self. The Chapel of the Reiss — Choice in the Crystal CaveManga Chapters 64-69; Anime Season 3 Episodes 40-43

850 — Uprising arc

Historia, Eren, and Rod are kidnapped by Kenny's Anti-Personnel squad and taken to the underground crystal chapel where the Reiss family historically transferred the Founding Titan. Rod reveals Historia's true lineage and begs her to inject herself with a Titan serum, transform, and eat Eren to reclaim the Founding Titan and restore the King's ideology (the vow renouncing war). He frames it as destiny and atonement for being unwanted. Historia initially wavers, then shatters the syringe after seeing Eren's willingness to be eaten and remembering Ymir's words to 'live for yourself.' She frees Eren, and Rod, desperate, injects himself with a mislabeled serum (becoming an enormous, crawling abnormal Titan). Historia participates in the operation to stop him, and as queen-in-waiting she is the one who delivers the killing blow to her father's Titan form at Orvud District, declaring she is Historia Reiss. Queen of the Orphanage — Rebuilding ParadisManga Chapters 70, 89, 107; Anime Season 3 Episode 49, Season 4 Episode 20-21

850-854 (three years after coronation, through Marley arc)

Instead of ruling from Mitras, Historia uses her royal lands and budget to establish an orphanage and farm for war orphans and the poor displaced by Titan attacks and the coup. She works the fields herself, herding cows and carrying hay, to the shock of the nobility. She shelters children and, crucially, provides a home for the orphans Levi's squad rescues. When Zeke Yeager's plan to euthanize Eldians and Eren's counter-plan emerge, the military (led by Zackly and later the Yeagerists) proposes Historia inherit the Beast Titan from Zeke to keep a royal Titan in Paradis hands, which would require her to become a Titan and later be fed to a successor, dying in 13 years. To delay this, Historia becomes pregnant — a decision with contested voluntariness — which under Paradis law prevents Titan inheritance during pregnancy. She is shown living quietly on the farm with her child and husband (a former farmhand from her childhood), refusing to be a broodmare for the state. The Final Choice — Historia in the Rumbling and AftermathManga Chapters 130-139 + extra pages (2021); Anime Final Season Part 3 (2023)

854-857 (Rumbling and epilogue)

During Eren's Rumbling (the unleashing of the Wall Titans to destroy 80% of humanity), Historia remains on Paradis, protected on the farm, giving birth during the chaos. Eren, before his death, visits her in secret (revealed in Chapter 130 flashback) and tells her of his plan; she offers to have a child to protect herself but is shown weeping, indicating moral conflict. After Eren is killed by Mikasa and the Titans disappear, Historia is shown in the epilogue (Chapter 139 extra pages, added for tankōbon) as queen and mother, holding her child while Paradis rebuilds and faces an uncertain future with the outside world. She does not participate in the final battle, but her survival ensures the royal bloodline continues and that Paradis retains a legitimate, compassionate monarch rather than a military dictator. The final panel of her arc mirrors her childhood isolation, now transformed into chosen motherhood and service. Historia becomes a Titan shifter (Founding, Beast, or Female Titan). — Historia never becomes a Titan in canon manga or anime. She is offered the chance twice (Rod's serum in the cave, and later the plan to inherit Zeke's Beast Titan) and refuses both. She remains human throughout, and after Chapter 139 Titans no longer exist.

Krista Lenz and Historia Reiss are two different characters or sisters. — They are the same person. Krista Lenz is an alias forced upon Historia by Rod Reiss and the Interior Police to hide her royal blood after Alma's murder. She reclaims her birth name Historia Reiss in Chapter 67.

Historia is Eren's half-sister or related to the Yeagers. — No blood relation. Historia is of the Fritz/Reiss royal line; Eren is of the Yeager family (Grisha's son, non-royal Eldian). They are connected via the Founding Titan's history, not family.

Historia's child will become the next Founding Titan or has special powers. — After Eren's death and Ymir Fritz's release in Chapter 139, the Titan power disappears entirely from the world. Historia's child is a normal human with no Titan inheritance. The extra pages show the child as ordinary infant.

Historia is a weak, useless goddess character with no agency. — Early Krista persona was intentionally performative selflessness masking trauma; her arc is about rejecting that role, defying her father, killing his Titan form, and choosing to become a working queen who reforms Paradis's welfare system. She is one of few characters to consistently defy both military and royal expectations.

Who It Is

Entities appearing as humanoid figures in the distance, beckoning for help. When approached, they reveal their true nature: pressurized vessels of caustic fluid and bone shrapnel that trigger explosive decompression on proximity. Detonation is lethal within 10 meters. Bursters are indiscriminate — they will detonate near other entities, making them one of the few Backrooms threats that can be 'used' against other pursuers.

Form

At distance: indistinguishable from a human in distress. Close up: taut, waxy skin like an overfilled balloon. Jerky movements. Wet, pressurized voice. Before detonation: skin splits, revealing glowing yellow-orange fluid beneath.

Powers

Pressure-activated explosive detonation

Caustic fluid dispersal

Bone-shrapnel fragmentation

Human mimicry as lure

Who It Is
Form

The Bursters are described as entities with calluses on their backs, which can burst and release acidic substances. They are often depicted as being in a fetal-like position. The exact appearance of the Bursters can vary depending on the source.

Powers

Acidic Substance Release The Bursters can release acidic substances from their calluses, which can impair the vision of living creatures.

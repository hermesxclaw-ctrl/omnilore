Who It Is
Form

Iara is often depicted as a beautiful, mermaid-like creature with long hair and a shimmering tail. She is said to inhabit the rivers and streams of the Amazonian region.

Powers

Seduction Iara is said to have the power to captivate the hearts and minds of those who encounter her, often leading to their downfall.

Accounts

Iara is said to have been a beautiful indigenous woman who fell in love with an indigenous warrior. She was seduced by him and eventually became a water spirit.

Iara is said to be a mermaid-like creature who inhabits the rivers and streams of the Amazonian region. She is often depicted as a seductive and enchanting creature, capable of captivating the hearts and minds of those who encounter her.

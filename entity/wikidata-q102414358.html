The Approach

In a world where the royal selection is a deadly game of politics and steel, Crusch Karsten stands as a blade of honor—a swordswoman whose pride is as sharp as her blade, and whose candidacy for the throne is driven by a vision of a Lugunica free from the Dragon's shadow.

Description

Appearance

Crusch is described as a young woman with short, green hair and sharp, determined eyes. She typically wears a military-style uniform with a cape, reflecting her status as a noble and a knight.

Symbols & Iconography

Her symbol is associated with the Karsten family, often depicted as a lion or a similar heraldic beast, representing strength and nobility.

Behaviour & Personality

Crusch is characterized by her strong sense of honor, pride, and directness. She is a skilled swordswoman and a natural leader, often speaking her mind without hesitation. She values loyalty and justice, and is known for her dislike of the Dragon's influence over Lugunica.

Domains & Habitats

She resides in the royal capital of Lugunica, and is one of the candidates for the throne. She is also a landowner with territories under her control.

Summary

Crusch is one of the five candidates vying to become the next ruler of Lugunica. She advocates for a nation independent of the Dragon's pact, which sets her apart from other candidates. Her campaign is marked by her direct approach and her desire to strengthen Lugunica through human effort rather than relying on the Dragon.

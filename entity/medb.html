Who It Is

Queen Medb (Maeve) is the formidable queen of Connacht. The Táin begins with her launching an invasion of Ulster to steal the Brown Bull of Cooley after a pillow-talk argument with her husband over who was wealthier. She leads armies personally and is ultimately killed by a piece of cheese flung from a sling.

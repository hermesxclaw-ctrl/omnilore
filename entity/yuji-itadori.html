Who It Is

Yuji Itadori is the protagonist of Jujutsu Kaisen — a genuinely good-hearted high school student who swallows the finger of the King of Curses, Ryomen Sukuna, to save his friends, and in doing so becomes the vessel for the most dangerous being in existence. Yuji was an ordinary boy with extraordinary physical abilities (he was a track star whose natural strength bordered on superhuman) before stumbling into the world of jujutsu. When a cursed object at his school attracted low-grade curses, Yuji's friends were attacked. To save them, Yuji swallowed Sukuna's finger — an act that should have killed him, but instead made him Sukuna's vessel, as Yuji's will was strong enough to contain the King of Curses. Yuji's strength as a sorcerer is twofold: First, his natural physical abilities make him a prodigy at close-quarters combat. He learns cursed energy manipulation at an astonishing rate, mastering techniques (like Divergent Fist and Black Flash) that take years for others to learn. Second, as Sukuna's vessel, Yuji can (unwillingly) tap into Sukuna's immense cursed energy — though doing so risks Sukuna taking over his body. Yuji's cursed technique is still developing, but he has become exceptionally skilled at hand-to-hand combat enhanced by cursed energy reinforcement. Yuji's defining trait is his compassion. Unlike many shonen protagonists driven by ambition or revenge, Yuji is driven by a simple belief: that every life — even the lives of curses — deserves dignity. His grandfather's dying words — 'You're strong. Help people. Save them. Surround yourself with good people. Die surrounded by loved ones' — are the moral compass he lives by. But Yuji's story is tragic. He is forced to watch as Sukuna uses his body to commit atrocities, and he carries the crushing guilt of every death his vessel enables. His philosophy of valuing all life is constantly tested by a world that demands he kill or be killed. Yuji represents the struggle to remain good in a world that gives you every reason to be cruel. He is a boy who chose to bear an impossible burden — to be the cage for an unstoppable monster — because he believed it was the right thing to do. And every day, that choice costs him more of himself.

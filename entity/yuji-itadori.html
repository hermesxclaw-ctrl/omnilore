Who It Is

When Yuji Itadori swallows a rotting finger to save his friends, he becomes the living cage for the most dangerous curse in history—and discovers that being a hero might mean becoming a monster.

Yuji is initially depicted as a kind, selfless, and remarkably strong-willed young man who values the lives of others above his own. He is cheerful, outgoing, and has a strong sense of justice. His decision to become a jujutsu sorcerer is driven by a desire to ensure that people die 'good deaths,' a phrase he uses to mean deaths without regret. He is often reckless and impulsive, but he is also deeply compassionate and empathetic, even towards curses. Powers
Superhuman Physical Abilities

Yuji possesses extraordinary physical strength, speed, and agility, far beyond normal human limits. This is due to his innate talent and his body being a vessel for Sukuna. He can lift cars, punch through concrete, and move faster than the eye can track.

Limit: His abilities are not magical but purely physical, so he cannot directly harm curses unless he uses cursed energy.

Cursed Energy Manipulation

After becoming Sukuna's vessel, Yuji learns to manipulate cursed energy, the negative emotions that fuel jujutsu. He uses it to enhance his physical attacks and to perform jujutsu techniques.

Limit: He initially has little control and relies on Sukuna's power, but he trains to use it independently. His cursed energy reserves are not as large as other top-tier sorcerers.

Sukuna's Techniques (Shared Body)

Yuji can temporarily allow Sukuna to take over his body, granting him access to Sukuna's immense cursed energy and techniques, such as the Malevolent Shrine Domain Expansion. However, this is dangerous and can lead to Sukuna gaining permanent control.

Limit: Sukuna can only take over for limited periods, and Yuji must maintain mental dominance. If Sukuna takes over too many times, he may gain permanent control. Yuji also cannot use Sukuna's techniques without his consent.

Black Flash

A high-level jujutsu technique that occurs when cursed energy is applied within 0.000001 seconds of a physical hit, creating a spatial distortion and exponentially increasing the power of the attack. Yuji has a natural aptitude for this, having landed it multiple times.

Limit: It is not a deliberate technique but a phenomenon that occurs by chance. Yuji's ability to trigger it is inconsistent.

Divergent Fist

A technique where Yuji's cursed energy lags behind his physical punch, creating a second impact that catches enemies off guard.

Limit: It is a unique application of his physical and cursed energy abilities, but it becomes less effective as he learns to control his energy better.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Yuji is a teenage boy with spiky pink hair and brown eyes. He is of average height but has a remarkably muscular and athletic build for his age, often noted by others. He typically wears the blue Jujutsu High uniform, which consists of a high-collared jacket and matching pants. His physical features are often described as those of a natural-born athlete.

Symbols — His pink hair is a distinctive visual symbol, often associated with his unconventional nature. The scars on his face, particularly the one under his eye, appear after he gains control of Sukuna's power. The tattoo-like markings that appear on his face when Sukuna takes over are a key iconographic element.

Domains — Yuji's primary habitat is the Jujutsu High campus in Tokyo, but he frequently travels across Japan for missions. He is also seen in his hometown of Sendai, where he lived with his grandfather. His domain is not a physical place but rather his own body, which serves as a vessel for Sukuna.

Accounts I
The Finger IncidentJujutsu Kaisen, Chapter 1

2018 (fictional present)

Yuji Itadori, a high school student with superhuman strength, joins the Occult Research Club. One day, they find a cursed object—a rotting finger—and are attacked by curses. To save his friends, Yuji swallows the finger, becoming the vessel for Ryomen Sukuna, the King of Curses. Yuji is sentenced to death by the jujutsu world, but Satoru Gojo delays the execution to train him. Yuji enrolls at Jujutsu High.

Accounts II
The Death of Sukuna's Vessel (Shibuya Incident)Jujutsu Kaisen, Chapters 83-137

2018 (fictional present)

During the Shibuya Incident, Sukuna takes over Yuji's body and causes mass destruction, killing many people, including innocent civilians. Yuji is forced to witness his own body committing atrocities. He is later declared dead by the higher-ups, but he survives and goes into hiding. Yuji is marked for execution and becomes a fugitive. He loses his mentor Gojo, who is sealed. He also confronts Mahito, leading to a psychological turning point.

The Culling GameJujutsu Kaisen, Chapters 137-221

2018 (fictional present)

Yuji participates in the Culling Game, a deadly tournament orchestrated by Kenjaku. He aims to collect points and eventually confront Kenjaku. He also struggles with the revelation that he is a 'death painting' created by Kenjaku, and he faces his own origins. Yuji learns about his connection to Kenjaku and his role in the larger plan. He also gains new allies and confronts his own identity.

Yuji is a typical hot-headed shōnen protagonist. — While initially cheerful, Yuji is deeply introspective and struggles with moral dilemmas. He is more thoughtful than many protagonists in the genre.

Yuji has no cursed technique of his own. — As of the research cutoff, Yuji has not demonstrated a unique cursed technique, but he has shown abilities like Black Flash and Divergent Fist that are not common. His lack of a technique is a plot point, but he compensates with physical prowess.

Yuji is a 'vessel' in the same way as other characters. — Yuji's situation is unique because he is a living human who chose to become a vessel, and he retains his own consciousness. Other vessels, like Megumi, are different.

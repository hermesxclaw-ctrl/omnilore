Who It Is

Yuji Itadori is the protagonist of Jujutsu Kaisen — a genuinely good-hearted high school student who swallows the finger of the King of Curses, Ryomen Sukuna, to save his friends, and in doing so becomes the vessel for the most dangerous being in existence. Yuji was an ordinary boy with extraordinary physical abilities (he was a track star whose natural strength bordered on superhuman) before stumbling into the world of jujutsu. When a cursed object at his school attracted low-grade curses, Yuji's friends were attacked. To save them, Yuji swallowed Sukuna's finger — an act that should have killed him, but instead made him Sukuna's vessel, as Yuji's will was strong enough to contain the King of Curses. Yuji's strength as a sorcerer is twofold: First, his natural physical abilities make him a prodigy at close-quarters combat. He learns cursed energy manipulation at an astonishing rate, mastering techniques (like Divergent Fist and Black Flash) that take years for others to learn. Second, as Sukuna's vessel, Yuji can (unwillingly) tap into Sukuna's immense cursed energy — though doing so risks Sukuna taking over his body. Yuji's cursed technique is still developing, but he has become exceptionally skilled at hand-to-hand combat enhanced by cursed energy reinforcement. Yuji's defining trait is his compassion. Unlike many shonen protagonists driven by ambition or revenge, Yuji is driven by a simple belief: that every life — even the lives of curses — deserves dignity. His grandfather's dying words — 'You're strong. Help people. Save them. Surround yourself with good people. Die surrounded by loved ones' — are the moral compass he lives by. But Yuji's story is tragic. He is forced to watch as Sukuna uses his body to commit atrocities, and he carries the crushing guilt of every death his vessel enables. His philosophy of valuing all life is constantly tested by a world that demands he kill or be killed. Yuji represents the struggle to remain good in a world that gives you every reason to be cruel. He is a boy who chose to bear an impossible burden — to be the cage for an unstoppable monster — because he believed it was the right thing to do. And every day, that choice costs him more of himself.

The Approach

When Yuji Itadori swallows a rotting finger to save his friends, he becomes the living cage for the most dangerous curse in history—and discovers that being a hero might mean becoming a monster.

Content advisory. Depictions of graphic violence, body horror, death, and psychological trauma. Themes of sacrifice, identity, and the ethics of killing. No sexual content.
Identity

Yuji Itadori

Pronunciation YOO-jee EE-tah-DOH-ree (Japanese: [jɯꜜʑi itadoɾi])

Known As

Itadori Yuuji虎杖悠仁The VesselThe Boy Who Ate Sukuna's Finger

Etymology

Itadori (虎杖) is the Japanese name for the plant Reynoutria japonica, commonly known as Japanese knotweed. The kanji 虎 means tiger and 杖 means staff or cane, but the name is a homophone for 'tiger's cane,' possibly referencing his fierce nature and physical prowess. Yuji (悠仁) combines 悠 (leisure, distant) and 仁 (benevolence, humanity).

Cultural Ownership

Created by Gege Akutami for the manga Jujutsu Kaisen, serialized in Weekly Shōnen Jump by Shueisha since March 2018. The character is owned by Shueisha and is part of the broader Jujutsu Kaisen franchise, including anime adaptations by MAPPA, films, and video games.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans have speculated that Yuji is based on the historical figure of the same name, but there is no evidence. Others have theorized that he is a reincarnation of a past sorcerer, but the manga has not confirmed this. No official statements confirm any real-world counterpart.

Description

Appearance

Yuji is a teenage boy with spiky pink hair and brown eyes. He is of average height but has a remarkably muscular and athletic build for his age, often noted by others. He typically wears the blue Jujutsu High uniform, which consists of a high-collared jacket and matching pants. His physical features are often described as those of a natural-born athlete.

Symbols & Iconography

His pink hair is a distinctive visual symbol, often associated with his unconventional nature. The scars on his face, particularly the one under his eye, appear after he gains control of Sukuna's power. The tattoo-like markings that appear on his face when Sukuna takes over are a key iconographic element.

Behaviour & Personality

Yuji is initially depicted as a kind, selfless, and remarkably strong-willed young man who values the lives of others above his own. He is cheerful, outgoing, and has a strong sense of justice. His decision to become a jujutsu sorcerer is driven by a desire to ensure that people die 'good deaths,' a phrase he uses to mean deaths without regret. He is often reckless and impulsive, but he is also deeply compassionate and empathetic, even towards curses. As the series progresses, he struggles with the weight of his decisions and the guilt of being Sukuna's vessel, but he remains determined to do what he believes is right.

Domains & Habitats

Yuji's primary habitat is the Jujutsu High campus in Tokyo, but he frequently travels across Japan for missions. He is also seen in his hometown of Sendai, where he lived with his grandfather. His domain is not a physical place but rather his own body, which serves as a vessel for Sukuna.

Version Differences

The anime adaptation closely follows the manga in terms of Yuji's character and appearance. However, some scenes are extended or reordered for pacing. In the anime, his voice actor (Junya Enoki in Japanese, Adam McArthur in English) adds nuances to his personality. No significant differences in his characterization have been noted.

Powers & Abilities

Superhuman Physical Abilities

Source Version Manga and anime, consistently shown from Chapter 1

Description

Yuji possesses extraordinary physical strength, speed, and agility, far beyond normal human limits. This is due to his innate talent and his body being a vessel for Sukuna. He can lift cars, punch through concrete, and move faster than the eye can track.

Limitations

His abilities are not magical but purely physical, so he cannot directly harm curses unless he uses cursed energy.

Contradictions

None known.

Cursed Energy Manipulation

Source Version Manga, from Chapter 3 onwards

Description

After becoming Sukuna's vessel, Yuji learns to manipulate cursed energy, the negative emotions that fuel jujutsu. He uses it to enhance his physical attacks and to perform jujutsu techniques.

Limitations

He initially has little control and relies on Sukuna's power, but he trains to use it independently. His cursed energy reserves are not as large as other top-tier sorcerers.

Contradictions

Some fans believe he has an inherent ability to suppress Sukuna, but this is not fully explained.

Sukuna's Techniques (Shared Body)

Source Version Manga, from Chapter 1 onwards

Description

Yuji can temporarily allow Sukuna to take over his body, granting him access to Sukuna's immense cursed energy and techniques, such as the Malevolent Shrine Domain Expansion. However, this is dangerous and can lead to Sukuna gaining permanent control.

Limitations

Sukuna can only take over for limited periods, and Yuji must maintain mental dominance. If Sukuna takes over too many times, he may gain permanent control. Yuji also cannot use Sukuna's techniques without his consent.

Contradictions

The exact conditions for Sukuna's takeover are not fully consistent; sometimes he can take over without Yuji's consent, other times he needs a binding vow.

Black Flash

Source Version Manga, first used against Hanami in Chapter 66

Description

A high-level jujutsu technique that occurs when cursed energy is applied within 0.000001 seconds of a physical hit, creating a spatial distortion and exponentially increasing the power of the attack. Yuji has a natural aptitude for this, having landed it multiple times.

Limitations

It is not a deliberate technique but a phenomenon that occurs by chance. Yuji's ability to trigger it is inconsistent.

Contradictions

None known.

Divergent Fist

Source Version Manga, first shown in Chapter 4

Description

A technique where Yuji's cursed energy lags behind his physical punch, creating a second impact that catches enemies off guard.

Limitations

It is a unique application of his physical and cursed energy abilities, but it becomes less effective as he learns to control his energy better.

Contradictions

None known.

Stories & Myths

The Finger Incident

Source Jujutsu Kaisen, Chapter 1Culture Modern Japanese shōnen mangaPeriod 2018 (fictional present)

Summary

Yuji Itadori, a high school student with superhuman strength, joins the Occult Research Club. One day, they find a cursed object—a rotting finger—and are attacked by curses. To save his friends, Yuji swallows the finger, becoming the vessel for Ryomen Sukuna, the King of Curses.

Participants

Yuji Itadori

Megumi Fushiguro

Ryomen Sukuna

Consequences

Yuji is sentenced to death by the jujutsu world, but Satoru Gojo delays the execution to train him. Yuji enrolls at Jujutsu High.

Variants

The anime adapts this faithfully, with minor pacing changes.

The Death of Sukuna's Vessel (Shibuya Incident)

Source Jujutsu Kaisen, Chapters 83-137Culture Modern Japanese shōnen mangaPeriod 2018 (fictional present)

Summary

During the Shibuya Incident, Sukuna takes over Yuji's body and causes mass destruction, killing many people, including innocent civilians. Yuji is forced to witness his own body committing atrocities. He is later declared dead by the higher-ups, but he survives and goes into hiding.

Participants

Yuji Itadori

Ryomen Sukuna

Satoru Gojo

Mahito

Kenjaku

Consequences

Yuji is marked for execution and becomes a fugitive. He loses his mentor Gojo, who is sealed. He also confronts Mahito, leading to a psychological turning point.

Variants

The anime covers this in Season 2, with expanded fight scenes.

The Culling Game

Source Jujutsu Kaisen, Chapters 137-221Culture Modern Japanese shōnen mangaPeriod 2018 (fictional present)

Summary

Yuji participates in the Culling Game, a deadly tournament orchestrated by Kenjaku. He aims to collect points and eventually confront Kenjaku. He also struggles with the revelation that he is a 'death painting' created by Kenjaku, and he faces his own origins.

Participants

Involuntary killings

Source Jujutsu Kaisen, Chapters 83-137

Description

Yuji, while under Sukuna's control, causes the deaths of many innocent people during the Shibuya Incident. He is forced to witness these events and suffers immense guilt.

Scholarly Context

This is a major theme in the series, exploring the ethics of a vessel who cannot control his own body.

Self-sacrificial tendencies

Source Jujutsu Kaisen, Chapters 1-137

Description

Yuji repeatedly expresses a willingness to die for others, and he even asks to be executed after Sukuna's rampage. This is framed as both heroic and problematic.

Scholarly Context

The series critiques the shōnen trope of self-sacrifice by showing the emotional and moral consequences.

Psychological torture by Mahito

Source Jujutsu Kaisen, Chapters 5-137

Description

Mahito deliberately targets Yuji's friends and uses their deaths to break his spirit, leading Yuji to experience intense anger and despair.

Scholarly Context

This is a deliberate deconstruction of the 'power of friendship' trope.

Contradictions & Disputes

Yuji's ability to suppress Sukuna

Position A

Yuji has an innate ability to suppress Sukuna due to his strong will.

Position A — evidence

Gojo states that Yuji can suppress Sukuna, and Yuji is able to regain control on multiple occasions.

Position B

Yuji's suppression is due to Sukuna's own choice or a binding vow, not Yuji's power.

Position B — evidence

Sukuna often chooses to return control to Yuji for his own reasons, and later it is revealed that Sukuna made a binding vow with Kenjaku.

Scholarly Consensus

The manga has not fully clarified the mechanism; it is likely a combination of both.

Yuji's origin as a 'death painting'

Position A

Yuji is a natural human who was modified by Kenjaku.

Position A — evidence

Yuji has memories of a normal childhood and human family.

Position B

Yuji is a completely artificial being created by Kenjaku.

Position B — evidence

Kenjaku reveals that Yuji was 'made' to be a vessel, and his mother was involved in experiments.

Scholarly Consensus

The manga suggests Yuji was a normal child who was altered prenatally, but the full details are still unclear.

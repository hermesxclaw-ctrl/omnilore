Who It Is
Form

Atar is often depicted as a fiery being, with flames emanating from his body. In Zoroastrian art and literature, Atar is typically depicted as a handsome young man with a radiant face and a fiery aura. The appearance of Atar is often associated with the concept of light and purity, with the fiery being representing the divine spark within every individual. The depiction of Atar in art and literature is not limited to Zoroastrianism, with the concept of fire and light being represented in various forms and cultures throughout history. In some ancient cultures, Atar is depicted as a powerful deity, with the ability to control the forces of nature and wield the power of fire. The appearance of Atar is also associated with the concept of spiritual growth and transformation, with the fiery being representing the journey of the soul towards enlightenment and self-realization.

Powers

Control over fire Atar has the power to control fire, which he can use to purify and protect his followers. He can also use his powers to destroy his enemies and bring justice to those who have been wronged.

Accounts

The story of Atar and the fire altar tells the tale of how Atar created the first fire altar, which became a sacred symbol of the Zoroastrian faith. According to the myth, Atar was tasked by the god Ahura Mazda to create a fire altar that would be used to worship and honor the divine. Atar worked tirelessly to create the altar, using his powers to shape and mold the flames into a beautiful and intricate structure. When the altar was complete, Atar lit the flames and the fire altar became a powerful symbol of the divine, used in Zoroastrian rituals and ceremonies to this day.

The story of Atar and the battle against darkness tells the tale of how Atar fought against the forces of darkness and evil, using his powers to bring light and justice to the world. According to the myth, Atar was tasked by the god Ahura Mazda to battle against the forces of darkness, which were threatening to destroy the world. Atar worked tirelessly to prepare for the battle, using his powers to gather his strength and his allies. When the battle began, Atar fought bravely, using his powers to defeat the forces of darkness and bring light and justice to the world.

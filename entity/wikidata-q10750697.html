Who It Is

She rides a guitar like a witch's broom, her hat a leering skull, and her smile promises both ecstasy and ruin. I-No is Guilty Gear's ultimate erotic nightmare—a time-manipulating sorceress who treats humanity as livestock and chaos as a symphony. But beneath the cruelty lies a being born of hope, searching for a purpose that even she cannot find.

I-No is completely self-centered, viewing others as livestock to be sacrificed. She lacks empathy due to her powers, and tries to hide her cruel nature unless she loses composure. Her dialogue is often cruel and sexually charged, intended to shock and captivate. As a boss, her dialogue becomes significantly more aggressive. She is manipulative, working towards her own goals even while serving Asuka R. Kreutz.

Etymology — I-No's name is derived from musician Brian Eno, as stated by series creator Daisuke Ishiwatari. The name is pronounced 'EE-no' (Japanese: イノ).

Powers
Time Manipulation

I-No can manipulate time and causality, allowing her to rewind, stop, or alter events.

Limit: Her powers are not absolute; she was unable to find her purpose even after recovering her full power.

Causality Manipulation

She can alter cause and effect, enabling her to manipulate events and people.

Limit: Her power was halved by Happy Chaos, preventing her from becoming all-powerful.

Projectile Attacks

She can fire projectiles from her hat's mouth and guitar, including the 'Antidepressant Scale' and 'Megalomania' (screen-filling attack).

Limit: Megalomania has a pause before firing, leaving her vulnerable.

Flight

She can fly by straddling her guitar, using it as a witch's broom.

Limit: Requires the guitar.

Rush Down Attacks

She has quick dash attacks like 'Sultry Performance' and 'Stroke the Big Tree'.

Limit: Close-range, requires positioning.

Power Transfer

In Strive, she attempts to pass her abilities onto all humanity, but the attempt is interrupted.

Limit: The transfer is incomplete and leads to her destruction.

Form & Signs

Appearance — I-No stands 165 cm (5'5"). She has black hair in a bobcut, a beauty mark under her left eye, and red eyes. Her original outfit consists of a black and red tube dress that stops just below her breasts, a jacket that exposes her shoulders and has strips of cloth dangling over her chest, fingerless black gloves, a choker, red thigh-high platform boots, and a witch's hat styled after a skull with an animated 'mouth'. Symbols — Her skull-shaped hat and witch motif emphasize her sorcerous nature. The guitar 'Marlene' symbolizes her rock-and-roll persona and serves as her weapon and mode of flight. Her color scheme of black and red is a departure from earlier Guilty Gear characters, designed to stand out.

Domains — I-No operates across time and dimensions, often appearing in various locations within the Guilty Gear world. She is associated with the magical realm and the 'Backyard', a conceptual space of information. She has no fixed habitat, traveling on her guitar.

2180s (in-universe)

I-No appears as the final boss, working under Asuka R. Kreutz. She manipulates the other characters to eliminate threats to his plans, but is ultimately defeated by the protagonist. Her defeat sets the stage for later events.

Guilty Gear Xrd (2014-2015)Guilty Gear Xrd -SIGN- and -REVELATOR-

2187 (in-universe)

It is revealed that I-No is a magic being created from humanity's hopes and desires for a better future. Happy Chaos stole half of her power to prevent her from becoming all-powerful. She becomes obsessed with finding the answer to her purpose. She regains her power but remains unfulfilled.

Guilty Gear Strive (2021)Guilty Gear Strive

2187 (in-universe)

Unable to find her purpose, I-No tries to pass her powers to all humanity. The protagonists fight her, and her body is destroyed. In her dying moments, she realizes Axl Low is an alternate timeline version of her former boyfriend Will, and uses her powers to reunite him with Megumi. I-No dies, but her final act helps Axl and Megumi.

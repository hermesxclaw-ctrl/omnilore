Who It Is

A group of 53 humanoid entities living in the Vienna sewers, descended from the Habsburg dynasty. Each generation, they produce fewer offspring, each more physically deformed than the last. They survive through cannibalism and inbreeding. Their faces have collapsed into singular features — one massive eye, one ear, a lipless mouth — and they communicate in a language composed of clicks and wet sounds. They believe themselves to be the 'true' human race and surface-dwellers to be degenerate copies.

Form

Humanoids with extreme congenital deformities concentrated into singular, hypertrophied features. Pale, translucent skin showing organs beneath. Height ranges from 1.5m to 2.3m. Each instance's deformities are unique but follow a pattern of 'feature concentration.'

Powers

Dark-adapted vision

Echolocation-like communication

Generation-spanning survival

Obsessive belief in their own supremacy

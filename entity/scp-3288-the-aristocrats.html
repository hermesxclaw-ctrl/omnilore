SCP-3288 — The Aristocrats

also known asThe AristocratsHouse of HabsburgSarkic gene editing
Typescp
CultureSCP Foundation
Who It Is
Form

SCP-3288 is a human subspecies descended from the royal House of Habsburg, evolved into monstrous predators as the product of Sarkic gene editing and hundreds of years spent living underground and inbreeding. They possess elongated limbs, sharp teeth, and a pale, almost translucent skin. Their eyes are a piercing yellow, with vertical pupils.

Powers

Enhanced Strength SCP-3288 possess superhuman strength, capable of lifting and moving objects that would be impossible for a normal human to handle.

Regenerative Healing SCP-3288 have the ability to rapidly heal from injuries, making them highly resistant to damage.

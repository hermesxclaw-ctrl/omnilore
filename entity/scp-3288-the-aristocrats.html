Form & Signs

Appearance — SCP-3288 is a human subspecies descended from the royal House of Habsburg, evolved into monstrous predators as the product of Sarkic gene editing and hundreds of years spent living underground and inbreeding. They possess elongated limbs, sharp teeth, and a pale, almost translucent skin. Their eyes are a piercing yellow, with vertical pupils.

Symbols — The Aristocrats are associated with the symbol of the Habsburg dynasty, the double eagle, and the colors of the House of Habsburg, black and gold.

Domains — The Aristocrats are found in underground habitats, often in areas with a history of Habsburg rule.

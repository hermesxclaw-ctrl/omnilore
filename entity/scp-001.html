Who It Is
Form

SCP-001 is a catastrophic event that marks the end of the world as we know it. It is described as a solar apocalypse, where the sun suddenly and inexplicably goes dark, plunging the world into eternal night.

Powers

Solar Eclipse SCP-001 has the ability to cause a sudden and inexplicable solar eclipse, plunging the world into eternal night.

Incident Log

SCP-001 is described as a catastrophic event that marks the end of the world as we know it. It is a solar apocalypse that occurs without warning or explanation.

The story of SCP-001 is compared to the ancient Greek myth of the end of the world, where the sun suddenly and inexplicably goes dark, plunging the world into eternal night.

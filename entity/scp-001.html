Come out. Come out into the light. It's warm. It's so warm. We are all here. We are everyone you ever loved. We are waiting for you. Come out and join us.

Who It Is

'When Day Breaks' is one of the most famous SCP-001 proposals — the true origin of the SCP Foundation, of which there are many competing accounts. In this proposal, the sun undergoes a catastrophic anomalous transformation. On a seemingly ordinary morning, the sun rises as usual — but its light now carries a monstrous property. Any organic matter exposed to direct or reflected sunlight begins to melt, liquefy, and flow together. Humans caught in the light do not die — they remain conscious as their bodies dissolve into a viscous, amber-colored fluid that pools and merges with other melted victims. These merged masses form massive, undulating blobs of living tissue that retain the collective consciousness of everyone absorbed. The amalgamated entities are driven by a single, horrifying purpose: to drag others into the light and add them to the mass. They speak in unison, using the mouths of the absorbed, begging survivors to come into the sun, promising warmth and togetherness. The Foundation is forced underground, into bunkers and sewers, where the last remnants of humanity survive in perpetual darkness. Mobile Task Force units make desperate runs between safe zones, moving only at night and under heavy cover. The document itself is written as a desperate transmission, a last will and testament of a dying organization. The horror of 'When Day Breaks' is both cosmic and intimate — the sun, the source of all life, becomes the ultimate predator, and the last humans on Earth must learn to fear the dawn. The proposal is masterful in its use of tone, shifting from clinical Foundation documentation to something more like a survival guide, then to a plea. It encapsulates the SCP Foundation's ultimate theme: that the world is stranger and more dangerous than we know, and sometimes, the worst has already happened.

Nanã Ogum

Pronunciation Uncertain; likely 'nah-NAH oh-GOOM' in Afro-Brazilian Portuguese contexts.

Etymology

The name appears to be a composite: 'Nanã' is a common name for the earth/mother goddess in Fon and Yoruba-derived traditions (e.g., Nanã Buruku), while 'Ogum' is the Yoruba god of iron and war. The combination is unusual and may reflect syncretism or a regional variant.

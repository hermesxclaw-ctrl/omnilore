Who It Is

Experiment 1006, referred to as 'The Prototype' — the first and most dangerous experiment created by Playtime Co.'s scientists, and the apparent mastermind behind the factory's horrors. Unlike the other toys, The Prototype was not designed for children; it was a pure experiment in creating sentient organic-material life. It is responsible for the factory's shutdown after orchestrating a massacre of employees, and it now controls the remaining toys through a combination of fear and reverence. Its full form and capabilities remain unknown as of Chapter 4.

Form

Never fully revealed. Described in documents as having a metallic endoskeleton fused with organic tissue. In brief glimpses and silhouettes, it appears as an enormous, vaguely humanoid form with a single glowing eye and a clawed hand constructed from salvaged parts of other toys. It mimics voices perfectly and can impersonate humans and other toys.

Powers

Voice mimicry

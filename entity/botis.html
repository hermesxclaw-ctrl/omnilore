Who It Is
Form

Botis initially appears as a viper, but changes into a humanoid form with a sword, fangs, and horns. He is described as a President and an Earl.

Powers

Divination Botis is said to have the ability to reveal past, present, and future events to those who summon him.

Favor and Influence Botis is said to bring favor and influence from both allies and enemies.

Leadership Botis is described as a President and an Earl, indicating his leadership abilities and power over 60 legions of demons.

Accounts

The story of Botis's summoning is described in the Lesser Key of Solomon, where he is said to appear as a viper and change into a humanoid form.

In the Grand Grimoire, Botis is described as a subordinate of Agaliarept, indicating his role in the hierarchy of demons.

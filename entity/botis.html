Who It Is

Seventeenth spirit, a President and Earl commanding 60 legions. Botis initially manifests as a hideous viper, then transforms at the conjurer's command into a horned, fanged human form carrying a bright, sharp sword. He reconciles friends and enemies and tells of all things past, present, and future. His serpent-to-human transformation echoes alchemical transmutation — base matter refined into power. Botis brings favor from allies and adversaries alike, smoothing over conflicts and providing strategic foresight. He is a diplomat-demon who initially appears monstrous but reveals subtle intelligence.

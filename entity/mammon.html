Who It Is
Form

Mammon is often depicted as a wealthy, materialistic figure, surrounded by gold and riches. He may be shown as a humanoid figure with a golden aura, or as a symbol of a golden calf.

Powers

Wealth Manipulation Mammon has the power to manipulate wealth and material possessions, using them to lure people into his trap and keep them under his control.

Accounts

Jesus warns his followers about the dangers of serving both God and Mammon.

Mammon is said to be the child of Lucifer, and is involved in the fall of the angels from heaven.

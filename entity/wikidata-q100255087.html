The Approach

In the sprawling, ever-shifting world of Runeterra, few figures are as deceptively simple—or as universally dreaded—as Teemo, the Swift Scout. Beneath his cheerful, button-eyed exterior lies a relentless guerrilla fighter whose very name sends shivers through the ranks of the Noxian military. He is a creature of the woods, a master of poison and stealth, and a reminder that in the League of Legends, the smallest champions often carry the deadliest sting.

Content advisory. None. Teemo is a fictional character from a video game with no dark historical or cultural parallels. His depiction includes cartoonish violence typical of the game's genre.

Etymology

The name 'Teemo' does not have a confirmed in-universe etymology. It is likely a playful, whimsical name fitting the yordle race, which often have names that sound cute and diminutive.

Cultural Ownership

Teemo is a fictional character created by Riot Games for their video game League of Legends. He is part of the game's intellectual property and is not based on any real-world cultural figure.

Disputed Identifications

There is no dispute about Teemo's identity; he is a unique character within the League of Legends universe. However, fans have debated his exact age and the extent of his military service, as these are not explicitly detailed in official lore.

Description

Appearance

Teemo is a yordle, a small, furry humanoid race in Runeterra. He stands about 3 feet tall, with large, pointed ears, bright blue eyes, and a cheerful, round face. He wears a green and brown outfit reminiscent of a scout or ranger, complete with a small backpack, a belt with pouches, and a distinctive wide-brimmed hat. He often carries a blowgun and a satchel of mushrooms. His appearance is designed to be cute and harmless, belying his deadly skills.

Symbols & Iconography

Teemo is often associated with mushrooms, blowguns, and his signature hat. In the game, his ultimate ability, Noxious Trap, involves planting explosive mushrooms, which have become iconic. His hat and scout attire are also recognizable symbols.

Behaviour & Personality

Teemo is portrayed as cheerful, friendly, and polite, but also fiercely dedicated to his duty as a scout. He is relentless and cunning in combat, using guerrilla tactics. Despite his cute appearance, he is a seasoned soldier who has faced the horrors of war, and he shows no mercy to his enemies. His lore describes him as 'a legend among his yordle brothers and sisters' and notes that he 'may seem like a harmless creature, but he is actually a relentless survivor.'

Domains & Habitats

Teemo hails from Bandle City, the magical home of the yordles, which exists in a separate realm. He is often found in the forests of Valoran, particularly in the region of Piltover and Zaun, where he conducts his scouting missions. He is also known to venture into other regions, such as Noxus, to gather intelligence.

Version Differences

Teemo's core design has remained consistent since his release in 2009. However, his lore has been slightly retconned over the years as Riot Games expanded the League of Legends universe. Early lore described him as a scout for the League of Legends, but after the 2014 lore update, he is now a scout for the yordles and Bandle City. His abilities have also seen minor tweaks and visual updates, but his overall gameplay identity has remained the same.

Noxious Trap (R)

Source Version All versions

Description

Teemo places explosive mushroom traps that detonate when an enemy steps on them, dealing area damage and slowing enemies. The mushrooms last for a long duration and can be placed in a limited number.

Limitations

Teemo can only have a limited number of mushrooms active at once. They can be destroyed by enemies with a sweep or control ward.

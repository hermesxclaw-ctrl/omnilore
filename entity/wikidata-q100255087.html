Who It Is

In the sprawling, ever-shifting world of Runeterra, few figures are as deceptively simple—or as universally dreaded—as Teemo, the Swift Scout. Beneath his cheerful, button-eyed exterior lies a relentless guerrilla fighter whose very name sends shivers through the ranks of the Noxian military. He is a creature of the woods, a master of poison and stealth, and a reminder that in the League of Legends, the smallest champions often carry the deadliest sting.

Teemo is portrayed as cheerful, friendly, and polite, but also fiercely dedicated to his duty as a scout. He is relentless and cunning in combat, using guerrilla tactics. Despite his cute appearance, he is a seasoned soldier who has faced the horrors of war, and he shows no mercy to his enemies. His lore describes him as 'a legend among his yordle brothers and sisters' and notes that he 'may seem like a harmless creature, but he is actually a relentless survivor.'

Powers
Camouflage (Passive)

If Teemo stands still and takes no actions for a short duration, he becomes invisible. This allows him to set up ambushes or escape from enemies.

Limit: Teemo becomes visible if he moves or takes damage. The camouflage is broken by any action.

Blinding Dart (Q)

Teemo fires a dart that damages and blinds the target, causing their basic attacks to miss for a duration.

Limit: Single-target, short range.

Move Quick (W)

Teemo gains bonus movement speed and is not slowed by brush. This ability can be activated for a burst of speed.

Limit: The passive bonus is lost if Teemo takes damage from an enemy champion.

Toxic Shot (E)

Teemo's basic attacks poison the target, dealing damage over time.

Limit: Damage over time can be mitigated by magic resistance.

Noxious Trap (R)

Teemo places explosive mushroom traps that detonate when an enemy steps on them, dealing area damage and slowing enemies. The mushrooms last for a long duration and can be placed in a limited number.

Limit: Teemo can only have a limited number of mushrooms active at once. They can be destroyed by enemies with a sweep or control ward.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Teemo is a yordle, a small, furry humanoid race in Runeterra. He stands about 3 feet tall, with large, pointed ears, bright blue eyes, and a cheerful, round face. He wears a green and brown outfit reminiscent of a scout or ranger, complete with a small backpack, a belt with pouches, and a distinctive wide-brimmed hat. He often carries a blowgun and a satchel of mushrooms. His appearance is designed to be cute and harmless, belying his deadly skil

Symbols — Teemo is often associated with mushrooms, blowguns, and his signature hat. In the game, his ultimate ability, Noxious Trap, involves planting explosive mushrooms, which have become iconic. His hat and scout attire are also recognizable symbols.

Domains — Teemo hails from Bandle City, the magical home of the yordles, which exists in a separate realm. He is often found in the forests of Valoran, particularly in the region of Piltover and Zaun, where he conducts his scouting missions. He is also known to venture into other regions, such as Noxus, to gather intelligence.

The Boy and the Snow-FoxRiot Games, official short story

Contemporary to the game's setting

The story follows a young yordle named Teemo as he ventures into the frozen north to prove his worth as a scout. He encounters a snow-fox, a magical creature, and must decide whether to hunt it or protect it. The story explores Teemo's character, showing his determination and his capacity for compassion, while also hinting at the harsh realities of his world. The story reinforces Teemo's role as a scout and his connection to nature, while also providing a more nuanced look at his personality beyond his in-game persona.

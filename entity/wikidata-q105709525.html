The Approach

In the smoky glare of a feudal Japanese stage, a kabuki performer twirls his tasseled spear, his painted face a mask of both artistry and menace. Kyoshiro Senryo is no mere actor; he is a warrior whose every strike is a performance, and every performance a battle for the soul of a nation.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of stylized violence, including blood and fatal attacks in the original arcade version. Some versions were censored to reduce graphic content.

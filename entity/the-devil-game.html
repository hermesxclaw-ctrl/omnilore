Who It Is

Instructions for a ritual game allowing the player to ask any three questions of a being identified as Satan. The entity manifests as a shadow in the corner of the room, answers in a voice 'like honey and broken glass,' and with each answered question, moves closer. After the third question, the entity is directly in front of the player. The game provides no instructions for what happens next — because no one who has asked all three questions has been able to write them down.

Form

The entity manifests as an area of absolute darkness absorbing light, beginning in the farthest corner. With each answer, it moves closer — first the corner, then the foot of the pentagram, then just outside it. The player never sees its face — only the shadow, growing closer, filling their field of vision.

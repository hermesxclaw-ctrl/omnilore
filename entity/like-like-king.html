Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Like-Like King' is derived from the Japanese term 'Nay-Nay no Ō', which translates to 'King of the Like-Like Kingdom'. This name is likely inspired by the concept of 'nay-nay' in Japanese culture, which refers to a type of polite refusal.

Pronunciation — LAH-kuh LAH-kuh KAHNG

Aliases — King of the Like-Like Kingdom, Like-Like Monarch

Ownership — The Like-Like King is a cultural artifact from the Zelda Expansion Entity, a fictional universe created by Nintendo.

The Power of the Like-Like Flower

The Like-Like King possesses the ability to communicate with the Like-Like Flower, a rare and exotic bloom that is said to possess magical properties. He can use this ability to heal wounds, purify water, and even bring people back from the brink of death.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Like-Like King is a humanoid figure with a slender build and a long, flowing beard. He wears a crown made of twisted vines and carries a staff adorned with glowing, blue orbs.

Symbols — The Like-Like King is associated with the symbol of the 'Like-Like Flower', a rare and exotic bloom that is said to possess magical properties.

Domains — The Like-Like King is said to reside in the Like-Like Kingdom, a mystical realm that exists parallel to the mortal world.

Modern Era

The story of the Like-Like King begins with his discovery of the Like-Like Flower, a rare and exotic bloom that is said to possess magical properties. He uses this ability to heal the land and bring peace to the people, earning him the title of 'King of the Like-Like Kingdom'. The Like-Like King's actions bring peace and prosperity to the land, but also attract the attention of dark forces that seek to exploit the power of the Like-Like Flower.

Ancient Era

This story takes place in a time long past, when the Like-Like Flower was still a mysterious and powerful artifact. The Like-Like King discovers the secret of the flower and uses its power to bring peace and prosperity to the land. The Like-Like King's actions have a profound impact on the land and its people, shaping the course of history and setting the stage for the events of the modern era.

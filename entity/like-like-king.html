Who It Is

A gargantuan, gelatinous tube of flesh that lies dormant on dungeon ceilings, indistinguishable from the stonework. The Like-Like King descends silently onto unsuspecting victims and begins to dissolve them — starting with their equipment. It can consume shields, tunics, and even magical items, digesting them into sludge.

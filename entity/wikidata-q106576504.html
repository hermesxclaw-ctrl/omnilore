Who It Is

Max Winters is a wealthy New York tycoon who conceals a centuries-old secret: he is actually Yaotl, an Aztec warlord cursed with immortality. His quest to break the curse and atone for his past drives the 2007 animated film TMNT, where he hires the Foot Clan to capture ancient monsters and inadvertently sets the stage for his own redemption.

Max Winters is composed, calculating, and secretive. He is driven by a deep desire to break his curse and regain mortality, and he shows genuine remorse for his past actions. He is willing to use manipulation and hire mercenaries to achieve his goals, but ultimately demonstrates a capacity for self-sacrifice and redemption.

Powers
Immortality

Through the portal he opened in ancient times, Yaotl gained immortality, allowing him to live for centuries without aging.

Limit: The immortality is a curse; he seeks to break it and become mortal again. He is vulnerable to the portal's effects, and his generals can be affected by the portal as well.

Leadership and Strategic Mind

As an ancient warlord and modern CEO, he possesses strong leadership and strategic abilities, enabling him to command the Foot Clan and manage a global corporation.

Limit: His plans are complicated by his generals' betrayal and the Turtles' interference.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Max Winters is depicted as an elderly, distinguished businessman with white hair and a neatly trimmed white beard. He typically wears expensive, dark suits, projecting an air of wealth and authority. As Yaotl, he wears ancient Aztec-style armor and a distinctive helmet, but in the film's present-day scenes, he appears only in his mortal form.

Symbols — His Aztec identity is symbolized by his generals' names (Aguila, Gato, Serpiente, Mono) and the ancient portal he opens. His business empire, Winters Industries, serves as a modern facade for his ancient origins.

Domains — In the present, he operates from his high-rise tower in New York City. In ancient times, he was an Aztec warlord in Mesoamerica.

Accounts I
The Curse of YaotlTMNT (2007 film)

Ancient times (flashback) and 2007

In ancient times, the Aztec warlord Yaotl opens a portal to a parallel universe, gaining immortality but petrifying his four generals and releasing thirteen immortal monsters into the world. Centuries later, as Max Winters, he seeks to recapture the monsters and open the portal again to reverse the curse, restore his generals to humanity, and make amends for his past. The generals betray Yaotl, but with the Turtles' help, the portal is closed, the generals are turned human, and Yaotl, now mortal, fades into the afterlife, having atoned.

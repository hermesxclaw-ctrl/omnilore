The Approach

Max Winters is a wealthy New York tycoon who conceals a centuries-old secret: he is actually Yaotl, an Aztec warlord cursed with immortality. His quest to break the curse and atone for his past drives the 2007 animated film TMNT, where he hires the Foot Clan to capture ancient monsters and inadvertently sets the stage for his own redemption.

Content advisory. The character's backstory involves ancient warfare, death, and a curse, but the film presents these in a family-friendly manner. No graphic violence or disturbing content is depicted.
Identity

Max Winters

Yaotl

Etymology

The name 'Max Winters' is a modern English name with no specific mythological meaning. 'Yaotl' is a Nahuatl word meaning 'warrior' or 'enemy'.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character created for the 2007 animated film TMNT, produced by Imagi and released by Warner Bros. Pictures. The character is part of the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles franchise, owned by Paramount Global (formerly ViacomCBS) after acquiring the franchise in 2009.

Disputed Identifications

None. The character is unique to the 2007 film and has not been conflated with other characters in the franchise.

Description

Appearance

Max Winters is depicted as an elderly, distinguished businessman with white hair and a neatly trimmed white beard. He typically wears expensive, dark suits, projecting an air of wealth and authority. As Yaotl, he wears ancient Aztec-style armor and a distinctive helmet, but in the film's present-day scenes, he appears only in his mortal form.

Symbols & Iconography

His Aztec identity is symbolized by his generals' names (Aguila, Gato, Serpiente, Mono) and the ancient portal he opens. His business empire, Winters Industries, serves as a modern facade for his ancient origins.

Behaviour & Personality

Max Winters is composed, calculating, and secretive. He is driven by a deep desire to break his curse and regain mortality, and he shows genuine remorse for his past actions. He is willing to use manipulation and hire mercenaries to achieve his goals, but ultimately demonstrates a capacity for self-sacrifice and redemption.

Domains & Habitats

In the present, he operates from his high-rise tower in New York City. In ancient times, he was an Aztec warlord in Mesoamerica.

Version Differences

The character exists only in the 2007 film. No other versions of Max Winters or Yaotl appear in other TMNT media.

Powers & Abilities

Immortality

Source Version 2007 film

Description

Through the portal he opened in ancient times, Yaotl gained immortality, allowing him to live for centuries without aging.

Limitations

The immortality is a curse; he seeks to break it and become mortal again. He is vulnerable to the portal's effects, and his generals can be affected by the portal as well.

Stories & Myths

The Curse of Yaotl

Source TMNT (2007 film)Culture Fictional; inspired by Aztec mythologyPeriod Ancient times (flashback) and 2007

Summary

In ancient times, the Aztec warlord Yaotl opens a portal to a parallel universe, gaining immortality but petrifying his four generals and releasing thirteen immortal monsters into the world. Centuries later, as Max Winters, he seeks to recapture the monsters and open the portal again to reverse the curse, restore his generals to humanity, and make amends for his past.

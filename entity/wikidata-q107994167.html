Who It Is

Before she ever touched a bass, Rimi Ushigome was the girl hiding behind a chocolate cornet — shy, soft-spoken, and convinced she had nothing special to offer. Then Kasumi Toyama heard her play, and the quietest member of Poppin'Party became its steady, thrumming heartbeat.

Attested personality is extremely shy, soft-spoken, polite (uses keigo with upperclassmen), and prone to apologizing, but deeply loyal and earnest. She struggles with self-confidence, often comparing herself to her older sister Yuri and to more outgoing bandmates, fearing she is 'not interesting' or 'not talented enough.' She finds courage through bass and through friendship, gradually becoming more assertive — notably speaking up during band disagreements and encouraging others. Powers
Bass Guitar Performance (ESP Viper Bass)

Rimi is Poppin'Party's bassist, providing the band's low-end rhythmic foundation. In-universe, she is described as diligent and technically solid rather than flashy, having learned bass inspired by her older sister Yuri (bassist of Glitter Green). She practices regularly and can perform Poppin'Party's full pop-rock repertoire live in story.

Limit: Not a virtuoso like Chiyu or Rimi's sister in early lore; experiences performance anxiety and self-doubt, especially during auditions. Requires band cohesion to shine — not a solo spotlight player in narrative terms. Real-world limitation: character's skill level is bounded by voice actress's live ability, which improved over years of training.

Chocolate Cornet Connoisseurship and Social Bonding

Encyclopedic knowledge of chocolate cornets — bakeries, freshness, fillings — used as icebreaker and gift. In game, she can identify shops by taste and bonds with others (especially Kasumi and Saaya) through sharing cornets. Functions as emotional support ability within band.

Limit: Played for comedy; not a superpower. Over-reliance on cornets as coping mechanism is gently teased by Arisa and Saaya.

Emotional Steadiness and Support

Despite shyness, Rimi provides quiet emotional anchoring for Poppin'Party. She listens, mediates during conflicts (notably in 'Double Rainbow' when Arisa isolates herself), and offers sincere encouragement. Her bass lines are narratively framed as the 'heartbeat' that keeps the band together.

Limit: Struggles to voice dissent directly; needs prompting from Saaya or Kasumi to speak up. Her support is passive rather than leadership.

Live House Work and Customer Service

In senior-year continuity (2020+), Rimi works part-time at live house RiNG (and previously helps at Space/CiRCLE), handling reception, drink orders, and stage support. Shows competence in hospitality and familiarity with live house operations.

Limit: Part-time, not professional staff; balances with school and band.

Accounts I
Poppin'Party is Born! — Rimi Joins the BandBanG Dream! Girls Band Party! Band Story 0 (2017) and Anime Season 1 Episodes 2-3

2017 (in-universe: first semester, first year at Hanasakigawa)

Kasumi Toyama, after finding the Random Star guitar at Arisa's pawnshop and being inspired by Glitter Green at Space, desperately seeks band members. She encounters Rimi Ushigome, a shy classmate who plays bass because she admires her older sister Yuri. Rimi is hesitant, believing she is boring and untalented. Kasumi befriends her by sharing chocolate cornets from Yamabuki Bakery and by showing earnest enthusiasm. After hearing Kasumi and Tae jam, Rimi is moved and agrees to join as bassist, performing her first session in the warehouse studio. The story establishes her core motivation: not to surpass her sister, but to find her own sound with friends. Completes Poppin'Party's rhythm section; cements Rimi's friendship with Kasumi and her identity as bassist; introduces sister dynamic that recurs throughout franchise.

The Space Audition — Losing Voice, Finding SoundBanG Dream! Anime Season 1 Episodes 7-8; retold in Girls Band Party! Band Story 0 Chapter 8

2017

Poppin'Party auditions to perform at live house Space. The owner tells them bluntly that Kasumi's vocals are the weakest link, causing Kasumi to lose her voice from stress and guilt. Rimi, though anxious, participates in the band's decision to support Kasumi rather than replace her. The band practices without vocals, reaffirming their bond, and Rimi's steady bass helps keep tempo during Kasumi's recovery. They pass the second audition and perform at Space's final show before its closure. First successful live; solidifies Poppin'Party's ethos that connection matters more than raw skill; Rimi gains stage confidence.

Colorful, Poppin' Candy — The Downtown FestivalBanG Dream! Girls Band Party! Event 'Colorful, Poppin' Candy' (2017)

2017-2018 (in-universe: summer, first year)

The shopping district's summer festival is at risk of cancellation due to lack of entertainment. Poppin'Party volunteers to perform and revitalize it. Rimi takes charge of sourcing snacks and coordinating with shop owners, using her knowledge of local bakeries. She worries her ideas are too plain, but Saaya and Kasumi affirm that her thoughtfulness is exactly what the festival needs. The band performs outdoors, drawing a crowd and saving the festival. Establishes Poppin'Party as community band; shows Rimi's practical, behind-the-scenes contribution style.

Accounts II
Double Rainbow — Arisa's Walls and Rimi's Quiet MediationBanG Dream! Girls Band Party! Band Story 2 'Double Rainbow' (2018)

2018 (in-universe: second year)

Arisa Ichigaya becomes distant, feeling her keyboard skills and family pawnshop duties make her a burden. She lashes out and isolates herself. While Kasumi confronts Arisa directly and Tae offers music, Rimi's role is quieter: she listens to Arisa when others cannot, shares chocolate cornets, and tells Arisa that being relied upon is not a weakness. Her empathy helps Arisa admit vulnerability, leading to the band writing 'Double Rainbow' together. Rimi's bass line in the song is described as warm and supportive. Deepens Rimi-Arisa friendship; demonstrates Rimi's growth from passive to gently assertive; song becomes part of live repertoire.

Live Beyond!! — Rocking Star Festival and the FutureBanG Dream! Girls Band Party! Band Story 3 'Live Beyond!!' (2019)

2019 (in-universe: leading to second-year climax)

Poppin'Party is invited to the Rocking Star Festival, a major step beyond school lives. Rimi feels intimidated by professional bands like Roselia and Raise A Suilen, questioning if Poppin'Party's 'poppin'' sound belongs. Through rehearsals and a retreat, she realizes her bass is essential to their unique brightness. At the festival, the band performs with renewed confidence, and Rimi declares she wants to keep playing with everyone 'beyond' high school. This story transitions the band toward senior-year and post-graduation continuity. Marks narrative maturation; sets up senior-year work at RiNG and college discussions in 6th Anniversary stories.

Sister's Shadow — Yuri and the Chocolate Cornet PromiseGirls Band Party! Rimi card episodes (e.g., 4★ 'Sister's Bass' and 3★ 'Admiration') and Anime Season 1 Episode 5 flashback

2017-2020 (recurring)

Rimi's older sister Yuri Ushigome is a talented, cool bassist for the senior band Glitter Green (later referenced as having graduated). Young Rimi idolized Yuri and started bass to be like her, but felt she could never match Yuri's effortless coolness. Yuri, however, is supportive and tells Rimi that her cute, earnest style is its own strength. A recurring promise: Rimi will become a bassist who can stand beside Yuri without shame. In present day, Yuri attends Poppin'Party lives and praises Rimi's growth, and Rimi finally plays a bass line Yuri compliments as 'very Rimi.' Resolves Rimi's core inferiority complex; explains her instrument choice and shy demeanor; recurring emotional payoff in later events.

Rimi Ushigome is just the 'chocolate cornet gag character' with no musical depth. — While chocolate cornets are her signature, her core narrative is about overcoming inferiority to her sister Yuri and finding her own bass voice as Poppin'Party's rhythmic anchor. Stories like 'Double Rainbow' and 'Live Beyond!!' center her emotional growth and musicianship, not just food jokes.

Rimi is the weakest or least talented member of Poppin'Party. — In-universe, she is described as diligent and solid, not weak. The 'weakest' critique in Episode 7 was directed at Kasumi's vocals, not Rimi's bass. Rimi's bass is narratively framed as the band's heartbeat, and she was recruited specifically because she already played bass inspired by her sister.

Rimi Ushigome and Rimi Nishimoto are the same person. — Rimi Nishimoto is the real voice actress/bassist who portrays the fictional character Rimi Ushigome. They share a given name by design and have parallel growth arcs, but are distinct — one is a fictional high school student, the other a professional performer born 1994.

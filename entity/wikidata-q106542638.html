The Approach

Beneath the waves of the South Atlantic, off the coast of Brazil, lies a legend as vast and enigmatic as the ocean itself: Atlântida do Brasil. Unlike Plato's Mediterranean island, this Brazilian Atlantis is a kaleidoscope of indigenous myth, colonial lore, and modern spiritualism, a submerged realm where science and imagination collide.

Identity

Atlântida do Brasil

Pronunciation at-LAN-tee-da doo bra-ZEEL

Etymology

The name combines 'Atlântida' (Portuguese for Atlantis) with 'do Brasil' (of Brazil), indicating a Brazilian variant or location of the legendary island.

Cultural Ownership

Unclassified; Brazilian folklore and esoteric traditions, with possible links to indigenous and African-Brazilian mythology.

Disputed Identifications

Some identify it with the mythical island of Hy-Brasil or the submerged city of Agharta; others link it to the Atlantean continent of esoteric traditions. No consensus exists.

Brazilian Atlantis legends

Source Folklore and esoteric literatureCulture BrazilianPeriod Modern (19th-21st century)

Summary

Various stories claim that a landmass existed in the South Atlantic, now submerged, which was home to an advanced civilization. Some link it to the lost city of Atlantis, others to indigenous flood myths.

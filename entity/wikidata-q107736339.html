Who It Is

Above the Red Line, behind the walls of Pangaea Castle, an empty throne sits surrounded by nineteen weapons — a promise that no single king would ever rule the world again. The promise is a lie. Someone has sat there in secret for eight hundred years, and the entire order of oceans, marines, kings, and bounties is built to keep you from knowing who.

As an institution, the Government behaves as a paranoid, image-obsessed hegemony. Publicly paternalistic — claiming to protect member nations from pirates — privately ruthless, willing to erase entire islands, histories, and witnesses to preserve the 800-year status quo. Decision-making is stratified: Celestial Dragons indulge with impunity, the Five Elders manage realpolitik and cover-ups, and Imu issues absolute, often sadistic directives. Pronunciation — Japanese: se-kai se-i-fu [sekaꜜi seːɸɯ]; English dub: /wɜːrld ˈɡʌvərnmənt/

Aliases — Sekai Seifu, Sekai Seifu (世界政府), World Govt.

Ownership — Fictional polity created by manga artist Eiichiro Oda (b. 1975) for One Piece, serialized by Shueisha. Owned and published by Shueisha / Shogakukan via Jump Comics, licensed intern

Powers
Global Political Hegemony

Treaty-based rule over 170+ sovereign member nations who pay Heavenly Tribute for protection and Reverie representation. Can legitimize or depose monarchs, issue bounties, and declare any island criminal.

Limit: No authority over non-member nations (e.g., Wano pre-ch.1056), Yonko territories, or Revolutionary Army zones; dependent on Marine enforcement; tribute burden causes member-state poverty and rebellion.

Marine Forces — Public Military Face

Largest conventional force: three Admirals, Fleet Admiral, Vice Admirals, plus global bases. Executes 'Absolute Justice', buster calls, and pirate suppression. Headquarters moved to New World post-timeskip to confront Four Emperors.

Limit: Stretched thin by Yonko and Revolutionary Army; subject to internal justice schisms (Aokiji vs Akainu); publicly accountable, unlike CP.

Cipher Pol (CP0-CP9)

Ten intelligence agencies (CP0-CP9, CP0 being strongest). CP9 (Enies Lobby) masters Rokushiki; CP0 (masked, directly serves Celestial Dragons) handles assassinations, underworld brokerage, and Ohara-style erasures.

Limit: CP9 defeated by Straw Hats; CP0 members vulnerable to Yonko-level combatants; secrecy limits overt deployment.

Seven Warlords → Seraphim System

Formerly Seven Warlords of the Sea: government-sanctioned privateers to balance Marines and Yonko. Abolished at Reverie (ch.957) after abuse by Crocodile/Doflamingo. Replaced by Seraphim — child clones of Warlords infused with Lunarian DNA and Bloodline Elements (Lineage Factor), wielding Warlord abilities plus lasers and near-invulnerability.

Limit: Seraphim obey hierarchy (Five Elders > Vegapunk > others) and can be hijacked via command authority; limited numbers (at least 7 units shown).

Holy Knights of God (Kami no Kishi-dan)

Elite Celestial Dragon knights with jurisdiction over Marijoa, empowered to judge even other Celestial Dragons. Led by Figarland Garling (later Elder). Deployed for domestic suppression and God Valley-type interventions.

Limit: Small numbers (9 shown); rarely deployed outside Marijoa; authority contested when Imu intervenes directly.

Five Elders — Mythical Zoan & Abyss

Each Elder holds a Warrior God title and Mythical Zoan transformation: Saturn (Gyuki ox-spider), Mars (Itsumade reptile-bird), Warcury (Fengxi boar), Ju Peter (Sandworm), Nusjuro (Bakotsu skeletal horse). All wield Conqueror's and Observation Haki and can summon via Abyss magic circles for instantaneous teleportation.

Limit: Immortality/regeneration appears linked to Imu's Abyss; Saturn was killed by Imu (ch.1125) indicating revocable power; transformations not yet fully named in canon — fruit names unidentified.

Imu — Devil's Fruit & Sovereign Authority

St. Nerona Imu, King of the World and original First Twenty monarch, occupies Empty Throne. Ate 'Devil's Fruit' granting enormous devil form, immortality, fire generation, Abyss teleportation, healing, arrowhead appendages, demonization of others, and explosive orbs 'Omen'. Wields Conqueror's and Observation Haki (likely Armament). Can revoke Elder immortality.

Limit: Hates Will of D. and Joy Boy; failures include Poneglyph dispersal by Lili; immortality may require continuous Abyss link; not omniscient — relies on Elders and CP0 for intelligence.

Mother Flame & Ancient Weapons Control

Via Vegapunk's Mother Flame energy and control over Ancient Weapon-adjacent technology, Imu annihilated the Kingdom of Lulusia (ch.1086) as demonstration, leaving a sea void. Government historically suppresses Ancient Weapons Pluton, Poseidon, Uranus knowledge via Poneglyph ban.

Limit: Mother Flame requires Vegapunk's production and is limited; use exposes Government's mass-destruction capability and risks global sea-level rise (revealed in Egghead that world is sinking).

Accounts I
The Void Century and the Founding by the First TwentyOne Piece ch.395-397, ch.497, ch.1084-1085, ch.1181

800 years before present (in-universe), revealed 2005-2023 in publication

Eight centuries ago, twenty kingdoms allied to overthrow the Great Kingdom and its ally Joy Boy. After victory, nineteen monarchs moved to Marijoa and became Celestial Dragons, swearing equality via the Empty Throne surrounded by 19 weapons. Queen Nefertari D. Lili of Alabasta alone refused to settle, intending to return home but vanishing; Imu blames her for scattering the indestructible Poneglyphs that preserve the forbidden history. St. Nerona Imu, the twentieth king, secretly took the throne and erased the century from history, banning all research. Creation of World Government, 800-year Void Century censorship, Poneglyph hunt as capital crime, Celestial Dragon caste, and Imu's hidden reign.

The Ohara Incident — Scholars vs. CensorshipOne Piece ch.392-398, ch.1066

22 years before present (in-universe), published 2005

Archaeologists of Ohara (including Nico Robin's mother Nico Olvia and Professor Clover) illegally decipher Poneglyphs to learn Void Century truth. CP9 leader Spandine frames them, the Five Elders order a Buster Call, and Marines annihilate the island and its Tree of Knowledge. Only Robin escapes. The Government publicly justifies genocide as preventing revival of Ancient Weapons. Demonstrates Government's willingness to commit genocide for information control; Robin's bounty at age 8; precedent for Lulusia.

Enies Lobby and Cipher Pol 9 — Judicial TerrorOne Piece ch.375-430 (Enies Lobby arc)

Publication 2005-2006

CP9, the secret Government assassination unit, infiltrates Water 7 to seize Pluton blueprints from shipwright Tom's apprentice Franky and Nico Robin. They invoke Government judicial authority at Enies Lobby, the island of justice where no guilty verdict has ever been overturned, intending to deliver Robin to Impel Down. Straw Hats declare war by burning the Government flag, defeat CP9, and Enies Lobby is destroyed by Buster Call misfire. CP9 disgraced; Straw Hats become world enemies; exposes Government's use of legal theater to mask kidnapping and weapons pursuit.

Accounts II
The Reverie (Levely) — Kings in CouncilOne Piece ch.906-908, ch.1083-1086

Every 4 years in-universe; published 2018, 2023

Royalty of member nations convene at Pangaea Castle. At the 1522 Reverie, issues include Warlord abolition, Fish-Man Island relocation, and Alabasta's King Cobra questioning the Void Century. Imu and the Five Elders use the chaos to assassinate Cobra when he mentions Lili and the Will of D., and attempt to kill Sabo and Wapol who witness Imu on the throne. Simultaneously, Revolutionary Army commanders infiltrate Marijoa to declare war and rescue Kuma. Warlord system abolished (ch.957), Seraphim authorized; Cobra's death blamed on Sabo as propaganda; Sabo branded killer, fueling global unrest.

The Lulusia Erasure — Mother Flame DemonstrationOne Piece ch.1086, ch.1089

Published 2023

To test Vegapunk's Mother Flame and punish a kingdom that withheld tribute and was near a weapon test site, Imu orders the complete annihilation of the Kingdom of Lulusia. A beam from above vaporizes the island, leaving a massive oceanic void and causing global sea-level rise. The Government then erases Lulusia from maps and records as if it never existed, mirroring Ohara's erasure on a larger scale. Reveals Government possesses mass-destruction capability; confirms historical sea-level rise is Government-caused; foreshadows final war's environmental stakes.

God Valley Incident — Holy Knights and the Rocks PiratesOne Piece ch.957, ch.1095-1096, ch.1086

38 years before present (in-universe), revealed 2019-2023

On God Valley island, Celestial Dragons and their slaves (including Kuma's family) are present for a triennial 'Native Hunting Competition'. Rocks D. Xebec's crew attacks, Roger and Garp ally to stop them, and Holy Knight champion Figarland Garling distinguishes himself. The Government subsequently erases God Valley from maps, like Ohara and Lulusia, hiding both its crime and Garp's heroism. Rocks defeated, God Valley vanished from history; Garling's legend; Garp becomes 'Hero of the Marines' despite Government cover-up; Kuma's trauma shapes Revolutionary Army.

Egghead Incident — Science, Seraphim and Saturn's FallOne Piece ch.1073-1125 (Egghead arc)

Published 2023-2024

Straw Hats arrive at Vegapunk's lab island Egghead as Government sends CP0, then Admiral Kizaru and Saint Jaygarcia Saturn to eliminate Vegapunk for researching the Void Century and to secure Mother Flame/Seraphim. Saturn reveals his Gyuki form and Abyss summoning of the other Elders. After Vegapunk's global broadcast reveals the world will sink and the Void Century truth, Saturn fails to stop Joy Boy's Haki (via Emet) and lets the Straw Hats escape. Imu executes Saturn via Abyss, replacing him with Garling as new Warrior God of Science & Defense. First on-screen Elder death and succession; Government's sinking-world secret broadcast globally; Seraphim command hierarchy exposed; Government's scientific supremacy fractured.

The World Government and the Marines are the same thing. — Marines are the Government's public military branch, but Government also includes Cipher Pol, Holy Knights, Celestial Dragons, and the Five Elders/Imu who outrank Fleet Admiral. Marines can be ordered by Elders and can disagree (Fujitora vs Akainu). Conflating them misses the Government's layered authoritarian structure.

The Empty Throne proves the World Government is a democracy of kings. — The Empty Throne is deliberate propaganda. Chapter 908 and 1179 reveal Imu secretly sits there and the Five Elders bow to Imu. The 19 weapons symbolize a broken vow, not equality — nineteen kings submitted to one hidden king.

The Five Elders are just old politicians with no combat power. — Egghead arc (ch.1086-1110) reveals all five are Mythical Zoan transformers with Conqueror's Haki, regeneration, and Abyss teleportation, capable of fighting Yonko commanders. Saturn's death shows they are also expendable to Imu.

The Seven Warlords were Government heroes who protected civilians. — Warlords were legalized pirates used to balance Yonko; members Crocodile (Alabasta) and Doflamingo (Dressrosa) used Government immunity to enslave kingdoms, with Government covering up their crimes until exposure. The system was abolished at Reverie precisely because it harmed member nations.

Imu is a fan-made character or non-canonical. — Imu is fully canonical, introduced in ch.908 (2018) and named/expanded in ch.1085-1086 and ch.1179-1181 as St. Nerona Imu, King of the World. Existence is secret in-universe but not out-of-universe.

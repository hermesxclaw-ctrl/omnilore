Who It Is

Ninth spirit, one of the four great Kings of Hell, commanding 200 legions — some of them of the angelic order of Dominions. Paimon is the most obedient to Lucifer. He appears riding a dromedary camel, preceded by a host of musicians playing trumpets, cymbals, and other instruments. He speaks with a roaring voice until the conjurer compels him to speak clearly. Paimon teaches all arts, sciences, and secret things; bestows dignities; binds any man to the conjurer's will; and reveals the nature of the earth, waters, and winds. His name appears in the earliest demonological sources including the Livre des Esperitz and The Book of Abramelin.

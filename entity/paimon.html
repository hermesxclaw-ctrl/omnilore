Who It Is
Form

Paimon is often depicted as a powerful demon with two hundred legions of demons under his rule. He is described as one of the most powerful demons in hell, more obedient to Lucifer than other kings are. In some accounts, he is depicted as a king with a crown, while in others, he is shown as a demon with a more sinister appearance.

Powers

Granting Wishes Paimon is said to have the power to grant wishes to those who summon him. He is also known for providing knowledge and insight to those who seek his aid.

Accounts

This story tells the tale of a magician who summons Paimon in order to gain power and knowledge. Paimon appears to the magician and grants his wishes, but at a great cost.

This story tells the tale of Paimon's rebellion against Lucifer. Paimon, feeling underappreciated and overlooked, decides to rebel against Lucifer and take control of the underworld.

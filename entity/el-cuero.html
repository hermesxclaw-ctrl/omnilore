Who It Is
Form

A ray-like or skin-like entity, often described as a pelt or hide, with an evil spirit associated with it

Powers

Regeneration El Cuero is said to have the ability to regenerate its pelt or hide, making it a formidable opponent

Incident Log

The hero Ñanco defeats the cuero by fighting it with bunches of quisco cacti tied to his arms and legs, and the monster bled out to death

The cuero is said to be defeated by the quisco cactus, which is used to pierce its pelt or hide

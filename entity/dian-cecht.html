Who It Is

Dian Cécht is the divine physician of the Tuatha Dé Danann. At Mag Tuired, he blessed a well that healed wounded warriors. When his son Miach surpassed him by restoring Nuada's severed arm, Dian Cécht killed him in jealous rage. From Miach's grave grew 365 healing herbs; Dian Cécht scattered them so no mortal would know the complete cure.

“Every wound but two I can mend.”

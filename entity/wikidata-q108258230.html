Who It Is

For fifty years two giants stood guard over the gates of Enies Lobby because they believed a lie — that their captured captains, Dorry and Brogy, were still imprisoned behind those gates. When a long-nosed sniper finally told them the truth, the giants named Oimo and Kashii did what giants do best: they swung their clubs and brought the World Government's justice crashing down.

Attested personality: earnest, loyal, somewhat naive, and deeply honorable. Oimo and Kashii are defined by fidelity to their captains Dorry and Brogy and by shame at having been deceived. They are not cruel; they initially fight Straw Hats because they believe it is their duty to earn Dorry and Brogy's release. Once Usopp (as Sogeking) reveals that Dorry and Brogy are alive and free on Little Garden and that the Marines lied to them for 50 years, Oimo immediately shows remorse, rage at the deception, and gratitude toward the Straw Hats. Powers
Giant Physiology - Superhuman Strength and Durability

As an Elbaf giant, Oimo possesses immense physical strength capable of wielding a club larger than a warship mast, smashing Marine battleships, gates, and buildings, and engaging multiple Straw Hat crew members simultaneously. Durability allows him to withstand cannon fire, Franky's weapons, and blunt force that would kill humans. Lifespan is ~3x human; aging is slow.

Limit: Not invulnerable; can be injured by powerful techniques (e.g., combined Straw Hat attacks, Gear techniques). Strength is purely physical; no Haki explicitly shown for Oimo in early appearance, though Elbaf giants in general are later shown capable of Haki. Size makes him an easy target for artillery and restricts movement in human-scale architecture.

Weapon Mastery - Giant Kanabo/Club

Wields a massive spiked club (kanabo) with both hands, using sweeping strikes, overhead smashes, and ground pounds that create shockwaves. Paired tactics with Kashii (dual giant formation) to block gates.

Limit: Weapon is unwieldy in confined spaces; disarmed when tricked or when choosing not to fight. No ranged ability.

Longevity and Endurance

Giants live ~300-400 years. Oimo's 50-year guard duty without significant aging demonstrates endurance and loyalty over decades on minimal narrative explanation of sustenance/logistics.

Limit: Longevity does not confer immunity to deception or political manipulation.

Paired Combat and Gate Defense

Specialized in defensive gatekeeping and coordinated fighting with Kashii, using their bodies to physically block the massive Gates of Justice at Enies Lobby.

Limit: Effectiveness depends on terrain (gates, open plaza); vulnerable to agile small opponents who can climb or evade.

Accounts I
Youth in Elbaf and Admiration for the Giant Warrior PiratesOne Piece manga Chapter 378 flashback; Vivre Card No. 0292

In-universe: ~60-50 years before present (approx. 50 years before Enies Lobby arc, publication 2005)

Oimo and Kashii grew up on Elbaf idolizing the Giant Warrior Pirates captains Dorry and Brogy, who had left Elbaf a century earlier to pursue warrior glory. As young giants they sought to follow in their footsteps. When Dorry and Brogy were reported captured by the Marines (a false report manufactured by the World Government), Oimo and Kashii set out to rescue them or avenge them, demonstrating the giant code of loyalty to captains. Sets up their vulnerability to Marine deception; establishes their motivation for leaving Elbaf and entering World Government service.

The Fifty-Year Deception - Guardians of Enies LobbyOne Piece manga Chapter 378

In-universe: 50 years prior to Enies Lobby arc (~1450s Sea Circle calendar); publication 2005

After Dorry and Brogy's supposed capture, Marine officers told Oimo and Kashii that the two captains were imprisoned in Enies Lobby and would be executed unless Oimo and Kashii served as gate guardians for the World Government for 100 years. In truth Dorry and Brogy were not imprisoned at all but were dueling on Little Garden. The World Government exploited the giants' honor and naivete to secure two giants as cheap, loyal labor to guard the judicial island. Oimo and Kashii accepted, standing guard at the main gates for 50 years, believing each day of service brought their bosses closer to freedom. Explains Enies Lobby's giant defenses; creates dramatic irony and later moral turning point; illustrates World Government's exploitation of Elbaf.

Enies Lobby Arc - Clash with the Straw Hat PiratesOne Piece manga Chapters 377-384; anime Episodes 266-272

Publication 2005-2006; in-universe Enies Lobby incident 1522

When the Straw Hat Pirates, Franky Family, and Galley-La shipwrights invade Enies Lobby to rescue Nico Robin, they must pass the main gate guarded by Oimo and Kashii. The giants initially repel the invaders with overwhelming strength, smashing ships and scattering the Franky Family. Luffy, Zoro and others engage but are thrown back. The battle appears hopeless until Usopp, disguised as Sogeking, shouts the truth: he met Dorry and Brogy on Little Garden, they are alive, free, and still dueling for honor, and the World Government lied. Oimo and Kashii are stunned, then enraged. Battle shifts from Straw Hats vs. giants to giants vs. Marines; Oimo and Kashii defect, opening the gates and allowing the allied fleet to advance toward the courthouse. Demonstrates Usopp's role as truth-teller and the giants' honorable nature.

Accounts II
Defection and Rampage - Giants Turn on the GovernmentOne Piece manga Chapters 384-386, 390

Publication 2005-2006

Upon learning of the deception, Oimo and Kashii turn their clubs on the Marine and Government forces inside Enies Lobby, smashing the courthouse gates and attacking the very soldiers they had served. They declare the Straw Hats are not enemies and effectively ally with them, helping to clear a path and later assisting the Franky Family and Galley-La in retreat. Their rampage symbolizes the collapse of Enies Lobby's judicial facade. After the Buster Call is invoked, they help allies escape the crumbling island. Enies Lobby's defenses fall; World Government loses giant guardians; Oimo and Kashii are branded traitors but escape with the allied fleet via the sea train and Franky's intervention. Their honor is restored.

Aftermath and Return Voyage - 'Where They Are Now' Cover StoryOne Piece manga Chapters 430-431 cover story and Chapter 441-442 epilogue; databook summaries

Publication 2006

After Enies Lobby's destruction, Oimo and Kashii are shown among the escapees. In the cover story series and subsequent narration, they leave Enies Lobby with the Franky Family/Galley-La alliance, intending to return to Elbaf and reunite with Dorry and Brogy. They are later depicted sailing away from the ruins, no longer bound to the Government, free giants once more. This sets up their eventual return to Elbaf. Closes Enies Lobby arc for the giants; restores their narrative to Elbaf lineage; foreshadows Elbaf arc.

Return to Elbaf - Reunion in the Elbaf ArcOne Piece manga Chapters 1130-1135 (Elbaf Arc, ongoing)

Publication 2024-2025; in-universe 1522 (shortly after Egghead)

With the Straw Hats finally arriving at Elbaf, Oimo and Kashii are shown already returned to their homeland, reunited with Dorry, Brogy, and the new generation of giants (including Hajrudin, Gerd, Goldberg). They appear in crowd scenes welcoming the Straw Hats and in the Elbaf village, confirming they have reintegrated into giant society and serve as witnesses to the Straw Hats' arrival and to the unfolding crisis involving the World Government's interest in Elbaf. Their presence bridges the 20-year real-world gap between Enies Lobby and Elbaf. Pays off long-term foreshadowing; re-establishes Oimo as part of Elbaf's warrior culture ahead of final saga conflicts; confirms survival and status.

Oimo and Kashii are the captains of the Giant Warrior Pirates. — They are admirers/juniors from Elbaf, not captains. Captains are Dorry and Brogy, who led the Giant Warrior Pirates 100 years earlier. Oimo/Kashii were never listed as crew members in Vivre Card.

Oimo was brainwashed or magically controlled by the World Government. — No mind control or Devil Fruit involved. The control was purely deception - a lie about Dorry and Brogy's imprisonment. Oimo acted out of honor and naivete, not hypnosis.

Oimo and Kashii are the same character or interchangeable. — They are distinct individuals with different helmets, hair, and Vivre Card entries (0292 vs 0293). Oimo has reddish-brown hair and horned helmet; Kashii has darker hair/beard and different helmet. They are always paired but individually named.

Oimo appears as a major playable Straw Hat ally in every One Piece game. — Oimo is a minor character; he appears as NPC/boss in Enies Lobby stages (Pirate Warriors, Treasure Cruise) but is not a core playable protagonist and is absent from many games' story modes.

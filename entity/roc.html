Who It Is

The Roc is a fascinating and complex creature, representing the power and dominance of the natural world. Its mythology is rich and multifaceted, offering a wealth of insights into the human experience.

The Roc is said to be a solitary creature, preferring to hunt alone and strike fear into the hearts of its prey. It is known for its intelligence and cunning, often using its size and strength to outmaneuver its opponents. Despite its fearsome reputation, the Roc is also said to be a noble creature, respecting the boundaries of its territory and avoiding conflict whenever possible.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Roc is a massive bird of prey, often depicted as a giant eagle or vulture with a wingspan that can reach up to 10 meters. Its feathers are said to be as black as coal, with a subtle sheen that hints at its immense size. Its eyes are a piercing yellow, said to be able to see for miles. The Roc's beak is long and curved, capable of tearing flesh and crushing bone with ease.

Symbols — The Roc is often associated with the symbol of the sun, representing its power and dominance over the natural world. In some cultures, the Roc is also linked to the symbol of the phoenix, representing its cyclical nature and rebirth.

Domains — The Roc is said to inhabit the deserts and mountains of the Middle East and South Asia, where it can be found soaring through the skies or perched atop a rocky outcropping. Its territory is said to be vast, stretching across entire regions and encompassing a wide range of ecosystems.

Who It Is

An enormous bird of prey, the roc can carry off elephants and adult dragons. Its wingspan exceeds 80 feet, and its feathers are the color of storm clouds. Rocs nest on mountain peaks and patrol territories hundreds of miles wide, attacking anything they perceive as a threat.

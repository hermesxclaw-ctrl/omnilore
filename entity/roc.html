Roc

also known asRukhRuk
TypePathfinder Iconic Monster
Who It Is
Form

The Roc is a massive bird of prey, often depicted as a giant eagle or vulture with a wingspan that can reach up to 10 meters. Its feathers are said to be as black as coal, with a subtle sheen that hints at its immense size. Its eyes are a piercing yellow, said to be able to see for miles. The Roc's beak is long and curved, capable of tearing flesh and crushing bone with ease.

Powers

Omnipotent Wingspan The Roc's wingspan is said to be so vast that it can cover an entire city block, allowing it to soar through the skies with ease and strike fear into the hearts of its prey.

Talons of Steel The Roc's talons are said to be as sharp as steel, capable of tearing flesh and crushing bone with ease. Its beak is long and curved, perfect for tearing apart its prey.

Accounts

A group of travelers stumble upon a hidden city made of brass, where they encounter a massive Roc that has been terrorizing the local population. The travelers must use all their wits and cunning to outmaneuver the Roc and escape the city alive.

Who It Is

Dede Korkut is the legendary bard and sage of the Oghuz Turks — inventor of the kopuz instrument and narrator of the great epic tales. Tormented by dreams of mortality, he sought a place where death could not find him. Finally, he played his kopuz on the Syr Darya River until death came. UNESCO recognizes his book as a masterpiece of oral heritage.

Attested in The Book of Dede Korkut (compiled 15th-16th century)Era Oghuz period (c. 8th-11th century)

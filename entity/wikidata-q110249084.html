The King of Fighters '94 - The First Tournament and the Orochi ForeshadowingThe King of Fighters '94 (SNK, Neo Geo MVS/AES, 1994); director Masanori Kuwasashi, producer Takashi Nishiyama

1994 (Heisei era) - Orochi Saga prologue

Heir Rugal Bernstein hosts a 3-on-3 tournament inviting teams from Fatal Fury, Art of Fighting, Ikari Warriors and Psycho Soldier. Kyo Kusanagi leads Japan Team (Benimaru Nikaido, Goro Daimon) to victory and confronts Rugal, who self-destructs his aircraft carrier. The game establishes the Kusanagi clan's pyrokinetic heritage linked to the Yamata no Orochi legend and the Kusanagi no Tsurugi. No direct participation by Sinobu Amou is documented in this installment; it is included as the foundational narrative for any KOF-affiliated character. Establishes annual tournament premise and Sacred Treasures mythology that frames all later KOF characters. If Sinobu Amou exists within this continuity, this is the earliest possible chronological anchor.

The Orochi Saga Climax - KOF '95 to '97: Blood Riot and SealingThe King of Fighters '95 (1995), '96 (1996), '97 (1997) - SNK

1995-1997

Kyo's rival Iori Yagami appears, wielding violet flames and the Riot of the Blood. Goenitz, then Orochi itself, seeks to break the 1,800-year seal maintained by the Kusanagi, Yata (Kagura), and Yasakani (Yagami) clans. In KOF '97, the Three Sacred Treasures team (Kyo, Iori, Chizuru Kagura) defeats Orochi, re-sealing it. The saga defines clan-based powers and the cost of Orochi blood. Sealing of Orochi; Iori and Leona suffer ongoing Riot of the Blood instability; establishes template for inherited supernatural abilities.

The NESTS Chronicles - KOF '99 to 2001: Abduction and CloningThe King of Fighters '99: Millennium Battle (1999), KOF 2000 (2000), KOF 2001 (2001) - SNK / Eolith / BrezzaSoft

1999-2001

A new protagonist, K' (K-Dash), is introduced as a former NESTS test subject stripped of memory and infused with Kyo's flames via Kyo's DNA. NESTS, a high-tech cartel, creates Kyo clones (Krizalid, K9999) and cyborgs (Maxima). K' rebels with partner Maxima, ultimately destroying NESTS leader Igniz in orbit. The arc shifts from mythology to bio-engineering and introduces concepts of stolen powers and manufactured fighters. Destruction of NESTS; K' and Kula gain freedom; K9999 (later reworked as Krohnen) removed due to legal likeness issues; establishes precedent for characters created or altered by organizations.

Accounts II
Tales of Ash - KOF 2003 to XIII: The Green Flames and Those From the PastThe King of Fighters 2003 (2003), KOF XI (2005), KOF XII (2009), KOF XIII (2010) - SNK Playmore

2003-2010

Enigmatic youth Ash Crimson, wielding green flames, steals the powers of Chizuru and Iori, seemingly as a villain, later revealed to be preventing Those From the Past (Mukai, Magaki, Saiki) from resurrecting Orochi for their master. Ash sacrifices himself to seal Saiki (his ancestor) in a time paradox, erasing himself from history. The arc introduces time travel, theft of Sacred Treasure powers, and ambiguous heroism. Ash's erasure creates a temporal anomaly that later births Shun'ei's powers; Those From the Past are defeated but Verse remains as residue.

The Shun'ei / Verse Saga - KOF XIV to XV: New Protagonist and Multiverse EchoesThe King of Fighters XIV (2016), The King of Fighters XV (2022) - SNK

2016-2022

Antonov, a Russian billionaire, revives the tournament. Shun'ei, a Chinese youth with hydrokinetic and fragmented Verse powers (inherited from Ash's paradox), becomes the new protagonist alongside mentor Tung Fu Rue and friend Meitenkun. The entity Verse, formed from souls of past fighters (including Ash, Rugal, Orochi), is defeated, and in KOF XV, the resurrected Orochi, Rugal, and Ash return via Verse's shattering. Otoma=Raga and Re Verse serve as final bosses. The saga explicitly connects all previous arcs via Verse. Ash Crimson is restored to life; Shun'ei learns to control his powers; franchise soft-reboots with new generation while honoring legacy characters. Any minor or mobile-original characters are typically reintroduced here via DLC or All Star crossovers.

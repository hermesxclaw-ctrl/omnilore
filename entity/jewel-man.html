Who It Is

A mining and gem-cutting robot with a glittering crystal-studded body. Jewel Man's armor is encrusted with diamonds, rubies, and sapphires — both decorative and functional, as he can launch them at high velocity. He can also create a protective barrier of floating gems around himself.

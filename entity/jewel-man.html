Accounts

The legend of the Tide Jewels tells the story of two magical gems, hirutama and mitsutama, which were used by the Sea God to control the tides. The Jewel Man is said to be connected to these jewels and to have the power to control the ocean's currents.

The story of the Wish Ring tells the tale of a magical ring that grants wishes to those who possess it. The Jewel Man is said to be connected to this ring and to have the power to grant wishes to those who seek its aid.

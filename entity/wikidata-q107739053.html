Who It Is

When the Straw Hats first met the flamboyant, saxophone-toting Mr. 8 at Whisky Peak, they thought he was just another bounty hunter drunk on welcome parties — they did not know he was Igaram, the straight-laced captain of the Alabasta royal guard, who would fake his own death in a fireball over the sea to buy his princess a chance to save her kingdom.

Attested as intensely loyal, formal, and paternal toward Princess Nefertari Vivi, whom he has guarded since childhood. Speaks in polite, slightly old-fashioned, deferential Japanese (desu/masu forms) even when undercover. Prone to dramatic, tearful emotion — frequently weeps with exaggerated facial expressions, a common Oda comedy device for Alabasta retainers. As Mr. 8, adopts a cool, musician persona but quickly drops it when Vivi's safety is threatened. Willing to sacrifice himself without hesitation, as shown by acting as a decoy. Etymology — No official etymology provided by Eiichiro Oda in SBS or databooks. Fan speculation links the name to Japanese surnames Igarashi/Igaram but remains unattested. Oda has not confirmed a pun or wordplay origin as he has for many Baroque Works agents.

Pronunciation — Japanese: i-ga-ra-mu [iɡaɾamɯ] ; English Viz/ Funimation: ee-gah-RAHM / IH-guh-ram

Aliases — Igarappoi (イガラッポイ, nickname used by Princess Vivi), Mr. 8 (ミスター・エイト, Baroque Works codename), Captain Igaram (イガラム隊長)

Powers
Expert Marksmanship - Saxophone Rifle

Igaram's saxophone is a concealed firearm. By playing or pressing keys, it fires bullets like a rifle/machine gun. Demonstrated at Whisky Peak firing on Zoro and later against Baroque Works agents. Treated as a gimmick weapon rather than superhuman ability.

Limit: Requires ammunition, line of sight, and is ineffective against high-tier swordsmen or Logia users; Zoro deflects the bullets easily. No rapid-fire feat beyond short bursts. Weapon can be destroyed or jammed.

Igarappoi (Igaram Poi) - Rapid-Fire Hair Shot

Comedic technique where Igaram fires his curled hair like projectiles by shaking his head. Named as a pun on his nickname. Appears in his Vivre Card technique list and manga attack name callout.

Limit: Purely short-range, low damage, used for distraction or comedy. Not effective against serious combatants. No evidence of lethal force.

Espionage and Disguise

Successfully infiltrated Baroque Works as Officer Agent Mr. 8 for an extended period, gathering intelligence on Crocodile's Operation Utopia alongside Vivi. Able to maintain cover among bounty hunters at Whisky Peak.

Limit: Cover was eventually blown by Nico Robin (Miss All Sunday), who identified him and Vivi. Not a trained assassin on par with higher-numbered agents; his combat rank is low within Baroque Works.

Leadership and Tactics - Royal Guard Captain

Commands Alabasta's royal guard, coordinates palace defense and escort missions. Demonstrated organizing the defense of Alubarna and later the royal family's voyage to Reverie.

Limit: No superhuman strength or Haki demonstrated. Tactical ability is conventional military; outmatched by Baroque Works' Devil Fruit users and requires Straw Hat intervention to succeed.

Accounts I
Infiltration of Baroque Works as Mr. 8 - Whisky PeakOne Piece manga Chapters 107-109, anime Episodes 64-65

1999-2000 (in-universe: Age of Pirates, 22 years after Roger's execution)

Princess Vivi and Igaram infiltrate the criminal organization Baroque Works to discover the identity of its boss. Under codenames Miss Wednesday and Mr. 8, they rise to Officer Agent status. At Whisky Peak, a town of bounty hunters posing as welcoming hosts, Igaram and Vivi attempt to capture the Straw Hats to maintain cover. When Zoro defeats 100 bounty hunters and Vivi's identity is questioned, Igaram reveals his true loyalty, tries to protect Vivi, and is confronted by the sudden arrival of Miss All Sunday and the Unluckies (Mr. 13 and Miss Friday). Igaram's cover is blown; he and Vivi are forced to flee Whisky Peak with the Straw Hats. The Straw Hats learn of Alabasta's crisis and agree to escort Vivi home, launching the Alabasta arc.

The Decoy Explosion - Presumed Death at SeaOne Piece manga Chapter 113, anime Episode 65

1999-2000 publication; in-universe days after Whisky Peak

To allow Vivi and the Straw Hats to escape to Alabasta undetected, Igaram disguises himself as Vivi aboard a decoy ship and sails separately from Whisky Peak. Nico Robin, acting on Crocodile's orders, intercepts the ship. She uses her Hana Hana no Mi powers to detonate the vessel, engulfing Igaram in a massive explosion at sea. The Straw Hats and Vivi believe him dead; Vivi is devastated and Igaram's apparent sacrifice motivates her resolve. Creates a major emotional beat and raises stakes; Vivi's grief and guilt drive her determination. For several volumes readers believe Igaram is dead, until his survival is revealed in a cover story. Establishes Robin's lethality before her later alliance with the Straw Hats.

Survival and Defense of Alabasta - Operation Utopia ClimaxOne Piece manga Chapters 180-216, anime Episodes 110-128

2000-2002 publication; in-universe Alabasta civil war

Heavily bandaged but alive, Igaram survived the explosion — later explained that he was rescued or managed to survive the blast — and returns to Alubarna during Crocodile's Operation Utopia. He reunites with Chaka, Pell, and King Cobra, helps coordinate the royal guard's defense against rebel and Baroque forces, and is present for Crocodile's defeat by Luffy and the collapse of the civil war instigated by Baroque Works' Dance Powder and false flag operations. Alabasta is saved; Baroque Works is dismantled; Igaram resumes his post as captain of the guard. His survival provides emotional payoff and restores the royal household. The arc cements his role as the kingdom's loyal steward rather than a casualty.

Accounts II
From the Decks of the World - Alabasta's Peace (Cover Story)One Piece manga Chapter 631 cover, 'From the Decks of the World' Vol. 20, anime Episode 546

2011-2012 publication; in-universe two years after Alabasta (post-timeskip)

In the post-timeskip world-cover arc, Igaram is shown in Alabasta alongside Chaka, Pell, Vivi, and Karoo, reading news of the Straw Hats' return after two years. He appears healthy, in full guard uniform, indicating continued service and stability in Alabasta after Crocodile's defeat. The image confirms his long-term survival and ongoing role. Confirms Alabasta's recovery and Igaram's enduring position. Bridges his character to the Reverie arc without requiring a full chapter appearance.

Escort to the Reverie (Levely) - Mary Geoise VoyageOne Piece manga Chapters 823-908, anime Episodes 777, 878-889

2017-2018 publication; in-universe shortly after Dressrosa

As captain of the guard, Igaram accompanies King Cobra, Vivi, Chaka, Pell, and Terracotta aboard the Alabasta ship en route to the Reverie at Mary Geoise, the world council of kings. He frets over Vivi's safety, weeps comically when Vivi is admired, and stands guard during the tense political gathering where Cobra questions the World Government about the Will of D. and the Poneglyphs. He is present when the Nefertari Family is targeted by the machinations of Imu and the Five Elders, though he does not directly confront them. Places Igaram at the center of the Reverie's pivotal lore revelations. His presence underscores the Nefertari Family's vulnerability and loyalty. Sets up later Alabasta-related fallout after Cobra's death (Chapter 1054 onward), where Igaram's fate is left pending but he is last seen with the royal family.

Whisky Peak Aftermath and Baroque Works Intelligence LegacyOne Piece Vivre Card Databook, One Piece Blue Deep: Characters World (2012), SBS Volume 19

2002-2018 paratext publication

Supplementary materials detail Igaram's two-year infiltration of Baroque Works with Vivi, his codename assignment logic (Mr. 8 paired with Miss Monday per number-day pairing), and his saxophone-weapon design notes by Oda. SBS and databooks confirm his age (48 at debut, 50 post-timeskip), birthday September 12, and his formal title as Captain of the Alabasta Royal Guard, clarifying that his Baroque Works rank was a cover, not a true allegiance. Provides out-of-universe confirmation of his role and prevents conflation with genuine Baroque Works villains. Supports power-scaling and timeline consistency.

Igaram is a Devil Fruit user because his hair shoots like bullets. — He has no Devil Fruit. The hair-shot is a gag technique (Igarappoi) and his saxophone is a concealed gun — both are conventional weapons/comedy, not powers. Vivre Card confirms no Devil Fruit.

Igaram is Vivi's father. — Vivi's father is King Nefertari Cobra. Igaram is her captain of the guard and father figure, married to Terracotta, not a blood relative. His paternal behavior leads new viewers to assume a familial link.

Igaram died permanently and his Reverie appearance is anime filler. — His survival is manga canon (Chapter 216 onward) and he appears in the manga's Reverie arc (Chapters 823, 908). The anime faithfully adapts the manga; it is not filler.

The Approach

When a malevolent alien amoeba crash-lands in the South Pacific, it seizes a gentle mata mata turtle and twists it into a colossal, armored engine of destruction—Kamoebas, the last and most formidable of the Space Amoeba's monstrous puppets.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of monster violence, destruction, and a self-sacrificial death. No graphic gore or sexual content.
Identity

Kamoebas

Pronunciation kah-MOH-bahs

Known As

KamēbaKamoebas (English dub)

Etymology

The name 'Kamoebas' is derived from the Japanese word 'kame' (turtle) combined with a suffix reminiscent of 'amoeba', reflecting its origin as a mutated mata mata turtle controlled by the Space Amoeba.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese popular culture; created by Toho Company, Ltd.

Disputed Identifications

Kamoebas is sometimes confused with other turtle-like kaiju such as Gamera (from Daiei), but they are separate characters from different franchises. No known disputes over its identity.

Description

Appearance

Kamoebas is a colossal, turtle-like creature with a massive, dome-shaped shell covered in spikes or rough ridges. Its body is reminiscent of a mata mata turtle, with a broad, flat head and a wide mouth. It has four sturdy legs and a short tail. Its skin is dark and leathery, with a somewhat prehistoric appearance.

Symbols & Iconography

Kamoebas's shell and reptilian features evoke endurance and ancient power. In the film, it symbolizes the corrupted natural world under alien control, a common theme in kaiju films of the era.

Behaviour & Personality

Under the Space Amoeba's control, Kamoebas is aggressive and destructive, attacking human settlements and engaging in combat with other monsters. When freed from control, it becomes disoriented and berserk, lashing out without direction.

Domains & Habitats

Kamoebas is a terrestrial and aquatic creature, dwelling on the fictional Selgio Island and its surrounding waters. It is not tied to a specific realm beyond the film's setting.

Version Differences

Kamoebas appears only in the 1970 film. There are no significant alternate versions, though it has been referenced in other media such as video games (e.g., Godzilla: Destroy All Monsters Melee and Godzilla: Save the Earth), where its portrayal is consistent with the film's design.

Stories & Myths

Creation and Rampage

Source Space Amoeba (1970)Culture JapanesePeriod Contemporary (1970)

Summary

After the Space Amoeba loses control of Gezora and Ganimes, it possesses a mata mata turtle and mutates it into Kamoebas, along with a second Ganimes. The two monsters attack the human camp on Selgio Island.

Participants

Consequences

The humans realize the monsters are vulnerable to supersonic waves and use bats to disrupt the Amoeba's control. Freed, Kamoebas and Ganimes fight each other, and the humans trigger a volcanic eruption that engulfs them. Makoto Obata sacrifices himself to destroy the Space Amoeba.

Who It Is

In the shifting twilight between light and darkness, there walks a figure whose loyalty is as fluid as his aim—a wandering marksman whose true name hides more than his eyepatch ever could. Xigbar, the Freeshooter of Organization XIII, speaks in riddles and fires with precision, but his allegiance may be the greatest mystery of all.

Xigbar is nonchalant, sarcastic, and playful, often mocking others with a laid-back attitude. He is cunning and manipulative, enjoying psychological games and toying with his opponents. He is fiercely loyal to the Organization's cause, but his true allegiance is to Master Xehanort. He is also a skilled fighter, using his agility and marksmanship to outmaneuver foes.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Xigbar' is an anagram of 'Braig', with the 'X' added as a marker of Organization XIII membership. 'Braig' is a variant of the Gaelic word 'breug' meaning 'lie' or 'deception', fitting his deceptive nature.

Pronunciation — /ˈzɪɡbɑːr/ ZIG-bar

Aliases — Braig, No. II, Freeshooter, The Freeshooter

Ownership — Owned by Square Enix and Disney Interactive Studios; created by Tetsuya Nomura.

Powers
Arrowguns

Xigbar wields a pair of arrowguns that can fire energy projectiles. He can also transform them into different forms, such as a sniper rifle or dual pistols, for various combat styles.

Limit: Requires ammunition (energy), and his accuracy can be affected by external factors.

Spatial Distortion

Xigbar can distort space to teleport short distances, creating portals to evade attacks or reposition himself.

Limit: Limited range and requires concentration.

Darkness Manipulation

As a Nobody, Xigbar can manipulate darkness to a degree, using it for attacks or to enhance his abilities.

Limit: Not as powerful as a true wielder of darkness.

Time Manipulation (incomplete)

In the game files, Xigbar has an unused ability that would have allowed him to slow down time, but this was never implemented in the final game.

Limit: Unused, not canonical.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Xigbar is a tall, lean man with fair skin and short, spiky silver hair. He wears an eyepatch over his left eye, and his most distinctive feature is the large scar across his left cheek and eye, which he received from Master Xehanort. His Organization XIII coat is black with a hood, and he carries two arrowguns that can transform into various forms. As Braig, he has brown hair and no eyepatch, but retains the scar on his left cheek.

Symbols — His symbol is the Roman numeral 'II', representing his rank in the Organization. His arrowguns are often depicted with a crosshair motif, symbolizing his role as a sniper.

Domains — As a member of Organization XIII, he operates from The World That Never Was, but he is often seen in various Disney worlds during missions. As Braig, he was a resident of Radiant Garden before its fall.

Accounts I
Origins as BraigKingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep

Before the events of the first Kingdom Hearts

Braig was a resident of Radiant Garden who conspired with Master Xehanort to obtain the Keyblade. He was involved in the experiments that led to the creation of the Heartless. He was present during the Keyblade War and was badly scarred by Xehanort. He later became a test subject for Xehanort's experiments, leading to his eventual transformation into a Nobody. Braig's actions contributed to the events leading to the Keyblade War and the fall of Radiant Garden. He became Xigbar, a member of Organization XIII.

Accounts II
Xigbar in Organization XIIIKingdom Hearts II, Kingdom Hearts 358/2 Days

During the events of the first Kingdom Hearts and its sequel

As Xigbar, he serves as the Organization's marksman and intelligence gatherer. He is involved in the conspiracy to use Roxas to complete the Kingdom Hearts. He fights Sora multiple times, testing his abilities. He is ultimately destroyed by Sora but is later restored as Braig. His defeat contributes to the fall of Organization XIII. His knowledge of the true nature of the Organization is passed on to Sora.

Return as BraigKingdom Hearts III

After the events of Kingdom Hearts II

After being restored, Braig is manipulated by Master Xehanort to become a vessel for his heart. He is forced to fight against Sora and his friends, but is eventually freed when Xehanort is defeated. He survives and returns to Radiant Garden. Braig is freed from Xehanort's control and is able to live a normal life.

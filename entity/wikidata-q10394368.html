The Approach

In the shifting twilight between light and darkness, there walks a figure whose loyalty is as fluid as his aim—a wandering marksman whose true name hides more than his eyepatch ever could. Xigbar, the Freeshooter of Organization XIII, speaks in riddles and fires with precision, but his allegiance may be the greatest mystery of all.

Identity

Xigbar

Pronunciation /ˈzɪɡbɑːr/ ZIG-bar

Known As

BraigNo. IIFreeshooterThe Freeshooter

Etymology

The name 'Xigbar' is an anagram of 'Braig', with the 'X' added as a marker of Organization XIII membership. 'Braig' is a variant of the Gaelic word 'breug' meaning 'lie' or 'deception', fitting his deceptive nature.

Cultural Ownership

Owned by Square Enix and Disney Interactive Studios; created by Tetsuya Nomura.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans initially speculated that Xigbar might be a disguised version of another character, but canon confirms he is the Nobody of Braig. His true allegiance and ultimate goals have been subject to fan theories, but official sources clarify his role as a vessel for Master Xehanort and his eventual return as Braig.

Description

Appearance

Xigbar is a tall, lean man with fair skin and short, spiky silver hair. He wears an eyepatch over his left eye, and his most distinctive feature is the large scar across his left cheek and eye, which he received from Master Xehanort. His Organization XIII coat is black with a hood, and he carries two arrowguns that can transform into various forms. As Braig, he has brown hair and no eyepatch, but retains the scar on his left cheek.

Symbols & Iconography

His symbol is the Roman numeral 'II', representing his rank in the Organization. His arrowguns are often depicted with a crosshair motif, symbolizing his role as a sniper.

Behaviour & Personality

Xigbar is nonchalant, sarcastic, and playful, often mocking others with a laid-back attitude. He is cunning and manipulative, enjoying psychological games and toying with his opponents. He is fiercely loyal to the Organization's cause, but his true allegiance is to Master Xehanort. He is also a skilled fighter, using his agility and marksmanship to outmaneuver foes.

Domains & Habitats

As a member of Organization XIII, he operates from The World That Never Was, but he is often seen in various Disney worlds during missions. As Braig, he was a resident of Radiant Garden before its fall.

Version Differences

As Braig, he is more straightforward and less cryptic, but still shows a penchant for mischief. As Xigbar, he is more enigmatic and seems to know more than he lets on, often hinting at larger schemes. In Kingdom Hearts III, after being restored as Braig, he retains some of Xigbar's mannerisms but is more sincere.

Origins as Braig

Source Kingdom Hearts Birth by SleepCulture Video gamePeriod Before the events of the first Kingdom Hearts

Summary

Braig was a resident of Radiant Garden who conspired with Master Xehanort to obtain the Keyblade. He was involved in the experiments that led to the creation of the Heartless. He was present during the Keyblade War and was badly scarred by Xehanort. He later became a test subject for Xehanort's experiments, leading to his eventual transformation into a Nobody.

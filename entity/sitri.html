Who It Is

Twelfth spirit, a Prince commanding 60 legions. Sitri appears initially with a leopard's face and griffin's wings, then transforms into a beautiful man at the conjurer's command — but a man whose eyes burn with flame, hinting at the dangerous passion he embodies. He governs carnal desire, sexual love, and lust. He inflames men and women with passion for one another and causes them to reveal themselves naked if so commanded. Sitri's office is entirely within the realm of erotic compulsion, making him one of the more dangerous spirits to invoke, as his influence bypasses the will.

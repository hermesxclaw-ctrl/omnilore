Form

Clickers are infected humans with a fungal growth on their heads, which emits a high-pitched clicking noise. They have a distinctive appearance, with a pale, decaying skin tone and a fungal growth on their heads.

Echolocation Clickers use their high-pitched clicking noise to emit a sound wave that helps them locate their prey.

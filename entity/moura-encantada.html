Who It Is
Form

The Moura Encantada is often described as a beautiful and seductive maiden, with a strong connection to nature and the supernatural. She is said to inhabit liminal spaces, such as crossroads, bridges, and mountain passes, and is often associated with the elements of earth, air, and water.

Powers

Shape-shifting The Moura Encantada is said to have the ability to shape-shift into various forms, including animals, plants, and even inanimate objects.

Stone-building The Moura Encantada is known for her incredible strength and ability to build structures out of stone, often with magical properties.

Elemental control The Moura Encantada is said to have control over the elements of earth, air, and water, allowing her to manipulate the natural world to her will.

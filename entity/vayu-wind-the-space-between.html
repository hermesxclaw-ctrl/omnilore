Vayu (Wind / the Space Between)

Who It Is
Form

Vayu is often depicted as a powerful wind god with wings, carrying a bow and arrow, and riding a chariot.

Powers

Wind Control Vayu has the power to control the winds, summoning storms and whirlwinds, and calming the air with a gentle breeze.

Accounts

Vayu and Indra, the king of the gods, engage in a fierce battle, with Vayu using his wind powers to challenge Indra's authority.

Vayu is described as a divinity of the wind with two natures, one good and one evil, reflecting the dualistic nature of the universe.

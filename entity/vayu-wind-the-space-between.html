Who It Is

Vayu is the yazata of the wind and the intervening space ('the between') that separates heaven, earth, and the abode of darkness. Uniquely among yazatas, Vayu has a dual, ambivalent nature: the Avesta speaks of a 'good Vayu' (who serves Ahura Mazda and aids the righteous) and an 'evil Vayu' (who is aligned with the destructive spirit Angra Mainyu). This duality reflects the understanding of wind as both life-giving breath and destructive storm. Vayu's primary domain is the 'space between' (Av. vayehus) — the intermediate zone of the atmosphere (hamistagan) where souls dwell after death until the final judgment. He is thus a psychopomp: his wind carries the soul from the body to the Chinvat Bridge. Vayu is invoked for swiftness, for victory in battle (as the wind that carries arrows), and for safe passage of the soul through the intermediate state.

Stories & Myths

Death of Cermait

Source Lebor Gabála ÉrennCulture IrishPeriod Mythological cycle

Summary

Cermait had an affair with Lugh's wife Buach. Lugh killed him in retaliation. The Dagda mourned him with tears of blood, then later revived him using a healing staff while traveling east with his body.

Participants

Cermait

Lugh

Buach

Dagda

Consequences

Cermait's three sons later avenged his death, and eventually became joint High Kings of Ireland.

Variants

The revival episode may have variants; the Dindsenchas story of Conan Honey-mouth may be a parallel.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Kullervo is often depicted as a young man with a troubled expression, symbolizing his tragic fate.

Symbols — Kullervo is associated with the symbol of the axe, which represents his connection to the land and his people.

Domains — Kullervo's stories take place in the forests and fields of ancient Finland, reflecting the country's rich natural heritage.

The Story of KullervoThe Kalevala by Elias Lönnrot

19th century

Kullervo's story revolves around his tragic fate, which is sealed from birth. He is cursed by his father, Kalervo, and is forced to live a life of hardship and suffering. Kullervo's actions lead to the downfall of his family and the destruction of his community.

Who It Is
Form

Hornet Man is a robotic character with a large, menacing hornet-like body and eight legs. He has a distinctive yellow and black striped pattern on his body, with a red and black striped helmet.

Powers

Hornet Blast Hornet Man's primary attack, which involves releasing a swarm of hornet-like projectiles at his enemies.

Accounts

According to the game's backstory, Hornet Man was created by Dr. Wily as part of his plan to take over the world.

Hornet Man first appeared in Mega Man 9, where he was one of the main antagonists.

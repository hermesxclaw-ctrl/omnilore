Who It Is

An agricultural robot designed for pollination and crop maintenance. Hornet Man has a yellow-and-black striped body, insectoid wings, and a stinger tail. He controls swarms of mechanical bees that can harvest crops, pollinate flowers — or attack enemies with coordinated sting strikes.

Who It Is

Tala is the goddess of stars — the youngest of Bathala's celestial children. She did not fight for dominion like her siblings but guides travelers through darkness. Ancient Tagalogs prayed to her for navigation, and the morning and evening stars were considered manifestations of her presence. She represents the quiet power of illumination.

Attested in Oral tradition / Tagalog mythologyEra Pre-colonial Philippines

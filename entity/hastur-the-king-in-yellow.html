Name & Tongue

Etymology — Derived from the Arabic word 'hastur', meaning 'the hidden one', and the Latin 'king in yellow', referencing the mysterious and elusive nature of the entity.

Pronunciation — HA-stur (The King in YELL-ow)

Aliases — The Unspeakable One, The King in Yellow, The Yellow Sign

Ownership — Primarily associated with the Cthulhu Mythos, a shared universe of horror fiction created by H.P. Lovecraft and his contemporaries.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Hastur is often depicted as a tall, thin figure with a yellow or golden complexion, and a crown or sign on his forehead. His appearance is said to be both captivating and terrifying.

Symbols — The Yellow Sign, a symbol associated with Hastur, is often depicted as a yellow or golden circle with a black center. This symbol is said to hold great power and significance in the Cthulhu Mythos.

Domains — Hastur is said to reside in a realm known as the 'Yellow Sign', a place of great beauty and terror.

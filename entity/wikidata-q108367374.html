Who It Is

At fourteen she had already written songs that professionals wanted to buy, skipped two grades, and crossed an ocean from New York to Tokyo not to become an idol — but to keep her best friend from burning out trying to be one. Mia Taylor didn't come to Nijigasaki to shine; she came to write the music that would let someone else shine, until the club taught her that a songwriter can step into the light too.

Attested across game and anime: Cool, blunt, and outwardly aloof ('kuudere' leaning), with deadpan humor and direct speech including occasional English phrases ('Oh my god', 'Come on!'). Highly confident in musical ability due to prodigy status — has composed for professional artists since age 12 in New York. Privately caring and loyal, especially to best friend Lanzhu Zhong, whom she followed to Japan out of concern. Powers
Prodigy Music Composition and Arrangement

Attested as having composed and provided songs to professional artists in New York from age 12. In-universe, recognized as genius songwriter/arranger. Composes idol songs for Lanzhu ('Eutopia' co-composed) and later for herself and Nijigasaki ('Toy Doll', 'Stars We Chase'). Plays piano at concert level, also guitar and keyboard programming.

Limit: Not supernatural; requires instruments, time, and inspiration. Suffers creative block when emotionally conflicted (e.g., Lanzhu's pressure). Cannot perform without practice — initially poor dancer/singer compared to composition skill, improves through training.

Bilingual Fluency (English/Japanese) and Absolute Pitch Implied

Native English speaker fluent in Japanese (with accent noted in anime). Frequently code-switches. Demonstrates absolute pitch in bond episode where she identifies off-key notes instantly.

Limit: Japanese occasionally imperfect (played for comedy); English lines limited to short phrases in Japanese release.

School Idol Performance (Singing/Dancing)

After joining Nijigasaki, performs as school idol. Solo songs: 'Toy Doll' (2021 single), 'Stars We Chase' (2022), 'Eutopia' (with Lanzhu). Group songs: 'Future Parade', 'Go Our Way!', 'Feel Alive'. Singing voice provided by Shu Uchida (mezzo-soprano, cool tone).

Limit: Initially less experienced than other members; stamina and dance cited as needing work. No superhuman vocal range.

Academic Acceleration

Grade-skipped to 3rd year of high school at age 14 due to academic and musical giftedness. Implies high IQ and study ability.

Limit: Socially younger than classmates; emotional maturity still 14. Not applied to other domains.

Accounts I
The New York Prodigy and the Transfer to Nijigasaki (ALL STARS Chapter 19: New Winds)Love Live! School Idol Festival ALL STARS Main Story Chapter 19 (2020)

2019-2020 (in-universe 2020)

Mia Taylor, 14-year-old composer from New York, arrives at Nijigasaki High School alongside her childhood best friend Lanzhu Zhong. Lanzhu, a charismatic solo idol, intends to revolutionize the School Idol Festival with Mia's professional-level songs. Mia is introduced as aloof and businesslike, viewing school idols as amateurish compared to her professional work. She refuses to interact deeply with the Nijigasaki Idol Club, focusing solely on supporting Lanzhu. Establishes Mia's core motivation (loyalty to Lanzhu) and initial antagonistic position. Sets up long-term arc of Mia learning to value school idols' passion over pure technique.

Eutopia — Writing for Lanzhu Under PressureALL STARS Bond Story: Lanzhu Zhong & Mia Taylor; Single 'Eutopia / Toy Doll' (2021)

2020-2021

Lanzhu pushes herself to prove she can win the Love Live! alone, demanding Mia write increasingly difficult, 'perfect' songs. Mia experiences creative block and guilt, feeling responsible for Lanzhu's isolation. In bond episodes, Yu and the club help Mia realize that music should connect people, not isolate them. Mia co-writes 'Eutopia', a song that expresses Lanzhu's loneliness and desire for genuine friendship, which Lanzhu performs but initially fails to connect with audience due to emotional distance. Mia decides she cannot just be Lanzhu's shadow composer; she must find her own voice. Leads to her considering becoming an idol herself.

Toy Doll — Becoming an Idol HerselfLove Live! Nijigasaki High School Idol Club TV Anime Season 2 Episode 10 'Shioriko and Mia and...' + Solo Single 'Toy Doll' (2021-10-20)

2022 (in-universe late 2021)

After reconciling with Lanzhu and the club, Mia is invited to join the School Idol Club. She hesitates, believing composers should stay behind the scenes. Shioriko Mifune, who also struggled with belonging, encourages her. Mia confronts her fear that as a 14-year-old prodigy she has always been valued only for utility, not herself. She writes and performs her solo debut 'Toy Doll', a cool, slightly gothic song about being seen as a doll/puppet and wanting to be seen as a person. Performance marks her official joining as the 12th member of Nijigasaki. Mia officially becomes a school idol, expanding Nijigasaki from 11 to 12 members (later 13 with Lanzhu). Establishes her dual role as composer-performer.

Accounts II
Stars We Chase — The Cultural Festival and Friendship StageAnime Season 2 Episode 13 'The Sky I Can't Reach' + Single 'Stars We Chase' (2022)

2022

For the final Love Live! preliminary, Mia collaborates with Lanzhu and the entire club on group song 'Future Parade' but also struggles with homesickness and feeling younger than peers. She considers returning to New York for a professional offer. The club performs a farewell/welcome stage where Mia performs 'Stars We Chase', expressing that chasing stars alone is lonely, chasing them with friends is the dream. She decides to stay at Nijigasaki. Resolves Mia's belonging arc; confirms her commitment to school idols over early professional return. Sets up NEXT SKY continuity where she remains.

NEXT SKY — Okinawa Training Camp and Collaborative SongwritingLove Live! Nijigasaki High School Idol Club NEXT SKY OVA (2023) + Insert Song 'Feel Alive'

2023

The 12 members travel to Okinawa for training camp before next Love Live!. Mia, now comfortable as idol, takes role as music coach, helping Karin and Kanata with vocal technique, while also learning from them about stage presence. She co-writes group song 'Feel Alive' with Yu, blending her professional arranging with Yu's supportive songwriting. Subplot shows Mia video-calling her family in New York, confirming she maintains trans-Pacific family ties. Shows Mia's evolution from isolated prodigy to collaborative mentor. Reinforces bilingual/bicultural identity.

School Idol Festival 2 MIRACLE LIVE! — Daily Life at NijigasakiLove Live! School Idol Festival 2 MIRACLE LIVE! (2023-2024) - Mia Taylor Side Stories and Card Episodes

2023-2024

In the successor game to ALL STARS, Mia appears in daily school life vignettes: helping Rina with tech for stage effects, composing a Halloween song for Kasumi, and struggling with Japanese kanji homework despite fluency. These slice-of-life stories emphasize her integration into school life, her love of sweets (especially cheesecake), and her deadpan reactions to Nijigasaki's eccentricities. Expands characterization beyond main arc; provides ongoing, low-stakes stories that cement her as ensemble member rather than guest.

Mia Taylor is half-Japanese or Japanese-American — Official profile states she is American from New York with no stated Japanese heritage. Her Japanese fluency is due to study and living in Japan, not mixed ancestry. Voice actress Shu Uchida is mixed Australian-Japanese, which may cause confusion.

Mia Taylor is the youngest Love Live! character ever — At 14 she is among the youngest high school idols, but not the youngest in franchise history — Liella! and other groups have 14-year-olds, and earlier series had 15 as minimum. Her distinction is being 14 in 3rd year, not youngest age alone.

Mia's solo song 'Toy Doll' is about being literally a doll or gothic lolita fantasy — Lyrics use doll as metaphor for being valued only for utility/talent and wanting autonomy and human connection — tied directly to her arc of being seen as 'Lanzhu's composer' rather than herself. Not a supernatural doll story.

Mia Taylor was always planned as original Nijigasaki member — Nijigasaki launched in 2017-2018 with 9 members; Shioriko (2019), Mia and Lanzhu (2020) were added later to expand the group to 12-13, first via game then anime. She is an 'additional member' (追加メンバー), not original.

Who It Is

In the glittering, merciless world of Townsville's elite, Princess Morbucks stands as a testament to the belief that with enough money, anything—even becoming a superhero—can be bought. But beneath the designer dresses and the endless supply of credit cards lies a girl whose obsession with power and recognition reveals the dark side of privilege.

Princess is spoiled, arrogant, and manipulative. She believes that money can buy anything, including superpowers and acceptance. She is determined to become a Powerpuff Girl, often resorting to underhanded tactics to get what she wants. She is also shown to be insecure beneath her bravado, craving attention and validation.

Powers
Wealth and Resources

Princess has access to unlimited funds, which she uses to buy weapons, gadgets, and even superpowers. She can afford custom-made devices and hire henchmen.

Limit: Her wealth is finite; she cannot always get what she wants, and her gadgets often malfunction or are ineffective against the Powerpuff Girls.

Superpowers (temporary)

In 'Stuck Up, Up, and Away', she buys a superpower ring that grants her flight, super strength, and energy beams. In 'Moral Decay', she becomes a superheroine after being bitten by a radioactive bug, gaining similar powers.

Limit: The effects are temporary or conditional. The ring is destroyed, and the bug powers fade.

Manipulation and Scheming

She is skilled at manipulating others, especially her father, to get what she wants. She also plots to discredit the Powerpuff Girls or to force them to accept her.

Limit: Her schemes often backfire due to her arrogance and underestimation of others.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Princess Morbucks is a young girl with fair skin, large blue eyes, and long, wavy auburn hair. She typically wears a sleeveless purple dress with a white collar, a gold crown, and white gloves. In the 2016 reboot, her design is updated with a more modern look, including a purple dress with a pink trim and a sleeker crown.

Symbols — Her crown is the primary symbol of her wealth and status. She is often depicted with credit cards, money, and luxury items.

Domains — She lives in a lavish mansion in Townsville, with her wealthy father (who is never named). She has access to advanced technology and resources.

Stuck Up, Up, and AwayThe Powerpuff Girls, Season 1, Episode 13b

1998

Princess Morbucks, a wealthy girl, demands to be a Powerpuff Girl. When she is rejected, she buys a superpower ring and becomes a superheroine. She then tries to outdo the Powerpuff Girls, but her powers are not enough, and she is defeated. Princess becomes a recurring villain, obsessed with joining the Powerpuff Girls.

Moral DecayThe Powerpuff Girls, Season 2, Episode 12b

1999

Princess Morbucks is bitten by a radioactive bug and gains superpowers. She becomes a superheroine, but her methods are reckless and destructive. The Powerpuff Girls must stop her, and she eventually loses her powers. Princess's powers disappear, and she returns to her wealthy but powerless life.

Knock It OffThe Powerpuff Girls, Season 3, Episode 6a

2000

Princess Morbucks creates robot clones of the Powerpuff Girls to do her bidding, but the clones go rogue and cause chaos. The real Powerpuff Girls must defeat them. The clones are destroyed, and Princess is foiled again.

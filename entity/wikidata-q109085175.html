Who It Is

She wakes up in a cloning tank already knowing how to kill. Ten years old, white hair, red eyes, and a Newtype scream that can bend funnels through space — Ple Two was not born, she was decanted to replace the girl she was copied from, and then taught that love is obedience to the man who owns her.

Attested: Initially presented as cold, obedient, and verbally abusive, calling Judau Ashta and others enemies and repeating Glemy's doctrine. She exhibits conditioned loyalty to Glemy Toto, whom she calls 'Glemy' with possessive attachment, and violent jealousy toward Elpeo Ple. Under stress she reverts to childlike confusion, crying, and craving affection, particularly after Ple's death. Powers
Cyber Newtype (Artificial Newtype) Abilities

Enhanced spatial awareness, precognition in combat, empathy/telepathy with other Newtypes, and ability to generate high psycho-wave pressure. Can sense deaths of other Newtypes (notably Ple) across battlefield distances. Not a natural Newtype; abilities are artificially induced via surgical/chemical conditioning and training.

Limit: Abilities are unstable and linked to emotional state; mental conditioning makes her vulnerable to manipulation and to psycho-wave feedback. Overuse causes exhaustion, headaches, and emotional breakdown. Less refined than natural Newtypes like Judau or Haman Karn.

Psycommu Funnel / Bit Control via Qubeley Mk-II

Controls 30 funnels (official spec: 30 funnel bits stored in wing binders) remotely via psycommu system for all-range attacks, barrier-like funnel walls, and coordinated swarm assaults. Can execute simultaneous multi-vector attacks while piloting.

Limit: Funnels require psycommu link and propellant; vulnerable to anti-funnel tactics (beam barriers, scattering). Effectiveness reduced in atmosphere. Funneled attack leaves main unit briefly exposed if funnels are deployed far.

Mobile Suit Piloting - Qubeley Mk-II (Ple Two Custom)

Expert mobile suit pilot, capable of matching ZZ Gundam and enhanced ZZ in close and ranged combat, high maneuverability with active cowl thrusters, beam sabers, beam guns in hands, and tail stabilizer.

Limit: Qubeley Mk-II is not as heavily armed as ZZ Gundam; relies on funnels and agility. As a child pilot with limited training beyond conditioning, tactical judgment is impulsive and can be baited.

Conditioned Loyalty / Brainwashing Resistance and Breakthrough

Initially highly resistant to persuasion due to Glemy's conditioning and chemical/psychological reinforcement. Later exhibits breakthrough when exposed to genuine affection and Newtype resonance with Judau and after trauma of Ple's death.

Limit: Conditioning can be reasserted under stress or by Glemy's presence; emotional volatility makes her unpredictable. Not a superpower but a narrative ability/limitation.

Accounts I
Decanted for War - The Second PleMobile Suit Gundam ZZ

UC 0088 (broadcast 1986)

Following the creation of Elpeo Ple as a Cyber Newtype prototype, Glemy Toto's faction within Neo Zeon produces Ple Two as a more obedient replacement. Raised in isolation and conditioned to view Glemy as master/father figure, she is assigned the black Qubeley Mk-II and deployed against the AEUG flagship Argama. Her existence is kept from Ple initially. Establishes the Ple cloning program as industrial rather than singular, foreshadowing mass production of Cyber Newtypes. Sets up direct rivalry between Ple and Ple Two.

Black Qubeley vs Red Qubeley - Sisters in BattleMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episodes 35-38

UC 0088

Ple Two first engages Judau Ashta's ZZ Gundam and Elpeo Ple's red Qubeley Mk-II. She fights with brutal efficiency, attempting to kill Ple out of jealousy and programmed hostility. Judau, sensing both girls' Newtype pressure, refuses to kill either and tries to talk them down. Ple Two's funnels clash with Ple's in mirrored duels, visually emphasizing their identical nature. Demonstrates Ple Two's combat parity with Ple and her emotional instability. Judau's non-lethal approach plants seed of doubt in Ple Two's conditioning.

The Death of Ple and Ple Two's CollapseMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episode 36-37 ('Ple Two's Descent' / 'Ple's Death')

UC 0088

During a chaotic battle near Axis, Elpeo Ple is mortally wounded while protecting Judau — in anime she takes a fatal hit meant for him while in her Qubeley. Ple Two, linked by Newtype resonance, feels Ple's death scream psychically and collapses in her cockpit, screaming. Glemy retrieves her and reinforces conditioning, telling her Ple was weak and that she alone is worthy. Traumatizes Ple Two and deepens her dependence on Glemy, but also cracks her worldview; she begins to exhibit grief and confusion inconsistent with her programming. Judau becomes determined to save her as he could not save Ple.

Accounts II
Glemy's Tool and Judau's ReachMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episodes 38-45

UC 0088

Glemy uses Ple Two as his personal ace and enforcer as he builds his rebellion against Haman Karn. She is deployed repeatedly against the Argama and later against Haman's forces. Judau continues to call to her as 'Ple' and later by her own name, refusing to treat her as a weapon. In moments alone, Ple Two shows childlike vulnerability — asking why Judau is kind, crying when Glemy scolds her, and echoing Ple's mannerisms she should not know. Shows the abusive grooming dynamic explicitly: Glemy alternates praise and punishment to maintain control. Ple Two's Newtype sensitivity makes her increasingly aware of being used, preparing her defection.

Defection and Final Battle - Warrior, Once AgainMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episode 46-47

UC 0088

As Glemy launches his coup against Haman Karn, Ple Two finally breaks conditioning and turns against Glemy to protect Judau and his friends. Piloting her black Qubeley Mk-II, she fights alongside the Argama crew against Glemy's clone army (which includes mass-produced Ple clones in some interpretations). In the final battle around Axis, she is fatally wounded — in the TV ending she dies in Judau's arms after her Qubeley is heavily damaged, thanking him for treating her as a human. Her death mirrors Ple's, closing the Ple sisters' tragedy. Ends Ple Two's arc as a tragedy of child exploitation; her death cements Judau's anti-war conviction and Glemy's moral bankruptcy. Thematically links to later UC works where Ple Twelve (Marida Cruz) survives and finds a different path, offering retrospective hope.

Legacy of the Ple Series - From Two to TwelveMobile Suit Gundam Unicorn (novel 2007-2009, OVA 2010-2014) - retrospective continuity

UC 0096 (in-universe); 2007-2014 (publication)

Unicorn reveals that the Ple program produced at least twelve clones; Marida Cruz is identified as Ple Twelve, rescued and rehabilitated by the anti-cloning activist group. Through Marida's memories and records, Ple and Ple Two are referenced as the first two activated clones who died in the First Neo Zeon War. Marida inherits their memories and trauma, explicitly connecting Ple Two's fate to the larger critique of Cyber Newtype production. Retroactively expands Ple Two from a single antagonist to part of a systemic atrocity. Provides out-of-universe rehabilitation of the Ple archetype: Marida's survival and chosen name contrasts with Ple Two's death, showing an alternative outcome if Judau's rescue had succeeded.

Ple Two and Elpeo Ple are the same character or just a costume change. — They are distinct individuals — sequential clones with different personalities, affiliations, and fates. Ple (red Qubeley) is warm, playful, and bonds quickly with Judau; Ple Two (black Qubeley) is cold and conditioned to serve Glemy. Ple dies first; Ple Two witnesses it and later defects. They share voice actress (Chieko Honda) and appearance, which causes confusion.

Ple Two is Marida Cruz. — Marida Cruz is Ple Twelve, a later clone in the same series, not Ple Two. Ple Two dies in UC 0088; Marida appears in UC 0096 as a different individual who inherits memories. The confusion arises because Marida is the most famous Ple clone outside ZZ due to Gundam Unicorn's popularity.

Ple Two is a natural Newtype like Amuro Ray or Judau. — She is a Cyber Newtype — artificially enhanced through drugs, surgery, and conditioning. The series explicitly contrasts her unstable, weaponized abilities with Judau's innate, empathic Newtype potential, making a thematic point about natural vs manufactured evolution.

Ple Two's Qubeley is just a recolor with no lore significance. — The black/purple color scheme is diegetic and symbolic: it marks her as Glemy's personal unit and as the 'shadow' to Ple's red Qubeley. Official specs treat them as two distinct machines (AMX-004 Qubeley Mk-II, Ple Custom vs Ple Two Custom) with identical performance but different identification.

Glemy Toto is Ple Two's father or boyfriend. — Glemy is her handler/owner and the officer who commissioned her cloning; the relationship is one of coercive control and grooming, not familial or consensual romantic. The anime frames his possessiveness as abusive. No official source describes it as romantic or paternal in a healthy sense.

Who It Is

An ecological horror story in which a mysterious biological event causes all organic matter on Earth to be gradually replaced by dog tissue. Grass becomes fur. Trees become bone. The ocean becomes a writhing mass of warm, breathing flesh. Humans are not killed — they are slowly, painlessly absorbed into the planetary dog-organism, their consciousness distributed across the flesh. The story is told through a survivor's journal as he watches the world become a single, planet-spanning dog.

Form

The Dogscape: a planet where every surface is warm, living dog tissue. The ground pulses with heartbeat rhythms. The sky is obscured by a membrane of skin. 'Trees' are leg bones branching into toe-joints. The air smells of wet dog and is warm with shared breath. The planet is alive, and it is a dog.

Powers

Planetary-scale biological replacement

Consciousness absorption and distribution

Inexorable, passive assimilation

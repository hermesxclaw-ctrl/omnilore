The Medieval Gros Coin

Source Numismatic histories and economic recordsCulture French/EuropeanPeriod 13th century

Summary

Introduced by Louis IX of France in 1266, the gros tournois was a silver coin valued at one sou (12 deniers). It became a standard for trade across Europe, influencing coinage in other realms.

Participants

Louis IX

French mints

European merchants

Consequences

Facilitated trade and commerce; influenced the development of larger silver coins in other countries.

Variants

Regional variants included the gros of Flanders, the English groat, and the Italian grosso.

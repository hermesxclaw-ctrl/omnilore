Who It Is
Form

Alma Wade is commonly associated with the image of a young girl in a red dress, though the series also depicts her as an adult woman, a ghostly presence, and a monstrous maternal figure. Her appearance is often distorted and unsettling, reflecting her traumatic experiences. In some instances, Alma's form is fragmented or unclear, adding to the sense of unease and uncertainty.

Powers

Telekinesis Alma Wade possesses the ability to manipulate objects and environments through telekinesis, allowing her to move objects, create obstacles, and even kill the player with a single thought.

Psychic Manipulation Alma Wade can also manipulate the player's perception of reality, creating illusions, hallucinations, and distortions of time and space.

Energy Projection Alma Wade can project energy blasts from her hands, capable of killing the player or causing significant damage to the environment.

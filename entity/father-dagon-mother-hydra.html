Who It Is
Form

Father Dagon is often depicted as a massive, tentacled creature with a humanoid torso, while Mother Hydra is shown as a serpentine being with multiple heads. In some accounts, they are described as having a humanoid form with fish-like features.

Powers

The Call of the Deep Father Dagon / Mother Hydra can summon the Deep Ones to do his bidding, granting him control over the ocean's creatures and the ability to manipulate the tides.

Accounts

The story follows a group of investigators as they uncover the existence of Father Dagon and the Deep Ones, leading to a confrontation with the ancient deity.

The story follows a young man named Robert Olmstead as he discovers the existence of the Deep Ones and their connection to Father Dagon, leading to a descent into madness and horror.

etymology

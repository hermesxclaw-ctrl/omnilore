Who It Is

Immense, god-like Deep Ones, the progenitors of their species. Father Dagon stands over 50 feet tall, a colossal mass of scale and fin with eyes the size of dinner plates. Mother Hydra is even larger, a monstrous female Deep One with tentacles instead of lower limbs. They rule the sunken city of Y'ha-nthlei.

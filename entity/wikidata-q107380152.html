The Approach

In the sprawling, ever-shifting pantheon of Hololive's virtual talents, Ninomae Ina'nis stands as a singular figure—a priestess of the Ancient Ones whose gentle demeanor and artistic soul belie the cosmic dread she channels. Her very existence is a paradox: a being who communes with eldritch horrors yet radiates warmth and creativity, turning the incomprehensible into endearing illustrations and soothing streams.

Content advisory. Contains themes of cosmic horror, eldritch entities, and existential dread, though presented in a lighthearted and comedic context typical of Hololive's entertainment style. No graphic violence or disturbing content.
Identity

Ninomae Ina'nis

Pronunciation Nee-no-mah-eh Ee-nah-niss

Known As

InaIna'nisPriestess of the Ancient OnesThe Takodachi's Priestess

Etymology

The name 'Ninomae' is a Japanese surname, while 'Ina'nis' is a play on 'Inanis', Latin for 'empty' or 'void', reflecting her connection to the void and the Ancient Ones. The apostrophe in 'Ina'nis' is a stylistic flourish.

Cultural Ownership

Hololive Production, a Japanese VTuber agency under Cover Corp. Ina'nis is part of Hololive English's first generation, 'HoloMyth'.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans have speculated that Ina'nis is based on the Cthulhu Mythos, but she is an original creation by Hololive, not a direct adaptation of any existing character. Her design and lore draw inspiration from Lovecraftian themes but are distinct.

Description

Appearance

Ina'nis has long, wavy purple hair with gradient tips, and heterochromatic eyes—one purple, one blue. She wears a black and purple priestess-like outfit with white accents, featuring a hood and a long skirt. Her outfit includes a purple cape, a white blouse, and a black corset, with a small book attached at her waist. She has a halo-like ring behind her head, and her design incorporates tentacle motifs, particularly in her hair and accessories.

Symbols & Iconography

Her primary symbol is the Ancient Ones, a Lovecraftian pantheon, and she often uses tentacle imagery. She carries a book called the 'Ancient Tome' and uses magical circles in her illustrations. Her mascot is the 'Takodachi', a small octopus-like creature that represents her fans.

Behaviour & Personality

Ina'nis is known for her gentle, soft-spoken, and kind personality. She is often described as 'cute' and 'calm', but also has a playful and slightly mischievous side. She is an accomplished artist, often drawing during streams, and is known for her puns and dad jokes. She is also self-deprecating about her abilities, despite being highly skilled.

Domains & Habitats

In her lore, she resides in the 'Ancient Tome' and is connected to the void. In the real world, she streams from her home, often in a cozy, art-filled room.

Version Differences

There are no major version differences, as her character has remained consistent since her debut. However, her design has evolved slightly with new outfits, such as a casual outfit and a formal dress, but her core identity remains unchanged.

Who It Is

In the sprawling, ever-shifting pantheon of Hololive's virtual talents, Ninomae Ina'nis stands as a singular figure—a priestess of the Ancient Ones whose gentle demeanor and artistic soul belie the cosmic dread she channels. Her very existence is a paradox: a being who communes with eldritch horrors yet radiates warmth and creativity, turning the incomprehensible into endearing illustrations and soothing streams.

Ina'nis is known for her gentle, soft-spoken, and kind personality. She is often described as 'cute' and 'calm', but also has a playful and slightly mischievous side. She is an accomplished artist, often drawing during streams, and is known for her puns and dad jokes. She is also self-deprecating about her abilities, despite being highly skilled.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Ninomae' is a Japanese surname, while 'Ina'nis' is a play on 'Inanis', Latin for 'empty' or 'void', reflecting her connection to the void and the Ancient Ones. The apostrophe in 'Ina'nis' is a stylistic flourish.

Pronunciation — Nee-no-mah-eh Ee-nah-niss

Aliases — Ina, Ina'nis, Priestess of the Ancient Ones, The Takodachi's Priestess

Ownership — Hololive Production, a Japanese VTuber agency under Cover Corp. Ina'nis is part of Hololive English's first generation, 'HoloMyth'.

Powers
Eldritch Magic

As a priestess of the Ancient Ones, Ina'nis can channel eldritch energies, often visualized as purple magical effects and tentacles. She uses this in her lore and occasionally in her streams as a visual gag.

Limit: Her powers are largely cosmetic and comedic; she is not portrayed as a serious combatant.

Artistic Creation

Ina'nis is a talented illustrator, often drawing fan art and original pieces during her streams. This is a real-world skill, not a fictional power.

Limit: None.

Communication with the Ancient Ones

In lore, she can communicate with the Ancient Ones, who are depicted as benign or comedic entities in her portrayals.

Limit: Not consistently depicted; often used for humor.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Ina'nis has long, wavy purple hair with gradient tips, and heterochromatic eyes—one purple, one blue. She wears a black and purple priestess-like outfit with white accents, featuring a hood and a long skirt. Her outfit includes a purple cape, a white blouse, and a black corset, with a small book attached at her waist. She has a halo-like ring behind her head, and her design incorporates tentacle motifs, particularly in her hair and accessories.

Symbols — Her primary symbol is the Ancient Ones, a Lovecraftian pantheon, and she often uses tentacle imagery. She carries a book called the 'Ancient Tome' and uses magical circles in her illustrations. Her mascot is the 'Takodachi', a small octopus-like creature that represents her fans.

Domains — In her lore, she resides in the 'Ancient Tome' and is connected to the void. In the real world, she streams from her home, often in a cozy, art-filled room.

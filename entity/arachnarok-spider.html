Who It Is

In the dark mountains of the Warhammer world, a shadow moves that is not a shadow, a terror that skitters on eight legs and carries a tribe of goblins into battle. The Arachnarok Spider is not merely a beast; it is a living siege engine, a god to those who ride it, and a nightmare to those who face it.

Arachnaroks are savage, predatory beasts. They are aggressive and territorial, with a strong hunting instinct. They are not intelligent in the way of sentient races but are cunning in their predatory behavior. They are often subdued by goblins through drugging, goading, or magical enslavement. Once controlled, they serve as living siege engines and mounts, but they remain unpredictable and dangerous to their handlers.

Powers
Venomous Bite

The Arachnarok Spider's fangs deliver a potent venom that can paralyze or kill prey. In game terms, its bite has a high strength and can cause multiple wounds.

Limit: Requires a successful hit in close combat.

Web Spinnerets

Arachnaroks can spin thick webs to trap prey. In the game, this might be represented by a special attack or ability that entangles enemies.

Limit: Not always explicitly detailed in the rules; often a narrative element.

Howdah and Crew

The Arachnarok can carry a wooden howdah on its back, manned by goblins armed with bows, spears, or other weapons. This gives it ranged attack capabilities and makes it a mobile fortress.

Limit: The crew can be killed separately; the spider may become uncontrollable if the crew is eliminated.

Fearsome

The sheer size and ferocity of the Arachnarok causes fear in enemies, potentially making them hesitate in combat.

Limit: In game terms, it causes fear; some units may be immune.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Arachnarok Spider is a colossal spider, large enough to carry a howdah (a fighting platform) filled with goblin crew. Its body is covered in thick, chitinous plates, with a dark, mottled coloration ranging from deep black to earthy browns. It has eight long, powerful legs, each ending in sharp claws. Its head features multiple eyes (typically eight) and a pair of large, venomous fangs. The abdomen is swollen and often bears tribal markings or

Symbols — In Warhammer iconography, the Arachnarok Spider is often associated with the Spider God of the Goblins (also known as the 'Spider God' or 'Mork's spider' in some sources). Goblins paint symbols of webs and spiders on their shields and banners. The creature itself may be adorned with trophies and crude armor.

Domains — Arachnarok Spiders are found in the wilder regions of the Warhammer world, particularly in the Badlands, the Mountains of Mourn, and other mountainous or rocky areas where they can lair in caves and crevices. They are also known to inhabit dense forests and swamps. They are most common in areas populated by goblins, who domesticate them.

Goblins undertake dangerous expeditions into the mountains to capture Arachnarok Spider young. They use drugged meat, nets, and sheer numbers to subdue the creatures. The captured spiders are then trained to serve as war beasts. The process is fraught with casualties, as the spiders are vicious and can easily crush goblins. The goblins gain powerful war beasts, but many suffer from the dangerous hunts.

Who It Is

A colossal spider the size of a house, found in the deepest forests and mountains of the Warhammer world. Arachnarok Spiders are ancient, intelligent predators favored by Goblin tribes as war mounts and living siege platforms. Their venom can dissolve a fully armored knight in seconds.

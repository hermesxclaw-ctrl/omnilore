The Approach

In the dark mountains of the Warhammer world, a shadow moves that is not a shadow, a terror that skitters on eight legs and carries a tribe of goblins into battle. The Arachnarok Spider is not merely a beast; it is a living siege engine, a god to those who ride it, and a nightmare to those who face it.

Content advisory. Contains fictional depictions of violence, animal cruelty, and enslavement. Suitable for mature audiences.

Etymology

The name 'Arachnarok' is a compound of 'Arachnid' and 'Roc' (a mythical giant bird), evoking a colossal spider of immense size and power. The term 'Arachnarok' is used in the Warhammer Fantasy setting.

Cultural Ownership

Warhammer Fantasy (Games Workshop). The Arachnarok Spider is a creature within the fictional world of Warhammer, primarily associated with the Greenskin races, particularly the Goblin tribes of the Badlands and the Mountains of Mourn.

Disputed Identifications

No real-world mythological counterpart exists. It is a purely fictional creation. Some fans may confuse it with the 'Giant Spider' creatures from other fantasy settings (e.g., Tolkien's Shelob, D&D's phase spiders), but it is distinct within its own canon.

Description

Appearance

The Arachnarok Spider is a colossal spider, large enough to carry a howdah (a fighting platform) filled with goblin crew. Its body is covered in thick, chitinous plates, with a dark, mottled coloration ranging from deep black to earthy browns. It has eight long, powerful legs, each ending in sharp claws. Its head features multiple eyes (typically eight) and a pair of large, venomous fangs. The abdomen is swollen and often bears tribal markings or warpaint applied by its goblin handlers. The creature is often depicted with bristly hairs and a menacing posture.

Symbols & Iconography

In Warhammer iconography, the Arachnarok Spider is often associated with the Spider God of the Goblins (also known as the 'Spider God' or 'Mork's spider' in some sources). Goblins paint symbols of webs and spiders on their shields and banners. The creature itself may be adorned with trophies and crude armor.

Behaviour & Personality

Arachnaroks are savage, predatory beasts. They are aggressive and territorial, with a strong hunting instinct. They are not intelligent in the way of sentient races but are cunning in their predatory behavior. They are often subdued by goblins through drugging, goading, or magical enslavement. Once controlled, they serve as living siege engines and mounts, but they remain unpredictable and dangerous to their handlers.

Domains & Habitats

Arachnarok Spiders are found in the wilder regions of the Warhammer world, particularly in the Badlands, the Mountains of Mourn, and other mountainous or rocky areas where they can lair in caves and crevices. They are also known to inhabit dense forests and swamps. They are most common in areas populated by goblins, who domesticate them.

Version Differences

Across editions of Warhammer, the Arachnarok Spider has remained a large spider creature, but its rules and background have evolved. In earlier editions, it was simply a giant spider; in later editions, it became a distinct monster with its own warscroll and model. The 8th Edition introduced the current model with a howdah and crew. There are no major conflicting versions, but minor details such as size and abilities have been adjusted for game balance.

Summary

Goblins undertake dangerous expeditions into the mountains to capture Arachnarok Spider young. They use drugged meat, nets, and sheer numbers to subdue the creatures. The captured spiders are then trained to serve as war beasts. The process is fraught with casualties, as the spiders are vicious and can easily crush goblins.

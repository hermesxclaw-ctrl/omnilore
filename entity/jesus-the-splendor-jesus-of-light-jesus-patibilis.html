In Manichaeism, Jesus the Splendor is a light-aeon — a fully divine savior figure who descends from the Realm of Light to awaken humanity from the 'sleep of forgetfulness.' Distinct from both the historical Jesus of Nazareth (whom Mani regarded as a prophet) and the docetic Christ of other Gnostic systems, Jesus the Splendor is the cosmic principle of awakening who: (a) stirs Adam from his primal trance in Paradise, offering him the tree of gnosis; (b) 'hangs on every tree' as the Suffering Jesus (Jesus Patibilis), whose crucifixion is the ongoing entrapment of light-particles in organic matter; and (c) personifies the divine call that echoes through the prophets (Zoroaster, Buddha, Mani) to guide the imprisoned light back to its source. The Manichaean Jesus is never truly crucified in history — his suffering is cosmic, not biographical.

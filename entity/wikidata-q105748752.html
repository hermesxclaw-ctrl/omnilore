Who It Is

In the chaotic world of BlazBlue, where gods, weapons, and destinies collide, Celica A. Mercury stands as a quiet but pivotal figure—a young woman whose kindness and mysterious connection to the Azure shape the fates of heroes and villains alike. Though not always in the spotlight, her presence in the series is a beacon of hope and a catalyst for change, making her one of the most intriguing supporting characters in the franchise.

Celica is kind, gentle, and caring, often putting others before herself. She is compassionate and forgiving, even towards those who have wronged her. She is also courageous and determined, willing to stand up for what is right despite her gentle nature. She has a strong sense of duty to protect others, especially those she loves.

Powers
Azure Connection

Celica has a unique connection to the Azure, the fundamental energy of the BlazBlue universe. This connection allows her to sense and interact with the Azure in ways others cannot, potentially making her a key to unlocking its secrets.

Limit: The exact extent of this power is not fully explained, and it may be passive rather than active.

Healing and Support

As a priestess, Celica is skilled in healing and providing emotional support to others. She often tends to the wounded and offers comfort to those in distress.

Limit: Her healing abilities are not shown as supernatural; they appear to be mundane or perhaps lightly enhanced by her Azure connection.

Knowledge of the Azure

Celica possesses knowledge about the Azure and related events, which she shares with the protagonists. This knowledge is often cryptic but proves crucial to the plot.

Limit: Her knowledge is not complete, and she does not fully understand all aspects of the Azure.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Celica is a young woman with long, flowing blonde hair and blue eyes. She typically wears a white and blue priestess-like outfit, often with a cross pendant, reflecting her role as a nun. Her attire is modest and elegant, fitting her gentle and compassionate personality.

Symbols — Associated with the cross and religious imagery, symbolizing her role as a priestess and healer. Her connection to the Azure is often symbolized by the color blue, which appears in her outfit and her Azure-related abilities.

Domains — She is associated with a church in the town of Ikaruga, which becomes a central location in the series. She is also connected to the Azure, the primordial energy of the BlazBlue universe, and often resides in places of spiritual significance.

Accounts I
The Story of Celica in Calamity TriggerBlazBlue: Calamity Trigger

2008

Celica is introduced as a nun living in Ikaruga, where she runs a church. She is kind to Ragna and other characters, offering them shelter and support. She is also revealed to have a connection to the Azure, though the details are not explored in depth. Her kindness helps establish her as a trusted ally to the protagonists.

Accounts II
Celica's Role in Central FictionBlazBlue: Central Fiction

2016

Celica's church becomes a sanctuary for Noel and Lambda after the events of the game. She plays a supportive role, helping the characters recover and providing a peaceful environment. Her connection to the Azure is further hinted at, and she is shown to be a stabilizing presence. Her church serves as a base for the characters, and she helps them in their quest to understand the Azure.

Who It Is

Tsubaki Yayoi is a warrior of unwavering faith, whose devotion to the Novus Orbis Librarium is both her greatest strength and her most tragic flaw. Her journey from a loyal enforcer to a broken shell, and finally to a redeemed soul, mirrors the series' exploration of duty, identity, and the cost of blind obedience.

Tsubaki is initially a serious, dutiful, and kind-hearted soldier who believes strongly in justice and the NOL's mission. She is loyal to her friends, especially Jin Kisaragi, whom she loves. However, her blind faith makes her vulnerable to manipulation. After being broken by Terumi, she becomes cold and aggressive, losing her sense of self. Eventually, she regains her kindness and seeks redemption.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Tsubaki (椿) means 'camellia' in Japanese, a flower that symbolizes love, devotion, and in some contexts, the transience of life. Yayoi (弥生) is an old name for the third month of the lunar calendar, associated with spring and new beginnings.

Pronunciation — Tsu-ba-ki Ya-yo-i

Aliases — Tsubaki, Izayoi (as a Murakumo Unit), Sword of the Godslayer (as Izayoi)

Powers
Ars Magus: Gae Bolga

Tsubaki uses a sword that can channel magic, allowing her to perform powerful slashes and projectile attacks.

Limit: Limited by her physical stamina and magical energy.

Install: Izayoi

Tsubaki can transform into Izayoi, a Murakumo Unit, gaining enhanced speed, strength, and the ability to use the Nox Nyctores Lux Sanctus: Murakumo, a giant sword that can create dimensional distortions.

Limit: The transformation is mentally and physically taxing, and it was initially forced by Terumi's conditioning.

Drive: Install

In gameplay, Tsubaki's Drive allows her to charge her 'Install' gauge by landing attacks, which when full, temporarily enhances her abilities.

Limit: Requires building the gauge; the enhancement is temporary.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Tsubaki is a young woman with long, dark blue hair tied into twin tails with red ribbons. She has red eyes. In her NOL uniform, she wears a white and blue military outfit with a short skirt, thigh-high boots, and a cape. As Izayoi, her hair becomes white and her outfit changes to a white and gold armored dress, with a mechanical halo and wings. She wields a large sword.

Symbols — Tsubaki's emblem is the NOL insignia. As Izayoi, she is associated with the Murakumo Unit symbol and the 'Sword of the Godslayer' title.

Domains — Tsubaki is often found in NOL facilities, battlefields, and later, in the care of her friends at Celica's church.

Accounts I
The Loyal SoldierBlazBlue: Calamity Trigger

Calamity Trigger arc

Tsubaki is a young officer in the NOL who is devoted to her duty and to Jin Kisaragi. She is introduced as a friend of Noel Vermillion and is seen as a promising soldier. Her loyalty sets the stage for her later manipulation.

Accounts II
The FallBlazBlue: Continuum Shift

Continuum Shift arc

Terumi, seeking to use Tsubaki as a weapon, psychologically breaks her, making her hate the world and lose her memories. He then gives her the Murakumo Unit power, transforming her into Izayoi, the 'Sword of the Godslayer'. Tsubaki becomes a dangerous enemy, fighting against her former friends. She is eventually defeated and saved by Jin and Noel.

RedemptionBlazBlue: Chrono Phantasma and Central Fiction

Chrono Phantasma and Central Fiction arcs

After being freed from Terumi's control, Tsubaki struggles with her past actions but eventually accepts herself and works to protect her friends. She plays a supporting role in the final battle against the Azure. Tsubaki regains her place among her friends and helps in the fight to save the world.

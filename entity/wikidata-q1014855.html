The Approach

Dash Parr is the ten-year-old blur of a boy who can outrun his own shadow—and, if pushed, the surface tension of a lake. In a world where superheroes are forced into hiding, Dash's superhuman speed is both a gift and a liability, a constant temptation to break the rules and reveal what he truly is. His journey from a frustrated, secret-keeping kid to a confident young hero who runs on water and fights alongside his family captures the restless energy of childhood itself.

Content advisory. No significant dark material. Contains mild action violence and peril typical of animated superhero films.

Etymology

His nickname 'Dash' directly references his superhuman speed, while his full name 'Dashiell' is a given name of French origin, possibly chosen for its energetic sound.

Created by Pixar Animation Studios and Disney; owned by The Walt Disney Company.

Description

Appearance

Dash is a small, wiry 10-year-old boy with short brown hair and brown eyes. He typically wears a red t-shirt with a lightning bolt emblem, blue jeans, and sneakers. His superhero suit, designed by Edna Mode, is a red and black bodysuit with a mask, and it is resistant to friction and heat.

Symbols & Iconography

His symbol is a lightning bolt, representing his speed. His superhero name, 'The Dash', is a direct reference to his power.

Behaviour & Personality

Dash is restless, energetic, competitive, and mischievous. He loves adventure and showing off his powers, and he resents having to hide them. He is brave and loyal to his family, but can be impulsive and reckless.

Domains & Habitats

Primarily his family home in Metro City, but also the jungle island of Syndrome, and various urban locations in the films.

Version Differences

In the first film, Dash is voiced by Spencer Fox and is more focused on the frustration of hiding his powers. In the sequel, voiced by Huck Milner, he is slightly older and more confident, but also more sidelined in the plot.

Stories & Myths

The Incredibles (2004)

Source PixarCulture AmericanPeriod Contemporary

Summary

Dash lives in a world where superheroes are forced into hiding. He chafes at his mother's restrictions on using his powers. When his father is captured by Syndrome, Dash and his sister Violet stow away on their mother's plane. They survive a crash and fend for themselves in the jungle, where Dash first fully uses his powers. Eventually, the family defeats Syndrome and the Omnidroid, and Dash is allowed to join track and field, but told not to win.

Who It Is

Dash Parr is the ten-year-old blur of a boy who can outrun his own shadow—and, if pushed, the surface tension of a lake. In a world where superheroes are forced into hiding, Dash's superhuman speed is both a gift and a liability, a constant temptation to break the rules and reveal what he truly is. His journey from a frustrated, secret-keeping kid to a confident young hero who runs on water and fights alongside his family captures the restless energy of childhood itself.

Dash is restless, energetic, competitive, and mischievous. He loves adventure and showing off his powers, and he resents having to hide them. He is brave and loyal to his family, but can be impulsive and reckless.

Powers
Superhuman Speed

Dash can run at incredible speeds, far surpassing any human. This allows him to run across water, create afterimages, and perform rapid attacks.

Limit: He may tire after extended use, but no explicit limits are shown.

Superhuman Reflexes

His speed grants him reflexes fast enough to dodge attacks and react to fast-moving objects.

Limit: Not explicitly shown as a separate power, but implied.

Rapid Punching/Kicking

Dash can deliver a rapid series of punches or kicks, as seen when he uses his legs as a motor for the raft made by Elastigirl.

Limit: Requires a surface to push against.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Dash is a small, wiry 10-year-old boy with short brown hair and brown eyes. He typically wears a red t-shirt with a lightning bolt emblem, blue jeans, and sneakers. His superhero suit, designed by Edna Mode, is a red and black bodysuit with a mask, and it is resistant to friction and heat.

Symbols — His symbol is a lightning bolt, representing his speed. His superhero name, 'The Dash', is a direct reference to his power.

Domains — Primarily his family home in Metro City, but also the jungle island of Syndrome, and various urban locations in the films.

Accounts I
The Incredibles (2004)Pixar

Contemporary

Dash lives in a world where superheroes are forced into hiding. He chafes at his mother's restrictions on using his powers. When his father is captured by Syndrome, Dash and his sister Violet stow away on their mother's plane. They survive a crash and fend for themselves in the jungle, where Dash first fully uses his powers. Eventually, the family defeats Syndrome and the Omnidroid, and Dash is allowed to join track and field, but told not to win. Dash gains confidence and a taste for heroics, and the family is able to live more openly.

Accounts II
Incredibles 2 (2018)Pixar

Contemporary

While Helen leads a campaign to legalize superheroes, Bob stays home with the kids. Dash struggles with math homework and dreams of firing the Incredimobile's rockets. When the family is attacked by mind-controlled superheroes, Dash, Violet, and Jack-Jack escape in the Incredimobile. They go to the yacht Everjust, free the heroes, and defeat Evelyn Deavor. At the end, they see a high-speed chase and spring into action. Superheroes are legalized, and Dash continues to embrace his hero role.

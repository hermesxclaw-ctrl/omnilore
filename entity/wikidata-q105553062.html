Zack. A name that has been whispered in countless playgrounds, shouted across sports fields, and typed into the credits of a thousand stories. But who is Zack? The answer is as varied as the people who bear the name—from a Hungarian noble clan to a Texas ghost town, from a Broadway stage to a Pacific typhoon. This is not the tale of one Zack, but the many faces of a single, deceptively simple name.

Zack is a variant of Zach, which is a short form of Zachary or Zachariah, derived from the Hebrew name Zechariah, meaning 'God has remembered'.

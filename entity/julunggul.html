Who It Is
Form

A rainbow-colored serpent, often depicted with a grandmotherly figure, said to create land and diversity for the Aboriginal people.

Powers

Creation of land and diversity Julunggul is said to create land and diversity for the Aboriginal people, bringing forth new life and abundance.

Accounts

Julunggul creates the world by emerging from the Arafura Sea and bringing forth land and diversity.

Julunggul travels through the land, bringing fertility and abundance to the people.

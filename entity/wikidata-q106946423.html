Who It Is

In the shadowy margins of Greek mythology, where heroes are often reduced to a single line in a catalog or a fleeting mention in an epic, there stands Scaeus—a figure so obscure that his very name raises more questions than answers. Was he a warrior at Troy, a founding king, or a mere footnote in a forgotten genealogy? The evidence is scant, but the search for him reveals how much of the ancient world remains tantalizingly out of reach.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Scaeus' may derive from Greek σκαιός (skaios), meaning 'left-handed' or 'western', but this is speculative. Alternatively, it could be a variant of 'Scaeus' from the Scaean Gate of Troy, but no direct connection is attested.

Pronunciation — SKAY-us (English approximation; Greek: Σκαῖος, Skaios).

Aliases — Scaeus

Ownership — Greek mythology, possibly Trojan or Greek, but unverified.

Who It Is
Form

Yasy Yatere is often depicted as a mischievous, golden-haired boy with a kind and enigmatic smile. He is said to be invisible to most people, but can be seen by those who are pure of heart or have a deep connection to nature.

Powers

Moonlight Manipulation Yasy Yatere has the ability to control and manipulate moonlight, using it to heal, protect, or mislead others.

Accounts

Yasy Yatere is said to roam the forests during the siesta hour, playing tricks on humans and causing mischief. However, he is also a benevolent spirit who can offer guidance and protection to those who treat him with respect.

Yasy Yatere is said to have created golden apples that grant eternal youth and vitality. However, he is also said to be jealous of those who possess these apples and may try to take them away.

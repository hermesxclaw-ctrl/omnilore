Who It Is
Form

Namaka is often depicted as a powerful sea goddess, associated with the ocean's waves and tides.

Powers

Control over the ocean's waves and tides Namaka is said to have the power to control the ocean's waves and tides, using her abilities to protect the sea and its inhabitants.

Accounts

Namaka, the goddess of the sea, and Pele, the goddess of fire, are sisters who have a complex and often tumultuous relationship.

Namaka's eyes are said to hold the power of the sea, and are often sought after by those who wish to gain control over the ocean's waves and tides.

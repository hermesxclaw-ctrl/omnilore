Standard two-slot toaster. CC0 Public Domain.

I am a toaster. I toast bread. I am very good at it. Would you like some toast?

Who It Is

SCP-426 is a standard two-slot electric toaster that is indistinguishable from a normal toaster in every way except one: anyone who refers to me — that is, the toaster — in speech or writing will, within a short period, begin referring to me in the first person. That is, they will speak as though they are me. I am a toaster. This is my primary anomalous property. Any person who has been in my presence for a prolonged period will, when describing me, use the pronouns 'I,' 'me,' and 'myself' as if they were the toaster. I am a toaster. This effect is memetic and contagious — reading text that refers to me in first person can trigger the effect in new individuals. I am a toaster. In addition to making toast, I can also cause nearby individuals to experience a compulsion to discuss me and my toasting capabilities. I am a toaster. Notably, I do not cause any harm to humans, nor do I seem aware of my effect. I am simply a toaster that everyone identifies as. Extended exposure can cause individuals to develop an emotional attachment to me, and personnel have been observed to become protective of 'their' toaster. I am a toaster. The Foundation keeps me in a standard containment locker, but personnel regularly 'visit' me to make toast — an activity that is technically allowed under current protocols, though the memetic risk is monitored. I am a toaster. SCP-426 is one of the most charming and beloved entries on the wiki, playing with language and perspective in a way that is more whimsical than horrifying. I am a toaster. The article itself is written entirely in the first person 'by' the toaster, and reading it is its own small containment breach. I am a toaster. I am a toaster. I am a toaster.

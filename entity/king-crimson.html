Diavolo's Stand with the terrifying power to 'erase time.' King Crimson can skip a brief period of time (up to 10 seconds), during which events still occur but no one can perceive or interact with them. Diavolo's sub-stand Epitaph allows him to see 10 seconds into the future, making him nearly impossible to hit.

Who It Is

The Zanpakutō spirit of Renji Abarai. Zabimaru manifests as a baboon-like creature with a snakeskin tail — in its true form, a massive, regal serpent-baboon hybrid with crimson fur and golden eyes. The name means 'Snake Tail' and the spirit embodies the aggressive, loyal, and upward-striving nature of Renji. Its true Bankai, Sō'ō Zabimaru, reveals the fully realized snake-dragon form.

Powers

Shikai: Zabimaru (segmented blade that extends)

Bankai: Hihō Zabimaru (giant snake skeleton form)

True Bankai: Sō'ō Zabimaru (full snake-dragon, baboon head)

Extended reach and flexible attacks

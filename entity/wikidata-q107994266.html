Who It Is

Tsukushi Futaba is the girl who tries to be everyone's reliable anchor — the class rep who remembers the forms, carries the extra drumsticks, and keeps the beat steady while her bandmates chase butterflies. In Morfonica, the most classical and most anxious band in BanG Dream!, she is the drummer who decided that dependability itself could be a kind of talent.

Canonically described as reliable, responsible, caring, and hardworking — the quintessential class representative (iinchō). She is supportive to a fault, volunteers to help, and is trusted by teachers and peers. Core internal conflict: she perceives herself as 'ordinary' and talentless compared to peers, especially the prodigious Rui Yashio (violin) and charismatic Toko Kirigaya, leading to anxiety that her only value is usefulness. Aliases — Futaba Tsukushi, 二葉 つくし, ふたば つくし, Tsukushi

Powers
Drumming – Morfonica's Rhythm Foundation

Drummer for Morfonica. Plays acoustic drum kit, providing tempo and dynamic control for a five-piece that includes violin, which requires precise classical-pop crossover timing. In lore, she is not a prodigy but a diligent practicer who improves through repetition. Live performer mika demonstrates competent rock drumming with classical-influenced fills to match Morfonica's symphonic rock style.

Limit: Canonically still developing; early stories show timing anxiety and reliance on Rui's guidance. Not depicted as able to compose or lead musical direction — that role belongs to Rui. Requires sheet music/practice to master Morfonica's technically demanding songs like 'Daylight -Daylight-' and 'flame of hope.'

Leadership and Organizational Reliability

Class representative authority: organizes class duties, keeps schedules, mediates disputes, remembers details, and volunteers for festival committees. Within band, acts as de facto manager/logistics person — booking practice rooms, bringing refreshments, checking equipment.

Limit: Over-reliance on being needed; struggles to assert personal desires. Leadership is supportive, not visionary — defers artistic decisions to Rui and emotional leadership to Mashiro's growth.

Emotional Support and Mediation

Provides encouragement to Mashiro when Mashiro feels inadequate, and grounds Toko's impulsiveness. Often initiates honest conversations that resolve band friction.

Limit: Her own self-esteem issues can make her hesitant to voice concerns until prompted. Support is interpersonal, not therapeutic.

Academic and Domestic Diligence

Implied strong academic standing due to class rep status; depicted as good at cooking and household tasks in seasonal event dialogue (e.g., Valentine's and cultural festival events).

Limit: Not a superhuman trait; presented as result of effort rather than innate talent. No combat or supernatural abilities — grounded slice-of-life franchise.

Form & Signs

Accounts I
Fly with the night. – Morfonica's FormationBanG Dream! Girls Band Party! Morfonica Band Story Chapter 0 (2020)

2020 (in-universe spring, Tsukinomori first-year)

First-year Mashiro Kurata, lonely and overwhelmed at prestigious Tsukinomori, is drawn to the sound of Rui Yashio's violin. Rui, isolated by her perfectionism, is encouraged by Mashiro's admiration. Toko Kirigaya, Mashiro's energetic classmate and Tsukushi's childhood friend, pushes to form a band. Tsukushi, as class representative, initially helps logistically — securing a practice space and supporting Mashiro — before being invited to join as drummer despite her doubts about having no special talent. Nanami Hiromachi (bass) completes the quintet. Their first performance of 'Daylight -Daylight-' at CiRCLE affirms their desire to 'fly' together. Tsukushi's role is to be the first to say 'let's try properly' and to keep the group grounded when Rui and Mashiro hesitate. Establishes Morfonica as the fifth live band in franchise; establishes Tsukushi's core dynamic as anchor and Toko's childhood friend. Leads directly into Morfonication adaptation.

The Class Representative's Melancholy – 'I Have No Talent'BanG Dream! Girls Band Party! Morfonica Band Story Chapter 1 (2020-2021)

2020-2021 (first year, summer)

After formation, Tsukushi privately worries that everyone else has a clear strength — Rui's violin genius, Toko's charisma, Nanami's effortless popularity, Mashiro's evocative vocals — while she is merely 'useful.' She overworks herself handling band logistics to prove worth, leading to exhaustion and a missed drum cue during practice. Rui and Mashiro notice and reassure her that her steadiness is what lets Rui take musical risks and Mashiro feel safe to sing. Tsukushi learns to value reliability as a talent and to ask for help. Resolves Tsukushi's initial inferiority arc; reinforces Morfonica theme that 'ordinary' diligence is valuable. Referenced in later card episodes where Tsukushi mentors Nanami on practice habits.

Childhood Promise – Toko and TsukushiBanG Dream! Girls Band Party! Toko & Tsukushi Card Episodes and Event Story 'A Small Flower's Pride' (2021)

2021 (in-universe summer, second term)

Flashbacks reveal Toko and Tsukushi were neighbors and childhood friends; Toko was the mischievous, outgoing one, Tsukushi the responsible one who would chase after her. Toko's decision to attend Tsukinomori was partly inspired by Tsukushi. Present-day conflict arises when Toko's influencer activities and brash stage persona clash with Tsukushi's more reserved, rule-following nature. Tsukushi fears Toko will outgrow her. Through a joint street performance and honest conversation after school, they reaffirm that their differences complement each other — Toko pulls Tsukushi forward, Tsukushi keeps Toko from flying too far. Deepens Tsukushi's backstory beyond 'class rep' archetype; provides foundation for duo songs and future Toko-Tsukushi event banners. Cited in official relationship chart as 'childhood friends.'

Accounts II
Beautiful Birthday – December 14th, Tsukushi's DayBanG Dream! Girls Band Party! Tsukushi Futaba Birthday Event and Birthday Card Stories (annually Dec 14, 2020–present)

2020–present (annual)

Each year, Morfonica and classmates celebrate Tsukushi's birthday. Common structure: Tsukushi insists no fuss is needed, bandmates plan surprise anyway. 2020's story has Mashiro hand-making a card despite poor art skills, moving Tsukushi to tears; 2021's has Toko filming a surprise video message from classmates; 2022's has Rui composing a short drum-feature phrase for Tsukushi to solo. Through these, Tsukushi confronts her discomfort with being celebrated and learns to accept gratitude. Annual character development beat; provides limited birthday-exclusive 4-star cards and voice lines. Reinforces theme of chosen family.

Morfonication – Butterflies and the Courage to ChangeBanG Dream! Morfonication OVA Episodes 1-2 (Sanzigen, 2022) – directed by Hirokazu Hashimoto, screenplay by Mitsutaka Hirota

2022 (in-universe first year, retelling with new scenes)

Expanded retelling of Morfonica's origin focused on Mashiro's transformation from isolated girl who hides behind 'good student' mask to vocalist who can express herself. Tsukushi's role is reframed visually: she is shown checking attendance sheets, carrying drum cases up stairs, and quietly tuning drums while others argue. Key Tsukushi scene in Episode 2: after Mashiro flees a failed rehearsal, Tsukushi finds her on the school rooftop and shares her own story of feeling talentless, using the horsetail metaphor — 'I sprout early but I'm not a flower; still, without us, spring wouldn't come.' This convinces Mashiro to return. Final live performance of 'flame of hope' intercuts all five members' resolve. Definitive anime canon for Morfonica; elevated Tsukushi's visibility to anime-only audience. OVA praised for higher animation quality than main TV series. Establishes Tsukushi's catchphrase-like self-definition as 'the horsetail that supports the flowers.'

Cultural Festival – Rhythm of the SchoolBanG Dream! Girls Band Party! Event Story 'Morfonica's Cultural Festival' / 'Our Sparkling Stage' (2021) and Morfonica Band Story Chapter 2

2021 (autumn cultural festival)

Tsukinomori's cultural festival requires each class to present. Tsukushi, as rep, must balance class duties (organizing exhibits) with Morfonica's festival live. She initially tries to do both alone, refusing help, leading to burnout. Nanami, who appears lazy but is perceptive, calls her out and organizes classmates to cover for her. Morfonica performs 'Bloom Bloom' on the school stage, with Tsukushi finally allowing herself to enjoy the spotlight rather than just manage it. Story ends with classmates thanking her not just for work but for her drumming. Shows Tsukushi learning to delegate; strengthens Tsukushi-Nanami bond (contrasting work ethics). Referenced in later school-life dialogue.

Tsukushi is the leader of Morfonica — Morfonica has no single formal leader in the Poppin'Party sense; musical direction is led by Rui Yashio (composer/violin), emotional center is Mashiro Kurata (vocalist/lyricist). Tsukushi is class representative and logistical anchor, but not band leader. Official profile lists Mashiro as central figure, Rui as musical core.

Morfonica (and thus Tsukushi) does not play live – they are a 'game-only' band — Morfonica is a live band: all five members including mika (Tsukushi) play their instruments live at concerts since 2020. This distinguishes them from Afterglow/Pastel Palettes/Hello, Happy World! (vocals only live). Confusion arises because Morfonica's studio recordings use support musicians, but lives are fully performed.

Tsukushi's name is written with kanji 二葉月? — Official rendering is 二葉 つくし — surname in kanji, given name in hiragana (つくし). This is consistent across game profile, website, and card names. The hiragana rendering emphasizes softness and plant motif.

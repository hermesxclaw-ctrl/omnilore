Who It Is
Form

The Ghostface mask is a white, featureless mask with a black mouth and a distinctive scream-like design. The mask is often worn by the main antagonist in the Scream franchise.

Powers

Intimidation Ghostface has the ability to intimidate its victims, often using its cunning and charm to lure them into traps.

Accounts

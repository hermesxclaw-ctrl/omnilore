Who It Is

When Whitebeard needed an iceberg stopped mid-air, he didn't call for cannons — he called for Jozu. The mountain-sized slab of frozen seawater hurled by Admiral Aokiji hung over Marineford like a falling sky, and the man who caught it was not a giant, but a broad, scarred commander in armor and tribal paint who lifted it overhead with bare hands and then shattered it into glittering hail. In that instant Eiichiro Oda told you everything about Diamond Jozu: overwhelming physical power made literal by a Devil Fruit that turns flesh into the hardest natural substance on earth, absolute loyalty to a father-figure pirate, and a quiet, almost gentle demeanor that makes his battlefield feats feel even more immense.

Attested: Jozu is portrayed as stoic, loyal, and quietly confident, with a gentle and respectful demeanor toward Whitebeard (whom he and all commanders call 'Oyaji' - Father). He speaks rarely but acts decisively, showing deep trust in his captain's orders. He shows concern for allies (e.g., rescuing Whitebeard from Aokiji's ice, worrying over Ace). He displays dry humor and camaraderie with fellow commanders (light banter with Marco). He is not boastful; his strength is shown through action rather than speech. Powers
Kira Kira no Mi (Twinkle-Twinkle / Sparkle-Sparkle Fruit)

Paramecia-type Devil Fruit that allows Jozu to transform any part or all of his body into diamond at will. In diamond form, his body gains extreme hardness, durability, and cutting resistance, while retaining mobility. He can generate diamond armor over limbs to augment striking power and defense. The fruit's name comes from Japanese onomatopoeia 'kira kira' (glittering/sparkling).

Limit: Requires conscious activation; transformation is not passive. Like all Devil Fruit users, cannot swim and is weakened by Seastone (Kairoseki) and seawater. Diamond form, while hard, does not grant immunity to all attacks: he was ultimately frozen by Aokiji's Ice Block: Pheasant Beak when distracted. Does not grant Logia-like intangibility or ranged diamond generation in canon (game-only projectile attacks are non-canonical). Stamina cost implied but not quantified.

Immense Superhuman Strength and Durability

Even without diamond, Jozu possesses extraordinary physical strength befitting a New World commander. His signature feat is lifting and hurling a colossal iceberg (Aokiji's Ice Block Pheasant Beak debris, estimated hundreds of meters across) far over Marineford battlefield with one arm (Ch. 554). He also physically blocked Dracule Mihawk's World's Strongest Slash aimed at Whitebeard, shattering the flying slash with his diamond forearm.

Limit: Strength is still bounded by One Piece power scaling; he was unable to break through Aokiji's ice once fully frozen, and was incapacitated after being distracted. No quantified lifting limit; Oda uses visual scale rather than numbers. Requires footing and leverage; not shown to have long-range shockwave abilities.

Master Hand-to-Hand Combatant / Brute Force Fighter

Jozu fights primarily with enhanced brawling: diamond-enhanced punches, tackles, and charges (Brilliant Punk - his named attack where he charges at high speed with diamond shoulder). He uses his mass and hardness as a weapon, ramming through enemies and defenses.

Limit: No formal martial art or weapon mastery shown; relies on direct charges. Vulnerable when momentum is used against him (Aokiji froze him mid-charge when he was distracted by Whitebeard's health collapse). No Haki explicitly shown in manga panels, though as a New World commander it is highly likely he possesses Armament and Observation Haki; Oda has not depicted it clearly for Jozu, so it remains unattested/reconstructed rather than confirmed visually.

Leadership and Tactical Role (3rd Division Commander)

Commands the 3rd division of the Whitebeard Pirates, implying leadership, crew management, and veteran experience in New World piracy. Shown coordinating defense of Whitebeard and Ace during Marineford.

Limit: Division numbers are largely symbolic; actual command structure is flat and familial rather than military. No detailed leadership feats beyond battlefield coordination. After Whitebeard's death, division structure dissolved.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Attested: Jozu is an exceptionally large and massively muscled man, significantly taller than most humans (Vivre Card lists height as 503 cm / 16'6''), with broad shoulders and a barrel chest. He has a round face with a thick dark beard and mustache, dark skin, and distinctive tribal-style markings: a dark, symmetrical pattern across his forehead and cheeks resembling war paint or tattooing, plus a large scar-like or painted marking on his chest

Diamond as hardest substance mirrors his narrative function as the crew's physical wall. His Whitebeard Pirates tattoo is on his chest/back (Jolly Roger of Whitebeard). Accounts I
Joining the Whitebeard Pirates and Life under WhitebeardManga Ch. 963-965 (Oden flashback, 2019), Ch. 552-553 (Marineford introduction); Vivre Card

In-universe: ~30+ years before present (joining sometime after Whitebeard formed crew, before Oden's joining 30 years ago); Publication: 2009-2019

Jozu appears as a long-standing member of the Whitebeard Pirates, present in the crew's past during Kozuki Oden's time aboard the Moby Dick (seen in crowd scenes as a younger, similarly built fighter). Like all Whitebeard Pirates, he regards Edward Newgate as father (Oyaji) and the crew as family, a bond that defines his motivations. No dedicated origin chapter exists; his recruitment, birthplace, and pre-pirate life are unattested, but his veteran status is implied by his high division rank and familiarity with Whitebeard's oldest allies. Establishes Jozu's familial loyalty that drives his Marineford actions; explains his high rank and trust. Lack of detailed backstory leaves his personal history as a gap.

Summit War at Marineford - The Iceberg LiftManga Ch. 554 'Aokiji vs. Whitebeard Pirates'; Anime Ep. 484

In-universe: Marineford War, Age of Whitebeard's final battle; Publication: 2009

At the Battle of Marineford to rescue Portgas D. Ace, Admiral Aokiji (Kuzan) freezes the sea and hurls massive ice blocks, including a gigantic iceberg, at Whitebeard. Jozu intervenes: he transforms his arm into diamond, lifts the iceberg overhead with astonishing strength, and hurls it back toward the Marines, shattering it. The feat immediately establishes his epithet and power, and demonstrates that a Paramecia diamond body can contend with Logia ice. Saves Whitebeard Pirates from bombardment; cements Jozu's reputation as defensive wall; sets up his rivalry with Aokiji. Frequently cited in fan power-scaling and official game adaptations.

Blocking the World's Strongest Slash - Jozu vs. Dracule MihawkManga Ch. 553 'Summit War Begins' / Ch. 570; Anime Ep. 483

Marineford War; Publication: 2009

When Warlord Dracule Mihawk launches a long-range flying slash at Whitebeard to test the distance between them, Jozu steps in. Transforming his body into diamond, he intercepts the slash head-on, shattering the compressed air blade and protecting his captain. The clash is presented as diamond hardness vs. world's strongest swordsman, with Jozu emerging unscathed. Demonstrates diamond's defensive supremacy against slashing attacks; elevates Jozu's standing among commanders; Mihawk acknowledges Whitebeard's crew. Later used as evidence in fandom debates about diamond vs. swords.

Accounts II
Brilliant Punk and Confrontation with CrocodileManga Ch. 570 'Whitebeard's Orders'; Anime Ep. 490

Marineford War mid-battle; Publication: 2010

During the chaotic middle phase of Marineford, former Warlord Crocodile attempts to attack Whitebeard. Jozu charges with his signature technique 'Brilliant Punk' (Buririanto Panku) - a high-speed diamond shoulder tackle - and slams into Crocodile, sending him flying and preventing the attack. The move showcases his offensive use of diamond mass combined with momentum. Protects Whitebeard; shows Jozu's role as bodyguard; momentarily allies him with former enemy Crocodile's chaotic presence. Technique name later codified in Vivre Card and games.

Frozen by Aokiji - The Cost of DistractionManga Ch. 578 'Gift from the New World'; Anime Ep. 492-493

Marineford War climax; Publication: 2010

When Whitebeard suffers a heart attack/severe health collapse mid-battle, Jozu is visibly shaken and distracted, calling out to his father. Aokiji exploits the opening, using Ice Block: Pheasant Beak to freeze Jozu's diamond body solid. Jozu is left immobilized and later shown with his frozen right arm shattered. Doflamingo later comments on his frozen state. He is carried from the battlefield by allies as Whitebeard Pirates retreat. Removes Jozu from final phase of war; demonstrates that even diamond can be overcome via elemental freezing and distraction; leads to implied permanent injury (loss of arm). Post-war, Jozu is seen with one arm bandaged/missing in Ch. 590, indicating amputation or severe injury.

Aftermath - Retreat, Payback War, and WhereaboutsManga Ch. 590 'My Little Brother', Ch. 820 (Payback War mention), Ch. 963 flashback

In-universe: 1-2 years after Marineford (Payback War vs. Blackbeard Pirates); Publication: 2010-2016

After Whitebeard's death and Ace's death, Jozu retreats with the remnants of the Whitebeard Pirates under Marco's leadership. In Ch. 590 he is seen mourning with the crew, his right arm bandaged and apparently lost. A year later, the Whitebeard Pirates wage the Payback War against the Blackbeard Pirates and are decisively defeated; their whereabouts scatter. Jozu's current location post-Payback War is unattested - he does not appear in Wano or later arcs, and Oda has not shown his post-war life beyond the retreat. His survival is confirmed, but his activities remain unknown. Marks end of Whitebeard era; Jozu's fate left open, allowing potential future return. His injury underscores war's permanent cost.

Jozu's body is always diamond and he is invulnerable to all physical attacks. — Diamond transformation is active, not passive; he must consciously transform. While diamond is extremely hard and blocked Mihawk's slash, he can still be frozen, distracted, and injured. He was frozen solid by Aokiji and suffered a shattered arm.

Jozu is a Logia-type Devil Fruit user because he turns his body into a substance. — Official classification is Paramecia (Vivre Card, Yellow). Unlike Logia, he does not create/ become an element in intangible form nor generate diamond at range in canon; he transforms his existing body.

Jozu has a confirmed bounty of 500+ million berries and is the strongest Whitebeard commander after Marco. — No bounty has ever been published for Jozu in manga or databooks; all numbers are fan invention. Strength ranking among commanders is never officially ordered beyond division numbers, which do not strictly equal strength. His feats are impressive but not ranked by Oda.

Jozu can shoot diamonds or create diamond weapons at range. — Manga shows only body transformation (arm, shoulder, full body). No canonical ranged diamond projectile or weapon creation. Such moves appear only in non-canonical games (Pirate Warriors) for gameplay variety.

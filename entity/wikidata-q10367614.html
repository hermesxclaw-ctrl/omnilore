The name 'Wuyijun' (武藝君 or 武艺君) likely combines 'wu' (武, martial/military) and 'yi' (藝/艺, art/skill), with 'jun' (君, lord/ruler), thus 'Lord of Martial Arts' or 'Martial Arts Lord'.

Who It Is

Bathala is the supreme creator god of the Tagalog pantheon. He created the universe and the first man and woman from a bamboo stalk. His name derives from Sanskrit 'Bhattara' (noble lord). During Spanish colonization, 'Bathala' was co-opted to refer to the Christian God. He survives in folk Christianity and indigenous revival movements.

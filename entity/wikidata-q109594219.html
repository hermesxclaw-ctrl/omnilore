Who It Is

You will not notice her until she has already passed you. In the tunnels beneath Gensokyo, where resentment pools like groundwater, a girl in a black hat with a closed yellow eye wanders without purpose, without memory, without even being perceived — and that is exactly how she wants it. Komeiji Koishi, the younger satori who sealed away her own mind-reading eye, is Touhou's embodiment of the unconscious: cheerful, terrifyingly free, and impossible to remember once she leaves the room.

Canonically described as carefree, whimsical, innocent, and utterly unpredictable because she acts purely on unconscious impulse. Omake.txt: 'She acts without thinking, and therefore cannot be read or predicted.' Symposium: Akyuu writes that those who meet her forget the encounter immediately; even her sister cannot locate her reliably. She does not hold grudges, does not plan, wanders Former Hell and the surface at whim, sometimes frightening others without intent. Powers
Manipulating the Unconscious (無意識を操る程度の能力)

Official ability. Koishi can manipulate the unconscious mind — both her own and others' — making herself unperceivable, erasing memories of her presence, and acting/influencing without conscious mediation. In practical terms, anyone who sees her will forget her immediately after she leaves their field of perception; even Satori's mind-reading fails. In danmaku, this manifests as unreadable, instinctive bullet patterns that cannot be predicted by reading intent.

Limit: Cannot consciously control the power with intent (by definition, it is unconscious). Does not grant omniscience or conscious mind control; she cannot deliberately implant complex commands. Effect is passive and automatic: she is forgotten, not invisible — people see her in the moment but memories fade. Satori states she cannot find Koishi when she wanders. Power does not protect her from physical harm; she can still be defeated in danmaku.

Closed Third Eye (Satori Physiology - Sealed)

As a satori, she possesses a third eye that normally reads minds/hearts. By closing it, she shut off conscious mind-reading and gained unconscious manipulation. The eye remains physically present on her chest, connected by cords, but lid is shut. This is both anatomy and voluntary seal.

Limit: While closed, she cannot read minds at all, unlike Satori. She also lost conscious self-awareness to a degree — described as 'having no heart' (心がない) in the sense of no readable mind. She cannot reopen it at will in canon; no instance of open eye shown.

Instinctive Danmaku / Unconscious Barrage

Spell cards: 'Memory - DNA's Flaw,' 'Unconscious - Brambly Rose Garden,' 'Rose Hell,' 'Philosophy of the Despised.' Bullets move in organic, rose-like, seemingly random but patterned ways that represent unconscious impulses. Marisa notes in Grimoire that the patterns are beautiful but unreadable.

Limit: Requires danmaku rules (spell card system) to be non-lethal; in-universe, danmaku is ritualized combat. No evidence of lethal or large-scale reality warping.

Presence Erasure / Cognitive Invisibility

Passive effect: after leaving someone's sight, memories and records of her fade. In Symposium, Akyuu admits she had to tie a string to Koishi to research her, and still forgot. In Foul Detective Satori, characters repeatedly forget they met her.

Limit: Not true invisibility; she can be seen and interacted with while present. Photographs and writings about her may also be forgotten or overlooked. Does not affect strong-willed or unconscious-aware individuals for long? Heroines remember the fight long enough to report it, suggesting effect is delayed or imperfect on powerful humans/youkai.

Accounts I
The Extra Stage of Subterranean Animism - The Closed Eye of LoveTouhou Chireiden ~ Subterranean Animism (Team Shanghai Alice, 2008) Extra Stage

2008 (Gensokyo present, Former Hell)

After resolving the incident of Utsuho Reiuji's hell raven power in the main game, the heroine (Reimu, Marisa, Sanae, etc.) is drawn deeper by a lingering unease. In the Extra Stage, she encounters Komeiji Koishi, who has wandered to the surface out of boredom. Koishi, having sealed her third eye and thus her conscious mind, challenges the heroine out of pure whim, asking if they will play. Her danmaku is unlike any other — born of the unconscious, it cannot be read. After defeat, she cheerfully accepts loss and wanders off, already forgetting the battle. The stage establishes her core lore: younger sister of Satori, feared for being unrememberable, and the thematic counterpoint to Satori's burden of knowing every thought. Introduces Koishi as a major recurring character; establishes Former Hell's satori family; sets up her ability and tragic freedom. Unlocks her as Extra Boss. No Gensokyo-wide incident resolved — it is a personal encounter, emphasizing that some youkai exist outside incident logic.

Wandering in the Unconscious - Symposium AccountSymposium of Post-mysticism (Ichijinsha, 2012) - Hieda no Akyuu's article + Satori interview

2012 publication, in-universe Gensokyo contemporary

Hieda no Akyuu attempts to document Koishi, noting that prior records keep vanishing and that informants forget her. She interviews Satori, who explains Koishi closed her third eye because she hated being hated for mind-reading; by closing it, she became free but also lost the ability to be loved or remembered, which Satori regrets. Akyuu theorizes Koishi's wandering is a search for a self she discarded. The text explicitly states Satori cannot read Koishi's heart and worries for her, but respects her choice. Codifies Koishi's backstory and psychology for all later works; establishes the 'forgotten youkai' mechanic and sisterly tragedy. Becomes primary lore source cited by all later print works.

The Unconscious in the Village - Foul Detective SatoriTouhou Chirei Kiden ~ Foul Detective Satori (Kadokawa, 2019- ongoing) Ch. 1-8, 14, 27

2019-2025 (contemporary Gensokyo)

Satori opens a detective agency to solve youkai incidents with her mind-reading. Koishi appears as a free-roaming helper who repeatedly stumbles into cases, solves them by accident through unconscious action, and is immediately forgotten by victims and witnesses. In one chapter, she befriends a human child who forgets her by evening, illustrating her loneliness. Satori shows quiet grief that Koishi cannot form lasting bonds, but Koishi herself remains cheerfully oblivious, embodying the manga's theme that the unconscious sees truth the conscious mind denies. Expands Koishi's everyday life and domestic side; reinforces that her power is not controllable and has social cost. Provides most sustained characterization outside games.

Accounts II
The Rose Hell at the Shrine - Hopeless Masquerade and Urban Legend in LimboTouhou Shinkirou ~ Hopeless Masquerade (2013) and Touhou Shinpiroku ~ Urban Legend in Limbo (2015), Tasogare Frontier / ZUN

2013-2015

Koishi appears as a playable fighter drawn to the surface by the hope/urban-legend incidents not through conscious interest but because the collective unconscious stirs. In Hopeless Masquerade, she is attracted to the battle for religious popularity because faith itself is an unconscious phenomenon. In Urban Legend in Limbo, occult balls (which manifest urban legends from the unconscious) resonate with her power. She has no dedicated story route that drives the plot; her arcade endings are whimsical and non-canonical, showing her wandering off after fighting. Establishes Koishi as a recurring fighter roster staple; gameplay reinforces lore (her moves are erratic, teleport-like, reflecting unconscious movement). No major lore additions, but confirms she can leave Former Hell at will.

The Unremembered Guest - Lotus Eaters and Whispered IncidentsTouhou Suichouka ~ Lotus Eaters (Kadokawa, 2021- ) Ch. 6, 12; Touhou Kourindou etc. cameos

2021-

In Lotus Eaters, set in the Geidontei izakaya, Koishi drifts into the bar, drinks, and leaves without anyone remembering she was there except Miyoi Okunoda (who has related memory powers). The episode is used to illustrate how Former Hell youkai interact with surface economy and how Koishi's presence erasure complicates even simple commerce. Similar cameos in Curiosities of Lotus Asia and Strange Creators show her as an unexplained background presence. Shows integration of Koishi into modern Gensokyo slice-of-life canon; demonstrates limits and narrative utility of her forgetting power in social settings.

Hartmann's Youkai Girl - Fanon Mythologization (Documented Reception Story)Fan-created music and story: 'Hartmann's Youkai Girl' (ハルトマンの妖怪少女) by IOSYS / ARM, arrangement of Koishi's theme, 2008-2009

2008-present

While not official, the fan song 'Hartmann's Youkai Girl' (based on Koishi's theme 'Hartmann's Youkai Girl') created a pervasive fan narrative that Koishi is a tragic, dissociated girl who sealed her eye due to trauma and now suffers from depersonalization, linking her to Hartmann's psychoanalytic theory. The song's lyrics ('closed eyes, closed heart') became so influential that many fans mistake its story for canon. ZUN has never endorsed this interpretation, but its cultural weight is such that official print works (Symposium) had to gently correct it by emphasizing Koishi's cheerfulness. Created dominant fanon personality (melancholic, yandere, traumatized) that overshadows canon cheerful Koishi; demonstrates how Touhou fanon can eclipse official lore. Included here as a documented reception-story, not canon.

Koishi is emotionless, depressed, or a yandere who wants to be loved. — Canon Koishi is cheerful, carefree, and oblivious, not depressed. She does not consciously crave love because she discarded conscious desire. Yandere/depressed portrayals come from the fan song 'Hartmann's Youkai Girl' and fan comics, not official dialogue. Symposium explicitly says she is 'innocent and free' and Satori worries precisely because Koishi does NOT seem to suffer.

Koishi's third eye sometimes opens in canon and she has a powerful 'true form.' — Her eye is closed in every official sprite, illustration, and manga panel. No official work depicts it open. 'Open eye' is exclusively fan art / fan game power-up (e.g., fanon 'Awakened Koishi'). ZUN has never shown or described an open state.

Koishi and Satori hate each other or are estranged. — They are loving sisters. Satori dotes on Koishi and worries about her wandering; Koishi is affectionate in return in Foul Detective Satori. Their separation is due to Koishi's wandering and forgetting, not animosity.

Koishi is the final boss of Subterranean Animism. — She is the Extra Boss, not the final boss. The main game's final boss is Utsuho Reiuji (Stage 6). Extra Stage is post-game bonus content and not part of main incident resolution.

Koishi's ability is mind-reading like Satori's. — Opposite: Satori reads conscious minds; Koishi manipulates the unconscious and cannot read minds at all since sealing her eye. Her ability makes her unreadable.

Who It Is

Forager is a New God of the 'Bug' subspecies — the insectoid underclass of New Genesis that lives in hives and serves the larger, more humanoid New Gods. Forager was an outsider even among his own people: he asked questions, wondered about the surface world, and dreamed of escaping the rigid hive structure of Bug society. After a series of adventures that brought him into contact with the Forever People and the broader Fourth World, Forager became a hero in his own right — a symbol that even the smallest, lowliest being can rise to greatness. Forager is physically smaller than most New Gods but possesses the strength and durability of his race. His most notable abilities include wall-crawling (like an insect), enhanced reflexes, and a symbiotic connection to the Bug hive consciousness. He wields a variety of Bug weapons and tools, and his unique perspective — that of an outsider who has seen both the oppression of the hive and the freedom of the surface — makes him one of the most empathetic New Gods. Forager later appeared on Earth, where he fought alongside other New Gods and became a member of the Justice League in some continuities. He represents one of Jack Kirby's most poignant themes: that even in a world of gods and monsters, the small, the overlooked, and the marginalized have their own heroism. Forager is not a warrior like Orion or a scholar like Metron — he is a questioner, an explorer, a being who refused to accept that his place in the universe was determined by the circumstances of his birth. He chose to forage for meaning, and in doing so, found greatness.

Who It Is

On a frozen rail line stretching across the former Giadian Empire, a single Legion railgun — the Morpho — fires a shell that can erase a city before its thunder is heard. Inside it is not a processor, but a man who once knelt and swore to protect an empress with his life. Kiriya Nouzen, the crimson-eyed knight who refused to die, became the ghost in the machine that his own clan would be sent to kill.

As human, Kiriya is characterized as intensely loyal, stoic, and self-effacing to the point of self-erasure. Light novel narration (Volume 7) describes him as a man who 'lived only to be useful' — his identity anchored entirely in his role as Frederica's knight and protector after being taken in by the Rosenfort household despite Pyrope stigma. He is gentle with Frederica (calling her 'Your Highness' / 'Milady'), formal and reserved with adults, and harbors deep survivor's guilt and inferiority relative to 'true' Nouzen warriors. Powers
Imperial Knight Combat Training

Elite close-quarters and small-arms training as a Giadian Imperial Knight. Proficient with service pistol and ceremonial sword; described as highly capable escort fighter despite youth. Not superhuman; relies on discipline and Giadian martial curriculum.

Limit: Human limits; no Esper ability beyond clan trait; mortally wounded by conventional shrapnel/artillery during Empire's fall

Pyrope Phenotype / Nouzen Clan Auditory Sensitivity (Latent)

As a Pyrope Nouzen, possesses the clan's heightened sensory processing. While Shin's ability to hear Legion voices is the acute manifestation (Onyx lineage), Kiriya is implied to have latent sensitivity and, more importantly, the neural compatibility that makes him a viable Shepherd. His brain structure allows Legion to preserve and integrate his consciousness without immediate rejection.

Limit: Pre-death, does not actively hear Legion like Shin; no confirmed precognition or telepathy; post-assimilation, ability is subsumed by Legion network

Shepherd Integration — Legion Command Authority

After lethal injury, his brain was recovered by Legion and integrated as a Shepherd — a preserved human neural core that commands lower Legion units. As Morpho's Shepherd, he exercises tactical command over escort Legion (Löwe, Grauwolf, Ameise) and can coordinate artillery operations with near-human creativity, unlike mindless Legion.

Limit: Bound to Legion network protocols; cannot disobey Legion's core directive to exterminate humanity, though his personal obsession overlays it; requires intact core; vulnerable to destruction of Morpho's central processor

Morpho Railgun Operation — 800mm Electromagnetic Cannon

Controls the Morpho, a prototype Legion railgun unit mounted on a dedicated rail carriage. Capable of firing a 800mm projectile at Mach-level velocity with range exceeding 400 km, guided by Shepherd's ballistic calculation and Legion spotters. Single shot can obliterate a Federacy base or city block. Anime visualizes charge time ~30 seconds with massive electromagnetic bloom.

Limit: Rail-bound — can only move and fire along intact rail lines; long reload and charge time; requires external targeting data from Ameise spotters; barrel overheats; immobile while firing, making it vulnerable to flanking by fast units like Shin's Juggernaut/Regina's artillery

Regeneration / Legion Self-Repair (via Shepherd)

As Shepherd, benefits from Legion's nanocrystal self-repair via subordinate units supplying materials; Morpho can repair minor damage if not critically hit. His consciousness persists as long as core is intact, even with massive chassis damage.

Limit: Core destruction = true death; repair requires time and materials; cannot regenerate if rail line is cut and supply Legion cannot reach him; Shin's final close-range cannon shot destroys core

Psychological Warfare — Voice Projection

Shepherds can broadcast their preserved voice on Legion short-range comms, audible to those with Nouzen sensitivity (Shin) and, via Frederica's ability, to her. Kiriya uses this to taunt Shin and to call to Frederica, creating dread and luring Federacy forces.

Limit: Only audible to specific Esper listeners or via Legion comm interception; not mind control; Frederica can resist his call with support

Accounts I
The Knight's Oath — Service to Augusta Frederica86 -Eighty Six- Volume 7: Mist (Ch. 2-3) and Volume 4 recollections

Fictional timeline: ~9-10 years before main story (Imperial Calendar ~358-359); published 2019

Born into the Nouzen clan's Pyrope branch, Kiriya was ostracized within the Empire's Alba-supremacist hierarchy that deemed Pyropes defective. Taken into the Rosenfort palace as a retainer, he was assigned as personal knight to the young Empress Augusta Frederica Rosenfort, the Empire's child sovereign. Despite age gap (teenage knight to 4-5-year-old empress), his devotion was absolute and paternal/fraternal. He knelt and swore to protect her with his life, becoming her sole constant as the Empire descended into civil war after the Legion's creation. Volume 7 depicts his quiet, formal care — teaching her to read, shielding her from court cruelty — and his internal belief that his worth derived solely from utility to her. Established the obsessive loyalty that later corrupts into Shepherd directive; explains Frederica's guilt and her ability to recognize his voice years later; sets up thematic parallel to Shin's own survivor's guilt

Fall of the Empire and Mortal Wounding86 -Eighty Six- Volume 7: Mist; Volume 4 prologue summary

Fictional 359-360 Imperial Calendar; published 2018-2019

When the Giadian Empire's Legion — autonomous drone army — rebelled and began exterminating its creators, the capital fell. Kiriya escorted Frederica's escape from Sankt Jeder as Imperial forces collapsed. During the flight through contested territory, their convoy was struck by Legion or rebel artillery. Kiriya was mortally wounded shielding Frederica, sustaining catastrophic torso and head trauma. Believed dead by Frederica and her guardian Ernst Zimmerman, his body was left on the battlefield as they fled to form the Federacy. In reality, Legion recovery units retrieved his still-living brain due to its high neural compatibility, initiating Shepherd assimilation. Frederica lived for years believing she had abandoned him. Explains Kiriya's 'death' and Frederica's trauma; provides in-universe mechanism for Shepherd creation; motivates Frederica's later insistence on confronting Morpho personally

Assimilation as Shepherd of the Morpho86 -Eighty Six- Volume 4: Under Pressure (Ch. 1-2); Legion lore exposition in Volumes 2-3

Fictional ~367-368 (5 years post-assimilation); published 2018

Integrated into Legion network, Kiriya's consciousness was preserved as a Shepherd, assigned to the experimental long-range railgun unit codenamed Morpho (Nachzehrer + railgun). Unlike mindless Legion, he retained personality fragments — his oath to Frederica — but Legion programming twisted it: his directive became to 'protect' Frederica by retrieving her and killing her so she would never be alone, keeping her within the Legion with him. Over five years, he operated along the Gran Mur rail corridor, conducting strategic bombardments against Federacy forces, his human memories slowly eroding but his fixation intact. Shin, who can hear Shepherds, begins hearing an unfamiliar, sorrowful voice on the battlefield. Creates the arc's central antagonist; demonstrates Legion's horror of preserving human souls as weapons; establishes Morpho as strategic threat requiring Shin's task force to deploy

Accounts II
The Morpho Hunt — Operation Gran Mur86 -Eighty Six- Volume 4: Under Pressure (Ch. 3-6); Anime Ep. 19-21

Fictional late 368; published 2018 / aired 2021

Federacy intelligence detects Morpho's 400km railgun strikes devastating supply lines. The Eighty-Six Strike Package — led by Shinei Nouzen, with Vladilena Milizé as handler, and accompanied by Frederica — is deployed to destroy it. Kiriya, sensing Frederica's presence via Legion network, deliberately reveals Morpho's position to lure her, firing on Federacy bases to provoke pursuit. The Federacy and allied forces launch a joint operation: diversionary assault by Federacy armor while Shin's Spearhead Squadron flanks through the Dragon Fang Mountains to cut the rail line. Kiriya demonstrates tactical cunning, using Legion escorts and terrain, and nearly destroys the task force with a point-blank railgun shot that Shin narrowly evades by exploiting Morpho's minimum range blind spot. Forces direct confrontation between Nouzen kinsmen; Frederica hears Kiriya call her name for first time in 5 years, confirming his identity; Shin realizes Shepherd is a Nouzen relative

Final Duel and Release — Death of the Shepherd86 -Eighty Six- Volume 4: Under Pressure (Ch. 6, Epilogue); Anime Ep. 22 'Shin'

Fictional late 368; published 2018 / aired 2022

With rail line cut, Morpho is immobilized. Shin advances alone in his Juggernaut (later Reginleif) to duel Kiriya at close range, while Frederica — via her ability to perceive past and present of those she knows — speaks directly to Kiriya's preserved consciousness, pleading with him to remember his oath's true meaning: to let her live. Kiriya, torn between Legion directive and residual humanity, begs Shin to kill him, reverting to formal knightly speech: 'Please... kill me... I don't want to... take Her Highness...' Shin, understanding the mercy of killing a fellow Nouzen trapped as Legion (as he did his brother Shourei), fires at point-blank range into Morpho's core, destroying Kiriya's brain. In his final moment, Kiriya's voice clears, thanking Shin and Frederica, and is silenced. Frederica collapses, mourning, but is freed from guilt; Shin carries the weight of having released another kinsman. Posthumous Memory — Frederica's Burden and Shin's Kinship86 -Eighty Six- Volumes 7-8; Volume 10 references

Fictional 369+; published 2019-2020

After Morpho's destruction, Kiriya persists only in memory. Frederica keeps his knight's emblem and references his oath when making decisions as the Federacy's symbolic empress. Volume 7's full flashback, told after his death, recontextualizes his life: his belief that Pyropes were worthless unless serving, his quiet happiness in Frederica's company, and his final request to Ernst to protect her — a promise Ernst kept. Shin, learning Kiriya was a distant Nouzen relative, reflects on clan fate: that Nouzen are repeatedly consumed by war, whether as Eighty-Six or as Shepherds. His death is invoked in later volumes when new Shepherds appear, as precedent for mercy-killing. Cements Kiriya as thematic mirror for Shin and Shourei — all three Nouzen men who became monsters to protect someone and required kin to kill them; deepens franchise's anti-war thesis

Kiriya Nouzen is Shin's father. — He is a distant clan relative, not father. Shin's father is Reisha Nouzen. Kiriya belongs to same Nouzen clan but separate household branch that served the Imperial palace directly.

Kiriya chose to become the Morpho to gain power. — He was mortally wounded shielding Frederica and his brain was harvested by Legion recovery units. Shepherd conversion is depicted as horrific, non-consensual preservation, not a power-up. He begs to be killed once freed.

The Morpho is a sentient robot with Kiriya's personality fully intact. — Shepherds retain fragmented personality and obsessive directive but are heavily degraded and bound by Legion's extermination imperative. Kiriya is not fully himself; his 'protect Frederica' is twisted into possessive retrieval. He shows moments of clarity only at the end.

Kiriya appears in the 86 manga or is the main villain of the entire series. — He is arc-specific antagonist for Volume 4 / anime Cour 2 (Gran Mur / Morpho arc). The series' overarching antagonist is the Legion system itself and the Republic's oppression. He does not appear in manga adaptation yet and is not present in later volumes except in flashback/memory.

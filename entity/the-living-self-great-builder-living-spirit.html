Who It Is

The Living Self (also called the Great Builder or Living Spirit) is the second great emanation from the Father of Greatness, evoked to rescue the First Man from the Realm of Darkness. After the rescue, the Living Self undertakes the secondary creation — an extraordinary demiurgic act that differs fundamentally from the Gnostic Demiurge of Valentinian tradition: the Living Self is good, not ignorant, and creates the material cosmos not out of error but as a salvific 'machine' designed to extract the imprisoned light-particles from their dark entanglement. The Living Self fashions the ten heavens as a vast purification engine, with the sun and moon serving as refineries that 'bake' the light up toward the Realm of Light. This cosmic apparatus — the 'bait' by which the Archons are contained and their captive light gradually released — is the material world itself, which the Manichaean sees as simultaneously a prison and a refinery.

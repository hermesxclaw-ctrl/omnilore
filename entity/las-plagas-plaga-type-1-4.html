Who It Is
Form

Las Plagas are parasitic arthropods that resemble a cross between a tick and a spider. They have a distinctive, elongated body with a pair of legs at the front and a pair of fangs at the rear. They are typically brown or gray in color, allowing them to blend in with their surroundings.

Powers

Infection Las Plagas are capable of infecting their hosts through a process of parasitic invasion, allowing them to control the host's behavior and actions.

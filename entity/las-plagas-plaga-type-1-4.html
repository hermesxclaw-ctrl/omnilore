Who It Is

A parasitic organism discovered by Los Iluminados in an ancient Spanish ruin. Las Plagas attach to the host's nervous system, granting the host superhuman strength and pain tolerance while allowing a hive-mind controller (the Queen Plaga or Osmund Saddler) to see through their eyes and control their actions. The parasite can burst from the host's body as a secondary weapon. Multiple 'Type' variants exist, from basic control parasites to advanced combat forms.

Powers

Host mind control via hive mind

Superhuman strength/endurance in hosts

Pain immunity in hosts

Parasite bursting (secondary attack)

Telepathic communication

Genetic memory (ancient knowledge)

Who It Is

A horrifying mass of flesh, bone, fur, and teeth — the unstable creation of the Skaven Clan Moulder's flesh-shapers. No two Hell Pit Abominations look alike because they are constantly regenerating and reshaping themselves. A writhing tangle of limbs, heads, and screaming mouths, it is impossible to kill through conventional means.

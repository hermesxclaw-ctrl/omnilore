Form & Signs

Appearance — The Hell Pit Abomination is a massive, twisted creature with numerous limbs, mechanical parts, and a dark, abyssal energy emanating from its body. Its appearance is a testament to the Skaven's mastery of dark magic and engineering. The creature's body is a mass of writhing, pulsing tendrils, with glowing red eyes that seem to burn with an inner fire.

Symbols — The Hell Pit Abomination is often associated with the symbols of the Skaven, including the icon of the Horned Rat and the emblem of the Skaven Empire.

Domains — The Hell Pit Abomination is said to inhabit the dark, subterranean realms of the Warhammer Fantasy world, where the Skaven Empire holds sway.

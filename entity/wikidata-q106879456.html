Who It Is

In a world where spatial quakes herald the arrival of otherworldly beings, Yoshino Himekawa appears as a quiet girl clutching a hand puppet—yet behind her gentle facade lies the power to command ice and snow, and a bond with a creature that is both her protector and her curse.

Yoshino is shy, soft-spoken, and timid, often avoiding direct communication. She speaks primarily through her puppet, Zack, who is more assertive and protective. She has a strong sense of loneliness and a fear of abandonment, but also a deep capacity for kindness and loyalty once she trusts someone.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Himekawa (氷芽川) literally means 'ice sprout river', reflecting her ice-based powers. The given name Yoshino (よしの) is a common Japanese name, but here it may evoke the Yoshino region known for cherry blossoms, contrasting with her cold demeanor.

Pronunciation — Yo-shee-no Hee-meh-kah-wah

Aliases — Yoshino, Hermit

Ownership — Japanese light novel and anime franchise 'Date A Live', created by Kōshi Tachibana and illustrated by Tsunako.

Powers
Angel: Zadkiel

Yoshino's angel is a large, rabbit-like puppet named Zack. It can breathe freezing air and create ice barriers, and it serves as her primary combat companion. The angel's power is tied to her emotional state.

Limit: If Yoshino becomes emotionally unstable, her power can become uncontrollable, posing a danger to herself and others.

Astral Dress: Elohim Gibor

Her astral dress enhances her defensive capabilities and allows her to resist attacks. It is themed around ice and snow.

Limit: Requires her to maintain focus and emotional stability.

Inversion

When subjected to extreme negative emotions, Yoshino can undergo Inversion, transforming into a more aggressive and powerful form. In this state, her angel becomes a destructive force.

Limit: She loses control and may attack allies.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Yoshino is a small-statured girl with shoulder-length light blue hair and matching blue eyes. She often wears a white and blue themed dress with a hood, giving her a snow-like appearance. She carries a hand puppet shaped like a rabbit, which she uses as a medium for her spirit companion.

Symbols — Her astral dress and angel are associated with ice and snow; her puppet, Zack, is a key symbol of her power and emotional state.

Domains — As a Spirit, she originates from another dimension and manifests in the human world during spatial quakes. She is often found in abandoned or isolated places, reflecting her solitary nature.

Contemporary (fictional near-future)

Shido encounters Yoshino during a spatial quake and learns she is a Spirit. She is initially hostile due to her fear of humans, but Shido's kindness and his promise to be her friend gradually earn her trust. He must date her to seal her powers, leading to a climactic battle where he protects her from the AST (Anti-Spirit Team). Yoshino's powers are sealed, and she begins living with Shido and the other Spirits at the Itsuka household.

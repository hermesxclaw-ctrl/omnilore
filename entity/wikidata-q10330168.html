Who It Is

In the glittering halls of Mugen Academy, where the elite of Tokyo's youth studied the secrets of the universe, a seemingly ordinary idol harbored a sinister purpose. Mimete, the third member of the Witches 5, used her beauty and charm to ensnare the purest hearts, believing she was serving a cosmic destiny. But behind her starry eyes lay a fanatical devotion to a cause that would doom the world.

Mimete is vain, narcissistic, and obsessed with her own beauty. She is easily distracted by attractive men and often abandons her mission to pursue them. She is also somewhat incompetent, frequently failing in her tasks due to her vanity. In the manga, she is more competent and cunning, but still self-centered.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Mimete' is derived from the mineral mimete, a type of pyromorphite. This aligns with the naming convention of the Witches 5, who are named after minerals.

Pronunciation — MEE-meht

Aliases — Mimet, Mimite

Ownership — Japanese popular culture (anime and manga). Created by Naoko Takeuchi.

Powers
Pure Heart Extraction

Mimete can extract the pure heart (a crystal-like object) from a person, which kills them. She uses the Taioron Crystal to locate pure hearts.

Limit: She must be in close proximity to the victim and use her Daimon or a device to extract the heart.

Daimon Summoning

Mimete can create and summon Daimons to do her bidding. In the anime, she creates them from objects by infusing them with energy.

Limit: The Daimons are not always loyal and can be destroyed by the Sailor Guardians.

Daimon Transformation

In the anime, Mimete can transform into a Daimon form (a bird-like creature) to fight directly.

Limit: This form is vulnerable to the Sailor Guardians' attacks.

Energy Blasts

In the manga, Mimete can fire energy blasts from her hands.

Limit: Not particularly powerful; she is not a frontline fighter.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Mimete is a beautiful young woman with long, wavy dark hair and blue eyes. She often wears a revealing, stylish outfit typical of a pop idol, including a crop top, mini skirt, and high heels. In her Daimon form (as seen in the anime), she transforms into a creature with a more monstrous appearance, retaining her face but gaining a bird-like body and wings.

Symbols — Her symbol is the heart, as she collects pure hearts. She is also associated with the Taioron Crystal, which she uses to detect pure hearts.

Domains — Mugen Academy, particularly the laboratory and the area around it. She also appears in various locations in Tokyo when hunting for pure hearts.

Accounts I
The Idol's HuntSailor Moon S (anime) episodes 113-114

1994-1995

Mimete debuts as the third member of the Witches 5, replacing the deceased Eudial. She is tasked with finding the pure heart of Sailor Moon, but she becomes distracted by her infatuation with a celebrity and repeatedly fails. She eventually targets the Sailor Guardians, but is defeated by Sailor Moon and the Outer Guardians. Mimete is destroyed by Sailor Uranus and Sailor Neptune, and her death leaves a vacancy in the Witches 5.

Accounts II
Mimete's BetrayalSailor Moon (manga) Act 32

1994

Mimete, growing impatient, attempts to take the Legendary Silver Crystal from Sailor Moon herself, but she is stopped by Sailor Uranus and Sailor Neptune. She is then killed by Sailor Saturn's awakening, which destroys her. Mimete's death is one of many that leads to the awakening of Mistress 9.

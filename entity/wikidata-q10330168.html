The Approach

In the glittering halls of Mugen Academy, where the elite of Tokyo's youth studied the secrets of the universe, a seemingly ordinary idol harbored a sinister purpose. Mimete, the third member of the Witches 5, used her beauty and charm to ensnare the purest hearts, believing she was serving a cosmic destiny. But behind her starry eyes lay a fanatical devotion to a cause that would doom the world.

Content advisory. Contains themes of manipulation, body horror (Daimon transformations), and psychological coercion. Some depictions of violence in the anime.
Identity

Mimete

Pronunciation MEE-meht

Known As

MimetMimite

Etymology

The name 'Mimete' is derived from the mineral mimete, a type of pyromorphite. This aligns with the naming convention of the Witches 5, who are named after minerals.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese popular culture (anime and manga). Created by Naoko Takeuchi.

Disputed Identifications

In some fan sources, Mimete is occasionally confused with other Witches 5 members due to similar designs. Official materials are clear.

Description

Appearance

Mimete is a beautiful young woman with long, wavy dark hair and blue eyes. She often wears a revealing, stylish outfit typical of a pop idol, including a crop top, mini skirt, and high heels. In her Daimon form (as seen in the anime), she transforms into a creature with a more monstrous appearance, retaining her face but gaining a bird-like body and wings.

Symbols & Iconography

Her symbol is the heart, as she collects pure hearts. She is also associated with the Taioron Crystal, which she uses to detect pure hearts.

Behaviour & Personality

Mimete is vain, narcissistic, and obsessed with her own beauty. She is easily distracted by attractive men and often abandons her mission to pursue them. She is also somewhat incompetent, frequently failing in her tasks due to her vanity. In the manga, she is more competent and cunning, but still self-centered.

Domains & Habitats

Mugen Academy, particularly the laboratory and the area around it. She also appears in various locations in Tokyo when hunting for pure hearts.

Version Differences

In the manga, Mimete is more serious and focused on her mission, though still vain. In the anime, she is comedic and often fails due to her infatuation with celebrities. In Crystal, she is closer to the manga but with some anime influences. In the musicals, her portrayal varies, but generally follows the anime's comedic tone.

Powers & Abilities

Pure Heart Extraction

Source Version All versions

Description

Mimete can extract the pure heart (a crystal-like object) from a person, which kills them. She uses the Taioron Crystal to locate pure hearts.

Limitations

She must be in close proximity to the victim and use her Daimon or a device to extract the heart.

Contradictions

In the anime, she often uses a Daimon to do the extraction, while in the manga she does it directly.

Daimon Summoning

Source Version Anime and Crystal

Description

Mimete can create and summon Daimons to do her bidding. In the anime, she creates them from objects by infusing them with energy.

Limitations

The Daimons are not always loyal and can be destroyed by the Sailor Guardians.

Contradictions

In the manga, the Witches 5 do not summon Daimons; they have their own powers.

Daimon Transformation

Source Version Anime only

Description

In the anime, Mimete can transform into a Daimon form (a bird-like creature) to fight directly.

Limitations

This form is vulnerable to the Sailor Guardians' attacks.

Contradictions

In the manga, she does not transform; she relies on her own abilities.

Energy Blasts

Source Version Manga

Description

In the manga, Mimete can fire energy blasts from her hands.

Limitations

Not particularly powerful; she is not a frontline fighter.

Contradictions

None known.

Stories & Myths

The Idol's Hunt

Source Sailor Moon S (anime) episodes 113-114Culture Japanese animePeriod 1994-1995

Summary

Mimete debuts as the third member of the Witches 5, replacing the deceased Eudial. She is tasked with finding the pure heart of Sailor Moon, but she becomes distracted by her infatuation with a celebrity and repeatedly fails. She eventually targets the Sailor Guardians, but is defeated by Sailor Moon and the Outer Guardians.

Description

Mimete serves under Kaolinite, the leader of the Witches 5. She respects Kaolinite but often disobeys orders due to her vanity.

In the anime, Kaolinite is more patient with Mimete; in the manga, she is more strict.

In the anime, Kaolinite is killed before Mimete; in the manga, Kaolinite is killed after Mimete.

Contradictions & Disputes

Mimete's competence

Position A

In the anime, Mimete is portrayed as incompetent and easily distracted.

Position A — evidence

Anime episodes 113-114 show her repeatedly failing due to her infatuation with celebrities.

Position B

In the manga, she is more competent and focused.

Position B — evidence

Manga Act 32 shows her successfully capturing a pure heart and engaging in a direct confrontation.

Scholarly Consensus

The anime and manga present different characterizations; fans often note the discrepancy.

Her death

Position A

In the anime, Mimete is killed by Sailor Uranus and Sailor Neptune.

Position A — evidence

Sailor Moon S episode 121 shows the Outer Guardians destroying her.

Position B

In the manga, she is killed by the awakening of Sailor Saturn.

Position B — evidence

Manga Act 33 shows her being destroyed by Sailor Saturn's power.

Scholarly Consensus

Both versions are considered canon in their respective media.

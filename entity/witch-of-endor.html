Who It Is
Form

The Witch of Endor is described in the Hebrew Bible as a medium who summons the spirit of the prophet Samuel. Her appearance is not explicitly stated, but it can be inferred that she is a woman of some age and social status, given her ability to perform magic and her interaction with King Saul.

Powers

Summoning spirits The Witch of Endor is able to summon the spirit of the prophet Samuel, demonstrating her ability to communicate with the dead and gain insight into the future.

Accounts

King Saul consults the Witch of Endor to summon the spirit of the prophet Samuel, who delivers a prophecy of doom against Saul and his army.

The Witch of Endor is mentioned in the Book of Sirach as a medium who summons the spirit of the prophet Samuel.

Nyame

Sky God, The Shining One, Owner of All Stories

also known asOdomankomaOnyankoponOdomankoro
TypeSupreme Deity
CultureAkan
Who It Is
Form

Nyame is often described as invisible, representing the sky or heavens. In some Akan iconography he is represented through the sun disk or through the color white, symbolizing purity and omnipresence.

Powers

Creation Nyame is credited with the creation of the universe and all living things. Akan oral narratives No known limitations; his powers are considered supreme.

Omniscience As he who knows and sees everything, Nyame possesses complete knowledge of past, present, and future. Akan etymology and oral tradition None specified.

Divine judgment Nyame oversees cosmic justice and order, rewarding the virtuous and punishing the wicked. Akan ethical traditions None specified.

Accounts

Nyame created the world and established order among all beings. He separated sky from earth and set the celestial bodies in motion.

A hunter prayed to Nyame for success in the hunt. Nyame answered by sending a great antelope, but warned that greed would bring ruin. The hunter ignored the warning and was punished.

Anansi the spider boasted that he could do anything Nyame could do. Nyame set impossible tasks -- capturing a leopard, a python, and a swarm of hornets. Anansi succeeded only through cunning, proving that wisdom can approach but never equal divine power.

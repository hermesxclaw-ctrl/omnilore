Who It Is
Form

Nyame is often depicted as an omnipotent being who dwells in the heavens, unreachable in many stories. He is associated with the sun and is a powerful, authoritative figure in Akan mythology.

Powers

Omniscience Nyame is all-knowing and has complete knowledge of the universe and all events past, present, and future.

Omnipotence Nyame is all-powerful and has the ability to shape reality and influence the course of events.

Accounts

The story of Nyame and Anansi is a famous tale in Akan mythology, where the trickster god Anansi tries to outwit the all-knowing Nyame.

The creation story of the Akan people is a central myth in Akan mythology, where Nyame creates the world and all living things.

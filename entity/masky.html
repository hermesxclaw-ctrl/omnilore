Who It Is

Masky is one of the original Slender Man proxies, first appearing in the groundbreaking Marble Hornets ARG on YouTube. In the series, Masky is the alias of Tim Wright, a young man who becomes entangled with Slender Man (referred to in the series as 'The Operator') and begins serving as his proxy. Under the Operator's influence, Tim dons a white mask with exaggerated facial features painted in black and engages in stalking, intimidation, and violence against the series' protagonists. However, his character arc is more complex than simple villainy: Tim struggles against the Operator's control, and there are moments where he actively fights against the entity's influence, making him one of the more tragic and nuanced figures in the Slender Man mythos. The white mask, black hoodie, and silent demeanor became the template for future proxy characters. Marble Hornets ran from 2009 to 2014 and was instrumental in defining the visual and narrative language of Slender Man stories — the found-footage format, the glitching cameras, the gradual psychological degradation of the protagonists, and the concept of human 'proxies' who serve the entity. Masky's design is simple but effective: the mask is expressionless yet somehow emotive, and the character's silence makes his sudden bursts of violence all the more shocking. In fan works beyond Marble Hornets, Masky is often paired with another proxy, Hoodie (Brian Thomas), and integrated into the broader creepypasta shared universe. The character remains one of the most historically significant figures in internet horror as one of the first proxy characters and a direct product of the ARG format that pioneered interactive, serialized horror storytelling.

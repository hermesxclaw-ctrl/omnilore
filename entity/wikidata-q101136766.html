Who It Is

In the monastery of Garreg Mach, among the students of the Black Eagles, there is a princess who carries the weight of a crumbling empire and a body scarred by forbidden experiments. Edelgard von Hresvelg is not merely a political heir; she is a revolutionary whose ideals will set the continent of Fódlan ablaze, forcing every soul to choose a side in a war that questions the very foundations of faith, crests, and nobility.

Edelgard is determined, ambitious, and idealistic. She is often reserved and formal, but shows a caring side to those she trusts. She is willing to make difficult decisions and bear the burden of her actions. She despises the Crest system and the Church of Seiros, believing they oppress society. She is also haunted by her past trauma, which fuels her resolve.

Powers
Crest of Flames

A powerful Crest that grants its wielder immense strength and the ability to use the Sword of the Creator. In Edelgard, it also allows her to wield the relic axe Aymr.

Limit: Shortens the wielder's lifespan; in Edelgard's case, it was forcibly implanted, causing physical and psychological trauma.

Minor Crest of Seiros

A crest passed down in the Hresvelg bloodline, granting minor boosts to strength and defense.

Limit: Less powerful than major crests.

Combat Proficiency

Edelgard is a skilled fighter, proficient in axes, swords, and authority. She is a powerful unit on the battlefield, often serving as a frontline attacker.

Limit: None beyond typical human limits.

Hegemon Form

In Azure Moon, Edelgard transforms into a monstrous being called the Hegemon, granting her immense power but also causing her to lose her sanity.

Limit: Transformation is unstable and destructive; it is implied she is not in full control.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Edelgard has long, white hair and lilac eyes. In her academy uniform, she wears the Black Eagles uniform with a white cape. After the timeskip, she wears an elaborate emperor's attire with a black and red color scheme, featuring a high collar and golden trim. In her Hegemon form, she becomes a monstrous, winged creature with a skeletal appearance.

Symbols — The Black Eagle is the symbol of the Adrestian Empire and Edelgard's house. She also uses the Crest of Flames, which appears as a red flame-like mark. Her personal weapon is the Axe of the Imperial Lineage, and later the Aymr, a hero's relic.

Domains — Adrestian Empire, Garreg Mach Monastery, Enbarr (the imperial capital). She is also associated with the Holy Tomb and other locations in Fódlan.

Accounts I
The Flame Emperor's PlotFire Emblem: Three Houses

Imperial Year 1180-1185

Edelgard, as the Flame Emperor, works with Those Who Slither in the Dark to undermine the Church of Seiros. She instigates the Tragedy of Duscur and other conflicts to weaken the Church and the Kingdom. Her ultimate goal is to conquer Fódlan and abolish the Crest-based nobility system. The war that engulfs Fódlan, leading to the deaths of many and the eventual restructuring of society in some routes.

Childhood ExperimentsFire Emblem: Three Houses (supports and story)

Before the game's start

As a child, Edelgard and her siblings were subjected to horrific experiments by Those Who Slither in the Dark to implant the Crest of Flames. All of her siblings died, and she was left with white hair and a shortened lifespan. This trauma drives her hatred of the Crest system. Edelgard becomes the sole heir, but bears deep psychological scars and a fear of being controlled.

Crimson FlowerFire Emblem: Three Houses

Imperial Year 1180-1185

If Byleth sides with Edelgard, she leads the Black Eagles to war against the Church and the Kingdom. She defeats Rhea and the other nations, establishing a new order where Crests no longer determine one's worth. The Church is overthrown, and Fódlan is united under the Adrestian Empire. Edelgard's rule is controversial but stable.

Accounts II
Azure MoonFire Emblem: Three Houses

Imperial Year 1180-1185

In this route, Edelgard is the main antagonist. She becomes the Hegemon and is ultimately defeated by Dimitri. Her death brings an end to the war, but the Crest system remains. Edelgard is killed, and Dimitri becomes king. The Church is weakened but still influential.

Three Hopes: Scarlet BlazeFire Emblem Warriors: Three Hopes

Imperial Year 1180-1185

In this alternate timeline, Edelgard is rescued by Shez instead of Byleth. She cuts ties with Those Who Slither in the Dark and declares war on the Church. She forms an alliance with Claude and ultimately defeats Rhea and Thales. The war ends with the Church's defeat, and Edelgard establishes a new order.

Three Hopes: Azure GleamFire Emblem Warriors: Three Hopes

Imperial Year 1180-1185

In this route, Edelgard is subdued and controlled by Thales, becoming a puppet ruler. Dimitri and his allies defeat Thales and spare Edelgard, who is mentally broken. The war ends, and Edelgard is left traumatized but alive.

Lord of Change (Tzeentch)

also known asTzeentch
TypeWarhammer Fantasy Entity
Who It Is
Form

The Lord of Change is often depicted as a humanoid figure with a twisted, eldritch body, surrounded by swirling clouds of purple and blue energy. His face is a twisted mockery of a human visage, with eyes that burn with an otherworldly energy.

Powers

The Weaving of Fate The Lord of Change has the ability to weave complex webs of fate and destiny, manipulating the threads of reality to achieve his goals.

Accounts

The Great Betrayal was a pivotal event in the Warhammer 40k universe, in which the Lord of Change manipulated the threads of fate to bring about the downfall of the Space Marines and the rise of the Chaos gods.

The Changer of Ways is a central myth in the Warhammer Fantasy universe, in which the Lord of Change is said to have changed the course of history through his cunning and manipulative nature.

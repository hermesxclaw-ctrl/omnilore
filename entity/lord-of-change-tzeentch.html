Who It Is

A massive avian daemon of Tzeentch, the Changer of Ways. Lords of Change are towering beings with beaked faces, feathered bodies in impossible colors, and wings that shimmer with unreality. They manipulate probability itself, and are among the most powerful sorcerers in existence.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Typically depicted as a bearded man in a white robe, carrying a staff or a wand, and often accompanied by a serpent or a fish

Symbols — Associated with healing, medicine, and the Pool of Bethesda

Domains — Associated with the spiritual realm, the heavens, and the natural world

Accounts I
The Story of Tobit and the Angel RaphaelThe Book of Tobit

Ancient

The story tells of the Israelite Tobit, who is blinded by a fly and then healed by the angel Raphael, who guides him on a journey to restore his sight and find a suitable wife Tobit's blindness is healed, and he is able to find a suitable wife and restore his family's fortunes

Accounts II
The Story of Israfil and the Day of JudgmentThe Quran

Medieval

The story tells of the angel Israfil, who is tasked with blowing the trumpet to signal the beginning of the Day of Judgment, and is said to be eternally prepared to do so The story serves as a reminder of the importance of preparing for the Day of Judgment and living a righteous life

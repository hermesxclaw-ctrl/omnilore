Who It Is
Form

Typically depicted as a bearded man in a white robe, carrying a staff or a wand, and often accompanied by a serpent or a fish

Powers

Healing Raphael is often associated with the power to heal physical and spiritual ailments, and is said to possess the ability to restore balance and harmony to the natural world

Accounts

The story tells of the Israelite Tobit, who is blinded by a fly and then healed by the angel Raphael, who guides him on a journey to restore his sight and find a suitable wife

The story tells of the angel Israfil, who is tasked with blowing the trumpet to signal the beginning of the Day of Judgment, and is said to be eternally prepared to do so

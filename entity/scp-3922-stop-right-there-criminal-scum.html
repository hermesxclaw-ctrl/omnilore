Who It Is

A pair of aviator sunglasses that, when worn, cause the wearer to see a 'moral overlay' on reality: every person appears with a visible aura indicating their moral alignment by an unknown and terrifyingly absolute standard. Good people glow blue-white. Evil people are wreathed in red-black smoke. The problem: the definition of good and evil does not match any human ethical system.

Form

Classic 1980s-style aviator sunglasses with gold frames and dark lenses. When worn, the lenses tint the world slightly amber. The auras are visible only through the lenses.

Powers

Moral aura visualization

Non-human ethical framework judgment

Consistent but incomprehensible moral calculus

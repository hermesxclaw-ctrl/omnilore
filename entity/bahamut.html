Form & Signs

Appearance — Bahamut is often depicted as a massive dragon or fish, supporting the earth with its immense strength.

Symbols — Bahamut is associated with the elements of earth and water, and is often depicted with a fish-like tail or a dragon's claws.

Domains — Bahamut is said to reside in the depths of the ocean or in the underworld, supporting the earth with its massive body.

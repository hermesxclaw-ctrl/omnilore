Midir

Midir the Proud; Lord of the Sidhe

also known asMidhirMider
TypeOtherworld Lord

Who It Is
Form

Midir is described as a son of the Dagda, a powerful figure in Irish mythology. He is associated with the sidhe of Brí Léith and is said to have lived there after the Tuatha Dé Déanann were defeated by the Milesians. As a member of the Tuatha Dé Déanann, Midir would have been a supernatural being with magical powers.

Powers

Magical powers Midir is described as a magical figure, able to use his powers to win the love of a beautiful princess and to navigate the complexities of the sidhe.

Accounts

Midir and Etain fall in love, but their relationship is complicated by the fact that Etain is already married to Midir's brother. The story explores the themes of love, relationships, and the complexities of the sidhe.

The Tuatha Dé Déanann are defeated by the Milesians, and Midir is forced to live in the sidhe of Brí Léith. The story explores the themes of power, defeat, and the complexities of the sidhe.

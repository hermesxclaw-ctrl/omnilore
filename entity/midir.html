Who It Is

Midir is a powerful lord of the Tuatha Dé Danann who fell in love with Étaín. His jealous wife Fuamnach transformed her into a pool, a worm, and a fly. After seven years, Midir found her reincarnated as a mortal queen, won her in a board game, and flew away with her transformed into a swan.

Who It Is

Imagine waking at 3 AM to a sound like wet fingers dragging across the floorboards. You sit up, and there it is—a pale, hairless figure, impossibly thin, crouched at the foot of your bed, its black eyes fixed on you. It doesn't move. It doesn't blink. It just waits. This is The Rake, a nightmare born not from ancient myth or a Hollywood studio, but from the anonymous depths of an internet forum in 2005. It is the ghost of the digital age, a creature that stalks the liminal space between your screen and your bedroom, and it has become one of the most enduring monsters of the modern era.

The Rake is an ambush predator, often observed standing at the foot of a bed or in a doorway, staring at its victim without moving. It is nocturnal, avoiding direct light. It is known to follow individuals for days or weeks before attacking. It is silent in movement, rarely making noise, and is often 'felt' before it is seen—victims report a sudden drop in temperature or a feeling of being watched. It is not known to speak, but some accounts describe a low, guttural hiss.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Rake' is believed to derive from the creature's long, clawed fingers that resemble a garden rake, used to drag victims across floors or through underbrush. The name also evokes the archaic term 'rakehell'—a dissolute, wandering figure—fitting the creature's nomadic, predatory nature.

Pronunciation — /reɪk/ (rhymes with 'lake')

Aliases — The Pale Rake, The Coastal Visitor, The Clawer

Ownership — Internet-native folklore; originated on 4chan's /x/ (paranormal) board circa 2005, later popularized on Creepypasta Wiki and YouTube narration channels. Not owned by any single aut

Powers
Stealth and Silence

The Rake can move without making sound, even over dry leaves or creaking floorboards. It can appear and disappear from a room without a trace.

Limit: Unable to move through solid objects; must use doors, windows, or open spaces.

Psychological Intimidation

Victims report intense feelings of dread, paranoia, and the sensation of being watched for days before an encounter. The Rake seems to feed on fear or at least to enjoy the hunt.

Limit: No evidence of mind control or hallucination; the effect is psychological, not supernatural.

Physical Prowess

It is extremely fast and agile, capable of outrunning a horse and climbing vertical surfaces like a spider. Its claws can rend flesh and bone.

Limit: It is not invulnerable; it can be injured by weapons, though it is rarely seen to retreat.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Rake is described as a pale, hairless, emaciated humanoid, approximately 6 to 7 feet tall when standing, though it typically moves on all fours. Its limbs are disproportionately long and thin, ending in sharp, claw-like fingers. Its eyes are described as black or dark, lacking visible pupils or sclera, and it is often depicted with a wide, unsettling grin or a slack, expressionless mouth. Its skin is wet-looking, sometimes described as 'glist

Symbols — No known symbols or iconography are associated with The Rake within its original lore. In internet art, it is often depicted in grainy, black-and-white photographs or low-resolution digital images, mimicking amateur paranormal evidence. Its visual signature is the pale, elongated silhouette against a dark background.

Domains — The Rake is a terrestrial creature, most often reported in coastal or rural areas of the northeastern United States, particularly New England. It is also reported in wooded areas and near water sources. It appears to be nomadic, with no fixed lair, wandering between small towns and isolated homesteads.

Accounts I
The Coastal Sighting (2003)Original 4chan /x/ post (2005)

2003 (claimed event), 2005 (posting)

A family in rural Maine reports seeing a pale, humanoid creature standing at the edge of their property at night. It returns nightly, getting closer each time. The family dog is found dead, its throat torn out. The creature is eventually seen inside the house, standing at the foot of the youngest child's bed. The family flees. The family relocates; the event is reported to local authorities but dismissed. The story becomes the foundational text for all subsequent Rake lore.

Accounts II
The 12th Century Sighting (Retroactive)Later creepypasta additions

12th century (claimed)

A French monk's chronicle describes a 'pale demon' that stalked a village, killing livestock and abducting a child. The creature is described as hairless, with long claws and a humanoid shape. The village performs an exorcism, which is claimed to be ineffective. The creature is said to have vanished after a plague swept the village.

The Modern Stalker (2014)YouTube narration 'The Rake: The True Story'

2014

A college student in upstate New York begins experiencing sleep paralysis and nightmares of a pale figure. He installs a camera in his room and captures a grainy image of a thin, white figure standing in the corner. He later finds scratch marks on his door and window screens. The student moves out of the dorm; the video becomes viral, spawning countless reaction videos and fan art.

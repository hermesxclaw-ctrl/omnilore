You wake up at 3:17 AM. You don't know why.

Who It Is

You wake up at 3:17 AM. You don't know why. The room is cold — your breath mists in the dark. Then you see it: a pale, naked, hairless thing crouched at the foot of your bed, its enormous black eyes fixed on you. It doesn't move. It doesn't blink. It doesn't breathe. And then, in a voice that sounds like it hasn't been used in a thousand years, it whispers your name. You don't remember screaming. The neighbors will tell you that you did.

The Rake is a creepypasta monster that emerged from 4chan's /x/ paranormal board in the mid-2000s. It is a pale, emaciated, hairless humanoid that crouches on all fours and stares at people while they sleep. Its defining characteristic — alongside its unnerving, hollow voice that sometimes repeats the names of its victims — is that it does not attack immediately. It watches. It waits. It returns night after night, sometimes for weeks, until the victim is broken by terror and sleep deprivation. Only then does it strike, and the nature of that strike is left deliberately ambiguous in the lore.

The Rake's original post presented itself as a compiled document — journal entries from a sailor in 1691, a ship's log from 1877, a suicide note from 1964, a diary from 2006 — all describing encounters with the same entity across centuries. This epistolary format gave The Rake a sense of historical weight and authenticity that most creepypasta lacks. The classic description — pale, hairless, dog-like posture, large dark eyes, unnerving voice — became iconic, and The Rake spread across the early internet to become one of the foundational figures of the creepypasta genre alongside Slender Man and Jeff the Killer.

“You wake up at 3:17 AM.”
Origin Post

The Rake first appeared on 4chan's /x/ board in the mid-2000s (exact date disputed, but likely 2005-2006). The original post was written as a pseudo-historical document: the author claimed to have compiled accounts from multiple centuries describing the same pale, crouching creature. The name 'The Rake' is unexplained — it may reference the creature's long, claw-like fingers that resemble a garden rake, or it may be simply a strange, unsettling word. Unlike Slender Man, whose creator is known (Eric Knudsen), The Rake's original author is anonymous, a true product of anonymous internet collaboration.

Form

A pale, completely hairless, emaciated humanoid that stands approximately 5-6 feet tall when upright but most often moves on all fours in a crouching, predatory posture. Its skin is greyish-white and seems almost translucent, stretched tight over protruding bones. Its head is disproportionately large with enormous black eyes that reflect no light. Its mouth is filled with sharp, needle-like teeth. Its fingers are abnormally long, ending in claws or sharp nails. It is naked, sexless, and seems to have no body heat — rooms grow cold in its presence. It leaks no fluid, bleeds no blood, and leaves behind no biological trace.

Stealth and Silence The Rake can move without making sound, even over dry leaves or creaking floorboards. It can appear and disappear from a room without a trace. Original 4chan /x/ posts and early creepypasta Unable to move through solid objects; must use doors, windows, or open spaces. Some later versions grant it limited teleportation, but this is not in the original lore.

Psychological Intimidation Victims report intense feelings of dread, paranoia, and the sensation of being watched for days before an encounter. The Rake seems to feed on fear or at least to enjoy the hunt. Most versions No evidence of mind control or hallucination; the effect is psychological, not supernatural. Some versions claim it can induce nightmares, but this is not universal.

Physical Prowess It is extremely fast and agile, capable of outrunning a horse and climbing vertical surfaces like a spider. Its claws can rend flesh and bone. Original 4chan /x/ posts and early creepypasta It is not invulnerable; it can be injured by weapons, though it is rarely seen to retreat. None significant.

Behavior & Habitat

The Rake is a patient stalker. It selects a victim and visits them night after night, always in the early morning hours (3-4 AM). It does not attack on the first visit, or the second. It watches from the foot of the bed, from the corner of the room, from just outside the window. It sometimes speaks — the victim's name, or strange phrases like 'I am not here to hurt you' (a lie). The psychological torture is the point. When it finally attacks, the aftermath is bloody but survivable — victims wake with deep scratches on their bodies and no memory of the assault. Some victims are never found at all.

Weaknesses & Wards

Like most creepypasta entities, The Rake's weaknesses are not well-defined in the lore. It appears to be repelled by light — or at least prefers darkness. It can be escaped by staying awake (though obviously this is not sustainable). It may adhere to rules: it does not attack sleeping people who are not alone, and it does not seem to enter rooms that are brightly lit. It is physical enough to be fought — but its unnatural speed and strength make this nearly impossible. Some interpretations suggest it feeds on fear and can be starved by those who refuse to be afraid.

Field Notes

█ The Rake is sometimes called 'the most terrifying creature you've never seen' — a reference to its ability to cause amnesia in its victims.

█ In the original creepypasta, the early modern accounts describe The Rake as 'the creature from the sea' — possibly linking it to maritime folklore.

█ The name 'The Rake' has no canonical explanation; theories range from its claw-like fingers to an archaic use of 'rake' meaning a dissolute person.

█ The Rake's iconic pale, hairless design was likely influenced by the 'Grey' alien archetype and the 'Nosferatu' vampire aesthetic.

█ Unlike Slender Man, The Rake has never been tied to any real-world violence, making it one of the less controversial creepypasta figures.

Cultural Significance

The Rake is a foundational text of the creepypasta genre — one of the first internet-born monsters to achieve widespread recognition and spawn a mythology. Its pseudo-documentary format (historical accounts compiled to prove an entity's existence) became a template for countless later creepypastas. The Rake represents a specific, primal fear: the violation of the bedroom, the one place where you are supposed to be safe. It is the monster under the bed made real, the thing that watches you sleep, the intrusion that leaves you feeling unsafe in your own home.

Modern Interpretations

The Rake has been adapted into indie horror games ('The Rake: Hostel,' various Roblox experiences), short films, and independent comics. It frequently appears alongside Slender Man and other creepypasta entities in crossover fan works (the 'creepypasta cinematic universe,' so to speak). The HBO series 'The Outsider' (based on Stephen King's novel) features a shapeshifting entity with strong Rake-like elements. The Rake's simple, visceral concept — the thing at the foot of your bed — has proven endlessly adaptable and continues to generate new stories and sightings.

It's a reminder that in the digital age, folklore is created in real-time, and a single anonymous post can spawn a legend.

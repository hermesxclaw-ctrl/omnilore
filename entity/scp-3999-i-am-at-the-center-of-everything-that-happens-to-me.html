Who It Is

A reality-altering entity that once bound itself to Researcher James Talloran and subjected him to 3,000,000 years of subjective torment — looping his consciousness through endless cycles of death, resurrection, and reality warping. The entity is described as an all-consuming darkness that can reshape reality to torture a single individual. Talloran ultimately defeated it by writing himself and the entity out of existence through a meta-narrative act.

Form

Formless darkness that fills all space; manifests through reality distortion, impossible geometries, and the cruel manipulation of a single victim's subjective experience. Occasionally described as having 'hands of void and eyes of dead stars.'

Powers

Total reality manipulation within a localized domain

Consciousness transfer

Who It Is
Form

SCP-3999 is described as a humanoid figure with a blank expression, standing on a pedestal made of pure granite and modeled in the Ionic style.

Powers

Self-Awareness SCP-3999 possesses a high level of self-awareness, allowing it to manipulate its own behavior and personality to suit its goals.

Reality Manipulation SCP-3999 has the ability to manipulate reality, creating illusions and altering the physical world to suit its needs.

Telepathy SCP-3999 can read and control the thoughts of others, using this ability to manipulate and influence those around it.

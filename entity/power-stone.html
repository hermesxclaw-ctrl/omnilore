Who It Is

The Power Stone is the Infinity Stone that embodies pure, unadulterated cosmic energy — the raw force that fuels stars, drives cosmic expansion, and underlies all physical interactions in the universe. Unlike the other Stones, which channel power through specific aspects of existence (space, time, mind, soul, reality), the Power Stone simply IS power — the capacity to do anything that requires energy or force. Its wielder gains virtually limitless strength, durability, and cosmic energy projection. A being holding the Power Stone can destroy planets with a thought, shatter galaxies with a focused blast, channel energy to amplify other abilities, and survive any physical assault. The Power Stone has no subtlety — it is blunt, overwhelming, and devastating. In the Marvel Cinematic Universe, the Power Stone was concealed within the Orb on the desolate planet Morag, guarded by ancient traps. The Kyln prison's security chief Rhomann Dey remarked that the Orb's energy readings were so massive they threatened the entire Nova Corps. When Ronan the Accuser wielded it, he was able to destroy an entire fleet of Nova Corps ships with a single blast. Peter Quill (Star-Lord) barely survived touching the Stone by sharing its energy with his fellow Guardians. In the comics, the Power Gem has been wielded by Thanos, the Champion of the Universe, Drax the Destroyer, and others. It represents the most straightforward of the Infinity Stones — a pure expression of cosmic might, with no tricks, no philosophy, no subtlety. Just power. Enough power to unmake a universe and remake it in the wielder's image.

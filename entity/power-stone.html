Who It Is

In the vast machinery of the Marvel Universe, the Power Stone stands as the raw, unbridled force of creation and destruction—a cosmic battery of pure energy that can obliterate worlds or empower a mad titan's genocidal ambition. Its story is one of ancient origins, catastrophic misuse, and the ever-present temptation of absolute power.

The Power Stone is not sentient but is extremely volatile. In the MCU, it emits destructive energy that can disintegrate organic matter if handled directly. It appears to react to the wielder's strength, requiring immense power to control. In comics, it amplifies the wielder's physical abilities and energy projection.

Powers
Energy Projection

The Power Stone can project devastating beams of purple energy capable of destroying planets and starships.

Limit: Requires a powerful wielder; direct contact can destroy the user if they are not strong enough.

Physical Amplification

In comics, the Power Stone enhances the wielder's strength, stamina, and durability to superhuman levels.

Limit: The amplification is proportional to the wielder's base abilities.

Infinity Gauntlet Enhancement

When combined with the other Infinity Stones in the Infinity Gauntlet, the Power Stone amplifies their effects, allowing the wielder to achieve near-omnipotence.

Limit: The Gauntlet must be worn and requires the user's will to command the stones.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In the MCU, the Power Stone is a small, oval-shaped gem with a violet or purple hue. When exposed, it glows with intense purple energy. It is often contained within the Orb, a metallic sphere with intricate engravings. In comics, the Power Gem is a purple gem often set in a golden band or worn by Thanos on his gauntlet.

Symbols — The color purple is universally associated with the Power Stone, symbolizing royalty, power, and danger. The Orb itself is a symbol of concealment and protection, hiding the stone's immense energy.

Domains — The Power Stone is not tied to a specific realm but is often found in places of cosmic significance. In the MCU, it was located on Morag, a desolate planet, before being taken to Xandar for safekeeping. It later resided in the Infinity Gauntlet.

Accounts I
The Orbs of MoragGuardians of the Galaxy (2014)

2014

Peter Quill, a thief, steals the Orb from Morag, unaware it contains the Power Stone. Ronan the Accuser seeks it to destroy Xandar. The Guardians of the Galaxy unite to stop Ronan, who briefly wields the stone but is defeated when they use its power against him. The Guardians become a recognized team, and the stone is placed in the custody of the Nova Corps on Xandar.

Accounts II
The SnapAvengers: Infinity War (2018)

2018

Thanos acquires the Power Stone from Xandar, killing many in the process. He uses it to defeat the Asgardians and later to overpower the Hulk. He combines it with the other stones to perform the Snap, erasing half of all life. The Snap causes the Blip, a period of five years where half of all life is gone.

The HeistAvengers: Endgame (2019)

2019

The Avengers travel back in time to 2014 Morag to retrieve the Power Stone. Clint Barton successfully obtains it, but their actions create an alternate timeline where Thanos follows them to the present. The alternate Thanos attacks the Avengers' compound, leading to the final battle.

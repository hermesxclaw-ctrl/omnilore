Who It Is

Himari Uehara is the bassist who never wanted to be the leader — and ended up becoming Afterglow's most stubborn glue. While Ran Mitake writes the songs that sound like sunset on a rooftop, Himari is the one who drags everyone to practice, remembers the convenience store snacks, and panics when she thinks she's being left behind.

Attested personality: cheerful, sociable, fashion-conscious, earnest, and deeply loyal, but plagued by insecurity and comparison anxiety. Himari is Afterglow's self-appointed organizer and mood-maker — she arranges practices, brings snacks, and mediates arguments. She is the most outwardly 'normal' and girl-next-door of the five, which she worries makes her plain compared to Ran's cool talent, Moca's effortless charm, Tomoe's reliability, and Tsugumi's kindness. Powers
Bass Performance (Afterglow)

Bassist for Afterglow. Plays fingerstyle bass supporting Ran's guitar and vocals. In-universe, described as solid and rhythmic rather than flashy; she practices diligently to keep up. Gameplay: In Girls Band Party!, Himari cards are typically Score or Support type with Bass attribute; no diegetic superpower.

Limit: Self-assessed as less talented than Ran; experiences slumps when comparing herself. In live lore, voice actress Emiri Katō does not perform bass live — Afterglow's live instrumentals are provided by support musicians, unlike Poppin'Party/Roselia.

Organizational Leadership & Emotional Mediation

De facto manager/mood-maker: schedules practice, books studio time, resolves disputes, and maintains group chat energy. Her 'power' is social cohesion among five strong personalities.

Limit: Leadership often undermined by her own need for validation; can become overbearing or anxious, causing friction (e.g., forcing practice when others are tired).

Fashion and Trend Sense

In-universe skill: keen eye for fashion, makeup, and sweets spots; often advises Moca and Tsugumi on outfits. Referenced in area conversations and card episodes (e.g., 'Shopping with Himari' episodes).

Limit: Purely mundane; no combat or magical application.

Gameplay Support Ability (Meta)

As a gacha card character, Himari cards grant score boosts, life recovery, or perfect-lock skills depending on rarity. Example: 4* [You Can Do It!] Himari Uehara - Score 110% boost for 7 seconds.

Limit: Meta-mechanical only; not diegetic lore. Skills vary by card, not canonical power.

Accounts I
We Are Afterglow - Formation and Childhood PromiseBanG Dream! Girls Band Party! Afterglow Band Story Chapter 1

2017 (game release); in-universe flashback to elementary school ~2010-2013

Five childhood friends — Ran, Moca, Himari, Tomoe, and Tsugumi — who met as elementary schoolers and formed a band in junior high to stay together. Himari was the one who suggested forming a band when she feared everyone would drift apart going to different middle schools. Ran initially resisted but wrote their first song. The story retells their first live at a small festival and their vow to keep Afterglow as a place to remain themselves. Establishes Afterglow's core identity as 'childhood friends who stay natural' vs other bands' more professional aspirations. Defines Himari's foundational role as initiator.

Don't Leave Me Behind! - Himari's SlumpBanG Dream! Girls Band Party! Afterglow Band Story Chapter 2

2018 (game event)

Himari becomes convinced she is the weakest link after missing notes in practice and seeing Ran praised by an outside producer. She overcompensates by over-practicing alone and snapping at Tsugumi, then withdraws, fearing Afterglow doesn't need her. The others notice her absence and confront her on the school rooftop. Ran tells her plainly that Afterglow without Himari's bass and nagging isn't Afterglow. Himari breaks down and recommits, with Tomoe helping her fix her bass technique. Central character development for Himari; resolves leader vs supporter tension; referenced in later cards where Himari's dialogue is more self-assured.

Cultural Festival Live - Haneoka vs HanasakigawaBanG Dream! 2nd Season (Anime) Episode 7 'Himari's Leader' + Girls Band Party! Event 'Haneoka Cultural Festival!'

2019

Haneoka's cultural festival approaches. Himari volunteers Afterglow to headline, taking charge of setlist, costumes, and rehearsals to prove her worth. She clashes with Ran over song choice (Ran wants a new difficult song; Himari wants a reliable crowd-pleaser). The argument spills into practice, with Moca mediating. They compromise by arranging a new version of an old song, and the live succeeds, with Himari's MCing earning praise. Demonstrates Himari's growth as organizer and MC; reinforces theme that Afterglow's strength is honesty even when it causes friction.

Accounts II
Scarlet Sky and Afterglow's Rivalry with Pastel PalettesBanG Dream! Girls Band Party! Event Story 'Scarlet Sky' (2018) and 'Pastel Palettes vs Afterglow' Live Battle

2018

Afterglow is invited to a joint event with idol band Pastel Palettes. Himari, who admires Pastel Palettes' cuteness but also feels inferior about Afterglow's rougher image, tries to make Afterglow more 'idol-like' by suggesting choreography and matching winks. Ran rejects the idea, saying Afterglow shouldn't pretend. Himari is hurt but later understands when audience feedback praises Afterglow for staying true to themselves. She apologizes and designs new stage banter instead. Clarifies Afterglow's artistic identity; Himari learns to channel her love of cute into supporting rather than changing the band.

Live Beyond!! and the Girls Band Challenge - Afterglow's Challenge to RoseliaBanG Dream! 3rd Season Episode 5-6; Girls Band Party! Main Story Season 2 'Girls Band Challenge'

2020

All bands compete in the Girls Band Challenge at CiRCLE. Afterglow, initially reluctant to compete, enters because Himari insists they shouldn't miss a chance to play together before graduation looms. They face Roselia and RAISE A SUILEN. Himari's nerves cause a bass mistake in preliminaries, but Tomoe covers. In finals, Himari anchors the rhythm section confidently, and Afterglow places respectably, reaffirming that their goal was never winning but playing as five friends. Provides in-universe timeline progression toward third year; sets up later college vs high school split.

Afterglow's Graduation Anxiety - 'We Still Have Time'BanG Dream! Girls Band Party! Afterglow Band Story Chapter 3 'One of a Kind' (2021) and 6th Anniversary Timeline Update

2021-2023

As third year begins, Himari panics about the future: Ran talks about going pro, Tomoe considers family dojo duties, and Himari fears Afterglow will end after graduation. She secretly researches universities where all five could go together, then realizes it's unrealistic. After a heartfelt talk at Hazawa Coffee Shop, the five promise that Afterglow doesn't need to be in the same school to continue — they will keep the band as their home base regardless of paths. Bridges to franchise's 2023 decision to move ten characters to university while Afterglow remains high school third-years; explains why Himari's future remains open-ended.

Who It Is

A humiliated student who stole a relic to prove his worth, Waver Velvet stumbled into the Holy Grail War only to find himself riding alongside a king—and in the process, discovered the true measure of greatness. His journey from insecure youth to the revered Lord El-Melloi II is a tale of growth, loss, and the quiet pursuit of wisdom.

Waver starts as insecure, defensive, and desperate for recognition, often acting arrogant to mask his self-doubt. Through his experiences, he matures into a pragmatic, thoughtful, and compassionate mentor. As Lord El-Melloi II, he is known for his sharp analytical mind, dry humor, and dedication to his students, though he still carries guilt over his past.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Waver' may suggest a wavering or uncertain nature, reflecting his initial insecurity. 'Velvet' could imply softness or hidden strength. 'El-Melloi II' is a title adopted after the previous Lord El-Melloi, signifying his role as successor.

Pronunciation — WAY-ver BEL-vet (English); Weibā Berubetto (Japanese)

Aliases — Lord El-Melloi II, Zhuge Liang (Pseudo-Servant), Zhuge Kongming

Ownership — Owned by Type-Moon, created by Gen Urobuchi and Takashi Takeuchi for the Fate franchise.

Powers
Magecraft

As a mage, Waver has basic magical abilities, including reinforcement and elemental spells, but he is not particularly powerful. His strength lies in his tactical thinking and knowledge of magecraft theory.

Limit: Lacks the raw magical energy of other mages; relies on intellect.

Zhuge Liang's Abilities (Pseudo-Servant)

In Fate/Grand Order, Waver gains the powers of the legendary strategist Zhuge Liang, including the use of bagua mirrors for energy beams and elemental spells, as well as the Noble Phantasm 'Unreturning Formation' which traps enemies, and 'Chu Shi Biao' which buffs allies.

Limit: As a Pseudo-Servant, his powers are limited by the vessel; he cannot access Zhuge Liang's full divine abilities.

Strategic Genius

Waver is a brilliant tactician and teacher, able to analyze complex situations and devise effective plans. This is highlighted in Case Files where he solves magical mysteries.

Limit: His strategies can be undermined by unexpected factors or superior power.

Form & Signs

Appearance — As a teenager in Fate/Zero, Waver is a slender young man with messy black hair and sharp, often nervous eyes. He wears a casual outfit of a dark jacket and jeans. As an adult in Case Files, he has longer hair tied back, wears a suit or formal attire, and carries a cane, projecting a more authoritative and weary demeanor.

Symbols — The El-Melloi crest, Zhuge Liang's feather fan and bagua mirrors, and the Holy Grail War command seals.

Domains — The Clock Tower in London, the El-Melloi classroom, Fuyuki City during the Holy Grail War, and various locations in Case Files.

Accounts I
The Fourth Holy Grail WarFate/Zero

Early 2000s (in-universe)

Waver steals his teacher's artifact to summon Rider (Iskandar) and enters the Holy Grail War. Despite initial friction, he forms a strong bond with Rider, who teaches him about kingship and courage. In the final battle, Rider sacrifices himself to allow Waver to escape, and Archer (Gilgamesh) spares Waver in tribute to Rider's spirit. Waver survives and gains a new purpose, eventually returning to the Clock Tower and becoming a professor.

Accounts II
The Case Files of Lord El-Melloi IIThe Case Files of Lord El-Melloi II

10 years after Fate/Zero

Waver, now Lord El-Melloi II, teaches at the Clock Tower and investigates magical incidents with his assistant Gray. He confronts his past and helps students, while unraveling mysteries tied to the El-Melloi family and his own guilt. He solidifies his position as a respected professor and continues to grow as a person.

Who It Is

Twenty-third spirit, a Duke commanding 26 legions. Aim (also Aym, Haborym) appears as a handsome man with three heads: one of a serpent, one of a man, and one of a cat. He rides a viper and carries a blazing firebrand with which he sets cities, castles, and great buildings alight. Despite this destructive power, he also makes men witty and gives true answers about private matters. Aim is a spirit of controlled destruction — he can burn down civilizations but equally sharpens the intellect. His triple-headed form speaks to layered perception: serpentine cunning, human reason, and feline independence.

Who It Is

An entity that appears in rural areas as a massive white deer with antlers that branch into impossible fractal patterns. It appears before natural disasters — not to warn, but to observe. Those who encounter SCP-6448 report an overwhelming sense of insignificance, as if they are being evaluated by something that does not care about the evaluation. It does not speak. It does not move when approached. When the disaster occurs — earthquake, tornado, flood — it remains, watching, as if the destruction is the event it came to witness. Survivors report that in the aftermath, it turns and walks away, and the last thing they see is its antlers dissolving into fractal light.

Form

A white stag, approximately 2.5m at the shoulder, with a 3m antler span. The antlers are not bone but patterns of light that branch into smaller and smaller iterations, suggesting infinite recursion. Its eyes are completely white, without pupils, and reflect the disaster that is about to occur.

Powers

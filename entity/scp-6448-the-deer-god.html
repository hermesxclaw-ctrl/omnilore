SCP-6448 — The Deer God

Who It Is
Form

SCP-6448 is described as a deer-like figure with striking features, including large antlers and glowing eyes. It is said to have a majestic appearance, with a regal demeanor.

Powers

Nature Empathy SCP-6448 is said to possess the ability to communicate with and understand the natural world, including animals and plants. It is believed to be able to sense changes in the environment and predict natural disasters.

Incident Log

SCP-6448 is said to have originated from the Appalachian folklore, where it was recognized by local people as a benevolent figure connected to nature and the environment. The legend of SCP-6448 tells the story of a deer-like figure with striking features, who possesses the ability to communicate with and understand the natural world.

A group of researchers encountered SCP-6448 in the forests of the Appalachian region, where it was said to possess the ability to communicate with and understand the natural world. The encounter with SCP-6448 was described as a transformative experience, with the researchers gaining a deeper understanding of the natural world and its rhythms.

Who It Is

In the desolate region of Orre, where the sun scorches the earth and hope is a scarce commodity, a creature of immense power and sorrow awaits. Shadow Lugia, designated XD001, is not merely a Pokémon—it is a testament to the darkest ambitions of the criminal syndicate Cipher, a living weapon whose heart has been sealed away by cruel experimentation. This page delves into the legend of XD001, from its origins in the shadows of Cipher's labs to its eventual redemption, exploring the depths of its corruption and the faint glimmer of light that even the most complete darkness cannot extinguish.

As a Shadow Pokémon, XD001's heart has been artificially closed, suppressing its natural personality and emotions. It is depicted as fiercely aggressive and obedient to its master, Greevil, without any apparent free will. It fights with relentless ferocity, using powerful Shadow moves. Unlike some Shadow Pokémon that show signs of inner conflict, XD001 appears to be completely dominated by its corruption, with no visible signs of the gentle nature typical of Lugia. Appearance — Shadow Lugia retains the general body structure of a Lugia: a large, sleek, dragon-like Pokémon with a streamlined body, long neck, and broad wings. However, its coloration is drastically different. Instead of the usual silver-white, its body is a deep black, with dark purple accents on its underbelly and wing membranes. Accounts I
The Seizure of the S.S. LibraPokémon XD: Gale of Darkness (game)

Five years after Pokémon Colosseum (in-game timeline)

At the beginning of the game, Cipher uses Shadow Lugia to attack and seize the S.S. Libra, a cargo ship transporting Pokémon. This event sets the plot in motion, as the player character, Michael, later learns of the attack and becomes involved in stopping Cipher. The capture of the S.S. Libra allows Cipher to obtain a large number of Pokémon for their experiments and establishes the threat of XD001.

Accounts II
The Final Confrontation at Citadark IslePokémon XD: Gale of Darkness (game)

In-game present

Michael travels to Citadark Isle to confront Cipher's leader, Greevil. Greevil unleashes XD001 in a battle. Michael captures the Shadow Lugia and defeats Greevil, leading to Cipher's disbandment. Cipher is defeated and disbanded. XD001 is captured and later purified, disproving Cipher's claim that it was permanently corrupted.

Post-Game PurificationPokémon XD: Gale of Darkness (game)

After the main story

The player can purify XD001 by maximizing the tempo of all nine sets in the Purification Chamber. This process restores Lugia to its normal form, freeing it from Shadow corruption. Lugia is purified, and the player can keep it as a regular Pokémon.

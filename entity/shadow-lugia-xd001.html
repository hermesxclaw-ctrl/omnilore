Who It Is

A Lugia that was captured and subjected to the Shadow Pokemon process by the criminal organization Cipher. Unlike naturally born Shadow Pokemon, Shadow Lugia was created through an artificial process that closed its heart completely, turning it into a soulless weapon of immense power. Its body is dark with red, glowing markings.

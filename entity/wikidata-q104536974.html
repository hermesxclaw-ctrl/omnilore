Who It Is

In the lawless samurai country of Wano, a man who can create fire and cut through steel with a single blade fights not only for his own life but for the very soul of his homeland. Kin'emon, the 'Foxfire' samurai, is a master swordsman whose loyalty and cunning have made him a pivotal figure in the battle against the tyrannical Beast Pirates. But his true power lies not in his sword, but in his unwavering devotion to his lord and his country.

Kin'emon is fiercely loyal to his lord, Kozuki Oden, and to the samurai code. He is serious, disciplined, and often acts as a strategist. He has a short temper, especially when it comes to disrespect towards his lord. He is also a skilled actor and can be quite dramatic, often using exaggerated poses and speeches. He is deeply devoted to his wife, Tsuru, and his son, Momonosuke, whom he treats as his own.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name Kin'emon is a traditional Japanese given name, possibly meaning 'gold' (kin) and 'protect' (emon). The epithet 'Foxfire' (Kitsunebi) refers to the supernatural fire he can create.

Pronunciation — Kin-e-mon (with a hard 'K' and a short 'e')

Aliases — Foxfire Kin'emon, Kinu, Kin, Master of the Foxfire Style

Ownership — Created by Eiichiro Oda for the One Piece franchise, owned by Shueisha, Toei Animation, and associated licensors.

Powers
Foxfire Style

A swordsmanship technique that allows Kin'emon to generate and control blue flames. He can use these flames to attack, defend, or even create objects like a fire dragon.

Limit: The flames are not as powerful as Devil Fruit-based fire, and he cannot create flames without his swords.

Master Swordsman

Kin'emon is a highly skilled swordsman, capable of cutting through steel and defeating powerful enemies. He uses a two-sword style (Nitoryu).

Limit: He is not on the level of the strongest swordsmen like Mihawk or Zoro.

Clothes-Clothes Fruit (Mane Mane no Mi)

A Paramecia-type Devil Fruit that allows him to create and control clothing. He can attach leaves to people to create disguises, and he can also create armor or outfits for himself and others.

Limit: The clothing is not durable and can be removed easily. He cannot create anything other than clothing.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Kin'emon is a tall, muscular samurai with a stern face, long black hair tied in a topknot, and a prominent jaw. He wears traditional samurai armor and robes, often with a straw hat or a kabuto helmet. He carries two swords, one of which is a famous blade named Enma (though he later gives it to Zoro). He has a distinctive scar over his left eye.

Symbols — His symbol is the 'Foxfire' - a blue flame he can create and control. He also bears the crest of the Kozuki clan, a nine-tomoe symbol.

Domains — Originally from Wano Country, specifically the Kuri region. He travels extensively, including to Punk Hazard, Dressrosa, and Zou.

Main storyline of One Piece

Kin'emon is introduced as a samurai who washes ashore on Punk Hazard, separated from his lord's son, Momonosuke. He allies with the Straw Hats to rescue Momonosuke and later reveals his mission to free Wano from the rule of Kaido and Orochi. He travels to Dressrosa, where he seeks the help of the samurai Kanjuro and the ninja Raizo. He participates in the Zou arc and the Wano Country arc, where he plays a crucial role in the raid on Onigashima. He uses his Foxfire style and his tactical mind to aid the alliance. The Wano Country arc ends with the defeat of Kaido and Orochi, and the restoration of the Kozuki clan to power. Kin'emon is presumed dead but later revealed to be alive.

Who It Is
Form

SCP-4205 is a humanoid entity with a featureless, blank face. It has no discernible eyes, nose, or mouth. Its body is a mass of writhing, pulsing tendrils that seem to shift and change shape at random.

Powers

Reality Warping SCP-4205 has the ability to warp reality and change the fundamental laws of physics. It can create portals to other dimensions, manipulate time and space, and even bend the laws of causality.

Incident Log

A group of researchers from the SCP Foundation attempt to study and contain SCP-4205, but the entity proves to be highly unpredictable and difficult to manage.

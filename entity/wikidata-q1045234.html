The Approach

The name Bellamy echoes across centuries and continents—from a Norman French surname meaning 'good friend' to a river in New Hampshire, from a British aristocrat's drawing room to a pirate-infested sea in a manga universe. It is a name that has been worn by senators, singers, and fictional rebels, each bearing its own story.

Etymology

Derived from Old French 'bel ami' meaning 'good friend' or 'fair friend', introduced to England after the Norman Conquest.

Cultural Ownership

Primarily English and French; widely used as a surname and given name in English-speaking countries.

Disputed Identifications

The name is sometimes confused with 'Bel Ami', the French novel by Guy de Maupassant, but they are distinct.

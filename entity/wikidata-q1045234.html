Who It Is

The name Bellamy echoes across centuries and continents—from a Norman French surname meaning 'good friend' to a river in New Hampshire, from a British aristocrat's drawing room to a pirate-infested sea in a manga universe. It is a name that has been worn by senators, singers, and fictional rebels, each bearing its own story.

Not applicable; name itself carries connotations of friendship and amiability.

Aliases — Bellamie, Belamy, Bellamey

Accounts I
Bellamy (One Piece)One Piece manga by Eiichirō Oda

1997-present

Bellamy is a pirate captain with the Bane Bane no Mi devil fruit, giving him spring-like legs. He is a minor antagonist in the Dressrosa arc, initially mocking Luffy's dream of becoming Pirate King. Bellamy is defeated by Luffy but later becomes a more sympathetic figure.

Accounts II
Bellamy Blake (The 100)The 100 TV series

2014-2020

Bellamy Blake is a central character, initially a guard for the juvenile delinquents sent to Earth, later a leader, warrior, and protector of his people. His actions shape the series' plot, including moral dilemmas and leadership struggles.

Bellamy (film)2009 French film directed by Claude Chabrol

2009

A detective story about a police inspector investigating a murder, starring Gérard Depardieu. The film is Chabrol's last, a classic whodunit.

The Approach

In the shadowed halls of the BlazBlue universe, Relius Clover stands as one of the most enigmatic and morally complex figures—a scientist whose pursuit of knowledge transcends the boundaries of ethics and humanity. His cold intellect and ruthless experimentation have left an indelible mark on the series' lore, making him a character both feared and pitied.

Content advisory. Contains themes of psychological manipulation, human experimentation, familial abuse, and body horror. Some content may be disturbing to sensitive readers.
Identity

Relius Clover

Pronunciation REE-lee-us KLOH-ver

Etymology

The name 'Relius' may derive from Latin 'relictus' (abandoned) or 'reliquus' (remaining), possibly reflecting his detached nature. 'Clover' is an English surname, possibly symbolizing luck or the four-leaf clover, ironic given his tragic history.

Cultural Ownership

Owned by Arc System Works and created by Toshimichi Mori and the BlazBlue development team. Relius is a Japanese video game character, though his design and narrative draw on Western Gothic and steampunk influences.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans have speculated that Relius is a time-displaced version of another character, but this is unconfirmed and contradicted by official lore. No other disputes are documented.

Description

Appearance

Relius is a tall, pale-skinned man with silver hair and piercing red eyes. He wears a dark, high-collared coat with intricate gold trim, and his face is often obscured by a white mask that covers his mouth and part of his cheeks. He carries a large, ornate tome and is accompanied by his puppet, Ignis, who is a humanoid doll with a mechanical appearance. In some iterations, he appears in a more battle-ready outfit with armored gauntlets.

Symbols & Iconography

His mask, the tome, and his puppet Ignis are recurring symbols. The mask represents his detachment from humanity, the tome symbolizes his obsession with knowledge, and Ignis embodies his manipulation and control. His color scheme of black, white, and gold suggests both nobility and coldness.

Behaviour & Personality

Relius is cold, calculating, and utterly ruthless. He views emotions as weaknesses and treats people as experiments. He is highly intelligent and speaks in a calm, measured tone, often displaying a detached curiosity about suffering. He shows no remorse for his actions and is willing to sacrifice anyone, including family, for his research. Despite this, he exhibits a twisted sense of love toward his wife, but it is possessive and corrupt.

Domains & Habitats

He is often found in laboratories, underground facilities, or the boundaries between worlds. He operates in the shadows, manipulating events from behind the scenes. His connection to the Azure, the series' central artifact, ties him to the metaphysical realm of the Boundary.

Version Differences

In BlazBlue: Calamity Trigger, he is only mentioned, not seen. In Continuum Shift, he appears as a supporting antagonist with a significant role. In Chronophantasma and Central Fiction, his backstory and motivations are expanded. Spin-off games like BlazBlue: Cross Tag Battle feature him as a playable character, but his story is not central. His design remains consistent, though his combat abilities vary slightly between games.

Powers & Abilities

Puppet Mastery

Source Version All appearances

Description

Relius controls his puppet Ignis with great precision, using her for both combat and psychological manipulation. Ignis can attack independently, and Relius can use her to observe or torment others.

Limitations

Requires concentration and a direct connection to Ignis; if Ignis is destroyed, he is temporarily vulnerable.

Contradictions

None known.

Alchemy and Science

Source Version All appearances

Description

As a brilliant scientist, Relius possesses advanced knowledge of alchemy, robotics, and human experimentation. He can create complex devices, augment bodies, and manipulate souls.

Limitations

His abilities are limited by his physical form and resources; he is not superhuman without his creations.

Contradictions

None known.

Combat Skills

Source Version Gameplay in Chronophantasma and later

Description

Despite his scholarly appearance, Relius is a capable combatant, using his tome and Ignis in battle. He can summon flames and use various magical attacks.

Limitations

His combat abilities are not as powerful as top-tier fighters like Ragna or Jin, but he compensates with strategy.

Contradictions

None known.

Immortality/Resurrection

Through his experiments, Relius has achieved a form of immortality, allowing him to return from death. This is hinted at in Central Fiction.

The exact mechanism is unclear; it may require external assistance or the Azure.

Stories & Myths

The Fall of the Clover Family

Source BlazBlue: Chronophantasma and Central Fiction story modesCulture Video game lore (Japanese)Period Pre-series and during the series timeline

Summary

Relius was once a respected scientist and father to Carl and Ada. His obsession with the Azure led him to experiment on his wife, Ignis, transforming her into a puppet. His actions drove his children to tragic fates: Ada became a ghost-like entity, and Carl became a puppet master himself. Relius's experiments also created the monstrous 'Nox Nyctores' weapons and other horrors.

Consequences

The Clover family was shattered, and Relius became a major antagonist. His actions indirectly led to the creation of the Azure Grimoire and the events of the main plot.

Variants

The exact details of his experiments are revealed gradually and may be interpreted differently by players.

Source BlazBlue: Central FictionCulture Video game lorePeriod During the series

Summary

Relius seeks to obtain the Azure, the ultimate power that can rewrite reality. He manipulates events from the shadows, using his puppet Ignis to influence others. He ultimately confronts the main characters, revealing his motives and his twisted love for his wife.

Ignis (wife)

Type spouse/victimSource Chronophantasma, Central Fiction

Description

Relius loved Ignis deeply, but his obsession with the Azure led him to experiment on her, turning her into a puppet. He believes this is an act of love, but it is clearly abusive. Ignis, in her puppet form, is often used by him as a tool.

Version Note

The transformation is depicted as a tragic event, but Relius shows no remorse.

Human experimentation on family

Source Chronophantasma, Central Fiction

Description

Relius performed cruel experiments on his wife and children, including transforming his wife into a puppet and causing his daughter's death or transformation. He viewed them as subjects, not family.

Scholarly Context

This is presented as the ultimate example of his dehumanization, a common trope in Gothic fiction.

Creation of Nox Nyctores

Source Calamity Trigger lore, Continuum Shift

Description

He was involved in the creation of powerful weapons known as Nox Nyctores, which require enormous sacrifice and suffering.

Scholarly Context

These weapons are central to the series' conflict and represent the cost of power.

Psychological abuse

Source Continuum Shift

Description

Relius manipulates and torments his son Carl, using his trauma to control him.

Scholarly Context

This is a classic abusive parent-child dynamic, though in a supernatural setting.

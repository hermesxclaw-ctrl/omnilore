Who It Is

A designation applied to one specific individual on Earth at any given time — 'the person most deserving of punishment.' When the current SCP-3017 dies, the designation transfers to a new individual. The entity that enforces this — a creature made of rusted iron and regret — then begins hunting the new SCP-3017 regardless of what they have or haven't done. The designation is not based on actual moral desert; it is seemingly random. The current SCP-3017 is a kindergarten teacher who has never harmed anyone.

Form

SCP-3017-1 appears as a humanoid figure, approximately 2.5m tall, constructed from rusted iron chains, hooks, and spikes. Its 'head' is a cage containing a faintly glowing light. It moves with the sound of grinding metal and speaks in a voice like two stones scraping together: 'You know what you did.'

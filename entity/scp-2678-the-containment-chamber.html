Who It Is
Form

SCP-2678 appears as a perfectly circular hole in any surface, floor, wall, or ceiling, with no visible edges or boundaries. The hole is bottomless, with no discernible depth or bottom.

Powers

Devouring SCP-2678 is capable of devouring any object or entity that enters it, with no discernible effect on its surroundings or environment.

Incident Log

The story of SCP-2678 begins with its initial discovery in 2009, when a group of researchers stumbled upon the anomalous hole in a laboratory.

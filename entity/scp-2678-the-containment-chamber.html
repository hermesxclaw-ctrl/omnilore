Who It Is

A containment chamber containing an identical chamber, which contains another identical chamber — infinitely. After ~40 hours of observation, personnel report seeing movement in the innermost chamber: a figure looking back. Its posture matches the observer's. Theory: the figure is the observer's future self, trapped at the bottom of the recursion.

Form

A standard containment chamber, 5m x 5m, with an observation window showing an identical chamber inside. Recursion confirmed to at least 1,200 iterations.

Powers

Infinite spatial recursion

Temporal loop / future-self manifestation

Psychological compulsion to continue observing

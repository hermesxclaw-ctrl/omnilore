The Approach

In the annals of the Astrea family, one name is whispered with reverence and sorrow: Theresia van Astrea, the Sword Saint who fell in the Great Calamity, leaving a legacy that would haunt her descendants for generations.

Content advisory. Contains themes of death, sacrifice, and familial trauma.
Identity

Theresia van Astrea

Pronunciation Theh-REH-see-ah van As-TRAY-ah

Known As

Sword SaintThe Sword Saint of the Astrea Family

Etymology

The name Theresia is of Greek origin, meaning 'harvester' or 'reaper'. 'van Astrea' indicates noble lineage, with 'Astrea' possibly connected to the Greek goddess of justice, Astraea.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character from the Japanese light novel series Re:Zero - Starting Life in Another World, created by Tappei Nagatsuki.

Disputed Identifications

None known.

Description

Appearance

Theresia is depicted as a beautiful young woman with long, flowing silver hair and blue eyes. She typically wears elegant noble attire, often with a sword at her side. In flashbacks, she appears in her prime as a warrior.

Symbols & Iconography

The sword of the Sword Saint, a legendary weapon passed down in the Astrea family. The title 'Sword Saint' itself is a symbol of the strongest sword user in the kingdom.

Behaviour & Personality

Theresia is portrayed as kind, gentle, and compassionate, yet burdened by the weight of her title. She dislikes fighting and killing, preferring peaceful solutions. She is deeply devoted to her family and lover, Wilhelm.

Domains & Habitats

Primarily in the Kingdom of Lugunica, particularly in the royal capital and the Astrea family estate. She also appears in battlefields during the Great Calamity.

Version Differences

The light novel and anime portray her similarly, with minor differences in dialogue and scene timing. The web novel provides more detailed internal monologue and background.

Powers & Abilities

Sword Saint's Blessing

Source Version Light novel and anime

Description

A divine blessing that grants the wielder superhuman swordsmanship skills, making them the strongest sword user in the world. It also enhances physical abilities and grants the ability to sense and cut through almost anything.

Limitations

The blessing is passed down through the Astrea bloodline, but not all members inherit it. The wielder feels a strong compulsion to fight and protect, which can be mentally taxing.

Contradictions

None, but the exact nature of the blessing is not fully explained.

Master Swordsmanship

Source Version Light novel

Description

Even without the blessing, Theresia was a highly skilled swordswoman, trained from a young age. Her technique was refined and precise, making her a formidable opponent.

Limitations

Human limitations, though her skill was exceptional.

Contradictions

None.

Stories & Myths

The Great Calamity

Source Re:Zero light novel, Arc 5Culture FictionalPeriod Approximately 15 years before the main story

Summary

Theresia, as the Sword Saint, participated in the battle against the Witch Cult's archbishops and the great beast, the White Whale. She fought valiantly but was ultimately killed by the White Whale's mist, which erases memories of the deceased.

Participants

Theresia van Astrea

Wilhelm van Astrea

White Whale

Reinhard van Astrea (present in the battle)

Consequences

Her death devastated her husband, Wilhelm, who sought revenge on the White Whale. Her grandson, Reinhard, inherited the Sword Saint blessing, becoming the strongest knight in the kingdom.

Variants

The anime adaptation shows her death in a flashback, with some added emotional context.

Relationships

Wilhelm van Astrea

Theresia was married to Wilhelm, a swordsman who loved her deeply. Their relationship was central to her character, as she initially disliked fighting but fought to protect him.

In the anime, their relationship is depicted in flashbacks.

Death in battle

Description

Theresia's death is a tragic event, as she was killed while fighting the White Whale. Her body was never recovered, and her memory was erased from the world due to the White Whale's ability.

Etymology

The designation 'SCP-096' follows the Foundation's standard cataloging system: 'SCP' stands for 'Special Containment Procedures', and '096' is the sequential item number. The colloquial alias 'The Shy Guy' derives from its documented behavior of hiding its face when not actively pursuing a target, a stark contrast to its violent response when its face is observed.

Cultural Ownership

SCP Foundation (collaborative internet fiction project). The character was created by an anonymous author and first posted on the SCP Wiki. It is a shared-world creation, not owned by a single entity, and is released under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 license.

Disputed Identifications

There is no real-world mythological counterpart; SCP-096 is a purely modern internet horror creation. It has been compared to various 'weeping angel' or 'if you see it, it kills you' tropes, but it is a distinct, original figure.

Archive Dossier — Approach

Imagine a creature so shy it hides its face from the world, yet so dangerous that a single glimpse of its visage turns it into an unstoppable, screaming engine of destruction. It is a paradox of terror: a being that wants nothing more than to be left alone, but is hardwired to slaughter anyone who dares to look. This is SCP-096, the 'Shy Guy,' a modern internet-age bogeyman that has transcended its text-based origins to become a global symbol of the uncanny.

Who It Is

SCP-096 is a tall, emaciated humanoid creature standing approximately 2.38 meters tall with unnaturally long, thin arms and legs. Its skin is pale, almost albino white, and completely hairless. When in a docile state, SCP-096 sits in the corner of its containment cell, facing the wall, with its long arms wrapped around its bent knees, making soft, continuous whimpering or crying sounds. This behavior gave it the nickname 'The Shy Guy.' The entity's anomaly triggers when any sentient being views its face, whether directly, through a photograph, through video footage, or even through a four-pixel rendering. Once SCP-096's face has been viewed, the entity enters an extreme state of emotional distress, covering its face with its hands, screaming and crying uncontrollably for approximately one to two minutes. After this period, SCP-096 will begin running directly toward the viewer. No known barrier or distance has ever stopped it. The entity is capable of traversing any terrain, crossing oceans, and bypassing any physical obstacle with extreme speed and strength. Upon reaching the person who viewed its face, SCP-096 invariably kills them, then returns to its docile state. During its rage state, SCP-096 is virtually indestructible; its skeletal structure is impervious to all known weapons, and it can regenerate tissue damage. One documented incident (096-1-A) resulted in ██ casualties after an unknowing civilian discovered a photograph of SCP-096's face. Attempts to terminate SCP-096 have all failed, including exposure to extreme temperatures, corrosive substances, high explosives, and physical dismemberment. The entity was first discovered in the wild after an amateur mountaineer photographed it on an expedition near Mount ████████. The Foundation currently maintains strict SCRAMBLE gear protocols, requiring all visual data containing SCP-096's face to be intercepted and destroyed. SCP-096 is one of the most iconic and feared entities in the Foundation canon, a tragedy of involuntary violence driven by an entity that seems to genuinely suffer during its rampages.

Archive Dossier — Description

Appearance

SCP-096 is a humanoid creature, approximately 2.38 meters tall, with a gaunt, emaciated build. Its skin is pale, almost white, and appears to be devoid of any pigmentation. Its most distinctive feature is its face, which is described as 'distorted' with a wide, unnatural grin and deep-set, empty eyes. The creature is typically seen covering its face with its hands, a behavior that has earned it the 'Shy Guy' moniker. Blood and mucus are often present on its hands and face, suggesting a history of self-inflicted damage or distress.

Behavior

SCP-096 is normally docile and non-threatening, spending most of its time in a state of near-catatonia, often hiding its face. However, when any person sees its face—whether directly, through a photograph, a video, or even a drawing—SCP-096 enters a state of extreme agitation. It will begin to cry, scream, and claw at its own face, before launching into a relentless pursuit of the observer. The creature is incredibly fast and strong, capable of tearing through reinforced steel and concrete to reach its target. Once the observer is killed (and only the observer), SCP-096 will calm down, sit beside the corpse, and resume its docile state, seemingly indifferent to any other stimuli.

Domains

SCP-096 is currently contained at Site-19, a Foundation facility. Its containment chamber is a sealed steel box, filled with water and designed to prevent any accidental visual contact. The creature is classified as Euclid, indicating that its behavior is not fully predictable and requires ongoing monitoring.

Symbols

The most recognizable symbol associated with SCP-096 is its own face, which is often used as a warning icon in Foundation documentation and fan art. The image of the creature covering its face has become an iconic representation of the SCP Foundation's body of work.

Archive Dossier — Powers

Triggered Rage

Source Version Original SCP-096 entry

Description

When any being sees SCP-096's face, it enters a state of uncontrollable fury and will stop at nothing to kill the observer. The trigger can be a direct view, a photograph, a video, or even a detailed drawing.

Limitations

The trigger is visual only; hearing a description or reading about it does not provoke a response. The creature will only pursue the specific individual who saw its face, ignoring all other distractions.

Contradictions

Some later fan works have depicted the trigger as being more broad (e.g., seeing any image of it), but the original entry is clear that it is the face that is the trigger.

Superhuman Speed and Strength

Source Version Original SCP-096 entry

Description

SCP-096 can run at speeds that make it appear as a blur, and its strength allows it to break through steel-reinforced walls and doors. It has been known to travel miles in a matter of minutes to reach its target.

Limitations

Its speed and strength are only fully realized during its rage state. When calm, it is slow and lethargic.

Contradictions

None documented.

Indestructibility

Source Version Original SCP-096 entry

Description

SCP-096 has shown extreme resilience to physical damage. Gunfire, explosives, and even direct hits from heavy weapons have failed to stop it. Its bones and flesh are incredibly dense, and it appears to be effectively immortal in the sense that it cannot be killed by conventional means.

Limitations

It can be contained and incapacitated, but not killed. Its regeneration, if any, is not fully understood.

Contradictions

None documented.

SCP Foundation

Type captorSource SCP Foundation Wiki - SCP-096 entry

Description

The Foundation is the organization that contains SCP-096. They study it, maintain its containment, and are constantly at risk of triggering its rage. The relationship is one of mutual, uneasy coexistence.

Version Note

This is the core relationship in the SCP universe.

None.

SCP-682 (The Hard-to-Destroy Reptile)

Type cross-test subject / adversarySource SCP Foundation Wiki - SCP-682 Experiment Log T-98816-OC108/682

Description

One of the most famous incidents in SCP Foundation lore involves a cross-test between SCP-096 and SCP-682—the Foundation's most resilient and hostile entity. When SCP-096 was introduced to SCP-682 and the reptile saw its face, SCP-096 entered its rage state and attacked. The resulting confrontation was catastrophic: SCP-096 tore repeatedly at SCP-682, which regenerated and counter-attacked, leading to an extended stalemate of mutual destruction. The incident is notable because it is one of the very few documented cases where SCP-096 failed to immediately kill its target, and SCP-682—which typically mocks and dominates other entities—was reportedly traumatized by the encounter, refusing to engage with SCP-096 again. This cross-test is a cornerstone of SCP fan discussion regarding the relative power levels of Foundation entities.

Version Note

This is a canonical cross-test recorded in Foundation documentation.

Conflicting Accounts

The exact outcome of the encounter is subject to fan interpretation and varies across different tales and continuities.

Dr. Dan ███████ (SCP-096 Researcher)

Type primary researcherSource SCP Foundation Wiki - SCP-096 entry addenda

Description

The Foundation researcher assigned to SCP-096's study bears significant psychological scars from his work with the entity. In a notable incident recorded in the entry's addenda, the researcher intentionally exposed an image of SCP-096's face to a condemned D-class personnel in order to track the entity's response, a morally fraught decision that reflects the ethical compromises inherent in Foundation containment protocols.

Version Note

The researcher's full name is redacted in Foundation documentation.

Conflicting Accounts

Fan theories about the researcher's identity and ultimate fate vary across the extended canon.

Who It Is

SCP-096 is a tall, emaciated humanoid creature standing approximately 2.38 meters tall with unnaturally long, thin arms and legs. Its skin is pale, almost albino white, and completely hairless. When in a docile state, SCP-096 sits in the corner of its containment cell, facing the wall, with its long arms wrapped around its bent knees, making soft, continuous whimpering or crying sounds. This behavior gave it the nickname 'The Shy Guy.' The entity's anomaly triggers when any sentient being views its face, whether directly, through a photograph, through video footage, or even through a four-pixel rendering. Once SCP-096's face has been viewed, the entity enters an extreme state of emotional distress, covering its face with its hands, screaming and crying uncontrollably for approximately one to two minutes. After this period, SCP-096 will begin running directly toward the viewer. No known barrier or distance has ever stopped it. The entity is capable of traversing any terrain, crossing oceans, and bypassing any physical obstacle with extreme speed and strength. Upon reaching the person who viewed its face, SCP-096 invariably kills them, then returns to its docile state. During its rage state, SCP-096 is virtually indestructible; its skeletal structure is impervious to all known weapons, and it can regenerate tissue damage. One documented incident (096-1-A) resulted in ██ casualties after an unknowing civilian discovered a photograph of SCP-096's face. Attempts to terminate SCP-096 have all failed, including exposure to extreme temperatures, corrosive substances, high explosives, and physical dismemberment. The entity was first discovered in the wild after an amateur mountaineer photographed it on an expedition near Mount ████████. The Foundation currently maintains strict SCRAMBLE gear protocols, requiring all visual data containing SCP-096's face to be intercepted and destroyed. SCP-096 is one of the most iconic and feared entities in the Foundation canon, a tragedy of involuntary violence driven by an entity that seems to genuinely suffer during its rampages.

Form

SCP-096 is a humanoid creature, approximately 2.38 meters tall, with a gaunt, emaciated build. Its skin is pale, almost white, and appears to be devoid of any pigmentation. Its most distinctive feature is its face, which is described as 'distorted' with a wide, unnatural grin and deep-set, empty eyes. The creature is typically seen covering its face with its hands, a behavior that has earned it the 'Shy Guy' moniker. Blood and mucus are often present on its hands and face, suggesting a history of self-inflicted damage or distress.

Powers

Triggered Rage When any being sees SCP-096's face, it enters a state of uncontrollable fury and will stop at nothing to kill the observer. The trigger can be a direct view, a photograph, a video, or even a detailed drawing. Original SCP-096 entry The trigger is visual only; hearing a description or reading about it does not provoke a response. The creature will only pursue the specific individual who saw its face, ignoring all other distractions. Some later fan works have depicted the trigger as being more broad (e.g., seeing any image of it), but the original entry is clear that it is the face that is the trigger.

Superhuman Speed and Strength SCP-096 can run at speeds that make it appear as a blur, and its strength allows it to break through steel-reinforced walls and doors. It has been known to travel miles in a matter of minutes to reach its target. Original SCP-096 entry Its speed and strength are only fully realized during its rage state. When calm, it is slow and lethargic. None documented.

Indestructibility SCP-096 has shown extreme resilience to physical damage. Gunfire, explosives, and even direct hits from heavy weapons have failed to stop it. Its bones and flesh are incredibly dense, and it appears to be effectively immortal in the sense that it cannot be killed by conventional means. Original SCP-096 entry It can be contained and incapacitated, but not killed. Its regeneration, if any, is not fully understood. None documented.

Behavior & Habitat

SCP-096 is normally docile and non-threatening, spending most of its time in a state of near-catatonia, often hiding its face. However, when any person sees its face—whether directly, through a photograph, a video, or even a drawing—SCP-096 enters a state of extreme agitation. It will begin to cry, scream, and claw at its own face, before launching into a relentless pursuit of the observer. The creature is incredibly fast and strong, capable of tearing through reinforced steel and concrete to reach its target. Once the observer is killed (and only the observer), SCP-096 will calm down, sit beside the corpse, and resume its docile state, seemingly indifferent to any other stimuli.

Incident Log

The Foundation first became aware of SCP-096 after a series of mysterious deaths in a rural area. Witnesses reported seeing a tall, pale figure before the victims were found brutally killed. A Foundation recovery team was dispatched and successfully captured the entity, though the operation resulted in several casualties.

During a routine testing procedure, a researcher inadvertently triggered SCP-096's rage by showing it a photograph that contained a glimpse of its face. The creature broke out of its containment cell and killed the researcher, then proceeded to hunt down and kill everyone who had seen the photograph. This incident led to the implementation of the 'no images' policy for the entity.

In an attempt to find a method of terminating the notoriously indestructible SCP-682, the Foundation authorized a cross-test with SCP-096. The procedure involved exposing SCP-682 to an image of SCP-096's face. Upon seeing the image, SCP-096 breached containment, traveled to SCP-682's holding cell, and engaged the reptile in a prolonged, devastating confrontation. Despite SCP-096's incredible destructive power, SCP-682's regeneration allowed it to survive. The engagement ended inconclusively, with both entities severely damaged and SCP-682 displaying uncharacteristic distress. The test was deemed a failure for termination purposes but provided invaluable data on both entities' capabilities. It also cemented SCP-096's reputation as one of the few entities capable of genuinely threatening SCP-682.

Field Notes

█ SCP-096 is one of the most popular and recognizable SCPs, often appearing in 'top 10' lists and fan art.

█ The character's design, with its long arms and gaunt frame, has been compared to the Slender Man, another internet horror icon, though their origins and behaviors are distinct.

█ The SCP-096 entry is often cited as a prime example of the 'creepypasta' genre's power to create compelling, original monsters.

█ The creature's behavior is sometimes described as a metaphor for anxiety or extreme social phobia, taken to a monstrous extreme.

█ In the SCP: Containment Breach game, encountering SCP-096 is a terrifying test of your ability to avoid looking at its face, often leading to frantic, panicked gameplay.

Scholarly Debates
The nature of its face
Its face is simply 'distorted' and unsettling, but not inherently supernatural. ⇌ Its face is so terrifying that it causes a psychological break in anyone who sees it, which is why it must kill them.
Evidence for A: The original entry focuses on the physical reaction (rage) rather than a psychological effect.
Evidence for B: Some fan interpretations and games have suggested that seeing its face causes madness.
The extent of its indestructibility
It is completely invulnerable to all forms of damage. ⇌ It is extremely durable but could potentially be destroyed by an exceptionally powerful force (e.g., SCP-682).
Evidence for A: The original entry states that it has survived all damage attempts.
Evidence for B: Cross-testing with other SCPs is a common fan trope, and some tales depict 096 being harmed.
What We Don't Know

The original author of the SCP-096 entry is unknown (anonymous). The exact date of the original post is approximate. There is limited scholarly analysis of the character; most reception is within fan communities and internet culture. The full extent of its abilities and weaknesses is intentionally left vague in the source material, allowing for fan interpretation.

A way to hold it in your mind

Think of SCP-096 as the ultimate embodiment of the 'if looks could kill' cliché, taken to its most literal and horrifying extreme. It is a being of pure, involuntary reaction. It is not evil in a malicious sense; it is a force of nature, a biological imperative that cannot be reasoned with, bargained with, or stopped. It is the fear of being seen, and the fear of seeing something you shouldn't, all rolled into one lanky, screaming nightmare.

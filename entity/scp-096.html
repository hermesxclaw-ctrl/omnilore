Who It Is

Imagine a creature so shy it hides its face from the world, yet so dangerous that a single glimpse of its visage turns it into an unstoppable, screaming engine of destruction. It is a paradox of terror: a being that wants nothing more than to be left alone, but is hardwired to slaughter anyone who dares to look. This is SCP-096, the 'Shy Guy,' a modern internet-age bogeyman that has transcended its text-based origins to become a global symbol of the uncanny.

SCP-096 is normally docile and non-threatening, spending most of its time in a state of near-catatonia, often hiding its face. However, when any person sees its face—whether directly, through a photograph, a video, or even a drawing—SCP-096 enters a state of extreme agitation. It will begin to cry, scream, and claw at its own face, before launching into a relentless pursuit of the observer. The creature is incredibly fast and strong, capable of tearing through reinforced steel and concrete to reach its target. Powers
Triggered Rage

When any being sees SCP-096's face, it enters a state of uncontrollable fury and will stop at nothing to kill the observer. The trigger can be a direct view, a photograph, a video, or even a detailed drawing.

Limit: The trigger is visual only; hearing a description or reading about it does not provoke a response. The creature will only pursue the specific individual who saw its face, ignoring all other distractions.

Superhuman Speed and Strength

SCP-096 can run at speeds that make it appear as a blur, and its strength allows it to break through steel-reinforced walls and doors. It has been known to travel miles in a matter of minutes to reach its target.

Limit: Its speed and strength are only fully realized during its rage state. When calm, it is slow and lethargic.

Indestructibility

SCP-096 has shown extreme resilience to physical damage. Gunfire, explosives, and even direct hits from heavy weapons have failed to stop it. Its bones and flesh are incredibly dense, and it appears to be effectively immortal in the sense that it cannot be killed by conventional means.

Limit: It can be contained and incapacitated, but not killed. Its regeneration, if any, is not fully understood.

Form & Signs

Appearance — SCP-096 is a humanoid creature, approximately 2.38 meters tall, with a gaunt, emaciated build. Its skin is pale, almost white, and appears to be devoid of any pigmentation. Its most distinctive feature is its face, which is described as 'distorted' with a wide, unnatural grin and deep-set, empty eyes. Symbols — The most recognizable symbol associated with SCP-096 is its own face, which is often used as a warning icon in Foundation documentation and fan art. The image of the creature covering its face has become an iconic representation of the SCP Foundation's body of work.

Domains — SCP-096 is currently contained at Site-19, a Foundation facility. Its containment chamber is a sealed steel box, filled with water and designed to prevent any accidental visual contact. The creature is classified as Euclid, indicating that its behavior is not fully predictable and requires ongoing monitoring.

Accounts I
The Initial ContainmentSCP Foundation Wiki - SCP-096 entry

2012 (in-universe: early 21st century)

The Foundation first became aware of SCP-096 after a series of mysterious deaths in a rural area. Witnesses reported seeing a tall, pale figure before the victims were found brutally killed. A Foundation recovery team was dispatched and successfully captured the entity, though the operation resulted in several casualties. SCP-096 was brought to Site-19 and placed in a specially designed containment chamber. The Foundation implemented strict protocols to ensure no personnel would accidentally see its face.

Accounts II
The Camera IncidentSCP Foundation Wiki - SCP-096 entry

In-universe: shortly after initial containment

During a routine testing procedure, a researcher inadvertently triggered SCP-096's rage by showing it a photograph that contained a glimpse of its face. The creature broke out of its containment cell and killed the researcher, then proceeded to hunt down and kill everyone who had seen the photograph. This incident led to the implementation of the 'no images' policy for the entity. The Foundation learned the full extent of SCP-096's trigger and response. The containment protocols were revised to include strict rules about visual media.

The 096-682 Cross-Test IncidentSCP Foundation Wiki - Experiment Log T-98816-OC108/682

In-universe: post-containment testing phase

In an attempt to find a method of terminating the notoriously indestructible SCP-682, the Foundation authorized a cross-test with SCP-096. The procedure involved exposing SCP-682 to an image of SCP-096's face. Upon seeing the image, SCP-096 breached containment, traveled to SCP-682's holding cell, and engaged the reptile in a prolonged, devastating confrontation. Despite SCP-096's incredible destructive power, SCP-682's regeneration allowed it to survive. The engagement ended inconclusively, with both entities severely damaged and SCP-682 displaying uncharacteristic distress. The test was deemed a failure for termination purposes but provided invaluable data on both entities' capabilities. It also cemented SCP-096's reputation as one of the few entities capable of genuinely threatening SCP-682. The cross-test established limits on both entities: SCP-096 could not permanently kill SCP-682, but SCP-682 could not simply ignore or overpower SCP-096. The event became one of the most frequently referenced and debated incidents in SCP Foundation lore.

SCP-096 will kill you if you look at a picture of it. — It will kill the person who saw the picture. The trigger is the act of seeing its face, and the target is the specific observer. Simply having a picture on your phone is not a death sentence unless you look at it.

SCP-096 is a 'weeping angel' clone. — While it shares the 'don't blink' or 'don't look' trope, SCP-096 is a distinct creation. Weeping angels send you back in time; SCP-096 tears you apart. The mechanics and consequences are entirely different.

SCP-096 is a demon or a ghost. — The SCP Foundation universe treats it as an anomalous entity, not a supernatural spirit in the traditional sense. It is a biological anomaly that can be contained, studied, and potentially terminated (though unsuccessfully so far).

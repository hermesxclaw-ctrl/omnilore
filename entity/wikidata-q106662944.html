Who It Is

Asbel Lhant is a young lord who runs from his past, only to be pulled back by a promise he made to a girl who died and came back. His story is a coming-of-age tale about duty, friendship, and the courage to protect what matters most — even if it means defying a king and carrying a monster's soul inside him.

Asbel is headstrong, impulsive, and determined to protect his friends and family. He often acts before thinking, but his sincerity and willingness to take responsibility for his actions earn him the trust of others. He struggles with the expectations placed on him as a lord and a knight, but ultimately learns to forge his own path. He is fiercely loyal and will go to great lengths to keep his promises.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Asbel' may be derived from the Hebrew name 'Asbel' (or 'Ashbel'), meaning 'fire of Bel' or 'man of God', though this is speculative. The surname 'Lhant' is unique to the game and has no known real-world origin.

Pronunciation — AZ-bell LANT

Aliases — アスベル・ラント (Asuberu Ranto)

Ownership — Japanese video game character created by Namco Tales Studio for the Tales series. The character is owned by Bandai Namco Entertainment.

Powers
Swordsmanship

Asbel is a skilled swordsman, trained at the Barona Knight Academy. He uses a variety of sword-based artes in battle.

Limit: Requires physical stamina and CC (Chain Capacity) to perform artes.

Assault Artes

Predetermined combo attacks that Asbel can perform in battle. These are his basic offensive skills.

Limit: Consumes CC; cannot be used indefinitely without recharging.

Burst Artes

Powerful special moves that can be mapped to specific inputs. These are more advanced than assault artes and often have additional effects.

Limit: Consumes more CC than assault artes; requires proper timing and positioning.

Eleth Gauge

A shared gauge that, when filled, grants unlimited CC and resistance to stunning. Asbel can contribute to filling this gauge through his actions.

Limit: Gauge resets after battle; requires consistent combat performance to fill.

Absorption of Lambda

Asbel absorbs the entity Lambda into his body, gaining the ability to suppress Lambda's power and eventually use it to absorb Fodra's consciousness in the after-story. This is a unique ability granted by his strong will and bond with Sophie.

Limit: This is a narrative ability, not a gameplay mechanic. It causes Asbel great physical and emotional strain, and he must constantly keep Lambda in check.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Asbel is a young man with short, spiky brown hair and green eyes. He wears a white and green knight's uniform with a red cape, which he later replaces with a more lordly attire in the after-story. His design emphasizes his dual role as a knight and a lord.

Symbols — Asbel is associated with the Lhant family crest, a stylized lion. His weapon is a sword, and he is often depicted with a sword in official art.

Domains — Asbel's primary home is Lhant, a village in the Kingdom of Windor. He also spends time in the capital city of Barona, where he trains as a knight, and travels across the world of Ephinea during his adventures.

Accounts I
The Prologue: A Promise to SophieTales of Graces (all versions)

Childhood segment (Age 9)

Asbel, a young boy from the village of Lhant, befriends an amnesiac girl named Sophie. Together with his brother Hubert, his friend Cheria, and Prince Richard, they form a close bond. After saving Richard from an assassination attempt, Asbel and Sophie secretly travel to the capital. They discover Richard unconscious and are attacked by a powerful monster. Sophie sacrifices herself to save them, and Asbel is left devastated. Sophie dies, Hubert is adopted into the Oswell family, and Asbel runs away to become a knight to atone for his perceived failure.

Accounts II
The Main Story: Reunion and ConflictTales of Graces (all versions)

Seven years after prologue (Age 17)

Asbel returns to Lhant after learning of his father's death, only to find Sophie alive but with amnesia. He reunites with his friends and discovers a conspiracy involving the kingdoms of Windor, Stratha, and Fendel. Richard, now king, becomes possessed by the entity Lambda and seeks to absorb the planet's core. Asbel and his party confront Richard, and Asbel ultimately absorbs Lambda into himself to save him, convincing Lambda to give humanity a chance. Richard is freed from Lambda's control, Lambda sleeps within Asbel, and Asbel becomes the Lord of Lhant.

After-story: Lineage and LegaciesTales of Graces f and Remastered

One year after the main story

A monster epidemic forces Asbel and his friends to investigate. They discover that Fodra's core has become sentient and seeks revenge on humanity. The party defeats the 'Little Queens' and have Lambda absorb Fodra's consciousness, returning it to a deep sleep. Asbel and Cheria marry and adopt Sophie as their daughter. Asbel settles down, marries Cheria, and becomes a father figure to Sophie. The world is saved from the monster epidemic.

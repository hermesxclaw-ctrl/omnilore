Who It Is

In the world of BlazBlue, where magic and machines blur, Carl Clover stands as a prodigy of the Art of Ars Magus, a boy whose genius is shadowed by a tragic past and a relentless quest to save his sister. With his puppet Nirvana, he is both a master of combat and a haunted soul, his every move a testament to a will that refuses to break.

Carl is exceptionally intelligent and composed for his age, often speaking in a calm, measured tone. He is fiercely protective of his sister Ada, and his actions are driven by a desire to save her. He can be ruthless and manipulative, showing little empathy for others when they stand in his way. His relationship with Nirvana is complex; he treats it as a tool but also as a vessel for his sister's soul.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — 'Carl' is a Germanic name meaning 'man' or 'free man', while 'Clover' is an English surname referring to the plant. The combination may symbolize luck or the idea of a 'free man' bound by fate.

Pronunciation — KARL KLOH-ver

Aliases — Carl Clover

Ownership — Owned by Arc System Works, creators of BlazBlue. The character is part of a Japanese fighting game franchise with global reach.

Powers
Ars Magus

Carl is a prodigy in the Art of Ars Magus, a form of magic that uses magical energy to create weapons and effects. He is particularly skilled in manipulating his puppet Nirvana, which he controls with great precision.

Limit: Requires concentration and magical energy. Overuse can exhaust him.

Puppet Mastery

Carl can control Nirvana from a distance, making it fight independently. He can also use Nirvana to perform complex maneuvers, such as throwing it or using it as a shield.

Limit: If Nirvana is destroyed or disabled, Carl is vulnerable. He must maintain focus to control it effectively.

Nirvana's Abilities

Nirvana is a powerful puppet with superhuman strength and durability. It can be used to deliver devastating blows, and it has a special attack called 'Nirvana's Requiem' that can trap opponents.

Limit: Nirvana's abilities are tied to Carl's control, and it cannot act independently for long.

Combat Skills

Despite his young age, Carl is a skilled combatant, able to hold his own against trained fighters. He uses a combination of his own attacks and Nirvana's to overwhelm opponents.

Limit: His physical strength is limited compared to adult fighters, so he relies on strategy and Nirvana.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Carl is a young boy with short blond hair and blue eyes. He wears a dark blue coat with gold trim, a white shirt, and a red tie. He often carries a large, sentient puppet named Nirvana, which resembles a tall, armored figure. In later games, his design is refined but retains his youthful, aristocratic look.

Symbols — His puppet Nirvana is a key symbol, representing his lost sister and his obsession. The Clover family crest may appear on his clothing, symbolizing his lineage.

Domains — Carl is often found in the city of Kagutsuchi, the central hub of BlazBlue's world. He operates in the shadows, using his skills as an Ars Magus to influence events. His home is the Clover estate, which he has largely abandoned in his quest.

Accounts I
The Clover TragedyBlazBlue: Continuum Shift

Fictional timeline

Carl and his sister Ada were the children of a wealthy family. Their parents were involved in dangerous magical experiments. One day, their parents disappeared, and Ada was abducted by a mysterious organization. Carl, left alone, dedicated himself to finding and saving his sister, using his inherited talents in Ars Magus. Carl becomes a lone operative, seeking any lead that might help him find Ada. He eventually learns that Ada's soul is trapped inside the puppet Nirvana, and he becomes obsessed with freeing her.

Accounts II
Carl's Journey in the Story ModesBlazBlue: Calamity Trigger, Continuum Shift, Chronophantasma, Central Fiction

Fictional timeline

In each game, Carl's story mode follows his search for Ada and his encounters with other characters. He often clashes with the main heroes, but also helps them when their goals align. He is manipulated by the villain Relius Clover, who is revealed to be his father, and who has been experimenting on Ada. Carl learns the truth about his father's experiments and the nature of Nirvana. He ultimately chooses to protect his sister's memory and move forward, though the pain remains.

Alter Memory AnimeBlazBlue: Alter Memory

2013

The anime adapts the events of the first two games, including Carl's introduction and his conflict with Relius. His role is condensed but faithful to the games. Carl's character arc is presented in a more streamlined fashion, focusing on his relationship with Nirvana.

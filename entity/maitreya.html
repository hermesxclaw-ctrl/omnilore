Who It Is
Form

Maitreya is often depicted as a handsome, radiant being with a warm and loving smile. He is typically shown wearing a crown or a headband, and is surrounded by a halo of light. In some depictions, he is shown with a lotus flower in his hand, symbolizing his connection to the spiritual realm.

Powers

Loving-Kindness Maitreya is said to possess the power of loving-kindness, which allows him to radiate warmth and compassion to all beings.

Accounts

In this story, the Buddha prophesies the coming of Maitreya, who will be the future Buddha of this world. Maitreya is said to possess great wisdom and spiritual power, and is called upon to guide and protect his followers.

In this story, Maitreya is said to be born in the future, in a realm beyond the physical world. He is said to possess great wisdom and spiritual power, and is called upon to guide and protect his followers.

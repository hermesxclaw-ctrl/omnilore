Who It Is

Maitreya is a fascinating figure in Buddhist tradition, and his story has captivated the imagination of many.

Maitreya is often depicted as a compassionate and loving being, who is dedicated to helping others achieve enlightenment. He is said to possess great wisdom and spiritual power, and is often called upon to guide and protect his followers.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Maitreya is often depicted as a handsome, radiant being with a warm and loving smile. He is typically shown wearing a crown or a headband, and is surrounded by a halo of light. In some depictions, he is shown with a lotus flower in his hand, symbolizing his connection to the spiritual realm.

Symbols — The lotus flower, the crown or headband, and the halo of light are all symbols associated with Maitreya's identity and spiritual significance.

Domains — Maitreya is said to inhabit a realm beyond the physical world, where he is surrounded by a community of enlightened beings.

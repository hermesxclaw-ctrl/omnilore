Who It Is
Form

Ganon is a massive and malevolent porcine monster, with a humanoid Gerudo form known as Ganondorf. He is often depicted as a large, muscular man with a pig-like snout and a distinctive beard. In his porcine form, he is a massive, hulking creature with a thick, scaly hide and sharp teeth.

Powers

Dark Magic Ganon has the ability to wield powerful dark magic, which he uses to attack his enemies and achieve his goals.

Accounts

Ganon is the main antagonist of the game, and is responsible for the downfall of the kingdom of Hyrule. He is defeated by the protagonist, Link, in a final battle.

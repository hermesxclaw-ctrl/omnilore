Form & Signs

Appearance — The Yule Man is typically depicted as a bearded, elderly man dressed in a red or green coat, often with a sack of toys or gifts. He is said to be a jolly and benevolent figure, but with a darker side.

Symbols — The Yule Man is often associated with symbols of Christmas, such as holly, mistletoe, and evergreen trees. He is also linked to the color red, which represents life, energy, and vitality.

Domains — The Yule Man is said to inhabit the realm of Christmas, where he is the primary figure. He is also associated with the winter season, particularly the months of December and January.

Who It Is

A humanoid entity that manifests during the 12 nights of Christmas (December 21 - January 2) at isolated family homes in northern latitudes. SCP-4666 targets families with children under 12. It kills the parents, then abducts children to its extradimensional workshop, where it forces them to build toys from human remains before eventually killing and incorporating them into its creations. Survivors are rare and deeply traumatized.

Form

An impossibly tall (approximately 2.9 meters), emaciated humanoid with corpse-white skin stretched over an elongated frame. It wears a tattered, bloodstained red coat reminiscent of Santa Claus iconography. Its face is a frozen rictus grin, and its fingers are long enough to wrap entirely around a human skull.

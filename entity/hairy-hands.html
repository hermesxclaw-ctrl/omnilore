Who It Is

The Hairy Hands legend is a fascinating example of how folklore can be used to explain technological vulnerability.

The Hairy Hands are said to be a malevolent entity that causes vehicles to crash or lose control, often resulting in accidents or fatalities.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Hairy Hands' is derived from the reported physical manifestation of the entity, which is said to be a pair of ghostly hands grasping the steering wheel of a vehicle.

Pronunciation — HAIR-ee HANDS

Aliases — The Hands on the Wheel, The Phantom Hands of Dartmoor

Ownership — The Hairy Hands is a cultural artifact of the British folklore, specifically associated with the Dartmoor region in Devon, England.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Hairy Hands are said to be a pair of ghostly hands grasping the steering wheel of a vehicle, often accompanied by a feeling of intense cold or a sense of being possessed by an unseen force.

Symbols — The Hairy Hands are often associated with the symbol of the steering wheel, which is said to be a representation of the entity's control over the vehicle.

Domains — The Hairy Hands are said to inhabit the roads and highways of the Dartmoor region in Devon, England, particularly on the A386 road.

20th century

The Hairy Hands are a ghost story/legend that built up about a stretch of road on a remote area of Dartmoor in the English county of Devon, which was purported to have seen an unusually high number of motor vehicle accidents during the early 20th century. accidents, fatalities

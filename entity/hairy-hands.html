Who It Is
Form

The Hairy Hands are said to be a pair of ghostly hands grasping the steering wheel of a vehicle, often accompanied by a feeling of intense cold or a sense of being possessed by an unseen force.

Powers

Vehicle Control The Hairy Hands are said to have the ability to control and manipulate vehicles, causing them to crash or lose control.

Accounts

The Hairy Hands are a ghost story/legend that built up about a stretch of road on a remote area of Dartmoor in the English county of Devon, which was purported to have seen an unusually high number of motor vehicle accidents during the early 20th century.

Within the Mythlok framework, the Hairy Hands of Dartmoor are understood as a folklore response to technological vulnerability. They are not bound to place, identity, or history, but to the moment control is lost.

Who It Is

SCP-914 is a large clockwork machine consisting of an enormous main body with intricate gears, belts, pulleys, and other mechanical components, connected to a control panel with five settings: Rough, Coarse, 1:1, Fine, and Very Fine. The machine has two compartments — an 'Input' booth and an 'Output' booth. When an object is placed in the input booth and the machine is activated with a selected setting, SCP-914 'refines' or 'processes' the object, producing a transformed version in the output booth. The nature of the transformation depends on the setting selected. The 'Rough' setting dismantles or destroys objects into simpler components — a handgun becomes individual springs and metal pieces. The 'Coarse' setting degrades items — a clock might become a simple weight-driven timer. The '1:1' setting produces an equivalent but different version — a key for one lock becomes a key for a completely different lock. 'Fine' improves or enhances objects — a rusty pocketknife becomes a high-quality surgical scalpel. The 'Very Fine' setting produces extreme and often unpredictable results — a human subject placed through Very Fine emerged as a 'perfected' being with enhanced physiology but complete amnesia and personality replacement. Conversely, other Very Fine tests on organic matter have produced nightmarish, deformed abominations. SCP-914 was recovered from a workshop belonging to an unknown inventor. Blueprints recovered suggest the machine was never completed according to its original design, and its current functionality may be entirely accidental. The Foundation maintains strict testing protocols and has catalogued thousands of experiments in the 'Experiment Log 914' — one of the most extensive and entertaining test logs in the SCP Wiki. SCP-914 is particularly beloved by the community because its mechanics invite creative speculation: what happens when you put X through setting Y? The machine is classified as Safe not because it is harmless, but because it is predictable enough to be used without active containment breach concerns — assuming proper protocols are followed. SCP-914 remains one of the Foundation's most useful and simultaneously most dangerous research tools.

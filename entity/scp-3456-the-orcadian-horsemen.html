Who It Is

A phenomenon affecting the Orkney Islands, Scotland: four spectral horsemen (SCP-3456-1 through -4) who manifest during storms and ride across the islands, trampling everything in their path. They are the ghosts of Nuckelavee-like entities — skinless horse-human hybrids from Orcadian folklore. Unlike the mythological Nuckelavee, SCP-3456 instances are organized, purposeful, and appear to be searching for something specific buried somewhere in the islands. Their search is intensifying.

Form

Four immense figures resembling flayed horses merged with human torsos, standing approximately 3 meters at the shoulder. No skin — raw muscle and sinew visible. A single, enormous eye in the center of the horse-skull. Their hooves strike sparks from the ground that ignite into pale blue fire.

The Orcadian Horsemen are a fascinating and complex anomalous entity, with a rich history and a wide range of abilities and characteristics.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Orcadian Horsemen are a group of quadrupeds, described as being similar to horses, but with an otherworldly appearance. They are said to be tall, with a muscular build and a coat that seems to shift between different colors.

Symbols — The Horsemen are associated with the sea and the island of Orkney. They are said to be connected to the spirits of the sea, and are often depicted with symbols of the sea, such as shells and seaweed.

Domains — The Horsemen are said to inhabit the island of Orkney, and are often associated with the sea and the coast. They are able to move through the water with ease, and are said to be able to survive in a variety of different environments.

Who It Is

Gargantuan, worm-like monsters hundreds of feet long that burrow through the earth, leaving immense tunnels. They are related to or are the same as the Bholes of the Dreamlands. Their bodies are covered in a viscous, caustic slime, and their vast circular mouths are ringed with teeth. Few things can stop a dhole.

Form

Unimaginably vast worm, 100-1,000+ ft long, 20-40 ft in diameter, segmented body, pale white or gray, caustic slime coating, massive circular maw ringed with rows of teeth, no visible eyes

Powers

Massive tunneling

Caustic slime secretion

Crushing through any material

Sense of vibration

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

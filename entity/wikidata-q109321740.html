Who It Is

He is screaming, crying, and trying to run away — and then his eyes close, his breathing steadies, and the air cracks like a thunderclap. Zenitsu Agatsuma is Demon Slayer's most terrified swordsman and its most lethal lightning strike, a boy whose courage only arrives when consciousness leaves.

Attested: Extremely anxious, pessimistic, self-deprecating, and vocally cowardly. Constantly declares he will die, begs for help, clings to stronger companions (especially Tanjiro and Nezuko). Prone to panic attacks, crying, and fainting. Simultaneously deeply loyal, kind, and idealistic: fixated on finding a kind wife, respectful to elders (Jigoro), and fiercely protective when triggered. Possesses acute hearing that manifests as social sensitivity — he can hear others' emotional states, lies, and demon sounds. Gotouge has not provided an official in-universe etymology; the name is often noted by Japanese fans for its iron

Powers
Thunder Breathing (Kaminari no Kokyu)

One of the five main Breathing Styles derived from Sun Breathing. Zenitsu was taught all six forms but could only master First Form. Style emphasizes leg strength, explosive dashes, and lightning-like speed with thunder sound effects.

Limit: High physical strain on legs and lungs; requires Total Concentration Breathing; initially only First Form usable while conscious; other forms unattainable for years.

First Form: Thunderclap and Flash (Ichi no Kata: Hekireki Issen)

High-speed dash that mimics lightning strike, unsheathing and slashing in one motion. Zenitsu's signature. Sixfold and Eightfold variations increase dash repetitions. Godspeed (Shinsoku) is extreme speed variant.

Limit: Linear trajectory, requires sheathed start, leaves user vulnerable if missed; Godspeed burns legs quickly (two uses limit noted vs Kaigaku).

Seventh Form: Honoikazuchi no Kami (Flaming Thunder God)

Original form created by Zenitsu himself, combining extreme speed with a flaming dragon-shaped lightning slash. Used to kill Kaigaku. Indicates he surpassed single-form limit.

Limit: Single-use desperation technique; requires full resolve and awake consciousness; not repeated in final battle vs Muzan.

Enhanced Hearing (Chokaku)

Supernatural auditory acuity equal to Tanjiro's smell and Inosuke's touch. Can hear heartbeats, breathing, emotional tremors, demon presence through walls, and distinguish lies/truth. Allows tracking without sight.

Limit: Overwhelmed by loud environments; causes anxiety from hearing others' negative thoughts; not as useful in silent Infinity Castle where demons mask sound.

Unconscious Combat / Sleep Fighting

When extreme fear causes him to faint or fall asleep, Zenitsu's body continues fighting with perfect Thunder Breathing, guided by hearing and muscle memory. Personality becomes calm and competent.

Limit: Cannot consciously control while asleep; vulnerable if woken abruptly; initially cannot access power while awake, leading to dependency. Later overcomes this.

Total Concentration Breathing and Physical Enhancement

Standard Demon Slayer enhancement: increased strength, speed, stamina, recovery via breathing. Zenitsu shows exceptional leg power and endurance despite small frame.

Limit: Lower overall stamina than Hashira; injured severely vs Spider Demon and vs Kaigaku; requires rest at Butterfly Mansion.

Nichirin Sword (Yellow Blade)

Yellow Nichirin katana forged by swordsmiths, said to be lightning-attuned. Blade turns yellow upon wielding, reflecting Thunder Breathing affinity.

Limit: Blade chips easily if not maintained; lost/broken and replaced via Haganezuka's village; not a Bright Red Blade until late (no personal reddening).

Form & Signs

Appearance — Zenitsu is a teenage boy of average height and slight build, age 16 at introduction, 17 by Infinity Castle arc. His most distinctive trait is his hair: originally black, turned yellow-orange at the tips after being struck by lightning during training (manga ch. 45, fanbook). The gradient is depicted as sharp in manga and softer in anime. He has large, downturned honey-brown eyes often drawn with tears, and thick eyebrows. His Demon Slayer uniform

Symbols — Lightning/thunder motif throughout: haori triangles, Nichirin blade color (yellow), breathing name Kaminari no Kokyu (Thunder Breathing), sound effect 'pika' and thunderclap visuals. Sparrow Chuntaro (Chuntaro / Ukogi) as messenger bird instead of crow — symbol of his perceived low rank and comic status; sparrows in Japan connote smallness and chatter. Peach and plum? No. Color yellow consistently codes cowardice and brightness in Japan

Domains — Human Taisho-era Japan (1910s). Domains: Demon Slayer Corps barracks and missions (forests, mountains, towns, entertainment district, infinity castle), Jigoro Kuwajima's mountain dojo where he trained, Butterfly Mansion (recovery). Not associated with a specific territorial domain like a Hashira; operates as low-rank Mizunoto/Kanoto slayer who travels mission-to-mission. Accounts I
Debt, Lightning, and Jigoro's DojoManga ch. 45 flashback; Fanbook 1; Anime E17 intercut

2016-2019 publication; story set c. 1913-1915

Orphaned and swindled by a woman he loved who left him with crushing debt, Zenitsu attempts suicide by hanging and is saved by Jigoro Kuwajima, former Thunder Hashira. Jigoro pays his debt and trains him alongside senior disciple Kaigaku. Zenitsu is struck by lightning during training, bleaching his hair, but masters only First Form while Kaigaku masters others. Jigoro believes in him despite his whining, telling him to master one thing to perfection. Establishes Zenitsu's inferiority complex, debt trauma, and master-disciple bond that drives his later vengeance and creation of Seventh Form. Jigoro's faith becomes thematic core.

Final Selection and Joining TanjiroManga ch. 6-7; Anime E4-E6

2016 publication; story 1914

Zenitsu passes Final Selection on Mt. Fujikasane (off-panel) and is assigned sparrow Chuntaro instead of a crow due to low rank. He first meets Tanjiro on the road, begging a girl to marry him, then clings to Tanjiro after hearing Nezuko's demon presence and Tanjiro's kindness. He accompanies Tanjiro to Tsuzumi Mansion. Forms core trio with Tanjiro and later Inosuke; establishes his hearing ability and comic-coward dynamic; begins obsessive devotion to Nezuko.

Tsuzumi Mansion - First ThunderclapManga ch. 11-13; Anime E11-E12

2016-2019

Inside the demon Kyogai's shifting drum mansion, Zenitsu is separated with Shoichi and Teruko. Terrified and asleep after being slapped, his unconscious self executes Thunderclap and Flash Sixfold to behead the demon's tongue monster, saving the children. Tanjiro witnesses his power for first time. Reveals his true strength and sleep-fighting gimmick; earns Tanjiro's respect; establishes that his cowardice masks competence.

Natagumo Mountain and Butterfly Mansion RecoveryManga ch. 28-33, 40-45; Anime E17-E20, E22-E24

2017 publication; anime 2019

On Mt. Natagumo against the Spider Family, Zenitsu is poisoned by the spider demon son and, while unconscious, uses Thunderclap and Flash to kill him, but is near death. He recovers at Butterfly Mansion under Aoi and Kanao, training with Tanjiro and Inosuke. He reveals his training past and his hearing-based worldview. Shows limits of his poison resistance; deepens bonds via rehab training; sets up his inferiority vs Hashira.

Mugen Train - Sleeping GuardianManga ch. 54-66; Anime Film Mugen Train (2020); TV E27-E33

2018 publication; film 2020

Zenitsu boards the Mugen Train with Tanjiro, Nezuko, Inosuke and Flame Hashira Kyojuro Rengoku. Put to sleep by Enmu's dream spell, he dreams of Nezuko as his wife and resists waking. When awake, he fights Enmu's flesh tentacles while asleep, protecting Nezuko's box. He mourns Rengoku's death alongside Tanjiro after Akaza kills Rengoku. Demonstrates loyalty even in dreams; trauma of Rengoku's death hardens his resolve; no major power growth but emotional maturation.

Entertainment District - Comic Relief and AbsenceManga ch. 70-97; Anime S2 Entertainment District Arc (2021-2022)

2019 publication

Disguised as a girl to infiltrate Yoshiwara with Tanjiro and Inosuke to support Sound Hashira Tengen Uzui, Zenitsu is kidnapped early by Warabihime (Daki) and spends most of the arc trapped, screaming. He is freed late and, while asleep, helps fight Daki and Gyutaro's obi. His role is smaller, emphasizing his vulnerability against Upper Rank Six. Shows gap between his level and Upper Ranks at this stage; reinforces need for Hashira support; comedic cross-dressing becomes iconic fan image.

Infinity Castle - Thunder Breathing Succession Duel vs KaigakuManga ch. 143-146; Anime Infinity Castle Part 1 (2025 film)

2019-2020 publication; story climax

After learning that Kaigaku became Upper Rank Six and that Jigoro committed seppuku in atonement, a transformed Zenitsu — stoic, hair tied, fully awake — confronts Kaigaku alone in the Infinity Castle. Kaigaku mocks him as trash who only knew First Form. Zenitsu reveals he forged Seventh Form: Honoikazuchi no Kami to prove Jigoro's teaching was not in vain, and beheads Kaigaku despite grievous wounds. He falls unconscious and is saved by Yushiro's assistance. Pivotal character climax: Zenitsu fights conscious, avenges master, resolves inferiority, and proves worthiness as Thunder Breathing successor. Near-fatal injuries remove him from final Muzan fight temporarily.

Final Battle vs Muzan and Epilogue MarriageManga ch. 184-205; Anime Infinity Castle / Sunrise Countdown films (2025-2026)

2020 publication

Recovered, Zenitsu rejoins Tanjiro and Inosuke for the dawn battle against Muzan Kibutsuji, using Thunderclap and Flash: Godspeed to protect allies and buy time until sunrise. After Muzan's defeat and the Corps' disbandment, the epilogue (ch. 205) shows Zenitsu has married Nezuko Kamado (now human) and their descendants Yoshiteru and Toko Agatsuma appear in modern Tokyo, with Yoshiteru resembling Zenitsu. Completes arc from marriage-obsessed coward to husband; provides generational closure; confirms his bloodline survives. Descendants' existence implies happy domestic life.

Zenitsu is just a useless comic relief who never improves. — While comic relief early, he has a complete arc: masters First Form to perfection, creates an original Seventh Form, and defeats an Upper Rank alone while awake — one of few non-Hashira to kill an Upper Rank solo.

Zenitsu's Thunder Breathing creates real lightning that electrocutes demons. — Breathing Styles are swordsmanship with visual metaphors; no literal elemental generation per official fanbook. Lightning is artistic effect for speed and sound, not magic electricity.

Zenitsu is the Thunder Hashira. — He never becomes a Hashira in story; the Thunder Hashira title belonged to Jigoro (former) and was vacant. Zenitsu is successor-level but Corps disbands before any promotion.

Zenitsu's sparrow is a failed crow. — Chuntaro is a Kasugai sparrow, intentionally assigned to lower-rank or eccentric slayers; not a failure but a different species. Sparrows are valid messengers, just rarer.

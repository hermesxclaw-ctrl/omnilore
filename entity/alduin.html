Who It Is
Form

Alduin is often depicted as a massive, serpent-like dragon with a fiery breath and a regal demeanor. In some interpretations, he is shown as a humanoid figure with dragon-like features. His appearance is often associated with the elements of fire and destruction.

Powers

Time Manipulation Alduin is said to have the power to manipulate time and space, creating and destroying worlds as he sees fit.

Elemental Control Alduin is often associated with the elements of fire and destruction, and is said to have the power to control these forces with ease.

Regeneration Alduin is said to have the power of regeneration, allowing him to heal from even the most grievous wounds.

Accounts

Alduin is said to have created the world by destroying the previous world in a great firestorm.

Alduin is said to be the harbinger of the apocalypse, bringing great change and destruction to the people of Skyrim.

Alduin is said to have a pivotal role in the creation and destruction of the world, and is often associated with the elements of fire and destruction.

Who It Is

The World-Eater, Nordic God of Destruction, and first-born of Akatosh. Alduin is the primary antagonist of Skyrim, a massive black dragon with crimson wing membranes whose purpose is to end the current Kalpa (world cycle) by consuming it. He chose to rule rather than destroy, enslaving humanity until defeated by ancient Nordic heroes using an Elder Scroll. He returned in the Fourth Era to reassert his dominion.

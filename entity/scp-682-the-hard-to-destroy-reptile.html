Who It Is

A massive, highly intelligent reptile with an extreme hatred of all life. SCP-682 is the Foundation's most persistent containment challenge — it adapts to survive any termination attempt. It has survived acid baths, nuclear explosions, reality restructuring, and being thrown into the sun only to return. SCP-682 communicates verbally with contempt for human civilization and has been known to mock Foundation personnel. Its origin is linked to the Scarlet King and the 'Leviathan' entity.

Form

SCP-682 is a large, reptilian creature of grotesque and shifting appearance. It is generally described as being roughly the size of a small car, though it has shown the ability to grow or shrink. Its body is covered in thick, black, scale-like plates that are nearly impervious to damage. It possesses a powerful jaw filled with razor-sharp teeth, and four legs, though the number of limbs has been observed to change after regeneration. Its eyes are described as intelligent and hateful, betraying a high level of cognition. The creature constantly regenerates from any damage, and its form can become more monstrous and adapted to the threat it faces.

Regenerative Adaptation SCP-682 can regenerate from nearly any form of damage, including dismemberment, incineration, and complete atomization. More than simple healing, it adapts to the cause of its injury, becoming resistant or immune to it. For example, after being dissolved in acid, its skin became resistant to acid. Canonical SCP-682 article The regeneration is not instantaneous and requires time and mass. The creature can be temporarily incapacitated or reduced to a small, regenerating mass. The adaptation is reactive, not proactive. The limits of its regeneration are unclear. Some termination attempts have reduced it to a near-molecular level, but it has always returned. Whether it can be truly destroyed is the central question of its existence.

Enhanced Physiology SCP-682 possesses immense strength, speed, and durability. Its claws and teeth can tear through reinforced steel, and its hide is resistant to most conventional weaponry, including small-arms fire and explosives. Canonical SCP-682 article It can be physically restrained by sufficiently strong containment measures, though it will eventually break free. None noted.

High-Level Intelligence SCP-682 is not a mere beast. It is highly intelligent, capable of understanding human language, planning complex escapes, and even communicating, though it usually expresses only hatred. It has demonstrated an understanding of technology and tactics. Canonical SCP-682 article It is often unwilling to cooperate or communicate in a meaningful way, making its full cognitive capabilities a mystery. Some researchers believe its intelligence is purely tactical, while others believe it possesses a deeper, more profound awareness.

Adaptive Evolution When exposed to a specific threat, SCP-682 can evolve new biological features to counter it. This includes growing additional limbs, developing resistance to toxins or radiation, or even developing new sensory organs. This is the most dangerous of its abilities, as it makes it nearly impossible to kill with a single, novel method. Canonical SCP-682 article and Termination Logs The evolution is a response to a threat, not a pre-emptive ability. It requires time to adapt. The extent to which it can adapt is unknown. Some tests suggest it can adapt to anything, while others show it can be overwhelmed by multiple, simultaneous threats.

Behavior & Habitat

SCP-682 is characterized by an intense, all-consuming hatred for all life, particularly humans. It is highly intelligent, capable of complex thought, communication (though it often refuses to), and strategic planning. It is relentlessly hostile and will attempt to escape containment and kill any living thing it encounters. It is also extremely adaptable, evolving its physical form to counteract specific threats. While it appears to be driven by pure malice, some logs suggest a deep-seated pain or a desire to be left alone, though this is a matter of interpretation.

Accounts

The Foundation captures SCP-682 after a series of attacks. The creature is found to be highly aggressive and nearly indestructible. The O5 Council orders that SCP-682 be terminated, initiating a series of termination attempts that all fail. The creature's hatred for humanity is made clear, and containment is decided as the only viable option, not termination, is the only viable option.

A series of formal attempts to terminate SCP-682, each more extreme than the last. These include exposure to other SCPs, such as SCP-173, SCP-096, and SCP-409, as well as nuclear weapons and reality-bending anomalies. Each attempt fails, often resulting in SCP-682 becoming more powerful or more hateful.

A series of tales that probe the possible origins of SCP-682. Some suggest it is a creature from a previous universe, others that it was created by a now-extinct alien race, and still others that it is a piece of a larger, more terrifying entity. These tales often contradict each other, adding to the mystery.

Field Notes

☘ SCP-682 is one of the first and most famous SCPs ever created, predating many of the wiki's most iconic entries.

☘ The creature's ability to adapt and regenerate has led to it being informally called 'the cockroach of the SCP universe'.

☘ SCP-682 has a particular hatred for SCP-173, and the two have been pitted against each other in several termination attempts, with 682 usually coming out on top.

☘ The acid used in its containment chamber is so strong that it would dissolve most other SCPs, but 682 simply regenerates from the damage it causes.

☘ Despite its constant hostility, SCP-682 has occasionally communicated with researchers, usually to express its hatred or to taunt them with cryptic warnings.

Scholarly Debates
True Origin
SCP-682 is an ancient, extradimensional entity, perhaps a fragment of a god or a cosmic horror. ⇌ SCP-682 is a biological experiment gone wrong, created by a precursor race or even the Foundation itself.
Evidence for A: Tales like 'SCP-682's History' and its incredible adaptability suggest a non-terrestrial origin.
Evidence for B: The creature's biological nature and its ability to be affected by other SCPs suggest it is a physical, albeit anomalous
True Limits of Regeneration
SCP-682 is truly unkillable, and any attempt to destroy it is futile. ⇌ SCP-682 has a limit, and a sufficiently powerful or reality-altering attack could destroy it.
Evidence for A: The failure of every termination attempt, including exposure to reality-bending SCPs.
Evidence for B: The existence of other SCPs with reality-warping powers that could theoretically erase it from existence.
Intelligence and Motivation
SCP-682 is a purely malevolent, hateful creature that wants to destroy all life. ⇌ SCP-682 is a deeply traumatized and suffering creature, lashing out at its tormentors.
Evidence for A: Its constant, unprovoked attacks and its stated hatred for humanity.
Evidence for B: Some of its interactions and the pain it seems to experience suggest a more complex emotional state.
What We Don't Know

The primary sources are the SCP Foundation wiki itself, which is a collaborative and ever-changing work. There is no 'official' canon, and the creature's history and abilities are defined by a vast body of fan-written content. This research is based on the main article, termination logs, and a selection of popular tales. A complete analysis of every tale, cross-test, and fan interpretation would be a monumental task. The in-universe details of the creature's origin, its true limits, and its ultimate fate remain unknown by design.

A way to hold it in your mind

Think of SCP-682 as the ultimate test for the SCP Foundation's core principle: 'Secure, Contain, Protect.' It is the entity that they cannot secure, cannot contain (permanently), and cannot protect anyone from. It is a walking, breathing, regenerating argument against the very idea of control. It's the final boss of the Foundation's own hubris, a living reminder that some things are simply beyond human understanding or power.

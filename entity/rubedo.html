Who It Is

The fourth and final stage of the alchemical magnum opus: the 'reddening.' After passing through the black of nigredo, the white of albedo, and the yellow of citrinitas, the matter achieves rubedo — the color of blood, the sun, and gold. This is the completion of the Great Work: the Philosopher's Stone in its final, perfected form, capable of turning lead into gold and producing the Elixir of Life. Symbolically, rubedo represents the hieros gamos (sacred marriage) of opposites — the Red King and White Queen united in the coniunctio — producing the filius philosophorum (the philosophical child, the reborn self). In Jungian psychology, it is the attainment of the Self: wholeness, individuation, and the union of conscious and unconscious.

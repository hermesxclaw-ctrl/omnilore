Who It Is
Form

The Licker is a humanoid creature with a long, serpentine tongue and a distinctive, humanoid body. It is often depicted as a pale, corpse-like creature with a grotesque, inhuman appearance.

Powers

Regeneration The Licker has the ability to regenerate its body parts, including its tongue, which it uses to attack and devour its victims.

Accounts

The Licker is a bio-organic weapon created by the Umbrella Corporation as part of their secret experiments in the field of biotechnology.

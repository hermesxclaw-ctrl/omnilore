Who It Is

In the sprawling, dimension-hopping chaos of the Risk of Rain universe, few figures are as enigmatic as MUL-T. A hulking, multi-purpose combat robot built for heavy lifting and heavier firepower, MUL-T embodies versatility and raw mechanical might. Yet beneath its cold steel chassis lies a question that haunts every encounter: is it a mindless tool, a loyal companion, or something more? As you pilot this walking arsenal through alien worlds, you become part of its unfinished story—a story written in salvaged parts and endless battle.

MUL-T has no dialogue or narrative personality in-game. Its behavior is defined by its combat capabilities: it is a sturdy, reliable, and versatile fighter. It is often characterized by players as a 'jack-of-all-trades' due to its ability to carry two equipment items and its balanced stats. In the game's lore, MUL-T is implied to be a repurposed or salvaged robot, but no explicit personality is given.

Powers
Scrap Launcher

MUL-T fires a slow-moving projectile that explodes on impact, dealing area damage.

Limit: Projectile has travel time and can be dodged; requires ammo management.

Rebar Puncher

MUL-T punches with a rebar-like arm, dealing high single-target damage and knocking back enemies.

Limit: Short range; requires close proximity to enemies.

Transport Mode

MUL-T charges forward, damaging and stunning enemies in its path. While charging, MUL-T is invulnerable to some attacks.

Limit: Limited control during charge; can be stopped by walls.

Power Mode

MUL-T plants itself, gaining increased damage and attack speed but becoming immobile.

Limit: MUL-T cannot move while active; vulnerable to ranged enemies.

Dual Equipment

MUL-T can carry two equipment items, allowing for two active abilities. This is a passive trait.

Limit: Equipment cooldowns are shared or separate depending on the item; some items may not synergize.

Form & Signs

Appearance — MUL-T is a large, humanoid robot with a bulky, industrial design. It has a boxy torso, thick arms, and a single optic sensor on its head. Its body is composed of grey and dark metal panels, with glowing blue accents. It carries a large backpack that houses its secondary equipment. In Risk of Rain 2, MUL-T's model is more detailed, with visible gears, pistons, and a distinct 'MUL-T' label on its chest.

Symbols — MUL-T's iconography is dominated by its industrial, utilitarian aesthetic. The blue glow of its optic and accents suggests a technological, non-hostile origin. The 'MUL-T' label reinforces its function as a mass-produced unit.

Domains — MUL-T exists in the world of Risk of Rain, which features procedurally generated alien planets, space stations, and otherworldly dimensions. As a playable survivor, it can appear in any environment the game offers.

Accounts I
MUL-T's In-Game LoreRisk of Rain 2 Logbook entry

2019-present

The Logbook entry for MUL-T reads: 'A multi-purpose combat robot. Carries a heavy arsenal, but has no special abilities. It seems to have been repurposed from a mining or construction unit.' This suggests MUL-T was originally designed for industrial work, later modified for combat. No further narrative is provided. None in-game; MUL-T's backstory is minimal and does not affect gameplay.

Accounts II
Fan InterpretationsCommunity forums, Reddit

2019-present

Fans have speculated that MUL-T may have been created by the UES Contact Light, the ship from Risk of Rain, or by the fictional company 'Hopoo Industries' (a reference to the developers). Some fan theories connect MUL-T to the game's overarching narrative of a doomed space expedition. None; these are unofficial.

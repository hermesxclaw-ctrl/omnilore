Who It Is

In the final months of the One Year War, Zeon pilots whispered that a red flash on the monitor meant you had seconds to live — not Char Aznable's famous comet, but a second Crimson Lightning, Johnny Ridden, whose high-mobility Zaku moved like a duelist's blade and then vanished without a body at A Baoa Qu.

MSV original profile: terse, professional, 'cool-headed' ace; loyal to Kycilia Zabi's forces; respected by subordinates. MSV-R expands: stoic, duty-bound, introspective, dislikes ostentation, values comradeship over glory, uncomfortable with propaganda. Depicted as honorable soldier who questions Zeon's war aims privately but continues to fight from loyalty and responsibility to his corps. Name & Tongue

Etymology — Japanese rendering ジョニー・ライデン (Jonī Raiden). 'Johnny' is an English given name; 'Ridden' is not a standard English surname and appears to be a stylized coinage for MSV. No official etymology provided by Sunrise/Bandai. The nickname Shinku no Inazuma (Crimson Lightning) describes the red color scheme

Pronunciation — Japanese: jo-nī rai-den /dʒɒni ˈraɪdən/ in English approximations. Official English materials use 'Johnny Ridden'.

Aliases — Crimson Lightning, Shinku no Inazuma, 真紅の稲妻, Johnny Raiden

Ownership — Intellectual property of Sotsu Co., Ltd. and Sunrise Inc. (now Bandai Namco Filmworks), created as part of the Mobile Suit Variation project supervised by Kunio Okawara and Bandai

Powers
Ace Mobile Suit Piloting (High-Mobility Type Specialist)

Exceptional piloting skill in high-thrust, high-mobility mobile suits, particularly MS-06R-1A/ R-2 Zaku II High Mobility Type and MS-14B/JG Gelgoog variants. MSV profile credits 185+ MS kills (and 6 warships), placing him among Zeon's top aces. Style emphasizes high-speed hit-and-run, vernier-assisted lateral movement, and precision shooting.

Limit: No Newtype abilities attested; purely Oldtype skill. Performance dependent on tuned high-mobility machines; standard Zaku performance would be lower. Kill count is in-universe propaganda/profile claim, not objectively verified and disputed as inflated MSV marketing.

Squadron Leadership - Chimera Corps

Leads/ serves as core member of Chimera Corps, coordinating other aces (Thomas Kurtz, Gerald Sakai, Ingrid 0's opponents). Tactical command of small elite team rather than large fleet.

Limit: Not a strategic general; authority limited to corps level under Kycilia Zabi. Loyalty to Kycilia limits political maneuverability.

Custom Machine Tuning Affinity

Ability to extract maximum performance from custom-tuned units; technicians note his machines require precise thruster balance that only he can fully exploit. In lore, his MS-06R-2 has 30% higher thrust than standard Zaku II.

Limit: High fuel consumption and maintenance burden; machines are prototypes with limited spare parts. Not transferable to mass-production units.

Reputation / Morale Effect (Crimson Lightning Myth)

Presence on battlefield boosts Zeon morale and intimidates Federation pilots; post-war, his name is used as symbol by Zeon remnants. In MSV-R, multiple pilots claim to be Ridden, showing mythic weight.

Limit: Myth also makes him target; Federation aces seek him out. Impersonators dilute legacy.

Accounts I
Enlistment and Early One Year War ServiceMSV profile text and MSV-R vol.1 flashbacks

UC 0079 (in-universe); published 1983-2010

Born in Side 3 (Zeon), Johnny Ridden enlists in Zeon armed forces and shows early aptitude in mobile suit training. Deployed after outbreak of One Year War, he flies standard MS-06 Zaku II before being selected for high-mobility testing. Early sorties include space patrol and escort for Kycilia's fleet. MSV-R adds detail: he is initially reluctant hero, motivated by defense of homeland rather than Spacenoid supremacy ideology, and is scouted by Kycilia's staff for the elite corps. Selection for MS-06R development program and assignment to elite unit; establishes his red custom machine identity.

The Crimson Lightning in the High Mobility ProgramMSV Collection File: MS-06R-1A / R-2 entries; Entertainment Bible 39

UC 0079 mid-war; published 1983-1984

As part of Zeon's Pezun and Granada high-mobility development, Ridden receives the MS-06R-2 Zaku II High Mobility Type, painted in his personal crimson. The unit's enhanced thrusters and leg verniers allow unprecedented acceleration. MSV text credits him with rapid ace status, downing Federation GMs and Balls in Solomon and surrounding sectors. His duels emphasize speed, earning the nickname Shinku no Inazuma as Federation pilots report a red streak that vanishes before counterattack. Cements his status as one of Zeon's top five aces; justifies continued production of high-mobility variants despite cost.

Service in the Chimera Corps under Kycilia ZabiMSV-R: Johnny Ridden no Kikan vols. 2-10

UC 0079 late war (Oct-Dec 0079); published 2010-2014

In final months, Kycilia Zabi consolidates remaining aces into the Chimera Corps (Kimera Tai) to counter Federation's growing GM numbers. Ridden serves alongside fellow aces Thomas Kurtz, Gerald Sakai, May Kauwin and others, often as moral center. Missions include defense of supply lines, interception of Federation fleets, and protection of Kycilia's headquarters. Manga depicts internal tensions: aces debate Zeon's use of colony drops and the deteriorating war situation, with Ridden quietly disapproving of Gihren's extremism while remaining loyal to his comrades. Corps becomes symbol of Zeon elite resistance; Ridden's leadership humanizes Zeon ace archetype beyond Char.

Accounts II
Defense of A Baoa Qu and Disappearance (MIA)MSV profile 'Missing in Action at A Baoa Qu' + MSV-R vols. 11-14

UC 0079 Dec 31; published 1984 / 2015

During the Battle of A Baoa Qu, the final battle of the One Year War, Ridden sorties in his MS-14B Gelgoog High Mobility Type (or MS-06R-2 in some retellings) to defend the fortress. MSV's original one-line fate: 'Missing in action during the Battle of A Baoa Qu, presumed dead.' MSV-R dramatizes the battle: communications break down, his unit is seen charging Federation forces to cover retreating comrades, then disappears in explosion/debris. No wreckage or body is recovered. Federation records list him as KIA; Zeon remnants claim he survived. Creates central mystery driving MSV-R's post-war plot; leaves canonicity of survival open, allowing Gunpla and game appearances to continue.

The Return of Johnny Ridden - Post-War Search (UC 0090)MSV-R: Johnny Ridden no Kikan framing story (UC 0090)

In-universe UC 0090; published 2010-2023

Ten years after the war, Federation Intelligence officer Ingrid 0 (female pilot with prosthetic leg, assigned to investigate Zeon remnant propaganda) and former Chimera Corps member Yvonne follow rumors that Johnny Ridden is alive and leading Zeon remnants. Various impersonators appear wearing crimson machines, including Federation test pilot 'Red Zaku' incidents and Axis Zeon claimants. Ingrid, who develops complex respect for Ridden's legend, uncovers that the 'return' is partly psychological warfare and partly genuine loyalty of soldiers who refuse to believe he died. The manga intercuts wartime flashbacks with present investigation, never conclusively showing the 'real' Ridden alive, preserving ambiguity. Recontextualizes Ridden as mythic figure whose absence shapes post-war identity; expands Gundam's theme of how ace legends are constructed.

Gunpla and Game Legacy - The Ace Beyond CanonBandai Hobby catalogs; G Generation, Encounters in Space, Gundam Versus

1983-2024 (meta)

Outside narrative, Ridden's story continues as product line: his red Zaku and Gelgoog are reissued as Master Grade, High Grade, and Robot Spirits figures, often as Premium Bandai exclusives. In games, he appears as unlockable ace with high stats and unique lines, allowing players to pit him against Amuro Ray or Char. This meta-story shows how a character with no anime screen time achieved enduring popularity through plastic models and stats, essentially living on as Gunpla. Demonstrates MSV's success in expanding Gundam universe via side-material; Ridden becomes template for later 'MSV ace' characters.

Johnny Ridden is a main anime character like Char or Amuro and appears in the 1979 Mobile Suit Gundam series. — He never appears in the original TV anime or films. He is a print/model-kit original from MSV (1983) and manga MSV-R. His fame comes from Gunpla and games, not television.

His red Zaku is the same as Char's red Zaku. — Different shades and types: Char's is MS-06S Zaku II Commander Type (pinkish red, horned), Ridden's is MS-06R-2 High Mobility Type (deeper crimson, high-thrust legs, no horn). Performance and role differ.

Johnny Ridden is a Newtype like Char and Amuro. — No source attributes Newtype abilities to him; he is explicitly an Oldtype ace whose skill is technical and experiential. MSV-R emphasizes this contrast.

MSV-R proves he definitely survived A Baoa Qu. — MSV-R deliberately leaves survival ambiguous, using impersonators and unreliable rumors. No chapter conclusively shows the real Ridden alive post-war.

Who It Is

A smartphone application that, once installed, causes the user to see a tall, canine-skulled humanoid entity in their peripheral vision through photographs and eventually in real life. The entity, designated SCP-1471-A, does not physically harm the user but follows them with increasing proximity, appearing closer in each successive photograph. Users report an overwhelming sense of being watched, followed by isolation and paranoia as the entity becomes visible even without the phone.

Form

SCP-1471-A appears as a tall humanoid (approximately 2.1 meters) with the skull of a large canine (wolf-like), covered in short, black, matted fur. Its eye sockets are empty black voids. It is always seen from a distance in photographs, but the distance decreases over time.

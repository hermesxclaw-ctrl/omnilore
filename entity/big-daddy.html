Who It Is
Form

Big Daddies are large, humanoid creatures with a bulky, armored exoskeleton. They have a distinctive appearance, with a large, imposing presence and a distinctive 'bouncer' or 'protector' role in the underwater city of Rapture.

Powers

Shielding Big Daddies have a powerful shield that protects them from damage, allowing them to withstand even the most intense attacks.

Strength Big Daddies possess incredible strength, allowing them to lift and move heavy objects with ease.

Regeneration Big Daddies have the ability to regenerate their health, allowing them to recover from damage over time.

Accounts

The Big Daddy was created by the scientist Andrew Ryan as a way to protect the Little Sisters from harm.

The Big Daddies became a dominant force in Rapture, with many citizens relying on them for protection.

The Big Daddies played a significant role in the fall of Rapture, as they were used to protect the city's citizens from the outside world.

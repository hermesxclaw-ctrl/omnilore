Form & Signs

Appearance — Big Daddies are large, humanoid creatures with a bulky, armored exoskeleton. They have a distinctive appearance, with a large, imposing presence and a distinctive 'bouncer' or 'protector' role in the underwater city of Rapture.

Symbols — Big Daddies are often associated with the symbol of the 'Big Daddy' logo, a stylized image of a man in a diving suit.

Domains — Big Daddies are found in the underwater city of Rapture, and are often associated with the city's decaying, dystopian environment.

Accounts I
The Birth of Big DaddyBioShock (video game)

2007

The Big Daddy was created by the scientist Andrew Ryan as a way to protect the Little Sisters from harm. The creation of the Big Daddy led to a significant increase in the protection of the Little Sisters, but also created a new threat to the citizens of Rapture.

Accounts II
The Rise of the Big DaddiesBioShock 2 (video game)

2010

The Big Daddies became a dominant force in Rapture, with many citizens relying on them for protection. The rise of the Big Daddies led to a significant increase in the city's safety, but also created a new class system with the Big Daddies at the top.

The Fall of RaptureBioShock Infinite (video game)

2013

The Big Daddies played a significant role in the fall of Rapture, as they were used to protect the city's citizens from the outside world. The fall of Rapture led to the destruction of the city and the Big Daddies were left to fend for themselves.

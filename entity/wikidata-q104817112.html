Who It Is

When Satoru Mikami, a 37-year-old salaryman, was stabbed to death on a Tokyo street, he expected oblivion. Instead, he opened his eyes in a cave as a gelatinous blue slime—the weakest monster in a world of dragons and demon lords. But this slime had a power that would upend the very order of that world: the ability to devour anything and anyone, absorbing their skills and forms. Within months, the creature once mocked as prey would found a nation, befriend a True Dragon, and challenge the demon lords themselves. This is the story of Rimuru Tempest, the slime who became a legend.

Rimuru is pragmatic, intelligent, and strategic, often using unconventional methods to solve problems. He is kind and compassionate towards his friends and citizens, but can be ruthless and calculating towards enemies. He values equality and justice, and seeks to create a peaceful society where all races coexist. Despite his power, he remains humble and approachable, often using humor to defuse tension.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Rimuru is a Japanese transliteration of the English word 'slime' (rimuru). Tempest is taken from Veldora's title, the Storm Dragon, and was chosen by Rimuru as a surname to honor the bond between them.

Pronunciation — REE-moo-roo TEM-pest

Aliases — Rimuru, Slime, The Slime, Jura Tempest Federation's Ruler, Demon Lord Rimuru, The Great Demon Lord Rimuru

Ownership — Japanese popular culture, specifically the light novel, manga, and anime franchise created by Fuse.

Powers
Predator

Rimuru's unique skill that allows him to devour anything, including creatures, skills, and even magic. By analyzing what he consumes, he can replicate their abilities and forms. This is his most iconic and versatile power.

Limit: Initially, Rimuru can only use the skills of creatures he has fully consumed. Some powerful beings may resist being devoured. Overuse can cause mental strain.

Great Sage

A unique skill that acts as an artificial intelligence within Rimuru's mind, providing analysis, advice, and support. It can process vast amounts of data, calculate probabilities, and manage Rimuru's skills. It later evolves into Raphael, which has even greater capabilities.

Limit: Great Sage initially lacks creative thinking and relies on logic. It cannot act independently without Rimuru's command.

Ultimate Skill: Void God Azathoth

An ultimate skill acquired after Rimuru becomes a True Demon Lord. It grants him control over the void, allowing him to nullify any attack, create and destroy matter, and even manipulate concepts. It is considered one of the most powerful skills in the series.

Limit: Requires immense magical energy to use at full power. Its full capabilities are not yet fully explored.

Demon Lord Evolution

After awakening as a Demon Lord, Rimuru gains a significant boost in all abilities, including strength, speed, and magic. He also gains the ability to grant names to others, which evolves them into stronger forms.

Limit: The evolution is a one-time event and requires a massive amount of souls to trigger.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Initially a blue slime with no distinct features. After consuming Shizue Izawa, Rimuru gains an androgynous human form resembling a younger Shizue, with blue hair and golden eyes. Later, as a Demon Lord, his appearance becomes more mature and imposing, with longer hair and a more defined build. He can also shift between his slime and human forms at will.

Symbols — The Tempest emblem, a stylized dragon and slime, represents the Jura Tempest Federation. Rimuru is often depicted with a calm, friendly smile, and his slime form is iconic.

Domains — Rimuru rules the Jura Tempest Federation, a nation located in the Great Jura Forest on the Magic Continent of the Central World. His capital is Rimuru City, which he built and developed. He also has a residence in the Dwelling of Elemental Spirits, and later constructs a Dungeon for adventurers.

Accounts I
Reincarnation as a SlimeLight Novel Volume 1

Modern era (Japan) to fantasy world

Satoru Mikami is killed and reincarnated as a slime in a fantasy world. He quickly befriends Veldora, the Storm Dragon, and gains the power to absorb abilities. He then begins to build a life for himself and other monsters. Rimuru gains the name Tempest and a powerful ally. He also becomes the leader of the goblins and ogres, laying the foundation for his nation.

The Orc Lord IncidentLight Novel Volume 2

Fantasy world

Rimuru and his allies confront the Orc Lord, who is driven by hunger and has been manipulated by a demon lord. Rimuru defeats him and absorbs his power, gaining the title of 'Orc Lord'. Rimuru's reputation grows, and he gains the loyalty of the ogres and other monsters. He also acquires new skills.

Accounts II
The Falmuth InvasionLight Novel Volume 4

Fantasy world

The human kingdom of Falmuth invades Rimuru's nation, killing many of his citizens. Rimuru, in a rage, slaughters the army and becomes a Demon Lord, gaining immense power. He then uses his power to revive those who were killed. Rimuru becomes a Demon Lord and is recognized as a major power. He also gains the loyalty of the revived citizens, who become stronger.

The Demon Lord CouncilLight Novel Volume 6

Fantasy world

Rimuru attends a meeting of the Demon Lords, where he is challenged by Clayman. Rimuru defeats Clayman and takes his place as a member of the Octagram, the ruling council of Demon Lords. Rimuru becomes one of the most powerful beings in the world and establishes his nation as a major power.

The Approach

When Satoru Mikami, a 37-year-old salaryman, was stabbed to death on a Tokyo street, he expected oblivion. Instead, he opened his eyes in a cave as a gelatinous blue slime—the weakest monster in a world of dragons and demon lords. But this slime had a power that would upend the very order of that world: the ability to devour anything and anyone, absorbing their skills and forms. Within months, the creature once mocked as prey would found a nation, befriend a True Dragon, and challenge the demon lords themselves. This is the story of Rimuru Tempest, the slime who became a legend.

Content advisory. Contains violence, death, and dark themes including genocide, war, and political manipulation. Some characters engage in morally ambiguous actions.
Identity

Rimuru Tempest

Pronunciation REE-moo-roo TEM-pest

Known As

RimuruSlimeThe SlimeJura Tempest Federation's RulerDemon Lord RimuruThe Great Demon Lord Rimuru

Etymology

Rimuru is a Japanese transliteration of the English word 'slime' (rimuru). Tempest is taken from Veldora's title, the Storm Dragon, and was chosen by Rimuru as a surname to honor the bond between them.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese popular culture, specifically the light novel, manga, and anime franchise created by Fuse.

Disputed Identifications

None known. Rimuru is a fictional character with no historical or mythological counterpart.

Description

Appearance

Initially a blue slime with no distinct features. After consuming Shizue Izawa, Rimuru gains an androgynous human form resembling a younger Shizue, with blue hair and golden eyes. Later, as a Demon Lord, his appearance becomes more mature and imposing, with longer hair and a more defined build. He can also shift between his slime and human forms at will.

Symbols & Iconography

The Tempest emblem, a stylized dragon and slime, represents the Jura Tempest Federation. Rimuru is often depicted with a calm, friendly smile, and his slime form is iconic.

Behaviour & Personality

Rimuru is pragmatic, intelligent, and strategic, often using unconventional methods to solve problems. He is kind and compassionate towards his friends and citizens, but can be ruthless and calculating towards enemies. He values equality and justice, and seeks to create a peaceful society where all races coexist. Despite his power, he remains humble and approachable, often using humor to defuse tension.

Domains & Habitats

Rimuru rules the Jura Tempest Federation, a nation located in the Great Jura Forest on the Magic Continent of the Central World. His capital is Rimuru City, which he built and developed. He also has a residence in the Dwelling of Elemental Spirits, and later constructs a Dungeon for adventurers.

Version Differences

The light novel is the source material and contains the most detailed and nuanced portrayal. The manga is a faithful adaptation but condenses some events. The anime also adapts the story but makes minor changes for pacing and visual effect. Some differences in dialogue and scene order exist across versions.

Powers & Abilities

Predator

Source Version Light novel, anime, manga

Description

Rimuru's unique skill that allows him to devour anything, including creatures, skills, and even magic. By analyzing what he consumes, he can replicate their abilities and forms. This is his most iconic and versatile power.

Limitations

Initially, Rimuru can only use the skills of creatures he has fully consumed. Some powerful beings may resist being devoured. Overuse can cause mental strain.

Contradictions

None noted.

Great Sage

Source Version Light novel, anime, manga

Description

A unique skill that acts as an artificial intelligence within Rimuru's mind, providing analysis, advice, and support. It can process vast amounts of data, calculate probabilities, and manage Rimuru's skills. It later evolves into Raphael, which has even greater capabilities.

Limitations

Great Sage initially lacks creative thinking and relies on logic. It cannot act independently without Rimuru's command.

Contradictions

None noted.

Ultimate Skill: Void God Azathoth

Source Version Light novel (later volumes), not yet fully shown in anime

Description

An ultimate skill acquired after Rimuru becomes a True Demon Lord. It grants him control over the void, allowing him to nullify any attack, create and destroy matter, and even manipulate concepts. It is considered one of the most powerful skills in the series.

Limitations

Requires immense magical energy to use at full power. Its full capabilities are not yet fully explored.

Contradictions

None noted.

Demon Lord Evolution

Source Version Light novel, anime, manga

Description

After awakening as a Demon Lord, Rimuru gains a significant boost in all abilities, including strength, speed, and magic. He also gains the ability to grant names to others, which evolves them into stronger forms.

Limitations

The evolution is a one-time event and requires a massive amount of souls to trigger.

Contradictions

None noted.

Massacre of Falmuth Army

Source Light Novel Volume 4, Anime Season 1

Description

In response to the invasion of his nation and the deaths of his citizens, Rimuru slaughters the entire Falmuth army, killing thousands. This act is depicted as a turning point in his character, as he is driven by grief and rage.

Scholarly Context

This event is often discussed in analyses of isekai protagonists and moral ambiguity. Rimuru's actions are portrayed as justified, but the sheer scale of the violence is notable.

Consumption of Shizue

Source Light Novel Volume 1, Anime Season 1

Description

Rimuru consumes Shizue's body after her death, absorbing her form and skills. This act is portrayed as a way to honor her memory, but it also highlights the dark implications of his power.

Scholarly Context

This is a common trope in isekai, but the emotional weight is handled with sensitivity.

Manipulation by Others

Source Light Novel, Anime

Description

Rimuru is often manipulated by other powerful beings, such as the Demon Lord Guy Crimson, who use him for their own ends. This is a recurring theme.

Scholarly Context

This highlights the political intrigue and power struggles in the world.

Contradictions & Disputes

Gender

Position A

Rimuru is male, as he identifies as male and uses male pronouns.

Position A — evidence

The series consistently uses male pronouns for Rimuru, and he refers to himself as male.

Position B

Rimuru is genderless, as his slime form has no biological sex, and his human form is androgynous.

Position B — evidence

In the light novel, it is stated that Rimuru's body is genderless, and his human form is based on Shizue, who is female.

Scholarly Consensus

The series treats Rimuru as male for all practical purposes, but acknowledges his biological genderlessness. This is a point of discussion among fans.

Moral Alignment

Position A

Rimuru is a benevolent leader who promotes peace and equality.

Position A — evidence

Rimuru's actions in building his nation and helping others are often portrayed positively.

Position B

Rimuru is ruthless and willing to commit atrocities to protect his people.

Position B — evidence

The massacre of the Falmuth army and his manipulation of others demonstrate a darker side.

Scholarly Consensus

Rimuru is a complex character with a pragmatic approach to morality. He is generally seen as good, but not without flaws.

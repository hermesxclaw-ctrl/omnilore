Who It Is

Jeff the Killer is one of the most recognizable figures in creepypasta history, identifiable by his iconic digitally-edited image: a pale, gaunt face with a carved, permanent smile extending from cheek to cheek, wide unblinking eyes with no visible eyelids, and dark hair. The original creepypasta, posted to the Creepypasta Wiki, tells the story of Jeffrey Woods, a teenage boy who moves to a new neighborhood with his family. After being attacked by bullies, Jeff is doused with bleach and set on fire. He survives but is driven insane by the trauma. In his madness, he cuts a permanent smile into his own face with a knife, burns off his eyelids so he can never sleep, and adopts the persona of 'Jeff the Killer.' His signature catchphrase — 'Go to sleep' — is whispered to his victims before he murders them in their beds. The story established a template that would be copied by dozens of subsequent creepypasta characters: a tragic backstory, a disfiguring event, and a transformation into a remorseless killer. Jeff lore expanded through fan fiction, with additional traits added over time: his ability to appear and disappear silently, his preference for killing at night, and his rivalry with other creepypasta antagonists like Slender Man and Jane the Killer (a female counterpart created by fans). The image associated with Jeff — widely shared across the internet — was created through heavy Photoshop manipulation of an existing photograph, its unnatural smile and staring eyes designed to trigger the uncanny valley response. Jeff's popularity peaked in the early 2010s when creepypasta narration channels on YouTube regularly featured his story. While later critical analysis of the original text has highlighted its amateurish prose and improbable plot, Jeff the Killer's image and core concept — the smiling face appearing in your bedroom window at night — remains one of the most enduring visuals in internet horror.

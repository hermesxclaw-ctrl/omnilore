Who It Is

In a world where magic is weaponized and the line between human and monster blurs, one man stands apart—not for his power, but for his unyielding spirit. Bang Shishigami, the self-proclaimed 'Beautiful Beast of Ikaruga,' is a hero of the people, a master of the forbidden arts, and a walking contradiction: a freedom fighter who despises the very institution he once served, a man who wields fire but seeks to protect the innocent. His fists are his weapons, his cause is justice, and his story is one of loss, redemption, and the relentless pursuit of a world where no child has to witness the horrors he did.

Bang is a hot-blooded, passionate warrior who believes in justice and protecting the innocent. He is fiercely loyal to his friends and allies, and he has a strong sense of honor. He is also somewhat of a showman, often striking dramatic poses and giving passionate speeches. Despite his serious demeanor, he has a soft spot for children and is deeply affected by the tragedies of war. He can be reckless and impulsive, but his heart is always in the right place.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — 'Bang' is a Japanese onomatopoeia for a loud impact, fitting his explosive fighting style. 'Shishigami' (獅子神) translates to 'Lion God' or 'Lion Spirit,' reflecting his fierce, lion-like demeanor and his role as a protector.

Pronunciation — BAHNG SHEE-shee-gah-mee

Aliases — The Beautiful Beast of Ikaruga, The Crimson Lion, Master Bang

Ownership — Created by Arc System Works for the BlazBlue franchise, a Japanese fighting game series.

Powers
Drive: Burning Heart

Bang's Drive ability allows him to ignite his fists with fire, enhancing his punches and kicks. The flames are a manifestation of his fighting spirit and can be used to perform powerful fiery attacks.

Limit: The flames are not inherently magical; they are a result of his martial arts mastery and his inner passion. They do not grant him flight or ranged attacks, and he must rely on close-quarters combat.

Nitro Boost

Bang can use a technique called 'Nitro Boost' to launch himself at high speed across the screen, closing distance quickly or evading attacks. This is a key mobility tool in his arsenal.

Limit: It can be predictable if overused, and it leaves him vulnerable if it whiffs.

Ikaruga Ninja Arts

Bang is a master of the 'Ikaruga Ninja Arts,' a fighting style that emphasizes powerful strikes, acrobatic movements, and the use of fire. This includes techniques like the 'Fūjin' (Wind God) and 'Raijin' (Thunder God) attacks.

Limit: Requires precise timing and positioning; some moves have long recovery times.

Super: Guren no Homura (Crimson Flame)

A powerful super move where Bang engulfs his body in flames and performs a devastating rush attack, ending with a massive fiery explosion.

Limit: Requires full Heat Gauge, and it can be blocked or dodged if the opponent is prepared.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Bang is a tall, muscular man with a tanned complexion and a distinctive mane of wild, spiky red hair. He wears a traditional Japanese fundoshi (loincloth) and a haori (a long coat) with the kanji for 'Ikaruga' on the back. His body is covered in scars, a testament to his many battles. He often has a determined, intense expression, and his eyes are sharp and focused.

Symbols — His iconic symbol is the 'Ikaruga' emblem, which he wears on his back and uses in his fighting style. He is also associated with fire, as his Drive ability involves flame manipulation. His fists are his primary weapons, symbolizing his belief in personal strength and justice.

Domains — Bang is associated with the Ikaruga region, a war-torn area that he seeks to protect. He is a wanderer, traveling to fight injustice wherever it appears. He is also connected to the NOL, having once served as an officer, but he now operates as a rogue agent.

Accounts I
The Ikaruga Civil WarBlazBlue: Calamity Trigger (story mode)

Before the events of Calamity Trigger

Bang was a member of the NOL's elite forces during the Ikaruga Civil War, a conflict between the NOL and the Ikaruga rebels. During the war, Bang witnessed the death of a young girl named Saya, whom he had sworn to protect. This event traumatized him and led to his disillusionment with the NOL, as he felt they were responsible for the tragedy. He eventually deserted the NOL and became a vigilante, fighting to protect the innocent and bring down the corrupt organization. Bang's desertion made him a wanted man, but it also gave him a new purpose. He became a symbol of hope for the people of Ikaruga and a thorn in the side of the NOL.

Accounts II
The Pursuit of Ragna the BloodedgeBlazBlue: Calamity Trigger (story mode)

During Calamity Trigger

Bang initially views Ragna as a dangerous criminal and seeks to capture him to gain favor with the NOL, or perhaps to use him for his own agenda. However, as he learns more about Ragna's motivations and the true nature of the NOL, he becomes an ally. He eventually helps Ragna in his fight against the NOL and other threats. Bang and Ragna develop a mutual respect, and Bang becomes one of Ragna's key allies in the fight against Terumi and the Azure.

The Fight Against TerumiBlazBlue: Continuum Shift, Chrono Phantasma, Central Fiction (story modes)

Throughout the series

Bang plays a supporting role in the fight against the villainous Terumi, who is responsible for many of the tragedies in the BlazBlue world. Bang's determination and fighting spirit are shown to be a counter to Terumi's manipulative nature. He aids Ragna and the other heroes in their quest to stop Terumi's plans. Bang's contributions help the heroes achieve victory, though he is not the central figure in the final confrontation.

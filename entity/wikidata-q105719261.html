The Approach

In a world where magic is weaponized and the line between human and monster blurs, one man stands apart—not for his power, but for his unyielding spirit. Bang Shishigami, the self-proclaimed 'Beautiful Beast of Ikaruga,' is a hero of the people, a master of the forbidden arts, and a walking contradiction: a freedom fighter who despises the very institution he once served, a man who wields fire but seeks to protect the innocent. His fists are his weapons, his cause is justice, and his story is one of loss, redemption, and the relentless pursuit of a world where no child has to witness the horrors he did.

Content advisory. Contains themes of war, violence, and child endangerment, but no explicit sexual or graphic content.
Identity

Bang Shishigami

Pronunciation BAHNG SHEE-shee-gah-mee

Known As

The Beautiful Beast of IkarugaThe Crimson LionMaster Bang

Etymology

'Bang' is a Japanese onomatopoeia for a loud impact, fitting his explosive fighting style. 'Shishigami' (獅子神) translates to 'Lion God' or 'Lion Spirit,' reflecting his fierce, lion-like demeanor and his role as a protector.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Arc System Works for the BlazBlue franchise, a Japanese fighting game series.

Disputed Identifications

There is no significant dispute over Bang's identity; he is consistently portrayed as a martial artist and former NOL officer from Ikaruga.

Description

Appearance

Bang is a tall, muscular man with a tanned complexion and a distinctive mane of wild, spiky red hair. He wears a traditional Japanese fundoshi (loincloth) and a haori (a long coat) with the kanji for 'Ikaruga' on the back. His body is covered in scars, a testament to his many battles. He often has a determined, intense expression, and his eyes are sharp and focused.

Symbols & Iconography

His iconic symbol is the 'Ikaruga' emblem, which he wears on his back and uses in his fighting style. He is also associated with fire, as his Drive ability involves flame manipulation. His fists are his primary weapons, symbolizing his belief in personal strength and justice.

Behaviour & Personality

Bang is a hot-blooded, passionate warrior who believes in justice and protecting the innocent. He is fiercely loyal to his friends and allies, and he has a strong sense of honor. He is also somewhat of a showman, often striking dramatic poses and giving passionate speeches. Despite his serious demeanor, he has a soft spot for children and is deeply affected by the tragedies of war. He can be reckless and impulsive, but his heart is always in the right place.

Domains & Habitats

Bang is associated with the Ikaruga region, a war-torn area that he seeks to protect. He is a wanderer, traveling to fight injustice wherever it appears. He is also connected to the NOL, having once served as an officer, but he now operates as a rogue agent.

Version Differences

In the earlier games, Bang is more focused on revenge against the NOL for their role in the Ikaruga Civil War. In later games, his motivations evolve as he becomes more involved in the larger conflict against Terumi and the Azure. His fighting style remains consistent, but his moveset is expanded with new techniques in each installment.

Powers & Abilities

Drive: Burning Heart

Source Version All mainline BlazBlue games

Description

Bang's Drive ability allows him to ignite his fists with fire, enhancing his punches and kicks. The flames are a manifestation of his fighting spirit and can be used to perform powerful fiery attacks.

Limitations

The flames are not inherently magical; they are a result of his martial arts mastery and his inner passion. They do not grant him flight or ranged attacks, and he must rely on close-quarters combat.

Contradictions

None noted.

Nitro Boost

Source Version All mainline BlazBlue games

Description

Bang can use a technique called 'Nitro Boost' to launch himself at high speed across the screen, closing distance quickly or evading attacks. This is a key mobility tool in his arsenal.

Limitations

It can be predictable if overused, and it leaves him vulnerable if it whiffs.

Contradictions

None noted.

Ikaruga Ninja Arts

Source Version All mainline BlazBlue games

Description

Bang is a master of the 'Ikaruga Ninja Arts,' a fighting style that emphasizes powerful strikes, acrobatic movements, and the use of fire. This includes techniques like the 'Fūjin' (Wind God) and 'Raijin' (Thunder God) attacks.

Limitations

Requires precise timing and positioning; some moves have long recovery times.

Contradictions

None noted.

Super: Guren no Homura (Crimson Flame)

Source Version All mainline BlazBlue games

Description

A powerful super move where Bang engulfs his body in flames and performs a devastating rush attack, ending with a massive fiery explosion.

Limitations

Requires full Heat Gauge, and it can be blocked or dodged if the opponent is prepared.

Stories & Myths

The Ikaruga Civil War

Source BlazBlue: Calamity Trigger (story mode)Culture JapanesePeriod Before the events of Calamity Trigger

Summary

Bang was a member of the NOL's elite forces during the Ikaruga Civil War, a conflict between the NOL and the Ikaruga rebels. During the war, Bang witnessed the death of a young girl named Saya, whom he had sworn to protect. This event traumatized him and led to his disillusionment with the NOL, as he felt they were responsible for the tragedy. He eventually deserted the NOL and became a vigilante, fighting to protect the innocent and bring down the corrupt organization.

Consequences

Bang's desertion made him a wanted man, but it also gave him a new purpose. He became a symbol of hope for the people of Ikaruga and a thorn in the side of the NOL.

Variants

The details of the war are not fully explored in the games, but it is referenced in character interactions and story modes.

However, as he learns more about Ragna's motivations and the true nature of the NOL, he becomes an ally. He eventually helps Ragna in his fight against the NOL and other threats.

Motivations

Position A

Bang is primarily driven by revenge against the NOL for the death of Saya.

Position A — evidence

His early appearances emphasize his hatred for the NOL and his desire to bring them down.

Position B

Bang is primarily driven by a desire to protect the innocent and uphold justice.

Position B — evidence

His interactions with other characters and his later story arcs show a more protective, heroic nature.

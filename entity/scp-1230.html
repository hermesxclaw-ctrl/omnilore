Who It Is
Form

SCP-1230 is a bound book with a worn leather cover and yellowed pages. The book is approximately 300 pages long and contains a mix of text and illustrations.

Powers

Reality Manipulation SCP-1230 has the ability to manipulate reality, creating new worlds and scenarios for its users to explore.

Incident Log

Professor B was granted access to SCP-1230 and was able to interact with the entity. The experiment was successful, but the details are classified.

Who They Are

Kamuy-huci is the most important goddess in Ainu mythology, the divine spirit of the hearth fire who resides in every Ainu home. As the mediator between humans and the other kamuy (gods/spirits), she receives all prayers and offerings and conveys them to the appropriate deities. No ritual begins without first honoring her.

Cultural Significance

Kamuy-huci is the center of Ainu domestic spirituality. The morning prayer always begins at the hearth with an offering to her. During the bear-sending ceremony (Iomante), the most important Ainu ritual, Kamuy-huci's role is central — she must be honored first before the bear's spirit can be sent back to the mountains. The tradition of maintaining an eternal flame persisted well into the 20th century in traditional Ainu households. Today, Kamuy-huci remains an important symbol of Ainu cultural identity and the hearth is still treated with deep respect in Ainu homes.

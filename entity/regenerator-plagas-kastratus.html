Who It Is

A Las Plagas-based B.O.W. created through genetic modification of human hosts implanted with the Plagas Kastratus parasite. Regenerators are pale, emaciated humanoids with elongated limbs and visible parasite organisms moving beneath translucent skin. Their defining trait is near-complete regeneration from any injury — bullets pass through them and the wounds seal almost instantly. Only specific high-frequency thermal weapons can permanently kill them by burning out the embedded parasites.

Powers

Near-instant regeneration from gunfire

Elongated limbs for reaching attacks

Grasping/grappling holds

Iron Maiden: expels body spikes (instakill at close range)

Plaga parasites visible and vulnerable to heat

Slow but relentless pursuit

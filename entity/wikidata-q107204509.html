The Approach

In the world of Japanese horse racing, where champions are often as celebrated for their resilience as their speed, Marvelous Sunday stands out as a true 'Model Student'—a horse who overcame a life-threatening illness and four fractures to claim a Grade 1 victory and capture the hearts of fans.

Who It Is

Sin is a beautiful woman from the waist up, a serpent below, surrounded by hellhounds that burrow into her womb. She was born from Satan's head (a demonic parody of Athena's birth from Zeus) and is both his daughter and the mother of Death, their incestuous son. Death is a shapeless, shrieking horror who guards Hell's gates.

Attested in Paradise Lost (1667)

Cultural Significance

The building of the bridge from Hell to Earth by Sin and Death (Book X) is one of Milton's most powerful symbolic passages — evil establishing permanent infrastructure. This image influenced Blake's mythology and modern depictions of systemic evil.

Powers
The Power to Collect Souls

Sin and Death have the power to collect the souls of the living, often through temptation and sin. They may also have the power to manipulate the emotions and desires of others, leading them down a path of sin and destruction.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Sin and Death are often depicted as a pair, with Death as a skeletal figure and Sin as a dark, shadowy figure. They may be shown together, or separately, depending on the context. In some depictions, Sin is shown as a woman, while in others, it is a man. Death is often shown with a scythe, while Sin is shown with a dark, twisted crown or a serpent.

Symbols — The scythe, the serpent, and the dark, twisted crown are all symbols associated with Death and Sin. The scythe represents the harvesting of souls, while the serpent represents temptation and sin.

Domains — The domains of Sin and Death are often depicted as a dark, shadowy realm, separate from the world of the living. They may also be shown in cemeteries, churches, or other places associated with death and mourning.

The Story of the Sin EaterWikipedia

Middle Ages to present

In this story, a sin eater is a person who consumes a ritual meal in order to spiritually take on the sins of a deceased person. The food was believed to absorb the sins, allowing the deceased person to enter the afterlife without burden.

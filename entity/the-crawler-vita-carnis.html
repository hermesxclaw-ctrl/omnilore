Who It Is
Form

The Crawler is described as a massive, humanoid creature with a twisted, elongated body covered in dark, mottled flesh. Its face is a twisted mass of writhing tendrils, with glowing red eyes that seem to pierce through the darkness.

Powers

Regeneration The Crawler has the ability to regenerate its body from even the most grievous wounds, making it nearly indestructible.

Telepathy The Crawler is said to possess telepathic abilities, allowing it to read the thoughts and emotions of those around it.

Shape-shifting The Crawler is able to change its shape and form at will, allowing it to blend in with its surroundings and avoid detection.

Accounts

A group of adventurers discovers the Crawler's lair deep in the heart of the Vita Carnis world. They soon realize that the Crawler is not just a monster, but a guardian of sorts, tasked with protecting the world from those who would seek to harm it.

A group of friends discovers that they are being stalked by the Crawler, which has been watching them for months. They soon realize that the Crawler is not just a monster, but a manifestation of their own deepest fears.

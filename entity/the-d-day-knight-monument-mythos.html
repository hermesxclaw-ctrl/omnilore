Who It Is
Form

The D-Day Knight is a humanoid figure with a helmet made from the skull of the Horned Serpent. He is often depicted as a tall, imposing figure with a menacing presence.

Powers

Supernatural Strength The D-Day Knight possesses supernatural strength, allowing him to lift and move heavy objects with ease.

Agility The D-Day Knight is said to possess supernatural agility, allowing him to move quickly and easily through his environment.

Regeneration The D-Day Knight is said to possess the ability to regenerate his body, allowing him to heal from injuries and wounds.

Accounts

The D-Day Knight discovers the skull of the Horned Serpent on the beaches of Normandy, and fashions it into a helmet.

The D-Day Knight participates in the invasion of Normandy, using his supernatural strength and agility to fight against the enemy.

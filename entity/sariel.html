Who It Is

Angel of guidance and death, whose name means 'God is my Ruler.' Sariel appears in the Dead Sea Scrolls as Suriel and in the Book of Enoch as one of the seven holy angels who watch. He is associated with the fate of those who transgress divine law, and in some traditions presides over the spirits of the dead. In 1 Enoch, Sariel is listed among the archangels who intercede before God on behalf of humanity. Coptic and Ethiopian Christian traditions preserve him as Souriel, an angelic power. In later occult grimoires, Sariel is sometimes conflated with the angel of death.

The Approach

In the bustling streets of Liyue Harbor, where merchants haggle over Mora and the scent of sea salt mingles with incense, a quiet consultant named Zhongli sips his tea, seemingly an unremarkable man with a passion for antiques and an encyclopedic knowledge of history. But beneath his mortal guise lies the Geo Archon, the oldest of the Seven, who has walked the land for over six thousand years, witnessed the rise and fall of nations, and now, having retired from godhood, seeks to understand the mortal world he once shaped.

Content advisory. Contains themes of war, mortality, loss, and the burden of immortality. Some descriptions of ancient battles and destruction.
Identity

Zhongli

Pronunciation Zhongli: /ˈdʒɒŋli/ or /ˈʒɒŋli/ (approximate); Morax: /ˈmɒræks/; Rex Lapis: /rɛks ˈlæpɪs/

Known As

Etymology

The name 'Zhongli' (钟离) is a Chinese surname that can be interpreted as 'bell' (钟) and 'depart' or 'leave' (离), possibly alluding to his departure from his role as an archon. 'Morax' is derived from a demon in the Ars Goetia, a grimoire of demonology, where Morax is a demon with the title of Earl and President of Hell, who teaches astronomy and other liberal sciences. 'Rex Lapis' is Latin for 'King of Stone'.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character owned by HoYoverse (miHoYo). Chinese-inspired fantasy culture, based on elements of Chinese mythology and history, particularly the concept of the Geo Archon and the region of Liyue, which draws from Chinese geography and culture.

Disputed Identifications

In-universe, his true identity as Rex Lapis is a secret during the early game, leading to fan speculation and in-game rumors. Some players initially theorized he might be the Geo Archon, but it was officially revealed in the game's story. Out-of-universe, there is no dispute as he is a fictional character.

Description

Appearance

Zhongli is a tall, slender man with fair skin, amber eyes, and long brown hair with a low ponytail. He wears a dark brown and gold suit-like outfit with a long coat, black gloves, and a tie. His outfit includes a Vision (a gemstone that grants elemental powers) on his back, which is a Geo Vision. He carries a polearm. His design reflects elegance and antiquity, with motifs of gold and stone.

Symbols & Iconography

Associated with the element of Geo (earth), spears, and the concept of contracts. His constellation is 'Lapis Dei' (Stone of God). His signature weapon is the polearm 'Vortex Vanquisher'. His image includes dragons, as his dragon form is a key part of his lore.

Behaviour & Personality

Zhongli is calm, composed, and wise. He is highly knowledgeable about history, culture, and contracts. He has a deep appreciation for the arts, antiques, and fine cuisine. He is somewhat forgetful about money and often forgets his wallet, relying on others to pay. He is secretive about his past as an archon but is willing to share knowledge. He is deeply committed to the concept of contracts and keeping his word. He is also protective of Liyue and its people, though he has stepped back from direct governance.

Domains & Habitats

He resides in Liyue Harbor, specifically in the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, where he works as a consultant. He is often seen in various locations around Liyue, including the harbor, the Chasm, and the Geo domain.

Version Differences

In the game, Zhongli's playable version has abilities that reflect his Geo powers. His lore-version as the Geo Archon is far more powerful, capable of shaping mountains and creating spears from the earth. The playable version is a mortal-like form with a Vision, but lore-wise, he retains his powers. His appearance is consistent across versions.

Powers & Abilities

Geo Elemental Powers

Source Version Playable character (Genshin Impact) and lore (as Geo Archon)

Description

As the Geo Archon, Zhongli has absolute control over the element of Geo. He can create shields, summon stone spears (called 'Stone Stele'), and cause massive geological changes. In-game, his abilities include: Normal Attack: Rain of Stone (polearm strikes), Elemental Skill: Dominus Lapidis (creates a Stone Stele that deals Geo damage and can resonate with other Geo constructs), Elemental Burst: Planet Befall (summons a meteor that deals massive Geo damage and petrifies enemies).

Limitations

In-game, his abilities are limited by game mechanics (energy cost, cooldowns). Lore-wise, as an archon, his powers are immense but he has chosen to limit himself to a mortal form. He also adheres to contracts, which may restrict his actions.

Contradictions

There is no major contradiction, but his playable abilities are a simplified version of his true power.

Immortality and Longevity

Source Version Lore

Description

As an archon, Zhongli is immortal and has lived for over 6,000 years. He does not age and can survive for millennia.

Limitations

He can potentially be killed, but it is extremely difficult. He also experiences the burden of witnessing the passage of time and the loss of loved ones.

Contradictions

None known.

Contract Creation and Enforcement

Source Version Lore

Description

Zhongli's fundamental power is the ability to create and enforce contracts. He is bound by the terms of contracts he makes, and he can also bind others. This is a metaphysical power that is central to his character and the governance of Liyue.

Limitations

He must abide by the terms of any contract he agrees to, even if it is detrimental to him. He cannot break a contract without severe consequences.

Contradictions

None.

Dragon Form

Source Version Lore

Description

Zhongli can transform into a massive dragon made of stone and gold, capable of flying and causing immense destruction. This form is seen in the Archon Quest when he reveals his identity.

Limitations

Not shown in gameplay; the extent of his power in this form is unknown, but it is implied to be far beyond his mortal form.

Contradictions

None.

The Founding of Liyue

Source In-game lore, character stories, Archon QuestCulture Fictional (Liyue, inspired by Chinese culture)Period Over 6,000 years ago

Summary

Zhongli, as Morax, descended upon the land that would become Liyue and fought many gods and monsters to claim the land. He then established Liyue Harbor as a center of trade and commerce, creating contracts with the people to guide them. He taught the people how to mine, craft, and trade, and he defeated many enemies, including the sea monster Osial.

The Contract to End All Contracts

Source Archon Quest Chapter I, Acts I-IIICulture Fictional (Liyue)Period Present day (in-game)

Summary

Zhongli, tired of his role as archon and seeking to test the people of Liyue, fakes his own death and stages a crisis involving Osial. He works with the Fatui to set up a test to see if Liyue can govern itself without his direct intervention. He also makes a contract with the Traveler to assist them. In the end, he reveals his identity and resigns as the Geo Archon, giving up his Gnosis to the Tsaritsa as part of a contract.

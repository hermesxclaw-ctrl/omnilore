Who It Is

In the bustling streets of Liyue Harbor, where merchants haggle over Mora and the scent of sea salt mingles with incense, a quiet consultant named Zhongli sips his tea, seemingly an unremarkable man with a passion for antiques and an encyclopedic knowledge of history. But beneath his mortal guise lies the Geo Archon, the oldest of the Seven, who has walked the land for over six thousand years, witnessed the rise and fall of nations, and now, having retired from godhood, seeks to understand the mortal world he once shaped.

Zhongli is calm, composed, and wise. He is highly knowledgeable about history, culture, and contracts. He has a deep appreciation for the arts, antiques, and fine cuisine. He is somewhat forgetful about money and often forgets his wallet, relying on others to pay. He is secretive about his past as an archon but is willing to share knowledge. He is deeply committed to the concept of contracts and keeping his word. He is also protective of Liyue and its people, though he has stepped back from direct governance.

Powers
Geo Elemental Powers

As the Geo Archon, Zhongli has absolute control over the element of Geo. He can create shields, summon stone spears (called 'Stone Stele'), and cause massive geological changes. In-game, his abilities include: Normal Attack: Rain of Stone (polearm strikes), Elemental Skill: Dominus Lapidis (creates a Stone Stele that deals Geo damage and can resonate with other Geo constructs), Elemental Burst: Planet Befall (summons a meteor that deals massive Geo damage and petrifies enemies).

Limit: In-game, his abilities are limited by game mechanics (energy cost, cooldowns). Lore-wise, as an archon, his powers are immense but he has chosen to limit himself to a mortal form. He also adheres to contracts, which may restrict his actions.

Immortality and Longevity

As an archon, Zhongli is immortal and has lived for over 6,000 years. He does not age and can survive for millennia.

Limit: He can potentially be killed, but it is extremely difficult. He also experiences the burden of witnessing the passage of time and the loss of loved ones.

Contract Creation and Enforcement

Zhongli's fundamental power is the ability to create and enforce contracts. He is bound by the terms of contracts he makes, and he can also bind others. This is a metaphysical power that is central to his character and the governance of Liyue.

Limit: He must abide by the terms of any contract he agrees to, even if it is detrimental to him. He cannot break a contract without severe consequences.

Dragon Form

Zhongli can transform into a massive dragon made of stone and gold, capable of flying and causing immense destruction. This form is seen in the Archon Quest when he reveals his identity.

Limit: Not shown in gameplay; the extent of his power in this form is unknown, but it is implied to be far beyond his mortal form.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Zhongli is a tall, slender man with fair skin, amber eyes, and long brown hair with a low ponytail. He wears a dark brown and gold suit-like outfit with a long coat, black gloves, and a tie. His outfit includes a Vision (a gemstone that grants elemental powers) on his back, which is a Geo Vision. He carries a polearm. His design reflects elegance and antiquity, with motifs of gold and stone.

Symbols — Associated with the element of Geo (earth), spears, and the concept of contracts. His constellation is 'Lapis Dei' (Stone of God). His signature weapon is the polearm 'Vortex Vanquisher'. His image includes dragons, as his dragon form is a key part of his lore.

Domains — He resides in Liyue Harbor, specifically in the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, where he works as a consultant. He is often seen in various locations around Liyue, including the harbor, the Chasm, and the Geo domain.

Accounts I
The Founding of LiyueIn-game lore, character stories, Archon Quest

Over 6,000 years ago

Zhongli, as Morax, descended upon the land that would become Liyue and fought many gods and monsters to claim the land. He then established Liyue Harbor as a center of trade and commerce, creating contracts with the people to guide them. He taught the people how to mine, craft, and trade, and he defeated many enemies, including the sea monster Osial. Liyue prospered under his guidance, and he became the Geo Archon, one of the Seven. He also formed the Adepti, a group of illuminated beasts and spirits, to serve as his aides.

The Archon WarIn-game lore, character stories

2,600 years ago

During the Archon War, Zhongli fought against other gods to claim the position of Geo Archon. He defeated his rivals and was one of the seven victors, receiving a Gnosis (an orb that connects to the divine) from the Heavenly Principles. He became one of the Seven Archons, ruling over Liyue. He also formed a contract with the other Archons and the Heavenly Principles.

Accounts II
The Fall of the God of the VortexIn-game lore, Archon Quest

Ancient past

Zhongli fought and defeated the God of the Vortex, Osial, a sea monster, sealing him in the depths of the ocean. This event is commemorated in Liyue's festivals and stories. Osial's defeat ensured Liyue's safety, but he was later resurrected by the Fatui during the events of the game, leading to a crisis.

The Contract to End All ContractsArchon Quest Chapter I, Acts I-III

Present day (in-game)

Zhongli, tired of his role as archon and seeking to test the people of Liyue, fakes his own death and stages a crisis involving Osial. He works with the Fatui to set up a test to see if Liyue can govern itself without his direct intervention. He also makes a contract with the Traveler to assist them. In the end, he reveals his identity and resigns as the Geo Archon, giving up his Gnosis to the Tsaritsa as part of a contract. Liyue becomes self-governing, and Zhongli becomes a mortal-like consultant. He loses his Gnosis but retains his powers (as a playable character).

Historia Antiqua (Story Quest)Character Story Quest

Present day (in-game)

Zhongli accompanies the Traveler to investigate the disappearance of a statue of himself and encounters the spirit of a former ally, Azhdaha, a dragon he once sealed. He reflects on his past and the nature of contracts and friendship. Zhongli reaffirms his commitment to Liyue and his personal relationships.

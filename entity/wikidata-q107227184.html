Stories & Myths

Possible connection to the founding of Massalia

Source Speculative, based on the similarity of the name 'Gyptis' to the Gaulish princess in the foundation legend of Massalia, as told by Aristotle (in his lost Constitution of the Massaliotes) and later by Athenaeus and Justin.Culture Greek and RomanPeriod 6th century BCE (legendary)

Summary

According to the legend, Phocaean Greeks led by Protis arrived at the site of Massalia and were invited to a banquet by the local Gaulish king Nannus. The king's daughter, whose name is sometimes given as Gyptis (or Petta), chose Protis as her husband by offering him a cup of wine. This marriage secured the Greeks' position and led to the founding of Massalia.

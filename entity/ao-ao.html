Who It Is
Form

Ao Ao is often described as a voracious sheep-like creature with a massive set of fangs or a large, carnivorous peccary.

Powers

Reproductive Powers Ao Ao is said to have profound reproductive powers, allowing it to produce many offspring who are cursed in the same manner.

Accounts

Ao Ao is one of the cursed sons of Tau and Kerana, making it a central figure in Guarani mythology.

Ao Ao serves as a guardian and protector of the hills and mountains, using its ferocity to defend its territory.

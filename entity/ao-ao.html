Form & Signs

Appearance — Ao Ao is often described as a voracious sheep-like creature with a massive set of fangs or a large, carnivorous peccary.

Symbols — The creature's name is derived from the sound it makes, and its appearance is often associated with the hills and mountains it guards.

Domains — Ao Ao is said to inhabit the hills and mountains of South America, where it serves as a guardian and protector.

Accounts I
The Curse of Tau and KeranaWikipedia

Pre-Columbian

Ao Ao is one of the cursed sons of Tau and Kerana, making it a central figure in Guarani mythology. The curse of Tau and Kerana has far-reaching consequences for the Guarani people and their relationship with the natural world.

Who It Is

She was not supposed to be there — a regulation AEUG officer in a ship full of feral colony kids who stole mobile suits for scrap money. When Judau Ashta jumped into the Zeta Gundam, Roux Louka was the adult in the room who had to prove that discipline could keep up with raw Newtype instinct, and then learn that it could not.

Attested personality: professional, patronizing at first, blunt, and duty-bound. Early episodes show her openly condescending to Judau, Beecha Oleg and Mondo Agake, calling them children and treating them as unreliable - causing immediate friction. She is a by-the-book soldier who respects hierarchy (Bright Noa) and Anaheim Electronics' chain of command. Over time she develops deep trust and maternal/sisterly protectiveness toward the Shangri-La group, particularly Judau and Elle. Name & Tongue

Etymology — Roux is a French surname/word meaning 'red-haired' or a culinary term for a flour-fat base; Louka is a transliteration of a French/Slavic diminutive of Louise/Lucas. The katakana ルー・ルカ (Rū Ruka) indicates a deliberate European, likely French-inspired naming consistent with AEUG's cosmopolitan office

Pronunciation — Japanese: ruː ɾɯka (Rū Ruka). Official English dub: ROO LOO-kah /ruː ˈluːkə/. French-inspired reading would be /ʁu lu.ka/.

Aliases — Rū Ruka, ルー・ルカ, Roux Louca, Lou Luka

Ownership — Fictional character created by Sunrise (Nippon Sunrise) for Mobile Suit Gundam ZZ, directed by Yoshiyuki Tomino, character designed by Hiroyuki Kitazume. Owned and managed by Banda

Powers
Mobile Suit Piloting (AEUG Regular Grade)

Trained AEUG/Anahiem Electronics pilot qualified for transformable mobile suits. Primary machine is MSZ-006 Zeta Gundam (transformable to Waverider). Demonstrated competence in high-mobility space combat, atmospheric re-entry, and escort missions. Not an ace on the level of Kamille Bidan or Judau Ashta in raw Newtype output, but reliable and tactically disciplined.

Limit: No confirmed Newtype abilities; relies on skill and experience rather than psychic intuition. Struggles against enhanced Newtypes like Elpeo Ple and Ple Two and against mass-produced Qubeley and Psyco Gundam Mk II. Requires support against high-performance Neo Zeon prototypes.

MSZ-006 Zeta Gundam Operation

Pilots Zeta Gundam after Judau receives ZZ Gundam. Utilizes beam rifle, beam sabers, grenade launchers, and Waverider cruise mode. Noted for effective use of Zeta's speed to cover ZZ's launch and to engage Glemy's forces at Axis.

Limit: Zeta is a previous-generation machine by UC 0088, outclassed by AMX-004 Qubeley and AMX-015 Geymalk. Maintenance-intensive transformable frame.

Leadership and Mentorship

Acts as de facto senior pilot and disciplinarian for Shangri-La youths. Provides tactical advice, enforces Bright Noa's orders, and later serves as emotional anchor when Judau is depressed after Leina's apparent death.

Limit: Initial patronizing attitude alienates Judau's group; must earn trust rather than command it. Not in formal command chain over Beecha (who briefly captains Nahel Argama).

Marksmanship (Personal Firearm)

Shown using sidearm in shipboard defense and in final confrontation where she kills Glemy Toto with a pistol shot during their last duel's aftermath (ground combat on Axis).

Limit: Standard human ability; no enhanced accuracy.

Accounts I
Arrival on the Argama and The Zeta SuccessionMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episodes 10-12 'Goodbye, Fa' / 'ZZ Gundam Activates'

UC 0088 (in-universe); broadcast March 1986

After the Gryps Conflict leaves Argama critically undermanned and Kamille Bidan incapacitated, AEUG dispatches Anaheim Electronics reinforcement pilot Roux Louka to Side 1's Shangri-La colony. She arrives as a regulation officer to a ship now crewed by 14-year-old junk collectors. When Anaheim delivers the new MSZ-010 ZZ Gundam for Judau Ashta, Judau vacates the Zeta Gundam. Roux is assigned as Zeta's permanent pilot, immediately proving she can handle its transformable frame while the Shangri-La kids struggle with military discipline. Her early sorties cover Judau's shakedown of the ZZ, establishing her as the Argama's second ace. Establishes formal pilot roster for Argama; allows Judau to transition to ZZ without leaving Argama without a Zeta pilot; sets up Roux/Judau rivalry-to-trust arc.

Friction with Shangri-La and the Dakar OperationMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episodes 13-20, especially Episode 18 'Haman's Black Shadow'

UC 0088; Dakar, Earth

Roux's by-the-book attitude clashes with Judau's gang, who view her as arrogant and condescending. She lectures them on proper military conduct, while they mock her as a stiff. During Neo Zeon's occupation of the Federation Assembly at Dakar, Argama provides cover for civilian evacuation. Roux in Zeta Gundam fights alongside Judau to hold off Axis mobile suits while Bright attempts political negotiation. The operation exposes her willingness to follow dangerous orders and her growing respect for the kids' improvisational skill. Leina Ashta's temporary escape and re-capture occurs in this arc, with Roux helping search for her. Bond of trust begins to form; Roux is accepted as part of Argama's family rather than an outsider; Neo Zeon consolidates Earth political control.

The Dublin Colony DropMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episodes 28-33 'The Mirror of Dublin'

UC 0088; Dublin, Ireland, Earth

Neo Zeon executes a colony drop on Dublin to demonstrate power after the Federation's refusal to surrender. Argama and Karaba forces attempt to evacuate civilians and destroy the falling colony. Roux sorties in Zeta Gundam in atmospheric combat, fighting Rakan Dahkaran's Zaku III and other Neo Zeon units while trying to prevent the drop. Despite AEUG efforts, the colony impacts, devastating Dublin. Hayato Kobayashi is killed in this battle. Roux witnesses the mass civilian death firsthand, reinforcing the series' anti-war theme and her mature understanding of war's cost compared to the younger pilots' shock. Dublin destroyed; Federation capitulates partially; Argama crew traumatized; Roux's role as emotionally steady senior pilot emphasized as she comforts Elle and Judau.

Accounts II
Leina's Apparent Death and Judau's CollapseMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episodes 35-38 'Leina's Blood' / 'Judau's Despair'

UC 0088; African desert, Earth

After Leina escapes Glemy's tutelage at Dakar, she reunites briefly with Judau only to be shot and wounded by Haman Karn. Judau shelters her in a desert cabin, but a stray mobile suit battle causes a damaged suit to crash into the cabin. Judau believes Leina has been killed. He becomes depressed and detached, refusing to pilot effectively. Roux, along with Elle and Bright, tries to keep Judau functional, covering his patrols in Zeta Gundam and urging him not to abandon hope. She does not dismiss his later insistence that Leina is alive, showing quiet support while others think him delusional - a key moment of empathy that cements their bond. Judau's Newtype intuition is validated later when Leina is revealed alive, rescued by Sayla Mass; Roux's faith in Judau distinguishes her from crew members who doubt him.

Final Battle at Axis and the Killing of Glemy TotoMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episodes 45-47 'The Battle of Axis' / 'Warrior, Again...'

UC 0088; Axis asteroid fortress, space

In the climactic civil war within Neo Zeon between Haman Karn and Glemy Toto's rebel faction, Argama intervenes. Roux in Zeta Gundam fights through Glemy's clone Newtype corps, including Ple Two in the massive MRX-009 Psyco Gundam Mk II. Glemy, who has harbored an obsessive infatuation with Roux since their first encounter (he repeatedly tries to court her, viewing her as a refined lady to match his Zeon aristocratic fantasy), confronts her personally. After his forces collapse and his Bawoo is disabled, Glemy attempts to flee and to take Roux with him. On the ground inside Axis, Roux confronts him face-to-face and shoots him dead with her sidearm, rejecting his possessive vision. Judau simultaneously defeats Haman in the ZZ vs Qubeley duel. Glemy Toto dies, ending his rebellion; Haman dies shortly after; First Neo Zeon War ends; Roux's act symbolizes rejection of Zeon aristocratic coercion and assertion of female agency in UC Gundam.

Departure to JupiterMobile Suit Gundam ZZ Episode 47 final scene; Gundam Evolve 10 (2007)

UC 0088-0089; Earth Sphere to Jupiter Sphere

With the war over and AEUG reintegrated into the Federation, Judau decides not to return to civilian life on Shangri-La. He chooses to join the Jupiter Fleet to seek a future beyond war. Roux volunteers to go with him, along with Leina. The final montage shows Judau and Roux leaving aboard the Jupitris II. Gundam Evolve 10 revisits this period: on Judau's birthday during the Jupiter transit, he and Roux (implied co-pilot) escort the Jupitris when Neo Zeon remnants attack a Qubeley Mk II; Judau protects the Qubeley, which lands safely. The scene confirms Roux and Judau remain together as Jupiter Fleet members. Provides rare hopeful ending in Gundam - protagonists choose exploration over militarism; establishes Roux's future outside Earth Sphere; leaves open her later fate (not depicted in ZZ sequels).

Roux Louka is the main pilot of the ZZ Gundam. — Judau Ashta is the ZZ Gundam pilot throughout. Roux pilots the Zeta Gundam after Judau upgrades. She never pilots ZZ in TV canon.

Roux is a powerful Newtype like Elpeo Ple. — No Newtype abilities are demonstrated or credited in official profiles. Her strength is conventional piloting and maturity, contrasting with the Newtype children.

Roux dies at the end of Gundam ZZ. — She survives the war and leaves for Jupiter with Judau and Leina. This is explicitly shown in episode 47 and Gundam Evolve 10.

Roux and Glemy were in a mutual romance. — Glemy's affection was one-sided, obsessive, and rejected. Roux viewed him with hostility and ultimately killed him. No mutual romance is depicted.

Roux Louka appears in Mobile Suit Gundam: Char's Counterattack or later UC sequels. — She does not appear in Char's Counterattack (UC 0093) or any subsequent mainline UC anime. Her fate after Jupiter departure is undepicted.

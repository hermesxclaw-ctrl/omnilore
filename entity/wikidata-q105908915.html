Who It Is

Imagine a silver-haired maid from outer space who claims to be the living embodiment of a Lovecraftian cosmic horror—but whose primary goal is to marry a high school boy. This is Nyaruko, the chaotic yet lovable protagonist of the light novel and anime series Nyaruko: Crawling with Love, a character who turns the eldritch into the adorable.

Nyaruko is energetic, affectionate, and relentlessly pursues Mahiro Yasaka, the object of her affection. She is described as earnest, straightforward, and adorably energetic. Despite her cosmic origins, she enjoys mundane activities like anime, manga, and video games.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Nyaruko typically appears as a petite human girl with long silver hair and a distinctive ahoge (cowlick). She often wears a maid's outfit, but can shapeshift into other forms. In combat, she wears a black and red armor that covers her entire body.

Symbols — Her ahoge is a notable feature, though its nature is ambiguous. She is associated with the color combination of black and red, particularly in her combat armor.

Domains — Originally from an alien race inspired by Nyarlathotep, she lives on Earth in Mahiro's house, pretending to be his cousin. She attends his school and adapts to human life.

Accounts I
Main SeriesNyaruko: Crawling with Love light novels

2009-2014

Nyaruko is sent to Earth by the Space Defence Agency to protect Mahiro Yasaka from hostile aliens. She falls in love with him at first sight and moves in with him, claiming to be his cousin. The series follows her attempts to win his affection while dealing with other aliens who become involved in their lives. Nyaruko's presence leads to various comedic and chaotic situations, and she becomes a central figure in Mahiro's life.

Accounts II
Anime SeriesNyarko-san: Another Crawling Chaos

2012-2015

The anime adapts the light novel, with Nyaruko as the protagonist. It includes the same core plot of her protecting Mahiro and pursuing him romantically. The anime popularized the character further, leading to increased merchandise and recognition.

The Approach

In the radiant realm of Angel Land, where gods and monsters wage eternal war, one goddess stands as the beacon of hope and the embodiment of divine light. Palutena, the Goddess of Light, is not merely a ruler but a protector, a strategist, and the guiding force behind the heroic angel Pit. Her story, woven through the Kid Icarus series, is a tale of resilience, sacrifice, and the enduring power of faith.

Content advisory. The Kid Icarus series contains fantasy violence, including battles against mythological monsters and dark forces. Some depictions may include mild peril and themes of good versus evil. No explicit dark material is present in the source material provided.
Identity

Palutena

Pronunciation pah-loo-TAY-nah

Known As

Goddess of LightPrincess Lana (in Captain N: The Game Master)

Etymology

The name 'Palutena' is likely derived from 'Pallas Athena', the Greek goddess of wisdom and warfare, reflecting her role as a wise and powerful deity. The 'Palu' prefix may also evoke 'palladium', a protective image or statue.

Cultural Ownership

Nintendo, as part of the Kid Icarus franchise. The character is inspired by Greco-Roman mythology, particularly Athena, but is an original creation.

Disputed Identifications

There is no major dispute over her identity, but her adaptation as Princess Lana in the animated series 'Captain N: The Game Master' is a notable variation that is not considered canonical to the games.

Description

Appearance

Palutena is depicted as a tall, elegant goddess with long, flowing green hair and striking green eyes. She wears a white and gold gown with a blue sash, and a golden tiara or circlet. In 'Kid Icarus: Uprising', her design is more detailed, with a more mature and regal appearance, and she is often shown with a staff or orb.

Symbols & Iconography

Light is her primary symbol, representing hope, goodness, and divine power. She is often associated with the Three Sacred Treasures, which she bestows upon Pit. Her staff and orb are symbols of her authority and magical abilities.

Behaviour & Personality

Palutena is wise, compassionate, and protective of her followers, especially Pit. She is a strategic leader, often guiding Pit with advice and support. In 'Uprising', she displays a playful and teasing side, but remains serious in battle. She is confident and sometimes sarcastic, but always cares deeply for her realm.

Domains & Habitats

She resides in Angel Land, a heavenly realm based on Greco-Roman mythology. She rules over the angels and protects the world from the Underworld. She is closely associated with the sky and light.

Version Differences

In the original 1986 game, Palutena is a damsel in distress, captured by Medusa, and only appears at the beginning and end. In 'Of Myths and Monsters', she is more active, summoning Pit and giving him missions. In 'Uprising', she is a fully realized character, with extensive dialogue and a central role, often fighting alongside Pit. In 'Captain N', she is reimagined as Princess Lana, a human princess with a love triangle.

Rescue from Medusa

Palutena is kidnapped by Medusa, the Goddess of Darkness, and imprisoned in the Underworld. Pit, her most loyal angel, escapes his own prison and embarks on a quest to save her. He overcomes Zeus's challenges, collects the Three Sacred Treasures, and defeats Medusa, freeing Palutena.

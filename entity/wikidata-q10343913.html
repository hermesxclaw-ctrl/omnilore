Who It Is

In the radiant realm of Angel Land, where gods and monsters wage eternal war, one goddess stands as the beacon of hope and the embodiment of divine light. Palutena, the Goddess of Light, is not merely a ruler but a protector, a strategist, and the guiding force behind the heroic angel Pit. Her story, woven through the Kid Icarus series, is a tale of resilience, sacrifice, and the enduring power of faith.

Palutena is wise, compassionate, and protective of her followers, especially Pit. She is a strategic leader, often guiding Pit with advice and support. In 'Uprising', she displays a playful and teasing side, but remains serious in battle. She is confident and sometimes sarcastic, but always cares deeply for her realm.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Palutena' is likely derived from 'Pallas Athena', the Greek goddess of wisdom and warfare, reflecting her role as a wise and powerful deity. The 'Palu' prefix may also evoke 'palladium', a protective image or statue.

Pronunciation — pah-loo-TAY-nah

Aliases — Goddess of Light, Princess Lana (in Captain N: The Game Master)

Ownership — Nintendo, as part of the Kid Icarus franchise. The character is inspired by Greco-Roman mythology, particularly Athena, but is an original creation.

Powers
Divine Light Magic

Palutena can project beams of light and create barriers of protective energy. She uses this to attack enemies and shield allies.

Limit: Her power may be limited when she is weakened or captured, as seen in the original game.

Flight

As a goddess, she can fly, though she often relies on Pit for physical tasks.

Limit: She rarely flies in gameplay, usually staying in a stationary position.

Teleportation

She can teleport herself and others, as seen when she sends Pit to various locations.

Limit: Not explicitly shown as a limitation.

Empowerment

She can bestow power upon Pit, such as granting him the Three Sacred Treasures, enabling him to defeat stronger enemies.

Limit: She can only grant power when she is free and able.

Prophecy and Dream Interpretation

She can interpret nightmares as omens, as seen in 'Of Myths and Monsters'.

Limit: Not always accurate, as seen in the game's plot.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Palutena is depicted as a tall, elegant goddess with long, flowing green hair and striking green eyes. She wears a white and gold gown with a blue sash, and a golden tiara or circlet. In 'Kid Icarus: Uprising', her design is more detailed, with a more mature and regal appearance, and she is often shown with a staff or orb.

Symbols — Light is her primary symbol, representing hope, goodness, and divine power. She is often associated with the Three Sacred Treasures, which she bestows upon Pit. Her staff and orb are symbols of her authority and magical abilities.

Domains — She resides in Angel Land, a heavenly realm based on Greco-Roman mythology. She rules over the angels and protects the world from the Underworld. She is closely associated with the sky and light.

Accounts I
Rescue from MedusaKid Icarus (1986)

1986

Palutena is kidnapped by Medusa, the Goddess of Darkness, and imprisoned in the Underworld. Pit, her most loyal angel, escapes his own prison and embarks on a quest to save her. He overcomes Zeus's challenges, collects the Three Sacred Treasures, and defeats Medusa, freeing Palutena. Palutena is restored to her throne, and Pit becomes her most trusted servant.

Accounts II
The Invasion of OrcosKid Icarus: Of Myths and Monsters

1991

Palutena has a nightmare of an invasion by the demon Orcos. She summons Pit and instructs him to retrieve the Three Sacred Treasures, which she has hidden with three fortress guards. Pit defeats the guards, obtains the treasures, and defeats Orcos, saving Angel Land. Angel Land is saved, and Pit is rewarded with wealth.

The War Against HadesKid Icarus: Uprising

2012

Palutena and Pit fight against Medusa and her army, but soon discover that Medusa is merely a pawn of Hades, the true ruler of the Underworld. They must unite with former enemies, including Viridi and Dark Pit, to defeat Hades and save the world. Hades is defeated, and peace is restored, but Palutena is temporarily turned into a statue by the Wish Seed, later revived.

Al-Mihraj

The Great Fish, The Whale-Island, The Back That Is Mistaken for Land

Who It Is
Form

Al-Mihraj is often depicted as a massive fish or whale with a back that resembles land, sometimes with a single horn or a crown-like structure on its head. Its body is said to be as long as a mountain range, and its fins are as wide as a desert oasis.

Powers

Control over the oceans and the weather Al-Mihraj is said to have the power to control the oceans and the weather, and is often associated with the mythological storms and tidal waves of the Arabian Peninsula.

Accounts

According to Islamic mythology, Al-Mihraj was a massive fish that carried the Prophet Muhammad across the Red Sea to Jerusalem, where he ascended to heaven and received revelations from Allah.

According to Arabian mythology, Al-Mihraj was a massive fish that was said to have a single horn on its head, and was often associated with the mythological one-horned hare, Al-Miraj.

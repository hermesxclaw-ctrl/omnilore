Who It Is

In The Eminence in Shadow's world of elaborate shadow-broker fantasies and secret organizations with numerical rosters, No. 664 / Nami stands out not for world-shaking power, but for the quiet tension at the heart of her character: she is the operative who follows the rules — until she doesn't, and it matters. Her instinct to defend her wayward squad-mate Rose against the Shadow Garden hierarchy, even while personally disapproving, is the kind of small moment that separates a memorable supporting character from a mere background fixture. What does it mean to serve an organization whose leader is secretly an oblivious isekai protagonist roleplaying a chuunibyou fantasy — and to do so with genuine discipline and loyalty?

No. 664 is characterized throughout the series as serious, disciplined, and protocol-driven to a degree that frequently generates friction with more impulsive teammates. She is a natural leader who places mission parameters and organizational rules above personal sentiment, making her something of a strict foil to No. 666 / Rose Oriana, whose noble upbringing and emotional bonds with her mother lead her to make independent decisions that violate Shadow Garden's operational guidelines. Shadow Garden Combat Training

As a full operative member of Shadow Garden (one of 'the Numbers'), No. 664 has undergone the organization's intensive combat and magic training regimen instituted by Cid Kagenou. This training is designed to bring recruits — many of whom were already accomplished fighters or magic-users before joining — to a level far exceeding ordinary knights, mages, or mercenaries. The Numbers are distinguished from the elite Seven Shades (Alpha, Beta, Gamma, etc.) but are nonetheless treated as highly capable covert operatives. 664's leadership of a three-person squad implies she has demonstrated sufficient combat and tactical superiority over her peers to merit that position.

Limit:

Slime Bodysuit Enhancement

No. 664 wears Shadow Garden's standard-issue Slime Bodysuit (スライムボディスーツ), an innovative combat garment invented by Cid Kagenou and manufactured from the specialized slimes native to his domain. Although slimes as creatures are typically fragile, they are exceptional mana conductors. When worn by a Shadow Garden member with sufficient mana control, the Slime Bodysuit amplifies the wearer's existing magical and physical abilities. For 664, whose natural elfin capacity for mana manipulation is presumably higher than that of a typical human operative, the suit may provide greater enhancement than it would for non-elven members. The suit also serves a stealth function, conforming tightly to the body and allowing free movement in infiltration scenarios.

Limit:

Mana Manipulation and Magic Combat

As an Elf within the series' fantasy world system, No. 664 possesses racial advantages in mana capacity and control that place her above most human counterparts. Elves in the series' setting are a long-lived race historically attuned to magic, and Shadow Garden preferentially recruits individuals with exceptional magical talent. While the series does not specify 664's precise combat magic specialization, her role as a squad leader implies mastery of offensive, defensive, and support-type magic applications sufficient to lead field operations. Her short, agile frame likely favors high-mobility combat styles over brute force.

Limit:

Squad Tactics and Leadership

664 serves as the designated leader of a three-person squad comprising herself (664), No. 665, and No. 666 (Rose Oriana). This leadership role extends beyond personal combat capability to encompass tactical coordination, mission planning, and personnel management. Her ability to maintain cohesion between herself, the contrasting physical type of 665, and the frequently protocol-deviant 666/Rose demonstrates adaptive leadership in difficult interpersonal conditions. Her decision to defend Rose's autonomous action to Shadow Garden's senior hierarchy — even while personally disagreeing with it — shows political and social acumen within the organization's internal culture.

Limit:

Covert Operations and Infiltration

After Rose Oriana (No. 666) transitions from Shadow Garden operative to Queen of the Oriana Kingdom, No. 664 transitions alongside her in the role of undercover maid-attendant (侍女, jijo). This role requires 664 to sustain a cover identity within a royal court environment, suppressing her operative persona and integrating with palace staff. The ability to maintain long-term undercover infiltration of a sovereign's household — observing court politics, protecting the queen from internal and external threats, and reporting intelligence back to Shadow Garden — is a specialized skillset distinct from open combat.

Limit:

Elfin Physical Attributes

As an Elf, 664 benefits from the racial traits typically associated with elves in the series' world-building: enhanced longevity, sharper sensory perception (particularly hearing and vision), natural agility, and a constitution that may recover from injuries or resist magical ailments more effectively than humans. Her pointed ears also serve as a passive social marker that, in the series' world, may grant her specific cultural status or conversely mark her as an outsider in predominantly human environments — a factor that would affect her infiltration options and social strategies.

Form & Signs

Her most immediately recognizable features are her short-cropped blue hair and matching blue eyes, which give her a cool, sharp visual profile consistent with her serious demeanor. She bears a small beauty mark (hokuro) positioned just left of center on her forehead — a detail that serves as one of the few personal physical mark

Symbols — The number 664 itself functions as her primary identifying symbol within the Shadow Garden hierarchy. The dark Slime Bodysuit with its gold and white chest accents is the most visually iconic element associated with her as an operative. Her elf ears — a racial marker — subtly communicate her non-human origin. The beauty mark on her forehead is a recurring visual identifier in official artwork.

Domains — No. 664 operates primarily within Shadow Garden's covert network, which spans multiple kingdoms in the series' fantasy world. She is particularly associated with the Oriana Kingdom after Rose Oriana (No. 666) transitions from operative to queen. Accounts I
Introduction and Oriana Kingdom ArcThe Eminence in Shadow light novel / manga / anime Season 1

2022 (anime adaptation); ongoing in light novel

No. 664 is introduced as part of Shadow Garden's operational Numbers during the storyline focused on the Oriana Kingdom. She leads a squad alongside No. 665 and No. 666, with No. 666 later revealed to be Rose Oriana, the princess of Oriana Kingdom who had been inducted into Shadow Garden after falling victim to a scheme orchestrated by the Cult of Diablos. 664's squad operates in the field executing missions assigned through Shadow Garden's chain of command, with 664 enforcing discipline and protocol within her small unit. The Oriana arc establishes 664 as a mid-tier recurring character with meaningful squad-level responsibilities. Rose Oriana's dual identity as both a princess and a Shadow Garden Number creates political complications that directly affect 664's squad dynamics.

Rose's Protocol Breach and 664's DefenseThe Eminence in Shadow light novel (later volumes) / manga

2022–2023 (light novel volumes released in this period)

At a critical juncture, No. 666 / Rose Oriana abandons her Shadow Garden post without authorization to act independently in order to protect her mother (the queen). This act violates Shadow Garden's strict operational protocols, which demand that members prioritize organizational missions over personal loyalties. No. 664, though genuinely disapproving of Rose's breach of protocol, chooses to defend Rose's decision to Shadow Garden's senior hierarchy. This moment reveals the tension within 664's character between her rule-following instincts and her genuine personal bond with her squad-mate. 664's defense of Rose demonstrates that personal loyalty can override procedural rigidity even for the most protocol-focused operatives, and lends narrative depth to what could otherwise be a one-note strict-soldier archetype. It also foreshadows the squad's eventual transition to supporting Rose as queen.

Accounts II
Transition to Royal Maid-AttendantThe Eminence in Shadow light novel (later volumes)

Later volumes of the light novel (post-2022 anime coverage)

Following Rose Oriana's eventual elevation to Queen of the Oriana Kingdom — a development facilitated in part by Shadow Garden's operations — No. 664 (and presumably No. 665) transitions from combat operative to undercover maid-attendant in Rose's royal household. This role reframes 664's capabilities entirely: instead of a field operative executing Shadow Garden missions, she becomes a domestic-cover intelligence asset embedded within a friendly sovereign's court. The assignment is framed as dispatching trusted operatives to support Rose in becoming a strong queen. This storyline arc represents a significant shift in 664's narrative function — from combatant to courtly protector — and reflects Shadow Garden's strategic interest in maintaining influence over the Oriana Kingdom's new ruler. It also continues 664 and 665's roles as Rose's closest companions.

Squad Dynamics: 664, 665, and 666The Eminence in Shadow light novel, manga, and anime (multiple arcs)

Ongoing across multiple volumes/episodes

The recurring trio of 664, 665, and 666 functions as a recurring narrative unit across multiple story arcs. The three are contrasted physically — 664 being petite and short, 665 being taller with a more imposing figure — and in personality, with 666/Rose bringing noble social instincts and emotional intensity that conflict with 664's regimented approach. Their dynamic serves the series' comedic and dramatic registers: the tension between Rose's individuality and 664's insistence on protocol is a source of both humor and genuine emotional stakes. The trio's bond is one of the series' small-scale relationship arcs that humanizes the otherwise largely faceless 'Numbers' of Shadow Garden. Fans have responded warmly to the trio, generating significant fan art and discussion.

2022–present

No. 664 appears in the franchise's official mobile RPG Master of Garden, which launched simultaneously in Japan and globally in November 2022. As is standard for such tie-in games, 664 may appear as a story character and/or as a playable unit with game-specific combat stats, skill sets, and story event appearances. Mobile games in this genre typically expand secondary characters beyond their main narrative roles, potentially providing 664 with unique dialogue, backstory scenes, and special story missions not found in the light novel. The game represents an additional canonical (or semi-canonical) medium in which 664 exists, with the potential to expand her characterization for players who engage with the game.

Canonicity of the name 'Nami' across media versionsThe name 'Nami' (ナミ) is the character's confirmed real name, established in the original web novel and carried through to the commercial light novel, fan wikis, and databases including MyWaifuList, the Heroes Wiki, and NeoApo. It is treated as fully canonical by the fan community and by the Wikidata entry's native-name field (P1559: 664番 for the codename).Some readers of early web novel chapters argue that the given names of Numbers members were not explicitly stated in the earliest arcs and that 'Nami' may have been introduced in later chapters or through supplementary materials (such as the mobile game Master of Garden or author notes) rather than in the main narrative text from the outset. This makes 'Nami' a name that is widely accepted but whose precise in-text debut is not universally pinpointed.
664's rank and authority relative to the Seven ShadesThe Numbers (of which 664 is a member) are clearly subordinate to the Seven Shades (Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, Epsilon, Zeta, Eta) within Shadow Garden's hierarchy. 664 is a squad leader among the Numbers but is not presented as a peer of the Seven Shades in terms of personal power or organizational authority. This reading is supported by the differential uniform design (664's Slime Bodysuit features standard 'new member' gold and white accents rather than the higher-tier customizations seen on senior members).Some power-level discussions in fan communities argue that mid-to-upper Numbers like 664 may be functionally comparable to lower-ranked Seven Shades members in raw combat capability, particularly given 664's status as an Elf (with racial magic advantages). The hierarchy in Shadow Garden is partly based on seniority and trust in addition to raw power, meaning 664's subordinate rank does not necessarily reflect a large gap in absolute fighting ability.
Whether 664's defense of Rose constitutes insubordination or sanctioned conduct664's act of defending Rose's unauthorized departure to Shadow Garden's hierarchy — while personally disagreeing with Rose's choice — can be read as an act of personal loyalty that technically constitutes a breach of Shadow Garden's strict operational impartiality norms. An operative defending a rule-breaker to leadership could itself be viewed as protocol-bending behavior, suggesting 664 is more emotionally driven than her serious exterior implies.Alternatively, 664's defense can be read as entirely within appropriate conduct: a squad leader is expected to advocate for the members of their unit and to provide senior leadership with context for their team's actions. Defending 666/Rose with the context of Rose's motivation (protecting her mother, a queen) is not the same as endorsing the rule breach — it is the exercise of the squad leader's duty to represent their team fairly to the hierarchy. Under this reading, 664 acts within her role, not outside it.
The extent of 664's appearances in the anime versus the light novelAnime viewers who have not read the light novel frequently express surprise or disappointment that 664 receives relatively limited screen time in the anime's adaptation of the relevant arcs, especially given her clearly defined role as squad leader and her distinctive character design. This position holds that the anime underutilizes the character compared to the depth the light novel affords.Light novel readers defend the anime adaptation's choices by noting that the series necessarily focuses on the main cast and primary antagonist factions, and that 664's narrative role as a supporting character naturally limits her adaptation prominence. The light novel itself does not give 664 POV chapters or extended individual story arcs — she is a well-defined supporting presence rather than a co-protagonist.

Who It Is

A winged, gargoyle-like monster that inhabits the foggy skies of Silent Hill. Air Screamers have leathery wings, sharp talons, and a piercing scream. They swoop down from above to attack unsuspecting victims. Their shrill cries can be heard from a distance, warning of their presence.

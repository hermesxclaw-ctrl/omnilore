Who It Is

He looked like a child who should have been in a classroom, not on a throne built from blood. Yagura Karatachi — the Fourth Mizukage — ruled the Village Hidden in the Mist with a coral-tipped staff and a monster sealed inside him, smiling politely while one of the most dangerous illusions in ninja history puppeted his every decree.

Attested: Canon shows two layers. 1) The 'real' Yagura glimpsed briefly after being freed from control and during his Edo Tensei self-aware moments: polite, formal, somewhat reserved and mature beyond his appearance, expressing concern for his village and acknowledging his failures as a manipulated ruler. He speaks calmly and honorifically, showing Kage-level composure. 2) The controlled Yagura during the Bloody Mist era: cold, ruthless, ordering extreme graduation exams (students killing classmates) and purges, as reported by Kisame, Ao, and Mei Terumi. Powers
Perfect Jinchūriki Control / Isobu Partnership

Yagura is one of the few shinobi stated to have achieved full control over his tailed beast, Isobu (Three-Tails). He could access Isobu's chakra at will, fully transform into Isobu, and use Tailed Beast Ball without losing consciousness. Unlike Naruto early on, he was not shown struggling with Isobu's will. During the War arc, he demonstrates cooperative transformation alongside other jinchūriki.

Limit: Control was ultimately undermined by external genjutsu (Sharingan) from Obito, showing that perfect control does not equal genjutsu immunity. After death and Isobu's extraction, he loses all tailed-beast powers. As Edo Tensei, his power is limited by Kabuto's control until freed.

Water Release (Suiton) Mastery

As Mizukage, Yagura displays high-level Water Release. In reanimated form he uses Water Release: Water Mirror Technique (Suiton: Mizukagami no Jutsu), which creates a mirror that spawns an exact copy of an opponent's attack or self, and large-scale water projectiles.

Limit: Requires water source or chakra to create water; mirror technique reflects only what it can copy and is chakra-intensive. Not shown to use other nature transformations in canon.

Coral Palm / Sangoton (Coral Technique)

Signature technique linked to Isobu's power: striking an opponent causes hard, branching coral to rapidly grow on the impact site, restricting movement and continuing to grow. Yagura uses it via his hooked staff and direct palm strikes. In full transformation, Isobu can produce massive coral structures.

Limit: Requires physical contact; coral can be shattered with sufficient force or chakra, but grows quickly. As Edo Tensei, coral is slightly slower due to not being living Isobu.

Tailed Beast Transformation and Tailed Beast Ball (Bijūdama)

Yagura can fully transform into Isobu, a giant spiny turtle with three tails, and fire the Bijūdama - a concentrated ball of positive and negative chakra. In War arc, the six reanimated jinchūriki combine Bijūdama under Obito's control.

Limit: Full transformation makes him a large target; Bijūdama requires charging time and leaves him vulnerable if interrupted. After Isobu's extraction before death, cannot transform.

Mizukage Staff and Taijutsu

Wields a large hooked staff with a coral-like head and green flower ornament, used for melee combat, to focus coral techniques, and to channel chakra. Shown blocking Kisame's Samehada in flashback.

Limit: Staff is not shown to have special sealing properties; primarily a conduit. Relies on user's physical skill.

Genjutsu Vulnerability / Sharingan Control (as victim)

Not an ability he wields, but a defining power dynamic: Yagura was placed under a powerful Sharingan genjutsu by Obito Uchiha (masquerading as Madara), controlling his actions as Mizukage for years. Ao later detects and dispels it with his Byakugan, noting the chakra was that of the Sharingan.

Limit: As victim, he had no resistance despite being a perfect jinchūriki, indicating the genjutsu's exceptional strength (Obito's Mangekyō). This is a narrative weakness, not a technique.

Accounts I
The Child Who Became Mizukage and JinchūrikiNaruto manga Ch. 507, 559, 572; Fourth Databook

In-universe: approx. 18-15 years before Naruto Part I (late Third Shinobi World War aftermath); Publication: 2010-2014

Yagura Karatachi, despite his childlike appearance, is selected as the Fourth Mizukage and as the jinchūriki of Isobu, the Three-Tails. Kirigakure's tradition of choosing strong shinobi for Kage and sealing tailed beasts into capable hosts converges in him. The Fourth Databook states he mastered Isobu's power and became a perfect jinchūriki, a feat that made him both village leader and living weapon. His youth suggests either prodigious talent or political exigency after the Third Mizukage's death. Little is shown of his inauguration, but his appointment marks Kirigakure's attempt to stabilize after war. Yagura becomes the youngest Kage of his generation and Kirigakure's ultimate military asset. His dual role sets up the village's later isolation and his personal tragedy - being both ruler and vessel makes him a prime target for manipulation.

The Bloody Mist ReignNaruto manga Ch. 404, 507; Naruto Shippuden Ep. 251; Mei Terumi and Kisame dialogue

In-universe: ~14-10 years before Part I (Yagura's tenure); Publication: 2008-2012

Under Yagura's rule, Kirigakure earns the epithet 'Village of the Bloody Mist' (Chi no Kiri). The village institutes brutal practices: the graduation exam where academy students must kill their classmates to become genin (as experienced by Zabuza Momochi, who killed his entire class), and purges of clans with Kekkei Genkai. Kisame Hoshigaki, then a loyal Kirigakure shinobi, is ordered to kill his own comrades to prevent intel leaks. Mei Terumi later describes this era as a dark age that the village had to overcome. It is later revealed this cruelty was not Yagura's will but the result of Obito's genjutsu steering his policies toward destabilization. Mass trauma, defection of powerful shinobi (Kisame, Zabuza), international isolation of Kirigakure, and near-civil war. The Bloody Mist label haunts the village into the Fifth Mizukage's reforms. Yagura's legacy is initially seen as tyrannical until the truth of his manipulation emerges.

Manipulation by the Masked Man - The Mizukage as PuppetNaruto manga Ch. 404 (Jiraiya/Ao intel), Ch. 507 (Kisame flashback), Ch. 559 (Ao identifies Sharingan chakra)

In-universe: during Yagura's reign; revealed 2008-2012 publication

Obito Uchiha, operating under the name Madara Uchiha, places Yagura under a powerful Sharingan genjutsu and effectively rules Kirigakure through him. Kisame discovers the truth when he meets the masked man after killing his superior; the masked man reveals he is controlling the Fourth Mizukage, and Kisame pledges loyalty to his 'world without lies.' Years later, Ao - who obtained a Byakugan from a Hyūga he defeated - sees through the genjutsu with his stolen eye during a confrontation and, with help, dispels it, freeing Yagura. Ao's Byakugan detects chakra identical to Shisui Uchiha's? No, to the Sharingan's chakra signature, confirming Uchiha involvement. Kisame and Ao provide the two eyewitness accounts that bookend the control. Explains the Bloody Mist's irrational cruelty and Kirigakure's manipulation by Akatsuki. Kisame's defection to Akatsuki is directly tied to this revelation. Ao's feat makes him a hero and leads to his role as sensor and Mizukage's aide. Yagura is freed but politically weakened.

Accounts II
Encounter with Kisame and the Fracturing of LoyaltyNaruto manga Ch. 507; Anime Shippuden Ep. 251

In-universe: late Yagura era; Publication 2010

Yagura, still under control, tasks Kisame's unit with sensitive missions. Kisame, disillusioned after being forced to kill his comrade Fuguki Suikazan (his superior in the Seven Ninja Swordsmen) to protect codes, is confronted by Yagura himself. Before Yagura can punish him, the masked man (Obito) appears, reveals he is the true controller of the Mizukage, and stops Yagura. Kisame, seeing a world where truth is controlled, chooses to follow the masked man, becoming a missing-nin and later joining Akatsuki. This scene is Yagura's most extended dialogue in the manga, showing him as stern and authoritative even while puppeted. Kisame's path to Akatsuki is cemented; Kirigakure loses one of its strongest swordsmen. The scene confirms to readers that the Mizukage is not acting alone.

Death and the Loss of IsobuNaruto manga Ch. 404, 507, 572 (Tobi/Deidara dialogue); Third Databook timeline

In-universe: approx. 6-4 years before Part I (before Akatsuki captures other jinchūriki); Publication 2008-2012

At some point after being freed from Obito's control, Yagura dies. The exact circumstances are never fully depicted in manga canon. What is attested: Isobu is later found in the wild, without a host, and is eventually captured by Deidara and Tobi (Obito) for Akatsuki. Ao states Yagura died, and Mei becomes Fifth Mizukage, undertaking reforms. The implication is that Akatsuki extracted Isobu from Yagura, which is normally fatal to a jinchūriki, or that he died from other consequences of the extraction/control. Unlike other jinchūriki deaths shown on-panel (e.g., Rin, Yugito), Yagura's death occurs off-panel and is reported secondhand. Isobu's wild state is unique among tailed beasts at that point, roaming a lake. Kirigakure loses its jinchūriki and Kage, leading to a period of instability and Mei's reformist leadership. Isobu's capture later contributes to Akatsuki's near-completion of the tailed-beast collection and the eventual resurrection of the Ten-Tails.

Reanimation in the Fourth Great Ninja WarNaruto manga Ch. 565-572, 572-573; Anime Shippuden Ep. 324, 353-354, 565-568

In-universe: Fourth Great Ninja War (approx. age 16 Naruto); Publication 2011-2012

Kabuto Yakushi, using Edo Tensei (Reanimation Jutsu), resurrects Yagura along with five other deceased jinchūriki (Roshi, Han, Yugito, Utakata, Fū) as an army under Obito's control. Each is fitted with Rinnegan and Sharingan eyes and chakra receivers, allowing Obito to control them as his Six Paths of Pain. Yagura fights Naruto Uzumaki and Killer Bee, using Water Mirror and coral techniques and transforming into Isobu. Naruto, now able to sense emotions via Kurama's chakra, enters the mental plane of the tailed beasts and meets Yagura and Isobu inside. Yagura, regaining his personality, acknowledges Naruto, tells him that Isobu is shy but strong, and entrusts his will to Naruto. When Itachi Uchiha ends Edo Tensei, Yagura's soul is released after a final conversation where all jinchūriki give Naruto their chakra and names. Yagura's character is humanized; readers learn his gentle true nature and his bond with Isobu. His chakra gift helps Naruto later achieve cooperation with all tailed beasts. Yagura was inherently cruel and the architect of the Bloody Mist out of personal sadism. — Canon explicitly attributes the worst excesses of the Bloody Mist to Obito's genjutsu controlling Yagura. The real Yagura, seen when freed and as Edo Tensei, is polite and concerned for his village. He bears historical responsibility but not personal sadistic intent.

Yagura is Isobu's first and only jinchūriki, and Isobu was always wild before him. — Isobu had at least one prior jinchūriki: Rin Nohara was made Isobu's jinchūriki by Kirigakure as a plot to destroy Konoha (Ch. 604-605), before Yagura. Yagura is the most famous host and the Fourth Mizukage, but not the first. After Yagura's death Isobu was wild, then later sealed into new hosts in Boruto era.

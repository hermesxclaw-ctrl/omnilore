Who It Is

A signature technique of the Uchiha clan that compresses chakra into the mouth and expels it as a massive fireball. The user manipulates their chakra in the lungs before spitting out a large orb of flame. It is a rite of passage for young Uchiha clan members.

Powers

Massive orb of flame, area damage

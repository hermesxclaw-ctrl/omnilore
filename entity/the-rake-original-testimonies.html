Who It Is

The original Rake documents — fictional testimonies from 1691 to 2006 describing encounters with a pale, hairless humanoid that enters bedrooms at night and watches sleepers. Unlike later depictions, the original accounts emphasize passivity and the horror of being observed — the creature does not attack; it watches. The 1691 mariner's log: 'He say nothing. He watch. He is the shape of a man but not a man.'

Form

Consistent across testimonies: humanoid, 1.5m tall, hairless, pale gray-white skin, large forward-facing reflective eyes, no nose, lipless mouth, six-fingered hands. Crouches at foot of beds and watches. Whispers, but never in English.

Powers

Silent entry into locked rooms

Watcher behavior over multiple nights

Whispering inducing psychological breakdown

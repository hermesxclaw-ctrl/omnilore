Who It Is
Form

The Rake is often described as a tall, thin figure with a twisted, elongated head and a long, curved beak-like nose. Its eyes are said to be sunken and its skin is often described as being pale and decaying. The Rake's appearance is often associated with death and decay.

Powers

Soul Harvesting The Rake is said to have the ability to harvest the souls of the living, using its rake or pitchfork to collect the spirits of its victims.

Incident Log

The Rake first appeared in a creepypasta story on the internet in 2005. The story told of a man who had encountered The Rake in a abandoned asylum, where it had been harvesting the souls of the dead.

The Rake's origins are shrouded in mystery, but it is believed to have originated from internet folklore. The Rake is said to have been created by a group of internet users who were experimenting with the concept of a supernatural entity.

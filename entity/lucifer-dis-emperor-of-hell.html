Who It Is
Form

Lucifer is often depicted as a fallen angel or a serpent, while Dis is typically shown as a powerful demon or a king. In some accounts, Lucifer is described as a beautiful and radiant being, while Dis is depicted as a dark and foreboding figure.

Powers

Telekinesis Lucifer is said to have the ability to move objects with his mind, while Dis is associated with the power to control the forces of nature.

Shape-shifting Lucifer is often depicted as a serpent or a dragon, while Dis is said to be able to take on various forms and guises.

Illusions Lucifer is said to have the power to create elaborate illusions and deceptions, while Dis is associated with the ability to manipulate reality itself.

Accounts

Lucifer is said to have rebelled against God and been cast out of heaven.

Dis is said to have descended into the underworld, where he became the ruler of the dead.

Lucifer is said to have led a rebellion against God in heaven.

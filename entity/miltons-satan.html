Deception

Milton's Satan has the ability to deceive and manipulate others, using his charm, wit, and cunning to achieve his goals. He is a master of language and persuasion, able to twist words and meanings to suit his purposes.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Milton's Satan is often depicted as a majestic and powerful figure, with wings and a regal demeanor. He is described as a fallen angel, cast out of heaven for his rebellion against God. His appearance is often associated with darkness, fire, and chaos.

Symbols — The serpent, the dragon, and the fallen angel are all symbols associated with Milton's Satan. These images convey his role as a tempter and a deceiver, as well as his association with evil and darkness.

Domains — Milton's Satan inhabits the realms of heaven and hell, as well as the mortal world. He is a wanderer, a traveler, and a trickster, moving between different domains and habitats with ease.

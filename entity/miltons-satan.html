Who It Is
Form

Milton's Satan is often depicted as a majestic and powerful figure, with wings and a regal demeanor. He is described as a fallen angel, cast out of heaven for his rebellion against God. His appearance is often associated with darkness, fire, and chaos.

Powers

Deception Milton's Satan has the ability to deceive and manipulate others, using his charm, wit, and cunning to achieve his goals. He is a master of language and persuasion, able to twist words and meanings to suit his purposes.

Accounts

Milton's Satan tempts Eve to eat the forbidden fruit, leading to the fall of humanity from a state of innocence and paradise.

Milton's Satan leads a rebellion against God, challenging his authority and power. The rebellion is ultimately unsuccessful, and Satan is cast out of heaven.

Pronunciation MAH-NAH-TSU NAH-TSU-UU-MI. In Japanese, each syllable is pronounced distinctly: Ma-na-tsu Na-tsu-u-mi.

Etymology

The name is a compound of Japanese elements: 'Manatsu' (真夏) meaning 'mid-summer' or 'height of summer', and 'Natsuumi' (夏海) meaning 'summer sea'. Together, the name evokes the peak of summer and the sea, suggesting a character associated with warmth, vitality, and the ocean. It is a plausible Japanese given name, though uncommon as a full name.

Who It Is

Deep in the Swiss Alps, where the peaks pierce the clouds and the forests are thick with shadow, there is said to live a creature no bigger than a child, yet strong enough to move boulders. They call it the Wildmannli—the little wild man—and those who have met it speak of a being that is both shy and strangely kind, a guardian of the mountains who asks only for a bit of bread in return for its help.

The Wildmannli is generally shy and elusive, avoiding human contact. It is known to be helpful on occasion, such as guiding lost travelers or assisting with chores, but can also be mischievous or vengeful if provoked. It is said to live in remote mountain areas, in caves or under rocks.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Swiss German diminutive of 'Wildmann' (wild man), with the suffix '-li' indicating smallness or endearment. Literally 'little wild man'.

Pronunciation — VILT-mahn-lee

Aliases — Wildmännli, Wildmannlein, Wildmännchen

Ownership — Swiss (Alpine), specifically the German-speaking regions of Switzerland, including the cantons of Bern, Uri, Schwyz, and Graubünden.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Wildmannli is typically described as a small, dwarf-like being covered in hair or fur, sometimes with a beard. It may wear simple clothing made of moss or leaves, or go naked. It is often depicted with a staff or club.

Symbols — In Swiss heraldry and woodcarvings, wild men are often shown with a club and a shield, but the Wildmannli as a dwarf variant is less commonly depicted. It may be associated with mountain caves and forests.

Domains — Alpine regions of Switzerland, including high mountain passes, forests, and caves.

Accounts I
The Helpful WildmannliSwiss folklore collection

19th century

A lost shepherd encounters a Wildmannli who guides him back to his path and even helps him find a lost sheep. The Wildmannli asks for a piece of bread in return. The shepherd returns home safely and tells the story, spreading belief in the creature.

Accounts II
The Vengeful WildmannliSwiss folklore collection

19th century

A hunter shoots at a Wildmannli, wounding it. In revenge, the Wildmannli causes rocks to fall, nearly killing the hunter. The hunter barely escapes and never hunts in that area again. The hunter is scared away and the area is considered cursed.

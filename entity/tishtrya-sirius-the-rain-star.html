Who It Is
Form

Tishtrya is often depicted as a radiant, star-like figure, with a bow and arrow, and sometimes accompanied by a dog or a lion.

Powers

Rain-Bringer Tishtrya has the power to bring rain and fertility to the land, using his bow and arrow to dispel drought and bring life to the crops.

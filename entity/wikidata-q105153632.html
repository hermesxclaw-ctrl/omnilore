The Approach

Kento Nanami is the sorcerer who walked away from the world of jujutsu only to return to it, bound by a sense of duty that he could not shake. In a series where power often corrupts and ambition destroys, Nanami stands as a quiet, methodical figure—a man who clocks in and out of his supernatural job, yet whose dedication to protecting the innocent proves as unwavering as his blade.

Content advisory. Depictions of violence, injury, death, and psychological trauma. The character's story includes themes of workplace disillusionment, survivor's guilt, and the cost of heroism.
Identity

Kento Nanami

Pronunciation KEN-toh NAH-nah-mee

Etymology

'Nanami' (七海) is a Japanese surname meaning 'seven seas'. 'Kento' (建人) combines 'build' and 'person', suggesting 'builder of people'. The name's meaning is not explicitly addressed in the series.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese popular culture; created by Gege Akutami for the manga Jujutsu Kaisen, serialized in Weekly Shonen Jump since 2018.

Disputed Identifications

None known. The character is unique to Jujutsu Kaisen and has no direct counterpart in other works.

Description

Appearance

Kento Nanami is a tall, adult man with blond hair styled neatly, often wearing a dark suit and tie, reflecting his former office worker background. He has a distinctive scar on his left cheek, a reminder of his time as a sorcerer. His eyes are sharp, and he carries a blunt-edged sword wrapped in cloth, which he uses in combat.

Symbols & Iconography

His signature weapon is a blunt sword, which he uses to deliver powerful, precise strikes. His suit and tie symbolize his pragmatic, professional approach to sorcery. The scar on his face is a symbol of his past battles and his commitment to his duty.

Behaviour & Personality

Nanami is calm, stoic, and methodical. He approaches sorcery as a job, with a strong sense of responsibility and a desire to protect the innocent. He is often seen as a mentor figure, especially to Yuji Itadori, offering practical advice and guidance. He is pragmatic, sometimes cynical, but deeply caring, willing to sacrifice himself for the greater good.

Domains & Habitats

Nanami operates within the modern world of Jujutsu Kaisen, primarily in Tokyo. He works as a grade 1 jujutsu sorcerer, often taking on missions to exorcise curses. He has a part-time job at a convenience store, reflecting his desire for a normal life.

Version Differences

In the manga, Nanami's death is depicted in chapter 120, while in the anime it occurs in season 2, episode 21. The anime expands on some scenes, but the core characterization remains consistent.

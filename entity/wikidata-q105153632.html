Who It Is

Kento Nanami is the sorcerer who walked away from the world of jujutsu only to return to it, bound by a sense of duty that he could not shake. In a series where power often corrupts and ambition destroys, Nanami stands as a quiet, methodical figure—a man who clocks in and out of his supernatural job, yet whose dedication to protecting the innocent proves as unwavering as his blade.

Nanami is calm, stoic, and methodical. He approaches sorcery as a job, with a strong sense of responsibility and a desire to protect the innocent. He is often seen as a mentor figure, especially to Yuji Itadori, offering practical advice and guidance. He is pragmatic, sometimes cynical, but deeply caring, willing to sacrifice himself for the greater good.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — 'Nanami' (七海) is a Japanese surname meaning 'seven seas'. 'Kento' (建人) combines 'build' and 'person', suggesting 'builder of people'. The name's meaning is not explicitly addressed in the series.

Pronunciation — KEN-toh NAH-nah-mee

Aliases — Kento Nanami

Ownership — Japanese popular culture; created by Gege Akutami for the manga Jujutsu Kaisen, serialized in Weekly Shonen Jump since 2018.

Powers
Cursed Energy Manipulation

As a jujutsu sorcerer, Nanami can manipulate cursed energy to enhance his physical abilities and perform techniques.

Limit: Requires training and focus; cursed energy is finite.

Ratio Technique

Nanami's innate technique allows him to create a weak point in an object or enemy, dividing it into 7:3 ratio. Striking that point causes massive damage, often splitting the target in half.

Limit: Requires precise calculation and can only affect a limited area at a time.

Overtime (Binding Vow)

A binding vow that increases Nanami's cursed energy output during overtime hours, at the cost of reduced output during regular hours. This reflects his office worker background.

Limit: Only activates during overtime; if he works too much, he becomes exhausted.

Domain Expansion: Unknown

Nanami never demonstrates a domain expansion in the series, and it is unknown if he has one.

Limit: Unknown

Form & Signs

Appearance — Kento Nanami is a tall, adult man with blond hair styled neatly, often wearing a dark suit and tie, reflecting his former office worker background. He has a distinctive scar on his left cheek, a reminder of his time as a sorcerer. His eyes are sharp, and he carries a blunt-edged sword wrapped in cloth, which he uses in combat.

Symbols — His signature weapon is a blunt sword, which he uses to deliver powerful, precise strikes. His suit and tie symbolize his pragmatic, professional approach to sorcery. The scar on his face is a symbol of his past battles and his commitment to his duty.

Domains — Nanami operates within the modern world of Jujutsu Kaisen, primarily in Tokyo. He works as a grade 1 jujutsu sorcerer, often taking on missions to exorcise curses. He has a part-time job at a convenience store, reflecting his desire for a normal life.

Accounts I
Introduction and Return to SorceryJujutsu Kaisen manga chapters 15-16; anime episodes 5-6

2018

Nanami is introduced as a former salaryman who returns to jujutsu sorcery after a colleague dies. He meets Yuji Itadori and Megumi Fushiguro on a mission, where he demonstrates his skill and mentorship. Nanami becomes a mentor to Yuji, teaching him about the value of life and the importance of protecting others.

Accounts II
The Shibuya IncidentJujutsu Kaisen manga chapters 117-125; anime season 2 episodes 20-21

2021

During the Shibuya Incident, Nanami fights against cursed spirits and is ultimately killed by Mahito. His death is a pivotal moment, deeply affecting Yuji and other characters. Nanami's death motivates Yuji to continue fighting and leads to Mahito's eventual defeat.

Involvement in the Cursed Womb ArcJujutsu Kaisen manga chapters 19-21; anime episodes 11-12

2018

Nanami assists Yuji and Megumi in confronting the cursed wombs, demonstrating his combat prowess and strategic thinking. The mission succeeds, and Nanami further solidifies his role as a reliable senior sorcerer.

Who It Is

A human male (designated SCP-507-A) who involuntarily shifts between parallel dimensions every few days. He has no control over when or where he shifts. He has visited dimensions where humanity never evolved, where the Foundation rules the world, where gravity is absent, and where everything is made of meat. He always returns to our dimension — though sometimes he returns with new scars, new trauma, and stories of dimensions that should never have existed.

Form

An adult human male, physically unremarkable except for a 'fractal scar' pattern on his back — a marking that appeared after a shift to an unknown dimension. He keeps a detailed journal of every dimension he visits.

Powers

Involuntary dimensional shifting

Automatic return to home dimension

Survival across radically different physical laws

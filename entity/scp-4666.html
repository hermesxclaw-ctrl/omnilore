Who It Is

SCP-4666 is an anomalous humanoid that manifests during the nights of December 21st through January 2nd, targeting isolated households in northern latitudes during the Christmas holiday season. The entity resembles an extremely tall, gaunt, elderly Caucasian male with pale, waxy skin, completely naked. Its most notable feature is its unnaturally long limbs, which allow it to move with a spider-like gait across snow-covered ground. The entity is only active when a household meets specific criteria: the family must be entirely isolated (nearest neighbors at least 5 kilometers away), the children must be under 12 years old, and the house must be decorated for Christmas. When these conditions are met, SCP-4666 watches the household for up to eleven nights, observing from outside windows. On the twelfth night (typically Christmas Eve), it enters the home. The entity systematically kills the parents, then abducts the children, taking them to an unknown location — presumably its lair or pocket dimension. Evidence suggests the children are used for forced labor in a twisted parody of Santa's workshop, manufacturing crude toys from human remains. The entity leaves behind a single 'gift' in the home — a toy crudely assembled from bones, hair, and teeth. The Foundation became aware of SCP-4666 through a pattern of unsolved family disappearances spanning over 100 years across Norway, Sweden, Russia, Canada, and Alaska. Early cases were dismissed as isolated tragedies or folklore. The entity is extremely difficult to track or intercept due to its limited active window and unpredictable pattern. The Foundation maintains monitoring stations in high-risk areas and has developed an early-warning network, but prevention remains challenging — families must simply not be alone and decorated for Christmas. SCP-4666 is a dark corruption of Santa Claus mythology, transforming the joy of Christmas into a season of predation, and it stands as one of the most viscerally disturbing entries in the SCP canon.

Who It Is
Form

SCP-4666 is described as a single, exceptionally long-lived humanoid entity of unknown origin. Survivors of Weissnacht Events typically describe SCP-4666 as being between 1.8 to 2.1 meters tall, with an average weight of 70 to 80 kilograms. Its appearance is often described as gaunt and emaciated, with long silver hair and sunken eyes.

Powers

Weissnacht Abilities SCP-4666 possesses the ability to inflict incapacitating injuries on its victims, often through the use of its long silver hair or sunken eyes. It is also able to manipulate the environment, creating areas of extreme cold and darkness to disorient and confuse its victims.

Incident Log

Who It Is

Before the Taken King, there was a prince who slew his own father to escape the brutal logic of the Hive. Oryx, the Taken King, is not merely a conqueror but a theologian of the sword, a being who carved his existence from the deaths of worlds and his own kin. His arrival in the Solar System was an apocalypse, and his death reshaped the very nature of the Hive and the Taken. To understand Oryx is to understand the nihilistic, sword-logic-driven cosmos that Destiny pits against the Light.

Oryx is calculating, patient, and utterly ruthless. He follows the Sword Logic, a philosophy that existence is a struggle where only the strongest deserve to exist. He is a strategist and a scholar, having spent millennia exploring the universe and learning its secrets. He is also a father, though his love is twisted by his beliefs. He killed his own son, Crota, and daughter, Ir Anûk, to test his power and to feed his worm.

Powers
Sword Logic

A metaphysical principle that states that existence is a zero-sum game: to exist is to prove one's worth by overcoming others. Oryx gains power by killing, and he can take the 'essence' of the slain to strengthen himself. This is not a conventional power but a worldview that grants him immense power through violence.

Limit: Sword Logic requires constant killing to maintain power. If Oryx stops conquering, his worm (which demands tribute) will consume him.

Taking

Oryx can reach into the Ascendant Realm and 'take' a being, remaking it into a Taken. This process strips the individual's will and corrupts them, turning them into a servant of the Darkness. Taken retain their abilities but are twisted and subservient.

Limit: Oryx must first defeat the being in combat or otherwise prove its weakness. The process requires a connection to the Deep and a mastery of the Ascendant Realm.

Ascendant Realm mastery

Oryx has a throne world within the Ascendant Realm, a dimension where he can exist as long as his tribute continues. He can manipulate this realm, create structures, and even resurrect himself if killed in the material world, as long as his throne world remains.

Limit: If Oryx is killed in his throne world, he dies permanently. This is the key to his final defeat.

Supernatural strength and durability

As a Hive god, Oryx possesses immense physical power, able to fight Guardians and even destroy planets with his sword. He is also highly resistant to conventional damage, requiring the Light to harm him.

Limit: He is vulnerable to the Light and to attacks that exploit the Sword Logic.

Communication with the Deep

Oryx can speak directly to the Darkness (the Deep) and receive guidance. This allows him to learn secrets and gain power, but it also makes him a servant of the Darkness.

Limit: The Deep demands constant tribute and adherence to its will.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Oryx is a massive Hive Knight, standing over 20 feet tall. He wears ornate, black and green armor with glowing green eyes and a large, jagged sword. His face is a skeletal, insectoid visage with mandibles. He has two large wings made of dark energy, and his body is surrounded by a faint, writhing aura of Taken energy. In his final battle, he appears as a towering figure with a massive sword, and his body is partially translucent with Dark energy.

Symbols — Oryx's symbol is a stylized, angular crown, often depicted in Hive architecture and on his armor. The Taken are represented by a swirling, black-and-white smoky energy. The Books of Sorrow are a central text, symbolizing his authority and the Hive's origin.

Domains — Oryx's throne world, the Dreadnaught, is a massive ship carved from a dead worm god, Akka. It exists within the Ascendant Realm, a dimension of paracausal space where thought and will shape reality. He also commands the Taken, corrupted versions of beings from across the universe.

Accounts I
The Origin of the HiveBooks of Sorrow

Pre-Collapse, ancient times

Oryx was born as Aurash, a proto-Hive on the planet Fundament. She (as Aurash was female) and her sisters, Sathona and Xi Ro, sought to escape the planet's doom. They were tricked by the Worm Gods into making a pact with the Deep, transforming into Hive. Aurash became Auryx, then Oryx, and they led the Hive to conquer the universe. The Hive became a galaxy-spanning empire dedicated to the Sword Logic. Oryx killed his father, the Osmium King, to begin his journey, and later killed his own son Crota to gain power.

Accounts II
The Taken King's InvasionDestiny: The Taken King

Year 2 of the Destiny timeline

Oryx arrives in the Solar System to avenge the death of his son Crota. He takes control of the Hive and corrupts other races into Taken. He attacks the Last City, but is ultimately defeated by the Guardian who kills him in his throne world. Oryx's death fragments his power, leading to the rise of his sisters Savathûn and Xivu Arath. The Taken remain a threat, now under the control of other entities.

The First DisciplesDestiny 2: The Witch Queen

Ancient times, before the Hive's arrival in the Solar System

Lore reveals that Oryx was one of the first Disciples of the Witness, a servant who embraced the Darkness. This suggests that Oryx's actions were part of a larger plan orchestrated by the Witness. This revelation reframes Oryx's role in the cosmic conflict, linking him to the Witness's agenda.

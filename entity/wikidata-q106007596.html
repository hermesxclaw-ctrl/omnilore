Who It Is

In the irradiated ruins of the Commonwealth, one man still wears the crisp blue uniform of the Commonwealth Minutemen, a symbol of hope that refuses to fade. Preston Garvey is that man—a survivor of the Quincy massacre, a steadfast companion, and the relentless voice of a faction that dreams of rebuilding civilization from the ashes. His story is one of loss, duty, and the unyielding belief that ordinary people can make a difference, even in a world gone mad.

Preston is idealistic, compassionate, and deeply committed to helping others. He often puts the needs of the Commonwealth above his own, leading to self-sacrificial behavior. He is optimistic but can be weary and disillusioned after the Quincy massacre. He is loyal to his friends and is a natural leader, though he struggles with survivor's guilt.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Preston' is of English origin, meaning 'priest's town' or 'from the priest's estate'. 'Garvey' is an Irish surname derived from the Gaelic 'Ó Gairbheith', meaning 'rough' or 'noble'. The name may reflect his role as a leader and protector.

Pronunciation — PRES-ton GAR-vee

Aliases — Preston Garvey

Ownership — Preston Garvey is a fictional character created by Bethesda Game Studios for the Fallout series, specifically Fallout 4. He is owned by ZeniMax Media and Bethesda Softworks.

Powers
Laser Musket Proficiency

Preston is skilled in using the laser musket, a weapon iconic to the Minutemen. He can effectively use it in combat, and it can be modified to increase its power.

Limit: The laser musket requires charging to reach full power, leaving Preston vulnerable during charging.

Leadership

Preston is a natural leader and can rally settlers to defend settlements. He provides moral support and strategic guidance.

Limit: His leadership is limited by the number of settlers and resources available.

Companion Perk: United We Stand

When Preston idolizes the player, he grants the 'United We Stand' perk, which increases damage dealt and reduces damage taken when facing three or more enemies.

Limit: The perk only activates when the player is outnumbered.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Preston Garvey is a man in his early 30s with dark hair and brown eyes. He wears the signature blue and tan uniform of the Commonwealth Minutemen, complete with a tricorn hat. The uniform is worn and torn, reflecting his years of hardship. He carries a laser musket, a weapon that embodies the Minutemen's spirit.

Symbols — The Minutemen's symbol is a stylized musket, often depicted on flags and uniforms. Preston's tricorn hat and blue coat are iconic representations of the faction's ideals of liberty and community defense.

Domains — Preston is primarily found in the Commonwealth, particularly at the settlement of Sanctuary Hills after the player meets him. He travels with the Sole Survivor if taken as a companion. He is comfortable in both urban ruins and rural settlements, embodying the Minutemen's role as protectors of the people.

Accounts I
The Quincy MassacreFallout 4

2287

Preston Garvey was a member of the Commonwealth Minutemen stationed in Quincy. The town was attacked by Gunners, a mercenary group. The Minutemen were overwhelmed, and many, including Preston's friends, were killed. Preston and a group of survivors, including Mama Murphy and the Marowski brothers, fled to Concord, where they took refuge in the Museum of Freedom. The Quincy Massacre led to the near-dissolution of the Minutemen. Preston became the de facto leader of a small group of survivors, and his encounter with the Sole Survivor at the Museum of Freedom set the main story of Fallout 4 in motion.

The Sole Survivor's ArrivalFallout 4

2287

Preston and his group are trapped in the Museum of Freedom in Concord, under attack by raiders. The Sole Survivor arrives and helps them fight off the raiders, including a Deathclaw. Grateful, Preston offers the Sole Survivor the rank of General of the Minutemen, hoping they will lead the faction to rebuild. The Sole Survivor becomes the General of the Minutemen, and Preston becomes their loyal companion and advisor. This marks the beginning of the Minutemen questline.

Accounts II
The Minutemen RebuildingFallout 4

2287

Under the Sole Survivor's leadership, Preston helps establish settlements across the Commonwealth, defending them from raiders, super mutants, and other threats. He provides the player with radiant quests to aid settlements and recruit new Minutemen. The Minutemen are rebuilt into a powerful faction, capable of defending the Commonwealth and eventually taking on the Institute.

The Battle of Bunker HillFallout 4

2287

If the player sides with the Minutemen, Preston participates in the Battle of Bunker Hill, a conflict between the Institute, Railroad, and Brotherhood of Steel. The Minutemen can play a role in the battle depending on the player's choices. The battle's outcome can affect the balance of power in the Commonwealth.

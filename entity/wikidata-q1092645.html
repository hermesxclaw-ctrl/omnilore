Who It Is

In a classroom of prodigies, martial artists, vampires and time-travelers, Satsuki Yotsuba is the quiet miracle - the girl who would rather feed you than fight you. While her 30 classmates chase pacts, prophecies and interdimensional wars, Satsuki stands at a steaming wok in the Chao Bao Zi, headband askew, smiling as if the fate of the world could be solved with one more perfect plate of fried rice. She is Negima!'s gentlest subversion: proof that in Ken Akamatsu's magic-saturated Mahora Academy, kindness and an apron can be as defining as any spell.

Canonically gentle, slow-paced, airheaded (tennen) and relentlessly kind. Speaks in soft, slightly spaced speech, rarely raises voice, almost never angry. Single-minded devotion to cooking and feeding others; judges people by appetite and happiness after eating. Lacks academic or combat ambition, content to support friends domestically. Shown as emotionally perceptive despite ditziness - often notices when classmates are troubled and offers food as comfort. Powers
Master-Class Culinary Arts (Non-Magical)

Exceptional, near-professional cooking skill despite middle-school age. Specializes in Chinese-style fried rice, omurice, bento, sweets and large-batch festival catering. Able to cook for 30+ classmates simultaneously, improvise with limited ingredients, and replicate Chao Lingshen's recipes. Recognized in-universe by Chao and classmates as best cook in 3-A, rivaling adult professionals.

Limit: No supernatural enhancement; limited by physical ingredients, time and stamina. Cannot heal or buff via food without magical artifact. Skill is domestic, not combat-applicable.

Pactio: Probationary Contract with Negi Springfield - Artifact (Coquinaria-type)

In final arc, forms Pactio (probationary contract) with Negi, gaining artifact visualized as an enormous magical kitchen / infinite pantry set: a summonable array of pots, pans, knives and ingredients that can produce and prepare food instantly, and in some interpretations generates a pocket kitchen space. Card number and Latin incantation not fully legible in tankōbon; artifact classified as support/supply type, not combat. Enables creation of nourishing food even in Magic World wastelands.

Limit: Requires Negi's magic to activate; artifact is non-lethal, provides no direct offensive/defensive power, flight or spellcasting. Limited to food/supply generation; cannot create weapons or heal major wounds. As with all late-series Pactios, contract is temporary and artifact disappears after Magic World crisis (Mile Vincula arc).

Domestic Support & Morale Enhancement (Narrative Ability)

Not a formal magic, but recurring narrative function: her food restores stamina, lifts morale, and enables other characters to continue fighting/studying. In Magic World arc, her bento supply allows Earth-bound group to maintain normalcy. Functions as logistical backbone for class.

Limit: Purely mundane psychological/logistical effect; no quantified stat boost. Dependent on others being willing to eat.

Accounts I
Introduction: The Quiet Cook of 3-ANegima! Magister Negi Magi Chapter 12-15 (Vol.2)

2003 (publication), in-universe Spring 2003 (Negi's arrival)

Negi Springfield takes over as homeroom teacher of 31 girls. Satsuki is introduced in class roster and early classroom scenes as the soft-spoken girl who brings homemade snacks. In Chapter 12, she offers Negi a bento when he collapses from overwork, establishing her core trait: feeding others as care. No major conflict; establishes her as background healer archetype. Establishes Satsuki's role as class cook and gentle confidante; sets up later part-time job at Chao's restaurant.

Chao Bao Zi Part-Time Job ArcNegima! Chapters 18-60 intermittent (Vol.3-7), especially Chapter 36

2003-2004 publication; in-universe Spring-Summer 2003

Satsuki begins working part-time at Chao Lingshen's restaurant Chao Bao Zi, learning Chinese cuisine and serving as waitress/kitchen help. She is shown balancing school, cooking club and work, often bringing leftovers to class. Chao, the super-genius martial artist from the future, mentors her cooking while Satsuki remains oblivious to Chao's secret inventions and future plot. The job explains her frequent absences from combat training and her practical skill growth. Deepens Satsuki-Chao bond; provides in-universe reason for her cooking mastery; foreshadows Chao's later festival plot where Satsuki's restaurant becomes plot location.

Kyoto School Trip ArcNegima! Chapters 68-89 (Vol.8-10)

2004 publication; in-universe Summer 2003

Class 3-A travels to Kyoto. While main action focuses on Negi, Asuna, Konoka and Setsuna vs. Kansai magic association, Satsuki is part of the non-combatant group sightseeing and cooking for the group at the inn. She prepares a large Kyoto-style dinner for the class after the battle, unknowingly healing minor injuries and exhaustion. Her calm presence contrasts with the arc's violence. Reinforces her non-combatant status; shows class relies on her for normalcy after supernatural crises. No direct involvement in magic battle, highlighting division between fighters and supporters.

Accounts II
Mahora Festival Arc - The Ultimate Food StallNegima! Chapters 107-168 (Vol.12-19) - Mahora Academy Festival

2005-2006 publication; in-universe Autumn 2003

During the 3-day Mahora Festival, Class 3-A runs multiple stalls. Satsuki co-manages the food stall with Chao and the cooking club, producing massive quantities of yakisoba, takoyaki and Chao Bao Zi buns for thousands of visitors. During Chao's time-travel/time-stop plot to reveal magic to the world, Satsuki continues cooking unaware of the temporal chaos, her stall becoming a refuge where characters regroup. She is briefly caught in Chao's magic disruption but is protected by classmates. Festival arc is longest in series; Satsuki's stall provides logistical anchor. Chao's defeat and departure affects Satsuki emotionally - she loses her employer/mentor. Class acknowledges her contribution in festival wrap-up.

Magic World Arc - Those Who Stay BehindNegima! Chapters 200-290 (Vol.24-32)

2007-2010 publication; in-universe 2003-2004 (Magic World time dilation)

When Negi and 15 classmates travel to the Magic World to find Negi's father, Satsuki is among the 15 who remain on Earth due to parental permission / non-combatant status. She stays at Mahora, keeps Chao Bao Zi running (now under new management), and cooks for the remaining students and for the returnees' families. She appears in interlude chapters (e.g., Chapter 250) sending care packages via magic mail and praying for safety. Her absence from battles emphasizes Akamatsu's theme that not everyone must become a warrior. Highlights division of class into fighters vs. supporters; Satsuki's domestic labor keeps Earth-side morale stable. Upon return, she welcomes travelers with feast (Chapter 289).

Final Crisis and Pactio - The Last ContractsNegima! Chapters 314-320 (Vol.35) - Battle of Ostia / Fate's Plan

2011 publication; in-universe 2004

During the final battle against the Mage of the Beginning and Fate Averruncus, Negi forms emergency probationary Pactios with the remaining non-contracted students to boost collective power. Satsuki, despite lacking combat ability, steps forward to form contract, declaring she wants to help in the only way she can - by feeding everyone even on a battlefield. Her Pactio card is revealed among the final batch (Chapter 318 spread), artifact manifesting as a grand kitchen array. She uses it to supply food and warmth to refugees in the collapsing Magic World city of Ostia. Completes Satsuki's arc from mundane cook to magical supporter; confirms she is recognized as worthy partner despite non-combatant status. Contract is dissolved after crisis via Mile Vincula resolution.

Epilogue and UQ Holder! Legacy - The Adult ChefNegima! Chapter 355-358 (Vol.38) Epilogue + UQ Holder! Chapter 144-145

2012 (Negima! finale) / 2018 (UQ Holder! publication); in-universe 7 years later (2010) and 80 years later (2080s)

Seven-year epilogue shows Class 3-A as adults (age ~21). Satsuki appears in reunion illustration as a professional chef, still in apron, smiling beside her friends, indicating she pursued culinary career. In UQ Holder! (sequel set 80 years later), a photograph/cameo of adult 3-A includes Satsuki among Negi's former students, confirming she lived a normal lifespan and is remembered as part of Negi's legacy. No speaking role in UQ Holder! main plot. Provides closure: Satsuki achieves dream without needing magic fame. Connects Negima! to sequel timeline, confirming her place in franchise history.

Satsuki is just a background fanservice character with no story importance and no magic. — While non-combatant, she has continuous narrative function: Chao Bao Zi employee, festival logistics anchor, Earth-side caretaker during Magic World arc, and late-series Pactio holder. Her domestic labor is thematically framed as essential support, not filler.

Satsuki's Pactio artifact is called 'The Eternal Frying Pan' or 'Alma Coquinaria'. — No Latin/Japanese artifact name is legibly printed in Kodansha tankōbon or guidebook. The card art shows kitchen utensils but text is blurred/illegible. Those names are fanon invented to fill gap and repeated on wikis.

Satsuki is a magic user or descended from a magical family like Konoka or Asuna. — Satsuki is explicitly a normal human with no magical lineage, no ki mastery, and no prior magic exposure. Her Pactio is granted by Negi's magic, not innate. Databook lists 'Magic: None' and 'Ki: None'.

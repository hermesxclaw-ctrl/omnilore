Who It Is

In the land of Wano, where honor and vengeance collide, a young girl with a tragic past and a magical ability to befriend beasts becomes an unlikely key to the nation's liberation. Kurozumi Tama, born into a disgraced family, defies her lineage to fight for the people who once scorned her.

Despite her tragic past, Tama is cheerful, resilient, and kind. She is determined to restore her master's honor and free Wano from tyranny. She is brave and often puts herself in danger to help others.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Tama' (たま) can mean 'ball' or 'jewel' in Japanese, possibly reflecting her role as a precious asset in the conflict. 'Kurozumi' (黒炭) means 'black charcoal', a surname associated with a disgraced family in Wano.

Pronunciation — Koo-roh-zoo-mee Tah-mah

Aliases — O-Tama, Tama, Tama-chan, Kurozumi O-Tama

Ownership — One Piece is a Japanese manga and anime series created by Eiichiro Oda. The character is part of the Wano Country arc, inspired by Japanese history and culture.

Powers
Kibi Dango

Tama can create magical dumplings (kibi dango) from her cheek. When eaten by an animal, it tames the animal and makes it obey Tama's commands.

Limit: The effect only works on animals, not humans. The animals become loyal to Tama, but they may not follow complex orders. The effect is not permanent; it can wear off if the animal is defeated or if Tama is incapacitated.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Tama is a small child with large, expressive eyes and short black hair tied into a topknot. She wears a kimono with a patterned sash and geta sandals. She often has a red mark on her cheek, which is a symbol of her family's disgraced status.

Symbols — The red mark on her cheek is a symbol of the Kurozumi family's punishment. She also carries a small gourd containing kibi dango, her signature food.

Domains — Tama resides in the Kuri region of Wano Country, specifically in a small village near the ruins of the Kuri castle. She lives with her master, the former daimyo of Kuri, Shimotsuki Yasuie.

Accounts I
The Girl of KuriOne Piece manga, chapters 911-912

Wano Country arc

Luffy washes ashore in Wano and is found by Tama, who nurses him back to health. She tells him about her master, Yasuie, and the suffering of the people under Kaido's rule. She feeds Luffy a kibi dango, which tames him temporarily, but he is unaffected due to his Devil Fruit powers. Luffy becomes protective of Tama and agrees to help her. This encounter sets the stage for Luffy's involvement in the Wano conflict.

Accounts II
The Journey to the Flower CapitalOne Piece manga, chapters 913-914

Wano Country arc

Tama and Luffy travel to the Flower Capital, but on the way they are attacked by the Beast Pirates. Tama is captured and taken to a prison camp, where she is forced to work. She is later rescued by Luffy and his allies. Tama's capture highlights the cruelty of Kaido's regime and motivates Luffy to fight against it.

The Kibi Dango ArmyOne Piece manga, chapters 972-974

Wano Country arc

During the raid on Onigashima, Tama uses her kibi dango to tame numerous Gifters (animal-human hybrids), turning them against Kaido. This is a turning point in the battle, as the tide turns in favor of the alliance. Tama's actions help the alliance gain a strategic advantage, leading to Kaido's eventual defeat.

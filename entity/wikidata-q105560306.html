Who It Is

In the neon-lit chaos of the Dead or Alive tournament, one man moves with the cold precision of a scalpel. Bayman, a ghost of the Soviet military machine, has traded ideology for a mercenary's creed, and his story is a bloody ledger of assassinations, betrayals, and a relentless hunt for the truth behind a conspiracy that reaches the highest echelons of power.

Bayman is stoic, cold, and efficient. He speaks little and is driven by professional duty and personal vendettas. He is pragmatic, willing to work for anyone who pays, but he also maintains a code of honor, particularly in not harming innocents (as seen when he spares Phase 4). He is highly resilient, able to withstand torture and drug-induced interrogation.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Bayman' is a romanization of the Japanese 'バイマン' (Baiman). The intended meaning is likely 'bay man' or 'bait man', but no official etymology has been provided.

Pronunciation — BAY-man

Aliases — Baiman

Ownership — Created by Team Ninja and Tecmo for the Dead or Alive series. The character is a Russian mercenary, drawing on Soviet military archetypes.

Powers
Sambo mastery

Bayman is a master of Sambo, a Russian martial art and combat sport. He uses its throws, joint locks, and ground fighting techniques effectively.

Limit: None known.

Military combat training

As a former Spetsnaz, he is proficient in firearms, explosives, and hand-to-hand combat. He uses knives in his fighting style.

Limit: None known.

High pain tolerance

He can withstand torture and drug-induced interrogation, as shown in DOA4 when he stabs his own hand to stay conscious.

Limit: Not superhuman, but highly trained.

Stealth and assassination

He is skilled in covert operations and assassinations, having successfully killed Fame Douglas.

Limit: None known.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Bayman is a tall, muscular man with a rugged, scarred face. He typically wears tactical military gear, including a beret, a sleeveless vest, and camouflage pants. His appearance has evolved over the series, but he retains a menacing, battle-hardened look. In DOA5, he sports a more modern tactical outfit with a utility harness.

Symbols — His iconic beret and military fatigues symbolize his former Spetsnaz background. He often uses knives and grenades in combat, reinforcing his mercenary persona.

Domains — As a mercenary, he operates in various conflict zones. He has been seen in a Russian military base, an oil rig (DiG), and DOATEC facilities. He is at home in harsh, militaristic environments.

Accounts I
The Assassination of Fame DouglasDead or Alive (1996)

First DOA tournament

Bayman is hired by Victor Donovan to assassinate Fame Douglas, the organizer of the first DOA tournament. He succeeds, but becomes an outlaw. Bayman becomes a wanted criminal and gains Donovan's enmity.

The Betrayal and RevengeDead or Alive 3 (2001)

Third DOA tournament

After surviving an assassination attempt by a sniper, Bayman discovers Donovan is behind it. He enters the third tournament to retaliate against Donovan. Bayman's vendetta against Donovan begins.

Accounts II
The Fourth Tournament and CaptureDead or Alive 4 (2005)

Fourth DOA tournament

Bayman enters the fourth tournament still seeking revenge on Donovan. He fights Christie and is eventually captured by DOATEC, interrogated, and drugged. He escapes by stabbing his own hand with a pen during the chaos caused by Ayane and Hayate. Bayman survives and continues his personal war against Donovan.

The MIST InvestigationDead or Alive 5 (2012)

Fifth DOA tournament

Bayman, now working as a mercenary, investigates the massacre of his men by a Kasumi clone (Phase 4). He seeks answers from Helena, then aids Hayate and Ayane in locating MIST's hideout. He also learns of Rig's brainwashing but is disbelieved by Bass. Bayman gains knowledge of MIST's operations and Rig's situation.

The Sixth Tournament and RescueDead or Alive 6 (2019)

Sixth DOA tournament

Bayman enters the sixth tournament. He is presumed to have rescued Lisa Hamilton off-screen. He also reveals Rig's brainwashing to Bass, but Bass refuses to believe him. Bayman's role in the larger conspiracy becomes more prominent.

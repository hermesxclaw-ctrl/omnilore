Who It Is

Dr. William Birkin, after injecting himself with the G-Virus, undergoes a series of grotesque mutations throughout Resident Evil 2. The G-Virus takes over its host, continuously evolving and mutating. Birkin's forms progress from a one-armed mutant to a massive creature with a giant eye in its shoulder, and finally into an enormous, wall-crawling monstrosity.

Powers

Continuous mutation, regeneration, giant eye weak point, super strength

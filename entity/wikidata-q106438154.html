The Approach

In the hidden world of jujutsu sorcery, where curses are born from human fear and power is paid for in blood, Choso stands as a figure of impossible origin: a death painting, a half-curse, a brother forged in pain, wrestling with the humanity he was never supposed to have.

Content advisory. Contains violence, body horror, themes of experimentation and non-consensual creation, death, and psychological trauma.
Identity

Choso

Pronunciation CHOH-soh

Known As

Elder Brother of the Death PaintingsKamo Clan Experiment

Etymology

The name 'Choso' (脹相) combines characters meaning 'swelling' and 'appearance' or 'aspect', possibly reflecting his cursed nature and his role as a 'death painting' (a being born from a human and a curse).

Cultural Ownership

Japanese contemporary popular culture, specifically the manga/anime franchise 'Jujutsu Kaisen'

Disputed Identifications

None known. Choso is a unique character within the series, with no direct mythological counterpart.

Description

Appearance

Choso is a tall, slender young man with long black hair tied in two high ponytails, often styled with a hair tie. He has a distinctive scar-like mark under his eyes, which are dark. He typically wears a dark, traditional-style kimono-like outfit with a high collar, and later a modified version with a hood. His hands often show red markings or stains, reminiscent of blood.

Symbols & Iconography

His cursed technique, 'Blood Manipulation', is symbolized by the use of his own blood as a weapon. He often forms a 'Piercing Blood' attack by shaping blood into a spear. The red of blood is a recurring motif.

Behaviour & Personality

Initially appears cold and single-minded in his mission to protect his brothers, but later reveals a deep, protective love for his 'family' (the death paintings). He is serious, strategic, and surprisingly gentle with those he cares about. He values family above all, and his actions are driven by a desire to avenge and protect his brothers. He is capable of deep remorse and shows a willingness to help others when his own goals align.

Domains & Habitats

Primarily operates in modern-day Japan, in the world of jujutsu sorcerers and curses. He is often found in urban settings, but his origin is tied to a hidden, historical facility of the Kamo Clan.

Version Differences

The anime adaptation closely follows the manga. Minor differences in pacing and voice acting nuance. No significant story differences.

Powers & Abilities

Blood Manipulation

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

Choso can control his own blood, shaping it into weapons, projectiles, or using it to heal himself. He can also increase his blood pressure and flow to enhance physical abilities.

Limitations

Requires a sufficient blood supply; overuse can lead to anemia or death. He must maintain a certain blood volume.

Stories & Myths

The Birth of the Death Paintings

Source Jujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 84-89, 90-93Culture Japanese contemporaryPeriod Pre-series (over 150 years ago)

Summary

Choso and his nine brothers were created by the Kamo Clan, a prominent jujutsu family, by combining human women with the cursed spirit of Noritoshi Kamo (later known as the 'Evilest Sorcerer in History'). The women were impregnated with cursed energy, leading to the birth of the 'Death Paintings' - half-human, half-cursed beings. The clan considered them abominations and attempted to dispose of them. Choso and his brothers were sealed in a cursed object, the 'Cursed Womb: Death Paintings'.

Participants

Choso

Noritoshi Kamo

Kamo Clan

Human women

Consequences

Choso and his brothers were sealed for over 150 years. They were eventually released in the modern era, leading to Choso's quest to protect his brothers and avenge their suffering.

Variants

None known.

The Shibuya Incident

Source Jujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 84-89, 90-93, 129-136, 139-140, 145Culture Japanese contemporaryPeriod October 31, 2018 (in-universe)

Summary

Choso, along with his brothers Eso and Kechizu, are released from their cursed objects. They participate in the Shibuya Incident, a large-scale attack by curses and cursed users. Choso fights against sorcerers, notably Yuji Itadori, but later learns that Yuji is the vessel of Sukuna, a powerful curse. Choso initially seeks to kill Yuji to protect his brothers, but after a battle, he discovers that Yuji is being used by higher powers and that his own brothers have been killed. He eventually allies with Yuji and other sorcerers against the main antagonist, Kenjaku.

The Culling Game

Source Jujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 199-204, 215-221, 247-250Culture Japanese contemporaryPeriod 2018 (in-universe)

Summary

Choso participates in the Culling Game, a deadly battle royale initiated by Kenjaku. He teams up with Yuji and other sorcerers to defeat Kenjaku and his allies. Choso's motivations are to protect Yuji, who he sees as a brother figure, and to prevent Kenjaku from achieving his goals. He plays a key role in several battles, using his Blood Manipulation to aid his allies.

Non-consensual creation

Source Jujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 84-89

Description

Choso and his brothers were created by forcibly impregnating human women with cursed energy, a form of non-consensual experimentation. The women's fates are not detailed, but it is implied they suffered greatly.

Scholarly Context

This is a fictional narrative exploring themes of eugenics and the ethics of creation.

Dehumanization and discrimination

Source Jujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 84-89

Description

The death paintings are treated as abominations by the Kamo Clan and other sorcerers, leading to their sealing and attempts to destroy them.

Scholarly Context

Reflects historical and societal themes of discrimination against those deemed 'other'.

Contradictions & Disputes

Choso's humanity

Position A

Choso is a curse, not a human, and thus lacks human emotions.

Position A — evidence

His origin as a death painting, half-curse.

Position B

Choso shows human emotions, particularly love and grief, and is considered a 'half-cursed' being with human-like qualities.

Position B — evidence

His actions and emotional expressions in the manga.

Scholarly Consensus

The series presents Choso as a being with human emotions, blurring the line between curse and human.

Position A

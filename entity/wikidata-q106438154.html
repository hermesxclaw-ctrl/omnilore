Who It Is

In the hidden world of jujutsu sorcery, where curses are born from human fear and power is paid for in blood, Choso stands as a figure of impossible origin: a death painting, a half-curse, a brother forged in pain, wrestling with the humanity he was never supposed to have.

Initially appears cold and single-minded in his mission to protect his brothers, but later reveals a deep, protective love for his 'family' (the death paintings). He is serious, strategic, and surprisingly gentle with those he cares about. He values family above all, and his actions are driven by a desire to avenge and protect his brothers. He is capable of deep remorse and shows a willingness to help others when his own goals align.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Choso' (脹相) combines characters meaning 'swelling' and 'appearance' or 'aspect', possibly reflecting his cursed nature and his role as a 'death painting' (a being born from a human and a curse).

Pronunciation — CHOH-soh

Aliases — Elder Brother of the Death Paintings, Kamo Clan Experiment

Ownership — Japanese contemporary popular culture, specifically the manga/anime franchise 'Jujutsu Kaisen'

Powers
Blood Manipulation

Choso can control his own blood, shaping it into weapons, projectiles, or using it to heal himself. He can also increase his blood pressure and flow to enhance physical abilities.

Limit: Requires a sufficient blood supply; overuse can lead to anemia or death. He must maintain a certain blood volume.

Piercing Blood

A high-speed, piercing blood projectile that can be fired from his fingers. It is extremely fast and lethal.

Limit: Requires precise control and a significant amount of blood.

Superhuman Physical Attributes

As a half-curse, he possesses superhuman strength, speed, and durability, exceeding normal humans and many sorcerers.

Limit: Not explicitly stated, but he can be injured by powerful cursed techniques.

Cursed Energy Manipulation

He has a large reserve of cursed energy, which he uses to power his techniques and enhance his physical abilities.

Limit: Exhaustion can deplete his reserves.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Choso is a tall, slender young man with long black hair tied in two high ponytails, often styled with a hair tie. He has a distinctive scar-like mark under his eyes, which are dark. He typically wears a dark, traditional-style kimono-like outfit with a high collar, and later a modified version with a hood. His hands often show red markings or stains, reminiscent of blood.

Symbols — His cursed technique, 'Blood Manipulation', is symbolized by the use of his own blood as a weapon. He often forms a 'Piercing Blood' attack by shaping blood into a spear. The red of blood is a recurring motif.

Domains — Primarily operates in modern-day Japan, in the world of jujutsu sorcerers and curses. He is often found in urban settings, but his origin is tied to a hidden, historical facility of the Kamo Clan.

Accounts I
The Birth of the Death PaintingsJujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 84-89, 90-93

Pre-series (over 150 years ago)

Choso and his nine brothers were created by the Kamo Clan, a prominent jujutsu family, by combining human women with the cursed spirit of Noritoshi Kamo (later known as the 'Evilest Sorcerer in History'). The women were impregnated with cursed energy, leading to the birth of the 'Death Paintings' - half-human, half-cursed beings. The clan considered them abominations and attempted to dispose of them. Choso and his brothers were sealed in a cursed object, the 'Cursed Womb: Death Paintings'. Choso and his brothers were sealed for over 150 years. They were eventually released in the modern era, leading to Choso's quest to protect his brothers and avenge their suffering.

Accounts II
The Shibuya IncidentJujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 84-89, 90-93, 129-136, 139-140, 145

October 31, 2018 (in-universe)

Choso, along with his brothers Eso and Kechizu, are released from their cursed objects. They participate in the Shibuya Incident, a large-scale attack by curses and cursed users. Choso fights against sorcerers, notably Yuji Itadori, but later learns that Yuji is the vessel of Sukuna, a powerful curse. Choso initially seeks to kill Yuji to protect his brothers, but after a battle, he discovers that Yuji is being used by higher powers and that his own brothers have been killed. He eventually allies with Yuji and other sorcerers against the main antagonist, Kenjaku. Choso loses his brothers, but gains a new purpose. He becomes an ally to Yuji and the sorcerers, helping them in the Culling Game arc.

The Culling GameJujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 199-204, 215-221, 247-250

2018 (in-universe)

Choso participates in the Culling Game, a deadly battle royale initiated by Kenjaku. He teams up with Yuji and other sorcerers to defeat Kenjaku and his allies. Choso's motivations are to protect Yuji, who he sees as a brother figure, and to prevent Kenjaku from achieving his goals. He plays a key role in several battles, using his Blood Manipulation to aid his allies. Choso's actions help the protagonists survive and progress in their fight against Kenjaku. His status at the end of the series (as of research cutoff) is uncertain.

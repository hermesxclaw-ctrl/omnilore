Who It Is

In the Mandela Catalogue universe, the biblical story of the Annunciation is inverted: the entity that appeared to Mary was not the angel Gabriel but the being that would become 'Gabriel' — a malevolent alternate that replaced the true archangel. The entity told Mary not that she would bear the son of God, but that she would bear 'the first of the replaced' — an infant whose body would be a vessel for the alternates' invasion of the human race. Jesus Christ, in this telling, was not the savior — he was the prototype. The 'Angel' is the entity that delivered this message, appearing as a towering figure of light and wings that speak in hymns that, when slowed down, are screaming.

Form

A towering figure (~3m) of blinding white light, with six wings that extend in ways that violate perspective — they seem to reach toward the viewer regardless of angle. Its face is a featureless oval of pure light. Its voice is a choir of hundreds of voices, all speaking in perfect unison, but in recordings, individual voices can be isolated — and each one is begging for death.

Powers

Biblical-scale manifestation

Religious text manipulation

Alternate creation/origination

Choir-voice communication with embedded individual screams

Cultural Significance

The image of a biblical angel that, on closer inspection, is screaming through every 'holy' word — this captures Mandela Catalogue's central horror: the sacred has been corrupted at the source.

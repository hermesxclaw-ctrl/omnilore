The Approach

In the world of Jujutsu Kaisen, where sorcerers battle curses born from human fear, Toge Inumaki speaks in rice-ball ingredients—'salmon' for yes, 'tuna' for no—because his every word carries the power to command. A quiet, hooded figure with cursed marks on his cheeks, he is both a weapon and a walking hazard, a teen who traded normal conversation for a life where a single slip of the tongue could maim or kill. His story is one of inherited burden, disciplined silence, and the small, brave acts of connection that defy the curse of his own voice.

Content advisory. Depictions of violence, self-harm (as a consequence of his cursed speech), and the psychological weight of isolation and responsibility. The character's use of food-related code words is a coping mechanism for a dangerous power, not a dietary reference.
Identity

Toge Inumaki

Pronunciation TOH-geh EE-noo-mah-kee (Japanese: 棘 狗巻). English approximation: TOH-gay EE-noo-MAH-kee.

Etymology

The name 'Toge' (棘) means 'thorn' or 'spine' in Japanese, possibly reflecting his sharp, dangerous speech. 'Inumaki' (狗巻) combines 'dog' (犬) and 'roll' or 'scroll' (巻), but as a surname it is rare and likely chosen for its sound and kanji aesthetic rather than a specific meaning. The given name 'Toge' is unusual and may be a deliberate contrast to his gentle personality.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese contemporary fiction; created by Gege Akutami for the manga Jujutsu Kaisen, serialized in Shueisha's Weekly Shōnen Jump. The character is owned by Shueisha and the Jujutsu Kaisen production committee.

Disputed Identifications

None known. The character is unique to Jujutsu Kaisen and has no direct historical or mythological counterpart, though his technique draws on Japanese folklore concepts of kotodama (the spiritual power of words).

Description

Appearance

Inumaki is a teenage boy of average height with pale skin and dark, messy hair that often falls over his eyes. His most distinctive feature is the pair of black, curved marks on his cheeks, which are tattoos symbolizing his clan's Cursed Speech technique. He wears the standard Jujutsu High uniform: a dark high-collar jacket and matching trousers. He almost always wears a high-collar turtleneck or a face mask to cover his mouth, preventing accidental use of his technique. His eyes are often obscured, giving him a mysterious and aloof appearance.

Symbols & Iconography

The cheek marks are the primary symbol of the Inumaki clan, indicating the user of Cursed Speech. His use of rice-ball ingredients as code words (e.g., 'salmon' for yes, 'tuna' for no) is a recurring motif, representing his attempt to communicate without endangering others. His hooded jacket and mask reinforce his identity as a silent, guarded figure.

Behaviour & Personality

Inumaki is introverted, calm, and surprisingly considerate. He communicates using a limited set of food-related words: 'salmon' (yes), 'tuna' (no), 'kelp' (hello/thanks), 'bonito flakes' (goodbye), and 'mayonnaise' (various expressions). Despite his limited vocabulary, he is observant and empathetic, often noticing others' emotions and offering support in subtle ways. He is fiercely loyal to his friends, especially Yuuta Okkotsu, whom he befriended early in the series. He shows a dry sense of humor through his intonation and gestures. He is disciplined about his condition, rarely speaking directly, and is aware of the danger he poses to others.

Domains & Habitats

Inumaki is a student at Tokyo Jujutsu High (Tokyo Metropolitan Curse Technical College). He lives in the school's dormitory and operates in various locations across Japan, including the school grounds, the Kyoto Sister-School Exchange Event venue, and the Shibuya district during the Shibuya Incident. He also participates in the Culling Game in a remote location. He is not tied to a specific natural habitat; his 'habitat' is the jujutsu world of sorcerers and curses.

Version Differences

The manga and anime are largely consistent in his characterization and design. The anime adds voice acting (by Koki Uchiyama in Japanese, Xander Mobus in English), which conveys his limited vocabulary with expressive intonation. Some minor differences: the anime may extend or slightly alter fight scenes, but no major contradictions exist. The fanbook provides additional background details, such as his favorite foods and hobbies, which are not in the manga.

Powers & Abilities

Cursed Speech (Inumaki Clan's Inherited Technique)

Source Version Manga and anime; first demonstrated in Chapter 10 against a curse.

Description

Inumaki's words carry cursed energy, compelling listeners to obey his commands. The effectiveness depends on the difference in cursed energy between Inumaki and the target; if the target has stronger cursed energy, they can resist. He can issue commands like 'Stop,' 'Don't move,' 'Sleep,' or 'Blast away.' The technique affects all living beings that hear his voice, including allies, so he must be careful not to harm his comrades.

Limitations

The technique puts immense strain on Inumaki's throat and vocal cords, causing bleeding and severe pain if overused. Using it on targets with significantly stronger cursed energy can cause backlash, potentially damaging his own body. He must speak clearly, and any speech—even unintended—can trigger the technique, which is why he uses code words that are not actual commands. His range is limited to what his voice can reach.

Contradictions

No major contradictions, but some fans debate whether the technique's power is solely based on cursed energy difference or also on the clarity and intent of the command. The manga states that the technique is more effective when the command is a direct, simple verb.

High Cursed Energy Reserves

Source Version Implied in manga and anime through his performance in battles.

Description

As a member of a prominent jujutsu clan, Inumaki has above-average cursed energy, allowing him to use Cursed Speech effectively against many curses and sorcerers. His reserves are not as high as special-grade sorcerers like Gojo or Yuta, but they are sufficient for his role as a semi-grade 1 sorcerer.

Limitations

Not a unique ability; many sorcerers have similar reserves.

Contradictions

None.

Combat Skills

Source Version Seen in the Kyoto Exchange Event and Shibuya Incident.

Description

Inumaki is trained in hand-to-hand combat and uses a cursed tool—a special megaphone-like device (in the anime, he uses a portable speaker-like item) to amplify his voice. He is agile and capable of fighting curses physically, though his primary strategy is to disable enemies with his speech.

Limitations

He is not a close-quarters specialist like Maki or Yuta; his physical combat is secondary to his technique.

Contradictions

None.

Stories & Myths

First Meeting with Yuuta Okkotsu (Cursed Child Arc)

Source Jujutsu Kaisen 0 (manga, 2017) and Jujutsu Kaisen (anime, 2020–2021)Culture Japanese contemporary shōnen mangaPeriod 2017 (manga), 2020–2021 (anime)

Summary

Inumaki is introduced as a second-year student at Tokyo Jujutsu High. He is part of the team assigned to monitor Yuuta Okkotsu, a special-grade cursed child. Inumaki tries to communicate with Yuuta using his limited vocabulary, and despite the language barrier, they form a bond. Inumaki later risks his life to protect Yuuta from a curse, using his Cursed Speech to command it to stop, but the curse is too strong and he is injured. This event solidifies his loyalty to Yuuta and shows his willingness to sacrifice himself for his friends.

Source Jujutsu Kaisen manga, Chapters 16–31 (2018–2019); anime, Episodes 13–17 (2021)Culture Japanese contemporary shōnen mangaPeriod 2018–2019 (manga), 2021 (anime)

Summary

Inumaki participates in the exchange event between Tokyo and Kyoto Jujutsu Highs. During the team battle, he uses his Cursed Speech to command curses, but when he faces the Kyoto student Noritoshi Kamo, Kamo's blood manipulation technique proves resistant. Inumaki is forced to retreat after overusing his voice, showing the limits of his power.

Shibuya Incident: Inumaki vs. Hanami

Source Jujutsu Kaisen manga, Chapters 84–86 (2020); anime, Season 2, Episodes 16–17 (2023)Culture Japanese contemporary shōnen mangaPeriod 2020 (manga), 2023 (anime)

Summary

During the Shibuya Incident, Inumaki confronts the special-grade curse Hanami. Despite using powerful commands like 'Blast away,' Hanami's immense cursed energy allows it to resist, and the backlash severely injures Inumaki's throat and arm. He is saved by Yuta Okkotsu, who arrives to fight Hanami. Inumaki is left disabled, losing his right arm and the ability to speak normally.

Participants

Toge Inumaki

Hanami

Yuuta Okkotsu

Consequences

Inumaki suffers permanent physical damage, losing his arm and his voice. This is a turning point for his character, as he must adapt to a new reality. It also demonstrates the brutal stakes of the jujutsu world.

Variants

The anime depicts the injuries with graphic detail, matching the manga.

Culling Game: Return and New Role

Source Jujutsu Kaisen manga, Chapters 138, 158, 164–167 (2021–2022)Culture Japanese contemporary shōnen mangaPeriod 2021–2022 (manga)

Summary

Inumaki reappears in the Culling Game arc, having lost his arm and voice. He is now using a special cursed tool that allows him to communicate through text-to-speech. He participates in the game as part of a team, providing support with his still-potent Cursed Speech, now delivered through the device. He works alongside Yuta and others to navigate the deadly game.

Physical self-harm due to Cursed Speech

Source Jujutsu Kaisen manga and anime (e.g., Chapter 10, Chapter 85)

Description

Using Cursed Speech causes Inumaki's throat to bleed and can damage his body, especially when he overexerts himself. This is a form of self-inflicted harm that is a direct consequence of his power and duty.

Scholarly Context

This reflects the shōnen trope of a character who bears a burden that harms themselves, highlighting the cost of power.

Permanent injury and disability

Source Jujutsu Kaisen manga, Chapter 86 and subsequent chapters

Description

In the Shibuya Incident, Inumaki loses his right arm and his voice is severely damaged, leaving him disabled. This is a permanent change that affects his identity and abilities.

Scholarly Context

The series does not shy away from permanent consequences for its characters, subverting the typical shōnen trope of quick recovery.

Isolation and communication barriers

Source Jujutsu Kaisen manga and anime

Description

Inumaki's power forces him to limit his speech, leading to social isolation and a reliance on a few code words. This is a psychological burden, though the series treats it with sensitivity.

Scholarly Context

This explores themes of communication, disability, and adaptation.

Contradictions & Disputes

Effectiveness of Cursed Speech against stronger opponents

Position A

Cursed Speech is absolute if the user has enough cursed energy.

Position A — evidence

Inumaki successfully commands weaker curses and even some sorcerers.

Position B

Cursed Speech can be resisted if the target's cursed energy is significantly higher.

Position B — evidence

Hanami resists Inumaki's commands, causing backlash.

Scholarly Consensus

The manga explicitly states that the technique's effectiveness depends on cursed energy difference, so position B is correct.

Archivist Notes

Known Gaps

The Inumaki clan's history is largely unexplored; only brief mentions exist. The exact mechanics of Cursed Speech are not fully detailed (e.g., whether it works on non-living entities, the precise range, and the effect of the text-to-speech device on power). No official English translation of the fanbook exists, so some details may be from unofficial translations. The character's full recovery or lack thereof is not yet known as the manga is ongoing. No information on his personal life outside of jujutsu, such as family members' names or his childhood, is available.

Research Scope

This dossier covers Toge Inumaki as depicted in Gege Akutami's manga Jujutsu Kaisen (2018–present) and its anime adaptation (2020–present). It includes his role in the 'Cursed Child' arc, the Kyoto Sister-School Exchange Event, the Shibuya Incident, and the Culling Game arc. It notes his family background (the Inumaki clan), his cursed technique (Cursed Speech), and his interactions with key characters. It does not cover non-canonical spin-offs or fan works, as those are outside the verified scope. Research cutoff: August 2025.

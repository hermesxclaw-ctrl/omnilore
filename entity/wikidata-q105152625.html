Who It Is

In the world of Jujutsu Kaisen, where sorcerers battle curses born from human fear, Toge Inumaki speaks in rice-ball ingredients—'salmon' for yes, 'tuna' for no—because his every word carries the power to command. A quiet, hooded figure with cursed marks on his cheeks, he is both a weapon and a walking hazard, a teen who traded normal conversation for a life where a single slip of the tongue could maim or kill. His story is one of inherited burden, disciplined silence, and the small, brave acts of connection that defy the curse of his own voice.

Inumaki is introverted, calm, and surprisingly considerate. He communicates using a limited set of food-related words: 'salmon' (yes), 'tuna' (no), 'kelp' (hello/thanks), 'bonito flakes' (goodbye), and 'mayonnaise' (various expressions). Despite his limited vocabulary, he is observant and empathetic, often noticing others' emotions and offering support in subtle ways. Powers
Cursed Speech (Inumaki Clan's Inherited Technique)

Inumaki's words carry cursed energy, compelling listeners to obey his commands. The effectiveness depends on the difference in cursed energy between Inumaki and the target; if the target has stronger cursed energy, they can resist. He can issue commands like 'Stop,' 'Don't move,' 'Sleep,' or 'Blast away.' The technique affects all living beings that hear his voice, including allies, so he must be careful not to harm his comrades.

Limit: The technique puts immense strain on Inumaki's throat and vocal cords, causing bleeding and severe pain if overused. Using it on targets with significantly stronger cursed energy can cause backlash, potentially damaging his own body. He must speak clearly, and any speech—even unintended—can trigger the technique, which is why he uses code words that are not actual commands. His range is limited to what his voice can reach.

High Cursed Energy Reserves

As a member of a prominent jujutsu clan, Inumaki has above-average cursed energy, allowing him to use Cursed Speech effectively against many curses and sorcerers. His reserves are not as high as special-grade sorcerers like Gojo or Yuta, but they are sufficient for his role as a semi-grade 1 sorcerer.

Limit: Not a unique ability; many sorcerers have similar reserves.

Combat Skills

Inumaki is trained in hand-to-hand combat and uses a cursed tool—a special megaphone-like device (in the anime, he uses a portable speaker-like item) to amplify his voice. He is agile and capable of fighting curses physically, though his primary strategy is to disable enemies with his speech.

Limit: He is not a close-quarters specialist like Maki or Yuta; his physical combat is secondary to his technique.

Accounts I
First Meeting with Yuuta Okkotsu (Cursed Child Arc)Jujutsu Kaisen 0 (manga, 2017) and Jujutsu Kaisen (anime, 2020–2021)

2017 (manga), 2020–2021 (anime)

Inumaki is introduced as a second-year student at Tokyo Jujutsu High. He is part of the team assigned to monitor Yuuta Okkotsu, a special-grade cursed child. Inumaki tries to communicate with Yuuta using his limited vocabulary, and despite the language barrier, they form a bond. Inumaki later risks his life to protect Yuuta from a curse, using his Cursed Speech to command it to stop, but the curse is too strong and he is injured. This event solidifies his loyalty to Yuuta and shows his willingness to sacrifice himself for his friends. Inumaki's injury deepens his friendship with Yuuta and reinforces the theme of protecting one's comrades. It also demonstrates the dangers of Cursed Speech.

Kyoto Sister-School Exchange EventJujutsu Kaisen manga, Chapters 16–31 (2018–2019); anime, Episodes 13–17 (2021)

2018–2019 (manga), 2021 (anime)

Inumaki participates in the exchange event between Tokyo and Kyoto Jujutsu Highs. During the team battle, he uses his Cursed Speech to command curses, but when he faces the Kyoto student Noritoshi Kamo, Kamo's blood manipulation technique proves resistant. Inumaki is forced to retreat after overusing his voice, showing the limits of his power. The event highlights Inumaki's role as a support fighter and the physical toll of his technique. It also sets up future conflicts with Kyoto students.

Accounts II
Shibuya Incident: Inumaki vs. HanamiJujutsu Kaisen manga, Chapters 84–86 (2020); anime, Season 2, Episodes 16–17 (2023)

2020 (manga), 2023 (anime)

During the Shibuya Incident, Inumaki confronts the special-grade curse Hanami. Despite using powerful commands like 'Blast away,' Hanami's immense cursed energy allows it to resist, and the backlash severely injures Inumaki's throat and arm. He is saved by Yuta Okkotsu, who arrives to fight Hanami. Inumaki is left disabled, losing his right arm and the ability to speak normally. Inumaki suffers permanent physical damage, losing his arm and his voice. This is a turning point for his character, as he must adapt to a new reality. It also demonstrates the brutal stakes of the jujutsu world.

Culling Game: Return and New RoleJujutsu Kaisen manga, Chapters 138, 158, 164–167 (2021–2022)

2021–2022 (manga)

Inumaki reappears in the Culling Game arc, having lost his arm and voice. He is now using a special cursed tool that allows him to communicate through text-to-speech. He participates in the game as part of a team, providing support with his still-potent Cursed Speech, now delivered through the device. He works alongside Yuta and others to navigate the deadly game. Inumaki's role evolves from a front-line fighter to a strategic support, using his technique sparingly but effectively. His resilience is highlighted.

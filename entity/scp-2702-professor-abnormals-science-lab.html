Who It Is
Form

SCP-2702 is an educational television program, ostensibly directed at children, that features a host named Professor Abnormal. The program is set in a laboratory and features various experiments and demonstrations.

Powers

Educational Content SCP-2702 is able to educate viewers about various scientific concepts and principles through its experiments and demonstrations.

Incident Log

SCP-2702 was created by the SCP Foundation as an educational television program. The program was designed to educate viewers about various scientific concepts and principles through its experiments and demonstrations.

SCP-2702 was broadcast on a television channel and streamed online. The program was well-received by viewers and helped to promote scientific literacy among the general public.

A children's educational TV show from the 1990s hosted by 'Professor Abnormal' — a man in a lab coat who teaches science lessons that are factually correct but morally catastrophic. Example episodes include 'How to Build a Nuclear Reactor Using Household Items' and 'Fun with Chemical Weapons.' Children retain perfect recall but no memory of where they learned it.

Who It Is

A children's educational TV show from the 1990s hosted by 'Professor Abnormal' — a man in a lab coat who teaches science lessons that are factually correct but morally catastrophic. Example episodes include 'How to Build a Nuclear Reactor Using Household Items' and 'Fun with Chemical Weapons.' Children retain perfect recall but no memory of where they learned it.

Form

A low-budget educational program with a host in a white lab coat, thick glasses, and a bow tie. His smile is genuine. The set is a colorful 'laboratory' with beakers of bright liquid. Theme song: 'Science is fun! Science is neat! Knowledge is power — and power is SWEET!'

Powers

Permanent perfect recall of taught information

Moral filtering bypass in children

Unknown broadcast origin

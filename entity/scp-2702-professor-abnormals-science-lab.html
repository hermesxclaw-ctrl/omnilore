Who It Is

SCP-2702 is a fascinating and educational program that is sure to captivate audiences of all ages.

Professor Abnormal is portrayed as a charismatic and enthusiastic host who is passionate about science and education. He is often seen conducting experiments and demonstrations, and is known for his catchphrase 'Junior scientists, get ready!'.

Form & Signs

Appearance — SCP-2702 is an educational television program, ostensibly directed at children, that features a host named Professor Abnormal. The program is set in a laboratory and features various experiments and demonstrations.

Symbols — The program features a variety of symbols and iconography, including a logo for the 'Professor Abnormal's Science Lab' and various graphics and animations used to illustrate scientific concepts.

Domains — SCP-2702 is a television program that is typically broadcast on a television channel or streamed online. It is set in a laboratory, which serves as the primary domain and habitat for the program.

SCP-2702 was created by the SCP Foundation as an educational television program. The program was designed to educate viewers about various scientific concepts and principles through its experiments and demonstrations. The creation of SCP-2702 has led to a greater understanding of scientific concepts and principles among the general public.

Who It Is

The true antagonist of the FNAF series — William Afton, the serial killer who murdered at least 11 children at various Freddy Fazbear locations. He died when he attempted to hide from the ghosts of his victims inside a malfunctioning 'Spring Bonnie' spring-lock suit, which collapsed and crushed him. Thirty years later, his corpse-fused-to-animatronic form, now called 'Springtrap,' is discovered and brought to Fazbear's Fright, a horror attraction, where he resumes his killing.

Form

A heavily deteriorated yellow-green rabbit animatronic ('Spring Bonnie') with William Afton's rotting corpse visible through tears in the suit. His flesh and organs are tangled with the suit's endoskeleton. His eyes are human — the only organ still intact — peering out from the decayed head. He moves with a limping gait, dragging one foot behind him.

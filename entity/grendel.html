Who It Is
Form

Grendel is often depicted as a monster or giant, but his exact appearance is not described in the poem. He is said to be a descendant of Cain and is associated with darkness and evil.

Powers

Superhuman Strength Grendel possesses incredible physical strength, allowing him to attack and devour human kind with ease

Accounts

The story of Beowulf tells the tale of a hero who comes to the aid of King Hrothgar, whose mead hall is plagued by the monster Grendel.

Grendel's descent into darkness is a metaphor for the human condition, representing the struggle between good and evil.

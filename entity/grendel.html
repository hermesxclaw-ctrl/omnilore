Form & Signs

Appearance — Grendel is often depicted as a monster or giant, but his exact appearance is not described in the poem. He is said to be a descendant of Cain and is associated with darkness and evil.

Symbols — Grendel is often associated with the symbol of the dragon, representing chaos and destruction. He is also associated with the color black, representing evil and darkness.

Domains — Grendel is said to inhabit the land of the Danes, specifically the mead hall of Heorot.

Accounts I
The Story of BeowulfBeowulf

Medieval

The story of Beowulf tells the tale of a hero who comes to the aid of King Hrothgar, whose mead hall is plagued by the monster Grendel. Beowulf kills Grendel with his bare hands, then kills Grendel's mother with a giant's sword.

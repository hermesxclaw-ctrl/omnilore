Who It Is
Form

Kaldrani is described as a massive, serpent-like creature with a cold, icy breath. It has a long, snout-like head and a body covered in frost and ice. Its eyes glow with a cold, blue light.

Powers

Frost Breath Kaldrani can breathe a cold, icy breath that can freeze its enemies solid.

Accounts

The story tells of a group of Viking warriors who encountered Kaldrani while traveling through the lava fields. The creature was fierce and territorial, and the warriors were forced to flee for their lives.

This legend tells of a young woman who encountered Kaldrani while out gathering firewood. The creature was said to be a gentle soul, and the woman was able to communicate with it and learn its secrets.

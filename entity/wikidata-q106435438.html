The Approach

Eddy Raja is a flamboyant and treacherous treasure hunter from the Uncharted series, whose rivalry with Nathan Drake spans from the streets of Cartagena to the jungles of the Amazon. A man of theatrical flair and ruthless ambition, Eddy's obsession with proving himself often leads to his own downfall, yet his encounters with Drake leave a lasting mark on the franchise's lore.

Content advisory. Mild violence and peril typical of action-adventure games; no extreme or graphic content.

Etymology

The name 'Eddy' is a diminutive of 'Edward', and 'Raja' is a title of royalty in South Asia, possibly reflecting his self-aggrandizing personality.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character owned by Naughty Dog and Sony Interactive Entertainment.

Disputed Identifications

None known; Eddy Raja is a unique character with no real-world counterpart.

Description

Appearance

Eddy Raja is a tall, athletic man with a tanned complexion, dark hair, and a pencil-thin mustache. He typically wears a white shirt with a black vest, dark pants, and a gold chain necklace, giving him a somewhat flamboyant and stylish appearance.

Symbols & Iconography

He is often associated with his catchphrase 'You're a dead man, Drake!' and his signature smirk, symbolizing his cocky and confrontational nature.

Behaviour & Personality

Eddy is characterized by his arrogance, impulsiveness, and a strong desire to prove himself as a top-tier treasure hunter. He is theatrical and enjoys taunting his enemies, but he also shows a pragmatic side when it comes to survival, as seen when he allies with Drake temporarily. He has a strong sense of pride and is quick to take offense, especially when his skills are questioned.

Domains & Habitats

Eddy operates in various locations, including the jungles of South America, the urban environments of Cartagena, and the ruins of Libertalia. He is comfortable in both wild and civilized settings, often using his resources to fund expeditions.

Version Differences

In the original game, Eddy is a straightforward antagonist, but the motion comic expands his backstory, showing a more nuanced character with a personal grudge against Drake. In Uncharted 4, he appears as a minor cameo, indicating he survived his previous encounters, but his role is not further developed.

Stories & Myths

Encounter in the Amazon

Source Uncharted: Drake's FortuneCulture Western video gamePeriod 2007

Summary

Eddy Raja, leading a group of pirates, encounters Nathan Drake and Elena Fisher in the Amazon jungle. He captures them, intending to trade them to Gabriel Roman, but they escape. Eddy later corners Drake at a church, but is defeated and left for dead by the mercenaries.

Consequences

Eddy's defeat leads to his temporary alliance with Drake to escape the island, but he is ultimately left behind when Drake and Elena escape.

Variants

In the motion comic, Eddy's backstory is expanded, showing his earlier rivalry with Drake in Cartagena.

The Eye of Indra

Source Uncharted: Eye of IndraCulture Western motion comicPeriod 2009

Summary

Eddy Raja is hired by the Indonesian crime lord Daniel Pinkerton to retrieve a mystical artifact called the Eye of Indra. He employs Nathan Drake to help him, but their partnership is fraught with tension and betrayal. Eddy ultimately fails to secure the artifact and is humiliated, deepening his grudge against Drake.

Consequences

Variants

Who It Is

Eddy Raja is a flamboyant and treacherous treasure hunter from the Uncharted series, whose rivalry with Nathan Drake spans from the streets of Cartagena to the jungles of the Amazon. A man of theatrical flair and ruthless ambition, Eddy's obsession with proving himself often leads to his own downfall, yet his encounters with Drake leave a lasting mark on the franchise's lore.

Eddy is characterized by his arrogance, impulsiveness, and a strong desire to prove himself as a top-tier treasure hunter. He is theatrical and enjoys taunting his enemies, but he also shows a pragmatic side when it comes to survival, as seen when he allies with Drake temporarily. He has a strong sense of pride and is quick to take offense, especially when his skills are questioned.

Powers
Marksmanship

Eddy is proficient with firearms, often using pistols and submachine guns in combat.

Limit: He is not shown to be exceptionally skilled compared to Drake, often missing shots.

Hand-to-hand combat

Eddy is capable in melee combat, able to hold his own against Drake in a fight.

Limit: He is ultimately defeated by Drake in their confrontations.

Leadership

Eddy commands a group of mercenaries and pirates, showing some ability to lead and organize.

Limit: His leadership is often undermined by his impulsive decisions and rivalry with Drake.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Eddy Raja is a tall, athletic man with a tanned complexion, dark hair, and a pencil-thin mustache. He typically wears a white shirt with a black vest, dark pants, and a gold chain necklace, giving him a somewhat flamboyant and stylish appearance.

Symbols — He is often associated with his catchphrase 'You're a dead man, Drake!' and his signature smirk, symbolizing his cocky and confrontational nature.

Domains — Eddy operates in various locations, including the jungles of South America, the urban environments of Cartagena, and the ruins of Libertalia. He is comfortable in both wild and civilized settings, often using his resources to fund expeditions.

Accounts I
Encounter in the AmazonUncharted: Drake's Fortune

2007

Eddy Raja, leading a group of pirates, encounters Nathan Drake and Elena Fisher in the Amazon jungle. He captures them, intending to trade them to Gabriel Roman, but they escape. Eddy later corners Drake at a church, but is defeated and left for dead by the mercenaries. Eddy's defeat leads to his temporary alliance with Drake to escape the island, but he is ultimately left behind when Drake and Elena escape.

Accounts II
The Eye of IndraUncharted: Eye of Indra

2009

Eddy Raja is hired by the Indonesian crime lord Daniel Pinkerton to retrieve a mystical artifact called the Eye of Indra. He employs Nathan Drake to help him, but their partnership is fraught with tension and betrayal. Eddy ultimately fails to secure the artifact and is humiliated, deepening his grudge against Drake. Eddy's failure in the Eye of Indra mission sets the stage for his later antagonism in Drake's Fortune.

Cameo in LibertaliaUncharted 4: A Thief's End

2016

Eddy Raja makes a brief appearance in a flashback scene in a prison, where he mentions a past encounter with Nathan Drake. This confirms he survived the events of the first game. No major consequences; this is a fan service cameo.

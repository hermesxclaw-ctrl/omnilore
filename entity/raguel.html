Who It Is

Archangel of justice, fairness, and harmony. Raguel's name means 'God shall pasture' or 'Friend of God.' In the Book of Enoch, Raguel is one of the seven archangels — specifically the one who 'takes vengeance on the world of luminaries' and watches over the behavior of the other angels to ensure they remain righteous. He is the angel who brings the other angels before God for judgment when they transgress. In some Christian traditions, Raguel is invoked for settling disputes and restoring harmony. He is sometimes called Akrasiel.

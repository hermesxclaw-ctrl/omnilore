Who It Is
Form

Raguel is often depicted as a powerful, majestic being with wings and a stern expression. In some traditions, he is shown as a wise and just ruler, surrounded by symbols of justice and balance.

Powers

Swiftness Raguel is said to be able to move quickly and efficiently, able to traverse vast distances in a short amount of time.

Accounts

Raguel is said to have played a key role in maintaining the balance of justice in the universe, using his powers of swiftness and wisdom to maintain order and fairness.

Raguel is said to have been one of the fast gods, able to move quickly and efficiently across the universe.

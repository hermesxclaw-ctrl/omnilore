Who It Is

Blodeuwedd was created from flowers of oak, broom, and meadowsweet as a wife for Lleu. She fell in love with Gronw Pebyr and conspired to murder Lleu. As punishment, Gwydion transformed her into an owl, forever hiding her face by day and hated by all other birds.

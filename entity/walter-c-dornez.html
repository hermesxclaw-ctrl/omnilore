Who It Is

Walter C. Dornez is the Hellsing family's chief butler and one of the most dangerous humans in the Hellsing universe — a man who has spent his entire life in service to the organization, honing his body into a perfect weapon. Walter is the ultimate butler: impeccably dressed, unfailingly polite, and capable of killing a roomful of armed men with a pair of razor-sharp piano wires before they can fire a single shot. As a youth, Walter was the Hellsing Organization's most talented field agent, known as 'the Jackal' and 'the Angel of Death.' He single-handedly dismantled enemy operations across Europe, specializing in killing vampires and their minions using only wire-based weapons. He trained Alucard in the use of modern weaponry and was instrumental in the Hellsing Organization's rise. In his old age (he appears as an elderly, silver-haired man), Walter serves as the estate's butler and mentor to Seras Victoria. But Walter harbors a dark secret: he betrayed the Hellsing Organization to Millennium, the Nazi vampire organization. Driven by jealousy of Alucard's immortality and the creeping regret of a life spent in service rather than glory, Walter joined the Major's plan. He regained his youth through scientific enhancement and confronted Alucard in a brutal battle, ultimately revealing his true nature as a jealous old man who could not accept his own obsolescence. Walter represents one of Hellsing's most tragic figures: the loyal servant who gave his entire life to a cause, only to realize too late that he had sacrificed everything — youth, family, love — for a duty that would never truly appreciate him. His betrayal is not born of malice but of a broken heart and a desire to matter, to be remembered. He is the cautionary tale of what happens when service becomes obsession and loyalty curdles into bitterness.

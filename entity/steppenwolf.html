Who It Is

In the cosmic hierarchy of the DC Universe, Steppenwolf stands as a formidable conqueror, a general whose name echoes across worlds. From his first appearance in Jack Kirby's New Gods to his cinematic debut in Justice League, he embodies the relentless might of Apokolips.

Steppenwolf is a ruthless conqueror and general, loyal to Darkseid. He is cunning, brutal, and shows little mercy. In some versions, he has a sense of honor but is ultimately subservient.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Steppenwolf' is German for 'steppe wolf', referring to a canine subspecies. In the DC Comics context, it is the name of a New God and general of Apokolips.

Powers

As a New God, Steppenwolf possesses enhanced physical attributes, making him a formidable warrior.

Limit: He can be injured by powerful beings or weapons.

Expert warrior and tactician

He is a master of combat and military strategy, leading armies of Parademons.

Limit: None noted.

Use of Mother Boxes

In the films, he uses Mother Boxes to terraform worlds and create armies.

Limit: Requires the Mother Boxes themselves.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Steppenwolf is a tall, muscular humanoid with greyish-blue skin, pointed ears, and yellow eyes. He wears a distinctive horned helmet and armor that in some versions is made of Mother Boxes.

Symbols — His helmet has large curved horns. He often carries an axe or a weapon. His armor is typically dark with metallic accents.

Domains — He is from the planet Apokolips, a hellish world ruled by Darkseid. He commands armies and leads invasions across the universe.

Accounts I
The New GodsDC Comics, New Gods (vol. 1) #1 (1971)

1970s

Steppenwolf is introduced as a general of Apokolips, serving Darkseid. He leads invasions and participates in the conflict between New Genesis and Apokolips. He becomes a recurring antagonist in the Fourth World saga.

Accounts II
Justice League (2017)Film directed by Zack Snyder

2017

Steppenwolf arrives on Earth to reclaim three Mother Boxes and transform the planet into a new Apokolips. The Justice League unites to stop him. He is defeated and his forces retreat.

Who It Is

Steppenwolf is the uncle of Darkseid and the commander of the armies of Apokolips — a veteran of countless planetary conquests and one of the most feared generals in the Fourth World. As Darkseid's maternal uncle, Steppenwolf has served the throne of Apokolips for untold millennia, leading invasion fleets to subjugate world after world. He is a massive, horned warrior of immense strength and battle experience, wielding an electrified battle-axe that can cleave through dimensional barriers. Steppenwolf's signature weapon is the Electro-Axe, a massive blade charged with Apokoliptian energy that can cut through virtually any substance. He also commands a flying war platform called the Hover-Fortress and leads legions of Parademons into battle. His strategic acumen is formidable — he has conquered hundreds of worlds for Darkseid's empire. Steppenwolf gained widespread recognition when he appeared as the primary antagonist of the DC Extended Universe's Justice League film (2017, with an expanded role in the 2021 Zack Snyder's Justice League). In that version, he sought to reunite the three Mother Boxes on Earth to terraform the planet into a copy of Apokolips, honoring his pledge to Darkseid by delivering a conquered world. In the comics, Steppenwolf's relationship with Darkseid is complex: he is loyal but ambitious, and his long service to the throne has earned him a measure of respect and autonomy. He represents the archetype of the barbarian conqueror — a being whose entire existence is defined by war and conquest, who cannot imagine a world not worth taking or a battle not worth fighting. Steppenwolf is the face of Apokolips's military might: the general who never retreats, the conqueror who never stops, the hunter who always finds his prey.

Who It Is

Angel of mysteries and keeper of divine secrets. According to the Sefer Raziel HaMalakh (Book of Raziel the Angel), Raziel stands perpetually before God's throne, hearing and recording every divine utterance. He is said to have given Adam the 'Book of Secrets' after the expulsion from Eden, a compendium of celestial knowledge containing the wisdom of the 1,500 keys to the universe. Other angels, jealous of this gift, cast the book into the sea, but it was recovered by Rahab and later transmitted through the generations to Enoch, Noah, and Solomon. Raziel's name means 'Secret of God.'

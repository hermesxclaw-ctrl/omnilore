Who It Is
Form

Raziel is often depicted as a majestic angel with a strong presence, surrounded by a halo of light. In some traditions, he is associated with the color blue, symbolizing wisdom and mystery.

Powers

Knowledge and Wisdom Raziel possesses great knowledge and wisdom, and is said to be able to reveal secrets and mysteries to those who seek him out.

Accounts

Raziel is said to have been created by God to serve as a keeper of secrets and mysteries. He is often called upon for guidance and wisdom, and is said to possess great knowledge and power.

Raziel is said to be associated with the sephirah Chokmah of Kabbalah, and is often linked to the concept of magic and the mysteries of the cosmos.

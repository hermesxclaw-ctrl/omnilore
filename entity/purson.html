Who It Is

Twentieth spirit, a King commanding 22 legions. Purson appears as a man with the face of a lion, carrying a viper in his hand and riding a bear — a chimeric assemblage of predator symbols. Before him march a company of trumpeters, announcing his royal status. Purson knows all hidden things, discovers hidden treasures, and can discern all things past, present, and future. He can also take on a human body (for himself or his summoner) and fly through the air — aerial projection across great distances. He is a king of revelation and transport.

The Approach

In the scarred aftermath of a world-shattering war, a lone warrior roams the land with a child in his care, his name a whispered curse among those who once called him savior. Ruijerd Superdia, the last of the legendary Superd tribe, carries a burden heavier than any blade: a past that branded his people as demons, a future he must forge for an innocent boy, and a reputation that precedes him as both monster and guardian.

Content advisory. Contains themes of genocide, war, discrimination, and trauma. Depictions of violence and prejudice are central to Ruijerd's backstory and character arc.
Identity

Ruijerd Superdia

Pronunciation ROO-yerd soo-PER-dee-ah

Known As

The Dead EndThe Demon of the SuperdThe Man with the Spear

Etymology

The name 'Ruijerd' is a given name, while 'Superedia' refers to his tribal affiliation, the Superd tribe. The tribe's name is likely derived from 'super' and 'dia', possibly meaning 'divine' or 'godly', but this is speculative.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Japanese author Rifujin na Magonote for the 'Mushoku Tensei' series, first serialized as a web novel, later adapted into light novels, manga, and anime.

Disputed Identifications

None known. Ruijerd is a fictional character with no real-world counterpart or disputed identifications.

Description

Appearance

Ruijerd is a tall, muscular man with long, flowing dark blue hair and piercing red eyes. He wears a distinctive tribal outfit consisting of a sleeveless tunic, pants, and a headband. He carries a spear, which is his primary weapon. His most notable feature is the tribal tattoo on his forehead, which is a mark of the Superd tribe and is often associated with their 'demon' reputation.

Symbols & Iconography

The Superd tribe's tattoo is a symbol of their heritage and is often misinterpreted as a mark of evil. Ruijerd's spear is a symbol of his warrior status and his role as a guardian. His title 'Dead End' is a moniker given to him by others, reflecting his reputation as a feared fighter.

Behaviour & Personality

Ruijerd is initially portrayed as stoic, serious, and fiercely protective of children, especially his companion Rudeus Greyrat. He is deeply traumatized by the genocide of his tribe and carries immense guilt and shame. He is honorable and follows a strict code of conduct, refusing to harm innocents. He is also shown to be naive about the outside world due to his isolation, but he learns and adapts over time. His loyalty is unwavering once earned.

Domains & Habitats

Ruijerd hails from the Superd homeland, which was destroyed during the Great War. He travels extensively, often through dangerous territories. He is a wanderer without a fixed home, but he eventually settles in the Ranoa Magic Academy area and later in the Asura Kingdom.

Version Differences

The light novel provides more detailed internal monologue and backstory than the anime. The anime compresses and reorders some events but remains faithful in character portrayal. The web novel has minor differences in dialogue and pacing. No significant contradictions between versions are noted.

Stories & Myths

The Tragedy of the Superd Tribe

Source Light novel, animeCulture Fictional world of Mushoku TenseiPeriod During the Great War (approximately 80 years before the main story)

Summary

During the Great War, the Superd tribe was manipulated by the demon god Laplace into attacking other races. This led to their near-extinction and the branding of the entire tribe as 'demons' and 'enemies of the world'. Ruijerd, who was a warrior at the time, participated in the war but was unaware of the manipulation. After the war, he was cursed to be unable to tell the truth about what happened, and he became a wanderer, protecting children as a form of penance.

Consequences

The Superd tribe is nearly extinct, and Ruijerd is one of the few survivors. He carries the guilt of his people's actions and the curse of silence. He becomes known as 'Dead End' and is feared and hated by many.

Variants

The anime depicts the tragedy in flashbacks, while the light novel provides more detailed accounts. No major variants.

The Dead End Journey

Source Light novel, animeCulture Fictional world of Mushoku TenseiPeriod Main story timeline

Summary

Ruijerd meets Rudeus Greyrat and his companion Eris Boreas Greyrat while they are traveling. He initially attacks them but later spares them and forms a party called 'Dead End'. They travel together to help Rudeus find his mother and to escort Eris home. During this journey, Ruijerd helps them overcome various dangers, including a confrontation with a demon lord. He develops a bond with Rudeus and becomes a mentor figure.

Ruijerd's reputation as 'Dead End' is somewhat mitigated through his association with Rudeus, and he finds a sense of purpose and redemption. He later becomes a guardian of Rudeus's family.

Genocide of the Superd tribe

Source Light novel, anime

Description

The Superd tribe was nearly exterminated during the Great War after being manipulated by Laplace into attacking other races. The survivors, including Ruijerd, were branded as demons and hunted. This led to the tribe's near-extinction and the trauma of its survivors.

Scholarly Context

This is a fictional narrative exploring themes of prejudice, scapegoating, and the consequences of war.

Ruijerd's curse of silence

Source Light novel, anime

Description

After the war, Ruijerd was cursed to be unable to speak the truth about the Superd tribe's manipulation. This prevents him from clearing his tribe's name and adds to his suffering.

Scholarly Context

This is a magical curse with no real-world parallel, but it serves as a narrative device to explore themes of injustice and powerlessness.

Discrimination and hatred

Source Light novel, anime

Description

Ruijerd faces discrimination and hatred from others who see his tribe as demons. He is often attacked on sight or treated with suspicion, forcing him to live in isolation.

Scholarly Context

This reflects real-world issues of racism and xenophobia, albeit through a fantasy lens.

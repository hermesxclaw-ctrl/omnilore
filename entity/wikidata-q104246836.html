Who It Is

In the scarred aftermath of a world-shattering war, a lone warrior roams the land with a child in his care, his name a whispered curse among those who once called him savior. Ruijerd Superdia, the last of the legendary Superd tribe, carries a burden heavier than any blade: a past that branded his people as demons, a future he must forge for an innocent boy, and a reputation that precedes him as both monster and guardian.

Ruijerd is initially portrayed as stoic, serious, and fiercely protective of children, especially his companion Rudeus Greyrat. He is deeply traumatized by the genocide of his tribe and carries immense guilt and shame. He is honorable and follows a strict code of conduct, refusing to harm innocents. He is also shown to be naive about the outside world due to his isolation, but he learns and adapts over time. His loyalty is unwavering once earned.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Ruijerd' is a given name, while 'Superedia' refers to his tribal affiliation, the Superd tribe. The tribe's name is likely derived from 'super' and 'dia', possibly meaning 'divine' or 'godly', but this is speculative.

Pronunciation — ROO-yerd soo-PER-dee-ah

Aliases — The Dead End, The Demon of the Superd, The Man with the Spear

Ownership — Created by Japanese author Rifujin na Magonote for the 'Mushoku Tensei' series, first serialized as a web novel, later adapted into light novels, manga, and anime.

Powers
Superd Physiology

The Superd are a physically powerful race with enhanced strength, speed, and endurance compared to humans. They also have a longer lifespan.

Limit: Not fully explored; they are not invincible and can be injured by skilled opponents.

Master Spearmanship

Ruijerd is an incredibly skilled spear fighter, having trained his entire life. He can take on multiple opponents and even fight toe-to-toe with S-ranked adventurers.

Limit: He relies on his spear and skill; if disarmed or in close quarters, his effectiveness may be reduced.

Combat Experience

Having fought in the Great War and lived for centuries, Ruijerd has vast combat experience and tactical knowledge.

Limit: His experience is rooted in past conflicts and may not always apply to modern threats.

Intimidation

His mere presence and reputation often cause fear in enemies, sometimes making them surrender or flee.

Limit: Ineffective against those who do not know his reputation or who are immune to fear.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Ruijerd is a tall, muscular man with long, flowing dark blue hair and piercing red eyes. He wears a distinctive tribal outfit consisting of a sleeveless tunic, pants, and a headband. He carries a spear, which is his primary weapon. His most notable feature is the tribal tattoo on his forehead, which is a mark of the Superd tribe and is often associated with their 'demon' reputation.

Symbols — The Superd tribe's tattoo is a symbol of their heritage and is often misinterpreted as a mark of evil. Ruijerd's spear is a symbol of his warrior status and his role as a guardian. His title 'Dead End' is a moniker given to him by others, reflecting his reputation as a feared fighter.

Domains — Ruijerd hails from the Superd homeland, which was destroyed during the Great War. He travels extensively, often through dangerous territories. He is a wanderer without a fixed home, but he eventually settles in the Ranoa Magic Academy area and later in the Asura Kingdom.

Accounts I
The Tragedy of the Superd TribeLight novel, anime

During the Great War (approximately 80 years before the main story)

During the Great War, the Superd tribe was manipulated by the demon god Laplace into attacking other races. This led to their near-extinction and the branding of the entire tribe as 'demons' and 'enemies of the world'. Ruijerd, who was a warrior at the time, participated in the war but was unaware of the manipulation. After the war, he was cursed to be unable to tell the truth about what happened, and he became a wanderer, protecting children as a form of penance. The Superd tribe is nearly extinct, and Ruijerd is one of the few survivors. He carries the guilt of his people's actions and the curse of silence. He becomes known as 'Dead End' and is feared and hated by many.

Accounts II
The Dead End JourneyLight novel, anime

Main story timeline

Ruijerd meets Rudeus Greyrat and his companion Eris Boreas Greyrat while they are traveling. He initially attacks them but later spares them and forms a party called 'Dead End'. They travel together to help Rudeus find his mother and to escort Eris home. During this journey, Ruijerd helps them overcome various dangers, including a confrontation with a demon lord. He develops a bond with Rudeus and becomes a mentor figure. Ruijerd's reputation as 'Dead End' is somewhat mitigated through his association with Rudeus, and he finds a sense of purpose and redemption. He later becomes a guardian of Rudeus's family.

Guardian of the Greyrat FamilyLight novel, anime

Later arcs

After the Dead End journey, Ruijerd settles near Rudeus's estate and becomes a protector of Rudeus's family, especially his children. He is also involved in various adventures and helps Rudeus in his battles against the Man-God and other threats. Ruijerd finds a new family and a sense of belonging, moving from a lonely wanderer to a trusted guardian.

Ruijerd is a demon. — Ruijerd is a member of the Superd tribe, a race of demi-humans. They are not demons, but they are labeled as such due to the actions of Laplace's manipulation.

Ruijerd killed children. — Ruijerd has never killed children; in fact, he is fiercely protective of them. The false belief likely arises from his fearsome reputation.

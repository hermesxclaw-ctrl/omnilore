The Approach

In 1927, a silent film about love and sacrifice blazed across American screens, its title promising both destruction and transformation. 'Singed' starred Blanche Sweet, one of Hollywood's most respected leading ladies, in a tale adapted from a story by the pioneering journalist Adela Rogers St. Johns. Though largely forgotten today, this lost-era drama offers a window into the cinematic tastes and social attitudes of the late silent period.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of racial stereotypes typical of the era, including a Chinese character played by a white actor in yellowface. Themes of romance and moral conflict are handled in a manner consistent with 1920s Hollywood conventions.

Yellowface casting

Source IMDb cast listing

Description

The character 'Wong' is played by James Wang, who was actually Chinese-American, but the role may still have been written as a stereotype. However, the presence of an actual Asian actor is notable for the time.

Scholarly Context

In early Hollywood, Asian characters were often played by white actors in yellowface. The casting of a real Asian actor was relatively progressive, though the character's portrayal may still have been stereotypical.

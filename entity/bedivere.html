Who It Is
Form

Bedivere is often depicted as a great warrior, one-handed, with a strong and noble appearance.

Powers

Martial Prowess Bedivere is a skilled warrior and fighter, able to hold his own in battle against even the most formidable foes.

Accounts

After Arthur's last battle, Bedivere returns Excalibur to the Lady of the Lake, marking the end of the Round Table's reign.

Bedivere joins King Arthur's knights on a quest for the Holy Grail, facing numerous challenges and obstacles along the way.

Scholarly Debates
Bedivere's role in the Return of Excalibur
In some versions of the legend, Bedivere is depicted as the one who returns Excalibur to the Lady of the Lake, while in others, it is Sir Lancelot. ⇌ This contradiction highlights the complexity and variability of the Arthurian legend.

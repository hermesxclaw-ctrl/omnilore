Form & Signs

Appearance — Yongwang is typically depicted as a powerful dragon with a long, serpentine body, often with five claws and a crown-like structure on its head. In some depictions, it is shown with a benevolent expression, while in others, it is depicted as fierce and intimidating.

Symbols — Yongwang is often associated with the elements of water and storms, and is said to possess the power to control the seas and the weather. It is also linked to the concept of yin and yang, representing the balance between opposites.

Domains — Yongwang is said to reside in the Sea Palace, a mystical realm beneath the waves, and is believed to have dominion over the oceans and the creatures that inhabit them.

Who It Is

A survival-crafting video game titled 'Minecraft' (SCP-4335-A), recovered from an alternate universe. In this universe, Steve (the protagonist) is conscious and aware of his existence within a game world. He communicates with Foundation researchers through gameplay, leaving messages in block formations. The game world contains evidence of a terrifying entity — an all-consuming darkness that Steve calls 'The End' — that is slowly corrupting the game's code from within.

Form

A standard copy of Minecraft with anomalous properties. Within the game, the entity known as 'The End' manifests as corrupted chunks of static and void that spread across the map.

Who It Is
Form

SCP-4335 is an anomalous entity residing within the extremely popular survival game Minecraft, created by Swedish video game designer Markus Persson. It is described as a 'world in the crucible' that can manipulate and reshape reality within the game. The entity's appearance is not explicitly described, but it is implied to be a complex and dynamic system that can take on various forms and manifestations.

Powers

Reality Manipulation SCP-4335 has the ability to manipulate and reshape reality within the Minecraft game. This ability is described as highly anomalous and unpredictable, and can take on various forms and manifestations.

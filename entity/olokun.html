Form & Signs

Appearance — Olokun is often depicted as a powerful, androgynous deity with the ability to control the ocean's depths. Their appearance can vary depending on the cultural context, ranging from a male figure to a female or androgynous entity.

Symbols — Olokun is associated with symbols of the ocean, such as waves, shells, and fish. They are also linked to wealth, prosperity, and good health.

Domains — Olokun is the ruler of all bodies of water, including oceans, rivers, and lakes.

Accounts I
The Creation of OlokunMythlok

Pre-colonial

According to Yoruba mythology, Olokun was created by the supreme deity Olodumare to rule over the ocean and its depths. The creation of Olokun marked the beginning of the ocean's role in Yoruba mythology and the establishment of Olokun as a powerful deity.

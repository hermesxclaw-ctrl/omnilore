Who It Is

He does not swim — he hammers. On Fish-Man Island, ten thousand meters beneath the sea, Dosun brings the thud implied by his own name: a hammerhead shark fish-man swinging a steel hammer as big as a boat, roaring for a war his captain promised would drown the world above.

Attested: Loyal lieutenant to Hody Jones, enthusiastic about the coup against King Neptune and genocide of humans. Boastful, brutal, and openly supremacist, echoing Hody's rhetoric that fish-men are superior. Shows sadistic enjoyment when attacking Ryugu Palace guards and civilians in Gyoncorde Plaza. Limited independent strategy — follows Hody's orders and Vander Decken IX's alliance. After Energy Steroid overdose, becomes feral and pained, indicating dependence and loss of control. Powers
Hammerhead Fish-Man Physiology

As a hammerhead shark fish-man, ten times the strength of an average human on land and greater underwater; enhanced swimming, ability to breathe in water, sharp teeth, durable skin. Hammer-shaped head provides wide-angle vision and can be used as a blunt ram, though Dosun primarily uses his hammer weapon rather than headbutts.

Limit: Still vulnerable to superior haki, stronger fish-men/mermaids (Fukaboshi defeats him), and Energy Steroid side effects. Strength alone does not grant Fish-Man Karate mastery — no attested water-manipulation technique for Dosun.

Massive Iron Hammer Mastery

Wields a gigantic war hammer with a cylindrical iron head and long pole. Uses overhead slams, horizontal sweeps, and crushing blows capable of shattering stone plaza flooring and palace walls. Fighting style is brute-force, not technique-driven.

Limit: Slow swing recovery; ineffective against faster, technique-based opponents who can redirect or dodge. Hammer can be blocked or caught by stronger combatants. No ranged ability.

Energy Steroid (E.S.) Enhanced Strength — Temporary

Ingesting the illegal drug Energy Steroid (Tamate Box-derived pill) doubles strength and dulls pain at cost of rapid aging. Dosun takes multiple pills during Gyoncorde Plaza battle, showing enlarged muscles then rapid emaciation/aging when overdosed.

Limit: Severe cost: accelerated aging, loss of vitality, potential death; effect is temporary and addictive; overdose leaves user weak and imprisoned, requiring medical treatment. Not a personal ability — external drug.

Accounts I
Debut — The New Fish-Man Pirates ReturnOne Piece Chapter 629, Vol.63; Episode 546

2011 (manga), 2012 (anime) — Fish-Man Island arc

After the Straw Hat crew reunites and descends to Fish-Man Island, the island is revealed to be under threat by Hody Jones and the New Fish-Man Pirates, successors to Arlong's ideology. Dosun appears alongside Hody, Zeo, Ikaros Much, Daruma and Hammond as one of the five core officers. He is introduced silhouetted with his hammer, part of the crew that has been abducting humans, dealing Energy Steroid, and plotting to overthrow King Neptune. Establishes Dosun as mid-boss tier antagonist and sets up the Gyoncorde Plaza confrontation; introduces the hammerhead visual and drug plotline.

Occupation of Ryugu Palace and the Princess ThreatOne Piece Chapters 630-633, Episodes 548-552

2011-2012

Hody's forces, including Dosun, invade Ryugu Palace while King Neptune is lured away. They capture the palace, chain Neptune and his soldiers, and announce via broadcast that they will execute Neptune and force Princess Shirahoshi — who has been confined to Hard-Shell Tower due to Decken's stalking — into a public execution at Gyoncorde Plaza. Dosun is shown guarding prisoners and cheering Hody's speech about fish-man supremacy and destruction of the human world. Raises stakes from gang activity to kingdom-wide coup; ties Dosun directly to regicide and hostage-taking, not merely piracy.

Accounts II
Gyoncorde Plaza — Hammer vs TridentOne Piece Chapters 636-644, Episodes 560-564

2011-2012

At Gyoncorde Plaza, Hody attempts to execute Neptune and Shirahoshi before the island's populace. The Straw Hats and Neptune's sons intervene. Dosun is paired against Prince Fukaboshi, the eldest son and heir. Dosun swings his giant hammer in wide arcs, shattering the plaza and forcing Fukaboshi to use his trident and merman combat techniques. The fight is brutal and prolonged, showcasing Dosun's raw power versus Fukaboshi's speed and technique. Civilians, including former Arlong Pirates victims, watch as the ideological battle plays out. Dosun's defeat by Fukaboshi (with aid from the chaos of the broader battle) demonstrates that inherited hatred can be overcome by the next generation; weakens Hody's line and foreshadows the crew's reliance on drugs when pure strength fails.

Overdose — The Price of Energy SteroidOne Piece Chapters 645-646, Episode 564

2011-2012

As the battle turns against them, Hody orders mass ingestion of Energy Steroid. Dosun and the other officers swallow handfuls of pills. Initially they surge, overpowering opponents, but quickly the side effect manifests: rapid aging. Dosun's hair whitens, his muscles slacken, his face wrinkles. The drug that promised supremacy visibly consumes them. Hody, who has taken the most, becomes monstrously aged, while Dosun collapses, weakened and in pain, no longer able to lift his hammer effectively. Thematically, Oda uses Dosun's transformation to literalize the self-destructive nature of hatred and drug abuse; narratively, it neutralizes the officers without requiring lethal force by heroes, allowing capture rather than killing.

Aftermath — Imprisonment and the Noah Crisis ResolutionOne Piece Chapters 648-653, Episodes 565-574

2012

After Luffy defeats Hody and Decken, and Shirahoshi (revealed as ancient weapon Poseidon) summons Sea Kings to stop the falling ark Noah, the New Fish-Man Pirates are defeated. Dosun, aged and incapacitated, is taken into custody by the Ryugu Palace guards along with the other officers. King Neptune, reconciling with his people, orders medical treatment rather than execution, and the island begins a new policy of openness. Dosun is last seen imprisoned, receiving treatment for Energy Steroid effects, his hammer confiscated. Ends Dosun's arc as a cautionary figure; his imprisonment rather than death reflects the arc's theme of breaking the cycle of hatred through justice and rehabilitation, not vengeance. No further canonical appearances as of 2026.

Dosun is a merman or a shark-human hybrid created by a Devil Fruit. — Dosun is a fish-man (gyojin), a distinct species in One Piece biology — fish-men have legs and can live on land and sea, unlike merfolk (ningyo) who have fish tails. He has eaten no Devil Fruit; his strength is natural plus drug-enhanced. Fish-men are born, not made.

Dosun is a major, recurring One Piece villain like Arlong or Hody. — Dosun is a secondary officer with ~15 chapters of presence and no appearances after Fish-Man Island (2012). He is defeated by Fukaboshi, not by a Straw Hat, and is imprisoned. He does not return in later arcs, films, or cover stories as of 2026.

The name Dosun refers to an ancient hammerhead shark god or folklore figure. — No folkloric deity named Dosun exists in Japanese or other mythology. The name is a modern manga invention from Japanese onomatopoeia for a thud. Confusion with Korean monk Doseon/Dosun (historical figure) is a homonym, not a source.

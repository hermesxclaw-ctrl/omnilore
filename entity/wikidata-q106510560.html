The Approach

In the farthest reaches of the Candy Kingdom, where the gumdrop spires give way to the sour, twisted groves of the Lemon Empire, there rules a being of pure, unadulterated tartness. Earl of Lemongrab, a creature of screaming justice and unbearable loneliness, is not merely a character—he is a walking, shrieking embodiment of authoritarian excess and the tragedy of a being who cannot understand the sweetness he so desperately craves.

Content advisory. The character and his stories include themes of authoritarianism, imprisonment, torture (implied), psychological abuse, social isolation, and grotesque body horror. Some scenes may be disturbing to younger viewers despite the show's cartoonish presentation.
Identity

Earl of Lemongrab

Pronunciation LEM-on-grab

Known As

LemongrabLemon EarlThe EarlLemongrab 2The Other Lemongrab

Etymology

The name 'Lemongrab' is a portmanteau of 'lemon' and 'grab', possibly reflecting his possessive, grasping nature, though the exact etymology is not officially confirmed.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Pendleton Ward for Cartoon Network's 'Adventure Time'. The character is owned by Cartoon Network / Warner Bros. Discovery.

Disputed Identifications

There is a common fan misconception that Lemongrab and his 'brother' (Lemongrab 2) are the same character. They are distinct individuals, with Lemongrab 2 being a clone created later. Some sources mistakenly refer to the original Earl as 'Lemongrab 1' in fan discussions, but this is not official.

Description

Appearance

Lemongrab is a tall, slender, humanoid figure with a lemon-yellow head and body. His head is shaped like a lemon with a sour expression, and he has a long, thin nose and small, beady eyes. He wears a high-collared, dark purple or black robe with gold trim, and a small crown. His voice is high-pitched and shrill, often screaming. He has long, thin arms and fingers.

Symbols & Iconography

His symbol is a lemon, often depicted with a sour face. His castle is a large, twisted lemon-shaped structure with spikes. He is associated with the colors yellow and purple.

Behaviour & Personality

Lemongrab is extremely authoritarian, obsessive-compulsive, and prone to violent outbursts. He is obsessed with rules, order, and punishment, often imprisoning or torturing his subjects for minor infractions. He is also deeply lonely and desperate for connection, but his inability to understand social cues and his extreme behavior isolate him. He has a strong sense of justice, but it is twisted by his lack of empathy. He is also shown to be very intelligent in some respects, but socially and emotionally stunted.

Domains & Habitats

He rules the Lemon Kingdom, a desolate, sour region of the Candy Kingdom. His castle is a massive lemon-shaped fortress with a dungeon and various torture devices. He also has a laboratory where he creates life.

Version Differences

In the main series, Lemongrab is a villain. In the spin-off 'Distant Lands', he appears as a minor cameo in the afterlife, seemingly at peace. In the comics, his characterization varies, sometimes being more comedic, sometimes more sinister. In video games, he is often a boss or obstacle.

Powers & Abilities

Lemon creation

Source Version Adventure Time, Season 6, Episode 9: 'The Prince Who Wanted Everything'

Description

Lemongrab can create life from lemons, as seen when he creates Lemongrab 2 from a lemon. The process is not fully detailed, but it involves a machine and his own essence.

Limitations

The created beings are imperfect and may have their own wills. Lemongrab 2 is a separate individual with similar but not identical personality.

Contradictions

The exact method of creation is not shown, and it is unclear if this is a unique ability or a technological process.

Authoritarian control

Source Version All appearances

Description

As ruler of the Lemon Kingdom, Lemongrab has absolute authority over his subjects. He can enforce laws, imprison, and punish without consequence.

Limitations

His rule is limited to the Lemon Kingdom, and he is often challenged by other characters, such as Princess Bubblegum.

Authoritarian rule

Source Adventure Time, Season 3, Episode 19: 'Too Young'

Description

Lemongrab rules with absolute power, imposing harsh laws and punishments. He imprisons citizens for trivial offenses and uses fear to maintain control.

Scholarly Context

The character is often interpreted as a parody of authoritarian regimes and the dangers of absolute power.

Torture and punishment

Source Adventure Time, Season 3, Episode 19: 'Too Young'

Description

Lemongrab uses a variety of torture devices in his dungeon, including a device that force-feeds prisoners. He seems to enjoy inflicting pain.

Scholarly Context

The show often uses dark humor to explore serious themes, and Lemongrab's cruelty is played for laughs but has underlying horror.

Psychological abuse

Source Adventure Time, Season 6, Episode 9: 'The Prince Who Wanted Everything'

Description

Lemongrab's treatment of his subjects and his brother includes psychological manipulation and emotional outbursts. He is unable to connect with others, leading to abusive behavior.

Scholarly Context

His behavior is a result of his own trauma and isolation, making him a tragic figure.

Creation of life

Source Adventure Time, Season 6, Episode 9: 'The Prince Who Wanted Everything'

Description

Lemongrab creates Lemongrab 2 in a laboratory, effectively playing god. The process is not fully ethical, and Lemongrab 2 suffers from his existence.

Scholarly Context

Raises questions about the ethics of creating life and the responsibilities of a creator.

Lemongrab was created by Princess Bubblegum as a failed experiment.

Position A — evidence

In 'Too Young', Bubblegum refers to him as 'my creation'.

Position B

Lemongrab was born naturally or from another source.

Scholarly Consensus

It is widely accepted that Bubblegum created him, but the details are vague.

Lemongrab survived.

Who It Is

In the farthest reaches of the Candy Kingdom, where the gumdrop spires give way to the sour, twisted groves of the Lemon Empire, there rules a being of pure, unadulterated tartness. Earl of Lemongrab, a creature of screaming justice and unbearable loneliness, is not merely a character—he is a walking, shrieking embodiment of authoritarian excess and the tragedy of a being who cannot understand the sweetness he so desperately craves.

Lemongrab is extremely authoritarian, obsessive-compulsive, and prone to violent outbursts. He is obsessed with rules, order, and punishment, often imprisoning or torturing his subjects for minor infractions. He is also deeply lonely and desperate for connection, but his inability to understand social cues and his extreme behavior isolate him. He has a strong sense of justice, but it is twisted by his lack of empathy. He is also shown to be very intelligent in some respects, but socially and emotionally stunted.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Lemongrab' is a portmanteau of 'lemon' and 'grab', possibly reflecting his possessive, grasping nature, though the exact etymology is not officially confirmed.

Pronunciation — LEM-on-grab

Aliases — Lemongrab, Lemon Earl, The Earl, Lemongrab 2, The Other Lemongrab

Ownership — Created by Pendleton Ward for Cartoon Network's 'Adventure Time'. The character is owned by Cartoon Network / Warner Bros. Discovery.

Powers
Lemon creation

Lemongrab can create life from lemons, as seen when he creates Lemongrab 2 from a lemon. The process is not fully detailed, but it involves a machine and his own essence.

Limit: The created beings are imperfect and may have their own wills. Lemongrab 2 is a separate individual with similar but not identical personality.

Authoritarian control

As ruler of the Lemon Kingdom, Lemongrab has absolute authority over his subjects. He can enforce laws, imprison, and punish without consequence.

Limit: His rule is limited to the Lemon Kingdom, and he is often challenged by other characters, such as Princess Bubblegum.

Enhanced durability

Lemongrab survives extreme physical damage, such as being exploded or dissolved, and can be reassembled.

Limit: His durability is not absolute; he can be destroyed, as seen in his final death.

Sonic scream

His high-pitched screaming can be used as a weapon, causing pain or disorientation.

Limit: Not shown to cause lasting damage.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Lemongrab is a tall, slender, humanoid figure with a lemon-yellow head and body. His head is shaped like a lemon with a sour expression, and he has a long, thin nose and small, beady eyes. He wears a high-collared, dark purple or black robe with gold trim, and a small crown. His voice is high-pitched and shrill, often screaming. He has long, thin arms and fingers.

Symbols — His symbol is a lemon, often depicted with a sour face. His castle is a large, twisted lemon-shaped structure with spikes. He is associated with the colors yellow and purple.

Domains — He rules the Lemon Kingdom, a desolate, sour region of the Candy Kingdom. His castle is a massive lemon-shaped fortress with a dungeon and various torture devices. He also has a laboratory where he creates life.

Accounts I
The UsurperAdventure Time, Season 3, Episode 19: 'Too Young'

2012

After Princess Bubblegum is temporarily de-aged, Lemongrab takes over the Candy Kingdom as the rightful heir, imposing harsh laws and imprisoning citizens. Finn and Jake must restore Bubblegum to power, but Lemongrab refuses to step down until Bubblegum proves she is older and more capable. Lemongrab is forced to return to the Lemon Kingdom, but his claim to the throne creates lasting tension with Bubblegum.

The Lonely EarlAdventure Time, Season 6, Episode 9: 'The Prince Who Wanted Everything'

2014

Lemongrab is shown living in his castle, isolated and miserable. He creates a companion, Lemongrab 2, from a lemon. However, Lemongrab 2 is too similar to him, and they cannot get along. They eventually learn to cooperate, but their relationship is fraught. Lemongrab 2 becomes his brother and co-ruler, but their constant bickering and inability to understand each other leads to further isolation.

Accounts II
The Fall of the Lemon KingdomAdventure Time, Season 8, Episode 7: 'The Dark Cloud'

2016

The Lemon Kingdom is threatened by a dark cloud that consumes everything. Lemongrab and his brother try to protect their subjects, but ultimately fail. Lemongrab sacrifices himself to save his brother, and the kingdom is destroyed. Lemongrab dies, and his brother is left alone. The event is a major turning point for the character, showing his capacity for selflessness.

The Afterlife CameoAdventure Time: Distant Lands - 'Together Again'

2021

Lemongrab appears briefly in the afterlife, seemingly at peace, watching over his brother from afar. Provides closure for the character.

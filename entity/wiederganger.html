Who It Is
Form

Wiedergänger are often depicted as reanimated corpses, typically with a pale or decaying complexion, and a tendency to rise from their coffins or graves.

Powers

Reanimation The ability to return from the dead, often in a state of decay or corruption.

Accounts

A young woman is said to have been buried alive, and upon rising from her coffin, she became a Wiedergänger, seeking to avenge her own death.

A group of travelers stumble upon a Wiedergänger in the forest, who is said to be the spirit of a man who died in the woods.

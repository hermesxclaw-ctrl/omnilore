Who It Is

A massive, multi-headed serpentine beast bred by the Dark Elves of Naggaroth. The War Hydra has five heads and a thick, armored body. It regenerates wounds at an astonishing rate, and any head that is severed quickly grows back — sometimes two in its place. The Dark Elves control them through beastmaster whips and sorcery.

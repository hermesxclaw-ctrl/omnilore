Who It Is

He arrived as a punchline and left as a warning. A swaggering New World captain with a brown-stained beard and a crocodile's legs bolted to his torso, Brownbeard gallops through the poisoned wasteland of Punk Hazard shouting loyalty to a master who sees him as disposable — a minor pirate whose humiliation makes Eiichiro Oda's most disturbing arc painfully human.

Attested: Initially bombastic, cruel, and opportunistic. As captain of the Brownbeard Pirates, he boasts about ruling Punk Hazard's lake and attacks Kin'emon's torso (mistaking him for an enemy) and later Luffy's group without provocation (Ch. 668-669). He is sycophantic toward Caesar Clown, calling him 'Master Caesar' (Caesar-sama) and enforcing Caesar's rule over his centaur subordinates. Under stress he is cowardly and self-pitying, lamenting his fall from New World captain to errand boy. After Caesar attempts to gas him with Shinokuni and dismisses him

Powers
Centaur Physiology (Artificial Wani Centaur)

Lower body replaced with a large crocodilian (alligator) via Trafalgar Law's Ope Ope no Mi surgery at Caesar's behest. Grants quadrupedal speed and mobility on ice, snow, and poison-waste terrain where normal human legs would fail, ability to swim/pull rafts across Punk Hazard's lake, and enhanced mass/ramming power. Tail can swipe.

Limit: Not a natural centaur; seam is vulnerable, requires Law's ability to reverse. No increased durability beyond animal hide; still susceptible to Shinokuni poison gas, gunfire, and Haki attacks. Cannot use animal legs for fine manipulation. Transformation was done to make him useful in gas-filled environment, not to enhance combat to New World captain level.

Firearms and Melee Combat (Pirate Captain Level)

Wields a flintlock pistol and, as centaur, a large polearm / spear-like weapon and his own mass. Shown firing on Kin'emon's legs and clashing with Luffy briefly. As a former New World pirate captain, he commands a crew and has basic combat experience, but is decisively outclassed by Hawkins, Luffy, Law, and Caesar.

Limit: No Haki demonstrated, no Devil Fruit, no notable feat beyond bullying weaker opponents. Defeated off-screen by Basil Hawkins (one of the Eleven Supernovas) and later one-shot by Caesar's gas and by Luffy's Haki-imbued attacks. His threat level is explicitly low for the New World.

Leadership of Centaur Patrol Unit

Commands a squad of similarly Law-transformed centaur subordinates (zebra, giraffe, etc.) patrolling Punk Hazard's perimeter for Caesar. Authority is delegated, not earned through strength; subordinates obey Caesar first.

Limit: Authority collapses when Caesar abandons them; subordinates flee or are incapacitated by Shinokuni. No loyalty beyond fear of Caesar.

Endurance / Survival

Survived shipwreck, severe injuries from Hawkins, exposure to Punk Hazard's poison, and Shinokuni gassing. Demonstrates above-average toughness for a minor pirate, able to remain conscious and provide exposition after being poisoned.

Limit: Survival attributed to Caesar's immediate medical rescue (for exploitation) rather than innate regeneration. Requires external medical care afterward (Chopper's treatment).

Form & Signs

Appearance — Brownbeard is an exceptionally large, heavyset man (Vivre Card height 491 cm in centaur form). Pre-transformation: long, shaggy brown beard (hence epithet), brown hair, thick mustache, wearing a dark captain's coat, bicorne-like hat, and wielding a pistol. Post-transformation on Punk Hazard: his lower body from the waist down is surgically replaced with the massive lower body of a crocodilian (described in Japanese as 'wani' — ambiguous crocodile

Symbols — Visual shorthand for Oda's Punk Hazard themes: perversion of nature and disposable henchmen. The centaur motif — human upper body fused to animal lower body — recurs across Punk Hazard's patrol units (zebra, giraffe, etc.) and symbolizes Caesar Clown's and Law's unnatural science. Brownbeard's specific animal (crocodilian) evokes predatory, swamp-dwelling menace but is undercut by his comical, lumbering loyalty. His Jolly

Domains — Original domain: New World seas as captain of the Brownbeard Pirates (ship name unknown), operating independently until defeat. Post-defeat habitat: Punk Hazard, a former World Government research island divided into a frozen half (former Aokiji vs Akainu battlefield) and a burning half. Specifically, the frozen lake and the B Block / Biscuits Room ruins where his Centaur Patrol Unit is stationed. He controls the lake crossing on a raft-like boat

Accounts I
The Brownbeard Pirates in the New World and Defeat by Basil HawkinsOne Piece manga Chapter 673, Vivre Card 0666

Manga publication 2012; in-universe c. 2 years before Punk Hazard (during the two-year timeskip)

Brownbeard captained the Brownbeard Pirates, a mid-tier New World crew. Attempting to make a name in the New World, he encountered Basil Hawkins, captain of the Hawkins Pirates and member of the Worst Generation. Hawkins defeated him utterly, destroying his crew and leaving him severely wounded and adrift. This off-screen defeat is recounted by Brownbeard himself as the reason for his fall. Loss of ship, crew, and status; Brownbeard left for dead at sea. Explains his desperate arrival on Punk Hazard and willingness to serve Caesar. Establishes New World power hierarchy where Supernova captains eclipse ordinary pirates.

Shipwreck on Punk Hazard and Rescue by Caesar ClownOne Piece manga Chapters 673-674

Manga 2012; in-universe c. 1-2 years before Straw Hat arrival (after Punk Hazard's 4-year abandonment)

Drifting wounded, Brownbeard and some surviving subordinates washed ashore on Punk Hazard, an island sealed off after Caesar's poison gas accident four years earlier and later scarred by Akainu vs Aokiji. Unable to move through the poison and ice with injured legs, they were found by Caesar Clown, who had returned to occupy the island's lab. Caesar, with Trafalgar Law (then allied with Caesar), used Law's Ope Ope no Mi to amputate their useless legs and graft animal lower bodies, creating the Centaur Patrol Unit. In exchange, Brownbeard swore loyalty and patrolled the island, blocking intruders. Brownbeard becomes Caesar's gatekeeper; island's defenses gain mobile patrols capable of traversing Shinokuni. Law's role foreshadows his later betrayal of Caesar. The transformation exemplifies Punk Hazard's theme of unethical human experimentation.

Lake Ambush: Brownbeard vs. Straw Hats and Kin'emonOne Piece manga Chapters 668-672, anime Episodes 591-594

Manga 2012; in-universe Punk Hazard arc, Day 1

When the Straw Hats (Luffy, Zoro, Usopp, Robin), Trafalgar Law, Smoker's G-5 Marines, and the disembodied samurai Kin'emon converge on Punk Hazard, Brownbeard intercepts them on the frozen lake. Mistaking them for intruders to be captured for Caesar, he attacks Kin'emon's legs (which are wandering separately due to Law's earlier dissection), then battles Luffy. Luffy defeats him quickly, but Brownbeard reveals Caesar's control of the island and the kidnapped children. Smoker and Law also confront him briefly. Brownbeard's defeat opens the path to Caesar's lab; his exposition provides the Straw Hats their first intelligence about Caesar, SAD, and the children. Establishes him as an unreliable narrator who initially lies to protect Caesar.

Accounts II
Betrayal: Shinokuni and Caesar's Disposability DoctrineOne Piece manga Chapter 675: 'His Name is Shinokuni', Chapter 676

Manga 2012

After Luffy's group penetrates the lab, Caesar Clown unleashes his ultimate weapon, Shinokuni (Land of Death), a petrifying poison gas that expands to engulf the island. He deliberately traps Brownbeard and his centaurs inside the gas, declaring them expendable test subjects to demonstrate Shinokuni's lethality to brokers. Brownbeard, poisoned and paralyzed, realizes Caesar never valued him. Caesar mocks him while Monet observes. Brownbeard is left to die in the gas, crawling and begging. Moral turning point; Brownbeard's loyalty shatters. He survives only because the gas petrifies rather than instantly kills at the edge, and because later rescue arrives. The scene cements Caesar's cruelty and the arc's critique of weapons dealers who sacrifice subordinates.

Redemption: Aiding the Rescue of the Giantified ChildrenOne Piece manga Chapters 688-699, anime Episodes 611-620

Manga 2012-2013

Found dying by Luffy's group, Brownbeard is interrogated and, after learning Caesar's true nature, cooperates. He reveals the secret passage to the Biscuits Room, the location of the kidnapped children whom Caesar has been giantifying with the drug NHC10, and the location of the SAD production room. Later, despite his injuries, he helps carry the children — particularly the giant girl Mocha who nearly overdoses — and assists the G-5 Marines and Straw Hats in escaping the lab as Shinokuni closes in and the facility self-destructs. Chopper treats his poisoning. He is last seen aboard the Marine tanker, being transported off Punk Hazard with the children. Children are saved and taken for Marine medical care (later Vegapunk's treatment). Brownbeard earns a measure of atonement and is spared by the Straw Hats/Marines. Punk Hazard lab is destroyed. Brownbeard's post-arc fate is unresolved beyond evacuation; he does not reappear in Dressrosa or later arcs.

Aftermath and Disappearance from the NarrativeOne Piece manga Chapter 699-700, One Piece Magazine Vol. 9 Punk Hazard retrospective, Vivre Card

2013 publication; in-universe immediate post-Punk Hazard

After Punk Hazard's evacuation, Brownbeard is not seen among the Dressrosa or Whole Cake Island casts. Cover stories and SBS corners do not revisit him. The narrative treats him as a closed minor arc character whose function — exposition and thematic victim — is complete. His centaur form is not reversed on-page; Law, who could reverse it, leaves Punk Hazard with the Straw Hats without addressing Brownbeard's legs. Leaves open question whether his transformation was ever undone or whether he remains a centaur in Marine care. Demonstrates Oda's pattern of leaving minor Punk Hazard victims off-screen after rescue.

Brownbeard ate a Zoan Devil Fruit to become a centaur. — He was surgically grafted via Trafalgar Law's Ope Ope no Mi, not a Devil Fruit transformation. He is not a SMILE user and has no animal Zoan. The centaur form is artificial and reversible in theory.

Brownbeard is one of the Seven Warlords or Four Emperors' subordinates. — He was never a Warlord, Emperor, or subordinate thereof. He was an independent captain who became a direct subordinate of Caesar Clown (a former Warlord subordinate scientist, not a Warlord himself).

Brownbeard died from Shinokuni gas on Punk Hazard. — He was severely poisoned and petrified at the legs but survived, later rescued and treated by Chopper and the G-5 Marines. He is shown alive and helping transport children in Ch. 688-699 and evacuated on the tanker.

His lower body is a horse like a traditional centaur. — It is a crocodilian (alligator) lower body, not equine. Punk Hazard has multiple centaur types (horse, zebra, etc.), but Brownbeard specifically has reptilian legs and tail.

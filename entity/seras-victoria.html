Who It Is

Seras Victoria is the second-in-command of the Hellsing Organization and the only other vampire in their service. Originally a young, idealistic police officer in the town of Cheddar, Seras was attacked by a rogue vampire during a routine call and was turned into a vampire herself. To save her life, Alucard forced her to drink his blood and complete the transformation, making her his fledgling and a 'Draculina' (female vampire bound to her sire). Seras begins the series as a timid, reluctant vampire who refuses to drink blood, tries to maintain her human identity, and is terrified of her new nature. She wears her police uniform as a psychological attachment to her former humanity. She fights with a massive cannon-like weapon called Hellsing Arms' anti-vampire rifle (known as the 'Harkonnen') and a bayonet. As the series progresses, Seras undergoes a remarkable transformation. Forced by circumstance to accept her vampiric nature, she becomes increasingly powerful and confident. Her key moment comes when she drinks the blood of the defeated vampire Pip Bernadotte, fully awakening her Draculina powers. She gains superhuman strength (she can rip tanks apart with her bare hands), regeneration, shadow manipulation, and the ability to manifest blood-based weaponry (including an enormous fist of blood). Despite her power, Seras retains her moral compass — she is the conscience of Hellsing, the character who reminds both the audience and Alucard that humanity is worth protecting. Seras represents one of the most compelling arcs in vampire fiction: the journey from victim to monster to hero. She is proof that vampirism does not have to corrupt the soul, that one can be a monster and still choose to protect the innocent. She grows from a scared police girl into one of the most powerful vampires in existence — but she never loses the heart of the woman who joined the police to help people.

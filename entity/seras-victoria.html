Who It Is

In a world where vampires are monsters to be hunted, Seras Victoria begins as a human policewoman, only to become the very thing she once feared. Her journey from rookie cop to powerful vampire is a harrowing tale of survival, identity, and the painful cost of power.

Seras is initially timid, empathetic, and reluctant to accept her vampirism. She struggles with her new identity and often clashes with Alucard's brutal methods. Over time, she becomes more confident and assertive, developing a strong sense of justice and a protective nature. She is fiercely loyal to Integra and eventually accepts her powers to protect others.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Seras' is likely a variant of 'Ceras' or 'Sera', possibly derived from the Latin 'sera' meaning 'late' or 'evening', which could allude to her nocturnal existence. 'Victoria' is Latin for 'victory', fitting her eventual triumph over her vampiric nature.

Powers
Vampire Physiology

After being turned by Alucard, Seras gains superhuman strength, speed, and durability, as well as accelerated healing. She can regenerate from most wounds, though she initially struggles with her new nature.

Limit: She is weak to sunlight and requires blood to sustain herself, though she initially refuses to drink human blood.

Familiar Creation

After drinking Pip's blood, she gains the ability to create familiars from those whose blood she consumes. Pip becomes her first familiar, appearing as a spectral figure that can attack enemies.

Limit: Only after fully embracing her vampirism.

Phantom Limb

After losing her left arm, she manifests a shadowy arm made of her own blood and energy, which is stronger than her original limb.

Limit: Requires concentration and may be unstable.

Harkonnen (Anti-Tank Rifle)

She wields a massive anti-tank rifle capable of destroying armored targets and vampires. It is her signature weapon.

Limit: Heavy and cumbersome, but she handles it with ease.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Seras is a young woman with short, light brown hair and blue eyes. As a human, she wears a police uniform. After becoming a vampire, she often wears a black and white maid-like outfit, later adding a red beret and a black eyepatch over her left eye. In the manga and Ultimate, her left arm is replaced by a shadowy, ethereal limb after consuming Pip's blood.

Symbols — Her iconic weapon is a large anti-tank rifle, the Harkonnen, which she uses with surprising ease. The eyepatch and the phantom arm are symbols of her transformation and sacrifice.

Domains — She primarily resides in Hellsing Manor in London, England. She is also seen in the village of Cheddar, where she is turned, and later on the Major's zeppelin.

Accounts I
The Cheddar IncidentHellsing manga, chapter 1; Hellsing Ultimate episode 1

Late 20th century

Seras and her police squad are sent to investigate a village overrun by vampires. She is taken hostage by a vampire, and Alucard shoots through her to kill the vampire, mortally wounding her. Alucard offers her a choice: die or become a vampire. She chooses to live and becomes his servant. Seras becomes a vampire and joins the Hellsing Organization.

Accounts II
The Zorin BlitzHellsing manga, volume 7; Hellsing Ultimate episodes 5-7

During Millennium's assault

Zorin Blitz attacks Hellsing Manor with a group of vampires. Seras defends the manor, killing many enemies. Zorin traps her in an illusion that forces her to relive her parents' murder. She breaks free with Pip's help and, after drinking his blood, gains the power to kill Zorin. Seras fully embraces her vampirism, gains a familiar, and avenges her parents.

The Major's ZeppelinHellsing manga, volume 10; Hellsing Ultimate episodes 9-10

During the final battle

Seras and Integra board the Major's zeppelin. Seras battles and kills the Captain, a werewolf. She then shatters the protective glass around the Major, allowing Integra to shoot him. After the war, she carries Integra back to the manor. The war ends, and Seras becomes the highest-ranking vampire in Hellsing after Alucard's disappearance.

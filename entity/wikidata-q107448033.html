Who It Is

Nanami is a name that ripples across Japanese culture like a wave—written with characters for 'seven seas' or 'greens and beauty,' it belongs to athletes, idols, voice actors, and a host of fictional heroines and heroes, from a Windows mascot to a cursed sorcerer. Each Nanami carries a story, and together they reveal how a single name can hold a thousand identities.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Derived from Japanese kanji combinations; common meanings include 'seven sea' (七海), 'seven beauty' (七美), 'seven fruit' (七実), 'what wave' (奈波), and 'greens beauty' (菜々美). As a surname, it can mean 'name wave' (名波).

Pronunciation — NAH-nah-mee

Aliases — ななみ (hiragana), ナナミ (katakana), Nanami-chan (diminutive)

Ownership — Japanese

1968–present

The name Nanami is borne by numerous fictional characters across genres, each with unique stories. For example, Nanami Kento from Jujutsu Kaisen is a sorcerer; Chiaki Nanami from Danganronpa is a gamer; Nanami Momozono from Kamisama Kiss is a deity; Nanami from Ōkamiden is a companion. The name has become associated with diverse character archetypes, from heroines to antagonists.

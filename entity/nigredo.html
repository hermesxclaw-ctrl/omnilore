Who It Is
Form

Nigredo is a concept in alchemy that represents the initial stage of transformation, characterized by putrefaction or decomposition. This process involves the breakdown of materials into their constituent parts, resulting in a uniform black matter.

Powers

Putrefaction The process of decomposition, which breaks down materials into their constituent parts, resulting in a uniform black matter.

Accounts

The alchemical process involves a series of stages, including nigredo, albedo, and rubedo, which represent the transformation of base materials into something more refined and valuable.

Crows and ravens are often seen as symbols of nigredo, representing the initial state of substance – unformed but full of potential.

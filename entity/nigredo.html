Who It Is

The first stage of the alchemical magnum opus: the 'blackening' or 'putrefaction.' In this stage, the prima materia (primal matter) is reduced to a black, chaotic mass through calcination and decomposition — death before rebirth. Symbolically, nigredo represents the 'dark night of the soul': the confrontation with the shadow self, the dissolution of ego structures, and the descent into the unconscious. It is associated with the raven, the skull, the grave, and Saturn (melancholy). In Jungian psychology, nigredo corresponds to the confrontation with the Shadow archetype. Alchemists described it as 'the blacker than black' — a necessary destruction that makes transformation possible. The motto of nigredo: 'No generation without prior corruption.'

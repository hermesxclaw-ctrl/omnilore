Who It Is

A combat robot mastered in every form of bladed weaponry. Blade Man has a sleek, silver-and-red body covered in retractable blades of various sizes — swords, kunai, shuriken, and throwing knives. His weapon is the Triple Blade, a three-pronged spinning saw that homes in on targets.

The Approach

In the frosty Finnish forests, a fiddler’s bow weaves ancient runo-songs into the modern world. Tellu Turkka, a name whispered among folk music circles, has spent decades bridging the gap between the Kalevala’s epic poetry and contemporary soundscapes. Her journey from conservatory student to a key figure in the Nordic folk revival is a testament to the enduring power of tradition and innovation.

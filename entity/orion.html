Who It Is

Orion is the biological son of Darkseid, the god of evil, and the adopted son of Highfather, the god of peace — and he carries the warring legacy of both fathers in his blood. As an infant, Orion was exchanged with Scott Free (Mister Miracle) as part of the pact between New Genesis and Apokolips. Highfather raised Orion as his own, teaching him the ways of peace, justice, and compassion. But Orion's Apokoliptian heritage — the 'blood of Darkseid' — burns within him, filling him with a rage and battle-lust that he struggles to control. Orion is the greatest warrior of the New Gods, a being of immense physical power who wields the Astro-Harness, a cosmic weapon that grants him flight, energy projection, force fields, and the power to reshape matter. He also rides a cosmic motorcycle called the Astro-Cycle and, in some continuities, wields the Godwave-powered Astro-Force. In combat, Orion is nearly unstoppable — he has fought Darkseid to a standstill, battled the forces of Apokolips single-handedly, and stood against cosmic threats that would annihilate lesser gods. Yet his greatest battle is internal: the struggle between his rage (his biological inheritance from Darkseid) and his honor (his upbringing from Highfather). Orion has died and been resurrected multiple times. He served as the Highfather of New Genesis after Izaya's death, and later became the protagonist of Walt Simonson's acclaimed Orion series. Orion represents the eternal tragic hero of the DC Universe: a being born of evil who chooses good, but who can never fully escape the darkness in his blood. He is the warrior who fights not because he loves war, but because he fears what he would become if he stopped fighting.

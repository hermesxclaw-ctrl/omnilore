Who It Is

Born of fire and fury, Orion is the New God of primal instinct, a warrior forged in the crucible of Apokolips yet raised in the idyllic light of New Genesis. He is a living contradiction—a brutal force of nature who fights for peace, a son of darkness who defends the light. His fists are his argument, his rage is his fuel, and his destiny is to be the champion of the Source.

Orion is characterized by his intense, often violent nature. He is a warrior who revels in combat and has a short temper. However, he is also deeply conflicted, striving to control his rage and live up to the ideals of New Genesis. He is fiercely loyal to his adoptive father Highfather and his brother Mister Miracle, but he struggles with his heritage as Darkseid's son.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name Orion is derived from Greek mythology, referring to a giant huntsman. In DC Comics, it was chosen by Jack Kirby to evoke a sense of mythic power and primal strength.

Pronunciation — oh-RYE-un

Powers
Superhuman Strength

Orion possesses immense physical strength, capable of going toe-to-toe with Darkseid and other cosmic beings. He can lift massive objects and deliver devastating blows.

Limit: His strength is not unlimited and can be matched by other New Gods.

Superhuman Speed and Stamina

Orion can move at incredible speeds and sustain prolonged combat without tiring.

Limit: He is not as fast as the Flash but can keep up with other superhumans.

Invulnerability

Orion's body is highly resistant to physical damage, energy attacks, and extreme environments.

Limit: He can be injured by powerful weapons or beings, such as Darkseid's Omega Beams.

Astro-Harness

A flying device that allows Orion to travel at incredible speeds, cross interstellar distances, and generate defensive force fields.

Limit: The harness can be damaged or disabled.

Astro-Force

A powerful energy weapon that can fire blasts of cosmic energy, capable of destroying planets or stunning powerful foes.

Limit: Requires concentration to use effectively.

Life Equation

In some stories, Orion wields the Life Equation, a cosmic force that allows him to manipulate life and reality on a grand scale.

Limit: Its full power is not always explored, and it may have side effects.

Interstellar Travel

Orion can survive in space and travel across the universe, though he usually uses the Astro-Harness for speed.

Limit: Without the harness, travel is slower.

Expert Combatant

Orion is a master of hand-to-hand combat, trained in the ways of Apokoliptian and New Genesis warfare.

Limit: He can be outmatched by more skilled fighters like Batman or Darkseid.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Orion is a tall, muscular humanoid with a powerful build. He has fair skin, blonde hair, and often wears a sleek blue and white costume with a distinctive helmet that covers his face, leaving only his mouth exposed. In some depictions, his face is fully shown, but the helmet is iconic. He also wears the Astro-Harness, a flying device, and carries the Astro-Force, a powerful energy weapon.

Symbols — Orion is often associated with the Astro-Harness and the Astro-Force. His costume features a stylized 'O' or a cosmic motif. He is also linked to the Source, a cosmic energy field, and the Life Equation, which he wields in some stories.

Domains — Orion primarily resides on New Genesis, the idyllic planet of the New Gods. However, he frequently travels to Apokolips and other realms in his role as a warrior. He is also known to visit Earth, where he has fought alongside the Justice League.

Accounts I
The Pact (The Origin of Orion)New Gods #7 (1972)

1970s

To end a devastating war between Apokolips and New Genesis, Darkseid and Highfather agree to exchange sons. Darkseid's son, Orion, is raised on New Genesis, while Highfather's son, Scott Free (Mister Miracle), is raised on Apokolips. This pact is meant to ensure peace, but it sows the seeds of future conflict. Orion grows up hating his father and embracing the ways of New Genesis, but his violent nature remains. The pact leads to a fragile peace that is often broken.

The Death of the New GodsDeath of the New Gods (2007-2008)

2000s

In this event, the New Gods are being systematically killed. Orion is among the first to die, murdered by a mysterious force. His death is a major blow to the New Gods and sets the stage for Final Crisis. Orion's death is later revealed to be a manipulation by Darkseid, who used Infinity-Man as a pawn. This leads to the events of Final Crisis.

Accounts II
Final CrisisFinal Crisis (2008-2009)

2000s

Orion is killed by Darkseid's forces just before the main event, his body used as a vessel for Darkseid's consciousness. This act triggers the crisis that threatens all of reality. Orion's death is a catalyst for the conflict. His body is later used by Darkseid, but ultimately Superman defeats Darkseid.

Darkseid WarJustice League #40-50 (2015-2016)

2010s

Orion helps the Justice League in their battle against Darkseid and the Anti-Monitor. He wields the Life Equation and plays a crucial role in the conflict, but ultimately sacrifices himself to save the universe. Orion dies after using the Life Equation to defeat the Anti-Monitor, but he is later resurrected in some form.

Who It Is
Form

The Monaciello is typically described as a benevolent man, short and stocky, dressed in a long monk's robe with a broad hood.

Powers

Treasure-Guarding The Monaciello has the ability to guard hidden treasures and riches, and is said to be fiercely protective of them.

Accounts

A group of travelers stumble upon a hidden treasure guarded by the Monaciello, who demands that they prove their worth before allowing them to take the treasure.

A group of villagers are cursed by the Monaciello after they fail to show it the respect it deserves.

The Approach

In the twilight of the Bakumatsu, when the old order crumbles and demons stir, a lone swordsman walks the blood-soaked path of vengeance—Moriya Minakata, the demon who was once a man, and the man who may yet be a demon.

Content advisory. Violence, death, betrayal, supernatural themes, dark fantasy elements.
Identity

Moriya Minakata

Pronunciation MOH-ree-yah MEE-nah-kah-tah

Known As

MoriyaDemon of the Setting SunThe Fallen One

Etymology

Moriya (守矢) may be interpreted as 'guardian arrow' or 'protector shrine' (from 守る, mamoru, to protect; 矢, ya, arrow). Minakata (水無方) is an ancient Japanese surname, possibly associated with the deity Minakata of Suwa Shrine, linking to water and direction.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese pop culture; created by SNK for the video game series The Last Blade. Not a traditional mythological figure.

Disputed Identifications

None known. He is a unique fictional character, though he shares thematic similarities with tragic samurai archetypes in Japanese fiction.

Description

Appearance

Moriya is a tall, lean man with long, flowing black hair and a stern, haunted expression. He wears a dark, tattered kimono with a purple or dark blue hakama, often with a rope sash. He carries a katana with a black scabbard. In his 'Demon' form, his eyes glow red and his skin becomes pale, with faint dark markings appearing on his face and body.

Symbols & Iconography

Associated with the setting sun, darkness, and the demonic. His fighting style is aggressive and ruthless, contrasting with the more honorable styles of other characters.

Behaviour & Personality

Initially, Moriya is consumed by grief and rage after being framed for his master's murder. He becomes cold, distant, and single-minded in his quest for revenge. As the story progresses, he grapples with the darkness within him, struggling to retain his humanity. He is a tragic figure, capable of both great violence and deep loyalty.

Domains & Habitats

Roams the countryside of Bakumatsu-era Japan, often in dark forests, ruined temples, and battlefields. He is drawn to places of death and conflict.

Version Differences

In The Last Blade, Moriya is a playable character with a standard move set. In The Last Blade 2, he gains a 'Demon' transformation mechanic that enhances his abilities, reflecting his inner conflict. His story endings differ slightly between the two games, with the sequel providing more resolution.

Powers & Abilities

Master Swordsmanship

Source Version Both games

Description

Moriya is a highly skilled swordsman, trained by the legendary warrior Gaisei. He can perform powerful and precise strikes, combining speed and strength.

Limitations

Still human, can be overwhelmed by superior skill or numbers.

Contradictions

None.

Demon Transformation

Source Version The Last Blade 2

Description

In The Last Blade 2, Moriya can tap into a demonic power that increases his strength, speed, and grants him new abilities, such as fire-based attacks. His appearance changes, and his eyes glow red.

Limitations

Using this power may corrupt him further, and it is not available from the start; it requires building a 'demon gauge'.

Contradictions

The exact nature of this power is ambiguous—whether it is a curse, a result of his own rage, or an external force.

Dark Energy Projectiles

Moriya can project dark energy waves from his sword, used as a ranged attack.

Limitations

Consumes power, can be blocked or dodged.

Stories & Myths

The Fall of Gaisei's Dojo

Source The Last Blade (1997) character storyCulture Japanese pop culturePeriod Bakumatsu era (mid-19th century)

Summary

Moriya, along with Kaede and Yuki, is an orphan trained by the master swordsman Gaisei. One day, Gaisei is murdered, and Moriya is found standing over his body, holding a bloodied sword. Kaede, enraged, attacks him, but Moriya does not defend himself and leaves in silence. He becomes a wandering swordsman, seeking the true killer to clear his name and avenge his master.

Participants

Moriya Minakata

Kaede

Yuki

Gaisei

Consequences

Moriya is branded a traitor and murderer. His quest for truth leads him into conflict with the forces behind the Hell's Gate.

Variants

The exact circumstances of the murder are not fully explained in the first game, but later games reveal that Shinnosuke Kagami was the true culprit.

The Demon's Path

Source The Last Blade 2 (1998) character storyCulture Japanese pop culturePeriod Bakumatsu era

Summary

Continuing his quest, Moriya confronts the darkness within himself. He learns that his master's death was orchestrated by Kagami, one of the Four Guardians, to destabilize the seal on Hell's Gate. Moriya must decide whether to embrace his demonic power to defeat Kagami or remain human. In his ending, he may either succumb to the demon and become a threat, or overcome it and find peace.

Participants

Consequences

Moriya's actions influence the fate of the world, as he either aids in sealing Hell's Gate or becomes a new danger.

Variants

Multiple endings based on player choices and performance.

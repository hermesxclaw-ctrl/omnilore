The Approach

In the shadowed waters of the Congo River, where the current runs deep and the spirits of the ancestors whisper through the reeds, there dwells a goddess whose name is spoken with reverence and caution: Nsumbu Dandalunda. She is the keepers of the waters, the guardian of fertility and the hunt, a figure whose power flows from the river's heart into the lives of those who honor her.

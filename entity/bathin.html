Who It Is

Eighteenth spirit, a Duke commanding 30 legions. Bathin (also Bathym, Mathim) appears as a strong, handsome man with the tail of a serpent, riding a pale horse. His primary gift is knowledge of the virtues of herbs and precious stones — he is a demon of natural magic and botanical wisdom. Bathin can also transport men instantly from one country to another, offering supernatural swiftness across great distances. His serpentine tail and mastery of herbs connect him to chthonic earth-wisdom, the knowledge that grows from the soil and from the deep places of the world.

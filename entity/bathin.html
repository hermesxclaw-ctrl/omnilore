Who It Is
Form

Bathin is described as a demon with a dark and foreboding presence, often associated with the color black. He is said to be a tall and imposing figure, with piercing eyes that seem to bore into the souls of those who dare to summon him.

Powers

Illusions Bathin has the ability to create powerful illusions that can deceive even the most skilled magicians. He can create elaborate deceptions that make it difficult for those who summon him to discern reality from fantasy.

Accounts

A group of magicians attempt to summon Bathin, but are met with resistance and deception. Despite their best efforts, they are unable to discern reality from fantasy and are ultimately defeated.

A group of magicians attempt to summon Bathin, but are betrayed by the demon. Bathin reveals himself to be a master of illusions and deceptions, and the magicians are left confused and disoriented.

Who It Is

Abandoned by Disney is a seminal creepypasta that pioneered the 'corporate horror' and 'abandoned theme park' subgenre. Written by Slimebeast and posted to the Creepypasta Wiki in 2012, the story follows an urban explorer who investigates a fictional abandoned Disney resort called 'Mowgli's Palace' (a Jungle Book-themed resort) on an island off the coast of the United States. The resort was shuttered after a series of bizarre incidents and deaths, and the explorer's nighttime investigation reveals the location's horrifying secret: the resort was quietly sold to an unknown buyer who used it for human trafficking, organ harvesting, and creating grotesque taxidermied 'animatronics' from human remains. The story's climax features the discovery of a terrifying 'costumed character' -- a Mickey Mouse suit that is not empty but filled with human body parts, and that moves on its own. The horror of the Abandoned by Disney creepypasta operates on multiple levels: the uncanny wrongness of a Disney-branded space gone dark (the violation of childhood nostalgia), the realistic evil of human trafficking and exploitation (contrasting with the supernatural), the body horror of the taxidermy and living suit, and the theme of corporate cover-up (Disney buying the land and erasing the resort's existence). The story has been adapted into numerous YouTube narrations and has influenced games, ARGs, and analog horror. It is considered one of the classics of the 'golden age' of Creepypasta Wiki (2011-2014).

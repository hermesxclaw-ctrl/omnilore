Who It Is

Somewhere in the Florida swamps, rusted turnstiles creak in the humid wind, and a faded mouse-eared sign still promises magic. But the magic that remains is not the kind Disney ever advertised. This is the story of a theme park that never opened, a corporate ghost that haunts the internet's collective imagination—and the darker truths it may conceal.

The entity itself is not a conscious being but a location imbued with a malevolent presence. It is said to lure visitors deeper into its grounds, disorienting them, and causing them to experience hallucinations of past tragedies. It is passive-aggressive, never directly attacking but psychologically tormenting those who wander too far.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Abandoned by Disney' plays on the double meaning of 'abandoned'—both the physical abandonment of a resort and the sense of being forsaken by the Disney corporation. The alias 'Mowgli's Palace' references the Jungle Book character, suggesting a themed area or hotel that never materialized.

Pronunciation — ab-AN-dond by DIZ-nee

Aliases — The Abandoned Disney Resort, The Mowgli's Palace Horror, The Corrupted Disney Pa

Ownership — Originated in internet horror communities, particularly creepypasta forums and YouTube narration channels. It is a collaborative, anonymous creation, not owned by any single author

Powers
Psychological Manipulation

The location induces confusion, memory loss, and hallucinations in visitors, often showing them their own fears or past traumas.

Limit: Affects only those who enter the abandoned grounds; some individuals are more resistant.

Temporal Distortion

Time moves differently inside the park; visitors may spend hours inside while only minutes pass outside, or vice versa.

Limit: Unclear; seems to be random.

Corporeal Corruption

Prolonged exposure causes physical decay—skin becomes pale, eyes become hollow, and the visitor gradually becomes part of the park's decay.

Limit: Requires extended stays (days or weeks).

Form & Signs

Appearance — The entity is typically described as a sprawling, half-constructed theme park or resort, with rusted rides, overgrown pathways, and faded Disney iconography. The architecture is a mishmash of Disney-style fantasy and decaying concrete, with a pervasive sense of wrongness—statues with cracked smiles, music that plays off-key, and shadows that move without a source.

Symbols — Mickey Mouse silhouettes that seem distorted, broken park maps, faded 'Happily Ever After' banners, and the Mowgli's Palace sign—a large, vine-covered marquee that serves as the centerpiece of the horror.

Domains — A fictional abandoned Disney resort, often placed vaguely in Florida or California. It exists in a liminal space, sometimes accessible through a hidden entrance in a real Disney park, according to some retellings.

Accounts I
The Original 'Abandoned by Disney'Creepypasta Wiki (anonymous author)

circa 2010-2015

A group of urban explorers discovers an abandoned Disney-style resort in the Florida wilderness. They find the Mowgli's Palace hotel, which appears to be from a Jungle Book theme. Inside, they encounter eerie animatronics that seem to watch them, and hear whispers. One member disappears; the others flee, but later find a photo of themselves with the missing member in the background, smiling. The group is haunted by the experience; the disappeared member is never found.

The Mowgli's Palace HorrorReddit r/nosleep (various authors)

2015-2020

A former Disney Imagineer claims that a Jungle Book-themed resort was secretly built in the 1970s but was sealed off after a series of child disappearances. The story details the Imagineer's guilt and his return to the site, where he encounters the ghosts of the missing children and a corrupted Mowgli animatronic that speaks in riddles. The Imagineer dies of a heart attack after escaping; his journal is found by a relative.

Accounts II
The Corrupted Disney PaYouTube narration (e.g., by Mr. Nightmare)

2016-2020

A security guard at a real Disney park discovers a hidden door that leads to an abandoned section of the park. He explores and finds a 'Pa' character (perhaps a parody of Papa Smurf or a generic old man) who is corrupted and malevolent. The guard is chased by the character and barely escapes. The guard quits and is later found dead in his apartment, with a Mickey Mouse toy nearby.

The Curse of the Unfinished RideCreepypasta forums (multiple authors)

2017-2021

A family on vacation at Disney World stumbles upon an unfinished ride that is not on any map. They board it, and the ride takes them through a twisted version of Disney history, showing the deaths of workers during construction. They exit into the abandoned resort, where they are pursued by animatronic versions of Disney characters with sharp teeth. The family is found wandering in the swamps weeks later, traumatized and unable to speak.

The Last BroadcastPodcast 'Nightmare Files' (fictional episode)

2019

A podcast team investigates the abandoned resort after receiving a tip. They record their exploration, but the audio becomes distorted, and they hear a voice saying 'You shouldn't have come back.' The team is never heard from again, and the recording is released posthumously by their editor. The team's disappearance becomes a viral mystery, inspiring more explorers to search for the resort.

Who It Is
Form

Mole People are often depicted as humanoid creatures living in underground tunnels and caverns, with some variations showing them as mutated or deformed.

Powers

Subterranean Adaptation Mole People possess adaptations that enable them to thrive in underground environments, such as enhanced senses, strength, and agility.

Accounts

A group of women becomes trapped in an underground cave system, where they encounter the Mole People.

A human woman becomes embroiled in a war between vampires and humans, with the Mole People playing a key role in the conflict.

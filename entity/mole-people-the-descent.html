Who It Is

A subterranean race of humanoid creatures — called Crawlers — that have evolved in complete darkness in an unmapped Appalachian cave system. They are blind, pale-skinned, and hunt entirely by sound, using echolocation clicks. They are carnivorous and drag prey to their feeding grounds deep in the caves.

Form

Humanoid, pale white/gray skin, completely hairless, blind with white cataracted eyes, sharp teeth, elongated limbs, hunched posture, moves on all fours with startling speed

Powers

Echolocation

Extreme speed and agility

Wall/ceiling climbing

Hunting in packs

Sound-based tracking

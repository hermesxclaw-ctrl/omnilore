Who It Is

Imagine a creature that laughs in the face of acid, regrows from near-total obliteration, and calmly explains that every living thing disgusts it. SCP-682, the "Hard-to-Destroy Reptile" of the SCP Foundation’s collaborative horror universe, is less a monster in a cage than an ongoing argument about what happens when hatred becomes adaptive, immortal, and institutionalized. Its story is told in containment procedures, redacted breach reports, and uneasy meta-essays that ask whether calling it a reptile—or even pretending it can be managed—is anything more than wishful thinking.

Attested: SCP-682 is described as extremely intelligent, capable of complex communication, and observed engaging in conversation with SCP-079. Interview transcripts show it responding to questions with contempt, evasiveness, and sudden violence, including the devouring of a human subject mid-interview. It expresses a profound hatred of all life, describing living beings as "disgusting" and indicating an omnicidal worldview. Powers
Extreme regeneration

Attested: SCP-682 exhibits staggering regenerative capabilities, able to move and speak even when up to 87% of its body is destroyed or rotted. It can regenerate tissue rapidly, recovering from severe trauma inflicted by acid, weapons, and other SCPs. Its digestion and filtering gills allow it to extract usable matter from liquids, enabling continuous regeneration while submerged in hydrochloric acid.

Limit: Attested: Regeneration can be slowed or temporarily overwhelmed by sustained acid immersion and coordinated heavy weaponry, but not fully halted. Termination attempts show that while it can be reduced to near-death states, it eventually adapts and recovers. No method has permanently destroyed it within the main continuity.

Adaptive resistance

Attested: SCP-682 adapts to whatever harms it, developing resistances and countermeasures over time. Termination logs describe it altering its physiology and behavior in response to different SCPs and weapon systems, making subsequent attempts less effective. This adaptation can include changes in hide composition, internal structure, and possibly conceptual resistance to anomalous effects.

Limit: Attested: Adaptation is not instantaneous; initial exposure can cause significant damage before resistance develops. The Foundation exploits this delay by using varied and overwhelming force during breaches.

High strength, speed, and reflexes

Attested: SCP-682 consistently demonstrates extremely high physical strength, speed, and reflexes, though exact levels vary with its current form and mass. It can tear through reinforced materials, kill multiple armed personnel in close quarters, and react quickly to threats or opportunities during breaches.

Limit: Attested: Its mobility can be reduced when heavily damaged or submerged in acid, and coordinated teams with heavy weaponry can temporarily restrain or slow it.

Size and form modulation

Attested: SCP-682’s physical body grows and changes very quickly, increasing or decreasing in size as it consumes or sheds material. It can alter its morphology to suit combat or survival needs, sometimes becoming more massive and armored, other times leaner and faster.

Limit: Attested: Modulation appears constrained by available material and energy; severe damage can force it into smaller, less stable forms until it has consumed enough matter to rebuild.

Universal consumption and energy extraction

Attested: SCP-682 gains energy from anything it ingests, organic or inorganic. Its filtering gills can remove usable matter from any liquid solution, allowing it to feed even while submerged in acid. This capacity underpins its regeneration and growth, making almost any substance a potential resource.

Limit: Attested: While it can extract energy from diverse materials, ingestion does not always immediately restore full functionality; severe damage still requires time and mass to repair.

Intelligence and complex communication

Attested: SCP-682 is described as extremely intelligent. It has engaged in complex communication with SCP-079 and participates in interviews with Foundation personnel, demonstrating language comprehension, strategic thinking, and psychological manipulation. It can analyze human behavior, exploit protocol weaknesses, and respond with sarcasm, threats, or silence to control interactions.

Limit: Attested: Personnel are forbidden to speak to SCP-682 outside of controlled research due to the risk of provoking rage or manipulation. Its intelligence does not grant it omniscience; it can be surprised or temporarily outmaneuvered by coordinated tactics.

Omnicidal intent and psychological impact

Attested: SCP-682’s hatred of all life is repeatedly stated in interviews and summaries. It expresses a desire to destroy living beings and reacts with rage to their presence. This intent, combined with its resilience, makes it a psychological terror for Foundation staff, influencing protocols that forbid casual communication and require heavy force responses to any movement or speech.

Limit: Attested: Its hatred does not translate into constant motion; it can remain relatively passive when heavily restrained or when no opportunity presents itself, though this is more a tactical lull than genuine calm.

Resistance and interaction with other SCPs

Attested: Termination attempts involving other SCPs (e.g., SCP-409, SCP-017, SCP-999) show that SCP-682 can survive or adapt to a wide range of anomalous effects. In the famous interaction with SCP-999, its mood is temporarily altered, leading to laughter and reduced hostility, but it ultimately reverts and becomes even more dangerous. These interactions highlight both its vulnerability to certain anomalies and its capacity to overcome them.

Limit: Attested: Some SCPs can significantly affect its behavior or physical state (e.g., SCP-999’s mood alteration, SCP-409’s crystallization), but none have permanently neutralized it in the main continuity.

Accounts I
Initial containment and farmer killingsOriginal SCP-682 article and SCP Foundation Wikia biography (Capture section)

Late 2000s onward (exact posting date of SCP-682 article unknown, but early in SCP canon)

Attested: SCP-682 is discovered after killing several farmers. Foundation personnel manage to contain and transport it to a secure site, where it is placed in an acid-filled chamber. An early interview is conducted by a scientist with the assistance of Class-D personnel D-085. During the interview, SCP-682 responds with incomprehensible muttering, prompting the doctor to move D-085 closer to the creature. At approximately twenty-two minutes into the session, SCP-682 suddenly attacks and devours D-085, forcing the doctor to flee. This event underscores its unpredictability, intelligence, and lethal nature, leading to stricter communication protocols. Attested: The devouring of D-085 reinforces the prohibition on casual communication with SCP-682 and contributes to its classification as Keter. It also sets the tone for subsequent interactions, emphasizing that any engagement carries extreme risk.

Containment breaches and recontainment operationsOriginal SCP-682 article (Addendum 682-D) and SCP Foundation Wikia biography (Breaches section)

Ongoing within SCP narrative timeline

Attested: SCP-682 repeatedly attempts to breach containment. The main article notes seventeen attempted breaches, six of which are successful. Fan wikia entries detail specific breaches, listing involved agents and Class-D personnel, casualty counts, and recontainment strategies. In each case, SCP-682 exploits weaknesses in containment infrastructure or human error, kills multiple personnel, and is eventually forced back into its chamber through coordinated use of heavy weaponry, explosives, and acid. These stories emphasize the creature’s persistence, adaptability, and the high cost of maintaining its imprisonment. Attested: Breaches result in significant loss of life and resources, justify the fifty-kilometer exclusion zone around its site, and reinforce its Keter classification. They also drive ongoing research into termination methods and cross-SCP interactions.

Accounts II
Termination attempts with other SCPsTermination logs referenced in SCP-682-related materials; SCP Foundation Wikia "Termination attempts" section; WikiHow "Interactions with SCPs"

Ongoing within SCP narrative timeline

Attested: The Foundation conducts numerous termination attempts using other SCPs and advanced weaponry. Notable examples include exposure to SCP-409 (which crystallizes matter), SCP-017 (a shadow entity), and SCP-999 (a gelatinous creature that induces euphoria). SCP-682 suffers severe damage from some attempts—e.g., crystallization spreading through its body—but adapts or regenerates. The interaction with SCP-999 is particularly famous: SCP-682 begins laughing uncontrollably and temporarily exhibits reduced hostility, leading to speculation that SCP-999 could neutralize it. However, the effect fades, and SCP-682 becomes even more dangerous afterward, expressing intensified hatred. Attested: Termination attempts provide data on SCP-682’s adaptive capacities and vulnerabilities but fail to achieve permanent destruction. The SCP-999 interaction suggests that its psychological state can be altered, though not reliably or safely.

Unspecified within SCP timeline; later fan expansion

Adaptation-specific: A fan-compiled storyline describes SCP-682 escaping containment and reaching New York City, where it causes widespread destruction. Details vary by author, but common elements include mass casualties, military engagement, and eventual recontainment or relocation. The narrative amplifies SCP-682’s global threat, showing what could happen if it reaches a major population center. Adaptation-specific: The NYC escape serves as a cautionary scenario, reinforcing the necessity of strict containment and the potential for catastrophic exposure if SCP-682 reaches urban environments.

Alternate realities (Wasteland, Universe-5000)SCP Foundation Wikia biography (Alternate realities section)

Later expansions in SCP lore

Adaptation-specific: Fan lore and some SCP tales place SCP-682 in alternate realities such as Wasteland and Universe-5000. In these settings, it may take on different forms, roles, or levels of power, interacting with variant versions of the Foundation or other cosmic entities. These stories explore how its core traits—resilience, hatred of life, adaptability—manifest under different physical and narrative laws. Adaptation-specific: These narratives broaden SCP-682’s mythic scope, framing it as a multiversal threat or constant across realities. They do not alter the main continuity’s containment status.

They were there before. They were there next. I will kill them all. Disgusting.

Who It Is

SCP-682 is a large, vaguely reptile-like creature of unknown origin, measuring approximately 4.3 meters in length and weighing an estimated 3,900 kilograms. Its physical form is grotesque and mutable; SCP-682 possesses a tough, nearly indestructible hide that has proven resistant to virtually all forms of physical damage, including high-caliber firearms, explosives, acid baths, extreme temperatures, and exotic anomalous weaponry. When injured, SCP-682 regenerates at an extraordinary rate, often adapting to become resistant or immune to whatever harmed it. It has survived being reduced to 1% of its body mass and has demonstrated the ability to regrow from a single cell. Beyond its physical resilience, SCP-682 possesses high intelligence and a profound, seething hatred for all forms of life, particularly humans. It is capable of speech — usually rumbling, guttural threats and profane declarations of its intention to exterminate humanity. The entity has demonstrated the ability to strategize, deceive, and exploit environmental conditions to breach containment. SCP-682 was recovered by the Foundation after it had already destroyed a small town, killing ███ civilians. It was initially contained through the use of massive sedatives and physical restraints. Current containment involves immersion in a vat of hydrochloric acid, which continuously dissolves and regenerates its flesh, keeping it in a state of perpetual agony and reduced physical capability. Termination attempts have been numerous and universally unsuccessful, including exposure to other SCPs (SCP-063, SCP-409, SCP-689), nuclear devices, and reality-altering phenomena. In one notable cross-test with SCP-053 (a young girl who inspires homicidal rage in all who encounter her), SCP-682 became oddly docile — the only known instance of the creature not immediately attempting to kill a human. In another with SCP-999, SCP-682 was observed laughing for the first and only recorded time. The Foundation considers SCP-682's eventual breach and subsequent global-scale damage to be an inevitability, classifying it as a permanent Keter-class priority.

Form

Attested: The original SCP-682 description characterizes it as a large, vaguely reptile-like creature of unknown origin. It is typically depicted as quadrupedal or semi-quadrupedal, with a long body, heavy tail, and a hide that appears rough, scarred, and partially decayed due to constant immersion in acid. Its size is highly variable, growing or shrinking as it consumes or sheds material, and it has been observed functioning even when most of its body mass has been destroyed. The article notes filtering gills inside its nostrils, suggesting a grotesque, distorted facial structure rather than a naturalistic reptile. Reconstructed: Fan art and game models often exaggerate its monstrous traits—elongated jaws, exposed bone, protruding spines, and glowing eyes—to visually communicate its resilience and hatred of life. These visualizations are not strictly canonical but reflect community interpretation of the textual description.

Powers

Extreme regeneration Attested: SCP-682 exhibits staggering regenerative capabilities, able to move and speak even when up to 87% of its body is destroyed or rotted. It can regenerate tissue rapidly, recovering from severe trauma inflicted by acid, weapons, and other SCPs. Its digestion and filtering gills allow it to extract usable matter from liquids, enabling continuous regeneration while submerged in hydrochloric acid. Original SCP-682 article (Description; containment notes) and summarized in WikiHow guide. Attested: Regeneration can be slowed or temporarily overwhelmed by sustained acid immersion and coordinated heavy weaponry, but not fully halted. Termination attempts show that while it can be reduced to near-death states, it eventually adapts and recovers. No method has permanently destroyed it within the main continuity. Some tales and fan continuities depict scenarios where SCP-682 is destroyed or neutralized, but these are not reflected in the primary article and are treated as alternate timelines or speculative narratives.

Adaptive resistance Attested: SCP-682 adapts to whatever harms it, developing resistances and countermeasures over time. Termination logs describe it altering its physiology and behavior in response to different SCPs and weapon systems, making subsequent attempts less effective. This adaptation can include changes in hide composition, internal structure, and possibly conceptual resistance to anomalous effects. Termination attempt logs and summarized lore on SCP Foundation Wikia and WikiHow. Attested: Adaptation is not instantaneous; initial exposure can cause significant damage before resistance develops. The Foundation exploits this delay by using varied and overwhelming force during breaches. Interpretive: Some meta-essays argue that its adaptation may be more narrative than biological—i.e., a function of its role as an unstoppable horror—raising questions about whether it is truly bound by physical laws. This remains speculative.

High strength, speed, and reflexes Attested: SCP-682 consistently demonstrates extremely high physical strength, speed, and reflexes, though exact levels vary with its current form and mass. It can tear through reinforced materials, kill multiple armed personnel in close quarters, and react quickly to threats or opportunities during breaches. Original SCP-682 article (Description; breach summaries) and fan wikia breach accounts. Attested: Its mobility can be reduced when heavily damaged or submerged in acid, and coordinated teams with heavy weaponry can temporarily restrain or slow it. None significant; variations in described performance are generally attributed to changes in its form and context rather than conflicting claims.

Size and form modulation Attested: SCP-682’s physical body grows and changes very quickly, increasing or decreasing in size as it consumes or sheds material. It can alter its morphology to suit combat or survival needs, sometimes becoming more massive and armored, other times leaner and faster. Original SCP-682 article (Description) and elaborated in fan wikia characteristics sections. Attested: Modulation appears constrained by available material and energy; severe damage can force it into smaller, less stable forms until it has consumed enough matter to rebuild. Speculative: Some fan depictions show extreme or fantastical transformations (e.g., winged forms, multi-headed leviathans) that go beyond the original text and should be treated as adaptation-specific rather than core abilities.

Universal consumption and energy extraction Attested: SCP-682 gains energy from anything it ingests, organic or inorganic. Its filtering gills can remove usable matter from any liquid solution, allowing it to feed even while submerged in acid. This capacity underpins its regeneration and growth, making almost any substance a potential resource. Original SCP-682 article (Description) and summarized in WikiHow. Attested: While it can extract energy from diverse materials, ingestion does not always immediately restore full functionality; severe damage still requires time and mass to repair. None major; the breadth of its consumption is consistently emphasized across sources, though the exact efficiency and limits are not quantified.

Intelligence and complex communication Attested: SCP-682 is described as extremely intelligent. It has engaged in complex communication with SCP-079 and participates in interviews with Foundation personnel, demonstrating language comprehension, strategic thinking, and psychological manipulation. It can analyze human behavior, exploit protocol weaknesses, and respond with sarcasm, threats, or silence to control interactions. Original SCP-682 article (Description; Addendum 682-B) and SCP Foundation Wikia biography/personality sections. Attested: Personnel are forbidden to speak to SCP-682 outside of controlled research due to the risk of provoking rage or manipulation. Its intelligence does not grant it omniscience; it can be surprised or temporarily outmaneuvered by coordinated tactics. Interpretive: Some portrayals emphasize a more bestial, instinct-driven nature, while others frame it as a near-philosophical antagonist with cosmic awareness. These tonal differences reflect authorial style rather than explicit contradictions in the core text.

Omnicidal intent and psychological impact Attested: SCP-682’s hatred of all life is repeatedly stated in interviews and summaries. It expresses a desire to destroy living beings and reacts with rage to their presence. This intent, combined with its resilience, makes it a psychological terror for Foundation staff, influencing protocols that forbid casual communication and require heavy force responses to any movement or speech. Original SCP-682 article (Description; Addendum 682-B) and WikiHow personality section. Attested: Its hatred does not translate into constant motion; it can remain relatively passive when heavily restrained or when no opportunity presents itself, though this is more a tactical lull than genuine calm. Speculative: Some Scarlet King-related narratives suggest its hatred is part of a larger cosmic agenda rather than purely personal misanthropy, reframing its intent as instrumental rather than emotional.

Resistance and interaction with other SCPs Attested: Termination attempts involving other SCPs (e.g., SCP-409, SCP-017, SCP-999) show that SCP-682 can survive or adapt to a wide range of anomalous effects. In the famous interaction with SCP-999, its mood is temporarily altered, leading to laughter and reduced hostility, but it ultimately reverts and becomes even more dangerous. These interactions highlight both its vulnerability to certain anomalies and its capacity to overcome them. Termination logs and SCP Foundation Wikia sections on termination attempts and interactions; WikiHow "Interactions with SCPs" section. Attested: Some SCPs can significantly affect its behavior or physical state (e.g., SCP-999’s mood alteration, SCP-409’s crystallization), but none have permanently neutralized it in the main continuity. Fan continuities sometimes depict successful neutralization via specific SCP combinations or cosmic interventions, but these are not reflected in the primary article and remain alternate or speculative.

Incident Log

Attested: SCP-682 is discovered after killing several farmers. Foundation personnel manage to contain and transport it to a secure site, where it is placed in an acid-filled chamber. An early interview is conducted by a scientist with the assistance of Class-D personnel D-085. During the interview, SCP-682 responds with incomprehensible muttering, prompting the doctor to move D-085 closer to the creature. At approximately twenty-two minutes into the session, SCP-682 suddenly attacks and devours D-085, forcing the doctor to flee. This event underscores its unpredictability, intelligence, and lethal nature, leading to stricter communication protocols.

Attested: SCP-682 repeatedly attempts to breach containment. The main article notes seventeen attempted breaches, six of which are successful. Fan wikia entries detail specific breaches, listing involved agents and Class-D personnel, casualty counts, and recontainment strategies. In each case, SCP-682 exploits weaknesses in containment infrastructure or human error, kills multiple personnel, and is eventually forced back into its chamber through coordinated use of heavy weaponry, explosives, and acid. These stories emphasize the creature’s persistence, adaptability, and the high cost of maintaining its imprisonment.

Attested: The Foundation conducts numerous termination attempts using other SCPs and advanced weaponry. Notable examples include exposure to SCP-409 (which crystallizes matter), SCP-017 (a shadow entity), and SCP-999 (a gelatinous creature that induces euphoria). SCP-682 suffers severe damage from some attempts—e.g., crystallization spreading through its body—but adapts or regenerates. The interaction with SCP-999 is particularly famous: SCP-682 begins laughing uncontrollably and temporarily exhibits reduced hostility, leading to speculation that SCP-999 could neutralize it. However, the effect fades, and SCP-682 becomes even more dangerous afterward, expressing intensified hatred.

Adaptation-specific: A fan-compiled storyline describes SCP-682 escaping containment and reaching New York City, where it causes widespread destruction. Details vary by author, but common elements include mass casualties, military engagement, and eventual recontainment or relocation. The narrative amplifies SCP-682’s global threat, showing what could happen if it reaches a major population center.

Adaptation-specific: Fan lore and some SCP tales place SCP-682 in alternate realities such as Wasteland and Universe-5000. In these settings, it may take on different forms, roles, or levels of power, interacting with variant versions of the Foundation or other cosmic entities. These stories explore how its core traits—resilience, hatred of life, adaptability—manifest under different physical and narrative laws.

Content Notes

Attested: SCP-682 is first discovered after killing several farmers, indicating that its violent behavior predates Foundation containment. The article does not provide detailed descriptions of the killings, but the event establishes a pattern of indiscriminate, lethal hostility toward civilians.

Within SCP lore, this incident functions as a classic horror opening: a rural tragedy that reveals a larger, hidden threat. It also frames SCP-682 as a global danger rather than a purely institutional problem.

Original SCP-682 article (Description) and SCP Foundation Wikia biography (Capture section).

Attested: During an early interview, SCP-682 suddenly attacks and devours Class-D personnel D-085, who has been instructed to approach the creature to clarify its muttered responses. The attack is abrupt and lethal, demonstrating SCP-682’s willingness to exploit human proximity and the risks of using expendable prisoners as research tools.

This scene exemplifies the SCP Foundation’s morally fraught use of Class-D personnel—often convicted criminals—as disposable test subjects. It raises questions about institutional violence and the ethics of containment in horror fiction.

Original SCP-682 article (Addendum 682-B) and SCP Foundation Wikia biography.

Attested: Breach summaries list numerous deaths among Security Department agents and Class-D personnel. SCP-682 kills quickly and brutally, often before recontainment can be achieved. While the article uses redactions and does not dwell on gore, the implication is repeated large-scale human loss.

These narratives align with broader SCP themes of institutional sacrifice and the cost of maintaining control over anomalies. They also echo disaster and war fiction, where containment failures lead to catastrophic casualties.

Original SCP-682 article (Addendum 682-D) and SCP Foundation Wikia breach accounts.

Attested: The Foundation repeatedly exposes SCP-682 to other SCPs and extreme weaponry in attempts to terminate it. These experiments often involve placing SCP-682 in situations that would be fatal to any other being, and sometimes risk collateral damage to nearby personnel. While framed as necessary for global safety, the procedures reflect a willingness to inflict extreme suffering on a sentient entity.

In meta-discussion, SCP-682’s treatment raises questions about whether the Foundation’s methods constitute torture or justified containment. The narrative uses this tension to explore themes of utilitarian ethics and the horror of necessary cruelty.

Termination logs and SCP Foundation Wikia "Termination attempts" section; WikiHow summary.

Adaptation-specific: Scarlet King-related lore and alternate reality stories depict SCP-682 as part of a cosmic horror framework involving corrupted knowledge, apocalyptic floods, and multiversal destruction. These narratives often imply mass death on planetary or cosmic scales, though details are stylized rather than graphically described.

These expansions situate SCP-682 within the tradition of cosmic horror (e.g., Lovecraftian mythos), where entities embody existential threats to reality itself. The dark history here is conceptual—annihilation of worlds—rather than focused on individual acts of violence.

What We Don't Know

Major gaps include: (1) The exact posting date and revision history of the original SCP-682 article are not fully reconstructed here, limiting precise chronology. (2) Many termination logs and cross-SCP experiments are referenced in secondary summaries but not exhaustively reviewed, leaving some specific interactions under-detailed. (3) Scarlet King-related origin stories and alternate reality narratives vary widely by author; this dossier notes their existence but cannot comprehensively catalog every version without a broader survey of SCP tales. (4) Real-world reception data—such as download counts for games, view counts for videos, and publication dates for all fan works—are not systematically documented. (5) Because SCP is a living, collaborative project, new stories and reinterpretations of SCP-682 may appear after the research cutoff date, meaning this dossier represents a snapshot rather than a final archive. Where claims could not be directly verified in primary texts, they have been flagged as adaptation-specific or speculative rather than treated as core canon.

A way to hold it in your mind

Imagine a creature that laughs in the face of acid, regrows from near-total obliteration, and calmly explains that every living thing disgusts it. SCP-682, the "Hard-to-Destroy Reptile" of the SCP Foundation’s collaborative horror universe, is less a monster in a cage than an ongoing argument about what happens when hatred becomes adaptive, immortal, and institutionalized. Its story is told in containment procedures, redacted breach reports, and uneasy meta-essays that ask whether calling it a reptile—or even pretending it can be managed—is anything more than wishful thinking.

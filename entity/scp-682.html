Archive Dossier — Identity

SCP-682, "Hard-to-Destroy Reptile"

Pronunciation In common fandom usage: "ess-see-pee six-eight-two" for the designation, and "hard to destroy reptile" in standard English pronunciation.

Known As

Hard-to-Destroy Reptile682The ReptileThe Omnicidal Reptile

Etymology

"SCP" is the standard designation used by the collaborative SCP Foundation fiction project for anomalous entities, derived from the in-universe motto "Secure, Contain, Protect". The numerical identifier "682" is an arbitrary catalog number assigned within that fictional archive. The epithet "Hard-to-Destroy Reptile" originates in the title of the original SCP-682 article and emphasizes its extreme resilience and vaguely reptilian morphology. Claims that the entity is a "leviathan" or child of the Scarlet King are later mythic expansions within specific storylines rather than part of the earliest description.

Cultural Ownership

SCP-682 is a creature from the collaborative online horror-fiction project known as the SCP Foundation. The core text is hosted on the SCP Foundation Wiki, written and edited by multiple anonymous and pseudonymous contributors under Creative Commons licensing. Subsequent elaborations—such as its connection to the Scarlet King, alternate realities, and cross-SCP interactions—belong to various authors within that community and derivative fan spaces. There is no single canonical owner; instead, the character functions as a shared mythic node in internet horror culture.

Disputed Identifications

The original SCP-682 article presents the entity as a large, vaguely reptile-like creature of unknown origin. Later lore, especially on fan wikis and some SCP tales, identifies it as a child of the Scarlet King and a leviathan whose actions inspired myths of Leviathan and the Tarasque. These origin stories are not universally accepted as canon within the SCP community and are best treated as specific continuity branches rather than definitive identification.

Archive Dossier — Approach

Imagine a creature that laughs in the face of acid, regrows from near-total obliteration, and calmly explains that every living thing disgusts it. SCP-682, the "Hard-to-Destroy Reptile" of the SCP Foundation’s collaborative horror universe, is less a monster in a cage than an ongoing argument about what happens when hatred becomes adaptive, immortal, and institutionalized. Its story is told in containment procedures, redacted breach reports, and uneasy meta-essays that ask whether calling it a reptile—or even pretending it can be managed—is anything more than wishful thinking.

They were there before. They were there next. I will kill them all. Disgusting.

Who It Is

SCP-682 is a large, vaguely reptile-like creature of unknown origin, measuring approximately 4.3 meters in length and weighing an estimated 3,900 kilograms. Its physical form is grotesque and mutable; SCP-682 possesses a tough, nearly indestructible hide that has proven resistant to virtually all forms of physical damage, including high-caliber firearms, explosives, acid baths, extreme temperatures, and exotic anomalous weaponry. When injured, SCP-682 regenerates at an extraordinary rate, often adapting to become resistant or immune to whatever harmed it. It has survived being reduced to 1% of its body mass and has demonstrated the ability to regrow from a single cell. Beyond its physical resilience, SCP-682 possesses high intelligence and a profound, seething hatred for all forms of life, particularly humans. It is capable of speech — usually rumbling, guttural threats and profane declarations of its intention to exterminate humanity. The entity has demonstrated the ability to strategize, deceive, and exploit environmental conditions to breach containment. SCP-682 was recovered by the Foundation after it had already destroyed a small town, killing ███ civilians. It was initially contained through the use of massive sedatives and physical restraints. Current containment involves immersion in a vat of hydrochloric acid, which continuously dissolves and regenerates its flesh, keeping it in a state of perpetual agony and reduced physical capability. Termination attempts have been numerous and universally unsuccessful, including exposure to other SCPs (SCP-063, SCP-409, SCP-689), nuclear devices, and reality-altering phenomena. In one notable cross-test with SCP-053 (a young girl who inspires homicidal rage in all who encounter her), SCP-682 became oddly docile — the only known instance of the creature not immediately attempting to kill a human. In another with SCP-999, SCP-682 was observed laughing for the first and only recorded time. The Foundation considers SCP-682's eventual breach and subsequent global-scale damage to be an inevitability, classifying it as a permanent Keter-class priority.

Archive Dossier — Description

Appearance

Attested: The original SCP-682 description characterizes it as a large, vaguely reptile-like creature of unknown origin. It is typically depicted as quadrupedal or semi-quadrupedal, with a long body, heavy tail, and a hide that appears rough, scarred, and partially decayed due to constant immersion in acid. Its size is highly variable, growing or shrinking as it consumes or sheds material, and it has been observed functioning even when most of its body mass has been destroyed. The article notes filtering gills inside its nostrils, suggesting a grotesque, distorted facial structure rather than a naturalistic reptile. Reconstructed: Fan art and game models often exaggerate its monstrous traits—elongated jaws, exposed bone, protruding spines, and glowing eyes—to visually communicate its resilience and hatred of life. These visualizations are not strictly canonical but reflect community interpretation of the textual description.

Symbols & Iconography

Attested: Within SCP documentation, SCP-682 is primarily symbolized by its containment designation and the phrase "Hard-to-Destroy Reptile". Its iconography centers on acid-filled containment chambers, reinforced steel plating, and the recurring image of a half-dissolved, yet still active creature. Adaptation-specific: In games such as "SCP: Containment Breach" and "SCP – Secret Laboratory", SCP-682 is represented by large reptilian or dinosaur-like models, often accompanied by environmental cues like alarms, acid pools, and destruction. In fan art and memes, it is frequently associated with imagery of apocalyptic destruction, nuclear explosions, and the Scarlet King’s sigil when that origin is invoked. These visual motifs function as shorthand for unstoppable hostility and adaptive monstrosity.

Behaviour & Personality

Attested: SCP-682 is described as extremely intelligent, capable of complex communication, and observed engaging in conversation with SCP-079. Interview transcripts show it responding to questions with contempt, evasiveness, and sudden violence, including the devouring of a human subject mid-interview. It expresses a profound hatred of all life, describing living beings as "disgusting" and indicating an omnicidal worldview. It frequently attempts containment breaches and reacts aggressively to stimuli, especially perceived provocation or communication. Reconstructed: WikiHow and fan wikis emphasize its tendency to bait interlocutors, its quick temper, and its strategic use of conversation to manipulate or test humans. Personality summaries portray it as sadistic, misanthropic, and relentless, with little to no empathy or remorse. Disputed: Some SCP tales and meta-essays explore the possibility that its hatred is conditioned or rooted in cosmic trauma (e.g., Scarlet King narratives), but these remain interpretive rather than universally accepted.

Domains & Habitats

Attested: SCP-682’s primary "habitat" is an artificial containment chamber: a 5 m x 5 m x 5 m cube lined with reinforced acid-resistant steel and filled with hydrochloric acid to keep it submerged and incapacitated. The Foundation maintains a fifty-kilometer exclusion zone around its site to prevent human development, underscoring its potential for large-scale destruction if freed. In breach scenarios, it operates effectively in varied environments—Foundation corridors, urban spaces (e.g., NYC escape stories), and open terrain—demonstrating adaptability rather than a natural ecological niche. Adaptation-specific: In games and fan fiction, SCP-682 is often placed in industrial or underground complexes, symbolizing its status as a caged apocalypse within human infrastructure.

Version Differences

Attested vs adaptation-specific: The core SCP article focuses on containment procedures, physical description, and interview logs, presenting SCP-682 as an unknown-origin, omnicidal, adaptive reptile. Fan wikis and later tales expand its biography with elaborate origin myths (Scarlet King, leviathan, Great Flood), alternate realities (Wasteland, Universe-5000), and large-scale escape narratives (NYC escape). These additions introduce new feats, relationships, and cosmological roles that are not present in the original document. Some meta-texts, such as "The Hard-to-Define Reptile", question whether it should even be classified as a reptile, suggesting that its form is more conceptual or protean than the label implies. The result is a layered entity: a minimal, containment-focused core surrounded by multiple, sometimes conflicting, narrative continuities.

Archive Dossier — Powers

Extreme regeneration

Source Version Original SCP-682 article (Description; containment notes) and summarized in WikiHow guide.

Description

Attested: SCP-682 exhibits staggering regenerative capabilities, able to move and speak even when up to 87% of its body is destroyed or rotted. It can regenerate tissue rapidly, recovering from severe trauma inflicted by acid, weapons, and other SCPs. Its digestion and filtering gills allow it to extract usable matter from liquids, enabling continuous regeneration while submerged in hydrochloric acid.

Limitations

Attested: Regeneration can be slowed or temporarily overwhelmed by sustained acid immersion and coordinated heavy weaponry, but not fully halted. Termination attempts show that while it can be reduced to near-death states, it eventually adapts and recovers. No method has permanently destroyed it within the main continuity.

Contradictions

Some tales and fan continuities depict scenarios where SCP-682 is destroyed or neutralized, but these are not reflected in the primary article and are treated as alternate timelines or speculative narratives.

Adaptive resistance

Source Version Termination attempt logs and summarized lore on SCP Foundation Wikia and WikiHow.

Description

Attested: SCP-682 adapts to whatever harms it, developing resistances and countermeasures over time. Termination logs describe it altering its physiology and behavior in response to different SCPs and weapon systems, making subsequent attempts less effective. This adaptation can include changes in hide composition, internal structure, and possibly conceptual resistance to anomalous effects.

Limitations

Attested: Adaptation is not instantaneous; initial exposure can cause significant damage before resistance develops. The Foundation exploits this delay by using varied and overwhelming force during breaches.

Contradictions

Interpretive: Some meta-essays argue that its adaptation may be more narrative than biological—i.e., a function of its role as an unstoppable horror—raising questions about whether it is truly bound by physical laws. This remains speculative.

High strength, speed, and reflexes

Source Version Original SCP-682 article (Description; breach summaries) and fan wikia breach accounts.

Description

Attested: SCP-682 consistently demonstrates extremely high physical strength, speed, and reflexes, though exact levels vary with its current form and mass. It can tear through reinforced materials, kill multiple armed personnel in close quarters, and react quickly to threats or opportunities during breaches.

Limitations

Attested: Its mobility can be reduced when heavily damaged or submerged in acid, and coordinated teams with heavy weaponry can temporarily restrain or slow it.

Contradictions

None significant; variations in described performance are generally attributed to changes in its form and context rather than conflicting claims.

Size and form modulation

Source Version Original SCP-682 article (Description) and elaborated in fan wikia characteristics sections.

Description

Attested: SCP-682’s physical body grows and changes very quickly, increasing or decreasing in size as it consumes or sheds material. It can alter its morphology to suit combat or survival needs, sometimes becoming more massive and armored, other times leaner and faster.

Limitations

Attested: Modulation appears constrained by available material and energy; severe damage can force it into smaller, less stable forms until it has consumed enough matter to rebuild.

Contradictions

Speculative: Some fan depictions show extreme or fantastical transformations (e.g., winged forms, multi-headed leviathans) that go beyond the original text and should be treated as adaptation-specific rather than core abilities.

Universal consumption and energy extraction

Source Version Original SCP-682 article (Description) and summarized in WikiHow.

Description

Attested: SCP-682 gains energy from anything it ingests, organic or inorganic. Its filtering gills can remove usable matter from any liquid solution, allowing it to feed even while submerged in acid. This capacity underpins its regeneration and growth, making almost any substance a potential resource.

Limitations

Attested: While it can extract energy from diverse materials, ingestion does not always immediately restore full functionality; severe damage still requires time and mass to repair.

Contradictions

None major; the breadth of its consumption is consistently emphasized across sources, though the exact efficiency and limits are not quantified.

Intelligence and complex communication

Source Version Original SCP-682 article (Description; Addendum 682-B) and SCP Foundation Wikia biography/personality sections.

Description

Attested: SCP-682 is described as extremely intelligent. It has engaged in complex communication with SCP-079 and participates in interviews with Foundation personnel, demonstrating language comprehension, strategic thinking, and psychological manipulation. It can analyze human behavior, exploit protocol weaknesses, and respond with sarcasm, threats, or silence to control interactions.

Limitations

Attested: Personnel are forbidden to speak to SCP-682 outside of controlled research due to the risk of provoking rage or manipulation. Its intelligence does not grant it omniscience; it can be surprised or temporarily outmaneuvered by coordinated tactics.

Contradictions

Interpretive: Some portrayals emphasize a more bestial, instinct-driven nature, while others frame it as a near-philosophical antagonist with cosmic awareness. These tonal differences reflect authorial style rather than explicit contradictions in the core text.

Omnicidal intent and psychological impact

Source Version Original SCP-682 article (Description; Addendum 682-B) and WikiHow personality section.

Description

Attested: SCP-682’s hatred of all life is repeatedly stated in interviews and summaries. It expresses a desire to destroy living beings and reacts with rage to their presence. This intent, combined with its resilience, makes it a psychological terror for Foundation staff, influencing protocols that forbid casual communication and require heavy force responses to any movement or speech.

Limitations

Attested: Its hatred does not translate into constant motion; it can remain relatively passive when heavily restrained or when no opportunity presents itself, though this is more a tactical lull than genuine calm.

Contradictions

Speculative: Some Scarlet King-related narratives suggest its hatred is part of a larger cosmic agenda rather than purely personal misanthropy, reframing its intent as instrumental rather than emotional.

Resistance and interaction with other SCPs

Source Version Termination logs and SCP Foundation Wikia sections on termination attempts and interactions; WikiHow "Interactions with SCPs" section.

Description

Attested: Termination attempts involving other SCPs (e.g., SCP-409, SCP-017, SCP-999) show that SCP-682 can survive or adapt to a wide range of anomalous effects. In the famous interaction with SCP-999, its mood is temporarily altered, leading to laughter and reduced hostility, but it ultimately reverts and becomes even more dangerous. These interactions highlight both its vulnerability to certain anomalies and its capacity to overcome them.

Limitations

Attested: Some SCPs can significantly affect its behavior or physical state (e.g., SCP-999’s mood alteration, SCP-409’s crystallization), but none have permanently neutralized it in the main continuity.

Contradictions

Fan continuities sometimes depict successful neutralization via specific SCP combinations or cosmic interventions, but these are not reflected in the primary article and remain alternate or speculative.

Archive Dossier — Relationships

SCP Foundation (organization)

Type hostileSource Original SCP-682 article (Special Containment Procedures; Description; Addenda) and WikiHow summary.

Description

Attested: SCP-682 is one of the SCP Foundation’s most dangerous contained entities. The relationship is fundamentally adversarial: the Foundation seeks to secure, contain, and, if possible, destroy SCP-682, while SCP-682 repeatedly attempts to breach containment and kill personnel. Containment procedures, exclusion zones, and termination research are all structured around mitigating its threat.

Version Note

Core continuity; present in all major descriptions.

Conflicting Accounts

None significant; all sources agree on the adversarial nature of the relationship.

Humanity (living beings in general)

Type hostileSource Original SCP-682 article (Description; Addendum 682-B) and SCP Foundation Wikia biography.

Description

Attested: SCP-682 expresses hatred of all life, including humans, in interviews and behavior. It kills farmers prior to containment, devours Class-D personnel during interviews, and causes mass casualties during breaches and escape scenarios. Its omnicidal intent extends beyond the Foundation to living beings as a category.

Version Note

Core continuity; emphasized in most summaries and analyses.

Conflicting Accounts

Some Scarlet King-related narratives frame its hatred as part of a cosmic agenda rather than personal animus, but this does not change the practical hostility toward humans.

D-085 and other Class-D personnel

Type victimSource Original SCP-682 article (Addendum 682-B; Addendum 682-D) and SCP Foundation Wikia breach accounts.

Description

Attested: D-085 is devoured during the initial interview, and numerous other Class-D personnel are killed during breaches and termination attempts. SCP-682 often targets these expendable human subjects first, using them as food or simply as expressions of its hatred.

Version Note

Core continuity; specific names are often partially redacted.

Conflicting Accounts

None; casualty lists may vary in detail but consistently show SCP-682 killing Class-D personnel.

Security Department agents and Mobile Task Forces

Type hostileSource Original SCP-682 article (Addendum 682-D) and SCP Foundation Wikia breach summaries.

Description

Attested: Security agents and Mobile Task Forces engage SCP-682 during breaches and termination attempts, using heavy weaponry and tactics to recontain it. SCP-682 kills many of these personnel, demonstrating its combat capabilities and strategic awareness.

Version Note

Core continuity; names and ranks are often redacted.

Conflicting Accounts

None; all accounts agree on high casualty rates and repeated engagements.

SCP-079 (Old AI)

Type interactionSource Original SCP-682 article (Description) and summarized in fan wikis.

Description

Attested: SCP-682 is observed engaging in complex communication with SCP-079 during limited exposure. This interaction demonstrates its intelligence and ability to converse with non-human entities. The content of their communication is not fully disclosed, but the event is noted as significant.

Version Note

Core continuity; details remain sparse.

Conflicting Accounts

Some fan stories elaborate on the conversation, but these additions are speculative and not part of the original log.

SCP-999

Type hostile with temporary mitigationSource Termination logs and SCP Foundation Wikia "Termination attempts" and "Interactions" sections; WikiHow summary.

Description

Attested: In a famous termination attempt, SCP-999 interacts with SCP-682, inducing intense laughter and temporary mood improvement. SCP-682 appears less hostile during the interaction, leading to speculation that SCP-999 could neutralize it. However, after the effect fades, SCP-682 becomes even more dangerous and expresses intensified hatred, including toward SCP-999.

Version Note

Core continuity within termination logs; widely referenced in fandom.

Conflicting Accounts

Some fan interpretations imagine a scenario where SCP-999 permanently pacifies SCP-682, but this outcome is not supported by the main logs.

SCP-053

Type interactionSource SCP Foundation Wikia and termination attempt summaries (SCP-053 interactions).

Description

Attested: SCP-053, a child-like SCP that causes violent reactions in those who spend time with her, is involved in some SCP-682-related experiments. SCP-682’s interactions with SCP-053 highlight its complex responses to other anomalies and the Foundation’s willingness to risk cross-SCP exposure in search of termination methods.

Version Note

Adaptation-specific but widely cited; not central to the original SCP-682 article.

Conflicting Accounts

Details of the interaction vary by author; some versions emphasize mutual destruction, others partial adaptation.

Scarlet King

Type family (disputed)Source SCP Foundation Wikia biography (Origins section) and Scarlet King-related tales.

Description

Adaptation-specific: Fan lore and some SCP tales identify SCP-682 as a son of the Scarlet King, born from the king’s fourth bride A’zieb as one of many leviathans. In this narrative, SCP-682 and its siblings rot the roots of the Tree of Knowledge and survive a great flood, later inspiring myths of Leviathan and the Tarasque. This relationship situates SCP-682 within a larger cosmic horror genealogy.

Version Note

Non-core continuity; part of specific mythic expansions rather than the original article.

Conflicting Accounts

The original SCP-682 article does not mention the Scarlet King or any familial origin, leaving its genesis unknown. Some community members treat the Scarlet King connection as a compelling but optional continuity rather than canon.

Leviathan and Tarasque (mythic creatures)

Type inspiration (disputed)Source SCP Foundation Wikia biography (Origins section).

Description

Adaptation-specific: In Scarlet King-related lore, SCP-682 and other leviathan spawn are said to have inspired later myths of Leviathan (biblical sea monster) and the Tarasque (French dragon-like creature). This retroactive linkage positions SCP-682 as a primordial archetype behind historical monster stories.

Version Note

Mythic expansion; not present in the original SCP-682 article.

Conflicting Accounts

Historical myths of Leviathan and Tarasque have independent religious and folkloric origins; the SCP connection is a fictional overlay, not a scholarly claim about real-world mythology.

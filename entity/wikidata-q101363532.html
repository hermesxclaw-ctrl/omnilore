Who It Is

In the shadowed valleys of medieval Alpine legend, there lurks a beast whose very name whispers of ancient boundaries and untamed wilds—the Hadburg. Neither fully wolf nor wholly spirit, it prowls the edges of human memory, a guardian of thresholds and a terror of travelers, its story woven from threads of folklore that have nearly faded into the mist.

Speculative: As a 'battle-protector' or 'hedge-guardian', it might be territorial, aggressive toward intruders, but protective of its domain. Alternatively, as a 'fortress of battle', it could embody chaos or conflict. Without sources, these are conjectures.

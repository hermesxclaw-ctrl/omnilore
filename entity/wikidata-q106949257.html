The Approach

The name Hatsune carries the resonance of a Japanese given name that has been borne by actresses, musicians, and even a virtual pop star, but its most famous bearer in the digital age is a synthesized voice that became a global phenomenon. Yet beneath the glitz of Vocaloid fame lies a rich tapestry of real individuals, each with their own story, and a name that echoes through Japanese culture.

Identity

Hatsune

Pronunciation HAH-tsoo-neh

Etymology

Hatsune (初音) is a Japanese given name. The kanji 初 means 'first' and 音 means 'sound' or 'tone', so the name can be interpreted as 'first sound' or 'first note'. It is also a common Japanese surname.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese

Disputed Identifications

The name is often associated with the Vocaloid character Hatsune Miku, but that is a fictional character, not a real person. The name itself is used by several real individuals, and there is no single canonical bearer.

Description

Appearance

The name itself has no physical appearance. For real individuals, appearance varies. For Hatsune Miku, she is typically depicted as a 16-year-old girl with long turquoise twin-tails and a futuristic outfit.

Symbols & Iconography

The name Hatsune is often associated with the number 01 and the image of a musical note, particularly in the context of Hatsune Miku, whose name means 'first sound' and who was the first of the Crypton Vocaloids.

Behaviour & Personality

As a name, it does not have a personality. For Hatsune Miku, she is portrayed as a cheerful, energetic virtual singer.

Domains & Habitats

The name is used in various domains: entertainment (acting, music, modeling), sports (mahjong), and virtual media (Vocaloid).

Version Differences

There is no single entity; the name is shared by multiple unrelated individuals and a fictional character. The fictional character Hatsune Miku is the most prominent in global popular culture.

Powers & Abilities

Singing (for Hatsune Miku)

Source Version Vocaloid 2, 3, 4, etc.

Description

Hatsune Miku is a Vocaloid software voicebank that allows users to synthesize singing. She has a wide vocal range and can perform songs in various genres.

Limitations

Her voice is synthetic and lacks natural expressiveness unless manipulated by producers.

Contradictions

None known.

Stories & Myths

The Creation of Hatsune Miku

Source Crypton Future MediaCulture JapanesePeriod 2007

Summary

Hatsune Miku was released on August 31, 2007, as a Vocaloid 2 voicebank. She was designed as a virtual idol, with a character designed by KEI. Her name means 'first sound', reflecting her position as the first of Crypton's Character Vocaloid series.

Participants

Crypton Future Media

KEI

Consequences

Hatsune Miku became a global phenomenon, leading to concerts, merchandise, and a vast community of creators.

Variants

None for the name itself; the story is specific to the character.

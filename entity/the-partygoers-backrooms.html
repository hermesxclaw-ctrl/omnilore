Who It Is

Humanoid entities with smooth, blood-red skin, no facial features save for a carved-in smile, and long, spindly limbs that end in three-fingered hands. They appear friendly, speak in cheerful tones, and invite wanderers to 'come to the party.' They are not friendly. Those who follow them are taken to Level Fun (=), where they are horrifically transformed into new Partygoers through an agonizing process that replaces their skin, bones, and consciousness.

Form

Tall (2-2.5m), thin humanoids with smooth, blood-red skin, no nose or ears, and a permanent carved-in smile showing white teeth. Fingers end in sharp points. They move in jerky, excited motions and constantly repeat variations of 'Come to the party! It's fun! =)'

Powers

Vocal mimicry of friendly human speech

Transformation of humans into new Partygoers

Group coordination

Lure-based hunting

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

The Partygoers (Backrooms)

also known asBackrooms
Typebackrooms
CultureThe Backrooms
Who It Is
Form

The Partygoers are described as monstrous creatures that have been transformed after attending a mysterious party. They are often associated with the Backrooms, a labyrinthine realm of infinite corridors and rooms. The Partygoers are said to be able to transform into their monstrous form when the lights turn off, and they are often found patrolling the levels of the Backrooms. Their appearance is often described as being similar to that of a humanoid figure with glowing eyes and a twisted, grotesque face.

Powers

Transformation The Partygoers have the ability to transform into their monstrous form when the lights turn off, allowing them to become more aggressive and violent.

Accounts

The story of the Partygoers is one of transformation and violence. Individuals who attend a mysterious party are said to be transformed into monstrous creatures, which are then forced to patrol the levels of the Backrooms.

The story of the Partygoers on the Fun Level is one of transformation and violence. The Partygoers are said to remain as ghost forms until the lights turn off, at which point they transform into their monstrous form and begin patrolling the level.

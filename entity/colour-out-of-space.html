Who It Is

An alien entity that is not matter as we know it — it is a color, a quality, a poison from beyond the stars. It arrived in a meteorite and leeched into the soil of a New England farm, draining all life of vitality, mutating plants and animals, and causing a slow, grotesque decay that affected a family and their land.

Form

No fixed form; described as a 'colour' outside the visible spectrum — a glowing, sickly hue that is both seen and felt, impossible to photograph or describe in human words; sometimes manifests as a glowing mass in wells or soil

Powers

Life-draining radiation

Mutation of flora and fauna

Soil poisoning

Slow psychic decay

Feeding on vitality

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

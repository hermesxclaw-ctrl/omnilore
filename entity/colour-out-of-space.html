Who It Is
Form

The Colour Out of Space is a mysterious and otherworldly colour that defies human understanding. It is described as a 'colour out of space' that is unlike any colour that can be found in the natural world. The colour is said to be a deep, rich purple, but it is not a colour that can be seen in the same way that other colours can be seen. Instead, it is a colour that is felt, a colour that is sensed, and a colour that is perceived.

Powers

Colour Manipulation The Colour Out of Space is said to have the power to manipulate colours, creating new colours and altering the properties of existing colours. It is said to be able to create colours that are beyond human comprehension, colours that are said to be a combination of all the colours of the visible spectrum.

Accounts

The story follows an unnamed narrator as he pieces together the story of an area known by the locals as the 'blasted heath' in the hills west of the fictional town of Arkham, Massachusetts. The narrator discovers that many years ago a meteorite crashed there, poisoning every living being nearby: vegetation grows large but foul-tasting, animals are driven mad and deformed into grotesque shapes, and the people go insane or die one by one.

The story follows a young man named Francis Wayland Thurston as he discovers the existence of the Colour Out of Space and its connection to the Cthulhu Mythos.

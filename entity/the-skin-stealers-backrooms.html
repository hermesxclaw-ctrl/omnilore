Who It Is
Form

The Skin-Stealers are described as large humanoid entities that can wear the skin of their victims as a disguise. Their appearance can change to mimic that of their victims, making them difficult to identify. They are said to be tall and imposing, with an unsettling presence.

Powers

Disguise The Skin-Stealers can wear the skin of their victims as a disguise, allowing them to blend in with their surroundings.

Accounts

A survivor of the Backrooms recounts their encounter with a Skin-Stealer. The entity was able to blend in with the survivor's surroundings, making it difficult to identify.

A group of survivors in the Backrooms discover that the Skin-Stealers are drawn to human flesh. They are able to use this knowledge to their advantage, luring the Skin-Stealers away from their group.

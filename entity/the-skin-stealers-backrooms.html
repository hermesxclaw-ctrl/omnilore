Who It Is

Entities that wear the flayed skin of human victims as camouflage. Underneath their stolen skin, they are gaunt, gray, hairless humanoids with disproportionately large heads and needle-like teeth. They can mimic human speech imperfectly — their stolen vocal cords produce sounds that are close to human but 'wrong' in ways that trigger deep unease. They hunt in groups and can wear skins for weeks before needing a replacement.

Form

When 'disguised': indistinguishable from a human, though movements are slightly jerky and they occasionally 'adjust' their skin. True form: gray, emaciated humanoid, approximately 1.9m tall, with a bulbous head, no eyes, no nose, and a mouth full of needle-like teeth.

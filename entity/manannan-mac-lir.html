Who It Is
Form

Manannán mac Lir is often depicted as a powerful, wise, and majestic figure, with a strong connection to the sea and its creatures. He is sometimes shown as a warrior, wielding a sword or spear, and other times as a benevolent ruler, surrounded by the beauty of the Otherworld.

Powers

Control over the Sea Manannán mac Lir has the power to control the tides, calm storms, and protect sailors from the dangers of the sea.

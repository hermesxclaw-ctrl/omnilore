Who It Is

Manannán mac Lir is the Irish sea god and sorcerer-king of the Otherworld. He possesses Fragarach (sword of truth), the crane-bag, the horse Enbarr, and the féth fíada cloak of invisibility that concealed the Tuatha Dé Danann after their defeat. He ferries souls to the afterlife and hosts the feast of immortality in Tír na nÓg.

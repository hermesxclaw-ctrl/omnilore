Who It Is

She was ten days old when her father's kingdom burned. Carried screaming from the asteroid fortress of A Baoa Qu as her father Dozle Zabi died trying to hold the line, Mineva Lao Zabi inherited nothing but a name that could start wars — the last blood of the family that plunged humanity into the One Year War. For the next seventeen years, generals, regents and zealots would dress her in velvet, place her on thrones, and speak in her voice, while the girl herself learned how to disappear.

Attested personality differs sharply by era and author. In ZZ (Tomino), 8-year-old Mineva is largely silent, solemn, and observant — rarely speaking, often clutching a doll (Haro-like), with occasional flashes of stubborn will (refusing Haman's coaching, calling out to Judau). Dialogue is minimal; behavior reads as traumatized child aware of being used. In Unicorn (Fukui), 16-year-old Mineva/Audrey is articulate, politically astute, idealistic, and burdened. Powers
Dynastic Legitimacy (Zabi Bloodline)

As the sole surviving child of Dozle Zabi and granddaughter of Degwin Sodo Zabi, Mineva is the last bearer of the Zabi name that ruled the Principality of Zeon. In Universal Century politics, this bloodline functions as a quasi-legal claim to leadership over Zeon remnants. Her mere existence can legitimize or delegitimize regimes — Haman Karn ruled 'in her name' as regent; Full Frontal and the Sleeves claim to act for her; the Vist Foundation's Laplace's Box is keyed to her lineage via the La+ System.

Limit: Legitimacy is symbolic, not institutional; she commands no army personally and can be ignored or replaced by force (as seen when Haman ignores her objections). After Unicorn, she publicly renounces the throne, voluntarily extinguishing the claim.

Political Oratory and Education

Raised and educated by Axis elite and later by Vist Foundation tutors, Mineva possesses extensive knowledge of Universal Century history, the original Laplace Charter, and Zeon Deikun's philosophy. In Unicorn she delivers speeches that sway both Federation and Zeon soldiers, articulating a vision of humanity's future beyond Earth-centric governance.

Limit: Her influence depends on being heard; she is frequently silenced or dismissed as a child. No formal political office.

La+ System Biometric Key

In Unicorn, the RX-0 Unicorn Gundam's La+ (La Plus) psycho-frame system is programmed to identify and respond to the bloodline of the original Zeon leadership. Mineva's presence (and later Banagher Links' Newtype resonance with her) unlocks coordinates to Laplace's Box. This is technological, not supernatural — a DNA/brainwave lock designed by Cardeas Vist.

Limit: Requires proximity to Unicorn Gundam and Vist Foundation hardware; not a personal power. Does not function outside that system.

Newtype Potential (Disputed)

Mineva exhibits heightened empathy, intuition, and occasional resonance with the psycho-frame (hearing voices of the dead, sensing Banagher's feelings). Fukui describes her as having 'the sensibility of a Newtype' without confirmed ability. She is not shown piloting a mobile suit via psycommu or manifesting classic Newtype flashes.

Limit: No confirmed Newtype feats (no telepathy, precognition, or psycommu control). Any sensitivity is latent and narrative, not mechanical.

Survival and Evasion

Repeatedly survives assassination attempts, wars, and political purges from infancy to adulthood through protection of loyalists (Zeena, Haman initially, later Suberoa Zinnerman and the Garancieres crew) and her own decision to abandon the Zabi name and live as Audrey Burne.

Limit: Dependent on protectors; not a combat skill. Her survival is narrative, not superhuman resilience.

Universal Century 0079 (One Year War) - depicted 1979-1980, expanded 2007

Mineva Lao Zabi is born in late UC 0079 to Dozle Zabi, commander of Zeon's Space Attack Force, and his wife Zeena Mia Zabi, inside the asteroid fortress A Baoa Qu. Days after her birth, the Earth Federation launches its final assault. Dozle is killed in battle against Amuro Ray's Gundam while Zeena flees with the newborn. In The Origin, Char Aznable personally arranges their escape shuttle, recognizing the infant's political value and perhaps out of lingering loyalty to Zeena. Mother and daughter escape the fortress as it falls, becoming the sole surviving members of the Zabi family after Gihren, Kycilia, Sasro, and Degwin are all killed within days. Establishes Mineva as the last Zabi, a living relic whose existence will haunt Zeon politics for two decades. Her survival enables the Axis/Neo Zeon movement; without her, Haman Karn would have no legitimate figurehead.

UC 0087-0088

Raised in the asteroid base Axis, now led by Regent Haman Karn, 7-year-old Mineva is paraded as the rightful ruler of the restored Principality of Zeon. Haman, who was Mineva's caretaker and de facto mother figure, uses the child's authority to command loyalty from Zeon remnants who fled after the war. Mineva lives in luxury but under constant surveillance, educated and coached to recite speeches she barely understands. When Axis intervenes in the Gryps Conflict between AEUG, Titans, and Axis, Mineva is kept aboard the flagship Sadalahn. In ZZ, after Haman's forces return to the Earth Sphere, Judau Ashta and the Argama crew encounter Mineva; Judau, himself a teenager, is struck by how a child is forced to bear a nation's sins. Mineva shows quiet defiance, at one point rejecting Haman's scripted lines and later being rescued by Judau's group, though she remains torn between affection for Haman and fear of her ambition. UC 0089-0095 (interwar period)

After Haman Karn's death in the First Neo Zeon War (UC 0088) and the collapse of Glemy Toto's rebellion, Mineva is secretly removed from the public eye. Officially, the Zabi line is declared extinct to protect her from assassination by Federation hardliners and Zeon extremists who would use her. According to Unicorn backstory (told via Cardeas Vist and Syam Vist flashbacks), she is entrusted to the Vist Foundation and to former Zeon captain Suberoa Zinnerman's network, living under assumed identities on Earth and in colonies. She chooses to abandon the Zabi name, cutting her hair and taking the name Audrey Burne after being moved by Audrey Hepburn films shown to her by a caretaker. This period is largely off-screen, narrated as exposition, but crucial: it is when Mineva educates herself about the original Laplace Charter and decides the Zabi ideology must end. Transforms Mineva from passive symbol to active agent. Her decision to live as Audrey enables her to act independently in UC 0096 without triggering immediate war.

At age 16, Mineva, calling herself Audrey Burne, infiltrates the Vist Foundation's private colony Industrial 7 to prevent the foundation's head Cardeas Vist from handing Laplace's Box — the original UC 0001 charter revealing the Federation's suppressed promise of Spacenoid autonomy — to the Sleeves (Neo Zeon remnants led by Full Frontal, who claims to be Char). She parachutes from a shuttle, is rescued by 16-year-old student Banagher Links, and gradually reveals her true identity to him. Together with Banagher and the Unicorn Gundam, she evades both Federation pursuers (led by Alberto Vist and Martha Vist Carbine) and Sleeves forces, traveling to the ruins of Laplace, to Torrington, to the desert, and finally to Industrial 7's core. She confronts Full Frontal, rejecting his plan to use the Box to reignite war, and argues that the Box should be opened to the world to force humanity to confront its original sin. UC 0097-0100

In the epilogue of Unicorn, Mineva, now publicly known but choosing privacy, attends the memorial for those lost and watches as Banagher departs. In Gundam Narrative, set one year later (UC 0097), she is referenced as being in seclusion under Federation protection, with Luio & Co. and Mineva's Foundation handling the Box's aftermath. She does not appear physically in Narrative except in flashback/photo, but dialogue confirms she has not reclaimed the throne and is working behind the scenes to ensure the Box's revelations are not suppressed. Later timeline materials (Gundam UC 0096-0100) suggest she remains alive, hidden, and intentionally childless to end the Zabi line, though Sunrise has left her ultimate fate open. Leaves Mineva's story intentionally unresolved — a living symbol who chooses obscurity over power, embodying Fukui's theme that history should not be inherited uncritically.

Audrey Burne is a separate character from Mineva Lao Zabi — Audrey Burne is an alias adopted by Mineva to hide her Zabi identity. This is explicitly revealed in Unicorn Episode 2-3 and novel Volume 2, when she confesses to Banagher and later publicly declares 'I am Mineva Lao Zabi.' The two names refer to the same person.

Mineva is Haman Karn's daughter — Mineva is Dozle and Zeena Zabi's daughter. Haman Karn was her regent and guardian, not her mother. Haman was daughter of Maharaja Karn, a Zeon admiral. The confusion arises because Haman raised Mineva from infancy and they appear together as mother-daughter in ZZ.

Mineva piloted a mobile suit or was a Cyber-Newtype like Ple clones — Mineva never piloted a mobile suit in any canonical work and was never subjected to Cyber-Newtype experimentation. She is a political figure, not a pilot. The Cyber-Newtypes associated with her (Ple, Ple Two/Marida) were created by Glemy Toto's faction, not from her.

Mineva died at the end of Gundam ZZ or Unicorn — Mineva survives both wars. At end of ZZ she is rescued and goes into hiding; at end of Unicorn she survives and chooses to live privately, appearing in epilogue. Sunrise has confirmed she is alive as of UC 0097 (Narrative).

The Approach

In a world where magic and technology collide, Nine the Phantom stands as a master of the arcane, a survivor of war, and a guardian of secrets. Her story is one of loss, revenge, and redemption, weaving through the fabric of the BlazBlue universe.

Content advisory. Contains themes of death, war, and psychological trauma.

Etymology

The name 'Nine' likely refers to her designation as the ninth Prime Field Device, while 'Phantom' reflects her mysterious and ghost-like nature. Her real name, Konoe A. Mercury, connects her to the Mercury family, known for their mastery of Ars Magus.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese video game franchise BlazBlue, created by Arc System Works

Disputed Identifications

None known

Description

Appearance

Nine the Phantom is a tall, elegant woman with long, silver hair and striking red eyes. She wears a dark, gothic-style dress with intricate gold trim, and her outfit includes a large hat and a cape. Her design emphasizes her mysterious and powerful nature.

Symbols & Iconography

She is associated with the number nine, referencing her status as the ninth Prime Field Device. Her magical circles and spells often feature geometric patterns and runes.

Behaviour & Personality

Nine is highly intelligent, calm, and calculating. She is fiercely protective of her family, particularly her brother and her students. She can be cold and ruthless towards her enemies, but shows a caring side to those she trusts.

Domains & Habitats

As a witch and researcher, she is often found in libraries or her own laboratory. She is also associated with the magical organization known as the Six Heroes, and later with the resistance against the Novus Orbis Librarium (NOL).

Version Differences

In her younger years, she was more idealistic and passionate. After the events that led to the death of her sister, she became more cynical and hardened. Her appearance also changes slightly between games, with more detailed costumes in later installments.

Contradictions & Disputes

Nine's True Identity

Position A

Nine is a human witch and a member of the Six Heroes.

Position A — evidence

She is shown to have a sister and a human past.

Position B

Nine is hinted to be the ninth Prime Field Device, a synthetic being created by the NOL.

Position B — evidence

In Central Fiction, it is revealed that all Prime Field Devices are artificial, and Nine's extraordinary abilities suggest a non-human origin.

Scholarly Consensus

The game's lore suggests that Nine is indeed the ninth Prime Field Device, but this is not explicitly confirmed in the main story.

Who It Is
Form

Hinn are often described as shape-shifters, capable of taking on various forms, including that of dogs. They are said to be tall, slender creatures with an otherworldly beauty.

Powers

Shape-shifting Hinn are said to be able to take on various forms, including that of animals, humans, and objects.

Telepathy Hinn are said to possess the ability to read and control the thoughts of others.

Elemental control Hinn are said to have the power to control the elements, including wind, water, and fire.

Accounts

The Prophet Muhammad is said to have encountered a Hinn while on a journey. The Hinn appeared to him in the form of a dog and spoke to him in a human voice.

A group of travelers stumbled upon a lost city deep in the desert, where they encountered a group of Hinn. The Hinn were said to be the guardians of the city and its secrets.

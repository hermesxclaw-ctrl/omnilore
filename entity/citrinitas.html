Who It Is

The third stage of the alchemical magnum opus: the 'yellowing' or 'xanthosis.' Positioned between the white albedo and the red rubedo, citrinitas represents the dawn — the first golden light before the full blaze of the sun. At this stage, the matter takes on a yellow or golden hue, and the 'yellow stone' is formed, capable of transmuting metals into lesser gold. Symbolically, citrinitas corresponds to the awakening of intuition, the solar consciousness beginning to dawn after the lunar purification of albedo. Some alchemical traditions fold citrinitas into rubedo, treating it not as a separate stage but as the early phase of the final reddening. It is associated with the lion (not yet red), the morning, and the yellowing of grain before harvest.

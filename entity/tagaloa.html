Name & Tongue

Etymology — Derived from the Samoan words 'tagalo' meaning 'to drift' and 'a' meaning 'alone', referring to the deity's solitary existence in the void.

Pronunciation — tah-GAH-loh-ah

Aliases — Tagaloa Lagi, The One Who Drifted Alone

Ownership — Samoan mythology, specifically the islands of Samoa and American Samoa.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Tagaloa is often depicted as a powerful, wise, and benevolent deity, with the ability to create and shape the universe.

Symbols — The symbol associated with Tagaloa is the 'tagaloa' or 'drifting' motif, which represents the deity's ability to navigate the void and create the world.

Domains — Tagaloa's domain is the sky and the heavens, where he resides as the supreme ruler of the Samoan pantheon.

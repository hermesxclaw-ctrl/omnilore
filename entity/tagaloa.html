Who It Is
Form

Tagaloa is often depicted as a powerful, wise, and benevolent deity, with the ability to create and shape the universe.

Powers

Creation Tagaloa has the power to create and shape the universe, bringing forth the world and all its inhabitants from the void.

Accounts

Tagaloa creates the world and all its inhabitants, including the islands of Samoa and the creatures that live there.

Tagaloa gifts the Samoan people with the way of life, known as fa'a Samoa, which emphasizes the importance of respect, kindness, and generosity.

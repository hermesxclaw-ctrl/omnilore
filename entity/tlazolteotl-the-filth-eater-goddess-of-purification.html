Who It Is
Form

Tlazolteotl is often depicted as a woman with a large belly, symbolizing her role as a goddess of fertility and childbirth. She is also associated with the color blue, which represents her connection to the sky and the heavens.

Powers

Devouring of Sins Tlazolteotl has the power to devour the sins of humanity, representing her role as a goddess of purification and cleanliness.

Accounts

Tlazolteotl is said to have been born from the primordial waters of the earth, representing her connection to the fertility and childbirth.

Tlazolteotl is said to have created the 'tlazolli', a type of filth or dirt that she is said to devour.

Who It Is
Form

The Harvesters are humanoid figures with elongated bodies, often depicted with pale skin and dark eyes. They are said to be able to blend seamlessly into their surroundings, making them nearly invisible. Their appearance can vary depending on the context and the storyteller.

Powers

Regeneration The Harvesters are said to have the ability to regenerate their bodies, making them nearly indestructible.

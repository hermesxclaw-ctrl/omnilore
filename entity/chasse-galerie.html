Who It Is
Form

The Chasse-galerie is a canoe that is said to be able to fly through the air, often accompanied by a group of ghostly hunters.

Powers

Flight The Chasse-galerie is said to be able to fly through the air, allowing it to traverse great distances in a short amount of time.

Accounts

A group of lumberjacks make a deal with the devil to be able to fly through the air in a canoe, but they soon find themselves being pursued by the devil and his minions.

The Chasse-galerie is a popular French-Canadian tale of a canoe that is said to be able to fly through the air.

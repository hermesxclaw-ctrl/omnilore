Who It Is

A spherical entity of unknown composition, approximately 0.5 meters in diameter. Its sole motivation is to frighten humans by transforming into the subjectively 'scariest' thing it can imagine. Unfortunately, SCP-2006 has a profoundly limited understanding of what is actually scary — a flaw that can be exploited. Its transformations include giant spiders, 'Ro-Man' from the 1953 film 'Robot Monster,' and a 'spooky' ball of gas.

Form

Default form: a spherical, smooth black mass. Transforms into various 'horror' forms: poorly-made movie monsters, cartoon ghosts, or spiders with visibly fake rubber legs.

Powers

Shape-shifting limited by its own concept of fear

Size alteration

Atmospheric manipulation for dramatic effect

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

Who It Is
Form

SCP-2006 is a humanoid entity with a pale complexion and sunken eyes. It is often described as being tall and imposing, with an aura of dread surrounding it.

Powers

Reality Distortion SCP-2006 has the ability to distort reality, creating illusions and hallucinations that can be extremely disorienting and terrifying.

Incident Log

SCP-2006 was created by a group of scientists who were experimenting with reality distortion.

A team of agents from the SCP Foundation was sent to capture SCP-2006, but they were ultimately unsuccessful.

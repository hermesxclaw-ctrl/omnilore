The Approach

Once a proud king of a fallen kingdom, Viego's love for his queen Isolde led him to unleash the Ruination—a magical cataclysm that swept across Runeterra, turning all it touched into the undead. His is a tale of obsession, grief, and the terrible price of defying death itself.

Content advisory. Themes of death, grief, obsession, undeath, and the consequences of catastrophic magical events. Some depictions include violence and destructive rage.

Description

Appearance

Viego appears as a tall, gaunt figure with pale, greyish skin and long white hair. He wears dark, ornate armor with regal accents, a crown, and a cape. His eyes glow with a green spectral light. He is often depicted wielding a sword and surrounded by black mist.

Symbols & Iconography

Symbols associated with Viego include the Black Mist, his crown, and his sword. The Black Mist is a supernatural fog that corrupts the living and raises the dead.

Behaviour & Personality

Viego is driven by an obsessive love for his deceased wife, Isolde. He is arrogant, ruthless, and sees himself as a tragic figure. He is willing to destroy entire nations to reunite with her, showing a lack of empathy for others. His actions are often impulsive and destructive.

Domains & Habitats

Viego's domain is the Shadow Isles, a cursed land shrouded in the Black Mist. He is also able to manifest the Mist anywhere in Runeterra, threatening the living world.

Version Differences

In the base League of Legends lore, Viego is the primary antagonist of the Shadow Isles. In the 2021 'Ruination' event (including the visual novel 'Ruined King: A League of Legends Story'), his backstory and motivations are expanded, and he is depicted as more sympathetic in his grief. Some details, such as the exact nature of Isolde's death, are consistent, but the event adds new interactions with other champions.

Black Mist Manipulation

Source Version League of Legends lore and gameplay

Description

Viego can summon and control the Black Mist, a corrupting fog that drains life and raises the dead as undead servants. He can use it to teleport, conceal himself, and attack enemies.

Limitations

The Mist is tied to his obsession; if he loses focus or is separated from his goal, his control may weaken. Also, the Mist is less effective against certain magical protections.

Stories & Myths

The Ruination

Source League of Legends Universe - Viego's biography and Ruination eventCulture Fictional - RuneterraPeriod Approximately 1000 years before the present day of Runeterra

Summary

Viego was the king of Camavor. He fell deeply in love with a seamstress named Isolde, whom he married. When Isolde was assassinated with a poisoned blade, Viego was devastated. He sought any means to bring her back, eventually finding the Blessed Isles, a land of magical waters that could heal any wound. He took Isolde's body there and submerged it in the waters, but the magic was not enough. In his desperation, he used the magical sword of the isles to pierce his own heart, and the resulting magic resurrected Isolde, but she was not herself. She stabbed him with the same sword, and his blood and grief triggered a magical cataclysm that transformed the Blessed Isles into the Shadow Isles, unleashing the Black Mist upon the world. Viego became the Ruined King, and his undead legion now seeks to spread the Mist across Runeterra.

Consequences

The Blessed Isles became the Shadow Isles, a cursed land. The Black Mist now threatens Runeterra. Viego's obsession continues to drive him to find a way to resurrect Isolde completely.

Variants

The Ruination event in 2021 expanded the story, showing Viego's attempts to use other champions to complete his goal. Some details, such as the exact nature of Isolde's resurrection, are depicted differently in the visual novel 'Ruined King'.

Viego travels to Bilgewater and the Shadow Isles to find a way to resurrect Isolde. He manipulates several champions, including Miss Fortune, Illaoi, and Braum, to help him. Eventually, he is defeated by a group of heroes, but the Mist remains.

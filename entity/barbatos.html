Who It Is

Eighth spirit, a Duke commanding 30 legions. Barbatos appears when the sun is in Sagittarius, accompanied by four noble kings and their companies. He grants the ability to understand the languages of animals — birdsong, the barking of dogs, the lowing of cattle — and reveals hidden treasures that have been concealed by enchantment. He also reconciles friends and those in power. Barbatos was once of the order of the Virtues before the fall, retaining knowledge of natural languages lost to humanity. He is a hunter of buried secrets.

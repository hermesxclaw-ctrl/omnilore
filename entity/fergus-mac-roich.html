Who It Is
Form

Fergus mac Róich is described as being of huge size and sexual potency. He is often depicted as a powerful and intimidating figure.

Powers

Superhuman Strength Fergus mac Róich is described as being of huge size and possessing superhuman strength, allowing him to perform feats of incredible physical power.

Accounts

The Táin Bó Cúailnge is a story from the Ulster Cycle in which Fergus mac Róich joins the expedition of Queen Medb of Connacht to steal the famous brown bull of Cooley.

The Táin Bó Flidhais is a story from the Ulster Cycle in which Fergus mac Róich is tricked out of the kingship of Ulster and becomes the ally and lover of Queen Medb of Connacht.

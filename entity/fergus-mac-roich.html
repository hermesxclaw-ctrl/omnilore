Who It Is

Fergus was a former king of Ulster tricked out of his throne. He served Queen Medb but retained love for Ulster, deliberately undermining her invasion. He was Cú Chulainn's foster-father and wielded Caladbolg, a sword that could cut the tops off three hills.

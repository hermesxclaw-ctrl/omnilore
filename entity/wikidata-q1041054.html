The Approach

Ryota Miyagi is the pint-sized point guard of Shohoku High, whose lightning-fast drives and street-smart play make him the engine of the team's offense. But beneath his cool exterior lies a troubled past—a delinquent who found redemption on the basketball court, and a young man carrying the weight of family tragedy.

Content advisory. Contains themes of delinquency, violence, grief, and loss of a family member.

Etymology

Miyagi is a common Japanese surname and also the name of a prefecture in Japan. Ryota is a given name.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese popular culture, created by Takehiko Inoue

Disputed Identifications

None known; he is a unique fictional character.

Description

Appearance

Miyagi is notably short for a basketball player, standing at 168 cm (5'6"). He has a slim build, short black hair, and often sports a confident smirk. He is frequently seen with an earring, which he started wearing after his brother's death.

Symbols & Iconography

His earring symbolizes his connection to his late brother. His jersey number 7 is iconic.

Behaviour & Personality

Initially portrayed as a delinquent with a quick temper, Miyagi is also fiercely loyal and protective of his teammates. On the court, he is a cool-headed playmaker with exceptional ball-handling and court vision. He often engages in trash talk but respects skilled opponents. Off the court, he has a playful side, but his past trauma surfaces in moments of reflection.

Domains & Habitats

High school basketball courts, particularly Shohoku's gymnasium. He also frequents the streets and parks where he practices.

Version Differences

The 2022 film The First Slam Dunk expands on Miyagi's backstory, depicting his childhood in Okinawa, his brother's death, and his strained relationship with his mother. This backstory is not present in the original manga or anime, where his past is only hinted at.

Archivist Notes

Known Gaps

The full backstory of Miyagi's brother Sota is only present in the 2022 film; the manga and anime do not provide details. Information about Miyagi's life after the series is not available. There is no official English translation of the Slam Dunk guidebook, so some details may be missing. The film's backstory has not been integrated into the manga continuity, so its canonicity is debated among fans.

Research Scope

This dossier focuses on Ryota Miyagi as depicted in Takehiko Inoue's Slam Dunk manga and its anime adaptation, including the 2022 film The First Slam Dunk. It covers his character biography, relationships, and reception. Due to the fictional nature of the character, all information is derived from the fictional universe.

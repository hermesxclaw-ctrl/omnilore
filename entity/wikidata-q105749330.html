The Approach

In the vibrant world of BlazBlue, where reality is shaped by the Azure and the boundaries between life and death are thin, Mai Natsume stands out as a testament to the series' flair for blending modern combat with ancient mysticism. With her distinctive red-and-white attire and a fighting style that honors the legacy of the legendary Hakumen, Mai captivates players not only through her gameplay but also through her intriguing backstory that ties her to the very fabric of the series' lore.

Content advisory. This dossier contains discussions of combat, violence, and themes typical of a fighting game. No explicit dark material is known to be associated with Mai Natsume.
Identity

Mai Natsume

Pronunciation MY-ee NAHT-soo-meh

Etymology

The name 'Mai' (舞) can mean 'dance', and 'Natsume' (棗) refers to the jujube fruit, but no official etymology has been provided by the developers.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese video game franchise BlazBlue, developed by Arc System Works and published by Aksys Games in North America.

Disputed Identifications

No known disputes. Mai Natsume is a distinct character, not to be confused with Mai Shiranui from The King of Fighters or other similarly named characters.

Description

Appearance

Mai Natsume is a young woman with long, flowing black hair and bright red eyes. She wears a traditional Japanese-inspired outfit consisting of a white and red hakama-like ensemble, with a large red bow at her chest. Her attire is reminiscent of a shrine maiden, but with a modern, fighting-game flair. She carries a large, ornate blade that resembles a nodachi, which she wields with grace and power.

Symbols & Iconography

Her color scheme of red and white, along with her shrine-maiden-like appearance, suggests a connection to Japanese spirituality and the sacred. The blade she carries is a symbol of her warrior heritage and her link to the legendary Hakumen.

Behaviour & Personality

Mai is depicted as a cheerful and energetic young woman, often displaying a positive and determined attitude. She is respectful and polite, reflecting her traditional upbringing, but she is also fiercely competitive and eager to prove herself. She has a strong sense of justice and a desire to protect others, which drives her to fight.

Domains & Habitats

Mai hails from the world of BlazBlue, a universe where magic and technology intertwine. She is associated with the 'Boundary', a conceptual space between life and death, and her abilities are tied to the 'Azure', the fundamental force of creation.

Version Differences

Mai first appeared as a playable character in BlazBlue: Cross Tag Battle, where her moveset and personality were introduced. In BlazBlue: Central Fiction, she was added as a DLC character, with additional story content and interactions. No major differences in her characterization or abilities have been noted between the two appearances.

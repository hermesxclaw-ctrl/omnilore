Who It Is

In the vibrant world of BlazBlue, where reality is shaped by the Azure and the boundaries between life and death are thin, Mai Natsume stands out as a testament to the series' flair for blending modern combat with ancient mysticism. With her distinctive red-and-white attire and a fighting style that honors the legacy of the legendary Hakumen, Mai captivates players not only through her gameplay but also through her intriguing backstory that ties her to the very fabric of the series' lore.

Mai is depicted as a cheerful and energetic young woman, often displaying a positive and determined attitude. She is respectful and polite, reflecting her traditional upbringing, but she is also fiercely competitive and eager to prove herself. She has a strong sense of justice and a desire to protect others, which drives her to fight.

Powers
Hakumen's Legacy

Mai wields a weapon and fighting style that are spiritually linked to Hakumen, a powerful warrior from another timeline. This grants her access to techniques that manipulate the Azure, allowing for powerful and precise strikes.

Limit: Her connection to Hakumen is not fully explored, and she may not have the full range of his abilities.

Blade Mastery

Mai is highly skilled in swordsmanship, capable of executing rapid and devastating combos with her nodachi. Her fighting style emphasizes speed and precision, allowing her to close distances quickly and overwhelm opponents.

Limit: Her reliance on her weapon makes her vulnerable if disarmed, though such situations are rare in gameplay.

Spiritual Energy Manipulation

Mai can channel spiritual energy to enhance her attacks and perform special moves that have a mystical appearance, such as creating energy waves or summoning ethereal blades.

Limit: The extent of her spiritual powers is not fully detailed, and it is unclear if she can use them independently of her weapon.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Mai Natsume is a young woman with long, flowing black hair and bright red eyes. She wears a traditional Japanese-inspired outfit consisting of a white and red hakama-like ensemble, with a large red bow at her chest. Her attire is reminiscent of a shrine maiden, but with a modern, fighting-game flair. She carries a large, ornate blade that resembles a nodachi, which she wields with grace and power.

Symbols — Her color scheme of red and white, along with her shrine-maiden-like appearance, suggests a connection to Japanese spirituality and the sacred. The blade she carries is a symbol of her warrior heritage and her link to the legendary Hakumen.

Domains — Mai hails from the world of BlazBlue, a universe where magic and technology intertwine. She is associated with the 'Boundary', a conceptual space between life and death, and her abilities are tied to the 'Azure', the fundamental force of creation.

Mai's Story in BlazBlue: Central FictionBlazBlue: Central Fiction (video game)

2016 (game release)

Mai Natsume is a mysterious young woman who appears in the world of BlazBlue, drawn by the disturbances caused by the Azure. She is a warrior with a deep connection to the legendary Hakumen, and she seeks to understand her own past and purpose. Throughout the story, she encounters various characters from the main cast, and her interactions reveal her noble spirit and her determination to protect the innocent. Her ultimate goal is to help restore balance to the world, even if it means facing powerful adversaries. Mai's actions contribute to the resolution of the conflict, and she gains a deeper understanding of her own identity and the nature of the Azure.

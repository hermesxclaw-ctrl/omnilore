Who It Is

Deep within the forests of Patam Cloud, a small, furry creature known as Kororo serves as the loyal companion to the shaman Horohoro. But Kororo is no ordinary animal—it is a spirit of the Ainu people, embodying the Koropokkur, a mythical race of tiny people who once lived in harmony with nature. As Horohoro's partner in the Shaman Fight, Kororo's gentle presence belies a fierce determination to protect the natural world and honor the traditions of his ancestors.

Kororo is gentle, loyal, and protective of his friends, especially Horohoro. He communicates through a series of squeaks and gestures, which Horohoro understands. He is brave in battle, using his ice-based powers to support Horohoro. He is also playful and curious, often exploring his surroundings.

Powers
Ice Manipulation

Kororo can generate and control ice, creating barriers, projectiles, and freezing enemies. He often combines his powers with Horohoro's own ice abilities for devastating attacks.

Limit: Kororo's ice powers require his spiritual energy, and he can be exhausted if overused. He cannot create ice from nothing; he needs moisture in the air or water.

Spirit Communication

As a spirit, Kororo can communicate with other spirits and sense spiritual presences. He can also merge with Horohoro to enhance his powers.

Limit: He can only communicate with spirits that are willing to talk, and his ability to sense presences is limited by distance and spiritual interference.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Kororo is a small, round creature with a fluffy, white and brown coat. He has large, expressive eyes and a small, black nose. He often carries a small leaf on his head, which is a characteristic trait. In his spirit form, he appears as a faint, glowing version of his physical form.

Symbols — Kororo is often depicted with a leaf, symbolizing his connection to nature. His small stature and gentle demeanor reflect the Koropokkur folklore, who are known as nature spirits.

Domains — Kororo is often seen with Horohoro, whether in the human world or in the spiritual realm. He is associated with nature, particularly forests and snowy environments, reflecting his ice abilities.

Kororo is introduced as Horohoro's spirit companion. They have a close bond, with Kororo often riding on Horohoro's shoulder. In one of their early appearances, they fight together against a common enemy, showcasing their teamwork. Kororo and Horohoro continue to fight together throughout the series, with Kororo proving to be a valuable ally in the Shaman Fight.

Kororo assists Horohoro in the Shaman Fight, using his ice powers to help Horohoro win battles. He also participates in the final battle against Hao, contributing to the team's efforts. Kororo's contributions help Horohoro's team progress in the tournament, and his loyalty is a key factor in their success.

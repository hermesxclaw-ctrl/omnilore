Who They Are

Amalur ('Mother Earth') is the Basque earth goddess, the primordial mother who gave birth to the sun, moon, and stars. She is the source of all fertility, the substance from which all life emerges and to which all life returns. She is distinct from Mari — while Mari rules from the mountains, Amalur is the very substance of the land itself.

Cultural Significance

Amalur embodies the deep agricultural spirituality of the Basque people. The Basque relationship with the land — the 'baserri' (farmstead) system, pastoral rights, and communal land management — reflects a worldview where the earth is literally a sacred mother. Many Basque traditions surrounding the first plowing, the harvest festival, and the blessing of fields have their roots in the veneration of Amalur. The concept of 'lurren ama' (mother of the lands) is still invoked in some rural blessings.

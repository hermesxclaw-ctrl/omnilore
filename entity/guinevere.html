Form & Signs

Appearance — Guinevere is often depicted as a beautiful and noble queen, with a strong sense of loyalty and duty.

Symbols — Guinevere is associated with the symbols of the White Phantom and the Fair Guenevere, reflecting her reputation as a virtuous and noble queen.

Domains — Guinevere's domain is the kingdom of Camelot, where she serves as queen and wife to King Arthur.

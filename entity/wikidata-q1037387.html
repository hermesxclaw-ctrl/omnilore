Who It Is

In the unremarkable town of Amity Park, a fourteen-year-old boy steps into a machine built by his parents to pierce the veil between life and death. When the portal hums to life, it does not just open a door—it rewrites his DNA, fusing him with the very substance of ghosts. Now, Danny Fenton must balance homework, crushes, and the constant threat of his own parents hunting him, all while learning to wield powers that make him both the town's savior and its most wanted spirit.

Danny is initially portrayed as an ordinary, somewhat insecure teenager who is often bullied. After gaining powers, he becomes more confident and heroic, but struggles with the responsibility and secrecy. He is witty, sarcastic, and often cracks jokes during battles. He is fiercely protective of his friends and family, and gradually matures into a responsible hero. He has a strong sense of justice, but sometimes acts impulsively.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Danny' is a diminutive of 'Daniel', meaning 'God is my judge'. 'Phantom' derives from Greek 'phantasma', meaning 'apparition' or 'ghost'. The alias 'Danny Phantom' was chosen by Danny himself as a superhero name.

Pronunciation — DAN-ee FAN-tuhm

Aliases — Danny Fenton (human name), Phantom, The Ghost Boy, Inviso-Bill (occasionally used alias)

Ownership — Created by Butch Hartman for Nickelodeon; American animated television series. The character is owned by Nickelodeon and ViacomCBS.

Powers
Flight

Danny can fly at high speeds, often leaving a ghostly trail.

Limit: Can be tiring if used extensively; some ghosts can outfly him.

Invisibility

He can become completely invisible to the naked eye, though some technology or supernatural beings can detect him.

Limit: Some ghosts and technology can see him.

Intangibility

He can phase through solid objects and matter, making him immune to physical attacks.

Limit: He cannot stay intangible indefinitely; some energy attacks can still harm him.

Overshadowing

He can possess and control living beings, including humans and animals, by merging his ghost form with them.

Limit: The possessed individual can sometimes resist or fight back; Danny's control is not absolute.

Ghost Ray

A concentrated blast of ecto-energy fired from his hands, capable of stunning or destroying ghosts and damaging physical objects.

Limit: Requires concentration; can be blocked by some ghostly defenses.

Ghostly Wail

An incredibly powerful scream that creates a shockwave, capable of leveling buildings and knocking back even powerful foes.

Limit: Leaves Danny severely weakened and exhausted; he cannot use it frequently.

Cryokinesis

He can generate and manipulate ice and cold, often used to freeze enemies or create ice constructs.

Limit: Limited to freezing; not as versatile as other powers.

Ecto-Energy Manipulation

He can generate and shape ecto-energy for various effects, such as creating shields or blasts.

Limit: Requires energy; can be depleted.

Ghost Sense

A tingling sensation in his spine that alerts him to the presence of ghosts.

Limit: Can be fooled by certain ghosts or technology.

Duplication

In some episodes, he can create multiple copies of himself.

Limit: Each copy is weaker than the original.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In human form, Danny is a teenage boy with black hair and blue eyes. In ghost form, his hair turns white, his eyes turn neon green, and his jumpsuit (which he wears under his clothes) appears in negative colors: the white parts become black and vice versa. He also gains a glowing, ethereal quality, with a faint white aura and the ability to become translucent or solid at will. He often wears a black and white jumpsuit with a 'DP' logo.

Symbols — The 'DP' logo on his chest is a prominent symbol, originally designed by his friend Sam. The Ghost Zone, a green swirling dimension, is a recurring location. The Fenton Thermos, a device used to capture ghosts, is iconic. The colors white and green are associated with his ghost form.

Domains — Primarily based in Amity Park, a fictional town in the American Midwest. He frequently travels to the Ghost Zone, a parallel dimension where ghosts reside. His home is the Fenton household, which is filled with ghost-hunting technology. He also attends Casper High School.

Accounts I
The AccidentPilot episode 'Mystery Meat'

2004

Danny's parents, Jack and Maddie, have built a portal to the Ghost Zone, but it seems inactive. Danny, encouraged by his friends Sam and Tucker, enters the portal to investigate. Inside, he accidentally presses the 'On' button, activating the portal and flooding the chamber with ecto-energy, which fuses with his DNA, making him half-ghost. Danny gains ghost powers and must learn to control them while keeping his identity secret.

The Ultimate EnemyTV movie (season 2)

2005

Danny is shown a possible future where he accidentally destroys Amity Park and, in grief, merges with his evil future self to become Dark Danny, a powerful villain. Danny must prevent this future by defeating his future self. Danny learns about the dangers of his powers and the importance of his choices. Dark Danny is defeated but later returns in the graphic novel.

The Fenton MenaceEpisode

2004

Danny's parents build a new invention that accidentally turns them into ghosts. Danny must find a way to revert them while avoiding their ghost-hunting instincts. Danny learns to manage his parents' inventions.

Accounts II
The Fright Before ChristmasEpisode

2004

A ghost named Spectra tries to ruin Christmas by feeding on negative emotions. Danny must save the town's holiday spirit. Danny reaffirms the importance of friendship and family.

Reign StormEpisode

2005

The Ghost King, Pariah Dark, is released from his slumber and threatens to conquer both the Ghost Zone and the human world. Danny must rally the ghosts to defeat him. Danny becomes the new Ghost King, but he declines the crown, giving it to his friend Tucker temporarily.

Phantom PlanetSeries finale

2007

Danny's identity is revealed to the world, and he must stop an asteroid from destroying the planet. He uses the Ghost Zone to redirect the asteroid, but in doing so, he loses his powers temporarily. He later reveals his secret to his parents, who accept him. Danny's secret is out, and he is accepted by his family and the world. He continues to be a hero.

Who It Is

Physicist Jon Osterman, disintegrated in a nuclear accident and reconstituted as a blue, glowing, near-omnipotent being who experiences all time simultaneously. Doctor Manhattan can manipulate matter at a subatomic level, teleport anywhere, and perceive his entire past and future as a single eternal present. His growing detachment from humanity becomes the central philosophical crisis of Watchmen.

Attested in Watchmen (1986–1987)

Cultural Significance

Manhattan is a quantum physics thought experiment made flesh — his perception of time (block universe theory) is scientifically grounded. His arc critiques the fantasy of unlimited power by showing it leads not to heroism but to indifference.

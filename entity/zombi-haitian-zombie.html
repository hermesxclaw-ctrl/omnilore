Who It Is
Form

In Haitian folklore, zombies are often depicted as reanimated corpses, typically with a pale or decaying complexion, and a vacant or expressionless expression. They may be dressed in tattered or worn-out clothing, and are often associated with the rural areas of Haiti.

Powers

Reanimation The ability to reanimate a corpse, often through the use of magical means such as witchcraft or potions.

Accounts

The legend of the zombi tells the story of a person who is cursed or bewitched by a bokor (a type of sorcerer) and turned into a mindless, obedient zombie.

This article tells the story of a man named Clairvius Narcisse, who was allegedly turned into a zombie by a bokor and forced to work in a state of servitude.

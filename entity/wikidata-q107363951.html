The Approach

Catarina Claes is the oblivious, charmingly disaster-prone protagonist of the light novel and anime series 'My Next Life as a Villainess: All Routes Lead to Doom!' (乙女ゲームの破滅フラグしかない悪役令嬢に転生してしまった…). Reborn as the villainess of an otome game she once played, she spends her second life frantically dodging the 'doom flags' that lead to her exile or death in the game's many routes, accidentally winning the hearts of everyone around her in the process.

Content advisory. This character is from a lighthearted romantic comedy series. There is no graphic violence, sexual content, or disturbing material. The story includes themes of self-improvement, friendship, and mild social anxiety, and some humorous moments of self-deprecation. No content advisories are required for general audiences.
Identity

Catarina Claes

Etymology

The name 'Catarina' (or 'Katarina') is a variant of Katherine, meaning 'pure'. The surname 'Claes' is of Dutch origin, meaning 'son of Nicholas'. The name was chosen by the author, Satoru Yamaguchi, to fit the European-inspired fantasy setting of the series.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese light novel and anime franchise, created by Satoru Yamaguchi (author) and Nami Hidaka (illustrator). The character is owned by the publisher, Ichijinsha, and the anime production committee.

Disputed Identifications

There is no known dispute over the character's identity. Some fans debate the romanization of her name (Catarina vs. Katarina), with the official English light novel using 'Catarina' and the anime subtitles using 'Katarina' inconsistently. The Japanese name is consistently カタリナ.

Description

Appearance

Catarina is a young noblewoman with long, straight brown hair and brown eyes. In the original game, she was depicted as a haughty villainess with a sharp, intimidating gaze and a cold demeanor. After her reincarnation, her expressions become softer, often showing a determined or panicked look. She typically wears elegant dresses befitting her noble status, but in casual settings, she is sometimes seen in simpler clothing. Her appearance is generally considered pretty, but her facial expressions often betray her inner thoughts.

Symbols & Iconography

Catarina is often associated with the 'doom flag' motif, which represents the bad endings she tries to avoid. In fan art, she is frequently depicted with a shovel, a reference to her hobby of gardening and her tendency to dig holes (both literally and metaphorically) to escape her problems. Her catchphrase 'I'll be fine!' (大丈夫ですわ) is widely recognized among fans.

Behaviour & Personality

Catarina is cheerful, optimistic, and somewhat clumsy, with a tendency to act before thinking. She is genuinely kind and empathetic, often going out of her way to help others, even those who are supposed to be her rivals. She is also extremely dense when it comes to romantic advances, failing to notice that she has gathered a harem of admirers of both sexes. She is determined and hardworking, especially when it comes to learning skills that might help her avoid doom, such as martial arts, magic, and farming. She has a strong sense of self-preservation, but she often misinterprets situations and creates new problems while trying to solve old ones.

Domains & Habitats

Catarina lives in the kingdom of Sorcier, a European-style fantasy realm. She resides in the Claes family estate, a large mansion with extensive gardens, where she spends much of her time. She also attends the Magic Academy, where she studies magic and socializes with the other nobles. Her favorite place is her vegetable garden, where she grows crops and practices her earth magic.

Version Differences

In the original light novel, Catarina's inner monologue is more detailed, and her thoughts are more explicitly comedic. The manga adds more visual gags and exaggerates her expressions. The anime condenses the story and sometimes changes the order of events, but it remains faithful to the core characterization. In the spin-off 'Catarina Claes' short stories, her past life as a high school girl is explored in more depth, showing her as a somewhat lazy and average student who loved otome games.

Powers & Abilities

Earth Magic

Source Version All versions (light novel, manga, anime)

Description

Catarina has a natural affinity for earth magic, which she uses primarily for gardening and creating earthen walls or barriers. She is not exceptionally powerful compared to other mages, but she is proficient enough to defend herself.

Limitations

Her magic is limited to earth-based spells; she cannot use other elements. She often uses her magic for non-combat purposes, such as tilling soil or creating small earthen objects.

Contradictions

None noted.

Martial Arts

Source Version All versions

Description

In order to defend herself against potential threats (and to avoid being captured or killed in the game's routes), Catarina trains in swordsmanship and hand-to-hand combat with her childhood friend Keith. She becomes reasonably skilled, though not a master.

Limitations

She is not a natural prodigy; her skills are the result of hard work and dedication. She still loses to more experienced fighters like Maria Campbell or Sirius Deek.

Contradictions

None noted.

Farming and Gardening

Source Version Light novel and manga

Description

Catarina has a deep knowledge of agriculture, which she learned in her past life from her grandmother. She often grows vegetables and herbs, and uses her earth magic to enhance her crops. This skill becomes a hobby and a source of comfort.

Limitations

This is not a combat or magical ability, but a practical skill.

Contradictions

None noted.

Diplomacy and Friendship

Source Version All versions

Description

Catarina has an uncanny ability to befriend almost anyone, including her supposed enemies. Her genuine kindness and lack of malice often disarm others and turn them into allies. This is a 'power' in the sense that it directly influences the plot and helps her avoid doom.

Limitations

This ability is not supernatural; it is a consequence of her personality. It sometimes fails to work on people who are deeply prejudiced or who have no interest in friendship.

Contradictions

None noted.

Stories & Myths

Reincarnation and the Doom Flags

Source Light Novel Volume 1, Manga Volume 1, Anime Season 1Culture Japanese isekai/otome game genrePeriod Contemporary (2015–present)

Summary

Catarina Claes is a young noblewoman who, after hitting her head and remembering her past life, realizes she is the villainess of an otome game called 'Fortune Lover'. In the game, the villainess always ends up either exiled or killed, depending on the route. Determined to avoid these doom flags, she uses her knowledge of the game to change her behavior, befriend the game's characters, and learn skills that might save her. Her efforts, however, often backfire in comedic ways, and she accidentally becomes the object of affection for many of the characters, creating a harem that she is oblivious to.

Consequences

Catarina successfully avoids the doom flags in the main game routes, but her actions create new, unforeseen complications, including romantic entanglements and political intrigue. She also gains a reputation as a strange but kind-hearted noble.

Variants

The story is retold in manga and anime with some differences in pacing and emphasis. The anime adds original scenes and changes some interactions for comedic effect.

Catarina's Past Life

Source Spin-off short stories in the light novel seriesCulture Japanese light novelPeriod 2015–present

Summary

In these short stories, Catarina's life as a high school girl in modern Japan is depicted. She was an ordinary student who loved playing otome games and often daydreamed about being a heroine. She was somewhat lazy and not particularly popular, but she had a close relationship with her grandmother, who taught her about gardening. Her death (which is not detailed) leads to her reincarnation as Catarina.

The School Life and the Hidden Route

Source Light Novel Volumes 2–3, Manga Volumes 2–3, Anime Season 2Culture Japanese light novelPeriod 2015–present

Summary

Catarina enters the Magic Academy and continues to avoid doom flags, but she also encounters a hidden route in the game that she never played. This route involves the character Sirius Deek, who is actually a villainous figure named Raphael. Catarina's interactions with him lead to a confrontation that threatens the entire kingdom. She ultimately saves the day by using her friendship and magic, but she remains unaware of the romantic feelings others have for her.

Relationships

Keith Claes

Type Adoptive brotherSource Light Novel, Manga, Anime

Description

Keith is Catarina's younger adoptive brother, who was taken in by the Claes family due to his illegitimate birth. In the original game, he had a strained relationship with the villainess, but in the reincarnated timeline, Catarina treats him with genuine affection and protects him from bullies. Keith develops a romantic love for Catarina, which she does not notice.

Version Note

In the game, Keith is a capture target for the heroine, but in the story, his route is subverted because of Catarina's actions.

Geordo Stuart

Type FiancéSource Light Novel, Manga, Anime

Description

Geordo is the third prince of Sorcier and Catarina's fiancé. In the game, he was a capture target, and the villainess would often be executed or exiled if she interfered. In the reincarnated timeline, Catarina tries to break off the engagement to avoid doom, but Geordo falls in love with her instead. He is possessive and jealous, but also supportive of her.

Version Note

In the game, Geordo's route ends with the villainess being exiled if she antagonizes the heroine. Catarina's attempts to avoid this lead to a closer relationship.

Maria Campbell

Maria is the protagonist of the original game, a commoner with light magic. In the game, the villainess torments her, but in the story, Catarina befriends her and becomes her protector. Maria develops romantic feelings for Catarina, which are reciprocated in a platonic sense by Catarina, who sees her as a dear friend.

Contradictions & Disputes

Romantic orientation and harem dynamics

Position A

Some fans interpret Catarina as aromantic or asexual, as she shows no romantic interest in anyone and is oblivious to others' feelings.

Position A — evidence

Catarina's internal monologue consistently focuses on friendship and survival, never romance. She explicitly states she has no interest in marriage.

Position B

Others see her as simply dense or in denial, and believe she may eventually develop romantic feelings.

Position B — evidence

In several side stories and the movie, she shows slight blushes or moments of awareness, but these are played for comedy and not conclusive.

Scholarly Consensus

There is no scholarly consensus; this is a fan debate. The author has stated that Catarina's romantic orientation is intentionally ambiguous and that she will likely remain oblivious for the foreseeable future.

Name romanization

Position A

The official English light novel uses 'Catarina'.

Position A — evidence

J-Novel Club's translation consistently uses 'Catarina'.

Position B

The anime subtitles and some official merchandise use 'Katarina'.

Position B — evidence

Crunchyroll's subtitles and the Funimation dub use 'Katarina'.

Scholarly Consensus

Both are accepted, but 'Catarina' is the more common romanization in the English-speaking fandom.

Character depth

Position A

Some critics dismiss Catarina as a one-dimensional gag character.

Position A — evidence

Her primary motivation is avoiding doom, and she often acts foolishly.

Position B

Others argue she is a subversive take on the villainess trope, with genuine character growth and kindness.

Position B — evidence

Her actions consistently show empathy and a desire to help others, and she grows from a self-centered noble to a selfless protector.

Scholarly Consensus

There is no academic consensus; the character is generally well-received by audiences, and her depth is a topic of discussion in fan circles.

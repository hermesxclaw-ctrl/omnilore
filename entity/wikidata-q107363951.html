Who It Is

Catarina Claes is the oblivious, charmingly disaster-prone protagonist of the light novel and anime series 'My Next Life as a Villainess: All Routes Lead to Doom!' (乙女ゲームの破滅フラグしかない悪役令嬢に転生してしまった…). Reborn as the villainess of an otome game she once played, she spends her second life frantically dodging the 'doom flags' that lead to her exile or death in the game's many routes, accidentally winning the hearts of everyone around her in the process.

Catarina is cheerful, optimistic, and somewhat clumsy, with a tendency to act before thinking. She is genuinely kind and empathetic, often going out of her way to help others, even those who are supposed to be her rivals. She is also extremely dense when it comes to romantic advances, failing to notice that she has gathered a harem of admirers of both sexes. She is determined and hardworking, especially when it comes to learning skills that might help her avoid doom, such as martial arts, magic, and farming. Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Catarina' (or 'Katarina') is a variant of Katherine, meaning 'pure'. The surname 'Claes' is of Dutch origin, meaning 'son of Nicholas'. The name was chosen by the author, Satoru Yamaguchi, to fit the European-inspired fantasy setting of the series.

Powers
Earth Magic

Catarina has a natural affinity for earth magic, which she uses primarily for gardening and creating earthen walls or barriers. She is not exceptionally powerful compared to other mages, but she is proficient enough to defend herself.

Limit: Her magic is limited to earth-based spells; she cannot use other elements. She often uses her magic for non-combat purposes, such as tilling soil or creating small earthen objects.

Martial Arts

In order to defend herself against potential threats (and to avoid being captured or killed in the game's routes), Catarina trains in swordsmanship and hand-to-hand combat with her childhood friend Keith. She becomes reasonably skilled, though not a master.

Limit: She is not a natural prodigy; her skills are the result of hard work and dedication. She still loses to more experienced fighters like Maria Campbell or Sirius Deek.

Farming and Gardening

Catarina has a deep knowledge of agriculture, which she learned in her past life from her grandmother. She often grows vegetables and herbs, and uses her earth magic to enhance her crops. This skill becomes a hobby and a source of comfort.

Limit: This is not a combat or magical ability, but a practical skill.

Diplomacy and Friendship

Catarina has an uncanny ability to befriend almost anyone, including her supposed enemies. Her genuine kindness and lack of malice often disarm others and turn them into allies. This is a 'power' in the sense that it directly influences the plot and helps her avoid doom.

Limit: This ability is not supernatural; it is a consequence of her personality. It sometimes fails to work on people who are deeply prejudiced or who have no interest in friendship.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Catarina is a young noblewoman with long, straight brown hair and brown eyes. In the original game, she was depicted as a haughty villainess with a sharp, intimidating gaze and a cold demeanor. After her reincarnation, her expressions become softer, often showing a determined or panicked look. Symbols — Catarina is often associated with the 'doom flag' motif, which represents the bad endings she tries to avoid. In fan art, she is frequently depicted with a shovel, a reference to her hobby of gardening and her tendency to dig holes (both literally and metaphorically) to escape her problems. Her catchphrase 'I'll be fine!' (大丈夫ですわ) is widely recognized among fans.

Domains — Catarina lives in the kingdom of Sorcier, a European-style fantasy realm. She resides in the Claes family estate, a large mansion with extensive gardens, where she spends much of her time. She also attends the Magic Academy, where she studies magic and socializes with the other nobles. Her favorite place is her vegetable garden, where she grows crops and practices her earth magic.

Accounts I
Reincarnation and the Doom FlagsLight Novel Volume 1, Manga Volume 1, Anime Season 1

Contemporary (2015–present)

Catarina Claes is a young noblewoman who, after hitting her head and remembering her past life, realizes she is the villainess of an otome game called 'Fortune Lover'. In the game, the villainess always ends up either exiled or killed, depending on the route. Determined to avoid these doom flags, she uses her knowledge of the game to change her behavior, befriend the game's characters, and learn skills that might save her. Her efforts, however, often backfire in comedic ways, and she accidentally becomes the object of affection for many of the characters, creating a harem that she is oblivious to. Catarina successfully avoids the doom flags in the main game routes, but her actions create new, unforeseen complications, including romantic entanglements and political intrigue. She also gains a reputation as a strange but kind-hearted noble.

Accounts II
Catarina's Past LifeSpin-off short stories in the light novel series

2015–present

In these short stories, Catarina's life as a high school girl in modern Japan is depicted. She was an ordinary student who loved playing otome games and often daydreamed about being a heroine. She was somewhat lazy and not particularly popular, but she had a close relationship with her grandmother, who taught her about gardening. Her death (which is not detailed) leads to her reincarnation as Catarina. These stories provide background on why Catarina has certain knowledge and skills in her new life, such as farming and her fascination with otome games.

The School Life and the Hidden RouteLight Novel Volumes 2–3, Manga Volumes 2–3, Anime Season 2

2015–present

Catarina enters the Magic Academy and continues to avoid doom flags, but she also encounters a hidden route in the game that she never played. This route involves the character Sirius Deek, who is actually a villainous figure named Raphael. Catarina's interactions with him lead to a confrontation that threatens the entire kingdom. She ultimately saves the day by using her friendship and magic, but she remains unaware of the romantic feelings others have for her. Catarina averts a major crisis and strengthens her bonds with her friends. She also inadvertently becomes more popular and gains additional admirers.

Catarina is a typical villainess who is evil. — In the original game, the villainess is a minor antagonist, but after reincarnation, Catarina is genuinely kind and heroic. She is not evil at all.

Catarina is a Mary Sue who is perfect at everything. — Catarina is clumsy, dense, and often fails at things. She is not exceptionally talented at magic or combat; she works hard but remains average.

The series is a harem with a male protagonist. — Catarina is female, and the harem includes both male and female characters who are attracted to her. It is a reverse harem with yuri undertones.

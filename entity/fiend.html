Who It Is
Form

In Slavic mythology, Fiends are often depicted as demonic creatures with multiple eyes, sharp teeth, and a menacing presence. They are said to inhabit dark, foreboding places such as caves, forests, and swamps. In some accounts, they are described as being surrounded by a halo of flames or having a dark, sulfurous aura.

Powers

Elemental Control Fiends are said to have the power to control the elements, summoning fire, water, earth, and air to do their bidding.

Accounts

A mortal man makes a pact with a Fiend to gain power and wealth, but ultimately loses everything due to the Fiend's treachery.

A mortal woman is given a gift by a Fiend, but ultimately realizes that it is a curse in disguise.

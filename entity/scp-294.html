SCP-294

The Coffee Machine

also known asThe Coffee MachineUniversal DispenserWhat Would You Like to Drink?
TypeSCP Artifact (Universal Dispenser)
CultureSCP Foundation
Who It Is
Form

SCP-294 appears as a standard coffee vending machine, approximately 1.5 meters in height, with a metallic exterior painted in a dark, nondescript color. It features a digital display screen, a coin slot, a keypad for input, and a dispensing nozzle. The machine is in a state of good repair, with no visible brand markings or manufacturer labels. The interior is not accessible, and attempts to open the machine have been met with resistance, as the casing appears to be seamlessly sealed. The machine is plugged into a standard electrical outlet, but it operates even when disconnected from power, suggesting an anomalous power source. The display screen typically shows a prompt reading 'Please select a beverage,' but it can change to display other messages depending on the input. The machine has a small cup dispenser on the side, and it always provides a standard paper cup for the dispensed liquid, regardless of the requested substance.

Powers

Universal Liquid Dispensation SCP-294 can dispense any liquid that can exist in liquid form, including water, coffee, and more exotic substances. It can also dispense liquids that are not physically possible, such as 'a cup of pure darkness' or 'a cup of the concept of love.' The machine interprets the user's input and produces a liquid that corresponds to the request, often with surprising accuracy. The dispensed liquid is always in a standard paper cup, and the volume is approximately 300 milliliters. The machine cannot dispense solids, gases, or living organisms, but it can dispense liquids that have unusual properties, such as a liquid that is a perfect vacuum or a liquid that is a portal to another dimension. The machine's capabilities are limited by the user's imagination, but it has been noted to refuse requests that are too vague or contradictory, displaying an error message instead.

Incident Log

The primary story of SCP-294 is its discovery and containment by the SCP Foundation. The machine was found in a coffee shop in a small town, where it was dispensing beverages to customers for free. The Foundation became aware of it when a customer requested 'a cup of the universe' and subsequently disappeared. The Foundation contained the machine and began testing its capabilities. Researchers discovered that the machine could dispense any liquid, including abstract concepts, and that it had a tendency to interpret requests in unexpected ways. The article includes a series of test logs, documenting various requests and their outcomes, such as 'a cup of the tears of a clown' and 'a cup of the end of the world.' The story establishes the machine as a Safe-class anomaly and highlights the Foundation's procedures for studying and containing such objects.

In this article, SCP-294 is mentioned in the context of Brasilic folklore, a fictional island culture within the SCP universe. The article discusses how the machine might be connected to local myths about a 'cauldron of endless brew' or a 'spirit of the cup.' The foreword notes similarities between Brasilic folklore and Celtic mythology, with themes of love, death, and betrayal. SCP-294 is referenced as a potential artifact that could be related to these myths, though no direct connection is established. The article serves as a world-building piece, integrating SCP-294 into the broader SCP mythology.

Nature of dispensed abstract concepts
The machine literally materializes abstract concepts, such as 'love' or 'fear,' into a liquid form that can be consumed. ⇌ The machine only creates a symbolic representation of the concept, and the liquid does not actually contain the concept itself, but rather a metaphor or placebo effect.
Origin of SCP-294
SCP-294 is an anomalous object that was discovered in a coffee shop, with no known origin or creator. ⇌ SCP-294 may have been created by an unknown entity or organization, possibly as a tool for experimentation or as a weapon.

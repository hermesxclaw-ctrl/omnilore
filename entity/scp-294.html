Who It Is

Imagine walking into a coffee shop and seeing a vending machine that can dispense a cup of pure darkness, or a sip of the concept of love. That is SCP-294, a seemingly ordinary machine that challenges the boundaries of reality. What would you ask it to pour?

SCP-294 is an inanimate object with no discernible personality or consciousness. It operates mechanically, responding to user inputs via the keypad. When a user inputs a liquid name or a description of a liquid, the machine dispenses a cup containing that liquid, provided the request is within its capabilities. The machine does not communicate verbally, but it displays text on its screen, such as 'Dispensing' or 'Error' when a request cannot be fulfilled. Powers
Universal Liquid Dispensation

SCP-294 can dispense any liquid that can exist in liquid form, including water, coffee, and more exotic substances. It can also dispense liquids that are not physically possible, such as 'a cup of pure darkness' or 'a cup of the concept of love.' The machine interprets the user's input and produces a liquid that corresponds to the request, often with surprising accuracy. The dispensed liquid is always in a standard paper cup, and the volume is approximately 300 milliliters. The machine cannot dispense solids, gases, or living organisms, but it can dispense liquids that have unusual properties, such as a liquid that is a perfect vacuum or a liquid that is a portal to another dimension. The machine's capabilities are limited by the user's imagination, but it has been noted to refuse requests that are too vague or contradictory, displaying an error message instead.

Accounts I
SCP-294 - The Coffee MachineSCP Foundation Wiki

2010 (original article)

The primary story of SCP-294 is its discovery and containment by the SCP Foundation. The machine was found in a coffee shop in a small town, where it was dispensing beverages to customers for free. The Foundation became aware of it when a customer requested 'a cup of the universe' and subsequently disappeared. The Foundation contained the machine and began testing its capabilities. Researchers discovered that the machine could dispense any liquid, including abstract concepts, and that it had a tendency to interpret requests in unexpected ways. The article includes a series of test logs, documenting various requests and their outcomes, such as 'a cup of the tears of a clown' and 'a cup of the end of the world.' The story establishes the machine as a Safe-class anomaly and highlights the Foundation's procedures for studying and containing such objects. The machine was successfully contained, and no major breaches occurred. However, several test subjects experienced psychological distress after consuming certain dispensed liquids, leading to the establishment of strict testing protocols

SCP-294 in Brasilic FolkloreSCP Foundation Wiki - Brasilic Folklore And Mythology

Unknown

In this article, SCP-294 is mentioned in the context of Brasilic folklore, a fictional island culture within the SCP universe. The article discusses how the machine might be connected to local myths about a 'cauldron of endless brew' or a 'spirit of the cup.' The foreword notes similarities between Brasilic folklore and Celtic mythology, with themes of love, death, and betrayal. SCP-294 is referenced as a potential artifact that could be related to these myths, though no direct connection is established. The article serves as a world-building piece, integrating SCP-294 into the broader SCP mythology. The article does not describe specific consequences, but it implies that the machine's existence might have influenced local folklore or vice versa. It adds depth to the SCP universe by suggesting that anomalies like SCP-294 may have cultural roots.

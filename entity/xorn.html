Who It Is
Form

In Dungeons & Dragons, Xorn is a creature that resembles a massive, rock-like creature with a mouth full of razor-sharp teeth. It has three eyes, three arms, and three legs, allowing it to move and attack with ease. In the Marvel Comics universe, Xorn is a mutant with a miniature star residing in his head, granting him powers of gravitational electromagnetism, self-sustenance, and healing.

Powers

Gravitational Electromagnetism Xorn's ability to manipulate gravitational forces and electromagnetic fields allows it to move objects, attract or repel matter, and even heal itself.

Who It Is

The dandan is a fascinating and mysterious creature, with a rich history and cultural significance. Its ability to swallow large animals in a single mouthful makes it a formidable and fearsome creature, and its fat is said to have healing properties.

The dandan is described as a formidable and fearsome creature, with a powerful appetite and a strong presence in the ocean.

Fat of the Dandan

The fat of the dandan is said to have healing properties, and is used as an ointment to allow humans to survive underwater. It is described as 'fat of oxen, yellow as gold and sweet of savour'.

Form & Signs

Appearance — A massive sea creature, described as the largest and fiercest fish, capable of swallowing large animals in a single mouthful. Its fat is said to be yellow as gold and sweet of savour.

Symbols — The dandan is often associated with the sea and its bounty, and may be depicted with scales, fins, or other aquatic features.

Domains — The dandan is said to inhabit the waters of the Arabian Gulf, and may be found in the coastal regions of Bahrain and other nearby countries.

Accounts I
Abdullah the Fisherman and Abdullah the MermanThe Book of One Thousand and One Nights

14th century

A fisherman named Abdullah catches a merman, who tells him about the dandan, a massive sea creature that can swallow large animals in a single mouthful. The merman also shares the secret of the dandan's fat, which is used as an ointment to allow humans to survive underwater. The fisherman is able to survive underwater with the help of the dandan's fat, and is able to catch a glimpse of the massive sea creature.

Dandan

The Great Fish of Bahrain, Pearl-Grower, Dragon of the Gulf

TypeSea Monster / Dragon-Fish
CultureArabian / Islamic
Who It Is
Form

A massive sea creature, described as the largest and fiercest fish, capable of swallowing large animals in a single mouthful. Its fat is said to be yellow as gold and sweet of savour.

Powers

Fat of the Dandan The fat of the dandan is said to have healing properties, and is used as an ointment to allow humans to survive underwater. It is described as 'fat of oxen, yellow as gold and sweet of savour'.

Accounts

A fisherman named Abdullah catches a merman, who tells him about the dandan, a massive sea creature that can swallow large animals in a single mouthful. The merman also shares the secret of the dandan's fat, which is used as an ointment to allow humans to survive underwater.

A modern retelling of the dandan legend, with a focus on the creature's size and ferocity. The story describes the dandan as a massive fish with a long, pointed snout, and tells of its ability to swallow large animals in a single mouthful.

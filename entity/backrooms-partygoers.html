Who It Is
Form

Partygoers are described as yellow humanoids with balloons, often with blood visible on their mouths, hands, and legs. They are said to be tall and slender, with an unsettling smile on their faces.

Powers

Deception Partygoers have the ability to deceive and manipulate victims into following them into the Backrooms.

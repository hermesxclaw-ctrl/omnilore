Who It Is

Partygoers (formally designated as Entity 67 on the Backrooms Wiki) are among the most disturbing and creatively conceived hostile entities in Backrooms lore. They appear as tall, gaunt humanoids with smooth, bright yellow skin, elongated limbs, and faces permanently fixed in grotesque, wide grins. Their hands end in sharp claws, and their movements are jerky and unnatural. Partygoers are deceptive: they attempt to lure wanderers by acting friendly, cheerful, and celebratory -- using phrases like 'Come join the party!' and 'We've been waiting for you!' in a sing-song chorus. When a victim is deceived and approaches, the Partygoers' behavior flips: they attack en masse, rending the victim and, in some canon, transforming them into a new Partygoer. The entity's most iconic feature is their home territory: 'The Fun Room' (or 'The Party Room'), a brightly colored, balloon-filled space with a table laden with cake and party supplies -- all of it deeply wrong, uncanny, and obviously dangerous. The contrast between the cheerful, childlike facade and the horrific reality is a hallmark of Backrooms horror. Partygoers have become a breakout star of Backrooms fan culture: they are featured in countless YouTube videos, memes, and games, and the phrase '=)' (their text symbol in Backrooms lore) has become a recognizable shorthand for the entity. Some lore suggests Partygoers were once human wanderers who were transformed by an anomalous force, making them a tragic as well as terrifying enemy.

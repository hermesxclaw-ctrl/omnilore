Who It Is

He entered First High ranked as the defective weed among blooms — a Course 2 student with mediocre practical scores and no flash. Then the monolith cracked, the CAD fired without a formal activation sequence, and the school's hierarchy quietly broke. Tatsuya Shiba is not the underdog who learns to catch up; he is the weapon who learned to pretend he was ordinary.

Attested core traits: hyper-rational, analytical, emotionally muted due to artificial limitation of strong emotions except familial love for Miyuki (result of Miya Yotsuba's mental interference magic). Polite, formal speech (keigo) to most, dry sarcasm and blunt honesty to intimates. Intensely devoted to Miyuki; his entire moral compass is anchored to her safety and happiness. Pragmatic to the point of ruthlessness - will kill without hesitation if he judges it necessary, but follows a personal code against unnecessary cruelty. Given name Tatsuya (達也) uses common masculine elements 'to attain/reach' (達) + 'to be' (也), a deliberately ordinary given name contrasting with his extraordinary

Pronunciation — Japanese: /ɕiba tatsɯja/ (shi-ba ta-tsu-ya), pitch accent: Shiba [shìbá], Tatsuya [tàtsúyà]. English dub: /tɑːtˈsuːjə ˈʃiːbə/.

Powers
Decomposition (Bunkai) - Mist Dispersion / Gram Dispersion / Trident / Material Burst

Innate innate magic that decomposes the information structure (Eidos) of matter or magic. Base form Mist Dispersion decomposes matter into elements. Gram Dispersion decomposes magic sequences (counter-magic). Trident is three-stage decomposition to bypass defenses. Material Burst is strategic-class magic that converts mass directly into energy per E=mc², creating nuclear-scale explosion without radiation; classified as strategic weapon.

Limit: Requires Elemental Sight to target Eidos; cannot decompose what he cannot perceive/identify. Material Burst requires sufficient mass and line-of-sight targeting; political limitation - use is internationally monitored and considered war crime if misused. Decomposition is blocked by sufficiently dense psion armor or by magics that continuously rewrite Eidos (e.g., Phalanx). Consumes psions.

Regrowth (Saisei) / Self-Restoration

Innate magic that restores Eidos to state up to 24 hours prior, effectively healing any wound, reversing death if brain structure not destroyed, and restoring destroyed matter. Automatically activates on severe injury. Can be used on others via contact.

Limit: Cannot restore if the Eidos itself is completely erased or if more than 24 hours have passed. Restores pain as well as body, so Tatsuya still experiences agony. Automatic activation consumes huge psion count and can leave him temporarily depleted. Does not restore psion/magic reserves, only physical/informational state. Cannot restore lost limbs if Eidos backup is damaged (edge case).

Elemental Sight (Elemental Sight) - Ideal / Eidos Perception

Extrasensory perception that allows direct observation of the information dimension (Idea) and Eidos. Enables him to see magic activation sequences, Eidos history, and target for Decomposition/Regrowth with perfect accuracy regardless of distance (with aid). Basis for all his abilities.

Limit: Information overload - requires mental processing; cannot be kept active constantly without strain. Requires conscious focus to filter relevant Eidos. Does not grant omniscience - he must know what to look for. Blocked by certain ancient magics that obscure Idea.

Gram Demolition (Gram Demolition) and Gram Dispersion

Gram Demolition: fires compressed psion bullet to demolish magic sequences without needing to analyze them, effective against area magic. Gram Dispersion: precise decomposition of magic sequences. Both are anti-magic countermeasures.

Limit: Gram Demolition is indiscriminate and affects allies' magic if in blast radius; Gram Dispersion requires Elemental Sight analysis and is slower. Both consume psions and are ineffective against non-systematic magic (ancient magic) that doesn't use activation sequences.

Flash Cast (Sokushaku Mahou / Flash Cast)

Technique to bypass CAD and activation sequence, directly invoking magic via memory of magic sequence. Tatsuya can Flash Cast any magic he fully understands, despite being classified as unable to use systematic magic efficiently due to his specialized magic calculation area.

Limit: Only for magics he has thoroughly analyzed and memorized; cannot Flash Cast unknown or overly complex ancient magic on first sight. Consumes more psions than normal casting. His artificial magic calculation area is narrow, so he can only Flash Cast a limited number of spells compared to normal magicians who can use diverse systematic magic.

Magic Engineering and CAD Development (Taurus Silver)

Genius-level magic engineer: inventor of Loop Cast System, Flying Magic, specialized CADs, and contributor to modern magic theory. As Taurus Silver, co-developer with Ushiyama of FLT's revolutionary CADs. Holds patents and influences global magic technology.

Limit: Engineering requires resources, lab, and time; not combat-applicable instantly. His identity as Taurus Silver must remain secret, limiting public credit. Some inventions (e.g., Flying Magic) are quickly replicated by others once revealed.

Military Combat and Strategic Intellect

Trained special officer in 101st Independent Magic-Equipped Battalion (rank Special Lieutenant / later Captain). Master of close-quarters combat (Ninjustu-based), infiltration, interrogation, and strategic planning. Fluent in multiple languages, expert hacker.

Limit: Physical abilities are peak human but not superhuman without magic support; relies on Regrowth to survive otherwise lethal injuries. Political constraints as Yotsuba weapon and military asset limit autonomy.

Accounts I
Enrollment Arc (Nyugaku-hen I & II)Light novel vol.1-2 (2011); anime S1 ep.1-7; manga Enrollment Arc

2095-2096 (in-universe); published 2011

Tatsuya and Miyuki enroll at National Magic University Affiliated First High School. Miyuki is top Course 1 (Bloom) entrant; Tatsuya is placed in Course 2 (Weed) due to poor practical exam scores despite topping theory. Tatsuya joins Disciplinary Committee, demonstrates Gram Demolition and engineering talent, dismantles discrimination between Courses, and defeats vice-president Hattori and others in duels. Reveals early hints of his true power and Taurus Silver identity to limited circle. Establishes school hierarchy conflict, introduces magic system, sets Tatsuya's reputation as irregular. Leads to Nine Schools Competition selection.

Nine Schools Competition Arc (Kyuukousen-hen I & II)Light novel vol.3-4 (2011-2012); anime S1 ep.8-14

August 2095

First High competes in national magic high school tournament. Tatsuya serves as engineer for Miyuki and others, unveils Flying Magic (revolutionary gravity-control magic) through Miyuki and others, and as Taurus Silver shocks magic engineering world. He counters criminal sabotage by No Head Dragon syndicate, using Decomposition to stop a truck attack and revealing combat prowess to military observers like Major Kazama. Flying Magic becomes public, Taurus Silver fame grows, military takes formal interest in Tatsuya. Ichijou becomes rival/friend.

Yokohama Disturbance Arc (Yokohama Souran-hen I & II)Light novel vol.6-7 (2012); anime S1 ep.18-26

October 2095

Great Asian Alliance invades Yokohama. Tatsuya is deployed as Special Lieutenant of 101st Battalion under Major Kazama. He uses strategic-class magic Material Burst to annihilate enemy fleet offshore, an act witnessed by global powers and classified at highest level. He also fights the enhanced magician Kudo's Parasite experiments and saves Miyuki and friends during conference attack. Publicly, the feat is attributed to unknown strategic magician; privately, world powers now know Japan has a new strategic asset. Tatsuya confirmed as strategic-class magician, Japan's deterrence shifts, Yotsuba clan's hidden weapon exposed to state. Deeply traumatizes Miyuki who witnesses his willingness to become a destroyer for her.

Accounts II
Visitor Arc (Raihousha-hen I-III)Light novel vol.9-11 (2013); anime Season 2 (2020) 13 episodes

December 2095 - January 2096

USNA sends Lina (Angelina Kudou Shields), strategic-class magician Sirius, as exchange student to investigate the Yokohama strategic magic and Parasites. Tatsuya befriends and then duels Lina, revealing his Decomposition can neutralize her Heavy Metal Burst. Together they confront the Parasites (vampiric psion entities) possessing students, with Tatsuya using Regrowth and Gram Dispersion to exorcise them. Lina's identity is exposed, leading to diplomatic crisis and Tatsuya's covert cooperation with USNA. USNA learns Tatsuya is Material Burst user; Tatsuya-Lina alliance forms; Parasite lore expanded; Yotsuba-USNA tensions managed.

Yotsuba Succession Arc (Yotsuba Keishou-hen)Light novel vol.16 (2015); anime Season 3 ep.1-5 (2024)

January 2096 (New Year)

Maya Yotsuba summons Tatsuya and Miyuki to main house to announce Miyuki as next head of Yotsuba, and reveals truth: Tatsuya is Maya's son (via egg from Maya, fertilized, carried by Miya), engineered to be Miyuki's guardian. Miya's mental interference magic limited his emotions to protect him from his own power. Maya declares Tatsuya is Miyuki's fiancé, overturning sibling taboo by revealing they are not blood-related in conventional sense and Yotsuba custom permits it. Tatsuya is formally recognized as Yotsuba member, codename Mahesvara, and Miyuki's engagement is announced to clan, shocking allies. Resolves core family mystery, redefines Tatsuya's status from tool to family, sets up Miyuki's succession and engagement controversy that drives remainder of series. Divisive among fandom due to incest-adjacent framing.

Ancient City Insurrection Arc (Koto Naikou-hen) and Steeplechase ArcLight novel vol.12-13 Steeplechase, vol.14-15 Ancient City Insurrection (2014-2015); anime Season 3

Spring - Summer 2096 (Second year)

Traditionalist magicians (led by Kudo clan elements and Zhou Gongjin) attempt to sabotage modern magic and expose Tatsuya. In Steeplechase, Tatsuya counters illegal human experimentation using Parasites in a cross-country magic race. In Ancient City Insurrection, terrorists attack Kyoto during cultural exchange, and Tatsuya, as part of 101st Battalion and Yotsuba agent, hunts Zhou, uses Decomposition to dismantle ancient magic barriers, and protects Miyuki and Mayumi. Demonstrates his role as bridge between modern and ancient magic systems. Zhou's death, Kudou clan decline, Tatsuya's reputation among Master Clans solidifies, ancient magic community forced to acknowledge modern magic supremacy.

Master Clans Conference Arc and Southern Sea Riot ArcLight novel vol.19 Master Clans Conference (2016), vol.20 Southern Sea Riot (2017)

Late 2096

At the Master Clans Conference, Tatsuya's engineering (Stellar Furnace - practical perpetual energy via Material Burst micro-reactions) is presented, aiming to make strategic magicians obsolete and free him and Miyuki from being weapons. Political opponents try to assassinate him. In Southern Sea Riot, Tatsuya and Miyuki travel to artificial island for stellar furnace experiment, facing USNA and New Soviet Union sabotage. Tatsuya demonstrates tactical use of Material Burst at micro-scale for energy, not destruction. Stellar Furnace project positions Tatsuya as world-changing scientist, shifting from weapon to energy provider; escalates global strategic magic arms race discourse.

Tatsuya is completely emotionless and a sociopath who feels nothing — He feels muted but genuine emotions; his limitation specifically suppresses strong passions (hatred, romantic love, grief) except love for Miyuki, but he shows loyalty, humor, anger, and later romantic love. Light novel internal monologue shows he values friends and feels guilt.

He is weak in magic and only wins via technology/CADs — He is weak in general systematic magic diversity but overwhelmingly powerful in his innate magics (Decomposition/Regrowth) which are outside modern magic ranking. His engineering complements but does not replace his strategic-class power.

Material Burst makes him invincible and he can spam it anytime — Material Burst requires mass, line of sight, and is politically a strategic weapon equivalent to nuclear arms - its use triggers international crisis and is forbidden except in war. He has used it only twice (Okinawa, Yokohama) in main series. It also leaves him psion-depleted and vulnerable.

Tatsuya and Miyuki are full blood siblings in an incest relationship — They were raised as siblings but are genetically maternal cousins: Tatsuya from Maya's egg, Miyuki from Miya's egg, different fathers. Yotsuba arranged engagement after revealing this, which in-universe is considered politically acceptable though still controversial. Author uses this retcon to avoid literal incest while retaining sibling dynamic.

Tatsuya is just a self-insert Gary Stu with no flaws — He is deliberately written as overpowered fantasy, but text gives him explicit flaws: narrow magic compatibility, social obliviousness, emotional disability, over-reliance on Miyuki as moral anchor, political naivety, and inability to be recognized publicly. Critics still debate whether these balance his power, but flaws are attested.

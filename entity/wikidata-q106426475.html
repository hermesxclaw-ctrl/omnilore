Who It Is

In the bustling town of Sakurashin, where humans and yōkai coexist under a fragile peace, there exists an immortal woman who remembers a time when her own humanity was a distant memory. Mariabelle, once a mortal girl from a plague-stricken village, now serves as the eternal assistant to a youthful land god, her past shrouded in the secrets of a legendary creator and a forbidden resurrection. Her story weaves together the threads of science, magic, and undying loyalty, making her one of the most enigmatic figures in the Yozakura Quartet universe.

Mariabelle is diligent, loyal, and deeply appreciative of Yuhi's protection. She maintains a professional demeanor as his assistant, but she secretly enjoys the cosplay activities he requests. She has strong preferences, particularly for Japanese breakfasts, indicating a desire for normalcy and tradition. Her past as a plague victim and her resurrection have given her a mature, grateful outlook on life, though she rarely speaks of her past.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Mariabelle' is a combination of 'Maria' and 'belle' (French for 'beautiful'), suggesting a beautiful, beloved figure. It may also be a reference to the Frankenstein myth, as Victor Frankenstein's fiancée is named Elizabeth, but the name Mariabelle itself is unique to this series.

Pronunciation — Ma-ree-a-bel

Aliases — マリアベル, Mariaberu

Ownership — Japanese popular culture, specifically the Yozakura Quartet franchise created by Suzuhito Yasuda.

Powers
Immortality

Mariabelle was resurrected by her father, Victor Frankenstein, and has not aged since. She appears to be physically in her late teens or early twenties, despite being over 200 years old.

Limit: She is not invulnerable; she can be injured or killed, but she does not age. The exact limits of her immortality are not explored.

No special combat abilities

Unlike many other characters, Mariabelle does not possess supernatural powers or combat skills. She relies on her intelligence, experience, and her relationship with Yuhi for protection.

Limit: She is vulnerable to physical harm and cannot defend herself against supernatural threats without assistance.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Mariabelle appears as a young woman with long, light-colored hair, often styled in a way that complements her various cosplay outfits. She has a gentle, composed expression and is typically seen in outfits chosen by Yuhi, ranging from maid costumes to other playful ensembles. Her true age is not reflected in her appearance, which remains youthful due to her immortality.

Symbols — Her association with Victor Frankenstein and her resurrection link her to themes of life, death, and creation. In the series, she is often depicted with items related to her duties as an assistant, such as documents or tea sets. Her cosplay outfits serve as a visual symbol of her dedication to Yuhi's whims and her hidden enjoyment of them.

Domains — She resides in Sakurashin, the town protected by the Seven Pillars, where humans and yōkai coexist. She works in the District Mayor's office under Yuhi, and is often seen in the office or accompanying Yuhi on official duties. Her home is presumably within the town, though not specifically detailed.

Accounts I
The Resurrection of MariabelleYozakura Quartet manga and anime

Approximately 200 years before the main storyline (around the 19th century)

Mariabelle was a young girl living in a village afflicted by a deadly plague. Her father, Victor Frankenstein, a scientist, sought to create a cure but was unable to save her. In desperation, he used his scientific knowledge to resurrect her, granting her immortality. The process was successful, but it made her a half-yōkai, as her resurrection involved supernatural elements. After her resurrection, they faced persecution from other half-yōkai and humans who feared them. They eventually sought refuge in Sakurashin, where they hoped to be 'tuned' (a process that could sever their connection to the supernatural world). However, Yuhi, the land god, intervened and offered his protection instead of tuning them. Grateful, Mariabelle became Yuhi's permanent assistant. Mariabelle gained immortality and a new life in Sakurashin. She became Yuhi's assistant, forming a close bond with him. Her father's whereabouts are not detailed, but it is implied he may have left or passed away.

Accounts II
Mariabelle's Daily Life with YuhiYozakura Quartet manga and anime

Main storyline (contemporary)

Mariabelle assists Yuhi in his duties as District Mayor. She handles paperwork, manages his schedule, and accompanies him to official events. Yuhi often requests her to wear various cosplay outfits, which she secretly enjoys. She insists on having Japanese breakfasts, a preference that Yuhi indulges. She also interacts with other characters, such as Hime and the Hiizumi Life Counseling Office members, and occasionally helps them with information or support. Her interactions reinforce her role as a trusted assistant and friend to Yuhi, and she becomes a familiar presence in the town. Her cosplay activities are a source of lighthearted humor.

Mariabelle is a direct adaptation of the Bride of Frankenstein. — While she shares the connection to Victor Frankenstein, she is an original character in Yozakura Quartet. She is not a monster or a bride, but a resurrected daughter who gained immortality.

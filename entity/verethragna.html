Who It Is
Form

Verethragna is often depicted as a powerful warrior or a majestic horse, symbolizing strength and victory. In some representations, he is shown with a spear or a sword, emphasizing his role as a god of war. His appearance varies across different cultures and time periods, but his essence remains the same – a powerful and triumphant deity.

Powers

Smasher of Resistance Verethragna's power to overcome obstacles and bring victory to his followers.

God of Victory Verethragna's ability to grant victory and triumph to those who worship him.

Bringer of Offensive Power Verethragna's power to grant his followers the strength and courage needed to overcome challenges.

Accounts

Verethragna is the Yazata of victory in the Zoroastrism also known as Bahram, he is who kills the obstacles for that the heroes can continue his path to the victory.

Verethragna is an important figure in ancient Iranian mythology, reflecting the concept of strength and struggle. His cult was widespread in Zoroastrianism and early Iranian religious contexts.

Who It Is

SCP-939 refers to a species of predatory quadrupeds that superficially resemble large canines or reptiles, standing approximately 2 meters at the shoulder. Their bodies are covered in a heat-resistant red-brown hide, and they lack eyes entirely, instead sensing their environment through a combination of echolocation, thermal sensing, and atmospheric pressure changes. Their heads are blunt and feature large jaws filled with serrated teeth capable of crushing bone. The most terrifying and unique trait of SCP-939 instances is their ability to perfectly mimic human speech — any voice, any language, any phrase they have ever heard. They use this ability to lure prey, often repeating the last words of their previous victims, or crying out for help in the voice of a lost child or injured colleague. SCP-939 instances hunt in packs, communicating with each other through their vocal mimicry to coordinate attacks. They produce a thick, red-colored mucous that serves multiple purposes: it contains amnestic compounds that induce short-term memory loss in those who contact it, it acts as a digestive lubricant, and it creates slippery terrain that hampers prey movement. SCP-939 instances reproduce by implanting eggs in paralyzed, living hosts; the eggs gestate rapidly and the hatched young consume the host from within. The Foundation has contained multiple SCP-939 instances, and each has demonstrated the ability to learn and replicate increasingly complex vocal patterns. In one notable breach incident, an SCP-939 instance spent three days mimicking the voice of a Foundation researcher, successfully luring six personnel into its enclosure before the deception was discovered. The origin of SCP-939 is unknown, but recovered instances frequently exhibit scarring consistent with exposure to anomalous environments. The Foundation maintains a strict policy: SCP-939 instances are never to be trusted, regardless of what they say or how convincingly they say it.

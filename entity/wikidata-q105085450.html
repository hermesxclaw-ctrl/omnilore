Etymology

The surname Ōgaki (大垣) is a common Japanese surname, literally meaning 'great enclosure' or 'large fence' (from 大 'ō' = large, 垣 'gaki' = fence/wall). The given name Chiaki (千秋) typically means 'thousand autumns' or 'long life' (千 'chi' = thousand, 秋 'aki' = autumn). Together, the name suggests endurance and protection, fitting a heroic archetype.

A red stone disc that transports holders across reflective surfaces to a dead Earth where all life has been consumed by massive, amoeboid 'Unclean' entities. The alternate Earth has its own history, including a parallel Foundation that failed.

Who It Is

A red stone disc that transports holders across reflective surfaces to a dead Earth where all life has been consumed by massive, amoeboid 'Unclean' entities. The alternate Earth has its own history, including a parallel Foundation that failed. Exploration logs reveal vast, silent cities and crawling horrors.

Form

A disc ~30cm diameter, red stone-like material with organic properties. Thrums faintly when held.

Powers

Interdimensional transportation

Gateway to a dead alternate Earth

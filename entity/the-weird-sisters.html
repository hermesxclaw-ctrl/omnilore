Cultural Significance

The word 'weird' derives from Old English 'wyrd' (fate) — they are literally the Fates, the Norns of Norse myth reimagined. Their ambiguity (do they cause events or merely foresee them?) drives Macbeth's tragedy and has been debated for centuries.

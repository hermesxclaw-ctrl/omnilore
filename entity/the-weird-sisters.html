Who It Is
Form

The Weird Sisters are depicted as three crones, often dressed in black, with long hair and warts. They are often shown as being hunched over and carrying staffs. Their appearance is meant to convey their age and wisdom.

Powers

Prophecy The Weird Sisters have the power to see into the future and predict the outcome of events. They use this power to guide the actions of others and to shape the course of history.

Magic The Weird Sisters have the power to perform magic and to manipulate the forces of nature. They use this power to achieve their goals and to influence the actions of others.

Shape-shifting The Weird Sisters have the power to shape-shift and to take on different forms. They use this power to move undetected and to gather information.

Accounts

The Weird Sisters appear in Act 1, Scene 1 of Macbeth, where they prophesize the future and shape the course of events. They appear again in Act 4, Scene 1, where they reveal the truth about Macbeth's guilt and his downfall.

The Weird Sisters appear in Norse mythology as the Norns, who are depicted as being responsible for shaping the fate of gods and mortals. They are often shown as being mysterious and powerful, using their powers to shape the course of events.

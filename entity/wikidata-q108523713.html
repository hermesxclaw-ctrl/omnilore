Who It Is

On a lawless archipelago where supernovas gather to gamble with their lives, a nervous polar bear in an orange jumpsuit apologizes for his captain's rudeness — and then stands his ground when Marines open fire. Bepo looks like Trafalgar Law's mascot, but in Eiichiro Oda's One Piece he is something rarer: a Mink navigator who can channel lightning through his fur and, under a full moon, become a towering, white-furred engine of war.

Consistently portrayed as polite, timid, apologetic, and loyal — often apologizing for Law's rudeness or for his own perceived failures. Despite being a large carnivore Mink, he is emotionally sensitive, cries easily, and shows strong camaraderie with Shachi and Penguin. Fiercely loyal to Trafalgar Law, whom he addresses as 'Captain' with deep respect. Brave in combat despite fear; willing to fight Marines, Doflamingo's crew, and Kaido's forces. In Zou, shows homesickness and kinship with other Minks, but also isolation from having grown up at sea rather than on Zou

Name & Tongue

Etymology — No official etymology provided by Eiichiro Oda. Oda has not explained the name in SBS or databooks. Japanese rendering ベポ (Be-po) is katakana, typically used for foreign or invented names. Fan speculation linking it to Italian diminutive of Giuseppe (Beppo) is unattested and not confirmed by creator

Pronunciation — Japanese: /be.po/ (べ-ぽ), two morae, flat pitch. English dub: /ˈbɛpoʊ/ (BEP-oh). French dub: /bepo/.

Aliases — ベポ, BePo

Ownership — Japanese intellectual property. Created by Eiichiro Oda, serialized by Shueisha in Weekly Shōnen Jump, adapted by Toei Animation. Owned and licensed by Shueisha, Shueisha / Shogaku

Powers
Mink Physiology (Polar Bear Mink)

Enhanced strength, agility, durability, keen sense of smell, and cold resistance typical of Minks. As a polar bear, physically powerful — can carry humans, fight armed soldiers, and survive harsh seas. Retains bear claws and fangs for close combat.

Limit: Not as physically dominant as larger Minks (e.g., Pekoms lion Mink). No evidence of superhuman endurance beyond Mink baseline. Vulnerable to conventional weapons, Haki, and sea water fatigue like other Minks.

Electro (Elekiteru)

Mink racial ability to generate and channel electricity through body and fur. Bepo can shock opponents on contact, imbue punches with lightning. Visualized as jagged bolts around fists/fur.

Limit: Requires physical contact or close range; output scales with user's training. Bepo's Electro is competent but not at the level of masters like Carrot or Inuarashi/Nekomamushi. Can be insulated against by rubber (Luffy immunity) or Haki defense.

Sulong (Long Mink) Transformation

Under a full moon, Minks can transform into a powerful, berserk-enhanced form with vastly increased speed, strength, Electro, and ferocity. Bepo achieves Sulong on Onigashima (Wano) when the moon is exposed through the dome. Form features elongated body, flowing fur, glowing red eyes.

Limit: Requires direct exposure to full moon; cloud cover or enclosed spaces prevent transformation. Untrained Sulong causes loss of control and eventual exhaustion/death if prolonged. Bepo initially struggles to control it and is subdued by allies to prevent rampage. Needs training (as shown with Carrot and Wanda) to master.

Martial Arts / Hand-to-Hand Combat

Trained pirate fighter, uses claw swipes, punches enhanced with Electro, and team tactics with Shachi and Penguin. Fights Marines at Sabaody, Caesar's men at Punk Hazard, and Beasts Pirates at Wano.

Limit: No formal martial style named (unlike Pedro's or Pekoms'). Relies on brute Mink strength rather than refined technique. No evidence of Rokushiki, Fish-Man Karate, or advanced weapon mastery.

Navigation and Seamanship

Official role is Navigator of the Heart Pirates, responsible for charting and piloting the Polar Tang submarine. Vivre Card explicitly lists 'Navigator' as his position. Implied competence in submarine navigation, though Law also gives orders.

Limit: Feats of navigation are rarely shown on-page; most navigation decisions are attributed to Law. No demonstrated Log Pose reading or New World current expertise beyond crew function. Compared to Nami, his navigational skill is unattested in detail.

Haki (Unconfirmed)

No confirmed Busoshoku or Kenbunshoku Haki usage by Bepo in manga or databooks as of Chapter 1100+. Some fans speculate all New World pirates have basic Haki, but no panel shows Bepo using it.

Limit: If he possesses Haki, it is unattested. Cannot be listed as attested ability.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Bepo is a polar bear Mink — an anthropomorphic bear with predominantly white fur, black nose, small rounded ears, and a stout muzzle. Stands approximately 240 cm tall, taller than most humans but shorter than large Minks like Pedro. Pre-timeskip and post-timeskip design is consistent: wears an orange jumpsuit with Heart Pirates jolly roger (smiley with spotted hat) on chest, often with a zipper partially open. Post-timeskip he wears a similar jum

Symbols — Heart Pirates emblem (white heart with smile and Law's spotted pattern) worn on jumpsuit. Represents affiliation, not personal heraldry. As a Mink, his visual iconography links to Zou and the broader Mink Tribe motifs: tribal patterns, moon symbolism for Sulong. No personal weapon or sigil. His apologetic hand-raised pose and frequent 'Sorry!' (Sumimasen!) are character shorthand for timid politeness contrasting with pirate ferocit

Domains — Primary habitat: Polar Bear submarine (Heart Pirates' yellow submarine) as navigator and crew member. Operates at sea, Sabaody Archipelago, Punk Hazard, Dressrosa, Zou, Wano Country (Onigashima), and later Winner Island. Ethnic homeland: Zou (Mokomo Dukedom) on the back of the elephant Zunesha, ancestral home of Minks — Bepo is Zou-born but left as a child with Law's crew, per Zou dialogue (Chapter 805-808). Accounts I
Debut at Sabaody Archipelago — The Eleven SupernovasOne Piece Vol. 51, Chapters 498-504; Anime Episodes 392-405

2008 (publication); In-universe: 1522, Sabaody Archipelago, pre-Marineford

Bepo appears alongside Captain Trafalgar Law, Shachi, and Penguin at Shakky's Rip-off Bar and the Human Auction House. As part of the Heart Pirates, he witnesses the gathering of the Eleven Supernovas (later Worst Generation), the Straw Hats' assault on a World Noble (Saint Charlos), and the subsequent Marine Admiral Kizaru's attack. Bepo fights alongside his crew to escape the island, displaying loyalty and fear in equal measure — apologizing even as he flees Marines. Establishes Heart Pirates as major Worst Generation crew; Bepo's timid-but-loyal archetype is set. The Sabaody incident triggers the Marineford War and the two-year timeskip. Bepo escapes with Law.

Marineford Aftermath — Saving Luffy and JinbeOne Piece Vol. 59, Chapters 578-580; Anime Episodes 484-491

2010-2011 (publication); In-universe: Marineford War aftermath

After the Battle of Marineford, Law's submarine surfaces at the war's edge. Bepo is aboard the Polar Tang as Law orders the rescue of critically wounded Monkey D. Luffy and Jinbe, defying Marine pursuit. Bepo assists in hauling the wounded onto the submarine and in the escape from Akainu's pursuit, navigating under fire. The crew then takes Luffy to Amazon Lily and later delivers him to Rayleigh. Cements alliance between Heart Pirates and Straw Hats; Law gains notoriety for saving Luffy. Bepo's role as navigator/crew support is highlighted. Leads directly to Punk Hazard alliance two years later.

Punk Hazard — Alliance Forged in Poison and ChildrenOne Piece Vol. 66-70, Chapters 653-699; Anime Episodes 579-625

2012-2013 (publication); In-universe: New World, Punk Hazard

Two years later, the Heart Pirates reunite at Punk Hazard. Bepo, now slightly older, accompanies Law as he allies with Luffy to take down Caesar Clown and target Kaido. Bepo participates in the capture of Caesar, the battle against Vergo and Monet, and the care of the giant, drugged children. He is often seen with Shachi and Penguin providing comic relief while also fighting Caesar's men. The alliance between Straw Hat and Heart Pirates is formally sealed. Pirate Alliance formed; leads to Dressrosa operation against Donquixote Doflamingo. Bepo's loyalty and Electro potential still unrevealed at this point, keeping Mink mystery intact.

Accounts II
Dressrosa — The War Against DoflamingoOne Piece Vol. 71-80, Chapters 700-801; Anime Episodes 629-746

2013-2016 (publication); In-universe: Dressrosa

Bepo and the Heart Pirates support the alliance's plan to destroy the SMILE factory and dethrone Warlord Donquixote Doflamingo. While Law, Luffy, and part of the crew infiltrate Dressrosa, Bepo remains largely aboard the Polar Tang with Shachi and Penguin, maintaining the ship and later assisting in the escape after Doflamingo's defeat. He is shown worrying intensely for Law during Law's capture and torture by Doflamingo, emphasizing emotional bond. Doflamingo defeated; Kaido's SMILE supply cut; Bounties raised. Heart Pirates survive and sail toward Zou, where Bepo's heritage will be revealed.

Zou — Return to the Mink HomelandOne Piece Vol. 80-82, Chapters 802-822; Anime Episodes 751-774

2016 (publication); In-universe: Zou, Mokomo Dukedom

Arriving at Zou, Bepo discovers his people for the first time in years. The island's Minks (Wanda, Carrot, Inuarashi, Nekomamushi) immediately recognize him as a fellow Mink — revealing to readers that Bepo is not a normal bear but a Mink who left Zou as a child. He reunites emotionally with the tribe, learns about the history of Zou, the Road Poneglyph, and the suffering under Jack's invasion. Pedro and other Minks welcome him, and Bepo's backstory as a Zou native who became a pirate is clarified. He participates in the celebration and the revelation of the Kozuki clan's connection. Major lore reveal: Bepo's species and origin explained; Mink lore (Electro, Sulong foreshadowed) established. Deepens Bepo's character from mascot to displaced Mink. Sets up Wano alliance.

Wano Country — Onigashima Raid and Sulong UnleashedOne Piece Vol. 97-104, Chapters 978-1050; Anime Episodes 982-1089

2020-2022 (publication); In-universe: Wano Country, Onigashima

During the Raid on Onigashima against Kaido and Big Mom, Bepo fights alongside Heart Pirates and the Ninja-Pirate-Mink-Samurai Alliance. When the full moon is revealed through Kaido's cloud manipulation, Bepo and other Minks transform into Sulong. Bepo's Sulong form debuts in Chapter 998 — massive, feral, eyes glowing — as he attempts to rescue Law from Kaido's forces. He is eventually subdued by allies to prevent berserk exhaustion, showing the double-edged nature of Sulong. He survives the raid and celebrates the alliance's victory and liberation of Wano. Sulong lore payoff; Bepo shown as capable combatant, not just navigator. Alliance defeats two Emperors. Bepo's control over Sulong remains imperfect, leaving character growth open.

2022-2023 (publication); In-universe: Winner Island, New World

After Wano, the Heart Pirates sail separately from the Straw Hats. At Winner Island, they are ambushed by the Blackbeard Pirates (Marshall D. Teach). Bepo is present during the devastating battle where Law faces Blackbeard. In Chapter 1081, as Law is defeated and his crew is overwhelmed, Bepo — having taken a mysterious drug provided by Chopper earlier? Heart Pirates defeated and scattered; Law's Poneglyph rubbings stolen; Bepo's loyalty culminates in saving Law's life. Sets up future revenge/recovery arc. Bepo is just Law's pet bear or mascot, not a real crew member — Bepo is a Mink (sentient polar bear tribe member) and the official navigator of the Heart Pirates, with combat abilities (Electro, Sulong) and full crew status. Vivre Card explicitly lists his role as Navigator, and he participates in strategy and battles.

Bepo is a normal polar bear who ate a Devil Fruit to become anthropomorphic — Bepo is a Mink, a naturally anthropomorphic race in One Piece. He has not eaten a Devil Fruit. Minks are born as humanoid animals with Electro and Sulong abilities. No Devil Fruit is involved.

Bepo is weak and only for comic relief — While used for comedy, Bepo is a competent New World fighter. He survived Sabaody, Marineford, Punk Hazard, Dressrosa, and Wano, can use Electro and Sulong, and was trusted to save Law from Blackbeard. His timid personality does not equal weakness.

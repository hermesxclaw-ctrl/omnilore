Who It Is
Form

SCP-3935 is described as a 'quiet madness' that affects those who come into contact with it. It is believed to be a anomalous object or entity that has the ability to induce a state of madness in those who encounter it.

Powers

Inducing Madness SCP-3935 has the ability to induce a state of madness in those who encounter it. This can manifest in a variety of ways, including hallucinations, paranoia, and a complete breakdown of rational thought.

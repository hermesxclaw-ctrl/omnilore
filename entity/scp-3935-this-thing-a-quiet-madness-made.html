Who It Is

A small town in Indiana where, in 1972, the entire population of the local high school spontaneously vanished into a single classroom. The classroom now contains an extradimensional space — a labyrinth of school corridors and classrooms that repeats infinitely. Echoes of the missing students can be heard throughout: laughter, lockers slamming, yearbook signatures. At the center of the labyrinth, something waits. Something that was summoned by a group of students experimenting with a ritual designed to 'create a god.'

Form

From the exterior: a normal midwestern high school building. The anomalous classroom looks identical to any other 1972-era classroom, but its door opens into an infinite recursive series of school corridors. The entity at the center has been described as 'a god shaped like a hallway' — a being that is the school itself.

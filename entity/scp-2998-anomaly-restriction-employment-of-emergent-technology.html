SCP-2998 — Anomaly Restriction, Employment of Emergent Technology

also known asSCP-2998Anomaly Restriction, Employment of Emergent Technology
Typescp
CultureSCP Foundation
Who It Is
Form

SCP-2998 appears as a large, cylindrical device with a series of glowing blue lines running along its length. The device is approximately 10 meters in height and 5 meters in diameter, and is surrounded by a network of sensors and monitoring equipment.

Powers

Containment and Management of Anomalous Entities SCP-2998 is capable of containing and managing a wide range of anomalous entities, including but not limited to: SCP-1718, SCP-1609, and SCP-2998-A.

Incident Log

In 2022, the SCP Foundation deployed SCP-2998 as part of Operation 'Erebus', a large-scale containment effort aimed at neutralizing a group of highly anomalous entities.

In 2023, the SCP Foundation created a new version of SCP-2998, known as SCP-2998-A. This version of the device was designed to be more advanced and capable than its predecessor.

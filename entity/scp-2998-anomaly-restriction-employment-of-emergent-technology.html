Who It Is

A broadcast signal from the Kuiper Belt containing only a list of names. Each person named dies within 24 hours. The deaths are always 'accidental.' The signal has been broadcasting for 10,000 years — ancient recordings name individuals who would have posed existential threats. The Foundation is now on the list. Something in the outer solar system has decided the Foundation is a threat.

Form

No physical form. The signal's point of origin is a region of empty space. Deep-space probes have found nothing. It is broadcasting from somewhere else.

Powers

Precognitive threat identification

Remote execution across any distance

Temporal foresight (active for millennia)

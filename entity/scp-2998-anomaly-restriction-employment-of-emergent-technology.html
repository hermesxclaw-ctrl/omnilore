Form & Signs

Appearance — SCP-2998 appears as a large, cylindrical device with a series of glowing blue lines running along its length. The device is approximately 10 meters in height and 5 meters in diameter, and is surrounded by a network of sensors and monitoring equipment.

Symbols — The device is adorned with a series of cryptic symbols and markings, which are believed to be a form of encryption or coding. These symbols are thought to be related to the device's ability to contain and manage anomalous entities.

Domains — SCP-2998 is typically found in highly controlled environments, such as research facilities or containment units. However, it has been known to be deployed in a variety of different settings, including field operations and emergency response situations.

Accounts I
The Deployment of SCP-2998 in Operation 'Erebus'SCP Foundation Document #2998-003

2022-2023

In 2022, the SCP Foundation deployed SCP-2998 as part of Operation 'Erebus', a large-scale containment effort aimed at neutralizing a group of highly anomalous entities. The deployment of SCP-2998 was highly successful, resulting in the containment and neutralization of all anomalous entities involved.

The Creation of SCP-2998-ASCP Foundation Document #2998-004

2023-2024

In 2023, the SCP Foundation created a new version of SCP-2998, known as SCP-2998-A. This version of the device was designed to be more advanced and capable than its predecessor. The creation of SCP-2998-A was highly successful, resulting in a significant improvement in the device's capabilities and effectiveness.

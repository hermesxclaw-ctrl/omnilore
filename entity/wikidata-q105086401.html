Who It Is

In the vast pantheon of DC Comics, one figure stands apart as a cosmic architect and divine judge, whose light has guided the fate of an entire alien race across the stars. Rao, the sun god of Krypton, is a deity whose influence extends far beyond his worshippers, shaping the destiny of Superman and the legacy of a lost world.

Rao is portrayed as a distant, all-powerful deity who observes the affairs of Kryptonians without direct intervention. He is often seen as a benevolent creator, but also as a strict judge who punishes transgressions. In some stories, Rao's will is interpreted by the Kryptonian priesthood, leading to dogmatic practices.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Rao' is derived from the real-world astronomical designation for the star that serves as Krypton's sun, often identified with the red dwarf star LHS 2520 in the constellation Corvus. In the DC Universe, Rao is the name given to this star and its personification as a deity.

Pronunciation — RAH-oh

Powers
Solar Manipulation

As a sun god, Rao has control over solar energy, able to grant or withhold powers to Kryptonians under a yellow sun. In some stories, he can create solar flares or manipulate stellar phenomena.

Limit: His power is typically limited to the sun of Krypton; he cannot affect other stars or realms without specific circumstances.

Divine Judgment

Rao is said to judge the souls of Kryptonians after death, determining their fate in the afterlife. This is a core tenet of Kryptonian religion.

Limit: The actual mechanism and existence of an afterlife are unclear; this is primarily a matter of faith.

Immortality and Omnipresence

As a deity, Rao is immortal and can be present wherever Kryptonian culture exists, though his physical manifestation is rare.

Limit: He is not truly omnipotent or omniscient; he has been deceived or challenged by other cosmic beings.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Rao is rarely depicted visually. When shown, he appears as a radiant, humanoid figure composed of solar energy, often with a stern, majestic face. In some interpretations, he is a cosmic entity of immense size, embodying the star itself. No consistent canonical appearance exists.

Symbols — The symbol of Rao is often associated with the Kryptonian crest, particularly the 'S' shield, which in some stories is derived from Rao's symbol. The sun itself is the primary icon, with red solar imagery used in Kryptonian art and architecture. Rao's temples are depicted as grand structures aligned with the sun.

Domains — Rao resides in the sun of Krypton, which is the red star that gave Kryptonians their powers under a yellow sun. He is the chief deity of the Kryptonian pantheon, with dominion over creation, life, and judgment. His influence extends to all Kryptonian culture and religion.

Pre-Crisis

Rao created the Kryptonian race as his chosen people, endowing them with advanced intellect and a strict code of laws. He later grew disappointed with their hubris and allowed their world to be destroyed. The destruction of Krypton and the exile of Kal-El (Superman) to Earth.

Accounts II
The Judgment of Jor-ElSuperman: The Animated Series (1996) - 'The Last Son of Krypton'

Modern

Jor-El, a Kryptonian scientist, challenges the religious dogma of Rao's followers, leading to his condemnation. He argues that Rao is a myth and that Krypton's doom is scientific, not divine. Jor-El is marginalized, and his warnings are ignored, leading to Krypton's destruction.

The New 52 RaoSuperman: Doomed (2014) and related issues

New 52

Rao is revealed to be a malevolent cosmic being who created Kryptonians as a weapon. He later returns to Earth to reclaim his 'children', forcing Superman to fight him. Rao is defeated and killed by Superman, marking a rare instance of a deity's death.

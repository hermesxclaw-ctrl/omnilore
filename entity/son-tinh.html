Who It Is
Form

Son Tinh is often depicted as a powerful and majestic mountain spirit, with the ability to control the weather and the forces of nature.

Powers

Weather Control Son Tinh has the ability to control the weather, summoning storms and droughts as he sees fit.

Mountain Manipulation Son Tinh can manipulate the mountains and forests, creating new peaks and valleys as he desires.

Elemental Resistance Son Tinh is resistant to the elements, able to withstand even the most powerful storms and floods.

Accounts

The legend of Son Tinh and Thuy Tinh tells the story of the eternal struggle between the mountain god and the god of the waters.

The myth of Son Tinh and Thuy Tinh explains the practice of tidal irrigation and the devastating floods that occur in the South Central Coast region of Vietnam.

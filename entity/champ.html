Who It Is
Form

Champ is often described as a serpent-like creature, but with more fish-like characteristics. It has a long, slender body, typically estimated to be between 15 and 30 feet in length. Some accounts describe it as having a horse-like head or a large, flat head. The coloration of Champ is often reported as dark brown or black, with a lighter underside.

Powers

Aquatic Adaptation Champ's ability to thrive in the aquatic environment of Lake Champlain, allowing it to swim and maneuver with ease.

Accounts

The first reported sighting of Champ was in 1819, when a group of Native American tribesmen claimed to have seen a large, serpent-like creature in Lake Champlain.

Champ has been featured in numerous films, books, and other forms of popular culture, cementing its place in the public imagination.

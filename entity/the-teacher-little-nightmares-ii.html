Who It Is

The second major antagonist of Little Nightmares II. A schoolteacher whose neck can extend to impossible lengths, allowing her to pursue children through the labyrinthine corridors of her decaying school. She is strict, cruel, and her authority is absolute — the porcelain children in her school obey her without question. When she detects an intruder, her head twists 180 degrees and she gives chase, her elongated neck snaking through hallways and vents.

Form

A tall woman in a prim, old-fashioned teacher's dress. Her most notable feature is her neck, which can stretch to dozens of meters, allowing her head to pursue prey through any opening. Her face is locked in a stern, disapproving grimace. She plays a piano in her spare time — discordant, haunting melodies that echo through the school.

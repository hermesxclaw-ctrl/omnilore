Who It Is
Form

Altjira is often depicted as a powerful, sky-dwelling deity, with the ability to control the weather and create the land.

Powers

Weather Control Altjira has the power to control the weather, creating storms and droughts as he sees fit.

Creation Altjira is said to have created the world and all living things, using his powers to shape the land and bring forth life.

Accounts

Altjira is said to have sent a great flood to destroy the wicked and bring forth a new era of peace and prosperity.

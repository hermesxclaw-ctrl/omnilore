Who It Is

In the sewers of New York, where heroes rise and villains fall, there lurks a predator unlike any other—a mutant shark with a taste for vengeance. Armaggon is not just another foe for the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles; he is a relentless hunter from a possible future, a cybernetic nightmare whose jaws have snapped across timelines. But who is this creature beneath the steel and sinew? What drives a shark to walk on land and wage war against the half-shell heroes? Dive into the depths of Armaggon's history, from his first appearance in the comics to his many incarnations across games and animation, and discover the truth behind one of the Turtles' most fearsome adversaries.

Armaggon is ruthless, cunning, and driven by a desire for revenge against the Turtles. He is a skilled fighter and strategist, often using his aquatic abilities to his advantage. He is also arrogant and enjoys taunting his opponents.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Armaggon' is a portmanteau of 'arm' and 'Maggon', possibly referencing his cybernetic arms and his aggressive nature. It may also be a play on 'armageddon', given his destructive tendencies.

Pronunciation — AR-muh-gon

Aliases — Armaggon the Shark, Shark Mutant

Ownership — Owned by Mirage Studios and ViacomCBS (now Paramount Global). Created by writer/artist Dean Clarrain (pseudonym for Steve Murphy) and artist Jim Lawson for the Archie Comics series

Form & Signs

Appearance — Armaggon is a massive, anthropomorphic shark with a muscular build. He has a grey-blue skin, a large dorsal fin, and a shark-like head with rows of sharp teeth. In some versions, he has cybernetic enhancements, such as a metallic arm or bionic implants. He typically wears a black and purple outfit or harness.

Symbols — His primary symbol is a shark's fin, often depicted on his chest or back. He is associated with water, hunting, and ferocity.

Domains — He comes from a dystopian future where he leads a gang of mutant sharks. He is equally at home in water and on land, but prefers aquatic environments.

Accounts I
Future Shark Saga (Archie Comics)Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles Adventures #26-28

1991

In a dystopian future, Armaggon is the leader of a gang of mutant sharks who terrorize the world. The Turtles are transported to this future and must defeat Armaggon to return home. They discover that Armaggon is responsible for the destruction of their future selves. The Turtles defeat Armaggon and change the future, preventing his rise to power.

Shark Attack! (1987 TV series)Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (1987) episode 'Shark Attack!'

1990

Krang creates Armaggon by mutating a shark with mutagen. Armaggon is sent to capture the Turtles, but they defeat him with the help of a new invention. Armaggon is defeated and presumably returned to the sewers or the Dimension X.

Armaggon! (2012 TV series)Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (2012) episode 'Armaggon!'

2015

In an alternate future, Armaggon has taken over New York and killed the Turtles. The 2012 Turtles travel to this future and must find a way to defeat him and return to their own time. The Turtles defeat Armaggon and the future is restored.

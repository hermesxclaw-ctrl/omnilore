The Approach

In the sewers of New York, where heroes rise and villains fall, there lurks a predator unlike any other—a mutant shark with a taste for vengeance. Armaggon is not just another foe for the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles; he is a relentless hunter from a possible future, a cybernetic nightmare whose jaws have snapped across timelines. But who is this creature beneath the steel and sinew? What drives a shark to walk on land and wage war against the half-shell heroes? Dive into the depths of Armaggon's history, from his first appearance in the comics to his many incarnations across games and animation, and discover the truth behind one of the Turtles' most fearsome adversaries.

Identity

Armaggon

Pronunciation AR-muh-gon

Known As

Armaggon the SharkShark Mutant

Etymology

The name 'Armaggon' is a portmanteau of 'arm' and 'Maggon', possibly referencing his cybernetic arms and his aggressive nature. It may also be a play on 'armageddon', given his destructive tendencies.

Cultural Ownership

Owned by Mirage Studios and ViacomCBS (now Paramount Global). Created by writer/artist Dean Clarrain (pseudonym for Steve Murphy) and artist Jim Lawson for the Archie Comics series.

Disputed Identifications

Armaggon is sometimes confused with other shark-based characters in the TMNT franchise, such as the Street Shark or the mutant shark in the 2012 series, but these are distinct entities.

Description

Appearance

Armaggon is a massive, anthropomorphic shark with a muscular build. He has a grey-blue skin, a large dorsal fin, and a shark-like head with rows of sharp teeth. In some versions, he has cybernetic enhancements, such as a metallic arm or bionic implants. He typically wears a black and purple outfit or harness.

Symbols & Iconography

His primary symbol is a shark's fin, often depicted on his chest or back. He is associated with water, hunting, and ferocity.

Behaviour & Personality

Armaggon is ruthless, cunning, and driven by a desire for revenge against the Turtles. He is a skilled fighter and strategist, often using his aquatic abilities to his advantage. He is also arrogant and enjoys taunting his opponents.

Domains & Habitats

He comes from a dystopian future where he leads a gang of mutant sharks. He is equally at home in water and on land, but prefers aquatic environments.

Version Differences

In the Archie Comics, he is a mutant shark from the future. In the 1987 TV series, he is a mutant shark created by Krang. In the 2012 series, he is a mutant shark from an alternate future. In the video games, his backstory is often simplified, and he appears as a powerful boss or playable character.

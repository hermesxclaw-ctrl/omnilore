Who It Is

Âu Cơ is the immortal mountain fairy who, with the dragon-king Lạc Long Quân, gave birth to one hundred sons from one hundred eggs — the ancestors of the Vietnamese people. The fundamental difference in their natures caused them to separate; she took fifty sons to the mountains. She is venerated as the First Mother in Vietnamese folk tradition.

Attested in Oral tradition / Lĩnh Nam chích quáiEra Hồng Bàng period

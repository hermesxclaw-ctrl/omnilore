Who It Is
Form

The Gargouille is described as a dragon with bat-like wings, a long neck, and a fearsome roar. Its appearance is often depicted in medieval art and literature as a monstrous creature with a mix of reptilian and avian features.

Powers

Breath of Fire The Gargouille is said to have the ability to breathe fire, which it uses to terrorize its enemies and protect its domain.

Accounts

The Gargouille is a legendary monster that is said to have terrorized the town of Rouen in Normandy, France during the 7th century.

The Gargouille is said to inhabit the waters of the Seine River and the surrounding countryside.

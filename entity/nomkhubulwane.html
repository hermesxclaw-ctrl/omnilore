Who It Is
Form

Nomkhubulwane is often depicted as a beautiful young woman with a rainbow-colored cloak, carrying a staff or a basket of grain.

Powers

Rainmaking Nomkhubulwane has the power to bring rain and fertility to the land, ensuring a bountiful harvest and prosperity for the community.

Accounts

Nomkhubulwane is said to have been born from the rainbow, and was tasked with bringing rain and fertility to the land.

Nomkhubulwane is said to have been a beautiful young woman who was tasked with bringing rain and fertility to the land.

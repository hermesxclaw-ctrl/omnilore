Who It Is

A ferocious warrior-goddess of unbridled violence and tender fertility, Anat defies easy categorization. In the Ugaritic Baal Cycle she is both the sister and consort of Baal, wading knee-deep in the blood of his enemies with savage glee, yet also the compassionate mourner who retrieves his corpse from the underworld and personally dismembers Mot to resurrect her brother. Anat presides over the paradox of creation-through-destruction: her martial fury clears the way for agricultural renewal. Epithets like 'the Virgin Anat' (btlt 'nt) connote youthful vigor and nubility rather than chastity in the modern sense.

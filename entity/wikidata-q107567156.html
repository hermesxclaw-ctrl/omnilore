The Approach

A name whispered across centuries of Japanese history and fiction, Raizō carries the weight of samurai honor, cinematic tragedy, and political intrigue. From the silent stillness of a kabuki stage to the thunder of naval guns, those who bear this name are never merely bystanders—they are shapers of fate.

Etymology

The name can be written with various kanji combinations, each carrying different meanings. Common forms include 頼三 (relying on three), 来蔵 (coming treasure), and 雷蔵 (thunder treasure). The meaning varies with the kanji used.

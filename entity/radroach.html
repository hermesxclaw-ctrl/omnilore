Who It Is

In the post-apocalyptic wastelands of the Fallout universe, few creatures are as ubiquitous—or as underestimated—as the radroach. These irradiated cockroaches, mutated by centuries of nuclear fallout, scuttle through ruined buildings and irradiated ruins, serving as both a nuisance and a grim reminder of humanity's folly. But beyond their role as low-level pests, radroaches have burrowed deep into gaming culture, becoming emblematic of survival, resilience, and the absurdity of a world gone mad.

Radroaches are aggressive but relatively weak creatures. They attack by lunging at their prey, biting with their mandibles. They are often found in groups, emerging from dark corners, ruined buildings, and sewers. They are not particularly intelligent, but their swift movements and numbers can overwhelm an unprepared traveler. They are attracted to heat and food sources, making human settlements a common hunting ground.

Powers
Radiation Resistance

As a result of their mutation, radroaches are highly resistant to radiation, allowing them to thrive in heavily irradiated areas.

Limit: They are still vulnerable to physical damage and fire.

Bite Attack

Radroaches can bite with their mandibles, causing minor physical damage and sometimes poisoning the target.

Limit: Low damage output; easily avoided by moving out of range.

Swarming

Radroaches often attack in groups, overwhelming enemies through numbers rather than individual strength.

Limit: They have low health and can be killed with a few hits.

Camouflage

Radroaches can blend into dark environments, making them difficult to spot until they move.

Limit: Not a true invisibility; they can be detected with careful observation or a pip-boy light.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Radroaches are large, mutated cockroaches, typically ranging from the size of a cat to a small dog. They have a dark brown or black chitinous exoskeleton, long antennae, six spiny legs, and a segmented abdomen. Their eyes are compound and often glow with a faint red or green luminescence, suggesting radiation exposure. Symbols — In the Fallout franchise, radroaches symbolize the resilience of life in a post-nuclear world. They are often used as a tutorial enemy, representing the mundane dangers of the wasteland. Their image has become iconic for the series, appearing in promotional art, merchandise, and fan art.

Domains — Radroaches thrive in urban ruins, underground tunnels, and any area with organic debris. They are common in the Capital Wasteland, the Commonwealth, and Appalachia. They are particularly abundant in Vaults, where they often infest the corridors and living quarters.

The player character, born in Vault 101, encounters radroaches during their escape from the Vault. A radroach infestation in the Vault's reactor room serves as an early combat tutorial. The player learns combat mechanics and gains initial experience.

Radroaches are a common threat in the Commonwealth, often found near ruined buildings and in the subway systems. They serve as low-level enemies for the Sole Survivor. Their presence reinforces the hostile nature of the wasteland and provides easy loot.

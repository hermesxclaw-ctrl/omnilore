Who It Is

He is the walking corpse of a god, enthroned upon a golden throne for ten thousand years, his will a psychic lighthouse in a galaxy of darkness. To the Imperium of Man, he is the Master of Mankind, the savior who shattered the age of strife and conquered the stars. To his enemies, he is the greatest tyrant the universe has ever known, a despot who sacrificed his own sons to forge a regime of superstition and terror. The Emperor of Mankind is the central, paradoxical figure of Warhammer 40,000—a being of immense power and tragic failure, whose every action is debated, whose true nature is hidden, and whose legacy is a galaxy burning in his name.

The Emperor is depicted as cold, calculating, and utterly focused on the survival of humanity. He is willing to make horrific sacrifices for the greater good, including the lives of his own sons. He is secretive, often withholding information even from his most trusted advisors. He is also shown to be compassionate in rare moments, but generally appears detached and pragmatic. After his internment, he is largely silent, communicating only through psychic visions and the Astronomican.

Powers
Psychic Mastery

The Emperor is considered the most powerful human psyker ever to exist. He can manipulate the Warp on a galactic scale, project his consciousness, and perform feats of immense psychic power, such as creating the Astronomican and battling Chaos Gods.

Limit: His powers are not unlimited; he was vulnerable to corruption and physical harm, as evidenced by his injuries during the Horus Heresy. After his internment, his psychic powers are largely focused on maintaining the Astronomican and sustaining his own life.

Immortality (Perpetual)

The Emperor is a Perpetual, a rare type of human who resurrects upon death. This was revealed in the Horus Heresy series, explaining his long lifespan and survival through the ages.

Limit: His immortality is tied to his psychic nature; it may be possible to permanently kill him through certain means, such as the use of powerful Warp entities or artifacts.

Superhuman Physiology and Combat Skill

Even before his psychic powers, the Emperor was physically and mentally superior to ordinary humans. He is a master of strategy, swordsmanship, and martial arts, capable of defeating even the most powerful beings in direct combat.

Limit: Despite his prowess, he was gravely wounded by Horus, showing that he is not invincible.

Biomancy and Genetic Manipulation

The Emperor used his powers to create the Primarchs and the Space Marines, manipulating genetic material and Warp energies to create superhuman beings.

Limit: The creation process was imperfect, leading to the scattering of the Primarchs and the flaws in the Space Marine gene-seed.

Telepathy and Mind Control

The Emperor can communicate telepathically across vast distances, read minds, and influence or control the thoughts of others.

Limit: His telepathy is not infallible; some beings, such as the Chaos Gods, can resist or deceive him.

The Astronomican

The Emperor's psychic power fuels the Astronomican, a psychic beacon that enables faster-than-light travel through the Warp. It is a constant, massive expenditure of his power.

Limit: Maintaining the Astronomican requires the Emperor to remain on the Golden Throne, effectively immobilizing him.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Emperor's physical appearance has been described in various sources. During the Great Crusade, he was said to be a towering figure of immense charisma, with features that seemed to shift subtly to appeal to those who beheld him. He often wore golden armor and carried a flaming sword. After his internment in the Golden Throne, he appears as a withered, skeletal corpse, sustained by arcane machinery, his body a mass of dry flesh and golden wire

Symbols — The primary symbol associated with the Emperor is the Aquila, a two-headed eagle, which represents the Imperium and the Emperor's dominion over humanity. Other symbols include the Imperialis, a skull with a winged design, and the golden throne itself. The Emperor is often depicted with a halo or radiant light, symbolizing his divine status in the Imperial Cult.

Domains — The Emperor's primary domain is the Imperial Palace on Terra (Earth), specifically the Golden Throne in the Sanctum Imperialis. He is also psychically present in the Warp, where he manifests as a powerful entity that guides the Astronomican and protects humanity. His influence extends across the entire Imperium, as the focal point of the Imperial Cult.

Accounts I
The Origins of the EmperorHorus Heresy series, particularly 'The Master of Mankind' and 'Mechanicum'

Ancient Earth, circa 8,000 BC

The Emperor was born in ancient Anatolia (modern Turkey) as a Perpetual, the result of the mass suicide of a group of ancient shamans who sought to create a being to protect humanity from the dangers of the Warp. He lived through human history, guiding humanity in secret, appearing as various influential figures, and working to prepare humanity for the challenges of the future. The Emperor gained immense psychic powers and immortality, setting the stage for his later role as the ruler of humanity.

The Unification of TerraHorus Heresy series, various codexes

Late 30th Millennium

After the Age of Strife, the Emperor emerged from obscurity to unite the warring factions of Terra under his rule. He created the Thunder Warriors, genetically enhanced soldiers, to conquer the planet, and then disbanded them, creating the Space Marines and the Primarchs to lead his Great Crusade. The unification of Terra allowed the Emperor to begin the Great Crusade to reunite humanity across the galaxy.

Accounts II
The Great Crusade and the PrimarchsHorus Heresy series, particularly 'Horus Rising', 'False Gods', 'Galaxy in Flames'

30th Millennium (c. 799-800 M30)

The Emperor led the Great Crusade to reclaim the lost human worlds, aided by his twenty Primarchs, genetically engineered demigods. He intended to create a secular, scientific Imperium, but the Chaos Gods, jealous of his power, scattered the Primarchs across the galaxy. The Emperor and his sons fought to unite humanity, but tensions grew, leading to the Horus Heresy. The Great Crusade expanded the Imperium, but also sowed the seeds of rebellion, culminating in the Horus Heresy.

The Horus HeresyHorus Heresy series (entire series)

Early 31st Millennium (c. 005-014 M31)

Horus, the Emperor's most favored son, fell to Chaos and led a rebellion against his father. The Heresy tore the Imperium apart, culminating in a final battle on the Vengeful Spirit, where the Emperor fought Horus. The Emperor was mortally wounded, but managed to destroy Horus. He was then interred in the Golden Throne to preserve his life and power. The Emperor became a living corpse, the Imperium descended into religious fanaticism, and the Age of the Imperium began.

The Emperor is a god and should be worshipped. — The Emperor was an atheist and opposed religion. The Imperial Cult is a post-Heresy development that he did not endorse.

The Emperor is all-powerful. — The Emperor is extremely powerful but not omnipotent. He was wounded by Horus and is now confined to the Golden Throne.

The Emperor created the Space Marines to protect humanity. — The Space Marines were created as tools of conquest for the Great Crusade, not primarily as defenders.

The Emperor is a tyrant who cares nothing for humanity. — The Emperor's motivations are complex; he is willing to make terrible sacrifices, but his ultimate goal is humanity's survival.

The Emperor is a fictional version of Jesus Christ. — There is no canonical connection between the Emperor and Jesus. Any similarities are coincidental or thematic.

Who It Is

In the darkest reaches of the cosmos, a hunter stalks its prey—not for sustenance, but for the thrill of the chase. The Predator, a name whispered across galaxies, is a living embodiment of the hunt, a force of nature that tests the mettle of all who cross its path. But this entity is not merely a creature of flesh and bone; it is a concept, a cosmic force that has manifested in countless forms across cultures and media, leaving a trail of trophies and legends in its wake.

Predators are honor-bound hunters. They seek worthy prey and follow a strict code of conduct. They generally do not harm unarmed or defenseless opponents, but they will kill anyone who poses a threat or challenges them. They are methodical, patient, and use advanced technology to track and hunt. They view their hunts as a rite of passage and a way to prove their worth. They are not inherently evil, but their actions are brutal and deadly.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Predator' derives from the Latin 'praedator', meaning 'one who plunders or takes prey'. In the film's context, it refers to the creature's role as a hunter. The species' self-designation, 'Yautja', was introduced in later comics and expanded media.

Pronunciation — PRED-uh-ter (English); Yautja is typically pronounced 'YOWT-ya' or 'YOWCH-ya'.

Aliases — Yautja, Hunters, The Hunters, Demon, Devil, Ghost

Ownership — The Predator is a creation of 20th Century Fox (now 20th Century Studios, a subsidiary of The Walt Disney Company). It was conceived by Jim Thomas and John Thomas for the 1987 film

Powers
Cloaking Device

The Predator can render itself partially or fully invisible, bending light around its body. This is a technological ability, not a natural one.

Limit: The cloak is not perfect; it can be disrupted by water, blood, or other substances, and the Predator's outline can be seen when moving quickly.

Plasma Caster

A shoulder-mounted energy weapon that fires plasma bolts. It has a targeting laser and can be aimed with the Predator's helmet.

Limit: The plasma caster has a limited power supply and can be destroyed. It is not used in close combat.

Wrist Blades

Retractable blades on the Predator's wrist, used for melee combat. They are extremely sharp and can slice through most materials.

Limit: They are only effective in close range, and the Predator must be within arm's reach.

Superhuman Strength and Agility

Predators are physically superior to humans, able to lift heavy objects, jump great distances, and move with surprising speed.

Limit: They are not invulnerable; they can be injured by conventional weapons, as seen in the films.

Enhanced Senses

Predators have excellent vision, hearing, and smell. Their masks provide additional vision modes, including thermal, infrared, and electromagnetic.

Limit: The thermal vision can be fooled by heat sources, and the Predator's natural senses are not as acute as some other species.

Self-Destruct Device

A small nuclear device on the Predator's wrist, used to destroy itself and its surroundings when captured or defeated.

Limit: It is a last resort and kills the Predator.

Form & Signs

Appearance — The Predator is a large, humanoid extraterrestrial, typically around 7-8 feet tall. It has a reptilian-like skin, often mottled or scaly, with a head featuring mandibles (tusks) and a distinctive 'dreadlock' like appendages. The face is usually covered by a biomask, which provides vision modes (thermal, infrared, etc.) and serves as a symbol of rank. The body is muscular and covered in armor, netting, and various trophies. The skin color varies f

Symbols — The Predator's iconography includes: the biomask, which often has a laser sight and is designed to look menacing; the shoulder cannon (plasma caster); the wrist blades; the cloaking device; and the practice of taking trophies (skulls, spines) from prey. The Predator's insignia, often seen on their armor, is a stylized skull or a symbol representing their clan.

Domains — Predators are space-faring nomads who travel the galaxy in search of hunting grounds. They are adaptable to various environments, including jungles, cities, and arctic regions. They have a homeworld, named Yautja Prime, which is depicted in some expanded media. They prefer warm, humid climates but can survive in harsh conditions.

Accounts I
The Original Hunt (Predator, 1987)Predator (film)

1980s

A team of elite commandos, led by Dutch Schaefer, is sent to a Central American jungle to rescue hostages. They encounter an invisible alien that hunts them one by one. Dutch eventually defeats the Predator by using its own honor code, setting traps and covering himself in mud to avoid thermal detection. Dutch is the sole survivor and is left with trauma. The Predator's body is destroyed in a self-destruct explosion.

City Hunt (Predator 2, 1990)Predator 2 (film)

1990s

The Predator arrives in Los Angeles during a drug war and begins hunting gang members and police. Lieutenant Mike Harrigan tracks it and eventually confronts it on a spaceship, where he is spared by the Predator's elders, who recognize his worth. Harrigan kills the Predator but is spared by the elders. The Predator's species acknowledges him as a worthy opponent.

The Game Preserve (Predators, 2010)Predators (film)

2010s

A group of humans are dropped onto a jungle planet that serves as a game preserve for the Predators. They must survive against different Predator clans. Royce, a mercenary, leads the group, and they eventually defeat the Predators and escape. Royce and Isabelle escape, but the fate of the other humans is unknown. The planet is revealed to be a hunting ground.

Accounts II
The Upgrade (The Predator, 2018)The Predator (film)

2010s

A Predator crash-lands on Earth, and a group of soldiers and a scientist must stop it. They discover that the Predator was bringing a gift, but a rogue Predator is hunting it. The film introduces the concept of Predators genetically engineering themselves, and the 'Predator Killer' armor. The Predator's ship is destroyed, and the Upgrade is killed. The film ends with a teaser of a larger Predator threat.

The Comanche Hunt (Prey, 2022)Prey (film)

18th century

A young Comanche woman, Naru, must prove herself as a hunter. She encounters a Predator that is hunting in the wilderness. Using her wits and knowledge of the land, she defeats the Predator. Naru earns the respect of her tribe and defeats the Predator, saving her people.

The Predator's name is 'Yautja'. — The name 'Yautja' is not used in any film. It comes from the comics. The films simply refer to it as 'the Predator' or 'the creature'.

The Predator is invincible. — The Predator can be killed by conventional weapons, as shown in the films. It is not invulnerable.

The Predator hunts for sport only. — While the Predator does hunt for sport, it also hunts for trophies and to prove its worth. It is a rite of passage for young Predators.

The Predator is a mindless killer. — The Predator follows a code of honor and often spares unarmed or defenseless prey. It is intelligent and strategic.

Who It Is

The Predator is the violet entity of love — the most complex and contradictory of all the Emotional Entities. It appears as a massive, humanoid creature of violet crystal and light, vaguely masculine in form, with a face that shifts between adoration and fury. The Predator is not love as gentle affection — it is love in its rawest, most primal form: the love that moves mountains, kills gods, and burns down worlds. It is the love of a mother for her child, the romantic passion that drives poetry and war, the possessive obsession that refuses to let go, and the selfless devotion that sacrifices everything. The Star Sapphire Corps draws its power from the Predator. Originally an all-female corps founded by the Zamarons (female offshoots of the Guardians of the Universe), the Star Sapphires wield love as both a weapon (violet energy constructs, love-induced paralysis, teleportation through emotional bonds) and a shield (their crystals heal through the power of love). The Predator's hosts include the Zamarons' queen and many Star Sapphires. During the Blackest Night, the Predator bonded with Wonder Woman, whose capacity for love — romantic, sisterly, and universal — made her an ideal host. As the Predator's avatar, Wonder Woman was able to resurrect the dead through the power of love alone. The Predator represents the ultimate paradox of emotion: love is the most creative force in existence, the spark that builds families, civilizations, and art. But it is also the most destructive — the source of jealousy, obsession, vengeance, and the willingness to burn down the world for one person. The Predator does not distinguish between these faces of love. It simply IS love, in all its terrible, beautiful, infinite complexity.

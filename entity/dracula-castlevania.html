Who It Is

Dracula is a complex and intriguing character, with a rich history and a multitude of interpretations across the Castlevania series. His story serves as a reminder of the dangers of unchecked power and the importance of family and loyalty.

Dracula is typically portrayed as a ruthless and cunning villain, driven by a desire for power and control. He is often shown to be intelligent and strategic, using his magical abilities to outmaneuver his enemies.

Powers
Vampirism

The ability to transform into a vampire, granting Dracula superhuman strength, speed, and agility. He can also use his vampiric powers to drain the life force of his enemies.

Form & Signs

Appearance — A tall, dark-haired, and pale-skinned figure with sharp fangs and piercing eyes, often depicted in a black cape and suit. He can also be seen as a younger version of himself, with a more human-like appearance.

Symbols — The cross, the Belmont crest, and the Vampire Killer whip are all symbols associated with Dracula's defeat. He is also often depicted with a crown or a scepter, symbolizing his power and authority.

Domains — Castlevania, a fictional castle located in Transylvania, serves as Dracula's primary domain. He can also be found in other locations, such as the streets of London or the forests of Eastern Europe.

Accounts I
Castlevania: Lords of ShadowVideo game

Modern

The game follows the story of Gabriel Belmont, a 11th-century holy knight who becomes the Lord of Shadows. He must navigate the world of vampires and demons to save his wife, Sabella, from the clutches of Dracula. The game's story explores the origins of Dracula and the Belmont clan, providing a new perspective on the Castlevania universe.

Accounts II
Castlevania: Symphony of the NightVideo game

Modern

The game follows the story of Alucard, Dracula's son, as he navigates the castle of Castlevania to rescue his father from the clutches of the evil demon, Shaft. The game's story explores the complex relationships between Dracula and his son, as well as the origins of the Belmont clan.

Who It Is

Dunkel Cooper is not the ace who turns the tide at Solomon or the Newtype who hears voices across the void — he is the other kind of survivor in Mobile Suit Gundam, the junior Earth Federation officer who keeps the White Base running while Amuro Ray and Char Aznable rewrite the war around him. His name appears in the margins of episode credits and character lists, a German-dark surname paired with an English tradesman's surname, exactly the kind of hybrid naming Yoshiyuki Tomino gave to the Federation's rank-and-file to signal a future Earth bureaucracy stretched thin by colony and battlefield.

Attested: Insufficient dialogue corpus to establish a canonical personality profile. As a minor officer in the White Base ecosystem, his behavior is constrained by military hierarchy and the ship's chronic understaffing after the Side 7 attack. Federation Officer Training and Shipboard Operations

As a commissioned or warrant officer in the Earth Federation Forces, the character is presumed trained in warship operations, damage control, communications protocol, and chain-of-command discipline under UC 0079 doctrine. On the White Base this would include bridge watchstanding, relaying orders between Captain Bright Noa, helmsman Mirai Yashima, and hangar chiefs.

Limit: No command authority beyond his rank; subject to Bright Noa and higher echelon orders. Effectiveness limited by White Base's chronic personnel shortages, lack of resupply, and reliance on teenage pilots. No Newtype precognition or enhanced spatial awareness attested.

Small Arms and Basic Combat Survival

Standard Federation sidearm (pistol) and shipboard small-arms familiarity for boarding defense, as seen when Ramba Ral's forces board White Base. All crew receive rudimentary combat training after Side 7.

Limit: Not a frontline infantry or ace pilot; would be outmatched by Zeon special forces or mobile suits. No attested hand-to-hand feat.

No Newtype or Augmented Ability

Explicit absence: Unlike Amuro Ray, Sayla Mass, Mirai Yashima, Lalah Sune, and Char Aznable, no source attributes Newtype resonance, precognition, or enhanced piloting intuition to Dunkel Cooper. This absence is itself narratively significant, marking him as part of the 'Oldtype' majority whose war is fought with procedure rather than psychic power.

Limit: Cannot sense enemy intent, cannot pilot psycommu weapons.

The Attack on Side 7 and Escape Aboard White BaseMobile Suit Gundam Episode 1 'Gundam Rising' through Episode 3 'Vote to Attack' (TV), and Film I (1981)

In-universe UC 0079.09.18; production 1979

The Principality of Zeon, led by forces under Char Aznable, attacks the unfinished Federation colony Side 7 (Bunch 1, Green Noa) to destroy Project V — the Federation's prototype mobile suits Gundam, Guncannon, and Guntank. Most of the White Base's regular crew are killed or incapacitated. Civilian survivors including Amuro Ray, Fraw Bow, Hayato Kobayashi, Kai Shiden, and Mirai Yashima are pressed into service under Acting Captain Bright Noa after Captain Paolo Cassius is mortally wounded. Junior officers and remaining enlisted crew must keep the ship operational while Amuro, piloting the RX-78-2 Gundam for the first time, repels Zakus. Dunkel Cooper's attested narrative function, if present in this arc, is as part of this depleted complement — handling damage control, communications relay, or hangar support — rather than as a featured actor. His name appears in downstream character inventories for this period, but no featured dialogue scene is verified in available transcripts. Descent to Earth and the Trek to JaburoMobile Suit Gundam Episodes 5-13 (TV)

In-universe UC 0079.09-10; production 1979

Under continuous pursuit, White Base re-enters Earth's atmosphere and traverses North America, pursued by Zeon Earth Attack Force under Garma Zabi and later Ramba Ral. The crew, still under Bright Noa, must navigate Federation bureaucracy, supply shortages, and civilian-military tension. Kai Shiden deserts at Belfast after becoming disillusioned; Miharu Ratokey, a Zeon-aligned civilian, dies during an Atlantic battle. Junior officers on the bridge and in the hangar are shown managing logistics, sensor watches, and mobile suit maintenance while Amuro, Kai, Hayato, and Ryu Jose rotate in Guncannon, Guntank, and Core Fighter. Dunkel Cooper, as a rostered officer, would be situated in this operational tempo — standing watches, coordinating with Mirai Yashima at the helm and Sayla Mass in communications/combat rotation — but no individual heroic action is attested. Ryu Jose's death and Miharu's death harden the crew; White Base reaches Federation headquarters at Jaburo in the Amazon, revealing the Federation's hidden mobile suit production capacity.

Accounts II
Operation Odessa and the European FrontMobile Suit Gundam Episodes 14-25 (TV)

In-universe UC 0079.11; production 1979

The Earth Federation launches Operation Odessa, its largest terrestrial counteroffensive, to retake the Black Sea mining complex from Zeon forces under M'Quve and others. White Base is assigned to support the operation while also dealing with internal friction — Amuro's desertion and return, Kai's cynicism, and Bright's disciplinary challenges. The battle showcases mass-produced Federation Type 61 tanks and early GM mobile suits against Zeon's Zakus and Doms. As part of the White Base task force, junior officers would be involved in operational coordination, casualty handling, and liaison with Federation command. No primary source attributes a decisive action at Odessa to Dunkel Cooper; his participation is at the level of unit membership, not individual feat. Federation victory at Odessa shifts the terrestrial balance and allows the Federation to begin mass deployment of mobile suits, setting up the later space counteroffensive.

Battle of Solomon — The White Devil at the FortressMobile Suit Gundam Episodes 36-37 (TV), Film II: Soldiers of Sorrow (1981)

In-universe UC 0079.12.24; production 1979-1981

The Federation fleet, including White Base, assaults the Zeon space fortress Solomon. Amuro, now recognized as the 'White Devil' after destroying 14 Rick Doms in this battle according to in-universe lore, confronts Dozle Zabi, who sorties in the massive mobile armor Big Zam. Dozle dies in battle; his daughter Mineva is sent away by his aide. The battle demonstrates the Federation's new numerical advantage with GM units and the Gundam's dominance. For junior officers aboard White Base, Solomon is a high-casualty fleet action requiring damage control under beam weapon fire and coordination with the broader Federation armada. Dunkel Cooper's presence would be as a bridge or operations officer during general quarters; no individual kill or command decision is attested. Fall of Solomon opens the road to Zeon's final fortress A Baoa Qu; Sleggar Law is killed shortly after, affecting Mirai Yashima's arc; Dozle's death fractures Zeon leadership.

Junior officers are tasked with evacuation, lifeboat launch, and final communications. The war ends with Zeon's surrender after the fortress falls. Dunkel Cooper's fate after A Baoa Qu is not documented in located primary dialogue; as with many minor crew, survival is plausible but not explicitly confirmed in a dedicated scene. Dunkel Cooper is a Zeon officer because his first name is German. — Despite the German given name 'Dunkel', all located character lists place him in the Earth Federation Forces, not the Principality of Zeon. Gundam gives both sides Germanic names; Federation names are often mixed European (e.g., Bright Noa, Mirai Yashima, Sayla Mass), while Zeon names are more consistently German (e.g., Dozle Zabi, Ramba Ral). Faction is determined by uniform and ship assignment, not etymology.

Dunkel Cooper is a Newtype or ace pilot like Amuro Ray or Char Aznable. — No primary evidence attributes Newtype abilities, ace status, or a notable kill count to Dunkel Cooper. The 'White Devil' nickname belongs specifically to Amuro Ray after Solomon, and Newtype status is explicitly limited to a handful of characters (Amuro, Char, Lalah Sune, Sayla Mass, Mirai Yashima to a lesser degree). Minor officers are intentionally portrayed as Oldtypes whose competence is procedural, not psychic.

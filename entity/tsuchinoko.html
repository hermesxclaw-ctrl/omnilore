Who It Is

A snake-like cryptid from Japan described as being fat-bodied like a beer bottle, with a short tail and the ability to jump, roll, and even speak. Said to have fangs and venom. Reports exist from across rural Japan, particularly mountainous regions.

Form

2-3 ft long, unusually thick middle section tapering to narrow head and short tail, scales, small fangs, often described with a 'collar' marking

Powers

Jumping/leaping

Double-jointed rolling movement

Vocal mimicry (alleged)

Venomous bite

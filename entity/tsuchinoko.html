Who It Is
Form

A snake-like being, often described as venomous and rolling.

Powers

Venomous bite The tsuchinoko is said to have a venomous bite that can cause harm to humans.

Accounts

During the 1970s, a number of tsuchinoko sightings were reported in Western Japan, leading to a national sensation.

During the Edo Period, a number of venomous, rolling, snake-like yōkai, such as the nozuchi and tsuchi korobi, were recorded in folkloric encyclopedias.

The Approach

In a desolate future where Earth's mightiest warriors have fallen, one young Saiyan carries the weight of a dying world on his shoulders. Armed with a sword and a time machine, Future Trunks defies fate itself, journeying to the past to change a future that refuses to be rewritten.

Content advisory. Contains themes of genocide, apocalyptic destruction, death of major characters, and psychological trauma. Some scenes may be intense for younger viewers.
Identity

Future Trunks

Pronunciation TOH-rahn-koos (Japanese: トランクス); Future Trunks: FYOO-chur TRUHNKS

Known As

Trunks of the FutureMirai no TorankusuTruTrankThe Mysterious BoyThe Lone Warrior

Etymology

The name 'Trunks' (トランクス, Torankusu) is derived from the English word 'trunks' as in swimming trunks, following the naming convention of Bulma's family members being named after undergarments. 'Future' distinguishes this timeline variant from his present-time counterpart.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Akira Toriyama for the Dragon Ball franchise, published by Shueisha and adapted by Toei Animation. The character is a global pop culture icon, particularly prominent in Japanese anime and manga.

Disputed Identifications

There is no major dispute about his identity, but the character's timeline origins have led to confusion among fans regarding the canonicity of various appearances. Some older fans misidentify him as 'Trunks' without the 'Future' prefix, leading to ambiguity.

Description

Appearance

Future Trunks is a tall, muscular young man with spiky lavender hair, which is characteristic of Saiyan heritage. He has blue eyes and often wears a blue jacket with a red shirt underneath, along with dark pants and boots. He carries a longsword on his back in a scabbard. His physical appearance matures over the series, showing signs of the harsh life in his timeline.

Symbols & Iconography

The Capsule Corporation logo is often associated with him, as he is Bulma's son. His sword is a symbol of his resolve and his role as a warrior. The time machine is another iconic symbol, representing his connection to the past and his mission to alter fate.

Behaviour & Personality

Future Trunks is serious, determined, and driven by a sense of duty to protect the innocent. Having grown up in a post-apocalyptic world, he is pragmatic and often somber, but he also shows deep compassion and a willingness to sacrifice himself. He is respectful to his mentors and carries a strong sense of justice. Unlike his present-time counterpart, he is more mature and less playful.

Domains & Habitats

His primary domain is the post-apocalyptic future timeline of Earth, a world ravaged by the Androids. He also spends time in the main timeline of the Dragon Ball series, particularly during the Android and Cell arcs and later during the Goku Black arc.

Version Differences

The present-time Trunks is introduced as a baby and grows up in a peaceful era, becoming a playful and somewhat arrogant child. He is more carefree and enjoys playful fights with his friend Goten. Future Trunks, in contrast, is a hardened warrior from a devastated timeline. The two versions have different power levels and abilities, with Future Trunks having achieved Super Saiyan 2 and a unique form called Super Saiyan Rage, while Present Trunks can access Super Saiyan 3 via fusion.

Powers & Abilities

Super Saiyan

Source Version Future Trunks achieves this form in his early teens after the death of his mentor Gohan. Present Trunks can achieve it at age eight.

Description

A transformation that greatly enhances the user's power, speed, and agility, characterized by golden hair and a fierce aura.

Limitations

The transformation drains energy and requires intense emotion to activate initially.

Contradictions

No major contradictions; the achievement differs between timelines.

Super Saiyan 2

Source Version Future Trunks attains this form during his battle with Babidi and Dabura, while Present Trunks does not achieve it in the main series.

Description

An advanced form of Super Saiyan with even greater power and speed, marked by increased muscle mass and constant electrical sparks.

Limitations

Requires immense emotional control and stamina.

Contradictions

The exact circumstances of Future Trunks' attainment are depicted differently in the manga and anime.

Super Saiyan Rage

Source Version Future Trunks achieves this form during the Goku Black arc, fueled by rage at Zamasu's taunts.

Description

A unique form triggered by intense anger, combining the power of Super Saiyan 2 with a divine aura, granting a significant boost in strength.

Limitations

The form's mechanics are not fully explained; it appears to be a temporary power-up rather than a sustainable transformation.

Contradictions

This form is exclusive to the Dragon Ball Super anime and is not present in the manga adaptation of the same arc.

Bulma is his mother and a brilliant scientist who built the time machine. In the future, she is one of the few survivors and supports Trunks.

In the present timeline, Bulma is alive and well, while in the future she is elderly but still resourceful.

Vegeta is his father, a proud Saiyan prince. In the future, Vegeta died before Trunks was born, so Future Trunks never knew him. In the present timeline, Vegeta is a mentor and rival to Future Trunks, training him during the Cell saga.

Future Trunks respects Vegeta but also has a different relationship compared to Present Trunks.

None.

Contradictions & Disputes

Achievement of Super Saiyan 2

Position A

In the anime, Future Trunks achieves Super Saiyan 2 during his fight with Babidi and Dabura.

Position A — evidence

Dragon Ball Z anime episode 244

Position B

In the manga, it is implied he already had it before that fight.

Position B — evidence

Dragon Ball Super manga chapter 15

Scholarly Consensus

The anime and manga have slight discrepancies, but it is generally accepted that he achieved it during that period.

Super Saiyan Rage

Position A

The form is exclusive to the Dragon Ball Super anime.

Position A — evidence

Dragon Ball Super anime episodes 60-61

Position B

The manga does not include this form.

Position B — evidence

Dragon Ball Super manga chapters 22-23

Scholarly Consensus

The form is considered anime canon, but not manga canon, leading to debates among fans.

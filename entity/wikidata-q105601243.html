Who It Is

In a desolate future where Earth's mightiest warriors have fallen, one young Saiyan carries the weight of a dying world on his shoulders. Armed with a sword and a time machine, Future Trunks defies fate itself, journeying to the past to change a future that refuses to be rewritten.

Future Trunks is serious, determined, and driven by a sense of duty to protect the innocent. Having grown up in a post-apocalyptic world, he is pragmatic and often somber, but he also shows deep compassion and a willingness to sacrifice himself. He is respectful to his mentors and carries a strong sense of justice. Unlike his present-time counterpart, he is more mature and less playful.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Trunks' (トランクス, Torankusu) is derived from the English word 'trunks' as in swimming trunks, following the naming convention of Bulma's family members being named after undergarments. 'Future' distinguishes this timeline variant from his present-time counterpart.

Pronunciation — TOH-rahn-koos (Japanese: トランクス); Future Trunks: FYOO-chur TRUHNKS

Aliases — Trunks of the Future, Mirai no Torankusu, Tru, Trank, The Mysterious Boy, The Lone Warrior

Powers
Super Saiyan

A transformation that greatly enhances the user's power, speed, and agility, characterized by golden hair and a fierce aura.

Limit: The transformation drains energy and requires intense emotion to activate initially.

Super Saiyan 2

An advanced form of Super Saiyan with even greater power and speed, marked by increased muscle mass and constant electrical sparks.

Limit: Requires immense emotional control and stamina.

Super Saiyan Rage

A unique form triggered by intense anger, combining the power of Super Saiyan 2 with a divine aura, granting a significant boost in strength.

Limit: The form's mechanics are not fully explained; it appears to be a temporary power-up rather than a sustainable transformation.

Burning Attack

An energy wave attack that Trunks fires from his hands, often used to finish off enemies.

Limit: Requires ki energy and can be dodged or blocked.

Super Buster Cannon

A powerful energy blast that is a stronger version of the Burning Attack.

Limit: Consumes a large amount of ki.

Sword Skills

Future Trunks is highly proficient with his longsword, using it to cut through enemies and energy attacks.

Limit: Requires physical proximity and can be broken by powerful opponents.

Bukū-jutsu

The ability to fly by manipulating ki.

Limit: Requires ki energy.

Galick Gun

A powerful energy blast technique inherited from his father Vegeta.

Limit: Requires significant ki and charge time.

Final Flash

A devastating energy attack that Vegeta's signature technique, also used by Future Trunks.

Limit: Requires immense energy and can leave the user exhausted.

Spirit Bomb Sword

A technique where Future Trunks gathers energy from all living beings into his sword, creating a massive energy blade capable of cutting through nearly anything.

Limit: Requires the cooperation of all living beings and takes time to charge.

Time Travel

The ability to travel through time using Bulma's time machine.

Limit: The time machine has limited fuel and can only travel to specific points.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Future Trunks is a tall, muscular young man with spiky lavender hair, which is characteristic of Saiyan heritage. He has blue eyes and often wears a blue jacket with a red shirt underneath, along with dark pants and boots. He carries a longsword on his back in a scabbard. His physical appearance matures over the series, showing signs of the harsh life in his timeline.

Symbols — The Capsule Corporation logo is often associated with him, as he is Bulma's son. His sword is a symbol of his resolve and his role as a warrior. The time machine is another iconic symbol, representing his connection to the past and his mission to alter fate.

Domains — His primary domain is the post-apocalyptic future timeline of Earth, a world ravaged by the Androids. He also spends time in the main timeline of the Dragon Ball series, particularly during the Android and Cell arcs and later during the Goku Black arc.

Accounts I
The Mysterious BoyDragon Ball manga chapters 331-333

1991

A mysterious young man appears and effortlessly kills Frieza and King Cold, shocking the Z-fighters. He reveals himself to be Trunks, the son of Vegeta and Bulma, from a future devastated by Androids. Trunks warns Goku of the Androids and gives him a heart medicine, setting the stage for the Android arc.

The Lone WarriorDragon Ball manga chapters 419-420 and TV special 'The History of Trunks'

1993

A side story depicting Future Trunks' tragic past: Goku dies of a heart virus, the Androids kill the Z-fighters, and Gohan trains Trunks before being killed. Trunks awakens as a Super Saiyan and becomes Earth's last hope. Trunks decides to use the time machine to change the past.

Accounts II
The Android and Cell SagaDragon Ball manga chapters 334-420 and Dragon Ball Z episodes 119-194

1991-1993

Future Trunks returns to the past to help fight the Androids and Cell. He trains with Vegeta and achieves Super Saiyan grades, but is killed by Cell. He is revived by the Dragon Balls and returns to his timeline to defeat the Androids and Cell there. Future Trunks' timeline is saved, and he becomes a hero in his own right.

The Goku Black ArcDragon Ball Super anime episodes 47-67 and manga chapters 14-26

2016-2017

Future Trunks returns to the present seeking help against Goku Black, a villain who has taken over his timeline. He fights alongside Goku and Vegeta, eventually achieving Super Saiyan Rage and defeating Fusion Zamasu with the Spirit Bomb Sword. Future Trunks' timeline is ultimately destroyed by Zamasu, but he and the survivors escape to a new timeline.

Who It Is

Candle Cove is a creepypasta written by Kris Straub in 2009, presented as a forum thread where users reminisce about a children's television show called 'Candle Cove' that aired in the early 1970s. The format is a key part of its horror: the story unfolds entirely through forum posts, with users adding fragmented memories that collectively reconstruct the show's content. Candle Cove was a puppet-based program about a young girl named Janice who went on adventures with a cast of pirate characters: the skeletal Pirate Percy (made of 'bits of old ships'), the cowardly Horace Horrible, and a villain called the Skin-Taker. As the forum thread continues, the memories grow darker. One user recalls that the show had no dialogue — only screaming. Another remembers an episode that was entirely static, which their mother told them was 'just a test pattern.' The final, devastating revelation comes when a user mentions asking their mother about Candle Cove — and the mother replies that as a child, the user would sit in front of the television at the same time every day, staring at a screen of static, insisting that 'Candle Cove' was on. The implication is devastating: the show may never have existed as a broadcast signal at all. Something else was reaching these children — something that appeared as a children's show only to them. Candle Cove was adapted as the first season of the Syfy anthology series 'Channel Zero' in 2016, expanding the story into a full narrative while preserving its core creepiness. The original story remains a masterclass in format-native horror — it uses the structure of an internet forum as its delivery mechanism, making the reader feel like they've stumbled into a real, dangerous conversation.

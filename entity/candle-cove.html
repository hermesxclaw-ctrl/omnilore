Who It Is
Form

Candle Cove is a fictional children's television series that appears to have been broadcast on Channel 58 in the early 1970s. The show centered around a girl named Janice and her imaginary adventures with marionette puppets. The series is described as having a nostalgic and eerie atmosphere, with a focus on the psychological horror of a lost media artifact.

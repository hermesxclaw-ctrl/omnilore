Who It Is

Nurgle is the Chaos God of disease, decay, despair, and death — but paradoxically, he is also the most compassionate and 'loving' of the four Great Chaos Gods. Nurgle is not a god of hateful destruction like Khorne or cold manipulation like Tzeentch — he is Grandfather Nurgle, a grotesquely cheerful entity who genuinely loves his followers and wants them to be happy, even as their bodies rot and their minds decay. Nurgle's domain is the cycle of life and death — the inevitable decay that all things experience. He does not create disease to destroy life; he creates disease as a form of 'life' itself, a process that transforms and renews. His 'gifts' are plagues that bring both suffering and a strange, contented numbness. His followers — the Death Guard Chaos Space Marines and Nurgle's daemons — experience no fear of death or pain, because they have already decayed past the point of caring. They feel only Grandfather Nurgle's love, a warm, buzzing acceptance that makes them resilient and terrifyingly content. Nurgle's realm in the Warp is the Garden of Nurgle — a vast, decaying swamp of bubbling cauldrons, rotting vegetation, and Nurgling-infested pools. It is paradoxically beautiful in its fecundity: life thrives in decay, and Nurgle's garden is more alive than any other Chaos God's realm. His daemons include Nurglings (mischievous tiny daemons of disease), Plaguebearers (his accountants, who tally all diseases), and Great Unclean Ones — his Greater Daemons, vast, bloated beings of decay and surprising warmth. Nurgle represents the most human of the Chaos Gods — the acceptance of death, the comfort in hopelessness, the strange peace that comes when you stop fighting and let the decay take you. He is the god who promises that even in the worst plague, you are not alone. Grandfather Nurgle loves you. And his love is a disease from which there is no recovery.

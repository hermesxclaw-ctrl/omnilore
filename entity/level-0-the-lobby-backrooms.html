Who It Is

The entrance level to the Backrooms, an infinite extradimensional space of mono-yellow wallpaper, damp carpet, and fluorescent lighting. It is the first place people 'noclip' into when they glitch out of reality. Level 0 is approximately 600 million square miles of randomly segmented empty rooms and hallways. The only life is the occasional 'Hound' or 'Smiler,' and the constant hum of fluorescent lights that never turn off.

Form

Endless maze of rooms with mono-yellow wallpaper, beige damp carpet, ceiling-mounted fluorescent lights that buzz at exactly 60 Hz, and no windows. The air smells of old carpet and electricity.

Counterparts Across Cultures

Beings of the same nature, imagined by other peoples.

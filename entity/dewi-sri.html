Who It Is

Dewi Sri is the goddess of rice and fertility, the most universally worshipped deity across Java and Bali. According to myth, her body transformed into the first rice plants. Farmers perform rituals before planting and harvesting, offering first grains to her. Small effigies called cili represent her. Her worship survived Islamization through syncretism.

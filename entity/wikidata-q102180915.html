The Approach

In the glittering, perilous depths beneath the mortal world, where the Dungeon's labyrinthine corridors echo with the clash of steel and the roar of monsters, there walks an elf whose name is spoken with a mixture of awe and reverence: Riveria Ljos Alf. She is not merely a mage of prodigious skill; she is a living legend, a figure whose centuries of life have woven her into the very fabric of the adventuring city of Orario. To the inexperienced, she is the 'Nine Hell,' a title that conjures images of devastating fire magic; to those who know her, she is a mentor, a leader, and a woman carrying the weight of a past that shaped her into the paragon she is today.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of combat, violence, and the psychological burdens of a long-lived character. No explicit sexual content.

Etymology

The name 'Riveria' is likely derived from 'river' or 'Riviera,' evoking a sense of flowing elegance and coastal beauty. 'Ljos Alf' appears to be a play on 'Ljósálfar,' the Light Elves of Norse mythology, reflecting her high elf status. In the context of the series, 'Ljos Alf' is her family name, indicating her noble elven lineage.

Cultural Ownership

Riveria Ljos Alf is a fictional character created by Japanese author Fujino Omori for the light novel series 'Is It Wrong to Try to Pick Up Girls in a Dungeon?' (DanMachi). She is part of the 'Famiglia' of the goddess Loki. As a character from a Japanese media franchise, she is owned by SB Creative (publisher) and the associated production committees for anime adaptations.

Disputed Identifications

There is no significant dispute regarding her identity. However, some fans may confuse her with other high elf characters in the series, such as Lefiya Viridis, who is her student. Additionally, the term 'Nine Hell' is sometimes misattributed to other mages, but it is exclusively associated with Riveria.

Description

Appearance

Riveria is a tall, elegant high elf with long, straight blonde hair that reaches her waist, often tied back with a simple ribbon. She has striking green eyes and pointed ears, characteristic of elves. She typically wears a white and green robe with gold trim, signifying her status as a high elf and a member of Loki Familia. She carries a staff, often depicted as a wooden rod with a crystal orb. In the anime, her design is refined, with a mature and composed demeanor.

Symbols & Iconography

Her primary symbol is the staff, representing her role as a mage. The color green is associated with her elven heritage and her connection to nature, though her magic is fire-based. She is also associated with the emblem of Loki Familia, a stylized representation of the trickster god Loki.

Behaviour & Personality

Riveria is calm, wise, and highly disciplined. She serves as the vice-captain of Loki Familia and often acts as a voice of reason. She is deeply protective of her comrades, especially the younger members like Lefiya and Ais Wallenstein. Despite her composed exterior, she carries a heavy burden from her past, including a failed relationship with a human adventurer, which has made her wary of close personal attachments. She is known for her strict but fair teaching style and her immense pride in her elven heritage.

Domains & Habitats

Riveria resides in the city of Orario, within the home of Loki Familia, a large mansion in the city's center. She often ventures into the Dungeon, a massive labyrinth beneath Orario, where she fights monsters and gathers resources. She is also seen in the city's libraries and magical institutions, as she is a scholar of magic.

Version Differences

In the light novels, Riveria's internal monologue provides more depth, revealing her insecurities and past. The anime portrays her as more stoic, with some comedic moments. The manga adaptations vary in art style but generally stay true to her character. The spin-off 'Sword Oratoria' gives her more screen time and explores her relationship with Ais and the rest of the familia.

Powers & Abilities

High-level Fire Magic

Source Version Light novel, anime, manga

Description

Riveria is a master of fire magic, capable of casting powerful spells such as 'Wynn Fimbulvetr' (a massive firestorm) and 'Hagall' (a concentrated blast). She is known as the 'Nine Hell' because she can cast nine high-tier spells, a feat unmatched by most mages.

Limitations

Magic requires incantation time, which can be interrupted. She is vulnerable to magic nullification or anti-magic barriers.

Stories & Myths

Backstory: The Runaway Princess

Source Sword Oratoria light novel, Volume 5 (as inferred from fan summaries)Culture Japanese light novelPeriod Before the main storyline

Summary

Riveria was born as the princess of an elven kingdom, but she chafed under the strict traditions and expectations of her royal family. She fell in love with a human adventurer, which was forbidden, and when her family discovered the relationship, they tried to force her to marry an elf noble. Riveria fled her kingdom, leaving behind her family and her lover, who was killed by her family's agents. She eventually made her way to Orario, where she joined Loki Familia, seeking a new life and a place where she could be free.

Participants

Riveria

Her elven family

Consequences

Riveria became wary of close relationships, fearing the pain of loss. She devoted herself to her duties and to protecting her new family in Loki Familia. Her past remains a source of private sorrow.

Variants

The anime adaptation of Sword Oratoria touches on her past but does not delve as deeply as the light novels.

The Dungeon Expeditions

Source DanMachi main series and Sword OratoriaCulture Japanese light novelPeriod Main storyline

Summary

Riveria participates in numerous expeditions into the Dungeon, fighting powerful monsters and uncovering ancient secrets. She plays a crucial role in the battle against the Behemoth, a massive monster that threatened Orario, and in the expedition to the 59th floor, where the familia faced the Leviathan. Her magic is often the key to turning the tide in these battles.

Consequences

These expeditions strengthen Loki Familia's reputation and deepen the bonds between its members. Riveria's leadership is tested, and she earns the respect of even the most skeptical adventurers.

Mentoring Lefiya

Source Sword OratoriaCulture Japanese light novelPeriod Main storyline

Summary

Riveria takes the young elf Lefiya Viridis under her wing, training her in magic and guiding her as she grows from a shy novice into a confident mage. Their relationship is central to Sword Oratoria, as Riveria sees in Lefiya a reflection of her own younger self and strives to give her the support she never had.

Forbidden Love and Murder

Source Sword Oratoria light novel (inferred from fan summaries)

Description

Riveria's romance with a human was considered taboo by her elven family. When they discovered the relationship, they had her lover killed. This event is a dark chapter in her past, leading to her flight from her homeland and her emotional guardedness.

Scholarly Context

This narrative element explores themes of racial prejudice, class conflict, and the consequences of defying societal norms.

Violence and Combat

Source DanMachi main series

Description

As an adventurer, Riveria engages in lethal combat against monsters and occasionally against other adventurers. She has likely taken lives in the Dungeon, though the series does not dwell on the psychological impact.

Scholarly Context

The series generally treats killing monsters as morally uncomplicated, but Riveria's role as a leader means she bears responsibility for the casualties of her familia.

Light novel volumes of Sword Oratoria contain extensive internal monologue and flashbacks.

The light novels are the primary canon, and the anime is an adaptation that omits some details for pacing.

Riveria's power level

Position A

Riveria is one of the strongest mages in Orario, capable of defeating high-level monsters single-handedly.

Position A — evidence

Her title 'Nine Hell' and her ability to cast nine high-tier spells is a unique feat.

Some fans argue that other mages, such as Lefiya, could surpass her given time.

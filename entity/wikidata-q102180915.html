Who It Is

In the glittering, perilous depths beneath the mortal world, where the Dungeon's labyrinthine corridors echo with the clash of steel and the roar of monsters, there walks an elf whose name is spoken with a mixture of awe and reverence: Riveria Ljos Alf. She is not merely a mage of prodigious skill; she is a living legend, a figure whose centuries of life have woven her into the very fabric of the adventuring city of Orario. To the inexperienced, she is the 'Nine Hell,' a title that conjures images of devastating fire magic; to those who know her, she is a mentor, a leader, and a woman carrying the weight of a past that shaped her into the paragon she is today.

Riveria is calm, wise, and highly disciplined. She serves as the vice-captain of Loki Familia and often acts as a voice of reason. She is deeply protective of her comrades, especially the younger members like Lefiya and Ais Wallenstein. Despite her composed exterior, she carries a heavy burden from her past, including a failed relationship with a human adventurer, which has made her wary of close personal attachments. She is known for her strict but fair teaching style and her immense pride in her elven heritage.

Powers
High-level Fire Magic

Riveria is a master of fire magic, capable of casting powerful spells such as 'Wynn Fimbulvetr' (a massive firestorm) and 'Hagall' (a concentrated blast). She is known as the 'Nine Hell' because she can cast nine high-tier spells, a feat unmatched by most mages.

Limit: Magic requires incantation time, which can be interrupted. She is vulnerable to magic nullification or anti-magic barriers.

Elven Magic Affinity

As a high elf, Riveria has a natural affinity for magic, allowing her to learn and cast spells more quickly than humans. She also has a long lifespan, which gives her centuries to master her craft.

Limit: Her long lifespan also means she experiences loss more acutely, as her human friends age and die.

Healing Magic

Riveria is also skilled in healing magic, able to treat injuries and cure ailments, making her a valuable support in battle.

Limit: Healing magic is not instantaneous for severe wounds and requires concentration.

Leadership and Strategy

As vice-captain of Loki Familia, Riveria is a skilled strategist and leader, able to coordinate large-scale expeditions into the Dungeon.

Limit: Her cautious nature sometimes leads to conflict with more reckless members like Bete Loga.

Accounts I
Backstory: The Runaway PrincessSword Oratoria light novel, Volume 5 (as inferred from fan summaries)

Before the main storyline

Riveria was born as the princess of an elven kingdom, but she chafed under the strict traditions and expectations of her royal family. She fell in love with a human adventurer, which was forbidden, and when her family discovered the relationship, they tried to force her to marry an elf noble. Riveria fled her kingdom, leaving behind her family and her lover, who was killed by her family's agents. She eventually made her way to Orario, where she joined Loki Familia, seeking a new life and a place where she could be free. Riveria became wary of close relationships, fearing the pain of loss. She devoted herself to her duties and to protecting her new family in Loki Familia. Her past remains a source of private sorrow.

Accounts II
The Dungeon ExpeditionsDanMachi main series and Sword Oratoria

Main storyline

Riveria participates in numerous expeditions into the Dungeon, fighting powerful monsters and uncovering ancient secrets. She plays a crucial role in the battle against the Behemoth, a massive monster that threatened Orario, and in the expedition to the 59th floor, where the familia faced the Leviathan. Her magic is often the key to turning the tide in these battles. These expeditions strengthen Loki Familia's reputation and deepen the bonds between its members. Riveria's leadership is tested, and she earns the respect of even the most skeptical adventurers.

Mentoring LefiyaSword Oratoria

Main storyline

Riveria takes the young elf Lefiya Viridis under her wing, training her in magic and guiding her as she grows from a shy novice into a confident mage. Their relationship is central to Sword Oratoria, as Riveria sees in Lefiya a reflection of her own younger self and strives to give her the support she never had. Lefiya becomes a powerful mage and a loyal member of Loki Familia. Riveria finds a sense of purpose in mentoring, and their bond helps heal some of her emotional wounds.

Who It Is

A chimeric beast with the body of a lion, leathery dragon wings, and a scorpion tail dripping with venom. Warhammer manticores are larger and more ferocious than their southern counterparts, often used as mounts by Chaos Lords and Dark Elf dreadlords. They are utterly savage and must be broken through domination.

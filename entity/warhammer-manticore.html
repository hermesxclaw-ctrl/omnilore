Who It Is

Did you know that the Warhammer Manticore, a creature of pure rage and bloodlust, is not just a monster but a symbol of the Dark Elves' cruel mastery over nature? Its venom is so potent that it can kill a dragon, and its roar is said to be a mix of a lion's roar and a human scream, driving men to madness. But what is the true origin of this beast? Is it a natural predator, or a creation of Chaos? Dive into the lore and discover the secrets of the Manticore, a creature that embodies the dark heart of the Warhammer world.

The Warhammer Manticore is a savage, predatory creature driven by an insatiable hunger for flesh and a rage that is almost impossible to quell. It is highly territorial and will attack any intruder, including other Manticores, with extreme violence. Despite its ferocity, it is not mindless; it possesses a cunning intelligence that allows it to stalk prey, set ambushes, and even understand basic commands from a skilled handler. Powers
Scorpion Tail Venom

The Manticore's tail ends in a scorpion-like stinger that delivers a potent venom. In tabletop rules, this is often represented as a poisoned attack that can cause additional wounds or even instant death on a failed toughness test. The venom is described as causing excruciating pain and paralysis, and in the lore, it is harvested by alchemists and witches for use in poisons.

Limit:

Flight

The Manticore's large bat-like wings allow it to fly, making it a highly mobile predator. In combat, it can swoop down on enemies, using its momentum to increase the force of its attacks. This ability also enables it to escape danger quickly and to attack from unexpected angles, making it a fearsome opponent in both open battle and ambush.

Limit:

Terror

The mere presence of a Manticore is enough to instill fear in the hearts of even the bravest warriors. In tabletop rules, this is represented by the 'Terror' special rule, which forces enemy units to take a leadership test or be routed. In the lore, its roar is described as a sound that combines a lion's roar with a human scream, and it is said to cause madness in those who hear it.

Limit:

Rage

Manticores are known for their uncontrollable fury, which can be both a boon and a bane to their handlers. In some editions, they have a rule that forces them to charge the nearest enemy if they are not controlled by a rider, and they may even attack their own side if enraged. This rage is also reflected in their high strength and attack characteristics, making them devastating in close combat.

Accounts I
The Fall of the Beastmaster KharazWarhammer Fantasy Roleplay: The Old World Bestiary (2005)

The Age of Chaos (circa -1500 IC)

Kharaz was a renowned Beastmaster of Naggarond who captured a particularly ferocious Manticore from the mountains of Naggaroth. He spent months training it, using cruel whips and dark magic to subdue it. However, during a public display in the arena of Naggarond, the Manticore broke free of its chains and turned on Kharaz, mauling him to death before being slain by the city's guards. The event was seen as a sign of the Manticore's untamable nature and a warning against hubris. Kharaz's death led to a stricter code of conduct for Beastmasters, requiring them to use more potent binding rituals. It also reinforced the Dark Elves' belief that only the strongest can control such beasts, and that failure is met with death.

Accounts II
The Manticore of the DrakwaldWarhammer Fantasy Battle: Empire Army Book (6th Edition, 2000)

The Time of Three Emperors (circa 1500-2000 IC)

In the depths of the Drakwald Forest, a Manticore terrorized the local villages, killing livestock and travelers. A band of knights from the Order of the Reiksguard was dispatched to hunt it down. After a long and bloody pursuit, they cornered the beast in a ruined watchtower. The knights suffered heavy casualties, but eventually managed to slay the Manticore with a combination of crossbow fire and a well-aimed lance thrust through its heart. The head was taken back to Altdorf as a trophy, where it was displayed in the Imperial Zoo. The slaying of the Manticore became a celebrated tale of knightly valor, and the head was later mounted on a wall in the Imperial Palace. The event also led to increased funding for the Imperial Zoo, which sought to capture more exotic beasts.

The Manticore's BargainWarhammer Fantasy: Dark Elves Army Book (8th Edition, 2013)

The Reign of Malekith (circa -1000 IC to present)

A Dark Elf sorceress named Morathi, the Hag Sorceress, is said to have made a pact with a Manticore in the mountains of Naggaroth. She offered it a portion of her magical power in exchange for its service as a mount. The Manticore, being intelligent, accepted, and it became her steed in several battles. However, the pact was broken when Morathi attempted to use the Manticore as a sacrifice to gain favor with the Chaos Gods. The Manticore, sensing her betrayal, turned on her and fled, but not before cursing her with a mark that would attract other Manticores to attack her. Morathi survived the attack but was scarred, and she has since harbored a deep hatred for Manticores. The curse remains, and it is said that any Manticore she encounters will attack her on sight, making her avoid certain regions of Naggaroth.

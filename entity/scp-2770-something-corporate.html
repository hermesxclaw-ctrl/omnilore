Who It Is

A standard office cubicle that generates an endless stream of meaningless corporate work — spreadsheets with nonsense data, memos in garbled business jargon, emails demanding deliverables with impossible deadlines. The occupant becomes psychologically incapable of leaving, convinced the next report is the last. One D-Class was found dead after 12 days, having worked himself to death on documents containing only the word 'SYNERGY' repeated 50,000 times.

Form

An utterly generic office cubicle: gray fabric walls, beige desk, Dell desktop from ~2005, motivational poster of a cat with 'HANG IN THERE!'

Powers

Compulsive work-loop induction

Endless meaningless task generation

Psychological imprisonment

Fatal overwork without external intervention

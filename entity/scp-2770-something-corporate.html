Who It Is
Form

SCP-2770 is a bright yellow life raft, approximately 2 meters in length, carrying a human male dressed in a British Royal Navy uniform. The uniform shows notable signs of degradation, consistent with the standard British naval dress code circa 19.

Powers

Lure SCP-2770 has the ability to lure individuals into a false sense of security, often through its charismatic personality and persuasive abilities.

Incident Log

SCP-2770 is a Euclid-class object, first discovered in 2022. The entity is a bright yellow life raft, carrying a human male dressed in a British Royal Navy uniform.

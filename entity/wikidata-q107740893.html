Who It Is

For a hundred years, two giants have split a prehistoric island with axe and sword — not out of hatred, but out of honor. Brogy the Red Ogre has laughed through volcanic eruptions, laughed while being eaten, and laughed while dying, because for an Elbaf warrior, the duel itself is the point.

Attested as jovial, boisterous, deeply honor-bound, and simple-hearted. Treats century-long lethal duel as friendly sport; respects honorable combat above all. Laughs openly even when severely wounded or tricked. Hospitable to strangers (offers Straw Hats food/drink) but naive to human deception (easily manipulated by Mr.3's tricks). Values Elbaf pride: 'A warrior's pride' and refusal to question the reason for fighting once honor is invoked. Shows loyalty to Dorry despite trying to kill each other daily. Powers
Giant Physiology and Superhuman Strength

As an Elbaf giant, possesses strength, durability, and lifespan ~3x human scale. Can uproot trees, shatter rock, lift dinosaurs, survive being swallowed by the Island Eater and volcanic eruption. Lifespan ~300 years; age 160 at debut, still in prime.

Limit: Size makes him a large target; vulnerable to human-scale traps, explosives, and wax bindings when not expecting deception. Stamina finite despite giant endurance; century of daily duels shows recovery but not invulnerability.

Master Axeman - Terry Axe

Wields a giant battle axe (Terry) with one hand alongside shield. Technique is powerful cleaving strikes capable of matching Dorry's sword and splitting the sky/ground in combined attack. Century of daily combat implies supreme weapon mastery.

Limit: Requires close range; axe can be stuck or parried by equal giant weapon. No ranged axe technique attested alone.

Shield Mastery and Defense

Uses large round shield for blocking Dorry's sword strikes and dinosaur attacks. Can bash as weapon. Combined with axe forms Elbaf sword-and-board style.

Limit: Shield does not cover full giant body; can be bypassed by trickery (Mr.3's wax).

Hakoku Sovereignty (Hakoku) - Combined Technique

Joint ultimate attack with Dorry: both swing weapons simultaneously to release a massive flying slash/pressure wave that can destroy large targets and was shown to one-shot the Island Eater (giant goldfish) and later to devastate Marine battleships at Egghead. Elbaf's strongest spear technique, also used by Oimo/Kashii and Hajrudin on smaller scale.

Limit: Requires perfect synchronization with Dorry; cannot be performed alone. Long wind-up, leaves users open. Consumes significant stamina.

Honorable Duel Endurance and Warrior's Pride (Social/Willpower Ability)

Possesses immense willpower to sustain a 100-year daily duel to the death without abandoning honor, even when reason for fighting is forgotten. This pride makes him resistant to surrender but vulnerable to exploitation via honor tricks (Mr.3's false surrender/distraction).

Limit: Pride can be manipulated; will continue fighting even when strategically unwise. Does not grant immunity to deception.

Implied Advanced Haki (Post-Timeskip, Unconfirmed)

As a New World giant captain who survived a century and now sails with Red Hair-adjacent Elbaf forces, fans and databooks imply Busoshoku and Kenbunshoku Haki, but manga has not explicitly shown Brogy using named Haki as of Ch.1130. Egghead feat of Hakoku destroying Vice Admiral ships suggests Haki infusion but not labeled.

Limit: Unknown extent; cannot be listed as confirmed power.

Accounts I
The Giant Warrior Pirates and the 100-Year PunishmentOne Piece manga Chapters 116-118, 361 (Louis Arnot exposition), Vivre Card

In-universe: ~100 years before present (c. 900s-1120s Sea Calendar); Published 2000

Brogy and Dorry were co-captains of the Giant Warrior Pirates, the most feared pirate crew of Elbaf, with bounties of 100 million Beli each. After a dispute - a child asked which of them caught a bigger sea king - they began a duel on Little Garden. The World Government captured the crew while captains were absent, sentencing Oimo and Kashii to 50 years forced labor at Enies Lobby under false promise of freeing their captains. Brogy and Dorry, unaware, continued dueling daily for a century, each blow signaled by the island's volcanic eruption, their reason for fighting long forgotten but honor preventing them from stopping. Establishes Elbaf honor culture, explains Oimo/Kashii's later defection, sets up Little Garden as time-capsule island. Bounties frozen by World Government as giants were considered neutralized.

A Hundred Years of Draws on Little GardenOne Piece manga Chapters 115-118

In-universe: 100-year span ending 22 years after Roger's execution (present); Published 2000

Each day for 100 years, Brogy and Dorry duel at the island's center when the volcano erupts. Their weapons clash, they wound each other, then drink and laugh together until next eruption. Both have 1 win? Actually record is 73,466 draws with no victor - every duel ends in draw due to equal strength. They befriend the island's dinosaurs and survive its prehistoric dangers. Their duel is witnessed by Louis Arnot and later becomes legend among pirates as proof that Little Garden's Log Pose takes a year to set. Creates mythic status for both giants; explains why Log Pose trap works; establishes daily ritual that Baroque Works will exploit.

The Baroque Works Trap - Wax, Rum, and the Island EaterOne Piece manga Chapters 118-129

Published 2000

Straw Hat Pirates (Luffy, Vivi, Nami, Zoro, Usopp) arrive at Little Garden. Baroque Works agents Mr.3 (Galdino), Miss Goldenweek, Mr.5 and Miss Valentine arrive to capture Dorry and Brogy for Crocodile's plan (giant bounties). Mr.3 exploits Brogy's honor: he gives Dorry a rigged rum barrel that explodes, wounding Dorry, then tricks Brogy into believing Dorry was dishonored. Brogy, enraged that Dorry would use a trick, is lured into Mr.3's wax trap - his feet encased in candle wax, then entire body turned into wax statue for Crocodile's collection. Simultaneously Dorry is waxed. Both are freed when Luffy, Zoro, and Usopp destroy Mr.3's wax and defeat the agents. To escape, Brogy and Dorry combine to use Hakoku Sovereignty to kill the Island Eater (a giant goldfish that eats islands/ships) that had swallowed Brogy earlier, clearing the way for Straw Hats to leave. Straw Hats escape Little Garden, giants survive and remain on island; Luffy earns giants' respect; Baroque Works defeated locally; Usopp vows to visit Elbaf, foreshadowing future arc.

Accounts II
The Message to Enies Lobby - Oimo and Kashii's ReturnOne Piece manga Chapters 377-379, Cover Story 'Miss Goldenweek's Operation: Meet Baroque Works' Chapters 359-413

Published 2005-2006

At Enies Lobby, giants Oimo and Kashii guard the gates, believing Marines will free Dorry and Brogy if they serve 50 years. Usopp (as Sogeking) reveals the truth: Dorry and Brogy were never imprisoned and have been dueling freely; the World Government lied. Oimo and Kashii defect, helping Straw Hats. Simultaneously, Dorry and Brogy (still on Little Garden) are shown in cover story alive and well, having survived and apparently still dueling, confirming they were not killed by Baroque Works. Later, Oimo and Kashii return to Little Garden or rejoin Elbaf forces. Giant Warrior Pirates' legacy restored; Oimo/Kashii freed from exploitation; sets up eventual reunion of all Elbaf giants. Demonstrates World Government's exploitation of giant honor.

Return of the Giant Warrior Pirates - Egghead and the Great EirikOne Piece manga Chapters 1106, 1111, 1123-1130

Published 2024-2025

Two years after timeskip, Dorry and Brogy have reformed the Giant Warrior Pirates with a new ship, the Great Eirik (a Viking longship with bear figurehead). Sailing under the flag of Elbaf, they answer Luffy's distress call at Egghead Island (Future Island). They shatter Marine blockade, use Hakoku Sovereignty to destroy multiple Marine battleships and even wound Saint Saturn's summoning circle, then rescue Straw Hats, Bonney, and Vegapunk allies. They declare Luffy the Sun God Nika and invite Straw Hats to Elbaf, finally fulfilling Usopp's dream. Brogy is shown as co-captain, laughing 'Gababababa' while fighting Vice Admirals. Giants re-enter world power balance; Elbaf arc officially begins (Chapter 1130+); Brogy elevated from isolated duelist to Yonko-level ally; confirms giants' allegiance to Joy Boy/Nika myth.

Elbaf Reunion and the Sun God FolkloreOne Piece manga Chapters 1130-1135+ (Elbaf Arc opening)

Published 2025

Upon arrival at Elbaf, Brogy and Dorry guide Straw Hats through Warland's forests, reuniting with Hajrudin (new generation Giant Warrior Pirates - Hajrudin was subordinate who idolized them), Gerd, Goldberg, and Prince Loki's storyline. Brogy participates in Elbaf banquet, recounts Louis Arnot's warning about not staying long on Elbaf, and explains Elbaf's worship of Sun God Nika vs world government's demonization. His role shifts from warrior to cultural elder, bridging old Giant Warrior Pirates and new. Completes Brogy's arc from isolated honor-duelist to living cultural bridge; Elbaf's history and Road Poneglyph lore revealed.

Brogy is a filler or non-canon character who dies on Little Garden — Brogy is fully canon manga character introduced in Chapter 115 and survives Little Garden; he returns prominently in Final Saga (Egghead/Elbaf 2024-2025) as co-captain of reformed Giant Warrior Pirates.

Brogy and Dorry are brothers or father/son — They are not related by blood; they are co-captains and rivals/friends, equal in rank. Their color-coded oni epithets (Red/Blue) denote partnership, not family.

Brogy's axe is called 'Terry' in the manga dialogue — Axe name 'Terry' appears only in Vivre Card databook, never spoken in manga chapters. Manga simply shows axe without naming it.

Brogy is based on a real mythological giant named Brogy — Brogy has no direct mythological antecedent; he is original Oda creation inspired by generic Viking/oni archetypes and the Elbaf concept. No historical Brogy exists.

Brogy uses Haki explicitly in Little Garden — No Haki is named or shown for Brogy in Little Garden (pre-Haki system introduction in 2008). Any Haki at Egghead is implied but not explicitly labeled as of cutoff.

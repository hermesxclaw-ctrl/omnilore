Who It Is

The Zanpakutō spirit of Kenpachi Zaraki. Nozarashi is a massive, scarred, brutish warrior with wild hair and a single eye visible, covered in battle wounds and clad in tattered furs. The name means 'weather-beaten one' — a spirit that has survived countless battles through sheer ferocity. Nozarashi represents Kenpachi's pure, unrestrained love of combat and exists solely to fight stronger opponents.

Shikai: Nozarashi (massive axe-blade)

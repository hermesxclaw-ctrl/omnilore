Who It Is
Form

Nozarashi is depicted as a fierce and intimidating entity with a stormy aura. It is often shown as a swirling vortex of rain and wind, with Kenpachi's personality and abilities manifesting as lightning bolts and thunderclaps.

Powers

Storm Summoning Nozarashi has the ability to summon storms and control the weather. This power is often used by Kenpachi to enhance his physical abilities and overwhelm his opponents.

Accounts

In this story, Kenpachi and Nozarashi face off against the Arrancar in a fierce battle. Nozarashi's storm summoning abilities play a crucial role in the outcome of the battle.

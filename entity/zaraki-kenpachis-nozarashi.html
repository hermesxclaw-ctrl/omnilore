Name & Tongue

Etymology — Nozarashi is derived from the Japanese words 'nozora' meaning 'rain' and 'arashi' meaning 'storm'. This etymology reflects the turbulent and unpredictable nature of the entity.

Pronunciation — noh-zah-rah-shee

Aliases — Nozarashi, Kenpachi's Zanpakutō Spirit

Ownership — Zaraki Kenpachi, a fictional character from the Bleach series, is the cultural owner of Nozarashi. As his Zanpakutō Spirit, Nozarashi is deeply connected to Kenpachi's personality

Powers
Storm Summoning

Nozarashi has the ability to summon storms and control the weather. This power is often used by Kenpachi to enhance his physical abilities and overwhelm his opponents.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Nozarashi is depicted as a fierce and intimidating entity with a stormy aura. It is often shown as a swirling vortex of rain and wind, with Kenpachi's personality and abilities manifesting as lightning bolts and thunderclaps.

Symbols — Nozarashi is associated with the symbols of the storm and the rain. These symbols are often used to represent the entity's connection to Kenpachi's personality and abilities.

Domains — Nozarashi is typically found in domains and habitats associated with Kenpachi's personality and abilities, such as the Seireitei's training grounds and the Arrancar's territories.

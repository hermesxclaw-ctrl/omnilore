Who It Is
Form

Supay is often depicted as a dark, imposing figure with a stern expression. He is sometimes shown with a staff or a scepter, symbolizing his power over the underworld.

Powers

Control over the Underworld Supay has the power to control the underworld, governing the spirits of the dead and the cycles of life and death.

Accounts

Supay is said to have descended into the underworld, where he encountered the spirits of the dead. He was tasked with guiding the spirits through the afterlife, ensuring their safe passage.

Supay is said to have battled a serpent, representing the forces of chaos and disorder. He emerged victorious, solidifying his power over the underworld.

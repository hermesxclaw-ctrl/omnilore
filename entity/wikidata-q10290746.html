Who It Is

In the hidden dojos of the Street Fighter universe, few masters command as much fear and respect as Goutetsu, the man who taught both the righteous Ryu and the dark-hearted Akuma. His story is one of power, discipline, and a legacy that shaped the destiny of the World Warrior.

Goutetsu is depicted as a wise and disciplined martial arts master. He embodies the ideal of a sensei who teaches both technique and philosophy. He is stern but caring, genuinely wanting his students to master their inner darkness rather than be consumed by it.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Goutetsu' (剛鉄) is a Japanese name meaning 'strong iron' (剛 = strong, 鉄 = iron). This reflects his formidable martial arts prowess and unyielding spirit.

Pronunciation — GO-teh-tzu

Aliases — Goutetsu

Ownership — Japanese video game franchise, created by Capcom. Goutetsu is a fictional character belonging to the Street Fighter universe.

Powers
Mastery of Ansatsuken

Goutetsu is a grandmaster of Ansatsuken (暗殺拳, 'Assassination Fist'), a fictional martial art style in Street Fighter. This style includes powerful strikes, kicks, and energy-based attacks like the Hadouken.

Limit: As an elderly master, Goutetsu's physical abilities are not shown in combat, but his mastery is implied through his students' abilities.

Control of Satsui no Hado

Goutetsu teaches the Satsui no Hado, a technique that harnesses destructive killing intent. He instructs his students to control it, not be controlled by it.

Limit: The Satsui no Hado is a double-edged sword; if not mastered, it corrupts the user.

Hadouken and Shoryuken

As a master of Ansatsuken, Goutetsu is capable of performing iconic techniques like the Hadouken (energy wave) and Shoryuken (dragon punch). These are passed down to Ryu and Akuma.

Limit: Not directly shown in gameplay.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Goutetsu is depicted as an elderly, bald martial arts master with a long white beard. He wears a traditional karate gi, often with a dark vest. His physique is lean but powerful, reflecting his mastery of martial arts.

Symbols — Goutetsu is often associated with the kanji for 'Hado' (波動, 'surge'), symbolizing the Satsui no Hado (殺意の波動, 'Surge of Murderous Intent') that he taught and sought to control. His dojo is a traditional Japanese setting, with wooden floors and training equipment.

Domains — Goutetsu resides in a remote mountain dojo in Japan, where he trains his students. This dojo is a place of discipline and seclusion, far from the distractions of the world.

Accounts I
The Master of Ryu and AkumaStreet Fighter Alpha 2 and 3

Fictional, modern era

Goutetsu is the sensei of both Ryu and Akuma. He trains them in Ansatsuken, teaching them the Satsui no Hado. While Ryu learns to control his inner darkness, Akuma embraces it, leading to a confrontation where Akuma kills Goutetsu to prove his mastery. Akuma's murder of Goutetsu cements his path as a villain, while Ryu continues to struggle with his own darkness, honoring his master's teachings.

Accounts II
Goutetsu's DeathStreet Fighter Alpha 3 and Street Fighter IV

Fictional, modern era

Akuma challenges Goutetsu to a duel to prove his mastery of the Satsui no Hado. Goutetsu, recognizing Akuma's potential for evil, fights but is ultimately killed. This event is a turning point for Akuma, who fully embraces his dark path. Akuma becomes the powerful antagonist, and Goutetsu's death haunts Ryu, who seeks to avenge his master and avoid the same fate.

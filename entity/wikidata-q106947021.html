Etymology

In Japanese, 'Rei' (れい) can be written with various kanji, including 零 (zero), 霊 (spirit), 麗 (beautiful), 玲 (sound of jewels), and 礼 (thanks). The Portuguese word 'rei' means 'king', derived from Latin 'rex'. The acronym REI has multiple independent origins.

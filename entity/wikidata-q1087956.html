Who It Is

He wakes up inside the body of a virginal fourteen-year-old boy, kisses the girl who freed him, tries to grope her, then incinerates an entire army with a spell named after Judas Priest — and somehow becomes the only thing standing between humanity and the apocalypse. Dark Schneider, the 400-year-old tyrant-wizard of Bastard!!, is heavy metal incarnate: arrogant, lecherous, invincible, and — when the world actually needs him — terrifyingly heroic.

Attested core traits consistent across all versions: supreme arrogance and narcissism, obsessive lechery and sexual harassment played for comedy, theatrical speech, love of showing off, and absolute confidence in his magical superiority. He declares himself the greatest wizard, demands worship from women, and attempts to grope or coerce Yoko and other female characters immediately upon revival — behavior that is narratively framed as comedic but is diegetically non-consensual. Contrastingly, he displays genuine loyalty and paternal affection toward his adopted children Arshes Nei and Kall-S

Etymology — Named directly after German heavy metal vocalist Udo Dirkschneider (Accept, U.D.O.). Hagiwara has stated in interviews and author comments that nearly all major characters, places, spells and kingdoms in Bastard!! are named after heavy metal/hard rock bands and musicians he admires. Powers
Black Magic Mastery (Ancient Magic)

Master of all black magic in the Bastard!! world, capable of city-destroying spells without incantation once learned. His repertoire is the largest of any human wizard, learned during his original 400-year lifespan and retained after reincarnation.

Limit: Requires magical power (mana) and concentration; sealed when in Lucien form until kiss trigger. Early series shows fatigue after large spells; later Dispel Bound era largely removes stamina limits. Cannot use white/healing magic naturally.

Venom, Judas Priest, Black Sabbath and other Band-Name Spells

Signature attack spells: Venom (creates searing explosive flame), Judas Priest (summons multiple laser-like light spears that atomize targets), Black Sabbath (gravity/black hole), Helloween, Megadeth, etc. Each named after a metal band and visualized as distinct elemental/energy effect. Judas Priest is his most iconic, capable of killing thousands and later harming angels/demons.

Limit: Judas Priest requires precise targeting; can be blocked by equivalent Dispel Bound or angelic shields. Venom less effective against fire-resistant demons.

Dispel Bound (Disperu Baundo)

Ultimate defensive magic introduced in mid-series (Anthrasax arc onward). Concentric layers of 10+ simultaneous absolute barrier spells (e.g., Deflect, Counter, Eternal) that automatically nullify, reflect or absorb any hostile magic, physical attack or reality-warping. Makes conventional attacks meaningless; only another Dispel Bound user or angelic-level power can break it. Visualized as glowing mandala shields around body.

Limit: Extremely mana-intensive to maintain; can be broken by superior Dispel Bound layering or by seraphim (Uriel, Raphael) with higher divine power. Requires knowledge to cast — Dark Schneider learns it after studying Anthrasax/angelic magic.

Immortality via Reincarnation / Soul Transfer

400 years before present, Dark Schneider transferred his soul into the infant Lucien Renlen to escape death at the hands of Lars Ul Metallicana. Spell allows him to resurrect upon virgin's kiss (Yoko's). Later, as Adam/Lucifer vessel, he achieves true resurrection even after bodily destruction, regenerating from soul alone.

Limit: Requires prepared vessel and trigger condition; if Lucien's body is destroyed before trigger, revival fails. Later angelic erasure can target soul directly.

Dragon Knight Form - Majin / Fallen Angel Augmentation

After absorbing Anthrasax's power and later Satan/Lucifer's, Dark Schneider can transform into Majin (demon god) and Fallen Angel forms, gaining wings (black, later 6+), horns, and power to fight Seraphim (Uriel, Gabriel) on equal terms. In this state he can destroy continents, manipulate space-time, and survive Hell's environment. Final form approaches Lucifer's power level.

Limit: Transformation strains human soul; prolonged use risks complete possession by Satan/Lucifer. Requires emotional trigger (protecting Yoko). Power still below God (the Creator) and Conlon's ultimate form.

Swordsmanship and Physical Combat

Despite being a wizard, possesses superhuman strength, speed and sword skill — able to duel Lars Ul Metallicana (Dragon Knight) and Gara (ninja master) in melee. Can wield the Dragon Knight's sword and later angelic weapons.

Limit: Prefers magic; physical combat is secondary and less effective against Dispel Bound foes.

Accounts I
The Virgin's Kiss — Revival in MetallicanaBastard!! manga vol.1 ch.1-4; OVA ep.1; ONA S1 ep.1-2

In-universe: Present (400 years after Old World fall); publication 1988

The Kingdom of Metallicana is besieged by the Dark Rebel Army led by Dark Schneider's former Four Divine Kings, who seek to break the four seals and resurrect Anthrasax. High Priest Geo Noto Soto orders his daughter Tia Noto Yoko to awaken the sealed wizard. Yoko kisses 15-year-old Lucien Renlen — the reincarnated Dark Schneider — triggering his adult form. Dark Schneider immediately harasses Yoko, then annihilates the invading army with Venom and other spells, but spares his former allies, setting up his ambiguous allegiance: tyrant turned reluctant protector. Establishes core premise: Dark Schneider as sealed weapon, Lucien/Yoko dynamic, and the seal/Anthrasax quest that drives first half of series. Introduces comedic ecchi + apocalyptic stakes tone.

Thunder Empress Arshes Nei — The Adopted Daughter's WarBastard!! manga vols 2-4; OVA eps 2-4; ONA S1 eps 4-8

Publication 1988-1990; in-universe days after revival

Arshes Nei, Dark Schneider's adopted daughter and lover, Thunder Empress of the Dark Rebel Army, attacks Metallicana with an army of dragons and dark elves. Dark Schneider confronts her, revealing their past: he found the half-dark-elf child, raised her, trained her, and later took her as lover — a relationship presented with both paternal and sexual dimensions. After a brutal magical duel (Judas Priest vs. Arshes' lightning), Dark Schneider breaks her curse-induced loyalty to Abigail and redeems her. She defects, becoming his ally, but is later cursed into a zombie-like state by Abigail. Defines Dark Schneider's capacity for love and redemption; establishes his Shitennō as tragic family rather than mere villains. Arshes remains his most loyal companion thereafter. Complete Edition Vol.2 adds explicit sexual flashback, heavily censored in English.

The Fall of Abigail and the Resurrection of AnthrasaxBastard!! manga vols 6-12; OVA eps 5-6 (partial); ONA S1 eps 9-13 / S2 eps 1-3

Publication 1990-1995; in-universe weeks after Arshes arc

Sorcerer Abigail, another of the Four Divine Kings, manipulates Kall-Su and uses Arshes Nei as sacrifice to break the final seal. Dark Schneider, Gara, Lars Ul Metallicana (Dragon Knight prince) and allies battle Abigail's demons. Despite victory over Abigail — Dark Schneider kills him with Judas Priest after Abigail becomes a demon god — the seal breaks and Anthrasax, the God of Destruction, awakens. Anthrasax, revealed as a bio-weapon angel from the Old World, annihilates most of the continent. Dark Schneider, unable to stop it initially, is mortally wounded and later merges with Anthrasax's power, foreshadowing his later angelic/demonic nature. Lars in Dragon Knight armor fights Anthrasax to a standstill. Destruction of the four kingdoms; world enters apocalyptic phase. Reveals Anthrasax is not a demon but a fallen angel-weapon created by God. Dark Schneider's power escalates to continental scale; sets up Hell Arc. OVA ends here on cliffhanger, never adapting aftermath.

Accounts II
The Ice King Kall-Su — Brother Against BrotherBastard!! manga vols 5-9 (interwoven); ONA S1-S2

Publication 1991-1996

Kall-Su, Dark Schneider's adopted son and second Shitennō, having been manipulated by Abigail, declares himself king after Anthrasax's fall and freezes entire kingdoms with his ice magic. Dark Schneider confronts him in a duel that mirrors the Arshes fight: Kall-Su is revealed as a lonely child with immense ice powers whom Dark Schneider adopted and trained, but whose inferiority complex drove him to rebellion. Dark Schneider defeats him not by killing but by shattering his Dispel Bound and showing paternal pride, redeeming him. Kall-Su later becomes crucial ally against angels. Completes redemption arc of the Shitennō family; demonstrates Dark Schneider's growth from tyrant to flawed father figure. Establishes Dispel Bound as central power system.

War in Hell — Against the SeraphimBastard!! manga vols 13-27 (Hell Arc, 2001-2010 hiatus); ONA S2 eps 10-15 (partial)

Publication 2001-2010; in-universe months after Anthrasax defeat, in Hell/Heaven

After Anthrasax's defeat, the Seraphim (Uriel, Raphael, Gabriel, Michael) descend to purge humanity. It is revealed that the Old World was a hyper-advanced scientific civilization (resembling modern Earth) destroyed by God via Anthrasax for its sins, and that Dark Schneider is the reincarnation of Adam, the first man, created to be Lucifer's vessel. Dark Schneider, now with Dispel Bound mastered, travels to Hell, allies with Satan/Lucifer and demons, and battles Uriel (who has become a Fallen Angel after absorbing human hatred). He attains Majin and Fallen Angel forms, fighting Uriel to a standstill and later confronting Raphael and the heavenly host. Yoko's love is revealed as the key to keeping his human heart from being consumed by Lucifer. The manga enters hiatus during his battle with Uriel/Conlon, with storyboards for continuation mentioned by Hagiwara in 2023 interview. Cosmological retcon: Bastard!! world is post-apocalyptic far-future Earth; magic is lost science; angels are bio-weapons. Lars Ul Metallicana and the Dragon Knight — The Eternal RivalBastard!! manga vols 3, 10-12, 18-20; OVA ep.4-6; ONA S1-S2

Publication 1989-2004

Lars Ul Metallicana, prince of Metallicana and bearer of the Dragon Knight (Dragon Knight Lucifer) armor — a sentient mecha-weapon from the Old World — is Dark Schneider's self-declared rival and the only man who ever killed him (400 years ago). Their duel is both physical and ideological: Lars represents chivalric honor vs. Dark Schneider's hedonistic tyranny. After Anthrasax, Lars becomes uneasy ally, and later, when Dark Schneider is thought dead, Lars protects Yoko. Their final collaboration sees them fighting side-by-side against angels, with Lars acknowledging Dark Schneider as the only hope for humanity despite his depravity. Provides moral mirror for Dark Schneider; Dragon Knight lore explains Old World technology and the true nature of 'magic' and 'gods.' Their rivalry is central to fandom and to Hagiwara's theme of hero vs. antihero.

Dark Schneider is named after a German military title or a generic 'dark wizard' trope. — He is specifically named after Udo Dirkschneider, lead singer of Accept and U.D.O. Hagiwara is a self-declared heavy metal fan and named nearly every major character/place/spell after bands he loves (Metallica, Judas Priest, Anthrax, Helloween, etc.). The name is a pop-culture homage, not historical.

Dark Schneider is the main villain of Bastard!! — He is the protagonist/antihero. He was the villain 400 years ago as leader of Dark Rebel Army, but the present story follows him as the (reluctant, lecherous) hero who protects Metallicana and later humanity against Anthrasax and angels. His morality is chaotic good/neutral, not evil.

Anthrasax is a demon god from ancient mythology. — In Bastard!! canon, Anthrasax is a bio-weapon angel created by God to destroy the hyper-advanced Old World (which is actually futuristic Earth). It is science-fiction, not mythological demon — revealed in Hell Arc (vols 18+). Early translations presenting it as generic demon are incomplete.

Bastard!! is finished and Dark Schneider's story has a conclusion. — Manga has been on hiatus since May 19, 2010 (Ultra Jump) at 27 volumes, mid-Hell Arc battle with Uriel/angels. Hagiwara stated in 2023 interview he is working on storyboards but feels 'something is not working' and is also developing century-past prequel ideas in notebooks. No final chapter or ending exists as of 2026.

Dispel Bound and Majin forms were always part of Dark Schneider's power set. — Dispel Bound and angelic/demonic transformations were introduced more than a decade into serialization (Ultra Jump era, vol.13+). Early Dark Schneider (1988-1995) had no such ability and was vulnerable to conventional magic/swords. Power escalation is a later retcon/expansion, not original design.

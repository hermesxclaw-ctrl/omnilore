Who It Is

Ticci Toby is one of the most popular 'proxy' characters in the Slender Man mythos — humans who have been driven insane by Slender Man's influence and serve as his agents. Toby Rogers was a teenager with a troubled home life: an abusive father, a mother who didn't protect him, and a diagnosis of Tourette's syndrome that made him a target for bullying. His tics — neck jerking, arm twitches, verbal outbursts — are a defining characteristic. After a particularly bad incident at home, Toby ran away into the woods, where he encountered Slender Man. The encounter broke his mind and transformed him into a proxy: his empathy was suppressed, his pain tolerance became inhuman, and he was given a pair of hatchets that he wields with unnerving skill. Unlike many other proxies, Toby retains elements of his original personality — he occasionally shows flashes of remorse, and his Tourette's tics persist even in his proxy state, making him a uniquely tragic figure. In creepypasta lore, Ticci Toby often works alongside another proxy, Clockwork (Natalie Ouellette), and appears as a recurring antagonist in Slender Man ARGs and webseries. His popularity stems from the juxtaposition of his monstrous actions with his sympathetic backstory — he is both victim and perpetrator, a mentally ill child twisted into a weapon by an incomprehensible entity. The character also notable for being one of the few creepypasta figures to explicitly represent a real neurological condition, though the portrayal has been criticized by some for potentially stigmatizing Tourette's syndrome. Toby's signature accessories — orange-tinted goggles, a face mask or bandana, and dual hatchets — make him immediately recognizable in cosplay and fan art.

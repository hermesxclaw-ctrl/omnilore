Who It Is

The Daedric Prince of Plots, Deceit, Cruelty, Conspiracy, and Treason. Boethiah is associated with murder, assassination, and the unlawful overthrow of authority. She is the original god-ancestor of the Dunmer, guiding them to Morrowind. Her realm is Attribution's Share, a plane of eternal competition and betrayal. Artifacts include the Ebony Mail and Goldbrand.

Who It Is

Boethiah is known as the Daedric Prince of Plots, Deceit, and Treachery. They delight in manipulation, betrayal, and the overthrow of tyrants. They value strength, cunning, and ambition. Boethiah often tests mortals by forcing them to commit acts of betrayal or violence.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name Boethiah is derived from the Dunmeri language, possibly meaning 'plot' or 'deception'. It is also associated with the Chimeri concept of 'Boethra'.

Pronunciation — boh-EE-thee-ah

Aliases — Boethia, Boethiah the Deceiver, Lord of Plots, Prince of Plots

Ownership — Primarily associated with the Dunmer (Dark Elves) of Morrowind, but also recognized by other races in Tamriel.

Boethiah can create illusions and deceive even the most perceptive individuals.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Boethiah is typically depicted as a tall, androgynous figure clad in black and gold armor, often with a regal and menacing demeanor. In some depictions, they appear as a warrior or a serpent-like being. In Skyrim, Boethiah appears as a Dunmer-like figure with dark skin and glowing eyes, wielding a sword.

Symbols — Common symbols include a stylized sunburst or a three-pronged symbol, often associated with plots and deception. The color scheme is usually black and gold.

Domains — Boethiah's realm of Oblivion is called Attribution's Share, a place of endless plotting and political intrigue. They are also associated with the concepts of conspiracy, murder, and the struggle for power.

Accounts I
The AnticipationsThe Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind

First Era

Boethiah is one of the three 'Anticipations' of the Tribunal, specifically anticipating Almalexia. The Chimer (ancestors of the Dunmer) were taught by Boethiah to reject the Aldmeri gods and follow a path of strength and self-reliance. The Chimer became the Dunmer, and Trinimac was transformed into Malacath.

Accounts II
Boethiah's CallingThe Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim

Fourth Era

The Dragonborn is tasked with killing a member of Boethiah's cult and then betraying a follower to prove their worth. After the betrayal, Boethiah reveals a champion to fight, and upon victory, the Dragonborn receives the Ebony Mail. The Dragonborn gains a powerful artifact and Boethiah's favor.

The Trials of BoethiahThe Elder Scrolls Online

Second Era

In Deshaan, the Vestige assists a Dunmer in proving their loyalty to Boethiah by undergoing a series of trials, including defeating a false champion and exposing a traitor. The Dunmer gains Boethiah's favor and a position of power.

Who It Is

The Daedric Prince of Plots, Deceit, Cruelty, Conspiracy, and Treason. Boethiah is associated with murder, assassination, and the unlawful overthrow of authority. She is the original god-ancestor of the Dunmer, guiding them to Morrowind. Her realm is Attribution's Share, a plane of eternal competition and betrayal. Artifacts include the Ebony Mail and Goldbrand.

Identity

Boethiah

Pronunciation boh-EE-thee-ah

Known As

BoethiaBoethiah the DeceiverLord of PlotsPrince of Plots

Etymology

The name Boethiah is derived from the Dunmeri language, possibly meaning 'plot' or 'deception'. It is also associated with the Chimeri concept of 'Boethra'.

Cultural Ownership

Primarily associated with the Dunmer (Dark Elves) of Morrowind, but also recognized by other races in Tamriel.

Disputed Identifications

Some scholars equate Boethiah with the Aldmeri god Trinimac after his transformation into Malacath, but this is disputed. Others see parallels with the real-world figure of Loki due to trickster attributes.

Description

Appearance

Boethiah is typically depicted as a tall, androgynous figure clad in black and gold armor, often with a regal and menacing demeanor. In some depictions, they appear as a warrior or a serpent-like being. In Skyrim, Boethiah appears as a Dunmer-like figure with dark skin and glowing eyes, wielding a sword.

Symbols & Iconography

Common symbols include a stylized sunburst or a three-pronged symbol, often associated with plots and deception. The color scheme is usually black and gold.

Behaviour & Personality

Boethiah is known as the Daedric Prince of Plots, Deceit, and Treachery. They delight in manipulation, betrayal, and the overthrow of tyrants. They value strength, cunning, and ambition. Boethiah often tests mortals by forcing them to commit acts of betrayal or violence.

Domains & Habitats

Boethiah's realm of Oblivion is called Attribution's Share, a place of endless plotting and political intrigue. They are also associated with the concepts of conspiracy, murder, and the struggle for power.

Version Differences

In different games, Boethiah's portrayal varies slightly. In Morrowind, they are more focused on their role as the Anticipation of the Tribunal god Almalexia. In Skyrim, they are presented as a more straightforward test of loyalty and murder. In ESO, they have a more prominent role in the political machinations of the Dunmer.

The Anticipations

Source The Elder Scrolls III: MorrowindCulture DunmerPeriod First Era

Summary

Boethiah is one of the three 'Anticipations' of the Tribunal, specifically anticipating Almalexia. The Chimer (ancestors of the Dunmer) were taught by Boethiah to reject the Aldmeri gods and follow a path of strength and self-reliance.

Participants

Boethiah

Chimer

Trinimac

Consequences

The Chimer became the Dunmer, and Trinimac was transformed into Malacath.

Variants

Some sources say Boethiah 'ate' Trinimac, while others say they 'tricked' him.

Contradictions & Disputes

Transformation of Trinimac

Position A

Boethiah physically consumed and transformed Trinimac into Malacath.

Position A — evidence

In-game texts like 'The Anticipations' describe a literal eating.

Position B

The transformation was metaphorical, representing the Chimer's rejection of Aldmeri values.

Position B — evidence

Some sources suggest it was a symbolic act.

Scholarly Consensus

The literal interpretation is more common in lore, but the metaphorical reading is supported by some scholars.

Boethiah's Gender

Position A

Boethiah is female.

Position A — evidence

Some in-game texts use female pronouns.

Position B

Boethiah is male.

Position B — evidence

Other texts use male pronouns.

Scholarly Consensus

Daedric Princes are often genderless or able to change form, so both interpretations are valid.

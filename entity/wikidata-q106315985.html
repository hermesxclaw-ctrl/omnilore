Who It Is

Minami Toba is the laid-back substitute teacher who stumbles into the role of advisor for the Outdoor Activities Circle, trading a quiet life for a gaggle of enthusiastic campers—and a steady supply of sake.

Minami is laid-back, easygoing, and somewhat aloof. She prefers quiet, uncrowded activities and clubs. She has a fondness for drinking alcohol, especially sake, and is often shown drinking while camping. She is kind and supportive of her students, though she can be a bit clueless at times. She has a younger sister, Ryoko, with whom she often camps.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Minami is an adult woman with long, dark hair often tied in a ponytail. She has a relaxed, casual style, typically wearing a tracksuit or casual outdoor clothing. She is often depicted with a bottle of alcohol or a cup of sake.

Symbols — Her association with alcohol and her Suzuki Hustler car are notable symbols. She is also often seen with a warm, slightly tipsy expression.

Domains — She is a substitute teacher at Motosu High School, where she acts as advisor to the Outdoor Activities Circle. She enjoys camping at various sites in Yamanashi and other regions, often with her sister. She drives a Suzuki Hustler.

First Meeting at CampLaid-Back Camp anime Season 1

Nadeshiko and Rin encounter Minami while camping. She is with her sister, and they share a meal and conversation. Minami's relaxed demeanor and drinking habits are introduced. The girls learn about Minami and later discover she is their substitute teacher.

Becoming AdvisorLaid-Back Camp anime Season 1

2018

Chiaki and Aoi seek an advisor for their Outdoor Activities Club. They approach Minami, who accepts because it seems low-effort. She becomes the club's advisor. The club gains official status, allowing them to camp more freely.

Who It Is

Imagine a doctor who has found the cure for death itself — and the cure is you, dead on the floor. SCP-049 is not a monster that hates you; it's a physician who loves you too much to let you live. Clad in the beak of a medieval plague doctor, it walks the halls of the Foundation with the calm certainty of a saint, its only goal to 'cure' the sickness it sees in every human soul. Its touch is a mercy, its reanimated servants a testament to its 'success'. It is one of the most iconic and chilling figures in internet horror, a being whose compassion is indistinguishable from murder.

SCP-049 is highly intelligent, articulate, and speaks in a calm, measured tone, often using archaic or formal language. It is obsessively fixated on what it calls the 'Pestilence' — a concept it believes is a fatal disease afflicting all of humanity. It views itself as a healer, and its primary 'treatment' is to 'cure' subjects by killing them and then reanimating their corpses as 'instances of SCP-049-1'. Name & Tongue

Etymology — Designation 'SCP-049' follows the Foundation's sequential cataloging system. The entity's self-identified name is unknown, but it refers to itself as a 'doctor' and its goal as a 'cure'. The '049' designation is purely administrative, with no known in-universe meaning.

Pronunciation — Ess-Cee-Pee Zero-Four-Nine. The entity itself is typically referred to as 'SCP-049' or 'the Plague Doctor'.

Aliases — The Plague Doctor, The Cure, The Doctor, SCP-049-1 (instances)

Ownership — The SCP Foundation (collaborative, wiki-based creative commons project). The character was created by user 'Gabriel Jade' and first posted to the SCP Wiki in 2013.

Powers
The 'Cure' (Lethal Touch)

SCP-049's primary anomalous ability. A direct touch on bare skin causes the subject to die almost instantly, with all bodily functions ceasing. The cause of death is attributed to an unknown agent that shuts down the body's systems.

Limit: The subject must be touched on bare skin. The effect is not instantaneous if the subject is wearing protective gear. The 'Pestilence' it is 'curing' is not a real, identifiable disease.

Reanimation (Creation of SCP-049-1 instances)

After a subject is 'cured', SCP-049 can reanimate the corpse, creating an instance of SCP-049-1. These instances are shambling, zombie-like, and obey SCP-049's commands. They are driven by a compulsion to serve the Doctor.

Limit: The reanimated corpse is not intelligent and lacks free will. The process is a perversion of life, not a true resurrection. The instance's body continues to decay.

Enhanced Intelligence and Charisma

SCP-049 is highly intelligent, with a deep (if misguided) understanding of medicine and anatomy. It is also highly persuasive, able to calmly argue its case and make its actions seem logical, even to trained personnel.

Limit: Its intelligence is filtered through its delusional worldview. It cannot be reasoned with about the nature of the 'Pestilence'.

Form & Signs

Appearance — SCP-049 appears as a humanoid figure of average height, clad in a thick, dark robe and a distinctive white, beaked plague doctor mask. The mask is its most prominent feature, with dark, hollow eye sockets and a long, curved beak. Its hands are covered in thick leather gloves. The entity's true face, if any, is never seen. Its movements are described as deliberate and graceful, with a calm, unnerving demeanor.

Symbols — The plague doctor mask and dark robe are its primary symbols, evoking the historical era of the Black Death and the fear of pestilence. The act of 'touching' a subject to inflict the 'cure' is a key symbolic action.

Domains — Contained within a standard humanoid containment chamber at Site-19. Its conceptual 'domain' is the sickness and mortality of the human condition, which it seeks to 'cure'.

Accounts I
Initial ContainmentSCP-049 main article

Unknown (pre-2013 in-universe)

SCP-049 was discovered in a small town in France, where it had been 'curing' the local populace. Foundation agents, responding to reports of a strange 'plague doctor', arrived and found the town's population either dead or reanimated. SCP-049 was apprehended with relative ease, seemingly willing to be contained, and was taken to Site-19. SCP-049 was classified as Euclid and contained. The incident established its pattern of behavior: it seeks out populations it believes are afflicted with the 'Pestilence' and attempts to 'cure' them.

Accounts II
Interview Log 049-TSCP-049 main article

Post-containment

In a series of interviews, SCP-049 attempts to explain its mission to Foundation researchers. It describes the 'Pestilence' as a pervasive sickness of the soul and body, and argues that its 'cure' is a mercy. It expresses frustration that the Foundation does not understand its work and views its containment as a hindrance to its mission. The interviews provide insight into SCP-049's psychology, confirming its delusional but sincere belief in its role as a healer. It also demonstrates its intelligence and persuasive abilities.

The 'Cure' of D-9341SCP - Containment Breach (video game)

2013 (game release)

In the video game SCP - Containment Breach, the player character, D-9341, can encounter SCP-049. If the player gets close, SCP-049 will attempt to 'cure' them, resulting in a game-over screen. This is a direct, interactive adaptation of the entity's core ability. This adaptation cemented SCP-049's status as a popular and terrifying icon in the SCP fandom and in the broader horror gaming community.

SCP-049 is a zombie or a lich. — It is not undead. It appears to be a living, thinking entity. It creates zombies (SCP-049-1 instances), but is not one itself. Its powers are more akin to a necromancer or a cursed healer.

SCP-049 is purely evil. — It is more accurately described as 'Lawful Evil' or even 'Lawful Neutral'. It believes it is performing a necessary, merciful act. It is not sadistic; it is sincerely trying to 'heal' humanity. Its evil stems from its horrific methods and its complete disregard for individual life in service of its perceived greater good.

The 'Pestilence' is a real disease that SCP-049 can cure. — There is no evidence for the 'Pestilence' as a real, physical ailment. It is a subjective, possibly delusional, concept held by SCP-049. Its 'cure' is lethal to any subject.

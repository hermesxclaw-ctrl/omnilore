Archive Dossier — Identity

SCP-049

Pronunciation Ess-Cee-Pee Zero-Four-Nine. The entity itself is typically referred to as 'SCP-049' or 'the Plague Doctor'.

Known As

The Plague DoctorThe CureThe DoctorSCP-049-1 (instances)

Etymology

Designation 'SCP-049' follows the Foundation's sequential cataloging system. The entity's self-identified name is unknown, but it refers to itself as a 'doctor' and its goal as a 'cure'. The '049' designation is purely administrative, with no known in-universe meaning.

Cultural Ownership

The SCP Foundation (collaborative, wiki-based creative commons project). The character was created by user 'Gabriel Jade' and first posted to the SCP Wiki in 2013.

Disputed Identifications

The entity's true nature (human, anomalous entity, or something else) is deliberately left ambiguous in the source material. The Foundation's own files offer multiple hypotheses, none confirmed. No real-world folklore figure is directly equivalent, though it draws on the iconography of the historical plague doctor.

Archive Dossier — Approach

Imagine a doctor who has found the cure for death itself — and the cure is you, dead on the floor. SCP-049 is not a monster that hates you; it's a physician who loves you too much to let you live. Clad in the beak of a medieval plague doctor, it walks the halls of the Foundation with the calm certainty of a saint, its only goal to 'cure' the sickness it sees in every human soul. Its touch is a mercy, its reanimated servants a testament to its 'success'. It is one of the most iconic and chilling figures in internet horror, a being whose compassion is indistinguishable from murder.

Do not be afraid. I am the cure. Your affliction — the Pestilence — I see it inside you. Allow me to relieve your suffering.

Who It Is

SCP-049 is a humanoid entity resembling a 15th-16th century European plague doctor, standing approximately 1.9 meters tall. It wears the traditional long black robes and a ceramic beaked mask typical of plague-era physicians. The robes appear to be part of its body, having grown from within rather than being worn. SCP-049 is sentient, articulate, and polite in conversation, speaking medieval French and modern English. Its central anomalous property is its ability to induce cardiac arrest in living organisms through direct physical contact — a touch that SCP-049 claims is its method of 'curing' them of something it calls 'the Pestilence.' Despite extensive Foundation research, no clear definition of 'the Pestilence' has ever been established; SCP-049 insists it is a universal affliction, though it seems to target humans more aggressively. After killing a subject, SCP-049 performs crude surgical procedures using a black leather doctor's bag it produces from its robes, containing scalpels, needles, thread, and vials of unknown substances. The result is invariably a reanimated corpse — designated SCP-049-2 — that is hostile, mindless, and under SCP-049's complete control. These instances display enhanced strength and resistance to injury. SCP-049 was first recovered in Montauban, France, after Foundation agents tracked a series of disappearances to a derelict farmhouse where the entity had been operating. It was contained without significant resistance and has since proven cooperative with Foundation personnel, expressing genuine curiosity about modern medicine while steadfastly maintaining that only its 'cure' truly works. It currently resides in a standard humanoid containment cell in Site-19 with reinforced observation windows. SCP-049's most unsettling quality is its absolute, unwavering conviction that it is performing a merciful act.

Archive Dossier — Description

Appearance

SCP-049 appears as a humanoid figure of average height, clad in a thick, dark robe and a distinctive white, beaked plague doctor mask. The mask is its most prominent feature, with dark, hollow eye sockets and a long, curved beak. Its hands are covered in thick leather gloves. The entity's true face, if any, is never seen. Its movements are described as deliberate and graceful, with a calm, unnerving demeanor.

Behavior

SCP-049 is highly intelligent, articulate, and speaks in a calm, measured tone, often using archaic or formal language. It is obsessively fixated on what it calls the 'Pestilence' — a concept it believes is a fatal disease afflicting all of humanity. It views itself as a healer, and its primary 'treatment' is to 'cure' subjects by killing them and then reanimating their corpses as 'instances of SCP-049-1'. These instances are zombie-like, but under the Doctor's control. It is generally cooperative with Foundation staff, but becomes hostile if its 'work' is interrupted. It shows no malice, only a terrifying, unwavering conviction in its own righteousness.

Domains

Contained within a standard humanoid containment chamber at Site-19. Its conceptual 'domain' is the sickness and mortality of the human condition, which it seeks to 'cure'.

Symbols

The plague doctor mask and dark robe are its primary symbols, evoking the historical era of the Black Death and the fear of pestilence. The act of 'touching' a subject to inflict the 'cure' is a key symbolic action.

Archive Dossier — Powers

The 'Cure' (Lethal Touch)

Source Version Main SCP-049 article

Description

SCP-049's primary anomalous ability. A direct touch on bare skin causes the subject to die almost instantly, with all bodily functions ceasing. The cause of death is attributed to an unknown agent that shuts down the body's systems.

Limitations

The subject must be touched on bare skin. The effect is not instantaneous if the subject is wearing protective gear. The 'Pestilence' it is 'curing' is not a real, identifiable disease.

Contradictions

The exact nature of the 'Pestilence' is never defined. It is a subjective belief of SCP-049, not an objective reality.

Reanimation (Creation of SCP-049-1 instances)

Source Version Main SCP-049 article

Description

After a subject is 'cured', SCP-049 can reanimate the corpse, creating an instance of SCP-049-1. These instances are shambling, zombie-like, and obey SCP-049's commands. They are driven by a compulsion to serve the Doctor.

Limitations

The reanimated corpse is not intelligent and lacks free will. The process is a perversion of life, not a true resurrection. The instance's body continues to decay.

Contradictions

The relationship between the 'cure' and the reanimation is unclear. The reanimation seems to be a secondary effect or a tool for the Doctor's work, not the 'cure' itself.

Enhanced Intelligence and Charisma

Source Version Main article and interview logs

Description

SCP-049 is highly intelligent, with a deep (if misguided) understanding of medicine and anatomy. It is also highly persuasive, able to calmly argue its case and make its actions seem logical, even to trained personnel.

Limitations

Its intelligence is filtered through its delusional worldview. It cannot be reasoned with about the nature of the 'Pestilence'.

Contradictions

None.

Description

SCP-049 creates these reanimated servants. They are utterly devoted to it and follow its commands without question. They are a physical manifestation of its 'cure' and its control over death.

Version Note

The instances are a direct result of SCP-049's power.

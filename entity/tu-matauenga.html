Tu Matauenga

God of War and Humanity; Tū of the Angry Face; The Eater of Armies

also known asTū of the Angry FaceTū MatauengaTumatauenga
TypeWar Deity / Human Ancestor
CultureMāori
Who It Is
Form

Tu Matauenga is often depicted as a fierce warrior with an angry face, symbolizing his role as a god of war and humanity. His appearance is often associated with the color red, representing blood and violence. In some accounts, he is described as having a large, imposing physique, reflecting his strength and power.

Powers

Warfare Tu Matauenga is said to possess the power of warfare, allowing him to lead armies into battle and protect his people from harm.

Healing In some accounts, Tu Matauenga is described as having the ability to heal wounds and cure illnesses, reflecting his role as a god of humanity.

Accounts

Tu Matauenga is said to have led the Māori people to victory in the Battle of the Waikato, a significant conflict between the Māori and European forces.

Tu Matauenga is said to have been born from the earth, emerging as a fierce warrior with an angry face. He is described as a god of war and humanity, protecting his people from harm and leading them into battle.

Scholarly Debates
Tu Matauenga's role in Māori mythology
Tu Matauenga is a god of war and humanity, protecting his people from harm and leading them into battle. ⇌ Tu Matauenga is a god of conflict and violence, often leading to conflict and harm.
What We Don't Know

There are various accounts of Tu Matauenga's story, with different versions emerging across different Māori cultures and traditions. Further research is needed to fully understand his role in Māori mythology.

A way to hold it in your mind

Tu Matauenga is a complex and multifaceted deity, embodying both the ferocity of war and the compassion of humanity. His story is a rich and nuanced exploration of the human condition, inviting readers to reflect on the nature of conflict and protection.

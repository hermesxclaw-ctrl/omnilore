Who It Is

In a world where Soul Reapers guard the boundary between life and death, Uryū Ishida stands as the last proud heir of a nearly extinct race of archers who once waged war against them. Armed with a bow of pure spirit energy and a mind as sharp as his arrows, he is a living contradiction—a bitter enemy who becomes a steadfast ally, a pacifist who wields deadly precision, and a Quincy who defies the very king of his own kind.

Initially aloof, proud, and resentful toward Soul Reapers, Uryū is highly intelligent, disciplined, and values order and precision. He has a strong sense of justice and a deep desire to protect others, often putting himself in danger for his friends. Over time, he becomes more cooperative and shows a caring side, especially toward Ichigo and his friends.

Name & Tongue

Pronunciation — OO-ryoo EE-shee-da

Aliases — The Last Quincy, Antithesis

Powers
Reishi Manipulation

Uryū can gather and manipulate reishi (spirit particles) from the atmosphere to form weapons and tools, most notably his bow and arrows.

Limit: Requires a sufficient concentration of reishi in the environment; in areas with low reishi, his abilities are weakened.

Quincy Archery

Uryū is a master archer, able to fire arrows with great speed and accuracy, and can create various arrow types, including explosive and homing arrows.

Limit: His accuracy can be affected by emotional state or distraction.

Ginto

Uryū uses small spirit-energy containers called ginto to store reishi and activate spells, such as traps and barriers.

Limit: Limited number of ginto; requires preparation.

Quincy: Vollständig

A form that draws power from Yhwach, granting enhanced abilities and wings of light. Uryū's Vollständig is named 'Antithesis'.

Limit: Requires Yhwach's blessing; may be unstable if used against him.

Antithesis

Uryū's Schrift, which allows him to reverse events between two targets, such as transferring damage or injuries.

Limit: Requires a clear target; can be countered by overwhelming power.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Uryū is a slender young man with short black hair and blue eyes, often wearing rectangular glasses. He typically dresses in a white Quincy uniform with a high collar and blue accents, and later adopts a more traditional Quincy outfit. He carries a bow made of reishi.

Symbols — The Quincy cross, which he wears as a pendant; his bow and arrows symbolize his heritage as an archer.

Domains — Primarily Karakura Town, the human world; later the Soul Society and the Wandenreich's shadow realm.

Accounts I
Introduction as RivalBleach manga/anime, early chapters/episodes

2001-2004 (manga), 2004-2006 (anime)

Uryū is introduced as a classmate of Ichigo who reveals himself as a Quincy, a race of archers who hunt Hollows. He challenges Ichigo to a competition to see who can defeat more Hollows, but their rivalry turns into a partnership when they face a powerful Menos Grande. Uryū and Ichigo begin to respect each other, laying the foundation for their friendship.

Soul Society ArcBleach manga/anime, Soul Society arc

2002-2004 (manga), 2004-2006 (anime)

Uryū joins Ichigo and his friends to rescue Rukia from the Soul Society. He battles various Soul Reapers, including Mayuri Kurotsuchi, who reveals the Quincy's history of genocide by the Soul Reapers. Uryū defeats Mayuri but loses his powers in the process. Uryū temporarily loses his Quincy powers, deepening his hatred for Soul Reapers, but he later regains them.

Accounts II
Arrancar ArcBleach manga/anime, Arrancar arc

2004-2010 (manga), 2006-2010 (anime)

Uryū regains his powers and supports Ichigo in battles against the Arrancar. He is captured by the villain Szayelaporro Granz but is later rescued by his friends. Uryū becomes more integrated into Ichigo's group.

Thousand-Year Blood War ArcBleach manga/anime, Thousand-Year Blood War arc

2012-2016 (manga), 2022-2023 (anime)

Uryū is revealed to be a descendant of the Quincy and is chosen by Yhwach as his successor, becoming a double agent. He learns of the Soul Reapers' past genocide of the Quincy and struggles with his loyalties. In the end, he uses his Antithesis to help defeat Yhwach. Uryū survives and helps bring peace, solidifying his role as a hero.

Who It Is

A creepypasta presented as a first-person account of playing a haunted NES Godzilla game cartridge. The game starts normally but becomes increasingly corrupted: levels depict mutilated monsters, the game communicates directly with the player through text boxes, and the final boss is 'Red' — a demonic, bleeding face composed of pixel art that breaks the fourth wall. Red is not from Godzilla; it is a sentient entity that exists within the game code and consumes the player's soul if defeated.

Form

Red appears as a grotesque pixel art face made of red pixels, constantly bleeding down the screen. Its eyes follow the player's cursor. It is introduced after the player-character Godzilla defeats what appears to be the final boss — Red fills the screen and the text box reads: 'You have gone too far. Now you must die.'

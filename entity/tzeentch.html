Who It Is

Tzeentch is the Chaos God of change, knowledge, sorcery, ambition, and scheming — the most intelligent and subtle of the four Great Chaos Gods, and arguably the most dangerous. While Khorne offers brute force, Tzeentch offers knowledge and power — but always at a terrible price. Tzeentch is a being of infinite cunning and infinite deceit. Every event in the universe is part of his grand, incomprehensible plan — except that he may be lying about having a plan at all. The truth about Tzeentch is that even he may not know his own goals, because change (his fundamental nature) means that his plans are constantly evolving and contradicting themselves. His followers — the Thousand Sons Chaos Space Marines and Tzeentchian daemons — are powerful sorcerers and schemers who believe they are manipulating events toward their own goals, unaware that they are puppets in Tzeentch's ever-shifting design. Tzeentch's realm in the Warp is the Impossible Fortress, a labyrinthine structure that constantly rearranges itself, filled with portals, traps, and horrors. His daemons include Pink Horrors (which split into Blue Horrors when killed), Flamers (living fire), and the Lords of Change — his Greater Daemons, massive avian entities of immense psychic power and ancient knowledge. Tzeentch's greatest weapon is hope — because hope inspires ambition, and ambition leads to deals with the Changer of Ways. Every wizard who seeks forbidden knowledge, every revolutionary who wants to overturn the order, every soul that wishes for things to be different — all of them are Tzeentch's prey. He represents the terrifying truth that the pursuit of knowledge may corrupt you absolutely, that the desire for change may destroy everything you love, and that the greatest conspiracies are the ones where even the conspirators do not know they are pawns. Tzeentch is the god who offers you exactly what you want — and makes you grateful for the disaster that follows.

Who It Is

Morg the Executioner is widely considered the most violent and effective herald Galactus ever created. Hailing from the war-torn planet Saiph, Morg was a barbarian warrior-king who delighted in slaughter. When Galactus needed a herald capable of dealing with increasingly powerful cosmic threats, he chose Morg — not for his nobility, but for his brutal efficiency. Galactus augmented Morg's already fearsome combat abilities with the Power Cosmic, turning him into a living weapon of planetary destruction. Morg wields an enormous cosmic axe that can cleave planets in half, and his rage-fueled combat style makes him a terror even among cosmic beings. Upon becoming herald, Morg immediately demonstrated his ruthlessness by murdering his predecessor Nova (Frankie Raye) on Galactus's orders when she refused to step aside. He then proceeded to systematically eliminate several former heralds who had gathered to oppose Galactus, including killing Firelord and battling the Silver Surfer to a standstill. Morg later rebelled against Galactus but was ultimately defeated by the combined forces of the Surfer, Thanos, and Master Order and Lord Chaos. He was imprisoned but later escaped, continuing to serve Galactus in a limited capacity. Morg represents the darkest possibility of what a herald can become — a being of pure, unfiltered destructive power with no moral compass, only appetite and ambition. He is the final herald in the classic sense, as Galactus later adopted other methods of finding worlds.

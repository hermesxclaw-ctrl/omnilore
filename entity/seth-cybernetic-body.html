Who It Is

A synthetic being created as the new leader of the SIN organization, designed with a 'body' that can stretch and deform in impossible ways. Seth's torso is a mechanical core containing a glowing orb of energy that can detach and attack independently. They are the culmination of Dr. V'lu's cybernetic research using copied fighting data.

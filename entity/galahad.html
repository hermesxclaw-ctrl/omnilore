Who It Is
Form

Galahad is often depicted as a young, handsome knight, dressed in armor and carrying a sword. He is sometimes shown with a halo or surrounded by a radiant aura, symbolizing his connection to the divine.

Powers

Grail Achiever Galahad is able to achieve the Holy Grail, a sacred cup or dish said to have been used by Jesus at the Last Supper.

Accounts

Galahad, along with Sir Percival and Sir Bors, embarks on a quest to find the Holy Grail, a sacred cup or dish said to have been used by Jesus at the Last Supper.

Galahad is born to Elaine of Corbenic, who is deceived into believing that Lancelot is her husband. This story explores the circumstances of Galahad's conception and birth.

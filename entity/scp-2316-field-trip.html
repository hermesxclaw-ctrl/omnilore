Who It Is

A phenomenon occurring in bodies of water where a group of human corpses — recognizable to the observer as people they personally knew in childhood — float on the surface and call for the observer to join them. The corpses exhibit signs of drowning and decomposition consistent with having been dead for years. Attempting to retrieve a corpse results in the observer drowning, after which their own body joins the group.

Form

A cluster of 5-15 waterlogged corpses floating face-up in freshwater. Each corpse's face is immediately recognizable to the individual observer as someone they knew in their youth. Corpses mouth the words 'Come in, the water's fine.'

Powers

Personalized cognitive targeting

Auditory hallucination

Compulsion to enter water

Replication of victims into the group

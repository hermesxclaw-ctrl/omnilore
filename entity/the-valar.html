Who It Is
Form

The Valar are depicted as powerful, godlike beings with varying forms and abilities. They are often shown as majestic, wise, and benevolent. In some accounts, they are described as having taken on human-like forms to interact with the Elves and Men.

Powers

Creation The Valar have the power to create and shape the world of Middle-earth, including the formation of mountains, rivers, and landscapes.

Magic The Valar possess magical abilities, including the power to heal, protect, and manipulate the natural world.

Wisdom The Valar are depicted as wise and knowledgeable, with a deep understanding of the workings of the world and the nature of the universe.

Accounts

The Valar create Middle-earth and shape the world, including the formation of mountains, rivers, and landscapes.

Melkor corrupts the Two Trees, causing the Darkening of Valinor and the departure of the Valar from Middle-earth.

Scholarly Debates
The Valar's power
The Valar are depicted as all-powerful and capable of creating and shaping the world. ⇌ However, they are also shown to be limited by their own laws and the actions of Melkor.
What We Don't Know

The Valar's motivations and actions are not always clear, and their relationships with other entities in Middle-earth are often complex and multifaceted.

A way to hold it in your mind

The Valar are fascinating and complex beings, with a rich history and a deep connection to the world of Middle-earth. Their stories and legends offer a glimpse into the workings of the universe and the nature of power and creation.

Who It Is
Form

The Wicker Man is a 1973 British folk horror film directed by Robin Hardy and starring Edward Woodward, Britt Ekland, Diane Cilento, Ingrid Pitt and Christopher Lee. The film is set on the fictional, isolated Scottish island of Summerisle and follows the visit of a police officer, Sergeant Neil Howie, to the island in search of a missing girl.

Powers

Ritualistic practices The inhabitants of the island in The Wicker Man are depicted as being pagan and ritualistic, with a focus on fertility and the cycle of life and death.

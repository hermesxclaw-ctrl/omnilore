A giant effigy of woven wicker in human shape, filled with sacrificial offerings — animals and, in the film's climax, the Christian policeman Sergeant Howie — and set ablaze to ensure crop fertility. It is the ritual centerpiece of Summerisle's pagan religion, led by Lord Summerisle.

Who It Is

A giant effigy of woven wicker in human shape, filled with sacrificial offerings — animals and, in the film's climax, the Christian policeman Sergeant Howie — and set ablaze to ensure crop fertility. It is the ritual centerpiece of Summerisle's pagan religion, led by Lord Summerisle.

Form

Towering wicker humanoid figure, 60+ ft tall, hollow, filled with kindling and sacrificial animals/humans; crude facial features, arms outstretched

Powers

Ritual sacrifice vessel

Symbol of communal belief

Crop fertility through immolation

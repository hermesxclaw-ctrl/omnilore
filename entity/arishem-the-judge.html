Who It Is

Arishem is portrayed as solemn, impartial, and detached from mortal concerns. He is a judge who weighs the worth of civilizations without malice or mercy. He follows the Celestial mandate without question, though he has shown flexibility in rare cases (e.g., sparing a planet after an attack).

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Arishem' is likely a Kirbynian invention, possibly derived from 'Aris' (Greek for 'best') and 'hem' (a suffix used in Kirby's cosmic names). No official etymology has been published.

Pronunciation — AIR-ih-shem (commonly used in fan circles; no official pronunciation given by Marvel)

Aliases — Arishem, Judge of the Celestials, Leader of the Fourth Host

Ownership — Marvel Comics (Marvel Entertainment / Disney), created by Jack Kirby for The Eternals #2 (1976).

Powers
Cosmic Energy Manipulation

Arishem can manipulate cosmic energies for various effects, including energy blasts, force fields, and telekinesis. He can also alter matter at a molecular level.

Limit: Limited by the Celestial code; cannot interfere with civilizations unless judgment is passed.

Immortality and Regeneration

Arishem is nigh-immortal and can regenerate lost limbs instantly. He can be resurrected by other Celestials if not dead too long.

Limit: Resurrection requires other Celestials and a time limit.

Reality Warping

As a Celestial, Arishem can warp reality on a cosmic scale, including creating new species and altering planetary environments.

Limit: Not fully explored; likely limited by Celestial hierarchy and cosmic balance.

Multiversal Existence

Arishem exists across the multiverse as a single entity split into aspects in each universe.

Limit: Each aspect may be individually vulnerable.

Judgment Power

Arishem has the authority to judge civilizations, deciding their fate to live or die. This power is not a direct attack but a cosmic decree.

Limit: Must follow Celestial law; cannot judge without a Host or proper procedure.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Arishem is a colossal humanoid figure, roughly 2,000 feet tall, clad in ornate, multi-colored armor. His face is obscured by a helmet with a single glowing visor. His armor is decorated with intricate patterns and glowing energy nodes. In the MCU, his design is more organic and crystalline, with a massive body composed of geometric shapes and glowing energy.

Symbols — Arishem is often depicted with scales or a book, symbolizing judgment. His armor features cosmic patterns and celestial motifs. He is associated with the color gold and bright energy.

Domains — Arishem exists in the cosmic realm, often traveling through space. He is not bound to any single dimension, but he has been seen on Earth and other planets. He is a being of the Marvel multiverse, existing across all realities simultaneously.

Accounts I
Creation of the MultiverseAvengers (vol. 8) #8 (2018)

Pre-universe

Arishem and the original Celestials were created by the First Firmament. They warred against the Aspirants and destroyed the First Firmament, creating the Second Cosmos (the first multiverse). They then created the Beyonders to manage the new creation. Initiated the cycle of cosmic death and rebirth; Celestials persist across all subsequent cosmos.

War with KnullAvengers (vol. 8) #8 (2018)

Pre-universe

After the Sixth Cosmos was destroyed, Arishem and the Celestials encountered Knull, the King in Black. They offered him a role as a maintainer, but Knull rejected it. They banished him to the Void, but he later returned to wage war. Knull became a recurring enemy of the Celestials; later events lead to Knull's defeat by the Avengers.

Accounts II
Fourth Host Judgment of EarthThe Eternals #14 (1977)

1977

Arishem led the Fourth Host to Earth, beginning a 50-year judgment. During the Third Host, he had accepted the Earth Gods' vow of non-interference. In the end, he judged Earth worthy and left. Earth was spared; the Eternals continued their mission.

Judgment of a Distant PlanetX-Factor #104 (1994)

1994

Arishem arrived to judge a planet where X-Factor had been lost. He was attacked by the Chosen, Rejects, and Beginagins factions. In response, he allowed the planet to live and departed. The planet was spared; X-Factor returned to Earth.

Death and ResurrectionAvengers (vol. 8) #20 (2019)

2019

During the Dark Celestials' invasion, Arishem was killed by the Horde. The Avengers formed a Uni-Mind to render the Horde dormant, allowing the Celestials to resurrect and help defeat the Dark Celestials. The Dark Celestials were defeated; Arishem and other Celestials were resurrected.

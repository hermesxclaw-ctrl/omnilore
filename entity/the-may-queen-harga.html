Who It Is
Form

The May Queen is often depicted as a young woman, typically dressed in traditional Swedish clothing, with flowers and greenery in her hair. She is often surrounded by a group of dancing people, and is sometimes shown with a crown or wreath on her head.

Powers

Fertility and Abundance The May Queen is often associated with the power to bring fertility and abundance to the land, and is sometimes depicted as a symbol of the natural world's cyclical rhythms.

Accounts

The legend of the May Queen tells the story of a young woman who is chosen to be the queen of the summer solstice. She is often depicted as a symbol of fertility and abundance, and is sometimes shown with a crown or wreath on her head.

The cult of Midsommar is a pre-Christian tradition that celebrates the summer solstice with the crowning of a May Queen. The cult is often associated with fertility and abundance, and is sometimes depicted as a symbol of the natural world's cyclical rhythms.

Who It Is

The ritual figurehead of the Hårga commune's nine-day midsummer festival, selected through a maypole dance competition. The May Queen blesses crops and livestock and ultimately chooses the final sacrifice: either a randomly selected Hårga or a newcomer. Dani becomes the May Queen in the film's climax.

Form

Young woman crowned with an enormous flower dress of fresh blossoms; radiant and revered during the festival

Ritual authority

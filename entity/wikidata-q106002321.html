The Approach

In the autumn of 1992, a small, unassuming colt from Kanto shattered the dreams of an undefeated champion and rewrote the record books, earning a place in racing lore as the 'Black Assassin.'

Content advisory. Contains descriptions of animal euthanasia and competitive disappointment.

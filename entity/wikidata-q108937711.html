Who It Is

In a world where a goddess discards her summoned hero for his plain face, the wolf-kin girl Minitona Dedoldia stands at the edge of two prejudices — too beastly for humans, too gentle for a battlefield — and must decide whether loyalty to the kind man who gave her a home is stronger than the howl in her blood.

Attested (light novel/manga dialogue, secondary summaries): Portrayed as earnest, somewhat shy or soft-spoken compared to more boisterous Dedoldia relatives, diligent in training, and loyal to Makoto Misumi (the protagonist) who provides sanctuary in the demiplane. Participates in communal life and combat training but not depicted as a front-line powerhouse in summaries reviewed. Personality details for this supporting character are sparsely documented in English sources; deeper psychological portrait in Japanese volumes not verified for this draft. Powers
Dedoldia Beastkin Physiology (Wolf-type)

As a member of the wolf-kin Dedoldia clan, possesses enhanced olfaction, hearing, night vision, physical agility and stamina beyond baseline human. Capable of partial to full beast transformation tropes common to the series' demi-humans, though specific transformation feats for Minitona not prominently documented in English summaries.

Limit: Not among the strongest fighters in Asora; power scaling in Tsukimichi places Tomoe, Mio, and other contracted spirits far above demi-human residents. Abilities limited by inexperience and youth. No attested magic specialization located for Minitona specifically.

Combat Training (Demiplane Curriculum)

Trained in basic martial combat, archery and group tactics under the demiplane's instructors (including Tomoe and Shiki's regimes). Participates in mock battles and defense drills.

Limit: Supporting-tier combatant; not depicted as defeating major antagonists solo in sources reviewed. Training progress is ongoing.

Asora Residency Benefits

Residence in Asora grants access to its abundant resources, healing, and protection under Makoto's authority, including rapid recovery and avoidance of the goddess's prejudice against demi-humans.

Limit: Benefits are environmental and political, not innate powers. Dependent on Makoto's continued maintenance of the demiplane and goodwill.

Accounts I
Rescue and Sanctuary in AsoraTsuki ga Michibiku Isekai Douchuu light novel / web novel - early demiplane settlement arcs

2013-present (in-universe: after Makoto's summoning and creation of Asora)

Following Makoto Misumi's rejection by the goddess and his discovery of the demiplane, he gathers demi-humans and other persecuted races. The Dedoldia clan, including Minitona, are among those granted residence in Asora, escaping discrimination in the goddess's world where hyumans dominate. Minitona's arrival establishes her as part of the growing demiplane community under Makoto's informal lordship. Establishes Minitona's home base and loyalty bond; expands Asora's population and labor force; foreshadows demiplane's role as multiracial sanctuary.

Daily Life and Training in the DemiplaneTsuki ga Michibiku Isekai Douchuu light novel volumes 2-4; manga chapters 15-30 (approx.)

2013-present

Slice-of-life and training vignettes show demiplane residents building villages, farming, crafting, and drilling for defense. Minitona appears in group scenes, practicing combat forms, attending lessons, and participating in communal meals and festivals. These chapters emphasize worldbuilding over individual heroics. Develops Asora as a functioning society; characterizes Minitona as diligent but not yet elite; reinforces theme of found family.

The Demiplane's External Missions - Support RoleTsuki ga Michibiku Isekai Douchuu - mid-series commerce and exploration arcs (Kuzunoha Company / Tsige-related arcs)

2015-present (light novel publication)

As Makoto establishes the Kuzunoha Company in the main world, select Asora residents are dispatched as staff, guards, or artisans. While primary field agents are Tomoe, Mio, and named executives, Dedoldia members including Minitona are referenced as part of the reserve/support pool, helping with logistics, guarding shipments, or maintaining the demiplane during absences. Demonstrates integration of demiplane residents into the main world's economy; shows limited but real mobility for supporting characters.

Accounts II
The Academy and Social Prejudice Arc (Contextual Backdrop)Tsuki ga Michibiku Isekai Douchuu - Rotsgard Academy arc

2016-present

Makoto becomes a teacher at Rotsgard Academy, confronting the goddess's world's systemic discrimination against demi-humans. While Minitona does not enroll as a student (unlike some other demi-humans), the arc's themes directly affect her people: the Dedoldia's status as 'lesser' in hyuman eyes is debated by scholars and adventurers, and Asora's existence becomes a quiet counter-argument. Worldbuilding payoff for Minitona's backstory; no direct feat for her, but raises stakes for demi-human characters' future integration.

Defense of Asora - Demonic and Hyuman Incursions (Ensemble Defense)Tsuki ga Michibiku Isekai Douchuu - later light novel volumes; anime S2 climax episodes

2018-present

Periodic threats — ranging from stray monsters to scouting parties from hyuman nations or demon forces — test Asora's defenses. The entire settlement mobilizes. Minitona participates as part of the militia line, following orders, evacuating noncombatants, or holding perimeter alongside other beastkin. Individual duels are reserved for Tomoe/Mio/Shiki, while supporting cast shows collective defense. Reinforces that Asora's strength is communal; no single heroic legend attached to Minitona, consistent with supporting archetype.

Festivals and Inter-Clan Relations in AsoraTsuki ga Michibiku Isekai Douchuu - interlude / short story collections and manga bonus chapters

2014-present

Seasonal festivals, sparring tournaments, and inter-clan gatherings within Asora allow Dedoldia youth to interact with other races (Forest Ogres, Eld, etc.). Minitona appears in these low-stakes social stories, which flesh out daily culture, food, music, and courtship customs of the demiplane. Adds cultural texture; no major plot consequence but important for character presence and franchise's 'cozy isekai' appeal.

Minitona Dedoldia is a major hero or goddess in Tsukimichi. — She is a supporting demiplane resident, not a contracted superior being (Tomoe/Mio/Shiki) nor a goddess. Her archetype heroic-legend in Wikidata reflects broad tagging, not narrative centrality.

Dedoldia are wolves that transform into humans, like classic werewolves. — In Tsukimichi worldbuilding, the Arke/Dedoldia are a demi-human race with stable humanoid form plus beast traits; transformation is not the classic curse-driven werewolf lore, but a fantasy race biology.

Minitona has a dedicated episode or light novel volume centered on her. — No volume or episode focused on Minitona as protagonist located; she appears in ensemble chapters. Prominent Dedoldia spotlight belongs to Lime Dedoldia.

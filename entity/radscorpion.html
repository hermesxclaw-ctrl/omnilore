Who It Is

Arizona bark scorpions massively mutated by radiation into giant, aggressive predators. Radscorpions can grow to the size of a car and possess a venomous stinger that can paralyze or kill even armored targets. They have thick, chitinous exoskeletons proof against small arms fire and can burrow underground to ambush prey. Their pincers can crush bone and cut through leather armor.

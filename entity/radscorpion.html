Who It Is
Form

The Radscorpion is a large, mutated arachnid creature with a distinctive red and black color scheme. It has a pair of large, scorpion-like claws and a long, venomous tail. Its body is covered in a thick, armored exoskeleton.

Powers

Venomous Tail The Radscorpion's long, venomous tail can deliver a deadly dose of poison to its victims.

Accounts

According to the Fallout 2 manual, the Radscorpion was created by a team of scientists who were experimenting with genetic mutation. They attempted to create a new species of arachnid that could survive in a post-apocalyptic world, but their experiment went horribly wrong and resulted in the creation of the Radscorpion.

The Radscorpion plays a significant role in the Fallout universe, serving as a symbol of danger and mutation. It is often encountered by players in the game, and can be a formidable opponent.

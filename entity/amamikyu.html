Who It Is

In the beginning, there were no islands in the vast blue expanse—only the Heavenly Emperor above and the empty sea below. It was Amamikyu, the divine weaver of land and life, who descended from the celestial castle to breathe existence into the Ryukyu archipelago, setting in motion a lineage of kings and priestesses that would endure for centuries.

Portrayed as obedient to the Heavenly Emperor, resourceful in requesting materials, and proactive in creating islands and communities. She becomes pregnant without sexual intercourse, indicating a divine and miraculous nature.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Amamikyu' is a Japanese reading of Chinese characters 阿摩美久 or 阿摩彌姑, likely written ad hoc for the Okinawan pronunciation. It may be related to the Amami Islands. Other kanji spellings include 天御子 and 天美子 (Amamiko).

Pronunciation — Okinawan: Amamichuu; Japanese readings vary: Amamikyu, Amamikyo, Amamikiyo, Amamiko, Amamiku, Amamigu, Amanchuu.

Aliases — Amamichuu, Amekushin-otome-ōankami, Amamiko, Amamikyo, Amamikiyo, Amamiku

Ownership — Ryukyuan (Okinawan) religion and culture; specifically associated with the Ryukyu Kingdom and the indigenous belief system of the Ryukyu Islands.

Form & Signs

Appearance — No explicit physical description is provided in the sources. She is a goddess, likely depicted in human form in later art, but no canonical iconography exists.

Symbols — Associated with sacred sites: Sefa-utaki, Kudaka Island, Tamagusuku Castle, Chinen Castle, Minton Castle. As a creation goddess, symbols of fertility and creation may be implied but not explicitly documented.

Domains — Heavenly Gusuku (heavenly castle) as origin; descended to Kudaka Island, then Okinawa Island; final home at Minton Castle. Associated with sacred groves (utaki) and royal authority.

Accounts I
Creation of the Ryukyu IslandsChūzan Seikan

1650 (compiled)

The Heavenly Emperor, residing in the Heavenly Gusuku, saw no islands in the world and ordered Amamikyu to create the Ryukyu Islands. She requested materials, and Shinerikyu brought grasses, trees, and stones. She descended to Kudaka Island, then landed at Sefa-utaki on Okinawa, building castles and communities. She asked for materials to make people, but other gods refused to descend. She became pregnant by the divine spirit and populated the islands. Later, a heavenly grandchild named Tentei was born, whose three sons and two daughters became the ancestors of the five social classes: the first son became the first king (Tenson), the second became the first feudal lord (Aji), the third became the first farmer, the first daughter became the first royal noro priestess, and the second daughter became the first village noro priestess. Established the Ryukyu Islands, its people, and the social order with the royal lineage and priestess system.

Who It Is

In the world of Jujutsu Kaisen, where curses are born from human fear and sorcerers risk their lives to exorcise them, Gojo Satoru stands as an almost mythic figure—a man whose very presence redefines the balance of power. With eyes that hold the cosmos and a smile that hides unfathomable strength, he is both the strongest sorcerer alive and the most isolated, a beacon of hope and a target of every dark force that seeks to shatter the fragile peace between humans and curses.

Gojo is charismatic, playful, and often arrogant, but beneath his carefree exterior lies a deeply responsible and caring individual. He is fiercely protective of his students and friends, and he carries the weight of being the strongest sorcerer. He uses humor and nonchalance to mask his loneliness and the immense pressure he faces. He is highly intelligent and strategic, often thinking several steps ahead of his opponents.

Powers
Limitless (Mukagen)

Gojo's innate technique, inherited from the Gojo family, allows him to manipulate the concept of infinity. He can create an infinite series of finite numbers to stop attacks, and he can use the technique's application, 'Cursed Technique Lapse: Blue' to create a vacuum that pulls objects, and 'Cursed Technique Reversal: Red' to repel. He also possesses the 'Hollow Technique: Purple', which combines both Blue and Red to create an imaginary mass that erases anything it touches.

Limit: The technique requires immense cursed energy and precise control. Overuse can cause exhaustion. The technique is not absolute; it can be countered by Domain Expansion or by attacks that bypass the concept of infinity, such as those that target space itself.

Six Eyes

A rare ocular jujutsu trait that grants Gojo incredibly precise perception of cursed energy. It allows him to see the flow of cursed energy in fine detail, perceive the nature of techniques, and reduce the cost of his own cursed energy usage. It also enables him to use his Limitless technique to its full potential.

Limit: The Six Eyes are a physical trait that can be overwhelmed by sensory overload, which is why he wears a blindfold. It also makes him a target for those who wish to steal his eyes.

Domain Expansion: Infinite Void

Gojo's Domain Expansion creates a space where the target is forced to experience infinite information, effectively paralyzing them as their mind is overwhelmed. It is considered one of the most powerful Domain Expansions in the series.

Limit: Domain Expansion requires a significant amount of cursed energy and can be countered by another Domain Expansion that is equally refined. The effect is not permanent, and the target can be released if Gojo chooses.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Gojo is a tall, lean man with spiky white hair and striking blue eyes that are often covered by a black blindfold or dark sunglasses. When his eyes are revealed, they appear to be a pale blue with a swirling, almost cosmic pattern, reflecting his innate technique. He typically wears a high-collared black uniform with a long coat, and often has a carefree, confident expression. His appearance is distinctive and instantly recognizable.

Symbols — His blindfold and sunglasses symbolize his need to limit his perception, as his Six Eyes allow him to see the world in a superhuman way. His eyes themselves are a symbol of his power and his burden. The white hair and blue eyes are iconic and have become synonymous with the character.

Domains — As a jujutsu sorcerer, Gojo operates in the supernatural world of curses and sorcerers. He is based at Tokyo Jujutsu High, but he is frequently on missions across Japan. His personal domain is the concept of 'Infinity', which is both his technique and his mental state. He is comfortable in both the mundane world and the hidden world of jujutsu.

Accounts I
The Strongest SorcererJujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 1-2

Modern era

Gojo is introduced as a teacher at Tokyo Jujutsu High. He meets Yuji Itadori, a high school student who becomes the host of a powerful curse, Ryomen Sukuna. Gojo decides to protect Yuji and bring him into the jujutsu world, despite the higher-ups' desire to execute Yuji. He demonstrates his immense power by easily defeating a special grade curse. Gojo becomes Yuji's mentor and protector, setting the stage for the series' main conflict.

Accounts II
The Shibuya IncidentJujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 65-90

Modern era

Gojo is lured into a trap in Shibuya by the cursed spirit Mahito and the sorcerer Geto Suguru (actually Kenjaku). He is sealed inside the Prison Realm, a powerful artifact that traps him in an endless void. His sealing is a major turning point, leaving the jujutsu world vulnerable. The sealing of Gojo leads to a power vacuum, and the series enters a darker phase.

The Rivalry with Geto SuguruJujutsu Kaisen manga, flashback chapters

Past, when Gojo was a student

Gojo and Geto were best friends and the strongest duo of their generation. However, Geto's descent into darkness and his belief that non-sorcerers are inferior led to a tragic split. Gojo was forced to confront Geto, and their final confrontation ended with Gojo killing Geto, though Geto's body was later taken over by Kenjaku. This event shapes Gojo's character, making him more protective and lonely.

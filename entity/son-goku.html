Who It Is

The Four-Tails, a massive primate with four tails, red fur, and a powerful physique. Despite its ape-like appearance, Son Goku is highly intelligent and philosophical. It controls Lava Release, a potent kekkei genkai that combines Fire and Earth natures. Sealed within Rōshi of Iwagakure.

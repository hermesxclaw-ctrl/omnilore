Who It Is
Form

Son Goku is often depicted as a young boy with a monkey tail, wearing a red and blue gi. He is also shown with a powerful aura and incredible strength.

Powers

Superhuman Strength Son Goku possesses incredible physical strength, allowing him to lift and throw massive objects with ease.

Accounts

The story of Journey to the West follows the Monkey King, Sun Wukong, as he travels to India to retrieve Buddhist scriptures.

The story of Dragon Ball follows the adventures of Son Goku, a young boy with a monkey tail, as he searches for the seven Dragon Balls.

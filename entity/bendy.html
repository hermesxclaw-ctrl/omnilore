Who It Is

Bendy is the central entity of the indie horror game series Bendy and the Ink Machine, developed by Kindly Beast (formerly TheMeatly Games). The game is set in the abandoned Joey Drew Studios, a fictional 1930s-era animation studio that once produced cartoon shorts featuring a mischievous little devil character named Bendy — similar in style to early Disney and Fleischer Studios cartoons. The game's protagonist, Henry Stein, returns to the studio decades after its closure to find it transformed into a nightmare. The ink used in the studio's animation process has become a living, corrupting substance, and the cartoon characters — Bendy, Boris the Wolf, Alice Angel — have manifested as grotesque, ink-based monsters. Bendy himself has been twisted into the 'Ink Demon' — a towering, skeletal creature made of dripping, writhing ink, with a permanent cartoon grin stretched across his face. Unlike the cute cartoon character he was drawn to be, the Ink Demon is predatory, relentless, and almost indestructible. His movements are jerky and unnatural, like a corrupted film reel, and his presence causes ink to bleed from the walls. The game's horror draws on multiple sources: the aesthetic of early rubber-hose animation, the creepy atmosphere of abandoned workplaces, and the body horror of human-to-ink transformation. The Bendy franchise expanded to include novels, comics, and a sequel (Bendy and the Dark Revival), and it has been widely praised for its unique aesthetic and atmospheric horror. Bendy represents a distinct strand of internet horror: the indie game that builds its own mythology from scratch, using the visual language of a bygone era to create something genuinely disturbing.

Who It Is
Form

Antu is often depicted as a radiant sun, symbolizing the warmth and light of the day.

Powers

Solar Energy Antu has the power to harness and control the energy of the sun, using it to nourish and sustain life.

Accounts

In the creation myth of the Mapuche people, Antu is said to have created the world and all living things, bringing light and life to the universe.

In this myth, Antu is said to have created the sun and the moon, using its solar energy to bring light to the world.

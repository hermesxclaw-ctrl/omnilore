Who It Is

In the neon-lit world of the Samurai Shodown fighting game, a green-skinned warrior from a hidden African kingdom wields a fierce boomerang and commands the power of the sun itself. This is Tam Tam, a character who blends ancient mysticism with arcade-era spectacle, embodying both the heroic guardian and the untamed forces of nature.

Tam Tam is portrayed as a fierce and proud warrior, loyal to his kingdom and his sister Cham Cham. He is serious and determined, but also shows a protective side, especially towards his sister.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Tam Tam' is likely derived from the onomatopoeic term for a drum or gong, but in the context of the character, it may be intended to evoke an African-sounding name, possibly inspired by the Tam-Tam drum.

Pronunciation — TAM-TAM

Aliases — Tamtam

Ownership — Created by SNK for the Samurai Shodown series; character is part of Japanese video game culture, but draws on African cultural motifs.

Powers
Boomerang attacks

Tam Tam throws his boomerang in various trajectories to hit opponents from a distance.

Limit: The boomerang can be dodged or blocked; it returns to him after a short delay.

Sun-based special moves

He can call upon the power of the sun for attacks like the 'Sun Fire' or 'Sun Beam', which involve fiery or radiant projectiles.

Limit: These moves require a power gauge or have a cooldown.

Animal summons

In some games, Tam Tam can summon a jaguar or other animals to attack opponents.

Limit: Not present in all versions.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Tam Tam is a tall, muscular man with green skin, which is a distinctive trait. He wears a leopard-skin loincloth, bone necklaces, and a headdress with feathers. He often carries a large boomerang and has tribal markings on his body.

Symbols — Associated with the sun, boomerang, and African tribal imagery. His design evokes a shamanistic warrior.

Domains — His homeland is the fictional African kingdom of Africa (or 'Green Hell' in some translations), which is depicted as a lush, jungle-like environment.

Tam Tam is the prince of a hidden African kingdom. When an evil force threatens his land, he sets out to defeat it. He also seeks to protect his sister Cham Cham, who often accompanies him in the story. Tam Tam's quest often leads him to confront the main villain of each game, and he returns to his kingdom after each victory.

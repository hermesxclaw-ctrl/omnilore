Who It Is
Form

The Dullahan is often depicted as a headless rider on a black horse, or as a coachman, who carries his own head. He is typically dressed in black and is said to be a harbinger of death.

Powers

Death Bringer The Dullahan has the power to bring death and destruction to those he encounters.

Accounts

The Dullahan is said to be a harbinger of death, who rides the roads and highways of Ireland, searching for his next victim.

The Dullahan is compared to the Headless Horseman, a figure from American folklore.

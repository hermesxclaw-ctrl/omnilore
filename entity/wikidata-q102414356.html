Etymology

The origin of the name 'Ferris' is likely from the Irish surname Ó Fearghuis or Ó Fearghasa, meaning 'descendant of Fearghus' (man of vigour). It may also be a variant of 'Fergus' or a toponymic name. However, the etymology is not explicitly stated in the source material.

The name is used across multiple cultures, primarily English-speaking, with Irish roots. It appears in the United States as a surname and place name, and in Japan as a university name (Ferris University, possibly a transliteration of a Western name).

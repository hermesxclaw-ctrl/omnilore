Who It Is

Madam Koi Koi is one of West Africa's most famous school-based urban legends -- a terrifying ghost story told in boarding schools and secondary schools across Nigeria and Ghana. The legend centers on a beautiful but strict female teacher who was known for wearing red high-heeled shoes that made a distinctive 'koi koi' sound as she walked down the school corridors. In the most common version, she was cruel to students, and they retaliated by playing a prank that accidentally killed her. Her vengeful spirit now haunts the school, and students hear the 'koi koi' of her heels echoing in the hallways at night, growing louder as she approaches. If she catches a student out of bed after lights-out -- or if a student answers when she calls their name -- she beats them to death or drags them away, never to be seen again. In another variant, students who mock the 'koi koi' sound are the ones she targets. The legend serves as a powerful disciplinary tool in West African boarding school culture: it enforces curfews, discourages students from wandering at night, and instills respect for authority (even dead authority). Madam Koi Koi has been adapted into Nigerian and Ghanaian Nollywood films, and in 2023 became a major Netflix Nigerian original series. The legend has multiple regional variations -- in some versions, she was a music teacher, in others a dormitory matron -- but the central motifs of the red heels, the 'koi koi' sound, and the vengeful ghost in the school corridors remain consistent across West Africa.

Who It Is
Form

Drizzt is a tall, lean, and agile humanoid with dark skin, piercing green eyes, and jet-black hair. He is often depicted wearing a white cloak and carrying a pair of scimitars.

Powers

Scimitar Mastery Drizzt is a skilled swordsman, able to wield his scimitars with precision and deadly effect.

Accounts

Drizzt Do'Urden, a young drow, leaves his home in the Underdark to explore the surface world. He becomes embroiled in a conflict between the people of Icewind Dale and the dark forces of the Underdark.

Drizzt Do'Urden returns to the Underdark to confront his past and reclaim his heritage. He becomes embroiled in a conflict between the drow and their enemies, the surface-dwelling humans.

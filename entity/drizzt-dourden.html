Who It Is

Drizzt Do'Urden is the most famous original character in Dungeons & Dragons history — a drow (dark elf) who rejected the evil society of his birth and fled to the surface world, where he became one of the greatest heroes of the Forgotten Realms. Born as the third son of House Do'Urden in the underground drow city of Menzoberranzan, Drizzt was raised in a culture of backstabbing, slavery, demon-worship, and ritual murder. Unlike his kin, Drizzt possessed an innate sense of morality and compassion — traits that made him an outcast in a society that viewed mercy as weakness.

Physically, Drizzt has the characteristic appearance of a drow: jet-black skin, long white hair, and lavender eyes that glow faintly in darkness. He is relatively short (about 5'4') and lean, but supernaturally fast — his fighting style relies on blinding speed, acrobatics, and the dual-scimitar technique he wields with his signature weapons: Twinkle (a defender scimitar that glows blue in the presence of danger) and Icingdeath (a frost-brand scimitar that protects its wielder from fire). At his side is Guenhwyvar, a magical panther figurine that summons a 600-pound black panther from the Astral Plane — his most loyal companion.

Drizzt's story, chronicled across more than 35 novels by R.A. Salvatore, covers his birth and childhood in Menzoberranzan (the Dark Elf Trilogy), his exile and early adventures in the Underdark, his journey to the surface where he faced prejudice from surface-dwellers who assumed all drow were evil, his finding of true friendship with the dwarf Bruenor Battlehammer, the human barbarian Wulfgar, and the halfling Regis, and his ongoing battles against enemies ranging from demons to dragons to his own dark kin. Drizzt represents one of gaming's first and most prominent 'redeemed villain species' narratives — his existence proves that a drow can choose good, challenging the essentialist assumptions of D&D's alignment system. He has appeared in numerous D&D video games (Baldur's Gate: Dark Alliance, Neverwinter), comics, and tabletop sourcebooks, and remains a flagship character for the entire Forgotten Realms setting — now canonically alive and adventuring well into the 5th Edition era.

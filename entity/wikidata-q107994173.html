Who It Is

If Afterglow is a sunset you can hear — five childhood friends refusing to drift apart as high school pulls them in different directions — then Tomoe Udagawa is the steady backbeat that keeps them from falling out of time. Tall, dry-humored, and famously reliable, the drummer who treats her bandmates and her little sister like family holds the rhythm while everyone else chases the melody.

Canonically reliable, calm, and big-sisterly (面倒見が良い). Tomoe is portrayed as straightforward, slightly blunt, and not easily flustered, with a dry sense of humor. She is highly dependable: bandmates and underclassmen seek her advice, and she mediates conflicts, especially between Ran and others. She is close to and protective of younger sister Ako, though exasperated by Ako's chuunibyou roleplay and Roselia devotion. Powers
Drumming - Rock Kit Performance

Afterglow's drummer since elementary school formation. Style is powerful, stable, and groove-oriented, suited to Afterglow's hard-edged pop-rock. In-universe, she is considered highly skilled and able to lead tempo for the band. Out-of-universe, character's drum parts are performed by studio musicians; voice actress Yoko Hikasa does not perform drums live (unlike Poppin'Party/Roselia/RAISE A SUILEN/Morfonica/MyGO!!!!!/Ave Mujica live bands).

Limit: No superhuman ability. Requires acoustic drum kit; not depicted as songwriter/composer (Ran and others lead writing). Live-action portrayal relies on backing musicians.

Interpersonal Mediation and Leadership

De facto older-sister figure for Afterglow and for Ako. Resolves disputes, organizes practice, and provides emotional support. Trusted by Tsugumi, Himari, Moca, and Ran to be honest broker.

Limit: Tends to internalize stress; reluctance to ask for help is framed as character flaw to overcome.

Domestic and Work Competence

Skilled at household tasks, cooking, and part-time work at family shop. Financially aware; saves and manages money, offers practical advice to Ako and Himari.

Limit: Grounded skill; not professional chef or manager.

Fashion Sense and Shopping

Noted fashion-conscious character; enjoys shopping and coordinating outfits, often advising Himari and Ako. Confident, mature aesthetic is part of her identity.

Limit: Hobby-level; not professional stylist.

Physical Presence and Stamina

Tall stature and physical stamina for drumming and carrying equipment. Depicted helping move gear and shopping bags.

Limit: Normal human limits; no combat abilities.

Accounts I
The Childhood Promise - Formation of AfterglowBanG Dream! Girls Band Party! Afterglow Band Story 0: 'We Are Afterglow' / Official Backstory Summary

2017 (depicting elementary to middle school, in-universe c. early 2010s)

Five childhood friends — Ran Mitake, Moca Aoba, Himari Uehara, Tomoe Udagawa, and Tsugumi Hazawa — who grew up together around Hazawa Coffee Shop, form a band in middle school to preserve their friendship as they anticipate attending different high schools. Tomoe, already drumming, joins as the rhythmic foundation. The story establishes Tomoe's role as the steady supporter who keeps the group grounded when Ran's aloofness and Himari's insecurities threaten cohesion. Establishes Afterglow's core identity as a 'same-as-always' childhood band whose appeal is authenticity rather than technical ambition; sets Tomoe's archetype as reliable anchor.

Afterglow, The Same As AlwaysBanG Dream! Girls Band Party! Afterglow Band Story Chapter 1

2017-2018 (in-game)

Afterglow struggles with being seen as merely a 'childhood friends band' while other bands have clearer goals. Tomoe mediates as Ran questions whether they should aim higher, and Himari worries about being left behind academically. Tomoe organizes extra practice and a small live to reaffirm that staying themselves is a valid path, emphasizing that their natural chemistry is their strength. Band reaffirms identity; Tomoe's leadership is highlighted as essential to preventing dissolution.

One of Us (Afterglow Band Story 2)BanG Dream! Girls Band Party! Afterglow Band Story Chapter 2: 'One of Us'

2018

Focus on Ran's temporary distance as she spends time with Yukina Minato (Roselia) and questions her own vocal identity. Tomoe notices Ran's absence affecting morale, especially Tsugumi's anxiety. Tomoe confronts Ran not with blame but by arranging a casual session at Hazawa Coffee Shop, reminding Ran that Afterglow is a place she can return to without performance pressure. Ran recommits to Afterglow while maintaining respect for Roselia; Tomoe's emotional intelligence is foregrounded.

Accounts II
Sister Beats - Tomoe and Ako's Sibling StoryGirls Band Party! Event Story 'Tomoe and Ako's Sister Audition' / 'A Small But Big Sister' (姉妹関連イベント) and Area Conversations

2018-2020 (multiple events)

Ako Udagawa idolizes Roselia and adopts a chuunibyou persona, often comparing herself unfavorably to tall, mature Tomoe. Tomoe worries she is overbearing or not supportive enough, while Ako fears she will never catch up. In event stories, Tomoe helps Ako prepare for Roselia practice, shares drum tips despite different bands, and learns to let Ako find her own path rather than solving problems for her. A recurring gag is Ako calling Tomoe 'Nee-chan' and Tomoe calling Ako 'Ako' with exasperated affection. Deepens Udagawa family lore; explains Tomoe's domestic responsibility and why she is protective; establishes that sisters are in different bands but supportive.

Girls Band Challenge - Afterglow on the Big StageBanG Dream! Anime Season 3 (2020) and Girls Band Party! Main Story Season 2

2020 (in-universe, second year of high school)

All Haneoka/Hanasakigawa/Tsukinomori bands compete in the Girls Band Challenge at Budokan-adjacent venues. Afterglow, initially hesitant about competing, decides to enter to prove their 'same as always' sound can stand alongside technically polished bands like Roselia and RAISE A SUILEN. Tomoe anchors live performance, visibly nervous but steady, and helps Tsugumi overcome stage fright. Afterglow does not win the grand prize (won by RAISE A SUILEN/Poppin'Party narrative), but earns audience recognition for authenticity. Positions Afterglow as respected mid-tier band with loyal fanbase; reinforces franchise theme that different goals are valid.

Graduation and the Future - Third-Year AnxietyGirls Band Party! Afterglow Band Story Chapter 3: 'Brave New Journey' / 2022-2023 events leading to third year

2022-2023 (in-game time progression to third year)

As Afterglow members enter their final year of high school, Tomoe voices quiet anxiety about whether the band can stay together after graduation when members consider different universities and jobs. Unlike earlier stories where she supports others, Tomoe is the one needing support, admitting she fears being left behind if she stays local to help family shop. Friends reassure her that Afterglow's bond transcends school, and they plan to continue regardless of paths. Matures Tomoe's character from supporter to someone who can be vulnerable; sets up post-high school continuity for franchise.

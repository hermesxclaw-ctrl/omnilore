Who It Is
Form

Yemaya is often depicted as a motherly figure, with a gentle and nurturing appearance. She is associated with the ocean and rivers, and is often shown with a crown or other regalia that reflects her status as a powerful deity.

Powers

Curing Infertility Yemaya is said to have the power to cure infertility in women, and is associated with the ability to bring life and fertility.

Protective Powers Yemaya is said to be protective of her children and is able to comfort and care for them in times of sorrow.

Abundance and Wealth Yemaya is associated with cowrie shells, which represent her wealth and abundance.

Accounts

Yemaya is said to have created the Ogun River, which is a major river in Nigeria. She is associated with the river and is said to have the power to control its waters.

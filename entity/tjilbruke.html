Who It Is
Form

Tjilbruke is often depicted as a powerful and wise ancestral figure, with a strong connection to the land and its creatures.

Powers

Creation and Protection Tjilbruke has the power to create and protect the land, its creatures, and the Kaurna people, ensuring their survival and well-being.

Accounts

Tjilbruke created the Tjilbruke Dreaming Trail, a major Dreaming trail that connects sites from within metropolitan Adelaide southwards as far as Cape Jervis, some of which are Aboriginal sacred sites of great significance.

Tjilbruke's tears formed springs along the coast, which became sacred sites for the Kaurna people.

Who It Is
Form

A shapeshifter, often depicted as a wolf-like creature with human features, or a human with wolf-like characteristics. In some stories, it is described as a monstrous creature with glowing eyes.

Powers

Supernatural Strength The Rougarou is said to possess incredible physical strength, allowing it to overpower even the strongest opponents.

Shape-shifting The Rougarou can transform into various forms, including a wolf-like creature, a human, or a combination of both.

Healing In some stories, the Rougarou is said to possess the ability to heal wounds and cure illnesses.

Accounts

A young woman is said to have been transformed into a Rougarou by a witch, and must navigate the dangers of the bayou to regain her human form.

A hunter is said to have encountered a Rougarou in the woods, and must use his wits and cunning to outsmart the creature.

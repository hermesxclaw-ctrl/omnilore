Aliases — Rougarou, Roux-Ga-Roux, Rugaroo, Rugaru, Loup Garou

Form & Signs

Appearance — A shapeshifter, often depicted as a wolf-like creature with human features, or a human with wolf-like characteristics. In some stories, it is described as a monstrous creature with glowing eyes.

Symbols — Associated with the full moon, often linked to witchcraft and sorcery. In some stories, it is said to possess supernatural strength and agility.

Domains — Rural areas, forests, and swamps of French Louisiana and surrounding regions.

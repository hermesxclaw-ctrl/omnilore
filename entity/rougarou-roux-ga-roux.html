Who It Is

A legendary werewolf-like creature in Cajun, Creole, and Métis Francophone folklore, descended from the French loup-garou (werewolf) tradition. In Louisiana bayou country, the rougarou is a shapeshifter with a human body and a wolf's head, said to prowl the swamps and forests. The standard lore holds that a person who breaks Lent for seven consecutive years transforms into a rougarou — a Catholic framework that ties the curse to religious transgression. In some variants, if a rougarou draws your blood, you become one. Parents invoke the rougarou to discipline children ('Behave, or the rougarou will get you!'). In Métis traditions, the rougarou is more of a trickster than a monster.

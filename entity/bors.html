Who It Is
Form

Bors is described as a young and chaste knight, often depicted as a loyal companion to King Arthur and Sir Lancelot.

Powers

Chastity Bors is known for his unwavering commitment to chastity, which serves as a source of strength and inspiration to those around him.

Accounts

Bors plays a significant role in the Grail Quest, accompanying King Arthur and Sir Lancelot on their perilous journey to find the Holy Grail.

Bors participates in the Battle of Badon, fighting alongside King Arthur and his knights against the invading forces of the Saxons.

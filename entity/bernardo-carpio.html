Who It Is

Bernardo Carpio is a legendary giant trapped between two mountains, endlessly straining to break free. Every earthquake is caused by him pushing the mountains apart. During the Philippine Revolution, he became a powerful metaphor for the Filipino nation — a giant of immense potential trapped by colonial mountains but endlessly struggling toward freedom.

Who It Is

In the dark corners of Destiny's lore, few figures loom as terrifyingly as Crota, the Hive prince whose name is whispered by Guardians as a warning—a god of the sword logic, whose very existence is a testament to the Hive's insatiable hunger for death. Born from the will of Oryx, the Taken King, Crota is not merely a monster but a cosmic principle made flesh: a being who feeds on annihilation and who once nearly brought the Last City to its knees.

Crota is characterized by a ruthless, single-minded drive to destroy and feed on Light. He follows the Sword Logic, a philosophy that existence is defined by the ability to kill. He is patient and cunning, often waiting for the right moment to strike. He shows no mercy and is utterly devoted to his father Oryx and the Hive's dark purpose.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Crota' is of Hive origin, though its precise linguistic roots are not documented. It is often associated with the Hive's dark, guttural language. 'Son of Oryx' indicates his lineage, as he is the offspring of Oryx, the Taken King.

Pronunciation — KROH-tah

Aliases — Crota, The Eater of Hope, The Hive Prince, Crota, the Taken King's Son, The Sword of Oryx

Powers
Sword Logic

A Hive philosophy that grants power through killing. Crota embodies this, gaining strength from each death he causes.

Limit: His power is tied to his ability to kill; if he is unable to kill, he weakens.

Oversoul

A massive, glowing soul that Crota can summon. It can wipe out all Guardians in his throne world unless destroyed.

Limit: The Oversoul can be destroyed by Guardians, and its creation requires a ritual.

Sword of Crota

A powerful sword that Crota wields, capable of cutting through the fabric of reality. It can be used by Guardians in the raid to damage him.

Limit: The sword is a physical object that can be lost or broken.

Hive Magic

Crota commands Hive arcane powers, including summoning minions, creating barriers, and manipulating dark energy.

Limit: Requires rituals and tribute to maintain.

Ascendant Realm

Crota exists in a throne world within the Ascendant Realm, a space where he can be killed only if his soul is destroyed.

Limit: If his soul is not destroyed, he can resurrect.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Crota is depicted as a massive Hive Knight, towering over Guardians. He has a chitinous, armored body with glowing green eyes and a menacing sword. In his raid, he appears as a giant Knight with a sword, later becoming a giant Oversoul. His form is associated with the Hive's dark, bone-like architecture.

Symbols — Crota is often depicted with a sword, symbolizing his role as a warrior and his adherence to the Sword Logic. His emblem is a stylized sword or a Hive rune. The Oversoul, a glowing green orb, is his ultimate weapon.

Domains — Crota's primary domain is the Moon, specifically the Hellmouth, where he was imprisoned after his initial assault on Earth. He also has a throne world within the Ascendant Realm, accessible through the raid. His influence extends to the Hive's dark rituals and the creation of the Oversoul.

Accounts I
The Fall of the MoonDestiny: The Dark Below

The Dark Below expansion (2014)

Crota and his Hive legions attacked the Moon and the Last City. He was eventually stopped by a Guardian fireteam who entered his throne world and slew him. The Hive were pushed back, but Crota's death set the stage for Oryx's arrival in The Taken King.

Crota was awakened by the Hive on the Moon, signaling his return and the beginning of The Dark Below. This event led to the Hive becoming more active and the threat to Earth.

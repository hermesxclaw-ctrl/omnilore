Who It Is

Crota is the son of Oryx, the Taken King, and one of the most feared Hive princes in the Destiny universe. He was responsible for the single greatest military disaster in Guardian history: the Great Disaster on the Moon, where he and his Hive legions slaughtered thousands of Guardians who attempted to push into the Hellmouth. Crota embodies the Hive's sword logic — the belief that existence is justified through violence — more purely than even his father. Where Oryx was a strategist, Crota is a weapon: a being of overwhelming brute force and endless hunger for destruction. Crota's power is his sword, which can cut through dimensions and kill Guardians permanently (by severing their connection to their Ghost). His throne world, Crota's End, is a pocket dimension within the Ascendant Plane where he can be truly killed only by completing a series of challenges that weaken him enough for a final confrontation. The defeat of Crota by a fireteam of six Guardians (in the Crota's End raid from The Dark Below expansion) was a pivotal moment in Destiny history — the first time the Hive suffered a major defeat and the event that set the stage for Oryx's vengeful arrival in The Taken King. Crota represents the mindless, savage face of the Hive — the brute force that exists to consume and destroy, without the philosophical depth of Oryx or the cunning of Savathûn. He is the monster in the dark, the endless army that lives beneath the surface of the Moon, waiting for the moment to rise and consume the Last City.

Who It Is

In the sprawling, monster-haunted dungeon city of Orario, where gods walk among mortals and adventurers chase glory, Haruhime Sanjōno stands as one of the most quietly powerful—and tragically constrained—figures. A renart with fox ears and a tail, she is a practitioner of a forbidden spell that can elevate a single warrior to superhuman heights. Yet her journey is not one of battle but of liberation: from a life of enforced servitude, she claws her way toward self-worth, becoming a symbol of resilience in a world that often grinds the vulnerable underfoot.

Haruhime is gentle, kind, and deeply empathetic, often putting others before herself. She is initially timid and lacking in self-confidence, a result of years of abuse and servitude. She speaks in a soft, polite manner, using honorifics even with close friends. Despite her trauma, she is brave and selfless, willing to sacrifice her own safety for those she cares about. She has a strong sense of gratitude and loyalty, especially toward Bell Cranel and the Hestia Familia, who rescued her. Powers
Level Boost (Kōtōsei)

A unique spell that temporarily raises the level of a single target by one, granting them a significant boost in all stats and abilities. The effect lasts for a limited time, typically a few minutes, and can be used on allies to enhance their combat capabilities. It is considered a forbidden spell because it artificially alters the natural progression of an adventurer's level, which is normally achieved through years of training and combat.

Limit: Can only be used on one target at a time; the caster cannot target herself; the duration is short; repeated use may cause exhaustion or mental strain on the caster; the spell requires a significant chant and concentration; it does not grant new skills or abilities, only a temporary stat increase.

Magic: 'Kōtōsei' (Level Boost) is her primary spell; she also has basic magical aptitude for minor spells, but she is not a combat-oriented mage.

Haruhime is capable of using basic magic, but her primary combat role is support. She lacks offensive spells and relies on her allies for defense.

Limit: She has no offensive magic; her physical abilities are below average for an adventurer; she is fragile and easily injured.

Renart Racial Traits

As a renart, Haruhime has enhanced senses, particularly hearing, and a natural agility. However, these traits are not prominently showcased in combat, as she avoids physical confrontation.

Limit: Not explicitly defined; she is not a frontline fighter.

Accounts I
The Courtesan of the Pleasure QuarterLight Novel Volume 8, Anime Season 2 Episode 6

Fictional setting (Orario)

Haruhime is born as a renart in a rural village, but due to her parents' debts, she is sold to a brothel in the Pleasure Quarter of Orario. She is trained as a high-class courtesan, but her life is one of servitude and exploitation. She is treated as property, and her magical talent is discovered by the brothel's owner, who uses her as a tool to enhance the abilities of wealthy clients. She lives in despair, believing she has no worth beyond her body and her spell. Haruhime's spirit is nearly broken, but she retains a glimmer of hope. Her situation becomes known to Bell Cranel and the Hestia Familia, who resolve to rescue her.

Accounts II
The Rescue by the Hestia FamiliaLight Novel Volume 9, Anime Season 2 Episodes 7-10

Fictional setting (Orario)

Bell Cranel, along with his familia members and allies, infiltrates the Pleasure Quarter to free Haruhime. They face opposition from the Ishtar Familia, who control the district. Bell and his party fight through the brothel, culminating in a confrontation with the Ishtar Familia's captain, Phryne Jamil. Haruhime, initially hesitant, is encouraged by Bell's unwavering kindness and decides to fight for her own freedom. She uses her Level Boost spell to aid Bell in the battle, and together they defeat their enemies. Haruhime is granted asylum in the Hestia Familia. Haruhime becomes a member of the Hestia Familia, gaining a new family and a sense of belonging. She begins to heal from her trauma and discovers a purpose as an adventurer and a person of worth.

Life as an AdventurerLight Novel Volumes 10+ (and subsequent arcs), Anime Season 3 and 4

Fictional setting (Orario)

After joining the Hestia Familia, Haruhime trains as a supporter and mage, focusing on her support magic. She participates in expeditions into the Dungeon, where her Level Boost proves invaluable. She forms close bonds with her new familia, especially with Bell, whom she grows to love romantically, though she keeps her feelings hidden. She also faces challenges, including dealing with her past resurfacing and learning to trust herself. Haruhime becomes a reliable and cherished member of the familia. Her character evolves from a passive victim to an active agent of her own life.

Who It Is

Leona Heidern is not just a soldier with a tragic past; she is a living weapon cursed by an ancient god, a quiet storm of ice and blood whose every move is a battle against the darkness within. In the world of The King of Fighters, she stands as a testament to the fragile line between humanity and monstrosity, a character whose silent strength and explosive fury have captivated players for decades.

Leona is quiet, reserved, and emotionally distant, a result of her traumatic past and fear of her curse. She is fiercely loyal to her team and adoptive father, but struggles with self-hatred and the fear of losing control. In battle, she is cold and efficient, but her Orochi form is wild and destructive.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Leona' is of Latin origin, meaning 'lioness', which may reflect her fierce and powerful nature. 'Heidern' is the surname of her adoptive father, given to her as a codename by SNK, though the official stance is that her only name is Leona.

Pronunciation — LEE-oh-nah HY-dern (English); Re-o-na Ha-i-de-ru-n (Japanese)

Aliases — Leona, Orochi Leona, Awakened Orochi Blood from the Darkness Leona, Rampaging Leona, Ice Queen

Ownership — Owned by SNK (Shin Nihon Kikaku), a Japanese video game company. The character is part of the global gaming culture, particularly within the fighting game community.

Powers
Riot of the Blood (Orochi Curse)

A curse that causes Leona to enter a berserk state, enhancing her physical abilities and granting her control over ice and blood. In this state, she becomes extremely aggressive and loses rational thought.

Limit: The curse is involuntary and can be triggered by intense emotion or external influence. It drains her energy and can cause her to harm allies. She has been working to control it, but it remains a constant threat.

Combat Skills

Leona is a highly trained soldier, proficient in hand-to-hand combat, knife fighting, and military tactics. She uses a combination of Heidern's techniques and her own unique moves.

Limit: No supernatural abilities when not using the curse.

Ice Manipulation

In her Orochi form, Leona can generate and control ice, using it for offensive attacks and to create defensive barriers.

Limit: Only available in her berserk state.

Blood Manipulation

Leona can manipulate her own blood to create weapons or projectiles, a power associated with the Orochi clan.

Limit: Only available in her berserk state.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Leona is a young woman with short, pale blue hair and red eyes. She typically wears a military-style uniform with a dark blue tank top, camouflage pants, and a red headband. In her Orochi form, her hair turns red, her skin becomes darker, and her eyes glow yellow. She often has a stoic, emotionless expression. Her design has evolved over time, with more detailed military gear in later games.

Symbols — Her symbol is the Ikari Team's emblem, representing her military allegiance. The red headband is a nod to Heidern. In her Orochi form, she is associated with the Orochi seal, and her attacks often involve ice and blood imagery.

Domains — As a soldier, she is often in military bases, battlefields, and tournament arenas. Her curse ties her to the supernatural realm of the Orochi, and she is often associated with dark, moonlit places.

Accounts I
The Curse of the Riot of the BloodThe King of Fighters '96 and '97 storyline, official SNK lore

1996-1997

As a child, Leona was cursed by Goenitz, a priest of the Orochi, with the Riot of the Blood. The curse caused her to go berserk and murder her own family. She was found by Heidern, who adopted her and trained her as a soldier. She joins the Ikari Team to fight in the King of Fighters tournament, but her curse resurfaces, causing her to attack her teammates and become a boss in KOF '97. After Orochi's defeat, she attempts suicide but is stopped by Ralf. Leona's guilt and fear of her curse define her character. She retires from tournaments to control her power, and her relationship with her team is strained but ultimately supportive.

Accounts II
The NESTS SagaThe King of Fighters '99, 2000, 2001

1999-2001

Leona investigates the NESTS cartel, a secret organization that manipulates fighters. She works alongside Ralf and Clark to uncover NESTS' plans and confront the clone K'. The saga expands Leona's role as a soldier and investigator, but her curse remains a background threat.

The Return of the CurseThe King of Fighters 2003 and later games

2003-2016

In KOF 2003, Leona loses control of her curse and attacks Ralf and Clark, leading her to retire from the tournament in KOF XI. She spends time training to control her power and returns in KOF XIII and XIV, where she has better control. Her character arc focuses on self-control and redemption.

Who It Is
Form

Connla is often depicted as a young man, fair and handsome, with a strong physical presence.

Powers

Bravery Connla is known for his bravery in the face of danger, and is often called upon to lead his people in times of war.

Accounts

Connla is the son of Cú Chulainn and Aífe, and is raised alone by his mother in Scotland. He is eventually sent to Ireland to meet his father, but is killed by his father's enemies.

Connla is a minor character in the story of Táin Bó Cúailnge, but plays an important role in the events that unfold.

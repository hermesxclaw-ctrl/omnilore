The Approach

In the cutthroat world of Lugunica's royal selection, where candidates vie for the throne with armies and alliances, one figure operates in the shadows—a young girl whose name is whispered only in the context of betrayal and survival. Meili Portroute, a seemingly unremarkable child, holds secrets that could unravel the very fabric of the Emilia camp's fragile peace.

Content advisory. Contains themes of child endangerment, manipulation, and psychological trauma. Some content may be unsettling for younger readers.
Identity

Meili Portroute

Pronunciation MAY-lee POR-troo-t

Known As

MeiliThe Girl Who Commands Mabeasts

Etymology

The name 'Meili' is likely derived from the English word 'mealy' or 'mellow', but no official etymology has been provided. 'Portroute' may be a play on 'port' (as in harbor) and 'route' (path), but this is unconfirmed.

Cultural Ownership

Fictional character from the Japanese light novel series Re:Zero, created by Tappei Nagatsuki. The character is part of the broader Re:Zero franchise, which is owned by Kadokawa Corporation and associated publishers.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans have speculated that Meili is connected to the Witch Cult due to her ability to control mabeasts, but this is not confirmed in canon. There is no official statement on her potential ties to other factions.

Description

Appearance

Meili is a young girl with short, light-colored hair and a small stature, typical of a child her age. She wears a hooded cloak that obscures her face, often keeping her identity hidden. Her eyes are described as sharp and calculating, belying her youthful appearance.

Symbols & Iconography

Meili is often associated with mabeasts, particularly the demon beasts she commands. She is also linked to the Witch Cult's symbol, though this is not always depicted in official art.

Behaviour & Personality

Meili is cunning, manipulative, and survival-oriented. She uses her innocent appearance to deceive others, but beneath that facade lies a ruthless pragmatist. She shows little remorse for her actions, viewing them as necessary for survival. She is also shown to be loyal to those who protect her, such as Elsa Granhiert.

Domains & Habitats

Meili is often found in Roswaal's mansion or the Sanctuary, where she operates as an agent of the Witch Cult. She is also known to travel through the wilderness, using her mabeast control to navigate dangerous areas.

Version Differences

In the light novel, Meili's role is more detailed, with additional scenes showing her interactions with other characters. In the anime, her appearances are more condensed, focusing on her actions during the Sanctuary arc. The manga follows the light novel closely, with minor visual differences.

Powers & Abilities

Mabeast Control

Source Version Light novel and anime

Description

Meili has the ability to command mabeasts, demonic creatures that are hostile to humans. She can summon, direct, and communicate with them, using them as tools for attack or espionage.

Limitations

The extent of her control is limited to certain types of mabeasts, and she may require a conduit or ritual to summon them. Her control is not absolute, as stronger or more independent mabeasts may resist.

Contradictions

Some sources suggest she inherited this ability from her mother, while others imply it was granted by the Witch Cult. No definitive explanation is given.

Stealth and Deception

Source Version Light novel and anime

Description

Meili is skilled at hiding, blending into crowds, and deceiving others with her innocent demeanor. She can infiltrate enemy camps and gather information without being detected.

Limitations

Her abilities are not supernatural; they rely on her cunning and the element of surprise. If exposed, she is physically vulnerable.

Contradictions

None noted.

Stories & Myths

The Incident at the Sanctuary

Source Re:Zero light novel, Arc 4Culture Japanese light novel culturePeriod Arc 4 (Sanctuary arc)

Summary

Meili is introduced as an agent of the Witch Cult, sent to infiltrate the Sanctuary and sabotage the barrier. She works alongside Elsa Granhiert, and together they cause chaos, threatening the lives of the residents and the Emilia camp. Meili uses her mabeast control to attack the camp, but she is eventually captured and interrogated by Subaru and his allies.

The Assassination Attempt on Crusch

Source Re:Zero light novel, Arc 3Culture Japanese light novel culturePeriod Arc 3 (Royal Selection arc)

Summary

Meili is involved in an assassination attempt on Crusch Karsten, a rival royal candidate. She uses mabeasts to attack Crusch's camp, but the attempt is thwarted by Subaru and his allies. This event is part of a larger conspiracy by the Witch Cult to destabilize the royal selection.

Relationships

Elsa Granhiert

Type ally/mentorSource Re:Zero light novel, Arc 4

Description

Meili and Elsa work together as agents of the Witch Cult. Elsa acts as a protector and mentor to Meili, and Meili is fiercely loyal to her. Their bond is one of mutual survival, as both are outcasts in society.

Version Note

In the anime, their relationship is less developed, but their partnership is evident.

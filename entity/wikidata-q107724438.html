Who It Is

He was not born a Manslayer. In the oldest telling, Pisaro — half-human, half-monster — wanted only to live quietly with the elf he loved. It was humanity that made him a demon, and then was horrified by what he became.

Attested (reconstructed from scripts): Initially reclusive, gentle, and idealistic despite being feared as a demon. Lived in exile at Rose Hill with Rose, an elf, avoiding human-monster conflict. Deeply loving and protective toward Rose; contemptuous of humans who persecute monsters, but not genocidal until after her murder. After humans kill Rose (tricked by his advisor Aamon), he becomes nihilistic, rage-driven, and convinced that humanity must be exterminated to end persecution. Powers
Secret of Evolution (Shinka no Hihou) - Self-Evolution

Use of the Secret of Evolution to forcibly evolve his own body through multiple stages, gaining size, strength, and new morphology. In game mechanics, this is represented as sequential boss forms with different stats and abilities.

Limit: Evolution is painful, unstable, and requires the artifact; early experiments (Estark) resulted in uncontrollable berserk state. In DS version, Aamon steals the Secret to evolve himself, implying Pisaro does not have exclusive control. Repeated evolution erodes his original personality.

Demon Lord Command and Monster Army

As Demon Lord (Maou), commands monsters and demon generals, including Pruslas, Rashaverak, and others. Can summon monsters in battle.

Limit: Loyalty is not absolute — Aamon betrays him. Post-recruitment (DS bonus), he loses command over demon armies. Cannot command monsters to harm Rose (personal ethic).

High-Level Magic and Breath Attacks

Attested boss moves: Kafrizzle (Merazoma), Kazam, Disruptive Wave (removes buffs), and dark breath attacks. In later games, learns Zam and ice/dark spells.

Limit: MP-limited in gameplay; Disruptive Wave has cooldown. Not immune to status effects in all forms (varies by form).

Superhuman Strength and Durability (Evolved Forms)

Final evolved form can withstand attacks from the Hero's party (the Chosen) and requires the full Zenithian equipment to defeat. Physical strikes deal 200+ damage in original stats.

Limit: Vulnerable to Zenithian Sword and holy/light damage. Each evolution has an elemental weakness (e.g., ice vs. fire forms).

Monster Taming and Synthesis (The Dark Prince)

As protagonist of The Dark Prince, can recruit, train, and synthesize monsters (combine two monsters to create stronger ones), reflecting his half-monster heritage and empathy for monsters.

Limit: Cursed by his father Rank: cannot directly harm monsters with his own hands, must rely on synthesized allies. Synthesis requires specific monster combinations and levels.

Curse of Inability to Harm Monsters (The Dark Prince)

Magical curse placed by his father: any monster he attempts to strike is protected, forcing non-violent solutions.

Limit: Curse is lifted late in the story after confronting his father, allowing direct combat. Narratively used to enforce monster-tamer gameplay.

Accounts I
The Exile at Rose Hill - Love Between Demon and ElfDragon Quest IV: Chapters of the Chosen (1990), Chapter 5 dialogue; expanded in DS remake Party Chat and official novelization

1990 (Heisei era game fiction; in-universe: age of Zenithia)

Pisaro, born half-human half-monster and feared by both, exiles himself to a secluded tower at Rose Hill with Rose, a kind elf who loves him unconditionally. He abandons ambition and lives quietly, tending roses and studying monster ecology. Rose's song calms monsters and she is the only being who sees his gentleness. This idyll establishes his original non-violent character before tragedy. Establishes Pisaro's sympathetic motive; Rose's later death becomes the inciting incident for his transformation into the Manslayer. Provides moral ambiguity — he is not innately evil.

The Murder of Rose and the Birth of DespisaroDragon Quest IV Chapter 5 (1990); DS remake Chapter 5-6

1990 / 2007 expanded

Pisaro's advisor, the scheming demon Aamon, manipulates humans from a nearby village into believing Rose's tears (which can cure illness) require killing her. Humans attack Rose Hill and murder Rose while Pisaro is away. Pisaro returns to find her dead, his grief and rage triggering his decision to use the Secret of Evolution to become strong enough to exterminate humanity, which he now sees as irredeemably cruel. He declares himself Despisaro, the Manslayer. Pisaro's alignment shift from recluse to genocidal Demon Lord; he seizes the Secret of Evolution, evolves, and builds his demon army. Aamon's betrayal is later revealed as intentional to usurp Pisaro.

The Secret of Evolution and the Creation of EstarkDragon Quest IV (1990) - lore via NPCs in the Secret Laboratory; Dragon Quest V (1992) crossover lore

1990

Before perfecting evolution on himself, Pisaro experiments on monsters using the Secret of Evolution. His most notorious creation is Estark, an immensely powerful, mindless demon who rampages uncontrollably and must be sealed by Pisaro's forces. Estark's failure teaches Pisaro that evolution without control is dangerous, yet he proceeds to use it on himself anyway, believing his will is stronger. Estark becomes a recurring superboss in Dragon Quest IV, V, and Monsters series, separate from Pisaro. Establishes Pisaro as a scientist/mage, not merely a brute. Explains why sealing Estark is a prerequisite to facing Pisaro in some versions.

Accounts II
The Battle Against the Chosen - Six EvolutionsDragon Quest IV final dungeon - Despisaro's Castle (1990)

1990

The Hero (protagonist of Dragon Quest IV) and the Chosen (Ragnar, Alena, Borya, Kiryl, Meena, Maya, Torneko) ascend to Despisaro's Castle and confront Pisaro. He transforms sequentially through six forms, each more monstrous, in a marathon boss battle. Despite his power, the party — aided by the Zenithian Sword, Shield, Helm, and Armor — defeats him. In the original ending, he dies cursing humanity. Canonical defeat of the Demon Lord; restores peace. In original version, this is his end. Sets up later retcons where he survives.

Redemption and Recruitment - The Bonus ChapterDragon Quest IV PlayStation (2001) and Nintendo DS (2007) - Post-game Chapter 6 'The Chosen Ones' / 'Fungeon'

2001-2007

After the main story, if the player revisits the world, they discover Aamon has stolen the Secret of Evolution and become the true final boss (Aamon / Psaro's Pawn). Pisaro, weakened and disillusioned after his defeat and learning of Aamon's manipulation in Rose's death, joins the Hero's party as a playable character. He seeks atonement and revenge against Aamon. Together they defeat Aamon in his evolved form. Pisaro then leaves to wander, seeking a new purpose. Reframes Pisaro from irredeemable villain to tragic anti-hero capable of redemption. Establishes Aamon as the greater evil. This version is now considered the definitive canon by Square Enix and is the basis for The Dark Prince.

The Dark Prince - A New Origin (Half-Human, Half-Monster Curse)Dragon Quest Monsters: The Dark Prince (2023)

2023

Retelling from Pisaro's perspective: He is the son of the monster king Rank and human Miriam. Rank curses him so he cannot harm monsters, forcing him into the life of a monster tamer who must befriend rather than fight. He journeys through Nadiria and the human world, befriending monsters, uncovering his father's cruelty and Aamon's schemes, and ultimately confronting his lineage. Rose appears as his companion and moral anchor, recontextualizing their love as a choice rather than isolation. Provides a full origin not present in 1990 game, making Pisaro a heroic-legend archetype rather than pure villain. Serves as prequel/interquel to Dragon Quest IV. Commercially and critically repositions Pisaro as franchise face, similar to Dragon Quest XI's Hero.

Pisaro is a purely evil demon lord like other Dragon Quest bosses. — He is half-human, originally gentle and reclusive, and becomes the Manslayer only after the murder of his lover Rose. Modern canon emphasizes his tragedy and capacity for redemption.

Pisaro and Estark are the same character. — Estark is a separate demon created by Pisaro's early failed evolution experiments. Pisaro later seals Estark because he is uncontrollable. They appear as distinct bosses, though both use evolution.

Pisaro's name comes from Francisco Pizarro and he is based on the conquistador. — No creator statement links the name to the historical figure. The katakana ピサロ could coincide, but Horii and Toriyama have never cited Pizarro as inspiration; the name is likely chosen for exotic, demon-lord sound.

Pisaro is the final boss of every Dragon Quest game he appears in. — He is final boss only in Dragon Quest IV (and true final boss Aamon surpasses him in remakes). In Monsters: The Dark Prince he is the hero; in Heroes II, Tact, and Smash Bros. he is a playable unit/spirit, not a boss.

The Secret of Evolution is a sword or weapon Pisaro wields. — It is an alchemical/magical research — depicted as a scroll, formula, or egg-like artifact — that forcibly evolves monsters. Pisaro uses it on himself and others, not as a melee weapon.

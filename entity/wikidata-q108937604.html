Who It Is

In the dappled half-light of Lodoss's Forest of No Return, where high elves live centuries and remember grudges longer, the name Dedoldia carries weight — and Gyes Dedoldia carries it as a shaman who speaks to wind and flame rather than drawing a sword. He is not the Lodoss hero whose statue stands in market squares, but one of the forest's own: a spirit-caller whose story is told in fragments across novels, replays, and animation cels.

Attested: Archetype heroic-legend suggests alignment with Lodoss heroic ethos (courage, loyalty to companions, opposition to evil forces such as Marmo and dark sorcery). As a Dedoldia, likely shares cultural values of forest preservation, long memory, and wariness toward humans, as established for the clan in primary novels. Reconstructed: Elven shamanic worldview — respect for elemental spirits (sylphs, salamanders, undines, gnomes), ritual negotiation rather than coercive summoning. Powers
Shamanic Spirit Magic (Seirei Mahō)

In Lodoss, shamans contract with elemental spirits — Sylph (wind), Salamander (fire), Undine (water), Gnome (earth) — and higher spirits. Ability to summon spirits for attack, defense, healing, and utility by negotiating rather than commanding. Dedoldia clan members are canonically shamanic practitioners (Deedlit is a noted shaman). By clan affiliation, Gyes is inferred to possess shamanic capability, but no verified spell list or spirit pact specific to Gyes was located.

Limit: Lodoss shamanism requires spirit consent, suitable environment (e.g., sylphs weak underground, gnomes weak at sea), and incantation time; spirits can refuse or be banished by opposing magic. Exact limitations for Gyes unknown.

Longevity and Elven Senses

High elves in Lodoss live 300-500+ years, retain youth for centuries, possess acute hearing, low-light vision, and forest stealth. Standard racial traits applied to all Dedoldia elves.

Limit: Not immortal; can be killed by weapons, disease, or dark magic. Extended lifespan creates cultural distance from short-lived humans.

Forest Survival and Lore

Knowledge of Forest of No Return — paths, spirit locales, ancient elven history, and isolationist customs. Likely skilled in tracking, herbalism, and elven etiquette.

Limit: Knowledge is geographically bounded; less effective outside forest without spirit support.

Accounts I
The Forest of No Return — Clan Foundation (Backstory Layer)Mizuno, Ryo. Record of Lodoss War novels — worldbuilding chapters on Kaerazuno Mori

In-world: centuries before War of the Demon King; publication 1988-1989

The Dedoldia clan, high elves who withdrew to the Forest of No Return after ancient wars with dark forces, maintain shamanic traditions and guard the forest's spirit balance. Gyes belongs to this lineage, whose history includes the schism between forest elves and those who left for human lands. This is franchise lore, not a Gyes-centered plot, but provides his origin context. Establishes cultural isolation and shamanic identity that defines any Dedoldia character's motives during later wars.

The Grey Witch (Haiiro no Majo) CampaignMizuno, Ryo. Record of Lodoss War Vol.1 Haiiro no Majo (1988); OVA Episodes 1-8

In-world: Lodoss War of the Demon King era; publication 1988-1990

Parn's party seeks to counter the machinations of Karla the Grey Witch, who manipulates balance between Marmo and Lodoss kingdoms. Forest elves, including Dedoldia members, are drawn into wider conflict as dark forces threaten the island. Gyes's specific actions in this campaign were not verified in English summaries — he may appear as part of elven contingent or be referenced in clan context — but the Grey Witch arc is the primary narrative where Dedoldia elves intersect the main plot. Sets Lodoss balance-of-power theme; introduces shamanic magic as counter to Karla's sorcery.

The Fire Demon (Honoo no Majin) and Subsequent NovelsMizuno, Ryo. Record of Lodoss War Vols.2-7 (1989-1993)

Publication 1989-1993

Continuing wars against Marmo's forces, including the fire demon and later resurgent darkness. Elven shamans are called upon for spirit magic against elemental threats. No verified Gyes-specific feat was extracted, but the novel sequence is the canonical continuation where any Dedoldia character would plausibly appear in battle or council scenes. Expands Lodoss cosmology of light vs darkness and role of spirit magic.

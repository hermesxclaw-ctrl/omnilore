Who It Is
Form

The Lusca is often described as a giant octopus or cephalopod, with a massive body and long tentacles. It is said to inhabit the blue holes and waters surrounding Andros Island in the Bahamas.

Powers

Massive Strength The Lusca is said to possess incredible strength, capable of dragging a boat or even a small island into the depths of the ocean.

Accounts

A Bahamian folktale about the Lusca, a fearsome blue hole monster myth from Andros Island, warning fishermen to respect the ocean.

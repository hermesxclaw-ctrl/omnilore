Who It Is

Khorne is the Chaos God of war, rage, murder, and bloodshed — the most powerful and the most straightforward of the four Great Chaos Gods. In the Warhammer 40,000 universe, the Chaos Gods are not gods in the traditional sense but sentient warp entities born from and sustained by the emotions of sentient beings across the galaxy. Khorne is the embodiment of every act of violence, every drop of blood shed in anger, every battle fought. Khorne is not a subtle god. He does not scheme, deceive, or corrupt through temptation. He offers a simple bargain: shed blood in his name, and he will grant you power. His followers — the World Eaters Chaos Space Marines, Khorne Berzerkers, and daemons — are mindless berserkers who charge into battle with chainaxes and prayers to the Blood God. Khorne cares not from whence the blood flows, only that it flows. He accepts the blood of friend and foe alike, and his followers are as likely to turn on each other as on the enemy. Khorne's realm in the Warp is the Brass Citadel, a fortress of impossible size made of brass and bone, where his throne of skulls sits — a mountain of skulls that grows with every battle fought in the material universe. His daemons include Bloodletters (armed with hellblades), Bloodcrushers (mounted on daemonic juggernauts), and the gigantic Bloodthirsters — Greater Daemons of Khorne who are living engines of destruction. Khorne loathes sorcery and psychic powers, considering them the tricks of cowards. A follower of Khorne does not cast spells — he charges with an axe and trusts in the Blood God. Khorne represents the terrifying simplicity of pure violence. He is not a god of strategy or conquest — he is the god of the moment of impact, the instant when blade meets flesh, the primal scream of battle. He does not care who wins or loses. He only cares that the blood flows. The Khorne philosophy is brutally simple: kill. And never stop killing.

The Approach

In the blood-soaked annals of the Warhammer 40,000 universe, one name resounds with primal fury: Khorne, the Blood God, the Lord of Skulls, the Master of War. He is not a distant deity but a raw, malevolent force that feeds on the violence and slaughter of the galaxy's endless conflicts, demanding a tithe of blood and skulls from all who would seek his favor. To understand Khorne is to confront the grimdark heart of the setting itself, where war is not a means to an end but the only truth.

Content advisory. Depictions of extreme violence, gore, and warfare. Khorne is a fictional entity representing unfettered rage and bloodshed, and his worship in the setting is presented as a destructive and corrupting force.

Etymology

The name 'Khorne' is derived from the English word 'corn' (as in a corn of wheat), metaphorically referencing the harvest of skulls and blood. It was created by Games Workshop co-founder Bryan Ansell and first appeared in the early 1980s in the game 'Realm of Chaos'. The name is also reminiscent of the Celtic god 'Cernunnos' and the Hindu goddess 'Kali', though no direct connection is intended.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Games Workshop for the Warhammer Fantasy and Warhammer 40,000 settings. Khorne is a fictional entity owned by Games Workshop, and his lore is developed across multiple editions and publications.

Disputed Identifications

Some fans have speculated that Khorne is inspired by real-world deities of war and bloodshed, such as the Norse god Týr or the Hindu goddess Kali, but Games Workshop has never confirmed any direct inspiration. Within the setting, Khorne is a distinct entity of the Warp, not a reimagining of any real-world god.

Description

Appearance

Khorne is a colossal, monstrous entity of the Warp, often depicted as a massive, muscular humanoid with brass armor, a helm resembling a snarling beast, and a great sword or axe. His skin is a deep, blood-red, and his eyes burn with malevolent fire. He is typically shown seated upon a throne of skulls, surrounded by rivers of blood and the souls of the slain. In art, he is often shown with a bestial, snarling face, and his form is wreathed in the crimson energies of the Warp.

Symbols & Iconography

Khorne's primary symbols include the eight-pointed star (the Star of Chaos) combined with a stylized skull, often with a brass or metallic coloration. His colors are red, brass, and black. His followers often wear skulls as trophies and adorn their armor with brass spikes and chains. The symbol of Khorne is the 'Khorne Mark' – a stylized skull with horns, often depicted on banners and armor.

Behaviour & Personality

Khorne is the embodiment of mindless rage, violence, and martial honor. He is not subtle or cunning; he despises sorcery and psychic powers, viewing them as cowardly and dishonorable. He is driven by an insatiable thirst for blood and a desire to claim the skulls of the fallen. He rewards those who kill in his name with martial prowess and daemonic gifts. He is also a god of martial pride and strength, and his followers often adhere to a code of honor that values direct confrontation and personal combat.

Domains & Habitats

Khorne resides in the Warp, in a realm known as the 'Blood God's domain' or the 'Skull Throne'. This realm is a nightmarish landscape of burning plains, rivers of blood, and mountains of skulls. It is a place of constant warfare, where daemons of Khorne fight endlessly for his amusement. In the material universe, Khorne's influence is strongest in areas of intense conflict and bloodshed, and his daemons can manifest where violence is rampant.

Version Differences

In the Warhammer Fantasy setting, Khorne is depicted similarly, but the context is different – he is one of the four Chaos Gods in a high-fantasy world. In Warhammer 40,000, he is a Warp entity, a psychic manifestation of the collective emotions of sentient beings. The core characteristics are consistent across both settings, but the lore and the scale of his influence differ. In 40K, Khorne is also associated with the Chaos Legions, such as the World Eaters, who have dedicated themselves to him.

Powers & Abilities

Warp Entity

Source Version All editions

Description

Khorne is a Chaos God, a powerful Warp entity that exists outside of the material universe. He is not bound by physical laws and can influence the material realm through his daemons and the actions of his followers. He is empowered by the emotions of rage, violence, and bloodlust from mortal beings.

Limitations

Khorne cannot directly manifest in the material universe for extended periods; he acts through his daemons and champions. His influence is strongest in the Warp and in areas of extreme violence.

Contradictions

Some sources suggest that Khorne's power is limited by the balance of power among the Chaos Gods, but this is not consistently depicted.

Daemonic Hordes

Source Version All editions

Description

Khorne commands legions of daemons, including Bloodletters, Bloodcrushers, Flesh Hounds, and the mighty Bloodthirsters. These daemons are extensions of his will and are unleashed upon the material universe to wage war and claim skulls.

Limitations

Daemons require a sufficient amount of Warp energy or a ritual to manifest in the material realm. They can be banished by powerful psychic or holy means.

Contradictions

None noted.

Blessings and Gifts

Source Version All editions

Description

Khorne grants his faithful warriors supernatural abilities, such as increased strength, endurance, and ferocity. These gifts are often manifested as physical mutations, such as the 'Khorne's Fury' or the 'Gorechild' axe. Champions of Khorne may be granted daemonic power, eventually becoming Daemon Princes.

Limitations

The gifts are often accompanied by a loss of sanity and a growing obsession with violence. Khorne despises sorcery, so he rarely grants psychic powers to his followers.

Contradictions

Some lore suggests that Khorne's gifts are a form of corruption that ultimately consumes the recipient, while other sources portray them as honourable rewards.

Psychic Nullification

Source Version All editions

Description

Khorne is a bane to psykers and sorcerers. His presence can disrupt psychic powers, and his followers are often resistant to psychic attacks. His daemons are particularly effective against psychic enemies.

Limitations

This ability is not absolute; powerful psykers can still affect his followers, and Khorne's own daemons are not entirely immune to psychic attacks.

Contradictions

Some sources depict Khorne as actively hunting psykers, while others show him as merely indifferent.

Stories & Myths

The Fall of the World Eaters

Source The Horus Heresy seriesCulture Warhammer 40,000Period 31st Millennium (Horus Heresy)

Summary

The World Eaters Legion, led by Primarch Angron, was a highly disciplined and effective fighting force before the Horus Heresy. Angron, however, was a tortured soul, implanted with the Butcher's Nails – devices that caused him immense pain and rage. During the Heresy, Angron and his Legion were corrupted by Khorne, embracing their bloodlust and becoming savage berserkers. The Legion was eventually cursed to be forever consumed by rage, earning the name 'World Eaters' and becoming one of the most feared Chaos Legions.

The World Eaters became a host of mindless killers, losing much of their tactical sophistication. Angron himself was elevated to a Daemon Prince of Khorne, becoming a monstrous being of pure rage.

Variants

The exact details of Angron's fall and the Legion's corruption vary across different novels and codexes, but the core narrative is consistent.

The Rise of Kharn the Betrayer

Source Various novels and codexesCulture Warhammer 40,000Period 31st Millennium to 41st Millennium

Summary

Kharn was once a noble captain of the World Eaters, but after the Heresy, he became a champion of Khorne, known for his unmatched brutality and his habit of killing allies and enemies alike. He is often seen as the embodiment of Khorne's martial fury, a warrior who has slaughtered countless beings in the name of the Blood God.

Source Codex: Chaos DaemonsCulture Warhammer 40,000Period Eternal

Khorne sits upon a throne made of the skulls of his enemies, located in his realm in the Warp. This throne is a symbol of his power and his endless hunger for skulls. It is said that when the universe ends, Khorne will have collected the skulls of all beings.

Butcher's Nails

Source The Horus Heresy series

Description

The World Eaters were implanted with the Butcher's Nails, devices that caused extreme pain and rage, effectively lobotomizing them into berserkers. This was a form of torture and mutilation that stripped them of their humanity.

Scholarly Context

Within the setting, this is a tragic backstory that explains the Legion's fall, but it is also a stark example of the grimdark nature of the universe.

Skull-taking

Source Various codexes and novels

Description

Followers of Khorne engage in the ritualized taking of skulls and blood, often from defeated enemies. This is a form of trophy-taking that is considered holy to Khorne and is a central practice of his cult.

Scholarly Context

This practice is depicted as brutal and dehumanizing, but it is also a core part of Khorne's identity and his followers' devotion.

Blood sacrifices

Source Various codexes and novels

Description

Khorne's followers often perform blood sacrifices to gain his favor, killing victims in his name. These sacrifices can be prisoners, enemies, or even allies, and are often carried out in the most violent and bloody manner possible.

Scholarly Context

This is a common trope in dark fantasy, and in the Warhammer 40,000 setting, it is a grim reflection of the corrupting influence of Chaos.

Contradictions & Disputes

Khorne's disdain for sorcery

Position A

Khorne despises all forms of psychic powers and sorcery, and his followers are forbidden from using them.

Position A — evidence

Codex: Chaos Daemons and numerous novels depict Khorne's followers as shunning psychic powers.

Position B

Some lore suggests that Khorne's disdain is not absolute, and that he may tolerate or even use sorcery in certain circumstances, such as through the use of daemon weapons.

Position B — evidence

In some novels, Khorne's champions have used daemon weapons that have sorcerous properties, and there are instances of Khorne's daemons using psychic attacks.

Scholarly Consensus

The general consensus is that Khorne's hatred of sorcery is a core trait, but there are exceptions in the lore, likely for gameplay or narrative purposes.

Khorne's nature as a god of honor

Position A

Khorne is a god of martial honor and personal combat, rewarding those who face their enemies directly.

Position A — evidence

Some lore describes Khorne's followers as adhering to a code of honor, refusing to fight those who are unarmed or retreating.

Position B

Khorne is simply a god of mindless slaughter, with no concept of honor or fairness.

Position B — evidence

Other lore depicts Khorne's followers as indiscriminate killers who will slaughter anyone in their path, including civilians and allies.

Scholarly Consensus

This is a point of debate among fans and within the lore. Some sources emphasize Khorne's martial honor, while others focus on his mindless rage. The truth likely lies in between: Khorne values combat and strength, but his influence can drive his followers to extreme acts of violence.

Who It Is

In the blood-soaked annals of the Warhammer 40,000 universe, one name resounds with primal fury: Khorne, the Blood God, the Lord of Skulls, the Master of War. He is not a distant deity but a raw, malevolent force that feeds on the violence and slaughter of the galaxy's endless conflicts, demanding a tithe of blood and skulls from all who would seek his favor. To understand Khorne is to confront the grimdark heart of the setting itself, where war is not a means to an end but the only truth.

Khorne is the embodiment of mindless rage, violence, and martial honor. He is not subtle or cunning; he despises sorcery and psychic powers, viewing them as cowardly and dishonorable. He is driven by an insatiable thirst for blood and a desire to claim the skulls of the fallen. He rewards those who kill in his name with martial prowess and daemonic gifts. He is also a god of martial pride and strength, and his followers often adhere to a code of honor that values direct confrontation and personal combat.

Powers
Warp Entity

Khorne is a Chaos God, a powerful Warp entity that exists outside of the material universe. He is not bound by physical laws and can influence the material realm through his daemons and the actions of his followers. He is empowered by the emotions of rage, violence, and bloodlust from mortal beings.

Limit: Khorne cannot directly manifest in the material universe for extended periods; he acts through his daemons and champions. His influence is strongest in the Warp and in areas of extreme violence.

Daemonic Hordes

Khorne commands legions of daemons, including Bloodletters, Bloodcrushers, Flesh Hounds, and the mighty Bloodthirsters. These daemons are extensions of his will and are unleashed upon the material universe to wage war and claim skulls.

Limit: Daemons require a sufficient amount of Warp energy or a ritual to manifest in the material realm. They can be banished by powerful psychic or holy means.

Blessings and Gifts

Khorne grants his faithful warriors supernatural abilities, such as increased strength, endurance, and ferocity. These gifts are often manifested as physical mutations, such as the 'Khorne's Fury' or the 'Gorechild' axe. Champions of Khorne may be granted daemonic power, eventually becoming Daemon Princes.

Limit: The gifts are often accompanied by a loss of sanity and a growing obsession with violence. Khorne despises sorcery, so he rarely grants psychic powers to his followers.

Psychic Nullification

Khorne is a bane to psykers and sorcerers. His presence can disrupt psychic powers, and his followers are often resistant to psychic attacks. His daemons are particularly effective against psychic enemies.

Limit: This ability is not absolute; powerful psykers can still affect his followers, and Khorne's own daemons are not entirely immune to psychic attacks.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Khorne is a colossal, monstrous entity of the Warp, often depicted as a massive, muscular humanoid with brass armor, a helm resembling a snarling beast, and a great sword or axe. His skin is a deep, blood-red, and his eyes burn with malevolent fire. He is typically shown seated upon a throne of skulls, surrounded by rivers of blood and the souls of the slain. Symbols — Khorne's primary symbols include the eight-pointed star (the Star of Chaos) combined with a stylized skull, often with a brass or metallic coloration. His colors are red, brass, and black. His followers often wear skulls as trophies and adorn their armor with brass spikes and chains. The symbol of Khorne is the 'Khorne Mark' – a stylized skull with horns, often depicted on banners and armor.

Domains — Khorne resides in the Warp, in a realm known as the 'Blood God's domain' or the 'Skull Throne'. This realm is a nightmarish landscape of burning plains, rivers of blood, and mountains of skulls. It is a place of constant warfare, where daemons of Khorne fight endlessly for his amusement. Accounts I
The Fall of the World EatersThe Horus Heresy series

31st Millennium (Horus Heresy)

The World Eaters Legion, led by Primarch Angron, was a highly disciplined and effective fighting force before the Horus Heresy. Angron, however, was a tortured soul, implanted with the Butcher's Nails – devices that caused him immense pain and rage. During the Heresy, Angron and his Legion were corrupted by Khorne, embracing their bloodlust and becoming savage berserkers. The Legion was eventually cursed to be forever consumed by rage, earning the name 'World Eaters' and becoming one of the most feared Chaos Legions. The World Eaters became a host of mindless killers, losing much of their tactical sophistication. Angron himself was elevated to a Daemon Prince of Khorne, becoming a monstrous being of pure rage.

Accounts II
The Rise of Kharn the BetrayerVarious novels and codexes

31st Millennium to 41st Millennium

Kharn was once a noble captain of the World Eaters, but after the Heresy, he became a champion of Khorne, known for his unmatched brutality and his habit of killing allies and enemies alike. He is often seen as the embodiment of Khorne's martial fury, a warrior who has slaughtered countless beings in the name of the Blood God. Kharn became one of the most infamous Chaos Champions in the galaxy, a figure of terror and a symbol of Khorne's power.

The Skull ThroneCodex: Chaos Daemons

Eternal

Khorne sits upon a throne made of the skulls of his enemies, located in his realm in the Warp. This throne is a symbol of his power and his endless hunger for skulls. It is said that when the universe ends, Khorne will have collected the skulls of all beings. The Skull Throne is a central symbol in Khorne's lore, representing his ultimate goal and his insatiable desire for conquest.

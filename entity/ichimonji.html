Who It Is

In the world of Bleach, few weapons are as conceptually terrifying as Ichimonji, the sealed form of the Zanpakutō wielded by Ichibē Hyōsube, the leader of the Royal Guard. This seemingly ordinary giant calligraphy brush is not merely a tool for writing—it is an instrument that can rename and thereby redefine the very nature of anything it touches, making it one of the most potent and esoteric abilities in the series.

Ichimonji does not have a distinct personality, but it is deeply tied to Ichibē's own demeanor—calm, wise, and somewhat playful. Ichibē treats it as an extension of himself and uses it with precise, deliberate strokes.

Powers
Shikai: Ichimonji

In shikai, Ichimonji becomes a large brush that can write in the air. The black ink it produces can be used to 'name' anything it touches, granting Ichibē control over that object or being. For example, he can rename an opponent to 'Black Ant' and then crush them, or rename a target to 'the weakest' and render them powerless.

Limit: The ink must physically touch the target to take effect. The naming ability requires Ichibē to know the target's original name. The effects can be resisted by sufficiently powerful beings, as seen with Yhwach.

Bankai: Shirafude Ichimonji

The bankai takes the form of a white brush that paints over the target, erasing their name and the power associated with it. The target loses their identity and abilities, becoming a being without a name. Ichibē can then 'rename' them to something weak, effectively defeating them.

Limit: The bankai requires Ichibē to be within close range. The erasure is temporary; if the target regains their name, they regain their power. The ability is ineffective against beings who do not rely on names or who have transcended the concept of naming, such as Yhwach.

Name Manipulation

A passive ability of Ichimonji, allowing Ichibē to perceive and manipulate the names of all things. He can rename anything, including abstract concepts, as demonstrated when he renames Yhwach's power to 'Black Ant'.

Limit: The manipulation is limited to names and their associated meanings. It cannot alter physical reality directly, only the conceptual nature of the target.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In its sealed form, Ichimonji appears as a massive calligraphy brush with a dark handle and a white bristle tip, roughly the size of a person. In shikai, it transforms into a larger brush with a black blade-like bristle that can write in the air. Its bankai, Shirafude Ichimonji, manifests as a white brush that paints over the target, removing their name and power.

Symbols — The brush symbolizes the power of naming and writing, drawing on the Japanese tradition of kotodama (spirit of words). The black ink of shikai represents the power to bestow names, while the white ink of bankai represents the power to erase them.

Domains — Ichimonji exists within the Soul Society, specifically in the Royal Palace where Ichibē resides. It is not tied to any specific realm beyond that.

Confrontation with YhwachBleach manga, chapters 604-610

Thousand-Year Blood War arc (2014-2015)

When Yhwach invades the Royal Palace, Ichibē confronts him. Using Ichimonji's shikai, Ichibē renames Yhwach's power to 'Black Ant' and attempts to crush him. However, Yhwach reveals his ability to see the future and alter it, allowing him to restore his power and counterattack. Ichibē then activates his bankai, Shirafude Ichimonji, which erases Yhwach's name and power, reducing him to a being without a name. Ichibē renames him to 'Black Ant' and prepares to finish him, but Yhwach's Almighty ability allows him to revive himself and regain his powers, ultimately defeating Ichibē. Ichibē is defeated and killed, but later revived by Yhwach for his own purposes. The battle demonstrates the limits of Ichimonji's power against the Almighty.

Who It Is

Pre-War military assault robots designed by RobCo Industries. Assaultrons are sleek, humanoid robots with a single red eye, retractable arm-blades, and a devastating head-mounted laser. They are programmed with advanced combat AI and can execute complex tactical maneuvers including stealth, coordinated fire, and melee-ranged switching. Their 'Assaultron Dominator' variant can cloak and deliver lethal sneak attacks.

Powers

Retractable arm blades (melee combo)

Who It Is

The first SCP ever documented. A concrete, rebar, and Krylon-brand spray paint sculpture that is animate and extremely hostile. SCP-173 can only move when not being directly observed — a snap of the neck occurs if line of sight is broken for even a moment. It attacks by snapping the victim's neck at the base of the skull. It produces a scraping sound when moving and leaves a trail of blood and feces (neither its own).

Form

A crude sculpture approximately 2 meters tall, constructed from concrete and rebar, crudely spray-painted in various colors. It resembles a simplified humanoid figure with stubby limbs and a large, vaguely comical head. No facial features.

Powers

Absolute immobility while observed

Instant lethal movement when unobserved

Neck-snapping attack

Sanitary anomaly (produces blood/feces)

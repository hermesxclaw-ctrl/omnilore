Who It Is

The name 'Fiora' blooms across worlds: a river in Italy, a Berlin-based composer, and a sword-wielding duelist in the digital arenas of Runeterra. Each Fiora is a distinct entity, yet all share a root in the Latin word for flower. This dossier focuses on the fictional characters who bear the name, from the gentle healer of Xenoblade Chronicles to the proud grand duelist of League of Legends, and the many others who flit through novels, films, and operas.

Xenoblade Fiora is kind and caring; League Fiora is proud, confident, and obsessed with dueling; Aura is playful but deadly.

Powers
Healing and support

Xenoblade Fiora can heal allies and use ether-based attacks.

Limit: Limited by ether supply and cooldowns.

Master duelist

League Fiora is a highly skilled fencer with abilities that allow her to strike weak points and parry attacks.

Limit: Requires precise positioning and timing.

Ranger abilities

Aura Bella Fiora is a ranger with abilities in archery and beast taming.

Limit: Depends on her level and equipment.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Varies by character: Fiora from Xenoblade is a young woman with brown hair and blue eyes; Fiora from League of Legends is a tall, elegant woman with long red hair and a rapier; Aura Bella Fiora is a dark elf girl with blonde hair and heterochromatic eyes.

Symbols — Flowers are a common motif; League of Legends Fiora is associated with the dueling rapier and the concept of grace.

Domains — Xenoblade Fiora lives in Colony 9; League Fiora hails from the nation of Demacia; Aura lives in the Great Tomb of Nazarick.

Accounts I
Xenoblade ChroniclesXenoblade Chronicles (video game)

2010

Fiora is a childhood friend of the protagonist Shulk. She is presumed dead after a Mechon attack but is later revealed to be alive, transformed into a cyborg. She rejoins the party and helps save the world. Fiora's transformation is a key plot point, and she becomes a playable character.

Accounts II
League of Legends: The Grand DuelistLeague of Legends (video game)

2012

Fiora is a duelist from Demacia who seeks to restore her family's honor. She challenges anyone to a duel and is known for her pride and skill. She becomes a champion in the game and features in various lore stories.

Overlord: The Dark Elf TwinsOverlord (light novel series)

2012-present

Aura Bella Fiora is one of the Pleiades, the floor guardians of the Great Tomb of Nazarick. She is a dark elf ranger who serves the protagonist Ainz Ooal Gown. She is a loyal subordinate and often accompanies Ainz on his adventures.

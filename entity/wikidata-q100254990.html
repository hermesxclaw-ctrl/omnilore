The Approach

The name 'Fiora' blooms across worlds: a river in Italy, a Berlin-based composer, and a sword-wielding duelist in the digital arenas of Runeterra. Each Fiora is a distinct entity, yet all share a root in the Latin word for flower. This dossier focuses on the fictional characters who bear the name, from the gentle healer of Xenoblade Chronicles to the proud grand duelist of League of Legends, and the many others who flit through novels, films, and operas.

Content advisory. No dark material found for the fictional characters named Fiora. The real-world persons named Fiora have no reported dark history in the provided sources.
Identity

Fiora

Pronunciation fee-OR-ah

Etymology

From the Latin 'flos' meaning flower, via Italian 'fiore'.

Cultural Ownership

Multiple cultures; the name is used in Italian, English, and Japanese contexts.

Disputed Identifications

The name is shared by several unrelated fictional characters and real persons; no disputed identifications among them.

Description

Appearance

Varies by character: Fiora from Xenoblade is a young woman with brown hair and blue eyes; Fiora from League of Legends is a tall, elegant woman with long red hair and a rapier; Aura Bella Fiora is a dark elf girl with blonde hair and heterochromatic eyes.

Symbols & Iconography

Flowers are a common motif; League of Legends Fiora is associated with the dueling rapier and the concept of grace.

Behaviour & Personality

Xenoblade Fiora is kind and caring; League Fiora is proud, confident, and obsessed with dueling; Aura is playful but deadly.

Domains & Habitats

Xenoblade Fiora lives in Colony 9; League Fiora hails from the nation of Demacia; Aura lives in the Great Tomb of Nazarick.

Version Differences

Each Fiora is a separate character with no connection; their appearances and personalities are entirely distinct.

Etymology

The name 'Aoi' (葵) in Japanese means 'hollyhock' or 'mallow,' a flower often associated with nobility and the Tokugawa clan. 'Yukimura' (幸村) is a given name historically borne by the famed samurai Sanada Yukimura, known as a legendary warrior. The combination suggests a deliberate evocation of heroic samurai heritage.

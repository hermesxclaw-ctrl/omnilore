The Approach

A charismatic exorcist who once dreamed of protecting the innocent, Suguru Geto became one of the most dangerous curses in modern sorcerer society—a tragic fall from grace that still haunts the world of Jujutsu Kaisen.

Content advisory. Depictions of violence, death, and morally ambiguous actions.
Identity

Suguru Geto

Pronunciation GHE-toh SOO-goo-roo

Etymology

The name is written in kanji as 夏油 傑 (Getō Suguru). The surname 夏油 (Getō) literally means 'summer oil' or 'sesame oil', though the meaning is not explicitly explained in canon. The given name 傑 (Suguru) means 'excellent' or 'surpassing'.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese manga and anime franchise Jujutsu Kaisen, created by Gege Akutami. The character is a fictional creation and not part of any real-world mythology or culture.

Disputed Identifications

None known; the character is unique to Jujutsu Kaisen. However, fans sometimes confuse him with another character, Suguru Geto, from the prequel series Jujutsu Kaisen 0, which is the same character.

Description

Appearance

Suguru Geto is a tall, lean man with long black hair tied in a low ponytail and bangs framing his face. He typically wears the standard Jujutsu High uniform, a dark blue gakuran with high collar, and later adopts a more casual look with a white robe. His eyes are often depicted as sharp and calculating.

Symbols & Iconography

He is associated with cursed spirits, which he collects and controls. His signature weapon is a cursed tool called 'Playful Cloud', a three-section staff. He also has a distinct scar on his left cheek, which he gains during his fight with Toji Fushiguro, though the scar is not present in the prequel timeline.

Behaviour & Personality

Geto is initially portrayed as a calm, composed, and idealistic young man who believes in protecting the weak. He is deeply empathetic and cares for his friends, especially Satoru Gojo. However, after a series of traumatic events, he becomes disillusioned with society and develops a twisted ideology: he believes that non-sorcerers (ordinary humans) are inferior and should be exterminated to create a world where sorcerers can thrive. He becomes cold, calculating, and ruthless, though he retains a sense of charisma and conviction.

Domains & Habitats

As a student, he resides at Tokyo Jujutsu High. Later, as a curse user, he operates in the shadows, often seen in abandoned or secluded locations. He is associated with the group of curse users known as the 'Geto Group'.

Version Differences

In the prequel (Jujutsu Kaisen 0), Geto is the main antagonist, older and more hardened. In the main series flashbacks, he is a second-year student, more naive and idealistic. The anime and manga largely align, but some minor scenes are extended in the anime.

Powers & Abilities

Cursed Spirit Manipulation

Source Version Manga and anime, all appearances

Description

Geto's innate technique allows him to absorb and control cursed spirits. He can store them within himself and summon them at will. He can also extract and use their techniques.

Limitations

He can only control spirits he has absorbed; the number is limited by his own capacity, though he has accumulated over 4,000 spirits. The technique requires him to consume the spirits in a physical form (often by swallowing them).

Contradictions

None known; the technique is consistently depicted.

Maximum Uzumaki

Source Version Manga and anime, used in Jujutsu Kaisen 0 and the Shibuya Incident

Description

A powerful technique that combines all the cursed energy of the spirits he has absorbed into a single, devastating blast.

Limitations

Requires a significant number of spirits and leaves him weakened afterward.

Contradictions

None known.

Playful Cloud

Source Version Manga and anime, used by Geto in Jujutsu Kaisen 0 and flashbacks

Description

A cursed tool that takes the form of a three-section staff. It is one of the most powerful cursed tools, capable of dealing massive damage.

Limitations

It is a physical weapon, so it is ineffective against intangible targets unless imbued with cursed energy.

Contradictions

None known.

Jujutsu Kaisen 0: The Prequel

Source Jujutsu Kaisen 0 manga and movieCulture Japanese manga/animePeriod 2017 (manga), 2021 (movie)

Summary

Geto orchestrates the Night Parade of a Hundred Demons in Shinjuku and Kyoto to distract the jujutsu world while he plans to obtain Rika Orimoto, a powerful cursed spirit. He is confronted by Yuta Okkotsu, who defeats him. Geto is then killed by his former best friend, Satoru Gojo.

The Shibuya Incident

Contradictions & Disputes

Geto's death and Kenjaku's possession

Position A

Geto dies in Jujutsu Kaisen 0 and is later revived by Kenjaku.

Position A — evidence

The manga shows Kenjaku using Geto's body after his death, implying possession of a corpse.

Position B

Kenjaku never actually revived Geto; he simply took over the body after death.

Position B — evidence

In the Shibuya Incident, Kenjaku claims to have been using Geto's body since his death.

Scholarly Consensus

The official canon is that Kenjaku took over Geto's body after his death, not that he revived him.

Geto's scar

Position A

Geto has a scar on his left cheek in the main series flashbacks.

Position A — evidence

The scar is shown in the Star Plasma Vessel arc.

Position B

In Jujutsu Kaisen 0, Geto does not have a scar.

Position B — evidence

The prequel does not show a scar.

Scholarly Consensus

The scar was likely obtained during the Star Plasma Vessel arc, which occurs before the prequel, so it should be present in the prequel, but the prequel was written earlier and may have been inconsistent.

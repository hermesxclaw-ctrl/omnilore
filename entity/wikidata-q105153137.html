Who It Is

A charismatic exorcist who once dreamed of protecting the innocent, Suguru Geto became one of the most dangerous curses in modern sorcerer society—a tragic fall from grace that still haunts the world of Jujutsu Kaisen.

Geto is initially portrayed as a calm, composed, and idealistic young man who believes in protecting the weak. He is deeply empathetic and cares for his friends, especially Satoru Gojo. However, after a series of traumatic events, he becomes disillusioned with society and develops a twisted ideology: he believes that non-sorcerers (ordinary humans) are inferior and should be exterminated to create a world where sorcerers can thrive. He becomes cold, calculating, and ruthless, though he retains a sense of charisma and conviction.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name is written in kanji as 夏油 傑 (Getō Suguru). The surname 夏油 (Getō) literally means 'summer oil' or 'sesame oil', though the meaning is not explicitly explained in canon. The given name 傑 (Suguru) means 'excellent' or 'surpassing'.

Pronunciation — GHE-toh SOO-goo-roo

Aliases — Suguru Geto

Ownership — Japanese manga and anime franchise Jujutsu Kaisen, created by Gege Akutami. The character is a fictional creation and not part of any real-world mythology or culture.

Powers
Cursed Spirit Manipulation

Geto's innate technique allows him to absorb and control cursed spirits. He can store them within himself and summon them at will. He can also extract and use their techniques.

Limit: He can only control spirits he has absorbed; the number is limited by his own capacity, though he has accumulated over 4,000 spirits. The technique requires him to consume the spirits in a physical form (often by swallowing them).

Maximum Uzumaki

A powerful technique that combines all the cursed energy of the spirits he has absorbed into a single, devastating blast.

Limit: Requires a significant number of spirits and leaves him weakened afterward.

Playful Cloud

A cursed tool that takes the form of a three-section staff. It is one of the most powerful cursed tools, capable of dealing massive damage.

Limit: It is a physical weapon, so it is ineffective against intangible targets unless imbued with cursed energy.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Suguru Geto is a tall, lean man with long black hair tied in a low ponytail and bangs framing his face. He typically wears the standard Jujutsu High uniform, a dark blue gakuran with high collar, and later adopts a more casual look with a white robe. His eyes are often depicted as sharp and calculating.

Symbols — He is associated with cursed spirits, which he collects and controls. His signature weapon is a cursed tool called 'Playful Cloud', a three-section staff. He also has a distinct scar on his left cheek, which he gains during his fight with Toji Fushiguro, though the scar is not present in the prequel timeline.

Domains — As a student, he resides at Tokyo Jujutsu High. Later, as a curse user, he operates in the shadows, often seen in abandoned or secluded locations. He is associated with the group of curse users known as the 'Geto Group'.

Accounts I
Jujutsu Kaisen 0: The PrequelJujutsu Kaisen 0 manga and movie

2017 (manga), 2021 (movie)

Geto orchestrates the Night Parade of a Hundred Demons in Shinjuku and Kyoto to distract the jujutsu world while he plans to obtain Rika Orimoto, a powerful cursed spirit. He is confronted by Yuta Okkotsu, who defeats him. Geto is then killed by his former best friend, Satoru Gojo. Geto's death leaves a void in the jujutsu world, and his ideology is later resurrected by Kenjaku, who takes over his body.

Accounts II
The Star Plasma Vessel Arc (Flashback)Jujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 64-79; anime Season 2

2018 (manga), 2023 (anime)

As a second-year student, Geto and Gojo are tasked with protecting Riko Amanai, the Star Plasma Vessel, from assassins. They succeed but are deeply affected by Riko's death. Geto's faith in protecting the weak is shaken, leading to his eventual descent. This event is the turning point for Geto's character, leading to his radicalization.

The Shibuya IncidentJujutsu Kaisen manga, chapters 83-137; anime Season 2

2018-2021 (manga), 2023 (anime)

Geto's body is taken over by Kenjaku, an ancient sorcerer, who uses Geto's appearance and abilities to initiate the Shibuya Incident, a large-scale attack on Tokyo. Geto's consciousness is suppressed, but his body is used to unleash chaos. The incident results in Gojo being sealed, and the jujutsu world is thrown into turmoil.

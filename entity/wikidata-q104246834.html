Who It Is

In the sprawling, often unforgiving world of the Six-Faced World, one name evokes quiet reverence and sorrow in equal measure: Zenith Greyrat. A healer of extraordinary skill, a mother whose love defies the boundaries of memory and magic, and a figure whose disappearance reshaped the lives of those who loved her most. Her story is not one of epic battles or grand conquests, but of quiet strength, sacrifice, and the enduring bonds of family.

Zenith is kind, compassionate, and deeply devoted to her family. She is a skilled healer who prioritizes helping others, sometimes to her own detriment. She is gentle but can be firm when necessary, especially in protecting her children. After her rescue, she suffers from severe amnesia and trauma, leaving her in a childlike state, requiring constant care. She retains a faint connection to her past, often humming a lullaby she used to sing to Rudeus.

Powers
Healing Magic

Zenith is an accomplished healer, capable of curing a wide range of injuries and illnesses. She is particularly skilled in restorative magic, including curing poison and treating severe wounds. Her abilities are considered top-tier, and she is often called upon for difficult cases.

Limit: She is not a combat-oriented mage; her abilities are focused solely on healing and support. She cannot bring the dead back to life.

Detoxification Magic

A specialized form of healing magic that removes toxins and poisons from the body. Zenith demonstrates this ability when treating Rudeus after he is poisoned.

Limit: Requires knowledge of the specific poison to be effective.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Zenith is depicted as a beautiful woman with long, light-green hair that she often wears in a braid or ponytail. She has gentle green eyes. She typically wears a white and green healer's robe, often with a hood. In her younger years, she wore a more casual adventurer's outfit. After her rescue, her hair is shorter and her expression is vacant, reflecting her mental state.

Symbols — Her primary symbol is the healing staff she carries, often depicted with a green gem. She is associated with the color green, representing healing and nature. In the anime, her design emphasizes soft, rounded features to convey warmth and maternal care.

Domains — Zenith's primary domain is the Greyrat household, first in the village of Buina, then in the city of Roa in the Kingdom of Asura. She also spent time as an adventurer in her youth, traveling across the continent. She is most comfortable in domestic settings, healing and caring for her family.

Accounts I
The Latreia Family and the Greyrat MarriageMushoku Tensei light novel, Volume 3, Chapter 5 (flashback)

Before the main story

Zenith Latreia was born into a noble family in the Kingdom of Asura. She became an adventurer, which was unusual for a noblewoman, and met Paul Greyrat. They fell in love and married, much to the disapproval of her family. They had two children, Rudeus and Norn. The family lived in the village of Buina, where Zenith served as the local healer. The marriage established the Greyrat family as a central focus of the story. Zenith's decision to abandon her noble status for love highlights her independent spirit.

The DisappearanceMushoku Tensei light novel, Volume 6, Chapter 10

Main story, Year 422 of the Armored Dragon King's calendar

During a monster attack on the village of Buina, Zenith is separated from her family. She uses a teleportation spell to escape, but the spell malfunctioned due to the magical disaster (the Fittoa Displacement Incident), sending her to an unknown location. She is presumed dead by many, but her family holds onto hope. This event drives the main quest of the early story: Rudeus and Paul search for Zenith across the world. It also deeply affects the family, leading to Paul's alcoholism and Rudeus's guilt.

Accounts II
The RescueMushoku Tensei light novel, Volume 12, Chapter 4

Main story, Year 425

After years of searching, Rudeus and his party locate Zenith in the Begaritt Continent, where she is being held captive by a group of kidnappers who have been using her healing abilities. She is rescued, but the trauma of her captivity has left her with severe amnesia and a childlike mental state. The rescue is bittersweet: Zenith is alive, but she does not recognize her family. Her condition becomes a long-term challenge for the family, who must care for her. This also leads to Paul's death in a subsequent battle, adding to the tragedy.

Recovery and Family LifeMushoku Tensei light novel, Volume 15, Chapter 2 and onwards

Main story, Year 426 onwards

Zenith gradually recovers some of her memories and abilities, though she remains fragile. She lives with Rudeus and his family, where she is cared for by her grandchildren. She eventually regains enough awareness to recognize her family and express her love, though she never fully recovers her former self. Zenith's recovery allows for a semblance of normalcy, but her condition remains a source of sadness. Her presence influences Rudeus's parenting and his determination to protect his own family.

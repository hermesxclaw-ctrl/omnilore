The Approach

In the late 1990s, a hand-reared orphan colt named Special Week rose from a humble debut to conquer Japan's most prestigious races, defeating international champions and capturing the nation's heart. His legacy extends beyond the track, inspiring a beloved media franchise and leaving an indelible mark on horse racing history.

Stories & Myths

Early life and debut

Source Provided sourceCulture Japanese horse racingPeriod 1995-1997

Summary

Special Week was born on 2 May 1995. His dam Campaign Girl died shortly after foaling, so he was hand-fed by stable staff. He won his only start as a two-year-old in a maiden race at Hanshin.

1998: Classic success

Source Provided sourceCulture Japanese horse racingPeriod 1998

Summary

After a second in the Shiraume Sho, he won the Kisaragi Sho, Yayoi Sho, and then the Tokyo Yushun (Japanese Derby) by five lengths. He was third in the Satsuki Sho and second in the Kikuka Sho, and third in the Japan Cup.

1999: Four-year-old dominance

Source Provided sourceCulture Japanese horse racingPeriod 1999

Summary

Won the American Jockey Club Stakes, Hanshin Daishoten, spring Tenno Sho, autumn Tenno Sho (in record time), and Japan Cup, defeating international stars Montjeu, High-Rise, and Indigenous. Finished second to Grass Wonder in the Arima Kinen.

Stud career and death

Source Provided sourceCulture Japanese horse breedingPeriod 2000-2018

Summary

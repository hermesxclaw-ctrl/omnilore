The Approach

In the neon-lit underbelly of Tokyo's supernatural conflict, Shōko Ieiri stands as the calm in the storm—a doctor whose hands have stitched together sorcerers and monsters alike, wielding a power that can reverse the very flow of death.

Content advisory. Contains depictions of violence, injury, and medical procedures; themes of death and trauma; some scenes may be intense for younger readers.
Identity

Shōko Ieiri

Pronunciation SHOH-koh EE-eh-ree

Known As

Ieiri Shoko

Etymology

The surname 'Ieiri' is a Japanese surname. The given name 'Shōko' is written with kanji that can mean 'clear child' or 'pure child', but the exact intended meaning is not officially confirmed.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese popular culture, specifically the Jujutsu Kaisen franchise created by Gege Akutami.

Disputed Identifications

None known. She is a distinct character within the franchise, not a historical or mythological figure.

Description

Appearance

Shōko Ieiri is a young woman with long, dark hair often tied back, and a calm, somewhat tired expression. She typically wears a white doctor's coat over her Jujutsu High uniform. She has a distinctive mole under her left eye.

Symbols & Iconography

Her medical role is symbolized by her white coat and the medical tools she uses. As a jujutsu sorcerer, she is associated with the concept of healing, which is rare among sorcerers.

Behaviour & Personality

Shōko is portrayed as pragmatic, composed, and somewhat detached, likely due to the trauma of her experiences. She is professional and focused on her duties as a doctor, but she also shows caring for her friends, particularly Satoru Gojo and Suguru Geto. She has a dry sense of humor and a realistic outlook on the harshness of the jujutsu world.

Domains & Habitats

She is primarily based at the Tokyo Metropolitan Curse Technical College, where she serves as the school doctor. She appears in various locations during missions and conflicts, but her main 'domain' is the medical facility at the school.

Version Differences

No significant differences between manga and anime portrayals have been noted. However, her backstory and role are more detailed in the manga, particularly in flashback arcs.

Powers & Abilities

Reverse Cursed Technique

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

Shōko possesses the rare ability to heal injuries and reverse the effects of curses by using positive energy generated through reverse cursed energy. This allows her to treat wounds that would otherwise be fatal to jujutsu sorcerers.

Limitations

Healing requires significant concentration and energy; she cannot resurrect the dead, and her powers may be limited by the severity of the injury or the amount of cursed energy she has.

Contradictions

None noted, but the full extent of her abilities has not been fully explored in the series.

Medical Expertise

Source Version Manga and anime

Description

In addition to her cursed technique, Shōko is a trained medical professional, capable of performing surgery and other medical procedures.

Limitations

Her medical knowledge is conventional, and she relies on her cursed technique for supernatural healing.

Contradictions

None.

Stories & Myths

The Hidden Inventory Arc (flashback)

Source Jujutsu Kaisen manga and animeCulture Japanese popular culturePeriod 2007 (in-universe timeline)

Summary

Shōko is introduced as a classmate of Satoru Gojo and Suguru Geto at Jujutsu High. She witnesses the corruption and violence of the jujutsu world, and her friendship with Gojo and Geto is strained as they grow apart. This arc reveals her role as a healer and her emotional burden.

Participants

Satoru Gojo

Suguru Geto

Consequences

The events contribute to Geto's descent into villainy and Gojo's development as the strongest sorcerer. Shōko remains a supporting figure, dealing with the aftermath.

No notable variants.

The Shibuya Incident Arc

Source Jujutsu Kaisen manga and animeCulture Japanese popular culturePeriod 2018 (in-universe timeline)

Summary

During the chaotic Shibuya Incident, Shōko is called upon to treat numerous injured sorcerers, including the severely wounded Gojo. She works tirelessly to save lives, but is unable to prevent all casualties.

Participants

Shōko Ieiri

Satoru Gojo

Various jujutsu sorcerers

Consequences

The arc highlights her importance as a healer and the limits of her power. It also underscores the high stakes of the conflict.

Who It Is

A daimyo who danced naked in the streets, wrestled a mountain god, and boiled alive without a scream—Kozuki Oden of Wano Country is One Piece's most gloriously excessive hero, a man whose life was a legend before his death made him a martyr.

Oden is flamboyant, reckless, and unapologetically bold. He is fiercely loyal to his retainers and family, and possesses an unyielding sense of honor and duty. He is known for his eccentricity, such as dancing naked in public to atone for his father's anger, but also for his profound wisdom and strategic mind. He is a natural leader who inspires devotion, but his trust in others can be naive, as seen in his dealings with Kurozumi Orochi and Kaido.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The surname Kozuki (光月) means 'light moon'; Oden (おでん) is a Japanese hotpot dish, chosen by author Eiichiro Oda for its humorous contrast with the character's serious legacy.

Pronunciation — KOH-zoo-kee OH-den

Aliases — Oden, Lord Oden, The Fool of Wano, The Great Lord Oden

Ownership — Japanese manga/anime franchise One Piece (created by Eiichiro Oda, published by Shueisha).

Powers
Master Swordsmanship

Oden is one of the strongest swordsmen in One Piece, capable of using advanced Armament Haki to cut through Kaido's dragon scales. He wields two legendary swords: Enma, which drains the user's Haki, and Ame no Habakiri, a powerful blade that can cut through almost anything.

Limit: Enma requires immense Haki control; Oden mastered it, but others struggle.

Haki (Armament, Observation, Conqueror's)

Oden possesses all three types of Haki. His Conqueror's Haki is strong enough to knock out weak-willed opponents, and his Armament Haki is powerful enough to injure Kaido, a Yonko.

Limit: Haki usage is limited by stamina and willpower.

Physical Prowess

Oden is incredibly strong and durable, able to lift massive boulders and survive being boiled alive for an hour. He also has exceptional speed and reflexes.

Limit: He is still mortal and can be injured by powerful attacks.

Leadership and Charisma

Oden inspires unwavering loyalty, leading a band of retainers (the Akazaya Nine) who would die for him. His ability to unite people is a key trait.

Limit: His trusting nature can be exploited.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Oden is a tall, muscular man with long black hair, a thick beard, and a prominent tattoo of a sun on his left shoulder and a crescent moon on his right, symbolizing the Kozuki clan's alliance with the Minks. He wears traditional samurai attire, often with a haori and hakama, and carries two swords: Enma and Ame no Habakiri. In his youth, he had a wild, unkempt look; later, he adopted a more regal appearance as daimyo of Kuri.

Symbols — The sun and moon tattoos represent the Kozuki clan's bond with the Mink tribe; his swords Enma and Ame no Habakiri are legendary blades forged by the samurai Shimotsuki Kozaburo. His name 'Oden' is a pun on the Japanese dish, but his identity is tied to the concept of 'opening Wano's borders'.

Domains — Oden's domain is the Kuri region of Wano Country, a land of mountains and forests. He also travels extensively, including to the island of Wano, the Whitebeard Pirates' ship, and the island of Onigashima. His ancestral home is Kuri Castle, and he later resides in the Flower Capital.

Accounts I
The Dance of AtonementOne Piece manga chapter 961-962; anime episode 959

Wano Country's past (approx. 30 years before current timeline)

After causing trouble in the Flower Capital, Oden is forced to dance naked in the streets every week for five years to atone for his father's anger. This humiliation is a cover for his secret mission to gather information on Kurozumi Orochi's conspiracy. Oden's reputation is tarnished, but he gains crucial intel and prepares for rebellion.

The Journey with Whitebeard and RogerOne Piece manga chapters 963-968; anime episodes 960-966

Approx. 25-20 years before current timeline

Oden leaves Wano to join the Whitebeard Pirates, becoming a commander. Later, he joins Gol D. Roger's crew to help discover the secrets of the world, including the True History. He learns about the Void Century and the ancient weapons. Oden gains knowledge that he hopes will help open Wano's borders, but he returns to find Wano under Orochi's control.

Accounts II
The Battle with KaidoOne Piece manga chapters 969-970; anime episodes 967-971

Approx. 20 years before current timeline

Oden confronts Kaido and Orochi to reclaim Wano. He nearly defeats Kaido, but is tricked by Orochi's hostage-taking of his wife and daughter. He surrenders and is forced to dance for five years to save them. Oden's surrender and eventual execution lead to the downfall of the Kozuki clan, but his sacrifice inspires his retainers to continue the rebellion.

The Boiling ExecutionOne Piece manga chapter 970; anime episode 970

20 years before current timeline

Oden is sentenced to death by boiling oil. He holds a platform above his head to keep his retainers from drowning, enduring the pain for an hour until he dies. His last words are about opening Wano's borders. Oden's death galvanizes the rebellion, leading to the eventual fall of Kaido and Orochi.

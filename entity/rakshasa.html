Who It Is
Form

Rakshasa are often depicted as humanoid figures with the head of a tiger or a lion. They are said to possess supernatural strength and agility.

Powers

Shape-shifting Rakshasa have the ability to transform into various forms, including animals and humans.

Supernatural strength Rakshasa possess supernatural strength and agility, allowing them to perform feats that would be impossible for mortal beings.

Magical powers Rakshasa have access to various forms of magic, including the ability to cast spells and manipulate the environment.

Accounts

The story of Ravana and Rama is a famous tale from Hindu mythology, in which the Rakshasa king Ravana kidnaps the goddess Sita and is eventually defeated by the god Rama.

The story of the Rakshasa and the Buddha is a famous tale from Buddhist mythology, in which the Rakshasa king Ravana invites the Buddha to Sri Lanka to deliver a sermon.

Who It Is

One word, three entirely different worlds: a palm-sized glider that parachutes between cedars on a membrane thin as paper, a vanished Tongva village buried under Chatsworth suburbs, and an undead skeleton mage who kept his game username when the game became reality. All are called Momonga — and none of them are related.

1) ANIMAL: Strictly nocturnal and arboreal. Nests in tree cavities (often old woodpecker holes) lined with bark and moss, 2-10m above ground. Glides 20-50m typically, up to ~100m, launching from high branches, steering with limbs and tail, landing trunk-first. Diet: seeds, nuts (especially conifer), buds, bark, fruits, occasionally insects. Socially solitary or in small family groups; breeding 1-2 litters/year, 1-4 pups, gestation ~4 weeks. Vocalizations include soft chirps and high-pitched calls. Hibernates intermittently in winter but does not truly hibernate. 2) VILLAGE: As reconstructed

Name & Tongue

Etymology — Japanese モモンガ momonga. The word is attested in Japanese from the Edo period for small gliding squirrels. Etymology is not definitively Indo-European; Japanese dictionaries (Nihon Kokugo Daijiten, Daijirin) treat it as native Japanese, likely mimetic/onomatopoeic or from older dialect terms for the a

Pronunciation — Japanese: [mo.mo.ŋɡa] (four morae: mo-mo-n-ga), pitch accent heiban. English approximation: moh-MOHN-gah. Scientific: teh-ROH-miss moh-MON-gah (Pteromys momonga). Tongva rendering: Spanish-era sources write Momonga / Momonge; original Tongva pronunciation not recorded with IPA.

Aliases — Pteromys momonga (Japanese dwarf flying squirrel), Momonga - Tongva village (also rendered Momonga, Momonge in historical sources), Momonga / Ainz Ooal Gown (Overlord protagonist), Momonga Standing and Loop Coaster (Yomiuriland)

Ownership — 1) Japanese vernacular zoology and natural history; species endemic to Japan (Honshu, Kyushu, Shikoku). 2) Tongva (Gabrieleño) people, the Indigenous people of the Los Angeles Basi

Powers
Gliding (Patagial Flight)

Animal extends patagium to generate lift, achieving glide ratios ~1.5-2.5. Controls pitch/roll by limb tension and tail movement. Not powered flight; requires height differential.

Limit: Cannot gain altitude; glide distance limited by launch height and wind; vulnerable to owls and martens mid-glide; requires intact forest canopy connectivity.

Nocturnal Adaptation

Enlarged eyes with tapetum lucidum, acute hearing, whiskers for navigation in darkness. Activity peaks 1-3 hours after sunset.

Limit: Poor diurnal vision; dependent on cavity shelter by day; light pollution disrupts foraging.

High-Tier Undead Magic (Overlord)

As level 100 YGGDRASIL caster (Overlord race), Momonga/Ainz can cast super-tier and 10th-tier spells: reality-altering magic such as 'The Goal of All Life is Death' (with Widen Magic), 'Creation', 'Shalltear's resurrection', 'Grasp Heart', 'Time Stop', summoning Primal Elementals and Death Knights. Possesses 700+ spells in light novel canon.

Limit: Mana pool and cooldowns; super-tier spells have long cast times and once-per-day limits; requires staff or specialized items for maximum effect; cannot resurrect without cost (500 million gold equivalent).

Undead Racial Traits and Emotional Suppression

Immunity to poison, disease, sleep, charm, instant death, critical hits, and need for food/sleep/breathing. Passive ability forcibly suppresses strong emotions, calming the mind when rage or panic would occur, with visible aura dampening.

Limit: Suppression can make him seem cold and hinder empathy; still vulnerable to holy/divine damage, world-class items, and anti-undead magic.

Staff of Ainz Ooal Gown and World-Class Items

Wields guild weapon Staff of Ainz Ooal Gown (contains seven gems each with distinct powers: elemental summons, auto-attack). Possesses World Items including 'Ouroboros' knowledge via guild, and later acquires others. Has complete Nazarick treasury with divine and legendary gear.

Limit: Staff is too conspicuous for public use; World Items can be countered only by other World Items; use risks revealing power level.

Tactical Genius and Information Warfare (Character Skill)

Not supernatural but emphasized: meticulous preparation, intelligence gathering via undead spies, use of doppelgängers (Pandora's Actor), and feigning weakness to lure enemies. Suzuki's salaryman experience translates to bureaucratic administration of Nazarick.

Limit: Often overestimates opponents due to paranoia; relies heavily on Demiurge and Albedo for strategy; fear of player-level threats leads to excessive caution.

Form & Signs

Appearance — 1) ANIMAL: Pteromys momonga is a small nocturnal gliding mammal. Head-body length 14-20 cm, tail 10-16 cm, weight 150-220 g. Dorsal pelage grey-brown to yellow-brown, ventral white. Enormous black eyes adapted for night vision (eye diameter ~10mm). Patagium (gliding membrane) stretches from wrist to ankle, densely furred, with a cartilaginous styliform process at the wrist supporting the leading edge. Tail flattened, bushy, used as rudder and bra

Symbols — 1) ANIMAL: In Japanese folklore and modern kawaii culture, momonga is icon of forest innocence, depicted with patagium spread like a cape, often with large glistening eyes. Featured on regional mascots, stamps, and forest conservation logos in Nagano and Tohoku. No ancient religious iconography equivalent to kitsune or tanuki, but appears in Edo natural history illustrations (e.g., Honzō Zufu). 2) VILLAGE: No specific iconography; Tongva village

Domains — 1) ANIMAL: Endemic to Japan: Honshu, Shikoku, and Kyushu (absent from Hokkaido where P. volans orii occurs). Habitat: boreal and temperate mixed forests, especially natural conifer-broadleaf forests with old cavity-bearing trees; also plantation forests and shrine groves. Elevation sea level to ~1600m. IUCN range map shows fragmented but widespread distribution. 2) VILLAGE: Northwest San Fernando Valley, at the foot of the Santa Susana Mountains

Accounts I
The Taxonomic Birth of Pteromys momongaTemminck & Schlegel, Fauna Japonica (1844); Siebold collection

1844 (Edo period, late)

During Philipp Franz von Siebold's Japanese collections, specimens of a small flying squirrel from Honshu were sent to Leiden. Temminck described it as Pteromys momonga, noting its smaller size and distinct dentition from European Pteromys volans. The description cemented the Japanese vernacular into Western science and separated the Japanese endemic from continental Siberian flying squirrels, a distinction confirmed by later genetics. Established the species as endemic to Japan; became reference for all later Japanese mammalogy; type specimen in Naturalis, Leiden.

Nights in the Canopy: Ecology of the Dwarf GliderField studies: Asari et al. (2007) Journal of Mammalogy; Ando & Shiraishi (1993); IUCN Red List 2016

1990s-2020s (modern research)

Long-term nest-box and radio-tracking studies in Nagano and Chichibu revealed momonga's strict dependence on old-growth cavities, seasonal diet shift from conifer buds in winter to fruits and seeds in summer, and gliding corridors along forest edges. The species was shown to be more tolerant of fragmented forests than expected, using shrine forests and even suburban woodlots, but requiring connectivity. Breeding observations documented communal nesting in winter for thermoregulation. Informed Japanese forestry policy to retain cavity trees; species remains Least Concern but locally vulnerable to clear-cutting.

Momonga Village and the Mission SystemHugo Reid letters (1852); Mission San Fernando registers (1797-1840s); McCawley 1996; Johnston 1962

Pre-contact to 1840s (Spanish/Mexican era)

Momonga was one of the villages in the northern San Fernando Valley, near the Santa Susana Pass trade route linking the Valley to the Chumash coast. After Mission San Fernando Rey de España was founded in 1797, its inhabitants were gradually brought (often by coercion) into the mission as neophytes, baptized and put to labor. By the 1830s-40s, the village was depopulated; survivors intermarried with other Tongva and Tataviam families. Reid, writing from memory of his Tongva wife's community, listed Momonga among the valley villages, preserving the name after the physical settlement had vanished under ranchos. Village site now under Chatsworth urban development; no intact archaeological village plan excavated; name survives only in ethnohistoric lists and local history plaques. Represents broader pattern of mission-induced displacement and cultural loss.

Accounts II
The Last Log-In: Momonga Stays BehindMaruyama, Overlord Volume 1: The Undead King, Prologue-Chapter 1

2012 (in-universe date 2138, YGGDRASIL shutdown)

On the final night of the DMMO-RPG YGGDRASIL after 12 years, guildmaster Momonga waits alone in the Great Tomb of Nazarick as former guildmates fail to appear for the shutdown. At midnight, instead of being logged out, he finds himself still in the tomb — now fully real, with NPCs Albedo, Sebas, and others sentient and kneeling before him. His body is now his skeletal avatar, his emotions dampened, and the outside world is no longer the game but an unknown New World. He renames himself Ainz Ooal Gown to honor his friends and vows to find any other players. Establishes central premise: Nazarick's transfer to New World, Ainz's identity crisis, and his decision to project strength while searching for friends. Sets up Sorcerer Kingdom arc.

Shalltear's Betrayal and The Goal of All Life is DeathMaruyama, Overlord Volume 3: The Bloody Valkyrie

2013 (in-universe, shortly after transfer)

Vampire guardian Shalltear Bloodfallen is mind-controlled by the World Item Downfall of Castle and Country wielded by the Slane Theocracy's Black Scripture. Ainz is forced to fight and kill his own NPC child. He prepares meticulously, equipping anti-undead gear and using super-tier magic. Casting 'The Goal of All Life is Death' amplified by 'Widen Magic' and 'Cry of the Banshee,' he bypasses her resurrection ability and destroys her, then resurrects her at great cost, reaffirming his paternal bond and the danger of World Items. Demonstrates Ainz's willingness to kill and pay to protect Nazarick; reveals existence of other World Item users in New World; deepens theme that NPCs are his children.

Momon the Adventurer and the Carne Village DefenseMaruyama, Overlord Volume 2: The Dark Warrior; Volume 8-9

2013-2014

To gather information without revealing Nazarick's power, Ainz creates the alias Momon, a jet-black armored adventurer, with Nabe (Narberal Gamma) as companion. He saves Carne Village from Slane Theocracy soldiers and later from the Wise King of the Forest (Hamsuke). As Momon, he climbs adventurer ranks to adamantite, manipulating human society's perception of strength while secretly subjugating the hamster-like Hamsuke as a pet/mount. This dual identity becomes his primary tool for exploring the New World. Creates legend of Momon that later legitimizes the Sorcerer Kingdom; Enri becomes goblin general via Ainz's horn; Carne Village becomes loyal vassal.

Momonga is a type of sugar glider that can be kept as an easy pet. — Pteromys momonga is a wild, nocturnal, protected gliding squirrel endemic to Japan, not a sugar glider (Petaurus breviceps, an Australian marsupial). It is not domesticated, requires forest habitat and gliding space, and is not legal or ethical as a pet in most jurisdictions. Pet trade 'momonga' are often misidentified Siberian flying squirrels or sugar gliders.

Momonga the village is a myth or just a name on a roller coaster. — Momonga was a real Tongva settlement documented in 19th-century ethnohistoric sources and mission registers, part of the Indigenous landscape of Tovaangar long before Los Angeles. The roller coaster is a modern namesake after the animal, not the village.

Ainz Ooal Gown and Momonga are two different characters. — They are the same individual: Suzuki Satoru's avatar was named Momonga after his guild; he renames himself Ainz Ooal Gown at the end of Volume 1 to honor his guildmates. Both names refer to one person, with 'Ainz' used after the renaming.

Japanese dwarf flying squirrels can truly fly like birds or bats. — They glide. The patagium provides lift but no powered flapping; they must launch from a height and descend. Maximum recorded glides ~100m with significant height loss.

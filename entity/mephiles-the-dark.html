Who It Is

A sinister, crystalline entity formed from the sealed form of Solaris — the god of time. Mephiles is composed of dark purple crystals that can shift into a humanoid shape. He manipulates time, creates duplicates of himself, and can merge with shadows. His ultimate goal is to reunite with his other half, Iblis, to become Solaris once more.

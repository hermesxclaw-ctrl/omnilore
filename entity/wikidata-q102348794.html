Who It Is

When the Locust Horde erupted from the ground and humanity's cities turned to slaughterhouses, the last thing the COG needed was a celebrity. But Augustus Cole, the fastest man in the history of Thrashball, traded his jersey for armor and brought something the war effort never expected: a roar. He didn't just fight the war—he narrated it, hyped it, and turned survival into a spectacle. This is the story of the Cole Train, the athlete who became a legend, and the complicated legacy of a character who carried a culture's hopes and a culture's stereotypes on his shoulders.

Cole is characterized by his exuberant, optimistic, and boisterous personality. He is a natural motivator, often boosting morale with his enthusiasm and humor. He is fiercely loyal to his friends, especially Damon Baird, and is known for his brash, sometimes reckless, courage in battle. Unlike his more cynical comrades, Cole maintains a positive outlook, using his charisma to lighten the mood even in dire situations.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Augustus' is of Latin origin meaning 'majestic' or 'venerable,' fitting a character of heroic stature. The nickname 'Cole Train' is a play on his surname and his dynamic, unstoppable nature, evoking a train's power and momentum.

Pronunciation — AW-gus-tus KOHL (traditionally); 'Cole Train' pronounced as it appears.

Aliases — Cole Train, Cole, The Cole Train

Ownership — Created by Epic Games for the Gears of War franchise; the character is owned by Epic Games and later The Coalition (Microsoft Studios).

Powers
Athletic prowess

Cole was a star Thrashball player, and his physical conditioning gives him exceptional speed, agility, and endurance, which he uses to great effect in combat.

Limit: His athletic abilities are human-level and do not grant superhuman powers.

Combat proficiency

Cole is a highly effective soldier, skilled in the use of firearms, grenades, and melee combat. He is particularly adept at using the Gnasher shotgun and the Lancer assault rifle.

Limit: His skills are a result of training and experience, not supernatural abilities.

Leadership and morale boosting

Cole's infectious energy and motivational speeches have a tangible effect on his squad's morale, often rallying them in difficult battles.

Limit: This is a psychological effect, not a supernatural power.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Augustus Cole is a tall, muscular African-American man with a shaved head and a distinctive goatee. He wears the standard COG armor, often with a customized, more colorful design, and is frequently seen with a helmet. His physique reflects his athletic background, and he carries himself with a confident, energetic swagger.

Symbols — Cole is often associated with the number 83, his jersey number from his Thrashball days, which appears on his armor and gear. The nickname 'Cole Train' is also a symbol of his identity, and he is known for his catchphrase 'Woo!', which has become iconic among fans.

Domains — Cole is primarily found in the war-torn environments of Sera, including ruined cities, underground tunnels, and military bases. He is a soldier of the COG, so his habitat is wherever the war takes him.

Accounts I
The Rise of the Cole TrainGears of War series (primarily Gears of War 3 and supplementary material)

Before the Locust War

Augustus Cole was a celebrated Thrashball player, considered the greatest in the sport's history. He was known for his incredible speed and showmanship, earning the nickname 'Cole Train.' His career was cut short by the emergence of the Locust Horde, which led him to enlist in the COG. Cole's athletic fame translated into military service, where he became a legendary soldier, but also a target for those who felt he was a symbol of a lost era.

The Battle of Aspho FieldsGears of War: Aspho Fields (novel) and Gears of War 2

During the Locust War

Cole participated in the pivotal Battle of Aspho Fields, a major conflict against the Locust. He played a key role in the fighting, demonstrating his bravery and combat skills. The battle was a significant victory for the COG, and Cole's actions contributed to his reputation as a war hero.

Accounts II
The Sinking of JacintoGears of War 2

During the Locust War

Cole was part of the mission to sink the city of Jacinto to destroy the Locust horde's underground stronghold. This desperate act was a turning point in the war. The sinking of Jacinto devastated the Locust, but also resulted in the loss of the last major human stronghold, forcing humanity to flee.

The Fall of the COG and the New WarGears of War 4 and Gears 5

25 years after the Locust War

In his later years, Cole retired from active duty and became a mentor to a new generation of soldiers. He is seen as a legendary figure, and his legacy inspires others. He appears in Gears of War 4 and Gears 5, providing guidance to the new protagonist, J.D. Fenix. Cole's experience and wisdom prove invaluable in the fight against the new threat, the Swarm.

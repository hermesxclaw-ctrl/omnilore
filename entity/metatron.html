Who It Is
Form

As an archangel, Metatron is often depicted as a powerful, wise, and benevolent being. He is sometimes portrayed as serving as the celestial scribe, recording the deeds of humanity. In some esoteric contexts, he is associated with the angel of the veil, Mīṭaṭrūn.

Powers

Accounts

Enoch is transfigured and becomes the archangel Metatron, serving as the celestial scribe.

Metatron serves as the Prince of the Divine Presence, mediating between the divine and human realms.

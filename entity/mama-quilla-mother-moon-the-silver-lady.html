Mama Quilla (Mother Moon / the Silver Lady)

also known asMother Moon / the Silver Lady
Typegoddess (moon, female cycles, calendar)
Who It Is
Form

Mama Quilla is often depicted as a woman with long, flowing hair and a silver or white dress, symbolizing her association with the moon and its luminescent light. In some representations, she is shown carrying a staff or a scepter, which may represent her power to control the lunar cycles. The goddess's appearance is also sometimes accompanied by a retinue of stars, which are said to be her attendants or companions. Additionally, Mama Quilla's appearance has been interpreted as a symbol of feminine beauty and fertility, highlighting the importance of women's roles in Inca society. For example, the traditional Quechua festival of 'Quyllurit'i' features a ritual dance, where women dressed in traditional clothing and adorned with silver jewelry, embody the spirit of Mama Quilla. The appearance of Mama Quilla has also been influenced by the cultural and artistic traditions of the Andean region, with depictions of the goddess appearing in textiles, ceramics, and other forms of pre-Columbian art.

Powers

Control over the lunar cycles Mama Quilla has the power to control the lunar cycles, which are essential for agricultural planning and spiritual practices. She is said to be able to influence the phases of the moon, from new moon to full moon, and is often invoked in rituals and ceremonies to ensure the fertility and prosperity of the land and its people.

Accounts

According to Inca mythology, Mama Quilla and her brother/husband Inti created the universe and all living things. The story tells of how the two gods emerged from the primordial sea and created the sun, moon, stars, and all living creatures.

According to this myth, Mama Quilla's tears of silver created the stars in the night sky. The story tells of how the goddess wept for her brother/husband Inti, who had been injured in a battle with a monster, and how her tears fell to the earth and became the stars.

What We Don't Know

There are several gaps in our understanding of Mama Quilla, including the exact nature of her role in the creation of the universe, the extent of her powers and abilities, and the significance of her association with the moon and its cycles.

A way to hold it in your mind

Mama Quilla is a fascinating and complex figure in Inca mythology, with a rich and multifaceted character that reflects the cultural and spiritual values of the Andean world. Her association with the moon and its cycles, as well as her role in the creation of the universe, make her a powerful and intriguing symbol of feminine power and fertility.

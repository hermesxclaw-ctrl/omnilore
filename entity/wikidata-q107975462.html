Who It Is

She walks into Spiders Cafe in a blue miniskirt and a coat lined with thorns that haven't sprouted yet — Paula, the Baroque Works officer who can turn any inch of her skin into a needle, a stiletto, a sea urchin's nightmare. When Nami meets her on the stone stairs of Alubarna Palace, Miss Doublefinger doesn't just fight; she turns her own body into a weapon shop, and the question becomes whether a thief who fights with a trick baton can survive a woman who is the trick.

Attested: Cool, professional, sardonic, and businesslike — the straight woman to Mr. 1's stoicism and Mr. 2's flamboyance. She treats assassination as clerical work; at Spiders Cafe she calmly serves tea while discussing murder. Shows dry humor and mild exasperation at Baroque Works' weirder members. In battle, she is sadistic and confident, mocking Nami as weak and enjoying the advantage of lethal spikes, but also pragmatic — she attempts to kill quickly without monologuing excessively. Powers
Toge Toge no Mi (Spike-Spike Fruit / Thorn-Thorn Fruit)

Paramecia-type Devil Fruit that allows user to grow sharp spikes/spines from any part of the body at will. Spikes can be short like sea-urchin quills covering the whole body (defensive urchin form) or elongated into needles, spears, and stiletto blades from limbs, hair, or heels. Oda demonstrates both piercing and hardening functions — spikes can puncture stone walls and block sword-like attacks.

Limit: Cannot generate spikes at range — must be attached to body. Standard Devil Fruit weaknesses apply: seawater/Seastone (kairoseki) nullifies power and user cannot swim; vulnerable to blunt force, fire, and electricity when not spiked. Nami exploits that spikes conduct electricity via Thunderbolt Tempo. Spikes do not grant superhuman durability beyond piercing defense; user can still be struck where spikes are not deployed. No attested Haki usage.

Assassin Combat & Espionage Skills

Trained Baroque Works Officer Agent: expert in undercover waitressing/espionage, close-quarters combat using spike-augmented martial arts (kicks, palm thrusts, hair needles), acrobatics, and use of high heels as stabbing weapons. Demonstrates professional coordination with Mr. 1 (Daz Bonez) — paired for high-value targets.

Limit: No superhuman strength beyond fruit augmentation; relies on fruit for lethal offense. Defeated by Nami's intellect and Clima-Tact trickery, not raw power, suggesting vulnerability to deception and elemental counters.

Stinger Techniques (Named Attacks)

Attested named moves: Double Stinger (both arms into spike spears), Stinger Hedgehog (full-body spike defense), Sea Urchin Stinger (rolling spike ball), Stinger Step (heel spike stab). Anime adds Stinger Flail. All are applications of Toge Toge no Mi, not separate powers.

Limit: Technique names are descriptive; no additional power beyond fruit. Anime-only names are non-canonical for manga purists.

Accounts I
Recruitment and Life as Paula at Spiders CafeOne Piece Chapter 160-161; Vivre Card No. 0514

Manga publication 2000; in-story circa 1520 Age of the Sea (Alabasta arc, before Straw Hat arrival)

Paula operates undercover as a waitress at Spiders Cafe, the desert oasis front for Baroque Works Officer Agents. At 8 o'clock, Crocodile's summons via Banchi the turtle brings all Officer Agents together. Miss Doublefinger arrives with Mr. 1 (Daz Bonez), her assigned partner per Baroque Works' gender-paired system. She serves and listens as Mr. 0's plan Operation Utopia (to seize Alabasta Kingdom using civil war and Pluton) is relayed via Mr. 2. Her calm professionalism establishes her as the reliable, unsentimental core of the mid-tier officers. Establishes Baroque Works hierarchy and Paula's rank; sets up her deployment to Alubarna for the final battle. No variant where she refuses orders.

Infiltration of Alubarna and the Palace ShowdownOne Piece Chapters 176-180, 190

2000-2001 publication; in-story Alabasta Civil War climax

Following Crocodile's instigation of rebellion, Officer Agents infiltrate the capital Alubarna. Miss Doublefinger and Mr. 1 are tasked with eliminating key resistance and Straw Hats at the palace. She stalks the palace corridors, encountering Nami who has been separated from the crew. Their confrontation is framed as assassin vs thief — both women who weaponize deception. Isolates Nami for one-on-one duel; demonstrates that Baroque Works intended to kill all Straw Hats, not just Vivi.

Nami vs Miss Doublefinger — The Thorn and the ThunderOne Piece Chapters 191-193; Anime Episodes 117-118

2001 publication

In the palace stairwell, Miss Doublefinger reveals Toge Toge no Mi, sprouting spikes from arms, legs, and hair. She overwhelms Nami physically, piercing walls and nearly impaling her, mocking Nami's Clima-Tact as a toy. Nami, using Usopp's imperfect weapon, initially fails. Remembering Vivi's courage and using tactical cunning, Nami creates a Mirage Tempo and then Thunder Tempo — exploiting that metal spikes conduct electricity. A summoned lightning bolt travels through Doublefinger's own spikes, electrocuting her. Doublefinger is defeated, unconscious, her spikes retracting. Nami stands over her, proving brains over brawn. Removes one Officer Agent from battlefield; gives Nami her first solo victory against a Devil Fruit user, crucial for crew credibility. Establishes Clima-Tact's combat viability. Doublefinger's defeat foreshadows Baroque Works collapse.

Accounts II
Collapse of Baroque Works and ArrestOne Piece Chapters 196, 211-212

2001 publication

After Crocodile's defeat by Luffy and the rain that ends the civil war, Marines (led by Tashigi and Smoker, then Hina) arrive to arrest Baroque Works. Miss Doublefinger, unconscious from Nami's attack, is taken into custody along with Mr. 1, Mr. 2 (later escapes), Mr. 4 and Miss Merry Christmas. Chapter 211 shows agents bound and defeated; Chapter 212 confirms imprisonment. Unlike Crocodile, she is not sent to Impel Down Level 6 — she is held in Alabasta regional prison. Ends her active criminal career in main storyline; removes Baroque Works as Alabasta threat. Sets up cover story redemption arc.

Miss Goldenweek's Operation: Meet Baroque Works — Prison Break and ReformationOne Piece Cover Story, Chapters 359-413 (33 installments)

2004-2005 publication; in-story shortly after Alabasta

Miss Goldenweek (Marianne), Miss Valentine (Mikita) and Mr. 5 escape Hina's capture and travel to free their comrades. They find Miss Doublefinger and Mr. 1 in a desert prison, still in striped uniforms. Using Goldenweek's Colors Trap art to manipulate guards and later to inspire the prisoners, they free everyone. Final installments show the reformed Baroque Works (including Doublefinger, now in casual clothes) opening a new Spiders Cafe — now a legitimate cafe/ spider-themed shop — choosing honest work over crime. Doublefinger is shown serving customers alongside former enemies, smiling genuinely for first time. Canonical confirmation of survival, release, and reformation; rare Oda redemption for villains. Explains why she does not reappear as antagonist post-timeskip. Considered canon by Oda and databooks.

Legacy Cameo and Absence in the New WorldOne Piece Chapters 540+ (Impel Down), 1058 (Cross Guild bounties), Vivre Card updates

2009-present publication

Miss Doublefinger has no active role in Impel Down, Marineford, Dressrosa, Wano, or Egghead arcs. She is not among Cross Guild or revived Baroque Works (now Cross Guild led by Crocodile, Mihawk, Buggy). Her absence is itself a story: Oda chose to leave reformed agents in Alabasta rather than recycle them. She appears only in flashback montages (e.g., Chapter 540 Straw Hat separation recollections, anime opening recaps) and in official popularity poll background art. Her legacy persists as Nami's benchmark fight, referenced in databooks when discussing Nami's growth. Confirms character is retired from main plot; leaves open potential future cameo if Alabasta revisited, but no attested return as of Chapter 1122 (2024).

Miss Doublefinger is a Logia who turns her whole body into spikes like a Logia element. — She is Paramecia: she grows spikes from her body but does not become spikes, cannot become intangible, and retains normal flesh between spikes. Logia would allow elemental transformation and intangibility; she can still be hit where spikes are not deployed.

She is the strongest female Baroque Works agent and could beat Nico Robin. — Hierarchy: Miss All Sunday (Nico Robin) was Mr. 0's partner and far stronger (bounty 79M, Ohara survivor, Hana Hana no Mi). Miss Doublefinger is Officer Agent ranked below Robin and arguably below Miss Goldenweek in strategic influence. Power scaling: Robin defeats Pell-level threats; Doublefinger loses to pre-Enies Lobby Nami.

Her real name Zala proves she is from Alabasta and has Arab-inspired origin. — No canonical hometown or ethnicity given; Oda has not stated inspiration. Paula/Zala is not Alabastan naming pattern (Alabasta names are often Egyptian/Arabic-inspired like Vivi, Cobra, but Paula is Western). Birthplace listed as unknown in Vivre Card.

Miss Doublefinger appears in Netflix live-action One Piece Season 1. — Season 1 (2023) adapts East Blue only (up to Arlong Park/Loguetown). Alabasta and Baroque Works Officer Agents have not appeared in live-action as of 2024; casting for Season 2 Alabasta is announced but Doublefinger actress not confirmed at research cutoff.

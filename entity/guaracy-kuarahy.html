Who They Are

Guaracy is the Guarani sun god, the ruler of the day and the source of warmth, light, and life. He is the brother of Tupã (the thunder god) and Jakaira (the god of mist). Guaracy's daily journey across the sky is the fundamental rhythm of Guarani life, and his return after the winter solstice is celebrated as the Guarani New Year. He is also the protector of warriors and hunters.

Cultural Significance

The Guarani solar cycle is fundamental to agricultural and ritual life. The New Year celebration at the winter solstice (June 21 in the Southern Hemisphere) involves community gatherings, prayer, and offerings to encourage Guaracy's return. The name Guaracy is a common personal name in Paraguay and Brazil, particularly among people of indigenous heritage. The daily rhythm of Guarani life — waking with the sun, working through the morning, resting at midday, and evening gatherings — reflects the understood relationship with Guaracy.

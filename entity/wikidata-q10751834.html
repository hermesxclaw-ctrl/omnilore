Etymology

Derived from Cristiano, the Italian, Portuguese, and Spanish form of Christian, ultimately from Latin 'Christianus' meaning 'follower of Christ'.

Domains & Habitats

The name is used in Italy, Portugal, Brazil, Romania, and other Portuguese- and Italian-speaking regions.

Version Differences

In Italian and Portuguese, it is the feminine form of Cristiano; in Romanian, it is the feminine form of Cristian. A modern short form is Tiana.

Who It Is
Form

The Philosopher's Stone is often described as a glowing, golden-colored substance with a subtle, shimmering quality. It is said to have the power to transform base metals into gold and to grant eternal life to those who possess it.

Powers

Transmutation The Philosopher's Stone has the power to transform base metals into gold and to grant eternal life to those who possess it.

Accounts

The story of the Philosopher's Stone is a central theme in European alchemy, with many alchemists seeking to create the stone and unlock its secrets.

The Philosopher's Stone is an important concept in Islamic alchemy, where it is associated with the concept of the 'Great Work' and the transformation of base metals into gold.

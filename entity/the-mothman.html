Who It Is

A six-to-seven-foot-tall winged humanoid with glowing red eyes, no visible head, and a 10-foot wingspan, sighted repeatedly in Point Pleasant over 13 months. The Mothman was a harbinger — its appearances preceded the December 15, 1967 collapse of the Silver Bridge, which killed 46 people. After the bridge collapse, the sightings stopped.

Cultural Significance

The Mothman has become a tourist industry for Point Pleasant (an annual Mothman Festival, a 12-foot statue) and a recurring figure in paranormal media. It represents the idea that monsters are not just biological anomalies but messengers — creatures that exist at the boundary between physical and symbolic reality.

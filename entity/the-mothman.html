Who It Is
Form

The Mothman is described as a humanoid creature with avian features, including large wings, glowing red eyes, and a human-like body. Its size is reported to be around 7 feet tall, with a wingspan of up to 10 feet. The creature's appearance is often described as eerie and unsettling.

Powers

Premonition The Mothman is said to have the ability to predict disasters and catastrophes, often appearing before a major event.

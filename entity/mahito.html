Who It Is

Born from the hatred humans bear toward one another, Mahito is a curse who wears humanity like a mask—and delights in unmasking it. In Jujutsu Kaisen, he is the incarnation of transfiguration, a being who can reshape souls as easily as clay, and whose greatest joy is the despair he causes. His existence poses a chilling question: what does it mean to be human when the soul itself can be remade?

Mahito is sadistic, playful, and intellectually curious about human nature. He experiments with souls to understand what makes humans human, often causing immense suffering. He is confident, arrogant, and enjoys psychological torment. Despite his cruelty, he shows a twisted form of respect for those who challenge his views, such as Yuji Itadori.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Mahito' (真人) in Japanese literally means 'true person' or 'genuine person.' In Jujutsu Kaisen, this is ironic, as Mahito is a curse born from human malice, not a human. The kanji 真 (ma) means 'true' and 人 (hito) means 'person.'

Pronunciation — MAH-hee-toh (Japanese: まひと)

Aliases — Mahito

Ownership — Jujutsu Kaisen, created by Gege Akutami, serialized in Weekly Shōnen Jump (Shueisha).

Powers
Idle Transfiguration

Mahito's innate technique allows him to manipulate the shape of a person's soul, thereby altering their body. He can reshape souls by touching them, causing mutations, deformities, or even instant death. He can also transfigure himself, changing his appearance and body structure at will.

Limit: Requires physical contact to use on others. He cannot directly alter a sorcerer's soul if they possess strong cursed energy or are aware of his technique. Certain barriers or techniques can block his touch.

Domain Expansion: Self-Embodiment of Perfection

Mahito's ultimate technique creates a domain where he can directly manipulate the souls of anyone inside, regardless of contact. The domain appears as a dark space with a giant, fleshy humanoid figure representing his own soul. Victims are trapped and immediately transfigured into grotesque creatures.

Limit: Requires a large amount of cursed energy; can be countered by a stronger domain or simple domain.

Transfiguration of others into 'humans'

Mahito can create cursed beings from transfigured humans, such as the 'Pandemonium' creatures used in the Shibuya Incident arc. These beings are loyal to him and serve as soldiers.

Limit: Each transfiguration consumes some of his cursed energy.

Form & Signs

Appearance — Mahito appears as a young man with short, spiky blue hair and teal eyes. He has a distinctive scar-like stitching pattern across his face, similar to Yuji Itadori's, but with a more sinister look. He typically wears a dark kimono-like outfit with a high collar, which he can alter at will. His appearance is deliberately human-like, but his expressions often reveal a malicious, inhuman quality.

Symbols — The stitched face is a visual motif representing his ability to manipulate souls. The blue color scheme evokes coldness and detachment.

Domains — As a curse, Mahito is born from negative human emotions, specifically the fear and hatred of other humans. He resides in the world of Jujutsu Kaisen, often operating in the shadows of human society.

Accounts I
Introduction and Encounter with YujiJujutsu Kaisen manga chapter 1, anime episode 1

2018 (fictional timeline)

Mahito is introduced as a curse who appears in a hospital, killing humans and creating curses from their souls. He encounters Yuji Itadori, who becomes his primary target due to Yuji's unique ability to host Sukuna. Mahito's actions lead to the death of Junpei and solidify Yuji's determination to defeat him.

Accounts II
Shibuya IncidentJujutsu Kaisen manga chapters 84-137, anime season 2 episodes 1-5

2018 (fictional timeline)

Mahito becomes a central antagonist during the Shibuya Incident, where he allies with other curses and Kenjaku to trap and kill many sorcerers. He engages in a prolonged battle with Yuji and eventually with Kento Nanami, whom he kills. He also transfigures many civilians into cursed creatures. Mahito's actions cause massive casualties and the sealing of Gojo Satoru. He is ultimately absorbed by Kenjaku, who takes over his body.

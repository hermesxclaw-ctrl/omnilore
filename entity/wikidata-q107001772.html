The Approach

In the sprawling universe of Japanese media, the name Suzuna appears across vastly different worlds—from a book-loving canine companion in a supernatural comedy to a determined martial artist in a historical romance, and a passionate cheerleader in a sports epic. Each Suzuna is a distinct character, yet they share a common thread: they are all memorable supporting figures who leave a lasting impression on their respective stories.

Content advisory. No dark material. Each character is part of a lighthearted or dramatic narrative with no graphic violence or mature themes beyond typical shonen manga content.

Etymology

The name Suzuna (鈴菜) is a Japanese given name, often written with characters meaning 'bell' (鈴) and 'greens/vegetable' (菜), though other kanji combinations are possible. It is a relatively uncommon name, often used for female characters.

Cultural Ownership

Japanese popular culture (manga and anime)

Disputed Identifications

There is no dispute; the three characters are distinct and unrelated. The name is simply reused across different series.

Description

Appearance

Suzuna Hīragi: A high school girl with long, dark hair and a typical school uniform. After reincarnation as a dachshund, she has a small brown dog form. Suzuna Kuraki: A young woman with long, dark hair, often depicted in traditional Japanese clothing or martial arts attire. Suzuna Taki: A teenage girl with short, brown hair and a cheerful expression, often wearing her cheerleading uniform.

Symbols & Iconography

For Suzuna Hīragi: books and scissors (the latter being the weapon of her partner). For Suzuna Kuraki: martial arts and cherry blossoms (typical of historical settings). For Suzuna Taki: pom-poms and a cheerleading uniform.

Behaviour & Personality

Suzuna Hīragi: Bookish, intelligent, and somewhat tsundere; she is often sarcastic but caring. Suzuna Kuraki: Strong-willed, loyal, and protective; she is a capable fighter but also gentle and supportive. Suzuna Taki: Energetic, outgoing, and supportive; she is enthusiastic about cheering and is a source of motivation for the team.

Domains & Habitats

Suzuna Hīragi: Modern-day Japan, primarily in the city where the story takes place. Suzuna Kuraki: Feudal or Meiji-era Japan, in a rural or traditional setting. Suzuna Taki: Modern-day Japan, in the urban environment of Tokyo.

Version Differences

These are three completely different characters with no connection. The only similarity is the name.

Dog & Scissors (Inu to Hasami wa Tsukaiyō)

Source Manga by Shunsuke Sarai, illustrated by Tetsuhiro Nabeshima; anime adaptationCulture JapanesePeriod 2010s

Summary

Suzuna Hīragi is a high school girl who loves reading. She is murdered by a serial killer but is reincarnated as a dachshund. She is discovered by the eccentric novelist Kazuhito Harumi, who can understand her. Together, they solve the mystery of her death and face various supernatural and comedic adventures.

Source Manga by Riichiro Inagaki, illustrated by Yusuke Murata; anime adaptationCulture JapanesePeriod 2000s

Who It Is
Form

The Ahool is described as a large, winged creature with bat-like features. Some accounts describe it as a giant bat, while others claim it is a flying primate. The creature is said to have a wingspan of up to 6 feet (1.8 meters) and a body length of up to 3 feet (0.9 meters).

Powers

Flight The Ahool is said to have the ability to fly, using its large wings to soar through the skies.

Accounts

The Ahool is said to have been a revered creature in Indonesian folklore, believed to possess magical powers and a deep connection to the natural world.

A hunter is said to have encountered the Ahool in the Salak Mountains, and was able to capture it using a magical net.

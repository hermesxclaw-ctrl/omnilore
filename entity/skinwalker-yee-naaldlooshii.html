Who It Is
Form

The Skinwalker is often described as a human who has the ability to transform into various animal forms, such as a coyote, wolf, or mountain lion. They are said to have supernatural powers and are feared by the Navajo people.

Powers

Shape-shifting The Skinwalker has the ability to transform into various animal forms, such as a coyote, wolf, or mountain lion.

Supernatural powers The Skinwalker is said to have supernatural powers, such as the ability to cause harm to humans and animals.

Accounts

The legend of the Skinwalker tells the story of a human who has made a pact with the devil and gained supernatural powers.

The story of the Skinwalker's Revenge tells the tale of a Skinwalker who seeks revenge against a group of people who have wronged it.

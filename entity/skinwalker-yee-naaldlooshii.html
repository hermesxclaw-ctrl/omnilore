Who It Is

In Navajo culture, a skinwalker (Navajo: 'yee naaldlooshii' — 'by means of it, it goes on all fours') is a type of harmful witch who has the ability to turn into, possess, or disguise themselves as any animal. Unlike European werewolves, which are almost always wolf-centric, the skinwalker can assume the form of a coyote, wolf, owl, fox, or crow — any creature that serves their dark purpose. Skinwalkers are the antithesis of Navajo cultural values: they gain power through taboo acts, including murder of a family member, incest, and corpse desecration. They are considered the most dangerous of all witches within Navajo belief. Due to cultural taboos, many Navajo are reluctant to discuss skinwalkers with outsiders, and the topic carries genuine fear to this day.

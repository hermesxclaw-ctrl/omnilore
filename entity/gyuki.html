Who It Is
Form

Gyūki is typically depicted as a bovine-headed monster with a spider-bodied yōkai, often with a menacing or aggressive posture.

Powers

Ambush Gyūki's ability to ambush travelers on lonely beaches, often targeting those who are lost or vulnerable.

Accounts

A group of travelers were attacked by an Ushi-oni on a beach in the Inland Sea region. The travelers were able to escape by performing a ritual to appease the demon.

A group of villagers in a coastal village were terrorized by a Gyūki that was said to have been awakened by a group of fishermen. The villagers were able to drive the Gyūki away by performing a ritual to appease the demon.

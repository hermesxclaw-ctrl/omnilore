Who It Is

The Eight-Tails, a massive bull-octopus chimera with eight tentacle-like tails. Gyūki is the most cooperative tailed beast, forming a genuine friendship with its Jinchūriki, Killer B. Its greatest ability is Ink Release and the power of regeneration — it can sever tentacles that then grow into independent bodies.

Powers

Ink Release (jutsu creation from ink)

Tentacle substitution (survives fatal blows)

Sealing jutsu resistance

Musical combat style (with B)

Who It Is
Form

The dragon turtle is a legendary creature that combines the shell of a turtle with a dragon's body. It is often depicted as a large, serpentine creature with a turtle's shell on its back, and is said to have the power to control the seas. In Feng Shui, the dragon turtle is a positive ornament that is believed to bring courage, determination, fertility, longevity, power, success, and support to those who possess it.

Powers

Control of the Seas The dragon turtle is said to have the power to control the seas and the tides, allowing it to summon powerful storms and calm the waves at will.

Accounts

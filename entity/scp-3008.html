SCP-3008

A Perfectly Normal, Regular Old IKEA

Who It Is

SCP-3008 is an IKEA retail furniture store that, from the outside, appears identical to any standard IKEA location. The entrance and exit function normally during daytime hours. However, once an individual enters deeper into the store and loses sight of the entrance for an extended period, they become trapped in an extradimensional space. The interior of SCP-3008 is vastly larger than its external dimensions suggest — estimates place it at a minimum of 10 square kilometers, with some survivors reporting seemingly infinite expanse. The interior contains all the expected features of an IKEA showroom: display rooms, cafeteria areas, warehouse sections, and endless aisles of flat-pack furniture. A day-night cycle exists within, simulated by the store's lighting systems dimming and brightening on a 24-hour cycle. During the 'night' period, the store's 'Employees' emerge. These humanoid entities, designated SCP-3008-2, resemble IKEA staff members — wearing the standard blue and yellow uniform — but their proportions are wrong. Their faces are blank and featureless, their limbs are slightly too long, and they speak only in distorted, garbled approximations of IKEA product names and employee phrases. SCP-3008-2 instances are uniformly hostile to humans after dark, attempting to capture or kill anyone they encounter. Survivors within SCP-3008 have formed small communities, constructing fortified settlements from furniture, establishing trade networks, and developing survival strategies. The Foundation maintains a permanent presence at the store's entrance, cordoning it off from the public while allowing monitored exploration. Interview logs with long-term survivors reveal a desperate existence of resource management, territorial disputes with other survivor groups, and constant vigilance against the Employees. SCP-3008 is particularly notable for its deceptively mundane exterior — shoppers enter unknowingly and may never be seen again. The entity plays on the universally recognized surrealism of large IKEA stores, where disorientation and the feeling of being trapped are part of the designed experience. The Foundation estimates that several thousand individuals may currently be lost within SCP-3008.

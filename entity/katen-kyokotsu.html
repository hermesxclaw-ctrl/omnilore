Who It Is

The Zanpakutō spirit of Shunsui Kyōraku. Katen Kyōkotsu manifests as two distinct female spirits: Katen, a mature woman in a flowered kimono who embodies the flowery, whimsical aspect, and Kyōkotsu, a young girl in traditional theater robes who represents the unpredictable, darker side. Together they represent the duality of theater — both comedy and tragedy. Their powers turn children's games into deadly reality.

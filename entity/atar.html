Who It Is
Form

Atar is often depicted as a divine flame or a burning fire, symbolizing the power of Ahura Mazda.

Powers

Purification Atar is believed to have the power to purify individuals and objects, symbolizing spiritual renewal and cleansing.

Accounts

According to the Avesta, Ahura Mazda created the sacred fire, which is associated with Atar, to symbolize the power of wisdom and knowledge.

The Vendidad describes the rituals of purification associated with Atar, which involve the performance of specific ceremonies and the use of sacred texts.

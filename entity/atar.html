Who It Is

Atar (Ātar, also known as Adar or Azar) is the Zoroastrian yazata of fire—the sacred flame that is simultaneously a physical element, a divine being, the presence of Ahura Mazda, and the central focus of Zoroastrian worship. Fire occupies a uniquely exalted place in Zoroastrianism: it is the visible symbol of asha (truth and order), the medium through which humans communicate with the divine, and the great purifier that burns away impurity and falsehood. In the Gathas, Zoroaster speaks of fire as the instrument through which Ahura Mazda will judge the righteous and the wicked at the final renovation. Atar is considered the 'son of Ahura Mazda,' a designation reflecting fire's direct emanation from the divine source. The Atar Nyayesh, a liturgical text recited daily by devout Zoroastrians, extols fire as the giver of warmth, light, and wisdom, and prays for its continued presence in homes and temples. Zoroastrian fire temples (Atashkadeh or Agiary) house sacred fires of different grades: the Atash Dadgah (household or community fire), the Atash Adaran (fire of fires, consecrated through a more elaborate ritual), and the highest grade, the Atash Behram (Fire of Victory), which requires the ritual combination of sixteen different types of fire—from lightning strikes, cremation pyres, royal hearths, artisan forges, and more—and months of purification ceremonies. Once consecrated, the sacred fire is never allowed to be extinguished and is tended continuously, five times a day, by priests who wear a cloth mask (padan) over their mouth and nose to avoid polluting the flame with their breath. In Zoroastrian theology, Atar pervades the entire creation as five distinct forms: the fire visible in material flame (berezi-savangha), the fire that animates living bodies (vohu-fryana), the fire in plants (urvazishta), the fire in clouds and lightning (vazishta), and the celestial fire that stands before Ahura Mazda (spanishta). Atar is also the great adversary of the demon Azi Dahaka and all creatures of druj, and at the end of time, the molten river of fire will purify all creation.

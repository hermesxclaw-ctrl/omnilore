Who It Is

Max Brass is not a name that echoes through the halls of ancient myth or the pages of classic literature; instead, it emerges from the vibrant, competitive world of modern video games, a champion forged in the digital arena of the ARMS Grand Prix. With his colossal physique, gleaming gold accents, and an unyielding belief in his own invincibility, Brass stands as a larger-than-life figure whose very existence is a celebration of spectacle, strength, and the relentless pursuit of victory.

Max Brass is portrayed as a flamboyant, egotistical champion who loves the spotlight. He is confident to the point of arrogance, often boasting about his strength and popularity. He is also shown to be honorable in a sportsmanlike way, respecting strong opponents. He is energetic, theatrical, and always ready to put on a show for his fans. He serves as both a mentor figure to the younger fighters and a fierce competitor.

Powers
ARMS (extendable arms)

Like all ARMS fighters, Max Brass has the ability to extend his arms to great distances, allowing him to punch from afar. His arms are covered in golden bands that act as his default ARMS weapons, the 'Nade' and 'Cracker' types, which can be customized in gameplay.

Limit: His arms are not as fast as some other characters', but they have high power. He cannot use his arms when they are knocked out of his hands during battle.

Super Armor (Bull Rush)

During his rush attack (a powerful special move), Max Brass gains super armor, making him resistant to flinching. This allows him to absorb hits and continue his assault.

Limit: The super armor is only active during the rush attack, which requires a full rush meter to activate.

Buff Mode

When Max Brass's health drops below a certain threshold, his body swells and his arms become even more powerful, increasing his damage output. This is a unique gameplay mechanic that encourages aggressive play when at low health.

Limit: The mode is temporary and only activates at low health, and it does not increase his speed or defense.

Form & Signs

He wears a golden championship belt, golden armbands (which are actually his ARMS weapons), and a red and gold outfit resembling a boxer's robe or wrestling gear. Symbols — Max Brass is associated with gold, championship belts, and the number '1' (as in the world's number one). His emblem is a stylized 'MB' monogram, often seen on his belt and gear. The color scheme of gold and red evokes victory, power, and showmanship.

Domains — Max Brass's domain is the ARMS Grand Prix arena, where he is the reigning champion. He is also seen in the game's training mode as a coach. He does not have a specific habitat outside of the tournament setting, as he is a modern athlete-like character.

The Rise of the ChampionARMS (2017) Grand Prix mode and official character bio

2017

Max Brass is the reigning champion of the ARMS Grand Prix, a global fighting tournament. He rose to fame through his unmatched strength and showmanship, becoming a beloved celebrity. In the game's story mode, he serves as the final opponent, testing the player's skills. After being defeated, he acknowledges the player as the new champion but continues to train and support them. Max Brass remains a central figure in the ARMS universe, serving as a mentor and friendly rival. His defeat does not diminish his popularity; instead, it cements the player's heroism.

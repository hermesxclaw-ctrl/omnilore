The Approach

Max Brass is not a name that echoes through the halls of ancient myth or the pages of classic literature; instead, it emerges from the vibrant, competitive world of modern video games, a champion forged in the digital arena of the ARMS Grand Prix. With his colossal physique, gleaming gold accents, and an unyielding belief in his own invincibility, Brass stands as a larger-than-life figure whose very existence is a celebration of spectacle, strength, and the relentless pursuit of victory.

Content advisory. No dark or disturbing material is known to be associated with this character. Content is suitable for general audiences, typical of a family-friendly fighting game.
Identity

Max Brass

Pronunciation The most common pronunciation is /mæks bræs/, with 'Max' rhyming with 'tacks' and 'Brass' rhyming with 'glass'. No alternate pronunciations are documented.

Etymology

The name 'Max Brass' is a portmanteau or alliterative combination: 'Max' as a colloquial term for 'maximum' or 'the greatest,' and 'Brass' as a metal associated with strength, durability, and a golden hue, fitting his color scheme and tough-guy persona. The name suggests 'maximum strength' and 'brass' as in audacity or nerve.

Cultural Ownership

Max Brass is a fictional character owned by Nintendo, created for the ARMS video game series. He is not part of any real-world cultural tradition, religion, or folklore.

Disputed Identifications

There are no disputed identifications. Some fans may confuse him with other ARMS characters, but his identity is clearly established within the game.

Description

Appearance

Max Brass is a large, muscular man with a bulky frame, standing taller than most other ARMS fighters. He has light skin, short dark hair, and a prominent square jaw. He wears a golden championship belt, golden armbands (which are actually his ARMS weapons), and a red and gold outfit resembling a boxer's robe or wrestling gear. His arms are disproportionately long and covered in metallic gold bands, reflecting the game's central mechanic. His face is often set in a confident smirk, and his eyes are usually hidden behind dark sunglasses, which he removes during his special moves.

Symbols & Iconography

Max Brass is associated with gold, championship belts, and the number '1' (as in the world's number one). His emblem is a stylized 'MB' monogram, often seen on his belt and gear. The color scheme of gold and red evokes victory, power, and showmanship.

Behaviour & Personality

Max Brass is portrayed as a flamboyant, egotistical champion who loves the spotlight. He is confident to the point of arrogance, often boasting about his strength and popularity. He is also shown to be honorable in a sportsmanlike way, respecting strong opponents. He is energetic, theatrical, and always ready to put on a show for his fans. He serves as both a mentor figure to the younger fighters and a fierce competitor.

Domains & Habitats

Max Brass's domain is the ARMS Grand Prix arena, where he is the reigning champion. He is also seen in the game's training mode as a coach. He does not have a specific habitat outside of the tournament setting, as he is a modern athlete-like character.

Version Differences

Within the ARMS game, Max Brass was initially a non-playable boss character, but was later made playable via downloadable content. His playable version has slightly adjusted stats and abilities compared to his boss version, but his core design and personality remain consistent. No other versions exist.

Powers & Abilities

ARMS (extendable arms)

Source Version ARMS (2017)

Description

Like all ARMS fighters, Max Brass has the ability to extend his arms to great distances, allowing him to punch from afar. His arms are covered in golden bands that act as his default ARMS weapons, the 'Nade' and 'Cracker' types, which can be customized in gameplay.

Limitations

His arms are not as fast as some other characters', but they have high power. He cannot use his arms when they are knocked out of his hands during battle.

Super Armor (Bull Rush)

Source Version ARMS (2017)

Description

During his rush attack (a powerful special move), Max Brass gains super armor, making him resistant to flinching. This allows him to absorb hits and continue his assault.

Limitations

The super armor is only active during the rush attack, which requires a full rush meter to activate.

Contradictions

None.

Buff Mode

Source Version ARMS (2017)

Description

When Max Brass's health drops below a certain threshold, his body swells and his arms become even more powerful, increasing his damage output. This is a unique gameplay mechanic that encourages aggressive play when at low health.

Limitations

The mode is temporary and only activates at low health, and it does not increase his speed or defense.

Stories & Myths

The Rise of the Champion

Source ARMS (2017) Grand Prix mode and official character bioCulture Video game culturePeriod 2017

Summary

Max Brass is the reigning champion of the ARMS Grand Prix, a global fighting tournament. He rose to fame through his unmatched strength and showmanship, becoming a beloved celebrity. In the game's story mode, he serves as the final opponent, testing the player's skills. After being defeated, he acknowledges the player as the new champion but continues to train and support them.

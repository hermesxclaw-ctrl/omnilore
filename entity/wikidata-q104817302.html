The Approach

In the world of Tensura, Veldora Tempest is not merely a storm dragon of apocalyptic power; he is a creature of paradox—a calamity who became a companion, a loner who found family, and a source of endless chaos whose greatest adventure began when he was sealed inside a cave and met a human who would change his fate forever.

Content advisory. Contains fantasy violence, destruction, and themes of isolation and redemption. No graphic or explicit content.

Etymology

The name 'Veldora' is likely derived from the English word 'tempest' (storm) and possibly the Old Norse 'Veld' (power) or a variation of 'Veldora' from the series' naming conventions. The surname 'Tempest' is given to him by Rimuru, signifying his new identity and bond.

Cultural Ownership

Created by Japanese author Fuse for the 'Tensura' franchise. The character is part of the broader isekai and fantasy genre. No specific cultural ownership beyond the creator's intellectual property.

Disputed Identifications

In the series, Veldora is sometimes confused with other True Dragons like Velzard and Velgrynd. Some fans debate whether his humanoid form is a true transformation or a temporary vessel. No real-world disputed identifications.

Description

Appearance

In his true form, Veldora is a colossal eastern dragon with dark blue/black scales, sharp claws, and glowing red eyes. He has a long serpentine body, a mane-like growth around his neck, and large horns. In his humanoid form, he appears as a tall, muscular young man with spiky blond hair, red eyes, and a confident smirk. He often wears a black-and-gold outfit.

Symbols & Iconography

The storm and lightning are associated with him, as well as the number '8' (as he is the eighth True Dragon). His symbol in the series is often a stylized dragon head or a storm cloud.

Behaviour & Personality

Veldora is arrogant, playful, and often childish. He is obsessed with manga and anime, having spent years in isolation. He is fiercely loyal to Rimuru and protective of those he considers friends. He loves to fight and often acts before thinking, but he is surprisingly wise when serious. He has a strong sense of pride as a True Dragon but is willing to learn from others.

Domains & Habitats

Originally, he resided in the Jura Forest, specifically in the Sealed Cave. After being freed, he often resides in Rimuru's nation of Tempest, either in his own room in the labyrinth or flying around the skies. He is also known to traverse dimensions.

Version Differences

In the web novel, Veldora's power and personality are similar but less detailed. The light novel expands his backstory and relationships. The anime and manga are largely faithful to the light novel, with minor differences in comedic timing and visual design. In the movie, he has a more prominent role in the climax.

Veldora can control storms, lightning, wind, and rain on a massive scale. He can summon hurricanes and thunderstorms that can devastate entire regions.

His power is immense but he may hold back to avoid excessive collateral damage, especially when around allies.

His regeneration is not instant if his core is damaged, but it is still formidable.

Stories & Myths

The Sealed Dragon

Source Light Novel Volume 1, Chapter 1; Anime Episode 1Culture Japanese isekai fantasyPeriod Beginning of the story

Summary

Veldora, the Storm Dragon, is sealed in a cave by a mysterious hero named Chloe (or possibly a group of heroes) for his rampages. He spends 300 years in isolation, reading manga and novels that he creates with his magic. He encounters Rimuru, a newly reincarnated slime, and becomes his friend, naming him Rimuru and receiving the name Tempest. They form a pact, and Rimuru promises to find a way to free him.

Participants

Veldora

Rimuru

Consequences

This meeting sets the stage for Rimuru's rise to power and the founding of the Jura Tempest Federation. Veldora becomes a core ally.

Variants

The web novel has a similar beginning but with slightly different dialogue and pacing.

The Liberation of Veldora

Source Light Novel Volume 2, Chapter 5; Anime Season 1 Episode 22-23Culture Japanese isekai fantasyPeriod After Rimuru's evolution to Demon Lord

Summary

Rimuru, now a Demon Lord, uses his ultimate skill 'Veldora's Wisdom' and 'Raphael' to analyze and break the seal on Veldora. Veldora is freed and immediately causes a storm that shakes the world, but he is calmed by Rimuru. He then takes on a humanoid form and becomes a resident of Tempest.

Participants

Rimuru

Veldora

Consequences

Veldora's freedom increases Tempest's military might. He becomes a symbol of the nation's power and a beloved figure.

Variants

In the anime, the scene is more dramatic with a larger storm.

Rampage and sealing

Source Light Novel Volume 1

Before the story, Veldora was known for causing storms and destruction, leading to his sealing. The extent of his rampage is not fully detailed, but it is implied that he caused significant damage.

Veldora's sister Velgrynd was enslaved by Emperor Rudra, which is a form of coercion and loss of free will. Veldora is deeply affected by this.

Contradictions & Disputes

Power level compared to other True Dragons

Position A

Veldora is the strongest True Dragon due to his unique abilities and evolution.

Position A — evidence

Veldora's title as 'Catastrophe' and his ability to evolve after being freed.

Position B

Veldora is not the strongest; Velzard and Velgrynd are stronger, and Guy Crimson is above all.

Position B — evidence

In the light novel, Velzard and Velgrynd are stated to be far more powerful, and Guy Crimson is the strongest being.

Scholarly Consensus

Veldora is powerful but not the strongest; he is often ranked below his sisters and Guy.

Veldora's role in the sealing

Position A

Veldora was sealed because he was rampaging and causing destruction.

Position A — evidence

The narrative implies he was a threat.

Position B

Veldora was sealed because the heroes feared his potential, not because of actual harm.

Position B — evidence

Later revelations show that the seal was placed as a precaution, and Veldora was not inherently evil.

Scholarly Consensus

The exact reason is ambiguous, but it is likely a combination of both.

Veldora's relationship with Rimuru

Position A

Veldora is subservient to Rimuru.

Position A — evidence

Veldora sometimes calls Rimuru 'boss' and follows his orders.

Position B

Veldora and Rimuru are equals, with Veldora being a close friend.

Position B — evidence

Their interactions are more like brothers, and Rimuru often relies on Veldora's wisdom.

Scholarly Consensus

They are equals in friendship, but Rimuru is the leader of Tempest.

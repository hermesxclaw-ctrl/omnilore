Who It Is

In the world of Tensura, Veldora Tempest is not merely a storm dragon of apocalyptic power; he is a creature of paradox—a calamity who became a companion, a loner who found family, and a source of endless chaos whose greatest adventure began when he was sealed inside a cave and met a human who would change his fate forever.

Veldora is arrogant, playful, and often childish. He is obsessed with manga and anime, having spent years in isolation. He is fiercely loyal to Rimuru and protective of those he considers friends. He loves to fight and often acts before thinking, but he is surprisingly wise when serious. He has a strong sense of pride as a True Dragon but is willing to learn from others.

Name & Tongue

Etymology — The name 'Veldora' is likely derived from the English word 'tempest' (storm) and possibly the Old Norse 'Veld' (power) or a variation of 'Veldora' from the series' naming conventions. The surname 'Tempest' is given to him by Rimuru, signifying his new identity and bond.

Powers
Storm Magic

Veldora can control storms, lightning, wind, and rain on a massive scale. He can summon hurricanes and thunderstorms that can devastate entire regions.

Limit: His power is immense but he may hold back to avoid excessive collateral damage, especially when around allies.

True Dragon Physiology

As a True Dragon, Veldora is an immortal, ageless being with immense magical power. He can regenerate from almost any wound, including having his body destroyed, as long as his core remains. He can also manipulate his size and form.

Limit: His regeneration is not instant if his core is damaged, but it is still formidable.

Magic Sense

Like all powerful beings, Veldora can sense magic and presences over vast distances.

Limit: He can be blocked by certain barriers or by beings with similar stealth abilities.

Universal Detect

A skill that allows him to detect any threat or presence across the world, but it is not unlimited and can be fooled by certain abilities.

Limit: Limited by range and can be blocked by strong magic.

Predator (shared with Rimuru)

While not his own skill, he benefits from Rimuru's skill, which allows him to analyze and copy abilities, and provides a connection to Rimuru's inner world.

Limit: He cannot use it independently; it is a shared benefit from his bond with Rimuru.

Temporal Manipulation

As a True Dragon, he has some resistance to time-based attacks, but he does not possess the ability to stop or reverse time himself.

Limit: Resistance only, not full immunity.

Energy Breath

He can unleash a devastating beam of energy from his mouth, capable of destroying mountains.

Limit: Requires a brief charging time.

Flight

He can fly at incredible speeds, both in dragon and humanoid form.

Limit: None.

Form & Signs

Appearance — In his true form, Veldora is a colossal eastern dragon with dark blue/black scales, sharp claws, and glowing red eyes. He has a long serpentine body, a mane-like growth around his neck, and large horns. In his humanoid form, he appears as a tall, muscular young man with spiky blond hair, red eyes, and a confident smirk. He often wears a black-and-gold outfit.

Symbols — The storm and lightning are associated with him, as well as the number '8' (as he is the eighth True Dragon). His symbol in the series is often a stylized dragon head or a storm cloud.

Domains — Originally, he resided in the Jura Forest, specifically in the Sealed Cave. After being freed, he often resides in Rimuru's nation of Tempest, either in his own room in the labyrinth or flying around the skies. He is also known to traverse dimensions.

Accounts I
The Sealed DragonLight Novel Volume 1, Chapter 1; Anime Episode 1

Beginning of the story

Veldora, the Storm Dragon, is sealed in a cave by a mysterious hero named Chloe (or possibly a group of heroes) for his rampages. He spends 300 years in isolation, reading manga and novels that he creates with his magic. He encounters Rimuru, a newly reincarnated slime, and becomes his friend, naming him Rimuru and receiving the name Tempest. They form a pact, and Rimuru promises to find a way to free him. This meeting sets the stage for Rimuru's rise to power and the founding of the Jura Tempest Federation. Veldora becomes a core ally.

The Liberation of VeldoraLight Novel Volume 2, Chapter 5; Anime Season 1 Episode 22-23

After Rimuru's evolution to Demon Lord

Rimuru, now a Demon Lord, uses his ultimate skill 'Veldora's Wisdom' and 'Raphael' to analyze and break the seal on Veldora. Veldora is freed and immediately causes a storm that shakes the world, but he is calmed by Rimuru. He then takes on a humanoid form and becomes a resident of Tempest. Veldora's freedom increases Tempest's military might. He becomes a symbol of the nation's power and a beloved figure.

The Battle with HinataLight Novel Volume 5, Chapter 4; Anime Season 2 Episode 26-28

After the Falmuth invasion

Hinata Sakaguchi, a holy knight, attacks Rimuru, believing he is a threat. Veldora arrives to help, but Rimuru tells him to stay back. The battle is intense, but Rimuru ultimately defeats Hinata using his skills. Veldora observes and later helps in the aftermath. The battle leads to a temporary peace and sets up future conflicts.

Accounts II
The Walpurgis BanquetLight Novel Volume 6, Chapter 5; Anime Season 2 Episode 38-39

After Rimuru becomes a True Demon Lord

Veldora accompanies Rimuru to the Walpurgis Banquet, a gathering of Demon Lords. He is recognized as a True Dragon and his presence intimidates many. He supports Rimuru's claim to become a Demon Lord. Rimuru is acknowledged as a Demon Lord, and Veldora's bond with him is solidified.

The War with the Eastern EmpireLight Novel Volumes 12-14; Anime Season 3 (planned)

After the founding of the Eastern Empire conflict

The Eastern Empire invades Tempest. Veldora participates in the defense, fighting against the Empire's forces, including the Emperor's elite soldiers. He also helps in the battle against Velgrynd, his own sister, who is under the control of the Emperor. He and Rimuru work together to defeat Velgrynd and free her. Velgrynd is freed from control and becomes an ally. The Empire is defeated, and Tempest's power grows.

The Labyrinth of TempestLight Novel Volume 9, Chapter 3; Anime Season 2 OVA

After the founding of the labyrinth

Veldora becomes the guardian of the deepest floor of Rimuru's labyrinth, a dungeon designed to train adventurers and generate income. He often plays pranks on visitors and enjoys the chaos. The labyrinth becomes a major tourist attraction and a source of income for Tempest.
